# decision-package

- **Runs where:** home, CI Linux
- **Schema impact:** none
- **Review:** auto

## Outcome

`fieldbook build` writes `templates/decision-package.xlsx` into the edition, byte-identical across builds of one commit: a synthetic example decision package of four sheets (Read-me, Summary, Cost build, Assumptions), labelled "preview", whose formulas use INDEX/MATCH lookups only and whose every formula cell carries a value cached by `fieldbook-calc`. The builder is two public functions, `decision_workbook(..) -> Result<Workbook, XlsxError>` and `save_workbook(&mut Workbook) -> Result<Vec<u8>, XlsxError>`, and its entry `render` calls both, so that `decide-port` can add a sheet between them. The home page lists the workbook.

## Why

- The workbook is the only one with formulas: [AD-13](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#ad-13-spreadsheet-outputs) (INDEX/MATCH only, cached values, a self-check cell whose cached value is "OK", `fullCalcOnLoad`, no `IFERROR` masking, an XML test, "preview" until spike S2 passes) and [Workbooks and templates](../reference/corpus-format.md#workbooks-and-templates) (the header block of edition number, tool version and as-of date, and the Read-me that explains the save prompt). Its content is O-05 (a one-page summary plus an appendix, every number pointing to an assumption id) and the trace columns of SM-03 and NFR-D-04 (claim id, source version, locator), in the requirements table of ADR 0001. Controls XL-02 (no active content) and DT-01 (byte-identical output) are in the [control register](../controls.md). S2 is in "Spikes" of ADR 0001; the Day-30 line of the AD-14 table asks for the calc types "as the decision package needs them".
- The two-function seam is what [decide-port](drafts/decide-port.md) needs: its step 8 calls `decision_workbook`, then `add_ai_sheet`, then `save_workbook`, and edits none of the existing sheets. [index-output-ports](drafts/index-output-ports.md) wraps the file this card writes as `decision-package@1`.
- Design: the [MAP.md](../design/MAP.md) rows `#decisions`, `#decision-<id>` (the workbook half: "`templates/decision-package.xlsx` first; pages with the R2 overlay") and `#templates` (TemplatesList lists `templates/<name>.xlsx`). [HANDOFF.md](../design/HANDOFF.md) section 5 (`DpSummary`: the seven summary rows; `KeyFigures`: a basis in every label; `OriginTag`: Quoted, Computed or Judgment, from `strings::ORIGIN_LABELS`; `OptionsCompare`: an unknown value reads "Not established") and the decision-package row of section 2. No token group: a workbook has none, and the home link reuses the `.tu-item` row. The six part headings of the Summary sheet are `strings::BRIEF_PARTS`; the seventh row is "Review state".
- Lead decisions where the repository is silent:
  1. **Content is a compiled-in synthetic example, never a read of the Corpus.** No package record exists before the R2 overlay, so the file is a fillable example in the shape of the mockup's synthetic package, with made-up figures and the word "synthetic" in its Read-me and Summary. It reads no row of the view, so the surface is content-free in the sense of the last bullet of "Writing a card" in the [cards README](README.md): the leak row proves public delivery and that the bytes do not depend on the view.
  2. **The trace columns hold synthetic placeholders, not Corpus ids.** A claim id, source version or locator that named a real Corpus row would dangle in a later edition. The example uses ids of the form `SYN-...` and "Not established" for an assumption with no source (the mockup's "No source yet"); the Read-me says to replace them. The five-minute trace of SM-03 is proved at Day 90 with R2.
  3. **No new `CALC_FNS` row.** The cached values come from the checked operations of `fieldbook-calc` (`mul`, `percent_of`, `add`, and `round_money_at_stage` at `Stage::Extension` for a line and `Stage::Total` for a total, scale 2). A named function needs cases, an epoch step and stored-run evidence ([Adding a calculation function](README.md#adding-a-calculation-function)), and the evidence needs the records of R2.
  4. **Preview is a constant.** `PREVIEW: bool = true` and the label text live in `decision.rs`. When the owner reports spike S2 passed, a one-line follow-up flips the constant and the label; this card does not.
  5. **Inputs are numbers, cached results are decimal text.** An input cell is written with `write_number` from the decimal string (`str::parse::<f64>`, no float arithmetic), and a formula's cached result is the exact decimal text of the calc value through `Formula::set_result`.
  6. **Errors.** A `CalcError` becomes `XlsxError::ParameterError` with its message; the site wrapper maps `XlsxError` to the existing `WorkbookError` (register `decision-package`), so no signature of `render_with_info` or `build.rs` changes.

## Steps

1. `decision.rs`, types. `DecisionInput` (package id, title, question, recommendation, a list of options, a list of unknowns, next action, review state and a list of `Assumption`), `Assumption` (id, text, unit word, `v1` and `v2` decimal strings, owner role, claim id, source version, locator, origin word from `strings::ORIGIN_LABELS`, status), and `synthetic_example()`: seven assumptions (direct material per unit, escalation percent, labour hours and labour rate per unit, overhead percent of labour, G&A percent of total cost input, profit percent of burdened cost) with made-up figures for version 1 and version 2, and at least one with no source (`Not established`). At least one labour figure and the overhead percent must be chosen so that the overhead line is not whole cents before rounding (for example 12.5 percent of 33.33 is 4.16625), so the rounding step does change a value and a skipped rounding is seen by the test. Mark every text synthetic. No real id, name or figure.
2. `decision.rs`, calculation. `cost_build(&DecisionInput, Version) -> Result<Vec<CostLine>, CalcError>`: material with escalation, labour, overhead, total cost input, G&A, burdened unit cost, profit and unit price, each line rounded to cents at `Stage::Extension` and each total the sum of the rounded lines at `Stage::Total`. Profit on cost is `div_at_stage` over the burdened cost: a zero denominator is `Value::Unknown(Reason::ZeroDenominator)`.
3. `decision.rs`, `decision_workbook(input: &DecisionInput, edition: &EditionInfo, workbook_epoch: i64) -> Result<Workbook, XlsxError>`. Sets the creation time from the epoch as `xlsx::render` does (the fixed fallback when the epoch is invalid) and writes, in this order:
   - **Read-me**: the preview label ("Preview. Not yet checked in the corporate Excel (spike S2).", from the constant), the header block (edition from `edition.edition`, tool version, as-of date from `edition.built`, or `strings::unreleased_build()` when it is empty), a line that the example is synthetic, the save prompt ("Excel asks to save after opening because the file recalculates on load; choose Save As into working\ and keep the edition copy unchanged."), and a line that an approved version is never overwritten, only superseded (O-05).
   - **Summary**: the six `BRIEF_PARTS` rows and a "Review state" row, as text; below them the key figures, each a label with its basis, its value found in Cost build by INDEX/MATCH on the line label, and its origin word.
   - **Cost build**: columns line, v1, v2, change, assumption id, origin. Each line's v1 and v2 is a formula over values that INDEX/MATCH finds in Assumptions by assumption id. The change column is a difference. A "Check" row holds the self-check formula (the sum of the lines equals the unit price, both rounded to cents) with cached value "OK". Profit on cost shows "n/a: zero denominator" for a zero denominator (a formula with an explicit zero test, never `IFERROR`).
   - **Assumptions**: columns id, text, unit, v1, v2, owner role, claim id, source version, locator, origin, status. Every cell is `write_string` or `write_number`; frozen header row and autofilter as `xlsx.rs` does.
   Every formula cell is written with `write_formula` and `Formula::set_result` from the calc value. Every sheet that holds a formula calls `Worksheet::set_formula_result_default("")` before its first formula, so a formula written without `set_result` has an empty cached value; the writer's own default, `0`, cannot be told from a computed zero. Formulas may use only `INDEX`, `MATCH` (exact, match type 0), `ROUND`, `SUM` and `IF`.
4. `decision.rs`, `save_workbook(&mut Workbook) -> Result<Vec<u8>, XlsxError>` calls `save_to_buffer`, and `render(edition: &EditionInfo, workbook_epoch: i64) -> Result<Vec<u8>, XlsxError>` calls `synthetic_example`, `decision_workbook` and `save_workbook` in that order and nothing else. Add `pub mod decision;` to `lib.rs`.
5. `site.rs`: `site.insert("templates/decision-package.xlsx", ..)` from `decision::render(context.edition_info(), info.workbook_epoch)`, mapping the error as lead decision 6 says, and the same path in `planned_paths`. `home.rs` and `index.html`: one `tu-item` row in the Templates panel, "Decision package (preview)", linked by `href_if_planned` and with a one-line description that says it is a synthetic example; with no planned path the row shows the frozen `not_available` line as the registers row does.
6. `xtask/src/edition.rs`: append `templates/decision-package.xlsx` to `REQUIRED_FILES`, and add `missing_decision_package_is_fb0601` to `mod tests` (delete the file from the staged tree and expect FB0601 to name it).
7. Tests in `crates/fieldbook-render/tests/decision.rs`, reading the workbook back with `zip` through a small reader of the sheet XML parts of this file (cell, formula, cached value): `core_xml_has_the_fixed_time`; `workbook_without_epoch_has_fixed_creation_time` (exactly `2000-01-01T00:00:00Z`); `full_calc_on_load_is_set` (`fullCalcOnLoad="1"` in `xl/workbook.xml`); `no_macro_external_link_hyperlink_dde_volatile_or_dynamic_array` (`// control: XL-02`; no `vbaProject.bin`, no `externalLinks/`, no hyperlink relationship, no `ddeLink`, none of `NOW`, `TODAY`, `RAND`, `RANDBETWEEN`, `OFFSET`, `INDIRECT`, `INFO`, `CELL` and none of `FILTER`, `SORT`, `UNIQUE`, `SEQUENCE`, `LET`, `LAMBDA`, `XLOOKUP`, no `cm=` dynamic-array attribute; the failure names the sheet and the cell); `readback_flags_a_web_hyperlink` (`// control: XL-02`; a workbook made in the test with `write_url("https://example.invalid")` is reported with its sheet and cell); `formulas_use_only_the_allowed_functions_and_exact_match`; `every_formula_cell_has_a_cached_value` (every formula cell on every sheet has a non-empty cached value, and the failure names the sheet and the cell); `cached_values_equal_an_independent_recomputation` (the test recomputes every line from the input strings with `fieldbook-calc` and compares the cached text, naming the cell such as `Cost build!C9`); `self_check_cell_is_ok`; `zero_denominator_reads_n_a_and_no_iferror` (an input whose burdened cost is zero); `summary_rows_use_the_frozen_part_headings_and_review_state`; `header_block_holds_edition_tool_version_and_as_of` (released and unreleased `EditionInfo`); `preview_label_is_on_read_me_and_summary`; `every_cost_line_names_an_assumption_that_exists` and `assumptions_have_the_trace_columns`; `decision_workbook_then_save_workbook_equals_render` (the two functions in order give the bytes of `render`, and a sheet added between them appears last with the other sheets unchanged: the decide-port seam); `two_builds_same_bytes` (`// control: DT-01`; two builds a second apart, then a build with another epoch differing only in `docProps/core.xml`); `site_plans_and_builds_the_template` (the key is planned and built and `planned_paths_equal_the_site_keys` holds); `home_links_the_decision_package`.
8. `leak_outputs.rs`: surface `decision_package`, test `delivered_decision_package_is_public_and_complete`: through the real edition handler and sink (as `delivered_workbook_and_csvs_filter_rows` does) read `templates/decision-package.xlsx` from the delivered folder, require it equals `decision::render` for the same `EditionInfo` and epoch, and require no canary of the mixed view in any cell. Register it in `BUILT_SINKS` and `SURFACES`.
9. `CHANGELOG.md` line. Run `just site` and record the file size in the pull request.

```sh
cargo nextest run --locked -p fieldbook-render --test decision
cargo nextest run --locked -p fieldbook-render --test frame planned_paths_equal_the_site_keys
cargo nextest run --locked -p xtask edition::tests::missing_decision_package_is_fb0601
cargo nextest run --locked -p fieldbook --test leak_outputs
just site
ls out/edition/templates
T="$(mktemp -d)" && cp out/edition/templates/decision-package.xlsx "$T/first.xlsx" && just site && cmp "$T/first.xlsx" out/edition/templates/decision-package.xlsx && echo same
just check
```

Expected: the tests pass; `ls` prints `decision-package.xlsx`; the second build prints `same`; `just check` exits 0.

## Negative control

In `decision.rs`, write one formula as `NOW()`: `cargo nextest run --locked -p fieldbook-render --test decision no_macro_external_link_hyperlink_dde_volatile_or_dynamic_array` must fail and name the sheet and the cell. Restore it. Drop the `set_result` of one formula: `every_formula_cell_has_a_cached_value` must fail. Restore it. Skip the rounding of the overhead line, whose example figure of step 1 is sub-cent before rounding: `cached_values_equal_an_independent_recomputation` must fail and name the overhead cell, such as `Cost build!B6`. Restore it. Ignore the epoch and use a fixed other date: `core_xml_has_the_fixed_time` must fail. Restore it. Remove the `site.insert` of the workbook: `site_plans_and_builds_the_template` must fail. Restore it.

## Rollback

Revert the merge commit. The edition loses `templates/decision-package.xlsx` and the Templates panel its row. Nothing else read the file: `decide-port` and `index-output-ports` are not built, and each names this card as a precondition.

## BLOCKED

`rust_xlsxwriter` 0.101 cannot write a text cached result for a formula, or cannot write a byte-identical file for one creation time: set the item to Blocked with a `BLOCKED:` comment that says so, and stop. Do not post-process the zip by hand and do not write a formula without its cached value. A step that needs a `fieldbook-calc` operation that does not exist (`percent_of`, `div_at_stage`, `round_money_at_stage`): comment `BLOCKED: decision-package needs <operation>` and do not write float arithmetic. A precondition has not merged: comment `BLOCKED: decision-package waits on <slug>`, push the branch and take the next card.
