# calc-day1

- **Runs where:** home, CI Linux
- **Schema impact:** none
- **Review:** auto

## Outcome

`fieldbook-calc` holds the Day-1 money core: the decimal grammar, `Money`, `Value<T>`, `RoundingPolicy`, checked operations, `Unit`, and the dates: the civil date, the partial date, the period and the duration. It uses no float, no clock and no unchecked index, and an overflow is an error, never a wrapped or rounded number. Text that is not valid is a typed error, never a panic.

## Why

- The decision, the grammar, `Value<T>`, the rounding stages and the Day-1 scope are in [AD-14](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#ad-14-money-and-the-calculation-core-fm0). The cases this core must answer are the Day-1 calculation cases CALC-01 to CALC-04 in [FM0](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#calculation-core-fm0); `calc-cases` adds them.
- The codes the errors map to are in [05: calculation](../reference/corpus-format.md#05-calculation). The controls are in [Determinism and arithmetic](../controls.md#determinism-and-arithmetic).
- The crate is the leaf of [AD-05](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#ad-05-crate-layout-and-enforcement). The bans on `Decimal::round` and `round_dp` are in the root `clippy.toml`.
- The dates, the period, the duration and `Unit` are in [AD-14](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#ad-14-money-and-the-calculation-core-fm0) (Dates and units), because rules R-02, R-04, R-06, R-15 and R-17 are date and unit rules. This card builds the types only: no function counts business days, adds months or reads a calendar yet (`later-calendar`, `later-calc-functions`).
- The seeded generator is in [Test data](../reference/testing.md#test-data). Every parser has a test that feeds it random and mutated bytes; they share one generator, so a seed means the same everywhere, and `one_rng_exists` fails a second copy. This card is the first in plan order that parses hand-written text, so it creates the generator; the other parser cards include it.

## Scope

- Files: `crates/fieldbook-calc/src/{lib.rs,decimal.rs,money.rs,value.rs,rounding.rs,ops.rs,error.rs,unit.rs,date.rs,period.rs}`, `crates/fieldbook-calc/tests/{types.rs,dates.rs,units.rs}`, `tests/support/rng.rs`, `xtask/tests/one_rng.rs`.
- Docs to update: none. The crate adds no command and no code.

## Out of scope

- Case files, the case evaluator, the function table `CALC_FNS`, the `Run` record and FB0500 to FB0504 in `check` (`calc-cases`).
- Any function on dates: adding months or years to a date, counting business days, a calendar. An `EffectivePeriod` and a `Duration` are values here and nothing computes with them beyond the methods of step 10.
- Rates, escalation, sensitivity and the other Day-30 calculations. They are the pricing module, built after the knowledge base: pricing, estimating and the indirect rate book first, then EAC and EVM ([PLAN](../plan/PLAN.md), P7 and P8).
- Any change to `Cargo.toml` or `Cargo.lock`. `rust_decimal` and `serde` are wired by `day1-dependencies-3`.

## Preconditions

- Cards merged: `day1-dependencies-3`.
- This card does not touch `fieldbook-core`, so it may run beside the check cards. It comes before `core-ids-and-dates` in the plan, because that card re-exports the dates and includes the generator that this card creates.

## Steps

1. `decimal.rs`: parse a decimal by the grammar of AD-14 with `Decimal::from_str_exact`, after a hand-written check of the grammar. Implement `Deserialize` and `Serialize` for the crate's own `Dec` type, so no caller reaches `rust_decimal`'s own serde. Text in, text out: the canonical form keeps the scale it was written with. `Dec::parse(&str) -> Result<Dec, CalcError>` follows the decimal grammar: optional minus, digits and an optional dot followed by digits; other forms are `CalcError::Grammar`.
2. `money.rs`: `pub struct Currency([u8; 3])`, with `Currency::parse(&str) -> Result<Currency, CalcError>` (exactly three ASCII capitals, no table lookup; otherwise `CalcError::Grammar`), `as_str(&self) -> &str` and `Display`; `Money { pub amount: Dec, pub currency: Currency }`. Adding or comparing money values with different currencies is an error. `Quantity` belongs to the later calculations; this card does not define it.
3. `value.rs`: `Value<T>` is `Known(T)` or `Unknown(Reason)`. Every operation on an `Unknown` returns it unchanged. `Reason` is a closed enum: `ZeroDenominator` and `Inexact` (`Inexact` is added in step 8). A zero denominator returns `Unknown(Reason::ZeroDenominator)`.
4. `rounding.rs`: `RoundingPolicy` with the shared scale, validated constructor and default from the rounding contract, `round_at_stage` with that contract’s exact signature, and `Stage`, a closed enum of the three stages of AD-14: `UnitPrice`, `Extension` and `Total`. A case file names a stage by its snake_case word (`unit_price`, `extension`, `total`); `Stage::word()` and `Stage::from_word()` convert, and an unknown word is `CalcError::Grammar`. The rounding module owns every rounding decision, taking a stage and policy. Decimal inputs use `round_dp_with_strategy`; the exact quotient helper of step 5 applies the same mode directly to its integer quotient and remainder. No intermediate decimal division or multiplication may round before this named stage.
5. `ops.rs`: `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `div` and `percent_of`, each using exact scaled-integer arithmetic and checked bounds, each returning `Result<Value<_>, CalcError>`. Signatures: `add(a: Value<Money>, b: Value<Money>) -> Result<Value<Money>, CalcError>` and `sub` the same; `mul(a: Value<Money>, factor: Value<Dec>) -> Result<Value<Money>, CalcError>`; `div(a: Value<Money>, divisor: Value<Dec>) -> Result<Value<Money>, CalcError>` (a zero divisor is `Unknown(ZeroDenominator)`); `percent_of(amount: Value<Money>, percent: Value<Dec>) -> Result<Value<Money>, CalcError>` (the amount times the percent divided by 100). For unit conversion, also expose `mul_dec(a: Value<Dec>, b: Value<Dec>) -> Result<Value<Dec>, CalcError>` and `div_dec(a: Value<Dec>, b: Value<Dec>) -> Result<Value<Dec>, CalcError>` with the same exactness, unknown propagation and zero-denominator rule. `Known` requires an exactly representable result: use the signed decimal coefficients and scales, retaining exact intermediate coefficients and remainders before testing the final decimal bounds. Precision loss is `Unknown(Inexact)`; a result outside the magnitude range is `CalcError::Overflow`. Do not infer exactness from a rounded decimal result or a reverse multiply. Exact fractions such as `1 / 2` succeed. `percent_of` evaluates its exact product divided by 100 before the representability test. Add `div_at_stage(a: Value<Money>, divisor: Value<Dec>, stage: Stage, policy: &RoundingPolicy) -> Result<Value<Money>, CalcError>`: round the exact rational quotient once at the named stage, using quotient/remainder and the policy scale/mode. It shares the rounding decision of step 4; it must not call ordinary `div` and then round its rounded approximation. No ordinary operation rounds.
6. `error.rs`: `CalcError` with `Grammar`, `Overflow`, `Mismatch` and `InvalidScale`. Their `code()` values are respectively `FB0500`, `FB0501`, `FB0502` and `FB0500`; `fieldbook-core` maps that text to its `Code`, because this crate cannot see `Code`.
7. In `lib.rs`, keep the deny line (the float and indexing lints and `unsafe`), and delete the test `float_and_indexing_bans_are_declared` and its comment if they are still there: the test searched its own source for text that it contains, so it could not fail. The weekly `lint-controls` job of `scheduled-workflow` is the control for the bans. Update the crate doc comment: the dependencies were wired by `day1-dependencies-3`, and the crate also owns the dates, the period, the duration and `Unit` (AD-14).
8. `unit.rs`: `Unit` is a closed enum of the non-currency words of the `unit` row of [Claim vocabularies](../reference/corpus-format.md#claim-vocabularies): `percent`, `basis_points`, `days`, `business_days`, `weeks`, `months`, `years`, `hours`, `count`, `ratio`, `each`, `per_100`, `per_1000` and `per_dozen`. A currency code is not a `Unit`; `Money` keeps its `currency`. Write `Unit::ALL`, `word()`, `from_word()` (an unknown word is `CalcError::Grammar`), `is_time()` (true for `days`, `business_days`, `weeks`, `months`, `years` and `hours`), `family()` and an exact rational conversion factor. Ordinary families and their multipliers to the base: days (`days` 1, `weeks` 7); months (`months` 1, `years` 12); hours (`hours` 1); business days (`business_days` 1); percent (`percent` 1, `basis_points` 1/100); ratio (`ratio` 1); count (`count` 1). Price bases are denominators, not multipliers: `each` 1, `per_100` 100, `per_1000` 1000, `per_dozen` 12. `convert(amount: Dec, from: Unit, to: Unit) -> Result<Value<Dec>, CalcError>` converts only inside one family (across two families it is `CalcError::Mismatch`), using one exact rational calculation: ordinary units use `amount × from_factor / to_factor`; price bases use `amount × to_basis / from_basis`. Cancel integer factors before the representability test; never construct a rounded reciprocal of twelve or use rounded reverse multiplication. A nonrepresentable exact result is `Unknown(Reason::Inexact)`, so no conversion rounds silently. Add `Reason::Inexact` to `value.rs`. A new unit is added by the recipe "Adding a unit" in the [cards README](README.md#adding-a-unit).
9. `date.rs`: `CivilDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, a real calendar with leap years) with `parse`, `Display`, ordering, `add_days(i64)` (checked: a result outside the years 0001 to 9999 is `CalcError::Overflow`), `days_between` (signed) and `from_epoch_seconds(i64)` for `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`. `PartialDate` (`YYYY`, `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY-MM-DD`) prints as written and compares as its first day; a year-only date is never turned into a full date by arithmetic (R-06): `to_civil()` is `None` unless it was written in full. Text that is not a date is `CalcError::Grammar`. Nothing here reads a clock. `fieldbook-core` re-exports these types (`core-ids-and-dates`).
10. `period.rs`: `EffectivePeriod { start: CivilDate, end: Option<CivilDate> }`, an effective period (R-15) whose two ends are inclusive and whose open end means "until superseded". `EffectivePeriod::parse` reads `YYYY-MM-DD/YYYY-MM-DD` and `YYYY-MM-DD/`; an end before the start is `CalcError::Grammar`. Methods: `contains(CivilDate) -> bool` and `overlaps(&EffectivePeriod) -> bool`. `Duration` has private `amount: i64` and `unit: Unit` fields, read-only accessors and `Duration::new(amount: i64, unit: Unit) -> Result<Duration, CalcError>`, which accepts only `is_time()` units (any other is `CalcError::Mismatch`). `days() -> Result<Option<i64>, CalcError>` returns `Some` for `days` and `weeks`, `None` for the other time units and `CalcError::Overflow` if checked week-to-day multiplication overflows; it is the form of a publication lag (R-17).
11. Write `tests/support/rng.rs` at the repository root, the one seeded generator of the workspace: SplitMix64 with `Rng::new(seed)`, `next_u64()` and `bytes(n)`. No `rand` crate, no float, no indexing and no `unwrap`, so it passes the workspace lints. A test in the file pins the first three outputs of seed 0 from the published reference implementation: `0xe220a8397b1dcdaf`, `0x6e789e6aa1b965f4` and `0x06c45d188009454f`. A test file includes it with `#[path = "../../../tests/support/rng.rs"] mod rng;` (the depth of the path follows the including file). Declare it at the top level of the test file, outside any inline `mod`, and reach it from an inline module as `super::rng`.

Write `xtask/tests/one_rng.rs` with `one_rng_exists`: scan every `.rs` file under `crates/` and `xtask/`, strip underscores, lower-case the text, and fail naming any file that contains the SplitMix64 constant `9e3779b97f4a7c15`. Build the constant from two halves inside the test, so the test file does not contain it whole. The generator at `tests/support/rng.rs` lies outside the two folders; a second copy inside them fails the test.
13. Tests in `tests/types.rs`, in `mod types`: `grammar_accepts` and `grammar_rejects` (tables of strings, each rejection named in the failure); `from_str_exact_keeps_scale`; `overflow_is_an_error`; `add_and_sub_are_exact`; `mul_scales_an_amount`; `div_divides_an_amount`; `percent_of_is_amount_times_percent_over_100`; `zero_denominator_is_unknown`; `unknown_propagates`; `decimal_ops_are_checked` (known, unknown and overflow for both decimal operations, plus a zero denominator for `div_dec`); `mixed_currency_is_an_error`; `midpoint_rounds_away_from_zero` (synthetic values at each stage); `serde_round_trip`; and `random_and_mutated_bytes_never_panic`, which uses the generator of step 11 (declared at the top of `tests/types.rs`, outside `mod types`): 200 byte strings of length 0 to 64 from `Rng::new(1)`, and single-byte flips and deletions of a valid example for each parser: `-1234.5000` for `Dec::parse`, `2024-02-29` for `CivilDate::parse`, `2024-02` for `PartialDate::parse`, `2024-01-01/2024-12-31` for `EffectivePeriod::parse`, `weeks` for `Unit::from_word`, `USD` for `Currency::parse` and `total` for `Stage::from_word`. Feed the random strings to every parser and each example's mutations to its own parser through `String::from_utf8_lossy`. ## Negative control

Add `let _ = 1.0_f64 * 2.0;` to a function in `ops.rs`. The clippy line must fail with `float_arithmetic`. Remove it. Then replace `checked_mul` with `*` in `mul`: `types::overflow_is_an_error` must fail or panic. Restore the file. Then treat 1900 as a leap year in `date.rs`: `dates::century_years_follow_the_leap_rule` must fail. Restore the line. Then change the factor of `weeks` to 6: `units::weeks_convert_to_days_exactly` must fail. Restore the line. Then write the SplitMix64 constant into a file under `crates/`: `one_rng_exists` must fail and name the file. Remove it.

Accept a two-letter currency: `currency_accepts_three_capitals_only` must fail. Restore it.

Make `div_dec` turn a zero divisor into zero or let `Duration::new` accept `each`: `decimal_ops_are_checked` or `duration_refuses_a_non_time_unit` must fail. Restore each.

Bypass the exact coefficient/remainder test: `ordinary_ops_refuse_precision_loss` and the ten-day conversion must fail. Pre-round the quotient to a Decimal before the named stage: `stage_quotient_rounds_once` must fail. Reverse the price-basis ratio: the price conversion cases must fail. Restore each.

Change the default scale from 2 or its mode: `default_policy_pins_stage_scales` must fail. Accept scale 29: the invalid-scale test must fail. Restore each.

## BLOCKED

- AD-14 and the 05 range disagree on which code an error maps to: name both lines and ask the lead.
- `rust_decimal` has no `from_str_exact` or `round_dp_with_strategy` at the locked version: stop. A version change is a lock change and needs the owner.
- The three outputs pinned for seed 0 in `rng.rs` differ from what the algorithm gives: recompute them with the published C reference of SplitMix64, write both sets in the pull request description and stop; the lead confirms which is right.
