# assistant-page

- **Runs where:** home, CI Linux
- **Schema impact:** none
- **Review:** auto

## Outcome

Every built edition has the Assistant page (`assistant.html`) and the Ask drawer on every page, both on Handoff: the page builds a context from a question with deterministic retrieval, the owner pins, removes and adds items and saves named context packs, **Copy context pack** checks the complete payload against separate clipboard permission before writing `ASSISTANT.md`, the question and the items; a refusal exposes selectable text for manual handoff, and the owner pastes the assistant's answer back and presses **Check citations**. The answer is shown with the nine cite-check statuses, and **Copy as draft note** uses the same check and manual fallback for draft-note text for `new note`. The page makes no request and says "not recorded". `assist.js` holds a port of `context_pack`, `apply_edits`, `resolve_pack` and `cite_check` that the Edge harness holds to the goldens of `handoff-context` and `handoff-cite-check`. The page has one seam, `FB_ASSIST.use(adapter)`, through which `chat-bridge` adds live answers; this card tests the seam with a stub adapter.

## Why

- The page, the drawer and the adapter states are the chat of [HANDOFF.md](../../design/HANDOFF.md) §9, as [AD-15](../../decisions/0001-foundation.md#ad-15-ui-technology-and-design-system) (The Assistant page) and AD-16 place them. Handoff is the default and the only transport of a page opened from a file. Control HG-01 (nothing reaches a context except what the `handoff` row of the policy allows), HG-03 (the question scan, in the browser too), HG-05 (the cite-check statuses), HG-06 (a note is AI-drafted and unreviewed) and WB-03 (script growth: `assist.js` at most 20 KB, no `fetch`, no module) are in [controls.md](../../controls.md); the storage rule is the Storage bullet of AD-15 and WB-04.
- Three engines, each held by goldens and none rewritten here: ranking by `search.js` (`search-js`), over the Assistant index of step 4 for retrieval and over `FB_INDEX` for the palette (the edition's `FB_INDEX` holds page and section units only, so the palette finds no claim; the Assistant index holds every unit of its permitted view), the context builder (`tests/golden/context/*.json` of `handoff-context`) and the cite-check (the 20 seed cases in `tests/golden/handoff/` of `handoff-cite-check`). The Rust code is the reference. A difference fails the Edge harness and names the case.
- The page learns nothing from the Corpus at run time except `assist-data.js`, which `fieldbook-core` writes from `assist::export_units` and `assist::cite_facts`. Those two functions are the only ones that say which units an assistant may see and what the cite-check needs, so the page cannot know a unit that the `handoff` row of the policy does not allow. The file also names the labels it was cut for (`allowed`), which the page shows and never widens. The frozen words, the nine status names and the eight labels come from `fieldbook-core::strings` through the same file; no script holds one as a literal (`cargo nextest run --locked -p xtask literals::` and a test).
- Capabilities, not modes. A page opened from a file uses `ChatCaps::HANDOFF` (everything false), shows "No model connected here" and "This session only", and offers **Copy context pack** and **Copy as draft note**. An adapter that registers through `FB_ASSIST.use` switches Send, Stop, Try again and **Save as note** on by its `caps` and its state; the layout is the same (the state and capability table is in step 8).
- The Meter reads RequestBudget: Unicode scalar characters used, limit and overage. A word estimate may be shown separately and never controls Send.
- Size: if sizing shows the card is too large, steps 9 and 10 (the drawer, the other entry points, the storage) are a card of their own, `assistant-entry-points`, and this card keeps the page. A readable `assist.js` that cannot stay within 20 KB is not minified: see BLOCKED.
- Design: the [MAP.md](../../design/MAP.md) rows `#assistant`, `#assistant/<id>` (ThreadList, Message, ThreadNote, AdapterStatus, Composer, CheckedAnswer, VerdictChips, Fold, ReadFold, AnswerActions, ContextPanel, ContextItem, ContextMeter, ContextBar, RelatedPanel), the Ask drawer (AskDrawer, SelectionToolbar), Today's composer and the area starters, and ShortcutsDialog (`.` for Ask). [HANDOFF.md](../../design/HANDOFF.md) §9 (Adapters, Assistant page, Context panel, Entry points, Context pack, Cite-check), §6 States and interactions, §7 Status display rules and §11 Edge cases (scripts blocked, storage). Token groups: colour (`--color-mark`, the status colours), type, space, shape and line, motion (`--dur-pulse`), layout (`--rail-w`). Interface lane: confirm the browser keys `fieldbook.packs` (localStorage; the saved context packs) and `fieldbook.ask` (sessionStorage; the question that Open in Assistant hands over); the card works without the answer.

## Out of scope

- Live answers, the token, the previewed request, Stop on the wire, **Save as note** and packs on the server (`chat-bridge`). This card ships the seam and a stub; `bridge.js` calls `FB_ASSIST.use` after its status call.
- Threads kept across a reload, and the model asking for more items: `later-chat-threads` and `later-chat-request-items`. Streaming: `later-streaming`. A thread is in the page's memory; a reload loses it.
- Company records. A built edition holds `public` records only, so no "Never sent" row appears in it. The rule for a page whose index lists a record with a label outside `allowed` (the company store's site, `r2-overlay`) is part of step 6, so that it holds from the first day, and nothing more is built for it.
- Anything that records: the page writes no journal event, no log and no file. The audit trail of an assistant round trip is `ask --resume` and `new note`.
- The labels' wording, the thread-note sentences and the screen layout: they are in HANDOFF §9, and the interface lane owns them.

## Steps

1. `ports/chat.rs`: `Transport { Handoff, Bridge }` (serde `handoff`, `bridge`), `ChatCaps { answer, stream, keep_threads, save_notes, request_items }` (five `bool`s, serde snake_case) and `impl ChatCaps { pub const HANDOFF: ChatCaps }` with every field false. Tests in `chat_caps.rs`: `handoff_caps_are_all_false`, `transport_names_are_handoff_and_bridge`.
2. `assist/data.rs`: `AssistData { rules_version, rules, view_fingerprint, allowed, clipboard_allowed, policy_hash, handoff_available, handoff_expires, clipboard_available, clipboard_expires, banners, left_out_policy, budget, strings, marking, units, facts }` and `write(&AssistData) -> String`: one assignment `window.FB_ASSIST_DATA = {"v":1,...};`, compact, keys in this order, no spaces, sorted vectors only, with the escape function of `strings::js` (no `</`). `rules` is `ASSISTANT.md`; `allowed` is the sorted ids of `Allowed.permitted: LabelAllow` from the selection token that built the `LabelledView` (including the empty set, never data labels), `clipboard_allowed` is the separate clipboard permission intersection with public-only, `policy_hash` identifies the shared operation snapshot; each destination has its own availability and optional inclusive UTC expiry (floor has no expiry); `banners` the banner text of each of those labels that the policy gives one, and `left_out_policy` the count of records the policy left out; `units` is `export_units(view)`, each unit with its `labels`, and `facts` is `cite_facts(view)`; units carry explicit complete-locator coverage metadata for the pure browser EvidenceText provider; `strings` holds `abstention`, `refusal`, `cite_statuses` (the nine statuses in precedence order) and `labels` (the eight pairs of `strings::ASSISTANT_LABELS`); `marking` is the pattern list of `scan::marking` as the `pub const` holds it, so the browser and `check` read one list. Tests: `assist_data_bytes_are_stable`, `a_record_outside_the_allowed_set_never_reaches_assist_data`, `allowed_and_banners_are_those_of_the_token` (empty and mixed public/company permissions survive unchanged), `labels_are_the_eight_in_order`, `marking_list_is_the_scan_list`, `the_file_is_within_3_mb_on_corpus_mini`. If FB0605 measures a named list of data files, add `assist-data.js`; if it measures every file, nothing changes.
3. The render crate writes `assistant.html`, `assets/assist.js` and `assist-data.js` into every edition and every Corpus site build. `assistant.html` is the three-column shell of §9 (Threads, Chat, Context), the eight labels in its static markup in the order of the row (written from `strings::ASSISTANT_LABELS`), the §11 line "The assistant needs scripts, which this browser blocks." for scripts blocked, `CSP_STATIC` once, no `style=` attribute and no inline script. `assist.js` is a classic ES2020 script; text goes in with `textContent` only; no `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `WebSocket`, `EventSource`, `sendBeacon`, `innerHTML`, `eval`, `new Function` or `import(`. `assist-data.js` and `search-index.js` are added as `<script src>` on the first open of the page or the drawer, by `assist.js`, and the page shows a Skeleton bar after 300 ms as `search.js` does. Add a golden snapshot of `assistant.html`.
4. Retrieval: `assist.js` ranks with `FB_SEARCH.rank` over the Assistant index built by `assist::index_of(handoff_view)` (the index's `docs[unit][0]` is the id) and never ranks by itself. A hit whose id has no record in `units` is skipped, and the harness fails on it (step 11).
5. The ports, in this order in the file so a reader can follow the Rust: `markingScan(question)` (HG-03; a question with a marking shape shows the FB0700 line from the catalogue and builds nothing; the page cannot read the policy's own marking rules, so it refuses every shape in the `marking` list, which is stricter than `context` on the command line and never looser), `contextPack(question, scope, budget, include)` (the steps of `assist::context_pack`, on `units`: hits, closure from `closure` and `conflicts`, exclusions from the flags, conflict dependencies kept or dropped together, whole blocks to `budget`), `applyEdits(pack, edits, budget)`, `resolvePack(named)` (check current availability first: an absent id is stale and excluded; a changed `view_fingerprint` marks available items stale without reviving an excluded item. An unavailable pin keeps its reason and makes the pack unsendable), `splitSentences(answer)` and `citeCheck(answer, context)` (the tolerant reading, the nine statuses in precedence, neutral references, uncited and abstention, from `facts`, with the locator test of `check_facts`: a page against `pages`, a label against `labels`, and `rec` and `sheet` locators against `tables` when it is not empty; with `context` a set of ids or `null`), and `packText(pack)` (the bytes of `ContextPack::to_text`: the `Labels:` line from the labels of the question and of the included units, the banner of each label from `banners`, and the last line, `Left out (policy): N records`, with N from `left_out_policy`). Where the port needs a field that `UnitRecord` or `CiteFact` lacks, add it to `export_units` or `cite_facts` in this card and say so in the pull request; neither is in a golden. The line `CAPS_HANDOFF = { answer: false, stream: false, keep_threads: false, save_notes: false, request_items: false }` is the one capability literal.
6. The context panel, from §9: header with the count and the scope chip; the Meter (Unicode scalar characters used and limit, from RequestBudget); items pinned first, then picked, then added, each with kind, id, title and its "Why:" line from `why` and `via` ("the page you asked from" for `scope`, "matches <question terms>" for `hit`, "cited by both pinned items" or "one link from <via>" for `closure`, "you added it" for `named`); **Pin**, **Remove** and **Add back**; the closed "Left out" fold with each item's reason read from its flags and from `included` (Tier D, superseded, contradicted, over budget, you removed it); "Add from the library" over the same index; saved context packs (**Save as context pack**, **Load**, a thread note); **Copy context pack**, always secondary in the panel. Changes apply to the next copy, and each answer keeps the items it read. An item that the page's index lists with a label that is not in `allowed` (the company store's site) shows "Never sent" in Left out, has no action, and its id is never part of a context, a pack text or a saved pack; a built edition, whose records are all `public`, shows no such row. Below 1040px of main width the panel is a sheet behind a "Context · N items" bar (a container query).
7. The thread and the Handoff composer, from §9: the question with any selected text quoted above it; a thread note for the first pick and for each later change (changes between two messages group); the composer's question field and scope select; in the state No model here **Copy context pack** is the one primary button, then a labelled paste area and **Check citations**. Automatic copy uses the clipboard serializer and full-payload check below; packText remains the Handoff representation. The page remembers the ids of the context it last copied, and a pasted answer is checked against those ids; if nothing was copied in this thread it is checked against the current context and the Message says so. The pasted answer becomes a Message headed "Pasted answer" with the verdict chips (verified, to review, abstentions), the annotated answer with a dotted underline on uncited sentences, citation chips that link to the unit, the refusal banner when nothing verifies, and the two closed folds "What was checked" and "Read N items" (the context as it was when the answer was checked). A citation that is outside the context offers "Add <id> and ask again", which adds the unit and starts a copy. **Copy as draft note** checks the complete draft-note payload before automatic copy, or exposes selectable manual text on refusal. It prepares the draft-note text (`Question: <one line>`, `Context: <ids>`, a blank line, the answer) and shows the command `fieldbook new note --ai-drafted --confirm-public --area <a> --from <file>` as a CommandChip with `<a>` from an area scope, else `<area>`; when the check has unresolved citations the Message adds the line `N of M citations do not resolve` and that `new note` will refuse the text. "Ask the company" and "Record as an assumption" stay in the closed "Next steps" fold as commands or landing lines. The page says "Not recorded" under the thread.
8. The seam: `window.FB_ASSIST.use(adapter)`, where `adapter` is `{ transport, status, context(edits), preview(request), send(request), cancel(), note(request), packs: { list(), put(pack), remove(name) }, end() }`; `cancel() -> Promise<{ state: "Cancelled" }>` maps to the authenticated POST /api/cancel. Each method returns a promise of the JSON of the matching route in `chat-bridge`, and a rejection `{ code, kind, retryable, fallback }` shows the Error state. `end()` is optional (the Bridge's `POST /api/quit`): with it the status line reads "Session active · N calls left" and has a ghost **End session** button that opens the ConfirmDialog and, once confirmed, calls `end()`, shows "Session ended.", keeps the context and offers the Handoff copy; without it there is no such line. Until `use` is called, the adapter is Handoff and `caps` is `CAPS_HANDOFF`. `use` replaces the context panel's source (`context(edits)` is the server's `apply_edits`, so `assist.js` stops running `contextPack` for that page), shows the previewed request before a send, renders the returned `AnswerCard` (verdicts, `neutral`, `unchecked`, `prov`) with the same chips, and switches Save as note by `caps.save_notes`. The five states of §9 map to the status `kind` this way: `ready` is Connected (Send is primary); `busy` is Answering (Stop replaces Send; Stop discards the reply and keeps the question, using the shared fixed line); `handoff` and `off` are No model here; `unavailable` is Error with **Try again** and the Handoff copy offered; `blocked` is Error with Send disabled, no Try again and the line for its code; Connecting is the page's own state from load until `status` answers. Capability rows: Answer by `caps.answer`; Stream by `caps.stream` (false: the page says Answering until the answer arrives whole); Keep threads by `caps.keep_threads` (false: the list is headed "This session only"); Save notes by `caps.save_notes` (**Save as note** or **Copy as draft note**); Request items by `caps.request_items` (false: no "asked for by the assistant" origin exists). `xtask/harness/assist_stub_adapter.js` registers a scripted adapter so the harness can show each state.
9. The Ask drawer and the entry points of §9: the top bar's Ask button and `.` (the ShortcutsDialog gets its row; the Switch turns `.` off like the other single-key shortcuts), the drawer with the page as scope, the thread, the composer and the Context bar with its sheet; "Ask about this page" in the reader's actions, selecting prose then "Ask about this" (the selection quoted), Shift+Enter on a palette result, the Today composer, the starters and **Ask about <area>** (links to `assistant.html#q=<encoded>` that open the drawer when `assist.js` runs), **Copy context pack** on any page whose labels are all in `allowed` (the page context pack of `page-context-packs`, no drawer; a page with another label hides it), and "Not established" in search with **Ask**. "Open in Assistant" moves the thread and its context ids to the Assistant page through `sessionStorage` (step 10). Without scripts the Ask button stays a link to `assistant.html`.
10. Storage, from the Storage bullet of AD-15 and WB-04, every access in `try`/`catch`: named context packs in `localStorage` under the key `fieldbook.packs` as a list of `{ name, ids, view_fingerprint }` (never unit text, a question or an answer); on failure the page says "Not remembered in this browser" and keeps the list for the session. "Open in Assistant" writes `{ question, scope, ids, notes }` to `sessionStorage` under the key `fieldbook.ask`; the Assistant page reads it once and removes it, and the thread is rebuilt from the ids through `resolvePack`. A stored pack whose `view_fingerprint` differs from the page's marks available items stale and says the pack was made on another edition; absent items stay excluded, and an unavailable pin blocks Copy/Send.

It checks `words_counts_runs_of_non_white_space` (`words` of an empty string is 0, of `a  b`, a line feed and `c` is 3, and any word estimate is advisory; the Meter uses RequestBudget Unicode scalars).
