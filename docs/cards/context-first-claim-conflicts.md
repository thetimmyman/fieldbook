# context-first-claim-conflicts

- **Runs where:** home, CI Linux
- **Schema impact:** none
- **Review:** auto

## Outcome

A claim that enters a context pack for the first time as a conflict opponent brings its own source version and linked terms and its own conflict row and opposing claims, exactly as a selected claim does, so a claim never appears without its source or a third conflicting claim because of the order the walk reached it.

## Why

- [ADR 0001, Handoff design](../decisions/0001-foundation.md) says a pack's selection is the top search hits plus their precomputed closure (the claims they cite, those claims' source versions and their linked terms, and each selected claim's conflict row and every claim in that conflict's `claim_ids`), and control HG-02 in [controls.md](../controls.md) holds that a claim in a conflict never appears in a pack without its conflict.
- PR #487 closed the promoted-claim half of this: a conflict dependency first reached as a default exclusion and later promoted by the claim that needs it now brings its own source, terms and conflicts. It left the other half open (its pull request body says so): an opponent claim added for the first time, not promoted from an exclusion, was pushed as a `Closure` item and never walked further. A claim could then appear without its source or a third conflicting claim depending on walk order, the HG-02 gap this card closes.
- The change reorders the existing context goldens (`tests/golden/context/cases.json`, `tests/golden/json/context.json`): the members of every pack are unchanged, and only the `via` that names the item that pulled in each `Closure` item, and the order of the blocks, change, because a first-time opponent's own closure now enters beside it. That is why it is a card of its own rather than part of #487.

## Scope

- Files: `crates/fieldbook-core/src/assist/pack.rs`, `crates/fieldbook-core/tests/context.rs`, `tests/golden/context/cases.json`, `tests/golden/json/context.json`, `docs/plan/PLAN.md`.
- Docs to update: `docs/plan/PLAN.md` (this card's line, added after `handoff-context`), `docs/reference/cli.md` regenerated by `cargo xtask docs` (its `context` sample prints a pack, so its block order follows this change). The change follows the existing Handoff design and HG-02 and adds no verb, key or output shape.

## Out of scope

- The promoted-claim path, which #487 already fixed; this card reuses the same rule for a first-time item and does not change the promoted branch.
- `apply_edits` (`crates/fieldbook-core/src/assist/edits.rs`): its own conflict and closure walk is the edits card's, and MSM-213 tracks its separate defect.
- Any change to what a claim's status line shows. Both claims in the fixture's one conflict are search hits, so the existing symmetric opponent styling is unchanged here.

## Preconditions

- Cards merged: `handoff-context` (`context_pack`, `add_claim_conflicts` and the golden cases), and the promotion fix merged as PR #487.

## Steps

1. In `add_claim_conflicts`, when the walk inserts an item it has not seen (`seen.insert(id)` is true) and that item is a claim, walk its own closure (`add_claim_closure`) and push it onto the pending list so its own conflicts are expanded, exactly as the promoted branch already does. The inserted item keeps `why = Closure` and `via` naming the claim that needed it.
2. Add the plant fields `opponent_own_source` (the first-time opponent `CL-CMP-0002` cites a source no other planted claim cites) and `opponent_third_claim` (a fourth claim `CL-CMP-0004` conflicts with `CL-CMP-0002` in a second conflict `XC-03`) to the `Plant` fixture.
3. Add `a_first_time_opponent_claim_brings_its_own_source` and `a_first_time_opponent_claim_brings_its_own_conflicts` to `crates/fieldbook-core/tests/context.rs`, each carrying `// control: HG-02`.
4. Update the two context goldens from the specification: the members of every pack are unchanged; a first-time opponent's own source and terms enter beside it, so the `via` of `S-0001-v1` and `billing_withhold` becomes `CL-CMP-0002` and the blocks follow that order.

## Acceptance

```sh
cargo nextest run --locked -p fieldbook-core --test context
cargo nextest run --locked -p fieldbook --test json_reports
just check
```

Expected: every test passes, including `a_first_time_opponent_claim_brings_its_own_source`, `a_first_time_opponent_claim_brings_its_own_conflicts` and `goldens_match`; `just check` exits 0.

## Negative control

With the step 1 walk removed (a first-time opponent inserted without `add_claim_closure` and without being pushed onto the pending list), `a_first_time_opponent_claim_brings_its_own_source` and `a_first_time_opponent_claim_brings_its_own_conflicts` fail, and `goldens_match` fails because the old order is restored. The fix is restored after each.

## Rollback

Revert the merge commit. The two golden files return to the order in which a first-time opponent brought only its own row, and a claim can again lack its source or a third conflicting claim by walk order.

## BLOCKED

- The walk no longer terminates on a conflict cycle: a unit already seen must never be re-walked, so if the change makes a cycle loop, stop and report it rather than adding a visited guard that hides a different defect.
- A change here alters pack membership (not only `via` and order) for a committed golden: stop. This card changes attribution and order only, and a membership change means the rule is wrong.
