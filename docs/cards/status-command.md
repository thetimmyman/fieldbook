# status-command

- **Runs where:** home, CI Linux
- **Schema impact:** none
- **Review:** auto

## Outcome

`fieldbook status [--today YYYY-MM-DD] [--page]` prints, read only: counts, stale items, unreviewed consequential claims, open day-one questions, this week's upkeep and review minutes from `docs/maintenance.csv` against the D-16 budget, and the next actions as copyable command chips. Held (quarantined), withdrawn and superseded items come first, then unverified and stale consequential claims, then the other stale items. When the state folder holds the data, it also prints the last verified backup; when `.fieldbook/settings.toml` names no role, it prints the two lines to add (the role and a backup target) and says that `verify` is off. A card that keeps state of its own adds its line to the same summary: `ask-log` the questions asked, `review-queue` the proposals waiting. An `insta` snapshot pins its output on the fixture. With `--page` it also writes the desk pages (Import report, Activity log and Backup status; the queue pages arrive with `review-queue`) into `pages/` of the state folder.

## Why

- The verb and what it lists are in [AD-17](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#ad-17-authoring-experience). Only `check` and `status` read today's date ([Dates](../reference/corpus-format.md#dates)).
- Stale means a `review_by` that has passed (FB0117, FB0221) or a claim marked `stale`; `review_by` is computed as [Dates](../reference/corpus-format.md#dates) says. A consequential claim is unreviewed until the owner verifies it ([Promotion rules](../reference/corpus-format.md#promotion-rules)).
- The minutes file and its rows are in [release.md, Log the time](../runbooks/release.md#log-the-time); the weekly budget is the Owner minutes paragraph of [PLAN.md](../plan/PLAN.md#how-codex-uses-this-plan).
- `status` prints the lines of a `StateSummary { lines: Vec<StateLine> }` after its sections, and a line prints only when its data exists: a build without the backup prints nothing for it. This card makes the backup line and the role lines. A card that keeps state of its own (`ask-log`, `review-queue`) adds one `StateLine` to `read_state_summary` and needs no change to `report` or to `status.rs`. The role is the label in `settings.toml` that the journal records ([the journal's role](../reference/audit.md)); `verify-status-fmt` prints the role line when it finds none.
- This is a Could ([Cut line and floor](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#cut-line-and-floor)).
- Design: the [MAP.md](../design/MAP.md) rows Import report, Activity log and Backup status (desk pages), and the row CommandChip, which gains `status` and `status --page`; next actions print only chip strings from the verb map in `fieldbook-core::strings`. [HANDOFF.md](../design/HANDOFF.md) §5 (CommandChip) and the specs ImportReport, ActivityLog and BackupStatus. Token groups: none beyond those the pages use.

## Out of scope

- `fmt` (`fmt-command`). Any write by `status` without `--page`: it changes no file.
- A page that shows the counts and stale items of status; the desk pages show state, not the Corpus. Live pages (`later-desktop-shell`).
- Changing what the date findings are: they stay warnings in `check`.

## Preconditions

- Cards merged: `verify-status-fmt`, `check-command` (`tests/chips.rs` and its `PENDING` list), `calc-day1` (the seeded generator `tests/support/rng.rs` that step 8 includes), `scheduled-workflow`, `audit-journal` (the state folder and the journal that the backup line reads), `build-v0` (the render crate and the CSP constants), `build-tokens` (the tokens the pages copy), `add-folder-mode` (the `AddReport` shape and `reports/last-add.json`) and `backup-snapshot` (the `BackupList` shape, `backup_age`, and the `backup` and `backup verify` verbs that the tests run).
- The Import report says "No add has run" when `reports/last-add.json` does not exist.

## Steps

1. `fieldbook_core::status::report(model, today, minutes) -> StatusReport`: counts per register and pages per status; claims and pages that rest on a quarantined, withdrawn or superseded source version; `unresolved` conflicts; stale pages and claims with their `review_by`; consequential claims without `verified_by_role = owner`; `day_one` questions still open; this ISO week's upkeep and review minutes. Items sort in three groups, and by id within each group: (1) the claims and pages on a quarantined, withdrawn or superseded version, and the unresolved conflicts; (2) unverified or stale consequential claims; (3) the other stale items.
2. Next actions: one line per kind of finding, in the order of step 1, each a chip string from `strings::chips` with its placeholders left in, such as the `verify` chip for an unreviewed claim. Never a free-form command.
3. `World` gains `read_maintenance() -> Result<String, WorldError>`; `Disk` reads `docs/maintenance.csv` from the repository root. A missing file prints "Unavailable" in the minutes line and is not an error. A malformed row exits 3 with its line. `fieldbook_core::status::parse_maintenance(text: &str) -> Result<Vec<MinuteRow>, MaintenanceError>` is the pure reader of that text (`MinuteRow { week, minutes, kind }` holds the `minutes` that `report` takes; `MaintenanceError` holds the line number, which the binary prints with exit 3). The header is `week,minutes,kind,note`; `week` is an ISO week (`YYYY-Www`), `minutes` a whole number, `kind` `upkeep` or `review`, and `note` free text; another header, or a row with another value, is malformed. The budget is two constants of `fieldbook-core::status`, in minutes a week: `UPKEEP: (u32, u32) = (30, 60)` and `REVIEW: (u32, u32) = (60, 120)` (the Owner minutes paragraph of PLAN.md, D-16 of ADR 0001). The minutes line prints `Upkeep this week: 45 min (budget 30 to 60)` and the same for review, with `over budget` after the figure when the sum is above the high end. Nothing stops.
4. Print sections in the order of AD-17, each heading followed by its lines or `none`. `--today` replaces the clock as for `check`; exit 0, except exit 3 for a malformed `docs/maintenance.csv` row (step 3) and, when the Corpus cannot be read, the exit code that `check` gives for that fault; a missing `docs/maintenance.csv` prints "Unavailable" and exits 0. Remove `status` from `PENDING` in `crates/fieldbook/tests/chips.rs` (`build-about`) and from `NOT_BUILT` in `crates/fieldbook/tests/cli.rs`. Register the cases of `status` and `status --page` in `tests/support/chip_cases.rs` (a fresh Home `corpus-mini` and State copy, exit 0).
5. State lines. `fieldbook_core::status` defines `StateLine { text: String }` and `StateSummary { lines: Vec<StateLine> }`. `World` gains `read_state_summary() -> Result<StateSummary, WorldError>`, which reads the state folder and the role in `settings.toml`; a part whose data is absent or cannot be read adds no line and is never an error. `report` takes the summary and prints each line of `lines` after the AD-17 sections, in the order the summary holds them. This card makes two parts, in this order. The backup: `Last verified backup: N days ago, target T (kind)`, where N is the number that `read_state_summary` fills in by calling `backup_age` of `backup-snapshot` (one function, so the figure cannot differ from the reminder that the write verbs print) (`status` prints the warning FB0814 when a target is set or a snapshot exists and no `backup.verify` has succeeded, with the line `Last verified backup: none yet`, and when the newest verified backup is older than `warn_after_days`; with no target and no snapshot it prints nothing about backups. The warning text is the string that `reminder` returns for the same age, built in the binary crate and held in the summary as text. FB0813 is not printed here: it belongs to a target under a synced location). The role: when `.fieldbook/settings.toml` has no `role` key (the one `audit-journal` reads), print the two lines to add to it, the role and a `[[backup.target]]` entry, as `verify::ROLE_LINE` (`verify-status-fmt`) and `backup::BACKUP_TARGET_TEMPLATE` (`backup-snapshot`), and the line `verify is off until a role is set`. A card that keeps state of its own adds one `StateLine` to `read_state_summary` after these two parts (`ask-log`: `N questions asked, M misses, K promoted`; `review-queue`: `N proposals waiting`) and edits neither `report` nor `crates/fieldbook-core/src/status.rs`. `status` still exits 0.
6. Desk pages. `status --page` writes `pages/{import,activity,backup}.html` and an `assets/` folder beside them (the site's tokens and frame, copied from the render crate's embedded copy) through one binary Desk writer, which calls State admission before the atomic helper under a `StateScope` token, and prints one `wrote <path>` line for each. `fieldbook-render::desk` has no I/O: `frame(title, written_utc, body) -> String`, `import(&ReportSource<AddReport>)`, `activity(&ReportSource<AuditShowReport>, &ReportSource<AuditVerifyReport>)` and `backup(&ReportSource<BackupList>)` take the reports of `fieldbook-core::report` and return pages with `CSP_STATIC`, no `style=""`, no inline script and every command as a `CommandChip`. A page names its source and "Written <UTC time>", the UTC time captured once by the binary handler from `World::now()` and passed to `frame`; tests supply a fixed `Now`, and the refresh chip `fieldbook status --page`. A source that does not exist gives the page its empty line ("No add has run", "None recorded"), never an error. `PAGES` is the one table of page names; the Activity page shows the newest 200 events (`PAGE_EVENTS`). A failed write of a page is exit 3 and leaves the old page.
7. Add the freshness report to `scheduled.yml` ([AD-08](../decisions/0001-foundation.md#ad-08-ci-design)) as a job `freshness`: `ubuntu-24.04`, `timeout-minutes: 10`, checkout, the pinned toolchain, and one step with `shell: bash` and `continue-on-error: true` that runs `cargo run --locked -p fieldbook -- status --today "$(git log -1 --format=%cs)" >> "$GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY"`, so the stale items are printed in the job summary. The job never fails the workflow and changes no permission.
8. Tests: an `insta` snapshot of `status` on a temporary copy of the fixture with a synthetic `maintenance.csv` and a `--today` that is past one fixture claim's `review_by` and before the others (the date in `tests/fixtures/corpus-mini/TODAY`, read with `include_str!("../../../tests/fixtures/corpus-mini/TODAY")` and trimmed, moved on by the whole days that put it there; no test reads the clock); a fixture claim past its `review_by` is listed under stale; a claim on a quarantined version is listed before a stale claim with a lower id, and so is a claim on a superseded version (`status_lists_held_withdrawn_and_superseded_first`); no `maintenance.csv` prints "Unavailable" and exits 0; a row of `maintenance.csv` whose `kind` is neither `upkeep` nor `review` exits 3 and prints its line number (`malformed_maintenance_row_exits_3`); a bad `--today` exits 2; `parser_never_panics_on_200_seeded_inputs` in `crates/fieldbook/tests/status.rs` (the file includes the generator with `#[path = "../../../tests/support/rng.rs"] mod rng;`, builds 200 byte strings of length 0 to 128 from `Rng::new(1)` and takes the synthetic `maintenance.csv` of the snapshot test with each byte once flipped and once deleted, each through `String::from_utf8_lossy` into `parse_maintenance`; the result is its rows or a `MaintenanceError`, never a panic); the state lines, on a temporary copy of `tests/fixtures/state-mini`, where a backup is made by the real `backup` and `backup verify` verbs under a fixed clock, so `reports/last-backup.json` comes from its writer and no test writes one by hand: with a verified backup 3 days old the backup line prints with its target and kind (`status_prints_the_backup_line`); with no backup data no backup line prints (`status_omits_absent_state_lines`); with the `role` key removed from the copy's `settings.toml` the two lines to add and the `verify is off` line print (`status_without_a_role_says_verify_is_off`; the two lines equal `verify::ROLE_LINE` and `backup::BACKUP_TARGET_TEMPLATE` byte for byte); with a backup 9 days old and `warn_after_days = 7` the warning line equals the string that `reminder` returns for the same age (`status_backup_warning_matches_the_reminder`); and with a `TestWorld` whose `set_state_summary` holds two lines, `report` prints them after the sections in the order given (`status_prints_each_summary_line_in_order`), which keeps a later card's line from needing a change to `report`; for the pages, `pages_are_static_and_have_the_csp`, `pages_write_nothing_outside_pages` (runs every row of `PAGES`, including later additions), `missing_sources_give_empty_pages`, `edition_never_holds_pages` (build an edition beside a state folder that has `pages/`; no `pages` path in the zip) and `status_without_page_writes_nothing`, and the goldens `tests/golden/pages/{import,activity,backup}.html` on the fixture state.
9. Run `cargo xtask docs` and commit the changed `docs/reference/cli.md`.

10. Implement HANDOFF’s Desk pages specification and MAP routes. Pure report.rs gains ReportSource<T> { Missing, Ready(T), Unavailable { diagnostic: String } }; the binary maps missing files to Missing, successful reads to Ready and parse/read failures to Unavailable, with a safe diagnostic. It distinguishes these states before rendering and passes audit show and verify independently. Desk-source failure renders its unavailable state and exits 3 after writing the diagnostic pages; a missing source remains exit 0. In tests/pages.rs, desk_sources_keep_missing_unavailable_and_failed_distinct and desk_commands_match_the_parser run the real status --page handler, verify all source states, nonzero valid records, escaped long filenames, a failed verification with readable events and all literal chips. The static goldens include these states and visible source/time. Extend the existing browser harness only when it has landed; then check 375px, zoom, keyboard, scripts-blocked and print as the handoff specifies.

11. Acquire state-only write authority with `journal.state_scope(&lease)?` under the retained exclusive store lease, or reuse the active operation lease. Propagate its acquisition/head errors through this producer’s existing failure path: optional saves warn and preserve the previous file and command exit; page-generation failures exit 3. No state token is fabricated on failure.

12. The saved Import report reader requires AddReport.labels and inputs and retains current effective labels while displaying names. Missing metadata or unreadable input provenance is unavailable, never a public report. Extend its JSON/page goldens and the real page handler tests with a correctly labelled report and a missing-classification report.

13. `backup_page_shows_failed_reverification` calls real backup, successful verify, data corruption with unchanged manifest identity, failed verify and status --page. Require the latest failure and time in the backup page despite last-success fields, with historical success labelled separately. Update the backup page golden; source availability is independent from verification outcome.

14. The shared binary Desk writer in pages.rs handles every PAGES entry and later expanded queue-detail route. From the same coherent read, union retained report/content labels with current effective labels of all its inputs and call labels::admit with the current State rule before replacement. Wire the operation’s policy (home floor before policy-load; loaded policy afterward) into this handler. FB0412 exits 3 and preserves the affected existing page. Missing provenance excludes source content and uses safe Missing/Unavailable diagnostics, never an assumed public label. State may hold valid unlabelled and, on floor B, marked content. Public embedded assets retain their public labels. In the pages suite, `desk_admission_preserves_existing_page` exercises refusal-before-replacement; later company-intake tests cover policy changes and queue transit.

## Acceptance

```sh
cargo nextest run --locked -p fieldbook --test status --test pages
cargo run -q --locked -p fieldbook -- status --today "$(git log -1 --format=%cs)"; echo "exit $?"
cargo build -q --locked -p fieldbook && B="$PWD/target/debug/fieldbook" && T="$(mktemp -d)" && cp -R tests/fixtures/corpus-mini/. "$T" && S="$T/state" && mkdir -p "$S"
(cd "$T" && "$B" --state "$S" status --page; echo "exit $?"; ls "$S/pages")
cargo xtask docs --check
just check
```

Expected: every test passes and the snapshot is unchanged; `status` on the real Corpus prints each section and `exit 0`, and `git status --short` is empty afterwards; `status --page` on the temporary copy prints one `wrote` line for each of the three pages and `exit 0`, and `ls` lists `activity.html`, `assets`, `backup.html` and `import.html`; `docs --check` and `just check` exit 0. Remove the temporary folder afterwards.

## Negative control

Remove the passed-`review_by` rule from `status::report`. The snapshot test must fail, showing the stale claim missing. Restore it. Then sort the lists by id alone: `status_lists_held_withdrawn_and_superseded_first` must fail. Restore it. Then remove the role from the copied `settings.toml` and stop `read_state_summary` from adding the two lines: `status_without_a_role_says_verify_is_off` must fail. Restore it. Then make `report` print only the first line of the summary: `status_prints_each_summary_line_in_order` must fail. Restore it. Then add a `<script>` with inline text to `crates/fieldbook-render/src/desk/frame.rs`: `pages_are_static_and_have_the_csp` must fail. Restore it. Then let `parse_maintenance` accept an unknown `kind`: `malformed_maintenance_row_exits_3` must fail. Restore it.

Render an unreadable source as empty or a failed audit verification as verified: desk_sources_keep_missing_unavailable_and_failed_distinct must fail. Replace a literal chip with a nonparseable command: desk_commands_match_the_parser must fail. Restore each.

Render verified=true as current success while ignoring latest_verification: `backup_page_shows_failed_reverification` must fail. Restore it.

Bypass State admission in the shared Desk writer: `desk_admission_preserves_existing_page` must fail. Restore it.

## Rollback

Revert the merge commit. `status` becomes a usage error; `check` still reports the date findings.

## BLOCKED
