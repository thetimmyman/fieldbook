# Changelog

Notes on tool releases (`vX.Y.Z`), newest first. Editions are noted in [corpus/changes.md](corpus/changes.md). The release steps are in [the release runbook](docs/runbooks/release.md).

## Unreleased

- Added: restore and restore --drill.
- Added: site search finds glossary terms by their labels and aliases.
- Changed: the home and area pages no longer show a placeholder that names an internal document.
- Added: a weekly `scheduled.yml` workflow runs the advisory check, a full-history secret scan, the lint controls (a planted banned call must fail clippy), the committed-lock check, an edition build for the cross-OS compare when `corpus/changes.md` has an edition heading, and the advisory scale table.
- Changed: the Markdown reader moves to pulldown-cmark 0.13.6, which fixes a published advisory about nested wikilinks; the program does not enable that option.
- Added: the three E1 workbook templates in the edition.
- Added: build --watch.
- Added: data/registers.xlsx and CSV copies of the registers.
- Changed: section snippets in the site search index are cut to 100 characters to keep the index within its size cap.
- Added: backup, backup verify and backup list, and a reminder after a write verb when the last verified backup is older than the warning age.
- Added: the day-one binder and print styles.
- Changed: the search palette has a placeholder, quoted search text and a named shortcuts dialog.
- Added: check matches claim quotes in the extracted text; source pages show the text with section anchors.
- Added: `cargo xtask scale` times `check`, `build` and `search` over a synthetic Corpus at 1, 5 and 20 times the Day 1 counts and prints one table with the index size and peak memory; it is advisory and fails nothing.
- Added: `cargo xtask extract` writes the text of a stored original, page by page, with its hashes, thin pages and locators, and `fieldbook check` reports an extraction that no longer matches its original (FB0212) and a consequential claim that rests on a metadata-only, thin or supplied version (FB0223).
- Added: `just release` and the release workflow.
- Added: `just edition` builds the edition and originals zips.
- Changed: the IT documents describe the silent build and the AI build separately.
- Changed: the ID list is an index with one page per group, and claims in parts of 500 per area.
- Added: `just new-card`, `cargo xtask cards --check` and `cargo xtask scope`, which lint the card files, the plan, the links and the documents, and fail a pull request that closes a card and changes a path the card does not list.
- Changed: an unreleased build shows the review notice in its build status banner, not as a second banner on each page.
- Changed: with no company data loaded, each page's Company calibration part is folded.
- Changed: the pre-push hook runs the secret and Corpus scans and the denylist; the full check runs in CI only.
- Changed: a page's Sources list each cited claim once, with links back to each place it is cited.
- Fixed: the linked build date in the top bar is at least 24px tall at 375px, and the narrow-width check no longer exempts it.
- Added: each page lists its related pages and the pages that link to it, by title.
- Changed: navigation entries that a build does not hold no longer fill the sidebar and tabs with "not available" lines.
- Added: a context pack on each page.
- Added: the verify command, for the owner at a terminal.
- Changed: the zip reader and writer move to zip 9, which fixes five published advisories.
- Changed: the regression set judges the page view of search, not the flat unit list.
- Added: every page of an unreleased build states that the build is not an edition and counts its AI-drafted, unchecked claims.
- Changed: a page's review date counts from its last change note as well as its as-of date.
- Changed: `cargo xtask harness` opens pages in several browser processes at once (`--jobs N`, default half the available cores), and the `corpus` job of CI builds the site with a release binary; the findings and the summary counts do not depend on the job count.
- Added: charts on the area pages: the price build-up waterfall from the calculation case CALC-02 on Indirect rates, an Unavailable panel for each chart that has no data yet, and the calc table on calc pages.
