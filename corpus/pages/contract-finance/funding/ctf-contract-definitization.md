## Purpose

Shows what the definitization clause requires after a letter contract is awarded, when the contracting officer may set price or fee, and what the contractor must do while negotiation is still open.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0412] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-25 [@CL-CTF-0412] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-25 [@CL-CTF-1134; @CL-CTF-1135; @CL-CTF-1136] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0412] |
| Applies to | Contracts in which a definitive contract is contemplated to supersede a letter contract [@CL-CTF-0412] |

The contractor agrees to begin promptly negotiating with the contracting officer the terms of a definitive contract [@CL-CTF-0609]. That contract will include all clauses required by the Federal Acquisition Regulation on the date of execution of the letter contract, all clauses required by law on the date of execution of the definitive contract, and any other mutually agreeable clauses, terms and conditions [@CL-CTF-0412].

The contractor agrees to submit a proposal of the specific type inserted in the clause (such as fixed-price or cost-and-fee), including data other than certified cost or pricing data, and certified cost or pricing data, in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2, supporting its proposal [@CL-CTF-0413]. If agreement on a definitive contract is not reached by the target date in the clause schedule or within any extension the contracting officer grants [@CL-CTF-0415], the contracting officer may, with the approval of the head of the contracting activity, determine a reasonable price or fee in accordance with FAR subpart 15.4 and part 31, subject to contractor appeal under the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-0414]. The contractor must proceed with completion in any event, subject only to the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-CTF-0416].

## How it works

The Contractor agrees to begin promptly negotiating with the Contracting Officer the terms of a definitive contract [@CL-CTF-0609]. The definitive contract will include all clauses required by the Federal Acquisition Regulation on the date of execution of the letter contract, all clauses required by law on the date of execution of the definitive contract, and any other mutually agreeable clauses, terms, and conditions [@CL-CTF-0412].

The Contractor agrees to submit a proposal, such as a fixed-price or cost-and-fee proposal, including data other than certified cost or pricing data, and certified cost or pricing data, in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2, supporting its proposal [@CL-CTF-0413].

If agreement on a definitive contract to supersede the letter contract is not reached by the target date in paragraph (b), or within any extension of it granted by the Contracting Officer, the Contracting Officer may, with the approval of the head of the contracting activity, determine a reasonable price or fee in accordance with FAR subpart 15.4 and part 31, subject to Contractor appeal as provided in the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-0414; @CL-CTF-0415].

In any event, the Contractor shall proceed with completion of the contract, subject only to the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-CTF-0416].

In letter contracts awarded on the basis of price competition, Alternate I adds paragraph (d) to the basic clause [@CL-CTF-0610]. It states that the definitive contract resulting from the letter contract will include a negotiated price ceiling or firm fixed price in no event to exceed the proposed price upon which the award was based [@CL-CTF-0417].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.216-25, the Contractor agrees to begin promptly negotiating with the Contracting Officer the terms of a definitive contract [@CL-CTF-1134], and the definitive contract will include all clauses required by the Federal Acquisition Regulation on the date of execution of the letter contract, all clauses required by law on the date of execution of the definitive contract, and any other mutually agreeable clauses, terms, and conditions [@CL-CTF-1135]. The Contractor agrees to submit a proposal, including data other than certified cost or pricing data, and certified cost or pricing data, in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-1, supporting its proposal [@CL-CTF-1136]. If agreement on a definitive contract to supersede the letter contract is not reached by the target date in paragraph (b) or within any extension of it granted by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1137], the Contracting Officer may, with the approval of the head of the contracting activity, determine a reasonable price or fee in accordance with subpart 15.4 and part 31 of the FAR, subject to Contractor appeal as provided in the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1138], and in any event the Contractor shall proceed with completion of the contract, subject only to the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-CTF-1139]. Under RFO FAR 52.216-25 Alternate I, as prescribed in 16.603-4(b)(3), paragraph (d) is added to the basic clause [@CL-CTF-1140], stating that the definitive contract resulting from the letter contract will include a negotiated price ceiling or firm fixed price in no event to exceed the proposed price upon which the award was based [@CL-CTF-1141].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A letter contract with a value of 2,000,000 is awarded with a target date for definitization, and the contractor proposes 2,400,000. Negotiation does not close by the target date or any extension, so the contracting officer determines a price of 2,200,000 with the approval of the head of the contracting activity, in accordance with the pricing rules in the FAR. The contractor keeps performing throughout, because the only limit on that duty is the Limitation of Government Liability clause.

## Never confuse

The definitive contract includes all clauses required by the FAR on the date of execution of the letter contract, all clauses required by law on the date of execution of the definitive contract, and any other mutually agreeable clauses, terms, and conditions [@CL-CTF-0412].

In any event, the Contractor shall proceed with completion of the contract, subject only to the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-CTF-0416].

Funding ceilings on incrementally funded work are covered in [Limitation of funds](ctf-limitation-of-funds.md).

Under FAR 52.216-25(a), the Contractor agrees to submit a proposal including data other than certified cost or pricing data, and certified cost or pricing data, in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2 [@CL-CTF-0413]. Under RFO FAR 52.216-25(a), the Contractor agrees to submit a proposal including data other than certified cost or pricing data, and certified cost or pricing data, in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-1 [@CL-CTF-1136]. Under FAR 52.216-25 Alternate I, paragraph (d) is added in letter contracts awarded on the basis of price competition [@CL-CTF-0610], and under RFO FAR 52.216-25 Alternate I, paragraph (d) is added as prescribed in 16.603-4(b)(3) [@CL-CTF-1140].

## Company calibration

Whether each letter contract has a target date, a proposal carrying both kinds of pricing data, and an owner for funding surveillance until the definitive contract is signed. [@Q-008]
