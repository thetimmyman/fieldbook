## Purpose

Shows what the definitization clause requires after a letter contract is awarded, when the contracting officer may set price or fee, and what the contractor must do while negotiation is still open.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0412] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-25 [@CL-CTF-0412] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0412] |
| Applies to | Contracts in which a definitive contract is contemplated to supersede a letter contract [@CL-CTF-0412] |

The contractor agrees to begin promptly negotiating with the contracting officer the terms of a definitive contract [@CL-CTF-0609]. That contract will include all clauses required by the Federal Acquisition Regulation on the date of execution of the letter contract, all clauses required by law on the date of execution of the definitive contract, and any other mutually agreeable clauses, terms and conditions [@CL-CTF-0412].

The proposal has to include data other than certified cost or pricing data and certified cost or pricing data, in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2, supporting the proposal [@CL-CTF-0413]. If agreement on a definitive contract is not reached by the target date in the clause schedule or within any extension the contracting officer grants [@CL-CTF-0415], the contracting officer may, with the approval of the head of the contracting activity, determine a reasonable price or fee in accordance with FAR subpart 15.4 and part 31, subject to contractor appeal under the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-0414]. The contractor must proceed with completion in any event, subject only to the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-CTF-0416].

## How it works

Definitization turns a letter contract into a definitive contract on negotiated terms. The clause ties FAR-required clauses to the date the letter contract was executed, clauses required by law to the date the definitive contract is executed, and allows any other mutually agreeable clauses, terms and conditions [@CL-CTF-0412].

The proposal has to include data other than certified cost or pricing data and certified cost or pricing data, in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2 [@CL-CTF-0413].

Where the target date or an extension passes without agreement, the contracting officer can set price or fee in accordance with FAR subpart 15.4 and part 31, with head-of-activity approval, subject to contractor appeal under the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-0414; @CL-CTF-0415].

Work continues regardless, because the contractor's duty to proceed is limited only by the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-CTF-0416].

Alternate I applies to letter contracts awarded on the basis of price competition [@CL-CTF-0610]. It adds a paragraph stating that the definitive contract will include a negotiated price ceiling or firm fixed price in no event to exceed the proposed price upon which the award was based [@CL-CTF-0417].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A letter contract with a value of 2,000,000 is awarded with a target date for definitization, and the contractor proposes 2,400,000. Negotiation does not close by the target date or any extension, so the contracting officer determines a price of 2,200,000 with the approval of the head of the contracting activity, in accordance with the pricing rules in the FAR. The contractor keeps performing throughout, because the only limit on that duty is the Limitation of Government Liability clause. Alternate I applies only if the letter contract was awarded on the basis of price competition; the negotiated price ceiling or firm fixed price may then not exceed the proposed price upon which the award was based, which is a separate figure from the 2,400,000 definitization proposal and is not given here.

## Never confuse

Definitization is the negotiated conversion of a letter contract into a definitive contract. FAR-required clauses are measured at the letter contract's execution date, but clauses required by law are measured at the later date the definitive contract is executed [@CL-CTF-0412].

The only stated limit on the duty to keep performing is the Limitation of Government Liability clause, not a funding ceiling set elsewhere in the contract [@CL-CTF-0416].

Funding ceilings on incrementally funded work are covered in [Limitation of funds](ctf-limitation-of-funds.md).

## Company calibration

Whether each letter contract has a target date, a proposal carrying both kinds of pricing data, and an owner for funding surveillance until the definitive contract is signed. [@Q-008]
