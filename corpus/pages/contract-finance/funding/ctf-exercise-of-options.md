## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer must do and determine before exercising an option, including the funds test and the price comparison, and what the contractor should watch.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1311] |
| Codified citation | FAR 17.207 [@CL-CTF-1311] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1311] |
| Applies to | Exercise of an option by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-1311] |

When exercising an option, the contracting officer shall provide written notice to the contractor within the time period specified in the contract [@CL-CTF-1311]. The contracting officer may exercise options only after determining, among other things listed in the same paragraph, that funds are available and that the requirement covered by the option fulfills an existing Government need [@CL-CTF-1313]. Two further determinations in that list are that the option was synopsized in accordance with part 5 unless exempted, and that the contractor does not have an active exclusion record in the System for Award Management [@CL-CTF-1313; @CL-CTF-1314]. Another determination in that list is that exercise of the option is the most advantageous method of fulfilling the Government's need, price and other factors considered [@CL-CTF-1396]. The list contains further determinations that this card does not state.

For the price comparison, the contracting officer, after considering price and other factors, shall make the determination on the basis of one of the listed methods, and the most-advantageous determination points to those methods [@CL-CTF-1315; @CL-CTF-1396]. The first is that a new solicitation fails to produce a better price or a more advantageous offer than the option [@CL-CTF-1315]. The second is an informal analysis of prices or an examination of the market showing that the option price is better than market prices or that the option is the more advantageous offer [@CL-CTF-1315; @CL-CTF-1317]. The third is that the time between award and exercise is so short that it indicates the option price is the lowest price obtainable or the more advantageous offer [@CL-CTF-1315; @CL-CTF-1318], and for it the contracting officer shall take into consideration such factors as market stability and comparison of the time since award with the usual duration of contracts for such supplies or services [@CL-CTF-1378].

## How it works

The funds test is a determination the contracting officer makes before exercise [@CL-CTF-1313]. The funds paragraph says only that funds are available; it does not state an amount or a fund type. Where the contract provides for economic price adjustment and the contractor requests a revision of the price, the contracting officer shall determine the effect of the adjustment on prices under the option before the option is exercised [@CL-CTF-1312].

If it is anticipated that the best price available is the option price or that this is the more advantageous offer, the contracting officer should not use a new solicitation as the method of testing the market [@CL-CTF-1315; @CL-CTF-1316]. The contractor should watch the notice period in its contract, any pending request to revise prices under an economic price adjustment provision, and its own exclusion status [@CL-CTF-1311; @CL-CTF-1312; @CL-CTF-1314]. This card does not state the written determination for the contract file or the rule on the document that notifies the contractor of exercise.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has an option year priced at 1,000,000. Eight months after award the contracting officer compares the option price with an informal review of market prices, which shows the option price is better. Funds are available, the need still exists, the contractor has no active exclusion record, and the other required determinations are made.

Answer: the contracting officer may exercise the option by written notice within the period set in the contract, based on the informal price analysis. A new solicitation is not required.

## Never confuse

Exercising an option is done by written notice after listed determinations, which include the funds test and a price comparison, and not by a new solicitation [@CL-CTF-1311; @CL-CTF-1313; @CL-CTF-1315]. A new solicitation is only one of the listed ways of making the price comparison, and it is not the way to test the market when the option price is expected to be best [@CL-CTF-1315; @CL-CTF-1316]. See also [Use of options](ctf-use-of-options.md), [Options in solicitations and contracts](ctf-options-in-solicitations-and-contracts.md), [Option clauses and provisions](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md), [Economic price adjustment, labor and material](../billing-payment/ctf-economic-price-adjustment-labor-and-material.md), [SAM exclusion records and the effect of listing](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-sam-exclusion-records-and-effect-of-listing.md) and [Publicizing requirements and exceptions](../../market/feeds/mkt-publicizing-requirements-and-exceptions.md).
