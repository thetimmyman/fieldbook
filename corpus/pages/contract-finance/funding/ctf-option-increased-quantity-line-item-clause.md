## Purpose

Shows how the Government exercises an option that is a numbered line item in the Schedule, how it gives notice and how the added items are delivered.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2302] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-7 [@CL-CTF-2302] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2302] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the separately priced line item clause [@CL-CTF-2302] |

The Government may require the delivery of the numbered line item, identified in the Schedule as an option item, in the quantity and at the price stated in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-2302]. The Contracting Officer may exercise the option by written notice to the Contractor within a period of time that the clause leaves as a contract-specific blank to be inserted [@CL-CTF-2303]. Delivery of added items shall continue at the same rate that like items are called for under the contract, unless the parties otherwise agree [@CL-CTF-2304].

## How it works

The quantity and price of the option are those stated in the Schedule for the numbered line item identified as an option item [@CL-CTF-2302]. The Contracting Officer exercises the option by written notice, and the clause text leaves a blank with a bracketed instruction to insert the period, so the period is contract-specific [@CL-CTF-2303]. Added items are then delivered at the rate that like items are called for, unless the parties agree otherwise [@CL-CTF-2304]. The prescription is covered on [Option solicitation provisions and contract clauses](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The Schedule shows line item 0003 as an option item, for 30 units at 40 each. Like items are called for at 5 units a month. The Contracting Officer gives written notice exercising the option within the period inserted in the clause.

Answer: the Government requires delivery of line item 0003 in the quantity and at the price stated in the Schedule, which is 30 units at 40 each and 1,200 in total. Delivery of the added units continues at the same rate that like items are called for, 5 a month, which is 6 months for the 30 units, unless the parties agree otherwise.

## Never confuse

This clause works on a numbered line item that the Schedule identifies as an option item [@CL-CTF-2302]. The increased quantity clause instead increases the quantity of supplies called for in the Schedule at the unit price specified [@CL-CTF-2299]. See [Option for increased quantity clause](ctf-option-increased-quantity-clause.md).
