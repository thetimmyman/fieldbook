## Purpose

Shows what obligated funds cover when a contract is fully funded and when it is incrementally funded. Subpart 32.7 describes basic requirements for contract funding and prescribes procedures for using limitation of cost or limitation of funds clauses, and detailed acquisition funding requirements are contained in agency fiscal regulations [@CL-CTF-3691].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3687] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.700 [@CL-CTF-3691] and FAR 32.703-1(a) and (b) [@CL-CTF-3687; @CL-CTF-3688] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.700 [@CL-CTF-3040] and RFO FAR 32.703-1(a) and (b) [@CL-CTF-3692; @CL-CTF-3693] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3687; @CL-CTF-3688; @CL-CTF-3692; @CL-CTF-3693] |
| Applies to | Paragraph (a): a fully funded contract, fixed-price or cost-reimbursement [@CL-CTF-3687]. Paragraph (b): an incrementally funded contract [@CL-CTF-3688] |

If a contract is fully funded, funds are obligated to cover the price or target price of a fixed-price contract, or the estimated cost and any fee of a cost-reimbursement contract [@CL-CTF-3687]. If a contract is incrementally funded, funds are obligated to cover the amount allotted and any corresponding increment of fee [@CL-CTF-3688]. Under RFO FAR 32.703-1(a) and (b), if the contract is fully funded, funds are obligated to cover the price or target price of a fixed-price contract or the estimated cost and any fee of a cost-reimbursement contract, and if the contract is incrementally funded, funds are obligated to cover the amount allotted and any corresponding increment of fee [@CL-CTF-3692; @CL-CTF-3693]. Under RFO FAR 32.700, the subpart describes basic requirements for contract funding and prescribes procedures for using limitation of cost or limitation of funds clauses, and detailed acquisition funding requirements are contained in agency fiscal regulations [@CL-CTF-3040].

## How it works

Under paragraph (a), if the contract is fully funded, funds are obligated to cover the price or target price of a fixed-price contract or the estimated cost and any fee of a cost-reimbursement contract [@CL-CTF-3687]. Under paragraph (b), if the contract is incrementally funded, funds are obligated to cover the amount allotted and any corresponding increment of fee [@CL-CTF-3688].

Before executing any contract, the contracting officer shall either obtain written assurance from responsible fiscal authority that adequate funds are available or expressly condition the contract upon availability of funds in accordance with FAR 32.703-2 [@CL-FPA-0051]; see [Funded versus unfunded contract value: the policy](../../fpa/backlog/fpa-funded-versus-unfunded-policy.md). Under DFARS 232.703-1, a fixed-price contract may be incrementally funded only if the contract (excluding any options) or any exercised option is for severable services, does not exceed one year in length, and is incrementally funded using funds available (unexpired) as of the date the funds are obligated, or if the contract uses funds available from multiple (two or more) fiscal years and either the contract is funded with research and development appropriations or Congress has otherwise authorized incremental funding [@CL-FPA-0069; @CL-FPA-0070; @CL-FPA-0071]. An incrementally funded fixed-price contract shall be fully funded as soon as funds are available [@CL-FPA-0072]; see [DoD incrementally funded fixed-price contracts](../../fpa/backlog/fpa-dod-incrementally-funded-fixed-price.md). For the clauses that govern cost-reimbursement contracts as funds run out, see [Limitation of Funds clause](ctf-limitation-of-funds.md) and [Contract funding: incremental funding policy](ctf-incremental-funding-policy.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fully funded fixed-price contract has a target price of 2,000,000, so funds are obligated for 2,000,000 [@CL-CTF-3687]. A fully funded cost-reimbursement contract has an estimated cost of 800,000 and a fee of 60,000, so funds are obligated for 860,000 [@CL-CTF-3687]. An incrementally funded cost-reimbursement contract has 300,000 allotted and a corresponding fee increment of 12,000, so funds are obligated for 312,000 [@CL-CTF-3688].

## Never confuse

Fully funded and incrementally funded contracts differ in what the obligation covers rather than in when funds arrive: if the contract is fully funded, funds are obligated to cover the price or target price of a fixed-price contract or the estimated cost and any fee of a cost-reimbursement contract [@CL-CTF-3687], while if the contract is incrementally funded, funds are obligated instead to cover the amount allotted and any corresponding increment of fee [@CL-CTF-3688].

Subpart 32.7 describes basic requirements for contract funding and prescribes procedures for using limitation of cost or limitation of funds clauses, and detailed acquisition funding requirements are contained in agency fiscal regulations [@CL-CTF-3691].
