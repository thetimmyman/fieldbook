## Purpose

Shows what obligated funds cover when a contract is fully funded and when it is incrementally funded. The section sits in the FAR subpart on contract funding, which describes basic requirements for contract funding and prescribes procedures for using limitation of cost or limitation of funds clauses. It also says that detailed acquisition funding requirements are contained in agency fiscal regulations [@CL-CTF-3691].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3687] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.703-1(a) and (b) [@CL-CTF-3687; @CL-CTF-3688] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.700 [@CL-CTF-3040] and RFO FAR 32.703-1(a) and (b) [@CL-CTF-3692; @CL-CTF-3693] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3687; @CL-CTF-3688; @CL-CTF-3692; @CL-CTF-3693] |
| Applies to | Paragraph (a): a fully funded contract, fixed-price or cost-reimbursement [@CL-CTF-3687]. Paragraph (b): an incrementally funded contract, with no contract type named [@CL-CTF-3688] |

If a contract is fully funded, funds are obligated to cover the price or target price of a fixed-price contract, or the estimated cost and any fee of a cost-reimbursement contract [@CL-CTF-3687]. If a contract is incrementally funded, funds are obligated to cover the amount allotted and any corresponding increment of fee [@CL-CTF-3688]. The RFO text has the same two paragraphs in the same words [@CL-CTF-3692; @CL-CTF-3693]. Its scope section, RFO FAR 32.700, says the subpart describes basic requirements for contract funding and prescribes procedures for using limitation of cost or limitation of funds clauses, and that detailed acquisition funding requirements are contained in agency fiscal regulations [@CL-CTF-3691; @CL-CTF-3040].

## How it works

The section describes what the obligation covers. It does not say who obligates the funds, and it sets no threshold or deadline [@CL-CTF-3687; @CL-CTF-3688].

Under paragraph (a), a fully funded fixed-price contract has funds obligated for the whole price or target price. A fully funded cost-reimbursement contract has funds obligated for the estimated cost and any fee [@CL-CTF-3687]. Under paragraph (b), an incrementally funded contract has funds obligated for the amount allotted and any corresponding increment of fee [@CL-CTF-3688]. Paragraph (b) names no contract type.

Other cards cover the rules around this section. Before executing any contract, the contracting officer shall either obtain written assurance from responsible fiscal authority that adequate funds are available or expressly condition the contract upon availability of funds [@CL-FPA-0051]; see [Funded versus unfunded contract value: the policy](../../fpa/backlog/fpa-funded-versus-unfunded-policy.md). The DFARS lets a fixed-price contract be incrementally funded only under either of two sets of conditions it lists [@CL-FPA-0069; @CL-FPA-0071], and requires an incrementally funded fixed-price contract to be fully funded as soon as funds are available [@CL-FPA-0072]; see [DoD incrementally funded fixed-price contracts](../../fpa/backlog/fpa-dod-incrementally-funded-fixed-price.md). For the clauses that govern cost-reimbursement contracts as funds run out, see [Limitation of Funds clause](ctf-limitation-of-funds.md) and [Contract funding: incremental funding policy](ctf-incremental-funding-policy.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fully funded fixed-price contract has a target price of 2,000,000, so funds are obligated for 2,000,000 [@CL-CTF-3687]. A fully funded cost-reimbursement contract has an estimated cost of 800,000 and a fee of 60,000, so funds are obligated for 860,000 [@CL-CTF-3687]. An incrementally funded cost-reimbursement contract has 300,000 allotted and a corresponding fee increment of 12,000, so funds are obligated for 312,000 [@CL-CTF-3688]. The example uses a cost-reimbursement contract for the third case because a fixed-price contract can be incrementally funded only under the DFARS conditions [@CL-FPA-0069].

## Never confuse

A fully funded contract and an incrementally funded contract have different obligation amounts. The first covers the whole price or target price, or the estimated cost and any fee [@CL-CTF-3687]. The second covers only the amount allotted and any corresponding increment of fee [@CL-CTF-3688].

This section states what funds are obligated [@CL-CTF-3687; @CL-CTF-3688]. The procedures for using limitation of cost or limitation of funds clauses are in the same subpart, not in this section. Detailed acquisition funding requirements are contained in agency fiscal regulations [@CL-CTF-3691].
