## Purpose

Shows which ordering, order-limitation, definite-quantity, requirements and indefinite-quantity clauses go into a solicitation or contract, and what triggers each.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1222] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.506 [@CL-CTF-1222] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1222] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that contemplate an indefinite-delivery contract [@CL-CTF-1222; @CL-CTF-1226] |

The clause at 52.216-18, Ordering, is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts when a definite-quantity contract, a requirements contract, or an indefinite-quantity contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1222]. A clause substantially the same as the clause at 52.216-19, Order Limitations, is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts when a definite-quantity contract, a requirements contract, or an indefinite-quantity contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1223]. The clause at 52.216-20, Definite Quantity, is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts when a definite-quantity contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1224].

The clause at 52.216-21, Requirements, is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts when a requirements contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1225]. The clause at 52.216-22, Indefinite Quantity, is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts when an indefinite-quantity contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1226]. The provision at 52.216-27, Single or Multiple Awards, is to be inserted in solicitations for indefinite-quantity contracts that may result in multiple contract awards, modified to specify the estimated number of awards [@CL-CTF-1227]. That provision is not to be used for advisory and assistance services contracts that exceed 3 years and $20 million, including all options [@CL-CTF-1227; @CL-CTF-1256].

## How it works

The clause at 52.216-18, Ordering, and a clause substantially the same as 52.216-19, Order Limitations, are inserted in solicitations and contracts when a definite-quantity contract, a requirements contract, or an indefinite-quantity contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1222; @CL-CTF-1223]. The clause at 52.216-20, Definite Quantity, is inserted in solicitations and contracts when a definite-quantity contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1224], the clause at 52.216-21, Requirements, when a requirements contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1225], and the clause at 52.216-22, Indefinite Quantity, when an indefinite-quantity contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1226]. The provision at 52.216-27, Single or Multiple Awards, is inserted in solicitations for indefinite-quantity contracts that may result in multiple contract awards, modified to specify the estimated number of awards, and is not used for advisory and assistance services contracts that exceed 3 years and $20 million (including all options) [@CL-CTF-1227; @CL-CTF-1256].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation contemplates a requirements contract for repair parts. The ordering clause and the order-limitations clause go in, and so does the requirements clause. The definite-quantity clause and the indefinite-quantity clause do not apply to this solicitation. The answer: the contract type in the solicitation decides which type-specific clause is inserted.

## Never confuse

The single-or-multiple-awards provision differs from the five clauses rather than belonging with them: the provision at 52.216-27, Single or Multiple Awards, is prescribed for solicitations for indefinite-quantity contracts that may result in multiple contract awards [@CL-CTF-1227], while the clauses at 52.216-18, 52.216-19, 52.216-20, 52.216-21, and 52.216-22 are prescribed for solicitations and contracts rather than for solicitations alone [@CL-CTF-1222; @CL-CTF-1223; @CL-CTF-1224; @CL-CTF-1225; @CL-CTF-1226]. For 52.216-19, Order Limitations, the prescription directs inserting a clause substantially the same as the clause at 52.216-19 rather than that exact text [@CL-CTF-1223]. The contract types themselves are on [Indefinite-delivery and indefinite-quantity contracts](ctf-indefinite-delivery-and-indefinite-quantity-contracts.md) and [Requirements contracts](ctf-requirements-contracts.md). What each contract type means for billing and risk is on [Contract types and what they mean for finance](../billing-payment/ctf-contract-types.md).
