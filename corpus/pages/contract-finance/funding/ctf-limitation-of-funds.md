## Purpose

Explains how an incrementally funded contract limits the work the contractor must perform to the funds actually allotted, and which notices apply.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0114] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-22 [@CL-CTF-0114] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-22 [@CL-CTF-1171] |
| Edition date | APR 1984 [@CL-CTF-9729] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0114] |
| Applies to | Contracts in which the Limitation of Funds clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 32.706-2(b) [@CL-CTF-0114] |

The parties estimate that performance will not cost the Government more than the estimated cost stated in the Schedule or, for a cost-sharing contract, the Government's share of that cost [@CL-CTF-0115]. The parties contemplate that the Government will allot funds incrementally up to the full estimated cost, exclusive of any fee, and the contractor agrees to perform, or have performed, work up to the point at which the total amount paid and payable approximates but does not exceed the total amount actually allotted [@CL-CTF-0116; @CL-CTF-0117].

Except as required by other contract provisions that specifically cite and are stated to be an exception to the clause, the Government is not obligated to reimburse costs incurred above the total amount allotted [@CL-CTF-0122].

## How it works

The Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing whenever it has reason to believe that the costs it expects to incur under the contract in the next 60 days, when added to all costs previously incurred, will exceed 75 percent of the total amount so far allotted to the contract by the Government or, if it is a cost-sharing contract, the amount then allotted to the contract by the Government plus the Contractor's corresponding share [@CL-CTF-0118; @CL-CTF-0276]. The notice shall state the estimated amount of additional funds required to continue performance for the period specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-1279].

Sixty days before the end of the period specified in the Schedule, the Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing of the estimated amount of additional funds, if any, required to continue timely performance under the contract or for any further period specified in the Schedule or otherwise agreed upon, and when the funds will be required [@CL-CTF-0119; @CL-CTF-0277].

If, after notification, additional funds are not allotted by the end of the period specified in the Schedule or another agreed-upon date, upon the Contractor's written request the Contracting Officer will terminate the contract on that date in accordance with the provisions of the Termination clause of the contract [@CL-CTF-0120; @CL-CTF-9564]. If the Contractor estimates that the funds available will allow it to continue to discharge its obligations beyond that date, it may specify a later date in its request, and the Contracting Officer may terminate the contract on that later date [@CL-CTF-0483].

The codified prescription permits the 60-day period to be varied from 30 to 90 days and the 75 percent from 75 to 85 percent [@CL-CTF-0121].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-22, the clause is inserted as prescribed in 32.706-2(b) [@CL-CTF-1171], the parties estimate that performance of the contract will not cost the Government more than the estimated cost specified in the Schedule or, if it is a cost-sharing contract, the Government's share of the estimated cost specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-1172], the parties contemplate that the Government will allot additional funds incrementally to the contract up to the fully estimated cost to the Government specified in the Schedule, exclusive of any fee [@CL-CTF-1173], and the Contractor agrees to perform, or have performed, work on the contract up to the point at which the total amount paid and payable by the Government under the contract approximates but does not exceed the total amount actually allotted by the Government to the contract [@CL-CTF-1174]. The Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing whenever it has reason to believe that the costs it expects to incur under the contract in the next 60 days, when added to all costs previously incurred, will exceed 75 percent of the total amount so far allotted to the contract by the Government or, if it is a cost-sharing contract, the amount then allotted to the contract by the Government plus the Contractor's corresponding share, and the notice shall state the estimated amount of additional funds required to continue performance for the period specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-1175; @CL-CTF-1176; @CL-CTF-1280]. Sixty days before the end of the period specified in the Schedule, the Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing of the estimated amount of additional funds, if any, required to continue timely performance under the contract or for any further period specified in the Schedule or otherwise agreed upon, and when the funds will be required [@CL-CTF-1177; @CL-CTF-1178]. If, after notification, additional funds are not allotted by the end of the period specified in the Schedule or another agreed-upon date, upon the Contractor's written request the Contracting Officer will terminate the contract on that date in accordance with the provisions of the Termination clause of the contract, or if the Contractor estimates that the funds available will allow it to continue to discharge its obligations beyond that date, it may specify a later date in its request, and the Contracting Officer may terminate the contract on that later date [@CL-CTF-1179; @CL-CTF-1180; @CL-CTF-1181]. Except as required by other provisions of the contract, specifically citing and stated to be an exception to the clause, the Government is not obligated to reimburse the Contractor for costs incurred in excess of the total amount allotted by the Government to the contract [@CL-CTF-1182].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An incrementally funded contract has 500,000 allotted against an estimated cost of 1,200,000. Costs of 300,000 are incurred and the contractor expects 100,000 more in the next 60 days, so the projected 400,000 exceeds 75 percent of the 500,000 allotted and the written notice is due.

## Never confuse

The Limitation of Funds clause ties the contractor's effort to the amount actually allotted and requires notices as that amount is used up, and, unless another provision specifically cites and is stated to be an exception to the clause, it does not oblige the Government to reimburse cost above the allotted amount [@CL-CTF-0117; @CL-CTF-0122].

A related but separate clause is covered on the [limitation of cost](../funding/ctf-limitation-of-cost.md) page.

Under FAR 52.232-22, the 60-day period may be varied from 30 to 90 days and the 75 percent from 75 to 85 percent [@CL-CTF-0121]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-22, the clause is inserted as prescribed in 32.706-2(b), paragraph (c) specifies 60 days and 75 percent, and paragraph (d) specifies sixty days [@CL-CTF-1171; @CL-CTF-1175; @CL-CTF-1177].
