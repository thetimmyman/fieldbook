## Purpose

Shows the preliminary notice, the exercise window and the total duration limit for the option to extend the term of the contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2308] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-9 [@CL-CTF-2308] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.217-9 [@CL-CTF-1354; @CL-CTF-1355; @CL-CTF-1356; @CL-CTF-1357; @CL-CTF-1358] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2308] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the option to extend the term clause [@CL-CTF-2308] |

The Government may extend the term of the contract by written notice to the Contractor within the period inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-2308], provided that the Government gives the Contractor a preliminary written notice of its intent to extend at least the inserted number of days before the contract expires (60 days unless a different number of days is inserted) [@CL-CTF-2309]. The preliminary notice does not commit the Government to an extension [@CL-CTF-2310].

The total duration of the contract, including the exercise of any options under the clause, shall not exceed the period in months or years inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-2312]. If the Government exercises the option, the extended contract shall be considered to include the option clause [@CL-CTF-2311].

## How it works

The Government may extend the term of the contract by written notice to the Contractor within the period inserted in the clause, provided that the Government gives the Contractor a preliminary written notice of its intent to extend at least the inserted number of days before the contract expires (60 days unless a different number of days is inserted) [@CL-CTF-2308; @CL-CTF-2309]. The preliminary notice does not commit the Government to an extension [@CL-CTF-2310]. If the Government exercises the option, the extended contract shall be considered to include the option clause, and the total duration of the contract, including the exercise of any options under the clause, shall not exceed the period in months or years inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-2311; @CL-CTF-2312]. The prescription is covered on [Option solicitation provisions and contract clauses](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-9, the clause is inserted as prescribed in 17.203(g) [@CL-CTF-1354]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-9(a), the Government may extend the term of the contract by written notice to the Contractor within the period inserted in the clause, provided that the Government gives the Contractor a preliminary written notice of its intent to extend at least the inserted number of days before the contract expires (60 days unless a different number of days is inserted) [@CL-CTF-1355], and the preliminary notice does not commit the Government to an extension [@CL-CTF-1356]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-9(b), if the Government exercises the option, the extended contract shall be considered to include the option clause [@CL-CTF-1357]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-9(c), the total duration of the contract, including the exercise of any options under the clause, shall not exceed the period in months or years inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-1358].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The contract's current term runs to the end of day 730. It was a base period of 1 year, and the Government has already exercised one 1-year extension. The clause keeps the default of 60 days for the preliminary notice, and the total duration limit inserted is 3 years. The clause also inserts a period for giving the written notice of extension, and the Government gives that notice within it.

Answer: the preliminary notice must be given at least 60 days before expiry, so on or before day 670. A preliminary notice on day 650 meets this, being 80 days before expiry, and one on day 690 does not, being 40 days before. The preliminary notice does not commit the Government to an extension. If the Government exercises a further 1-year extension, the total duration becomes 3 years, which does not exceed the limit, and the extended contract is considered to include the option clause. Another 1-year extension after that would bring the total to 4 years, which exceeds the limit, so the clause does not support it.

## Never confuse

This clause extends the term of the contract and requires a preliminary written notice of intent to extend [@CL-CTF-2308; @CL-CTF-2309]. The clause to extend services allows continued performance of services with a total extension of no more than 6 months [@CL-CTF-2305; @CL-CTF-2306]. See [Option to extend services](ctf-option-to-extend-services-clause.md).
