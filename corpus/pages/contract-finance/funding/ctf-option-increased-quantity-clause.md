## Purpose

Shows how the Government increases the supply quantity under this option, how it gives notice and how delivery of the added items proceeds.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2299] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-6 [@CL-CTF-2299] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.217-6 [@CL-CTF-1272] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2299] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the increased quantity clause [@CL-CTF-2299] |

The Government may increase the quantity of supplies called for in the Schedule at the unit price specified [@CL-CTF-2299]. The Contracting Officer may exercise the option by written notice to the Contractor within a period of time that the clause leaves as a contract-specific blank to be inserted [@CL-CTF-2300]. Delivery of the added items shall continue at the same rate as the like items called for under the contract, unless the parties otherwise agree [@CL-CTF-2301].

## How it works

The Government may increase the quantity of supplies called for in the Schedule at the unit price specified, so the added items carry the unit price specified [@CL-CTF-2299]. The Contracting Officer exercises the option by written notice, and the clause text leaves a blank with a bracketed instruction to insert the period, so the period is contract-specific [@CL-CTF-2300]. After exercise, delivery of the added items continues at the rate of the like items, unless the parties agree otherwise [@CL-CTF-2301]. The prescription is covered on [Option solicitation provisions and contract clauses](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the Government's right to increase the quantity of supplies called for in the Schedule at the unit price specified [@CL-CTF-1272; @CL-CTF-2299]. It keeps the Contracting Officer's exercise by written notice within a period that the clause leaves as a blank to be inserted [@CL-CTF-1273; @CL-CTF-2300]. It keeps delivery of the added items at the same rate as the like items called for under the contract, unless the parties otherwise agree [@CL-CTF-1274; @CL-CTF-2301].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The Schedule prices a supply item at 50 per unit, and like items are delivered at 10 units a month. The Contracting Officer sends written notice exercising the option for 20 more units within the period inserted in the clause.

Answer: the 20 added units are priced at the specified unit price of 50 each, which is 1,000. Delivery of the added units continues at the same rate as the like items, 10 a month, which is 2 months for the 20 units, unless the parties agree otherwise.

## Never confuse

This clause increases the quantity of supplies called for in the Schedule at the unit price specified [@CL-CTF-2299]. The separately priced line item clause instead lets the Government require delivery of a numbered line item identified in the Schedule as an option item, in the quantity and at the price stated in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-2302]. See [Option for increased quantity as a separately priced line item](ctf-option-increased-quantity-line-item-clause.md).
