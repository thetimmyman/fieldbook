## Purpose

Shows how option prices are added in evaluation under the two provisions that count options, and that evaluation of options does not commit the Government to exercise them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-4 and FAR 52.217-5 [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include either provision [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |

Under the provision for options exercised at the time of award, the Government evaluates the total price for the basic requirement together with any option(s) exercised at the time of award, except when it is determined in accordance with FAR 17.206(b) not to be in the Government's best interests [@CL-CTF-2296]. Under the evaluation of options provision, the Government evaluates offers for award purposes by adding the total price for all options to the total price for the basic requirement, with the same exception [@CL-CTF-2297]. Under the evaluation of options provision, evaluation of options will not obligate the Government to exercise the option(s) [@CL-CTF-2298].

## How it works

The first provision evaluates any option or options exercised at the time of award together with the basic requirement price [@CL-CTF-2296]. The second provision adds the total price for all options to the total price for the basic requirement [@CL-CTF-2297]. Both give way when it is determined in accordance with FAR 17.206(b) not to be in the Government's best interests [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297]. The evaluation of options provision adds that evaluation of options does not obligate the Government to exercise the options [@CL-CTF-2298]. The text of the options exercised at award provision does not say this.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Offer X prices the basic requirement at 1,000,000 and two options at 200,000 and 150,000. Offer Y prices the basic requirement at 1,100,000 and one option at 100,000. No determination has been made that evaluating options is not in the Government's best interests.

Answer: under the evaluation of options provision, Offer X is evaluated at 1,350,000 and Offer Y at 1,200,000. Evaluating the options does not obligate the Government to exercise any of them. Under the options exercised at award provision, if only the 200,000 option of Offer X is exercised at award, Offer X is evaluated at 1,200,000, the basic price plus that option. If no option is exercised at award, Offer Y is evaluated at its basic price of 1,100,000.

## Never confuse

The evaluation exclusive of options provision counts no option price, while these two provisions add option prices, either those exercised at award or all options [@CL-CTF-2295; @CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297]. The printed text of these two provisions does not say when an offer may be rejected as unbalanced, so this card states no rule for rejecting an offer on that ground. See [Evaluation exclusive of options provision](ctf-evaluation-exclusive-of-options-provision.md) and [Option solicitation provisions and contract clauses](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md).
