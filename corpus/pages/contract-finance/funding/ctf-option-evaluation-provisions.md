## Purpose

Shows how option prices are added in evaluation under the two provisions that count options, and that evaluation of options does not commit the Government to exercise them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-4 and FAR 52.217-5 [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.217-4 [@CL-CTF-1269] and RFO FAR 52.217-5 [@CL-CTF-1270; @CL-CTF-1271] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include either provision [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297] |

Under the codified provision for options exercised at the time of award, the Government evaluates the total price for the basic requirement together with any option(s) exercised at the time of award, except when it is determined in accordance with FAR 17.206(b) not to be in the Government's best interests [@CL-CTF-2296]. Under the codified evaluation of options provision, the Government evaluates offers for award purposes by adding the total price for all options to the total price for the basic requirement, with the same exception [@CL-CTF-2297]. Under the evaluation of options provision, evaluation of options will not obligate the Government to exercise the option(s) [@CL-CTF-2298].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.217-4, except when it is determined in accordance with FAR 17.206(b) not to be in the Government's best interests, the Government will evaluate the total price for the basic requirement together with any option(s) exercised at the time of award [@CL-CTF-2296]. Under FAR 52.217-5, except when it is determined in accordance with FAR 17.206(b) not to be in the Government's best interests, the Government will evaluate offers for award purposes by adding the total price for all options to the total price for the basic requirement [@CL-CTF-2297], and evaluation of options will not obligate the Government to exercise the option(s) [@CL-CTF-2298].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-4, except when it is determined in accordance with FAR 17.202(b) not to be in the Government's best interests, the Government will evaluate the total price for the basic requirement together with any option(s) exercised at the time of award [@CL-CTF-1269]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-5, except when it is determined in accordance with FAR 17.202(b) not to be in the Government's best interests, the Government will evaluate offers for award purposes by adding the total price for all options to the total price for the basic requirement [@CL-CTF-1270], and evaluation of options will not obligate the Government to exercise the option(s) [@CL-CTF-1271].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Offer X prices the basic requirement at 1,000,000 and two options at 200,000 and 150,000. Offer Y prices the basic requirement at 1,100,000 and one option at 100,000. No determination has been made that evaluating options is not in the Government's best interests.

Answer: under the evaluation of options provision, Offer X is evaluated at 1,350,000 and Offer Y at 1,200,000. Evaluating the options does not obligate the Government to exercise any of them. Under the options exercised at award provision, if only the 200,000 option of Offer X is exercised at award, Offer X is evaluated at 1,200,000, the basic price plus that option. If no option is exercised at award, Offer Y is evaluated at its basic price of 1,100,000.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.217-3, the Government will evaluate offers for award purposes by including only the price for the basic requirement, whereas FAR 52.217-4 and FAR 52.217-5 evaluate option prices exercised at the time of award or add the total price for all options to the total price for the basic requirement [@CL-CTF-2295; @CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297]. See [Evaluation exclusive of options provision](ctf-evaluation-exclusive-of-options-provision.md) and [Option solicitation provisions and contract clauses](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md).

Under FAR 52.217-4 and FAR 52.217-5, the exception refers to a determination in accordance with FAR 17.206(b) [@CL-CTF-2296; @CL-CTF-2297]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-4 and RFO FAR 52.217-5, the exception refers to a determination in accordance with FAR 17.202(b) [@CL-CTF-1269; @CL-CTF-1270].
