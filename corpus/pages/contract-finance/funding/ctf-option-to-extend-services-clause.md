## Purpose

Shows the price basis, the six-month limit on extension and the notice for the option to extend services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2305] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-8 [@CL-CTF-2305] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.217-8 [@CL-CTF-1351; @CL-CTF-1352; @CL-CTF-1353] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2305] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the option to extend services clause [@CL-CTF-2305] |

The Government may require continued performance of any services within the limits and at the rates specified in the contract, and the rates may be adjusted only as a result of revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CTF-2305]. The option may be exercised more than once, but the total extension of performance shall not exceed 6 months [@CL-CTF-2306]. The Contracting Officer may exercise the option by written notice to the Contractor within a period of time that the clause leaves as a contract-specific blank to be inserted [@CL-CTF-2307].

## How it works

The Government may require continued performance of any services within the limits and at the rates specified in the contract, and only revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor can adjust those rates [@CL-CTF-2305]. The option may be exercised more than once, but the total extension of performance shall not exceed 6 months [@CL-CTF-2306]. Each exercise is by written notice within the period inserted in the clause, which the clause text leaves as a blank with a bracketed instruction to insert it, so the period is contract-specific [@CL-CTF-2307]. The prescription is covered on [Option solicitation provisions and contract clauses](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md).

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.217-8 keeps the rule that the Government may require continued performance of any services within the limits and at the rates specified in the contract, with those rates adjusted only as a result of revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CTF-1351; @CL-CTF-2305]. It keeps the rule that the option may be exercised more than once, but the total extension of performance shall not exceed 6 months [@CL-CTF-1352; @CL-CTF-2306]. It keeps the rule that the Contracting Officer may exercise the option by written notice to the Contractor within the period inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-1353; @CL-CTF-2307].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A services contract has a monthly rate of 100,000. The Contracting Officer gives written notice within the inserted period and extends performance by 4 months. Later the Contracting Officer gives written notice for 2 more months. Then the Contracting Officer asks for 1 more month.

Answer: the first two extensions total 6 months, which is the limit, and are priced at the contract rate, which is 600,000 for the 6 months. The extra month would bring the total to 7 months, which exceeds the limit, so the option does not support it. If the Contractor asked to raise the rate to 110,000 because its own costs went up, the clause would not allow it, because the rates may be adjusted only as a result of revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor.

## Never confuse

This clause covers continued performance of services, with a total extension of no more than 6 months [@CL-CTF-2306]. The clause to extend the term of the contract needs a preliminary written notice, and its total duration limit is a contract-specific fill-in [@CL-CTF-2309; @CL-CTF-2312]. See [Option to extend the term of the contract](ctf-option-to-extend-term-clause.md).
