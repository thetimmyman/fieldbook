## Purpose

Shows the price basis, the six-month limit on extension and the notice for the option to extend services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2305] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-8 [@CL-CTF-2305] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.217-8 [@CL-CTF-1351; @CL-CTF-1352; @CL-CTF-1353] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2305] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the option to extend services clause [@CL-CTF-2305] |

The Government may require continued performance of any services within the limits and at the rates specified in the contract, and these rates may be adjusted only as a result of revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CTF-2305]. The option provision may be exercised more than once, but the total extension of performance hereunder shall not exceed 6 months [@CL-CTF-2306]. The Contracting Officer may exercise the option by written notice to the Contractor within the period of time inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-2307].

## How it works

The Government may require continued performance of any services within the limits and at the rates specified in the contract, and these rates may be adjusted only as a result of revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CTF-2305]. The option provision may be exercised more than once, but the total extension of performance shall not exceed 6 months [@CL-CTF-2306]. The Contracting Officer may exercise the option by written notice to the Contractor within the period of time inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-2307]. The prescription is covered on [Option solicitation provisions and contract clauses](ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.217-8, the Government may require continued performance of any services within the limits and at the rates specified in the contract, and these rates may be adjusted only as a result of revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CTF-1351]. The option provision may be exercised more than once, but the total extension of performance hereunder shall not exceed 6 months [@CL-CTF-1352]. The Contracting Officer may exercise the option by written notice to the Contractor within the period of time inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-1353].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A services contract has a monthly rate of 100,000. The Contracting Officer gives written notice within the inserted period and extends performance by 4 months. Later the Contracting Officer gives written notice for 2 more months. Then the Contracting Officer asks for 1 more month.

Answer: the first two extensions total 6 months, which is the limit, and are priced at the contract rate, which is 600,000 for the 6 months. The extra month would bring the total to 7 months, which exceeds the limit, so the option does not support it. If the Contractor asked to raise the rate to 110,000 because its own costs went up, the clause would not allow it, because the rates may be adjusted only as a result of revisions to prevailing labor rates provided by the Secretary of Labor.

## Never confuse

This clause covers continued performance of services, with a total extension of no more than 6 months [@CL-CTF-2305; @CL-CTF-2306]. Under FAR 52.217-9, the extension is provided that the Government gives the Contractor a preliminary written notice of its intent to extend at least the inserted number of days before the contract expires, and the total duration of the contract, including the exercise of any options under the clause, shall not exceed the period in months or years inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-2309; @CL-CTF-2312]. See [Option to extend the term of the contract](ctf-option-to-extend-term-clause.md).
