## Purpose

Shows when a contractor must warn the contracting officer that costs are approaching the estimated cost of a fully funded cost-reimbursement contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR clause [@CL-CTF-0003] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-20(b)(1) [@CL-CTF-0003] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-20(b)(1) [@CL-CTF-1156] |
| Edition date | 1984-04 [@CL-CTF-0003] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0003] |
| Applies to | Fully funded cost-reimbursement contracts with the clause [@CL-FPA-0091] |

The contractor must notify the contracting officer in writing whenever it has reason to believe that the costs it expects to incur in the next 60 days, when added to all costs previously incurred, will exceed 75 percent of the estimated cost specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-2968; @CL-CTF-2919; @CL-CTF-0003]. The contractor must also notify the contracting officer in writing whenever it has reason to believe that the total cost of performance, exclusive of any fee, will be either greater or substantially less than had been previously estimated [@CL-CTF-2968; @CL-CTF-2920]. The codified prescription lets the 60-day period be varied from 30 to 90 days and the 75 percent from 75 to 85 percent, so read the clause in the contract [@CL-CTF-2917].

## How it works

Whenever the Contractor has reason to believe that the costs it expects to incur under the contract in the next 60 days, when added to all costs previously incurred, will exceed 75 percent of the estimated cost specified in the Schedule, or that the total cost for the performance of the contract, exclusive of any fee, will be either greater or substantially less than had been previously estimated, the Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing and, as part of the notification, provide a revised estimate of the total cost of performing the contract [@CL-CTF-2968; @CL-CTF-2919; @CL-CTF-2920; @CL-CTF-2921]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-20(b)(1), the Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing whenever it has reason to believe that the costs the Contractor expects to incur under the contract in the next 60 days, when added to all costs previously incurred, will exceed 75 percent of the estimated cost specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-1155; @CL-CTF-1156]. Except as required by other provisions of the contract, specifically citing and stated to be an exception to the clause, the Government is not obligated to reimburse the Contractor for costs incurred in excess of the estimated cost specified in the Schedule or, if it is a cost-sharing contract, the estimated cost to the Government specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-2969; @CL-CTF-2922], and the Contractor is not obligated to continue performance under the contract (including actions under the Termination clause of the contract) or otherwise incur costs in excess of the estimated cost specified in the Schedule, until the Contracting Officer notifies the Contractor in writing that the estimated cost has been increased and provides a revised estimated total cost of performing the contract [@CL-CTF-2923; @CL-CTF-2924].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has an estimated cost of 4,000,000. Costs incurred are 2,500,000, and 600,000 more is expected over the next 60 days. The total, 3,100,000, is above 75 percent of the estimate, 3,000,000, so the notice is due now.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-20, Limitation of Cost, in solicitations and contracts if a fully funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated, whether or not the contract provides for payment of a fee [@CL-FPA-0091]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-22, Limitation of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if an incrementally funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0092], where the notice threshold is measured against the total amount so far allotted to the contract by the Government or, in a cost-sharing contract, the amount then allotted by the Government plus the Contractor's corresponding share [@CL-CTF-0118; @CL-CTF-0276].
