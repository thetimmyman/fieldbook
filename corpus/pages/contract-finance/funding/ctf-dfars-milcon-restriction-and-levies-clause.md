## Purpose

DFARS 232.703-70 states that annual military construction appropriations acts restrict the use of funds appropriated by the acts for payments under cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts and points to DFARS 216.306(c) [@CL-CTF-3689]. DFARS 232.7102 directs use of the Levies on Contract Payments clause [@CL-CTF-3690], within the subpart that prescribes policies and procedures concerning the effect of tax levies under Internal Revenue Code section 6331(h) on contract payments [@CL-CTF-3694].

Annual military construction appropriations acts restrict the use of funds appropriated by the acts for payments under cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts (see 216.306(c)) [@CL-CTF-3689].

Use the clause at 252.232-7010, Levies on Contract Payments, in all solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CTF-3690].

## How it works

Annual military construction appropriations acts restrict the use of funds appropriated by the acts for payments under cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts and cross-reference DFARS 216.306(c) [@CL-CTF-3689], which is covered on [Cost-plus-fixed-fee and incentive-fee contract approvals](../../market/programs/mkt-cost-and-incentive-fee-contract-approvals.md). Under DFARS 216.306(c), annual military construction appropriations acts prohibit the use of cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts that are funded by a military construction appropriations act, are estimated to exceed $25,000, and will be performed within the United States, except Alaska [@CL-MKT-1582]. The prohibition does not apply to contracts that are specifically approved in writing, setting forth the reasons therefor, in accordance with the approval rules in paragraph (c)(ii) [@CL-MKT-1633]. The Secretaries of the military departments are authorized to approve such contracts that are for environmental work only, provided the environmental work is not classified as construction, as defined by title 10, United States Code, section 2801 [@CL-MKT-1583]. The Secretary of Defense or designee must approve such contracts that are not for environmental work only or are for environmental work classified as construction [@CL-MKT-1584].

DFARS 232.7102 directs use of the clause at 252.232-7010, Levies on Contract Payments, in all solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CTF-3690].

DFARS 232.7100 states that the Internal Revenue Service is authorized to levy up to 100 percent of all payments made under a DoD contract, up to the amount of the tax debt [@CL-CTF-3695]. The contractor's and contracting officer's notice duties are on [Levies on contract payments](../cash/ctf-levies-on-contract-payments.md) and [Tax levies that may keep a contractor from performing](../financing/ctf-tax-levy-notification.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-fixed-fee contract is funded by a military construction appropriations act, is estimated at 400,000 dollars, and will be performed in Texas. All three conditions of the prohibition in 216.306(c) are met [@CL-MKT-1582], so the contract is prohibited unless it is specifically approved in writing, setting forth the reasons [@CL-MKT-1633]. The example says nothing of environmental work, so this assumes the work is not for environmental work only; then the Secretary of Defense or designee must approve it [@CL-MKT-1584]. DFARS 232.703-70 itself states only the restriction on use of the appropriated funds [@CL-CTF-3689].

A commercial services contract is solicited under FAR part 12 procedures. The Levies on Contract Payments clause is still used, because the direction covers solicitations and contracts using those procedures [@CL-CTF-3690].

## Never confuse

DFARS 232.703-70 restricts the use of funds appropriated by annual military construction appropriations acts for payments under cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts [@CL-CTF-3689].

DFARS 232.7102 prescribes the clause at 252.232-7010, Levies on Contract Payments, in all solicitations and contracts [@CL-CTF-3690]. The clause at 252.232-7003, Electronic Submission of Payment Requests and Receiving Reports, is prescribed by DFARS 232.7004(a) and is covered on [DoD electronic payment request clause](../financing/ctf-dod-electronic-payment-request-clause.md) [@CL-CTF-1745; @CL-CTF-1788].
