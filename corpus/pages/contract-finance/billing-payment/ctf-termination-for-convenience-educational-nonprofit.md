## Purpose

Shows what an educational or other nonprofit contractor can recover on a termination for convenience, how the settlement proposal works, how costs are paid along the way, and which cost principles govern the amounts by type of institution.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2069] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.249-5 [@CL-CTF-2069] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.249-5 [@CL-CTF-0714] |
| Edition date | Aug 2016 [@CL-CTF-0714] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2069] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when either a fixed-price or cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated for research and development work with an educational or nonprofit institution [@CL-CLS-1021], on a no-profit or no-fee basis [@CL-CLS-1056] |

The Government may terminate performance of work under the contract in whole or, from time to time, in part if the contracting officer determines that a termination is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-2069]. The contracting officer shall terminate by delivering to the contractor a Notice of Termination specifying the extent of termination and the effective date [@CL-CTF-1489]. After termination, the contractor shall submit a final termination settlement proposal to the contracting officer in the form and with the certification prescribed by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-2070]. The contractor shall submit the proposal promptly, but no later than 1 year from the effective date of termination, unless extended in writing by the contracting officer upon written request of the contractor within this 1-year period [@CL-CTF-2071].

If the contractor fails to submit the termination settlement proposal within the time allowed, the contracting officer may determine, on the basis of information available, the amount, if any, due the contractor because of the termination and shall pay the amount determined [@CL-CTF-2072]. Subject to paragraph (d) of the clause, the contractor and the contracting officer may agree upon the whole or any part of the amount to be paid because of the termination, and this amount may include reasonable cancellation charges incurred by the contractor [@CL-CTF-2073]. It may also include any reasonable loss on outstanding commitments for personal services that the contractor is unable to cancel, provided that the contractor exercised reasonable diligence in diverting such commitments to other operations, and the contract shall be amended and the contractor paid the agreed amount [@CL-CTF-2120; @CL-CTF-1490]. After receipt of a Notice of Termination and except as directed by the contracting officer, the contractor shall immediately proceed with its listed obligations [@CL-CTF-9575], including terminating all applicable subcontracts and canceling or diverting applicable commitments covering personal services that extend beyond the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-2237].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.249-5(g), the Government may, under the terms and conditions it prescribes, make partial payments against costs incurred by the contractor for the terminated portion of the contract, if the contracting officer believes the total of these payments will not exceed the amount to which the contractor will be entitled [@CL-CTF-1491; @CL-CTF-2074]. See [Partial payments and final payment under a terminated contract](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-termination-partial-payments-and-final-payment.md). The contractor has the right of appeal as provided under the Disputes clause, except that if the contractor failed to submit the termination settlement proposal within the time provided in paragraph (d) of the clause and failed to request a time extension, there is no right of appeal [@CL-CTF-2075].

Under FAR 52.249-5(f), the cost principles and procedures in subpart 31.3 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation, Contracts with Educational Institutions (48 CFR 31.3), as in effect on the date of the contract, shall govern all costs claimed, agreed to, or determined under the clause [@CL-CTF-1492; @CL-CTF-2121; @CL-CTF-9576], with educational institutions defined as institutions of higher education in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart A, and 20 USC 1001 [@CL-CTF-2121]. However, if the contractor is not an educational institution and is a nonprofit organization as defined in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, the cost principles and procedures in subpart 31.7 of the FAR, Contracts with Nonprofit Organizations, shall apply [@CL-CTF-2122], unless the contractor is a nonprofit institution listed in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, appendix VIII, as exempted from the cost principles in subpart E, in which case the cost principles at FAR 31.2 for commercial organizations shall apply to such contractor [@CL-CTF-2123]. The commercial-organization principles are on [Cost principles for contracts with commercial organizations](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-commercial-organizations.md), and the shared definitions are on [Definitions used in the FAR cost principles](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-far-cost-principles-definitions.md).

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-5(a), (b)(3), and (d)-(e), the Government may terminate performance of work under the contract in whole or, from time to time, in part if the contracting officer determines that a termination is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0714], after receipt of a Notice of Termination and except as directed by the contracting officer, the contractor shall immediately proceed with its listed obligations [@CL-CTF-9146], including terminating all applicable subcontracts and canceling or diverting applicable commitments covering personal services that extend beyond the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-0724], the contractor shall submit the proposal promptly, but no later than 1 year from the effective date of termination, unless extended in writing by the contracting officer upon written request of the contractor within this 1-year period [@CL-CTF-0715], and subject to paragraph (d) of the clause, the contractor and the contracting officer may agree upon the whole or any part of the amount to be paid because of the termination, which may include reasonable cancellation charges incurred by the contractor and any reasonable loss on outstanding commitments for personal services that the contractor is unable to cancel, provided that the contractor exercised reasonable diligence in diverting such commitments to other operations [@CL-CTF-0716; @CL-CTF-0717]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-5(f)-(h), the cost principles and procedures in subpart 31.3 of the FAR, Contracts with Educational Institutions (48 CFR 31.3), as in effect on the date of the contract, shall govern all costs claimed, agreed to, or determined under the clause [@CL-CTF-0721; @CL-CTF-9143], except that if the contractor is not an educational institution and is a nonprofit organization as defined in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart 31.7 of the FAR, Contracts with Nonprofit Organizations, shall apply [@CL-CTF-0722], unless the contractor is a nonprofit institution listed in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, appendix VIII, as exempted from the cost principles in subpart E, in which case the cost principles at FAR 31.2 for commercial organizations shall apply to such contractor [@CL-CTF-0723], the Government may, under the terms and conditions it prescribes, make partial payments against costs incurred by the contractor for the terminated portion of the contract, if the contracting officer believes the total of these payments will not exceed the amount to which the contractor will be entitled [@CL-CTF-1493; @CL-CTF-0718], and the contractor has the right of appeal as provided under the Disputes clause, except that if the contractor failed to submit the termination settlement proposal within the time provided in paragraph (d) of the clause and failed to request a time extension, there is no right of appeal [@CL-CTF-0719].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A university has a research agreement that is terminated in part. It has a commitment of 30,000 for a visiting researcher that it cannot cancel, and it moves 10,000 of that work to other projects with reasonable diligence. The university has also terminated its applicable subcontracts and tried to cancel or divert the commitment. Assume the remaining 20,000 is a reasonable loss on the commitment, so it can be part of the agreed amount. The university's costs are judged under the educational institution cost principles. A nonprofit research institute that is not an educational institution would instead use the nonprofit principles, unless it is on the exempt list, in which case the commercial-organization principles apply.

If the university never sends the settlement proposal and never asks for more time, the contracting officer may decide the amount from available information, and the university has no appeal.

## Never confuse

Partial payments differ from the settlement rather than replace it: under FAR 52.249-5(g), the Government may, under the terms and conditions it prescribes, make partial payments against costs incurred by the contractor for the terminated portion of the contract, if the contracting officer believes the total of these payments will not exceed the amount to which the contractor will be entitled [@CL-CTF-1491; @CL-CTF-2074]. Under FAR 52.249-5(h), the contractor has the right of appeal as provided under the Disputes clause, except that if the contractor failed to submit the termination settlement proposal within the time provided in paragraph (d) of the clause and failed to request a time extension, there is no right of appeal [@CL-CTF-2075]. Under FAR 52.249-5(f), if the contractor is a nonprofit institution listed in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, appendix VIII, as exempted from the cost principles in subpart E, the cost principles at FAR 31.2 for commercial organizations shall apply to such contractor [@CL-CTF-2123] rather than the educational or nonprofit principles. Contracts for research and development work with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-profit basis are excepted from the FAR 49.502(a)(1) prescription for FAR 52.249-1 [@CL-CLS-1014; @CL-CLS-1051] and from the FAR 49.504(b) prescription for FAR 52.249-9 [@CL-CLS-1026; @CL-CLS-1059], and the short-form clauses are on [Termination for convenience: fixed-price and services short forms](ctf-termination-for-convenience-short-forms.md). For the general fixed-price version, see [Termination for Convenience of the Government (Fixed-Price): settlement](ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md). A similar long-form clause for other work is on [Termination for convenience: dismantling, demolition or removal of improvements](ctf-termination-for-convenience-dismantling-demolition-removal.md).
