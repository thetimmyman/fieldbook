## Purpose

Says what a contractor must notify when it ends a pension plan, recaptures pension assets or has an ownership change, when it must notify, and how pension adjustments are made. The segment-closing calculation is on a linked card and is not repeated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1692; @CL-CTF-1696] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-15 [@CL-CTF-1692]; FAR 52.215-19 [@CL-CTF-1696] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-15 [@CL-CTF-0576; @CL-CTF-0577; @CL-CTF-0578; @CL-CTF-0579; @CL-CTF-0580; @CL-CTF-0581]; RFO FAR 52.215-19 [@CL-CTF-0582; @CL-CTF-0583; @CL-CTF-0584; @CL-CTF-0585] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1692; @CL-CTF-1696] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clauses, when the contractor ends a defined-benefit plan, recaptures plan assets or has an ownership change [@CL-CTF-1692; @CL-CTF-1696] |

The contractor shall promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing when it determines that it will terminate a defined-benefit pension plan or otherwise recapture such pension fund assets [@CL-CTF-1692]. For segment closings, pension plan terminations, or curtailment of benefits, the amount of the adjustment is as listed in the clause [@CL-CTF-1693]. For contracts and subcontracts subject to full coverage under the Cost Accounting Standards, one listed measure is the amount measured, assigned, and allocated in accordance with the CAS pension standard at the cited paragraph [@CL-CTF-1693; @CL-CTF-1694]. For contracts and subcontracts that are not subject to full coverage under the CAS, the amount of the adjustment is measured, assigned, and allocated in accordance with the CAS pension standard at the cited paragraph, with an exception [@CL-CTF-1693; @CL-CTF-1719]: the clause states that the numerator of the fraction at the cited subparagraph of that standard shall be the sum of the pension plan costs allocated to all non-CAS covered contracts and subcontracts that are subject to FAR Subpart 31.2 or for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted [@CL-CTF-1720]. For all other situations where assets revert to the contractor, or are constructively received by it for any reason, the contractor shall, at the Government's option, make a refund or give a credit to the Government for its equitable share of the gross amount withdrawn [@CL-CTF-1695]. The Government's equitable share shall reflect the Government's participation in pension costs through those contracts for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted or that are subject to FAR Subpart 31.2 [@CL-CTF-9626].

When the contractor becomes aware that a change in its ownership has occurred or is certain to occur that could change the valuation of its capitalized assets in the accounting records, it shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer within 30 days [@CL-CTF-1696], and the clause requires that notification to be in writing [@CL-CTF-1723]. The contractor shall also notify the Administrative Contracting Officer within 30 days whenever changes to asset valuations or any other cost changes have occurred or are certain to occur as a result of a change in ownership [@CL-CTF-1721]. The contractor shall maintain current, accurate, and complete inventory records of assets and their costs and provide the Administrative Contracting Officer or designated representative ready access to the records upon request [@CL-CTF-1722]. This card does not state the other record duties.

## How it works

Written notice goes to the Contracting Officer when the contractor determines it will terminate a defined-benefit plan or recapture plan assets [@CL-CTF-1692]. The adjustment for a segment closing, plan termination or curtailment of benefits is then measured as the clause lists [@CL-CTF-1693], using the CAS pension standard for full-coverage contracts [@CL-CTF-1694]. In all other situations where assets revert to the contractor or are constructively received by it, the contractor makes a refund or gives a credit at the Government's option, for the Government's equitable share, which reflects the Government's participation in pension costs through contracts for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted or that are subject to FAR Subpart 31.2 [@CL-CTF-1695; @CL-CTF-9626]. For the full adjustment mechanics see [Pension cost adjustment when a segment is closed](../../mna/diligence/mna-segment-closing-pension-adjustment.md); the measure for contracts not subject to full coverage is stated above [@CL-CTF-1719]. The ownership-change notice is separate and goes to the Administrative Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1696].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.215-15 keeps the rule that the contractor must promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing when it determines that it will terminate a defined-benefit pension plan or otherwise recapture such pension fund assets [@CL-CTF-0576; @CL-CTF-1692]. It keeps the rule that, for segment closings, pension plan terminations, or curtailment of benefits, the amount of the adjustment is as listed, with the CAS pension standard measure for full-coverage contracts and subcontracts [@CL-CTF-0577; @CL-CTF-0578; @CL-CTF-1693; @CL-CTF-1694]. It keeps the measure for contracts and subcontracts not subject to full coverage under the CAS, including the exception on the numerator of the fraction [@CL-CTF-0579; @CL-CTF-0580; @CL-CTF-1719; @CL-CTF-1720]. It keeps the rule that, in all other situations where assets revert to the contractor or are constructively received by it, the contractor makes a refund or gives a credit, at the Government's option for its equitable share of the gross amount withdrawn [@CL-CTF-0581; @CL-CTF-1695], and that the Government's equitable share reflects the Government's participation in pension costs through those contracts for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted or that are subject to FAR subpart 31.2 [@CL-CTF-9627; @CL-CTF-9626]. RFO FAR 52.215-19 keeps the written notice to the Administrative Contracting Officer within 30 days of an ownership change, and the second 30-day notice of asset valuation and other cost changes [@CL-CTF-0582; @CL-CTF-0583; @CL-CTF-0584; @CL-CTF-1723; @CL-CTF-1696; @CL-CTF-1721]. It keeps the duty to maintain current, accurate, and complete inventory records of assets and their costs and to give the Administrative Contracting Officer or designated representative ready access to them on request [@CL-CTF-0585; @CL-CTF-1722].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor decides to recapture 40,000 of pension fund assets in a way that is not a segment closing, plan termination or curtailment. It notifies the Contracting Officer in writing promptly. The Government's equitable share is 25 percent, so 10,000 is refunded or credited, at the Government's option.

Separately, on day 1 the contractor learns that an ownership change is certain to occur and could change its asset valuations. It must notify the Administrative Contracting Officer in writing by day 31.

## Never confuse

The pension notice goes to the Contracting Officer, and the ownership-change notice goes to the Administrative Contracting Officer within 30 days [@CL-CTF-1692; @CL-CTF-1696]. The adjustment for segment closings and plan terminations differs from the refund or credit for other situations where assets revert [@CL-CTF-1693; @CL-CTF-1695].

## Company calibration

Confirm who monitors pension plan decisions and ownership changes, and how any refund or credit to the Government flows through billing and collection. [@Q-009]
