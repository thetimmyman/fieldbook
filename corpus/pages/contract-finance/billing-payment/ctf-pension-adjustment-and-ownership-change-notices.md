## Purpose

Says what a contractor must notify when it ends a pension plan, recaptures pension assets or has an ownership change, when it must notify, and how pension adjustments are made. The segment-closing calculation is on a linked card and is not repeated here.

## The rule

The contractor shall promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing when it determines that it will terminate a defined-benefit pension plan or otherwise recapture such pension fund assets [@CL-CTF-1692]. For segment closings, pension plan terminations, or curtailment of benefits, the amount of the adjustment shall be as listed in the clause [@CL-CTF-1693]: for contracts and subcontracts that are subject to full coverage under the Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) Board rules and regulations (48 CFR Chapter 99), the amount measured, assigned, and allocated in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.413-50(c)(12) [@CL-CTF-1694]; and for contracts and subcontracts that are not subject to full coverage under the CAS, the amount measured, assigned, and allocated in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.413-50(c)(12) [@CL-CTF-1719], except that the numerator of the fraction at 48 CFR 9904.413-50(c)(12)(vi) shall be the sum of the pension plan costs allocated to all non-CAS covered contracts and subcontracts that are subject to Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Subpart 31.2 or for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted [@CL-CTF-1720]. For all other situations where assets revert to the contractor, or such assets are constructively received by it for any reason, the contractor shall, at the Government's option, make a refund or give a credit to the Government for its equitable share of the gross amount withdrawn [@CL-CTF-1695]. The Government's equitable share shall reflect the Government's participation in pension costs through those contracts for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted or that are subject to FAR Subpart 31.2 [@CL-CTF-9626]. For the segment-closing calculation, see [Pension cost adjustment when a segment is closed](../../mna/diligence/mna-segment-closing-pension-adjustment.md).

## How it works

Under the Notification of Ownership Changes clause, the contractor shall make the listed notifications in writing [@CL-CTF-1723]: when the contractor becomes aware that a change in its ownership has occurred, or is certain to occur, that could result in changes in the valuation of its capitalized assets in the accounting records, the contractor shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer (ACO) within 30 days [@CL-CTF-1696]; and the contractor shall also notify the ACO within 30 days whenever changes to asset valuations or any other cost changes have occurred or are certain to occur as a result of a change in ownership [@CL-CTF-1721]. The contractor shall maintain current, accurate, and complete inventory records of assets and their costs and provide the ACO or designated representative ready access to the records upon request [@CL-CTF-1722]. The contractor shall also ensure that all individual and grouped assets, their capitalized values, accumulated depreciation or amortization, and remaining useful lives are identified accurately before and after each of the contractor's ownership changes [@CL-CTF-3054], and retain and continue to maintain depreciation and amortization schedules based on the asset records maintained before each contractor ownership change [@CL-CTF-3055].

DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 52 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-15, the contractor shall promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing when it determines that it will terminate a defined-benefit pension plan or otherwise recapture such pension fund assets [@CL-CTF-0576], and the codified clause states the same rule [@CL-CTF-1692]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-15, for segment closings, pension plan terminations, or curtailment of benefits, the amount of the adjustment shall be, for contracts and subcontracts that are subject to full coverage under the Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) Board rules and regulations (48 CFR Chapter 99), the amount measured, assigned, and allocated in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.413-50(c)(12) [@CL-CTF-0577; @CL-CTF-0578], and for contracts and subcontracts that are not subject to full coverage under the CAS, the amount measured, assigned, and allocated in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.413-50(c)(12), except that the numerator of the fraction at 48 CFR 9904.413-50(c)(12)(vi) shall be the sum of the pension plan costs allocated to all non-CAS covered contracts and subcontracts that are subject to Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) subpart 31.2 or for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted [@CL-CTF-0579; @CL-CTF-0580], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1693; @CL-CTF-1694; @CL-CTF-1719; @CL-CTF-1720]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-15, for all other situations where assets revert to the contractor, or such assets are constructively received by it for any reason, the contractor shall, at the Government's option, make a refund or give a credit to the Government for its equitable share of the gross amount withdrawn [@CL-CTF-0581], and the Government's equitable share shall reflect the Government's participation in pension costs through those contracts for which certified cost or pricing data were submitted or that are subject to FAR subpart 31.2 [@CL-CTF-9627], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1695; @CL-CTF-9626]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-19, the contractor shall make the listed notifications in writing [@CL-CTF-0582]: when the contractor becomes aware that a change in its ownership has occurred, or is certain to occur, that could result in changes in the valuation of its capitalized assets in the accounting records, the contractor shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer (ACO) within 30 days [@CL-CTF-0583], and the contractor shall also notify the ACO within 30 days whenever changes to asset valuations or any other cost changes have occurred or are certain to occur as a result of a change in ownership [@CL-CTF-0584], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1723; @CL-CTF-1696; @CL-CTF-1721]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-19, the contractor shall maintain current, accurate, and complete inventory records of assets and their costs, provide the ACO or designated representative ready access to the records upon request [@CL-CTF-0585], ensure that all individual and grouped assets, their capitalized values, accumulated depreciation or amortization, and remaining useful lives are identified accurately before and after each of the contractor's ownership changes [@CL-CTF-3203], and retain and continue to maintain depreciation and amortization schedules based on the asset records maintained before each contractor ownership change [@CL-CTF-3204], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1722; @CL-CTF-3054; @CL-CTF-3055].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor decides to recapture 40,000 of pension fund assets in a way that is not a segment closing, plan termination or curtailment. It notifies the Contracting Officer in writing promptly. The Government's equitable share is 25 percent, so 10,000 is refunded or credited, at the Government's option.

Separately, on day 1 the contractor learns that an ownership change is certain to occur and could change its asset valuations. It must notify the Administrative Contracting Officer in writing by day 31.

## Never confuse

The contractor must promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing when it determines that it will terminate a defined-benefit pension plan or otherwise recapture such pension fund assets [@CL-CTF-1692], whereas when the contractor becomes aware that a change in its ownership has occurred, or is certain to occur, that could result in changes in the valuation of its capitalized assets in the accounting records, it must notify the Administrative Contracting Officer in writing within 30 days [@CL-CTF-1696; @CL-CTF-1723]. For segment closings, pension plan terminations, or curtailment of benefits, the amount of the adjustment is measured, assigned, and allocated in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.413-50(c)(12) (with the fraction numerator exception for contracts and subcontracts not subject to full CAS coverage) [@CL-CTF-1693; @CL-CTF-1694; @CL-CTF-1719; @CL-CTF-1720], whereas for all other situations where assets revert to the contractor or are constructively received by it for any reason, the contractor must, at the Government's option, make a refund or give a credit to the Government for its equitable share of the gross amount withdrawn [@CL-CTF-1695].

## Company calibration

Confirm who monitors pension plan decisions and ownership changes, and how any refund or credit to the Government flows through billing and collection. [@Q-009]
