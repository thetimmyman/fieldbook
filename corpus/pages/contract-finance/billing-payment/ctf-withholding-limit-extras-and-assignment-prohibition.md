## Purpose

Explains the cap on withholding when several clauses allow it, when extras are paid, and what the prohibition of assignment of claims clause bars.

Under FAR 52.232-9, if more than one clause or Schedule term of the contract authorizes the temporary withholding of amounts otherwise payable to the Contractor for supplies delivered or services performed, the total of the amounts withheld at any one time shall not exceed the greatest amount that may be withheld under any one clause or Schedule term at that time [@CL-CTF-2016; @CL-CTF-2017]. However, this limitation shall not apply to (a) withholdings pursuant to any clause relating to wages or hours of employees, (b) withholdings not specifically provided for by the contract, (c) recovery of overpayments, or (d) any other withholding for which the Contracting Officer determines that this limitation is inappropriate [@CL-CTF-2017; @CL-CTF-2018].

Under FAR 52.232-11, except as otherwise provided in the contract, no payment for extras shall be made unless such extras and the price therefor have been authorized in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2019]. Under FAR 52.232-24, the assignment of claims under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940, as amended (31 USC 3727, 41 USC 6305), is prohibited for the contract [@CL-CTF-2020].

## How it works

When more than one clause or Schedule term of the contract authorizes the temporary withholding of amounts otherwise payable to the Contractor for supplies delivered or services performed, the total of the amounts withheld at any one time shall not exceed the greatest amount that may be withheld under any one clause or Schedule term at that time, except for the four listed categories in paragraphs (a) through (d) [@CL-CTF-2016; @CL-CTF-2017; @CL-CTF-2018]. Except as otherwise provided in the contract, no payment for extras shall be made unless such extras and the price therefor have been authorized in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2019]. Under FAR 32.806(b) and FAR 52.232-24, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-24 in solicitations and contracts for which a determination has been made under agency regulations that the prohibition of assignment of claims is in the Government's interest, and the clause prohibits the assignment of claims under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940, as amended (31 USC 3727, 41 USC 6305), for the contract [@CL-CTF-3380; @CL-CTF-2020].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-9, if more than one clause or Schedule term of the contract authorizes the temporary withholding of amounts otherwise payable to the Contractor for supplies delivered or services performed, the total of the amounts withheld at any one time shall not exceed the greatest amount that may be withheld under any one clause or Schedule term at that time, provided that this limitation shall not apply to (a) withholdings pursuant to any clause relating to wages or hours of employees, (b) withholdings not specifically provided for by the contract, (c) recovery of overpayments, or (d) any other withholding for which the Contracting Officer determines that this limitation is inappropriate [@CL-CTF-0805; @CL-CTF-0806; @CL-CTF-0807]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-11, except as otherwise provided in the contract, no payment for extras shall be made unless such extras and the price therefor have been authorized in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0808]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-24, the assignment of claims under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940, as amended (31 USC 3727, 41 USC 6305), is prohibited for the contract [@CL-CTF-0809].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has two terms that each allow temporary withholding: one up to 5,000 and one up to 8,000. The total withheld at one time cannot exceed 8,000. A separate withholding under a clause on employee wages or hours is outside that limit. A supplier does an extra task for 2,000 without written authorization from the Contracting Officer, so the clause bars payment for it unless the contract provides otherwise.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-9(a)-(d), the limitation on withholding of payments shall not apply to withholdings pursuant to any clause relating to wages or hours of employees, withholdings not specifically provided for by the contract, recovery of overpayments, or any other withholding for which the Contracting Officer determines that the limitation is inappropriate [@CL-CTF-2017; @CL-CTF-2018]. Under FAR 32.111(a)(7), if the contracting officer determines that it is necessary to withhold payment to protect the Government's interests, paragraph (a)(7) of the clause at FAR 52.232-7 permits the contracting officer to unilaterally issue a modification requiring the contractor to withhold 5 percent of amounts due, up to a maximum of $50,000 under the contract, and the amounts withheld shall be retained until the contractor executes and delivers the release required by the clause [@CL-CTF-1566; @CL-CTF-1567], which is on [Contract financing methods and payment clauses for noncommercial purchases](../financing/ctf-financing-methods-and-noncommercial-payment-clauses.md). Ordinary payment for accepted work is on [Payments under fixed-price research and development, personal services and transportation contracts](ctf-payments-rd-personal-services-and-transportation.md), and construction retainage is on [Payments under fixed-price construction contracts and communication service contracts with common carriers](ctf-payments-fixed-price-construction-and-common-carriers.md).

Under FAR 32.803(b), a contract may prohibit the assignment of claims if the agency determines the prohibition to be in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0775]. For how assignment of claims works when it is allowed, see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md), and for the clause on interest and assignment, see [Interest on amounts due the Government and the assignment of claims clause](ctf-interest-and-assignment-of-claims-clauses.md).
