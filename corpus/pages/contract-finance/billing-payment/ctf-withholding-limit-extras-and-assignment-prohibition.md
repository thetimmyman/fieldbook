## Purpose

Explains the cap on withholding when several clauses allow it, when extras are paid, and what the prohibition of assignment of claims clause bars.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2017; @CL-CTF-2019; @CL-CTF-2020] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-9, FAR 52.232-11 and FAR 52.232-24 [@CL-CTF-2017; @CL-CTF-2019; @CL-CTF-2020] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2017; @CL-CTF-2019; @CL-CTF-2020] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause: for the withholding limit, a supply, research and development, service, time-and-materials or labor-hour contract with two or more terms authorizing temporary withholding [@CL-CTF-1564; @CL-CTF-1565]; for extras, a fixed-price supply, fixed-price service or transportation contract [@CL-CTF-1522]; for the prohibition, any contract that includes it [@CL-CTF-2020] |

The withholding limit addresses the case where more than one clause or Schedule term of the contract authorizes the temporary withholding of amounts otherwise payable to the Contractor for supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-2016]. The total withheld at any one time may not exceed the greatest amount that may be withheld under any one clause or Schedule term at that time [@CL-CTF-2017]. The limit does not apply to withholdings under any clause relating to wages or hours of employees, to withholdings the contract does not specifically provide for, to the recovery of overpayments, or to any other withholding for which the Contracting Officer determines the limit is inappropriate [@CL-CTF-2018].

No payment for extras is made unless the extras and their price have been authorized in writing by the Contracting Officer, except as the contract otherwise provides [@CL-CTF-2019]. The assignment of claims under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 is prohibited for a contract that includes the prohibition clause [@CL-CTF-2020].

## How it works

Take the amount that each clause or Schedule term authorizes to be withheld and find the largest single amount [@CL-CTF-2017]. That largest amount is the ceiling for the total withheld at that time, unless the withholding is one of the listed exceptions [@CL-CTF-2017; @CL-CTF-2018]. Unless the contract provides otherwise, an extra is paid only if the extra and its price were authorized in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2019]. A contract with the prohibition clause cannot have its claims assigned under the Act [@CL-CTF-2020].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has two terms that each allow temporary withholding: one up to 5,000 and one up to 8,000. The total withheld at one time cannot exceed 8,000. A separate withholding under a clause on employee wages or hours is outside that limit. A supplier does an extra task for 2,000 without written authorization from the Contracting Officer, so the clause bars payment for it unless the contract provides otherwise.

## Never confuse

The cap is not a limit on all withholding: it does not apply to the listed exceptions [@CL-CTF-2018]. The clause for time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts carries its own withholding rule [@CL-CTF-1566], which is on [Contract financing methods and payment clauses for noncommercial purchases](../financing/ctf-financing-methods-and-noncommercial-payment-clauses.md). Ordinary payment for accepted work is on [Payments under fixed-price research and development, personal services and transportation contracts](ctf-payments-rd-personal-services-and-transportation.md), and construction retainage is on [Payments under fixed-price construction contracts and communication service contracts with common carriers](ctf-payments-fixed-price-construction-and-common-carriers.md).

A contract may prohibit assignment of claims if the agency determines the prohibition to be in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0775]. For how assignment of claims works when it is allowed, see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md), and for the clause on interest and assignment, see [Interest on amounts due the Government and the assignment of claims clause](ctf-interest-and-assignment-of-claims-clauses.md).
