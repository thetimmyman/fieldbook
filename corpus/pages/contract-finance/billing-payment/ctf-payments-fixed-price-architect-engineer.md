## Purpose

Shows how monthly estimates are paid under the fixed-price architect-engineer payments clause, the retention rules, the limit on undefinitized work and the final payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2021; @CL-CTF-2022] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-10 [@CL-CTF-2021; @CL-CTF-2022] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-10 [@CL-CTF-0478] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2021; @CL-CTF-2022] |
| Applies to | Fixed-price architect-engineer contracts [@CL-CTF-1521] |

Estimates of the amount and value of the work and services performed that meet the quality standards of the contract are made monthly [@CL-CTF-2021]. The estimates, with any supporting data the Contracting Officer requires, are prepared by the Contractor and submitted along with its voucher [@CL-CTF-2195]. After receipt of each substantiated voucher, the Government pays the voucher as approved by the Contracting Officer or authorized representative [@CL-CTF-2022].

The Contracting Officer requires a withholding of up to 10 percent of the amounts due only if the Contracting Officer determines the withholding is necessary to protect the Government's interest and ensure satisfactory completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-2023]. The amount withheld is determined based upon the Contractor's performance record under the contract [@CL-CTF-2194]. When the work is substantially complete and the amount retained is more than adequate for the protection of the Government, the Contracting Officer releases the excess to the Contractor [@CL-CTF-2024].

Notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, and specifically the retention paragraph, progress payments shall not exceed 80 percent on work accomplished on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-2127]. A contract action there is any action resulting in a contract, as defined in FAR subpart 2.1, including contract modifications for additional supplies or services, but not including contract modifications within the scope and under the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-2197], such as modifications issued pursuant to the Changes clause, or funding and other administrative changes [@CL-CTF-2198].

Upon satisfactory completion by the Contractor and final acceptance by the Contracting Officer of all the work under the Statement of Architect-Engineer Services, the Contractor is paid the unpaid balance of any money due for the work [@CL-CTF-2025]. That unpaid balance includes all withheld amounts [@CL-CTF-2139]. Before final payment, or before settlement upon termination of the contract, and as a condition precedent, the Contractor executes and delivers to the Contracting Officer a release of all claims against the Government [@CL-CTF-2196]. That release, which covers claims arising under or by virtue of the contract, does not cover claims the Contractor has specifically excepted from the release in amounts stated in the release [@CL-CTF-2026].

## How it works

Each month an estimate of the value of the work and services performed is made [@CL-CTF-2021]. The Government pays each substantiated voucher as approved [@CL-CTF-2022]. Withholding is not routine: it is required only where the Contracting Officer determines it is necessary to protect the Government's interest and ensure satisfactory completion, in an amount based on the Contractor's performance record [@CL-CTF-2023; @CL-CTF-2194]. Any amount retained beyond what is adequate is released once the work is substantially complete, and the unpaid balance, including all withheld amounts, is paid after satisfactory completion and final acceptance [@CL-CTF-2024; @CL-CTF-2025; @CL-CTF-2139].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the rule that estimates of the amount and value of the work and services performed that meet the quality standards of the contract are made monthly [@CL-CTF-0478] [@CL-CTF-2021], prepared by the contractor with any supporting data the contracting officer requires and submitted with its voucher [@CL-CTF-0479] [@CL-CTF-2195]. It keeps the rule that, after receipt of each substantiated voucher, the Government pays the voucher as approved by the contracting officer or authorized representative [@CL-CTF-0480] [@CL-CTF-2022]. It keeps the rule that the contracting officer requires a withholding of up to 10 percent only if the contracting officer determines the withholding is necessary to protect the Government's interest and ensure satisfactory completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-0492] [@CL-CTF-2023], in an amount based upon the contractor's performance record [@CL-CTF-0493] [@CL-CTF-2194], and the release of the excess once the work is substantially complete and the amount retained is more than adequate for the protection of the Government [@CL-CTF-0494] [@CL-CTF-2024]. It keeps the rule that, upon satisfactory completion and final acceptance of all the work under the Statement of Architect-Engineer Services, the contractor is paid the unpaid balance of any money due for work under the statement [@CL-CTF-0495] [@CL-CTF-2025], including all withheld amounts [@CL-CTF-0496] [@CL-CTF-2139]. It keeps the release of claims as a condition precedent to final payment or settlement upon termination [@CL-CTF-0497] [@CL-CTF-2196], other than claims specifically excepted in amounts stated in the release [@CL-CTF-0498] [@CL-CTF-2026]. It keeps the limit that, notwithstanding any other provision in the contract and specifically paragraph (b), progress payments shall not exceed 80 percent on work accomplished on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-0499] [@CL-CTF-2127], and the definition of a contract action as an action resulting in a contract, including modifications for additional supplies or services but not modifications within the scope and under the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-0500] [@CL-CTF-2197], such as modifications issued pursuant to the Changes clause or funding and other administrative changes [@CL-CTF-0501] [@CL-CTF-2198].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A monthly estimate of 60,000 is submitted with a voucher. The Contracting Officer finds withholding necessary and, as an assumed amount within the permitted range, withholds 6,000, which is 10 percent, an amount based on the Contractor's performance record under the contract. Over three months the retained amount reaches 18,000. At substantial completion the Contracting Officer finds 5,000 would be adequate and releases 13,000. After satisfactory completion and final acceptance, the Contractor is paid the unpaid balance, including the 5,000 still withheld.

On a separate modification that adds work beyond the scope and terms of the contract and is not yet definitized, work accomplished is 30,000, so progress payments for that work cannot exceed 24,000.

## Never confuse

Retention here is withholding of up to 10 percent that is required only if the Contracting Officer determines it is necessary [@CL-CTF-2023], whereas the construction clause allows retention of a maximum of 10 percent where satisfactory progress has not been made [@CL-CTF-2010]. See [Payments under fixed-price construction contracts and communication service contracts with common carriers](ctf-payments-fixed-price-construction-and-common-carriers.md). The limit on undefinitized work is not the customary progress payment rate, which is on [Progress payment rates and unusual progress payments](../financing/ctf-progress-payment-rates.md). For prompt payment of architect-engineer contracts, see [Prompt payment for architect-engineer contracts and discounts for prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment-architect-engineer-and-discounts.md), and for settlement when such a contract is terminated, see [Termination of fixed-price architect-engineer and personal services contracts](ctf-termination-architect-engineer-and-personal-services.md). What makes a voucher proper is on [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md).
