## Purpose

Shows how monthly estimates are paid under the fixed-price architect-engineer payments clause, the retention rules, the limit on undefinitized work and the final payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2021; @CL-CTF-2022] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-10 [@CL-CTF-2021; @CL-CTF-2022] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-10 [@CL-CTF-0478] |
| Edition date | Apr 2010 [@CL-CTF-0478] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2021; @CL-CTF-2022] |
| Applies to | Fixed-price architect-engineer contracts, appropriately modified with respect to payment due dates in accordance with agency regulations [@CL-CTF-1521] |

Estimates must be made monthly of the amount and value of the work and services performed by the Contractor under the contract which meet the standards of quality established under the contract [@CL-CTF-2021]. The estimates, along with any supporting data required by the Contracting Officer, must be prepared by the Contractor and submitted along with its voucher [@CL-CTF-2195]. After receipt of each substantiated voucher, the Government must pay the voucher as approved by the Contracting Officer or authorized representative [@CL-CTF-2022].

The Contracting Officer must require a withholding from amounts due under paragraph (a) of the clause of up to 10 percent only if the Contracting Officer determines that such a withholding is necessary to protect the Government's interest and ensure satisfactory completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-2023]. The amount withheld must be determined based upon the Contractor's performance record under the contract [@CL-CTF-2194]. Whenever the Contracting Officer determines that the work is substantially complete and that the amount retained is in excess of the amount adequate for the protection of the Government, the Contracting Officer must release the excess amount to the Contractor [@CL-CTF-2024].

## How it works

Notwithstanding any other provision in the contract, and specifically paragraph (b) of the clause, progress payments must not exceed 80 percent on work accomplished on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-2127]. A contract action is any action resulting in a contract, as defined in FAR subpart 2.1, including contract modifications for additional supplies or services, but not including contract modifications that are within the scope and under the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-2197], such as contract modifications issued pursuant to the Changes clause, or funding and other administrative changes [@CL-CTF-2198].

Upon satisfactory completion by the Contractor and final acceptance by the Contracting Officer of all the work done by the Contractor under the Statement of Architect-Engineer Services, the Contractor will be paid the unpaid balance of any money due for work under the statement [@CL-CTF-2025], including all withheld amounts [@CL-CTF-2139]. Before final payment under the contract, or before settlement upon termination of the contract, and as a condition precedent thereto, the Contractor must execute and deliver to the Contracting Officer a release of all claims against the Government [@CL-CTF-2196] arising under or by virtue of the contract, other than any claims that are specifically excepted by the Contractor from the operation of the release in amounts stated in the release [@CL-CTF-2026].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. RFO FAR 52.232-10 provides that estimates must be made monthly of the amount and value of the work and services performed by the contractor under the contract which meet the standards of quality established under the contract [@CL-CTF-0478], and that the estimates, along with any supporting data required by the contracting officer, must be prepared by the contractor and submitted along with its voucher [@CL-CTF-0479], matching FAR 52.232-10(a) [@CL-CTF-2021; @CL-CTF-2195]. RFO FAR 52.232-10 provides that after receipt of each substantiated voucher, the Government must pay the voucher as approved by the contracting officer or authorized representative [@CL-CTF-0480], that the contracting officer must require a withholding from amounts due under paragraph (a) of the clause of up to 10 percent only if the contracting officer determines that such a withholding is necessary to protect the Government's interest and ensure satisfactory completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-0492], that the amount withheld must be determined based upon the contractor's performance record under the contract [@CL-CTF-0493], and that whenever the contracting officer determines that the work is substantially complete and that the amount retained is in excess of the amount adequate for the protection of the Government, the contracting officer must release the excess amount to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0494], matching FAR 52.232-10(b) [@CL-CTF-2022; @CL-CTF-2023; @CL-CTF-2194; @CL-CTF-2024]. RFO FAR 52.232-10 provides that upon satisfactory completion by the contractor and final acceptance by the contracting officer of all the work done by the contractor under the Statement of Architect-Engineer Services, the contractor will be paid the unpaid balance of any money due for work under the statement [@CL-CTF-0495], including all withheld amounts [@CL-CTF-0496], matching FAR 52.232-10(c) [@CL-CTF-2025; @CL-CTF-2139]. RFO FAR 52.232-10 also provides that before final payment under the contract, or before settlement upon termination of the contract, and as a condition precedent thereto, the contractor must execute and deliver to the contracting officer a release of all claims against the Government [@CL-CTF-0497] arising under or by virtue of the contract, other than any claims that are specifically excepted by the contractor from the operation of the release in amounts stated in the release [@CL-CTF-0498], matching FAR 52.232-10(d) [@CL-CTF-2196; @CL-CTF-2026]. RFO FAR 52.232-10 further provides that notwithstanding any other provision in the contract, and specifically paragraph (b) of the clause, progress payments must not exceed 80 percent on work accomplished on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-0499], and defines a contract action as any action resulting in a contract, as defined in FAR subpart 2.1, including contract modifications for additional supplies or services, but not including contract modifications that are within the scope and under the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-0500], such as contract modifications issued pursuant to the Changes clause, or funding and other administrative changes [@CL-CTF-0501], matching FAR 52.232-10(e) [@CL-CTF-2127; @CL-CTF-2197; @CL-CTF-2198].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A monthly estimate of 60,000 is submitted with a voucher. The Contracting Officer finds withholding necessary and, as an assumed amount within the permitted range, withholds 6,000, which is 10 percent, an amount based on the Contractor's performance record under the contract. Over three months the retained amount reaches 18,000. At substantial completion the Contracting Officer finds 5,000 would be adequate and releases 13,000. After satisfactory completion and final acceptance, the Contractor is paid the unpaid balance, including the 5,000 still withheld.

On a separate modification that adds work beyond the scope and terms of the contract and is not yet definitized, work accomplished is 30,000, so progress payments for that work cannot exceed 24,000.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-10, the Contracting Officer must require a withholding from amounts due under paragraph (a) of the clause of up to 10 percent only if the Contracting Officer determines that such a withholding is necessary to protect the Government's interest and ensure satisfactory completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-2023], whereas under the construction clause, if satisfactory progress has not been made, the Contracting Officer may retain a maximum of 10 percent of the amount of the payment until satisfactory progress is achieved [@CL-CTF-2010]. See [Payments under fixed-price construction contracts and communication service contracts with common carriers](ctf-payments-fixed-price-construction-and-common-carriers.md). For customary progress payment rates and unusual progress payments, see [Progress payment rates and unusual progress payments](../financing/ctf-progress-payment-rates.md). For prompt payment of architect-engineer contracts, see [Prompt payment for architect-engineer contracts and discounts for prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment-architect-engineer-and-discounts.md), and for settlement when such a contract is terminated, see [Termination of fixed-price architect-engineer and personal services contracts](ctf-termination-architect-engineer-and-personal-services.md). For proper invoice content and payment documentation, see [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md).
