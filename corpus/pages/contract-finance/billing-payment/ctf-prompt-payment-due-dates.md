## Purpose

Names the periods that fix an invoice payment due date and what starts each one, so the cash forecast can place a receipt. This card covers the general invoice due date, the cost-reimbursement interim rule, construction progress payments and retained amounts, and mixed invoices only. The Prompt Payment clause itself is on the linked card.

## The rule

Except as prescribed in paragraphs (c) through (f) of FAR 32.904, or as authorized in FAR 32.908(a)(2) or (c)(2), the due date for making an invoice payment is the later of two listed events [@CL-CTF-1337]: the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (b)(3) of the section), and the 30th day after Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-1338]. Under FAR 2.101, proper invoice means an invoice that meets the minimum standards specified in FAR 32.905(b) [@CL-CTF-9421]. As provided in FAR 32.903, agency policies and procedures may authorize amendment of paragraphs (a)(1)(i) and (ii) of the clause to insert a period shorter than 30 days (but not less than 7 days) for making contract invoice payments [@CL-CTF-1391].

For purposes of computing late payment interest penalties that may apply, the due date for making interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services is 30 days after the date of receipt of a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-1341].

For payments on construction contracts [@CL-CTF-1394], the due date for making progress payments based on contracting officer approval of the estimated amount and value of work or services performed, including payments for reaching milestones in any project, is 14 days after the designated billing office receives a proper payment request [@CL-CTF-1382], and the due date for payment of any amounts retained by the contracting officer in accordance with the clause at 52.232-5, Payments Under Fixed-Price Construction Contracts, will be as specified in the contract or, if not specified, 30 days after approval by the contracting officer for release to the contractor [@CL-CTF-1383].

When an invoice contains items with different payment due dates (i.e., a mixed invoice), the payment office will, subject to agency policy, either pay the entire invoice on the earliest due date or split invoice payments, making payments by the applicable due dates [@CL-CTF-1384].

## How it works

For the sole purpose of computing an interest penalty that might be due the contractor [@CL-CTF-1339], Government acceptance is deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after the contractor delivers supplies or performs services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the contract, unless there is a disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with a contract requirement [@CL-CTF-1340]. If actual acceptance occurs within the constructive acceptance period, the Government must base the determination of an interest penalty on the actual date of acceptance [@CL-CTF-1392]. For the Prompt Payment clause, see [Prompt payment for invoices](ctf-prompt-payment.md); for what makes an invoice proper, see [Proper invoice content](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md); and for when an interest penalty is owed, see [Prompt payment interest and clauses](ctf-prompt-payment-interest-and-clauses.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 32 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 2.101, proper invoice means an invoice that meets the minimum standards specified in FAR 32.905(b) [@CL-CTF-9452], matching the codified definition [@CL-CTF-9421]. Under RFO FAR 32.904(b), except as prescribed in paragraphs (c) through (f) of that section, or as authorized in 32.908(a)(2) or (c)(2), the due date for making an invoice payment is the later of two events [@CL-CTF-4062]: the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (b)(3) of the section), and the 30th day after Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-4063], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1337; @CL-CTF-1338]. Under RFO FAR 32.904(b)(1)(ii)(B), for the sole purpose of computing an interest penalty that might be due the contractor, Government acceptance is deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after the contractor delivers supplies or performs services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the contract, unless there is a disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with a contract requirement [@CL-CTF-4064; @CL-CTF-4065], and if actual acceptance occurs within the constructive acceptance period, the Government must base the determination of an interest penalty on the actual date of acceptance [@CL-CTF-4066], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1339; @CL-CTF-1340; @CL-CTF-1392]. Under RFO FAR 32.904(d)(1), for payments on construction contracts [@CL-CTF-4657], the due date for making progress payments based on contracting officer approval of the estimated amount and value of work or services performed, including payments for reaching milestones in any project, is 14 days after the designated billing office receives a proper payment request [@CL-CTF-4067], and the due date for payment of any amounts retained by the contracting officer in accordance with the clause at 52.232-5, Payments Under Fixed-Price Construction Contracts, will be as specified in the contract or, if not specified, 30 days after approval by the contracting officer for release [@CL-CTF-4068], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1394; @CL-CTF-1382; @CL-CTF-1383]. Under RFO FAR 32.904(e), for purposes of computing late payment interest penalties that may apply, the due date for making interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services is 30 days after the date of receipt of a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-4069], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1341]. Under RFO FAR 32.904(g), when an invoice contains items with different payment due dates (i.e., a mixed invoice), the payment office will, subject to agency policy, either pay the entire invoice on the earliest due date or split invoice payments, making payments by the applicable due dates [@CL-CTF-4070], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1384]. Under RFO FAR 32.908(c)(2), as provided in 32.903, agency policies and procedures may authorize amendment of paragraphs (a)(1)(i) and (ii) of the clause to insert a period shorter than 30 days (but not less than 7 days) for making contract invoice payments [@CL-CTF-4071], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1391].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers supplies on day 1, and a proper invoice is received on day 2. The Government has not yet accepted the supplies. For interest purposes only, acceptance is deemed on day 8.

Answer: the later of day 32 and day 38 is day 38, so a payment made after day 38 is late for interest purposes. If the Government disagreed with the contractor on quantity, the deemed acceptance on day 8 would not apply. If the Government actually accepted on day 5, the actual date would be used instead.

## Never confuse

Constructive Government acceptance on the 7th day after delivery or performance applies for the sole purpose of computing an interest penalty that might be due the contractor (unless there is a disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with a contract requirement), and if actual acceptance occurs within the constructive acceptance period, the Government must base the determination of an interest penalty on the actual date of acceptance [@CL-CTF-1339; @CL-CTF-1340; @CL-CTF-1392]. Under FAR 32.904(b), the general invoice payment due date is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (b)(3)) and the 30th day after Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-1337; @CL-CTF-1338], whereas for purposes of computing late payment interest penalties that may apply, the due date for making interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services is 30 days after the date of receipt of a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-1341].
