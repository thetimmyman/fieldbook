## Purpose

Shows how the two short-form clauses let the contracting officer end a fixed-price contract for the Government's convenience, what the contractor recovers, which contracts get which form, and what these short forms leave to other rules.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2051] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.249-1 and FAR 52.249-4 [@CL-CTF-2051; @CL-CTF-2056] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.249-1 and RFO FAR 52.249-4 [@CL-CTF-0736; @CL-CTF-0739] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2051] |
| Applies to | The fixed-price short form goes into solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contract amount is not expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, except in the listed cases [@CL-CLS-1014]. The services short form goes into solicitations and contracts for services, regardless of value, when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contracting officer makes the determination described in the prescription [@CL-CTF-2053; @CL-CLS-1019] |

Under the fixed-price short form, the contracting officer, by written notice, may terminate the contract in whole or in part when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-2051]. The rights, duties and obligations of the parties, including compensation to the contractor, are then in accordance with part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-2052].

The services short form gives the same power of written notice, in whole or in part, when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-2056]. If a contract with that form is terminated, the Government is liable only for payment, under the payment provisions of the contract, for services rendered before the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-2057].

## How it works

The fixed-price short form does not set out the settlement itself. It sends compensation to part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation as in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-2052]. The settlement mechanics are on [Termination for Convenience of the Government (Fixed-Price): settlement](ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md) and [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md).

The services short form is used when the contracting officer determines that, because of the kind of services required, the successful offeror will not incur substantial charges in preparation for and in carrying out the contract, and that it would, if terminated for the convenience of the Government, limit termination settlement charges to services rendered before the date of termination [@CL-CTF-2054; @CL-CTF-2055].

The prescription for the fixed-price short form excepts cases where the services short form is appropriate and research and development work with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-profit basis [@CL-CLS-1051]. Those institutions have their own clause, on [Termination for convenience: educational and other nonprofit institutions](ctf-termination-for-convenience-educational-nonprofit.md).

If the contract is for dismantling, demolition or removal of improvements, the contracting officer uses the fixed-price short form with its Alternate I [@CL-CLS-1053]. Alternate I adds a paragraph under which, on receipt of the termination notice, title to property vested in the contractor revests in the Government regardless of any other clause of the contract, except for property the contractor disposed of by bona fide sale or removed from the site [@CL-CTF-2124]. Above the simplified acquisition threshold, that work has a long-form clause, on [Termination for convenience: dismantling, demolition or removal of improvements](ctf-termination-for-convenience-dismantling-demolition-removal.md) [@CL-CLS-1018].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because DoD's class deviation directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.249-1 keeps the power of the contracting officer, by written notice, to terminate in whole or in part when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0736; @CL-CTF-2051], and keeps the rule that compensation follows part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-0737; @CL-CTF-2052]. Its Alternate I keeps the added paragraph under which title revests in the Government, except for property disposed of by bona fide sale or removed from the site [@CL-CTF-0738; @CL-CTF-2124]. RFO FAR 52.249-4 keeps the same written-notice power [@CL-CTF-0739; @CL-CTF-2056] and keeps the limit that the Government is liable only for payment under the payment provisions for services rendered before the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-0740; @CL-CTF-2057].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price services contract with the services short form pays 10,000 a month. The contracting officer gives written notice, and the termination takes effect at the end of month four. The Government owes payment under the payment provisions for the four months of services rendered, which is 40,000. The contractor's start-up spending is not a separate recovery under this form, because the Government is liable only for services rendered.

A second contract, for removing a small structure, carries the fixed-price short form with Alternate I. After the notice, scrap that the contractor already sold in a bona fide sale stays with the contractor, and any salvage still on the site whose title was vested in the contractor revests in the Government.

## Never confuse

The services short form limits payment to services rendered before the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-2057], while the fixed-price short form leaves compensation to part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CTF-2052]. Neither is the long-form fixed-price clause. The long-form deadlines and settlement caps are on [Termination for Convenience of the Government (Fixed-Price): settlement](ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md), and cost-reimbursement contracts are on [Settlement under the termination clause for cost-reimbursement contracts](ctf-termination-cost-reimbursement-settlement.md). The clause set by contract type is on [Termination for convenience clauses and the contracts they go in](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md).
