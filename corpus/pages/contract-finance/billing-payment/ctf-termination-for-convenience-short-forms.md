## Purpose

Shows how the two short-form clauses let the contracting officer end a fixed-price contract for the Government's convenience, what the contractor recovers, which contracts get which form, and what these short forms leave to other rules.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2051] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.249-1 and FAR 52.249-4 [@CL-CTF-2051; @CL-CTF-2056] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.249-1 and RFO FAR 52.249-4 [@CL-CTF-0736; @CL-CTF-0739] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.249-1: Apr 1984 [@CL-CTF-0736]; FAR 52.249-4: Apr 1984 [@CL-CTF-0739] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2051] |
| Applies to | The fixed-price short form goes into solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contract amount is not expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, except in one of the listed alternative cases [@CL-CLS-1014; @CL-CLS-1051; @CL-CLS-1052]. The services short form goes into solicitations and contracts for services, regardless of value, when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contracting officer determines that, because of the kind of services required, the successful offeror will not incur substantial charges in preparation for and in carrying out the contract, and would, if terminated for the convenience of the Government, limit termination settlement charges to services rendered before the date of termination [@CL-CTF-1494; @CL-CTF-2053; @CL-CTF-2054; @CL-CTF-2055; @CL-CLS-1019; @CL-CLS-1020; @CL-CLS-1073] |

Under the fixed-price short form, the contracting officer, by written notice, may terminate the contract in whole or in part when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-2051]. If the contract is terminated, the rights, duties, and obligations of the parties, including compensation to the contractor, shall be in accordance with part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-2052].

Under the services short form, the contracting officer, by written notice, may terminate the contract in whole or in part when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-2056]. If the contract is terminated, the Government shall be liable only for payment under the payment provisions of the contract for services rendered before the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-2057].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.249-1, if the contract is terminated, the rights, duties, and obligations of the parties, including compensation to the contractor, shall be in accordance with part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-2052]. The settlement mechanics are on [Termination for Convenience of the Government (Fixed-Price): settlement](ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md) and [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md).

Under FAR 49.502(c) and the introductory text of FAR 52.249-4, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.249-4 in solicitations and contracts for services, regardless of value, when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contracting officer determines that, because of the kind of services required, the successful offeror will not incur substantial charges in preparation for and in carrying out the contract, and would, if terminated for the convenience of the Government, limit termination settlement charges to services rendered before the date of termination [@CL-CTF-1494; @CL-CTF-2053; @CL-CTF-2054; @CL-CTF-2055; @CL-CLS-1019; @CL-CLS-1020; @CL-CLS-1073].

Under FAR 49.502(a)(1), the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.249-1 in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contract amount is not expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, except (i) if use of the clause at FAR 52.249-4 is appropriate, (ii) in contracts for research and development work with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-profit basis [@CL-CLS-1014; @CL-CLS-1051], (iii) in contracts for architect-engineer services, or (iv) if one of the clauses prescribed or cited at FAR 49.505(a) or (c) is appropriate [@CL-CLS-1052]. Those institutions have their own clause, on [Termination for convenience: educational and other nonprofit institutions](ctf-termination-for-convenience-educational-nonprofit.md).

Under FAR 49.502(a)(2), if the contract is for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements, the contracting officer shall use the clause at FAR 52.249-1 with its Alternate I [@CL-CLS-1053]. Under Alternate I to FAR 52.249-1, if the contract is for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements, the basic clause is designated as paragraph (a) and paragraph (b) is added, under which upon receipt of the termination notice, if title to property is vested in the contractor under the contract, it shall revest in the Government regardless of any other clause of the contract, except for property that the contractor (a) disposed of by bona fide sale or (b) removed from the site [@CL-CTF-1495; @CL-CTF-2124]. When a fixed-price contract for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.249-3, on [Termination for convenience: dismantling, demolition or removal of improvements](ctf-termination-for-convenience-dismantling-demolition-removal.md) [@CL-CLS-1017; @CL-CLS-1018].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-1, the contracting officer, by written notice, may terminate the contract, in whole or in part, when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0736], and if the contract is terminated, the rights, duties, and obligations of the parties, including compensation to the contractor, shall be in accordance with part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-0737]. Under Alternate I to RFO FAR 52.249-1, if the contract is for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements, the basic clause is designated as paragraph (a) and paragraph (b) is added, under which upon receipt of the termination notice, if title to property is vested in the contractor under the contract, it shall revest in the Government regardless of any other clause of the contract, except for property that the contractor (a) disposed of by bona fide sale or (b) removed from the site [@CL-CTF-1496; @CL-CTF-0738]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-4, the contracting officer, by written notice, may terminate the contract, in whole or in part, when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0739], and if the contract is terminated, the Government shall be liable only for payment under the payment provisions of the contract for services rendered before the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-0740].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price services contract with the services short form pays 10,000 a month. The contracting officer gives written notice, and the termination takes effect at the end of month four. The Government owes payment under the payment provisions for the four months of services rendered, which is 40,000. The contractor's start-up spending is not a separate recovery under this form, because the Government is liable only for services rendered.

A second contract, for removing a small structure, carries the fixed-price short form with Alternate I. After the notice, scrap that the contractor already sold in a bona fide sale stays with the contractor, and any salvage still on the site whose title was vested in the contractor revests in the Government.

## Never confuse

The two short forms differ in what the Government owes on termination rather than in name: under FAR 52.249-4, if the contract is terminated, the Government shall be liable only for payment under the payment provisions of the contract for services rendered before the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-2057], whereas under FAR 52.249-1, if the contract is terminated, the rights, duties, and obligations of the parties, including compensation to the contractor, shall be in accordance with part 49 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-2052]. The long-form deadlines and settlement caps are on [Termination for Convenience of the Government (Fixed-Price): settlement](ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md), and cost-reimbursement contracts are on [Settlement under the termination clause for cost-reimbursement contracts](ctf-termination-cost-reimbursement-settlement.md). The clause set by contract type is on [Termination for convenience clauses and the contracts they go in](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md).
