## Purpose

Shows how the payments clause for time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts sets what a voucher may bill, when payment is due, and how the ceiling price limits the Government's obligation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0401] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-7 [@CL-CTF-0401] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-7 [@CL-CTF-0741] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0401] |
| Applies to | Time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts that include the payments clause [@CL-CTF-0401] |

The amounts for hourly-rate labor shall be computed by multiplying the appropriate hourly rates prescribed in the Schedule by the number of direct labor hours performed [@CL-CTF-0401]. The hourly rates shall include wages, indirect costs, general and administrative expense, and profit, and fractional parts of an hour shall be payable on a prorated basis [@CL-CTF-0402].

The hourly rates shall be paid for all labor performed on the contract that meets the labor qualifications specified in the contract [@CL-CTF-2207], and labor hours incurred to perform tasks for which labor qualifications were specified in the contract will not be paid to the extent the work is performed by employees that do not meet the qualifications specified in the contract, unless specifically authorized by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0403]. Unless otherwise prescribed in the Schedule, the contracting officer may unilaterally issue a contract modification requiring the contractor to withhold amounts from its billings until a reserve is set aside in an amount that the contracting officer considers necessary to protect the Government's interests [@CL-CTF-0404; @CL-CTF-9574]. The contracting officer may require a withhold of 5 percent of the amounts due under paragraph (a) of the clause, but the total amount withheld for the contract shall not exceed $50,000 [@CL-CTF-0405; @CL-CTF-0406]. The amounts withheld shall be retained until the contractor executes and delivers the release required by paragraph (g) of the clause [@CL-CTF-0605].

The Government will not be obligated to pay the contractor any amount in excess of the ceiling price in the Schedule, and the contractor shall not be obligated to continue performance if to do so would exceed the ceiling price set forth in the Schedule, unless and until the contracting officer notifies the contractor in writing that the ceiling price has been increased and specifies in the notice a revised ceiling that shall constitute the ceiling price for performance under the contract [@CL-CTF-2208; @CL-CTF-0407; @CL-CTF-2209].

## How it works

The hourly rates shall include wages, indirect costs, general and administrative expense, and profit, and fractional parts of an hour shall be payable on a prorated basis [@CL-CTF-0402]. Payment for materials is subject to the Allowable Cost and Payment clause of the contract, and the contracting officer will determine allowable costs of materials in accordance with FAR subpart 31.2 in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-0607]. Except as provided for in FAR 31.205-26(e) and (f), the Government will not pay profit or fee to the prime contractor on materials [@CL-CTF-0608].

Unless otherwise prescribed in the Schedule, the contracting officer may unilaterally issue a contract modification requiring the contractor to withhold amounts from its billings until a reserve is set aside in an amount that the contracting officer considers necessary to protect the Government's interests, and may require a withhold of 5 percent of the amounts due under paragraph (a) of the clause, but the total amount withheld for the contract shall not exceed $50,000 [@CL-CTF-0404; @CL-CTF-9574; @CL-CTF-0405; @CL-CTF-0406].

The Government will not be obligated to pay the contractor any amount in excess of the ceiling price in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-2208]. If at any time the contractor has reason to believe that the hourly rate payments and material costs that will accrue in performing the contract in the next succeeding 30 days, if added to all other payments and costs previously accrued, will exceed 85 percent of the ceiling price in the Schedule, the contractor shall notify the contracting officer giving a revised estimate of the total price to the Government for performing the contract with supporting reasons and documentation [@CL-CTF-0606; @CL-CTF-0408].

The completion voucher, and supporting documentation, shall be submitted by the contractor as promptly as practicable following completion of the work under the contract, but in no event later than 120 days (or such longer period as the contracting officer may approve in writing) from the date of completion [@CL-CTF-2210; @CL-CTF-0409]. On contracts for other than services, interim payments made prior to the final payment under the contract are contract financing payments, and contract financing payments are not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-0410]. On contracts for services, for interim payments made prior to the final payment under the contract, the Government will make payment in accordance with the Prompt Payment Act (31 USC 3903) and prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-0411].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A labor-hour task is priced at 100 per hour against a Schedule ceiling of 400,000. The team charges 3,000 direct hours, all worked by employees who meet the specified qualifications, so billed labor is 300,000. Projected accruals in the next 30 days would push total payments and costs to 350,000, which is more than 85 percent of the ceiling (340,000), so the contractor sends the contracting officer a revised estimate. Assume the Schedule does not prescribe otherwise and the contracting officer requires the reserve: the contracting officer withholds 5 percent of the 300,000 of hourly-rate amounts, or 15,000, which is under the 50,000 cap.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-7(a)(4), the hourly rates shall include wages, indirect costs, general and administrative expense, and profit, and fractional parts of an hour shall be payable on a prorated basis [@CL-CTF-0402]. Under FAR 52.232-7(b)(4) and (7), payment for materials is subject to the Allowable Cost and Payment clause of the contract, the contracting officer will determine allowable costs of materials in accordance with FAR subpart 31.2 in effect on the date of the contract, and except as provided for in FAR 31.205-26(e) and (f), the Government will not pay profit or fee to the prime contractor on materials [@CL-CTF-0607; @CL-CTF-0608].

Under FAR 52.232-7(h)(1) and (i), on contracts for other than services, interim payments made prior to the final payment under the contract are contract financing payments and are not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-0410], whereas on contracts for services, for interim payments made prior to the final payment under the contract, the Government will make payment in accordance with the Prompt Payment Act (31 USC 3903) and prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-0411].
