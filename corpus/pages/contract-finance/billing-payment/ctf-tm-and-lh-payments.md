## Purpose

Shows how the payments clause for time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts sets what a voucher may bill, when payment is due, and how the ceiling price limits the Government's obligation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0401] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-7 [@CL-CTF-0401] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-7 [@CL-CTF-0741] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0401] |
| Applies to | Time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts that include the payments clause [@CL-CTF-0401] |

Labor is paid at the hourly rates in the Schedule times the direct labor hours performed, and those rates carry wages, indirect costs, general and administrative expense, and profit [@CL-CTF-0401; @CL-CTF-0402].

Work by employees who do not meet a specified labor qualification is not paid to the extent it is performed by them, unless the contracting officer specifically authorizes it [@CL-CTF-0403]. Unless the Schedule prescribes otherwise, the contracting officer may, by unilateral contract modification, require the contractor to withhold amounts from its billings until a reserve is set aside [@CL-CTF-0404]. The withhold may be 5 percent of the amounts due for hourly-rate labor, and the total withheld for the contract may not exceed $50,000 [@CL-CTF-0405; @CL-CTF-0406]. The amounts withheld are retained until the contractor executes and delivers the release required for final payment [@CL-CTF-0605].

The contractor is not obligated to continue performance if doing so would exceed the ceiling price in the Schedule, unless and until the contracting officer notifies it in writing that the ceiling price has been increased and specifies a revised ceiling [@CL-CTF-0407].

## How it works

The clause divides the bill into hourly-rate labor and materials. The hourly rate carries wages, indirect costs, general and administrative expense, and profit [@CL-CTF-0402]. Materials are paid separately, and payment for them is subject to the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, with allowable costs determined under FAR subpart 31.2 in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-0607]. Except as provided in FAR 31.205-26(e) and (f), the Government will not pay profit or fee to the prime contractor on materials [@CL-CTF-0608].

Unless the Schedule prescribes otherwise, the contracting officer can, by unilateral modification, require a reserve through a withhold of 5 percent of the hourly-rate amounts due, up to $50,000 in total for the contract [@CL-CTF-0404; @CL-CTF-0405; @CL-CTF-0406].

The ceiling price caps the Government's obligation. If the contractor has reason to believe that hourly rate payments and material costs accruing in the next succeeding 30 days, added to all other payments and costs previously accrued, will exceed 85 percent of the ceiling price in the Schedule, it must notify the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0606]. The notice gives a revised estimate of the total price to the Government with supporting reasons and documentation [@CL-CTF-0408].

Payment after completion runs through a completion voucher [@CL-CTF-0409]. On contracts for other than services, interim payments made before final payment are contract financing payments, which are not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-0410]. On contracts for services, interim payments are made in accordance with the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-0411].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because DoD's class deviation directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-7 keeps the labor computation from hourly rates in the Schedule and direct labor hours performed [@CL-CTF-0741; @CL-CTF-0401], the contents of the hourly rates [@CL-CTF-0743; @CL-CTF-0402] and the rule that work by employees who do not meet specified qualifications is not paid unless the contracting officer specifically authorizes it [@CL-CTF-0742; @CL-CTF-0403]. It keeps the unilateral-modification reserve, unless the Schedule prescribes otherwise [@CL-CTF-0744; @CL-CTF-9142; @CL-CTF-0404], the 5 percent withhold with its $50,000 total limit [@CL-CTF-0745; @CL-CTF-0405; @CL-CTF-0406] and the retention of withheld amounts until the release is executed and delivered [@CL-CTF-0746; @CL-CTF-0605]. It keeps the rules that payment for materials is subject to the Allowable Cost and Payment clause with allowable costs determined under FAR subpart 31.2 [@CL-CTF-0747; @CL-CTF-0607] and that the Government will not pay profit or fee to the prime contractor on materials, except as provided in FAR 31.205-26(e) and (f) [@CL-CTF-0748; @CL-CTF-0608]. It keeps the 85 percent notice trigger [@CL-CTF-0749; @CL-CTF-0606] and the revised estimate that the notice must give [@CL-CTF-0784; @CL-CTF-0408], the ceiling-price limit on the duty to continue performance [@CL-CTF-0785; @CL-CTF-0407], the 120-day completion voucher deadline [@CL-CTF-0786; @CL-CTF-0409], and the treatment of interim payments on contracts for other than services [@CL-CTF-0787; @CL-CTF-0410] and for services [@CL-CTF-0788; @CL-CTF-0411].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A labor-hour task is priced at 100 per hour against a Schedule ceiling of 400,000. The team charges 3,000 direct hours, all worked by employees who meet the specified qualifications, so billed labor is 300,000. Projected accruals in the next 30 days would push total payments and costs to 350,000, which is more than 85 percent of the ceiling (340,000), so the contractor sends the contracting officer a revised estimate. Assume the Schedule does not prescribe otherwise and the contracting officer requires the reserve: the contracting officer withholds 5 percent of the 300,000 of hourly-rate amounts, or 15,000, which is under the 50,000 cap.

## Never confuse

In a time-and-materials or labor-hour contract the hourly rate carries wages, indirect costs, general and administrative expense, and profit [@CL-CTF-0402]. Materials are paid separately at allowable cost, and the prime contractor receives no profit or fee on them except where the clause provides [@CL-CTF-0607; @CL-CTF-0608].

Interim payments on contracts for other than services are contract financing payments with no prompt payment interest penalty, while interim payments on contracts for services follow the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-0410; @CL-CTF-0411].

Rates for each labor category come from the Schedule; see [Billing rates](ctf-billing-rates.md).
