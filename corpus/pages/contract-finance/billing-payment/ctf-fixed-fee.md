## Purpose

Explains payment of fixed fee on cost-reimbursement contracts, withholding of fee reserves, and conditions for releasing fee withholds.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0263] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-8 [@CL-CTF-0263] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0263] |
| Applies to | Cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts other than facilities or construction [@CL-CTF-0263] |

The Government pays the fixed fee specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-0532]. Payment is made as specified in the Schedule, provided the contracting officer withholds a reserve not to exceed 15 percent of the total fixed fee or $100,000, whichever is less, to protect the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0264]. After receipt of an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal covering the year of physical completion, the contracting officer releases 75 percent of all fee withholds, provided the contractor has satisfied the other conditions of the clause [@CL-CTF-0265; @CL-CTF-0281]. The contracting officer may release up to 90 percent of the fee withholds based on the contractor's past performance [@CL-CTF-0266].

## How it works

Fixed fee is paid as specified in the Schedule, but the contracting officer withholds a reserve [@CL-CTF-0264]. To obtain release of 75 percent of withholds, the contractor must submit an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal covering the year of physical completion, have satisfied all other contract terms and conditions, including the final patent and royalty reports, and not be delinquent in submitting final vouchers on prior years' settlements [@CL-CTF-0265; @CL-CTF-0281]. A release of up to 90 percent is at the contracting officer's option, based on the contractor's past performance related to the submission and settlement of final indirect cost rate proposals [@CL-CTF-0266].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-fixed-fee contract specifies a fixed fee of 200,000. During contract performance, the contracting officer withholds 15 percent of fee billings, reaching a reserve withhold of 30,000 (which is less than 100,000). Following physical completion, the contractor submits an adequate certified final indirect rate proposal covering the year of physical completion along with the final patent and royalty reports, has satisfied all other contract terms and conditions, and is not delinquent in submitting final vouchers on prior years' settlements. The contracting officer releases 75 percent of the reserve, or 22,500, leaving 7,500 withheld, and may release more based on past performance, up to 90 percent of the withholds (27,000) in total.

## Never confuse

A fixed fee withhold is a reserve held to protect the Government's interest and released in part after an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal is received [@CL-CTF-0264; @CL-CTF-0265], not a disallowance of costs or a business system penalty withhold. See [Contract types and what they mean for finance](ctf-contract-types.md) and [Final indirect cost rate proposal deadline](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).
