## Purpose

Shows the contractor's performance duty, the guarantee on repaired work, and how over and above work is identified, approved and priced.

## The rule

Upon the award of a job order, the contractor shall promptly start the work specified and diligently prosecute the work to completion [@CL-CTF-1967]. The contractor may not start work until the job order has been awarded, except for emergency work ordered by the Contracting Officer under the Job Orders and Compensation clause of the Master Agreement [@CL-CTF-2372]. If any work performed or materials furnished by the contractor under the Master Agreement prove defective or deficient within 90 days from the date of redelivery of the vessel, the contractor, as directed by the Contracting Officer and at its own expense, shall correct and repair the deficiency to the satisfaction of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1968; @CL-CTF-2178]. If the contractor or any subcontractor has a guarantee that exceeds the 90 day period, the Government is entitled to rely upon the longer guarantee until its expiration [@CL-CTF-1995]. For any individual work item identified as incomplete when the vessel is redelivered, the guarantee period shall run from the date the item is completed [@CL-CTF-2153].

Over and above work is work discovered during overhaul, maintenance and repair efforts that is within the general scope of the contract, not covered by the line items for the basic work, and necessary in order to satisfactorily complete the contract [@CL-CTF-1969; @CL-CTF-2172].

Follow the procedures at PGI 217.7701 when acquiring over and above work [@CL-CTF-9281]. Use the clause at 252.217-7028, Over and Above Work, in solicitations and contracts containing requirements for over and above work, except as provided for in subpart 217.71 [@CL-CTF-9282].

## How it works

### Performance

The Government delivers the vessel at the time and location the job order specifies and, on completion of the work, accepts delivery at that time and location [@CL-CTF-2373]. The contractor shall, without charge and without specific requirement in a job order, do all of the following [@CL-CTF-2180]. It makes available at the plant to personnel of the vessel while in dry dock or on a marine railway sanitary lavatory and similar facilities acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2180]. It supplies and maintains suitable brows and gangways from the pier, dry dock or marine railway to the vessel [@CL-CTF-2374]. It treats salvage, scrap or other ship's material of the Government resulting from performance of the work as items of Government-furnished property, in accordance with the Government Property clause [@CL-CTF-2375]. It performs, or pays the cost of, any repair, reconditioning or replacement made necessary as the result of the contractor's use of any of the vessel's machinery, equipment or fittings, including, but not limited to, winches, pumps, rigging or pipe lines [@CL-CTF-2181]. It furnishes suitable offices, office equipment and telephones at or near the site of the work for the Government's use [@CL-CTF-2376]. The job order will state whether dock and sea trials are required to determine whether or not the contractor has satisfactorily performed the work [@CL-CTF-2418]. If dock and sea trials are required, the vessel shall be under the control of the vessel's commander and crew [@CL-CTF-2370]. The contractor shall provide and install all fittings and appliances necessary for dock and sea trials, and shall be responsible for care, installation and removal of instruments and apparatus furnished by the Government for use in the trials [@CL-CTF-2371]. The contractor shall not conduct dock and sea trials not specified in the job order without advance approval of the Contracting Officer, and dock and sea trials not specified in the job order shall be at the contractor's expense and risk [@CL-CTF-2182].

### Guarantee

If practicable, the Government shall give the contractor an opportunity to correct the deficiency [@CL-CTF-2179]. If the Contracting Officer determines it is not practicable or otherwise not advisable to return the vessel to the contractor, or the contractor fails to proceed with the repairs promptly, the Contracting Officer may direct that the repairs be performed elsewhere, at the contractor's expense [@CL-CTF-1996]. If correction and repairs are performed by someone other than the contractor, the Contracting Officer may discharge the contractor's liability by making an equitable deduction in the price of the job order [@CL-CTF-1997]. The contractor's liability shall extend for an additional 90 day guarantee period on those defects or deficiencies that the contractor corrected [@CL-CTF-1998]. At the option of the Contracting Officer, defects and deficiencies may be left uncorrected; in that event, the contractor and the Contracting Officer shall negotiate an equitable reduction in the job price [@CL-CTF-1999]. Failure to agree upon an equitable reduction shall constitute a dispute under the Disputes clause of the agreement [@CL-CTF-2377].

### Over and above work

Under the Over and Above Work clause, the contractor and the Administrative Contracting Officer shall mutually agree to procedures for Government administration and contractor performance of over and above work requests, and if the parties cannot agree, the Administrative Contracting Officer has the unilateral right to direct the procedures to be followed [@CL-CTF-9589; @CL-CTF-1990]. Upon discovery of the need for over and above work, the contractor shall prepare and furnish to the Government a work request in accordance with the agreed-to procedures [@CL-CTF-2378]. These procedures shall, as a minimum, cover [@CL-CTF-2316] the format, content and submission of work requests by the contractor; work requests shall contain data on the type of discrepancy disclosed, the specific location of the discrepancy, and the estimated labor hours and material required to correct it [@CL-CTF-2379]. Data shall be sufficient to satisfy contract requirements and obtain the authorization of the Contracting Officer to perform the proposed work; the procedures shall also cover Government review, verification and authorization of the work, and proposal pricing, submission, negotiation and definitization [@CL-CTF-2420]. The Government shall promptly review the work request, verify that the proposed work is required and not covered under the basic line items, verify that the proposed corrective action is appropriate, and authorize the over and above work as necessary [@CL-CTF-1970]. The contractor shall promptly submit to the Contracting Officer a proposal for the over and above work; the Government and the contractor will then negotiate a settlement, and contract modifications will be executed to definitize all over and above work [@CL-CTF-1971]. Failure to agree on the price of over and above work shall be a dispute within the meaning of the Disputes clause of the contract [@CL-CTF-1991].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The yard may not start a job order's work before award, except emergency work that the Contracting Officer orders, and the Government delivers and accepts the vessel at the times and places the job order sets. A vessel is redelivered, and a valve fails 60 days later, inside the 90 day period, so the contractor corrects it as directed and at its own expense. Its liability for that corrected defect then runs for a further 90 days. In another case the Government, as the clause requires where practicable, gives the contractor the chance to correct a deficiency, but the contractor does not proceed promptly with the repair, so the Contracting Officer has the repair done elsewhere at the contractor's expense and may discharge the contractor's liability by an equitable deduction in the job order price. During an overhaul, workers also find a corroded pipe that is within the contract's scope, not covered by the basic line items and needed to finish the job. The contractor prepares a work request under the agreed procedures that gives the type and location of the corrosion and the estimated labor hours and material, the Government verifies and authorizes it, and the contractor submits a proposal. The parties negotiate, and a modification definitizes the price. Separately, the job order states that dock and sea trials are required to decide whether the work was done satisfactorily, and it requires a dock trial, so the vessel stays under the control of its commander and crew while the contractor installs the fittings and appliances the trial needs and looks after the Government's test instruments. The contractor also runs a second dock trial that the job order did not specify and did not get advance approval for, so that trial is at its own expense and risk. If the parties cannot agree on the price of the over and above work, it is a dispute under the Disputes clause.

## Never confuse

Over and above work is work discovered during the course of performing overhaul, maintenance, and repair efforts [@CL-CTF-1969]. The Contracting Officer may, at any time and without notice to the sureties, by written change order, make changes within the general scope of any job order issued under the Master Agreement in drawings, designs, plans and specifications; work itemized; place of performance; time of commencement or completion; and any other requirement of the job order [@CL-CTF-1938; @CL-CTF-2380].
