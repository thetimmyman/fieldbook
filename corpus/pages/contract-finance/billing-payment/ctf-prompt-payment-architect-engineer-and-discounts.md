## Purpose

Gives the invoice due dates, interest penalties and discount-period rules in the fixed-price architect-engineer prompt payment clause and the clause on discounts for prompt payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1668] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-26 [@CL-CTF-1663]; FAR 52.232-8 [@CL-CTF-1668] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-26 [@CL-CTF-0593; @CL-CTF-0594; @CL-CTF-0595; @CL-CTF-0596; @CL-CTF-0597; @CL-CTF-0598; @CL-CTF-0599; @CL-CTF-0621; @CL-CTF-0622; @CL-CTF-0623]; RFO FAR 52.232-8 [@CL-CTF-0624; @CL-CTF-0625; @CL-CTF-0626] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1668] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include either clause [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1668] |

For work or services completed by the contractor, the due date for invoice payments is the later of two events: the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice, except as provided for a missing receipt annotation, and the 30th day after Government acceptance [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1664]. If the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice or payment request with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice or payment request [@CL-CTF-1705], provided the office receives a proper invoice or payment request and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-1706]. For a final invoice subject to contract settlement actions such as release of claims, acceptance is deemed to occur on the effective date of the settlement [@CL-CTF-1664]. The due date for progress payments is the 30th day after Government approval of contractor estimates of work or services accomplished [@CL-CTF-1665].

The payment office pays an interest penalty automatically, without a request from the contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the listed conditions are met [@CL-CTF-1666], except that when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday the payment office may make payment on the following working day without incurring a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-9496]. An additional penalty amount is payable only if listed conditions are met, one of which is that the Government owes an interest penalty of 1 dollar or more [@CL-CTF-1667]. For the sole purpose of computing an interest penalty that might be due the contractor, Government acceptance or approval is deemed to occur constructively as shown in the clause [@CL-CTF-1707]. For work or services completed by the contractor, acceptance is deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after the contractor completes the work or services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the contract [@CL-CTF-1708]. These constructive requirements do not apply if there is a disagreement over quantity, quality, contractor compliance with a contract provision, or requested progress payment amounts [@CL-CTF-1709]. This card does not state the other listed conditions for the additional penalty, how the penalty amount is computed, or the constructive approval date for progress payments.

## How it works

For any discount offered for prompt payment, time is computed from the date of the invoice [@CL-CTF-1668]. If the contractor has not dated the invoice, the due date is calculated from the date the designated billing office receives a proper invoice, provided the agency annotates the invoice with the date of receipt at the time of receipt [@CL-CTF-1669]. For computing the discount earned, payment is considered made on the date that appears on the payment check or, for an electronic funds transfer, on the specified payment date [@CL-CTF-1670]. See [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md) for the general due-date rules and [Prompt payment interest and clauses](ctf-prompt-payment-interest-and-clauses.md) for interest penalties in general.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-26 keeps the due date for invoice payments for work or services completed by the contractor, which is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice (except as provided for a missing receipt annotation) and the 30th day after Government acceptance, with acceptance of a final invoice subject to settlement actions deemed to occur on the effective date of the settlement [@CL-CTF-0593; @CL-CTF-0594; @CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1664]. It keeps the due date for progress payments, which is the 30th day after Government approval of contractor estimates of work or services accomplished [@CL-CTF-0595; @CL-CTF-1665]. It keeps the fallback to the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice or payment request when the billing office does not annotate the date of receipt, provided the office receives a proper invoice or payment request and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0596; @CL-CTF-0597; @CL-CTF-1705; @CL-CTF-1706]. It keeps the automatic interest penalty when payment is not made by the due date and the listed conditions are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-0598; @CL-CTF-1666], with the exception that when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday the payment office may make payment on the following working day without incurring a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-9502; @CL-CTF-9496], and the additional penalty only if listed conditions are met, one being that the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more [@CL-CTF-0599; @CL-CTF-1667]. It keeps the constructive acceptance rules for computing an interest penalty, including acceptance on the 7th day after the contractor completes the work or services and the exception for disagreements over quantity, quality, contractor compliance with a contract provision, or requested progress payment amounts [@CL-CTF-0621; @CL-CTF-0622; @CL-CTF-0623; @CL-CTF-1707; @CL-CTF-1708; @CL-CTF-1709]. RFO FAR 52.232-8 keeps the discount rules: time is computed from the date of the invoice, an undated invoice is dated from the billing office's receipt of a proper invoice if the agency annotates the date of receipt at the time of receipt, and payment is considered made on the date on the check or the specified electronic funds transfer date [@CL-CTF-0624; @CL-CTF-0625; @CL-CTF-0626; @CL-CTF-1668; @CL-CTF-1669; @CL-CTF-1670].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A design firm completes its work on day 20, and the billing office receives a proper invoice on day 22. There is no disagreement over quantity, quality or compliance. The payment due date is the later of the 30th day after receipt of the invoice, day 52, and the 30th day after Government acceptance, so it cannot be fixed until the Government accepts the work.

For computing interest only, acceptance is deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after completion, which is day 27. The interest deadline is the later of day 52 and day 57 (day 27 plus 30), which is day 57, so a payment made after day 57 draws an automatic interest penalty if the listed conditions are met. If the Government actually accepts within the 7 days, on day 25, the interest computation uses day 25 instead, and the later of day 52 and day 55 is day 55.

For a discount, suppose an invoice is dated day 1 and offers a discount for payment within 10 days. The discount period ends on day 11. A payment check dated day 11 earns the discount.

## Never confuse

The 30-day progress payment period runs from Government approval of the contractor's estimates, not from receipt of an invoice [@CL-CTF-1665; @CL-CTF-1663]. A discount period is counted from the invoice date, not from the date the invoice is received, unless the invoice is undated [@CL-CTF-1668; @CL-CTF-1669]. The construction clause instead sets a 14-day due date for progress payments counted from receipt of a proper payment request [@CL-CTF-1651]; see [Prompt payment clause for construction contracts](ctf-prompt-payment-construction-contracts.md). The interest penalty the payment office pays a contractor for late payment [@CL-CTF-1666] is also not the simple interest on amounts payable by the contractor to the Government [@CL-CTF-1671]; see [Interest on amounts due the Government and the assignment of claims clause](ctf-interest-and-assignment-of-claims-clauses.md).
