## Purpose

Gives the invoice due dates, interest penalties and discount-period rules in the fixed-price architect-engineer prompt payment clause and the clause on discounts for prompt payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1668] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-26 [@CL-CTF-1663]; FAR 52.232-8 [@CL-CTF-1668] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-26 [@CL-CTF-0593; @CL-CTF-0594; @CL-CTF-0595; @CL-CTF-0596; @CL-CTF-0597; @CL-CTF-0598; @CL-CTF-0599; @CL-CTF-0621; @CL-CTF-0622; @CL-CTF-0623; @CL-CTF-9502]; RFO FAR 52.232-8 [@CL-CTF-0624; @CL-CTF-0625; @CL-CTF-0626] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1668] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include either clause [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1668] |

For work or services completed by the contractor, the due date for making invoice payments is the later of the following two events: the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (a)(1)(iii) of the clause) [@CL-CTF-1663], and the 30th day after Government acceptance of the work or services completed by the contractor [@CL-CTF-1664]. For a final invoice, when the payment amount is subject to contract settlement actions (e.g., release of claims), acceptance is deemed to occur on the effective date of the settlement [@CL-CTF-1664]. If the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice or payment request with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice or payment request [@CL-CTF-1705], provided the designated billing office receives a proper invoice or payment request and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-1706]. The due date for progress payments is the 30th day after Government approval of contractor estimates of work or services accomplished [@CL-CTF-1665].

The designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions listed in paragraphs (a)(3)(i) through (a)(3)(iii) of the clause are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-1666], except that when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the designated payment office may make payment on the following working day without incurring a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-9496]. The designated payment office will pay an additional penalty amount, calculated in accordance with the prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315, in addition to the interest penalty amount only if the listed conditions are met, the first being that the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more [@CL-CTF-1667]. For the sole purpose of computing an interest penalty that might be due the contractor, Government acceptance or approval is deemed to occur constructively as shown in paragraphs (a)(4)(i)(A) and (B) of the clause [@CL-CTF-1707]. For work or services completed by the contractor, Government acceptance is deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after the contractor completes the work or services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the contract [@CL-CTF-1708]. Constructive acceptance or constructive approval requirements do not apply if there is a disagreement over quantity, quality, contractor compliance with a contract provision, or requested progress payment amounts [@CL-CTF-1709].

## How it works

In connection with any discount offered for prompt payment, time shall be computed from the date of the invoice [@CL-CTF-1668]. If the contractor has not placed a date on the invoice, the due date shall be calculated from the date the designated billing office receives a proper invoice, provided the agency annotates such invoice with the date of receipt at the time of receipt [@CL-CTF-1669]. For the purpose of computing the discount earned, payment shall be considered to have been made on the date that appears on the payment check or, for an electronic funds transfer, the specified payment date [@CL-CTF-1670]. For the general due-date rules, see [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md), and for interest penalties in general, see [Prompt payment interest and clauses](ctf-prompt-payment-interest-and-clauses.md).

DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 52 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-26, the due date for making invoice payments for work or services completed by the contractor is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (a)(1)(iii) of the clause) [@CL-CTF-0593] and the 30th day after Government acceptance of the work or services completed by the contractor, with acceptance for a final invoice subject to contract settlement actions (e.g., release of claims) deemed to occur on the effective date of the settlement [@CL-CTF-0594], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1664]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-26, the due date for progress payments is the 30th day after Government approval of contractor estimates of work or services accomplished [@CL-CTF-0595], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1665]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-26, if the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice or payment request with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice or payment request [@CL-CTF-0596], provided the designated billing office receives a proper invoice or payment request and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0597], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1705; @CL-CTF-1706]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-26, the designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions listed in paragraphs (a)(3)(i) through (a)(3)(iii) of the clause are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-0598], except that when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the designated payment office may make payment on the following working day without incurring a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-9502], and the designated payment office will pay a penalty amount, calculated in accordance with the prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315, in addition to the interest penalty amount only if the listed conditions are met, including that the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more [@CL-CTF-0599], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1666; @CL-CTF-9496; @CL-CTF-1667]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-26, for the sole purpose of computing an interest penalty that might be due the contractor, Government acceptance or approval is deemed to occur constructively as shown in paragraphs (a)(4)(i)(A) and (B) of the clause [@CL-CTF-0621], with acceptance for work or services completed by the contractor deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after the contractor completes the work or services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the contract [@CL-CTF-0622], and constructive acceptance or constructive approval requirements do not apply if there is a disagreement over quantity, quality, contractor compliance with a contract provision, or requested progress payment amounts [@CL-CTF-0623], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1707; @CL-CTF-1708; @CL-CTF-1709]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-8, in connection with any discount offered for prompt payment, time shall be computed from the date of the invoice [@CL-CTF-0624], if the contractor has not placed a date on the invoice the due date shall be calculated from the date the designated billing office receives a proper invoice provided the agency annotates such invoice with the date of receipt at the time of receipt [@CL-CTF-0625], and for the purpose of computing the discount earned payment shall be considered to have been made on the date that appears on the payment check or, for an electronic funds transfer, the specified payment date [@CL-CTF-0626], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1668; @CL-CTF-1669; @CL-CTF-1670].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A design firm completes its work on day 20, and the billing office receives a proper invoice on day 22. There is no disagreement over quantity, quality or compliance. The payment due date is the later of the 30th day after receipt of the invoice, day 52, and the 30th day after Government acceptance, so it cannot be fixed until the Government accepts the work.

For computing interest only, acceptance is deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after completion, which is day 27. The interest deadline is the later of day 52 and day 57 (day 27 plus 30), which is day 57, so a payment made after day 57 draws an automatic interest penalty if the listed conditions are met. If the Government actually accepts within the 7 days, on day 25, the interest computation uses day 25 instead, and the later of day 52 and day 55 is day 55.

For a discount, suppose an invoice is dated day 1 and offers a discount for payment within 10 days. The discount period ends on day 11. A payment check dated day 11 earns the discount.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-26, the due date for progress payments is the 30th day after Government approval of contractor estimates of work or services accomplished [@CL-CTF-1665], whereas for work or services completed by the contractor the due date for invoice payments is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (a)(1)(iii) of the clause) and the 30th day after Government acceptance [@CL-CTF-1663; @CL-CTF-1664]. In connection with any discount offered for prompt payment under FAR 52.232-8, time shall be computed from the date of the invoice, and if the contractor has not placed a date on the invoice, the due date shall be calculated from the date the designated billing office receives a proper invoice, provided the agency annotates such invoice with the date of receipt at the time of receipt [@CL-CTF-1668; @CL-CTF-1669]. Under FAR 52.232-27, for progress payments (if provided for elsewhere in the contract) based on Contracting Officer approval of the estimated amount and value of work or services performed, including payments for reaching milestones in any project, the due date for making such payments is 14 days after the designated billing office receives a proper payment request [@CL-CTF-1650; @CL-CTF-1651]; see [Prompt payment clause for construction contracts](ctf-prompt-payment-construction-contracts.md). Under FAR 52.232-26, the designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions listed in paragraphs (a)(3)(i) through (a)(3)(iii) of the clause are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-1666], whereas under FAR 52.232-17, except as otherwise provided in the contract under a Price Reduction for Defective Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause or a Cost Accounting Standards clause, all amounts that become payable by the contractor to the Government under the contract shall bear simple interest from the date due until paid unless paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1672]; see [Interest on amounts due the Government and the assignment of claims clause](ctf-interest-and-assignment-of-claims-clauses.md).
