## Purpose

Shows when DoD treats the FAR prompt payment subpart as not applying, which cases are exempt from electronic submission of payment requests, and what the DoD procedures for performance-based payments say about the solicitation, the analysis tool and the contract file.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1438; @CL-CTF-1441; @CL-CTF-1449] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.901, DFARS 232.1004 and DFARS 232.7002 [@CL-CTF-1438; @CL-CTF-1449; @CL-CTF-1441] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1441] |
| Applies to | DoD payment requests and receiving reports, with the listed exceptions, and DoD solicitations and contracts that consider progress payments or performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-1441; @CL-CTF-1449] |

Except for FAR 32.908, FAR subpart 32.9, Prompt Payment, does not apply when the three listed conditions are all met [@CL-CTF-1438; @CL-CTF-1439; @CL-CTF-1440]. One listed condition is that there is an emergency as defined in the Disaster Relief Act of 1974, a contingency operation, or the release or threatened release of hazardous substances [@CL-CTF-1438]. A further listed condition, joined by and to the others, is that the head of the contracting activity has made a determination, after consultation with the cognizant comptroller, that conditions exist that limit normal business operations [@CL-CTF-1439]. A third listed condition is that payments will be made in the operational area or made contingent upon receiving supporting documentation, meaning contract, invoice, and receiving report, from the operational area [@CL-CTF-1440].

Payment requests and receiving reports are required to be submitted in electronic form, except for the cases listed in the paragraph [@CL-CTF-1441]. The listed exceptions are classified contracts or purchases when electronic submission and processing could compromise the safeguarding of classified information or national security [@CL-CTF-1465], cases in which contractor electronic submission is not feasible, such as where internet connectivity is not available [@CL-CTF-1442], and cases in which DoD is unable to receive payment requests or provide acceptance in electronic form [@CL-CTF-1443]. They also include cases in which the contractor has requested permission in writing to use nonelectronic means and the contracting officer has provided instructions for a temporary alternative method [@CL-CTF-1444], which are provided for in the contract administration data section of the contract or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-1473], and use of the Governmentwide commercial purchase card as the method of payment, in which case only the receiving report must be electronic [@CL-CTF-1445].

For performance-based payments, the contracting officer should include in a solicitation both the progress payments and performance-based payments provisions and clauses prescribed in the part, when considering both types of payment methods [@CL-CTF-1449]. Only one type of financing will be included in the resultant contract, except as may be authorized on separate orders subject to FAR 32.1003(c) [@CL-CTF-1468]. The contracting officer shall analyze the performance-based payment schedule using the performance-based payments (PBP) analysis tool [@CL-CTF-1469]. When considering performance-based payments, the procedure says to obtain from the offeror or contractor a proposed schedule that includes all events, completion criteria and event values along with the projected monthly expenditure profile [@CL-CTF-1471]. The contracting officer shall document in the contract file that the performance-based payment schedule provides a mutually beneficial settlement position that reflects adequate consideration to the Government for the improved contractor cash flow [@CL-CTF-1470]. If the contract contains foreign military sales requirements, the contracting officer shall provide instructions for distribution of the contract financing payments to each country's account [@CL-CTF-1472].

## How it works

The only acceptable electronic form for payment requests and receiving reports is Wide Area WorkFlow, except as listed in the paragraph [@CL-CTF-1446]. One exception concerns payment of commercial transportation services under a Government rate tender, contract, or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-1447], where the use of a DoD-approved electronic third party payment system or other exempted vendor payment or invoicing system is permitted [@CL-CTF-1466]. For rendered health care services, use of the TRICARE Encounter Data System is permitted as the electronic form [@CL-CTF-1448]. Facsimile, email, and scanned documents are not acceptable electronic forms of payment requests or receiving reports [@CL-CTF-1467]. The prompt payment due dates and the proper invoice contents are in [Prompt payment for invoices](ctf-prompt-payment.md) and [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md). The FAR conditions for performance-based payments are on [Performance-based payments](../financing/ctf-performance-based-payments.md), [Administration and payment of performance-based payments](../financing/ctf-pbp-administration-and-payment.md) and [Performance-based payments title and risk of loss](../financing/ctf-pbp-title-and-risk-of-loss.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD supply contract is performed in a contingency operation. The head of the contracting activity has determined, after consulting the comptroller, that conditions limit normal business operations, and payments will be made in the operational area. Separately, a contractor with a nonclassified contract and normal internet access submits a 30,000 invoice by email. Separately again, a contracting officer considering both progress payments and performance-based payments for a 10,000,000 contract includes both sets of provisions and clauses in the solicitation.

Answer: the first case meets all three listed conditions, so the prompt payment subpart does not apply, except for its one carved-out section. The email invoice is not in an acceptable electronic form, because Wide Area WorkFlow is the only acceptable electronic form unless a listed exception applies, and none does here. The resulting contract carries one type of financing, unless separate orders are authorized under the FAR.

## Never confuse

The prompt payment exception removes the FAR prompt payment subpart, except one section, when the listed conditions are met [@CL-CTF-1438; @CL-CTF-1439; @CL-CTF-1440]. The electronic submission rule is a different rule about how payment requests are sent [@CL-CTF-1441; @CL-CTF-1446]. A purchase card payment is an exception that still requires an electronic receiving report [@CL-CTF-1445]. Progress payments and performance-based payments are alternatives in the resulting contract, not a combination [@CL-CTF-1468]. The same section on prompt payment also sets how later determinations are made as the operational area changes, which this card does not restate. For the Wide Area WorkFlow clause itself, see [Electronic submission of payment requests: Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md), and for the financing types see [Providing contract financing and customary contract financing](../financing/ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md).
