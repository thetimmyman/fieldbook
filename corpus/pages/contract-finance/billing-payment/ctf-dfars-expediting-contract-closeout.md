## Purpose

Shows how the DFARS closeout clause treats a small residual balance at closeout, what counts as a residual amount, and what the clause says about offsets.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1806] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.204-7022 [@CL-CTF-1806] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1806] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause, at the conclusion of the applicable closeout requirements [@CL-CTF-1806] |

At the conclusion of all applicable closeout requirements of the FAR closeout section the clause cites, the Government and the Contractor shall mutually agree on the residual dollar amount remaining on the contract [@CL-CTF-1806]. Both agree to waive payment of any residual dollar amount of $1,000 or less to which either party may be entitled at the time of contract closeout [@CL-CTF-1807].

A residual dollar amount includes all money owed to either party at the end of the contract and as a result of the contract, but it excludes amounts connected in any way with taxation or a violation of law or regulation, so the waiver reaches residual amounts only as the clause defines them [@CL-CTF-1808; @CL-CTF-1807]. For determining residual amounts, offsets (for example across multiple contracts or orders) may be considered only to the extent permitted by law [@CL-CTF-1809]. The DFARS directs use of the clause as prescribed in DFARS 204.804-70 [@CL-CTF-1832]. The printed clause sets one dollar threshold and no other [@CL-CTF-1807].

## How it works

The agreement on the residual amount comes after the closeout steps are done, and the waiver then applies to either party's entitlement at or below the threshold [@CL-CTF-1806; @CL-CTF-1807]. The residual amount is measured across all money owed at the end of the contract, less the excluded amounts [@CL-CTF-1808]. Offsets are allowed only as far as the law permits [@CL-CTF-1809]. The FAR closeout steps and the DFARS closeout procedures are on [Procedures for closing out contract files](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-contract-closeout-procedures.md) and [DFARS contract closeout and file disposal](ctf-dfars-contract-closeout-and-file-disposal.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

After the closeout steps, the contractor and the Government agree that the contractor is owed 600 on a final adjustment that has no link to taxes or a violation of law. Answer: the 600 is below the waiver amount, so both sides waive it. If the agreed balance were 1,500, it is above the waiver amount, so this clause would not waive it.

## Never confuse

The waiver covers only residual dollar amounts as the clause defines them, and the definition excludes amounts connected in any way with taxation or a violation of law or regulation [@CL-CTF-1808]. It is also not the closeout without a reconciliation audit that the DFARS allows for old contracts under listed conditions, which is on [DFARS contract closeout and file disposal](ctf-dfars-contract-closeout-and-file-disposal.md).
