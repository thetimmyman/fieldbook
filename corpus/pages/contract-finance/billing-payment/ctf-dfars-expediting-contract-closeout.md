## Purpose

Shows how the DFARS closeout clause treats a small residual balance at closeout, what counts as a residual amount, and what the clause says about offsets.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1806] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.204-7022 [@CL-CTF-1806] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1806] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause, at the conclusion of the applicable closeout requirements [@CL-CTF-1806] |

At the conclusion of all applicable closeout requirements of Federal Acquisition Regulation 4.804, the Government and the Contractor shall mutually agree on the residual dollar amount remaining on the contract [@CL-CTF-1806]. Both agree to waive payment of any residual dollar amount of $1,000 or less to which either party may be entitled at the time of contract closeout [@CL-CTF-1807].

A residual dollar amount includes all money owed to either party at the end of the contract and as a result of the contract, excluding amounts connected in any way with taxation or a violation of law or regulation [@CL-CTF-1808]. For determining residual amounts, offsets (for example across multiple contracts or orders) may be considered only to the extent permitted by law [@CL-CTF-1809]. The DFARS directs use of the clause as prescribed in DFARS 204.804-70 [@CL-CTF-1832].

## How it works

At the conclusion of all applicable closeout requirements of Federal Acquisition Regulation 4.804, the Government and the contractor shall mutually agree on the residual dollar amount remaining on the contract [@CL-CTF-1806], and both agree to waive payment of any residual dollar amount of $1,000 or less to which either party may be entitled at the time of contract closeout [@CL-CTF-1807]. A residual dollar amount includes all money owed to either party at the end of the contract and as a result of the contract, excluding amounts connected in any way with taxation or a violation of law or regulation [@CL-CTF-1808]. For determining residual dollar amounts, offsets may be considered only to the extent permitted by law [@CL-CTF-1809]. The FAR closeout steps and the DFARS closeout procedures are on [Procedures for closing out contract files](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-contract-closeout-procedures.md) and [DFARS contract closeout and file disposal](ctf-dfars-contract-closeout-and-file-disposal.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

After the closeout steps, the contractor and the Government agree that the contractor is owed 600 on a final adjustment that has no link to taxes or a violation of law. Answer: the 600 is below the waiver amount, so both sides waive it. If the agreed balance were 1,500, it is above the waiver amount, so this clause would not waive it.

## Never confuse

A residual dollar amount excludes amounts connected in any way with taxation or a violation of law or regulation [@CL-CTF-1808]. The DFARS closeout without a reconciliation audit for contracts meeting listed conditions is on [DFARS contract closeout and file disposal](ctf-dfars-contract-closeout-and-file-disposal.md).
