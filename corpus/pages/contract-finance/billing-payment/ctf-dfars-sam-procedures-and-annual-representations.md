## Purpose

Shows what is checked against the System for Award Management record before an option or a modification, and which provision goes with the annual representations provision.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1618] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 204.1103 [@CL-CTF-1618; @CL-CTF-1619; @CL-CTF-1620; @CL-CTF-1621; @CL-CTF-1647]; DFARS 204.1202 [@CL-CTF-1622; @CL-CTF-1648] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1618] |
| Applies to | Option exercise determinations and modifications other than unilateral administrative changes, and solicitations that use the annual representations provision [@CL-CTF-1618; @CL-CTF-1622] |

On contract award documents, the contractor's legal or doing business as name and physical address information is used as recorded in the SAM database at the time of award [@CL-CTF-1647]. When making a determination to exercise an option, or at any other time before issuing a modification other than a unilateral modification making an administrative change, the DFARS says to ensure that the contractor's record is active in the SAM database [@CL-CTF-1618] and that the contractor's unique entity identifier number, Commercial and Government Entity code, name, and physical address are accurately reflected in the contract document [@CL-CTF-1619].

At any time, if the UEI number, CAGE code, contractor name, or physical address on a contract no longer matches the contractor's record in the SAM database, the contracting officer shall process a novation or change-of-name agreement, or an address change, as appropriate [@CL-CTF-1620]. On contractual documents transmitted to the payment office, the CAGE code is provided instead of the UEI, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CTF-1621].

When using the provision at FAR 52.204-8, Annual Representations and Certifications, the provision is used with DFARS 252.204-7007, Alternate A [@CL-CTF-1622]. When using that provision, and the provision at FAR 52.204-7, System for Award Management, is included in the solicitation, the provisions that the DFARS lists as included in DFARS 252.204-7007 are not included separately in the solicitation [@CL-CTF-1648].

## How it works

Award documents use the name and address recorded in SAM at the time of award [@CL-CTF-1647]. When making a determination to exercise an option, or at any other time before issuing a modification other than a unilateral modification making an administrative change, the DFARS says to ensure that the contractor's record is active in the SAM database [@CL-CTF-1618] and that the contractor's unique entity identifier number, Commercial and Government Entity code, name, and physical address are accurately reflected in the contract document [@CL-CTF-1619]. If the UEI number, CAGE code, contractor name, or physical address on a contract no longer matches the contractor's record in the SAM database, the contracting officer shall process a novation or change-of-name agreement, or an address change, as appropriate [@CL-CTF-1620]. On contractual documents transmitted to the payment office, the CAGE code is provided instead of the UEI [@CL-CTF-1621]. When using the annual representations provision at FAR 52.204-8, it is used with DFARS 252.204-7007, Alternate A [@CL-CTF-1622], and when the SAM provision at FAR 52.204-7 is included in the solicitation, the listed provisions are not included separately [@CL-CTF-1648].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor changes its legal name after award, and its SAM record shows the new name, but the contract still shows the old name. An option is about to be exercised.

The answer: the name in the contract no longer matches the SAM record, so the contracting officer processes a change-of-name agreement, and the payment office is sent the CAGE code, not the UEI, under agency procedures.
