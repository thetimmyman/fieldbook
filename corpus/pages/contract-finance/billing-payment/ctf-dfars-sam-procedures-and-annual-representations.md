## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer checks against the System for Award Management record before an option or a modification, and which provision goes with the annual representations provision.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1618] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 204.1103 [@CL-CTF-1618; @CL-CTF-1619; @CL-CTF-1620; @CL-CTF-1621; @CL-CTF-1647]; DFARS 204.1202 [@CL-CTF-1622; @CL-CTF-1648] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1618] |
| Applies to | Option exercise determinations and modifications other than unilateral administrative changes, and solicitations that use the annual representations provision [@CL-CTF-1618; @CL-CTF-1622] |

On contract award documents, the contractor's legal or doing business as name and physical address information is used as recorded in the SAM database at the time of award [@CL-CTF-1647]. When making a determination to exercise an option, or at any other time before issuing a modification other than a unilateral modification making an administrative change, one listed check is that the contractor's record is active in the SAM database [@CL-CTF-1618]. Another is that the contractor's unique entity identifier number, Commercial and Government Entity code, name, and physical address are accurately reflected in the contract document [@CL-CTF-1618; @CL-CTF-1619].

If the UEI number, CAGE code, contractor name, or physical address on a contract no longer matches the contractor's record in the SAM database, the contracting officer shall process a novation or change-of-name agreement, or an address change, as appropriate [@CL-CTF-1620]. On contractual documents transmitted to the payment office, the CAGE code is provided instead of the UEI, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CTF-1621].

When using the provision at FAR 52.204-8, Annual Representations and Certifications, the provision is used with DFARS 252.204-7007, Alternate A [@CL-CTF-1622]. When the provision at FAR 52.204-7, System for Award Management, is included in the solicitation, the solicitation does not include separately the provisions that the DFARS lists as included in DFARS 252.204-7007 [@CL-CTF-1648]. This card does not set out that list.

## How it works

Award documents use the name and address recorded in SAM at the time of award [@CL-CTF-1647]. Before an option is exercised or a modification is issued, the contracting officer confirms an active SAM record and accurate identifying data in the contract document [@CL-CTF-1618; @CL-CTF-1619]. A mismatch between the contract and the SAM record is fixed by a novation, a change-of-name agreement or an address change [@CL-CTF-1620]. The payment office receives the CAGE code rather than the UEI [@CL-CTF-1621]. The solicitation side pairs the annual representations provision with its Alternate A [@CL-CTF-1622], and the SAM provision in the solicitation removes the need to include the listed provisions separately [@CL-CTF-1648].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor changes its legal name after award, and its SAM record shows the new name, but the contract still shows the old name. An option is about to be exercised.

The answer: the name in the contract no longer matches the SAM record, so the contracting officer processes a change-of-name agreement, and the payment office is sent the CAGE code, not the UEI, under agency procedures.

## Never confuse

The use of the recorded name and address on award documents is a different step from the checks before an option or a modification [@CL-CTF-1647; @CL-CTF-1618]. Registration is on [SAM registration and awards](../../market/feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md). The FAR annual representations rules are on [Annual representations and certifications in SAM](../../compliance/trade-cyber/cmp-annual-representations-and-certifications-in-sam.md), and the Alternate A content is on [Contracted services reporting and Alternate A representations](../../compliance/trade-cyber/cmp-contracted-services-reporting-and-alternate-a-representations.md). A novation is on [Novation agreements](../../mna/diligence/mna-novation-agreements.md), a change of name on [Change-of-name agreements](../../mna/diligence/mna-change-of-name-agreements.md), and CAGE codes on [CAGE code reporting](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-cage-code-reporting.md).
