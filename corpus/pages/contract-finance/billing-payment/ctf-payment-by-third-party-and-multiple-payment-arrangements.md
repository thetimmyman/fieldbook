## Purpose

Shows how a contractor is paid when a third party, such as a purchase card issuer, pays instead of the Government, and how the contract designates the payment method when several are allowed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2094] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-36 and FAR 52.232-37 [@CL-CTF-2094; @CL-CTF-2100] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-36 [@CL-CTF-0450]; RFO FAR 52.232-37 [@CL-CTF-0468] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2094] |
| Applies to | The third-party clause goes into solicitations and contracts when payment under a written contract will be made by a charge to a Government account with a third party such as a Governmentwide commercial purchase card [@CL-CTF-1546]. The multiple payment arrangements clause goes into the solicitation and contract or agreement when it provides for delivery orders and provides that the ordering office designates the method of payment for individual orders [@CL-CTF-1547] |

Except as provided in paragraph (a)(2) of the clause, the contractor agrees to accept payments due under the contract through payment by a third party in lieu of payment directly from the Government, in accordance with the terms of the clause [@CL-CTF-2094]. The Governmentwide commercial purchase card is not authorized as a method of payment during any period the System for Award Management indicates that the contractor has delinquent debt that is subject to collection under the Treasury Offset Program [@CL-CTF-2095]. If the System for Award Management subsequently indicates that the contractor no longer has delinquent debt, the contractor may request the contracting officer to authorize payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card [@CL-CTF-2251]. Except as provided in paragraph (b)(2) of the clause, the contractor makes payment requests through a charge to the Government account with the third party, at the time and for the amount due under the clauses of the contract that authorize it to submit invoices [@CL-CTF-2096], contract financing requests, other payment requests, or as provided in other clauses providing for payment to the contractor [@CL-CTF-2255]. When the contracting officer has notified the contractor that the Governmentwide commercial purchase card is no longer an authorized method of payment, the contractor makes such payment requests in accordance with the contracting officer's instructions [@CL-CTF-2252], which apply during the period when the purchase card is not authorized [@CL-CTF-2257]. Documentation of each charge against the Government's account must be provided to the contracting officer on request [@CL-CTF-2125].

No payment is due the contractor until the agreement between the contractor, the contractor's financial agent if any, and the third party and its agents if any is made [@CL-CTF-2097]. Payments made or due by the third party under the clause are not payments made by the Government and are not subject to the Prompt Payment Act or any implementation of it in the contract [@CL-CTF-2098].

## How it works

When any payment is made under the third-party clause, no payment under the contract may be assigned under the assignment of claims terms of the contract or the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 [@CL-CTF-2099]. The other payment terms of the contract govern the content and submission of payment requests [@CL-CTF-2254]. If any clause requires information or documents in or with the payment request that the third party agreement does not provide, the contractor must obtain instructions from the contracting officer before submitting the request [@CL-CTF-2253]. The multiple payment arrangements clause does not set the method itself. The applicability of specific methods of payment and the designation of the payment offices are stated either elsewhere in the contract or agreement or in individual orders placed under it [@CL-CTF-2100]. To find the method for a given order, read the order and the rest of the contract.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO third-party clause keeps the rule that, except as provided in paragraph (a)(2) of the clause, the contractor agrees to accept payments due under the contract through payment by a third party in lieu of payment directly from the Government, in accordance with the terms of the clause [@CL-CTF-0450] [@CL-CTF-2094]. It keeps the bar on the Governmentwide commercial purchase card during any period the System for Award Management indicates that the contractor has delinquent debt that is subject to collection under the Treasury Offset Program [@CL-CTF-0456] [@CL-CTF-2095], and the contractor's option to ask the contracting officer to authorize the card if the System for Award Management subsequently indicates that the contractor no longer has delinquent debt [@CL-CTF-0457] [@CL-CTF-2251]. The RFO third-party clause prints a different web address for information on the Treasury Offset Program than the codified paragraph does [@CL-CTF-9097] [@CL-CTF-9098]. It keeps the rule that, except as provided in paragraph (b)(2) of the clause, the contractor makes payment requests through a charge to the Government account with the third party, at the time and for the amount due in accordance with those clauses of the contract that authorize it to submit invoices [@CL-CTF-0458] [@CL-CTF-2096], contract financing requests, other payment requests, or as provided in other clauses providing for payment to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0459] [@CL-CTF-2255]. When the contracting officer has notified the contractor that the card is no longer an authorized method of payment, it keeps the rule that the contractor makes such payment requests in accordance with the contracting officer's instructions [@CL-CTF-0460] [@CL-CTF-2252], which apply during the period when the card is not authorized [@CL-CTF-0461] [@CL-CTF-2257]. It keeps the rules that no payment is due the contractor until the agreement among the contractor, its financial agent if any, and the third party and its agents if any is made [@CL-CTF-0462] [@CL-CTF-2097], that payments made or due by the third party are not Government payments and are not subject to the Prompt Payment Act or any implementation of it in the contract [@CL-CTF-0463] [@CL-CTF-2098], and that documentation of each charge must be provided to the contracting officer on request [@CL-CTF-0464] [@CL-CTF-2125]. It keeps the bar on assigning any payment under the assignment of claims terms of the contract or the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 once any payment is made under the clause [@CL-CTF-0465] [@CL-CTF-2099], and the rules that the other payment terms govern the content and submission of payment requests [@CL-CTF-0466] [@CL-CTF-2254] and that the contractor obtains instructions from the contracting officer where a clause requires information the third party agreement does not provide [@CL-CTF-0467] [@CL-CTF-2253]. The RFO multiple payment arrangements clause keeps the rule that the applicability of specific methods of payment and the designation of the payment offices are stated either elsewhere in the contract or agreement or in individual orders [@CL-CTF-0468] [@CL-CTF-2100].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor invoices 5,000 for a delivery on a contract that allows payment by the Governmentwide commercial purchase card. The contractor charges the Government account with the third party for the amount due, and keeps documentation of the charge ready to give the contracting officer on request. The payment is not a Government payment, so the Prompt Payment Act does not apply to it. If the System for Award Management shows the contractor has delinquent debt under the Treasury Offset Program, the card cannot be used as the payment method for that period. If the contracting officer has notified the contractor that the card is no longer authorized, the contractor follows the contracting officer's instructions for its payment requests during that period. If the System for Award Management later shows no delinquent debt, the contractor may ask the contracting officer to authorize the card again.

## Never confuse

A payment by the third party is not a payment made by the Government, so Prompt Payment Act terms in the contract do not apply to it [@CL-CTF-2098]. For ordinary invoice payments, see [Prompt payment for invoices](ctf-prompt-payment.md), for the designated payment office under electronic funds transfer see [Payment by electronic funds transfer and the designated office for EFT information](ctf-eft-payment-clauses-and-designated-office.md), and for the card rules on electronic funds transfer, see [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](../financing/ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md). Assignment of claims is also affected once a payment is made this way [@CL-CTF-2099], and the conditions are on [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md).
