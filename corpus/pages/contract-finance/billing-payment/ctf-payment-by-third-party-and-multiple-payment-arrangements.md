## Purpose

Shows how a contractor is paid when a third party, such as a purchase card issuer, pays instead of the Government, and how the contract designates the payment method when several are allowed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2094] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-36 and FAR 52.232-37 [@CL-CTF-2094; @CL-CTF-2100] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-36 [@CL-CTF-0450]; RFO FAR 52.232-37 [@CL-CTF-0468] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2094] |
| Applies to | If payment under a written contract will be made by a charge to a Government account with a third party such as a Governmentwide commercial purchase card, the contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.232-36, Payment by Third Party, in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CTF-1546]. If the contract or agreement provides for the use of delivery orders and provides that the ordering office designate the method of payment for individual orders, the contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.232-37 in the solicitation and contract or agreement [@CL-CTF-1547] |

Except as provided in paragraph (a)(2) of the clause, the contractor agrees to accept payments due under the contract through payment by a third party in lieu of payment directly from the Government, in accordance with the terms of the clause [@CL-CTF-2094]. The Governmentwide commercial purchase card is not authorized as a method of payment during any period the System for Award Management indicates that the contractor has delinquent debt that is subject to collection under the Treasury Offset Program [@CL-CTF-2095]. Information on the Treasury Offset Program is available at http://fms.treas.gov/debt/index.html [@CL-CTF-9098]. If the System for Award Management subsequently indicates that the contractor no longer has delinquent debt, the contractor may request the contracting officer to authorize payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card [@CL-CTF-2251]. Except as provided in paragraph (b)(2) of the clause, the contractor must make payment requests through a charge to the Government account with the third party, at the time and for the amount due in accordance with those clauses of the contract that authorize the contractor to submit invoices [@CL-CTF-2096], contract financing requests, other payment requests, or as provided in other clauses providing for payment to the contractor [@CL-CTF-2255]. When the contracting officer has notified the contractor that the Governmentwide commercial purchase card is no longer an authorized method of payment, the contractor must make such payment requests in accordance with instructions provided by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-2252] during the period when the purchase card is not authorized [@CL-CTF-2257]. Documentation of each charge against the Government's account must be provided to the contracting officer upon request [@CL-CTF-2125].

The contractor and the third party must agree that payments due under the contract will be made upon submittal of payment requests to the third party in accordance with the terms and conditions of an [@CL-CTF-3052] agreement between the contractor, the contractor's financial agent if any, and the third party and its agents if any, and no payment is due the contractor until such agreement is made [@CL-CTF-2097]. Payments made or due by the third party under the clause are not payments made by the Government and are not subject to the Prompt Payment Act or any implementation thereof in the contract [@CL-CTF-2098].

## How it works

Notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, if any payment is made under the third-party clause, no payment under the contract may be assigned under the provisions of the assignment of claims terms of the contract or the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 [@CL-CTF-2099]. The other payment terms of the contract govern the content and submission of payment requests [@CL-CTF-2254]. If any clause requires information or documents in or with the payment request that is not provided in the third party agreement referenced in paragraph (c) of the clause, the contractor must obtain instructions from the contracting officer before submitting such a payment request [@CL-CTF-2253]. Under the multiple payment arrangements clause, the applicability of specific methods of payment and the designation of the payment offices are either stated elsewhere in the contract or agreement or in individual orders placed under the contract or agreement [@CL-CTF-2100].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. RFO FAR 52.232-36 provides that, except as provided in paragraph (a)(2) of the clause, the contractor agrees to accept payments due under the contract through payment by a third party in lieu of payment directly from the Government, in accordance with the terms of the clause [@CL-CTF-0450], matching FAR 52.232-36(a)(1) [@CL-CTF-2094]. RFO FAR 52.232-36 states that the Governmentwide commercial purchase card is not authorized as a method of payment during any period the System for Award Management indicates that the contractor has delinquent debt that is subject to collection under the Treasury Offset Program [@CL-CTF-0456], that information on the Treasury Offset Program is available at https://www.fiscal.treasury.gov/TOP/ [@CL-CTF-9097], and that if the System for Award Management subsequently indicates that the contractor no longer has delinquent debt, the contractor may request the contracting officer to authorize payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card [@CL-CTF-0457], whereas FAR 52.232-36(a)(2) states the same purchase-card restriction [@CL-CTF-2095] and authorization request rule [@CL-CTF-2251] and lists http://fms.treas.gov/debt/index.html for Treasury Offset Program information [@CL-CTF-9098]. RFO FAR 52.232-36 provides that, except as provided in paragraph (b)(2) of the clause, the contractor must make payment requests through a charge to the Government account with the third party, at the time and for the amount due in accordance with those clauses of the contract that authorize the contractor to submit invoices [@CL-CTF-0458], contract financing requests, other payment requests, or as provided in other clauses providing for payment to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0459], matching FAR 52.232-36(b)(1) [@CL-CTF-2096; @CL-CTF-2255]. When the contracting officer has notified the contractor that the Governmentwide commercial purchase card is no longer an authorized method of payment, RFO FAR 52.232-36 requires the contractor to make such payment requests in accordance with instructions provided by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0460] during the period when the purchase card is not authorized [@CL-CTF-0461], matching FAR 52.232-36(b)(2) [@CL-CTF-2252; @CL-CTF-2257]. RFO FAR 52.232-36 provides that the contractor and the third party must agree that payments due under the contract will be made upon submittal of payment requests to the third party in accordance with the terms and conditions of [@CL-CTF-3053] an agreement between the contractor, the contractor's financial agent if any, and the third party and its agents if any, and that no payment is due the contractor until such agreement is made [@CL-CTF-0462], that payments made or due by the third party under the clause are not payments made by the Government and are not subject to the Prompt Payment Act or any implementation thereof in the contract [@CL-CTF-0463], and that documentation of each charge against the Government's account must be provided to the contracting officer upon request [@CL-CTF-0464], matching FAR 52.232-36(c) and (d) [@CL-CTF-3052; @CL-CTF-2097; @CL-CTF-2098; @CL-CTF-2125]. RFO FAR 52.232-36 also provides that, notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, if any payment is made under the clause, no payment under the contract may be assigned under the provisions of the assignment of claims terms of the contract or the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 [@CL-CTF-0465], that the other payment terms of the contract govern the content and submission of payment requests [@CL-CTF-0466], and that if any clause requires information or documents in or with the payment request that is not provided in the third party agreement referenced in paragraph (c) of the clause, the contractor must obtain instructions from the contracting officer before submitting such a payment request [@CL-CTF-0467], matching FAR 52.232-36(e) and (f) [@CL-CTF-2099; @CL-CTF-2254; @CL-CTF-2253]. RFO FAR 52.232-37 provides that the applicability of specific methods of payment and the designation of the payment offices are either stated elsewhere in the contract or agreement or in individual orders placed under the contract or agreement [@CL-CTF-0468], matching FAR 52.232-37 [@CL-CTF-2100].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor invoices 5,000 for a delivery on a contract that allows payment by the Governmentwide commercial purchase card. The contractor charges the Government account with the third party for the amount due, and keeps documentation of the charge ready to give the contracting officer on request. The payment is not a Government payment, so the Prompt Payment Act does not apply to it. If the System for Award Management shows the contractor has delinquent debt under the Treasury Offset Program, the card cannot be used as the payment method for that period. If the contracting officer has notified the contractor that the card is no longer authorized, the contractor follows the contracting officer's instructions for its payment requests during that period. If the System for Award Management later shows no delinquent debt, the contractor may ask the contracting officer to authorize the card again.

## Never confuse

Payments made or due by the third party under the clause differ from payments made by the Government rather than merely in who sends them: they are not payments made by the Government and are not subject to the Prompt Payment Act or any implementation thereof in the contract [@CL-CTF-2098], and if any payment is made under the third-party clause, no payment under the contract may be assigned under the provisions of the assignment of claims terms of the contract or the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 [@CL-CTF-2099]. For ordinary invoice payments, see [Prompt payment for invoices](ctf-prompt-payment.md), for the designated payment office under electronic funds transfer see [Payment by electronic funds transfer and the designated office for EFT information](ctf-eft-payment-clauses-and-designated-office.md), and for the card rules on electronic funds transfer, see [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](../financing/ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md); for assignment of claims conditions and policies, see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md).
