## Purpose

Shows how incentive fee payments are billed during contract performance, adjusted for cost performance, and finalized upon contract completion.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0267] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-10 [@CL-CTF-0267] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0267] |
| Applies to | Cost-plus-incentive-fee contracts other than facilities contracts [@CL-CTF-0267] |

Normally fee is paid as specified in the Schedule, but when the contracting officer considers that performance or cost indicates the contractor will not achieve target, the Government pays on the basis of an appropriate lesser fee [@CL-CTF-0268]. The contracting officer withholds a reserve not to exceed 15 percent of the total incentive fee or $100,000, whichever is less, and the total allowable cost and adjusted fee are evidenced by a contract modification [@CL-CTF-0269; @CL-CTF-0270].

## How it works

The fee payable is the target fee increased by the contractor's participation share for every dollar that total allowable cost is less than target cost [@CL-CTF-0534], or decreased by that share for every dollar that it exceeds target cost [@CL-CTF-0535], and in no event more than the maximum or less than the minimum percentage of target cost stated in the clause [@CL-CTF-0536]. Certain allowable costs, such as costs arising from causes covered by the Excusable Delays clause, are left out of total allowable cost for fee adjustment [@CL-CTF-0537]. During performance, the Government pays an appropriate lesser fee when the contracting officer considers that performance or cost indicates the contractor will not achieve target [@CL-CTF-0268], and may pay an appropriate higher fee, at the sole discretion of the contracting officer, only when the contractor demonstrates that performance or cost clearly indicates it will earn a fee significantly above target [@CL-CTF-0533]. The contracting officer withholds a reserve not to exceed 15 percent of the total incentive fee or $100,000, whichever is less [@CL-CTF-0269], and releases 75 percent of all fee withholds after receipt of an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal covering the year of physical completion [@CL-CTF-0538], provided the contractor has satisfied all other contract terms and conditions, including the final patent and royalty reports, and is not delinquent in submitting final vouchers on prior years' settlements [@CL-CTF-0539]. Final fee settlement is executed by a bilateral contract modification signed by both parties [@CL-CTF-0270].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-incentive-fee contract establishes a target cost of 1,000,000, a target fee of 80,000, and an incentive sharing ratio of 20 cents on the dollar for cost overruns. Incurred allowable costs at completion equal 1,100,000 (an overrun of 100,000). The fee is decreased by 20,000, yielding a final adjusted fee of 60,000 (assuming the contract's minimum fee is no more than 60,000), which is documented in a bilateral modification.

## Never confuse

An incentive fee is adjusted from the target fee according to how total allowable cost compares with target cost, and the Government may pay a lesser or higher fee based on performance or cost indications [@CL-CTF-0534; @CL-CTF-0535; @CL-CTF-0268; @CL-CTF-0533], whereas a fixed fee is the fee specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-0532]. See [Contract types and what they mean for finance](ctf-contract-types.md).
