## Purpose

Shows how incentive fee payments are billed during contract performance, adjusted for cost performance, and finalized upon contract completion.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0267] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-10 [@CL-CTF-0267] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-10 [@CL-CTF-0298] |
| Edition date | Jun 2011 [@CL-CTF-0298] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0267] |
| Applies to | Cost-plus-incentive-fee contracts other than facilities contracts [@CL-CTF-0267] |

Normally, the Government pays the fee to the Contractor as specified in the Schedule, but when the Contracting Officer considers that performance or cost indicates that the Contractor will not achieve target, the Government must pay on the basis of an appropriate lesser fee [@CL-CTF-9617; @CL-CTF-0268]. Payment of the incentive fee is made as specified in the Schedule, provided that the Contracting Officer withholds a reserve not to exceed 15 percent of the total incentive fee or $100,000, whichever is less, to protect the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0269], and the total allowable cost and the adjusted fee determined as provided in the clause are evidenced by a modification to the contract signed by the Contractor and the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0270].

## How it works

The fee payable under the contract is the target fee increased by the Contractor's participation in cents for every dollar that total allowable cost is less than target cost [@CL-CTF-0534], or decreased by the Contractor's participation in cents for every dollar that total allowable cost exceeds target cost [@CL-CTF-0535], and in no event may the fee be greater than the maximum percentage or less than the minimum percentage of target cost inserted by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0536]. For the purpose of fee adjustment, total allowable cost does not include allowable costs arising out of any of the causes covered by the Excusable Delays clause to the extent that they are beyond the control and without the fault or negligence of the Contractor or any subcontractor [@CL-CTF-0537]. Normally, the Government pays the fee to the Contractor as specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-9617], but when the Contracting Officer considers that performance or cost indicates that the Contractor will not achieve target, the Government must pay on the basis of an appropriate lesser fee [@CL-CTF-0268], and when the Contractor demonstrates that performance or cost clearly indicates that the Contractor will earn a fee significantly above the target fee, the Government may, at the sole discretion of the Contracting Officer, pay on the basis of an appropriate higher fee [@CL-CTF-0533]. Payment of the incentive fee is made as specified in the Schedule, provided that the Contracting Officer withholds a reserve not to exceed 15 percent of the total incentive fee or $100,000, whichever is less, to protect the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0269], and the Contracting Officer must release 75 percent of all fee withholds under the contract after receipt of an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal covering the year of physical completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-0538], provided the Contractor has satisfied all other contract terms and conditions, including the submission of the final patent and royalty reports, and is not delinquent in submitting final vouchers on prior years' settlements [@CL-CTF-0539]. The total allowable cost and the adjusted fee determined as provided in the clause must be evidenced by a modification to the contract signed by the Contractor and the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0270].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.216-10 provides that normally the Government pays the fee to the Contractor as specified in the Schedule, that when the Contracting Officer considers that performance or cost indicates that the Contractor will not achieve target the Government must pay on the basis of an appropriate lesser fee [@CL-CTF-0298], and that when the Contractor demonstrates that performance or cost clearly indicates that the Contractor will earn a fee significantly above the target fee, the Government may, at the sole discretion of the Contracting Officer, pay on the basis of an appropriate higher fee [@CL-CTF-0299]. It provides that payment of the incentive fee is made as specified in the Schedule, provided that the Contracting Officer withholds a reserve not to exceed 15 percent of the total incentive fee or $100,000, whichever is less, to protect the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0300], and that the Contracting Officer must release 75 percent of all fee withholds under the contract after receipt of an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal covering the year of physical completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-0327], provided the Contractor has satisfied all other contract terms and conditions, including the submission of the final patent and royalty reports, and is not delinquent in submitting final vouchers on prior years' settlements [@CL-CTF-0328]. It provides that the fee payable under the contract is the target fee increased by the Contractor's participation in cents for every dollar that total allowable cost is less than target cost [@CL-CTF-0329] or decreased by the Contractor's participation in cents for every dollar that total allowable cost exceeds target cost [@CL-CTF-0330], within the maximum and minimum percentages of target cost inserted by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0331], that for the purpose of fee adjustment total allowable cost does not include allowable costs arising out of any of the causes covered by the Excusable Delays clause to the extent that they are beyond the control and without the fault or negligence of the Contractor or any subcontractor [@CL-CTF-0332], and that the total allowable cost and the adjusted fee determined as provided in the clause are evidenced by a modification to the contract signed by the Contractor and the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0333]. RFO FAR 52.216-10 says to insert the clause as prescribed in 16.305(d) [@CL-CTF-0334], where the codified prescription directs insertion when a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract other than a facilities contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-0267].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-incentive-fee contract establishes a target cost of 1,000,000, a target fee of 80,000, and an incentive sharing ratio of 20 cents on the dollar for cost overruns. Incurred allowable costs at completion equal 1,100,000 (an overrun of 100,000). The fee is decreased by 20,000, yielding a final adjusted fee of 60,000 (assuming the contract's minimum fee is no more than 60,000), which is documented in a bilateral modification.

## Never confuse

An incentive fee is adjusted from the target fee according to how total allowable cost compares with target cost [@CL-CTF-0534; @CL-CTF-0535]; the Government pays on the basis of an appropriate lesser fee when the contracting officer considers that performance or cost indicates the contractor will not achieve target [@CL-CTF-0268], and may pay on the basis of an appropriate higher fee, at the contracting officer's sole discretion, when the contractor demonstrates that performance or cost clearly indicates it will earn a fee significantly above the target fee [@CL-CTF-0533], whereas under the Fixed Fee clause the Government pays the contractor for performing the contract the fixed fee specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-0532]. See [Contract types and what they mean for finance](ctf-contract-types.md).
