## Purpose

Shows when a late payment interest penalty is owed, where its computation comes from, the additional penalty, the disagreement exception, and which prompt payment clause is prescribed for which contract. The Prompt Payment clause itself is on the linked card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1342] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.907 and 32.908 [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1347] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.907 [@CL-CTF-4072; @CL-CTF-4073; @CL-CTF-4074; @CL-CTF-4075; @CL-CTF-4076; @CL-CTF-4077; @CL-CTF-4078; @CL-CTF-4079; @CL-CTF-4080; @CL-CTF-4081]; RFO FAR 32.908 [@CL-CTF-4082; @CL-CTF-4083; @CL-CTF-4084; @CL-CTF-4085; @CL-CTF-4086] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1342] |
| Applies to | Late invoice payments where the listed conditions are met, and contracts in which a prompt payment clause is prescribed [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1349] |

The designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, when all of the following conditions, if applicable, have been met [@CL-CTF-1342]. The first is that the designated billing office received a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-1342]. Another is that the Government processed a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment, with no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with any contract requirement [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1343]. Two more are that, for a final invoice, the payment amount is not subject to further contract settlement actions, and that the designated payment office paid the contractor after the due date [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1344]. The last listed condition is that, for interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services, payment is made more than 30 days after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1385].

The designated payment office will pay a penalty amount in addition to the interest penalty amount only if, among the listed conditions, the Government owes an interest penalty of 1 dollar or more [@CL-CTF-1386]. A further listed condition is that the payment office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-1395]. Another is that the contractor makes a written demand for the additional penalty, postmarked not later than 40 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-1387].

The payment office will not pay interest penalties if payment delays are due to disagreement between the Government and contractor concerning the payment amount, contract compliance, or amounts temporarily withheld or retained in accordance with the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-1345]. The Government and the contractor must resolve claims involving disputes, and any interest that may be payable, in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1389]. The temporary unavailability of funds to make a timely payment does not relieve an agency from the obligation to pay interest penalties [@CL-CTF-1388].

Three clauses are prescribed. The clause at 52.232-26 is inserted in solicitations and contracts that contain the clause at 52.232-10 [@CL-CTF-1347]. The clause at 52.232-27 is inserted in all solicitations and contracts for construction [@CL-CTF-1348]. The clause at 52.232-25 is prescribed for all other solicitations and contracts, except when the clause at 52.212-4 applies or when payment terms and late payment penalties are established by other governmental authority [@CL-CTF-1349]. If the contract is a cost-reimbursement contract for services, the clause at 52.232-25 is used with its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-1349; @CL-CTF-1390].

## How it works

The section on interest penalties says that the Government will compute interest penalties in accordance with OMB prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-1346]. The printed section does not itself state an interest rate. The sections also cover interest on an improperly taken discount, the form of the written demand and changes to clause periods; this card does not state those rules. See [Prompt payment for invoices](ctf-prompt-payment.md) for the clause, [Proper invoice content](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md) for what makes an invoice proper and [Prompt payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md) for the due dates.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.907(a) keeps the rule that the designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, when all the following conditions, if applicable, have been met, the first being that the designated billing office received a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-4072]. It keeps the conditions that the Government processed a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment and there was no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with any contract requirement [@CL-CTF-4073]; that, in the case of a final invoice, the payment amount is not subject to further contract settlement actions between the Government and the contractor, and the designated payment office paid the contractor after the due date [@CL-CTF-4074]; and that, in the case of interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services, payment is made more than 30 days after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-4075]. RFO FAR 32.907(c)(1) keeps the rule that the designated payment office will pay a penalty amount, in addition to the interest penalty amount, only if the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more [@CL-CTF-4076], the designated payment office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-4077], and the contractor makes a written demand to the designated payment office for additional penalty payment in accordance with paragraph (c)(2), postmarked not later than 40 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-4078]. It keeps the bar on interest: the payment office will not pay interest penalties if payment delays are due to disagreement between the Government and contractor concerning the payment amount, contract compliance, or amounts temporarily withheld or retained in accordance with the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-4079]. It keeps the Disputes clause rule [@CL-CTF-4080], and it keeps both the computation of interest penalties in accordance with OMB prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 and the rule that the temporary unavailability of funds to make a timely payment does not relieve an agency from the obligation to pay interest penalties [@CL-CTF-4081]. RFO FAR 32.908 keeps the clause prescriptions: the clause at 52.232-26 in solicitations and contracts that contain the clause at 52.232-10 [@CL-CTF-4082], and the clause at 52.232-27 in all solicitations and contracts for construction [@CL-CTF-4083]. For 52.232-25, it keeps insertion in all other solicitations and contracts, except when the clause at 52.212-4 applies [@CL-CTF-4084] or when payment terms and late payment penalties are established by other governmental authority [@CL-CTF-4085], and it keeps use of the clause with its Alternate I if the contract is a cost-reimbursement contract for services [@CL-CTF-4086].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A proper invoice for 80,000 is received, the receiving report is processed with no disagreement, and the payment office pays 10 days after the due date. It is not a final invoice subject to settlement.

Answer: the listed conditions are met, so an interest penalty is owed and paid automatically without a request. It is computed under the OMB regulation, and section 32.907 gives no rate to apply here.

## Never confuse

An interest penalty for a late invoice payment is not the same as a withholding dispute. If the delay is due to disagreement over the payment amount, contract compliance, or amounts withheld under the contract, no interest penalty is paid [@CL-CTF-1345]. The additional penalty amount is separate from the interest penalty and is paid only on a written demand and the other listed conditions [@CL-CTF-1386; @CL-CTF-1387]. The clause prescriptions say which clause goes into which solicitation or contract [@CL-CTF-1347; @CL-CTF-1348; @CL-CTF-1349], while the interest conditions say when a late payment draws a penalty [@CL-CTF-1342].
