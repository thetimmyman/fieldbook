## Purpose

Shows when a late payment interest penalty is owed, where its computation comes from, the additional penalty, the disagreement exception, and which prompt payment clause is prescribed for which contract. The Prompt Payment clause itself is on the linked card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1342] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.907 and 32.908 [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1347] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.907 [@CL-CTF-4072; @CL-CTF-4073; @CL-CTF-4074; @CL-CTF-4075; @CL-CTF-4076; @CL-CTF-4077; @CL-CTF-4078; @CL-CTF-4079; @CL-CTF-4080; @CL-CTF-4081]; RFO FAR 32.908 [@CL-CTF-4082; @CL-CTF-4083; @CL-CTF-4084; @CL-CTF-4085; @CL-CTF-4086] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1342] |
| Applies to | Late invoice payments where the listed conditions are met, and contracts in which a prompt payment clause is prescribed [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1349] |

The designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, when all of the following conditions, if applicable, have been met [@CL-CTF-1342]: the designated billing office received a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-1342]; the Government processed a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment, and there was no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with any contract requirement [@CL-CTF-1343]; in the case of a final invoice, the payment amount is not subject to further contract settlement actions between the Government and the contractor [@CL-CTF-1344]; the designated payment office paid the contractor after the due date [@CL-CTF-1344]; and in the case of interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services, payment is made more than 30 days after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-1385].

The designated payment office will pay a penalty amount, in addition to the interest penalty amount, only if the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more [@CL-CTF-1386], the designated payment office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-1395], and the contractor makes a written demand to the designated payment office for additional penalty payment in accordance with paragraph (c)(2) of the section, postmarked not later than 40 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-1387].

The payment office will not pay interest penalties if payment delays are due to disagreement between the Government and contractor concerning the payment amount, contract compliance, or amounts temporarily withheld or retained in accordance with the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-1345]. The Government and the contractor must resolve claims involving disputes, and any interest that may be payable, in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1389]. The Government will compute interest penalties in accordance with OMB prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-1346]. The temporary unavailability of funds to make a timely payment does not relieve an agency from the obligation to pay interest penalties [@CL-CTF-1388].

## How it works

Under FAR 32.908, the clause at 52.232-26, Prompt Payment for Fixed-Price Architect-Engineer Contracts, is inserted in solicitations and contracts that contain the clause at 52.232-10, Payments Under Fixed-Price Architect-Engineer Contracts [@CL-CTF-1347]. The clause at 52.232-27, Prompt Payment for Construction Contracts, is inserted in all solicitations and contracts for construction (see part 36) [@CL-CTF-1348]. The clause at 52.232-25, Prompt Payment, is inserted in all other solicitations and contracts, except when the clause at 52.212-4, Contract Terms and Conditions—Commercial Products and Commercial Services, applies, or when payment terms and late payment penalties are established by other governmental authority [@CL-CTF-1349]. If the contract is a cost-reimbursement contract for services, the clause at 52.232-25 is used with its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-1349; @CL-CTF-1390]. For the Prompt Payment clause, see [Prompt payment for invoices](ctf-prompt-payment.md); for what makes an invoice proper, see [Proper invoice content](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md); and for due dates, see [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 32 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 32.907(a), the designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, when all the following conditions, if applicable, have been met: the designated billing office received a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-4072]; the Government processed a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment, and there was no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with any contract requirement [@CL-CTF-4073]; in the case of a final invoice, the payment amount is not subject to further contract settlement actions between the Government and the contractor, and the designated payment office paid the contractor after the due date [@CL-CTF-4074]; and in the case of interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services, payment is made more than 30 days after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-4075], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1342; @CL-CTF-1343; @CL-CTF-1344; @CL-CTF-1385]. Under RFO FAR 32.907(c)(1), the designated payment office will pay a penalty amount, in addition to the interest penalty amount, only if the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more [@CL-CTF-4076], the designated payment office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-4077], and the contractor makes a written demand to the designated payment office for additional penalty payment in accordance with paragraph (c)(2) of the section, postmarked not later than 40 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-4078], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1386; @CL-CTF-1395; @CL-CTF-1387]. Under RFO FAR 32.907(d), the payment office will not pay interest penalties if payment delays are due to disagreement between the Government and contractor concerning the payment amount, contract compliance, or amounts temporarily withheld or retained in accordance with the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-4079], and the Government and the contractor must resolve claims involving disputes, and any interest that may be payable, in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-4080], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1345; @CL-CTF-1389]. Under RFO FAR 32.907(e) and (f), the Government will compute interest penalties in accordance with OMB prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315, and the temporary unavailability of funds to make a timely payment does not relieve an agency from the obligation to pay interest penalties [@CL-CTF-4081], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1346; @CL-CTF-1388]. Under RFO FAR 32.908, the clause at 52.232-26 is inserted in solicitations and contracts that contain the clause at 52.232-10 [@CL-CTF-4082], the clause at 52.232-27 is inserted in all solicitations and contracts for construction (see part 36) [@CL-CTF-4083], the clause at 52.232-25 is inserted in all other solicitations and contracts, except when the clause at 52.212-4 applies or when payment terms and late payment penalties are established by other governmental authority (e.g., tariffs) [@CL-CTF-4084; @CL-CTF-4085], and if the contract is a cost-reimbursement contract for services, the clause is used with its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-4086], matching the codified section [@CL-CTF-1347; @CL-CTF-1348; @CL-CTF-1349; @CL-CTF-1390].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A proper invoice for 80,000 is received, the receiving report is processed with no disagreement, and the payment office pays 10 days after the due date. It is not a final invoice subject to settlement.

Answer: the listed conditions are met, so an interest penalty is owed and paid automatically without a request. It is computed under the OMB regulation, and section 32.907 gives no rate to apply here.

## Never confuse

The payment office will not pay interest penalties if payment delays are due to disagreement between the Government and contractor concerning the payment amount, contract compliance, or amounts temporarily withheld or retained in accordance with the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-1345], and the Government and the contractor must resolve claims involving disputes, and any interest that may be payable, in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1389]. The designated payment office will pay an additional penalty amount, in addition to the interest penalty amount, only if the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more, the designated payment office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid, and the contractor makes a written demand to the designated payment office in accordance with paragraph (c)(2) of the section, postmarked not later than 40 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-1386; @CL-CTF-1395; @CL-CTF-1387].
