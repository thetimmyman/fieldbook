## Purpose

Shows which contractors the DoD rate clause changes the progress payment and liquidation rates for, and what the clause on foreign military sales requires of progress payment requests.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1810] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7004 [@CL-CTF-1810]; DFARS 252.232-7002 [@CL-CTF-1811] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1810] |
| Applies to | Small business concerns, for the rate change [@CL-CTF-1810]; contracts that include foreign military sales requirements, for the separate requests [@CL-CTF-1811] |

If the Contractor is a small business concern, the Progress Payments clause is modified to change each mention of the progress payment rate and liquidation rate to 90 percent, excepting paragraph (k), Limitations on Undefinitized Contract Actions [@CL-CTF-1810]. The clause changes the rates only for small business concerns [@CL-CTF-1810]. The DFARS directs use of the rate clause as prescribed in DFARS 232.502-4-70(b) and of the foreign military sales clause as prescribed in DFARS 232.502-4-70(a) [@CL-CTF-1833; @CL-CTF-1834]. The text printed for this card does not state the customary rate for other contractors; the customary rates are on [Customary progress payment rates (DoD)](../financing/ctf-progress-payments.md) and [Progress payment rates and unusual progress payments](../financing/ctf-progress-payment-rates.md).

If a contract includes foreign military sales (FMS) requirements, the Contractor shall submit separate progress payment requests for the FMS and U.S. line items in the contract [@CL-CTF-1811]. One listed duty is to submit a supporting schedule showing the amount of each request distributed to each country's requirements [@CL-CTF-1812]. One listed duty is to identify in each progress payment request the contract requirements to which it applies, that is, FMS or U.S. [@CL-CTF-1813]. One listed duty is to calculate each request on the basis of the prices, costs (including costs to complete), subcontract financing, and progress payment liquidations of the contract requirements to which it applies [@CL-CTF-1814]. One listed duty is to distribute costs among the countries in a manner acceptable to the Administrative Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1815]. These duties are listed under one lead-in, which applies when the contract includes foreign military sales requirements [@CL-CTF-1811].

## How it works

For small business concerns, the rate clause changes each mention of the progress payment rate and the liquidation rate in the Progress Payments clause to the one stated rate, except in the paragraph on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-1810]. The FMS clause keeps the FMS and domestic work apart in the paperwork: separate requests, a schedule by country, a label on each request, a calculation limited to the requirements the request covers, and cost distribution that the Administrative Contracting Officer accepts [@CL-CTF-1811; @CL-CTF-1812; @CL-CTF-1813; @CL-CTF-1814; @CL-CTF-1815]. The printed text here gives no payment limit of its own. The liquidation method is on [Progress payment liquidation rates](../financing/ctf-progress-payment-liquidation-rates.md). Performance-based payments are a separate financing method, covered on [Performance-based payments representation](ctf-dfars-pbp-representation.md), and the advance payment pool clause is on [Mandatory purchase card payment and the advance payment pool clause](ctf-dfars-purchase-card-and-advance-payment-pool.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A small business holds one contract with FMS and domestic line items. Eligible costs on the FMS items are 100,000 and on the domestic items are 50,000. The contractor sends two requests, one for each group, with a schedule that shows the FMS amount by country. If the 90 percent rate is applied to those costs and no other limit in the Progress Payments clause bites, the FMS request is 90,000 and the domestic request is 45,000.

Answer: the two requests stay separate and each is calculated only from its own group of costs.

## Never confuse

The small business rate in this clause is a change to the Progress Payments clause and is not the customary rate for every contractor. The clause changes the liquidation rate too, except for mentions in the paragraph on limitations on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-1810]. Separate FMS requests are a billing and accounting separation within one contract, not a second contract.
