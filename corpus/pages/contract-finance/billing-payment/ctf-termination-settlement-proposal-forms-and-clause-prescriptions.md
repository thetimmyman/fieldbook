## Purpose

Which standard form carries each kind of settlement proposal, what a contractor provides when it asks for authority to settle subcontractor settlement proposals, and which other termination clauses the FAR prescribes for personal services, cost-reimbursement, time-and-material and labor-hour contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3148] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.602-1, FAR 49.605 and FAR 49.505 [@CL-CTF-3148; @CL-CTF-3152; @CL-CTF-3153] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3148] |
| Applies to | Settlement proposals after a termination, requests to settle subcontractor settlement proposals, and the termination clauses prescribed for personal services and excusable delays [@CL-CTF-3148; @CL-CTF-3152; @CL-CTF-3153] |

Standard Form 1435, Settlement Proposal (Inventory Basis), is used to submit settlement proposals resulting from the termination of fixed-price contracts if the proposals are computed on an inventory basis [@CL-CTF-3148]. Standard Form 1436, Settlement Proposal (Total Cost Basis), is used for fixed-price terminations if the proposals are computed on a total cost basis [@CL-CTF-3149]. When the inventory basis is not practicable or would unduly delay settlement, the total-cost basis may be used if the termination contracting officer approves it in advance [@CL-CTF-3338]. Standard Form 1437, Settlement Proposal for Cost-Reimbursement Type Contracts, is used for terminations of cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-CTF-3150]. Standard Form 1438, Settlement Proposal (Short Form), is used for fixed-price terminations if the total proposal is less than $10,000 [@CL-CTF-3151]. The contractor may use it for a total proposal under $10,000 unless the termination contracting officer instructs otherwise [@CL-CTF-3336], and proposals that would normally be one settlement proposal should be consolidated whenever possible and not divided to bring them below $10,000 [@CL-CTF-3337].

## How it works

Contractors requesting authority to settle subcontractor settlement proposals furnish applicable information from the list in FAR 49.605 and any additional information required by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-3152]. The list covers:

- The contractor's name and principal office address, the name and location of the plant divisions for which authorization is requested, and an explanation of the necessity and justification for the authorization requested [@CL-CTF-3297].
- A full description of the applicant's organization for handling terminations, including the names of the officials in charge of processing and settling proposals [@CL-CTF-3298].
- The number and dollar amount, estimated if necessary, of uncompleted contracts with Government agencies and the percentage applicable to each agency [@CL-CTF-3299].
- The number and dollar amount, estimated if necessary, of uncompleted subcontracts under Government contracts and the percentage applicable to each agency [@CL-CTF-3300].
- The extent of the applicant's experience in termination matters, including the handling of subcontractors' proposals, and the approximate amount and general nature of the applicant's terminations currently in process [@CL-CTF-3301].
- A statement that no other application has been made for any division of the applicant's plant covered by the application or, if one has been made, a full statement of the facts, and the limit of authorization requested [@CL-CTF-3302].

The contracting officer inserts the clause at FAR 52.249-12, Termination (Personal Services), in solicitations and contracts for personal services [@CL-CTF-3153]. The contracting officer inserts the clause at FAR 52.249-14, Excusable Delays, in solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, construction, and research and development on a fee basis, when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-3154]. The contracting officer also inserts the Excusable Delays clause in time-and-material contracts and labor-hour contracts [@CL-CTF-3303]. The cost-reimbursement termination clause is FAR 52.249-6, inserted as prescribed in FAR 49.503(a)(1) [@CL-CTF-0612].

The FAR does not prescribe a clause for the cancellation or termination of orders under communication service contracts with common carriers because of special agency requirements that apply to these services [@CL-CTF-3155]. An appropriate clause for those orders is to be prescribed at agency level, within the agencies contracting for these services [@CL-CTF-3304].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price contract is terminated and the contractor computes a 250,000 proposal on an inventory basis, so it uses Standard Form 1435 [@CL-CTF-3148]. A second fixed-price contract is terminated, inventory-basis settlement is not practicable, and the termination contracting officer approves the total cost basis in advance, so the contractor computes a 400,000 proposal on that basis and uses Standard Form 1436 [@CL-CTF-3338; @CL-CTF-3149]. A third fixed-price termination has a single total proposal of 8,000, which is less than 10,000 and has not been divided to get below that figure, so the contractor uses the short form, Standard Form 1438, unless the termination contracting officer instructs otherwise [@CL-CTF-3151; @CL-CTF-3336; @CL-CTF-3337]. A terminated cost-reimbursement contract uses Standard Form 1437 [@CL-CTF-3150].

A prime contractor that wants authority to settle its subcontractors' proposals sends a request that lists its organization for handling terminations, its experience, its terminations in process and the limit of authorization requested, and any further information the contracting officer requires [@CL-CTF-3298; @CL-CTF-3301; @CL-CTF-3302; @CL-CTF-3152].

For clauses, a personal services contract takes the personal services termination clause [@CL-CTF-3153]. A time-and-material contract also takes the Excusable Delays clause [@CL-CTF-3303]. For orders under a communication service contract with a common carrier, the FAR prescribes no clause, and an appropriate clause is prescribed at agency level [@CL-CTF-3155; @CL-CTF-3304].

## Never confuse

A settlement proposal form is not an agreement format. The form carries the contractor's proposal [@CL-CTF-3148], and the agreement that settles it uses its own format, such as those on [the cost-reimbursement settlement formats card](ctf-cost-reimbursement-settlement-agreement-formats.md).

The Excusable Delays clause is not the personal services termination clause. They are separate clauses prescribed for different contract types [@CL-CTF-3153; @CL-CTF-3154].

A request for authority to settle subcontractor proposals is not itself the authority. The contracting officer may require more information with the request [@CL-CTF-3152]; the authorization and award mechanics are on [the subcontract settlements card](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-subcontract-settlements-authorization-and-awards.md). Submission of fixed-price proposals is on [the fixed-price settlement proposal card](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md), personal services terminations are on [the architect-engineer and personal services card](ctf-termination-architect-engineer-and-personal-services.md), and cost-reimbursement termination is on [the cost-reimbursement termination settlement card](ctf-termination-cost-reimbursement-settlement.md).
