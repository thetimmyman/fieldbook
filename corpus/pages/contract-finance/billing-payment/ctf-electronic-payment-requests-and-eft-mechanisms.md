## Purpose

Explains how DoD processes payment requests electronically, what the contracting officer does when an exception to electronic submission applies, and which electronic funds transfer mechanisms the EFT clauses reach.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS and codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3381; @CL-CTF-3387] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.7003 and FAR 32.1106 [@CL-CTF-3381; @CL-CTF-3387] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1106 [@CL-CTF-4587; @CL-CTF-4589] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3381] |
| Applies to | Payment requests and receiving reports that DoD receives in electronic form, and payments under the EFT clauses [@CL-CTF-3381; @CL-CTF-3387] |

DoD officials receiving payment requests in electronic form must process them in electronic form, and the WAWF system provides the method to process payment requests and receiving reports electronically [@CL-CTF-3381]. Documents necessary for payment, such as receiving reports, invoice approvals, contracts, contract modifications, and required certifications, must also be processed in electronic form [@CL-CTF-3382]. Scanned documents and other commonly used file formats are acceptable only for processing supporting documentation [@CL-CTF-3383].

If one of the exceptions to submission in electronic form at 232.7002(a) applies, the contracting officer must consult the payment office and the contract administration office regarding the alternative method to be used for submission of payment requests or receiving reports [@CL-CTF-3384]. The contracting officer must also provide procedures for submission of invoices by nonelectronic means in the contract administration data section of the contract or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-3385]. If submission by nonelectronic means is temporary, the procedures should specify the time period for which they apply [@CL-CTF-3386]. The exceptions themselves are on [DoD payment applicability and electronic submission](ctf-dod-payment-applicability-and-electronic-submission.md).

## How it works

The EFT clauses at FAR 52.232-33 and 52.232-34 are designed for use with the domestic United States banking system, using United States currency, and only the Automated Clearing House and Fedwire Transfer System mechanisms of EFT [@CL-CTF-3387]. The head of an agency may authorize any other EFT mechanism for domestic EFT with the concurrence of the office or agency responsible for making payments [@CL-CTF-3388].

The Government must provide payment by other than EFT for payments received by or on behalf of the contractor outside the United States and Puerto Rico, or for contracts paid in other than United States currency [@CL-CTF-3389]. The head of an agency may still authorize appropriate use of EFT for those payments, with the concurrence of the office or agency responsible for making payments, if either of two grounds holds: the political, financial, and communications infrastructure in a foreign country supports payment by EFT [@CL-CTF-3390], or payments of other than United States currency may be made safely [@CL-CTF-3419].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified FAR part 32 text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.1106 keeps the FAR EFT mechanisms rule described above [@CL-CTF-4587; @CL-CTF-4588; @CL-CTF-4589; @CL-CTF-4590; @CL-CTF-4591]. The EFT clauses at FAR 52.232-33 and 52.232-34 are designed for use with the domestic United States banking system, using United States currency, and only the specified mechanisms, the Automated Clearing House and the Fedwire Transfer System [@CL-CTF-4587]. The head of an agency may authorize the use of any other EFT mechanism for domestic EFT with the concurrence of the office or agency responsible for making payments [@CL-CTF-4588]. The Government must provide payment by other than EFT for payments received by or on behalf of the contractor outside the United States and Puerto Rico or for contracts paid in other than United States currency [@CL-CTF-4589]. The head of an agency may still authorize appropriate use of EFT, with the concurrence of the office or agency responsible for making payments, if the political, financial, and communications infrastructure in a foreign country supports payment by EFT [@CL-CTF-4590], or if payments of other than United States currency may be made safely [@CL-CTF-4591].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD buyer receives 40 invoices through WAWF in a month, and one supplier also sends a scanned invoice as a file. The 40 are processed in electronic form [@CL-CTF-3381]. The scanned file is acceptable only as supporting documentation, so it cannot serve as the payment request itself [@CL-CTF-3383].

A contract is performed where internet connectivity is not available, so contractor submission of electronic payment requests is not feasible, which is one of the listed exceptions [@CL-CTF-1442]. The contracting officer consults the payment office and the contract administration office on the alternative method and states the nonelectronic invoicing procedures in the contract administration data section [@CL-CTF-3384; @CL-CTF-3385]. If the arrangement is temporary, the procedures should name the period for which they apply [@CL-CTF-3386].

A contract is paid in euros. The Government must provide payment by other than EFT [@CL-CTF-3389]. The head of the agency could authorize EFT, with the paying office's concurrence, if euros can be paid safely [@CL-CTF-3419].

## Never confuse

Electronic submission is not electronic payment. A request can be submitted and processed in electronic form [@CL-CTF-3381] and still be paid by other than EFT, where the payment is received outside the United States and Puerto Rico or the contract is paid in other than United States currency [@CL-CTF-3389]. For the clauses and the rules on when EFT applies, see [DoD electronic payment request clause](../financing/ctf-dod-electronic-payment-request-clause.md), [Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md), [EFT payment clauses and designated office](ctf-eft-payment-clauses-and-designated-office.md) and [EFT applicability and purchase card payment](../financing/ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md).
