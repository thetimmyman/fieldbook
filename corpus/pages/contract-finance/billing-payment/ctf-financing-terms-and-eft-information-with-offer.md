## Purpose

Shows what an offeror may propose for contract financing and what electronic funds transfer information it must submit with its offer.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1682] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-31 [@CL-CTF-1678]; FAR 52.232-38 [@CL-CTF-1682] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-31 [@CL-CTF-0250]; RFO FAR 52.232-38 [@CL-CTF-0287] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1682] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to a solicitation that contains the provisions [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1682] |

The offeror is invited to propose terms under which the Government shall make contract financing payments during contract performance, and the proposed financing terms shall be a factor in the evaluation of the proposal [@CL-CTF-1678]. The proposed financing shall not be acceptable if it does not conform to the listed limitations, the first being that delivery payments are made only for supplies delivered and accepted, or services rendered and accepted, in accordance with the payment terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-1679]. Another listed limitation is that contract financing payments shall not exceed 15 percent of the contract price in advance of any performance of work under the contract [@CL-CTF-1680]. The offeror's proposal of financing terms shall include two listed items [@CL-CTF-9613]: the proposed contractual language describing the contract financing [@CL-CTF-1681], and a listing of the earliest date and greatest amount at which each contract financing payment may be payable and the amount of each delivery payment [@CL-CTF-9614]. Two further listed limitations are that the terms and conditions of the contract financing must be appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace and must be in the best interests of the United States [@CL-CTF-1679; @CL-CTF-1712]. Any resulting contract shall provide that no contract financing payment shall be made at any earlier date or in a greater amount than shown in the offeror's listing [@CL-CTF-1713].

The offeror shall provide with its offer the listed information required to make payment by electronic funds transfer under any contract that results from the solicitation [@CL-CTF-1682]. The listed information includes the solicitation number, the offeror's name and remittance address as stated in the offer, and the signature, title, and telephone number of the official authorized to provide it [@CL-CTF-1683]. It also includes the name, address, and 9-digit Routing Transit Number of the offeror's financial agent, the account number and type of account, and, if applicable, the Fedwire telegraphic abbreviation of the financial agent [@CL-CTF-1684]. If applicable, the offeror shall also provide the name, address, telegraphic abbreviation, and 9-digit Routing Transit Number of the correspondent financial institution receiving the wire transfer payment [@CL-CTF-3024], if the offeror's financial agent is not directly on-line to the Fedwire and, therefore, not the receiver of the wire transfer payment [@CL-CTF-3025]. See [EFT solicitation provision and contract clauses](../financing/ctf-eft-solicitation-provision-and-clauses.md) for the EFT solicitation provision in general.

## How it works

The offeror may propose financing terms, and those terms count in the evaluation of its proposal [@CL-CTF-1678]. The offeror's proposed financing shall not be acceptable if it does not conform to the listed limitations [@CL-CTF-1679; @CL-CTF-1680; @CL-CTF-1712]. The offeror shall provide, with its offer, the listed information that is required to make payment by electronic funds transfer under any contract that results from the solicitation [@CL-CTF-1682].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO provision at FAR 52.232-31 keeps the invitation to propose terms under which the Government makes contract financing payments during contract performance, with the proposed financing terms a factor in the evaluation of the proposal [@CL-CTF-0250] [@CL-CTF-1678]. Proposed financing is not acceptable if it does not conform to the listed limitations, the first being that delivery payments are made only for supplies delivered and accepted, or services rendered and accepted, in accordance with the payment terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-0282] [@CL-CTF-1679]. Another limitation is that contract financing payments may not exceed 15 percent of the contract price in advance of any performance of work under the contract [@CL-CTF-0283] [@CL-CTF-1680]. The terms must be appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace and in the best interests of the United States [@CL-CTF-0284] [@CL-CTF-1712]. The RFO provision says the offeror's proposal of financing terms shall include the proposed contractual language and a listing of the earliest date and greatest amount at which each contract financing payment may be payable and the amount of each delivery payment [@CL-CTF-9615] [@CL-CTF-0285] [@CL-CTF-9613] [@CL-CTF-1681] [@CL-CTF-9614]. Any resulting contract provides that no contract financing payment is made at an earlier date or in a greater amount than shown in the listing [@CL-CTF-0286] [@CL-CTF-1713].

The RFO provision at FAR 52.232-38 keeps the requirement that the offeror provide, with its offer, the listed information required to make payment by EFT under any resulting contract [@CL-CTF-0287] [@CL-CTF-1682]. The listed information includes the solicitation number, the offeror's name and remittance address as stated in the offer, and the signature, title and telephone number of the official authorized to provide it [@CL-CTF-0288] [@CL-CTF-1683], and the name, address and 9-digit Routing Transit Number of the financial agent, the account number and type of account and, if applicable, the Fedwire telegraphic abbreviation [@CL-CTF-0289] [@CL-CTF-1684].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes that a financing payment of 20 percent of a 1,000,000 contract price be payable before any work starts. The limit is 15 percent, so the proposal is not acceptable as written. The most that could be paid before performance starts is 150,000. With its offer, the same bidder also sends the solicitation number, remittance address, routing number and account number.

## Never confuse

The financing terms proposed by the offeror shall be a factor in the evaluation of the offeror's proposal, and the proposed financing shall not be acceptable if it does not conform to the listed limitations [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1679]. The offeror shall provide the EFT information with its offer, for payment under any contract that results from the solicitation [@CL-CTF-1682].

## Company calibration

Confirm which financing terms the company proposes in offers and who prepares and signs the EFT submission. [@Q-026]
