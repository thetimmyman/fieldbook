## Purpose

Shows what an offeror may propose for contract financing and what electronic funds transfer information it must submit with its offer.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1682] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-31 [@CL-CTF-1678]; FAR 52.232-38 [@CL-CTF-1682] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1682] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to a solicitation that contains the provisions [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1682] |

The offeror is invited to propose terms under which the Government shall make contract financing payments during contract performance, and the proposed financing terms shall be a factor in the evaluation of the proposal [@CL-CTF-1678]. The proposed financing shall not be acceptable if it does not conform to the listed limitations, the first being that delivery payments are made only for supplies delivered and accepted, or services rendered and accepted, in accordance with the payment terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-1679]. Another listed limitation is that contract financing payments shall not exceed 15 percent of the contract price in advance of any performance of work under the contract [@CL-CTF-1680]. Two listed items for the proposal are the proposed contractual language describing the contract financing and a listing of the earliest date and greatest amount at which each contract financing payment may be payable [@CL-CTF-1681]. Two further listed limitations are that the terms and conditions of the contract financing must be appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace and must be in the best interests of the United States [@CL-CTF-1679; @CL-CTF-1712]. Any resulting contract shall provide that no contract financing payment shall be made at any earlier date or in a greater amount than shown in the offeror's listing [@CL-CTF-1713].

The offeror shall provide with its offer the listed information required to make payment by electronic funds transfer under any contract that results from the solicitation [@CL-CTF-1682]. The listed information includes the solicitation number, the offeror's name and remittance address as stated in the offer, and the signature, title, and telephone number of the official authorized to provide it [@CL-CTF-1683]. It also includes the name, address, and 9-digit Routing Transit Number of the offeror's financial agent, the account number and type of account, and, if applicable, the Fedwire telegraphic abbreviation of the financial agent [@CL-CTF-1684]. The provision lists a further item for a correspondent financial institution that this card does not state. See [EFT solicitation provision and contract clauses](../financing/ctf-eft-solicitation-provision-and-clauses.md) for the EFT solicitation provision in general.

## How it works

The offeror may propose financing terms, and those terms count in the evaluation of its proposal [@CL-CTF-1678]. A proposal whose financing does not meet the listed limitations is not acceptable [@CL-CTF-1679; @CL-CTF-1712]. The EFT information is submitted with the offer, so that payment can be made by electronic funds transfer under any resulting contract [@CL-CTF-1682].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes that a financing payment of 20 percent of a 1,000,000 contract price be payable before any work starts. The limit is 15 percent, so the proposal is not acceptable as written. The most that could be paid before performance starts is 150,000. With its offer, the same bidder also sends the solicitation number, remittance address, routing number and account number.

## Never confuse

The invitation to propose financing terms is not a promise of the financing: the proposed terms are an evaluation factor, and they must conform to the listed limitations [@CL-CTF-1678; @CL-CTF-1679]. The EFT submission is made with the offer, before award, and covers payment under any resulting contract [@CL-CTF-1682].

## Company calibration

Confirm which financing terms the company proposes in offers and who prepares and signs the EFT submission. [@Q-026]
