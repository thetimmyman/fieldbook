## Purpose

Shows when the Government may exercise the foreign military sales option and the surge option, how the surge option is scheduled and priced, and what the contractor must do after exercise.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1926; @CL-CTF-1928] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.217-7000 and DFARS 252.217-7001 [@CL-CTF-1926; @CL-CTF-1928] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1926] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the foreign military sales option clause or the surge option clause [@CL-CTF-1926; @CL-CTF-1928] |

The Government may exercise the option or options of the contract to fulfill foreign military sales commitments [@CL-CTF-1926]. In the basic clause, paragraph (b) states the commitments by an inserted country name and applicable contract line item number [@CL-CTF-1926]. Under Alternate I of that clause, the Government identifies the foreign country on the date the option is exercised so that any equitable adjustment attributable to foreign military sales can be negotiated, and failure to agree is treated as a dispute under the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1927].

The surge option lets the Government increase the quantity called for by no more than the percent or the quantity inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-1928], or accelerate the rate of delivery at a price or cost set before award or negotiated as the clause provides [@CL-CTF-1929]. The Contracting Officer may exercise it at any time before the Government accepts the final scheduled delivery [@CL-CTF-1930].

## How it works

The option delivery schedule depends on the Capabilities Analysis Plan (CAP). When a CAP is included in the contract, the option delivery schedule is the production rate provided with the Plan, and if the Plan was negotiated before contract award, the negotiated schedule is used [@CL-CTF-2000]. Where the contract has no CAP, the contractor must give the Contracting Officer a delivery schedule showing its maximum sustainable rate within 30 days of award [@CL-CTF-1933]. The contractor must not revise the option delivery schedule without the Contracting Officer's approval [@CL-CTF-1930].

The notice and modification under the surge option set a not-to-exceed price equal to the highest contract unit price or cost of the added or accelerated items as of the date of the notice [@CL-CTF-1931]. The contractor need not deliver at a rate greater than the maximum sustainable delivery rate under the schedule paragraph for contracts without a CAP, and exercise cannot extend delivery more than 24 months beyond the scheduled final delivery [@CL-CTF-1932; @CL-CTF-1933]. Unless the price was already agreed, the contractor must submit a cost or price proposal with a cost breakdown within 30 days of exercise [@CL-CTF-1934]. Failure to agree is a dispute concerning a question of fact [@CL-CTF-1935], and the contractor must keep performing the contract as modified while a claim is settled [@CL-CTF-1936].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has a surge option, and the Contracting Officer exercises it to add 10 units. The highest contract unit price is 500, so the notice sets a not-to-exceed price of 5,000. The price was not agreed in advance, so the contractor sends a cost breakdown within 30 days. The final price is then negotiated and may be below 5,000. The contractor keeps delivering the modified contract while any claim is settled.

## Never confuse

The foreign military sales option is not the surge option. The first lets the Government use the contract's existing options to fulfill foreign military sales commitments [@CL-CTF-1926]. The second adds quantity or speeds delivery under its own schedule and pricing steps [@CL-CTF-1928; @CL-CTF-1929]. The not-to-exceed price in the notice is a ceiling and not the agreed price, because the contractor still submits a proposal and the parties negotiate unless the price was previously agreed [@CL-CTF-1931; @CL-CTF-1934; @CL-CTF-1935]. For options in general, see [Exercise of options](../funding/ctf-exercise-of-options.md) and [Option clauses and provisions](../funding/ctf-option-clauses-and-provisions.md). For foreign military sales progress payments, see [DoD small business progress payment rate and FMS progress payment requests](ctf-dfars-progress-payment-rates-and-fms.md).
