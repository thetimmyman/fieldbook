## Purpose

Shows how progress payments are requested, certified, retained and refunded under the fixed-price construction payments clause, how final payment is made, and how common carriers are paid for communication services.

### Request and certification

The Government must make progress payments monthly as the work proceeds, or at more frequent intervals as determined by the Contracting Officer, on estimates of work accomplished which meets the standards of quality established under the contract, as approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2006]. The Contractor's request for progress payments must include substantiation, the first item of which is an itemization of the amounts requested, related to the various elements of work required by the contract covered by the payment requested [@CL-CTF-2007]. The substantiation also includes a listing of the amount included for work performed by each subcontractor under the contract, a listing of the total amount of each subcontract under the contract, and a listing of the amounts previously paid to each such subcontractor under the contract [@CL-CTF-2190], plus additional supporting data in a form and detail required by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2191]. In the preparation of estimates, the Contracting Officer may authorize material delivered on the site and preparatory work done to be taken into consideration [@CL-CTF-2136]. Material delivered to the Contractor at locations other than the site also may be taken into consideration if consideration is specifically authorized by the contract and the Contractor furnishes satisfactory evidence that it has acquired title to such material and that the material will be used to perform the contract [@CL-CTF-2137].

Along with each request for progress payments, the Contractor must furnish the required certification, or payment must not be made [@CL-CTF-2008]. The certification includes statements that the amounts requested are only for performance in accordance with the specifications, terms, and conditions of the contract, and that all payments due to subcontractors and suppliers from previous payments received under the contract have been made [@CL-CTF-2009] and timely payments will be made from the proceeds of the payment covered by the certification, in accordance with subcontract agreements and the requirements of chapter 39 of Title 31, United States Code [@CL-CTF-2189]. It also states that the request for progress payments does not include any amounts which the prime contractor intends to withhold or retain from a subcontractor or supplier in accordance with the terms and conditions of the subcontract, and that the certification is not to be construed as final acceptance of a subcontractor's performance [@CL-CTF-2133]. However, if the Contractor elects to delete paragraph (c)(4) from the certification, the certification is still acceptable [@CL-CTF-2188].

### Retainage, limits and title

If the Contracting Officer finds that satisfactory progress was achieved during any period for which a progress payment is to be made, the Contracting Officer must authorize payment to be made in full [@CL-CTF-2186]. However, if satisfactory progress has not been made, the Contracting Officer may retain a maximum of 10 percent of the amount of the payment until satisfactory progress is achieved [@CL-CTF-2010]. When the work is substantially complete, the Contracting Officer may retain from previously withheld funds and future progress payments that amount the Contracting Officer considers adequate for protection of the Government and must release to the Contractor all the remaining withheld funds [@CL-CTF-2011]. Also, on completion and acceptance of each separate building, public work, or other division of the contract, for which the price is stated separately in the contract, payment must be made for the completed work without retention of a percentage [@CL-CTF-2012]. In making these progress payments, the Government must, upon request, reimburse the Contractor for the amount of premiums paid for performance and payment bonds, including coinsurance and reinsurance agreements when applicable, after the Contractor has furnished evidence of full payment to the surety [@CL-CTF-2134]. The retainage provisions in paragraph (e) of the clause do not apply to that portion of progress payments attributable to bond premiums [@CL-CTF-2187].

Notwithstanding any provision of the contract, progress payments must not exceed 80 percent on work accomplished on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-2126]. A contract action is any action resulting in a contract, as defined in FAR subpart 2.1, including contract modifications for additional supplies or services, but not including contract modifications that are within the scope and under the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-2128], such as contract modifications issued pursuant to the Changes clause, or funding and other administrative changes [@CL-CTF-2193]. All material and work covered by progress payments made become the sole property of the Government at the time of payment [@CL-CTF-9622], but this must not be construed as relieving the Contractor from the sole responsibility for all material and work upon which payments have been made or the restoration of any damaged work [@CL-CTF-2138], or as waiving the right of the Government to require the fulfillment of all of the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-9623].

## How it works

### Refund of unearned amounts

If the Contractor, after making a certified request for progress payments, discovers that a portion or all of such request constitutes a payment for performance by the Contractor that fails to conform to the specifications, terms, and conditions of the contract [@CL-CTF-2129], the Contractor must notify the Contracting Officer of such performance deficiency [@CL-CTF-2192] and is obligated to pay the Government an amount, computed by the Contracting Officer in the manner provided in paragraph (j) of the clause, equal to interest on the unearned amount from the 8th day after the date of receipt of the unearned amount [@CL-CTF-2130] until the date the Contractor notifies the Contracting Officer that the performance deficiency has been corrected, or the date the Contractor reduces the amount of any subsequent certified request for progress payments by an amount equal to the unearned amount [@CL-CTF-2131]. The interest is computed at the rate of average bond equivalent rates of 91-day Treasury bills auctioned at the most recent auction of such bills prior to the date the Contractor receives the unearned amount, and deducted from the next available payment to the Contractor [@CL-CTF-2132].

### Final payment and common carriers

The Government must pay the amount due the Contractor under the contract after completion and acceptance of all work, presentation of a properly executed voucher, and presentation of a release of all claims against the Government arising by virtue of the contract [@CL-CTF-2013], other than claims, in stated amounts, that the Contractor has specifically excepted from the operation of the release [@CL-CTF-2014]. Under the common carrier clause, the Government must pay the Contractor, in arrears, upon submission of invoices for services and facilities furnished in accordance with the terms of CSAs issued under the contract, the rates and charges for the services and facilities as set forth in the clause entitled Rates, Charges and Services [@CL-CTF-2015].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. RFO FAR 52.232-5 provides that the Government must make progress payments monthly as the work proceeds, or at more frequent intervals as determined by the contracting officer, on estimates of work accomplished which meets the standards of quality established under the contract, as approved by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0502], that the contractor's request for progress payments must include substantiation, the first item of which is an itemization of the amounts requested, related to the various elements of work required by the contract covered by the payment requested [@CL-CTF-0503], and that along with each request for progress payments, the contractor must furnish the required certification, or payment must not be made [@CL-CTF-0504], matching FAR 52.232-5(b), (b)(1)(i), and (c) [@CL-CTF-2006; @CL-CTF-2007; @CL-CTF-2008]. RFO FAR 52.232-5 requires the certification to state that the amounts requested are only for performance in accordance with the specifications, terms, and conditions of the contract, and that all payments due to subcontractors and suppliers from previous payments received under the contract have been made [@CL-CTF-0505] and timely payments will be made from the proceeds of the payment covered by the certification, in accordance with subcontract agreements and the requirements of Chapter 39 of Title 31, United States Code [@CL-CTF-0506], matching FAR 52.232-5(c)(1) and (2) [@CL-CTF-2009; @CL-CTF-2189]. RFO FAR 52.232-5 provides that if the contracting officer finds that satisfactory progress was achieved during any period for which a progress payment is to be made, the contracting officer must authorize payment to be made in full [@CL-CTF-0507], that if satisfactory progress has not been made, the contracting officer may retain a maximum of 10 percent of the amount of the payment until satisfactory progress is achieved [@CL-CTF-0508], and that when the work is substantially complete, the contracting officer may retain from previously withheld funds and future progress payments that amount the contracting officer considers adequate for protection of the Government and must release to the contractor all the remaining withheld funds [@CL-CTF-0509], matching FAR 52.232-5(e) [@CL-CTF-2186; @CL-CTF-2010; @CL-CTF-2011]. RFO FAR 52.232-5 also provides that in making these progress payments, the Government must, upon request, reimburse the contractor for the amount of premiums paid for performance and payment bonds, including coinsurance and reinsurance agreements when applicable, after the contractor has furnished evidence of full payment to the surety [@CL-CTF-0510], and that the retainage provisions in paragraph (e) of the clause do not apply to that portion of progress payments attributable to bond premiums [@CL-CTF-0511], matching FAR 52.232-5(g) [@CL-CTF-2134; @CL-CTF-2187]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-5, if the contractor, after making a certified request for progress payments, discovers that a portion or all of such request constitutes a payment for performance by the contractor that fails to conform to the specifications, terms, and conditions of the contract [@CL-CTF-9100], the contractor must notify the contracting officer of such performance deficiency [@CL-CTF-9102] and is obligated to pay the Government an amount, computed by the contracting officer in the manner provided in paragraph (j) of the clause, equal to interest on the unearned amount from the 8th day after the date of receipt of the unearned amount [@CL-CTF-0512] until the date the contractor notifies the contracting officer that the performance deficiency has been corrected, or the date the contractor reduces the amount of any subsequent certified request for progress payments by an amount equal to the unearned amount [@CL-CTF-0513], computed at the rate of average bond equivalent rates of 91-day Treasury bills auctioned at the most recent auction of such bills prior to the date the contractor receives the unearned amount [@CL-CTF-0514] and deducted from the next available payment to the contractor [@CL-CTF-9101], matching FAR 52.232-5(d) and (j) [@CL-CTF-2129; @CL-CTF-2192; @CL-CTF-2130; @CL-CTF-2131; @CL-CTF-2132]. RFO FAR 52.232-5 requires the Government to pay the amount due the contractor under the contract after completion and acceptance of all work, presentation of a properly executed voucher, and presentation of release of all claims against the Government arising by virtue of the contract [@CL-CTF-0515], other than claims, in stated amounts, that the contractor has specifically excepted from the operation of the release [@CL-CTF-0516], matching FAR 52.232-5(h) [@CL-CTF-2013; @CL-CTF-2014]. RFO FAR 52.232-5 further provides that notwithstanding any provision of the contract, progress payments must not exceed 80 percent on work accomplished on undefinitized contract actions [@CL-CTF-0517], defining a contract action as any action resulting in a contract, as defined in FAR subpart 2.1, including contract modifications for additional supplies or services, but not including contract modifications that are within the scope and under the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-0518], such as contract modifications issued pursuant to the Changes clause, or funding and other administrative changes [@CL-CTF-0519], matching FAR 52.232-5(i) [@CL-CTF-2126; @CL-CTF-2128; @CL-CTF-2193]. RFO FAR 52.232-6 provides that as prescribed in FAR 32.111(a)(6), the contracting officer inserts the Payment under Communication Service Contracts with Common Carriers clause [@CL-CTF-9099], under which the Government must pay the contractor, in arrears, upon submission of invoices for services and facilities furnished in accordance with the terms of CSAs issued under the contract, the rates and charges for the services and facilities as set forth in the clause entitled Rates, Charges and Services [@CL-CTF-0520], matching FAR 52.232-6 [@CL-CTF-2015].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A builder submits a monthly request, with the itemization, the subcontractor listings and the certification, for an approved estimate of 500,000, of which 10,000 is reimbursement of bond premiums. The Contracting Officer finds progress unsatisfactory for the period, so the Contracting Officer may retain up to 10 percent of the 490,000 not attributable to bond premiums, which is 49,000, and pay 451,000. Had the Contracting Officer found satisfactory progress, payment would be authorized in full. Later the work is substantially complete, and the Contracting Officer keeps only what is adequate for protection of the Government and releases the rest of the withheld funds.

In a later month the builder finds that 20,000 of an earlier certified payment was for work that did not conform to the contract. The builder notifies the Contracting Officer. Interest on the 20,000 runs from the 8th day after receipt until the builder reports the deficiency corrected or cuts a later request by 20,000, and the Contracting Officer deducts it from the next available payment.

A modification that adds work beyond the scope and terms of the contract is not yet definitized. Work accomplished on it is 100,000, so progress payments for that work cannot exceed 80,000.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-5, if satisfactory progress has not been made, the Contracting Officer may retain a maximum of 10 percent of the amount of the payment until satisfactory progress is achieved [@CL-CTF-2010], and the retainage provisions in paragraph (e) do not apply to that portion of progress payments attributable to bond premiums [@CL-CTF-2187], whereas the architect-engineer clause requires a withholding from amounts due under paragraph (a) of up to 10 percent only if the Contracting Officer determines that such a withholding is necessary to protect the Government's interest and ensure satisfactory completion of the contract [@CL-CTF-2023]. See [Payments under fixed-price architect-engineer contracts](ctf-payments-fixed-price-architect-engineer.md). For customary progress payment rates and unusual progress payments, see [Progress payment rates and unusual progress payments](../financing/ctf-progress-payment-rates.md). Under FAR 52.232-6, the Government must pay a common carrier, in arrears, upon submission of invoices for services and facilities furnished in accordance with the terms of CSAs issued under the contract, the rates and charges set forth in the clause entitled Rates, Charges and Services [@CL-CTF-2015], whereas under FAR 52.232-5 the Government must make construction progress payments monthly as the work proceeds, or at more frequent intervals as determined by the Contracting Officer, on estimates of work accomplished which meets the standards of quality established under the contract, as approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2006]. For due dates of construction progress payments and retained amounts, see [Prompt payment clause for construction contracts](ctf-prompt-payment-construction-contracts.md). For the rules on assigning claims under a contract, see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md). For payment clauses for other contract types, see [Payments under fixed-price research and development, personal services and transportation contracts](ctf-payments-rd-personal-services-and-transportation.md). Under FAR 32.111(a), the contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.232-6, appropriately modified with respect to payment due dates, in accordance with agency regulations [@CL-CTF-1514], in solicitations and contracts for regulated communication services by common carriers [@CL-CTF-1519], whereas RFO FAR 52.232-6 states that as prescribed in FAR 32.111(a)(6), the contracting officer inserts the Payment under Communication Service Contracts with Common Carriers clause [@CL-CTF-9099].
