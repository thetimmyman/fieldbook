## Purpose

Says when a contractor pays the Government under the dismantling and demolition payment clauses, both the lump-sum and the incremental version.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1698; @CL-CTF-1699] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.237-5 [@CL-CTF-1698]; FAR 52.237-6 [@CL-CTF-1699] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.237-5 [@CL-CTF-0448]; RFO FAR 52.237-6 [@CL-CTF-0449] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1698; @CL-CTF-1699] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements whenever the contractor is to receive title to dismantled or demolished property and a net amount of compensation is due to the Government [@CL-CTF-3048; @CL-CTF-3049] |

Under the Payment by Contractor to Government clause, the contractor must perform the work called for under the contract and, within the number of days of receipt of notice of award stated in the clause unless otherwise provided in the Schedule and before proceeding with the work, must pay the amount stated in the clause [@CL-CTF-1698]. As prescribed in FAR 37.304(b), the payment clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements whenever the contractor is to receive title to dismantled or demolished property and a net amount of compensation is due to the Government, except if the contracting officer determines that it would be advantageous to the Government for the contractor to pay in increments and for the Government to transfer title to the contractor for increments of property only upon receipt of those payments [@CL-CTF-3048; @CL-CTF-1697; @CL-CTF-9273]. As prescribed in FAR 37.304(c), the incremental payment clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements if the contractor is to receive title to dismantled or demolished property and a net amount of compensation is due the Government, and if the contracting officer determines that it would be advantageous to the Government for the contractor to pay in increments, and for the Government to transfer title to the contractor for increments of property, only upon receipt of those payments [@CL-CTF-3049; @CL-CTF-1724].

Under the Incremental Payment by Contractor to Government clause, the contractor must perform the work called for under the contract and, within the number of days of receipt of notice of award stated in the clause unless otherwise provided in the Schedule and before proceeding with the work, must pay the initial amount stated in the clause [@CL-CTF-3050]. Thereafter, the contractor must make payment to the Government in the amount and frequency specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-1699].

## How it works

Under the payment clause, the contractor must perform the work called for under the contract and, within the stated number of days of receipt of notice of award unless otherwise provided in the Schedule and before proceeding with the work, must pay the stated amount [@CL-CTF-1698]. Under the incremental payment clause, after the initial payment within the stated number of days of receipt of notice of award unless otherwise provided in the Schedule and before proceeding with the work [@CL-CTF-3050], the contractor must make payment to the Government in the amount and frequency specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-1699]. The prescription for the payment clause applies to solicitations and contracts for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements whenever the contractor is to receive title to dismantled or demolished property and a net amount of compensation is due to the Government, except if the contracting officer determines that it would be advantageous to the Government for the contractor to pay in increments and for the Government to transfer title to the contractor for increments of property only upon receipt of those payments [@CL-CTF-3048; @CL-CTF-1697; @CL-CTF-9273].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.237-5 provides that the contractor must perform the work called for under the contract and, within the stated number of days of receipt of notice of award unless otherwise provided in the Schedule and before proceeding with the work, must pay the stated amount [@CL-CTF-0448]. RFO FAR 52.237-6 provides that the contractor must perform the work called for under the contract and, within the stated number of days of receipt of notice of award unless otherwise provided in the Schedule and before proceeding with the work, must pay the stated initial amount [@CL-CTF-3051], and that thereafter the contractor must make payment to the Government in the amount and frequency specified in the Schedule [@CL-CTF-0449].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A demolition contract leaves a net amount of 90,000 due to the Government. Under the payment clause, the contractor pays 90,000 within the stated number of days after notice of award and before starting work. If the incremental clause is used instead, the Schedule might call for 30,000 at the start and 30,000 in each of the next two months.

## Never confuse

In solicitations and contracts for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements where the contractor is to receive title to dismantled or demolished property and a net amount of compensation is due to the Government, the payment clause is inserted unless the contracting officer determines that it would be advantageous to the Government for the contractor to pay in increments and for the Government to transfer title to the contractor for increments of property only upon receipt of those payments, in which case the incremental payment clause is inserted [@CL-CTF-3048; @CL-CTF-1697; @CL-CTF-9273; @CL-CTF-3049; @CL-CTF-1724]. For simple interest on amounts that become payable by the contractor to the Government under a contract that contains the Interest clause, see [Interest on amounts due the Government and the assignment of claims clause](ctf-interest-and-assignment-of-claims-clauses.md) [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1672].
