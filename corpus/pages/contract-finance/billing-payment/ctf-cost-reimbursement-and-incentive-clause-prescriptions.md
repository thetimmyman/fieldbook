## Purpose

Shows which allowable-cost, fee, cost-sharing and incentive price revision clauses the contracting officer inserts, and what contract type triggers each.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1242] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.307 and FAR 16.406 [@CL-CTF-1242; @CL-CTF-1248] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1242] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts in which the listed contract types are contemplated [@CL-CTF-1242; @CL-CTF-1248] |

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.216-7, Allowable Cost and Payment, in solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement contract or a time-and-materials contract, other than a contract for a commercial product or commercial service, is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1242]. For a time-and-materials contract, that clause applies in conjunction with the clause at 52.232-7, but only to the portion of the contract that provides for reimbursement of materials at actual cost [@CL-CTF-1263], and it does not apply to labor-hour contracts [@CL-CTF-1264]. The clause at 52.216-8, Fixed Fee, goes in when a cost-plus-fixed-fee contract other than a construction contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1243], and the clause at 52.216-9, Fixed-Fee—Construction, when a cost-plus-fixed-fee construction contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1244]. The clause at 52.216-10, Incentive Fee, goes in when a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1245]. The clause at 52.216-11, Cost Contract—No Fee, goes in when a cost-reimbursement contract that provides no fee and is not a cost-sharing contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1246], and the clause at 52.216-12, Cost-Sharing Contract—No Fee, when a cost-sharing contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1247].

The contracting officer is to insert the clause at 52.216-16, Incentive Price Revision—Firm Target, when a fixed-price incentive (firm target) contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1248]. The contracting officer is to insert the clause at 52.216-17, Incentive Price Revision—Successive Targets, when a fixed-price incentive (successive targets) contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-1249].

## How it works

Each prescription is keyed to the contract type the contracting officer contemplates [@CL-CTF-1242; @CL-CTF-1245; @CL-CTF-1248]. Fixed fee has one clause for work other than construction and a separate clause for construction [@CL-CTF-1243; @CL-CTF-1244]. The two no-fee clauses split on whether the contract is a cost-sharing contract [@CL-CTF-1246; @CL-CTF-1247]. Each fixed-price incentive type has its own incentive price revision clause [@CL-CTF-1248; @CL-CTF-1249]. The printed sections also prescribe alternates, further clauses and an award-fee clause, so this card is not a complete list.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply contract is a cost-plus-fixed-fee contract that is not a construction contract and not for a commercial product. It is a cost-reimbursement contract, so the allowable cost and payment clause and the fixed fee clause go in. The construction fixed-fee clause and the incentive clauses do not apply. The answer: the contract type decides the clause set.

## Never confuse

This card tells which clause goes in and when, not what the clause does. The Fixed Fee clause is on [Fixed Fee clause](ctf-fixed-fee.md) and the incentive fee clause is on [Incentive Fee clause](ctf-incentive-fee.md). The incentive fee clause belongs to a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract [@CL-CTF-1245], whereas the incentive price revision clauses belong to fixed-price incentive contracts [@CL-CTF-1248; @CL-CTF-1249]; see [Fixed-price incentive (firm target) contracts](../../estimating/pricing/est-fpi-firm-target.md) and [Fixed-price incentive (successive targets) contracts](../../estimating/pricing/est-fpi-successive-targets.md). For cost contracts and cost-sharing contracts, see [Cost contracts and cost-sharing contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-and-cost-sharing-contracts.md), for the time-and-materials payments clause, see [Payments under time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts](ctf-tm-and-lh-payments.md), and for the time-and-materials contract type, see [Time-and-materials contracts: use, ceiling and surveillance](ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md).
