## Purpose

Gives the prompt payment definitions that set payment dates, and shows where a contractor takes an inquiry about a late, short or disputed payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3584] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.901, 32.902 and 32.909 [@CL-CTF-3745; @CL-CTF-3746; @CL-CTF-3584; @CL-CTF-3588]; FAR 32.900 [@CL-CTF-9305]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CTF-9407; @CL-CTF-9408] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.900 [@CL-CTF-3041]; RFO FAR 32.901 [@CL-CTF-3042]; RFO FAR 32.902 [@CL-CTF-4055; @CL-CTF-4056; @CL-CTF-4057; @CL-CTF-4058]; RFO FAR 32.909 [@CL-CTF-4059; @CL-CTF-4060; @CL-CTF-4061]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-CTF-9464; @CL-CTF-9465] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3584] |
| Applies to | Contractor questions about delinquent payments and about disagreements in payment amount or timing [@CL-CTF-3588; @CL-CTF-3589] |

The prompt payment subpart applies to invoice payments on all contracts, except contracts with payment terms and late payment penalties established by other governmental authority, for example tariffs [@CL-CTF-3745]. It does not apply to contract financing payments, and the text points to the definition at FAR 32.001 [@CL-CTF-3746].

In the prompt payment subpart, a discount for prompt payment means an invoice payment reduction offered by the contractor for payment prior to the due date [@CL-CTF-9632; @CL-CTF-3584]. A mixed invoice means an invoice that contains items with different payment due dates [@CL-CTF-3585]. Payment date means the date on which a check for payment is dated or, for an electronic funds transfer, the settlement date [@CL-CTF-3586]. Settlement date, as it applies to electronic funds transfer, means the date on which an electronic funds transfer payment is credited to the contractor's financial institution [@CL-CTF-3587].

Questions about delinquent payments are directed to the designated billing office or the designated payment office [@CL-CTF-3588]. Disagreements in payment amount or timing are directed to the contracting officer for resolution, and the contracting officer must coordinate within appropriate contracting channels and seek the advice of other offices as necessary to resolve them [@CL-CTF-3589].

Subpart 32.9 prescribes policies, procedures, and clauses for implementing Office of Management and Budget (OMB) prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-9305].

FAR 2.101 defines an electronic funds transfer as any transfer of funds, other than a transaction originated by cash, check, or similar paper instrument, that is initiated through an electronic terminal, telephone, computer, or magnetic tape, for the purpose of ordering, instructing, or authorizing a financial institution to debit or credit an account [@CL-CTF-9407; @CL-CTF-9408]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines an electronic funds transfer the same way, word for word [@CL-CTF-9464; @CL-CTF-9465], and goes on to say that the term includes Automated Clearing House transfers, Fedwire transfers, and transfers made at automatic teller machines and point-of-sale terminals, and that, for purposes of compliance with 31 U.S.C.3332 and implementing regulations at 31 CFR part 208, it includes a Governmentwide commercial purchase card transaction [@CL-CTF-9465; @CL-CTF-9486; @CL-CTF-9487].

## How it works

The contractor sorts an inquiry by its kind. A question about a delinquent payment goes to the designated billing office or the designated payment office [@CL-CTF-3588]. A disagreement about the amount or timing of a payment goes to the contracting officer, who resolves it and must coordinate within contracting channels and seek the advice of other offices as necessary [@CL-CTF-3589].

Small business concerns have an additional route. They may contact the agency's local small business specialist, or a representative from the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization, to obtain additional assistance on payment issues, late payment interest penalties and information on the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-3590]. The definitions apply as used in the subpart, and the date a payment counts depends on whether it was made by check or by electronic funds transfer [@CL-CTF-9632; @CL-CTF-3586; @CL-CTF-3587]. Due dates and interest are covered on separate cards.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.900 says the subpart prescribes policies, procedures, and clauses for implementing Office of Management and Budget (OMB) prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-3041]. RFO FAR 32.901 keeps the applicability rule: the subpart applies to invoice payments on all contracts, except contracts with payment terms and late payment penalties established by other governmental authority, for example tariffs, and does not apply to contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-3745; @CL-CTF-3746; @CL-CTF-3042]. RFO FAR 32.902 keeps the definitions of discount for prompt payment [@CL-CTF-4055], mixed invoice [@CL-CTF-4056], payment date [@CL-CTF-4057] and settlement date [@CL-CTF-4058]. RFO FAR 32.909 keeps the routing of inquiries: questions involving delinquent payments go to the designated billing office or designated payment office [@CL-CTF-4059], and disagreements in payment amount or timing go to the contracting officer for resolution, who must coordinate within appropriate contracting channels and seek the advice of other offices as necessary to resolve disagreements [@CL-CTF-4060]. It also keeps the route for small business concerns: they may contact the agency's local small business specialist or a representative from the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization to obtain additional assistance related to payment issues, late payment interest penalties, and information on the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-4061].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor sends one invoice covering two line items with different due dates, so it is a mixed invoice [@CL-CTF-3585]. For one item the agency pays by check dated on the 10th of the month, and the contractor's bank receives it on the 14th. The payment date is the 10th, the date on the check [@CL-CTF-3586]. For the other item the agency pays by electronic funds transfer that is credited to the contractor's bank on the 14th, so the settlement date and the payment date are both the 14th [@CL-CTF-3586; @CL-CTF-3587]. When the contractor does not receive a payment, it directs the question about the delinquent payment to the designated billing office or the designated payment office [@CL-CTF-3588]. Later the contractor and the agency disagree on the amount paid, so the contractor directs the disagreement to the contracting officer, who coordinates to resolve it [@CL-CTF-3589]. If the contractor is a small business concern, it may also ask the local small business specialist for additional help [@CL-CTF-3590].

## Never confuse

Payment date and settlement date are separate defined terms. For a check, the payment date is the date the check is dated. For an electronic funds transfer, the payment date is the settlement date, the date the payment is credited to the contractor's financial institution [@CL-CTF-3586; @CL-CTF-3587]. A delinquent payment question is also not a disagreement on amount or timing. The first goes to the designated billing office or payment office, and the second to the contracting officer for resolution [@CL-CTF-3588; @CL-CTF-3589]. A discount for prompt payment is offered by the contractor for payment before the due date, so it is not a reduction the agency sets [@CL-CTF-3584]. The [prompt payment card](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md) covers due dates.

## Company calibration

Which office the company contacts about a late payment, who owns a disagreement on amount or timing before it reaches the contracting officer, and whether the company qualifies for the small business route. [@Q-009]
