## Purpose

States how long the materials must stay available under the sealed bidding audit and records clause, which events add other periods, and which subcontracts must carry the clause, in the basic clause and in Alternate I.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2948] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-26 [@CL-CTF-2948] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2948] |
| Applies to | Sealed bidding contracts in which the clause is inserted under its own prescription [@CL-CTF-2948] |

The Contractor must make the materials described in paragraph (b) available at its office at all reasonable times for examination, audit or reproduction until 3 years after final payment under the contract [@CL-CTF-2954], or for any other period specified in subpart 4.7 of the FAR [@CL-CTF-2955]. Subpart 4.7 as in effect on the date of the contract is incorporated by reference in its entirety and made part of the contract [@CL-CTF-2955].

If the contract is completely or partially terminated, the records relating to the work terminated must be made available for 3 years after any resulting final termination settlement [@CL-CTF-2956]. Records pertaining to appeals under the Disputes clause, or to litigation or the settlement of claims arising under or relating to performance, must be made available until disposition of those appeals, litigation or claims [@CL-CTF-2957].

In the basic clause, the Contractor must insert a clause containing all the provisions of the clause, including the subcontracts paragraph, in all subcontracts expected to exceed the threshold for submission of certified cost or pricing data in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) on the date of subcontract award [@CL-CTF-2958].

Under Alternate I, the Contractor must insert a clause containing the provisions of the clause, including the subcontracts paragraph, in all subcontracts, except as paragraph (e)(2) provides [@CL-CTF-2959]. The authority of the Inspector General in paragraph (c)(2) does not flow down to subcontracts [@CL-CTF-2960].

## How it works

The baseline period is 3 years after final payment, and the period in subpart 4.7 applies where it specifies one [@CL-CTF-2954; @CL-CTF-2955]. Records of terminated work and records on appeals, litigation or claims have their own periods, which run from the final termination settlement and until disposition [@CL-CTF-2956; @CL-CTF-2957].

Alternate I substitutes only its paragraphs (c) and (e), so the availability paragraph is the one in the basic clause [@CL-CTF-2974]. The examination rights themselves are on the [examination](../billing-payment/ctf-audit-and-records-sealed-bidding-examination.md) page.

The flowdown in the basic clause tests the certified cost or pricing data threshold on the date of subcontract award [@CL-CTF-2958]. Alternate I covers all subcontracts, but the Inspector General's authority in paragraph (c)(2) does not flow down to them [@CL-CTF-2959; @CL-CTF-2960].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A sealed bid contract with the basic clause runs from month 0. Part of the work is terminated and the final termination settlement comes in month 12. Final payment is made in month 30. A claim under the Disputes clause is disposed of in month 40. Assume subpart 4.7 specifies no other period for these records. The materials described in paragraph (b) must be available until month 66, which is 3 years after final payment. The records relating to the terminated work must be available until month 48, which is 3 years after the settlement. The records pertaining to the claim must be available until month 40, when it is disposed of. The Contractor awards one subcontract expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold on its award date and another expected to stay below it. The basic clause requires the clause in the first subcontract. Under Alternate I the clause would go into both, but the Inspector General's interview authority would not flow down to either.

## Never confuse

Availability is not the examination right. The examination right attaches to records related to a modification priced with certified cost or pricing data, while paragraph (d) fixes how long the materials described in paragraph (b) must stay available [@CL-CTF-2950; @CL-CTF-2954].

The period of 3 years after final payment is not the only one: subpart 4.7 can specify another, terminated work runs from the final termination settlement, and appeals, litigation and claims run until disposition [@CL-CTF-2954; @CL-CTF-2955; @CL-CTF-2956; @CL-CTF-2957].

The flowdown differs by version. The basic clause covers subcontracts expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold on the award date, and Alternate I covers all subcontracts, except that the Inspector General's authority in paragraph (c)(2) does not flow down [@CL-CTF-2958; @CL-CTF-2959; @CL-CTF-2960].
