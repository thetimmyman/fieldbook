## Purpose

Shows when title passes, who bears responsibility and risk of loss under the Fast Payment Procedure clause, and states what the Contractor owes for supplies that are not received, damaged in transit or nonconforming.

## The rule

| Codified citation | FAR 52.213-1 [@CL-CTF-2930] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-90 [@CL-CTF-0222]; FAR 52.213-1 is [Reserved] in the RFO text [@CL-CTF-0241] |

Title to the supplies passes to the Government on delivery to a post office or common carrier for shipment to the specific destination or, where shipment is by means other than the Postal Service or a common carrier, on delivery to the point of first receipt by the Government [@CL-CTF-2945].

Notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement, the Contractor assumes all responsibility and risk of loss for supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase requirements [@CL-CTF-2946].

Notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement, the Contractor must also replace, repair or correct those supplies promptly at its own expense, if instructed to do so by the Contracting Officer within 180 days from the date title to the supplies vests in the Government [@CL-CTF-2946; @CL-CTF-2947].

## How it works

The Government will pay invoices based on the Contractor's delivery to a post office or common carrier or, if shipped by other means, to the point of first receipt by the Government [@CL-CTF-2931]. Title passes to the Government upon delivery to a post office or common carrier for shipment to the specific destination, or to the point of first receipt by the Government if shipment is by means other than Postal Service or common carrier [@CL-CTF-2945]. Notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement, the Contractor assumes all responsibility and risk of loss for supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit, or not conforming to purchase requirements [@CL-CTF-2946].

The Contractor replaces, repairs or corrects those supplies promptly at its expense if instructed to do so by the Contracting Officer within 180 days from the date title to the supplies vests in the Government [@CL-CTF-2947]. The date title vests is the date of delivery to the carrier or point of first receipt [@CL-CTF-2945; @CL-CTF-2947]. The invoice and shipment requirements are on the [eligibility and invoicing](../billing-payment/ctf-fast-payment-eligibility-and-invoicing.md) page.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints the Fast Payment Procedure clause as FAR 52.232-90 and says to insert it as prescribed in 32.1204, where the codified clause says 13.404 [@CL-CTF-0222] [@CL-CTF-2930]. FAR 52.213-1 is marked [Reserved] in the RFO text [@CL-CTF-0241]. The RFO clause keeps the title rule: title passes on delivery to a post office or common carrier for shipment to the specific destination or, where shipment is by means other than the Postal Service or a common carrier, to the point of first receipt by the Government [@CL-CTF-0238] [@CL-CTF-2945]. It keeps the rule that, notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement, the contractor assumes all responsibility and risk of loss for supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase requirements [@CL-CTF-0239] [@CL-CTF-2946]. The replacement, repair or correction duty is the same, notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement: promptly at the contractor's expense if the contracting officer so instructs within 180 days from the date title vests in the Government [@CL-CTF-0239] [@CL-CTF-0240] [@CL-CTF-2947]. The payment basis is also the same [@CL-CTF-0223] [@CL-CTF-2931].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An order worth 9,000 ships by common carrier in three crates. Title passes when the Contractor delivers the shipment to the carrier, and the invoice is paid on that delivery. Two crates never arrive at destination and one is damaged in transit. The Contractor assumes the responsibility and risk of loss for all three. Forty days after title vested, the Contracting Officer instructs the Contractor to replace the lost and damaged crates. Forty days is inside the 180-day period, so the Contractor replaces them promptly at its own expense. An instruction first given 200 days after title vested would not meet the 180-day condition stated for the replacement duty.

## Never confuse

Title passes to the Government upon delivery to the carrier or point of first receipt [@CL-CTF-2945], and the Contractor assumes all responsibility and risk of loss for supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase requirements [@CL-CTF-2946].

The Contractor replaces, repairs or corrects those supplies promptly at its expense if instructed to do so by the Contracting Officer within 180 days from the date title to the supplies vests in the Government [@CL-CTF-2947]. The codified clause is FAR 52.213-1 [@CL-CTF-2930], but the RFO text prints it as FAR 52.232-90 [@CL-CTF-0222] and marks FAR 52.213-1 [Reserved] [@CL-CTF-0241].
