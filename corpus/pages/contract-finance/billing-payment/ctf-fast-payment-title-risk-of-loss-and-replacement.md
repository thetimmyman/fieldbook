## Purpose

Separates when title passes from who bears responsibility and risk of loss under the Fast Payment Procedure clause, and states what the Contractor owes for supplies that are not received, damaged in transit or nonconforming.

| Codified citation | FAR 52.213-1 [@CL-CTF-2930] |

Title to the supplies passes to the Government on delivery to a post office or common carrier for shipment to the specific destination or, where shipment is by means other than the Postal Service or a common carrier, on delivery to the point of first receipt by the Government [@CL-CTF-2945].

Notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement, the Contractor assumes all responsibility and risk of loss for supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase requirements [@CL-CTF-2946].

The Contractor must replace, repair or correct supplies that were not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase requirements promptly at its own expense, if the Contracting Officer so instructs within 180 days from the date title to the supplies vests in the Government [@CL-CTF-2947].

## How it works

Payment and title use the same delivery events: delivery to a post office or common carrier or, if shipped by other means, to the point of first receipt by the Government [@CL-CTF-2931; @CL-CTF-2945]. Title passing at that point does not move the Contractor's responsibility and risk of loss for the three listed kinds of supplies, which the Contractor assumes notwithstanding any other contract provision [@CL-CTF-2946].

The replacement, repair or correction duty depends on a Contracting Officer instruction. The instruction must come within 180 days from the date title vests, and the work is done promptly at the Contractor's expense [@CL-CTF-2947]. The date title vests is the date of delivery to the carrier or point of first receipt [@CL-CTF-2945; @CL-CTF-2947]. The invoice and shipment requirements are on the [eligibility and invoicing](../billing-payment/ctf-fast-payment-eligibility-and-invoicing.md) page.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An order worth 9,000 ships by common carrier in three crates. Title passes when the Contractor delivers the shipment to the carrier, and the invoice is paid on that delivery. Two crates never arrive at destination and one is damaged in transit. The Contractor assumes the responsibility and risk of loss for all three. Forty days after title vested, the Contracting Officer instructs the Contractor to replace the lost and damaged crates. Forty days is inside the 180-day period, so the Contractor replaces them promptly at its own expense. An instruction first given 200 days after title vested would not meet the 180-day condition stated for the replacement duty.

## Never confuse

Title passing is not the end of the Contractor's responsibility. Title passes at delivery to the carrier or point of first receipt, while the Contractor still assumes responsibility and risk of loss for supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase requirements [@CL-CTF-2945; @CL-CTF-2946].

The replacement duty is not automatic. It applies if the Contracting Officer instructs within 180 days from the date title vests [@CL-CTF-2947].
