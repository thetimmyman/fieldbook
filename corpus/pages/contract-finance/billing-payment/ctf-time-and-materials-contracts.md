## Purpose

Shows when a time-and-materials contract may be used, the determination and ceiling price it needs, who decides, and what finance tracks against the ceiling.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1229] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.600 [@CL-CTF-2359] and FAR 16.601 [@CL-CTF-1229] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1229] |
| Applies to | Time-and-materials contracts and orders [@CL-CTF-1229; @CL-CTF-1231] |

Time-and-materials contracts and labor-hour contracts are not fixed-price contracts [@CL-CTF-2359].

A time-and-materials contract provides for acquiring supplies or services on the basis of both direct labor hours at specified fixed hourly rates that include wages, overhead, general and administrative expenses, and profit, and actual cost for materials, except as provided for in 31.205-26(e) and (f) [@CL-CTF-1228; @CL-CTF-1259]. It may be used only when it is not possible at the time of placing the contract to estimate accurately the extent or duration of the work or to anticipate costs with any reasonable degree of confidence [@CL-CTF-1229].

A time-and-materials contract or order may be used only if listed conditions are met, one being that the contracting officer prepares a determination and findings that no other contract type is suitable [@CL-CTF-1231]. The determination and findings shall be signed by the contracting officer prior to the execution of the base period or any option periods [@CL-CTF-1233; @CL-CTF-1231], and approved by the head of the contracting activity prior to the execution of the base period when the base period plus any option periods exceeds three years [@CL-CTF-1258; @CL-CTF-1231]. Another listed condition is that the contract or order includes a ceiling price that the contractor exceeds at its own risk [@CL-CTF-1232; @CL-CTF-1231].

## How it works

The contracting officer prepares the determination and findings, and the head of the contracting activity approves them in the listed case [@CL-CTF-1231; @CL-CTF-1258]. The contract type provides no positive profit incentive for cost control or labor efficiency [@CL-CTF-1230], so appropriate Government surveillance of contractor performance is required to give reasonable assurance that efficient methods and effective cost controls are being used [@CL-CTF-1257]. Before an increase in the ceiling price of a time-and-materials or labor-hour contract or order, the contracting officer shall, as the first listed step, conduct an analysis of pricing and other relevant factors to determine if the action is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-1234], and a second listed step is to document the decision in the contract or order file [@CL-CTF-1260; @CL-CTF-1234]. A finance reader should therefore expect to track cumulative billed labor and materials against the ceiling price, because the contractor exceeds the ceiling at its own risk [@CL-CTF-1232].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A time-and-materials order has a ceiling price of 600,000. Billed labor and materials reach 480,000 with work still ahead, and the contractor asks for a higher ceiling. The answer: finance reports billings against the 600,000 ceiling each period, and the contracting officer analyses pricing and other relevant factors before any increase. Until a ceiling increase is made, costs above the ceiling are at the contractor's own risk.

## Never confuse

This card covers when the type may be used and what limits and surveillance apply. How vouchers, withholds and the notice before the ceiling are handled is on [Payments under time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts](ctf-tm-and-lh-payments.md). Labor under a time-and-materials contract is priced at specified fixed hourly rates that include wages, overhead, general and administrative expenses, and profit, while materials are priced at actual cost, except as provided for in 31.205-26(e) and (f) [@CL-CTF-1228; @CL-CTF-1259]. The clause at 52.216-7 applies to a time-and-materials contract in conjunction with the clause at 52.232-7, but only to the portion that provides for reimbursement of materials (as defined in the clause at 52.232-7) at actual cost, and it does not apply to labor-hour contracts [@CL-CTF-1263; @CL-CTF-1264]; see [Clause prescriptions for cost-reimbursement and incentive contracts](ctf-cost-reimbursement-and-incentive-clause-prescriptions.md). How the contract type is negotiated and documented is on [Negotiating and selecting contract type](../funding/ctf-contract-type-negotiation-and-selection.md). The wider effect of contract type is on [Contract types and what they mean for finance](ctf-contract-types.md).
