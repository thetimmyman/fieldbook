## Purpose

Shows what the fast payment procedure allows, the conditions under which it may be used, what the purchasing instrument must include and when the clause is inserted. The procedure allows payment under limited conditions to a contractor before the Government verifies that supplies have been received and accepted [@CL-CTF-2978].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | RFO model text [@CL-CTF-2978] |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1201 to 32.1204 [@CL-CTF-2978; @CL-CTF-2993] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text [@CL-CTF-2978] |
| Applies to | Contracts and orders for which the conditions for use are met [@CL-CTF-2995] |

Payment for supplies is based on the contractor's submission of an invoice that constitutes a certification of two things [@CL-CTF-2979; @CL-CTF-2980]. The first is that the contractor has delivered the supplies to a post office, common carrier or point of first receipt by the Government [@CL-CTF-2979]. The second is that the contractor must replace, repair or correct supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase agreements [@CL-CTF-2980]. The contracting officer must be primarily responsible for determining the amount of debts that result when a contractor fails to properly replace, repair or correct supplies lost, damaged or not conforming to purchase requirements, and the paragraph cross-refers to 32.602 and 32.603 [@CL-CTF-2981].

Fast payment procedures may be used under all of the conditions listed in 32.1202 [@CL-CTF-2995; @CL-CTF-2986]. They are:

- Amount: the contract or order does not exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, except that executive agencies may permit higher dollar limitations for specified activities or items on a case-by-case basis [@CL-CTF-2982].
- Place of delivery: deliveries are to occur at locations with both a geographical separation and a lack of adequate communications facilities between Government receiving and disbursing activities, which will make timely payment based on evidence of Government acceptance impractical [@CL-CTF-2983].
- Title: title to the supplies will pass to the Government upon delivery to a post office or common carrier for mailing or shipment to destination, or upon receipt by the Government if the shipment is by means other than the Postal Service or a common carrier [@CL-CTF-2984].
- Supplier agreement: the supplier agrees to replace, repair or correct supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit or not conforming to purchase requirements [@CL-CTF-2985].
- Price type: the contract or order will be firm-fixed-price [@CL-CTF-2986].
- System: a system is in place to document contractor performance under fast payment purchases, give timely feedback to the contracting officer in case of contractor deficiencies, and identify suppliers that have a current history of abusing the procedure [@CL-CTF-2987].

When fast payment procedures are used, the purchasing instrument must include three items [@CL-CTF-2988; @CL-CTF-2989; @CL-CTF-2990]:

- A requirement that the supplies be shipped transportation or postage prepaid [@CL-CTF-2988].
- A requirement that invoices be submitted directly to the finance or other office designated in the order, or, for unpriced purchase orders, to the contracting officer, with a cross-reference to 12.201(e)(2) [@CL-CTF-2989].
- On the consignee's copy, the statement "Consignee's Notification to Purchasing Activity of Nonreceipt, Damage, or Nonconformance" [@CL-CTF-2990].

The consignee shall notify the purchasing office promptly after the specified date of delivery of supplies not received, damaged in transit or not conforming to specifications of the purchase order [@CL-CTF-2991]. Unless extenuating circumstances exist, the notification should be made not later than 60 days after the specified date of delivery [@CL-CTF-2992].

Insert the clause at 52.232-90, Fast Payment Procedure, in solicitations and contracts when the conditions in 32.1202 are applicable and it is intended that the fast payment procedure be used in the contract [@CL-CTF-2993]. In the case of BPAs, the contracting officer may elect to insert the clause either in the BPA or in orders under the BPA [@CL-CTF-2994].

## How it works

The fast payment procedure allows payment under limited conditions to a contractor prior to the Government's verification that supplies have been received and accepted, based on the contractor's submission of an invoice [@CL-CTF-2978; @CL-CTF-2979]. The conditions for use cover the amount of the contract or order, the place of delivery, the passing of title, the supplier's agreement to replace, repair, or correct supplies, a firm-fixed-price contract or order, and a system to document contractor performance, give timely feedback to the contracting officer and identify suppliers abusing the procedure [@CL-CTF-2982; @CL-CTF-2983; @CL-CTF-2984; @CL-CTF-2985; @CL-CTF-2986; @CL-CTF-2987]. Executive agencies may permit higher dollar limitations for specified activities or items on a case-by-case basis [@CL-CTF-2982]. The contracting officer must be primarily responsible for determining the amount of debts [@CL-CTF-2981], and may elect, in the case of BPAs, to insert the clause either in the BPA or in orders under the BPA [@CL-CTF-2994]. The consignee shall notify the purchasing office promptly after the specified date of delivery of supplies not received, damaged in transit or not conforming to specifications of the purchase order [@CL-CTF-2991].

The contractor must replace, repair, or correct supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit, or not conforming to purchase agreements [@CL-CTF-2980]. The [eligibility and invoicing](../billing-payment/ctf-fast-payment-eligibility-and-invoicing.md) card covers the invoice and marking rules of the clause, and the [title, risk of loss and replacement](../billing-payment/ctf-fast-payment-title-risk-of-loss-and-replacement.md) card covers title and replacement under it.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buying office plans an order of 40,000 for supplies to be shipped to a remote receiving point. The order is firm-fixed-price [@CL-CTF-2986] and below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CTF-2982; @CL-FND-0001]. Assume that deliveries go where geographical separation and poor communications make payment on evidence of acceptance impractical [@CL-CTF-2983], that title passes on delivery to the carrier [@CL-CTF-2984], that the supplier agrees to replace, repair or correct lost or defective supplies [@CL-CTF-2985], and that a performance system is in place [@CL-CTF-2987]. With those conditions met, fast payment procedures may be used [@CL-CTF-2995]. Because the buying office intends to use the procedure, the clause is inserted [@CL-CTF-2993]. The purchasing instrument requires prepaid shipment, direct invoicing to the designated finance office and the consignee statement [@CL-CTF-2988; @CL-CTF-2989; @CL-CTF-2990]. The contractor delivers the supplies to a common carrier and invoices 40,000, which certifies that it delivered them and that it must replace, repair or correct supplies not received, damaged or nonconforming [@CL-CTF-2979; @CL-CTF-2980]. Payment is made before the receiving point confirms receipt [@CL-CTF-2978]. Twenty days after the specified delivery date, the consignee tells the purchasing office that supplies worth 3,000 were damaged in transit, which is within the period of not later than 60 days that the text says the notification should meet [@CL-CTF-2991; @CL-CTF-2992]. The contractor must replace, repair or correct them [@CL-CTF-2980; @CL-CTF-2985]. If it does not do so properly, the contracting officer must be primarily responsible for determining the amount of the debt [@CL-CTF-2981].

## Never confuse

Fast payment pays before the Government verifies receipt and acceptance, on the contractor's invoice certification [@CL-CTF-2978; @CL-CTF-2979]. The clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when the conditions in 32.1202 are applicable and it is intended that the fast payment procedure be used in the contract [@CL-CTF-2993]. The consignee shall notify the purchasing office promptly after the specified date of delivery [@CL-CTF-2991], and the contractor must replace, repair, or correct supplies not received at destination, damaged in transit, or not conforming to purchase agreements [@CL-CTF-2980].
