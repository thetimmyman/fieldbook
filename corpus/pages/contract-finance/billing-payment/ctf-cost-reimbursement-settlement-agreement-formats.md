## Purpose

The supplemental agreement formats for a completely terminated cost-reimbursement contract settled on fee alone, the rights and liabilities that format reserves, and the format for a partial termination.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3271] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.603-4 and FAR 49.603-5 [@CL-CTF-3271; @CL-CTF-3285]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CTF-9424] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3271] |
| Applies to | Cost-reimbursement contracts that are completely terminated with settlement limited to fee, and cost-reimbursement contracts that are partially terminated [@CL-CTF-3271; @CL-CTF-3285] |

For a completely terminated cost-reimbursement contract where settlement is limited to fee, the format is inserted in Block 14 of Standard Form 30 [@CL-CTF-3271]. The supplemental agreement settles the amount of fee due under the contract, terminated in its entirety by a notice of termination [@CL-CTF-3272]. It states the amount the contractor has received on account of its fee under the contract before the effective date of termination [@CL-CTF-3273]. The FAR definitions section describes a supplemental agreement as a contract modification that is accomplished by the mutual action of the parties [@CL-CTF-9424]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines a supplemental agreement as a contract modification that is accomplished by the mutual action of the parties, the same in substance as the codified definition [@CL-CTF-9454; @CL-CTF-9424].

It also states that the Government agrees to pay the contractor or its assignee, upon presentation of a proper invoice or voucher, the net amount to be paid on account of fee [@CL-CTF-3274]. That net sum, with sums previously paid, constitutes payment in full and complete settlement of the amount due the contractor on account of its fee under the contract [@CL-CTF-3131]. The agreement states that the contractor's allowable costs under the contract will be paid under the terms and conditions of the contract and parts 31 and 49 of the FAR [@CL-CTF-3275]. That statement is inserted only if there are costs to be vouchered out or if there are costs to be covered later by a separate settlement agreement [@CL-CTF-3276].

## How it works

Regardless of any other provision of the agreement, the listed rights and liabilities of the parties under the contract are reserved [@CL-CTF-3277]. The suggested language of the excepted items may be varied at the discretion of the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-3278]. If accuracy or completeness can be achieved by referencing the number of a contract clause or provision covering the matter, that method of enumerating reserved rights and liabilities is to be followed [@CL-CTF-3279]. Items that are not applicable are omitted, and any additional exceptions or reservations required are added [@CL-CTF-3280]. The complete-termination format lists these reserved items:

- Matters covered by any renegotiation authority: all rights and liabilities, if any, of the parties [@CL-CTF-3132].
- Clauses inserted in the contract because of the requirements of Acts of Congress and Executive Orders: all rights and liabilities, if any, of the parties [@CL-CTF-3281], including, without limitation, any applicable clauses relating to labor law, contingent fees, domestic articles and employment of aliens [@CL-CTF-3282]. If the contract contains clauses of that character inserted for other reasons, the suggested language should be appropriately modified [@CL-CTF-3283].
- Reproduction rights, patent infringements, inventions or applications for patents: all rights and liabilities of the parties arising under the contract [@CL-CTF-3133], including rights to assignments, invention reports, licenses, covenants of indemnity against patent risks, and bonds for patent indemnity obligations, together with all rights and liabilities under the bonds [@CL-CTF-3284].
- Defects, guarantees or warranties: all rights and liabilities of the parties, arising under the contract or otherwise, relating to any articles or component parts furnished to the Government by the contractor under the contract or the agreement [@CL-CTF-3134].
- Agreements on the future care and disposition by the contractor of Government-owned property remaining in its custody: all rights and liabilities of the parties [@CL-CTF-3135].
- Government property furnished to, or acquired by, the contractor for the performance of the contract: all rights and liabilities of the parties [@CL-CTF-3136].
- Options other than options to continue or increase the work under the contract, covenants not to compete, and covenants of indemnity: all rights and liabilities of the parties under the contract [@CL-CTF-3137].
- Clauses relating to price reductions for defective certified cost or pricing data: all rights and liabilities, if any, of the parties [@CL-CTF-3138].

For a partial termination, the format is inserted in Block 14 of Standard Form 30 [@CL-CTF-3285]. The supplemental agreement settles the termination settlement proposal resulting from the notice of termination [@CL-CTF-3286]. It amends the contract by deleting the terminated portion, specified clearly as to line item numbers, descriptions and quantity terminated [@CL-CTF-3287], and also as to the unit and total price of terminated items and any other explanation necessary to avoid uncertainty or misunderstanding [@CL-CTF-3288]. It decreases the fee stated in the contract and, if appropriate, the estimated cost of the contract [@CL-CTF-3139]. The contractor's allowable costs and earned fee, if any, for the terminated portion continue to be reimbursed on Standard Form 1034 under the applicable provisions of the contract and part 31 of the FAR [@CL-CTF-3140].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement contract is completely terminated and settlement is limited to fee. The contractor received 40,000 of fee before the effective date, and the agreed fee settlement is 55,000 in total, so the net amount to be paid on account of fee is 15,000 [@CL-CTF-3273; @CL-CTF-3274]. That 15,000, with the 40,000 previously paid, is payment in full and complete settlement of the fee [@CL-CTF-3131]. Some allowable costs are still to be vouchered out, so the statement that allowable costs are paid under the contract and parts 31 and 49 of the FAR is inserted [@CL-CTF-3275; @CL-CTF-3276].

The contracting officer keeps the reserved items on warranties and Government property, omits the patent item because the contract has no such clause, and adds one reservation for a disputed claim [@CL-CTF-3134; @CL-CTF-3136; @CL-CTF-3280]. The suggested wording may be varied at the contracting officer's discretion [@CL-CTF-3278].

A second cost-reimbursement contract is partially terminated. The agreement deletes line item 3 with its quantity and price, decreases the fee from 100,000 to 80,000, and, because a cost decrease is appropriate here, decreases the estimated cost from 1,000,000 to 800,000 [@CL-CTF-3287; @CL-CTF-3288; @CL-CTF-3139]. Allowable costs and earned fee on the deleted line item continue to be reimbursed on Standard Form 1034 [@CL-CTF-3140].

## Never confuse

Where settlement is limited to fee, the contractor's allowable costs under the contract will be paid under the terms and conditions of the contract and parts 31 and 49 of the FAR [@CL-CTF-3275], and that subparagraph is inserted only if there are costs to be vouchered out or if there are costs to be covered later by a separate settlement agreement [@CL-CTF-3276].

The partial-termination format deletes the terminated portion and decreases the fee and, if appropriate, the estimated cost [@CL-CTF-3287; @CL-CTF-3139], and the contractor's allowable costs and earned fee, if any, for the terminated portion continue to be reimbursed on SF 1034 under the applicable provisions of the contract and part 31 [@CL-CTF-3140].

The authorization and delinquency notices for these agreements are on [the cost-reimbursement settlement format card](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-reimbursement-settlement-format-and-delinquency.md).
