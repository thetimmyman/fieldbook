## Purpose

Explains when an invoice payment is due, when a late payment interest penalty arises, and why that treatment does not extend to contract financing.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0129] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-25 [@CL-CTF-0129] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-25 [@CL-CTF-0642; @CL-CTF-0643; @CL-CTF-0644; @CL-CTF-0645; @CL-CTF-0646; @CL-CTF-0647; @CL-CTF-0648; @CL-CTF-0649; @CL-CTF-0650; @CL-CTF-0651; @CL-CTF-0652; @CL-CTF-9132; @CL-CTF-3205; @CL-CTF-3206; @CL-CTF-3207] |

Except as indicated in paragraph (a)(2) of the clause, which is headed certain food products and other payments [@CL-CTF-9634], and in paragraph (c), which covers fast payment procedure due dates [@CL-CTF-0135], the due date for making invoice payments by the designated payment office is the later of two events [@CL-CTF-0130]: the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (a)(1)(ii) of the clause) [@CL-CTF-0130; @CL-CTF-9633], and the 30th day after Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-9633].

If the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the invoice payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice [@CL-CTF-0485], provided the designated billing office receives a proper invoice and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0486]. The designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions listed in paragraphs (a)(4)(i) through (a)(4)(iii) of the clause are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-0131]. However, when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the designated payment office may make payment on the following working day without incurring a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-0484].

## How it works

All days referred to in the clause are calendar days, unless otherwise specified [@CL-CTF-0133]. If an invoice does not comply with the clause's content requirements, the designated billing office will return it within 7 days after receipt (3 days for meat, meat food products, or fish [@CL-CTF-0132]; 5 days for perishable agricultural commodities, dairy products, edible fats or oils, and food products prepared from edible fats or oils), with the reasons why it is not a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-0132; @CL-CTF-0279].

The designated payment office will pay a penalty amount, calculated in accordance with the prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315, in addition to the interest penalty amount only if [@CL-CTF-9525] the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more [@CL-CTF-9523], the designated payment office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-9524], and the contractor makes a written demand to the designated payment office for additional penalty payment, in accordance with paragraph (a)(7)(ii) of the clause, postmarked not later than 40 days after the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-0134]. If the contract contains the clause at 52.213-1, Fast Payment Procedure, payments will be made within 15 days after the date of receipt of the invoice [@CL-CTF-0135].

DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 52 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-25 is prescribed in FAR 32.908(c) [@CL-CTF-0642], matching the codified prescription [@CL-CTF-0129], and states that all days referred to in the clause are calendar days, unless otherwise specified [@CL-CTF-3207], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-0133]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-25, except as indicated in paragraphs (a)(2) and (c) of the clause, the due date for making invoice payments by the designated payment office is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor (except as provided in paragraph (a)(1)(ii) of the clause) [@CL-CTF-0643; @CL-CTF-9132] and the 30th day after Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-0644], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-0130; @CL-CTF-9633]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-25, if the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the invoice payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice [@CL-CTF-0645], provided the designated billing office receives a proper invoice and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0646], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-0485; @CL-CTF-0486]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-25, the designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions listed in paragraphs (a)(4)(i) through (a)(4)(iii) of the clause are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-0647], and when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the designated payment office may make payment on the following working day without incurring a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-0648], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-0131; @CL-CTF-0484]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-25, if the invoice does not comply with the requirements, the designated billing office will return it within 7 days after receipt (3 days for meat, meat food products, or fish [@CL-CTF-0649]; 5 days for perishable agricultural commodities, dairy products, edible fats or oils, and food products prepared from edible fats or oils), with the reasons why it is not a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-0649; @CL-CTF-0650], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-0132; @CL-CTF-0279]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-25, the designated payment office will pay a penalty amount, calculated in accordance with the prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315, in addition to the interest penalty amount only if [@CL-CTF-3205] the Government owes an interest penalty of $1 or more, the designated payment office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-3206], and the contractor makes a written demand to the designated payment office for additional penalty payment, in accordance with paragraph (a)(7)(ii) of the clause, postmarked not later than 40 days after the invoice amount is paid [@CL-CTF-0651], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-9525; @CL-CTF-9523; @CL-CTF-9524; @CL-CTF-0134]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-25, if the contract contains the clause at 52.213-1, Fast Payment Procedure, payments will be made within 15 days after the date of receipt of the invoice [@CL-CTF-0652], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-0135].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A proper invoice for 60,000 is received on the first day of a month and no acceptance question arises, so payment is due on the 30th day after receipt, which is day 31 counting the day of receipt as day 1. Payment is made on day 41, and an interest penalty for the 10 late days is computed automatically and paid without any request.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-25, the designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions listed in paragraphs (a)(4)(i) through (a)(4)(iii) of the clause are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-0131], whereas under FAR 52.216-7(a)(2) contract financing payments are not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-0136].

Under FAR 52.216-7(a)(2), interim payments made prior to the final payment under the contract are contract financing payments, except interim payments if the contract contains Alternate I to the clause at 52.232-25 [@CL-CTF-0488]. Under FAR 52.232-16(b), except as provided in the Termination for Convenience of the Government clause, all progress payments shall be liquidated by deducting from any payment under the contract, other than advance or progress payments, the unliquidated progress payments, or 80 percent of the amount invoiced, whichever is less [@CL-CTF-0104; @CL-CTF-0491]; see [Progress payments](../financing/ctf-progress-payments.md).
