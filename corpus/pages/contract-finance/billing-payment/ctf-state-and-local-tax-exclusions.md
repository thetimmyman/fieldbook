## Purpose

Explains how the State and Local Taxes clause takes State and local taxes out of the contract price, and how the Federal, State, and Local Taxes clause with State and local adjustments puts applicable taxes in the price and then moves the price when a tax is imposed, relieved or borne through the Contractor's fault.

Under FAR 52.229-1, State and Local Taxes, notwithstanding the terms of the Federal, State, and Local Taxes clause, the contract price excludes all State and local taxes levied on or measured by the contract or sales price of the services or completed supplies furnished under the contract [@CL-CTF-2521]. The Contractor shall state separately on its invoices taxes excluded from the contract price, and the Government agrees either to pay the amount of the taxes to the Contractor or provide evidence necessary to sustain an exemption [@CL-CTF-2522]. Under FAR 2.101, State and local taxes means taxes levied by the States, the District of Columbia, outlying areas of the United States, or their political subdivisions [@CL-CTF-9466].

Under FAR 52.229-4, Federal, State, and Local Taxes (State and Local Adjustments), unless otherwise provided in the contract, the contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties, except as provided in subparagraph (b)(2)(i) of the clause [@CL-CTF-2523]. Taxes imposed [@CL-CTF-2524] under 26 U.S.C. 5000C may not be included in the contract price nor reimbursed [@CL-CTF-2524].

In FAR 52.229-4(a), all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties means all taxes and duties, in effect on the contract date, that the taxing authority is imposing and collecting on the transactions or property covered by the contract [@CL-CTF-2791]. Contract date means the effective date of the contract and, for any modification to the contract, the effective date of the modification [@CL-CTF-2792]. Local taxes includes taxes imposed by a possession or territory of the United States, Puerto Rico, or the Northern Mariana Islands, if the contract is performed wholly or partly in any of those areas [@CL-CTF-2793].

## How it works

In FAR 52.229-4(a), after-imposed tax means any new or increased Federal, State, or local tax or duty, or tax that was excluded on the contract date but whose exclusion was later revoked or amount of exemption reduced during the contract period, other than an excepted tax [@CL-CTF-2612], on the transactions or property covered by the contract that the Contractor is required to pay or bear as the result of legislative, judicial, or administrative action taking effect after the contract date [@CL-CTF-2613].

After-relieved tax means any amount of Federal, State, or local tax or duty, other than an excepted tax, that would otherwise have been payable on the transactions or property covered by the contract [@CL-CTF-2614], but which the Contractor is not required to pay or bear, or for which the Contractor obtains a refund or drawback, as the result of legislative, judicial, or administrative action taking effect after the contract date [@CL-CTF-2615].

Excepted tax means social security or other employment taxes, net income and franchise taxes, excess profits taxes, capital stock taxes, transportation taxes, unemployment compensation taxes, and property taxes [@CL-CTF-2616]. Excepted tax does not include gross income taxes levied on or measured by sales or receipts from sales, property taxes assessed on completed supplies covered by the contract, or any tax assessed on the Contractor's possession of, interest in, or use of property, title to which is in the Government [@CL-CTF-2617].

The contract price shall be increased by the amount of any after-imposed tax, or of any tax or duty specifically excluded from the contract price by a term or condition of the contract that the Contractor is required to pay or bear, including any interest or penalty [@CL-CTF-2567], if the Contractor states in writing that the contract price does not include any contingency for such tax and if liability for such tax, interest, or penalty was not incurred through the Contractor's fault, negligence, or failure to follow instructions of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2525].

The contract price shall be decreased by the amount of any after-relieved tax, and the Government shall be entitled to interest received by the Contractor incident to a refund of taxes to the extent that such interest was earned after the Contractor was paid by the Government for such taxes [@CL-CTF-2526]. The Government shall also be entitled to repayment of any penalty refunded to the Contractor to the extent that the penalty was paid by the Government [@CL-CTF-2794]. The contract price shall also be decreased by the amount of any Federal, State, or local tax, other than an excepted tax, that was included in the contract price and that the Contractor is required to pay or bear, or does not obtain a refund of [@CL-CTF-2568], through the Contractor's fault, negligence, or failure to follow instructions of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2569].

No adjustment shall be made in the contract price under the clause unless the amount of the adjustment exceeds $250 [@CL-CTF-2527].

The Contractor shall promptly notify the Contracting Officer of all matters relating to Federal, State, and local taxes and duties that reasonably may be expected to result in either an increase or decrease in the contract price and shall take appropriate action as the Contracting Officer directs [@CL-CTF-2528]. The contract price shall be equitably adjusted to cover the costs of action taken by the Contractor at the direction of the Contracting Officer, including any interest, penalty, and reasonable attorneys' fees [@CL-CTF-2570].

The Government shall furnish evidence appropriate to establish exemption from any Federal, State, or local tax when the Contractor requests such exemption and states in writing that it applies to a tax excluded from the contract price and a reasonable basis exists to sustain the exemption [@CL-CTF-2611].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A firm-fixed-price service contract with the clause with State and local adjustments has 40,000 of Federal and State taxes in its price. After the contract date a State law adds 3,000 of tax on the same transactions. The Contractor must pay it, states in writing that the price carries no contingency for it, and did not incur the liability through its own fault, negligence, or failure to follow the Contracting Officer's instructions. The price increases by 3,000, which exceeds 250. A later court ruling relieves 200 of tax, an after-relieved tax, and no adjustment is made because 200 does not exceed 250. A later change in law gives the Contractor a refund of 1,000 of tax, with 40 of interest earned after the Government paid the Contractor for the tax, so the price decreases by 1,000 and the Government is entitled to the 40. The Contractor also receives a refund of a 300 penalty, of which the Government had paid 120, so the Government is entitled to repayment of 120.

A tax of 5,000 that the State was already imposing and collecting on the covered transactions on the contract date is among the applicable taxes already in the price, so the price does not move for it. On a contract performed partly in Puerto Rico, a tax imposed by Puerto Rico is a local tax. For a later modification, the contract date is the modification's effective date.

An increase in social security tax is an excepted tax, so it is not an after-imposed tax. The Contractor also pays 1,500 of State tax that was in the price because it failed to follow the Contracting Officer's instructions, so the price decreases by 1,500.

Under the State and Local Taxes clause, the Contractor shows 2,000 of State sales tax separately on an invoice. That tax is outside the price, and the Government either pays the 2,000 or provides evidence that sustains an exemption.

## Never confuse

The two clauses take opposite sides on price rather than overlapping: under FAR 52.229-1, the contract price excludes all State and local taxes levied on or measured by the contract or sales price of the services or completed supplies furnished under the contract, and the Contractor shall state separately on its invoices taxes excluded from the contract price while the Government agrees either to pay the amount of the taxes to the Contractor or provide evidence necessary to sustain an exemption [@CL-CTF-2521; @CL-CTF-2522], whereas under FAR 52.229-4, unless otherwise provided in the contract, the contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties (except as provided in subparagraph (b)(2)(i) of the clause) [@CL-CTF-2523]. Under FAR 52.229-4(a), an excepted tax (social security or other employment taxes, net income and franchise taxes, excess profits taxes, capital stock taxes, transportation taxes, unemployment compensation taxes, and property taxes, subject to the exclusions in the clause) is excluded from the definitions of after-imposed tax and after-relieved tax [@CL-CTF-2612; @CL-CTF-2614; @CL-CTF-2616; @CL-CTF-2617].

For the separate Federal, State, and Local Taxes clause, see [Federal, State, and local taxes](ctf-federal-state-local-taxes.md).
