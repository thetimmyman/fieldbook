## Purpose

Defines the electronic payment terms, shows that an electronic transfer does not replace an assignment of claims, and covers the EFT information an offeror submits.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3591] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1102, 32.1105 and 32.1109 [@CL-CTF-3591; @CL-CTF-3595; @CL-CTF-3597] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1102 [@CL-CTF-4047; @CL-CTF-4048; @CL-CTF-4049; @CL-CTF-4050]; RFO FAR 32.1105 [@CL-CTF-4051; @CL-CTF-4052]; RFO FAR 32.1109 [@CL-CTF-4053; @CL-CTF-4054] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3591] |
| Applies to | Electronic funds transfer payments, including purchase card payments, and EFT information that offerors are required to submit before award [@CL-CTF-3591; @CL-CTF-3593; @CL-CTF-3597] |

In the electronic funds transfer subpart, EFT information means the information necessary for making a payment by electronic funds transfer through specified EFT mechanisms [@CL-CTF-3591]. A Governmentwide commercial purchase card means a card that is similar in nature to a commercial credit card and is used to make financing and delivery payments for supplies and services [@CL-CTF-3592]. The purchase card is an EFT method and may be used to meet the requirement to pay by EFT to the extent that purchase card limits do not preclude such payments [@CL-CTF-3593]. Payment information means the payment advice provided by the Government to the contractor that identifies what the payment is for, any computations or adjustments made by the Government, and any information required by the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-3594].

## How it works

Electronic payment does not replace an assignment of claims. The use of EFT payment methods is not a substitute for a properly executed assignment of claims in accordance with Subpart 32.8 [@CL-CTF-3595]. EFT information that shows the ultimate recipient of the transfer to be other than the contractor, in the absence of a proper assignment of claims, is considered incorrect EFT information within the meaning of the Suspension of Payment paragraphs of the EFT clauses at 52.232-33 and 52.232-34 [@CL-CTF-3596]. The clauses' own paragraphs say what follows; see the [EFT clauses card](ctf-eft-payment-clauses-and-designated-office.md).

On the offeror side, if offerors are required to submit EFT information prior to award, the successful offeror is not responsible for resubmitting it after award of the contract, except to make changes or to place the information on invoices if agency procedures require that [@CL-CTF-3597]. Where the successful offeror is not responsible for resubmitting, contracting officers shall forward the EFT information the successful offeror provided to the appropriate office [@CL-CTF-3598].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.1102 keeps the definitions of Electronic Funds Transfer information (EFT), meaning information necessary for making a payment by EFT through specified EFT mechanisms [@CL-CTF-4047], of the Governmentwide commercial purchase card [@CL-CTF-4048] and of payment information [@CL-CTF-4050]. It keeps the rule that the purchase card is an EFT method that may be used as a means to meet the requirement to pay by EFT, to the extent that purchase card limits do not preclude such payments [@CL-CTF-4049]. RFO FAR 32.1105 keeps the rule that the use of EFT payment methods is not a substitute for a properly executed assignment of claims in accordance with Subpart 32.8 [@CL-CTF-4051], and the rule that EFT information that shows the ultimate recipient of the transfer to be other than the contractor, in the absence of a proper assignment of claims, is considered to be incorrect EFT information within the meaning of the Suspension of Payment paragraphs of the EFT clauses at 52.232-33 and 52.232-34 [@CL-CTF-4052]. RFO FAR 32.1109 keeps the rule that, if offerors are required to submit EFT information prior to award, the successful offeror is not responsible for resubmitting this information after award of the contract except to make changes, or to place the information on invoices if required by agency procedures [@CL-CTF-4053]. Where the codified text says contracting officers "shall" forward the EFT information provided by the successful offeror to the appropriate office [@CL-CTF-3598], the RFO text says "must" [@CL-CTF-4054]; the duty is the same.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation requires EFT information with the offer, and the offeror submits it. After award the contractor does not resubmit the information, because the successful offeror is not responsible for resubmitting it [@CL-CTF-3597]. The contractor later changes banks, so it submits the change, because the rule's exception for making changes applies [@CL-CTF-3597]. If the agency's procedures require the information on invoices, the contractor places it there [@CL-CTF-3597]. The contracting officer forwards the information provided by the successful offeror to the appropriate office [@CL-CTF-3598]. Separately, a lender finances the contractor, but no assignment of claims exists, and an invoice names the lender's account as the ultimate recipient of the transfer. That is considered incorrect EFT information within the meaning of the Suspension of Payment paragraphs of the EFT clauses [@CL-CTF-3596]. Paying by card under the purchase card method counts toward the requirement to pay by EFT unless card limits preclude the payment [@CL-CTF-3593].

## Never confuse

EFT information is often confused with an assignment of claims. Paying through an electronic transfer does not assign the claim, and information naming an ultimate recipient other than the contractor without a proper assignment is incorrect EFT information [@CL-CTF-3595; @CL-CTF-3596]. EFT information is also confused with payment information. EFT information is what is needed to make the payment, while payment information is the advice the Government sends the contractor about what the payment is for and any adjustments [@CL-CTF-3591; @CL-CTF-3594]. The [assignment of claims card](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md) covers a proper assignment.

## Company calibration

Whether the EFT information on file matches the account used for each contract, whether any lender account appears without a proper assignment, and where changes are submitted after award. [@Q-026]
