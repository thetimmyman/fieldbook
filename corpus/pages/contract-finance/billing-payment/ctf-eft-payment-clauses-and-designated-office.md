## Purpose

Shows what the contractor must register or provide for payment by electronic funds transfer, when a payment is treated as timely and what follows from wrong information.

### System for Award Management clause

All payments under the contract are made by electronic funds transfer, except as provided in paragraph (a)(2) of the clause, which applies where the Government is unable to release one or more payments by EFT [@CL-CTF-2041; @CL-CTF-2201]. In that event the Contractor agrees to accept payment by check or some other mutually agreeable method, or to request that the Government extend the payment due date until it can pay by EFT (but see the suspension of payment paragraph of the clause) [@CL-CTF-2201; @CL-CTF-9611]. The Government pays using the EFT information in the System for Award Management, and the Contractor is responsible for providing updated information to SAM if it changes [@CL-CTF-2042].

If the EFT information in SAM is incorrect, the Government need not make payment until correct information is entered [@CL-CTF-2043]. Any invoice or contract financing request is then deemed not to be a proper invoice for the purpose of prompt payment, and the prompt payment terms on notice of an improper invoice and on delays in accrual of interest penalties apply [@CL-CTF-2147].

### Timeliness and erroneous transfers

A payment is deemed timely under the prompt payment terms if the settlement date in the EFT instruction released to the Federal Reserve System is on or before the prompt payment due date [@CL-CTF-2044], subject to a proviso that the specified payment date is a valid date under the rules of the Federal Reserve System [@CL-CTF-2135]. Under the liability rule for uncompleted or erroneous transfers, if an uncompleted or erroneous transfer occurs because the Contractor's EFT information was incorrect, or was revised within 30 days of Government release of the EFT payment transaction instruction to the Federal Reserve System, and the funds are no longer under the control of the payment office, the Government is deemed to have made payment and the Contractor is responsible for recovery of any erroneously directed funds [@CL-CTF-3022; @CL-CTF-2045; @CL-CTF-9612]. If instead the funds remain under the control of the payment office, the Government shall not make payment and the provisions of the suspension of payment paragraph shall apply [@CL-CTF-3022; @CL-CTF-2202]. If an uncompleted or erroneous transfer occurs because the Government used the Contractor's EFT information incorrectly, the Government remains responsible for making a correct payment, paying any prompt payment penalty due and recovering any erroneously directed funds [@CL-CTF-2203].

### Other-than-SAM clause and designated office

Under the other-than-SAM clause, the Contractor provides the information required to pay by EFT directly to the designated office [@CL-CTF-2046]. That information includes the name, address and routing number of the Contractor's financial agent, the account number and type of account, and, if applicable, the Fedwire abbreviation of the financial agent [@CL-CTF-2146]. The Government need not pay until that office has the correct information [@CL-CTF-2047]. The clause text tells the Contracting Officer to insert a date, a number of days after award, a number of days before the first request, the date specified for receipt of offers if the provision at FAR 52.232-38 is utilized, or concurrent with first request, as prescribed by the head of the agency [@CL-CTF-2204]. If the date is not prescribed, the date to insert is "no later than 15 days prior to submission of the first request for payment" [@CL-CTF-2048].

Under the designation clause, the Contractor sends all EFT information and changes to the designated office and not to the payment office or any other office [@CL-CTF-2049]. The Government need not use information sent elsewhere [@CL-CTF-2050].

## How it works

Under the System for Award Management clause, the Government makes payment using the EFT information contained in SAM, and the Contractor is responsible for providing the updated information to SAM if the EFT information changes [@CL-CTF-2042]. Under the other-than-SAM clause, the Contractor provides the information required to make payment by EFT directly to the office designated in the contract to receive it [@CL-CTF-2046]. Under the System for Award Management clause, the Government need not pay until correct information is entered into SAM [@CL-CTF-2043]. Under the other-than-SAM clause, it is not required to pay until the designated office has received the correct information from the Contractor [@CL-CTF-2047]. Under the System for Award Management clause, a payment is timely if the settlement date in the instruction released to the Federal Reserve System is on or before the prompt payment due date, provided the specified payment date is a valid date under the rules of the Federal Reserve System [@CL-CTF-2044; @CL-CTF-2135].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause at FAR 52.232-33 keeps the rule that all payments are made by EFT except as provided in paragraph (a)(2) of the clause [@CL-CTF-0200] [@CL-CTF-2041], and if the Government is unable to release a payment by EFT the contractor agrees to accept payment by check or another mutually agreeable method or to request an extension of the payment due date until the Government can pay by EFT (but see paragraph (d) of the clause) [@CL-CTF-0206] [@CL-CTF-9027] [@CL-CTF-2201]. The Government pays using the EFT information in SAM, and the contractor is responsible for providing updated information to SAM if it changes [@CL-CTF-0207] [@CL-CTF-2042]. If the EFT information in SAM is incorrect, the Government need not pay until correct information is entered [@CL-CTF-0208] [@CL-CTF-2043], and any invoice or contract financing request is deemed not to be a proper invoice, with the prompt payment terms on notice of an improper invoice and delays in accrual of interest penalties applying [@CL-CTF-0209] [@CL-CTF-2147].

The liability rules are the same: if the Government used the information incorrectly, it remains responsible for making a correct payment, paying any prompt payment penalty due and recovering any erroneously directed funds [@CL-CTF-0210] [@CL-CTF-2203]. If an uncompleted or erroneous transfer occurs because the contractor's information was incorrect, or was revised within 30 days of Government release of the EFT payment transaction instruction to the Federal Reserve System, and the funds are no longer under the control of the payment office, the Government is deemed to have made payment and the contractor is responsible for recovery of any erroneously directed funds [@CL-CTF-3023] [@CL-CTF-0211] [@CL-CTF-9026] [@CL-CTF-3022] [@CL-CTF-2045]; if the funds remain under the control of the payment office, the Government shall not make payment and the provisions of paragraph (d) of the clause shall apply [@CL-CTF-3023] [@CL-CTF-0212] [@CL-CTF-3022] [@CL-CTF-2202]. Payment is timely if the settlement date in the instruction released to the Federal Reserve System is on or before the prompt payment due date [@CL-CTF-0213] [@CL-CTF-2044], provided the specified date is a valid date under the rules of the Federal Reserve System [@CL-CTF-0214] [@CL-CTF-2135].

The RFO clause at FAR 52.232-34 keeps the requirement that the contractor provide the information required for EFT payment directly to the office designated in the contract [@CL-CTF-0215] [@CL-CTF-2046]. That information includes the name, address and 9-digit Routing Transit Number of the financial agent, the account number and type of account, and, if applicable, the Fedwire telegraphic abbreviation [@CL-CTF-0219] [@CL-CTF-2146]. The Government is not required to pay until the designated office receives the correct information [@CL-CTF-0218] [@CL-CTF-2047]. The bracketed instruction to the contracting officer lists the same date options [@CL-CTF-0216] [@CL-CTF-2204] and the same default of no later than 15 days prior to submission of the first request for payment [@CL-CTF-0217] [@CL-CTF-2048]. The RFO clause at FAR 52.232-35 keeps the rule that the contractor sends all EFT information and changes to the designated office and not to the payment office or any other office [@CL-CTF-0220] [@CL-CTF-2049], and that the Government need not use information sent elsewhere [@CL-CTF-0221] [@CL-CTF-2050].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor changes banks but does not update its record in SAM, and it submits an invoice. The Government need not pay until the correct information is entered, and the invoice is treated as not proper for prompt payment. Once the correct information is entered, the payment is timely if the settlement date in the EFT instruction is on or before the prompt payment due date and is a valid Federal Reserve System date.

On a contract under the other-than-SAM clause with no date prescribed by the head of the agency (assumed), the first request for payment is planned for the 45th day after award. The contractor must give the designated office its financial agent's name, address and routing number, its account number and type, and any Fedwire abbreviation no later than the 30th day after award.

## Never confuse

Under the System for Award Management clause, the Contractor is responsible for providing updated information to SAM if the EFT information changes [@CL-CTF-2042], whereas under the designation clause the Contractor sends all EFT information and changes to the designated office and not to the payment office or any other office [@CL-CTF-2049]. For the SAM record itself, see [SAM registration and maintenance clauses: keeping the record current](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-sam-registration-and-maintenance-clauses.md). Which clause is inserted, and when the Government pays by EFT or purchase card, are on [EFT solicitation provision and contract clauses](../financing/ctf-eft-solicitation-provision-and-clauses.md) and [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](../financing/ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md). The EFT information an offeror sends with its offer is on [Invitation to propose financing terms and EFT information with an offer](ctf-financing-terms-and-eft-information-with-offer.md). Payment by a third party is on [Payment by third party and multiple payment arrangements](ctf-payment-by-third-party-and-multiple-payment-arrangements.md), what makes an invoice proper is on [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md), and due dates are on [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md).
