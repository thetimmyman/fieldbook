## Purpose

Shows who may end a fixed-price architect-engineer contract or a personal services contract, on what notice, and what is paid.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2076] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.249-7 and FAR 52.249-12 [@CL-CTF-2076; @CL-CTF-2081] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.249-7 [@CL-CTF-0653; @CL-CTF-0654; @CL-CTF-0655; @CL-CTF-0656; @CL-CTF-0657; @CL-CTF-0658; @CL-CTF-9131]; RFO FAR 52.249-12 [@CL-CTF-0659] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2076] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for architect-engineer services when a fixed-price contract is contemplated for FAR 52.249-7 [@CL-CLS-1023], and solicitations and contracts for personal services for FAR 52.249-12 [@CL-CTF-3153] |

Under the fixed-price architect-engineer clause, the Government may terminate the contract in whole or, from time to time, in part, for the Government's convenience or because of the failure of the contractor to fulfill the contract obligations [@CL-CTF-2076]. The contracting officer shall terminate by delivering to the contractor a Notice of Termination specifying the nature, extent, and effective date of the termination [@CL-CTF-2077].

Under the personal services clause, the Government may terminate the contract at any time upon at least 15 days' written notice by the contracting officer to the contractor, and the contractor, with the written consent of the contracting officer, may terminate the contract upon at least 15 days' written notice to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-2081].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.249-7(a), upon receipt of the notice, the contractor shall immediately discontinue all services affected, unless the notice directs otherwise, and deliver to the contracting officer all data, drawings, specifications, reports, estimates, summaries, and other information and materials accumulated in performing the contract, whether completed or in process [@CL-CTF-1475; @CL-CTF-2078; @CL-CTF-9635]. If the termination is for the convenience of the Government, the contracting officer shall make an equitable adjustment in the contract price but shall allow no anticipated profit on unperformed services [@CL-CTF-2079]. If the termination is for failure of the contractor to fulfill the contract obligations, the Government may complete the work by contract or otherwise and the contractor shall be liable for any additional cost incurred by the Government [@CL-CTF-2080]. If, after termination for failure to fulfill contract obligations, it is determined that the contractor had not failed, the rights and obligations of the parties shall be the same as if the termination had been issued for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-2238].

Payment terms for these contracts are on [Payments under fixed-price architect-engineer contracts](ctf-payments-fixed-price-architect-engineer.md) and [Payments under fixed-price research and development, personal services and transportation contracts](ctf-payments-rd-personal-services-and-transportation.md).

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-7(a), the Government may terminate the contract in whole or, from time to time, in part, for the Government's convenience or because of the failure of the contractor to fulfill the contract obligations [@CL-CTF-0653], and the contracting officer shall terminate by delivering to the contractor a Notice of Termination specifying the nature, extent, and effective date of the termination [@CL-CTF-0654]. Upon receipt of the notice, the contractor shall immediately discontinue all services affected, unless the notice directs otherwise, and deliver to the contracting officer all data, drawings, specifications, reports, estimates, summaries, and other information and materials accumulated in performing the contract, whether completed or in process [@CL-CTF-0655; @CL-CTF-9131]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-7(b)-(d), if the termination is for the convenience of the Government, the contracting officer shall make an equitable adjustment in the contract price but shall allow no anticipated profit on unperformed services [@CL-CTF-0656], if the termination is for failure of the contractor to fulfill the contract obligations, the Government may complete the work by contract or otherwise and the contractor shall be liable for any additional cost incurred by the Government [@CL-CTF-0657], and if, after termination for failure to fulfill contract obligations, it is determined that the contractor had not failed, the rights and obligations of the parties shall be the same as if the termination had been issued for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-0658]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-12, the Government may terminate the contract at any time upon at least 15 days' written notice by the contracting officer to the contractor, and the contractor, with the written consent of the contracting officer, may terminate the contract upon at least 15 days' written notice to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0659].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An architect-engineer firm holds a fixed-price design contract for 200,000. The Government terminates for convenience after the firm has done 120,000 of the services. The contracting officer makes an equitable adjustment in the price, but allows no anticipated profit on the 80,000 of services not performed. On a personal services contract, if the contracting officer gives notice on the first of the month, the termination cannot take effect earlier than 15 days later.

## Never confuse

The two termination grounds pay out differently rather than alike: under FAR 52.249-7, if the termination is for the convenience of the Government, the contracting officer shall make an equitable adjustment in the contract price but shall allow no anticipated profit on unperformed services [@CL-CTF-2079], whereas if the termination is for failure of the contractor to fulfill the contract obligations, the Government may complete the work by contract or otherwise and the contractor shall be liable for any additional cost incurred by the Government [@CL-CTF-2080] instead of collecting an adjustment; if, after termination for failure to fulfill contract obligations, it is determined that the contractor had not failed, the rights and obligations of the parties shall be the same as if the termination had been issued for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-2238]. Default terms for other fixed-price contracts are on [Default clauses for fixed-price contracts and when each goes in](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-default-clauses.md), and the supply and service default clause is on [Default under fixed-price supply and service contracts](ctf-default-fixed-price-supply-and-service.md). The short-form convenience clauses are on [Termination for convenience: fixed-price and services short forms](ctf-termination-for-convenience-short-forms.md).
