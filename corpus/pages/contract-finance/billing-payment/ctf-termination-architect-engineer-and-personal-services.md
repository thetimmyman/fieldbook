## Purpose

Shows who may end a fixed-price architect-engineer contract or a personal services contract, on what notice, and what is paid.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2076] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.249-7 and FAR 52.249-12 [@CL-CTF-2076; @CL-CTF-2081] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.249-7 [@CL-CTF-0653; @CL-CTF-0654; @CL-CTF-0655; @CL-CTF-0656; @CL-CTF-0657; @CL-CTF-0658; @CL-CTF-9131]; RFO FAR 52.249-12 [@CL-CTF-0659] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2076] |
| Applies to | The architect-engineer clause goes into solicitations and contracts for architect-engineer services when a fixed-price contract is contemplated [@CL-CLS-1023]. Not established for the personal services clause, whose prescription this card does not state. |

Under the fixed-price architect-engineer clause, the Government may terminate the contract in whole or from time to time in part, for the Government's convenience or because of the contractor's failure to fulfill the contract obligations [@CL-CTF-2076]. The contracting officer must terminate by delivering a Notice of Termination specifying the nature, extent and effective date of the termination [@CL-CTF-2077].

Under the personal services clause, the Government may terminate at any time on at least 15 days' written notice from the contracting officer, and the contractor may terminate, with the written consent of the contracting officer, on at least 15 days' written notice to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-2081].

## How it works

Under the notice paragraph of the architect-engineer clause, the contractor must immediately discontinue all services affected, unless the notice directs otherwise, and deliver to the contracting officer all data, drawings, specifications, reports, estimates, summaries and other information and materials accumulated in performing the contract, whether completed or in process [@CL-CTF-2078; @CL-CTF-9635]. If the termination is for the convenience of the Government, the contracting officer must make an equitable adjustment in the contract price but must allow no anticipated profit on unperformed services [@CL-CTF-2079]. If the termination is for the contractor's failure to fulfill the contract obligations, the Government may complete the work by contract or otherwise, and the contractor is liable for any additional cost the Government incurs [@CL-CTF-2080]. If, after a termination for failure to fulfill the contract obligations, it is determined that the contractor had not failed, the rights and obligations of the parties are the same as if the termination had been issued for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-2238].

The personal services clause prints no rule on what is paid after termination. Payment terms for these contracts are on [Payments under fixed-price architect-engineer contracts](ctf-payments-fixed-price-architect-engineer.md) and [Payments under fixed-price research and development, personal services and transportation contracts](ctf-payments-rd-personal-services-and-transportation.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.249-7 keeps the Government's right to terminate the contract in whole or from time to time in part, for the Government's convenience or because of the contractor's failure to fulfill the contract obligations [@CL-CTF-0653; @CL-CTF-2076], and the Notice of Termination specifying the nature, extent, and effective date of the termination [@CL-CTF-0654; @CL-CTF-2077]. It keeps the contractor's duties on receiving the notice to discontinue immediately all services affected, unless the notice directs otherwise, and to deliver to the contracting officer all data, drawings, specifications, reports, estimates, summaries, and other information and materials accumulated in performing the contract, whether completed or in process [@CL-CTF-0655; @CL-CTF-9131; @CL-CTF-2078]. It keeps the equitable adjustment with no anticipated profit on unperformed services after a termination for the Government's convenience [@CL-CTF-0656; @CL-CTF-2079], the Government's right to complete the work by contract or otherwise and the contractor's liability for any additional cost after a termination for failure to fulfill the contract obligations [@CL-CTF-0657; @CL-CTF-2080], and the rule that the parties' rights and obligations are the same as for a convenience termination if it is later determined that the contractor had not failed [@CL-CTF-0658; @CL-CTF-2238]. RFO FAR 52.249-12 keeps the Government's right to terminate at any time on at least 15 days' written notice from the contracting officer, and the contractor's right to terminate, with the written consent of the contracting officer, on at least 15 days' written notice to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0659; @CL-CTF-2081].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An architect-engineer firm holds a fixed-price design contract for 200,000. The Government terminates for convenience after the firm has done 120,000 of the services. The contracting officer makes an equitable adjustment in the price, but allows no anticipated profit on the 80,000 of services not performed. On a personal services contract, if the contracting officer gives notice on the first of the month, the termination cannot take effect earlier than 15 days later.

## Never confuse

The same architect-engineer clause pays differently by ground. A convenience termination brings an equitable adjustment with no anticipated profit on unperformed services [@CL-CTF-2079]. A termination for the contractor's failure can leave the contractor liable for the Government's additional cost of completing the work [@CL-CTF-2080], unless it is later determined that the contractor had not failed [@CL-CTF-2238]. Default terms for other fixed-price contracts are on [Default clauses for fixed-price contracts and when each goes in](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-default-clauses.md), and the supply and service default clause is on [Default under fixed-price supply and service contracts](ctf-default-fixed-price-supply-and-service.md). The short-form convenience clauses are on [Termination for convenience: fixed-price and services short forms](ctf-termination-for-convenience-short-forms.md).
