## Purpose

Shows when the contractor indemnifies the Government for infringement claims, when risk of loss passes, which taxes the contract price includes, and when title passes.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2439] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.212-4(h), (j), (k) and (n) [@CL-CTF-2439; @CL-CTF-2441; @CL-CTF-2442; @CL-CTF-2443] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.212-4(h) [@CL-CTF-0140; @CL-CTF-0141]; RFO FAR 52.212-4(j) [@CL-CTF-0142; @CL-CTF-0143]; RFO FAR 52.212-4(k) [@CL-CTF-0143]; RFO FAR 52.212-4(n) [@CL-CTF-0144] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2439] |
| Applies to | Contracts that contain the commercial products and services terms clause, as to the paragraphs quoted [@CL-CTF-2439] |

The contractor shall indemnify the Government and its officers, employees and agents against liability, including costs, for actual or alleged direct or contributory infringement of, or inducement to infringe, any United States or foreign patent, trademark or copyright [@CL-CTF-2439]. The patent indemnity covers liability arising out of the performance of the contract, provided the contractor is reasonably notified of such claims and proceedings [@CL-CTF-2440].

Unless the contract specifically provides otherwise, risk of loss or damage to the supplies remains with the contractor until, and passes to the Government upon, delivery of the supplies to a carrier if transportation is free on board origin [@CL-CTF-2441]. The second listed point at which risk of loss or damage passes to the Government is delivery of the supplies to the Government at the destination specified in the contract if transportation is free on board destination [@CL-CTF-2442]. The contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties [@CL-CTF-2442]. Unless specified elsewhere in the contract, title to items furnished under the contract passes to the Government upon acceptance, regardless of when or where the Government takes physical possession [@CL-CTF-2443].

## How it works

Risk of loss follows the transportation term. With a contract that does not provide otherwise, it stays with the contractor until delivery to a carrier under an origin term, or until delivery to the Government at the destination under a destination term [@CL-CTF-2441; @CL-CTF-2442]. Unless specified elsewhere in the contract, title passes to the Government upon acceptance, regardless of when or where the Government takes physical possession [@CL-CTF-2443]. The contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties [@CL-CTF-2442]. The indemnity is provided on the condition that the contractor is reasonably notified of such claims and proceedings [@CL-CTF-2440].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text keeps the patent indemnity in paragraph (h), the risk of loss and taxes rules in paragraphs (j) and (k), and the title rule in paragraph (n), under the same letters as the codified text [@CL-CTF-0140; @CL-CTF-0141; @CL-CTF-0142; @CL-CTF-0143; @CL-CTF-0144; @CL-CTF-2439; @CL-CTF-2440; @CL-CTF-2441; @CL-CTF-2442; @CL-CTF-2443].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor ships supplies priced at 10,000 under a contract that does not provide otherwise, with free on board origin transportation. The price already includes 500 of applicable taxes and duties, so the contractor does not add them on top. The supplies are handed to a carrier and are damaged in transit. Risk of loss passed to the Government on delivery to the carrier, so the loss falls on the Government. Under a second contract that says nothing elsewhere about title, the Government accepts supplies while they sit in a warehouse it does not yet occupy. Title passes to the Government on acceptance, whether or not it has taken physical possession. Had the first contract used free on board destination transportation, risk of loss would have passed on delivery to the Government at the destination specified in the contract instead. Separately, a third party sues the Government over a patent in the supplies, and the contractor is reasonably notified of the claim. Answer: the contractor indemnifies the Government for the liability, including costs.

## Never confuse

Unless specified elsewhere in the contract, title passes on acceptance, regardless of when or where the Government takes physical possession [@CL-CTF-2443], whereas risk of loss turns on the delivery term instead of on acceptance: unless the contract specifically provides otherwise, risk of loss passes on delivery to a carrier if transportation is free on board origin, or on delivery at the destination if it is free on board destination [@CL-CTF-2441; @CL-CTF-2442]. The patent indemnity in paragraph (h) covers liability for infringement of a United States or foreign patent, trademark or copyright, arising out of the performance of the contract [@CL-CTF-2439; @CL-CTF-2440]. See [Free on board origin delivery term](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-fob-origin-delivery-term.md), [Title and risk of loss under performance-based payments](../financing/ctf-pbp-title-and-risk-of-loss.md), [Federal, state and local taxes](ctf-federal-state-local-taxes.md) and [Patent indemnity and refund of royalties](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-patent-indemnity-and-refund-of-royalties.md).
