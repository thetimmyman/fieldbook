## Purpose

Shows what the supplemental cost principles clause adds to FAR allowability, and what data the Canadian Commercial Corporation provision requires in place of certified data.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1822] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.231-7000 [@CL-CTF-1822]; DFARS 252.215-7003 [@CL-CTF-1823] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1822] |
| Applies to | Contracts where allowability is determined under the FAR cost principles part, for the clause [@CL-CTF-1822]; the provision for the Canadian Commercial Corporation, for the provision [@CL-CTF-1823] |

When the allowability of costs under the contract is determined in accordance with part 31 of the FAR, allowability shall also be determined in accordance with part 231 of the DFARS in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-1822]. The DFARS directs use of the clause as prescribed in DFARS 231.100-70 [@CL-CTF-1838]. The FAR allowability tests are on [Factors determining cost allowability](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md), and how the FAR and its agency supplements fit together is on [The FAR System and agency supplements](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-far-system-and-agency-supplements.md).

The provision for the Canadian Commercial Corporation states that submission of certified cost or pricing data is not required [@CL-CTF-1823]. The DFARS directs use of the provision as prescribed in DFARS 215.408(2)(i) [@CL-CTF-1839]. The provision requires the Canadian Commercial Corporation to obtain and provide the profit rate or fee (as applicable) [@CL-CTF-1824]. One listed item is the analysis provided by Public Works and Government Services Canada to the Canadian Commercial Corporation to determine a fair and reasonable price, comparable to the analysis required at FAR 15.404-1 [@CL-CTF-1825]. One listed item is data other than certified cost or pricing data necessary to permit a determination by the United States Contracting Officer that the proposed price is fair and reasonable, with the Contracting Officer to insert a description of the data required [@CL-CTF-1826]. Each listed item is listed under the lead-in duty to obtain and provide the listed items [@CL-CTF-1824].

As specified in FAR 15.403-3(a)(4), an offeror who does not comply with a requirement to submit data that the United States Contracting Officer has deemed necessary to determine price reasonableness or cost realism is ineligible for award unless the head of the contracting activity determines that it is in the best interest of the Government to make the award to that offeror [@CL-CTF-1827; @CL-CTF-1828; @CL-CTF-1829]. The United States Contracting Officer is to insert the description of the data required, in accordance with FAR 15.403-3(a)(1) [@CL-CTF-1826]. The general exceptions to the requirement for certified data are on [Exceptions to the requirement for certified cost or pricing data](../../estimating/pricing/est-certified-data-exceptions.md).

## How it works

Under the cost principles clause, when allowability is determined in accordance with FAR part 31, it shall also be determined in accordance with DFARS part 231 in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-1822]. Under the Canadian provision, submission of certified cost or pricing data is not required, and the Canadian Commercial Corporation shall obtain and provide the listed items [@CL-CTF-1823; @CL-CTF-1824; @CL-CTF-1825; @CL-CTF-1826]. An offeror who does not comply with a requirement to submit data that the United States Contracting Officer has deemed necessary to determine price reasonableness or cost realism is ineligible for award unless the head of the contracting activity determines that it is in the best interest of the Government to make the award to that offeror [@CL-CTF-1827; @CL-CTF-1828]. The general data rules are on [Data other than certified data: what an auditor can examine](../../estimating/pricing/est-data-other-than-certified-audit-scope.md) and [Instructions for submitting cost or pricing data](../../estimating/pricing/est-data-submission-format-and-instructions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost passes the FAR allowability tests, but a DFARS cost principle in effect on the contract date makes it unallowable.

Answer: the cost is not allowable, because the clause applies both sets of principles.

The Canadian Commercial Corporation sends an offer that lists a profit rate and the Canadian analysis, but not the data the Contracting Officer deemed necessary to determine price reasonableness.

Answer: the offer is ineligible for award unless the head of the contracting activity decides the award is in the best interest of the Government.

## Never confuse

The Canadian provision states that certified cost or pricing data is not required, yet it still lists data the corporation shall obtain and provide [@CL-CTF-1823; @CL-CTF-1824].
