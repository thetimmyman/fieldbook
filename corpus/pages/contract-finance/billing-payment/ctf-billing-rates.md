## Purpose

Explains how billing rates are set for interim reimbursement under cost-reimbursement contracts and how they relate to the final indirect cost rates.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0123] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.704 [@CL-CTF-0123] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.504 [@CL-CTF-4798; @CL-CTF-4800] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0123] |
| Applies to | Cost-reimbursement contracts reimbursed at billing rates until final rates are established [@CL-CTF-0127] |

The contracting officer, cognizant Federal agency official, or auditor responsible for the final indirect cost rates also determines the billing rates, which should be as close as possible to the anticipated final rates, adjusted for unallowable costs [@CL-CTF-0123; @CL-CTF-0124].

Until final annual indirect cost rates are established, the Government reimburses at those billing rates and adjusts when the final rates are settled [@CL-CTF-0127].

## How it works

Billing rates may be revised prospectively or retroactively by mutual agreement at either party's request, to prevent substantial overpayment or underpayment [@CL-CTF-0125].

Where agreement cannot be reached, the contracting officer may determine the billing rates unilaterally [@CL-CTF-0125; @CL-CTF-0278].

Under the allowable cost and payment clause, interim amounts are reimbursed at the billing rates established by the contracting officer or the cognizant auditor [@CL-CTF-0127]. Under the basic clause the Government makes payments when requested as work progresses but, except for small business concerns, not more often than once every two weeks [@CL-CTF-0128]. Alternate II replaces that paragraph with one that has no small business exception [@CL-CTF-0487].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs it, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.504(a) keeps the rule that the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) or auditor responsible under RFO 42.505 for establishing the final indirect cost rates also determines the billing rates, but it says "must also determine" where the codified text says "shall also be responsible for determining" [@CL-CTF-4798; @CL-CTF-0123]. RFO FAR 42.504(b) tells the official, when establishing billing rates, to ensure that they are as close as possible to the final indirect cost rates anticipated for the contractor's fiscal period, as adjusted for any unallowable costs, where the codified text says billing rates should be as close as possible to those rates [@CL-CTF-4799; @CL-CTF-0124]. RFO FAR 42.504(c) keeps the rule that, once established, billing rates may be prospectively or retroactively revised by mutual agreement of the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) or auditor and the contractor at either party's request, to prevent substantial overpayment or underpayment [@CL-CTF-4800; @CL-CTF-0125]. It also keeps the rule that, when agreement cannot be reached, the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) may unilaterally determine the billing rates [@CL-CTF-4801; @CL-CTF-0278]. RFO FAR 42.504(d) keeps the rule that the elements of indirect cost and the base or bases used in computing billing rates are not to be construed as determinative of the indirect costs to be distributed or of the bases of distribution to be used in the final settlement, written as "Do not construe" in place of "shall not be construed" [@CL-CTF-4802; @CL-CTF-0126].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Overhead is billed at a provisional rate of 40 percent of direct labor while the fiscal year is open, so 500,000 of direct labor draws 200,000 of overhead reimbursement. The final rate settles lower, and the difference is recovered through the adjustment that follows settlement of the final rates.

## Never confuse

A billing rate is an interim reimbursement rate, not the final indirect cost rate, and the elements and bases used in computing billing rates are not determinative of the indirect costs distributed in final settlement [@CL-CTF-0126].

The final rates are settled separately; see [Final indirect cost rate proposal](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).

The codified text says billing rates should be as close as possible to the anticipated final indirect cost rates [@CL-CTF-0124], while the RFO text tells the official to ensure that they are [@CL-CTF-4799].
