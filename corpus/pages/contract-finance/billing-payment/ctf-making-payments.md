## Purpose

Shows how early the Government may pay an invoice, what the payment office does, and how partial deliveries are paid.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CTF-0914; @CL-CTF-0915] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.906 and DFARS 232.906 [@CL-CTF-0914; @CL-CTF-0915] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.906 [@CL-CTF-4592; @CL-CTF-4595] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0914] |
| Applies to | Invoice payments due under contract due dates [@CL-CTF-0914] |

The Government will not make invoice payments earlier than 7 days before the contract due date, unless the agency head determines to pay earlier case by case or that accelerated payment methods are necessary [@CL-CTF-0914]. The DFARS says the FAR 32.906 restriction does not apply to invoice payments made to small business concerns, but contractors are not entitled to interest penalties if the Government fails to make early payment [@CL-CTF-0915].

Where the nature of the work permits, contracting officers must write contract statements of work and pricing arrangements that allow contractors to deliver and receive invoice payments for discrete portions of the work as soon as completed and found acceptable by the Government [@CL-CTF-0918]. Unless specifically prohibited by the contract, the Payments clause at FAR 52.232-1 provides that the contractor is entitled to payment for accepted partial deliveries of supplies or partial performance of services that comply with all applicable contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0919] and for which prices can be calculated from the contract terms [@CL-CTF-0920].

## How it works

The designated payment office will mail checks on the same day they are dated and, for payments made by electronic funds transfer, will specify a date on or before the established due date for settlement of the payment at a Federal Reserve Bank [@CL-CTF-0916]. When it is determined that the designated billing office erroneously rejected a proper invoice and upon resubmission of the invoice, the designated payment office will enter in the payment system the original date the invoice was received by the designated billing office [@CL-CTF-0917] for the purpose of calculating the correct payment due date and any interest penalties that may be due [@CL-CTF-3026]. Under the DFARS, generally, the contracting officer must insert the standard due date of 14 days for interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services in the clause at FAR 52.232-25, Prompt Payment, when using the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-0921].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 32.906, the Government will not make invoice payments earlier than 7 days prior to the due dates specified in the contract unless the agency head determines to make earlier payment on a case-by-case basis or that the use of accelerated payment methods is necessary [@CL-CTF-4592]. The designated payment office will mail checks on the same day they are dated and, for payments made by EFT, will specify a date on or before the established due date for settlement of the payment at a Federal Reserve Bank [@CL-CTF-4593]. When it is determined that the designated billing office erroneously rejected a proper invoice and upon resubmission of the invoice, the designated payment office will enter in the payment system the original date the invoice was received by the designated billing office [@CL-CTF-4594] for the purpose of calculating the correct payment due date and any interest penalties that may be due [@CL-CTF-3027]. Where the nature of the work permits, contracting officers must write contract statements of work and pricing arrangements that allow contractors to deliver and receive invoice payments for discrete portions of the work as soon as completed and found acceptable by the Government [@CL-CTF-4595]. Unless specifically prohibited by the contract, the clause at 52.232-1, Payments, provides that the contractor is entitled to payment for accepted partial deliveries of supplies or partial performance of services that comply with all applicable contract requirements [@CL-CTF-4596] and for which prices can be calculated from the contract terms [@CL-CTF-4597].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract sets a due date of day 30 after receipt of a proper invoice. The Government generally will not pay before day 23, because that is 7 days before the due date, unless the agency head has made the listed determination. A payment on day 25 is allowed.
