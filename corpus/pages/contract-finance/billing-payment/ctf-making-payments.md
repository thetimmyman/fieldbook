## Purpose

Shows how early the Government may pay an invoice, what the payment office does, and how partial deliveries are paid.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CTF-0914; @CL-CTF-0915] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.906 and DFARS 232.906 [@CL-CTF-0914; @CL-CTF-0915] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.906 [@CL-CTF-4592; @CL-CTF-4595] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0914] |
| Applies to | Invoice payments due under contract due dates [@CL-CTF-0914] |

The Government will not make invoice payments earlier than 7 days before the contract due date, unless the agency head determines to pay earlier case by case or that accelerated payment methods are necessary [@CL-CTF-0914]. The DFARS says the FAR 32.906 restriction does not apply to invoice payments made to small business concerns, but contractors are not entitled to interest penalties if the Government fails to make early payment [@CL-CTF-0915].

Where the work permits, contracting officers must write statements of work and pricing arrangements so that contractors can deliver and receive invoice payments for discrete portions of the work as soon as they are completed and found acceptable [@CL-CTF-0918]. Unless the contract specifically prohibits it, the Payments clause provides that the contractor is entitled to payment for accepted partial deliveries of supplies or partial performance of services that comply with all applicable contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0919] and for which prices can be calculated from the contract terms [@CL-CTF-0920].

## How it works

The payment office mails checks on the day they are dated and, for electronic funds transfer, specifies a settlement date on or before the established due date [@CL-CTF-0916]. When it is determined that the billing office erroneously rejected a proper invoice, the payment office, upon resubmission, enters in the payment system the original date the billing office received the invoice [@CL-CTF-0917]. Under the DFARS, for interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services, the contracting officer generally inserts a standard due date of 14 days in the Prompt Payment clause when using its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-0921].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified FAR part 32 text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.906 keeps the FAR early payment limit, the check mailing, EFT settlement date and resubmission date steps, and the partial delivery rules described above [@CL-CTF-4592; @CL-CTF-4593; @CL-CTF-4594; @CL-CTF-4595; @CL-CTF-4596; @CL-CTF-4597]. The Government will not make invoice payments earlier than 7 days prior to the due dates specified in the contract unless the agency head determines to make earlier payment on a case-by-case basis or that the use of accelerated payment methods is necessary [@CL-CTF-4592]. The designated payment office mails checks on the same day they are dated and, for payments made by EFT, specifies a date on or before the established due date for settlement [@CL-CTF-4593]. When it is determined that the billing office erroneously rejected a proper invoice, the payment office, upon resubmission, enters in the payment system the original date the billing office received the invoice [@CL-CTF-4594]. Where the nature of the work permits, contracting officers must write statements of work and pricing arrangements that allow contractors to deliver and receive invoice payments for discrete portions of the work as soon as completed and found acceptable by the Government [@CL-CTF-4595]. Unless the contract specifically prohibits it, the Payments clause provides that the contractor is entitled to payment for accepted partial deliveries of supplies or partial performance of services that comply with all applicable contract requirements [@CL-CTF-4596] and for which prices can be calculated from the contract terms [@CL-CTF-4597].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract sets a due date of day 30 after receipt of a proper invoice. The Government generally will not pay before day 23, because that is 7 days before the due date, unless the agency head has made the listed determination. A payment on day 25 is allowed.

## Never confuse

The limit on early payment is a different idea from the due date and the interest penalty on late payment, including payment on the following working day when a due date falls on a weekend or holiday, which the prompt payment card explains; see [Prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment.md). The DFARS small business exception lifts the FAR early payment limit, but it gives no interest penalty if the Government fails to make early payment [@CL-CTF-0915]. For how requests are filed, see [Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md).
