## Purpose

Shows the penalty amounts in the unallowable cost penalty clause and the wording and signing duties of the certificate of final indirect costs. Which contracts are covered and the proposal deadline are on linked cards and are not repeated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1689] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.242-3 [@CL-CTF-1685]; FAR 52.242-4 [@CL-CTF-1689]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CTF-9426] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.242-3 [@CL-CTF-0789]; RFO FAR 52.242-4 [@CL-CTF-0795]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-CTF-9457] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1689] |
| Applies to | Costs submitted in a proposal under the penalty clause, and proposals to establish final indirect cost rates under the certification clause [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1716] |

If the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted by the contractor in its proposal is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR, or an executive agency supplement to the FAR, that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, the contractor shall be assessed a penalty [@CL-CTF-1685]. That penalty is equal to (1) the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract plus (2) simple interest, to be computed (i) on the amount the contractor was paid (whether as a progress or billing payment) in excess of the amount to which the contractor was entitled, and (ii) using the applicable rate effective for each six-month interval prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to Public Law 92-41 (85 Stat 97) [@CL-CTF-1686; @CL-CTF-9145]. If the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted by the contractor in its proposal includes a cost previously determined to be unallowable for that contractor [@CL-CTF-1718], then the contractor will be assessed a penalty in an amount equal to two times the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract [@CL-CTF-1687]. Payment by the contractor of any penalty assessed under the clause does not constitute repayment to the Government of any unallowable cost which has been paid by the Government to the contractor [@CL-CTF-1714]. Pursuant to the criteria in FAR 42.709-6, the Contracting Officer may waive the penalties in paragraph (d) or (e) of the clause [@CL-CTF-1688]. For the waiver criteria, see [Penalties on expressly unallowable costs: assessment, interest and waiver](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-expressly-unallowable-penalty-assessment-and-waiver.md).

Under FAR 2.101, unallowable cost means any cost that, under the provisions of any pertinent law, regulation, or contract, cannot be included in prices, cost-reimbursements, or settlements under a Government contract to which it is allocable [@CL-CTF-9426]. Under RFO FAR 2.101, unallowable cost means any cost that, under the provisions of any pertinent law, regulation, or contract, cannot be included in prices, cost-reimbursements, or settlements under a Government contract to which it is allocable [@CL-CTF-9457].

Under FAR 52.242-4(a), the contractor shall (1) certify any proposal to establish or modify final indirect cost rates, (2) use the format in paragraph (c) of the clause to certify, and (3) have the certificate signed by an individual of the contractor's organization at a level no lower than a vice president or chief financial officer of the business segment of the contractor that submits the proposal [@CL-CTF-1716; @CL-CTF-1689]. In the certificate format in paragraph (c), the signer certifies that the signer has reviewed the proposal to establish final indirect cost rates and to the best of the signer's knowledge and belief [@CL-CTF-1717]: (1) all costs included in the proposal to establish final indirect cost rates are allowable in accordance with the cost principles of the FAR and its supplements applicable to the contracts to which the final indirect cost rates will apply [@CL-CTF-1690; @CL-CTF-1485], and (2) the proposal does not include any costs which are expressly unallowable under applicable cost principles of the FAR or its supplements [@CL-CTF-1691]. Failure by the contractor to submit a signed certificate, as described in the clause, may result in final indirect costs at rates unilaterally established by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1715].

## How it works

The Contracting Officer determines whether a cost draws the penalty, and pursuant to the criteria in FAR 42.709-6, the Contracting Officer may waive the penalties in paragraph (d) or (e) of the clause [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1688]. Payment by the contractor of any penalty assessed under the clause does not constitute repayment to the Government of any unallowable cost which has been paid by the Government to the contractor [@CL-CTF-1714]. The certificate states that the proposal does not include any costs which are expressly unallowable under applicable cost principles of the FAR or its supplements [@CL-CTF-1691], while the penalty in FAR 52.242-3(d) applies if the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted by the contractor in its proposal is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR, or an executive agency supplement to the FAR, that defines the allowability of specific selected costs [@CL-CTF-1685]. The scope of the penalty by contract value is on [Penalties for unallowable costs in final rate proposals](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md), and the proposal and its deadline are on [Final indirect cost rate proposal deadline](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. Under RFO FAR 52.242-3(d), if the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted by the contractor in its proposal is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR, or an executive agency supplement to the FAR, that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, the contractor must be assessed a penalty equal to (1) the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract plus (2) simple interest, to be computed (i) on the amount the contractor was paid (whether as a progress or billing payment) in excess of the amount to which the contractor was entitled and (ii) using the applicable rate effective for each six-month interval prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to 41 USC 7109 [@CL-CTF-0789; @CL-CTF-0790; @CL-CTF-9144]. Under RFO FAR 52.242-3(e), (g), and (h), if the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted by the contractor in its proposal includes a cost previously determined to be unallowable for that contractor, then the contractor will be assessed a penalty in an amount equal to two times the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract [@CL-CTF-0791; @CL-CTF-0792], pursuant to the criteria in FAR 42.508-6, the Contracting Officer may waive the penalties in paragraph (d) or (e) of the clause [@CL-CTF-0793], and payment by the contractor of any penalty assessed under the clause does not constitute repayment to the Government of any unallowable cost which has been paid by the Government to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0794]. Under RFO FAR 52.242-4(a)-(c), the contractor shall (1) certify any proposal to establish or modify final indirect cost rates, (2) use the format in paragraph (c) of the clause to certify, and (3) have the certificate signed by an individual of the contractor's organization at a level no lower than a vice president or chief financial officer of the business segment of the contractor that submits the proposal [@CL-CTF-0795; @CL-CTF-0796], failure by the contractor to submit a signed certificate, as described in the clause, may result in final indirect costs at rates unilaterally established by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0797], and the certificate states that the signer has reviewed the proposal to establish final indirect cost rates and to the best of the signer's knowledge and belief (1) all costs included in the proposal to establish final indirect cost rates are allowable in accordance with the cost principles of the FAR and its supplements applicable to the contracts to which the final indirect cost rates will apply and (2) the proposal does not include any costs which are expressly unallowable under applicable cost principles of the FAR or its supplements [@CL-CTF-0798; @CL-CTF-0799; @CL-CTF-0800; @CL-CTF-0801].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A final rate proposal includes 30,000 of a cost that a cost principle expressly names as unallowable, and 12,000 of it is allocated to this contract. The penalty is 12,000 plus simple interest on any amount paid in excess of entitlement. If the same cost had already been determined unallowable for the contractor, the penalty would be 24,000.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.242-3(d) and (e), the penalty for an expressly unallowable cost is the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract plus simple interest, whereas the penalty for a cost previously determined to be unallowable for that contractor is two times the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract [@CL-CTF-1686; @CL-CTF-1718; @CL-CTF-1687]. Under FAR 52.242-4, the contractor certifies that all costs included in the proposal are allowable in accordance with the applicable cost principles and that the proposal does not include any costs which are expressly unallowable [@CL-CTF-1716; @CL-CTF-1690; @CL-CTF-1485; @CL-CTF-1691], while under FAR 52.242-3, the Contracting Officer determines whether a submitted cost is expressly unallowable and assesses the penalty [@CL-CTF-1685]. Under FAR 52.242-3(g), the Contracting Officer may waive the penalties in paragraph (d) or (e) of the clause pursuant to the criteria in FAR 42.709-6 [@CL-CTF-1688], whereas under RFO FAR 52.242-3(g), the Contracting Officer may waive the penalties in paragraph (d) or (e) of the clause pursuant to the criteria in FAR 42.508-6 [@CL-CTF-0793].
