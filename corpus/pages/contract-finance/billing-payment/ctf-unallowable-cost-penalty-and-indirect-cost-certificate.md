## Purpose

Shows the penalty amounts in the unallowable cost penalty clause and the wording and signing duties of the certificate of final indirect costs. Which contracts are covered and the proposal deadline are on linked cards and are not repeated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1689] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.242-3 [@CL-CTF-1685]; FAR 52.242-4 [@CL-CTF-1689] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1689] |
| Applies to | Costs submitted in a proposal under the penalty clause, and proposals to establish final indirect cost rates under the certification clause [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1716] |

If the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted in the proposal is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR or an agency supplement that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, the contractor shall be assessed a penalty [@CL-CTF-1685]. The penalty for an expressly unallowable cost is the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract plus simple interest, to be computed on the amount the contractor was paid, whether as a progress or billing payment, in excess of the amount to which it was entitled, and as further listed in the clause [@CL-CTF-1686]. Where the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted in the proposal includes a cost previously determined to be unallowable for that contractor [@CL-CTF-1718], the contractor will be assessed a penalty equal to two times the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract [@CL-CTF-1687]. Payment of an assessed penalty does not constitute repayment to the Government of any unallowable cost which has been paid by the Government to the contractor [@CL-CTF-1714]. The Contracting Officer may waive the penalties in paragraph (d) or (e) of the clause, pursuant to the criteria in the FAR section on waiver [@CL-CTF-1688]. The clause itself does not set out those criteria; the criteria are on [Penalties on expressly unallowable costs: assessment, interest and waiver](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-expressly-unallowable-penalty-assessment-and-waiver.md).

The contractor shall certify any proposal to establish or modify final indirect cost rates and use the format in the clause's certificate paragraph to certify [@CL-CTF-1716]. One listed duty is to have the certificate signed by an individual at a level no lower than a vice president or chief financial officer of the business segment that submits the proposal [@CL-CTF-1689]. The certifying official states that the official has reviewed the proposal to establish final indirect cost rates and makes the listed statements to the best of the official's knowledge and belief [@CL-CTF-1717]. The first statement is that all costs in the proposal are allowable in accordance with the cost principles of the FAR and its supplements [@CL-CTF-1690], and the second is that the proposal does not include any costs which are expressly unallowable under applicable cost principles of the FAR or its supplements [@CL-CTF-1691]. Failure to submit a signed certificate, as described in the clause, may result in final indirect costs at rates unilaterally established by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1715].

## How it works

The Contracting Officer determines whether a cost draws the penalty, and the clause says the Contracting Officer may waive it [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1688]. Paying a penalty does not repay the unallowable cost itself [@CL-CTF-1714]. The certificate speaks to the same kind of cost that draws the penalty, because it states that the proposal includes no expressly unallowable costs [@CL-CTF-1691; @CL-CTF-1685]. The scope of the penalty by contract value is on [Penalties for unallowable costs in final rate proposals](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md), and the proposal and its deadline are on [Final indirect cost rate proposal deadline](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A final rate proposal includes 30,000 of a cost that a cost principle expressly names as unallowable, and 12,000 of it is allocated to this contract. The penalty is 12,000 plus simple interest on any amount paid in excess of entitlement. If the same cost had already been determined unallowable for the contractor, the penalty would be 24,000.

## Never confuse

The penalty for an expressly unallowable cost, which is measured by the allocated cost plus interest, differs from the penalty for a cost previously determined unallowable for the same contractor, which is two times the allocated cost [@CL-CTF-1686; @CL-CTF-1687]. The certification clause concerns a signed statement about the proposal, while the penalty clause concerns the cost determination and the amount assessed [@CL-CTF-1690; @CL-CTF-1685].

## Company calibration

Confirm who signs the certificate, who screens costs for expressly unallowable items, and who handles any resulting penalty bill or collection. [@Q-009]
