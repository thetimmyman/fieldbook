## Purpose

Shows the penalty amounts in the unallowable cost penalty clause and the wording and signing duties of the certificate of final indirect costs. Which contracts are covered and the proposal deadline are on linked cards and are not repeated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1689] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.242-3 [@CL-CTF-1685]; FAR 52.242-4 [@CL-CTF-1689]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CTF-9426] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.242-3 [@CL-CTF-0789]; RFO FAR 52.242-4 [@CL-CTF-0795] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1689] |
| Applies to | Costs submitted in a proposal under the penalty clause, and proposals to establish final indirect cost rates under the certification clause [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1716] |

If the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted in the proposal is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR or an agency supplement that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, the contractor shall be assessed a penalty [@CL-CTF-1685]. The penalty for an expressly unallowable cost is the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract plus simple interest, to be computed on the amount the contractor was paid, whether as a progress or billing payment, in excess of the amount to which it was entitled, and as further listed in the clause [@CL-CTF-1686]. Where the Contracting Officer determines that a cost submitted in the proposal includes a cost previously determined to be unallowable for that contractor [@CL-CTF-1718], the contractor will be assessed a penalty equal to two times the amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract [@CL-CTF-1687]. Payment of an assessed penalty does not constitute repayment to the Government of any unallowable cost which has been paid by the Government to the contractor [@CL-CTF-1714]. The Contracting Officer may waive the penalties in paragraph (d) or (e) of the clause, pursuant to the criteria in the FAR section on waiver [@CL-CTF-1688]. The clause itself does not set out those criteria; the criteria are on [Penalties on expressly unallowable costs: assessment, interest and waiver](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-expressly-unallowable-penalty-assessment-and-waiver.md).

The FAR definitions section describes an unallowable cost as any cost that, under the provisions of any pertinent law, regulation, or contract, cannot be included in prices, cost-reimbursements, or settlements under a Government contract to which it is allocable [@CL-CTF-9426].

The contractor shall certify any proposal to establish or modify final indirect cost rates and use the format in the clause's certificate paragraph to certify [@CL-CTF-1716]. One listed duty is to have the certificate signed by an individual at a level no lower than a vice president or chief financial officer of the business segment that submits the proposal [@CL-CTF-1689]. The certifying official states that the official has reviewed the proposal to establish final indirect cost rates and makes the listed statements to the best of the official's knowledge and belief [@CL-CTF-1717]. The first statement is that all costs in the proposal are allowable in accordance with the cost principles of the FAR and its supplements applicable to the contracts to which the final indirect cost rates will apply [@CL-CTF-1690; @CL-CTF-1485], and the second is that the proposal does not include any costs which are expressly unallowable under applicable cost principles of the FAR or its supplements [@CL-CTF-1691]. Failure to submit a signed certificate, as described in the clause, may result in final indirect costs at rates unilaterally established by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1715].

## How it works

The Contracting Officer determines whether a cost draws the penalty, and the clause says the Contracting Officer may waive it [@CL-CTF-1685; @CL-CTF-1688]. Paying a penalty does not repay the unallowable cost itself [@CL-CTF-1714]. The certificate speaks to the same kind of cost that draws the penalty, because it states that the proposal includes no expressly unallowable costs [@CL-CTF-1691; @CL-CTF-1685]. The scope of the penalty by contract value is on [Penalties for unallowable costs in final rate proposals](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md), and the proposal and its deadline are on [Final indirect cost rate proposal deadline](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because DoD's class deviation directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.242-3 keeps the penalty for a cost that the contracting officer determines is expressly unallowable, with "must" in place of "shall" [@CL-CTF-0789; @CL-CTF-1685], and keeps the penalty amount of the disallowed cost allocated to the contract plus simple interest on the excess payment, computed at the applicable rate for each six-month interval prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury [@CL-CTF-0790; @CL-CTF-9144; @CL-CTF-1686]. For the interest rate, RFO FAR 52.242-3(d)(2)(ii) says the Secretary of the Treasury prescribes it pursuant to title 41 of the United States Code, section 7109 [@CL-CTF-9144], where the codified paragraph names Public Law 92-41 instead [@CL-CTF-9145]. It keeps the case of a cost previously determined to be unallowable for that contractor [@CL-CTF-0791; @CL-CTF-1718] and the penalty of two times the disallowed cost allocated to the contract [@CL-CTF-0792; @CL-CTF-1687]. It keeps the rule that paying a penalty does not repay the unallowable cost [@CL-CTF-0794; @CL-CTF-1714]. On waiver, RFO FAR 52.242-3(g) says the criteria are those in FAR 42.508-6 [@CL-CTF-0793], where the codified paragraph names FAR 42.709-6 [@CL-CTF-1688]. RFO FAR 52.242-4 keeps the duty to certify any proposal to establish or modify final indirect cost rates, using the certificate format [@CL-CTF-0795; @CL-CTF-1716], and the signature by an individual no lower than a vice president or chief financial officer of the business segment [@CL-CTF-0796; @CL-CTF-1689]. It keeps the effect of failing to submit a signed certificate [@CL-CTF-0797; @CL-CTF-1715], the certifying official's review statement [@CL-CTF-0798; @CL-CTF-1717] and the two certified statements [@CL-CTF-0799; @CL-CTF-0800; @CL-CTF-1690; @CL-CTF-0801; @CL-CTF-1691].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A final rate proposal includes 30,000 of a cost that a cost principle expressly names as unallowable, and 12,000 of it is allocated to this contract. The penalty is 12,000 plus simple interest on any amount paid in excess of entitlement. If the same cost had already been determined unallowable for the contractor, the penalty would be 24,000.

## Never confuse

The penalty for an expressly unallowable cost, which is measured by the allocated cost plus interest, differs from the penalty for a cost previously determined unallowable for the same contractor, which is two times the allocated cost [@CL-CTF-1686; @CL-CTF-1687]. The certification clause concerns a signed statement about the proposal, while the penalty clause concerns the cost determination and the amount assessed [@CL-CTF-1690; @CL-CTF-1685]. The waiver criteria are cited differently in the two texts: the codified paragraph names FAR 42.709-6 [@CL-CTF-1688], and the RFO paragraph names FAR 42.508-6 [@CL-CTF-0793].

## Company calibration

Confirm who signs the certificate, who screens costs for expressly unallowable items, and who handles any resulting penalty bill or collection. [@Q-009]
