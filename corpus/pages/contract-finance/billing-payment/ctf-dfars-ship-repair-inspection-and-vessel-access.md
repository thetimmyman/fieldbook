## Purpose

Shows the Government's inspection rights, the standards the work must meet, and who may be admitted to the contractor's facilities and the vessel under a ship repair Master Agreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1950; @CL-CTF-1953] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.217-7005 and DFARS 252.217-7011 [@CL-CTF-1950; @CL-CTF-1953] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1950] |
| Applies to | Job orders under a ship repair Master Agreement that includes the Inspection and Manner of Doing Work clause or the Access to Vessel clause [@CL-CTF-1950; @CL-CTF-1953] |

The Government may inspect and test all material and workmanship at any time during the contractor's performance of the work [@CL-CTF-1950]. Subject to the Navy-specification exception, and unless the job order specifically provides otherwise, the standards paragraph covers all operational practices of the contractor and all workmanship, material, equipment and articles used in the work under the Master Agreement [@CL-CTF-1947; @CL-CTF-1949]. Those practices, workmanship, material, equipment and articles are to be in accordance with the best commercial marine practices and the rules and requirements of the American Bureau of Shipping, the United States Coast Guard and the Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineers [@CL-CTF-1948]. Where Navy specifications are specified in the job order, the contractor follows Navy standards of material and workmanship [@CL-CTF-1949].

## How it works

If the Government finds, before delivery, material or workmanship that is defective or not in accordance with the job order, it may reject the defective or nonconforming material, in addition to its rights under the Guarantees clause [@CL-CTF-1951]. The contractor must keep complete records of all inspection work and make them available during performance and for 90 days after all work required is complete [@CL-CTF-1952].

On the Contracting Officer's request, the contractor must grant admission to its facilities and access to the vessel, on a non-interference basis and as necessary for their respective responsibilities, to a reasonable number of the listed people [@CL-CTF-1953]. Those people are Government and other Government contractor employees, in addition to Government employees attached to the vessel, and representatives of offerors on other contemplated Government work [@CL-CTF-1954]. All personnel granted access must comply with the contractor's rules governing personnel at its shipyard [@CL-CTF-1954].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A job order covers a pump replacement and does not specify Navy standards. The yard follows the best commercial marine practices and the classification and Coast Guard rules in effect. Before delivery the inspector finds a pump that does not meet the job order. The Government may reject it, in addition to its guarantee rights. The contractor keeps its inspection records for the work available for 90 days after all work is complete. Separately, the Contracting Officer asks the yard to admit a few employees of another Government contractor to the vessel. The yard admits a reasonable number without interfering with its work, and those visitors follow the yard's shipyard rules.

## Never confuse

Inspection before delivery is separate from the guarantee after redelivery. The right to reject before delivery is in addition to the Government's rights under the Guarantees clause [@CL-CTF-1951]; for the guarantee, see [Ship repair master agreement: performance, guarantees and over and above work (DFARS)](ctf-dfars-ship-repair-performance-guarantees-and-over-and-above.md). The admission clause is also separate from the Government employees already attached to the vessel, because it covers further Government and other Government contractor employees and offeror representatives [@CL-CTF-1954]. For how job orders are issued and changed, see [Ship repair master agreement: property exchange, changes and job orders (DFARS)](ctf-dfars-ship-repair-exchange-changes-and-job-orders.md). For the general inspection clause for fixed-price supplies, see [Inspection of Supplies—Fixed-Price clause](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-inspection-of-supplies-fixed-price.md), and for liability for loss of or damage to the vessel, see [Liability and insurance for vessel repair (DFARS)](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-dfars-ship-repair-liability-and-insurance.md).
