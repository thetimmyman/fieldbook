## Purpose

Shows when amounts the contractor owes the Government start to bear interest and at what rate, and what the assignment of claims clause lets a contractor do with its right to be paid.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1675] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-17 [@CL-CTF-1671]; FAR 52.232-23 [@CL-CTF-1675] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-17 [@CL-CTF-0383]; RFO FAR 52.232-23 [@CL-CTF-0387] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1675] |
| Applies to | Amounts payable by the contractor to the Government under the contract, and the contractor's right to be paid under the contract [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1675] |

Except as otherwise provided under a Price Reduction for Defective Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause or a Cost Accounting Standards clause, all amounts that become payable by the contractor to the Government under the contract bear simple interest [@CL-CTF-1671]. The interest runs from the date due until paid, unless the amount is paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-1672]. The interest rate is the rate established by the Secretary of the Treasury as provided in section 7109 of title 41 of the United States Code that applies to the period in which the amount becomes due, as provided in paragraph (e) of the clause, and then at the rate applicable for each six-month period as fixed by the Secretary until the amount is paid [@CL-CTF-1673]. Amounts are due at the earliest of the date fixed under the contract and the date of the first written demand for payment, including any demand for payment resulting from a default termination [@CL-CTF-1674]. The interest charge made under the clause may be reduced under the procedures prescribed in 32.608-2 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-1710].

Under the Assignment of Claims Act, the contractor may assign its rights to be paid amounts due or to become due as a result of performance of the contract to a bank, trust company, or other financing institution [@CL-CTF-1675], including any Federal lending agency, and the assignee may thereafter further assign or reassign its right under the original assignment to any type of financing institution described in the preceding sentence [@CL-CTF-9618]. Any assignment or reassignment authorized under the Act and the clause must cover all unpaid amounts payable under the contract and must not be made to more than one party, except that an assignment or reassignment may be made to one party as agent or trustee for two or more parties participating in the financing of the contract [@CL-CTF-1676; @CL-CTF-9619]. The contractor shall not furnish or disclose to any assignee any classified document, including the contract, or information related to work under the contract until the Contracting Officer authorizes such action in writing [@CL-CTF-1677]. If a no-setoff commitment is to be included in the contract, Alternate I adds at the end of paragraph (a) a sentence that, unless otherwise stated in the contract, payments to an assignee of any amounts due or to become due under the contract shall not, to the extent specified in the Act, be subject to reduction or setoff [@CL-CTF-9620; @CL-CTF-1711].

## How it works

Amounts are due at the earliest of the date fixed under the contract and the date of the first written demand for payment, including any demand for payment resulting from a default termination [@CL-CTF-1674], and except as otherwise provided under a Price Reduction for Defective Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause or a Cost Accounting Standards clause, all amounts that become payable by the Contractor to the Government under the contract bear simple interest from the date due until paid unless paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1672]. Any assignment or reassignment authorized under the Act and the clause must cover all unpaid amounts payable under the contract and must not be made to more than one party, except to one party as agent or trustee for two or more parties participating in the financing of the contract [@CL-CTF-1676; @CL-CTF-9619]. For assignment conditions, notices, and policy, see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-17 provides that, except as otherwise provided under a Price Reduction for Defective Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause or a Cost Accounting Standards clause, all amounts that become payable by the Contractor to the Government under the contract bear simple interest [@CL-CTF-0383] from the date due until paid unless paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-0384], that amounts are due at the earliest of the date fixed under the contract and the date of the first written demand for payment, including any demand for payment resulting from a default termination [@CL-CTF-0385], and that the interest charge made under the clause may be reduced under the procedures prescribed in 32.608-2 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-0386]. RFO FAR 52.232-23 provides that the Contractor, under the Assignment of Claims Act, may assign its rights to be paid amounts due or to become due as a result of performance of the contract to a bank, trust company, or other financing institution [@CL-CTF-0387], including any Federal lending agency, with the assignee able to further assign or reassign its right under the original assignment to any type of financing institution described in the preceding sentence [@CL-CTF-9075], that any assignment or reassignment authorized under the Act and the clause must cover all unpaid amounts payable under the contract and must not be made to more than one party [@CL-CTF-0388] except to one party as agent or trustee for two or more parties participating in the financing of the contract [@CL-CTF-0389], that the Contractor shall not furnish or disclose to any assignee any classified document, including the contract, or information related to work under the contract until the Contracting Officer authorizes such action in writing [@CL-CTF-0390], and that, if a no-setoff commitment is to be included in the contract [@CL-CTF-9076], Alternate I adds at the end of paragraph (a) a sentence that, unless otherwise stated in the contract, payments to an assignee of any amounts due or to become due under the contract shall not, to the extent specified in the Act, be subject to reduction or setoff [@CL-CTF-0391].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor owes the Government 20,000 under a contract. The contract fixes no earlier date, and the first written demand sets the due date at day 1. If the contractor pays on day 20, the clause adds no interest. If it pays on day 60, simple interest runs from day 1 until paid, at the Treasury rate.

Separately, the contractor assigns all 500,000 of its unpaid amounts to one bank. Splitting them between two banks is not allowed unless one party acts as agent or trustee for both.

## Never confuse

Under the Interest clause, except as otherwise provided under a Price Reduction for Defective Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause or a Cost Accounting Standards clause, all amounts that become payable by the Contractor to the Government under the contract bear simple interest from the date due until paid unless paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1672]. Under the Prompt Payment for Fixed-Price Architect-Engineer Contracts clause, the designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the Contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions listed in paragraphs (a)(3)(i) through (a)(3)(iii) of the clause are met, if applicable [@CL-CTF-1666], except that when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the designated payment office may make payment on the following working day without incurring a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-9496]; see [Prompt payment for architect-engineer contracts and discounts for prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment-architect-engineer-and-discounts.md). Under the Assignment of Claims clause, the Contractor may assign its rights to be paid amounts due or to become due as a result of performance of the contract to a bank, trust company, or other financing institution, including any Federal lending agency [@CL-CTF-1675; @CL-CTF-9618], and shall not furnish or disclose to any assignee any classified document, including the contract, or information related to work under the contract until the Contracting Officer authorizes such action in writing [@CL-CTF-1677]. For payments the contractor owes the Government under the dismantling and demolition payment clauses, see [Payment by the contractor to the Government, including incremental payment](ctf-payment-by-contractor-to-government.md).
