## Purpose

Shows when amounts the contractor owes the Government start to bear interest and at what rate, and what the assignment of claims clause lets a contractor do with its right to be paid. The conditions and policies for assignment are on a linked card and are not repeated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1675] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-17 [@CL-CTF-1671]; FAR 52.232-23 [@CL-CTF-1675] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-17 [@CL-CTF-0383]; RFO FAR 52.232-23 [@CL-CTF-0387] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1675] |
| Applies to | Amounts payable by the contractor to the Government under the contract, and the contractor's right to be paid under the contract [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1675] |

Except as otherwise provided under a price reduction for defective certified cost or pricing data clause or a Cost Accounting Standards clause, all amounts that become payable by the contractor to the Government under the contract bear simple interest [@CL-CTF-1671]. The interest runs from the date due until paid, unless the amount is paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-1672]. The rate is the rate established by the Secretary of the Treasury under the cited statute that applies to the period in which the amount becomes due, and then the rate fixed by the Secretary for each six-month period until the amount is paid [@CL-CTF-1673]. Amounts are due at the earliest of the date fixed under the contract and the date of the first written demand for payment, including any demand resulting from a default termination [@CL-CTF-1674]. The interest charge made under the clause may be reduced under the procedures prescribed in the FAR section it names, in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-1710].

Under the Assignment of Claims Act, the contractor may assign its rights to be paid amounts due or to become due as a result of performance of the contract to a bank, trust company, or other financing institution [@CL-CTF-1675], including any Federal lending agency, and the assignee may thereafter further assign or reassign its right under the original assignment to any type of financing institution described in the preceding sentence [@CL-CTF-9618]. Any assignment or reassignment authorized under the Act and the clause must cover all unpaid amounts payable under the contract and must not be made to more than one party, except to one party as agent or trustee for two or more parties participating in the financing of the contract [@CL-CTF-1676; @CL-CTF-9619]. The contractor shall not furnish or disclose to any assignee any classified document, including the contract, or information related to work under the contract until the Contracting Officer authorizes it in writing [@CL-CTF-1677]. If a no-setoff commitment is to be included in the contract, Alternate I adds at the end of paragraph (a) a sentence that, unless otherwise stated in the contract, payments to an assignee of any amounts due or to become due under the contract shall not, to the extent specified in the Act, be subject to reduction or setoff [@CL-CTF-9620; @CL-CTF-1711].

## How it works

A debt starts to bear interest on its due date, which is the earlier of the date fixed in the contract and the first written demand [@CL-CTF-1674; @CL-CTF-1672]. No interest runs on an amount paid within 30 days of becoming due, subject to the listed exceptions for the price reduction and Cost Accounting Standards clauses [@CL-CTF-1672; @CL-CTF-1671]. An assignment covers everything still unpaid and goes to one party at a time, or to one agent or trustee for several parties participating in the financing of the contract [@CL-CTF-1676; @CL-CTF-9619]. Further assignment, conditions, notices and policy are on [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md); this card covers the contract clause only.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.232-17 keeps the simple interest on amounts payable by the Contractor to the Government, except as otherwise provided under a Price Reduction for Defective Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause or a Cost Accounting Standards clause [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-0383], the interest running from the date due until paid unless paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-1672; @CL-CTF-0384], the due date at the earliest of the date fixed under the contract and the date of the first written demand, including a demand resulting from a default termination [@CL-CTF-1674; @CL-CTF-0385], and the reduction of the interest charge under the procedures in the FAR section it names, in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-1710; @CL-CTF-0386]. The RFO text of FAR 52.232-23 keeps the Contractor's right to assign its rights to be paid to a bank, trust company, or other financing institution [@CL-CTF-1675; @CL-CTF-0387], including any Federal lending agency, with the assignee able to further assign or reassign its right under the original assignment to any type of financing institution described in the preceding sentence [@CL-CTF-9075], the rule that an assignment covers all unpaid amounts and is not made to more than one party [@CL-CTF-1676; @CL-CTF-0388] except to one party as agent or trustee for two or more parties participating in the financing of the contract [@CL-CTF-1676; @CL-CTF-9619; @CL-CTF-0389], the bar on giving an assignee classified documents or related information until the Contracting Officer authorizes it in writing [@CL-CTF-1677; @CL-CTF-0390], and, if a no-setoff commitment is to be included in the contract, the Alternate I no-setoff sentence [@CL-CTF-1711; @CL-CTF-0391; @CL-CTF-9076].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor owes the Government 20,000 under a contract. The contract fixes no earlier date, and the first written demand sets the due date at day 1. If the contractor pays on day 20, the clause adds no interest. If it pays on day 60, simple interest runs from day 1 until paid, at the Treasury rate.

Separately, the contractor assigns all 500,000 of its unpaid amounts to one bank. Splitting them between two banks is not allowed unless one party acts as agent or trustee for both.

## Never confuse

Interest on amounts payable to the Government is not the late payment interest penalty the payment office pays to a contractor, which is automatic when payment is late and the listed conditions are met, except that payment on the following working day when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday does not incur it [@CL-CTF-1671; @CL-CTF-1666; @CL-CTF-9496]; see [Prompt payment for architect-engineer contracts and discounts for prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment-architect-engineer-and-discounts.md). An assignment moves the right to be paid; it does not let the assignee receive classified material without written authorization from the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1675; @CL-CTF-1677]. Payments the contractor owes the Government under the dismantling and demolition payment clauses are on [Payment by the contractor to the Government, including incremental payment](ctf-payment-by-contractor-to-government.md).
