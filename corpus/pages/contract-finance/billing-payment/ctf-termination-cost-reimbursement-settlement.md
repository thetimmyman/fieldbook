## Purpose

Shows how a cost-reimbursement contract is terminated and settled: the grounds and the notice, the two submission clocks, the agreement on the amount, and the fee on a convenience termination.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0418] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.249-6 [@CL-CTF-0418] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.249-6 [@CL-CTF-0681; @CL-CTF-0682] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0418] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the cost-reimbursement termination clause, which is inserted as prescribed in FAR 49.503(a)(1) [@CL-CTF-0612] |

The Government may terminate performance of work under the contract in whole or, from time to time, in part, if the contracting officer determines that a termination is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0418], or if the contractor defaults in performing the contract and fails to cure the default within 10 days (unless extended by the contracting officer) after receiving a notice specifying the default, where default includes failure to make progress in the work so as to endanger performance [@CL-CTF-0611]. The contracting officer shall terminate by delivering to the contractor a Notice of Termination specifying whether termination is for default of the contractor or for convenience of the Government, the extent of termination, and the effective date [@CL-CTF-0419].

After receipt of a Notice of Termination, and except as directed by the contracting officer, the contractor shall immediately proceed with the listed obligations, regardless of any delay in determining or adjusting any amounts due under the clause, beginning with stopping work as specified in the notice [@CL-CTF-0420]. The contractor shall submit complete termination inventory schedules no later than 120 days from the effective date of termination, unless extended in writing by the contracting officer upon written request of the contractor within this 120-day period [@CL-CTF-0421]. The contractor shall submit the final termination settlement proposal promptly, but no later than 1 year from the effective date of termination, unless extended in writing by the contracting officer upon written request of the contractor within this 1-year period [@CL-CTF-0422]. However, if the contracting officer determines that the facts justify it, a termination settlement proposal may be received and acted on after 1 year or any extension [@CL-CTF-0613].

Subject to paragraph (f) of the clause, the contractor and the contracting officer may agree on the whole or any part of the amount to be paid (including an allowance for fee) because of the termination, and the contract shall be amended, and the contractor paid the agreed amount [@CL-CTF-0423]. If the contractor and the contracting officer fail to agree in whole or in part on the amount of costs and/or fee to be paid because of the termination of work, the contracting officer shall determine, on the basis of information available, the amount, if any, due the contractor, and shall pay that amount, which shall include a portion of the fee payable under the contract [@CL-CTF-0614; @CL-CTF-1476; @CL-CTF-1477]. If the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government, the settlement includes a percentage of the fee equal to the percentage of completion of work contemplated under the contract, but excluding subcontract effort included in subcontractors' termination proposals, less previous payments for fee [@CL-CTF-0424; @CL-CTF-0615]. The cost principles and procedures in part 31 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation, in effect on the date of the contract, shall govern all costs claimed, agreed to, or determined under the clause [@CL-CTF-0425]. The provisions of the clause relating to fee are inapplicable if the contract does not include a fee [@CL-CTF-0616].

## How it works

After receipt of a Notice of Termination, and except as directed by the contracting officer, the contractor shall immediately proceed with the obligations in paragraph (c), regardless of any delay in determining or adjusting any amounts due under the clause, starting with stopping work as specified in the notice [@CL-CTF-0420]. Under the same lead-in, the contractor shall place no further subcontracts or orders except as necessary to complete the continued portion of the contract, terminate all subcontracts to the extent they relate to the work terminated, complete performance of the work not terminated, and take any action that may be necessary, or that the contracting officer may direct, for the protection and preservation of the property related to the contract that is in the possession of the contractor and in which the Government has or may acquire an interest [@CL-CTF-0420].

Complete termination inventory schedules are due no later than 120 days from the effective date of termination, and the final termination settlement proposal is due promptly, but no later than 1 year from the effective date of termination, each unless extended in writing by the contracting officer upon written request of the contractor within that period, except that if the contracting officer determines that the facts justify it, a termination settlement proposal may be received and acted on after 1 year or any extension [@CL-CTF-0421; @CL-CTF-0422; @CL-CTF-0613].

Subject to paragraph (f) of the clause, the contractor and the contracting officer may agree on the whole or any part of the amount to be paid (including an allowance for fee) because of the termination, and the contract shall be amended, and the contractor paid the agreed amount [@CL-CTF-0423].

The Government may, under the terms and conditions it prescribes, make partial payments and payments against costs incurred by the contractor for the terminated portion of the contract, if the contracting officer believes the total of these payments will not exceed the amount to which the contractor will be entitled [@CL-CTF-0617; @CL-CTF-0426].

When the contractor and the contracting officer fail to agree in whole or in part on the amount of costs and/or fee to be paid because of the termination of work, the contracting officer shall determine, on the basis of information available, the amount, if any, due the contractor, and shall pay that amount, which shall include a portion of the fee payable under the contract [@CL-CTF-0614; @CL-CTF-1476; @CL-CTF-1477]. If the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government, the fee portion is a percentage of the fee equal to the percentage of completion of work contemplated under the contract, but excluding subcontract effort included in subcontractors' termination proposals, less previous payments for fee [@CL-CTF-0424; @CL-CTF-0615].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-6(a)-(c), the Government may terminate performance of work under the contract in whole or, from time to time, in part, if the contracting officer determines that a termination is in the Government's interest, or if the contractor defaults in performing the contract and fails to cure the default within 10 days (unless extended by the contracting officer) after receiving a notice specifying the default, where default includes failure to make progress in the work so as to endanger performance [@CL-CTF-0681; @CL-CTF-0682], the contracting officer shall terminate by delivering to the contractor a Notice of Termination specifying whether termination is for default of the contractor or for convenience of the Government, the extent of termination, and the effective date [@CL-CTF-0683], and after receipt of a Notice of Termination, and except as directed by the contracting officer, the contractor shall immediately proceed with its obligations, regardless of any delay in determining or adjusting any amounts due under the clause, including stopping work as specified in the notice [@CL-CTF-0684]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-6(d) and (f), the contractor shall submit complete termination inventory schedules no later than 120 days from the effective date of termination, unless extended in writing by the contracting officer upon written request of the contractor within this 120-day period [@CL-CTF-0685], and shall submit the proposal promptly, but no later than 1 year from the effective date of termination, unless extended in writing by the contracting officer upon written request of the contractor within this 1-year period [@CL-CTF-0686], except that if the contracting officer determines that the facts justify it, a termination settlement proposal may be received and acted on after 1 year or any extension [@CL-CTF-0687]. If the contractor fails to submit the proposal within the time allowed, the contracting officer may determine, on the basis of information available, the amount, if any, due the contractor because of the termination and shall pay the amount determined [@CL-CTF-9147]. Under RFO FAR 52.249-6(g)-(i), (m)(1), and (n), subject to paragraph (f), the contractor and the contracting officer may agree on the whole or any part of the amount to be paid (including an allowance for fee) because of the termination, and the contract shall be amended, and the contractor paid the agreed amount [@CL-CTF-0688], or if they fail to agree in whole or in part on the amount of costs and/or fee to be paid because of the termination of work, the contracting officer shall determine, on the basis of information available, the amount, if any, due the contractor because of the termination [@CL-CTF-0689], including a portion of the fee payable under the contract [@CL-CTF-1478]: if terminated for convenience, a percentage of the fee equal to the percentage of completion of work contemplated under the contract, but excluding subcontract effort included in subcontractors' termination proposals, less previous payments for fee [@CL-CTF-0690; @CL-CTF-0691], or if terminated for default, the total fee payable shall be such proportionate part of the fee as the total number of articles (or amount of services) delivered to and accepted by the Government bears to the total number of articles (or amount of services) of a like kind required by the contract [@CL-CTF-0692; @CL-CTF-9141], with the cost principles and procedures in part 31 of the FAR in effect on the date of the contract governing all costs claimed, agreed to, or determined under the clause [@CL-CTF-0693], partial payments and payments against costs incurred by the contractor for the terminated portion of the contract permitted under the terms and conditions the Government prescribes if the contracting officer believes the total of these payments will not exceed the amount to which the contractor will be entitled [@CL-CTF-1479; @CL-CTF-0694], and the fee provisions inapplicable if the contract does not include a fee [@CL-CTF-0695].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement contract is terminated for convenience at 60 percent complete, with 900,000 of costs incurred and unpaid, 100,000 of subcontract settlement costs, and 50,000 of settlement expenses. The contract fee is 200,000. The parties fail to agree, so the contracting officer determines the amount. With the 60 percent completion measured excluding subcontract effort already in subcontractors' termination proposals, the fee portion is 60 percent of 200,000, or 120,000. Of that fee, 40,000 was already paid, so 80,000 of fee remains. The settlement is 900,000 plus 100,000 plus 50,000 plus 80,000, or 1,130,000, and the inventory schedules are due 120 days after the effective date of termination.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.249-6(h)(4), if the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government, the fee portion is a percentage of the fee equal to the percentage of completion of work contemplated under the contract, but excluding subcontract effort included in subcontractors' termination proposals, less previous payments for fee [@CL-CTF-1477; @CL-CTF-0424; @CL-CTF-0615]. If the contract is terminated for default, the total fee payable shall be such proportionate part of the fee as the total number of articles (or amount of services) delivered to and accepted by the Government bears to the total number of articles (or amount of services) of a like kind required by the contract [@CL-CTF-1477; @CL-CTF-0618; @CL-CTF-1483].

Under FAR 52.249-6(m)(1), the Government may, under the terms and conditions it prescribes, make partial payments and payments against costs incurred by the contractor for the terminated portion of the contract, if the contracting officer believes the total of these payments will not exceed the amount to which the contractor will be entitled [@CL-CTF-0617; @CL-CTF-0426].
