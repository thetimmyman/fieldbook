## Purpose

Shows what a proper invoice must contain and what Government documentation must support an invoice payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CTF-0900; @CL-CTF-0912] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.905 and DFARS 232.905 [@CL-CTF-0900; @CL-CTF-0912] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0900] |
| Applies to | Invoice payments, with an exception for interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services [@CL-CTF-0901; @CL-CTF-0908] |

Payment is based on receipt of a proper invoice and satisfactory contract performance [@CL-CTF-0900]. A proper invoice must include the listed items, except for interim payments on cost reimbursement contracts for services [@CL-CTF-0901]. All invoice payments, with the same exception, must be supported by a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment [@CL-CTF-0908].

The listed items start with the contractor's name and address and the invoice date and number [@CL-CTF-0901]. They go on to the contract number or other authorization with the order and line item number, and the description, quantity, unit of measure, unit price, and extended price [@CL-CTF-0902]. They also include shipping and payment terms [@CL-CTF-0903], and the payee official and the person to notify of a defective invoice [@CL-CTF-0904]. The taxpayer identification number and electronic funds transfer banking information go on the invoice only if agency procedures require them [@CL-CTF-0905; @CL-CTF-0906], and the banking information is not required at all if the Government waived the requirement to pay by electronic funds transfer [@CL-CTF-1001]. Where banking information is not required on the invoice, the section adds a further condition on banking information submitted elsewhere, which this card does not set out. A proper invoice also includes any other information or documentation the contract requires [@CL-CTF-1000]. For task and delivery orders, the defense supplement lets the 13-character order number serve as the contract number [@CL-CTF-0912], and an order with a four-position serial number must show both the basic contract identifier and the order number [@CL-CTF-0913].

## How it works

An interim payment request under a cost-reimbursement contract for services is a proper invoice if it includes all of the information the contract requires [@CL-CTF-0907]. The receiving official should forward the receiving report or other documentation to the payment office by the fifth working day after acceptance or approval, unless other arrangements have been made [@CL-CTF-0909]. That period does not extend the payment due dates [@CL-CTF-1002]. The documentation must at a minimum show the contract number or other authorization and a description of what was delivered or performed [@CL-CTF-0910], and the quantities received and accepted or performed, if applicable, and the date of delivery or performance [@CL-CTF-1003]. The billing office annotates each invoice with the actual date it receives it, and the payment office annotates each invoice and receiving report with the date it receives them [@CL-CTF-0911].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers goods under a fixed-price order and sends an invoice for 40,000. The invoice shows the contractor's name and address, an invoice date and number, the contract and order number, each line with quantity and price, shipping terms, the remit-to official, and a contact for defects. The Government's receiving report shows the contract number, a description of the goods and the acceptance. The invoice is proper and the payment is supported.

## Never confuse

Payment rests on a proper invoice from the contractor [@CL-CTF-0900], and an invoice payment must also be supported by the Government's receiving report or other documentation authorizing payment [@CL-CTF-0908]. An interim request under a cost-reimbursement contract for services is the exception on both counts [@CL-CTF-0907; @CL-CTF-0908]. For due dates, interest on late payment and the return of an invoice that is not proper, see [Prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment.md); for how requests are submitted electronically, see [Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md).
