## Purpose

Shows what a proper invoice must contain and what Government documentation must support an invoice payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CTF-0900; @CL-CTF-0912] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.905 and DFARS 232.905 [@CL-CTF-0900; @CL-CTF-0912] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.905 [@CL-CTF-4598; @CL-CTF-4599] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0900] |
| Applies to | Invoice payments, with an exception for interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services [@CL-CTF-0901; @CL-CTF-0908] |

Payment is based on receipt of a proper invoice and satisfactory contract performance [@CL-CTF-0900]. A proper invoice must include the listed items, except for interim payments on cost reimbursement contracts for services [@CL-CTF-0901]. All invoice payments, with the same exception, must be supported by a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment [@CL-CTF-0908].

The listed items start with the contractor's name and address and the invoice date and number [@CL-CTF-0901]. They go on to the contract number or other authorization with the order and line item number, and the description, quantity, unit of measure, unit price, and extended price [@CL-CTF-0902]. They also include shipping and payment terms [@CL-CTF-0903], and the payee official and the person to notify of a defective invoice [@CL-CTF-0904]. The taxpayer identification number and electronic funds transfer banking information go on the invoice only if agency procedures require them [@CL-CTF-0905; @CL-CTF-0906], and the banking information is not required if the Government waived the requirement to pay by electronic funds transfer [@CL-CTF-1001]. Where banking information is not required on the invoice, the invoice is proper only if the contractor has submitted correct banking information in accordance with the applicable solicitation provision [@CL-CTF-1018], contract clause, or agency procedures [@CL-CTF-1019]. A proper invoice also includes any other information or documentation the contract requires [@CL-CTF-1000]. For task and delivery orders numbered in accordance with FAR 4.1603 and 204.1603, the defense supplement lets the 13-character order number serve as the contract number [@CL-CTF-0912], and an order with a four-position serial number must show both the basic contract identifier and the order number [@CL-CTF-0913].

FAR 2.101 defines an invoice as a contractor's bill or written request for payment under the contract for supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-9406]. The RFO model text of FAR 2.101 defines an invoice in the same words as the codified definition, apart from the printed form of the quotation marks and the apostrophe [@CL-CTF-9443; @CL-CTF-9406].

## How it works

An interim payment request under a cost-reimbursement contract for services is a proper invoice for purposes of that subsection of FAR 32.905 if it includes all of the information the contract requires [@CL-CTF-0907]. The receiving official should forward the receiving report or other documentation to the payment office by the fifth working day after acceptance or approval, unless other arrangements have been made [@CL-CTF-0909]. That period does not extend the payment due dates [@CL-CTF-1002]. The documentation must at a minimum show the contract number or other authorization and a description of what was delivered or performed [@CL-CTF-0910], and the quantities received and accepted or performed, if applicable, and the date of delivery or performance [@CL-CTF-1003]. The paragraph lists two further minimum items that this card does not restate. The designated billing office must immediately annotate each invoice with the actual date it receives the invoice, and the designated payment office will annotate each invoice and receiving report with the actual date it receives the invoice [@CL-CTF-0911].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified FAR part 32 text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.905 keeps the FAR proper invoice and payment documentation rules described above [@CL-CTF-4598; @CL-CTF-4599; @CL-CTF-4600; @CL-CTF-4601; @CL-CTF-4602; @CL-CTF-4603; @CL-CTF-4604; @CL-CTF-4605; @CL-CTF-4606; @CL-CTF-4607; @CL-CTF-4608; @CL-CTF-4609; @CL-CTF-4610; @CL-CTF-4611; @CL-CTF-4612; @CL-CTF-4613; @CL-CTF-4614; @CL-CTF-4615]. Payment will be based on receipt of a proper invoice and satisfactory contract performance [@CL-CTF-4598]. A proper invoice must include the listed items, except for interim payments on cost reimbursement contracts for services, starting with the contractor's name and address and the invoice date and invoice number [@CL-CTF-4599]. The list goes on with the contract number or other authorization, including the order number and line item number, and the description, quantity, unit of measure, unit price, and extended price [@CL-CTF-4600], shipping and payment terms [@CL-CTF-4601], the contractor official to whom payment is to be sent and the person to notify in the event of a defective invoice [@CL-CTF-4602], and any other information or documentation the contract requires [@CL-CTF-4610]. The contractor must include its taxpayer identification number on the invoice only if required by agency procedures [@CL-CTF-4603], and likewise its EFT banking information [@CL-CTF-4604]. EFT banking information is not required if the Government waived the requirement to pay by EFT [@CL-CTF-4611]. If EFT banking information is not required to be on the invoice, in order for the invoice to be a proper invoice the contractor must have submitted correct EFT banking information in accordance with the applicable solicitation provision [@CL-CTF-4614], contract clause or applicable agency procedures [@CL-CTF-4615]. An interim payment request under a cost-reimbursement contract for services constitutes a proper invoice for purposes of the section if it includes all of the information required by the contract [@CL-CTF-4605]. All invoice payments, with the exception of interim payments on cost-reimbursement contracts for services, must be supported by a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment [@CL-CTF-4606]. The agency receiving official should forward that documentation to the designated payment office by the 5th working day after Government acceptance or approval, unless other arrangements have been made [@CL-CTF-4607], and that period does not extend the due dates prescribed in the section [@CL-CTF-4612]. The documentation must, as a minimum, include the contract number or other authorization and a description of the supplies delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-4608], and also the quantities received and accepted or services performed, if applicable, and the date the supplies were delivered or services performed [@CL-CTF-4613]. The RFO paragraph lists two further minimum items that this card does not restate. The designated billing office must immediately annotate each invoice with the actual date it receives the invoice, and the designated payment office will annotate each invoice and receiving report with the actual date it receives the invoice [@CL-CTF-4609].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers goods under a fixed-price order and sends an invoice for 40,000. The invoice shows the contractor's name and address, an invoice date and number, the contract and order number, each line with quantity and price, shipping terms, the remit-to official, and a contact for defects, but no payment terms. The Government's receiving report shows the contract number and a description of the goods, but not the quantities received and accepted or the date of delivery.

Answer: the invoice is not shown to be proper, because it shows no payment terms, and the payment is not shown to be supported, because the receiving report lacks required minimum items. The contractor adds the payment terms and the receiving official completes the report.

## Never confuse

Payment rests on a proper invoice from the contractor [@CL-CTF-0900], and an invoice payment must also be supported by the Government's receiving report or other documentation authorizing payment [@CL-CTF-0908]. An interim request under a cost-reimbursement contract for services is the exception on both counts [@CL-CTF-0907; @CL-CTF-0908]. For due dates, interest on late payment and the return of an invoice that is not proper, see [Prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment.md); for how requests are submitted electronically, see [Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md).

## Company calibration
