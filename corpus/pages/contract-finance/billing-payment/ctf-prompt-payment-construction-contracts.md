## Purpose

Sets out what the construction prompt payment clause says about due dates for progress and final payments, invoice contents, retainage, and the payment and interest terms that must reach subcontractors. The general due-date rules sit on a linked card and are not repeated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1651] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-27 [@CL-CTF-1651] |

The clause treats as progress payments, if provided for elsewhere in the contract, payments based on Contracting Officer approval of the estimated amount and value of work or services performed, including payments for reaching milestones in any project [@CL-CTF-1650]. The due date for making such payments is 14 days after the designated billing office receives a proper payment request [@CL-CTF-1651]. If the designated billing office fails to annotate the payment request with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the payment due date is the 14th day after the date of the contractor's payment request [@CL-CTF-1700], provided the designated billing office receives a proper payment request and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-1702]. The due date for payment of any amounts retained by the Contracting Officer in accordance with the clause at 52.232-5, Payments Under Fixed-Price Construction Contracts, is as specified in the contract or, if not specified, 30 days after approval by the Contracting Officer for release to the contractor [@CL-CTF-1652; @CL-CTF-9628].

The clause treats as final payments those based on completion and acceptance of all work and presentation of release of all claims against the Government arising by virtue of the contract, and payments for partial deliveries that have been accepted by the Government [@CL-CTF-1653]. The due date for making such payments is the later of the following two events: the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor and the 30th day after Government acceptance of the work or services completed by the contractor [@CL-CTF-1654]. For a final invoice when the payment amount is subject to contract settlement actions (e.g., release of claims), acceptance is deemed to occur on the effective date of the contract settlement [@CL-CTF-1800]. If the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice with the date of actual receipt at the time of receipt, the invoice payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice [@CL-CTF-1701], provided the designated billing office receives a proper invoice and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-1703].

## How it works

A proper invoice must include the items listed in paragraphs (a)(2)(i) through (a)(2)(xi) of the clause, and if the invoice does not comply with these requirements, the designated billing office must return it within 7 days after receipt, with the reasons why it is not a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-1655]. The listed items include the name and address of the contractor, the invoice date and invoice number (with the contractor dating invoices as close as possible to the date of mailing or transmission), and the contract number or other authorization for work or services performed (including order number and line item number) [@CL-CTF-1656], as well as the description of work or services performed, delivery and payment terms (e.g., discount for prompt payment terms), and the name and address of the contractor official to whom payment is to be sent (which must be the same as that in the contract or in a proper notice of assignment) [@CL-CTF-1657]. The contractor shall include in each subcontract for property or services (including a material supplier) for the purpose of performing the contract the required payment terms [@CL-CTF-1658]: a payment clause that obligates the contractor to pay the subcontractor for satisfactory performance under its subcontract not later than 7 days from receipt of payment out of such amounts as are paid to the contractor under the contract [@CL-CTF-1659], and a clause that obligates the contractor to pay to the subcontractor an interest penalty for each payment not made in accordance with the payment clause for the period beginning on the day after the required payment date and ending on the date on which payment of the amount due is made [@CL-CTF-1660], computed at the rate of interest established by the Secretary of the Treasury, and published in the Federal Register, for interest payments under 41 U.S.C. 7109 in effect at the time the contractor accrues the obligation to pay an interest penalty [@CL-CTF-1661]. The clauses required by paragraph (c) of the clause shall not be construed to impair the right of the contractor or a subcontractor at any tier to negotiate, and to include in their subcontract, the provisions listed in paragraph (d) [@CL-CTF-9629]. One listed provision, headed retainage permitted, permits the contractor or a subcontractor to retain (without cause) a specified percentage of each progress payment otherwise due to a subcontractor for satisfactory performance under the subcontract without incurring any obligation to pay a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-1662], in accordance with terms and conditions agreed to by the parties to the subcontract, giving such recognition as the parties deem appropriate to the ability of a subcontractor to furnish a performance bond and a payment bond [@CL-CTF-9630]. Another listed subcontract provision permits such withholding without incurring any obligation to pay a late payment penalty if a notice conforming to the standards of paragraph (g) of the clause previously has been furnished to the subcontractor and the contractor furnishes to the Contracting Officer a copy of any notice issued by a contractor pursuant to paragraph (d)(3)(i) of the clause [@CL-CTF-1704; @CL-CTF-9631]. For the general due-date rules, see [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md), and for what makes an invoice proper, see [Proper invoice content](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md).

DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 52 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-27, for progress payments (if provided for elsewhere in the contract) based on Contracting Officer approval of the estimated amount and value of work or services performed, including payments for reaching milestones in any project [@CL-CTF-0627], the due date for making such payments is 14 days after the designated billing office receives a proper payment request [@CL-CTF-0628], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1650; @CL-CTF-1651]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-27, if the designated billing office fails to annotate the payment request with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the payment due date is the 14th day after the date of the contractor's payment request [@CL-CTF-0629], provided the designated billing office receives a proper payment request and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0630], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1700; @CL-CTF-1702]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-27, the due date for payment of any amounts retained by the Contracting Officer in accordance with the clause at 52.232-5, Payments Under Fixed-Price Construction Contracts, is as specified in the contract or, if not specified, 30 days after approval by the Contracting Officer for release to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0631; @CL-CTF-9123], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1652; @CL-CTF-9628]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-27, for final payments based on completion and acceptance of all work and presentation of release of all claims against the Government arising by virtue of the contract, and payments for partial deliveries that have been accepted by the Government [@CL-CTF-0632], the due date is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the contractor and the 30th day after Government acceptance of the work or services completed by the contractor [@CL-CTF-0633], with acceptance for a final invoice subject to contract settlement actions (e.g., release of claims) deemed to occur on the effective date of the contract settlement [@CL-CTF-0634], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1653; @CL-CTF-1654; @CL-CTF-1800]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-27, if the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice with the date of actual receipt at the time of receipt, the invoice payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice [@CL-CTF-0635], provided the designated billing office receives a proper invoice and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-0636], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1701; @CL-CTF-1703]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-27, a proper invoice must include the items listed in paragraphs (a)(2)(i) through (a)(2)(xi) of the clause, and if the invoice does not comply with these requirements, the designated billing office must return it within 7 days after receipt, with the reasons why it is not a proper invoice [@CL-CTF-0637], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1655]. Among the listed invoice items, RFO FAR 52.232-27 includes the contractor's name and address, the invoice date and invoice number (with the contractor dating invoices as close as possible to the date of mailing or transmission), and the contract number or other authorization for work or services performed (including order number and line item number) [@CL-CTF-9119], and the description of work or services performed, delivery and payment terms (e.g., discount for prompt payment terms), and the name and address of the contractor official to whom payment is to be sent (which must be the same as that in the contract or in a proper notice of assignment) [@CL-CTF-9120], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-1656; @CL-CTF-1657]. Under RFO FAR 52.232-27(d), the clauses required by paragraph (c) of the clause shall not be construed to impair the right of the contractor or a subcontractor at any tier to negotiate, and to include in their subcontract, the provisions listed in paragraph (d) [@CL-CTF-9124], including the retainage provision permitting the contractor or a subcontractor to retain (without cause) a specified percentage of each progress payment otherwise due to a subcontractor for satisfactory performance under the subcontract without incurring any obligation to pay a late payment interest penalty, in accordance with terms and conditions agreed to by the parties to the subcontract, giving such recognition as the parties deem appropriate to the ability of a subcontractor to furnish a performance bond and a payment bond [@CL-CTF-0640; @CL-CTF-9125], and the withholding provision permitting such withholding without incurring any obligation to pay a late payment penalty if a notice conforming to the standards of paragraph (g) of the clause previously has been furnished to the subcontractor and the contractor furnishes to the Contracting Officer a copy of any notice issued by a contractor pursuant to paragraph (d)(3)(i) of the clause [@CL-CTF-0641; @CL-CTF-9126], matching the codified clause [@CL-CTF-9629; @CL-CTF-1662; @CL-CTF-9630; @CL-CTF-1704; @CL-CTF-9631].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A builder submits a proper progress payment request on day 1, and the billing office marks the receipt date. The payment is due on day 15. The builder receives 100,000 on day 15 and owes a subcontractor 40,000 of it. The subcontract holds back 10 percent as retainage, so 4,000 is held without a late payment interest penalty. The other 36,000 is due to the subcontractor by day 22.

For a final invoice, suppose a proper invoice is received on day 5 and the work is accepted on day 20. The later of day 35 and day 50 is day 50, so the due date is day 50. If instead the final invoice is subject to a release of claims and the settlement takes effect on day 60, acceptance is deemed to occur on day 60, and the later of day 35 and day 90 is day 90.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-27(d)(1), a subcontract provision may permit the contractor or a subcontractor to retain (without cause) a specified percentage of each progress payment otherwise due to a subcontractor for satisfactory performance under the subcontract without incurring any obligation to pay a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-1662], whereas the due date for payment of any amounts retained by the Contracting Officer in accordance with FAR 52.232-5 is as specified in the contract or, if not specified, 30 days after approval by the Contracting Officer for release to the contractor [@CL-CTF-1652; @CL-CTF-9628]. Under FAR 52.232-27, the 14-day due date after the designated billing office receives a proper payment request applies to progress payments (if provided for elsewhere in the contract) based on Contracting Officer approval of the estimated amount and value of work or services performed [@CL-CTF-1650; @CL-CTF-1651], whereas the due date for final payments based on completion and acceptance of all work and presentation of release of all claims, and for accepted partial deliveries, is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice and the 30th day after Government acceptance [@CL-CTF-1653; @CL-CTF-1654]. Under the architect-engineer prompt payment clause at FAR 52.232-26, the due date for progress payments is the 30th day after Government approval of contractor estimates of work or services accomplished [@CL-CTF-1665]; see [Prompt payment for architect-engineer contracts and discounts for prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment-architect-engineer-and-discounts.md).
