## Purpose

Sets out what the construction prompt payment clause says about due dates for progress and final payments, invoice contents, retainage, and the payment and interest terms that must reach subcontractors. The general due-date rules sit on a linked card and are not repeated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1651] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-27 [@CL-CTF-1651] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1651] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, for the payment types it lists [@CL-CTF-1650; @CL-CTF-1653] |

The clause treats as progress payments, if provided for elsewhere in the contract, payments based on Contracting Officer approval of the estimated amount and value of work or services performed, including milestone payments [@CL-CTF-1650]. One listed due date for them is 14 days after the designated billing office receives a proper payment request [@CL-CTF-1651]. If the office fails to annotate the payment request with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the payment due date is the 14th day after the date of the contractor's payment request [@CL-CTF-1700], provided the office receives a proper payment request and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-1702]. Amounts retained by the Contracting Officer under the fixed-price construction payments clause are due as the contract specifies or, if it does not, 30 days after the Contracting Officer approves them [@CL-CTF-1652].

The clause treats as final payments those based on completion and acceptance of all work and a release of all claims against the Government, together with payments for accepted partial deliveries [@CL-CTF-1653]. One listed due date for them is the later of the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice and the 30th day after Government acceptance of the work or services completed [@CL-CTF-1653; @CL-CTF-1654]. For a final invoice when the payment amount is subject to contract settlement actions such as release of claims, acceptance is deemed to occur on the effective date of the contract settlement [@CL-CTF-1800]. If the office fails to annotate the invoice with the date of actual receipt at the time of receipt, the invoice payment due date is the 30th day after the date of the contractor's invoice [@CL-CTF-1701], provided the office receives a proper invoice and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or contractor compliance with contract requirements [@CL-CTF-1703].

## How it works

A proper invoice must include the listed items, and an invoice that does not comply must be returned within 7 days after receipt with the reasons [@CL-CTF-1655]. The listed items include the contractor's name and address, the invoice date and number, and the contract number or other authorization for the work [@CL-CTF-1656], as well as the description of the work, the delivery and payment terms, and the name and address of the contractor official to be paid, which must match the contract or a proper notice of assignment [@CL-CTF-1657]. The contractor must put payment terms in each subcontract [@CL-CTF-1658]: one listed term obliges it to pay the subcontractor not later than 7 days after it receives payment [@CL-CTF-1659], and another provides an interest penalty for late payment, counted from the day after the required payment date until payment, at the rate the Secretary of the Treasury established for interest payments under the cited provision of title 41 of the United States Code and in effect when the contractor accrues the obligation to pay it [@CL-CTF-1660; @CL-CTF-1661]. One listed subcontract provision, headed retainage permitted, lets the contractor or a subcontractor hold back a specified percentage of each progress payment due to a subcontractor, without cause and without owing a late payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-1662]. Another listed subcontract provision permits withholding without any obligation to pay a late payment penalty if a notice conforming to the clause's notice standards has previously been furnished to the subcontractor and the contractor gives the Contracting Officer a copy of any notice [@CL-CTF-1704]. This card does not state the other withholding procedures. See [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md) for the general due-date rules and [Proper invoice content](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md) for what makes an invoice proper.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A builder submits a proper progress payment request on day 1, and the billing office marks the receipt date. The payment is due on day 15. The builder receives 100,000 on day 15 and owes a subcontractor 40,000 of it. The subcontract holds back 10 percent as retainage, so 4,000 is held without a late payment interest penalty. The other 36,000 is due to the subcontractor by day 22.

For a final invoice, suppose a proper invoice is received on day 5 and the work is accepted on day 20. The later of day 35 and day 50 is day 50, so the due date is day 50. If instead the final invoice is subject to a release of claims and the settlement takes effect on day 60, acceptance is deemed to occur on day 60, and the later of day 35 and day 90 is day 90.

## Never confuse

Retainage held back from a subcontractor is not the same as the amounts the Contracting Officer retains from the contractor: the first is a subcontract provision that avoids a late payment interest penalty, and the second has its own due date [@CL-CTF-1662; @CL-CTF-1652]. The 14-day due date applies to the progress payments described above and the 30-day due date to the final payments described above, so do not apply one to the other [@CL-CTF-1651; @CL-CTF-1654]. The architect-engineer clause instead sets the due date for progress payments at the 30th day after Government approval of the contractor's estimates of work or services accomplished [@CL-CTF-1665]; see [Prompt payment for architect-engineer contracts and discounts for prompt payment](ctf-prompt-payment-architect-engineer-and-discounts.md).
