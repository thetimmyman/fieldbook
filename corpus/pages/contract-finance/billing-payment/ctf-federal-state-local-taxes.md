## Purpose

Shows how the Federal, State, and Local Taxes clause puts applicable taxes inside the contract price, when later Federal tax changes adjust that price, and the exception for taxes imposed under section 5000C of title 26 of the United States Code [@CL-CTF-0428].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0427] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.229-3 [@CL-CTF-0427] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.229-3 [@CL-CTF-0242] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0427] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Federal, State, and Local Taxes clause [@CL-CTF-0427] |

The contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties, with one exception: taxes imposed under section 5000C of title 26 of the United States Code may not be included in the price and may not be reimbursed [@CL-CTF-0427; @CL-CTF-0428].

The contract price is increased by the amount of any after-imposed Federal tax, provided the contractor warrants in writing that no amount for such newly imposed Federal excise tax or duty or rate increase was included in the contract price, as a contingency reserve or otherwise [@CL-CTF-0429].

The contract price is decreased by the amount of any after-relieved Federal tax [@CL-CTF-0430]. No adjustment is made unless the amount of the adjustment exceeds $250 [@CL-CTF-0431]. The definition of an after-imposed Federal tax does not include social security tax or other employment taxes [@CL-CTF-0620].

## How it works

Contract date means the date set for bid opening or, if the contract is a negotiated contract or a modification, the effective date of the contract or modification [@CL-CTF-0432].

Taxes and duties in effect on that date that the taxing authority is imposing and collecting on the transactions or property covered by the contract are included in the contract price [@CL-CTF-0427; @CL-CTF-0619]. The contract price shall be increased by the amount of any after-imposed Federal tax, subject to the written warranty, and decreased by the amount of any after-relieved Federal tax [@CL-CTF-0429; @CL-CTF-0430].

The increase is provided that the contractor warrants in writing that no amount for the newly imposed tax or rate increase was included in the contract price, as a contingency reserve or otherwise [@CL-CTF-0429], and no adjustment is made in the contract price unless the amount of the adjustment exceeds $250 [@CL-CTF-0431].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the rule that the contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties, except as provided in subparagraph (b)(2)(i) of the clause [@CL-CTF-0242] [@CL-CTF-0427], and that taxes imposed under 26 U.S.C. 5000C may not be included in the contract price and may not be reimbursed [@CL-CTF-0243] [@CL-CTF-0428]. It keeps the meaning of all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties, those in effect on the contract date that the taxing authority is imposing and collecting on the transactions or property covered by the contract [@CL-CTF-0249] [@CL-CTF-0619], and the meaning of contract date [@CL-CTF-0248] [@CL-CTF-0432]. The definition of an after-imposed Federal tax still does not include social security tax or other employment taxes [@CL-CTF-0247] [@CL-CTF-0620].

The RFO wording of the three price adjustments differs from the codified wording. The RFO clause says the Government will increase the contract price by the amount of any after-imposed Federal tax, provided the contractor warrants in writing that no amount for the newly imposed tax or rate increase was included in the contract price as a contingency reserve or otherwise [@CL-CTF-0244]. It says the Government will decrease the contract price by the amount of any after-relieved Federal tax [@CL-CTF-0245], and will not adjust the contract price unless the amount of the adjustment exceeds $250 [@CL-CTF-0246]. The codified clause says the contract price shall be increased or decreased by those amounts, and that no adjustment shall be made unless the amount exceeds $250, without naming the Government as the actor [@CL-CTF-0429] [@CL-CTF-0430] [@CL-CTF-0431]. The warranty condition and the $250 threshold are stated in both versions [@CL-CTF-0244] [@CL-CTF-0246] [@CL-CTF-0429] [@CL-CTF-0431].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract is priced with all applicable taxes in effect on the contract date. A new Federal excise tax later adds 4,000 to the contractor's cost, and the contractor warrants in writing that nothing for that tax was included in the price, so the price rises by 4,000. A later change relieves 1,000 of Federal excise tax, so the price falls by 1,000. A separate adjustment of 180 is not made, because it does not exceed the clause threshold.

## Never confuse

The definition of an after-imposed Federal tax does not include social security tax or other employment taxes [@CL-CTF-0620]. The contract price shall be increased by the amount of any after-imposed Federal tax, subject to the written warranty, and decreased by the amount of any after-relieved Federal tax [@CL-CTF-0429; @CL-CTF-0430].

Taxes imposed under section 5000C of title 26 of the United States Code are the exception to the rule that the contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties, and may not be included in the contract price or reimbursed [@CL-CTF-0427; @CL-CTF-0428].
