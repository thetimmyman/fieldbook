## Purpose

Explains when the examination right under the sealed bidding audit and records clause arises, who holds it and how far it reaches, in the basic clause and in Alternate I.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2948] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-26 [@CL-CTF-2948] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.214-26 [@CL-CTF-0006; @CL-CTF-0007; @CL-CTF-0008; @CL-CTF-0009; @CL-CTF-0010]; No RFO equivalent for Alternate I, which the RFO section does not print [@CL-CTF-0016] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2948] |
| Applies to | Sealed bidding contracts in which the clause is inserted under its own prescription [@CL-CTF-2948] |

As used in the clause, records includes books, documents, accounting procedures and practices and other data, regardless of type and regardless of whether the items are in written form, in computer data or in any other form [@CL-CTF-2949].

In the basic clause, the right arises where the Contractor has been required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with the pricing of any modification to the contract [@CL-CTF-2950]. The Contracting Officer, or an authorized representative, may then examine and audit the Contractor's records, including computations and projections, that are related to the proposal for the modification, the discussions conducted on the proposal including those related to negotiating, the pricing of the modification, or the performance of the modification, in order to evaluate the accuracy, completeness and currency of the certified cost or pricing data [@CL-CTF-2951; @CL-CTF-2952].

In the case of pricing any modification, the Comptroller General of the United States or an authorized representative has the same rights as paragraph (b) gives and also the right to interview any current employee regarding such transactions [@CL-CTF-2953].

Alternate I is prescribed under FAR 14.201-7(a)(2) and substitutes its paragraphs (c) and (e) for paragraphs (c) and (e) of the basic clause [@CL-CTF-2974]. Under the alternate, the Comptroller General of the United States, an appropriate Inspector General or an authorized representative of either has access to and the right to take the actions listed in its paragraph (c) [@CL-CTF-2975]. Those officials have the right to examine any of the Contractor's or any subcontractors' records that pertain to, and involve transactions relating to, the contract or a subcontract under it [@CL-CTF-2976]. They also have the right to interview any officer or employee regarding such transactions [@CL-CTF-2977].

## How it works

Three questions fix the right in the basic clause. Was the Contractor required to submit certified cost or pricing data for a modification, which is the condition on the Contracting Officer's right [@CL-CTF-2950]? Is the record related to the proposal, the discussions, the pricing or the performance of that modification [@CL-CTF-2952]? Is the examiner the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative, who may examine and audit [@CL-CTF-2951], or the Comptroller General or an authorized representative, who in the case of pricing any modification holds the same rights and may also interview current employees [@CL-CTF-2953]?

Under Alternate I the examiners in paragraph (c) are the Comptroller General, an appropriate Inspector General or an authorized representative of either [@CL-CTF-2975]. The records they may examine are described by the transactions relating to the contract or a subcontract under it, and the interview right covers any officer or employee [@CL-CTF-2976; @CL-CTF-2977]. Whether a contract carries the basic clause or Alternate I is a contract-specific fact.

How long the records must stay available, and the subcontract flowdown, are on the [availability and flowdown](../billing-payment/ctf-audit-and-records-sealed-bidding-availability-and-flowdown.md) page.

Under the RFO text, which DoD contracting officers use in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762], the comparison with the codified text is as follows. RFO FAR 52.214-26 keeps the definition of records [@CL-CTF-2949; @CL-CTF-0006] and the condition that the right arises where the Contractor has been required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with the pricing of any modification [@CL-CTF-2950; @CL-CTF-0007]. It keeps the right of the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative to examine and audit the Contractor's records, including computations and projections, to evaluate the certified data [@CL-CTF-2951; @CL-CTF-0008], limited to records related to the four listed matters [@CL-CTF-2952; @CL-CTF-0009]. It also keeps the Comptroller General's same rights and interview right for current employees [@CL-CTF-2953; @CL-CTF-0010]. The RFO section prints no Alternate I [@CL-CTF-0016], where the codified text has an Alternate I with access for the Comptroller General and an appropriate Inspector General [@CL-CTF-2974; @CL-CTF-2975].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A sealed bid contract with the basic clause is modified, and the Contractor was required to submit certified cost or pricing data for the change. The Contracting Officer's auditor asks for the cost build-up that priced the modification, the notes of the negotiation on the proposal, the labor projections behind the price and the shop records showing how the changed work was performed. Each falls within the proposal, the discussions, the pricing or the performance of the modification, so each may be examined and audited to evaluate the accuracy, completeness and currency of the certified data. A representative of the Comptroller General may also interview a current employee about those transactions. The auditor's request for records of an unrelated contract is outside the four listed matters. If the contract instead carried Alternate I, the Comptroller General, an appropriate Inspector General or a representative of either could examine records of the Contractor or a subcontractor that pertain to transactions relating to the contract, and could interview any officer or employee about them. The RFO section prints no Alternate I, so that last case has no counterpart in the RFO text [@CL-CTF-0016].

## Never confuse

In the basic clause the Contracting Officer's examination right follows certified cost or pricing data required for a modification [@CL-CTF-2950]. Alternate I words the access right by the transactions relating to the contract or a subcontract [@CL-CTF-2976].

The right to examine and audit is not a right to every record. It reaches records related to the four listed matters [@CL-CTF-2952].

The interview right differs by version. In the basic clause it belongs to the Comptroller General or an authorized representative and runs to current employees [@CL-CTF-2953]. Under Alternate I it belongs to the Comptroller General, an appropriate Inspector General or an authorized representative of either and runs to any officer or employee [@CL-CTF-2975; @CL-CTF-2977]. In the codified text the interview right differs by version [@CL-CTF-2953; @CL-CTF-2977], but the RFO section prints only the Comptroller General interview right for current employees and no Alternate I [@CL-CTF-0010; @CL-CTF-0016].
