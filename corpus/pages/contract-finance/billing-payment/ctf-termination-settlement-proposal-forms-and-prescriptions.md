## Purpose

Which standard form carries each kind of settlement proposal, what a contractor provides when it asks for authority to settle subcontractor settlement proposals, and which other termination clauses the FAR prescribes for personal services, cost-reimbursement, time-and-material and labor-hour contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3148] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.602-1, FAR 49.605 and FAR 49.505 [@CL-CTF-3148; @CL-CTF-3152; @CL-CTF-3153] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3148] |
| Applies to | Settlement proposals after a termination, requests to settle subcontractor settlement proposals, and the termination clauses prescribed for personal services and excusable delays [@CL-CTF-3148; @CL-CTF-3152; @CL-CTF-3153] |

Standard Form 1435, Settlement Proposal (Inventory Basis), shall be used to submit settlement proposals resulting from the termination of fixed-price contracts if the proposals are computed on an inventory basis [@CL-CTF-3148]. Standard Form 1436, Settlement Proposal (Total Cost Basis), shall be used to submit settlement proposals resulting from the termination of fixed-price contracts if the proposals are computed on a total cost basis [@CL-CTF-3149]. When use of the inventory basis is not practicable or will unduly delay settlement, the total-cost basis (SF 1436) may be used if approved in advance by the termination contracting officer (TCO) as in the following examples: (i) if production has not commenced and the accumulated costs represent planning and preproduction or get ready expenses, (ii) if, under the contractor's accounting system, unit costs for work in process and finished products cannot readily be established, (iii) if the contract does not specify unit prices, or (iv) if the termination is complete and involves a letter contract [@CL-CTF-3338; @CL-CLS-1593; @CL-CTF-1497]. Standard Form 1437, Settlement Proposal for Cost-Reimbursement Type Contracts, shall be used to submit settlement proposals resulting from the termination of cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-CTF-3150]. Standard Form 1438, Settlement Proposal (Short Form), shall be used to submit settlement proposals resulting from the termination of fixed-price contracts if the total proposal is less than $10,000 [@CL-CTF-3151]. The contractor may use the Settlement Proposal (Short Form), SF 1438, when the total proposal is less than $10,000, unless otherwise instructed by the TCO [@CL-CTF-3336], and settlement proposals that would normally be included in a single settlement proposal, such as those based on a series of separate orders for the same item under one contract, should be consolidated whenever possible and not divided to bring them below $10,000 [@CL-CTF-3337].

## How it works

Contractors requesting authority to settle their subcontractor settlement proposals shall furnish applicable information from the list in FAR 49.605 and any additional information required by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-3152]. The list covers:

- Name of contractor and address of principal office, name and location of divisions of the applicant's plant for which authorization is requested, and an explanation of the necessity and justification for the authorization requested [@CL-CTF-3297].
- A full description of the applicant's organization for handling terminations, including the names of the officials in charge of processing and settling proposals [@CL-CTF-3298].
- The number and dollar amount (estimated if necessary) of uncompleted contracts with Government agencies and the percentage applicable to each agency [@CL-CTF-3299].
- The number and dollar amount (estimated if necessary) of uncompleted subcontracts under Government contracts and the percentage applicable to each agency [@CL-CTF-3300].
- The extent of the applicant's experience in termination matters, including the handling of proposals of subcontractors, and the approximate amount and general nature of terminations of the applicant currently in process [@CL-CTF-3301].
- A statement that no other application has been made for any division of the applicant's plant covered by the application or, if one has been made, a full statement of the facts, and the limit of authorization requested [@CL-CTF-3302].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.249-12, Termination (Personal Services), in solicitations and contracts for personal services (see FAR part 37) [@CL-CTF-3153]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.249-14, Excusable Delays, in solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, construction, and research and development on a fee basis, when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-3154]. The contracting officer shall also insert the clause in time-and-material contracts, and labor-hour contracts [@CL-CTF-3303]. The cost-reimbursement termination clause is FAR 52.249-6, inserted as prescribed in FAR 49.503(a)(1) [@CL-CTF-0612].

This regulation does not prescribe a clause for the cancellation or termination of orders under communication service contracts with common carriers because of special agency requirements that apply to these services [@CL-CTF-3155]. An appropriate clause, however, shall be prescribed at agency level, within the agencies contracting for these services [@CL-CTF-3304].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price contract is terminated and the contractor computes a 250,000 proposal on an inventory basis, so it uses Standard Form 1435. A second fixed-price contract is terminated, inventory-basis settlement is not practicable, and the termination contracting officer approves the total cost basis in advance, so the contractor computes a 400,000 proposal on that basis and uses Standard Form 1436. A third fixed-price termination has a single total proposal of 8,000, which is less than 10,000 and has not been divided to get below that figure, so the contractor uses the short form, Standard Form 1438, unless the termination contracting officer instructs otherwise. A terminated cost-reimbursement contract uses Standard Form 1437.

A prime contractor that wants authority to settle its subcontractors' proposals sends a request that lists its organization for handling terminations, its experience, its terminations in process and the limit of authorization requested, and any further information the contracting officer requires.

For clauses, a personal services contract takes the personal services termination clause. A time-and-material contract also takes the Excusable Delays clause. For orders under a communication service contract with a common carrier, the FAR prescribes no clause, and an appropriate clause is prescribed at agency level.

## Never confuse

Standard Form 1435, Standard Form 1436, Standard Form 1437, and Standard Form 1438 are used to submit settlement proposals [@CL-CTF-3148; @CL-CTF-3149; @CL-CTF-3150; @CL-CTF-3151], and the formats for settlement agreements on cost-reimbursement contracts are on [the cost-reimbursement settlement formats card](ctf-cost-reimbursement-settlement-agreement-formats.md).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.249-12, Termination (Personal Services), in solicitations and contracts for personal services [@CL-CTF-3153], whereas the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.249-14, Excusable Delays, in solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, construction, and research and development on a fee basis when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated, and in time-and-material contracts and labor-hour contracts [@CL-CTF-3154; @CL-CTF-3303].

Contractors requesting authority to settle subcontractor settlement proposals shall furnish applicable information from the list in FAR 49.605 and any additional information required by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-3152]. The authorization and award mechanics are on [the subcontract settlements card](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-subcontract-settlements-authorization-and-awards.md), submission of fixed-price proposals is on [the fixed-price settlement proposal card](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md), personal services terminations are on [the architect-engineer and personal services card](ctf-termination-architect-engineer-and-personal-services.md), and cost-reimbursement termination is on [the cost-reimbursement termination settlement card](ctf-termination-cost-reimbursement-settlement.md).
