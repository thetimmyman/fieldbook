## Purpose

Shows what the Government pays under the fixed-price research and development, personal services and transportation payment clauses, when it pays and on what invoice.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2003; @CL-CTF-2005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-2, FAR 52.232-3 and FAR 52.232-4 [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2003; @CL-CTF-2005] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2003; @CL-CTF-2005] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for fixed-price research and development, for personal services, and for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-CTF-1516; @CL-CTF-1517] |

Under the research and development clause, the Government pays the contract prices for work delivered or rendered and accepted, on proper invoices or vouchers, less any deductions the contract provides [@CL-CTF-2001]. Unless the contract says otherwise, payment is made on acceptance of any portion of the work that has a separately stated price [@CL-CTF-2002].

Under the personal services clause, payment is for the services performed, as set forth in the Schedule, at the rates prescribed, on proper invoices or time statements sent to the office or officer designated and at the time the contract provides [@CL-CTF-2003]. The Government also pays a per diem rate in lieu of subsistence for each day the Contractor is in travel status away from home or the regular place of employment, in accordance with the Federal Travel Regulations as authorized in appropriate Travel Orders [@CL-CTF-2004].

Under the transportation clause, the Government pays on properly certified invoices or vouchers the amount due for services rendered and accepted, less any deductions the clause provides [@CL-CTF-2005].

## How it works

Each clause ties payment to a document the Contractor submits: proper invoices or vouchers for research and development, proper invoices or time statements for personal services, and properly certified invoices or vouchers for transportation [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2003; @CL-CTF-2005]. For research and development, acceptance of a separately priced portion of the work is the payment trigger unless the contract specifies otherwise [@CL-CTF-2002]. The contracting officer inserts these clauses modified with respect to payment due dates in accordance with agency regulations [@CL-CTF-1514].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price research and development contract states a separate price of 80,000 for a test report. The Contractor delivers the report and the Government accepts it. The Contractor submits a proper invoice. The Government owes 80,000 less any deductions stated in the contract, payable on acceptance of that portion of the work, unless the contract specifies another time.

On a personal services contract at an assumed rate of 50 per hour, a time statement for 100 hours sent to the designated office supports a payment of 5,000. Travel days away from home add per diem as the Travel Orders authorize.

## Never confuse

The research and development clause pays for work delivered or rendered and accepted [@CL-CTF-2001], whereas the construction clause makes progress payments monthly as the work proceeds, on estimates of work accomplished [@CL-CTF-2006]. The construction clause is on [Payments under fixed-price construction contracts and common carrier payments](ctf-payments-fixed-price-construction-and-common-carriers.md). Withholding and extras for supply and service contracts are on [Limitation on withholding of payments, extras and prohibition of assignment of claims](ctf-withholding-limit-extras-and-assignment-prohibition.md).
