## Purpose

Shows what the Government pays under the fixed-price research and development, personal services and transportation payment clauses, when it pays and on what invoice.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2003; @CL-CTF-2005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-2, FAR 52.232-3 and FAR 52.232-4 [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2003; @CL-CTF-2005] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-2 [@CL-CTF-0541]; RFO FAR 52.232-3 [@CL-CTF-0543]; RFO FAR 52.232-4 [@CL-CTF-0545] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2003; @CL-CTF-2005] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price research and development contract is contemplated, for personal services, and for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-CTF-1516; @CL-CTF-1517], appropriately modified with respect to payment due dates in accordance with agency regulations [@CL-CTF-1514] |

Under the fixed-price research and development clause, the Government must pay the Contractor, upon submission of proper invoices or vouchers, the prices stipulated in the contract for work delivered or rendered and accepted, less any deductions provided in the contract [@CL-CTF-2001]. Unless otherwise specified, payment must be made upon acceptance of any portion of the work delivered or rendered for which a price is separately stated in the contract [@CL-CTF-2002].

Under the personal services clause, the Government must pay the Contractor for the services performed by the Contractor, as set forth in the Schedule of the contract, at the rates prescribed, upon the submission by the Contractor of proper invoices or time statements to the office or officer designated and at the time provided for in the contract [@CL-CTF-2003]. The Government must also pay the Contractor a per diem rate in lieu of subsistence for each day the Contractor is in a travel status away from home or regular place of employment in accordance with Federal Travel Regulations (41 CFR 101-7) as authorized in appropriate Travel Orders [@CL-CTF-2004]. Under FAR 2.101, a personal services contract means a contract that, by its express terms or as administered, makes the contractor personnel appear to be, in effect, Government employees (see FAR 37.104) [@CL-CTF-9471], and RFO FAR 2.101 states the same definition [@CL-CTF-9472].

Under the transportation clause, the Government must pay the Contractor upon the submission of properly certified invoices or vouchers, the amount due for services rendered and accepted, less deductions, if any, as provided in the clause [@CL-CTF-2005].

## How it works

Under FAR 32.111(a), the contracting officer must insert the clauses, appropriately modified with respect to payment due dates, in accordance with agency regulations [@CL-CTF-1514]: the clause at FAR 52.232-2, Payment under Fixed-Price Research and Development Contracts, in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price research and development contract is contemplated, the clause at FAR 52.232-3, Payments under Personal Services Contracts, in solicitations and contracts for personal services [@CL-CTF-1516], and the clause at FAR 52.232-4, Payments under Transportation Contracts and Transportation-Related Services Contracts, in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-CTF-1517].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. RFO FAR 52.232-2 states that as prescribed in FAR 32.111(a)(2), the contracting officer inserts the Payments under Fixed-Price Research and Development Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-0546], under which the Government must pay the contractor, upon submission of proper invoices or vouchers, the prices stipulated in the contract for work delivered or rendered and accepted, less any deductions provided in the contract [@CL-CTF-0541], and unless otherwise specified, payment must be made upon acceptance of any portion of the work delivered or rendered for which a price is separately stated in the contract [@CL-CTF-0542], matching FAR 52.232-2 [@CL-CTF-2001; @CL-CTF-2002]. RFO FAR 52.232-3 states that as prescribed in FAR 32.111(a)(3), the contracting officer inserts the Payments under Personal Services Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-0547], under which the Government must pay the contractor for the services performed by the contractor, as set forth in the Schedule of the contract, at the rates prescribed, upon the submission by the contractor of proper invoices or time statements to the office or officer designated and at the time provided for in the contract [@CL-CTF-0543], matching FAR 52.232-3 [@CL-CTF-2003]. RFO FAR 52.232-3 also requires the Government to pay the contractor a per diem rate in lieu of subsistence for each day the contractor is in a travel status away from home or regular place of employment in accordance with Federal Travel Regulations (41 CFR Subtitle F) as authorized in appropriate Travel Orders [@CL-CTF-0544], whereas FAR 52.232-3(a) cites Federal Travel Regulations (41 CFR 101-7) [@CL-CTF-2004]. RFO FAR 52.232-4 states that as prescribed in FAR 32.111(a)(4), the contracting officer inserts the Payments under Transportation Contracts and Transportation-Related Services Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-0548], under which the Government must pay the contractor upon the submission of properly certified invoices or vouchers, the amount due for services rendered and accepted, less deductions, if any, as provided in the clause [@CL-CTF-0545], matching FAR 52.232-4 [@CL-CTF-2005].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price research and development contract states a separate price of 80,000 for a test report. The Contractor delivers the report and the Government accepts it. The Contractor submits a proper invoice. The Government owes 80,000 less any deductions stated in the contract, payable on acceptance of that portion of the work, unless the contract specifies another time.

On a personal services contract at an assumed rate of 50 per hour, a time statement for 100 hours sent to the designated office supports a payment of 5,000. Travel days away from home add per diem as the Travel Orders authorize.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.232-2, the Government must pay the Contractor, upon submission of proper invoices or vouchers, the prices stipulated in the contract for work delivered or rendered and accepted, less any deductions provided in the contract [@CL-CTF-2001], whereas under the construction clause the Government must make progress payments monthly as the work proceeds, or at more frequent intervals as determined by the Contracting Officer, on estimates of work accomplished which meets the standards of quality established under the contract, as approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2006]. For the construction clause, see [Payments under fixed-price construction contracts and common carrier payments](ctf-payments-fixed-price-construction-and-common-carriers.md). For withholding and extras on supply and service contracts, see [Limitation on withholding of payments, extras and prohibition of assignment of claims](ctf-withholding-limit-extras-and-assignment-prohibition.md). For contract financing methods and noncommercial payment clauses, see [Contract financing methods and payment clauses for noncommercial purchases](../financing/ctf-financing-methods-and-noncommercial-payment-clauses.md). For determining invoice payment due dates, see [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md), and for proper invoice content, see [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md). Under FAR 32.111(a), the contracting officer must insert the clauses at FAR 52.232-2, FAR 52.232-3, and FAR 52.232-4, appropriately modified with respect to payment due dates, in accordance with agency regulations [@CL-CTF-1514; @CL-CTF-1516; @CL-CTF-1517], whereas RFO FAR 52.232-2, RFO FAR 52.232-3, and RFO FAR 52.232-4 state that as prescribed in FAR 32.111(a)(2), (a)(3), and (a)(4), the contracting officer inserts each respective clause [@CL-CTF-0546; @CL-CTF-0547; @CL-CTF-0548].
