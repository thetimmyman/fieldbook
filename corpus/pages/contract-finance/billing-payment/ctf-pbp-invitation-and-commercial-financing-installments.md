## Purpose

Shows what an offeror proposes under the invitation to propose performance-based payments, the financing conditions and repayment rules for commercial products and services, and how installment payments are computed, capped, requested and liquidated.

### Invitation to propose performance-based payments

The Government invites the offeror to propose terms under which the Government will make performance-based contract financing payments during contract performance [@CL-CTF-2031]. The offeror's proposal of performance-based payment financing must include the proposed contractual language describing the performance-based payments (see FAR 32.1004 for appropriate criteria for establishing performance bases and performance-based finance payment amounts) [@CL-CTF-2144], a listing of the projected performance-based payment dates and the projected payment amounts and of the projected delivery date and the projected payment amount, and information addressing the Contractor's investment in the contract [@CL-CTF-2145].

The Contracting Officer will not accept the offeror's proposed performance-based payment financing if the financing does not conform to the listed limitations [@CL-CTF-2032]. Under paragraph (c)(2)(iii), their total must not exceed 90 percent of the contract price if on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price if on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-2033]. In the event of any conflict between the terms proposed by the offeror and the terms in the clause at FAR 52.232-32, Performance-Based Payments, the terms of the clause at FAR 52.232-32 govern [@CL-CTF-2034].

### Terms for financing of purchases of commercial products and services

The Contractor may request, and the Government must pay, a contract financing payment as specified elsewhere in the contract when [@CL-CTF-9624] the payment requested is properly due in accordance with the contract, the supplies deliverable or services due under the contract will be delivered or performed in accordance with the contract, and there has been no impairment or diminution of the Government's security under the contract [@CL-CTF-2035]. In the event the Contractor fails to provide adequate security, as required in the contract, no financing payment may be made under the contract [@CL-CTF-2037]. Upon receipt of adequate security, financing payments must be made, including all previous payments to which the Contractor is entitled, in accordance with the terms of the provisions for contract financing [@CL-CTF-2200]. Contractor financing payments must be provided no more frequently than monthly [@CL-CTF-2141]. A payment under the clause is a contract financing payment and not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act, and the designated payment office will pay approved payment requests within 30 days of submittal of a proper request for payment [@CL-CTF-2036]. If the contract is terminated for cause, the Contractor must, on demand, repay to the Government the amount of unliquidated contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-2140].

## How it works

### Installment payments

Installment payment financing must be paid to the Contractor when requested for each separately priced unit of supply, but not for services, of each line item in amounts approved by the Contracting Officer pursuant to the clause [@CL-CTF-2199]. The number of installment payments authorized for each unit of a line item is equal to the number of months from the date of contract award to the date one month before the first delivery of the first separately priced unit of the line item [@CL-CTF-2038]. The amount of each installment payment for each separately priced unit of each line item is equal to 70 percent of the unit price divided by the number of installment payments authorized for that unit [@CL-CTF-2039]. Prior to the delivery payment for a separately priced unit of a line item, the sum of all installment payments for that unit must not exceed 70 percent of the price of that unit [@CL-CTF-2142]. The Contractor may submit requests for payment of installment payments not more frequently than monthly, in a form and manner acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2143], and, unless otherwise authorized by the Contracting Officer, all installment payments in any month for which payment is being requested must be included in a single request, appropriately itemized and totaled [@CL-CTF-9497]. Installment payments must be liquidated by deducting from the delivery payment of each item the total unliquidated amount of installment payments made for that separately priced unit of that line item [@CL-CTF-2040].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. RFO FAR 52.232-28 provides that the Government invites the offeror to propose terms under which the Government will make performance-based contract financing payments during contract performance [@CL-CTF-0549], and that in the event of any conflict between the terms proposed by the offeror and the terms in the clause at FAR 52.232-32, Performance-Based Payments, the terms of the clause at FAR 52.232-32 govern [@CL-CTF-0550], matching FAR 52.232-28(a) and (b) [@CL-CTF-2031; @CL-CTF-2034]. RFO FAR 52.232-28 provides that the contracting officer will not accept the offeror's proposed performance-based payment financing if the financing does not conform to the listed limitations [@CL-CTF-0551], including that their total must not exceed 90 percent of the contract price if on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price if on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-0552], matching FAR 52.232-28(c) and (c)(2)(iii) [@CL-CTF-2032; @CL-CTF-2033]. RFO FAR 52.232-28 requires the offeror's proposal of performance-based payment financing to include the proposed contractual language describing the performance-based payments (see FAR 32.1004 for appropriate criteria for establishing performance bases and performance-based finance payment amounts) [@CL-CTF-0553], a listing of the projected performance-based payment dates and the projected payment amounts and of the projected delivery date and the projected payment amount, and information addressing the contractor's investment in the contract [@CL-CTF-0554], matching FAR 52.232-28(d)(1) through (3) [@CL-CTF-2144; @CL-CTF-2145]. RFO FAR 52.232-29 provides that the contractor may request, and the Government must pay, a contract financing payment as specified elsewhere in the contract [@CL-CTF-9625] when the payment requested is properly due in accordance with the contract, the supplies deliverable or services due under the contract will be delivered or performed in accordance with the contract, and there has been no impairment or diminution of the Government's security under the contract [@CL-CTF-0555], matching FAR 52.232-29(a) [@CL-CTF-9624; @CL-CTF-2035]. RFO FAR 52.232-29 provides that if the contract is terminated for cause, the contractor must, on demand, repay to the Government the amount of unliquidated contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-0556], that in the event the contractor fails to provide adequate security, as required in the contract, no financing payment may be made under the contract [@CL-CTF-0557], that upon receipt of adequate security, financing payments must be made, including all previous payments to which the contractor is entitled, in accordance with the terms of the provisions for contract financing [@CL-CTF-0558], that contractor financing payments must be provided no more frequently than monthly [@CL-CTF-0559], and that a payment under the clause is a contract financing payment and not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act, with the designated payment office paying approved payment requests within 30 days of submittal of a proper request for payment [@CL-CTF-0568], matching FAR 52.232-29(b), (c), (f), and (g) [@CL-CTF-2140; @CL-CTF-2037; @CL-CTF-2200; @CL-CTF-2141; @CL-CTF-2036]. RFO FAR 52.232-30 provides that installment payment financing must be paid to the contractor when requested for each separately priced unit of supply, but not for services, of each line item in amounts approved by the contracting officer pursuant to the clause [@CL-CTF-0569], that the number of installment payments authorized for each unit of a line item is equal to the number of months from the date of contract award to the date one month before the first delivery of the first separately priced unit of the line item [@CL-CTF-0570], that the amount of each installment payment for each separately priced unit of each line item is equal to 70 percent of the unit price divided by the number of installment payments authorized for that unit [@CL-CTF-0571], and that prior to the delivery payment for a separately priced unit of a line item, the sum of all installment payments for that unit must not exceed 70 percent of the price of that unit [@CL-CTF-0572], matching FAR 52.232-30(b), (b)(1), (b)(2), and (b)(4) [@CL-CTF-2199; @CL-CTF-2038; @CL-CTF-2039; @CL-CTF-2142]. RFO FAR 52.232-30 also provides that the contractor may submit requests for payment of installment payments not more frequently than monthly, in a form and manner acceptable to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0573], that unless otherwise authorized by the contracting officer, all installment payments in any month for which payment is being requested must be included in a single request, appropriately itemized and totaled [@CL-CTF-9503], and that installment payments must be liquidated by deducting from the delivery payment of each item the total unliquidated amount of installment payments made for that separately priced unit of that line item [@CL-CTF-0574], matching FAR 52.232-30(c) and (e) [@CL-CTF-2143; @CL-CTF-9497; @CL-CTF-2040].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply line item has separately priced units priced at 60,000 each, and the first delivery is 8 months after award. The number of installments is the months from award to one month before first delivery, so 7 for each unit. Each installment is 70 percent of 60,000, which is 42,000, divided by 7, so 6,000. The seven installments add up to 42,000, which meets the limit of 70 percent of the unit price. At delivery the Government deducts the 42,000 already paid from the 60,000 delivery payment and pays 18,000.

An offeror proposes performance-based payments on a whole contract basis for a contract priced at 1,000,000. The proposed terms total at most 900,000, so they do not exceed the 90 percent limit.

Under a financing clause for a commercial purchase, 100,000 of financing is unliquidated when the contract is terminated for cause. The Government demands repayment, and the Contractor repays the 100,000.
