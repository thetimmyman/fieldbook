## Purpose

Shows what an offeror proposes under the invitation to propose performance-based payments, the financing conditions and repayment rules for commercial products and services, and how installment payments are computed, capped, requested and liquidated.

### Invitation to propose performance-based payments

The Government invites the offeror to propose terms under which it will make performance-based contract financing payments during contract performance [@CL-CTF-2031]. The offeror's proposal shall include the proposed contractual language describing the performance-based payments, among other listed items [@CL-CTF-2144]. Two further items are a listing of the projected performance-based payment dates and amounts and of the projected delivery date and payment amount, and information addressing the Contractor's investment in the contract [@CL-CTF-2145].

The Contracting Officer will not accept the proposed financing if it does not conform to the listed limitations [@CL-CTF-2032]. One limitation is that the total of the terms may not exceed 90 percent of the contract price on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-2033]. If the proposed terms conflict with the Performance-Based Payments clause, the clause governs [@CL-CTF-2034].

### Terms for financing of purchases of commercial products and services

The Contractor may request, and the Government shall pay, a contract financing payment as specified elsewhere in the contract when three conditions are met [@CL-CTF-9624]: the payment requested is properly due, the supplies or services will be delivered or performed in accordance with the contract, and the Government's security has not been impaired or diminished [@CL-CTF-2035]. If the Contractor fails to provide adequate security, as required in the contract, no financing payment is made [@CL-CTF-2037]. Upon receipt of adequate security, financing payments are made, including all previous payments to which the Contractor is entitled, in accordance with the terms of the provisions for contract financing [@CL-CTF-2200]. Financing payments are provided no more frequently than monthly [@CL-CTF-2141]. The payment office pays approved requests within 30 days of a proper request, and the payment is not subject to the interest penalty of the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CTF-2036]. If the contract is terminated for cause, the Contractor shall, on demand, repay the amount of unliquidated contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-2140].

### Installment payments

Installment payment financing is paid to the Contractor when requested for each separately priced unit of supply, but not for services, of each line item, in amounts the Contracting Officer approves [@CL-CTF-2199]. The number of installment payments authorized for each unit of a line item equals the number of months from contract award to one month before the first delivery of the first separately priced unit [@CL-CTF-2038]. Each installment equals 70 percent of the unit price divided by the number of installments authorized for that unit [@CL-CTF-2039]. Before the delivery payment for a unit, the installments paid for that unit may not add up to more than 70 percent of its price [@CL-CTF-2142]. The Contractor may request installment payments no more frequently than monthly, in a form and manner acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2143], and, unless the Contracting Officer authorizes otherwise, all installment payments in any month for which payment is requested must be included in a single request, appropriately itemized and totaled [@CL-CTF-9497]. Installments are liquidated by deducting from the delivery payment of each item the unliquidated installments paid for that unit [@CL-CTF-2040].

## How it works

The offeror's proposal supplies the performance-based payment terms, and the clause terms win if the two conflict [@CL-CTF-2031; @CL-CTF-2034]. Financing payments under the commercial financing clause depend on three conditions and, where the contract requires it, on adequate security, and unliquidated amounts are repayable on demand if the contract is terminated for cause [@CL-CTF-2035; @CL-CTF-2037; @CL-CTF-2140]. Installment payments are computed from the unit price: the number of installments is the number of months from award to one month before the first delivery, and each installment is a share of 70 percent of the unit price [@CL-CTF-2038; @CL-CTF-2039]. At delivery, the installments already paid for that unit are deducted from the delivery payment [@CL-CTF-2040].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO invitation provision keeps the rule that the Government invites the offeror to propose terms under which it will make performance-based contract financing payments during contract performance [@CL-CTF-0549] [@CL-CTF-2031], and that the performance-based payments clause at FAR 52.232-32 governs any conflict with the offeror's proposed terms [@CL-CTF-0550] [@CL-CTF-2034]. It keeps the rule that the contracting officer will not accept proposed financing that does not conform to the limitations it lists [@CL-CTF-0551] [@CL-CTF-2032], including that the total of the terms shall not exceed 90 percent of the contract price on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-0552] [@CL-CTF-2033]. It keeps the proposal contents, which are the proposed contractual language [@CL-CTF-0553] [@CL-CTF-2144], a listing of the projected payment dates and amounts and of the projected delivery date and payment amount, and information on the contractor's investment in the contract [@CL-CTF-0554] [@CL-CTF-2145]. The RFO commercial financing clause keeps the rule that the contractor may request, and the Government shall pay, a contract financing payment as specified elsewhere in the contract [@CL-CTF-9625] [@CL-CTF-9624] when three conditions hold: the payment requested is properly due, the supplies or services will be delivered or performed in accordance with the contract, and there has been no impairment or diminution of the Government's security [@CL-CTF-0555] [@CL-CTF-2035]. It keeps the rules that the contractor repays unliquidated contract financing payments on demand if the contract is terminated for cause [@CL-CTF-0556] [@CL-CTF-2140], that no financing payment is made if the contractor fails to provide adequate security as the contract requires [@CL-CTF-0557] [@CL-CTF-2037], that financing payments are made, including all previous payments to which the contractor is entitled, upon receipt of adequate security, in accordance with the terms of the provisions for contract financing [@CL-CTF-0558] [@CL-CTF-2200], that financing payments are provided no more frequently than monthly [@CL-CTF-0559] [@CL-CTF-2141], and that the payment is a contract financing payment, not subject to the Prompt Payment Act interest penalty, paid within 30 days of a proper request [@CL-CTF-0568] [@CL-CTF-2036]. The RFO installment payments clause keeps the rules that installment financing is paid to the contractor when requested for each separately priced unit of supply, but not for services, of each line item in amounts the contracting officer approves [@CL-CTF-0569] [@CL-CTF-2199], that the number of installments equals the number of months from contract award to one month before the first delivery of the first separately priced unit [@CL-CTF-0570] [@CL-CTF-2038], that each installment equals 70 percent of the unit price divided by the number of installments authorized for that unit [@CL-CTF-0571] [@CL-CTF-2039], and that the installments for a unit may not exceed 70 percent of its price before the delivery payment [@CL-CTF-0572] [@CL-CTF-2142]. It keeps the rules that the contractor may request installments no more frequently than monthly, in a form and manner acceptable to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0573] [@CL-CTF-2143], and, unless the contracting officer authorizes otherwise, that all installment payments in any month for which payment is requested are included in a single request, appropriately itemized and totaled [@CL-CTF-9503] [@CL-CTF-9497], and that installments are liquidated by deducting from the delivery payment of each item the unliquidated installments for that unit [@CL-CTF-0574] [@CL-CTF-2040].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply line item has separately priced units priced at 60,000 each, and the first delivery is 8 months after award. The number of installments is the months from award to one month before first delivery, so 7 for each unit. Each installment is 70 percent of 60,000, which is 42,000, divided by 7, so 6,000. The seven installments add up to 42,000, which meets the limit of 70 percent of the unit price. At delivery the Government deducts the 42,000 already paid from the 60,000 delivery payment and pays 18,000.

An offeror proposes performance-based payments on a whole contract basis for a contract priced at 1,000,000. The proposed terms total at most 900,000, so they do not exceed the 90 percent limit.

Under a financing clause for a commercial purchase, 100,000 of financing is unliquidated when the contract is terminated for cause. The Government demands repayment, and the Contractor repays the 100,000.

## Never confuse

Performance-based payments are proposed by the offeror and capped in total at 90 percent [@CL-CTF-2033], whereas each installment payment is 70 percent of the unit price divided by the number of installment payments authorized for that unit [@CL-CTF-2039]. For the performance-based payments clause, see [Performance-Based Payments clause](../financing/ctf-performance-based-payments.md), and for when they may be used and how they are set, see [Performance-based payments: when they may be used and how they are set](../financing/ctf-pbp-criteria.md). The invitation here is for performance-based payments, not for customary progress payments, whose solicitation notices are on [Solicitation notices on progress payments](ctf-progress-payment-solicitation-notices.md). The invitation to propose other financing terms is on [Invitation to propose financing terms and EFT information with an offer](ctf-financing-terms-and-eft-information-with-offer.md). Which security the contracting officer may accept, and when the installment clause may be used, are on [Commercial contract financing: security, offeror-proposed financing and clauses](../financing/ctf-commercial-financing-security-and-clauses.md) and [Contract financing for commercial purchases](../financing/ctf-contract-financing-for-commercial-purchases.md). How the Government pays the amounts is on [Payment by electronic funds transfer and the designated office for EFT information](ctf-eft-payment-clauses-and-designated-office.md).
