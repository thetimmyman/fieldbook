## Purpose

Shows when a contractor must accept the Governmentwide commercial purchase card, and what the advance payment pool clause provides.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1818] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7009 [@CL-CTF-1818]; DFARS 252.232-7000 [@CL-CTF-1819] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1818] |
| Applies to | Orders or calls at or below the micro-purchase threshold under the contract or agreement [@CL-CTF-1818]; payments under the advance payment pool clause [@CL-CTF-1819] |

The Contractor agrees to accept the Governmentwide commercial purchase card as the method of payment for orders or calls valued at or below the micro-purchase threshold in the cited part of the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement, under the contract or agreement [@CL-CTF-1818]. The DFARS directs use of the card clause as prescribed in DFARS 232.1110 and of the pool clause as prescribed in DFARS 232.412-70(a) [@CL-CTF-1836; @CL-CTF-1837]. The Federal Acquisition Regulation definition of the micro-purchase threshold is on [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).

Under the advance payment pool clause, advance payments will be made for contract performance in accordance with the Determinations, Findings, and Authorization for Advance payment, notwithstanding any other provision of the contract [@CL-CTF-1819]. Payments made in accordance with the clause are governed by the terms and conditions of the Advance Payment Pool Agreement, which is incorporated in the contract by reference [@CL-CTF-1820]. When a letter of credit has not been issued to the Contractor in conjunction with the contract, payment will be by a dual payee Treasury check made payable to the Contractor or the disbursing office in the Advance Payment Pool Agreement and will be forwarded to that disbursing office [@CL-CTF-1821] for appropriate disposition [@CL-CTF-1845].

## How it works

Under the card clause, the contractor agrees to accept the Governmentwide commercial purchase card as the method of payment for orders or calls valued at or below the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-CTF-1818]. Under the pool clause, payments made in accordance with the clause shall be governed by the terms and conditions of the Advance Payment Pool Agreement [@CL-CTF-1820], and when a letter of credit has not been issued to the contractor in conjunction with the contract, payment will be by a dual payee Treasury check [@CL-CTF-1821]. The pool agreement card is on [DoD advance payment pool agreements](../financing/ctf-dod-advance-payment-pool-agreements.md) and the FAR clause is on [Advance Payments clause](../financing/ctf-advance-payments-clause.md). Other financing clauses are on [Progress payment rates and FMS requests](ctf-dfars-progress-payment-rates-and-fms.md) and [Performance-based payments representation](ctf-dfars-pbp-representation.md). Card payment in the FAR is on [EFT applicability and purchase card payment](../financing/ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract includes the card clause, and the applicable micro-purchase threshold is taken to be 10,000. An order under it is valued at 2,000.

Answer: the contractor must accept the card for that order, because 2,000 is at or below the threshold. An order of 15,000 would fall outside the clause.

On a separate contract with a pool agreement and no letter of credit, the pool payment comes by dual payee Treasury check.

## Never confuse

The card clause and the pool clause differ in what kind of payment each governs rather than in wording: under the card clause, the contractor agrees to accept the Governmentwide commercial purchase card as the method of payment for orders or calls valued at or below the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-CTF-1818], while under the advance payment pool clause, payments made in accordance with the clause shall be governed by the terms and conditions of the Advance Payment Pool Agreement, which is incorporated in the contract by reference rather than printed in it [@CL-CTF-1820].
