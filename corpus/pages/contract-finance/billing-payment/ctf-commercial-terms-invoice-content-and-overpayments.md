## Purpose

Shows how the commercial terms clause says an invoice is submitted and what it must include, including the electronic funds transfer banking information rule, how payment, prompt payment and any early payment discount work, what the contractor does when it learns of an overpayment, and how interest runs on amounts the contractor owes the Government.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2434] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.212-4(g) and (i) [@CL-CTF-2434; @CL-CTF-2457; @CL-CTF-2461; @CL-CTF-2438; @CL-CTF-2467] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2434] |
| Applies to | Contracts that contain the commercial products and services terms clause, as to the paragraphs quoted [@CL-CTF-2434] |

The contractor shall submit an original invoice and three copies, or an electronic invoice if authorized, to the address designated in the contract to receive invoices, and the invoice must include the contractor's name and address and the invoice date and number [@CL-CTF-2434]. The list of required invoice contents continues with the contract number, line item number and, if applicable, the order number, and the description, quantity, unit of measure, unit price and extended price of the items delivered [@CL-CTF-2435]. The list also includes the shipping number and date of shipment, including the bill of lading number and weight of shipment if shipped on a Government bill of lading, and the terms of any discount for prompt payment offered [@CL-CTF-2436]. The list also includes the name and address of the official to whom payment is to be sent, the name, title, and phone number of the person to notify in the event of a defective invoice, and the Taxpayer Identification Number, which the contractor shall include on the invoice only if required elsewhere in the contract [@CL-CTF-2437].

The contractor shall include electronic funds transfer banking information on the invoice only if the contract requires it elsewhere [@CL-CTF-2457]. If that information is not required to be on the invoice, then in order for the invoice to be a proper invoice the contractor shall have submitted correct banking information in accordance with the applicable solicitation provision or contract clause [@CL-CTF-2458]. The contract clauses given as examples are 52.232-33, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—System for Award Management, and 52.232-34, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—Other Than System for Award Management, and the submission may alternatively be in accordance with applicable agency procedures [@CL-CTF-2459]. The banking information is not required if the Government waived the requirement to pay by electronic funds transfer, and invoices will be handled in accordance with the Prompt Payment Act and the Office of Management and Budget prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-2460].

Payment shall be made for items accepted by the Government that have been delivered to the delivery destinations set forth in the contract [@CL-CTF-2461]. The Government will make payment in accordance with the Prompt Payment Act and the prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315 [@CL-CTF-2462]. If the Government makes payment by electronic funds transfer, the appropriate electronic funds transfer clause is found by seeing 52.212-5(b), and in connection with any discount offered for early payment, time shall be computed from the date of the invoice [@CL-CTF-2463]. For the purpose of computing the discount earned, payment shall be considered to have been made on the date which appears on the payment check, or on the specified payment date if an electronic funds transfer payment is made [@CL-CTF-2464].

If the contractor becomes aware of a duplicate contract financing or invoice payment, or that the Government has otherwise overpaid on a contract financing or invoice payment, the contractor shall remit the overpayment amount to the payment office cited in the contract [@CL-CTF-2438]. The remittance goes along with a description of the overpayment that includes the circumstances of the overpayment, for example a duplicate payment, an erroneous payment, liquidation errors, or the dates of the overpayment [@CL-CTF-2465]. The description also includes the affected contract number and delivery order number, if applicable, the affected line item or subline item, if applicable, and the contractor point of contact, and the contractor shall provide a copy of the remittance and supporting documentation to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2466].

All amounts that become payable by the contractor to the Government under the contract shall bear simple interest from the date due until paid, unless paid within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-2467]. The interest rate shall be the rate established by the Secretary of the Treasury as provided in section 7109 of title 41 of the United States Code, which is applicable to the period in which the amount becomes due, as provided in paragraph (i)(6)(v) of the clause [@CL-CTF-2468]. The interest rate then becomes the rate applicable for each six-month period as fixed by the Secretary until the amount is paid [@CL-CTF-2469]. Amounts shall be due at the earliest of the date fixed under the contract and the date of the first written demand for payment, including any demand for payment resulting from a default termination [@CL-CTF-2655]. The Government may issue a demand for payment to the contractor upon finding a debt is due under the contract [@CL-CTF-2470].

The Contracting Officer will issue a final decision as required by 33.211 if the Contracting Officer and the contractor are unable to reach agreement on the existence or amount of a debt within 30 days [@CL-CTF-2651]. A second listed circumstance for the final decision is that the contractor fails to liquidate a debt previously demanded by the Contracting Officer within the timeline specified in the demand for payment, unless the amounts were not repaid because the contractor has requested an installment payment agreement [@CL-CTF-2652]. A third listed circumstance is that the contractor requests a deferment of collection on a debt previously demanded by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2653]. If a demand for payment was previously issued for the debt, the demand for payment included in the final decision shall identify the same due date as the original demand for payment [@CL-CTF-2654].

The interest charge shall be computed for the actual number of calendar days involved, beginning on the due date and ending on the date on which the designated office receives payment from the contractor, among the listed end dates [@CL-CTF-2656]. The other listed end dates are the date of issuance of a Government check to the contractor from which an amount otherwise payable has been withheld as a credit against the contract debt, and the date on which an amount withheld and applied to the contract debt would otherwise have become payable to the contractor [@CL-CTF-2657]. The interest charge may be reduced under the procedures prescribed in FAR 32.608-2 in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CTF-2658].

## How it works

The invoice goes to the address the contract designates for invoices and carries the listed elements [@CL-CTF-2434; @CL-CTF-2435; @CL-CTF-2436; @CL-CTF-2437]. The taxpayer number goes on the invoice only if the contract requires it elsewhere [@CL-CTF-2437]. Electronic funds transfer banking information goes on the invoice only if the contract requires it elsewhere, and if it is not required there, the invoice is a proper invoice only if the contractor had already submitted correct banking information through the applicable solicitation provision, contract clause or agency procedures [@CL-CTF-2457; @CL-CTF-2458; @CL-CTF-2459]. Payment is for accepted items delivered to the contract's delivery destinations and follows the Prompt Payment Act and its regulations [@CL-CTF-2461; @CL-CTF-2462]. An early payment discount is timed from the invoice date, and payment counts as made on the check date or the specified electronic payment date [@CL-CTF-2463; @CL-CTF-2464]. A contractor that learns of a duplicate or erroneous payment on an invoice or on contract financing must send the overpayment amount to the payment office named in the contract with the description of the overpayment, and must give the Contracting Officer a copy of the remittance and supporting documentation [@CL-CTF-2438; @CL-CTF-2465; @CL-CTF-2466].

Interest on a debt the contractor owes the Government runs from the due date unless the contractor pays within 30 days of becoming due [@CL-CTF-2467]. The due date is the earlier of the date fixed under the contract and the date of the first written demand [@CL-CTF-2655]. Interest is counted in actual calendar days from the due date to the applicable listed end date, and the charge may be reduced under the procedures in FAR 32.608-2 [@CL-CTF-2656; @CL-CTF-2657; @CL-CTF-2658]. A demand for payment may come from the Government on finding a debt, and a final decision follows in the listed circumstances, repeating the original due date if a demand was already issued [@CL-CTF-2470; @CL-CTF-2651; @CL-CTF-2652; @CL-CTF-2653; @CL-CTF-2654].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 500 units at 40 each, an extended price of 20,000. The invoice shows the contractor's name and address, the invoice date and number, the contract number and line item number, the description, quantity, unit of measure, unit price and extended price. The units shipped on a Government bill of lading, so the invoice also shows the shipping number, date, bill of lading number and weight. It shows the terms of a 2 percent prompt payment discount offered for payment within 10 days, the payment official's name and address, and the name, title and phone number of the person to notify of a defective invoice. The contract does not require the Taxpayer Identification Number or the electronic funds transfer banking information on the invoice, so the contractor leaves both off, and it had already submitted correct banking information through the applicable contract clause, so the invoice is a proper invoice. The contractor sends the original and three copies to the designated address. The invoice is dated Day 1, so the discount period is counted from Day 1. The Government pays by electronic funds transfer on the specified payment date of Day 9, and payment is considered made on Day 9, within the 10 days. Later the contractor finds that the Government paid the same 20,000 twice. It remits the 20,000 overpayment to the payment office cited in the contract with a description that gives the circumstances (duplicate payment), the contract number, the line item and its point of contact, and it gives the Contracting Officer a copy of the remittance and supporting documentation. Separately the Government finds a 5,000 debt owed by the contractor and issues a written demand for payment, and no earlier date is fixed under the contract, so the debt is due on the date of that demand. If the contractor pays on the 20th day after the due date, no interest is charged. If the contractor instead pays on the 45th day, simple interest runs from the due date for the 45 actual calendar days, ending on the date the designated office receives payment, at the Treasury rate applicable to the period when the debt became due and then at each six-month rate until paid. If the contractor and the Contracting Officer cannot agree on the amount within 30 days, the Contracting Officer issues a final decision, and the demand in it keeps the same due date. The interest charge may be reduced under the procedures in FAR 32.608-2.

## Never confuse

The list of invoice contents is not the late payment interest rule. This card lists what the invoice carries, and the interest penalty rules for late Government payments sit on [Prompt payment interest penalties and the prompt payment clauses](ctf-prompt-payment-interest-and-clauses.md). The interest in this card runs on amounts that become payable by the contractor to the Government [@CL-CTF-2467]. The duty to remit an overpayment covers contract financing as well as invoice payments [@CL-CTF-2438]; for financing terms see [Commercial financing security and clauses](../financing/ctf-commercial-financing-security-and-clauses.md). Electronic funds transfer banking information goes on the invoice only if the contract requires it, but when it is not required on the invoice, a proper invoice still needs correct banking information submitted earlier [@CL-CTF-2457; @CL-CTF-2458]. For the proper invoice rules in the FAR see [Proper invoice content](ctf-proper-invoice-content.md).
