## Purpose

Sets out where the Fast Payment Procedure clause is inserted, what delivery event the payment is based on, and what the invoice, shipment and containers must show.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2930] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.213-1 [@CL-CTF-2930] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2930] |
| Applies to | Purchases in which the clause is inserted under FAR 13.404 [@CL-CTF-2930] |

The clause is inserted in purchases as FAR 13.404 prescribes [@CL-CTF-2930]. Under it the Government pays invoices based on the Contractor's delivery to a post office or common carrier or, if shipped by other means, to the point of first receipt by the Government [@CL-CTF-2931].

On that delivery the Contractor must prepare an invoice as the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement provides and display "FAST PAY" prominently on it [@CL-CTF-2932].

## How it works

Invoice marking. Invoices not prominently marked "FAST PAY" via manual or electronic means may be accepted by the payment office for fast payment [@CL-CTF-2933]. If the payment office declines to make fast payment, the Contractor is paid under the procedures that apply to invoices outside the Fast Payment clause [@CL-CTF-2934].

Transportation. If the purchase price excludes transportation, the Contractor must enter the prepaid shipping cost on the invoice as a separate item and must not include the cost of parcel post insurance [@CL-CTF-2935]. If transportation charges are stated separately on the invoice, the Contractor must retain related paid freight bills or other separately paid transportation billings for 3 years and furnish them to the Government on request [@CL-CTF-2936].

Receiving report. Where the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement requires a receiving report, the Contractor must either submit it on the prescribed form with the invoice or include the listed information on the invoice [@CL-CTF-2937]. The listed information is the shipment number, the mode of shipment and, at line item level, the national stock number and/or manufacturer's part number, the unit of measure, the ship-to point, the mark-for point if in the contract and the FEDSTRIP/MILSTRIP document number if in the contract [@CL-CTF-2938]. In that list, the mark-for point and the document number apply only where the contract contains them [@CL-CTF-2938].

No receiving report on a prescribed form. Where the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement does not require a receiving report on a prescribed form, the Contractor must include the listed information on the invoice at the line item level, in addition to what paragraph (c)(1) requires [@CL-CTF-2939]. That information is the ship-to point, the mark-for point and, if in the contract, the FEDSTRIP/MILSTRIP document number [@CL-CTF-2940]. Where a receiving report is not required, the Contractor must include a copy of the invoice in each shipment [@CL-CTF-2941].

Certification. By submitting an invoice the Contractor certifies that the supplies billed have been shipped or delivered in accordance with the ordering officer's shipping instructions and in the quantities shown on the invoice [@CL-CTF-2942]. The submission also certifies that the supplies are in the quantity and of the quality the contract, order or blanket purchase agreement designates [@CL-CTF-2943].

Containers. The Contractor must mark all outer shipping containers "FAST PAY", and where a container is not so marked the payment office may still make fast payment [@CL-CTF-2944]. If the payment office declines to make fast payment, the Contractor is paid under the procedures that apply to invoices outside the Fast Payment clause [@CL-CTF-2973].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price purchase order for 400 units has a purchase price of 12,000 that excludes transportation, and the order requires no receiving report. The Contractor delivers the shipment to a common carrier and prepays 600 of shipping. The invoice shows the 12,000 price, the 600 shipping cost as a separate item, "FAST PAY" displayed prominently and, at line item level, the ship-to point and mark-for point. The order contains no FEDSTRIP/MILSTRIP document number, so none is shown. A copy of the invoice goes into the shipment and the outer containers are marked "FAST PAY". Payment is based on the delivery to the carrier. By submitting the invoice the Contractor certifies that the 400 units were shipped as instructed, in the billed quantity, and in the quantity and quality the order designates. Because the 600 is stated separately, the Contractor keeps the paid freight bill for 3 years and furnishes it to the Government on request. Had the invoice lacked the "FAST PAY" mark, the payment office could still have accepted it, and if it had declined, the invoice would be paid under the procedures for invoices outside the clause.

## Never confuse

The delivery that triggers payment is not the transfer of responsibility for the supplies, which the [title, risk of loss and replacement](../billing-payment/ctf-fast-payment-title-risk-of-loss-and-replacement.md) page covers.

A missing "FAST PAY" mark does not by itself bar fast payment, because the payment office may still accept the invoice or make fast payment [@CL-CTF-2933; @CL-CTF-2944]. If it declines, the Contractor is paid under the procedures that apply to invoices outside the clause [@CL-CTF-2934; @CL-CTF-2973].

Whether the order requires a receiving report changes what the invoice must carry [@CL-CTF-2937; @CL-CTF-2939].
