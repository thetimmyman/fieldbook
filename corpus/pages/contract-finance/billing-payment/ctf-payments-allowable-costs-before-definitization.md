## Purpose

Shows the reimbursement rates and caps for allowable costs paid on a letter contract before the definitive contract is placed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0318] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-26 [@CL-CTF-0318] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-26 [@CL-CTF-0469] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0318] |
| Applies to | Letter contracts, pending the definitive contract [@CL-CTF-0703] |

Pending the placing of the definitive contract referred to in the letter contract, the Government will promptly reimburse the Contractor for all allowable costs under the contract at the rates stated in the clause [@CL-CTF-0703]. Those rates are one hundred percent of approved costs representing financing payments to subcontractors under fixed-price subcontracts, provided that the Government's payments to the Contractor will not exceed 80 percent of the allowable costs of those subcontractors [@CL-CTF-0319]. For approved costs representing cost-reimbursement subcontracts, the rate is one hundred percent, provided that the Government's payments to the Contractor must not exceed 85 percent of the allowable costs of those subcontractors [@CL-CTF-0320]. All other approved costs are reimbursed at 85 percent [@CL-CTF-0321], and the total reimbursement made under paragraph (b) of the clause must not exceed 85 percent of the maximum amount of the Government's liability, as stated in the contract [@CL-CTF-0322].

## How it works

To determine the amounts payable to the Contractor under the letter contract, the Contracting Officer must determine allowable costs in accordance with the applicable cost principles in part 31 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CTF-0704]. Payments must be made promptly to the Contractor when requested as work progresses, in amounts approved by the Contracting Officer, but, except for small business concerns, not more often than every 2 weeks [@CL-CTF-0323]. A small business concern may receive more frequent payments than every 2 weeks [@CL-CTF-0324]. At any time before final payment, the Contracting Officer may have the Contractor's invoices or vouchers and statements of costs audited [@CL-CTF-0325]. Any payment may be reduced by any amounts found by the Contracting Officer not to constitute allowable costs or adjusted for overpayments or underpayments made on preceding invoices or vouchers [@CL-CTF-0326].

Effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762], DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760]. RFO FAR 52.216-26 provides that, pending the placing of the definitive contract referred to in the letter contract, the Government will promptly reimburse the contractor for all allowable costs under the contract at the rates listed in the clause [@CL-CTF-0469], matching FAR 52.216-26(a) [@CL-CTF-0703]. RFO FAR 52.216-26 sets those rates at one hundred percent of approved costs representing financing payments to subcontractors under fixed-price subcontracts, provided that the Government's payments to the contractor will not exceed 80 percent of the allowable costs of those subcontractors [@CL-CTF-0470], one hundred percent of approved costs representing cost-reimbursement subcontracts, provided that the Government's payments to the contractor must not exceed 85 percent of the allowable costs of those subcontractors, and 85 percent of all other approved costs [@CL-CTF-0471], matching FAR 52.216-26(a)(1) through (3) [@CL-CTF-0319; @CL-CTF-0320; @CL-CTF-0321]. RFO FAR 52.216-26 provides that, to determine the amounts payable to the contractor under the letter contract, the contracting officer must determine allowable costs in accordance with the applicable cost principles in part 31 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CTF-0472], and that the total reimbursement made under paragraph (b) of the clause must not exceed 85 percent of the maximum amount of the Government's liability, as stated in the contract [@CL-CTF-0473], matching FAR 52.216-26(b) [@CL-CTF-0704; @CL-CTF-0322]. RFO FAR 52.216-26 provides that payments must be made promptly to the contractor when requested as work progresses, in amounts approved by the contracting officer, and, except for small business concerns, not more often than every 2 weeks [@CL-CTF-0474], and that a small business concern may receive more frequent payments than every 2 weeks [@CL-CTF-0475], matching FAR 52.216-26(c) and (e) [@CL-CTF-0323; @CL-CTF-0324]. RFO FAR 52.216-26 also provides that at any time before final payment, the contracting officer may have the contractor's invoices or vouchers and statements of costs audited [@CL-CTF-0476], and that any payment may be reduced by any amounts found by the contracting officer not to constitute allowable costs or adjusted for overpayments or underpayments made on preceding invoices or vouchers [@CL-CTF-0477], matching FAR 52.216-26(f) [@CL-CTF-0325; @CL-CTF-0326].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Before definitization, a contractor incurs 1 million of approved costs, of which 500,000 is financing payments to a fixed-price subcontractor whose allowable costs are 800,000. Eighty percent of 800,000 is 640,000, so the 500,000 is within that cap and is reimbursed in full at one hundred percent. The other 500,000 is reimbursed at 85 percent, which is 425,000. The Government pays 925,000 in all, which is within the cap of 85 percent of the maximum Government liability, assumed here to be 2 million.

## Never confuse

Reimbursement under this clause is made pending the placing of the definitive contract referred to in the letter contract [@CL-CTF-0703], and to determine the amounts payable to the Contractor under the letter contract, the Contracting Officer must determine allowable costs in accordance with the applicable cost principles in FAR part 31 [@CL-CTF-0704]. Total reimbursement made under paragraph (b) of the clause must not exceed 85 percent of the maximum amount of the Government's liability, as stated in the contract [@CL-CTF-0322], and any payment may be reduced by any amounts found by the Contracting Officer not to constitute allowable costs or adjusted for overpayments or underpayments made on preceding invoices or vouchers [@CL-CTF-0326].
