## Purpose

Shows the reimbursement rates and caps for allowable costs paid on a letter contract before the definitive contract is placed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0318] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-26 [@CL-CTF-0318] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0318] |
| Applies to | Letter contracts, pending the definitive contract [@CL-CTF-0703] |

Pending the placing of the definitive contract referred to in the letter contract, the Government promptly reimburses the Contractor for all allowable costs at the rates the clause states [@CL-CTF-0703]. Those rates are one hundred percent of approved costs representing financing payments to subcontractors under fixed-price subcontracts, provided the Government's payments to the Contractor do not exceed 80 percent of the allowable costs of those subcontractors [@CL-CTF-0319]. For cost-reimbursement subcontracts the rate is one hundred percent, provided the payments do not exceed 85 percent of the allowable costs of those subcontractors [@CL-CTF-0320]. All other approved costs are reimbursed at 85 percent [@CL-CTF-0321], and total reimbursement will not exceed 85 percent of the maximum amount of the Government's liability stated in the contract [@CL-CTF-0322].

## How it works

While the letter contract awaits definitization, the Government reimburses allowable costs at the stated rates, with caps on the share of subcontractor costs it will pay [@CL-CTF-0703; @CL-CTF-0319; @CL-CTF-0320; @CL-CTF-0321]. The Contracting Officer determines allowable costs under the applicable cost principles in FAR part 31 [@CL-CTF-0704]. Payments are made promptly to the Contractor when requested as work progresses, in amounts the Contracting Officer approves, but, except for small business concerns, not more often than every two weeks [@CL-CTF-0323]. A small business concern may receive more frequent payments [@CL-CTF-0324]. Before final payment, the Contracting Officer may have the invoices, vouchers, and statements of costs audited [@CL-CTF-0325]. Any payment may be reduced by amounts found not to be allowable costs, or adjusted for overpayments or underpayments on preceding invoices or vouchers [@CL-CTF-0326].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Before definitization, a contractor incurs 1 million of approved costs, of which 500,000 is financing payments to a fixed-price subcontractor whose allowable costs are 800,000. Eighty percent of 800,000 is 640,000, so the 500,000 is within that cap and is reimbursed in full at one hundred percent. The other 500,000 is reimbursed at 85 percent, which is 425,000. The Government pays 925,000 in all, which is within the cap of 85 percent of the maximum Government liability, assumed here to be 2 million.

## Never confuse

Reimbursement under this clause is made pending the definitive contract, and it does not settle that every cost billed is allowable [@CL-CTF-0703]. Total reimbursement is capped at a share of the maximum amount of the Government's liability [@CL-CTF-0322], and any payment may still be reduced or adjusted after audit [@CL-CTF-0326].
