## Purpose

Shows who certifies a defense production loan guarantee, who prepares the certificate of eligibility, and what that certificate must state.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1423; @CL-CTF-1425] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.303 and FAR 32.304-2 [@CL-CTF-1424; @CL-CTF-1425] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.303 [@CL-CTF-4531; @CL-CTF-4528; @CL-CTF-4542]; RFO FAR 32.304, the number of FAR 32.304-2, is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4532] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1423] |
| Applies to | Loan guarantees for contract performance or other operations related to national defense under section 301 of the Defense Production Act [@CL-CTF-1423] |

Section 301 of the Defense Production Act authorizes loan guarantees for contract performance or other operations related to national defense, subject to amounts annually authorized by Congress on the maximum obligation of any guaranteeing agency under any loan, discount, advance, or commitment in connection therewith, entered into under section 301 [@CL-CTF-1423; @CL-CTF-3000]. The guaranteeing agency is responsible for certifying eligibility for the guarantee and fixing the maximum dollar amount and maturity date of the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-1424]. The amount and maturity date are fixed to meet the contractor's requirement for financing performance of the defense production contract on hand at the time the guarantee application is submitted [@CL-CTF-1461].

The contracting officer shall prepare the certificate of eligibility for a contract that the contracting officer deems to be of material consequence, when the contract financing office requests it, another interested agency requests it, or the application for a loan guarantee relates to a contract or subcontract within the cognizance of the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-1462; @CL-CTF-9595]. The certificate of eligibility shall include the determinations that the supplies or services to be acquired are essential to the national defense and that the contractor has the facilities and the technical and management ability required for contract performance [@CL-CTF-1425]. It shall also include the determination that there is no practicable alternate source for the acquisition without prejudice to the national defense, and that statement shall not be included if the contractor is a small business concern [@CL-CTF-1425; @CL-CTF-1426].

## How it works

The guaranteeing agency is responsible for certifying eligibility for the guarantee and fixing the maximum dollar amount and maturity date of the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-1424]. The contracting officer shall prepare the certificate of eligibility for a contract that the contracting officer deems to be of material consequence, when one of the following applies: the contract financing office requests it, another interested agency requests it, or the application for a loan guarantee relates to a contract or subcontract within the cognizance of the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-1462; @CL-CTF-9595]. The certificate of eligibility shall include the determinations that the supplies or services to be acquired are essential to the national defense and that the contractor has the facilities and the technical and management ability required for contract performance [@CL-CTF-1425], and the statement that there is no practicable alternate source for the acquisition without prejudice to the national defense, which shall not be included if the contractor is a small business concern [@CL-CTF-1426].

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.303(a) says that 50 USC 4531 authorizes loan guarantees for contract performance or other operations related to national defense, subject to amounts annually authorized by Congress on the maximum obligation of any guaranteeing agency under any loan, discount, advance, or commitment entered into under this authority [@CL-CTF-4531], where the codified text cites section 301 of the Defense Production Act [@CL-CTF-1423]. RFO FAR 32.303(f) says the guaranteeing agency is responsible for certifying eligibility for the guarantee and fixing the maximum dollar amount and maturity date of the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-4528], to meet the contractor’s requirement for financing performance of the defense production contract on hand at the time the guarantee application is submitted [@CL-CTF-4542], as codified FAR 32.303(g) says [@CL-CTF-1424; @CL-CTF-1461]. RFO FAR 32.304 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-CTF-4532]; the codified text prints the certificate of eligibility in FAR 32.304-2 [@CL-CTF-1462].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor with a defense production contract asks its lender for a loan of 2,000,000. The guaranteeing agency certifies eligibility and fixes a maximum amount of 1,500,000 and a maturity date. The certificate for a large business states that the supplies are essential, that the contractor has the facilities and ability, and that there is no practicable alternate source.

Answer: if the contractor were a small business concern, the alternate source statement would be left out and the other two determinations would stay. This example uses the codified text on the certificate of eligibility only, and RFO FAR 32.304 is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4532].

## Never confuse

The statement that there is no practicable alternate source for the acquisition without prejudice to the national defense shall not be included if the contractor is a small business concern [@CL-CTF-1426], while the certificate of eligibility shall include the determinations that the supplies or services to be acquired are essential to the national defense and that the contractor has the facilities and the technical and management ability required for contract performance [@CL-CTF-1425]. Loan guarantees are subject to amounts annually authorized by Congress on the maximum obligation of any guaranteeing agency [@CL-CTF-1423]. The guaranteeing agency is responsible for certifying eligibility for the guarantee [@CL-CTF-1424], and the contracting officer shall prepare the certificate of eligibility for a contract that the contracting officer deems to be of material consequence, when one of the alternatives applies [@CL-CTF-1462; @CL-CTF-9595]. For the other forms of contract financing, see [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md) and [Providing contract financing and customary contract financing](ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md).

The paragraph that makes the guaranteeing agency responsible for certifying eligibility is FAR 32.303(g) in the codified text [@CL-CTF-1424] and RFO FAR 32.303(f) in the RFO text [@CL-CTF-4528]. The codified text says the contracting officer shall prepare the certificate of eligibility for a contract that the contracting officer deems to be of material consequence, when one of the alternatives listed in FAR 32.304-2(a) applies [@CL-CTF-1462; @CL-CTF-9595], while RFO FAR 32.304 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-CTF-4532].
