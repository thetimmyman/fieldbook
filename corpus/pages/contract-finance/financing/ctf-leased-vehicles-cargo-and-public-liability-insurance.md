## Purpose

Shows who bears loss or damage on leased vehicles, the insurance minimums for leased vehicles, and the cargo and public liability insurance that a contractor keeps under the transportation clauses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2502] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.228-8, FAR 52.228-9 and FAR 52.228-10 [@CL-CTF-2502; @CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.228-8, RFO FAR 52.228-9 and RFO FAR 52.228-10 [@CL-CTF-1026; @CL-CTF-1035; @CL-CTF-1041] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.228-8: May 1999 [@CL-CTF-1026]; FAR 52.228-9: May 1999 [@CL-CTF-1035]; FAR 52.228-10: APR 1984 [@CL-CTF-9723] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2502] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the leased motor vehicles clause, the cargo insurance clause or the vehicular and general public liability insurance clause [@CL-CTF-2502; @CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505] |

The Government shall be responsible for loss of or damage to leased vehicles, except for normal wear and tear and loss or damage caused by the negligence of the Contractor, its agents, or employees [@CL-CTF-2502]. The Government shall also be responsible for loss of or damage to property of third persons, or the injury or death of third persons, if the Government is liable for such loss, damage, injury, or death under the Federal Tort Claims Act [@CL-CTF-2597]. The Contractor shall be liable for, and shall indemnify and hold harmless the Government against, all actions or claims for loss of or damage to property or the injury or death of persons, resulting from the fault, negligence, or wrongful act or omission of the Contractor, its agents, or employees [@CL-CTF-2598].

The Contractor shall provide and maintain insurance covering its liabilities under paragraph (b) in amounts of at least $200,000 per person and $500,000 per occurrence for death or bodily injury and $20,000 per occurrence for property damage or loss [@CL-CTF-2503]. Before commencing work under the contract, the Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing that the required insurance has been obtained [@CL-CTF-2599]. The policies evidencing required insurance shall contain an endorsement to the effect that any cancellation or any material change adversely affecting the interests of the Government shall not be effective for the periods that follow [@CL-CTF-2600]. The endorsement's period is the period that the laws of the State in which the contract is to be performed prescribe, or until 30 days after written notice to the Contracting Officer, whichever period is longer [@CL-CTF-2601]. The policies shall exclude any claim by the insurer for subrogation against the Government by reason of any payment under the policies [@CL-CTF-2602]. The contract price shall not include any costs for insurance or contingency to cover losses, damage, injury, or death for which the Government is responsible under paragraph (a) [@CL-CTF-2603].

The Contractor, at the Contractor's expense, shall provide and maintain, during the continuance of the contract, cargo insurance in a per-vehicle amount and a total shipment amount that are filled in for each contract [@CL-CTF-2504]. All insurance shall be written on companies acceptable to the contracting agency named in the clause, and the policies shall include such terms and conditions as that agency requires [@CL-CTF-2604]. The Contractor shall provide evidence of acceptable cargo insurance to the contracting agency named in the clause before commencing operations under the contract [@CL-CTF-2605]. Each cargo insurance policy shall include a statement that it is a condition of the policy that the Company shall furnish the contracting agency written notice 30 days in advance of the effective date of any reduction in, or cancellation of, the policy, and a second item that follows [@CL-CTF-2606]. The statement in each cargo insurance policy also requires the Company to furnish evidence of any renewal policy to the specified address not less than 15 days prior to the expiration of any current policy on file with the contracting agency [@CL-CTF-2607].

The Contractor agrees to maintain at its own expense, during the continuance of the contract, vehicular and general public liability insurance with bodily injury limits of not less than a per-person and a per-occurrence dollar amount that are filled in for each contract [@CL-CTF-2505]. The limits of liability for property damage are not less than a per-accident dollar amount and an aggregate dollar amount filled in for each contract, and the Contractor also agrees to maintain workers' compensation and other legally required insurance with respect to the Contractor's own employees and agents [@CL-CTF-2608].

## How it works

For leased vehicles, the Government shall be responsible for loss of or damage to the vehicles, except for normal wear and tear and loss or damage caused by the negligence of the Contractor, its agents, or employees [@CL-CTF-2502], and for loss of or damage to property of third persons, or the injury or death of third persons, if the Government is liable under the Federal Tort Claims Act [@CL-CTF-2597]. The Contractor shall be liable for, and shall indemnify and hold harmless the Government against, actions or claims resulting from the fault, negligence, or wrongful act or omission of the Contractor, its agents, or employees [@CL-CTF-2598]. The Contractor shall provide and maintain insurance covering its liabilities under paragraph (b) in amounts of at least the stated minimums [@CL-CTF-2503], shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing before commencing work that the required insurance has been obtained [@CL-CTF-2599], and the policies shall contain the cancellation and material change endorsement and shall exclude any insurer claim for subrogation against the Government [@CL-CTF-2600; @CL-CTF-2601; @CL-CTF-2602]. The contract price carries no cost for insurance against losses for which the Government is responsible [@CL-CTF-2603]. The cargo and public liability clauses leave the dollar amounts to be filled in, and the Contractor provides the insurance at its own expense during the continuance of the contract [@CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505]. For cargo insurance, all insurance shall be written on companies acceptable to the named agency, the Contractor shall provide evidence of acceptable cargo insurance to that agency before commencing operations, and each policy shall include the notice and renewal evidence statement [@CL-CTF-2604; @CL-CTF-2605; @CL-CTF-2606; @CL-CTF-2607].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.228-8 keeps the rule that the Government is responsible for loss of or damage to leased vehicles, except for normal wear and tear and loss or damage caused by the negligence of the Contractor, its agents, or employees [@CL-CTF-2502] [@CL-CTF-1026], and for loss or damage to property of third persons, or the injury or death of third persons, if the Government is liable for it under the Federal Tort Claims Act [@CL-CTF-2597] [@CL-CTF-1027]. It keeps the Contractor's liability for, and duty to indemnify and hold harmless the Government against, actions or claims resulting from the fault, negligence, or wrongful act or omission of the Contractor, its agents, or employees [@CL-CTF-2598] [@CL-CTF-1028], and the insurance amounts of at least $200,000 per person and $500,000 per occurrence for death or bodily injury and $20,000 per occurrence for property damage or loss [@CL-CTF-2503] [@CL-CTF-1029]. It keeps the Contractor's written notice to the Contracting Officer, before commencing work, that the required insurance has been obtained [@CL-CTF-2599] [@CL-CTF-1030]. It keeps the endorsement that any cancellation or material change adversely affecting the interests of the Government is not effective for the period the State law prescribes or until 30 days after written notice to the Contracting Officer, whichever is longer [@CL-CTF-2600; @CL-CTF-2601] [@CL-CTF-1031; @CL-CTF-1032], the exclusion of any insurer claim for subrogation against the Government [@CL-CTF-2602] [@CL-CTF-1033], and the rule that the contract price includes no costs for insurance or contingency to cover losses, damage, injury, or death for which the Government is responsible under paragraph (a) [@CL-CTF-2603] [@CL-CTF-1034].

RFO FAR 52.228-9 keeps the Contractor's cargo insurance, at its own expense and during the continuance of the contract, in a per-vehicle amount and a total shipment amount that are filled in [@CL-CTF-2504] [@CL-CTF-1035], insurance written on companies acceptable to the named contracting agency on the terms that agency requires [@CL-CTF-2604] [@CL-CTF-1036], and evidence of acceptable cargo insurance to that agency before commencing operations [@CL-CTF-2605] [@CL-CTF-1037]. It keeps the policy statement that the Company shall furnish written notice 30 days in advance of the effective date of any reduction in, or cancellation of, the policy, and evidence of any renewal policy not less than 15 days prior to the expiration of any current policy on file with the agency [@CL-CTF-2606; @CL-CTF-2607] [@CL-CTF-1038; @CL-CTF-1039; @CL-CTF-1040]. RFO FAR 52.228-10 keeps the Contractor's vehicular and general public liability insurance, maintained at its own expense during the continuance of the contract, with bodily injury limits and property damage limits that are filled in, and the workers' compensation and other legally required insurance for the Contractor's own employees and agents [@CL-CTF-2505; @CL-CTF-2608] [@CL-CTF-1041; @CL-CTF-1042; @CL-CTF-1043].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A leased vehicle is damaged by a falling tree while parked, a loss that is neither normal wear and tear nor caused by the negligence of the Contractor, its agents or employees, so the Government is responsible for it. A second leased vehicle shows only normal wear and tear, and a third is damaged by the negligence of a Contractor employee; the Government is responsible for neither. A third person's injury is one for which the Government is liable under the Federal Tort Claims Act, so the Government is also responsible for it.

The Contractor's policy has 250,000 per person and 400,000 per occurrence for bodily injury and 15,000 per occurrence for property damage. The per occurrence bodily injury figure is below 500,000 and the property damage figure is below 20,000, so the policy falls short of the stated minimums. The State endorsement period is 20 days and the notice period is 30 days after written notice, so the endorsement period is 30 days.

A cargo clause fills in 50,000 per vehicle and 200,000 for the whole shipment, and the Contractor buys that cover at its own expense from an insurer the named agency accepts. The agency requires evidence before operations begin. The policy's renewal evidence is due not less than 15 days before the current policy expires. A public liability clause fills in property damage limits of 100,000 per accident and 300,000 in the aggregate, and the Contractor also keeps workers' compensation for its own employees.

## Never confuse

Cargo insurance differs from vehicular and general public liability insurance in what each pays rather than overlapping: cargo insurance covers the value of property on a vehicle or in a shipment, while vehicular and general public liability insurance carries limits of liability for bodily injury and for property damage [@CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505; @CL-CTF-2608]. For leased vehicles, the Government shall be responsible for loss of or damage to the vehicles except as stated, and the Contractor shall provide and maintain insurance covering its liabilities under paragraph (b) of the clause [@CL-CTF-2502; @CL-CTF-2503]. Whether insurance cost is allowable is a separate question; see [Insurance and indemnification](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-insurance-and-indemnification.md).
