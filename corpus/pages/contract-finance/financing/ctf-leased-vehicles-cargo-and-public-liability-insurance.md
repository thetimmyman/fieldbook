## Purpose

Shows who bears loss or damage on leased vehicles, the insurance minimums for leased vehicles, and the cargo and public liability insurance that a contractor keeps under the transportation clauses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2502] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.228-8, FAR 52.228-9 and FAR 52.228-10 [@CL-CTF-2502; @CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2502] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the leased motor vehicles clause, the cargo insurance clause or the vehicular and general public liability insurance clause [@CL-CTF-2502; @CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505] |

The Government is responsible for loss of or damage to leased vehicles, except normal wear and tear and loss or damage caused by the negligence of the Contractor, its agents or employees [@CL-CTF-2502]. The Government shall also be responsible for loss of or damage to property of third persons, or the injury or death of third persons, if the Government is liable for such loss, damage, injury, or death under the Federal Tort Claims Act [@CL-CTF-2597]. The Contractor shall be liable for, and shall indemnify and hold harmless the Government against, all actions or claims for loss of or damage to property or the injury or death of persons, resulting from the fault, negligence, or wrongful act or omission of the Contractor, its agents, or employees [@CL-CTF-2598].

The Contractor shall provide and maintain insurance covering its liabilities under paragraph (b) in amounts of at least $200,000 per person and $500,000 per occurrence for death or bodily injury and $20,000 per occurrence for property damage or loss [@CL-CTF-2503]. Before commencing work under the contract, the Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing that the required insurance has been obtained [@CL-CTF-2599]. The policies evidencing required insurance shall contain an endorsement to the effect that any cancellation or any material change adversely affecting the interests of the Government shall not be effective for the periods that follow [@CL-CTF-2600]. The endorsement's period is the period that the laws of the State in which the contract is to be performed prescribe, or until 30 days after written notice to the Contracting Officer, whichever period is longer [@CL-CTF-2601]. The policies shall exclude any claim by the insurer for subrogation against the Government by reason of any payment under the policies [@CL-CTF-2602]. The contract price shall not include any costs for insurance or contingency to cover losses, damage, injury, or death for which the Government is responsible under paragraph (a) [@CL-CTF-2603].

The Contractor provides and maintains cargo insurance at its own expense, in a per-vehicle amount and a total shipment amount that are filled in for each contract [@CL-CTF-2504]. All insurance shall be written on companies acceptable to the contracting agency named in the clause, and the policies shall include such terms and conditions as that agency requires [@CL-CTF-2604]. The Contractor shall provide evidence of acceptable cargo insurance to the contracting agency named in the clause before commencing operations under the contract [@CL-CTF-2605]. Each cargo insurance policy shall include a statement that it is a condition of the policy that the Company shall furnish the contracting agency written notice 30 days in advance of the effective date of any reduction in, or cancellation of, the policy, and a second item that follows [@CL-CTF-2606]. The statement in each cargo insurance policy also requires the Company to furnish evidence of any renewal policy to the specified address not less than 15 days prior to the expiration of any current policy on file with the contracting agency [@CL-CTF-2607].

The Contractor agrees to maintain at its own expense, during the continuance of the contract, vehicular and general public liability insurance with bodily injury limits of not less than a per-person and a per-occurrence dollar amount that are filled in for each contract [@CL-CTF-2505]. The limits of liability for property damage are not less than a per-accident dollar amount and an aggregate dollar amount filled in for each contract, and the Contractor also agrees to maintain workers' compensation and other legally required insurance with respect to the Contractor's own employees and agents [@CL-CTF-2608].

## How it works

For leased vehicles, the Government takes the loss or damage to the vehicles unless the cause is normal wear and tear or the Contractor's negligence, and it also answers for third person loss or injury where it is liable under the Federal Tort Claims Act [@CL-CTF-2502; @CL-CTF-2597]. The Contractor indemnifies the Government for claims that result from the Contractor's fault, negligence or wrongful act or omission [@CL-CTF-2598]. The Contractor must carry at least the stated insurance minimums for those liabilities, give written notice that the insurance is in place before work starts, and have an endorsement and a no-subrogation term in the policies [@CL-CTF-2503; @CL-CTF-2599; @CL-CTF-2600; @CL-CTF-2601; @CL-CTF-2602]. The contract price carries no cost for insurance against losses for which the Government is responsible [@CL-CTF-2603]. The cargo and public liability clauses leave the dollar amounts to be filled in, and the Contractor pays for the insurance [@CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505; @CL-CTF-2608]. For cargo insurance, the insurer must be acceptable to the named agency, evidence goes to that agency before operations begin, and the policy carries the notice and renewal evidence terms [@CL-CTF-2604; @CL-CTF-2605; @CL-CTF-2606; @CL-CTF-2607].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A leased vehicle is damaged in normal wear, and the Government is responsible for it. A second leased vehicle is damaged by a driver's negligence, and the Contractor, not the Government, is responsible for that loss. A third person's injury is one for which the Government is liable under the Federal Tort Claims Act, so the Government is also responsible for it.

The Contractor's policy has 250,000 per person and 400,000 per occurrence for bodily injury and 15,000 per occurrence for property damage. The per occurrence bodily injury figure is below 500,000 and the property damage figure is below 20,000, so the policy falls short of the stated minimums. The State endorsement period is 20 days and the notice period is 30 days after written notice, so the endorsement period is 30 days.

A cargo clause fills in 50,000 per vehicle and 200,000 for the whole shipment, and the Contractor buys that cover at its own expense from an insurer the named agency accepts. The agency requires evidence before operations begin. The policy's renewal evidence is due not less than 15 days before the current policy expires. A public liability clause fills in property damage limits of 100,000 per accident and 300,000 in the aggregate, and the Contractor also keeps workers' compensation for its own employees.

## Never confuse

Cargo insurance covers the value of property on a vehicle or in a shipment, while vehicular and general public liability insurance covers the liability limits for injury [@CL-CTF-2504; @CL-CTF-2505]. For leased vehicles, the Government's responsibility for loss or damage is different from the Contractor's duty to carry insurance for its own liabilities [@CL-CTF-2502; @CL-CTF-2503]. Whether insurance cost is allowable is a separate question; see [Insurance and indemnification](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-insurance-and-indemnification.md).
