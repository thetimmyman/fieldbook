## Purpose

Shows where DoD contract financing policy authority sits, what agency heads and contracting officers must do under the prompt payment subpart, and what the annual remedy coordination report contains.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS and codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3392; @CL-CTF-3395] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.070, FAR 32.903 and FAR 32.006-5 [@CL-CTF-3392; @CL-CTF-3395; @CL-CTF-3400] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3392] |
| Applies to | DoD contract financing administration, agency prompt payment responsibilities, and remedy coordination reports [@CL-CTF-3392; @CL-CTF-3395; @CL-CTF-3400] |

The Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy, in the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment) (OUSD(A&S)DPCAP), is responsible for ensuring uniform administration of DoD contract financing [@CL-CTF-3392]. That responsibility includes DoD contract financing policies and important related procedures [@CL-CTF-3486]. Agency discretion under FAR part 32 is at the DoD level and is not delegated to the departments and agencies [@CL-CTF-3393]. Proposals by the departments and agencies to exercise it must be submitted to OUSD(A&S)DPCAP [@CL-CTF-3393].

Departments and agencies are responsible for their day-to-day contract financing operations [@CL-CTF-3394]. They must refer specific cases involving financing policy or important procedural issues to OUSD(A&S)DPCAP through the department or agency Contract Finance Committee members [@CL-CTF-3394]. For deviation request and approval procedures, the section refers the reader to subpart 201.4 [@CL-CTF-3417].

## How it works

Prompt payment subpart. Agency heads must establish the policies and procedures necessary to implement the subpart, and may prescribe additional standards for establishing invoice payment due dates necessary to support agency programs and foster prompt payment to contractors [@CL-CTF-3395]. They may adopt different payment procedures to accommodate unique circumstances, provided the procedures are consistent with the policies in the subpart [@CL-CTF-3396]. They must inform contractors of points of contact within their cognizant payment offices so that contractors can obtain the status of invoices, and may authorize the accelerated payment methods specified at 5 CFR 1315.5, with a cross-reference to FAR 32.009-1(a) [@CL-CTF-3397]. That cross-referenced paragraph lists, for accelerated payments, small business contractors and prime contractors that subcontract with a small business concern, on the conditions it sets [@CL-SUP-0707]. Which of these agency-head choices count as agency discretion that sits at the DoD level for DoD is not stated in these sections.

Contracting officers. When drafting solicitations and contracts, contracting officers must identify certain items for each line item number, subline item number, or exhibit line item number [@CL-CTF-3487]. They must identify the applicable Prompt Payment clauses that apply to each item when the solicitation or contract contains items that will be subject to different payment terms [@CL-CTF-3398]. They must also identify the applicable Prompt Payment food category when the solicitation or contract contains multiple payment terms for various classes of foods and edible products [@CL-CTF-3399].

Remedy coordination reports. The recommendations come from the fraud-based reduction or suspension procedure, which applies when an agency's remedy coordination official finds substantial evidence that a contractor's request for advance, partial, or progress payments under a contract awarded by that agency is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-1402]. The head of an agency other than the Department of Defense must prepare a report for each fiscal year in which a recommendation has been received under FAR 32.006-4(a), and reports within the Department of Defense must be prepared in accordance with the Title 10 provision that the section cites [@CL-CTF-3400]. Each report must contain each recommendation made by the remedy coordination official, the actions taken on the recommendation or recommendations with reasons for those actions, and an assessment of the effects of each action on the Government [@CL-CTF-3401].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A department wants to exercise a discretion that FAR part 32 gives to the agency. That discretion is at the DoD level and is not delegated, so the department submits its proposal to OUSD(A&S)DPCAP instead of exercising it [@CL-CTF-3393]. A department faces a specific case with an important procedural issue, and refers it to OUSD(A&S)DPCAP through its Contract Finance Committee member [@CL-CTF-3394].

A solicitation has 2 line items, one on standard payment terms and one on different payment terms. The contracting officer identifies the applicable Prompt Payment clauses for each item [@CL-CTF-3398].

An agency other than the Department of Defense received one remedy coordination recommendation in a fiscal year. Its report for that year states the recommendation, the action taken with the reasons, and an assessment of the effects of that action on the Government [@CL-CTF-3400; @CL-CTF-3401].

## Never confuse

Day-to-day financing operations belong to the departments and agencies, while financing policy and agency discretion under FAR part 32 sit at the DoD level [@CL-CTF-3393; @CL-CTF-3394]. A department's own rule cannot stand in for a DoD-level decision [@CL-CTF-3393]. The contracting officer's duty to identify Prompt Payment clauses by line item [@CL-CTF-3398] is separate from the agency head's duties to set policies and procedures [@CL-CTF-3395]. For the payment rules themselves, see [Prompt payment](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md), [Prompt payment due dates](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md) and [Financing consideration and fraud payment procedures](ctf-financing-consideration-and-fraud-payment-procedures.md).
