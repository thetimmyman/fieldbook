## Purpose

Explains that FAR 32.109 lets the contract financing procedures of FAR part 32 be applied to the financing of terminations, with or without financing for contract performance, and gives the reason the section states [@CL-CTF-3420; @CL-CTF-3421]. The section sits in the subpart for contract financing and payment on purchases other than commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CTF-3434].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3420] and RFO model text [@CL-CTF-3433] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.109 [@CL-CTF-3420] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.109 [@CL-CTF-3433]; RFO FAR 32.100 [@CL-CTF-9274] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final for the codified text [@CL-CTF-3420]; deviation text for the RFO text [@CL-CTF-3433] |
| Applies to | Financing of terminations under the contract financing procedures of FAR part 32, in the subpart for purchases other than commercial products or commercial services under part 12 [@CL-CTF-3420; @CL-CTF-3434] |

The contract financing procedures of the part may be applied to the financing of terminations, either in connection with financing for contract performance or independently of it [@CL-CTF-3420].

The section states why: to encourage contractors to invest their own funds in performance despite the susceptibility of the contract to termination for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-3421].

The sentence is permissive. It says the procedures may be applied. It names no person who decides, no threshold, no deadline and no exception, and it puts no duty on a contractor [@CL-CTF-3420].

## How it works

Financing of a termination can follow the part's contract financing procedures [@CL-CTF-3420]. It can run alongside financing for performance or on its own [@CL-CTF-3420].

The codified sentence ends with a pointer to FAR 49.112-1 [@CL-CTF-3420]. The RFO sentence has the same words but points to part 49 instead [@CL-CTF-3420; @CL-CTF-3433]. This card does not state what those termination provisions require.

From February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers use the RFO text of FAR part 32 in place of the codified text [@CL-CTF-4046]. The RFO text of the subpart scope says the subpart addresses policies and procedures applicable to contract financing and payment for any purchases other than purchases of commercial products or commercial services in accordance with part 12 [@CL-CTF-9274].

The section does not mention partial payments or subcontractors [@CL-CTF-3420]. The [subcontract termination](../billing-payment/ctf-subcontract-termination-and-direct-settlement.md) card covers terminated subcontracts.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has invested 250,000 of its own funds in performing a contract. The Government then terminates the contract for its convenience. That kind of own-funds investment is what the section aims to encourage [@CL-CTF-3421].

The section says the contract financing procedures may be applied to financing the termination, either together with financing for performance or independently of it [@CL-CTF-3420]. It does not say they must be applied, and it names no decision maker [@CL-CTF-3420]. The example therefore does not conclude that the contractor will receive any financing for the terminated work.

## Never confuse

Termination financing is not the termination settlement. This section lets the financing procedures be applied to the financing of terminations [@CL-CTF-3420]. The [termination for convenience settlement](../billing-payment/ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md) card covers the settlement.

A permission to apply the procedures is not a promise of financing, because the sentence says may [@CL-CTF-3420].

The codified and RFO sentences are not identical. They point to different termination provisions [@CL-CTF-3420; @CL-CTF-3433].

## Company calibration

Whether the company's cash forecast separates financing received during a termination from settlement amounts. [@Q-026]
