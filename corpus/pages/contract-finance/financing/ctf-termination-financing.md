## Purpose

Explains that FAR 32.109 lets the contract financing procedures of FAR part 32 be applied to the financing of terminations, with or without financing for contract performance, and gives the reason the section states [@CL-CTF-3420; @CL-CTF-3421]. The section sits in the subpart for contract financing and payment on purchases other than commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CTF-3434].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3420] and RFO model text [@CL-CTF-3433] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.109 [@CL-CTF-3420] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.109 [@CL-CTF-3433]; RFO FAR 32.100 [@CL-CTF-9274] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final for the codified text [@CL-CTF-3420]; deviation text for the RFO text [@CL-CTF-3433] |
| Applies to | Financing of terminations under the contract financing procedures of FAR part 32, in the subpart for purchases other than commercial products or commercial services under part 12 [@CL-CTF-3420; @CL-CTF-3434] |

The contract financing procedures of the part may be applied to the financing of terminations, either in connection with financing for contract performance or independently of it [@CL-CTF-3420].

The section states why: to encourage contractors to invest their own funds in performance despite the susceptibility of the contract to termination for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-3421].

## How it works

Financing of a termination can follow the part's contract financing procedures [@CL-CTF-3420]. It can run alongside financing for performance or on its own [@CL-CTF-3420]. Codified FAR 32.109 refers to FAR 49.112-1 [@CL-CTF-3420].

From February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 32.109, to encourage contractors to invest their own funds in performance despite the susceptibility of the contract to termination for the convenience of the Government, the contract financing procedures under the part may be applied to the financing of terminations either in connection with or independently of financing for contract performance (see part 49) [@CL-CTF-3433]. RFO FAR 32.100 states that the subpart addresses policies and procedures applicable to contract financing and payment for any purchases other than purchases of commercial products or commercial services in accordance with part 12 [@CL-CTF-9274].

The [subcontract termination](../billing-payment/ctf-subcontract-termination-and-direct-settlement.md) card covers terminated subcontracts.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has invested 250,000 of its own funds in performing a contract. The Government then terminates the contract for its convenience. That kind of own-funds investment is what the section aims to encourage [@CL-CTF-3421].

Under FAR 32.109, the contract financing procedures of FAR part 32 may be applied to the financing of the termination, either in connection with or independently of financing for contract performance [@CL-CTF-3420].

## Never confuse

Termination financing can run alongside performance financing rather than instead of it: FAR 32.109 states that the contract financing procedures under FAR part 32 may be applied to the financing of terminations either in connection with or independently of financing for contract performance [@CL-CTF-3420]. The codified text refers to FAR 49.112-1, unlike RFO FAR 32.109, which instead refers to part 49 [@CL-CTF-3420; @CL-CTF-3433]. The [termination for convenience settlement](../billing-payment/ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md) card covers termination for convenience settlements.

## Company calibration

Whether the company's cash forecast separates financing received during a termination from settlement amounts. [@Q-026]
