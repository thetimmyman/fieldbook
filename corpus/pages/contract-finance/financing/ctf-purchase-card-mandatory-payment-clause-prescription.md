## Purpose

Shows when the Defense supplement requires the mandatory purchase card payment clause in a solicitation, contract or agreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3840] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.1110 [@CL-CTF-3840] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3840] |
| Applies to | Solicitations, contracts and agreements where orders or calls at or below the micro-purchase threshold are anticipated and card payment is required for them [@CL-CTF-3841] |

The section directs use of the clause at 252.232-7009, Mandatory Payment by Governmentwide Commercial Purchase Card, in solicitations, contracts and agreements, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services, when the conditions listed after it are met [@CL-CTF-3840].

The two listed conditions are joined by "and": placement of orders or calls valued at or below the micro-purchase threshold is anticipated, and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card is required for orders or calls valued at or below the micro-purchase threshold under the contract or agreement [@CL-CTF-3841].

## How it works

Because the two conditions are joined by "and", the section calls for the clause at 252.232-7009 when both are met [@CL-CTF-3840; @CL-CTF-3841]. The clause itself is covered on [Mandatory purchase card payment and the advance payment pool clause](../billing-payment/ctf-dfars-purchase-card-and-advance-payment-pool.md).

The commercial acquisition procedures of FAR part 12 do not take a solicitation, contract or agreement out of the prescription, because the section names them expressly [@CL-CTF-3840]. The FAR definition of the micro-purchase threshold is on [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md). The FAR rules on when payment is made by card are on [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md), and the other payment clause prescriptions are on [EFT solicitation provision and contract clauses](ctf-eft-solicitation-provision-and-clauses.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The applicable micro-purchase threshold is taken to be 10,000. An agreement is expected to produce orders of about 4,000 each, and it requires card payment for them. Both conditions are met, so the section calls for the clause at 252.232-7009 in the agreement [@CL-CTF-3841]. If the same agreement were a commercial acquisition under FAR part 12 procedures, the result would be the same [@CL-CTF-3840]. A second contract is expected to produce only orders above the threshold, so the first condition is not met and this section does not prescribe the clause [@CL-CTF-3841]. A third contract is expected to produce small orders but does not require card payment for them, so the second condition is not met and this section does not prescribe the clause [@CL-CTF-3841].

## Never confuse

The prescription of the mandatory card payment clause is a different rule from the prohibition on card payment where the foreign procurement tax clause applies, which is on [Prohibition on purchase card payment when the foreign procurement tax clause applies](ctf-purchase-card-foreign-tax-prohibition.md) [@CL-CTF-3838]. DFARS 232.1110 directs use of the clause at 252.232-7009 when both conditions on orders or calls at or below the micro-purchase threshold are met [@CL-CTF-3840; @CL-CTF-3841], whereas DFARS 232.1108-70 bars the contracting officer from authorizing the card as a method of payment during any contract period of performance if the contract includes FAR 52.229-12 without 252.229-7014 [@CL-CTF-3838; @CL-CTF-3839].

## Company calibration

Whether each solicitation, contract and agreement expected to produce orders at or below the micro-purchase threshold requires card payment [@Q-004; @Q-026].
