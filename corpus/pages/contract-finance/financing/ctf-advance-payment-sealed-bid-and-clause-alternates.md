## Purpose

Explains when advance payments may be offered on sealed bid contracts under the national defense authority, how a bidder request is handled, and which alternate of the advance payments clause matches which terms.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3087] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.405 and FAR 32.412 [@CL-CTF-3087; @CL-CTF-3091] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3087] |
| Applies to | Sealed bid contracts, and solicitations and contracts under which the Government will provide advance payments [@CL-CTF-3087; @CL-CTF-3091] |

Actions that designated agencies may take to facilitate the national defense without regard to other provisions of law relating to contracts, as explained in FAR 50.101-1(a), also include making advance payments [@CL-CTF-3184]. These advance payments may be made at or after award of sealed bid contracts as well as negotiated contracts [@CL-CTF-3087]. Bidders may request advance payments before or after award, even if the invitation for bids does not contain an advance payment provision [@CL-CTF-3088]. The contracting officer rejects any bid requiring that advance payments be provided as a basis for acceptance [@CL-CTF-3089].

When advance payments are requested, the agency may enter into the contract and provide for conforming advance payments, or enter into the contract without them if the contractor does not actually need them [@CL-CTF-3090]. The agency may deny award of the contract if the request has been disapproved under FAR 32.409-2 and funds adequate for performance are not otherwise available to the offeror [@CL-CTF-3185].

The contracting officer inserts the clause at FAR 52.232-12, Advance Payments, in solicitations and contracts under which the Government will provide advance payments, except as provided in FAR 32.412(b) [@CL-CTF-3091]. The officer uses the clause with Alternate I if the agency desires to waive the countersignature requirement because of the contractor's financial strength, good performance record and favorable experience concerning cost disallowances [@CL-CTF-3186]. The officer uses Alternate II if a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-3187], Alternate III if the agency considers a more rapid liquidation appropriate [@CL-CTF-3188], and Alternate IV if the agency provides advance payments under the contract at no interest to the prime contractor [@CL-CTF-3189]. If the requirement for a special account is eliminated in accordance with FAR 32.409-3(e) or (g), the officer inserts the Alternate V version of the clause in the solicitation or contract instead of the basic clause [@CL-CTF-3092].

## How it works

A sealed bid contract can carry advance payments under the national defense authority, but a bid cannot make advance payments a condition of acceptance [@CL-CTF-3184; @CL-CTF-3087; @CL-CTF-3089]. A bidder may ask before or after award even when the invitation is silent [@CL-CTF-3088]. On a request, the agency chooses between awarding with conforming advance payments, awarding without them where they are not actually needed, and, in the stated case, denying award [@CL-CTF-3090; @CL-CTF-3185]. The contracting officer then uses the basic clause or the alternate that matches the terms [@CL-CTF-3091; @CL-CTF-3186; @CL-CTF-3092].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An invitation for bids says nothing about advance payments. One bidder asks for an advance of 200,000 before performance starts, and the bidder may ask even though the invitation is silent [@CL-CTF-3088]. The agency may award with conforming advance payments, or award without them if the bidder does not actually need them [@CL-CTF-3090]. If the request was disapproved under FAR 32.409-2 and adequate funds are not otherwise available to the bidder, the agency may deny award [@CL-CTF-3185]. A second bid states that it can be accepted only if advance payments are provided, and the contracting officer rejects it [@CL-CTF-3089]. For the awarded contract, the agency considers a more rapid liquidation appropriate, so the officer uses the Advance Payments clause with Alternate III [@CL-CTF-3188].

## Never confuse

An advance payment is not a progress payment based on costs. These rules concern advance payments, which the Government provides under the Advance Payments clause [@CL-CTF-3091]. The alternates adjust the terms of the clause, namely the countersignature, the contract type, the liquidation speed, interest and the special account [@CL-CTF-3186; @CL-CTF-3187; @CL-CTF-3188; @CL-CTF-3189; @CL-CTF-3092]. The [advance payments clause](ctf-advance-payments-clause.md) card covers the clause itself.
