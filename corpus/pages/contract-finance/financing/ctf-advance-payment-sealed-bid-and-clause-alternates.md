## Purpose

Explains when advance payments may be offered on sealed bid contracts under the national defense authority, how a bidder request is handled, and which alternate of the advance payments clause matches which terms.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3087] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.405 and FAR 32.412 [@CL-CTF-3087; @CL-CTF-3091] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.405 [@CL-CTF-4178; @CL-CTF-4182] and RFO FAR 32.412 [@CL-CTF-4184] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3087] |
| Applies to | Sealed bid contracts, and solicitations and contracts under which the Government will provide advance payments [@CL-CTF-3087; @CL-CTF-3091] |

Actions that designated agencies may take to facilitate the national defense without regard to other provisions of law relating to contracts, as explained in FAR 50.101-1(a), also include making advance payments [@CL-CTF-3184]. These advance payments may be made at or after award of sealed bid contracts as well as negotiated contracts [@CL-CTF-3087]. Bidders may request advance payments before or after award, even if the invitation for bids does not contain an advance payment provision [@CL-CTF-3088]. However, the contracting officer shall reject any bid requiring that advance payments be provided as a basis for acceptance [@CL-CTF-3089].

When advance payments are requested, the agency may enter into the contract and provide for conforming advance payments, or enter into the contract without them if the contractor does not actually need them [@CL-CTF-3090]. The agency may deny award of the contract if the request has been disapproved under FAR 32.409-2 and funds adequate for performance are not otherwise available to the offeror [@CL-CTF-3185].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-12, Advance Payments, in solicitations and contracts under which the Government will provide advance payments, except as provided in FAR 32.412(b) [@CL-CTF-3091]. The officer shall use the clause with Alternate I if the agency desires to waive the countersignature requirement because of the contractor's financial strength, good performance record and favorable experience concerning cost disallowances [@CL-CTF-3186]. The officer shall use Alternate II if a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-3187], Alternate III if the agency considers a more rapid liquidation appropriate [@CL-CTF-3188], and Alternate IV if the agency provides advance payments under the contract at no interest to the prime contractor [@CL-CTF-3189]. If the requirement for a special account is eliminated in accordance with FAR 32.409-3(e) or (g), the officer shall insert in the solicitation or contract the clause set forth in Alternate V of FAR 52.232-12 instead of the basic clause [@CL-CTF-3092].

## How it works

A sealed bid contract can carry advance payments under the national defense authority [@CL-CTF-3184; @CL-CTF-3087], but the contracting officer shall reject any bid requiring that advance payments be provided as a basis for acceptance [@CL-CTF-3089]. A bidder may ask before or after award even when the invitation is silent [@CL-CTF-3088]. On a request, the agency may enter into the contract with conforming advance payments, enter into it without them if the contractor does not actually need them, or, in the stated case, deny award [@CL-CTF-3090; @CL-CTF-3185]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-12 in solicitations and contracts under which the Government will provide advance payments [@CL-CTF-3091], and shall use the alternates in the circumstances FAR 32.412(b) through (f) describe [@CL-CTF-3186; @CL-CTF-3187; @CL-CTF-3188; @CL-CTF-3189; @CL-CTF-3092].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.405 keeps the rules described above: the actions that designated agencies may take to facilitate the national defense without regard to other provisions of law relating to contracts, as explained in 50.101-1(a), also include making advance payments [@CL-CTF-4177]; advance payments may be made at or after award of sealed bid contracts, as well as negotiated contracts [@CL-CTF-4178]; bidders may request advance payments before or after award [@CL-CTF-4179]; and the contracting officer must reject any bid requiring that advance payments be provided as a basis for acceptance [@CL-CTF-4180]. When advance payments are requested, the agency may enter into the contract and provide for advance payments conforming to part 32, or enter into the contract without advance payments if the contractor does not actually need them [@CL-CTF-4181]. RFO FAR 32.412 keeps the same clause and alternates: the clause at 52.232-12 is inserted in solicitations and contracts under which the Government will provide advance payments, except as provided in 32.412(b) [@CL-CTF-4184]; the contracting officer must use Alternate I if the agency desires to waive the countersignature requirement because of the contractor’s financial strength, good performance record, and favorable experience concerning cost disallowances [@CL-CTF-4185], Alternate II if a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-CTF-4186], Alternate III if the agency considers a more rapid liquidation appropriate [@CL-CTF-4187], and Alternate IV if the agency provides advance payments under the contract at no interest to the prime contractor [@CL-CTF-4188]; and the contracting officer must insert Alternate V instead of the basic clause if the requirement for a special account is eliminated in accordance with 32.409-3(e) or (g) [@CL-CTF-4189]. One difference: RFO FAR 32.405(c)(3) lets the agency deny award if the request has been disapproved under 32.408-2 and funds adequate for performance are not otherwise available to the offeror [@CL-CTF-4182], where the codified paragraph names 32.409-2 [@CL-CTF-3185], and the RFO section titled Recommendation for disapproval is numbered 32.409-2 [@CL-CTF-4183].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An invitation for bids says nothing about advance payments. One bidder asks for an advance of 200,000 before performance starts, and the bidder may ask even though the invitation is silent [@CL-CTF-3088]. The agency may award with conforming advance payments, or award without them if the bidder does not actually need them [@CL-CTF-3090]. If the request was disapproved under FAR 32.409-2 and adequate funds are not otherwise available to the bidder, the agency may deny award [@CL-CTF-3185]; under the RFO text the paragraph names 32.408-2 instead, with the same condition on adequate funds [@CL-CTF-4182]. A second bid states that it can be accepted only if advance payments are provided, and the contracting officer rejects it [@CL-CTF-3089]. For the awarded contract, the agency considers a more rapid liquidation appropriate, so the officer uses the Advance Payments clause with Alternate III [@CL-CTF-3188].

## Never confuse

The clause at FAR 52.232-12, Advance Payments, is inserted in solicitations and contracts under which the Government will provide advance payments [@CL-CTF-3091]. The alternates apply to different circumstances: a waiver of the countersignature requirement, a contemplated cost-reimbursement contract, a more rapid liquidation, advance payments at no interest to the prime contractor, and elimination of the special account [@CL-CTF-3186; @CL-CTF-3187; @CL-CTF-3188; @CL-CTF-3189; @CL-CTF-3092]. The [advance payments clause](ctf-advance-payments-clause.md) card covers the clause itself. The codified FAR 32.405(c)(3) points to FAR 32.409-2 for the disapproved request [@CL-CTF-3185], while RFO FAR 32.405(c)(3) points to 32.408-2 [@CL-CTF-4182].
