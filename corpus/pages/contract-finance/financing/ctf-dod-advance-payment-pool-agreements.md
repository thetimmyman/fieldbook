## Purpose

Shows what an advance payment pool agreement is and when it is convenient.

An advance payment pool agreement is a means of financing the performance of more than one contract held by a single contractor [@CL-CTF-0948]. It is especially convenient for financing cost-type contracts with nonprofit educational or research institutions for experimental or research and development work when several contracts require advance payment financing [@CL-CTF-0949].

## How it works

An advance payment pool agreement is a means of financing the performance of more than one contract held by a single contractor [@CL-CTF-0948]. It is especially convenient for financing cost-type contracts with nonprofit educational or research institutions for experimental or research and development work when several contracts require financing by advance payments [@CL-CTF-0949], and when appropriate, pooled advance payments may also be used to finance other types of contracts held by a single contractor [@CL-CTF-1017]. A pool agreement may be established in one of several alternative ways: without regard to the number of appropriations involved, or to finance contracts for one or more departments or contracting activities [@CL-CTF-1015]. A further alternative is to establish it in addition to any other advance payment pool agreement at a single contractor location when it is more convenient or otherwise preferable to have more than one agreement [@CL-CTF-1016].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A nonprofit research institute holds three cost-type research contracts with the same agency and all three need advance payments. The three contracts can be financed together under one pool agreement.

## Never confuse

An advance payment pool agreement is a means of financing the performance of more than one contract held by a single contractor [@CL-CTF-0948]. When appropriate, pooled advance payments may also be used to finance other types of contracts held by a single contractor [@CL-CTF-1017]. For the general advance payment rules, see [Advance payments for non-commercial items](ctf-advance-payments-for-non-commercial-items.md) and [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md).
