## Purpose

Shows the form and amount of a bid guarantee, when it is returned or used, when a contractor must add bond security, and what a supplier may ask for about the payment bond.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2471] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.228-1, FAR 52.228-2 and FAR 52.228-12 [@CL-CTF-2471; @CL-CTF-2476; @CL-CTF-2479] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.228-1(a) [@CL-CTF-0839]; RFO FAR 52.228-1(b) [@CL-CTF-0840; @CL-CTF-0841]; RFO FAR 52.228-1(c) [@CL-CTF-0842]; RFO FAR 52.228-1(d) [@CL-CTF-0843]; RFO FAR 52.228-1(e) [@CL-CTF-0844]; RFO FAR 52.228-2 [@CL-CTF-0845; @CL-CTF-0846; @CL-CTF-0847; @CL-CTF-0848]; RFO FAR 52.228-12 [@CL-CTF-0849; @CL-CTF-0883] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2471] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that contain the bid guarantee provision, the additional bond security clause or the prospective subcontractor request clause [@CL-CTF-2471; @CL-CTF-2476; @CL-CTF-2479] |

Failure to furnish a bid guarantee in the proper form and amount, by the time set for opening of bids, may be cause for rejection of the bid [@CL-CTF-2506]. The bidder shall furnish a bid guarantee in the form of a firm commitment, for example a bid bond supported by good and sufficient surety or sureties acceptable to the Government, a postal money order, a certified check, a cashier's check, an irrevocable letter of credit, or, under Treasury Department regulations, certain bonds or notes of the United States [@CL-CTF-2914; @CL-CTF-2471]. The amount of the bid guarantee shall be the stated percent of the bid price or the stated dollar amount, whichever is less [@CL-CTF-2473].

The Contracting Officer will return guarantees other than bid bonds to unsuccessful bidders as soon as practicable after bids are opened, and to the successful bidder upon execution of contractual documents and bonds, including any necessary coinsurance or reinsurance agreements [@CL-CTF-2472]. The successful bidder's guarantee, other than a bid bond, is returned upon execution of contractual documents and bonds, including any necessary coinsurance or reinsurance agreements, as required by the bid as accepted [@CL-CTF-2507].

If the successful bidder, upon acceptance of its bid by the Government within the period specified for acceptance, fails to execute all contractual documents or furnish executed bonds within 10 days after receipt of the forms by the bidder, the Contracting Officer may terminate the contract for default [@CL-CTF-2474]. In the event the contract is terminated for default, the bidder is liable for any cost of acquiring the work that exceeds the amount of its bid, and the bid guarantee is available to offset the difference [@CL-CTF-2475].

Under the additional bond security clause, the Contractor shall promptly furnish additional security required to protect the Government and persons supplying labor or materials under the contract if any surety upon any bond, or issuing financial institution for other security, furnished with the contract becomes unacceptable to the Government [@CL-CTF-2476]. Any one of the other events listed in the clause also triggers that duty: a surety failing to furnish financial condition reports as the Government requires, or a price increase that makes the penal sum of any bond inadequate in the opinion of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2477]. A further alternative event is that a letter of credit used as security will expire before the end of the period of required security [@CL-CTF-2508]. In that case, if the Contractor does not furnish an acceptable extension or replacement letter of credit, or other acceptable substitute, at least 30 days before its scheduled expiration, the Contracting Officer has the right to immediately draw on it [@CL-CTF-2478].

For a contract for which a payment bond has been furnished to the Government under chapter 31, subchapter III, of title 40, upon the request of a prospective subcontractor or supplier offering to furnish labor or material for the performance of the contract, the Contractor shall promptly provide a copy of the payment bond to the requester [@CL-CTF-2479; @CL-CTF-2741].

## How it works

Failure to furnish a bid guarantee in the proper form and amount, by the time set for opening of bids, may be cause for rejection of the bid [@CL-CTF-2506]. The Contracting Officer will return bid guarantees, other than bid bonds, to unsuccessful bidders as soon as practicable after the opening of bids, and to the successful bidder upon execution of contractual documents and bonds [@CL-CTF-2472]. If the successful bidder fails to execute all contractual documents or furnish executed bonds within 10 days after receipt of the forms by the bidder, the Contracting Officer may terminate the contract for default [@CL-CTF-2474], and in the event of a termination for default the bid guarantee is available to offset any cost of acquiring the work that exceeds the amount of the bid [@CL-CTF-2475]. Under the additional bond security clause, any one of the listed events requires the Contractor to promptly furnish additional security [@CL-CTF-2476; @CL-CTF-2477; @CL-CTF-2508], and if the Contractor does not furnish an acceptable extension or replacement letter of credit, or other acceptable substitute, at least 30 days before its scheduled expiration, the Contracting Officer has the right to immediately draw on it [@CL-CTF-2478]. Upon the request of a prospective subcontractor or supplier offering to furnish labor or material for the performance of a contract for which a payment bond has been furnished to the Government under chapter 31, subchapter III, of title 40, the Contractor shall promptly provide a copy of the payment bond to the requester [@CL-CTF-2479; @CL-CTF-2741].

The RFO text applies to DoD contracting because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.228-1 keeps the rejection rule in paragraph (a) [@CL-CTF-0839; @CL-CTF-2506], the forms of bid guarantee and the return rules in paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-0840; @CL-CTF-0841; @CL-CTF-2471; @CL-CTF-2472; @CL-CTF-2507], the amount in paragraph (c) [@CL-CTF-0842; @CL-CTF-2473], the 10-day default termination rule in paragraph (d) [@CL-CTF-0843; @CL-CTF-2474], and the liability and offset rule in paragraph (e) [@CL-CTF-0844; @CL-CTF-2475]. RFO FAR 52.228-2 keeps the events that require additional security and the right to immediately draw on a letter of credit if the Contractor does not furnish an acceptable extension or replacement, or other acceptable substitute, at least 30 days before its scheduled expiration [@CL-CTF-0845; @CL-CTF-0846; @CL-CTF-0847; @CL-CTF-0848; @CL-CTF-2476; @CL-CTF-2477; @CL-CTF-2508; @CL-CTF-2478]. RFO FAR 52.228-12 keeps the duty that, for a contract for which a payment bond has been furnished to the Government under chapter 31, subchapter III, of title 40, the Contractor promptly provides a copy of the payment bond to a prospective subcontractor or supplier that offers to furnish labor or material for the performance of the contract and asks for it [@CL-CTF-0849; @CL-CTF-0883; @CL-CTF-2741; @CL-CTF-2479].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The solicitation fills in 10 percent of the bid price or 150,000, whichever is less. A bidder bids 2,000,000, so the guarantee is 150,000, the lesser of 200,000 and 150,000. A second bidder's check reaches the Contracting Officer after the time set for opening of bids, so its bid may be rejected. The first bidder posts a certified check. Unsuccessful bidders get their checks back as soon as practicable after bid opening. The first bidder wins, and its check goes back once it executes the contractual documents and bonds as the bid as accepted requires.

Suppose instead that the winner does not execute the documents or furnish executed bonds within 10 days after receiving the forms, and the Contracting Officer terminates for default. Another source completes the work for 2,300,000, which is 300,000 above the bid. The bidder is liable for that 300,000, and the 150,000 check is available to offset it, leaving 150,000.

After award on a different contract, a letter of credit held as security will expire in 100 days and no extension or replacement arrives by day 70, which is 30 days before expiry. The Contracting Officer may draw on it immediately.

On a contract for which a payment bond has been furnished to the Government under chapter 31, subchapter III, of title 40, a prospective subcontractor offering to furnish labor asks the Contractor for a copy of the payment bond, and the Contractor promptly provides it.

## Never confuse

The bidder shall furnish a bid guarantee in the form of a firm commitment [@CL-CTF-2914; @CL-CTF-2471], and the additional security required under FAR 52.228-2 is security required to protect the Government and persons supplying labor or materials under the contract [@CL-CTF-2476]. For surety takeover after a default termination, see [Surety-takeover agreements after a default termination](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-surety-takeover-agreements.md). The default clauses are on [Default clauses for fixed-price contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-default-clauses.md).
