## Purpose

Fixes the meaning of electronic form, payment request and receiving report for the Defense subpart on electronic submission and processing of payment requests and receiving reports.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3842] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.7000 and DFARS 232.7001 [@CL-CTF-3752; @CL-CTF-3842]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CTF-9422] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3842] |
| Applies to | The terms used in the subpart on electronic submission and processing of payment requests and receiving reports [@CL-CTF-3842; @CL-CTF-3843; @CL-CTF-3844] |

The subpart prescribes policies and procedures for submitting and processing payment requests in electronic form to comply with section 4601 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CTF-3752].

The definitions apply as used in the subpart on electronic submission and processing of payment requests and receiving reports [@CL-CTF-9636]. An electronic form is any automated system that transmits information electronically from the initiating system to affected systems [@CL-CTF-3842].

A payment request is any request for contract financing payment or invoice payment submitted by the contractor under a contract or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-3843]. A receiving report is the data prepared in the manner and to the extent required by appendix F of this chapter, Material Inspection and Receiving Report [@CL-CTF-3844]. FAR 2.101 defines a receiving report as written evidence that indicates Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed (see subpart 46.6), and says receiving reports must meet the requirements of 32.905(c) [@CL-CTF-9422]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines a receiving report as written evidence that indicates Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed (see subpart 46.6), and says receiving reports must meet the requirements of 32.905(c) [@CL-CTF-9453].

## How it works

For the operative rules that use the terms, see [DoD payment applicability and electronic submission](../billing-payment/ctf-dod-payment-applicability-and-electronic-submission.md) and [DoD clause on electronic submission of payment requests and receiving reports](ctf-dod-electronic-payment-request-clause.md).

A payment request covers both a request for contract financing payment and a request for invoice payment [@CL-CTF-3843]. The definition covers a request submitted by the contractor under a contract or under a task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-3843]. A receiving report is the data prepared in the manner and to the extent required by appendix F of this chapter [@CL-CTF-3844]. An electronic form is any automated system that transmits information electronically from the initiating system to affected systems [@CL-CTF-3842]. For which electronic form is acceptable, see [Electronic submission of payment requests: Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](../billing-payment/ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor sends a request for an invoice payment of 900,000 under a task order through an automated system that passes the data from its own system to the affected payment systems. The request is a payment request, because it is a request for invoice payment submitted by the contractor under a task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-3843]. The system is an electronic form under the definition, because it transmits information electronically from the initiating system to affected systems [@CL-CTF-3842]. The matching receiving report is the data prepared as appendix F of the chapter requires [@CL-CTF-3844]. Whether that system is an acceptable electronic form under the submission rules is not answered by these definitions.

## Never confuse

A payment request differs from a receiving report in kind rather than in name: a payment request is any request for contract financing payment or invoice payment submitted by the contractor under a contract or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-3843], while a receiving report is not a request but the data prepared in the manner and to the extent required by appendix F of this chapter [@CL-CTF-3844].

## Company calibration

Whether the company's systems for submitting payment requests and receiving reports meet the defined electronic form and the appendix requirements [@Q-009].
