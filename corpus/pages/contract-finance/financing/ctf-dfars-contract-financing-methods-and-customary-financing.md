## Purpose

Explains which contracts may receive progress payments measured by percentage or stage of completion, that performance-based payments may still use that measure, and DoD's determination that customary contract financing needs no further justification for covered fixed-price contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3788] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.102(e)(2) and DFARS 232.104 [@CL-CTF-3788; @CL-CTF-3790] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3790] |
| Applies to | Progress payments measured by percentage or stage of completion; fixed-price contracts with a period of performance over a year that meet the FAR dollar thresholds, and solicitations expected to result in them [@CL-CTF-3788; @CL-CTF-3790] |

Progress payments based on percentage or stage of completion are authorized only for contracts for construction as defined in FAR 36.102, shipbuilding, and ship conversion, alteration, or repair [@CL-CTF-3788]. Percentage or stage of completion methods of measuring contractor performance may be used for performance-based payments in accordance with FAR Subpart 32.10 [@CL-CTF-3789].

For fixed-price contracts with a period of performance in excess of a year that meet the dollar thresholds established in FAR 32.104(d), and for solicitations expected to result in such contracts, DoD has determined that the use of customary contract financing described at FAR 32.113, other than loan guarantees and advance payments, is in DoD's best interest [@CL-CTF-3790; @CL-CTF-3791]. DoD has also determined that further justification of its use in individual acquisitions is unnecessary [@CL-CTF-3886]. The determination is made in lieu of the requirement at FAR 32.104(d)(1)(ii) for the contractor to demonstrate actual financial need or the unavailability of private financing [@CL-CTF-3792].

## How it works

Progress payments based on percentage or stage of completion are authorized only for contracts for construction (as defined in FAR 36.102), shipbuilding, and ship conversion, alteration, or repair [@CL-CTF-3788]. Progress payments based on costs are covered on [Progress payments based on costs](ctf-progress-payments-based-on-costs.md).

However, percentage or stage of completion methods of measuring contractor performance may be used for performance-based payments in accordance with FAR Subpart 32.10 [@CL-CTF-3789]. See [Performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).

For fixed-price contracts with a period of performance in excess of a year that meet the dollar thresholds established in FAR 32.104(d), and for solicitations expected to result in such contracts [@CL-CTF-3790], in lieu of the requirement at FAR 32.104(d)(1)(ii) for the contractor to demonstrate actual financial need or the unavailability of private financing, DoD has determined that the use of customary contract financing, other than loan guarantees and advance payments, is in DoD's best interest [@CL-CTF-3792; @CL-CTF-3791], and that further justification of its use in individual acquisitions is unnecessary [@CL-CTF-3886].

The remaining conditions for providing financing are on [Providing contract financing and customary contract financing](ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor that is not a small business concern holds a fixed-price contract to repair a ship, with a 24-month period of performance and a price of 8,000,000. That price is above the 3,500,000 alternative for a contractor that is not a small business concern [@CL-CTF-1456]. Assume each contract in this example meets the other dollar thresholds in FAR 32.104(d). Ship repair is within the listed work, so this limit does not bar progress payments measured by stage of completion for it [@CL-CTF-3788].

A second contract is also fixed-price, with the same period and price, but covers supplies. It is neither construction nor ship work, so progress payments measured by percentage of completion are not authorized for it [@CL-CTF-3788]. Percentage of completion may still measure performance for performance-based payments under FAR Subpart 32.10 [@CL-CTF-3789].

For both contracts, DoD has determined that customary contract financing other than loan guarantees and advance payments is in its best interest [@CL-CTF-3791]. The contractor need not demonstrate financial need or the unavailability of private financing [@CL-CTF-3792], and the file needs no further justification of that financing in the individual acquisition [@CL-CTF-3886]. A request for a loan guarantee or an advance payment on either contract is outside that determination [@CL-CTF-3791].

## Never confuse

Progress payments based on percentage or stage of completion are authorized only for contracts for construction (as defined in FAR 36.102), shipbuilding, and ship conversion, alteration, or repair [@CL-CTF-3788], unlike the same percentage or stage of completion methods of measuring contractor performance, which may be used for performance-based payments in accordance with FAR Subpart 32.10 rather than being limited to that work [@CL-CTF-3789].

DoD has determined that the use of customary contract financing, other than loan guarantees and advance payments, is in DoD's best interest [@CL-CTF-3791], and that further justification of its use in individual acquisitions is unnecessary [@CL-CTF-3886] rather than a demonstration of actual financial need or the unavailability of private financing [@CL-CTF-3792].
