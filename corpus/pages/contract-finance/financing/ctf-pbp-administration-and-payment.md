## Purpose

Outlines the contracting officer's responsibilities for reviewing, approving and transmitting performance-based payments.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0973] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1007 [@CL-CTF-0973] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1007 [@CL-CTF-4365; @CL-CTF-4366; @CL-CTF-4367; @CL-CTF-4368; @CL-CTF-4369; @CL-CTF-4370; @CL-CTF-4371; @CL-CTF-4372] and RFO FAR 32.1008 [@CL-CTF-4373] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0973] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments for the contract that the contracting officer administers [@CL-CTF-0973] |

The contracting officer responsible for administering performance-based payments for the contract shall review and approve all performance-based payments for that contract [@CL-CTF-0973]. Unless otherwise provided in agency regulations or by agreement with the appropriate payment official, the contracting officer is responsible for receiving, approving and transmitting all performance-based payment requests to the appropriate payment office [@CL-CTF-1079].

The contracting officer shall not approve a performance-based payment until the specified event or performance criterion has been successfully accomplished in accordance with the contract [@CL-CTF-0974]. If an event is cumulative, the contracting officer shall not approve the payment unless all identified preceding events or criteria are accomplished [@CL-CTF-0975].

## How it works

Entitlement to a performance-based payment is solely on the basis of successful performance of the specified events or performance criteria [@CL-CTF-0976]. If there is a Government-caused delay, the contracting officer may renegotiate the performance-based payment schedule to facilitate contractor billings for any successfully accomplished portions of the delayed event or criterion [@CL-CTF-1084].

The contracting officer is responsible for determining what reviews are required for protection of the Government's interests [@CL-CTF-1080], and should consider the contractor's experience, performance record, reliability, financial strength and the adequacy of controls established by the contractor for administering the payments [@CL-CTF-1081]. Based upon the risk to the Government, post-payment reviews and verifications should normally be arranged as the contracting officer considers appropriate [@CL-CTF-1082], and pre-payment reviews may be required if the contracting officer considers them necessary [@CL-CTF-1083].

When exercising the Government's rights to suspend or reduce performance-based payments, the contracting officer shall apply the policy and procedures in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), and (e) of section 32.503-6 [@CL-CTF-0977]. Those rights are exercised in accordance with paragraph (e) of the Performance-Based Payments clause [@CL-CTF-1085]. The remedies are on [Suspension or reduction of progress payments](ctf-suspension-or-reduction-of-progress-payments.md), the clause-side payment conditions are on [Performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payments.md), and the event criteria are on [PBP criteria](ctf-pbp-criteria.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO section 32.1007 keeps the contracting officer's duty to review and approve all performance-based payments for the contract [@CL-CTF-4365] and to receive, approve and transmit the requests unless agency regulations or an agreement with the payment official provide otherwise [@CL-CTF-4366]. It keeps the responsibility for determining what reviews are required for protection of the Government's interests [@CL-CTF-4367], and the rules that, based upon the risk to the Government, post-payment reviews and verifications should normally be arranged as the contracting officer considers appropriate and that pre-payment reviews may be required if the contracting officer considers them necessary [@CL-CTF-4368]. It keeps the bar on approving a payment until the event or criterion is accomplished [@CL-CTF-4369], and for a cumulative event until all identified preceding events or criteria are accomplished [@CL-CTF-4370]. It keeps entitlement solely on successful performance [@CL-CTF-4371] and the option to renegotiate the schedule after a Government-caused delay [@CL-CTF-4372]. RFO section 32.1008 keeps the duty to apply the policy and procedures in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), and (e) of 32.503-6 whenever the contracting officer exercises the Government's rights to suspend or reduce performance-based payments in accordance with paragraph (e) of the clause at 52.232-32 [@CL-CTF-4373].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has three performance-based payment events valued at 100,000 each. Event two is cumulative, so it depends on event one. Even if the work for event two is done, the contracting officer cannot approve the 100,000 payment until event one has been accomplished in accordance with the contract.

## Never confuse

Entitlement to a performance-based payment rests solely on successful performance of the specified events or criteria, not on incurring costs [@CL-CTF-0976; @CL-CTF-3359]. The contracting officer's approval duty here is separate from the clause-side payment conditions on [Performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).
