## Purpose

Outlines the contracting officer's responsibilities for reviewing, approving and transmitting performance-based payments.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0973] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1007 [@CL-CTF-0973] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1007 [@CL-CTF-4365; @CL-CTF-4366; @CL-CTF-3221; @CL-CTF-4367; @CL-CTF-4368; @CL-CTF-4369; @CL-CTF-4370; @CL-CTF-4371; @CL-CTF-4372] and RFO FAR 32.1008 [@CL-CTF-4373] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0973] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments for the contract that the contracting officer administers [@CL-CTF-0973] |

The contracting officer responsible for administering performance-based payments for the contract shall review and approve all performance-based payments for that contract [@CL-CTF-0973]. Unless otherwise provided in agency regulations or by agreement with the appropriate payment official, the contracting officer is responsible for receiving, approving and transmitting all performance-based payment requests to the appropriate payment office, and each approval shall specify the amount to be paid, necessary contractual information, and the appropriation account(s) (see FAR 32.1004(c)) to be charged for the payment [@CL-CTF-1079; @CL-CTF-3220].

The contracting officer shall not approve a performance-based payment until the specified event or performance criterion has been successfully accomplished in accordance with the contract [@CL-CTF-0974]. If an event is cumulative, the contracting officer shall not approve the payment unless all identified preceding events or criteria are accomplished [@CL-CTF-0975].

## How it works

Entitlement to a performance-based payment is solely on the basis of successful performance of the specified events or performance criteria [@CL-CTF-0976]. However, if there is a Government-caused delay, the contracting officer may renegotiate the performance-based payment schedule to facilitate contractor billings for any successfully accomplished portions of the delayed event or criterion [@CL-CTF-1084].

The contracting officer is responsible for determining what reviews are required for protection of the Government's interests [@CL-CTF-1080], and should consider the contractor's experience, performance record, reliability, financial strength and the adequacy of controls established by the contractor for administering the payments [@CL-CTF-1081]. Based upon the risk to the Government, post-payment reviews and verifications should normally be arranged as the contracting officer considers appropriate [@CL-CTF-1082], and pre-payment reviews may be required if the contracting officer considers them necessary [@CL-CTF-1083].

Whenever exercising the Government's rights to suspend or reduce performance-based payments in accordance with paragraph (e) of the clause at FAR 52.232-32, Performance-Based Payments, the contracting officer shall apply the policy and procedures in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), and (e) of section 32.503-6 [@CL-CTF-0977; @CL-CTF-1085]. The remedies are on [Suspension or reduction of progress payments](ctf-suspension-or-reduction-of-progress-payments.md), the clause-side payment conditions are on [Performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payments.md), and the event criteria are on [PBP criteria](ctf-pbp-criteria.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 32 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO section 32.1007 keeps the contracting officer's duty to review and approve all performance-based payments for the contract [@CL-CTF-4365] and, unless agency regulations or an agreement with the appropriate payment official provide otherwise, to receive, approve and transmit all performance-based payment requests to the appropriate payment office and specify in each approval the amount to be paid, necessary contractual information, and the appropriation account(s) (see 32.1004(c)) to be charged for the payment [@CL-CTF-4366; @CL-CTF-3221]. It keeps the responsibility for determining what reviews are required for protection of the Government's interests [@CL-CTF-4367], and the rules that, based upon the risk to the Government, post-payment reviews and verifications should normally be arranged as the contracting officer considers appropriate and that pre-payment reviews may be required if the contracting officer considers them necessary [@CL-CTF-4368]. It keeps the bar on approving a payment until the event or criterion is accomplished [@CL-CTF-4369], and for a cumulative event until all identified preceding events or criteria are accomplished [@CL-CTF-4370]. It keeps entitlement solely on successful performance [@CL-CTF-4371] and the option to renegotiate the schedule after a Government-caused delay [@CL-CTF-4372]. RFO section 32.1008 keeps the duty to apply the policy and procedures in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), and (e) of 32.503-6 whenever the contracting officer exercises the Government's rights to suspend or reduce performance-based payments in accordance with paragraph (e) of the clause at 52.232-32 [@CL-CTF-4373].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has three performance-based payment events valued at 100,000 each. Event two is cumulative, so it depends on event one. Even if the work for event two is done, the contracting officer cannot approve the 100,000 payment until event one has been accomplished in accordance with the contract.

## Never confuse

Entitlement to a performance-based payment is solely on the basis of successful performance of the specified events or performance criteria [@CL-CTF-0976], and under the DFARS, in accordance with 10 U.S.C. 3802(a)(2), performance-based payments shall not be conditioned upon costs incurred in contract performance, but on the achievement of performance outcomes [@CL-CTF-3359]. The clause-side payment conditions are on [Performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).
