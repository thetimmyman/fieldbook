## Purpose

Shows which advance payments fall outside the advance payment rules and how interest on the unliquidated balance works.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0922; @CL-CTF-0925] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.404 and FAR 32.407 [@CL-CTF-0922; @CL-CTF-0925] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0922] |
| Applies to | Advance payments, with exclusions for payments authorized by law for the listed purposes [@CL-CTF-0922] |

The advance payments subpart does not apply to advance payments authorized by law for rent, tuition, insurance premiums, expenses of investigations in foreign countries, extension or connection of public utilities for Government buildings or installations, and subscriptions to publications, among other listed items [@CL-CTF-0922]. One of the listed items is a purchase of supplies or services in a foreign country, if the price does not exceed $20,000 (or the equivalent in the foreign currency) and the advance payment is required by that country's laws or government regulations [@CL-CTF-0923]. The list also covers enforcement of the customs or narcotics laws and other types of transactions excluded by agency procedures under statutory authority, and agencies may issue their own instructions dealing with the listed items authorized under statutes relevant to them [@CL-CTF-0924].

Except as provided in the interest-free paragraph, the contracting officer shall charge interest on the daily unliquidated balance of all advance payments at the higher of two rates [@CL-CTF-0925]. Those rates are the published prime rate of the depository in which the special account is established and the rate set by the Secretary of the Treasury under the cited statute [@CL-CTF-0926].

## How it works

Interest is required on contracts for the acquisition, at cost, of property for Government ownership, if the contracts are awarded in combination with, or in contemplation of, supply contracts or subcontracts [@CL-CTF-1006]. The agency head or designee may authorize advance payments without interest under certain types of contracts, if in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0927]. Those types include contracts for experimental, research, or development work with nonprofit education or research institutions and contracts solely for the management and operation of Government-owned plants [@CL-CTF-1004], cost-reimbursement contracts with governments or their instrumentalities, and other classes or unusual cases that agency procedures specifically authorize [@CL-CTF-1005]. The contracting officer shall not allow interest charges required by this section as reimbursable costs under cost-reimbursement contracts, whether a prime contractor or a subcontractor incurred them [@CL-CTF-0928].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds an unliquidated advance balance of 200,000 for a full month. The depository prime rate is 8 percent a year and the Treasury rate is 6 percent a year. The higher rate, 8 percent, applies, so interest for the month is about 1,330. The contractor may not charge that interest to a cost-reimbursement contract.

## Never confuse

An advance payment that falls under a listed exclusion is outside the advance payments subpart [@CL-CTF-0922]. An advance payment made without interest is a different case, because the interest-free authority only removes the interest charge for the listed types of contracts when the agency head or designee authorizes it [@CL-CTF-0927]. Interest that is charged is a further matter, because it is not reimbursable under cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-CTF-0928]. See [Advance payments for non-commercial items](ctf-advance-payments-for-non-commercial-items.md).
