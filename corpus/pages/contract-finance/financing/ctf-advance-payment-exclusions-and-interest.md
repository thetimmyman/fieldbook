## Purpose

Shows which advance payments fall outside the advance payment rules and how interest on the unliquidated balance works.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0922; @CL-CTF-0925] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.404 and FAR 32.407 [@CL-CTF-0922; @CL-CTF-0925] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.404 [@CL-CTF-4153; @CL-CTF-4154; @CL-CTF-4155; @CL-CTF-4156] and RFO FAR 32.407 [@CL-CTF-4157; @CL-CTF-4158; @CL-CTF-4159; @CL-CTF-4160; @CL-CTF-4161; @CL-CTF-4162; @CL-CTF-4163] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0922] |
| Applies to | Advance payments, with exclusions for payments authorized by law for the listed purposes [@CL-CTF-0922] |

The advance payments subpart does not apply to advance payments authorized by law for rent, tuition, insurance premiums, expenses of investigations in foreign countries, extension or connection of public utilities for Government buildings or installations, and subscriptions to publications, among other listed items [@CL-CTF-0922]. One of the listed items is a purchase of supplies or services in a foreign country, if the price does not exceed $20,000 (or the equivalent in the foreign currency) and the advance payment is required by that country's laws or government regulations [@CL-CTF-0923]. The list also covers enforcement of the customs or narcotics laws and other types of transactions excluded by agency procedures under statutory authority, and agencies may issue their own instructions dealing with the listed items authorized under statutes relevant to them [@CL-CTF-0924].

Except as provided in paragraph (d) of FAR 32.407, the contracting officer shall charge interest on the daily unliquidated balance of all advance payments at the higher of two rates [@CL-CTF-0925]. Those rates are the published prime rate of the financial institution (depository) in which the special account is established, and the rate established by the Secretary of the Treasury under section 1215(b)(2) of the appendix to title 50, United States Code [@CL-CTF-0926].

## How it works

Interest is required on contracts for the acquisition, at cost, of property for Government ownership, if the contracts are awarded in combination with, or in contemplation of, supply contracts or subcontracts [@CL-CTF-1006]. The agency head or designee may authorize advance payments without interest under certain types of contracts, if in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0927]. Those types include contracts for experimental, research, or development work with nonprofit education or research institutions and contracts solely for the management and operation of Government-owned plants [@CL-CTF-0927; @CL-CTF-1004], cost-reimbursement contracts with governments, including State or local governments, or their instrumentalities, and other classes of contracts or unusual cases for which the exclusion of interest on advances is specifically authorized by agency procedures [@CL-CTF-1005]. The contracting officer shall not allow interest charges required by FAR 32.407 as reimbursable costs under cost-reimbursement contracts, whether a prime contractor or a subcontractor incurred them [@CL-CTF-0928].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.404(a) says the subpart does not apply to advance payments "for" the listed items, without the codified words "authorized by law" [@CL-CTF-4153; @CL-CTF-0922]. It keeps the foreign-purchase ceiling, the customs and narcotics item and the agency instructions [@CL-CTF-4154; @CL-CTF-4155; @CL-CTF-4156]. RFO FAR 32.407 keeps the interest rule: the higher of the depository prime rate and the Treasury rate [@CL-CTF-4157; @CL-CTF-4158], interest required on at-cost acquisitions of property for Government ownership [@CL-CTF-4159], the listed interest-free contract types [@CL-CTF-4160; @CL-CTF-4161; @CL-CTF-4162], and no reimbursement of the interest as a cost under cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-CTF-4163]. RFO FAR 32.407(a)(2) cites section 7109(b) of title 41, United States Code, for the Treasury rate, where the codified paragraph cites section 1215(b)(2) of the appendix to title 50 [@CL-CTF-4158; @CL-CTF-0926].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds an unliquidated advance balance of 200,000 for a full month. The depository prime rate is 8 percent a year and the Treasury rate is 6 percent a year. The higher rate, 8 percent, applies, so interest for the month is about 1,330. The contractor may not charge that interest to a cost-reimbursement contract.

## Never confuse

The advance payments subpart does not apply to an advance payment authorized by law for a listed purpose [@CL-CTF-0922]. Advance payments without interest are authorized separately: the agency head or designee may authorize them under the listed types of contracts, if in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0927]. Interest charges required by FAR 32.407 are not allowed as reimbursable costs under cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-CTF-0928]. See [Advance payments for non-commercial items](ctf-advance-payments-for-non-commercial-items.md).

The codified exclusions apply to advance payments authorized by law for the listed items [@CL-CTF-0922], while the RFO text lists the exclusions for advance payments "for" those items without the words "authorized by law" [@CL-CTF-4153].
