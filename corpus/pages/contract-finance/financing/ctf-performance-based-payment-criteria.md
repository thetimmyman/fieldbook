## Purpose

Shows the condition the Defense supplement prints for a contractor to receive performance-based payments, and the limit that the statute it cites places on the audit agency.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3836] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.1003-70 [@CL-CTF-3836] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3836] |
| Applies to | Contracts whose contractor seeks performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836] |

In accordance with the statute it cites, a contractor's financial statements shall be in compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in order to receive performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836]. The same section says that the statute does not grant the Defense Contract Audit Agency the authority to audit compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CTF-3837].

## How it works

The duty falls on the contractor: its financial statements must comply with those accounting principles, and compliance is the condition for receiving performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836]. The section names no official who decides whether the condition is met, and it prescribes no approval step. It does not say that the audit agency may never examine a contractor; it says that the cited statute does not give that agency authority to audit compliance with the accounting principles [@CL-CTF-3837]. The section states no other eligibility condition for performance-based payments. The FAR criteria for these payments, the DoD clauses and the offeror representation on financial statements are on [Performance-based payments: when they may be used and how they are set](ctf-pbp-criteria.md), [Performance-Based Payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md), [DoD performance-based payments: whole-contract basis](ctf-dod-pbp-whole-contract-basis.md), [DoD performance-based payments: deliverable-item basis](ctf-dod-pbp-deliverable-item-basis.md) and [Performance-based payments representation](../billing-payment/ctf-dfars-pbp-representation.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Contractor A prepares financial statements that comply with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles, so it meets the condition this section prints for receiving performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836]. Contractor B's statements do not comply, so under this section it does not meet the condition [@CL-CTF-3836]. An auditor from the Defense Contract Audit Agency is asked to audit whether Contractor A's statements comply. The statute cited in this section does not supply that agency with authority for that audit [@CL-CTF-3837]. The section does not say whether another source of authority exists.

## Never confuse

Compliance with the accounting principles is not an audit of that compliance. The section requires the first from the contractor and says the cited statute does not give the audit agency authority for the second [@CL-CTF-3836; @CL-CTF-3837]. This section is not the FAR payment basis, event or liquidation rules, which are on the linked cards.

## Company calibration

Whether the company's financial statements comply with the accounting standards before it accepts performance-based payment terms [@Q-039].
