## Purpose

Shows the condition the Defense supplement prints for a contractor to receive performance-based payments, and the limit that the statute it cites places on the audit agency.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3836] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.1003-70 [@CL-CTF-3836] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3836] |
| Applies to | Contracts whose contractor seeks performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836] |

In accordance with 10 USC 3802(c)(1), a contractor's financial statements shall be in compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in order to receive performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836]. Under 10 USC 3802(c)(2), that statute does not grant the Defense Contract Audit Agency the authority to audit compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CTF-3837].

## How it works

A contractor's financial statements must be in compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in order to receive performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836], and 10 USC 3802(c)(2) specifies that it does not grant the Defense Contract Audit Agency the authority to audit compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CTF-3837]. The FAR criteria for these payments, the DoD clauses and the offeror representation on financial statements are on [Performance-based payments: when they may be used and how they are set](ctf-pbp-criteria.md), [Performance-Based Payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md), [DoD performance-based payments: whole-contract basis](ctf-dod-pbp-whole-contract-basis.md), [DoD performance-based payments: deliverable-item basis](ctf-dod-pbp-deliverable-item-basis.md) and [Performance-based payments representation](../billing-payment/ctf-dfars-pbp-representation.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Contractor A prepares financial statements that comply with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles, so it meets the condition in DFARS 232.1003-70 for receiving performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836]. Contractor B's statements do not comply, so under DFARS 232.1003-70 it does not meet the condition [@CL-CTF-3836]. Under 10 USC 3802(c)(2), that statute does not grant the Defense Contract Audit Agency the authority to audit compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CTF-3837].

## Never confuse

In accordance with 10 USC 3802(c)(1), a contractor's financial statements shall be in compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in order to receive performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3836], while 10 USC 3802(c)(2) specifies that it does not grant the Defense Contract Audit Agency the authority to audit compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CTF-3837].
