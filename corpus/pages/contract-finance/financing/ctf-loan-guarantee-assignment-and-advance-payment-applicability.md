## Purpose

Shows the default assignment of claims for a contractor with a guaranteed loan, the condition that releases the agency from it, and cases the FAR lists for advance payments.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1523] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.304-5 and FAR 32.403 [@CL-CTF-1523; @CL-CTF-1525] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.304 and RFO FAR 32.403 are marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4540; @CL-CTF-4541] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1523] |
| Applies to | Contractors provided a guaranteed loan under defense production contracts, and the listed advance payment cases [@CL-CTF-1523; @CL-CTF-1525] |

The agency shall generally require a contractor that is provided a guaranteed loan to execute an assignment of claims under defense production contracts, including any contracts entered into during the term of the guaranteed loan that are eligible for financing under the loan [@CL-CTF-1523]. However, the agency need not require assignment if any of the following conditions are present [@CL-CTF-1524]. One is that the contractor's financial condition is so strong that the protection to the Government provided by an assignment of claims is unnecessary [@CL-CTF-1524]. Another is that, in connection with the assignment of claims under a major contract, the increased protection of the loan that would be provided by the assignments under additional, relatively smaller contracts is not considered necessary by the agency [@CL-CTF-3215]. A third is that the assignment of claims would create an administrative burden disproportionate to the protection required, for example if the contractor has a large number of contracts with individually small dollar amounts [@CL-CTF-3216]. The contractor shall also execute an assignment of claims if requested to do so by the guarantor or the financing institution [@CL-CTF-1574]. A subcontract or purchase order issued to a subcontractor shall not be considered eligible for financing under guaranteed loans when the issuer of the subcontract or purchase order reserves the privilege of making payments directly to the assignor or to the assignor and assignee jointly, after notice of the assignment, or the right to reduce or set off assigned proceeds under defense production contracts by reason of claims against the borrower arising after notice of assignment and independently of defense production contracts under which the borrower is the seller [@CL-CTF-3217; @CL-CTF-3218].

Advance payments may be considered useful and appropriate for the listed cases, two of which are contracts for experimental, research, or development work with nonprofit educational or research institutions and contracts solely for the management and operation of Government-owned plants [@CL-CTF-1525].

## How it works

The agency shall generally require a contractor that is provided a guaranteed loan to execute an assignment of claims, but need not require it if any of the listed conditions is present [@CL-CTF-1523; @CL-CTF-1524; @CL-CTF-3215; @CL-CTF-3216]. The contractor shall also execute an assignment of claims if requested to do so by the guarantor or the financing institution [@CL-CTF-1574]. Advance payments may be considered useful and appropriate for the listed cases [@CL-CTF-1525]. The methods of financing are described on [Contract financing methods and payment clauses for noncommercial purchases](ctf-financing-methods-and-noncommercial-payment-clauses.md), and advance payments are among the contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-1501] in [FAR 32 definitions and contract financing payment timing](ctf-far-32-definitions-and-financing-payment-timing.md). Whether to authorize advance payments, and on what terms, is covered on [Advance payments for non-commercial items](ctf-advance-payments-for-non-commercial-items.md), and the loan guarantee process is on [Loan guarantees for defense production and the certificate of eligibility](ctf-defense-production-loan-guarantees.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.304, the section under which the codified text places FAR 32.304-5 on the assignment of claims for a contractor with a guaranteed loan, is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-1523; @CL-CTF-4540]. RFO FAR 32.403, the number of the codified list of cases where advance payments may be considered useful and appropriate, is also marked reserved [@CL-CTF-1525; @CL-CTF-4541].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds a guaranteed loan of 3,000,000 and a defense production contract. The agency finds the contractor's financial condition so strong that an assignment of claims adds no needed protection.

Answer: the agency need not require the assignment. Without that condition or another listed condition, the general rule is to require it. This example uses the codified text only, and RFO FAR 32.304 is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4540].

## Never confuse

A listed advance payment case is a case for which advance payments may be considered useful and appropriate [@CL-CTF-1525]. The general assignment of claims rules are on [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](../cash/ctf-assignment-of-claims.md), and the financing types are on [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md).

The codified text says the agency shall generally require an assignment of claims from a contractor with a guaranteed loan, though it need not require it if any of the listed conditions is present [@CL-CTF-1523; @CL-CTF-1524; @CL-CTF-3215; @CL-CTF-3216], and lists cases where advance payments may be considered useful and appropriate [@CL-CTF-1525], but RFO FAR 32.304 and RFO FAR 32.403 are marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4540; @CL-CTF-4541].
