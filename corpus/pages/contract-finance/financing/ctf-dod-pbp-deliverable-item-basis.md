## Purpose

Shows how the DoD deliverable-item clause ties performance-based payment events to contract line items, and how it differs from the whole-contract clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1731] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7013 [@CL-CTF-1731] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1731] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments made on a deliverable-item basis, for the contract line items that the Contracting Officer inserts in the clause [@CL-CTF-1785; @CL-CTF-1731] |

DFARS directs the contracting officer to include this clause, with appropriate fill-ins, in solicitations and contracts that include performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3005], for performance-based payments made on a deliverable-item basis [@CL-CTF-1785]. Performance-based payments shall form the basis for the contract financing payments provided under the contract and shall apply to the contract line items that the Contracting Officer inserts [@CL-CTF-1731]. The schedule describes the basis for payment, including each payment event, the line items to which each event applies, the evidence of completion and the amount due on completion of each event [@CL-CTF-1732].

In accordance with section 3802(c) of title 10 of the United States Code, the contractor's financial statements shall be in compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in order to receive performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-1733]. FAR 52.232-32(m) does not require certification of incurred costs [@CL-CTF-1781]. Title to the property described in paragraph (f) of the FAR performance-based payments clause is the preferred security for receipt of performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-1782]. Paragraph (f) of the FAR clause does not apply to the extent that the contractor and the Contracting Officer agree on alternative forms of security [@CL-CTF-1783]. The FAR clause is covered on [Performance-based payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).

## How it works

The Contracting Officer names the line items that the financing covers, and each event in the schedule is tied to the line items it applies to [@CL-CTF-1731; @CL-CTF-1732]. For the whole-contract version of the clause, see [DoD performance-based payments: whole-contract basis](ctf-dod-pbp-whole-contract-basis.md). For the event criteria, see [Performance-based payment criteria](ctf-pbp-criteria.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has two line items. The Contracting Officer inserts line item one in the clause. Line item one has two events worth 200,000 each, and line item two is delivered and paid in the ordinary way. Answer: only the events tied to line item one are financing events, and each is paid when its own evidence of completion exists.

## Never confuse

The whole-contract clause applies to the whole contract [@CL-CTF-1725], while this clause applies to the contract line items that the Contracting Officer inserts [@CL-CTF-1731] and its schedule identifies the CLINs to which each event applies [@CL-CTF-1732]. For which kind of financing a contract carries, performance-based or progress payments, see [DoD financing notice and progress payments for multiple lots](ctf-dod-financing-notice-and-multiple-lot-progress-payments.md).
