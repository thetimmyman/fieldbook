## Purpose

Shows how the DoD deliverable-item clause ties performance-based payment events to contract line items, and how it differs from the whole-contract clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1731] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7013 [@CL-CTF-1731] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1731] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments made on a deliverable-item basis, for the contract line items that the Contracting Officer inserts in the clause [@CL-CTF-1785; @CL-CTF-1731] |

DFARS directs use of this clause for performance-based payments made on a deliverable-item basis [@CL-CTF-1785]. Performance-based payments form the basis for the contract financing payments and apply to the contract line items that the Contracting Officer inserts [@CL-CTF-1731]. The schedule describes the basis for payment, including each payment event, the line items to which each event applies, the evidence of completion and the amount due on completion of each event [@CL-CTF-1732].

To receive these payments, the contractor's financial statements must comply with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CTF-1733]. The clause states that paragraph (m) of the FAR performance-based payments clause does not require certification of incurred costs [@CL-CTF-1781]. Title to the property described in the FAR clause is the preferred security [@CL-CTF-1782]. Paragraph (f) of the FAR clause does not apply to the extent that the contractor and the Contracting Officer agree on alternative forms of security [@CL-CTF-1783]. The FAR clause is covered on [Performance-based payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).

## How it works

The Contracting Officer names the line items that the financing covers, and each event in the schedule is tied to the line items it applies to [@CL-CTF-1731; @CL-CTF-1732]. The paragraphs of this clause on payment request information, incurred cost reporting and security read the same as those of the whole-contract clause, which are set out on [DoD performance-based payments: whole-contract basis](ctf-dod-pbp-whole-contract-basis.md). The text of this clause sets no payment limit or liquidation rate of its own. The event criteria themselves are on [Performance-based payment criteria](ctf-pbp-criteria.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has two line items. The Contracting Officer inserts line item one in the clause. Line item one has two events worth 200,000 each, and line item two is delivered and paid in the ordinary way. Answer: only the events tied to line item one are financing events, and each is paid when its own evidence of completion exists.

## Never confuse

The deliverable-item clause is not the whole-contract clause. The whole-contract clause applies to the whole contract, while this clause applies to the line items that the contracting officer inserts and its schedule also names the line items for each event [@CL-CTF-1731; @CL-CTF-1732; @CL-CTF-1725]. Which kind of financing a contract carries, performance-based or progress payments, is set out on [DoD financing notice and progress payments for multiple lots](ctf-dod-financing-notice-and-multiple-lot-progress-payments.md).
