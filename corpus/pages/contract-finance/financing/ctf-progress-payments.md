## Purpose

Gives the customary progress payment rates on DoD contracts for large and small business concerns.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD class deviation [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.501-1(a) [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| RFO citation | Class Deviation 2026-O0022, DFARS 232.501-1(a) [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| Applies to | DoD contracts, from 2026-02-01 [@CL-CTF-0001] |

The customary progress payment rate on DoD contracts is 80 percent for large business concerns [@CL-CTF-0001] and 90 percent for small business concerns [@CL-CTF-0002]. The codified FAR small business rate is lower [@CL-CTF-0002].

## How it works

Progress payments based on costs are contract financing paid as costs are incurred on fixed-price work, before delivery. The contractor bills the customary rate times the costs incurred, and the payments are liquidated as deliveries are invoiced. The contract's progress payments clause states the rate that applies; a rate above the customary rate is an unusual financing case and needs approval.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A large business has incurred 1,000,000 of allowable costs on a fixed-price DoD contract. At an 80 percent rate it may request 800,000. A small business with the same costs at a 90 percent rate may request 900,000.

## Never confuse

A progress payment is financing tied to costs; a performance-based payment is financing tied to events or milestones. A contract that provides for progress payments, or for an indefinite-delivery contract an order that does, cannot also use performance-based payments. Neither is a payment for delivered items.
