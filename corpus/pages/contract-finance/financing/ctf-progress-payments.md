## Purpose

Gives the customary progress payment rates on DoD contracts for large and small business concerns.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD class deviation [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.501-1(a) [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| RFO citation | Class Deviation 2026-O0022, DFARS 232.501-1(a) [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text [@CL-CTF-0001] |
| Applies to | DoD contracts, from 2026-02-01 [@CL-CTF-0001] |

The customary progress payment rates for DoD contracts, including contracts that contain foreign military sales requirements, are 80 percent for large business concerns and 90 percent for small business concerns [@CL-CTF-0001; @CL-CTF-0002; @CL-CTF-9601]. Under codified FAR 32.501-1(a), the customary progress payment rate is 80 percent, applicable to the total costs of performing the contract, and the customary rate for contracts with small business concerns is 85 percent [@CL-CTF-0950; @CL-CTF-0951].

## How it works

Customary progress payments are those made under the general guidance in FAR subpart 32.5, using the customary progress payment rate, the cost base and frequency of payment established in the Progress Payments clause, and either the ordinary liquidation method or the alternate method as provided in subsections 32.503-8 and 32.503-9 [@CL-CTF-3548; @CL-CTF-3549]. Under the Progress Payments clause at FAR 52.232-16(a)(1), unless the contractor requests a smaller amount, the Government computes each progress payment as 80 percent of the contractor's total costs incurred under the contract whether or not actually paid, plus financing payments to subcontractors, less the sum of all previous progress payments made by the Government under the contract [@CL-CTF-0102; @CL-CTF-0271]. The contracting officer must consider any rate higher than those permitted in FAR 32.501-1(a) an unusual progress payment and must not include a higher rate in a contract unless advance agency approval is obtained as prescribed in 32.501-2 [@CL-CTF-1052].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A large business has incurred 1,000,000 of allowable costs on a fixed-price DoD contract and has received no progress payments yet. At an 80 percent rate its first request is 800,000. If it had already received 600,000, the next request would be 200,000. A small business with the same costs and no prior payments at a 90 percent rate may request 900,000.

## Never confuse

Subpart 32.5 provides contract financing through progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3845], whereas performance-based payments under subpart 32.10 are contract financing payments that are not payment for accepted items and are made on the basis of performance measured by objective, quantifiable methods, accomplishments of defined events, or other quantifiable measures of results [@CL-CTF-3064; @CL-CTF-1513; @CL-CTF-3222]. For contracts for supplies or services awarded under the competitive negotiation method of procurement in accordance with part 15, customary contract financing is through either progress payments based on costs in accordance with subpart 32.5 or performance-based payments in accordance with subpart 32.10, but not both [@CL-CTF-1412].
