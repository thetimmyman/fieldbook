## Purpose

Sets out when the contracting officer may use performance-based payments, and which solicitation provision and contract clause the prescriptions require.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3561] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1003 and 32.1005 [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3564] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1003 [@CL-CTF-4374; @CL-CTF-4375] and RFO FAR 32.1005 [@CL-CTF-4376; @CL-CTF-4377; @CL-CTF-4378] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3561] |
| Applies to | Individual orders and contracts for which the contracting officer and the offeror agree the payment terms and the other listed conditions are met [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3562; @CL-CTF-3563] |

The contracting officer may use performance-based payments for individual orders and contracts provided that the contracting officer and the offeror agree on the performance-based payment terms [@CL-CTF-3561]. A further condition is that the contract, individual order or line item is a fixed-price type [@CL-CTF-3562]. Two further conditions depend on the contract type: for an indefinite delivery contract the individual order does not provide for progress payments, and for other than an indefinite delivery contract the contract does not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3563].

The prescriptions direct that the clause at 52.232-32, Performance-Based Payments, be inserted in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for performance-based payments, and in fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide them [@CL-CTF-3564]. They direct that the solicitation provision at 52.232-28, Invitation to Propose Performance-Based Payments, be inserted in negotiated solicitations that invite offerors to propose performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3565]. The prescription directs use of the provision with its Alternate I in competitive negotiated solicitations if the Government intends to adjust proposed prices for proposal evaluation purposes [@CL-CTF-3566].

## How it works

The contracting officer decides whether to use performance-based payments, and the criteria are conditions for that choice, not a duty to use them. The policy section prefers performance-based payments when the contracting officer finds them practical and the contractor agrees to their use [@CL-CTF-3063]. The criteria require agreed terms, a fixed-price type at the level of the contract, the individual order or the line item, and no progress payments on the individual order for an indefinite delivery contract or on the contract in any other case [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3562; @CL-CTF-3563].

The criteria are not the only limits. The policy section bars performance-based payments for payments under cost-reimbursement line items [@CL-CTF-3066]. It also bars them for contracts for architect-engineer services or construction, or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration or repair, when those contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3159], and for contracts awarded through sealed bid procedures [@CL-CTF-3160]. Meeting the criteria here therefore does not allow use in those cases.

The prescriptions then place the clause in the solicitation and the contract, and the invitation provision in negotiated solicitations that invite proposals [@CL-CTF-3564; @CL-CTF-3565]. Alternate I of the provision follows the intent to adjust prices at evaluation [@CL-CTF-3566].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO section 32.1003 keeps the provisos for use of performance-based payments that the contracting officer and offeror agree on the performance-based payment terms and that the contract, individual order, or line item is a fixed-price type [@CL-CTF-4374], and the conditions that, for indefinite delivery contracts, the individual order does not provide for progress payments and, for other than indefinite-delivery contracts, the contract does not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-4375]. RFO section 32.1005 keeps the insertion of the clause at 52.232-32 in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for performance-based payments and in fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide them [@CL-CTF-4376], and the insertion of the provision at 52.232-28 in negotiated solicitations that invite offerors to propose performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-4377]. It keeps the use of the provision with its Alternate I in competitive negotiated solicitations if the Government intends to adjust proposed prices for proposal evaluation purposes [@CL-CTF-4378].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A competitive negotiated solicitation for a fixed-price order under an indefinite delivery contract invites offerors to propose performance-based payments. The order provides no progress payments, it is a fixed-price type, and the contracting officer and the selected offeror agree the payment terms, so the criteria are met [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3562; @CL-CTF-3563]. The order is not a cost-reimbursement line item, was not awarded through sealed bid procedures and provides no progress payments, so the policy bars do not apply [@CL-CTF-3066; @CL-CTF-3159; @CL-CTF-3160]. Because the solicitation invites offerors to propose the payments, it carries the provision at 52.232-28 [@CL-CTF-3565]. Because it may result in a contract providing for the payments, it also carries the clause at 52.232-32, and the resulting fixed-price contract carries the clause again [@CL-CTF-3564]. The agency does not intend to adjust proposed prices at evaluation, so the Alternate I prescription is not triggered [@CL-CTF-3566].

## Never confuse

Performance-based payments are often confused with progress payments. Both are contract financing, but the criteria exclude an indefinite delivery order that provides for progress payments and any contract other than an indefinite delivery contract that provides for them [@CL-CTF-3563]. The invitation provision is also mistaken for the clause. The clause at 52.232-32 goes in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for the payments and in fixed-price contracts that provide them [@CL-CTF-3564]. The provision at 52.232-28 goes in negotiated solicitations that invite offerors to propose them [@CL-CTF-3565]. The [procedure card](ctf-pbp-criteria.md) covers the payment bases and liquidation.

The Defense supplement's condition for receiving performance-based payments is covered in [DoD criteria for using performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payment-criteria.md).
