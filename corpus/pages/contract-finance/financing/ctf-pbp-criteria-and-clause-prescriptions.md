## Purpose

Sets out when the contracting officer may use performance-based payments, and which solicitation provision and contract clause the prescriptions require.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3561] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1003 and 32.1005 [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3564] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1003 [@CL-CTF-4374; @CL-CTF-4375] and RFO FAR 32.1005 [@CL-CTF-4376; @CL-CTF-4377; @CL-CTF-4378] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3561] |
| Applies to | Individual orders and contracts for which the contracting officer and the offeror agree the payment terms and the other listed conditions are met [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3562; @CL-CTF-3563] |

The contracting officer may use performance-based payments for individual orders and contracts provided that the contracting officer and the offeror agree on the performance-based payment terms [@CL-CTF-3561]. A further condition is that the contract, individual order or line item is a fixed-price type [@CL-CTF-3562]. Two further conditions depend on the contract type: for an indefinite delivery contract the individual order does not provide for progress payments, and for other than an indefinite delivery contract the contract does not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3563].

The prescriptions direct that the clause at 52.232-32, Performance-Based Payments, be inserted in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for performance-based payments, and in fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide them [@CL-CTF-3564]. They direct that the solicitation provision at 52.232-28, Invitation to Propose Performance-Based Payments, be inserted in negotiated solicitations that invite offerors to propose performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3565]. The prescription directs use of the provision with its Alternate I in competitive negotiated solicitations if the Government intends to adjust proposed prices for proposal evaluation purposes [@CL-CTF-3566].

## How it works

Performance-based payments are the preferred Government financing method when the contracting officer finds them practical, and the contractor agrees to their use [@CL-CTF-3063]. The contracting officer may use performance-based payments for individual orders and contracts provided the contracting officer and offeror agree on the performance-based payment terms, the contract, individual order, or line item is a fixed-price type, for indefinite delivery contracts the individual order does not provide for progress payments, and for other than indefinite delivery contracts the contract does not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3562; @CL-CTF-3563].

Performance-based payments shall not be used for payments under cost-reimbursement line items [@CL-CTF-3066], for contracts for architect-engineer services or construction, or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration, or repair, when those contracts provide for progress payments based upon a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3159], or for contracts awarded through sealed bid procedures [@CL-CTF-3160].

The prescriptions direct inserting the clause at 52.232-32 in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for performance-based payments and in fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide them, and inserting the solicitation provision at 52.232-28 in negotiated solicitations that invite offerors to propose performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3564; @CL-CTF-3565]. The provision is used with its Alternate I in competitive negotiated solicitations if the Government intends to adjust proposed prices for proposal evaluation purposes [@CL-CTF-3566].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 32 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO section 32.1003 keeps the provisos for use of performance-based payments that the contracting officer and offeror agree on the performance-based payment terms and that the contract, individual order, or line item is a fixed-price type [@CL-CTF-4374], and the conditions that, for indefinite delivery contracts, the individual order does not provide for progress payments and, for other than indefinite-delivery contracts, the contract does not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-4375]. RFO section 32.1005 keeps the insertion of the clause at 52.232-32 in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for performance-based payments and in fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide them [@CL-CTF-4376], and the insertion of the provision at 52.232-28 in negotiated solicitations that invite offerors to propose performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-4377]. It keeps the use of the provision with its Alternate I in competitive negotiated solicitations if the Government intends to adjust proposed prices for proposal evaluation purposes [@CL-CTF-4378].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A competitive negotiated solicitation for a fixed-price order under an indefinite delivery contract invites offerors to propose performance-based payments. The order provides no progress payments, it is a fixed-price type, and the contracting officer and the selected offeror agree the payment terms, so the criteria are met [@CL-CTF-3561; @CL-CTF-3562; @CL-CTF-3563]. The order is not a cost-reimbursement line item, was not awarded through sealed bid procedures and provides no progress payments, so the policy bars do not apply [@CL-CTF-3066; @CL-CTF-3159; @CL-CTF-3160]. Because the solicitation invites offerors to propose the payments, it carries the provision at 52.232-28 [@CL-CTF-3565]. Because it may result in a contract providing for the payments, it also carries the clause at 52.232-32, and the resulting fixed-price contract carries the clause again [@CL-CTF-3564]. The agency does not intend to adjust proposed prices at evaluation, so the Alternate I prescription is not triggered [@CL-CTF-3566].

## Never confuse

Under the criteria for using performance-based payments, for indefinite delivery contracts the individual order does not provide for progress payments, and for other than indefinite delivery contracts the contract does not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3563]. The clause at 52.232-32 is inserted in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for performance-based payments and in fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide them [@CL-CTF-3564], while the solicitation provision at 52.232-28 is inserted in negotiated solicitations that invite offerors to propose performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3565]. The [procedure card](ctf-pbp-criteria.md) covers the payment bases and liquidation.

The Defense supplement's condition for receiving performance-based payments is covered in [DoD criteria for using performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payment-criteria.md).
