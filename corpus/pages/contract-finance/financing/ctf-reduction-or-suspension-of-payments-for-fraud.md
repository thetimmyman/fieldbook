## Purpose

Shows when an agency head may reduce or suspend further payments because a contractor's request for advance, partial or progress payments is based on fraud, which agencies have the authority, who holds the decision, and where the contractor's chance to respond is set out.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3515] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.006-1 and FAR 32.006-2 [@CL-CTF-3515; @CL-CTF-3521] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.006-1 [@CL-CTF-4483; @CL-CTF-4484; @CL-CTF-4485; @CL-CTF-4486; @CL-CTF-4487; @CL-CTF-4488] and RFO FAR 32.006-2 [@CL-CTF-4489] and RFO FAR 32.006-4 [@CL-CTF-4490; @CL-CTF-4491; @CL-CTF-4779; @CL-CTF-4492] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3515] |
| Applies to | A contractor's request for advance, partial or progress payments, excluding commercial interim payments and performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3517; @CL-CTF-3518] |

The statutes provide for a reduction or suspension of further payments to a contractor when the agency head determines there is substantial evidence that the contractor's request for advance, partial or progress payments is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-3517].

Under the title 10 provision the authority is available to the Department of Defense and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, and it is not available to the United States Coast Guard [@CL-CTF-3515]. Under the title 41 provision it is available to all agencies subject to Division C of subtitle I of title 41 [@CL-CTF-3516].

The authority does not apply to commercial interim payments under the commercial purchase financing subpart or to performance-based payments under the performance-based payments subpart [@CL-CTF-3518].

The agency head may not delegate these responsibilities below Level IV of the Executive Schedule [@CL-CTF-3519]. Agency head determinations and decisions may be made for an individual contract or for any group of contracts affected by the fraud [@CL-CTF-3520]. The authority is in addition to other Government rights, remedies and procedures [@CL-CTF-3608].

As the section uses the term, a remedy coordination official is the person or entity in the agency who coordinates within that agency the administration of criminal, civil, administrative and contractual remedies resulting from investigations of fraud or corruption related to procurement activities [@CL-CTF-3521].

## How it works

The agency head decides. The trigger is the agency head's determination that there is substantial evidence that the payment request is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-3517].

The FAR's general definitions say agency head or head of the agency means the Secretary, Attorney General, Administrator, Governor, Chairperson, or other chief official of an executive agency, unless otherwise indicated, including any deputy or assistant chief official of an executive agency [@CL-CTF-9394]. The limit on delegating these responsibilities is stated in the section this card covers [@CL-CTF-3519].

The contractor's opportunity to respond comes from the procedures section, not from the two sections on this card. There the remedy coordination official recommends the reduction or suspension [@CL-CTF-1450]. Before deciding, the agency head shall, in accordance with agency procedures, notify the contractor in writing of the action proposed by the remedy coordination official and the reasons for it, with notice sufficiently specific to permit the contractor to collect and present evidence addressing those reasons [@CL-CTF-1404; @CL-CTF-2221], and provide an opportunity to submit information within a reasonable time in response to the action proposed by the remedy coordination official [@CL-CTF-1405]. The steps, the factors the agency head weighs and the review deadline are covered in [Financing consideration and fraud payment procedures](ctf-financing-consideration-and-fraud-payment-procedures.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.006-1 keeps the authority: under title 10 it is available to the Department of Defense and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration and not to the United States Coast Guard [@CL-CTF-4483], and under title 41 it is available to all agencies subject to Division C of subtitle I of title 41 [@CL-CTF-4484]. It keeps the trigger, which is the agency head's determination that there is substantial evidence that the contractor's request for advance, partial or progress payments is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-4485], the exclusion of commercial interim payments and performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-4486], the limit on delegation below Level IV of the Executive Schedule and the authority being in addition to other Government rights, remedies and procedures [@CL-CTF-4487], and the choice of an individual contract or any group of contracts affected by the fraud [@CL-CTF-4488]. RFO FAR 32.006-2 keeps the definition of a remedy coordination official [@CL-CTF-4489]. RFO FAR 32.006-4 keeps the recommendation by the remedy coordination official [@CL-CTF-4490], and before deciding the agency head must, in accordance with agency procedures, notify the contractor in writing [@CL-CTF-4491], with a notice sufficiently specific to allow the contractor to collect and present evidence addressing the reasons given [@CL-CTF-4779], and provide an opportunity to submit information within a reasonable time [@CL-CTF-4492].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency subject to Division C of subtitle I of title 41 holds a contract worth 4 million with a contractor whose progress payment request is under review, and a second contract with the same contractor is also affected by the same fraud [@CL-CTF-3516]. The agency head determines there is substantial evidence that the progress payment request is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-3517]. The determination and decision may be made for the first contract alone or for both contracts together [@CL-CTF-3520]. Before deciding, the agency head notifies the contractor in writing, in notice sufficiently specific to permit it to collect and present evidence, and gives it an opportunity to submit information in response [@CL-CTF-1404; @CL-CTF-2221; @CL-CTF-1405]. A third contract pays the contractor by performance-based payments, and this authority does not apply to those payments [@CL-CTF-3518]. The authority is in addition to other Government rights, remedies and procedures, so the agency may use those as well [@CL-CTF-3608].

## Never confuse

The remedy coordination official is not the deciding official. The official coordinates the agency's remedies and recommends action, while the agency head makes the determination and decides [@CL-CTF-3521; @CL-CTF-1450; @CL-CTF-3517].

This authority is also not the Government's right under the Progress Payments clause to reduce or suspend progress payments, or to increase the liquidation rate, under specified conditions [@CL-CTF-0251]. That right is covered in [Suspension or reduction of progress payments](ctf-suspension-or-reduction-of-progress-payments.md).
