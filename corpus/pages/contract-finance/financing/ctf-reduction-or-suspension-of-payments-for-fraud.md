## Purpose

Shows when an agency head may reduce or suspend further payments because a contractor's request for advance, partial or progress payments is based on fraud, which agencies have the authority, who holds the decision, and where the contractor's chance to respond is set out.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3515] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.006-1 and FAR 32.006-2 [@CL-CTF-3515; @CL-CTF-3521] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3515] |
| Applies to | A contractor's request for advance, partial or progress payments, excluding commercial interim payments and performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3517; @CL-CTF-3518] |

The statutes provide for a reduction or suspension of further payments to a contractor when the agency head determines there is substantial evidence that the contractor's request for advance, partial or progress payments is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-3517].

Under the title 10 provision the authority is available to the Department of Defense and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, and it is not available to the United States Coast Guard [@CL-CTF-3515]. Under the title 41 provision it is available to all agencies subject to Division C of subtitle I of title 41 [@CL-CTF-3516].

The authority does not apply to commercial interim payments under the commercial purchase financing subpart or to performance-based payments under the performance-based payments subpart [@CL-CTF-3518].

The agency head may not delegate these responsibilities below Level IV of the Executive Schedule [@CL-CTF-3519]. Agency head determinations and decisions may be made for an individual contract or for any group of contracts affected by the fraud [@CL-CTF-3520]. The authority is in addition to other Government rights, remedies and procedures [@CL-CTF-3608].

As the section uses the term, a remedy coordination official is the person or entity in the agency who coordinates within that agency the administration of criminal, civil, administrative and contractual remedies resulting from investigations of fraud or corruption related to procurement activities [@CL-CTF-3521].

## How it works

The agency head decides. The trigger is the agency head's determination that there is substantial evidence that the payment request is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-3517].

The contractor's opportunity to respond comes from the procedures section, not from the two sections on this card. There the remedy coordination official recommends the reduction or suspension [@CL-CTF-1450]. Before deciding, the agency head shall, in accordance with agency procedures, notify the contractor in writing of the action proposed by the remedy coordination official and the reasons for it [@CL-CTF-1404], and provide an opportunity to submit information within a reasonable time in response to the action proposed by the remedy coordination official [@CL-CTF-1405]. The steps, the factors the agency head weighs and the review deadline are covered in [Financing consideration and fraud payment procedures](ctf-financing-consideration-and-fraud-payment-procedures.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency subject to Division C of subtitle I of title 41 holds a contract worth 4 million with a contractor whose progress payment request is under review, and a second contract with the same contractor is also affected by the same fraud [@CL-CTF-3516]. The agency head determines there is substantial evidence that the progress payment request is based on fraud [@CL-CTF-3517]. The determination and decision may be made for the first contract alone or for both contracts together [@CL-CTF-3520]. Before deciding, the agency head notifies the contractor in writing and gives it an opportunity to submit information in response [@CL-CTF-1404; @CL-CTF-1405]. A third contract pays the contractor by performance-based payments, and this authority does not apply to those payments [@CL-CTF-3518]. The authority is in addition to other Government rights, remedies and procedures, so the agency may use those as well [@CL-CTF-3608].

## Never confuse

The remedy coordination official is not the deciding official. The official coordinates the agency's remedies and recommends action, while the agency head makes the determination and decides [@CL-CTF-3521; @CL-CTF-1450; @CL-CTF-3517].

This authority is also not the Government's right under the Progress Payments clause to reduce or suspend progress payments, or to increase the liquidation rate, under specified conditions [@CL-CTF-0251]. That right is covered in [Suspension or reduction of progress payments](ctf-suspension-or-reduction-of-progress-payments.md).
