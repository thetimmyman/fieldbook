## Purpose

Explains how the administrative contracting officer approves progress payment requests and reviews them after payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0962] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.503-3 to 32.503-5 [@CL-CTF-0961; @CL-CTF-0962; @CL-CTF-0964] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.503-3 [@CL-CTF-4394; @CL-CTF-4395; @CL-CTF-4396; @CL-CTF-4397]; RFO FAR 32.503-4 [@CL-CTF-4398; @CL-CTF-4399; @CL-CTF-4400; @CL-CTF-4401; @CL-CTF-4402]; and RFO FAR 32.503-5 [@CL-CTF-4403; @CL-CTF-4404; @CL-CTF-4405] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0962] |
| Applies to | Progress payment requests approved and administered by the administrative contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0962] |

For a contractor found by previous experience or recent audit review (within the last 12 months) to be reliable, competent, and capable of satisfactory performance, possessed of an adequate accounting system and controls, and in sound financial condition, progress payments in amounts requested by the contractor should be approved as a matter of course [@CL-CTF-0961; @CL-CTF-1066]. For all other contractors, the administrative contracting officer shall not approve progress payments before determining that the contractor will be capable of liquidating any progress payments or that the Government is otherwise protected against loss by additional protective provisions [@CL-CTF-0983], and that the contractor's accounting system and controls are adequate for proper administration of progress payments [@CL-CTF-1070].

The administrative contracting officer should not routinely ask for audits of progress payment requests [@CL-CTF-0962]. However, when there is reason to question the reliability or accuracy of the contractor's certification or to believe that the contract will involve a loss, the officer should ask for a review or audit of the request before payment is approved or the request is otherwise disposed of [@CL-CTF-1067].

## How it works

When the reliability of the contractor and the adequacy of its accounting system and controls have been established, the administrative contracting officer may, in approving any particular request, including initial requests on new contracts, rely upon the accounting system and the contractor's certification without requiring audit or review of the request before payment [@CL-CTF-1069; @CL-CTF-0982]. When there is reason to doubt the amount of a request, only the doubtful amount should be withheld, subject to later adjustment after review or audit, and any clearly proper and due amounts should be paid without awaiting resolution of the differences [@CL-CTF-0963].

Postpayment reviews, including audits when considered necessary, shall be made periodically, or when considered desirable by the administrative contracting officer, to determine the validity of progress payments already made and expected to be made [@CL-CTF-0964]. These reviews or audits shall, as a minimum, include a determination of whether or not four things hold: the unliquidated progress payments are fairly supported by the value of the work accomplished on the undelivered portion of the contract [@CL-CTF-1068]; the applicable limitation on progress payments in the Progress Payments clause has been exceeded; either the unpaid balance of the contract price will be adequate to cover the anticipated cost of completion or the contractor has adequate resources to complete the contract [@CL-CTF-9598]; and there is reason to doubt the adequacy and reliability of the contractor's accounting system and controls and certification [@CL-CTF-9599]. Under indefinite-delivery contracts, the contracting officer should administer progress payments made under each individual order as if the order constituted a separate contract, unless agency procedures provide otherwise [@CL-CTF-0965]. System controls are reviewed against [Accounting system criteria](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-accounting-system-criteria.md), and the remedies for problems are on [Suspension or reduction of progress payments](ctf-suspension-or-reduction-of-progress-payments.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.503-3 keeps the same findings for approval as a matter of course [@CL-CTF-4394; @CL-CTF-4395] and the same determinations for all other contractors [@CL-CTF-4396; @CL-CTF-4397]. RFO FAR 32.503-4 keeps the rule that, once the contractor's reliability and accounting system are established, the officer may rely on them and on the contractor's certification without requiring audit or review before payment [@CL-CTF-4398; @CL-CTF-4399], that audits are not routinely asked for except where there is reason to question the certification or to believe the contract will involve a loss [@CL-CTF-4400; @CL-CTF-4401], and that only a doubtful amount is withheld [@CL-CTF-4402]. RFO FAR 32.503-5 keeps the postpayment reviews [@CL-CTF-4403], the four minimum determinations [@CL-CTF-4404; @CL-CTF-3223; @CL-CTF-3224], and order-by-order administration under indefinite-delivery contracts unless agency procedures provide otherwise [@CL-CTF-4405].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor submits a progress payment request for 500,000. The administrative contracting officer has reason to doubt 40,000 of it but finds the other 460,000 clearly proper and due. The officer pays 460,000 without awaiting resolution and withholds only the 40,000, subject to later adjustment after review or audit.

## Never confuse

Reliance before payment differs from review after it rather than excluding review altogether: even when the administrative contracting officer relies upon the accounting system and the contractor's certification without requiring audit or review of a request before payment [@CL-CTF-0982], postpayment reviews, including audits when considered necessary, shall be made periodically, or when considered desirable by the administrative contracting officer, to determine the validity of progress payments already made and expected to be made [@CL-CTF-0964]. When there is reason to doubt the amount of a progress payment request, only the doubtful amount should be withheld rather than the whole request, subject to later adjustment after review or audit, and any clearly proper and due amounts should be paid without awaiting resolution of the differences [@CL-CTF-0963]. For the general financing concept, see [Progress payments](ctf-progress-payments.md).
