## Purpose

Shows the agreement, supervision and lien that protect the Government when advance payments are approved, and the form of the special account agreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0935; @CL-CTF-0938] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.409-3 and FAR 32.411 [@CL-CTF-0935; @CL-CTF-0938] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.409-3 [@CL-CTF-4190; @CL-CTF-4191; @CL-CTF-4192; @CL-CTF-4193; @CL-CTF-4194] and RFO FAR 32.411 [@CL-CTF-4195; @CL-CTF-4196] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0935] |
| Applies to | Approved advance payments [@CL-CTF-0935] |

If advance payments are approved, the contracting officer shall enter into an agreement with the contractor covering special accounts and suitable covenants protecting the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0935]. That requirement generally applies under all statutory authorities, but modified requirements for certain specific cases are prescribed in later paragraphs of the section [@CL-CTF-1008]. The agency shall ensure that the amount of advance payments does not exceed the contractor's financial needs and shall closely supervise the contractor's withdrawal of funds from the special accounts [@CL-CTF-0936].

In the terms of the agreement, the contracting officer should provide for a paramount lien in favor of the Government that may supplement or replace other security requirements, and the lien should cover the supplies being acquired [@CL-CTF-0937], any credit balance in the special account, and all property the contractor acquires for performing the contract, except to the extent the Government otherwise has valid title to the property [@CL-CTF-1009]. The contracting officer must use substantially the prescribed form of agreement for a special account [@CL-CTF-0938].

## How it works

The agreement covers special accounts, and the agency closely supervises the contractor's withdrawals from the accounts in which advance payments are deposited [@CL-CTF-0935; @CL-CTF-0936]. In the prescribed form, the Government has a lien on the credit balance in the account to secure repayment of all advance payments, and that lien is paramount to any lien or claim of the financial institution regarding the account [@CL-CTF-0939].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.409-3 keeps the rules described above: if advance payments are approved, the contracting officer must enter into an agreement covering special accounts and suitable covenants protecting the Government’s interest [@CL-CTF-4190], and that requirement generally applies under all statutory authorities, with modified requirements for certain specific cases [@CL-CTF-4191]. The agency must ensure that the amount of advance payments does not exceed the contractor’s financial needs and closely supervise the contractor’s withdrawals from the special accounts [@CL-CTF-4192]. The paramount lien should cover supplies being acquired [@CL-CTF-4193], any credit balance in the special account, and all property the contractor acquires for performing the contract, except to the extent the Government otherwise has valid title [@CL-CTF-4194]. RFO FAR 32.411 keeps the requirement to use substantially the form of agreement for a special account [@CL-CTF-4195], in which the Government must have a lien on the credit balance that is paramount to any lien or claim of the financial institution regarding the account [@CL-CTF-4196].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency approves an advance payment of 300,000. The contracting officer signs the special account agreement with the contractor and the bank. The agency checks that 300,000 does not exceed the contractor's needs and reviews each withdrawal. The Government's lien sits ahead of the bank's claim on the balance.

## Never confuse

The paramount lien that the contracting officer should provide for in the agreement terms covers supplies, the special account balance and acquired property [@CL-CTF-0937; @CL-CTF-1009], while the lien in the special account form is the Government's lien on the credit balance in the account, paramount to any claim of the financial institution regarding the account [@CL-CTF-0939]. The agency's supervision of withdrawals is a separate duty from the limit on the amount of advance payments [@CL-CTF-0936]. For the clause that governs the account in the contract, see [Advance payments for non-commercial items](ctf-advance-payments-for-non-commercial-items.md).
