## Purpose

Names the EFT clauses and the solicitation provision the contracting officer inserts, with the trigger for each.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1541] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1110 [@CL-CTF-1541] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1110 [@CL-CTF-4303; @CL-CTF-4304; @CL-CTF-4305; @CL-CTF-4306; @CL-CTF-4307; @CL-CTF-4308; @CL-CTF-4309; @CL-CTF-4310; @CL-CTF-4311; @CL-CTF-4312; @CL-CTF-4313] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1541] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that include the SAM registration provision or an agency clause requiring SAM registration, or that require EFT as the method for payment but do not include that provision or a similar agency clause, subject to the stated exceptions [@CL-CTF-1541; @CL-CTF-1542; @CL-CTF-1543; @CL-CTF-1544] |

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-33, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—System for Award Management, in solicitations and contracts that include the provision at 52.204-7, System for Award Management [@CL-CTF-1541]. The same insertion applies to solicitations and contracts that include an agency clause that requires a contractor to be registered in SAM and maintain registration until final payment, unless one of two alternatives applies: payment will be made through a third party arrangement (see 13.301 and paragraph (d) of this section), or an exception listed in 32.1103(a) through (i) applies [@CL-CTF-1542]. The contracting officer shall also insert the clause at 52.232-34, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—Other than System for Award Management, in solicitations and contracts that require EFT as the method for payment but do not include the provision at 52.204-7, System for Award Management, or a similar agency clause that requires the contractor to be registered in SAM [@CL-CTF-1541; @CL-CTF-1543; @CL-CTF-1544]. For the exceptions to EFT payment, see [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md).

If EFT information is to be submitted to other than the payment office in accordance with agency procedures, the contracting officer shall insert in solicitations and contracts the clause at 52.232-35, Designation of Office for Government Receipt of Electronic Funds Transfer Information [@CL-CTF-1545], or a clause substantially the same as 52.232-35 that clearly informs the contractor where to send the EFT information [@CL-CTF-1573]. If payment under a written contract will be made by a charge to a Government account with a third party such as a Governmentwide commercial purchase card, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-36, Payment by Third Party, in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CTF-1546]. When the clause at 52.232-36 is included in a solicitation or contract, the contracting officer shall also insert the clause at 52.232-33 or the clause at 52.232-34 [@CL-CTF-1571], as appropriate [@CL-CTF-1572]. If the contract or agreement provides for the use of delivery orders and provides that the ordering office designate the method of payment for individual orders, the contracting officer shall insert in the solicitation and contract or agreement the clause at 52.232-37 and, to the extent they are applicable, the clauses at 52.232-33, 52.232-34 and 52.232-36 [@CL-CTF-1547; @CL-CTF-3010]. If the solicitation contains the clause at 52.232-34, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—Other Than System for Award Management, and an offeror is required to submit EFT information prior to award [@CL-CTF-1548], the contracting officer shall insert in the solicitation the provision at 52.232-38, Submission of Electronic Funds Transfer Information with Offer, or a provision substantially the same [@CL-CTF-1548; @CL-CTF-1549]; and, for sealed bid solicitations, the contracting officer shall amend 52.232-38 to ensure that a bidder's EFT information is not a part of the bid to be opened at the public opening and may not be released to members of the general public who request a copy of the bid [@CL-CTF-1548; @CL-CTF-3012].

## How it works

The clause at 52.232-33 is inserted in solicitations and contracts that include the provision at 52.204-7 or an agency clause that requires SAM registration, unless one of the two stated alternatives applies [@CL-CTF-1541; @CL-CTF-1542]. The clause at 52.232-34 is inserted in solicitations and contracts that require EFT as the method for payment but do not include the provision at 52.204-7 or a similar agency clause [@CL-CTF-1543; @CL-CTF-1544]. The offeror EFT information provision is inserted where the solicitation contains the clause at 52.232-34 and an offeror is required to submit EFT information prior to award [@CL-CTF-1548; @CL-CTF-1549].

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.1110 says to insert the clause at 52.232-33 in solicitations and contracts that include the provision at 52.204-7 [@CL-CTF-4303] or an agency clause that requires a contractor to be registered in SAM and maintain registration until final payment, unless one of two alternatives applies: payment will be made through a third party arrangement, or an exception listed in 32.1103(a) through (i) applies [@CL-CTF-4304]. It says to insert the clause at 52.232-34 in solicitations and contracts that require EFT as the method for payment [@CL-CTF-4305] but do not include that provision or a similar agency clause [@CL-CTF-4306]. If EFT information is to be submitted to other than the payment office in accordance with agency procedures, it says to insert the clause at 52.232-35 [@CL-CTF-4307], or a clause substantially the same as 52.232-35 [@CL-CTF-4308]. If payment under a written contract will be made by a charge to a Government account with a third party, it says to insert the clause at 52.232-36 [@CL-CTF-4309], and when that clause is included, to insert the clause at 52.232-33 or 52.232-34 as appropriate [@CL-CTF-4310]. If the contract or agreement provides for the use of delivery orders and provides that the ordering office designate the method of payment for individual orders, it says to insert the clause at 52.232-37 [@CL-CTF-4311] and, to the extent they are applicable, the clauses at 52.232-33, 52.232-34 and 52.232-36 [@CL-CTF-3011]. If the solicitation contains the clause at 52.232-34 and an offeror is required to submit EFT information prior to award, it says to insert the provision at 52.232-38 in the solicitation [@CL-CTF-4312; @CL-CTF-4313] and, for sealed bid solicitations, to amend 52.232-38 to ensure that a bidder's EFT information is not a part of the bid to be opened at the public opening and may not be released to members of the general public who request a copy of the bid [@CL-CTF-3013].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a 600,000 supply contract includes the SAM registration provision. No third party arrangement is planned and no listed EFT exception applies, so the contracting officer inserts the EFT clause that goes with SAM registration.

Answer: the SAM-based EFT clause. If the solicitation lacked the registration provision and any similar agency clause but required EFT, the other EFT clause would be listed instead.

## Never confuse

When the clause at 52.232-36, Payment by Third Party, is included in a solicitation or contract, the contracting officer shall also insert the clause at 52.232-33 or 52.232-34 [@CL-CTF-1571; @CL-CTF-1572]. The offeror EFT information provision at 52.232-38 is a provision inserted in the solicitation where the solicitation contains the clause at 52.232-34 and an offeror is required to submit EFT information prior to award [@CL-CTF-1548; @CL-CTF-1549]. For the rules on when the Government pays by EFT and by purchase card, see [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md). For invoice due dates, see [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md), and for DoD electronic submission, see [DoD payment rules: prompt payment applicability, electronic submission and performance-based payment procedures](../billing-payment/ctf-dod-payment-applicability-and-electronic-submission.md).

Codified FAR 32.1110(a) says the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-33 [@CL-CTF-1541]; RFO FAR 32.1110(a) says "Insert the clause at—" [@CL-CTF-4303].
