## Purpose

Names the EFT clauses and the solicitation provision the contracting officer inserts, with the trigger for each.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1541] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1110 [@CL-CTF-1541] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1110 [@CL-CTF-4303; @CL-CTF-4304; @CL-CTF-4305; @CL-CTF-4306; @CL-CTF-4307; @CL-CTF-4308; @CL-CTF-4309; @CL-CTF-4310; @CL-CTF-4311; @CL-CTF-4312; @CL-CTF-4313] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1541] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that include the SAM registration provision or an agency clause requiring SAM registration, or that require EFT as the method for payment, subject to the stated exceptions [@CL-CTF-1541; @CL-CTF-1542; @CL-CTF-1543; @CL-CTF-1544] |

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-33, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—System for Award Management, in solicitations and contracts that include the provision at 52.204-7, System for Award Management [@CL-CTF-1541]. The same paragraph names an agency clause that requires a contractor to be registered in SAM and maintain registration until final payment as a second trigger, and it closes with two exceptions, which are payment through a third party arrangement and an exception listed in 32.1103(a) through (i) [@CL-CTF-1542]. Under the same opening words, the clause at 52.232-34, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—Other than System for Award Management, is listed for solicitations and contracts that require EFT as the method for payment but do not include the provision at 52.204-7 or a similar agency clause that requires the contractor to be registered in SAM [@CL-CTF-1541; @CL-CTF-1543; @CL-CTF-1544]. The exceptions to EFT payment are on [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md).

If EFT information is to be submitted to other than the payment office in accordance with agency procedures, the contracting officer shall insert in solicitations and contracts the clause at 52.232-35, Designation of Office for Government Receipt of Electronic Funds Transfer Information [@CL-CTF-1545], or a clause substantially the same as 52.232-35 that clearly informs the contractor where to send the EFT information [@CL-CTF-1573]. If payment under a written contract will be made by a charge to a Government account with a third party such as a Governmentwide commercial purchase card, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-36, Payment by Third Party, in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CTF-1546]. When the clause at 52.232-36 is included in a solicitation or contract, the contracting officer shall also insert the clause at 52.232-33 or the clause at 52.232-34 [@CL-CTF-1571], as appropriate [@CL-CTF-1572]. If the contract or agreement provides for the use of delivery orders and provides that the ordering office designate the method of payment for individual orders, the contracting officer shall insert in the solicitation and contract or agreement the clause at 52.232-37, and the printed paragraph goes on to list further clauses that this card does not restate [@CL-CTF-1547]. If the solicitation contains the clause at 52.232-34, Payment by Electronic Funds Transfer—Other Than System for Award Management, and an offeror is required to submit EFT information prior to award [@CL-CTF-1548], one listed action is that the contracting officer shall insert in the solicitation the provision at 52.232-38, Submission of Electronic Funds Transfer Information with Offer, or a provision substantially the same [@CL-CTF-1548; @CL-CTF-1549].

## How it works

Which EFT clause is listed depends first on the registration provision: the clause at 52.232-33 goes with the provision at 52.204-7 or an agency clause that requires SAM registration, unless one of the stated exceptions applies, and the clause at 52.232-34 goes with solicitations and contracts that require EFT but do not include them [@CL-CTF-1541; @CL-CTF-1542; @CL-CTF-1543; @CL-CTF-1544]. The offeror EFT information provision is listed for the case where the solicitation contains the clause at 52.232-34 and an offeror is required to submit EFT information prior to award [@CL-CTF-1548; @CL-CTF-1549]. The section also has paragraphs on the time allowed for submitting EFT information, on nondomestic EFT mechanisms, on identifying the EFT clause for each disbursing office, and on sealed bid handling of offeror EFT information, which this card does not restate.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.1110 keeps the triggers described above but states its insertion instructions as the bare imperative "Insert", without naming the contracting officer, where the codified text says the contracting officer shall insert [@CL-CTF-4303; @CL-CTF-4307; @CL-CTF-4309; @CL-CTF-4310; @CL-CTF-4311; @CL-CTF-4313; @CL-CTF-1541; @CL-CTF-1545; @CL-CTF-1546; @CL-CTF-1571; @CL-CTF-1547; @CL-CTF-1549]. The clause at 52.232-33 goes with the provision at 52.204-7 [@CL-CTF-4303] or an agency SAM registration clause, unless payment is through a third party arrangement or a 32.1103(a) through (i) exception applies [@CL-CTF-4304]. The clause at 52.232-34 goes with contracts that require EFT as the method for payment [@CL-CTF-4305] but do not include that provision or a similar agency clause [@CL-CTF-4306]. Clause 52.232-35, or a substantially similar clause, is used where EFT information is to be submitted to other than the payment office in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CTF-4307; @CL-CTF-4308]. Clause 52.232-36 is used where payment under a written contract will be made by a charge to a Government account with a third party [@CL-CTF-4309], with 52.232-33 or 52.232-34 as appropriate [@CL-CTF-4310]. Clause 52.232-37 is used where the contract or agreement provides for the use of delivery orders and provides that the ordering office designate the method of payment for individual orders [@CL-CTF-4311]. The offeror EFT information provision at 52.232-38 is used where the solicitation contains the clause at 52.232-34 and an offeror must submit EFT information before award [@CL-CTF-4312; @CL-CTF-4313].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a 600,000 supply contract includes the SAM registration provision. No third party arrangement is planned and no listed EFT exception applies, so the contracting officer inserts the EFT clause that goes with SAM registration.

Answer: the SAM-based EFT clause. If the solicitation lacked the registration provision and any similar agency clause but required EFT, the other EFT clause would be listed instead.

## Never confuse

The clause for payment by a third party is a different clause from the EFT clauses, although the same paragraph also calls for the clause at 52.232-33 or 52.232-34 to be inserted when it is used [@CL-CTF-1571]. The offeror EFT information provision is a solicitation provision, not a contract clause, and it is listed for a solicitation that contains the clause at 52.232-34 where an offeror is required to submit EFT information prior to award [@CL-CTF-1548; @CL-CTF-1549]. The rules on when the Government pays by EFT and by purchase card are on [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md). Invoice due dates are on [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md), and DoD electronic submission is on [DoD payment rules: prompt payment applicability, electronic submission and performance-based payment procedures](../billing-payment/ctf-dod-payment-applicability-and-electronic-submission.md).

The codified section says the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-33 [@CL-CTF-1541], while the RFO text gives the instruction "Insert the clause at—" without naming who inserts it [@CL-CTF-4303].
