## Purpose

Explains who may use a Progress Payments clause under FAR 32.502-1, the duty to reject a bid conditioned on progress payments that the solicitation did not offer, how FAR 32.503 treats postaward matters that come up before award, and how DFARS 232.501-2 sends approval of unusual progress payments to PGI procedures [@CL-CTF-3424; @CL-CTF-3425; @CL-CTF-3426; @CL-CTF-3427; @CL-CTF-3428]. The subpart covers contract financing through progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3441].

The contracting officer may use a Progress Payments clause in solicitations and contracts, in accordance with the subpart [@CL-CTF-3424]. The section sets no rate, threshold or deadline.

The contracting officer must reject as nonresponsive bids conditioned on progress payments when the solicitation did not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3425]. The duty falls on the contracting officer. The bidder's bid is the one rejected. The sentence speaks of bids and states no exception [@CL-CTF-3425].

The postaward section covers matters that are generally relevant only after award of a contract [@CL-CTF-3426]. It does not preclude taking the actions it discusses before award, if appropriate. A preaward review of accounting systems and controls is its example [@CL-CTF-3427]. The sentence does not say who decides when an earlier action is appropriate [@CL-CTF-3427].

DFARS 232.501-2 says to follow the procedures at PGI 232.501-2 for approval of unusual progress payments [@CL-CTF-3428]. The DFARS section does not say who approves or what the procedures are [@CL-CTF-3428].

## How it works

The subpart prescribes policies, procedures, forms, solicitation provisions and contract clauses for contract financing through progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3441]. It does not apply to payments under cost-reimbursement contracts, though FAR 32.110 covers progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts [@CL-CTF-3442]. It also does not apply to contracts for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration, or repair, when the contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3442; @CL-CTF-3443]. The [subcontractor payments under cost-reimbursement primes](ctf-subcontractor-payments-under-cost-reimbursement-primes.md) card covers the first exception.

The contracting officer may use the clause, in accordance with the subpart [@CL-CTF-3424]. A bid conditioned on progress payments is rejected as nonresponsive when the solicitation did not provide for them [@CL-CTF-3425].

Progress payments are customary or unusual. Customary payments use the rate, cost base and frequency set in the Progress Payments clause. Any other progress payments are unusual and may be used only in exceptional cases when authorized under FAR 32.501-2 [@CL-CTF-3548; @CL-CTF-3612]. DFARS 232.501-2 covers approval of the unusual kind through PGI [@CL-CTF-3428]. The conditions for unusual progress payments are on the [progress payment rates and unusual progress payments](ctf-progress-payment-rates.md) card.

From February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers use the RFO text of FAR part 32 in place of the codified text [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.502-1 has the same two sentences as the codified section [@CL-CTF-3437; @CL-CTF-3438; @CL-CTF-3424; @CL-CTF-3425]. RFO FAR 32.503 has the same two sentences apart from punctuation [@CL-CTF-3439; @CL-CTF-3440; @CL-CTF-3426; @CL-CTF-3427].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation does not provide for progress payments. One bidder conditions its bid on receiving progress payments of 150,000 a month. The contracting officer must reject that bid as nonresponsive, because the bid is conditioned on progress payments and the solicitation did not provide for them [@CL-CTF-3425]. The section states no exception to that duty [@CL-CTF-3425].

Separately, before award, a contracting officer considers a preaward review of the offeror's accounting systems and controls. The postaward section does not preclude that action before award, if appropriate [@CL-CTF-3427]. The section does not say who decides what is appropriate, so the example does not conclude that the review must happen [@CL-CTF-3427].

## Never confuse

The contracting officer may use the clause, but must reject a bid conditioned on progress payments that the solicitation did not provide for [@CL-CTF-3424; @CL-CTF-3425]. The first is permission and the second is a duty.

The postaward section is not limited to postaward use. It is generally relevant only after award, but its actions are not precluded before award, if appropriate [@CL-CTF-3426; @CL-CTF-3427].

DFARS 232.501-2 does not itself say how unusual progress payments are approved [@CL-CTF-3428]. It points to the PGI procedures [@CL-CTF-3428].

The [customary progress payment rates](ctf-progress-payments.md) and [progress payment administration](ctf-progress-payment-administration.md) cards cover the rates and the administration steps.
