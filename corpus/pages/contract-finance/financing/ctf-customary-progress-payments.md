## Purpose

Explains who may use a Progress Payments clause under FAR 32.502-1, the duty to reject a bid conditioned on progress payments that the solicitation did not offer, how FAR 32.503 treats postaward matters that come up before award, and how DFARS 232.501-2 sends approval of unusual progress payments to PGI procedures [@CL-CTF-3424; @CL-CTF-3425; @CL-CTF-3426; @CL-CTF-3427; @CL-CTF-3428]. The subpart covers contract financing through progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3441].

## How it works

The subpart prescribes policies, procedures, forms, solicitation provisions and contract clauses for contract financing through progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3441]. It does not apply to payments under cost-reimbursement contracts, but see FAR 32.110 for progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts [@CL-CTF-3442]. It also does not apply to contracts for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration, or repair, when the contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3442; @CL-CTF-3443]. See [subcontractor payments under cost-reimbursement primes](ctf-subcontractor-payments-under-cost-reimbursement-primes.md) for the progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts [@CL-CTF-3442].

The contracting officer may use a Progress Payments clause in solicitations and contracts, in accordance with the subpart [@CL-CTF-3424]. The contracting officer must reject as nonresponsive bids conditioned on progress payments when the solicitation did not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3425].

Progress payments may be customary or unusual [@CL-CTF-3548]. Customary progress payments are those made under the general guidance in the subpart, using the customary progress payment rate, the cost base, and frequency of payment established in the Progress Payments clause [@CL-CTF-3548], and either the ordinary liquidation method or the alternate method as provided in subsections 32.503-8 and 32.503-9 [@CL-CTF-3549]. Any other progress payments are considered unusual, and may be used only in exceptional cases when authorized in accordance with subsection 32.501-2 [@CL-CTF-3612]. DFARS 232.501-2 says to follow the procedures at PGI 232.501-2 for approval of unusual progress payments [@CL-CTF-3428]. The conditions for unusual progress payments are on the [progress payment rates and unusual progress payments](ctf-progress-payment-rates.md) card.

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.502-1 says the contracting officer may use a Progress Payments clause in solicitations and contracts, in accordance with this subpart [@CL-CTF-3437], and must reject as nonresponsive bids conditioned on progress payments when the solicitation did not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3438]. RFO FAR 32.503 says it covers matters that are generally relevant only after award of a contract [@CL-CTF-3439], and that this does not preclude taking actions discussed there before award, if appropriate, e.g., preaward review of accounting systems and controls [@CL-CTF-3440].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation does not provide for progress payments. One bidder conditions its bid on receiving progress payments of 150,000 a month. The contracting officer must reject that bid as nonresponsive, because the bid is conditioned on progress payments and the solicitation did not provide for them [@CL-CTF-3425]. The section states no exception to that duty [@CL-CTF-3425].

Separately, before award, a contracting officer considers a preaward review of the offeror's accounting systems and controls. The postaward section does not preclude that action before award, if appropriate [@CL-CTF-3427]. The section does not say who decides what is appropriate, so the example does not conclude that the review must happen [@CL-CTF-3427].

## Never confuse

The contracting officer may use a Progress Payments clause in solicitations and contracts, in accordance with the subpart [@CL-CTF-3424], and must reject as nonresponsive bids conditioned on progress payments when the solicitation did not provide for progress payments [@CL-CTF-3425].

FAR 32.503 covers matters that are generally relevant only after award of a contract [@CL-CTF-3426], and does not preclude taking actions discussed there before award, if appropriate [@CL-CTF-3427].

DFARS 232.501-2 says to follow the procedures at PGI 232.501-2 for approval of unusual progress payments [@CL-CTF-3428].

The [customary progress payment rates](ctf-progress-payments.md) and [progress payment administration](ctf-progress-payment-administration.md) cards cover the rates and the administration steps.
