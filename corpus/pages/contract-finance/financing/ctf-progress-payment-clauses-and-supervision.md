## Purpose

Names the progress payments clause, its alternates and when each is used, and states how closely the FAR says progress payments are supervised.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1526] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.502-4 and FAR 32.503-2 [@CL-CTF-1526; @CL-CTF-1529] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.502-4 [@CL-CTF-4406; @CL-CTF-4407; @CL-CTF-4408; @CL-CTF-4409; @CL-CTF-4410; @CL-CTF-4411] and RFO FAR 32.503-2 [@CL-CTF-4412; @CL-CTF-4413] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1526] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that may result in contracts providing for progress payments based on costs, and fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide them [@CL-CTF-1526] |

The FAR says to insert the clause at 52.232-16, Progress Payments, in solicitations that may result in contracts providing for progress payments based on costs and in fixed-price contracts under which the Government will provide progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-1526]. The clause is used with its Alternate I if the contractor is a small business concern and with its Alternate II if the contract is a letter contract [@CL-CTF-1526; @CL-CTF-1527]. It is used with its Alternate III if the contractor is not a small business concern and progress payments are authorized under an indefinite-delivery contract, basic ordering agreement, or their equivalent [@CL-CTF-1526; @CL-CTF-1528]. If advance agency approval has been given in accordance with 32.501-1, the contracting officer may substitute a different customary rate for other than small business concerns for the progress payment and liquidation rate indicated [@CL-CTF-1568]. If an unusual progress payment rate is approved for the prime contractor, the approved rate is substituted for the customary rate in paragraphs (a)(1), (a)(6), and (b) of the clause [@CL-CTF-1569]. If the liquidation rate is changed from the customary progress payment rate, the new rate is substituted for the rate in those same paragraphs of the clause [@CL-CTF-1570].

Supervision of progress payments, by prepayment review or periodic review, should vary inversely with the contractor's experience, performance record, reliability, quality of management, and financial strength, and with the adequacy of the contractor's accounting system and controls [@CL-CTF-1529]. The administering office must keep itself informed of the contractor's overall operations and financial condition [@CL-CTF-1530].

## How it works

The clause choice follows the contract and the contractor: the base clause for progress payments based on costs, with an alternate for small business concerns, letter contracts, or indefinite-delivery type arrangements [@CL-CTF-1526; @CL-CTF-1527; @CL-CTF-1528]. The extent of progress payments supervision, by prepayment review or periodic review, should vary inversely with the contractor's experience, performance record, reliability, quality of management, and financial strength, and with the adequacy of the contractor's accounting system and controls [@CL-CTF-1529]. Progress payments based on costs are one of the contract financing payments listed in [FAR 32 definitions and contract financing payment timing](ctf-far-32-definitions-and-financing-payment-timing.md) [@CL-CTF-1501], and they are one of the financing methods described on [Contract financing methods and payment clauses for noncommercial purchases](ctf-financing-methods-and-noncommercial-payment-clauses.md) [@CL-CTF-1511]. The approval and postpayment review steps are on [Progress payment administration and accounting system reviews](ctf-progress-payment-administration.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.502-4 keeps the instruction to insert the Progress Payments clause in the same solicitations and contracts [@CL-CTF-4406], the substitution of a different customary rate after advance agency approval [@CL-CTF-4407], the substitution of an approved unusual rate or a changed liquidation rate in the same clause paragraphs [@CL-CTF-4408; @CL-CTF-4409] and the alternates for small business concerns, letter contracts and indefinite-delivery type arrangements [@CL-CTF-4410; @CL-CTF-4411]. RFO FAR 32.503-2 keeps the rule that supervision should vary inversely with the contractor's experience, record and systems [@CL-CTF-4412] and the duty of the administering office to keep itself informed [@CL-CTF-4413]. Under RFO FAR 32.001, contract financing payments may include advance payments, performance-based payments, commercial advance, installment, and interim payments, and progress payments based on cost under the clause at FAR 52.232-16, Progress Payments [@CL-CTF-4414; @CL-CTF-4319; @CL-CTF-4320], and RFO FAR 32.102(b) describes progress payments based on costs as made on the basis of costs incurred by the contractor as work progresses under the contract [@CL-CTF-4415].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price contract worth 5,000,000 will provide progress payments based on costs to a small business concern. The base progress payments clause goes in, and because the contractor is a small business concern it is used with its first alternate.

Answer: the first alternate applies. A contractor with a long record and strong controls would be supervised less closely than a new contractor with weak controls.

## Never confuse

FAR 32.502-4 prescribes Alternate I for a small business concern, Alternate II for a letter contract, and Alternate III when the contractor is not a small business concern and progress payments are authorized under an indefinite-delivery contract, basic ordering agreement, or their equivalent [@CL-CTF-1527; @CL-CTF-1528], and provides for substituting an approved different customary rate, an approved unusual progress payment rate, or a changed liquidation rate in the clause [@CL-CTF-1568; @CL-CTF-1569; @CL-CTF-1570]. Suspension or reduction of payments is on [Suspension or reduction of progress payments](ctf-suspension-or-reduction-of-progress-payments.md), liquidation is on [Progress payment liquidation](ctf-progress-payment-liquidation.md), customary rates are on [Customary progress payment rates (DoD)](ctf-progress-payments.md), and accounting system tests are on [Contractor accounting system criteria](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-accounting-system-criteria.md).
