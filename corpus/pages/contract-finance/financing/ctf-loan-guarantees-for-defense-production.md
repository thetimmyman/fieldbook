## Purpose

Shows what the loan guarantee subpart means by a guaranteed loan, a borrower and a guaranteeing agency, which agencies are designated to guarantee loans, and how a subcontractor's weak finances can be handled with progress payments from the contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3541] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.301, FAR 32.302 and FAR 32.306 [@CL-CTF-3541; @CL-CTF-3544; @CL-CTF-3546] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3541] |
| Applies to | Guaranteed loans made through Federal Reserve Banks for defense production, including loans that concern a subcontractor [@CL-CTF-3543; @CL-CTF-3544; @CL-CTF-3546] |

As used in the subpart, a borrower is a contractor, subcontractor at any tier, or other supplier who receives a guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-3541]. A guaranteed loan, or V loan, is a loan, revolving credit fund, or other financial arrangement made pursuant to Regulation V of the Federal Reserve Board, under which the guaranteeing agency is obligated, on demand of the lender, to purchase a stated percentage of the loan [@CL-CTF-3542]. The guaranteeing agency is also obligated to share any losses in the amount of the guaranteed percentage [@CL-CTF-3611]. The Federal Reserve Board is the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System [@CL-CTF-3610]. A guaranteeing agency is any agency that the President has authorized to guarantee loans, through Federal Reserve Banks, for expediting national defense production [@CL-CTF-3543].

Congress has authorized Federal Reserve Banks to act, on behalf of guaranteeing agencies, as fiscal agents of the United States in the making of loan guarantees for defense production [@CL-CTF-3544]. The President has designated as guaranteeing agencies the Departments of Defense, Energy, Commerce, the Interior and Agriculture, the General Services Administration and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration [@CL-CTF-3545].

If the request for a loan guarantee concerns a subcontractor that is financially weak in comparison with its contractor, the Government's interests may be fostered by the contractor making progress payments to the subcontractor [@CL-CTF-3546]. In that case the agency shall try to arrange for the contractor to provide the progress payments, and as a result the need for the loan guarantee may be reduced or eliminated and the contractor would bear part or all of the risk of loss arising from the selection of the subcontractor [@CL-CTF-3547].

## How it works

A guarantee is made through a Federal Reserve Bank acting as fiscal agent for a guaranteeing agency, and only an agency the President has authorized can be a guaranteeing agency [@CL-CTF-3544; @CL-CTF-3543]. The designated agencies are listed in the subpart [@CL-CTF-3545].

The section places on the agency the duty to try to arrange progress payments from the contractor when a loan guarantee request concerns a financially weak subcontractor [@CL-CTF-3547]. The duty to try arises where the Government's interests may be fostered by that arrangement [@CL-CTF-3546; @CL-CTF-3547]. The text says the need for the guarantee may be reduced or eliminated, not that it will be [@CL-CTF-3547]. The application route and the other guarantee terms are covered in [Defense production loan guarantees](ctf-defense-production-loan-guarantees.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subcontractor that is financially weak in comparison with its contractor requests a guaranteed loan of 5 million [@CL-CTF-3546]. If the Government's interests may be fostered by the contractor making progress payments to the subcontractor, the agency shall try to arrange for the contractor to provide them [@CL-CTF-3546; @CL-CTF-3547]. If that succeeds, the need for the guarantee may be reduced or eliminated, and the contractor would bear part or all of the risk of loss arising from its selection of the subcontractor [@CL-CTF-3547]. If a guarantee is made, the subcontractor is a borrower, and the guaranteeing agency is obligated, on demand of the lender, to purchase the stated percentage of the loan and to share losses in that percentage [@CL-CTF-3541; @CL-CTF-3542; @CL-CTF-3611].

## Never confuse

A loan guarantee is not a progress payment. Under a guaranteed loan the guaranteeing agency is obligated to the lender to purchase a stated percentage of the loan [@CL-CTF-3542], while the progress payments in the subcontract case are paid by the contractor to the subcontractor [@CL-CTF-3546].

A guaranteeing agency is also not a Federal Reserve Bank. The bank acts as fiscal agent on behalf of the agency [@CL-CTF-3543; @CL-CTF-3544]. The Federal Reserve Board, under whose Regulation V a guaranteed loan is made, is a different body from the Federal Reserve Banks [@CL-CTF-3542; @CL-CTF-3610; @CL-CTF-3544].
