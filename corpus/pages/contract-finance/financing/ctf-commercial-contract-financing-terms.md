## Purpose

Shows the statutory authority for commercial payment terms, how market research can cover financing terms, and how the contracting officer settles the financing terms for a commercial purchase.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3535] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.201, FAR 32.202-3 and FAR 32.203 [@CL-CTF-3535; @CL-CTF-3536; @CL-CTF-3539] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.201 [@CL-CTF-4212; @CL-CTF-4213], RFO FAR 32.202-3 [@CL-CTF-4214; @CL-CTF-4215; @CL-CTF-4216; @CL-CTF-4217] and RFO FAR 32.203 [@CL-CTF-4218; @CL-CTF-4219] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3535] |
| Applies to | Payment for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CTF-3535] |

The statutes provide that payment for commercial products or commercial services may be made under such terms and conditions as the head of the agency determines are appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace and are in the best interest of the United States [@CL-CTF-3535].

Contract financing may be a subject included in the market research conducted in accordance with part 10 [@CL-CTF-3536]. If market research for contract financing is conducted, the contracting officer should consider the extent to which other buyers provide contract financing for purchases in that market and the overall level of financing normally provided [@CL-CTF-3537]. The contracting officer should also consider the amount or percentages of any payments equivalent to commercial advance payments, and the basis for any payments equivalent to commercial interim payments, with their frequency and amounts or percentages [@CL-CTF-3538]. The listed matters further include methods of liquidation of contract financing payments and any special or unusual payment terms applicable to delivery payments [@CL-CTF-3609].

When the criteria in FAR 32.202-1(b) are met, the contracting officer may either specify the financing terms in the solicitation or permit each offeror to propose its own customary financing terms [@CL-CTF-3539]. When the contracting officer has sufficient information on financing terms that are customary in the commercial marketplace for the item, those terms may be specified in the solicitation [@CL-CTF-3540].

Commercial interim payments and commercial advance payments may be made under the following circumstances, and because the last two are joined by "; and", all of them apply [@CL-CTF-0260; @CL-CTF-9592]. They include that the item financed is a commercial supply or service and that the contract price exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CTF-0260]. They also include that the contracting officer determines it is appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace to make financing payments for the item and that this form of financing is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-0527]. They also include that adequate security is obtained [@CL-CTF-0528], and that commercial advance payments before any performance of work do not exceed 15 percent of the contract price in the aggregate [@CL-CTF-0261]. They include that the contract is awarded on the basis of competitive procedures or, if only one offer is solicited, adequate consideration is obtained (based on the time value of the additional financing to be provided) if the financing is expected to be substantially more advantageous to the offeror than the offeror's normal method of customer financing [@CL-CTF-0529; @CL-CTF-0540]. They also include that the contracting officer obtains concurrence from the payment office concerning liquidation provisions when required by FAR 32.206(e) [@CL-CTF-0530]. Any contract financing arrangement not in accord with the requirements of agency regulations or the part is unusual contract financing and requires advance approval in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3614], and if not otherwise specified such unusual contract financing shall be approved by the head of the contracting activity [@CL-CTF-3615].

## How it works

The head of the agency determines which terms are appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace under the statutes [@CL-CTF-3535]. The contracting officer determines, as one of the criteria in FAR 32.202-1(b), that it is appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace to make financing payments for the item [@CL-CTF-0527].

Contract financing may be a subject included in the market research conducted in accordance with part 10, and if market research for contract financing is conducted, the contracting officer should consider the extent to which other buyers provide contract financing for purchases in that market and the overall level of financing normally provided [@CL-CTF-3536; @CL-CTF-3537]. When the criteria in FAR 32.202-1(b) are met, the contracting officer may either specify the financing terms in the solicitation or permit each offeror to propose its own customary financing terms [@CL-CTF-3539]. When the contracting officer has sufficient information on financing terms that are customary in the commercial marketplace for the item, those terms may be specified in the solicitation [@CL-CTF-3540].

Under FAR 32.204, the financing terms shall be included in the solicitation, contract financing shall not be a factor in the evaluation of resulting proposals, and proposals of alternative financing terms shall not be accepted (but see 14.208 and 15.206 concerning amendments of solicitations) [@CL-CTF-3069]. Under FAR 32.205(a), each offeror may propose financing terms, and the contracting officer must then determine which offer is in the best interests of the United States [@CL-CTF-1417]. See [Contracting officer-specified financing for commercial purchases](ctf-commercial-financing-terms.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.201(a) keeps the statutory rule that payment for commercial products or commercial services may be made under such terms and conditions as the head of the agency determines are appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace and are in the best interest of the United States [@CL-CTF-4212]. RFO FAR 32.202-3 keeps the rule that contract financing may be a subject included in the market research conducted in accordance with part 10 [@CL-CTF-4214], with the same matters to consider: the extent of other buyers’ financing and the overall level of financing [@CL-CTF-4215], payments equivalent to commercial advance and interim payments [@CL-CTF-4216], and methods of liquidation and special or unusual delivery payment terms [@CL-CTF-4217]. RFO FAR 32.203 keeps the choice, when the criteria in 32.202-1(b) are met, between specifying the financing terms in the solicitation and permitting each offeror to propose its own customary financing terms [@CL-CTF-4218], and the rule that terms may be specified when the contracting officer has sufficient information on customary terms [@CL-CTF-4219]. One addition: RFO FAR 32.201(b) states that section 3324(d)(2) of title 31 of the United States Code authorizes advance payments for subscriptions or other charges for newspapers, magazines, periodicals, or other publications [@CL-CTF-4213].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Market research shows that buyers in the market normally pay a 10 percent advance before work starts and recover it from the delivery payments. The item is a commercial supply, its price exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, the contract will be awarded competitively, and the other criteria are met [@CL-CTF-0260; @CL-CTF-0529]. The advance of 10 percent stays below the 15 percent aggregate ceiling [@CL-CTF-0261]. The contracting officer has sufficient information that these terms are customary for the item, so the terms may be specified in the solicitation [@CL-CTF-3540]. The contracting officer may instead permit each offeror to propose its own customary financing terms [@CL-CTF-3539]. Had buyers normally paid a 20 percent advance before work starts, that arrangement would exceed the 15 percent aggregate ceiling, would not be in accord with the part, and would be unusual contract financing needing advance approval [@CL-CTF-0261; @CL-CTF-3614]. If the terms are specified, contract financing is not a factor in evaluating the proposals [@CL-CTF-3069].

## Never confuse

Subpart 32.2, Commercial Product and Commercial Service Purchase Financing, is applicable only to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under authority of part 12, while the subparts listed in FAR 32.002(c), including Subpart 32.1 and Subpart 32.4, are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512].

The head of the agency determines which terms are appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace under the statutes [@CL-CTF-3535], and the contracting officer determines, as one of the criteria, that it is appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace to make financing payments for the item [@CL-CTF-0527].

Contract financing may be a subject included in the market research conducted in accordance with part 10 [@CL-CTF-3536].

RFO FAR 32.201(b) states that section 3324(d)(2) of title 31 of the United States Code authorizes advance payments for subscriptions or other charges for newspapers, magazines, periodicals, or other publications [@CL-CTF-4213], and RFO FAR 32.201(a) states the commercial payment terms rule [@CL-CTF-4212].
