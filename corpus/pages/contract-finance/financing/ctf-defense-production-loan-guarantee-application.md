## Purpose

Explains how a defense production loan guarantee is applied for, how the asset formula sizes the guarantee, and what changes its amount or maturity.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3073] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.304-1, FAR 32.304-3 and FAR 32.304-4 [@CL-CTF-3073; @CL-CTF-3075; @CL-CTF-3174] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.303(f) [@CL-CTF-4528; @CL-CTF-4542]; RFO FAR 32.304 is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4527] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3073] |
| Applies to | Guaranteed loans for contractors, subcontractors and suppliers performing contracts related to national defense [@CL-CTF-3073] |

A contractor, subcontractor or supplier that needs operating funds to perform a contract related to national defense may apply to a financing institution for a loan [@CL-CTF-3073]. If the institution is willing to extend credit but considers a Government guarantee necessary, it may apply to the Federal Reserve Bank of its district for the guarantee, and application forms and guidance are available at all Federal Reserve Banks [@CL-CTF-3167]. The Reserve Bank will promptly send a copy of the application, including a list of the relevant defense contracts held by the contractor, to the Federal Reserve Board [@CL-CTF-3168]. The Board will transmit the application and the list to the interested guaranteeing agency, so that the agency can determine the contractor's eligibility [@CL-CTF-3074].

To expedite the process, the Reserve Bank may, pursuant to instructions of a guaranteeing agency, submit lists of the defense contracts to the interested contracting officers [@CL-CTF-3169]. While eligibility is being determined, the Reserve Bank will make any necessary credit investigations to supplement the information furnished by the applicant institution, in order to expedite necessary defense financing and to protect the Government against monetary loss [@CL-CTF-3170]. The Reserve Bank will send its report and recommendation to the Board, and the Board will transmit them to the interested guaranteeing agency [@CL-CTF-3171].

Under guaranteed loans made primarily for working capital purposes, the agency shall normally limit the guarantee, by use of an asset formula, to an amount that does not exceed a specified percentage (90 percent or less) of the contractor's investment (e.g., payrolls and inventories) in defense production contracts [@CL-CTF-9594; @CL-CTF-3075]. The asset formula may include all items under defense contracts for which the contractor would be entitled to payment on performance or termination [@CL-CTF-3172]. The formula shall exclude amounts for which the contractor has not done any work or made any expenditure, amounts that would become due as the result of later performance under the contracts, and cash collateral or bank deposit balances [@CL-CTF-3076]. Progress payments are deducted from the asset formula [@CL-CTF-3077]. The agency may relax the asset formula to an appropriate extent for the time actually necessary for contract performance, if the contractor's working capital and credit are inadequate [@CL-CTF-3173].

The agency may change the guarantee amount or maturity date, within the limitations of the asset formula section, in two cases [@CL-CTF-3174]. If the contractor enters into additional defense production contracts after the application but before authorization of the guarantee, the agency may adjust the guarantee amount or maturity date to meet any significant increase in financing need [@CL-CTF-3078]. If the contractor enters into defense production contracts during the term of the guaranteed loan, the parties may adjust the existing guarantee agreement to provide for financing the new contracts [@CL-CTF-3079]. In that case pertinent information and the Federal Reserve Bank reports are submitted to the guaranteeing agency under the original application procedures, and normally a new certificate of eligibility is required [@CL-CTF-3175].

## How it works

A contractor, subcontractor or supplier that needs operating funds may apply to a financing institution for a loan, and the institution may apply to the Federal Reserve Bank of its district for a guarantee [@CL-CTF-3073; @CL-CTF-3167]. The Reserve Bank will send the application and the list of contracts to the Federal Reserve Board, and the Board will transmit them to the interested guaranteeing agency, so that the agency can determine the eligibility of the contractor [@CL-CTF-3168; @CL-CTF-3074]. For loans made primarily for working capital purposes, the agency shall normally limit the guarantee by use of an asset formula [@CL-CTF-9594; @CL-CTF-3075]. The agency may change the guarantee amount or maturity date, within the limitations at 32.304-3, as described in 32.304-4 [@CL-CTF-3174; @CL-CTF-3078; @CL-CTF-3079].

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.304 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-CTF-4527]; the codified text prints the application route in FAR 32.304-1 [@CL-CTF-3073], the asset formula in FAR 32.304-3 [@CL-CTF-3075] and the guarantee amount and maturity adjustments in FAR 32.304-4 [@CL-CTF-3174]. RFO FAR 32.303(f) says the guaranteeing agency is responsible for certifying eligibility for the guarantee and fixing the maximum dollar amount and maturity date of the guaranteed loan to meet the contractor’s requirement for financing performance of the defense production contract on hand at the time the guarantee application is submitted [@CL-CTF-4528; @CL-CTF-4542], as codified FAR 32.303(g) says [@CL-CTF-1424; @CL-CTF-1461].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has 2,000,000 of items under defense contracts that the asset formula can include, after leaving out amounts for work not yet done, amounts that would become due as the result of later performance, and cash collateral or bank deposit balances [@CL-CTF-3172; @CL-CTF-3076]. The agency applies a 90 percent formula, so the formula amount before any progress payment deduction is 1,800,000 [@CL-CTF-3075]. Progress payments the contractor has received are also deducted from the asset formula [@CL-CTF-3077], so, unless the agency relaxes the formula because the contractor's working capital and credit are inadequate [@CL-CTF-3173], the limit is lower than 1,800,000, and the section does not give the order of the arithmetic. Before authorization, the contractor wins a further defense production contract, and the agency may adjust the amount or maturity date to meet a significant increase in financing need [@CL-CTF-3078]. If the contractor wins a contract during the loan term, the parties may adjust the existing agreement, and normally a new certificate of eligibility is required [@CL-CTF-3079; @CL-CTF-3175]. This example uses the codified text only, and RFO FAR 32.304 is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4527].

## Never confuse

A contractor, subcontractor or supplier that needs operating funds may apply to a financing institution for a loan, and a financing institution that considers a Government guarantee necessary may apply to the Federal Reserve Bank of its district for the guarantee [@CL-CTF-3073; @CL-CTF-3167]. Progress payments are deducted from the asset formula [@CL-CTF-3077]. The [defense production loan guarantees](ctf-defense-production-loan-guarantees.md) card covers the rest of the regime, and the [surety, other borrowing and terminated contracts](ctf-defense-production-loan-guarantee-surety-and-termination.md) card covers the conditions that follow.

Under codified FAR 32.304-3 the agency shall normally limit a guarantee for a loan made primarily for working capital purposes with an asset formula of 90 percent or less of the contractor's investment [@CL-CTF-3075; @CL-CTF-9594], and under FAR 32.304-4 the agency may change the guarantee amount or maturity date, within the limitations at 32.304-3, as described there [@CL-CTF-3174]. RFO FAR 32.304 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-CTF-4527].
