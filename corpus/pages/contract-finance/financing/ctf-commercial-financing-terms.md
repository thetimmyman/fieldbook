## Purpose

Explains how contracting officer-specified financing works for commercial product and commercial service purchases: what a commercial advance payment is, how the solicitation records the terms, and who approves and monitors each request.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3067] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.202-2, FAR 32.204 and FAR 32.207 [@CL-CTF-3067; @CL-CTF-3069; @CL-CTF-3163] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.202-2 [@CL-CTF-4235; @CL-CTF-4236; @CL-CTF-4237], RFO FAR 32.204 [@CL-CTF-4238; @CL-CTF-4239; @CL-CTF-4240; @CL-CTF-4241] and RFO FAR 32.207 [@CL-CTF-4242; @CL-CTF-4243; @CL-CTF-4244; @CL-CTF-4245; @CL-CTF-4246] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3067] |
| Applies to | Commercial product and commercial service purchases financed on contracting officer-specified terms [@CL-CTF-3067; @CL-CTF-3069] |

As used in FAR 32.202-2, a commercial advance payment is a payment made before any performance of work under the contract, and the aggregate of these payments shall not exceed 15 percent of the contract price [@CL-CTF-3067]. These payments are contract financing payments for prompt payment purposes (i.e., not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act in accordance with subpart 32.9), and they are not subject to subpart 32.4, Advance Payments for Other Than Commercial Acquisitions [@CL-CTF-3068]. For commercial interim payment and delivery payment, the section refers to FAR 32.001 [@CL-CTF-3161].

The financing terms shall be included in the solicitation [@CL-CTF-3069]. Contract financing shall not be a factor in the evaluation of resulting proposals, and proposals of alternative financing terms shall not be accepted (but see 14.208 and 15.206 concerning amendments of solicitations) [@CL-CTF-3069]. However, an offer stating that the contracting officer-specified contract financing terms will not be used by the offeror does not alter the evaluation of the offer, nor does it render the offer nonresponsive or otherwise unacceptable [@CL-CTF-3162]. In the event of award to an offeror who declined the proposed contract financing, the contract financing provisions shall not be included in the resulting contract [@CL-CTF-3070]. Contract financing shall not be a basis for adjusting offerors' proposed prices, because the effect of contract financing is reflected in each offeror's proposed prices [@CL-CTF-3071].

The contracting officer responsible for administration of the contract is responsible for review and approval of contract financing requests [@CL-CTF-3163]. Unless otherwise provided in agency regulations, or by agreement with the appropriate payment official, the contracting officer shall be responsible for receiving, approving, and transmitting all contract financing requests to the appropriate payment office [@CL-CTF-3164], and each approval shall specify the amount to be paid, necessary contractual information, and the accounts to be charged for the payment [@CL-CTF-3072].

After contract award, the contracting officer responsible for approving requests for financing payments is responsible for determining that the security continues to be adequate [@CL-CTF-3165]. If the contractor's financial condition is the Government's security, that officer is also responsible for monitoring the contractor's financial condition [@CL-CTF-3166].

## How it works

The terms are set in the solicitation, and their effect is reflected in each offeror's proposed prices [@CL-CTF-3069; @CL-CTF-3071]. An offer stating that the contracting officer-specified financing terms will not be used does not alter the evaluation of the offer, and in the event of award to an offeror who declined the proposed contract financing, the contract financing provisions shall not be included in the resulting contract [@CL-CTF-3162; @CL-CTF-3070]. The contracting officer responsible for administration of the contract shall be responsible for review and approval of contract financing requests [@CL-CTF-3163], and after contract award the contracting officer responsible for approving requests for financing payments shall be responsible for determining that the security continues to be adequate [@CL-CTF-3165].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.202-2 keeps the definition: a commercial advance payment is made before any performance of work, and the aggregate of these payments must not exceed 15 percent of the contract price [@CL-CTF-4235]; these payments are contract financing payments not subject to the Prompt Payment Act interest penalty or to subpart 32.4 [@CL-CTF-4236]; and commercial interim payment and delivery payment are as defined in 32.001 [@CL-CTF-4237]. RFO FAR 32.204 keeps the procedure: the financing terms must be included in the solicitation, contract financing must not be a factor in the evaluation of resulting proposals, and proposals of alternative financing terms must not be accepted (but see 14.208 and 15.206 concerning amendments of solicitations) [@CL-CTF-4238]; an offer that declines the terms does not alter the evaluation and is not nonresponsive or otherwise unacceptable [@CL-CTF-4239]; the contract financing provisions must not be included in the contract awarded to an offeror who declined the proposed financing [@CL-CTF-4240]; and contract financing must not be a basis for adjusting offerors’ proposed prices, because its effect is reflected in each offeror’s proposed prices [@CL-CTF-4241]. RFO FAR 32.207 keeps the administration rules: the contracting officer responsible for administration of the contract is responsible for review and approval of contract financing requests [@CL-CTF-4242]; unless otherwise provided in agency regulations or by agreement with the designated payment official, the contracting officer is responsible for receiving, approving, and transmitting all contract financing requests to the designated payment office [@CL-CTF-4243], and each approval must specify the amount to be paid, necessary contractual information, and the accounts to be charged [@CL-CTF-4244]; and after contract award the contracting officer responsible for approving requests for financing payments is responsible for determining that the security continues to be adequate [@CL-CTF-4245] and, if the contractor’s financial condition is the Government’s security, for monitoring it [@CL-CTF-4246]. One difference: the codified text refers to the appropriate payment official and office [@CL-CTF-3164], where the RFO text refers to the designated payment official and office [@CL-CTF-4243].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial purchase has a price of 400,000. The solicitation offers commercial advance payments, and their aggregate may not exceed 60,000, which is 15 percent of the price [@CL-CTF-3067]. One offeror states that it will not use the financing. Its statement does not alter the evaluation of its offer and does not make the offer nonresponsive or otherwise unacceptable [@CL-CTF-3162], and if it wins, the contract is awarded without the financing provisions [@CL-CTF-3070]. No offeror's price is adjusted for the financing [@CL-CTF-3071]. After award, the administering contracting officer approves a first request for 40,000. Unless agency regulations or an agreement with the payment official provide otherwise, the officer transmits it to the payment office [@CL-CTF-3164], and the approval states the amount, the necessary contractual information and the accounts to be charged [@CL-CTF-3072]. Total payments of 40,000 stay within the cap of 60,000 [@CL-CTF-3067].

## Never confuse

Commercial advance payments are not subject to subpart 32.4, Advance Payments for Other Than Commercial Acquisitions [@CL-CTF-3068]. A commercial advance payment is a payment made before any performance of work under the contract, and the aggregate of these payments shall not exceed 15 percent of the contract price [@CL-CTF-3067]. Contract financing shall not be a factor in the evaluation of resulting proposals [@CL-CTF-3069], and an offer stating that the contracting officer-specified contract financing terms will not be used does not alter the evaluation of the offer [@CL-CTF-3162]. The [commercial purchase financing](ctf-contract-financing-for-commercial-purchases.md) and [security and clauses](ctf-commercial-financing-security-and-clauses.md) cards carry the detail.
