## Purpose

Explains how contracting officer-specified financing works for commercial product and commercial service purchases: what a commercial advance payment is, how the solicitation records the terms, and who approves and monitors each request.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3067] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.202-2, FAR 32.204 and FAR 32.207 [@CL-CTF-3067; @CL-CTF-3069; @CL-CTF-3163] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3067] |
| Applies to | Commercial product and commercial service purchases financed on contracting officer-specified terms [@CL-CTF-3067; @CL-CTF-3069] |

As used in FAR 32.202-2, a commercial advance payment is a payment made before any performance of work under the contract, and the aggregate of these payments may not exceed 15 percent of the contract price [@CL-CTF-3067]. These payments are contract financing payments for prompt payment purposes, so they are not subject to the interest penalty of the Prompt Payment Act, and they are not subject to the subpart on advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions [@CL-CTF-3068]. For commercial interim payment and delivery payment, the section refers to FAR 32.001 [@CL-CTF-3161].

The financing terms are included in the solicitation. Contract financing is not a factor in evaluating the resulting proposals, and proposals of alternative financing terms are not accepted, with the text referring to FAR 14.208 and FAR 15.206 for amendments of solicitations [@CL-CTF-3069]. An offer stating that the offeror will not use the contracting officer-specified financing terms does not alter the evaluation and does not make the offer nonresponsive or otherwise unacceptable [@CL-CTF-3162]. If award goes to an offeror that declined the proposed financing, the financing provisions are not included in the resulting contract [@CL-CTF-3070]. Contract financing is not a basis for adjusting offerors' proposed prices, because its effect is already reflected in each offeror's proposed prices [@CL-CTF-3071].

The contracting officer responsible for administration of the contract is responsible for review and approval of contract financing requests [@CL-CTF-3163]. Unless agency regulations or an agreement with the appropriate payment official provide otherwise, the contracting officer is responsible for receiving, approving and transmitting all contract financing requests to the appropriate payment office [@CL-CTF-3164], and each approval specifies the amount to be paid, the necessary contractual information and the accounts to be charged [@CL-CTF-3072].

After contract award, the contracting officer responsible for approving requests for financing payments is responsible for determining that the security continues to be adequate [@CL-CTF-3165]. If the contractor's financial condition is the Government's security, that officer is also responsible for monitoring the contractor's financial condition [@CL-CTF-3166].

## How it works

The terms are set in the solicitation, and their effect is reflected in each offeror's proposed prices [@CL-CTF-3069; @CL-CTF-3071]. An offeror that does not want the financing may say so without a change to its evaluation, and the financing provisions are then left out of its contract [@CL-CTF-3162; @CL-CTF-3070]. After award, the administering contracting officer reviews and approves each request [@CL-CTF-3163], and the officer who approves requests also determines that the security remains adequate [@CL-CTF-3165].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial purchase has a price of 400,000. The solicitation offers commercial advance payments, and their aggregate may not exceed 60,000, which is 15 percent of the price [@CL-CTF-3067]. One offeror states that it will not use the financing. Its statement does not alter the evaluation of its offer and does not make the offer nonresponsive or otherwise unacceptable [@CL-CTF-3162], and if it wins, the contract is awarded without the financing provisions [@CL-CTF-3070]. No offeror's price is adjusted for the financing [@CL-CTF-3071]. After award, the administering contracting officer approves a first request for 40,000. Unless agency regulations or an agreement with the payment official provide otherwise, the officer transmits it to the payment office [@CL-CTF-3164], and the approval states the amount, the necessary contractual information and the accounts to be charged [@CL-CTF-3072]. Total payments of 40,000 stay within the cap of 60,000 [@CL-CTF-3067].

## Never confuse

A commercial advance payment is not an advance payment under the subpart for other than commercial acquisitions. It is made before any performance, its aggregate is capped against the contract price, and it is outside the prompt payment interest penalty [@CL-CTF-3067; @CL-CTF-3068]. Contract financing is also not an evaluation factor, and an offeror's choice to decline it does not change the evaluation [@CL-CTF-3069; @CL-CTF-3162]. The [commercial purchase financing](ctf-contract-financing-for-commercial-purchases.md) and [security and clauses](ctf-commercial-financing-security-and-clauses.md) cards carry the detail.
