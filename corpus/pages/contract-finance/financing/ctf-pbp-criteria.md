## Purpose

Explains how performance-based payments are tied to defined events or performance criteria, capped against the contract price, and recovered from delivery payments.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0108] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1004 [@CL-CTF-0108] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1004 [@CL-CTF-4379; @CL-CTF-4380; @CL-CTF-4381; @CL-CTF-4382; @CL-CTF-4383; @CL-CTF-4384; @CL-CTF-4385; @CL-CTF-4386] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0108] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments made on a whole contract basis or on a deliverable item basis [@CL-CTF-0108] |

Payments may be made on a whole contract basis or on a deliverable item basis, unless agency regulations prescribe otherwise [@CL-CTF-0108]. The basis for a payment may be a specifically described event, such as a milestone, or a measurable criterion of performance [@CL-CTF-0109].

Total performance-based payments shall meet both of two requirements: they reflect prudent contract financing provided only to the extent needed for contract performance [@CL-CTF-9597], and they do not exceed 90 percent of the contract price on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-0112].

## How it works

Each event or criterion that triggers a payment must be an integral and necessary part of contract performance and must be identified in the contract, along with a description of what constitutes successful performance [@CL-CTF-0489]. The Government must be able to readily verify successful performance of each event or criterion, although an event need not be a critical event to trigger a payment [@CL-CTF-0111].

Signing a contract or modification, exercising an option, and the passage of time cannot serve as events or criteria [@CL-CTF-0110].

Amounts are recovered by deducting a percentage or a designated dollar amount from the delivery payments [@CL-CTF-0113], and the method of liquidation must ensure complete liquidation no later than final payment [@CL-CTF-0490].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO section 32.1004 keeps payment on a whole contract or deliverable item basis unless agency regulations prescribe otherwise [@CL-CTF-4379], and a basis of specifically described events or a measurable criterion of performance [@CL-CTF-4380]. It keeps the rule that each triggering event or criterion must be an integral and necessary part of performance and identified in the contract [@CL-CTF-4381], and that signing a contract or modification, exercising an option and the passage of time cannot serve as events or criteria [@CL-CTF-4382]. It keeps the limit that total performance-based payments must not exceed 90 percent of the contract price if on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price if on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-4383; @CL-CTF-4384], liquidation by deducting a percentage or designated dollar amount from delivery payments [@CL-CTF-4385], and complete liquidation no later than final payment [@CL-CTF-4386].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract price is 1,000,000, with one milestone worth 200,000 and a liquidation rate of 20 percent set at award. At the milestone the Government pays 200,000. A later delivery invoice of 100,000 is reduced by 20,000, so 80,000 is paid, and the financing is fully recovered by final payment.

## Never confuse

This page covers the procedure for setting up performance-based payments: the payment basis, the payment amounts and their liquidation [@CL-CTF-0108; @CL-CTF-0112; @CL-CTF-0113]. It is not the contract clause, which is covered on the [Performance-Based Payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md) page.

Progress payments are computed from costs incurred [@CL-CTF-0102], while performance-based payments rest on specifically described events or a measurable criterion of performance [@CL-CTF-0109]. Both are recovered by deduction: progress payments are liquidated by deducting from any payment under the contract other than advance or progress payments, except as provided in the Termination for Convenience of the Government clause [@CL-CTF-0104], and performance-based amounts by deducting a percentage or a designated dollar amount from the delivery payments [@CL-CTF-0113]; see [Progress payments](ctf-progress-payments.md).
