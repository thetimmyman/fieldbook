## Purpose

Explains how performance-based payments are tied to defined events or performance criteria, capped against the contract price, and recovered from delivery payments.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0108] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.1004 [@CL-CTF-0108] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.1004 [@CL-CTF-4379; @CL-CTF-4380; @CL-CTF-4381; @CL-CTF-4382; @CL-CTF-4383; @CL-CTF-4384; @CL-CTF-4385; @CL-CTF-4386] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0108] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments made on a whole contract basis or on a deliverable item basis [@CL-CTF-0108] |

Payments may be made on a whole contract basis or on a deliverable item basis, unless agency regulations prescribe otherwise [@CL-CTF-0108]. The basis for a payment may be a specifically described event, such as a milestone, or a measurable criterion of performance [@CL-CTF-0109].

Total performance-based payments shall meet both of two requirements: they reflect prudent contract financing provided only to the extent needed for contract performance [@CL-CTF-9597], and they do not exceed 90 percent of the contract price on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-0112].

## How it works

Each event or performance criterion that will trigger a finance payment shall be an integral and necessary part of contract performance and shall be identified in the contract, along with a description of what constitutes successful performance of the event or attainment of the performance criterion [@CL-CTF-0489; @CL-CTF-3222]. An event need not be a critical event in order to trigger a payment, but the Government must be able to readily verify successful performance of each such event or performance criterion [@CL-CTF-0111].

The signing of contracts or modifications, the exercise of options, the passage of time, or other such occurrences do not represent meaningful efforts or actions and shall not be identified as events or criteria for performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-0110].

Amounts are recovered by deducting a percentage or a designated dollar amount from the delivery payments [@CL-CTF-0113], and the method of liquidation must ensure complete liquidation no later than final payment [@CL-CTF-0490].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 32 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO section 32.1004 keeps payment on a whole contract or deliverable item basis unless agency regulations prescribe otherwise [@CL-CTF-4379], and a basis of specifically described events or some measurable criterion of performance [@CL-CTF-4380]. It keeps the rule that each event or performance criterion that will trigger a finance payment must be an integral and necessary part of contract performance and must be identified in the contract, along with a description of what constitutes successful performance of the event or attainment of the performance criterion [@CL-CTF-4381], and that the signing of contracts or modifications, the exercise of options, the passage of time, or other such occurrences do not represent meaningful efforts or actions and must not be identified as events or criteria for performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-4382]. It keeps the requirements that total performance-based payments reflect prudent contract financing provided only to the extent needed for contract performance and not exceed 90 percent of the contract price if on a whole contract basis, or 90 percent of the delivery item price if on a delivery item basis [@CL-CTF-4383; @CL-CTF-4384], liquidation by deducting a percentage or a designated dollar amount from the delivery payments [@CL-CTF-4385], and complete liquidation no later than final payment [@CL-CTF-4386].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract price is 1,000,000, with one milestone worth 200,000 and a liquidation rate of 20 percent set at award. At the milestone the Government pays 200,000. A later delivery invoice of 100,000 is reduced by 20,000, so 80,000 is paid, and the financing is fully recovered by final payment.

## Never confuse

FAR 32.1004 sets out the payment basis, the total payment limits and the liquidation rules for performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-0108; @CL-CTF-9597; @CL-CTF-0112; @CL-CTF-0113]; the contract clause is covered on the [Performance-Based Payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md) page.

Under the basic progress payments clause, unless the contractor requests a smaller amount, the Government computes each progress payment as 80 percent of the contractor's total costs incurred under the contract whether or not actually paid, plus financing payments to subcontractors, less the sum of all previous progress payments made by the Government under the contract [@CL-CTF-0102; @CL-CTF-0271], whereas the basis for performance-based payments may be either specifically described events or some measurable criterion of performance [@CL-CTF-0109]. Except as provided in the Termination for Convenience of the Government clause, all progress payments are liquidated by deducting from any payment under the contract, other than advance or progress payments, the unliquidated progress payments, or 80 percent of the amount invoiced, whichever is less [@CL-CTF-0104; @CL-CTF-0491], while performance-based amounts are liquidated by deducting a percentage or a designated dollar amount from the delivery payments [@CL-CTF-0113]; see [Progress payments](ctf-progress-payments.md).
