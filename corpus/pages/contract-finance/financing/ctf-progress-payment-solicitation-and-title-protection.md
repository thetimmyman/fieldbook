## Purpose

Sets out the provisions a solicitation carries when progress payments are on offer, and the title protection the administrative contracting officer applies once payments begin.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3093] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.502, FAR 32.502-3 and FAR 32.503-14 [@CL-CTF-3743; @CL-CTF-3744; @CL-CTF-3093; @CL-CTF-3097] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.502 [@CL-CTF-3032], RFO FAR 32.502-3 [@CL-CTF-4439; @CL-CTF-4440; @CL-CTF-4441; @CL-CTF-4442; @CL-CTF-4443] and RFO FAR 32.503-14 [@CL-CTF-4444; @CL-CTF-4445; @CL-CTF-4446; @CL-CTF-4447] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3093] |
| Applies to | Invitations for bids and requests for proposals that include a Progress Payments clause, and contracts under which progress payments have been made [@CL-CTF-3093; @CL-CTF-3191] |

The contracting officer is to insert the provision at FAR 52.232-13, Notice of Progress Payments, in invitations for bids and requests for proposals that include a Progress Payments clause [@CL-CTF-3093]. Under the authority of the statutes cited in FAR 32.101, an invitation for bids may restrict the availability of progress payments to small business concerns only [@CL-CTF-3094]. The contracting officer is to insert the provision at FAR 52.232-14, Notice of Availability of Progress Payments Exclusively for Small Business Concerns, in invitations for bids [@CL-CTF-3190], if it is anticipated that both small business concerns and others may submit bids in response to the same invitation and only the small business bidders would need progress payments [@CL-CTF-3095]. The contracting officer is to insert the provision at FAR 52.232-15, Progress Payments Not Included, in invitations for bids if the solicitation will not contain one of the provisions prescribed in the two earlier paragraphs [@CL-CTF-3096].

The Progress Payments clause gives the Government title to all of the materials, work-in-process, finished goods, and other items of property described in paragraph (d) of the clause, under the contract under which progress payments have been made [@CL-CTF-3191]. The administrative contracting officer must ensure that this title is not compromised by other encumbrances [@CL-CTF-3192]. Ordinarily, and in the absence of reason to believe otherwise, the officer may rely on the contractor's certification in the progress payment request [@CL-CTF-3097]. If the officer becomes aware of any arrangement or condition that would impair the Government's title to the property affected by progress payments, the officer shall require additional protective provisions to establish and protect that title [@CL-CTF-3098].

Any such encumbrance is a violation of the contractor's obligations under the contract, and the officer may, if necessary, suspend or reduce progress payments under the terms of the Progress Payments clause covering failure to comply with any material requirement of the contract [@CL-CTF-3193]. If the contractor fails to disclose an existing encumbrance in the progress payments certification, the officer should consult legal counsel about a possible False Claims Act violation [@CL-CTF-3099].

## How it works

FAR 32.502, the section on preaward matters, covers matters that generally are relevant only before contract award [@CL-CTF-3743]. It does not preclude taking actions discussed there after award, if appropriate, for example the postaward addition of a Progress Payments clause for consideration [@CL-CTF-3744].

The solicitation provision depends on whether the invitation or request includes a Progress Payments clause, whether it is anticipated that both small business concerns and others may bid and only the small business bidders would need progress payments, and whether the solicitation will contain one of the first two provisions [@CL-CTF-3093; @CL-CTF-3095; @CL-CTF-3096]. The Progress Payments clause gives the Government title to the described property under the contract under which progress payments have been made, and the administrative contracting officer may ordinarily rely on the contractor's certification in the progress payment request absent reason to believe otherwise [@CL-CTF-3191; @CL-CTF-3097]. On becoming aware of an arrangement or condition that would impair the Government's title, the administrative contracting officer shall require additional protective provisions [@CL-CTF-3098]; the existence of an encumbrance is a violation of the contractor's obligations, and that officer may, if necessary, suspend or reduce progress payments under the Progress Payments clause terms covering failure to comply with any material requirement [@CL-CTF-3193].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.502 keeps the preaward matters scope: the section covers matters that generally are relevant only before contract award, and does not preclude taking actions discussed there after award, if appropriate, for example the postaward addition of a Progress Payments clause for consideration [@CL-CTF-3743; @CL-CTF-3744; @CL-CTF-3032]. RFO FAR 32.502-3 keeps the same solicitation provisions, written as instructions to insert them without naming the contracting officer: the Notice of Progress Payments provision [@CL-CTF-4439], the exclusive small business notice [@CL-CTF-4441; @CL-CTF-4442] and the Progress Payments Not Included provision [@CL-CTF-4443], and it keeps the option to restrict progress payments to small business concerns in an invitation for bids [@CL-CTF-4440]. RFO FAR 32.502-3(b)(2) labels its two conditions (i) and (ii) where the codified text labels them (1) and (2) [@CL-CTF-4442]. RFO FAR 32.503-14 keeps the Government title under the Progress Payments clause [@CL-CTF-4444], the administrative contracting officer's duty to ensure that title is not compromised [@CL-CTF-4445], the duty to require additional protective provisions when the administrative contracting officer becomes aware of an arrangement or condition that would impair the Government's title, now with "must" in place of "shall" [@CL-CTF-4446] and the option, if necessary, to suspend or reduce progress payments under the Progress Payments clause terms covering failure to comply with any material requirement, because the existence of an encumbrance is a violation of the contractor's obligations [@CL-CTF-4447].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An invitation for bids includes a Progress Payments clause, so the contracting officer inserts the Notice of Progress Payments provision [@CL-CTF-3093]. Small business concerns and others are expected to bid, and only the small business bidders would need progress payments, so the contracting officer inserts the exclusive small business provision [@CL-CTF-3190; @CL-CTF-3095]. A different invitation will contain neither of those provisions, so the officer inserts the Progress Payments Not Included provision [@CL-CTF-3096]. After award and payment, the administrative contracting officer may rely on the certification in each request [@CL-CTF-3097] until a lender's security interest in the inventory comes to light. The officer then requires additional protective provisions [@CL-CTF-3098] and, if necessary, may suspend or reduce progress payments [@CL-CTF-3193]. Had the contractor failed to disclose that interest in its certification, the officer should consult legal counsel about the False Claims Act [@CL-CTF-3099].

## Never confuse

Title under the Progress Payments clause is not the payment itself. The clause gives the Government title to the described property [@CL-CTF-3191], and the administrative contracting officer may ordinarily rely on the contractor's certification, absent reason to believe otherwise [@CL-CTF-3097]. The existence of any such encumbrance is a violation of the contractor's obligations under the contract [@CL-CTF-3193]. The [progress payment solicitation notices](../billing-payment/ctf-progress-payment-solicitation-notices.md) card covers the notices in detail.

The two conditions for the exclusive small business notice carry the labels (1) and (2) in the codified text [@CL-CTF-3095] and (i) and (ii) in the RFO text [@CL-CTF-4442], so a citation to one label does not match the other text.
