## Purpose

Shows how the DoD clause ties performance-based payments to the whole contract, and what the DoD clause adds to the FAR clause on financial statements, incurred cost reporting and security.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1725] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7012 [@CL-CTF-1725] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1725] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments made on a whole-contract basis [@CL-CTF-1785] |

DFARS directs use of this clause for performance-based payments made on a whole-contract basis [@CL-CTF-1785]. Performance-based payments form the basis for the contract financing payments under the contract and apply to the whole contract [@CL-CTF-1725]. The attached schedule describes the basis for payment, including each individual payment event, its evidence of completion and the amount due on completion of the event [@CL-CTF-1726]. To receive these payments, the contractor's financial statements must comply with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CTF-1727].

Incurred cost is determined by the contractor's accounting books and records, to which the contractor must give the Contracting Officer access on request [@CL-CTF-1728]. The clause states that paragraph (m) of the FAR performance-based payments clause does not require certification of incurred costs [@CL-CTF-1775]. Title to the property described in the FAR clause is the preferred security for these payments [@CL-CTF-1730]. Paragraph (f) of the FAR clause does not apply to the extent that the contractor and the Contracting Officer agree on alternative forms of security [@CL-CTF-1776]. The FAR clause is covered on [Performance-based payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).

## How it works

Because the clause applies to the whole contract, the payment events sit in one schedule that is not limited to chosen line items [@CL-CTF-1725; @CL-CTF-1726]. An acceptable accounting system under the cited DFARS accounting system clause is not required for reporting of incurred costs [@CL-CTF-1728]. A contractor whose accounting system cannot track costs on a job order basis must give a realistic approximation of the allocation of incurred costs attributable to the contract, in accordance with its accounting system [@CL-CTF-1729]. If the accounting system cannot identify and track through the build cycle the property that is allocable and properly chargeable to the contract, the Contracting Officer may consider acceptance of one or a combination of the clause's listed alternative forms of security [@CL-CTF-1777]. If the contractor fails to provide adequate security as required, no financing payment will be made, and on receipt of adequate security financing payments will be made, including all previous payments to which the contractor is entitled [@CL-CTF-1778].

If the Contracting Officer determines at any time that the security provided is insufficient, the contractor shall promptly provide such additional security as the Contracting Officer determines necessary [@CL-CTF-1779]. If the contractor fails to provide it, the Contracting Officer may collect or liquidate the security that has been provided and suspend further payments [@CL-CTF-1780]. The text of this clause sets no payment limit or liquidation rate of its own; see the linked FAR clause card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply contract with a single price carries a payment schedule with three events worth 400,000, 300,000 and 300,000. The schedule covers the whole contract, so every event is paid against the contract as a whole and no event is tied to a single line item. Answer: the contractor requests each amount only after the evidence of completion named in the schedule exists for that event.

## Never confuse

The whole-contract clause is not the deliverable-item clause: the deliverable-item clause applies to line items that the Contracting Officer inserts, and its schedule also names the line items for each event, while this clause applies to the whole contract [@CL-CTF-1725; @CL-CTF-1731; @CL-CTF-1732]. That clause is on [DoD performance-based payments: deliverable-item basis](ctf-dod-pbp-deliverable-item-basis.md). Whether a contract carries performance-based payments or progress payments is set out on [DoD financing notice and progress payments for multiple lots](ctf-dod-financing-notice-and-multiple-lot-progress-payments.md). For the title and risk of loss rules, see [Title and risk of loss under performance-based payments](ctf-pbp-title-and-risk-of-loss.md). The contracting officer's approval duties are on [Administration and payment of performance-based payments](ctf-pbp-administration-and-payment.md).
