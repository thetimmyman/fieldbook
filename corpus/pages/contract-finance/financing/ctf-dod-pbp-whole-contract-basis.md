## Purpose

Shows how the DoD clause ties performance-based payments to the whole contract, and the DoD requirements on financial statements, incurred cost reporting and security.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1725] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7012 [@CL-CTF-1725] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | DEC 2022 [@CL-CTF-9721] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1725] |
| Applies to | Performance-based payments made on a whole-contract basis [@CL-CTF-1785] |

DFARS directs the contracting officer to include this clause, with appropriate fill-ins, in solicitations and contracts that include performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-3005], for performance-based payments made on a whole-contract basis [@CL-CTF-1785]. Performance-based payments shall form the basis for the contract financing payments provided under the contract and shall apply to the whole contract [@CL-CTF-1725]. The attached schedule describes the basis for payment, including each individual payment event, its evidence of completion and the amount due on completion of the event [@CL-CTF-1726]. In accordance with section 3802(c) of title 10 of the United States Code, the contractor's financial statements shall be in compliance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in order to receive performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-1727].

Incurred cost is determined by the contractor's accounting books and records, to which the contractor shall provide access upon request of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1728]. FAR 52.232-32(m) does not require certification of incurred costs [@CL-CTF-1775]. Title to the property described in paragraph (f) of the FAR performance-based payments clause is the preferred security for receipt of performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-1730]. Paragraph (f) of the FAR clause does not apply to the extent that the contractor and the Contracting Officer agree on alternative forms of security [@CL-CTF-1776]. The FAR clause is covered on [Performance-based payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).

## How it works

The clause applies to the whole contract [@CL-CTF-1725], and its schedule describes the basis for payment, including identification of the individual payment events, evidence of completion and the amount due on completion of each event [@CL-CTF-1726]. An acceptable accounting system in accordance with DFARS 252.242-7006 is not required for reporting of incurred costs under this clause [@CL-CTF-1728]. If the contractor's accounting system is not capable of tracking costs on a job order basis, the contractor shall provide a realistic approximation of the allocation of incurred costs attributable to the contract, in accordance with its accounting system [@CL-CTF-1729]. If the accounting system cannot identify and track through the build cycle the property that is allocable and properly chargeable to the contract, the Contracting Officer may consider acceptance of one or a combination of the clause's listed alternative forms of security sufficient to constitute adequate security for the performance-based payments and so specify in the contract, consistent with FAR 32.202-4 [@CL-CTF-1777; @CL-CTF-3006]. If the contractor fails to provide adequate security as required in the contract, no financing payment will be made under the contract, and upon receipt of adequate security financing payments will be made, including all previous payments to which the contractor is entitled, in accordance with the terms of the provisions for contract financing [@CL-CTF-1778; @CL-CTF-3007].

If the Contracting Officer determines at any time that the security provided is insufficient, the contractor shall promptly provide such additional security as the Contracting Officer determines necessary [@CL-CTF-1779]. If the contractor fails to provide it, the Contracting Officer may collect or liquidate the security that has been provided and suspend further payments [@CL-CTF-1780].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply contract with a single price carries a payment schedule with three events worth 400,000, 300,000 and 300,000. The schedule covers the whole contract, so every event is paid against the contract as a whole and no event is tied to a single line item. Answer: the contractor requests each amount only after the evidence of completion named in the schedule exists for that event.

## Never confuse

This clause applies to the whole contract rather than to individual line items [@CL-CTF-1725], unlike the deliverable-item clause, which applies to the contract line items that the Contracting Officer inserts and whose schedule identifies the CLINs to which each event applies [@CL-CTF-1731; @CL-CTF-1732]. For that clause, see [DoD performance-based payments: deliverable-item basis](ctf-dod-pbp-deliverable-item-basis.md). For whether a contract carries performance-based payments or progress payments, see [DoD financing notice and progress payments for multiple lots](ctf-dod-financing-notice-and-multiple-lot-progress-payments.md). For the title and risk of loss rules, see [Title and risk of loss under performance-based payments](ctf-pbp-title-and-risk-of-loss.md). For the Contracting Officer's approval duties, see [Administration and payment of performance-based payments](ctf-pbp-administration-and-payment.md).
