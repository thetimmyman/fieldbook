## Purpose

Shows the system DoD requires for payment requests and receiving reports, the cases where another method is allowed, and what the contractor must furnish.

DFARS directs use of the clause in solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CTF-3003], unless an exception to submission in electronic form applies and instructions for invoices are contained in the contract administration data section of the contract, task order or delivery order [@CL-CTF-1788]. Except as the listed exceptions allow, the contractor shall submit payment requests and receiving reports in electronic form using Wide Area WorkFlow [@CL-CTF-1745]. Payment requests and receiving reports are submitted to Wide Area WorkFlow in one of three electronic formats: Electronic Data Interchange, Secure File Transfer Protocol, or direct input through the Wide Area WorkFlow website [@CL-CTF-1747].

A contractor may submit a payment request and receiving report using methods other than Wide Area WorkFlow only when one of the following alternatives applies [@CL-CTF-1748]. The contractor shall also prepare and furnish to the Government a receiving report at the time of each delivery of supplies or services under the contract or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-1746].

## How it works

One alternative is where the contractor has requested permission in writing and the Contracting Officer has provided instructions for a temporary alternative method of submission of payment requests and receiving reports in the contract administration data section of the contract or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-1748; @CL-CTF-3004]. Another alternative is where DoD pays for commercial transportation services under a Government rate tender or a transportation services contract using a DoD-approved electronic third party payment system or other exempted vendor payment or invoicing system [@CL-CTF-1748; @CL-CTF-1749]. A third is where DoD makes payment on a contract or task or delivery order for rendered health care services using the TRICARE Encounter Data System [@CL-CTF-1748; @CL-CTF-1750]. A fourth is where the Governmentwide commercial purchase card is the method of payment, in which case submission of only the receiving report in Wide Area WorkFlow is required [@CL-CTF-1748; @CL-CTF-1751]. In addition to the requirements of the clause, the contractor shall meet the requirements of the appropriate payment clauses in the contract when submitting payment requests [@CL-CTF-1752].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers goods under a DoD supply contract and sends its invoice and receiving report by email. Answer: email is not one of the listed formats, and no listed exception applies, so the contractor must resubmit through Wide Area WorkFlow. If the buyer had paid by Governmentwide commercial purchase card, only the receiving report would go into Wide Area WorkFlow.

## Never confuse

The electronic submission rule and its exceptions differ in what each requires rather than coexisting by default: the contractor shall submit payment requests and receiving reports in electronic form using Wide Area WorkFlow [@CL-CTF-1745], and only one of the listed alternatives instead permits a method other than Wide Area WorkFlow [@CL-CTF-1748]. For the content of a proper invoice and the due date, see [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](../billing-payment/ctf-proper-invoice-content.md). For the routing and document type instructions, see [Electronic submission of payment requests: Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](../billing-payment/ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md), and for the DFARS policy on when the exceptions apply, see [DoD payment rules](../billing-payment/ctf-dod-payment-applicability-and-electronic-submission.md). For the payment terms that apply in emergencies and contingency operations, see [DoD payments in support of emergencies and contingency operations](ctf-dod-emergency-and-contingency-payments.md).
