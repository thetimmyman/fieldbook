## Purpose

Shows the system DoD requires for payment requests and receiving reports, the cases where another method is allowed, and what the contractor must furnish.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1745] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7003 [@CL-CTF-1745] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1745] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts, unless an exception to electronic submission applies and invoice instructions are in the contract administration data section [@CL-CTF-1788]; payment requests and receiving reports under the clause, subject to the listed exceptions [@CL-CTF-1745; @CL-CTF-1748] |

DFARS directs use of the clause in solicitations and contracts unless an exception to submission in electronic form applies and instructions for invoices are contained in the contract administration data section of the contract, task order or delivery order [@CL-CTF-1788]. Except as the listed exceptions allow, the contractor shall submit payment requests and receiving reports in electronic form using Wide Area WorkFlow [@CL-CTF-1745]. The contractor must submit them in one of three electronic formats: Electronic Data Interchange, Secure File Transfer Protocol, or direct input through the Wide Area WorkFlow website [@CL-CTF-1747].

A contractor may use other methods only in the listed cases [@CL-CTF-1748]. The contractor shall also prepare and furnish a receiving report at the time of each delivery of supplies or services under the contract or task or delivery order [@CL-CTF-1746].

## How it works

One listed case is where the contractor has requested permission in writing and the Contracting Officer has provided instructions for a temporary alternative method [@CL-CTF-1748]. Another listed case is where DoD pays for commercial transportation services under a Government rate tender or a transportation services contract using a DoD-approved electronic third party payment system or other exempted vendor payment or invoicing system [@CL-CTF-1748; @CL-CTF-1749]. A third is where DoD pays for rendered health care services using the TRICARE Encounter Data System [@CL-CTF-1748; @CL-CTF-1750]. A fourth is where the Governmentwide commercial purchase card is the method of payment, and then only the receiving report must be submitted in Wide Area WorkFlow [@CL-CTF-1748; @CL-CTF-1751]. The contractor must also meet the requirements of the appropriate payment clauses when submitting payment requests [@CL-CTF-1752].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers goods under a DoD supply contract and sends its invoice and receiving report by email. Answer: email is not one of the listed formats, and no listed exception applies, so the contractor must resubmit through Wide Area WorkFlow. If the buyer had paid by Governmentwide commercial purchase card, only the receiving report would go into Wide Area WorkFlow.

## Never confuse

This clause says how payment requests and receiving reports are sent. It does not set the content of a proper invoice or the due date. Those are on [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](../billing-payment/ctf-proper-invoice-content.md). The routing and document type instructions are on [Electronic submission of payment requests: Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](../billing-payment/ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md), and the DFARS policy on when the exceptions apply is on [DoD payment rules](../billing-payment/ctf-dod-payment-applicability-and-electronic-submission.md). The payment terms that apply in emergencies and contingency operations are on [DoD payments in support of emergencies and contingency operations](ctf-dod-emergency-and-contingency-payments.md). The clause text printed here does not list the data fields of a payment request beyond requiring the contractor to meet the appropriate payment clauses [@CL-CTF-1752].
