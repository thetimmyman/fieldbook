## Purpose

Explains the policy, statutory requirements, and limits for government financing of commercial supply and service contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0259] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.202-1 [@CL-CTF-0259] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0259] |
| Applies to | Contracts for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CTF-0259] |

For purchases of commercial products or services, contract financing is normally the contractor's responsibility [@CL-CTF-0259]. Where buyer financing is a commercial practice in a market, the contracting officer may include appropriate financing terms in contracts for commercial purchases when doing so will be in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-0526]. The statutes do not allow Government contract financing unless it is in the best interest of the United States [@CL-CTF-0262]. Prior to any performance of work, the aggregate of commercial advance payments shall not exceed 15 percent of the contract price [@CL-CTF-0261].

## How it works

Commercial interim payments and commercial advance payments may be made under the circumstances listed in the section, which include that the contract item financed is a commercial supply or service and that the contract price exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CTF-0260]. The listed circumstances also include that the contracting officer determines that it is appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace to make financing payments for the item and that this form of financing is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-0527], and that adequate security is obtained [@CL-CTF-0528]. They further include award on the basis of competitive procedures or, if only one offer is solicited, adequate consideration based on the time value of the additional financing [@CL-CTF-0529], if the financing is expected to be substantially more advantageous to the offeror than its normal method of customer financing [@CL-CTF-0540], and payment office concurrence on liquidation provisions when the regulation requires it [@CL-CTF-0530].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial supply contract is awarded competitively for 1,000,000 with commercial financing terms. Before work begins, the contractor requests an advance payment. The maximum permissible commercial advance payment prior to beginning performance is 150,000, which represents 15 percent of the contract price.

## Never confuse

Government financing of commercial purchases is expected to differ from financing for other than commercial purchases, although the contracting officer may adapt techniques and procedures from the other than commercial subparts [@CL-CTF-0531]. See [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md) and [Simplified acquisition threshold](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
