## Purpose

Explains the policy, statutory requirements, and limits for government financing of commercial supply and service contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0259] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.202-1 [@CL-CTF-0259]; FAR 32.200 [@CL-CTF-9304] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.200 [@CL-CTF-9275]; RFO FAR 32.202-1 [@CL-CTF-4249; @CL-CTF-4250; @CL-CTF-4251; @CL-CTF-4252; @CL-CTF-4253; @CL-CTF-4254; @CL-CTF-4255; @CL-CTF-4256; @CL-CTF-4257; @CL-CTF-4258; @CL-CTF-4259] |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | Contracts for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CTF-0259] |

For purchases of commercial products or commercial services, financing of the contract is normally the contractor's responsibility [@CL-CTF-0259]. However, in some markets the provision of financing by the buyer is a commercial practice, and in these circumstances the contracting officer may include appropriate financing terms in contracts for commercial purchases when doing so will be in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-0526]. The statutes cited in 32.201 do not allow contract financing by the Government unless it is in the best interest of the United States [@CL-CTF-0262]. One of the circumstances for commercial interim payments and commercial advance payments is that, prior to any performance of work under the contract, the aggregate of commercial advance payments shall not exceed 15 percent of the contract price [@CL-CTF-0260; @CL-CTF-0261].

Subpart 32.2 provides policies and procedures for commercial financing arrangements under commercial purchases pursuant to Part 12 [@CL-CTF-9304].

## How it works

Commercial interim payments and commercial advance payments may be made under the following circumstances, and the list is joined by the word and, so all of them apply together [@CL-CTF-0260; @CL-CTF-9592]. The circumstances include that the contract item financed is a commercial supply or service and that the contract price exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CTF-0260]. They also include that the contracting officer determines it is appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace to make financing payments for the item, that authorizing this form of contract financing is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-0527], and that adequate security is obtained [@CL-CTF-0528]. They further include that the contract is awarded on the basis of competitive procedures or, if only one offer is solicited, adequate consideration is obtained (based on the time value of the additional financing to be provided) [@CL-CTF-0529] if the financing is expected to be substantially more advantageous to the offeror than the offeror's normal method of customer financing [@CL-CTF-0540], and that the contracting officer obtains concurrence from the payment office concerning liquidation provisions when required by 32.206(e) [@CL-CTF-0530].

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.202-1(a) says that for purchases of commercial products or commercial services, financing of the contract is normally the contractor’s responsibility [@CL-CTF-4249], and that in some markets the provision of financing by the buyer is a commercial practice, in which circumstances the contracting officer may include appropriate financing terms in contracts for commercial purchases when doing so will be in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-4250]. RFO FAR 32.202-1(b) says commercial interim payments and commercial advance payments may be made under the following circumstances, and the list is joined by the word and, so all of them apply together [@CL-CTF-4251; @CL-CTF-2963]. They include that the contract item financed is a commercial supply or service and that the contract price exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CTF-4251]. They also include that the contracting officer determines it is appropriate or customary in the commercial marketplace to make financing payments for the item, that authorizing this form of contract financing is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CTF-4252], and that adequate security is obtained [@CL-CTF-4253]. They further include that the contract is awarded on the basis of competitive procedures or, if only one offer is solicited, adequate consideration is obtained (based on the time value of the additional financing to be provided) if the financing is expected to be substantially more advantageous to the offeror than the offeror’s normal method of customer financing [@CL-CTF-4255; @CL-CTF-4256], and that the contracting officer obtains concurrence from the payment office concerning liquidation provisions when required by 32.206(e) [@CL-CTF-4257]. Under RFO FAR 32.202-1(b)(6), prior to any performance of work under the contract, the aggregate of commercial advance payments must not exceed 15 percent of the contract price [@CL-CTF-4254], where the codified text says "shall not exceed" [@CL-CTF-0261]. RFO FAR 32.202-1(e) says the statutes cited in 32.201 do not allow contract financing by the Government unless it is in the best interest of the United States [@CL-CTF-4259]. RFO FAR 32.202-1(c) says Government financing of commercial purchases under this subpart is expected to be different from that used for other than commercial purchases under subpart 32.1 and its related subparts [@CL-CTF-4258]. Paragraph (b)(4) of RFO FAR 32.202-1 points to paragraph (e) of "this section" [@CL-CTF-4252], and codified FAR 32.202-1(b)(4) points to paragraph (e) of "this subsection" [@CL-CTF-0527].

RFO FAR 32.200 says this subpart provides policies and procedures for commercial financing arrangements under commercial purchases pursuant to part 12 [@CL-CTF-9275].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial supply contract is awarded competitively for 1,000,000 with commercial financing terms. Before work begins, the contractor requests an advance payment. The maximum permissible commercial advance payment prior to beginning performance is 150,000, which represents 15 percent of the contract price.

## Never confuse

Government financing of commercial purchases under this subpart is expected to differ from that used for other than commercial purchases under subpart 32.1 and its related subparts rather than to match it [@CL-CTF-0531]. While the contracting officer may adapt techniques and procedures from the other than commercial subparts for use in implementing commercial contract financing arrangements, the contracting officer must have a full understanding of the effects of the differing contract environments and of what is needed to protect the interests of the Government in commercial contract financing [@CL-CTF-0531; @CL-CTF-9593]. See [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md) and [Simplified acquisition threshold](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
