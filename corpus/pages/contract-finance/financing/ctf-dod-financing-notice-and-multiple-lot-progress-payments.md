## Purpose

Shows what a DoD solicitation tells offerors about progress payments and performance-based payments, and how the multiple-lots clause splits progress payment requests and liquidation by lot.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1734; @CL-CTF-1740] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7016 and DFARS 252.232-7018 [@CL-CTF-1734; @CL-CTF-1740] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1734; @CL-CTF-1740] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that contain clauses for both progress payments and performance-based payments, and contracts where separate progress payment requests are authorized for multiple lots [@CL-CTF-1784; @CL-CTF-1786] |

DFARS directs use of the notice provision in lieu of the FAR notice of progress payments provision when the solicitation contains clauses for progress payments and performance-based payments [@CL-CTF-1784]. The notice tells offerors that the resulting contract will include either performance-based payments or progress payments and not both, except as may be authorized on separate orders subject to the FAR [@CL-CTF-1734]. The performance-based payments clauses are included if all of the listed conditions are met [@CL-CTF-1735; @CL-CTF-1736]. One listed condition is a positive representation from the offeror in response to the DFARS representation provision [@CL-CTF-1735]. A further listed condition is that the offeror proposes an arrangement in accordance with the FAR invitation to propose performance-based payments, including proposed events and timing, event completion criteria, event values and an expected expenditure profile [@CL-CTF-1735; @CL-CTF-1736]. Another listed condition is agreement between the offeror and the Government on all aspects of the arrangement [@CL-CTF-1735; @CL-CTF-1737].

If the performance-based payments clauses are not included, the progress payments clause in the solicitation is included in the resulting contract, modified or altered if necessary in accordance with FAR 52.232-16 and its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-1738]. That clause is inoperative during any time the Government determines the contractor's accounting system and controls to be inadequate for segregation and accumulation of contract costs [@CL-CTF-1739]. DFARS directs use of the multiple-lots clause to authorize separate progress payment requests for multiple lots [@CL-CTF-1786].

## How it works

A lot is a single, severable group of fixed-price deliverable line items or subline items whose costs are segregated and that uses one progress payment rate [@CL-CTF-1740]. With multiple lots, progress payment proration is done lot by lot instead of across the whole contract [@CL-CTF-1741]. The duties in the clause apply when the contractor submits progress payment requests under the billing instructions in FAR clause 52.232-16 or DFARS clause 252.232-7002 [@CL-CTF-1787]. Under those instructions the contractor submits separate progress payment requests for each lot identified in the contract [@CL-CTF-1787; @CL-CTF-1742]. Each request is calculated from the price, costs, subcontractor financing and progress payment liquidations of its own lot, so the liquidations that count are those of that lot [@CL-CTF-1787; @CL-CTF-1743]. Costs are distributed among lots in a manner the Administrative Contracting Officer finds acceptable [@CL-CTF-1787; @CL-CTF-1744].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has two lots, each a group of fixed-price line items with its own price and progress payment rate. The contractor sends one progress payment request for lot one and a separate request for lot two. Each request uses only the price, costs, subcontractor financing and liquidations of its own lot. Answer: a payment made on lot one is liquidated against lot one, not against lot two.

## Never confuse

A lot is a group of one or more fixed-price deliverable line items or subline items, so it is not necessarily a single line item [@CL-CTF-1740]. The notice provision is also not the clause that sets rates or liquidation percentages. The customary rates and the basic liquidation rules are on [Customary progress payment rates](ctf-progress-payments.md) and [Progress payment liquidation](ctf-progress-payment-liquidation.md), and the contract price used for progress payments is on [Contract price for progress payments](ctf-contract-price-for-progress-payments.md). The FAR performance-based payments clause is on [Performance-based payments clause](ctf-performance-based-payments.md), and the two DoD performance-based payments clauses are on [DoD performance-based payments: whole-contract basis](ctf-dod-pbp-whole-contract-basis.md) and [DoD performance-based payments: deliverable-item basis](ctf-dod-pbp-deliverable-item-basis.md).
