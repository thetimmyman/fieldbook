## Purpose

Sets out what DoD says about the usual acceptance and construction payment periods, when the contracting officer may specify a longer period, the coordination expected, and the rule that a constructive acceptance period shorter than the cited periods is not authorized.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3805] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.904(d) [@CL-CTF-3805] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3806] |
| Applies to | Government acceptance or approval and payment timing under the FAR Prompt Payment clauses and the Prompt Payment for Construction Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-3805; @CL-CTF-3806] |

In most cases, Government acceptance or approval can occur within the 7-day constructive acceptance period specified in the FAR Prompt Payment clauses [@CL-CTF-3805]. Government payment of construction progress payments can, in most cases, be made within the 14-day period allowed by the Prompt Payment for Construction Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-3806].

The contracting officer may specify a longer period because the period specified in the contract is not reasonable or practical [@CL-CTF-3807]. Such a change should be coordinated with the Government offices responsible for acceptance or approval and for payment [@CL-CTF-3889]. Reasons for specifying a longer period include but are not limited to the nature of the work or supplies or services, inspection or testing requirements, shipping and acceptance terms, and resources available at the acceptance activity [@CL-CTF-3808].

A constructive acceptance period of less than the cited 7 or 14 days is not authorized [@CL-CTF-3809].

## How it works

In most cases, Government acceptance or approval can occur within the 7-day constructive acceptance period specified in the FAR Prompt Payment clauses [@CL-CTF-3805], and Government payment of construction progress payments can, in most cases, be made within the 14-day period allowed by the Prompt Payment for Construction Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-3806].

The contracting officer may specify a longer period because the period specified in the contract is not reasonable or practical, and such change should be coordinated with the Government offices responsible for acceptance or approval and for payment [@CL-CTF-3807; @CL-CTF-3889].

Reasons for specifying a longer period include but are not limited to the nature of the work or supplies or services, inspection or testing requirements, shipping and acceptance terms, and resources available at the acceptance activity [@CL-CTF-3808].

A constructive acceptance period of less than the cited 7 or 14 days is not authorized [@CL-CTF-3809]. How constructive acceptance and due dates work for interest purposes is on [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md), and the construction clause is on [Prompt payment for construction contracts](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-construction-contracts.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply contract specifies the 7-day constructive acceptance period, but the inspection regime needs 10 days of testing. The contracting officer concludes the period specified in the contract is not reasonable or practical and may specify a longer period [@CL-CTF-3807]. Inspection or testing requirements are a listed reason [@CL-CTF-3808]. The contracting officer sets 10 days and should coordinate the change with the Government offices responsible for acceptance or approval and for payment [@CL-CTF-3889].

A 5-day constructive acceptance period would be shorter than the cited 7 days, so it is not authorized [@CL-CTF-3809].

## Never confuse

The contracting officer may specify a longer period than the usual ones rather than a shorter one, because the period specified in the contract is not reasonable or practical [@CL-CTF-3807]; in most cases, Government acceptance or approval can occur within the 7-day constructive acceptance period [@CL-CTF-3805], and Government payment of construction progress payments can be made within the 14-day period [@CL-CTF-3806]. The 7-day constructive acceptance period relates to Government acceptance or approval rather than to payment, while the 14-day period relates to Government payment of construction progress payments [@CL-CTF-3805; @CL-CTF-3806]. Due dates are on [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md).
