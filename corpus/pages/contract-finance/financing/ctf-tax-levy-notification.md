## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer must require a contractor to report when a tax levy may stop it performing, and how that notice travels through the contracting officer to the DoD policy office and back.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3402] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.7101 [@CL-CTF-3402] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3402] |
| Applies to | Contracts where a levy may result in the contractor's inability to perform [@CL-CTF-3402] |

The contracting officer must require the contractor to notify the contracting officer promptly when a levy may result in an inability to perform the contract, and to advise the contracting officer whether that inability may adversely affect national security [@CL-CTF-3402]. The contracting officer must promptly notify the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy, when the contractor's inability to perform will adversely affect national security or will result in significant additional costs to the Government [@CL-CTF-3403]. The contracting officer is to follow the procedures at PGI 232.7101(b) for reviewing the contractor's rationale and submitting the required notification [@CL-CTF-3418].

The Principal Director will promptly evaluate the contractor's rationale and will notify the IRS, the contracting officer, and the payment office, as appropriate, in accordance with the procedures at PGI 232.7101(c) [@CL-CTF-3404]. The contracting officer must then notify the contractor in accordance with paragraph (c) of the clause at 252.232-7010 and in accordance with the procedures at PGI 232.7101(d) [@CL-CTF-3405].

## How it works

The notice runs from the contractor to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-3402], then, when the contractor's inability to perform will adversely affect national security or will result in significant additional costs to the Government, from the contracting officer to the Principal Director [@CL-CTF-3403], then from the Principal Director to the IRS, the contracting officer and the payment office, as appropriate [@CL-CTF-3404], and last from the contracting officer to the contractor [@CL-CTF-3405].

The clause itself, summarized on [Levies on contract payments](../cash/ctf-levies-on-contract-payments.md), requires the contractor, when a levy is imposed and it believes the levy may result in an inability to perform, to notify the Procuring Contracting Officer promptly in writing, with a copy to the Administrative Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0778]. The contractor shall provide the total dollar amount of the levy, a statement that it believes the levy may result in an inability to perform the contract, including rationale and adequate supporting documentation, and advice as to whether the inability to perform may adversely affect national security, including rationale and adequate supporting documentation [@CL-CTF-9603; @CL-CTF-0868; @CL-CTF-9602]. DoD shall promptly review the contractor's assessment, and the Procuring Contracting Officer shall provide a written notification to the contractor that includes a statement as to whether DoD agrees that the levy may result in an inability to perform [@CL-CTF-0869; @CL-CTF-2265] and, if the levy may result in an inability to perform and the lack of performance will adversely affect national security, the total amount of the monies collected that should be returned to the contractor, or, if the levy may result in an inability to perform the contract and will not impact national security, a recommendation that the contractor promptly notify the Internal Revenue Service to attempt to resolve the tax situation [@CL-CTF-0779; @CL-CTF-9528; @CL-CTF-0870; @CL-CTF-9604].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A levy of 80,000 reaches a contractor during performance. The contractor promptly tells the contracting officer that it may be unable to finish two lines, and advises that this does not appear to affect national security [@CL-CTF-3402]. The contracting officer concludes that the inability will result in significant additional costs to the Government, and promptly notifies the Principal Director [@CL-CTF-3403]. The Principal Director evaluates the contractor's rationale and notifies the IRS, the contracting officer and the payment office, as appropriate [@CL-CTF-3404]. The contracting officer then notifies the contractor under paragraph (c) of the levy clause [@CL-CTF-3405].

## Never confuse

The contractor's notice duty and the contracting officer's differ in trigger rather than duplicating each other: the contracting officer must require the contractor to give notice when a levy may result in an inability to perform [@CL-CTF-3402], whereas the contracting officer's own duty to notify the Principal Director arises only when the inability will adversely affect national security or will result in significant additional costs to the Government rather than on any levy [@CL-CTF-3403]. For the clause terms, see [Levies on contract payments](../cash/ctf-levies-on-contract-payments.md).
