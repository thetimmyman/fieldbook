## Purpose

Shows the DoD definitization schedule, the financing withhold that can follow a missed proposal date, and the certification a request for equitable adjustment must carry.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1767; @CL-CTF-1771] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.217-7027 and DFARS 252.243-7002 [@CL-CTF-1767; @CL-CTF-1771] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | DFARS 252.217-7027: MAY 2023 [@CL-CTF-9714]; DFARS 252.243-7002: DEC 2022 [@CL-CTF-9715] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1767; @CL-CTF-1771] |
| Applies to | Among others, all UCAs and solicitations associated with UCAs for the definitization clause [@CL-CTF-1797]; requests for equitable adjustment under contracts with the request clause [@CL-CTF-1771] |

Among the places where DFARS directs use of the definitization clause are all UCAs, solicitations associated with UCAs, basic ordering agreements, indefinite-delivery contracts, any other type of contract providing for the use of UCAs [@CL-CTF-1797], and unpriced change orders with an estimated value exceeding $5 million [@CL-CTF-3002]. The clause schedule paragraph calls for the target date for definitization of the contract action and the dates for submission of proposal, beginning of negotiations and, if appropriate, submission of the make-or-buy and subcontracting plans and certified cost or pricing data [@CL-CTF-1768]. Notwithstanding the FAR clause on payments of allowable costs before definitization, failure to meet the qualifying proposal date in the schedule could result in the Contracting Officer withholding an amount up to 5 percent of all subsequent requests for financing until the Contracting Officer determines that a proposal is qualifying [@CL-CTF-1798; @CL-CTF-1767]. If agreement on a definitive contract action is not reached by the target date or within any extension of it granted by the Contracting Officer, the Contracting Officer may, with the approval of the head of the contracting activity, determine a reasonable price or fee in accordance with FAR subpart 15.4 and part 31, and that determination is subject to contractor appeal as provided in the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1769; @CL-CTF-1770]. In any event the contractor shall proceed with completion of the contract, subject only to the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-CTF-1796].

A request for equitable adjustment shall include only costs for performing the change, and no costs already reimbursed or separately claimed [@CL-CTF-1771]. A request that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold shall bear, at the time of submission, a certificate executed by an individual authorized to certify the request on behalf of the contractor [@CL-CTF-1772]. The certificate states that the request is made in good faith and that the supporting data are accurate and complete to the best of the certifier's knowledge and belief [@CL-CTF-1773]. The certification requirement does not apply to either of two alternatives, the first being requests for routine contract payments, such as requests for payment for accepted supplies and services, routine vouchers under a cost-reimbursement type contract, or progress payment invoices [@CL-CTF-1774]. The other alternative is a final adjustment under an incentive provision of the contract [@CL-CTF-1799; @CL-CTF-1774].

## How it works

On the definitization side, the schedule dates drive the financing effect: a missed qualifying proposal date can mean a withhold of financing until a proposal qualifies [@CL-CTF-1767; @CL-CTF-1768]. The withhold is up to 5 percent of all subsequent requests for financing, and failure to meet the qualifying proposal date could result in it [@CL-CTF-1767]. For the FAR clause on payments of allowable costs before definitization, see [Payments of Allowable Costs Before Definitization](../billing-payment/ctf-payments-allowable-costs-before-definitization.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An undefinitized action has a qualifying proposal date in the schedule, and the contractor misses it. The contractor has requested 200,000 of financing. Answer: the Contracting Officer could withhold up to 10,000 of that request until the proposal is qualifying, because 10,000 is 5 percent of 200,000. Separately, a request for equitable adjustment of 1,000,000 is above the simplified acquisition threshold, so it needs the signed certificate when it is submitted.

## Never confuse

The definitization clause withhold differs from the FAR financing rule rather than replacing it: the definitization clause withhold applies notwithstanding FAR 52.216-26, Payments of Allowable Costs Before Definitization [@CL-CTF-1798]; for that FAR clause, see [Payments of Allowable Costs Before Definitization](../billing-payment/ctf-payments-allowable-costs-before-definitization.md). For letter contracts, see [Contract definitization for letter contracts](../funding/ctf-contract-definitization.md). The request clause differs from the adjustment right rather than granting it: it covers the costs a request may include and the certificate a request above the simplified acquisition threshold must bear [@CL-CTF-1771; @CL-CTF-1772]; for the right to an adjustment itself, see [Changes, Fixed-Price clause and equitable adjustment](../funding/ctf-changes-fixed-price-equitable-adjustment.md).
