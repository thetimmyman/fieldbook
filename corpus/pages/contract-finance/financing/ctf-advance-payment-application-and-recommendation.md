## Purpose

Shows how a contractor applies for advance payments and what the contracting officer sends when recommending approval or disapproval.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0929; @CL-CTF-0933] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.408 and FAR 32.409 [@CL-CTF-0929; @CL-CTF-3742; @CL-CTF-0933] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.408 [@CL-CTF-4144; @CL-CTF-4145; @CL-CTF-4146; @CL-CTF-4147], RFO FAR 32.409 [@CL-CTF-4149], RFO FAR 32.409-1 [@CL-CTF-4150; @CL-CTF-4151] and RFO FAR 32.409-2 [@CL-CTF-4152] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0929] |
| Applies to | Contractors requesting advance payments before or after contract award [@CL-CTF-0929] |

A contractor may apply for advance payments before or after the award of a contract [@CL-CTF-0929]. The contractor shall submit any request in writing to the contracting officer, with a reference to the existing contract or to the solicitation for a proposed contract, among other information [@CL-CTF-0930]. The request must include a cash flow forecast of estimated disbursements and receipts for the period of contract performance, and if the application pertains to a type of contract described in FAR 32.403(a) or (b), the contractor shall limit the forecast to the contract to be financed by advance payments [@CL-CTF-0931]. It must also describe the contractor's efforts to obtain unguaranteed private financing or a V-loan under eligible contracts, a requirement that is not applicable to the contract types described in FAR 32.403(a) or (b) [@CL-CTF-0932].

After analysis of the contractor's application and any appropriate investigation, the contracting officer shall recommend approval or disapproval and transmit the request and recommendation to the approving authority designated under FAR 32.402(e) [@CL-CTF-3742].

If recommending approval, the contracting officer shall transmit contract data to the approving authority under agency procedures, including the identification and date of the award, the citation of the appropriation, and the type and dollar amount of the contract [@CL-CTF-0933]. The contracting officer shall also transmit the contractor's request and supporting information and a report on the contractor's past performance, responsibility, technical ability, and plant capacity [@CL-CTF-0933; @CL-CTF-1007]. If recommending disapproval, the contracting officer shall, under agency procedures, transmit the items prescribed for the approval recommendation in FAR 32.409-1(a), (b), and (c), together with the recommendation for disapproval and the reasons [@CL-CTF-0934].

## How it works

The contractor files a written request that carries a cash flow forecast [@CL-CTF-0931] and, except for the contract types described in FAR 32.403(a) or (b), a description of its efforts to obtain unguaranteed private financing or a V-loan [@CL-CTF-0932]. The contracting officer then recommends approval or disapproval and transmits the request and recommendation to the approving authority [@CL-CTF-3742]. A recommendation to disapprove carries the contract data, the request and the past-performance report, plus the reasons for disapproval [@CL-CTF-0934].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.408 keeps the written request to the contracting officer, with a reference to the contract or the solicitation [@CL-CTF-4145], and RFO FAR 32.409 keeps the recommendation of approval or disapproval to the approving authority [@CL-CTF-4149; @CL-CTF-4150; @CL-CTF-4152]. RFO FAR 32.408(b)(2) limits the cash flow forecast to the contract to be financed without the codified condition on contract type [@CL-CTF-4146; @CL-CTF-0931]. RFO FAR 32.408(b)(5) asks only about unguaranteed private financing, where the codified paragraph also names a V-loan [@CL-CTF-4147; @CL-CTF-0932], and it exempts the contract types described in 32.407(d)(1) and (2), which are contracts for experimental, research, or development work with nonprofit education or research institutions and contracts solely for the management and operation of Government-owned plants [@CL-CTF-4147; @CL-CTF-4161]. The codified paragraphs cite FAR 32.403(a) or (b) for those types [@CL-CTF-0931; @CL-CTF-0932], and the RFO text prints section 32.403 as reserved [@CL-CTF-4148].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor files a written request for advance payments before award. It attaches a cash flow forecast for the contract period and a note on its attempts to obtain private financing, because its contract is not of a type that is exempt from that requirement. The contracting officer reviews the file and sends the approving authority a recommendation to approve, with the contract data and the contractor's request.

## Never confuse

The contractor submits its request to the contracting officer, who recommends approval or disapproval and transmits the request and recommendation to the approving authority [@CL-CTF-0930; @CL-CTF-3742]. For progress payments, see [Progress payments](ctf-progress-payments.md) and [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md).

The codified text requires the cash flow forecast to be limited to the financed contract when the application pertains to a contract type described in FAR 32.403(a) or (b) [@CL-CTF-0931], while the RFO text states the limit without that condition [@CL-CTF-4146]. The codified text also asks about efforts to obtain a V-loan [@CL-CTF-0932], but the RFO text asks only about unguaranteed private financing [@CL-CTF-4147].
