## Purpose

Shows when performance and payment bonds are required, the penal amounts, when the bonds are furnished and how the protection grows when the contract price rises.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-2489] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.228-15 and FAR 52.228-16 [@CL-CTF-2489; @CL-CTF-2493] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.228-15 and RFO FAR 52.228-16 [@CL-CTF-1046; @CL-CTF-1099] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-2489] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the construction bonds clause or the other than construction bonds clause [@CL-CTF-2489; @CL-CTF-2493] |

For construction, unless the resulting contract price is at or below the threshold in the Federal Acquisition Regulation on the date of award, the successful offeror furnishes performance and payment bonds to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-2489]. At the time of contract award the penal amount of each of the performance bond and the payment bond is 100 percent of the original contract price [@CL-CTF-2490]. The Government may require additional performance and payment bond protection if the contract price is increased, and the increase in protection generally equals 100 percent of the increase in contract price [@CL-CTF-2491]. The Government may secure the additional protection by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bond or to obtain an additional bond [@CL-CTF-2774].

In the construction bonds clause, the original contract price means the award price of the contract or, for requirements contracts, the price payable for the estimated total quantity or, for indefinite-quantity contracts, the price payable for the specified minimum quantity [@CL-CTF-2772]. In the construction bonds clause, the original contract price does not include the price of any options, except those options exercised at the time of contract award [@CL-CTF-2773]. In the construction bonds clause the performance bond uses Standard Form 25 and the payment bond uses Standard Form 25-A [@CL-CTF-2785].

The Contractor furnishes all executed bonds, including any necessary reinsurance agreements, to the Contracting Officer within the time period specified in the Bid Guarantee provision of the solicitation or otherwise by the Contracting Officer, and in any event before starting work [@CL-CTF-2492]. The bonds shall be in the form of firm commitment, supported by corporate sureties whose names appear on the list contained in Treasury Department Circular 570, or individual sureties [@CL-CTF-2775]. The bonds may instead be supported by other acceptable security such as postal money order, certified check, cashier's check, irrevocable letter of credit, or, in accordance with Treasury Department regulations, certain bonds or notes of the United States [@CL-CTF-2776]. Any waiver of the right to sue on the payment bond is void unless it is in writing, signed by the person whose right is waived, and executed after such person has first furnished labor or material for use in the performance of the contract [@CL-CTF-2777].

Under the basic clause for other than construction, the Contractor furnishes a performance bond and a payment bond in amounts equal to percentages of the original contract price that are filled in for each contract [@CL-CTF-2493]. The Contractor shall furnish all executed bonds, including any necessary reinsurance agreements, to the Contracting Officer within a number of days filled in for each contract, but in any event before starting work [@CL-CTF-2578]. The Government may require additional performance and payment bond protection if the contract price is increased, and may secure it by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bonds or to obtain additional bonds [@CL-CTF-2579].

In the other than construction bonds clause, the original contract price means the award price of the contract or, for requirements contracts, the price payable for the estimated quantity or, for indefinite-quantity contracts, the price payable for the specified minimum quantity [@CL-CTF-2778]. In the other than construction bonds clause, the original contract price does not include the price of any options, except those options exercised at the time of contract award [@CL-CTF-2779]. In the other than construction bonds clause the performance bond uses Standard Form 1418 and the payment bond uses Standard Form 1416 [@CL-CTF-2786]. The bonds shall be in the form of firm commitment, supported by corporate sureties whose names appear on the list contained in Treasury Department Circular 570, or individual sureties [@CL-CTF-2780]. The bonds may instead be supported by other acceptable security such as postal money order, certified check, cashier's check, irrevocable letter of credit, or, in accordance with Treasury Department regulations, certain bonds or notes of the United States [@CL-CTF-2781].

Alternate I substitutes its own paragraphs (b) and (d) for paragraphs (b) and (d) of the basic clause [@CL-CTF-2782]. Under Alternate I the Contractor shall furnish a performance bond for the protection to the Government in an amount equal to a percentage of the original contract price that is filled in for each contract [@CL-CTF-2783]. Under Alternate I the Government may require additional performance bond protection if the contract price is increased, and may secure it by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bond or to obtain an additional bond [@CL-CTF-2784].

## How it works

For construction, the penal amount of each of the performance bond and the payment bond at the time of contract award is 100 percent of the original contract price [@CL-CTF-2490]. If the contract price is increased, the Government may require additional performance and payment bond protection, and the increase in protection generally equals 100 percent of the increase in contract price [@CL-CTF-2491]. The Contractor furnishes all executed bonds, including any necessary reinsurance agreements, to the Contracting Officer within the time period specified in the Bid Guarantee provision of the solicitation or otherwise by the Contracting Officer, and in any event before starting work [@CL-CTF-2492]. For other than construction under the basic clause, the penal amounts are the percentages of the original contract price filled in for each contract, the executed bonds are due within the number of days filled in for each contract and before starting work, and additional protection may be secured by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bonds or to obtain additional bonds [@CL-CTF-2493; @CL-CTF-2578; @CL-CTF-2579]. Under Alternate I, the performance bond is the percentage of the original contract price filled in for each contract, and additional performance bond protection may be secured by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bond or to obtain an additional bond [@CL-CTF-2783; @CL-CTF-2784]. The original contract price is the award price or the specified quantity price and does not include the price of any options, except options exercised at the time of contract award [@CL-CTF-2772; @CL-CTF-2773; @CL-CTF-2778; @CL-CTF-2779]. The bonds shall be in the form of firm commitment supported by corporate sureties on the Treasury Department Circular 570 list, individual sureties, or other acceptable security [@CL-CTF-2775; @CL-CTF-2776; @CL-CTF-2780; @CL-CTF-2781]. A waiver of the right to sue on the payment bond is void unless it is in writing, signed by the person whose right is waived, and executed after that person first furnished labor or material for use in the performance of the contract [@CL-CTF-2777].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.228-15 keeps the construction rule that, unless the resulting contract price is valued at or below the threshold specified in Federal Acquisition Regulation 28.102-1(a) on the date of award, the successful offeror furnishes performance and payment bonds to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1046]. It keeps the penal amount of performance bonds (Standard Form 25) at 100 percent of the original contract price at the time of contract award [@CL-CTF-1047], and sets the penal amount of payment bonds (Standard Form 25A) at 100 percent of the original contract price at the time of contract award [@CL-CTF-1048]. It keeps the rule that the Government may require additional performance and payment bond protection if the contract price is increased, with the increase generally equal to 100 percent of the increase in contract price [@CL-CTF-1049], and may secure it by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bond or to obtain an additional bond [@CL-CTF-1092]. It keeps the definition of original contract price and its exclusion of option prices, except options exercised at the time of contract award [@CL-CTF-1044; @CL-CTF-1045]. It keeps the deadline for furnishing all executed bonds, including any necessary reinsurance agreements, within the time period in the Bid Guarantee provision or otherwise specified by the Contracting Officer, but in any event before starting work [@CL-CTF-1093]. It keeps the firm commitment supported by corporate sureties on the Treasury Department Circular 570 list, individual sureties, or other acceptable security such as postal money order, certified check, cashier's check, irrevocable letter of credit, or, in accordance with Treasury Department regulations, certain bonds or notes of the United States [@CL-CTF-1094; @CL-CTF-1095], and the rule that a waiver of the right to sue on the payment bond is void unless it is in writing, signed by the person whose right is waived, and executed after that person has first furnished labor or material for use in the performance of the contract [@CL-CTF-1096].

For other than construction, RFO FAR 52.228-16 keeps the performance bond (Standard Form 1418) and the payment bond (Standard Form 1416) in amounts equal to percentages of the original contract price that are filled in [@CL-CTF-1099]. It also keeps the rule that the executed bonds are due within the number of days that is filled in, but in any event before starting work [@CL-CTF-1100]. It keeps the Government's right to require additional performance and payment bond protection if the contract price is increased and to secure it by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bonds or to obtain additional bonds [@CL-CTF-1101]. It keeps the original contract price definition and its exclusion of option prices [@CL-CTF-1097; @CL-CTF-1098], and the firm commitment and security rules [@CL-CTF-1102; @CL-CTF-1103]. Its Alternate I keeps the substitution of its own paragraphs (b) and (d) for those of the basic clause [@CL-CTF-1104], the performance bond in a percentage of the original contract price that is filled in [@CL-CTF-1105], and the additional performance bond protection rule [@CL-CTF-1106].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A construction contract is awarded at 5,000,000, above the threshold. The performance bond and the payment bond are each 5,000,000. The bonds, with any reinsurance agreements, are due by the date the Bid Guarantee provision gives, and before work starts. A change later raises the price by 800,000. The Government may require additional protection, generally about 800,000, and may secure it by directing an increase of the penal amount of the existing bond or an additional bond. The contract also has an option priced at 700,000 that is not exercised at award, so the original contract price is 5,000,000 and the option price is left out. The bonds are supported by a corporate surety on the Treasury Department list, and the contractor could instead use a certified check or an irrevocable letter of credit. A subcontractor signs a waiver of its right to sue on the payment bond before it has furnished any labor or material, so the waiver is void.

A service contract that is not construction fills in 50 percent for the performance bond, 40 percent for the payment bond and 20 days for furnishing the bonds, and the original contract price is 3,000,000. The bonds are 1,500,000 and 1,200,000, and both are due within 20 days and before work starts. A change later raises the price by 600,000, and the Government may direct the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bonds or to obtain additional bonds.

A second service contract uses Alternate I and fills in 50 percent for the performance bond, with an original contract price of 3,000,000, so the performance bond is 1,500,000. A change raises the price by 600,000, and the Government may require additional performance bond protection and may secure it by directing the Contractor to increase the penal amount of the existing bond or to obtain an additional bond.

## Never confuse

In the construction bonds clause, the penal amount of each of the performance bond and the payment bond at the time of contract award is 100 percent of the original contract price [@CL-CTF-2490], whereas in the other than construction bonds clause the Contractor furnishes a performance bond and a payment bond in amounts equal to percentages of the original contract price filled in for each contract [@CL-CTF-2493]. Related topics are on [Surety-takeover agreements after a default termination](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-surety-takeover-agreements.md) and [Default clauses for fixed-price contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-default-clauses.md).
