## Purpose

Lists the bases on which FAR 32.1002 says performance-based payments may be made on noncommercial purchases [@CL-CTF-3429; @CL-CTF-3445]. It also states two DoD points: DFARS 232.202-4(a)(2) on an offeror's financial condition as security for commercial contract financing, and DFARS 232.072 on determining the financial capability of current or prospective contractors [@CL-CTF-3431; @CL-CTF-3432].

Performance-based payments may be made on any of the following bases: performance measured by objective, quantifiable methods, accomplishment of defined events, or other quantifiable measures of results [@CL-CTF-3429]. The subpart provides policy and procedures for performance-based payments under noncommercial purchases pursuant to Subpart 32.1 [@CL-CTF-3445].

When determining whether an offeror's financial condition is adequate security, DFARS 232.202-4(a)(2) says to see DFARS 232.072-2 and 232.072-3 for guidance [@CL-CTF-3430]. It also notes that an offeror's financial condition may be sufficient to make the contractor responsible for award purposes, but may not be adequate security for commercial contract financing [@CL-CTF-3431].

DFARS 232.072 says to use the policies and procedures in that section in determining the financial capability of current or prospective contractors [@CL-CTF-3432].

## How it works

Performance-based payments may be made on performance measured by objective, quantifiable methods, accomplishment of defined events, or other quantifiable measures of results [@CL-CTF-3429].

On security for commercial contract financing, subject to agency regulations, the contracting officer may determine the offeror's financial condition to be adequate security, provided the offeror agrees to provide additional security should that financial condition become inadequate as security [@CL-CTF-1413]. DFARS 232.202-4(a)(2) points to DFARS 232.072-2 and 232.072-3 for guidance and notes that an offeror's financial condition may be sufficient to make the contractor responsible for award purposes, but may not be adequate security for commercial contract financing [@CL-CTF-3430; @CL-CTF-3431]. The [commercial financing security](ctf-commercial-financing-security-and-clauses.md) card covers the FAR rules on security, and related DoD financial capability topics are on [required financial reviews](../cash/ctf-dod-required-financial-reviews.md), [financial review information](../cash/ctf-dod-financial-review-information.md) and [cash flow forecasts](../cash/ctf-dod-cash-flow-forecasts.md).

From February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers use the revised FAR part 32 in place of the codified text [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.1002 states the same three bases as the codified section [@CL-CTF-3444], and RFO FAR 32.1000 states that the subpart provides policy and procedures for performance-based payments under noncommercial purchases pursuant to Subpart 32.1 [@CL-CTF-3043].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A noncommercial purchase contract could set a performance-based payment of 100,000 on completion of a defined test event. Accomplishment of defined events is one of the bases on which performance-based payments may be made [@CL-CTF-3429].

Separately, an offeror shows 2,000,000 of working capital and is found responsible for award. An offeror's financial condition may be sufficient to make the contractor responsible for award purposes, but may not be adequate security for 1,500,000 of commercial contract financing [@CL-CTF-3431]. Subject to agency regulations, the contracting officer may determine the financial condition to be adequate security if the offeror agrees to provide additional security should that condition become inadequate [@CL-CTF-1413]. The guidance for that determination is at DFARS 232.072-2 and 232.072-3 [@CL-CTF-3430].

## Never confuse

An offeror's financial condition may be sufficient to make the contractor responsible for award purposes, but may not be adequate security for commercial contract financing [@CL-CTF-3431].

FAR Subpart 32.10 provides policy and procedures for performance-based payments under noncommercial purchases pursuant to Subpart 32.1 [@CL-CTF-3445], whereas DFARS 232.202-4(a)(2) addresses whether an offeror's financial condition is adequate security for commercial contract financing [@CL-CTF-3430; @CL-CTF-3431].

DFARS 232.072 directs use of its policies and procedures in determining the financial capability of current or prospective contractors [@CL-CTF-3432], and DFARS 232.202-4(a)(2) points to DFARS 232.072-2 and 232.072-3 for guidance on whether an offeror's financial condition is adequate security [@CL-CTF-3430]. Related payment mechanics are on [performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).
