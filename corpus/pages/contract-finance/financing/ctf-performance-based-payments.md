## Purpose

Explains how performance-based payment requests relate to contractual payment events, request limits, and successful performance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0201] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-32 [@CL-CTF-0201] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-32 [@CL-CTF-1107] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0201] |
| Applies to | Contracts containing this clause [@CL-CTF-0201] |

The contract's basis for payment sets payment amounts and limitations, subject to the contract and clause [@CL-CTF-0201]. Payment is not due before successful accomplishment of the event or performance criterion, and the Contracting Officer determines that accomplishment under the contract terms [@CL-CTF-0204; @CL-CTF-0205].

## How it works

The contractor submits requests no more often than monthly in a form and manner acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0202]. Unless otherwise authorized, each period's payments are combined into one appropriately itemized and totaled request [@CL-CTF-0203].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-32 keeps the rule that, subject to the other limitations and conditions in the contract and the clause, the contract's description of the basis for payment specifies payment amounts and limitations [@CL-CTF-0201] [@CL-CTF-1107]. It keeps the monthly limit on requests, in a form and manner acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0202] [@CL-CTF-1108], and the single, appropriately itemized and totaled request for each period unless the Contracting Officer otherwise authorizes [@CL-CTF-0203] [@CL-CTF-1109]. It keeps the rule that the Contractor is not entitled to payment before successful accomplishment of the event or performance criterion [@CL-CTF-0204] [@CL-CTF-1110], which the Contracting Officer determines in accordance with the contract terms [@CL-CTF-0205] [@CL-CTF-1111].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract sets a 100,000 payment for successful completion of a test event. The contractor requests payment before completing the event. The contractor is not yet entitled to that payment.

## Never confuse

Performance-based payment is tied to successful accomplishment, not simply to submitting a request. Monthly request frequency does not create an entitlement to payment [@CL-CTF-0202; @CL-CTF-0204].
