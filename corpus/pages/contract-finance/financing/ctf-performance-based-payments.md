## Purpose

Explains how performance-based payment requests relate to contractual payment events, request limits, and successful performance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0201] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-32 [@CL-CTF-0201] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-32 [@CL-CTF-1107] |
| Edition date | Apr 2012 [@CL-CTF-1107] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0201] |
| Applies to | Contracts containing this clause [@CL-CTF-0201] |

Subject to such other limitations and conditions as are specified in the contract and the clause, the amount of payments and limitations on payments shall be specified in the contract's description of the basis for payment [@CL-CTF-0201]. The Contractor shall not be entitled to payment of a request for performance-based payment prior to successful accomplishment of the event or performance criterion for which payment is requested, and the Contracting Officer shall determine whether the event or performance criterion has been successfully accomplished in accordance with the terms of the contract [@CL-CTF-0204; @CL-CTF-0205].

## How it works

The contractor may submit requests not more frequently than monthly in a form and manner acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0202]. Unless otherwise authorized by the Contracting Officer, each period's payments are combined into one appropriately itemized and totaled request [@CL-CTF-0203].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-32 keeps the rule that, subject to the other limitations and conditions in the contract and the clause, the contract's description of the basis for payment specifies payment amounts and limitations [@CL-CTF-1107]. It keeps the monthly limit on requests, in a form and manner acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1108], and the single, appropriately itemized and totaled request for each period unless the Contracting Officer otherwise authorizes [@CL-CTF-1109]. It keeps the rule that the Contractor is not entitled to payment before successful accomplishment of the event or performance criterion [@CL-CTF-1110], which the Contracting Officer determines in accordance with the contract terms [@CL-CTF-1111].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract sets a 100,000 payment for successful completion of a test event. The contractor requests payment before completing the event. The contractor is not yet entitled to that payment.

## Never confuse

The Contractor may submit requests for payment of performance-based payments not more frequently than monthly, in a form and manner acceptable to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-0202], and the Contractor is not entitled to payment of a request for performance-based payment prior to successful accomplishment of the event or performance criterion for which payment is requested [@CL-CTF-0204].
