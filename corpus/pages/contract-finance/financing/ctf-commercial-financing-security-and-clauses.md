## Purpose

Shows some forms of security the contracting officer may find acceptable for commercial contract financing, how an offeror may propose financing terms, and which solicitation provision and contract clauses the section names.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1413; @CL-CTF-1417; @CL-CTF-1419] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.202-4, FAR 32.205 and FAR 32.206 [@CL-CTF-1413; @CL-CTF-1417; @CL-CTF-1419] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1413] |
| Applies to | Contracts that will provide for contract financing under the FAR subpart on commercial product and commercial service purchase financing [@CL-CTF-1458] |

The contracting officer may determine the offeror's financial condition to be adequate security, subject to agency regulations and provided the offeror agrees to provide additional security should that condition become inadequate [@CL-CTF-1413]. If the Government's security is in the form of a lien, the statutes cited in FAR 32.201 make the lien paramount to all other liens and effective immediately upon the first payment, without filing, notice, or other action by the United States [@CL-CTF-1414]. Other acceptable forms of security include an irrevocable letter of credit from a federally insured financial institution and a bond from a surety acceptable under FAR part 28 that guarantees repayment of the unliquidated contract financing [@CL-CTF-1415]. They also include a guarantee of repayment from a person or corporation of demonstrated liquid net worth connected by significant ownership to the contractor, and title to identified contractor assets of adequate worth [@CL-CTF-1416].

Under the procedure in FAR 32.205, each offeror may propose financing terms, and the contracting officer must then determine which offer is in the best interests of the United States [@CL-CTF-1417]. If that procedure is used, the solicitation provision at FAR 52.232-31, Invitation to Propose Financing Terms, shall be included [@CL-CTF-1459].

## How it works

If the contract will provide for contract financing, the contracting officer shall construct a solicitation provision and contract clause [@CL-CTF-1458]. The contract shall contain the paragraph entitled Payment of the clause at FAR 52.212-4 [@CL-CTF-1418]. Each contract financing clause shall include the items listed in paragraph (b) of FAR 32.206 [@CL-CTF-1460], among them a description of the computation of the financing payment amounts [@CL-CTF-1419] and, unless agency regulations authorize alterations, the unaltered text of the clause at FAR 52.232-29 [@CL-CTF-1420]. Contracting officers may insert the clause at FAR 52.232-30 in lieu of constructing a specific clause under paragraphs (b) through (e) of that section [@CL-CTF-1421], if the contract action qualifies under the criteria at FAR 32.202-1(b) and installment payments for the item are either customary or authorized in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CTF-1422].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a commercial service invites each offeror to propose financing terms, so it includes the provision for inviting financing terms. One offeror offers a bond from an acceptable surety, and the bond guarantees repayment of the 400,000 of unliquidated financing. Another offers its own financial condition and agrees to add security if that condition worsens.

Answer: both are listed forms of acceptable security, subject to agency regulations and the contracting officer's findings. The resulting financing clause carries the unaltered standard financing terms clause unless agency regulations authorize changes.

## Never confuse

The offeror-proposed procedure uses the provision at FAR 52.232-31 [@CL-CTF-1459], while the installment payment clause at FAR 52.232-30 may be inserted in place of a specific clause if the stated conditions are met [@CL-CTF-1421; @CL-CTF-1422]. Accepting the contractor's financial condition as security is also different from taking a lien, bond or other listed form [@CL-CTF-1413; @CL-CTF-1415]. For when commercial financing may be provided at all, see [Contract financing for commercial purchases](ctf-contract-financing-for-commercial-purchases.md) and [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md). For the wider rules on providing financing, see [Providing contract financing and customary contract financing](ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md).
