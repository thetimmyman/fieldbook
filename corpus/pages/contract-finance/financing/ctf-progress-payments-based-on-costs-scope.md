## Purpose

Shows what the subpart on progress payments based on costs covers, and which payments and contracts it leaves out.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3845] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.500 [@CL-CTF-3845] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3845] |
| Applies to | Contract financing through progress payments based on costs, except the payments and contracts the section excludes [@CL-CTF-3845; @CL-CTF-3966; @CL-CTF-3846; @CL-CTF-3847] |

The subpart prescribes policies, procedures, forms, solicitation provisions and contract clauses for providing contract financing through progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3845]. It does not apply to the payments and contracts listed in paragraphs (a) and (b) of the section [@CL-CTF-3966].

Paragraph (a) lists payments under cost-reimbursement contracts, and it points to section 32.110 for progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts [@CL-CTF-3846]. Paragraph (b) lists contracts for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration or repair, when the contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3847].

## How it works

The section is a scope statement. It sets no duty, rate, deadline or threshold, and it names no official who decides; it fixes which payments and contracts the later sections of the subpart reach [@CL-CTF-3845; @CL-CTF-3966]. A payment or contract that falls within either paragraph is outside the subpart [@CL-CTF-3966; @CL-CTF-3846; @CL-CTF-3847].

Paragraph (a) removes payments under cost-reimbursement contracts. The cross-reference points the reader to section 32.110 for progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts, and this section does not say what section 32.110 provides, which is covered on [Payment of subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts](ctf-subcontractor-payments-under-cost-reimbursement-primes.md) [@CL-CTF-3846].

Paragraph (b) is conditional. A contract for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration or repair is outside the subpart under paragraph (b) only when the contract provides for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion, so such a contract that does not provide for those payments is not removed by paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-3847]. Paragraph (a) is a separate exclusion that can still apply to it [@CL-CTF-3846]. The general rules for cost-based progress payments are on [Progress payments based on costs: general rules, protective terms and finance office clearance](ctf-progress-payments-based-on-costs.md), and the scope of the wider contract financing part is on [Scope of the contract financing part and financing performance in foreign countries](ctf-contract-financing-scope-and-foreign-performance.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A production contract provides for progress payments based on costs incurred. It is not a cost-reimbursement contract and it is not a construction or shipbuilding contract paid by stage of completion, so neither exclusion reaches it and it is not removed from the subpart's scope [@CL-CTF-3845; @CL-CTF-3966]. A construction contract provides for progress payments based on stage of completion, so it is outside the subpart under paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-3847]. A cost-reimbursement contract's own payments are outside the subpart under paragraph (a), while progress payments that the prime contractor makes to a subcontractor under that prime contract are pointed to section 32.110 [@CL-CTF-3846]. A shipbuilding contract that provides for cost-based progress payments and not payments by percentage or stage of completion is not removed by paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-3847].

## Never confuse

Progress payments based on costs are not the payments made under cost-reimbursement contracts, which paragraph (a) excludes [@CL-CTF-3846]. Nor does the subpart reach the contracts that paragraph (b) lists when they provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3847].

## Company calibration

Whether each contract's progress payments are cost-based, or fall in one of the two carve-outs the subpart excludes [@Q-039].
