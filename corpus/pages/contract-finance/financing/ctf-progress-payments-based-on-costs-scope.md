## Purpose

Shows what the subpart on progress payments based on costs covers, and which payments and contracts it leaves out.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3845] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.500 [@CL-CTF-3845] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.500 [@CL-CTF-4448; @CL-CTF-4449; @CL-CTF-4450] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3845] |
| Applies to | Contract financing through progress payments based on costs, except the payments and contracts the section excludes [@CL-CTF-3845; @CL-CTF-3966; @CL-CTF-3846; @CL-CTF-3847] |

The subpart prescribes policies, procedures, forms, solicitation provisions and contract clauses for providing contract financing through progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3845]. It does not apply to the payments and contracts listed in paragraphs (a) and (b) of the section [@CL-CTF-3966].

Paragraph (a) lists payments under cost-reimbursement contracts, and it points to section 32.110 for progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts [@CL-CTF-3846]. Paragraph (b) lists contracts for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration or repair, when the contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3847].

## How it works

The subpart prescribes policies, procedures, forms, solicitation provisions and contract clauses for providing contract financing through progress payments based on costs, and does not apply to the payments and contracts listed in paragraphs (a) and (b) [@CL-CTF-3845; @CL-CTF-3966; @CL-CTF-3846; @CL-CTF-3847].

Paragraph (a) excludes payments under cost-reimbursement contracts and points to section 32.110 for progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts, covered on [Payment of subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts](ctf-subcontractor-payments-under-cost-reimbursement-primes.md) [@CL-CTF-3846].

Under paragraph (b), the subpart does not apply to contracts for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration or repair, when the contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3847]. The general rules for cost-based progress payments are on [Progress payments based on costs: general rules, protective terms and finance office clearance](ctf-progress-payments-based-on-costs.md), and the scope of the wider contract financing part is on [Scope of the contract financing part and financing performance in foreign countries](ctf-contract-financing-scope-and-foreign-performance.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.500 keeps the scope statement [@CL-CTF-4448], the exclusion of payments under cost-reimbursement contracts with the pointer to section 32.110 for progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts [@CL-CTF-4449], and the exclusion of contracts for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration or repair, when the contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion (see 10 U.S.C. 3808) [@CL-CTF-4450].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A production contract provides for progress payments based on costs incurred. It is not a cost-reimbursement contract and it is not a construction or shipbuilding contract paid by stage of completion, so neither exclusion reaches it and it is not removed from the subpart's scope [@CL-CTF-3845; @CL-CTF-3966]. A construction contract provides for progress payments based on stage of completion, so it is outside the subpart under paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-3847]. A cost-reimbursement contract's own payments are outside the subpart under paragraph (a), while progress payments that the prime contractor makes to a subcontractor under that prime contract are pointed to section 32.110 [@CL-CTF-3846]. A shipbuilding contract that provides for cost-based progress payments and not payments by percentage or stage of completion is not removed by paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-3847].

## Never confuse

The subpart does not apply to payments under cost-reimbursement contracts, and points to section 32.110 for progress payments made to subcontractors under cost-reimbursement prime contracts [@CL-CTF-3966; @CL-CTF-3846]. It also does not apply to contracts for construction or for shipbuilding or ship conversion, alteration or repair, when the contracts provide for progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CTF-3966; @CL-CTF-3847].

## Company calibration

Whether each contract's progress payments are cost-based, or fall in one of the two carve-outs the subpart excludes [@Q-039].
