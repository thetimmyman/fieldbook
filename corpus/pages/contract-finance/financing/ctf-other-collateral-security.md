## Purpose

Lists the examples of other forms of security that FAR 32.304-6 says may be required on a guaranteed loan when considered necessary for protection of the Government interest [@CL-CTF-3422; @CL-CTF-3423]. The section sits in the subpart on designated agencies' guarantees of loans made by private financial institutions to borrowers performing contracts related to national defense [@CL-CTF-3435].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3422] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.304-6 [@CL-CTF-3422] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.300 [@CL-CTF-9276]; RFO FAR 32.304 is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-3436] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final for the codified text [@CL-CTF-3422]; deviation text for the RFO reservation [@CL-CTF-3436] |
| Applies to | Guaranteed loans where other forms of security are considered necessary for protection of the Government interest [@CL-CTF-3422; @CL-CTF-3435] |

The section gives examples of other forms of security that may be required when considered necessary for protection of the Government interest. It adds that these forms are seldom invoked under guaranteed loans [@CL-CTF-3422].

The examples are mortgages on fixed assets, liens against inventories, endorsements, guarantees, and subordinations or standbys of other indebtedness [@CL-CTF-3423].

The sentence is passive. It does not say who considers the security necessary, who may require it, or from whom. It sets no threshold or deadline and states no exception [@CL-CTF-3422].

## How it works

The section ties the possible requirement to a judgment that the form of security is necessary for protection of the Government interest [@CL-CTF-3422].

The list is of examples, so the section does not limit the possible forms to the five listed [@CL-CTF-3422; @CL-CTF-3423]. It also says they are seldom invoked under guaranteed loans [@CL-CTF-3422].

The subpart as a whole prescribes policies and procedures for designated agencies' guarantees of loans [@CL-CTF-3435]. Other sections of the subpart deal with assignment of claims, surety bonds and other borrowing [@CL-CTF-1523; @CL-CTF-3080; @CL-CTF-3083]. The RFO text of the subpart scope says the subpart addresses policies and procedures for designated agencies' guarantees of loans made by private financial institutions to borrowers performing contracts related to national defense [@CL-CTF-9276]. See the [assignment of claims](ctf-loan-guarantee-assignment-and-advance-payment-applicability.md) and [surety bonds and other borrowing](ctf-defense-production-loan-guarantee-surety-and-termination.md) cards.

From February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers use the RFO text of FAR part 32 in place of the codified text [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.304 is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-3436]. This card states only the codified FAR 32.304-6 text [@CL-CTF-3422]. Whether the codified list applies to a DoD action after that date is not established here.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A borrower holds a guaranteed loan of 6,000,000. The party that decides under the loan arrangements considers a lien against the borrower's inventories necessary for protection of the Government interest. The section does not say who that party is [@CL-CTF-3422].

A lien against inventories is one of the listed examples of other forms of security that may be required [@CL-CTF-3422; @CL-CTF-3423]. The section does not make any listed form mandatory, and it says such forms are seldom invoked under guaranteed loans [@CL-CTF-3422]. The example therefore shows only that the form is a listed example, not that it will be required.

## Never confuse

Other collateral security is not the loan guarantee itself. It is a further form of security that may be required when considered necessary for protection of the Government interest, and the section says such forms are seldom invoked [@CL-CTF-3422]. The [defense production loan guarantee](ctf-loan-guarantees-for-defense-production.md) card covers the guarantee itself.

The five listed items are examples, not a closed list and not required terms [@CL-CTF-3422; @CL-CTF-3423].
