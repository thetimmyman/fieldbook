## Purpose

Lists the examples of other forms of security that FAR 32.304-6 says may be required on a guaranteed loan when considered necessary for protection of the Government interest [@CL-CTF-3422; @CL-CTF-3423]. The section sits in the subpart on designated agencies' guarantees of loans made by private financial institutions to borrowers performing contracts related to national defense [@CL-CTF-3435].

FAR 32.304-6 gives examples of other forms of security that, although seldom invoked under guaranteed loans, may be required when considered necessary for protection of the Government interest [@CL-CTF-3422].

The examples are mortgages on fixed assets, liens against inventories, endorsements, guarantees, and subordinations or standbys of other indebtedness [@CL-CTF-3423].

## How it works

FAR 32.304-6 lists mortgages on fixed assets, liens against inventories, endorsements, guarantees, and subordinations or standbys of other indebtedness as examples of other forms of security that, although seldom invoked under guaranteed loans, may be required when considered necessary for protection of the Government interest [@CL-CTF-3422; @CL-CTF-3423].

FAR 32.300 prescribes policies and procedures for designated agencies' guarantees of loans made by private financial institutions to borrowers performing contracts related to national defense [@CL-CTF-3435]. Other sections of the subpart address assignment of claims, surety bonds and other borrowing [@CL-CTF-1523; @CL-CTF-3080; @CL-CTF-3083]. RFO FAR 32.300 states that the subpart addresses policies and procedures for designated agencies' guarantees of loans made by private financial institutions to borrowers performing contracts related to national defense [@CL-CTF-9276]. See the [assignment of claims](ctf-loan-guarantee-assignment-and-advance-payment-applicability.md) and [surety bonds and other borrowing](ctf-defense-production-loan-guarantee-surety-and-termination.md) cards.

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 32 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.304 is marked reserved [@CL-CTF-3436].
