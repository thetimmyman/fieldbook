## Purpose

Covers the rules around a guaranteed loan: surety bonds on bonded contracts, borrowing outside the guarantee, and guarantees for terminated contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3080] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.304-7, FAR 32.304-8 and FAR 32.305 [@CL-CTF-3080; @CL-CTF-3083; @CL-CTF-3085] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.304 and RFO FAR 32.305 are marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4529; @CL-CTF-4530] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3080] |
| Applies to | Guaranteed loans on bonded contracts, guaranteed loans with other borrowing, and guarantees for terminated contracts [@CL-CTF-3081; @CL-CTF-3083; @CL-CTF-3085] |

Contract surety bonds are incompatible with the Government's interests under guaranteed loans, unless the interests of the surety are subordinated to the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-3080]. Where a substantial share of the contractor's defense contracts are covered by surety bonds, or the amount of the bond is substantial in relation to the contractor's net worth, the agency shall not authorize the guarantee of a loan on a bonded contract unless the surety enters into an agreement [@CL-CTF-3081]. That agreement is one with the financing institution to subordinate the surety's rights and claims in favor of the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-3176].

The agency approval of a guarantee for a loan involving relatively substantial subcontracts covered by surety bonds shall also depend on the establishment of a reasonable allocation agreement between the sureties and the financing institution [@CL-CTF-3082]. That agreement should give the financing institution the benefit, with regard to payments to be made on the contract, of the portion of its loans fairly attributable to expenditures made under the bonded subcontracts before notice of default [@CL-CTF-3177].

It has been recognized in practice that, while prohibition of borrowings outside the guaranteed loan is preferable when practicable in a given V-loan case, such other borrowings should be permitted when necessary [@CL-CTF-3083]. If the agency consents to the contractor obtaining other borrowing during the guaranteed loan period, the agency shall apply the following restrictions, the first being a reasonable limit on the amount of other borrowing [@CL-CTF-3178]. The second applies if guaranteed and unguaranteed loans are made by the same financing institution: a requirement that any collateral security requested by the institution under the unguaranteed loan is also to be secondary collateral for the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-3179]. The third is a requirement that the contractor provide appropriate documentation to the guaranteeing agency, at intervals not longer than 30 days, to disclose outstanding unguaranteed borrowings [@CL-CTF-3084].

The purpose of guaranteed loans, financing based on the borrower's recoverable investment in defense production contracts, may also apply to contracts that have been terminated, partially or totally, for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-3085]. Guaranteed loans also may be made before termination if it is known that termination of particular contracts for the convenience of the Government is about to occur, and they are expected to provide necessary financing pending termination settlements and payments [@CL-CTF-3180]. They may also finance continuing performance of defense production contracts that are eligible for guaranteed loans [@CL-CTF-3181]. The procedure for such guarantees is substantially the same as that outlined in 32.304, except that certificates of eligibility are not required for contracts that have been totally terminated or for the terminated portion of contracts that have been partially terminated [@CL-CTF-3086]. The agency shall take precautions necessary to avoid Government losses and to ensure the loans will be self-liquidating from the proceeds of defense production contracts [@CL-CTF-3182]. Loan guarantees for contract termination financing shall not be provided before specific contract terminations are certain [@CL-CTF-3183].

## How it works

Where a substantial share of the contractor's defense contracts are covered by surety bonds, or the amount of the bond is substantial in relation to the contractor's net worth, the agency shall not authorize the guarantee of a loan on a bonded contract unless the surety enters into an agreement with the financing institution to subordinate the surety's rights and claims in favor of the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-3081; @CL-CTF-3176]. The agency approval of a guarantee for a loan involving relatively substantial subcontracts covered by surety bonds shall also depend on the establishment of a reasonable allocation agreement [@CL-CTF-3082]. If the agency consents to other borrowing, the agency shall apply a reasonable limit on the amount of other borrowing [@CL-CTF-3178], a requirement about secondary collateral where the same financing institution makes the guaranteed and unguaranteed loans [@CL-CTF-3179], and a requirement that the contractor provide appropriate documentation to the guaranteeing agency at intervals not longer than 30 days [@CL-CTF-3084]. The purpose of guaranteed loans, financing based on the borrower's recoverable investment in defense production contracts, may also apply to contracts that have been terminated for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CTF-3085]. Certificates of eligibility are not required for contracts that have been totally terminated or the terminated portion of contracts that have been partially terminated [@CL-CTF-3086], and loan guarantees for contract termination financing shall not be provided before specific contract terminations are certain [@CL-CTF-3183].

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.304 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-CTF-4529]; the codified text prints the surety bond rules in FAR 32.304-7 [@CL-CTF-3080] and the rules on other borrowing in FAR 32.304-8 [@CL-CTF-3083]. RFO FAR 32.305 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-CTF-4530]; the codified text prints the rules for guarantees for terminated contracts in FAR 32.305 [@CL-CTF-3085].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A substantial share of a contractor's defense contracts are covered by surety bonds, so the agency does not authorize the guarantee of a loan on a bonded contract until the surety enters into an agreement with the lender to subordinate its rights and claims to the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-3081; @CL-CTF-3176]. The contractor later asks to borrow 400,000 outside the guarantee, and the agency consents with a reasonable limit of 250,000, so the contractor may borrow no more than that [@CL-CTF-3178]. If both loans come from the same lender, any collateral the lender requests for the unguaranteed loan must also be secondary collateral for the guaranteed loan [@CL-CTF-3179]. The contractor sends documentation to the guaranteeing agency at intervals not longer than 30 days, so a 45-day gap would not meet the requirement [@CL-CTF-3084]. When a contract is later terminated in total for the convenience of the Government and the termination is certain, a guarantee for that work does not need a certificate of eligibility [@CL-CTF-3086; @CL-CTF-3183]. This example uses the codified text only, and RFO FAR 32.304 and RFO FAR 32.305 are marked reserved [@CL-CTF-4529; @CL-CTF-4530].
