## Purpose

Defines the contract price used to make progress payments and to apply their limitation, and sets the contracting officer's adjustments after a price reduction.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0955] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.501-3 [@CL-CTF-0955] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0955] |
| Applies to | Making progress payments and determining the limitation on progress payments [@CL-CTF-1057] |

For the purpose of making progress payments and determining the limitation on progress payments, the contract price under firm-fixed price contracts is the current amount fixed by the contract plus the not-to-exceed amount for any unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-0955]. If the contract is redeterminable or subject to economic price adjustment, the contract price is the initial price until modified [@CL-CTF-1058]. Under a fixed-price incentive contract, the contract price is the target price plus the not-to-exceed amount of unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-1059], and if the contractor's properly incurred costs exceed the target price, the contracting officer may provisionally increase the price up to the ceiling or maximum price [@CL-CTF-1060].

Under a letter contract, the contract price is the maximum amount obligated by the contract as modified [@CL-CTF-1061]. Under an unpriced order issued against a basic ordering agreement, it is the maximum amount obligated by the order, as modified [@CL-CTF-1062]. Any portion of the contract specifically providing for reimbursement of costs only is excluded from the contract price [@CL-CTF-0960; @CL-CTF-1057].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall not make progress payments or increase the contract price beyond the funds obligated under the contract, as amended [@CL-CTF-0956]. The DFARS lets the contracting officer approve progress payments when the contract price exceeds the funds obligated under the contract, provided the contract limits the Government's liability [@CL-CTF-1063]. The limit is the lesser of the applicable rate, meaning the lower of the progress payment rate, the liquidation rate or the loss-ratio adjusted rate, or 100 percent of the funds obligated [@CL-CTF-0957].

If a retroactive downward price reduction occurs under a redeterminable contract that provides for progress payments, the contracting officer has the duties listed in the paragraph [@CL-CTF-1064]. The contracting officer determines the refund due and obtains repayment from the contractor for the excess of payments made for delivered items over amounts due as recomputed at the reduced prices [@CL-CTF-0958], and increases the unliquidated progress payments amount for overdeductions made from the contractor's billings for items delivered [@CL-CTF-1065]. The contracting officer shall also increase the unliquidated progress payments amount if the contractor makes an interim or voluntary price reduction under a redeterminable or incentive contract [@CL-CTF-0959]. Funding limits are treated in [Limitation of cost](../funding/ctf-limitation-of-cost.md) and [Limitation of funds](../funding/ctf-limitation-of-funds.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A firm-fixed-price contract has a fixed price of 1,000,000 and an unpriced modification with a not-to-exceed amount of 200,000, so the contract price for progress payments is 1,200,000. If the funds obligated are 1,000,000, the contracting officer may not make progress payments beyond 1,000,000. On a separate incentive contract, if the contractor makes a voluntary price reduction, the contracting officer also increases the unliquidated progress payments amount.

## Never confuse

The contract price for progress payments is set by contract type and is not always the amount first awarded: a firm-fixed price contract adds the not-to-exceed amount for unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-0955], while a redeterminable contract uses the initial price until modified [@CL-CTF-1058]. The funds obligated are a separate ceiling on progress payments and on the contract price [@CL-CTF-0956]. For the financing itself, see [Progress payments](ctf-progress-payments.md).
