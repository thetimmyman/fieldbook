## Purpose

Defines the contract price used to make progress payments and to apply their limitation, and sets the contracting officer's adjustments after a price reduction.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0955] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.501-3 [@CL-CTF-0955] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.501-3 [@CL-CTF-4287; @CL-CTF-4288; @CL-CTF-4289; @CL-CTF-4290; @CL-CTF-4291; @CL-CTF-4292; @CL-CTF-4293]; RFO FAR 32.503-11 [@CL-CTF-9277; @CL-CTF-9278; @CL-CTF-9279; @CL-CTF-9280] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0955] |
| Applies to | Making progress payments and determining the limitation on progress payments [@CL-CTF-1057] |

For the purpose of making progress payments and determining the limitation on progress payments, the contract price under firm-fixed price contracts is the current amount fixed by the contract plus the not-to-exceed amount for any unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-0955]. If the contract is redeterminable or subject to economic price adjustment, the contract price is the initial price until modified [@CL-CTF-1058]. Under a fixed-price incentive contract, the contract price is the target price plus the not-to-exceed amount of unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-1059], and if the contractor's properly incurred costs exceed the target price, the contracting officer may provisionally increase the price up to the ceiling or maximum price [@CL-CTF-1060].

Under a letter contract, the contract price is the maximum amount obligated by the contract as modified [@CL-CTF-1061]. Under an unpriced order issued against a basic ordering agreement, it is the maximum amount obligated by the order, as modified [@CL-CTF-1062]. Any portion of the contract specifically providing for reimbursement of costs only shall be excluded from the contract price [@CL-CTF-0960; @CL-CTF-1057].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall not make progress payments or increase the contract price beyond the funds obligated under the contract, as amended [@CL-CTF-0956]. DFARS 232.501-3(b) says the contracting officer may approve progress payments when the contract price exceeds the funds obligated under the contract, provided the contract limits the Government's liability to the lesser of [@CL-CTF-1063] the applicable rate (the lower of the progress payment rate, the liquidation rate, or the loss-ratio adjusted rate) or 100 percent of the funds obligated [@CL-CTF-0957].

If a retroactive downward price reduction occurs under a redeterminable contract that provides for progress payments, the contracting officer shall determine the refund due and obtain repayment from the contractor for the excess of payments made for delivered items over amounts due as recomputed at the reduced prices [@CL-CTF-1064; @CL-CTF-0958], and shall increase the unliquidated progress payments amount for overdeductions made from the contractor's billings for items delivered [@CL-CTF-1065]; the two duties are joined by "; and" [@CL-CTF-2996]. The contracting officer shall also increase the unliquidated progress payments amount if the contractor makes an interim or voluntary price reduction under a redeterminable or incentive contract [@CL-CTF-0959]. Funding limits are treated in [Limitation of cost](../funding/ctf-limitation-of-cost.md) and [Limitation of funds](../funding/ctf-limitation-of-funds.md).

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.501-3(a) says that for the purpose of making progress payments and determining the limitation on progress payments, the contract price must be as follows: under firm-fixed price contracts, the current amount fixed by the contract plus the not-to-exceed amount for any unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-4287]; if the contract is redeterminable or subject to economic price adjustment, the initial price until modified [@CL-CTF-4288]; and under a fixed-price incentive contract, the target price plus the not-to-exceed amount of unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-4289]. However, if the contractor’s properly incurred costs exceed the target price, the contracting officer may provisionally increase the price up to the ceiling or maximum price [@CL-CTF-4290]. Under a letter contract, the contract price is the maximum amount obligated by the contract as modified, and under an unpriced order issued against a basic ordering agreement, it is the maximum amount obligated by the order, as modified [@CL-CTF-4291]. Any portion of the contract specifically providing for reimbursement of costs only must be excluded from the contract price [@CL-CTF-4292]. The contracting officer must not make progress payments or increase the contract price beyond the funds obligated under the contract, as amended [@CL-CTF-4293]. Where the codified text says "shall", RFO FAR 32.501-3 says "must" [@CL-CTF-0955; @CL-CTF-0960; @CL-CTF-0956; @CL-CTF-4287; @CL-CTF-4292; @CL-CTF-4293].

RFO FAR 32.503-11(a) says that if a retroactive downward price reduction occurs under a redeterminable contract that provides for progress payments, the contracting officer must [@CL-CTF-9277] determine the refund due and obtain repayment from the contractor for the excess of payments made for delivered items over amounts due as recomputed at the reduced prices [@CL-CTF-9278], and increase the unliquidated progress payments amount for overdeductions made from the contractor's billings for items delivered [@CL-CTF-9279]; the two duties are joined by "; and" [@CL-CTF-2997]. The contracting officer must also increase the unliquidated progress payments amount if the contractor makes an interim or voluntary price reduction under a redeterminable or incentive contract [@CL-CTF-9280]. Where the codified text says "shall", RFO FAR 32.503-11 says "must" [@CL-CTF-1064; @CL-CTF-0959; @CL-CTF-9277; @CL-CTF-9280].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A firm-fixed-price contract has a fixed price of 1,000,000 and an unpriced modification with a not-to-exceed amount of 200,000, so the contract price for progress payments is 1,200,000. If the funds obligated are 1,000,000, the contracting officer may not make progress payments beyond 1,000,000. On a separate incentive contract, if the contractor makes a voluntary price reduction, the contracting officer also increases the unliquidated progress payments amount.

## Never confuse

Under firm-fixed price contracts, the contract price for progress payments is the current amount fixed by the contract plus the not-to-exceed amount for any unpriced modifications [@CL-CTF-0955], while if the contract is redeterminable or subject to economic price adjustment it is the initial price until modified [@CL-CTF-1058]. The contracting officer shall not make progress payments or increase the contract price beyond the funds obligated under the contract, as amended [@CL-CTF-0956]. For the financing itself, see [Progress payments](ctf-progress-payments.md).
