## Purpose

Shows what the Advance Payments clause requires for payment, the special account, the use of funds and liquidation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0940] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-12 [@CL-CTF-0940] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0940] |
| Applies to | Contracts that contain the basic Advance Payments clause [@CL-CTF-0940; @CL-CTF-1014] |

Under the basic clause, advance payments will be made upon submission of properly certified invoices or vouchers by the contractor and approval by the administering office, or under a letter of credit [@CL-CTF-0940]. The special account paragraph applies until two conditions are both met: the contractor has liquidated all advance payments and related interest charges, and the administering office has approved in writing the release of any funds due and payable to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0941]. While it applies, in the basic clause, all advance payments and other payments are made by check payable to the contractor marked for deposit only in the contractor's special account [@CL-CTF-0942]. Withdrawals may be made only by a check of the contractor countersigned by the contracting officer or a designated Government countersigning agent [@CL-CTF-0943].

The contractor may withdraw funds from the special account only to pay properly allocable, allowable, and reasonable costs for direct materials, direct labor, and indirect costs, and other withdrawals require written approval by the administering office [@CL-CTF-0944].

These paragraphs describe the basic clause. Alternate I is for an agency that wants to waive the countersignature requirement because of the contractor's financial strength, good performance record, and favorable experience concerning cost disallowances [@CL-CTF-1010]. For a cost-reimbursement contract, Alternate II substitutes its own paragraphs (c) and (e) and subparagraphs (f)(1) and (f)(2) [@CL-CTF-1011]; it limits withdrawals to allowable costs as prescribed by the cost clause of the contract, with other expenses approved in writing by the administering office [@CL-CTF-1012], and it withholds further payments when the sum of unliquidated advance payments, unpaid interest and other payments equals the total estimated cost, not including fixed fee [@CL-CTF-1013]. Alternate V is inserted instead of the basic clause if the requirement for a special account is eliminated under the security provisions of the advance payments subpart [@CL-CTF-1014].

## How it works

When the administering office asks in writing, the contractor shall repay any unliquidated advance payments that the office considers to exceed its current requirements or the amount specified in the clause [@CL-CTF-0945]. In the basic clause, when the sum of all unliquidated advance payments, unpaid interest and other payments exceeds the inserted percent of the contract price, the Government shall withhold further payments [@CL-CTF-0946]. On completion or termination, the Government deducts all unliquidated advance payments and interest charges payable from the amount due [@CL-CTF-0947].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has a price of 2,000,000 and the clause sets the percent at 25, so the limit is 500,000. Unliquidated advance payments are 300,000, unpaid interest is 20,000 and other payments are 200,000. The sum is 520,000, which is over the limit, so the Government withholds further payments. At completion it deducts the remaining advance balance and interest from the final amount due.

## Never confuse

Repayment on written request is different from the deduction at completion or termination [@CL-CTF-0945; @CL-CTF-0947]. The special account's check-and-countersignature rule governs withdrawals, while the use of funds paragraph limits what the money may be spent on [@CL-CTF-0943; @CL-CTF-0944]. For interest on unliquidated balances, see [Advance payments: exclusions and interest](ctf-advance-payment-exclusions-and-interest.md); for the agreement and liens that protect the Government, see [Advance payments: security and the special account](ctf-advance-payment-security-and-special-account.md); for how advance payments are authorized in the first place, see [Advance payments for non-commercial items](ctf-advance-payments-for-non-commercial-items.md); for liquidation of progress payments, see [Progress payment liquidation](ctf-progress-payment-liquidation.md).
