## Purpose

Shows what the Advance Payments clause requires for payment, the special account, the use of funds and liquidation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0940] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-12 [@CL-CTF-0940] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-12(a) [@CL-CTF-0810]; RFO FAR 52.232-12(b) [@CL-CTF-0811; @CL-CTF-0812; @CL-CTF-0813]; RFO FAR 52.232-12(c) [@CL-CTF-0814]; RFO FAR 52.232-12(d) [@CL-CTF-0815]; RFO FAR 52.232-12(e) [@CL-CTF-0816; @CL-CTF-0817]; RFO FAR 52.232-12 Alternate I [@CL-CTF-0818]; RFO FAR 52.232-12 Alternate II(c) and (e) [@CL-CTF-0819; @CL-CTF-0820]; RFO FAR 52.232-12 Alternate V [@CL-CTF-0821] |
| Edition date | Apr 1984 [@CL-CTF-0818] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0940] |
| Applies to | Contracts that contain the basic Advance Payments clause [@CL-CTF-0940; @CL-CTF-1014] |

Under the basic clause, advance payments will be made upon submission of properly certified invoices or vouchers by the contractor and approval by the administering office, or under a letter of credit [@CL-CTF-0940], but the amount of the invoice or voucher submitted plus all advance payments previously approved shall not exceed the dollar amount inserted in the clause [@CL-CTF-9517], and if a letter of credit is used, the contractor shall withdraw cash only when needed for disbursements acceptable under the contract and report cash disbursements and balances as required by the administering office [@CL-CTF-9518]. The special account paragraph applies until two conditions are both met: the contractor has liquidated all advance payments and related interest charges, and the administering office has approved in writing the release of any funds due and payable to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0941]. While it applies, in the basic clause, all advance payments and other payments shall be made by check payable to the contractor marked for deposit only in the contractor's special account [@CL-CTF-0942]. Withdrawals may be made only by a check of the contractor countersigned by the contracting officer or by a Government countersigning agent the contracting officer designates in writing [@CL-CTF-0943].

The contractor may withdraw funds from the special account only to pay properly allocable, allowable, and reasonable costs for direct materials, direct labor, and indirect costs, and other withdrawals require written approval by the administering office [@CL-CTF-0944].

These paragraphs describe the basic clause. Alternate I is for an agency that wants to waive the countersignature requirement because of the contractor's financial strength, good performance record, and favorable experience concerning cost disallowances, adding a sentence to paragraph (b) of the basic clause if appropriate, and that sentence says that, for this contract, countersignature on behalf of the Government will not be required unless it is determined necessary by the administering office [@CL-CTF-1010; @CL-CTF-9495]. For a cost-reimbursement contract, Alternate II substitutes its own paragraphs (c) and (e) and subparagraphs (f)(1) and (f)(2) [@CL-CTF-1011]; it limits withdrawals to allowable costs as prescribed by the clause of the contract named in the blank of that paragraph, with other expenses approved in writing by the administering office [@CL-CTF-1012], and it withholds further payments when the sum of unliquidated advance payments, unpaid interest and other payments equals the total estimated cost, not including fixed fee [@CL-CTF-1013]. Alternate V is inserted instead of the basic clause if the requirement for a special account is eliminated in accordance with FAR 32.409-3(e) or (g) [@CL-CTF-1014].

## How it works

When the administering office asks in writing, the contractor shall repay any part of unliquidated advance payments that the office considers to exceed the contractor's current requirements or the amount specified in paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-CTF-0945]. In the basic clause, when the sum of all unliquidated advance payments, unpaid interest and other payments exceeds the inserted percent of the contract price, the Government shall withhold further payments [@CL-CTF-0946]. On completion or termination of the contract, the Government shall deduct from the amount due to the contractor all unliquidated advance payments and all interest charges payable [@CL-CTF-0947].

The RFO text applies to DoD contracting because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-12 keeps the payment requirement in paragraph (a) [@CL-CTF-0810; @CL-CTF-0940], together with the limit on the invoice or voucher amount plus advance payments previously approved and the rule that cash is withdrawn under a letter of credit only when needed for disbursements acceptable under the contract [@CL-CTF-9519; @CL-CTF-9520; @CL-CTF-9517; @CL-CTF-9518]. It keeps, in paragraph (b) of the basic clause, the two conditions for the end of the special account paragraph, the rule that payments are made by check marked for deposit only in the special account, and the countersignature rule for withdrawals [@CL-CTF-0811; @CL-CTF-0812; @CL-CTF-0813; @CL-CTF-0941; @CL-CTF-0942; @CL-CTF-0943]. It keeps the use-of-funds rule in paragraph (c) [@CL-CTF-0814; @CL-CTF-0944], the repayment rule in paragraph (d) [@CL-CTF-0815; @CL-CTF-0945], and the withholding and deduction rules in paragraph (e) [@CL-CTF-0816; @CL-CTF-0817; @CL-CTF-0946; @CL-CTF-0947]. Alternate I keeps the waiver of the countersignature requirement [@CL-CTF-0818; @CL-CTF-1010]. The sentence that Alternate I adds to paragraph (b) says that, for the contract, countersignature on behalf of the Government will not be required unless the administering office determines it necessary [@CL-CTF-9185], as the codified added sentence does [@CL-CTF-9495]. Alternate II keeps its paragraph (c) on use of funds and its paragraph (e) on the maximum payment [@CL-CTF-0819; @CL-CTF-0820; @CL-CTF-1012; @CL-CTF-1013], and Alternate V keeps the rule that its clause is inserted instead of the basic clause if the requirement for a special account is eliminated [@CL-CTF-0821; @CL-CTF-1014]. RFO FAR 52.232-12(p) says that, to the extent the Government considers it necessary while advance payments remain outstanding, the Contractor, without the prior written consent of the administering office, shall not take the listed actions [@CL-CTF-9186]. RFO FAR 52.232-12(p) adds one of them to the basic clause, as item (11): the Contractor shall not deposit any of its funds except in a bank or trust company insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation or a credit union insured by the National Credit Union Administration [@CL-CTF-9187]. The title of the basic clause prints "Advance Payments (Deviation Date)" [@CL-CTF-2911], and the title of the Alternate V clause prints "Advance Payments Without Special Account (Date Deviation)" [@CL-CTF-2912].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has a price of 2,000,000 and the clause sets the percent at 25, so the limit is 500,000. Unliquidated advance payments are 300,000, unpaid interest is 20,000 and other payments are 200,000. The sum is 520,000, which is over the limit, so the Government withholds further payments. At completion it deducts the remaining advance balance and interest from the final amount due.

## Never confuse

The contractor repays on written request of the administering office [@CL-CTF-0945] rather than only at the end, and on completion or termination of the contract the Government deducts the unliquidated advance payments and interest charges payable from the amount due [@CL-CTF-0947]. The special account's check-and-countersignature rule governs withdrawals rather than what the money may be spent on, which the use of funds paragraph limits [@CL-CTF-0943; @CL-CTF-0944]. For interest on unliquidated balances, see [Advance payments: exclusions and interest](ctf-advance-payment-exclusions-and-interest.md); for the agreement and liens that protect the Government, see [Advance payments: security and the special account](ctf-advance-payment-security-and-special-account.md); for how advance payments are authorized in the first place, see [Advance payments for non-commercial items](ctf-advance-payments-for-non-commercial-items.md); for liquidation of progress payments, see [Progress payment liquidation](ctf-progress-payment-liquidation.md).

## Company calibration

Confirm the percent and dollar amounts inserted in the clause, the bank holding the special account, and who tracks the unliquidated balance. [@Q-026]
