## Purpose

Gives the FAR definitions that mark out a contract financing payment, and the rules for when the paying office must pay a financing request.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1500] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.001 and FAR 32.007 [@CL-CTF-1500; @CL-CTF-1505] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.001 [@CL-CTF-4317; @CL-CTF-4318; @CL-CTF-4319; @CL-CTF-4320; @CL-CTF-4321; @CL-CTF-4322; @CL-CTF-4323], RFO FAR 32.007 [@CL-CTF-4324; @CL-CTF-4325; @CL-CTF-4326] and RFO FAR 32.009 [@CL-CTF-4758] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1500] |
| Applies to | Contract financing requests received by a designated billing office, subject to agency policy and the separate rule for advance payments and similar arrangements [@CL-CTF-1505; @CL-CTF-1508] |

A contract financing payment is an authorized Government disbursement of monies to a contractor prior to acceptance of supplies or services by the Government [@CL-CTF-1500]. The definition lists advance payments, performance-based payments, commercial advance and interim payments, and progress payments based on cost under the Progress Payments clause among the contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-1501]. The list also includes progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion, except those made under the Fixed-Price Construction Contracts clause or the Fixed-Price Architect-Engineer Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-1555], and interim payments under a cost reimbursement contract, except for a cost reimbursement contract for services when Alternate I of the Prompt Payment clause is used [@CL-CTF-1556]. Contract financing payments do not include invoice payments, payments for partial deliveries, or lease and rental payments [@CL-CTF-1502].

A commercial interim payment is any payment that is not a commercial advance payment or a delivery payment, and these payments are contract financing payments for prompt payment purposes [@CL-CTF-1557]. A delivery payment is a payment for accepted supplies or services, including accepted partial deliveries; commercial financing payments are liquidated by deduction from delivery payments, and delivery payments are invoice payments for prompt payment purposes [@CL-CTF-1550]. An invoice payment is a Government disbursement of monies to a contractor under a contract or other authorization for supplies or services accepted by the Government [@CL-CTF-1551]. Invoice payments include payments for accepted partial deliveries and final cost or fee payments where amounts owed have been settled [@CL-CTF-1552]. For purposes of subpart 32.9 only, they also include all payments made under the Fixed-Price Construction Contracts clause and the Fixed-Price Architect-Engineer Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-1553]. They include interim payments under a cost-reimbursement contract for services when Alternate I of the Prompt Payment clause is used, and invoice payments do not include contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-1554].

To liquidate is to decrease a payment for an accepted supply item or service under a contract so as to recoup financing payments previously paid, and unusual contract financing is any financing not deemed customary contract financing by the agency [@CL-CTF-1503]. The designated payment office is the office designated in the contract to make invoice payments or contract financing payments, normally the Government disbursing office, and the due date is the date on which payment should be made [@CL-CTF-1504]. The designated billing office is the office or person, governmental or nongovernmental, designated in the contract where the contractor first submits invoices and contract financing requests [@CL-CTF-1558].

## How it works

Unless otherwise prescribed in agency policies and procedures or otherwise specified in the rule for advance payments, loans, or other arrangements that do not involve recurrent submission of contract financing requests, the due date for making contract financing payments by the designated payment office is the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper contract financing request [@CL-CTF-1505; @CL-CTF-1508]. If an audit or other review of a specific financing request is required to ensure compliance with the terms and conditions of the contract, the designated payment office is not compelled to make payment by the specified due date [@CL-CTF-1506]. Agency heads may prescribe shorter periods for payment based on contract pricing or administrative considerations [@CL-CTF-1559]. For example, a shorter period may be justified by an agency if the nature and extent of contract financing arrangements are integrated with agency contract pricing policies, and agency heads must not prescribe a period shorter than 7 days or longer than 30 days [@CL-CTF-1507]. For advance payments, loans, or other arrangements that do not involve recurrent submission of contract financing requests, the designated payment office will make payment in accordance with the applicable contract financing terms or as directed by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-1508]. A proper contract financing request must comply with the terms and conditions specified by the contract, and the contractor must correct any defects in requests submitted in the manner specified in the contract or as directed by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-1560]. The designated billing office and designated payment office must annotate each contract financing request with the date their respective offices received the request [@CL-CTF-1561]. The Government will not pay an interest penalty to the contractor for delayed contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-1509].

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.001 provides that contract financing payments may include the listed payments [@CL-CTF-4317], among them commercial advance, installment, and interim payments and progress payments based on cost under the clause at 52.232-16, Progress Payments [@CL-CTF-4318]. The list also includes progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion, except those made under the Fixed-Price Construction Contracts clause or the Fixed-Price Architect-Engineer Contracts clause [@CL-CTF-4319], and interim payments under a cost reimbursement contract, except for a cost reimbursement contract for services when Alternate I of the Prompt Payment clause is used [@CL-CTF-4320]. Contract financing payments do not include invoice payments, payments for partial deliveries, or lease and rental payments [@CL-CTF-4321]. Invoice payments include payments for partial deliveries that have been accepted by the Government and final cost or fee payments where amounts owed have been settled between the Government and the contractor [@CL-CTF-4323]. RFO FAR 32.001 defines a commercial interim payment as any payment that is not a commercial advance payment or a delivery payment, and these payments are contract financing payments and not subject to the interest penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act in subpart 32.9 [@CL-CTF-4322]. RFO FAR 32.007(a) provides that agency heads must not establish a time frame for making contract financing payments that is less than 7 days or exceeds 30 days from receipt of a proper contract financing request, subject to the provisions outlined in 32.009 [@CL-CTF-4324]. RFO FAR 32.009 is titled Providing accelerated payments to small business contractors and to prime contractors that subcontract with a small business concern [@CL-CTF-4758]. RFO FAR 32.007(b) provides that a proper contract financing request must comply with the terms and conditions specified by the contract, and that the contractor must correct any defects in requests submitted in the manner specified in the contract or as directed by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-4325]. RFO FAR 32.007(c) provides that the Government will not pay an interest penalty to the contractor as a result of delayed contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-4326].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A billing office receives a proper financing request for 200,000 on a given day, and the agency has set no shorter period. The payment office is due to pay on the thirtieth day after receipt. If an audit of that request is required to ensure compliance with the contract terms, the payment office is not compelled to pay by that day, and no interest penalty is owed for the delay. The thirtieth-day answer comes from the codified text [@CL-CTF-1505]. Under the RFO text, an agency head must not establish a time frame shorter than 7 days or longer than 30 days from receipt of a proper request, subject to the provisions outlined in 32.009 [@CL-CTF-4324], no paragraph of RFO section 32.007 states the thirtieth-day due date or the audit exception [@CL-CTF-4324; @CL-CTF-4325; @CL-CTF-4326], and no interest penalty is paid on delayed contract financing payments [@CL-CTF-4326].

Answer: payment is due on day thirty, and a required audit can defer it without an interest penalty.

## Never confuse

A contract financing payment is an authorized Government disbursement of monies to a contractor prior to acceptance of supplies or services by the Government [@CL-CTF-1500], and contract financing payments do not include invoice payments or payments for partial deliveries [@CL-CTF-1502]. A delivery payment is a payment for accepted supplies or services, and commercial financing payments are liquidated by deduction from delivery payments [@CL-CTF-1550]. For invoice payment due dates, see [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md). Under codified FAR 32.007, unless otherwise prescribed in agency policies and procedures or otherwise specified in the rule for advance payments, loans, or other arrangements, the due date for making contract financing payments is the 30th day after the designated billing office receives a proper contract financing request [@CL-CTF-1505; @CL-CTF-1508]. Under RFO FAR 32.007(a), agency heads must not establish a time frame for making contract financing payments that is less than 7 days or exceeds 30 days from receipt of a proper contract financing request, subject to the provisions outlined in 32.009 [@CL-CTF-4324]. For customary and unusual financing, see [Providing contract financing and customary contract financing](ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md), and for the financing types, see [Contract financing at a glance](ctf-financing-overview.md). For the FAR descriptions of each financing method and the payment clauses, see [Contract financing methods and payment clauses for noncommercial purchases](ctf-financing-methods-and-noncommercial-payment-clauses.md); for the progress payments clause, see [Progress payments clauses and supervision of progress payments](ctf-progress-payment-clauses-and-supervision.md), and for advance payments, see [Assignment of claims under guaranteed loans and when advance payments may be considered](ctf-loan-guarantee-assignment-and-advance-payment-applicability.md).
