## Purpose

Shows when a contracting officer may not authorize the Governmentwide commercial purchase card as a method of payment under a contract that carries the tax on certain foreign procurements.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3838] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.1108-70 [@CL-CTF-3838] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3838] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the foreign procurement tax clause [@CL-CTF-3838] |

The contracting officer shall not authorize the Governmentwide commercial purchase card as a method of payment during any contract period of performance if the contract includes the clause at FAR 52.229-12, Tax on Certain Foreign Procurements [@CL-CTF-3838]. The prohibition does not apply if the contract also includes the clause at 252.229-7014, Full Exemption from Two-Percent Excise Tax on Certain Foreign Procurements, indicating that the contractor is fully exempt from the tax [@CL-CTF-3839].

## How it works

The trigger is the presence of the FAR 52.229-12 clause in the contract, and the exception is the presence of the 252.229-7014 clause in that same contract [@CL-CTF-3838; @CL-CTF-3839]. The prohibition runs during any contract period of performance [@CL-CTF-3838]. The duty falls on the contracting officer, who decides whether to authorize the card, and no contractor action is required by this section [@CL-CTF-3838]. For a contractor, the effect is that the contracting officer is barred from authorizing card payment under a contract with the tax clause unless the exemption clause is also in the contract [@CL-CTF-3838; @CL-CTF-3839].

This section only removes its own bar. It does not say that the card may be authorized once the exemption clause is present, and the card rules in the FAR are on [EFT applicability and payment by Governmentwide commercial purchase card](ctf-eft-applicability-and-purchase-card-payment.md). The tax and its clauses are on [The excise tax on certain foreign procurements](../billing-payment/ctf-foreign-procurement-excise-tax.md) and [Foreign tax reporting and the two-percent excise tax exemption](../billing-payment/ctf-dfars-foreign-tax-reporting-and-excise-tax-exemption.md). The card clause that makes card payment mandatory for small orders is on [Mandatory purchase card payment and the advance payment pool clause](../billing-payment/ctf-dfars-purchase-card-and-advance-payment-pool.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract includes the clause at FAR 52.229-12 and does not include the clause at 252.229-7014. The contracting officer shall not authorize the purchase card as the method of payment during any period of performance of that contract, even for an order of 2,500 [@CL-CTF-3838; @CL-CTF-3839]. On a second contract that includes both clauses, indicating that the contractor is fully exempt from the tax, this prohibition does not apply [@CL-CTF-3839]. Whether the card may then be authorized depends on the other card rules, not on this section.

## Never confuse

This is not a general ban on the purchase card. The prohibition applies to a contract that includes the FAR 52.229-12 clause, and it does not apply where the 252.229-7014 clause is also included [@CL-CTF-3838; @CL-CTF-3839]. It also does not decide whether a contract includes either clause.
