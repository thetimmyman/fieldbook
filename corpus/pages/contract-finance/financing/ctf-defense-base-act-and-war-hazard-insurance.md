## Purpose

Shows what workers' compensation coverage the contractor sets up before work, the reports and payments it must make, how that duty reaches subcontractors, and what applies to employees for whom the Defense Base Act has been waived.

The Contractor shall, before commencing performance under this contract, establish provisions to provide for the payment of disability compensation and medical benefits to covered employees and death benefits to their eligible survivors, by purchasing workers' compensation insurance or qualifying as a self-insurer [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2494]. The self-insurer route is qualifying under the Longshore and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act as extended by the Defense Base Act, and the Contractor shall continue to maintain provisions to provide such Defense Base Act benefits until contract performance is completed [@CL-CTF-2721]. The Contractor shall insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), in all subcontracts to which the Defense Base Act applies [@CL-CTF-2495].

Within ten days of an employee's injury or death or from the date the Contractor has knowledge of the injury or death, the Contractor shall submit Form LS-202, Employee's First Report of Injury or Occupational Illness, to the Department of Labor [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2722]. The Contractor shall pay all compensation due for disability or death within the time frames required by the Longshore and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2723]. The Contractor shall provide for medical care as required by that Act [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2724]. If controverting the right to compensation, the Contractor shall submit Form LS-207, Notice of Controversion of Right to Compensation, to the Department of Labor in accordance with that Act [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2725]. Immediately upon making the first payment of compensation in any case, the Contractor shall submit Form LS-206, Payment Of Compensation Without Award, to the Department of Labor in accordance with that Act [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2726]. When payments are suspended or when making the final payment, the Contractor shall submit Form LS-208, Notice of Final Payment or Suspension of Compensation Payments, to the Department of Labor in accordance with that Act [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2727]. The Contractor shall adhere to all other provisions of that Act as extended by the Defense Base Act, and Department of Labor regulations at 20 CFR Parts 701 to 704 [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2728].

Paragraph (a) of the overseas clause applies if the Contractor employs any person who, but for a waiver granted by the Secretary of Labor, would be subject to workers' compensation insurance under the Defense Base Act [@CL-CTF-2729]. For employees for whom the applicability of the Defense Base Act has been waived, the Contractor shall provide, before commencing performance, at least the workers' compensation insurance or equivalent that the laws of the country of which these employees are nationals may require [@CL-CTF-2496]. The Contractor shall continue to maintain that insurance or equivalent until performance is completed [@CL-CTF-2730]. The Contractor shall insert, in all subcontracts under the contract to which the Defense Base Act would apply but for the waiver, a clause similar to paragraph (a), including the sentence requiring that insertion, imposing on those subcontractors the requirement to provide such workers' compensation insurance coverage [@CL-CTF-2580].

Paragraph (b) of the overseas clause applies if the Contractor or any subcontractor employs any person who, but for a waiver granted by the Secretary of Labor, would be subject to the War Hazards Compensation Act [@CL-CTF-2581]. On behalf of employees for whom the applicability of the Defense Base Act has been waived, the Contractor shall, subject to reimbursement as provided elsewhere in the contract, afford the same protection as that provided in the War Hazards Compensation Act [@CL-CTF-2582]. That protection has one exception: the level of benefits shall conform to any law or international agreement controlling the benefits to which the employees may be entitled, and in all other respects the standards of the War Hazards Compensation Act shall apply [@CL-CTF-2583]. The standards of that Act that apply include, for example, the definition of war-hazard risks (injury, death, capture, or detention as the result of a war hazard as defined in the Act), proof of loss, and exclusion of benefits otherwise covered by workers' compensation insurance or the equivalent [@CL-CTF-2731]. Unless the Contractor elects to assume directly the liability to subcontractor employees created by the clause, the Contractor shall insert, in all subcontracts to which the War Hazards Compensation Act would apply but for the waiver, a clause similar to paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-2584].

## How it works

Before commencing performance under the contract, the Contractor shall establish provisions to provide for the payment of disability compensation and medical benefits to covered employees and death benefits to their eligible survivors, by purchasing workers' compensation insurance or qualifying as a self-insurer [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2494]. The Contractor shall continue to maintain provisions to provide such Defense Base Act benefits until contract performance is completed [@CL-CTF-2721]. The Contractor shall also submit the first report of injury or death, pay compensation, provide for medical care, and submit the controversion, first-payment and final-payment or suspension forms to the Department of Labor [@CL-CTF-2998; @CL-CTF-2722; @CL-CTF-2723; @CL-CTF-2724; @CL-CTF-2725; @CL-CTF-2726; @CL-CTF-2727; @CL-CTF-2728]. The Contractor shall insert the substance of the clause in all subcontracts to which the Defense Base Act applies [@CL-CTF-2495]. For employees for whom the applicability of the Defense Base Act has been waived, the Contractor shall provide, before commencing performance, at least the workers' compensation insurance or the equivalent that the laws of the country of which these employees are nationals may require, and continue to maintain it until performance is completed [@CL-CTF-2496; @CL-CTF-2730], and shall insert a clause similar to paragraph (a), including the insertion sentence and imposing on those subcontractors the requirement to provide such workers' compensation insurance coverage, in subcontracts under the contract to which the Defense Base Act would apply but for the waiver [@CL-CTF-2580]. The overseas insurance paragraph applies if the Contractor employs any person who, but for a waiver granted by the Secretary of Labor, would be subject to workers' compensation insurance under the Defense Base Act [@CL-CTF-2729]. For employees for whom the applicability of the Defense Base Act has been waived under the war-hazard paragraph, the Contractor shall, subject to reimbursement as provided elsewhere in the contract, afford the same protection as that provided in the War Hazards Compensation Act [@CL-CTF-2582], except that the level of benefits shall conform to any law or international agreement controlling the benefits to which the employees may be entitled, and in all other respects the standards of that Act shall apply, for example the definition of war-hazard risks, proof of loss and the exclusion of benefits otherwise covered [@CL-CTF-2583; @CL-CTF-2731]. Unless the Contractor elects to assume directly the liability to subcontractor employees created by the clause, the Contractor shall insert in subcontracts to which the War Hazards Compensation Act would apply but for the waiver a clause similar to paragraph (b) [@CL-CTF-2584].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract with 40 covered employees is awarded. Before starting work the contractor buys workers' compensation insurance for them, as the clause allows. Had it qualified as a self-insurer under the Longshore and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act as extended by the Defense Base Act instead, it would keep those provisions in place until contract performance is completed. An employee is injured on day 3 of performance. The contractor submits the first report of injury to the Department of Labor within ten days of the injury or of learning of it, provides medical care, and pays the compensation due within the required time frames. If it disputes the claim it files a notice of controversion, on the first payment it files the payment form, and when it suspends payments or makes the final payment it files the notice of final payment or suspension. A subcontract with 8 employees falls under the Defense Base Act, so the contractor inserts the substance of the clause in it. Six other workers have a waiver and are nationals of a third country. Before work starts the contractor provides at least the workers' compensation insurance or equivalent that their country's laws require, and it keeps it in place until performance is completed, and it inserts a similar clause in any subcontract that would have been covered but for the waiver.

The same six workers would otherwise be subject to the War Hazards Compensation Act. The contractor affords them the same protection, subject to reimbursement as provided elsewhere in the contract. Where a law or international agreement controls their benefit levels, the benefits follow it, and all other standards of that Act apply, for example the definition of war-hazard risks, proof of loss, and the exclusion of benefits otherwise covered by workers' compensation insurance or the equivalent. In the subcontracts the contractor inserts a similar clause, unless it elects to assume the liability to subcontractor employees directly.

## Never confuse

Coverage for waived employees differs from Defense Base Act coverage rather than repeating it: for employees for whom the applicability of the Defense Base Act has been waived, the Contractor shall provide at least that workers' compensation insurance or the equivalent as the laws of the country of which these employees are nationals may require [@CL-CTF-2496], unlike covered employees, for whom the Contractor establishes provisions for disability compensation, medical benefits and death benefits by purchasing workers' compensation insurance or qualifying as a self-insurer [@CL-CTF-2494]. Whether a premium is allowable as a cost is covered in [Insurance and indemnification](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-insurance-and-indemnification.md).
