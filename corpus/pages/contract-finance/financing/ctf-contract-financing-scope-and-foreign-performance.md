## Purpose

Shows which sections and subparts of the contract financing part apply to which purchases, which financing methods the part addresses, and what the contracting officer must do before providing financing where a foreign jurisdiction may become involved.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3506] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.000, FAR 32.002 and FAR 32.004 [@CL-CTF-3506; @CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3514] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3506] |
| Applies to | All purchases subject to part 32, with the commercial purchase financing subpart limited to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under part 12 authority [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3511] |

The part prescribes policies and procedures for contract financing and other payment matters [@CL-CTF-3506]. It addresses payment methods, including partial payments and progress payments based on percentage or stage of completion, together with loan guarantees, advance payments and progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3507]. It also addresses administration of debts to the Government arising out of contracts and contract funding, including contract clauses that limit costs or funds [@CL-CTF-3508]. Its remaining topics are assignment of claims to aid in private financing, selected payment clauses, financing of purchases of commercial products and commercial services, performance-based payments and electronic funds transfer payments [@CL-CTF-3606].

Sections 32.000 through 32.009 are applicable to all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-3509]. The same list adds the subparts on loan guarantees for defense production, contract debts, contract funding, assignment of claims, prompt payment and electronic funds transfer [@CL-CTF-3510]. The subpart on commercial product and commercial service purchase financing is applicable only to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under authority of part 12 [@CL-CTF-3511]. The subparts on financing for other than a commercial purchase and on advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-3512]. The subparts on progress payments based on costs and on performance-based payments complete that list [@CL-CTF-3513].

The enforceability of contract provisions for security of Government financing in a foreign jurisdiction depends on local law and procedure [@CL-CTF-3607]. Before providing contract financing where foreign jurisdictions may become involved, the contracting officer shall ensure the Government's security is enforceable, and this may require additional or different security than that normally provided for in the standard contract clauses [@CL-CTF-3514].

## How it works

A purchase subject to part 32 is reached by the opening sections and by the first group of subparts [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3510]. Beyond that group, the authority under which the purchase is made sorts the financing subparts: a purchase under part 12 authority falls under the commercial purchase financing subpart, and a purchase under any other authority falls under the four non-commercial financing subparts [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512; @CL-CTF-3513].

Where foreign jurisdictions may become involved, the duty to ensure enforceable security falls on the contracting officer and comes before the financing is provided [@CL-CTF-3514]. Local law and procedure decide whether the security can be enforced [@CL-CTF-3607]. The section names no particular form of security and no approval route. It says only that additional or different security may be required [@CL-CTF-3514].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buying office awards a commercial product order under part 12 authority and, separately, a development contract under another authority [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512]. Financing of the commercial order falls under the commercial purchase financing subpart, which applies only to part 12 purchases [@CL-CTF-3511]. Financing of the development contract falls under the non-commercial subparts, such as the subpart on progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3512; @CL-CTF-3513]. The prompt payment subpart applies to both purchases [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3510]. Part of the development contract will be performed in a foreign country, so foreign jurisdictions may become involved. Before providing financing, the contracting officer shall ensure that the Government's security is enforceable, and this may require additional or different security than the standard clauses provide [@CL-CTF-3514].

## Never confuse

Applicability is not the order of preference. The authority for the purchase decides which financing subparts apply [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512], while the order in which the contracting officer considers financing methods is covered in [Order of preference among financing methods](ctf-financing-method-order-of-preference.md).

Applicability is also not authority to provide financing. That a subpart applies to a purchase does not by itself settle whether financing is provided, which is covered in [Providing customary contract financing](ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md).
