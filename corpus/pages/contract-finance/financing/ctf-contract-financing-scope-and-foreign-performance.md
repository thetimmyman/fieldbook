## Purpose

Shows which sections and subparts of the contract financing part apply to which purchases, which financing methods the part addresses, and what the contracting officer must do before providing financing where a foreign jurisdiction may become involved.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3506] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.000, FAR 32.002, FAR 32.003 and FAR 32.004 [@CL-CTF-3506; @CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3737; @CL-CTF-3514] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.000, RFO FAR 32.002, RFO FAR 32.003 and RFO FAR 32.004 [@CL-CTF-4274; @CL-CTF-4276; @CL-CTF-4280; @CL-CTF-4281; @CL-CTF-3028; @CL-CTF-4285] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3506] |
| Applies to | All purchases subject to part 32, with the commercial purchase financing subpart limited to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under part 12 authority [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3511] |

The part prescribes policies and procedures for contract financing and other payment matters [@CL-CTF-3506]. It addresses payment methods, including partial payments and progress payments based on percentage or stage of completion, together with loan guarantees, advance payments and progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3507]. It also addresses administration of debts to the Government arising out of contracts and contract funding, including contract clauses that limit costs or funds [@CL-CTF-3508]. Its remaining topics are assignment of claims to aid in private financing, selected payment clauses, financing of purchases of commercial products and commercial services, performance-based payments and electronic funds transfer payments [@CL-CTF-3606].

Sections 32.000 through 32.009 are applicable to all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-3509]. The same list adds the subparts on loan guarantees for defense production, contract debts, contract funding, assignment of claims, prompt payment and electronic funds transfer [@CL-CTF-3510]. The subpart on commercial product and commercial service purchase financing is applicable only to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under authority of part 12 [@CL-CTF-3511]. The subparts on financing for other than a commercial purchase and on advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-3512]. The subparts on progress payments based on costs and on performance-based payments complete that list [@CL-CTF-3513]. Unless agency regulations otherwise permit, contract financing shall not be provided for purchases made under the authority of part 13 [@CL-CTF-3737]. The text names no official who permits or provides it.

The enforceability of contract provisions for security of Government financing in a foreign jurisdiction depends on local law and procedure [@CL-CTF-3607]. Before providing contract financing where foreign jurisdictions may become involved, the contracting officer shall ensure the Government's security is enforceable, and this may require additional or different security than that normally provided for in the standard contract clauses [@CL-CTF-3514].

## How it works

A purchase subject to part 32 is reached by the opening sections and by the first group of subparts [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3510]. Beyond that group, the authority under which the purchase is made sorts the financing subparts: a purchase of commercial products or commercial services under part 12 authority falls under the commercial purchase financing subpart, and a purchase under any other authority falls under the four non-commercial financing subparts [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512; @CL-CTF-3513].

Where foreign jurisdictions may become involved, the duty to ensure enforceable security falls on the contracting officer and comes before the financing is provided [@CL-CTF-3514]. Local law and procedure decide whether the security can be enforced [@CL-CTF-3607]. The section names no particular form of security and no approval route. It says only that additional or different security may be required [@CL-CTF-3514].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.000 says the part addresses policies and procedures for contract financing, funding and other payment matters, where the codified section says the part prescribes them for contract financing and other payment matters [@CL-CTF-4274; @CL-CTF-3506]. It adds a sentence naming three statutory chapters that the part implements and that authorize contract financing guarantees [@CL-CTF-4275]. Its topic list keeps payment methods and loan guarantees, advances and cost-based progress payments [@CL-CTF-3507; @CL-CTF-4276], debts and contract funding [@CL-CTF-3508; @CL-CTF-4277], and assignment of claims, selected payment clauses and commercial financing [@CL-CTF-3606; @CL-CTF-4278]. It then lists financing of other than commercial purchases, performance-based payments, electronic funds transfer payments and fast payment procedures as items (h) to (k), so performance-based payments and electronic funds transfer payments move from (h) and (i) to (i) and (j) [@CL-CTF-3606; @CL-CTF-4279]. RFO FAR 32.002(a) says the listed sections are applicable to all purchases, where the codified lead-in says all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-4280; @CL-CTF-3509], and its list adds Subpart 32.12, Fast Payment Procedure [@CL-CTF-4281]. The rest of RFO FAR 32.002 keeps the rule: the commercial financing subpart is applicable only to commercial purchases under part 12 authority [@CL-CTF-4282], and the subparts on financing for other than a commercial purchase, advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions, progress payments based on costs and performance-based payments are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-4283; @CL-CTF-4284]. RFO FAR 32.003 keeps the limit on financing for part 13 purchases: unless agency regulations otherwise permit, contract financing must not be provided for purchases made under the authority of part 13, with "must not" where the codified text says "shall not" [@CL-CTF-3737; @CL-CTF-3028]. It too names no official who permits or provides it. RFO FAR 32.004 keeps the foreign jurisdiction rule: enforceability of security depends on local law and procedure [@CL-CTF-4285], and before providing financing where foreign jurisdictions may become involved the contracting officer must ensure the Government’s security is enforceable, which may require additional or different security [@CL-CTF-4286].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buying office awards a commercial product order under part 12 authority and, separately, a development contract under another authority [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512]. Financing of the commercial order falls under the commercial purchase financing subpart, which applies only to part 12 purchases [@CL-CTF-3511]. Financing of the development contract falls under the non-commercial subparts, such as the subpart on progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3512; @CL-CTF-3513]. The prompt payment subpart applies to both purchases [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3510]. Part of the development contract will be performed in a foreign country, so foreign jurisdictions may become involved. Before providing financing, the contracting officer shall ensure that the Government's security is enforceable, and this may require additional or different security than the standard clauses provide [@CL-CTF-3514].

## Never confuse

Applicability is not the order of preference. The authority for the purchase decides which financing subparts apply [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512], while the order in which the contracting officer considers financing methods is covered in [Order of preference among financing methods](ctf-financing-method-order-of-preference.md).

Applicability is also not authority to provide financing. That a subpart applies to a purchase does not by itself settle whether financing is provided, which is covered in [Providing customary contract financing](ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md).

The codified lead-in applies the listed sections to all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-3509], while the RFO lead-in says all purchases and its list of subparts adds Subpart 32.12, Fast Payment Procedure [@CL-CTF-4280; @CL-CTF-4281].
