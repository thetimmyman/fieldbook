## Purpose

Shows which sections and subparts of the contract financing part apply to which purchases, which financing methods the part addresses, and what the contracting officer must do before providing financing where a foreign jurisdiction may become involved.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3506] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.000, FAR 32.002, FAR 32.003 and FAR 32.004 [@CL-CTF-3506; @CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3737; @CL-CTF-3514] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.000, RFO FAR 32.002, RFO FAR 32.003 and RFO FAR 32.004 [@CL-CTF-4274; @CL-CTF-4276; @CL-CTF-4280; @CL-CTF-4281; @CL-CTF-3028; @CL-CTF-4285] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3506] |
| Applies to | All purchases subject to part 32, with the commercial purchase financing subpart limited to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under part 12 authority [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3511] |

The part prescribes policies and procedures for contract financing and other payment matters [@CL-CTF-3506]. It addresses payment methods, including partial payments and progress payments based on percentage or stage of completion, together with loan guarantees, advance payments and progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3507]. It also addresses administration of debts to the Government arising out of contracts and contract funding, including contract clauses that limit costs or funds [@CL-CTF-3508]. Its remaining topics are assignment of claims to aid in private financing, selected payment clauses, financing of purchases of commercial products and commercial services, performance-based payments and electronic funds transfer payments [@CL-CTF-3606].

Sections 32.000 through 32.009 are applicable to all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-3509]. The subparts on loan guarantees for defense production, contract debts, contract funding, assignment of claims, prompt payment and electronic funds transfer are also listed as applicable to all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3510]. The subpart on commercial product and commercial service purchase financing is applicable only to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under authority of part 12 [@CL-CTF-3511]. The subparts on financing for other than a commercial purchase, advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions, progress payments based on costs and performance-based payments are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-3512; @CL-CTF-3513]. Unless agency regulations otherwise permit, contract financing shall not be provided for purchases made under the authority of part 13 [@CL-CTF-3737].

The enforceability of contract provisions for security of Government financing in a foreign jurisdiction depends on local law and procedure [@CL-CTF-3607]. Before providing contract financing where foreign jurisdictions may become involved, the contracting officer shall ensure the Government's security is enforceable, and this may require additional or different security than that normally provided for in the standard contract clauses [@CL-CTF-3514].

## How it works

Sections 32.000 through 32.009 and the subparts on loan guarantees for defense production, contract debts, contract funding, assignment of claims, prompt payment and electronic funds transfer are applicable to all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3510]. Subpart 32.2 is applicable only to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under authority of part 12 [@CL-CTF-3511], and the four subparts on financing for other than a commercial purchase, advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions, progress payments based on costs and performance-based payments are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-3512; @CL-CTF-3513].

Where foreign jurisdictions may become involved, the contracting officer shall ensure the Government's security is enforceable before providing contract financing [@CL-CTF-3514]. The enforceability of contract provisions for security of Government financing in a foreign jurisdiction is dependent upon local law and procedure [@CL-CTF-3607]. Ensuring enforceable security may require additional or different security than that normally provided for in the standard contract clauses [@CL-CTF-3514].

Class Deviation 2026-O0022 says that, effective February 1, 2026, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.000 says the part addresses policies and procedures for contract financing, funding and other payment matters, where the codified section says the part prescribes policies and procedures for contract financing and other payment matters [@CL-CTF-4274; @CL-CTF-3506]. RFO FAR 32.000 also says it implements chapter 277 of title 10, chapter 45 of title 41, and chapter 55 of title 50 of the United States Code, which authorize contract financing guarantees [@CL-CTF-4275]. Its topic list includes payment methods and loan guarantees, advance payments and cost-based progress payments [@CL-CTF-3507; @CL-CTF-4276], debts and contract funding [@CL-CTF-3508; @CL-CTF-4277], and assignment of claims, selected payment clauses and commercial financing [@CL-CTF-3606; @CL-CTF-4278]. It then lists financing of other than commercial purchases, performance-based payments, electronic funds transfer payments and fast payment procedures as items (h) to (k), so performance-based payments and electronic funds transfer payments move from (h) and (i) to (i) and (j) [@CL-CTF-3606; @CL-CTF-4279]. RFO FAR 32.002(a) says the listed sections are applicable to all purchases, where the codified text says all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-4280; @CL-CTF-3509]. Its list includes Subpart 32.12, Fast Payment Procedure [@CL-CTF-4281], which the codified list does not [@CL-CTF-3510]. RFO FAR 32.002(b) says the commercial financing subpart is applicable only to purchases of commercial products and commercial services under authority of part 12 [@CL-CTF-4282]. RFO FAR 32.002(c) says the subparts on financing for other than a commercial purchase, advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions, progress payments based on costs and performance-based payments are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-4283; @CL-CTF-4284]. RFO FAR 32.003 says that, unless agency regulations otherwise permit, contract financing must not be provided for purchases made under the authority of part 13, where the codified text says "shall not" [@CL-CTF-3028; @CL-CTF-3737]. RFO FAR 32.004 says the enforceability of contract provisions for security of Government financing in a foreign jurisdiction is dependent upon local law and procedure [@CL-CTF-4285], and that prior to providing contract financing where foreign jurisdictions may become involved, the contracting officer must ensure the Government’s security is enforceable, which may require the provision of additional or different security than that normally provided for in the standard contract clauses [@CL-CTF-4286].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buying office awards a commercial product order under part 12 authority and, separately, a development contract under another authority [@CL-CTF-3511; @CL-CTF-3512]. Financing of the commercial order falls under the commercial purchase financing subpart, which applies only to part 12 purchases [@CL-CTF-3511]. Financing of the development contract falls under the non-commercial subparts, such as the subpart on progress payments based on costs [@CL-CTF-3512; @CL-CTF-3513]. The prompt payment subpart applies to both purchases [@CL-CTF-3509; @CL-CTF-3510]. Part of the development contract will be performed in a foreign country, so foreign jurisdictions may become involved. Before providing financing, the contracting officer shall ensure that the Government's security is enforceable, and this may require additional or different security than the standard clauses provide [@CL-CTF-3514].

## Never confuse

Subpart 32.2 is applicable only to purchases under authority of part 12 [@CL-CTF-3511], and the subparts on financing for other than a commercial purchase and on advance payments for other than commercial acquisitions are applicable to all purchases made under any authority other than part 12 [@CL-CTF-3512]. The order in which the contracting officer considers financing methods is covered in [Order of preference among financing methods](ctf-financing-method-order-of-preference.md).

Unless agency regulations otherwise permit, contract financing shall not be provided for purchases made under the authority of part 13 [@CL-CTF-3737]. Providing customary contract financing is covered in [Providing customary contract financing](ctf-providing-customary-contract-financing.md).

Codified FAR 32.002(a) applies its listed sections and subparts to all purchases subject to part 32 [@CL-CTF-3509], while RFO FAR 32.002(a) applies its listed sections to all purchases, and its list includes Subpart 32.12, Fast Payment Procedure [@CL-CTF-4280; @CL-CTF-4281].
