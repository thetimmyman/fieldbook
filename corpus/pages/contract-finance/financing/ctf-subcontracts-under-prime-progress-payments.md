## Purpose

Sets the standards for financing payments to subcontractors that a prime contractor includes in its progress payment requests.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0966] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.504 [@CL-CTF-0966] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.504 [@CL-CTF-4499; @CL-CTF-4500; @CL-CTF-4501; @CL-CTF-4502; @CL-CTF-4503; @CL-CTF-4504; @CL-CTF-4505; @CL-CTF-4506; @CL-CTF-4507; @CL-CTF-4508; @CL-CTF-4509; @CL-CTF-4510] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0966] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts under prime contracts providing progress payments [@CL-CTF-0966] |

Subcontracts may include either performance-based payments, provided they meet the criteria in section 32.1003, or progress payments, provided they meet the criteria in subpart 32.5 for customary progress payments, but not both [@CL-CTF-0966]. A contractor's requests for progress payments may include the full amount of commercial product or commercial service purchase financing payments, performance-based payments or progress payments to a subcontractor, whether paid or unpaid [@CL-CTF-1071], provided that unpaid amounts are limited to amounts determined due and that the contractor will pay in accordance with the terms and conditions of a subcontract or invoice and ordinarily within 30 days of the submission of the contractor's progress payment request to the Government [@CL-CTF-0967].

Where financing payments are progress payments, the subcontract terms on title to property must provide for vesting of title in the Government, not the prime contractor [@CL-CTF-0969]. A reference to the contractor may, however, be substituted for the word Government in paragraph (d)(2)(iv) of the Progress Payments clause when adapting it for the subcontract [@CL-CTF-1077].

## How it works

The contractor has a duty to ensure that financing payments to subcontractors conform to the standards and principles prescribed in paragraph (j) of the Progress Payments clause in the prime contract [@CL-CTF-1075]. When financing payments are in the form of progress payments, the Progress Payments clause at FAR 52.232-16 requires that a subcontract include the substance of the prime contract's Progress Payments clause, modified to indicate that the contractor, not the Government, awards the subcontract and administers the progress payments [@CL-CTF-3233; @CL-CTF-1076].

The contracting officer should, to the extent appropriate, review the subcontract as part of the overall administration of progress payments in the prime contract [@CL-CTF-1072]. There is no special requirement for contracting officer review or consent merely because the subcontract includes financing payments, except as provided in paragraph (c) of the section [@CL-CTF-0968], although the contracting officer must ensure that the contractor has installed the necessary management control systems, including internal audit procedures [@CL-CTF-9493]. Paragraph (c) covers unusual progress payments to a subcontractor: if the contractor is considering making them, the parties will be guided by the policies in section 32.501-2 [@CL-CTF-1073], and if the Government approves them, the contracting officer must issue a contract modification to specify the new rate in paragraph (j)(6) of the Progress Payments clause in the prime contract [@CL-CTF-1074]. For the unusual-rate policy, see [Progress payment rates](ctf-progress-payment-rates.md). Title to the property is covered on [Government title to progress payment inventory](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-government-title-progress-payment-inventory.md), and general financing concepts are on [Progress payments](ctf-progress-payments.md) and [Performance-based payments](ctf-performance-based-payments.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.504 keeps the rule that subcontracts may include either performance-based payments or progress payments, but not both [@CL-CTF-4499]. The contractor's requests may include the full amount of subcontractor financing payments, whether paid or unpaid [@CL-CTF-4500], provided that unpaid amounts are limited to amounts determined due and that the contractor will pay in accordance with the terms and conditions of a subcontract or invoice and ordinarily within 30 days of the submission of the progress payment request to the Government [@CL-CTF-4501]. For unusual progress payments to a subcontractor, the parties are guided by the policies in 32.501-2 [@CL-CTF-4502], and if the Government approves them the contracting officer must issue a contract modification to specify the new rate in paragraph (j)(6) of the Progress Payments clause in the prime contract [@CL-CTF-4503]. The contractor has a duty to ensure that financing payments conform to the standards in paragraph (j) of the Progress Payments clause [@CL-CTF-4504], the contracting officer should, to the extent appropriate, review the subcontract [@CL-CTF-4505], and there is no special requirement for review or consent merely because the subcontract includes financing payments, except as provided in paragraph (c) [@CL-CTF-4506], although the contracting officer must ensure that the contractor has installed the necessary management control systems, including internal audit procedures [@CL-CTF-9500]. When financing payments are in the form of progress payments, the Progress Payments clause requires that the subcontract include the substance of the prime contract's clause [@CL-CTF-4507], modified to indicate that the contractor, not the Government, awards the subcontract and administers the progress payments [@CL-CTF-4508], and the subcontract terms on title to property must provide for vesting of title in the Government, not the contractor [@CL-CTF-4509], though a reference to the contractor may be substituted for the word Government in paragraph (d)(2)(iv) of the clause [@CL-CTF-4510].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor's progress payment request includes 100,000 that it owes a subcontractor and has determined to be due. The prime submits the request to the Government on the first day of a month. The prime is to pay the subcontractor in line with the subcontract or invoice terms, and ordinarily within 30 days of that submission.

## Never confuse

When financing payments are in the form of progress payments, the prime contractor, not the Government, awards the subcontract and administers the progress payments, while the subcontract terms on title to property must provide for vesting of title in the Government rather than the prime [@CL-CTF-3233; @CL-CTF-1076; @CL-CTF-0969]. A subcontract may carry performance-based payments or progress payments, but not both [@CL-CTF-0966].
