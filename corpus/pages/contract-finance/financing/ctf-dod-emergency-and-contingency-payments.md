## Purpose

Shows when the emergencies and contingency operations clause governs invoice payments and how its payment terms differ from the FAR prompt payment terms.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-1753] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7011 [@CL-CTF-1753] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1753] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts in acquisitions that meet the applicability criteria in the DFARS prompt payment applicability rule [@CL-CTF-1789]; the clause applies until the Contracting Officer notifies otherwise [@CL-CTF-1758] |

The DFARS prescription for the clause covers solicitations and contracts, including those using FAR part 12 procedures, in acquisitions that meet the applicability criteria in section 232.901(1) [@CL-CTF-1789]. That rule requires, among its conditions, an emergency, a contingency operation or the release or threatened release of hazardous substances [@CL-CTF-1790]. A further condition is a determination by the head of the contracting activity, after consultation with the cognizant comptroller, that conditions exist that limit normal business operations [@CL-CTF-1791]. The last condition is that payments will be made in the operational area or made contingent upon receiving supporting documentation from the operational area [@CL-CTF-1792].

Under paragraph (1) of the FAR's general definition, a contingency operation is a military operation that is designated by the Secretary of Defense as an operation in which members of the armed forces are or may become involved in military actions, operations, or hostilities against an enemy of the United States or against an opposing military force [@CL-CTF-9393]. Paragraph (2) of the definition covers a military operation that results in the call or order to, or retention on, active duty of members of the uniformed services under sections 688, 12301(a), 12302, 12304, 12304a, 12305, or 12406 of title 10 of the United States Code, Chapter 13 of title 10 of the United States Code, and section 3713 of title 14 of the United States Code, or any other provision of law during a war or during a national emergency declared by the President or Congress [@CL-CTF-9488; @CL-CTF-9489].

Notwithstanding any other payment clause in the contract, the Government will make invoice payments under the terms and conditions of this clause [@CL-CTF-1753]. The clause applies until the Contracting Officer notifies otherwise, and on notification by contract modification the appropriate FAR Prompt Payment clause in the contract becomes applicable [@CL-CTF-1758]. Payment will be made as soon as possible once a proper invoice is received and matched with the contract and the receiving or acceptance report [@CL-CTF-1755]. If the contract does not require an invoice for payment, such as for periodic lease payments, the due date will be as specified in the contract [@CL-CTF-1756].

## How it works

The Government considers payment as made on the day a check is dated or the date of an electronic funds transfer [@CL-CTF-1754]. If the contract provides for contract financing, contract financing payments are made in accordance with the applicable contract financing clause, so the financing clause continues to govern [@CL-CTF-1757]. If the contractor becomes aware of a duplicate contract financing or invoice payment, or that the Government has otherwise overpaid on a contract financing or invoice payment, it shall remit the overpayment amount to the payment office cited in the contract, along with a description of the overpayment that includes the circumstances of the overpayment, the affected contract number and delivery order number if applicable, the affected contract line item or subline item if applicable and the contractor point of contact, and provide a copy of the remittance and supporting documentation to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CTF-1759; @CL-CTF-2258; @CL-CTF-2259]. The clause text sets no fixed number of days for payment, so payment is made as soon as possible once a proper invoice is received and matched, rather than after a fixed period [@CL-CTF-1755].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract with this clause is performed in a contingency operation. The contractor sends a proper invoice for 80,000, and the Government matches it with the contract and the receiving report. Answer: payment is due as soon as possible after the match, not after a fixed number of days. Later the contractor notices that the same invoice was paid twice, so it remits the extra 80,000 to the payment office cited in the contract.

## Never confuse

This clause changes the due date for invoice payments, not the terms of contract financing, which follow their own clause [@CL-CTF-1757]. It is also not the FAR prompt payment due date rule, which is on [Prompt payment: determining invoice payment due dates](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-due-dates.md). The proper invoice content is on [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](../billing-payment/ctf-proper-invoice-content.md). How payment requests and receiving reports are submitted is on [DoD clause on electronic submission of payment requests and receiving reports](ctf-dod-electronic-payment-request-clause.md). The related DoD prompt payment exception is on [DoD payment rules](../billing-payment/ctf-dod-payment-applicability-and-electronic-submission.md).
