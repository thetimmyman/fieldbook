## Purpose

Explains the annual reports that DoD departments and agencies must prepare and submit under the DFARS reporting section, where those reports go, and where their content comes from [@CL-CTF-3786; @CL-CTF-3787].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3786] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.006-5 [@CL-CTF-3786] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3787] |
| Applies to | DoD departments and agencies, which prepare the reports under their own procedures [@CL-CTF-3786] |

Departments and agencies shall prepare and submit annual reports, in accordance with department/agency procedures, to the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment) through the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy [@CL-CTF-3786].

Each report carries Report Control Symbol DD-AT&L(A) 1891 and contains the information required by FAR 32.006-5 [@CL-CTF-3787]. That FAR section requires each report to contain each recommendation made by the remedy coordination official, the actions taken on the recommendation or recommendations with reasons for those actions, and an assessment of the effects of each action on the Government [@CL-CTF-3401].

## How it works

Departments and agencies, in accordance with department/agency procedures, shall prepare and submit annual reports [@CL-CTF-3786].

The reports go to the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment) through the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy [@CL-CTF-3786].

The reports contain the information required by FAR 32.006-5 [@CL-CTF-3787].

See [Reduction or suspension of payments on a finding of fraud](ctf-reduction-or-suspension-of-payments-for-fraud.md) for the remedy and [Contract financing authority and prompt payment responsibilities](ctf-financing-authority-and-prompt-payment-duties.md) for the FAR reporting rule.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A department's remedy coordination official made two recommendations during the year. The department prepares its annual report under its own procedures and submits it to the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment) through the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy [@CL-CTF-3786]. The report carries the control symbol and states each recommendation, the action taken with reasons, and an assessment of the effects of each action on the Government [@CL-CTF-3787; @CL-CTF-3401]. A contractor affected by those recommendations files nothing under this section [@CL-CTF-3786].

## Never confuse

Departments and agencies shall prepare and submit annual reports [@CL-CTF-3786]. The decision to reduce or suspend payments is covered on [Financing consideration and fraud payment procedures](ctf-financing-consideration-and-fraud-payment-procedures.md).
