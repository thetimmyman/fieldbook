## Purpose

Covers three DFARS sections on advance payments: the exclusion in DFARS 232.404(a)(9) from FAR subpart 32.4, the recommendation for approval procedures in DFARS 232.409-1, and the findings, determination, and authorization procedures in DFARS 232.410 [@CL-CTF-3627; @CL-CTF-3628; @CL-CTF-3629].

The requirements of FAR subpart 32.4 do not apply to advertisements in high school and college publications for military recruitment efforts under section 503 of title 10 of the United States Code when the contract cost does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-CTF-3627]. Under FAR 2.101, the micro-purchase threshold means $15,000, except as specified in the definition's listed exceptions [@CL-FND-0002].

Under DFARS 232.409-1, for preparation of the documents required by FAR 32.409-1(e) and (f), contracting officers follow the procedures at PGI 232.409-1 [@CL-CTF-3628]. Under FAR 32.409-1, if recommending approval, the contracting officer shall transmit the listed items, under agency procedures, to the approving authority, including at paragraphs (e) and (f) the proposed advance payment contract terms (including proposed security requirements) and the findings, determination, and authorization (see FAR 32.410) [@CL-CTF-3634; @CL-CTF-3633].

Under DFARS 232.410, if an advance payment procedure is used without a special bank account, contracting officers follow the procedures at PGI 232.410 [@CL-CTF-3629].

## How it works

DFARS 232.404(a)(9) excludes advertisements in high school and college publications for military recruitment efforts under section 503 of title 10 of the United States Code from the requirements of FAR subpart 32.4 when the contract cost does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-CTF-3627].

When an advance payment request is processed under FAR 32.409-1 and the contracting officer recommends approval, the contracting officer transmits the required items under agency procedures to the approving authority [@CL-CTF-3634]. Two of those items—the proposed advance payment contract terms, including proposed security requirements, under FAR 32.409-1(e), and the findings, determination, and authorization under FAR 32.409-1(f)—are prepared by following the procedures at PGI 232.409-1 [@CL-CTF-3628; @CL-CTF-3633]. When an advance payment procedure is used without a special bank account, the procedures at PGI 232.410 apply [@CL-CTF-3629].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD office buys recruitment advertisements in college newspapers to support military recruitment efforts under section 503 of title 10 of the United States Code, for a contract cost of 6,000. The general micro-purchase threshold in FAR 2.101 is $15,000, and assume no exception in the definition applies [@CL-FND-0002]. Because the contract cost does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold, the requirements of FAR subpart 32.4 do not apply to this purchase [@CL-CTF-3627].

A separate supply contract has an advance payment request. When the contracting officer recommends approval, the contracting officer transmits the listed items, under agency procedures, to the approving authority [@CL-CTF-3634]. Two of those items are the proposed advance payment contract terms (including proposed security requirements) and the findings, determination, and authorization [@CL-CTF-3633], and they are prepared following the procedures at PGI 232.409-1 [@CL-CTF-3628]. If the advance payment procedure is used without a special bank account, the procedures at PGI 232.410 are followed [@CL-CTF-3629].

## Never confuse

The exclusion in DFARS 232.404(a)(9) applies to advertisements in high school and college publications for military recruitment efforts under section 503 of title 10 of the United States Code when the contract cost does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-CTF-3627].

DFARS 232.409-1 directs contracting officers to PGI 232.409-1 for preparation of the documents required by FAR 32.409-1(e) and (f), while the contracting officer transmits those items under agency procedures to the approving authority when recommending approval [@CL-CTF-3628; @CL-CTF-3633; @CL-CTF-3634].

DFARS 232.410 directs contracting officers to follow the procedures at PGI 232.410 if an advance payment procedure is used without a special bank account [@CL-CTF-3629].
