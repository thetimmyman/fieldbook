## Purpose

Covers three DFARS sections on advance payments: one exclusion from the FAR advance payment subpart, the section headed Recommendation for approval, and the section headed Findings, determination, and authorization. Two of them send the reader to the PGI [@CL-CTF-3628; @CL-CTF-3629].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-3627] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.404(a)(9), DFARS 232.409-1 and DFARS 232.410 [@CL-CTF-3627; @CL-CTF-3628; @CL-CTF-3629] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3627] |
| Applies to | Recruitment advertisements whose contract cost does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold; documents for a recommendation for approval; and advance payment procedures used without a special bank account [@CL-CTF-3627; @CL-CTF-3628; @CL-CTF-3629] |

The requirements of FAR subpart 32.4 do not apply to advertisements in high school and college publications for military recruitment efforts under section 503 of title 10, United States Code, when the contract cost does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-CTF-3627]. The threshold is measured on the contract cost [@CL-CTF-3627]. The micro-purchase threshold is set in FAR 2.101 at 15,000, with listed exceptions [@CL-FND-0002].

The section headed Recommendation for approval says to follow the procedures at PGI 232.409-1 for preparation of the documents required by FAR 32.409-1(e) and (f) [@CL-CTF-3628]. Those two FAR paragraphs are the proposed advance payment contract terms, including proposed security requirements, and the findings, determination, and authorization [@CL-CTF-3633]. If recommending approval, the contracting officer must transmit the items the FAR section lists, under agency procedures, to the approving authority [@CL-CTF-3634].

The section headed Findings, determination, and authorization says that, if an advance payment procedure is used without a special bank account, the procedures at PGI 232.410 must be followed [@CL-CTF-3629]. The section does not say what applies when a special bank account is used.

## How it works

The exclusion removes the FAR subpart's requirements for one kind of purchase: military recruitment advertisements in high school and college publications, within the cost limit [@CL-CTF-3627]. A purchase outside that description stays under the subpart unless another exclusion applies.

For an advance payment that goes through the subpart, if the contracting officer recommends approval, the contracting officer transmits the FAR items to the approving authority under agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3634]. The DFARS adds a PGI procedure for preparing two of those items [@CL-CTF-3628; @CL-CTF-3633]. The DFARS text does not say who approves the request. The PGI text is not printed in this corpus, so this card does not state its steps.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD office buys recruitment advertisements in college newspapers to support military recruitment efforts under section 503 of title 10, for a contract cost of 6,000. The micro-purchase threshold is 15,000, and assume no listed exception applies [@CL-FND-0002]. The contract cost does not exceed the threshold, so the requirements of FAR subpart 32.4 do not apply to this purchase [@CL-CTF-3627].

A separate supply contract has an advance payment request. If the contracting officer recommends approval, the contracting officer transmits the listed items, under agency procedures, to the approving authority [@CL-CTF-3634]. Two of them are the proposed contract terms and the findings, determination and authorization [@CL-CTF-3633], and they are prepared following PGI 232.409-1 [@CL-CTF-3628]. If that advance payment procedure is used without a special bank account, PGI 232.410 also applies [@CL-CTF-3629].

## Never confuse

The advertising exclusion covers only the advertisements described, at or under the cost limit. It is not an exclusion for all recruitment spending or for every purchase under the threshold [@CL-CTF-3627].

The PGI procedure for preparing the documents is not the approval decision. It covers the documents that FAR 32.409-1(e) and (f) require [@CL-CTF-3628].

The PGI 232.410 reference applies if a procedure is used without a special bank account [@CL-CTF-3629]. It does not say that every advance payment is made that way [@CL-CTF-3629].

The FAR exclusions and interest on unliquidated balances are in [Advance payments: exclusions and interest on unliquidated balances](../financing/ctf-advance-payment-exclusions-and-interest.md). The letter of credit and findings rules are in [Advance payments: letters of credit and findings, determination and authorization](../financing/ctf-advance-payment-letters-of-credit-and-findings.md). The FAR recommendation items are in [Advance payments: application and contracting officer recommendation](../financing/ctf-advance-payment-application-and-recommendation.md). This card does not restate them.
