## Purpose

Shows what an assignee files to give notice of an assignment of claims or a release, and who decides whether a contractor may pay a contract debt in installments or defer collection.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1433; @CL-CTF-1435] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.805 and FAR 32.607 [@CL-CTF-1433; @CL-CTF-1435] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.805 [@CL-CTF-4107; @CL-CTF-4108; @CL-CTF-1289; @CL-CTF-4109; @CL-CTF-1290; @CL-CTF-4110; @CL-CTF-1291; @CL-CTF-1292] and RFO FAR 32.607 [@CL-CTF-4111; @CL-CTF-4112; @CL-CTF-4113; @CL-CTF-1294] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1433] |
| Applies to | Assignments of claims noticed under the filing procedure, and contractor proposals for debt deferment or installment payments where the contractor has not appealed the debt or filed an action under the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1433; @CL-CTF-1436] |

The assignee shall forward to each party specified in FAR 32.802(e) an original and three copies of the notice of assignment, together with one true copy of the instrument of assignment, and the true copy shall be a certified duplicate or photostat copy of the original assignment [@CL-CTF-1433]. The assignee, under a further assignment or reassignment, in order to establish a right to receive payment from the Government, must file with the addressees listed in FAR 32.802(e): (i) a written notice of release of the contractor by the assigning financing institution, (ii) a copy of the release instrument, (iii) a written notice of the further assignment or reassignment, and (iv) a copy of the further assignment or reassignment instrument [@CL-CTF-1434; @CL-CTF-1288]. If the assignee releases the contractor from an assignment of claims under a contract, the contractor, in order to establish a right to receive payment of the balance due under the contract, must file a written notice of release together with a true copy of the release of assignment instrument with the addressees noted in FAR 32.802(e) [@CL-CTF-1464; @CL-CTF-9567].

The contracting officer shall not approve or deny a contractor's request for installment payments or deferment of collections, and the office designated in agency procedures is responsible for approving or denying requests for installment payments or deferment of collections [@CL-CTF-1435]. If a contractor has not appealed the debt or filed an action under the Disputes clause of the contract and the contractor has submitted a proposal for debt deferment or installment payments, the office designated in agency procedures may arrange for deferment or installment payments if the contractor is unable to pay at once in full or the contractor's operations under national defense contracts would be seriously impaired, and the arrangement shall include appropriate covenants and securities and should be limited to the shortest practicable maturity, in addition to the schedule and payment terms in FAR 32.607(b)(2) [@CL-CTF-1436; @CL-CTF-1437; @CL-CTF-1293].

## How it works

The assignee shall forward to each party specified in FAR 32.802(e) an original and three copies of the notice of assignment together with one true copy of the instrument of assignment (a certified duplicate or photostat copy of the original assignment) [@CL-CTF-1433]. See [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](ctf-assignment-of-claims.md). Under a further assignment or reassignment, the assignee must file with the addressees listed in FAR 32.802(e) the written notice of release of the contractor by the assigning financing institution, a copy of the release instrument, a written notice of the further assignment or reassignment, and a copy of the further assignment or reassignment instrument [@CL-CTF-1434; @CL-CTF-1288]. If the assignee releases the contractor from an assignment of claims under a contract, the contractor must file a written notice of release together with a true copy of the release of assignment instrument with the addressees noted in FAR 32.802(e) [@CL-CTF-1464; @CL-CTF-9567]. For contract debts, the office designated in agency procedures is responsible for approving or denying requests for installment payments or deferment of collections, and the contracting officer shall not approve or deny them [@CL-CTF-1435]. See [Contract debts demand deferment and interest](ctf-contract-debts.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, under DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0022, contracting officers shall use the revised RFO FAR part 32, Contract Financing, in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 32.805(b), the assignee must forward to each party specified in RFO FAR 32.802(e) an original and three copies of the notice of assignment, together with one true copy of the instrument of assignment, and the true copy must be a certified duplicate or photostat copy of the original assignment [@CL-CTF-4107]. Under RFO FAR 32.805(e)(2), the assignee under a further assignment or reassignment, in order to establish a right to receive payment from the Government, must file with the addressees listed in RFO FAR 32.802(e) a written notice of release of the contractor by the assigning financing institution, a copy of the release instrument, a written notice of the further assignment or reassignment, and a copy of the further assignment or reassignment instrument [@CL-CTF-4108; @CL-CTF-1289]. Under RFO FAR 32.805(e)(3), if the assignee releases the contractor from an assignment of claims under a contract, the contractor, in order to establish a right to receive payment of the balance due under the contract, must file a written notice of release together with a true copy of the release of assignment instrument with the addressees noted in RFO FAR 32.802(e) [@CL-CTF-4109; @CL-CTF-1290]. Under RFO FAR 32.805(d), in examining and processing notices of assignment and before acknowledging their receipt, contracting officers should ensure that any additional conditions specified in agency regulations and the following conditions have been met: (1) the contract has been properly approved and executed, (2) the contract is one under which claims may be assigned, (3) the assignment covers only money due or to become due under the contract, and (4) the assignee is registered separately in the System for Award Management unless one of the exceptions in FAR 4.1102 applies [@CL-CTF-4110; @CL-CTF-1291; @CL-CTF-1292]. Under RFO FAR 32.607(a), the contracting officer must not approve or deny a contractor's request for installment payments or deferment of collections, and the office designated in agency procedures is responsible for approving or denying such requests [@CL-CTF-4111]. Under RFO FAR 32.607(b)(1), if a contractor has not appealed the debt or filed an action under the Disputes clause of the contract and has submitted a proposal for debt deferment or installment payments, the office designated in agency procedures may arrange for deferment or installment payments if the contractor is unable to pay at once in full or the contractor's operations under national defense contracts would be seriously impaired, and the arrangement must include appropriate covenants and securities and should be limited to the shortest practicable maturity, in addition to the schedule and payment terms in RFO FAR 32.607(b)(2) [@CL-CTF-4112; @CL-CTF-4113; @CL-CTF-1294].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bank takes an assignment of a supply contract. It sends each required party an original and three copies of its notice of assignment with one certified true copy of the assignment instrument. Later the contractor owes a debt of 40,000, has not appealed it or filed under the Disputes clause, and proposes installment payments because it cannot pay at once in full.

Answer: the bank files the original and three copies with each specified party. The office designated in agency procedures, not the contracting officer, may arrange the installments.

## Never confuse

The assignee shall forward to each party specified in FAR 32.802(e) an original and three copies of the notice of assignment together with one true copy of the instrument of assignment [@CL-CTF-1433], while under FAR 32.607(b)(1), if a contractor has not appealed the debt or filed an action under the Disputes clause of the contract and has submitted a proposal for debt deferment or installment payments, the office designated in agency procedures may arrange for deferment or installment payments if the contractor is unable to pay at once in full or the contractor's operations under national defense contracts would be seriously impaired [@CL-CTF-1436; @CL-CTF-1437]. The contracting officer shall not approve or deny a contractor's request for installment payments or deferment of collections [@CL-CTF-1435]. For the assignment conditions and the debt demand, see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](ctf-assignment-of-claims.md) and [Contract debts demand deferment and interest](ctf-contract-debts.md).
