## Purpose

Shows what an assignee files to give notice of an assignment of claims or a release, and who decides whether a contractor may pay a contract debt in installments or defer collection.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-1433; @CL-CTF-1435] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.805 and FAR 32.607 [@CL-CTF-1433; @CL-CTF-1435] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-1433] |
| Applies to | Assignments of claims that are noticed under the filing procedure, and contractor proposals for debt deferment or installment payments where the contractor has not appealed the debt or filed under the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-1433; @CL-CTF-1436] |

The assignee shall forward to each party specified in FAR 32.802(e) an original and three copies of the notice of assignment together with one true copy of the instrument of assignment, and the true copy shall be a certified duplicate or photostat copy of the original assignment [@CL-CTF-1433]. An assignee under a further assignment or reassignment, in order to establish a right to receive payment from the Government, must file with the addressees listed in FAR 32.802(e) a written notice of release of the contractor by the assigning financing institution, among other listed documents [@CL-CTF-1434]. If the assignee releases the contractor from an assignment of claims, the contractor, in order to establish a right to receive payment of the balance due, must file a written notice of release, among other items the paragraph requires [@CL-CTF-1464].

The contracting officer shall not approve or deny a contractor's request for installment payments or deferment of collections, and the office designated in agency procedures is responsible for approving or denying such requests [@CL-CTF-1435]. The listed provisions on deferment and installment arrangements apply if the contractor has not appealed the debt or filed an action under the Disputes clause of the contract and has submitted a proposal for debt deferment or installment payments [@CL-CTF-1436]. One listed provision is that the office designated in agency procedures may arrange for deferment or installment payments if the contractor is unable to pay at once in full or the contractor's operations under national defense contracts would be seriously impaired [@CL-CTF-1437].

## How it works

The assignee forwards the notice of assignment and the instrument copy to each party specified in FAR 32.802(e) [@CL-CTF-1433]. The linked assignment card states who receives the written notice of assignment; see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](ctf-assignment-of-claims.md). On a further assignment or reassignment, the assignee files with the same addressees [@CL-CTF-1434]. After the assignee releases the contractor, the contractor files the notice of release [@CL-CTF-1464]. The same section also sets how assignments by corporations, partnerships and individuals are executed, and what the contracting officer should check before acknowledging a notice. This card does not restate those formalities. For debts, the designated office decides on installment or deferment proposals, not the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-1435]. The demand, interest and deferment request steps are in [Contract debts demand deferment and interest](ctf-contract-debts.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bank takes an assignment of a supply contract. It sends each required party an original and three copies of its notice of assignment with one certified true copy of the assignment instrument. Later the contractor owes a debt of 40,000, has not appealed it or filed under the Disputes clause, and proposes installment payments because it cannot pay at once in full.

Answer: the bank files the original and three copies with each specified party. The office designated in agency procedures, not the contracting officer, may arrange the installments.

## Never confuse

A notice of assignment is filed by the assignee [@CL-CTF-1433], while an installment or deferment arrangement concerns a proposal for debt deferment or installment payments by the contractor [@CL-CTF-1436; @CL-CTF-1437]. The contracting officer does not approve or deny installment requests [@CL-CTF-1435]. For the assignment conditions and the debt demand, see [Assignment of claims conditions and policies](ctf-assignment-of-claims.md) and [Contract debts demand deferment and interest](ctf-contract-debts.md).
