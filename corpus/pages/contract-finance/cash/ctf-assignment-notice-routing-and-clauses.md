## Purpose

Sets out where the assignee of a claim sends the notice of assignment and who files, forwards and returns each copy, and when the contracting officer inserts the assignment and prohibition clauses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS and codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3377] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.805 and FAR 32.806 [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3377] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.806 [@CL-CTF-4616; @CL-CTF-4619] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3371] |
| Applies to | Assignments of claims noticed under the DoD procedure, and solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold unless the contract will prohibit assignment [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3377] |

To the administrative contracting officer (ACO), the assignee shall forward a true copy of the instrument of assignment and an original and three copies of the notice of assignment [@CL-CTF-3371]. The ACO shall acknowledge receipt by signing and dating all copies of the notice of assignment, file the true copy of the instrument of assignment and the original of the notice in the contract file, forward two copies of the notice to the disbursing officer of the payment office cited in the contract, return a copy of the notice to the assignee, and advise the contracting officer of the assignment [@CL-CTF-3414; @CL-CTF-3372].

To the surety or sureties, if any, the assignee shall forward a true copy of the instrument of assignment and an original and three copies of the notice of assignment [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3373]. The surety shall return three acknowledged copies of the notice to the assignee, who shall forward two copies to the disbursing officer designated in the contract [@CL-CTF-3374]. To the disbursing officer of the payment office cited in the contract, the assignee shall forward a true copy of the instrument of assignment and an original and one copy of the notice of assignment [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3375]. The disbursing officer shall acknowledge and return to the assignee the copy of the notice and shall file the true copy of the instrument and original notice [@CL-CTF-3376].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-23, Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, unless the contract will prohibit the assignment of claims (see FAR 32.803(b)) [@CL-CTF-3377]. FAR 2.101 defines the micro-purchase threshold as $15,000, with listed exceptions [@CL-FND-0002]. A contract may prohibit the assignment of claims if the agency determines the prohibition to be in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0775]. The use of the clause is not required for purchase orders, but the clause may be used in purchase orders expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, that are accepted in writing by the contractor, if such use is consistent with agency policies and regulations [@CL-CTF-3378].

If a no-setoff commitment has been authorized (see FAR 32.803(d)), the contracting officer shall use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-3379]. Any contract of a designated agency (see FAR 32.801), except a contract under which full payment has been made, may include a no-setoff commitment only when a determination of need is made by the head of the agency, in accordance with the Presidential delegation of authority dated October 3, 1995, and after such determination has been published in the Federal Register [@CL-CTF-3415; @CL-CTF-3416]. When an assigned contract does not include a no-setoff commitment, the Government may apply against payments to the assignee any liability of the contractor to the Government arising independently of the assigned contract if the liability existed at the time notice of the assignment was received, even though that liability had not yet matured so as to be due and payable [@CL-CTF-3495; @CL-CTF-1365]. Under a requirements or indefinite quantity type contract that authorizes ordering and payment by multiple Government activities, amounts due for individual orders for $1,000 or more may be assigned [@CL-CTF-3494].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-24, Prohibition of Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts for which a determination has been made under agency regulations that the prohibition of assignment of claims is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-3380].

Effective February 1, 2026, under DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0022, contracting officers shall use the revised RFO FAR part 32, Contract Financing, in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 32.806, the contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.232-23, Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, unless the contract will prohibit the assignment of claims (see RFO FAR 32.803(b)) [@CL-CTF-4616]. The use of the clause is not required for purchase orders, but the clause may be used in purchase orders expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, that are accepted in writing by the contractor, if such use is consistent with agency policies and regulations [@CL-CTF-4617]. If a no-setoff commitment has been authorized (see RFO FAR 32.803(d)), the contracting officer must use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-4618]. The contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.232-24, Prohibition of Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts for which a determination has been made under agency regulations that the prohibition of assignment of claims is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-4619].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bank takes an assignment of a contractor's claims. The bank sends the administrative contracting officer one true copy of the instrument and an original and three copies of the notice, so four notices in all [@CL-CTF-3371]. The officer signs and dates all four and files the instrument and the original notice in the contract file [@CL-CTF-3414]. The officer forwards two copies to the disbursing officer and returns one to the bank, which accounts for the other three, and tells the contracting officer of the assignment [@CL-CTF-3372]. The bank also sends a surety the instrument and an original and three copies of the notice, and the surety returns three acknowledged copies to the bank, which forwards two to the disbursing officer [@CL-CTF-3373; @CL-CTF-3374]. The bank also sends the disbursing officer of the payment office cited in the contract the instrument and an original and one copy of the notice, and that officer acknowledges and returns the copy and files the instrument and the original notice [@CL-CTF-3375; @CL-CTF-3376].

A solicitation for a supply contract is expected to be worth 20,000, which is above a micro-purchase threshold of $15,000, and the contract will not prohibit assignment. The contracting officer inserts the Assignment of Claims clause [@CL-CTF-3377; @CL-FND-0002]. A purchase order of the same value need not carry it, but may if the contractor accepts in writing and agency policy allows [@CL-CTF-3378].

## Never confuse

The two clauses take opposite sides on assignability rather than overlapping: the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-23, Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, unless the contract will prohibit the assignment of claims (see FAR 32.803(b)) [@CL-CTF-3377], whereas the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-24, Prohibition of Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts for which a determination has been made under agency regulations that the prohibition of assignment of claims is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-3380]. See [Assignment of claims](ctf-assignment-of-claims.md), [Assignment procedure and debt installments](ctf-assignment-procedure-and-debt-installments.md), [Interest and assignment of claims clauses](../billing-payment/ctf-interest-and-assignment-of-claims-clauses.md) and [Withholding limit, extras and assignment prohibition](../billing-payment/ctf-withholding-limit-extras-and-assignment-prohibition.md).
