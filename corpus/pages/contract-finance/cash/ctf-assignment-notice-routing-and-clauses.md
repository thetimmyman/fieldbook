## Purpose

Sets out where the assignee of a claim sends the notice of assignment and who files, forwards and returns each copy, and when the contracting officer inserts the assignment and prohibition clauses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS and codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3377] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.805 and FAR 32.806 [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3377] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3371] |
| Applies to | Assignments of claims noticed under the DoD procedure, and solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold unless the contract will prohibit assignment [@CL-CTF-3371; @CL-CTF-3377] |

To the administrative contracting officer, the assignee must forward a true copy of the instrument of assignment and an original and three copies of the notice of assignment [@CL-CTF-3371]. The administrative contracting officer must acknowledge receipt by signing and dating all copies of the notice, and must file the true copy of the instrument and the original of the notice in the contract file [@CL-CTF-3414]. The officer must also forward two copies of the notice to the disbursing officer of the payment office cited in the contract, return a copy of the notice to the assignee, and advise the contracting officer of the assignment [@CL-CTF-3372].

To the surety or sureties, if any, the assignee must also forward a true copy of the instrument and an original and three copies of the notice [@CL-CTF-3373]. The surety must return three acknowledged copies of the notice to the assignee, who must forward two copies to the disbursing officer designated in the contract [@CL-CTF-3374]. To the disbursing officer of the payment office cited in the contract, the assignee must also forward a true copy of the instrument and an original and one copy of the notice [@CL-CTF-3375]. The disbursing officer must acknowledge and return the copy of the notice to the assignee, and must file the true copy of the instrument and the original notice [@CL-CTF-3376].

## How it works

The contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.232-23, Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, unless the contract will prohibit the assignment of claims [@CL-CTF-3377]. The micro-purchase threshold is $15,000, with listed exceptions [@CL-FND-0002]. A contract may prohibit assignment of claims if the agency determines the prohibition to be in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0775]. The clause is not required for purchase orders, but it may be used in purchase orders expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold that the contractor accepts in writing, if that use is consistent with agency policies and regulations [@CL-CTF-3378].

If a no-setoff commitment has been authorized, the contracting officer must use the clause at FAR 52.232-23 with its Alternate I [@CL-CTF-3379]. A contract of a designated agency, except one under which full payment has been made, may include a no-setoff commitment only when the head of the agency makes a determination of need [@CL-CTF-3415]. The head of the agency must make that determination in accordance with the Presidential delegation of authority dated October 3, 1995, and after the determination has been published in the Federal Register [@CL-CTF-3416]. When an assigned contract does not include a no-setoff commitment, the Government may apply against payments to the assignee any liability of the contractor to the Government arising independently of the assigned contract if the liability existed at the time notice of the assignment was received [@CL-CTF-3495]. Under a requirements or indefinite quantity type contract that authorizes ordering and payment by multiple Government activities, amounts due for individual orders for $1,000 or more may be assigned [@CL-CTF-3494].

The contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.232-24, Prohibition of Assignment of Claims, in solicitations and contracts for which a determination has been made under agency regulations that the prohibition of assignment of claims is in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-3380].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bank takes an assignment of a contractor's claims. The bank sends the administrative contracting officer one true copy of the instrument and an original and three copies of the notice, so four notices in all [@CL-CTF-3371]. The officer signs and dates all four and files the instrument and the original notice in the contract file [@CL-CTF-3414]. The officer forwards two copies to the disbursing officer and returns one to the bank, which accounts for the other three, and tells the contracting officer of the assignment [@CL-CTF-3372]. The bank also sends a surety the instrument and an original and three copies of the notice, and the surety returns three acknowledged copies to the bank, which forwards two to the disbursing officer [@CL-CTF-3373; @CL-CTF-3374]. The bank also sends the disbursing officer of the payment office cited in the contract the instrument and an original and one copy of the notice, and that officer acknowledges and returns the copy and files the instrument and the original notice [@CL-CTF-3375; @CL-CTF-3376].

A solicitation for a supply contract is expected to be worth 20,000, which is above a micro-purchase threshold of $15,000, and the contract will not prohibit assignment. The contracting officer inserts the Assignment of Claims clause [@CL-CTF-3377; @CL-FND-0002]. A purchase order of the same value need not carry it, but may if the contractor accepts in writing and agency policy allows [@CL-CTF-3378].

## Never confuse

The Assignment of Claims clause and the Prohibition of Assignment of Claims clause are opposite choices. The first goes into solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, unless the contract will prohibit assignment [@CL-CTF-3377]. The second goes in where a determination under agency regulations finds the prohibition to be in the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-3380]. The notice routing here only settles where the copies go. The conditions for a valid assignment and the clauses' own terms are on [Assignment of claims](ctf-assignment-of-claims.md), [Assignment procedure and debt installments](ctf-assignment-procedure-and-debt-installments.md), [Interest and assignment of claims clauses](../billing-payment/ctf-interest-and-assignment-of-claims-clauses.md) and [Withholding limit, extras and assignment prohibition](../billing-payment/ctf-withholding-limit-extras-and-assignment-prohibition.md).
