## Purpose

Covers contracting officer demands for payment of contract debts, deferment procedures, interest accrual, and interest credits.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0766] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.604 [@CL-CTF-0766] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0766] |
| Applies to | Contractors determined to owe an actual debt to the Government [@CL-CTF-0766] |

Except as provided in paragraph (c) of that section, the contracting officer shall issue a demand for payment as soon as the contracting officer has determined that an actual debt is due the Government and the amount [@CL-CTF-0766]. Except as provided in paragraph (d), the contracting officer should not issue a demand if the contracting officer only becomes aware of the debt when the contractor provides a lump sum payment or submits a credit invoice [@CL-CTF-0856]. If a demand was not issued on that basis, the contracting officer shall issue one no sooner than 30 days after becoming aware of the debt unless a listed exception applies [@CL-CTF-0857], namely that the contractor has liquidated the debt, has requested an installment payment agreement, or the payment office has issued a demand [@CL-CTF-0873].

For contract debts other than those resulting from specific contract terms, a notification states that any amounts not paid within 30 days from the date of the demand will bear interest computed from the demand date until repayment [@CL-CTF-0767]. The demand must also notify the contractor that it may request installment payments or deferment of collection if immediate payment is not practicable or if the amount is disputed [@CL-CTF-0859].

Deferment requests must be submitted in writing to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0768]. If no appeal is pending, the request should include information about financial condition, contract backlog, and projected cash receipts and requirements [@CL-CTF-0860]. Actions filed by contractors under the Disputes Clause shall not suspend or delay collection [@CL-CTF-0769].

## How it works

The office designated in agency procedures may authorize a deferment pending the resolution of appeal to avoid possible overcollections [@CL-CTF-0861]. An equitable interest credit shall be applied when the amount of debt initially determined is subsequently reduced, for example through a successful appeal [@CL-CTF-0770]. The credit is computed by charging interest at the rate under the Interest clause on the reduced debt from the date of collection until the date the monies are remitted to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0858].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer determines an overpayment of 50,000 and issues a demand for payment. The contractor does not pay within 30 days, so interest computed from the demand date applies. The contractor sends a written deferment request to the contracting officer and also appeals under the Disputes clause; the appeal does not delay collection, and the Government collects the full 50,000 by offset. The appeal succeeds and reduces the debt to 20,000, so 30,000 is remitted and an equitable interest credit is computed under the interest-credit procedures.

## Never confuse

A demand for payment of a contract debt is the Government collecting from the contractor; it is a different matter from a contractor's own bad debts, see [Bad debts](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-bad-debts.md). It is also not a revision of billing rates; see [Billing rates](../billing-payment/ctf-billing-rates.md).
