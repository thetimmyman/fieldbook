## Purpose

Covers contracting officer demands for payment of contract debts, deferment procedures, interest accrual, and interest credits.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0766] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.008 and FAR 32.604 [@CL-CTF-3740; @CL-CTF-0766] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.008 [@CL-CTF-3030] and RFO FAR 32.604 [@CL-CTF-4135; @CL-CTF-4136; @CL-CTF-4137; @CL-CTF-4138; @CL-CTF-4139; @CL-CTF-4140] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0766] |
| Applies to | Contractors determined to owe an actual debt to the Government [@CL-CTF-0766] |

Except as provided in paragraph (c) of that section, the contracting officer shall issue a demand for payment as soon as the contracting officer has determined that an actual debt is due the Government and the amount [@CL-CTF-0766]. Except as provided in paragraph (d), the contracting officer should not issue a demand if the contracting officer only becomes aware of the debt when the contractor provides a lump sum payment or submits a credit invoice [@CL-CTF-0856]. If a demand was not issued on that basis, the contracting officer shall issue one no sooner than 30 days after becoming aware of the debt unless a listed exception applies [@CL-CTF-0857], namely that the contractor has liquidated the debt, has requested an installment payment agreement, or the payment office has issued a demand [@CL-CTF-0873].

The demand for payment shall include the items listed in paragraph (b) of that section [@CL-CTF-9565]. For contract debts other than those resulting from specific contract terms, one of those items is a notification stating that any amounts not paid within 30 days from the date of the demand will bear interest computed from the demand date until repayment [@CL-CTF-0767]. The demand must also notify the contractor that it may request installment payments or deferment of collection if immediate payment is not practicable or if the amount is disputed [@CL-CTF-0859].

Deferment requests must be submitted in writing to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0768]. If there is no appeal pending or action filed under the Disputes clause, the information about the contractor that should be submitted with the request is its financial condition, contract backlog, projected cash receipts and requirements, the feasibility of immediate payment of the debt, and the probable effect on operations of immediate payment in full [@CL-CTF-0860; @CL-CTF-9566]. Actions filed by contractors under the Disputes Clause shall not suspend or delay collection [@CL-CTF-0769].

If the contractor notifies the contracting officer of a duplicate payment or that the Government has otherwise overpaid, the contracting officer shall follow the procedures at FAR 32.604 [@CL-CTF-3740].

## How it works

The office designated in agency procedures may authorize a deferment pending the resolution of appeal to avoid possible overcollections [@CL-CTF-0861]. An equitable interest credit shall be applied under the circumstances the section lists, the first being when the amount of debt initially determined is subsequently reduced, for example through a successful appeal [@CL-CTF-0770]. Under the first listed procedure for computing any appropriate credit, interest at the rate under the Interest clause is charged on the reduced debt from the date of collection until the date the monies are remitted to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0858].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.008 keeps the overpayment notice rule: if the contractor notifies the contracting officer of a duplicate payment or that the Government has otherwise overpaid, the contracting officer must follow the procedures at 32.604, with "must" where the codified text says "shall" [@CL-CTF-3740; @CL-CTF-3030]. RFO FAR 32.604 keeps the demand rules described above: issue the demand as soon as the debt is determined [@CL-CTF-4135], do not issue it when the contracting officer only becomes aware of the debt through a lump sum payment or credit invoice, except under paragraph (d) [@CL-CTF-4137], and, if a demand for payment was not issued as provided for in paragraph (c), issue it no sooner than 30 days after becoming aware of the debt unless a listed exception applies [@CL-CTF-4138; @CL-CTF-4139]. RFO FAR 32.604(b) keeps the notice that amounts not paid within 30 days of the demand bear interest and the notice of the right to request installment payments or deferment [@CL-CTF-4136; @CL-CTF-4140]. RFO FAR 32.608-2 keeps the equitable interest credit circumstance and the first computation procedure described above [@CL-CTF-4142; @CL-CTF-4143]. RFO FAR 32.607-2 keeps the rule that deferment requests must be submitted in writing to the contracting officer, and says deferments pending disposition of appeal may be granted to small business concerns and financially weak contractors, balancing the need for Government security against loss and undue hardship on the contractor [@CL-CTF-4141; @CL-CTF-4737]. The RFO text of that section prints no counterpart to the codified list of information to submit with a request when no appeal is pending [@CL-CTF-0860; @CL-CTF-4141], or to the codified statement that the office designated in agency procedures may authorize a deferment pending the resolution of appeal to avoid possible overcollections [@CL-CTF-0861; @CL-CTF-4141].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer determines an overpayment of 50,000 and issues a demand for payment. The contractor does not pay within 30 days, so interest computed from the demand date applies. The contractor sends a written deferment request to the contracting officer and also appeals under the Disputes clause; under the codified text the appeal does not delay collection [@CL-CTF-0769] (the RFO text of FAR 32.607-2 prints no such sentence [@CL-CTF-4141]), and the Government collects the full 50,000 by offset. The appeal succeeds and reduces the debt to 20,000, so 30,000 is remitted and an equitable interest credit is computed under the interest-credit procedures.

## Never confuse

A demand for payment of a contract debt is the Government collecting from the contractor; it is a different matter from a contractor's own bad debts, see [Bad debts](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-bad-debts.md). It is also not a revision of billing rates; see [Billing rates](../billing-payment/ctf-billing-rates.md).

Under the codified text, actions filed under the Disputes Clause shall not suspend or delay collection [@CL-CTF-0769], but the RFO text of FAR 32.607-2 prints no such sentence [@CL-CTF-4141].
