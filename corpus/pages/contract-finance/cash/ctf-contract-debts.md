## Purpose

Covers contracting officer demands for payment of contract debts, deferment procedures, interest accrual, and interest credits.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-0766] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.008, FAR 32.604, FAR 32.607-2 and FAR 32.608-2 [@CL-CTF-3740; @CL-CTF-0766; @CL-CTF-0768; @CL-CTF-0770] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.008 [@CL-CTF-3030], RFO FAR 32.604 [@CL-CTF-4135; @CL-CTF-1359; @CL-CTF-4136; @CL-CTF-4140; @CL-CTF-4137; @CL-CTF-1297; @CL-CTF-1298; @CL-CTF-4138; @CL-CTF-4139], RFO FAR 32.607-2 [@CL-CTF-4141; @CL-CTF-4737] and RFO FAR 32.608-2 [@CL-CTF-4142; @CL-CTF-4143] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0766] |
| Applies to | Contractors determined to owe an actual debt to the Government [@CL-CTF-0766] |

Except as provided in FAR 32.604(c), the contracting officer shall issue the demand for payment as soon as the contracting officer has determined that an actual debt is due the Government and the amount [@CL-CTF-0766]. Under FAR 32.604(c), except as provided in paragraph (d) of that section, the contracting officer should not issue a demand for payment if the contracting officer only becomes aware of the debt when the contractor either provides a lump sum payment or submits a credit invoice (which is a contractor's request to liquidate the debt against existing unpaid bills due the contractor), or notifies the contracting officer that the payment office overpaid on an invoice payment, in which case the contracting officer shall notify the payment office of the overpayment [@CL-CTF-0856; @CL-CTF-1295; @CL-CTF-1296]. Under FAR 32.604(d), if a demand for payment was not issued as provided for in paragraph (c), the contracting officer shall issue a demand for payment no sooner than 30 days after the contracting officer becomes aware of the debt unless the contractor has liquidated the debt, the contractor has requested an installment payment agreement, or the payment office has issued a demand for payment [@CL-CTF-0857; @CL-CTF-0873].

Under FAR 32.604(b), the demand for payment shall include the listed items [@CL-CTF-9565]. For debts resulting from specific contract terms (for example, debts resulting from incentive clause provisions, Quarterly Limitation on Payments Statement, Cost Accounting Standards, or price reduction for defective pricing), the demand includes a notification stating that payment should be made promptly and that interest is due in accordance with the terms of the contract, whereas for all other contract debts it includes a notification stating that any amounts not paid within 30 days from the date of the demand for payment will bear interest and that interest shall be computed from the date of the demand for payment until repayment by the contractor [@CL-CTF-9565; @CL-CTF-1299; @CL-CTF-1350; @CL-CTF-0767]. The demand also includes notification that the contractor may submit a request for installment payments or deferment of collection if immediate payment is not practicable or if the amount is disputed [@CL-CTF-9565; @CL-CTF-0859].

All requests for deferment of collection must be submitted in writing to the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-0768]. If there is no appeal pending or action filed under the Disputes clause of the contract, the information about the contractor that should be submitted with the request is its financial condition, contract backlog, projected cash receipts and requirements, the feasibility of immediate payment of the debt, and the probable effect on operations of immediate payment in full [@CL-CTF-0860; @CL-CTF-9566]. Actions filed by contractors under the Disputes Clause shall not suspend or delay collection [@CL-CTF-0769].

If the contractor notifies the contracting officer of a duplicate payment or that the Government has otherwise overpaid, the contracting officer shall follow the procedures at FAR 32.604 [@CL-CTF-3740].

## How it works

Under FAR 32.607-2(d), the office designated in agency procedures may authorize a deferment pending the resolution of appeal to avoid possible overcollections [@CL-CTF-0861]. Under FAR 32.608-2(a)(1), an equitable interest credit shall be applied under the listed circumstances, including when the amount of debt initially determined is subsequently reduced, for example through a successful appeal [@CL-CTF-0770]. Under FAR 32.608-2(b)(1), any appropriate interest credits shall be computed under the listed procedures, including charging interest at the rate under FAR 52.232-17 on the reduced debt from the date of collection by the Government until the date the monies are remitted to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0858].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 text published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 32.008, if the contractor notifies the contracting officer of a duplicate payment or that the Government has otherwise overpaid, the contracting officer must follow the procedures at 32.604 [@CL-CTF-3030]. Under RFO FAR 32.604, except as provided in paragraph (c), the contracting officer must issue the demand for payment as soon as the contracting officer has determined that an actual debt is due the Government and the amount [@CL-CTF-4135]. Under RFO FAR 32.604(c), except as provided in paragraph (d), the contracting officer should not issue a demand for payment if the contracting officer only becomes aware of the debt when the contractor provides a lump sum payment or submits a credit invoice (a contractor's request to liquidate the debt against existing unpaid bills due the contractor) or notifies the contracting officer that the payment office overpaid on an invoice payment, in which case the contracting officer must notify the payment office of the overpayment [@CL-CTF-4137; @CL-CTF-1297; @CL-CTF-1298]. Under RFO FAR 32.604(d), if a demand for payment was not issued as provided for in paragraph (c), the contracting officer must issue a demand for payment no sooner than 30 days after the contracting officer becomes aware of the debt unless the contractor has liquidated the debt, the contractor has requested an installment payment agreement, or the payment office has issued a demand for payment [@CL-CTF-4138; @CL-CTF-4139]. Under RFO FAR 32.604(b), the demand for payment must include, for all other contract debts, a notification stating that any amounts not paid within 30 days from the date of the demand for payment will bear interest computed from the date of the demand for payment until repayment by the contractor, and a notification that the contractor may submit a request for installment payments or deferment of collection if immediate payment is not practicable or if the amount is disputed [@CL-CTF-1359; @CL-CTF-4136; @CL-CTF-4140]. Under RFO FAR 32.607-2, all requests for deferment of collection must be submitted in writing to the contracting officer, and deferments pending disposition of appeal may be granted to small business concerns and financially weak contractors, balancing the need for Government security against loss and undue hardship on the contractor [@CL-CTF-4141; @CL-CTF-4737]. Under RFO FAR 32.608-2, an equitable interest credit must be applied when the amount of debt initially determined is subsequently reduced, for example through a successful appeal, and any appropriate interest credits must be computed under the listed procedures, including charging interest at the rate under 52.232-17 on the reduced debt from the date of collection by the Government until the date the monies are remitted to the contractor [@CL-CTF-4142; @CL-CTF-4143].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer determines an overpayment of 50,000 and issues a demand for payment [@CL-CTF-0766]. The contractor does not pay within 30 days, so interest computed from the demand date applies [@CL-CTF-0767]. The contractor sends a written deferment request to the contracting officer and also appeals under the Disputes clause [@CL-CTF-0768]. Under FAR 32.607-2(a)(2), actions filed under the Disputes Clause do not suspend or delay collection [@CL-CTF-0769], and the Government collects the full 50,000 by offset. The appeal succeeds and reduces the debt to 20,000, so 30,000 is remitted and an equitable interest credit is applied and computed under the interest-credit procedures [@CL-CTF-0770; @CL-CTF-0858].

## Never confuse

A demand for payment of a contract debt is the Government collecting from the contractor; it is a different matter from a contractor's own bad debts, see [Bad debts](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-bad-debts.md). It is also not a revision of billing rates; see [Billing rates](../billing-payment/ctf-billing-rates.md).

Under FAR 32.607-2(a)(2), actions filed by contractors under the Disputes Clause shall not suspend or delay collection, while under FAR 32.607-2(d) the office designated in agency procedures may authorize a deferment pending the resolution of appeal to avoid possible overcollections [@CL-CTF-0769; @CL-CTF-0861].
