## Purpose

Shows when a contract debt becomes a final decision, how collection escalates, and when the Interest clause is inserted or left out.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3100] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.605, FAR 32.606 and FAR 32.611 [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3102; @CL-CTF-3105] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.605 [@CL-CTF-4114; @CL-CTF-4115; @CL-CTF-4116; @CL-CTF-4117; @CL-CTF-4118], RFO FAR 32.606 [@CL-CTF-4119; @CL-CTF-4120; @CL-CTF-4121; @CL-CTF-4122] and RFO FAR 32.611 [@CL-CTF-4123; @CL-CTF-4124; @CL-CTF-4125; @CL-CTF-4126; @CL-CTF-4127] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3100] |
| Applies to | Contract debts demanded by the contracting officer, and solicitations and contracts that may carry the Interest clause [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3105] |

The contracting officer shall issue a final decision as required by FAR 33.211 if the contracting officer and the contractor are unable to reach agreement on the existence or amount of a debt in a timely manner [@CL-CTF-3100]. A contractor's failure to liquidate a debt previously demanded within the timeline specified in the demand, unless the amounts were not repaid because the contractor has requested an installment payment agreement, is another listed ground [@CL-CTF-3194]. A contractor's request for a deferment of collection on a debt previously demanded by the contracting officer is a further listed ground [@CL-CTF-3195]. If a demand was previously issued, the demand in the final decision must identify the same due date as the original demand [@CL-CTF-3101]. The contracting officer furnishes the decision to the contractor by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by any other method that provides evidence of receipt [@CL-CTF-3104], and forwards a copy to the payment office identified in the contract [@CL-CTF-3196].

If the contractor has not liquidated the debt within 30 days of the date due and has not requested installment payments or deferment of collection, the payment office initiates withholding of principal, interest, penalties and administrative charges [@CL-CTF-3102]. If the contract is assigned under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940, the rights of the assignee will be scrupulously respected and withholding of payments shall be consistent with those rights [@CL-CTF-3197]. Payment offices must transfer any debt that is delinquent more than 180 days to the Department of the Treasury for collection [@CL-CTF-3103]. The contracting officer shall periodically follow up with the payment office to determine whether the debt has been collected and credited to the correct appropriation or appropriations [@CL-CTF-3198].

The contracting officer inserts the clause at FAR 52.232-17, Interest, in solicitations and contracts unless it is contemplated that the contract will be in one or more of the listed categories [@CL-CTF-3105]. The categories are contracts at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, contracts with Government agencies, and contracts with a State or local government or instrumentality [@CL-CTF-3199]. They also include contracts with a foreign government or instrumentality, and contracts without any provision for profit or fee with a nonprofit organization [@CL-CTF-3200]. They also include contracts described in Subpart 5.5, Paid Advertisements, and any other exceptions authorized under agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3201]. The contracting officer may still insert the clause when it is contemplated that the contract will be in any of those categories [@CL-CTF-3202].

## How it works

A demand sets a due date, and the contracting officer issues a final decision when agreement fails, a demanded debt is not liquidated within the demanded timeline, or the contractor requests a deferment of collection [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3194; @CL-CTF-3195]. The final decision repeats the original due date and is sent by a method that gives evidence of receipt [@CL-CTF-3101; @CL-CTF-3104]. If the debt is still not liquidated 30 days after the due date and no installment or deferment request is on file, the payment office starts withholding, while respecting any assignee's rights [@CL-CTF-3102; @CL-CTF-3197]. A debt delinquent more than 180 days goes to the Department of the Treasury [@CL-CTF-3103].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.605 and RFO FAR 32.606 keep the final decision and collection rules described above, including the same due date in a final decision, the withholding point 30 days after the date due, and the transfer of debts delinquent more than 180 days to the Department of Treasury [@CL-CTF-4117; @CL-CTF-4119; @CL-CTF-4121]. RFO FAR 32.611(a) keeps the same excepted categories for the Interest clause, and RFO FAR 32.611(b) still lets the contracting officer insert the clause for them [@CL-CTF-4124; @CL-CTF-4125; @CL-CTF-4126; @CL-CTF-4127]. RFO FAR 32.611(a) states the insertion as a bare direction to insert the clause and does not name who inserts it, where the codified paragraph names the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-4123; @CL-CTF-3105].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The contracting officer demands 25,000 from a contractor, with a due date of Day 0. The contractor pays nothing by Day 0 and has not requested an installment payment agreement, so the contracting officer issues a final decision as required by FAR 33.211 [@CL-CTF-3194; @CL-CTF-3100]. The demand in the final decision keeps the Day 0 due date [@CL-CTF-3101], and the officer sends the decision by certified mail, return receipt requested, with a copy to the payment office [@CL-CTF-3104; @CL-CTF-3196]. On Day 31 the debt is still unpaid, more than 30 days after the due date, and no installment or deferment request is on file, so the payment office initiates withholding of principal, interest, penalties and administrative charges [@CL-CTF-3102]. If the contract is assigned, the withholding respects the assignee's rights [@CL-CTF-3197]. If the debt is still unpaid when it has been delinquent more than 180 days, the payment office transfers it to the Department of the Treasury [@CL-CTF-3103].

## Never confuse

A demand for payment is not a final decision. The demand states a due date, and the final decision follows when agreement fails, a demanded debt is not liquidated, or deferment is requested, and it must repeat the original due date [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3194; @CL-CTF-3195; @CL-CTF-3101]. An excepted category for the Interest clause is also not a ban, because the contracting officer may still insert the clause [@CL-CTF-3202]. The [contract debts](ctf-contract-debts.md) and [general rules](../financing/ctf-contract-debts-general-rules.md) cards cover the rest.
