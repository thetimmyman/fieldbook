## Purpose

Shows when a contract debt becomes a final decision, how collection escalates, and when the Interest clause is inserted or left out.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CTF-3100] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.605, FAR 32.606 and FAR 32.611 [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3102; @CL-CTF-3105] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.605 [@CL-CTF-4114; @CL-CTF-4115; @CL-CTF-4116; @CL-CTF-4117; @CL-CTF-4118], RFO FAR 32.606 [@CL-CTF-4119; @CL-CTF-4120; @CL-CTF-4121; @CL-CTF-4122] and RFO FAR 32.611 [@CL-CTF-4123; @CL-CTF-4124; @CL-CTF-4125; @CL-CTF-4126; @CL-CTF-4127] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-3100] |
| Applies to | Contract debts demanded by the contracting officer, and solicitations and contracts that may carry the Interest clause [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3105] |

The contracting officer shall issue a final decision as required by FAR 33.211 if one of three alternatives applies: (1) the contracting officer and the contractor are unable to reach agreement on the existence or amount of a debt in a timely manner [@CL-CTF-3100], (2) the contractor fails to liquidate a debt previously demanded by the contracting officer within the timeline specified in the demand for payment, unless the amounts were not repaid because the contractor has requested an installment payment agreement [@CL-CTF-3194], or (3) the contractor requests a deferment of collection on a debt previously demanded by the contracting officer (see FAR 32.607-2) [@CL-CTF-3195]. If a demand for payment was previously issued for the debt, the demand for payment included in the final decision shall identify the same due date as the original demand for payment [@CL-CTF-3101]. The contracting officer shall furnish the decision to the contractor by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by any other method that provides evidence of receipt [@CL-CTF-3104], and forward a copy to the payment office identified in the contract [@CL-CTF-3196].

If the contractor has not liquidated the debt within 30 days of the date due or requested installment payments or deferment of collection, the payment office shall initiate withholding of principal, interest, penalties, and administrative charges [@CL-CTF-3102]. In the event the contract is assigned under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 (section 3727 of title 31 and section 6305 of title 41 of the United States Code), the rights of the assignee will be scrupulously respected and withholding of payments shall be consistent with those rights [@CL-CTF-3197]. As provided for in the Debt Collection Improvement Act of 1996 (section 3711(g)(1) of title 31 of the United States Code), payment offices are required to transfer any debt that is delinquent more than 180 days to the Department of the Treasury for collection [@CL-CTF-3103]. The contracting officer shall periodically follow up with the payment office to determine whether the debt has been collected and credited to the correct appropriation or appropriations [@CL-CTF-3198].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.232-17, Interest, in solicitations and contracts unless it is contemplated that the contract will be in one or more of the following categories: (1) contracts at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, (2) contracts with Government agencies, (3) contracts with a State or local government or instrumentality [@CL-CTF-3105; @CL-CTF-3199], (4) contracts with a foreign government or instrumentality, (5) contracts without any provision for profit or fee with a nonprofit organization [@CL-CTF-3200], (6) contracts described in FAR subpart 5.5, Paid Advertisements, or (7) any other exceptions authorized under agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3201]. The contracting officer may insert the FAR clause at 52.232-17, Interest, in solicitations and contracts when it is contemplated that the contract will be in any of the categories specified in FAR 32.611(a) [@CL-CTF-3202].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall issue a final decision as required by FAR 33.211 if the contracting officer and the contractor are unable to reach agreement on the existence or amount of a debt in a timely manner, the contractor fails to liquidate a debt previously demanded by the contracting officer within the timeline specified in the demand for payment (unless the amounts were not repaid because the contractor has requested an installment payment agreement), or the contractor requests a deferment of collection on a debt previously demanded by the contracting officer [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3194; @CL-CTF-3195]. If a demand for payment was previously issued for the debt, the demand for payment included in the final decision shall identify the same due date as the original demand for payment, and the contracting officer shall furnish the decision to the contractor by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by any other method that provides evidence of receipt, and forward a copy to the payment office identified in the contract [@CL-CTF-3101; @CL-CTF-3104; @CL-CTF-3196]. If the contractor has not liquidated the debt within 30 days of the date due or requested installment payments or deferment of collection, the payment office shall initiate withholding of principal, interest, penalties, and administrative charges, and if the contract is assigned under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940, the rights of the assignee will be scrupulously respected and withholding of payments shall be consistent with those rights [@CL-CTF-3102; @CL-CTF-3197]. Payment offices are required to transfer any debt that is delinquent more than 180 days to the Department of the Treasury for collection [@CL-CTF-3103].

Effective February 1, 2026, under DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0022, contracting officers shall use the revised RFO FAR part 32, Contract Financing, in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. Under RFO FAR 32.605, the contracting officer must issue a final decision as required by FAR 33.211 if: (1) the contracting officer and the contractor are unable to reach agreement on the existence or amount of a debt in a timely manner [@CL-CTF-4114], (2) the contractor fails to liquidate a debt previously demanded by the contracting officer within the timeline specified in the demand for payment unless the amounts were not repaid because the contractor has requested an installment payment agreement [@CL-CTF-4115], or (3) the contractor requests a deferment of collection on a debt previously demanded by the contracting officer (see RFO FAR 32.607-2) [@CL-CTF-4116]. If a demand for payment was previously issued for the debt, the demand for payment included in the final decision must identify the same due date as the original demand for payment, and the contracting officer must furnish the decision to the contractor by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by any other method that provides evidence of receipt, and forward a copy to the payment office identified in the contract [@CL-CTF-4117; @CL-CTF-4118]. Under RFO FAR 32.606, if the contractor has not liquidated the debt within 30 days of the date due or requested installment payments or deferment of collection, the payment office must initiate withholding of principal, interest, penalties, and administrative charges, respecting the rights of any assignee under the Assignment of Claims Act of 1940 [@CL-CTF-4119; @CL-CTF-4120]; payment offices are required to transfer any debt that is delinquent more than 180 days to the Department of Treasury for collection, and the contracting officer must periodically follow up with the payment office to determine whether the debt has been collected and credited to the correct appropriation(s) [@CL-CTF-4121; @CL-CTF-4122]. Under RFO FAR 32.611(a), the clause at FAR 52.232-17, Interest, is inserted in solicitations and contracts unless it is contemplated that the contract will be in one or more of the seven listed categories [@CL-CTF-4123; @CL-CTF-4124; @CL-CTF-4125; @CL-CTF-4126], and under RFO FAR 32.611(b), the contracting officer may insert the clause when it is contemplated that the contract will be in any of the categories specified in RFO FAR 32.611(a) [@CL-CTF-4127].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The contracting officer demands 25,000 from a contractor, with a due date of Day 0. The contractor pays nothing by Day 0 and has not requested an installment payment agreement, so the contracting officer issues a final decision as required by FAR 33.211 [@CL-CTF-3194; @CL-CTF-3100]. The demand in the final decision keeps the Day 0 due date [@CL-CTF-3101], and the officer sends the decision by certified mail, return receipt requested, with a copy to the payment office [@CL-CTF-3104; @CL-CTF-3196]. On Day 31 the debt is still unpaid, more than 30 days after the due date, and no installment or deferment request is on file, so the payment office initiates withholding of principal, interest, penalties and administrative charges [@CL-CTF-3102]. If the contract is assigned, the withholding respects the assignee's rights [@CL-CTF-3197]. If the debt is still unpaid when it has been delinquent more than 180 days, the payment office transfers it to the Department of the Treasury [@CL-CTF-3103].

## Never confuse

The contracting officer shall issue a final decision as required by FAR 33.211 if agreement on the existence or amount of a debt is not reached in a timely manner, if the contractor fails to liquidate a previously demanded debt within the demand timeline (unless the amounts were not repaid because an installment payment agreement was requested), or if the contractor requests a deferment of collection on a previously demanded debt, and if a demand for payment was previously issued for the debt, the demand for payment included in the final decision shall identify the same due date as the original demand for payment [@CL-CTF-3100; @CL-CTF-3194; @CL-CTF-3195; @CL-CTF-3101]. Under FAR 32.611(b), the contracting officer may insert the FAR clause at 52.232-17, Interest, in solicitations and contracts when it is contemplated that the contract will be in any of the categories specified in FAR 32.611(a) [@CL-CTF-3202].
