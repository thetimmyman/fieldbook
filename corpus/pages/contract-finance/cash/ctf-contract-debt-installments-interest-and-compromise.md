## Purpose

Covers four sections of the FAR contract debts subpart: how a contractor asks to pay a contract debt in installments, when interest charges apply, how a Government delay in delivering a demand letter moves the interest date, and who may compromise a debt. The subpart prescribes policies and procedures for identifying, collecting and deferring collection of contract debts, including interest if applicable [@CL-CTF-3630].

FAR subpart 32.6 prescribes policies and procedures for identifying, collecting, and deferring collection of contract debts, including interest if applicable [@CL-CTF-3630]. Sections 32.607, 32.608, and 32.610 of the subpart do not apply to claims against common carriers for transportation overcharges and freight and cargo losses under section 3726 of title 31 of the United States Code [@CL-CTF-3631].

If a contractor requests an installment payment agreement, the contracting officer shall notify the contractor to send a written request for installment payments to the office designated in agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3616]. Under FAR 32.607(a), the contracting officer shall not approve or deny a contractor's request for installment payments or deferment of collections, and the office designated in agency procedures is responsible for approving or denying requests for installment payments or deferment of collections [@CL-CTF-1435].

Unless specified otherwise in the clause at FAR 52.232-17, Interest, interest charges shall apply to any contract debt unpaid after 30 days from the issuance of a demand unless the contract is a kind excluded under FAR 32.611 or the contract or debt has been exempted from interest charges under agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3617; @CL-CTF-3618].

If interest is accrued based on the date of the demand letter and delivery of the demand letter is delayed by the Government (for example, undue delay after dating at the originating office or delays in the mail), the date of the debt and accrual of interest shall be extended to a time that is fair and reasonable under the particular circumstances [@CL-CTF-3619; @CL-CTF-3632].

For debts under $100,000, excluding interest, the designated agency official may compromise the debt pursuant to the Federal Claims Collection Standards (31 CFR part 902) and agency regulations [@CL-CTF-3620]. Unless specifically authorized by agency procedures, contracting officers cannot compromise debts [@CL-CTF-3621].

## How it works

When a contractor asks for an installment payment agreement, the contracting officer notifies the contractor to send a written request for installment payments to the office designated in agency procedures, and that designated office is responsible for approving or denying the request while the contracting officer shall not approve or deny it [@CL-CTF-3616; @CL-CTF-1435]. For debts under $100,000, excluding interest, the designated agency official may compromise the debt under the Federal Claims Collection Standards and agency regulations, whereas contracting officers cannot compromise debts unless specifically authorized by agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3620; @CL-CTF-3621].

Unless the Interest clause at FAR 52.232-17 specifies otherwise, interest charges apply to a contract debt unpaid after 30 days from the issuance of a demand unless the contract is a kind excluded under FAR 32.611 or the contract or debt has been exempted from interest charges under agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3617; @CL-CTF-3618]. The excluded kinds in FAR 32.611 [@CL-CTF-3617] are covered on [Contract debts: final decisions, collection and the Interest clause](ctf-contract-debt-final-decisions-and-collection.md). When interest is accrued based on the date of the demand letter and the Government delays delivery of the demand letter, the date of the debt and accrual of interest are extended to a time that is fair and reasonable under the particular circumstances [@CL-CTF-3619; @CL-CTF-3632].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 text published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.600 states that the subpart prescribes policies and procedures for identifying, collecting, and deferring collection of contract debts, including interest if applicable, and that sections 32.607, 32.608, and 32.610 of the subpart do not apply to claims against common carriers for transportation overcharges and freight and cargo losses under section 3726 of title 31 of the United States Code [@CL-CTF-3033; @CL-CTF-9521]. RFO FAR 32.607-1 requires that if a contractor requests an installment payment agreement, the contracting officer must notify the contractor to send a written request for installment payments to the office designated in agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3034]. RFO FAR 32.608-1 provides that unless specified otherwise in the clause at 52.232-17, Interest, interest charges must apply to any contract debt unpaid after 30 days from the issuance of a demand unless the contract is a kind excluded under 32.611 or the contract or debt has been exempted from interest charges under agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3035; @CL-CTF-3036]. RFO FAR 32.609 provides that if interest is accrued based on the date of the demand letter and delivery of the demand letter is delayed by the Government (for example, undue delay after dating at the originating office or delays in the mail), the date of the debt and accrual of interest must be extended to a time that is fair and reasonable under the particular circumstances [@CL-CTF-3037; @CL-CTF-3038]. RFO FAR 32.610 provides that for debts under $100,000, excluding interest, the designated agency official may compromise the debt pursuant to the Federal Claims Collection Standards (31 CFR part 902) and agency regulations, and that unless specifically authorized by agency procedures, contracting officers cannot compromise debts [@CL-CTF-3039].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor owes 80,000 and cannot pay at once. It requests an installment payment agreement. The contracting officer notifies the contractor to send a written request to the office designated in agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3616]. The contracting officer must not approve or deny it, and the designated office is responsible for approving or denying it [@CL-CTF-1435].

Separately, a demand for 12,000 is dated 1 April. Assume the contract carries the Interest clause with no change to the 30 days, is not a kind excluded under FAR 32.611, and has no exemption under agency procedures. Before any delay is considered, thirty days after issuance ends on 1 May, so a debt still unpaid on 15 May would be past that point and interest charges would apply [@CL-CTF-3617; @CL-CTF-3618]. Now suppose the Government's mail delay meant the letter arrived on 15 April and interest runs from the date of the demand letter. The date of the debt and accrual of interest must then be extended to a time that is fair and reasonable under the particular circumstances [@CL-CTF-3619; @CL-CTF-3632].

A third debt is 98,000 plus 6,000 of interest. With interest it totals 104,000, but excluding interest it is 98,000, which is under 100,000, so the designated agency official may compromise it under the Federal Claims Collection Standards and agency regulations [@CL-CTF-3620]. The contracting officer cannot compromise the debt unless specifically authorized by agency procedures [@CL-CTF-3621]. Under the DoD supplement, only the department or agency contract financing offices are authorized to compromise debts covered by that subpart [@CL-CTF-3624].

## Never confuse

An installment payment request and a debt compromise are governed by separate rules: the office designated in agency procedures is responsible for approving or denying requests for installment payments or deferment of collections [@CL-CTF-1435], while the designated agency official may compromise debts under $100,000, excluding interest, pursuant to the Federal Claims Collection Standards and agency regulations [@CL-CTF-3620].

Interest charges apply to a contract debt unpaid after 30 days from the issuance of a demand unless the Interest clause specifies otherwise or one of the two exceptions applies [@CL-CTF-3617; @CL-CTF-3618]. When interest is accrued based on the date of the demand letter and delivery of the demand letter is delayed by the Government, the date of the debt and accrual of interest are extended to a time that is fair and reasonable under the particular circumstances [@CL-CTF-3619; @CL-CTF-3632].

The $100,000 compromise threshold in FAR 32.610 is measured excluding interest [@CL-CTF-3620].

The demand and deferment steps are in [Contract debts demand deferment and interest](ctf-contract-debts.md).
