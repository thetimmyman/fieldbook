## Purpose

Identifies the types and depth of financial information a DoD contracting officer requests to determine contractor capability.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-0754] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 232.072-2 [@CL-CTF-0754] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0754] |
| Applies to | Contractors from whom financial capability or condition information is required [@CL-CTF-0754] |

The contracting officer shall obtain the type and depth of financial and other information required to establish a contractor's financial capability or disclose a contractor's financial condition [@CL-CTF-0754]. The contracting officer should not request information that is not necessary for protection of the Government's interests, but must insist upon obtaining the information that is necessary [@CL-CTF-0755]. The listed information is obtained to the extent required to protect the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0850].

The information must be for the person(s) who are legally liable for contract performance, and if the contractor is not a corporation, the contracting officer shall obtain the required information for each individual, joint venturer, or partner [@CL-CTF-0756]. Under DFARS 232.072-2(b), the information to be obtained to the extent required to protect the Government's interest includes: at item (1), balance sheet and income statement for the current fiscal year (interim), for the most recent fiscal year and, preferably, for the 2 preceding fiscal years (which should be certified by an independent public accountant or by an appropriate officer of the firm), and forecasted for each fiscal year for the remainder of the period of contract performance [@CL-CTF-0850; @CL-CTF-0783; @CL-CTF-9527]. It also includes: at item (2), a summary history of the contractor and its principal managers, disclosing any previous insolvencies—corporate or personal—and describing its products or services [@CL-CTF-0851], at item (5), a business base and forecast that shows, by significant markets, existing contracts and outstanding offers, including those under negotiation, and is reconcilable to indirect cost rate projections [@CL-CTF-0757], and at item (7), financing arrangement information [@CL-CTF-0852; @CL-CTF-1363; @CL-CTF-1364].

## How it works

The contracting officer obtains the type and depth of financial and other information that is required to establish a contractor's financial capability or disclose a contractor's financial condition [@CL-CTF-0754]. The unwillingness or inability of a contractor to present reasonably requested information in a timely manner, especially information that a prudent business person would be expected to have and to use in the professional management of a business, may be a material fact in the determination of the contractor's responsibility and prospects for contract completion [@CL-CTF-0853; @CL-CTF-9569]. Under DFARS 232.072-2(b)(7), the financing arrangement information discloses: (i) availability of cash to finance contract performance, (ii) the contractor's exposure to financial crisis from creditor's demands, (iii) the degree to which credit security provisions could conflict with Government title terms under contract financing, (iv) clearly stated confirmations of credit with no unacceptable qualifications, and (v) an unambiguous written agreement by a creditor if credit arrangements include deferred trade payments or creditor subordinations or repayment suspensions [@CL-CTF-0852; @CL-CTF-1363; @CL-CTF-1364].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer reviewing a new supplier requests balance sheets and income statements for the current interim year, the most recent fiscal year, and preferably the 2 preceding fiscal years, with 5,000,000 in assets and 3,000,000 in liabilities on the latest statement [@CL-CTF-0783]. The supplier provides statements certified by an independent public accountant, a summary history of the contractor and its principal managers disclosing any previous corporate or personal insolvencies and describing its products or services, a business base and forecast that reconciles to its indirect cost rate projections, and its financing arrangement information [@CL-CTF-0783; @CL-CTF-0851; @CL-CTF-0757; @CL-CTF-0852].

## Never confuse

While the contracting officer should not request information that is not necessary for protection of the Government's interests, the contracting officer must insist upon obtaining the information that is necessary, and obtains the listed information to the extent required to protect the Government's interest [@CL-CTF-0755; @CL-CTF-0850]. What triggers a financial review is covered on [DoD required financial reviews](ctf-dod-required-financial-reviews.md), and for financing generally see [Contract financing overview](../financing/ctf-financing-overview.md).

## Company calibration

Confirm that balance sheets, financing agreements, and business base forecasts are prepared and certified for contracting officer review. [@Q-026]
