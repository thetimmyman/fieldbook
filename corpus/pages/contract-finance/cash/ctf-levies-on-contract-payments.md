## Purpose

Explains contractor notification duties and DoD evaluation standards when continuous tax levies are imposed on contract payments.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CTF-0776] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7010 [@CL-CTF-0776] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CTF-0776] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use the Levies on Contract Payments clause as prescribed in DFARS 232.7102 [@CL-CTF-0776] |

The Levies on Contract Payments clause is the clause to be used as prescribed in DFARS 232.7102 [@CL-CTF-0776]. The clause states that section 6331(h) of title 26 of the United States Code authorizes the Internal Revenue Service to continuously levy up to 100 percent of contract payments, up to the amount of tax debt [@CL-CTF-0777].

When a levy is imposed on a payment and the contractor believes that it may result in an inability to perform, the contractor shall promptly notify the Procuring Contracting Officer in writing, with a copy to the Administrative Contracting Officer, and shall provide the items the clause lists [@CL-CTF-0778; @CL-CTF-9570]. The notice must provide the total dollar amount of the levy, a statement that the contractor believes the levy may result in an inability to perform with rationale and adequate supporting documentation, and advice as to whether the inability to perform may adversely affect national security, including rationale and adequate supporting documentation [@CL-CTF-0868; @CL-CTF-9571].

## How it works

DoD shall promptly review the contractor's assessment, and the Procuring Contracting Officer shall provide a written notification to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0869]. The notification includes a statement as to whether DoD agrees that the levy may result in an inability to perform the contract [@CL-CTF-9528], and it includes one of two alternatives. One alternative applies if the levy may result in an inability to perform and the lack of performance will adversely affect national security: the total amount of the monies collected that should be returned to the contractor [@CL-CTF-0779]. The other applies if the levy may result in an inability to perform but will not impact national security: a recommendation that the contractor promptly notify the Internal Revenue Service to attempt to resolve the tax situation [@CL-CTF-0870; @CL-CTF-9529]. Any DoD determination under the clause is not subject to appeal under the Contract Disputes Act [@CL-CTF-0780].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The Internal Revenue Service levies 100 percent of a 200,000 payment under a defense contract. The contractor promptly notifies the Procuring Contracting Officer in writing, with a copy to the Administrative Contracting Officer, with the levy amount, its rationale and supporting documents, and advice that the lost performance may affect national security. DoD agrees, and the written notification states that 150,000 of the collected monies should be returned to the contractor.

## Never confuse

The clause rests on the authority of the Internal Revenue Service to continuously levy contract payments [@CL-CTF-0777], and any DoD determination under the clause is not subject to appeal under the Contract Disputes Act [@CL-CTF-0780]. For payment timing see [Prompt payment](../billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md), and for tax costs see [Federal, state, and local taxes](../billing-payment/ctf-federal-state-local-taxes.md).
