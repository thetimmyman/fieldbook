## Purpose

Shows the notes a registrant gives about restrictions on dividends and on transfers from subsidiaries, summarized information on unconsolidated subsidiaries and persons owned 50 percent or less [@CL-CLS-0843; @CL-CLS-0844; @CL-CLS-0845; @CL-CLS-0846], related-party transactions and intercompany profits [@CL-CLS-0847; @CL-CLS-0848], omitting substantially identical notes [@CL-CLS-1453; @CL-CLS-1454], and when separate financial statements of a majority-owned subsidiary not consolidated must be filed [@CL-CLS-0849; @CL-CLS-0855].

If applicable to the person for which the financial statements are filed, the general notes on dividend restrictions, summarized information and related party transactions shall be set forth on the face of the appropriate statement or in appropriately captioned notes [@CL-CLS-0852]. The information shall be provided for each statement required to be filed, except that the information required by paragraphs (b), (c), (d), (e), and (f) of the section shall be provided as of the most recent audited balance sheet being filed and for paragraph (j) of the section as specified therein [@CL-CLS-1572; @CL-CLS-1573], and when specific statements are presented separately, the pertinent notes shall accompany such statements unless cross-referencing is appropriate [@CL-CLS-1574]. The registrant shall describe the most significant restrictions on the payment of dividends by the registrant, indicating their sources, their pertinent provisions and the amount of retained earnings or net income restricted or free of restrictions [@CL-CLS-0843]. When material, it shall also describe the nature of any restrictions on the ability of consolidated subsidiaries and unconsolidated subsidiaries to transfer funds to the registrant in the form of cash dividends, loans or advances [@CL-CLS-0844]. Summarized information as to assets, liabilities and results of operations as detailed in § 210.1-02(bb) shall be presented in notes to the financial statements on an individual or group basis for subsidiaries not consolidated, which is one of two listed groups [@CL-CLS-0845]. The other listed group is 50 percent or less owned persons accounted for by the equity method by the registrant or by a subsidiary of the registrant, if the criteria in § 210.1-02(w) for a significant subsidiary are met [@CL-CLS-0846]. The rule then gives two alternatives: (A) individually by any subsidiary not consolidated or any 50 percent or less owned person, or (B) on an aggregated basis by any combination of such subsidiaries and persons [@CL-CLS-1586]. Summarized information of subsidiaries not consolidated shall not be combined for disclosure purposes with the summarized information of 50 percent or less owned persons [@CL-CLS-0853]. Amounts of related party transactions should be stated on the face of the balance sheet, statement of comprehensive income, or statement of cash flows [@CL-CLS-0847]. In cases where separate financial statements are presented for the registrant, certain investees, or subsidiaries, any intercompany profits or losses resulting from transactions with related parties and the effects thereof shall be disclosed [@CL-CLS-0848].

If a note covering substantially the same subject matter is required with respect to two or more financial statements relating to the same or affiliated persons, for which separate sets of notes are presented, the required information may be shown in a note to only one of such statements [@CL-CLS-1453], provided that a clear and specific reference thereto is made in each of the other statements with respect to which the note is required [@CL-CLS-1454].

If any of the conditions set forth in § 210.1-02(w), substituting 20 percent for 10 percent in the tests used therein to determine a significant subsidiary, are met for a majority-owned subsidiary not consolidated by the registrant or by a subsidiary of the registrant, separate financial statements of such subsidiary must be filed [@CL-CLS-0855; @CL-CLS-0849].

## How it works

The registrant describes the dividend and transfer restrictions in its notes [@CL-CLS-0843; @CL-CLS-0844]. For subsidiaries not consolidated, and for equity-method persons owned 50 percent or less that meet the criteria in § 210.1-02(w) for a significant subsidiary, it presents summarized information as detailed in § 210.1-02(bb) [@CL-CLS-0845; @CL-CLS-0846]. It states related-party amounts on the face of the statements and discloses intercompany profits or losses and their effects where separate financial statements are presented [@CL-CLS-0847; @CL-CLS-0848]. A majority-owned subsidiary not consolidated by the registrant or by a subsidiary of the registrant for which any of the conditions in § 210.1-02(w) are met, substituting 20 percent for 10 percent in the significant-subsidiary tests, requires separate financial statements [@CL-CLS-0855; @CL-CLS-0849].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant holds a majority-owned subsidiary that it does not consolidate. The subsidiary meets the significance tests at the raised percentage, so the registrant files the subsidiary's separate financial statements. It also notes a restriction that stops the subsidiary from paying cash dividends to the registrant, and it discloses 25 of intercompany profit from sales between the two.

## Never confuse

The summarized information note is not the separate statements rule: the note presents summarized information as detailed in § 210.1-02(bb), while the separate statements rule requires separate financial statements of a majority-owned subsidiary not consolidated when any of the conditions in § 210.1-02(w) are met with 20 percent substituted for 10 percent [@CL-CLS-0845; @CL-CLS-0855; @CL-CLS-0849]. Summarized information of subsidiaries not consolidated shall not be combined for disclosure purposes with the summarized information of 50 percent or less owned persons [@CL-CLS-0853; @CL-CLS-0846].
