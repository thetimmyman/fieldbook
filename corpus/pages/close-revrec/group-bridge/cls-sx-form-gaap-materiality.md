## Purpose

Shows three ground rules for financial statements filed under Regulation S-X: statements not prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles are presumed misleading or inaccurate, the required information is a minimum requirement, and items whose amounts are not material need not be separately set forth [@CL-CLS-0814; @CL-CLS-0815; @CL-CLS-0816]. It also shows the rules on inapplicable captions and omitted financial statements and who sections 210.5-01 to 210.5-04 apply to [@CL-CLS-1450; @CL-CLS-1451; @CL-CLS-1452; @CL-CLS-0817; @CL-CLS-0818].

Financial statements filed with the Commission that are not prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles will be presumed to be misleading or inaccurate, despite footnote or other disclosures, unless the Commission has otherwise provided [@CL-CLS-0815]. The information required with respect to any statement shall be furnished as a minimum requirement to which shall be added such further material information as is necessary to make the required statements, in the light of the circumstances under which they are made, not misleading [@CL-CLS-0814]. If the amount that would otherwise be required to be shown with respect to any item is not material, it need not be separately set forth, and the combination of insignificant amounts is permitted [@CL-CLS-0816].

Sections 210.5-01 to 210.5-04 shall be applicable to financial statements filed for all persons except those listed [@CL-CLS-0817]. The first two listed exclusions are registered investment companies (see §§ 210.6-01 through 210.6-11) and employee stock purchase, savings and similar plans (see §§ 210.6A-01 to 210.6A-05) [@CL-CLS-0817]. Further listed exclusions cover insurance companies (see §§ 210.7-01 to 210.7-05), bank holding companies and banks (see §§ 210.9-01 to 210.9-07), and brokers and dealers when filing Form X-17A-5 [249.617] (see §§ 240.17a-5 and 240.17a-10 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934) [@CL-CLS-0818].

No caption should be shown in any financial statement as to which the items and conditions are not present [@CL-CLS-1450]. Financial statements not required or inapplicable because the required matter is not present need not be filed [@CL-CLS-1451]. The reasons for the omission of any required financial statements shall be indicated [@CL-CLS-1452].

## How it works

The registrant prepares its statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, because statements filed with the Commission that are not prepared that way will be presumed to be misleading or inaccurate despite footnote or other disclosures, unless the Commission has otherwise provided [@CL-CLS-0815]. It furnishes the required information as a minimum requirement and adds such further material information as is necessary to make the required statements, in the light of the circumstances under which they are made, not misleading [@CL-CLS-0814]. It need not separately set forth an item whose amount is not material, and it may combine insignificant amounts [@CL-CLS-0816]. Sections 210.5-01 to 210.5-04 shall be applicable to financial statements filed for all persons except those in the listed exclusions [@CL-CLS-0817; @CL-CLS-0818].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant prepares its inventory note on a non-GAAP basis and explains the difference in a footnote. The statements are still presumed misleading unless the Commission has otherwise provided. Separately, one item of 40,000 sits in total assets of 90 million, and the registrant judges it not material, so it does not show the item on its own line.

## Never confuse

The floor and the materiality relief are not the same rule: the information required with respect to any statement shall be furnished as a minimum requirement to which shall be added such further material information as is necessary to make the required statements, in the light of the circumstances under which they are made, not misleading [@CL-CLS-0814], while if the amount that would otherwise be required to be shown with respect to any item is not material, it need not be separately set forth, and the combination of insignificant amounts is permitted [@CL-CLS-0816]. For materiality under cost accounting standards, see [CAS materiality](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-materiality.md). For the line items themselves, see [Receivables and long-term contract amounts on the balance sheet](../revenue/cls-sx-receivables-balance-sheet.md), [Inventories and long-term contract disclosures](../inventory-wip/cls-sx-inventories-long-term-contracts.md) and [Income statement captions](../revenue/cls-sx-income-statement-captions.md). For the registrant's discussion of estimates, see [Critical accounting estimates in MD&A](../../fpa/forecast-vintage/fpa-critical-accounting-estimates.md).
