## Purpose

Shows how many years of audited statements of comprehensive income and cash flows shall be filed for a registrant, its consolidated subsidiaries and its predecessors, what interim periods are provided, and how changes in stockholders' equity and noncontrolling interests are analysed [@CL-CLS-0807; @CL-CLS-0810; @CL-CLS-0812; @CL-CLS-0813].

| Applies to | Financial statements filed with the Commission for the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated, and for its predecessors [@CL-CLS-0807; @CL-CLS-0850]. A smaller reporting company that elects the Article 8 form and content follows Article 8 instead, which requires two fiscal years [@CL-CLS-0859; @CL-CLS-0860; @CL-CLS-0861]. |

Outside the Article 8 election for smaller reporting companies [@CL-CLS-0860], there shall be filed, for the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated and for its predecessors, audited statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for each of the three fiscal years preceding the date of the most recent audited balance sheet being filed, or such shorter period as the registrant, including predecessors, has been in existence [@CL-CLS-0807; @CL-CLS-0808]. In a Securities Act registration statement for the initial public offering of the emerging growth company's equity securities, an emerging growth company may provide audited statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for each of the two fiscal years preceding the date of the most recent audited balance sheet, or such shorter period as the registrant has been in existence [@CL-CLS-0809; @CL-CLS-1585].

In addition, for any interim period between the latest audited balance sheet and the date of the most recent interim balance sheet being filed, and for the corresponding period of the preceding fiscal year, statements of comprehensive income and cash flows shall be provided [@CL-CLS-0810]. Such interim financial statements may be unaudited and need not be presented in greater detail than is required by § 210.10-01 [@CL-CLS-0811]. A separate rule on changes in stockholders' equity and noncontrolling interests requires that an analysis of the changes in each caption of stockholders' equity and noncontrolling interests presented in the balance sheets shall be given in a note or separate statement [@CL-CLS-0812]. That analysis shall be presented in the form of a reconciliation of the beginning balance to the ending balance for each period for which a statement of comprehensive income is required to be filed, with all significant reconciling items described by appropriate captions and with contributions from and distributions to owners shown separately [@CL-CLS-0813; @CL-CLS-0483].

## How it works

The registrant counts back from the date of the most recent audited balance sheet being filed and files audited statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for each of the three fiscal years, or for such shorter period as the registrant, including predecessors, has been in existence [@CL-CLS-0807; @CL-CLS-0808]. An emerging growth company may provide two fiscal years (or such shorter period in existence) in a Securities Act registration statement for the initial public offering of its equity securities [@CL-CLS-0809; @CL-CLS-1585]. Separately, sections 210.8-01 through 210.8-08 (Article 8) shall be applicable to financial statements filed for smaller reporting companies [@CL-CLS-0859]. Smaller reporting companies electing to prepare their financial statements with the form and content required in Article 8 need not apply the other form and content requirements in Regulation S-X except for the listed exceptions [@CL-CLS-0860], and under Article 8 they file audited statements of comprehensive income, cash flows and changes in stockholders' equity for each of the two fiscal years preceding the date of the most recent audited balance sheet, or such shorter period as the registrant has been in business [@CL-CLS-0861]. Interim statements shall be provided for any interim period between the latest audited balance sheet and the date of the most recent interim balance sheet being filed, and for the corresponding period of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-CLS-0810]. The equity analysis reconciles the beginning balance to the ending balance for each period for which a statement of comprehensive income is required to be filed [@CL-CLS-0813].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant with a long history files its annual report. It files audited statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for the 3 latest fiscal years. It also files a 6-month interim period and the matching 6 months of the prior year, and these may be unaudited. The equity note reconciles each equity caption from its opening to its closing balance for each of the 3 years.

## Never confuse

The three-year rule is not the only period: an emerging growth company may provide two fiscal years (or such shorter period in existence) in a Securities Act registration statement for the initial public offering of its equity securities [@CL-CLS-0809; @CL-CLS-1585], and a smaller reporting company that elects the Article 8 form and content files audited statements of comprehensive income, cash flows and changes in stockholders' equity for each of the two fiscal years preceding the date of the most recent audited balance sheet (or such shorter period in business) [@CL-CLS-0860; @CL-CLS-0861]. For the balance sheet dates rule, see [Consolidated balance sheets required by Regulation S-X](cls-sx-consolidated-balance-sheets.md). An analysis of the changes in each caption of stockholders' equity and noncontrolling interests presented in the balance sheets shall be given in a note or separate statement as a reconciliation of the beginning balance to the ending balance for each period for which a statement of comprehensive income is required to be filed [@CL-CLS-0812; @CL-CLS-0813]. For the management discussion of results, see [MD&A results of operations](../../fpa/kpi/fpa-mdna-results-of-operations.md).
