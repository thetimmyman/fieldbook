## Purpose

Shows which audited consolidated balance sheets must be filed for a registrant, its consolidated subsidiaries and its predecessors, how a registrant in existence for less than one fiscal year and an early filing change the balance sheet dates, when an interim balance sheet is required, and how discount on shares is shown [@CL-CLS-0800; @CL-CLS-0801; @CL-CLS-0802; @CL-CLS-0803; @CL-CLS-1455].

| Applies to | Financial statements filed with the Commission for the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated, and for its predecessors [@CL-CLS-0800; @CL-CLS-0850] |

There must be filed, for the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated and for its predecessors, audited balance sheets as of the end of each of the two most recent fiscal years [@CL-CLS-0800]. If the registrant has been in existence for less than one fiscal year, there must be filed an audited balance sheet as of a date within 135 days of the date of filing the registration statement [@CL-CLS-0801].

For a filing other than a filing on Form 10-K or Form 10, made within 45 days after the end of the registrant's fiscal year, where audited financial statements for the most recent fiscal year are not available, the balance sheets may be as of the end of the two preceding fiscal years [@CL-CLS-0802], and the filing shall include an additional balance sheet as of an interim date at least as current as the end of the registrant's third fiscal quarter of the most recently completed fiscal year [@CL-CLS-0803]. Paragraph (c) of the rule extends the instruction in paragraph (b) to filings, other than on Form 10-K or Form 10, made after 45 days but within the number of days of the end of the registrant's fiscal year specified in paragraph (i), provided that all three listed conditions are met [@CL-CLS-0804; @CL-CLS-0857]. The first condition is that the registrant files annual, quarterly and other reports pursuant to section 13 or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and all reports due have been filed [@CL-CLS-0856]. The second condition is that for the most recent fiscal year for which audited financial statements are not yet available the registrant reasonably and in good faith expects to report income attributable to the registrant, after taxes [@CL-CLS-0857]. The third condition is that for at least one of the two fiscal years immediately preceding the most recent fiscal year the registrant reported income attributable to the registrant, after taxes [@CL-CLS-0858]. For filings made after 45 days but within the number of days of the end of the registrant's fiscal year specified in paragraph (i) where the conditions set forth in paragraph (c) are not met, the filing must include the audited balance sheets required by paragraph (a) [@CL-CLS-0805]. Paragraph (i)(1) sets the number of days at 60 days (75 days for fiscal years ending before December 15, 2006) for large accelerated filers (as defined in § 240.12b-2 of the chapter), 75 days for accelerated filers (as defined in § 240.12b-2 of the chapter) and 90 days for all other registrants [@CL-CLS-0851]. Any interim balance sheet provided in accordance with the requirements of the section may be unaudited and need not be presented in greater detail than is required by § 210.10-01 [@CL-CLS-0806], and notwithstanding the requirements of the section, the most recent interim balance sheet included in a filing shall be at least as current as the most recent balance sheet filed with the Commission on Form 10-Q [@CL-CLS-1566].

A separate rule covers discount on shares: discount on shares, or any unamortized balance thereof, shall be shown separately as a deduction from the applicable account or accounts as circumstances require [@CL-CLS-1455].

## How it works

The registrant starts with two audited year-end balance sheets, consolidated with its subsidiaries and covering its predecessors [@CL-CLS-0800]. Two cases change the dates: a registrant in existence for less than one fiscal year [@CL-CLS-0801], and an early filing made before audited financial statements for the most recent fiscal year are available [@CL-CLS-0802; @CL-CLS-0803]. The later window is measured in a number of days that paragraph (i) of the rule sets by type of filer [@CL-CLS-0804; @CL-CLS-0851]. Any interim balance sheet provided in accordance with the requirements of the section may be unaudited and need not be presented in greater detail than is required by § 210.10-01 [@CL-CLS-0806], and the most recent interim balance sheet included in a filing shall be at least as current as the most recent balance sheet filed with the Commission on Form 10-Q [@CL-CLS-1566].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A new registrant has existed for 8 months and files a registration statement. Its audited balance sheet is dated 100 days before the filing date. That is within 135 days, so it meets the new-registrant rule. A balance sheet dated 150 days before filing would not.

## Never confuse

The 135 days measure the age of a new registrant's audited balance sheet at the filing date, and the 45 days measure how soon after year end a filing may use the earlier year-end balance sheets [@CL-CLS-0801; @CL-CLS-0802]. The statements of comprehensive income and cash flows follow their own period rule, covered in [Statements of comprehensive income, cash flows and equity under Regulation S-X](cls-sx-income-cash-flow-equity-statements.md). Article 4 and other articles of Regulation S-X clarify disclosures that must be included in any event in financial statements filed with the Commission [@CL-CLS-0850]. For the management discussion that accompanies the financial statements, see [MD&A objective and liquidity](../../fpa/planning-chain/fpa-mdna-objective-and-liquidity.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company is a registrant, and if not, which lender or investor calendar sets its audited balance sheet dates. [@Q-006]
