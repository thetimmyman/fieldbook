## Purpose

Covers the unaudited statements that an inactive registrant may file for reports under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, the conditions that make a registrant inactive, and the Commission's powers to permit omitted or substituted statements and to require other statements [@CL-CLS-0383; @CL-CLS-0390; @CL-CLS-0391].

| Applies to | Inactive entities, for reports under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and filings in which the Commission permits omission or substitution or requires other statements [@CL-CLS-0383; @CL-CLS-0390; @CL-CLS-0391] |

If a registrant is an inactive entity as defined in the section, the financial statements required by the regulation for purposes of reports under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 may be unaudited [@CL-CLS-0383]. An inactive entity is one meeting all of the listed conditions [@CL-CLS-0384]. Gross receipts from all sources for the fiscal year are not in excess of $100,000 [@CL-CLS-0385]. The registrant has not purchased or sold any of its own stock, granted options for its stock, or levied assessments upon outstanding stock [@CL-CLS-0386]. Expenditures for all purposes for the fiscal year are not in excess of $100,000 [@CL-CLS-0387]. No material change in the business has occurred during the fiscal year, including any bankruptcy, reorganization, readjustment or succession or any material acquisition or disposition of plants, mines, mining equipment, mine rights or leases [@CL-CLS-0388]. No exchange upon which the shares are listed, or governmental authority having jurisdiction, requires the furnishing to it or the publication of audited financial statements [@CL-CLS-0389].

Upon the informal written request of the registrant, and where consistent with the protection of investors, the Commission may permit the omission of one or more of the required financial statements or the filing in substitution for them of appropriate statements of comparable character [@CL-CLS-0390]. The Commission may also, by informal written notice, require the filing of other financial statements in addition to, or in substitution for, the required statements [@CL-CLS-0391]. That notice power applies in any case where such statements are necessary or appropriate for an adequate presentation of the financial condition of any person whose financial statements are required, or whose statements are otherwise necessary for the protection of investors [@CL-CLS-0392]. The condition "where consistent with the protection of investors" is stated for the permission on request, not for the notice power [@CL-CLS-0390; @CL-CLS-0391].

## How it works

The inactive-entity route removes the audit, not the statements: for reports under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 the required statements may be unaudited [@CL-CLS-0383]. Every listed condition must hold together [@CL-CLS-0384]. The receipts and expenditure figures are measured on the registrant's fiscal year [@CL-CLS-0385; @CL-CLS-0387]. The section names no decision-maker for inactive status [@CL-CLS-0383]. The other route runs through the Commission, which decides: it may permit omission or substitution on the registrant's informal written request, and it may require other statements by informal written notice [@CL-CLS-0390; @CL-CLS-0391]. The notice power does not name who must file the other statements [@CL-CLS-0391]. The Commission's permission is also one of the conditions in the nine-to-twelve-month rule, covered on [Financial statements covering nine to twelve months](cls-sx-nine-to-twelve-month-statements.md) [@CL-CLS-0379].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Case A. A registrant has gross receipts of 60,000 and expenditures of 40,000 for the fiscal year. It did not buy or sell its own stock, grant options or levy assessments. No material change occurred in its business, and no exchange or governmental authority requires audited statements. It meets every listed condition, so the statements required for its reports under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 may be unaudited [@CL-CLS-0383; @CL-CLS-0384; @CL-CLS-0385; @CL-CLS-0386; @CL-CLS-0387; @CL-CLS-0388; @CL-CLS-0389].

Case B. A registrant that sold some of its own stock during the year does not meet the stock condition. It is not an inactive entity under this section [@CL-CLS-0384; @CL-CLS-0386].

## Never confuse

An inactive entity is not simply a registrant with little activity; all of the listed conditions must hold, including the two $100,000 ceilings and the stock, business-change and audit-demand conditions [@CL-CLS-0384; @CL-CLS-0385; @CL-CLS-0387]. Nor is the Commission's power the same route: it lets the Commission permit omission or substitution on request, and separately lets it require other statements, and each power carries its own conditions [@CL-CLS-0390; @CL-CLS-0391; @CL-CLS-0392].

## Company calibration

Whether a dormant or run-off registrant still meets every inactivity condition, and whether any omission or substitution has been requested in writing. [@Q-006]
