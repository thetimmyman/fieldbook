## Purpose

Sets out the columns of the valuation and qualifying accounts schedule and what each shows, how the accounts are listed and grouped, and when the schedule is filed [@CL-CLS-0399; @CL-CLS-0403; @CL-CLS-0406]. The schedule sections prescribe the form and content of schedules; the schedule itself is required by other sections, including § 210.5-04, § 210.6-10 and § 210.7-05 [@CL-CLS-0405; @CL-CLS-0406; @CL-CLS-0411; @CL-CLS-0412]. The condensed parent-only schedule is on [Schedules and condensed financial information of the registrant](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-schedules-and-parent-only-information.md).

| Applies to | The schedule prescribed by § 210.12-09 that § 210.5-04 requires in support of valuation and qualifying accounts included in each balance sheet but not included in Schedule VI; §§ 210.6-10 and 210.7-05 also call for it [@CL-CLS-0406; @CL-CLS-0411; @CL-CLS-0412] |

Section 12-01 states that the schedule sections prescribe the form and content of the schedules required by §§ 210.5-04, 210.6-10, 210.6A-05, and 210.7-05 [@CL-CLS-0405]. Under § 210.5-04, the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-09, Schedule II, shall be filed in support of valuation and qualifying accounts included in each balance sheet but not included in Schedule VI [@CL-CLS-0406]. Except as expressly provided otherwise in the applicable form, Schedule II shall be filed as of the date of the most recent audited balance sheet for each person or group [@CL-CLS-0407]. Subject to the same exception, it shall be filed for each period for which an audited statement of comprehensive income is required to be filed for each person or group [@CL-CLS-0408]. If the information may be shown in the related financial statement or in a note without making the statement unclear or confusing, that procedure may be followed and the schedule omitted [@CL-CLS-0409]. The schedules shall be examined by the independent accountant if the related financial statements are so examined [@CL-CLS-0410]. The dates, omission route and examination rule in this paragraph come from § 210.5-04 and are stated here for that section only [@CL-CLS-0407; @CL-CLS-0408; @CL-CLS-0409; @CL-CLS-0410]. Section 210.6-10 also calls for the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-09, as Schedule VII, in support of all other reserves included in the balance sheet [@CL-CLS-0411]. Section 210.7-05 calls for it as Schedule V, in support of valuation and qualifying accounts included in the balance sheet [@CL-CLS-0412].

The columns are A, Description; B, Balance at beginning of period; C, Additions; D, Deductions, with a description; and E, Balance at end of period [@CL-CLS-0399]. Under column C, additions are shown as (1) charged to costs and expenses and (2) charged to other accounts, which are described [@CL-CLS-0400].

The note to the schedule gives the listing rules. All valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves not included in specific schedules must be listed by major classes [@CL-CLS-0401]. Each class must be identified by descriptive title [@CL-CLS-0402]. The accounts must be grouped as (a) those deducted in the balance sheet from the assets to which they apply and (b) the reserves that support the balance sheet caption, Reserves [@CL-CLS-0403]. Accounts and reserves whose additions, deductions and balances were not individually significant may be grouped in one total, and in that case the information called for under columns C and D need not be given [@CL-CLS-0404].

## How it works

Section 5-04 says when Schedule II is filed, and § 12-09 gives its form; §§ 6-10 and 7-05 call for the same form under their own schedule numbers [@CL-CLS-0405; @CL-CLS-0406; @CL-CLS-0411; @CL-CLS-0412]. The columns run from the balance at the beginning of the period, through additions and deductions, to the balance at the end of the period [@CL-CLS-0399]. Additions are split between amounts charged to costs and expenses and amounts charged to other accounts, which are described [@CL-CLS-0400]. Deductions are described [@CL-CLS-0399]. The classes are listed, titled and grouped under the note [@CL-CLS-0401; @CL-CLS-0402; @CL-CLS-0403]. The paragraph on small accounts sets no figure for "individually significant" and does not say who judges it [@CL-CLS-0404].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company's balance sheet deducts an allowance from the receivables it applies to, and the allowance is not included in Schedule VI, so under § 210.5-04 Schedule II supports it [@CL-CLS-0406]. The balance at beginning of period is 100, additions charged to costs and expenses are 40, and deductions described as write-offs are 25, and the balance at end of period is 115, which is 100 plus 40 less 25 [@CL-CLS-0399; @CL-CLS-0400]. The allowance goes in the group of accounts deducted in the balance sheet from the assets to which they apply [@CL-CLS-0403]. A separate reserve that supports the balance sheet caption Reserves goes in the other group [@CL-CLS-0403]. Three small reserves whose additions, deductions and balances are each not individually significant may be grouped in one total, and the information called for under columns C and D need not be given for it [@CL-CLS-0404]. If the same information is shown in a note to the financial statements without making them unclear or confusing, the schedule may instead be omitted [@CL-CLS-0409].

## Never confuse

A valuation and qualifying account deducted in the balance sheet from the asset to which it applies is not a reserve that supports the balance sheet caption Reserves; the schedule groups the two separately [@CL-CLS-0403]. The form of the schedule comes from § 210.12-09, but for Schedule II the duty to file it, and the route to omit it, come from § 210.5-04, not from § 210.12-09 [@CL-CLS-0405; @CL-CLS-0406; @CL-CLS-0409]; other sections call for the same form on their own terms [@CL-CLS-0411; @CL-CLS-0412].

## Company calibration

Which valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves the company carries, their opening and closing balances, how additions and deductions are described, and whether the information is shown in the financial statements or a note instead. [@Q-027]
