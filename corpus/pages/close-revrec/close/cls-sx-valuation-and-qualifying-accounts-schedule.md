## Purpose

Sets out the columns of the valuation and qualifying accounts schedule and what each shows, how the accounts are listed and grouped, and when the schedule is filed [@CL-CLS-0399; @CL-CLS-0403; @CL-CLS-0406]. The schedule sections prescribe the form and content of schedules; the schedule itself is required by other sections, including § 210.5-04, § 210.6-10 and § 210.7-05 [@CL-CLS-0405; @CL-CLS-0406; @CL-CLS-0411; @CL-CLS-0412]. The condensed parent-only schedule is on [Schedules and condensed financial information of the registrant](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-schedules-and-parent-only-information.md).

Section 210.12-01 states that these sections prescribe the form and content of the schedules required by §§ 210.5-04, 210.6-10, 210.6A-05, and 210.7-05 [@CL-CLS-0405]. Under § 210.5-04, Schedule II, the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-09, shall be filed in support of valuation and qualifying accounts included in each balance sheet but not included in Schedule VI [@CL-CLS-0406]. Under § 210.5-04(a), except as expressly provided otherwise in the applicable form, the schedules specified in § 210.5-04 as Schedules II and III shall be filed as of the date of the most recent audited balance sheet for each person or group [@CL-CLS-0407], and Schedule II of § 210.5-04 shall be filed for each period for which an audited statement of comprehensive income is required to be filed for each person or group [@CL-CLS-0407; @CL-CLS-0408]. Under § 210.5-04(b), if the information required by any schedule (including the notes thereto) may be shown in the related financial statement or in a note thereto without making such statement unclear or confusing, that procedure may be followed and the schedule omitted [@CL-CLS-0409]. Under § 210.5-04(c), the schedules shall be examined by the independent accountant if the related financial statements are so examined [@CL-CLS-0410]. Under § 210.6-10, Schedule VII—Valuation and qualifying accounts, the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-09, shall be filed in support of all other reserves included in the balance sheet [@CL-CLS-0411]. Under § 210.7-05, Schedule V—Valuation and qualifying accounts, the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-09, shall be filed in support of valuation and qualifying accounts included in the balance sheet (see § 210.4-02) [@CL-CLS-0412].

The columns in § 210.12-09 are Column A—Description (with footnote 1) [@CL-CLS-0399]; Column B—Balance at beginning of period; Column C—Additions; Column D—Deductions—describe; and Column E—Balance at end of period [@CL-CLS-0399]. Under Column C, additions are shown as (1)—Charged to costs and expenses and (2)—Charged to other accounts—describe [@CL-CLS-0400].

Footnote 1 to Column A directs listing, by major classes, all valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves not included in specific schedules [@CL-CLS-0401], identifying each class of valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves by descriptive title [@CL-CLS-0402], and grouping (a) those valuation and qualifying accounts which are deducted in the balance sheet from the assets to which they apply and (b) those reserves which support the balance sheet caption, Reserves [@CL-CLS-0403]. Valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves as to which the additions, deductions, and balances were not individually significant may be grouped in one total and in such case the information called for under columns C and D need not be given [@CL-CLS-0404].

## How it works

Section 210.12-01 states that these sections prescribe the form and content of the schedules required by §§ 210.5-04, 210.6-10, 210.6A-05, and 210.7-05 [@CL-CLS-0405], while §§ 210.5-04, 210.6-10, and 210.7-05 require the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-09 as Schedule II, Schedule VII, and Schedule V respectively [@CL-CLS-0406; @CL-CLS-0411; @CL-CLS-0412]. The columns in § 210.12-09 are Column A—Description (with footnote 1) [@CL-CLS-0399]; Column B—Balance at beginning of period; Column C—Additions, divided into (1)—Charged to costs and expenses and (2)—Charged to other accounts—describe [@CL-CLS-0399; @CL-CLS-0400]; Column D—Deductions—describe; and Column E—Balance at end of period [@CL-CLS-0399]. Footnote 1 directs listing all valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves not included in specific schedules by major classes [@CL-CLS-0401], identifying each class by descriptive title [@CL-CLS-0402], and grouping (a) those valuation and qualifying accounts which are deducted in the balance sheet from the assets to which they apply and (b) those reserves which support the balance sheet caption, Reserves [@CL-CLS-0403], while valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves as to which the additions, deductions, and balances were not individually significant may be grouped in one total and in such case the information called for under columns C and D need not be given [@CL-CLS-0404].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company's balance sheet deducts an allowance from the receivables it applies to, and the allowance is not included in Schedule VI, so under § 210.5-04 Schedule II supports it. The balance at beginning of period is 100, additions charged to costs and expenses are 40, and deductions described as write-offs are 25, and the balance at end of period is 115, which is 100 plus 40 less 25. The allowance goes in the group of accounts deducted in the balance sheet from the assets to which they apply. A separate reserve that supports the balance sheet caption Reserves goes in the other group. Three small reserves whose additions, deductions and balances are each not individually significant may be grouped in one total, and the information called for under columns C and D need not be given for it. If the same information is shown in a note to the financial statements without making them unclear or confusing, the schedule may instead be omitted.

## Never confuse

The schedule requirement and the note alternative are not the same route: footnote 1 in § 210.12-09 groups (a) those valuation and qualifying accounts which are deducted in the balance sheet from the assets to which they apply and (b) those reserves which support the balance sheet caption, Reserves [@CL-CLS-0403]. Section 210.12-01 states that the Article 12 sections prescribe the form and content of the schedules required by §§ 210.5-04, 210.6-10, 210.6A-05, and 210.7-05 [@CL-CLS-0405], whereas under § 210.5-04 Schedule II shall be filed in support of valuation and qualifying accounts included in each balance sheet but not included in Schedule VI [@CL-CLS-0406] and if the information required by any schedule (including the notes thereto) may be shown in the related financial statement or in a note thereto without making such statement unclear or confusing, that procedure may be followed and the schedule omitted [@CL-CLS-0409].

## Company calibration

Which valuation and qualifying accounts and reserves the company carries, their opening and closing balances, how additions and deductions are described, and whether the information is shown in the financial statements or a note instead. [@Q-027]
