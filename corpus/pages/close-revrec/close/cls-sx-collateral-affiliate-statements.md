## Purpose

Explains when the financial statements of an affiliate whose securities collateralize a registered issue shall be filed, which affiliate crosses the collateral threshold, and which registered securities the section covers [@CL-CLS-0393; @CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0397]. The later disclosure section that can displace it is on [Affiliates whose securities collateralize registered securities](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-collateral-affiliate-disclosures.md).

The requirements of the section shall apply to each registered security issued and outstanding before January 4, 2021, unless the requirements of § 210.13-02 apply [@CL-CLS-0393]. For each of the registrant's affiliates whose securities constitute a substantial portion of the collateral for any class of securities registered or being registered, there shall be filed the financial statements that would be required if the affiliate were a registrant and required to file financial statements [@CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0395]. However, financial statements need not be filed pursuant to the section for any person whose statements are otherwise separately included in the filing on an individual basis or on a basis consolidated with its subsidiaries [@CL-CLS-0396].

Securities of a person shall be deemed to constitute a substantial portion of collateral if the aggregate principal amount, par value, or book value of the securities as carried by the registrant, or the market value of such securities, whichever is the greatest, equals 20 percent or more of the principal amount of the secured class of securities [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398].

## How it works

The requirements of the section apply to each registered security issued and outstanding before January 4, 2021, unless the requirements of § 210.13-02 apply [@CL-CLS-0393]. For each of the registrant's affiliates whose securities constitute a substantial portion of the collateral for any class of securities registered or being registered, there shall be filed the financial statements that would be required if the affiliate were a registrant and required to file financial statements [@CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0395], except that financial statements need not be filed pursuant to the section for any person whose statements are otherwise separately included in the filing on an individual basis or on a basis consolidated with its subsidiaries [@CL-CLS-0396]. Securities of a person shall be deemed to constitute a substantial portion of collateral if the aggregate principal amount, par value, or book value of the securities as carried by the registrant, or the market value of such securities, whichever is the greatest, equals 20 percent or more of the principal amount of the secured class of securities [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registered class was issued and outstanding before January 4, 2021, and § 210.13-02 does not apply. The secured class has a principal amount of 100 million.

Affiliate X, an affiliate of the registrant. The registrant carries X's securities at an aggregate principal amount of 20 million, par value of 15 million and book value of 10 million, and their market value is 30 million. The greatest is 30 million, which is 30 percent of the class and above the 20 percent threshold, so the securities are deemed a substantial portion of the collateral. X's statements, as they would be required if X were a registrant, shall be filed, unless X's statements are already separately included in the filing, individually or consolidated with its subsidiaries.

Affiliate Y, also an affiliate of the registrant. The greatest of the listed amounts for Y's securities is 15 million, which is 15 percent of the class and below the 20 percent threshold, so Y's securities are not deemed a substantial portion under this test.

## Never confuse

Securities of a person shall be deemed to constitute a substantial portion of collateral if the aggregate principal amount, par value, or book value of the securities as carried by the registrant, or the market value of such securities, whichever is the greatest, equals 20 percent or more of the principal amount of the secured class of securities [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398], and for each of the registrant's affiliates whose securities constitute a substantial portion of the collateral for any class of securities registered or being registered, there shall be filed the financial statements that would be required if the affiliate were a registrant and required to file financial statements [@CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0395]. However, financial statements need not be filed pursuant to the section for any person whose statements are otherwise separately included in the filing on an individual basis or on a basis consolidated with its subsidiaries [@CL-CLS-0396]. See [Affiliates whose securities collateralize registered securities](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-collateral-affiliate-disclosures.md).
