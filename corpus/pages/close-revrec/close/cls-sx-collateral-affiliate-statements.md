## Purpose

Explains when the financial statements of an affiliate whose securities collateralize a registered issue shall be filed, which affiliate crosses the collateral threshold, and which registered securities the section covers [@CL-CLS-0393; @CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0397]. The later disclosure section that can displace it is on [Affiliates whose securities collateralize registered securities](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-collateral-affiliate-disclosures.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Each registered security issued and outstanding before January 4, 2021, unless the requirements of § 210.13-02 apply, where securities of the registrant's affiliate are a substantial portion of the collateral [@CL-CLS-0393; @CL-CLS-0394] |

The requirements of the section shall apply to each registered security issued and outstanding before January 4, 2021, unless the requirements of § 210.13-02 apply [@CL-CLS-0393]. For each of the registrant's affiliates whose securities constitute a substantial portion of the collateral for any class of securities registered or being registered, there shall be filed the financial statements that would be required if the affiliate were a registrant and required to file financial statements [@CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0395]. However, financial statements need not be filed under the section for any person whose statements are otherwise separately included in the filing on an individual basis or on a basis consolidated with its subsidiaries [@CL-CLS-0396].

Securities of a person are deemed to constitute a substantial portion of collateral if the greatest of the listed amounts equals 20 percent or more of the principal amount of the secured class of securities [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398]. The listed amounts are the aggregate principal amount, par value or book value of the securities as carried by the registrant, or the market value of the securities, whichever is the greatest [@CL-CLS-0397]. The section does not name who files or who determines the values [@CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0397].

## How it works

First check whether the section applies to the registered security by its issue date, and whether § 210.13-02 applies instead [@CL-CLS-0393]. The test then runs affiliate by affiliate, and the statements that would be required if the affiliate were a registrant are the measure of what is filed [@CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0395]. For each affiliate, compare the greatest of the listed amounts for its securities with the principal amount of the secured class; the threshold is measured on that class, not on the affiliate [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398]. A market value above book value can carry an affiliate over the threshold [@CL-CLS-0397]. A person whose statements are already separately included in the filing is outside the requirement [@CL-CLS-0396].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registered class was issued and outstanding before January 4, 2021, and § 210.13-02 does not apply [@CL-CLS-0393]. The secured class has a principal amount of 100 million.

Affiliate X, an affiliate of the registrant. The registrant carries X's securities at an aggregate principal amount of 20 million, par value of 15 million and book value of 10 million, and their market value is 30 million. The greatest is 30 million, which is 30 percent of the class and above the 20 percent threshold, so the securities are deemed a substantial portion of the collateral [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398]. X's statements, as they would be required if X were a registrant, shall be filed [@CL-CLS-0394; @CL-CLS-0395], unless X's statements are already separately included in the filing, individually or consolidated with its subsidiaries [@CL-CLS-0396].

Affiliate Y, also an affiliate of the registrant. The greatest of the listed amounts for Y's securities is 15 million, which is 15 percent of the class and below the 20 percent threshold, so Y's securities are not deemed a substantial portion under this test [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398].

## Never confuse

A substantial portion of the collateral is a value test: it compares the greatest of the listed amounts for the securities with the principal amount of the secured class, and it does not look at control of the affiliate [@CL-CLS-0397; @CL-CLS-0398]. Nor does the section make the affiliate a registrant: the statements to be filed are those that would be required if it were one [@CL-CLS-0395]. The later disclosure section for collateralized securities is a different rule; see [Affiliates whose securities collateralize registered securities](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-collateral-affiliate-disclosures.md).
