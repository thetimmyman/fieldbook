## Purpose

Explains when financial statements covering nine to twelve months are deemed to satisfy a requirement for one year of financial statements, and what must be true for that treatment [@CL-CLS-0376; @CL-CLS-0381]. Paragraph (a) excludes registered investment companies [@CL-CLS-0376].

| Applies to | Requirements to file financial statements for one year, and for a time period of more than one but not more than three consecutive years; paragraph (a) excludes registered investment companies [@CL-CLS-0376; @CL-CLS-0380] |

Paragraph (a): except with respect to registered investment companies, filing financial statements covering a period of 9 to 12 months will be deemed to satisfy a requirement for filing financial statements for a period of 1 year where one of three conditions exists [@CL-CLS-0376]. The first is that the issuer has changed its fiscal year [@CL-CLS-0377]. The second is that the issuer has made a significant business acquisition for which financial statements are required under § 210.3-05, § 210.3-14, § 210.8-04, or § 210.8-06, and the financial statements covering the interim period pertain to the business being acquired [@CL-CLS-0378]. The third is that the Commission so permits pursuant to § 210.3-13 or § 210.8-01(e) [@CL-CLS-0379].

Paragraph (b) covers a requirement for financial statements for a time period exceeding one year but not exceeding three consecutive years, with not more than 12 months included in any period reported upon [@CL-CLS-0380]. There, a filing covering 9 to 12 months satisfies the requirement for one year of that time period only if the conditions in paragraph (a)(1), (2), or (3) exist [@CL-CLS-0381], and only if financial statements are filed that cover the full fiscal year or years for all other years in the time period [@CL-CLS-0382].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) is a deeming rule: where one of its conditions exists, a filing covering 9 to 12 months is treated as satisfying a requirement for one year [@CL-CLS-0376]. The first two conditions state facts about the issuer; the third turns on the Commission's permission under § 210.3-13 or § 210.8-01(e) [@CL-CLS-0377; @CL-CLS-0378; @CL-CLS-0379]. The Commission decides the third condition, and paragraph (a) names no decision-maker for the first two [@CL-CLS-0377; @CL-CLS-0378; @CL-CLS-0379]. The Commission's power to permit omitted or substituted statements is on [Inactive registrant statements and substituted financial statements](cls-sx-inactive-registrant-and-substitute-statements.md). Under paragraph (b), the deeming reaches only one year of the longer time period: a paragraph (a) condition must exist, and full-year statements must be filed for every other year [@CL-CLS-0381; @CL-CLS-0382].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Case A. An issuer that is not a registered investment company changes its fiscal year and files statements covering a nine-month period. A filing requirement calls for statements for one year. The nine-month statements are deemed to satisfy that requirement, because the issuer is not a registered investment company and has changed its fiscal year [@CL-CLS-0376; @CL-CLS-0377].

Case B. A filing requirement covers three consecutive years, with no more than 12 months in any period reported upon. The issuer is not a registered investment company, changed its fiscal year and files nine-month statements for one of the three years [@CL-CLS-0376; @CL-CLS-0377]. They satisfy the requirement for one year of that time period only if full-year statements are filed for the other two years [@CL-CLS-0380; @CL-CLS-0381; @CL-CLS-0382]. If full-year statements for the other two years are not filed, they do not satisfy it [@CL-CLS-0382].

## Never confuse

A period of 9 to 12 months is not deemed to satisfy a requirement for one year by its length alone [@CL-CLS-0376]; paragraph (a) ties the deeming to its listed conditions and excludes registered investment companies [@CL-CLS-0376]. Nor does it satisfy a longer requirement in full. It can satisfy one year of that time period, and full-year statements must be filed for the other years [@CL-CLS-0381; @CL-CLS-0382].
