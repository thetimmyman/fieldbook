## Purpose

Explains when the separate report of an independent accountant other than the principal accountant shall be filed, and when an annual report to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules need not present it [@CL-CLS-0373; @CL-CLS-0374]. Accountant independence is on [Auditor independence](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-auditor-independence.md). The content of an accountant's report is on [Accountants' reports and retention of audit records](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-accountants-reports-and-record-retention.md).

| Applies to | Filings where the principal accountant elects to rely on another independent accountant's work and refers to that in the principal accountant's report, and annual reports to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules [@CL-CLS-0373; @CL-CLS-0374] |

If part of the examination of the financial statements is made by an independent accountant other than the principal accountant, and the principal accountant elects to place reliance on the other accountant's work and makes reference to that effect in the principal accountant's report, the separate report of the other accountant shall be filed [@CL-CLS-0373].

Notwithstanding that rule, reports of other accountants that may otherwise be required in filings need not be presented in annual reports to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 [@CL-CLS-0374]. The relief is stated for those annual reports, not for other filings [@CL-CLS-0374]. The rules the section names are §§ 240.14a-3 and 240.14c-3 [@CL-CLS-0375].

## How it works

The principal accountant decides two things: whether to elect to place reliance on the other accountant's work, and whether to make reference to that effect in the principal accountant's report [@CL-CLS-0373]. The separate report shall be filed when the other accountant did part of the examination and both of those things happen [@CL-CLS-0373]. The section does not name who files the report [@CL-CLS-0373]. The relief then lets an annual report to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules leave out the other accountant's report, even where a filing may otherwise require it [@CL-CLS-0374].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An issuer splits its annual examination, and an independent accountant other than the principal accountant examines one division.

Case A. The principal accountant elects to rely on that work and says so in the principal accountant's report, so the other accountant's separate report shall be filed [@CL-CLS-0373]. The annual report to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules need not present that report [@CL-CLS-0374].

Case B. The principal accountant does not elect to rely on the other accountant's work. The condition for filing the separate report under this section is not met [@CL-CLS-0373].

## Never confuse

This section is not the independence rule; see [Auditor independence](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-auditor-independence.md). It decides whether the other accountant's separate report shall be filed once the principal accountant elects to rely on that work and makes reference to that effect [@CL-CLS-0373]. Nor does the relief remove the report from filings: it is stated for annual reports to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules [@CL-CLS-0374].
