## Purpose

Explains when the separate report of an independent accountant other than the principal accountant shall be filed, and when an annual report to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules need not present it [@CL-CLS-0373; @CL-CLS-0374]. Accountant independence is on [Auditor independence](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-auditor-independence.md). The content of an accountant's report is on [Accountants' reports and retention of audit records](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-accountants-reports-and-record-retention.md).

| Applies to | Filings where the principal accountant elects to rely on another independent accountant's work and refers to that in the principal accountant's report, and annual reports to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules [@CL-CLS-0373; @CL-CLS-0374] |

If, with respect to the examination of the financial statements, part of the examination is made by an independent accountant other than the principal accountant and the principal accountant elects to place reliance on the work of the other accountant and makes reference to that effect in the principal accountant's report, the separate report of the other accountant shall be filed [@CL-CLS-0373].

However, notwithstanding the provisions of the section, reports of other accountants that may otherwise be required in filings need not be presented in annual reports to security holders furnished pursuant to the proxy and information statement rules under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (17 CFR 240.14a-3 and 240.14c-3) [@CL-CLS-0374; @CL-CLS-0375].

Notwithstanding any requirements as to examination by independent accountants, the financial statements of any foreign governmental agency may be examined by the regular and customary auditing staff of the respective government if public financial statements of that governmental agency are customarily examined by that auditing staff [@CL-CLS-1238; @CL-CLS-1448]. If a registrant is required to file financial statements of any other person, those statements need not be examined if examination of those statements would not be required if that person were itself a registrant [@CL-CLS-1449].

## How it works

Where part of the examination is made by an independent accountant other than the principal accountant, and the principal accountant elects to place reliance on the work of the other accountant and makes reference to that effect in the principal accountant's report, the separate report of the other accountant shall be filed [@CL-CLS-0373]. Notwithstanding the provisions of the section, reports of other accountants that may otherwise be required in filings need not be presented in annual reports to security holders furnished pursuant to the proxy and information statement rules under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (17 CFR 240.14a-3 and 240.14c-3) [@CL-CLS-0374; @CL-CLS-0375].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An issuer splits its annual examination, and an independent accountant other than the principal accountant examines one division.

Case A. The principal accountant elects to rely on that work and says so in the principal accountant's report, so the other accountant's separate report shall be filed. The annual report to security holders furnished under the proxy and information statement rules need not present that report.

Case B. The principal accountant does not elect to rely on the other accountant's work. The condition for filing the separate report under this section is not met.

## Never confuse

If part of the examination is made by an independent accountant other than the principal accountant and the principal accountant elects to place reliance on the work of the other accountant and makes reference to that effect in the principal accountant's report, the separate report of the other accountant shall be filed [@CL-CLS-0373], whereas notwithstanding the provisions of the section, reports of other accountants that may otherwise be required in filings need not be presented in annual reports to security holders furnished pursuant to the proxy and information statement rules under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (17 CFR 240.14a-3 and 240.14c-3) [@CL-CLS-0374; @CL-CLS-0375]. See [Auditor independence](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-auditor-independence.md).
