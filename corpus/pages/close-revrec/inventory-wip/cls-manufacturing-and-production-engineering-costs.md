## Purpose

Explains the allowability of manufacturing and production engineering effort and distinguishes production engineering from research and capitalized assets.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0210] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-25 [@CL-CLS-0210] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-25 [@CL-CLS-1219; @CL-CLS-1220; @CL-CLS-1221; @CL-CLS-1222; @CL-CLS-1223; @CL-CLS-1224; @CL-CLS-1225] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0210] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the cost principles clause [@CL-CLS-0210] |

The costs of manufacturing and production engineering effort described in paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(4) of the cost principle are all allowable [@CL-CLS-0210].

The principle does not cover basic and applied research effort (as defined in FAR 31.205-18(a)) related to new technology, materials, systems, processes, methods, equipment, tools and techniques [@CL-CLS-0308]. Where manufacturing or production development costs are capitalized or required to be capitalized under the contractor's capitalization policies, allowable cost will be determined in accordance with the requirements of FAR 31.205-11, Depreciation [@CL-CLS-0212].

## How it works

The allowable effort includes developing and deploying new or improved materials, systems, processes, methods, equipment, tools and techniques that are or are expected to be used in producing products or services, and developing and deploying pilot production lines [@CL-CLS-0305]. It also includes improving current production functions, such as plant layout, production scheduling and control, methods and job analysis, equipment capabilities and capacities, inspection techniques, and tooling analysis (including tooling design and application improvements) [@CL-CLS-0306], and material and manufacturing producibility analysis for production suitability and to optimize manufacturing processes, methods, and techniques [@CL-CLS-0307]. Development effort for manufacturing or production materials, systems, processes, methods, equipment, tools and techniques that are intended for sale is also governed by FAR 31.205-18 [@CL-CLS-0211]. Where manufacturing or production development costs are capitalized or required to be capitalized under the contractor's capitalization policies, allowable cost will be determined in accordance with the requirements of FAR 31.205-11, Depreciation [@CL-CLS-0212].

DoD contracting officers must use the FAR part 31 text published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9350], under Revision 1 effective immediately and superseding Class Deviation 2026-O0032 issued on December 19, 2025 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.205-25(a) states the list of allowable manufacturing and production engineering effort [@CL-CLS-1219; @CL-CLS-1220; @CL-CLS-1221; @CL-CLS-1222], and RFO FAR 31.205-25(c) states that where manufacturing or production development costs are capitalized or required to be capitalized under the contractor's capitalization policies, allowable cost will be determined in accordance with the requirements of 31.205-11 [@CL-CLS-1225]. RFO FAR 31.205-25(b)(1) excludes basic and applied research effort related to new technology, materials, systems, processes, methods, equipment, tools and techniques (see 31.205-18) [@CL-CLS-1223], and RFO FAR 31.205-25(b)(2) excludes development effort for manufacturing or production materials, systems, processes, methods, equipment, tools and techniques that are intended for sale (see 31.205-18) [@CL-CLS-1224].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor incurs 80,000 to analyze manufacturing producibility and to improve the plant layout of an active production line. The contractor's policies do not require the cost to be capitalized, and the effort does not produce anything intended for sale. The answer: the effort is producibility analysis and an improvement to a current production function, so the 80,000 falls within the allowable effort.

## Never confuse

The manufacturing and production engineering cost principle does not cover basic and applied research effort as defined in FAR 31.205-18(a) [@CL-CLS-0308], and development effort for manufacturing or production materials, systems, processes, methods, equipment, tools and techniques that are intended for sale is also governed by FAR 31.205-18 [@CL-CLS-0211]. The independent research and development rules are described in [IR&D and B&P costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-ird-and-bp-costs.md), and depreciation of capitalized development cost in [Depreciation](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-depreciation.md).
