## Purpose

Shows which contracts the general termination clause subpart covers, and which standard forms the FAR names for settling terminated contracts, supporting a settlement proposal, applying for partial payments and executing the settlement agreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1348] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.501, 49.602, 49.602-2, 49.602-3, 49.602-4 and 49.602-5 [@CL-CLS-1348; @CL-CLS-1350; @CL-CLS-1352; @CL-CLS-1353; @CL-CLS-1354; @CL-CLS-1355] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1348] |
| Applies to | Termination clauses in contracts other than those that use the clause at 52.213-4; the standard forms for settling terminated prime contracts [@CL-CLS-1348; @CL-CLS-1350] |

The subpart prescribes the principal contract termination clauses [@CL-CLS-1348]. It does not apply to contracts that use the clause at 52.213-4, Terms and Conditions—Simplified Acquisitions (Other Than Commercial Products and Commercial Services) [@CL-CLS-1348]. In appropriate cases, agencies may authorize the use of special purpose clauses, if consistent with this chapter [@CL-CLS-1349]. The clause prescriptions are on [Termination for convenience clauses](cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md).

The standard forms listed in FAR 49.602 shall be used for settling terminated prime contracts [@CL-CLS-1350]. The forms at 49.602-1 and 49.602-2 may also be used for settling terminated subcontracts [@CL-CLS-1351]. A listing of the Standard forms is located in subpart 53.3 [@CL-CLS-1362].

## How it works

Each form's section states its use:

- Settlement proposal. Under FAR 49.602-1, Standard Form 1436, Settlement Proposal (Total Cost Basis), shall be used to submit settlement proposals resulting from the termination of fixed-price contracts if the proposals are computed on a total cost basis [@CL-CTF-3149], and Standard Form 1438, Settlement Proposal (Short Form), shall be used to submit settlement proposals resulting from the termination of fixed-price contracts if the total proposal is less than $10,000 [@CL-CTF-3151]. See [Settlement proposal forms, subcontract settlement requests and clause prescriptions](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-termination-settlement-proposal-forms-and-prescriptions.md). Settlement proposals must be on the forms prescribed in 49.602 unless the forms are inadequate for a particular contract [@CL-CLS-1004], as covered on [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md).
- Inventory. SF 1428, Inventory Disposal Schedule, and SF 1429, Inventory Disposal Schedule—Continuation Sheet, shall be used to support settlement proposals submitted on the forms specified in 49.602-1(b) and (d) [@CL-CLS-1352], which are SF 1436 and SF 1438 [@CL-CTF-3149; @CL-CTF-3151].
- Accounting information. SF 1439, Schedule of Accounting Information, shall be filed in support of a settlement proposal unless the proposal is filed on SF 1438, Settlement Proposal (Short Form) (see 49.206-1(e)) [@CL-CLS-1353].
- Partial payments. SF 1440, Application for Partial Payment, shall be used to apply for partial payments (see 49.112-1) [@CL-CLS-1354]. See [Partial payments and final payment under a terminated contract](cls-termination-partial-payments-and-final-payment.md).
- Settlement agreement. SF 30, Amendment of Solicitation/Modification of Contract, shall be used to execute a settlement agreement (see 49.109-1) [@CL-CLS-1355]. The formats of the agreements are on [Settlement agreement formats for terminated fixed-price contracts](cls-settlement-agreement-formats-fixed-price.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A terminated fixed-price contract leaves 80,000 of work in process and 12,000 of material on hand. The proposal is computed on a total cost basis, which is assumed to have been approved in advance by the termination contracting officer because the inventory basis was not practicable [@CL-CTF-3338], and the total proposal is well above 10,000, so the short form does not apply [@CL-CTF-3151]. It therefore goes on SF 1436 [@CL-CTF-3149]. The inventory is listed on SF 1428, with SF 1429 as the continuation sheet, because the proposal is on a form specified in 49.602-1(b) [@CL-CLS-1352]. Because the proposal is not on the short form, SF 1439 is also filed [@CL-CLS-1353]. A request for 40,000 of partial payment goes on SF 1440 [@CL-CLS-1354]. When the settlement is agreed, it is executed on SF 30 [@CL-CLS-1355].

A second terminated fixed-price contract has a total proposal of 8,000, which is less than 10,000, and the proposal is filed on SF 1438 [@CL-CTF-3151], the termination contracting officer not having instructed otherwise [@CL-CTF-3336]. It is supported by SF 1428 and SF 1429, because the short form is specified in 49.602-1(d) [@CL-CLS-1352]. SF 1439 is not required, because the proposal is on the short form [@CL-CLS-1353].

## Never confuse

The termination clauses are covered in [Termination for convenience clauses](cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md). For terminated prime contracts the listed standard forms shall be used [@CL-CLS-1350], and the forms at 49.602-1 and 49.602-2 may also be used for settling terminated subcontracts [@CL-CLS-1351]. The general termination clause subpart does not apply to contracts that use the clause at 52.213-4 [@CL-CLS-1348].
