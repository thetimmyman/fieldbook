## Purpose

Shows which contracts the general termination clause subpart covers, and which standard forms the FAR names for settling terminated contracts, supporting a settlement proposal, applying for partial payments and executing the settlement agreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1348] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.501, 49.602, 49.602-2, 49.602-3, 49.602-4 and 49.602-5 [@CL-CLS-1348; @CL-CLS-1350; @CL-CLS-1352; @CL-CLS-1353; @CL-CLS-1354; @CL-CLS-1355] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1348] |
| Applies to | Termination clauses in contracts other than those that use the clause at 52.213-4; the standard forms for settling terminated prime contracts [@CL-CLS-1348; @CL-CLS-1350] |

The subpart prescribes the principal contract termination clauses [@CL-CLS-1348]. It does not apply to contracts that use the clause at 52.213-4, Terms and Conditions—Simplified Acquisitions (Other Than Commercial Products and Commercial Services) [@CL-CLS-1348]. In appropriate cases, agencies may authorize special purpose clauses, if they are consistent with this chapter [@CL-CLS-1349]. This section does not itself say which clause goes in which contract; the clause prescriptions are on [Termination for convenience clauses](cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md).

The standard forms listed below in the forms section shall be used for settling terminated prime contracts [@CL-CLS-1350]. The forms at 49.602-1 and 49.602-2 may also be used for settling terminated subcontracts [@CL-CLS-1351]. The sentence names only those two sections. A listing of the Standard forms is located in subpart 53.3 [@CL-CLS-1362].

## How it works

The form for each step is set by its own section. The sections do not say here who files each form, so this card does not assign the duty to either side.

- Settlement proposal. The settlement proposal forms are in 49.602-1, which this card does not cover [@CL-CTF-3149; @CL-CTF-3151]. Two of them matter below: SF 1436 for a fixed-price proposal computed on a total cost basis [@CL-CTF-3149], and SF 1438, the short form, for a fixed-price proposal whose total is less than $10,000 [@CL-CTF-3151]. They are covered on [Settlement proposal forms, subcontract settlement requests and clause prescriptions](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-termination-settlement-proposal-forms-and-clause-prescriptions.md). Settlement proposals must be on the forms prescribed in 49.602 unless the forms are inadequate for a particular contract [@CL-CLS-1004]; see [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md).
- Inventory. SF 1428, Inventory Disposal Schedule, and SF 1429, its continuation sheet, shall be used to support settlement proposals submitted on the forms specified in 49.602-1(b) and (d) [@CL-CLS-1352]. Those paragraphs are the total cost basis form and the short form [@CL-CTF-3149; @CL-CTF-3151].
- Accounting information. SF 1439, Schedule of Accounting Information, shall be filed in support of a settlement proposal unless the proposal is filed on SF 1438, the short form [@CL-CLS-1353].
- Partial payments. SF 1440, Application for Partial Payment, shall be used to apply for partial payments [@CL-CLS-1354]. See [Partial payments and final payment under a terminated contract](cls-termination-partial-payments-and-final-payment.md).
- Settlement agreement. SF 30, Amendment of Solicitation/Modification of Contract, shall be used to execute a settlement agreement [@CL-CLS-1355]. The formats of the agreements are on [Settlement agreement formats for terminated fixed-price contracts](cls-settlement-agreement-formats-fixed-price.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A terminated fixed-price contract leaves 80,000 of work in process and 12,000 of material on hand. The proposal is computed on a total cost basis, which is assumed to have been approved in advance by the termination contracting officer because the inventory basis was not practicable [@CL-CTF-3338], and the total proposal is well above 10,000, so the short form does not apply [@CL-CTF-3151]. It therefore goes on SF 1436 [@CL-CTF-3149]. The inventory is listed on SF 1428, with SF 1429 as the continuation sheet, because the proposal is on a form specified in 49.602-1(b) [@CL-CLS-1352]. Because the proposal is not on the short form, SF 1439 is also filed [@CL-CLS-1353]. A request for 40,000 of partial payment goes on SF 1440 [@CL-CLS-1354]. When the settlement is agreed, it is executed on SF 30 [@CL-CLS-1355].

A second terminated fixed-price contract has a total proposal of 8,000, which is less than 10,000, and the proposal is filed on SF 1438 [@CL-CTF-3151], the termination contracting officer not having instructed otherwise [@CL-CTF-3336]. It is supported by SF 1428 and SF 1429, because the short form is specified in 49.602-1(d) [@CL-CLS-1352]. SF 1439 is not required, because the proposal is on the short form [@CL-CLS-1353].

## Never confuse

These forms are the paperwork for proposing, supporting and closing a settlement; they do not create the entitlement to settle. The clauses that entitle the contractor to settle are covered in [Termination for convenience clauses](cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md). For terminated prime contracts the listed forms shall be used [@CL-CLS-1350]. For terminated subcontracts, the text says only that the forms at 49.602-1 and 49.602-2 may also be used [@CL-CLS-1351]. The general termination clause subpart does not apply to a contract that uses the simplified acquisitions clause at 52.213-4 [@CL-CLS-1348].
