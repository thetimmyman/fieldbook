## Purpose

Fixes the meaning of the terms that the section on definitions gives, so that a settlement proposal, a settlement agreement and the inventory record use the same words.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0250] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.001 [@CL-CLS-0250] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0250] |
| Applies to | The terms the section defines, as used in part 49; the plant clearance period is defined as used in the subpart [@CL-CLS-0250; @CL-CLS-0252] |

The section says that the terms it defines have the stated meanings as used in part 49, and it words the plant clearance period definition for use in the subpart [@CL-CLS-0250; @CL-CLS-0252; @CL-CLS-1605]. It defines six terms: other work, plant clearance period, the final phase of the plant clearance period, settlement agreement, settlement proposal and unsettled contract change [@CL-CLS-0251; @CL-CLS-0252; @CL-CLS-0253; @CL-CLS-0254; @CL-CLS-0255; @CL-CLS-0257].

The stored text of the section holds no definition of partial termination or termination inventory. Their meaning is not established here.

### Terms

| Term | Full definition |
|---|---|
| Other work | Any current or scheduled work of the contractor, whether Government or commercial, other than work related to the terminated contract [@CL-CLS-0251] |
| Plant clearance period | As used in the subpart, the period beginning on the effective date of contract completion or termination and ending 90 days (or such longer period as may be agreed to) after receipt by the contracting officer of acceptable inventory schedules for each property classification [@CL-CLS-1605; @CL-CLS-0252] |
| Final phase of the plant clearance period | That period after receipt of acceptable inventory schedules [@CL-CLS-0253] |
| Settlement agreement | A written agreement in the form of a contract modification settling all or a severable portion of a settlement proposal [@CL-CLS-0254] |
| Settlement proposal | A proposal for effecting settlement of a contract terminated in whole or in part, submitted by a contractor or subcontractor in the form, and supported by the data, required by part 49 [@CL-CLS-0255] |
| Unsettled contract change | Any contract change or contract term for which a definitive modification is required but has not been executed [@CL-CLS-0257] |

A settlement proposal is included within the generic meaning of the word claim under false claims acts; the paragraph refers to section 287 of title 18 and section 3729 of title 31 of the United States Code [@CL-CLS-0256].

## How it works

The section is only a set of definitions. It imposes no deadline on any party, sets no dollar amount and assigns no decision to any officer. The one period it sets is the plant clearance period, which refers to the contracting officer's receipt of acceptable inventory schedules [@CL-CLS-0252].

A contractor or subcontractor submits a settlement proposal to settle a contract terminated in whole or in part [@CL-CLS-0255]. A settlement agreement is the written contract modification that settles the proposal [@CL-CLS-0254]. An agreement may settle all of the proposal or only a severable portion of it [@CL-CLS-0254].

The plant clearance period starts on the effective date of contract completion or termination [@CL-CLS-0252]. It ends 90 days after the contracting officer receives acceptable inventory schedules for each property classification, or after a longer period if one is agreed to [@CL-CLS-0252]. The text does not say who agrees to a longer period. The definition is worded for use in the subpart, while the other definitions apply as used in part 49 [@CL-CLS-1605; @CL-CLS-0252; @CL-CLS-0250].

The final phase of the plant clearance period is the period after receipt of acceptable inventory schedules [@CL-CLS-0253].

A change is an unsettled contract change when a definitive modification is required and has not been executed [@CL-CLS-0257].

In the FAR's general definitions, contract modification means any written change in the terms of a contract, and the definition points to 43.103 [@CL-CLS-1499]. The RFO model text of FAR 2.101 defines a contract modification in the same words as the codified definition [@CL-CLS-1530; @CL-CLS-1499].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract is terminated in part, with the termination effective on Day 0. The contractor also holds a commercial contract that is scheduled work and is unrelated to the terminated contract. That commercial work is other work [@CL-CLS-0251]. The contractor submits a proposal to settle the terminated part [@CL-CLS-0255]. A contract modification that settles one severable portion of that proposal is a settlement agreement [@CL-CLS-0254]. A change order is issued but the definitive modification it needs is not yet executed, so it is an unsettled contract change [@CL-CLS-0257].

The contracting officer receives acceptable inventory schedules for each property classification on Day 40, and no longer period is agreed. The plant clearance period then runs from Day 0 to Day 130, which is 40 plus 90 days [@CL-CLS-0252]. The final phase of the period is the time after the Day 40 receipt [@CL-CLS-0253].

## Never confuse

Other work is not limited to Government work. It covers any current or scheduled work of the contractor, Government or commercial, apart from work related to the terminated contract [@CL-CLS-0251].

A settlement proposal is not a settlement agreement. The proposal is what the contractor or subcontractor submits. The agreement is the written contract modification that settles all or a severable portion of it [@CL-CLS-0255; @CL-CLS-0254].

The final phase is not the whole plant clearance period. It is the period after receipt of acceptable inventory schedules [@CL-CLS-0253; @CL-CLS-0252].

The cost treatment of the terminated work is a separate question; see [Termination costs](../inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md). The submission of a proposal is on [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md).
