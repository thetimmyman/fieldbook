## Purpose

Fixes the meaning of the terms that the section on definitions gives, so that a settlement proposal, a settlement agreement and the inventory record use the same words.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0250]; RFO model text [@CL-CLS-1530] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.001 [@CL-CLS-0250]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CLS-1499] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-CLS-1530] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0250] |
| Applies to | The terms the section defines, as used in part 49; the plant clearance period is defined as used in the subpart [@CL-CLS-0250; @CL-CLS-1605; @CL-CLS-0252] |

Under FAR 49.001, the terms defined in the section apply as used in part 49, and the plant clearance period is defined as used in the subpart [@CL-CLS-0250; @CL-CLS-0252; @CL-CLS-1605]. It defines six terms: other work, plant clearance period, the final phase of the plant clearance period, settlement agreement, settlement proposal and unsettled contract change [@CL-CLS-0251; @CL-CLS-0252; @CL-CLS-0253; @CL-CLS-0254; @CL-CLS-0255; @CL-CLS-0257].

### Terms

| Term | Full definition |
|---|---|
| Other work | Any current or scheduled work of the contractor, whether Government or commercial, other than work related to the terminated contract [@CL-CLS-0251] |
| Plant clearance period | As used in the subpart, the period beginning on the effective date of contract completion or termination and ending 90 days (or such longer period as may be agreed to) after receipt by the contracting officer of acceptable inventory schedules for each property classification [@CL-CLS-1605; @CL-CLS-0252] |
| Final phase of the plant clearance period | That period after receipt of acceptable inventory schedules [@CL-CLS-0253] |
| Settlement agreement | A written agreement in the form of a contract modification settling all or a severable portion of a settlement proposal [@CL-CLS-0254] |
| Settlement proposal | A proposal for effecting settlement of a contract terminated in whole or in part, submitted by a contractor or subcontractor in the form, and supported by the data, required by part 49 [@CL-CLS-0255] |
| Unsettled contract change | Any contract change or contract term for which a definitive modification is required but has not been executed [@CL-CLS-0257] |

A settlement proposal is included within the generic meaning of the word claim under false claims acts (see title 18 of the United States Code, section 287, and title 31 of the United States Code, section 3729) [@CL-CLS-0256].

## How it works

A contractor or subcontractor submits a settlement proposal, in the form and supported by the data required by part 49, for effecting settlement of a contract terminated in whole or in part [@CL-CLS-0255]. A settlement agreement is a written agreement in the form of a contract modification settling all or a severable portion of a settlement proposal [@CL-CLS-0254].

The plant clearance period, as used in the subpart, begins on the effective date of contract completion or termination and ends 90 days (or such longer period as may be agreed to) after receipt by the contracting officer of acceptable inventory schedules for each property classification [@CL-CLS-1605; @CL-CLS-0252]. The other definitions in the section apply as used in part 49 [@CL-CLS-0250].

The final phase of the plant clearance period is that period after receipt of acceptable inventory schedules [@CL-CLS-0253].

An unsettled contract change is any contract change or contract term for which a definitive modification is required but has not been executed [@CL-CLS-0257].

In FAR 2.101, contract modification means any written change in the terms of a contract (see 43.103) [@CL-CLS-1499]. In RFO FAR 2.101, contract modification means any written change in the terms of a contract (see 43.103) [@CL-CLS-1530].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract is terminated in part, with the termination effective on Day 0. The contractor also holds a commercial contract that is scheduled work and is unrelated to the terminated contract. That commercial work is other work [@CL-CLS-0251]. The contractor submits a proposal to settle the terminated part [@CL-CLS-0255]. A contract modification that settles one severable portion of that proposal is a settlement agreement [@CL-CLS-0254]. A change order is issued but the definitive modification it needs is not yet executed, so it is an unsettled contract change [@CL-CLS-0257].

The contracting officer receives acceptable inventory schedules for each property classification on Day 40, and no longer period is agreed. The plant clearance period then runs from Day 0 to Day 130, which is 40 plus 90 days [@CL-CLS-0252]. The final phase of the period is the time after the Day 40 receipt [@CL-CLS-0253].

## Never confuse

Other work covers any current or scheduled work of the contractor, whether Government or commercial, other than work related to the terminated contract [@CL-CLS-0251].

A settlement proposal and a settlement agreement are not the same document: a settlement proposal is a proposal submitted by a contractor or subcontractor for effecting settlement of a contract terminated in whole or in part, whereas a settlement agreement is a written agreement in the form of a contract modification settling all or a severable portion of a settlement proposal [@CL-CLS-0255; @CL-CLS-0254].

The final phase of the plant clearance period is the period after receipt of acceptable inventory schedules, whereas the plant clearance period begins on the effective date of contract completion or termination [@CL-CLS-0253; @CL-CLS-0252].

The cost treatment of the terminated work is covered on [Termination costs](../inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md), and the submission of a proposal is on [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md).
