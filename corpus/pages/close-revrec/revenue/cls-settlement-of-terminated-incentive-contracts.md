## Purpose

Prescribes how terminated fixed-price incentive and cost-plus-incentive-fee contracts are settled under partial or complete termination.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0532] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.115 [@CL-CLS-0532] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0532] |
| Applies to | Terminated fixed-price incentive contracts and terminated cost-plus-incentive-fee contracts [@CL-CLS-0532; @CL-CLS-0661] |

Under a partially terminated fixed-price incentive contract, the termination contracting officer shall negotiate a settlement as provided in the termination clause of the contract, and paragraph (j) of the clause at 52.216-16, Incentive Price Revision—Firm Target, or paragraph (1) of the clause at 52.216-17, Incentive Price Revision—Successive Targets [@CL-CLS-1601; @CL-CLS-1607]. The termination contracting officer shall reimburse the contractor at target price for completed articles included in the settlement proposal for which a final price has not been established, and shall incorporate in the settlement agreement an appropriate reservation as to final price for these completed articles [@CL-CLS-0532].

Under a partial termination of a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract, the termination contracting officer shall limit the settlement to an adjustment of target fee as provided in paragraph (e) of the clause at 52.216-10, Incentive Fee [@CL-CLS-0535].

## How it works

On the complete termination of a fixed-price incentive contract, if any items were delivered and accepted by the Government, the contracting officer shall establish prices under the incentive provisions of the contract [@CL-CLS-0659]. On the terminated portion of the contract, the provisions of the termination clause shall govern and the provisions of the incentive clause shall not apply [@CL-CLS-0533]. The termination contracting officer responsible for the termination settlement will ensure, on the basis of evidence considered proper (including coordination with the contracting officer), that no portion of the costs considered in the negotiations under the incentive provisions are included in the termination settlement [@CL-CLS-0534].

For a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract, the termination contracting officer shall settle the terminated contract under the clause at 52.249-6, Termination (Cost-Reimbursement) [@CL-CLS-0661]. Under a partial termination, the settlement agreement shall include a reservation regarding any adjustment of target cost resulting from the partial termination, and the contracting officer shall adjust the target cost, if required [@CL-CLS-0536]. Under a complete termination, the parties shall negotiate the settlement under the provisions of subpart 49.3 and the clause at 52.249-6, Termination (Cost-Reimbursement) [@CL-CLS-1602]; the fee shall be adjusted on the basis of the target fee, and the incentive provisions shall not be applied or considered [@CL-CLS-0537].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-incentive-fee contract has a target cost of 2,000,000 and a target fee of 160,000. It is completely terminated for convenience before completion. The parties negotiate the settlement under the cost-reimbursement termination rules. The answer: the fee is adjusted on the basis of the 160,000 target fee, and no incentive sharing calculation on actual cost is applied or considered.

## Never confuse

On a complete termination of a fixed-price incentive contract, if any items were delivered and accepted by the Government, the contracting officer shall establish prices under the incentive provisions of the contract [@CL-CLS-0659]; on the terminated portion, the provisions of the termination clause shall govern and the provisions of the incentive clause shall not apply [@CL-CLS-0533]. On a complete termination of a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract, the fee shall be adjusted on the basis of the target fee, and the incentive provisions shall not be applied or considered [@CL-CLS-0537].
