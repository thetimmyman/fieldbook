## Purpose

Shows what the TCO must do when the TCO suspects fraud or other criminal conduct related to the settlement of a terminated contract, and what the section says about agency review of proposed termination settlements.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1266] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.106 [@CL-CLS-1266]; FAR 49.111 [@CL-CLS-1267; @CL-CLS-1268] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1266] |
| Applies to | Fraud rule: the TCO, for the settlement of a terminated contract [@CL-CLS-1266]. Review rule: each agency, and an agency that provides termination settlement services for another agency [@CL-CLS-1267; @CL-CLS-1268] |

If the TCO suspects fraud or other criminal conduct related to the settlement of a terminated contract, the TCO shall discontinue negotiations and report the facts under agency procedures [@CL-CLS-1266].

Each agency shall establish procedures, when necessary, for the administrative review of proposed termination settlements [@CL-CLS-1267]. When one agency provides termination settlement services for another agency, the agency providing the services shall also perform the settlement review function [@CL-CLS-1268].

During negotiation of a proposal of 300,000, the TCO sees support for a claimed cost that looks altered and suspects fraud. The TCO shall discontinue negotiations and report the facts under agency procedures [@CL-CLS-1266].

Separately, Agency A provides termination settlement services for Agency B. Agency A also performs the settlement review function for the proposed settlements it handles for Agency B [@CL-CLS-1268].
