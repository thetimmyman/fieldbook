## Purpose

Shows the file memorandum, the reporting, the completion route and the liquidation of liability that the FAR subpart on termination for default provides for after a default termination. The memorandum also applies when a procedure authorized by 49.402-4 is followed [@CL-CLS-1322].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1322] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.402-5, 49.402-8, 49.405 and 49.406 [@CL-CLS-1322; @CL-CLS-1323; @CL-CLS-1324; @CL-CLS-1327] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1322] |
| Applies to | Contracts terminated for default, and contracts where a procedure authorized by 49.402-4 is followed [@CL-CLS-1322]; reporting after a default notice [@CL-CLS-1323]; completion where the surety does not arrange it [@CL-CLS-1324]; liability of the contractor and the surety [@CL-CLS-1327] |

When a contract is terminated for default, or a procedure authorized by 49.402-4 is followed, the contracting officer shall prepare a memorandum for the contract file explaining the reasons for the action taken [@CL-CLS-1322]. The memorandum section and the reporting section sit in FAR 49.402, which is headed termination of fixed-price contracts for default [@CL-CLS-1335]. The procedures authorized by 49.402-4 [@CL-CLS-1322] are described in [Courses of action in lieu of termination for default](cls-default-termination-courses-of-action-in-lieu.md).

The contracting officer, in accordance with agency procedures, shall ensure that information relating to the termination for default notice and a subsequent withdrawal or a conversion to a termination for convenience is reported in accordance with 42.1503(h) [@CL-CLS-1323].

If the surety does not arrange for completion of the contract, the contracting officer normally will arrange for completion of the work by awarding a new contract based on the same plans and specifications [@CL-CLS-1324]. The new contract may be the result of sealed bidding or any other appropriate contracting method or procedure [@CL-CLS-1325]. The contracting officer shall exercise reasonable diligence to obtain the lowest price available for completion [@CL-CLS-1326].

The contract provides that the contractor and the surety are liable to the Government for resultant damages [@CL-CLS-1327]. The contracting officer shall use all retained percentages of progress payments previously made to the contractor and any progress payments due for work completed before the termination to liquidate the contractor's and the surety's liability to the Government [@CL-CLS-1328]. If the retained and unpaid amounts are insufficient, the contracting officer shall take steps to recover the additional sum from the contractor and the surety [@CL-CLS-1329].

## How it works

| Step | Who | Trigger or condition |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare a memorandum for the contract file explaining the reasons | The contracting officer | A default termination, or a procedure authorized by 49.402-4 [@CL-CLS-1322] |
| Ensure the notice, withdrawal or conversion is reported | The contracting officer | In accordance with agency procedures and 42.1503(h) [@CL-CLS-1323] |
| Arrange completion by a new contract on the same plans and specifications | The contracting officer, who normally will | The surety does not arrange for completion [@CL-CLS-1324] |
| Choose the contracting method | The contracting officer, who normally will arrange completion by awarding a new contract | Sealed bidding or any other appropriate method or procedure [@CL-CLS-1324; @CL-CLS-1325] |
| Obtain the lowest price available for completion | The contracting officer | Reasonable diligence [@CL-CLS-1326] |
| Bear liability for resultant damages | The contractor and the surety | The contract so provides [@CL-CLS-1327] |
| Apply retained percentages and progress payments due to that liability | The contracting officer | Retained percentages of progress payments previously made, and progress payments due for work completed before the termination [@CL-CLS-1328] |
| Recover any shortfall | The contracting officer | The retained and unpaid amounts are insufficient; recovery is from the contractor and the surety [@CL-CLS-1329] |

The duties in these sections fall on the contracting officer, and the liability falls on the contractor and the surety [@CL-CLS-1322; @CL-CLS-1323; @CL-CLS-1324; @CL-CLS-1326; @CL-CLS-1327; @CL-CLS-1328; @CL-CLS-1329]. The contracting officer shall use retained percentages and progress payments due to liquidate the liability, and if the retained and unpaid amounts are insufficient, shall take steps to recover the additional sum from the contractor and the surety [@CL-CLS-1328; @CL-CLS-1329].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price contract is terminated for default [@CL-CLS-1335], so the contracting officer prepares a memorandum for the contract file explaining the reasons for the action taken [@CL-CLS-1322]. The contracting officer also ensures, in accordance with agency procedures, that information about the default notice is reported in accordance with 42.1503(h) [@CL-CLS-1323].

The surety does not arrange for completion. The contracting officer normally will then arrange completion by awarding a new contract based on the same plans and specifications [@CL-CLS-1324]. The contracting officer chooses sealed bidding and exercises reasonable diligence to obtain the lowest price available [@CL-CLS-1325; @CL-CLS-1326]. Had the surety arranged completion, the condition of that sentence would not be met [@CL-CLS-1324].

The resultant damages come to 120,000. The contractor has 60,000 of retained percentages of progress payments, and 25,000 of progress payments is due for work completed before the termination. The contracting officer applies the 85,000 to liquidate the liability [@CL-CLS-1328]. The amounts are insufficient by 35,000, so the contracting officer takes steps to recover that 35,000 from the contractor and the surety [@CL-CLS-1329; @CL-CLS-1327].

## Never confuse

The memorandum and the report are not the same document: the memorandum explains the reasons for the action taken and goes in the contract file [@CL-CLS-1322], while the report concerns information relating to the termination for default notice and a later withdrawal or conversion to a termination for convenience [@CL-CLS-1323].

If the surety does not arrange for completion of the contract, the contracting officer normally will arrange for completion of the work [@CL-CLS-1324]. If the retained and unpaid amounts are insufficient, the contracting officer shall take steps to recover the additional sum from the contractor and the surety [@CL-CLS-1329]. The effect of a default termination and the repurchase itself are covered in [Termination for default effect and repurchase](cls-termination-for-default-effect-and-repurchase.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the file memorandum, the reporting entry and the liquidation calculation are complete before the contract file is closed. [@Q-006]
