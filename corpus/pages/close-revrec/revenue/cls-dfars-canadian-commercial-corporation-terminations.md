## Purpose

Explains how DoD terminates contracts placed with the Canadian Commercial Corporation, what each party must do, and who settles Canadian and United States subcontract claims.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Codified citation | DFARS 249.7000 [@CL-CLS-0275] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Applies to | Contracts with the Canadian Commercial Corporation [@CL-CLS-0275] |

### Authorities

Contracts with the Canadian Commercial Corporation are to be terminated in accordance with the Letter of Agreement between the Department of Defence Production (Canada) and the United States Department of Defense, called the Canadian Agreement, and with the other authorities the paragraph lists [@CL-CLS-0275]. The other authorities are the policies in the Canadian Agreement and part 249, and the Canadian Supply Manual, Chapter 8, Annex 8.3 [@CL-CLS-0276].

### Proposals and agreements

Contracting officers must ensure that the Canadian Commercial Corporation submits termination settlement proposals in the format prescribed in FAR 49.602 and that the proposals contain the amount of settlements with subcontractors [@CL-CLS-0277]. The termination contracting officer (TCO) must prepare an appropriate settlement agreement [@CL-CLS-0278].

The letter that transmits a settlement proposal must certify two matters [@CL-CLS-0279]. One is that disposition of inventory has been completed. The other is that the Contract Claims Resolution Board of the Public Works and Government Services Canada has approved settlements with Canadian subcontractors, when the Procedures Manual on Termination of Contracts requires such approval [@CL-CLS-0280].

### Inventory and Canadian subcontract claims

The Canadian Commercial Corporation will settle all Canadian subcontractor termination claims under the Canadian Agreement [@CL-CLS-0281]. It will also submit schedules listing serviceable and usable contractor inventory for screening to the TCO [@CL-CLS-0282]. After screening, the TCO must provide guidance to the Canadian Commercial Corporation for disposition of the contractor inventory [@CL-CLS-0283].

Settlement of Canadian subcontractor claims is not subject to the approval and ratification of the TCO [@CL-CLS-0284]. However, when the proposed negotiated settlement exceeds the total contract price of the prime contract, the TCO must obtain from the United States contracting officer, before final settlement, ratification of the proposed settlement and a contract modification increasing the contract price and obligating the additional funds [@CL-CLS-0285; @CL-CLS-0286].

### United States subcontractors, administration and services

The Canadian Commercial Corporation should send all termination settlement proposals submitted by United States subcontractors and suppliers to the TCO of the cognizant contract administration office of the Defense Contract Management Agency for settlement [@CL-CLS-0287]. That TCO will inform the Canadian Commercial Corporation of the amount of the net settlement of those subcontractors and suppliers, so that the amount can be included in the Canadian Commercial Corporation termination proposal [@CL-CLS-0288]. The Canadian Commercial Corporation is responsible for execution of the settlement agreement with them [@CL-CLS-0289].

The Canadian Commercial Corporation will continue administering contracts that the United States contracting officer terminates [@CL-CLS-0290]. It will settle all Canadian subcontracts in accordance with the policies, practices, and procedures of the Canadian Government [@CL-CLS-0291].

The United States agency administering the contract with the Canadian Commercial Corporation must provide any services the Canadian Commercial Corporation requires, including disposal of inventory, for settlement of any subcontracts placed in the United States [@CL-CLS-0292]. Settlement of such United States subcontracts will be in accordance with this regulation [@CL-CLS-0293].

## How it works

| Party | Duty |
|---|---|
| Contracting officers | Ensure that the Canadian Commercial Corporation submits proposals in the prescribed format with the amount of settlements with subcontractors [@CL-CLS-0277] |
| Termination contracting officer | Prepare an appropriate settlement agreement [@CL-CLS-0278]; give guidance on inventory disposition after screening [@CL-CLS-0283]; obtain ratification and a modification when the proposed negotiated settlement exceeds the total contract price of the prime contract [@CL-CLS-0285; @CL-CLS-0286] |
| Termination contracting officer of the Defense Contract Management Agency office | Receive, for settlement, the United States subcontractor proposals that the corporation should send, and inform the corporation of the net amount [@CL-CLS-0287; @CL-CLS-0288] |
| Canadian Commercial Corporation | Settle Canadian subcontractor claims and subcontracts [@CL-CLS-0281; @CL-CLS-0291]; submit inventory schedules for screening [@CL-CLS-0282]; execute settlement agreements with United States subcontractors [@CL-CLS-0289]; continue administering the terminated contract [@CL-CLS-0290] |
| United States contracting officer | Give the ratification and the modification that the termination contracting officer obtains when the price test is met [@CL-CLS-0285; @CL-CLS-0286] |
| United States agency administering the contract | Provide the services the corporation requires, including disposal of inventory, for settlement of subcontracts placed in the United States [@CL-CLS-0292] |

The Canadian Commercial Corporation settles Canadian subcontractor claims and keeps administering contracts that the United States contracting officer terminates [@CL-CLS-0281; @CL-CLS-0290].

The amount test compares the proposed negotiated settlement with the total contract price of the prime contract [@CL-CLS-0285]. Settlement of Canadian subcontractor claims is not subject to the approval and ratification of the TCO [@CL-CLS-0284].

The Canadian Commercial Corporation will settle all Canadian subcontracts in accordance with the policies, practices, and procedures of the Canadian Government [@CL-CLS-0291]. Settlement of such United States subcontracts will be in accordance with this regulation [@CL-CLS-0293].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contract with the Canadian Commercial Corporation has a total contract price of 5,000,000. The proposed negotiated settlement is 5,400,000, which exceeds the total contract price. Before final settlement, the TCO must obtain from the United States contracting officer ratification of the proposed settlement and a contract modification increasing the contract price and obligating the additional funds [@CL-CLS-0285; @CL-CLS-0286].

On a second contract with a total contract price of 5,000,000, the proposed negotiated settlement is 4,500,000. This paragraph does not require ratification or a modification, and the settlement of Canadian subcontractor claims is not subject to the TCO's approval and ratification [@CL-CLS-0284; @CL-CLS-0285].

## Never confuse

Settlement of Canadian subcontractor claims is not subject to the approval and ratification of the TCO [@CL-CLS-0284]. However, when the proposed negotiated settlement exceeds the total contract price of the prime contract, the TCO shall obtain from the United States contracting officer, prior to final settlement, ratification of the proposed settlement and a contract modification increasing the contract price and obligating the additional funds [@CL-CLS-0285; @CL-CLS-0286].

The Canadian Commercial Corporation should send United States subcontractor proposals to the TCO of the Defense Contract Management Agency office for settlement, and the corporation is responsible for executing the settlement agreement [@CL-CLS-0287; @CL-CLS-0289].

Settlement of subcontracts placed in the United States will be in accordance with this regulation [@CL-CLS-0293], and the Canadian Commercial Corporation will settle all Canadian subcontracts in accordance with the policies, practices, and procedures of the Canadian Government [@CL-CLS-0291].

The authorization rules for settling subcontracts in the ordinary case are separate; see [Subcontract settlements, authorization and awards](cls-subcontract-settlements-authorization-and-awards.md).
