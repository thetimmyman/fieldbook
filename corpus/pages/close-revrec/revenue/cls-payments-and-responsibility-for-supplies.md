## Purpose

Explains what the Payments clause pays for, namely supplies delivered and accepted and services rendered and accepted, and how title and risk of loss pass between the parties under the Responsibility for Supplies clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0200] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-1 [@CL-CLS-0200] and FAR 46.316 [@CL-CLS-1232; @CL-CLS-1233; @CL-CLS-1234] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-1 [@CL-CLS-0140]; the title and risk-of-loss paragraphs described on this page are in RFO FAR 52.246-16 [@CL-CLS-0142] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.232-1: Apr 1984 [@CL-CLS-0140]; FAR 52.246-16: Apr 1984 [@CL-CLS-0142] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0200] |
| Applies to | Fixed-price supply contracts, fixed-price service contracts and contracts for nonregulated communication services [@CL-CLS-0300] |

Under the Payments clause, the Government pays the contractor the stipulated contract prices for delivered and accepted supplies or rendered and accepted services upon submission of proper invoices or vouchers, less any deductions provided in the contract [@CL-CLS-0200]. Unless otherwise specified in the contract, payment shall be made on partial deliveries accepted by the Government if the amount due on the deliveries warrants it [@CL-CLS-1559], or if the Contractor requests it and the amount due on the deliveries is at least $1,000 or 50 percent of the total contract price [@CL-CLS-1560].

Title to supplies furnished under the contract passes to the Government upon formal acceptance, regardless of when or where the Government takes physical possession, unless the contract specifically provides for earlier passage of title [@CL-CLS-0201].

## How it works

The clause pays for supplies delivered and accepted or services rendered and accepted, on submission of proper invoices or vouchers [@CL-CLS-0200], and, unless the contract specifies otherwise, also on accepted partial deliveries on either of two alternative grounds the clause sets: the amount due on the deliveries warrants it, or the Contractor requests it and the amount due is at least $1,000 or 50 percent of the total contract price [@CL-CLS-1559; @CL-CLS-1560]. The clause is to be inserted, appropriately modified with respect to payment due date in accordance with agency regulations, in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price supply contract, a fixed-price service contract, or a contract for nonregulated communication services is contemplated [@CL-CLS-0300]. Payment timing under the Prompt Payment clause is covered in [Prompt payment](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md).

Unless the contract specifically provides otherwise, risk of loss of or damage to supplies shall remain with the Contractor until, and shall pass to the Government upon delivery of the supplies to a carrier, if transportation is f.o.b. origin [@CL-CLS-0301]; or upon acceptance by the Government or delivery of the supplies to the Government at the destination specified in the contract, whichever is later, if transportation is f.o.b. destination [@CL-CLS-0302]. Paragraph (b) shall not apply to supplies that so fail to conform to contract requirements as to give a right of rejection, and the risk of loss of or damage to such nonconforming supplies remains with the Contractor until cure or acceptance [@CL-CLS-0202]. Under paragraph (b), the Contractor shall not be liable for loss of or damage to supplies caused by the negligence of officers, agents, or employees of the Government acting within the scope of their employment [@CL-CLS-0203].

The prescription for the Responsibility for Supplies clause is in section 46.316 [@CL-CLS-1232]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.246-16 in solicitations and contracts for supplies, services involving the furnishing of supplies, or research and development, when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CLS-1232; @CL-CLS-1233]. The contracting officer may insert the clause in such solicitations and contracts when the contract amount is not expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold and inclusion of the clause is authorized under agency procedures [@CL-CLS-1234].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 provides that contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760], effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-1 provides that the Government shall pay the Contractor, upon the submission of proper invoices or vouchers, the prices stipulated in the contract for supplies delivered and accepted or services rendered and accepted, less any deductions provided in the contract [@CL-CLS-0140]. Unless otherwise specified in the contract, payment shall be made on partial deliveries accepted by the Government if (a) the amount due on the deliveries warrants it [@CL-CLS-1561]; or (b) the Contractor requests it and the amount due on the deliveries is at least $1,000 or 50 percent of the total contract price [@CL-CLS-1562]. RFO FAR 52.246-16(a) provides that title to supplies furnished under the contract shall pass to the Government upon formal acceptance, regardless of when or where the Government takes physical possession, unless the contract specifically provides for earlier passage of title [@CL-CLS-0142]. Unless the contract specifically provides otherwise, risk of loss of or damage to supplies shall remain with the Contractor until, and shall pass to the Government upon delivery of the supplies to a carrier, if transportation is f.o.b. origin [@CL-CLS-0143]; or upon acceptance by the Government or delivery of the supplies to the Government at the destination specified in the contract, whichever is later, if transportation is f.o.b. destination [@CL-CLS-0144]. Paragraph (b) of the clause shall not apply to supplies that so fail to conform to contract requirements as to give a right of rejection, and the risk of loss of or damage to such nonconforming supplies remains with the Contractor until cure or acceptance [@CL-CLS-0145]. Under paragraph (b) of the clause, the Contractor shall not be liable for loss of or damage to supplies caused by the negligence of officers, agents, or employees of the Government acting within the scope of their employment [@CL-CLS-0146].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers a batch of widgets priced at 100,000 under a firm-fixed-price supply contract with transportation f.o.b. destination. The widgets arrive on Day 10 and the Government formally accepts them on Day 14. The contractor submits a proper invoice for 100,000 after acceptance. The answer: title passes on formal acceptance on Day 14, and risk of loss passes on the later of acceptance and delivery at the destination, which is also Day 14.

## Never confuse

Title and risk of loss are not the same transfer: title to supplies shall pass to the Government upon formal acceptance, regardless of when or where the Government takes physical possession, unless the contract specifically provides for earlier passage of title [@CL-CLS-0201].

With f.o.b. origin shipment, risk of loss of or damage to supplies shall pass to the Government upon delivery of the supplies to a carrier, unless the contract specifically provides otherwise [@CL-CLS-0301].

The codified prescription for the Payments clause is to insert the clause, appropriately modified with respect to payment due date in accordance with agency regulations, in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price supply contract, a fixed-price service contract, or a contract for nonregulated communication services is contemplated [@CL-CLS-0300]. The RFO prescription line for FAR 52.232-1 says that, as prescribed in 32.111(a)(1), the following clause is to be inserted [@CL-CLS-0141].
