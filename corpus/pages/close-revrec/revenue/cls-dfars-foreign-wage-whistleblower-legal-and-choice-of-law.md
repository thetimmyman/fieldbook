## Purpose

Shows what an offeror represents about prices tied to foreign-government-controlled wage rates or material prices, which whistleblower proceeding costs are unallowable, and which law and forum govern an overseas contract.

## The rule

| Codified citation | DFARS 252.216-7008, 252.216-7009 and 252.233-7001 [@CL-CLS-1192; @CL-CLS-1195; @CL-CLS-1197] |

| Applies to | Offers with the representation provision, which DFARS 216.203-4-70(c)(2) prescribes, and contracts with the legal costs clause, which DFARS 216.307(a) prescribes, and the choice of law clause [@CL-CLS-1214; @CL-CLS-1215; @CL-CLS-1197] |

By submission of its offer under the provision, the offeror represents that its prices are based on the wage rate or material price established and controlled by the government of the host country the offeror names [@CL-CLS-1192]. The second listed representation is that the prices in the offer do not include contingency allowances to pay for possible increases in wage rates or material prices [@CL-CLS-1193]. The provision is titled as a representation [@CL-CLS-1214].

The legal costs clause applies listed rules pursuant to section 3750 of title 10 of the United States Code, notwithstanding FAR clause 52.216-7, Allowable Cost and Payment [@CL-CLS-1194]. One listed rule is that the restrictions of FAR 31.205-47(b) on allowability of costs related to legal and other proceedings also apply to any proceeding brought by a contractor employee submitting a complaint under section 4701 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CLS-1195]. Another is that costs incurred in connection with such a proceeding are also unallowable if the result is an order to take corrective action under that law [@CL-CLS-1196].

Under the choice of law clause, the contract shall be construed and interpreted in accordance with the substantive laws of the United States of America [@CL-CLS-1197]. By executing the contract, the Contractor expressly agrees to waive any rights to invoke the jurisdiction of local national courts where the contract is performed [@CL-CLS-1198], and to accept the exclusive jurisdiction of the United States Armed Services Board of Contract Appeals and the United States Court of Federal Claims for disputes under the Disputes clause [@CL-CLS-1199].

## How it works

By the representation, the offeror states that its prices are based on the wage rate or material price established and controlled by the host-country government and do not include contingency allowances for possible increases in wage rates or material prices [@CL-CLS-1192; @CL-CLS-1193]. The legal costs clause applies the FAR 31.205-47(b) restrictions also to a proceeding brought by a contractor employee submitting a complaint, and makes costs also unallowable if the result is an order to take corrective action [@CL-CLS-1195; @CL-CLS-1196]. Under the choice of law clause the contract shall be construed and interpreted in accordance with the substantive laws of the United States of America, and the Contractor agrees to accept the exclusive jurisdiction of the United States Armed Services Board of Contract Appeals and the United States Court of Federal Claims for disputes under the Disputes clause [@CL-CLS-1197; @CL-CLS-1199].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror abroad bases its price on wage rates fixed by the host-country government and adds no allowance for later rate increases. In a separate matter, a contractor spends 80,000 defending a proceeding started by an employee complaint, and the result is an order to take corrective action, so the 80,000 is unallowable. A dispute under the Disputes clause goes to the Armed Services Board of Contract Appeals, not to local courts.

## Never confuse

The three clauses on this card govern different subjects instead of one rule: the wage-price representation speaks to the offer, the legal costs clause to allowable costs, and the choice-of-law clause to law and forum. Economic price adjustment clauses are on [Economic Price Adjustment—Labor and Material](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-economic-price-adjustment-labor-and-material.md) and [Economic price adjustments in proposals and proposal audits](../../estimating/escalation/est-economic-price-adjustments.md). The wider cost rules are on [Costs related to legal and other proceedings](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-legal-proceedings-costs.md), and the employee protections are on [Whistleblower protections for contractor employees: policy and remedies](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-contractor-employee-whistleblower-protections.md). The claims process is on [Disputes clause, claims and certification](cls-disputes-clause.md).
