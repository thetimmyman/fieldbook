## Purpose

Shows what the Contractor must notify under the notification of changes clause, to whom, what the notice must contain, how the Government responds, and how the notice affects an equitable adjustment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1094] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.243-7 [@CL-CLS-1094] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.243-7 [@CL-CLS-0124] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1094] |
| Applies to | Contracts containing the Notification of Changes clause [@CL-CLS-1094] |

Except for changes identified as such in writing and signed by the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer in writing promptly [@CL-CLS-1094]. The notice period is a number of calendar days left blank in the printed clause to be negotiated, running from the date that the Contractor identifies any Government conduct, including actions, inactions, and written or oral communications, that the Contractor regards as a change to the contract terms and conditions [@CL-CLS-1138].

On the basis of the most accurate information available to the Contractor, the notice shall state the matters that the clause lists [@CL-CLS-1095]. The list includes the date, nature, and circumstances of the conduct regarded as a change, and the name, function, and activity of each Government individual and Contractor official or employee involved in or knowledgeable about the conduct [@CL-CLS-1096]. Following submission of the notice, the Contractor shall diligently continue performance of the contract to the maximum extent possible, in accordance with its terms and conditions as construed by the Contractor, unless the notice reports a direction of the Contracting Officer or a communication from a SAR of the Contracting Officer, in either of which events the Contractor shall continue performance [@CL-CLS-1139; @CL-CLS-0601]. If the Contractor regards that direction or communication as a change as described in paragraph (b), notice shall be given in the manner provided [@CL-CLS-0602].

The Contracting Officer shall promptly, within a number of calendar days left blank in the printed clause to be negotiated, after receipt of the notice, respond to the notice in writing [@CL-CLS-1097]. In responding, the Contracting Officer shall either (1) confirm that the conduct of which the Contractor gave notice constitutes a change and when necessary direct the mode of further performance [@CL-CLS-1098]; (2) countermand any communication regarded as a change [@CL-CLS-1098]; (3) deny that the conduct of which the Contractor gave notice constitutes a change and when necessary direct the mode of further performance [@CL-CLS-1140]; or (4) in the event the Contractor's notice information is inadequate to make a decision under (1), (2), or (3), advise the Contractor what additional information is required, and establish the date by which it should be furnished and the date thereafter by which the Government will respond [@CL-CLS-1140; @CL-CLS-0235].

## How it works

Except for changes identified as such in writing and signed by the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer in writing promptly of Government conduct that the Contractor regards as a change [@CL-CLS-1094; @CL-CLS-1138], and the notice shall state the matters that the clause lists [@CL-CLS-1095; @CL-CLS-1096]. Following submission of the notice, the Contractor shall diligently continue performance of the contract to the maximum extent possible, and where the notice reports a direction of the Contracting Officer or a communication from a SAR of the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall continue performance [@CL-CLS-1139; @CL-CLS-0601]. The Contracting Officer shall respond in writing, taking one of the listed alternatives [@CL-CLS-1097; @CL-CLS-1098; @CL-CLS-1140; @CL-CLS-0235]. If the Contracting Officer confirms that Government conduct effected a change as alleged by the Contractor, and the conduct causes an increase or decrease in the Contractor's cost of, or the time required for, performance of any part of the work under the contract, whether changed or not changed by such conduct [@CL-CLS-1099; @CL-CLS-1600], an equitable adjustment shall be made in the contract price or delivery schedule or both, and in such other provisions of the contract as may be affected [@CL-CLS-1100]. The equitable adjustment shall not include increased costs or time extensions for delay resulting from the Contractor's failure to provide notice or to continue performance as provided, respectively, in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the clause [@CL-CLS-1101].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 provides that contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760], effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.243-7(b) says that, except for changes identified in writing and signed by the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer in writing promptly [@CL-CLS-0124], where the codified clause says changes identified as such in writing [@CL-CLS-1094]. The RFO notice period is a number of calendar days left blank to be negotiated, running from the date the Contractor identifies any Government conduct that it regards as a change to the contract terms and conditions [@CL-CLS-0125]. The RFO text gives actions, inactions, and written or oral communications as examples of conduct that may be regarded as a change, in a separate sentence [@CL-CLS-0126], where the codified clause puts them in a parenthetical inside the notice-period sentence [@CL-CLS-1138]. On the basis of the most accurate information available to the Contractor, the RFO notice shall state (1) the date, nature, and circumstances of the conduct regarded as a change [@CL-CLS-0127] and (2) the name, function, and activity of each Government individual and Contractor official or employee involved in or knowledgeable about the conduct [@CL-CLS-0128]. In RFO paragraph (c), following submission of the notice required by paragraph (b) of the clause, the Contractor shall diligently continue performance of the contract to the maximum extent possible in accordance with its terms and conditions as construed by the Contractor [@CL-CLS-0129], unless the notice reports a direction of the Contracting Officer or a communication from a SAR of the Contracting Officer, in either of which events the Contractor shall continue performance [@CL-CLS-0130]. However, if the Contractor regards the direction or communication as a change as described in paragraph (b) of the clause, notice shall be given in the manner provided [@CL-CLS-1563]. In RFO paragraph (d), the Contracting Officer shall promptly, within a number of calendar days left blank to be negotiated after receipt of notice, respond to the notice in writing [@CL-CLS-0131]. In responding, the Contracting Officer shall either (1) confirm that the conduct of which the Contractor gave notice constitutes a change and when necessary direct the mode of further performance [@CL-CLS-0132]; (2) countermand any communication regarded as a change [@CL-CLS-0132]; (3) deny that the conduct of which the Contractor gave notice constitutes a change and when necessary direct the mode of further performance [@CL-CLS-0133]; or (4) in the event the Contractor's notice information is inadequate to make a decision under (1), (2), or (3) of paragraph (d) of the clause, advise the Contractor what additional information is required, and establish the date by which it should be furnished and the date thereafter by which the Government will respond [@CL-CLS-0134; @CL-CLS-0135]. In RFO paragraph (e)(1), if the Contracting Officer confirms that Government conduct effected a change as alleged by the Contractor [@CL-CLS-0136], and the conduct causes an increase or decrease in the Contractor's cost of, or the time required for, performance of any part of the work under the contract, whether changed or not changed by such conduct [@CL-CLS-0137], an equitable adjustment shall be made in the contract price or delivery schedule or both, and in such other provisions of the contract as may be affected [@CL-CLS-0138]. The equitable adjustment shall not include increased costs or time extensions for delay resulting from the Contractor's failure to provide notice or to continue performance as provided, respectively, in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the clause [@CL-CLS-0139].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A site engineer gives a verbal direction that the Contractor regards as a change. The Contractor notifies the Administrative Contracting Officer in writing within the negotiated number of days, with the date, the people involved and an estimated price effect of 80,000. The Contracting Officer confirms the change in writing. An equitable adjustment is then made to price and schedule; because the notice was timely, the exclusion for delay resulting from a failure to give notice does not apply.

## Never confuse

A contractor's notice under this clause is not a change order: change order accounting is on [Change order accounting](cls-change-order-accounting.md), and the changes clause for construction contracts is on [Changes clause for construction contracts](cls-changes-construction.md).
