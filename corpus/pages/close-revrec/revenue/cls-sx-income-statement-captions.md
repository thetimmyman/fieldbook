## Purpose

Shows how a registrant presents net sales and revenues by type, the costs applicable to them, and selling, general and administrative expenses on the face of the statement of comprehensive income, including the combination rule for small classes and the related-party disclosure. Regulation S-X is not FAR, DFARS or CAS. It binds financial statements filed with the Commission, which is the Securities and Exchange Commission, and a contractor that files nothing with the Commission is not bound [@CL-CLS-0850]. Whether it binds a company depends on the company's filing obligations with the Commission.

The net sales and gross revenues caption requires net sales of tangible products, operating revenues of public utilities or others, income from rentals, revenues from services and other revenues to be stated separately [@CL-CLS-0839]. The costs and expenses caption requires cost of tangible goods sold, operating expenses of public utilities or others, expenses applicable to rental income, cost of services and expenses applicable to other revenues to be stated separately [@CL-CLS-0840]. Amounts earned from transactions with related parties shall be disclosed as required under the related party note rule, section 210.4-08(k) [@CL-CLS-0841].

If income is derived from more than one of the subcaptions of the net sales and revenues caption, each class that is not more than 10 percent of the sum of the items may be combined with another class [@CL-CLS-0837]. If classes of income are combined under that rule, the related costs and expenses are combined in the same manner [@CL-CLS-0838]. The caption list includes selling, general and administrative expenses as its own caption, and other general expenses are items not normally included in that caption, with any material item stated separately [@CL-CLS-0842].

## How it works

The registrant shows revenue by type, with the matching costs by type [@CL-CLS-0839; @CL-CLS-0840]. A small class may be folded into another class, and when it is, the matching costs are folded the same way [@CL-CLS-0837; @CL-CLS-0838]. Selling, general and administrative expenses sit in their own caption, apart from other general expenses [@CL-CLS-0842]. Related-party revenue amounts are disclosed under the related party note rule [@CL-CLS-0841]. The text sets the captions but does not tell a registrant when revenue is recognised, so recognition timing is not established here.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant earns 900 from product sales, 80 from services and 20 from rentals, a total of 1,000. Rentals are 2 percent and services are 8 percent of the total. Each is not more than 10 percent, so either may be combined with another class. If rentals are combined into services, the rental expenses are combined with the cost of services in the same way.

## Never confuse

The 10 percent test compares a class of income with the sum of the items, and it is a separate rule from the one on immaterial items, which names no number [@CL-CLS-0837; @CL-CLS-0816]. For that rule, see [Form, GAAP presumption and materiality under Regulation S-X](../group-bridge/cls-sx-form-gaap-materiality.md). Combining classes on the revenue side forces the same combination of the matching costs [@CL-CLS-0838]. The captions set how revenue and costs are presented, and the text printed here does not say when revenue is earned. For how management explains movement in these lines, see [MD&A results of operations](../../fpa/kpi/fpa-mdna-results-of-operations.md), and for segment information see [Business description by segment](../../fpa/kpi/fpa-business-description-by-segment.md).
