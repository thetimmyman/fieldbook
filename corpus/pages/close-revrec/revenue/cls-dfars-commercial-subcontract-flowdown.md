## Purpose

Shows when FAR and DFARS clause terms may be included in commercial subcontracts, and what the clause itself requires to flow down.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CLS-1170] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.244-7000 [@CL-CLS-1169]; DFARS 244.403 [@CL-CLS-1477] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | NOV 2023 [@CL-CLS-1617] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1170] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, prescribed at DFARS 244.403 [@CL-CLS-1169; @CL-CLS-1477] |

The Contractor shall not include the terms of any FAR clause or DFARS clause in subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services at any tier under the contract, unless an exception listed in the clause applies [@CL-CLS-1170]. The clause lists two exceptions as alternatives, joined by "or": one is that, for a DFARS clause, it is so specified in the particular clause, and the other is that, for a FAR clause, the clause is listed at FAR 12.301(d) or specified in paragraph (e)(1) of FAR 52.212-5 or paragraph (b)(1) of FAR 52.244-6, as applicable [@CL-CLS-1171].

The Contractor shall treat as commercial products any items valued at less than $10,000 per item that were purchased by the Contractor for use in the performance of multiple contracts with the Department of Defense and other parties and are not identifiable to any particular contract when purchased [@CL-CLS-1172]. The Contractor shall ensure that any items to be used in performance of the contract that are treated as commercial products under that rule meet all terms and conditions of the contract that are applicable to commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CLS-1204]. Those terms and conditions are the ones applicable in accordance with the clause at FAR 52.244-6 and paragraph (a) of this clause [@CL-CLS-1205]. The Contractor shall include the terms of this clause, including this paragraph (c), in subcontracts awarded under the contract, including subcontracts for the acquisition of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CLS-1173].

The prescription at DFARS 244.403 says to use the clause at 252.244-7000, Subcontracts for Commercial Products and Commercial Services, in solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CLS-1477].

## How it works

The clause works in two directions. It restricts passing FAR and DFARS clause terms into commercial subcontracts at any tier [@CL-CLS-1170], and it requires its own terms to go into the subcontracts awarded under the contract, commercial ones included [@CL-CLS-1173]. The terms of a DFARS clause are not included unless that clause so specifies, and the terms of a FAR clause are not included unless it falls within the FAR exception [@CL-CLS-1170; @CL-CLS-1171]. A separate rule treats certain low-value items bought for multiple contracts as commercial products, and the items so treated must meet the contract terms applicable to commercial products or services [@CL-CLS-1172; @CL-CLS-1204].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor buys a commercial item from a supplier. One DFARS clause says in its own text that it applies to commercial subcontracts, so the prime includes its terms. Another DFARS clause says nothing on commercial subcontracts, so the prime leaves its terms out. The prime still includes the terms of this commercial subcontracts clause in the subcontract.

## Never confuse

The flowdown of this clause is broader than the general rule instead of narrower: the Contractor shall not include the terms of any FAR or DFARS clause in subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services at any tier unless an exception applies [@CL-CLS-1170; @CL-CLS-1171], yet the Contractor shall include the terms of this clause, including paragraph (c), in subcontracts awarded under the contract, including subcontracts for the acquisition of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CLS-1173]. The wider rules for subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services are on [Subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md). Flowdown of the small business subcontracting plan clause is a separate matter on [Subcontracting plan clause: master and commercial plans, reporting and flowdown](../../market/programs/mkt-subcontracting-plan-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md), and the FAR and DFARS structure is on [The FAR System and agency supplements](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-far-system-and-agency-supplements.md).
