## Purpose

Shows what the Contracting Officer may change under the time-and-materials or labor-hours changes clause, what the equitable adjustment can reach, and when the Contractor must assert its right.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1083] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.243-3 [@CL-CLS-1083] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.243-3 [@CL-CLS-0042] |
| Edition date | SEP 2000 [@CL-CLS-1615] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1083] |
| Applies to | Contracts containing the Changes—Time-and-Materials or Labor-Hours clause [@CL-CLS-1083] |

The Contracting Officer may at any time, by written order and without notice to the sureties, if any, make changes within the general scope of the contract in any one or more of the areas the clause lists [@CL-CLS-1083]. The list includes the description of services to be performed, the time of performance, and the place of performance of the services [@CL-CLS-1084]. It also includes drawings, designs, or specifications when the supplies to be furnished are to be specially manufactured for the Government in accordance with them, the method of shipment or packing of supplies, the place of delivery, and the amount of Government-furnished property [@CL-CLS-1130].

The equitable adjustment provision applies where a change causes an increase or decrease in any hourly rate, the ceiling price, or the time required for performance of any part of the work, whether or not changed by the order, or otherwise affects any other terms and conditions [@CL-CLS-1085]. The Contracting Officer then makes the adjustment in any one or more of the ceiling price, the hourly rates, the delivery schedule and other affected terms, and modifies the contract accordingly [@CL-CLS-1086].

The Contractor shall assert its right to an adjustment within 30 days from the date of receipt of the written order [@CL-CLS-0227; @CL-CLS-1087]. However, if the Contracting Officer decides that the facts justify it, the Contracting Officer may receive and act upon a proposal submitted before final payment of the contract [@CL-CLS-1087].

## How it works

A written order changes the work within the general scope of the contract [@CL-CLS-1083]. If the change moves an hourly rate, the ceiling price, the time for performance or other terms, the Contracting Officer makes an equitable adjustment in any one or more of the ceiling price, the hourly rates, the delivery schedule and other affected terms [@CL-CLS-1085; @CL-CLS-1086]. The Contractor shall assert its right within the stated period, and the Contracting Officer may receive and act upon a proposal submitted before final payment if the Contracting Officer decides the facts justify it [@CL-CLS-0227; @CL-CLS-1087]. Failure to agree to any adjustment will be a dispute under the Disputes clause, and nothing in the clause excuses the Contractor from proceeding with the contract as changed [@CL-CLS-1131]. The claims process is on [Disputes clause, claims and certification](cls-disputes-clause.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.243-3 has the same equitable adjustment condition [@CL-CLS-0042; @CL-CLS-1085] and the same adjustment in the ceiling price, hourly rates, delivery schedule and other affected terms [@CL-CLS-0043; @CL-CLS-1086]. It has the same 30-day assertion period and the same exception for a proposal submitted before final payment [@CL-CLS-0044; @CL-CLS-0045; @CL-CLS-1087], and the same dispute rule [@CL-CLS-0046; @CL-CLS-1131]. Paragraph (a) differs in wording: the RFO text prints that the Contracting Officer may issue a written order to make changes in within the scope of this contract [@CL-CLS-0038], where the codified paragraph says within the general scope [@CL-CLS-1083]. Its first three items are the same description of services, time of performance and place of performance [@CL-CLS-0039; @CL-CLS-1084]. Its supplies items begin by printing drawings, designs, or specifications which require special manufacturing of supplies for the Government, followed by method of shipment or packing of supplies, place of delivery, and amount of Government-furnished property [@CL-CLS-0040], where the codified first supplies item says when the supplies to be furnished are to be specially manufactured for the Government in accordance with them [@CL-CLS-1130]. The RFO text states in a separate subparagraph that the Contracting Officer does not need to notify any sureties of a written order [@CL-CLS-0041].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A labor-hour contract has a ceiling price of 600,000. A written order moves the place of performance, which adds 40 labor hours and two weeks to the schedule. The Contractor asserts its right within the assertion period. The Contracting Officer raises the ceiling price for the added hours, extends the delivery schedule, and modifies the contract.

## Never confuse

The equitable adjustment lands on terms this clause names rather than on the estimated cost and fee of the cost-reimbursement version: under this clause the Contracting Officer will make the equitable adjustment in any one or more of the ceiling price, hourly rates, delivery schedule and other affected terms [@CL-CLS-1086]. The fixed-price clause is on [Changes, Fixed-Price clause and equitable adjustment](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-changes-fixed-price-equitable-adjustment.md), and the contract type is on [Time-and-materials contracts](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md). The cost-reimbursement version is on [Changes clause for cost-reimbursement contracts](cls-changes-cost-reimbursement.md), and the construction version is on [Changes clause for construction contracts](cls-changes-construction.md).

The RFO text numbers the paragraph (a) items (a)(1)(i) to (vii) and puts the sureties rule at (a)(2) [@CL-CLS-0039; @CL-CLS-0040; @CL-CLS-0041], where the codified clause numbers the items (1) to (7) under paragraph (a) and puts the words on the sureties in the lead-in of paragraph (a) [@CL-CLS-1084; @CL-CLS-1130; @CL-CLS-1083].
