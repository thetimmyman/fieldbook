## Purpose

Shows what the Contracting Officer may change under the time-and-materials or labor-hours changes clause, what the equitable adjustment can reach, and when the Contractor must assert its right.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1083] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.243-3 [@CL-CLS-1083] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1083] |
| Applies to | Contracts containing the Changes—Time-and-Materials or Labor-Hours clause [@CL-CLS-1083] |

The Contracting Officer may at any time, by written order and without notice to the sureties, if any, make changes within the general scope of the contract in any one or more of the areas the clause lists [@CL-CLS-1083]. The list includes the description of services to be performed, the time of performance, and the place of performance of the services [@CL-CLS-1084]. It also includes drawings, designs, or specifications when the supplies to be furnished are to be specially manufactured for the Government in accordance with them, the method of shipment or packing of supplies, the place of delivery, and the amount of Government-furnished property [@CL-CLS-1130].

The equitable adjustment provision applies where a change causes an increase or decrease in any hourly rate, the ceiling price, or the time required for performance of any part of the work, whether or not changed by the order, or otherwise affects any other terms and conditions [@CL-CLS-1085]. The Contracting Officer then makes the adjustment in any one or more of the ceiling price, the hourly rates, the delivery schedule and other affected terms, and modifies the contract accordingly [@CL-CLS-1086].

The Contractor must assert its right to an adjustment within 30 days from the date of receipt of the written order, but the Contracting Officer may receive and act upon a proposal submitted before final payment if the Contracting Officer decides that the facts justify it [@CL-CLS-1087]. The printed section carries no note that allows the Contractor's assertion period to be varied for this clause.

## How it works

A written order changes the work within the general scope of the contract [@CL-CLS-1083]. If the change moves an hourly rate, the ceiling price, the time for performance or other terms, the Contracting Officer adjusts the ceiling price, the hourly rates, the delivery schedule or other affected terms, as the facts require [@CL-CLS-1085; @CL-CLS-1086]. The Contractor asserts its right within the stated period, and a later proposal may still be acted upon before final payment where the Contracting Officer decides the facts justify it [@CL-CLS-1087]. Failure to agree to any adjustment will be a dispute under the Disputes clause, and the Contractor must still proceed with the contract as changed [@CL-CLS-1131]. The claims process is on [Disputes clause, claims and certification](cls-disputes-clause.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A labor-hour contract has a ceiling price of 600,000. A written order moves the place of performance, which adds 40 labor hours and two weeks to the schedule. The Contractor asserts its right within the assertion period. The Contracting Officer raises the ceiling price for the added hours, extends the delivery schedule, and modifies the contract.

## Never confuse

This clause is not the fixed-price changes clause. Here the adjustment can reach the hourly rates and the ceiling price [@CL-CLS-1086], where the fixed-price clause has its own adjustment rule. The fixed-price clause is on [Changes, Fixed-Price clause and equitable adjustment](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-changes-fixed-price-equitable-adjustment.md), and the contract type is on [Time-and-materials contracts](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md). The cost-reimbursement version is on [Changes clause for cost-reimbursement contracts](cls-changes-cost-reimbursement.md), and the construction version is on [Changes clause for construction contracts](cls-changes-construction.md).
