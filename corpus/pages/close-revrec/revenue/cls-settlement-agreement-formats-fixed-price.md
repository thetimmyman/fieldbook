## Purpose

Shows which settlement agreement format applies to a fixed-price contract after a complete termination, a partial termination, or a settlement with subcontractors only, and what each format contains.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1037] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.603-1, 49.603-2 and 49.603-8 [@CL-CLS-1037; @CL-CLS-1041; @CL-CLS-1043] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1037] |
| Applies to | Settlements of fixed-price contracts after a complete termination, a partial termination, or a settlement covering only subcontractors [@CL-CLS-1037; @CL-CLS-1041; @CL-CLS-1043]. |

The complete-termination format is inserted in Block 14 of SF 30 for settlements of fixed-price contracts completely terminated [@CL-CLS-1037]. The partial-termination format is inserted in Block 14 of SF 30 for settlements of fixed-price contracts partially terminated [@CL-CLS-1041]. The subcontractors-only format is inserted in Block 14 of SF 30 for settlements of fixed-price contracts covering only settlements with subcontractors [@CL-CLS-1043].

The complete-termination format has the contractor certify that all termination inventory, including scrap, is properly accounted for [@CL-CLS-1038], and it reserves listed rights and liabilities regardless of any other provision [@CL-CLS-1040]. The partial-termination format has the parties specify the terminated portion by line item, description, quantity, unit price and total price [@CL-CLS-1042]. The subcontractors-only format settles the part of the proposal based on a listed set of subcontracts [@CL-CLS-1044].

## How it works

The complete-termination format has the Government pay the net amount of settlement on a proper invoice or voucher, arrived at by deducting from a stated sum [@CL-CLS-1039]. The deductions include unliquidated partial or progress payments, unliquidated advance payments with any interest, and applicable property disposal credits [@CL-CLS-1068]. The partial-termination format provides that nothing in the agreement impairs or affects the contract's covenants, terms, or conditions relating to the completed or continued portion of the contract [@CL-CLS-1069]. The choice of format follows what was terminated: the whole contract, a portion of it, or only the subcontracts [@CL-CLS-1037; @CL-CLS-1041; @CL-CLS-1043].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price contract has three line items and the Government terminates line item 2 only. The partial-termination format lists that line item with its description, the quantity terminated, the unit price and the total price. The gross settlement is 120,000. The contractor deducts 70,000 of unliquidated progress payments for the terminated portion and 5,000 of property disposal credits. The net settlement is 45,000, and lines 1 and 3 carry on under the contract.

## Never confuse

These formats record a settlement the parties have agreed. A settlement fixed by the contracting officer without agreement is covered on [Settlement by determination under a terminated contract](cls-settlement-by-determination.md). The format for a partial termination is also not the equitable adjustment of the continued portion, which is covered on [Partial termination: equitable adjustment and partial settlements](cls-partial-termination-equitable-adjustment-and-settlements.md). The complete-termination format for a cost-reimbursement contract is on [Cost-reimbursement settlement format, subcontract settlement authorization and delinquency notices](cls-cost-reimbursement-settlement-format-authorization-and-delinquency-notices.md). The proposal that the agreement settles is submitted as shown on [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md).
