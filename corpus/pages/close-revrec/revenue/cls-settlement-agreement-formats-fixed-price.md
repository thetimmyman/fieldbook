## Purpose

Shows which settlement agreement format applies to a fixed-price contract after a complete termination, a partial termination, or a settlement with subcontractors only, and what each format contains.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1037] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.603-1, 49.603-2 and 49.603-8 [@CL-CLS-1037; @CL-CLS-1041; @CL-CLS-1043] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1037] |
| Applies to | Settlements of fixed-price contracts after a complete termination, a partial termination, or a settlement covering only subcontractors [@CL-CLS-1037; @CL-CLS-1041; @CL-CLS-1043]. |

The complete-termination format is inserted in Block 14 of SF 30 for settlements of fixed-price contracts completely terminated [@CL-CLS-1037]. The partial-termination format is inserted in Block 14 of SF 30 for settlements of fixed-price contracts partially terminated [@CL-CLS-1041]. The subcontractors-only format is inserted in Block 14 of SF 30 for settlements of fixed-price contracts covering only settlements with subcontractors [@CL-CLS-1043].

The complete-termination format has the contractor certify that all contract termination inventory (including scrap) has been retained or acquired by the Contractor, sold to third parties, returned to suppliers, delivered to or stored for the Government, or otherwise properly accounted for, and that all proceeds and retention credits were used in arriving at the agreement [@CL-CLS-1038; @CL-CLS-0185], and it reserves listed rights and liabilities regardless of any other provision [@CL-CLS-1040]. The partial-termination format provides for specifying the terminated portion clearly as to (i) line item numbers, (ii) descriptions, (iii) quantity terminated, (iv) unit price of items, (v) total price of terminated items, and (vi) any other explanation necessary to avoid uncertainty or misunderstanding [@CL-CLS-1042]. The subcontractors-only format settles that portion of the settlement proposal of the Contractor that is based upon termination of the following subcontracts entered into in performing the contract [@CL-CLS-1044].

## How it works

The complete-termination format has the Government pay the Contractor or its assignee the net amount of settlement upon presentation of a proper invoice or voucher, arrived at by deducting from a stated sum, which the form's bracketed instruction makes the gross amount of settlement for proposals on an inventory basis and that amount less the amount shown in subdivision (6)(i) for proposals on a total cost basis [@CL-CLS-1039; @CL-CLS-0186]. The deductions include unliquidated partial or progress payments, unliquidated advance payments with any interest, and applicable property disposal credits [@CL-CLS-1068]. The partial-termination format provides that nothing in the agreement impairs or affects the contract's covenants, terms, or conditions relating to the completed or continued portion of the contract [@CL-CLS-1069].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price contract has three line items and the Government terminates line item 2 only. The partial-termination format lists that line item with its description, the quantity terminated, the unit price and the total price. The gross settlement is 120,000. The contractor deducts 70,000 of unliquidated progress payments for the terminated portion and 5,000 of property disposal credits. The net settlement is 45,000, and lines 1 and 3 carry on under the contract.
