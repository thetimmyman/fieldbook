## Purpose

Shows which costs the special termination costs clause covers, how the dollar amount in the clause caps what the Government pays, and what the clause leaves unchanged.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CLS-1150] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.249-7000 [@CL-CLS-1150] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1150] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the special termination costs clause, which DFARS 249.501-70 prescribes [@CL-CLS-1200] |

The clause defines special termination costs as only costs in listed categories defined in part 31 of the FAR, the first being severance pay as provided in FAR 31.205-6(g) [@CL-CLS-1150]. Two more listed categories are reasonable costs continuing after termination, as provided in FAR 31.205-42(b), and settlement of expenses, as provided in FAR 31.205-42(g) [@CL-CLS-1151]. The last two are costs of return of field service personnel from sites, as provided in FAR 31.205-35 and FAR 31.205-46(c), and the costs in the first four categories to which subcontractors may be entitled in the event of termination [@CL-CLS-1152].

The Contractor shall not include in its estimate of costs incurred or to be incurred any amount for special termination costs to which it may be entitled if the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government [@CL-CLS-1153]. That paragraph applies notwithstanding the Limitation of Cost/Limitation of Funds clause of the contract [@CL-CLS-1201]. The Contractor also agrees to perform so that its claim for special termination costs will not exceed the dollar amount inserted in the clause, and the Government has no obligation to pay special termination costs in excess of that amount [@CL-CLS-1154].

## How it works

The clause keeps special termination costs out of the Contractor's estimate of costs incurred or to be incurred, notwithstanding the Limitation of Cost/Limitation of Funds clause [@CL-CLS-1153; @CL-CLS-1201]. The Government shall have no obligation to pay the Contractor any amount for special termination costs in excess of the inserted amount [@CL-CLS-1154]. In the event of termination for the convenience of the Government, the clause shall not be construed as affecting the allowability of special termination costs in any manner other than limiting the maximum amount of the costs payable by the Government [@CL-CLS-1155]. The clause stays in force until the contract is fully funded [@CL-CLS-1156].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract inserts 400,000 as the amount in the clause. After a termination for convenience, the contractor's special termination costs, including severance pay and amounts its subcontractors may claim, come to 450,000. The Government has no obligation to pay the 50,000 above the inserted amount, so its obligation for these costs stops at 400,000. Before termination, the contractor left all of these costs out of its cost estimate.

## Never confuse

In the event of termination for the convenience of the Government, the clause shall not be construed as affecting the allowability of special termination costs in any manner other than limiting the maximum amount of the costs payable by the Government [@CL-CLS-1155]. The paragraph on the cost estimate applies notwithstanding the Limitation of Cost/Limitation of Funds clause [@CL-CLS-1201], and those clauses are covered on [Limitation of Cost notice](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-limitation-of-cost.md) and [Limitation of Funds clause](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-limitation-of-funds.md). How a termination settlement is built is on [Termination costs](../inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md) and [Termination for convenience clauses and the contracts they go in](cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md), the conditions for authorizing use of this clause are on [DoD special termination costs and the limitation on pricing the terminated effort](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-special-termination-costs.md), and the separate notice to employees and subcontractors of an anticipated termination is on [Notification of anticipated contract termination or reduction (DFARS)](cls-dfars-anticipated-termination-notification.md).
