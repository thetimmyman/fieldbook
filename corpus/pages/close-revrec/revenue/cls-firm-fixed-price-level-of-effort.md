## Purpose

Describes the firm-fixed-price, level-of-effort term contract, including payment based on effort expended rather than results and the conditions for its use.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0562] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.207-1 [@CL-CLS-0562] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0562] |
| Applies to | Investigation or study in a specific research and development area [@CL-CLS-0563] |

A firm-fixed-price, level-of-effort term contract requires the contractor to provide a specified level of effort, over a stated period of time, on work that can be stated only in general terms, and requires the Government to pay the contractor a fixed dollar amount [@CL-CLS-0562].

Payment under this contract type is based on the effort expended rather than on the results achieved [@CL-CLS-0564].

## How it works

The contract type is suitable for investigation or study in a specific research and development area, and its product is usually a report showing the results achieved through application of the required level of effort [@CL-CLS-0563], although payment is based on the effort expended rather than on the results achieved [@CL-CLS-0564]. It may be used only when the work required cannot otherwise be clearly defined, the required level of effort is identified and agreed upon in advance, there is reasonable assurance that the intended result cannot be achieved by expending less than the stipulated effort, and the contract price is the simplified acquisition threshold or less, unless approved by the chief of the contracting office [@CL-CLS-0565; @CL-CLS-0566].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor agrees to provide 1,000 hours of engineering study over six months for a fixed dollar amount of 150,000. It delivers the study report, and the required 1,000 hours were expended. The answer: the contractor is paid the 150,000, because payment is based on the effort expended rather than on the results achieved.

## Never confuse

A fixed price for effort is not a reimbursement of cost: under a firm-fixed-price, level-of-effort term contract the Government pays the contractor a fixed dollar amount [@CL-CLS-0562], while cost-reimbursement types of contracts provide for payment of allowable incurred costs, to the extent prescribed in the contract [@CL-CLS-0567]. The term form of a cost-plus-fixed-fee contract obligates the contractor to devote a specified level of effort for a stated time period [@CL-CLS-0579], and a cost-plus-fixed-fee contract is a cost-reimbursement contract [@CL-CLS-0576]. For that type, see [Cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts](cls-cost-plus-fixed-fee-contracts.md). For general contract type context, see [Contract types](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-contract-types.md).
