## Purpose

Names each termination for convenience clause that the FAR prescribes, and the kind of contract each clause goes in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1014] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.502 and 49.503 [@CL-CLS-1014; @CL-CLS-1022]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CLS-1521] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1014] |
| Applies to | Fixed-price, cost-reimbursement and architect-engineer contracts, by the clause prescriptions below [@CL-CLS-1014; @CL-CLS-1015; @CL-CLS-1022; @CL-CLS-1023]. |

For fixed-price contracts, the contracting officer shall insert the short-form clause at 52.249-1 when the contract amount is not expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, except in the cases the sentence lists [@CL-CLS-1014]. The exceptions are alternatives: cases where use of the services short-form clause at 52.249-4 is appropriate, contracts for research and development work with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-profit basis [@CL-CLS-1051], contracts for architect-engineer services, or cases where one of the clauses prescribed or cited in FAR 49.505(a) or (c) is appropriate [@CL-CLS-1052]. For dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements, the contracting officer shall use the short-form clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CLS-1053].

The long-form clause at 52.249-2 goes in when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the amount is expected to exceed that threshold, except in contracts of the types the sentence lists [@CL-CLS-1015]. Those types are alternatives: dismantling and demolition, research and development work with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-profit basis, or architect-engineer services [@CL-CLS-1016]. The long-form clause shall not be used if the services short-form clause at 52.249-4 is appropriate, or if one of the clauses prescribed or cited in FAR 49.505(a), (b), or (e) is appropriate [@CL-CLS-1054]. For construction, the contracting officer shall use the long-form clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CLS-1055].

The clause at 52.249-3 goes in solicitations and contracts for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements [@CL-CLS-1017], when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CLS-1018]. The services short-form clause at 52.249-4 goes in services contracts, regardless of value, when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contracting officer makes a determination [@CL-CLS-1019]. The determination includes that, because of the kind of services, the offeror will not incur substantial charges in preparation for and in carrying out the contract [@CL-CLS-1020], and that the offeror would, if terminated for the convenience of the Government, limit termination settlement charges to services rendered before the date of termination [@CL-CLS-1073].

The clause at 52.249-5, titled for educational and other nonprofit institutions, goes in when either a fixed-price or cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated for research and development work [@CL-CLS-1021], with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-profit or no-fee basis [@CL-CLS-1056]. The clause at 52.249-6 is inserted when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated, except contracts for research and development with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-fee basis [@CL-CLS-1022]. If the contract is for construction, the contracting officer shall use it with its Alternate I [@CL-CLS-1057]. If the contract is a time-and-material or labor-hour contract, the contracting officer shall use it with its Alternate IV [@CL-CLS-1058]. The clause at 52.249-7 is inserted for architect-engineer services when a fixed-price contract is contemplated [@CL-CLS-1023].

The FAR definitions section describes termination for convenience as the exercise of the Government's right to completely or partially terminate performance of work under a contract when it is in the Government's interest [@CL-CLS-1521]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines termination for convenience as the exercise of the Government's right to completely or partially terminate performance of work under a contract when it is in the Government's interest, the same in substance as the codified definition [@CL-CLS-1540; @CL-CLS-1521].

## How it works

For a fixed-price contract, the simplified acquisition threshold is the dividing line between the prescription for the short-form clause and the prescription for the long-form clause, within the exceptions stated for each [@CL-CLS-1014; @CL-CLS-1015]. The long-form prescription also bars use where the services short-form clause is appropriate [@CL-CLS-1054]. The nonprofit-institution clause is limited to research and development work with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-profit or no-fee basis [@CL-CLS-1056]. The cost-reimbursement clause is not prescribed for research and development with an educational or nonprofit institution on a no-fee basis [@CL-CLS-1022]. The sections also prescribe other alternates, including alternates for partial payments, and give guidance on use of some of these clauses in subcontracts.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds a fixed-price supply contract expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, and none of the listed exceptions or the bar applies, so it carries the long-form fixed-price clause. The same contractor holds a cost-reimbursement development contract with a commercial customer, and that one carries the cost-reimbursement clause. A small fixed-price contract for parking space rental may carry the services short form if the contracting officer makes the stated determination.

## Never confuse

The fixed-price clauses here govern termination for the Government's convenience, not for default, and the cost-reimbursement clause at 52.249-6 is prescribed in a FAR section headed for both convenience and default [@CL-CLS-1022]. The default clauses are a separate set, shown on [Default clauses for fixed-price contracts and when each goes in](cls-fixed-price-default-clauses.md), and their effects are on [Termination for default: effect, notices and repurchase against the contractor](cls-termination-for-default-effect-and-repurchase.md). How a fixed-price convenience settlement is computed is on [Termination for Convenience of the Government (Fixed-Price): settlement](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md). The cost-reimbursement settlement is on [Termination (Cost-Reimbursement): settlement](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-termination-cost-reimbursement-settlement.md). Submitting a settlement proposal is on [Submission of settlement proposals after termination of a fixed-price contract](cls-fixed-price-settlement-proposal-submission.md), the notice of termination is on [Termination notices: electronic notice and letter notice](cls-termination-electronic-and-letter-notice.md), and the agreement formats are on [Settlement agreement formats for terminated fixed-price contracts](cls-settlement-agreement-formats-fixed-price.md).
