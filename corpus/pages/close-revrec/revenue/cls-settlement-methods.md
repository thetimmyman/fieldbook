## Purpose

Shows the methods for settling a terminated cost-reimbursement contract or a fixed-price contract terminated for convenience, and the order of preference the section sets between agreement and determination.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1248] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.103 [@CL-CLS-1248] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1248] |
| Applies to | Terminated cost-reimbursement contracts and fixed-price contracts terminated for convenience [@CL-CLS-1248] |

Settlement of a terminated cost-reimbursement contract, or of a fixed-price contract terminated for convenience, may be effected by any one of four alternative methods: negotiated agreement, determination by the TCO [@CL-CLS-1248], costing-out under vouchers using SF 1034 for cost-reimbursement contracts (as prescribed in subpart 49.3), or a combination of these methods [@CL-CLS-1249]. Where possible, the TCO should negotiate a fair and prompt settlement with the contractor [@CL-CLS-1250]. The TCO shall settle a settlement proposal by determination only when it cannot be settled by agreement [@CL-CLS-1251].

## How it works

The TCO shall settle a proposal by determination only when it cannot be settled by agreement [@CL-CLS-1251]. When possible, the TCO should negotiate a fair and prompt settlement with the contractor [@CL-CLS-1250].

Costing-out under vouchers using SF 1034 is a method for cost-reimbursement contracts, as prescribed in subpart 49.3 [@CL-CLS-1249]. A combination of these methods is also a method of settlement [@CL-CLS-1249]. The TCO's duties elsewhere include settling by determination the elements that cannot be agreed on when a complete settlement cannot be negotiated [@CL-CLS-0906]. See [Termination authority, the notice of termination and the TCO's duties](cls-termination-authority-notice-and-tco-duties.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement contract is terminated, and the contractor files a settlement proposal of 500,000. The TCO negotiates, and the two sides agree on 460,000 for the whole proposal. Because the proposal is settled by agreement, the TCO does not settle it by determination [@CL-CLS-1250; @CL-CLS-1251].

Suppose instead that the sides agree on 400,000 of the proposal and cannot agree on the other 100,000. The TCO settles the elements that cannot be agreed on by determination, so the settlement combines agreement and determination [@CL-CLS-0906; @CL-CLS-1248; @CL-CLS-1249; @CL-CLS-1251]. Because the contract is cost-reimbursement, costing-out under vouchers using SF 1034, as prescribed in subpart 49.3, is also a listed method for it [@CL-CLS-1249].

## Never confuse

Negotiated agreement and determination by the TCO are separate items in the list of methods [@CL-CLS-1248]. The TCO should negotiate where possible, and shall settle by determination only when the proposal cannot be settled by agreement [@CL-CLS-1250; @CL-CLS-1251]. Costing-out under vouchers is listed as its own method, for cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-CLS-1249]. The determination route itself is the subject of [Settlement by determination](cls-settlement-by-determination.md).
