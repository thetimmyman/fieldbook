## Purpose

Shows what warranty data an offeror furnishes for serialized warranted items, and which safety standard applies to facilities, infrastructure and equipment for military operations.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CLS-1174; @CL-CLS-1179] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.246-7005 and 252.246-7004 [@CL-CLS-1174; @CL-CLS-1179] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1174; @CL-CLS-1179] |
| Applies to | Offers with the warranty tracking provision and contracts with the safety clause, each prescribed at its own DFARS section [@CL-CLS-1174; @CL-CLS-1179] |

The Offeror shall provide with its offer the information required by the attachment entitled Warranty Tracking Information on each contract line item number, subline item number, or exhibit line item number for warranted items [@CL-CLS-1175]. That information shall include such information as duration, fixed expiration, item type, starting event, usage, warranty administrator enterprise identifier, and warranty guarantor enterprise identifier [@CL-CLS-1176]. The successful offeror will be required to provide the listed information no later than when the warranted items are presented for receipt and/or acceptance, in accordance with the clause at DFARS 252.246-7006 [@CL-CLS-1177]. That information is the unique item identifier for each warranted item required by the Warranty Tracking Information attachment, and all information required by the Source of Repair Instructions attachment for each warranted item [@CL-CLS-1178].

The Contractor shall ensure, consistent with the requirements of the applicable inspection clause in the contract, that the facilities, infrastructure, and equipment acquired, constructed, installed, repaired, maintained, or operated under the contract comply with Unified Facilities Criteria (UFC) 1-200-01 for fire protection, structural integrity, electrical systems, plumbing, water treatment, waste disposal, and telecommunications networks [@CL-CLS-1180; @CL-CLS-1181]. The Contractor may apply a standard equivalent to or more stringent than UFC 1-200-01 upon a written determination of the acceptability of the standard by the Contracting Officer with the concurrence of the relevant Discipline Working Group [@CL-CLS-1182]. A Discipline Working Group means representatives from the DoD Components, as defined in MIL-STD-3007F, who are responsible for the unification and maintenance of the Unified Facilities Criteria documents for a particular discipline area [@CL-CLS-1213].

## How it works

The warranty provision asks for data in two steps. The Warranty Tracking Information comes with the offer [@CL-CLS-1175], and the unique item identifier and source of repair information come when the items are presented for receipt or acceptance [@CL-CLS-1177; @CL-CLS-1178]. A standard equivalent to or more stringent than UFC 1-200-01 may be applied upon a written determination of its acceptability by the Contracting Officer with the concurrence of the relevant Discipline Working Group [@CL-CLS-1182]. The matching clause for warranty tracking and the safety issue notification clause are covered on [Notification of potential safety issues and warranty tracking of serialized items (DFARS)](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-dfars-safety-notification-and-warranty-tracking.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes 40 serialized warranted items. It completes the Warranty Tracking Information attachment for those items with its offer. After award, as the items are presented for acceptance, it supplies each unique item identifier and the source of repair information. Separately, a contract to build a maintenance shed requires its fire protection and electrical systems to meet the Unified Facilities Criteria, unless the Contracting Officer, with the concurrence of the relevant Discipline Working Group, determines in writing that an equivalent or more stringent standard is acceptable.

## Never confuse

Warranty criteria and remedies are on [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-warranties.md), a construction warranty with a remedy is on [Warranty of construction (Germany) (DFARS)](cls-dfars-warranty-of-construction-germany.md), and warranty cost is on [Service and warranty costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-service-and-warranty-costs.md). The notice of a potential safety issue is covered on [Notification of potential safety issues and warranty tracking of serialized items (DFARS)](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-dfars-safety-notification-and-warranty-tracking.md). The unique item identifier itself is on [Item unique identification and valuation clause (DFARS)](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-dfars-item-unique-identification.md).
