## Purpose

Sets out what a no-cost settlement agreement releases and what it leaves standing, for a complete termination, a partial termination and a settlement of reservations.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0262] |
| Codified citation | FAR 49.603-6, 49.603-7 and 49.603-9 [@CL-CLS-0262; @CL-CLS-0265; @CL-CLS-0427]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-CLS-1517] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0262] |
| Applies to | No-cost settlement agreements for complete and partial terminations, and settlements of reservations [@CL-CLS-0262; @CL-CLS-0265; @CL-CLS-0427] |

The sections give agreement text for Block 14 of SF 30 [@CL-CLS-0262; @CL-CLS-0265; @CL-CLS-0427]. The text is bilateral: the contractor waives or releases, and the Government agrees or acknowledges [@CL-CLS-0263; @CL-CLS-0264; @CL-CLS-0267; @CL-CLS-0269]. The sections print no deadline and no dollar threshold. They do not say who decides to execute a no-cost agreement. FAR 49.402-4 lists, among the courses of action available to the contracting officer in lieu of termination for default when in the Government's interest, executing a no-cost termination settlement agreement if the requirement for the supplies and services no longer exists and the contractor is not liable to the Government for damages as provided in 49.402-7 [@CL-CLS-0258; @CL-CLS-0261]; see [Courses of action in lieu of termination for default](cls-default-termination-courses-of-action-in-lieu.md).

The FAR definitions section describes a partial termination as the termination of a part, but not all, of the work that has not been completed and accepted under a contract [@CL-CLS-1517]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines a partial termination as the termination of a part, but not all, of the work that has not been completed and accepted under a contract, the same in substance as the codified definition [@CL-CLS-1536; @CL-CLS-1517].

### Complete termination

The text goes in Block 14 of SF 30 if a no-cost settlement agreement under a complete termination is to be executed [@CL-CLS-0262]. The supplemental agreement either modifies the contract to reflect a no-cost settlement with respect to the dated Notice of Termination or, if the contract was not previously terminated, terminates the contract in its entirety [@CL-CLS-0423].

The contractor unconditionally waives any charges against the Government because of the termination of the contract. Except as set forth below, it releases the Government from all obligations under the contract or due to its termination [@CL-CLS-0263].

The Government agrees that all obligations under the contract are concluded, except for the reserved or excepted rights and liabilities that the agreement lists. The bracketed instruction refers to 49.109-2 and 49.603-1(b)(7) for what to list [@CL-CLS-0264].

### Partial termination

The text goes in Block 14 of SF 30 if a no-cost settlement agreement under a partial termination is to be executed [@CL-CLS-0265]. The supplemental agreement modifies the contract to reflect a no-cost settlement agreement with respect to the dated Notice of Termination [@CL-CLS-0266].

The parties specify the terminated portion by line item numbers, descriptions, quantity terminated, unit and total price of the terminated items, and any other explanation needed to avoid uncertainty or misunderstanding [@CL-CLS-0424].

The contractor unconditionally waives any charges against the Government arising under the terminated portion of the contract or by reason of its termination [@CL-CLS-0267]. The waiver includes, without limitation, all obligations of the Government to make further payments or to carry out any further undertakings under the terminated portion [@CL-CLS-0268].

The Government acknowledges that the contractor has no obligation to perform further work or services or to make further deliveries under the terminated portion [@CL-CLS-0269]. Under the terminated portion, the rights and liabilities of the parties that the agreement lists are reserved [@CL-CLS-0270]. Nothing in paragraph (b)(2) of the text affects any other covenants, terms, or conditions of the contract [@CL-CLS-0425].

### Settlement of reservations

The text goes in Block 14 of SF 30 for settlement of reservations [@CL-CLS-0427]. It follows an earlier supplemental agreement that settled the termination and excepted certain items [@CL-CLS-0426]. The new supplemental agreement settles the items listed in its paragraph (b) [@CL-CLS-0271].

The Government agrees to pay the contractor the amount stated for those items [@CL-CLS-0272]. The contractor releases and forever discharges the Government from all liability and from all existing and future claims and demands that it may have under the contract, insofar as it pertains to the contract, for those items [@CL-CLS-0273].

When payment is due the Government rather than the contractor, the words Government and Contractor are reversed in subparagraphs (b)(1) and (b)(2) [@CL-CLS-0274].

## How it works

| Agreement | What the contractor releases | What stays outside the release |
|---|---|---|
| Complete termination | Charges because of the termination, and, except as set forth below, all obligations under the contract or due to its termination [@CL-CLS-0263] | The reserved or excepted rights and liabilities that the agreement lists [@CL-CLS-0264] |
| Partial termination | Charges arising under the terminated portion or by reason of its termination, including the Government's obligations to make further payments or carry out further undertakings under that portion [@CL-CLS-0267; @CL-CLS-0268] | The reserved rights and liabilities that the agreement lists, and the other covenants, terms and conditions of the contract that paragraph (b)(2) does not affect [@CL-CLS-0270; @CL-CLS-0425] |
| Settlement of reservations | All liability and all existing and future claims and demands under the contract, insofar as it pertains to the contract, for the items in subparagraph (1) [@CL-CLS-0273] | Anything that is not an item in subparagraph (1) [@CL-CLS-0273] |

What the contractor keeps is what the release does not reach. Under a complete termination, that is whatever the agreement lists as excepted from the release [@CL-CLS-0263; @CL-CLS-0264]. Under a partial termination, it is the listed reserved rights and liabilities, and the paragraph does not affect any other covenants, terms, or conditions of the contract [@CL-CLS-0270; @CL-CLS-0425]. Under a settlement of reservations, it is every claim that is not for an item in subparagraph (1) [@CL-CLS-0273]. The lists keep rights and liabilities in place. The partial-termination text calls them rights and liabilities of the parties [@CL-CLS-0270], and the complete-termination text does not say whose they are [@CL-CLS-0264]. The text does not say who decides what goes on the list. It refers to 49.109-2 and 49.603-1(b)(7) for the listing [@CL-CLS-0264; @CL-CLS-0270]. This card does not restate those sections.

In a settlement of reservations, the Government pays the amount stated and the contractor gives the release [@CL-CLS-0272; @CL-CLS-0273]. Reversing the words in the two subparagraphs swaps the paying party and the releasing party [@CL-CLS-0272; @CL-CLS-0273; @CL-CLS-0274].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has five line items. Line items 3 and 4, worth 300,000 together, are terminated, and the parties sign a no-cost settlement agreement for the partial termination. The agreement lists those line items as the terminated portion, with their descriptions, quantities and unit and total prices [@CL-CLS-0424].

The contractor waives any charges arising under the terminated portion [@CL-CLS-0267]. That waiver includes the Government's obligations to make further payments under that portion [@CL-CLS-0268]. The Government acknowledges that the contractor owes no further work or deliveries under line items 3 and 4 [@CL-CLS-0269].

The agreement reserves two items on the terminated portion, a repair liability and a disputed storage charge. They stay outstanding because the agreement lists them [@CL-CLS-0270]. Line items 1, 2 and 5 continue, and paragraph (b)(2) does not affect any other covenants, terms, or conditions of the contract [@CL-CLS-0425].

Later the parties agree the storage charge is worth 40,000. A further supplemental agreement, which follows the earlier one that excepted the item, settles it [@CL-CLS-0426], the Government agrees to pay the contractor 40,000, and the contractor releases the Government for that item [@CL-CLS-0271; @CL-CLS-0272; @CL-CLS-0273]. The repair liability is not an item in subparagraph (1), so that release does not reach it [@CL-CLS-0273]. If 40,000 were instead due to the Government, the words Government and Contractor would be reversed [@CL-CLS-0274].

## Never confuse

A no-cost settlement does not mean that nothing stays open. The agreement reserves or excepts the rights and liabilities it lists [@CL-CLS-0264; @CL-CLS-0270].

A settlement of reservations is a later supplemental agreement. It settles items that an earlier agreement excepted, and it is not the original no-cost agreement [@CL-CLS-0426; @CL-CLS-0271].

The partial-termination waiver is stated for the terminated portion of the contract [@CL-CLS-0267].

The standard formats for a priced settlement are a different instrument; see [Settlement agreement formats for fixed-price contracts](cls-settlement-agreement-formats-fixed-price.md).
