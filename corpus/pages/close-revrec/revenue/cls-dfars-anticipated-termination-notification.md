## Purpose

Shows who the Contractor must notify, and by when, once the Contracting Officer says contract funding will be terminated or substantially reduced, and how the duty passes to subcontractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CLS-1157] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.249-7002 [@CL-CLS-1157] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | DEC 2022 [@CL-CLS-1616] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1157] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, which DFARS 249.7004 prescribes [@CL-CLS-1157] |

Within 2 weeks after the Contracting Officer notifies the Contractor that contract funding will be terminated or substantially reduced, the Contractor shall provide notice of the anticipated termination or reduction to the listed recipients [@CL-CLS-1158]. One listed recipient is each employee representative of the Contractor's employees whose work is directly related to the defense contract or, if there is no such representative, each such employee [@CL-CLS-1159]. The other listed recipients are the State or the entity it designates for rapid response activities, and the chief elected official of the local government unit where the adverse effect may occur [@CL-CLS-1160]. The notice given an employee has the same effect as a notice of termination to the employee for determining whether the employee is eligible for training, adjustment assistance, and employment services under the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act [@CL-CLS-0231] (29 U.S.C. Chapter 32) [@CL-CLS-1203].

The clause implements section 1372 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1994 and section 824 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1997 [@CL-CLS-1597], which are intended to help establish benefit eligibility under the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act for employees of DoD contractors and subcontractors adversely affected by contract terminations or substantial reductions under major defense programs [@CL-CLS-1202]. A major defense program, as the clause uses the term, is a program carried out to produce or acquire a major system as defined in section 3041(a) of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CLS-1218].

Not later than 60 days after the Contractor receives the Contracting Officer's notice, the Contractor shall take the subcontractor notice steps listed in the clause [@CL-CLS-1161]. One step is notice to each first-tier subcontractor whose subcontract equals or exceeds the threshold specified in DFARS 249.7003(c)(1) at the time of the notice [@CL-CLS-1162].

## How it works

The employee notice period runs from the Contracting Officer's notice to the Contractor, and the subcontractor period runs from the Contractor's receipt of that notice [@CL-CLS-1158; @CL-CLS-1161]. The flowdown has two parts. The Contractor must require each notified subcontractor to notify its own subcontractors at or above the threshold in DFARS 249.7003(c)(2)(i) [@CL-CLS-1163]. It must also require each of them to impose a similar notice and flowdown requirement on subcontractors at or above the threshold in DFARS 249.7003(c)(2)(ii) [@CL-CLS-1164].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor on a defense program is told that funding will be substantially reduced. Within two weeks it notifies the employee representative, the State rapid response entity and the local chief elected official. Not later than 60 days after receiving the Contracting Officer's notice it notifies a first-tier subcontractor holding a 5 million subcontract, assumed to be above the threshold. It also requires that subcontractor to notify its own subcontractors above the applicable threshold.

## Never confuse

This notice is not the termination notice itself: the notice of the anticipated termination or reduction is due within 2 weeks after the Contracting Officer notifies the Contractor that contract funding will be terminated or substantially reduced [@CL-CLS-1158]. The termination notice itself and the Contractor's duties after that notice are on [Prime contractor duties after a notice of termination](cls-prime-contractor-duties-after-termination-notice.md), [Termination authority, the notice of termination and the TCO's duties](cls-termination-authority-notice-and-tco-duties.md) and [Termination for convenience clauses and the contracts they go in](cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md). The clause on special termination costs is on [Special termination costs clause (DFARS)](cls-dfars-special-termination-costs-clause.md).
