## Purpose

Shows when congressional notification is required for a termination, what must happen before the termination notice or any information about a proposed termination reaches the contractor, and which contracts the DFARS requires to carry the anticipated termination notice clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CLS-1358] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 249.7001 and 249.7004 [@CL-CLS-1358; @CL-CLS-1359; @CL-CLS-1360; @CL-CLS-1361] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1358] |
| Applies to | Notification: a termination involving a reduction in employment of 100 or more contractor employees. Clearance: proposed terminations. Clause: contracts under a major defense program [@CL-CLS-1358; @CL-CLS-1359; @CL-CLS-1361] |

Congressional notification is required for any termination involving a reduction in employment of 100 or more contractor employees [@CL-CLS-1358]. Proposed terminations must be cleared through department or agency liaison offices before the termination notice, or any information on the proposed termination, is released to the contractor [@CL-CLS-1359]. The procedures at PGI 249.7001 are to be followed for congressional notification and release of information [@CL-CLS-1360].

The clause at 252.249-7002, Notification of Anticipated Contract Termination or Reduction, shall be used in all contracts under a major defense program [@CL-CLS-1361].

## How it works

The 100 figure is counted in contractor employees, and it measures the reduction in employment that the termination involves [@CL-CLS-1358]. Proposed terminations must be cleared through department or agency liaison offices before release of the termination notice, or any information on the proposed termination, to the contractor [@CL-CLS-1359].

The procedures at PGI 249.7001 are to be followed for congressional notification and release of information [@CL-CLS-1360].

The clause at 252.249-7002 is to be used in all contracts under a major defense program [@CL-CLS-1361]. The clause defines a major defense program as a program carried out to produce or acquire a major system as defined in section 3041(a) of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CLS-1218]. The clause's own notice duties are on [Notification of anticipated contract termination or reduction (DFARS)](cls-dfars-anticipated-termination-notification.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program office plans to terminate a production contract, and the termination would reduce employment by 140 contractor employees. The reduction is 100 or more, so congressional notification is required [@CL-CLS-1358]. The proposed termination is cleared through the department liaison office before the termination notice, or any information on the proposed termination, is released to the contractor [@CL-CLS-1359]. The procedures at PGI 249.7001 are followed for the notification and for release of information [@CL-CLS-1360].

If the contract is under a major defense program, it also carries the clause at 252.249-7002 [@CL-CLS-1361]. A second proposed termination would reduce employment by 60 contractor employees. The 100 figure is not met, so the notification sentence states no requirement for it, and the text does not say what applies below 100 [@CL-CLS-1358]. The clearance sentence names no such figure, so clearance before release is still stated for that proposed termination [@CL-CLS-1359].

## Never confuse

Proposed terminations must be cleared through department or agency liaison offices before release of the termination notice, or any information on the proposed termination, to the contractor [@CL-CLS-1359]. The clause at 252.249-7002 is to be used in all contracts under a major defense program [@CL-CLS-1361]. The clause itself is covered in [Anticipated termination notification](cls-dfars-anticipated-termination-notification.md). The termination notice is covered in [Termination authority, the notice of termination and the TCO's duties](cls-termination-authority-notice-and-tco-duties.md).

## Company calibration

Whether a proposed termination would reduce contractor employment by 100 or more [@CL-CLS-1358], and whether the program is a major defense program [@CL-CLS-1361]. [@Q-006]
