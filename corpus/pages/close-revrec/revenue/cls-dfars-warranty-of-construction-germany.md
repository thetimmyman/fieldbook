## Purpose

Shows how long the German construction warranty runs, what the Contractor must correct, and how the notice and remedy steps work.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CLS-1183] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.246-7002 [@CL-CLS-1183] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1183] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, which DFARS 246.710(2) prescribes [@CL-CLS-1206] |

The Contractor warrants, except as provided in paragraph (j) of the clause, that the work performed under the contract conforms to the contract requirements [@CL-CLS-1183]. The warranty also states that the work is free of any defect of equipment, material, or design furnished or workmanship performed by the Contractor or any subcontractor or supplier at any tier [@CL-CLS-1207]. The warranty continues for the period or periods specified in Section 13, VOB, Part B, commencing from the date of final acceptance of the work under the contract [@CL-CLS-1184]. If the Government takes possession of any part of the work before final acceptance, the warranty continues for those periods from the date the Government takes possession [@CL-CLS-1185]. The printed clause does not state the length of the period, and it points to that VOB section instead.

The Contractor shall remedy, at its own expense, any failure to conform or any defect [@CL-CLS-1186]. It shall also remedy, at its own expense, any damage to Government-owned or -controlled real or personal property when that damage is the result of the causes listed in the clause [@CL-CLS-1187]. The two listed causes are the Contractor's failure to conform to contract requirements and any defect of equipment, material, or design furnished or workmanship performed [@CL-CLS-1208]. The Contractor shall also restore any work damaged in fulfilling the terms and conditions of the clause [@CL-CLS-1209].

With respect to all warranties, express or implied, from subcontractors, manufacturers, or suppliers for work performed and materials furnished under the contract, the Contractor shall obtain all warranties that would be given in normal commercial practice [@CL-CLS-1210]. It shall also require all warranties to be executed in writing for the benefit of the Government if directed by the Contracting Officer, and enforce all warranties for the benefit of the Government as directed by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CLS-1211]. If the Contractor's warranty under paragraph (b) of the clause has expired, the Government may bring suit at its expense to enforce a subcontractor's, manufacturer's, or supplier's warranty [@CL-CLS-1212].

## How it works

The Contracting Officer shall notify the Contractor, in writing, within a reasonable period of time after the discovery of any failure, defect, or damage [@CL-CLS-1188]. If the Contractor fails to remedy any failure, defect, or damage within a reasonable period of time after receipt of notice, the Government has the right to replace, repair, or otherwise remedy it at the Contractor's expense [@CL-CLS-1189]. The printed clause does not define a reasonable period. Two limits apply: the Contractor is not liable for the repair of defects of material or design furnished by the Government unless a defect is caused by negligence of the Contractor or of a subcontractor or supplier at any tier [@CL-CLS-1190], and the warranty does not limit the Government's right under the Inspection clause for latent defects, gross mistakes, or fraud [@CL-CLS-1191].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A construction contract in Germany is finally accepted, and a defective drain is found later within the warranty period. The Contracting Officer notifies the contractor in writing. The contractor fixes the drain at its own expense, and also repairs the damage that the defect caused to Government property. If it had not acted within a reasonable time after the notice, the Government could repair the drain and charge the cost to the contractor.

## Never confuse

The warranty period here is set by reference to the VOB, and it starts at final acceptance or at possession if the Government takes part of the work earlier [@CL-CLS-1184; @CL-CLS-1185]. It is separate from the Government's Inspection clause rights for latent defects, gross mistakes, or fraud [@CL-CLS-1191]. General warranty clauses for construction are on [Warranty of services and warranty of construction](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-warranty-services-and-construction.md), warranty criteria and remedies are on [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-warranties.md), the offer-stage warranty tracking data is on [Warranty tracking notice and safety of facilities for military operations (DFARS)](cls-dfars-warranty-tracking-notice-and-military-safety.md), warranty cost is on [Service and warranty costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-service-and-warranty-costs.md), and disputes are on [Disputes clause, claims and certification](cls-disputes-clause.md).
