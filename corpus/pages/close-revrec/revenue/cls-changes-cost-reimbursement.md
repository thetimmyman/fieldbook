## Purpose

Shows what the Contracting Officer may change under the cost-reimbursement changes clause, how an equitable adjustment follows, and when the Contractor must assert its right to one.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-1075] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.243-2 [@CL-CLS-1075] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.243-2 [@CL-CLS-0028] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-1075] |
| Applies to | Contracts containing the Changes—Cost-Reimbursement clause [@CL-CLS-1075] |

The Contracting Officer may at any time, by written order and without notice to the sureties, if any, make changes within the general scope of the contract in any one or more of the areas the clause lists [@CL-CLS-1075]. The basic clause lists drawings, designs, or specifications when the supplies to be furnished are to be specially manufactured for the Government in accordance with them, the method of shipment or packing, and the place of delivery [@CL-CLS-1125]. The printed section also gives alternates that substitute a different paragraph (a): Alternate I applies if the requirement is for services and no supplies are to be furnished [@CL-CLS-1126], Alternate II if the requirement is for services and supplies are to be furnished [@CL-CLS-1127], and Alternate III if the requirement is for construction [@CL-CLS-1128].

The equitable adjustment provision applies where a change causes an increase or decrease in the estimated cost of, or the time required for, performance of any part of the work, whether or not changed by the order, or otherwise affects any other terms and conditions [@CL-CLS-1077]. The Contracting Officer shall make any such adjustment in the estimated cost, delivery or completion schedule, or both, in the amount of any fixed fee, and in other affected terms, and modifies the contract accordingly [@CL-CLS-1078].

The Contractor must assert its right to an adjustment within 30 days from the date of receipt of the written order [@CL-CLS-1079]. The prescription note states that this period may be varied according to agency procedures [@CL-CLS-1076]. If the Contracting Officer decides that the facts justify it, the Contracting Officer may receive and act upon a proposal submitted before final payment [@CL-CLS-1080].

## How it works

The Contracting Officer orders a change in writing within the general scope of the contract [@CL-CLS-1075]. If the change moves estimated cost or time, or affects other terms, the Contracting Officer adjusts the estimated cost, the schedule, the fixed fee and other affected terms [@CL-CLS-1077; @CL-CLS-1078]. Failure to agree to any adjustment shall be a dispute under the Disputes clause, and nothing in the clause excuses the Contractor from proceeding with the contract as changed [@CL-CLS-1081]. The claims process is on [Disputes clause, claims and certification](cls-disputes-clause.md). Notwithstanding the terms and conditions of paragraphs (a) and (b), the estimated cost, and any funds allotted if the contract is incrementally funded, shall not be increased or considered to be increased except by specific written modification [@CL-CLS-0226; @CL-CLS-1082]. Until the specific written modification described in the clause is made, the Contractor is not obligated to continue performance or incur costs beyond the point established in the Limitation of Cost or Limitation of Funds clause [@CL-CLS-1129].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.243-2 has the same equitable adjustment condition and the same adjustment in estimated cost, schedule, fixed fee and other terms [@CL-CLS-0028; @CL-CLS-0029; @CL-CLS-1077; @CL-CLS-1078]. It has the same 30-day assertion period and the same exception for a proposal submitted before final payment [@CL-CLS-0030; @CL-CLS-0031; @CL-CLS-1079; @CL-CLS-1080], and the same dispute rule [@CL-CLS-0032; @CL-CLS-1081]. It also has the same limits on increasing the estimated cost and allotted funds and on continuing performance [@CL-CLS-0033; @CL-CLS-0034; @CL-CLS-1082; @CL-CLS-1129], and the RFO paragraph also says the specific written modification indicates the new contract estimated cost and, if the contract is incrementally funded, the new amount allotted to the contract [@CL-CLS-0074], and its prescription also says the 30-day period may be varied according to agency procedures [@CL-CLS-0027; @CL-CLS-1076]. Paragraph (a) differs in wording: the RFO text says the Contracting Officer may issue a written order to make changes within the scope of the contract [@CL-CLS-0024], where the codified paragraph says within the general scope [@CL-CLS-1075]. The RFO list of areas prints drawings, designs, or specifications which require special manufacturing of supplies for the Government, method of shipment or packing, and place of delivery [@CL-CLS-0025], where the codified list words the first area as drawings, designs, or specifications when the supplies to be furnished are to be specially manufactured for the Government in accordance with them [@CL-CLS-1125]. The RFO text states in a separate subparagraph that the Contracting Officer does not need to notify any sureties of a written order [@CL-CLS-0026]. Alternates I, II and III open with the same conditions as the codified alternates [@CL-CLS-0035; @CL-CLS-1126; @CL-CLS-0036; @CL-CLS-1127; @CL-CLS-0037; @CL-CLS-1128].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-fixed-fee contract has an estimated cost of 2,000,000 and a fixed fee of 140,000. A written order changes a drawing, which adds 100,000 to the estimated cost and three weeks to the schedule. The Contractor asserts its right within the assertion period. The Contracting Officer adjusts the estimated cost, the schedule and, if affected, the fee, and modifies the contract. The estimated cost and any allotted funds rise only through that written modification.

## Never confuse

Under this clause the equitable adjustment is made in the estimated cost, delivery or completion schedule, the amount of any fixed fee and other affected terms [@CL-CLS-1078]. The fixed-price clause is on [Changes, Fixed-Price clause and equitable adjustment](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-changes-fixed-price-equitable-adjustment.md). The versions for other contract types are on [Changes clause for time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts](cls-changes-time-and-materials-labor-hours.md) and [Changes clause for construction contracts](cls-changes-construction.md). The estimated cost and any allotted funds are not increased or considered to be increased except by specific written modification [@CL-CLS-0226; @CL-CLS-1082], and the limit on spending is on [Limitation of Cost](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-limitation-of-cost.md).

The RFO text numbers the paragraph (a) list as (a)(1)(i) to (iii) and puts the sureties rule at (a)(2) [@CL-CLS-0025; @CL-CLS-0026], where the codified clause numbers the same list (a)(1) to (3) and puts the words on the sureties in the lead-in of paragraph (a) [@CL-CLS-1125; @CL-CLS-1075].
