## Purpose

States what the Government property part covers and which property and transactions it does not cover.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0323] |
| Codified citation | FAR 45.000 [@CL-CLS-0323] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0323] |
| Applies to | The subject matter of the part, less the exclusions in paragraph (b) [@CL-CLS-0323; @CL-CLS-0324; @CL-CLS-0325; @CL-CLS-0326; @CL-CLS-0327] |

Paragraph (a) says the part prescribes policies and procedures for three things: providing Government property to contractors, contractors' management and use of Government property, and reporting, redistributing and disposing of contractor inventory [@CL-CLS-0323].

Paragraph (b) lists what the part does not apply to. Government property provided under any statutory leasing authority is outside the part, except as to non-Government use of property under 45.301(f) [@CL-CLS-0324]. Property to which the Government has acquired a lien or title solely because of partial, advance, progress, or performance based payments is outside the part [@CL-CLS-0325]. Disposal of real property is outside the part, and so are software and intellectual property [@CL-CLS-0326].

The last exclusion covers Government property that is incidental to the place of performance. It applies when the contract requires contractor personnel to be located on a Government site or installation, and when the property used by the contractor within that location remains accountable to the Government [@CL-CLS-0327]. The section gives these examples of incidental items: office space, desks, chairs, telephones, computers, and fax machines [@CL-CLS-0453].

The section names no decision maker, deadline or threshold, and it states no duty on a named party. It is a scope rule only.

## How it works

Paragraph (a) fixes what the part is about [@CL-CLS-0323]. Paragraph (b) then removes particular property or transactions from the part [@CL-CLS-0324; @CL-CLS-0325; @CL-CLS-0326; @CL-CLS-0327].

Each exclusion is narrow. The leasing exclusion has its own exception for non-Government use of property under 45.301(f) [@CL-CLS-0324]. The payment exclusion reaches only a lien or title acquired solely because of the listed payments, so a lien or title that does not arise solely from those payments is not within this exclusion [@CL-CLS-0325]. The real property exclusion reaches the disposal of real property, not real property in general [@CL-CLS-0326].

The place-of-performance exclusion has two conditions that both must hold. The contract must require contractor personnel to be located on a Government site or installation, and the property used within that location must remain accountable to the Government [@CL-CLS-0327]. The list of examples is introduced by the words "for example", so the section does not present it as complete [@CL-CLS-0453].

For non-Government use of leased property under 45.301(f), see [Rent-free use and rental of Government property](../../industrialization/cash-returns/ind-rent-free-use-and-rental-government-property.md) [@CL-CLS-0324]. For what counts as Government property and contractor inventory, see [Definitions used for Government property](cls-government-property-definitions.md). For the contract clause itself, see [Government Property clause](cls-government-property-clause.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract requires 12 contractor staff to work in a Government building. They use 12 desktop computers there. Assume the computers are Government property and remain accountable to the Government. Computers are a listed example of incidental items, and both conditions of the place-of-performance exclusion hold, so the part does not apply to those computers [@CL-CLS-0453; @CL-CLS-0327].

The same contractor also keeps a machine at its own plant, where the contract does not require personnel to be located on a Government site or installation. The place-of-performance exclusion does not reach that machine [@CL-CLS-0327]. Whether the part applies to it depends on the subject matter in paragraph (a) and on the other exclusions in paragraph (b) [@CL-CLS-0323; @CL-CLS-0324; @CL-CLS-0325; @CL-CLS-0326].

The answer is that the 12 computers are outside the part, and the plant machine is not excluded by the place-of-performance exclusion.

## Never confuse

The place-of-performance exclusion is not an exclusion for everything located at a Government site. It applies only when the contract requires contractor personnel to be located there and the property used within the location remains accountable to the Government [@CL-CLS-0327].

The payment exclusion is narrower than a rule that all financed property leaves the part. It reaches only a lien or title acquired solely because of the listed payments [@CL-CLS-0325].

The exclusion for real property concerns disposal, not every use of real property. Software and intellectual property are a separate exclusion [@CL-CLS-0326].
