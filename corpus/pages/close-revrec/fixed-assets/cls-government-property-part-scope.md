## Purpose

States what the Government property part covers and which property and transactions it does not cover.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0323] |
| Codified citation | FAR 45.000 [@CL-CLS-0323] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0323] |
| Applies to | The subject matter of the part, less the exclusions in paragraph (b) [@CL-CLS-0323; @CL-CLS-0324; @CL-CLS-0325; @CL-CLS-0326; @CL-CLS-0327] |

Paragraph (a) states that FAR part 45 prescribes policies and procedures for providing Government property to contractors, contractors' management and use of Government property, and reporting, redistributing, and disposing of contractor inventory [@CL-CLS-0323].

Under paragraph (b), the part does not apply to (1) Government property provided under any statutory leasing authority, except as to non-Government use of property under 45.301(f) [@CL-CLS-0324]; (2) property to which the Government has acquired a lien or title solely because of partial, advance, progress, or performance based payments [@CL-CLS-0325]; (3) disposal of real property [@CL-CLS-0326]; or (4) software and intellectual property [@CL-CLS-0326].

Under paragraph (b)(5), the part also does not apply to Government property that is incidental to the place of performance, when the contract requires contractor personnel to be located on a Government site or installation, and when the property used by the contractor within the location remains accountable to the Government [@CL-CLS-0327]. Items considered to be incidental to the place of performance include, for example, office space, desks, chairs, telephones, computers, and fax machines [@CL-CLS-0453].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) prescribes policies and procedures for providing Government property to contractors, contractors' management and use of Government property, and reporting, redistributing, and disposing of contractor inventory [@CL-CLS-0323], while paragraph (b) lists five categories to which the part does not apply [@CL-CLS-0324; @CL-CLS-0325; @CL-CLS-0326; @CL-CLS-0327].

Under paragraph (b)(1)-(4), the part does not apply to Government property provided under any statutory leasing authority, except as to non-Government use of property under 45.301(f) [@CL-CLS-0324], property to which the Government has acquired a lien or title solely because of partial, advance, progress, or performance based payments [@CL-CLS-0325], disposal of real property, or software and intellectual property [@CL-CLS-0326].

Under paragraph (b)(5), the part does not apply to Government property that is incidental to the place of performance, when the contract requires contractor personnel to be located on a Government site or installation, and when the property used by the contractor within the location remains accountable to the Government [@CL-CLS-0327]. Items considered to be incidental to the place of performance include, for example, office space, desks, chairs, telephones, computers, and fax machines [@CL-CLS-0453].

For non-Government use of leased property under 45.301(f), see [Rent-free use and rental of Government property](../../industrialization/cash-returns/ind-rent-free-use-and-rental-government-property.md) [@CL-CLS-0324]. For what counts as Government property and contractor inventory, see [Definitions used for Government property](cls-government-property-definitions.md). For the contract clause itself, see [Government Property clause](cls-government-property-clause.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract requires 12 contractor staff to work in a Government building. They use 12 desktop computers there. Assume the computers are Government property and remain accountable to the Government. Computers are a listed example of incidental items, and both conditions of the place-of-performance exclusion hold, so the part does not apply to those computers.

The same contractor also keeps a machine at its own plant, where the contract does not require personnel to be located on a Government site or installation. The place-of-performance exclusion does not reach that machine. Whether the part applies to it depends on the subject matter in paragraph (a) and on the other exclusions in paragraph (b).

The answer is that the 12 computers are outside the part, and the plant machine is not excluded by the place-of-performance exclusion.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 45.000(b)(5), the part does not apply to Government property that is incidental to the place of performance, when the contract requires contractor personnel to be located on a Government site or installation, and when the property used by the contractor within the location remains accountable to the Government [@CL-CLS-0327].

Under FAR 45.000(b)(1)-(4), the part does not apply to Government property provided under any statutory leasing authority, except as to non-Government use of property under 45.301(f) [@CL-CLS-0324], property to which the Government has acquired a lien or title solely because of partial, advance, progress, or performance based payments [@CL-CLS-0325], disposal of real property, or software and intellectual property [@CL-CLS-0326].
