## Purpose

Specifies the contractor's duties under the Government Property clause for internal controls and authorized use, and the rules on title to property across contract types.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0581] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.245-1 [@CL-CLS-0581] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.245-1 [@CL-CLS-0001; @CL-CLS-0002; @CL-CLS-0003; @CL-CLS-0004; @CL-CLS-0005; @CL-CLS-0006; @CL-CLS-0007; @CL-CLS-0008; @CL-CLS-0009] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0581] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Government Property clause [@CL-CLS-0581] |

Under the clause, the Contractor shall have a system of internal controls to manage (control, use, preserve, protect, repair, and maintain) Government property in its possession, and the system shall be adequate to satisfy the requirements of the clause [@CL-CLS-0581].

The Contractor shall use Government property, either furnished or acquired under the contract, only for performing the contract, unless otherwise provided for in the contract or approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CLS-0582].

## How it works

The Government shall retain title to all Government-furnished property, and title to Government property shall not be affected by its incorporation into or attachment to any property not owned by the Government, nor shall Government property become a fixture or lose its identity as personal property by being attached to any real property [@CL-CLS-0583; @CL-CLS-1581]. Under fixed price type contracts, in the absence of financing provisions or other specific requirements for passage of title in the contract, the Contractor retains title to all property acquired by the Contractor for use on the contract, except for property identified as a deliverable end item [@CL-CLS-0584].

The paragraph on reimbursable property is headed Title under Cost-Reimbursement or Time-and-Material Contracts or Cost-Reimbursable line items under Fixed-Price contracts [@CL-CLS-0692]. Under it, title to all property purchased by the Contractor for which the Contractor is entitled to be reimbursed as a direct item of cost under the contract shall pass to and vest in the Government upon the vendor's delivery of such property [@CL-CLS-0585]. Title to all other property, the cost of which is reimbursable to the Contractor, shall pass to and vest in the Government upon (A) issuance of the property for use in contract performance, (B) commencement of processing of the property for use in contract performance, or (C) reimbursement of the cost of the property by the Government, whichever occurs first [@CL-CLS-0693; @CL-CLS-0586].

DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the FAR part 52 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.245-1, the Contractor shall have a system of internal controls to manage (control, use, preserve, protect, repair, and maintain) Government property in its possession, and the system shall be adequate to satisfy the requirements of the clause [@CL-CLS-0001], and the Contractor shall use Government property, either furnished or acquired under the contract, only for performing the contract, unless otherwise provided for in the contract or approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CLS-0002], matching the codified clause [@CL-CLS-0581; @CL-CLS-0582]. Under RFO FAR 52.245-1(e)(1)-(2), the Government shall retain title to all Government-furnished property [@CL-CLS-0003], title to Government property shall not be affected by its incorporation into or attachment to any property not owned by the Government, nor shall Government property become a fixture or lose its identity as personal property by being attached to any real property [@CL-CLS-0004], and under fixed price type contracts, in the absence of financing provisions or other specific requirements for passage of title in the contract, the Contractor retains title to all property acquired by the Contractor for use on the contract, except for property identified as a deliverable end item [@CL-CLS-0005], matching the codified clause [@CL-CLS-0583; @CL-CLS-1581; @CL-CLS-0584]. Under RFO FAR 52.245-1(e)(3), headed Title under Cost-Reimbursement or Time-and-Material Contracts or Cost-Reimbursable line items under Fixed-Price contracts [@CL-CLS-0006], title to all property purchased by the Contractor for which the Contractor is entitled to be reimbursed as a direct item of cost under the contract shall pass to and vest in the Government upon the vendor's delivery of such property [@CL-CLS-0007], and title to all other property, the cost of which is reimbursable to the Contractor, shall pass to and vest in the Government upon (A) issuance of the property for use in contract performance, (B) commencement of processing of the property for use in contract performance, or (C) reimbursement of the cost of the property by the Government, whichever occurs first [@CL-CLS-0008; @CL-CLS-0009], matching the codified clause [@CL-CLS-0692; @CL-CLS-0585; @CL-CLS-0693; @CL-CLS-0586].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor on a cost-reimbursement contract buys electronic parts for 50,000 as a direct item of cost, and the vendor delivers them to the contractor on Day 5. The answer: title to the parts passes to and vests in the Government when the vendor delivers them on Day 5, before the contractor bills for reimbursement.

## Never confuse

Under fixed price type contracts, in the absence of financing provisions or other specific requirements for passage of title in the contract, the Contractor retains title to all property acquired by the Contractor for use on the contract, except for property identified as a deliverable end item [@CL-CLS-0584], whereas under the paragraph on cost-reimbursement or time-and-material contracts or cost-reimbursable line items under fixed-price contracts [@CL-CLS-0692], title to all property purchased by the Contractor for which the Contractor is entitled to be reimbursed as a direct item of cost under the contract shall pass to and vest in the Government upon the vendor's delivery of such property [@CL-CLS-0585].
