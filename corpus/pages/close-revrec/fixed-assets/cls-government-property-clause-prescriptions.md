## Purpose

Shows which solicitation clauses the contracting officer inserts for Government property and in which situations.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0723] |
| Codified citation | FAR 45.107 [@CL-CLS-0723; @CL-CLS-0724; @CL-CLS-0725] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0723] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts in the listed situations [@CL-CLS-0723] |

Except as provided in paragraph (d) of that section, the contracting officer shall insert the Government Property clause in the solicitations and contracts that the paragraph lists [@CL-CLS-0723].

The first listed situation is all cost-reimbursement and time-and-material type solicitations and contracts, and labor-hour solicitations when property is expected to be furnished for the labor-hour contracts [@CL-CLS-0724]. The second is fixed-price solicitations and contracts when the Government will provide Government property [@CL-CLS-0725]. The third is contracts or modifications awarded under FAR Part 12 procedures where Government property that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101, is furnished or where the contractor is directed to acquire property for use under the contract that is titled in the Government [@CL-CLS-0726].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall use the clause with its Alternate I in contracts other than those identified in paragraph (a) of FAR 45.104, the section on responsibility and liability for Government property [@CL-CLS-0727]. That section is on [Responsibility and liability for Government property](cls-responsibility-liability-government-property.md). The contracting officer shall use the clause with its Alternate II when a contract for the conduct of basic or applied research at nonprofit institutions of higher education or at nonprofit organizations whose primary purpose is the conduct of scientific research is contemplated [@CL-CLS-0728]. The Installation Operation Services clause is prescribed for fixed-price service contracts to be performed on a Government installation when Government-furnished property will be provided for initial provisioning only and the Government is not responsible for repair or replacement [@CL-CLS-0729]. The Use and Charges clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when the Government Property clause is included [@CL-CLS-0731]. Purchase orders for property repair need not include a Government property clause when the unit acquisition cost of the property to be repaired does not exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, unless other Government property not for repair is provided [@CL-CLS-0732]. Listing the property in a solicitation is covered on [Offering Government property in a solicitation and evaluating its advantage](../../industrialization/investment-case/ind-offering-government-property-in-solicitations.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price contract has the Government supplying a test fixture. The answer: the Government Property clause goes in, because fixed-price contracts when the Government will provide property are a listed place. A separate purchase order to repair a fixture with a unit acquisition cost of 2,000, below the threshold, with no other property provided, need not include a Government property clause.

## Never confuse

A clause being prescribed is not the same as the duties it contains. This card covers only which situations call for which clause. Duties are on [Government Property clause](cls-government-property-clause.md), and rental use is on [Use and Charges clause for Government property](cls-use-and-charges-government-property.md).
