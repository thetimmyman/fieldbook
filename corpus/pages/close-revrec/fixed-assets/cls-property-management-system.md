## Purpose

Explains the clause that tests the contractor's system for managing and controlling Government property, and what follows when material weaknesses are found.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CLS-0101] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.245-7003 [@CL-CLS-0101] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0101] |
| Applies to | Contracts carrying the Contractor Property Management System Administration clause [@CL-CLS-0101] |

The contractor must establish and maintain an acceptable property management system, and the property management system is the contractor's system or systems for managing and controlling Government property [@CL-CLS-0101; @CL-CLS-0107].

The system must be in accordance with paragraphs (b) and (f) of the contract clause at Federal Acquisition Regulation 52.245-1 [@CL-CLS-0103].

Acceptable means compliance with the system criteria in the clause [@CL-CLS-0108].

## How it works

The contracting officer gives an initial written determination of material weaknesses, and the contractor shall respond within 30 days [@CL-CLS-0104] and, if it disagrees, state its rationale in writing [@CL-CLS-0162]. The final determination concerns remaining material weaknesses and the adequacy of any proposed or completed corrective action [@CL-CLS-0160], and system disapproval if one or more material weaknesses remain [@CL-CLS-0161].

A final determination follows, and the contractor has 45 days from receipt to correct the weaknesses or to submit an acceptable corrective action plan with milestones [@CL-CLS-0105].

If the final determination disapproves the system and the contract carries the Contractor Business Systems clause, payments are withheld in accordance with that clause [@CL-CLS-0106].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An initial determination lists 4 material weaknesses. The contractor replies within the 30 day window and fixes 3, so the final determination keeps 1. The contractor submits a corrective action plan within the 45 day window, and the system is later approved.

## Never confuse

This review is not the property record itself, and it is not a depreciation or capitalisation question.

It tests whether the management and control system meets criteria that reach the Government property clause requirements [@CL-CLS-0103].

Approval of the system is not the same as approval of individual property records.
