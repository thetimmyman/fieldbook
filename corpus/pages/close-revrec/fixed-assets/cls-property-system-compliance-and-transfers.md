## Purpose

Shows how the agency checks the contractor's property management system, how a written notice and corrective action plan lead to remedies, and when property may move from one contract to another.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CLS-0704] |
| Codified citation | FAR 45.105 and 45.106 [@CL-CLS-0704; @CL-CLS-0705; @CL-CLS-0709] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CLS-0704] |
| Applies to | Contractor property management systems and Government property moved between contracts [@CL-CLS-0704; @CL-CLS-0709] |

The agency responsible for contract administration shall conduct an analysis of the contractor's property management policies, procedures, practices, and systems, and this analysis shall be accomplished as frequently as conditions warrant, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CLS-0704].

Government property shall be transferred from one contract to another only when firm requirements exist under the gaining contract (see FAR 45.102), and such transfers shall be documented by modifications to both gaining and losing contracts [@CL-CLS-0709]. Once transferred, all property shall be considered Government-furnished property to the gaining contract [@CL-CLS-0710]. The warranties of suitability of use and timely delivery of Government-furnished property do not apply to property acquired or fabricated by the contractor as contractor-acquired property that is subsequently transferred to another contract with the same contractor [@CL-CLS-0711].

## How it works

The property administrator shall notify the contractor in writing when the contractor's property management system does not comply with contractual requirements, shall request prompt correction of deficiencies, and shall request from the contractor a corrective action plan, including a schedule for correction of the deficiencies [@CL-CLS-0705; @CL-CLS-0750]. If the contractor does not correct the deficiencies in accordance with the schedule, the contracting officer shall notify the contractor, in writing, that failure to take the required corrective action(s) may result in (1) revocation of the Government's assumption of risk for loss of Government property and/or (2) the exercise of other rights or remedies available to the contracting officer [@CL-CLS-0706; @CL-CLS-0707]. If the contractor fails to take the required corrective action(s) in response to the notification provided by the contracting officer in accordance with paragraph (b) of the section, the contracting officer shall notify the contractor in writing of any Government decision to apply the remedies described in paragraphs (b)(1) and (b)(2) of the section [@CL-CLS-0708; @CL-CLS-0751]. The clause that sets the system criteria and approval process is on [Contractor Property Management System Administration clause](cls-property-management-system.md). The rules on when property may be furnished at all are on [Contractor-furnished property as the default](../../industrialization/investment-case/ind-contractor-furnished-property-default.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A property administrator finds that a contractor's system does not comply and requests a corrective action plan with a correction schedule. The contractor misses the schedule, so the contracting officer sends a written warning that revocation of the Government's assumption of risk may follow. Separately, the contractor asks to move 500 units of Government property to a second contract that has no firm requirement for them. The answer: the transfer is not permitted until a firm requirement exists, and an approved transfer needs modifications to both contracts.

## Never confuse

Government property shall be transferred from one contract to another only when firm requirements exist under the gaining contract (see FAR 45.102), such transfers shall be documented by modifications to both gaining and losing contracts, and once transferred, all property shall be considered Government-furnished property to the gaining contract [@CL-CLS-0709; @CL-CLS-0710], although the warranties of suitability of use and timely delivery of Government-furnished property do not apply to property acquired or fabricated by the contractor as contractor-acquired property that is subsequently transferred to another contract with the same contractor [@CL-CLS-0711]. See [Contractor Property Management System Administration clause](cls-property-management-system.md).
