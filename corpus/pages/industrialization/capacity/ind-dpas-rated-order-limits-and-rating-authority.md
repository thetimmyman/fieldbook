## Purpose

Shows what may not be bought with a rated order, and how priority rating authority is requested for projects believed to maximize domestic energy supplies.

## The rule

| Applies to | Persons placing rated orders [@CL-IND-0176] |

A person may not place a rated order unless the person is in receipt of a rated order, has been explicitly authorized to do so by the Department of Commerce or a Delegate Agency, or is otherwise permitted to do so by the part [@CL-IND-0176]. Rated orders may not be used to obtain delivery on a date earlier than needed, or a greater quantity of the item than needed, except to obtain a minimum procurable quantity [@CL-IND-0177]. Separate rated orders may not be placed solely for obtaining minimum procurable quantities on each order if the minimum procurable quantity would be sufficient to cover more than one rated order [@CL-IND-0227]. Rated orders may not be used to obtain items in advance of the receipt of a rated order, except as specifically authorized by the Department of Commerce (see § 700.51(c) for information on obtaining authorization for priorities support in advance of receiving a rated order) [@CL-IND-0177; @CL-IND-0178].

Rated orders may not be used to obtain any of the following items unless specific priority rating authority has been obtained from a Delegate Agency or the Department of Commerce: items for plant improvement, expansion or construction, unless they will be physically incorporated into a construction project covered by a rated order; and production or construction equipment or items to be used for the manufacture of production equipment [@CL-IND-0177; @CL-IND-0179; @CL-IND-0180]. Notwithstanding any authorization or requirement to place a rated order stated elsewhere in the part, no person may place a rated order to obtain listed items, one of which is copper raw materials, unless such order is authorized by an official action of the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0181].

For projects believed to maximize domestic energy supplies, a person may request priority rating authority for scarce, critical and essential supplies of materials, equipment, and services (related to the production of materials or equipment, or the installation, repair, or maintenance of equipment) by submitting a request to the Department of Energy [@CL-IND-0182; @CL-IND-0183]. If the Department of Energy notifies the Department of Commerce that the project maximizes domestic energy supplies and that the materials, equipment, or services are critical and essential, the Department of Commerce must determine whether the items in question are scarce and whether there is a need to use the priorities authority [@CL-IND-0184; @CL-IND-0216]. The Department of Commerce will notify the Department of Energy of the results of its analysis, and if these two findings are satisfied, the Department of Commerce will authorize the Department of Energy to grant the use of a priority rating to the applicant [@CL-IND-0216; @CL-IND-0185].

Section 101(c) of the Defense Production Act authorizes the use of priority ratings for projects which maximize domestic energy supplies [@CL-IND-1015]. Projects which maximize domestic energy supplies include those which maintain or further domestic energy exploration, production, refining, and transportation; maintain or further the conservation of energy; or are involved in the construction or maintenance of energy facilities [@CL-IND-1016].

## How it works

A person may not place a rated order unless the person is in receipt of a rated order, has been explicitly authorized to do so by the Department of Commerce or a Delegate Agency, or is otherwise permitted to do so by the part [@CL-IND-0176]. Under 15 CFR 700.18(a), rated orders may not be used to obtain delivery earlier than needed, a greater quantity than needed except a minimum procurable quantity, items in advance of the receipt of a rated order except as specifically authorized by the Department of Commerce (see § 700.51(c)), or items such as plant improvement, expansion or construction items (unless physically incorporated into a construction project covered by a rated order) and production or construction equipment unless specific priority rating authority has been obtained from a Delegate Agency or the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0177; @CL-IND-0178; @CL-IND-0179; @CL-IND-0180]. Section 700.51 is on the [priority rating authority requests](ind-dpas-priority-rating-authority-requests.md) card [@CL-IND-0178]. For projects believed to maximize domestic energy supplies, a person submits a request to the Department of Energy, and if the Department of Energy notifies the Department of Commerce that the project maximizes domestic energy supplies and that the materials, equipment, or services are critical and essential and the Department of Commerce finds scarcity and a need to use the priorities authority, the Department of Commerce authorizes the Department of Energy to grant the use of a priority rating to the applicant [@CL-IND-0182; @CL-IND-0183; @CL-IND-0184; @CL-IND-0216; @CL-IND-0185].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A shop holds a rated order for 200 housings. It wants to buy a new press for its plant, and it also wants its steel delivered a month before it is needed. The answer: it may not use the rated order for the press unless it has specific priority rating authority, and it may not use the rated order to get delivery earlier than needed.

## Never confuse

15 CFR 700.18(a) lists items and uses for which rated orders may not be used, and 15 CFR 700.21 sets out the application to the Department of Energy for priority rating authority for scarce, critical and essential supplies of materials, equipment, and services for projects believed to maximize domestic energy supplies [@CL-IND-0177; @CL-IND-0182; @CL-IND-0183]. Under 15 CFR 700.18(a)(2)(iv), items for plant improvement, expansion or construction (unless physically incorporated into a construction project covered by a rated order) and production or construction equipment or items to be used for the manufacture of production equipment require specific priority rating authority from a Delegate Agency or the Department of Commerce, whereas under 15 CFR 700.18(b), notwithstanding any authorization or requirement to place a rated order stated elsewhere in the part, no person may place a rated order to obtain listed items such as copper raw materials unless such order is authorized by an official action of the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0177; @CL-IND-0179; @CL-IND-0180; @CL-IND-0181].
