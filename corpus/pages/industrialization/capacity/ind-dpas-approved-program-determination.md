## Purpose

Explains where a person sends the written request under 15 CFR 700.55 for a determination that a program is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense [@CL-IND-0270].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | A person requesting priority rating authority or requiring assistance in obtaining rated items under the part, for the activities that each paragraph lists [@CL-IND-0372; @CL-IND-0374; @CL-IND-0377] |

Each paragraph says that such a person should submit a request for a determination in writing that the request supports a program that is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense (an "approved program") [@CL-IND-0372; @CL-IND-0375; @CL-IND-0377; @CL-IND-0270].

The section has three paragraphs, one for each category of activity within the United States [@CL-IND-0269; @CL-IND-0272; @CL-IND-0274]. Each paragraph names the office to receive the request [@CL-IND-0271; @CL-IND-0273; @CL-IND-0275].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) is headed "Homeland security, emergency response, and critical infrastructure protection and restoration assistance programs within the United States," and its text covers homeland security, emergency preparedness, and critical infrastructure protection and restoration related activities [@CL-IND-0269; @CL-IND-0372]. The written request goes to the Office of Policy and Program Analysis, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security, 500 C Street SW, Washington, DC 20472, telephone (202) 212-2900, fax (202) 646-4601 [@CL-IND-0271; @CL-IND-0373].

Paragraph (b) is headed "Defense, space, and military assistance programs within the United States," and its text covers military production and construction, military use of civil transportation, stockpiles managed by the Department of Defense, space, and directly related activities [@CL-IND-0272; @CL-IND-0374; @CL-IND-0375]. The written request goes to the Department of Defense DPA Title I Lead, Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Industrial Base Policy, 3330 Defense Pentagon, Room 3B854, Washington, DC 20301, telephone (703) 697-0051, fax (703) 695-4885 [@CL-IND-0273; @CL-IND-0376].

Paragraph (c) is headed "Energy assistance programs within the United States," and its text covers energy production and construction, distribution and use, and directly related activities [@CL-IND-0274; @CL-IND-0377]. The written request goes to the Department of Energy, Attn: Office of Cybersecurity, Energy Security, and Emergency Response, 1000 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20585, telephone (202) 586-3832 [@CL-IND-0275; @CL-IND-0378].

Under 15 CFR 700.51(b)(2), priority rating authority may be used only to support approved programs, and if a person does not have priority rating authority from a Delegate Agency or the Department of Commerce, that person must request a determination by the appropriate Determination Department and the request must be approved before the person may place a rated order [@CL-IND-0244; @CL-IND-0245]. Determination Departments are defined on [the definitions card](ind-dpas-definitions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company wants authority to use a priority rating for a machine tool that supports a military production program within the United States.

Paragraph (b) covers military production and construction [@CL-IND-0374]. The company writes a request for a determination that the request supports a program that is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense [@CL-IND-0375; @CL-IND-0270]. It sends the request to the Department of Defense DPA Title I Lead, Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Industrial Base Policy, 3330 Defense Pentagon, Room 3B854, Washington, DC 20301 [@CL-IND-0273; @CL-IND-0376].

Under § 700.51(b)(2), priority rating authority may be used only to support approved programs [@CL-IND-0244].

A second company runs a pipeline project. It sends its request to the Department of Energy office that paragraph (c) names, not to the Defense Department office [@CL-IND-0377; @CL-IND-0275].

## Never confuse

Under 15 CFR 700.55, a person submits a written request for a determination that the request supports a program that is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense (an "approved program") [@CL-IND-0270]. Under 15 CFR 700.51(b)(2), priority rating authority may be used only to support approved programs [@CL-IND-0244]. See [the rating authority card](ind-dpas-priority-rating-authority-requests.md).

Each category names its own office, so a request for one category should go to the office that its paragraph names [@CL-IND-0271; @CL-IND-0273; @CL-IND-0275].
