## Purpose

Shows when a person may ask Commerce for relief from the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation, and the steps for an appeal when the request is denied.

| Applies to | The part and official actions issued under the part, except Directive Allocation Orders issued by a temporary final rule [@CL-IND-0198] |

The adjustments or exceptions section applies to the part and to official actions issued under the part, except for Directive Allocation Orders issued by a temporary final rule [@CL-IND-0198]. A person may submit a request to the Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security of the Department of Commerce for an adjustment or exception on the ground that a provision of the part or an official action results in an undue or exceptional hardship on that person not suffered generally by others in similar situations and circumstances, or that the consequence of following a provision of the part or an official action is contrary to the intent of the Defense Production Act, the Selective Service Act and related statutes, or the part [@CL-IND-0199; @CL-IND-0200; @CL-IND-0327; @CL-IND-0201]. Each request for adjustment or exception must be in writing and contain a complete statement of all the facts and circumstances related to the provision of the part or official action from which adjustment is sought and a full and precise statement of the reasons why relief should be provided [@CL-IND-0220]. The adjustment or exception process for Directive Allocation Orders issued by a temporary final rule is contained in each Directive Allocation Order (see supplement no. 1 to part 700, paragraph (c)) [@CL-IND-0223].

The Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security shall respond to requests for adjustment of or exceptions to compliance with the provisions of the part or an official action within 25 days, not including Saturdays, Sundays or Government holidays, of the date of receipt [@CL-IND-0202]. A request for adjustment or exception does not relieve any person from the obligation of complying with the provisions of the part or official action in question while the request is being considered, unless such interim relief is granted in writing by the Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security [@CL-IND-0203; @CL-IND-9001]. At 91 FR 50705, August 6, 2026, § 700.80 was amended, effective August 27, 2026, through August 27, 2027 [@CL-IND-0207].

Any person who has had a request for adjustment or exception denied by the Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security under § 700.80 may appeal to the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration of the Department of Commerce, who shall review and reconsider the denial [@CL-IND-0204]. An appeal of a denied request for adjustment or exception to compliance with the provisions of the part or an official action must be received by the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration no later than 45 days after receipt of a written notice of denial from the Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security [@CL-IND-0205; @CL-IND-0329]. After this 45-day period, an appeal may be accepted at the discretion of the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration [@CL-IND-0219]. The decision of the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration shall be made within a reasonable time after receipt of the appeal and shall be the final administrative action, and it shall be issued to the appellant in writing with a statement of the reasons for the decision [@CL-IND-0206; @CL-IND-0328]. The submission of an appeal does not relieve any person from the obligation of complying with the provisions of the part or official action in question while the appeal is being considered, unless such relief is granted in writing by the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration [@CL-IND-0224; @CL-IND-9002].

## How it works

A person may submit a written request to the Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security on one of the two grounds in § 700.80(a), with a complete statement of all the facts and circumstances related to the provision of the part or official action from which adjustment is sought and a full and precise statement of the reasons why relief should be provided, and remains obligated to comply with the provisions of the part or official action in question while the request is being considered unless interim relief is granted in writing by that Office [@CL-IND-0199; @CL-IND-0200; @CL-IND-0327; @CL-IND-0201; @CL-IND-0220; @CL-IND-0203; @CL-IND-9001]. The Office responds within 25 days, not including Saturdays, Sundays or Government holidays, of the date of receipt [@CL-IND-0202]. A person whose request under § 700.80 has been denied by the Office may appeal to the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration no later than 45 days after receipt of a written notice of denial from the Office, and after that 45-day period an appeal may be accepted at the discretion of the Assistant Secretary [@CL-IND-0204; @CL-IND-0205; @CL-IND-0329; @CL-IND-0219]. The submission of an appeal does not relieve any person from the obligation of complying with the provisions of the part or official action in question while the appeal is being considered unless relief is granted in writing by the Assistant Secretary, whose decision is made within a reasonable time after receipt of the appeal, is the final administrative action, and is issued to the appellant in writing with a statement of the reasons for the decision [@CL-IND-0224; @CL-IND-9002; @CL-IND-0206; @CL-IND-0328]. The adjustment or exception process for Directive Allocation Orders issued by a temporary final rule is contained in each Directive Allocation Order (see supplement no. 1 to part 700, paragraph (c)) [@CL-IND-0223]. Allocation orders are on [DPAS: allocation orders and their required elements](ind-dpas-allocation-orders.md), and audits and penalties are on [DPAS: audits, investigations, violations and penalties](ind-dpas-audits-investigations-and-penalties.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier believes a provision causes it an undue hardship that others in similar situations do not suffer. It writes to the Office and keeps filling its orders. The Office denies the request, and the supplier receives the written notice. The answer: the supplier may appeal to the Assistant Secretary, and the appeal should be received within the 45-day period counted from receipt of the notice.

## Never confuse

A request for an adjustment or exception is submitted to the Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security under § 700.80, whereas an appeal is submitted to the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration by a person whose request for adjustment or exception under § 700.80 has been denied by that Office [@CL-IND-0199; @CL-IND-0204]. Neither a request for adjustment or exception nor the submission of an appeal relieves any person from the obligation of complying with the provisions of the part or official action in question while the request or appeal is being considered, unless relief is granted in writing by the Office of Strategic Industries and Economic Security for a request or by the Assistant Secretary for Export Administration for an appeal [@CL-IND-0203; @CL-IND-9001; @CL-IND-0224; @CL-IND-9002].
