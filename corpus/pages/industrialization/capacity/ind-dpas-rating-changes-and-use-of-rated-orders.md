## Purpose

Shows how a priority rating on a rated order is changed or cancelled, and how a contractor uses its own rating to order the supplies it needs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | The priority rating on a rated order [@CL-IND-0169] |

The priority rating on a rated order may be changed or cancelled by an official action of the Department of Commerce or by written notification from the person who placed the rated order (including a Delegate Agency) [@CL-IND-0169]. An amendment to a rated order that significantly alters a supplier's original production or delivery schedule shall constitute a new rated order as of the date of its receipt, and the supplier must accept or reject the amended order according to the provisions of § 700.13 [@CL-IND-0170]. A person must cancel any rated orders that the person (or a predecessor in interest) has placed with suppliers, or cancel the priority ratings on those orders, if the person no longer needs the items in those orders to fill a rated order [@CL-IND-0171]. A person adding a rating to an unrated order, or changing or cancelling a priority rating, must promptly notify all suppliers to whom the order was sent of the addition, change or cancellation [@CL-IND-0225]. If an unrated order is amended so as to make it a rated order, or a DO rating is changed to a DX rating, the supplier must give the appropriate preferential treatment to the order as of the date the change is received by the supplier [@CL-IND-0226]. The following amendments do not constitute a new rated order: a change in shipping destination; a reduction in the total amount of the order; an increase in the total amount of the order which has negligible impact upon deliveries; a minor variation in size or design (prior to the start of production); or a change which is agreed upon between the supplier and the customer [@CL-IND-0215; @CL-IND-0228].

A person must use rated orders to obtain items which will be physically incorporated into other items to fill rated orders, including that portion of such items normally consumed, or converted into scrap or by-products, in the course of processing [@CL-IND-0172]. A person must also use rated orders to obtain maintenance, repair, and operating supplies (MRO) needed to produce the finished items to fill rated orders, and the priority rating used for MRO must contain the program identification symbol H7 along with the rating symbol on the customer's rated order (for example, a person in receipt of a DO-A3 rated order who needs MRO supplies would place a DO-H7 rated order with the person's supplier) [@CL-IND-0172; @CL-IND-0173]. A person may use a rated order to replace inventoried items (including finished items) that were used to fill rated orders, under the conditions that the order must be placed within 90 days of the date of use of the inventory, that a DO rating symbol and the program identification symbol indicated on the customer's rated order must be used on the order and a DX rating symbol may not be used even if the inventory was used to fill a DX rated order, and that if the priority ratings on rated orders from one customer or several customers contain different program identification symbols, the rated orders may be combined and the program identification symbol H1 must be used (i.e., DO-H1) [@CL-IND-0174; @CL-IND-0175; @CL-IND-0380]. A person is not required to place a priority rating on an order for less than $125,000, or one half of the Simplified Acquisition Threshold, whichever amount is greater, provided that delivery can be obtained in a timely fashion without the use of the priority rating [@CL-IND-0213; @CL-IND-0214].

## How it works

The priority rating on a rated order may be changed or cancelled by an official action of the Department of Commerce or by written notification from the person who placed the rated order (including a Delegate Agency) [@CL-IND-0169]. An amendment to a rated order that significantly alters a supplier's original production or delivery schedule constitutes a new rated order as of the date of its receipt, and the supplier must accept or reject the amended order according to the provisions of § 700.13 on [DPAS: acceptance, rejection, scheduling and flowdown of rated orders](ind-dpas-acceptance-rejection-and-flowdown.md), whereas the five amendments listed in § 700.16(d) do not constitute a new rated order [@CL-IND-0170; @CL-IND-0215; @CL-IND-0228]. A person must use rated orders to obtain physically incorporated items and MRO needed to produce the finished items to fill rated orders, and may use a rated order to replace inventoried items that were used to fill rated orders under the conditions in § 700.17(b) [@CL-IND-0172; @CL-IND-0173; @CL-IND-0174; @CL-IND-0175; @CL-IND-0380]. A person must cancel any rated orders that the person (or a predecessor in interest) has placed with suppliers, or cancel the priority ratings on those orders, if the person no longer needs the items in those orders to fill a rated order [@CL-IND-0171]. What may not be bought with a rated order is on [DPAS: limits on rated orders and requests for rating authority](ind-dpas-rated-order-limits-and-rating-authority.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds a DO-A3 rated order for 100 gearboxes and orders bearings for them with a DO-A3 rated order. It also orders maintenance supplies with a DO-H7 rating. The customer then cancels its order. The answer: the contractor no longer needs the items, so it must cancel the bearing order and the maintenance order, or their ratings.

## Never confuse

An amendment to a rated order that significantly alters a supplier's original production or delivery schedule constitutes a new rated order as of the date of its receipt, whereas a change in shipping destination, a reduction in the total amount of the order, an increase in the total amount of the order which has negligible impact upon deliveries, a minor variation in size or design prior to the start of production, or a change agreed upon between the supplier and the customer does not constitute a new rated order [@CL-IND-0170; @CL-IND-0215; @CL-IND-0228]. For MRO needed to produce the finished items to fill rated orders, the priority rating used must contain the program identification symbol H7 along with the rating symbol on the customer's rated order, whereas for an order replacing inventoried items used to fill rated orders, a DO rating symbol and the program identification symbol indicated on the customer's rated order must be used (or DO-H1 if rated orders with different program identification symbols are combined) and a DX rating symbol may not be used even if the inventory was used to fill a DX rated order [@CL-IND-0173; @CL-IND-0174; @CL-IND-0175; @CL-IND-0380].
