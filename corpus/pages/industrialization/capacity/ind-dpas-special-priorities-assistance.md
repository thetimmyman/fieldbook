## Purpose

Explains how a person gets special priorities assistance under 15 CFR 700.50 when a problem arises on a rated order after a priority rating has been authorized [@CL-IND-0229; @CL-IND-0276].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | A person who has a problem on a rated order and needs additional formal aid [@CL-IND-0230; @CL-IND-0232; @CL-IND-0234] |

Once a priority rating has been authorized, further action by the Department of Commerce generally is not needed [@CL-IND-0229]. The text anticipates that problems will occur from time to time [@CL-IND-0276]. Special priorities assistance is a service provided to alleviate the problems that do arise [@CL-IND-0231].

When problems occur, a person should immediately contact the appropriate contract administration officer or the Department of Commerce for guidance or assistance [@CL-IND-0230].

Requests for special priorities assistance should be timely, meaning the request has been submitted promptly and enough time exists for the Determination Department, Delegate Agency, or the Department of Commerce to effect a meaningful resolution to the problem [@CL-IND-1019; @CL-IND-1020]. A request must also establish that there is an urgent need for the item and that the applicant has made a reasonable effort to resolve the problem [@CL-IND-1019; @CL-IND-1020].

## How it works

The route depends on who placed the rated order. If a Delegate Agency placed it and additional formal aid is needed, assistance should be sought from the Delegate Agency through the contract administration officer [@CL-IND-0232]. If other authorized persons placed it and additional formal aid is needed, assistance should be sought from the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0234]. The Delegate Agency is defined on [the definitions card](ind-dpas-definitions.md).

A Delegate Agency decides whether it can resolve the problem or authorize the use of a priority rating. If it cannot and believes additional assistance is warranted, it may forward the request to the Department of Commerce for action [@CL-IND-0233]. The text says the Delegate Agency "may" forward; it does not say it must [@CL-IND-0233].

Commerce decides in the same way. If it is unable to resolve the problem or to authorize the use of a priority rating and believes additional assistance is warranted, it may forward the request to another agency identified in § 700.10(c), as appropriate, for action [@CL-IND-0236].

Assistance may be provided for any reason consistent with the part [@CL-IND-0235]. The text gives two examples: assisting in obtaining timely deliveries of items needed to satisfy rated orders, and authorizing the use of priority ratings on orders to obtain items not otherwise ratable under the part [@CL-IND-0235]. Section 700.54 says assistance is provided at the discretion of the Determination Departments, Delegate Agencies and the Department of Commerce when it is determined that such assistance is warranted to meet the objectives of the regulation [@CL-IND-0266]. Usual situations and the situations where assistance may not be provided are on [the examples card](ind-dpas-assistance-examples-and-exclusions.md).

The sentence is in the passive and names no submitter. A request must be submitted on Form BIS-999 (OMB control number 0694-0057) to the local contract administration representative or to the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0237]. Paragraph (a)(1) names the contract administration officer, and paragraph (c) names the local contract administration representative [@CL-IND-0232; @CL-IND-0237]. Form BIS-999 may be obtained from the Delegate Agency representative or from the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0238]. A sample form is attached at appendix I, and a fillable form may be obtained from the website that the paragraph gives [@CL-IND-0239]. Section 700.93 adds that requests for special priorities assistance under § 700.50, adjustments or exceptions under § 700.80, or appeals under § 700.81 must be submitted in the manner specified in those sections [@CL-IND-1035].

A request for authority to use a priority rating is a separate request, covered on [the rating authority card](ind-dpas-priority-rating-authority-requests.md). Letters of understanding in assistance cases are covered on [that card](ind-dpas-letters-of-understanding.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company holds a rated order that a Delegate Agency placed. Its supplier will not confirm a delivery date, and the company needs additional formal aid.

Because a Delegate Agency placed the order, the company seeks assistance from that agency through the contract administration officer [@CL-IND-0232]. It completes Form BIS-999 and submits it to the local contract administration representative [@CL-IND-0237].

The Delegate Agency cannot resolve the problem. It believes more assistance is warranted, so it forwards the request to the Department of Commerce for action [@CL-IND-0233]. Commerce resolves the problem and the supplier confirms a date. Had Commerce been unable to resolve it, Commerce could have forwarded the request to another agency identified in § 700.10(c) [@CL-IND-0236].

If other authorized persons had placed the order, assistance should have been sought from Commerce [@CL-IND-0234].

## Never confuse

A priority rating is an identifying code placed on rated orders [@CL-IND-0076]. Special priorities assistance is the service provided when a problem arises on such an order, and once a rating is authorized further action by Commerce generally is not needed [@CL-IND-0229; @CL-IND-0231].

The text points to the Delegate Agency route or the Commerce route according to who placed the rated order [@CL-IND-0232; @CL-IND-0234]. Paragraph (c) nonetheless lets the request go to the local contract administration representative or to the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0237].

Assistance may be provided for any reason consistent with the part, but under § 700.54 it is provided at the discretion of the Determination Departments, Delegate Agencies and the Department of Commerce when it is determined to be warranted, and the request must still be submitted on Form BIS-999 [@CL-IND-0235; @CL-IND-0266; @CL-IND-0237]. Section 700.54 is headed "Instances where assistance will not be provided" [@CL-IND-0278], but its text lists examples where assistance may not be provided, such as situations when a person is attempting to secure a price advantage or gain competitive advantage [@CL-IND-0267].
