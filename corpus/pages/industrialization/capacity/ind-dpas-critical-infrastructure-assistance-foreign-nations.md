## Purpose

Shows how a person in a foreign nation, or representing an international organization, asks for priority rating authority or help to obtain items in the United States for critical infrastructure protection and restoration, and which office takes each request.

| Applies to | Persons in foreign nations or representing international organizations that need items bought in the United States for critical infrastructure protection and restoration [@CL-IND-0302; @CL-IND-0304] |

15 CFR 700.58 provides for authorizing priority ratings to persons in foreign nations or international organizations to obtain items in the United States in support of approved programs [@CL-IND-0301].

The first request. A person in a foreign nation or representing an international organization that needs help to obtain items under this part, bought in the United States for critical infrastructure protection and restoration, should submit a request for priority rating authority on Form BIS-999 to the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0302; @CL-IND-0303]. The paragraph points to the section that describes the form [@CL-IND-0303].

The second request. A person in a foreign nation or representing an international organization that requests priority rating authority, or needs help to obtain rated items under this part, in support of critical infrastructure protection and restoration related activities should submit a request for a determination in writing [@CL-IND-0304]. The determination asked for is that the request supports a program that is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense, and the request goes to the Office of Policy and Program Analysis, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security [@CL-IND-0305]. The second paragraph then gives the office's address, telephone, fax, email and website [@CL-IND-0305; @CL-IND-0324].

## How it works

The requester starts each request, and each duty is stated as "should" [@CL-IND-0303; @CL-IND-0305]. Commerce receives the request for priority rating authority [@CL-IND-0303]. The Federal Emergency Management Agency office receives the request for the written determination [@CL-IND-0305]. The section does not say who grants the rating authority or makes the determination, does not give the test for either, and states no deadline or dollar threshold. It does not say whether one request must come before the other.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A foreign nation needs 200 replacement transformers for a grid restoration program and buys them in the United States. It submits a Form BIS-999 request for priority rating authority to the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0303]. It also submits a request for a written determination that the request supports a program necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense to the Federal Emergency Management Agency office [@CL-IND-0305]. The section does not say what either office decides or when, so the nation cannot tell from this section whether or when a rating will be authorized.

## Never confuse

The two requests are different. One asks Commerce for priority rating authority, and the other asks the Federal Emergency Management Agency office for a written determination [@CL-IND-0303; @CL-IND-0305]. This route is also not the military assistance route for other nations and international organizations, where the request goes to the Department of Defense Lead [@CL-IND-0294]. The general determination request is on [Requests for a determination that a program is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense](ind-dpas-approved-program-determination.md).

## Company calibration

Confirm that the program a foreign customer cites for a rated order is a critical infrastructure protection or restoration program, and which of the two requests has been made. [@Q-031]
