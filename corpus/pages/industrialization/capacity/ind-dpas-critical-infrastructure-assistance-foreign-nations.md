## Purpose

Shows how a person in a foreign nation, or representing an international organization, asks for priority rating authority or help to obtain items in the United States for critical infrastructure protection and restoration, and which office takes each request.

To promote critical infrastructure assistance to foreign nations, 15 CFR 700.58 provides for authorizing priority ratings to persons in foreign nations or international organizations (for example, the North Atlantic Treaty Organization or the United Nations) to obtain items in the United States in support of approved programs [@CL-IND-0332; @CL-IND-0301].

Any person in a foreign nation or representing an international organization may request assistance in obtaining items under the part to be purchased in the United States for use in critical infrastructure protection and restoration by submitting a request for priority rating authority on Form BIS-999 to the Department of Commerce (see § 700.50 in the part) [@CL-IND-0302; @CL-IND-0303].

Any person in a foreign nation or representing an international organization requesting priority rating authority or requiring assistance in obtaining rated items under the part in support of critical infrastructure protection and restoration related activities should submit a request for a determination in writing that the request supports a program that is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense to the Office of Policy and Program Analysis, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security, 500 C Street SW, Washington, DC 20472, telephone (202) 212-2900, fax (202) 646-4601 [@CL-IND-0304; @CL-IND-0305; @CL-IND-0324].

## How it works

Under 15 CFR 700.58(b)(1), a person in a foreign nation or representing an international organization that needs assistance in obtaining items to be purchased in the United States for use in critical infrastructure protection and restoration submits a request for priority rating authority on Form BIS-999 to the Department of Commerce (see § 700.50 in the part) [@CL-IND-0302; @CL-IND-0303]. Under 15 CFR 700.58(b)(2), a person in a foreign nation or representing an international organization requesting priority rating authority or requiring assistance in obtaining rated items in support of critical infrastructure protection and restoration related activities submits a request for a determination in writing that the request supports a program that is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense to the Office of Policy and Program Analysis, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security [@CL-IND-0304; @CL-IND-0305].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A foreign nation needs 200 replacement transformers for a grid restoration program and buys them in the United States. It submits a Form BIS-999 request for priority rating authority to the Department of Commerce [@CL-IND-0303]. It also submits a request for a written determination that the request supports a program necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense to the Federal Emergency Management Agency office [@CL-IND-0305].

## Never confuse

Under 15 CFR 700.58, a request for priority rating authority on Form BIS-999 goes to the Department of Commerce, and a request for a written determination that the request supports a program that is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense goes to the Office of Policy and Program Analysis, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security [@CL-IND-0302; @CL-IND-0303; @CL-IND-0304; @CL-IND-0305]. Under 15 CFR 700.57, a person in a foreign nation other than Canada, or in an international organization, requiring assistance in obtaining items in the United States or priority rating authority for items to be purchased in the United States submits a request to the Department of Defense DPA Title I Lead, Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Industrial Base Policy [@CL-IND-0293; @CL-IND-0294]. The domestic determination request is on [Requests for a determination that a program is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense](ind-dpas-approved-program-determination.md).
