## Purpose

Shows what a public company discloses about its principal physical properties, including their suitability, adequacy, productive capacity and use. The item is addressed to the registrant.

## The rule

|---|---|

| Applies to | The registrant's principal physical properties, to the extent material [@CL-IND-0043] |

Item 102 of Regulation S-K, which is 17 CFR 229.102, is addressed to the registrant [@CL-IND-0043].

To the extent material, the registrant is to disclose the location and general character of its principal physical properties [@CL-IND-0043]. It is also to identify the segments, as reported in the financial statements, that use the properties described [@CL-IND-0044]. If a property is not held in fee or is held subject to a material encumbrance, the registrant shall so state and describe briefly how it is held [@CL-IND-0045].

## How it works

The first instruction says the item requires information that will reasonably inform investors as to the suitability, adequacy, productive capacity, and extent of utilization of the principal physical properties of the registrant and its subsidiaries, to the extent the described properties are material [@CL-IND-0046]. Descriptions may be provided on a collective basis if appropriate, and detailed descriptions of the physical characteristics of individual properties or legal descriptions by metes and bounds are not required and shall not be given [@CL-IND-0047]. In determining materiality, the registrant should take into account both quantitative and qualitative factors [@CL-IND-0048]. The cost treatment of idle capacity is a separate question, on [Idle facilities and idle capacity costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-idle-facilities-and-capacity.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A public company owns one plant outright and leases a second. Its filing describes where each plant is and what it makes, names the segment that uses each one, and says the second plant is leased and briefly how it is held. It also states that the owned plant runs well below its productive capacity. The answer: the filing informs investors about suitability, adequacy, productive capacity and utilization without legal descriptions of the land.

## Never confuse

Item 102 is a disclosure by a registrant that is meant to inform investors [@CL-IND-0043; @CL-IND-0046]. It is not a cost principle: whether idle capacity costs are allowable under a Government contract is covered on [Idle facilities and idle capacity costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-idle-facilities-and-capacity.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company is an SEC registrant and, if so, how its property disclosure describes capacity and utilization. [@Q-031]
