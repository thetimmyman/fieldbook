## Purpose

Shows what a registrant discloses under Item 102 of Regulation S-K about its principal physical properties, including their suitability, adequacy, productive capacity and extent of utilization [@CL-IND-0043; @CL-IND-0046].

## The rule

| Applies to | The registrant's principal physical properties, to the extent material [@CL-IND-0043] |

To the extent material, the registrant must disclose the location and general character of the registrant's principal physical properties [@CL-IND-0043]. In addition, the registrant must identify the segment(s), as reported in the financial statements, that use the properties described [@CL-IND-0044]. If any such property is not held in fee or is held subject to an encumbrance that is material to the registrant, the registrant must so state and describe briefly how held [@CL-IND-0045].

## How it works

Instruction 1 to Item 102 states that a registrant should engage in a comprehensive consideration of the materiality of its properties [@CL-IND-0381]. This item requires information that will reasonably inform investors as to the suitability, adequacy, productive capacity, and extent of utilization of the principal physical properties of the registrant and its subsidiaries, to the extent the described properties are material [@CL-IND-0046]. Detailed descriptions of the physical characteristics of individual properties or legal descriptions by metes and bounds are not required and shall not be given, and disclosure may be provided on a collective basis, if appropriate [@CL-IND-0047]. Instruction 2 to Item 102 states that in determining materiality, a registrant should take into account both quantitative and qualitative factors, and refers to Instruction 1 to Item 101 of Regulation S-K (§ 229.101) [@CL-IND-0048; @CL-IND-0382]. Idle facilities and idle capacity costs are on [Idle facilities and idle capacity costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-idle-facilities-and-capacity.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A public company owns one plant outright and leases a second. Its filing describes where each plant is and what it makes, names the segment that uses each one, and says the second plant is leased and briefly how it is held. It also states that the owned plant runs well below its productive capacity. The answer: the filing informs investors about suitability, adequacy, productive capacity and utilization without legal descriptions of the land.

## Never confuse

Item 102 requires disclosure, to the extent material, of the location and general character of the registrant's principal physical properties and requires information that will reasonably inform investors as to the suitability, adequacy, productive capacity, and extent of utilization of the principal physical properties of the registrant and its subsidiaries, to the extent the described properties are material [@CL-IND-0043; @CL-IND-0046]. Idle facilities and idle capacity costs are covered on [Idle facilities and idle capacity costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-idle-facilities-and-capacity.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company is an SEC registrant and, if so, how its property disclosure describes capacity and utilization. [@Q-031]
