## Purpose

Collects the defined terms that the priority, rating and allocation cards use, so that a rating, an allocation order or a program reference is read against one vocabulary. The [priority ratings and rated orders](ind-dpas-rated-orders.md) card and the [allocation orders](ind-dpas-allocation-orders.md) card apply these terms.

| Applies to | Rated orders, allocation orders and the approved programs they reference [@CL-IND-0079; @CL-IND-0064] |

The definitions in 15 CFR 700.8 apply throughout the part [@CL-IND-0063].

Construction is the erection, addition, extension, or alteration of any building, structure, or project, using materials or products which are to be an integral and permanent part of it, and does not include maintenance and repair [@CL-IND-0068]. Facilities includes all types of buildings, structures, or other improvements to real property, excluding farms, churches or other houses of worship, and private dwelling houses, and services relating to the use of any such building, structure, or other improvement [@CL-IND-0355]. Industrial resources are all materials, services, and facilities, including construction materials, the authority for which has not been delegated to other agencies under Executive Order 13603 [@CL-IND-0073]. Production equipment is any item of capital equipment used in producing materials or furnishing services that has a unit acquisition cost of $2,500 or more, an anticipated service life in excess of one year, and the potential for maintaining its integrity as a capital item [@CL-IND-0077]. Maintenance and repair and/or operating supplies (MRO) does not include items produced or obtained for sale to other persons or for installation upon or attachment to the property of another person, or items required for the production of such items; items needed for the replacement of any plant, facility, or equipment; or items for the improvement of any plant, facility, or equipment by replacing items which are still in working condition with items of a new or different kind, quality, or design [@CL-IND-0360; @CL-IND-0361].

At 91 FR 50704, August 6, 2026, § 700.8 was amended, effective August 27, 2026, through August 27, 2027 [@CL-IND-0371].

## How it works

A priority rating is an identifying code, consisting of the rating symbol and the program identification symbol, assigned by the Department of Commerce, a Delegate Agency, or authorized person and placed on all rated orders [@CL-IND-0076]. A rated order is a prime contract, a subcontract, or a purchase order in support of an approved program issued in accordance with the provisions of the part [@CL-IND-0079]. The rated order duties are on the [rated orders](ind-dpas-rated-orders.md) card.

An allocation order is an official action to control the distribution of materials, services, or facilities for a purpose deemed necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense [@CL-IND-0064]. A set-aside is an official action that requires a person to reserve materials, services, or facilities capacity in anticipation of the receipt of rated orders [@CL-IND-0081]. A directive is an official action which requires a person to take or refrain from taking certain actions in accordance with its provisions [@CL-IND-0072]. An allotment is an official action that specifies the maximum quantity of a material, service, or facility authorized for a specific use to promote the national defense [@CL-IND-0065]. The [allocation orders](ind-dpas-allocation-orders.md) card and the [allocation order policy and acceptance](ind-dpas-allocation-order-policy-and-acceptance.md) card apply these terms.

A Determination Department is any of the three Federal departments whose head is delegated authority by the President under section 202 of Executive Order 13603 to determine in writing that a program is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense [@CL-IND-0071]. An approved program is a program determined in writing as necessary or appropriate for priorities and allocations support to promote the national defense by the Secretary of Defense, the Secretary of Energy, or the Secretary of Homeland Security, under the authority of the Defense Production Act and Executive Order 13603, or the Selective Service Act and Executive Order 12742 [@CL-IND-0066; @CL-IND-0067]. A Delegate Agency is a government agency authorized by delegation from the Department of Commerce to place priority ratings on contracts or orders needed to support approved programs [@CL-IND-0070].

Person means any individual, corporation, partnership, association, or any other organized group of persons, or legal successor or representative thereof; or any authorized State or local government or agency thereof; and for purposes of administration of the part, includes the United States Government and any authorized foreign government or international organization or agency thereof, delegated authority as provided in the part (see supplement no. 1 to part 700, paragraph (b) for Directive Allocation Orders issued by a temporary final rule) [@CL-IND-0367; @CL-IND-0368]. Working day means any day that the recipient of an order is open for business [@CL-IND-0082].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A plant buys a press for $3,000 to use in producing parts, and expects to run it for four years as a capital machine. It meets the $2,500 cost test, its service life exceeds one year, and it can maintain its integrity as a capital item, so it is production equipment [@CL-IND-0077]. A hand tool bought for $1,500 for the same line fails the cost test, so it is not production equipment [@CL-IND-0077]. If the plant carries the tool as an operating supply under its established accounting practice, it is an operating supply, and operating supplies may include hand tools [@CL-IND-0359]. If the plant buys a new press to replace a worn press, the new press is an item needed for the replacement of equipment, and MRO does not include it [@CL-IND-0361].

## Never confuse

A rated order is a prime contract, a subcontract, or a purchase order in support of an approved program issued in accordance with the provisions of the part, whereas an allocation order is an official action to control the distribution of materials, services, or facilities for a purpose deemed necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense [@CL-IND-0079; @CL-IND-0064]. Allocation means the control of the distribution of materials, services, or facilities for a purpose deemed necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense, and an allotment is an official action that specifies the maximum quantity of a material, service, or facility authorized for a specific use to promote the national defense [@CL-IND-0063; @CL-IND-0065]. A Determination Department is any of the three Federal departments whose head is delegated authority by the President under section 202 of Executive Order 13603 to determine in writing that a program is necessary or appropriate to promote the national defense, and a Delegate Agency is a government agency authorized by delegation from the Department of Commerce to place priority ratings on contracts or orders needed to support approved programs [@CL-IND-0071; @CL-IND-0070]. In 15 CFR 700.8, national defense means programs for military and energy production or construction, military or critical infrastructure assistance to any foreign nation, homeland security, stockpiling, space, and any directly related activity, and includes emergency preparedness activities conducted under Title VI of the disaster relief and emergency assistance statute codified at section 5121 and following of title 42 of the United States Code and critical infrastructure protection and restoration [@CL-IND-0364; @CL-IND-0365]. In codified FAR 2.101, national defense means any activity related to programs for military or atomic energy production or construction, military assistance to any foreign nation, stockpiling, or space, except that for use in Subpart 11.6, see the definition in 11.601 [@CL-IND-1059]. In RFO FAR 2.101, national defense means any activity related to programs for military or atomic energy production or construction, military assistance to any foreign nation, stockpiling, or space, except that for use in subpart 11.6, see the definition in 11.601 [@CL-IND-1070]. A directive is an official action which requires a person to take or refrain from taking certain actions in accordance with its provisions, and official actions include the issuance of rating authorizations, directives, letters of understanding, demands for information, inspection authorizations, administrative subpoenas, and allocation orders [@CL-IND-0072; @CL-IND-0366].
