## Purpose

Shows who bears the cost of materials and production before first article approval, how a contracting officer can authorize early spending, and what the solicitation and administration steps are.

## The rule

Before first article approval, the acquisition of materials or components, or commencement of production, is normally at the sole risk of the contractor [@CL-IND-0129]. To minimize this risk, the contracting officer shall provide sufficient time in the delivery schedule for acquisition of materials and components, and for production after receipt of first article approval [@CL-IND-0153].

When Government requirements preclude giving that time, the contracting officer may, before approval of the first article, authorize the contractor to acquire specific materials or components or commence production to the extent essential to meet the delivery schedule [@CL-IND-0130; @CL-IND-0153]. Costs incurred based on that authorization are allocable to the contract for progress payments and for termination settlements if the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government [@CL-IND-0131; @CL-IND-0130].

## How it works

Before first article approval, the acquisition of materials or components, or commencement of production, is normally at the sole risk of the contractor [@CL-IND-0129]. To minimize this risk, the contracting officer shall provide sufficient time in the delivery schedule for acquisition of materials and components, and for production after receipt of first article approval, and when Government requirements preclude this action, the contracting officer may, before approval of the first article, authorize the contractor to acquire specific materials or components or commence production to the extent essential to meet the delivery schedule [@CL-IND-0153; @CL-IND-0130]. Costs incurred based on this authorization are allocable to the contract for progress payments and for termination settlements if the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government [@CL-IND-0131]. In the circumstance where the contractor is to be responsible for the first article approval testing, solicitations containing a testing and approval requirement shall provide the performance or other characteristics that the first article must meet for approval, the detailed technical requirements for the tests that must be performed for approval, and the necessary data that must be submitted to the Government in the first article approval test report, among other solicitation requirements [@CL-IND-0132; @CL-IND-0399]. The Government laboratory or other activity responsible for first article testing or evaluation shall inform the contracting office whether to approve, conditionally approve, or disapprove the first article [@CL-IND-0133]. The contracting officer shall then notify the contractor of the action taken and furnish a copy of the notice to the contract administration office, and the notice shall include the first article shipment number, when available, and the applicable line item number [@CL-IND-9006; @CL-IND-0400]. Any changes in the drawings, designs, or specifications determined by the contracting officer to be necessary shall be made under the Changes clause, and not by the notice of approval, conditional approval, or disapproval furnished the contractor [@CL-IND-0401]. When testing and approval is used, and when it may be waived, are on [First article testing and approval](ind-first-article-testing-and-approval.md). Progress payment inventory and termination costs are covered on [Government title to progress payment inventory](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-government-title-progress-payment-inventory.md) and [Termination costs](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A delivery schedule leaves no time to buy long-lead material after first article approval. The contracting officer authorizes the contractor to buy 2 million of specific material before approval, to the extent essential to meet the delivery schedule. The answer: without that authorization, the purchase is normally at the contractor's sole risk. With it, the 2 million is allocable to the contract for progress payments and, if the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government, for termination settlements.

## Never confuse

An authorization before approval of the first article to acquire specific materials or components or commence production to the extent essential to meet the delivery schedule makes costs incurred based on that authorization allocable to the contract for progress payments and for termination settlements if the contract is terminated for the convenience of the Government [@CL-IND-0130; @CL-IND-0131]. For first article approval itself, the Government laboratory or other activity responsible for first article testing or evaluation shall inform the contracting office whether to approve, conditionally approve, or disapprove the first article, and the contracting officer shall then notify the contractor of the action taken and furnish a copy of the notice to the contract administration office [@CL-IND-0133; @CL-IND-9006]. Any changes in the drawings, designs, or specifications determined by the contracting officer to be necessary shall be made under the Changes clause, and not by the notice of approval, conditional approval, or disapproval furnished the contractor [@CL-IND-0401].
