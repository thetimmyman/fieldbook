## Purpose

Gives the dollar thresholds, notice periods and waiting day that apply before a multiyear contract is awarded or terminated, and how advance procurement and economic order quantity purchases come in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-IND-0113; @CL-IND-0116] |
| Codified citation | FAR 17.108; DFARS 217.170 [@CL-IND-0113; @CL-IND-0114; @CL-IND-0116; @CL-IND-0117] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-IND-0113] |
| Applies to | Multiyear contracts whose cancellation ceiling or procurement type crosses the stated thresholds [@CL-IND-0113; @CL-IND-0117] |

Except for DoD, NASA and the Coast Guard, a multiyear contract that includes a cancellation ceiling in excess of $20 million may not be awarded until the head of the agency gives written notification of the proposed contract and of the proposed cancellation ceiling [@CL-IND-0113]. For DoD, NASA and the Coast Guard, the matching threshold is a cancellation ceiling in excess of $200 million [@CL-IND-0114]. For that DoD, NASA and Coast Guard notice, the notification goes to the committees on armed services and on appropriations of the House of Representatives and Senate [@CL-IND-0115; @CL-IND-0114], and the contract may not be awarded until the thirty-first day after the date of notification [@CL-IND-0151; @CL-IND-0114]. The text states the recipients and the wait for the other agencies separately, and this card does not draft them.

Under the DFARS, the head of the agency must provide written notice to the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before termination of any multiyear contract [@CL-IND-0116]. DoD must also provide notification to those committees at least 30 days before entering into a multiyear contract for certain procurements [@CL-IND-0117]. The listed procurements include those expected to employ an unfunded contingent liability in excess of $20 million [@CL-IND-0118; @CL-IND-0117], to employ economic order quantity procurement in excess of $20 million in any one year of the contract [@CL-IND-0119; @CL-IND-0117], to involve a contract for advance procurement leading to a multiyear contract that employs economic order quantity procurement in excess of $20 million in any one year [@CL-IND-0120; @CL-IND-0117], or to include a cancellation ceiling in excess of $200 million [@CL-IND-0121; @CL-IND-0117].

## How it works

Two different waits apply. The FAR holds the DoD, NASA and Coast Guard contract until the thirty-first day after notification [@CL-IND-0151; @CL-IND-0114], and the DFARS notice must come at least 30 days before entry into a listed multiyear contract or before termination of any multiyear contract [@CL-IND-0116; @CL-IND-0117]. Economic order quantity and advance procurement purchases matter because each can trigger the DFARS notice at the stated dollar level [@CL-IND-0119; @CL-IND-0120; @CL-IND-0117]. The DFARS section on multiyear contracts for supplies also addresses advance procurement, and this card does not draft those rules. The DFARS condition on recurring manufacturing costs in cancellation provisions is on [Multiyear cancellation ceilings and nonrecurring costs](ind-multiyear-cancellation-ceilings.md), the approval conditions are on [Multiyear contracting](ind-multiyear-contracting.md), and termination cost rules are in [Termination costs](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD component plans a multiyear contract with a cancellation ceiling of 150 million and economic order quantity purchases of 25 million in the second year. The ceiling is below the 200 million threshold, so the ceiling alone does not trigger notice. The economic order quantity purchase is above the 20 million threshold in one year. The answer: the DoD notification must reach the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before the contract is entered into.

## Never confuse

The FAR notice is tied to the size of the cancellation ceiling [@CL-IND-0113; @CL-IND-0114], while the DFARS notice also covers procurements by unfunded contingent liability and by economic order quantity [@CL-IND-0118; @CL-IND-0119; @CL-IND-0117]. The 30 days before entry are not the same as the thirty-first day wait after the FAR notification, and the 30 days before termination is a third period [@CL-IND-0151; @CL-IND-0116; @CL-IND-0117].
