## Purpose

Gives the dollar thresholds, notice periods and waiting day that apply before a multiyear contract is awarded or terminated, and how advance procurement and economic order quantity purchases come in.

## The rule

Except for DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard, a multiyear contract which includes a cancellation ceiling in excess of $20 million may not be awarded until the head of the agency gives written notification of the proposed contract and of the proposed cancellation ceiling for that contract to the committees on appropriations of the House of Representatives and Senate and the appropriate oversight committees of the House and Senate for the agency in question, and the contract may not be awarded until the thirty-first day after the date of notification [@CL-IND-0113; @CL-IND-0405; @CL-IND-0406]. For DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard, a multiyear contract which includes a cancellation ceiling in excess of $200 million may not be awarded until the head of the agency gives written notification of the proposed contract and of the proposed cancellation ceiling for that contract to the committees on armed services and on appropriations of the House of Representatives and Senate, and the contract may not be awarded until the thirty-first day after the date of notification [@CL-IND-0114; @CL-IND-0407; @CL-IND-0115; @CL-IND-0151].

Under DFARS 217.170(b), the head of the agency must provide written notice to the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before termination of any multiyear contract [@CL-IND-0116]. Under DFARS 217.170(d)(1), DoD must provide notification to the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before entering into a multiyear contract for certain procurements, including those expected to meet one of four alternatives [@CL-IND-0117; @CL-IND-0408]: employ an unfunded contingent liability in excess of $20 million [@CL-IND-0118; @CL-IND-0117], employ economic order quantity procurement in excess of $20 million in any one year of the contract [@CL-IND-0119; @CL-IND-0117], involve a contract for advance procurement leading to a multiyear contract that employs economic order quantity procurement in excess of $20 million in any one year [@CL-IND-0120; @CL-IND-0408; @CL-IND-0117], or include a cancellation ceiling in excess of $200 million [@CL-IND-0121; @CL-IND-0117].

## How it works

Under FAR 17.108, a multiyear contract that includes a cancellation ceiling in excess of $20 million (except for DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard) or in excess of $200 million (for DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard) may not be awarded until the head of the agency gives the required written notification and may not be awarded until the thirty-first day after the date of notification [@CL-IND-0113; @CL-IND-0405; @CL-IND-0406; @CL-IND-0114; @CL-IND-0407; @CL-IND-0151]. Under DFARS 217.170, the head of the agency must provide written notice to the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before termination of any multiyear contract [@CL-IND-0116], and DoD must provide notification to the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before entering into a multiyear contract for certain procurements, including those expected to employ economic order quantity procurement in excess of $20 million in any one year of the contract or to involve a contract for advance procurement leading to a multiyear contract that employs economic order quantity procurement in excess of $20 million in any one year [@CL-IND-0117; @CL-IND-0119; @CL-IND-0120; @CL-IND-0408]. The DFARS condition on recurring manufacturing costs in cancellation provisions is on [Multiyear cancellation ceilings and nonrecurring costs](ind-multiyear-cancellation-ceilings.md), the approval conditions are on [Multiyear contracting](ind-multiyear-contracting.md), and termination cost rules are in [Termination costs](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD component plans a multiyear contract with a cancellation ceiling of 150 million and economic order quantity purchases of 25 million in the second year. The ceiling is below the 200 million threshold, so the ceiling alone does not trigger notice. The economic order quantity purchase is above the 20 million threshold in one year. The answer: the DoD notification must reach the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before the contract is entered into.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 17.108, written notification by the head of the agency and the wait until the thirty-first day after the date of notification apply when a multiyear contract includes a cancellation ceiling in excess of $20 million (except for DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard) or in excess of $200 million (for DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard) [@CL-IND-0113; @CL-IND-0406; @CL-IND-0114; @CL-IND-0151]. Under DFARS 217.170, DoD must provide notification to the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before entering into a multiyear contract for certain procurements, including those expected to meet one of the four alternatives in DFARS 217.170(d)(1)(i) through (iv) [@CL-IND-0117; @CL-IND-0118; @CL-IND-0119; @CL-IND-0408; @CL-IND-0121], and the head of the agency must provide written notice to the congressional defense committees at least 30 days before termination of any multiyear contract [@CL-IND-0116].
