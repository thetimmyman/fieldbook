## Purpose

Shows the conditions for a contracting officer to authorize Government property on an IR&D program and the two money effects: no claim for rental value, and a rental deduction for the commercial share.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-IND-0027] |
| Codified citation | FAR 45.303 [@CL-IND-0027] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-IND-0027] |
| Applies to | A contractor's IR&D program using Government property [@CL-IND-0027] |

The contracting officer may authorize a contractor to use the property on an IR&D program if listed conditions are met [@CL-IND-0027]. One condition is that such use will not conflict with the primary use of the property or enable the contractor to retain property that could otherwise be released [@CL-IND-0027]. Another is that the contractor agrees not to claim reimbursement against any Government contract for the rental value of the property [@CL-IND-0028].

A further condition is that a rental charge for the portion of the contractor's IR&D program cost allocated to commercial work is deducted from the claim for reimbursement of any agreed-upon Government share of the contractor's IR&D costs [@CL-IND-0029].

## How it works

The contracting officer may authorize the use, if all the listed conditions are met [@CL-IND-0027; @CL-IND-0028; @CL-IND-0029]. The contractor gives up any claim for the rental value of the property against a Government contract [@CL-IND-0028]. The rental charge for the commercial share of the program cost comes off the claim for the agreed Government share of IR&D costs [@CL-IND-0029]. The cost rules for IR&D itself are on [IR&D and B&P costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-ird-and-bp-costs.md) and [IR&D and B&P project and pool techniques](../../indirect-rates/pools-bases/rte-ird-bp-project-and-pool-techniques.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor runs an IR&D program costing 400,000, of which 100,000 is allocated to commercial work. The rental charge for that commercial portion is 8,000. The claim for the agreed Government share of IR&D costs is reduced by 8,000, and the contractor makes no claim for the rental value of the property against any Government contract. The answer: the commercial share bears its own rent, and the Government share is not charged for it.

## Never confuse

Authorizing use of property is not the same as allowing the IR&D cost. This card covers the property conditions only, and the allowability and allocation of IR&D cost are on [IR&D and B&P costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-ird-and-bp-costs.md) and [IR&D and B&P project and pool techniques](../../indirect-rates/pools-bases/rte-ird-bp-project-and-pool-techniques.md).

## Company calibration

Whether any advance agreement covers IR&D or the use of Government property on it. [@Q-043]
