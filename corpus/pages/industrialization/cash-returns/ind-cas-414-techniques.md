## Purpose

Shows how the Cost Accounting Standard on facilities capital cost of money turns facilities capital into a cost of money factor for each indirect cost pool and then into an amount for each CAS-covered contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-IND-0035] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.414-50 [@CL-IND-0035] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-IND-0035] |
| Applies to | Facilities capital cost of money for CAS-covered contracts [@CL-IND-0040] |

The investment base used in computing the cost of money for facilities capital shall be computed from accounting data used for contract cost purposes, using the form and instructions stipulated in the Standard [@CL-IND-0035].

The cost of money rate for any cost accounting period shall be the arithmetic mean of the interest rates specified by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to Public Law 92-41 [@CL-IND-0036]. Where the cost of money must be determined on a prospective basis, the cost of money rate shall be based on the most recent available rate published by the Secretary of the Treasury [@CL-IND-0037].

## How it works

A facilities capital cost of money factor shall be determined for each indirect cost pool to which a significant amount of facilities capital has been allocated and which is used to allocate indirect costs to final cost objectives [@CL-IND-0038]. The facilities capital cost of money factor for an indirect cost pool shall be determined in accordance with Form CASB CMF and its instructions, which are set forth in appendix A to 9904.414, and one form will serve for all the indirect cost pools of a business unit [@CL-IND-0039]. For each CAS-covered contract, the applicable cost of capital committed to facilities for a given cost accounting period is the sum of the products obtained by multiplying the amount of allocation base units [@CL-IND-0040], identified with the contract for the cost accounting period, such as direct labor hours or dollars of total cost input, by the facilities capital cost of money factor for the corresponding indirect cost pool [@CL-IND-0041]. In the case of process cost accounting systems, the contracting parties may agree to substitute an appropriate statistical measure for the allocation base units identified with the contract [@CL-IND-0042].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Pool A has a factor of 0.50 dollars per direct labor hour, and the contract used 10,000 direct labor hours in the period, so Pool A gives 5,000. Pool B has a factor of 0.004 per dollar of total cost input, and the contract has 2,000,000 of total cost input, so Pool B gives 8,000. The answer: the cost of capital committed to facilities for the contract and period is 13,000.

## Never confuse

The cost of money rate for any cost accounting period shall be the arithmetic mean of the interest rates specified by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to Public Law 92-41 [@CL-IND-0036], whereas a facilities capital cost of money factor shall be determined in accordance with Form CASB CMF and its instructions in appendix A to 9904.414 for each indirect cost pool to which a significant amount of facilities capital has been allocated and which is used to allocate indirect costs to final cost objectives [@CL-IND-0038; @CL-IND-0039]. The profit side is a different use of these figures, covered on [DoD weighted guidelines: facilities capital employed](../../estimating/pricing/est-weighted-guidelines-facilities-capital.md). The allowability conditions are on [Cost of money as an imputed cost](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cost-of-money.md), and the requirement itself is on [Cost of money on facilities capital](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-414-facilities-capital-cost-of-money.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company computes a factor for each significant pool and keeps the form current. [@Q-015]
