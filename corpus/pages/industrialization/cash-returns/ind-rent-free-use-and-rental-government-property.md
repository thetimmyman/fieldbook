## Purpose

Shows when Government property carries no rent, when rent or other consideration applies, and where requests from foreign governments go.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-IND-0018; @CL-IND-0026] |
| Codified citation | FAR 45.301 and FAR 45.302 [@CL-IND-0018; @CL-IND-0026] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-IND-0018; @CL-IND-0026] |
| Applies to | Contractor use of Government property [@CL-IND-0018] |

Government property shall normally be provided on a rent-free basis in performance of the contract under which it is accountable or otherwise authorized [@CL-IND-0018]. Rental charges, to the extent authorized, do not apply to Government property that is left in place or installed on contractor-owned property for mobilization or future Government production purposes [@CL-IND-0019]; however, rental charges shall apply to that portion of property or its capacity used for non-government commercial purposes or otherwise authorized for use [@CL-IND-0020].

In exchange for consideration as determined by the cognizant contracting officer or officers, the contractor may use Government property under fixed-price contracts other than the contract to which it is accountable [@CL-IND-0021]. When, after contract award, a contractor requests the use of Government property, the contracting officer shall obtain a fair rental or other adequate consideration if use is authorized [@CL-IND-0022].

## How it works

Government property shall normally be provided on a rent-free basis in performance of the contract under which it is accountable or otherwise authorized [@CL-IND-0018]. The cognizant contracting officer or officers may authorize the use of Government property on a rent-free basis on a cost type Government contract other than the contract to which it is accountable [@CL-IND-0023]. The contracting officer cognizant of the Government property may also authorize the rent-free use of property in the possession of nonprofit organizations when used for research, development, or educational work and all three listed conditions are met [@CL-IND-9004]: the use of the property is in the national interest, the property will not be used for the direct benefit of a profit-making organization, and the Government receives some direct benefit, such as rights to use the results of the work without charge, from its use [@CL-IND-9005]. In exchange for consideration as determined by the cognizant contracting officer, the contractor may use Government property for commercial use [@CL-IND-0024]. Prior approval of the Head of the Contracting Activity is required where non-Government use is expected to exceed 25 percent of the total use of Government and commercial work performed [@CL-IND-0025]. Requests by, or for the benefit of, foreign Governments or international organizations to use Government property shall be processed in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-IND-0026].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A plant holds Government machine tools accountable under Contract A. In a year the tools run 800 hours on Contract A and 200 hours on commercial work, so commercial use is 20 percent of 1,000 total hours. That is below the 25 percent level, so no prior approval by the Head of the Contracting Activity is needed. The contracting officer still sets consideration for the 200 commercial hours, say 5,000. The answer: Contract A use is rent-free, and the commercial hours pay 5,000.

## Never confuse

Government property shall normally be provided on a rent-free basis in performance of the contract under which it is accountable or otherwise authorized [@CL-IND-0018], and the cognizant contracting officer or officers may authorize rent-free use on a cost type Government contract other than the contract to which it is accountable [@CL-IND-0023] or, when all three conditions are met, for property in the possession of nonprofit organizations used for research, development, or educational work [@CL-IND-9004; @CL-IND-9005]. In exchange for consideration as determined by the cognizant contracting officer, the contractor may use Government property for commercial use [@CL-IND-0024]. The rental formula and payment terms sit in the [Use and Charges clause for Government property](../../close-revrec/fixed-assets/cls-use-and-charges-government-property.md), and the title and control duties sit in the [Government Property clause](../../close-revrec/fixed-assets/cls-government-property-clause.md).
