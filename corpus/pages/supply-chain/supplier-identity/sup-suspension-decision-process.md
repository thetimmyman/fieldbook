## Purpose

Shows how a suspension is imposed and decided: the general rules, the notice, the contractor's response, fact-finding and the decision.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0926] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.407-1 and FAR 9.407-3 [@CL-SUP-0926; @CL-SUP-0928] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0926] |
| Applies to | Suspension actions decided by the suspending and debarring official [@CL-SUP-0926; @CL-SUP-0932] |

Suspension is a serious action to be imposed on the basis of adequate evidence, pending the completion of an investigation or legal proceedings, when it has been determined that immediate action is necessary to protect the Government's interest [@CL-SUP-0926]. A contractor has the burden of promptly presenting to the suspending and debarring official evidence of remedial measures or mitigating factors when it has reason to know that a cause for suspension exists [@CL-SUP-0927] (see [Causes for suspension](sup-causes-for-suspension.md)).

Per one listed item of the suspension notice, the contractor may, within 30 days after receipt of the notice, submit, in person, in writing, or through a representative, information and argument in opposition to the suspension, including any additional specific information that raises a genuine dispute over the material facts [@CL-SUP-0930; @CL-SUP-0929]. The paragraph on administrative agreements and the paragraph on voluntary exclusions each provide that the suspending and debarring official is responsible for the timely and accurate submission of documentation reflecting the agreement or exclusion, and that the submission should be made within 3 working days [@CL-SUP-0990; @CL-SUP-0991].

## How it works

The agency suspension decision-making procedures shall afford the contractor and any specifically named affiliates an opportunity, following the imposition of suspension, to submit information and argument in opposition to the suspension in person, in writing or through a representative [@CL-SUP-0928]. When a contractor and any specifically named affiliates are suspended, they shall be immediately advised of the matters listed in the paragraph, using the notice procedures cross-referenced there [@CL-SUP-0929]. One listed item is that additional proceedings to determine disputed material facts will be conducted unless one of two alternatives applies: the action is based on an indictment [@CL-SUP-0931], or a determination is made, on the basis of advice by the Department of Justice, a U.S. Attorney's office, State attorney general's office, or a State or local prosecutor's office, that the substantial interests of the Government in pending or contemplated legal proceedings based on the same facts as the suspension would be prejudiced [@CL-SUP-9723; @CL-SUP-9724].

The suspending and debarring official's decision shall be based on all the information in the administrative record, including any submission made by the contractor, for actions in any one of three alternatives: actions based on an indictment [@CL-SUP-0992], actions in which the contractor's submission does not raise a genuine dispute over material facts [@CL-SUP-9725], or actions in which additional proceedings to determine disputed material facts have been denied on the basis of advice from the Department of Justice, a U.S. Attorney's office, State attorney general's office, or a State or local prosecutor's office [@CL-SUP-9726]. In suspension actions in which additional proceedings are necessary as to disputed material facts, written findings of fact shall be prepared and the official shall base the decision on the facts as found, together with any information and argument submitted by the contractor and any other information in the administrative record [@CL-SUP-0989; @CL-SUP-9721]. The official may modify or terminate the suspension or leave it in force, but a decision to modify or terminate the suspension is without prejudice to the later imposition of suspension by any other agency or debarment by any agency [@CL-SUP-0932; @CL-SUP-9722].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor is suspended on an indictment. It receives the notice and files its opposition on day 25.

Answer: the filing is inside the 30 days after receipt. Because the action rests on an indictment, the exception to additional fact-finding proceedings applies, and the official decides on the record whether to modify, terminate or keep the suspension.

## Never confuse

A suspended contractor is afforded an opportunity following the imposition of suspension to submit information and argument in opposition [@CL-SUP-0928], while a notice of proposed debarment advises that the contractor and any specifically named affiliates may submit information and argument in opposition to the proposed debarment within 30 days after receipt of the notice [@CL-SUP-0921] (see [Debarment: general rules, procedures and the decision](sup-debarment-decision-process.md)). Suspension is imposed on the basis of adequate evidence, pending the completion of an investigation or legal proceedings [@CL-SUP-0926] (see also [Causes for debarment](sup-causes-for-debarment.md) and [SAM exclusion records and the effect of listing](sup-sam-exclusion-records-and-effect-of-listing.md)).
