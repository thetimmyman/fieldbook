## Purpose

Shows when contracts and subcontracts already in place may continue after a party is listed, which new orders and extensions are barred, and the consent bar and compelling-reason route for subcontracting with a listed party.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0757] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.405-1 and FAR 9.405-2 [@CL-SUP-0757; @CL-SUP-0763] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0757] |
| Applies to | Contracts and subcontracts with a contractor that is debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment, or voluntarily excluded [@CL-SUP-0757; @CL-SUP-0762] |

Notwithstanding the debarment, suspension, proposed debarment, or voluntary exclusion of a contractor, agencies may continue contracts or subcontracts in existence at the time the contractor was debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment, or voluntarily excluded, unless the agency head directs otherwise [@CL-SUP-0084; @CL-SUP-0757]. For such a contractor, ordering activities shall not take certain listed actions unless the agency head makes a written determination of the compelling reasons for doing so [@CL-SUP-0759].

When such a contractor is proposed as a subcontractor for a subcontract subject to Government consent, contracting officers shall not consent to the subcontract, unless the agency head states in writing the compelling reasons for this approval action [@CL-SUP-0762; @CL-SUP-0763].

## How it works

Existing contracts and subcontracts may continue notwithstanding the debarment, suspension, proposed debarment, or voluntary exclusion of a contractor unless the agency head directs otherwise [@CL-SUP-0084; @CL-SUP-0757], and a decision as to the type of termination action, if any, to be taken should be made only after review by agency contracting and technical personnel and by counsel to ensure the propriety of the proposed action [@CL-SUP-0758]. New ordering is limited: absent the written determination, no orders exceeding the guaranteed minimum under indefinite quantity contracts [@CL-SUP-0759; @CL-SUP-0760], and no orders under Federal Supply Schedule contracts, blanket purchase agreements, or basic ordering agreements, nor added work, exercised options or extended duration of current contracts or orders [@CL-SUP-0759; @CL-SUP-0761]. Separately, the same section sets $45,000 as the subcontract amount for the contractor's own prohibition on subcontracting with such a contractor, other than for a commercially available off-the-shelf item and unless there is a compelling reason [@CL-SUP-0764] (see [Protecting the Government's interest when subcontracting with debarred or suspended contractors](sup-debarred-suspended-subcontractors.md)). The contractor's compliance with the clause on protecting the Government's interest when subcontracting is reviewed during Contractor Purchasing System Reviews [@CL-SUP-0765], which are covered on the [Contractor Purchasing System Administration clause](../procure-to-pay/sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor is proposed for debarment while it holds an indefinite quantity contract with a guaranteed minimum of 100 units. The ordering office has already ordered 100 units and wants to order 40 more. The agency head has made no written determination.

Answer: the contract may continue unless the agency head directs otherwise, but the 40 extra units exceed the guaranteed minimum. The order may not be placed without the written determination of the compelling reasons.

## Never confuse

Continuation of existing contracts or subcontracts is allowed unless the agency head directs otherwise [@CL-SUP-0084; @CL-SUP-0757], while the listed orders, new work, option exercises and extensions are barred unless the agency head makes a written determination of the compelling reasons for doing so [@CL-SUP-0759; @CL-SUP-0760; @CL-SUP-0761] (see also [SAM exclusion records and the effect of listing](sup-sam-exclusion-records-and-effect-of-listing.md)). The consent bar applies when a contractor that is debarred, suspended, or proposed for debarment is proposed as a subcontractor for any subcontract subject to Government consent [@CL-SUP-0762] (see [Evaluation of subcontract consent requests and contracting officer considerations](../procure-to-pay/sup-subcontract-consent-evaluation.md)).
