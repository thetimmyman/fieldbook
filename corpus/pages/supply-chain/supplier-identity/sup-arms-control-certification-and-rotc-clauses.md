## Purpose

Shows what an offeror certifies about arms control determinations, when the certification does not apply, and what follows for an institution of higher education that blocks ROTC or recruiting.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1043; @CL-SUP-1047] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.209-13 [@CL-SUP-1043; @CL-SUP-1044; @CL-SUP-1458; @CL-SUP-1459; @CL-SUP-1045; @CL-SUP-1460; @CL-SUP-1536; @CL-SUP-1046; @CL-SUP-1073; @CL-SUP-1543] and FAR 52.209-14 [@CL-SUP-1047; @CL-SUP-1048; @CL-SUP-1049; @CL-SUP-1050; @CL-SUP-1074] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.209-13 [@CL-SUP-3541; @CL-SUP-3542; @CL-SUP-3543; @CL-SUP-3544; @CL-SUP-3545; @CL-SUP-3546; @CL-SUP-3547] and RFO FAR 52.209-14 [@CL-SUP-3548; @CL-SUP-3549; @CL-SUP-3550; @CL-SUP-3551] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1043] |
| Applies to | Offerors, for the certification, other than for the excluded acquisitions, and institutions of higher education, for the clause [@CL-SUP-1043; @CL-SUP-1047] |

The certification provision does not apply to acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold or to acquisitions of commercial products and commercial services as defined in FAR 2.101 [@CL-SUP-1043]. Under paragraph (b) the Offeror shall check either (1) or (2), and (1) is a certification by the Offeror that the items that follow apply [@CL-SUP-3969]. The first item is that the Offeror does not engage and has not engaged in any activity that contributed to or was a significant factor in the President's or Secretary of State's determination that a foreign country is in violation of its obligations [@CL-SUP-1044]. The obligations at issue are those undertaken in an arms control, nonproliferation, or disarmament agreement to which the United States is a party [@CL-SUP-1458]. The determination can alternatively be that a foreign country is not adhering to its arms control, nonproliferation, or disarmament commitments in which the United States is a participating state [@CL-SUP-1459]. The first item ends with the word and, joining it to the second item [@CL-SUP-3970], which is that no entity owned or controlled by the Offeror has engaged in any activity that contributed to or was a significant factor in the President's or Secretary of State's determination that a foreign country is in violation of its obligations [@CL-SUP-1045]. The same limit applies there: the obligations are those undertaken in an arms control, nonproliferation, or disarmament agreement to which the United States is a party [@CL-SUP-1460]. The same alternative of non-adherence to commitments in which the United States is a participating state also applies there [@CL-SUP-1536].

Except as provided in the clause's exception paragraph, an institution of higher education is ineligible for contract award if the Secretary of Defense determines that the institution has a policy or practice, regardless of when implemented, that prohibits or in effect prevents any one of the alternative actions listed in the clause [@CL-SUP-1047; @CL-SUP-9732]. The limitation on contract award does not apply to an institution of higher education if the Secretary of Defense determines that the institution has ceased the policy or practice or has a long-standing policy of pacifism based on historical religious affiliation [@CL-SUP-1047; @CL-SUP-1074].

## How it works

If it is later determined that the Offeror knowingly submitted a false certification, then in addition to other remedies such as suspension or debarment, the Contracting Officer may terminate any contract resulting from the false certification [@CL-SUP-1046]. For the ROTC clause, notwithstanding any other clause of the contract, if the Secretary of Defense determines that the institution has violated the contract in the limitation paragraph, the listed consequences follow [@CL-SUP-1048]. One is that the institution will be ineligible for further payments under this and any other contracts with this agency and any other covered agency, except for contracts at or below the simplified acquisition threshold or contracts for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-SUP-1049]. The other is that the Government will terminate the contract for default for the institution's material failure to comply with the terms and conditions of award [@CL-SUP-1050]. The provision says not to submit an offer unless a certification is provided in the certification paragraph and submitted with the offer, or the Offeror provides with its offer information, in accordance with the separate-information paragraph, that the President of the United States has taken one of the actions the provision then lists [@CL-SUP-1073]. The information is that the President has waived application under the cited statutory subsections or has determined that the entity has ceased all activities for which measures were imposed [@CL-SUP-1543]. The provision also sets procedures for reviewing the annual unclassified report [@CL-SUP-3971].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.209-13 keeps the exclusion for acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold and for acquisitions of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-SUP-3541; @CL-SUP-1043]. It keeps the certification about the Offeror's own activity and the certification about entities the Offeror owns or controls [@CL-SUP-3542; @CL-SUP-1044; @CL-SUP-3543; @CL-SUP-1045], and in each it keeps the wording on violated obligations and on non-adherence to commitments [@CL-SUP-3544; @CL-SUP-1458; @CL-SUP-1459; @CL-SUP-1460; @CL-SUP-1536]. It keeps the instruction not to submit an offer unless a certification is provided or the Offeror provides information that the President of the United States has taken one of the listed actions [@CL-SUP-3545; @CL-SUP-1073]. For that information the RFO text says the President has waived application under section 2593e(d) or (e) of title 22 of the United States Code [@CL-SUP-3546], where the codified paragraph prints the citation 2593e(d) or (e) with no title number [@CL-SUP-1543]. For a false certification the RFO text says that if the Government later determines that the Offeror knowingly submitted it, the Contracting Officer may terminate any contract resulting from it [@CL-SUP-3547; @CL-SUP-1046]. RFO FAR 52.209-14 keeps the exception for an institution of higher education that has ceased the policy or practice or has a long-standing policy of pacifism based on historical religious affiliation [@CL-SUP-3548; @CL-SUP-1074]. It keeps the rule that, notwithstanding any other clause of the contract, if the Secretary of Defense determines that the institution has violated the contract in the limitation paragraph, listed consequences follow [@CL-SUP-3549; @CL-SUP-1048]: the institution is ineligible for further payments under this and any other contracts with this agency and any other covered agency, except for contracts at or below the simplified acquisition threshold or contracts for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-SUP-3550; @CL-SUP-1049], and the Government terminates the contract for default for the institution's material failure to comply with the terms and conditions of award [@CL-SUP-3551; @CL-SUP-1050].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A manufacturer bidding above the simplified acquisition threshold on an acquisition that is not for commercial products or commercial services reviews the annual report for countries where it or an owned entity has done business. It finds none with a determination of violation or of non-adherence. The answer: it gives the certification with its offer. A university contractor has a campus policy that blocks military recruiters, and the Secretary of Defense so determines. The answer: it is ineligible for award unless an exception applies.

## Never confuse

The arms control certification is made by offerors about their own activity and that of owned or controlled entities [@CL-SUP-1044; @CL-SUP-1045]. The ROTC clause applies to institutions of higher education and turns on a determination by the Secretary of Defense [@CL-SUP-1047]. For the offeror responsibility certifications, see [Responsibility certification and information provisions](sup-responsibility-certification-and-information-provisions.md), and for the tax and felony representations, see [Inverted domestic corporations and tax or felony representations](sup-inverted-corporation-and-tax-felony-provisions.md).

The codified false-certification sentence says if it is later determined, without saying who determines [@CL-SUP-1046]; the RFO text says if the Government later determines [@CL-SUP-3547].
