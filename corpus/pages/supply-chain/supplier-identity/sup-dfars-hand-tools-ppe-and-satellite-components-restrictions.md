## Purpose

Shows what each of three DoD clauses restricts: hand or measuring tools, personal protective equipment and certain other items from listed countries, and star trackers.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1487; @CL-SUP-1488; @CL-SUP-1493] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7015 [@CL-SUP-1487]; DFARS 252.225-7061 [@CL-SUP-1488]; DFARS 252.225-7064 [@CL-SUP-1493] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1487] |
| Applies to | Hand or measuring tools, covered items, and star trackers delivered under contracts with the matching clause [@CL-SUP-1487; @CL-SUP-1488; @CL-SUP-1493] |

Hand or measuring tools delivered under the contract shall be produced in the United States or its outlying areas [@CL-SUP-1487]. Under the personal protective equipment clause the Contractor shall not deliver under the contract a covered item from a covered country [@CL-SUP-1488]. The covered countries are North Korea, China, the Russian Federation, and Iran [@CL-SUP-1489]. The covered item definition includes personal protective equipment for use in preventing spread of disease, with nitrile and vinyl gloves and surgical masks among the listed examples [@CL-SUP-1490]. The list goes on with respirator masks and powered air purifying respirators and required filters, face shields and protective eyewear, surgical and isolation gowns and head and foot coverings, or clothing, each an alternative joined by "or" [@CL-SUP-1519]. It also lists the materials and components thereof, other than sensors, electronics, or other items added to and not normally associated with such personal protective equipment or clothing [@CL-SUP-1520]. The definition's second numbered group is an alternative to the first, joined by "or": sanitizing and disinfecting wipes, testing swabs, gauze, and bandages [@CL-SUP-1520; @CL-SUP-1518]. The Contractor shall insert the clause, including paragraph (c), without alteration other than to identify the appropriate parties, in subcontracts valued above $150,000 that are for the acquisition of covered items [@CL-SUP-1491]. That subcontracts paragraph reaches subcontracts valued above $150,000 for covered items, including subcontracts for commercial products, including commercially available off-the-shelf items, and commercial services [@CL-SUP-1521].

A star tracker is a navigational tool used in a satellite weighing more than 400 pounds whose principal purpose is to support the national security, defense, or intelligence needs of the United States Government [@CL-SUP-1492]. The Contractor shall deliver under the contract only star trackers manufactured in the United States, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, or the United Kingdom [@CL-SUP-1493]. The Contractor shall insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), in subcontracts for star trackers that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, including subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-SUP-1494].

## How it works

The hand tools clause tests where the tool is produced [@CL-SUP-1487]. The personal protective equipment clause blocks listed items from listed countries [@CL-SUP-1488; @CL-SUP-1489]. The satellite components clause allows star trackers only from listed places [@CL-SUP-1493]. The personal protective equipment definition excludes sensors, electronics, or other items added to and not normally associated with such personal protective equipment or clothing from the listed materials and components [@CL-SUP-1520].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 50 star trackers for a satellite above the weight in the definition, whose principal purpose is to support the national security, defense, or intelligence needs of the Government. Thirty were manufactured in Canada and 20 in a country that is not on the clause's list.

Answer: the 30 built in Canada meet the restriction, and the 20 built elsewhere do not.

## Never confuse

Under the personal protective equipment clause the Contractor shall not deliver a covered item from a covered country [@CL-SUP-1488; @CL-SUP-1489], while hand or measuring tools shall be produced in the United States or its outlying areas [@CL-SUP-1487] and only star trackers manufactured in the listed countries may be delivered [@CL-SUP-1493]. For magnets, tantalum and tungsten, see [Restriction on acquisition of certain magnets, tantalum and tungsten](sup-dfars-magnets-tantalum-tungsten-restriction.md). For the clause on commercial satellite services, see [Prohibition on acquisition of certain foreign commercial satellite services](sup-dfars-foreign-commercial-satellite-services-prohibition.md). For other clauses that restrict where an item is made, see [Restrictions on anchor and mooring chain, forgings and steel plate](sup-dfars-anchor-chain-forgings-and-steel-plate-restrictions.md); for the Xinjiang prohibition, see [Prohibition on certain procurements from the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region](sup-dfars-xinjiang-uyghur-region-prohibition.md). The flowdown paragraph of the personal protective equipment clause applies to subcontracts valued above $150,000 for covered items [@CL-SUP-1491], and that of the star tracker clause to subcontracts for star trackers that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-SUP-1494].
