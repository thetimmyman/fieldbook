## Purpose

Shows which end products may be delivered under the DoD trade agreements clause, the definitions the clause adds, and how the certificate provision evaluates offers.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1346] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7021 and DFARS 252.225-7020 [@CL-SUP-1346; @CL-SUP-1350] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1346] |
| Applies to | Deliveries of end products under a contract with the Trade Agreements clause [@CL-SUP-1346] |

The Contractor shall deliver only U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products unless, in its offer, it specified delivery of other nondesignated country end products in the Trade Agreements Certificate provision, and a further condition listed in the clause is met [@CL-SUP-1346]. The further condition is that offers of U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products from responsive, responsible offerors are either not received or are insufficient to fill the Government's requirements, or that a national interest waiver has been granted [@CL-SUP-1347]. A U.S.-made end product is an article that is mined, produced, or manufactured in the United States, or is substantially transformed in the United States into a new and different article of commerce [@CL-SUP-1348]. A designated country end product is a WTO GPA country end product, a Free Trade Agreement country end product, a least developed country end product, or a Caribbean Basin country end product [@CL-SUP-1349]. The clause also defines each of those end product types and lists the countries, which this card does not set out. Under the certificate provision the Government will evaluate offers in accordance with the policies and procedures of DFARS Part 225 [@CL-SUP-1350]. Unless there are no offers of such end products, those offers are insufficient to fulfill the Government's requirements, or a national interest waiver has been granted, the Government will consider only offers of U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1350; @CL-SUP-1375]. The clause and provision sections printed for this card state no dollar threshold for when the trade agreements rules apply.

## How it works

The clause sets what the contractor delivers [@CL-SUP-1346], and the provision gathers the offeror's certification and states how offers are evaluated [@CL-SUP-1350]. The clause bars delivery of other nondesignated country end products unless the offer specified them and a further listed condition is met [@CL-SUP-1346; @CL-SUP-1347].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation seeks one line item. Every offer received is for a nondesignated country end product, and no offer of a U.S.-made, qualifying country or designated country end product arrives. The Government may then consider the nondesignated offers, because no offers of the preferred end products were received.

## Never confuse

The Buy American clause requires domestic end products unless the offer says otherwise [@CL-SUP-1311], while this clause allows U.S.-made, qualifying country or designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1346]. The two clauses and their certificate provisions are separate and carry separate definitions. The DoD Buy American delivery rule is on [DoD Buy American clause: definitions and delivery rule](sup-dfars-buy-american-definitions-and-delivery-rule.md), and its FAR counterpart is on [Buy American clause for supplies](sup-buy-american-supplies-clause.md).

The FAR trade agreements clause is covered on [Trade Agreements clause](sup-trade-agreements-clause.md). Where the DoD version differs, its delivery rule admits qualifying country end products as well as U.S.-made and designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1346], while the FAR rule names U.S.-made or designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1253]. In the DoD clause, delivery of other nondesignated country end products also requires that offers of the preferred end products are not received or are insufficient, or that a national interest waiver has been granted [@CL-SUP-1347]. The DoD certificate provision is separate from the DoD Buy American certificate, which is on [DoD Buy American clause: exceptions, certificate and evaluation of offers](sup-dfars-buy-american-exceptions-certificate-and-evaluation.md).
