## Purpose

Shows which end products may be delivered under the DoD trade agreements clause, the definitions the clause adds, and how the certificate provision evaluates offers.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1346] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7021 and DFARS 252.225-7020 [@CL-SUP-1346; @CL-SUP-1350] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | DFARS 252.225-7021: FEB 2024 [@CL-SUP-9945]; DFARS 252.225-7020: NOV 2014 [@CL-SUP-9946] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1346] |
| Applies to | Deliveries of end products under a contract with the Trade Agreements clause [@CL-SUP-1346] |

The Contractor shall deliver only U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products unless, in its offer, it specified delivery of other nondesignated country end products in the Trade Agreements Certificate provision, and a further condition listed in the clause is met [@CL-SUP-1346]. The further condition is that offers of U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products from responsive, responsible offerors are either not received or are insufficient to fill the Government's requirements, or that a national interest waiver has been granted [@CL-SUP-1347]. A U.S.-made end product is an article that is mined, produced, or manufactured in the United States, or that is substantially transformed in the United States into a new and different article of commerce with a name, character, or use distinct from that of the article or articles from which it was transformed [@CL-SUP-1348; @CL-SUP-0072]. A designated country end product is a WTO GPA country end product, a Free Trade Agreement country end product, a least developed country end product, or a Caribbean Basin country end product [@CL-SUP-1349]. Under the certificate provision, for all line items subject to the Trade Agreements—Basic of the solicitation, the offeror certifies that each end product to be delivered under the contract, except those listed in paragraph (c)(2) of the provision, is a U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end product [@CL-SUP-0073]. Under the certificate provision the Government will evaluate offers in accordance with the policies and procedures of DFARS Part 225 [@CL-SUP-1350]. Unless there are no offers of such end products, those offers are insufficient to fulfill the Government's requirements, or a national interest waiver has been granted, the Government will consider only offers of U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1350; @CL-SUP-1375].

## How it works

The clause sets what the contractor delivers [@CL-SUP-1346], and the provision gathers the offeror's certification and states how offers are evaluated [@CL-SUP-0073; @CL-SUP-1350]. The clause bars delivery of other nondesignated country end products unless the offer specified them and a further listed condition is met [@CL-SUP-1346; @CL-SUP-1347].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation seeks one line item. Every offer received is for a nondesignated country end product, and no offer of a U.S.-made, qualifying country or designated country end product arrives. The Government may then consider the nondesignated offers, because no offers of the preferred end products were received.

## Never confuse

The Buy American clause requires domestic end products unless the offer specifies delivery of other end products [@CL-SUP-1311] (see [DoD Buy American clause: definitions and delivery rule](sup-dfars-buy-american-definitions-and-delivery-rule.md), [DoD Buy American clause: exceptions, certificate and evaluation of offers](sup-dfars-buy-american-exceptions-certificate-and-evaluation.md), and [Buy American clause for supplies](sup-buy-american-supplies-clause.md)), while this clause allows U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1346].

In the DoD version, the delivery rule admits qualifying country end products as well as U.S.-made and designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1346], while the FAR rule on [Trade Agreements clause](sup-trade-agreements-clause.md) names U.S.-made or designated country end products [@CL-SUP-1253]. In the DoD clause, delivery of other nondesignated country end products also requires that offers of the preferred end products from responsive, responsible offerors are either not received or are insufficient to fill the Government's requirements, or that a national interest waiver has been granted [@CL-SUP-1347].
