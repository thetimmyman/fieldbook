## Purpose

Shows what an offeror represents about forced labor from the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region, what the contract clause prohibits, and how the clause flows down to subcontracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1461; @CL-SUP-1464] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7059 [@CL-SUP-1464] and DFARS 252.225-7060 [@CL-SUP-1461] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1461] |
| Applies to | Solicitations with the representation provision, and contracts with the clause [@CL-SUP-1464; @CL-SUP-1461] |

Under the clause, none of the funds appropriated or otherwise made available for DoD may be used to knowingly procure any products mined, produced, or manufactured wholly or in part by forced labor from XUAR or from an entity that has used labor from within or transferred from XUAR [@CL-SUP-1461]. The Contractor shall make a good faith effort to determine that forced labor from XUAR will not be used in the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-1462]. The clause defines forced labor as any work or service that is exacted from any person under the menace of any penalty for nonperformance and that the worker does not offer to perform [@CL-SUP-1465].

By submission of its offer, the Offeror represents that it has made a good faith effort to determine that forced labor from XUAR will not be used in the performance of a contract resulting from the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1464]. The Contractor shall insert the clause, including its subcontracts paragraph, without alteration other than to identify the appropriate parties, in subcontracts, including subcontracts for commercial products, commercial services, and commercially available off-the-shelf items [@CL-SUP-1463].

## How it works

The representation is made by submitting the offer, and it refers to a contract resulting from the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1464]. The same good faith effort then becomes a duty of the Contractor under the clause [@CL-SUP-1462]. The clause passes down to subcontracts with only the parties changed, and its text names subcontracts for commercial products, commercial services and commercially available off-the-shelf items [@CL-SUP-1463].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor offers to deliver 100 cable assemblies. Before it submits the offer, it checks each of its 8 suppliers for forced labor from XUAR and keeps a record of the checks. By submitting the offer it represents that it made a good faith effort. After award it places a subcontract for a commercial connector worth 5,000 and inserts the clause with only the parties changed.

Answer: the representation is the good faith effort statement made in the offer, and the flowdown reaches the commercial connector subcontract.

## Never confuse

The representation provision is a statement the offeror makes in its offer that it has made a good faith effort, while the clause is the contract duty with its flowdown [@CL-SUP-1464; @CL-SUP-1462; @CL-SUP-1463] (see also [Forced or indentured child labor violations, remedies and clauses](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-forced-child-labor-violations-remedies-and-clauses.md) and [Subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](../sourcing-evidence/sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md)). The personal protective equipment clause also reaches commercial subcontracts, in subcontracts valued above $150,000 that are for the acquisition of covered items [@CL-SUP-1521]; it is covered on [Restrictions on hand tools, personal protective equipment and satellite components](sup-dfars-hand-tools-ppe-and-satellite-components-restrictions.md).
