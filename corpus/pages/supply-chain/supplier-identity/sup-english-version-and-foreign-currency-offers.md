## Purpose

Explains which language controls when a contract term and its translation disagree, and how the Government converts offers in a foreign currency for evaluation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2807; @CL-SUP-2808] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.225-14 [@CL-SUP-2807]; FAR 52.225-17 [@CL-SUP-2808] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.225-14 [@CL-SUP-3649]; RFO FAR 52.225-17 [@CL-SUP-3650] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2807; @CL-SUP-2808] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the English version clause [@CL-SUP-2807]; solicitations that include the foreign currency provision when offers arrive in more than one currency [@CL-SUP-2808] |

If a term of the contract and a translation into another language are inconsistent, the English language meaning controls [@CL-SUP-2807].

If the Government receives offers in more than one currency, it evaluates them by converting the foreign currency to United States currency using the source of rate that the Contracting Officer inserts, in effect at the times set out in the provision [@CL-SUP-2808]. For acquisitions conducted using sealed bidding procedures, the rate in effect on the date of bid opening is used [@CL-SUP-2809]. For acquisitions conducted using negotiation procedures, the rate in effect on the date specified for receipt of offers is used if award is based on initial offers, and otherwise the rate in effect on the date specified for receipt of proposal revisions [@CL-SUP-2810].

## How it works

The English version clause settles an inconsistency between a contract term and a translation into another language for the English language meaning [@CL-SUP-2807].

The foreign currency provision applies when the Government receives offers in more than one currency, and it converts the foreign currency to United States currency for evaluation using the source of rate that the Contracting Officer inserts [@CL-SUP-2808].

For acquisitions conducted using sealed bidding procedures, the rate in effect on the date of bid opening is used [@CL-SUP-2809]. For acquisitions conducted using negotiation procedures, the rate in effect on the date specified for receipt of offers is used if award is based on initial offers, and otherwise the rate in effect on the date specified for receipt of proposal revisions [@CL-SUP-2810].

In RFO FAR part 52, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.225-14 keeps the rule that in the event of inconsistency between any terms of the contract and any translation into another language, the English language meaning shall control [@CL-SUP-3649; @CL-SUP-2807]. RFO FAR 52.225-17 keeps the conversion of offers in more than one currency to United States currency using the source of rate the Contracting Officer inserts [@CL-SUP-3650; @CL-SUP-2808], the rate on the date of bid opening for sealed bidding [@CL-SUP-3651; @CL-SUP-2809], and for negotiation the rate on the date specified for receipt of offers if award is based on initial offers, otherwise on the date specified for receipt of proposal revisions [@CL-SUP-3652; @CL-SUP-2810].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A negotiated solicitation includes the foreign currency provision, with a rate source inserted by the Contracting Officer. Offers are due on June 1 and proposal revisions on July 15. Offeror P bids 500,000 units of a foreign currency. Offeror Q bids $540,000.

Award is based on proposal revisions and not on initial offers, so the rate in effect on July 15 applies. On July 15 the rate is 1.05 dollars per unit, so P's offer is evaluated at 500,000 x 1.05 = $525,000, which is lower than Q's $540,000.

Had award been based on initial offers, the rate in effect on June 1 would apply. At 1.10 dollars per unit, P's offer would be evaluated at 500,000 x 1.10 = $550,000, which is higher than Q's $540,000. Had the acquisition used sealed bidding, the rate on the date of bid opening would apply instead.

Separately, the contract is signed in English with a translation. The English text says delivery within 45 days and the translation says 30 days. The terms are inconsistent, so the English language meaning controls and delivery is within 45 days.

## Never confuse

The English version clause provides that in the event of inconsistency between any terms of the contract and any translation into another language, the English language meaning shall control [@CL-SUP-2807], whereas the foreign currency provision evaluates offers received in more than one currency by converting the foreign currency to United States currency [@CL-SUP-2808] (see also [Trade Agreements Certificate](sup-trade-agreements-certificate.md) and [Trade Agreements clause](sup-trade-agreements-clause.md)).

## Company calibration
