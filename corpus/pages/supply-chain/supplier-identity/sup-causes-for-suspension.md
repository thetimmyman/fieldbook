## Purpose

Shows the offenses and conduct for which the suspending and debarring official may suspend a contractor upon adequate evidence.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0776] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.407-2 [@CL-SUP-0776; @CL-SUP-0779] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0776] |
| Applies to | Contractors suspected, upon adequate evidence, of the listed offenses or conduct [@CL-SUP-0776; @CL-SUP-0779] |

The suspending and debarring official may suspend a contractor suspected, upon adequate evidence, of the listed offenses and conduct, the first being commission of fraud or a criminal offense in connection with obtaining, attempting to obtain, or performing a public contract or subcontract [@CL-SUP-0776].

The official may upon adequate evidence also suspend a contractor for any other cause of so serious or compelling a nature that it affects the present responsibility of a Government contractor or subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0779].

## How it works

The listed causes include violation of Federal or State antitrust statutes relating to the submission of offers, and embezzlement, theft, forgery, bribery, falsification or destruction of records, false statements, tax evasion, violating Federal criminal tax laws, or receiving stolen property [@CL-SUP-0776; @CL-SUP-0777]. Indictment for any of the causes in paragraph (a) of the section constitutes adequate evidence for suspension [@CL-SUP-0778].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's owner is indicted for bribery of a purchasing official. No trial has taken place.

Answer: the indictment is adequate evidence for suspension, so the official may suspend the contractor without waiting for a conviction.

## Never confuse

Suspension is not debarment. Suspension needs adequate evidence of a suspected cause [@CL-SUP-0776], and an indictment is enough [@CL-SUP-0778]. Debarment on a listed offense rests on a conviction or civil judgment [@CL-SUP-0766]; see the [Causes for debarment](sup-causes-for-debarment.md) card. The effect of either listing is on the [SAM exclusion records and the effect of listing](sup-sam-exclusion-records-and-effect-of-listing.md) card.
