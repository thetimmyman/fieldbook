## Purpose

Names Alternates I to VII of the construction material under trade agreements clause and shows how they differ from [the basic clause](sup-dfars-bop-construction-material-trade-agreements-clause.md): which countries each reaches, which add a domestic content threshold, and how each is prescribed. It does not repeat the basic clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1890] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7045, Alternates I to VII [@CL-SUP-1890] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1890] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use an alternate as prescribed in the clause text [@CL-SUP-1890; @CL-SUP-1892] |

Alternate I is used as prescribed in 225.7503(b) and (b)(2), adds Bahraini or Mexican construction material to paragraph (a), and uses a different paragraph (b) and (c) than the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1890]. Under Alternate I the Contracting Officer has determined that the WTO GPA and all Free Trade Agreements except the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement and the Bahrain Free Trade Agreement apply to the acquisition, and the Balance of Payments Program restrictions are therefore waived for designated country construction material other than Bahraini or Mexican construction material [@CL-SUP-3986; @CL-SUP-1891]. Alternate II is used as prescribed in 225.7503(b) and (b)(3), adds South Caucasus/Central and South Asian (SC/CASA) state and SC/CASA state construction material to paragraph (a), and uses a different paragraph (b) and introductory text for paragraph (c) than the basic clause, and adds paragraph (d) [@CL-SUP-1892; @CL-SUP-4071]. Under Alternate II the Contracting Officer has determined that the WTO GPA, Free Trade Agreements, and other waivers relating to acquisitions in support of operations in Afghanistan apply to the acquisition, and the Balance of Payments Program restrictions are therefore waived for SC/CASA state and designated country construction materials [@CL-SUP-3987; @CL-SUP-1893].

Under Alternates I and V the Contractor shall use only domestic or designated country construction material other than Bahraini or Mexican construction material, except for the listed exceptions [@CL-SUP-1957]. Under Alternates II and VI the Contractor shall use only domestic, SC/CASA state, or designated country construction material, except for the listed exceptions [@CL-SUP-1958]. Under Alternates III and VII the Contractor shall use only domestic, SC/CASA state, or designated country construction material other than Bahraini or Mexican construction material, except for the listed exceptions [@CL-SUP-1959].

Under Alternates III and VII the Contracting Officer has determined that the WTO GPA, all Free Trade Agreements except the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement and the Bahrain Free Trade Agreement, and other waivers relating to acquisitions in support of operations in Afghanistan apply to the acquisition [@CL-SUP-3988], and the Balance of Payments Program restrictions are therefore waived for SC/CASA state and designated country construction material other than Bahraini or Mexican construction material [@CL-SUP-1895]. Alternate V adds Bahraini or Mexican construction material to paragraph (a) and uses different paragraphs (b) and (c) than the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1919]. Alternates VI and VII each add SC/CASA state construction material to paragraph (a), use a different paragraph (b) and introductory text for paragraph (c) than the basic clause, and add paragraph (d) [@CL-SUP-1920].

Alternates IV, V, VI and VII each include, in the definition of domestic construction material at paragraph (1)(ii)(A), the domestic content threshold that will apply to the entire contract period of performance [@CL-SUP-1896].

## How it works

The alternates differ in the following ways.

- Bahraini or Mexican construction material is left out of the waiver under Alternates I, III, V and VII [@CL-SUP-1891; @CL-SUP-1895].
- SC/CASA state construction material is reached by Alternates II, III, VI and VII [@CL-SUP-1893; @CL-SUP-1895].
- Alternates IV to VII each include, in the definition of domestic construction material, the domestic content threshold that will apply to the entire contract period of performance [@CL-SUP-1896].

Under Alternates II, III, VI and VII a Contractor from an SC/CASA state shall inform its government of its participation in the acquisition and that it generally will not have such opportunity in the future unless its government provides reciprocal procurement opportunities to United States products and services and suppliers of such products and services [@CL-SUP-1894; @CL-SUP-1956].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract uses Alternate I and the contractor buys cement made in Mexico. The cement is Mexican construction material, so the waiver does not reach it, and it may be used only if it is domestic or an exception applies. Cement made in a designated country other than Mexico and Bahrain is covered by the waiver. Assume no exception applies.

## Never confuse

Under Alternate II the restrictions are waived for SC/CASA state and designated country construction materials [@CL-SUP-1893], while under Alternate III they are waived for SC/CASA state and designated country construction material other than Bahraini or Mexican construction material [@CL-SUP-1895]. For the basic clause and the domestic-only clause, see [the basic clause card](sup-dfars-bop-construction-material-trade-agreements-clause.md) and [the construction material clause card](sup-dfars-bop-construction-material-clause.md). For the FAR clause for construction materials, see [Buy American for construction materials](sup-buy-american-construction-materials.md).
