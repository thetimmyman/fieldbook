## Purpose

Shows what happens if a contractor becomes an inverted domestic corporation, and what an offeror represents about unpaid tax liability and felony convictions.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1037; @CL-SUP-1040] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.209-10 [@CL-SUP-1037; @CL-SUP-1038; @CL-SUP-1039; @CL-SUP-1069; @CL-SUP-1538] and FAR 52.209-11 [@CL-SUP-1040; @CL-SUP-1041; @CL-SUP-1042; @CL-SUP-1070; @CL-SUP-1071] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1037] |
| Applies to | Contractors that become inverted domestic corporations during performance, for the clause, and offerors, for the provision [@CL-SUP-1037; @CL-SUP-1041] |

If the contractor reorganizes as an inverted domestic corporation or becomes a subsidiary of an inverted domestic corporation at any time during the period of performance of the contract, the Government may be prohibited from paying for Contractor activities performed after the date when it becomes an inverted domestic corporation or subsidiary [@CL-SUP-1037; @CL-SUP-1069]. The clause says that exceptions to this prohibition are located at FAR 9.108-2 [@CL-SUP-1538]. The clause defines a subsidiary as an entity in which more than 50 percent of the entity is owned directly by a parent corporation or through another subsidiary of a parent corporation [@CL-SUP-1038]. The clause defines an inverted domestic corporation by reference to a statute, which this card does not draft.

If the Contractor becomes either an inverted domestic corporation or a subsidiary of one during contract performance, the Contractor shall give written notice to the Contracting Officer within five business days from the date of the inversion event [@CL-SUP-1039].

## How it works

The provision states that, as required by appropriations act sections and similar provisions if contained in subsequent appropriations acts, the Government will not enter into a contract with any corporation that meets one of the descriptions listed after that phrase, and each description carries its own awareness and determination conditions, stated next [@CL-SUP-1040]. For the unpaid Federal tax liability description, the Government will not contract with the corporation where the awarding agency is aware of the liability, unless an agency has considered suspension or debarment of the corporation and made a determination that suspension or debarment is not necessary to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-SUP-1070]. For the felony conviction description, the Government will not contract with the corporation where the awarding agency is aware of the conviction, unless an agency has considered suspension or debarment of the corporation and made a determination that this action is not necessary to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-SUP-1071]. The Offeror represents whether it is a corporation that has any unpaid Federal tax liability that has been assessed, for which all judicial and administrative remedies have been exhausted or have lapsed, and that is not being paid in a timely manner pursuant to an agreement with the authority responsible for collecting the tax liability [@CL-SUP-1041; @CL-SUP-1072]. One listed representation asks whether the Offeror is or is not a corporation that was convicted of a felony criminal violation under a Federal law within the preceding 24 months [@CL-SUP-1042].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier's parent company reorganizes abroad during performance of a contract. The answer: the supplier checks whether the change makes it an inverted domestic corporation or a subsidiary of one, and if so gives written notice to the Contracting Officer within the stated number of business days.

## Never confuse

The clause applies when a status change happens during performance, and the provision asks for a representation at the offer stage [@CL-SUP-1037; @CL-SUP-1041]. Neither is the same as the debarment and responsibility certifications on [Responsibility certification and information provisions](sup-responsibility-certification-and-information-provisions.md), or the arms control certification on [Arms control treaty certification and ROTC and military recruiting access](sup-arms-control-certification-and-rotc-clauses.md).
