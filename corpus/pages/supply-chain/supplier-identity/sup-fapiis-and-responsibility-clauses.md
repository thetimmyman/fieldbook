## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer must review in the Federal Awardee Performance and Integrity Information System before award, what follows when that review turns up integrity matters, the contractor's update duty, and which responsibility provisions and clause go into solicitations.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0900; @CL-SUP-0907] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.104-6, FAR 9.104-7 and FAR 52.209-9 [@CL-SUP-0900; @CL-SUP-0905; @CL-SUP-0907] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.209-9 [@CL-SUP-3809; @CL-SUP-3810; @CL-SUP-3811; @CL-SUP-3812; @CL-SUP-3813; @CL-SUP-3814] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0900; @CL-SUP-0907] |
| Applies to | Contracts above the simplified acquisition threshold for the review; solicitations and contracts as each provision or clause states [@CL-SUP-0900; @CL-SUP-0904; @CL-SUP-0905; @CL-SUP-0906] |

Before awarding a contract in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold, the contracting officer shall review the performance and integrity information available in the Federal Awardee Performance and Integrity Information System (FAPIIS), available at https://www.cpars.gov, including FAPIIS information from the System for Award Management (SAM) Exclusions and the Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System (CPARS) [@CL-SUP-0900; @CL-SUP-0076]. Under the clause on updates, the contractor shall update the information in FAPIIS on a semi-annual basis throughout the life of the contract by posting the required information in the System for Award Management [@CL-SUP-0907].

The contracting officer shall insert the provision on certification regarding responsibility matters in solicitations where the contract value is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-SUP-0904]. The provision on information regarding responsibility matters goes into solicitations where the resultant contract value is expected to exceed $750,000 [@CL-SUP-0905]. The clause on updates goes into solicitations where the resultant contract value is expected to exceed $750,000 and into contracts in which the offeror checked "has" in paragraph (b) of the provision at 52.209-7 [@CL-SUP-0906; @CL-SUP-0077; @CL-SUP-0905].

## How it works

The additional-information paragraph applies if the contracting officer obtains relevant information from FAPIIS on any one of four alternatives: criminal, civil or administrative proceedings in connection with the award or performance of a Government contract; terminations for default or cause [@CL-SUP-0902]; determinations of nonresponsibility because the contractor does not have a satisfactory performance record or a satisfactory record of integrity and business ethics; or comparable information relating to a grant [@CL-SUP-9728]. In that paragraph, one listed duty is that, unless the contractor has already been debarred, suspended or has agreed to a voluntary exclusion, the contracting officer shall promptly request the additional information the offeror deems necessary to demonstrate its responsibility [@CL-SUP-0903]. The prime contractor may post mitigating factors in FAPIIS, and the contracting officer shall consider any mitigating factors posted in FAPIIS by the prime contractor, such as degree of compliance by the prime contractor with the terms of FAR clause 52.222-50 [@CL-SUP-0901; @CL-SUP-0078].

Under the clause on updates, all data in the non-public segment of FAPIIS is automatically transferred to the publicly-available segment after a waiting period of 14 calendar days, except for past performance reviews required by subpart 42.15, information that was entered prior to April 15, 2011, or information that is withdrawn during the 14-calendar-day waiting period by the Government official who posted it in accordance with paragraph (c)(1) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0975; @CL-SUP-9423; @CL-SUP-0079]. A contractor seeking removal of a posting from FAPIIS under the clause must cite 52.209-9 and request removal within 7 calendar days of the posting [@CL-SUP-0976]. If a contractor asserts in writing within 7 calendar days, to the Government official who posted the information, that some of the information posted to the non-public segment is covered by a disclosure exemption under the Freedom of Information Act, that official must within 7 calendar days remove the posting from FAPIIS and resolve the issue in accordance with agency Freedom of Information procedures prior to reposting the releasable information [@CL-SUP-1000; @CL-SUP-0977; @CL-SUP-4049; @CL-SUP-0976].

The cadence a finance or compliance team must keep under the updates clause is the semi-annual update of FAPIIS information [@CL-SUP-0907].

In RFO FAR part 52, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.209-9(a) says the same as the codified clause: the contractor shall update the information in FAPIIS on a semi-annual basis, throughout the life of the contract, by posting the required information in the System for Award Management [@CL-SUP-0907; @CL-SUP-3809]. RFO FAR 52.209-9(b)(2) says all data in the non-public segment of FAPIIS is automatically transferred to the publicly-available segment after a waiting period of 14 calendar days, except for past performance reviews required by part 42, information that was entered prior to April 15, 2011, or information that is withdrawn during the 14-calendar-day waiting period by the Government official who posted it in accordance with paragraph (c)(1) of the clause [@CL-SUP-3810; @CL-SUP-3811], where the codified clause names past performance reviews required by subpart 42.15 along with the same two other excepted categories and 14-calendar-day waiting period [@CL-SUP-0975; @CL-SUP-9423; @CL-SUP-0079]. RFO FAR 52.209-9(c)(1) says the same as the codified clause on the contractor's written assertion within 7 calendar days to the Government official who posted the information, on that official's duty to remove the posting within 7 calendar days, and on the contractor's duty to cite 52.209-9 and request removal within 7 calendar days of the posting [@CL-SUP-1000; @CL-SUP-0977; @CL-SUP-0976; @CL-SUP-3812; @CL-SUP-3813; @CL-SUP-3814].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation is expected to produce a contract worth 900,000, and the offeror checked "has" in the information provision. The contract runs for 3 years.

Answer: the value is above 750,000, so the information provision and the updates clause both apply. The contractor then updates FAPIIS twice a year for as long as the contract runs, which is 6 updates over the 3 years.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer's review of FAPIIS information before awarding a contract in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold includes FAPIIS information from SAM Exclusions and CPARS, and unless the contractor has already been debarred, suspended, or has agreed to a voluntary exclusion, the contracting officer shall promptly request such additional information from the offeror as the offeror deems necessary in order to demonstrate the offeror's responsibility [@CL-SUP-0900; @CL-SUP-0076; @CL-SUP-0903] (see also [SAM exclusion records and the effect of listing](sup-sam-exclusion-records-and-effect-of-listing.md), [Contractor responsibility standards](../../mna/screening/mna-contractor-responsibility-standards.md), [Obtaining responsibility information and documenting the determination](sup-responsibility-information-and-determination.md), [Ownership or control of offeror](sup-ownership-or-control-of-offeror.md), and [Predecessor of offeror](../../mna/diligence/mna-predecessor-of-offeror.md)).
