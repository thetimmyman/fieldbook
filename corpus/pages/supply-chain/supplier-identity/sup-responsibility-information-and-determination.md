## Purpose

Shows how the contracting officer gathers information before a responsibility determination, what financial information comes from the auditor, and what the contract file and FAPIIS must show.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0908; @CL-SUP-0913] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.105-1 and FAR 9.105-2 [@CL-SUP-0908; @CL-SUP-0913] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0908] |
| Applies to | Contracting officer determinations of responsibility made before award [@CL-SUP-0908] |

Before making a determination of responsibility, the contracting officer shall possess or obtain information sufficient to be satisfied that a prospective contractor currently meets the standards in 9.104 [@CL-SUP-0908] (see [Contractor responsibility standards](../../mna/screening/mna-contractor-responsibility-standards.md)). Generally, the contracting officer shall obtain information regarding the responsibility of prospective contractors, including requesting preaward surveys when necessary, promptly after a bid opening or receipt of offers [@CL-SUP-0981]. However, in negotiated contracting, especially when research and development is involved, the contracting officer may obtain this information before issuing the request for proposals [@CL-SUP-9654]. Requests for information shall ordinarily be limited to information concerning either the low bidder or those offerors in range for award [@CL-SUP-9752]. Information on financial resources and performance capability shall be obtained or updated on as current a basis as is feasible up to the date of award [@CL-SUP-0910].

If the surveying activity is not a contract administration office, the contracting officer shall obtain from the auditor any information required concerning prospective contractors' financial competence and credit needs, the adequacy of their accounting systems, and these systems' suitability for use in administering the proposed type of contract [@CL-SUP-0909; @CL-SUP-9753].

## How it works

In making the determination of responsibility, the contracting officer shall consider information available through FAPIIS with regard to the offeror and any immediate owner, predecessor or subsidiary identified for that offeror in FAPIIS, including information that is linked to FAPIIS such as from SAM and CPARS, as well as any other relevant past performance information on the offeror [@CL-SUP-0980; @CL-SUP-9754]. In addition, the contracting officer should use the listed sources of information to support such determinations [@CL-SUP-0911; @CL-SUP-0980]. One listed source is the prospective contractor, including its bid or proposal information, questionnaire replies, financial data, information on production equipment and personnel information [@CL-SUP-0912].

When an offer on which an award would otherwise be made is rejected because the prospective contractor is found nonresponsible, the contracting officer shall make, sign and place in the contract file a determination of nonresponsibility that states its basis [@CL-SUP-0913]. Documents and reports supporting a determination of responsibility or nonresponsibility, including any preaward survey reports, the use of FAPIIS information and any applicable Certificate of Competency, must be included in the contract file [@CL-SUP-0914].

The contracting officer shall document the determination of nonresponsibility in FAPIIS if the conditions listed next in the paragraph are met [@CL-SUP-0915]. Those conditions are that the contract is valued at more than the simplified acquisition threshold, that the determination rests on lack of a satisfactory performance record or record of integrity and business ethics, and that the Small Business Administration does not issue a Certificate of Competency [@CL-SUP-0916]. The paragraph that follows makes the contracting officer responsible for the timely submission, within 3 working days, and the sufficiency and accuracy of the documentation regarding the nonresponsibility determination [@CL-SUP-0979].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer plans a 2,000,000 award. The preaward survey is run by an office that is not a contract administration office, so the contracting officer asks the auditor about the offeror's financial competence and credit needs.

Answer: the auditor's information and the survey report go into the contract file. If the offeror is found nonresponsible, the contracting officer signs a determination that states its basis and files it too.

## Never confuse

Before making a determination of responsibility, the contracting officer shall possess or obtain information sufficient to be satisfied that a prospective contractor currently meets the standards in 9.104 [@CL-SUP-0908], and when an offer on which an award would otherwise be made is rejected because the prospective contractor is found to be nonresponsible, the contracting officer shall make, sign, and place in the contract file a determination of nonresponsibility, which shall state the basis for the determination [@CL-SUP-0913] (see also [Contractor responsibility standards](../../mna/screening/mna-contractor-responsibility-standards.md), [SAM exclusion records and the effect of listing](sup-sam-exclusion-records-and-effect-of-listing.md), and [FAPIIS review and the responsibility provisions and clauses](sup-fapiis-and-responsibility-clauses.md)).

## Company calibration

Confirm who supplies financial data, credit information and accounting system information when a contracting officer or auditor asks, and how quickly. [@Q-014]
