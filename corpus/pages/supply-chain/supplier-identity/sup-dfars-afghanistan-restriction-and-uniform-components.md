## Purpose

Shows what must come from Afghanistan and what must be produced in the United States under the restricted acquisition clause and the uniform components clause, and what exceptions the printed text gives.

The contractor shall provide only products from Afghanistan or services from Afghanistan under the contract [@CL-SUP-1803].

As required by section 826 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2013, the contractor shall deliver under the contract only textile components that have been produced in the United States [@CL-SUP-1805]. The clause states that there are no exceptions or waivers to this requirement [@CL-SUP-1807]. In the uniform components clause the United States means the 50 States, the District of Columbia, and outlying areas [@CL-SUP-1831].

## How it works

In the restricted acquisition clause, a product from Afghanistan is a product mined, produced, or manufactured in Afghanistan, and a service from Afghanistan is a service, including construction, performed in Afghanistan predominantly by citizens or permanent resident aliens of Afghanistan [@CL-SUP-1804]. In the uniform components clause, a textile component is any item consisting of fibers, yarns, or fabric, supplied for incorporation into a uniform or a component of a uniform [@CL-SUP-1806]. The definition does not include items that do not contain fibers, yarns, or fabric, such as the metallic or plastic elements of buttons, zippers, or other clothing fasteners [@CL-SUP-1830]. The uniform clause sets the place of production as the United States, and it states that there are no exceptions or waivers [@CL-SUP-1805; @CL-SUP-1807]. Each clause text says it is prescribed in a DFARS section it names [@CL-SUP-1842; @CL-SUP-1843].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume a contract that includes the uniform components clause supplies uniforms for the Afghan National Police and has a line for 1,000 shirts.

The fabric supplied for the shirts is a textile component and must be produced in the United States, and no waiver is available. The metallic or plastic elements of the buttons are not textile components. A separate construction contract that includes the restricted acquisition clause must use construction services performed in Afghanistan predominantly by citizens or permanent resident aliens of Afghanistan.

## Never confuse

The restricted acquisition clause allows only products or services from Afghanistan [@CL-SUP-1803], while the requirement clause lets the contractor supply other items when its offer specified them [@CL-SUP-1802]; see [Preference for, and requirement for, products or services from Afghanistan](sup-dfars-afghanistan-preference-and-requirement.md). The uniform clause is different: it requires textile components to be produced in the United States [@CL-SUP-1805]. A separate clause sets a United States manufacturing rule for American flags; see [American flag and supercomputer manufacturing requirements](sup-dfars-american-flag-and-supercomputers.md) [@CL-SUP-1780].
