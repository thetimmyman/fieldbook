## Purpose

Shows what energy furnished to covered military installations may not be sourced from inside the Russian Federation, the stated exceptions, and what an offeror represents.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1706] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7053 [@CL-SUP-1709] and DFARS 252.225-7054 [@CL-SUP-1710] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1710] |
| Applies to | Solicitations with the provision [@CL-SUP-1709], and contracts with the clause where DFARS 225.7019-4(b) prescribes it [@CL-SUP-1765], for furnished energy at a covered military installation [@CL-SUP-1710] |

Contracts for the acquisition of furnished energy for a covered military installation shall not use any energy sourced from inside the Russian Federation as a means of generating that furnished energy, unless a waiver is approved [@CL-SUP-1706]. A covered military installation is a military installation in Europe identified by DoD as a main operating base [@CL-SUP-1708].

The prohibition applies to all forms of energy furnished to a covered military installation, and does not apply to energy converted by a third party into another form of energy and not directly delivered to a covered military installation [@CL-SUP-1707]. By submitting its offer, the offeror represents that it will not use or provide any energy sourced from inside the Russian Federation as a means of generating the furnished energy for the covered military installation in the performance of any contract, subcontract, or other contractual instrument resulting from the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1709; @CL-SUP-9730].

## How it works

The solicitation provision asks for the representation with the offer [@CL-SUP-1709]. The contract clause then bars the Contractor from using such energy in performing the contract, unless a waiver is approved [@CL-SUP-1710]. The clause substance goes into subcontracts and other commercial instruments that are for furnished energy at a covered military installation, including those for commercial products [@CL-SUP-1711]. The prohibition gives way to an approved waiver [@CL-SUP-1706], and it does not apply to energy converted by a third party into another form and not directly delivered [@CL-SUP-1707].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume a contract supplies electricity to a covered military installation, a main operating base in Europe. The supplier generates the electricity from gas that comes from inside the Russian Federation, and no waiver is approved. The prohibition bars that use. If a third party instead converts the energy into another form and does not deliver it directly to the installation, the stated exception for that energy applies.

## Never confuse

The representation is made once, with the offer, and speaks to use or provision of the energy in performing any contract, subcontract or other instrument resulting from the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1709; @CL-SUP-9730]. The clause prohibition applies to use in performing the contract [@CL-SUP-1710]. Neither text states a general ban on all Russian energy, because the prohibition concerns energy used to generate furnished energy for a covered installation [@CL-SUP-1706]. Another provision restricts fuel for overseas contingency operations through a certification [@CL-SUP-1737; @CL-SUP-1738]; see [Diesel engines, vessel components and overseas contingency operation fuel](sup-dfars-diesel-engines-tao-vessel-components-and-ocs-fuel.md). The Maduro regime provision is a separate representation made with the offer [@CL-SUP-1714]; see [Business operations with the Maduro regime](sup-dfars-maduro-regime-business-operations-representation-and-prohibition.md). For commercial flowdown generally, see [Subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](../sourcing-evidence/sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md), and for the purchasing system, see [Contractor Purchasing System Administration clause](../procure-to-pay/sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md).
