## Purpose

Shows who is a covered employee, what the Contractor must do to screen, prevent and report personal conflicts of interest, and which subcontracts carry the clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1007] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-16 [@CL-SUP-1007; @CL-SUP-1008; @CL-SUP-1010; @CL-SUP-1011; @CL-SUP-1057] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1007] |
| Applies to | The Contractor, with respect to its covered employees [@CL-SUP-1007; @CL-SUP-1008] |

A covered employee is an individual who performs an acquisition function closely associated with inherently governmental functions and is either an employee of the contractor or a subcontractor that is a self-employed individual treated as a covered employee of the contractor [@CL-SUP-1007].

Under its listed requirements, the Contractor shall have procedures in place to screen covered employees for potential personal conflicts of interest, by obtaining and maintaining from each covered employee, when the employee is initially assigned to the task under the contract, a disclosure of interests [@CL-SUP-1008]. One listed duty of the Contractor for each covered employee is to prevent personal conflicts of interest [@CL-SUP-1009].

## How it works

The screening procedures and the duty to prevent conflicts are two of the Contractor's listed requirements [@CL-SUP-1008; @CL-SUP-1009]. One further listed duty is to report to the Contracting Officer any personal conflict-of-interest violation by a covered employee as soon as it is identified, with a description of the violation and the proposed actions the Contractor will take in response [@CL-SUP-1010]. The Contractor shall include the substance of the clause in subcontracts that meet two conditions, the first being that they exceed the simplified acquisition threshold as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award [@CL-SUP-1011]. The second listed condition is that subcontractor employees will perform acquisition functions closely associated with inherently governmental functions [@CL-SUP-1011; @CL-SUP-1012]. In exceptional circumstances, if the Contractor cannot satisfactorily prevent a personal conflict of interest as required by the prevention paragraph, the Contractor may submit a request through the Contracting Officer to the Head of the Contracting Activity for the items the clause then lists [@CL-SUP-1057]. The listed items are agreement to a plan to mitigate the personal conflict of interest, or a waiver of the requirement [@CL-SUP-1058]. The clause text lists further duties, which this card does not draft.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A support contractor assigns an analyst to review contract proposals for an agency. The analyst is a covered employee. The contractor collects the analyst's disclosure at assignment. Later the analyst's spouse buys shares in a bidder. The answer: the contractor treats this as a possible new conflict, and if a violation is identified it reports the violation and its proposed response to the Contracting Officer.
