## Purpose

Shows what the contractor must allow for qualifying country sources as subcontractors, and when it reports intended performance outside the United States and Canada, with the threshold and timing.

## The rule

| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7002 [@CL-SUP-1505]; DFARS 252.225-7003 [@CL-SUP-1506]; DFARS 252.225-7004 [@CL-SUP-1509] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1505] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the subcontractors clause, offers with the offer report provision, and contracts with the after-award report clause [@CL-SUP-1505; @CL-SUP-1506; @CL-SUP-1509] |

Subject to the restrictions in section 225.872 of the Defense FAR Supplement, the Contractor shall not preclude qualifying country sources or United States sources from competing for subcontracts under the contract [@CL-SUP-1505].

The offer provision requires the Offeror to submit with its offer a report of intended performance outside the United States and Canada if the offer exceeds $20 million in value and a second listed condition is also met [@CL-SUP-1506]. The second listed condition is that the Offeror is aware that it or a first-tier subcontractor intends to perform any part of the contract outside the United States and Canada that exceeds $900,000 in value and could be performed inside the United States or Canada [@CL-SUP-1507].

The after-award clause requires the Contractor to submit a report if the Contractor or a first-tier subcontractor will perform any part of the contract outside the United States and Canada that meets the listed conditions [@CL-SUP-1509]. The listed conditions are that the part exceeds the threshold specified in Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement 225.7201(a) on the date of award of the contract and could be performed inside the United States or Canada [@CL-SUP-1510]. On timing, the Contractor shall submit a report as soon as practical after the information is known and, to the maximum extent practicable, shall submit a report regarding a first-tier subcontractor at least 30 days before award of the subcontract [@CL-SUP-1508]. The Contractor need not resubmit information submitted with its offer unless the information changes, and shall submit all reports to the Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-1535]. The Contractor shall submit reports using DD Form 2139 or a computer-generated report that contains all information required by DD Form 2139 [@CL-SUP-1534]. The offer provision likewise requires the Offeror to submit its report using DD Form 2139 or a computer-generated report that contains all information required by DD Form 2139 [@CL-SUP-1533].

## How it works

The subcontractors clause is a competition rule: qualifying country sources and U.S. sources are not to be precluded from competing for subcontracts, subject to the restrictions in section 225.872 of the Defense FAR Supplement [@CL-SUP-1505]. The offer provision sets a dollar test on the offer and a dollar test on the part to be performed abroad [@CL-SUP-1506; @CL-SUP-1507]. After award, the clause uses the threshold specified in Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement 225.7201(a) on the date of award [@CL-SUP-1510], and it sets timing for reports [@CL-SUP-1508]. Both texts use the same two report formats [@CL-SUP-1533; @CL-SUP-1534].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offer is worth 30 million. The offeror intends a first-tier subcontractor to perform 1.2 million of work outside the United States and Canada, and that work could be done inside the United States or Canada.

Answer: both listed conditions of the offer provision are met, so the offeror submits the report with its offer. After award, assume the threshold in force is 1 million, a made-up figure. A report is then due as soon as practical, and, to the maximum extent practicable, a first-tier subcontract report is due at least 30 days before that subcontract is awarded.

## Never confuse

The offer provision and the after-award clause use different triggers. The offer provision has a dollar test on the offer and a dollar test on the part to be performed abroad [@CL-SUP-1506; @CL-SUP-1507], while the clause uses the threshold specified in Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement 225.7201(a) on the date of award [@CL-SUP-1510]. For the general duty on competition in subcontracting, see [Competition in Subcontracting clause](../sourcing-evidence/sup-competition-in-subcontracting.md). For the photovoltaic devices clause, see [Photovoltaic devices clause and certificate](sup-dfars-photovoltaic-devices-clause-and-certificate.md).
