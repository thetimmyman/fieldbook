## Purpose

Shows which countries, materials and deliveries the DoD clause on magnets, tantalum and tungsten restricts, the exceptions it states, and the flowdown.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1331] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7052 [@CL-SUP-1331] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1331] |
| Applies to | Deliveries under the contract of covered material, and of end items that contain covered material [@CL-SUP-1331] |

The covered countries are North Korea, China, Russia and Iran [@CL-SUP-1329]. Covered material means samarium-cobalt magnets, neodymium-iron-boron magnets, tantalum metals and alloys, tungsten metal powder, and tungsten heavy alloy or any finished or semi-finished component containing tungsten heavy alloy [@CL-SUP-1330]. Except as provided in the exceptions paragraph, effective through December 31, 2026, the Contractor shall not deliver under the contract any covered material melted or produced in any covered country, or any end item, manufactured in any covered country, that contains a covered material [@CL-SUP-1331]. A further listed bar, effective January 1, 2027, is that the Contractor shall not deliver under the contract any covered material mined, refined, separated, melted, or produced in any covered country, or any end item, manufactured in any covered country, that contains a covered material [@CL-SUP-1332]. The exceptions lead-in opens both the earlier and the later bar [@CL-SUP-1331; @CL-SUP-1332].

One listed exception covers an end item containing a covered material that is a commercially available off-the-shelf item, other than the listed carve-outs, the first of which is an item that is 50 percent or more tungsten by weight effective through December 31, 2026 [@CL-SUP-1333]. The exception for end items containing a covered material that are commercially available off-the-shelf items does not cover, effective January 1, 2027, an item that is 50 percent or more covered material by weight [@CL-SUP-1368]. One listed exception applies if the authorized agency official concerned has made a nonavailability determination in accordance with DFARS 225.7018-4 [@CL-SUP-1369]. The nonavailability determination is that compliant covered materials of satisfactory quality and quantity, in the required form, cannot be procured as and when needed at a reasonable price [@CL-SUP-1374].

## How it works

The restriction looks at where the covered material was produced and where the end item was manufactured [@CL-SUP-1331]. From the later date the barred stages widen to include mining, refining and separating [@CL-SUP-1332]. The Contractor shall insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (d), in subcontracts and other contractual instruments that are for items containing a covered material, including those for commercial products, unless an exception in paragraph (c) applies [@CL-SUP-3996; @CL-SUP-1334].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor plans to deliver, before the later date, a line item that is a neodymium-iron-boron magnet whose alloy was melted in a covered country. No exception applies. The clause bars that delivery. A subcontract for items containing the magnet carries the flowdown paragraph, because no exception applies.

## Never confuse

Under the specialty metals clause, except as provided in paragraph (c) of that clause, any specialty metals incorporated in items delivered under the contract shall be melted or produced in the United States, its outlying areas, or a qualifying country [@CL-SUP-1324]; the restriction does not apply to electronic components, the first listed exception [@CL-SUP-1325]. Under this clause, except as provided in the exceptions paragraph and effective through December 31, 2026, the Contractor shall not deliver covered material melted or produced in a covered country [@CL-SUP-1331; @CL-SUP-1329]. The restriction on melting and producing samarium-cobalt magnets is in addition to any applicable restrictions on melting of specialty metals if the specialty metals clause is included in the contract [@CL-SUP-1370]; see [Restriction on acquisition of articles containing specialty metals](sup-dfars-specialty-metals-restriction.md). For flowdown into commercial subcontracts generally, see [Subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](../sourcing-evidence/sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md).
