## Purpose

Shows what an offeror states about where its end products are manufactured, and what it represents and certifies about Iran-related activities.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1269; @CL-SUP-1271] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.225-18 [@CL-SUP-1269]; FAR 52.225-25 [@CL-SUP-1271] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.225-18 [@CL-SUP-3735]; RFO FAR 52.240-90(f) [@CL-SUP-3740], as RFO FAR part 52 lists FAR 52.225-25 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-3739] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1269; @CL-SUP-1271] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to solicitations that include the provisions [@CL-SUP-1269; @CL-SUP-1271] |

For statistical purposes only, the offeror shall indicate whether the place of manufacture of the end products it expects to provide in response to the solicitation is predominantly in the United States (checking that box if the total anticipated price of offered end products manufactured in the United States exceeds the total anticipated price of offered end products manufactured outside the United States) or outside the United States [@CL-SUP-1269; @CL-SUP-1270; @CL-SUP-0277]. Place of manufacture means the place where an end product is assembled out of components, or otherwise made or processed from raw materials into the finished product that is to be provided to the Government [@CL-SUP-1297]. If a product is disassembled and reassembled, the place of reassembly is not the place of manufacture [@CL-SUP-1298].

By submitting its offer, the offeror makes the listed representation and certifications, except as provided in the trade agreements exception or where a waiver has been granted under FAR 25.703-4 [@CL-SUP-1271].

## How it works

Under the Iran provision, the offeror represents, to the best of its knowledge and belief, that it does not export any sensitive technology to the government of Iran or to entities or individuals owned or controlled by, or acting on behalf or at the direction of, the government of Iran [@CL-SUP-1272]. It certifies that it, or any person owned or controlled by it, does not engage in any activities for which sanctions may be imposed under section 5 of the Iran Sanctions Act [@CL-SUP-1273]. These sanctioned activities are in the areas of development of the petroleum resources of Iran, production of refined petroleum products in Iran, sale and provision of refined petroleum products to Iran, and contributing to Iran's ability to acquire or develop certain weapons or technologies [@CL-SUP-0278]. It certifies that it, and any person owned or controlled by it, does not knowingly engage in any transaction that exceeds the threshold at FAR 25.703-2(a)(2) with Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps or any of its officials, agents, or affiliates [@CL-SUP-1274]. The provision describes the property and interests in property of the Revolutionary Guard Corps or any of its officials, agents, or affiliates as blocked pursuant to the International Emergency Economic Powers Act, title 50 of the United States Code, section 1701 et seq, with a reference to OFAC's Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List [@CL-SUP-1300; @CL-SUP-0279].

The trade agreements exception applies when both of two listed conditions are met [@CL-SUP-1275; @CL-SUP-0283; @CL-SUP-1299]. One is that the solicitation includes a trade agreements notice or certification, such as 52.225-4, 52.225-6, 52.225-12, 52.225-24, or a comparable agency provision [@CL-SUP-1275; @CL-SUP-0283]. The other is that the offeror has certified that all the offered products to be supplied are designated country end products or designated country construction material [@CL-SUP-1299].

In RFO FAR part 52, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.225-18 keeps the statistical indication of the place of manufacture, the United States and outside-the-United-States boxes, the definition of place of manufacture and the reassembly rule [@CL-SUP-3735; @CL-SUP-3736; @CL-SUP-3737; @CL-SUP-3738; @CL-SUP-1269; @CL-SUP-1270; @CL-SUP-0277; @CL-SUP-1297; @CL-SUP-1298]. RFO FAR part 52 lists FAR 52.225-25 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-3739]. In RFO FAR part 52, the Iran representation and certifications are in paragraph (f) of FAR 52.240-90, which says that, except as provided in paragraph (f)(2) or if a waiver has been granted in accordance with FAR 40.203-3, the offeror, after conducting a reasonable inquiry (that looks at any information in the offeror's possession but does not need to include an internal or third-party audit), makes the representation and certifications [@CL-SUP-3740]. Its paragraph (f)(1)(i) keeps the representation about sensitive technology [@CL-SUP-3741; @CL-SUP-1272]. Its paragraph (f)(1)(ii) says the offeror certifies that it, or any person (as defined at section 15 of the Iran Sanctions Act of 1996) owned or controlled by it, does not engage in any activities for which sanctions may be imposed under section 5 of the Act, in the same sanctioned areas of development of the petroleum resources of Iran, production of refined petroleum products in Iran, sale and provision of refined petroleum products to Iran, and contributing to Iran's ability to acquire or develop certain weapons or technologies [@CL-SUP-3742; @CL-CMP-9260; @CL-SUP-1273; @CL-SUP-0278]. Its paragraph (f)(1)(iii) says the offeror certifies that it, and any person owned or controlled by it, does not knowingly engage in any transaction that exceeds $15,000 with Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps or any of its officials, agents, or affiliates [@CL-SUP-3743], and keeps the statement describing their property and interests in property as blocked pursuant to the International Emergency Economic Powers Act, title 50 of the United States Code, section 1701 et seq, with a reference to OFAC's Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List [@CL-SUP-3744; @CL-CMP-1495; @CL-SUP-1300; @CL-SUP-0279]. Its paragraph (f)(2) says the requirements of paragraph (f)(1) do not apply if the solicitation includes a trade agreements notice or certification, such as 52.225-4, 52.225-6, 52.225-12, 52.225-24, or a comparable agency provision, and a second condition in paragraph (f)(2)(ii) is also met [@CL-SUP-3745]; that second condition, the designated country certification, is kept [@CL-SUP-3746; @CL-SUP-1299]. The codified exception names paragraphs (c)(1), (c)(2) and (c)(3) of the provision [@CL-SUP-1275; @CL-SUP-0283], where the RFO exception names paragraph (f)(1) as a whole [@CL-SUP-3745].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror offers two end products. Product A is made in the United States and priced at 80,000. Product B is made abroad and priced at 30,000. The total anticipated price of the end products made in the United States is higher than the total for those made outside, so the offeror checks the box for the United States.

## Never confuse

The place of manufacture answer is for statistical purposes only [@CL-SUP-1269]. The domestic end product test is a separate test under the Buy American supplies clause [@CL-SUP-1230], covered on [Buy American clause for supplies](sup-buy-american-supplies-clause.md) (see also [Buy American certificates in offers](sup-buy-american-certificates.md) and [Buy American, Free Trade Agreements and Israeli Trade Act clause](sup-buy-american-fta-israeli-clause.md)). The Iran representation is about exports of sensitive technology [@CL-SUP-1272]. The two certifications are about sanctionable activities and about transactions with the Revolutionary Guard Corps [@CL-SUP-1273; @CL-SUP-1274]. The codified provision refers to the threshold at FAR 25.703-2(a)(2) [@CL-SUP-1274], unlike RFO FAR 52.240-90(f)(1)(iii), which specifies $15,000 [@CL-SUP-3743]. The codified provision cites a waiver under FAR 25.703-4 [@CL-SUP-1271], while RFO FAR 52.240-90(f)(1) cites a waiver in accordance with FAR 40.203-3 and has the offeror make the representation and certifications after a reasonable inquiry [@CL-SUP-3740].
