## Purpose

Shows how Alternates I to XI of the basic clause differ from it: which country groups each alternate adds to or drops from the delivery paragraph, which fix the domestic content threshold for the whole contract, and what the clause text says about when each is used. It does not repeat the basic clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1871] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7036, Alternates I to XI [@CL-SUP-1871; @CL-SUP-1909] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1871] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use an alternate together with the matching alternate certificate provision [@CL-SUP-1872; @CL-SUP-1874; @CL-SUP-1876] |

Alternate I is used as prescribed in 225.1101(10)(i) and (10)(i)(B), and it uses a different paragraph (c) than the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1871]. Under Alternate I the Contractor shall deliver only domestic end products unless, in its offer, it specified delivery of qualifying country, or other foreign end products in the Alternate I certificate provision of the solicitation, so the list names no Free Trade Agreement country end products [@CL-SUP-1872]. Alternate II is used as prescribed in 225.1101(10)(i) and (10)(i)(C), adds South Caucasus/Central and South Asian (SC/CASA) state and SC/CASA state end product to paragraph (a), and uses a different paragraph (c) than the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1873]. Under Alternates II and VIII the delivery paragraph departs from the domestic end product requirement if, in its offer, the Contractor specified delivery of qualifying country end products, SC/CASA state end products, Free Trade Agreement country end products other than Bahraini, Moroccan, Panamanian or Peruvian end products, or other foreign end products [@CL-SUP-1874].

Alternate III is used as prescribed in 225.1101(10)(i) and (10)(i)(D), adds SC/CASA state and SC/CASA state end product to paragraph (a), and uses a different paragraph (c) than the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1875]. Under Alternates III and IX the Contractor delivers only domestic end products unless, in its offer, it specified delivery of qualifying country end products, SC/CASA state end products, or other foreign end products in the Alternate III certificate provision of the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1876]. Under Alternate III, if the Contractor certified in its offer that it will deliver a qualifying country end product or SC/CASA state end products, the Contractor shall deliver a qualifying country end product, an SC/CASA state end product or, at its option, a domestic end product [@CL-SUP-1938]. Alternate IV is used as prescribed in 225.1101(10)(i) and (10)(i)(E), adds Korean end product to paragraph (a), and uses a different paragraph (c) than the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1903]. Alternate V is used as prescribed in 225.1101(10)(i) and (10)(i)(F), adds Korean end product and the SC/CASA terms to paragraph (a), and uses a different paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-1904].

The later alternates fix the threshold. Alternates VI to XI each include the domestic content threshold that will apply to the entire contract period of performance [@CL-SUP-1905], in the definitions of domestic end product at paragraph (1)(ii)(A) and qualifying country end product at paragraph (2)(i) [@CL-SUP-1939]. Where the clause says the cost of the counted components exceeds a percentage for the entire period of performance, the percentage is 60 percent for a contract awarded in calendar year 2023, 65 percent for calendar years 2024 through 2028, and 75 percent for calendar year 2029 or later [@CL-SUP-1921]. Alternate VI keeps the basic delivery text [@CL-SUP-1861] and adds the threshold [@CL-SUP-1905]. Alternate VII adds a different paragraph (c) to the threshold [@CL-SUP-1906]. Alternates VIII and IX each add the SC/CASA terms to paragraph (a) and use a different paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-1907]. Alternate X adds Korean end product to paragraph (a) and uses a different paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-1908]. Alternate XI adds Korean end product and the SC/CASA terms to paragraph (a) and uses a different paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-1909].

## How it works

Each alternate swaps the basic delivery paragraph, or the threshold, or both, and the clause text prints the change at the head of the alternate. The country groups move in three ways. Alternate I drops the Free Trade Agreement country end products from the list of what an offer may specify [@CL-SUP-1872]. The SC/CASA state end product group is added by Alternates II, III, V, VIII, IX and XI [@CL-SUP-1873; @CL-SUP-1875; @CL-SUP-1904; @CL-SUP-1907; @CL-SUP-1909]. Korean end product is added to paragraph (a) by Alternates IV, V, X and XI [@CL-SUP-1903; @CL-SUP-1904; @CL-SUP-1908; @CL-SUP-1909].

Each alternate is printed with its own prescription cross-reference into DFARS 225.1101(10)(i) [@CL-SUP-1871; @CL-SUP-1873; @CL-SUP-1875; @CL-SUP-1903; @CL-SUP-1904]. The prescription text that says when each one goes into a solicitation is not among the sources here, and a contract's own clause list shows which alternate it carries. The delivery paragraph of an alternate refers to the matching alternate of the certificate provision [@CL-SUP-1872; @CL-SUP-1876].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation uses Alternate III. An offeror specifies a South Caucasus/Central and South Asian state end product for line item one and a domestic end product for line item two. Under the delivery paragraph the contractor delivers the state end product for line item one, or at its option a domestic end product, and a domestic end product for line item two. A second contract is awarded in calendar year 2026 and uses Alternate VI. The percentage is then 65 percent for the entire period of performance, even for items delivered in 2029, where the basic clause would step to 75 percent.

## Never confuse

Alternate II is not Alternate III. Alternate II still lists Free Trade Agreement country end products other than Bahraini, Moroccan, Panamanian or Peruvian end products [@CL-SUP-1874], and Alternate III names qualifying country, SC/CASA state and other foreign end products [@CL-SUP-1876]. The threshold alternates are not a separate family: Alternates VI to XI all fix the threshold [@CL-SUP-1905], and Alternates VIII to XI also add country terms [@CL-SUP-1907; @CL-SUP-1908; @CL-SUP-1909]. The basic clause and its definitions are on the [basic clause card](sup-dfars-buy-american-fta-bop-clause.md), and the offeror's side, with its own alternates, is on the [certificate card](sup-dfars-buy-american-fta-bop-certificate.md). The older DoD clause is on [DoD Buy American and Balance of Payments Program clause: definitions and delivery rule](sup-dfars-buy-american-definitions-and-delivery-rule.md). The FAR clause with the Israeli Trade Act is a separate clause, covered on [Buy American, Free Trade Agreements and Israeli Trade Act clause](sup-buy-american-fta-israeli-clause.md).
