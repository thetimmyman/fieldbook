## Purpose

Shows what an offeror certifies about debarment, convictions, taxes and default terminations, and what it represents about its Federal contract total and listed proceedings.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1018] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.209-5 [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1017; @CL-SUP-1059] and FAR 52.209-7 [@CL-SUP-1018; @CL-SUP-1019; @CL-SUP-1062] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.209-5 [@CL-SUP-3767]; RFO FAR 52.209-7 [@CL-SUP-3775] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1013] |
| Applies to | Offerors, for the certification and information provisions [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1018] |

The Offeror certifies, to the best of its knowledge and belief, whether it and/or any of its Principals are or are not presently debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment, or declared ineligible for the award of contracts by any Federal agency [@CL-SUP-1013]. One listed certification asks whether, within a three-year period preceding the offer, the Offeror and/or any of its Principals have been convicted of or had a civil judgment rendered against them for commission of fraud or a criminal offense in connection with obtaining, attempting to obtain, or performing a public contract or subcontract, among other listed offenses [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1014]. Another asks whether the Offeror and/or any of its Principals have been notified, within a three-year period preceding the offer, of any delinquent Federal taxes in an amount that exceeds the threshold at FAR 9.104-5(a)(2) for which the liability remains unsatisfied [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1015]. Federal taxes are considered delinquent if both of two criteria apply: the tax liability is finally determined (which is so if the liability has been assessed, and not so if there is a pending administrative or judicial challenge; in the case of a judicial challenge, not until all judicial appeal rights have been exhausted), and the taxpayer is delinquent in making payment (which is so if the taxpayer has failed to pay the tax liability when full payment was due and required, and not so where enforced collection action is precluded) [@CL-SUP-9747; @CL-SUP-9755; @CL-SUP-9748]. The Offeror also certifies whether it has, within a 3-year period preceding the offer, had one or more contracts terminated for default by any Federal agency [@CL-SUP-1016].

In the information provision, the offeror states whether it has or does not have current active Federal contracts and grants with total value greater than $10,000,000 [@CL-SUP-1018]. The provision defines that total as two amounts together: the total value of all current, active contracts and grants, including all priced options, and the total value of all current, active orders, including all priced options, under indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity, 8(a), or requirements contracts, including those under task and delivery and multiple-award Schedules [@CL-SUP-9750; @CL-SUP-9751]. If it checked that it has such contracts and grants, it represents by submitting its offer that the information it has entered in the Federal Awardee Performance and Integrity Information System is current, accurate, and complete as of the date of submission of the offer with regard to the information the provision then lists [@CL-SUP-1019; @CL-SUP-1062].

## How it works

A certification that any of the listed items exists will not necessarily result in withholding of an award, but the certification will be considered in connection with a determination of the Offeror's responsibility [@CL-SUP-1017]. In the certification provision, the Offeror shall provide immediate written notice to the Contracting Officer if, at any time prior to contract award, the Offeror learns that its certification was erroneous when submitted or has become erroneous by reason of changed circumstances [@CL-SUP-1059]. Failure of the Offeror to furnish a certification or provide such additional information as requested by the Contracting Officer may render the Offeror nonresponsible [@CL-SUP-1061]. If it is later determined that the Offeror knowingly rendered an erroneous certification, in addition to other remedies available to the Government, the Contracting Officer may terminate the contract resulting from the solicitation for default [@CL-SUP-1060]. If the offeror checked that it has such contracts and grants, one listed item of the information provision asks whether the offeror and/or any of its principals has or has not, within the last five years and in connection with the award to or performance by the offeror of a Federal contract or grant, been the subject of a proceeding at the Federal or State level that resulted in any of the listed dispositions [@CL-SUP-1020]. One listed disposition is, in a civil proceeding, a finding of fault and liability that results in the payment of a monetary fine, penalty, reimbursement, restitution, or damages of $5,000 or more [@CL-SUP-1021]. Another is, in an administrative proceeding, a finding of fault and liability that results in payment of a fine or penalty of $5,000 or more, or payment of a reimbursement, restitution, or damages in excess of $100,000 [@CL-SUP-1022]. A second listed item asks, if the offeror has been involved in the last five years in any of the occurrences listed in the first item, whether the offeror has provided the requested information with regard to each occurrence [@CL-SUP-9749].

In RFO FAR part 52, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.209-5 keeps the certifications in paragraph (a) about debarment and related status, convictions and judgments, delinquent Federal taxes and default terminations [@CL-SUP-3767; @CL-SUP-3768; @CL-SUP-3769; @CL-SUP-3770; @CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1014; @CL-SUP-1015; @CL-SUP-1016]. It keeps the notice duty in paragraph (b) [@CL-SUP-3772; @CL-SUP-1059] and the nonresponsibility rule in paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-3773; @CL-SUP-1061]. Its paragraph (c) says the Government will consider the certification in connection with a determination of the Offeror's responsibility, where the codified provision says the certification will be considered [@CL-SUP-3771; @CL-SUP-1017]. Its paragraph (e) says that if the Government later determines that the Offeror knowingly rendered an erroneous certification, the Contracting Officer may terminate the contract for default, where the codified provision says it is later determined [@CL-SUP-3774; @CL-SUP-1060]. RFO FAR 52.209-7 keeps the contract-and-grant total representation in paragraph (b) [@CL-SUP-3775; @CL-SUP-1018] and the representation about the entries in paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-3776; @CL-SUP-3777; @CL-SUP-1019; @CL-SUP-1062]. It keeps the five-year proceedings in paragraph (c)(1), including the $5,000 civil and administrative dispositions and the $100,000 amount [@CL-SUP-3778; @CL-SUP-3779; @CL-SUP-3780; @CL-SUP-1020; @CL-SUP-1021; @CL-SUP-1022].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror has current active Federal contracts and grants well above the stated total. It checks the box that it has them. It confirms that its entries in the performance and integrity system are current. The answer: it makes the representation and must keep the entries accurate as of the offer date, while the certification provision separately asks about debarment, convictions, tax notices and default terminations.

## Never confuse

The certification provision looks back three years: for the Offeror and/or any of its Principals on convictions and civil judgments and on delinquent tax notices [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1014; @CL-SUP-1015], and for the Offeror on default terminations [@CL-SUP-1016] (see also [Inverted domestic corporations and tax or felony representations](sup-inverted-corporation-and-tax-felony-provisions.md) and [Arms control treaty certification and ROTC and military recruiting access](sup-arms-control-certification-and-rotc-clauses.md)). The information provision is a separate five-year look-back at proceedings in connection with a Federal contract or grant, again for the offeror and/or any of its principals, and the representation about the entries applies if the offeror checked that it has current active Federal contracts and grants above the stated total [@CL-SUP-1019; @CL-SUP-1020] (see [FAPIIS and responsibility clauses](sup-fapiis-and-responsibility-clauses.md) and [Responsibility information and determination](sup-responsibility-information-and-determination.md)).
