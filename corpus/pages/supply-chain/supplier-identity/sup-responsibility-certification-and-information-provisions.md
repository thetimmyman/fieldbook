## Purpose

Shows what an offeror certifies about debarment, convictions, taxes and default terminations, and what it represents about its Federal contract total and listed proceedings.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1018] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.209-5 [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1017; @CL-SUP-1059] and FAR 52.209-7 [@CL-SUP-1018; @CL-SUP-1019; @CL-SUP-1062] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1013] |
| Applies to | Offerors, for the certification and information provisions [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1018] |

The Offeror certifies, to the best of its knowledge and belief, whether it and any of its Principals are or are not presently debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment, or declared ineligible for the award of contracts by any Federal agency [@CL-SUP-1013]. One listed certification asks whether, within a three-year period preceding the offer, the Offeror and/or any of its Principals have been convicted of or had a civil judgment rendered against them for commission of fraud or a criminal offense in connection with obtaining, attempting to obtain, or performing a public contract or subcontract, among other listed offenses [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1014]. Another asks whether the Offeror and/or any of its Principals have been notified, within a three-year period preceding the offer, of any delinquent Federal taxes in an amount that exceeds the threshold at FAR 9.104-5(a)(2) for which the liability remains unsatisfied [@CL-SUP-1013; @CL-SUP-1015]. The source does not establish the amount of that tax threshold, because the provision points to another FAR section [@CL-SUP-1015]. The Offeror also certifies whether it has, within a 3-year period preceding the offer, had one or more contracts terminated for default by any Federal agency [@CL-SUP-1016].

In the information provision, the offeror states whether it has or does not have current active Federal contracts and grants with total value greater than $10,000,000 [@CL-SUP-1018]. If it checked that it has such contracts and grants, it represents by submitting its offer that the information it has entered in the Federal Awardee Performance and Integrity Information System is current, accurate, and complete as of the date of submission of the offer with regard to the information the provision then lists [@CL-SUP-1019; @CL-SUP-1062].

## How it works

A certification that any of the listed items exists will not necessarily result in withholding of an award, but the certification will be considered in connection with a determination of the Offeror's responsibility [@CL-SUP-1017]. In the certification provision, the Offeror shall provide immediate written notice to the Contracting Officer if, at any time prior to contract award, the Offeror learns that its certification was erroneous when submitted or has become erroneous by reason of changed circumstances [@CL-SUP-1059]. Failure of the Offeror to furnish a certification or provide such additional information as requested by the Contracting Officer may render the Offeror nonresponsible [@CL-SUP-1061]. If it is later determined that the Offeror knowingly rendered an erroneous certification, in addition to other remedies available to the Government, the Contracting Officer may terminate the contract resulting from the solicitation for default [@CL-SUP-1060]. The information provision looks back over the last five years at proceedings in connection with the award to or performance of a Federal contract or grant [@CL-SUP-1020]. One listed disposition is, in a civil proceeding, a finding of fault and liability that results in a payment of $5,000 or more [@CL-SUP-1021]. Another is, in an administrative proceeding, a finding of fault and liability that results in payment of a fine or penalty of $5,000 or more, or payment of a reimbursement, restitution, or damages in excess of $100,000 [@CL-SUP-1022]. The linked pages cover the review and posting of this information.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror has current active Federal contracts and grants well above the stated total. It checks the box that it has them. It confirms that its entries in the performance and integrity system are current. The answer: it makes the representation and must keep the entries accurate as of the offer date, while the certification provision separately asks about debarment, convictions, tax notices and default terminations.

## Never confuse

The certification provision looks back three years for convictions, tax notices and default terminations [@CL-SUP-1014; @CL-SUP-1015; @CL-SUP-1016], while the information provision looks back five years for listed proceedings [@CL-SUP-1020]. For the separate representation on unpaid tax liability and felony convictions, see [Inverted domestic corporations and tax or felony representations](sup-inverted-corporation-and-tax-felony-provisions.md), and for the arms control certification, see [Arms control treaty certification and ROTC and military recruiting access](sup-arms-control-certification-and-rotc-clauses.md). For posting, review and determination, see [FAPIIS and responsibility clauses](sup-fapiis-and-responsibility-clauses.md) and [Responsibility information and determination](sup-responsibility-information-and-determination.md).
