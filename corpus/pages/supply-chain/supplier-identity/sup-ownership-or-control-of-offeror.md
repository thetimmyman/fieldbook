## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must say about its immediate owner and highest-level owner, including their CAGE codes and legal names.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0562] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-17 [@CL-SUP-0562] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0562] |
| Applies to | Offerors, where the provision is used as prescribed in FAR 4.1804(b) [@CL-SUP-0695] |

An immediate owner means an entity, other than the offeror, that has direct control of the offeror [@CL-SUP-0562]. A highest-level owner means the entity that owns or controls an immediate owner of the offeror, or that owns or controls one or more entities that control an immediate owner of the offeror, and no entity owns or exercises control of the highest level owner [@CL-SUP-0563].

Indicators of control include, but are not limited to, ownership or interlocking management, identity of interests among family members, shared facilities and equipment, and the common use of employees [@CL-SUP-0696].

## How it works

The offeror represents whether it has or does not have an immediate owner [@CL-SUP-0697]. If it has one, it enters the immediate owner CAGE code, the immediate owner legal name, and whether the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity [@CL-SUP-0564]. If the offeror has more than one immediate owner, such as a joint venture, it shall respond for each participant in the joint venture [@CL-SUP-0698]. If the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity, the offeror enters the highest-level owner CAGE code and legal name [@CL-SUP-0565].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subsidiary submits an offer. It states that it has an immediate owner and gives that parent's CAGE code and legal name. The parent is itself owned by a holding company.

Answer: the offeror also states that the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity and gives the holding company's CAGE code and legal name as the highest-level owner.

## Never confuse

An immediate owner is not the highest-level owner. The immediate owner has direct control of the offeror [@CL-SUP-0562], while the highest-level owner is an entity that no other entity owns or controls [@CL-SUP-0563]. How an offeror gets a CAGE code is on the [CAGE code reporting](sup-cage-code-reporting.md) card.
