## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must say about its immediate owner and highest-level owner, including their CAGE codes and legal names.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0562] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-17 [@CL-SUP-0562] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.204-7 [@CL-SUP-3730; @CL-SUP-3731; @CL-SUP-3733], as RFO FAR part 52 lists FAR 52.204-17 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-3729] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0562] |
| Applies to | Offerors, where the provision is used as prescribed in FAR 4.1804(b) [@CL-SUP-0695] |

An immediate owner means an entity, other than the offeror, that has direct control of the offeror [@CL-SUP-0562]. A highest-level owner means the entity that owns or controls an immediate owner of the offeror, or that owns or controls one or more entities that control an immediate owner of the offeror, and no entity owns or exercises control of the highest level owner [@CL-SUP-0563].

Indicators of control include, but are not limited to, one or more of the following: ownership or interlocking management, identity of interests among family members, shared facilities and equipment, and the common use of employees [@CL-SUP-0696].

## How it works

The offeror represents whether it has or does not have an immediate owner [@CL-SUP-0697]. If it has one, it enters the immediate owner CAGE code, the immediate owner legal name (not using a "doing business as" name), and whether the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity [@CL-SUP-0564]. If the offeror has more than one immediate owner, such as a joint venture, it shall respond to the paragraph on the immediate owner's details and, if applicable, the paragraph on the highest-level owner for each participant in the joint venture [@CL-SUP-0698]. If the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity, the offeror enters the highest-level owner CAGE code and legal name (not using a "doing business as" name) [@CL-SUP-0565].

In RFO FAR part 52, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR part 52 lists FAR 52.204-17 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-3729]. RFO FAR 52.204-7(a) keeps the definitions of immediate owner and highest-level owner [@CL-SUP-3730; @CL-SUP-3731; @CL-SUP-0562; @CL-SUP-0563]. Its paragraph (a) says indicators of control include, but are not limited to, one or more of the following: ownership or interlocking management, identity of interests among family members, shared facilities and equipment, and the common use of employees, and that there may be more than one immediate owner, for example joint ventures [@CL-SUP-3732; @CL-SUP-0696]. Paragraph (b)(3)(i) of RFO FAR 52.204-7 says the Offeror shall provide a CAGE code and legal business name, without using a doing-business-as name, for itself, for its immediate owner(s), if any, for its highest-level owner, if any, and for any predecessor(s), or predecessor of an Offeror's predecessor, that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-SUP-3733; @CL-SUP-9394]. In paragraph (a) a predecessor means an entity whose assets were acquired by the offeror or another entity (most often through merger or acquisition) and whose affairs are now carried out by the offeror or the other entity under a new name [@CL-SUP-9395]. RFO FAR 52.204-7 also says the Offeror shall have an active Federal Government contracts registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) when submitting an offer or quotation in response to the solicitation and at the time of award [@CL-SUP-3734].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subsidiary submits an offer. It states that it has an immediate owner and gives that parent's CAGE code and legal name. The parent is itself owned by a holding company.

Answer: the offeror also states that the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity and gives the holding company's CAGE code and legal name as the highest-level owner.

## Never confuse

The immediate owner is an entity, other than the offeror, that has direct control of the offeror [@CL-SUP-0562], while the highest-level owner is an entity that owns or controls an immediate owner of the offeror, or that owns or controls one or more entities that control an immediate owner of the offeror, and that no entity owns or exercises control of [@CL-SUP-0563]. The codified provision has the offeror represent whether it has or does not have an immediate owner [@CL-SUP-0697], while RFO FAR 52.204-7(b)(3)(i) says the Offeror shall provide a CAGE code and legal business name, without using a doing-business-as name, for itself, its immediate owner(s), if any, its highest-level owner, if any, and any predecessor(s) described there [@CL-SUP-3733; @CL-SUP-9394] (see also [CAGE code reporting](sup-cage-code-reporting.md) and [Contractor Purchasing System Administration clause](../procure-to-pay/sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md)).
