## Purpose

Shows what an offeror certifies and discloses about payments to influence a Federal award, what the Contractor may not pay for with appropriated funds, and how the duty reaches subcontractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1001; @CL-SUP-1004] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-11 [@CL-SUP-1001; @CL-SUP-1002; @CL-SUP-1545; @CL-SUP-1056] and FAR 52.203-12 [@CL-SUP-1004; @CL-SUP-1006; @CL-SUP-1053; @CL-SUP-1537] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.203-11 [@CL-SUP-3682; @CL-SUP-3683]; RFO FAR 52.203-12 [@CL-SUP-3687; @CL-SUP-3688] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.203-11: SEP 2024 [@CL-SUP-9954]; FAR 52.203-12: Jun 2020 [@CL-SUP-9303] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1001] |
| Applies to | Offerors, for the provision, and the Contractor, for the clause [@CL-SUP-1001; @CL-SUP-1004] |

By signing its offer, the offeror certifies to the best of its knowledge and belief that no Federal appropriated funds have been paid or will be paid to any person for influencing or attempting to influence an officer or employee of any agency, a Member of Congress, an officer or employee of Congress, or an employee of a Member of Congress on its behalf in connection with the awarding of this contract [@CL-SUP-1001; @CL-SUP-1545]. In the clause, the Contractor shall not use appropriated funds to pay any person for influencing or attempting to influence the listed Government and Congressional personnel in connection with the award of this contract [@CL-SUP-1004]. The clause also bars such payments in connection with the extension, continuation, renewal, amendment, or modification of the contract [@CL-SUP-1051]. The prohibition does not apply under the conditions listed in the clause's exceptions paragraph [@CL-SUP-1537].

If any registrants under the Lobbying Disclosure Act of 1995 have made a lobbying contact on behalf of the offeror with respect to this contract, the offeror must complete and submit OMB Standard Form LLL with its offer to provide the name of the registrants [@CL-SUP-1002]. The offeror need not report regularly employed officers or employees of the offeror to whom payments of reasonable compensation were made [@CL-SUP-1003]. In the clause, regularly employed is defined, for an officer or employee of a person requesting or receiving a Federal contract, as employed by such person for at least 130 working days within 1 year immediately preceding the date of the submission that initiates agency consideration of such person for receipt of such contract [@CL-SUP-1052; @CL-SUP-0082]. An officer or employee employed by that person for fewer than 130 working days within that year is considered regularly employed as soon as he or she has been employed by that person for 130 working days [@CL-SUP-9741].

## How it works

Submission of the certification and disclosure in the provision is a prerequisite for making or entering into the contract, as imposed by section 1352 of title 31 of the United States Code [@CL-SUP-1056]. Any person who makes an expenditure prohibited under the clause, or who fails to file or amend the disclosure the clause requires, shall be subject to civil penalties as provided for by section 1352 of title 31 of the United States Code [@CL-SUP-1053]. An imposition of a civil penalty under the clause does not prevent the Government from seeking any other remedy that may be applicable [@CL-SUP-1054]. For subcontracts, the Contractor shall include the substance of the clause in any subcontract that exceeds the threshold specified in FAR 3.808 on the date of subcontract award [@CL-SUP-1006]. The Contractor shall obtain a declaration, including the certification and disclosure in paragraphs (c) and (d) of the provision at FAR 52.203-11, from each person requesting or receiving a subcontract under this contract that exceeds the threshold specified in FAR 3.808 on the date of subcontract award, and the Contractor or subcontractor that awards the subcontract shall retain the declaration [@CL-SUP-0083; @CL-SUP-1055]. The prime Contractor shall, at the end of the calendar quarter in which a subcontractor submits its disclosure form, submit to the Contracting Officer within 30 days a copy of all disclosures [@CL-SUP-1005].

In RFO FAR part 52, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.203-11 keeps the certification, by signing the offer, that no Federal appropriated funds have been or will be paid to influence the listed Government and Congressional personnel on its behalf in connection with the awarding of the contract [@CL-SUP-3682; @CL-SUP-3683; @CL-SUP-1001; @CL-SUP-1545]. It keeps the Standard Form LLL disclosure with the offer when registrants have made a lobbying contact [@CL-SUP-3684; @CL-SUP-1002], the relief for regularly employed officers or employees paid reasonable compensation [@CL-SUP-3685; @CL-SUP-1003], and the statement that submission is a prerequisite for making or entering into the contract [@CL-SUP-3686; @CL-SUP-1056]. RFO FAR 52.203-12(b) keeps the ban on using appropriated funds for that influence in connection with the award [@CL-SUP-3687; @CL-SUP-1004] and with the extension, continuation, renewal, amendment, or modification of the contract [@CL-SUP-3688; @CL-SUP-1051], and paragraph (c) keeps the exceptions paragraph [@CL-SUP-3689; @CL-SUP-1537]. That paragraph includes the exception headed agency and legislative liaison by Contractor employees for payment of reasonable compensation made to an officer or employee of the Contractor if the payment is for agency and legislative liaison activities not directly related to this contract [@CL-SUP-9303], an exception headed professional and technical services [@CL-SUP-9304], and the rule that only those communications and services expressly authorized by paragraphs (c)(1) and (2) are permitted [@CL-SUP-9305]. It keeps the definition of regularly employed at 130 working days within 1 year immediately preceding the date of the submission that initiates agency consideration of such person for receipt of such contract [@CL-SUP-3690; @CL-SUP-3691; @CL-SUP-1052; @CL-SUP-0082], and the rule that an officer or employee employed by such person for less than 130 working days within that year is considered regularly employed as soon as employed by such person for 130 working days [@CL-SUP-9306; @CL-SUP-9741], the civil penalties [@CL-SUP-3692; @CL-SUP-1053] and the remedy preserved after a penalty [@CL-SUP-3693; @CL-SUP-1054]. For subcontracts it keeps the declaration, including the certification and disclosure in paragraphs (c) and (d) of FAR 52.203-11, from each person requesting or receiving a subcontract under the contract that exceeds the threshold specified in FAR 3.808 on the date of subcontract award, and its retention by the Contractor or subcontractor that awards the subcontract [@CL-SUP-3694; @CL-SUP-3695; @CL-SUP-0083; @CL-SUP-1055]. It keeps the forwarding of each subcontractor disclosure form, but not certifications, from tier to tier until received by the prime Contractor [@CL-SUP-9307], and the prime Contractor's submission to the Contracting Officer, at the end of the calendar quarter in which the subcontractor submits the disclosure form, within 30 days, of a copy of all disclosures [@CL-SUP-3696; @CL-SUP-1005]. It keeps the inclusion of the substance of the clause in any subcontract that exceeds the threshold specified in FAR 3.808 on the date of subcontract award [@CL-SUP-3697; @CL-SUP-1006].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company submits an offer and learns that a registered lobbyist contacted an agency official about the solicitation on its behalf. It completes the disclosure form and submits it with the offer. After award, it plans a subcontract that is above the threshold named in the clause. The answer: the prime obtains a declaration from the subcontractor, includes the substance of the clause in the subcontract, and forwards a copy of the disclosure form to the Contracting Officer after the quarter in which the subcontractor submits it.

## Never confuse

The clause bars the Contractor from using appropriated funds to pay any person for influencing or attempting to influence the listed personnel in connection with the award, extension, continuation, renewal, amendment, or modification of the contract [@CL-SUP-1004; @CL-SUP-1051], unlike reasonable compensation paid to regularly employed officers or employees for agency and legislative liaison activities not directly related to the contract [@CL-SUP-1003; @CL-SUP-9303], and subcontractor disclosure forms—but not certifications—are forwarded from tier to tier to the prime Contractor [@CL-SUP-9307] (see also [Lobbying and political activity costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-lobbying-and-political-activity-costs.md) and [Limitation on payments to influence Federal transactions](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-limitation-on-payments-to-influence-federal-transactions.md)).
