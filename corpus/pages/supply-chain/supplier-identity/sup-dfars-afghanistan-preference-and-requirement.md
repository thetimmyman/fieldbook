## Purpose

Shows how offers are evaluated under the Afghanistan preference provision, what the offeror represents, and what the matching clause requires of the contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1797; @CL-SUP-1802] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7023 [@CL-SUP-1797; @CL-SUP-1799; @CL-SUP-1840]; DFARS 252.225-7024 [@CL-SUP-1802; @CL-SUP-1841] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | DFARS 252.225-7023: SEP 2013 [@CL-SUP-9896]; DFARS 252.225-7024: SEP 2013 [@CL-SUP-9897] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1797; @CL-SUP-1802] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include the provision [@CL-SUP-1797; @CL-SUP-1840], and contracts that include the clause [@CL-SUP-1802; @CL-SUP-1841] |

The offeror represents that all products or services to be delivered under a contract resulting from the solicitation are products from Afghanistan or services from Afghanistan, except those listed in one of two places [@CL-SUP-1797; @CL-SUP-1800]. For evaluating competitive offers, the Contracting Officer will increase by 50 percent the prices of offers of products or services that are not products or services from Afghanistan [@CL-SUP-1799].

Under the clause, the contractor shall provide only products from Afghanistan or services from Afghanistan, unless its offer specified that it would provide other products or services [@CL-SUP-1802].

## How it works

A product from Afghanistan is a product mined, produced, or manufactured in Afghanistan, and a service from Afghanistan is a service, including construction, performed in Afghanistan predominantly by citizens or permanent resident aliens of Afghanistan [@CL-SUP-1801]. The exceptions to the representation are listed in the provision, by line item number and country of origin [@CL-SUP-1798], or in the Trade Agreements Certificate provision if it is included in the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1800]. The clause ties its exception to the offer [@CL-SUP-1802]. The price increase is applied for the purpose of evaluating competitive offers [@CL-SUP-1799]. The provision is prescribed in DFARS 225.7703-4(a) [@CL-SUP-1840], and the clause in DFARS 225.7703-4(b) [@CL-SUP-1841].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume the solicitation includes the provision and the resulting contract includes the clause. An offer for a product made in another country is priced at 200,000.

The offeror lists that product with its country of origin. For evaluating competitive offers the price is increased by 50 percent, so it is evaluated at 300,000. If that offer wins, the contractor may supply that product because the offer specified it.

## Never confuse

The provision raises by 50 percent the evaluated price of offers of products or services that are not from Afghanistan [@CL-SUP-1799]. The clause limits what the contractor provides, with the offer-based exception [@CL-SUP-1802]. Under the clause for acquisition restricted to products or services from Afghanistan, the contractor shall provide only products from Afghanistan or services from Afghanistan under the contract [@CL-SUP-1803]; see [Restriction to products or services from Afghanistan and uniform components](sup-dfars-afghanistan-restriction-and-uniform-components.md). For the Trade Agreements Certificate provision, see [DoD trade agreements clause and certificate](sup-dfars-trade-agreements-clause-and-certificate.md) [@CL-SUP-1800].
