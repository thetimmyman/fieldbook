## Purpose

Shows how the defense supplement has the contracting officer evaluate a purchasing system, handle material weaknesses and approve or disapprove the system, and how deficiencies are handled in specific proposals.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-0533] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 244.305-70 [@CL-SUP-0533] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0533] |
| Applies to | The contracting officer's evaluation of whether a contractor's purchasing system is acceptable [@CL-SUP-0672] |

The defense policy section is used instead of FAR 44.305-2(c) and FAR 44.305-3(b) [@CL-SUP-0533].

The cognizant contracting officer, in consultation with the purchasing system analyst or auditor, shall determine the acceptability of the contractor's purchasing system and approve or disapprove it, and shall pursue correction of any deficiencies [@CL-SUP-0534]. In evaluating acceptability, the contracting officer, in consultation with the purchasing system analyst or auditor, shall determine whether the system complies with the system criteria for an acceptable purchasing system [@CL-SUP-0672]. The criteria and their consequences are covered on the [Contractor purchasing system](sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md) card.

## How it works

The purchasing system analyst or auditor shall document findings and recommendations in a report to the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-0535]. If material weaknesses are identified, the report shall describe the underlying deficiencies in sufficient detail to allow the contracting officer to understand them [@CL-SUP-0650]. The contracting officer shall review all findings and recommendations and, if there are no material weaknesses, shall promptly notify the contractor that the system is acceptable and approved [@CL-SUP-0536]. If the contracting officer finds one or more material weaknesses due to the contractor's failure to meet the criteria, the contracting officer shall take the steps listed in paragraph (c)(2)(ii) [@CL-SUP-0673], the first being to promptly make an initial written determination that describes each weakness in sufficient detail for the contractor to understand it [@CL-SUP-0674].

The contracting officer shall request the contractor to respond in writing to the initial determination within 30 days [@CL-SUP-0537]. If material weaknesses remain at the final determination, the contracting officer shall request that the contractor, within 45 days of receipt of the final determination, either correct the weaknesses or submit an acceptable corrective action plan showing milestones and actions to eliminate the weaknesses [@CL-SUP-0538]. The clause at 252.244-7001 also requires the Contractor to respond within 30 days to a written initial determination that identifies material weaknesses [@CL-SUP-0106] and, if it receives the final determination of material weaknesses, to act within 45 days of receipt either to correct the material weaknesses or to submit an acceptable corrective action plan [@CL-SUP-0107]; the [Contractor purchasing system](sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md) card covers the clause. If material weaknesses remain at the final determination, the written notice identifies them and indicates the adequacy of any proposed or completed corrective action [@CL-SUP-0675]. The contracting officer shall promptly approve a previously disapproved system and notify the contractor when no material weaknesses remain [@CL-SUP-0539]. The cognizant contracting officer shall promptly distribute copies of a determination to approve, to disapprove and withhold payments, or to approve a previously disapproved system and release withheld payments to the auditor, the payment office, affected contracting officers at the buying activities, and cognizant contracting officers in contract administration activities [@CL-SUP-0676; @CL-SUP-3863].

Source selection evaluation teams shall discuss identified deficiencies and their impact in all reports on contractor proposals until the deficiencies are resolved [@CL-SUP-0677]. The contracting officer responsible for negotiation of a proposal generated by a purchasing system with an identified deficiency shall evaluate whether the deficiency impacts the negotiations [@CL-SUP-0678]. If it does, the officer should consider alternatives such as allowing the contractor additional time to correct the deficiency and submit a corrected proposal [@CL-SUP-0679], considering another type of contract or using additional cost analysis techniques [@CL-SUP-0680], segregating the questionable areas as a cost-reimbursable line item, reducing the negotiation objective for profit or fee, or including a reopener clause [@CL-SUP-0681].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A negotiator is about to price a proposal produced by a purchasing system with an identified deficiency in the analysis of quoted prices. The negotiator evaluates whether the deficiency impacts the negotiations, and it does.

Answer: the negotiator considers allowing the contractor additional time to correct the deficiency and submit a corrected proposal, or considers segregating the questionable areas as a cost-reimbursable line item.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer requests the contractor to respond in writing to the initial determination within 30 days [@CL-SUP-0537]. The final determination notice identifies any remaining material weaknesses and indicates the adequacy of any proposed or completed corrective action [@CL-SUP-0675]. Payment withholds under the business systems clause are on the [Business system withholds](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-business-system-withholds.md) card.
