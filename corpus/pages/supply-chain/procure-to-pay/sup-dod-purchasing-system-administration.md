## Purpose

Shows how the defense supplement has the contracting officer evaluate a purchasing system, handle material weaknesses and approve or disapprove the system, and how deficiencies are handled in specific proposals.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-0533] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 244.305-70 [@CL-SUP-0533] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0533] |
| Applies to | The contracting officer's evaluation of whether a contractor's purchasing system is acceptable [@CL-SUP-0672] |

The defense policy section is used instead of FAR 44.305-2(c) and FAR 44.305-3(b) [@CL-SUP-0533].

The cognizant contracting officer, in consultation with the purchasing system analyst or auditor, determines the acceptability of the contractor's purchasing system, approves or disapproves it, and pursues correction of any deficiencies [@CL-SUP-0534]. In evaluating acceptability, the contracting officer shall determine whether the system complies with the system criteria for an acceptable purchasing system [@CL-SUP-0672]. The criteria and their consequences are covered on the [Contractor purchasing system](sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md) card.

## How it works

The purchasing system analyst or auditor shall document findings and recommendations in a report to the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-0535]. If material weaknesses are identified, the report shall describe the underlying deficiencies in sufficient detail to allow the contracting officer to understand them [@CL-SUP-0650]. The contracting officer shall review all findings and recommendations and, if there are no material weaknesses, shall promptly notify the contractor that the system is acceptable and approved [@CL-SUP-0536]. If the contracting officer finds one or more material weaknesses due to the contractor's failure to meet the criteria, the officer takes the steps listed in paragraph (c)(2)(ii) [@CL-SUP-0673], starting with a prompt initial written determination that describes each weakness in sufficient detail for the contractor to understand it [@CL-SUP-0674].

The contractor is asked to respond in writing to the initial determination within 30 days, and, if weaknesses remain at the final determination, to correct them or submit an acceptable corrective action plan within 45 days of receipt [@CL-SUP-0537; @CL-SUP-0538]. Those periods are also set by the clause, which the [Contractor purchasing system](sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md) card covers. If material weaknesses remain at the final determination, the written notice identifies them and indicates the adequacy of any proposed or completed corrective action [@CL-SUP-0675]. The contracting officer shall promptly approve a previously disapproved system and notify the contractor when no material weaknesses remain [@CL-SUP-0539]. The cognizant contracting officer shall promptly distribute copies of a determination to approve, to disapprove and withhold payments, or to approve a previously disapproved system and release withheld payments to at least the auditor and the payment office [@CL-SUP-0676].

Source selection evaluation teams shall discuss identified deficiencies and their impact in all reports on contractor proposals until the deficiencies are resolved [@CL-SUP-0677]. The contracting officer responsible for negotiation of a proposal generated by a purchasing system with an identified deficiency shall evaluate whether the deficiency impacts the negotiations [@CL-SUP-0678]. If it does, the officer should consider alternatives such as allowing the contractor additional time to correct the deficiency and submit a corrected proposal [@CL-SUP-0679], considering another type of contract or using additional cost analysis techniques [@CL-SUP-0680], segregating the questionable areas as a cost-reimbursable line item, reducing the negotiation objective for profit or fee, or including a reopener clause [@CL-SUP-0681].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A negotiator is about to price a proposal produced by a purchasing system with an identified deficiency in the analysis of quoted prices. The negotiator checks whether the deficiency affects the cost elements in the proposal, and it does.

Answer: the negotiator allows the contractor extra time to submit a corrected proposal. For the questionable items that remain, the negotiator segregates them as a cost-reimbursable line item.

## Never confuse

An initial determination is not the final determination. The contractor is asked to respond to the initial determination [@CL-SUP-0537], and the final determination notice identifies any material weaknesses that remain [@CL-SUP-0675]. Payment withholds under the business systems clause are on the [Business system withholds](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-business-system-withholds.md) card.
