## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer may do when a subcontractor or supplier says it was not paid, and what the contracting officer must tell a subcontractor or supplier that asks about payments to the prime contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0576] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.112-1 and FAR 32.112-2 [@CL-SUP-0576; @CL-SUP-0579] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0576] |
| Applies to | Federal contracts, on a nonpayment assertion by a subcontractor or supplier, or on its request for payment information under a contract other than for a commercial product or commercial service [@CL-SUP-0576; @CL-SUP-0579] |

Upon the assertion by a subcontractor or supplier of a Federal contractor that it has not been paid in accordance with the payment terms of its subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor, the contracting officer may make the determinations listed in paragraph (a) [@CL-SUP-0576]. For a contract other than construction, paragraph (a)(2) lists whether the contractor has made progress payments, final payments, or other payments to the subcontractor or supplier in compliance with the terms of the agreement with the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0708]. For any contract, paragraph (a)(3) lists whether the contractor's certification of payment of a subcontractor or supplier accompanying its payment request to the Government is accurate [@CL-SUP-0709].

## How it works

The contracting officer's options in paragraph (b) arise when, in making the determination in paragraphs (a)(1) and (2), the contracting officer finds the prime contractor is not in compliance [@CL-SUP-0683]. The officer may then encourage the contractor to make timely payment to the subcontractor or supplier or, if authorized by the applicable payment clauses, reduce or suspend progress payments to the contractor [@CL-SUP-0577]. If the officer determines that a certification referred to in paragraph (a)(3) is inaccurate in any material respect, the officer shall initiate administrative or other remedial action [@CL-SUP-0578]. Upon the request of a subcontractor or supplier under a Federal contract other than for a commercial product or commercial service, the officer shall promptly advise as to the matters listed in paragraph (a) [@CL-SUP-0579]. The first matter is whether the prime contractor has submitted requests for progress payments or other payments to the Federal Government under the contract [@CL-SUP-0684]. The second is whether final payment under the contract has been made by the Federal Government to the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0580]. The section does not apply to matters that are specifically authorized under criteria established by an Executive order to be kept classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy and are properly classified pursuant to that Executive order [@CL-SUP-0710].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier under a noncommercial defense contract tells the contracting officer that the prime contractor has not paid an invoice under their purchase order. The contracting officer determines that the prime contractor is not in compliance with the payment terms. Separately, the supplier asks whether the prime contractor has submitted payment requests.

Answer: the contracting officer may encourage timely payment or, if the payment clauses allow, reduce or suspend progress payments. The officer must also promptly tell the supplier whether the prime contractor has submitted payment requests and whether final payment has been made, unless the matter is properly classified.

## Never confuse

A nonpayment assertion and an information request are different triggers. The assertion is a statement that the subcontractor or supplier was not paid, and it lets the contracting officer make determinations [@CL-SUP-0576]. The information request obliges the contracting officer to advise, for contracts other than for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-SUP-0579]. Payment due dates and progress financing are on the [Prompt payment](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md) and [Progress payments](../../contract-finance/financing/ctf-progress-payments.md) cards.
