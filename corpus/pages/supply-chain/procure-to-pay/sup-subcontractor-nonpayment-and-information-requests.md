## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer may do when a subcontractor or supplier says it was not paid, and what the contracting officer must tell a subcontractor or supplier that asks about payments to the prime contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0576] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.112-1 and FAR 32.112-2 [@CL-SUP-0576; @CL-SUP-0579] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.112-1; RFO FAR 32.112-2 [@CL-SUP-3406; @CL-SUP-0040; @CL-SUP-0041; @CL-SUP-3407; @CL-SUP-3408; @CL-SUP-3409; @CL-SUP-3410; @CL-SUP-3411; @CL-SUP-3412; @CL-SUP-3413; @CL-SUP-3414; @CL-SUP-3415] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0576] |
| Applies to | Federal contracts, on a nonpayment assertion by a subcontractor or supplier, or on its request for payment information under a contract other than for a commercial product or commercial service [@CL-SUP-0576; @CL-SUP-0579] |

Upon the assertion by a subcontractor or supplier of a Federal contractor that the subcontractor or supplier has not been paid in accordance with the payment terms of the subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor, the contracting officer may determine any of three alternatives [@CL-SUP-0576; @CL-SUP-0038; @CL-SUP-0039; @CL-SUP-0708; @CL-SUP-0709]. For a construction contract, paragraph (a)(1) covers whether the contractor has made progress payments to the subcontractor or supplier in compliance with Chapter 39 of Title 31, United States Code (Prompt Payment Act), or final payment to the subcontractor or supplier in compliance with the terms of the subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0038; @CL-SUP-0039]. For a contract other than construction, paragraph (a)(2) covers whether the contractor has made progress payments, final payments, or other payments to the subcontractor or supplier in compliance with the terms of the subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0708]. For any contract, paragraph (a)(3) covers whether the contractor's certification of payment of a subcontractor or supplier accompanying its payment request to the Government is accurate [@CL-SUP-0709].

## How it works

If, in making the determination in paragraphs (a)(1) and (2), the contracting officer finds the prime contractor is not in compliance [@CL-SUP-0683], the contracting officer may encourage the contractor to make timely payment to the subcontractor or supplier or, if authorized by the applicable payment clauses, reduce or suspend progress payments to the contractor [@CL-SUP-0577]. If the contracting officer determines that a certification referred to in paragraph (a)(3) is inaccurate in any material respect, the contracting officer shall initiate administrative or other remedial action [@CL-SUP-0578]. Upon the request of a subcontractor or supplier under a Federal contract other than for a commercial product or commercial service, the contracting officer shall promptly advise the subcontractor or supplier as to both whether the prime contractor has submitted requests for progress payments or other payments to the Federal Government under the contract and whether final payment under the contract has been made by the Federal Government to the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0579; @CL-SUP-0684; @CL-SUP-0580]. FAR 32.112-2 does not apply to matters that are specifically authorized under criteria established by an Executive order to be kept classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy and are properly classified pursuant to such Executive order [@CL-SUP-0710].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.112-1(a) keeps the contracting officer's authority, upon the assertion by a subcontractor or supplier that it has not been paid in accordance with the payment terms of the subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor, to make the determinations in paragraph (a), and refers to the subcontractor or supplier of "a prime contractor" where the codified text says "a Federal contractor" [@CL-SUP-3406; @CL-SUP-0576]. It keeps the determination in paragraph (a)(1), for a construction contract, of whether the contractor has made progress payments to the subcontractor or supplier in compliance with the Prompt Payment Act (Chapter 39 of Title 31, United States Code) or final payment to the subcontractor or supplier in compliance with the terms of the subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0040; @CL-SUP-0041; @CL-SUP-0038; @CL-SUP-0039]. It keeps the determination in paragraph (a)(2), for a contract other than construction, of whether the contractor has made progress payments, final payments, or other payments to the subcontractor or supplier in compliance with the terms of the subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-3407; @CL-SUP-0708]. It keeps the determination in paragraph (a)(3), for any contract, of whether the contractor's certification of payment of a subcontractor or supplier is accurate, and says the certification is submitted with its payment request to the Government where the codified text says accompanying its payment request to the Government [@CL-SUP-3408; @CL-SUP-0709]. RFO FAR 32.112-1(b) keeps the options that arise if, in making the determination in paragraphs (a)(1) and (2), the contracting officer finds the prime contractor is not in compliance [@CL-SUP-3409; @CL-SUP-0683]: encourage the contractor to make timely payment to the subcontractor or supplier or, if authorized by the applicable payment clauses, reduce or suspend progress payments to the contractor [@CL-SUP-3410; @CL-SUP-0577]. RFO FAR 32.112-1(c) says the contracting officer must initiate administrative or other remedial action if the contracting officer determines that a certification referred to in paragraph (a)(3) is inaccurate in any material respect, where the codified text says shall [@CL-SUP-3411; @CL-SUP-0578]. Upon the request of a subcontractor or supplier under a Federal contract other than for a commercial product or commercial service, RFO FAR 32.112-2(a) says the contracting officer must promptly inform the subcontractor or supplier, where the codified text says shall promptly advise [@CL-SUP-3412; @CL-SUP-0579], as to whether the prime contractor has submitted requests for progress payments or other payments to the Federal Government under the contract [@CL-SUP-3413; @CL-SUP-0684] and whether final payment under the contract has been made by the Federal Government to the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-3414; @CL-SUP-0580]. RFO FAR 32.112-2(b) keeps the exclusion for matters that are specifically authorized under criteria established by an Executive order to be kept classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy and are properly classified pursuant to such Executive order [@CL-SUP-3415; @CL-SUP-0710].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier under a noncommercial defense contract tells the contracting officer that the prime contractor has not paid an invoice under their purchase order. The contracting officer determines that the prime contractor is not in compliance with the payment terms. Separately, the supplier asks whether the prime contractor has submitted payment requests.

Answer: the contracting officer may encourage timely payment or, if the payment clauses allow, reduce or suspend progress payments. The officer must also promptly tell the supplier whether the prime contractor has submitted payment requests and whether final payment has been made, unless the matter is properly classified.

## Never confuse

Upon the assertion by a subcontractor or supplier of a Federal contractor that it has not been paid in accordance with the payment terms of the subcontract, purchase order, or other agreement with the prime contractor, the contracting officer may make the determinations in FAR 32.112-1(a) [@CL-SUP-0576; @CL-SUP-0038; @CL-SUP-0039; @CL-SUP-0708; @CL-SUP-0709]. Upon the request of a subcontractor or supplier under a Federal contract other than for a commercial product or commercial service, the contracting officer shall promptly advise the subcontractor or supplier as to whether the prime contractor has submitted requests for progress payments or other payments to the Federal Government under the contract and whether final payment under the contract has been made by the Federal Government to the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0579; @CL-SUP-0684; @CL-SUP-0580]. Payment due dates and progress financing are on the [Prompt payment](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md) and [Progress payments](../../contract-finance/financing/ctf-progress-payments.md) cards.
