## Purpose

Shows who inspects under the contractor inspection clause, what the Government may inspect and evaluate under the short-form research and development clause, and which quality standard binds, and which subcontracts carry it, under the higher-level quality clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-3105; @CL-SUP-3107; @CL-SUP-3109] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-1, FAR 52.246-9 and FAR 52.246-11 [@CL-SUP-3105; @CL-SUP-3107; @CL-SUP-3109] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-1 [@CL-SUP-0408]; RFO FAR 52.246-9 [@CL-SUP-0410]; RFO FAR 52.246-11 [@CL-SUP-0412] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-3105; @CL-SUP-3107; @CL-SUP-3109] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include one or more of the three clauses, each rule below applying where its own clause is included [@CL-SUP-3105; @CL-SUP-3107; @CL-SUP-3109] |

Contractor Inspection Requirements: the contractor is responsible for performing or having performed all inspections and tests necessary to substantiate that the supplies or services furnished under the contract conform to contract requirements, including any applicable technical requirements for specified manufacturers' parts [@CL-SUP-3105]. This clause takes precedence over any Government inspection and testing required in the contract's specifications, except for specialized inspections or tests specified to be performed solely by the Government [@CL-SUP-3106].

Inspection of Research and Development (Short Form): the Government has the right to inspect and evaluate the work performed or being performed under the contract, and the premises where the work is being performed, at all reasonable times and in a manner that will not unduly delay the work [@CL-SUP-3107]. If the Government inspects or evaluates on the premises of the contractor or a subcontractor, the contractor shall furnish and shall require subcontractors to furnish all reasonable facilities and assistance for the safe and convenient performance of these duties [@CL-SUP-3108].

Higher-Level Contract Quality Requirement: the contractor shall comply with the higher-level quality standard or standards listed in the clause [@CL-SUP-3109]. The clause directs the contracting officer to insert the title, number, date and tailoring (if any) of the higher-level quality standards [@CL-SUP-3190]. The contractor shall include the applicable requirements of the listed standards, and the requirement to flow down such standards as applicable, to lower-tier subcontracts, in one of two alternatives described next [@CL-SUP-3110]. The first alternative is any subcontract for critical and complex items, a term for which the clause cross-refers to FAR 46.203(b) and (c) [@CL-SUP-3111]. The second alternative is when the technical requirements of a subcontract require one of two things: control of such things as design, work operations, in-process control, testing, and inspection; or attention to such factors as organization, planning, work instructions, documentation control, and advanced metrology [@CL-SUP-3112].

## How it works

Under the contractor inspection clause, inspection is a contractor duty: the contractor is responsible for performing or having performed the inspections and tests necessary to substantiate that the supplies or services conform [@CL-SUP-3105]. That clause takes precedence over any Government inspection and testing required in the contract's specifications, except for specialized inspections or tests specified to be performed solely by the Government [@CL-SUP-3106]. The short-form research and development clause states a Government right to inspect and evaluate, and if the Government performs inspection or evaluation on the premises of the contractor or a subcontractor, the contractor shall furnish and shall require subcontractors to furnish all reasonable facilities and assistance for the safe and convenient performance of these duties [@CL-SUP-3107; @CL-SUP-3108]. Under the higher-level quality clause the contractor shall comply with the listed standard or standards and shall include their applicable requirements, and the requirement to flow them down, to the lower-tier subcontracts described above [@CL-SUP-3109; @CL-SUP-3110].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.246-1 provides that the Contractor is responsible for performing or having performed all inspections and tests necessary to substantiate that the supplies or services furnished under the contract conform to contract requirements, including any applicable technical requirements for specified manufacturers' parts [@CL-SUP-0408], and that the clause takes precedence over any Government inspection and testing required in the contract's specifications, except for specialized inspections or tests specified to be performed solely by the Government [@CL-SUP-0409]. RFO FAR 52.246-9 provides that the Government has the right to inspect and evaluate the work performed or being performed under the contract, and the premises where the work is being performed, at all reasonable times and in a manner that will not unduly delay the work [@CL-SUP-0410], and that, if the Government performs inspection or evaluation on the premises of the Contractor or a subcontractor, the Contractor shall furnish and shall require subcontractors to furnish all reasonable facilities and assistance for the safe and convenient performance of these duties [@CL-SUP-0411]. RFO FAR 52.246-11 provides that the Contractor shall comply with the higher-level quality standard(s) listed [@CL-SUP-0412], with the Contracting Officer to insert the title, number, date, and tailoring (if any) of the higher-level quality standards [@CL-SUP-0413]. Its paragraph (b) provides that the Contractor shall include applicable requirements of those standards, and the requirement to flow down such standards, as applicable, to lower-tier subcontracts [@CL-SUP-0414], in any subcontract for critical and complex items (see 46.203(b) and (c)) [@CL-SUP-0415], or when the technical requirements of a subcontract require control of such things as design, work operations, in-process control, testing, and inspection, or attention to such factors as organization, planning, work instructions, documentation control, and advanced metrology [@CL-SUP-0416].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply contract that includes the contractor inspection clause calls for 500 machined housings. The specifications call for a Government test of the housings and reserve one pressure test to the Government alone. The contractor runs its own dimension and material tests on all 500 and keeps the records. The answer: the contractor is responsible for the inspections and tests needed to substantiate conformance, and the clause takes precedence over the Government test in the specifications, except for the pressure test that the specifications reserve solely to the Government.

A research and development contract includes the short form, and a Government evaluator visits a subcontractor's site. The answer: the Government may inspect and evaluate at reasonable times without unduly delaying the work, and the contractor furnishes reasonable facilities and assistance for safe and convenient performance and requires the subcontractor to do the same.

A third contract lists a synthetic Standard X in the higher-level quality clause. A subcontract to make a critical and complex item must carry the applicable requirements of Standard X and the flow-down requirement. A subcontract for another item carries them as well if its technical requirements require control of testing and inspection, but the contractor does not choose the standard, because the contracting officer inserts it.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer inserts the title, number, date and tailoring (if any) of the higher-level quality standards [@CL-SUP-3190], and the contractor shall comply with the listed standards and include their applicable requirements, and the requirement to flow them down, to the lower-tier subcontracts described [@CL-SUP-3109; @CL-SUP-3110]. The short form gives the Government the right to inspect and evaluate the work [@CL-SUP-3107], while the contractor inspection clause makes the contractor responsible for performing or having performed all inspections and tests necessary to substantiate conformance [@CL-SUP-3105].
