## Purpose

Shows when a Contractor needs the Contracting Officer's consent before placing a subcontract, and when it must notify the Contracting Officer in advance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0201] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.244-2 [@CL-SUP-0201] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.244-2 [@CL-SUP-0547; @CL-SUP-0548; @CL-SUP-0549; @CL-SUP-0581; @CL-SUP-0582; @CL-SUP-0583; @CL-SUP-0584; @CL-SUP-0585; @CL-SUP-0586; @CL-SUP-0587; @CL-SUP-0588; @CL-SUP-0589; @CL-SUP-0590] and RFO FAR 52.244-2 Alternate I [@CL-SUP-0591; @CL-SUP-0592; @CL-SUP-0593; @CL-SUP-0594] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0201] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts for which consent is required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) [@CL-SUP-0207] |

Consent to subcontract means the Contracting Officer's written consent for the Contractor to enter into a particular subcontract [@CL-SUP-0202]. In a fixed-price type contract, consent is required only on unpriced contract actions, and only when paragraph (c) or (d) of the clause requires it [@CL-SUP-0203]. If the Contractor does not have an approved purchasing system, consent is required for a cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials, or labor-hour subcontract [@CL-SUP-0204], and for a fixed-price subcontract that exceeds the amount the clause sets for the awarding agency [@CL-SUP-0300].

For a contract awarded by the Department of Defense, the Coast Guard, or the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, that amount is the greater of the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in the Federal Acquisition Regulation on the date of subcontract award, or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract [@CL-SUP-0205]. For a contract awarded by a civilian agency other than the Coast Guard and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the clause states it as either the simplified acquisition threshold on that date or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract [@CL-SUP-0206]. The two groups are worded differently, so read the clause in the contract against the awarding agency.

Even if the Contractor has an approved purchasing system, it must obtain the Contracting Officer's written consent before placing the subcontracts listed in paragraph (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0301]. The Contractor must notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance of placing any subcontract or modification for which consent is required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) [@CL-SUP-0207].

## How it works

When the clause is in a fixed-price type contract, consent is required only on unpriced contract actions, and only if paragraph (c) or (d) requires it [@CL-SUP-0203]. Within that limit, consent turns on whether the Contractor has an approved purchasing system and on the type and value of the subcontract [@CL-SUP-0204; @CL-SUP-0300]. Without an approved purchasing system, cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials, and labor-hour subcontracts all need consent, as do fixed-price subcontracts above the amount set for the awarding agency [@CL-SUP-0204; @CL-SUP-0205; @CL-SUP-0206; @CL-SUP-0300]. With an approved purchasing system, paragraph (d) still requires consent for the subcontracts it lists [@CL-SUP-0301]. The Contractor gives the Contracting Officer advance notice before placing any subcontract for which consent is required, with the listed supporting information [@CL-SUP-0207]. Under the basic clause, the Contractor need not notify the Contracting Officer in advance for a subcontract for which consent is not required [@CL-SUP-0208]. Alternate I replaces that paragraph [@CL-SUP-0340]. Under it, a Contractor with an approved purchasing system must still notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance of entering into any cost-plus-fixed-fee subcontract, or any fixed-price subcontract that exceeds either the simplified acquisition threshold or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract, even when consent is not required [@CL-SUP-0341; @CL-SUP-0342]. That notice carries the first four items of the paragraph (e)(1) list [@CL-SUP-0342]. No subcontract or modification may pay on a cost-plus-a-percentage-of-cost basis, and any fee under a cost-reimbursement subcontract may not exceed the fee limitations the clause cites [@CL-SUP-0209].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.244-2 keeps the meaning of consent to subcontract as the Contracting Officer's written consent for the Contractor to enter into a particular subcontract [@CL-SUP-0547; @CL-SUP-0202]. It keeps the rule that in a fixed-price type contract consent is required only on unpriced contract actions, and only if paragraph (c) or (d) requires it [@CL-SUP-0548; @CL-SUP-0203]. Without an approved purchasing system it keeps the consent requirement for cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials and labor-hour subcontracts [@CL-SUP-0549; @CL-SUP-0204] and for fixed-price subcontracts that exceed the amounts set for the awarding agency [@CL-SUP-0581; @CL-SUP-0300]: the greater of the simplified acquisition threshold or 5 percent of the total estimated cost for a DoD, Coast Guard or NASA contract [@CL-SUP-0582; @CL-SUP-0205], and either of the two for another civilian agency's contract [@CL-SUP-0583; @CL-SUP-0206]. With an approved purchasing system it keeps the requirement of written consent before placing the subcontracts listed in paragraph (d) [@CL-SUP-0584; @CL-SUP-0301]. It keeps the duty to notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance of any subcontract or modification for which consent is required [@CL-SUP-0585; @CL-SUP-0207]. The RFO list of information for that notice has a description of the supplies or services to be subcontracted, the type of subcontract, the proposed subcontractor and the proposed subcontract price [@CL-SUP-0586]; the subcontractor's certified cost or pricing data and Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data, if required by other contract provisions [@CL-SUP-9150]; the subcontractor's Cost Accounting Standards Disclosure Statement or Certificate when other provisions of the contract require such data [@CL-SUP-9151]; and a negotiation memorandum [@CL-SUP-9152; @CL-SUP-9153; @CL-SUP-9154; @CL-SUP-9155; @CL-SUP-9156]. The RFO text also keeps the basic clause's statement that no advance notice is required where consent is not required [@CL-SUP-0587; @CL-SUP-0208]. It keeps the rule that, unless the consent or approval specifically provides otherwise, consent and approval of the purchasing system do not determine the acceptability of subcontract terms, the allowability of any cost or relief from responsibility for performing the contract [@CL-SUP-0588; @CL-SUP-0589; @CL-SUP-0302; @CL-SUP-0303]. It keeps the bar on cost-plus-a-percentage-of-cost payment and the fee limitation [@CL-SUP-0590; @CL-SUP-0209]. RFO Alternate I keeps the replacement of paragraph (e)(2) [@CL-SUP-0591; @CL-SUP-0340] and the advance notice for a Contractor with an approved purchasing system, where consent is not required under paragraph (c) or (d), for a cost-plus-fixed-fee subcontract or a fixed-price subcontract that exceeds either the simplified acquisition threshold or 5 percent of the total estimated cost, with the information in paragraphs (e)(1)(i) through (iv) [@CL-SUP-0592; @CL-SUP-0593; @CL-SUP-0594; @CL-SUP-0341; @CL-SUP-0342].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement contract awarded by the Department of Defense has a total estimated cost of 10 million. The Contractor has no approved purchasing system and plans a fixed-price subcontract worth 2 million. Five percent of the total estimated cost is 500,000. The subcontract exceeds that figure and also exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, so it exceeds the greater of the two. The Contractor must obtain the Contracting Officer's written consent before placing it and must notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance.

## Never confuse

Consent to subcontract is not approval of the subcontract's terms or costs. Unless the consent or approval specifically provides otherwise, neither consent nor approval of the purchasing system determines the acceptability of subcontract terms or conditions, the allowability of any cost under the contract, or relief of the Contractor from responsibility for performing the contract [@CL-SUP-0302; @CL-SUP-0303].
