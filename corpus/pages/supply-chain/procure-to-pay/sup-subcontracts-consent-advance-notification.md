## Purpose

Shows when a Contractor needs the Contracting Officer's consent before placing a subcontract, and when it must notify the Contracting Officer in advance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0201] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.244-2 [@CL-SUP-0201] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.244-2 [@CL-SUP-0547; @CL-SUP-0548; @CL-SUP-0549; @CL-SUP-0581; @CL-SUP-0582; @CL-SUP-0583; @CL-SUP-0584; @CL-SUP-0585; @CL-SUP-0586; @CL-SUP-0587; @CL-SUP-0588; @CL-SUP-0589; @CL-SUP-0590] and RFO FAR 52.244-2 Alternate I [@CL-SUP-0591; @CL-SUP-0592; @CL-SUP-0593; @CL-SUP-0594] |
| Edition date | Jun 2020 [@CL-SUP-0586] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0201] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts for which consent is required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) [@CL-SUP-0207] |

Consent to subcontract means the Contracting Officer's written consent for the Contractor to enter into a particular subcontract [@CL-SUP-0202]. When the clause is included in a fixed-price type contract, consent to subcontract is required only on unpriced contract actions (including unpriced modifications or unpriced delivery orders), and only if required in accordance with paragraph (c) or (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0203]. If the Contractor does not have an approved purchasing system, consent is required for any subcontract that meets either of two alternatives: it is of the cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials, or labor-hour type [@CL-SUP-0204], or it is fixed-price and exceeds the amount the clause sets for the awarding agency [@CL-SUP-0300].

For a contract awarded by the Department of Defense, the Coast Guard, or the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, that amount is the greater of the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract [@CL-SUP-0205]. For a contract awarded by a civilian agency other than the Coast Guard and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the clause states it as either the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract [@CL-SUP-0206].

Even if the Contractor has an approved purchasing system, it must obtain the Contracting Officer's written consent before placing the subcontracts listed in paragraph (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0301]. The Contractor must notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance of placing any subcontract or modification for which consent is required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) [@CL-SUP-0207].

## How it works

When the clause is in a fixed-price type contract, consent to subcontract is required only on unpriced contract actions (including unpriced modifications or unpriced delivery orders), and only if required in accordance with paragraph (c) or (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0203]. If the Contractor does not have an approved purchasing system, consent to subcontract is required for any subcontract that is either of the cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials, or labor-hour type or fixed-price and above the amount set for the awarding agency [@CL-SUP-0204; @CL-SUP-0205; @CL-SUP-0206; @CL-SUP-0300]. If the Contractor has an approved purchasing system, the Contractor nevertheless shall obtain the Contracting Officer's written consent before placing the subcontracts listed in paragraph (d) [@CL-SUP-0301]. The Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance of placing any subcontract or modification thereof for which consent is required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) of the clause, including the information listed in paragraph (e)(1) [@CL-SUP-0207]. Under the basic clause, the Contractor is not required to notify the Contracting Officer in advance of entering into any subcontract for which consent is not required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0208]. Alternate I substitutes its paragraph (e)(2) for paragraph (e)(2) of the basic clause [@CL-SUP-0340]. Under it, if the Contractor has an approved purchasing system and consent is not required under paragraph (c) or (d) of the clause, the Contractor nevertheless shall notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance of entering into any cost-plus-fixed-fee subcontract, or any fixed-price subcontract that exceeds either the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract [@CL-SUP-0341; @CL-SUP-0342]. That notification shall include the information required by paragraphs (e)(1)(i) through (iv) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0342]. No subcontract or modification thereof placed under the contract shall provide for payment on a cost-plus-a-percentage-of-cost basis, and any fee payable under cost-reimbursement type subcontracts shall not exceed the fee limitations in FAR 15.404-4(c)(4)(i) [@CL-SUP-0209].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.244-2 keeps the meaning of consent to subcontract as the Contracting Officer's written consent for the Contractor to enter into a particular subcontract [@CL-SUP-0547; @CL-SUP-0202]. It keeps the rule that when the clause is included in a fixed-price type contract, consent to subcontract is required only on unpriced contract actions (including unpriced modifications or unpriced delivery orders), and only if required in accordance with paragraph (c) or (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0548; @CL-SUP-0203]. Without an approved purchasing system it keeps the consent requirement for cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials and labor-hour subcontracts [@CL-SUP-0549; @CL-SUP-0204] and for fixed-price subcontracts that exceed the amounts set for the awarding agency [@CL-SUP-0581; @CL-SUP-0300]: the greater of the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract for a DoD, Coast Guard or NASA contract [@CL-SUP-0582; @CL-SUP-0205], and either the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract for another civilian agency's contract [@CL-SUP-0583; @CL-SUP-0206]. With an approved purchasing system it keeps the requirement of the Contracting Officer's written consent before placing the subcontracts listed in paragraph (d) [@CL-SUP-0584; @CL-SUP-0301]. It keeps the duty to notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance of placing any subcontract or modification thereof for which consent is required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0585; @CL-SUP-0207]. The RFO list of information for that notice has a description of the supplies or services to be subcontracted, identification of the type of subcontract to be used, identification of the proposed subcontractor and the proposed subcontract price [@CL-SUP-0586]; the subcontractor's current, complete, and accurate certified cost or pricing data and Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data, if required by other contract provisions [@CL-SUP-9150]; the subcontractor's Disclosure Statement or Certificate relating to Cost Accounting Standards when such data are required by other provisions of the contract [@CL-SUP-9151]; and a negotiation memorandum reflecting the principal elements of the subcontract price negotiations, the most significant considerations controlling establishment of initial or revised prices, the reason certified cost or pricing data were or were not required, the extent, if any, to which the Contractor did not rely on the subcontractor's certified cost or pricing data in determining the price objective and in negotiating the final price, the extent to which it was recognized in the negotiation that the subcontractor's certified cost or pricing data were not accurate, complete, or current, the action taken by the Contractor and the subcontractor, and the effect of any such defective data on the total price negotiated, the reasons for any significant difference between the Contractor's price objective and the price negotiated, and a complete explanation of the incentive fee or profit plan when incentives are used (identifying each critical performance element, management decisions used to quantify each incentive element, reasons for the incentives, and a summary of all trade-off possibilities considered) [@CL-SUP-9152; @CL-SUP-9153; @CL-SUP-9154; @CL-SUP-9155; @CL-SUP-9156]. RFO FAR 52.244-2 also keeps the basic clause's statement that the Contractor is not required to notify the Contracting Officer in advance of entering into any subcontract for which consent is not required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) of the clause [@CL-SUP-0587; @CL-SUP-0208]. It keeps the rule that, unless the consent or approval specifically provides otherwise, neither consent by the Contracting Officer to any subcontract nor approval of the Contractor's purchasing system shall constitute a determination of the acceptability of any subcontract terms or conditions, of the allowability of any cost under the contract, or to relieve the Contractor of any responsibility for performing the contract [@CL-SUP-0588; @CL-SUP-0589; @CL-SUP-0302; @CL-SUP-0303]. It keeps the bar on payment on a cost-plus-a-percentage-of-cost basis and the fee limitation in FAR 15.404-4(c)(4)(i) [@CL-SUP-0590; @CL-SUP-0209]. RFO Alternate I keeps the substitution of paragraph (e)(2) [@CL-SUP-0591; @CL-SUP-0340] and the advance notice requirement when the Contractor has an approved purchasing system and consent is not required under paragraph (c) or (d), for any cost-plus-fixed-fee subcontract or any fixed-price subcontract that exceeds either the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, or 5 percent of the total estimated cost of the contract, with the information required by paragraphs (e)(1)(i) through (iv) [@CL-SUP-0592; @CL-SUP-0593; @CL-SUP-0594; @CL-SUP-0341; @CL-SUP-0342].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement contract awarded by the Department of Defense has a total estimated cost of 10 million. The Contractor has no approved purchasing system and plans a fixed-price subcontract worth 2 million. Five percent of the total estimated cost is 500,000. The subcontract exceeds that figure and also exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, so it exceeds the greater of the two. The Contractor must obtain the Contracting Officer's written consent before placing it and must notify the Contracting Officer reasonably in advance.

## Never confuse

Unless the consent or approval specifically provides otherwise, neither consent by the Contracting Officer to any subcontract nor approval of the Contractor's purchasing system constitutes a determination of the acceptability of any subcontract terms or conditions, of the allowability of any cost under the contract, or relieves the Contractor of any responsibility for performing the contract [@CL-SUP-0302; @CL-SUP-0303], and under the basic clause advance notification is not required when consent is not required under paragraph (b), (c), or (d) [@CL-SUP-0208], unlike Alternate I, which requires advance notification even with an approved purchasing system for cost-plus-fixed-fee or qualifying fixed-price subcontracts [@CL-SUP-0341; @CL-SUP-0342].
