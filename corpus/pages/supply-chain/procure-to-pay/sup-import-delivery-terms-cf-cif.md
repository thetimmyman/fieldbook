## Purpose

Explains what the contractor arranges and pays, which documents it provides, and when it answers for loss or damage under the two ocean delivery terms that deliver goods on board the ocean vessel at a specified destination point: cost and freight (C&F) destination and cost, insurance and freight (CIF) destination.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-41 and FAR 52.247-42 [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426] |
| RFO citation | RFO part 52 lists 52.247-41 and 52.247-42 as reserved [@CL-SUP-0398; @CL-SUP-0399] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when the delivery term is cost and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2471] or cost, insurance and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2474] |

The C&F destination clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when the delivery term is cost and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2471]. The CIF destination clause is inserted when the delivery term is cost, insurance and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2474].

C&F destination means free of expense to the Government delivered on board the ocean vessel to the specified point of destination, with the cost of transportation paid by the contractor [@CL-SUP-2421]. CIF destination means the same, with the cost of transportation and marine insurance paid by the contractor [@CL-SUP-2426].

Under paragraph (b) of both clauses the contractor shall pack and mark the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepare it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements [@CL-SUP-3960; @CL-SUP-3961; @CL-SUP-2472; @CL-SUP-2475]. It shall deliver the shipment in good order and condition [@CL-SUP-2473; @CL-SUP-2476]. It shall pay and bear all applicable charges to the point of destination specified in the contract, including transportation costs and export taxes or other fees or charges levied because of exportation [@CL-SUP-2422; @CL-SUP-2476].

Under both clauses the contractor shall obtain and dispatch promptly to the Government clean on-board ocean bills of lading to the specified point of destination [@CL-SUP-2423; @CL-SUP-2477]. It shall be responsible for any loss of and/or damage to the goods occurring before delivery [@CL-SUP-2424; @CL-SUP-2478]. At the Government's request and expense, it shall provide certificates of origin, consular invoices, or any other documents issued in the country of origin or of shipment, or both, that may be required for importation into the country of destination [@CL-SUP-2425; @CL-SUP-2479].

Under CIF destination the contractor must also obtain and dispatch to the Government an insurance policy or certificate providing the amount and extent of marine insurance coverage specified in the contract or agreed upon by the Government Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-3961; @CL-SUP-2427].

## How it works

The point of destination is specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2422], packing and marking follow the contract specifications [@CL-SUP-2472], and under CIF destination the marine insurance coverage is the amount and extent specified in the contract or agreed upon by the Government Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-2427]. The contractor shall pay and bear all applicable charges to the point of destination, including transportation costs and export taxes or other fees or charges levied because of exportation [@CL-SUP-2422], and under CIF destination the cost of marine insurance is paid by the contractor [@CL-SUP-2426]. The certificates of origin, consular invoices or other documents that the Government requests are provided at the Government's expense [@CL-SUP-2425; @CL-SUP-2479]. The contractor shall be responsible for any loss of and/or damage to the goods occurring before delivery [@CL-SUP-2424; @CL-SUP-2478], and delivery under these terms is on board the ocean vessel to the specified point of destination [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO part 52 text lists section 52.247-41, the C&F destination clause, as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0398], and section 52.247-42, the CIF destination clause, as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0399].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply contract with a price of 400,000 uses the CIF destination term. The contractor pays and bears 30,000 of ocean freight, 6,000 of marine insurance and 1,500 of export fees. It sends the Government the clean on-board ocean bills of lading and the insurance certificate for the coverage the contract states. The Government asks for a certificate of origin that costs 300 to issue in the country of origin, and it bears that 300. If the goods are damaged before delivery, the contractor is responsible for the damage. Under the C&F destination term the same facts apply except that the contractor buys no marine insurance and sends no insurance certificate.

## Never confuse

Under C&F destination the contractor pays the cost of transportation [@CL-SUP-2421], unlike CIF destination, under which it pays the cost of transportation and marine insurance [@CL-SUP-2426] and shall also obtain and dispatch the insurance policy or certificate [@CL-SUP-3961; @CL-SUP-2427]. Both terms deliver on board the ocean vessel to the specified point of destination [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426]. The export delivery terms are covered on their own card; see [Export delivery terms](sup-export-delivery-terms-fas-fob-vessel-and-inland-carrier.md). The inland delivery terms at the consignee are covered on their own card; see [F.o.b. destination terms](sup-fob-destination-terms.md). The RFO part 52 text lists 52.247-41 and 52.247-42 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0398; @CL-SUP-0399]; the codified clauses set the C&F and CIF destination terms [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426].
