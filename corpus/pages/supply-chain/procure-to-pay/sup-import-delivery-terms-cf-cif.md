## Purpose

Explains what the contractor arranges and pays, which documents it provides, and when it answers for loss or damage under the two ocean delivery terms that deliver goods on board the ocean vessel at a specified destination point: cost and freight (C&F) destination and cost, insurance and freight (CIF) destination.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-41 and FAR 52.247-42 [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when the delivery term is cost and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2471] or cost, insurance and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2474] |

The C&F destination clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when the delivery term is cost and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2471]. The CIF destination clause is inserted when the delivery term is cost, insurance and freight destination [@CL-SUP-2474].

Under C&F destination the contractor delivers the goods free of expense to the Government on board the ocean vessel at the specified destination point and pays the cost of transportation [@CL-SUP-2421]. Under CIF destination the delivery is the same, and the contractor pays the cost of transportation and marine insurance [@CL-SUP-2426].

Under both clauses the contractor packs and marks the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepares it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements [@CL-SUP-2472; @CL-SUP-2475]. It delivers the shipment in good order and condition [@CL-SUP-2473; @CL-SUP-2476]. It pays and bears all applicable charges to the destination point specified in the contract, including transportation costs and export taxes or other fees or charges levied because of exportation [@CL-SUP-2422; @CL-SUP-2476].

Under both clauses the contractor obtains and dispatches promptly to the Government clean on-board ocean bills of lading to the specified destination point [@CL-SUP-2423; @CL-SUP-2477]. It is responsible for any loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery [@CL-SUP-2424; @CL-SUP-2478]. At the Government's request and expense, it provides certificates of origin, consular invoices or any other documents issued in the country of origin or of shipment, or both, that may be required for importation into the country of destination [@CL-SUP-2425; @CL-SUP-2479].

Under CIF destination the contractor must also obtain and dispatch to the Government an insurance policy or certificate providing the amount and extent of marine insurance coverage specified in the contract or agreed upon by the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-2427].

## How it works

The destination point and the packing and marking specifications are supplied by the contract, and the marine insurance coverage is specified in the contract or agreed upon by the Government Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2472; @CL-SUP-2427]. The contractor carries the freight and export charges, and under CIF destination it also carries the marine insurance [@CL-SUP-2422; @CL-SUP-2426]. The Government bears the cost of the origin and importation documents that it requests [@CL-SUP-2425; @CL-SUP-2479]. The clauses make the contractor responsible for loss or damage before delivery, and they define delivery as on board the ocean vessel to the specified destination point [@CL-SUP-2424; @CL-SUP-2421].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supply contract with a price of 400,000 uses the CIF destination term. The contractor pays and bears 30,000 of ocean freight, 6,000 of marine insurance and 1,500 of export fees. It sends the Government the clean on-board ocean bills of lading and the insurance certificate for the coverage the contract states. The Government asks for a certificate of origin that costs 300 to issue in the country of origin, and it bears that 300. If the goods are damaged before delivery, the contractor is responsible for the damage. Under the C&F destination term the same facts apply except that the contractor buys no marine insurance and sends no insurance certificate.

## Never confuse

C&F destination and CIF destination differ only in marine insurance: the contractor pays the cost of transportation under both, and also marine insurance under CIF [@CL-SUP-2421; @CL-SUP-2426]. These terms deliver on board the ocean vessel at a destination point, which differs from the export terms that deliver at the port of shipment; see [Export delivery terms](sup-export-delivery-terms-fas-fob-vessel-and-inland-carrier.md). They also differ from the inland delivery terms at the consignee; see [F.o.b. destination terms](sup-fob-destination-terms.md).

## Company calibration

Which import term each order carries and who confirms that its clause applies, because the term decides who pays freight and marine insurance and who answers for loss before delivery. [@Q-004]
