## Purpose

Shows how termination liability is computed when utility service is discontinued before the facilities cost recovery period ends, and what the nonrefundable, nonrecurring service charge covers.

### Termination liability

If the Government discontinues utility service before completion of the facilities cost recovery period, then, in consideration of the contractor furnishing and installing the new facility at its expense, the Government pays termination charges calculated as the clause sets out [@CL-SUP-2697; @CL-SUP-2698].

The facilities cost recovery period, a negotiated number of months that the contract inserts, may not exceed the term of the contract and is the period during which the contractor recovers the net cost of the new facility [@CL-SUP-2699]. The net facility cost is the cost of the new facility less the agreed salvage value [@CL-SUP-2700]. The contract's instruction for the monthly facility cost recovery rate is to divide the net facility cost by the facility's cost recovery period and insert the result [@CL-SUP-2777]. The Government pays that monthly rate whether or not service is received [@CL-SUP-2701]. Termination charges are the remaining months of the cost recovery period multiplied by the monthly facility cost recovery rate [@CL-SUP-2702]. If the contractor has recovered its capital costs at the time of termination, there is no termination liability charge [@CL-SUP-2703]. The period, the net facility cost, the monthly rate and the termination charge are blanks that the contract fills in [@CL-SUP-2699; @CL-SUP-2700; @CL-SUP-2701; @CL-SUP-2702].

### Nonrefundable, nonrecurring service charge

The Government pays a nonrefundable, nonrecurring charge when the rules and regulations of a contractor require that a customer pay a charge for the initiation of service, a contribution in aid of construction, or a nonrefundable membership fee [@CL-SUP-2704]. That charge may be in addition to or in lieu of a connection charge [@CL-SUP-2705]. The clause adds to the contractor's schedule a nonrefundable, nonrecurring charge for the item, in the amount and payable on the dates or schedule that the contract fills in [@CL-SUP-2769].

## How it works

The contract fixes the cost recovery period and the net facility cost, and the monthly rate is the net cost divided by the period [@CL-SUP-2699; @CL-SUP-2700; @CL-SUP-2777]. The Government owes that rate whether or not it receives service [@CL-SUP-2701]. If service is discontinued early, the charge is the months left in the period multiplied by the rate, and it falls away once the contractor has recovered its capital costs [@CL-SUP-2702; @CL-SUP-2703]. The nonrefundable charge belongs to a different event: a charge that the contractor's own rules require a customer to pay [@CL-SUP-2704].

Under the RFO text, the DoD part 52 class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 in place of the codified part 52 text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The following rules appear in the RFO text of these clauses. If the Government discontinues utility service before completion of the facilities cost recovery period stated in the clause [@CL-SUP-9185; @CL-SUP-2697], the Government pays termination charges calculated as set forth in the clause, in consideration of the contractor furnishing and installing the new facility at its expense [@CL-SUP-9186; @CL-SUP-2698]. The facilities cost recovery period, a negotiated number of months that the contract inserts, may not exceed the term of the contract and is the period during which the contractor recovers the net cost of the new facility [@CL-SUP-2416; @CL-SUP-2699]. Net facility cost is the cost of the new facility less the agreed salvage value [@CL-SUP-2417; @CL-SUP-2700]. The contract's instruction for the monthly facility cost recovery rate is to divide the net facility cost by the facility's cost recovery period and insert the result [@CL-SUP-2418; @CL-SUP-2777], and the Government pays that rate whether or not service is received [@CL-SUP-2419; @CL-SUP-2701]. The termination charges figure that the contract inserts is the remaining months of the facility's cost recovery period multiplied by the monthly facility cost recovery rate [@CL-SUP-2420; @CL-SUP-2702]. There is no termination liability charge where the contractor has recovered its capital costs at the time of termination [@CL-SUP-2469; @CL-SUP-2703]. As provided in the clause, the Government pays a nonrefundable, nonrecurring charge where the contractor's rules and regulations require a customer to pay a charge for the initiation of service, a contribution in aid of construction, or a nonrefundable membership fee [@CL-SUP-2470; @CL-SUP-2704]. That charge may be in addition to or in lieu of a connection charge [@CL-SUP-9189; @CL-SUP-2705].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A new facility costs 240,000 and the agreed salvage value is 40,000, so the net facility cost is 200,000. The contract term is 24 months and the contract inserts a cost recovery period of 20 months, which does not exceed the term and gives a monthly rate of 10,000. The Government discontinues service after 8 months. The remaining months are 12, so the termination charge is 12 times 10,000, or 120,000. Had the contractor already recovered its capital costs when service stopped, the termination liability charge would be zero. Separately, the contractor's rules require a service initiation charge of 1,500 from every new customer, so the Government pays that nonrefundable, nonrecurring charge, in addition to or in lieu of any connection charge, on the dates or schedule the contract states along with the item and the amount.

## Never confuse

Termination liability applies when the Government discontinues service before the cost recovery period is complete [@CL-SUP-2697]. The connection charge is a different clause; see [Utility connection charge for new facilities](sup-utility-connection-charge.md). The nonrefundable, nonrecurring charge is a separate charge that may be in addition to or in lieu of a connection charge [@CL-SUP-2705].

## Company calibration

Confirm, for each new facility, the cost recovery period, the monthly rate, the remaining exposure at any exit date, and any nonrefundable charge in the contractor's rules. [@Q-022]
