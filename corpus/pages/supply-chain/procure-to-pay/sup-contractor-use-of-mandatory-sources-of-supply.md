## Purpose

Shows which sources the contractor must buy from for Government use, what it must do when a mandatory source cannot deliver, and where price, delivery and payment fit.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2944] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.208-9 [@CL-SUP-2944] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.208-9 [@CL-SUP-0141] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2944] |
| Applies to | Contracts where the schedule names mandatory sources for supplies or services provided for Government use [@CL-SUP-2946] |

Certain supplies or services to be provided under the contract for use by the Government are required by law to be obtained from nonprofit agencies participating in the program operated by the Committee for Purchase From People Who Are Blind or Severely Disabled [@CL-SUP-2944]. Additionally, certain of these supplies are available from the Defense Logistics Agency, the General Services Administration or the Department of Veterans Affairs [@CL-SUP-2945]. The contractor obtains mandatory supplies or services from the specific sources indicated in the contract schedule [@CL-SUP-2946].

The contractor immediately notifies the Contracting Officer if a mandatory source is unable to provide the supplies or services by the time required, or if the quality of what it provides is unsatisfactory [@CL-SUP-2947]. The contractor does not purchase the supplies or services from other sources until the Contracting Officer has notified it that the Committee or an AbilityOne central nonprofit agency has authorized purchase from other sources [@CL-SUP-2948].

Price and delivery information for mandatory supplies obtained through the distribution facilities of the Defense Logistics Agency, the General Services Administration or the Department of Veterans Affairs is available from the Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-2987]. For mandatory supplies or services not available from those three agencies, price and delivery information is available from the appropriate central nonprofit agency [@CL-SUP-2988]. Payments are made directly to the source making delivery [@CL-SUP-2989]. The clause also lists points of contact for AbilityOne central nonprofit agencies, with an address and telephone number for each [@CL-SUP-2990; @CL-SUP-9791].

## How it works

The law requires certain supplies or services to be obtained from nonprofit agencies participating in the Committee's program [@CL-SUP-2944], and the contractor obtains mandatory supplies or services from the specific sources indicated in the contract schedule [@CL-SUP-2946].

If a mandatory source is unable to provide the supplies or services by the time required, or the quality is unsatisfactory, the contractor immediately notifies the Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-2947], and does not purchase from other sources until the Contracting Officer has notified the contractor that the Committee or an AbilityOne central nonprofit agency has authorized purchase from other sources [@CL-SUP-2948].

Price and delivery information is available from the Contracting Officer for supplies obtained through the DLA, GSA or VA distribution facilities [@CL-SUP-2987], and from the appropriate central nonprofit agency for mandatory supplies or services not available from them [@CL-SUP-2988].

Payments go directly to the source making delivery [@CL-SUP-2989].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.208-9 adds, in paragraph (a), a sentence saying that the Committee operates under the name AbilityOne Commission [@CL-SUP-0142], and in the same paragraph it says that some, where the codified text says certain, of these supplies are available from the Defense Logistics Agency, the General Services Administration or the Department of Veterans Affairs [@CL-SUP-0143; @CL-SUP-2945]. It keeps the requirement that certain supplies or services for Government use be obtained from nonprofit agencies participating in the Committee's program [@CL-SUP-0141; @CL-SUP-2944], and the duty to obtain mandatory supplies or services from the specific sources indicated in the contract schedule [@CL-SUP-0144; @CL-SUP-2946]. It keeps the duty to notify the Contracting Officer immediately if a mandatory source is unable to provide the supplies or services by the time required or the quality is unsatisfactory [@CL-SUP-0145; @CL-SUP-2947], and the bar on purchasing from other sources until the Contracting Officer has notified the Contractor that the Committee or an AbilityOne central nonprofit agency has authorized purchase from other sources [@CL-SUP-0146; @CL-SUP-2948]. It keeps the Contracting Officer as the source of price and delivery information for supplies obtained through the DLA/GSA/VA distribution facilities [@CL-SUP-0147; @CL-SUP-2987], the appropriate central nonprofit agency for mandatory supplies or services not available from them [@CL-SUP-0148; @CL-SUP-2988], and payment directly to the source making delivery [@CL-SUP-0149; @CL-SUP-2989]. It still lists points of contact for AbilityOne central nonprofit agencies with an address and telephone number for each, and adds a web address for each and the name NISH/SourceAmerica for the second [@CL-SUP-0150; @CL-SUP-9030; @CL-SUP-2990; @CL-SUP-9791].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The schedule lists a custodial supply item to be bought from a central nonprofit agency, which is not available from the three distribution agencies, so the contractor gets price and delivery information from that agency. The agency says it cannot ship for eight weeks, which is later than the delivery time required. The contractor notifies the Contracting Officer the same day. It does not buy elsewhere until the Contracting Officer reports that the Committee or an AbilityOne central nonprofit agency has authorized purchase from other sources. After that notice it buys 5,000 units from another supplier and pays that supplier directly.

## Never confuse

The contractor obtains mandatory supplies or services from the specific sources indicated in the contract schedule [@CL-SUP-2946] and does not purchase from other sources until the Contracting Officer has notified it that the Committee or an AbilityOne central nonprofit agency has authorized purchase from other sources [@CL-SUP-2948]. Certain of these supplies are also available from the Defense Logistics Agency, the General Services Administration or the Department of Veterans Affairs [@CL-SUP-2945], and for those obtained through their distribution facilities price and delivery information is available from the Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-2987]. Buying practice beyond this clause sits with [Contractor purchasing system](sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md).

## Company calibration

Whether each Government-use purchase is checked against the mandatory sources in the schedule before a buyer places the order. [@Q-022]
