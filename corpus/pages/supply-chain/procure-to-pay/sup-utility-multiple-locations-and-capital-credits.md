## Purpose

Shows how the Contracting Officer starts or stops service at a location, how the monthly charge is prorated, and how capital credits from a cooperative reach the Government.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2706] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.241-11 and 52.241-13 [@CL-SUP-2706; @CL-SUP-2709] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.241-11 and 52.241-13 [@CL-SUP-2300; @CL-SUP-2395] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2706] |
| Applies to | Contracts where the clauses are inserted as prescribed [@CL-SUP-2771; @CL-SUP-2774] |

### Multiple service locations

At any time by written order, the Contracting Officer may designate any location within the contractor's service area at which utility service is to commence or be discontinued [@CL-SUP-2706]. Any changes to the service specifications are made part of the contract by contract modification that includes the name and location of the service, any different rate, the point of delivery, different service specifications and any other terms and conditions [@CL-SUP-2707]. The applicable monthly charge is equitably prorated from the period in which commencement or discontinuance of service at a designated service location becomes effective [@CL-SUP-2708].

### Capital credits

The Government is a member of the cooperative that the contract names and, as any other member, is entitled to capital credits consistent with the bylaws of the cooperative, which state the contractor's obligation to pay capital credits and specify the method and time of payment [@CL-SUP-2709]. The contractor must furnish the Contracting Officer, or the designated representative, in writing and on the periodic basis the contract inserts, a list of accrued credits by contract number, year and delivery point [@CL-SUP-2772].

Capital credits are paid by check payable to the agency the contract names and forwarded to the Contracting Officer at the address the contract inserts, unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise in writing [@CL-SUP-2710]. Checks must cite the current or last contract number and indicate whether the check is partial or final payment for all capital credits accrued [@CL-SUP-2773].

## How it works

A written order from the Contracting Officer starts or stops service at a location within the contractor's service area, and a contract modification records any changes to the service specifications, including the name and location of the service, any different rate, the point of delivery, different service specifications and any other terms and conditions [@CL-SUP-2706; @CL-SUP-2707]. The applicable monthly charge is equitably prorated from the period in which commencement or discontinuance of service at a designated service location becomes effective [@CL-SUP-2708]. For capital credits, the Government is a member of the cooperative the contract names and, as any other member, is entitled to capital credits consistent with the bylaws of the cooperative, which state the contractor's obligation to pay capital credits and specify the method and time of payment; the contractor furnishes the Contracting Officer, or the designated representative, in writing on the periodic basis the contract inserts, a list of accrued credits by contract number, year and delivery point; and capital credits are paid by check payable to the agency the contract names and forwarded to the Contracting Officer at the address the contract inserts, unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise in writing [@CL-SUP-2709; @CL-SUP-2772; @CL-SUP-2710].

Under the RFO text, the DoD part 52 class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 in place of the codified part 52 text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. In RFO FAR 52.241-11 and 52.241-13, at any time by written order, the Contracting Officer may designate a location within the contractor's service area at which utility service shall commence or be discontinued [@CL-SUP-2300; @CL-SUP-2706]. Changes to the service specifications are made part of the contract by contract modification stating the name and location of the service, any different rate, the point of delivery, different service specifications, and any other terms and conditions [@CL-SUP-2393; @CL-SUP-2707]. The applicable monthly charge is equitably prorated from the period in which commencement or discontinuance of service at a designated location becomes effective [@CL-SUP-2394; @CL-SUP-2708]. The Government is a member of the cooperative and, as any other member, is entitled to capital credits consistent with the bylaws, which state the contractor's obligation to pay and specify the method and time of payment [@CL-SUP-2395; @CL-SUP-2709]. The contractor must furnish the Contracting Officer, or the designated representative, in writing and on the periodic basis the contract inserts, a list of accrued credits by contract number, year and delivery point [@CL-SUP-2396; @CL-SUP-2772]. Capital credits are paid by check payable to the agency the contract names and forwarded to the Contracting Officer at the address the contract inserts, unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise in writing [@CL-SUP-2397; @CL-SUP-2710].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract covers one site. In the third month the Contracting Officer orders service to start at a second site by written order. A modification records the name and location, a different rate that gives a monthly charge of 3,000, the point of delivery, any different service specifications and any other terms and conditions. Service starts on the eleventh day of a 30-day month, so 20 days of service are billed. The equitable proration by days is 20 of 30, or 2,000 for that month. Later, the contractor lists the accrued credits by contract number, year and delivery point. The contractor pays them by check payable to the named agency, forwarded to the Contracting Officer, and the check cites the contract number and says whether it is partial or final payment.

## Never confuse

Under the multiple service locations clause, the Contracting Officer may at any time by written order designate any location within the contractor's service area at which utility service is to commence or be discontinued, any changes to the service specifications are made part of the contract by a contract modification that includes the name and location of the service, any different rate, the point of delivery, different service specifications and any other terms and conditions, and the applicable monthly charge is equitably prorated from the period in which commencement or discontinuance of service at a designated service location becomes effective [@CL-SUP-2706; @CL-SUP-2707; @CL-SUP-2708]. Under the capital credits clause, the Government is a member of the named cooperative and, as any other member, is entitled to capital credits consistent with the cooperative's bylaws, which state the contractor's obligation to pay capital credits and specify the method and time of payment, and capital credits are paid by check payable to the named agency and forwarded to the Contracting Officer at the inserted address unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise in writing [@CL-SUP-2709; @CL-SUP-2710].

The clauses on termination liability and the nonrefundable service charge are covered in [Utility termination liability and nonrefundable service charge](sup-utility-termination-liability-and-service-charge.md).
