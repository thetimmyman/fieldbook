## Purpose

Shows who provides tags for a leased vehicle, how interagency fleet management system vehicles and related services are authorized and governed, and what happens to title when the contractor buys through Government supply sources.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2938] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.208-7, FAR 52.251-2 and FAR 52.251-1 [@CL-SUP-2938; @CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2941] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.208-90 [@CL-SUP-0630; @CL-SUP-0631; @CL-SUP-0632]; RFO FAR 52.208-91 [@CL-SUP-0633; @CL-SUP-0634]; RFO FAR 52.208-7, RFO FAR 52.251-1, and RFO FAR 52.251-2 are marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0627; @CL-SUP-0628; @CL-SUP-0629] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2938] |
| Applies to | Leased vehicles under a contract that carries the tagging clause, and contracts where the Contracting Officer authorizes fleet vehicles or Government supply sources [@CL-SUP-2938; @CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2941] |

As prescribed in FAR 8.1104(d), the tagging clause is inserted substantially as follows [@CL-SUP-2984]. While it is the intent that vehicles leased under the contract will operate on Federal tags, the Government reserves the right to utilize State tags if necessary to accomplish its mission [@CL-SUP-2938]. Should State tags be required, the contractor shall furnish the Government documentation necessary to allow acquisition of such tags [@CL-SUP-2985]. Federal tags are the responsibility of the Government [@CL-SUP-2939].

The Contracting Officer may issue the contractor an authorization to obtain interagency fleet management system vehicles and related services for use in the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-2940]. The use, service and maintenance of those vehicles, and the contractor's use of related services, shall be in accordance with 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1 [@CL-SUP-2986].

The Contracting Officer may issue the contractor an authorization to use Government supply sources in the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-2941]. Title to all property acquired by the contractor under such an authorization vests in the Government unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2942]. The provisions of the clause at FAR 52.245-1, Government Property, apply to all property acquired under such authorization [@CL-SUP-2943].

## How it works

Under the tagging clause, while it is the intent that vehicles leased under the contract will operate on Federal tags, the Government reserves the right to utilize State tags if necessary to accomplish its mission, and Federal tags are the responsibility of the Government [@CL-SUP-2938; @CL-SUP-2939].

Should State tags be required, the contractor shall furnish the Government documentation necessary to allow acquisition of such tags [@CL-SUP-2985].

Under the fleet vehicles clause, the Contracting Officer may issue the contractor an authorization to obtain interagency fleet management system (IFMS) vehicles and related services for use in the performance of the contract, and the use, service, and maintenance of the IFMS vehicles and the use of related services by the contractor shall be in accordance with 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1 [@CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2986].

Under the Government supply sources clause, the Contracting Officer may issue the contractor an authorization to use Government supply sources in the performance of the contract; title to all property acquired by the contractor under such an authorization shall vest in the Government unless otherwise specified in the contract, and the provisions of the clause at FAR 52.245-1, Government Property, apply to all property acquired under such authorization [@CL-SUP-2941; @CL-SUP-2942; @CL-SUP-2943].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.208-7 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0627], and RFO FAR 52.251-1 and RFO FAR 52.251-2 are marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0628; @CL-SUP-0629]. RFO FAR 52.208-90 keeps the Contracting Officer's authority to issue the contractor an authorization to use Government supply sources in the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-0630; @CL-SUP-2941], the vesting of title in the Government unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-0631; @CL-SUP-2942], and the application of the provisions of the clause at FAR 52.245-1, Government Property, to all property acquired under such authorization [@CL-SUP-0632; @CL-SUP-2943]. RFO FAR 52.208-91 states that the Contracting Officer may issue the contractor an authorization to obtain GSA Fleet vehicles and related services for use in the performance of the contract, whereas codified FAR 52.251-2 refers to interagency fleet management system (IFMS) vehicles and related services [@CL-SUP-0633; @CL-SUP-2940]. RFO FAR 52.208-91 states that the use, service, and maintenance of the GSA Fleet vehicles and the use of related services by the contractor must be in accordance with 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 102-34, whereas codified FAR 52.251-2 requires compliance with 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1 [@CL-SUP-0634; @CL-SUP-2986].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A mission needs State tags for six leased trucks, so the contractor sends the Government the paperwork needed to acquire them, and the Government stays responsible for any Federal tags. Separately, the Contracting Officer authorizes the contractor to obtain interagency fleet management system vehicles for the work, and the contractor uses, services and maintains them as the named regulations require. Later the Contracting Officer authorizes the contractor to use Government supply sources, and the contractor buys spares worth 20,000 under that authorization. The contract does not specify otherwise, so title to the spares vests in the Government and the Government Property clause applies to them.

## Never confuse

Under the tagging clause, while it is the intent that vehicles leased under the contract will operate on Federal tags, the Government reserves the right to utilize State tags if necessary to accomplish its mission [@CL-SUP-2938], whereas under the Government supply sources clause, title to all property acquired by the contractor under such an authorization shall vest in the Government unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2942]. Under the fleet vehicles clause, the use, service, and maintenance of IFMS vehicles and the use of related services by the contractor shall be in accordance with 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1 [@CL-SUP-2986]. Codified FAR 52.251-2 covers interagency fleet management system vehicles and cites 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1, while RFO FAR 52.208-91 covers GSA Fleet vehicles and cites 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 102-34 [@CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2986; @CL-SUP-0633; @CL-SUP-0634].

The clauses on vehicle lease payments, condition of leased vehicles and marking of leased vehicles are covered in [Vehicle lease payments, condition of leased vehicles and marking of leased vehicles](sup-vehicle-lease-payments-condition-and-marking.md).
