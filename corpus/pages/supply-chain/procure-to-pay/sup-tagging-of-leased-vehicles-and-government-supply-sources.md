## Purpose

Shows who provides tags for a leased vehicle, how interagency fleet management system vehicles and related services are authorized and governed, and what happens to title when the contractor buys through Government supply sources.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2938] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.208-7, FAR 52.251-2 and FAR 52.251-1 [@CL-SUP-2938; @CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2941] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.208-90 [@CL-SUP-0630; @CL-SUP-0631; @CL-SUP-0632]; RFO FAR 52.208-91 [@CL-SUP-0633; @CL-SUP-0634]; No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.208-7, which the RFO text prints as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0627]; FAR 52.251-1 and FAR 52.251-2 print as [Reserved] under their codified numbers [@CL-SUP-0628; @CL-SUP-0629] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2938] |
| Applies to | Leased vehicles under a contract that carries the tagging clause, and contracts where the Contracting Officer authorizes fleet vehicles or Government supply sources [@CL-SUP-2938; @CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2941] |

The tagging clause is inserted in a form substantially as the FAR text shows [@CL-SUP-2984]. While it is the intent that vehicles leased under the contract will operate on Federal tags, the Government reserves the right to use State tags if necessary to accomplish its mission [@CL-SUP-2938]. Should State tags be required, the contractor furnishes the Government documentation necessary to allow acquisition of those tags [@CL-SUP-2985]. Federal tags are the responsibility of the Government [@CL-SUP-2939].

The Contracting Officer may issue the contractor an authorization to obtain interagency fleet management system vehicles and related services for use in the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-2940]. The use, service and maintenance of those vehicles, and the contractor's use of related services, shall be in accordance with 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1 [@CL-SUP-2986].

The Contracting Officer may issue the contractor an authorization to use Government supply sources in the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-2941]. Title to all property acquired by the contractor under such an authorization vests in the Government unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2942]. The provisions of the clause at FAR 52.245-1, Government Property, apply to all property acquired under such authorization [@CL-SUP-2943].

## How it works

Tags are split: the intent is Federal tags and the Government carries the responsibility for them, but the Government may use State tags if its mission needs them [@CL-SUP-2938; @CL-SUP-2939].

Where State tags are required, the contractor supplies the documentation the Government needs to acquire them [@CL-SUP-2985].

Fleet vehicles and related services come through a Contracting Officer authorization to the contractor, and their use, service and maintenance follow the named regulations [@CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2986].

Government supply sources also depend on a Contracting Officer authorization. Property bought under it vests in the Government unless the contract says otherwise, and the Government Property clause applies to that property [@CL-SUP-2941; @CL-SUP-2942; @CL-SUP-2943].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.208-7 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0627], and prints FAR 52.251-1 and FAR 52.251-2 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0628; @CL-SUP-0629]. RFO FAR 52.208-90 keeps the Contracting Officer's authorization to use Government supply sources [@CL-SUP-0630; @CL-SUP-2941], the vesting of title in the Government unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-0631; @CL-SUP-2942], and the application of FAR 52.245-1, Government Property, to property acquired under such authorization [@CL-SUP-0632; @CL-SUP-2943]. RFO FAR 52.208-91 says the Contracting Officer may issue an authorization to obtain GSA Fleet vehicles and related services, where the codified clause says interagency fleet management system vehicles [@CL-SUP-0633; @CL-SUP-2940]. It says use, service and maintenance must follow 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 102-34, where the codified clause names 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1 [@CL-SUP-0634; @CL-SUP-2986].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A mission needs State tags for six leased trucks, so the contractor sends the Government the paperwork needed to acquire them, and the Government stays responsible for any Federal tags. Separately, the Contracting Officer authorizes the contractor to obtain interagency fleet management system vehicles for the work, and the contractor uses, services and maintains them as the named regulations require. Later the Contracting Officer authorizes the contractor to use Government supply sources, and the contractor buys spares worth 20,000 under that authorization. The contract does not specify otherwise, so title to the spares vests in the Government and the Government Property clause applies to them.

## Never confuse

Tagging is about plates, while a supply source authorization is about ownership: property bought that way vests in the Government unless the contract says otherwise [@CL-SUP-2938; @CL-SUP-2942]. The fleet vehicle clause governs how authorized vehicles and related services are used, serviced and maintained, while the supply sources clause decides who owns what is bought [@CL-SUP-2986; @CL-SUP-2942]. The codified fleet vehicle clause covers interagency fleet management system vehicles and cites 41 CFR 101-38.301-1, while the RFO clause covers GSA Fleet vehicles and cites 41 CFR 102-34 [@CL-SUP-2940; @CL-SUP-2986; @CL-SUP-0633; @CL-SUP-0634].

The clauses on vehicle lease payments, condition of leased vehicles and marking of leased vehicles are covered in [Vehicle lease payments, condition of leased vehicles and marking of leased vehicles](sup-vehicle-lease-payments-condition-and-marking.md).
