## Purpose

Describes the supervision, labor and packing duties of a moving contractor and shows what the accessorial services clause covers.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2222] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-12 and FAR 52.247-13 [@CL-SUP-2222; @CL-SUP-2223] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.247-13 [@CL-SUP-0596; @CL-SUP-0597; @CL-SUP-0598; @CL-SUP-0599; @CL-SUP-0600; @CL-SUP-0601; @CL-SUP-0602; @CL-SUP-0603; @CL-SUP-0604]; no RFO equivalent for FAR 52.247-12 [@CL-SUP-0595] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2222; @CL-SUP-2223] |
| Applies to | The supervision, labor or materials clause when the contractor is required to furnish supervision, labor or materials [@CL-SUP-2259]; the accessorial services clause in solicitations and contracts for the transportation of household goods or office furniture [@CL-SUP-2221] |

The supervision, labor or materials clause is inserted, substantially as follows, in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services when the contractor is required to furnish supervision, labor, or materials [@CL-SUP-2259]. The contractor shall furnish adequate supervision, labor, materials, supplies, and equipment necessary to perform all the services contemplated under the contract in an orderly, timely, and efficient manner [@CL-SUP-2222].

The accessorial services clause is inserted, substantially as follows, in solicitations and contracts for the transportation of household goods or office furniture [@CL-SUP-2221]. Under its packing, crating and padding paragraph, the contractor shall perform all of the packing and/or crating and padding necessary for the protection of the goods to be transported [@CL-SUP-2223], and shall furnish packing containers, including, but not limited to, barrels, boxes, wardrobes, and cartons; all crating materials; and all padding materials and equipment [@CL-SUP-2224]. It shall furnish or cause to be furnished, when necessary, padding or other protective material for the interior of the buildings, including elevators, from and to which the property will be moved under the contract [@CL-SUP-2260]. It shall also ensure that all containers and materials are clean and of quality sufficient for protection of the goods [@CL-SUP-2261].

## How it works

The contractor shall disassemble all property such as beds and sectional bookcases and prepare all appliances such as washers, driers, and record players for shipment [@CL-SUP-2225]. The contractor shall reassemble the property and service the appliances upon delivery at the new location [@CL-SUP-2226]. The contractor shall unpack and/or uncrate all property that was packed and/or crated for movement under the contract [@CL-SUP-2262]. The contractor shall also place the property in the new location as instructed by the owner of the property or authorized representative, and shall remove all packing and similar or related material from the premises as requested by the owner [@CL-SUP-2263].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.247-13 keeps the prescription to insert a clause substantially as follows in solicitations and contracts for the transportation of household goods or office furniture [@CL-SUP-0596; @CL-SUP-2221] and the duty to perform all of the packing and/or crating and padding necessary for the protection of the goods to be transported [@CL-SUP-0597; @CL-SUP-2223]. It keeps the duty to furnish packing containers, including, but not limited to, barrels, boxes, wardrobes, and cartons; all crating materials; and all padding materials and equipment [@CL-SUP-0598; @CL-SUP-2224], and to furnish or cause to be furnished, when necessary, padding or other protective material for the interior of the buildings, including elevators, from and to which the property will be moved under the contract [@CL-SUP-0599; @CL-SUP-2260]. It keeps the duty to ensure that all containers and materials are clean and of quality sufficient for protection of the goods [@CL-SUP-0600; @CL-SUP-2261]. It keeps the duties to disassemble all property such as beds and sectional bookcases and prepare all appliances such as washers, driers, and record players for shipment [@CL-SUP-0601; @CL-SUP-2225], to reassemble the property and service the appliances upon delivery at the new location [@CL-SUP-0602; @CL-SUP-2226], to unpack and/or uncrate all property that was packed and/or crated for movement under the contract [@CL-SUP-0603; @CL-SUP-2262], and to place the property in the new location as instructed by the owner of the property or authorized representative and remove all packing and similar or related material from the premises as requested by the owner [@CL-SUP-0604; @CL-SUP-2263]. RFO FAR 52.247-12 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0595].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An office relocation covers 20 workstations, 200 cartons and 3 appliances. The contractor supplies the 200 cartons, the crating and the padding, and where necessary protects the building interiors and elevators with padding. It checks that all containers and materials are clean and good enough to protect the goods, packs and crates the goods, disassembles the 20 workstations, and prepares the 3 appliances for shipment. On delivery it reassembles the workstations, services the appliances, unpacks and uncrates everything it packed or crated, places the property where the owner's representative instructs, and removes the packing material when the owner asks.

## Never confuse

The supervision clause is a general duty to furnish adequate supervision, labor, materials, supplies and equipment, and it applies when the contractor is required to furnish them [@CL-SUP-2222; @CL-SUP-2259]. The accessorial clause applies to the transportation of household goods or office furniture and names the packing, crating, disassembly, reassembly and unpacking duties of such a move [@CL-SUP-2221; @CL-SUP-2223; @CL-SUP-2225; @CL-SUP-2262]. Codified FAR 52.247-12 sets out the supervision, labor, or materials clause [@CL-SUP-2222], unlike RFO FAR 52.247-12, which is marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0595] and RFO FAR 52.247-13 sets out the accessorial services clause [@CL-SUP-0596].

## Company calibration

Confirm that the company's move estimate itemizes packing, crating and disassembly duties and who confirms that the clause applies to each order. [@Q-004]
