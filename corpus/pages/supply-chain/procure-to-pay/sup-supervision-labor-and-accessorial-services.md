## Purpose

Describes the supervision, labor and packing duties of a moving contractor and shows what the accessorial services clause covers and what its text leaves unsaid.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2222] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-12 and FAR 52.247-13 [@CL-SUP-2222; @CL-SUP-2223] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2222; @CL-SUP-2223] |
| Applies to | The supervision, labor or materials clause when the contractor is required to furnish supervision, labor or materials [@CL-SUP-2259]; the accessorial services clause in solicitations and contracts for the transportation of household goods or office furniture [@CL-SUP-2221] |

The supervision, labor or materials clause is inserted, substantially as printed, in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services when the contractor is required to furnish supervision, labor or materials [@CL-SUP-2259]. The contractor must furnish adequate supervision, labor, materials, supplies and equipment necessary to perform all the services contemplated under the contract in an orderly, timely and efficient manner [@CL-SUP-2222].

The accessorial services clause is inserted, substantially as printed, in solicitations and contracts for the transportation of household goods or office furniture [@CL-SUP-2221]. Under its packing, crating and padding paragraph, the contractor must perform all packing, crating and padding necessary for the protection of the goods to be transported [@CL-SUP-2223], and furnish the packing containers, all crating materials, and all padding materials and equipment [@CL-SUP-2224]. It must furnish or cause to be furnished, when necessary, padding or other protective material for the interior of the buildings, including elevators, from and to which the property will be moved [@CL-SUP-2260]. It must also ensure that all containers and materials are clean and of quality sufficient for protection of the goods [@CL-SUP-2261].

## How it works

The contractor must disassemble property such as beds and sectional bookcases and prepare appliances such as washers, driers and record players for shipment [@CL-SUP-2225]. It must reassemble the property and service the appliances upon delivery at the new location [@CL-SUP-2226]. It must unpack or uncrate all property that was packed or crated for movement under the contract [@CL-SUP-2262]. It must also place the property in the new location as instructed by the owner of the property or authorized representative, and remove all packing and similar or related material from the premises as requested by the owner [@CL-SUP-2263]. The clause text does not say how accessorial services are ordered or how they are paid.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An office relocation covers 20 workstations, 200 cartons and 3 appliances. The contractor supplies the 200 cartons, the crating and the padding, and where necessary protects the building interiors and elevators with padding. It checks that all containers and materials are clean and good enough to protect the goods, packs and crates the goods, disassembles the 20 workstations, and prepares the 3 appliances for shipment. On delivery it reassembles the workstations, services the appliances, unpacks and uncrates everything it packed or crated, places the property where the owner's representative instructs, and removes the packing material when the owner asks. The clause text gives no ordering or payment mechanics for this work.

## Never confuse

The supervision clause is a general duty to furnish adequate supervision, labor, materials, supplies and equipment, and it applies when the contractor is required to furnish them [@CL-SUP-2222; @CL-SUP-2259]. The accessorial clause applies to the transportation of household goods or office furniture and names the packing, crating, disassembly, reassembly and unpacking duties of such a move [@CL-SUP-2221; @CL-SUP-2223; @CL-SUP-2225; @CL-SUP-2262].

## Company calibration

Confirm that the company's move estimate itemizes packing, crating and disassembly duties and who confirms that the clause applies to each order. [@Q-004]
