## Purpose

Shows what a services solicitation asks offerors to do about visiting the site, and what the contractor owes when its work damages Government buildings, equipment or vegetation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2911] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.237-1 and FAR 52.237-2 [@CL-SUP-2911; @CL-SUP-2914] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.237-1 [@CL-SUP-0489; @CL-SUP-0490; @CL-SUP-0491] and RFO FAR 52.237-2 [@CL-SUP-0492; @CL-SUP-0493; @CL-SUP-0494; @CL-SUP-0495] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2911] |
| Applies to | The site visit provision goes in solicitations, and the protection clause in solicitations and contracts, for services performed on Government installations, unless the work is construction [@CL-SUP-2911; @CL-SUP-2914] |

The site visit provision is inserted in solicitations for services to be performed on Government installations, unless the solicitation is for construction [@CL-SUP-2911]. Offerors or quoters are urged and expected to inspect the site where services are to be performed and to satisfy themselves regarding all general and local conditions that may affect the cost of contract performance, to the extent that the information is reasonably obtainable [@CL-SUP-2912]. In no event shall failure to inspect the site constitute grounds for a claim after contract award [@CL-SUP-2913].

The protection clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for services to be performed on Government installations, unless a construction contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-2914]. The contractor shall use reasonable care to avoid damaging existing buildings, equipment, and vegetation on the Government installation [@CL-SUP-2915]. If the contractor's failure to use reasonable care causes damage to any of this property, the contractor shall replace or repair the damage at no expense to the Government as the Contracting Officer directs [@CL-SUP-2916]. If the contractor fails or refuses to make such repair or replacement, the contractor shall be liable for the cost, which may be deducted from the contract price [@CL-SUP-2917].

## How it works

Under the site visit provision, offerors or quoters are urged and expected to inspect the site where services are to be performed and to satisfy themselves regarding all general and local conditions that may affect the cost of contract performance, to the extent that the information is reasonably obtainable [@CL-SUP-2912].

In no event shall failure to inspect the site constitute grounds for a claim after contract award [@CL-SUP-2913].

Under the protection clause, the contractor shall use reasonable care to avoid damaging existing buildings, equipment, and vegetation on the Government installation [@CL-SUP-2915].

If the contractor's failure to use reasonable care causes damage to any of this property, the contractor shall replace or repair the damage at no expense to the Government as the Contracting Officer directs [@CL-SUP-2916].

If the contractor fails or refuses to make such repair or replacement, the contractor shall be liable for the cost, which may be deducted from the contract price [@CL-SUP-2917].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.237-1 keeps the rule that offerors or quoters are urged and expected to inspect the site where services are to be performed and to satisfy themselves regarding all general and local conditions that may affect the cost of contract performance, to the extent that the information is reasonably obtainable [@CL-SUP-0489; @CL-SUP-2912]. It keeps the rule that in no event shall failure to inspect the site constitute grounds for a claim after contract award [@CL-SUP-0490; @CL-SUP-2913]. The RFO introductory text for FAR 52.237-1 states "As prescribed in 37.802-5(a), insert the following provision:" [@CL-SUP-0491], while the codified introductory text states "As prescribed in 37.110(a), insert the following provision in solicitations for services to be performed on Government installations, unless the solicitation is for construction:" [@CL-SUP-2911]. The RFO introductory text for FAR 52.237-2 cites 37.802-5(b) and directs inserting the clause in solicitations and contracts for services to be performed on Government installations, unless a construction contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-0492], while the codified introductory text cites 37.110(b) for the same insertion scope [@CL-SUP-2914]. RFO FAR 52.237-2 keeps the duty to use reasonable care to avoid damaging existing buildings, equipment, and vegetation on the Government installation [@CL-SUP-0493; @CL-SUP-2915]. It keeps the duty to replace or repair damage caused by the contractor's failure to use reasonable care at no expense to the Government as the Contracting Officer directs [@CL-SUP-0494; @CL-SUP-2916]. It keeps the contractor's liability for the cost, which may be deducted from the contract price, if the contractor fails or refuses to make such repair or replacement [@CL-SUP-0495; @CL-SUP-2917].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cleaning contract is for services at a warehouse on a Government installation, and the solicitation was not for construction. An offeror priced the work without visiting the site. After award it finds the loading dock slower to reach than it assumed and asks for more money. The provision gives no ground for that claim, because failure to inspect is not grounds for a claim after award.

During performance, careless handling of a pallet jack by the contractor's crew chips a door frame. That is damage caused by a failure to use reasonable care, so the Contracting Officer directs the contractor to repair it at no expense to the Government. The contractor refuses. The repair costs 4,800, the contractor is liable for that cost, and the Government deducts 4,800 from the contract price.

## Never confuse

Under the site visit provision, offerors or quoters are urged and expected to inspect the site and in no event shall failure to inspect the site constitute grounds for a claim after contract award, while under the protection clause, if the contractor fails or refuses to make repair or replacement for damage caused by its failure to use reasonable care, the contractor shall be liable for the cost, which may be deducted from the contract price [@CL-SUP-2912; @CL-SUP-2913; @CL-SUP-2916; @CL-SUP-2917]. Under the codified text, the site visit provision is inserted in solicitations for services to be performed on Government installations unless the solicitation is for construction, and the protection clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for services to be performed on Government installations unless a construction contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-2911; @CL-SUP-2914].

Codified FAR 52.237-1 states that the provision is inserted in solicitations for services to be performed on Government installations, unless the solicitation is for construction [@CL-SUP-2911], while RFO FAR 52.237-1 states "As prescribed in 37.802-5(a), insert the following provision:" [@CL-SUP-0491]. RFO FAR 52.237-2 states that the clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for services to be performed on Government installations, unless a construction contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-0492].

The construction counterparts, the preconstruction site visit provision and the protection of existing vegetation, structures and equipment, are covered in [Preconstruction conference, site visit and preparation of construction proposals](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-construction-preconstruction-conference-and-site-visit.md) and [Other contracts at the site, protection of existing property and cleaning up](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-construction-other-contracts-protection-and-cleanup.md).
