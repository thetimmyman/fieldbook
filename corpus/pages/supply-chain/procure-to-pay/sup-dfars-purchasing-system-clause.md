## Purpose

Shows which version of the DoD Contractor Purchasing System Administration clause is used, and which other clauses in the solicitation or contract decide the choice.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-3853] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 244.305-71 [@CL-SUP-3853] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-3853] |
| Applies to | DoD solicitations and contracts containing the FAR Subcontracts clause, or the counterfeit electronic part clause without it [@CL-SUP-3853; @CL-SUP-3854] |

The section says to use the basic clause or its alternate as its two paragraphs set out [@CL-SUP-3855]. Paragraph (a) directs use of the clause at 252.244-7001, Contractor Purchasing System Administration—Basic, in solicitations and contracts containing the clause at FAR 52.244-2, Subcontracts [@CL-SUP-3853]. Paragraph (b) directs use of Alternate I of the same clause in solicitations and contracts that contain the clause at 252.246-7007, Contractor Counterfeit Electronic Part Detection and Avoidance System, but do not contain FAR 52.244-2, Subcontracts [@CL-SUP-3854].

The text states no threshold, deadline or exception. It does not say who inserts the clause. It does not cover a solicitation or contract that contains neither the FAR Subcontracts clause nor the counterfeit electronic part clause, so that case is not established here.

## How it works

The choice depends on which clauses the solicitation or contract already contains. If it contains the FAR Subcontracts clause, paragraph (a) directs the basic version [@CL-SUP-3853]. If it contains the counterfeit electronic part clause and not the FAR Subcontracts clause, paragraph (b) directs Alternate I [@CL-SUP-3854]. The FAR Subcontracts clause is itself prescribed by FAR 44.204 [@CL-SUP-3844]; see [clause prescriptions for subcontracts](sup-subcontract-clause-prescriptions.md). What the clause requires of the contractor is in [the Contractor Purchasing System Administration clause](sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md), and the counterfeit electronic part clause is in [the counterfeit part detection system card](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-dfars-counterfeit-part-detection-system.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD solicitation contains both the FAR Subcontracts clause and the counterfeit electronic part clause. The basic clause is used, because paragraph (a) applies to solicitations containing the FAR Subcontracts clause [@CL-SUP-3853]. Alternate I is not used, because paragraph (b) covers only solicitations and contracts that do not contain that clause [@CL-SUP-3854].

## Never confuse

The DoD clause is not the FAR Subcontracts clause. Paragraph (a) keys the DoD clause to the presence of the FAR clause and does not replace it [@CL-SUP-3853].

Alternate I is not a free substitute for the basic version. It is directed for solicitations and contracts that have the counterfeit electronic part clause and lack the FAR Subcontracts clause [@CL-SUP-3854]. This section does not decide whether a purchasing system is approved or reviewed.
