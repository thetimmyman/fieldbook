## Purpose

Shows when the contracting officer must or may put each subcontract clause that this section covers into a solicitation or contract, and the thresholds that decide it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-3844] |
| Codified citation | FAR 44.204 [@CL-SUP-3844] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-3844] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts of the types listed in the prescriptions [@CL-SUP-3844] |

FAR 44.204 directs the contracting officer with "shall" in paragraphs (a) and (c) and permits insertion with "may" in paragraph (b), while FAR 44.403 directs the contracting officer with "shall" for the commercial products and commercial services subcontract clause [@CL-SUP-3844; @CL-SUP-3850; @CL-SUP-3851; @CL-SUP-3857].

The Subcontracts clause. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.244-2, Subcontracts, in solicitations and contracts when contemplating any one of five alternatives [@CL-SUP-3844]. The first is a cost-reimbursement contract [@CL-SUP-3844]. The second is a letter contract that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-SUP-3845]. The third is a fixed-price contract that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and under which unpriced contract actions, including unpriced modifications or unpriced delivery orders, are anticipated [@CL-SUP-3846]. The fourth is a time-and-materials contract that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, and the fifth is a labor-hour contract that exceeds it [@CL-SUP-3847]. If a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated for a civilian agency other than the Coast Guard and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the officer shall use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-SUP-3848]. Use of the clause is not required in either of two cases: fixed-price architect-engineer contracts, or contracts for mortuary services, refuse services, or shipment and storage of personal property, when an agency-prescribed clause on approval of subcontractors' facilities is required [@CL-SUP-3849].

The architect-engineer clause. The contracting officer may insert the clause at 52.244-4, Subcontractors and Outside Associates and Consultants (Architect-Engineer Services), in architect-engineer contracts [@CL-SUP-3850].

The competition clause. When contracting by negotiation, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.244-5, Competition in Subcontracting, in solicitations and contracts when the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, unless (1) a firm-fixed-price contract, awarded on the basis of adequate price competition or whose prices are set by law or regulation, is contemplated, or (2) a time-and-materials, labor-hour, or architect-engineer contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-3851; @CL-SUP-3852].

The commercial products and commercial services clause. Under FAR 44.403, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.244-6, Subcontracts for Commercial Products and Commercial Services, in solicitations and contracts other than those for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-SUP-3857]. See [subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](../sourcing-evidence/sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md).

## How it works

In FAR 44.204(a)(1), the triggers for a letter contract, a fixed-price contract under which unpriced contract actions are anticipated, and a time-and-materials or labor-hour contract require that the contract exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, and FAR 44.204(c) requires that the contract amount be expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-SUP-3845; @CL-SUP-3846; @CL-SUP-3847; @CL-SUP-3851]. The contracting officer shall insert the Subcontracts clause at 52.244-2 when contemplating any of the contract types in FAR 44.204(a)(1) (subject to the exclusions in FAR 44.204(a)(2)), shall insert the Competition in Subcontracting clause at 52.244-5 when contracting by negotiation and the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold (unless an exception in FAR 44.204(c)(1) or (2) applies), and may insert the clause at 52.244-4 in architect-engineer contracts [@CL-SUP-3844; @CL-SUP-3849; @CL-SUP-3851; @CL-SUP-3852; @CL-SUP-3850]. For subcontract consent requirements, see [consent to subcontract](sup-consent-to-subcontract.md) and [consent and advance notification](sup-subcontracts-consent-advance-notification.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A defense agency negotiates a time-and-materials contract expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold. The contracting officer inserts the Subcontracts clause, because the contract is a time-and-materials contract that exceeds that threshold [@CL-SUP-3847]. The officer does not insert the Competition in Subcontracting clause, because a time-and-materials contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-3852]. Had the contract been cost-reimbursement at a civilian agency other than the Coast Guard and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the officer would use the Subcontracts clause with its Alternate I [@CL-SUP-3848].

## Never confuse

FAR 44.204(a)(1) prescribes when the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.244-2, Subcontracts, in solicitations and contracts, whereas FAR 44.201-1 states when consent to subcontracts is required under contracts containing the Subcontracts clause [@CL-SUP-3844; @CL-SUP-3830; @CL-SUP-3833; @CL-SUP-3834]. See [consent to subcontract](sup-consent-to-subcontract.md).

Under FAR 44.204(b), the contracting officer may insert the clause at 52.244-4 in architect-engineer contracts, whereas under FAR 44.204(a)(1) the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.244-2 when contemplating the listed contract types [@CL-SUP-3850; @CL-SUP-3844]. Use of the clause at 52.244-2 is not required in fixed-price architect-engineer contracts, while a cost-reimbursement contract is one of the triggers requiring insertion of 52.244-2 [@CL-SUP-3849; @CL-SUP-3844]. Under FAR 44.204(c)(2), the Competition in Subcontracting clause at 52.244-5 is not required when a time-and-materials, labor-hour, or architect-engineer contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-3851; @CL-SUP-3852].
