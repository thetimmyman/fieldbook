## Purpose

Shows who grants, withholds or withdraws approval of a contractor's purchasing system under the FAR, what the approval notification says, and the timing steps after a withhold or withdrawal.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0527] |
| Codified citation | FAR 44.305-1, FAR 44.305-2 and FAR 44.305-3 [@CL-SUP-0527; @CL-SUP-0528; @CL-SUP-0530] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0527] |
| Applies to | A contractor whose purchasing system approval is granted, withheld or withdrawn [@CL-SUP-0527] |

The cognizant administrative contracting officer is responsible for granting, withholding, or withdrawing approval of a contractor's purchasing system [@CL-SUP-0527]. The officer shall approve a purchasing system only after determining that the contractor's purchasing policies and practices are efficient and provide adequate protection of the Government's interests [@CL-SUP-0666]. The officer shall promptly notify the contractor in writing of the granting, withholding, or withdrawal of approval [@CL-SUP-0667].

For defense contracts, the defense policy section is used instead of the FAR paragraph on the contractor's reply to improvement recommendations and the FAR paragraph on the plan and follow-up after a withhold or withdrawal, as the [DoD purchasing system administration](sup-dod-purchasing-system-administration.md) card explains [@CL-SUP-0533].

## How it works

The officer shall withhold or withdraw approval when there are major weaknesses or when the contractor is unable to provide sufficient information upon which to make an affirmative determination [@CL-SUP-0530]. The officer may withdraw approval at any time on the basis of a determination that there has been a deterioration of the contractor's purchasing system or to protect the Government's interest [@CL-SUP-0531]. Approval shall be withheld or withdrawn when there is a recurring noncompliance with requirements, including but not limited to certified cost or pricing data and implementation of cost accounting standards [@CL-SUP-0670].

The notification granting approval includes a statement that approval waives the contractual requirement for advance notification in fixed-price contracts, but not for cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-SUP-0528]. It also states that approval waives the requirement for consent to subcontracts in fixed-price contracts and for specified subcontracts in cost-reimbursement contracts, but not for those subcontracts, if any, selected for special surveillance and identified in the contract Schedule [@CL-SUP-0668]. In exceptional circumstances, consent to certain subcontracts or classes of subcontracts may be required even though the contractor's purchasing system has been approved [@CL-SUP-0669].

When approval is withheld or withdrawn, the officer shall within 10 days after completing the in-plant review inform the contractor in writing and specify the deficiencies that must be corrected to qualify the system for approval [@CL-SUP-0671]. The request that the contractor furnish within 15 days a plan for accomplishing the necessary actions is followed, if the plan is accepted, by a follow-up review as soon as the contractor notifies the officer that the deficiencies have been corrected [@CL-SUP-0532]. When recommendations are made for improvement of an approved system, the contractor shall be requested to reply within 15 days with a position regarding the recommendations [@CL-SUP-0529].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds approval at one plant, and its fixed-price subcontracts need no consent under the approval notice. At a later review the officer finds a recurring failure to obtain certified cost or pricing data from subcontractors, and the in-plant review ends on day 100.

Answer: approval shall be withheld or withdrawn for recurring noncompliance. The officer informs the contractor in writing and lists the deficiencies within 10 days after day 100, and asks for a plan within 15 days. If the plan is accepted, a follow-up review comes once the contractor reports the fixes.

## Never confuse

FAR withholding or withdrawal of approval is not the same as the defense disapproval and payment withhold. For defense contracts the defense policy is used instead of two FAR paragraphs [@CL-SUP-0533], and payment withholds under the business systems clause are on the [Business system withholds](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-business-system-withholds.md) card.
