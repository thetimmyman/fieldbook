## Purpose

Shows the Government's right to inspect and test a freight carrier's services, facilities and equipment, the access and help the contractor must give, and the transportation services for which the clause is not used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2465] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-14 [@CL-SUP-2465] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2465] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for freight transportation services by rail, motor, domestic freight forwarder and domestic water carriers, except the services the clause excludes [@CL-SUP-2465; @CL-SUP-2466; @CL-SUP-2531] |

The clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for freight transportation services, including local drayage, by rail, motor (including bus), domestic freight forwarder and domestic water carriers, including inland, coastwise and intercoastal service [@CL-SUP-2465]. The contracting officer must not use the clause for transportation services by domestic or international air carriers or by international ocean carriers [@CL-SUP-2466]. The contracting officer must also not use it for freight services provided under bills of lading or for those negotiated for reduced rates under sections 10721 or 13712 of title 49 of the United States Code [@CL-SUP-2531].

The Government has the right to inspect and test the contractor's services, facilities and equipment at all reasonable times [@CL-SUP-2467]. The contractor must furnish Government representatives with the free access and reasonable facilities and assistance required to accomplish their inspections and tests [@CL-SUP-2468].

## How it works

The clause gives the Government a right of inspection and testing over the carrier's services, facilities and equipment, limited to reasonable times [@CL-SUP-2467]. The contractor's matching duty is free access plus the reasonable facilities and assistance the inspectors need [@CL-SUP-2468]. The clause has no entries to complete. Whether it applies depends on the mode of carriage and on the exclusions for air carriers, international ocean carriers, services under bills of lading and reduced-rate services [@CL-SUP-2465; @CL-SUP-2466; @CL-SUP-2531].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A domestic motor carrier holds a freight transportation contract that contains the clause. A Government representative arrives during a working shift and asks to inspect the loading dock and two tractor units. The carrier gives free access and reasonable assistance, because the inspection takes place at a reasonable time and is within the Government's right. If the same freight were moved by an international ocean carrier, the contracting officer would not use the clause.

## Never confuse

Inspection of transportation concerns the carrier's services, facilities and equipment for freight transportation [@CL-SUP-2467]. It does not govern inspection of supplies that a contractor delivers; for that, see [Inspection of supplies, fixed-price](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-inspection-of-supplies-fixed-price.md). It also leaves out air carriage, international ocean carriage, freight services under bills of lading and reduced-rate services [@CL-SUP-2466; @CL-SUP-2531].

## Company calibration

Whether each freight service the company performs or buys sits inside the carrier scope of the clause, and whether each site can give access on request. [@Q-022]
