## Purpose

Shows how a prime contractor passes accelerated payments from the Government on to small business subcontractors, and the Government-side policy behind them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0570] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-40, FAR 32.009-1 and FAR 32.009-2 [@CL-SUP-0570; @CL-SUP-0575; @CL-SUP-0706; @CL-SUP-0707; @CL-SUP-4110]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-SUP-9569]; DFARS 232.009-1 [@CL-SUP-0574] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.232-40 [@CL-SUP-0001; @CL-SUP-0002; @CL-SUP-0003; @CL-SUP-0004; @CL-SUP-0005], RFO FAR 32.009-1 [@CL-SUP-4103; @CL-SUP-4104; @CL-SUP-4105; @CL-SUP-4106; @CL-SUP-4107; @CL-SUP-4108] and RFO FAR 32.009-2 [@CL-SUP-4109]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-SUP-9606] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0570] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause inserted as prescribed in FAR 32.009-2 [@CL-SUP-0704; @CL-SUP-4110] |

Within 15 days after receipt of accelerated payments from the Government, the contractor shall make accelerated payments to its small business subcontractors under the contract, to the maximum extent practicable and prior to when such payment is otherwise required under the applicable contract [@CL-SUP-0570] or subcontract [@CL-SUP-0713]. The payment to a small business subcontractor follows receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation from that subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0705]. The contractor agrees to make such payments without any further consideration from or fees charged to the subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0571]. The FAR definitions section describes a small business subcontractor as a concern that does not exceed the size standard for the North American Industry Classification Systems code that the prime contractor determines best describes the product or service being acquired by the subcontract [@CL-SUP-9569]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines a small business subcontractor as a concern that does not exceed the size standard for the North American Industry Classification Systems code that the prime contractor determines best describes the product or service being acquired by the subcontract, the same in substance as the codified definition [@CL-SUP-9606; @CL-SUP-9569].

## How it works

The acceleration of payments under the clause does not provide any new rights under the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-SUP-0572]. The FAR text on accelerated payments adds that the acceleration does not affect the application of the late payment interest provisions of the Act [@CL-SUP-0706]. The contractor shall include the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), in all subcontracts with small business concerns, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-SUP-0573]. The FAR prescribes inserting the clause in all solicitations and contracts [@CL-SUP-4110]. On the Government side, agencies other than DoD shall provide accelerated payments, to the fullest extent permitted by law, with a goal of 15 days after receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation, if a specific payment date is not established by contract, to the recipients listed in the same paragraph [@CL-SUP-0575]. Those recipients include small business contractors and prime contractors that subcontract with a small business concern, if the prime contractor agrees to make payments to the small business subcontractor within 15 days of receiving the accelerated payment from the Government, and each sentence continues with further terms of that agreement: after receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation from the small business subcontractor, to the maximum extent practicable, without any further consideration from or fees charged to the subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0707]. Under the defense supplement, the statute requires DoD to provide accelerated payments to small business contractors and subcontractors, to the fullest extent permitted by law, with a goal of 15 days [@CL-SUP-0574].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.232-40 keeps the rule described above: within 15 days after receipt of accelerated payments from the Government, the contractor makes accelerated payments to its small business subcontractors under the contract, to the maximum extent practicable and prior to when such payment is otherwise required under the applicable contract [@CL-SUP-0001] [@CL-SUP-0570] or subcontract [@CL-SUP-0002] [@CL-SUP-0713], after receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation from the small business subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0002] [@CL-SUP-0705]. It keeps the rule that the contractor makes those payments without any further consideration from or fees charged to the subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0003] [@CL-SUP-0571], and the rule that the acceleration of payments does not provide any new rights under the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-SUP-0004] [@CL-SUP-0572]. It also keeps the rule that the contractor includes the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), in all subcontracts with small business concerns, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-SUP-0005] [@CL-SUP-0573].

DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text for the Government-side policy [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.009-1(a)(1) keeps the rule that agencies other than DoD must provide accelerated payments, to the fullest extent permitted by law, with a goal of 15 days after receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation, if a specific payment date is not established by contract, with "must" where the codified text says "shall" [@CL-SUP-0575; @CL-SUP-4103]. RFO FAR 32.009-1(a)(2) says that, pursuant to section 3801(b) of title 10, United States Code, DoD must provide accelerated payments, to the fullest extent permitted by law, with a goal of 15 days after receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation [@CL-SUP-4104]. Both paragraphs list small business contractors and prime contractors that subcontract with a small business concern, if the prime contractor agrees to make payments to the small business subcontractor within 15 days of receiving the accelerated payment from the Government [@CL-SUP-0707; @CL-SUP-4105], and they add that the agreement applies after receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation from the small business subcontractor, to the maximum extent practicable, without any further consideration from or fees charged to the subcontractor [@CL-SUP-4106]. RFO FAR 32.009-1(b) keeps the rule that the acceleration does not provide any new rights under the Prompt Payment Act and does not affect the application of its late payment interest provisions [@CL-SUP-0706; @CL-SUP-4107]. RFO FAR 32.009-1(c) says agencies may use the Governmentwide commercial purchase card as a method of payment to facilitate accelerated payment, to earn refunds, and to reduce invoice processing costs [@CL-SUP-4108]. RFO FAR 32.009-2 keeps the prescription to insert clause 52.232-40 in all solicitations and contracts [@CL-SUP-4110; @CL-SUP-4109].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The Government pays a prime contractor on an accelerated basis on the fifth day of a month. A small business subcontractor has already sent a proper invoice with all required documentation.

Answer: the prime contractor pays the subcontractor within 15 days after receiving the Government payment, to the maximum extent practicable, and charges no fee for doing so.

## Never confuse

Acceleration is not a new Prompt Payment Act entitlement. It provides no new rights under the Act [@CL-SUP-0572] and does not affect the application of its late payment interest provisions [@CL-SUP-0706]. The ordinary due date and interest penalty rules are on the [Prompt payment](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md) card.
