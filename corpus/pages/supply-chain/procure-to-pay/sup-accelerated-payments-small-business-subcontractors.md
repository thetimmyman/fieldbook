## Purpose

Shows how a prime contractor passes accelerated payments from the Government on to small business subcontractors, and the Government-side policy behind them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0570] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.232-40 [@CL-SUP-0570] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0570] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause inserted as prescribed in FAR 32.009-2 [@CL-SUP-0704] |

Within 15 days after receipt of accelerated payments from the Government, the contractor shall make accelerated payments to its small business subcontractors under the contract, to the maximum extent practicable and prior to when such payment is otherwise required under the applicable contract [@CL-SUP-0570] or subcontract [@CL-SUP-0713]. The payment to a small business subcontractor follows receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation from that subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0705]. The contractor agrees to make such payments without any further consideration from or fees charged to the subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0571].

## How it works

The acceleration of payments under the clause does not provide any new rights under the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-SUP-0572]. The FAR text on accelerated payments adds that the acceleration does not affect the application of the late payment interest provisions of the Act [@CL-SUP-0706]. The contractor shall include the substance of the clause in all subcontracts with small business concerns, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-SUP-0573]. On the Government side, agencies other than DoD shall provide accelerated payments, to the fullest extent permitted by law, with a goal of 15 days after receipt of a proper invoice and all other required documentation, if a specific payment date is not established by contract, to the recipients listed in the same paragraph [@CL-SUP-0575]. Those recipients include small business contractors and prime contractors that subcontract with a small business concern, if the prime contractor agrees to make payments to the small business subcontractor within 15 days of receiving the accelerated payment from the Government [@CL-SUP-0707]. Under the defense supplement, the statute requires DoD to provide accelerated payments to small business contractors and subcontractors, to the fullest extent permitted by law, with a goal of 15 days [@CL-SUP-0574].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The Government pays a prime contractor on an accelerated basis on the fifth day of a month. A small business subcontractor has already sent a proper invoice with all required documentation.

Answer: the prime contractor pays the subcontractor within 15 days after receiving the Government payment, to the maximum extent practicable, and charges no fee for doing so.

## Never confuse

Acceleration is not a new Prompt Payment Act entitlement. It provides no new rights under the Act [@CL-SUP-0572] and does not affect the application of its late payment interest provisions [@CL-SUP-0706]. The ordinary due date and interest penalty rules are on the [Prompt payment](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md) card.
