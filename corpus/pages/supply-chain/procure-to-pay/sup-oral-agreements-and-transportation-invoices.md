## Purpose

Explains which changes to a transportation contract must be in writing, and what a contractor's invoice must show so the charge can be matched to its order.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2320] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-27 and FAR 52.247-28 [@CL-SUP-2320; @CL-SUP-2321] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent [@CL-SUP-0481; @CL-SUP-0482] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2320] |
| Applies to | The oral agreement clause in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2355]; the invoices clause in solicitations and contracts for drayage or other term contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2323] |

The oral agreement clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2355]. No oral statement of any person modifies or otherwise affects the terms, conditions or specifications stated in the contract, and all modifications to the contract must be made in writing by the contracting officer or an authorized representative [@CL-SUP-2320].

The invoices clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for drayage or other term contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2323]. The contractor submits itemized invoices as instructed by the agency ordering services under the contract [@CL-SUP-2321]. The contractor annotates each invoice with the contract number and other ordering office document identification [@CL-SUP-2322].

## How it works

Under the oral agreement clause a change to terms, conditions or specifications must be made in writing by the contracting officer or an authorized representative [@CL-SUP-2320]. Under the invoices clause the ordering agency gives the invoicing instructions, and the contractor itemizes accordingly [@CL-SUP-2321]. Each invoice carries the contract number and the ordering office document identification [@CL-SUP-2322].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.247-27 as reserved with no clause text [@CL-SUP-0481], and prints FAR 52.247-28 as reserved with no clause text [@CL-SUP-0482]. The codified oral agreement clause and the codified invoices clause described above therefore have no text under those numbers in the RFO text [@CL-SUP-2320; @CL-SUP-2321; @CL-SUP-0481; @CL-SUP-0482].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A carrier receives a telephone request from an agency employee for 10 extra pickups. The telephone request does not modify the contract's terms, because no oral statement of any person does. Only a written modification from the contracting officer or an authorized representative changes them. The carrier's invoice for the ordered pickups lists each pickup as an item, as the ordering agency instructed, and shows the contract number and the ordering office's document number. The example applies the codified clauses; the RFO text prints both clause numbers as reserved [@CL-SUP-0481; @CL-SUP-0482].

## Never confuse

An invoice annotation is not a modification. The annotation identifies the contract and order the invoice relates to [@CL-SUP-2322], while a change to the contract's terms needs a written modification from the contracting officer or an authorized representative [@CL-SUP-2320]. The ordering agency's invoicing instructions govern how invoices are itemized [@CL-SUP-2321].

The codified text prints the oral agreement clause at FAR 52.247-27 [@CL-SUP-2320], but the RFO text prints that number as reserved [@CL-SUP-0481]. The codified text prints the invoices clause at FAR 52.247-28 [@CL-SUP-2321], but the RFO text prints that number as reserved [@CL-SUP-0482].

## Company calibration

Confirm which ordering office document identifiers the contract requires on each invoice, and who checks that only written modifications are priced and billed. [@Q-021]
