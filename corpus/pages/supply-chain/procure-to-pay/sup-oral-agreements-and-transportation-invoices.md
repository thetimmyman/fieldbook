## Purpose

Explains which changes to a transportation contract must be in writing, and what a contractor's invoice must show so the charge can be matched to its order.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2320] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-27 and FAR 52.247-28 [@CL-SUP-2320; @CL-SUP-2321] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent [@CL-SUP-0481; @CL-SUP-0482] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.247-27: APR 1984 [@CL-SUP-9822]; FAR 52.247-28: APR 1984 [@CL-SUP-9823] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2320] |
| Applies to | The oral agreement clause in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2355]; the invoices clause in solicitations and contracts for drayage or other term contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2323] |

The oral agreement clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2355]. No oral statement of any person shall modify or otherwise affect the terms, conditions, or specifications stated in the contract, and all modifications to the contract must be made in writing by the contracting officer or an authorized representative [@CL-SUP-2320].

The invoices clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for drayage or other term contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2323]. The contractor shall submit itemized invoices as instructed by the agency ordering services under the contract [@CL-SUP-2321]. The contractor shall annotate each invoice with the contract number and other ordering office document identification [@CL-SUP-2322].

## How it works

Under the oral agreement clause, no oral statement of any person shall modify or otherwise affect the terms, conditions, or specifications stated in the contract, and all modifications to the contract must be made in writing by the contracting officer or an authorized representative [@CL-SUP-2320]. Under the invoices clause, the contractor shall submit itemized invoices as instructed by the agency ordering services under the contract [@CL-SUP-2321]. The contractor shall annotate each invoice with the contract number and other ordering office document identification [@CL-SUP-2322].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.247-27 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0481], and RFO FAR 52.247-28 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0482].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A carrier receives a telephone request from an agency employee for 10 extra pickups. The telephone request does not modify the contract's terms, because no oral statement of any person does. Only a written modification from the contracting officer or an authorized representative changes them. The carrier's invoice for the ordered pickups lists each pickup as an item, as the ordering agency instructed, and shows the contract number and the ordering office's document number. RFO FAR 52.247-27 and RFO FAR 52.247-28 are marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0481; @CL-SUP-0482].

## Never confuse

Under the invoices clause, the contractor shall annotate each invoice with the contract number and other ordering office document identification [@CL-SUP-2322], and shall submit itemized invoices as instructed by the agency ordering services under the contract [@CL-SUP-2321]. Under the oral agreement clause, no oral statement of any person shall modify or otherwise affect the terms, conditions, or specifications stated in the contract, and all modifications to the contract must be made in writing by the contracting officer or an authorized representative [@CL-SUP-2320]. Codified FAR 52.247-27 sets out the oral agreement clause [@CL-SUP-2320], unlike RFO FAR 52.247-27, which is marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0481]. Codified FAR 52.247-28 sets out the invoices clause [@CL-SUP-2321], while RFO FAR 52.247-28 is marked [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0482].

## Company calibration
