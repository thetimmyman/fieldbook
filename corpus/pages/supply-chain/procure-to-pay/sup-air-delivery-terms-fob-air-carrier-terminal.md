## Purpose

Shows where delivery happens under the two air terminal terms, what the contractor pays and provides at each, and which loss or damage is the contractor's responsibility.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2432] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-43 and FAR 52.247-44 [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2432] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent; the RFO text prints FAR 52.247-43 and FAR 52.247-44 each as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0016; @CL-SUP-0017] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2432] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when the delivery term is f.o.b. designated air carrier's terminal, point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2480], or point of importation [@CL-SUP-2484] |

The exportation-point clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when the delivery term is f.o.b. designated air carrier's terminal, point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2480]. The importation-point clause is inserted when the delivery term is the same terminal term at the point of importation [@CL-SUP-2484].

### Point of exportation

At the point of exportation the term means free of expense to the Government, loaded aboard the aircraft, or delivered to the custody of the air carrier if only the air carrier performs the loading, at the air carrier's terminal specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2428]. The contractor packs and marks the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepares it for air transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-2481].

The contractor delivers the shipment in good order and condition into the carrier's conveyance, or to the carrier's custody if only the carrier performs the loading, at the delivery point and on the date or within the period specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2429]. It pays and bears all applicable charges up to that point [@CL-SUP-2430] and provides a clean bill of lading and/or air waybill [@CL-SUP-2482]. It is responsible for any loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery of the goods to the point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2431]. At the Government's request and expense, it assists in obtaining the documents required for exportation [@CL-SUP-2483].

### Point of importation

At the point of importation the term means free of expense to the Government delivered to the air carrier's terminal at the point of importation specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2432]. The contractor packs and marks the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepares it for air transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods [@CL-SUP-2485]. It prepares and distributes bills of lading or air waybills [@CL-SUP-2486] and delivers the shipment in good order and condition to the delivery point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2487].

The contractor pays and bears all charges incurred up to the delivery point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2433]. The clause lists transportation costs, export, import or other fees or taxes, the cost of landing if any, customs duties, and the costs of certificates of origin, consular invoices or other documents that may be required for exportation or importation [@CL-SUP-2434]. The contractor is responsible for loss of or damage to the goods until delivery of the goods to the Government at the designated air carrier's terminal [@CL-SUP-2435].

## How it works

The delivery point sets what the contractor pays and answers for: the named terminal at the point of exportation under the first clause and the named terminal at the point of importation under the second [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2432]. The terminal, the delivery point and the packing specifications are contract-specific, and the exportation-point clause also refers to a date or period specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2429; @CL-SUP-2432; @CL-SUP-2481]. The exportation-point clause treats assistance with export documents as a service at the Government's request and expense [@CL-SUP-2483], whereas the importation-point clause puts document, duty and landing costs among the contractor's charges up to delivery [@CL-SUP-2434].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.247-43 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0016] and FAR 52.247-44 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0017], where the codified text prints the exportation-point clause [@CL-SUP-2428] and the importation-point clause [@CL-SUP-2432].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An air shipment priced at 50,000 moves on the exportation-point term. The contractor pays and bears 4,000 of charges up to delivery at the terminal named in the contract and provides an air waybill. The Government asks for help in obtaining export documents, and the contractor assists at the Government's expense. If the goods are damaged before delivery at the named terminal, the damage is the contractor's responsibility. On the importation-point term the same contractor would also pay 2,500 of customs duties and 500 of landing costs, and it would stay responsible for loss or damage until delivery to the Government at the designated terminal.

This example applies the codified clauses [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2432]; the RFO text prints those two section numbers as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0016; @CL-SUP-0017].

## Never confuse

The two terms deliver at different terminals. The exportation-point term delivers at the terminal named in the contract at the point of exportation and the contractor's charges run up to that point [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2430]. The importation-point term delivers at the terminal named at the point of importation, and the contractor's charges run up to the delivery point and include import fees, landing costs and duties [@CL-SUP-2432; @CL-SUP-2433; @CL-SUP-2434]. For the ocean terms, see [Export delivery terms](sup-export-delivery-terms-fas-fob-vessel-and-inland-carrier.md) and [Import delivery terms](sup-import-delivery-terms-cf-cif.md). The rules above come from the codified clauses at FAR 52.247-43 and 52.247-44 [@CL-SUP-2428; @CL-SUP-2432]; the RFO text prints those two section numbers as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0016; @CL-SUP-0017].

## Company calibration

Which air term each shipment carries and where the named terminal sits, because the delivery point fixes the charges and the loss responsibility. [@Q-004]
