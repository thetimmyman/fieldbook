## Purpose

Shows the two offeror duties that sit beside price in a transportation solicitation: learning the conditions of performance and being ready to prove financial standing.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2204] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-5 and FAR 52.247-6 [@CL-SUP-2204; @CL-SUP-2206] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.247-5 [@CL-SUP-0238; @CL-SUP-0239; @CL-SUP-0244]. No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.247-6: the RFO text lists it as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0245] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2204; @CL-SUP-2206] |
| Applies to | The familiarization clause in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services, to ensure that offerors become familiar with the conditions under which and where the services will be performed [@CL-SUP-2203]; the financial statement provision in solicitations for transportation or transportation-related services, to ensure that offerors are prepared to furnish financial statements [@CL-SUP-2250] |

The offeror must become familiar with all available information about the difficulties that may be encountered and the conditions, including safety precautions, under which the work must be accomplished under the contract [@CL-SUP-2204]. The offeror is not relieved of responsibility for properly estimating the difficulties and the cost of performance because it failed to investigate the conditions or to become acquainted with all information concerning the services to be performed [@CL-SUP-2205].

On request, the offeror must promptly furnish the Government a current certified statement of its financial condition and any data the Government requests about its operations [@CL-SUP-2206].

## How it works

The Government will use that information to determine the offeror's financial responsibility and ability to perform under the contract [@CL-SUP-2207]. An offeror's failure to comply with a request for information will subject its offer to possible rejection on responsibility grounds [@CL-SUP-2208]. The clause text does not say what form the request takes or how soon "promptly" is.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.247-5 keeps the familiarization rules described above [@CL-SUP-2203; @CL-SUP-2204; @CL-SUP-2205; @CL-SUP-0238; @CL-SUP-0239; @CL-SUP-0244]. The RFO text lists FAR 52.247-6, the financial statement provision, as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0245].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror prices a household goods move at 18,000 without reading the safety notes in the solicitation and without asking about access at the loading site. The crew later loses 6 hours on a narrow stairwell, which adds 900 of labor cost. Because the offeror did not become familiar with the conditions, the clause gives it no relief from its responsibility for estimating the difficulties and cost, so it gets no relief on the 900. When the Government asks for a current certified financial statement, the offeror sends it promptly. Had the offeror not complied with the request, its offer would have been subject to possible rejection on responsibility grounds. The financial statement part of this example follows the codified provision, not the RFO text, which lists FAR 52.247-6 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0245].

## Never confuse

Familiarization with conditions is a duty to learn, and failing to learn does not relieve the offeror of responsibility for its estimate [@CL-SUP-2205]. The financial statement is a separate request: the Government uses the information to determine financial responsibility and ability to perform [@CL-SUP-2206; @CL-SUP-2207]. The codified text prints a provision on furnishing a current certified statement of financial condition [@CL-SUP-2206], while the RFO text lists FAR 52.247-6 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0245].

## Company calibration

Confirm that the company can produce a current certified financial statement on request and records the conditions it reviewed for each bid. [@Q-004]
