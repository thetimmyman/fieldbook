## Purpose

Explains where the Government delivers Government-furnished property and where the contractor returns it, and how returnable cylinders are loaned, charged and replaced.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2051] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-55 and 52.247-66 [@CL-SUP-2051; @CL-SUP-2056] |
| RFO citation | RFO part 52 lists 52.247-55 and 52.247-66 as reserved [@CL-SUP-0388; @CL-SUP-0389] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2051] |
| Applies to | Government-furnished property for use within the contiguous United States or Canada, or at a contractor plant outside them [@CL-SUP-2051; @CL-SUP-2053], and returnable cylinders, meaning pressure vessels designed for pressures higher than 40 psia with a circular cross section, excluding the tank types the clause names [@CL-SUP-2181] |

Unless the solicitation provides otherwise, the Government delivers Government-furnished property for use within the contiguous United States or Canada to a point the contractor specifies in the offer [@CL-SUP-2051]. If the Government makes delivery by railroad, the f.o.b. point is private siding at the contractor's plant [@CL-SUP-2173]; if the plant is not served by rail, it is railroad cars in the same or nearest city having rail service [@CL-SUP-2174]. The Government may choose the mode of transportation and the carriers, and bears the cost of all line-haul transportation to the specified destination [@CL-SUP-2052]. Returnable cylinders remain the contractor's property and are loaned without charge to the Government for the loan period the contract states [@CL-SUP-2056], which runs following the day of delivery to the f.o.b. point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2177].

The FAR's general definitions say the contiguous United States (CONUS) means the 48 contiguous States and the District of Columbia [@CL-SUP-9543]. The RFO model text of FAR 2.101 defines the Contiguous United States (CONUS) in the same words as the codified definition [@CL-SUP-9593; @CL-SUP-9543].

## How it works

Where the destination is a contractor's plant outside the contiguous United States or Canada, the f.o.b. point is a contractor-specified location in the contiguous United States [@CL-SUP-2053]. If the contractor fails to name a point, the Government selects the port city in the contiguous United States nearest to the property that has regular commercial water transportation services to the offshore port nearest the plant [@CL-SUP-2175]. Unless the contracting officer directs otherwise or the contract provides otherwise, the contractor returns all Government-furnished equipment, supplies and property [@CL-SUP-2055], including property not returned in the form of acceptable end items, to the point at which the Government property was originally furnished to the contractor under the contract [@CL-SUP-2054]. Although the Government may have furnished the property at the contractor's plant, the contracting officer may direct the contractor to deliver the returned property to, and load, block and brace it in, railway cars in the city in which the contractor's plant is located [@CL-SUP-9708; @CL-SUP-2116], or, if that city is not served by rail service, in the nearest city having rail service [@CL-SUP-2190]. Unless the contract specifies otherwise, returned property is packed in containers conforming with the rules of common carrier published tariffs so as to be free of penalty charges by the carrier designated for shipment by the Government [@CL-SUP-2176].

A cylinder not returned within the loan period draws a daily rental from the first day after the loan period expires [@CL-SUP-2057]. The rental runs to and including the day the cylinders are delivered to the contractor where the original delivery was f.o.b. origin, or are delivered or made available for delivery to the contractor's designated carrier where the original delivery was f.o.b. destination [@CL-SUP-2178]. The Government pays the per-cylinder, per-day rental the contracting officer inserts, computed separately for cylinders by type, size and capacity and for each point of delivery named in the contract [@CL-SUP-2058]. No rental accrues to the contractor above the replacement value per cylinder specified in the clause [@CL-SUP-2059]. For a cylinder lost or damaged beyond repair while in the Government's possession, the Government pays the contractor the replacement value, less the allocable rental paid for that cylinder [@CL-SUP-2060], and the cylinders paid for in this way become Government property [@CL-SUP-2179]. If a lost cylinder is located within the number of calendar days the contracting officer inserts after payment by the Government [@CL-SUP-2117], the Government may return it and the contractor pays the Government the replacement value less rental computed under paragraph (b) [@CL-SUP-2180], with the rental running from the expiration of the loan period to the date on which the cylinder was delivered to the contractor [@CL-SUP-2118].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO part 52 text lists section 52.247-55, the delivery point clause, as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0388], and section 52.247-66, the returnable cylinders clause, as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0389]. The rules described above come from the codified clause text [@CL-SUP-2051; @CL-SUP-2056].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The Government furnishes property to a contractor whose plant is served by rail, and the solicitation does not provide otherwise. If the Government ships by rail, the f.o.b. point is private siding at the contractor's plant, and the Government bears the line-haul cost. A second contractor has its plant in Hawaii and names no delivery point in its offer. The plant is outside the contiguous United States, so the Government selects the port city in the contiguous United States nearest the property that has regular commercial water transportation to the offshore port nearest the plant. When the property comes back, the contractor returns it to the point at which it was originally furnished unless the contracting officer directs otherwise or the contract provides otherwise. The example applies the codified clause text; RFO part 52 lists 52.247-55 and 52.247-66 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0388; @CL-SUP-0389].

A contractor loans cylinders to the Government on a 30-day loan period at a rental of 5 per cylinder per day and a replacement value of 300 per cylinder, with original delivery f.o.b. origin. One cylinder is delivered back to the contractor on day 45 after delivery to the f.o.b. point, so rental runs from day 31 through day 45, which is 15 days, or 75. Another cylinder stays out until day 100, so 70 days of rental would be 350, but no rental accrues above the replacement value, so rental stops at 300. A third cylinder is lost after 10 days of rental, so rental paid is 50; the Government pays 300 less 50, which is 250, and the cylinder becomes Government property. Suppose the inserted number of days has not run and that lost cylinder is then located and delivered back to the contractor on day 50 after delivery to the f.o.b. point. The Government may return it, and rental from day 31 through day 50 is 20 days, or 100, so the contractor pays the Government 300 less 100, which is 200.

## Never confuse

A returnable cylinder is not Government-furnished property. It remains the contractor's property and is loaned to the Government [@CL-SUP-2056], and it becomes Government property when the Government pays for it as lost or damaged beyond repair [@CL-SUP-2179]. The f.o.b. point for delivery of Government-furnished property does not set the return point: property returns to the point at which it was originally furnished unless the contracting officer directs otherwise or the contract provides otherwise [@CL-SUP-2054; @CL-SUP-2055]. The RFO part 52 text lists 52.247-55 and 52.247-66 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0388; @CL-SUP-0389]; the codified clauses set the delivery point and the cylinder loan terms [@CL-SUP-2051; @CL-SUP-2056].
