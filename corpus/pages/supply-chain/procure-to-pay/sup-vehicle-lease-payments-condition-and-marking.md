## Purpose

Shows what the Government pays for a leased vehicle, when rent stops, what condition the vehicle must be in, what the Government can do about a defective vehicle, and how a leased vehicle may be marked.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2929] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.208-4, FAR 52.208-5 and FAR 52.208-6 [@CL-SUP-2929; @CL-SUP-2934; @CL-SUP-2936] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.208-4, 52.208-5 and 52.208-6 [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-2830; @CL-SUP-2901; @CL-SUP-2902] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2929] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for leasing motor vehicles, unless the vehicles are leased in foreign countries, under each of the three clauses [@CL-SUP-2937; @CL-SUP-2978; @CL-SUP-2979] |

The lease payment clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for leasing motor vehicles, unless the vehicles are leased in foreign countries [@CL-SUP-2937]. Upon the submission of proper invoices or vouchers, the Government pays rent for each vehicle at the rate or rates specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2929]. Rent accrues from the beginning of the contract or from the date each vehicle is delivered to the Government, whichever is later, and continues until the contract term expires or the contract is terminated [@CL-SUP-2930]. However, rent accrues only for the period that each vehicle is in the possession of the Government [@CL-SUP-2931].

Rent does not accrue for a vehicle that the Contracting Officer determines does not comply with the Condition of Leased Vehicles clause or otherwise does not comply with the requirements of the contract, until the vehicle is replaced or the defects are corrected [@CL-SUP-2932]. Rent does not accrue for a vehicle during any period when it is unavailable or unusable as a result of the contractor's failure to render the services for operation and maintenance that the contract prescribes [@CL-SUP-2933]. Rent stated in monthly terms is prorated on the basis of 1/30th of the monthly rate for each day the vehicle is in the Government's possession [@CL-SUP-2976]. If the contract contains a mileage provision, the Government pays rent as provided in the Schedule [@CL-SUP-2977].

The condition clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for leasing motor vehicles, unless the vehicles are leased in foreign countries [@CL-SUP-2978]. Each vehicle furnished under the contract must be of good quality and in safe operating condition, and must comply with the Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards and the State safety regulations applicable to the vehicle [@CL-SUP-2934]. The Government accepts or rejects the vehicles promptly after receipt [@CL-SUP-2980]. If the Contracting Officer determines that a vehicle furnished does not comply with the contract, the Contracting Officer promptly informs the contractor in writing [@CL-SUP-2981]. If the contractor then fails to replace the vehicle or correct the defects as the Contracting Officer requires, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, correct the defect or arrange for the lease of a similar vehicle [@CL-SUP-2935]. The Government charges or sets off against the contractor any excess costs occasioned by that correction or lease [@CL-SUP-3006]. In the same circumstance, as an alternative, the Government may terminate the contract under the Default clause of the contract [@CL-SUP-2982].

The marking clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for leasing motor vehicles, unless the vehicles are leased in foreign countries [@CL-SUP-2979]. The Government may place nonpermanent markings or decals, identifying the using agency, on each side and on the front and rear bumpers of any motor vehicle leased under the contract, and it must use markings or decals that are removable without damage to the vehicle [@CL-SUP-2936]. The contractor may use placards for temporary identification of vehicles, except that the placards may not contain any references to the contractor that may be construed as advertising or endorsement by the Government of the contractor [@CL-SUP-2983].

## How it works

Rent accrues from the beginning of the contract or from the date each vehicle is delivered to the Government, whichever is later, and continues until the expiration of the contract term or the termination of the contract; however, rent accrues only for the period that each vehicle is in the possession of the Government, and rent does not accrue for any vehicle that the Contracting Officer determines does not comply with the Condition of Leased Vehicles clause or otherwise does not comply with the requirements of the contract, until the vehicle is replaced or the defects are corrected [@CL-SUP-2930; @CL-SUP-2931; @CL-SUP-2932].

A vehicle that is unavailable or unusable because the contractor failed to render the prescribed operation and maintenance services earns no rent for that period [@CL-SUP-2933].

Where the contract states rent monthly, each day of Government possession earns 1/30th of the monthly rate, unless a mileage provision makes the Schedule govern the payment [@CL-SUP-2976; @CL-SUP-2977].

Under the condition clause, the Government accepts or rejects the vehicles promptly after receipt; if the Contracting Officer determines that any vehicle furnished is not in compliance with the contract, the Contracting Officer promptly informs the contractor in writing; and if the contractor fails to replace the vehicle or correct the defects as required by the Contracting Officer, the Government may choose one of the two alternatives in paragraph (c): either by contract or otherwise correct the defect or arrange for the lease of a similar vehicle and charge or set off against the contractor any excess costs occasioned thereby, or terminate the contract under the Default clause of the contract [@CL-SUP-2980; @CL-SUP-2981; @CL-SUP-2935; @CL-SUP-3006; @CL-SUP-2982].

The Government may place nonpermanent markings or decals, identifying the using agency, on each side and on the front and rear bumpers of any motor vehicle leased under the contract, using markings or decals that are removable without damage to the vehicle, and the contractor may use placards for temporary identification of vehicles except that the placards may not contain any references to the contractor that may be construed as advertising or endorsement by the Government of the contractor [@CL-SUP-2936; @CL-SUP-2983].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.208-4 [@CL-SUP-2830], RFO FAR 52.208-5 [@CL-SUP-2901], and RFO FAR 52.208-6 [@CL-SUP-2902] are marked [Reserved], whereas codified FAR 52.208-4 sets out the Vehicle Lease Payments clause [@CL-SUP-2929], codified FAR 52.208-5 sets out the Condition of Leased Vehicles clause [@CL-SUP-2934], and codified FAR 52.208-6 sets out the Marking of Leased Vehicles clause [@CL-SUP-2936].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Four vans are leased in the United States at 900 a month each, and the contract has no mileage provision. Take a 30-day month. Van A is delivered with 12 days of the month left, so rent is 12 times 1/30th of 900, which is 360. Van B arrives with bald tires, the Contracting Officer determines it does not comply and informs the contractor in writing, and the contractor does not replace the tires that month, so no rent accrues for it. Because the contractor does not correct the defect, the Government arranges the lease of a similar van and the excess cost of 1,200 is set off against the contractor's invoice. Van C is unusable for 5 days because the contractor failed to render the maintenance services the contract prescribes, so rent for those 5 days is not owed: 900 less 5 times 30 is 750. Van D is in service all month at 900. The rent due for the month, before the 1,200 set-off, is 360 plus 0 plus 750 plus 900, which is 2,010.

## Never confuse

Rent does not accrue for any vehicle that the Contracting Officer determines does not comply with the Condition of Leased Vehicles clause or otherwise does not comply with the requirements of the contract, until the vehicle is replaced or the defects are corrected, and rent does not accrue for any vehicle during any period when the vehicle is unavailable or unusable as a result of the contractor's failure to render services for the operation and maintenance of the vehicle as prescribed by the contract [@CL-SUP-2932; @CL-SUP-2933]. If the contractor fails to replace the vehicle or correct the defects as required by the Contracting Officer, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, correct the defect or arrange for the lease of a similar vehicle [@CL-SUP-2935]. Insurance for leased vehicles is a separate question, covered on [Leased vehicles, cargo and public liability insurance](../../contract-finance/financing/ctf-leased-vehicles-cargo-and-public-liability-insurance.md).

The clauses on tagging of leased vehicles, fleet vehicles and Government supply sources are covered in [Tagging of leased vehicles, fleet vehicles and Government supply sources](sup-tagging-of-leased-vehicles-and-government-supply-sources.md).
