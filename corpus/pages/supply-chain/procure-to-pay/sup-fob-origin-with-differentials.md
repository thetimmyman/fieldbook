## Purpose

Sets out how an f.o.b. origin delivery term works with priced differentials for mode, vehicle type or delivery place, what the contractor does at loading, what its bill of lading shows, and what loss or damage it answers for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2324] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-33 [@CL-SUP-2324] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.247-33 [@CL-SUP-0343; @CL-SUP-0360] |
| Edition date | Feb 2006 [@CL-SUP-0343] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2324] |
| Applies to | Contracts containing the F.o.b. Origin, With Differentials clause [@CL-SUP-2324] |

F.o.b. origin, with differentials, means delivery free of expense to the Government in one of these alternative ways: on board the indicated type of conveyance of the carrier (or of the Government, if specified) at a designated point in the city, county and State from which the shipments will be made [@CL-SUP-2324], a point from which line-haul transportation service (as distinguished from switching, local drayage or other terminal service) will begin [@CL-SUP-2356]. Another alternative is delivery to, and placement on, the carrier's wharf (at shipside, within reach of the ship's loading tackle, when the shipping point is within a port area having water transportation service) or the carrier's freight station [@CL-SUP-2357]. Another is delivery to a United States Postal Service facility [@CL-SUP-2358; @CL-SUP-9707]. If stated in the solicitation, a further alternative is delivery to any Government-designated point located within the same city or commercial zone as the f.o.b. origin point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-9707; @CL-SUP-2359].

Differentials for mode of transportation, type of vehicle or place of delivery, as indicated in the contractor's offer, may be added to the contract price [@CL-SUP-2325]. They must be expressed as a rate in cents for each 100 pounds of the supplies, for one or more of the options under the clause that the Government may specify at the time of shipment [@CL-SUP-2326]. Paragraph (c)(4) concerns the offeror's differentials in cents for each 100 pounds for optional mode of transportation, types of vehicle, transportation within a mode, or place of delivery [@CL-SUP-2371], specified by the Government at the time of shipment and not included in the f.o.b. origin price indicated in the Schedule by the offeror [@CL-SUP-2371].

If, at the time of shipment, the Government specifies a mode of transportation, type of vehicle or place of delivery for which the offeror set a differential, the contractor must include the total of those differential costs [@CL-SUP-2328]. That total is the applicable differential multiplied by the actual weight, and the contractor must include it as a separate reimbursable item on its invoice for the supplies [@CL-SUP-2372]. The Government has the option of performing or arranging, at its own expense, any transportation from the contractor's shipping plant or point to the carrier's facility at the time of shipment, and whenever it exercises that option it must make no reimbursement based on a quoted differential [@CL-SUP-2329].

Paragraph (b) says the contractor shall do the items that follow [@CL-SUP-3878]. They include packing and marking the shipment to comply with contract specification or, in the absence of specifications, preparing it in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-2360]. The contractor must order specified carrier equipment when the Government requests it or, if none is specified, order appropriate carrier equipment not in excess of capacity to accommodate the shipment [@CL-SUP-2361]. It must deliver the shipment in good order and condition to the carrier and, when it loads a carload or truckload shipment, load, stow, trim, block and/or brace it on or in the carrier's conveyance as carrier rules and regulations require [@CL-SUP-2332]. It must distribute the copies of the bill of lading, or other transportation receipts, as the ordering agency directs [@CL-SUP-2367].

The contractor must complete the Government bill of lading supplied by the ordering agency or, when none is supplied, prepares a commercial bill of lading or other transportation receipt [@CL-SUP-2362]. The bill of lading shall show a description of the shipment in terms of the governing freight classification or tariff (or Government rate tender) under which lowest freight rates are applicable, and the seals affixed to the conveyance with their serial numbers or other identification [@CL-SUP-3879; @CL-SUP-2363]. It shall also show the lengths and capacities of cars or trucks ordered and furnished, and other pertinent information required to effect prompt delivery to the consignee, including name, delivery address, postal address and ZIP code of consignee, routing, etc. [@CL-SUP-2364]. It shall show special instructions or annotations requested by the ordering agency, for example that the shipment is the property of the Government and that the Government will reimburse the freight charges paid to the carrier [@CL-SUP-2365], and the signature of the carrier's agent and the date the shipment is received by the carrier [@CL-SUP-2366].

The contractor must be responsible for any loss of or damage to the goods that occurs before delivery to the carrier and any that results from improper packing and marking [@CL-SUP-3878; @CL-SUP-2330]. It must also be responsible for any that results from improper loading, stowing, trimming, blocking, and/or bracing of the shipment, if the contractor loaded it on or in the carrier's conveyance [@CL-SUP-2331].

## How it works

The clause says it may be advantageous to the offeror to submit f.o.b. origin prices that include only the lowest cost to the contractor for loading at its plant or most favorable shipping point [@CL-SUP-2368]. The cost beyond that point of bringing the supplies to the place of delivery, and of loading, blocking and bracing on the vehicle the Government specifies at shipment, may exceed that lowest cost when the offeror ships for its own account [@CL-SUP-2369]. Accordingly, the offeror may indicate differentials that may be added to the offered price [@CL-SUP-2370]. The Government considers the differentials when it evaluates offers, to determine the lowest overall cost to the Government [@CL-SUP-2327]. At shipment, when the Government specifies a mode of transportation, type of vehicle or place of delivery for which the offeror set a differential, the contractor must include the total of those differential costs as a separate reimbursable item on its invoice for the supplies [@CL-SUP-2328; @CL-SUP-2372]. Whenever the Government exercises its option of performing or arranging, at its own expense, transportation from the contractor's shipping plant or point to the carrier's facility, it must make no reimbursement based on a quoted differential [@CL-SUP-2329].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO paragraph (a) covers delivery, free of expense to the Government, in one of several alternative ways, the first being on board the indicated type of conveyance of the carrier (or of the Government, if specified) at a designated point in the city, county and State from which the shipments will be made [@CL-SUP-0343], a point from which line-haul transportation service (as distinguished from switching, local drayage or other terminal service) will begin [@CL-SUP-0344]. The other alternatives are delivery to and placement on the carrier's wharf (at shipside, within reach of the ship's loading tackle, when the shipping point is within a port area having water transportation service) or the carrier's freight station, or delivery to a United States Postal Service facility [@CL-SUP-0345], or, if stated in the solicitation, delivery to any Government-designated point located within the same city or commercial zone as the f.o.b. origin point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-0346]. RFO paragraph (a)(2) says that differentials for mode of transportation, type of vehicle or place of delivery, as indicated in the contractor's offer, may be added to the contract price [@CL-SUP-0347]. RFO paragraph (b) covers packing and marking the shipment to comply with contract specification or, in the absence of specifications, preparing it in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-0348], ordering specified carrier equipment when requested by the Government or, if not specified, appropriate carrier equipment not in excess of capacity to accommodate the shipment [@CL-SUP-0349], and delivering the shipment in good order and condition to the carrier and loading, stowing, trimming, blocking, and/or bracing a carload or truckload shipment (when loaded by the Contractor) on or in the carrier's conveyance as required by carrier rules and regulations [@CL-SUP-0350]. Its paragraph (b)(4) covers responsibility for any loss of and/or damage to the goods occurring before delivery to the carrier, resulting from improper packing and marking [@CL-SUP-0351], or resulting from improper loading, stowing, trimming, blocking, and/or bracing of the shipment, if loaded by the Contractor on or in the carrier's conveyance [@CL-SUP-0352]. Its paragraph (b)(5) covers completing the Government bill of lading supplied by the ordering agency or, when none is supplied, preparing a commercial bill of lading or other transportation receipt [@CL-SUP-0353]. Its listed items are a description of the shipment in terms of the governing freight classification or tariff (or Government rate tender) under which lowest freight rates are applicable, and the seals affixed to the conveyance with their serial numbers or other identification [@CL-SUP-0354]; the lengths and capacities of cars or trucks ordered and furnished, and other pertinent information required to effect prompt delivery to the consignee [@CL-SUP-0355]; special instructions or annotations requested by the ordering agency [@CL-SUP-0356]; and the signature of the carrier's agent and the date the shipment is received by the carrier, followed by distribution of the copies of the bill of lading, or other transportation receipts, as directed by the ordering agency [@CL-SUP-0357]. It may be advantageous to the offeror to submit f.o.b. origin prices that include only the lowest cost to the Contractor for loading of shipment at the Contractor's plant or most favorable shipping point [@CL-SUP-0358]. RFO paragraph (c)(1) adds that the cost beyond that plant or point of bringing the supplies to the place of delivery, and the cost of loading, blocking and bracing on the type vehicle specified by the Government at the time of shipment, may exceed the offeror's lowest cost when the offeror ships for the offeror's account [@CL-SUP-0359]. It says the offeror may indicate differentials that may be added to the offered price, expressed as a rate in cents for each 100 pounds of the supplies for one or more of the options under the clause that the Government may specify at the time of shipment [@CL-SUP-0360]. Under RFO paragraph (c)(2), the differentials will be considered in the evaluation of offers to determine the lowest overall cost to the Government [@CL-SUP-0361]. If, at the time of shipment, the Government specifies a mode of transportation, type of vehicle or place of delivery for which the offeror has set forth a differential, the contractor shall include the total of such differential costs [@CL-SUP-0362], the applicable differential multiplied by the actual weight, as a separate reimbursable item on the contractor's invoice for the supplies [@CL-SUP-0363]. Under RFO paragraph (c)(3), the Government shall have the option of performing or arranging at its own expense any transportation from the contractor's shipping plant or point to the carrier's facility at the time of shipment [@CL-SUP-0364], and, whenever this option is exercised, shall make no reimbursement based on a quoted differential [@CL-SUP-0365]. RFO paragraph (c)(4) concerns the offeror's differentials in cents for each 100 pounds for optional mode of transportation, types of vehicle, transportation within a mode, or place of delivery [@CL-SUP-0366], specified by the Government at the time of shipment and not included in the f.o.b. origin price indicated in the Schedule by the offeror [@CL-SUP-0366], followed by blanks with the examples carload, truckload, less-load, wharf, flatcar, driveaway, etc. [@CL-SUP-0367].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offer prices 20,000 pounds of supplies at 10,000 dollars f.o.b. origin, with a differential of 300 cents for each 100 pounds for flatbed truckload. The Government considers that differential when it evaluates offers. At shipment the Government specifies a flatbed, and the actual weight is 20,000 pounds, which is 200 units of 100 pounds. The differential cost is 200 times 300 cents, or 600 dollars, which the contractor shows as a separate reimbursable item on the invoice next to the 10,000 dollars for the supplies. Had the Government instead arranged the haul from the plant to the carrier's facility at its own expense, it would reimburse nothing based on the quoted differential.

On the same shipment, if the contractor loads the truck itself and poor bracing lets the load shift and break, the loss results from improper bracing by the contractor, and the contractor is responsible. A pallet the contractor's forklift drops in the plant yard before the carrier receives it is also the contractor's responsibility.

## Never confuse

A differential is indicated in the contractor's offer [@CL-SUP-2325], whereas when the Government specifies at shipment a mode of transportation, type of vehicle or place of delivery for which the offeror set a differential, the contractor must include the total of the differential costs as a separate reimbursable item on its invoice rather than absorbing them in the base price [@CL-SUP-2328; @CL-SUP-2372]. The f.o.b. origin variants (contractor's facility, freight allowed and freight prepaid) and plain f.o.b. origin are covered on their own cards.
