## Purpose

Shows how a change order grants time for a contract change and how that extension interacts with the completion dates and the liquidated damages computation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2575] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.211-13 [@CL-SUP-2575] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.211-13 [@CL-SUP-0635; @CL-SUP-0636; @CL-SUP-0637] |
| Edition date | SEP 2000 [@CL-SUP-9833] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2575] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Time extensions for contract changes depend upon the extent, if any, by which the changes cause delay in the completion of the various elements of construction [@CL-SUP-2575].

The change order granting the time extension may provide that the contract completion date is extended only for those specific elements related to the changed work, and that the remaining completion dates for all other portions of the work are not altered [@CL-SUP-2576]. The change order may also provide an equitable readjustment of liquidated damages under the new completion schedule [@CL-SUP-2577].

## How it works

Time extensions for contract changes will depend upon the extent, if any, by which the changes cause delay in the completion of the various elements of construction [@CL-SUP-2575]. The change order granting the time extension may provide that the contract completion date will be extended only for those specific elements related to the changed work and that the remaining contract completion dates for all other portions of the work will not be altered [@CL-SUP-2576]. The change order also may provide an equitable readjustment of liquidated damages under the new completion schedule [@CL-SUP-2577].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.211-13 keeps the rule that time extensions for contract changes depend upon the extent, if any, by which the changes cause delay in the completion of the various elements of construction [@CL-SUP-0635; @CL-SUP-2575]. It keeps the change order's option to extend the completion date only for the specific elements related to the changed work, leaving the remaining completion dates for all other portions of the work unaltered [@CL-SUP-0636; @CL-SUP-2576], and its option to provide an equitable readjustment of liquidated damages under the new completion schedule [@CL-SUP-0637; @CL-SUP-2577].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A change order adds work to one element of a construction contract and delays that element by 12 days, while the other elements are not delayed. The change order provides that the completion date is extended by 12 days for that element only and that the other completion dates are not altered, and it provides an equitable readjustment of liquidated damages under the new completion schedule. The clause permits each of these provisions but does not require them.

## Never confuse

Time extensions for contract changes will depend upon the extent, if any, by which the changes cause delay in the completion of the various elements of construction [@CL-SUP-2575], and the change order granting the time extension may provide that the contract completion date will be extended only for those specific elements related to the changed work rather than altering the remaining contract completion dates for all other portions of the work [@CL-SUP-2576].
