## Purpose

Explains how a transportation solicitation uses estimated shipment quantities or weights to evaluate offers, and what responsibility, insurance and indemnity the contractor carries for personal injury and property damage while performing.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2301] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-20 and FAR 52.247-21 [@CL-SUP-2301; @CL-SUP-2302] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.247-21 [@CL-SUP-0198; @CL-SUP-0199; @CL-SUP-0200; @CL-SUP-0210; @CL-SUP-0211; @CL-SUP-0212]. No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.247-20: the RFO text lists it as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0197] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.247-20: APR 1984 [@CL-SUP-9809]; FAR 52.247-21: Apr 1984 [@CL-SUP-0198] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2301] |
| Applies to | The estimates provision in solicitations for transportation or transportation-related services when quantities or weights between each origin and destination are not known [@CL-SUP-2351]; the liability clause in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-2302] |

The estimates provision is inserted in solicitations for transportation or transportation-related services when quantities or weights of shipments between each origin and destination are not known, and it states an estimated quantity or weight for each origin and destination pair [@CL-SUP-2351]. For the purpose of evaluating offers, and for no other purpose, those estimated quantities or weights are considered the quantities or weights to be shipped between each origin and destination listed [@CL-SUP-2301].

The contractor assumes responsibility for all damage or injury to persons or property occasioned through the use, maintenance and operation of its vehicles or other equipment by, or the action of, the contractor or its employees and agents [@CL-SUP-2303]. At its own expense, the contractor must maintain adequate public liability and property damage insurance during the continuance of the contract, insuring it against all claims for injury or damage [@CL-SUP-2304]. It must also maintain workers' compensation and other legally required insurance for its own employees and agents [@CL-SUP-2305].

The Government is in no event liable or responsible for damage or injury to any person or property occasioned through the use, maintenance or operation of any vehicle or other equipment by, or the action of, the contractor or its employees and agents in performing under the contract [@CL-SUP-2306]. The Government is also to be indemnified and saved harmless against claims for damage or injury occasioned in that way [@CL-SUP-2307].

## How it works

The solicitation states an estimated quantity or weight for each origin and destination pair, and evaluation treats those figures as the quantities or weights to be shipped [@CL-SUP-2351; @CL-SUP-2301]. The estimates are considered the quantities or weights to be shipped for the purpose of evaluating offers, and for no other purpose [@CL-SUP-2301]. For liability, the contractor assumes responsibility for damage or injury occasioned through the use, maintenance and operation of its vehicles or other equipment by, or the action of, the contractor or its employees and agents [@CL-SUP-2303], maintains adequate public liability and property damage insurance insuring it against all claims for injury or damage [@CL-SUP-2304], and maintains workers' compensation and other legally required insurance with respect to its own employees and agents [@CL-SUP-2305]. The Government shall in no event be liable or responsible for that damage or injury, and the Government shall be indemnified and saved harmless against claims for damage or injury in such cases [@CL-SUP-2306; @CL-SUP-2307].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text lists FAR 52.247-20, the estimates provision, as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0197]. RFO FAR 52.247-21 is inserted in solicitations and contracts for transportation or for transportation-related services [@CL-SUP-0198]. Under it, the Contractor assumes responsibility for all damage or injury to persons or property occasioned through the use, maintenance, and operation of the Contractor's vehicles or other equipment by, or the action of, the Contractor or the Contractor's employees and agents [@CL-SUP-0199]. The Contractor, at its own expense, shall maintain adequate public liability and property damage insurance during the continuance of the contract, insuring the Contractor against all claims for injury or damage [@CL-SUP-0200], and shall maintain Workers' Compensation and other legally required insurance with respect to its own employees and agents [@CL-SUP-0210]. The Government shall in no event be liable or responsible for damage or injury to any person or property occasioned through the use, maintenance, or operation of any vehicle or other equipment by, or the action of, the Contractor or the Contractor's employees and agents in performing under the contract, and the Government shall be indemnified and saved harmless against claims for damage or injury in such cases [@CL-SUP-0211; @CL-SUP-0212].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation lists two lanes with estimated weights of 40,000 pounds and 10,000 pounds. An offeror quotes 2 dollars per 100 pounds on both lanes. Evaluation applies the estimates, so the offer is scored on 50,000 pounds in total, which is 500 hundredweight, and its evaluated price is 1,000 dollars. The estimates serve evaluation only and no other purpose. This part of the example follows the codified estimates provision, not the RFO text, which lists FAR 52.247-20 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0197].

Later, while performing, a contractor's truck backs into a customer's fence and the repair costs 3,000 dollars. The contractor is responsible for that damage because it arose from the use of its vehicle by its employee. The Government is not liable or responsible, and the contractor holds the insurance the clause requires, including workers' compensation for its own driver.

## Never confuse

The estimates provision considers the estimated quantities or weights as the quantities or weights to be shipped for the purpose of evaluating offers, and for no other purpose [@CL-SUP-2301]. Under FAR 52.247-21 the Contractor assumes responsibility for damage or injury to persons or property occasioned through the use, maintenance and operation of its vehicles or other equipment [@CL-SUP-2303]. Under FAR 52.247-22 the Contractor shall assume full liability for any and all goods lost and/or damaged in the movement covered by the contract, except when loss and/or damage arises out of causes beyond the control and without the fault or negligence of the Contractor [@CL-SUP-2308]; see [Freight loss and damage liability](sup-freight-loss-and-damage-liability.md). The codified text prints an estimates provision at FAR 52.247-20 that confines the estimates to evaluating offers [@CL-SUP-2301], unlike the RFO text, which lists that number as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0197].

## Company calibration

Confirm which awards carry the estimates provision and the liability clause, whether the contractor's insurance meets the clause, and where the estimates stated in each solicitation are kept. [@Q-004]
