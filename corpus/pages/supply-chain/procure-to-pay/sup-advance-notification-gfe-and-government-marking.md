## Purpose

Shows what notice the Government gives before a shipment, what equipment it furnishes to help load or unload, and how an agency marks property and directs placement during an office relocation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-2314] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.247-24, FAR 52.247-25 and FAR 52.247-26 [@CL-SUP-2314; @CL-SUP-2317; @CL-SUP-2318] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent; the RFO text prints FAR 52.247-24, FAR 52.247-25 and FAR 52.247-26 each as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0006; @CL-SUP-0007; @CL-SUP-0008] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-2314] |
| Applies to | The advance notification clause when the Government is responsible for notifying the contractor of specific service times or unusual shipments [@CL-SUP-2316]; the equipment clause when the Government furnishes equipment with or without operators [@CL-SUP-2353]; the direction and marking clause when office relocations are involved [@CL-SUP-2354] |

The advance notification clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services when the Government is responsible for notifying the contractor of specific service times or unusual shipments [@CL-SUP-2316]. The Government notifies the contractor a stated number of hours in advance of the number of pieces and weight of all normal shipments and of the time the shipment will be available for pickup [@CL-SUP-2314]. The number of hours is a blank in the clause and is specific to each contract [@CL-SUP-2314]. For other-than-normal shipments the Government furnishes additional information, such as the dimensions of oversized pieces, as necessary to determine the amount of equipment and/or manpower needed to perform the required services [@CL-SUP-2315].

The equipment clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services when the Government furnishes equipment with or without operators [@CL-SUP-2353]. The Government provides the equipment named in the clause, with or without operators, at the origin, destination or both, to assist in loading, unloading or both, when required [@CL-SUP-2317]. The equipment, the choice of with or without operators, the location and the task are bracketed fill-ins that are specific to each contract [@CL-SUP-2317].

The direction and marking clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services when office relocations are involved [@CL-SUP-2354]. The agency being relocated tags or marks property, showing the floor, room number and the location where the property is to be placed in the new building [@CL-SUP-2318]. The agency provides sufficient personnel to direct the contractor's personnel in the placement of the property at destination [@CL-SUP-2319].

## How it works

The three clauses divide the work between the Government and the contractor. The Government gives notice of the pieces, weight and pickup time of normal shipments [@CL-SUP-2314]. For other-than-normal shipments it adds information so that the equipment and manpower needed can be determined [@CL-SUP-2315]. Where the equipment clause applies, the Government provides the named equipment to assist in loading or unloading when required [@CL-SUP-2317]. In an office relocation the agency being relocated marks the property and supplies the people who direct placement [@CL-SUP-2318; @CL-SUP-2319]. Each clause is inserted when its own condition is met [@CL-SUP-2316; @CL-SUP-2353; @CL-SUP-2354].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.247-24 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0006], FAR 52.247-25 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0007] and FAR 52.247-26 as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0008], where the codified text prints the advance notification clause [@CL-SUP-2314], the equipment clause [@CL-SUP-2317] and the direction and marking clause [@CL-SUP-2318].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An office relocation has 12 normal shipments and one oversized piece. The notification clause reads 48 hours in its blank, so the Government gives the contractor the number of pieces and weight of the 12 normal shipments and their pickup time 48 hours in advance. For the oversized piece it also gives the dimensions, so the amount of equipment and manpower needed can be determined. The equipment clause names one forklift with operator at destination to assist in unloading. The agency tags each item with floor 3, room 305 and the location in the new building, and provides staff to direct the contractor's crew in placing the items.

This example applies the codified clauses [@CL-SUP-2314; @CL-SUP-2317; @CL-SUP-2318]; the RFO text prints those three section numbers as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0006; @CL-SUP-0007; @CL-SUP-0008].

## Never confuse

Advance notification is the Government giving information about shipments [@CL-SUP-2314; @CL-SUP-2315]. Furnishing equipment is a separate duty that arises under the equipment clause, to assist in loading or unloading when required [@CL-SUP-2317]. Marking property and directing its placement fall on the agency being relocated under the direction and marking clause, which is inserted when office relocations are involved [@CL-SUP-2318; @CL-SUP-2319; @CL-SUP-2354]. The rules above come from the codified clauses at FAR 52.247-24, 52.247-25 and 52.247-26 [@CL-SUP-2314; @CL-SUP-2317; @CL-SUP-2318]; the RFO text prints those three section numbers as [Reserved] [@CL-SUP-0006; @CL-SUP-0007; @CL-SUP-0008].
