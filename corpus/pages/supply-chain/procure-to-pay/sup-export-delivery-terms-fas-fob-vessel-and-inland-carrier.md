## Purpose

Compares the three export delivery terms, showing where delivery is complete in each, which charges the contractor bears to that point, what documents it provides and when its responsibility for loss or damage ends.

Each clause is inserted when its own delivery term applies: f.a.s. vessel, port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2342]; f.o.b. vessel, port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2376]; and f.o.b. inland carrier, point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2381].

F.a.s. vessel, port of shipment, means free of expense to the Government delivered alongside the ocean vessel and within reach of its loading tackle at the specified port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2333]. Under paragraph (b), the contractor shall pack and mark the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepare it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-3866; @CL-SUP-2373]. It shall deliver the shipment in good order and condition alongside the ocean vessel and within reach of its loading tackle, at the point of delivery and on the date or within the period specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2390]. It shall pay and bear all applicable charges, including transportation costs, wharfage, handling and heavy lift charges if necessary, up to that point [@CL-SUP-2334]. It shall provide a clean dock or ship's receipt [@CL-SUP-2374]. It shall be responsible for any loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery of the shipment to the point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2335]. At the Government's request and expense, it shall assist in obtaining the documents required for exportation or for importation at destination [@CL-SUP-2375].

F.o.b. vessel, port of shipment, means free of expense to the Government loaded, stowed and trimmed on board the ocean vessel at the specified port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2336]. Under paragraph (b), the contractor shall pack and mark the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepare it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-3867; @CL-SUP-2377]. It shall deliver the shipment on board the ocean vessel in good order and condition on the date or within the period fixed [@CL-SUP-2378]. It shall pay and bear all charges incurred in placing the shipment actually on board [@CL-SUP-2337]. It shall provide a clean ship's receipt or on-board ocean bill of lading [@CL-SUP-2379]. It shall be responsible for any loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery of the shipment on board the ocean vessel [@CL-SUP-2338]. At the Government's request and expense, it shall assist in obtaining the documents required for exportation or for importation at destination [@CL-SUP-2380].

F.o.b. inland carrier, point of exportation, means free of expense to the Government, on board the conveyance of the inland carrier, delivered to the specified point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2339]. Under paragraph (b), the contractor shall pack and mark the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepare it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-3868; @CL-SUP-2382]. It shall prepare and distribute commercial bills of lading or other transportation receipt [@CL-SUP-2383]. It shall deliver the shipment in good order and condition in or on the conveyance of the carrier on the date or within the period specified [@CL-SUP-2384]. It shall pay and bear all applicable charges, including transportation costs, to the point of delivery specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2340]. It shall be responsible for any loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery of the shipment to the point of delivery in the contract [@CL-SUP-2341]. At the Government's request and expense, it shall assist in obtaining the documents required for exportation or for importation at destination [@CL-SUP-2385].

## How it works

Under f.a.s. vessel the contractor shall deliver alongside the ocean vessel within reach of its loading tackle and pay and bear the applicable charges up to that point [@CL-SUP-2390; @CL-SUP-2334], and shall be responsible for loss or damage before delivery to the point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2335]. Under f.o.b. vessel it shall deliver on board the ocean vessel and pay and bear the charges incurred in placing the shipment actually on board [@CL-SUP-2378; @CL-SUP-2337], and shall be responsible for loss or damage before delivery on board the ocean vessel [@CL-SUP-2338]. Under f.o.b. inland carrier it shall deliver in or on the conveyance of the carrier and pay and bear the applicable charges to the point of delivery specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2384; @CL-SUP-2340], and shall be responsible for loss or damage before delivery to the point of delivery in the contract [@CL-SUP-2341]. The f.a.s. vessel and f.o.b. inland carrier clauses refer to the date or period specified, and the f.o.b. vessel clause to the date or period fixed [@CL-SUP-2390; @CL-SUP-2384; @CL-SUP-2378]. The clauses differ in the receipt or bill of lading the contractor provides: a clean dock or ship's receipt, a clean ship's receipt or on-board ocean bill of lading, or commercial bills of lading or other transportation receipt [@CL-SUP-2374; @CL-SUP-2379; @CL-SUP-2383].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. When the delivery term is f.a.s. vessel, port of shipment, RFO FAR 52.247-36 is inserted in solicitations and contracts [@CL-SUP-0214], RFO FAR 52.247-37 when the delivery term is f.o.b. vessel, port of shipment [@CL-SUP-0222], and RFO FAR 52.247-38 when the delivery term is f.o.b. inland carrier, point of exportation [@CL-SUP-0230]. Each RFO clause defines its delivery term in paragraph (a) [@CL-SUP-0215; @CL-SUP-0223; @CL-SUP-0231]. Paragraph (b) of RFO FAR 52.247-36 covers packing and marking [@CL-SUP-0216], delivery alongside the ocean vessel and within reach of its loading tackle and the charges up to that point [@CL-SUP-0217; @CL-SUP-0218], the clean dock or ship's receipt [@CL-SUP-0219], responsibility for loss or damage before delivery to the point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-0220], and assistance with documents for exportation or importation at destination [@CL-SUP-0221]. Paragraph (b) of RFO FAR 52.247-37 covers packing and marking [@CL-SUP-0224], delivery on board the ocean vessel and the charges incurred in placing the shipment on board [@CL-SUP-0225; @CL-SUP-0226], the clean ship's receipt or on-board ocean bill of lading [@CL-SUP-0227], responsibility for loss or damage before delivery on board the ocean vessel [@CL-SUP-0228], and assistance with the same documents [@CL-SUP-0229]. Paragraph (b) of RFO FAR 52.247-38 covers packing and marking [@CL-SUP-0232], commercial bills of lading or other transportation receipt [@CL-SUP-0233], delivery in or on the conveyance of the carrier and the charges to the point of delivery specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-0234; @CL-SUP-0235], responsibility for loss or damage before delivery to the point of delivery in the contract [@CL-SUP-0236], and assistance with the same documents [@CL-SUP-0237].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor ships 8,000 pounds of machine parts under each term in turn.

Under f.a.s. vessel, a crate is damaged on the dock before it reaches the point alongside the vessel within reach of the loading tackle. Delivery has not happened, so the contractor is responsible for that damage. If transportation to the port cost 1,200 dollars, wharfage 300 dollars and handling 150 dollars, the contractor bears all 1,650 dollars. It also provides a clean dock or ship's receipt.

Under f.o.b. vessel, the same crate is damaged while being hoisted but before it is on board. The contractor is responsible, because it answers for loss or damage before delivery on board, and it bears the charges of placing the shipment on board.

Under f.o.b. inland carrier, the shipment is damaged on the road before it reaches the specified point of exportation. The contractor is responsible, and it bears the transportation costs to that point.

## Never confuse

F.a.s. vessel, port of shipment, means free of expense to the Government delivered alongside the ocean vessel and within reach of its loading tackle at the specified port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2333]. F.o.b. vessel, port of shipment, means free of expense to the Government loaded, stowed, and trimmed on board the ocean vessel at the specified port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2336]. F.o.b. inland carrier, point of exportation, means free of expense to the Government, on board the conveyance of the inland carrier, delivered to the specified point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2339].

## Company calibration

Confirm which delivery term each export order carries, the delivery date or period and the point specified in each contract, and who confirms where the contractor's charges and responsibility for loss or damage stop. [@Q-004]
