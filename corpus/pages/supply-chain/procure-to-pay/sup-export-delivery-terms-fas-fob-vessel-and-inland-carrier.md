## Purpose

Compares the three export delivery terms, showing where delivery is complete in each, which charges the contractor bears to that point, what documents it provides and when its responsibility for loss or damage ends.

Each clause is inserted when its own delivery term applies: f.a.s. vessel, port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2342]; f.o.b. vessel, port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2376]; and f.o.b. inland carrier, point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2381].

F.a.s. vessel, port of shipment, means free of expense to the Government delivered alongside the ocean vessel and within reach of its loading tackle at the specified port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2333]. The contractor packs and marks the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepares it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-2373]. It delivers the shipment in good order and condition alongside the ocean vessel and within reach of its loading tackle, at the point of delivery and on the date or within the period specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2390]. It pays and bears all applicable charges, including transportation costs, wharfage, handling and heavy lift charges if necessary, up to the point of delivery alongside the vessel [@CL-SUP-2334]. It provides a clean dock or ship's receipt [@CL-SUP-2374]. It is responsible for loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery of the shipment to the point specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2335]. At the Government's request and expense, it assists in obtaining the documents required for exportation or for importation at destination [@CL-SUP-2375].

F.o.b. vessel, port of shipment, means free of expense to the Government loaded, stowed and trimmed on board the ocean vessel at the specified port of shipment [@CL-SUP-2336]. The contractor packs and marks the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepares it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-2377]. It delivers the shipment on board the ocean vessel in good order and condition on the date or within the period fixed [@CL-SUP-2378]. It pays and bears all charges incurred in placing the shipment actually on board the ocean vessel [@CL-SUP-2337]. It provides a clean ship's receipt or on-board ocean bill of lading [@CL-SUP-2379]. It is responsible for loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery of the shipment on board the ocean vessel [@CL-SUP-2338]. At the Government's request and expense, it assists in obtaining the documents required for exportation or for importation at destination [@CL-SUP-2380].

F.o.b. inland carrier, point of exportation, means free of expense to the Government, on board the conveyance of the inland carrier, delivered to the specified point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2339]. The contractor packs and marks the shipment to comply with contract specifications or, in the absence of specifications, prepares it for ocean transportation in conformance with carrier requirements to protect the goods and to ensure assessment of the lowest applicable transportation charge [@CL-SUP-2382]. It prepares and distributes commercial bills of lading or other transportation receipt [@CL-SUP-2383]. It delivers the shipment in good order and condition in or on the conveyance of the carrier on the date or within the period specified [@CL-SUP-2384]. It pays and bears all applicable charges, including transportation costs, to the point of delivery specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-2340]. It is responsible for loss of or damage to the goods occurring before delivery of the shipment to the point of delivery in the contract [@CL-SUP-2341]. At the Government's request and expense, it assists in obtaining the documents required for exportation or for importation at destination [@CL-SUP-2385].

## How it works

The delivery point sets where the contractor's charges and its responsibility for loss or damage end. Under f.a.s. vessel they end alongside the vessel within reach of its loading tackle [@CL-SUP-2390; @CL-SUP-2334; @CL-SUP-2335]. Under f.o.b. vessel they end on board the ocean vessel [@CL-SUP-2378; @CL-SUP-2337; @CL-SUP-2338]. Under f.o.b. inland carrier they end at the specified point of exportation [@CL-SUP-2384; @CL-SUP-2340; @CL-SUP-2341]. The delivery dates, periods and points are contract-specific, and the clauses refer to them as those specified or fixed in the contract [@CL-SUP-2390; @CL-SUP-2378; @CL-SUP-2384]. The clauses differ in the receipt or bill of lading the contractor provides: a clean dock or ship's receipt, a clean ship's receipt or on-board ocean bill of lading, or commercial bills of lading or other transportation receipt [@CL-SUP-2374; @CL-SUP-2379; @CL-SUP-2383].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.247-36 keeps the rules described above for its delivery term: when the clause is inserted, what the term means, packing and marking, delivery alongside the vessel, the charges the contractor bears, the clean dock or ship's receipt, responsibility for loss or damage, and help with export and import documents [@CL-SUP-2342; @CL-SUP-2333; @CL-SUP-2373; @CL-SUP-2390; @CL-SUP-2334; @CL-SUP-2374; @CL-SUP-2335; @CL-SUP-2375; @CL-SUP-0214; @CL-SUP-0215; @CL-SUP-0216; @CL-SUP-0217; @CL-SUP-0218; @CL-SUP-0219; @CL-SUP-0220; @CL-SUP-0221]. RFO FAR 52.247-37 keeps its rules in the same way, with delivery on board the ocean vessel and the clean ship's receipt or on-board ocean bill of lading [@CL-SUP-2376; @CL-SUP-2336; @CL-SUP-2377; @CL-SUP-2378; @CL-SUP-2337; @CL-SUP-2379; @CL-SUP-2338; @CL-SUP-2380; @CL-SUP-0222; @CL-SUP-0223; @CL-SUP-0224; @CL-SUP-0225; @CL-SUP-0226; @CL-SUP-0227; @CL-SUP-0228; @CL-SUP-0229]. RFO FAR 52.247-38 keeps its rules in the same way, with commercial bills of lading or other transportation receipt and delivery in or on the carrier's conveyance [@CL-SUP-2381; @CL-SUP-2339; @CL-SUP-2382; @CL-SUP-2383; @CL-SUP-2384; @CL-SUP-2340; @CL-SUP-2341; @CL-SUP-2385; @CL-SUP-0230; @CL-SUP-0231; @CL-SUP-0232; @CL-SUP-0233; @CL-SUP-0234; @CL-SUP-0235; @CL-SUP-0236; @CL-SUP-0237].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor ships 8,000 pounds of machine parts under each term in turn.

Under f.a.s. vessel, a crate is damaged on the dock before it reaches the point alongside the vessel within reach of the loading tackle. Delivery has not happened, so the contractor is responsible for that damage. If transportation to the port cost 1,200 dollars, wharfage 300 dollars and handling 150 dollars, the contractor bears all 1,650 dollars. It also provides a clean dock or ship's receipt.

Under f.o.b. vessel, the same crate is damaged while being hoisted but before it is on board. The contractor is responsible, because it answers for loss or damage before delivery on board, and it bears the charges of placing the shipment on board.

Under f.o.b. inland carrier, the shipment is damaged on the road before it reaches the specified point of exportation. The contractor is responsible, and it bears the transportation costs to that point.

## Never confuse

F.a.s. vessel delivery is alongside the ocean vessel within reach of its loading tackle, while f.o.b. vessel delivery is loaded, stowed and trimmed on board [@CL-SUP-2333; @CL-SUP-2336]. F.o.b. inland carrier delivers on board the inland carrier's conveyance at the point of exportation, not to the vessel [@CL-SUP-2339]. These export terms are separate from the import terms on their own card, and from the f.o.b. origin and f.o.b. destination terms on theirs.

## Company calibration

Confirm which delivery term each export order carries, the delivery date or period and the point specified in each contract, and who confirms where the contractor's charges and responsibility for loss or damage stop. [@Q-004]
