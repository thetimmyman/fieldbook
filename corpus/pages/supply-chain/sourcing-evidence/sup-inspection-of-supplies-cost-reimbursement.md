## Purpose

Shows what the inspection clause for cost-reimbursement supply contracts asks of the contractor and the Government, and how the cost of fixing nonconforming supplies is treated.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1077] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-3 [@CL-SUP-1076] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-3 [@CL-SUP-3244] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1077] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for supplies, or services that involve the furnishing of supplies, when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-1076] |

The contractor shall provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government covering the supplies, fabricating methods, and special tooling under the contract [@CL-SUP-1077]. The Government has the right to inspect and test the contract supplies, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of manufacture, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1078]. The Government may also inspect the plant or plants of the contractor or any subcontractor engaged in the contract performance [@CL-SUP-1127]. Complete records of all inspection work performed by the contractor shall be maintained and made available to the Government during contract performance and for as long afterwards as the contract requires [@CL-SUP-1126].

At any time during contract performance, but no later than 6 months (or such other time as may be specified in the contract) after acceptance of the supplies to be delivered under the contract, the Government may require the contractor to replace or correct any supplies that are nonconforming at time of delivery [@CL-SUP-1128; @CL-SUP-1079]. Supplies are nonconforming when they are defective in material or workmanship or are otherwise not in conformity with contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1129].

Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h) of the clause, the cost of replacement or correction shall be included in allowable cost, determined as provided in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1080]. Under paragraph (h), notwithstanding paragraphs (f) and (g), the Government may at any time require the contractor to correct or replace, without cost to the Government, nonconforming supplies, if the nonconformances are due to one of two alternative causes: fraud, lack of good faith, or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of the contractor's managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-9778; @CL-SUP-1081; @CL-SUP-4169; @CL-SUP-1131].

## How it works

The contractor shall provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government covering the supplies, fabricating methods, and special tooling under the contract, and the Government has the right to inspect and test the contract supplies, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of manufacture, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1077; @CL-SUP-1078]. At any time during contract performance, but no later than 6 months (or such other time as may be specified in the contract) after acceptance of the supplies to be delivered under the contract, the Government may require the contractor to replace or correct any supplies that are nonconforming at time of delivery [@CL-SUP-1128; @CL-SUP-1079]. Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be included in allowable cost, determined as provided in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1080; @CL-SUP-9778; @CL-SUP-1081; @CL-SUP-4169; @CL-SUP-1131]. If the contractor fails to proceed with reasonable promptness to perform required replacement or correction, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, perform the replacement or correction and charge to the contractor any increased cost or make an equitable reduction in any fixed fee paid or payable under the contract, require delivery of undelivered supplies at an equitable reduction in any fixed fee paid or payable under the contract, or terminate the contract for default [@CL-SUP-1082; @CL-SUP-4168; @CL-SUP-1130]. The fixed-price clause is covered in [Inspection of supplies, fixed-price](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-inspection-of-supplies-fixed-price.md). The services version for cost-reimbursement contracts is on [Inspection of services, cost-reimbursement](sup-inspection-of-services-cost-reimbursement.md), and the certificate that may replace source inspection is on [Certificate of conformance](sup-certificate-of-conformance-and-dismantling-inspection.md).

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-3 require the contractor to provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government covering the supplies, fabricating methods, and special tooling under the contract [@CL-SUP-1077; @CL-SUP-3244], require complete records of all inspection work performed by the contractor to be maintained and made available to the Government during contract performance and for as long afterwards as the contract requires [@CL-SUP-1126; @CL-SUP-3245], and give the Government the right to inspect and test the contract supplies, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of manufacture, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1078; @CL-SUP-3246] and to inspect the plant or plants of the contractor or any subcontractor engaged in the contract performance [@CL-SUP-1127; @CL-SUP-3247]. Both provide that at any time during contract performance, but no later than 6 months (or such other time as may be specified in the contract) after acceptance of the supplies to be delivered under the contract, the Government may require the contractor to replace or correct any supplies that are nonconforming at time of delivery [@CL-SUP-1128; @CL-SUP-3248; @CL-SUP-1079; @CL-SUP-3249], and that supplies are nonconforming when they are defective in material or workmanship or are otherwise not in conformity with contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1129; @CL-SUP-3250]. Both provide that except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be included in allowable cost, determined as provided in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1080; @CL-SUP-3251]. Both provide that if the contractor fails to proceed with reasonable promptness to perform required replacement or correction, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, perform the replacement or correction and charge to the contractor any increased cost or make an equitable reduction in any fixed fee paid or payable under the contract, require delivery of undelivered supplies at an equitable reduction in any fixed fee paid or payable under the contract, or terminate the contract for default [@CL-SUP-1082; @CL-SUP-4168; @CL-SUP-3252; @CL-SUP-3253; @CL-SUP-1130; @CL-SUP-3254]. Under paragraph (h) of both, notwithstanding paragraphs (f) and (g), the Government may at any time require the contractor to correct or replace, without cost to the Government, nonconforming supplies, if the nonconformances are due to fraud, lack of good faith, or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or to the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of the contractor's managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-9778; @CL-SUP-3255; @CL-SUP-1081; @CL-SUP-4169; @CL-SUP-3256; @CL-SUP-1131; @CL-SUP-3257].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement contract carries a fixed fee of 100,000. After delivery, a lot of supplies is found to be nonconforming through a workmanship defect, and none of the listed no-cost causes applies. The contractor corrects the lot at a cost of 8,000. The 8,000 is included in allowable cost, and no additional fee is paid for the correction, so the fee stays at 100,000.

## Never confuse

Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be included in allowable cost, determined as provided in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1080], unlike paragraph (h), under which—notwithstanding paragraphs (f) and (g)—the Government may at any time require the contractor to correct or replace, without cost to the Government, nonconforming supplies if the nonconformances are due to fraud, lack of good faith, or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or to the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of the contractor's managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-9778; @CL-SUP-1081; @CL-SUP-4169; @CL-SUP-1131]. The cost-reimbursement contract framework is on [Cost-reimbursement contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-reimbursement-contracts.md), and the general policy on rejecting nonconforming supplies is on [Rejection of nonconforming supplies](../../operating-model/corrective-action/opm-rejection-of-nonconforming-supplies.md).
