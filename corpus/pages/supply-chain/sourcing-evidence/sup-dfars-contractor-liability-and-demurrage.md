## Purpose

Shows when the contractor is liable for loss or damage to shipped property, how the amount depends on the schedule and on negligence, and when it owes demurrage.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1615] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.247-7016 and 252.247-7014 [@CL-SUP-1615; @CL-SUP-1621] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1615] |
| Applies to | Shipments picked up under Schedule I, Outbound Services, or delivered under Schedule II, Inbound Services, and shipments picked up or delivered under Schedule III, Intra-City and Intra-Area, for the liability clause; and a failure to load or unload within the free time, for the demurrage clause [@CL-SUP-1615; @CL-SUP-1619; @CL-SUP-1621] |

The liability rules in the first paragraph apply to shipments picked up under Schedule I, Outbound Services, or delivered under Schedule II, Inbound Services [@CL-SUP-1615]. If notified within one year after delivery that the owner has discovered loss or damage to the owner's property, the contractor agrees to indemnify the Government for loss or damage to the property which arises from any cause while it is in the contractor's possession [@CL-SUP-1616]. Two listed rules follow: indemnification for damage from any cause other than the contractor's negligence shall be at a rate not to exceed sixty cents per pound per article [@CL-SUP-1617], and when loss or damage is caused by the contractor's negligence, the liability is for the full cost of satisfactory repair or for the current replacement value of the article [@CL-SUP-1618].

For shipments picked up or delivered under Schedule III, Intra-City and Intra-Area, if notified of loss or damage within 75 days following delivery, the contractor agrees to indemnify the Government for loss or damage to the owner's property [@CL-SUP-1619]. The liability is the full cost of satisfactory repair, or the current replacement value of the article less depreciation, up to a maximum liability of $1.25 per pound times the net weight of the shipment [@CL-SUP-1620].

## How it works

The first set of rules ties the contractor's indemnity to notice within one year after delivery, and provides separate rules for non-negligent and negligent damage [@CL-SUP-1616; @CL-SUP-1617; @CL-SUP-1618]. The Schedule III rule uses notice within 75 days following delivery and a maximum liability of $1.25 per pound times the net weight of the shipment [@CL-SUP-1619; @CL-SUP-1620]. Among the Schedule I and Schedule II rules, the rate not to exceed sixty cents per pound per article is the rule for damage from a cause other than the contractor's negligence [@CL-SUP-1617], while negligent damage carries liability for the full cost of satisfactory repair or the current replacement value [@CL-SUP-1618]. Separately, the contractor shall be liable for all demurrage, detention, or other charges as a result of its failure to load or unload trucks, freight cars, freight terminals, vessel piers, or warehouses within the free time allowed under applicable rules and tariffs [@CL-SUP-1621].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A shipment is picked up under Schedule I and the owner reports, within one year after delivery, that a 10 pound article was damaged. If the cause was not the contractor's negligence, the indemnity is capped at 0.60 per pound per article, so at most 6. If the contractor's negligence caused the damage, the contractor owes the full repair cost or the replacement value. A different shipment moves under Schedule III with a net weight of 200 pounds, and damage is reported within 75 days after delivery. The contractor's liability cannot exceed 1.25 times 200, which is 250, however high the replacement value. Separately, a truck waits at a freight terminal past the free time because the contractor was late unloading, and the contractor pays the detention charge.

## Never confuse

Liability for loss or damage to the owner's property is in one clause [@CL-SUP-1616], and liability for demurrage, detention, or other charges from a failure to load or unload within the free time is in another [@CL-SUP-1621]. Liability after the Government accepts supplies is a separate subject; see [Limitation of liability clauses: basic, high-value items and services](sup-limitation-of-liability-clauses.md). The stevedoring liability clause covers loss or damage to Government property and injury on the contract work; see [Liability and insurance in stevedoring contracts (DFARS)](sup-dfars-stevedoring-liability-and-insurance.md). Laundry articles have their own count and weight loss clauses; see [Laundry delivery tickets and loss or damage by count or by weight (DFARS)](sup-dfars-laundry-delivery-tickets-and-loss-or-damage.md). Responsibility for property furnished under the Government property clause is separate; see [Responsibility and liability for Government property](../../close-revrec/fixed-assets/cls-responsibility-liability-government-property.md).
