## Purpose

Shows what authorization a contractor needs to order from Government supply sources, how it orders and pays, what happens if it pays late, and what it may and may not do.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1651] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.251-7000 [@CL-SUP-1651] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1651] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use the clause as prescribed in section 251.107 of the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement [@CL-SUP-1651], for orders under the listed schedules and agreements [@CL-SUP-1567] and orders for Government stock [@CL-SUP-1570; @CL-SUP-1660] |

When placing orders under Federal Supply Schedules, Personal Property Rehabilitation Price Schedules, or Enterprise Software Agreements, the contractor shall follow the terms of the applicable schedule or agreement and authorization [@CL-SUP-1567]. One item the clause requires in each order is a copy of the authorization, unless a copy was previously furnished to the schedule or agreement contractor [@CL-SUP-1568]. Another item the clause requires in each order is a statement that any price reductions negotiated as part of an Enterprise Software Agreement issued under a Federal Supply Schedule contract shall control [@CL-SUP-9785]; the statement continues that, in the event of any other inconsistencies between an Enterprise Software Agreement established as a Federal Supply Schedule blanket purchase agreement and the Federal Supply Schedule contract, the latter shall govern [@CL-SUP-1569].

Paragraph (b) of the clause covers orders under nonmandatory schedule contracts and requirements contracts issued by the General Services Administration Office of Information Resources Management for automated data processing equipment, software and maintenance, among other listed items [@CL-SUP-1659]. For those orders, the contractor shall follow the terms of the applicable contract and the procedures in paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-SUP-1664].

When placing orders for Government stock on a reimbursable basis, the contractor shall comply with the Contracting Officer's authorization, using FEDSTRIP or MILSTRIP procedures as appropriate [@CL-SUP-1570]. The clause's list of duties for those orders also includes using only the GSA Form 1948-A, Retail Services Shopping Plate, when ordering from GSA Self-Service Stores [@CL-SUP-1570; @CL-SUP-1661]. The same list includes ordering only those items required in the performance of Government contracts [@CL-SUP-1570; @CL-SUP-1571]. It also includes paying invoices from Government supply sources promptly, which for purchases from DoD supply sources means within 30 days of the date of a proper invoice [@CL-SUP-1570; @CL-SUP-1572]. The contractor shall annotate each invoice with the date of receipt [@CL-SUP-1662]. For purposes of computing interest for late contractor payments, the Government's invoice is deemed to be a demand for payment in accordance with the Interest clause of the contract [@CL-SUP-1663].

Failure to pay invoices from Government supply sources may also result in the DoD supply source refusing to honor the requisition or in the Contracting Officer terminating the contractor's authorization to use DoD supply sources [@CL-SUP-1572; @CL-SUP-1573]. If the Contracting Officer decides to terminate for late payment, the Contracting Officer shall give the contractor prompt written notice of the intent and the basis [@CL-SUP-1574]. The contractor then has 10 days after receipt of the notice to provide additional information as to why the authorization should not be terminated [@CL-SUP-1575]. The termination gives the contractor no excusable delay for failure to perform or complete the contract in accordance with its terms, and the contractor is solely responsible for any increased costs [@CL-SUP-1576].

When placing orders for Government stock on a non-reimbursable basis, the contractor shall comply with the requirements of the Contracting Officer's authorization, among the duties the clause lists [@CL-SUP-1660].

Only the contractor may request authorization for subcontractor use of Government supply sources, and the Contracting Officer will not grant it without the contractor's approval [@CL-SUP-1577].

## How it works

The contractor follows the terms of the applicable schedule or agreement and its authorization, and each order carries a copy of the authorization unless one was already furnished [@CL-SUP-1567; @CL-SUP-1568]. For reimbursable stock orders, the contractor follows the authorization and the stated requisition procedures, and it orders only what contract performance requires [@CL-SUP-1570; @CL-SUP-1571]. The contractor pays supply source invoices promptly, and late payment can end the authorization after notice and a short response period [@CL-SUP-1572; @CL-SUP-1574; @CL-SUP-1575]. The response period is the contractor's stated opportunity to explain why the authorization should not be terminated [@CL-SUP-1575]. For conflicts between terms, the statement the clause requires in each order says that for other inconsistencies between an Enterprise Software Agreement established as a blanket purchase agreement and the Federal Supply Schedule contract, the latter governs [@CL-SUP-1569; @CL-SUP-9785].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor orders stock on a reimbursable basis from a DoD supply source and receives a proper invoice. It must pay within the stated period. It pays late, and the Contracting Officer decides to end the authorization. The Contracting Officer sends written notice, the contractor has the stated response period to explain, and if the authorization still ends, the contractor gets no excusable delay and carries any added cost.

## Never confuse

This clause is about the contractor paying Government supply sources, and for purchases from DoD supply sources the period is measured from the date of a proper invoice [@CL-SUP-1572]. It is not the rule on the Government's payment of contractor invoices; for that, see [Prompt payment for invoices](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment.md). Ordering under a schedule is also not the same as ordering Government stock on a reimbursable basis, because each has its own listed steps [@CL-SUP-1567; @CL-SUP-1570]. Use of Government-fleet vehicles is a separate clause; see [Interagency Fleet Management System vehicles and related services](sup-ifms-vehicles-and-related-services.md).
