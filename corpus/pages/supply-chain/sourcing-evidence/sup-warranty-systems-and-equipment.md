## Purpose

Shows the warranty period, the contractor's correction duty, the limits on the contractor's liability and the Government's remedies for systems and equipment bought on performance specifications or design criteria.

## The rule

The Contractor's warranties under the clause shall apply only to those defects discovered by either the Government or the Contractor within the warranty period (for example, at the time of delivery, within 45 days after delivery, or the specified event whose occurrence will terminate the warranty period, such as the number of miles or hours of use, or combination of any applicable events or periods of time) that the Contracting Officer states in the clause [@CL-SUP-1165; @CL-SUP-0440]. The Contractor shall promptly comply with any timely written direction from the Contracting Officer to correct or partially correct a defect, at no increase in the contract price [@CL-SUP-1166].

The Contractor shall not be responsible under the clause for the correction of defects in Government-furnished property, except for defects in installation, unless the Contractor performs, or is obligated to perform, any modifications or other work on such property, in which event the Contractor shall be responsible for correction of defects that result from the modifications or other work [@CL-SUP-1168; @CL-SUP-0441]. If the Government returns supplies to the Contractor for correction or replacement under the clause, the Contractor shall be liable for transportation charges up to an amount equal to the cost of transportation by the usual commercial method of shipment from the place of delivery specified in the contract (irrespective of the FOB point or the point of acceptance) to the Contractor's plant and return to the place of delivery specified in the contract, and the Contractor shall also bear the responsibility for the supplies while in transit [@CL-SUP-1169; @CL-SUP-0442].

## How it works

If the Contracting Officer determines that a defect exists in any of the supplies or services accepted by the Government under the contract, the Contracting Officer shall promptly notify the Contractor of the defect, in writing, within the specific period of time that the Contracting Officer inserts in the clause [@CL-SUP-1167]. The Contracting Officer shall give the Contractor a written notice specifying any failure or refusal of the Contractor to: (A) present a detailed recommendation for corrective action as required by paragraph (b)(3) of the clause, (B) correct defects as directed under paragraph (b)(4) of the clause, or (C) prepare and furnish data and reports as required by paragraph (b)(5) of the clause, and the notice shall specify a period of time following receipt of the notice by the Contractor in which the Contractor must remedy the failure or refusal specified in the notice [@CL-SUP-1220; @CL-SUP-0443]. If the Contractor does not comply with the Contracting Officer's written notice in paragraph (c)(5)(i) of the clause, the Contracting Officer may by contract or otherwise: (i) obtain detailed recommendations for corrective action and either (A) correct the supplies or services or (B) replace the supplies or services, and if the Contractor fails to furnish timely disposition instructions, the Contracting Officer may dispose of the nonconforming supplies for the Contractor's account in a reasonable manner, in which case the Government is entitled to reimbursement from the Contractor, or from the proceeds, for the reasonable expenses of care and disposition, as well as for excess costs incurred or to be incurred, (ii) obtain applicable data and reports, and (iii) charge the Contractor for the costs incurred by the Government [@CL-SUP-1170; @CL-SUP-0444; @CL-SUP-0445].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers a monitoring system built to a performance specification, and the clause says the warranty covers defects discovered within 90 days after delivery. A fault is found on day 60, and the contracting officer notifies the contractor in writing within the inserted period. The contractor recommends a corrective action, and the contracting officer then gives written direction to correct the fault. The contractor corrects it at no increase in the contract price. The answer: the fault was within the warranty period, and the contractor must follow the direction to correct it with the price unchanged.
