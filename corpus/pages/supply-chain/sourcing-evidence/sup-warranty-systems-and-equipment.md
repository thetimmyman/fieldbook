## Purpose

Shows the warranty period, the contractor's correction duty, the limits on the contractor's liability and the Government's remedies for systems and equipment bought on performance specifications or design criteria.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1165] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-19 [@CL-SUP-1165; @CL-SUP-1166; @CL-SUP-1167; @CL-SUP-1168; @CL-SUP-1169; @CL-SUP-1170; @CL-SUP-1220] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-19 [@CL-SUP-3504; @CL-SUP-9344; @CL-SUP-3505; @CL-SUP-3506; @CL-SUP-3507; @CL-SUP-3508; @CL-SUP-3509; @CL-SUP-3510; @CL-SUP-9345; @CL-SUP-3511; @CL-SUP-3512; @CL-SUP-9346; @CL-SUP-9347; @CL-SUP-9348; @CL-SUP-3513; @CL-SUP-9349; @CL-SUP-9350] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1165] |
| Applies to | Supplies or services accepted by the Government under a contract that includes the clause [@CL-SUP-1167] |

The contractor's warranties under the clause apply only to defects discovered by either the Government or the contractor within a warranty period that the contracting officer states in the clause [@CL-SUP-1165]. The source text does not establish a standard length for that period. The contractor shall promptly comply with any timely written direction from the contracting officer to correct or partially correct a defect, at no increase in the contract price [@CL-SUP-1166].

The contractor is not responsible under the clause for correcting defects in Government-furnished property, except for defects in installation, unless the contractor performs, or is obligated to perform, modifications or other work on that property [@CL-SUP-1168]. If the Government returns supplies to the contractor for correction or replacement, the contractor is liable for transportation charges up to the cost of transportation by the usual commercial method of shipment, measured from the place of delivery [@CL-SUP-1169].

## How it works

If the contracting officer determines that a defect exists in supplies or services accepted by the Government, the contracting officer shall promptly notify the contractor of the defect in writing within a period of time that the contracting officer inserts in the clause [@CL-SUP-1167]. The contracting officer shall give the contractor a written notice specifying any failure or refusal of the contractor to present a detailed recommendation for corrective action as required or to correct defects as directed, among the failures that the clause lists [@CL-SUP-1220]. If the contractor does not comply with the contracting officer's written notice about a listed failure, the contracting officer may by contract or otherwise obtain detailed recommendations for corrective action and either correct the supplies or services or take the alternative that the clause lists [@CL-SUP-1170]. The printed clause carries further terms on the contractor's recommendation, a decision not to correct, schedule effects and charging the Government's costs, and this card does not state them.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of 52.246-19 keeps the limit of the warranties to defects discovered within the warranty period that the contracting officer states [@CL-SUP-3504; @CL-SUP-1165], where the bracketed instruction also allows the period to be the specified event whose occurrence will terminate the warranty period, or a combination of any applicable events or periods of time [@CL-SUP-9344], and the duty to comply with a timely written direction to correct or partially correct a defect at no increase in the contract price [@CL-SUP-3505; @CL-SUP-1166]. It keeps the written notice of a defect in accepted supplies or services [@CL-SUP-3506; @CL-SUP-3507; @CL-SUP-1167] and the rule for defects in Government-furnished property [@CL-SUP-3508; @CL-SUP-3509; @CL-SUP-1168]. It keeps the liability for transportation charges up to the cost of the usual commercial method of shipment [@CL-SUP-3510; @CL-SUP-1169]. The RFO text measures that liability from the place of delivery specified in the contract, irrespective of the f.o.b. point or the point of acceptance, to the contractor's plant and return to that place of delivery, and has the contractor also bear the responsibility for the supplies while in transit [@CL-SUP-9345]. It keeps the written notice of a failure or refusal [@CL-SUP-3513; @CL-SUP-1220], a listed failure or refusal being the failure or refusal to prepare and furnish data and reports as required by paragraph (b)(5) [@CL-SUP-9349]. The notice specifies a period of time following receipt in which the contractor must remedy the failure or refusal [@CL-SUP-9350]. It keeps the contracting officer's right, if the contractor does not comply with the notice, to obtain detailed recommendations for corrective action and either correct or replace the supplies or services [@CL-SUP-3511; @CL-SUP-3512; @CL-SUP-1170]. Following the alternative to replace, if the contractor fails to furnish timely disposition instructions, the contracting officer may dispose of the nonconforming supplies for the contractor's account in a reasonable manner [@CL-SUP-9346], and the Government is entitled to reimbursement from the contractor, or from the proceeds, for the reasonable expenses of care and disposition, as well as for excess costs incurred or to be incurred [@CL-SUP-9347]. The RFO text also lets the contracting officer obtain applicable data and reports and charge the contractor for the costs incurred by the Government [@CL-SUP-9348].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers a monitoring system built to a performance specification, and the clause says the warranty covers defects discovered within 90 days after delivery. A fault is found on day 60, and the contracting officer notifies the contractor in writing within the inserted period. The contractor recommends a corrective action, and the contracting officer then gives written direction to correct the fault. The contractor corrects it at no increase in the contract price. The answer: the fault was within the warranty period, and the contractor must follow the direction to correct it with the price unchanged.

## Never confuse

Here the warranties apply only to defects discovered by either the Government or the contractor within the stated period [@CL-SUP-1165], so coverage is tied to when a defect is discovered. The [noncomplex supplies clause](sup-warranty-noncomplex-supplies.md) and the [complex supplies clause](sup-warranty-complex-supplies.md) are separate clauses with their own wording. A defect in Government-furnished property is not the contractor's to correct, except for defects in installation or work the contractor performs [@CL-SUP-1168]. The rule that lets the contracting officer insert the clause is on [Warranty contract clauses and their triggers](sup-warranty-contract-clauses.md), and the criteria for choosing a warranty are on [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](sup-warranties.md).

Whether the contract carries this clause, what its warranty-period, notice and recommendation blanks state, and how the contractor tracks defects found after acceptance. [@Q-004]
