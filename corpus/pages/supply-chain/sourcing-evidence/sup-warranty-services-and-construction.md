## Purpose

Shows what the services and construction warranties cover, when the Government must give notice, what the contractor must correct and what the Government may do if the contractor does not.

## The rule

Under FAR 52.246-20, notwithstanding inspection and acceptance by the Government or any provision concerning the conclusiveness thereof, the Contractor warrants that all services performed under the contract will, at the time of acceptance, be free from defects in workmanship and conform to the requirements of the contract [@CL-SUP-1171; @CL-SUP-0437]. The Contracting Officer shall give written notice of any defect or nonconformance to the Contractor within the specific period of time in which notice shall be given to the Contractor (for example, within 30 days from the date of acceptance by the Government, within 1,000 hours of use by the Government, or other specified event whose occurrence will terminate the period of notice, or combination of any applicable events or period of time) that the Contracting Officer inserts in the clause [@CL-SUP-1172; @CL-SUP-0438]. If the Contractor is required to correct or reperform, it shall be at no cost to the Government, and any services corrected or reperformed by the Contractor shall be subject to the clause to the same extent as work initially performed [@CL-SUP-1173]. If the Contractor fails or refuses to correct or reperform, the Contracting Officer may, by contract or otherwise, correct or replace with similar services and charge to the Contractor the cost occasioned to the Government thereby, or make an equitable adjustment in the contract price [@CL-SUP-1174]. If the Government does not require correction or reperformance, the Contracting Officer shall make an equitable adjustment in the contract price [@CL-SUP-1221].

Under FAR 52.246-21, in addition to any other warranties in the contract, the Contractor warrants, except as provided in paragraph (i) of the clause, that work performed under the contract conforms to the contract requirements and is free of any defect in equipment, material, or design furnished, or workmanship performed by the Contractor or any subcontractor or supplier at any tier [@CL-SUP-0439; @CL-SUP-1175]. Under paragraph (i), unless a defect is caused by the negligence of the Contractor or subcontractor or supplier at any tier, the Contractor shall not be liable for the repair of any defects of material or design furnished by the Government nor for the repair of any damage that results from any defect in Government-furnished material or design [@CL-SUP-1222; @CL-SUP-4051]. This warranty shall continue for a period of 1 year from the date of final acceptance of the work, and if the Government takes possession of any part of the work before final acceptance, this warranty shall continue for a period of 1 year from the date the Government takes possession [@CL-SUP-1176]. The Contractor shall remedy at the Contractor's expense any failure to conform, or any defect [@CL-SUP-1177]. The Contracting Officer shall notify the Contractor, in writing, within a reasonable time after the discovery of any failure, defect, or damage [@CL-SUP-1178].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.246-20, the Contracting Officer shall give written notice of any defect or nonconformance to the Contractor within the specific period of time in which notice shall be given to the Contractor, or other specified event whose occurrence will terminate the period of notice, or combination of any applicable events or period of time, that the Contracting Officer inserts in the clause, whereas under FAR 52.246-21 the Contracting Officer shall notify the Contractor, in writing, within a reasonable time after the discovery of any failure, defect, or damage [@CL-SUP-1172; @CL-SUP-0438; @CL-SUP-1178]. Under FAR 52.246-21, if the Contractor fails to remedy any failure, defect, or damage within a reasonable time after receipt of notice, the Government shall have the right to replace, repair, or otherwise remedy the failure, defect, or damage at the Contractor's expense [@CL-SUP-1179]. The construction warranty shall not limit the Government's rights under the Inspection and Acceptance clause of the contract with respect to latent defects, gross mistakes, or fraud [@CL-SUP-1223].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-20, notwithstanding inspection and acceptance by the Government or any provision concerning the conclusiveness thereof, the Contractor warrants that all services performed under the contract will, at the time of acceptance, be free from defects in workmanship and conform to the requirements of the contract [@CL-SUP-3515; @CL-SUP-3514]. The Contracting Officer shall give written notice of any defect or nonconformance to the Contractor within the specific period of time in which notice shall be given to the Contractor, or other specified event whose occurrence will terminate the period of notice, or combination of any applicable events or period of time, that the Contracting Officer inserts in the clause [@CL-SUP-3516; @CL-SUP-9342]. If the Contractor is required to correct or reperform, it shall be at no cost to the Government, and any services corrected or reperformed by the Contractor shall be subject to the clause to the same extent as work initially performed [@CL-SUP-3517]. If the Contractor fails or refuses to correct or reperform, the Contracting Officer may, by contract or otherwise, correct or replace with similar services and charge to the Contractor the cost occasioned to the Government thereby, or make an equitable adjustment in the contract price [@CL-SUP-3518]. If the Government does not require correction or reperformance, the Contracting Officer shall make an equitable adjustment in the contract price [@CL-SUP-3519]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-21, in addition to any other warranties in the contract, the Contractor warrants, except as provided in paragraph (i) of the clause, that work performed under the contract conforms to the contract requirements and is free of any defect in equipment, material, or design furnished, or workmanship performed by the Contractor or any subcontractor or supplier at any tier [@CL-SUP-9343; @CL-SUP-3520]. Unless a defect is caused by the negligence of the Contractor or subcontractor or supplier at any tier, the Contractor shall not be liable for the repair of any defects of material or design furnished by the Government nor for the repair of any damage that results from any defect in Government-furnished material or design [@CL-SUP-3521; @CL-SUP-3522]. This warranty shall continue for a period of 1 year from the date of final acceptance of the work, and if the Government takes possession of any part of the work before final acceptance, this warranty shall continue for a period of 1 year from the date the Government takes possession [@CL-SUP-3523]. The Contractor shall remedy at the Contractor's expense any failure to conform, or any defect [@CL-SUP-3524]. The Contracting Officer shall notify the Contractor, in writing, within a reasonable time after the discovery of any failure, defect, or damage [@CL-SUP-3525]. If the Contractor fails to remedy any failure, defect, or damage within a reasonable time after receipt of notice, the Government shall have the right to replace, repair, or otherwise remedy the failure, defect, or damage at the Contractor's expense [@CL-SUP-3526]. This warranty shall not limit the Government's rights under the Inspection and Acceptance clause of the contract with respect to latent defects, gross mistakes, or fraud [@CL-SUP-3527].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor services 200 hours of equipment, and the clause lets the contracting officer give notice within 30 days of acceptance. A defect is found on day 20, and the contracting officer gives notice and requires reperformance at no cost to the Government. For a separate building contract, a roof leak appears 8 months after final acceptance, which is inside the 1-year warranty, so the contractor remedies it at its own expense. A leak first found at 14 months is outside that warranty. The answer: the first two are covered and the last is not covered by this warranty, though the Government's inspection-clause rights for latent defects, gross mistakes or fraud are separate.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.246-20, the Contractor warrants that all services performed under the contract will, at the time of acceptance, be free from defects in workmanship and conform to the requirements of the contract [@CL-SUP-1171; @CL-SUP-0437], unlike under FAR 52.246-21, where the warranty shall continue for a period of 1 year from the date of final acceptance of the work, or for a period of 1 year from the date the Government takes possession if the Government takes possession of any part of the work before final acceptance [@CL-SUP-1176]. See [Service and warranty costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-service-and-warranty-costs.md), [Inspection of Services—Fixed-Price clause](sup-inspection-of-services-fixed-price.md), [Inspection of Construction clause](sup-inspection-of-construction.md), [Warranty of supplies of a noncomplex nature](sup-warranty-noncomplex-supplies.md), [Warranty contract clauses and their triggers](sup-warranty-contract-clauses.md), and [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](sup-warranties.md).
