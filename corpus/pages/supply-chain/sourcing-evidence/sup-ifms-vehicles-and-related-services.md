## Purpose

Shows who may use Interagency Fleet Management System vehicles under the clause, how requests are made, who bears the cost of unrelated use, and what the contractor must enforce.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1652] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.251-7001 [@CL-SUP-1652] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1652] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use the clause as prescribed in section 251.205 of the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement [@CL-SUP-1652], for a contractor authorized use of Interagency Fleet Management System (IFMS) vehicles [@CL-SUP-1578] |

The contractor, if authorized use of Interagency Fleet Management System (IFMS) vehicles, shall submit requests for five or fewer vehicles and related services in writing to the appropriate General Services Administration (GSA) Regional Customer Service Bureau, Attention: Motor Equipment Activity [@CL-SUP-1578]. The contractor shall submit requests for more than five vehicles to GSA headquarters [@CL-SUP-1579]. Each request must include two copies of the agency authorization to obtain vehicles and related services from GSA, as item (1) of what is included in each request [@CL-SUP-1580].

The contractor shall establish and enforce suitable penalties for employees who use or authorize the use of Government vehicles for other than performance of Government contracts [@CL-SUP-1581]. The contractor shall assume, without the right of reimbursement from the Government, the cost or expense of any use of IFMS vehicles and services not related to the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-1582]. Only the contractor may request authorization for subcontractor use of IFMS vehicles, and the Contracting Officer will not grant authorization for subcontractor use without approval of the contractor [@CL-SUP-1583].

## How it works

The contractor, if authorized use of IFMS vehicles, shall submit requests for five or fewer vehicles and related services in writing to the appropriate GSA Regional Customer Service Bureau, Attention: Motor Equipment Activity, and shall submit requests for more than five vehicles to GSA headquarters [@CL-SUP-1578; @CL-SUP-1579]. Each request includes two copies of the agency authorization to obtain vehicles and related services from GSA [@CL-SUP-1580]. The contractor shall establish and enforce suitable penalties for employees who use or authorize the use of Government vehicles for other than performance of Government contracts, and shall assume, without the right of reimbursement from the Government, the cost or expense of any use of IFMS vehicles and services not related to the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-1581; @CL-SUP-1582]. Only the contractor may request authorization for subcontractor use of IFMS vehicles, and the Contracting Officer will not grant authorization for subcontractor use without approval of the contractor [@CL-SUP-1583].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor with an agency authorization needs four vehicles for three months. It writes to the regional office and encloses two copies of the authorization. An employee later uses one vehicle for a private errand. The contractor applies its penalty rules and pays the cost of that trip without reimbursement.

## Never confuse

The contractor shall assume, without the right of reimbursement from the Government, the cost or expense of any use of IFMS vehicles and services not related to the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-1582], and only the contractor—rather than a subcontractor acting without the contractor's approval—may request authorization from the Contracting Officer for subcontractor use of IFMS vehicles [@CL-SUP-1583]. Ordering from Government supply sources is covered on [Ordering from Government supply sources](sup-ordering-from-government-supply-sources.md).
