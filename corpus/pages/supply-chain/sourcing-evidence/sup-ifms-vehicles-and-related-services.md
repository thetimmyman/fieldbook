## Purpose

Shows who may use Interagency Fleet Management System vehicles under the clause, how requests are made, who bears the cost of unrelated use, and what the contractor must enforce.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1652] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.251-7001 [@CL-SUP-1652] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1652] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use the clause as prescribed in section 251.205 of the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement [@CL-SUP-1652], for a contractor authorized use of the vehicles [@CL-SUP-1578] |

A contractor authorized use of the vehicles shall submit requests for five or fewer vehicles and related services in writing to the appropriate General Services Administration Regional Customer Service Bureau, Attention: Motor Equipment Activity [@CL-SUP-1578]. Requests for more than five vehicles go to General Services Administration headquarters [@CL-SUP-1579]. Each request must include two copies of the agency authorization to obtain vehicles and related services, among its listed contents [@CL-SUP-1580].

The contractor shall establish and enforce suitable penalties for employees who use or authorize the use of Government vehicles for other than performance of Government contracts [@CL-SUP-1581]. It shall assume, without the right of reimbursement from the Government, the cost or expense of any use of the vehicles and services not related to the performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-1582]. Only the contractor may request authorization for subcontractor use, and the Contracting Officer will not grant it without the contractor's approval [@CL-SUP-1583].

The printed clause text has no paragraph on insurance.

## How it works

A contractor that is authorized use of the vehicles asks the General Services Administration in writing for the vehicles and services it needs, and each request includes two copies of the agency authorization [@CL-SUP-1578; @CL-SUP-1580]. The office that receives the request depends on whether it covers five or fewer vehicles or more than five [@CL-SUP-1578; @CL-SUP-1579]. The contractor polices misuse by its employees and bears the full cost of any use not related to the contract [@CL-SUP-1581; @CL-SUP-1582]. Subcontractors reach the vehicles only through the contractor's request [@CL-SUP-1583].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor with an agency authorization needs four vehicles for three months. It writes to the regional office and encloses two copies of the authorization. An employee later uses one vehicle for a private errand. The contractor applies its penalty rules and pays the cost of that trip without reimbursement.

## Never confuse

Use not related to performance of the contract is at the contractor's own cost, without reimbursement [@CL-SUP-1582]. Only the contractor may request authorization for subcontractor use [@CL-SUP-1583]. The printed clause has no insurance paragraph, so do not read an insurance rule into it. Ordering Government stock and schedule items is a separate clause; see [Ordering from Government supply sources](sup-ordering-from-government-supply-sources.md).
