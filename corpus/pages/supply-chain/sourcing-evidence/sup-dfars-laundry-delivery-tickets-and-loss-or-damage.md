## Purpose

Shows what the delivery ticket clause requires and how the contractor's liability for lost or damaged laundry articles is worked out by count or by weight.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1639] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.237-7016, 252.237-7014 and 252.237-7015 [@CL-SUP-1639; @CL-SUP-1642; @CL-SUP-1644] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1639] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the basic delivery tickets clause or the clause printed as Alternate I or Alternate II, and the matching count-of-articles or weight-of-articles loss or damage clause [@CL-SUP-1639; @CL-SUP-1692; @CL-SUP-1693; @CL-SUP-1642; @CL-SUP-1644] |

In the basic delivery tickets clause, the contractor shall complete delivery tickets in the number of copies required and in the form approved by the contracting officer when it receives the articles to be serviced, and shall include one copy of each ticket with its invoice for payment [@CL-SUP-1639]. The clause is also printed as two alternates, and each is identified by its own heading. The clause printed under the heading Alternate I is to be used as prescribed in DFARS 237.7101(e) and (e)(2) and includes paragraphs (c), (d), and (e) not included in the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1692]. The clause printed under the heading Alternate II is to be used as prescribed in DFARS 237.7101(e) and (e)(3) and includes paragraphs (c), (d), and (e) not included in the basic clause [@CL-SUP-1693].

Under Alternate I, whose pickup paragraph refers to bag type, the contractor shall at time of pickup verify the weight and bag type and record them on the delivery ticket, and provide the contracting officer or representative a copy of the ticket [@CL-SUP-1640]. At the time of delivery it shall record the weight and bag type of serviced laundry on the delivery ticket, and the contracting officer will ensure that this weight and bag type are verified at time of delivery [@CL-SUP-1686]. Under Alternate II, whose pickup paragraph refers to weight only, the contractor shall at time of pickup verify and record the weight on the delivery ticket, and provide the contracting officer or representative a copy of the ticket [@CL-SUP-1641]. At the time of delivery it shall record the weight of serviced laundry on the delivery ticket, and the contracting officer will ensure that this weight is verified at time of delivery [@CL-SUP-1687].

## How it works

The basic ticket text does not itself list what a ticket must show: it requires the form approved by the contracting officer, and the alternates add the weight, and in Alternate I the bag type, to record at pickup and at delivery [@CL-SUP-1639; @CL-SUP-1640; @CL-SUP-1641; @CL-SUP-1686; @CL-SUP-1687]. Under the count-of-articles clause, the contractor shall be liable for return of the number and kind of articles furnished for service and shall indemnify the Government for any loss or damage to those articles [@CL-SUP-1642], paying the value of any lost or damaged property using Federal supply schedule price lists, or a fair and reasonable price set by the contracting officer if the property is not on those lists [@CL-SUP-1643]. The contracting officer will allow credit for depreciation in the value of the property at the time of loss or damage, and the two parties mutually determine the amount of the credit [@CL-SUP-1694]. Under the weight-of-articles clause, the contractor shall pay the Government a per-pound amount, left blank in the printed text, for lost or damaged articles, and only for losses which exceed the maximum weight loss in the clause [@CL-SUP-1644], and that maximum is a percentage, left blank in the printed text, of the weight recorded on delivery tickets when the laundry is picked up, with any weight loss above it subject to the loss provisions of the clause [@CL-SUP-1645]. The source does not establish the rate per pound or the percentage, so they must be read in the executed contract.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume a weight-of-articles clause where the contract fills the blanks with a 2 percent maximum weight loss and 3 per pound. The ticket records 1,000 pounds at pickup, and 950 pounds come back. The allowed loss is 20 pounds. The loss is 50 pounds, so 30 pounds are excess, and the contractor pays 30 times 3, which is 90. Under a count-of-articles clause, a lost article valued at 40 on the supply schedule price list, with a depreciation credit of 10 mutually determined, would cost the contractor 30. Under Alternate II, the pickup ticket would show the 1,000 pounds, verified by the contractor, and the contractor would hand the contracting officer or representative a copy.

## Never confuse

Count and weight are different bases for liability. The count clause is about the number and kind of articles and their value from the price lists [@CL-SUP-1642; @CL-SUP-1643]. The weight clause allows a maximum weight loss and charges only the excess [@CL-SUP-1644; @CL-SUP-1645]. Alternate I and Alternate II differ in whether bag type is recorded alongside weight; each is identified by its printed heading [@CL-SUP-1640; @CL-SUP-1641; @CL-SUP-1692; @CL-SUP-1693]. A copy of each delivery ticket goes with the invoice [@CL-SUP-1639]; for what a proper invoice must contain, see [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-proper-invoice-content.md). The loss or damage clause for shipments under the transportation schedules is a different clause; see [Contractor liability for loss or damage, and demurrage (DFARS)](sup-dfars-contractor-liability-and-demurrage.md).
