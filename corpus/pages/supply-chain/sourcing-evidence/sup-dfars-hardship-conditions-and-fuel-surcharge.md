## Purpose

Shows when the contractor may ask for extra payment for unusual loading or unloading conditions, and how a motor carrier fuel surcharge must be passed through.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1634] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.247-7000 and 252.247-7003 [@CL-SUP-1634; @CL-SUP-1637] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1634] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Hardship Conditions clause, and contracts that include the fuel surcharge pass-through clause [@CL-SUP-1634; @CL-SUP-1637] |

If the contractor finds unusual ship, dock, or cargo conditions associated with loading or unloading a particular cargo that will work a hardship on it at the basic commodity rates, the contractor shall take the steps listed after that condition [@CL-SUP-1634]. The listed steps are to notify the contracting officer before performing the work, if feasible but no later than the vessel sailing time, and to submit any associated request for price adjustment to the contracting officer within 10 working days of the vessel sailing time [@CL-SUP-1635]. If the contracting officer finds that the conditions are unusual and do materially affect the cost of loading or unloading, the contracting officer will authorize payment at the applicable man-hour rates set forth in the schedule of rates of the contract [@CL-SUP-1636].

Unless the contracting officer authorizes an exception, the contractor shall pass through any motor carrier fuel-related surcharge adjustments to the person, corporation, or entity that directly bears the cost of fuel for shipments transported under the contract [@CL-SUP-1637]. The contractor shall insert the substance of the fuel surcharge clause, including its subcontracts paragraph, in all subcontracts with motor carriers, brokers, or freight forwarders, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-SUP-1638].

## How it works

If the contractor finds the unusual conditions, it shall notify the contracting officer and submit any associated request for price adjustment to the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-1634; @CL-SUP-1635]. If the contracting officer finds the conditions unusual and materially cost-affecting, payment is authorized at the man-hour rates in the schedule of rates of the contract [@CL-SUP-1636]. For fuel, the surcharge adjustment is passed to whoever directly bears the cost of fuel for the shipments under the contract, unless the contracting officer authorizes an exception [@CL-SUP-1637]. The clause also requires the substance of the fuel surcharge clause in the listed subcontracts [@CL-SUP-1638].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume a contractor finds unusual dock conditions that will work a hardship on it at the basic commodity rates. It notifies the contracting officer before sailing and files its request 8 working days after sailing. The request is in time. The contracting officer finds the cost materially affected and authorizes payment at the schedule man-hour rates. Separately, a motor carrier adds a fuel surcharge of 500 on a shipment where, assume, the Government directly bears the fuel cost, and the contractor passes the 500 to the Government, absent an authorized exception. The contractor's subcontract with that motor carrier also carries the substance of the fuel surcharge clause.

## Never confuse

A hardship request concerns unusual loading or unloading conditions, and payment is authorized at the applicable man-hour rates in the schedule of rates [@CL-SUP-1636]. A fuel surcharge adjustment is passed through to the person, corporation, or entity that directly bears the cost of fuel, unless the contracting officer authorizes an exception [@CL-SUP-1637]. For the negotiated revision of prices, see [Revision of prices in transportation and stevedoring contracts (DFARS)](sup-dfars-stevedoring-revision-of-prices.md), and for formula-based adjustments, see [Economic price adjustments in proposals and proposal audits](../../estimating/escalation/est-economic-price-adjustments.md). The application for shipping documentation is another transportation clause; see [Riding gang member requirements and shipping documentation applications (DFARS)](sup-dfars-riding-gang-members-and-shipping-documentation.md). For the wider rules on commercial subcontract flowdown, see [Subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md) and [Subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services (DFARS)](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-dfars-commercial-subcontract-flowdown.md).
