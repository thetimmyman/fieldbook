## Purpose

Shows what the contractor must send after each ocean shipment, the representation it makes with its final invoice, what follows if the representation is missing or foreign-flag vessels were used without authority, and how the clause flows down.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-SUP-1396] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.247-7023(f) to (h) and (j) [@CL-SUP-1396; @CL-SUP-1771; @CL-SUP-1397; @CL-SUP-1399; @CL-SUP-1401] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1396] |
| Applies to | Shipments covered by the clause, and for the final-invoice representation, contracts above the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-SUP-1396; @CL-SUP-1397] |

Within 30 days after each shipment covered by the clause, the contractor shall provide the contracting officer and MARAD one copy of the rated on board vessel operating carrier's ocean bill of lading, which shall contain the information the clause lists [@CL-SUP-1396; @CL-SUP-1771]. If the contract exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, the contractor shall provide with its final invoice a representation, to the best of its knowledge and belief, about its use of ocean transportation [@CL-SUP-1397].

In the basic clause the representation is one of the listed alternatives, and two of them are that ocean transportation was used and the contractor had received a prior-approved waiver for U.S.-flag vessels for all foreign-flag ocean transportation, or that ocean transportation was used and some or all of the shipments were made on foreign-flag vessels without the written consent of DoD [@CL-SUP-1397; @CL-SUP-1398]. In Alternate II, one of the listed alternatives is instead that ocean transportation was used and the contractor had the written consent of the contracting officer for all foreign-flag ocean transportation [@CL-SUP-1445].

## How it works

If the contract exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and the final invoice lacks the representation, the Government rejects and returns it as an improper invoice for the purposes of the Prompt Payment clause [@CL-SUP-1399]. If foreign-flag vessels were used without authorization, the contracting officer is entitled to equitably adjust the contract based on that use [@CL-SUP-1400]. For flowdown, in the award of subcontracts for the types of supplies described in paragraph (c)(2) of the clause, including subcontracts for commercial products, the contractor shall flow down the requirements of the clause as the listed paragraphs provide [@CL-SUP-1401]. See [U.S.-flag requirement card](sup-dfars-sea-transport-us-flag-requirement.md). Subcontracts above the simplified acquisition threshold receive the substance of the whole clause [@CL-SUP-1402]. Subcontracts at or below it receive the substance of paragraphs (a) through (f) and paragraph (j) [@CL-SUP-1403].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract above the simplified acquisition threshold ends with one shipment on a foreign-flag vessel that had no waiver. The contractor describes the shipment with its final invoice in the format the clause gives. If it omitted the representation, the Government would reject and return the invoice as an improper invoice for the purposes of the Prompt Payment clause [@CL-SUP-1399]. The contracting officer would also be entitled to equitably adjust the contract based on the unauthorized use [@CL-SUP-1400].

## Never confuse

A missing representation and an unauthorized foreign-flag shipment have different consequences. The first makes the final invoice improper [@CL-SUP-1399]. The second lets the contracting officer equitably adjust the contract [@CL-SUP-1400]. The companion card, [Transportation of supplies by sea: definitions and the U.S.-flag requirement](sup-dfars-sea-transport-us-flag-requirement.md), covers the U.S.-flag requirement, the advance notification and the waiver request. For how an invoice is judged proper, see [Proper invoice: required content and payment documentation](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-proper-invoice-content.md) and [Prompt payment interest penalties and the prompt payment clauses](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-prompt-payment-interest-and-clauses.md). For flowdown into commercial subcontracts generally, see [Subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md).
