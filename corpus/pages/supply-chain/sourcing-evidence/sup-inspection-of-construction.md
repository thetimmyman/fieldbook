## Purpose

Shows the inspection system duty, the Government's right to inspect and test, the duty to correct or remove rejected work, and when acceptance of construction is final.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1114] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-12 [@CL-SUP-1114] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-12 [@CL-SUP-3168] |
| Edition date | Aug 1996 [@CL-SUP-3168] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1114] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Inspection of Construction clause [@CL-SUP-1114]; the clause is directed for solicitations and contracts for construction when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-SUP-1141] |

The contractor shall maintain an adequate inspection system and perform such inspections as will ensure that the work performed under the contract conforms to contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1114]. All work shall be conducted under the general direction of the Contracting Officer and is subject to Government inspection and test at all places and at all reasonable times before acceptance to ensure strict compliance with the terms of the contract [@CL-SUP-1115].

The contractor shall, without charge, replace or correct work found by the Government not to conform to contract requirements, unless in the public interest the Government consents to accept the work with an appropriate adjustment in contract price [@CL-SUP-1117]. The contractor shall promptly segregate and remove rejected material from the premises [@CL-SUP-1118]. If the contractor does not promptly replace or correct rejected work, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, replace or correct the work and charge the cost to the contractor, or terminate for default the contractor's right to proceed [@CL-SUP-1119].

Acceptance shall be final and conclusive except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or the Government's rights under any warranty or guarantee [@CL-SUP-1120].

## How it works

The contractor shall maintain an adequate inspection system and perform such inspections as will ensure that the work performed under the contract conforms to contract requirements, and all work shall be conducted under the general direction of the Contracting Officer and is subject to Government inspection and test at all places and at all reasonable times before acceptance to ensure strict compliance with the terms of the contract [@CL-SUP-1114; @CL-SUP-1115]. The contractor shall, without charge, replace or correct work found by the Government not to conform to contract requirements, unless in the public interest the Government consents to accept the work with an appropriate adjustment in contract price [@CL-SUP-1117]. The contractor shall promptly segregate and remove rejected material from the premises [@CL-SUP-1118]. If the contractor does not promptly replace or correct rejected work, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, replace or correct the work and charge the cost to the contractor, or terminate for default the contractor's right to proceed [@CL-SUP-1119]. Acceptance shall be final and conclusive except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or the Government's rights under any warranty or guarantee [@CL-SUP-1120].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-12 require the contractor to maintain an adequate inspection system and perform such inspections as will ensure that the work performed under the contract conforms to contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1114; @CL-SUP-3168], and provide that all work shall be conducted under the general direction of the Contracting Officer and is subject to Government inspection and test at all places and at all reasonable times before acceptance to ensure strict compliance with the terms of the contract [@CL-SUP-1115; @CL-SUP-3169]. Both provide that Government inspections and tests are for the sole benefit of the Government and do not relieve the contractor of responsibility for providing adequate quality control measures or of responsibility for damage to or loss of the material before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1116; @CL-SUP-3170], do not constitute or imply acceptance [@CL-SUP-1142; @CL-SUP-3171], and do not affect the continuing rights of the Government after acceptance of the completed work under paragraph (i) [@CL-SUP-9775; @CL-SUP-9271]. Both require the contractor, without charge, to replace or correct work found by the Government not to conform to contract requirements unless in the public interest the Government consents to accept the work with an appropriate adjustment in contract price [@CL-SUP-1117; @CL-SUP-3172], and to promptly segregate and remove rejected material from the premises [@CL-SUP-1118; @CL-SUP-3173]. Both provide that if the contractor does not promptly replace or correct rejected work, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, replace or correct the work and charge the cost to the contractor, or terminate for default the contractor's right to proceed [@CL-SUP-1119; @CL-SUP-3174], and that acceptance shall be final and conclusive except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or the Government's rights under any warranty or guarantee [@CL-SUP-1120; @CL-SUP-3175].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A construction contract is worth 500,000. The Government rejects a concrete pour that does not meet the specification, which is 20,000 of the work. The contractor must replace it without charge and promptly remove the rejected material from the premises. If the contractor does nothing, the Government may have the pour redone by another party and charge the 20,000 cost to the contractor. After acceptance, acceptance is final and conclusive except for the listed exceptions.

## Never confuse

Government inspections and tests are for the sole benefit of the Government and do not relieve the contractor of responsibility for providing adequate quality control measures or of responsibility for damage to or loss of the material before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1116]. They also do not constitute or imply acceptance [@CL-SUP-1142]. Acceptance shall be final and conclusive except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or the Government's rights under any warranty or guarantee [@CL-SUP-1120]. Contractor liability after acceptance under the limitation of liability clauses is on [Contractor liability after acceptance](sup-contractor-liability-after-acceptance.md), and the general inspection policy is on [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md).
