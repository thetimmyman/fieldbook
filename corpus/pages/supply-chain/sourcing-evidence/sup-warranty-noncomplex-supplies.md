## Purpose

Shows the warranty a contractor gives for noncomplex supplies, how the Government gives notice of a breach, and the Government's remedies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1151] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-17 [@CL-SUP-1151; @CL-SUP-1152; @CL-SUP-1153; @CL-SUP-1154; @CL-SUP-1155; @CL-SUP-1156; @CL-SUP-1212; @CL-SUP-1213; @CL-SUP-1214; @CL-SUP-1215; @CL-SUP-1216; @CL-SUP-1225] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-17 and its Alternate II [@CL-SUP-3490; @CL-SUP-3491; @CL-SUP-3492; @CL-SUP-9339; @CL-SUP-3493; @CL-SUP-3494; @CL-SUP-3495; @CL-SUP-3496; @CL-SUP-3497; @CL-SUP-3498; @CL-SUP-3499; @CL-SUP-3500; @CL-SUP-9340; @CL-SUP-9341; @CL-SUP-3501; @CL-SUP-3502; @CL-SUP-3503] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1151] |
| Applies to | Supplies furnished under a contract that includes the clause [@CL-SUP-1151] |

Notwithstanding Government inspection and acceptance of the supplies, or any contract condition on the conclusiveness of acceptance, the contractor warrants for a period of time after delivery that the contracting officer states in the clause [@CL-SUP-1151]. The contracting officer states in the clause either a specific period of time after delivery or the specified event whose occurrence will terminate the warranty period, such as the number of miles or hours of use, or a combination of applicable events or periods of time [@CL-SUP-1225]. The first listed item of the contractor's warranty is that all supplies furnished under the contract will be free from defects in material or workmanship and will conform with all contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1152]. The source text does not establish a standard length for the warranty period, because the clause leaves it for the contracting officer to fill in.

Supplies or parts corrected or furnished in replacement under the clause are also subject to its terms to the same extent as supplies initially delivered [@CL-SUP-1156]. The effect of acceptance is that it does not end the warranty, because the warranty applies notwithstanding inspection and acceptance [@CL-SUP-1151]. All implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose are excluded from any obligation contained in the contract [@CL-SUP-1212].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall give the contractor written notice of any breach of the listed warranties within a period of time that the contracting officer inserts in the clause [@CL-SUP-1153]. Within a reasonable time after the notice, the contracting officer may, as one of two listed options, require by written notice the prompt correction or replacement of supplies or parts that do not conform with the contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1154]. The second listed option is to retain the supplies and reduce the contract price by an amount equitable under the circumstances [@CL-SUP-1155]. The contracting officer may, by contract or otherwise, correct or replace the nonconforming supplies with similar supplies from another source and charge the contractor the cost occasioned to the Government if the contractor is in a circumstance that the clause then lists [@CL-SUP-1213], and one listed circumstance is that the contractor fails to make redelivery of the corrected or replaced supplies within the time established for their return [@CL-SUP-1214]. If the contractor fails to furnish timely disposition instructions, the contracting officer may dispose of the nonconforming supplies for the contractor's account in a reasonable manner [@CL-SUP-1215]. Alternate II provides a substitute transportation paragraph for use if it is desirable to specify that necessary transportation incident to correction or replacement will be at the Government's expense [@CL-SUP-1216]. The printed clause also has terms on sampling and screening, a second circumstance for buying elsewhere, transportation under the basic clause and its other alternates, which this card does not summarise. The conditions under which the contracting officer may insert the clause are on [Warranty contract clauses and their triggers](sup-warranty-contract-clauses.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of 52.246-17 keeps the warranty that applies notwithstanding inspection and acceptance for the period that the contracting officer states [@CL-SUP-3490; @CL-SUP-3491; @CL-SUP-1151; @CL-SUP-1225] and the first listed item of the warranty [@CL-SUP-3492; @CL-SUP-1152]. The RFO text lists a second item, which is that the preservation, packaging, packing, and marking, and the preparation for, and method of, shipment of the supplies will conform with the requirements of the contract [@CL-SUP-9339]. It keeps the rule for corrected or replaced supplies [@CL-SUP-3497; @CL-SUP-1156] and the exclusion of implied warranties [@CL-SUP-3498; @CL-SUP-1212]. It keeps the written notice of breach [@CL-SUP-3493; @CL-SUP-1153] and the two options of correction or replacement or retention with a price reduction [@CL-SUP-3494; @CL-SUP-3495; @CL-SUP-3496; @CL-SUP-1154; @CL-SUP-1155]. It keeps the right to correct or replace from another source and charge the contractor [@CL-SUP-3499; @CL-SUP-1213], with the circumstance that the contractor fails to make redelivery of the corrected or replaced supplies within the time established for their return [@CL-SUP-3500; @CL-SUP-1214]. The RFO text lists a second circumstance, that the contractor fails either to accept return of the nonconforming supplies or fails to make progress after their return to correct or replace them so as to endanger performance of the delivery schedule [@CL-SUP-9340], and it says that in either of these circumstances the contractor does not cure the failure within 10 days, or a longer period that the contracting officer authorizes in writing, after receipt of notice from the contracting officer specifying the failure [@CL-SUP-9341]. It also keeps the right to dispose of the nonconforming supplies if disposition instructions are not timely [@CL-SUP-3501; @CL-SUP-1215]. It keeps Alternate II, which substitutes a paragraph for use if it is desirable to specify that necessary transportation will be at the Government's expense [@CL-SUP-3502; @CL-SUP-3503; @CL-SUP-1216].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 500 catalog fittings under a contract whose clause sets a warranty of 12 months after delivery and notice within 30 days of discovery. In month 8 a leak is found in 20 fittings, and the contracting officer gives written notice in week 2 after discovery. The contracting officer may require prompt correction or replacement of the 20 fittings, or keep them and reduce the price by an equitable amount. The answer: the notice was in time, and the Government chooses between those two remedies.

## Never confuse

Acceptance does not end this warranty, because the warranty applies notwithstanding acceptance [@CL-SUP-1151]. The warranty is also a different thing from the rule that lets the contracting officer insert the clause, which is on [Warranty contract clauses and their triggers](sup-warranty-contract-clauses.md). The criteria for deciding whether a warranty is appropriate are on [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](sup-warranties.md), the complex supplies clause has its own remedies on [Warranty of supplies of a complex nature](sup-warranty-complex-supplies.md), the liability limit after acceptance is a separate rule on [Limitation of liability clauses](sup-limitation-of-liability-clauses.md), and the inspection clause is a separate clause, covered on [Inspection of Supplies—Fixed-Price clause](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-inspection-of-supplies-fixed-price.md).
