## Purpose

Shows the Government's inspection rights, the correction duty and how acceptance is made under the research and development inspection clauses for fixed-price and cost-reimbursement contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1105; @CL-SUP-1109] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-7 and 52.246-8 [@CL-SUP-1105; @CL-SUP-1109] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-7 [@CL-SUP-3176] and RFO FAR 52.246-8 [@CL-SUP-3180] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1105; @CL-SUP-1109] |
| Applies to | Research and development contracts that include the fixed-price or the cost-reimbursement inspection clause [@CL-SUP-1105; @CL-SUP-1109]; the fixed-price clause is prescribed for research and development solicitations and contracts when the primary objective of the contract is the delivery of end items other than designs, drawings, or reports, a fixed-price contract is contemplated, and the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-SUP-1136], unless use of the clause is impractical and the clause prescribed in 46.309 is considered to be more appropriate [@CL-SUP-1137]; the cost-reimbursement clause is prescribed for research and development solicitations and contracts when the primary objective of the contract is the delivery of end items other than designs, drawings, or reports, and a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-1138], unless use of the clause is impractical and the clause prescribed in 46.309 is considered to be more appropriate [@CL-SUP-1139] |

Under the fixed-price clause, the Government has the right to inspect and test all work called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of performance, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1105]. The Government shall accept or reject the work as promptly as practicable after delivery, unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-1106]. If the contractor fails or is unable to correct or to replace nonconforming work within the delivery schedule (or such later time as the Contracting Officer may authorize), the Contracting Officer may accept the work and make an equitable price reduction [@CL-SUP-1107]. Acceptance shall be conclusive, except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or as otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-1108].

Under the cost-reimbursement clause, the Government has the right to inspect and test all work called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of performance, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1109]. Unless otherwise provided in the contract, the Government shall accept work as promptly as practicable after delivery, and work shall be deemed accepted 90 days after delivery, unless accepted earlier [@CL-SUP-1110]. At any time during contract performance, but no later than 6 months (or such other time as may be specified in the contract) after acceptance of all of the end items (other than designs, drawings, or reports) to be delivered under the contract, the Government may require the contractor to replace or correct work not meeting contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1111; @CL-SUP-1112]. Time devoted to the replacement or correction of such work shall not be included in the computation of that time period [@CL-SUP-9776]. Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h) of the clause, the cost of replacement or correction shall be determined as specified in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1113]. Under paragraph (h), notwithstanding paragraphs (f) and (g), the Government may at any time require the contractor to remedy by correction or replacement, without cost to the Government, any failure by the contractor to comply with the requirements of the contract, if the failure is due to one of two alternative causes: fraud, lack of good faith, or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of the contractor's managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-9671; @CL-SUP-9672; @CL-SUP-9673]. If it is contemplated that the contract will be on a no-fee basis, the contracting officer shall use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-SUP-1140].

## How it works

Both clauses give the Government the right to inspect and test all work called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of performance, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1105; @CL-SUP-1109]. Under the fixed-price clause, if the contractor fails or is unable to correct or to replace nonconforming work within the delivery schedule (or such later time as the Contracting Officer may authorize), the Contracting Officer may accept the work and make an equitable price reduction [@CL-SUP-1107], and acceptance shall be conclusive, except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or as otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-1108]. Under the cost-reimbursement clause, unless otherwise provided in the contract, the Government shall accept work as promptly as practicable after delivery, and work shall be deemed accepted 90 days after delivery, unless accepted earlier [@CL-SUP-1110]. At any time during contract performance, but no later than 6 months (or such other time as may be specified in the contract) after acceptance of all of the end items (other than designs, drawings, or reports) to be delivered under the contract, the Government may require the contractor to replace or correct work not meeting contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1111; @CL-SUP-1112], and except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be determined as specified in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1113; @CL-SUP-9671].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-7 provide the Government's right to inspect and test all work called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of performance, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1105; @CL-SUP-3176], the duty to accept or reject the work as promptly as practicable after delivery unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-1106; @CL-SUP-3177], the option for the Contracting Officer to accept the work and make an equitable price reduction if the contractor fails or is unable to correct or to replace nonconforming work within the delivery schedule (or such later time as the Contracting Officer may authorize) [@CL-SUP-1107; @CL-SUP-3178], and conclusive acceptance except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or as otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-1108; @CL-SUP-3179]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-8 provide the right to inspect and test all work called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times, including the period of performance, and in any event before acceptance [@CL-SUP-1109; @CL-SUP-3180], acceptance as promptly as practicable after delivery and deemed acceptance 90 days after delivery unless accepted earlier or otherwise provided in the contract [@CL-SUP-1110; @CL-SUP-3181], and the right at any time during contract performance, but no later than 6 months (or such other time as may be specified in the contract) after acceptance of all of the end items (other than designs, drawings, or reports) to be delivered under the contract, to require the contractor to replace or correct work not meeting contract requirements [@CL-SUP-1111; @CL-SUP-3182; @CL-SUP-1112; @CL-SUP-3183]. Both provide that time devoted to the replacement or correction of such work shall not be included in the computation of that time period [@CL-SUP-9776; @CL-SUP-9273], and that except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be determined as specified in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1113; @CL-SUP-3184]. Under paragraph (h) of both, notwithstanding paragraphs (f) and (g), the Government may at any time require the contractor to remedy by correction or replacement, without cost to the Government, any failure by the contractor to comply with the requirements of the contract, if the failure is due to fraud, lack of good faith, or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or to the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of the contractor's managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-9671; @CL-SUP-9672; @CL-SUP-9673; @CL-SUP-9674; @CL-SUP-9675; @CL-SUP-9676].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price research contract is worth 300,000. The contractor delivers a prototype that does not conform and cannot correct it within the delivery schedule. The Contracting Officer accepts the prototype and makes an equitable price reduction of 30,000. Under a cost-reimbursement research contract, the same prototype would be deemed accepted on day 90 after delivery unless accepted earlier, and any correction would carry no additional fee.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.246-7, acceptance shall be conclusive, except for latent defects, fraud, gross mistakes amounting to fraud, or as otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-SUP-1108], whereas under FAR 52.246-8, unless otherwise provided in the contract, the Government shall accept work as promptly as practicable after delivery, and work shall be deemed accepted 90 days after delivery, unless accepted earlier [@CL-SUP-1110]. Under FAR 52.246-7, if the contractor fails or is unable to correct or to replace nonconforming work within the delivery schedule (or such later time as the Contracting Officer may authorize), the Contracting Officer may accept the work and make an equitable price reduction [@CL-SUP-1107], whereas under FAR 52.246-8, except as otherwise provided in paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be determined as specified in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1113]. Under FAR 52.246-3 on [Inspection of supplies, cost-reimbursement](sup-inspection-of-supplies-cost-reimbursement.md), except as otherwise provided in its paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be included in allowable cost, determined as provided in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1080], and under its paragraph (h), notwithstanding paragraphs (f) and (g), the Government may at any time require the contractor to correct or replace, without cost to the Government, nonconforming supplies, if the nonconformances are due to one of two alternative causes: fraud, lack of good faith, or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of the contractor's managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-9778; @CL-SUP-1081; @CL-SUP-4169; @CL-SUP-1131]. The general policy on rejecting nonconforming supplies is on [Rejection of nonconforming supplies](../../operating-model/corrective-action/opm-rejection-of-nonconforming-supplies.md), and inspection places are on [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md).
