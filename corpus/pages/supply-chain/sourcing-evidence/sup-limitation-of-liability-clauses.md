## Purpose

Shows what loss of Government property the contractor is not liable for after acceptance under each of the three limitation of liability clauses, the exceptions, and how the high-value items clause differs.

## The rule

|---|---|

Under paragraph (a) of the basic clause (FAR 52.246-23), except as provided in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the clause, and except for remedies expressly provided elsewhere in the contract, the contractor shall not be liable for loss of or damage to property of the Government (excluding the supplies delivered under the contract) that occurs after Government acceptance of the supplies delivered under the contract and results from any defects or deficiencies in the supplies [@CL-SUP-1201; @CL-SUP-1180]. Under paragraph (b), the limitation of liability under paragraph (a) shall not apply when a defect or deficiency in, or the Government's acceptance of, the supplies results from willful misconduct or lack of good faith on the part of any of the contractor's managerial personnel [@CL-SUP-1181]. Under paragraph (c), if the contractor carries insurance, or has established a reserve for self-insurance, covering liability for loss or damage suffered by the Government through purchase or use of the supplies required to be delivered under the contract, the contractor shall be liable to the Government, to the extent of such insurance or reserve, for loss of or damage to property of the Government occurring after Government acceptance of, and resulting from any defects or deficiencies in, the supplies delivered under the contract [@CL-SUP-9781; @CL-SUP-1182; @CL-SUP-9782].

Under paragraph (a) of the high-value items clause (FAR 52.246-24), except as provided in paragraphs (b) through (e) of the clause, and notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, the contractor shall not be liable for loss of or damage to property of the Government (including the supplies delivered under the contract) that occurs after Government acceptance of the supplies delivered under the contract and results from any defects or deficiencies in the supplies [@CL-SUP-1202; @CL-SUP-1183]. Under paragraph (b), the limitation of liability under paragraph (a) shall not apply when a defect or deficiency in, or the Government's acceptance of, the supplies results from willful misconduct or lack of good faith on the part of any of the contractor's managerial personnel [@CL-SUP-9677]. Under paragraph (c), if the contractor carries insurance, or has established a reserve for self-insurance, covering liability for loss or damage suffered by the Government through purchase or use of the supplies required to be delivered under the contract, the contractor shall be liable to the Government, to the extent of such insurance or reserve, for loss of or damage to property of the Government occurring after Government acceptance of, and resulting from any defects or deficiencies in, the supplies delivered under the contract [@CL-SUP-9678; @CL-SUP-9679]. Under paragraph (d)(1), the clause does not diminish the contractor's obligations, to the extent that they arise otherwise under the contract, relating to correction, repair, replacement, or other relief for any defect or deficiency in supplies delivered under the contract [@CL-SUP-1184]. Under paragraph (d)(2), unless the contract is a cost-reimbursement contract, if loss or damage occurs and correction, repair, or replacement is not feasible or desired by the Government, the contractor shall, as determined by the Contracting Officer, pay the Government the amount it would have cost the contractor to make correction, repair, or replacement before the loss or damage occurred, or provide other equitable relief [@CL-SUP-1203; @CL-SUP-1204]. Under paragraph (e), the clause shall not limit or otherwise affect the Government's rights under clauses, if included in the contract, that cover warranty of technical data, ground and flight risks or aircraft flight risks, or Government property [@CL-SUP-1205].

Under paragraph (a) of the services clause (FAR 52.246-25), except as provided in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the clause, and except to the extent that the contractor is expressly responsible under the contract for deficiencies in the services required to be performed under it (including any materials furnished in conjunction with those services), the contractor shall not be liable for loss of or damage to property of the Government that occurs after Government acceptance of services performed under the contract and results from any defects or deficiencies in the services performed or materials furnished [@CL-SUP-1186; @CL-SUP-9783; @CL-SUP-1185]. Under paragraph (b), the limitation of liability under paragraph (a) shall not apply when a defect or deficiency in, or the Government's acceptance of, services performed or materials furnished results from willful misconduct or lack of good faith on the part of any of the contractor's managerial personnel [@CL-SUP-9683]. Under paragraph (c), if the contractor carries insurance, or has established a reserve for self-insurance, covering liability for loss or damage suffered by the Government through the contractor's performance of services or furnishing of materials under the contract, the contractor shall be liable to the Government, to the extent of such insurance or reserve, for loss of or damage to property of the Government occurring after Government acceptance of, and resulting from any defects and deficiencies in, services performed or materials furnished under the contract [@CL-SUP-9684; @CL-SUP-9685].

## How it works

Subject to each clause's stated exceptions, paragraph (a) of each clause limits the contractor's liability for loss of or damage to property of the Government that occurs after Government acceptance and results from any defects or deficiencies [@CL-SUP-1201; @CL-SUP-1180; @CL-SUP-1202; @CL-SUP-1183; @CL-SUP-1186; @CL-SUP-9783; @CL-SUP-1185]. In the basic clause, the limitation excludes the supplies delivered under the contract, whereas in the high-value items clause, the limitation includes the supplies delivered under the contract [@CL-SUP-1180; @CL-SUP-1183]. In all three clauses, paragraph (b) states the exception for willful misconduct or lack of good faith on the part of any of the contractor's managerial personnel, and paragraph (c) states the liability to the extent of insurance or a reserve for self-insurance [@CL-SUP-1181; @CL-SUP-9781; @CL-SUP-1182; @CL-SUP-9782; @CL-SUP-9677; @CL-SUP-9678; @CL-SUP-9679; @CL-SUP-9683; @CL-SUP-9684; @CL-SUP-9685]. Under paragraph (d)(2) of the high-value items clause, unless the contract is a cost-reimbursement contract, if loss or damage occurs and correction, repair, or replacement is not feasible or desired by the Government, the contractor shall, as determined by the Contracting Officer, pay the Government the amount it would have cost the contractor to make correction, repair, or replacement before the loss or damage occurred, or provide other equitable relief [@CL-SUP-1203; @CL-SUP-1204].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-23 state the limitation of liability in paragraph (a) with its exceptions [@CL-SUP-1201; @CL-SUP-1180; @CL-SUP-3446; @CL-SUP-3447], the managerial misconduct exception in paragraph (b) [@CL-SUP-1181; @CL-SUP-3448], and the insurance or self-insurance reserve liability in paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-9781; @CL-SUP-1182; @CL-SUP-9782; @CL-SUP-3449; @CL-SUP-3450]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-24 state the limitation of liability in paragraph (a) with its exceptions [@CL-SUP-1202; @CL-SUP-1183; @CL-SUP-3451; @CL-SUP-3452], the managerial misconduct exception in paragraph (b) [@CL-SUP-9677; @CL-SUP-9680], the insurance or self-insurance reserve liability in paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-9678; @CL-SUP-9679; @CL-SUP-9681; @CL-SUP-9682], the rule in paragraph (d)(1) that the clause does not diminish the contractor's obligations, to the extent that they arise otherwise under the contract, relating to correction, repair, replacement, or other relief for any defect or deficiency in supplies delivered under the contract [@CL-SUP-1184; @CL-SUP-3453], the lead-in in paragraph (d)(2) for contracts other than cost-reimbursement contracts when loss or damage occurs and correction, repair, or replacement is not feasible or desired by the Government [@CL-SUP-1203; @CL-SUP-3454], and the saving in paragraph (e) for clauses, if included in the contract, that cover warranty of technical data, ground and flight risks or aircraft flight risks, or Government property [@CL-SUP-1205; @CL-SUP-3456]. Under codified FAR 52.246-24(d)(2), the listed relief is to pay the Government the amount it would have cost the contractor to make correction, repair, or replacement before the loss or damage occurred, or to provide other equitable relief [@CL-SUP-1204], and under revised FAR 52.246-24(d)(2), the listed items are to pay the Government the amount it would have cost the contractor to make correction, repair, or replacement before the loss or damage occurred, and to provide other equitable relief, with the RFO text printing no "or" between the two items [@CL-SUP-3455]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-25 state the limitation of liability in paragraph (a) with its exceptions [@CL-SUP-1186; @CL-SUP-9783; @CL-SUP-1185; @CL-SUP-3457; @CL-SUP-3458], the managerial misconduct exception in paragraph (b) [@CL-SUP-9683; @CL-SUP-9686; @CL-SUP-9687], and the insurance or self-insurance reserve liability in paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-9684; @CL-SUP-9685; @CL-SUP-9688; @CL-SUP-9689].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 10 control units, and a defect in one unit, found after acceptance, damages a Government test bench worth 80,000. Under the basic clause, with no managerial misconduct, no insurance and no remedy expressly provided elsewhere in the contract, the contractor is not liable for the bench. The unit itself is not within that limit, so other contract terms govern it. If the contractor carries insurance of 50,000 that covers this liability, it is liable to that extent. The answer: no liability for the bench without insurance, and up to 50,000 with it.

## Never confuse

Under paragraph (d)(1) of the high-value items clause, the clause does not diminish the contractor's obligations, to the extent that they arise otherwise under the contract, relating to correction, repair, replacement, or other relief for any defect or deficiency in supplies delivered under the contract [@CL-SUP-1184]. Under paragraph (a) of the basic clause, the limitation excludes the supplies delivered under the contract, whereas under paragraph (a) of the high-value items clause, the limitation includes the supplies delivered under the contract [@CL-SUP-1180; @CL-SUP-1183]. The warranty clauses are separate, for example [Warranty of supplies of a noncomplex nature](sup-warranty-noncomplex-supplies.md), [Warranty of supplies of a complex nature](sup-warranty-complex-supplies.md), [Warranty of systems and equipment](sup-warranty-systems-and-equipment.md) and [Warranty of services and warranty of construction](sup-warranty-services-and-construction.md). The general policy and prescription for these clauses are on [Contractor liability for Government property loss after acceptance](sup-contractor-liability-after-acceptance.md), and warranty remedies are on [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](sup-warranties.md). Under codified FAR 52.246-24(d)(2), the listed relief is to pay the Government the amount it would have cost the contractor to make correction, repair, or replacement before the loss or damage occurred, or to provide other equitable relief [@CL-SUP-1204], and under revised FAR 52.246-24(d)(2), the listed items are to pay the Government the amount it would have cost the contractor to make correction, repair, or replacement before the loss or damage occurred, and to provide other equitable relief, with the RFO text printing no "or" between the two items [@CL-SUP-3455].
