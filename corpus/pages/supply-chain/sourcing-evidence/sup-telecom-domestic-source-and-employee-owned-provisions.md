## Purpose

Shows what the offeror represents in the covered defense telecommunications provision and in the employee-owned business provision, and what the domestic source restriction limits.

## The rule

Under DFARS 252.204-7016, Covered Defense Telecommunications Equipment or Services—Representation (prescribed in DFARS 204.2105(a)), the offeror represents that it does or does not provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract, or other contractual instrument [@CL-SUP-1653; @CL-SUP-1586]. The offeror shall review the list of excluded parties in the System for Award Management (SAM) (https://www.sam.gov) for entities excluded from receiving federal awards for "covered defense telecommunications equipment or services" [@CL-SUP-1585]. Covered defense telecommunications equipment or services has the meaning provided in the clause DFARS 252.204-7018, Prohibition on the Acquisition of Covered Defense Telecommunications Equipment or Services [@CL-SUP-1584].

Under DFARS 252.206-7000, Domestic Source Restriction (prescribed at DFARS 206.302-3-70), the solicitation is restricted to domestic sources under the authority of 10 USC 3204(a)(3), and foreign sources, except Canadian sources, are not eligible for award [@CL-SUP-1654; @CL-SUP-1587].

Under DFARS 252.270-7000, Pilot Program To Incentivize Contracting With Employee-Owned Businesses—Representation (prescribed in DFARS 270.105(a)), the offeror represents that it is a qualified business [@CL-SUP-1655; @CL-SUP-1589]. Qualified business has the meaning given in the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement 252.270-7002, Pilot Program to Incentivize Contracting with Employee-Owned Businesses, clause of the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1588]. Under DFARS 252.270-7002, qualified business means an S corporation as defined in 26 USC 1361(a)(1) for which 100 percent of the outstanding stock is held through an employee stock ownership plan as defined in 26 USC 4975(e)(7) [@CL-SUP-1665].

## How it works

Under DFARS 252.204-7016, the offeror shall review the list of excluded parties in SAM (https://www.sam.gov) for entities excluded from receiving federal awards for "covered defense telecommunications equipment or services" and represents that it does or does not provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract, or other contractual instrument [@CL-SUP-1585; @CL-SUP-1586]. Under DFARS 252.206-7000, the solicitation is restricted to domestic sources under the authority of 10 USC 3204(a)(3), and foreign sources, except Canadian sources, are not eligible for award [@CL-SUP-1587]. Under DFARS 252.270-7000, the offeror represents that it is a qualified business as defined in the DFARS 252.270-7002, Pilot Program to Incentivize Contracting with Employee-Owned Businesses, clause of the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1588; @CL-SUP-1589; @CL-SUP-1665].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror reviews the excluded parties list, finds nothing, and marks that it does not provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services in performing any contract. On another solicitation, marked as restricted to domestic sources, a foreign firm based in a non-Canadian country is not eligible for award, while a Canadian firm is not barred by that restriction.

## Never confuse

In DFARS 252.204-7016, the offeror represents that it does or does not provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract, or other contractual instrument, whereas in DFARS 252.270-7000, the offeror represents that it is a qualified business [@CL-SUP-1586; @CL-SUP-1589]. Under DFARS 252.206-7000, the solicitation is restricted to domestic sources under the authority of 10 USC 3204(a)(3), and foreign sources, except Canadian sources, are not eligible for award [@CL-SUP-1587]. See [Prohibition on covered defense telecommunications equipment or services](../../compliance/trade-cyber/cmp-covered-defense-telecommunications-equipment-prohibition.md) and [Annual representations and certifications provision](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-annual-representations-provision-paragraphs.md).
