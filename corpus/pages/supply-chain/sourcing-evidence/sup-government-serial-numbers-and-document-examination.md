## Purpose

Shows how a contractor marks Government-assigned serial numbers on major end items, what it does with the unique item identifier, and where cited specifications and drawings may be examined when they are not distributed.

| Applies to | Contracts that use the serial numbers clause as prescribed in section 211.274-5(b) of the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement [@CL-SUP-1647], for the major end items specified by line item in the Schedule [@CL-SUP-1554]; solicitations that use the examination provision as prescribed in section 211.204(c) [@CL-SUP-1648] and cite documents available only for examination [@CL-SUP-1557] |

A Government-assigned serial number is a combination of letters or numerals in a fixed human-readable information format that conveys information about a major end item and is provided to a contractor by the requiring activity with accompanying technical data instructions for marking it on major end items to be delivered to the Government [@CL-SUP-1552; @CL-SUP-4042]. Major end items include aircraft, ships, boats, motorized wheeled, tracked, and towed vehicles for use on highway or rough terrain, weapon and missile end items, ammunition, and sets, assemblies, or end items having a major end item as a component [@CL-SUP-1553].

The contractor shall mark the Government-assigned serial numbers on the major end items specified by line item in the Schedule, following the technical instructions for placement and method of application identified in the contract terms and conditions [@CL-SUP-1554]. It shall register the serial number along with the major end item's unique item identifier at the time of delivery, in accordance with paragraph (d) of the clause at DFARS 252.211-7003 [@CL-SUP-1555], which is covered in [Item unique identification and valuation clause](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-dfars-item-unique-identification.md). It shall establish the unique item identifier for major end items for use throughout their life, and it may elect, but is not required, to use the serial number to construct it [@CL-SUP-1556].

Under the examination provision, the specifications, standards, plans, drawings, data item descriptions, and other pertinent documents cited in the solicitation are not available for distribution but may be examined at the location the provision inserts [@CL-SUP-1557].

## How it works

The requiring activity provides the serial number [@CL-SUP-1552], and the contractor applies the mark as the Schedule line item and the contract's technical instructions direct [@CL-SUP-1554]. At delivery the contractor registers the serial number with the unique item identifier [@CL-SUP-1555]. The contractor establishes the identifier for use throughout the life of the major end item, and may elect, but is not required, to use the serial number to construct it [@CL-SUP-1556]. The examination provision states that the cited documents are not available for distribution but may be examined at the location it inserts [@CL-SUP-1557].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Schedule line item calls for three trucks, and the requiring activity sends a serial number format with placement instructions. The contractor marks each truck as the instructions say and registers each serial number with the truck's unique item identifier at delivery. It builds the identifier from its own data rather than from the serial number, which the clause allows.

## Never confuse

The Government-assigned serial number comes from the requiring activity, while the contractor establishes the unique item identifier [@CL-SUP-1552; @CL-SUP-1556]. They are registered together at delivery [@CL-SUP-1555]. The registration is made in accordance with paragraph (d) of the clause at DFARS 252.211-7003 [@CL-SUP-1555], covered in [Item unique identification and valuation clause](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-dfars-item-unique-identification.md). The examination provision concerns documents cited in a solicitation [@CL-SUP-1557], while the marking duty is in the serial numbers clause [@CL-SUP-1554].
