## Purpose

Gives the meaning of the terms that the quality assurance part of the FAR defines. They decide whether supplies or services conform, who accepts them and how serious a nonconformance is. The duties that use these terms are in [contract quality requirements](sup-contract-quality-requirements.md) and [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-3980] |
| Codified citation | FAR 46.101 [@CL-SUP-3980]; FAR 46.103 [@CL-SUP-3906; @CL-SUP-3911]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-SUP-9554; @CL-SUP-9555] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-SUP-9595; @CL-SUP-9596] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-3980] |
| Applies to | The defined terms as used in FAR part 46 [@CL-SUP-3980] |

FAR 46.101 defines terms as used in FAR part 46 [@CL-SUP-3980]. FAR 2.101 defines inspection as examining and testing supplies or services (including, when appropriate, raw materials, components, and intermediate assemblies) to determine whether they conform to contract requirements [@CL-SUP-9554], and defines a latent defect as a defect that exists at the time of acceptance but cannot be discovered by a reasonable inspection [@CL-SUP-9555]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines inspection as examining and testing supplies or services (including, when appropriate, raw materials, components, and intermediate assemblies) to determine whether they conform to contract requirements [@CL-SUP-9595], and defines a latent defect as a defect that exists at the time of acceptance but cannot be discovered by a reasonable inspection [@CL-SUP-9596].

## How it works

Acceptance means the act of an authorized representative of the Government by which the Government, for itself or as agent of another, assumes ownership of existing identified supplies tendered or approves specific services rendered as partial or complete performance of the contract [@CL-SUP-3880]. Conditional acceptance means acceptance of supplies or services that do not conform to contract quality requirements, or are otherwise incomplete, that the contractor is required to correct or otherwise complete by a specified date [@CL-SUP-3881]. See [rejection of nonconforming supplies](../../operating-model/corrective-action/opm-rejection-of-nonconforming-supplies.md).

Critical nonconformance means a nonconformance that is likely to result in hazardous or unsafe conditions for individuals using, maintaining, or depending upon the supplies or services, or is likely to prevent performance of a vital agency mission [@CL-SUP-3888]. Major nonconformance means a nonconformance, other than critical, that is likely to result in failure of the supplies or services, or to materially reduce the usability of the supplies or services for their intended purpose [@CL-SUP-3892]. Minor nonconformance means a nonconformance that is not likely to materially reduce the usability of the supplies or services for their intended purpose, or is a departure from established standards having little bearing on the effective use or operation of the supplies or services [@CL-SUP-3893]. Contracting offices are responsible for receiving from the activity responsible for technical requirements any specifications for inspection, testing, and other contract quality requirements essential to ensure the integrity of the supplies or services, and for ensuring that nonconformances are identified and establishing the significance of a nonconformance when considering the acceptability of supplies or services which do not meet contract requirements; see [quality assurance policy and responsibilities](sup-quality-assurance-policy-and-responsibilities.md) [@CL-SUP-3906; @CL-SUP-3911].

Government contract quality assurance means the various functions, including inspection, performed by the Government to determine whether a contractor has fulfilled the contract obligations pertaining to quality and quantity [@CL-SUP-3891]. Testing means that element of inspection that determines the properties or elements, including functional operation of supplies or their components, by the application of established scientific principles and procedures [@CL-SUP-3897].

Counterfeit item means an unlawful or unauthorized reproduction, substitution, or alteration that has been knowingly mismarked, misidentified, or otherwise misrepresented to be an authentic, unmodified item from the original manufacturer or a source with the express written authority of the original manufacturer or current design activity, including an authorized aftermarket manufacturer [@CL-SUP-3884; @CL-SUP-3885]. Unlawful or unauthorized substitution includes used items represented as new, or the false identification of grade, serial number, lot number, date code, or performance characteristics [@CL-SUP-3886]. Suspect counterfeit item means an item for which credible evidence (including, but not limited to, visual inspection or testing) provides reasonable doubt that the item is authentic [@CL-SUP-3896].

Design activity means an organization, Government or contractor, that has responsibility for the design and configuration of an item, including the preparation or maintenance of design documents; design activity could be the original organization, or an organization to which design responsibility has been transferred [@CL-SUP-3889; @CL-SUP-3890]. Off-the-shelf item means an item produced and placed in stock by a contractor, or stocked by a distributor, before receiving orders or contracts for its sale; the item may be commercial or produced to military or Federal specifications or description [@CL-SUP-3894]. Patent defect means any defect which exists at the time of acceptance and is not a latent defect [@CL-SUP-3895]. For subcontractor, FAR 46.101 cross-references FAR 44.101 [@CL-SUP-3981].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier tenders 100 pumps. Inspection finds 3 pumps with a nonconformance that is likely to result in hazardous or unsafe conditions for individuals using, maintaining, or depending upon the supplies; each is a critical nonconformance [@CL-SUP-3888]. It finds 5 pumps with a nameplate that is a departure from established standards having little bearing on the effective use or operation of the supplies; each is a minor nonconformance [@CL-SUP-3893]. The other 92 pumps conform. For the 3 critical units, the contracting officer ordinarily must give the contractor an opportunity to correct or replace nonconforming supplies or services when this can be accomplished within the required delivery schedule [@CL-OPM-0063]. In situations not covered by FAR 46.407(b), the contracting officer ordinarily must reject supplies or services when the nonconformance is critical or major or the supplies or services are otherwise incomplete [@CL-OPM-0065]; however, there may be circumstances (for example, reasons of economy or urgency) when the contracting officer determines acceptance or conditional acceptance of supplies or services is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-OPM-0066]. If the 5 minor units are accepted under conditional acceptance, the contractor is required to correct or otherwise complete them by a specified date [@CL-SUP-3881].

## Never confuse

Critical item means an item, the failure of which is likely to result in hazardous or unsafe conditions for individuals using, maintaining, or depending upon the item, or is likely to prevent performance of a vital agency mission, whereas critical nonconformance means a nonconformance that is likely to result in hazardous or unsafe conditions for individuals using, maintaining, or depending upon the supplies or services, or is likely to prevent performance of a vital agency mission [@CL-SUP-3887; @CL-SUP-3888]. Counterfeit item means an unlawful or unauthorized reproduction, substitution, or alteration that has been knowingly mismarked, misidentified, or otherwise misrepresented to be an authentic, unmodified item from the original manufacturer or a source with the express written authority of the original manufacturer or current design activity (including an authorized aftermarket manufacturer), whereas suspect counterfeit item means an item for which credible evidence (including, but not limited to, visual inspection or testing) provides reasonable doubt that the item is authentic [@CL-SUP-3884; @CL-SUP-3885; @CL-SUP-3896]. Acceptance means the act of an authorized representative of the Government by which the Government, for itself or as agent of another, assumes ownership of existing identified supplies tendered or approves specific services rendered as partial or complete performance of the contract, whereas conditional acceptance means acceptance of supplies or services that do not conform to contract quality requirements, or are otherwise incomplete, that the contractor is required to correct or otherwise complete by a specified date [@CL-SUP-3880; @CL-SUP-3881]. Related: [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md).
