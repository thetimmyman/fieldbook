## Purpose

Shows when the Government performs quality assurance at the subcontract level and what that does not relieve the prime contractor of.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0869] |
| Codified citation | FAR 46.405 [@CL-SUP-0869; @CL-SUP-0870; @CL-SUP-0871; @CL-SUP-0972] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0869] |
| Applies to | Government quality assurance on subcontracted supplies or services [@CL-SUP-0869] |

Government contract quality assurance on subcontracted supplies or services shall be performed only when required in the Government's interest [@CL-SUP-0869]. The Government shall perform quality assurance at the subcontract level when one of the listed alternatives applies. One is an item that is to be shipped from the subcontractor's plant to the using activity where inspection at source is required [@CL-SUP-0871]; another is that it is otherwise required by the contract or determined to be in the Government's interest [@CL-SUP-9787].

Government quality assurance at the subcontract level does not relieve the prime contractor of any responsibilities under the contract [@CL-SUP-0870].

## How it works

Government quality assurance reaches a subcontractor's plant only when the Government's interest requires it [@CL-SUP-0869]. The listed circumstances include inspection at source of an item shipped from the subcontractor's plant to the using activity [@CL-SUP-0871]. That assurance does not relieve the prime contractor of any responsibilities under the contract [@CL-SUP-0870]. Statements and contract terms about that assurance shall be worded so as not to do any of the listed things, one of which is to affect the contractual relationship between the prime contractor and the Government, or between the prime contractor and the subcontractor [@CL-SUP-0972]. This section does not itemize the prime contractor's responsibilities, so they come from the prime contract's own terms; they include ensuring that vendors have an acceptable quality control system, which is on [Contractor responsibilities for quality](sup-contractor-quality-responsibilities.md) [@CL-SUP-0851].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor buys 500 valves from a subcontractor, and the contract calls for Government inspection at source before shipment to the using activity. The Government inspector passes the lot. Later the valves are found nonconforming. The answer: the source inspection does not relieve the prime contractor of its responsibilities under the contract, so the prime contractor still answers to the Government.

## Never confuse

Government inspection at a subcontractor's plant is not a release of the prime contractor [@CL-SUP-0870]. The Government's own inspection and its places are on [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md). Control over subcontracting is a separate matter, covered in [Contractor purchasing system clause](../procure-to-pay/sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md) and [Evaluation of subcontract consent requests](../procure-to-pay/sup-subcontract-consent-evaluation.md).
