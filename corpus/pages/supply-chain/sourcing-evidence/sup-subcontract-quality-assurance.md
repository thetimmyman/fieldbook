## Purpose

Shows when the Government performs quality assurance at the subcontract level and what that does not relieve the prime contractor of.

## The rule

| Field | Value |

| Codified citation | FAR 46.405 and 46.105 [@CL-SUP-0869; @CL-SUP-0870; @CL-SUP-0871; @CL-SUP-0294; @CL-SUP-9787; @CL-SUP-0972; @CL-SUP-0295; @CL-SUP-0850; @CL-SUP-0851] |

| Applies to | Government quality assurance on subcontracted supplies or services [@CL-SUP-0869] |

Government contract quality assurance on subcontracted supplies or services shall be performed only when required in the Government's interest [@CL-SUP-0869]. The Government shall perform quality assurance at the subcontract level when (1) the item is to be shipped from the subcontractor's plant to the using activity and inspection at source is required, (2) the conditions for quality assurance at source are applicable (see FAR 46.402), (3) the contract specifies that certain quality assurance functions, which can be performed only at the subcontractor's plant, are to be performed by the Government, or (4) it is otherwise required by the contract or determined to be in the Government's interest [@CL-SUP-0871; @CL-SUP-0294; @CL-SUP-9787].

Government contract quality assurance on subcontracted supplies or services, performed for determining the conformance of subcontracted supplies or services with contract requirements or to satisfy one or more of the factors included in paragraph (b), does not relieve the prime contractor of any responsibilities under the contract [@CL-SUP-0869; @CL-SUP-0870].

## How it works

Government contract quality assurance on subcontracted supplies or services shall be performed only when required in the Government's interest, and it does not relieve the prime contractor of any responsibilities under the contract [@CL-SUP-0869; @CL-SUP-0870]. Statements and contract terms and conditions relating to Government quality assurance actions at the subcontract level shall be worded so as not to (1) affect the contractual relationship between the prime contractor and the Government, or between the prime contractor and the subcontractor, (2) establish a contractual relationship between the Government and the subcontractor, or (3) constitute a waiver of the Government's right to accept or reject the supplies or services [@CL-SUP-0972; @CL-SUP-0295]. Under FAR 46.105(a), the contractor is responsible for carrying out its obligations under the contract by controlling the quality of supplies or services, tendering to the Government for acceptance only those supplies or services that conform to contract requirements, and ensuring that vendors or suppliers of raw materials, parts, components, subassemblies, etc., have an acceptable quality control system [@CL-SUP-0850; @CL-SUP-0851]. See [Contractor responsibilities for quality](sup-contractor-quality-responsibilities.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor buys 500 valves from a subcontractor, and the contract calls for Government inspection at source before shipment to the using activity. The Government inspector passes the lot. Later the valves are found nonconforming. The answer: the source inspection does not relieve the prime contractor of its responsibilities under the contract, so the prime contractor still answers to the Government.

## Never confuse

Government contract quality assurance on subcontracted supplies or services does not relieve the prime contractor of any responsibilities under the contract [@CL-SUP-0869; @CL-SUP-0870], and statements and contract terms and conditions relating to Government quality assurance actions at the subcontract level shall be worded so as not to affect the contractual relationship between the prime contractor and the Government or between the prime contractor and the subcontractor, establish a contractual relationship between the Government and the subcontractor, or constitute a waiver of the Government's right to accept or reject the supplies or services [@CL-SUP-0972; @CL-SUP-0295]. See [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md), [Contractor purchasing system clause](../procure-to-pay/sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md) and [Evaluation of subcontract consent requests](../procure-to-pay/sup-subcontract-consent-evaluation.md).
