## Purpose

Shows the Government's inspection rights and the correction rule in the services inspection clause for cost-reimbursement contracts, and what the printed text does and does not say about the cost of reperformance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1090] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-5 [@CL-SUP-1090] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-5 [@CL-SUP-3185] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1090] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Inspection of Services—Cost-Reimbursement clause [@CL-SUP-1090]; the clause is directed for solicitations and contracts for services, or supplies that involve the furnishing of services, when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-1133] |

The contractor must provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government that covers the services under the contract [@CL-SUP-1090]. The Government has the right to inspect and test all services called for by the contract, to the extent practicable, at all places and times during the term of the contract [@CL-SUP-1091].

If any services performed do not conform with contract requirements, the Government may require the contractor to perform them again in conformity with contract requirements for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092]. When the defects cannot be corrected by reperformance, the Government may require the necessary action to ensure future performance conforms and may reduce any fee payable under the contract to reflect the reduced value [@CL-SUP-1093].

The printed clause text says reperformance is for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092]. It does not state a rule on reimbursing the cost of reperformance, so the printed section does not establish one.

## How it works

The contractor keeps an inspection system acceptable to the Government [@CL-SUP-1090], and the right to inspect and test applies during the term of the contract [@CL-SUP-1091]. A nonconforming service is performed again for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092]. Where reperformance cannot correct the defects, the remedy reaches the fee: the Government may reduce any fee payable [@CL-SUP-1093]. The clause lists further remedies if the contractor fails to promptly perform again or to take the action necessary to ensure future conformity [@CL-SUP-1094]. One is that the Government performs the services and reduces any fee payable by an equitable amount; another is termination for default [@CL-SUP-1095].

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the contractor's duty to provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government [@CL-SUP-1090] [@CL-SUP-3185] and the Government's right to inspect and test all services during the term of the contract [@CL-SUP-1091] [@CL-SUP-3231]. It keeps reperformance of nonconforming services for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092] [@CL-SUP-3232]. When defects cannot be corrected by reperformance, it keeps the option to require action to ensure future conformity [@CL-SUP-1093] [@CL-SUP-3233] and the option to reduce any fee payable [@CL-SUP-1093] [@CL-SUP-3234]. It keeps the listed remedies if the contractor fails to promptly perform again or take the necessary action [@CL-SUP-1094] [@CL-SUP-1095] [@CL-SUP-3235] [@CL-SUP-3236].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement services contract carries a fixed fee of 50,000. One task is found nonconforming and is performed again. No additional fee is paid for the second performance, so the fee stays at 50,000. The source does not say how the cost of the second performance is reimbursed. If a defect could not be corrected by reperformance, the Government could reduce the fee to reflect the reduced value, for example to 48,000.

## Never confuse

The remedy here reaches any fee payable, while the fixed-price services clause on [Inspection of services, fixed-price](sup-inspection-of-services-fixed-price.md) reduces the contract price [@CL-SUP-1093; @CL-SUP-1087]. The supplies clause for cost-reimbursement contracts, on [Inspection of supplies, cost-reimbursement](sup-inspection-of-supplies-cost-reimbursement.md), instead includes correction cost in allowable cost with no added fee [@CL-SUP-1080], except that, under its paragraph (h), the Government may at any time require the contractor to correct or replace nonconforming supplies, without cost to the Government, if the nonconformances are due to fraud, lack of good faith or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or to the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of its managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-1081; @CL-SUP-1131]. The services clause text states a no-additional-fee rule for reperformance [@CL-SUP-1092], and the printed clause has no allowable-cost rule for it. The contract type framework is on [Cost-reimbursement contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-reimbursement-contracts.md), and inspection places and methods are on [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md).
