## Purpose

Shows the Government's inspection rights, the reperformance rule, and the remedies when defects cannot be corrected by reperformance or the contractor fails to act promptly under the services inspection clause for cost-reimbursement contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1090] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-5 [@CL-SUP-1090] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-5 [@CL-SUP-3185] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1090] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Inspection of Services—Cost-Reimbursement clause [@CL-SUP-1090]; the clause is directed for solicitations and contracts for services, or supplies that involve the furnishing of services, when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-SUP-1133] |

The contractor shall provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government covering the services under the contract [@CL-SUP-1090]. The Government has the right to inspect and test all services called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times during the term of the contract [@CL-SUP-1091].

If any of the services performed do not conform with contract requirements, the Government may require the contractor to perform the services again in conformity with contract requirements, for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092]. When the defects in services cannot be corrected by reperformance, the Government may require the contractor to take necessary action to ensure that future performance conforms to contract requirements and reduce any fee payable under the contract to reflect the reduced value of the services performed [@CL-SUP-1093; @CL-SUP-4167].

## How it works

The contractor shall provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government covering the services under the contract [@CL-SUP-1090], and the Government has the right to inspect and test all services called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times during the term of the contract [@CL-SUP-1091]. If any of the services performed do not conform with contract requirements, the Government may require the contractor to perform the services again in conformity with contract requirements, for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092]. When the defects in services cannot be corrected by reperformance, the Government may require the contractor to take necessary action to ensure that future performance conforms to contract requirements and reduce any fee payable under the contract to reflect the reduced value of the services performed [@CL-SUP-1093; @CL-SUP-4167]. If the contractor fails to promptly perform the services again or take the action necessary to ensure future performance in conformity with contract requirements, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, perform the services and reduce any fee payable by an amount that is equitable under the circumstances, or terminate the contract for default [@CL-SUP-1094; @CL-SUP-1095].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Both the codified and revised FAR 52.246-5 require the contractor to provide and maintain an inspection system acceptable to the Government covering the services under the contract [@CL-SUP-1090; @CL-SUP-3185] and give the Government the right to inspect and test all services called for by the contract, to the extent practicable at all places and times during the term of the contract [@CL-SUP-1091; @CL-SUP-3231]. Both provide that if any of the services performed do not conform with contract requirements, the Government may require the contractor to perform the services again in conformity with contract requirements, for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092; @CL-SUP-3232], and that when the defects in services cannot be corrected by reperformance, the Government may require the contractor to take necessary action to ensure that future performance conforms to contract requirements and reduce any fee payable under the contract to reflect the reduced value of the services performed [@CL-SUP-1093; @CL-SUP-4167; @CL-SUP-3233; @CL-SUP-3234]. Both provide that if the contractor fails to promptly perform the services again or take the action necessary to ensure future performance in conformity with contract requirements, the Government may, by contract or otherwise, perform the services and reduce any fee payable by an amount that is equitable under the circumstances, or terminate the contract for default [@CL-SUP-1094; @CL-SUP-1095; @CL-SUP-3235; @CL-SUP-3236].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement services contract carries a fixed fee of 50,000. One task is found nonconforming and is performed again. No additional fee is paid for the second performance, so the fee stays at 50,000. If a defect could not be corrected by reperformance, the Government could require action to ensure future conformity and reduce the fee to reflect the reduced value of the services performed, for example to 48,000.

## Never confuse

When the defects in services cannot be corrected by reperformance, FAR 52.246-5 provides that the Government may require the contractor to take necessary action to ensure that future performance conforms to contract requirements and reduce any fee payable under the contract to reflect the reduced value of the services performed, whereas the fixed-price services clause on [Inspection of services, fixed-price](sup-inspection-of-services-fixed-price.md) provides that the Government may require the contractor to take necessary action to ensure that future performance conforms to contract requirements and reduce the contract price to reflect the reduced value of the services performed [@CL-SUP-1093; @CL-SUP-4167; @CL-SUP-1087]. Under FAR 52.246-5, if any of the services performed do not conform with contract requirements, the Government may require the contractor to perform the services again in conformity with contract requirements, for no additional fee [@CL-SUP-1092]. Under the supplies clause for cost-reimbursement contracts on [Inspection of supplies, cost-reimbursement](sup-inspection-of-supplies-cost-reimbursement.md), except as otherwise provided in its paragraph (h), the cost of replacement or correction shall be included in allowable cost, determined as provided in the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, but no additional fee shall be paid [@CL-SUP-1080], and under its paragraph (h), notwithstanding paragraphs (f) and (g), the Government may at any time require the contractor to correct or replace, without cost to the Government, nonconforming supplies, if the nonconformances are due to one of two alternative causes: fraud, lack of good faith, or willful misconduct on the part of the contractor's managerial personnel, or the conduct of one or more of the contractor's employees selected or retained by the contractor after any of the contractor's managerial personnel has reasonable grounds to believe that the employee is habitually careless or unqualified [@CL-SUP-9778; @CL-SUP-1081; @CL-SUP-4169; @CL-SUP-1131]. The contract type framework is on [Cost-reimbursement contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-reimbursement-contracts.md), and inspection places and methods are on [Government contract quality assurance](sup-government-contract-quality-assurance.md).
