## Purpose

Shows the new-material rule and its exceptions, how used or reconditioned material is proposed, and what an offer of an equal product must show.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1195] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.211-5 and 52.211-6 [@CL-SUP-1195; @CL-SUP-1196; @CL-SUP-1197; @CL-SUP-1198; @CL-SUP-1199; @CL-SUP-1200; @CL-SUP-1224] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.211-5 [@CL-SUP-3459; @CL-SUP-9331; @CL-SUP-3460; @CL-SUP-3461; @CL-SUP-3462]. No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.211-6, which the RFO text of part 52 prints as reserved [@CL-SUP-3463] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1195] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the material requirements clause, and solicitations that include the brand name or equal provision [@CL-SUP-1195; @CL-SUP-1197] |

Unless the contract otherwise requires virgin material or supplies composed of or manufactured from virgin material, the contractor shall provide supplies that are new, reconditioned, or remanufactured, as the clause defines those terms [@CL-SUP-1195]. A proposal to provide used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies shall include a detailed description of those supplies and shall be submitted to the contracting officer for approval [@CL-SUP-1196]. Used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies, or unused former Government surplus property, may be used in contract performance if the contractor has proposed the use of such supplies and the contracting officer has authorized their use [@CL-SUP-1224]. The source text states no separate notice period for used, reconditioned or remanufactured supplies; it calls for a proposal [@CL-SUP-1196].

To be considered for award, offers of equal products, including equal products of the brand name manufacturer, must meet the salient physical, functional, or performance characteristic specified in the solicitation, as one listed requirement [@CL-SUP-1197]. Two further listed requirements are that an offer of an equal product clearly identify the item by brand name, if any, and by make or model number, and include descriptive literature or a clear reference to previously furnished descriptive data available to the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-1198]. A further listed requirement is that an offer of an equal product clearly describe any modifications the offeror plans to make to conform to the solicitation requirements and mark the descriptive material to show them [@CL-SUP-1199].

## How it works

The material requirements clause sets a default of new, reconditioned or remanufactured supplies [@CL-SUP-1195], and it routes used, reconditioned or remanufactured material through a proposal and approval [@CL-SUP-1196; @CL-SUP-1224]. The brand name or equal provision tells offerors what an offer of an equal product must show to be considered for award [@CL-SUP-1197; @CL-SUP-1198; @CL-SUP-1199]. Unless the offeror clearly indicates in its offer that the product is an equal product, the offeror shall provide the brand name product referenced in the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1200]. The printed text also covers what a proposal for unused former Government surplus property must describe and how the contracting officer evaluates equal products, and this card does not restate those terms.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of 52.211-5 prints no paragraph that sets the default of new, reconditioned or remanufactured supplies: its paragraph (a) defines only Reconditioned and Remanufactured [@CL-SUP-3459; @CL-SUP-9331], and its paragraph (b) is the proposal paragraph for unused former Government surplus property [@CL-SUP-3460; @CL-SUP-9331], where the codified paragraph (b) sets that default [@CL-SUP-1195]. The RFO text prints the proposal and approval rule for used, reconditioned or remanufactured supplies as paragraph (c), where codified paragraph (d) has it [@CL-SUP-3461; @CL-SUP-1196], and prints the authorization rule as paragraph (d), where codified paragraph (e) has it [@CL-SUP-3462; @CL-SUP-1224]. The RFO text of part 52 prints section 52.211-6 as reserved [@CL-SUP-3463], and the codified section 52.211-6 is the brand name or equal provision [@CL-SUP-1197].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation lists a pump by brand and model number, or equal. An offeror proposes a different make, names the brand and model, attaches a data sheet, and describes a change to the mounting plate, marking the sheet. Another offeror says nothing about being an equal. A third offeror proposes remanufactured motors and sends a detailed description to the contracting officer. The answer: the first offer shows what an equal must show, the second is held to the brand name product, and the third needs approval first. The first two offers illustrate the codified provision at section 52.211-6, which the RFO text of part 52 prints as reserved [@CL-SUP-3463]. The third offeror's proposal and approval step is in paragraph (d) of the codified material requirements clause [@CL-SUP-1196], and the RFO text prints that step as paragraph (c) [@CL-SUP-3461].

## Never confuse

The material requirements rule is a contract clause about what supplies the contractor provides [@CL-SUP-1195], and the brand name or equal rule is a solicitation provision about how an offer of an equal product is considered [@CL-SUP-1197]. An offer that does not clearly indicate that the product is an equal carries the duty to provide the brand name product [@CL-SUP-1200]. Reconditioned and remanufactured supplies are named in both the default rule and the proposal-and-approval rule [@CL-SUP-1195; @CL-SUP-1196]. The codified clause sets the default of new, reconditioned or remanufactured supplies [@CL-SUP-1195], but the RFO clause prints no paragraph with that default and defines only Reconditioned and Remanufactured [@CL-SUP-3459; @CL-SUP-9331].

Whether the contract or solicitation carries these terms, whether it requires virgin material, and how used or reconditioned material proposals and equal offers are documented. [@Q-004]
