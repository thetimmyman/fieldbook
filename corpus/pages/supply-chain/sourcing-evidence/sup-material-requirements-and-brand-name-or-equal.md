## Purpose

Shows the new-material rule and its exceptions, how used or reconditioned material is proposed, and what an offer of an equal product must show.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1195] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.211-5 and 52.211-6 [@CL-SUP-1195; @CL-SUP-1196; @CL-SUP-1197; @CL-SUP-1198; @CL-SUP-1199; @CL-SUP-1200; @CL-SUP-1224] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.211-5 [@CL-SUP-3459; @CL-SUP-9331; @CL-SUP-3460; @CL-SUP-3461; @CL-SUP-3462]; RFO FAR 52.211-6 is reserved [@CL-SUP-3463] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-1195] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the material requirements clause, and solicitations that include the brand name or equal provision [@CL-SUP-1195; @CL-SUP-1197] |

Unless the contract otherwise requires virgin material or supplies composed of or manufactured from virgin material, the contractor shall provide supplies that are new, reconditioned, or remanufactured, as defined in the clause [@CL-SUP-1195]. A proposal to provide used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies shall include a detailed description of such supplies and shall be submitted to the Contracting Officer for approval [@CL-SUP-1196]. Used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies, or unused former Government surplus property, may be used in contract performance if the contractor has proposed the use of such supplies, and the Contracting Officer has authorized their use [@CL-SUP-1224].

To be considered for award, offers of equal products, including equal products of the brand name manufacturer, must meet all four requirements in paragraph (b) of the provision: (1) meet the salient physical, functional, or performance characteristic specified in the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1197]; (2) clearly identify the item by brand name, if any, and make or model number, and (3) include descriptive literature such as illustrations, drawings, or a clear reference to previously furnished descriptive data or information available to the Contracting Officer [@CL-SUP-1198]; and (4) clearly describe any modifications the offeror plans to make in a product to make it conform to the solicitation requirements, and mark any descriptive material to clearly show the modifications [@CL-SUP-1199].

## How it works

Unless the contract otherwise requires virgin material or supplies composed of or manufactured from virgin material, the contractor shall provide supplies that are new, reconditioned, or remanufactured, as defined in the clause [@CL-SUP-1195]. A proposal to provide used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies shall include a detailed description of such supplies and shall be submitted to the Contracting Officer for approval, and used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies, or unused former Government surplus property, may be used in contract performance if the contractor has proposed the use of such supplies and the Contracting Officer has authorized their use [@CL-SUP-1196; @CL-SUP-1224]. Under the brand name or equal provision, offers of equal products, including equal products of the brand name manufacturer, must meet all four requirements in paragraph (b) to be considered for award [@CL-SUP-1197; @CL-SUP-1198; @CL-SUP-1199]. Unless the offeror clearly indicates in its offer that the product being offered is an equal product, the offeror shall provide the brand name product referenced in the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1200].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under revised FAR 52.211-5, paragraph (a) defines Reconditioned as restored to the original normal operating condition by readjustments and material replacement and Remanufactured as factory rebuilt to original specifications, and is followed by paragraph (b), under which a proposal to provide unused former Government surplus property shall include a complete description of the material, the quantity, the name of the Government agency from which acquired, and the date of acquisition [@CL-SUP-3459; @CL-SUP-9331; @CL-SUP-3460]. Under codified FAR 52.211-5(b), unless the contract otherwise requires virgin material or supplies composed of or manufactured from virgin material, the contractor shall provide supplies that are new, reconditioned, or remanufactured, as defined in the clause [@CL-SUP-1195]. Both codified FAR 52.211-5(d) and revised FAR 52.211-5(c) provide that a proposal to provide used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies shall include a detailed description of such supplies and shall be submitted to the Contracting Officer for approval [@CL-SUP-1196; @CL-SUP-3461], and both codified FAR 52.211-5(e) and revised FAR 52.211-5(d) provide that used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies, or unused former Government surplus property, may be used in contract performance if the contractor has proposed the use of such supplies, and the Contracting Officer has authorized their use [@CL-SUP-1224; @CL-SUP-3462]. Codified FAR 52.211-6 is the brand name or equal solicitation provision [@CL-SUP-1197], and revised FAR 52.211-6 is reserved [@CL-SUP-3463].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation lists a pump by brand and model number, or equal. An offeror proposes a different make, names the brand and model, attaches a data sheet, and describes a change to the mounting plate, marking the sheet. Another offeror says nothing about being an equal. A third offeror proposes remanufactured motors and sends a detailed description to the contracting officer. The answer: the first offer shows what an equal must show, the second is held to the brand name product, and the third needs approval first.

## Never confuse

Under codified FAR 52.211-5(b), unless the contract otherwise requires virgin material or supplies composed of or manufactured from virgin material, the contractor shall provide supplies that are new, reconditioned, or remanufactured, as defined in the clause [@CL-SUP-1195], whereas under codified FAR 52.211-6(b), to be considered for award, offers of equal products, including equal products of the brand name manufacturer, must meet the salient physical, functional, or performance characteristic specified in the solicitation and the other requirements in paragraph (b) [@CL-SUP-1197; @CL-SUP-1198; @CL-SUP-1199]. Unless the offeror clearly indicates in its offer that the product being offered is an equal product, the offeror shall provide the brand name product referenced in the solicitation [@CL-SUP-1200]. Under codified FAR 52.211-5(b) and (d), even though the contractor shall provide supplies that are new, reconditioned, or remanufactured unless virgin material is required, a proposal to provide used, reconditioned, or remanufactured supplies shall include a detailed description of such supplies and shall be submitted to the Contracting Officer for approval [@CL-SUP-1195; @CL-SUP-1196]. Under revised FAR 52.211-5(a) and (b), paragraph (a) defines Reconditioned and Remanufactured and is followed directly by paragraph (b) on a proposal to provide unused former Government surplus property [@CL-SUP-3459; @CL-SUP-9331].

Whether the contract or solicitation carries these terms, whether it requires virgin material, and how used or reconditioned material proposals and equal offers are documented. [@Q-004]
