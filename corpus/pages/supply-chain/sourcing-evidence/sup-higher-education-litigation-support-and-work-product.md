## Purpose

Shows what the offeror represents about institutions of higher education that host a Confucius Institute, when the Government may disclose information to litigation support contractors, and what control Government personnel keep over their work product.

By submission of an offer, the offeror represents either that it is not an institution of higher education that hosts a Confucius Institute or that it has obtained a waiver approved by the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering [@CL-SUP-1592]. The restriction paragraph states that, as required by section 1062 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2021, DoD may not award a contract with any institution of higher education that hosts a Confucius Institute [@CL-SUP-1666]. The restriction paragraph also states that section 1062 prohibits DoD from providing funding to any United States institution of higher education hosting a Confucius Institute unless that institution receives a waiver from the Department of Defense Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering [@CL-SUP-1590]. The waiver authority terminates on October 1, 2026, and any waiver issued will not apply on or after that date [@CL-SUP-1591].

The provision defines a Confucius Institute as one of two alternatives. One is any program that receives funding or support from the Chinese International Education Foundation or from the Center for Language Exchange Cooperation of the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China [@CL-SUP-1667]. The other is any cultural institute directly or indirectly funded by the government of the People's Republic of China [@CL-SUP-1668].

The defense supplement's litigation support subpart prescribes policies and procedures for the release and safeguarding of information to litigation support contractors and implements the requirements of section 129d of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-SUP-4121]. The litigation support notice clause defines a litigation support contractor as a contractor, including its experts, technical consultants, subcontractors, and suppliers, providing litigation support under a contract that contains the clause at 252.204-7014, Limitations on the Use or Disclosure of Information by Litigation Support Contractors [@CL-SUP-1670; @CL-SUP-9773]. Under the notice, notwithstanding any other provision of the solicitation or contract [@CL-SUP-9774], the Government may disclose to a litigation support contractor, for the sole purpose of litigation support activities, any information, including sensitive information, received within or in connection with a quotation or offer, or in the performance of or in connection with a contract [@CL-SUP-1593]. The contractor shall include the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), in all subcontracts, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-SUP-1594].

Under the work product clause, the contractor's procedures for protecting against unauthorized disclosure of information shall not require Department of Defense employees or members of the Armed Forces to relinquish control of their work products, whether classified or not, to the contractor [@CL-SUP-1595]. A separate section says DoD employees or members of the Armed Forces who are assigned to or visiting a contractor facility and are engaged in oversight of an acquisition program will retain control of their work products, both classified and unclassified [@CL-SUP-4120].

## How it works

The higher education representation is made by submitting an offer, and it is satisfied by one of two statements, the second of which depends on a waiver [@CL-SUP-1592]. The provision states that the waiver authority terminates on October 1, 2026, and that any waiver issued will not apply on or after that date [@CL-SUP-1591]. The restriction paragraph states both that DoD may not award a contract with an institution of higher education that hosts a Confucius Institute [@CL-SUP-1666] and that section 1062 prohibits DoD funding of such an institution unless it receives a waiver [@CL-SUP-1590]. The litigation support notice states that the Government may disclose to a litigation support contractor, for the sole purpose of litigation support activities, any information received within or in connection with a quotation or offer, or in the performance of or in connection with a contract [@CL-SUP-1593], and the contractor shall include the substance of the clause in all subcontracts [@CL-SUP-1594]. The work product clause provides that the contractor's procedures for protecting against unauthorized disclosure of information shall not require Department of Defense employees or members of the Armed Forces to relinquish control of their work products [@CL-SUP-1595].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A university submits an offer and represents that it does not host a Confucius Institute, so the first statement applies. A prime contractor places a subcontract for commercial services, and it includes the substance of the litigation support notice in that subcontract. The Government later shares the prime contractor's proposal with a litigation support contractor for a case, which the notice allows when it is solely for litigation support.

## Never confuse

The notice states that the Government may disclose information to a litigation support contractor [@CL-SUP-1593]. The clause defines that contractor by reference to a contract that contains the clause at 252.204-7014, Limitations on the Use or Disclosure of Information by Litigation Support Contractors [@CL-SUP-1670; @CL-SUP-9773], covered in [Limits on use of cyber incident and litigation support information](../../compliance/trade-cyber/cmp-cyber-incident-information-and-litigation-support-limits.md). The higher education restriction paragraph states both that DoD may not award a contract with an institution of higher education that hosts a Confucius Institute [@CL-SUP-1666] and that section 1062 prohibits DoD funding of such an institution unless it receives a waiver [@CL-SUP-1590]. The notice flows to subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services as well [@CL-SUP-1594]; for commercial subcontracts in general, see [Subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services](sup-subcontracts-commercial-products-and-services.md). The work product clause concerns the contractor's procedures for protecting against unauthorized disclosure of information [@CL-SUP-1595], while the litigation support notice concerns disclosure by the Government to a litigation support contractor [@CL-SUP-1593].
