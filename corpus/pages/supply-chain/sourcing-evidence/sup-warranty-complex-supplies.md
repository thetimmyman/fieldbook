## Purpose

Shows the warranty a contractor gives for complex supplies, the notice rule, who bears transportation costs and the Government's remedies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-1157] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.246-18 [@CL-SUP-1157; @CL-SUP-0387; @CL-SUP-1158; @CL-SUP-1159; @CL-SUP-1160; @CL-SUP-1161; @CL-SUP-1162; @CL-SUP-0428; @CL-SUP-1163; @CL-SUP-1164; @CL-SUP-0429; @CL-SUP-0430; @CL-SUP-1217; @CL-SUP-1218; @CL-SUP-1219]; FAR 52.246-17 [@CL-SUP-1154; @CL-SUP-0434; @CL-SUP-1155] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.246-18 [@CL-SUP-3479; @CL-SUP-3480; @CL-SUP-3481; @CL-SUP-3482; @CL-SUP-3483; @CL-SUP-3484; @CL-SUP-3485; @CL-SUP-3486; @CL-SUP-9336; @CL-SUP-3487; @CL-SUP-3488; @CL-SUP-9337; @CL-SUP-9338; @CL-SUP-3489] |

| Applies to | Supplies furnished under a contract that includes the clause [@CL-SUP-1157] |

The contractor warrants that for the specific warranty period after delivery, or the specified event whose occurrence will terminate the warranty period (such as the number of miles or hours of use, or combinations of any applicable events or periods of time) stated by the contracting officer in the clause, all supplies furnished under the contract will be free from defects in material and workmanship and will conform with all requirements of the contract, provided, however, that with respect to Government-furnished property, the contractor's warranty shall extend only to its proper installation, unless the contractor performs some modification or other work on the property, in which case the contractor's warranty shall extend to the modification or other work [@CL-SUP-1157; @CL-SUP-0387; @CL-SUP-1158; @CL-SUP-1159]. All implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose are excluded from any obligation contained in the contract [@CL-SUP-1218]. The contractor shall not be obligated to correct or replace supplies if the facilities, tooling, drawings, or other equipment or supplies necessary to accomplish the correction or replacement have been made unavailable to the contractor by action of the Government [@CL-SUP-1217].

When supplies are returned to the contractor, the contractor shall bear the transportation costs from the place of delivery specified in the contract (irrespective of the FOB point or the point of acceptance) to the contractor's plant and return [@CL-SUP-1160].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall notify the contractor in writing of any breach of the warranty in paragraph (b) of the clause within the specific period of time in which notice shall be given to the contractor, as inserted by the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-1164]. The contractor shall submit to the contracting officer a written recommendation within the period of time inserted by the contracting officer as to the corrective action required to remedy the breach [@CL-SUP-0429]. In the event of a breach of the contractor's warranty in paragraph (b)(1) of the clause, the Government may, at no increase in contract price, either (i) require the contractor, at the place of delivery specified in the contract (irrespective of the FOB point or the point of acceptance) or at the contractor's plant, to repair or replace, at the contractor's election, defective or nonconforming supplies, or (ii) require the contractor to furnish at the contractor's plant the materials or parts and installation instructions required to successfully accomplish the correction [@CL-SUP-1161; @CL-SUP-1162; @CL-SUP-0428]. If the contracting officer does not require correction or replacement of defective or nonconforming supplies or the contractor is not obligated to correct or replace under paragraph (b)(3) of the clause, the Government shall be entitled to an equitable reduction in the contract price [@CL-SUP-1163; @CL-SUP-1217]. After the notice of breach, but not later than the period within which the warranty remedies should be exercised (as inserted by the contracting officer) after receipt of the contractor's recommendation for corrective action, the contracting officer may, in writing, direct correction or replacement as in paragraph (c)(1) of the clause, and the contractor shall, notwithstanding any disagreement regarding the existence of a breach of warranty, comply with this direction [@CL-SUP-0430; @CL-SUP-1219].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-18, the contractor warrants that for the specific warranty period after delivery, or the specified event whose occurrence will terminate the warranty period (such as the number of miles or hours of use, or combinations of any applicable events or periods of time) stated by the contracting officer in the clause, all supplies furnished under the contract will be free from defects in material and workmanship and will conform with all requirements of the contract, provided that with respect to Government-furnished property, the contractor's warranty shall extend only to its proper installation, unless the contractor performs some modification or other work on the property, in which case the contractor's warranty shall extend to the modification or other work [@CL-SUP-3479; @CL-SUP-3480; @CL-SUP-3481]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-18, the contractor shall not be obligated to correct or replace supplies if the facilities, tooling, drawings, or other equipment or supplies necessary to accomplish the correction or replacement have been made unavailable to the contractor by action of the Government, when supplies are returned to the contractor the contractor shall bear the transportation costs from the place of delivery specified in the contract (irrespective of the FOB point or the point of acceptance) to the contractor's plant and return, and all implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose are excluded from any obligation contained in the contract [@CL-SUP-3482; @CL-SUP-3483; @CL-SUP-3484]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-18(c), in the event of a breach of the contractor's warranty in paragraph (b)(1) of the clause, the Government may, at no increase in contract price, either (i) require the contractor, at the place of delivery specified in the contract (irrespective of the FOB point or the point of acceptance) or at the contractor's plant, to repair or replace, at the contractor's election, defective or nonconforming supplies, or (ii) require the contractor to furnish at the contractor's plant the materials or parts and installation instructions required to successfully accomplish the correction, and if the contracting officer does not require correction or replacement of defective or nonconforming supplies or the contractor is not obligated to correct or replace under paragraph (b)(3) of the clause, the Government shall be entitled to an equitable reduction in the contract price [@CL-SUP-3485; @CL-SUP-3486; @CL-SUP-9336; @CL-SUP-3487]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-18(c)(3), the contracting officer shall notify the contractor in writing of any breach of the warranty in paragraph (b) of the clause within the specific period of time inserted by the contracting officer, the contractor shall submit to the contracting officer a written recommendation within the period of time inserted by the contracting officer as to the corrective action required to remedy the breach, and after the notice of breach, but not later than the period inserted by the contracting officer after receipt of the contractor's recommendation for corrective action, the contracting officer may, in writing, direct correction or replacement as in paragraph (c)(1) of the clause, and the contractor shall, notwithstanding any disagreement regarding the existence of a breach of warranty, comply with this direction [@CL-SUP-3488; @CL-SUP-9337; @CL-SUP-9338; @CL-SUP-3489].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 40 pumps under the basic clause, with no alternate, that sets a warranty of 24 months after delivery. A casing defect appears in 3 pumps in month 10, and the contracting officer notifies the contractor in writing within the period in the clause. The contractor elects to repair the 3 pumps at its plant and bears the transportation to the plant and back. The price does not increase. The answer: the contractor pays the repair and the transportation, and the Government pays nothing extra.

## Never confuse

Under the [noncomplex supplies clause](sup-warranty-noncomplex-supplies.md), within a reasonable time after the notice, the contracting officer may either require, by written notice, the prompt correction or replacement of any supplies or parts thereof (including preservation, packaging, packing, and marking) that do not conform with the requirements of the contract within the meaning of paragraph (b)(1) of that clause, or retain such supplies and reduce the contract price by an amount equitable under the circumstances [@CL-SUP-1154; @CL-SUP-0434; @CL-SUP-1155], whereas under FAR 52.246-18(c)(1) the Government may, at no increase in contract price, either require the contractor, at the place of delivery specified in the contract (irrespective of the FOB point or the point of acceptance) or at the contractor's plant, to repair or replace, at the contractor's election, defective or nonconforming supplies, or require the contractor to furnish at the contractor's plant the materials or parts and installation instructions required to successfully accomplish the correction [@CL-SUP-1161; @CL-SUP-1162; @CL-SUP-0428]. When supplies are returned to the contractor under FAR 52.246-18(b)(5), the contractor shall bear the transportation costs from the place of delivery specified in the contract (irrespective of the FOB point or the point of acceptance) to the contractor's plant and return [@CL-SUP-1160]. See [Warranty of systems and equipment under performance specifications or design criteria](sup-warranty-systems-and-equipment.md), [Warranty contract clauses and their triggers](sup-warranty-contract-clauses.md) and [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](sup-warranties.md).
