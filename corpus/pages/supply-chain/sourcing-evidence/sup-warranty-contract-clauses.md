## Purpose

Names each prescribed warranty clause by number and the trigger that lets the contracting officer insert it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0880] |
| Codified citation | FAR 46.710 [@CL-SUP-0961; @CL-SUP-0962; @CL-SUP-9789; @CL-SUP-0880; @CL-SUP-0971; @CL-SUP-0881; @CL-SUP-0882; @CL-SUP-0883; @CL-SUP-0884; @CL-SUP-0885; @CL-SUP-0886; @CL-SUP-0887] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0880] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts in which inclusion of a warranty is appropriate [@CL-SUP-0961] |

The clauses and alternates prescribed in FAR 46.710 may be used in solicitations and contracts in which inclusion of a warranty is appropriate (see FAR 46.709 for warranties for commercial products and commercial services) [@CL-SUP-0961]. In each of the five paragraphs, subject to the further conditions and the exception stated below, the contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the prescribed clause in solicitations and contracts when the specified fixed-price contract is contemplated and the use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0880; @CL-SUP-0971; @CL-SUP-0881; @CL-SUP-0882; @CL-SUP-0883; @CL-SUP-0884; @CL-SUP-0885; @CL-SUP-0886; @CL-SUP-0887].

However, because of the many situations that may influence the warranty terms and conditions appropriate to a particular acquisition, the contracting officer may vary the terms and conditions of the clauses and alternates to the extent necessary [@CL-SUP-0962]. The alternates prescribed in FAR 46.710 address the clauses, however, the conditions pertaining to each alternate must be considered if the terms and conditions are varied to meet a particular need [@CL-SUP-9789].

Under FAR 46.710(d), the contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-20, Warranty of Services, in solicitations and contracts for services when a fixed-price contract for services is contemplated and the use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures, unless a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-19, Warranty of Systems and Equipment under Performance Specifications or Design Criteria, has been used [@CL-SUP-0885; @CL-SUP-0886].

## How it works

The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-17, Warranty of Supplies of a Noncomplex Nature, in solicitations and contracts for noncomplex items when a fixed-price supply contract is contemplated and the use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0880; @CL-SUP-0971]. The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-18, Warranty of Supplies of a Complex Nature, in solicitations and contracts for deliverable complex items when a fixed-price supply or research and development contract is contemplated and the use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0881; @CL-SUP-0882]. The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-19, Warranty of Systems and Equipment under Performance Specifications or Design Criteria, in solicitations and contracts when performance specifications or design are of major importance, a fixed-price supply, service, or research and development contract for systems and equipment is contemplated, and the use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0883; @CL-SUP-0884]. The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-20, Warranty of Services, in solicitations and contracts for services when a fixed-price contract for services is contemplated and the use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures, unless a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-19, Warranty of Systems and Equipment under Performance Specifications or Design Criteria, has been used [@CL-SUP-0885; @CL-SUP-0886]. The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.246-21, Warranty of Construction, in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price construction contract (see FAR 46.705(c)) is contemplated and the use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0887].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price supply contract covers noncomplex catalog items, and agency procedures have approved a warranty clause. The contracting officer may insert the noncomplex-supplies clause. A fixed-price service contract that already carries the systems and equipment clause does not take the services clause as well. A cost-reimbursement contract meets none of these fixed-price triggers.

## Never confuse

The clauses and alternates prescribed in FAR 46.710 may be used in solicitations and contracts in which inclusion of a warranty is appropriate (see FAR 46.709 for warranties for commercial products and commercial services), and in each prescription the contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the prescribed clause when the stated conditions are met [@CL-SUP-0961; @CL-SUP-0880; @CL-SUP-0971; @CL-SUP-0881; @CL-SUP-0882; @CL-SUP-0883; @CL-SUP-0884; @CL-SUP-0885; @CL-SUP-0886; @CL-SUP-0887]. See [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](sup-warranties.md), [Contractor liability for Government property loss after acceptance](sup-contractor-liability-after-acceptance.md) and [Service and warranty costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-service-and-warranty-costs.md).
