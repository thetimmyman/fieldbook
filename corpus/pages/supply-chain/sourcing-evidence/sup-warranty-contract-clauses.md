## Purpose

Names each prescribed warranty clause by number and the trigger that lets the contracting officer insert it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-SUP-0880] |
| Codified citation | FAR 46.710 [@CL-SUP-0880; @CL-SUP-0881; @CL-SUP-0883; @CL-SUP-0885; @CL-SUP-0887; @CL-SUP-0961] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-SUP-0880] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts in which inclusion of a warranty is appropriate [@CL-SUP-0961] |

The clauses and alternates prescribed in the section may be used in solicitations and contracts in which inclusion of a warranty is appropriate [@CL-SUP-0961]. The five warranty clauses are permissive: in each case the contracting officer may insert the clause, and each paragraph ties it to a fixed-price contract type and to approval of a warranty clause under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0880; @CL-SUP-0971; @CL-SUP-0881; @CL-SUP-0882; @CL-SUP-0883; @CL-SUP-0884; @CL-SUP-0885; @CL-SUP-0887].

The contracting officer may also vary the terms and conditions of the clauses and alternates to the extent necessary, because of the many situations that may influence the warranty terms and conditions appropriate to a particular acquisition [@CL-SUP-0962].

The permission to insert the Warranty of Services clause does not apply where a clause substantially the same as FAR 52.246-19 has been used [@CL-SUP-0886]. The section also prescribes alternates for several of these clauses, which this card does not list [@CL-SUP-0962].

## How it works

FAR 52.246-17, Warranty of Supplies of a Noncomplex Nature, is for noncomplex items when a fixed-price supply contract is contemplated and use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0880; @CL-SUP-0971]. The contracting officer may insert FAR 52.246-18, Warranty of Supplies of a Complex Nature, for deliverable complex items [@CL-SUP-0881], when a fixed-price supply or research and development contract is contemplated and use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0882]. FAR 52.246-19, Warranty of Systems and Equipment under Performance Specifications or Design Criteria, may be inserted when performance specifications or design are of major importance [@CL-SUP-0883], a fixed-price supply, service, or research and development contract for systems and equipment is contemplated, and use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0884]. FAR 52.246-20, Warranty of Services, is for services when a fixed-price contract for services is contemplated and use of a warranty clause has been approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0885], unless a clause substantially the same as FAR 52.246-19 has been used [@CL-SUP-0886]. FAR 52.246-21, Warranty of Construction, is for a fixed-price construction contract that is contemplated, with use of a warranty clause approved under agency procedures [@CL-SUP-0887].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price supply contract covers noncomplex catalog items, and agency procedures have approved a warranty clause. The contracting officer may insert the noncomplex-supplies clause. A fixed-price service contract that already carries the systems and equipment clause does not take the services clause as well. A cost-reimbursement contract meets none of these fixed-price triggers.

## Never confuse

A prescribed clause is not a requirement to warrant: each paragraph says the contracting officer may insert the clause. The criteria for deciding whether a warranty is appropriate are on [Warranties: criteria, terms and remedies](sup-warranties.md), liability for Government property loss after acceptance is on [Contractor liability for Government property loss after acceptance](sup-contractor-liability-after-acceptance.md), and the allowability of warranty costs is on [Service and warranty costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-service-and-warranty-costs.md).
