## Purpose

Shows what a contractor must screen and report under the reporting nonconforming items clause, by when, to whom, and which subcontracts the clause flows to.

## The rule

The contractor shall screen Government-Industry Data Exchange Program (GIDEP) reports, available at www.gidep.org, as a part of the contractor's inspection system or program for the control of quality, to avoid the use and delivery of counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items or delivery of items that contain a major or critical nonconformance [@CL-SUP-1187; @CL-SUP-1206]. This requirement does not apply if the contractor is a foreign corporation or partnership that does not have an office, place of business, or fiscal paying agent in the United States [@CL-SUP-1188].

The contractor shall provide written notification to the contracting officer within 60 days of becoming aware or having reason to suspect, such as through inspection, testing, record review, or notification from another source, that any end item, component, subassembly, part, or material contained in supplies purchased by the contractor for delivery to, or for, the Government is counterfeit or suspect counterfeit [@CL-SUP-1187; @CL-SUP-1189; @CL-SUP-1190]. The contractor shall retain counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items in its possession at the time of discovery until disposition instructions have been provided by the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-1187; @CL-SUP-1192]. Except as provided in paragraph (c) of the clause, the contractor shall submit a report to GIDEP at www.gidep.org within 60 days of becoming aware or having reason to suspect, such as through inspection, testing, record review, or notification from another source (such as a seller, customer, or third party), that an item purchased by the contractor for delivery to, or for, the Government is either a counterfeit or suspect counterfeit item or a common item that has a major or critical nonconformance [@CL-SUP-1187; @CL-SUP-1191; @CL-SUP-0286; @CL-SUP-1207]. The contractor shall not submit a report as required by paragraph (b)(4) of the clause if (1) the contractor is a foreign corporation or partnership that does not have an office, place of business, or fiscal paying agent in the United States, (2) the contractor is aware that the counterfeit, suspect counterfeit, or nonconforming item is the subject of an on-going criminal investigation, unless the report is approved by the cognizant law-enforcement agency, or (3) for nonconforming items other than counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items, it can be confirmed that the organization where the defect was generated (such as an original component manufacturer, original equipment manufacturer, aftermarket manufacturer, or distributor that alters item properties or configuration) has not released the item to more than one customer [@CL-SUP-1208; @CL-SUP-1209; @CL-SUP-0287; @CL-SUP-0288].

Except as provided in paragraph (g)(2) of the clause, the contractor shall insert the clause, including paragraph (g), in subcontracts that are for (i) items subject to higher-level quality standards in accordance with the clause at FAR 52.246-11, Higher-Level Contract Quality Requirement, (ii) items that the contractor determines to be critical items for which use of the clause is appropriate, (iii) electronic parts or end items, components, parts, or materials containing electronic parts, whether or not covered in paragraph (g)(1)(i) or (ii) of the clause, if the subcontract exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, and the contract is by, or for, the Department of Defense, or (iv) for the acquisition of services, if the subcontractor will furnish, as part of the service, any items that meet the criteria specified in paragraphs (g)(1)(i) through (g)(1)(iii) of the clause [@CL-SUP-1193; @CL-SUP-0289; @CL-SUP-1210; @CL-SUP-9786; @CL-SUP-1211; @CL-SUP-0290]. The contractor shall not insert the clause in subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services or for medical devices that are subject to the Food and Drug Administration reporting requirements at 21 CFR 803 [@CL-SUP-1194].

## How it works

Screening GIDEP reports is part of the contractor's inspection system or program for the control of quality [@CL-SUP-1187]. Written notification goes to the contracting officer within 60 days of becoming aware or having reason to suspect, and the report to GIDEP at www.gidep.org is submitted within 60 days of becoming aware or having reason to suspect, except as provided in paragraph (c) of the clause [@CL-SUP-1189; @CL-SUP-1191]. See [Rejection of nonconforming supplies and who may accept them](../../operating-model/corrective-action/opm-rejection-of-nonconforming-supplies.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 solicitation provisions and contract clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-26, the contractor shall screen GIDEP reports, available at www.gidep.org, as a part of the contractor's inspection system or program for the control of quality, to avoid the use and delivery of counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items or delivery of items that contain a major or critical nonconformance, except that this requirement does not apply if the contractor is a foreign corporation or partnership that does not have an office, place of business, or fiscal paying agent in the United States [@CL-SUP-3464; @CL-SUP-3465; @CL-SUP-3466]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-26, the contractor shall provide written notification to the contracting officer within 60 days of becoming aware or having reason to suspect, such as through inspection, testing, record review, or notification from another source (such as a seller, customer, or third party), that any end item, component, subassembly, part, or material contained in supplies purchased by the contractor for delivery to, or for, the Government is counterfeit or suspect counterfeit, and shall retain counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items in its possession at the time of discovery until disposition instructions have been provided by the contracting officer [@CL-SUP-3467; @CL-SUP-3468; @CL-SUP-3469]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-26, except as provided in paragraph (c) of the clause, the contractor shall submit a report to GIDEP at www.gidep.org within 60 days of becoming aware or having reason to suspect, such as through inspection, testing, record review, or notification from another source (such as a seller, customer, or third party), that an item purchased by the contractor for delivery to, or for, the Government is either a counterfeit or suspect counterfeit item or a common item that has a major or critical nonconformance [@CL-SUP-3470; @CL-SUP-3471]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-26(c), the contractor shall not submit a report as required by paragraph (b)(4) if (1) the contractor is a foreign corporation or partnership that does not have an office, place of business, or fiscal paying agent in the United States, (2) the contractor is aware that the counterfeit, suspect counterfeit, or nonconforming item is the subject of an on-going criminal investigation, unless the report is approved by the cognizant law-enforcement agency, or (3) for nonconforming items other than counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items, it can be confirmed that the organization where the defect was generated (such as an original component manufacturer, original equipment manufacturer, aftermarket manufacturer, or distributor that alters item properties or configuration) has not released the item to more than one customer [@CL-SUP-3472; @CL-SUP-3473; @CL-SUP-9332; @CL-SUP-9333]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-26(g), except as provided in paragraph (g)(2) of the clause, the contractor shall insert the clause, including paragraph (g), in subcontracts that are for (i) items subject to higher-level quality standards in accordance with the clause at FAR 52.246-11, Higher-Level Contract Quality Requirement, (ii) items that the contractor determines to be critical items for which use of the clause is appropriate, (iii) electronic parts or end items, components, parts, or materials containing electronic parts, whether or not covered in paragraph (g)(1)(i) or (ii) of the clause, if the subcontract exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, and the contract is by, or for, the Department of Defense, or (iv) for the acquisition of services, if the subcontractor will furnish, as part of the service, any items that meet the criteria specified in paragraphs (g)(1)(i) through (g)(1)(iii) of the clause [@CL-SUP-3474; @CL-SUP-3475; @CL-SUP-3476; @CL-SUP-9334; @CL-SUP-3477; @CL-SUP-9335]. Under RFO FAR 52.246-26(g)(2), the contractor shall not insert the clause in subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services or for medical devices that are subject to the Food and Drug Administration reporting requirements at 21 CFR 803 [@CL-SUP-3478].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor buys 1,000 electronic parts for delivery to the Government. A record review shows that 50 parts in one lot are suspect counterfeit. Within 60 days of becoming aware, the contractor notifies the contracting officer in writing and, unless an exception applies, reports to GIDEP. It keeps the 50 parts until the contracting officer gives disposition instructions. The answer: two filings inside the 60 days, and the parts are held and not scrapped or returned.

## Never confuse

Screening GIDEP reports is part of the contractor's inspection system or program for the control of quality [@CL-SUP-1187], unlike submitting a report to GIDEP at www.gidep.org, which is required, except as provided in paragraph (c) of the clause, within 60 days of becoming aware or having reason to suspect that an item purchased by the contractor for delivery to, or for, the Government is a counterfeit or suspect counterfeit item or a common item that has a major or critical nonconformance [@CL-SUP-1191; @CL-SUP-0286; @CL-SUP-1207]. See [Rejection of nonconforming supplies and who may accept them](../../operating-model/corrective-action/opm-rejection-of-nonconforming-supplies.md) and [Costs of counterfeit and suspect counterfeit electronic parts](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-counterfeit-electronic-part-costs.md).
