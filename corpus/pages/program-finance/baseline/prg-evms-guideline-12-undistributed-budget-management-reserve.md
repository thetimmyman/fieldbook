## Purpose

Shows how EVMS Guideline 12 defines and controls undistributed budget within the performance measurement baseline and management reserve outside the performance measurement baseline [@CL-PRG-0269; @CL-PRG-0279].

Provide and control undistributed budgets, and establish and maintain a management reserve budget for solving problems and managing risks [@CL-PRG-0269]. Undistributed budget is budget that is applicable to specific contractual effort that has not yet been distributed to control accounts or summary level planning packages [@CL-PRG-0270]. It is part of the performance measurement baseline and is tied to contractually authorized scope that has not yet been distributed to an organizational element at or below the reporting level [@CL-PRG-0272].

Management reserve belongs to the contractor Program Manager rather than the government and provides the contractor with a budget for unplanned activities within the current program scope [@CL-PRG-0274]. Management reserve has no defined scope, is not included in the performance measurement baseline, is always reported as a positive value, and is not a contingency fund [@CL-PRG-0275; @CL-PRG-0277; @CL-PRG-0276].

## How it works

Undistributed budget is used to accommodate temporary contract situations where authorized work scope and budget cannot be distributed within the performance measurement baseline to control accounts or summary level planning packages [@CL-PRG-0271]. Because undistributed budget is a transitional budget, it should be distributed in a timely manner [@CL-PRG-0273]. Management reserve is budget set aside to cover documented program risks or future unplanned events that are in scope to the contract, and a major subcontractor’s management reserve must be incorporated into the prime’s EVMS with traceability to the reported management reserve [@CL-PRG-0277; @CL-PRG-0278]. Management reserve is not a contingency fund, may not be eliminated from contract prices by the customer during subsequent negotiations, and may not be used to absorb the cost of contract changes [@CL-PRG-0276].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program has 900,000 of budget distributed to control accounts and planning packages. A modification is signed but not yet planned in detail, so 100,000 sits in undistributed budget inside the baseline. Program management separately holds 60,000 of management reserve outside the baseline. The baseline is 1,000,000 and the contract budget base is 1,060,000.

## Never confuse

Undistributed budget has defined scope, is traceable to contractual actions, and is part of the performance measurement baseline, whereas management reserve does not have defined scope and is separately identified outside the performance measurement baseline [@CL-PRG-0279].
