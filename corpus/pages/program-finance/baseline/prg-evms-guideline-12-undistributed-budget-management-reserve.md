## Purpose

Separates two budgets that are easy to lump together: undistributed budget, which sits inside the baseline, and management reserve, which sits outside it [@CL-PRG-0279].

Undistributed budget is budget for specific contractual effort that has not yet been distributed to control accounts or summary level planning packages [@CL-PRG-0270]. It is part of the baseline and is tied to contractually authorized scope that has not yet been distributed to an organizational element at or below the reporting level [@CL-PRG-0272].

Management reserve belongs to the contractor program manager rather than the Government and gives the contractor budget for unplanned activities within the current program scope [@CL-PRG-0274]. It carries no defined scope, is not included in the baseline, is always reported as a positive value, and is not a contingency fund [@CL-PRG-0275; @CL-PRG-0277; @CL-PRG-0276].

## How it works

Undistributed budget accommodates contract situations where authorized scope and budget cannot yet be distributed within the baseline to control accounts or summary level planning packages [@CL-PRG-0271]. It is a transitional budget and should be distributed in a timely manner [@CL-PRG-0273]. Management reserve is set aside for program risks or unplanned events that are in scope to the contract, and a major subcontractor’s reserve is brought into the prime system with traceability to the reported figure [@CL-PRG-0277; @CL-PRG-0278]. Management reserve is not a contingency fund: the customer may not eliminate it from contract prices during later negotiations, and it may not be used to absorb the cost of contract changes [@CL-PRG-0276].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program has 900,000 of budget distributed to control accounts and planning packages. A modification is signed but not yet planned in detail, so 100,000 sits in undistributed budget inside the baseline. Program management separately holds 60,000 of management reserve outside the baseline. The baseline is 1,000,000 and the contract budget base is 1,060,000.

## Never confuse

Undistributed budget is not management reserve. Undistributed budget has defined scope, is traceable to contractual actions and is part of the baseline, while management reserve has no defined scope and is identified outside the baseline [@CL-PRG-0279].
