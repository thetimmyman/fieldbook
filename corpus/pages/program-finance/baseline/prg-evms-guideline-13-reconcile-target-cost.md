## Purpose

Shows how EVMS Guideline 13 requires reconciling the program target cost goal with the sum of all internal program budgets and management reserve [@CL-PRG-0280; @CL-PRG-0281].

Reconcile the program target cost goal with the sum of all internal program budgets and management reserve [@CL-PRG-0280; @CL-PRG-0281]. The DoD EVMS Interpretation Guide considers contract target cost to be synonymous with negotiated contract cost [@CL-PRG-0282].

The contract target cost plus authorized unpriced work must reconcile with the contract budget base and the total allocated budget [@CL-PRG-0283]. Control account budgets, summary level planning packages and undistributed budget are summed to determine the budget at completion of the performance measurement baseline, and that total plus management reserve establishes the contract budget base [@CL-PRG-0284].

## How it works

Guideline 13 requires that the program target cost goal be reconciled with the sum of all internal program budgets and management reserve [@CL-PRG-0281]. Control account budgets, summary level planning packages and undistributed budget are summed to determine the budget at completion of the performance measurement baseline, and that total plus management reserve establishes the contract budget base [@CL-PRG-0284]. The contract target cost plus authorized unpriced work must reconcile with the contract budget base and the total allocated budget [@CL-PRG-0283]. The contract budget base and the total allocated budget are the same value unless there is a recognized over-target baseline, in which case the total allocated budget equals the contract budget base plus the above target budget [@CL-PRG-0285].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract target cost is 2,000,000 and authorized unpriced work is estimated at 200,000. Control accounts, planning packages and undistributed budget total 1,900,000, and management reserve is 300,000, so the contract budget base is 2,200,000 and reconciles with the target cost plus the unpriced work. No over-target baseline is recognized, so the total allocated budget is also 2,200,000.

## Never confuse

Contract target cost is synonymous with negotiated contract cost [@CL-PRG-0282], whereas the contract budget base is established by the sum of control account budgets, summary level planning packages, and undistributed budget plus management reserve, and reconciles with the contract target cost plus authorized unpriced work [@CL-PRG-0283; @CL-PRG-0284].
