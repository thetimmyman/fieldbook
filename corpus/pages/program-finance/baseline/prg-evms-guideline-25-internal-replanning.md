## Purpose

Shows how the contractor documents and reconciles changes to scope, schedule, and budget that come from internal replanning inside the authorized contract.

| Applies to | Internal replanning of the remaining in-scope work on the contract [@CL-PRG-0367] |

Internal replanning is intended to maintain an executable baseline for the remaining in-scope work on the contract [@CL-PRG-0367]. Contract budget logs provide traceability of authorized baseline changes [@CL-PRG-0368].

## How it works

The system description must describe and establish a freeze period suitable for forward planning discipline and the integrity of the performance measurement baseline [@CL-PRG-0369]. Baseline changes within the freeze period are limited, for example to customer-directed contract actions, rate changes, and economic price adjustments [@CL-PRG-0370]. The contractor should define the process for internal replanning related to setting budgeted cost for work scheduled equal to budgeted cost for work performed, not the reverse, when eliminating schedule variances for in-process work [@CL-PRG-0371].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A control account shows an in-process work package with a schedule variance. The contractor replans it by setting budgeted cost for work scheduled equal to budgeted cost for work performed, never the reverse. The change is logged in the contract budget log so the new plan reconciles to the prior plan.

## Never confuse

Internal replanning stays inside the authorized contract scope, while a customer directed change adds or removes authorized scope. This guideline governs the contractor's own rearrangement of the remaining in-scope work, kept traceable through the budget log.
