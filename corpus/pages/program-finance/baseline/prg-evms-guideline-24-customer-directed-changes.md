## Purpose

Shows how an authorized customer directed change is incorporated into the contract budget base and schedule in a timely and systematic manner.

| Applies to | Authorized changes incorporated into the performance measurement baseline [@CL-PRG-0366] |

When incorporating a customer directed change prior to negotiation, the work is planned based on the estimated value of the scope [@CL-PRG-0363]. Authorized changes are incorporated into the performance measurement baseline, and authorization documents are updated accordingly, before the commencement of work [@CL-PRG-0366].

## How it works

Undistributed budget is distributed to summary level planning packages and control accounts in a timely manner following authorization to proceed for a change order, letter contract, or negotiations and definitization of a contract action [@CL-PRG-0364]. Changes should never result in negative undistributed budget or management reserve values [@CL-PRG-0365].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A change order adds 30 of authorized scope to a program. The undistributed budget of 30 is distributed to control accounts after authorization to proceed, and the authorization documents are updated before work starts. The budget and management reserve stay at zero or above, so no negative undistributed budget or management reserve appears.

## Never confuse

A customer directed change is driven by the customer, while internal replanning is the contractor rearranging work inside the existing authorized scope. This guideline governs the timely incorporation of the customer's change into the baseline.
