## Purpose

Shows when and how EVMS Guideline 27 proposes, documents, and establishes a total program budget greater than the contract budget base (an over-target baseline) and/or a total program schedule exceeding the contractual period of performance (an over-target schedule) to support management of the remaining authorized work [@CL-PRG-0376].

| Applies to | A program whose remaining authorized work needs a budget above the contract budget base or a schedule beyond the period of performance [@CL-PRG-0376] |

When necessary, a total program budget greater than the contract budget base, called an over-target baseline, and/or a total program schedule exceeding the contractual period of performance, called an over-target schedule, is proposed, documented, and established to support management of the remaining authorized work [@CL-PRG-0376]. Advance notification must be provided to the customer prior to implementation [@CL-PRG-0377].

## How it works

The contract budget base is a controlled value and cannot be changed by the contractor except as a result of customer contract actions [@CL-PRG-0378]. Requests for establishing an over-target baseline or an over-target schedule are initiated by the contractor and approved by the customer contracting authority [@CL-PRG-0379]. Implementing an over-target baseline or over-target schedule does not change the terms and conditions of the contract but merely serves to improve management of the remaining work [@CL-PRG-0380].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program has a contract budget base of 100, but the remaining work now forecasts a total budget of 130. The contractor proposes an over-target baseline of 130 and gives the customer advance notification. The customer approves, and the contract terms and conditions are unchanged while the program manages the remaining work against 130.

## Never confuse

An over-target baseline is a total program budget greater than the contract budget base [@CL-PRG-0376], while a single point adjustment sets some or all existing contract cost and/or schedule variances to zero [@CL-PRG-0374]. The contract budget base is a controlled value and cannot be changed by the contractor except as a result of customer contract actions [@CL-PRG-0378].

## Company calibration

Whether a forecast above the contract budget base is an over-target baseline or over-target schedule that needs customer approval before implementation. [@Q-025]
