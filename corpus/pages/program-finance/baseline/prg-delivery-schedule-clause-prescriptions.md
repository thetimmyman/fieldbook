## Purpose

Shows when the contracting officer may or shall put a delivery or completion schedule clause into a solicitation or contract, which alternate the section ties to each basis for the schedule, and which clauses may be changed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0457] |
| Codified citation | FAR 11.404 [@CL-PRG-0457] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0457] |
| Applies to | Delivery clauses in solicitations and contracts other than those for construction and architect-engineering, and the completion clause in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price construction contract is contemplated [@CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0464; @CL-PRG-0469] |

The contracting officer may use a time of delivery clause to set forth a required delivery schedule and to allow an offeror to propose an alternative delivery schedule [@CL-PRG-0457]. The clauses and their alternates may be used in solicitations and contracts for other than construction and architect-engineering substantially as shown, or they may be changed or new clauses written [@CL-PRG-0458].

The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at 52.211-8, Time of Delivery, in solicitations and contracts other than those for construction and architect-engineering, if the Government requires delivery by a particular time and the delivery schedule is to be based on the date of the contract [@CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0460]. If the delivery schedule is expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods and is based on an assumed date of award, the contracting officer may use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-PRG-0461]. If the delivery schedule is expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods and is based on an assumed date the contractor will receive notice of award, the contracting officer may use the clause with its Alternate II [@CL-PRG-0462]. If the delivery schedule is to be based on the actual date the contractor receives a written notice of award, the contracting officer may use the clause with its Alternate III [@CL-PRG-0463].

The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at 52.211-9, Desired and Required Time of Delivery, in solicitations and contracts for other than construction and architect-engineering, if the Government desires delivery by a certain time but requires delivery by a specified later time, and the delivery schedule is to be based on the date of the contract [@CL-PRG-0464; @CL-PRG-0465]. If the delivery schedule is expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods and is based on an assumed date of award, the contracting officer may use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-PRG-0466]. If the delivery schedule is expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods and is based on an assumed date the contractor receives notice of award, the contracting officer may use the clause with its Alternate II [@CL-PRG-0467]. If the delivery schedule is to be based on the actual date the contractor receives a written notice of award, the contracting officer may use the clause with its Alternate III [@CL-PRG-0468].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.211-10, Commencement, Prosecution, and Completion of Work, in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price construction contract is contemplated [@CL-PRG-0469]. The clause may be changed to accommodate the issuance of orders under indefinite-delivery contracts [@CL-PRG-0470]. If the completion date is expressed as a specific calendar date, computed on the basis of the contractor receiving the notice to proceed by a certain day, the contracting officer may use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-PRG-0471].

## How it works

Each delivery clause and each alternate is written with may [@CL-PRG-0457; @CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0464; @CL-PRG-0461; @CL-PRG-0462; @CL-PRG-0463; @CL-PRG-0466; @CL-PRG-0467; @CL-PRG-0468]. The construction clause itself is written with shall when a fixed-price construction contract is contemplated, while its Alternate I and its change to accommodate the issuance of orders under indefinite-delivery contracts are written with may [@CL-PRG-0469; @CL-PRG-0470; @CL-PRG-0471].

In codified FAR 2.101, an alternate is a substantive variation of a basic provision or clause prescribed for use in a defined circumstance, which adds wording to, deletes wording from, or substitutes specified wording for a portion of the basic provision or clause, and the alternate version of a provision or clause is the basic provision or clause as changed by the addition, deletion, or substitution (see 52.105(a)) [@CL-PRG-9067; @CL-PRG-9130]. In RFO FAR 2.101, an alternate is a substantive variation of a basic provision or clause prescribed for use in a defined circumstance, which adds wording to, deletes wording from, or substitutes specified wording for a portion of the basic provision or clause, and the alternate version of a provision or clause is the basic provision or clause as changed by the addition, deletion, or substitution (see 52.105(a)) [@CL-PRG-9101; @CL-PRG-9128].

Under FAR 11.404(a), a requirement for delivery by a particular time uses the Time of Delivery clause [@CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0460], while a desired delivery by a certain time with a required delivery by a specified later time uses the Desired and Required Time of Delivery clause [@CL-PRG-0464; @CL-PRG-0465], in both cases when the delivery schedule is to be based on the date of the contract [@CL-PRG-0460; @CL-PRG-0465].

For both 52.211-8 and 52.211-9, Alternate I may be used when the delivery schedule is expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods and is based on an assumed date of award [@CL-PRG-0461; @CL-PRG-0466], Alternate II may be used when it is expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods and is based on an assumed date the contractor will receive (or receives) notice of award [@CL-PRG-0462; @CL-PRG-0467], and Alternate III may be used when the delivery schedule is to be based on the actual date the contractor receives a written notice of award [@CL-PRG-0463; @CL-PRG-0468].

For the delivery clauses, the clauses and their alternates may be used in solicitations and contracts for other than construction and architect-engineering substantially as shown, or they may be changed or new clauses written [@CL-PRG-0458]. For the construction clause, the clause may be changed to accommodate the issuance of orders under indefinite-delivery contracts [@CL-PRG-0470]. The clause texts are on [time of delivery](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-time-of-delivery.md), [desired and required time of delivery](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-desired-and-required-time-of-delivery.md) and [commencement, prosecution and completion of work](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-commencement-prosecution-and-completion.md). The factors that go into the schedule itself are on [the schedule factors card](prg-delivery-schedule-factors.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for 500 pumps says the Government requires delivery by a particular time, and the schedule is to be based on the date of the contract. The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the Time of Delivery clause [@CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0460].

A second solicitation, for 300 valves, uses the Time of Delivery clause because the Government requires delivery by a particular time [@CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0460], and it states delivery in specific calendar dates based on an assumed date of award. The contracting officer may use the clause with Alternate I [@CL-PRG-0461]. Had the dates been based on an assumed date the contractor will receive notice of award, the contracting officer could use Alternate II [@CL-PRG-0462]. Had the schedule been based on the actual date the contractor receives a written notice of award, the contracting officer could use Alternate III [@CL-PRG-0463]. In any of these cases the section also allows a changed clause or a new clause [@CL-PRG-0458].

A third solicitation, for 200 gaskets, says the Government desires delivery by one date but requires delivery by a later date, based on the date of the contract. The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as the Desired and Required Time of Delivery clause [@CL-PRG-0464; @CL-PRG-0465].

A fixed-price construction contract is contemplated for a warehouse. The contracting officer shall insert the Commencement, Prosecution, and Completion of Work clause [@CL-PRG-0469]. The completion date is a specific calendar date computed on the basis of the contractor receiving the notice to proceed by a certain day, so the contracting officer may use the clause with Alternate I [@CL-PRG-0471]. The contract is an indefinite-delivery contract under which orders will be issued, so the contracting officer may change the clause to accommodate the issuance of those orders [@CL-PRG-0470].

## Never confuse

The Time of Delivery clause at 52.211-8 applies if the Government requires delivery by a particular time [@CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0460], whereas the Desired and Required Time of Delivery clause at 52.211-9 applies if the Government desires delivery by a certain time but requires delivery by a specified later time [@CL-PRG-0464; @CL-PRG-0465].

For 52.211-8 and 52.211-9, Alternates I, II, and III depend on the date the delivery schedule is based on and, for Alternates I and II, on the schedule being expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods [@CL-PRG-0461; @CL-PRG-0462; @CL-PRG-0463; @CL-PRG-0466; @CL-PRG-0467; @CL-PRG-0468].

Under 52.211-10, Alternate I applies if the completion date is expressed as a specific calendar date, computed on the basis of the contractor receiving the notice to proceed by a certain day [@CL-PRG-0471], whereas under 52.211-8 and 52.211-9, Alternate I applies if the delivery schedule is expressed in terms of specific calendar dates or specific periods and is based on an assumed date of award [@CL-PRG-0461; @CL-PRG-0466].

The delivery clauses in paragraph (a) are for solicitations and contracts other than those for construction and architect-engineering [@CL-PRG-0459; @CL-PRG-0464].
