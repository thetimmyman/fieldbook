## Purpose

Shows what the final solicitation for a major system must state, what each offeror is told it is free to propose, and which presolicitation steps the contracting officer should take before issuing the solicitation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0410] |
| Codified citation | FAR 34.005-2 [@CL-PRG-0410] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0410] |
| Applies to | The contracting officer issuing the solicitation, and all prospective offerors receiving the final solicitation [@CL-PRG-0410; @CL-PRG-0416] |

Before issuing the solicitation, whenever practicable and consistent with agency procedures, the contracting officer should take the actions outlined in paragraphs (a)(1) and (a)(2) [@CL-PRG-0410].

Under paragraph (a)(1), the contracting officer should take the widest practicable dissemination of advance notification of the acquisition, including publicizing through the Governmentwide point of entry (see subpart 5.2) [@CL-PRG-0410; @CL-PRG-0411]. Advance notification should also be sent to as wide a selection of potential sources as practicable, including smaller and newer firms, Government laboratories, federally funded research and development centers, educational institutions and other not-for-profit organizations [@CL-PRG-0410; @CL-PRG-0412]. If it would be beneficial and is not prohibited, it should also be sent to foreign sources [@CL-PRG-0410; @CL-PRG-0413].

Under paragraph (a)(2), if appropriate, the contracting officer should hold a presolicitation conference (see 15.201) and/or send copies of the proposed solicitation to all prospective offerors for their comments [@CL-PRG-0410; @CL-PRG-0414]. After evaluation of the comments, the solicitation should be revised, if appropriate [@CL-PRG-0410; @CL-PRG-0415].

The contracting officer shall send the final solicitation to all prospective offerors, and it shall [@CL-PRG-0416]:

- Describe the nature of the need in terms of mission capabilities required, without reference to any specific systems to satisfy the need [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0417].
- Indicate, and explain when appropriate, the schedule, capability, and cost objectives and any known constraints in the acquisition [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0418].
- Provide, or indicate how access can be obtained to, all Government data related to the acquisition [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0419].
- Include selection requirements consistent with the acquisition strategy [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0420; @CL-PRG-0114].
- Clearly state that each offeror is free to propose its own technical approach, main design features, subsystems, and alternatives to schedule, cost, and capability goals [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0114; @CL-PRG-0423].
- Require the use of an Earned Value Management System that complies with the guidelines of Electronic Industries Alliance Standard 748 (EIA-748) (current version at time of solicitation) [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0421].

Paragraph (b)(6) also refers to FAR 34.201 for earned value management systems and reporting requirements [@CL-PRG-0424].

Under paragraph (c), to the extent practicable, the solicitation shall not reference or mandate Government specifications or standards, unless the agency is mandating a subsystem or other component as approved under agency procedure [@CL-PRG-0422].

## How it works

Under paragraph (b), the contracting officer shall send the final solicitation to all prospective offerors, and the final solicitation shall include the six items in paragraphs (b)(1) through (b)(6), including requiring the use of an Earned Value Management System that complies with the guidelines of EIA-748 (current version at time of solicitation) [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0421].

The presolicitation steps in paragraph (a) are written with "should" and apply whenever practicable and consistent with agency procedures [@CL-PRG-0410]. Holding a presolicitation conference and/or sending copies of the proposed solicitation to all prospective offerors for their comments applies if appropriate, and revising the solicitation after evaluation of comments also applies if appropriate [@CL-PRG-0414; @CL-PRG-0415]. Sending the final solicitation and the content requirements in paragraph (b) are written with "shall" [@CL-PRG-0416].

The final solicitation describes the nature of the need in terms of mission capabilities required, without reference to any specific systems to satisfy the need, and clearly states that each offeror is free to propose its own technical approach, main design features, subsystems, and alternatives to schedule, cost, and capability goals [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0417; @CL-PRG-0423]. Paragraph (b)(6) requires the use of an Earned Value Management System that complies with the guidelines of EIA-748 (current version at time of solicitation) and refers to FAR 34.201 for earned value management systems and reporting requirements [@CL-PRG-0421; @CL-PRG-0424]. For what FAR 34.201 requires, see [When the FAR requires an earned value management system](../evms/prg-far-evms-requirement.md) [@CL-PRG-0424].

For the policy behind the section, see [Acquisition policy for major systems](prg-far-policy-major-system-acquisition.md). For the wider competition duties, see [Competition throughout a major system acquisition](prg-far-competition-major-system-acquisition.md). For the defense supplement's cross-reference on these solicitations, see [Major system acquisition definitions and mission-oriented solicitations](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-major-system-acquisition-definitions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer drafts a final solicitation for a mission system. The draft names a specific radar to be bought, and the radar is not a subsystem or component that the agency is mandating as approved under agency procedure. Paragraph (b)(1) requires the final solicitation to describe the nature of the need in terms of mission capabilities required, without reference to any specific systems to satisfy the need [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0417]. Naming a specific system to satisfy the need conflicts with paragraph (b)(1) [@CL-PRG-0417].

The draft also says nothing about whether an offeror may propose a different design. Paragraph (b)(5) requires the solicitation to clearly state that each offeror is free to propose its own technical approach, main design features, subsystems, and alternatives to schedule, cost, and capability goals [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0423]. The draft must include that statement [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0423]. It must also require the use of an Earned Value Management System that complies with the guidelines of EIA-748, the current version at the time of solicitation [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0421].

Suppose the draft also mandates a Government standard for the radar mounting, and no approval under agency procedure covers it. Under paragraph (c), to the extent practicable, the solicitation shall not reference or mandate Government specifications or standards, unless the agency is mandating a subsystem or other component as approved under agency procedure [@CL-PRG-0422].

The contracting officer held no presolicitation conference. Paragraph (a)(2) states that, if appropriate, the contracting officer should hold a presolicitation conference and/or send copies of the proposed solicitation to all prospective offerors for their comments [@CL-PRG-0410; @CL-PRG-0414].

## Never confuse

Under paragraph (b)(1), the final solicitation shall describe the nature of the need in terms of mission capabilities required, without reference to any specific systems to satisfy the need [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0417], and under paragraph (b)(5), it shall clearly state that each offeror is free to propose its own technical approach, main design features, subsystems, and alternatives to schedule, cost, and capability goals [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0114; @CL-PRG-0423]. The presolicitation steps in paragraph (a) are written with "should" whenever practicable and consistent with agency procedures [@CL-PRG-0410], unlike sending the final solicitation to all prospective offerors and meeting the paragraph (b) content requirements, which are written with "shall" [@CL-PRG-0416]. Paragraph (b)(6) requires the use of an Earned Value Management System that complies with the guidelines of EIA-748 (current version at time of solicitation) [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0421], while paragraph (b)(5) requires stating that each offeror is free to propose its own technical approach, main design features, subsystems, and alternatives to schedule, cost, and capability goals [@CL-PRG-0416; @CL-PRG-0423].
