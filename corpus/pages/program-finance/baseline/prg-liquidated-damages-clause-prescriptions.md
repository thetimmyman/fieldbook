## Purpose

Shows when the contracting officer's determination calls for a liquidated damages clause, which clause goes in which kind of contract, and when a construction contract also needs the Time Extensions clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0472] |
| Codified citation | FAR 11.500 [@CL-PRG-1000; @CL-PRG-1001], FAR 11.502 [@CL-PRG-1002; @CL-PRG-1003; @CL-PRG-1004; @CL-PRG-9040] and FAR 11.503 [@CL-PRG-0472] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0472] |
| Applies to | Fixed-price solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, or research and development (paragraph (a)), solicitations and contracts for construction other than cost-plus-fixed-fee (paragraph (b)), in each of those two cases when the contracting officer determines that liquidated damages are appropriate [@CL-PRG-0472; @CL-PRG-0473], and, for the Time Extensions clause (paragraph (c)), solicitations and contracts for construction that use the construction liquidated damages clause when it has been revised as provided in paragraph (b) [@CL-PRG-0475] |

Paragraph (a) says to use the clause at 52.211-11, Liquidated Damages—Supplies, Services, or Research and Development, in fixed-price solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, or research and development when the contracting officer determines that liquidated damages are appropriate (see 11.501(a)) [@CL-PRG-0472].

Paragraph (b) says to use the clause at 52.211-12, Liquidated Damages—Construction, in solicitations and contracts for construction, other than cost-plus-fixed-fee, when the contracting officer determines that liquidated damages are appropriate (see 11.501(a)) [@CL-PRG-0473]. If the contract specifies more than one completion date for separate parts or stages of the work, paragraph (a) of the clause is to be revised to state the amount of liquidated damages for delay of each separate part or stage of the work [@CL-PRG-0474].

Paragraph (c) says to use the clause at 52.211-13, Time Extensions, in solicitations and contracts for construction that use the clause at 52.211-12 when that clause has been revised as provided in paragraph (b) [@CL-PRG-0475].

## How it works

Subpart 11.5 prescribes policies and procedures for using liquidated damages clauses in solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, research and development, and construction [@CL-PRG-1000]. This subpart does not apply to liquidated damages for subcontracting plans (see 19.705-7), liquidated damages related to the Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards statute (see subpart 22.3), or liquidated damages related to paid sick leave for Federal contractors (see subpart 22.21) [@CL-PRG-1001].

Under FAR 11.502, the applicable liquidated damages clause and liquidated damages rates shall be included in solicitations when the contract will contain liquidated damages provisions [@CL-PRG-1002]. Construction contracts with liquidated damages provisions must describe the rate(s) of liquidated damages assessed per day of delay [@CL-PRG-1003]. The rate(s) should include the estimated daily cost of Government inspection and superintendence [@CL-PRG-1004], and should also include an amount for other expected expenses associated with delayed completion, such as renting substitute property or paying additional allowance for living quarters [@CL-PRG-9040].

Both clause prescriptions in FAR 11.503(a) and (b) apply when the contracting officer determines that liquidated damages are appropriate (see 11.501(a)) [@CL-PRG-0472; @CL-PRG-0473]. The card [delivery schedules and liquidated damages](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-delivery-schedules-and-liquidated-damages.md) covers that policy.

Paragraph (a) applies to fixed-price solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, or research and development [@CL-PRG-0472], while paragraph (b) applies to solicitations and contracts for construction, other than cost-plus-fixed-fee [@CL-PRG-0473].

When a construction contract specifies more than one completion date for separate parts or stages of the work, paragraph (a) of the clause at 52.211-12 is revised to state the amount of liquidated damages for delay of each separate part or stage of the work [@CL-PRG-0474]. The Time Extensions clause at 52.211-13 is prescribed for solicitations and contracts for construction that use the clause at 52.211-12 when that clause has been revised as provided in paragraph (b) [@CL-PRG-0475].

The clause texts, daily amounts and accrual rules are on [liquidated damages for late delivery and for late construction](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-liquidated-damages.md) and [time extensions](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-time-extensions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price construction contract has two completion dates, one for site preparation and one for the finished building. The contracting officer has determined that liquidated damages are appropriate. The contract is not cost-plus-fixed-fee, so paragraph (b) calls for the Liquidated Damages—Construction clause [@CL-PRG-0473]. Because the contract specifies more than one completion date for separate parts or stages of the work, paragraph (a) of the clause is revised to state an amount for each part, for example 400 per day of delay for site preparation and 900 per day for the finished building [@CL-PRG-0474]. A single amount for the whole job would not follow that revision [@CL-PRG-0474]. Because the clause was revised that way, the Time Extensions clause is also prescribed [@CL-PRG-0475].

A cost-plus-fixed-fee construction contract is outside paragraph (b), so paragraph (b) does not prescribe the construction clause for it, even if the contracting officer considers liquidated damages appropriate [@CL-PRG-0473]. If the contracting officer has not determined that liquidated damages are appropriate for a fixed-price supply contract, the condition in paragraph (a) is not met [@CL-PRG-0472].

## Never confuse

Paragraph (c) prescribes the clause at 52.211-13, Time Extensions, in solicitations and contracts for construction that use the clause at 52.211-12 when that clause has been revised as provided in paragraph (b) [@CL-PRG-0475]. Paragraph (a) applies to fixed-price solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, or research and development [@CL-PRG-0472], unlike paragraph (b), which applies to solicitations and contracts for construction, other than cost-plus-fixed-fee [@CL-PRG-0473].

Who confirms that each executed construction contract states damages by stage and carries the time extensions clause where it should. [@Q-004]
