## Purpose

Shows how the guide builds the performance measurement baseline from scope, schedule and budget, and where management reserve sits.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD EVMS Interpretation Guide, not a codified instrument [@CL-PRG-0639] |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Programs that plan, schedule and budget work against a performance measurement baseline [@CL-PRG-0639] |

The performance measurement baseline is a dollarized time-phased plan, set primarily at the control account level, that reflects how the contractor intends to use its resources for all authorized work [@CL-PRG-0639]. The sum of management reserve and the baseline is the contract budget base, also called the total allocated budget, which is reconcilable with the contract target cost [@CL-PRG-0640]. A portion of the contract budget base may be withheld outside the baseline as management reserve, to manage risk within the established contract scope [@CL-PRG-0642].

The integrated network schedule gives visibility into the accomplishment of the tasks required for the contractual scope and is the basis for creating the baseline [@CL-PRG-0641]. The category stresses the integrity of the baseline: the sum of lower-level budgets in the baseline must not exceed the total time-phased budget, and all internal program budgets and management reserve reconcile to the contractual target cost [@CL-PRG-0643]. Among the planning parameters the guide lists is ensuring that the budgets of work packages and planning packages sum to the total budget authorized for the control account [@CL-PRG-0644].

## How it works

This page is the overview of the category: the schedule underlies the baseline [@CL-PRG-0641], and the guideline pages hold the detail on the schedule, the work and planning packages, the baseline, the reserve and the reconciliation to target cost.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has a target cost of 10,000,000. The baseline holds 9,200,000 and the company keeps 800,000 as management reserve. The contract budget base is 10,000,000, which reconciles to the target cost. The answer: baseline plus reserve equals the budget base.

## Never confuse

Management reserve is held outside the baseline and is not part of it [@CL-PRG-0642], while the contract budget base is the sum of the two [@CL-PRG-0640]. For how reserve and undistributed budget are handled, see [the reserve guideline](prg-evms-guideline-12-undistributed-budget-management-reserve.md).
