## Purpose

Shows how the Planning, Scheduling, and Budgeting category establishes the performance measurement baseline, the contract budget base, and management reserve [@CL-PRG-0639; @CL-PRG-0640; @CL-PRG-0642].

The performance measurement baseline is a dollarized time-phased plan established primarily at the control account level that reflects how the contractor intends to use its resources to accomplish all the authorized work (Guidelines 7 and 8) [@CL-PRG-0639; @CL-PRG-0108]. The sum of management reserve and the performance measurement baseline equals the contract budget base, which is also the total allocated budget and is reconcilable with the contract target cost (Guideline 8) [@CL-PRG-0640]. A portion of the contract budget base may be withheld outside of the performance measurement baseline as management reserve, which is not part of the performance measurement baseline and is intended to provide the contractor with budget to manage risk within the established contract scope (Guideline 12) [@CL-PRG-0642; @CL-PRG-0110].

The integrated network schedule provides visibility into the accomplishment of the tasks required for execution of the contractual scope of work and is the basis for creating the performance measurement baseline (Guideline 6) [@CL-PRG-0641]. The guidelines in this category emphasize maintaining the integrity of the performance measurement baseline by ensuring the sum of lower level budgets in the performance measurement baseline does not exceed the total time-phased budget and that all internal program budgets and management reserve reconcile to the contractual target cost (Guideline 13) [@CL-PRG-0110; @CL-PRG-0643]. The guidelines further establish planning parameters associated with the performance measurement baseline, including ensuring that the budgets of work packages and planning packages sum to the total budget authorized for the control account (Guideline 11) [@CL-PRG-0109; @CL-PRG-0644].

## How it works

The integrated network schedule provides visibility into the accomplishment of the tasks required for execution of the contractual scope of work and is the basis for creating the performance measurement baseline [@CL-PRG-0641]. Within the category, the guidelines cover the schedule on [Guideline 6](../evms/prg-evms-guideline-6-schedule-work.md), the performance measurement baseline on [Guideline 7](prg-evms-guideline-7-performance-measurement-baseline.md), work and planning packages on [Guideline 9](../evms/prg-evms-guideline-9-work-and-planning-packages.md), undistributed budget and management reserve on [Guideline 12](prg-evms-guideline-12-undistributed-budget-management-reserve.md), and reconciliation to target cost on [Guideline 13](prg-evms-guideline-13-reconcile-target-cost.md) [@CL-PRG-0639; @CL-PRG-0108; @CL-PRG-0641; @CL-PRG-0642; @CL-PRG-0110; @CL-PRG-0643].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has a target cost of 10,000,000. The baseline holds 9,200,000 and the company keeps 800,000 as management reserve. The contract budget base is 10,000,000, which reconciles to the target cost. The answer: baseline plus reserve equals the budget base.

## Never confuse

Management reserve is withheld outside of the performance measurement baseline and is not part of the performance measurement baseline [@CL-PRG-0642; @CL-PRG-0110], while the sum of management reserve and the performance measurement baseline equals the contract budget base [@CL-PRG-0640]. For how reserve and undistributed budget are handled, see [the reserve guideline](prg-evms-guideline-12-undistributed-budget-management-reserve.md).
