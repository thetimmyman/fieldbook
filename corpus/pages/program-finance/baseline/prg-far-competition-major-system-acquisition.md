## Purpose

Shows who carries the competition duty during a major system acquisition, what notice the proposed acquisition must get, when foreign contractors, technology and equipment may be considered, and how the contracting officer should time solicitation and award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0406] |
| Codified citation | FAR 34.005-1 [@CL-PRG-0406]; FAR 34.001 [@CL-PRG-9045; @CL-PRG-9046]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-PRG-9094] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-PRG-9103] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0406] |
| Applies to | The program manager and the contracting officer in a major system acquisition [@CL-PRG-0406; @CL-PRG-0409] |

The program manager must, throughout the acquisition process, promote full and open competition and sustain effective competition between alternative major system concepts and sources, as long as it is economically beneficial and practicable to do so [@CL-PRG-0406]. Codified FAR 2.101 defines full and open competition, when used with respect to a contract action, to mean that all responsible sources are permitted to compete [@CL-PRG-9094]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines full and open competition, when used with respect to a contract action, to mean that all responsible sources are permitted to compete [@CL-PRG-9103].

Notice of the proposed acquisition must be given the broadest and most effective circulation practicable throughout the business, academic, and Government communities [@CL-PRG-0407]. Foreign contractors, technology, and equipment may be considered when it is feasible and permissible to do so [@CL-PRG-0408].

The contracting officer should time solicitation issuance and contract award to maintain continuity of concept development during the transition from withdrawing concept proposer to new contractor [@CL-PRG-0409].

As used in part 34, effective competition is a market condition that exists when two or more contractors, acting independently, actively contend for the Government's business in a manner that ensures that the Government will be offered the lowest cost or price alternative or best technical design meeting its minimum needs [@CL-PRG-9045; @CL-PRG-9046].

## How it works

Under FAR 34.005-1(a), the program manager shall, throughout the acquisition process, promote full and open competition and sustain effective competition between alternative major system concepts and sources, as long as it is economically beneficial and practicable to do so [@CL-PRG-0406].

Notice of the proposed acquisition shall be given the broadest and most effective circulation practicable throughout the business, academic, and Government communities [@CL-PRG-0407]. Foreign contractors, technology, and equipment may be considered when it is feasible and permissible to do so [@CL-PRG-0408].

Under FAR 34.005-1(b), the contracting officer should time solicitation issuance and contract award to maintain continuity of concept development during the transition from withdrawing concept proposer to new contractor [@CL-PRG-0409].

For the policy that sits behind these duties, see [Acquisition policy for major systems](prg-far-policy-major-system-acquisition.md). For the solicitation itself, see [Mission-oriented solicitations for major systems](prg-far-mission-oriented-solicitation.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program has two competing concept sources. The program manager must promote full and open competition and sustain effective competition between the alternative concepts and sources, as long as it is economically beneficial and practicable to do so [@CL-PRG-0406]. Suppose funding the second source is no longer economically beneficial. The condition "as long as it is economically beneficial and practicable to do so" is then no longer met for sustaining that competition [@CL-PRG-0406].

Notice of the proposed acquisition must be given the broadest and most effective circulation practicable throughout the business, academic, and Government communities [@CL-PRG-0407]. Suppose the first concept proposer withdraws and a new contractor is to take over. The contracting officer should time solicitation issuance and contract award to maintain continuity of concept development during the transition from withdrawing concept proposer to new contractor [@CL-PRG-0409].

## Never confuse

Paragraph (a) states that the program manager shall promote full and open competition and sustain effective competition between alternative major system concepts and sources, as long as it is economically beneficial and practicable to do so [@CL-PRG-0406], while paragraph (b) states that the contracting officer should time solicitation issuance and contract award to maintain continuity of concept development during the transition from withdrawing concept proposer to new contractor [@CL-PRG-0409]. Under paragraph (a), notice of the proposed acquisition shall be given the broadest and most effective circulation practicable throughout the business, academic, and Government communities [@CL-PRG-0407], while foreign contractors, technology, and equipment may be considered when it is feasible and permissible to do so [@CL-PRG-0408].
