## Purpose

Shows how program management turns a significant cost or schedule variance into a corrective action management plan that is tracked to resolution.

A corrective action management plan indicates that the program manager has recognized a cost or schedule variance significant enough to require management actions through a closed-loop process that runs from identification through closure [@CL-PRG-0355]. The plan states what, how, who, and when for the actions taken to address identified root causes and minimize their impact, and it is documented, implemented, and monitored until the problem is resolved [@CL-PRG-0356].

## How it works

The guideline’s purpose is to ensure that all levels of program management review performance measurement data, implement corrective action management plans, and use the information for decision making [@CL-PRG-0354]. Each plan states what, how, who, and when for the management actions taken to address identified root causes and minimize their impact, and it is documented, implemented, and monitored until resolution [@CL-PRG-0356]. The internal reports and the reports forwarded to the customer must indicate the overall cost and schedule impacts of the issues [@CL-PRG-0357].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A control account shows an unfavorable schedule variance of 40 against its plan. The program manager opens a corrective action plan that names the root cause, assigns an owner, and sets a recovery date. The plan is tracked monthly until the variance is closed, and the cost and schedule impact appears in both the internal review and the customer report.

## Never confuse

Reviewing performance data is not the same as acting on it. The summarize-and-review guideline rolls variance data up to management, while this guideline requires a tracked corrective action plan once a variance is significant enough to need management action.

## Company calibration

Whether each significant cost or schedule variance is tied to a corrective action management plan that is tracked to closure. [@Q-037]
