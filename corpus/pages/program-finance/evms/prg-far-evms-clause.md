## Purpose

Shows the contractor duties under the earned value management system clause: the system it must use, the baseline review, changes to the system and flowdown to subcontractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0909] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.234-4 [@CL-PRG-0909] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0909] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-PRG-0909] |

The Contractor must use an EVMS that the Cognizant Federal Agency has determined to be compliant with the EIA-748 guidelines, in the current version at the time of award, to manage the contract [@CL-PRG-0910]. If the Contractor's current EVMS has not been determined compliant at the time of award, the clause sends it to paragraph (b) [@CL-PRG-0911]. That paragraph applies if, at the time of award, the system has not been determined by the Cognizant Federal Agency as complying with the EVMS guidelines or the Contractor does not have an existing cost/schedule control system that is compliant with the EIA-748 guidelines [@CL-PRG-0957]. Where it applies, the Contractor must apply the current system to the contract and take necessary actions to meet the milestones in the EVMS plan approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0912].

The Government will conduct an Integrated Baseline Review, and if no pre-award review has been conducted, a post award review must be conducted as early as practicable after contract award [@CL-PRG-0913]. The Contracting Officer may also require a review at the exercise of significant options or the incorporation of major modifications [@CL-PRG-0914].

## How it works

Unless the Cognizant Federal Agency grants a waiver, proposed EVMS changes need its approval before implementation, and it will advise the Contractor of their acceptability within 30 calendar days after receipt of the notice of proposed changes [@CL-PRG-0915]. If it waives the advance approval requirements, the Contractor must disclose the changes to it at least 14 calendar days before the effective date of implementation [@CL-PRG-0916]. The Contractor must give access to all pertinent records and data that the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative requests for Government surveillance of whether the EVMS conforms and continues to conform [@CL-PRG-0917]. The Contractor must also require the subcontractors specified in the clause to comply with its requirements [@CL-PRG-0918]. The clause leaves the list of subcontractors to be inserted, so the printed clause does not name the subcontractors that apply on a given contract [@CL-PRG-0918].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor plans to change how it sets up control accounts in its EVMS. The cognizant agency has waived advance approval for such changes. The change is planned to take effect at the start of a month, so the contractor must disclose it to the agency at least 14 days before that date. The answer: the waiver removes the wait for approval but not the duty to disclose.

## Never confuse

This card is the contract clause. The solicitation notices that ask an offeror to show its system or file a plan are covered by [Notice of earned value management system, preaward and postaward review](prg-far-evms-notice-provisions.md) and are not restated here. Whether a contract needs an earned value system at all is on [When the FAR requires an earned value management system](prg-far-evms-requirement.md), the provisions and clause the contracting officer inserts are on [Integrated Baseline Review timing, cost, provisions and clause](../risk-opportunity/prg-far-ibr-timing-clauses.md), and the reports and subcontractor rule are on [EVMS monthly reports and subcontractors under the FAR](../eac/prg-far-evms-reports-subcontractors.md). A waiver of advance approval is also not a waiver of disclosure [@CL-PRG-0916].

## Company calibration

Whether the cognizant agency has determined the contract's system compliant, which subcontractors the contract lists for flowdown, and who tracks EVMS changes before they take effect. [@Q-037]
