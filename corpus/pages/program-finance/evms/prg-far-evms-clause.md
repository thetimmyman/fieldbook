## Purpose

Shows the contractor duties under the earned value management system clause: the system it must use, the baseline review, changes to the system and flowdown to subcontractors.

The Contractor shall use an earned value management system (EVMS) that has been determined by the Cognizant Federal Agency to be compliant with the guidelines in Electronic Industries Alliance Standard 748 (EIA-748) (current version at the time of award) to manage this contract [@CL-PRG-0910]. If the Contractor's current EVMS has not been determined compliant at the time of award, see paragraph (b) of the clause [@CL-PRG-0911]. Under paragraph (b), if, at the time of award, the Contractor's EVM System has not been determined by the Cognizant Federal Agency as complying with EVMS guidelines or the Contractor does not have an existing cost/schedule control system that is compliant with the guidelines in EIA-748 (current version at time of award), the Contractor shall apply the current system to the contract and take necessary actions to meet the milestones in the Contractor's EVMS plan approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0957; @CL-PRG-0912].

The Government will conduct an Integrated Baseline Review, and if a pre-award Integrated Baseline Review has not been conducted, a post award Integrated Baseline Review shall be conducted as early as practicable after contract award [@CL-PRG-0913]. The Contracting Officer may require an Integrated Baseline Review at the exercise of significant options or the incorporation of major modifications [@CL-PRG-0914].

## How it works

Unless a waiver is granted by the Cognizant Federal Agency, Contractor proposed EVMS changes require approval of the Cognizant Federal Agency prior to implementation, and the Cognizant Federal Agency will advise the Contractor of the acceptability of such changes within 30 calendar days after receipt of the notice of proposed changes from the Contractor [@CL-PRG-0915]. If the advance approval requirements are waived by the Cognizant Federal Agency, the Contractor shall disclose EVMS changes to the Cognizant Federal Agency at least 14 calendar days prior to the effective date of implementation [@CL-PRG-0916]. The Contractor shall provide access to all pertinent records and data requested by the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative as necessary to permit Government surveillance to ensure that the EVMS conforms, and continues to conform, with the performance criteria referenced in paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-PRG-0917; @CL-PRG-0077]. The Contractor shall require the subcontractors specified in the clause to comply with the requirements of the clause [@CL-PRG-0918].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-4(a), the Contractor must use an EVMS that has been determined by the Cognizant Federal Agency to be compliant with the guidelines in EIA-748 (current version at the time of award) to manage this contract [@CL-PRG-0005], and if the Contractor's current EVMS has not been determined compliant at the time of award, see paragraph (b) of the clause [@CL-PRG-0006]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-4(b), if the Contractor's EVM System is not compliant with the requirements in paragraph (a) of the clause, or the Contractor's existing cost/schedule control system is not compliant with the guidelines in EIA-748 (current version at time of award), the Contractor must apply the current system to the contract and take necessary actions to meet the milestones in the Contractor's approved EVMS plan [@CL-PRG-0007; @CL-PRG-0008], whereas codified FAR 52.234-4(b) applies if, at the time of award, the Contractor's EVM System has not been determined by the Cognizant Federal Agency as complying with EVMS guidelines or the Contractor does not have an existing cost/schedule control system that is compliant with the guidelines in EIA-748 (current version at time of award), and requires the Contractor to apply the current system to the contract and take necessary actions to meet the milestones in the Contractor's EVMS plan approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0957; @CL-PRG-0912]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-4(c), when an EVMS is required, the Government will conduct an Integrated Baseline Review, and agencies may require an additional Integrated Baseline Review at exercise of significant options or incorporation of major modifications [@CL-PRG-0009], whereas codified FAR 52.234-4 requires a post award Integrated Baseline Review to be conducted as early as practicable after contract award if a pre-award Integrated Baseline Review has not been conducted [@CL-PRG-0913] and states that the Contracting Officer may require an Integrated Baseline Review at the exercise of significant options or incorporation of major modifications [@CL-PRG-0914]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-4(d), unless the Cognizant Federal Agency grants a waiver, Contractor-proposed EVMS changes require Cognizant Federal Agency approval prior to implementation [@CL-PRG-0010], the Cognizant Federal Agency will notify the Contractor of its acceptance or rejection of the proposed EVMS changes within 30 days of receipt [@CL-PRG-0011], and if the Cognizant Federal Agency waives the advance approval requirements, the Contractor must disclose EVMS changes to the Cognizant Federal Agency at least 14 days prior to the effective date of implementation [@CL-PRG-0012], whereas codified FAR 52.234-4 states that the Cognizant Federal Agency will advise the Contractor of the acceptability of such changes within 30 calendar days after receipt of the notice of proposed changes from the Contractor [@CL-PRG-0915] and that if the advance approval requirements are waived by the Cognizant Federal Agency, the Contractor shall disclose EVMS changes to the Cognizant Federal Agency at least 14 calendar days prior to the effective date of implementation [@CL-PRG-0916]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-4(e), the Contractor must provide the Government access to all pertinent records and data necessary to conduct surveillance and validate that the EVMS complies with the requirements in paragraph (a) of the clause for the duration of the contract [@CL-PRG-0013], whereas codified FAR 52.234-4 requires the Contractor to provide access to all pertinent records and data requested by the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative as necessary to permit Government surveillance to ensure that the EVMS conforms, and continues to conform, with the performance criteria referenced in paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-PRG-0917; @CL-PRG-0077]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-4(f), the Contractor must require the subcontractors specified in the clause to comply with the requirements of the clause [@CL-PRG-0014].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor plans to change how it sets up control accounts in its EVMS. The cognizant agency has waived advance approval for such changes. The change is planned to take effect at the start of a month, so the contractor must disclose it to the agency at least 14 days before that date. The answer: the waiver removes the wait for approval but not the duty to disclose.
