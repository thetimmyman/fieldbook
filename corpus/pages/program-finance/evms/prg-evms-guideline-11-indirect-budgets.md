## Purpose

Shows how indirect budgets are built from indirect rates and carried in the performance measurement baseline at a level where they can be seen and managed [@CL-PRG-0262].

| Applies to | Programs that operate an earned value management system [@CL-PRG-0262] |

Indirect rates are developed by the responsible organizations and applied to the program’s direct resource budgets at the applicable level, which produces the indirect budgets that support the baseline [@CL-PRG-0262]. Indirect budgets such as overhead, general and administrative cost, and cost of money are to be established and included in the baseline at the appropriate level for visibility [@CL-PRG-0263].

The budgets are established at the appropriate organizational level for each pool and cost sub-element, and they are developed and planned in conjunction with the direct budgets consistent with the contractor’s documented procedures for approving and allocating indirect cost [@CL-PRG-0265; @CL-PRG-0266].

## How it works

Actual indirect expense is compared with the established indirect budgets, which tells the contractor whether absorption under the existing allocation schemes is on track or whether a rate needs adjustment [@CL-PRG-0268]. The indirect budgets are planned alongside the direct budgets and are consistent with the contractor’s documented procedures for approving and allocating indirect cost [@CL-PRG-0266]. The budgets enter the baseline with the current rates, whether those are approved, provisional or proposed [@CL-PRG-0267]. The guide treats it as critical to the contractor that all indirect expenses are allocated by the end of the accounting year [@CL-PRG-0264].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program carries 500,000 of direct labour budget. The responsible organization sets an overhead rate of 40 percent, so the baseline carries 200,000 of overhead budget alongside that direct labour. If actual absorption of overhead runs ahead of the budgeted amount, the contractor checks whether the allocation rate needs adjustment rather than waiting for year end.

## Never confuse

An indirect budget is not a direct resource budget. The direct resource budgets are the base the rates are applied to, and the indirect budget is what applying those rates at the applicable level produces [@CL-PRG-0262].

## Company calibration

Whether each pool carries an indirect budget in the baseline at the level the responsible organization manages it, and which rate vintage was used. [@Q-037]
