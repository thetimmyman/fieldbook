## Purpose

Shows what a value engineering change proposal is under the value engineering clause, what it must contain, where it goes and how the Government acts on it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0919] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.248-1 [@CL-PRG-0919]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-PRG-9084; @CL-PRG-9085] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.248-1 [@CL-PRG-0038; @CL-PRG-0041; @CL-PRG-0042; @CL-PRG-0043; @CL-PRG-0044; @CL-PRG-0045; @CL-PRG-0046; @CL-PRG-0047; @CL-PRG-0048], with Alternate I [@CL-PRG-0039; @CL-PRG-0040]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-PRG-9107; @CL-PRG-9108] |
| Edition date | JUN 2020 [@CL-PRG-9170] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0919] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-PRG-0919] |

Under paragraph (a) of the basic clause, the Contractor is encouraged to develop, prepare, and submit value engineering change proposals (VECPs) voluntarily, and shall share in any net acquisition savings realized from accepted VECPs, in accordance with the incentive sharing rates in paragraph (f) [@CL-PRG-0920]. If the contracting officer selects a mandatory value engineering program requirement, Alternate I substitutes a paragraph (a) under which the Contractor shall engage in a value engineering program, and submit value engineering progress reports as specified in the Schedule, and submit to the Contracting Officer any resulting VECPs [@CL-PRG-0958; @CL-PRG-9146]. A VECP means a proposal that requires a change to the instant contract to implement and results in reducing the overall projected cost to the agency without impairing essential functions or characteristics, provided that it does not involve a change in deliverable end item quantities only, in research and development (R&D) end items or R&D test quantities that is due solely to results of previous testing under the contract, or to the contract type only [@CL-PRG-0921; @CL-PRG-0922].

Under codified FAR 2.101, value engineering means an analysis of the functions of a program, project, system, product, item of equipment, building, facility, service, or supply of an executive agency, performed by qualified agency or contractor personnel [@CL-PRG-9084], directed at improving performance, reliability, quality, safety, and life-cycle costs (41 U.S.C. 1711) [@CL-PRG-9085]. Under RFO FAR 2.101, value engineering means an analysis of the functions of a program, project, system, product, item of equipment, building, facility, service, or supply of an executive agency, performed by qualified agency or contractor personnel [@CL-PRG-9107], directed at improving performance, reliability, quality, safety, and life-cycle costs (41 U.S.C. 1711) [@CL-PRG-9108].

As a minimum, the Contractor shall include in each VECP the information described in subparagraphs (1) through (8) of paragraph (c) [@CL-PRG-0923]. The Contractor shall submit VECPs to the Contracting Officer, unless the contract states otherwise, and if the contract is administered by other than the contracting office, the Contractor shall submit a copy of the VECP simultaneously to the Contracting Officer and to the Administrative Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0089; @CL-PRG-0924].

## How it works

Under the basic clause, the Contractor is encouraged to develop, prepare, and submit VECPs voluntarily [@CL-PRG-0920], including at a minimum in each VECP the information described in subparagraphs (1) through (8) [@CL-PRG-0923], and shall submit VECPs to the Contracting Officer, unless the contract states otherwise [@CL-PRG-0089; @CL-PRG-0924]. The Contracting Officer will notify the Contractor of the status of the VECP within 45 calendar days after the contracting office receives it [@CL-PRG-0925]. If additional time is required, the Contracting Officer will notify the Contractor within the 45-day period and provide the reason for the delay and the expected date of the decision [@CL-PRG-0969]. Until such a contract modification applies a VECP to the contract, the Contractor shall perform in accordance with the existing contract, and the decision to accept or reject all or part of any VECP is a unilateral decision made solely at the discretion of the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0926]. See [Value engineering clause: sharing rates and savings calculation](prg-far-value-engineering-sharing.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(a), the Contractor is encouraged to develop, prepare, and submit VECPs voluntarily, and shall share in any net acquisition savings realized from accepted VECPs, in accordance with the incentive sharing rates in paragraph (f) of the clause [@CL-PRG-0038], whereas codified FAR 52.248-1(a) refers to paragraph (f) below [@CL-PRG-0920]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1 Alternate I, if the contracting officer selects a mandatory value engineering program requirement, a substituted paragraph (a) requires the Contractor to engage in a value engineering program, and submit value engineering progress reports, as specified in the Schedule and submit to the Contracting Officer any resulting VECPs [@CL-PRG-0039; @CL-PRG-0040]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(b), a VECP means a proposal that requires a change to the instant contract to implement and results in reducing the overall projected cost to the agency without impairing essential functions or characteristics, provided that it does not involve a change in deliverable end item quantities only, in R&D end items or R&D test quantities that is due solely to results of previous testing under the contract, or to the contract type only [@CL-PRG-0041; @CL-PRG-0042]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(c), as a minimum, the Contractor shall include in each VECP the information described in paragraphs (c)(1) through (8) of the clause [@CL-PRG-0043], whereas codified FAR 52.248-1(c) refers to subparagraphs (1) through (8) below [@CL-PRG-0923]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(d), the Contractor shall submit VECPs to the Contracting Officer, unless the contract states otherwise [@CL-PRG-0044], and if the contract is administered by other than the contracting office, the Contractor shall submit a copy of the VECP simultaneously to the Contracting Officer and to the Administrative Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0045]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(e), the Contracting Officer will notify the Contractor of the status of the VECP within 45 calendar days after the contracting office receives it [@CL-PRG-0046], if additional time is required, the Contracting Officer will notify the Contractor within the 45-day period and provide the reason for the delay and the expected date of the decision [@CL-PRG-0047], and until such a contract modification applies a VECP to the contract, the Contractor shall perform in accordance with the existing contract and the decision to accept or reject all or part of any VECP is a unilateral decision made solely at the discretion of the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0048].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor finds a design change that lowers projected agency cost without harming essential functions. It sends the proposal to the Contracting Officer. The Contracting Officer must give a status within 45 days after the contracting office receives it. A second idea only cuts the delivered quantity by 100 units, so it is outside the VECP definition. The answer: the first is a VECP and the second is not.

## Never confuse

Under codified FAR 52.248-1(e)(3) [@CL-PRG-0926] and RFO FAR 52.248-1(e)(3) [@CL-PRG-0048], the decision to accept or reject all or part of any VECP is a unilateral decision made solely at the discretion of the Contracting Officer, and until a contract modification applies a VECP to the contract, the Contractor shall perform in accordance with the existing contract. Under codified FAR 52.248-1(b) [@CL-PRG-0921; @CL-PRG-0922] and RFO FAR 52.248-1(b) [@CL-PRG-0041; @CL-PRG-0042], a VECP does not involve a change in deliverable end item quantities only, in R&D end items or R&D test quantities that is due solely to results of previous testing under the contract, or to the contract type only. See [Value engineering clause: sharing rates and savings calculation](prg-far-value-engineering-sharing.md).
