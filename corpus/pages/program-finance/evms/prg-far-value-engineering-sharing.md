## Purpose

Shows how the value engineering clause sets the contractor share of savings, offsets Government costs, adjusts the contract and calculates concurrent and future shares.

## The rule

If a VECP is accepted, the Contractor shall share in net acquisition savings according to the percentages shown in the sharing table [@CL-PRG-0959]. The percentage paid the Contractor depends upon this contract's type (fixed-price, incentive, or cost-reimbursement), the sharing arrangement specified in paragraph (a) above (incentive, program requirement, or a combination as delineated in the Schedule), and the source of the savings (the instant contract, or concurrent and future contracts) [@CL-PRG-0927; @CL-PRG-0090].

For fixed-price contracts, including fixed-price-award-fee and excluding other fixed-price incentive contracts, the table gives 50 percent for the instant contract rate and 50 percent for the concurrent and future contract rate under the incentive (voluntary) arrangement, both marked with note 1, and 25 percent for the instant contract rate and 25 percent for the concurrent and future contract rate under the program requirement (mandatory) arrangement [@CL-PRG-0928]. Note 1 states that the Contracting Officer may increase the Contractor's sharing rate to as high as 75 percent for each VECP [@CL-PRG-0929].

For incentive contracts (fixed-price or cost) (other than award fee), the concurrent and future contract rate is 50 percent under the incentive (voluntary) arrangement, marked with note 1, and 25 percent under the program requirement (mandatory) arrangement, and both instant contract rates are marked with note 2 [@CL-PRG-0928; @CL-PRG-0960]. Note 2 states that the sharing arrangement is the same as the contract's profit or fee adjustment formula [@CL-PRG-0962].

For cost-reimbursement contracts, including cost-plus-award-fee and excluding other cost-type incentive contracts, the incentive (voluntary) arrangement gives 25 percent for both the instant contract rate and the concurrent and future contract rate, both marked with note 3, and the program requirement (mandatory) arrangement gives 15 percent for both [@CL-PRG-0928; @CL-PRG-0961]. Note 3 states that the Contracting Officer may increase the Contractor's sharing rate to as high as 50 percent for each VECP [@CL-PRG-0963].

Net acquisition savings are first realized, and the Contractor shall be paid a share, when Government costs and any negative instant contract savings have been fully offset against acquisition savings [@CL-PRG-0964]. Except in incentive contracts, Government costs and any price or cost increases resulting from negative instant contract savings shall be offset against acquisition savings each time such savings are realized until they are fully offset [@CL-PRG-0930]. If this is an incentive contract, recovery of Government costs on the instant contract shall be deferred and offset against concurrent and future contract savings [@CL-PRG-0965].

## How it works

Under codified FAR 52.248-1(h), the modification accepting the VECP (or a subsequent modification issued as soon as possible after any negotiations are completed) shall reduce the contract price or estimated cost by the amount of instant contract savings, unless this is an incentive contract [@CL-PRG-0931]; when the amount of instant contract savings is negative, increase the contract price, target price and ceiling price, target cost, or estimated cost by that amount [@CL-PRG-0092]; specify the Contractor's dollar share per unit on future contracts, or provide the lump-sum payment [@CL-PRG-0092]; specify the amount of any Government costs or negative instant contract savings to be offset in determining net acquisition savings realized from concurrent or future contract savings [@CL-PRG-0093]; and provide the Contractor's share of any net acquisition savings under the instant contract by adding it to the contract price for fixed-price contracts and to the contract fee for cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-PRG-0932]. For concurrent contract savings, the Contracting Officer shall calculate the Contractor's share by subtracting from the reduction in price negotiated on the concurrent contract any Government costs or negative instant contract savings not yet offset and multiplying the result by the Contractor's sharing rate [@CL-PRG-9151; @CL-PRG-0933]. For future contract savings, the Contracting Officer shall calculate the Contractor's share by multiplying the future unit cost reduction by the number of future contract units scheduled for delivery during the sharing period, subtracting any Government costs or negative instant contract savings not yet offset, and multiplying the result by the Contractor's sharing rate [@CL-PRG-9152; @CL-PRG-0934].

Under codified FAR 52.248-1(j), for collateral savings, if a VECP is accepted, the Contracting Officer will increase the instant contract amount, as specified in paragraph (h)(5) of the clause, by a rate from 20 to 100 percent, as determined by the Contracting Officer, of any projected collateral savings determined to be realized in a typical year of use after subtracting any Government costs not previously offset [@CL-PRG-9147; @CL-PRG-0966]. However, the Contractor's share of collateral savings will not exceed the contract's firm-fixed-price, target price, target cost, or estimated cost, at the time the VECP is accepted, or $100,000, whichever is greater [@CL-PRG-0967].

Under codified FAR 52.248-1(l), the Contractor shall include an appropriate value engineering clause in any subcontract valued at or above the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, and may include one in subcontracts of lesser value [@CL-PRG-0968].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(f), if a VECP is accepted, the Contractor shall share in net acquisition savings according to the percentages shown in the table [@CL-PRG-0049], and the percentage paid the Contractor depends upon this contract's type (fixed-price, incentive, or cost-reimbursement), the sharing arrangement specified in paragraph (a) of the clause (incentive, program requirement, or a combination as delineated in the Schedule), and the source of the savings (the instant contract, or concurrent and future contracts) [@CL-PRG-0091; @CL-PRG-0050; @CL-PRG-9002], whereas codified FAR 52.248-1(f) refers to paragraph (a) above [@CL-PRG-0927]. The RFO table gives 50 and 25 percent for fixed-price contracts, including fixed-price-award-fee and excluding other fixed-price incentive contracts [@CL-PRG-0051], note (**) for the instant contract rates and 50 and 25 percent for the concurrent and future contract rates for incentive contracts (fixed-price or cost) (other than award fee) where note (**) states the same sharing arrangement as the contract's profit or fee adjustment formula [@CL-PRG-0052; @CL-PRG-0055], and 25 and 15 percent for cost-reimbursement contracts, including cost-plus-award-fee and excluding other cost-type incentive contracts [@CL-PRG-0053]. In the RFO table footnotes, the Contracting Office may increase the Contractor's sharing rate to as high as 75 percent for each VECP under note (*) [@CL-PRG-0054] and to as high as 50 percent for each VECP under note (***) [@CL-PRG-0056], whereas in the codified table notes 1 and 3 state that the Contracting Officer may increase the Contractor's sharing rate to as high as 75 percent for each VECP [@CL-PRG-0929] and 50 percent for each VECP [@CL-PRG-0963]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(g), net acquisition savings are first realized, and the Contractor shall be paid a share, when Government costs and any negative instant contract savings have been fully offset against acquisition savings [@CL-PRG-0057]; except in incentive contracts, Government costs and any price or cost increases resulting from negative instant contract savings shall be offset against acquisition savings each time such savings are realized until they are fully offset [@CL-PRG-0058]; and if this is an incentive contract, recovery of Government costs on the instant contract shall be deferred and offset against concurrent and future contract savings [@CL-PRG-0059]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(h), the modification accepting the VECP (or a subsequent modification issued as soon as possible after any negotiations are completed) shall reduce the contract price or estimated cost by the amount of instant contract savings, unless this is an incentive contract [@CL-PRG-0060]; when the amount of instant contract savings is negative, increase the contract price, target price and ceiling price, target cost, or estimated cost by that amount [@CL-PRG-0094]; specify the Contractor's dollar share per unit on future contracts, or provide the lump-sum payment [@CL-PRG-0094]; specify the amount of any Government costs or negative instant contract savings to be offset in determining net acquisition savings realized from concurrent or future contract savings [@CL-PRG-0095]; and provide the Contractor's share of any net acquisition savings under the instant contract by adding it to the contract price for fixed-price contracts and to the contract fee for cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-PRG-0061]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(i), the Contracting Officer calculates the Contractor's share of concurrent contract savings by subtracting from the reduction in price negotiated on the concurrent contract any Government costs or negative instant contract savings not yet offset and multiplying the result by the Contractor's sharing rate [@CL-PRG-0062], and calculates the Contractor's share of future contract savings by multiplying the future unit cost reduction by the number of future contract units scheduled for delivery during the sharing period, subtracting any Government costs or negative instant contract savings not yet offset, and multiplying the result by the Contractor's sharing rate [@CL-PRG-9000; @CL-PRG-0063]. Under RFO FAR 52.248-1(j), if a VECP is accepted, the Contracting Officer will increase the instant contract amount, as specified in paragraph (h)(5) of the clause, by a rate from 20 to 100 percent, as determined by the Contracting Officer, of any projected collateral savings determined to be realized in a typical year of use after subtracting any Government costs not previously offset [@CL-PRG-9001; @CL-PRG-0064], and the Contractor's share of collateral savings will not exceed the contract's firm-fixed-price, target price, target cost, or estimated cost, at the time the VECP is accepted, or $100,000, whichever is greater [@CL-PRG-0065]. ## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An accepted proposal leads to a price reduction of 100,000 on a concurrent contract. Government costs not yet offset are 20,000. The sharing rate is 50 percent. The Contractor's share is 50 percent of the 80,000 left after the offset, which is 40,000. The answer: the Government costs come off first, then the rate applies.

## Never confuse

Under codified FAR 52.248-1(g) [@CL-PRG-0964; @CL-PRG-0930; @CL-PRG-0965] and RFO FAR 52.248-1(g) [@CL-PRG-0057; @CL-PRG-0058; @CL-PRG-0059], net acquisition savings are first realized, and the Contractor shall be paid a share, when Government costs and any negative instant contract savings have been fully offset against acquisition savings; except in incentive contracts, those costs and increases shall be offset against acquisition savings each time such savings are realized until they are fully offset, and in an incentive contract, recovery of Government costs on the instant contract shall be deferred and offset against concurrent and future contract savings. Under codified FAR 52.248-1(i) [@CL-PRG-9151; @CL-PRG-0933; @CL-PRG-9152; @CL-PRG-0934] and RFO FAR 52.248-1(i) [@CL-PRG-0062; @CL-PRG-9000; @CL-PRG-0063], the Contractor's share of concurrent contract savings is calculated from the reduction in price negotiated on the concurrent contract, whereas the Contractor's share of future contract savings is calculated by multiplying the future unit cost reduction by the number of future contract units scheduled for delivery during the sharing period. See [Value engineering clause: change proposals and submission](prg-far-value-engineering-general.md). In the codified sharing table notes, the Contracting Officer may increase the Contractor's sharing rate [@CL-PRG-0929; @CL-PRG-0963], whereas in the RFO sharing table footnotes, the Contracting Office may increase the Contractor's sharing rate [@CL-PRG-0054; @CL-PRG-0056].
