## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must show about its earned value management system (EVMS) under the preaward and postaward notice provisions, and when the Integrated Baseline Review is held.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.234-2 and 52.234-3 [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |
| Applies to | Solicitations where the FAR prescribes the provision [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |

Both notices are provisions for solicitations, one titled for a preaward review and the other for a postaward review [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902]. In both, the offeror must provide documentation that the Cognizant Federal Agency has determined that the proposed system complies with the EVMS guidelines in EIA-748, in the current version at the time of solicitation [@CL-PRG-0903; @CL-PRG-0907].

An offeror that proposes a system not determined to comply must submit a comprehensive plan for compliance with the EVMS guidelines [@CL-PRG-0904; @CL-PRG-0908]. The Government will review and approve that plan before contract award in both notices [@CL-PRG-0951; @CL-PRG-0952], and the plan must provide milestones that indicate when the offeror anticipates that the system will be compliant with the EIA-748 guidelines [@CL-PRG-0953; @CL-PRG-0954]. In the preaward notice the plan shall describe the EVMS the offeror intends to use in performance of the contracts and distinguish the offeror's existing management system from the modifications proposed to meet the guidelines, among other listed items [@CL-PRG-0956].

## How it works

In the printed text the two notices carry the same documentation and plan paragraphs [@CL-PRG-0903; @CL-PRG-0904; @CL-PRG-0907; @CL-PRG-0908]. Each notice also has a subcontractor paragraph: the offeror must identify the major subcontractors, or the major subcontracted effort if major subcontractors have not been selected, and the prime Contractor and the Government must agree on the subcontractors selected for application of the guidelines; the preaward text says the major subcontractors or effort are identified as subject to the guidelines, and the postaward text says they are identified as planned for application of the guidelines [@CL-PRG-0905; @CL-PRG-0955]. The preaward notice also sets the review date: the Government will hold an Integrated Baseline Review, as the agency designates, before contract award [@CL-PRG-0906]. The text printed for the postaward notice has no paragraph on when a review is held; the earned value clause says that where no pre-award review has been conducted, a post award review is conducted as early as practicable after contract award [@CL-PRG-0913].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror answers a solicitation that carries the preaward notice. Its system has not been determined compliant, so it files a compliance plan and names 2 major subcontractors. The Government holds the baseline review before award. The answer: the offeror owes a plan because it lacks the determination, and the review comes before award.

## Never confuse

The notices are not the [earned value management system clause](prg-far-evms-clause.md): the notices are provisions [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902], while that clause is a clause [@CL-PRG-0909]. Whether a system is required at all is on [When the FAR requires an earned value management system](prg-far-evms-requirement.md), and which notice goes into which solicitation is on [Integrated Baseline Review timing, cost, provisions and clause](../risk-opportunity/prg-far-ibr-timing-clauses.md); neither is restated here. A compliance plan is also not a determination of compliance: the plan is what an offeror owes when its system has not been determined to comply [@CL-PRG-0904].
