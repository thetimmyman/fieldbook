## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must show about its earned value management system (EVMS) under the preaward and postaward notice provisions, and when the Integrated Baseline Review is held.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.234-2 and 52.234-3 [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent [@CL-PRG-0015; @CL-PRG-0016] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |
| Applies to | Solicitations where the FAR prescribes the provision [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902] |

FAR 52.234-2 is a provision titled Notice of Earned Value Management System—Preaward Integrated Baseline Review, prescribed in FAR 34.203(a) [@CL-PRG-0901], and FAR 52.234-3 is a provision titled Notice of Earned Value Management System—Postaward Integrated Baseline Review, prescribed in FAR 34.203(b) [@CL-PRG-0902]. Under both provisions, the offeror shall provide documentation that the Cognizant Federal Agency has determined that the proposed earned value management system (EVMS) complies with the EVMS guidelines in Electronic Industries Alliance Standard 748 (EIA-748) (current version at time of solicitation) [@CL-PRG-0903; @CL-PRG-0907].

Under both provisions, if the offeror proposes to use a system that has not been determined to be in compliance with the requirements of paragraph (a) of the provision, the offeror shall submit a comprehensive plan for compliance with the EVMS guidelines [@CL-PRG-0904; @CL-PRG-0908]. Under both provisions, the plan shall describe the EVMS the offeror intends to use in performance of the contracts, distinguish between the offeror's existing management system and modifications proposed to meet the guidelines, describe the management system and its application in terms of the EVMS guidelines, describe the proposed procedure for administration of the guidelines as applied to subcontractors, and provide documentation describing the process and results of any third-party or self-evaluation of the system's compliance with the EVMS guidelines [@CL-PRG-0956; @CL-PRG-0078; @CL-PRG-0079; @CL-PRG-0080; @CL-PRG-0081; @CL-PRG-0082]. Under both provisions, the offeror shall provide information and assistance as required by the Contracting Officer to support review of the plan [@CL-PRG-0083; @CL-PRG-0084], the Government will review and approve the offeror's plan for an EVMS before contract award [@CL-PRG-0951; @CL-PRG-0952], and the offeror's EVMS plan must provide milestones that indicate when the offeror anticipates that the EVM system will be compliant with the EIA-748 guidelines [@CL-PRG-0953; @CL-PRG-0954].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.234-2, offerors shall identify the major subcontractors, or major subcontracted effort if major subcontractors have not been selected subject to the guidelines, and the prime Contractor and the Government shall agree to subcontractors selected for application of the EVMS guidelines [@CL-PRG-0905]. Under FAR 52.234-3, offerors shall identify the major subcontractors, or major subcontracted effort if major subcontractors have not been selected, planned for application of the guidelines, and the prime Contractor and the Government shall agree to subcontractors selected for application of the EVMS guidelines [@CL-PRG-0955]. Under FAR 52.234-2, the Government will conduct an Integrated Baseline Review, as designated by the agency, prior to contract award [@CL-PRG-0906]. Under FAR 52.234-4, the Government will conduct an Integrated Baseline Review, and if a pre-award Integrated Baseline Review has not been conducted, a post award Integrated Baseline Review shall be conducted as early as practicable after contract award [@CL-PRG-0913].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. In the revised FAR Part 52, 52.234-2 is [Reserved] [@CL-PRG-0015] and 52.234-3 is [Reserved] [@CL-PRG-0016]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-4(c), when an EVMS is required, the Government will conduct an Integrated Baseline Review, and agencies may require an additional Integrated Baseline Review at exercise of significant options or incorporation of major modifications [@CL-PRG-0009].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror answers a solicitation that carries the preaward notice. Its system has not been determined compliant, so it files a compliance plan and names 2 major subcontractors. The Government holds the baseline review before award. The answer: the offeror owes a plan because it lacks the determination, and the review comes before award.

## Never confuse

FAR 52.234-2 and FAR 52.234-3 are provisions [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902], whereas [FAR 52.234-4](prg-far-evms-clause.md) is a clause [@CL-PRG-0909]. See [When the FAR requires an earned value management system](prg-far-evms-requirement.md) and [Integrated Baseline Review timing, cost, provisions and clause](../risk-opportunity/prg-far-ibr-timing-clauses.md). Under both FAR 52.234-2 and FAR 52.234-3, if the offeror proposes to use a system that has not been determined to be in compliance with the requirements of paragraph (a) of the provision, the offeror shall submit a comprehensive plan for compliance with the EVMS guidelines [@CL-PRG-0904; @CL-PRG-0908]. In the codified FAR, 52.234-2 and 52.234-3 set out preaward and postaward notice provisions [@CL-PRG-0901; @CL-PRG-0902; @CL-PRG-0903; @CL-PRG-0907], whereas in RFO FAR Part 52, 52.234-2 and 52.234-3 are [Reserved] [@CL-PRG-0015; @CL-PRG-0016].
