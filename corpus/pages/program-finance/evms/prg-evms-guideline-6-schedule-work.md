## Purpose

Governs the requirement to schedule authorized work in an integrated network describing work sequence and task interdependencies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD EVMS Interpretation Guide [@CL-PRG-0206] |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | Programs that schedule authorized work in an Integrated Master Schedule [@CL-PRG-0207] |

The contractor must schedule the authorized work in a manner that describes the sequence of work and identifies task interdependencies required to meet the requirements of the program [@CL-PRG-0206]. The Integrated Master Schedule includes a baseline and forecast schedule, and the forecast schedule is the basis for the time-phased Estimate to Complete [@CL-PRG-0207; @CL-PRG-0208]. The prime contractor is responsible for planning and integrating material and subcontracted effort into the prime's Integrated Master Schedule [@CL-PRG-0210].

## How it works

The logical sequence of the schedule is horizontally and vertically integrated [@CL-PRG-0564]. The Integrated Master Schedule contains all authorized discrete work assigned to program organizational elements, including subcontractors, responsible for performing the work consistent with the Work Breakdown Structure [@CL-PRG-0565]. In the event Level of Effort is included in the Integrated Master Schedule, it will not impact discrete work or the calculation of the critical and driving paths [@CL-PRG-0209]. Schedule Visibility Tasks have no resources and provide management visibility into a timespan that may otherwise be modelled in the Integrated Master Schedule with a lag or constrained milestone [@CL-PRG-0211]. A Schedule Visibility Task is not part of the budgeted program scope in the Performance Measurement Baseline nor used to assess earned value performance [@CL-PRG-0212]. Schedule margin, if used, is identifiable, derived from a risk management approach, justifiable in duration, and may only be represented in the schedule as the last task before key contractual events, significant logical integration/test milestones, end item deliverables, and/or contract completion [@CL-PRG-0213]. Schedule margin may not represent any specific scope or resources, and may not be used to assess earned value performance [@CL-PRG-0566].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An integration schedule contains 50 linked discrete tasks culminating in a delivery milestone at month 12. Management identifies technical delivery risk and inserts a 1-month schedule margin task immediately preceding the final delivery milestone, supported by schedule risk assessment simulations. The schedule margin carries 0 budget and earns 0 value, preserving baseline integrity while buffering critical path handoffs.

## Never confuse

A Schedule Visibility Task is not part of the budgeted program scope in the Performance Measurement Baseline nor used to assess earned value performance [@CL-PRG-0212]. Schedule margin may not represent any specific scope or resources, and may not be used to assess earned value performance [@CL-PRG-0566]. See [Earned Value Management System clause](prg-earned-value-management-system.md).
