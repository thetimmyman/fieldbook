## Purpose

Shows when the contractor must notify the contracting officer of a change or addition to the make-or-buy program, and what price adjustment the alternate paragraph adds.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0935] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-9 [@CL-PRG-0935] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-9 [@CL-PRG-0017; @CL-PRG-0018; @CL-PRG-0019; @CL-PRG-0020], with Alternate I [@CL-PRG-0021; @CL-PRG-0022; @CL-PRG-0023] and Alternate II [@CL-PRG-0024; @CL-PRG-0025; @CL-PRG-0026] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0935] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-PRG-0935] |

If the Contractor proposes to change the program, the Contractor shall, reasonably in advance of the proposed change, notify the Contracting Officer in writing and submit justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0936]. Changes in the place of performance of any "make" items in the program are subject to this requirement [@CL-PRG-0970]. For items deferred at the time of negotiation of this contract for later addition to the program, the Contractor shall, at the earliest possible time, notify the Contracting Officer of each proposed addition and provide justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0937]. Modification of the make-or-buy program to incorporate proposed changes or additions shall be effective upon the Contractor's receipt of the Contracting Officer's written approval [@CL-PRG-0971].

As prescribed in FAR 15.408(a)(1), Alternate I adds paragraph (d) to the basic clause, under which if the Contractor desires to reverse the categorization of "make" or "buy" for any item or items designated in the contract as subject to that paragraph, it shall support its proposal with certified cost or pricing data in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2 when required by FAR 15.403, and data other than certified cost or pricing data, to permit evaluation, and after approval is granted, promptly negotiate with the Contracting Officer an equitable reduction in the contract price in accordance with paragraph (k) of the Incentive Price Revision—Firm Target clause or paragraph (m) of the Incentive Price Revision—Successive Targets clause of this contract [@CL-PRG-0938; @CL-PRG-0972; @CL-PRG-0939].

As prescribed in FAR 15.408(a)(2), Alternate II adds paragraph (d) to the basic clause, under which if the Contractor desires to reverse the categorization of "make" or "buy" for any item or items designated in the contract as subject to that paragraph, it shall support its proposal with certified cost or pricing data in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2, when required by FAR 15.403, and data other than certified cost or pricing data, to permit evaluation, support its proposal with cost or pricing data to permit evaluation, and after approval is granted, promptly negotiate with the Contracting Officer an equitable reduction in the contract's total estimated cost and fee in accordance with paragraph (e) of the Incentive Fee clause of this contract [@CL-PRG-0973; @CL-PRG-0085; @CL-PRG-0974].

## How it works

If the Contractor proposes to change the program, the Contractor shall, reasonably in advance of the proposed change, notify the Contracting Officer in writing and submit justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0936], and for items deferred at the time of negotiation of this contract for later addition to the program, the Contractor shall, at the earliest possible time, notify the Contracting Officer of each proposed addition and provide justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0937]. Modification of the make-or-buy program to incorporate proposed changes or additions shall be effective upon the Contractor's receipt of the Contracting Officer's written approval [@CL-PRG-0971]. Under paragraph (d) added by Alternate I or Alternate II, if the Contractor desires to reverse the categorization of "make" or "buy" for any item or items designated in the contract as subject to that paragraph, it shall support its proposal with the required data and, after approval is granted, promptly negotiate with the Contracting Officer an equitable reduction in the contract price or in the contract's total estimated cost and fee [@CL-PRG-0938; @CL-PRG-0972; @CL-PRG-0939; @CL-PRG-0973; @CL-PRG-0085; @CL-PRG-0974].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-9(a), if the Contractor proposes to change the program, the Contractor shall, reasonably in advance of the proposed change, notify the Contracting Officer in writing and submit justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0017], and changes in the place of performance of any "make" items in the program are subject to this requirement [@CL-PRG-0018]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-9(b), for items deferred at the time of negotiation of this contract for later addition to the program, the Contractor shall, at the earliest possible time, notify the Contracting Officer of each proposed addition and provide justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0019]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-9(c), modification of the make-or-buy program to incorporate proposed changes or additions shall be effective upon the Contractor's receipt of the Contracting Officer's written approval [@CL-PRG-0020]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-9 Alternate I paragraph (d), if the Contractor desires to reverse the categorization of "make" or "buy" for any item or items designated in the contract as subject to that paragraph, it shall support its proposal with certified cost or pricing data in accordance with FAR 15.408-2, Table 15-1, when required by FAR 15.403-3, and data other than certified cost or pricing data, to permit evaluation, and after approval is granted, promptly negotiate with the Contracting Officer an equitable reduction in the contract price in accordance with paragraph (k) of the Incentive Price Revision-Firm Target clause or paragraph (m) of the Incentive Price Revision-Successive Targets clause of this contract [@CL-PRG-0021; @CL-PRG-0022; @CL-PRG-0023], whereas codified Alternate I paragraph (d)(1) cites FAR 15.408, Table 15-2 and FAR 15.403 [@CL-PRG-0972]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-9 Alternate II paragraph (d), if the Contractor desires to reverse the categorization of "make" or "buy" for any item or items designated in the contract as subject to that paragraph, it shall support its proposal with certified cost or pricing data in accordance with FAR 15.408-2, Table 15-1, when required by FAR 15.403-3, and data other than certified cost or pricing data, to permit evaluation, support its proposal with cost or pricing data to permit evaluation, and in paragraph (d)(3), after approval is granted, promptly negotiate with the Contracting Officer an equitable reduction in the contract's total estimated cost and fee in accordance with paragraph (e) of the Incentive Fee clause of this contract [@CL-PRG-0024; @CL-PRG-0025; @CL-PRG-0026].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract designates an item as subject to the Alternate I paragraph. The contractor wants to switch the item from make to buy. It notifies the Contracting Officer in advance with justification, gets approval and then negotiates an equitable reduction in the contract price. The answer: the price reduction follows approval, and it applies because the item was designated.

## Never confuse

See [Make-or-buy programs: when they are required and what they contain](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-make-or-buy-programs.md). Under codified FAR 52.215-9(c) [@CL-PRG-0971] and RFO FAR 52.215-9(c) [@CL-PRG-0020], modification of the make-or-buy program to incorporate proposed changes or additions shall be effective upon the Contractor's receipt of the Contracting Officer's written approval. Under both Alternate I and Alternate II of codified FAR 52.215-9 [@CL-PRG-0938; @CL-PRG-0939; @CL-PRG-0973; @CL-PRG-0974] and RFO FAR 52.215-9 [@CL-PRG-0021; @CL-PRG-0023; @CL-PRG-0024; @CL-PRG-0026], the duty to promptly negotiate an equitable reduction after approval is granted applies if the Contractor desires to reverse the categorization of "make" or "buy" for any item or items designated in the contract as subject to paragraph (d). Under Alternate II, the equitable reduction in the contract's total estimated cost and fee is in paragraph (d)(2) in the codified text [@CL-PRG-0974] and in paragraph (d)(3) in the RFO text [@CL-PRG-0026].
