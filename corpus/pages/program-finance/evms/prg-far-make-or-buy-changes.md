## Purpose

Shows when the contractor must notify the contracting officer of a change or addition to the make-or-buy program, and what price adjustment the alternate paragraph adds.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0935] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-9 [@CL-PRG-0935] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0935] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-PRG-0935] |

If the Contractor proposes to change the make-or-buy program, it must, reasonably in advance of the proposed change, notify the Contracting Officer in writing and submit justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0936]. For items deferred at the time of negotiation for later addition to the program, the Contractor must, at the earliest possible time, notify the Contracting Officer of each proposed addition and provide justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0937]. Changes in the place of performance of any make items in the program are subject to the requirement to notify the Contracting Officer in writing and submit justification in sufficient detail to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0970]. Modification of the program to incorporate proposed changes or additions is effective upon the Contractor's receipt of the Contracting Officer's written approval [@CL-PRG-0971].

The printed text of the basic clause holds no price adjustment rule. Alternate I adds a paragraph (d) under which a Contractor that desires to reverse the make or buy categorization of an item designated in the contract as subject to that paragraph must do the listed things [@CL-PRG-0938]. One listed duty is to support its proposal with certified cost or pricing data when required by FAR 15.403 and with data other than certified cost or pricing data to permit evaluation [@CL-PRG-0972]. Another is, after approval is granted, to promptly negotiate with the Contracting Officer an equitable reduction in the contract price in accordance with the named paragraph of the Incentive Price Revision, Firm Target or Successive Targets clause [@CL-PRG-0939].

Alternate II also adds a paragraph (d) for items designated in the contract as subject to it [@CL-PRG-0973]. One listed duty there is, after approval is granted, to promptly negotiate with the Contracting Officer an equitable reduction in the contract's total estimated cost and fee in accordance with the named paragraph of the Incentive Fee clause [@CL-PRG-0974].

## How it works

A proposed change needs advance written notice with justification [@CL-PRG-0936], and a deferred item needs notice at the earliest possible time [@CL-PRG-0937]. A change takes effect when the Contractor receives the Contracting Officer's written approval [@CL-PRG-0971]. The basic clause has no price adjustment; a price adjustment follows approval under the paragraph that Alternate I or Alternate II adds, for items designated as subject to it [@CL-PRG-0938; @CL-PRG-0939; @CL-PRG-0973; @CL-PRG-0974]. This card does not restate the support-data items printed under Alternate II.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract designates an item as subject to the Alternate I paragraph. The contractor wants to switch the item from make to buy. It notifies the Contracting Officer in advance with justification, gets approval and then negotiates an equitable reduction in the contract price. The answer: the price reduction follows approval, and it applies because the item was designated.

## Never confuse

The program itself, who must have one and what it contains, is on [Make-or-buy programs: when they are required and what they contain](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-make-or-buy-programs.md) and is not restated here. Giving notice is also not an approval: a change takes effect when the Contractor receives the Contracting Officer's written approval [@CL-PRG-0971]. The price reduction is not a general rule for every change, because the printed text ties it to the paragraph that an alternate adds [@CL-PRG-0938; @CL-PRG-0973].
