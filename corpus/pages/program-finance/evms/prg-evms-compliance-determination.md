## Purpose

Shows how compliance of an earned value management system is judged, what the contractor must document and demonstrate, and how subcontractor work is treated.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD EVMS Interpretation Guide, not a codified instrument [@CL-PRG-0627] |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Contractors with a contractual earned value requirement, and primes that integrate subcontractor data [@CL-PRG-0629; @CL-PRG-0630] |

There are three steps for determining compliance [@CL-PRG-0627]. The first is to assess whether the contractor's EVM System Description adequately documents the processes and procedures which support how its system meets the intent of the 27 Guidelines [@CL-PRG-0627]. The second is to evaluate the contractor's ability to demonstrate the EVMS implementation as described in the System Description and supplemental procedures, and the third is to ensure the EVMS is providing timely, accurate, reliable, and auditable data [@CL-PRG-0628]. When contractually required, contractors are required to demonstrate compliance with the 27 Guidelines, regardless of EVM reporting requirements defined in the contractual Contract Data Requirements List (CDRL) [@CL-PRG-0629].

To address this requirement, the contractor maintains a stand-alone EVM System Description (SD) or a set or series of integrated process descriptions/procedures that describe its approach to a compliant EVMS [@CL-PRG-0633]. It is incumbent upon the prime contractor to document and demonstrate an effective methodology for managing the integrating subcontractor's data into its EVMS [@CL-PRG-0630]. The term "Subcontractors" includes inter-company work agreements whose planning, execution, and reporting of scope and schedule are independent of the prime [@CL-PRG-0631], whereas inter-company work scope and schedule whose daily operations are under the direct management control of the prime and are directly integrated into the prime effort are not considered a subcontract [@CL-PRG-0632]. The criteria for determining a subcontractor or directly integrated approach for inter-company work should be documented in the contractor's system description [@CL-PRG-0804].

## How it works

The three steps for determining compliance are to assess whether the contractor's EVM System Description adequately documents the processes and procedures which support how its system meets the intent of the 27 Guidelines, evaluate the contractor's ability to demonstrate the EVMS implementation as described in the System Description and supplemental procedures, and ensure the EVMS is providing timely, accurate, reliable, and auditable data [@CL-PRG-0627; @CL-PRG-0628]. It is incumbent upon the prime contractor to document and demonstrate an effective methodology for managing the integrating subcontractor's data into its EVMS [@CL-PRG-0630]. The term "Subcontractors" includes inter-company work agreements whose planning, execution, and reporting of scope and schedule are independent of the prime, while inter-company work scope and schedule whose daily operations are under the direct management control of the prime and are directly integrated into the prime effort are not considered a subcontract, and the criteria for determining a subcontractor or directly integrated approach for inter-company work should be documented in the contractor's system description [@CL-PRG-0631; @CL-PRG-0632; @CL-PRG-0804]. See [the Interpretation Guide page](prg-evms-interpretation-guide.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime passes an earned value requirement down to one outside supplier. It also has an affiliate whose daily work is run by the prime's own managers and built into the prime schedule. The prime treats the supplier as a subcontractor and sets up a method to bring its data into the prime system. The affiliate is not treated as a subcontract. The answer: one subcontractor to integrate, and one directly managed team.

## Never confuse

When contractually required, contractors must demonstrate compliance with the 27 Guidelines regardless of the EVM reporting requirements defined in the contractual Contract Data Requirements List (CDRL) [@CL-PRG-0629], and while inter-company work agreements whose planning, execution, and reporting of scope and schedule are independent of the prime are treated as subcontractors, inter-company work scope and schedule under the direct daily management control of the prime and directly integrated into the prime effort are not considered a subcontract [@CL-PRG-0631; @CL-PRG-0632]. See [Earned value management system requirement](prg-evms-requirement.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company's System Description is current, has been shown in practice, and covers subcontractors. [@Q-037]
