## Purpose

Shows when a DoD contract needs an earned value management system, when that system must be formally determined compliant, and which contracts are exempt.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD class deviation [@CL-PRG-0001] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 234.201(1)(i) and (ii) [@CL-PRG-9144; @CL-PRG-9145] |
| RFO citation | Class Deviation 2026-O0011 Rev 1, DFARS 234.201-70(a)(1) and (2) [@CL-PRG-0001; @CL-PRG-0002] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text [@CL-PRG-0001] |
| Applies to | DoD cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts, from 2026-08-05 [@CL-PRG-0001] |

Except for the exemption at paragraph (j) of the deviation section, DoD applies the earned value management system requirement as follows [@CL-PRG-9140]. For cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts valued at $50 million or more, the clause at 252.234-7999, Earned Value Management System, requires the contractor's earned value management system to comply with the guidelines in Electronic Industries Alliance Standard 748, Earned Value Management Systems (EIA-748) [@CL-PRG-0001; @CL-PRG-9141]. For cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts valued at $100 million or more, the clause at 252.234-7999, Earned Value Management System, requires the contractor to have an earned value management system that the cognizant Federal agency has determined to comply with the guidelines in EIA-748 [@CL-PRG-0002; @CL-PRG-9142]. The subpart does not apply to contracts primarily for software, including those using a software acquisition pathway in accordance with DoD Instruction 5000.87, Operation of the Software Acquisition Pathway, or awarded to nontraditional defense contractors [@CL-PRG-0003]. The head of the contracting activity may waive the exemption relating to nontraditional defense contractors by written determination that applying the requirement for an earned value management system to a nontraditional defense contractor is in the best interest of DoD, and may delegate this authority to no lower than the chief of the contracting office [@CL-PRG-0004; @CL-PRG-9143].

## How it works

Under the deviation, for cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts valued at $50 million or more, the clause at 252.234-7999 requires the contractor's earned value management system to comply with the guidelines in EIA-748 [@CL-PRG-0001; @CL-PRG-9141], and for cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts valued at $100 million or more, the clause requires the contractor to have an earned value management system that the cognizant Federal agency has determined to comply with the guidelines in EIA-748 [@CL-PRG-0002; @CL-PRG-9142]. In the codified DFARS, for cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts valued at $20,000,000 or more, the earned value management system must comply with the guidelines in ANSI/EIA-748 [@CL-PRG-9144], and for cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts valued at $50,000,000 or more, the contractor must have an earned value management system that has been determined by the cognizant Federal agency to be in compliance with the guidelines in ANSI/EIA-748 [@CL-PRG-9145].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-incentive-fee subcontract is valued at 70 million, above a lower threshold of 50 million and below a higher threshold of 100 million. The subcontractor's system must follow the guidelines, but the requirement for a system determined compliant starts at the higher threshold. A firm-fixed-price subcontract of the same value is outside the requirement.

## Never confuse

The codified DFARS requires compliance with the ANSI/EIA-748 guidelines at $20,000,000 or more [@CL-PRG-9144] and a system determined by the cognizant Federal agency to be in compliance at $50,000,000 or more [@CL-PRG-9145], whereas the deviation requires compliance with the EIA-748 guidelines at $50 million or more [@CL-PRG-0001; @CL-PRG-9141] and a system determined by the cognizant Federal agency to comply at $100 million or more [@CL-PRG-0002; @CL-PRG-9142]. Under the deviation, the subpart does not apply to contracts primarily for software, including those using a software acquisition pathway in accordance with DoD Instruction 5000.87, Operation of the Software Acquisition Pathway, or awarded to nontraditional defense contractors [@CL-PRG-0003], though the head of the contracting activity may waive the exemption relating to nontraditional defense contractors by written determination that applying the requirement is in the best interest of DoD [@CL-PRG-0004; @CL-PRG-9143].
