## Purpose

Shows when a DoD contract needs an earned value management system, when that system must be formally determined compliant, and which contracts are exempt.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD class deviation [@CL-PRG-0001] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 234.201(1)(i) and (ii) [@CL-PRG-0001] [@CL-PRG-0002] |
| RFO citation | Class Deviation 2026-O0011 Rev 1, DFARS 234.201-70(a)(1) and (2) [@CL-PRG-0001] [@CL-PRG-0002] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text [@CL-PRG-0001] |
| Applies to | DoD cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts, from 2026-08-05 [@CL-PRG-0001] |

For DoD cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts of $50 million or more, the earned value management system must comply with the EIA-748 guidelines [@CL-PRG-0001]. At $100 million or more, the contractor must have a system the cognizant agency has determined compliant [@CL-PRG-0002]. The requirement does not apply to contracts primarily for software or to contracts awarded to nontraditional defense contractors, [@CL-PRG-0003]. The head of the contracting activity may waive the nontraditional-contractor exemption, in writing [@CL-PRG-0004].

## How it works

Check the exemptions first, then the contract type, then the value. Between the two thresholds the system must comply with the guidelines but needs no formal determination; at or above the higher one it needs a compliance determination by the cognizant agency [@CL-PRG-0002]. The system measures progress against the Performance Measurement Baseline and reports cost and schedule variances. The codified DFARS values are lower than the deviation values [@CL-PRG-0001] [@CL-PRG-0002].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-incentive-fee subcontract is valued at 70 million, above a lower threshold of 50 million and below a higher threshold of 100 million. The subcontractor's system must follow the guidelines, but no formal compliance determination is required. A firm-fixed-price subcontract of the same value is outside the requirement.

## Never confuse

An earned value management system is the management system; the Performance Measurement Baseline is the time-phased budget it measures against. Being a nontraditional defense contractor is not the same as being a small business; the exemption follows the defined term, not company size.
