## Purpose

Shows how the contracting officer approves or disapproves a contractor's earned value management system, from the first findings to the final determination, and what follows a disapproval.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-PRG-0600] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 234.201(5) to (9) [@CL-PRG-0600; @CL-PRG-0612; @CL-PRG-0614] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0600] |
| Applies to | Contractor earned value management systems reviewed by the cognizant contracting officer [@CL-PRG-0600] |

The cognizant contracting officer, in consultation with the functional specialist and auditor, decides whether the contractor's system is acceptable, approves or disapproves it, and pursues correction of weaknesses [@CL-PRG-0600]. The test is whether the system meets the system criteria in the earned value management system clause [@CL-PRG-0601]. With no material weaknesses, the contracting officer promptly tells the contractor in writing that the system is acceptable and approved [@CL-PRG-0602]. With material weaknesses, the contracting officer makes an initial written determination and notifies the contractor in writing with a description of each material weakness in enough detail for the contractor to understand it [@CL-PRG-0603; @CL-PRG-0535] and asks for a written response within 30 days [@CL-PRG-0604].

The final determination says either that the system is acceptable and approved with no material weaknesses left, or that material weaknesses remain [@CL-PRG-0605]. If they remain, the notice names them and indicates the adequacy of any proposed or completed corrective action, and the contracting officer then takes the actions listed after the notice [@CL-PRG-0606]. One listed action is to ask the contractor to correct the deficiencies or submit an acceptable corrective action plan within 45 days of receipt of the final determination [@CL-PRG-0607]. Another is to disapprove the system when initial validation is not completed in the timeframe the contracting officer approved [@CL-PRG-0608], or when the contracting officer determines that the existing system contains a material weakness in a high-risk guideline of the ANSI/EIA-748 standards, namely guidelines 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 16, 21, 23, 26, 27, 28, 30 and 32 [@CL-PRG-0609]. A material weakness in one of the remaining 16 guidelines leaves disapproval to the contracting officer's discretion [@CL-PRG-0610], exercised on input from the functional specialists and the auditor [@CL-PRG-0805].

## How it works

The route runs from the findings, to an initial determination and the contractor's written response, to a final determination [@CL-PRG-0602; @CL-PRG-0603; @CL-PRG-0604; @CL-PRG-0605]. When material weaknesses remain, a further listed action is to withhold payments under the Contractor Business Systems clause if that clause is in the contract [@CL-PRG-0606; @CL-PRG-0611]. The contracting officer must promptly approve a previously disapproved system, and notify the contractor, when the contracting officer determines that no material weaknesses remain [@CL-PRG-0612]. Copies of a determination to approve a system, to disapprove it and withhold payments, or to approve a previously disapproved system and release withheld payments go to the auditor, the payment office, affected contracting officers at the buying activities and cognizant contracting officers in contract administration [@CL-PRG-0613; @CL-PRG-0614].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer reviews the findings and sends an initial determination that lists 2 material weaknesses, one of them in a high-risk guideline. The contractor replies after 20 days, inside the 30-day window. The final determination says 1 weakness remains, and it is in a high-risk guideline. The contracting officer asks for a corrective action plan within 45 days and disapproves the system. The answer: the system is disapproved, and payments are withheld if the business systems clause is in the contract.

## Never confuse

Approval of the system is not the same as the dollar thresholds that decide which contracts need a system; see [Earned value management system requirement](prg-evms-requirement.md). Disapproval is also not the same as a payment withhold, which is a separate listed action that applies if the business systems clause is in the contract [@CL-PRG-0611]; see [Business system withholds](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-business-system-withholds.md). The high-risk list is written in terms of the ANSI/EIA-748 standard and runs up to guideline 32 [@CL-PRG-0609], while the Interpretation Guide interprets 27 guidelines [@CL-PRG-0623]; check the standard's own numbering before mapping a number to a guideline page.
