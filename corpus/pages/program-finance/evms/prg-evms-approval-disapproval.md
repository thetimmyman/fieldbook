## Purpose

Shows how the contracting officer approves or disapproves a contractor's earned value management system, from the first findings to the final determination, and what follows a disapproval.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-PRG-0600] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 234.201(5) to (9) [@CL-PRG-0600; @CL-PRG-0612; @CL-PRG-0614] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0600] |
| Applies to | Contractor earned value management systems reviewed by the cognizant contracting officer [@CL-PRG-0600] |

The cognizant contracting officer, in consultation with the functional specialist and auditor, shall determine the acceptability of the contractor's earned value management system and approve or disapprove the system, and pursue correction of any weaknesses or deficiencies [@CL-PRG-0600]. In evaluating the acceptability of a contractor's earned value management system, the contracting officer, in consultation with the functional specialist and auditor, shall determine whether the contractor's earned value management system complies with the system criteria for an acceptable earned value management system as prescribed in the clause at 252.234-7002, Earned Value Management System [@CL-PRG-0067; @CL-PRG-0601; @CL-PRG-0068]. The contracting officer shall review all findings and recommendations and, if there are no material weaknesses, shall promptly notify the contractor, in writing, that the contractor's earned value management system is acceptable and approved [@CL-PRG-0602]. If the contracting officer finds that there are one or more material weaknesses due to the contractor's failure to meet one or more of the earned value management system criteria in the clause at 252.234-7002, the contracting officer shall promptly make an initial written determination of any material weaknesses and notify the contractor, in writing, providing a description of each material weakness in sufficient detail to allow the contractor to understand the weaknesses [@CL-PRG-0069; @CL-PRG-0603; @CL-PRG-0535], request the contractor to respond, in writing, to the initial determination within 30 days [@CL-PRG-0604], and evaluate the contractor's response to the initial determination, in consultation with the auditor or functional specialist, and make a final determination [@CL-PRG-0070].

The contracting officer shall make a final determination and notify the contractor, in writing, either that the contractor's earned value management system is acceptable and approved, and no material weaknesses remain, or that material weaknesses remain [@CL-PRG-0605]. Where material weaknesses remain, the notice shall identify any remaining material weaknesses and indicate the adequacy of any proposed or completed corrective action, and the contracting officer shall take the following actions [@CL-PRG-0606]. The contracting officer shall request that the contractor, within 45 days of receipt of the final determination, either correct the deficiencies or submit an acceptable corrective action plan showing milestones and actions to eliminate the weaknesses [@CL-PRG-0607]. The contracting officer shall disapprove the system in accordance with the clause at 252.234-7002 when initial validation is not successfully completed within the timeframe approved by the contracting officer [@CL-PRG-0608], or the contracting officer determines that the existing earned value management system contains one or more material weaknesses in high-risk guidelines in ANSI/EIA-748 standards (guidelines 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 16, 21, 23, 26, 27, 28, 30, or 32) [@CL-PRG-0609]. When the contracting officer determines that the existing earned value management system contains one or more material weaknesses in one or more of the remaining 16 guidelines in ANSI/EIA-748 standards, the contracting officer shall use discretion to disapprove the system based on input received from functional specialists and the auditor [@CL-PRG-0610; @CL-PRG-0805]. The contracting officer shall also withhold payments in accordance with the clause at 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, if the clause is included in the contract [@CL-PRG-0606; @CL-PRG-0611].

## How it works

The contracting officer reviews all findings and recommendations, makes an initial determination, requests a written response within 30 days if the contracting officer finds that there are one or more material weaknesses due to the contractor's failure to meet one or more of the earned value management system criteria in the clause at 252.234-7002, evaluates the contractor's response in consultation with the auditor or functional specialist, and makes a final determination [@CL-PRG-0602; @CL-PRG-0069; @CL-PRG-0603; @CL-PRG-0604; @CL-PRG-0070; @CL-PRG-0605]. Where material weaknesses remain, the contracting officer shall withhold payments in accordance with the clause at 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, if the clause is included in the contract [@CL-PRG-0606; @CL-PRG-0611]. The contracting officer shall promptly approve a previously disapproved earned value management system and notify the contractor when the contracting officer determines that there are no remaining material weaknesses [@CL-PRG-0612]. The cognizant contracting officer shall promptly distribute copies of a determination to approve a system, disapprove a system and withhold payments, or approve a previously disapproved system and release withheld payments to the auditor, payment office, affected contracting officers at the buying activities, and cognizant contracting officers in contract administration activities [@CL-PRG-0613; @CL-PRG-0614].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer reviews the findings and sends an initial determination that lists 2 material weaknesses, one of them in a high-risk guideline. The contractor replies after 20 days, inside the 30-day window. The final determination says 1 weakness remains, and it is in a high-risk guideline. The contracting officer asks for a corrective action plan within 45 days and disapproves the system. The answer: the system is disapproved, and payments are withheld if the business systems clause is in the contract.

## Never confuse

See [Earned value management system requirement](prg-evms-requirement.md) and [Business system withholds](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-business-system-withholds.md). Where material weaknesses remain, the contracting officer shall withhold payments in accordance with the clause at 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, if the clause is included in the contract [@CL-PRG-0606; @CL-PRG-0611]. Under DFARS 234.201, the high-risk guidelines in ANSI/EIA-748 standards are guidelines 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 16, 21, 23, 26, 27, 28, 30, or 32, with 16 remaining guidelines [@CL-PRG-0609; @CL-PRG-0610], while the DoD EVMS Interpretation Guide provides the overarching DoD interpretation of the 27 Guidelines where an EVMS requirement is applied and is used as the basis for the DoD to assess EVMS compliance with the 27 Guidelines [@CL-PRG-0623; @CL-PRG-0071].
