## Purpose

Shows what the contractor must do about Title III industrial resources under the clause, and how testing and qualification costs are handled.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0946] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.234-1 [@CL-PRG-0946] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.234-1 [@CL-PRG-0034; @CL-PRG-0035; @CL-PRG-0036; @CL-PRG-0037] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0946] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-PRG-0946] |

The Contractor must refer any request from a Title III project contractor for testing and qualification of a Title III industrial resource to the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0947]. Upon the direction of the Contracting Officer, the Contractor must test Title III industrial resources for qualification and provide the test results to the Defense Production Act Office, Title III Program [@CL-PRG-0948].

When the Contracting Officer modifies the contract to direct testing, the Government will provide the Title III industrial resource to be tested and will make an equitable adjustment in the contract for the costs of testing and qualification of that resource [@CL-PRG-0949]. The Contractor agrees to insert the substance of the clause, including its paragraph (e), in every subcontract issued in performance of the contract [@CL-PRG-0950].

## How it works

The contractor does not decide on its own whether to test: it refers the request and tests when directed [@CL-PRG-0947; @CL-PRG-0948]. A directing modification brings the resource from the Government and an equitable adjustment for the testing and qualification costs [@CL-PRG-0949]. The flowdown reaches every subcontract issued in performance of the contract [@CL-PRG-0950]. The printed clause does not state what happens to a request that the Contracting Officer does not direct to be tested.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text keeps the duty to refer any request from a Title III project contractor for testing and qualification of a Title III industrial resource to the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0034; @CL-PRG-0947] and the flowdown of the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e), in every subcontract issued in performance of the contract [@CL-PRG-0037; @CL-PRG-0950]. It changes how testing is directed: paragraph (c) says the Contracting Officer will modify the contract to authorize testing and qualification and to provide an equitable adjustment for the costs to test and qualify [@CL-PRG-0035], where the codified text says the Government will provide the resource and make an equitable adjustment when the Contracting Officer modifies the contract to direct testing [@CL-PRG-0949]. Paragraph (d) says that upon receipt of a contract modification and the Title III industrial resources the Contractor must test them for qualification and provide the test results to the Defense Production Act Office, Title III Program [@CL-PRG-0036], where the codified text ties testing to the direction of the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0948].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor receives a request to test a new material made under Title III assistance. It refers the request to the Contracting Officer. The Contracting Officer directs the test by modification. The Government supplies the material, the contractor tests it and sends results to the Title III office, and the contract price is adjusted for the testing cost. The answer: the cost is recovered by equitable adjustment, not absorbed.

## Never confuse

The Government policy that it pays for testing, how the contracting officer evaluates a request and where the clause is inserted are on [Testing and qualification of Title III industrial resources](../risk-opportunity/prg-far-title-iii-testing-qualification.md) and are not restated here. Under the codified clause, a request from a Title III project contractor is also not a direction to test: the testing duty arises upon the Contracting Officer's direction [@CL-PRG-0947; @CL-PRG-0948]. Under the codified clause the Contracting Officer's direction triggers the testing duty [@CL-PRG-0948], while the RFO text ties the Contractor's testing duty to receipt of a contract modification and the Title III industrial resources [@CL-PRG-0036].
