## Purpose

Shows what the contractor must do about Title III industrial resources under the clause, and how testing and qualification costs are handled.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0946] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.234-1 [@CL-PRG-0946] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.234-1 [@CL-PRG-0034; @CL-PRG-0035; @CL-PRG-0036; @CL-PRG-0037] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0946] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-PRG-0946] |

The Contractor shall refer any request from a Title III project contractor for testing and qualification of a Title III industrial resource to the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0947]. Upon the direction of the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall test Title III industrial resources for qualification and provide the test results to the Defense Production Act Office, Title III Program, located at Wright Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio 45433-7739 [@CL-PRG-0948; @CL-PRG-0086].

When the Contracting Officer modifies the contract to direct testing pursuant to the clause, the Government will provide the Title III industrial resource to be tested and will make an equitable adjustment in the contract for the costs of testing and qualification of the Title III industrial resource [@CL-PRG-0087; @CL-PRG-0949]. The Contractor agrees to insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e), in every subcontract issued in performance of the contract [@CL-PRG-0950].

## How it works

Under codified FAR 52.234-1, the Contractor shall refer any request from a Title III project contractor for testing and qualification of a Title III industrial resource to the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0947], and upon the direction of the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall test Title III industrial resources for qualification and provide the test results to the Defense Production Act Office, Title III Program, located at Wright Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio 45433-7739 [@CL-PRG-0948; @CL-PRG-0086]. When the Contracting Officer modifies the contract to direct testing pursuant to the clause, the Government will provide the Title III industrial resource to be tested and will make an equitable adjustment in the contract for the costs of testing and qualification of the Title III industrial resource [@CL-PRG-0087; @CL-PRG-0949]. The Contractor agrees to insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e), in every subcontract issued in performance of the contract [@CL-PRG-0950].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-1(b), the Contractor must refer any request from a Title III project contractor for testing and qualification of a Title III industrial resource to the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0034], and under RFO FAR 52.234-1(e), the Contractor agrees to insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e), in every subcontract issued in performance of the contract [@CL-PRG-0037]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-1(c), the Contracting Officer will modify the contract to authorize testing and qualification of the Title III industrial resource and provide an equitable adjustment for the costs to test and qualify the Title III industrial resource [@CL-PRG-0035], whereas under codified FAR 52.234-1(d), when the Contracting Officer modifies the contract to direct testing pursuant to the clause, the Government will provide the Title III industrial resource to be tested and will make an equitable adjustment in the contract for the costs of testing and qualification of the Title III industrial resource [@CL-PRG-0087; @CL-PRG-0949]. Under RFO FAR 52.234-1(d), upon receipt of a contract modification and the Title III industrial resources, the Contractor must test the Title III industrial resources for qualification and provide the test results to the Defense Production Act Office, Title III Program, located at Wright Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio 45433-7739 [@CL-PRG-0036; @CL-PRG-0088], whereas under codified FAR 52.234-1(c), upon the direction of the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall test Title III industrial resources for qualification and provide the test results to the Defense Production Act Office, Title III Program, located at Wright Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio 45433-7739 [@CL-PRG-0948; @CL-PRG-0086].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor receives a request to test a new material made under Title III assistance. It refers the request to the Contracting Officer. The Contracting Officer directs the test by modification. The Government supplies the material, the contractor tests it and sends results to the Title III office, and the contract price is adjusted for the testing cost. The answer: the cost is recovered by equitable adjustment, not absorbed.

## Never confuse

See [Testing and qualification of Title III industrial resources](../risk-opportunity/prg-far-title-iii-testing-qualification.md). Under codified FAR 52.234-1, the Contractor shall refer any request from a Title III project contractor for testing and qualification of a Title III industrial resource to the Contracting Officer [@CL-PRG-0947], and upon the direction of the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall test Title III industrial resources for qualification [@CL-PRG-0948], whereas under RFO FAR 52.234-1(d), upon receipt of a contract modification and the Title III industrial resources, the Contractor must test the Title III industrial resources for qualification [@CL-PRG-0036].
