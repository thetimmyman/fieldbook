## Purpose

Sets out the requirement to identify in the schedule the physical products, milestones, technical performance goals or other objective indicators used to measure progress.

The contractor must identify, in the schedule, the physical products, milestones, technical performance goals, or other objective indicators that will be used to measure progress [@CL-PRG-0201]. This is to ensure program schedules establish and maintain a relationship between technical achievement and progress statusing and provide objective performance data that accurately reflects the progress of the work [@CL-PRG-0202]. Objective program milestones aligned with the accomplishment of the program's technical requirements and goals are determined in advance, documented, and used to plan and measure progress [@CL-PRG-0205].

## How it works

Interim measures are defined as the detailed schedule is established to ensure performance is measured as objectively as possible [@CL-PRG-0203]. Identifying objective criteria linked to technical progress indicators ensures performance assessments reflect the true technical performance of the program [@CL-PRG-0204]. Objective performance data tied to technical milestones provides management with accurate progress visibility and early indicators of program risks requiring corrective action [@CL-PRG-0563].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A software build activity spans 6 months with an authorized budget of 120,000. Rather than claiming progress based on elapsed time, the contractor establishes 3 interim milestones: architecture completion at month 2 (40,000), module coding at month 4 (40,000), and unit testing completion at month 6 (40,000). Progress is earned only as each objective milestone passes technical verification.

## Never confuse

Guideline 5 requires identifying, in the schedule, the physical products, milestones, technical performance goals, or other objective indicators that will be used to measure progress [@CL-PRG-0201], unlike Guideline 6, which requires scheduling the authorized work in a manner that describes the sequence of work and identifies task interdependencies required to meet the requirements of the program [@CL-PRG-0206]. See [Earned value management system requirement](prg-evms-requirement.md).
