## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must identify in its proposal about subcontract effort, and about the added value it provides when most of the work is subcontracted.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0940] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-22 [@CL-PRG-0940] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-22 [@CL-PRG-0027; @CL-PRG-0028; @CL-PRG-0029; @CL-PRG-0030; @CL-PRG-0031; @CL-PRG-0032; @CL-PRG-0033] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0940] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to solicitations that include the provision [@CL-PRG-0940] |

The offeror's proposal shall exclude excessive pass-through charges [@CL-PRG-0975].

The offeror shall identify in its proposal the total cost of the work to be performed by the offeror and the total cost of the work to be performed by each subcontractor under the contract, task order, or delivery order [@CL-PRG-0941].

If the offeror intends to subcontract more than 70 percent of the total cost of work to be performed under the contract, task order, or delivery order, it must identify in its proposal the items listed in that paragraph [@CL-PRG-0942]. One listed item is the amount of the offeror's indirect costs and profit/fee applicable to the work to be performed by the subcontractors, and another is a description of the added value provided by the offeror as related to that work [@CL-PRG-0943].

## How it works

The first duty applies to every proposal: split the total cost between the offeror and each subcontractor [@CL-PRG-0941]. The second duty applies where the offeror intends to subcontract more than 70 percent of the total cost [@CL-PRG-0942; @CL-PRG-0943]. A third duty reaches down one level: if a proposed subcontractor intends to subcontract more than 70 percent of its own work to a lower-tier subcontractor, the offeror identifies the subcontractor's indirect costs and profit/fee and a description of the added value the subcontractor provides [@CL-PRG-0944; @CL-PRG-0945]. The provision defers the definitions of added value and excessive pass-through charge to another clause, so this card does not restate them.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text keeps the rule that the offeror's proposal shall exclude excessive pass-through charges [@CL-PRG-0028; @CL-PRG-0975] and the identification of the total cost of the work performed by the offeror and by each subcontractor [@CL-PRG-0029; @CL-PRG-0941]. It keeps the 70 percent test for the offeror and the two items to identify, the offeror's indirect costs and profit/fee and a description of the added value [@CL-PRG-0030; @CL-PRG-0031; @CL-PRG-0942; @CL-PRG-0943]. It keeps the same test and items one level down for a proposed subcontractor and its lower-tier subcontractor [@CL-PRG-0032; @CL-PRG-0033; @CL-PRG-0944; @CL-PRG-0945]. Its definitions paragraph says the defined terms are in the clause of this request for proposals entitled Limitations on Pass-Through Charges (FAR 52.215-23) [@CL-PRG-0027].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes total work of 1,000,000 and plans to subcontract 800,000 of it. It lists its own cost and each subcontractor's cost. Because 80 percent is over the line, it also states its indirect costs and profit on the subcontracted work and describes the added value it provides. The answer: the extra statement is owed because the share is above 70 percent.

## Never confuse

This card is the proposal-stage identification. The limits on pass-through charges, the meaning of excessive charges and the later notification duty are on [Limitations on Pass-Through Charges clause](../../estimating/pricing/est-pass-through-charge-limitations.md) and are not restated here. Identifying the added value is also not a finding that the charge is allowable: the provision asks for a description [@CL-PRG-0943].
