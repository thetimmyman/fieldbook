## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must identify in its proposal about subcontract effort, and about the added value it provides when most of the work is subcontracted.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-PRG-0940] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-22 [@CL-PRG-0940] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-22 [@CL-PRG-0027; @CL-PRG-0028; @CL-PRG-0029; @CL-PRG-0030; @CL-PRG-0031; @CL-PRG-0032; @CL-PRG-0033] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0940] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to solicitations that include the provision [@CL-PRG-0940] |

The offeror's proposal shall exclude excessive pass-through charges [@CL-PRG-0975].

The offeror shall identify in its proposal the total cost of the work to be performed by the offeror, and the total cost of the work to be performed by each subcontractor, under the contract, task order, or delivery order [@CL-PRG-0941].

If the offeror intends to subcontract more than 70 percent of the total cost of work to be performed under the contract, task order, or delivery order, the offeror shall identify in its proposal the amount of the offeror's indirect costs and profit/fee applicable to the work to be performed by the subcontractor(s), and a description of the added value provided by the offeror as related to the work to be performed by the subcontractor(s) [@CL-PRG-0942; @CL-PRG-0943].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.215-22(c)(1), the offeror shall identify in its proposal the total cost of the work to be performed by the offeror, and the total cost of the work to be performed by each subcontractor, under the contract, task order, or delivery order [@CL-PRG-0941]. Under FAR 52.215-22(c)(2), if the offeror intends to subcontract more than 70 percent of the total cost of work to be performed under the contract, task order, or delivery order, the offeror shall identify in its proposal the amount of the offeror's indirect costs and profit/fee applicable to the work to be performed by the subcontractor(s), and a description of the added value provided by the offeror as related to the work to be performed by the subcontractor(s) [@CL-PRG-0942; @CL-PRG-0943]. Under FAR 52.215-22(c)(3), if any subcontractor proposed under the contract, task order, or delivery order intends to subcontract to a lower-tier subcontractor more than 70 percent of the total cost of work to be performed under its subcontract, the offeror shall identify in its proposal the amount of the subcontractor's indirect costs and profit/fee applicable to the work to be performed by the lower-tier subcontractor(s), and a description of the added value provided by the subcontractor as related to the work to be performed by the lower-tier subcontractor(s) [@CL-PRG-0944; @CL-PRG-0945]. Under FAR 52.215-22(a), added value, excessive pass-through charge, subcontract, and subcontractor, as used in the provision, are defined in the clause of the solicitation entitled "Limitations on Pass-Through Charges" (FAR 52.215-23) [@CL-PRG-9150].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-22(a), added value, excessive pass-through charge, subcontract, and subcontractor, as used in the provision, are defined in the clause of the request for proposals entitled "Limitations on Pass-Through Charges" (FAR 52.215-23) [@CL-PRG-0027]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-22(b), the offeror's proposal shall exclude excessive pass-through charges [@CL-PRG-0028]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-22(c)(1), the offeror shall identify in its proposal the total cost of the work to be performed by the offeror, and the total cost of the work to be performed by each subcontractor, under the contract, task order, or delivery order [@CL-PRG-0029]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-22(c)(2), if the offeror intends to subcontract more than 70 percent of the total cost of work to be performed under the contract, task order, or delivery order, the offeror shall identify in its proposal the amount of the offeror's indirect costs and profit/fee applicable to the work to be performed by the subcontractor(s), and a description of the added value provided by the offeror as related to the work to be performed by the subcontractor(s) [@CL-PRG-0030; @CL-PRG-0031]. Under RFO FAR 52.215-22(c)(3), if any subcontractor proposed under the contract, task order, or delivery order intends to subcontract to a lower-tier subcontractor more than 70 percent of the total cost of work to be performed under its subcontract, the offeror shall identify in its proposal the amount of the subcontractor's indirect costs and profit/fee applicable to the work to be performed by the lower-tier subcontractor(s), and a description of the added value provided by the subcontractor as related to the work to be performed by the lower-tier subcontractor(s) [@CL-PRG-0032; @CL-PRG-0033].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes total work of 1,000,000 and plans to subcontract 800,000 of it. It lists its own cost and each subcontractor's cost. Because 80 percent is over the line, it also states its indirect costs and profit on the subcontracted work and describes the added value it provides. The answer: the extra statement is owed because the share is above 70 percent.

## Never confuse

See [Limitations on Pass-Through Charges clause](../../estimating/pricing/est-pass-through-charge-limitations.md). Under codified FAR 52.215-22(a), added value, excessive pass-through charge, subcontract, and subcontractor are defined in the clause of the solicitation entitled "Limitations on Pass-Through Charges" (FAR 52.215-23) [@CL-PRG-9150], whereas under RFO FAR 52.215-22(a) they are defined in the clause of the request for proposals entitled "Limitations on Pass-Through Charges" (FAR 52.215-23) [@CL-PRG-0027].
