## Purpose

Shows who sets the timing of an Integrated Baseline Review, what a solicitation must say about a pre-award review and offeror costs, and which provisions and clause the contracting officer inserts.

The timing and conduct of the Integrated Baseline Review (IBR) shall be in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-PRG-0866]. If a pre-award IBR will be conducted, the solicitation must include the procedures for conducting the IBR and address whether offerors will be reimbursed for the associated costs [@CL-PRG-0867]. If permitted, reimbursement of offerors' pre-award IBR costs is governed by the provisions of FAR Part 31 [@CL-PRG-0868].

The contracting officer shall insert a provision that is substantially the same as the provision at FAR 52.234-2, Notice of Earned Value Management System—Preaward Integrated Baseline Review, in solicitations for contracts that require the contractor to use an Earned Value Management System (EVMS) and for which the Government requires an IBR prior to award [@CL-PRG-0869; @CL-PRG-0870]. The contracting officer shall insert a provision that is substantially the same as the provision at 52.234-3, Notice of Earned Value Management System—Postaward Integrated Baseline Review, in solicitations for contracts that require the contractor to use an EVMS and for which the Government requires an IBR after contract award [@CL-PRG-0871; @CL-PRG-0872]. The contracting officer shall insert a clause that is substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.234-4, Earned Value Management System, in solicitations and contracts that require a contractor to use an EVMS [@CL-PRG-0873].

## How it works

The timing and conduct of the IBR shall be in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-PRG-0866]. If a pre-award IBR will be conducted, the solicitation must include the procedures for conducting the IBR and address whether offerors will be reimbursed for the associated costs [@CL-PRG-0867], and if permitted, reimbursement of offerors' pre-award IBR costs is governed by the provisions of FAR Part 31 [@CL-PRG-0868]. In solicitations for contracts that require the contractor to use an EVMS, the contracting officer shall insert a provision that is substantially the same as the provision at FAR 52.234-2, Notice of Earned Value Management System—Preaward Integrated Baseline Review, when the Government requires an IBR prior to award [@CL-PRG-0869; @CL-PRG-0870], and a provision that is substantially the same as the provision at 52.234-3, Notice of Earned Value Management System—Postaward Integrated Baseline Review, when the Government requires an IBR after contract award [@CL-PRG-0871; @CL-PRG-0872]. The contracting officer shall insert a clause that is substantially the same as the clause at FAR 52.234-4, Earned Value Management System, in solicitations and contracts that require a contractor to use an EVMS [@CL-PRG-0873].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency plans to hold a review before award on a contract that requires an EVMS. Its solicitation lists the review procedures and says that offerors will not be reimbursed for their review costs. The contracting officer inserts the pre-award provision and the earned value clause. A second solicitation requires the review after award, so it carries the post-award provision instead. The answer: the review timing decides which provision is used, and the clause is used in both.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer shall insert a provision that is substantially the same as the provision at FAR 52.234-2, Notice of Earned Value Management System—Preaward Integrated Baseline Review, in solicitations for contracts that require the contractor to use an EVMS and for which the Government requires an IBR prior to award [@CL-PRG-0869; @CL-PRG-0870], unlike a postaward IBR, for which the contracting officer inserts a provision substantially the same as 52.234-3, Notice of Earned Value Management System—Postaward Integrated Baseline Review [@CL-PRG-0871; @CL-PRG-0872], while inserting a clause substantially the same as FAR 52.234-4, Earned Value Management System, in solicitations and contracts that require a contractor to use an EVMS [@CL-PRG-0873]. If permitted, reimbursement of offerors' pre-award IBR costs is governed by the provisions of FAR Part 31 [@CL-PRG-0868]; see [Allowability factors](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md), [Earned value management system requirement](../evms/prg-evms-requirement.md), [Integrated Baseline Reviews under the FAR](prg-far-integrated-baseline-review.md), and [When the FAR requires an earned value management system](../evms/prg-far-evms-requirement.md).
