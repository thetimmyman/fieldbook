## Purpose

Shows who selects the contract type for a major defense acquisition program at Milestone B, how program risk must be documented, and what a cost-reimbursement choice requires.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-PRG-0615] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 234.004(2)(i) and (ii) [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0615; @CL-PRG-0132; @CL-PRG-0620] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0615] |
| Applies to | Major defense acquisition programs at Milestone B, and the acquisition of production of major defense acquisition programs [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0132; @CL-PRG-0620] |

In accordance with section 818 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2007 (Public Law 109-364), for major defense acquisition programs at Milestone B, the milestone decision authority shall select, with the advice of the contracting officer, the contract type for a development program at the time of Milestone B approval or, in the case of a space program, Key Decision Point B approval [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0615]. The basis for the contract type selection shall be documented in the acquisition strategy, and the documentation shall include an explanation of the level of program risk and, if program risk is determined to be high, shall outline the steps taken to reduce program risk and the reasons for proceeding with Milestone B approval despite the high level of program risk [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0616; @CL-PRG-0617].

In accordance with section 818 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2007 (Public Law 109-364), for major defense acquisition programs at Milestone B, if a cost-reimbursement type contract is selected, the contract file shall include the milestone decision authority's written determination that (1) the program is so complex and technically challenging that it would not be practicable to reduce program risk to a level that would permit the use of a fixed-price type contract, and (2) the complexity and technical challenge of the program is not the result of a failure to meet the requirements of section 4251 of title 10, United States Code [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0618; @CL-PRG-0619; @CL-PRG-0626].

In accordance with section 811 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2013 (Public Law 112-239), the contracting officer shall not use cost-reimbursement line items for the acquisition of production of major defense acquisition programs, unless the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD(A&S)), or the milestone decision authority when the milestone decision authority is the service acquisition executive of the military department that is managing the program, submits to the congressional defense committees both (1) a written certification that the particular cost-reimbursement line items are needed to provide a required capability in a timely and cost effective manner, and (2) an explanation of the steps taken to ensure that cost-reimbursement line items are used only to achieve the purposes of the exception [@CL-PRG-0132; @CL-PRG-0620; @CL-PRG-0801; @CL-PRG-0621; @CL-PRG-0802]. In accordance with that section, the contracting officer shall also include a copy of such congressional certification in the contract file [@CL-PRG-0132; @CL-PRG-0803].

## How it works

In accordance with section 818 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2007 (Public Law 109-364), for major defense acquisition programs at Milestone B, the basis for the contract type selection shall be documented in the acquisition strategy, and the documentation shall include an explanation of the level of program risk and, if program risk is determined to be high, shall outline the steps taken to reduce program risk and the reasons for proceeding with Milestone B approval despite the high level of program risk [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0616; @CL-PRG-0617]. If a cost-reimbursement type contract is selected for such a program at Milestone B, the contract file shall include the milestone decision authority's written determination that the program is so complex and technically challenging that it would not be practicable to reduce program risk to a level that would permit the use of a fixed-price type contract, and that the complexity and technical challenge of the program is not the result of a failure to meet the requirements of section 4251 of title 10, United States Code [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0618; @CL-PRG-0619; @CL-PRG-0626].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A development program reaches Milestone B and the strategy rates its risk as high. The strategy file records 3 steps taken to cut risk and gives the reasons for going ahead. The authority picks a cost-reimbursement contract, so the contract file also holds the written determination on complexity. The answer: the strategy holds the risk explanation and the steps, and the contract file holds the determination.
