## Purpose

Shows who selects the contract type for a major defense acquisition program at Milestone B, how program risk must be documented, and what a cost-reimbursement choice requires.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-PRG-0615] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 234.004(2) [@CL-PRG-0615; @CL-PRG-0620] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-PRG-0615] |
| Applies to | Major defense acquisition programs at Milestone B, and production of those programs for cost-reimbursement line items [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0620] |

For a major defense acquisition program at Milestone B, the milestone decision authority shall select the contract type for a development program, with the advice of the contracting officer, at Milestone B approval or Key Decision Point B approval for a space program [@CL-PRG-0615]. The basis for the contract type selection shall be documented in the acquisition strategy, and the documentation shall include an explanation of the level of program risk [@CL-PRG-0616]. If program risk is determined to be high, the documentation shall also outline the steps taken to reduce program risk and the reasons for proceeding with Milestone B approval despite the high level of program risk [@CL-PRG-0617].

For those programs at Milestone B, if a cost-reimbursement contract is selected, the contract file shall include the milestone decision authority's written determination [@CL-PRG-0800; @CL-PRG-0618]. It says that the program is so complex and technically challenging that reducing program risk enough for a fixed-price contract is not practicable [@CL-PRG-0619], and that the complexity is not the result of a failure to meet the statutory requirements it names [@CL-PRG-0626].

For production, the contracting officer shall not use cost-reimbursement line items for the acquisition of production of major defense acquisition programs, unless the exception described next applies [@CL-PRG-0620]. The exception applies when the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment submits to the congressional defense committees the items listed in the provision, or the milestone decision authority does so where that authority is the service acquisition executive of the military department that is managing the program [@CL-PRG-0801]. One item is a written certification that the line items are needed to provide a required capability in a timely and cost effective manner [@CL-PRG-0621]. The other is an explanation of the steps taken to keep their use to the purposes of the exception [@CL-PRG-0802]. The same paragraph also calls for a copy of the congressional certification in the contract file [@CL-PRG-0803].

## How it works

Contract type is tied to risk. The acquisition strategy records the level of program risk, and a high rating needs the risk-reduction steps and the reasons for going ahead [@CL-PRG-0616; @CL-PRG-0617]. A cost-reimbursement choice adds a written determination to the contract file [@CL-PRG-0618; @CL-PRG-0619; @CL-PRG-0626]. Production has its own bar on cost-reimbursement line items, with its own exception [@CL-PRG-0620; @CL-PRG-0801].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A development program reaches Milestone B and the strategy rates its risk as high. The strategy file records 3 steps taken to cut risk and gives the reasons for going ahead. The authority picks a cost-reimbursement contract, so the contract file also holds the written determination on complexity. The answer: the strategy holds the risk explanation and the steps, and the contract file holds the determination.

## Never confuse

Selecting the contract type for development at Milestone B is a different decision from the bar on cost-reimbursement line items in production. The first is a selection by the milestone decision authority [@CL-PRG-0615], and the second is a limit with an exception [@CL-PRG-0620]. For contract types in general, see [Contract types](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-contract-types.md).
