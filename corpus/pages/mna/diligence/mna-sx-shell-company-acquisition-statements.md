## Purpose

Says which financial statements of a business a shell company registrant must file when the business will combine with it, and the timing rules the section sets. The rule is part of Regulation S-X, which is not FAR, DFARS or CAS. Pro forma information for these deals is on [Pro forma financial information: when it is required](mna-sx-pro-forma-presentation.md).

| Applies to | Acquisitions by a shell company, other than a business combination related shell company [@CL-MNA-0952; @CL-MNA-0905] |

When a registrant is a shell company, other than a business combination related shell company, and the financial statements of a business that will be combining with the registrant are required in a registration statement or proxy statement [@CL-MNA-0952], the registrant must file them in accordance with the rules for Articles 3 and 10 of Regulation S-X as if the filing were a Securities Act registration statement for the initial public offering of the business's equity securities [@CL-MNA-0904]. The financial statements of a business that will be acquired by a shell company, other than a business combination related shell company, must comply with the requirements of the cited section on the age of financial statements [@CL-MNA-0905]. The age requirements of the smaller reporting company section apply instead when that business would qualify as a smaller reporting company based on its annual revenues as of the most recently completed fiscal year for which audited financial statements are available, if it were filing a registration statement alone [@CL-MNA-0955].

When the financial statements of a recently acquired business or real estate operation that is not or will not be the predecessor are omitted from a registration statement or proxy statement under the cited omission provisions [@CL-MNA-0953], those financial statements must be filed in a Form 8-K by the later of the filing of the Form 8-K filed pursuant to Item 2.01(f) of Form 8-K or 75 days after consummation of the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0906]. In transactions involving a shell company that is not a SPAC, as defined in the cited section, the predecessor must be audited by an independent accountant registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board [@CL-MNA-0954]. The source text printed for this card does not set out the number of periods; they follow the articles the section cites, which this card does not reproduce.

## How it works

The shell company registrant files the target's statements as if the target were making an initial public offering [@CL-MNA-0904]. The age of those statements is tested under the age rules the section cites [@CL-MNA-0905]. Statements of a recently acquired business that is not the predecessor, omitted under the cited omission provisions, are filed later in a Form 8-K [@CL-MNA-0953; @CL-MNA-0906]. The section also defines the audit requirement, covers acquisitions of a business by a predecessor and sets rules for the shell company's own statements. This card draws out only the predecessor audit rule.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A shell company registrant is to combine with an operating business whose annual revenues are small enough for it to qualify as a smaller reporting company. The registrant files the target's statements as if the filing were the target's initial public offering, and tests their age under the smaller reporting company section.

## Never confuse

The ordinary acquired-business rule uses significance bands to decide how many periods of the target's statements are filed, as described on [Financial statements of businesses acquired or to be acquired](mna-sx-acquired-business-financial-statements.md). Here the statements are filed as if for an initial public offering [@CL-MNA-0904]. The ordinary age rules are on [Age of financial statements and reporting currency](mna-sx-age-of-statements-and-currency.md). The significance tests that the section points to for a business that is not or will not be the predecessor are on [Application of Regulation S-X and its key defined terms](mna-sx-application-and-defined-terms.md).
