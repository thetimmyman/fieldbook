## Purpose

Says which financial statements of a business a shell company registrant must file when the business will combine with it, and the timing rules the section sets. Pro forma information for these deals is on [Pro forma financial information: when it is required](mna-sx-pro-forma-presentation.md).

Under § 210.15-01(b), when a registrant is a shell company (other than a business combination related shell company) and the financial statements of a business that will be combining with such registrant are required in a registration statement or proxy statement, such registrant must file financial statements of the business in accordance with §§ 210.3-01 through 210.3-12 and 210.10-01 (Articles 3 and 10 of Regulation S-X) as if the filing were a Securities Act registration statement for the initial public offering of the business's equity securities [@CL-MNA-0952; @CL-MNA-0904]. The financial statements of the business may be filed pursuant to §§ 210.8-01 through 210.8-08 (Article 8) when that business would qualify to be a smaller reporting company based on its annual revenues as of the most recently completed fiscal year for which audited financial statements are available, if it were filing a registration statement alone [@CL-MNA-0137; @CL-MNA-0138]. Under paragraph (c), the financial statements of a business that will be acquired by a shell company (other than a business combination related shell company) must comply with the requirements in § 210.3-12 (§ 210.8-08 when that business would qualify to be a smaller reporting company based on its annual revenues as of the most recently completed fiscal year for which audited financial statements are available, if it were filing a registration statement alone) as if the financial statements were included in an initial registration statement in determining the age of financial statements of the business in the registration statement or proxy statement of the registrant [@CL-MNA-0905; @CL-MNA-0955; @CL-MNA-1585].

Under § 210.15-01(d)(2), when the financial statements of a recently acquired business or real estate operation that is not or will not be the predecessor are omitted from a registration statement or proxy statement pursuant to § 210.3-05(b)(4)(i) or § 210.3-14(b)(3)(i), those financial statements must be filed in a Form 8-K by the later of the filing of the Form 8-K filed pursuant to Item 2.01(f) of Form 8-K or 75 days after consummation of the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0953; @CL-MNA-0906]. Under § 210.15-01(a), in transactions involving a shell company that is not a SPAC (as defined in § 229.1601(b) of the chapter), the predecessor must be audited by an independent accountant registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board [@CL-MNA-0954].

## How it works

Under § 210.15-01(b), when a shell company registrant (other than a business combination related shell company) is the filer and the financial statements of a combining business are required in a registration statement or proxy statement, the registrant must file them in accordance with §§ 210.3-01 through 210.3-12 and 210.10-01 as if the filing were a Securities Act registration statement for the initial public offering of the business's equity securities, or pursuant to §§ 210.8-01 through 210.8-08 (Article 8) when that business would qualify to be a smaller reporting company based on its annual revenues as of the most recently completed fiscal year for which audited financial statements are available, if it were filing a registration statement alone [@CL-MNA-0952; @CL-MNA-0904; @CL-MNA-0137; @CL-MNA-0138]. Under paragraph (c), the financial statements of a business that will be acquired by such a shell company must comply with the age requirements in § 210.3-12 (or § 210.8-08 under the same smaller reporting company qualification) as if the financial statements were included in an initial registration statement [@CL-MNA-0905; @CL-MNA-0955; @CL-MNA-1585]. Under paragraph (d)(2), when the financial statements of a recently acquired business or real estate operation that is not or will not be the predecessor are omitted from a registration statement or proxy statement pursuant to § 210.3-05(b)(4)(i) or § 210.3-14(b)(3)(i), they must be filed in a Form 8-K by the later of the filing of the Form 8-K filed pursuant to Item 2.01(f) of Form 8-K or 75 days after consummation of the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0953; @CL-MNA-0906].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A shell company registrant is to combine with an operating business whose annual revenues are small enough for it to qualify as a smaller reporting company. The registrant files the target's statements as if the filing were the target's initial public offering, and tests their age under the smaller reporting company section.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.15-01(b), when a registrant is a shell company (other than a business combination related shell company) and the financial statements of a business that will be combining with the registrant are required in a registration statement or proxy statement, the registrant must file financial statements of the business in accordance with §§ 210.3-01 through 210.3-12 and 210.10-01 as if the filing were a Securities Act registration statement for the initial public offering of the business's equity securities (or pursuant to Article 8 when the business would qualify as a smaller reporting company based on its annual revenues as of the most recently completed fiscal year for which audited financial statements are available, if it were filing a registration statement alone) [@CL-MNA-0952; @CL-MNA-0904; @CL-MNA-0137; @CL-MNA-0138], whereas the significance bands for acquired businesses generally are on [Financial statements of businesses acquired or to be acquired](mna-sx-acquired-business-financial-statements.md). The general age rules are on [Age of financial statements and reporting currency](mna-sx-age-of-statements-and-currency.md), and the significant subsidiary definitions are on [Application of Regulation S-X and its key defined terms](mna-sx-application-and-defined-terms.md).
