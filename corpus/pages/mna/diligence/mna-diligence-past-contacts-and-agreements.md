## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A filing must say about past transactions, negotiations and contacts, conflicts of interest and agreements between the filing person and the subject company, its people or others.

### Transactions

Paragraph (a) requires a brief statement of the nature and approximate dollar amount of any transaction, other than those described in paragraphs (b) or (c), that occurred during the past two years between the filing person (including any person specified in Instruction C) and either of two groups [@CL-MNA-1055]. The first group is the subject company and its affiliates that are not natural persons, if the aggregate value of the transactions is more than one percent of the subject company's consolidated revenues for the fiscal year when the transaction occurred [@CL-MNA-1056]. As an alternative revenue period, paragraph (a)(1) uses the past portion of the current fiscal year, if the transaction occurred in the current year [@CL-MNA-1057]. The second group is any executive officer, director or affiliate of the subject company that is a natural person, if the aggregate value of the transaction or series of similar transactions with that person exceeds $60,000 [@CL-MNA-1058]. Under the instruction to paragraph (a)(1), the information required by the item may be based on information in the subject company's most recent filing with the Commission, unless the filing person has reason to believe the information is not accurate [@CL-MNA-1120].

### Significant corporate events, negotiations and contacts

Paragraph (b) requires a description of any negotiations, transactions or material contacts during the past two years between the filing person (including subsidiaries of the filing person and any person specified in Instruction C) and the subject company or its affiliates concerning any listed matter [@CL-MNA-1059]. The listed matters are any merger, consolidation, acquisition, tender offer for or other acquisition of any class of the subject company's securities, election of the subject company's directors, and sale or other transfer of a material amount of assets of the subject company [@CL-MNA-1060].

Paragraph (c) requires a description of any negotiations or material contacts concerning the paragraph (b) matters during the past two years between any affiliates of the subject company [@CL-MNA-1061]. It also reaches such negotiations or contacts between the subject company or any of its affiliates and any person not affiliated with the subject company who would have a direct interest in such matters [@CL-MNA-1062]. The instruction to paragraphs (b) and (c) requires identification of the person who initiated the contacts or negotiations [@CL-MNA-1062].

### Conflicts of interest

Paragraph (d) requires, if material, a description of any agreement, arrangement or understanding and any actual or potential conflict of interest between the filing person or its affiliates and the subject company, its executive officers, directors or affiliates [@CL-MNA-1063]. It covers the same matters between the filing person or its affiliates and the offeror, its executive officers, directors or affiliates [@CL-MNA-1064].

If the filing person is the subject company, no disclosure called for by paragraph (d) is required in the document disseminated to security holders [@CL-MNA-1064]. That relief applies so long as substantially the same information was filed with the Commission previously and disclosed in a proxy statement, report or other communication sent to security holders by the subject company in the past year [@CL-MNA-1065]. Where the relief is used, the document must refer specifically to the earlier discussion, and the information also must be filed as an exhibit to the schedule [@CL-MNA-1066].

### Agreements involving the subject company's securities

Paragraph (e) requires a description of any agreement, arrangement or understanding, whether or not legally enforceable, between the filing person (including any person specified in Instruction C) and any other person with respect to any securities of the subject company [@CL-MNA-1067]. It also requires naming all persons that are a party and describing all material provisions [@CL-MNA-1068]. Instruction 1 states that the information required by the item includes the transfer or voting of securities, joint ventures, loan or option arrangements, puts or calls, guarantees of loans, guarantees against loss, or the giving or withholding of proxies, consents or authorizations [@CL-MNA-1121]. Instruction 2 requires information for any securities that are pledged or otherwise subject to a contingency, the occurrence of which would give another person the power to direct the voting or disposition of the subject securities [@CL-MNA-1069]. It states that no disclosure is required about standard default and similar provisions contained in loan agreements [@CL-MNA-1070].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) is bounded by exclusion: transactions that paragraphs (b) or (c) describe are left out of it [@CL-MNA-1055]. Its two tests use different measures: one percent of the subject company's consolidated revenues for the subject company and its affiliates that are not natural persons, and $60,000 for an executive officer, director or affiliate that is a natural person, with a series of similar transactions counted together [@CL-MNA-1056; @CL-MNA-1058]. Paragraph (b) looks at the filing person's dealings with the subject company or its affiliates, while paragraph (c) looks at contacts among affiliates and with unaffiliated persons who have a direct interest [@CL-MNA-1059; @CL-MNA-1061; @CL-MNA-1062]. The relief from the conflicts disclosure is stated for the case where the filing person is the subject company [@CL-MNA-1064]. Ownership of and dealings in the subject securities are covered on [another card](../valuation/mna-interest-in-securities-and-retained-persons.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The target has consolidated revenues of 200 million in its last fiscal year. A filing person bought goods worth 1.5 million in total from a non-natural-person affiliate of the target in that year, and assume those are its only transactions with the target and its non-natural-person affiliates that year. That is 0.75 percent of revenues, below the one percent test, so paragraph (a)(1) does not reach those purchases [@CL-MNA-1056]. During the past two years the filing person also paid a target director consulting fees of 40,000 and then 30,000 under similar arrangements. The series totals 70,000, which exceeds 60,000, so paragraph (a)(2) reaches it and the filing states its nature and approximate amount [@CL-MNA-1055; @CL-MNA-1058]. Eight months ago the filing person held merger talks with an affiliate of the target. Those talks fall under paragraph (b), not paragraph (a), and the filing identifies who started them [@CL-MNA-1055; @CL-MNA-1059; @CL-MNA-1060; @CL-MNA-1062].

## Never confuse

The revenue test and the dollar test reach different counterparties and are measured differently: the one percent test applies to the subject company and its affiliates that are not natural persons and is measured on the subject company's consolidated revenues, while the $60,000 test applies to an executive officer, director or affiliate that is a natural person and is measured on the aggregate value of the transaction or series of similar transactions [@CL-MNA-1056; @CL-MNA-1058]. A paragraph (b) contact is not a paragraph (a) transaction, because paragraph (a) leaves out what paragraphs (b) and (c) describe [@CL-MNA-1055]. An agreement about the subject company's securities must be described whether or not it is legally enforceable [@CL-MNA-1067].

## Company calibration

The company confirms which counterparties and agreements fall inside these tests, how it will add up similar transactions, and what it will screen for. [@Q-028]
