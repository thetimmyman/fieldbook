## Purpose

Says what a registered public accounting firm must report to the audit committee and when, and sets out the instructions for the statement of comprehensive income on reconciliation and unaudited interim statements.

| Applies to | Registered public accounting firms performing covered audits for issuers and registered investment companies, and statements of comprehensive income whose periods include pre-acquisition operations or differ from earlier reports [@CL-MNA-0883; @CL-MNA-0884; @CL-MNA-0889] |

Under § 210.2-07(a), each registered public accounting firm that performs for an audit client that is an issuer (as defined in section 10A(f) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 USC 78j-1(f))), other than an issuer that is an Asset-Backed Issuer as defined in § 229.1101 of the chapter, or an investment company registered under section 8 of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 USC 80a-8), other than a company that is a unit investment trust as defined in section 4(2) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 USC 80a-4(2)), any audit required under the securities laws shall report, prior to the filing of such audit report with the Commission (or in the case of a registered investment company, annually, and if the annual communication is not within 90 days prior to the filing, provide an update, in the 90 day period prior to the filing, of any changes to the previously reported information), to the audit committee of the issuer or registered investment company [@CL-MNA-0883; @CL-MNA-0884; @CL-MNA-0885; @CL-MNA-0886; @CL-MNA-0887]. The required report covers: (1) all critical accounting policies and practices to be used [@CL-MNA-0887], (2) all alternative treatments within Generally Accepted Accounting Principles for policies and practices related to material items that have been discussed with management of the issuer or registered investment company, including (i) ramifications of the use of such alternative disclosures and treatments and (ii) the treatment preferred by the registered public accounting firm [@CL-MNA-0888; @CL-MNA-0127], (3) other material written communications between the registered public accounting firm and the management of the issuer or registered investment company, such as any management letter or schedule of unadjusted differences [@CL-MNA-0947], and (4) if the audit client is an investment company, all non-audit services provided to any entity in an investment company complex, as defined in § 210.2-01(f)(14), that were not pre-approved by the registered investment company's audit committee pursuant to § 210.2-01(c)(7) [@CL-MNA-0948].

Under § 210.3-03(c), if a period or periods reported on include operations of a business prior to the date of acquisition, or for other reasons differ from reports previously issued for any period, the statements shall be reconciled as to sales or revenues and net income in the statement or in a note thereto with the amounts previously reported, provided, however, that such reconciliations need not be made (1) if they have been made in filings with the Commission in prior years or (2) the financial statements which are being retroactively adjusted have not previously been filed with the Commission or otherwise made public [@CL-MNA-0889; @CL-MNA-1582; @CL-MNA-0890]. Under § 210.3-03(d), any unaudited interim financial statements furnished shall reflect all adjustments which are, in the opinion of management, necessary to a fair statement of the results for the interim periods presented, and a statement to that effect shall be included [@CL-MNA-0891]. If all such adjustments are of a normal recurring nature, a statement to that effect shall be made, and otherwise, there shall be furnished information describing in appropriate detail the nature and amount of any adjustments other than normal recurring adjustments entering into the determination of the results shown [@CL-MNA-1536; @CL-MNA-1537].

## How it works

Under § 210.2-07(a), the registered public accounting firm reports the four categories of matters to the audit committee of the issuer or registered investment company prior to the filing of the audit report with the Commission (or for a registered investment company, annually, with an update in the 90 day period prior to the filing if the annual communication is not within 90 days prior to the filing) [@CL-MNA-0885; @CL-MNA-0886; @CL-MNA-0887; @CL-MNA-0888; @CL-MNA-0127; @CL-MNA-0947; @CL-MNA-0948]. Under § 210.3-03(c), if a period or periods reported on include operations of a business prior to the date of acquisition, or for other reasons differ from reports previously issued for any period, the statements must be reconciled as to sales or revenues and net income in the statement or in a note thereto with the amounts previously reported, unless the reconciliations have been made in filings with the Commission in prior years or the financial statements being retroactively adjusted have not previously been filed with the Commission or otherwise made public [@CL-MNA-0889; @CL-MNA-1582; @CL-MNA-0890]. Under § 210.3-03(d), unaudited interim financial statements furnished must reflect all adjustments which are, in the opinion of management, necessary to a fair statement of the results for the interim periods presented, include a statement to that effect, and either state that all such adjustments are of a normal recurring nature or describe in appropriate detail the nature and amount of any adjustments other than normal recurring adjustments entering into the determination of the results shown [@CL-MNA-0891; @CL-MNA-1536; @CL-MNA-1537].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant files a statement of comprehensive income that now includes a target's results for periods before the acquisition. Revenue reported earlier was 500 and the new statement shows 560. The registrant reconciles sales or revenues and net income to the earlier amounts in the statement or in a note, unless the proviso applies, for example because that reconciliation was already made in a filing with the Commission.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.2-07(a), a registered public accounting firm reports to the audit committee prior to the filing of the audit report with the Commission on critical accounting policies and practices, alternative GAAP treatments (including ramifications and the firm's preferred treatment), other material written communications with management, and, for an investment company audit client, non-audit services provided to an entity in an investment company complex not pre-approved pursuant to § 210.2-01(c)(7) [@CL-MNA-0885; @CL-MNA-0887; @CL-MNA-0888; @CL-MNA-0127; @CL-MNA-0947; @CL-MNA-0948], whereas the requirements for the accountant's report itself are on [Accountants' reports and retention of audit records](mna-sx-accountants-reports-and-record-retention.md), the condensed format and review of interim statements are on [Interim financial statements: condensed form, periods and review](mna-sx-interim-financial-statements.md), and the independence restrictions are on [Qualifications of accountants and auditor independence](mna-sx-auditor-independence.md).
