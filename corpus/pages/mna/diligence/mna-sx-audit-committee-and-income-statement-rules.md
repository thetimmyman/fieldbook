## Purpose

Says what a registered public accounting firm must report to the audit committee and when, and sets out the instructions for the statement of comprehensive income on reconciliation and unaudited interim statements. The rules are part of Regulation S-X, which is not FAR, DFARS or CAS.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Registered public accounting firms performing covered audits for issuers and registered investment companies, and statements of comprehensive income whose periods include pre-acquisition operations or differ from earlier reports [@CL-MNA-0883; @CL-MNA-0884; @CL-MNA-0889] |

The rule is aimed at each registered public accounting firm that performs for an audit client that is an issuer, as defined in section 10A(f) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, other than an Asset-Backed Issuer [@CL-MNA-0883]. It also reaches an audit client that is an investment company registered under section 8 of the Investment Company Act of 1940, other than a unit investment trust [@CL-MNA-0884]. For any audit required under the securities laws, the covered firm shall report prior to the filing of the audit report with the Commission, with different timing for a registered investment company [@CL-MNA-0885; @CL-MNA-0886]. For a registered investment company the report is made annually, with an update in the 90 day period prior to the filing if the annual communication is not within 90 days prior to it [@CL-MNA-0886]. The firm reports to the audit committee, and the first listed matter is all critical accounting policies and practices to be used [@CL-MNA-0887]. One listed matter is all alternative treatments within Generally Accepted Accounting Principles for policies and practices related to material items that have been discussed with management [@CL-MNA-0888]. Another listed matter is other material written communications between the firm and the management of the issuer or registered investment company, such as any management letter or schedule of unadjusted differences [@CL-MNA-0947]. If the audit client is an investment company, a further listed matter is all non-audit services provided to any entity in an investment company complex that were not pre-approved by the registered investment company's audit committee under the cited pre-approval provision [@CL-MNA-0948]. The alternative treatments matter also lists the ramifications of using the alternatives and the treatment the firm prefers, which this card does not draw out.

If a period reported on includes operations of a business prior to the date of acquisition, or for other reasons differs from reports previously issued for any period, the statements shall be reconciled as to sales or revenues and net income, subject to the proviso in the next sentence [@CL-MNA-0889; @CL-MNA-0890]. The reconciliations need not be made if they have been made in filings with the Commission in prior years, or if the financial statements being retroactively adjusted have not previously been filed with the Commission or otherwise made public [@CL-MNA-0890]. Any unaudited interim financial statements furnished shall reflect all adjustments which are, in the opinion of management, necessary to a fair statement of the results for the interim periods presented, and a statement to that effect shall be included [@CL-MNA-0891]. The instruction section also has an option for certain utility registrants, which this card does not draw out.

## How it works

The accounting firm reports the listed matters to the audit committee before the audit report is filed with the Commission, and for a registered investment company the report is annual with an update where the annual communication is not within the stated window [@CL-MNA-0885; @CL-MNA-0886; @CL-MNA-0887]. In the income statement instructions, a statement that includes a business's pre-acquisition operations is reconciled to earlier reports for sales or revenues and net income, unless the proviso removes the need [@CL-MNA-0889; @CL-MNA-0890]. Unaudited interim statements carry management's statement on adjustments [@CL-MNA-0891].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant files a statement of comprehensive income that now includes a target's results for periods before the acquisition. Revenue reported earlier was 500 and the new statement shows 560. The registrant reconciles sales or revenues and net income to the earlier amounts in the statement or in a note, unless the proviso applies, for example because that reconciliation was already made in a filing with the Commission.

## Never confuse

The communication to the audit committee is not the accountant's report on the financial statements, which is on [Accountants' reports and retention of audit records](mna-sx-accountants-reports-and-record-retention.md). The reconciliation duty here is also not the form of the interim statements, which is on [Interim financial statements: condensed form, periods and review](mna-sx-interim-financial-statements.md). The communication is also not a test of independence; the restrictions on relationships and non-audit services are on [Qualifications of accountants and auditor independence](mna-sx-auditor-independence.md).
