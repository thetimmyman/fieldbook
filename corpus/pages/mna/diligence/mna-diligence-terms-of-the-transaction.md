## Purpose

Shows which terms of a tender offer or merger a Regulation M-A filing must state, and what it must say about purchases from insiders, unequal treatment of holders, appraisal rights, protections for unaffiliated holders and trading eligibility.

### Material terms

Paragraph (a) requires the material terms of the transaction to be stated [@CL-MNA-0287]. For a tender offer, the information must include the total number and class of securities sought and the type and amount of consideration offered to security holders [@CL-MNA-0287]. It must also include the scheduled expiration date, whether a subsequent offering period will be available if the transaction is a third-party tender offer, and whether the offer may be extended and, if so, how [@CL-MNA-0288]. It must include the dates before and after which security holders may withdraw tendered securities, the procedures for tendering and withdrawing, and the manner in which securities will be accepted for payment [@CL-MNA-0289].

If the offer is for less than all securities of a class, the information must include the periods for accepting securities on a pro rata basis and the offeror's present intentions in the event that the offer is oversubscribed [@CL-MNA-0290]. Three more entries are required if material: an explanation of any material differences in the rights of security holders as a result of the transaction, a brief statement on the accounting treatment, and the federal income tax consequences [@CL-MNA-0291].

For a merger or similar transaction, the information must include a brief description of the transaction, the consideration offered to security holders, the reasons for engaging in the transaction and the vote required for approval [@CL-MNA-0292]. It must also include, if material, the same three entries on rights, accounting treatment and federal income tax consequences [@CL-MNA-0293].

If the consideration offered includes securities exempt from registration under the Securities Act of 1933, the instruction to Item 1004(a) requires furnishing a description of the securities that complies with Item 202 of Regulation S-K (§ 229.202) [@CL-MNA-0294]. This description is not required if the issuer of the securities meets the requirements of General Instructions I.A, I.B.1 or I.B.2, as applicable, or I.C of Form S-3 (§ 239.13 of the same chapter) and elects to furnish information by incorporation by reference, only capital stock is to be issued, and securities of the same class are registered under section 12 of the Exchange Act and either are listed for trading or admitted to unlisted trading privileges on a national securities exchange or are securities for which bid and offer quotations are reported in an automated quotations system operated by a national securities association [@CL-MNA-0295; @CL-MNA-0296; @CL-MNA-0297].

### Other required statements

Paragraph (b) requires a statement whether any securities are to be purchased from any officer, director or affiliate of the subject company, and the details of each transaction [@CL-MNA-0298]. Paragraph (c) requires a description of any term or arrangement in the Rule 13e-3 transaction that treats any subject security holders differently from other subject security holders [@CL-MNA-0299].

Paragraph (d) requires a statement whether or not dissenting security holders are entitled to any appraisal rights and, if so, a summary of the appraisal rights [@CL-MNA-0299]. If there are no appraisal rights available under state law for security holders who object to the transaction, it requires a brief outline of any other rights that may be available to security holders under the law [@CL-MNA-0251].

Paragraph (e) requires a description of any provision made by the filing person in connection with the transaction to grant unaffiliated security holders access to the corporate files of the filing person or to obtain counsel or appraisal services at the expense of the filing person, and, if none, a statement to that effect [@CL-MNA-1052]. Under paragraph (f), if the transaction involves the offer of securities of the filing person in exchange for equity securities held by unaffiliated security holders of the subject company, the filing person must describe whether or not it will take steps to assure that the securities offered are or will be eligible for trading on an automated quotations system operated by a national securities association [@CL-MNA-1053; @CL-MNA-1054].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) requires stating the material terms of the transaction, with specific items for tender offers under paragraph (a)(1) and for mergers or similar transactions under paragraph (a)(2) [@CL-MNA-0287; @CL-MNA-0292]. In both paragraphs (a)(1) and (a)(2), an explanation of any material differences in the rights of security holders as a result of the transaction, a brief statement as to the accounting treatment of the transaction, and the federal income tax consequences of the transaction are required if material [@CL-MNA-0291; @CL-MNA-0293]. Paragraph (c) applies to a Rule 13e-3 transaction, and paragraph (f) applies if the transaction involves the offer of securities of the filing person in exchange for equity securities held by unaffiliated security holders of the subject company [@CL-MNA-0299; @CL-MNA-1053; @CL-MNA-1054]. Paragraph (e) requires describing any provision made by the filing person in connection with the transaction to grant unaffiliated security holders access to the corporate files of the filing person or to obtain counsel or appraisal services at the expense of the filing person, or stating that there is none [@CL-MNA-1052].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder offers 40 in cash per share for up to 2 million of a target's 8 million shares, and the offer expires in 30 days. The filing states the 2 million shares sought, the cash consideration of 40 per share and the scheduled expiration date [@CL-MNA-0287; @CL-MNA-0288]. The offer is for less than all securities of the class, so the filing also states the periods for accepting shares pro rata and the bidder's present intentions if more than 2 million shares are tendered [@CL-MNA-0290]. If the offer is a third-party tender offer, the filing states whether a subsequent offering period will be available [@CL-MNA-0288]. It states the withdrawal dates, the tendering procedures and the manner of acceptance for payment [@CL-MNA-0289]. The accounting treatment is not material and is left out, while the federal income tax consequences of a cash purchase are material and are stated [@CL-MNA-0291]. The bidder also buys 100,000 shares from a director of the target, so paragraph (b) requires a statement of that purchase and its details [@CL-MNA-0298].

## Never confuse

Under paragraph (a), the items in paragraphs (a)(1)(i) through (ix) for a tender offer and (a)(2)(i) through (iv) for a merger or similar transaction must be included (with (a)(1)(iv) applying if the transaction is a third-party tender offer and (a)(1)(ix) applying if the offer is for less than all securities of a class), whereas the items on material differences in security holders' rights, accounting treatment, and federal income tax consequences in paragraphs (a)(1)(x) through (xii) and (a)(2)(v) through (vii) are required if material [@CL-MNA-0287; @CL-MNA-0288; @CL-MNA-0289; @CL-MNA-0290; @CL-MNA-0291; @CL-MNA-0292; @CL-MNA-0293]. For a merger or similar transaction, paragraph (a)(2) includes the reasons for engaging in the transaction and the vote required for approval, whereas for a tender offer, paragraph (a)(1) includes the dates and procedures for tendering and withdrawing securities and the manner in which securities will be accepted for payment [@CL-MNA-0289; @CL-MNA-0292]. Under paragraph (d), the filing states whether or not dissenting security holders are entitled to any appraisal rights and summarizes them if so, and if there are no appraisal rights available under state law for security holders who object to the transaction, it briefly outlines any other rights that may be available to security holders under the law [@CL-MNA-0299; @CL-MNA-0251]. The going-private reasons and effects are on a [separate card](../valuation/mna-going-private-purposes-alternatives-reasons-effects.md), and the defined terms are on the [definitions card](mna-diligence-regulation-m-a-definitions-and-term-sheet.md).

## Company calibration

The company confirms which deal form it is disclosing, which paragraphs apply, and that every entry for that form has an answer. [@Q-029]
