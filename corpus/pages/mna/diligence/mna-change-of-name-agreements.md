## Purpose

Shows when a renamed contractor needs an agreement with the Government and what it must send to the responsible contracting officer.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MNA-0364] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1205 [@CL-MNA-0364] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.904 [@CL-MNA-1003; @CL-MNA-1004] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0364] |
| Applies to | Contractors whose only change is the name, with the rights and obligations of both parties unaffected [@CL-MNA-0364] |

If only a change of the contractor's name is involved and the rights and obligations of the Government and the contractor remain unaffected, the parties must execute an agreement to reflect the name change [@CL-MNA-0364].

The FAR's general definitions say a change-of-name agreement is a legal instrument executed by the contractor and the Government that recognizes the legal change of name of the contractor without disturbing the original contractual rights and obligations of the parties [@CL-MNA-1401]. The RFO model text of FAR 2.101 defines a change-of-name agreement in the same words as the codified definition [@CL-MNA-1466; @CL-MNA-1401].

The contractor forwards three signed copies of the Change-of-Name Agreement to the responsible contracting officer, with one copy each of the listed documents [@CL-MNA-0365]. The first is the document effecting the name change, authenticated by a proper official of the State having jurisdiction [@CL-MNA-0365]. The second is the opinion of the contractor's legal counsel that the change was properly effected under applicable law, showing the effective date [@CL-MNA-0365; @CL-MNA-0366]. The third is a list of all affected contracts and purchase orders remaining unsettled between the contractor and the Government [@CL-MNA-0365; @CL-MNA-0367].

## How it works

The section calls for an agreement where only the name changes and the rights and obligations of both parties remain unaffected [@CL-MNA-0364]. For each unsettled contract or purchase order, the list gives the contract number and type and the name and address of the contracting office [@CL-MNA-0367]. Who the responsible contracting officer is, the written request, the information request and the legal review before signing are set out on [Novation agreements: who executes them and how they are processed](../deal-gates/mna-novation-processing.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs it, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.904 says that if only a change of the contractor's name is involved and the Government's and contractor's rights and obligations remain unaffected, the parties must execute an agreement to reflect the name change [@CL-MNA-1003; @CL-MNA-0364]. It says the contractor must forward to the responsible contracting officer three signed copies of the Change-of-Name Agreement and one copy each of the document effecting the name change, authenticated by a proper official of the State having jurisdiction [@CL-MNA-1004; @CL-MNA-0365], the opinion of the contractor's legal counsel that the change was properly effected under applicable law, showing the effective date [@CL-MNA-1005; @CL-MNA-0366], and a list of all affected contracts and purchase orders remaining unsettled between the contractor and the Government, showing for each the contract number and type and the name and address of the contracting office [@CL-MNA-1006; @CL-MNA-0367]. It adds that the contracting officer may request the total dollar value as amended and the remaining unpaid balance for each contract [@CL-MNA-1010].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor amends its certificate of incorporation to take a new corporate name. Nothing else changes, and it still holds the same contracts. It sends three signed copies of the agreement, the State-authenticated document, its counsel's opinion and a list of its unsettled contracts, each with number, type and contracting office. This is a name change only, so the change-of-name route applies.

## Never confuse

A name change leaves the rights and obligations of both parties unaffected [@CL-MNA-0364]. A transfer of all the contractor's assets, or of the entire portion involved in performing the contract, to a different entity is a different case, because the Government may, when in its interest, recognize a third party as the successor in interest when the third party's interest in the contract arises out of such a transfer [@CL-MNA-0102; @CL-MNA-0103]; see [Novation and change-of-name agreements](mna-novation-agreements.md). A company that only changes its name is also not a successor for the predecessor representation in offers [@CL-MNA-0387].

## Company calibration

Whether any target or its subsidiaries have recently changed name, and where the authenticated name-change documents and counsel's opinion are kept. [@Q-028]
