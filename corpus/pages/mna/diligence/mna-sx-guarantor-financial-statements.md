## Purpose

Shows when separate financial statements of an issuer or guarantor of a guaranteed security can be left out, and what alternative disclosures the parent company gives instead. The rules are part of Regulation S-X, which is not FAR, DFARS or CAS. Separate subsidiary statements in group reporting are on [Group reporting notes and separate subsidiary statements under Regulation S-X](../../close-revrec/group-bridge/cls-sx-group-reporting-notes.md).

| Applies to | Issuers and guarantors of guaranteed securities that are registered or being registered and would otherwise be required to file financial statements, where the parent company's consolidated statements are filed [@CL-MNA-0951; @CL-MNA-0892] |

The financial statements that an issuer or guarantor of a guaranteed security would otherwise be required to file may be omitted if the issuer or guarantor is a consolidated subsidiary of the parent company, the parent company's consolidated financial statements have been filed, and the conditions in paragraphs (a)(1) and (2) have been met [@CL-MNA-0951; @CL-MNA-0892]. One listed way to meet the first condition is that the guaranteed security is debt or debt-like and the parent company issues the security or co-issues it, jointly and severally, with one or more of its consolidated subsidiaries [@CL-MNA-0893]. One other listed way is that the guaranteed security is debt or debt-like, a consolidated subsidiary issues the security or co-issues it with one or more other consolidated subsidiaries of the parent company, and the parent company guarantees the security fully and unconditionally [@CL-MNA-0893; @CL-MNA-0894]. One listed condition is that the parent company provides the disclosures specified in the guarantor disclosure section [@CL-MNA-0895].

The disclosure section applies to each guaranteed security subject to section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and to each guaranteed security the offer and sale of which is being registered under the Securities Act of 1933, for which the registrant is the parent company [@CL-MNA-0949]. Where the registrant is the parent company of one or more subsidiaries that issue or guarantee the guaranteed security, the section requires the listed disclosures to the extent material, the first being a description of the issuers and guarantors [@CL-MNA-0896]. One listed disclosure is summarized financial information of each issuer and guarantor, with an accompanying note that briefly describes the basis of presentation [@CL-MNA-0897]. One listed requirement is that the summarized financial information is disclosed as of and for the most recently ended fiscal year and year-to-date interim period included in the parent company's consolidated financial statements [@CL-MNA-0898]. The parent company may elect to provide the disclosures in a footnote to its consolidated financial statements or alternatively in management's discussion and analysis of financial condition and results of operations [@CL-MNA-0899]. The summarized financial information may be omitted if it is not material, and may also be omitted if one of the circumstances in the listed paragraphs is true and disclosed [@CL-MNA-0958]. The disclosure section also lists further items and conditions on those circumstances, which this card does not draw out.

## How it works

The parent company first checks that the issuer or guarantor is a consolidated subsidiary and that its own consolidated statements have been filed [@CL-MNA-0892]. It then checks the security type and the issuer and guarantee pattern, and that it provides the alternative disclosures [@CL-MNA-0893; @CL-MNA-0894; @CL-MNA-0895]. If all of that holds, the separate statements can be omitted, and the parent company may elect to give the disclosures in a footnote or in the discussion and analysis [@CL-MNA-0892; @CL-MNA-0899].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A parent company registers debt notes that a consolidated subsidiary issues and the parent guarantees fully and unconditionally. The parent's consolidated statements are on file and it gives the guarantor disclosures in a footnote, including summarized financial information for the issuer and guarantor. The subsidiary's own separate financial statements are then not needed.

## Never confuse

The alternative disclosures are not full financial statements of each guarantor. They are summarized financial information with a note on the basis of presentation [@CL-MNA-0897]. The rule on [Affiliates whose securities collateralize registered securities](mna-sx-collateral-affiliate-disclosures.md) is a different rule: it concerns securities of affiliates that are pledged as collateral [@CL-MNA-0901], not subsidiaries that issue or guarantee a security [@CL-MNA-0896].
