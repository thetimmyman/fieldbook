## Purpose

Shows when separate financial statements of an issuer or guarantor of a guaranteed security can be left out, and what alternative disclosures the parent company gives instead. Separate subsidiary statements in group reporting are on [Group reporting notes and separate subsidiary statements under Regulation S-X](../../close-revrec/group-bridge/cls-sx-group-reporting-notes.md).

| Applies to | Issuers and guarantors of guaranteed securities that are registered or being registered and would otherwise be required to file financial statements, where the parent company's consolidated statements are filed [@CL-MNA-0951; @CL-MNA-0892] |

Under § 210.3-10(a), if an issuer or guarantor of a guaranteed security that is registered or being registered is required to file financial statements required by Regulation S-X with respect to the guarantee or guaranteed security, such financial statements may be omitted if the issuer or guarantor is a consolidated subsidiary of the parent company, the parent company's consolidated financial statements have been filed, and the conditions in paragraphs (a)(1) and (2) of the section have been met [@CL-MNA-0951; @CL-MNA-0892]. Under paragraph (a)(1), the guaranteed security is debt or debt-like, and either (i) the parent company issues the security or co-issues the security, jointly and severally, with one or more of its consolidated subsidiaries [@CL-MNA-0893], or (ii) a consolidated subsidiary issues the security or co-issues the security with one or more other consolidated subsidiaries of the parent company, and the security is guaranteed fully and unconditionally by the parent company [@CL-MNA-0893; @CL-MNA-0894]. Under paragraph (a)(2), the parent company provides the disclosures specified in § 210.13-01 [@CL-MNA-0895].

Under § 210.13-01(a), for each guaranteed security subject to Section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and for each guaranteed security the offer and sale of which is being registered under the Securities Act of 1933, for which the registrant is the parent company (as defined in § 210.3-10(b)(1)) of one or more subsidiaries that issue or guarantee the guaranteed security, the registrant must provide the required disclosures to the extent material, including (1) a description of the issuers and guarantors of the guaranteed security [@CL-MNA-0949; @CL-MNA-0896] and (4) summarized financial information as specified in § 210.1-02(bb)(1) of each issuer and guarantor of the guaranteed security with an accompanying note that briefly describes the basis of presentation [@CL-MNA-0897]. Under paragraph (a)(4)(v), this summarized financial information is disclosed as of and for the most recently ended fiscal year and year-to-date interim period included in the parent company's consolidated financial statements [@CL-MNA-0898]. Under paragraph (b), the parent company may elect to provide the disclosures required by § 210.13-01 in a footnote to its consolidated financial statements or alternatively, in management's discussion and analysis of financial condition and results of operations described in § 229.303 [@CL-MNA-0899]. Under paragraph (a)(4)(vi), notwithstanding that a parent company may omit this summarized financial information if not material, it may also be omitted if one of the items in paragraphs (a)(4)(vi)(A) through (D) of the section is true and disclosed [@CL-MNA-0958], except that paragraph (a)(4)(vi)(A) does not apply if separate disclosure of summarized financial information applicable to one or more, but not all, issuers and/or guarantors is required by paragraph (a)(4)(iv) of the section [@CL-MNA-1526].

## How it works

Under § 210.3-10(a), the financial statements required by Regulation S-X for an issuer or guarantor of a guaranteed security that is registered or being registered may be omitted if the issuer or guarantor is a consolidated subsidiary of the parent company, the parent company's consolidated financial statements have been filed, the guaranteed security is debt or debt-like and satisfies paragraph (a)(1)(i) or (ii), and the parent company provides the disclosures specified in § 210.13-01 [@CL-MNA-0951; @CL-MNA-0892; @CL-MNA-0893; @CL-MNA-0894; @CL-MNA-0895]. Under § 210.13-01(b), the parent company may elect to provide the disclosures required by § 210.13-01 in a footnote to its consolidated financial statements or alternatively in management's discussion and analysis of financial condition and results of operations described in § 229.303 [@CL-MNA-0899].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A parent company registers debt notes that a consolidated subsidiary issues and the parent guarantees fully and unconditionally. The parent's consolidated statements are on file and it gives the guarantor disclosures in a footnote, including summarized financial information for the issuer and guarantor. The subsidiary's own separate financial statements are then not needed.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.3-10(a) and § 210.13-01(a)(4), when the conditions for omitting separate financial statements of a consolidated-subsidiary issuer or guarantor are met, the parent company provides summarized financial information as specified in § 210.1-02(bb)(1) of each issuer and guarantor of the guaranteed security, with an accompanying note that briefly describes the basis of presentation, to the extent material and subject to paragraph (a)(4)(vi) [@CL-MNA-0951; @CL-MNA-0892; @CL-MNA-0897; @CL-MNA-0958]. Under § 210.13-02(a), the collateral disclosures apply to a security collateralized by a security of the registrant's affiliate or affiliates [@CL-MNA-0901], whereas § 210.13-01(a) applies where the registrant is the parent company of one or more subsidiaries that issue or guarantee the guaranteed security [@CL-MNA-0949; @CL-MNA-0896]; see [Affiliates whose securities collateralize registered securities](mna-sx-collateral-affiliate-disclosures.md).
