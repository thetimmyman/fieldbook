## Purpose

Shows which ownership and control events a contractor with access to classified information must report to its cognizant security agency.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Contractors with an entity eligibility determination for access to classified information [@CL-MNA-0536] |

Under 32 CFR 117.8, contractors are required to report certain events that affect the status of the entity eligibility determination (e.g., FCL), affect the status of an employee's PCL, may indicate an employee poses an insider threat, affect the proper safeguarding of classified information, or indicate classified information has been lost or compromised [@CL-MNA-0536; @CL-MNA-0079; @CL-MNA-0537]. A change of ownership or control of the contractor, including stock transfers that affect control of the entity, is among the events listed in the changed-conditions paragraph [@CL-MNA-0537].

Any material change concerning the information previously reported concerning foreign ownership, control, or influence (FOCI) will be reported by the submission of an updated SF 328, "Certificate Pertaining to Foreign Interests," in accordance with CSA-provided guidance, and when submitting this information, it is not necessary to repeat answers that have not changed [@CL-MNA-0538; @CL-MNA-0100]. When entering into discussion, consultations, or agreements that may reasonably lead to effective ownership or control by a foreign interest, the contractor will report the details to the CSA in writing, and if the contractor has received a Schedule 13D from the investor, the contractor will forward a copy with the report [@CL-MNA-0539; @CL-MNA-0112].

## How it works

A change of ownership or control of the contractor, including stock transfers that affect control of the entity, is among the changed conditions that contractors must report [@CL-MNA-0536; @CL-MNA-0537]. When entering into discussion, consultations, or agreements that may reasonably lead to effective ownership or control by a foreign interest, the contractor will report the details to the CSA in writing and, if the contractor has received a Schedule 13D from the investor, will forward a copy with the report [@CL-MNA-0539; @CL-MNA-0112]. For any material change concerning the information previously reported concerning FOCI, the report will be made by submitting an updated SF 328 in accordance with CSA-provided guidance, and when submitting this information, it is not necessary to repeat answers that have not changed [@CL-MNA-0538; @CL-MNA-0100].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cleared contractor's parent agrees to sell all its shares to a new owner. The contractor reports the change of ownership or control to its CSA. Earlier, when the new owner turned out to be a foreign interest, the contractor had already reported the discussions in writing.

Answer: the share sale is a change of ownership or control of the kind the paragraph lists, and the foreign ownership information on file is updated with a new SF 328. Whether the entity stays eligible is the CSA's decision under the FOCI rules.

## Never confuse

Under 32 CFR 117.8, a material change concerning previously reported FOCI information is reported by submitting an updated SF 328 in accordance with CSA-provided guidance without needing to repeat answers that have not changed, whereas entering into discussion, consultations, or agreements that may reasonably lead to effective ownership or control by a foreign interest is reported to the CSA in writing, with a copy of any Schedule 13D received from the investor forwarded with the report [@CL-MNA-0538; @CL-MNA-0100; @CL-MNA-0539; @CL-MNA-0112]. The contract-side steps for a successor are separate and sit on the [Novation and change-of-name agreements](mna-novation-agreements.md) card.
