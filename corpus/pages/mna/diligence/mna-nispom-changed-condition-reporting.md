## Purpose

Shows which ownership and control events a contractor with access to classified information must report to its cognizant security agency.

| Applies to | Contractors with an entity eligibility determination for access to classified information [@CL-MNA-0536] |

This card rests on the NISPOM, 32 CFR 117.8, a DoD rule for classified work, and not on the FAR [@CL-MNA-0536]. Contractors are required to report certain events that affect the status of the entity eligibility determination [@CL-MNA-0536]. A change of ownership or control of the contractor, including stock transfers that affect control of the entity, is among the events listed in the changed-conditions paragraph [@CL-MNA-0537].

A material change concerning the information previously reported on foreign ownership, control or influence is reported by submitting an updated SF 328 in accordance with CSA-provided guidance [@CL-MNA-0538].

## How it works

A transaction that changes ownership or control, including a stock transfer that affects control, is among the listed events [@CL-MNA-0537]. When the contractor enters into discussion, consultations or agreements that may reasonably lead to effective ownership or control by a foreign interest, it reports the details to the CSA in writing [@CL-MNA-0539]. If the foreign ownership information already on file changes materially, the contractor files an updated SF 328 [@CL-MNA-0538]. What the CSA then decides is covered on the NISPOM FOCI card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cleared contractor's parent agrees to sell all its shares to a new owner. The contractor reports the change of ownership or control to its CSA. Earlier, when the new owner turned out to be a foreign interest, the contractor had already reported the discussions in writing.

Answer: the share sale is a change of ownership or control of the kind the paragraph lists, and the foreign ownership information on file is updated with a new SF 328. Whether the entity stays eligible is the CSA's decision under the FOCI rules.

## Never confuse

Reporting a change is not the same as the FOCI determination. The report puts the event in front of the CSA, and whether the entity is under FOCI is decided under the FOCI rules. The contract-side steps for a successor are separate and sit on the [Novation and change-of-name agreements](mna-novation-agreements.md) card.
