## Purpose

Explains when the Government may recognize a successor in interest after a transfer of assets, what the contractor must file, and why a stock purchase or a change of name is handled differently.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MNA-0101] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1204 [@CL-MNA-0101] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0101] |
| Applies to | Transfers of all the contractor's assets or of the entire portion of the assets involved in performing the contract [@CL-MNA-0103] |

A statute prohibits transferring a Government contract from the contractor to a third party, but the Government may, when in its interest, recognize a third party as the successor in interest when that party's interest arises out of the transfer of all the contractor's assets or of the entire portion of the assets involved in performing the contract [@CL-MNA-0101; @CL-MNA-0102; @CL-MNA-0103].

The transfer must be of all the contractor's assets or of the entire portion of the assets involved in performing the contract, and a sale of the assets with a provision for assuming liabilities or a transfer incident to a merger or corporate consolidation are examples [@CL-MNA-0103; @CL-MNA-0104]. A stock purchase that changes ownership without a legal change in the contracting party, where that party stays in control of the assets and performs the contract, needs no novation agreement [@CL-MNA-0105].

## How it works

The responsible contracting officer identifies and evaluates any significant organizational conflicts of interest under the conflict of interest rules before recognizing a successor, and may seek a waiver where a conflict cannot be resolved but approval serves the Government's interest [@CL-MNA-0106; @CL-MNA-0107].

When a contractor asks the Government to recognize a successor in interest, it submits three signed copies of the proposed agreement to the responsible contracting officer [@CL-MNA-0108], together with one copy each, as applicable, of the transaction document, a list of the affected contracts with their numbers, types, values and unpaid balances, and evidence of the transferee's capability to perform.

Further documents are submitted, as applicable, as they become available, unless the contracting officer modifies the list under paragraph (g), including the opinion of legal counsel for the transferor and transferee that the transfer was properly effected under applicable law and its effective date [@CL-MNA-0109].

The executed agreement ordinarily provides that the transferee assumes all the transferor's obligations under the contract and that the transferor waives all rights under the contract against the Government [@CL-MNA-0110].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buyer acquires all the assets of a division that performs two cost-reimbursement contracts with a combined value of 4,000,000 and an unpaid balance of 900,000, and assumes the related liabilities. Because the transfer covers all the assets used to perform those contracts, the Government may recognize the buyer as successor in interest if that is in its interest, and the seller as contractor submits three signed copies of the proposed agreement with a list showing each contract's number, type, value and unpaid balance. A stock purchase of the seller, leaving the contracting party unchanged and in control of the assets, would need no novation agreement.

## Never confuse

A novation agreement addresses a change in the party that performs the contract, while a stock purchase that leaves the contracting party in place and in control of the assets needs none [@CL-MNA-0105]. A change of name is different again: where only the contractor's name changes and the rights and obligations of both parties are unaffected, the parties execute an agreement to reflect the name change.

Recognition is not automatic, and when the Government does not concur the original contractor stays contractually obligated and may face termination for default if it does not perform [@CL-MNA-0111].
