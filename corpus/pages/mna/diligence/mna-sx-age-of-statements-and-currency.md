## Purpose

Shows how old the latest financial statements in a filing may be when the filing becomes effective, and which currency the statements are stated in. The age rule for smaller reporting companies is on [Smaller reporting company interim statements and age of statements](mna-sx-smaller-company-interim-statements-and-age.md), and the condensed form of the interim statements is on [Interim financial statements: condensed form, periods and review](mna-sx-interim-financial-statements.md).

| Applies to | Financial statements in a filing that is expected to become effective, or in a proxy statement that is to be mailed [@CL-MNA-0697] |

Under § 210.3-12(a), if the financial statements in a filing are as of a date the number of days specified in paragraph (g) of the section or more before the date the filing is expected to become effective, or proposed mailing date in the case of a proxy statement, the financial statements shall be updated, except as specified in the following paragraphs, with a balance sheet as of an interim date within the number of days specified in paragraph (g) of the section and with statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for the interim period between the end of the most recent fiscal year and the date of the interim balance sheet provided and for the corresponding period of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0697; @CL-MNA-0120; @CL-MNA-0121]. Under paragraph (g)(1), for purposes of paragraph (a) of the section, the number of days shall be 130 days for large accelerated filers and accelerated filers (as defined in § 240.12b-2 of the chapter) and 135 days for all other registrants [@CL-MNA-0698].

Under § 210.3-12(d), the age of the registrant's most recent audited financial statements included in a registration statement filed under the Securities Act of 1933 or filed on Form 10 (17 CFR 249.210) under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 shall not be more than one year and 45 days old at the date the registration statement becomes effective if the registration statement relates to the security of an issuer that was not subject, immediately before the time of filing the registration statement, to the reporting requirements of section 13 or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 [@CL-MNA-0699; @CL-MNA-0700].

Under § 210.3-20(a), a foreign private issuer, as defined in § 230.405 of the chapter, shall state amounts in its primary financial statements in the currency which it deems appropriate, and an issuer that is not a foreign private issuer shall present its financial statements in US dollars [@CL-MNA-0701]. Under § 210.3-20(e), the issuer shall state its primary financial statements in the same currency for all periods for which financial information is presented [@CL-MNA-0702]. If the financial statements are stated in a currency that is different from that used in financial statements previously filed with the Commission, the issuer shall recast its financial statements as if the newly adopted currency had been used since at least the earliest period presented in the filing, and the decision to change and the reason for the change in the reporting currency shall be disclosed in a note to the financial statements in the period in which the change occurs [@CL-MNA-0122; @CL-MNA-0123].

## How it works

Under § 210.3-12(a) and (g)(1), if the financial statements in a filing are as of a date 130 days (for large accelerated filers and accelerated filers) or 135 days (for all other registrants) or more before the expected effective date or proposed proxy mailing date, they shall be updated, except as specified in the following paragraphs, with a balance sheet as of an interim date within the specified number of days and with statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for the interim period between the end of the most recent fiscal year and the date of the interim balance sheet provided and for the corresponding period of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0697; @CL-MNA-0120; @CL-MNA-0121; @CL-MNA-0698]. Under paragraph (d), the most recent audited financial statements in a Securities Act registration statement or Form 10 registration statement shall not be more than one year and 45 days old at the effective date if the registration statement relates to the security of an issuer that was not subject, immediately before the time of filing, to the reporting requirements of section 13 or 15(d) of the Exchange Act [@CL-MNA-0699; @CL-MNA-0700]. Under § 210.3-20(a) and (e), a foreign private issuer states amounts in its primary financial statements in the currency it deems appropriate, an issuer that is not a foreign private issuer presents its financial statements in US dollars, and the issuer states its primary financial statements in the same currency for all periods presented (recasting prior periods as if a newly adopted currency had been used since at least the earliest period presented in the filing and disclosing the decision and reason in a note in the period of change) [@CL-MNA-0701; @CL-MNA-0702; @CL-MNA-0122; @CL-MNA-0123].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant that is neither a large accelerated filer nor an accelerated filer has statements as of a date 140 days before the expected effective date. The line for such a registrant is 135 days, so the statements are out of date and an interim update is needed. Separately, a company files a Securities Act registration statement and was not subject to the reporting requirements immediately before filing. Its audited statements are 14 months old at effectiveness, so the one year and 45 days limit applies and the audited statements are too old.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.3-12(a) and (g)(1), the 130-day and 135-day thresholds determine when financial statements in a filing must be updated with an interim balance sheet and interim statements of comprehensive income and cash flows [@CL-MNA-0697; @CL-MNA-0120; @CL-MNA-0121; @CL-MNA-0698], whereas under § 210.3-12(d), the one-year-and-45-day limit applies to the age of the registrant's most recent audited financial statements included in a Securities Act registration statement or Form 10 registration statement at the effective date if the registration statement relates to the security of an issuer that was not subject, immediately before the time of filing, to the reporting requirements of section 13 or 15(d) of the Exchange Act [@CL-MNA-0699; @CL-MNA-0700].
