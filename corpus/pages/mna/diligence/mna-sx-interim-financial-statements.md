## Purpose

Shows what condensed interim financial statements contain, which periods a quarterly report presents and when an accountant must review them. The interim rules for smaller reporting companies are on [Smaller reporting company interim statements and age of statements](mna-sx-smaller-company-interim-statements-and-age.md), and the age of statements is on [Age of financial statements and reporting currency](mna-sx-age-of-statements-and-currency.md).

| Applies to | Interim financial statements required under the interim section, for the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated and its predecessors [@CL-MNA-0708] |

Under § 210.10-01(a)(1), interim financial statements required by the section need only be provided as to the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated and may be unaudited, and separate financial statements of the registrant's predecessors shall be presented to the same extent as required for audited financial statements [@CL-MNA-0708]. Under paragraph (a)(2), where any major balance sheet caption is less than 10% of total assets, and the amount in the caption has not increased or decreased by more than 25% since the end of the preceding fiscal year, the caption may be combined with others [@CL-MNA-0709]. Under paragraph (a)(4), the statement of cash flows may be abbreviated starting with a single figure of net cash flows from operating activities and showing cash changes from investing and financing activities individually only when they exceed 10% of the average of net cash flows from operating activities for the most recent three years [@CL-MNA-0782; @CL-MNA-0783], except that notwithstanding this test, § 210.4-02 applies and de minimis amounts therefore need not be shown separately [@CL-MNA-1525].

Under § 210.10-01(c), for filings on Form 10-Q, financial statements shall be provided as set forth in paragraph (c): (1) an interim balance sheet as of the end of the most recent fiscal quarter and a balance sheet as of the end of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0710], (2) interim statements of comprehensive income for the most recent fiscal quarter, for the period between the end of the preceding fiscal year and the end of the most recent fiscal quarter, and for the corresponding periods of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0711], and (3) interim statements of cash flows for the period between the end of the preceding fiscal year and the end of the most recent fiscal quarter, and for the corresponding period of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0712]. Under paragraph (c)(4), registrants engaged in seasonal production and sale of a single-crop agricultural commodity may provide interim statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for the twelve month period ended during the most recent fiscal quarter and for the corresponding preceding period in lieu of the year-to-date statements specified in paragraphs (c)(2) and (3) of the section [@CL-MNA-0784; @CL-MNA-0785].

Under § 210.10-01(d), prior to filing, interim financial statements included in quarterly reports on Form 10-Q (17 CFR 249.308(a)) must be reviewed by an independent public accountant using applicable professional standards and procedures for conducting such reviews, as may be modified or supplemented by the Commission [@CL-MNA-0713; @CL-MNA-1583]. If, in any filing, the company states that interim financial statements have been reviewed by an independent public accountant, a report of the accountant on the review must be filed with the interim financial statements [@CL-MNA-0133].

## How it works

Under § 210.10-01(a), interim financial statements required by the section need only be provided as to the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated (with separate financial statements of the registrant's predecessors presented to the same extent as required for audited financial statements) and may be unaudited, major balance sheet captions less than 10% of total assets that have not increased or decreased by more than 25% since the end of the preceding fiscal year may be combined with others, and the statement of cash flows may be abbreviated under paragraph (a)(4) (with § 210.4-02 applying so de minimis amounts need not be shown separately) [@CL-MNA-0708; @CL-MNA-0709; @CL-MNA-0782; @CL-MNA-0783; @CL-MNA-1525]. Under paragraph (c), for filings on Form 10-Q, the registrant provides the balance sheets, interim statements of comprehensive income, and interim statements of cash flows for the periods specified in paragraphs (c)(1) through (c)(3), or the twelve-month statements under paragraph (c)(4) for registrants engaged in seasonal production and sale of a single-crop agricultural commodity [@CL-MNA-0710; @CL-MNA-0711; @CL-MNA-0712; @CL-MNA-0784; @CL-MNA-0785]. Under paragraph (d), prior to filing, interim financial statements included in quarterly reports on Form 10-Q must be reviewed by an independent public accountant using applicable professional standards and procedures for conducting such reviews, as may be modified or supplemented by the Commission, and if in any filing the company states that interim financial statements have been reviewed by an independent public accountant, a report of the accountant on the review must be filed with the interim financial statements [@CL-MNA-0713; @CL-MNA-1583; @CL-MNA-0133].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant files a quarterly report. A major balance sheet caption is 6% of total assets and has risen 10% since the prior year end. Both figures are inside the lines of 10% and 25%, so the caption may be combined with others. The accountant reviews the statements before the report is filed.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.10-01(a)(1) and (d), interim financial statements required by the section may be unaudited, but prior to filing, interim financial statements included in quarterly reports on Form 10-Q must be reviewed by an independent public accountant using applicable professional standards and procedures for conducting such reviews, as may be modified or supplemented by the Commission, and if in any filing the company states that interim financial statements have been reviewed by an independent public accountant, a report of the accountant on the review must be filed with the interim financial statements [@CL-MNA-0708; @CL-MNA-0713; @CL-MNA-1583; @CL-MNA-0133]. Under paragraph (c), the periods in paragraphs (c)(1) through (4) apply to filings on Form 10-Q, whereas the periods for which interim financial statements are to be provided in registration statements are prescribed elsewhere in Regulation S-X [@CL-MNA-0710; @CL-MNA-0786]. The accountant's report and record retention rules are on [Accountants' reports and retention of audit records](mna-sx-accountants-reports-and-record-retention.md).
