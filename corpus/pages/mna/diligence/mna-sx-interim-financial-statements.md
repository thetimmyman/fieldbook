## Purpose

Shows what condensed interim financial statements contain, which periods a quarterly report presents and when an accountant must review them. The rules are part of Regulation S-X, which is not FAR, DFARS or CAS. The interim rules for smaller reporting companies are on [Smaller reporting company interim statements and age of statements](mna-sx-smaller-company-interim-statements-and-age.md), and the age of statements is on [Age of financial statements and reporting currency](mna-sx-age-of-statements-and-currency.md).

| Applies to | Interim financial statements required under the interim section, for the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated and its predecessors [@CL-MNA-0708] |

Interim statements required by the interim section need only be provided for the registrant and its subsidiaries consolidated and its predecessors, and may be unaudited [@CL-MNA-0708]. A major interim balance sheet caption may be combined with others where it is less than 10% of total assets and its amount has not increased or decreased by more than 25% since the end of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0709]. The interim statement of cash flows may be abbreviated, starting with a single figure of net cash flows from operating activities [@CL-MNA-0782]. In that case cash changes from investing and financing activities are shown individually only when they exceed 10% of the average of net cash flows from operating activities for the most recent three years [@CL-MNA-0783], but section 210.4-02 still applies, so de minimis amounts need not be shown separately [@CL-MNA-1525].

For filings on Form 10-Q, financial statements are provided as set forth in the periods paragraph, and one listed item is an interim balance sheet as of the end of the most recent fiscal quarter and a balance sheet as of the end of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0710]. Another listed item is interim statements of comprehensive income for the most recent fiscal quarter, for the period from the end of the preceding fiscal year to the end of the most recent quarter, and for the corresponding periods of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0711]. A third is interim statements of cash flows for the period from the end of the preceding fiscal year to the end of the most recent fiscal quarter and for the corresponding period of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0712]. A registrant engaged in seasonal production and sale of a single-crop agricultural commodity may instead provide interim statements of comprehensive income and cash flows for the twelve month period ended during the most recent fiscal quarter and for the corresponding preceding period [@CL-MNA-0784]. Those statements are provided in lieu of the year-to-date statements specified for the Form 10-Q periods [@CL-MNA-0785].

Before filing, interim financial statements included in quarterly reports on Form 10-Q must be reviewed by an independent public accountant [@CL-MNA-0713]. The section also sets caption tests for the income statement and lists extra disclosures. This card does not draw out those thresholds or lists.

## How it works

The registrant prepares interim statements for itself and its consolidated subsidiaries, and they may be unaudited [@CL-MNA-0708]. It may combine major balance sheet captions that pass both the size test and the change test, and it may abbreviate the cash flow statement [@CL-MNA-0709; @CL-MNA-0782; @CL-MNA-0783]. For a quarterly report it presents the balance sheets, income statements and cash flow statements for the periods listed, subject to the seasonal single-crop alternative [@CL-MNA-0710; @CL-MNA-0711; @CL-MNA-0712; @CL-MNA-0784; @CL-MNA-0785]. An independent public accountant reviews the statements before filing [@CL-MNA-0713].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant files a quarterly report. A major balance sheet caption is 6% of total assets and has risen 10% since the prior year end. Both figures are inside the lines of 10% and 25%, so the caption may be combined with others. The accountant reviews the statements before the report is filed.

## Never confuse

Condensed does not mean unreviewed, because the accountant review applies to interim statements in a quarterly report before filing [@CL-MNA-0713]. The periods listed here are for filings on the quarterly report form [@CL-MNA-0710]. The periods for interim statements in registration statements are prescribed elsewhere in Regulation S-X [@CL-MNA-0786]. The accountant review is covered on [Accountants' reports and retention of audit records](mna-sx-accountants-reports-and-record-retention.md).
