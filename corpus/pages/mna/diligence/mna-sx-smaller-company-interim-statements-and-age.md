## Purpose

Shows what interim statements a smaller reporting company provides, whether they must be audited or reviewed, and how current its statements must be at effectiveness. The annual periods for income, cash flow and equity statements are on [Statements of comprehensive income, cash flows and equity under Regulation S-X](../../close-revrec/group-bridge/cls-sx-income-cash-flow-equity-statements.md). The age rules for other registrants are on [Age of financial statements and reporting currency](mna-sx-age-of-statements-and-currency.md), and the interim rules for other registrants are on [Interim financial statements: condensed form, periods and review](mna-sx-interim-financial-statements.md).

| Applies to | Financial statements filed for smaller reporting companies [@CL-MNA-0791] |

Under § 210.8-01(a), §§ 210.8-01 through 210.8-08 (Article 8) shall be applicable to financial statements filed for smaller reporting companies, and shall not be applicable to financial statements prepared for the purposes of Item 17 or Item 18 of Form 20-F [@CL-MNA-0791]. Under § 210.8-03, interim financial statements shall include a balance sheet as of the end of the issuer's most recent fiscal quarter, a balance sheet as of the end of the preceding fiscal year, and statements of comprehensive income and statements of cash flows for the interim period up to the date of such balance sheet and the comparable period of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0703; @CL-MNA-0704]. Interim financial statements may be unaudited, except that before filing, interim financial statements included in quarterly reports on Form 10-Q (§ 249.308(a) of the chapter) must be reviewed by an independent public accountant using applicable professional standards and procedures for conducting such reviews, as may be modified or supplemented by the Commission [@CL-MNA-0705; @CL-MNA-1586]. If, in any filing, the issuer states that interim financial statements have been reviewed by an independent public accountant, a report of the accountant on the review must be filed with the interim financial statements [@CL-MNA-0139].

Under § 210.8-08, if required financial statements are as of a date 135 days or more before the date a registration statement becomes effective or proxy material is expected to be mailed, the financial statements shall be updated to include financial statements for an interim period ending within 135 days of the effective or expected mailing date [@CL-MNA-0706; @CL-MNA-1587]. Under paragraph (b), if the effective date or anticipated mailing date falls after 45 days but within 90 days of the end of the smaller reporting company's fiscal year, the smaller reporting company is not required to provide the audited financial statements for such year end provided that the following conditions are met: (1) if the smaller reporting company is a reporting company, all reports due must have been filed [@CL-MNA-0707; @CL-MNA-0779], (2) for the most recent fiscal year for which audited financial statements are not yet available, the smaller reporting company reasonably and in good faith expects to report income from continuing operations attributable to the registrant before taxes [@CL-MNA-0780], and (3) for at least one of the two fiscal years immediately preceding the most recent fiscal year the smaller reporting company reported income from continuing operations attributable to the registrant before taxes [@CL-MNA-0781].

Under § 210.8-08(a), when the anticipated effective or mailing date falls within 45 days after the end of the fiscal year, the filing may include financial statements only as current as of the end of the third fiscal quarter, provided, however, that if the audited financial statements for the recently completed fiscal year are available or become available before effectiveness or mailing, they must be included in the filing [@CL-MNA-0777; @CL-MNA-0778].

## How it works

Under § 210.8-03, interim financial statements include a balance sheet as of the end of the issuer's most recent fiscal quarter, a balance sheet as of the end of the preceding fiscal year, and statements of comprehensive income and statements of cash flows for the interim period up to the date of such balance sheet and the comparable period of the preceding fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0703; @CL-MNA-0704]. They may be unaudited, except that before filing, interim financial statements included in quarterly reports on Form 10-Q must be reviewed by an independent public accountant using applicable professional standards and procedures for conducting such reviews, as may be modified or supplemented by the Commission, and if in any filing the issuer states that interim financial statements have been reviewed by an independent public accountant, a report of the accountant on the review must be filed with the interim financial statements [@CL-MNA-0705; @CL-MNA-1586; @CL-MNA-0139]. Under § 210.8-08, if required financial statements are as of a date 135 days or more before the date a registration statement becomes effective or proxy material is expected to be mailed, they must be updated to include financial statements for an interim period ending within 135 days of the effective or expected mailing date [@CL-MNA-0706; @CL-MNA-1587]. Under paragraph (a), when the anticipated effective or mailing date falls within 45 days after the end of the fiscal year, the filing may include financial statements only as current as of the end of the third fiscal quarter unless the audited financial statements for the recently completed fiscal year are available or become available before effectiveness or mailing, in which case they must be included in the filing [@CL-MNA-0777; @CL-MNA-0778]. Under paragraph (b), if the effective date or anticipated mailing date falls after 45 days but within 90 days of the end of the smaller reporting company's fiscal year, the smaller reporting company is not required to provide the audited financial statements for such year end provided that all three conditions in paragraphs (b)(1) through (3) are met [@CL-MNA-0707; @CL-MNA-0779; @CL-MNA-0780; @CL-MNA-0781].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A smaller reporting company expects its registration statement to become effective 150 days after its last balance sheet date. That is more than 135 days, so it adds interim statements ending within 135 days of effectiveness. The interim package has balance sheets at the latest quarter end and the prior year end, plus income and cash flow statements for the interim period and the matching prior-year period. The statements can be unaudited, but a quarterly report would need an accountant review.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.8-03, interim financial statements may be unaudited, except that before filing, interim financial statements included in quarterly reports on Form 10-Q must be reviewed by an independent public accountant using applicable professional standards and procedures for conducting such reviews, as may be modified or supplemented by the Commission, and if in any filing the issuer states that interim financial statements have been reviewed by an independent public accountant, a report of the accountant on the review must be filed with the interim financial statements [@CL-MNA-0705; @CL-MNA-1586; @CL-MNA-0139]. Under § 210.8-08, if required financial statements are as of a date 135 days or more before the date a registration statement becomes effective or proxy material is expected to be mailed, they must be updated to include financial statements for an interim period ending within 135 days of the effective or expected mailing date, whereas under paragraph (b), if the effective date or anticipated mailing date falls after 45 days but within 90 days of the end of the smaller reporting company's fiscal year, the smaller reporting company is not required to provide the audited financial statements for such year end provided that the conditions in paragraphs (b)(1) through (3) are met [@CL-MNA-0706; @CL-MNA-1587; @CL-MNA-0707; @CL-MNA-0779; @CL-MNA-0780; @CL-MNA-0781].
