## Purpose

Shows the defined terms that Regulation M-A uses and what the summary term sheet in a Regulation M-A disclosure document must contain.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Terms used throughout Regulation M-A and the summary term sheet given to security holders [@CL-MNA-0250; @CL-MNA-0259] |

Unless specified otherwise, the definitions in § 229.1000 apply to the terms used in Regulation M-A (§§ 229.1000 through 229.1016) [@CL-MNA-0250]. Associate has the same meaning as in § 240.12b-2 of the same chapter, issuer tender offer has the same meaning as in § 240.13e-4(a)(2) of the same chapter, and Rule 13e-3 transaction has the same meaning as in § 240.13e-3(a)(3) of the same chapter [@CL-MNA-0250; @CL-MNA-0253; @CL-MNA-0255].

### Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Associate | The same meaning as in § 240.12b-2 of the same chapter [@CL-MNA-0250] |
| Instruction C | General Instruction C to Schedule 13E-3 (§ 240.13e-100) and General Instruction C to Schedule TO (§ 240.14d-100), each section being in the same chapter [@CL-MNA-0252] |
| Issuer tender offer | The same meaning as in § 240.13e-4(a)(2) of the same chapter [@CL-MNA-0253] |
| Offeror | Any person who makes a tender offer or on whose behalf a tender offer is made [@CL-MNA-0254] |
| Rule 13e-3 transaction | The same meaning as in § 240.13e-3(a)(3) of the same chapter [@CL-MNA-0255] |
| Subject company | The company or entity whose securities are sought to be acquired in the transaction (for example, the target), or that is otherwise the subject of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0256] |
| Subject securities | The securities or class of securities that are sought to be acquired in the transaction or that are otherwise the subject of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0257] |
| Third-party tender offer | A tender offer that is not an issuer tender offer [@CL-MNA-0258] |

Section 229.1001 requires providing security holders with a summary term sheet that is written in plain English, and the summary term sheet must briefly describe in bullet point format the most material terms of the proposed transaction [@CL-MNA-0259]. The summary term sheet must provide security holders with sufficient information to understand the essential features and significance of the proposed transaction [@CL-MNA-0260]. The bullet points must cross-reference a more detailed discussion contained in the disclosure document that is disseminated to security holders [@CL-MNA-0261].

Under the instructions to Item 1001, the summary term sheet must not recite all information contained in the disclosure document that will be provided to security holders, and it is intended to serve as an overview of all material matters that are presented in the accompanying documents provided to security holders [@CL-MNA-0262]. The summary term sheet must begin on the first or second page of the disclosure document provided to security holders, and Instruction 3 refers to Rule 421(b) and (d) of Regulation C under the Securities Act (§ 230.421(b) and (d) of the same chapter) for a description of plain English disclosure [@CL-MNA-0263].

## How it works

Under § 229.1000, Instruction C means General Instruction C to Schedule 13E-3 and General Instruction C to Schedule TO, associate has the same meaning as in § 240.12b-2, issuer tender offer has the same meaning as in § 240.13e-4(a)(2), Rule 13e-3 transaction has the same meaning as in § 240.13e-3(a)(3), and third-party tender offer means a tender offer that is not an issuer tender offer [@CL-MNA-0250; @CL-MNA-0252; @CL-MNA-0253; @CL-MNA-0255; @CL-MNA-0258]. Subject company means the company or entity whose securities are sought to be acquired in the transaction (for example, the target), or that is otherwise the subject of the transaction, subject securities means the securities or class of securities that are sought to be acquired in the transaction or that are otherwise the subject of the transaction, and offeror means any person who makes a tender offer or on whose behalf a tender offer is made [@CL-MNA-0254; @CL-MNA-0256; @CL-MNA-0257]. Under § 229.1001, the summary term sheet serves as an overview of all material matters presented in the accompanying documents provided to security holders and uses bullet points that cross-reference a more detailed discussion contained in the disclosure document disseminated to security holders [@CL-MNA-0259; @CL-MNA-0261; @CL-MNA-0262]. The other Regulation M-A cards, such as [Terms of the transaction](mna-diligence-terms-of-the-transaction.md) and [Subject company information and filing person background](mna-diligence-subject-company-and-filing-person-background.md), use these terms.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder offers 50 per share in cash for all the shares of a listed target. The target is the subject company, its shares are the subject securities, and the bidder is the offeror because the offer is made by it [@CL-MNA-0254; @CL-MNA-0256; @CL-MNA-0257]. Assume the offer is not an issuer tender offer under the cross-referenced rule. It is then a third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-0253; @CL-MNA-0258]. The disclosure document begins its summary term sheet on its first page, which satisfies the rule that the sheet begin on the first or second page [@CL-MNA-0263]. The sheet states the price, the expiry date and the main conditions in bullet points, each pointing to the fuller discussion later in the document [@CL-MNA-0259; @CL-MNA-0261]. It does not repeat the whole document [@CL-MNA-0262].

## Never confuse

Under § 229.1000, an issuer tender offer has the same meaning as in § 240.13e-4(a)(2) of the same chapter, whereas a third-party tender offer means a tender offer that is not an issuer tender offer [@CL-MNA-0253; @CL-MNA-0258]. The subject company is the company or entity whose securities are sought to be acquired in the transaction (for example, the target), or that is otherwise the subject of the transaction, whereas the offeror is any person who makes a tender offer or on whose behalf a tender offer is made [@CL-MNA-0254; @CL-MNA-0256]. Under Instruction 1 to Item 1001, the summary term sheet must not recite all information contained in the disclosure document that will be provided to security holders and is intended to serve as an overview of all material matters presented in the accompanying documents provided to security holders [@CL-MNA-0262].

## Company calibration

The company confirms which of its filings use these definitions and whether each summary term sheet meets the plain English, bullet point, placement and cross-reference rules. [@Q-028]
