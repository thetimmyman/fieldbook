## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must report about predecessors when it is the successor to a company that held a Federal contract or grant.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-20 [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| Applies to | Offerors, who represent whether they are a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0388] |

In the provision, the offeror represents whether or not it is a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0388]. An offeror that indicates it is a successor enters the following information for all predecessors that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years, listing them in reverse chronological order if there is more than one [@CL-MNA-0389]. The information includes the predecessor CAGE code, which the offeror may mark Unknown [@CL-MNA-0458], and the predecessor's legal name, never a doing-business-as name [@CL-MNA-0390].

## How it works

A predecessor is an entity that is replaced by a successor, and the term includes any predecessors of the predecessor [@CL-MNA-0385]. A successor is an entity that has replaced a predecessor by acquiring its assets and carrying out its affairs under a new name [@CL-MNA-0386]. The term does not include new offices or divisions of the same company or a company that only changes its name [@CL-MNA-0387].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buyer acquired the assets and operations of a target and now operates them under a new name. The target held a Federal contract two years ago. The buyer indicates that it is a successor, enters the target's CAGE code, or marks it Unknown, and gives the target's legal name. A second company only changed its own name, so it indicates that it is not a successor.

## Never confuse

The provision asks who the offeror replaced; it is a representation in an offer, not a novation. A novation agreement is the Government's recognition of a successor in interest to a contractor's contracts [@CL-MNA-0354]; see [Novation and change-of-name agreements](mna-novation-agreements.md). A company that only changes its name is not a successor here [@CL-MNA-0387].
