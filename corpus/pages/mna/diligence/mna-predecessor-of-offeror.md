## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must report about predecessors when it is the successor to a company that held a Federal contract or grant.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-20 [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent section: the RFO text prints FAR 52.204-20 as reserved, and the predecessor information appears in RFO FAR 52.204-7 and RFO FAR 52.204-90 [@CL-MNA-0001; @CL-MNA-0005; @CL-MNA-0008] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| Applies to | Offerors, who represent whether they are a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0388] |

In the provision, the offeror represents whether or not it is a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0388]. An offeror that indicates it is a successor enters the following information for all predecessors that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years, listing them in reverse chronological order if there is more than one [@CL-MNA-0389]. The information includes the predecessor CAGE code, which the offeror may mark Unknown [@CL-MNA-0458], and the predecessor's legal name, never a doing-business-as name [@CL-MNA-0390].

## How it works

A predecessor is an entity that is replaced by a successor, and the term includes any predecessors of the predecessor [@CL-MNA-0385]. A successor is an entity that has replaced a predecessor by acquiring its assets and carrying out its affairs under a new name [@CL-MNA-0386]. The term does not include new offices or divisions of the same company or a company that only changes its name [@CL-MNA-0387].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.204-20 as reserved [@CL-MNA-0001]. RFO FAR 52.204-7 defines a predecessor as an entity whose assets were acquired by the offeror or another entity, most often through merger or acquisition, and whose affairs are now carried out by the offeror or the other entity under a new name, which words the definition differently from the codified provision [@CL-MNA-0002] [@CL-MNA-0385]. In RFO FAR 52.204-7, the Offeror shall have an active registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) when submitting an offer or quotation and at the time of award [@CL-MNA-0003], and shall provide a CAGE code and legal business name, without using a doing business as name, for itself, its immediate owner(s), if any, and its highest-level owner, if any [@CL-MNA-0004], and for any predecessor, or predecessor of the offeror's predecessor, that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0005] [@CL-MNA-0389] [@CL-MNA-0390]. RFO FAR 52.204-90 says an Offeror that will not have an active SAM registration when submitting its offer shall complete paragraphs (c) and (d) of that provision and include its responses with its offer [@CL-MNA-0006]. In its paragraph (d), the Offeror provides the CAGE code and legal business name, without using a doing business as name, for its immediate owner(s), if any, its highest-level owner, if any, and any predecessor, or predecessor of the offeror's predecessor, that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0007] [@CL-MNA-0008]. RFO FAR 52.204-90 also repeats the same definition of a predecessor [@CL-MNA-0011]. Its table note lets the predecessor CAGE code be marked Unknown, as the codified provision does [@CL-MNA-0009] [@CL-MNA-0458], and it says that an offeror with more than one predecessor provides information for each in reverse chronological order, as the codified provision does [@CL-MNA-0010] [@CL-MNA-0389].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buyer acquired the assets and operations of a target and now operates them under a new name. The target held a Federal contract two years ago. The buyer indicates that it is a successor, enters the target's CAGE code, or marks it Unknown, and gives the target's legal name. A second company only changed its own name, so it indicates that it is not a successor. Under the RFO text, the buyer instead provides the target's CAGE code and legal business name under paragraph (b)(3)(i)(D) of RFO FAR 52.204-7 or paragraph (d)(2)(iii) of RFO FAR 52.204-90, as a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0005] [@CL-MNA-0008].

## Never confuse

The provision asks who the offeror replaced; it is a representation in an offer, not a novation. A novation agreement is the Government's recognition of a successor in interest to a contractor's contracts [@CL-MNA-0354]; see [Novation and change-of-name agreements](mna-novation-agreements.md). A company that only changes its name is not a successor here [@CL-MNA-0387]. The codified provision has the offeror represent whether it is a successor [@CL-MNA-0388], while the RFO text prints FAR 52.204-20 as reserved [@CL-MNA-0001] and gives the predecessor information under RFO FAR 52.204-7 and RFO FAR 52.204-90 [@CL-MNA-0005] [@CL-MNA-0008].
