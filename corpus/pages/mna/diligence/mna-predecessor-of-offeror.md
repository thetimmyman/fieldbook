## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must report about predecessors when it is the successor to a company that held a Federal contract or grant.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-20 [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.204-20 is reserved, and predecessor information appears in RFO FAR 52.204-7 and RFO FAR 52.204-90 [@CL-MNA-0001; @CL-MNA-0005; @CL-MNA-0008] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0388] |
| Applies to | Offerors, who represent whether they are a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0388] |

In the provision, the offeror represents whether it is or is not a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0388]. If the offeror has indicated that it is a successor in paragraph (b) of the provision, it must enter the following information for all predecessors that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years (if more than one predecessor, list in reverse chronological order) [@CL-MNA-0389]. The information includes the predecessor CAGE code (or mark "Unknown") [@CL-MNA-0458] and the predecessor legal name (do not use a "doing business as" name) [@CL-MNA-0390].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.204-20(a), a predecessor means an entity that is replaced by a successor and includes any predecessors of the predecessor [@CL-MNA-0385]. A successor means an entity that has replaced a predecessor by acquiring the assets and carrying out the affairs of the predecessor under a new name (often through acquisition or merger) [@CL-MNA-0386]. The term "successor" does not include new offices or divisions of the same company or a company that only changes its name [@CL-MNA-0387]. The extent of the responsibility of the successor for the liabilities of the predecessor may vary, depending on State law and specific circumstances [@CL-MNA-0116].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR Part 52 in place of FAR part 52 under Class Deviation 2026-O0038 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.204-20 is marked reserved [@CL-MNA-0001]. RFO FAR 52.204-7(a) defines a predecessor as an entity whose assets were acquired by the offeror or another entity (most often through merger or acquisition) and whose affairs are now carried out by the offeror or the other entity under a new name [@CL-MNA-0002]. In RFO FAR 52.204-7, the Offeror must have an active Federal Government contracts registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) when submitting an offer or quotation in response to the solicitation and at the time of award [@CL-MNA-0003], and must provide a CAGE code and legal business name (do not use a "doing business as" name) for itself, its immediate owner(s), if any, and its highest-level owner, if any [@CL-MNA-0004], and for any predecessor(s), or predecessor of an Offeror's predecessor, that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0005]. RFO FAR 52.204-90 says that if the Offeror will not have an active Federal Government contracts registration in SAM when submitting its offer, it must complete paragraphs (c) and (d) of that provision and include its responses with its offer [@CL-MNA-0006]. In paragraph (d)(2) of RFO FAR 52.204-90, the Offeror must provide the CAGE code and legal business name (do not use a "doing business as" name) for its immediate owner(s), if any, its highest-level owner, if any, and any predecessor(s), or predecessor of an Offeror's predecessor, that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0007; @CL-MNA-0008]. RFO FAR 52.204-90(a) also defines a predecessor as an entity whose assets were acquired by the offeror or another entity (most often through merger or acquisition) and whose affairs are now carried out by the offeror or the other entity under a new name [@CL-MNA-0011]. Under RFO FAR 52.204-90, the predecessor CAGE code may be marked "Unknown" [@CL-MNA-0009], and if the Offeror has more than one predecessor, it must provide information for each predecessor in reverse chronological order [@CL-MNA-0010].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buyer acquired the assets and operations of a target and now operates them under a new name. The target held a Federal contract two years ago. The buyer indicates that it is a successor, enters the target's CAGE code, or marks it Unknown, and gives the target's legal name. A second company only changed its own name, so it indicates that it is not a successor. Under the RFO text, the buyer instead provides the target's CAGE code and legal business name under paragraph (b)(3)(i)(D) of RFO FAR 52.204-7 or paragraph (d)(2)(iii) of RFO FAR 52.204-90, as a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0005] [@CL-MNA-0008].

## Never confuse

Under FAR 2.101, a novation agreement is a legal instrument executed by the contractor (transferor), the successor in interest (transferee) and the Government, by which, among other things, the transferor guarantees performance of the contract, the transferee assumes all obligations under the contract, and the Government recognizes the transfer of the contract and related assets [@CL-MNA-1409; @CL-MNA-1410]; see [Novation and change-of-name agreements](mna-novation-agreements.md). Under FAR 52.204-20(a), the term "successor" does not include new offices or divisions of the same company or a company that only changes its name [@CL-MNA-0387]. Under FAR 52.204-20(b), the Offeror represents whether it is or is not a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0388], whereas RFO FAR 52.204-20 is reserved [@CL-MNA-0001] and RFO FAR 52.204-7 and RFO FAR 52.204-90 require the Offeror to provide the CAGE code and legal business name for any predecessor(s), or predecessor of an Offeror's predecessor, that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-MNA-0004; @CL-MNA-0005; @CL-MNA-0007; @CL-MNA-0008].
