## Purpose

Says when schedules are filed with financial statements under the schedules section, and when condensed parent-only information is needed. Separate subsidiary statements in group reporting are on [Group reporting notes and separate subsidiary statements under Regulation S-X](../../close-revrec/group-bridge/cls-sx-group-reporting-notes.md).

| Applies to | Schedules filed with financial statements, except as the applicable form expressly provides otherwise [@CL-MNA-0907] |

Under § 210.5-04(a), except as expressly provided otherwise in the applicable form: (1) the schedules specified in the section as Schedules II and III shall be filed as of the date of the most recent audited balance sheet for each person or group [@CL-MNA-0907], and (2) Schedule II of the section shall be filed for each period for which an audited statement of comprehensive income is required to be filed for each person or group [@CL-CLS-0408]. Under § 210.5-04(b), if the information required by any schedule (including the notes thereto) may be shown in the related financial statement or in a note thereto without making such statement unclear or confusing, that procedure may be followed and the schedule omitted [@CL-MNA-0957]. Under § 210.5-04(c), the schedules shall be examined by the independent accountant if the related financial statements are so examined [@CL-CLS-0410].

Under § 210.5-04(c) Schedule I, the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-04 (condensed financial information of registrant) shall be filed when the restricted net assets (§ 210.1-02(dd)) of consolidated subsidiaries exceed 25 percent of consolidated net assets as of the end of the most recently completed fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0908]. Under § 210.12-04(a), the registrant must provide condensed financial information as to financial position, cash flows and results of operations of the registrant as of the same dates and for the same periods for which audited consolidated financial statements are required [@CL-MNA-0909]. Under § 210.12-04(b), the registrant must disclose separately the amounts of cash dividends paid to the registrant for each of the last three fiscal years by consolidated subsidiaries, unconsolidated subsidiaries and 50 percent or less owned persons accounted for by the equity method, respectively [@CL-MNA-0956].

## How it works

Under § 210.5-04(c) Schedule I and § 210.12-04(a) and (b), when the restricted net assets (§ 210.1-02(dd)) of consolidated subsidiaries exceed 25 percent of consolidated net assets as of the end of the most recently completed fiscal year, the registrant files the schedule prescribed by § 210.12-04, providing condensed financial information as to financial position, cash flows and results of operations of the registrant as of the same dates and for the same periods for which audited consolidated financial statements are required and disclosing separately the amounts of cash dividends paid to the registrant for each of the last three fiscal years by consolidated subsidiaries, unconsolidated subsidiaries and 50 percent or less owned persons accounted for by the equity method, respectively [@CL-MNA-0908; @CL-MNA-0909; @CL-MNA-0956]. Under § 210.5-04(b) and (c), if the information required by any schedule (including the notes thereto) may be shown in the related financial statement or in a note thereto without making such statement unclear or confusing, that procedure may be followed and the schedule omitted, and the schedules must be examined by the independent accountant if the related financial statements are so examined [@CL-MNA-0957; @CL-CLS-0410].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant has consolidated net assets of 1,000 at year end. Restricted net assets of its consolidated subsidiaries are 300, which is 30 percent of that amount. The line is exceeded, so it files condensed information of the registrant alone for the same dates and periods as its audited consolidated statements. Had restricted net assets been 200, which is 20 percent, the schedule would not have been triggered by this test.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.5-04(c) Schedule I and § 210.12-04(a), Schedule I provides condensed financial information as to financial position, cash flows and results of operations of the registrant as of the same dates and for the same periods for which audited consolidated financial statements are required when the restricted net assets of consolidated subsidiaries exceed 25 percent of consolidated net assets as of the end of the most recently completed fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0908; @CL-MNA-0909], whereas separate financial statements of unconsolidated subsidiaries and 50 percent or less owned persons are covered on [Group reporting notes and separate subsidiary statements under Regulation S-X](../../close-revrec/group-bridge/cls-sx-group-reporting-notes.md).
