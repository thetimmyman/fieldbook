## Purpose

Says when schedules are filed with financial statements under the schedules section, and when condensed parent-only information is needed. The rules are part of Regulation S-X, which is not FAR, DFARS or CAS. Separate subsidiary statements in group reporting are on [Group reporting notes and separate subsidiary statements under Regulation S-X](../../close-revrec/group-bridge/cls-sx-group-reporting-notes.md).

| Applies to | Schedules filed with financial statements, except as the applicable form expressly provides otherwise [@CL-MNA-0907] |

Except as expressly provided otherwise in the applicable form, the schedules specified as Schedules II and III shall be filed as of the date of the most recent audited balance sheet for each person or group [@CL-MNA-0907]. A schedule may be omitted if the information it requires, including the notes, may be shown in the related financial statement or in a note to it without making the statement unclear or confusing [@CL-MNA-0957].

The condensed financial information of the registrant, under the section for Schedule I, shall be filed when the restricted net assets of consolidated subsidiaries exceed 25 percent of consolidated net assets as of the end of the most recently completed fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0908]. That information is condensed financial information as to financial position, cash flows and results of operations of the registrant, provided as of the same dates and for the same periods for which audited consolidated financial statements are required [@CL-MNA-0909]. The registrant must also disclose separately the amounts of cash dividends paid to it for each of the last three fiscal years by consolidated subsidiaries, unconsolidated subsidiaries and 50 percent or less owned persons accounted for by the equity method, respectively [@CL-MNA-0956]. The section also describes further schedules and their conditions, and the parent-only section has further content rules, which this card does not draw out.

## How it works

The registrant compares the restricted net assets of its consolidated subsidiaries with consolidated net assets at the end of the most recently completed fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0908]. If the 25 percent line is exceeded, it files parent-only condensed information for the same dates and periods as its audited consolidated statements [@CL-MNA-0908; @CL-MNA-0909], and it separately discloses the cash dividends paid to it by the three groups the section lists [@CL-MNA-0956].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant has consolidated net assets of 1,000 at year end. Restricted net assets of its consolidated subsidiaries are 300, which is 30 percent of that amount. The line is exceeded, so it files condensed information of the registrant alone for the same dates and periods as its audited consolidated statements. Had restricted net assets been 200, which is 20 percent, the schedule would not have been triggered by this test.

## Never confuse

Parent-only condensed information is not the consolidated financial statements. It presents the registrant on its own as to financial position, cash flows and results of operations [@CL-MNA-0909]. It is also not the separate statements of subsidiaries, which are on the linked group reporting card.
