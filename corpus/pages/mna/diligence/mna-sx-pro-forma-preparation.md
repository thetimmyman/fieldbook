## Purpose

Shows what pro forma financial information contains, which adjustments it may include and which periods it covers. It does not say when the information is required; that is on [Pro forma financial information: when it is required](mna-sx-pro-forma-presentation.md). The rule is part of Regulation S-X, which is not FAR, DFARS or CAS.

## The rule

| Applies to | Pro forma financial information, which must consist of the parts the rule lists [@CL-MNA-0689] |

Pro forma financial information must consist of a pro forma condensed balance sheet, pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income and accompanying explanatory notes [@CL-MNA-0689]. Where a limited number of adjustments are required and they are easily understood, a narrative description of the pro forma effects may be disclosed in place of the condensed statements [@CL-MNA-0690].

The pro forma condensed balance sheet and condensed statements of comprehensive income must include, and be limited to, the listed adjustments, except as noted in the later paragraph on Management's Adjustments [@CL-MNA-0691; @CL-MNA-0693]. The first listed adjustment is Transaction Accounting Adjustments [@CL-MNA-0691]. Another is Autonomous Entity Adjustments, which depict the registrant as an autonomous entity if the stated condition is met [@CL-MNA-0692]. Management's Adjustments for synergies and dis-synergies may be presented in the registrant's discretion [@CL-MNA-0693]. They may be presented if, in management's opinion, they would enhance an understanding of the pro forma effects and the listed conditions are met [@CL-MNA-0694]. One condition is that there is a reasonable basis for each adjustment [@CL-MNA-0775]. Another is that, when synergies are presented, any related dis-synergies must also be presented [@CL-MNA-0776].

A pro forma condensed balance sheet as of the end of the most recent period for which a consolidated balance sheet is required must be filed unless the transaction is already reflected in that balance sheet [@CL-MNA-0695]. Pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income must be filed for only the most recent fiscal year, except as noted in a later paragraph of the section, and for the period from the most recent fiscal year end to the most recent interim date for which a balance sheet is required [@CL-MNA-0696]. A pro forma condensed statement of comprehensive income must not be filed when the historical statement of comprehensive income reflects the transaction for the entire period [@CL-MNA-1584]. The exception covers transactions required to be accounted for under U.S. GAAP or, as applicable, IFRS-IASB by retrospectively revising the historical statements of comprehensive income, such as a combination of entities under common control or discontinued operations [@CL-MNA-0773]. For those transactions, pro forma statements of comprehensive income must be filed for all periods for which historical financial statements of the registrant are required [@CL-MNA-0774]. The section also sets rules for captions, per-share data, notes and tax effects that this card does not draw out.

## How it works

The registrant builds a condensed balance sheet and condensed statements of comprehensive income, with notes, unless a narrative description is allowed [@CL-MNA-0689; @CL-MNA-0690]. Required adjustments are the listed ones, led by the Transaction Accounting Adjustments [@CL-MNA-0691]. Management's Adjustments are optional and depend on management's opinion and the listed conditions [@CL-MNA-0693; @CL-MNA-0694; @CL-MNA-0775; @CL-MNA-0776]. The balance sheet is as of the latest required period end, and the income statements cover the latest year plus the period to the latest interim date, unless the transaction is one the retrospective revision exception covers [@CL-MNA-0695; @CL-MNA-0696; @CL-MNA-0773; @CL-MNA-0774].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant with a December year end files with a balance sheet as of the end of March. It buys a target in April. The pro forma balance sheet is as of the end of March, and the transaction is not one that retrospectively revises the historical statements. The pro forma income statements cover the last fiscal year and the three months to the end of March. The registrant may add a synergy adjustment only if its management's opinion and the listed conditions allow it.

## Never confuse

Transaction Accounting Adjustments and Management's Adjustments are not the same. The first are among the listed adjustments the statements must include, and the second are optional and depend on management's opinion and conditions [@CL-MNA-0691; @CL-MNA-0693; @CL-MNA-0694]. Pro forma statements are also not the target's own historical statements, which are covered by the acquired-business rule on [Financial statements of businesses acquired or to be acquired](mna-sx-acquired-business-financial-statements.md).
