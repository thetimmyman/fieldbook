## Purpose

Shows what pro forma financial information contains, which adjustments it may include and which periods it covers. When pro forma financial information is required is on [Pro forma financial information: when it is required](mna-sx-pro-forma-presentation.md).

Under § 210.11-02(a)(1), pro forma financial information must consist of a pro forma condensed balance sheet, pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income, and accompanying explanatory notes [@CL-MNA-0689]. Under paragraph (a)(2), in certain circumstances (that is, where a limited number of pro forma adjustments are required and those adjustments are easily understood), a narrative description of the pro forma effects of the transaction may be disclosed in lieu of the statements described in paragraph (a)(1) [@CL-MNA-0690].

Under § 210.11-02(a)(6), the pro forma condensed balance sheet and pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income must include, and be limited to, the following pro forma adjustments, except as noted in paragraph (a)(7) of the section: (i) Transaction Accounting Adjustments [@CL-MNA-0691] and (ii) Autonomous Entity Adjustments, which are adjustments that depict the registrant as an autonomous entity if the condition in § 210.11-01(a)(7) is met, and must be presented in a separate column from Transaction Accounting Adjustments [@CL-MNA-0692; @CL-MNA-0134]. Under paragraph (a)(7), Management's Adjustments depicting synergies and dis-synergies of the acquisitions and dispositions for which pro forma effect is being given may, in the registrant's discretion, be presented if in its management's opinion, such adjustments would enhance an understanding of the pro forma effects of the transaction and the conditions in paragraph (a)(7) are met [@CL-MNA-0693; @CL-MNA-0694]. Those conditions include under paragraph (a)(7)(i)(A) that there is a reasonable basis for each such adjustment [@CL-MNA-0775] and under paragraph (a)(7)(i)(C) that when synergies are presented, any related dis-synergies must also be presented [@CL-MNA-0776].

Under § 210.11-02(c)(1), a pro forma condensed balance sheet as of the end of the most recent period for which a consolidated balance sheet of the registrant is required by § 210.3-01 must be filed unless the transaction is already reflected in such balance sheet [@CL-MNA-0695]. Under paragraph (c)(2)(i), pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income must be filed for only the most recent fiscal year, except as noted in paragraph (c)(2)(ii) of the section, and for the period from the most recent fiscal year end to the most recent interim date for which a balance sheet is required, and a pro forma condensed statement of comprehensive income must not be filed when the historical statement of comprehensive income reflects the transaction for the entire period [@CL-MNA-0696; @CL-MNA-1584]. Under paragraph (c)(2)(ii), for transactions required to be accounted for under US GAAP or, as applicable, IFRS-IASB by retrospectively revising the historical statements of comprehensive income (for example, combination of entities under common control and discontinued operations), pro forma statements of comprehensive income must be filed for all periods for which historical financial statements of the registrant are required, and retrospective revisions stemming from the registrant's adoption of a new accounting principle must not be reflected in pro forma statements of comprehensive income until they are depicted in the registrant's historical financial statements [@CL-MNA-0773; @CL-MNA-0774; @CL-MNA-0135].

## How it works

Under § 210.11-02(a)(1) and (2), pro forma financial information consists of a pro forma condensed balance sheet, pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income, and accompanying explanatory notes, except that a narrative description of the pro forma effects of the transaction may be disclosed in lieu of the statements where a limited number of pro forma adjustments are required and those adjustments are easily understood [@CL-MNA-0689; @CL-MNA-0690]. Under paragraphs (a)(6) and (a)(7), the pro forma condensed balance sheet and pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income must include, and be limited to, Transaction Accounting Adjustments and (if the condition in § 210.11-01(a)(7) is met, in a separate column) Autonomous Entity Adjustments, except that Management's Adjustments depicting synergies and dis-synergies may, in the registrant's discretion, be presented if in management's opinion such adjustments would enhance an understanding of the pro forma effects of the transaction and the conditions in paragraph (a)(7) (including a reasonable basis for each adjustment and presenting any related dis-synergies when synergies are presented) are met [@CL-MNA-0691; @CL-MNA-0692; @CL-MNA-0134; @CL-MNA-0693; @CL-MNA-0694; @CL-MNA-0775; @CL-MNA-0776]. Under paragraph (c), the pro forma condensed balance sheet is as of the end of the most recent period for which a consolidated balance sheet of the registrant is required by § 210.3-01 (unless the transaction is already reflected in such balance sheet), and the pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income cover only the most recent fiscal year and the period from the most recent fiscal year end to the most recent interim date for which a balance sheet is required (except for transactions required to be accounted for under US GAAP or, as applicable, IFRS-IASB by retrospectively revising the historical statements of comprehensive income, for which pro forma statements of comprehensive income must be filed for all periods for which historical financial statements of the registrant are required, without reflecting retrospective revisions from adopting a new accounting principle until depicted in the registrant's historical financial statements) [@CL-MNA-0695; @CL-MNA-0696; @CL-MNA-1584; @CL-MNA-0773; @CL-MNA-0774; @CL-MNA-0135].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant with a December year end files with a balance sheet as of the end of March. It buys a target in April. The pro forma balance sheet is as of the end of March, and the transaction is not one that retrospectively revises the historical statements. The pro forma income statements cover the last fiscal year and the three months to the end of March. The registrant may add a synergy adjustment only if its management's opinion and the listed conditions allow it.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.11-02(a)(6) and (7), the pro forma condensed balance sheet and pro forma condensed statements of comprehensive income must include, and be limited to, Transaction Accounting Adjustments and Autonomous Entity Adjustments (presented in a separate column if the condition in § 210.11-01(a)(7) is met), except that Management's Adjustments depicting synergies and dis-synergies may, in the registrant's discretion, be presented if in management's opinion such adjustments would enhance an understanding of the pro forma effects of the transaction and the conditions in paragraph (a)(7) are met [@CL-MNA-0691; @CL-MNA-0692; @CL-MNA-0134; @CL-MNA-0693; @CL-MNA-0694]. Separate historical financial statements of a business acquired or to be acquired are covered on [Financial statements of businesses acquired or to be acquired](mna-sx-acquired-business-financial-statements.md).
