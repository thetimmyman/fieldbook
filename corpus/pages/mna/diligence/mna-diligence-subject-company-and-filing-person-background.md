## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A filing must say about the subject company and its securities and about the identity and background of each filing person.

### Subject company information

The item requires the name of the subject company (or of the issuer in the case of an issuer tender offer) and the address and telephone number of its principal executive offices [@CL-MNA-0264]. It requires the exact title and number of shares outstanding of the subject class of equity securities as of the most recent practicable date [@CL-MNA-0265]. That information may be based on the subject company's most recently available filing with the Commission, unless the filing person has more current information [@CL-MNA-0266].

It requires identifying the principal market in which the subject securities are traded and stating the high and low sales prices for the subject securities in that principal market (or, if there is no principal market, the range of high and low bid quotations and the source of such quotations) for each quarter during the past two years [@CL-MNA-0267; @CL-MNA-0268]. If there is no established trading market for the securities (excluding limited or sporadic quotations), the filing must state that fact [@CL-MNA-0268].

It requires stating the frequency and amount of any dividends paid during the past two years with respect to the subject securities and briefly describing any restriction on the subject company's current or future ability to pay dividends [@CL-MNA-0269]. If the filing person is not the subject company, it furnishes this information to the extent known after making reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0270].

If the filing person has made an underwritten public offering of the subject securities for cash during the past three years that was registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or exempt from registration under Regulation A, it states the date of the offering, the amount of securities offered, the offering price per share (adjusted for stock splits, stock dividends and the like as appropriate) and the aggregate proceeds received by the filing person [@CL-MNA-0271; @CL-MNA-0272]. If the filing person purchased any subject securities during the past two years, it states the amount of securities purchased, the range of prices paid and the average purchase price for each quarter during that period [@CL-MNA-0273]. Affiliates need not give information for purchases made before becoming an affiliate [@CL-MNA-0274].

### Identity and background of the filing person

The item requires stating the name, business address and business telephone number of each filing person, and also stating the name and address of each person specified in Instruction C to the schedule (except for Schedule 14D-9) [@CL-MNA-0275]. If the filing person is an affiliate of the subject company, it states the nature of the affiliation, and if the filing person is the subject company, it states that fact [@CL-MNA-0276].

If any filing person (other than the subject company) or any person specified in Instruction C to the schedule is not a natural person, the filing must state the person's principal business, state the person's state or other place of organization, and provide the conviction and proceeding information required by paragraphs (c)(3) and (c)(4) of the section for each such person [@CL-MNA-0277; @CL-MNA-0278; @CL-MNA-0281; @CL-MNA-0282; @CL-MNA-0283; @CL-MNA-0284; @CL-MNA-0285].

If any filing person or any person specified in Instruction C to the schedule is a natural person, the filing must provide the person's current principal occupation or employment and the name, principal business and address of any corporation or other organization in which the employment or occupation is conducted [@CL-MNA-0278; @CL-MNA-0279]. It must also provide the person's material occupations, positions, offices or employment during the past five years, giving the starting and ending dates of each and the name, principal business and address of any corporation or other organization in which the occupation, position, office or employment was carried on [@CL-MNA-0280]. It must provide a statement whether or not the person was convicted in a criminal proceeding during the past five years (excluding traffic violations or similar misdemeanors) [@CL-MNA-0281]. If the person was convicted, it describes the criminal proceeding, including the dates, nature of conviction, name and location of court, and penalty imposed or other disposition of the case [@CL-MNA-0282].

It must also provide a statement whether or not the person was a party to any judicial or administrative proceeding during the past five years (except for matters that were dismissed without sanction or settlement) that resulted in a judgment, decree or final order enjoining the person from future violations of, or prohibiting activities subject to, federal or state securities laws, or a finding of any violation of federal or state securities laws [@CL-MNA-0283; @CL-MNA-0284]. It must describe the proceeding, including a summary of the terms of the judgment, decree or final order, and state the person's country of citizenship [@CL-MNA-0285].

For a tender offer, the filing identifies the tender offer and the class of securities to which the offer relates and states the name of the offeror and its address (which may be based on the offeror's Schedule TO filed with the Commission) [@CL-MNA-0286]. Under the instruction to Item 1003, if the filing person is making information relating to the transaction available on the Internet, the filing must state the address where the information can be found [@CL-MNA-1119].

## How it works

Paragraphs (e) and (f) of § 229.1002 apply to underwritten public offerings for cash during the past three years and purchases of subject securities during the past two years by the filing person, while paragraph (d) requires stating the frequency and amount of any dividends paid during the past two years with respect to the subject securities and briefly describing any restriction on the subject company's current or future ability to pay dividends [@CL-MNA-0269; @CL-MNA-0271; @CL-MNA-0273]. Under paragraph (b), the exact title and number of shares outstanding of the subject class of equity securities as of the most recent practicable date may be based upon information in the most recently available filing with the Commission by the subject company unless the filing person has more current information [@CL-MNA-0265; @CL-MNA-0266]. Under paragraph (c), the filing states the high and low sales prices for the subject securities in the principal market (or, if there is no principal market, the range of high and low bid quotations and the source of such quotations) for each quarter during the past two years, or states that there is no established trading market for the securities (excluding limited or sporadic quotations) [@CL-MNA-0267; @CL-MNA-0268]. Under § 229.1003(b) and (c), a filing person (other than the subject company) or Instruction C person that is not a natural person states its principal business, its state or other place of organization, and the conviction and proceeding information required by paragraphs (c)(3) and (c)(4), while a filing person or Instruction C person that is a natural person provides current occupation, five-year employment history, criminal convictions, securities-law proceedings and country of citizenship [@CL-MNA-0277; @CL-MNA-0278; @CL-MNA-0279; @CL-MNA-0280; @CL-MNA-0281; @CL-MNA-0283; @CL-MNA-0285].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder files for a tender offer for a target. Assume the principal market for the target's common stock is one exchange [@CL-MNA-0267]. The filing gives that exchange's high and low sales prices for each of the eight quarters in the past two years [@CL-MNA-0268]. The target paid 0.10 per share each quarter. The filing states that frequency and amount and any restriction on paying dividends [@CL-MNA-0269]. The bidder is not the subject company, so it states the dividend information to the extent known after reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0270]. The bidder is a company, so it gives its business address, business telephone number, principal business and place of organization, and the conviction and proceeding information that the natural-person paragraph calls for [@CL-MNA-0275; @CL-MNA-0277; @CL-MNA-0278].

Assume an individual director of the bidder is a person specified in Instruction C and the filing is not on Schedule 14D-9 [@CL-MNA-0275]. The filing gives her name and address, her current occupation and her positions over the past five years [@CL-MNA-0275; @CL-MNA-0279; @CL-MNA-0280]. A speeding fine she paid last year is a traffic violation and is excluded from the conviction statement [@CL-MNA-0281]. A securities case against her three years ago was dismissed without sanction or settlement, so it falls within the exception and is left out of the proceeding statement [@CL-MNA-0283].

## Never confuse

Under § 229.1002(d) and § 229.1003(a), if the filing person is the subject company it states that fact, and if the filing person is not the subject company it furnishes the dividend information to the extent known after making reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0270; @CL-MNA-0276]. Under § 229.1003(b) and (c), a filing person (other than the subject company) or Instruction C person that is not a natural person states its principal business, its state or other place of organization, and the information required by paragraphs (c)(3) and (c)(4), whereas a filing person or Instruction C person that is a natural person provides the occupation, five-year employment history, conviction, proceeding and citizenship information in paragraphs (c)(1) through (c)(5) [@CL-MNA-0277; @CL-MNA-0278; @CL-MNA-0279; @CL-MNA-0280; @CL-MNA-0281; @CL-MNA-0283; @CL-MNA-0285]. Under § 229.1002(c) through (f), market prices or bid quotations, dividends, and prior stock purchases use a past-two-years period, whereas underwritten public offerings for cash use a past-three-years period [@CL-MNA-0268; @CL-MNA-0269; @CL-MNA-0271; @CL-MNA-0273]. The defined terms are on the [definitions card](mna-diligence-regulation-m-a-definitions-and-term-sheet.md).

## Company calibration

The company confirms which entity is the subject company, which persons must be named, and whether its price and dividend look-back data is complete, within the diligence scope it has authorized. [@Q-028]
