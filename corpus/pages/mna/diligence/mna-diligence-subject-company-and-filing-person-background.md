## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A filing must say about the subject company and its securities and about the identity and background of each filing person.

### Subject company information

The item requires the name of the subject company (or of the issuer in the case of an issuer tender offer) and the address and telephone number of its principal executive offices [@CL-MNA-0264]. It requires the exact title and number of shares outstanding of the subject class of equity securities as of the most recent practicable date [@CL-MNA-0265]. That information may be based on the subject company's most recently available filing with the Commission, unless the filing person has more current information [@CL-MNA-0266].

It requires the principal market in which the subject securities are traded and the high and low sales prices there [@CL-MNA-0267]. Where there is no principal market, it calls for the range of high and low bid quotations and their source instead, and the prices or quotations are given for each quarter during the past two years [@CL-MNA-0268]. If there is no established trading market (limited or sporadic quotations aside), the filing must say so [@CL-MNA-0268].

It requires the frequency and amount of any dividends paid during the past two years and a brief description of any restriction on the subject company's current or future ability to pay dividends [@CL-MNA-0269]. If the filing person is not the subject company, it furnishes that dividend information to the extent known after making reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0270].

If the filing person has made an underwritten public offering of the subject securities for cash during the past three years that was registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or exempt from registration under Regulation A, it states the date, the amount of securities offered, the offering price per share (adjusted for stock splits, stock dividends and the like as appropriate) and the aggregate proceeds received by the filing person [@CL-MNA-0271; @CL-MNA-0272]. If the filing person purchased any subject securities during the past two years, it states the amount purchased, the range of prices paid and the average purchase price for each quarter during that period [@CL-MNA-0273]. The prior stock purchases paragraph adds that affiliates need not give information for purchases made before becoming an affiliate [@CL-MNA-0274].

### Identity and background of the filing person

The item requires the name, business address and business telephone number of each filing person, and also the name and address of each person specified in Instruction C to the schedule; the second requirement excepts Schedule 14D-9 [@CL-MNA-0275]. A filing person that is an affiliate of the subject company states the nature of the affiliation, and a filing person that is the subject company says so [@CL-MNA-0276].

For a filing person (other than the subject company) or a person specified in Instruction C that is not a natural person, the filing states the principal business and the state or other place of organization [@CL-MNA-0277]. It also gives, for each such person, the conviction and proceeding information that the natural-person paragraph calls for [@CL-MNA-0278; @CL-MNA-0281; @CL-MNA-0283].

For each filing person or Instruction C person that is a natural person, the filing gives the current principal occupation or employment, with the name, principal business and address of the organization [@CL-MNA-0278; @CL-MNA-0279]. It gives the material occupations, positions, offices or employment during the past five years, with starting and ending dates and the same organization details [@CL-MNA-0280]. It states whether or not the person was convicted in a criminal proceeding during the past five years, excluding traffic violations or similar misdemeanors [@CL-MNA-0281]. If the person was convicted, it describes the proceeding, with the dates, nature of conviction, name and location of court, and penalty or other disposition [@CL-MNA-0282].

It also states whether or not the person was a party to any judicial or administrative proceeding during the past five years, except for matters dismissed without sanction or settlement, that resulted in a judgment, decree or final order enjoining future violations of, or prohibiting activities subject to, federal or state securities laws, or a finding of any violation of those laws [@CL-MNA-0283; @CL-MNA-0284]. It describes that proceeding, including a summary of the terms of the judgment, decree or final order, and gives the country of citizenship [@CL-MNA-0285].

For a tender offer, the filing identifies the tender offer and the class of securities to which it relates and gives the name of the offeror and its address (which may be based on the offeror's Schedule TO filed with the Commission) [@CL-MNA-0286]. If the filing person is making information relating to the transaction available on the Internet, the instruction to the item requires the filing to state the address where the information can be found [@CL-MNA-1119].

## How it works

The offering and purchase paragraphs apply to what the filing person itself did, while the dividend paragraph applies to dividends paid on the subject securities [@CL-MNA-0269; @CL-MNA-0271; @CL-MNA-0273]. The filing person may use the subject company's most recent Commission filing for the share count unless it has more current information [@CL-MNA-0266]. Price information follows the market: sales prices where there is a principal market, bid quotations and their source where there is none, and a statement when there is no established trading market [@CL-MNA-0267; @CL-MNA-0268]. Entities state principal business and place of organization, while natural persons give occupation, a five-year history, convictions, proceedings and citizenship [@CL-MNA-0277; @CL-MNA-0279; @CL-MNA-0280; @CL-MNA-0281; @CL-MNA-0285].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder files for a tender offer for a target. Assume the principal market for the target's common stock is one exchange [@CL-MNA-0267]. The filing gives that exchange's high and low sales prices for each of the eight quarters in the past two years [@CL-MNA-0268]. The target paid 0.10 per share each quarter. The filing states that frequency and amount and any restriction on paying dividends [@CL-MNA-0269]. The bidder is not the subject company, so it states the dividend information to the extent known after reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0270]. The bidder is a company, so it gives its business address, business telephone number, principal business and place of organization, and the conviction and proceeding information that the natural-person paragraph calls for [@CL-MNA-0275; @CL-MNA-0277; @CL-MNA-0278].

Assume an individual director of the bidder is a person specified in Instruction C and the filing is not on Schedule 14D-9 [@CL-MNA-0275]. The filing gives her name and address, her current occupation and her positions over the past five years [@CL-MNA-0275; @CL-MNA-0279; @CL-MNA-0280]. A speeding fine she paid last year is a traffic violation and is excluded from the conviction statement [@CL-MNA-0281]. A securities case against her three years ago was dismissed without sanction or settlement, so it falls within the exception and is left out of the proceeding statement [@CL-MNA-0283].

## Never confuse

The subject company is not always the filing person: where they differ, the filing person furnishes dividend information to the extent known after reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0270; @CL-MNA-0276]. An entity's background is not an individual's: an entity states principal business and place of organization, while a natural person gives occupation, positions, convictions, proceedings and citizenship [@CL-MNA-0277; @CL-MNA-0279; @CL-MNA-0280; @CL-MNA-0281; @CL-MNA-0285]. A filing person's own offerings and purchases have their own paragraphs and look-backs, separate from the market price and dividend paragraphs [@CL-MNA-0268; @CL-MNA-0271; @CL-MNA-0273]. The defined terms are on the [definitions card](mna-diligence-regulation-m-a-definitions-and-term-sheet.md).

## Company calibration

The company confirms which entity is the subject company, which persons must be named, and whether its price and dividend look-back data is complete, within the diligence scope it has authorized. [@Q-028]
