## Purpose

Shows when a registrant must file separate financial statements of a business it has acquired or is about to acquire, how the significance conditions set the number of periods, and when the separate statements can be left out. Whether Regulation S-X binds a company depends on its filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission; see [Form, GAAP presumption and materiality under Regulation S-X](../../close-revrec/group-bridge/cls-sx-form-gaap-materiality.md).

Under § 210.3-05(a)(1), financial statements (except the related schedules specified in § 210.12) prepared and audited in accordance with Regulation S-X (including the independence standards in § 210.2-01 or, alternatively if the business is not a registrant, the applicable independence standards) must be filed for the periods specified in paragraph (b) of the section if any of the following conditions exist: (i) during the most recent fiscal year or subsequent interim period for which a balance sheet is required by § 210.3-01, a business acquisition has occurred, or (ii) after the date of the most recent balance sheet filed pursuant to § 210.3-01, consummation of a business acquisition has occurred or is probable [@CL-MNA-0670; @CL-MNA-0671; @CL-MNA-0672]. Under paragraph (b)(1), when registering an offering of securities to the security holders of the business to be acquired, the financial statements covering fiscal years must be audited except as provided in Item 14 of Schedule 14A (§ 240.14a-101 of the chapter) with respect to certain proxy statements or in registration statements filed on Forms N-14, S-4, or F-4 (§ 239.23, § 239.25, or § 239.34 of the chapter) [@CL-MNA-0119]. This section does not apply to a real estate operation subject to § 210.3-14 or a business which is totally held by the registrant prior to consummation of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0673].

Under paragraph (b)(2), in all cases not specified in paragraph (b)(1) of the section, financial statements of the business acquired or to be acquired must be filed for the periods specified in paragraph (b)(2) or such shorter period as the business has been in existence [@CL-MNA-0674]. Those periods are determined using the conditions specified in the definition of significant subsidiary in § 210.1-02(w)(1)(i) through (w)(1)(iii), using the lower of the total revenue component or income or loss from continuing operations component for evaluating the income test [@CL-MNA-0675]. If none of the conditions exceeds 20 percent, financial statements are not required [@CL-MNA-0676]. If any of the conditions exceeds 20 percent, but none exceed 40 percent, financial statements must be filed for at least the most recent fiscal year and the most recent interim period specified in §§ 210.3-01 and 210.3-02 [@CL-MNA-0677]. If any of the conditions exceeds 40 percent, financial statements must be filed for at least the two most recent fiscal years and any interim periods specified in §§ 210.3-01 and 210.3-02 [@CL-MNA-0678]. Under paragraph (b)(1), if registering an offering of securities to the security holders of the business to be acquired, the financial statements specified in §§ 210.3-01 and 210.3-02 must be filed for the business to be acquired, except as provided otherwise for filings on Form N-14, S-4, or F-4 [@CL-MNA-0679].

Under paragraph (b)(4)(iii), separate financial statements of the acquired business specified in paragraph (b)(2)(ii) need not be presented once the operating results of the acquired business have been reflected in the audited consolidated financial statements of the registrant for at least nine months [@CL-MNA-0680]. Separate financial statements of the acquired business specified in paragraph (b)(2)(iii) need not be presented once the operating results of the acquired business have been reflected in the audited consolidated financial statements of the registrant for a complete fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0681]. Under paragraph (b)(4)(ii), a registrant, other than a foreign private issuer required to file reports on Form 6-K or a shell company (other than a business combination related shell company), that omits from its initial registration statement financial statements of a recently consummated business acquisition pursuant to paragraph (b)(4)(i) of the section must file those financial statements and any pro forma information specified by §§ 210.11-01 through 210.11-03 (Article 11) under cover of Form 8-K no later than 75 days after consummation of the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0770; @CL-MNA-1581; @CL-MNA-0771].

Under § 210.8-01(a), §§ 210.8-01 through 210.8-08 (Article 8) are applicable to financial statements filed for smaller reporting companies, and are not applicable to financial statements prepared for the purposes of Item 17 or Item 18 of Form 20-F [@CL-MNA-0791]. For the financial statements of businesses acquired or to be acquired, § 210.8-04 applies § 210.3-05 substituting §§ 210.8-02 and 210.8-03, as applicable, wherever § 210.3-05 references §§ 210.3-01 and 210.3-02 [@CL-MNA-1336]. For real estate operations acquired or to be acquired, § 210.8-06 applies § 210.3-14 substituting §§ 210.8-02 and 210.8-03, as applicable, wherever § 210.3-14 references §§ 210.3-01 and 210.3-02 [@CL-MNA-1337].

## How it works

Under § 210.3-05(a)(1), the requirement to file financial statements of the acquired business applies when a business acquisition has occurred during the most recent fiscal year or subsequent interim period for which a balance sheet is required by § 210.3-01, or when consummation of a business acquisition has occurred or is probable after the date of the most recent balance sheet filed pursuant to § 210.3-01 [@CL-MNA-0671; @CL-MNA-0672]. Under paragraph (b)(2), in all cases not specified in paragraph (b)(1), the periods to be filed are determined using the conditions in the definition of significant subsidiary in § 210.1-02(w)(1)(i) through (w)(1)(iii), using the lower of the total revenue component or income or loss from continuing operations component for evaluating the income test: if none of the conditions exceeds 20 percent, financial statements are not required, if any exceeds 20 percent but none exceed 40 percent, financial statements must be filed for at least the most recent fiscal year and the most recent interim period specified in §§ 210.3-01 and 210.3-02, and if any exceeds 40 percent, financial statements must be filed for at least the two most recent fiscal years and any interim periods specified in §§ 210.3-01 and 210.3-02 (or such shorter period as the business has been in existence) [@CL-MNA-0674; @CL-MNA-0675; @CL-MNA-0676; @CL-MNA-0677; @CL-MNA-0678]. Under paragraph (b)(4)(iii), separate financial statements specified in paragraph (b)(2)(ii) need not be presented once the operating results of the acquired business have been reflected in the audited consolidated financial statements of the registrant for at least nine months, and separate financial statements specified in paragraph (b)(2)(iii) need not be presented once the operating results of the acquired business have been reflected in the audited consolidated financial statements of the registrant for a complete fiscal year [@CL-MNA-0680; @CL-MNA-0681].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant buys a business after its last balance sheet date. The highest of the conditions measures 30 percent. That is above 20 percent and not above 40 percent, so the registrant files at least the target's most recent fiscal year and its latest interim period. If the highest condition had measured 45 percent, at least two fiscal years would be needed. Nine months after the purchase the target's results sit in the registrant's audited consolidated statements, so separate statements for the middle band need no longer be presented.

## Never confuse

Under § 210.11-01(b)(1)(i), a business acquisition is considered significant for pro forma financial information if it meets the definition of a significant subsidiary in § 210.1-02(w)(1), substituting 20 percent for 10 percent each place it appears therein [@CL-MNA-0687], whereas under § 210.3-05(b)(2), the 20 percent and 40 percent thresholds determine the periods for which separate financial statements of the business acquired or to be acquired must be filed [@CL-MNA-0675; @CL-MNA-0676; @CL-MNA-0677; @CL-MNA-0678]; see [Pro forma financial information: when it is required](mna-sx-pro-forma-presentation.md).
