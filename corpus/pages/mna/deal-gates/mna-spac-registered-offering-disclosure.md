## Purpose

Shows what the filing for a registered offering by a special purpose acquisition company puts on the prospectus cover page, in the summary and in the dilution disclosure, and what it says about the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and promoters, conflicts of interest and officer and director fiduciary duties [@CL-MNA-1152; @CL-MNA-1156; @CL-MNA-1160; @CL-MNA-1161; @CL-MNA-1163; @CL-MNA-1164]. The defined terms are on the [definitions card](mna-spac-definitions.md).

### Cover page

The cover-page rule is in 17 CFR 229.1602(a) [@CL-MNA-1152]. In addition to the information that Item 501 of Regulation S-K requires, the filing provides the listed information on the outside front cover page of the prospectus, in plain English as 17 CFR 230.421(d) requires [@CL-MNA-1152]. Item 501 itself says the registrant must furnish the information that follows in that section in plain English [@CL-MNA-1340]. The cover page states the time frame for the special purpose acquisition company to consummate a de-SPAC transaction and whether it may be extended [@CL-MNA-1153]. It states whether security holders will have the opportunity to redeem the securities offered and whether the redemptions will be subject to any limitations [@CL-MNA-1193]. It states the amount of compensation received or to be received by the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and promoters, the amount of securities issued or to be issued by the SPAC to them and the price paid or to be paid for the securities [@CL-MNA-1194]. It also states whether that compensation and securities issuance may result in a material dilution of the purchasers' equity interests, with a cross-reference, highlighted by prominent type or in another manner, to the locations of related disclosures in the prospectus [@CL-MNA-1195]. It states whether there may be actual or potential material conflicts of interest between the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates or promoters and purchasers in the offering, with a cross-reference, highlighted by prominent type or in another manner, to the locations of related disclosures in the prospectus [@CL-MNA-1155; @CL-MNA-1232].

The cover page also has a table. It discloses, at quartile intervals based on percentages of the maximum redemption threshold, the offering price and, as of the most recent balance sheet date filed, the net tangible book value per share, as adjusted [@CL-MNA-1196]. The adjusted value is stated as if the offering and assumed redemption levels have occurred and to give effect to material probable or consummated transactions, other than the completion of a de-SPAC transaction, and the table also shows the difference between the offering price and that adjusted value [@CL-MNA-1197]. Paragraph (a)(4) also requires a cross-reference, highlighted by prominent type or in another manner, to the locations of related disclosures in the prospectus [@CL-MNA-1231]. If the offering includes an over-allotment option, the table has separate rows with and without the exercise of that option [@CL-MNA-1208].

Item 502 of Regulation S-K calls for a reasonably detailed table of contents on either the inside front or the outside back cover page of the prospectus [@CL-MNA-1341]. The table of contents must be included immediately following the cover page in any prospectus the registrant delivers electronically [@CL-MNA-1342].

### Prospectus summary and dilution

The summary information that Item 503(a) of Regulation S-K requires must include a brief description of the matters listed in the summary paragraph, in plain English as 17 CFR 230.421(d) requires [@CL-MNA-1156]. Those matters include the manner in which the company will identify and evaluate potential business combination candidates and whether it will solicit shareholder approval for the de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1233]. They include the material terms of the trust or escrow account and the amount or percentage of the gross offering proceeds that the company will place in it [@CL-MNA-1154]. They include the material terms of the securities being offered, including redemption rights, and whether the securities are the same class as those held by the SPAC sponsor and its affiliates [@CL-MNA-1234]. They include the period in which the company intends to consummate a de-SPAC transaction, its plans if it does not, and whether and how it may extend the period [@CL-MNA-1157]. The summary also describes any limitations on extensions, including the number of times, the consequences to the SPAC sponsor of not completing an extension, and whether security holders will have voting or redemption rights with respect to an extension [@CL-MNA-1198]. The summary also describes any plans to seek additional financings and how the terms of additional financings may impact unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-1235]. The summary presents in a table the nature and amount of the compensation received or to be received by the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and promoters and the amount of securities issued or to be issued by the SPAC to them [@CL-MNA-1158]. The table also covers the price paid or to be paid for the securities, and outside the table the summary states the extent to which that compensation and securities issuance may result in a material dilution of the purchasers' equity interests [@CL-MNA-1159]. The summary also describes any actual or potential material conflict of interest between the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates or promoters and purchasers in the offering, including those that may arise in determining whether to pursue a de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1236].

A separate dilution disclosure is in a table at the same quartile intervals as the cover-page table and shows the offering price and the net tangible book value per share, as adjusted and determined in the same manner [@CL-MNA-1160]. It also shows the difference between the offering price and that adjusted value, and must show the nature and amounts of each source of dilution used to determine the adjusted value [@CL-MNA-1199]. The table must also show the number of shares used to determine the adjusted value and any adjustments to the number of shares used to determine the per share component [@CL-MNA-1237]. Outside the table, the filing describes each material potential source of future dilution following the registered offering, including sources not included in the table [@CL-MNA-1238]. It also describes the model, methods, assumptions, estimates and parameters necessary to understand the tabular disclosure [@CL-MNA-1239].

Item 503 of Regulation S-K adds general rules for the summary [@CL-MNA-1343; @CL-MNA-1344; @CL-MNA-1345]. Where the length or complexity of the prospectus makes a summary useful, a summary of the information in the prospectus is to be provided [@CL-MNA-1343], and it should be brief [@CL-MNA-1344]. The complete mailing address and telephone number of the principal executive offices are to be included either on the cover page or in the summary section of the prospectus [@CL-MNA-1345].

Item 506 is the general dilution item [@CL-MNA-0096; @CL-MNA-1346]. Where common equity securities are being registered and there is substantial disparity between the public offering price and the effective cash cost to officers, directors, promoters and affiliated persons of common equity acquired by them in transactions during the past five years, or which they have the right to acquire, and the registrant is not subject to the reporting requirements of section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Exchange Act immediately prior to filing of the registration statement, there shall be included a comparison of the public contribution under the proposed public offering and the effective cash contribution of such persons [@CL-MNA-0096; @CL-MNA-0097; @CL-MNA-0098]. In such cases and in other instances where common equity securities are being registered by a registrant that has had losses in each of its last three fiscal years and there is a material dilution of the purchasers' equity interest, the following shall be disclosed [@CL-MNA-1346]: the net tangible book value per share before and after the distribution; the amount of the increase in such value per share attributable to the cash payments made by purchasers of the shares being offered [@CL-MNA-1347]; and the amount of the immediate dilution from the public offering price which will be absorbed by such purchasers [@CL-MNA-1348].

### SPAC sponsor and conflicts of interest

The filing provides the listed information about the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and the promoters of the company [@CL-MNA-1240]. It states the SPAC sponsor's name, describes its form of organization and describes the general character of its business [@CL-MNA-1241]. It describes the experience of the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and any promoters in organizing special purpose acquisition companies, and the extent to which they are involved in other special purpose acquisition companies [@CL-MNA-1161]. It describes the material roles and responsibilities of the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and any promoters in directing and managing the company's activities [@CL-MNA-1242]. It describes any agreement, arrangement or understanding between the SPAC sponsor and the company, its officers, directors or affiliates on determining whether to proceed with a de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1209]. It discloses the nature and amounts of all compensation that has been or will be awarded to, earned by or paid to the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and any promoters for all services rendered or to be rendered in all capacities [@CL-MNA-1200]. The services are those rendered to the company and its affiliates, and the filing also discloses the amount of securities issued or to be issued by the SPAC to the sponsor, its affiliates and any promoters and the price paid or to be paid for them [@CL-MNA-1201]. It discloses any circumstances or arrangements under which the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and promoters, directly or indirectly, have transferred or could transfer ownership of securities of the SPAC, or that have resulted or could result in the surrender or cancellation of those securities [@CL-MNA-1243]. It discloses the nature and amounts of any reimbursements to be paid to them upon the completion of a de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1210]. It identifies the controlling persons of the SPAC sponsor and discloses, as of the most recent practicable date, the persons who have direct and indirect material interests in the sponsor, and the nature and amount of those interests [@CL-MNA-1162]. It describes any agreement, arrangement or understanding, including any payments, between the SPAC sponsor and unaffiliated security holders regarding the redemption of outstanding securities [@CL-MNA-1211]. It discloses, in a tabular format to the extent practicable, the material terms of any agreement, arrangement or understanding regarding restrictions on whether and when the SPAC sponsor and its affiliates may sell securities of the company, including the dates on which it may expire, the natural persons and entities subject to it, any exceptions and any terms that would result in earlier expiration [@CL-MNA-1244; @CL-MNA-1245; @CL-MNA-1246].

The filing describes any actual or potential material conflict of interest, including any that may arise in determining whether to proceed with a de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1163]. That includes any material conflict arising from the manner in which the company compensates a SPAC sponsor, officers or directors or the manner in which a SPAC sponsor compensates its officers and directors [@CL-MNA-1212]. The conflicts are those between the SPAC sponsor or its affiliates, the company's officers, directors or promoters, or the target company's officers or directors, on one side, and unaffiliated security holders of the SPAC, on the other [@CL-MNA-1213]. The filing also briefly describes the fiduciary duties of each officer and director of the company to other companies to which they have fiduciary duties [@CL-MNA-1164].

## How it works

The cover page gives the reader the key terms first: the time frame and whether it can be extended [@CL-MNA-1153], the redemption opportunity and any limits on it [@CL-MNA-1193], the sponsor's compensation [@CL-MNA-1194] and any conflicts [@CL-MNA-1155]. The summary repeats the economics in plain English and in a table, and states the extent of dilution outside the table [@CL-MNA-1158; @CL-MNA-1159]. The dilution disclosure uses the same quartile intervals as the cover-page table, so the two can be compared [@CL-MNA-1160]. The sponsor items then identify who stands behind the company [@CL-MNA-1162] and where their interests may differ from those of unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-1213].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offering price is 10.00 per share and the offering includes an over-allotment option. The cover-page table shows the adjusted net tangible book value per share at 25, 50 and 75 percent of the maximum redemption threshold and at maximum redemption, and has separate rows with and without the over-allotment option [@CL-MNA-1196; @CL-MNA-1208]. The adjusted values are 8.20, 6.90, 5.30 and 3.10, so the differences from the offering price are 1.80, 3.10, 4.70 and 6.90 [@CL-MNA-1197]. The dilution disclosure uses the same four intervals [@CL-MNA-1160]. The cover page also states the time frame of 18 months and whether it may be extended [@CL-MNA-1153].

## Never confuse

The cover-page table and the dilution disclosure are separate items. Both use the same quartile intervals, but the cover-page table sits in the cover-page paragraph [@CL-MNA-1196] and the dilution disclosure is its own paragraph that must also show each source of dilution [@CL-MNA-1160; @CL-MNA-1199]. For conflicts of interest, the rules require the filing to state on the cover page whether there may be actual or potential material conflicts of interest and to describe any actual or potential material conflict of interest [@CL-MNA-1155; @CL-MNA-1163]. A roll-up transaction of partnerships has its own disclosure items, see [Disclosure in a roll-up transaction of partnerships](mna-roll-up-transaction-disclosure.md).

## Company calibration

Confirm how the company fixes the maximum redemption threshold, the adjusted net tangible book value per share and the sponsor compensation figures, so the cover-page, summary and dilution tables agree. [@Q-029]
