## Purpose

Shows how a declaration is submitted, how a filed notice is withdrawn, and how the Committee treats a contingent equity interest at the time of acquisition.

## The rule

| Applies to | A party submitting a declaration, a party that filed a notice, and a holder of a contingent equity interest [@CL-MNA-0850; @CL-MNA-0851; @CL-MNA-0853] |

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text.

A party or parties submitting a declaration of a transaction under § 800.401 or § 800.402 shall submit electronically the information set out in § 800.404, including the certifications required thereunder, to the Staff Chairperson in accordance with the submission instructions on the Committee's section of the Department of the Treasury website [@CL-MNA-0850; @CL-MNA-0066]. Persons filing a declaration shall, while the matter is pending before the Committee, promptly advise the Staff Chairperson of any material changes in plans, facts or circumstances regarding the transaction, and of any material change in information provided or required to be provided to the Committee under § 800.404 [@CL-MNA-0928; @CL-MNA-0086]. The section has further rules on other communications, on filed material and on a declaration after a notice that this card does not restate. The confidentiality of what is filed is on the [information duty and confidentiality card](mna-cfius-information-duty-confidentiality-and-penalties.md), and the certification a notice carries is on the [notice contents card](mna-cfius-voluntary-notice-contents-and-certification.md).

A party to a transaction that has filed notice under § 800.501(a) may request in writing, at any time prior to conclusion of all action under section 721, that such notice be withdrawn [@CL-MNA-0851]. A request to withdraw a notice shall be directed to the Staff Chairperson and shall state the reasons why the request is being made, and such requests will ordinarily be granted unless otherwise determined by the Committee [@CL-MNA-0852]. Any notice submitted pursuant to paragraph (c)(2) of the withdrawal section shall be deemed a new notice for purposes of the regulations in the part, including § 800.701 [@CL-MNA-0929]. The section has further rules on agency notices and on what the Committee sets when a withdrawal is granted that this card does not restate.

A contingent equity interest is a financial instrument that currently does not constitute an equity interest but is convertible into, or provides the right to acquire, an equity interest upon the occurrence of a contingency or defined event [@CL-MNA-1310]. Conversion means the exercise of a right inherent in the ownership or holding of a particular financial instrument to exchange any such instrument for an equity interest [@CL-MNA-1311]. Completion date means, with respect to a transaction, the earliest date upon which any ownership interest, including a contingent equity interest, is conveyed, assigned, delivered, or otherwise transferred to a person, or a change in rights that could result in a covered control transaction or covered investment occurs [@CL-MNA-1308; @CL-MNA-1309].

For purposes of determining whether to include the rights that a holder of a contingent equity interest will acquire upon conversion of, or exercise of a right provided by, that interest in the Committee's analysis of whether a notified transaction is a covered transaction, the Committee will consider factors that include the imminence of conversion or satisfaction of contingent conditions [@CL-MNA-0067; @CL-MNA-0853]. A further factor is whether conversion or satisfaction of contingent conditions depends on factors within the control of the acquiring party [@CL-MNA-0930]. The section lists a third factor that this card does not restate. Paragraph (b) operates when the Committee, applying paragraph (a), determines that the rights a holder will acquire upon conversion or satisfaction of contingent condition will not be included in the Committee's analysis of whether a notified or submitted transaction is a covered transaction [@CL-MNA-0854]. Under paragraph (b), the Committee will disregard the contingent equity interest for purposes of that transaction except to the extent that they convey immediate rights to the holder with respect to the entity that issued the interest [@CL-MNA-0855]. A conversion of a contingent equity interest is also one of the listed kinds of transaction, which is on the [party and transaction card](mna-cfius-party-transaction-and-applicability.md) [@CL-MNA-0916].

## How it works

A declaration goes to the Staff Chairperson electronically with the required certifications in accordance with the submission instructions on the Committee's section of the Department of the Treasury website, and the filer keeps the Staff Chairperson promptly advised of material changes in plans, facts or circumstances and in information provided or required under § 800.404 while the matter is pending [@CL-MNA-0850; @CL-MNA-0066; @CL-MNA-0928; @CL-MNA-0086]. A party to a transaction that has filed notice under § 800.501(a) can request in writing to withdraw it at any time prior to conclusion of all action under section 721, and the request will ordinarily be granted unless otherwise determined by the Committee [@CL-MNA-0851; @CL-MNA-0852]. A notice submitted pursuant to paragraph (c)(2) of the withdrawal section is deemed a new notice for purposes of the regulations in the part, including § 800.701 [@CL-MNA-0929]. For a contingent equity interest, in determining whether to include the rights acquired upon conversion of, or exercise of a right provided by, that interest in its analysis of whether a notified transaction is a covered transaction, the Committee considers factors that include the imminence of conversion or satisfaction of contingent conditions and whether conversion or satisfaction depends on factors within the control of the acquiring party [@CL-MNA-0067; @CL-MNA-0853; @CL-MNA-0930]. When the Committee determines under paragraph (a) that those rights will not be included in its analysis of whether a notified or submitted transaction is a covered transaction, it disregards the contingent equity interest for purposes of that transaction except to the extent that they convey immediate rights to the holder with respect to the entity that issued the interest [@CL-MNA-0854; @CL-MNA-0855]. Deadlines for mandatory declarations are on the [mandatory declarations card](mna-cfius-mandatory-declarations.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Foreign Fund F plans to buy common stock and a convertible debenture of Company T. The debenture converts only if an event outside F's control occurs, and the share count on conversion cannot be known now. Separately, the parties filed a notice and, on day 40 of the process, ask in writing to withdraw it.

Answer: the Committee may disregard the debenture for now, except for any immediate rights it gives F. The withdrawal request goes to the Staff Chairperson with reasons, and it will ordinarily be granted unless the Committee decides otherwise.

## Never confuse

Withdrawal is not rejection. A party asks to withdraw, and the request is ordinarily granted unless the Committee decides otherwise [@CL-MNA-0852], while rejection is a Committee response covered on the [notice procedures card](mna-cfius-notice-procedures-and-rejection.md). A declaration is also different from a notice, and the choice between them is on the [voluntary declarations card](mna-cfius-voluntary-declarations-and-notices.md). Disregarding a contingent interest still leaves any immediate rights it conveys in view [@CL-MNA-0855].
