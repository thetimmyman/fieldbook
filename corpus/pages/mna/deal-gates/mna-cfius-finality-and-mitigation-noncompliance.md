## Purpose

Shows when the authority of the President and the Committee over a covered transaction remains available, when it shall not be exercised, and what the Committee or a lead agency may do when a party is not in compliance with a mitigation agreement or condition.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Covered transactions within the listed dates, and parties to a mitigation agreement or condition entered into or imposed under section 721(l) [@CL-MNA-0039; @CL-MNA-0576] |

All authority available to the President or the Committee under section 721(d), including divestment authority, shall remain available at the discretion of the President [@CL-MNA-0038]. It remains available for covered control transactions proposed or pending on or after August 23, 1988, for transactions that fell within the scope of part 801 between November 10, 2018 and February 12, 2020, and for covered investments proposed or pending after February 13, 2020 [@CL-MNA-0039].

Subject to § 800.501(c)(1)(ii), that authority shall not be exercised if any one of three events has occurred [@CL-MNA-0569; @CL-MNA-0040; @CL-MNA-0041; @CL-MNA-0042]. Divestment or other relief under section 721 shall not be available for transactions completed before August 23, 1988 [@CL-MNA-0043].

## How it works

The three events that bar the authority are these.

| Event | Text |
|---|---|
| Not a covered transaction | Subject to § 800.219(d), the Committee, through its Staff Chairperson, has advised a party or the parties in writing that a particular transaction for which a voluntary notice or a declaration has been filed is not a covered transaction [@CL-MNA-0040] |
| All action concluded | The parties have been advised in writing under § 800.407(a)(4), § 800.506 or § 800.508(d) that the Committee has concluded all action under section 721 with respect to the covered transaction [@CL-MNA-0041] |
| Presidential decision | The President has previously announced under section 721(d) a decision not to exercise the President's authority under section 721 with respect to the covered transaction [@CL-MNA-0042] |

The first event is also stated subject to § 800.219(d) [@CL-MNA-0040]; that paragraph sits in the excepted-investor definition and is not restated here.

The bar is stated subject to § 800.501(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MNA-0569]. Under § 800.501(c)(1), any member of the Committee, or the member's designee at or above the Under Secretary or equivalent level, may, subject to paragraph (c)(2) of that section, which this card does not restate, file an agency notice to the Committee through the Staff Chairperson regarding a transaction if a listed condition is met [@CL-MNA-0577]. The bar names only paragraph (c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MNA-0569]. The other alternative in paragraph (c)(1) is not restated here [@CL-MNA-0577]. Paragraph (c)(1)(ii) applies to a covered transaction for which the Committee has informed the parties in writing that it has concluded all action under section 721 or determined that the transaction is not a covered transaction, or for which the President has announced a decision not to exercise authority under section 721(d) [@CL-MNA-0578; @CL-MNA-0579]. It also needs either of two further events [@CL-MNA-0579]. The first is that a party submitted false or misleading material information to the Committee in connection with its consideration of the transaction, or omitted material information, including material documents [@CL-MNA-0580]. The second is that a party to the transaction, or the entity resulting from its consummation, materially breaches a mitigation agreement or condition described in section 721(l)(3)(A), the lead department or agency monitoring and enforcing it certifies the breach to the Committee as a material breach, and the Committee determines that there are no other adequate and appropriate remedies or enforcement tools available to address the breach [@CL-MNA-0581; @CL-MNA-0582; @CL-MNA-0583]. For a review or investigation initiated under section 721 before August 13, 2018, the breach must be an intentional material breach [@CL-MNA-0581; @CL-MNA-0583].

The [review and investigation card](mna-cfius-review-and-investigation.md) and the [notice procedures card](mna-cfius-notice-procedures-and-rejection.md) cover the filings that lead to these events.

The next rules apply at any time after a mitigation agreement or condition is entered into or imposed under section 721(l) [@CL-MNA-0576]. If the Committee, or a lead agency in coordination with the Staff Chairperson, as the case may be, determines that a party or parties are not in compliance with the terms of the agreement or condition, it may act [@CL-MNA-0044; @CL-MNA-0045]. Its responses are in addition to the Committee's authority to impose penalties under section 721(h) and to unilaterally initiate a review of any covered transaction under section 721(b)(1)(D)(iii) [@CL-MNA-0045].

There are three responses it may use [@CL-MNA-0046; @CL-MNA-0047; @CL-MNA-0049]. The first is to negotiate a plan of action for the party or parties to remediate the lack of compliance. Failure to abide by the plan or otherwise remediate serves as the basis for the Committee to find a material breach of the agreement or condition [@CL-MNA-0046]. The second is to require the party or parties to submit a written notice or declaration under clause (i) of section 721(b)(1)(C) for a covered transaction initiated after the date of the determination of noncompliance and before the date five years after it [@CL-MNA-0047]. The notice or declaration goes to the Committee to initiate a review under section 721(b) [@CL-MNA-0048]. The third is to seek injunctive relief [@CL-MNA-0049].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buyer owns a business that carries a mitigation agreement with a data-location condition. On March 1 of Year 1, the Committee determines that the buyer moved the data to an unapproved location and is not in compliance with the terms of the agreement [@CL-MNA-0044]. The Committee negotiates a plan of action with a ninety-day deadline, and the buyer misses it [@CL-MNA-0046]. The failure serves as the basis for the Committee to find a material breach [@CL-MNA-0046].

The Committee may also require a notice or declaration for a covered transaction that the buyer initiates after March 1 of Year 1 and before March 1 of Year 6 [@CL-MNA-0047]. A covered transaction the buyer initiates in Year 4 falls inside that window [@CL-MNA-0047]. One initiated in Year 7 falls outside it [@CL-MNA-0047]. The Committee may also seek injunctive relief, and its penalty authority and its power to start a review of its own accord remain [@CL-MNA-0049; @CL-MNA-0045].

A second buyer was advised in writing under § 800.506 that the Committee had concluded all action under section 721 with respect to its covered transaction [@CL-MNA-0041]. Assume the review was initiated on or after August 13, 2018 and the mitigation agreement is one described in section 721(l)(3)(A) [@CL-MNA-0581; @CL-MNA-0582; @CL-MNA-0583]. The lead agency monitoring that buyer's mitigation agreement later certifies a material breach to the Committee, and the Committee determines that no other adequate and appropriate remedies or enforcement tools are available [@CL-MNA-0581; @CL-MNA-0582; @CL-MNA-0583]. A Committee member may then file an agency notice through the Staff Chairperson [@CL-MNA-0577; @CL-MNA-0578; @CL-MNA-0579]. The bar in § 800.701(b) is stated subject to § 800.501(c)(1)(ii), so the written advice is not by itself the end of the matter [@CL-MNA-0569].

## Never confuse

A bar on the authority is not unconditional, because it is stated subject to § 800.501(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MNA-0569]. The authority also stays available for the transactions in the dates listed [@CL-MNA-0038; @CL-MNA-0039].

A determination of noncompliance is not the same as a finding of material breach. The determination opens the responses; the Committee may find a material breach where a party fails to abide by a negotiated plan or otherwise remediate [@CL-MNA-0044; @CL-MNA-0046]. The requirement to submit a notice or declaration is a separate response, and the text does not make it depend on a finding of material breach [@CL-MNA-0047].
