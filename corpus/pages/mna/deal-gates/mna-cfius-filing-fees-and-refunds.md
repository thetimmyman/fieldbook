## Purpose

Sets out the fee the parties pay with a formal written notice, and the cases in which the Department of the Treasury refunds it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Parties filing a formal written notice of a transaction under § 800.501(a) on or after May 1, 2020 [@CL-MNA-0056] |

Except as otherwise provided in the fee subpart, the parties filing a formal written notice of a transaction with the Committee under § 800.501(a) on or after May 1, 2020 shall pay a filing fee [@CL-MNA-0056]. The fee depends on the value of the transaction.

| Value of the transaction | Fee |
|---|---|
| Less than $500,000 | No fee [@CL-MNA-0056] |
| $500,000 or more but less than $5,000,000 | $750 [@CL-MNA-0057] |
| $5,000,000 or more but less than $50,000,000 | $7,500 [@CL-MNA-0057] |
| $50,000,000 or more but less than $250,000,000 | $75,000 [@CL-MNA-0058] |
| $250,000,000 or more but less than $750,000,000 | $150,000 [@CL-MNA-0058] |
| $750,000,000 or more | $300,000 [@CL-MNA-0059] |

Except as provided in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the refund section, the Department of the Treasury shall not refund a filing fee in whole or in part [@CL-MNA-0060]. If the Committee determines that the transaction is not a covered transaction, the filing fee shall be refunded [@CL-MNA-0060]. In response to a petition by a party, if the Staff Chairperson determines, based on the information and representations contained in the formal written notice, as well as any other information provided by the parties, that a party or the parties to a transaction paid a filing fee in an amount greater than was required at the time of filing, the Department of the Treasury shall refund the amount of the overpayment to the party or parties who paid the filing fee [@CL-MNA-0061; @CL-MNA-0062].

Subject to §§ 800.1106 through 800.1108, the Staff Chairperson shall not accept a formal written notice under § 800.503(a) until payment of any fee required under the fee subpart is received by the Department of the Treasury [@CL-MNA-1327], in the manner specified on the Committee's section of the Department of the Treasury website [@CL-MNA-1328]. Parties to a transaction must pay any filing fee by electronic payment [@CL-MNA-1329]. The filing fee must be paid in U.S. dollars [@CL-MNA-1330].

If the Staff Chairperson determines that extraordinary circumstances relating to national security warrant, the Staff Chairperson may waive the filing fee in whole or in part and will notify the parties in writing [@CL-MNA-1331]. No waiver shall be implied, even where the Staff Chairperson does not reject a voluntary notice under § 800.1108 for failure to pay the required filing fee [@CL-MNA-1332]. The parties to a transaction shall not be required to pay an additional filing fee in the event that the Staff Chairperson permits the parties to withdraw and refile a notice under § 800.509(c)(2) [@CL-MNA-1333], unless the Staff Chairperson determines that a material change to the transaction has occurred, or a material inaccuracy or omission was made by the parties in information provided to the Committee, that requires the Committee to consider new information [@CL-MNA-1334], in which case the Staff Chairperson will inform the parties in writing [@CL-MNA-1335].

## How it works

The duty to pay falls on the parties filing the notice [@CL-MNA-0056]. The value of the transaction fixes the band, and each band starts at its lower figure, so a value of exactly $5,000,000 is in the $7,500 band [@CL-MNA-0057]. The fee duty is stated subject to other provisions of the fee subpart [@CL-MNA-0056]. How the value is determined is on the [filing fee valuation card](mna-cfius-filing-fee-valuation.md), and the [notice procedures card](mna-cfius-notice-procedures-and-rejection.md) covers rejection of a notice.

Except as provided in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the refund section, the Department of the Treasury does not refund a filing fee in whole or in part [@CL-MNA-0060]. Under paragraph (b), if the Committee determines that the transaction is not a covered transaction, the filing fee shall be refunded [@CL-MNA-0060]. Under paragraph (c), in response to a petition by a party, if the Staff Chairperson determines, based on the information and representations contained in the formal written notice, as well as any other information provided by the parties, that a party or the parties to a transaction paid a filing fee in an amount greater than was required at the time of filing, the Department of the Treasury refunds the amount of the overpayment to the party or parties who paid the filing fee [@CL-MNA-0061; @CL-MNA-0062].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Two parties file a formal written notice on or after May 1, 2020 for a transaction valued at 5,000,000. It is in the band that carries a fee of 7,500 [@CL-MNA-0056; @CL-MNA-0057]. A transaction valued at 4,999,999 would be in the band that carries 750 [@CL-MNA-0057].

The parties mistakenly pay 75,000 for the 5,000,000 transaction. A party petitions, and the Staff Chairperson determines that the parties paid more than required [@CL-MNA-0061]. The Department of the Treasury refunds the overpayment, which is 75,000 minus 7,500, or 67,500 [@CL-MNA-0062; @CL-MNA-0058].

A second filing is valued at 100,000,000, so the fee is 75,000 [@CL-MNA-0058]. If the Committee later determines that the transaction is not a covered transaction, the fee shall be refunded [@CL-MNA-0060]. If the Committee instead treats it as covered and the parties paid the right amount and file no petition, no refund provision applies, and the Department of the Treasury shall not refund any of it [@CL-MNA-0060].

## Never confuse

The filing fee is set by the value of the transaction and is not refunded in whole or in part except as provided in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the refund section [@CL-MNA-0056; @CL-MNA-0060]. Penalties for noncompliance with a mitigation agreement come from a separate authority [@CL-MNA-0045].
