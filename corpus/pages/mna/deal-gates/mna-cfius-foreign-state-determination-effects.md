## Purpose

Explains what a determination about a foreign state is, when it and its rescission take effect, and which transactions each one leaves alone. The determination concerns a foreign state's own investment review process [@CL-MNA-0570]. It is one of the two criteria for an excepted foreign state [@CL-MNA-0024; @CL-MNA-0025].

## The rule

|---|---|

| Applies to | Transactions affected by a determination about a foreign state under § 800.1001(a), or by its rescission [@CL-MNA-0050; @CL-MNA-0051] |

The Committee may determine at any time that a foreign state has established and is effectively utilizing a robust process to analyze foreign investments for national security risks and to facilitate coordination with the United States on matters relating to investment security [@CL-MNA-0570]. The Committee may rescind a determination under paragraph (a) of § 800.1001 if the Committee determines that such a rescission is appropriate [@CL-MNA-0571]. The Chairperson of the Committee shall publish a notice of any determination or rescission of a determination under paragraph (a) or (b) of § 800.1001, respectively, in the Federal Register [@CL-MNA-0572].

A determination under paragraph (a) of § 800.1001 shall take effect immediately upon publication of notice of such determination in the Federal Register and remain in effect unless rescinded under paragraph (b) of § 800.1001 [@CL-MNA-0050]. A rescission of a determination under paragraph (b) of § 800.1001 shall take effect on the date specified in the notice of such rescission published in the Federal Register [@CL-MNA-0051].

## How it works

The Committee decides whether to make a determination and whether to rescind it, and the Chairperson of the Committee publishes the notice in the Federal Register [@CL-MNA-0570; @CL-MNA-0571; @CL-MNA-0572].

A determination does not apply to any transaction for which the Staff Chairperson has accepted a declaration under § 800.405(a)(1) or a notice under § 800.503(a) [@CL-MNA-0052].

A rescission does not apply to any transaction for which one of two things is true. The first is that the completion date is before the date on which the rescission becomes effective [@CL-MNA-0053]. The second is that, before publication of the rescission, any one of three events has occurred [@CL-MNA-0054; @CL-MNA-0055]. Those events are listed below.

| Event before publication of the rescission | Text |
|---|---|
| Binding agreement | The parties to the transaction have executed a binding written agreement, or other binding document, establishing the material terms of the transaction that is ultimately consummated [@CL-MNA-0054] |
| Public offer | A party has made a public offer to shareholders to buy shares of a United States business [@CL-MNA-0055] |
| Proxy solicitation or conversion request | A shareholder has solicited proxies in connection with an election of the board of directors of a United States business, or has requested the conversion of convertible voting securities [@CL-MNA-0055] |

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A rescission notice is published on March 3 and specifies March 10 as the date it takes effect [@CL-MNA-0051; @CL-MNA-0572]. A first deal completes on March 6, before the rescission becomes effective, so the rescission does not apply to it [@CL-MNA-0053]. A second deal completes on March 20. Before March 3, the parties executed a binding written agreement establishing the material terms of that transaction, so the rescission does not apply to it [@CL-MNA-0054]. A third deal also completes on March 20. Before March 3, its parties had signed no binding document, and no party had made a public offer, solicited proxies or requested a conversion, so neither exception applies and the rescission applies to it [@CL-MNA-0053; @CL-MNA-0054; @CL-MNA-0055].

Separately, a determination about a different foreign state is published on March 3. A transaction whose notice the Staff Chairperson had already accepted under § 800.503(a) is not subject to that determination [@CL-MNA-0050; @CL-MNA-0052].

## Never confuse

A rescission takes effect on the date specified in its notice, while the binding-agreement, public-offer and proxy exceptions are measured against publication of the rescission [@CL-MNA-0051; @CL-MNA-0054; @CL-MNA-0055].

A determination under § 800.1001(a) does not by itself make a foreign state an excepted foreign state [@CL-MNA-0024; @CL-MNA-0025]. From February 13, 2023, the definition needs both criteria [@CL-MNA-0024].
