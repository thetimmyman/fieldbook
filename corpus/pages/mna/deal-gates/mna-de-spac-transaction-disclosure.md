## Purpose

Shows what a de-SPAC registration statement says on its cover page, in its prospectus summary and in its dilution disclosure, about the background, terms, effects and material interests of the transaction, and about the board determination and approvals [@CL-MNA-1165; @CL-MNA-1253; @CL-MNA-1215; @CL-MNA-1170; @CL-MNA-1272; @CL-MNA-1174]. The defined terms are on the [definitions card](mna-spac-definitions.md); reports, opinions and projections are on the [reports card](mna-de-spac-reports-projections-tender-offers.md).

### Cover page and dilution

In addition to the information that Item 501 of Regulation S-K requires, the filing provides the listed information on the outside front cover page of the prospectus, in plain English as 17 CFR 230.421(d) requires [@CL-MNA-1165]. The cover page states the determination, if any, of the board of directors or similar governing body of the special purpose acquisition company disclosed in response to Item 1606(a) of Regulation S-K [@CL-MNA-1166]. It also states, if applicable, that the company or the SPAC sponsor has received a report, opinion or appraisal referred to in Item 1607(a) of Regulation S-K [@CL-MNA-1167]. It describes briefly any material financing transactions that have occurred since the initial public offering of the company or will occur in connection with the consummation of the de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1247]. It states the compensation received or to be received by the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and promoters in connection with the de-SPAC transaction or any related financing transaction, the securities issued or to be issued by the SPAC to them and the price paid or to be paid for those securities, and whether this compensation and securities issuance may result in a material dilution of the equity interests of non-redeeming shareholders who hold the securities until the consummation of the de-SPAC transaction, with a cross-reference, highlighted by prominent type or in another manner, to the locations of related disclosures in the prospectus [@CL-MNA-1248; @CL-MNA-1249; @CL-MNA-1250; @CL-MNA-1251]. It states whether, in connection with the de-SPAC transaction, there may be any actual or potential material conflict of interest, including any that may arise in determining whether to proceed with the transaction [@CL-MNA-1168]. The conflicts stated include any material conflict arising from the manner in which the company compensates a SPAC sponsor, officers and directors or the manner in which a SPAC sponsor compensates its officers and directors [@CL-MNA-1214]. Those conflicts are between, on one hand, the SPAC sponsors, their affiliates, SPAC officers, SPAC directors or promoters, target company officers or target company directors and, on the other hand, unaffiliated security holders of the SPAC, with a cross-reference, highlighted by prominent type or in another manner, to the locations of related disclosures in the prospectus [@CL-MNA-1252; @CL-MNA-1251].

The prospectus summary information that Item 503(a) of Regulation S-K requires must include a brief description, in plain English as 17 CFR 230.421(d) requires, of the matters listed in paragraph (b) [@CL-MNA-1253]. Those matters include the background and material terms of the de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1253]. They include the board determination, if any, disclosed in response to Item 1606(a) of Regulation S-K, the material factors the board considered in making it, and any report, opinion or appraisal referred to in Item 1607(a) of Regulation S-K [@CL-MNA-1254; @CL-MNA-1255]. They include any actual or potential material conflict of interest, in connection with the transaction, between the SPAC sponsor, SPAC officers, SPAC directors, SPAC affiliates or promoters, target company officers or target company directors and unaffiliated security holders of the SPAC [@CL-MNA-1256]. The summary presents in a table the terms and amount of the compensation received or to be received by the SPAC sponsor, its affiliates and promoters in connection with the transaction or any related financing transaction and the securities issued or to be issued to them and the price paid or to be paid, and states outside the table the extent to which that compensation and securities issuance has resulted or may result in a material dilution of the equity interests of non-redeeming shareholders [@CL-MNA-1257; @CL-MNA-1258; @CL-MNA-1259]. It also describes the material terms of any material financing transactions that have occurred or will occur in connection with the consummation of the transaction, the anticipated use of proceeds and the dilutive impact, if any, on non-redeeming shareholders [@CL-MNA-1260; @CL-MNA-1261]. It describes the rights of security holders to redeem the outstanding securities of the company and the potential dilutive impact of redemptions on non-redeeming shareholders [@CL-MNA-1262].

The dilution disclosure is a table that includes intervals representing selected potential redemption levels across a reasonably likely range of outcomes, and it shows the offering price disclosed under Item 1602(a)(4) in the initial registered offering by the SPAC [@CL-MNA-1215]. For each redemption level, the filing states the company valuation at or above which the potential dilution results in the non-redeeming shareholders' interest per share being at least the initial public offering price per share of common stock [@CL-MNA-1169]. The table also shows, as of the most recent balance sheet date filed, the net tangible book value per share, as adjusted, as if the selected redemption levels have occurred and to give effect to, while excluding the de-SPAC transaction itself, material probable or consummated transactions and other material effects of the de-SPAC transaction on it, and the difference between the offering price and that adjusted value [@CL-MNA-1263; @CL-MNA-1264; @CL-MNA-1265]. The table must show the nature and amounts of each source of dilution used to determine the adjusted value, the number of shares used and any adjustments to the number of shares used to determine the per share component [@CL-MNA-1265; @CL-MNA-1266]. Outside the table, the filing describes each material potential source of future dilution that non-redeeming shareholders may experience by electing not to tender their shares in connection with the transaction, including sources not included in the table [@CL-MNA-1267; @CL-MNA-1268]. It also includes a description of the model, methods, assumptions, estimates and parameters necessary to understand the tabular disclosure [@CL-MNA-1269].

### Background, terms and effects

The filing provides a summary of the background of the de-SPAC transaction, and the summary must include a description of any contacts, negotiations or transactions that have occurred concerning the de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1170]. It states the material terms of the de-SPAC transaction, including but not limited to a brief description of the transaction and of any related financing transaction, including any payments from the SPAC sponsor to investors in connection with the financing transaction [@CL-MNA-1216]. The material terms include a reasonably detailed discussion of the reasons of the SPAC and the target company for the transaction and of the reasons of the SPAC for its structure and timing and that of any related financing transaction [@CL-MNA-1217]. They include an explanation of any material differences in the rights of SPAC and target company security holders as compared with security holders of the combined company, and a brief statement as to the accounting treatment [@CL-MNA-1218]. They include the Federal income tax consequences of the de-SPAC transaction to the SPAC, the target company and their respective security holders [@CL-MNA-1171].

The filing describes the effects of the de-SPAC transaction and any related financing transaction on the special purpose acquisition company and its affiliates, the SPAC sponsor and its affiliates, the target company and its affiliates, and unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-1219; @CL-MNA-1271]. The description must include a reasonably detailed discussion of both the benefits and detriments to those same parties [@CL-MNA-1270; @CL-MNA-1271]. The benefits and detriments of the transaction and any related financing transaction must be quantified to the extent practicable [@CL-MNA-1172]. The filing states whether or not security holders are entitled to any redemption or appraisal rights and, if so, summarizes those rights [@CL-MNA-1173]. If there are no redemption or appraisal rights available for security holders who object to the transaction, the filing briefly outlines any other rights that may be available to them [@CL-MNA-1220]. The filing also discloses any material interests in the transaction or any related financing transaction held by the SPAC sponsor or the company's officers or directors, including fiduciary or contractual obligations to other entities and any interest in, or affiliation with, the target company, or held by the target company's officers or directors that consist of any interest in, or affiliation with, the SPAC sponsor or the company [@CL-MNA-1272; @CL-MNA-1273; @CL-MNA-1274].

### Board determination

The board-determination disclosure applies where the law of the jurisdiction in which the company is organized requires its board of directors or similar governing body to determine whether the de-SPAC transaction is advisable and in the best interests of the company and its security holders, or otherwise to make any comparable determination, and the filing then discloses that determination [@CL-MNA-1174; @CL-MNA-1175]. The filing discusses the material factors the board considered in making any determination disclosed [@CL-MNA-1176]. To the extent considered, those factors must include, but need not be limited to, the valuation of the target company, the financial projections relied upon by the board and the terms of financing materially related to the transaction [@CL-MNA-1177]. They must also include, to the extent considered, any report, opinion or appraisal referred to in Item 1607(a) of Regulation S-K and the dilution described in Item 1604(c) of Regulation S-K [@CL-MNA-1221].

The filing states whether or not the de-SPAC transaction is structured so that approval of at least a majority of unaffiliated security holders of the company is required [@CL-MNA-1178]. It states whether or not a majority of the directors, or members of a similar governing body, who are not employees of the company has retained an unaffiliated representative to act solely on behalf of unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-1222]. The representative is retained for purposes of negotiating the terms of the transaction or preparing a report concerning its approval [@CL-MNA-1223]. The filing states whether or not the transaction was approved by a majority of the directors, or members of a similar governing body, who are not employees of the company [@CL-MNA-1224]. If any director voted against, or abstained from voting on, approval, the filing identifies those persons [@CL-MNA-1225]. It also indicates, if known after making reasonable inquiry, the reasons for the vote against the transaction or abstention [@CL-MNA-1226].

## How it works

The cover page tells a holder first what the board decided, whether the company or sponsor received outside advice, and where the conflicts sit [@CL-MNA-1166; @CL-MNA-1167; @CL-MNA-1168]. The background and terms items supply the history and the economics, including the tax treatment and the quantified benefits and detriments [@CL-MNA-1170; @CL-MNA-1171; @CL-MNA-1172]. The board items tie the determination to the company's governing law and then name the factors that must appear when the board considered them [@CL-MNA-1174; @CL-MNA-1176; @CL-MNA-1177]. The approval statements then tell a reader whether approval by unaffiliated security holders is required, whether a majority of the non-employee directors approved, and which directors voted against or abstained [@CL-MNA-1178; @CL-MNA-1224; @CL-MNA-1225].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company is organized in a jurisdiction whose law requires its board to determine that a de-SPAC transaction is advisable and in the best interests of the company and its security holders. The board weighed a target valuation of 900,000,000, a financing of 150,000,000, its financial projections, a fairness opinion from an outside firm and the dilution described in Item 1604(c). The filing discloses the determination and discusses the material factors [@CL-MNA-1174; @CL-MNA-1175; @CL-MNA-1176]. Because the board considered them, the factors include the valuation, the projections, the financing terms, the fairness opinion and the dilution [@CL-MNA-1177; @CL-MNA-1221]. The cover page states the determination and that the company received a report, opinion or appraisal [@CL-MNA-1166; @CL-MNA-1167]. One non-employee director votes against the transaction, so the filing identifies that director and, if known after reasonable inquiry, the reasons [@CL-MNA-1225; @CL-MNA-1226]. The filing also states whether approval by at least a majority of unaffiliated security holders is required [@CL-MNA-1178].

## Never confuse

A board determination is disclosed where the company's governing law requires the board to make it or a comparable determination [@CL-MNA-1174; @CL-MNA-1175]; the cover page states the determination "if any" [@CL-MNA-1166]. The quantified discussion of benefits and detriments is not the dilution table: one addresses the effects on each party and the other is a table at selected redemption levels that states, for each level, a company valuation [@CL-MNA-1270; @CL-MNA-1172; @CL-MNA-1215; @CL-MNA-1169]. A statement that an unaffiliated representative was retained is separate from the statement on approval by unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-1222; @CL-MNA-1178]. For the going-private counterpart on fairness, see the [going-private fairness card](../valuation/mna-going-private-fairness.md).

## Company calibration

Confirm what the company's governing law requires of its board, so the determination and the factor discussion are scoped correctly. [@Q-028]
