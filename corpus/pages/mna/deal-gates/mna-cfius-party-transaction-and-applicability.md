## Purpose

Shows who counts as a party to a transaction, what counts as a transaction, and which transactions the foreign investment review rules reach by date.

## The rule

| Field | Value |

| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Applies to | Parties to, and transactions of, the kinds the definitions list [@CL-MNA-0816; @CL-MNA-0820] |

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text.

In the case of an acquisition of an ownership interest in an entity, the parties to the transaction include the person acquiring the interest, the person from whom it is acquired and the entity whose interest is being acquired [@CL-MNA-0816]. In that case the listed parties are determined without regard to any person providing brokerage or underwriting services for the transaction [@CL-MNA-0817]. One listed rule makes the surviving entity and the entities merged with or into it the parties in a merger, and another makes the entities being consolidated and the new consolidated entity the parties in a consolidation [@CL-MNA-0818]. In a proxy solicitation, the parties are the person soliciting proxies and the person who issued the voting interest [@CL-MNA-0910]. In the acquisition or conversion of contingent equity interests, the parties are the issuer and the person holding the contingent equity interests [@CL-MNA-0911]. Where a person's rights change with respect to an entity in which that person has an investment, the parties are the person whose rights change as a result of the transaction and the entity to which those rights apply [@CL-MNA-0912]. For any other transaction, transfer, agreement or arrangement whose structure is designed or intended to evade or circumvent the application of the statute, any person that participates in it is a party [@CL-MNA-0913]. For the statute's mitigation provision, the term also includes any affiliate of a listed party that the Committee, or a lead agency acting on its behalf, determines is relevant to mitigating a risk to national security [@CL-MNA-0819]. A person means any individual or entity [@CL-MNA-1313]. The definition lists further party rules, for other types of transaction and for each party that submitted a declaration or notice, that this card does not restate.

A transaction, whether proposed or completed, includes a merger, acquisition or takeover, including the acquisition of an ownership interest in an entity and the acquisition of proxies from holders of a voting interest in an entity [@CL-MNA-0820]. Further listed forms are a merger or consolidation, the formation of a joint venture, and a long-term lease or concession arrangement under which the lessee makes substantially all business decisions concerning the operation of the leased entity as if it were the owner [@CL-MNA-0821]. The definition of transaction also lists an investment and the conversion of a contingent equity interest [@CL-MNA-0916]. Investment means the acquisition of equity interest, including contingent equity interest [@CL-MNA-1312]. The definition has a further example that this card does not restate.

Except as provided in the listed exceptions and otherwise in the part, the regulations in the part apply from February 13, 2020 [@CL-MNA-0822]. Subject to the part 801 paragraph, for a transaction for which a listed event, one being the completion date, occurred before February 13, 2020, the provisions of the regulations in effect on February 12, 2020 apply [@CL-MNA-0823]. For any transaction to which part 801 was applicable from November 10, 2018, through February 12, 2020, the regulations in part 801 in effect during that time continue to apply [@CL-MNA-0914]. Subject to the earlier paragraphs, for a transaction for which a listed event has occurred on or after February 13, 2020, and before October 15, 2020, the corresponding provisions of the regulations in effect during that time apply [@CL-MNA-0915]. Except as the earlier transitional paragraphs provide, the amendments to the part published in the Federal Register on September 15, 2020 apply from October 15, 2020 [@CL-MNA-0824]. Notwithstanding the earlier transitional paragraphs, the amendments published on November 26, 2024 apply from December 26, 2024 [@CL-MNA-0825]. The listed events other than the completion date are not restated on this card.

## How it works

Party status depends on the form of the deal, so the first step is to name the form: acquisition of an interest, merger, consolidation, proxy solicitation, contingent equity interest, change in rights or another listed form [@CL-MNA-0816; @CL-MNA-0818; @CL-MNA-0910; @CL-MNA-0911; @CL-MNA-0912]. A transaction counts whether it is proposed or completed [@CL-MNA-0820]. The date rules are layered: the part applies from one date, earlier transactions can stay under the older provisions or under part 801, and later amendments apply from their own dates [@CL-MNA-0822; @CL-MNA-0823; @CL-MNA-0914; @CL-MNA-0915; @CL-MNA-0824; @CL-MNA-0825]. The timing rule for a contingent equity interest is on the [contingent equity card](mna-cfius-declaration-procedures-withdrawal-and-contingent-interests.md). Whether a transaction is a covered transaction is a separate question on the [jurisdiction card](mna-cfius-jurisdiction.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Buyer A agrees to buy all the shares of Company T from Seller S. Broker B arranges the sale and Bank U underwrites part of the financing. The deal completes after every transitional date.

Answer: A, S and T are parties because the deal is an acquisition of an ownership interest. B and U are not made parties by their brokerage or underwriting services. A deal that had completed before the part applied would instead be tested under the older provisions, subject to the rule for transactions that part 801 covered.

## Never confuse

A transaction is not the same as a covered transaction. The definition only says what kinds of deals count as transactions [@CL-MNA-0820], and whether one is covered is on the [jurisdiction card](mna-cfius-jurisdiction.md). A party is also not the same as an advisor, because the acquisition rule applies without regard to brokerage or underwriting services [@CL-MNA-0817]. Control transactions are on the [covered control transactions card](mna-cfius-covered-control-transactions.md). The terms foreign person, parent and U.S. business are on the [foreign person card](mna-cfius-foreign-person-parent-us-business.md), and a date test in the sensitive personal data definition points back to this applicability rule on the [sensitive personal data card](mna-cfius-sensitive-personal-data.md).
