## Purpose

Shows when the parties to a covered transaction must file a declaration with the Committee, the voting-interest and timing rules, and the penalty for failing to.

| Applies to | Parties to a transaction described in the section's two trigger paragraphs [@CL-MNA-0625] |

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text. The mandatory-declaration duty binds the parties to a transaction described in the section's two trigger paragraphs, except as three listed paragraphs provide [@CL-MNA-0625]. Under paragraph (d), a declaration is not required under the first trigger for a covered transaction by an investment fund if the fund is managed exclusively by a general partner, a managing member or an equivalent, the general partner, managing member or equivalent is not a foreign person, and the fund satisfies, for any foreign person who is a limited partner on an advisory board or committee of the fund, the criteria in § 800.307(a)(3) and (4) [@CL-MNA-1548; @CL-MNA-1549; @CL-MNA-1550]. Paragraph (d) also removes the duty under the first trigger for a covered control transaction involving an air carrier, as defined in 49 USC 40102(a)(2), that holds a certificate issued under 49 USC 41102 [@CL-MNA-1551]. The other two paragraphs are covered below [@CL-MNA-0631; @CL-MNA-0664]. The penalty can reach any person who fails to comply [@CL-MNA-0632].

Subject to paragraph (b)(2) of the section [@CL-MNA-0073], the first trigger is a covered transaction that results in the acquisition of a substantial interest in a TID U.S. business by a foreign person in which the national or subnational governments of a single foreign state (other than an excepted foreign state) have a substantial interest [@CL-MNA-0625; @CL-MNA-0073; @CL-MNA-0626]. Under paragraph (b)(2), for purposes of paragraph (b)(1), the assessment of what constitutes a critical technology, as relevant to § 800.248(a), shall be as of the first date on which one of the conditions set forth in § 800.104(b)(1) through (4) is met with respect to a covered transaction [@CL-MNA-0074]. In the context of an acquisition by a foreign person, a substantial interest is a voting interest, direct or indirect, of 25 percent or more [@CL-MNA-0659]. In the context of a foreign person in which the national or subnational governments of a single foreign state have an interest, it is a voting interest, direct or indirect, of 49 percent or more, subject to paragraph (b) of the substantial-interest section, which this card does not restate [@CL-MNA-0660].

Subject to paragraph (c)(3) of the section [@CL-MNA-0075], the second trigger is a covered transaction involving a TID U.S. business [@CL-MNA-0075] that produces, designs, tests, manufactures, fabricates, or develops one or more critical technologies for which a U.S. regulatory authorization [@CL-MNA-0075] would be required for the export, reexport, transfer (in-country), or retransfer of such critical technology to a person that meets one of the listed descriptions [@CL-MNA-0625; @CL-MNA-0075; @CL-MNA-0627]. Two listed persons are a person that could directly control the TID U.S. business as a result of the covered transaction and a person that is directly acquiring an interest that is a covered investment in the TID U.S. business [@CL-MNA-0663]. Under paragraph (c)(3), for purposes of paragraph (c)(1), the assessment of what constitutes a critical technology shall be as of the first date on which one of the conditions set forth in § 800.104(b)(1) through (4) is met with respect to a covered transaction [@CL-MNA-0076; @CL-MNA-0668]. A voting interest for critical technology mandatory declarations means, for the purposes of § 800.401(c)(1)(v), a voting interest, direct or indirect, of 25 percent or more, subject to paragraphs (b) and (c) of the voting-interest section [@CL-MNA-0628]. A U.S. regulatory authorization includes a license or other approval issued by the Department of State under the ITAR and a license from the Department of Commerce under the EAR [@CL-MNA-1326]. The definition lists further authorizations that this card does not restate.

Parties may elect to submit a written notice instead of a declaration [@CL-MNA-0664]. Parties must submit the declaration, or a written notice in its place, no later than the dates the paragraph lists [@CL-MNA-0629]. One listed deadline is thirty days before the completion date of the transaction, if the completion date is after March 14, 2020 [@CL-MNA-0630]. Another listed deadline covers completion dates in a short earlier window, which this card does not restate.

No declaration is required under the second trigger for a covered control transaction by an excepted investor [@CL-MNA-0631]. The section lists further exceptions, among them one for an entity operating under an approved arrangement that offsets foreign ownership, control or influence, which this card does not restate; see [Foreign ownership, control or influence and its mitigation](../diligence/mna-nispom-foci-and-mitigation.md).

A person who fails to comply with the mandatory-declaration section may be liable to the United States for a civil penalty not to exceed $5,000,000 or the value of the transaction, whichever is greater [@CL-MNA-0632].

## How it works

The duty depends on the parties' transaction matching a trigger and on no listed exception applying [@CL-MNA-0625]. A declaration, or a written notice in its place, goes in thirty days before completion when the completion date is after March 14, 2020 [@CL-MNA-0629; @CL-MNA-0630]. Failure to file can draw a civil penalty [@CL-MNA-0632].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Foreign buyer X plans to buy all of Company T, a business that designs a critical technology that needs a U.S. export authorization for X's home country. X is not an excepted investor, X could directly control T, and no other listed exception applies. The parties plan to complete the deal on day 90. Answer: the critical-technology trigger applies, so the parties file a declaration, or a written notice, no later than day 60, thirty days before the completion date.

## Never confuse

A mandatory declaration is not a voluntary one, which a party to any proposed or completed transaction may choose to file instead of a written notice and which sits on the [voluntary declarations card](mna-cfius-voluntary-declarations-and-notices.md). Do not mix the voting-interest terms. For critical-technology declarations, for the purposes of § 800.401(c)(1)(v), a voting interest is 25 percent or more [@CL-MNA-0628]. For the government-interest trigger, the acquirer's substantial interest is also 25 percent or more [@CL-MNA-0659], and the governments' interest in the acquirer must be 49 percent or more [@CL-MNA-0660]. Coverage by the Committee is on the [jurisdiction card](mna-cfius-jurisdiction.md).
