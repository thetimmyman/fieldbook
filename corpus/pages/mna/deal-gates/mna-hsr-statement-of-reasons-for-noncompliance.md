## Purpose

Shows what the person filing notification must do when it cannot answer an item on the Notification and Report Form completely, and what its statement of reasons for noncompliance must contain.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | The person filing notification that cannot supply a complete response to an item [@CL-MNA-0201] |

A complete response must be supplied to each item on the Notification and Report Form and to any request for additional information made under section 7A(e) and § 803.20 [@CL-MNA-0200]. Whenever the person filing notification is unable to supply a complete response, that person must provide a statement of reasons for noncompliance for each item for which less than a complete response has been supplied [@CL-MNA-0201]. The duty falls on the person filing notification. The section does not say who decides whether a statement is adequate.

The statement must contain all information on which the person relies to explain its noncompliance. It must include at least the items the section lists [@CL-MNA-0202].

- Paragraph (a): why the person is unable to supply a complete response [@CL-MNA-0202].
- Paragraph (b): what information, and what specific documents or categories of documents, a complete response would have required [@CL-MNA-0203].
- Paragraph (c): who, if anyone, has that information and those documents, and a description of all efforts made to obtain them, including the names of the persons who searched and where the search was conducted [@CL-MNA-0204]. If no such efforts were made, the statement must explain why and must describe all efforts necessary to obtain the information and documents [@CL-MNA-0205].
- Paragraph (d), where noncompliance is based on a claim of privilege: the claim and all facts relied on in support of it, including the identity of each document, its author, the author's title or position, and the addressee [@CL-MNA-0206]. It also lists the addressee's title or position, the date, the subject matter, all recipients of the original and of any copies and their titles or positions, the document's present location, and who has control of it [@CL-MNA-0207].

## How it works

The duty to respond completely covers the form and any request for additional information [@CL-MNA-0200]. The statement duty is stated for each item for which less than a complete response has been supplied [@CL-MNA-0201]. The person puts into the statement all information on which it relies to explain the noncompliance [@CL-MNA-0202]. A person that files under § 803.4 on behalf of a foreign person that refuses to file, in an acquisition of the kind that section describes, is treated differently for information or documentary material that is not reasonably available [@CL-MNA-0210; @CL-MNA-0211; @CL-MNA-0216]; see [the foreign person card](mna-hsr-foreign-person-refusal-to-file.md). Requests for additional information are covered on [the expiry and second request card](mna-hsr-expiry-withdrawal-and-second-request.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A filing person cannot supply the 12 supply contracts that a divested unit holds. For that item it files a statement of reasons. The statement says why it cannot supply them, lists the contracts as the documents a complete response would need, names the unit's records office as their holder, describes two searches, and names the two employees who searched and the site they searched [@CL-MNA-0202; @CL-MNA-0203; @CL-MNA-0204]. Answer: the statement covers that item. Each other item still needs a complete response, or its own statement [@CL-MNA-0200; @CL-MNA-0201].

## Never confuse

A statement of reasons explains an incomplete answer. It does not replace the rule that each item gets a complete response [@CL-MNA-0200; @CL-MNA-0201]. A claim of privilege is one basis for noncompliance, and paragraph (d) gives it its own required content [@CL-MNA-0206; @CL-MNA-0207]. Only a filer acting under § 803.4 for a foreign person that refuses to file, in an acquisition of the kind that section describes, need not supply this statement, and only for information or documentary material that is not reasonably available [@CL-MNA-0210; @CL-MNA-0211; @CL-MNA-0216]. Which person files and certifies the notification is on [the filing card](mna-hsr-filing-and-who-files.md).
