## Purpose

Shows when another party to an acquisition may file notification for a foreign acquired person that refuses to file, what that filing and its affidavit must contain, and what stays the foreign person's duty. The section states the permission for an acquisition to which § 801.30 does not apply and in which no assets other than investment assets located in the United States, and no voting securities of a United States issuer, will be acquired directly or indirectly [@CL-MNA-0210].

| Applies to | A foreign acquired person that refuses to file, and any other party to the acquisition that files for it, in an acquisition of the kind described in the section [@CL-MNA-0210; @CL-MNA-0211] |

The section states its permission for an acquisition to which § 801.30 does not apply and in which no assets other than investment assets located in the United States, and no voting securities of a United States issuer, will be acquired directly or indirectly [@CL-MNA-0210]. In an acquisition of that kind, if a foreign acquired person refuses to file notification, any other person that is a party to the acquisition may file notification on behalf of the foreign person [@CL-MNA-0211]. The text says "may", so it permits the filing and does not require it. A notification filed that way constitutes the notification that the act and the rules required of the foreign person [@CL-MNA-0212].

The person filing on behalf of the foreign person must state in the affidavit required by § 803.5(b) that the foreign person has refused to file notification [@CL-MNA-0213]. It must also explain all efforts it made to obtain the foreign person's compliance with the act and the rules [@CL-MNA-0214]. That affidavit is the one for acquisitions to which § 801.30 does not apply, described on [the affidavits card](mna-hsr-affidavits-required-with-a-filing.md) [@CL-MNA-0233].

## How it works

The notification filed for the foreign person must contain all information and documentary material reasonably available to the filer that the foreign person would be required to provide [@CL-MNA-0215]. Where information or documentary material is not reasonably available, the filer must indicate that on the Notification and Report Form and need not supply the statement of reasons for noncompliance required by § 803.3 [@CL-MNA-0216]. The foreign person on whose behalf another person files is a "person filing notification" for purposes of the act and the rules [@CL-MNA-0217]. The section does not exempt that foreign person from the requirements that apply to a request for additional information or an extension of the waiting period [@CL-MNA-0218]. The statement of reasons is on [its own card](mna-hsr-statement-of-reasons-for-noncompliance.md), and the waiting period extension is on [the expiry and second request card](mna-hsr-expiry-withdrawal-and-second-request.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume that § 801.30 does not apply and that no assets other than investment assets located in the United States, and no voting securities of a United States issuer, will be acquired [@CL-MNA-0210]. A foreign acquired person refuses to file. The buyer, a party to the acquisition, files on its behalf [@CL-MNA-0211]. The buyer's affidavit states the refusal and explains the two written requests and three calls it made to obtain compliance [@CL-MNA-0213; @CL-MNA-0214]. It marks 2 items on the form as not reasonably available and includes the documents it could obtain [@CL-MNA-0215; @CL-MNA-0216]. Answer: the filing stands as the foreign person's notification, and the buyer need not supply a statement of reasons for those 2 items [@CL-MNA-0212; @CL-MNA-0216]. The foreign person remains a person filing notification and still has to meet any later request for additional information [@CL-MNA-0217; @CL-MNA-0218].

## Never confuse

Filing for a foreign person is not the foreign person filing. The other party acts on its behalf, and it must state the refusal in the affidavit and explain its own efforts [@CL-MNA-0213; @CL-MNA-0214]. It is not an exemption either, because the foreign person stays a person filing notification and is not exempt from the rules on a request for additional information or an extension of the waiting period [@CL-MNA-0217; @CL-MNA-0218]. Marking material as not reasonably available is not a statement of reasons: the filer is excused from the statement for material that is not reasonably available [@CL-MNA-0216]. Which person normally files and certifies is on [the filing card](mna-hsr-filing-and-who-files.md).
