## Purpose

Shows the disclosure for a report, opinion or appraisal that the company or the SPAC sponsor receives in a de-SPAC transaction, the filing rule when the company files a Schedule TO under Rule 13e-4(c)(2) for a redemption, and the disclosure for projections [@CL-MNA-1179; @CL-MNA-1204; @CL-MNA-1187]. The defined terms are on the [definitions card](mna-spac-definitions.md); the board items are on the [de-SPAC disclosure card](mna-de-spac-transaction-disclosure.md).

### Reports, opinions, appraisals and negotiations

The filing must disclose the information required by paragraph (b) of the section if the special purpose acquisition company or the SPAC sponsor has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party or an unaffiliated representative [@CL-MNA-1179]. The unaffiliated representative is the one referred to in Item 1606(d) of Regulation S-K, and the report, opinion or appraisal triggers the disclosure if it relates materially to any board determination disclosed under Item 1606(a) or to the approval of the de-SPAC transaction [@CL-MNA-1180]. It also triggers the disclosure if it relates materially to the consideration or the fairness of the consideration to be offered to security holders of the target company in the transaction, or to the fairness of the transaction to the company, its security holders or SPAC sponsor [@CL-MNA-1227].

The preparer and summary items apply to each report, opinion or appraisal referred to in paragraph (a) and to any negotiation or report described in response to Item 1606(d) of Regulation S-K concerning the terms of the transaction [@CL-MNA-1275]. The filing identifies the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative, briefly describes the qualifications of the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative, and describes the method of selection [@CL-MNA-1228]. It describes any material relationship that existed during the past two years or is mutually understood to be contemplated, and any compensation received or to be received as a result of the relationship [@CL-MNA-1181]. The relationship described is between the outside party, its affiliates, and/or unaffiliated representative, on one side, and the special purpose acquisition company, the SPAC sponsor, and/or their respective affiliates, on the other [@CL-MNA-1229]. If the report, opinion or appraisal relates to the fairness of the consideration to be offered to security holders of the target company in the de-SPAC transaction, the filing states whether the special purpose acquisition company or SPAC sponsor determined the amount of consideration to be paid to the target company or its security holders, or the valuation of the target company, or whether the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative recommended the amount of consideration to be paid or the valuation of the target company [@CL-MNA-1202; @CL-MNA-1203].

The filing furnishes a summary concerning the negotiation, report, opinion or appraisal [@CL-MNA-1182]. The summary must include but need not be limited to the procedures followed and the findings and recommendations, the bases for and methods of arriving at them, instructions received from the company or SPAC sponsor, and any limitation imposed by the company or sponsor on the scope of the investigation [@CL-MNA-1182; @CL-MNA-1230]. The items on the outside party, its qualifications and its selection must be given with respect to the firm that provides the report, opinion or appraisal or participates in the negotiation, rather than the employees of the firm that prepared the report, opinion or appraisal or participated in the negotiation [@CL-MNA-1183]. All reports, opinions or appraisals referred to must be, as applicable, filed as exhibits to the registration statement or schedule or included in the schedule if the schedule does not have exhibit filing requirements [@CL-MNA-1184].

### Tender offer filing obligations

The tender offer filing rule applies if the special purpose acquisition company files a Schedule TO pursuant to Rule 13e-4(c)(2) for any redemption of securities offered to security holders [@CL-MNA-1204]. Such a Schedule TO must provide the information required by General Instruction L.2 to Form S-4, General Instruction I.2 to Form F-4 and Item 14(f)(2) of Schedule 14A, as applicable, in addition to the information otherwise required by Schedule TO [@CL-MNA-1185]. The redemption must be conducted in compliance with all other provisions of Rule 13e-4 and Regulation 14E [@CL-MNA-1186].

### Projections

With respect to any projections disclosed in the filing or any exhibit to it, the filing discloses the purpose for which the projections were prepared and the party that prepared them [@CL-MNA-1187]. It discloses all material bases of the projections, all material assumptions underlying them and any material factors that may affect those assumptions [@CL-MNA-1188]. The disclosure should include a discussion of any material growth or reduction rates or discount rates used and the reasons for selecting them [@CL-MNA-1189]. If the projections relate to the performance of the special purpose acquisition company, the filing states whether or not they reflect the view of the company's management or board about its future performance [@CL-MNA-1190]. If they relate to the target company, the filing discloses whether or not the target company has affirmed to the company that its projections reflect the view of the target company's management or board about its future performance [@CL-MNA-1191]. Those statements are made as of the most recent practicable date prior to the date of the disclosure document required to be disseminated to security holders [@CL-MNA-1205]. If the projections no longer reflect the views of the management or board of the special purpose acquisition company or the target company on its future performance as of that date [@CL-MNA-1206; @CL-MNA-1205], the filing states the purpose of disclosing the projections and the reasons for any continued reliance on them [@CL-MNA-1207].

## How it works

The report disclosure is triggered by receipt of a report, opinion or appraisal that relates materially to one of the four listed matters, so the first questions are who received what, what it relates to and whether the opinion of counsel exclusion applies [@CL-MNA-1179; @CL-MNA-1180; @CL-MNA-1227]. The filing then covers the preparer, any relationship, who set the consideration where fairness is addressed, and a summary of procedures, findings, methods, instructions and scope limits [@CL-MNA-1228; @CL-MNA-1181; @CL-MNA-1202; @CL-MNA-1182; @CL-MNA-1230]. Where a redemption runs through a Schedule TO under the cited rule, the schedule carries the additional items and the redemption must still satisfy the tender offer rules [@CL-MNA-1204; @CL-MNA-1185; @CL-MNA-1186]. Projections carry their own trail: purpose, preparer, bases, assumptions, rates and the management or board statement [@CL-MNA-1187; @CL-MNA-1188; @CL-MNA-1189; @CL-MNA-1190; @CL-MNA-1191].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company receives a fairness opinion on the consideration to be paid to the target company's holders from an outside firm, and a legal opinion from its own counsel. The fairness opinion triggers the disclosure and the legal opinion does not [@CL-MNA-1179; @CL-MNA-1227]. The firm had a material advisory relationship with the company that existed 18 months ago, which is within the past two years, so the filing describes that relationship and any compensation received as a result [@CL-MNA-1181]. The filing states whether the company or the sponsor set the target valuation of 900,000,000 or the firm recommended it [@CL-MNA-1202; @CL-MNA-1203]. The fairness opinion is, as applicable, filed as an exhibit to the registration statement or schedule [@CL-MNA-1184]. The company files a Schedule TO under Rule 13e-4(c)(2) for a redemption, so the schedule carries the additional items that apply [@CL-MNA-1204; @CL-MNA-1185]. Projections for the target use a growth rate of 20 percent and a discount rate of 10 percent, so, if both rates are material, the disclosure should discuss them and the reasons for selecting them [@CL-MNA-1189]. The filing also discloses whether the target company has affirmed the projections as of the most recent practicable date [@CL-MNA-1191; @CL-MNA-1205].

## Never confuse

An opinion of counsel is not a report, opinion or appraisal that triggers the preparer disclosure [@CL-MNA-1179]. The identity, qualifications and selection items are given for the firm, not its employees [@CL-MNA-1183]. With respect to any projections disclosed in the filing or any exhibit to it, the filing must disclose the purpose, preparer, material bases and assumptions, and any material factors that may affect those assumptions, and the disclosure should discuss any material growth, reduction or discount rates used and the reasons for selecting them [@CL-MNA-1187; @CL-MNA-1188; @CL-MNA-1189]. The extra Schedule TO items are in addition to the information otherwise required by Schedule TO [@CL-MNA-1185]. For the going-private counterpart, see the [going-private reports card](../valuation/mna-going-private-reports-opinions-appraisals.md).

## Company calibration

Confirm who prepares and affirms the projections, and on what bases, so the projection disclosure and the dilution tables rest on the same conventions. [@Q-029]
