## Purpose

Shows which transactions are covered investments and which are not, with the listed examples that the claims on this card cover.

| Applies to | A foreign person's investment in an unaffiliated TID U.S. business [@CL-MNA-0738] |

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text. Covered investments include a transaction that meets the requirements of the covered investment definition irrespective of the percentage of voting interest acquired [@CL-MNA-0736]. The definition itself is on the [jurisdiction card](mna-cfius-jurisdiction.md).

Not covered investments include an investment by a foreign person in an unaffiliated TID U.S. business that does not afford the foreign person any of the access, rights, or involvement specified in the covered investment definition [@CL-MNA-0738]. One listed type is an investment by a foreign person who is an excepted investor in an unaffiliated TID U.S. business [@CL-MNA-0739]. Another listed type is a transaction that results or could result in control by a foreign person of an unaffiliated TID U.S. business [@CL-MNA-0740]. The sections list further types and examples that this card does not restate. Involvement means the right or ability to participate, whether or not exercised, including by doing any of the listed things, two of which are providing input into a final decision and consulting with or providing advice to a decisionmaker [@CL-MNA-1321]. The definition lists further ways that this card does not restate.

An unaffiliated TID U.S. business, with respect to a foreign person, is a TID U.S. business in which that foreign person does not directly hold more than 50 percent of the outstanding voting interest [@CL-MNA-1364]. The foreign person also must not have the right to appoint more than half of the members of the board of directors or equivalent governing body [@CL-MNA-1364; @CL-MNA-1365].

## How it works

A transaction is a covered investment because of what the definition requires, not because of the size of the stake [@CL-MNA-0736]. An investment that gives none of the listed access, rights or involvement is not a covered investment [@CL-MNA-0738]. In one listed example, a foreign person that is not an excepted investor proposes to acquire a four percent, non-controlling equity interest in an entity in which it has no voting interests or any rights [@CL-MNA-0798]. A proposed transaction with an unaffiliated TID U.S. business is, in that example, a covered investment where a designee of the foreign person will have the right to observe the meetings of the target's board of directors [@CL-MNA-0737]. In another, a foreign person that is an excepted investor proposes to purchase all of the shares of an unaffiliated TID U.S. business [@CL-MNA-1558]. As the sole owner it will have the right to elect directors and appoint other primary officers, and, assuming no other relevant facts, the proposed transaction is not a covered investment but is a covered control transaction [@CL-MNA-0741].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Foreign Fund F is not an excepted investor. It buys 4 percent of Company T, an unaffiliated TID U.S. business, and gets the right to send an observer to T's board meetings. Answer: the observer right is an access or right of the kind the definition lists, so this is a covered investment. If F were an excepted investor, the same facts would not be a covered investment.

## Never confuse

A covered investment is not a covered control transaction, which the [covered control transactions card](mna-cfius-covered-control-transactions.md) describes; the sole-owner example is a covered control transaction but not a covered investment [@CL-MNA-0741]. A transaction that results or could result in control falls outside the covered investment class [@CL-MNA-0740]. The investor's status also matters, because an investment by an excepted investor is not a covered investment [@CL-MNA-0739]. Coverage in general is on the [jurisdiction card](mna-cfius-jurisdiction.md).

## Company calibration

Whether any foreign investor in or of the company holds observer, board, information or decision rights, and whether that investor is an excepted investor. [@Q-045]
