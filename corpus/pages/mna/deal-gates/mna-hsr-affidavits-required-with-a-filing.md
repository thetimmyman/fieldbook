## Purpose

Shows which affidavit a notification must contain, what each affidavit must attest, and what must be attached or submitted with it.

| Applies to | The notification of each acquiring person in an acquisition to which § 801.30 applies, and the notification in an acquisition to which it does not apply [@CL-MNA-0220; @CL-MNA-0233] |

Which affidavit a notification must contain depends on whether § 801.30 applies to the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0220; @CL-MNA-0233]. The affidavit is part of the notification [@CL-MNA-0220; @CL-MNA-0233].

### Acquisitions to which § 801.30 applies

The notification required from each acquiring person must contain an affidavit attesting that the issuer or unincorporated entity whose voting securities or non-corporate interests are to be acquired has received written notice [@CL-MNA-0220]. The notice must be delivered to an officer, or to a person exercising similar functions where the entity has no officers, by email, certified or registered mail, wire, or hand delivery, at the entity's principal executive offices [@CL-MNA-0221]. In the example to paragraph (a)(1)(vi), “A” intends to acquire voting securities of Company B, and if “A” sends, via email, a notice letter to a general email account, information@CompanyB.com, “A” has not provided sufficient notice, whereas if “A” sends, via email, a notice letter to “B's” President, Jane Doe, at Jane.Doe@CompanyB.com, “A” has provided email notice to a specific officer of “B” [@CL-MNA-0208; @CL-MNA-0090; @CL-MNA-0209; @CL-MNA-0219].

The notice must state the identity of the acquiring person and that it intends to acquire voting securities or non-corporate interests of the issuer or unincorporated entity [@CL-MNA-0222]. It must state the specific classes sought and, if known, the number of each class that the acquiring person would hold as a result of the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0223]. If the number of voting securities is not known in the case of an issuer, it must state the specific notification threshold that the acquiring person intends to meet or exceed [@CL-MNA-0224]. If the acquiring person designates one, it must also state a higher threshold for additional voting securities that the person may hold in the year following the expiration of the waiting period [@CL-MNA-0225].

The notice must also state that the acquisition may be subject to the act and that the acquiring person will file notification with the Federal Trade Commission and the Assistant Attorney General [@CL-MNA-0226]. It must state the anticipated date of receipt of the notification under § 803.10(c) [@CL-MNA-0227]. It must state that the person within which the issuer or unincorporated entity is included may be required to file notification under the act [@CL-MNA-0228].

The affidavit must also state the good faith intention of the person filing notification to make the acquisition and, in the case of a tender offer, that the intention to make the tender offer has been publicly announced [@CL-MNA-0229]. A copy of the written notice that the acquired person received must be attached to the affidavit [@CL-MNA-0232].

### Acquisitions to which § 801.30 does not apply

The notification must contain an affidavit attesting that a contract, agreement in principle, or letter of intent to merge or acquire has been executed [@CL-MNA-0233]. The affidavit must further attest to the good faith intention of the person filing notification to complete the transaction [@CL-MNA-0234]. If the executed agreement is not the definitive agreement, the affidavit must attest that a dated document that provides sufficient detail about the scope of the entire transaction that the parties intend to consummate has also been submitted [@CL-MNA-0235].

### Examples in paragraph (a)(2)

In examples 2 through 5 the section assumes that one percent of the target's shares are valued at $15 million [@CL-MNA-1025]. In example 2, the acquiring company holds 100,000 shares, has a good faith intention to acquire 900,000 more, and states in its notice that it will hold 1,000,000 shares [@CL-MNA-1026]. If those 1,000,000 shares are 20 percent of the target's outstanding voting securities, the enforcement agencies will deem the statement a notification for the $100 million threshold (as adjusted) [@CL-MNA-0245]. In example 3, the acquiring company intends to acquire voting securities of the target, does not know exactly how many shares it will acquire, knows it will definitely acquire in excess of $50 million (as adjusted) worth, and may acquire 50 percent of the target's shares [@CL-MNA-1027]. Its notice would meet paragraph (a)(1)(iii) if it states, among other things, either of two wordings [@CL-MNA-0246; @CL-MNA-0247; @CL-MNA-0248]. Each wording states a present good faith intention to acquire in excess of $50 million (as adjusted) of the target's outstanding voting securities and either designates the 50 percent threshold or says that the company may acquire 50 percent or more [@CL-MNA-0247; @CL-MNA-0248]. The Commission would deem either wording as intending to give notice for the 50 percent threshold [@CL-MNA-0249].

In example 4, a notice that states, among other things, that depending on market conditions the company may acquire 100 percent of the target's shares does not comply with § 803.5, because it does not state an intent to meet or exceed any notification threshold [@CL-MNA-0231]. The filing will be considered deficient within the meaning of § 803.10(c)(2) [@CL-MNA-1022]. The same holds in example 5 for a notice that states that the company has commenced a tender offer for up to 55 percent of the target's outstanding voting securities, because the term "up to" does not state such an intent [@CL-MNA-1023; @CL-MNA-1024].

## How it works

The affidavit attests that the issuer or unincorporated entity received the written notice, and a copy of that notice is attached to it [@CL-MNA-0220; @CL-MNA-0232]. A tender offeror may file notification at any time after the intention to make the tender offer has been publicly announced [@CL-MNA-0230]. Where § 801.30 does not apply, the affidavit instead attests the executed agreement and the intention to complete the transaction [@CL-MNA-0233; @CL-MNA-0234]. The foreign person card shows how a filer acting for a refusing foreign person states the refusal in the paragraph (b) affidavit; see [the foreign person card](mna-hsr-foreign-person-refusal-to-file.md) [@CL-MNA-0213].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume § 801.30 applies [@CL-MNA-0220]. A buyer hand-delivers written notice to an officer of the target, its president, at the target's principal executive offices [@CL-MNA-0221]. The notice names the buyer, says it intends to acquire voting securities, names the class of common shares sought and says the buyer would hold 400,000 shares of it as a result [@CL-MNA-0222; @CL-MNA-0223]. It says the acquisition may be subject to the act and that the buyer will file with the two agencies, gives the anticipated date of receipt of the notification, and says that the person within which the target is included may be required to file [@CL-MNA-0226; @CL-MNA-0227; @CL-MNA-0228]. The buyer's notification contains an affidavit that attests that notice, states the buyer's good faith intention to make the acquisition, and has a copy of the notice attached [@CL-MNA-0220; @CL-MNA-0229; @CL-MNA-0232]. Answer: the affidavit covers each item the section lists for a notification of this kind [@CL-MNA-0220; @CL-MNA-0232].

## Never confuse

The paragraph (a) affidavit attests notice to the issuer or unincorporated entity and states the filer's good faith intention to make the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0220; @CL-MNA-0229]. The paragraph (b) affidavit attests an executed contract, agreement in principle or letter of intent and the intention to complete the transaction [@CL-MNA-0233; @CL-MNA-0234]. In a tender offer, the first affidavit (paragraph (a)(2)) adds the attestation that the intention to make the offer has been publicly announced [@CL-MNA-0229]. In examples 4 and 5, a notice that did not state an intent to meet or exceed any notification threshold did not comply [@CL-MNA-0231; @CL-MNA-1023]. Paragraph (a)(1)(iii) asks for that threshold when the number of voting securities is not known in the case of an issuer [@CL-MNA-0224]. A filing made on behalf of a refusing foreign person has its own affidavit statement of the refusal, described on [the foreign person card](mna-hsr-foreign-person-refusal-to-file.md) [@CL-MNA-0213].
