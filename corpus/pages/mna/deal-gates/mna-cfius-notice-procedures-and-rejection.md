## Purpose

Shows how a voluntary notice is filed, when the Staff Chairperson may defer acceptance, and when the Committee may reject a notice.

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text. Except as otherwise prohibited under paragraph (j) of the notice-procedures section, a party or parties to a proposed or completed transaction may file a voluntary notice of the transaction with the Committee [@CL-MNA-0756]. That paragraph says that no party to a transaction may file a voluntary notice if the transaction has been the subject of a declaration submitted under the declarations subpart and the Committee has not yet taken action on it under the section on Committee responses [@CL-MNA-0799]. A voluntary notice is filed by sending an electronic copy of the notice that includes, in English, the information the notice-contents section sets out, including the required certification, and by paying the fee required under the filing-fee subpart [@CL-MNA-0757]. Notwithstanding the authority of the Staff Chairperson under paragraph (a) of § 800.504 to reject an incomplete notice, the Staff Chairperson may defer acceptance of the notice, and the beginning of the review period specified by § 800.503, to obtain any information required under the section that has not been submitted by the notifying party or parties or other parties to the transaction [@CL-MNA-0077; @CL-MNA-0761].

The Committee, acting through the Staff Chairperson, may reject any voluntary notice that does not comply with the notice-procedures section or the notice-contents section and so inform the parties promptly in writing [@CL-MNA-0758]. One listed ground for rejecting a voluntary notice at any time is that, after submission and before action by the Committee or the President has been concluded, there is a material change in the transaction as to which notification has been made, and the Committee must so inform the parties promptly in writing [@CL-MNA-0759]. One listed ground for rejecting a voluntary notice before the conclusion of a review or investigation is that one of the parties submitting it has not submitted the final certification required by the notice-contents section, and the Committee must so inform the parties promptly in writing [@CL-MNA-0760]. One listed ground for rejecting a voluntary notice at any time after it has been accepted is that the submitting party or parties do not provide follow-up information requested by the Staff Chairperson within three business days of the request, or within a longer time frame if the parties so request in writing and the Staff Chairperson grants that request in writing, and the Committee must so inform the parties promptly in writing [@CL-MNA-0806; @CL-MNA-0078; @CL-MNA-0807]. Rejection for an insufficient filing fee is covered on the [filing fee card](mna-cfius-filing-fee-valuation.md) [@CL-MNA-0769].

## How it works

A voluntary notice is filed by sending the electronic copy with the required information and certification and by paying the fee [@CL-MNA-0757]. Notwithstanding the authority under paragraph (a) of § 800.504 to reject an incomplete notice, the Staff Chairperson may defer acceptance and the start of the review period specified by § 800.503 to obtain missing information required under the section [@CL-MNA-0077; @CL-MNA-0761]. The Committee may reject a notice that does not comply with the notice sections [@CL-MNA-0758], after a material change in the transaction [@CL-MNA-0759], if a party has not submitted the final certification [@CL-MNA-0760], or after acceptance if follow-up information requested by the Staff Chairperson is not provided within three business days of the request (or within a longer time frame requested and granted in writing) [@CL-MNA-0806; @CL-MNA-0078; @CL-MNA-0807]. What happens after acceptance is on the [review and investigation card](mna-cfius-review-and-investigation.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Foreign Buyer B and Seller S file a joint notice that leaves out information about classified work. The Staff Chairperson may reject the notice, or defer acceptance and the start of the review until the information is supplied. Later, B tells the Committee that it will also buy a division that was first left out. Answer: that is a material change in the transaction as originally notified, so the Committee may reject the notice.

## Never confuse

Deferral and rejection are different tools: notwithstanding the authority to reject an incomplete notice, deferral holds back acceptance and the start of the review period while required information is obtained [@CL-MNA-0077; @CL-MNA-0761], while rejection is the response to a notice that fails a listed test [@CL-MNA-0758]. A voluntary notice is also not a declaration; see the [voluntary declarations card](mna-cfius-voluntary-declarations-and-notices.md), and see the [declaration contents card](mna-cfius-declaration-contents-and-rejection.md) for the rejection of a declaration.
