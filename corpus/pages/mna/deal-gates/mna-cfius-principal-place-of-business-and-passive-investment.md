## Purpose

Defines where an entity's principal place of business is, and when ownership interests are held or acquired solely for the purpose of passive investment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | The location of an entity, and a person who holds or acquires ownership interests [@CL-MNA-0029; @CL-MNA-0035] |

The location test looks to where management directs the entity, and the passive test looks to the intent, rights, access, purpose and actions of the holder [@CL-MNA-0029; @CL-MNA-0035]. The glossary gives each full definition.

| Term | Full definition |
|---|---|
| Principal place of business | Paragraph (a), subject to paragraph (b): the primary location where an entity's management directs, controls or coordinates the entity's activities, or, for an investment fund, where the fund's activities are primarily directed, controlled or coordinated by or on behalf of the general partner, managing member or equivalent [@CL-MNA-0029; @CL-MNA-0030]. Paragraph (b): if the location under paragraph (a) is in the United States and the entity has represented to the United States Government, a subnational government of the United States or any foreign government, in its most recent submission or filing to that government (other than one to the Committee) that identifies its principal place of business, principal office and place of business, address of principal executive offices, address of headquarters or equivalent, that any of them is outside the United States, the location in that submission or filing is deemed the principal place of business, unless the entity can demonstrate that the location has changed to the United States since the submission or filing [@CL-MNA-0031; @CL-MNA-0032; @CL-MNA-0033; @CL-MNA-0034] |
| Solely for the purpose of passive investment | Ownership interests are held or acquired solely for the purpose of passive investment if the person holding or acquiring them does not plan or intend to exercise control and (1) is not afforded any rights that if exercised would constitute control, (2) does not acquire any access, rights or involvement specified in § 800.211(b), (3) does not possess or develop any purpose other than passive investment, and (4) does not take any action inconsistent with holding or acquiring the interests solely for the purpose of passive investment [@CL-MNA-0035; @CL-MNA-0036; @CL-MNA-0037] |

## How it works

Start with paragraph (a): find where management directs, controls or coordinates the entity's activities, or, for a fund, where its general partner, managing member or equivalent primarily directs it [@CL-MNA-0029; @CL-MNA-0030].

Paragraph (b) applies only if that location is in the United States [@CL-MNA-0031]. It then looks at the entity's most recent submission or filing, other than one to the Committee, to the United States Government, a subnational government of the United States or any foreign government that identifies a principal place of business, headquarters or equivalent [@CL-MNA-0031; @CL-MNA-0032]. If that filing says any of those is outside the United States, the filed location is deemed the principal place of business [@CL-MNA-0033; @CL-MNA-0034]. The entity can displace the deemed location by demonstrating that the location has changed to the United States since the filing [@CL-MNA-0034].

For the passive test, the holder must not plan or intend to exercise control, and must meet each of the four conditions in the glossary [@CL-MNA-0035; @CL-MNA-0036; @CL-MNA-0037]. The definition refers the reader to § 800.302(b) [@CL-MNA-0037].

The section gives one example. Corporation A, a foreign person, acquires a voting interest in Corporation B, a United States business [@CL-MNA-0567]. Corporation A also negotiates the right to appoint a member of Corporation B's board of directors. The section says the acquisition is not solely for the purpose of passive investment [@CL-MNA-0568].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A holding company is organized abroad and its board has met abroad, but its most recent filing with a foreign regulator named a headquarters address in that country. It then opens an office in the United States, and its management now directs the company's activities from that office, so the location under paragraph (a) is in the United States [@CL-MNA-0029]. Paragraph (b) applies because the company represented to a foreign government, in a filing other than one to the Committee, that its headquarters is outside the United States [@CL-MNA-0031; @CL-MNA-0032; @CL-MNA-0033]. The address in that filing is deemed its principal place of business unless the company demonstrates that the location changed to the United States after the filing [@CL-MNA-0034]. The company bears the demonstration. If it shows that the move to the United States came after the filing, the deemed foreign location does not apply [@CL-MNA-0034].

A fund holds a minority stake. It plans no control, is afforded no right that would constitute control, gets none of the access or involvement in § 800.211(b), has no other purpose and takes no inconsistent action. It meets the stated conditions, so its interest is held solely for the purpose of passive investment [@CL-MNA-0035; @CL-MNA-0036; @CL-MNA-0037]. A second fund holds the same stake but also negotiates the right to appoint a board member. The section's example treats that as not solely passive [@CL-MNA-0568].

## Never confuse

A passive interest is not defined by size. The conditions the section lists turn on intent, rights, access, purpose and action, and none of them states a size limit [@CL-MNA-0035; @CL-MNA-0036; @CL-MNA-0037].

The deemed location in paragraph (b) is not the same as the location in paragraph (a). Paragraph (b) replaces the paragraph (a) location only in the case described, and not where the entity demonstrates the change [@CL-MNA-0031; @CL-MNA-0034].
