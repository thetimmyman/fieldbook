## Purpose

Defines three terms used in the foreign investment review rules: material nonpublic technical information, substantive decisionmaking, and targets or tailors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Information, decisions and marketing tested under the three definitions [@CL-MNA-0836; @CL-MNA-0839; @CL-MNA-0841] |

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text.

Material nonpublic technical information is information that provides knowledge, know-how, or understanding, in each case not available in the public domain, of the design, location, or operation of covered investment critical infrastructure, including vulnerability information such as that related to physical security or cybersecurity [@CL-MNA-0087; @CL-MNA-0836]. A second listed kind is information that is not available in the public domain and is necessary to design, fabricate, develop, test, produce, or manufacture a critical technology, including processes, techniques, or methods [@CL-MNA-0837]. The term does not include financial information regarding the performance of an entity [@CL-MNA-0838].

Substantive decisionmaking is the process through which decisions regarding significant matters affecting an entity are undertaken, including, as applicable, pricing, sales, and specific contracts, including the license, sale, or transfer of sensitive personal data to any third party, including pursuant to a customer, vendor, or joint venture agreement [@CL-MNA-0839; @CL-MNA-0088]. The term does not include strictly administrative decisions [@CL-MNA-0840]. The listed matters also include supply arrangements, corporate strategy and business development, research and development including location and budget allocation, and manufacturing locations [@CL-MNA-0922]. They also include access to critical technologies, covered investment critical infrastructure, material nonpublic technical information or sensitive personal data, including pursuant to a customer, vendor or joint venture agreement [@CL-MNA-0923]. They also include physical and cyber security protocols, including the storage and protection of critical technologies, covered investment critical infrastructure or sensitive personal data [@CL-MNA-0924]. The definition lists further matters, on practices for sensitive personal data and on strategic partnerships, that this card does not restate.

Targets or tailors means customizing products or services for use by a person or group of persons or actively marketing to or soliciting a person or group of persons [@CL-MNA-0841]. The term is used in one of the listed business tests for sensitive personal data [@CL-MNA-0827], which is on the [sensitive personal data card](mna-cfius-sensitive-personal-data.md).

## How it works

Each term is a test used elsewhere in the rules: what information is held, what decisions are made, or how a business markets itself [@CL-MNA-0087; @CL-MNA-0836; @CL-MNA-0839; @CL-MNA-0841]. Material nonpublic technical information covers information not available in the public domain of either listed kind, including vulnerability information for covered investment critical infrastructure, and excludes financial information regarding the performance of an entity [@CL-MNA-0087; @CL-MNA-0836; @CL-MNA-0837; @CL-MNA-0838]. Substantive decisionmaking covers decisions regarding significant matters affecting an entity, including the listed matters, and does not include strictly administrative decisions [@CL-MNA-0839; @CL-MNA-0088; @CL-MNA-0922; @CL-MNA-0923; @CL-MNA-0924; @CL-MNA-0840]. Targets or tailors means customizing products or services for use by a person or group of persons or actively marketing to or soliciting a person or group of persons [@CL-MNA-0841]. The [covered investments card](mna-cfius-covered-investments.md) explains where these terms matter, and this card does not restate it.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Company T runs a control system that is covered investment critical infrastructure. Its source code is not public. A foreign investor receives the source code under an investment agreement. Separately, the investor receives only T's quarterly revenue figures.

Answer: the source code is information of the first kind, because it gives understanding of the design or operation of covered infrastructure that is not public. The revenue figures are financial information about performance and are excluded. A right to approve which customers T may serve concerns significant matters, while a decision that is strictly administrative is excluded.

## Never confuse

Material nonpublic technical information does not include financial information regarding the performance of an entity [@CL-MNA-0838]. Substantive decisionmaking does not include strictly administrative decisions [@CL-MNA-0840]. Targets or tailors means customizing products or services for use by, or actively marketing to or soliciting, a person or group of persons [@CL-MNA-0841].
