## Purpose

Shows which contracting officer owns a novation request and the steps that officer follows from the written request to the signed modification.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MNA-0350] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1202 and FAR 42.1203 [@CL-MNA-0350; @CL-MNA-0354] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.901 [@CL-MNA-0971; @CL-MNA-0973]; RFO FAR 42.902 [@CL-MNA-0976; @CL-MNA-0981] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0350] |
| Applies to | Novation and change-of-name agreements, which go to the responsible contracting officer [@CL-MNA-0350; @CL-MNA-0354] |

The responsible contracting officer is set by the rules in the section [@CL-MNA-0350]. If any of the affected contracts held by the transferor have been assigned to an administrative contracting officer, that officer is responsible or, if affected contracts are in more than one plant or division of the transferor, the one responsible for the corporate office [@CL-MNA-0351; @CL-MNA-0451]. If none of those contracts has been assigned to such an officer, the contracting officer responsible for the largest unsettled dollar balance of contracts is responsible, and unsettled means unbilled plus billed but unpaid [@CL-MNA-0352]. With several transferors, it is the administrative contracting officer administering the largest unsettled dollar balance [@CL-MNA-0353], or else the contracting officer or administrative contracting officer the agency with the largest unsettled dollar balance designates, if none of the affected contracts has been assigned to such an officer [@CL-MNA-0368].

A contractor that wants the Government to recognize a successor in interest or a name change must send a written request to that officer [@CL-MNA-0354]. A contractor that received its contract through the Committee for Purchase from People Who Are Blind or Severely Disabled uses the procedures at 8.716 instead of the written request [@CL-MNA-0450]. After the agreement is distributed, the officer prepares a Standard Form 30 that summarizes it and attaches a complete list of the contracts affected [@CL-MNA-0363].

## How it works

The officer identifies the information needed to evaluate the proposed agreement and asks the contractor to submit it [@CL-MNA-0355]. For a proposed successor-in-interest agreement, the officer notifies each affected contract administration office and contracting office and gives them a list of all affected contracts [@CL-MNA-0356], then requests comments or objections within 30 days after notification [@CL-MNA-0357]. Upon receipt of the necessary information, the officer decides whether recognition is in the Government's interest [@CL-MNA-0358], on the basis of the comments received from the affected contract administration offices and contracting offices [@CL-MNA-0452], the proposed successor's responsibility as a prospective contractor [@CL-MNA-0453] and any factor about the successor's performance of Government contracts that the Government determines would impair its ability to perform satisfactorily [@CL-MNA-0359]. The officer must ensure that Government counsel has reviewed novation and change-of-name agreements for legal sufficiency before they are executed [@CL-MNA-0362], and any separate agreement between the transferor and transferee regarding the assumption of liabilities (for example, long-term incentive compensation plans, cost accounting standards noncompliances, environmental cleanup costs, and final overhead costs) should be referenced specifically in the novation agreement [@CL-MNA-1566; @CL-MNA-0361]. Signing does not stop the officer from using other methods for other issues in the transfer, including the treatment of costs [@CL-MNA-0360].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs it, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.901 says that, if any of the affected contracts held by the transferor have been assigned to an administrative contracting officer, the responsible contracting officer will be the assigned administrative contracting officer [@CL-MNA-0971; @CL-MNA-0351] or the one responsible for the corporate office if affected contracts are in more than one plant or division of the transferor [@CL-MNA-0972; @CL-MNA-0451], and that if none have been assigned it will be the contracting officer responsible for the largest unsettled (unbilled plus billed but unpaid) dollar balance of contracts [@CL-MNA-0973; @CL-MNA-0352]. With several transferors it says the responsible officer is the administrative contracting officer administering the largest unsettled dollar balance [@CL-MNA-0974; @CL-MNA-0353] or, if none of the affected contracts have been assigned to an administrative contracting officer, the contracting officer designated by the agency having the largest unsettled dollar balance, where the codified wording is 'contracting officer (or ACO)' [@CL-MNA-0975; @CL-MNA-0368]. RFO FAR 42.902 says the contractor must submit a written request to the responsible contracting officer [@CL-MNA-0976; @CL-MNA-0354], and that the officer will identify and request the information necessary to evaluate the proposed agreement [@CL-MNA-0978; @CL-MNA-0355], for a proposed agreement for recognizing a successor in interest notify each affected contract administration office and contracting office and provide those offices with a list of all affected contracts [@CL-MNA-0979; @CL-MNA-0356], and request submission of any comments or objections to the proposed transfer within 30 days after notification, and says any submission should be accompanied by supporting documentation [@CL-MNA-0980; @CL-MNA-0357]. It says that, upon receipt of the necessary information, the officer will determine whether recognizing the proposed successor is in the Government's interest on the basis of the comments received from the affected contract administration offices and contracting offices [@CL-MNA-0981; @CL-MNA-0982; @CL-MNA-0358; @CL-MNA-0452], the proposed successor's responsibility under part 9, where the codified text names subpart 9.1, Responsible Prospective Contractors, and any factor relating to its performance of Government contracts that the Government determines would impair its ability to perform satisfactorily [@CL-MNA-0983; @CL-MNA-0453; @CL-MNA-0359]. It also says that executing a novation agreement does not preclude other methods for other issues, including the treatment of costs [@CL-MNA-0984; @CL-MNA-0360], that any separate agreement between the transferor and transferee regarding the assumption of liabilities (for example, long-term incentive compensation plans, cost accounting standards noncompliances, environmental cleanup costs, and final overhead costs) should be referenced specifically in the novation agreement [@CL-MNA-0985; @CL-MNA-1566; @CL-MNA-0361], that the responsible contracting officer must ensure that Government counsel has reviewed the agreements for legal sufficiency before they are executed [@CL-MNA-0986; @CL-MNA-0362], and that, following distribution of the agreement, the officer must prepare a Standard Form 30 summarizing the agreement with a complete list of the contracts affected [@CL-MNA-0987; @CL-MNA-0363]. Where the codified text sends a contractor that received its contract under subpart 8.7 to the procedures at 8.716, the RFO text says that, if the contractor is an AbilityOne Nonprofit Agency, paragraphs (a) through (g) of RFO 42.902 do not apply and the Committee will provide a notice of change to the cognizant contracting officer for execution of an SF-30 in accordance with paragraph (h) [@CL-MNA-0977; @CL-MNA-0450].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A seller is to transfer its assets to a buyer. None of the seller's affected contracts is assigned to an administrative contracting officer. Office X has 500,000 unbilled plus 400,000 billed but unpaid, so 900,000 is unsettled there. Office Y has 700,000 unsettled. The officer at office X is the responsible contracting officer, and the seller sends the written request to that officer. The officer notifies office Y and every other affected office and asks for comments or objections within 30 days after notification.

## Never confuse

This card is about who processes and executes the agreement and in what order. When the Government may recognize a successor at all, and what the contractor must file, is covered in [Novation and change-of-name agreements](../diligence/mna-novation-agreements.md). What a name-change agreement requires is on [Change-of-name agreements](../diligence/mna-change-of-name-agreements.md).

The codified text sends a contractor that received its contract under subpart 8.7 to the procedures at 8.716 [@CL-MNA-0450], while the RFO text says that, for an AbilityOne Nonprofit Agency, paragraphs (a) through (g) of RFO 42.902 do not apply [@CL-MNA-0977].
