## Purpose

Shows what a declaration must contain, how it is certified, and the grounds on which the Committee may reject it or the parties may withdraw it.

| Applies to | Parties submitting a declaration of a transaction [@CL-MNA-0749] |

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text. The party or parties submitting a declaration shall provide the information set out in the declaration-contents section, which must be accurate and complete with respect to all parties and to the transaction [@CL-MNA-0749]. Subject to paragraph (e), the stipulation paragraph, a declaration shall describe or provide, as applicable, the listed items, the first being the names of the foreign persons and United States businesses that are parties to or the subject of the transaction, as well as the name, telephone number and email address of the primary point of contact for each party [@CL-MNA-0751; @CL-MNA-0068]. Under paragraph (e), a party that offers a stipulation under paragraph (c)(3) of that section acknowledges that the Committee and the President are entitled to rely on the stipulation in determining whether the transaction is a covered investment, a covered control transaction or a foreign government-controlled transaction for the purposes of section 721 and all authorities thereunder, and waives the right to challenge any such determination [@CL-MNA-1552; @CL-MNA-1553]. Neither the Committee nor the President is bound by any such stipulation, nor does a stipulation limit the ability of the Committee or the President to act on any authority provided under section 721 with respect to any covered transaction [@CL-MNA-1554]. One listed item of information about the transaction is the total transaction value in U.S. dollars [@CL-MNA-0750]. Each party submitting a declaration shall provide a certification of the information contained in the declaration consistent with § 800.204 [@CL-MNA-0752]. The section lists many further items that this card does not restate.

The Committee, acting through the Staff Chairperson, may reject any declaration that does not comply with the contents section and so inform the parties promptly in writing [@CL-MNA-0753]. One listed ground for rejecting a declaration at any time after submission is that the submitting party or parties do not provide follow-up information requested by the Staff Chairperson within two business days of the request [@CL-MNA-0754], or within a longer time frame if the party or parties so request in writing and the Staff Chairperson grants that request in writing [@CL-MNA-0804]. Parties that have submitted a declaration may request in writing, at any time prior to the Committee taking action under § 800.407, that such declaration be withdrawn [@CL-MNA-0755]. A party or parties may not submit more than one declaration for the same or a substantially similar transaction without approval from the Staff Chairperson [@CL-MNA-0805]. The sections list other grounds for rejection and further rules on withdrawal and refiling that this card does not restate.

## How it works

The submitting parties prepare the listed information and a certification consistent with § 800.204 [@CL-MNA-0749; @CL-MNA-0752]. The Committee may reject a declaration that does not comply with the contents section [@CL-MNA-0753]. It may also reject a declaration at any time after submission if requested follow-up information is not provided within two business days of the request, or within a longer time frame if the party or parties so request in writing and the Staff Chairperson grants that request in writing [@CL-MNA-0754; @CL-MNA-0804]. Parties that have submitted a declaration may request in writing, at any time prior to the Committee taking action under § 800.407, that such declaration be withdrawn [@CL-MNA-0755].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Buyer B and Target T file a declaration. The Staff Chairperson asks on a Monday for a missing organization chart. Answer: if the chart does not arrive within two business days, the Committee may reject the declaration, unless a longer time frame was requested in writing and granted in writing. If B and T drop the deal before the Committee acts, they may ask in writing to withdraw the declaration.

## Never confuse

A declaration is not a written notice; the [voluntary declarations card](mna-cfius-voluntary-declarations-and-notices.md) covers the choice between them, the assessment period and the Committee's responses, and the [notice procedures card](mna-cfius-notice-procedures-and-rejection.md) covers filing and rejecting a notice. A rejection is not a withdrawal: the Committee rejects a declaration [@CL-MNA-0753], while the parties request withdrawal [@CL-MNA-0755].
