## Purpose

Shows what parties must provide to the Committee, how the Committee runs a general assessment, what the Committee must keep confidential and may disclose, and what the printed text says about penalties.

This card rests on the regulations on certain investments in the United States by foreign persons. They are not FAR, DFARS or CAS text.

Parties to a transaction that is notified or declared under subpart D or E of the part shall provide information to the Staff Chairperson that will enable the Committee to conduct a full assessment, review, and/or investigation of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0856]. Parties to a transaction that have filed information with the Committee shall promptly advise the Staff Chairperson of any material changes to such information [@CL-MNA-0933]. The section has further duties, on a transaction with no notice or declaration, on compliance, on requests about misstatements, on subpoenas, on English translations and on certifications, that this card does not restate. For a declaration, the duty to report material changes is also on the [declaration procedures card](mna-cfius-declaration-procedures-withdrawal-and-contingent-interests.md).

The general section on Committee procedure says that in any assessment, review, or investigation of a covered transaction, the Committee should consider the factors specified in section 721(f) and, as appropriate, require the parties to provide to the Committee the information necessary to consider such factors [@CL-MNA-0934]. The Staff Chairperson is the point of contact for receiving material filed with the Committee, including notices and declarations [@CL-MNA-0931]. During an investigation, a party to the transaction under investigation may request a meeting with the Committee staff, and such a request ordinarily will be granted [@CL-MNA-0932]. The later stages of a case are on the [review and investigation card](mna-cfius-review-and-investigation.md).

In response to a request from the Chairperson of the Committee, the Secretary of Labor shall identify for the Committee any risk mitigation provisions proposed to or by the Committee that would violate U.S. employment laws or require a party to violate U.S. employment laws [@CL-MNA-1305]. The Secretary of Labor shall serve no policy role on the Committee [@CL-MNA-1306]. On materiality, the Committee generally will not consider as material minor inaccuracies, omissions, or changes relating to financial or commercial factors not having a bearing on national security [@CL-MNA-1307].

Except as provided in paragraph (b) of the confidentiality section, any information or documentary material filed with the Committee pursuant to the part, including information or documentary material filed under § 800.501(g), shall be exempt from disclosure under the Freedom of Information Act, and no such information or documentary material may be made public [@CL-MNA-0857; @CL-MNA-1563]. The confidentiality paragraph does not prohibit disclosure of information relevant to any administrative or judicial action or proceeding, or of information to Congress or to any duly authorized committee or subcommittee of Congress [@CL-MNA-0858]. The section lists further disclosures that this card does not restate.

A certification given with a filing is made under the penalties of the False Statements Accountability Act of 1996 [@CL-MNA-0847]. The certification is on the [notice contents card](mna-cfius-voluntary-notice-contents-and-certification.md). The provisions of the Defense Production Act Reauthorization of 2003, as amended (50 USC 4555(d)) relating to fines and imprisonment shall apply with respect to the disclosure of information or documentary material filed with the Committee under these regulations [@CL-MNA-0859].

## How it works

Parties to a transaction notified or declared under subpart D or E owe the Staff Chairperson the information the Committee needs to conduct a full assessment, review, and/or investigation, and they promptly advise the Staff Chairperson of material changes to what they filed [@CL-MNA-0856; @CL-MNA-0933]. The Committee considers the factors specified in section 721(f) and, as appropriate, requires the information needed to consider them [@CL-MNA-0934]. Except as paragraph (b) of the confidentiality section provides, material filed under the part, including under § 800.501(g), is exempt from disclosure under the Freedom of Information Act and may not be made public [@CL-MNA-0857; @CL-MNA-1563]. The confidentiality rule does not bar disclosure in listed cases, such as to Congress or in a relevant administrative or judicial action or proceeding [@CL-MNA-0858]. The provisions of the Defense Production Act Reauthorization of 2003, as amended (50 USC 4555(d)) relating to fines and imprisonment apply to disclosure of filed material [@CL-MNA-0859].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Company A files a notice. A reporter then asks the Committee for A's filed documents, and a congressional committee separately asks for the same documents.

Answer: the filed material is generally exempt from public release, so the reporter's request is refused. The paragraph on listed disclosures allows information to go to Congress or a duly authorized committee, so the congressional request is not blocked by the confidentiality rule.

## Never confuse

The duty to provide information is not the confidentiality rule. The first is what parties owe the Committee [@CL-MNA-0856], and the second is how the Committee treats what it receives [@CL-MNA-0857; @CL-MNA-1563]. Confidentiality is also not absolute, because the listed disclosures stay open [@CL-MNA-0858]. The penalty for failing to file a mandatory declaration is on the [mandatory declarations card](mna-cfius-mandatory-declarations.md), and the later stages of a case are on the [review and investigation card](mna-cfius-review-and-investigation.md).

## Company calibration

Who in the company prepares information for the Committee, and whether any ownership or control arrangement limits who may see filed material or the company's finance data. [@Q-045]
