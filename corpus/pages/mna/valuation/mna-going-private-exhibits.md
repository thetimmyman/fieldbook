## Purpose

Shows which categories of documents the exhibits item says to file as exhibits to the schedule, how the exhibit table marks them against the 13E-3, TO and 14D-9 columns, and the rules on omitted schedules, the identity of a financing bank and redaction of personal information [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-1114; @CL-MNA-0440; @CL-MNA-0447; @CL-MNA-0448].

| Applies to | Exhibits to the schedule, by the columns 13E-3, TO and 14D-9 of the exhibit table [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-1114] |

The item lists categories of documents to file as exhibits to the schedule [@CL-MNA-0436]. The exhibit table that follows the item has columns for 13E-3, TO and 14D-9 and marks each category with one, two or three X marks, so the table does not mark every category for every column [@CL-MNA-1114]. The column of a category with fewer than three marks is read from the table itself [@CL-MNA-1114].

The categories are these. Any disclosure materials furnished to security holders by or on behalf of the filing person, including tender offer materials with the transmittal letter, the solicitation or recommendation, the going-private disclosure document, a prospectus used for an exchange offer where the securities are registered under the Securities Act of 1933, and any other disclosure materials [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0437; @CL-MNA-0438]. Any loan agreement referred to in response to the loan paragraph of the financing item [@CL-MNA-0439]. Any report, opinion or appraisal referred to in response to the representative paragraph of the fairness item or the reports and opinions item [@CL-MNA-0441]. Any document setting forth the terms of any agreement, arrangement, understanding or relationship referred to in response to the two schedule items that the subparagraph cites [@CL-MNA-0442]. Any agreement, arrangement or understanding referred to in response to the schedule item that the subparagraph cites, or the pertinent portions of any proxy statement, report or other communication containing the disclosure that item requires [@CL-MNA-0443]. A detailed statement describing security holders' appraisal rights and the procedures for exercising them [@CL-MNA-0444]. Any written instruction, form or other material furnished to persons making an oral solicitation or recommendation by or on behalf of the filing person for their use directly or indirectly in connection with the transaction [@CL-MNA-0445]. Any written opinion prepared by legal counsel at the filing person's request and communicated to the filing person on the tax consequences of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0446].

If the filing relates to a third-party tender offer and a request is made under the loan paragraph of the financing item, the identity of the bank providing financing may be omitted from the loan agreement filed as an exhibit [@CL-MNA-0440]. Schedules or similar attachments to the required exhibits need not be filed if they do not contain information material to an investment or voting decision and that information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document [@CL-MNA-0447]. Each exhibit filed must contain a list briefly identifying the contents of all omitted schedules [@CL-MNA-1115]. A registrant need not prepare a separate list if the information is already included within the exhibit in a manner that conveys the subject matter of the omitted schedules and attachments [@CL-MNA-1116]. The registrant must provide a copy of any omitted schedule to the Commission or its staff upon request [@CL-MNA-1117]. The registrant may redact information from the required exhibits if disclosing it would be a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy, such as bank account numbers, social security numbers and home addresses [@CL-MNA-0448].

## How it works

A filer first finds the column of the exhibit table that matches its schedule, because the table marks the categories against the 13E-3, TO and 14D-9 columns [@CL-MNA-1114]. The documents that the schedule refers to are then filed with it, not only summarised, where a category covers them [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0441; @CL-MNA-0442; @CL-MNA-0443]. Two reliefs apply at the margin. A schedule or attachment to an exhibit may stay out if it is immaterial and not otherwise disclosed, and the exhibit must then list what was omitted, unless it already conveys the subject matter of the omitted schedules, and the registrant must supply a copy on request [@CL-MNA-0447; @CL-MNA-1115; @CL-MNA-1116; @CL-MNA-1117]. Personal information may be redacted [@CL-MNA-0448]. The related cards on the [source and amount of funds](mna-source-and-amount-of-funds.md) and on the [solicitation or recommendation](mna-solicitation-or-recommendation.md) cover the items that some categories cite.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A filer sends holders a proxy statement and gives its callers a written script for an oral solicitation, in a 900 million transaction. The answer: the proxy statement is disclosure material and the script is written material furnished to persons making an oral solicitation, and the exhibit table marks both categories with three marks, so both are filed as exhibits [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0445; @CL-MNA-1114]. The filer also holds a fairness report, a loan agreement and a statement of appraisal rights. Each of those rows has fewer than three marks, so the filer reads its own column of the table before treating each as required [@CL-MNA-1114]. If the merger agreement is filed as a required exhibit [@CL-MNA-0442; @CL-MNA-0443] and has a 40-page schedule of property lists that holds no information material to an investment or voting decision, and whose information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document, the schedule need not be filed [@CL-MNA-0447]. The merger agreement exhibit must then contain a list briefly identifying the schedule's contents, unless the exhibit already conveys the schedule's subject matter [@CL-MNA-1115; @CL-MNA-1116]. The filer must give the Commission or its staff a copy of the schedule on request [@CL-MNA-1117].

## Never confuse

Describing a report in the statement is not the same as filing it: the [reports and opinions item](mna-going-private-reports-opinions-appraisals.md) governs the description, and the exhibits item lists the report itself as an exhibit category [@CL-MNA-0424; @CL-MNA-0426; @CL-MNA-0441]. The detailed statement of appraisal rights is an exhibit category and is separate from the fairness discussion [@CL-MNA-0444; @CL-MNA-0411]. Omitting a schedule does not remove the exhibit: the exhibit stays filed and carries the list of omitted schedules, unless it already conveys their subject matter [@CL-MNA-0447; @CL-MNA-1115; @CL-MNA-1116].
