## Purpose

Shows which categories of documents the exhibits item says to file as exhibits to the schedule, how the exhibit table marks them against the 13E-3, TO and 14D-9 columns, and the rules on omitted schedules, the identity of a financing bank and redaction of personal information [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-1114; @CL-MNA-0440; @CL-MNA-0447; @CL-MNA-0448].

Under 17 CFR 229.1016 (Item 1016), the filing person must file as an exhibit to the schedule the documents in paragraphs (a) through (h) [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0437; @CL-MNA-0438; @CL-MNA-0439; @CL-MNA-0441; @CL-MNA-0442; @CL-MNA-0443; @CL-MNA-0444; @CL-MNA-0445; @CL-MNA-0446]. Under the Exhibit Table to Item 1016 of Regulation M-A (with columns 13E-3, TO, and 14D-9), Disclosure Material, Contracts, Arrangements or Understandings, and Oral Solicitation Materials have three X marks, Loan Agreement has two X marks, and Report, Opinion or Appraisal, Statement re: Appraisal Rights, and Tax Opinion each have one X mark [@CL-MNA-1114].

The exhibit categories are: (a) any disclosure materials furnished to security holders by or on behalf of the filing person, including (1) tender offer materials (including transmittal letter), (2) solicitation or recommendation (including those referred to in Item 1012 of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1012), see also the [solicitation or recommendation](mna-solicitation-or-recommendation.md) card), (3) going-private disclosure document, (4) prospectus used in connection with an exchange offer where securities are registered under the Securities Act of 1933, and (5) any other disclosure materials [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0437; @CL-MNA-0438]; (b) any loan agreement referred to in response to Item 1007(d) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1007(d), see also the [source and amount of funds](mna-source-and-amount-of-funds.md) card) [@CL-MNA-0439]; (c) any report, opinion or appraisal referred to in response to Item 1014(d) or Item 1015 of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1014(d) or § 229.1015) [@CL-MNA-0441]; (d) any document setting forth the terms of any agreement, arrangement, understanding or relationship referred to in response to Item 1005(e) or Item 1011(a)(1) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1005(e) or § 229.1011(a)(1)) [@CL-MNA-0442]; (e) any agreement, arrangement or understanding referred to in response to § 229.1005(d), or the pertinent portions of any proxy statement, report or other communication containing the disclosure required by Item 1005(d) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1005(d)) [@CL-MNA-0443]; (f) a detailed statement describing security holders' appraisal rights and the procedures for exercising those appraisal rights referred to in response to Item 1004(d) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1004(d)) [@CL-MNA-0444]; (g) any written instruction, form or other material that is furnished to persons making an oral solicitation or recommendation by or on behalf of the filing person for their use directly or indirectly in connection with the transaction [@CL-MNA-0445]; and (h) any written opinion prepared by legal counsel at the filing person's request and communicated to the filing person pertaining to the tax consequences of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0446].

Under the Instruction to Item 1016(b), if the filing relates to a third-party tender offer and a request is made under Item 1007(d) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1007(d)), the identity of the bank providing financing may be omitted from the loan agreement filed as an exhibit [@CL-MNA-0440]. Under Instruction 1 to Item 1016, schedules (or similar attachments) to the exhibits required by Item 1016 are not required to be filed provided that they do not contain information material to an investment or voting decision and that information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document [@CL-MNA-0447]. Each exhibit filed must contain a list briefly identifying the contents of all omitted schedules [@CL-MNA-1115]. Registrants need not prepare a separate list of omitted information if such information is already included within the exhibit in a manner that conveys the subject matter of the omitted schedules and attachments [@CL-MNA-1116]. In addition, the registrant must provide a copy of any omitted schedule to the Commission or its staff upon request [@CL-MNA-1117]. Under Instruction 2 to Item 1016, the registrant may redact information from exhibits required to be filed by Item 1016 if disclosure of such information would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (e.g., disclosure of bank account numbers, social security numbers, home addresses and similar information) [@CL-MNA-0448].

## How it works

Item 1016 requires filing as an exhibit to the schedule the documents in paragraphs (a) through (h), and the Exhibit Table to Item 1016 marks those exhibit categories across the 13E-3, TO, and 14D-9 columns [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0439; @CL-MNA-0441; @CL-MNA-0442; @CL-MNA-0443; @CL-MNA-0444; @CL-MNA-0445; @CL-MNA-0446; @CL-MNA-1114]. Under the Instruction to Item 1016(b), if the filing relates to a third-party tender offer and a request is made under Item 1007(d) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1007(d)), the identity of the bank providing financing may be omitted from the loan agreement filed as an exhibit [@CL-MNA-0440]. Under Instruction 1 to Item 1016, schedules (or similar attachments) to the required exhibits are not required to be filed provided that they do not contain information material to an investment or voting decision and that information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document [@CL-MNA-0447], and each exhibit filed must contain a list briefly identifying the contents of all omitted schedules [@CL-MNA-1115], though registrants need not prepare a separate list of omitted information if such information is already included within the exhibit in a manner that conveys the subject matter of the omitted schedules and attachments [@CL-MNA-1116], and the registrant must provide a copy of any omitted schedule to the Commission or its staff upon request [@CL-MNA-1117]. Under Instruction 2 to Item 1016, the registrant may redact information from required exhibits if disclosure of such information would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (e.g., disclosure of bank account numbers, social security numbers, home addresses and similar information) [@CL-MNA-0448].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A filer sends holders a proxy statement and gives its callers a written script for an oral solicitation, in a 900 million transaction. The answer: the proxy statement is disclosure material and the script is written material furnished to persons making an oral solicitation, and the exhibit table marks both categories with three marks, so both are filed as exhibits [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0445; @CL-MNA-1114]. The filer also holds a fairness report, a loan agreement and a statement of appraisal rights. Each of those rows has fewer than three marks, so the filer reads its own column of the table before treating each as required [@CL-MNA-1114]. If the merger agreement is filed as a required exhibit [@CL-MNA-0442; @CL-MNA-0443] and has a 40-page schedule of property lists that holds no information material to an investment or voting decision, and whose information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document, the schedule need not be filed [@CL-MNA-0447]. The merger agreement exhibit must then contain a list briefly identifying the schedule's contents, unless the exhibit already conveys the schedule's subject matter [@CL-MNA-1115; @CL-MNA-1116]. The filer must give the Commission or its staff a copy of the schedule on request [@CL-MNA-1117].

## Never confuse

Under Item 1015(a) and (b), the filing states whether the subject company or affiliate has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party that is materially related to the Rule 13e-3 transaction and furnishes a summary and preparer disclosures (see the [reports and opinions item](mna-going-private-reports-opinions-appraisals.md) card) [@CL-MNA-0424; @CL-MNA-0426; @CL-MNA-0431], whereas Item 1016(c) requires filing as an exhibit to the schedule any report, opinion or appraisal referred to in response to Item 1014(d) or Item 1015 [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0441]. Item 1016(f) requires filing as an exhibit a detailed statement describing security holders' appraisal rights and the procedures for exercising those appraisal rights referred to in response to Item 1004(d) [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0444], whereas Item 1014(b) requires discussing in reasonable detail the material factors upon which the fairness belief stated in Item 1014(a) is based and, to the extent practicable, the weight assigned to each factor [@CL-MNA-0411]. Under Instruction 1 to Item 1016, schedules (or similar attachments) to the required exhibits are not required to be filed provided that they do not contain information material to an investment or voting decision and that information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document, and each exhibit filed must contain a list briefly identifying the contents of all omitted schedules unless such information is already included within the exhibit in a manner that conveys the subject matter of the omitted schedules and attachments [@CL-MNA-0447; @CL-MNA-1115; @CL-MNA-1116].
