## Purpose

Shows the financial information a Regulation M-A statement must carry: audited annual statements, unaudited interim statements, book value per share, pro forma effects of the transaction and a summary.

## The rule

| Applies to | Regulation M-A Item 1010 disclosures of financial information, pro forma information if material, and summary information [@CL-MNA-0320; @CL-MNA-0324; @CL-MNA-0327] |

Under 17 CFR 229.1010(a) (Item 1010(a)), the filing must furnish the following financial information: (1) audited financial statements for the two fiscal years required to be filed with the company's most recent annual report under sections 13 and 15(d) of the Exchange Act [@CL-MNA-0320]; (2) unaudited balance sheets, comparative year-to-date statements of comprehensive income (as defined in § 210.1-02 of Regulation S-X) and related earnings per share data and statements of cash flows [@CL-MNA-0321] required to be included in the company's most recent quarterly report filed under the Exchange Act [@CL-MNA-0322]; and (4) book value per share as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented [@CL-MNA-0323].

## How it works

Under paragraph (b), if material, the filing must furnish pro forma information disclosing the effect of the transaction on [@CL-MNA-0324]: (1) the company's balance sheet as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented under paragraph (a) [@CL-MNA-0325]; (2) the company's statement of comprehensive income and earnings per share for the most recent fiscal year and the latest interim period provided under paragraph (a)(2) [@CL-MNA-1091]; and (3) the company's book value per share as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented under paragraph (a) [@CL-MNA-0326].

Under paragraph (c), the filing must furnish a fair and adequate summary of the information specified in paragraphs (a) and (b) for the same periods specified [@CL-MNA-0327]. A fair and adequate summary includes: (1) the summarized financial information specified in § 210.1-02(bb)(1), (2) income per common share from continuing operations (basic and diluted, if applicable), and (3) net income per common share (basic and diluted, if applicable) [@CL-MNA-1092]; and (5) book value per share as of the date of the most recent balance sheet, and (6) if material, pro forma data for the summarized financial information specified in paragraphs (c)(1) through (c)(5) disclosing the effect of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0328].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company has 10,000,000 shares and equity of 120,000,000 at its latest balance sheet date, so book value is 12 per share. The transaction would add expenses of 20,000,000 that cut equity to 100,000,000, or 10 per share, and the filer treats that effect as material. The filing furnishes the audited statements for the two fiscal years [@CL-MNA-0320], the unaudited statements from the latest quarterly report [@CL-MNA-0321; @CL-MNA-0322] and book value per share of 12 [@CL-MNA-0323]. Because the effect is material, it also furnishes pro forma information on the balance sheet, on comprehensive income and earnings per share, and on book value per share, which is 10 [@CL-MNA-0324; @CL-MNA-0325; @CL-MNA-1091; @CL-MNA-0326]. The summary shows the reported and pro forma figures for the same periods [@CL-MNA-0327; @CL-MNA-1092; @CL-MNA-0328].

## Never confuse

Under Item 1010(b), if material, the filing must furnish pro forma information disclosing the effect of the transaction on the company's balance sheet as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented under paragraph (a), the company's statement of comprehensive income and earnings per share for the most recent fiscal year and the latest interim period provided under paragraph (a)(2), and the company's book value per share as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented under paragraph (a) (see also [Pro forma financial information: how it is prepared](../diligence/mna-sx-pro-forma-preparation.md)) [@CL-MNA-0324; @CL-MNA-0325; @CL-MNA-1091; @CL-MNA-0326]. The unaudited balance sheets, comparative year-to-date statements of comprehensive income (as defined in § 210.1-02 of Regulation S-X) and related earnings per share data and statements of cash flows in paragraph (a)(2) are those required to be included in the company's most recent quarterly report filed under the Exchange Act (see also [Interim financial statements: condensed form, periods and review](../diligence/mna-sx-interim-financial-statements.md)) [@CL-MNA-0321; @CL-MNA-0322], and paragraph (c) requires furnishing a fair and adequate summary of the information specified in paragraphs (a) and (b) for the same periods specified [@CL-MNA-0327].
