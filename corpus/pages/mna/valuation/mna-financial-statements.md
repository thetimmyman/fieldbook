## Purpose

Shows the financial information a Regulation M-A statement must carry: audited annual statements, unaudited interim statements, book value per share, pro forma effects of the transaction and a summary.

| Applies to | Transaction statements under Regulation M-A that must include financial statements [@CL-MNA-0320] |

The item is printed at 17 CFR 229.1010 [@CL-MNA-0320]. Paragraph (a) lists the financial information to be furnished [@CL-MNA-0320]. Paragraph (a)(1) is audited financial statements for the two fiscal years required to be filed with the company's most recent annual report under sections 13 and 15(d) of the Exchange Act [@CL-MNA-0320]. Paragraph (a)(2) is unaudited balance sheets, comparative year-to-date statements of comprehensive income (as defined in Regulation S-X), related earnings per share data and statements of cash flows [@CL-MNA-0321], those required to be included in the company's most recent quarterly report filed under the Exchange Act [@CL-MNA-0322]. Paragraph (a)(4) is book value per share as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented [@CL-MNA-0323].

Each paragraph is an instruction to furnish and names no party. The text speaks of "the company" and does not define it in the item [@CL-MNA-0320; @CL-MNA-0324].

## How it works

Paragraph (b) says that, if material, the filing must furnish pro forma information disclosing the effect of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0324]. That information covers the company's balance sheet as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented under paragraph (a) [@CL-MNA-0325]. It also covers the company's statement of comprehensive income and earnings per share for the most recent fiscal year and the latest interim period provided under paragraph (a)(2) [@CL-MNA-1091]. It also covers the company's book value per share as of the date of the most recent balance sheet presented under paragraph (a) [@CL-MNA-0326].

Paragraph (c) requires the filing to furnish a fair and adequate summary of the information specified in paragraphs (a) and (b) for the same periods specified [@CL-MNA-0327]. A fair and adequate summary includes the summarized financial information specified in § 210.1-02(bb)(1), income per common share from continuing operations and net income per common share, each basic and diluted if applicable [@CL-MNA-1092]. It also includes book value per share as of the date of the most recent balance sheet and, if material, pro forma data for the summarized financial information specified in paragraphs (c)(1) through (c)(5), disclosing the effect of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0328].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company has 10,000,000 shares and equity of 120,000,000 at its latest balance sheet date, so book value is 12 per share. The transaction would add expenses of 20,000,000 that cut equity to 100,000,000, or 10 per share, and the filer treats that effect as material. The filing furnishes the audited statements for the two fiscal years [@CL-MNA-0320], the unaudited statements from the latest quarterly report [@CL-MNA-0321; @CL-MNA-0322] and book value per share of 12 [@CL-MNA-0323]. Because the effect is material, it also furnishes pro forma information on the balance sheet, on comprehensive income and earnings per share, and on book value per share, which is 10 [@CL-MNA-0324; @CL-MNA-0325; @CL-MNA-1091; @CL-MNA-0326]. The summary shows the reported and pro forma figures for the same periods [@CL-MNA-0327; @CL-MNA-1092; @CL-MNA-0328].

## Never confuse

Pro forma information under paragraph (b) shows the effect of this transaction on the company's own balance sheet, statement of comprehensive income and earnings per share, and book value per share [@CL-MNA-0324; @CL-MNA-0325; @CL-MNA-1091; @CL-MNA-0326]. The Regulation S-X rules for preparing pro forma financial information are on a separate card ([Pro forma financial information: how it is prepared](../diligence/mna-sx-pro-forma-preparation.md)). The interim statements in paragraph (a)(2) are those required in the company's most recent quarterly report [@CL-MNA-0322]; the Regulation S-X rules on interim statements are on [Interim financial statements: condensed form, periods and review](../diligence/mna-sx-interim-financial-statements.md). The summary in paragraph (c) condenses the information specified in paragraphs (a) and (b) for the same periods specified [@CL-MNA-0327].
