## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A statement must say about the subject securities that named persons own, recent transactions in those securities, the persons employed, retained or compensated to make solicitations or recommendations, and the subject company officers, employees and assets that the filing person uses.

| Applies to | Transaction statements under Regulation M-A that must report interests in the subject securities and persons or assets retained, employed, compensated or used [@CL-MNA-0310; @CL-MNA-0316] |

The items are printed at 17 CFR 229.1008 and 17 CFR 229.1009 [@CL-MNA-0310; @CL-MNA-0316]. Paragraph (a) of Item 1008 requires the filing to state the aggregate number and percentage of subject securities beneficially owned by each person named in response to Item 1003 of Regulation M-A and by each associate and majority-owned subsidiary of those persons [@CL-MNA-0310]. Item 1003 is covered on [Subject company information and filing person background](../diligence/mna-diligence-subject-company-and-filing-person-background.md) [@CL-MNA-0310]. Paragraph (a) also requires the name and address of any associate or subsidiary [@CL-MNA-1082].

Three instructions follow paragraph (a). Beneficial ownership is determined under Rule 13d-3 under the Exchange Act, and the filing must identify the shares that the person has a right to acquire [@CL-MNA-0311]. The information may be based on the number of outstanding securities disclosed in the subject company's most recently available filing with the Commission, unless the filing person has more current information [@CL-MNA-0312]. Information about officers, directors and associates of the subject company must be given to the extent known after making reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0313].

## How it works

Paragraph (b) of Item 1008 requires the filing to describe any transaction in the subject securities during the past 60 days [@CL-MNA-0314]. The description must include, but not necessarily be limited to, the identity of the persons specified in the Instruction who effected the transaction, the date and the amount of securities involved [@CL-MNA-1083], and the price per share and where and how the transaction was effected [@CL-MNA-1084].

Instruction 1 to paragraph (b) lists whose transactions the filing must report [@CL-MNA-1085]. They are the filing person, for all schedules [@CL-MNA-1085]; any person named in Instruction C of the schedule and any associate or majority-owned subsidiary of the issuer or filing person, for all schedules except Schedule 14D-9 [@CL-MNA-1086]; any executive officer, director, affiliate or subsidiary of the filing person, for Schedule 14D-9 [@CL-MNA-1087]; the issuer and any executive officer or director of any subsidiary of the issuer or filing person, for an issuer tender offer on Schedule TO [@CL-MNA-1088]; and the issuer and any pension, profit-sharing or similar plan of the issuer or affiliate filing the schedule, for a going-private transaction on Schedule 13E-3 [@CL-MNA-1089].

Instruction 2 to paragraph (b) says to provide the information if it is available to the filing person when the statement is initially filed [@CL-MNA-1090]. If it is not initially available, it must be obtained and filed with the Commission promptly, but in no event later than three business days after the date of the initial filing, and, if material, disclosed in a manner reasonably designed to inform security holders [@CL-MNA-0315]. The instruction says the procedure is provided to maintain the confidentiality of information in order to avoid possible misuse of inside information [@CL-MNA-1111].

Paragraph (a) of Item 1009 requires the filing to identify all persons and classes of persons directly or indirectly employed, retained or to be compensated to make solicitations or recommendations in connection with the transaction [@CL-MNA-0316]. It also requires a summary of all material terms of employment, retainer or other arrangement for compensation [@CL-MNA-0317].

Paragraph (b) of Item 1009 requires the filing to identify any officer, class of employees or corporate assets of the subject company that has been or will be employed or used by the filing person in connection with the transaction, and to describe the purpose for their employment or use [@CL-MNA-0318]. The instruction to paragraph (b) says to provide all information required by Item 1009 except for the information required by paragraph (a) of that section and Item 1007 of Regulation M-A [@CL-MNA-0319]. Item 1007 is covered on [Source and amount of funds or other consideration](mna-source-and-amount-of-funds.md) [@CL-MNA-0319].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A filing person that is named in response to Item 1003 of Regulation M-A beneficially holds 2,000,000 of 20,000,000 outstanding subject shares, which is 10 percent, using the outstanding count in the subject company's latest filing and no more current figure [@CL-MNA-0310; @CL-MNA-0312]. It bought 100,000 shares 30 days ago at 25 per share on an exchange. That purchase falls inside the past 60 days [@CL-MNA-0314]. The filing describes it, giving who effected it, the date, the amount, the price and where and how it was effected [@CL-MNA-1083; @CL-MNA-1084], and the filing person's own transactions are reported for all schedules [@CL-MNA-1085]. If that information is not available at the first filing, the filing person files it no later than three business days after the first filing [@CL-MNA-0315]. The filing also names an adviser retained to make recommendations in connection with the transaction and summarises its flat fee [@CL-MNA-0316; @CL-MNA-0317].

## Never confuse

The ownership statement and the transaction description are separate duties: paragraph (a) of Item 1008 states the aggregate number and percentage of subject securities beneficially owned, and paragraph (b) describes transactions in the subject securities during the past 60 days [@CL-MNA-0310; @CL-MNA-0314]. Paragraph (a) of Item 1009 concerns persons employed, retained or to be compensated to make solicitations or recommendations, and paragraph (b) concerns subject company officers, employees and assets used by the filing person [@CL-MNA-0316; @CL-MNA-0318].
