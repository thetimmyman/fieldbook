## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A statement must say about the subject securities that named persons own, recent transactions in those securities, the persons employed, retained or compensated to make solicitations or recommendations, and the subject company officers, employees and assets that the filing person uses.

Under 17 CFR 229.1008(a) (Item 1008(a)), the filing must state the aggregate number and percentage of subject securities that are beneficially owned by each person named in response to Item 1003 of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1003, see also [Subject company information and filing person background](../diligence/mna-diligence-subject-company-and-filing-person-background.md)) and by each associate and majority-owned subsidiary of those persons [@CL-MNA-0310], and give the name and address of any associate or subsidiary [@CL-MNA-1082].

Under Instruction 1 to Item 1008(a), for purposes of Item 1008, beneficial ownership is determined in accordance with Rule 13d-3 (§ 240.13d-3) under the Exchange Act, and the filing must identify the shares that the person has a right to acquire [@CL-MNA-0311]. Under Instruction 2 to Item 1008(a), the information required by Item 1008 may be based on the number of outstanding securities disclosed in the subject company's most recently available filing with the Commission, unless the filing person has more current information [@CL-MNA-0312]. Under Instruction 3 to Item 1008(a), the information required by Item 1008 with respect to officers, directors and associates of the subject company must be given to the extent known after making reasonable inquiry [@CL-MNA-0313].

## How it works

Under paragraph (b) of Item 1008, the filing must describe any transaction in the subject securities during the past 60 days [@CL-MNA-0314]. The description of transactions required must include, but not necessarily be limited to: (1) the identity of the persons specified in the Instruction to Item 1008(b) who effected the transaction, (2) the date of the transaction, and (3) the amount of securities involved [@CL-MNA-1083]; and (4) the price per share, and (5) where and how the transaction was effected [@CL-MNA-1084].

Under Instruction 1 to Item 1008(b), the filing must provide the required transaction information for the following persons: (a) the filing person (for all schedules) [@CL-MNA-1085]; (b) any person named in Instruction C of the schedule and any associate or majority-owned subsidiary of the issuer or filing person (for all schedules except Schedule 14D-9 (§ 240.14d-101)) [@CL-MNA-1086]; (c) any executive officer, director, affiliate or subsidiary of the filing person (for Schedule 14D-9 (§ 240.14d-101)) [@CL-MNA-1087]; (d) the issuer and any executive officer or director of any subsidiary of the issuer or filing person (for an issuer tender offer on Schedule TO (§ 240.14d-100)) [@CL-MNA-1088]; and (e) the issuer and any pension, profit-sharing or similar plan of the issuer or affiliate filing the schedule (for a going-private transaction on Schedule 13E-3 (§ 240.13e-100)) [@CL-MNA-1089].

Under Instruction 2 to Item 1008(b), the filing must provide the information required by Item 1008 if it is available to the filing person at the time the statement is initially filed with the Commission [@CL-MNA-1090]. If the information is not initially available, it must be obtained and filed with the Commission promptly, but in no event later than three business days after the date of the initial filing, and if material, disclosed in a manner reasonably designed to inform security holders [@CL-MNA-0315]. The procedure specified by Instruction 2 is provided to maintain the confidentiality of information in order to avoid possible misuse of inside information [@CL-MNA-1111].

Under 17 CFR 229.1009(a) (Item 1009(a)), the filing must identify all persons and classes of persons that are directly or indirectly employed, retained, or to be compensated to make solicitations or recommendations in connection with the transaction [@CL-MNA-0316], and provide a summary of all material terms of employment, retainer or other arrangement for compensation [@CL-MNA-0317].

Under paragraph (b) of Item 1009, the filing must identify any officer, class of employees or corporate assets of the subject company that has been or will be employed or used by the filing person in connection with the transaction, and describe the purpose for their employment or use [@CL-MNA-0318]. Under the Instruction to Item 1009(b), the filing must provide all information required by Item 1009 except for the information required by paragraph (a) of Item 1009 and Item 1007 of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1007, see also [Source and amount of funds or other consideration](mna-source-and-amount-of-funds.md)) [@CL-MNA-0319].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A filing person that is named in response to Item 1003 of Regulation M-A beneficially holds 2,000,000 of 20,000,000 outstanding subject shares, which is 10 percent, using the outstanding count in the subject company's latest filing and no more current figure [@CL-MNA-0310; @CL-MNA-0312]. It bought 100,000 shares 30 days ago at 25 per share on an exchange. That purchase falls inside the past 60 days [@CL-MNA-0314]. The filing describes it, giving who effected it, the date, the amount, the price and where and how it was effected [@CL-MNA-1083; @CL-MNA-1084], and the filing person's own transactions are reported for all schedules [@CL-MNA-1085]. If that information is not available at the first filing, the filing person files it no later than three business days after the first filing [@CL-MNA-0315]. The filing also names an adviser retained to make recommendations in connection with the transaction and summarises its flat fee [@CL-MNA-0316; @CL-MNA-0317].

## Never confuse

Item 1008(a) requires stating the aggregate number and percentage of subject securities that are beneficially owned by each person named in response to Item 1003 of Regulation M-A and by each associate and majority-owned subsidiary of those persons (and giving the name and address of any associate or subsidiary) [@CL-MNA-0310; @CL-MNA-1082], whereas Item 1008(b) requires describing any transaction in the subject securities during the past 60 days for the persons specified in Instruction 1 to Item 1008(b) [@CL-MNA-0314; @CL-MNA-1083; @CL-MNA-1084; @CL-MNA-1085]. Item 1009(a) requires identifying all persons and classes of persons that are directly or indirectly employed, retained, or to be compensated to make solicitations or recommendations in connection with the transaction and summarizing all material terms of employment, retainer or other arrangement for compensation [@CL-MNA-0316; @CL-MNA-0317], whereas Item 1009(b) requires identifying any officer, class of employees or corporate assets of the subject company that has been or will be employed or used by the filing person in connection with the transaction and describing the purpose for their employment or use (except for the information required by Item 1009(a) and Item 1007) [@CL-MNA-0318; @CL-MNA-0319].
