## Purpose

Governs allowable depreciation, amortization, and cost of money for tangible and intangible capital assets following a business combination accounted for under the purchase method.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MNA-0151] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-52 [@CL-MNA-0151] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-52 [@CL-MNA-0961; @CL-MNA-0962; @CL-MNA-0963] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0151] |
| Applies to | Business combinations accounted for under the purchase method, whether or not the contract or subcontract is subject to CAS [@CL-MNA-0151; @CL-MNA-0160] |

For tangible capital assets when the purchase method of accounting for a business combination is used, whether or not the contract or subcontract is subject to CAS, allowable depreciation and cost of money are based on the capitalized asset values measured and assigned in accordance with the cost accounting standard cited in the principle, if allocable, reasonable, and not otherwise unallowable [@CL-MNA-0151; @CL-MNA-0160]. For intangible capital assets, allowable amortization and cost of money shall be limited to the total of the amounts that would have been allowed had the combination not taken place [@CL-MNA-0152].

## How it works

When a contractor accounts for a business combination under the purchase method, depreciation of tangible assets follows capitalized asset values measured and assigned under the cost accounting standards, and amortization of intangible assets is limited to what would have been allowed without the combination [@CL-MNA-0151; @CL-MNA-0152]. For tangible capital assets, allowable depreciation and cost of money are based on those capitalized asset values, if allocable, reasonable, and not otherwise unallowable, whether or not the contract or subcontract is subject to CAS [@CL-MNA-0151; @CL-MNA-0160]. For intangible capital assets, allowable amortization and cost of money are limited to the total of the amounts that would have been allowed had the combination not taken place [@CL-MNA-0152].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.205-52(a) keeps the scope for tangible capital assets under the purchase method, whether or not the contract or subcontract is subject to CAS [@CL-MNA-0961], and keeps the rule that allowable depreciation and cost of money are based on the capitalized asset values measured and assigned under 48 CFR 9904.404-50(d), if allocable, reasonable, and not otherwise unallowable [@CL-MNA-0962]. RFO FAR 31.205-52(b) keeps the limit on amortization and cost of money for intangible capital assets to the total of the amounts that would have been allowed had the combination not taken place [@CL-MNA-0963].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor acquires a company for 20,000,000 in a business combination using the purchase method of accounting. The target company had intangible assets on its books with remaining allowable annual amortization of 100,000. In the purchase price allocation, the contractor revalues these intangible assets upward to reflect a fair market value yielding 300,000 in annual commercial amortization. Under the cost principle, allowable annual amortization for contract costing is strictly capped at 100,000, which is the amount that would have been allowable had the business combination not occurred; the remaining 200,000 is unallowable.

## Never confuse

Tangible capital asset values after a purchase-method business combination follow the capitalized values measured under the cost accounting standards, whereas allowable amortization and cost of money for intangible capital assets are limited to the amounts that would have been allowed had the combination not taken place [@CL-MNA-0151; @CL-MNA-0152]. For how goodwill and depreciation are treated, see [Goodwill](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-goodwill.md) and [Depreciation](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-depreciation.md); this card covers only the limits on capital asset values after a purchase-method business combination.
