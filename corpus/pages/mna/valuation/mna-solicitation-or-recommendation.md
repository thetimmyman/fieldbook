## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A statement must say about the nature of a solicitation or recommendation, the reasons for the position, whether named persons intend to tender, sell, hold or vote, and any recommendation by others.

| Applies to | Transaction statements under Regulation M-A that must state a solicitation or recommendation [@CL-MNA-0339] |

The item is printed at 17 CFR 229.1012 [@CL-MNA-0339]. Paragraph (a) requires the filing to state the nature of the solicitation or the recommendation [@CL-MNA-0339]. If the statement relates to a recommendation, the filing must state whether the filing person is advising holders of the subject securities to accept or reject the tender offer or to take other action [@CL-MNA-0339]. If the filing person is advising other action with respect to the tender offer, the filing must describe the other action recommended [@CL-MNA-0340]. A filing person that is the subject company and is not making a recommendation must state whether the subject company is expressing no opinion and is remaining neutral toward the tender offer or is unable to take a position with respect to it [@CL-MNA-0341].

## How it works

Paragraph (b) requires the filing to state the reasons for the position (including the inability to take a position) stated in paragraph (a) [@CL-MNA-0342]. Conclusory statements such as "The tender offer is in the best interests of shareholders" are not considered sufficient disclosure [@CL-MNA-1108].

Paragraph (c) requires the filing, to the extent known by the filing person after making reasonable inquiry, to state whether the filing person or any executive officer, director, affiliate or subsidiary of the filing person currently intends to tender, sell or hold the subject securities [@CL-MNA-0343] that are held of record or beneficially owned by that person [@CL-MNA-0344].

Paragraph (d) is headed intent to tender or vote in a going-private transaction. It requires the filing, to the extent known by the filing person after making reasonable inquiry, to state whether or not any executive officer, director or affiliate of the issuer (or any person specified in Instruction C to the schedule) [@CL-MNA-0345] currently intends to tender or sell subject securities owned or held by that person [@CL-MNA-1110], and/or how each person currently intends to vote subject securities, including any securities the person has proxy authority for [@CL-MNA-0346]. The filing must state the reasons for the intended action [@CL-MNA-0347].

The instruction to paragraph (d) says to provide the information required by the section if it is available to the filing person at the time the statement is initially filed with the Commission [@CL-MNA-1109]. If the information is not available, it must be filed with the Commission promptly, but in no event later than three business days after the date of the initial filing, and, if material, disclosed in a manner reasonably designed to inform security holders [@CL-MNA-0348].

Paragraph (e) requires the filing, to the extent known by the filing person after making reasonable inquiry, to state whether or not any person specified in paragraph (d) has made a recommendation either in support of or opposed to the transaction and the reasons for the recommendation [@CL-MNA-0349].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subject company is the filing person, and its board recommends that holders accept a third-party tender offer at 40 per share. The board weighed rival indications at 36 and 38. The statement gives the nature of the recommendation and says that the company advises holders to accept [@CL-MNA-0339]. It gives reasons that rest on specifics, namely the 40 price against the 36 and 38 indications, and not on the conclusory line that the offer is in the best interests of shareholders [@CL-MNA-0342; @CL-MNA-1108]. To the extent known after reasonable inquiry, it states whether the company or any of its executive officers, directors, affiliates or subsidiaries currently intends to tender, sell or hold the subject securities that are held of record or beneficially owned by that person [@CL-MNA-0343; @CL-MNA-0344]. Because the transaction is not a going-private transaction, the paragraph (d) statement does not arise [@CL-MNA-0345].

## Never confuse

A solicitation is not a recommendation: the statement must give the nature of whichever it is, and a recommendation must also say whether the filing person advises holders to accept, reject or take other action [@CL-MNA-0339; @CL-MNA-0340]. A neutral position is not silence: a subject company that makes no recommendation must say whether it is expressing no opinion and remaining neutral or is unable to take a position [@CL-MNA-0341]. The paragraph (c) intent statement covers the filing person and its executive officers, directors, affiliates and subsidiaries, while paragraph (d) covers executive officers, directors and affiliates of the issuer (or persons specified in Instruction C) in a going-private transaction [@CL-MNA-0343; @CL-MNA-0345].
