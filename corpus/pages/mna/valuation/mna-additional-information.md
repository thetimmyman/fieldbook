## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A statement must add on material agreements, regulatory requirements and approvals, the anti-trust laws and margin requirements, legal proceedings, compensation tied to the transaction for named executive officers, and other material information.

Under 17 CFR 229.1011(a) (Item 1011(a)), if material to a security holder's decision whether to sell, tender or hold the securities sought in the tender offer, the filing must furnish the information in paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(5) [@CL-MNA-0329].

Paragraph (a)(1) requires any present or proposed material agreement, arrangement, understanding or relationship between the offeror or any of its executive officers, directors, controlling persons or subsidiaries and the subject company or any of its executive officers, directors, controlling persons or subsidiaries [@CL-MNA-0330], other than any agreement, arrangement or understanding disclosed under any other sections of Regulation M-A (§§ 229.1000 through 229.1016) [@CL-MNA-0331]. Under the Instruction to paragraph (a)(1), in an issuer tender offer, the filing must disclose any material agreement, arrangement, understanding or relationship between the offeror and any of its executive officers, directors, controlling persons or subsidiaries [@CL-MNA-0332].

## How it works

If material to a security holder's decision whether to sell, tender or hold the securities sought in the tender offer [@CL-MNA-0329], paragraph (a)(2) requires, to the extent known by the offeror after reasonable investigation, the applicable regulatory requirements which must be complied with or approvals which must be obtained in connection with the tender offer [@CL-MNA-0333]; paragraph (a)(3) requires the applicability of any anti-trust laws (see also the [Premerger notification form, filer and certification](../deal-gates/mna-hsr-filing-and-who-files.md) card) [@CL-MNA-0334]; and paragraph (a)(4) requires the applicability of margin requirements under section 7 of the Act and the applicable regulations [@CL-MNA-0335].

Paragraph (a)(5) requires, if material to a security holder's decision whether to sell, tender or hold the securities sought in the tender offer [@CL-MNA-0329], any material pending legal proceedings relating to the tender offer, including the name and location of the court or agency in which the proceedings are pending, the date instituted, the principal parties, and a brief summary of the proceedings and the relief sought [@CL-MNA-0336]. Under the Instruction to Item 1011(a)(5), a copy of any document relating to a major development (such as pleadings, an answer, complaint, temporary restraining order, injunction, opinion, judgment or order) in a material pending legal proceeding must be furnished promptly to the Commission staff on a supplemental basis [@CL-MNA-0337].

Paragraph (b) requires furnishing the information required by Item 402(t)(2) and (3) of part 229 (§ 229.402(t)(2) and (3)) and in the tabular format set forth in Item 402(t)(1) of part 229 (§ 229.402(t)(1)) with respect to each named executive officer [@CL-MNA-1093] (1) of the subject company in a Rule 13e-3 transaction, or (2) of the issuer whose securities are the subject of a third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-1094], regarding any agreement or understanding, whether written or unwritten, between such named executive officer and the subject company, issuer, bidder, or the acquiring company, as applicable [@CL-MNA-1095], concerning any type of compensation, whether present, deferred or contingent, that is based upon or otherwise relates to the Rule 13e-3 transaction or third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-1096].

Under Instruction 1 to Item 1011(b), the obligation to provide the information in paragraph (b) shall not apply where the issuer whose securities are the subject of the Rule 13e-3 transaction or tender offer is a foreign private issuer, as defined in § 240.3b-4, or an emerging growth company [@CL-MNA-1097], as defined in Rule 405 of the Securities Act (§ 230.405) or Rule 12b-2 of the Exchange Act (§ 240.12b-2) [@CL-MNA-1098].

Under Instruction 2 to Item 1011(b), for purposes of Instruction 1 to Item 402(t)(2), if the disclosure is included in a Schedule 13E-3 (§ 240.13e-100) or Schedule 14D-9 (§ 240.14d-101) [@CL-MNA-1099], the disclosure provided by this table shall be quantified assuming that the triggering event took place on the latest practicable date and that the price per share [@CL-MNA-1100] of the securities of the subject company in a Rule 13e-3 transaction, or of the issuer whose securities are the subject of the third-party tender offer, shall be determined as follows [@CL-MNA-1101]: if the shareholders are to receive a fixed dollar amount, the price per share shall be that fixed dollar amount [@CL-MNA-1102], and if such value is not a fixed dollar amount, the price per share shall be the average closing market price of such securities over the first five business days following the first public announcement of the transaction [@CL-MNA-1103]. Under Instruction 2, the filer must compute the dollar value of in-the-money option awards for which vesting would be accelerated by determining the difference between this price and the exercise or base price of the options [@CL-MNA-1104], include only compensation that is based on or otherwise relates to the subject transaction [@CL-MNA-1105], and apply Instruction 1 to Item 402(t) with respect to those executive officers for whom disclosure was required in the most recent filing by the subject company in a Rule 13e-3 transaction or by the issuer whose securities are the subject of a third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-1106] with the Commission under the Securities Act or Exchange Act that required disclosure pursuant to Item 402(c) [@CL-MNA-1107].

Paragraph (c) requires furnishing such additional material information, if any, as may be necessary to make the required statements, in light of the circumstances under which they are made, not materially misleading [@CL-MNA-0338].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder makes a third-party tender offer for an issuer that is neither a foreign private issuer nor an emerging growth company. Holders will receive a fixed 30 per share. A named executive officer of the issuer has a written agreement with the bidder that gives a cash payment of 3,000,000 on a change of control and accelerates the vesting of 100,000 options with an exercise price of 20. A suit challenging the offer was filed 3 months ago, and the bidder knows that the offer needs a merger clearance filing. The bidder treats all of this as material to holders' decisions.

Under paragraph (a), the filing describes the suit by court, date instituted, parties, summary and relief sought [@CL-MNA-0329; @CL-MNA-0336], and promptly furnishes the Commission staff, on a supplemental basis, a copy of any document relating to a major development in the suit, such as the complaint or a court order [@CL-MNA-0337]. It states the clearance requirement as far as the bidder knows after reasonable investigation [@CL-MNA-0333], and it states whether the anti-trust laws apply [@CL-MNA-0334]. An agreement between bidder and subject company that is already disclosed under another section of Regulation M-A is not repeated [@CL-MNA-0331]. Paragraph (b) applies because the offer is a third-party tender offer for an issuer outside the exclusions [@CL-MNA-1094; @CL-MNA-1097; @CL-MNA-1098], and the written agreement concerns compensation that relates to the offer [@CL-MNA-1095; @CL-MNA-1096]. If the table is included in a Schedule 14D-9, the price per share is the fixed 30 [@CL-MNA-1099; @CL-MNA-1102], the in-the-money option value is 100,000 times the difference of 10, or 1,000,000 [@CL-MNA-1104], and the 3,000,000 payment is included as compensation that relates to the transaction [@CL-MNA-1105].

## Never confuse

Paragraph (a) requires furnishing the information in paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(5) if material to a security holder's decision whether to sell, tender or hold the securities sought in the tender offer [@CL-MNA-0329], and paragraph (a)(1) excludes any agreement, arrangement or understanding disclosed under any other sections of Regulation M-A (§§ 229.1000 through 229.1016) [@CL-MNA-0330; @CL-MNA-0331]. Under the Instruction to paragraph (a)(1), in an issuer tender offer, the filing must disclose any material agreement, arrangement, understanding or relationship between the offeror and any of its executive officers, directors, controlling persons or subsidiaries [@CL-MNA-0332], whereas paragraph (b) requires the information required by Item 402(t)(2) and (3) and in the tabular format set forth in Item 402(t)(1) with respect to each named executive officer (1) of the subject company in a Rule 13e-3 transaction, or (2) of the issuer whose securities are the subject of a third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-1093; @CL-MNA-1094].
