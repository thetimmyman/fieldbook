## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A statement must add on material agreements, regulatory requirements and approvals, the anti-trust laws and margin requirements, legal proceedings, compensation tied to the transaction for named executive officers, and other material information.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Tender offer statements under Regulation M-A that must add material agreements, regulatory and litigation information, and, for paragraph (b), Rule 13e-3 transaction statements [@CL-MNA-0329; @CL-MNA-1094] |

The item is printed at 17 CFR 229.1011 [@CL-MNA-0329]. Paragraph (a) says that, if the information is material to a security holder's decision whether to sell, tender or hold the securities sought in the tender offer, the filing must furnish the information listed in paragraphs (a)(1) to (a)(5) [@CL-MNA-0329]. The paragraph does not say who judges whether the information is material.

Paragraph (a)(1) calls for any present or proposed material agreement, arrangement, understanding or relationship between the offeror or any of its executive officers, directors, controlling persons or subsidiaries and the subject company or any of its executive officers, directors, controlling persons or subsidiaries [@CL-MNA-0330]. It excludes any agreement, arrangement or understanding disclosed under any other section of Regulation M-A [@CL-MNA-0331]. The instruction to paragraph (a)(1) says that, in an issuer tender offer, the filing must disclose any material agreement, arrangement, understanding or relationship between the offeror and any of its executive officers, directors, controlling persons or subsidiaries [@CL-MNA-0332].

## How it works

Paragraph (a)(2) calls for the applicable regulatory requirements that must be complied with or approvals that must be obtained in connection with the tender offer, to the extent known by the offeror after reasonable investigation [@CL-MNA-0333]. Paragraph (a)(3) calls for the applicability of any anti-trust laws [@CL-MNA-0334]. Paragraph (a)(4) calls for the applicability of margin requirements under section 7 of the Act and the applicable regulations [@CL-MNA-0335].

Paragraph (a)(5) calls for any material pending legal proceedings relating to the tender offer, including the name and location of the court or agency, the date instituted, the principal parties, and a brief summary of the proceedings and the relief sought [@CL-MNA-0336]. The instruction to paragraph (a)(5) says that a copy of any document relating to a major development (such as pleadings, an answer, complaint, temporary restraining order, injunction, opinion, judgment or order) in a material pending legal proceeding must be furnished promptly to the Commission staff on a supplemental basis [@CL-MNA-0337]. The instruction names no party who furnishes it.

Paragraph (b) requires the filing to furnish the information required by Item 402(t)(2) and (3) and in the tabular format set forth in Item 402(t)(1), with respect to each named executive officer [@CL-MNA-1093]. The officers covered are those of the subject company in a Rule 13e-3 transaction or of the issuer whose securities are the subject of a third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-1094]. The paragraph applies to any agreement or understanding, whether written or unwritten, between the named executive officer and the subject company, issuer, bidder or acquiring company, as applicable [@CL-MNA-1095], concerning any type of compensation, whether present, deferred or contingent, that is based upon or otherwise relates to the Rule 13e-3 transaction or third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-1096].

Instruction 1 to paragraph (b) says that the obligation does not apply where the issuer whose securities are the subject of the Rule 13e-3 transaction or tender offer is a foreign private issuer, as defined in § 240.3b-4, or an emerging growth company, as defined in Rule 405 of the Securities Act or Rule 12b-2 of the Exchange Act [@CL-MNA-1097; @CL-MNA-1098].

Instruction 2 to paragraph (b) applies if the disclosure is included in a Schedule 13E-3 or Schedule 14D-9 [@CL-MNA-1099]. The table is then quantified assuming that the triggering event took place on the latest practicable date and that the price per share is determined as follows [@CL-MNA-1100; @CL-MNA-1101]. If the shareholders are to receive a fixed dollar amount, that amount is the price per share [@CL-MNA-1102]. If the value is not a fixed dollar amount, the price per share is the average closing market price of the securities over the first five business days following the first public announcement of the transaction [@CL-MNA-1103]. The dollar value of in-the-money option awards for which vesting would be accelerated is the difference between this price and the exercise or base price of the options [@CL-MNA-1104]. Only compensation that is based on or otherwise relates to the subject transaction is included [@CL-MNA-1105]. Instruction 1 to Item 402(t) is applied to the executive officers for whom disclosure was required in the most recent filing by the subject company in a Rule 13e-3 transaction or by the issuer whose securities are the subject of a third-party tender offer, with the Commission under the Securities Act or the Exchange Act, that required disclosure pursuant to Item 402(c) [@CL-MNA-1106; @CL-MNA-1107].

Paragraph (c) requires the filing to furnish such additional material information, if any, as may be necessary to make the required statements, in light of the circumstances under which they are made, not materially misleading [@CL-MNA-0338].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder makes a third-party tender offer for an issuer that is neither a foreign private issuer nor an emerging growth company. Holders will receive a fixed 30 per share. A named executive officer of the issuer has a written agreement with the bidder that gives a cash payment of 3,000,000 on a change of control and accelerates the vesting of 100,000 options with an exercise price of 20. A suit challenging the offer was filed 3 months ago, and the bidder knows that the offer needs a merger clearance filing. The bidder treats all of this as material to holders' decisions.

Under paragraph (a), the filing describes the suit by court, date instituted, parties, summary and relief sought [@CL-MNA-0329; @CL-MNA-0336], and promptly furnishes the Commission staff, on a supplemental basis, a copy of any document relating to a major development in the suit, such as the complaint or a court order [@CL-MNA-0337]. It states the clearance requirement as far as the bidder knows after reasonable investigation [@CL-MNA-0333], and it states whether the anti-trust laws apply [@CL-MNA-0334]. An agreement between bidder and subject company that is already disclosed under another section of Regulation M-A is not repeated [@CL-MNA-0331]. Paragraph (b) applies because the offer is a third-party tender offer for an issuer outside the exclusions [@CL-MNA-1094; @CL-MNA-1097; @CL-MNA-1098], and the written agreement concerns compensation that relates to the offer [@CL-MNA-1095; @CL-MNA-1096]. If the table is included in a Schedule 14D-9, the price per share is the fixed 30 [@CL-MNA-1099; @CL-MNA-1102], the in-the-money option value is 100,000 times the difference of 10, or 1,000,000 [@CL-MNA-1104], and the 3,000,000 payment is included as compensation that relates to the transaction [@CL-MNA-1105].

## Never confuse

Paragraph (a)(3) asks for the applicability of any anti-trust laws [@CL-MNA-0334]. The premerger notification form, filer and certification are on a separate card ([Premerger notification form, filer and certification](../deal-gates/mna-hsr-filing-and-who-files.md)). The materiality condition is stated in paragraph (a), and paragraph (b) is worded without it [@CL-MNA-0329; @CL-MNA-1093]. The instruction to paragraph (a)(1) addresses an issuer tender offer, while paragraph (b) names a Rule 13e-3 transaction and a third-party tender offer [@CL-MNA-0332; @CL-MNA-1094].
