## Purpose

Shows when a contractor may recover the cost of restructuring that follows a business combination under DoD contracts, and which conditions must be met first.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MNA-0369] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 231.205-70 [@CL-MNA-0369] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MNA-0369] |
| Applies to | Contractor external restructuring costs after a business combination that affects the operations of companies not previously under common ownership or control [@CL-MNA-0369; @CL-MNA-0370; @CL-MNA-0371] |

DFARS 231.205-70(a)(1) prescribes policies and procedures for allowing contractor external restructuring costs when savings would result for DoD [@CL-MNA-0369]. Under paragraph (b)(1), business combination means a transaction whereby assets or operations of two or more companies not previously under common ownership or control are combined, whether by merger, acquisition, or sale/purchase of assets [@CL-MNA-0370]. Under paragraph (b)(2), external restructuring activities means restructuring activities occurring after a business combination that affect the operations of companies not previously under common ownership or control [@CL-MNA-0371]. They do not include restructuring activities occurring after a business combination that affect the operations of only one of the companies not previously under common ownership or control, or, when there has been no business combination, restructuring activities undertaken within one company [@CL-MNA-0454]. External restructuring activities are a direct outgrowth of a business combination, and they normally will be initiated within 3 years of the business combination [@CL-MNA-0372].

Under paragraph (c), restructuring costs associated with external restructuring activities shall not be allowed unless: (1) such costs are allowable in accordance with FAR part 31 and DFARS part 231, and (2) an audit of projected restructuring costs and restructuring savings is performed [@CL-MNA-0375]; (3) the cognizant administrative contracting officer (ACO) reviews the audit report and the projected costs and projected savings, and negotiates an advance agreement in accordance with paragraph (d) of the subsection [@CL-MNA-0376]; and (4)(i) the official designated in paragraph (c)(4)(ii) of the subsection determines in writing that the audited projected savings, on a present value basis, for DoD resulting from the restructuring will exceed either (A) the costs allowed by a factor of at least two to one [@CL-MNA-0377], or (B) the costs allowed, and the business combination will result in the preservation of a critical capability that might otherwise be lost to DoD [@CL-MNA-0378]. Under paragraph (b)(4), restructuring costs means the costs, including both direct and indirect, of restructuring activities [@CL-MNA-0155], and for purposes of this definition, if restructuring costs associated with external restructuring activities allocated to DoD contracts are less than $2.5 million, the costs shall not be subject to the audit, review, and determination requirements of paragraph (c)(4) of the subsection [@CL-MNA-0156; @CL-MNA-0374]; instead, the normal rules for determining cost allowability in accordance with FAR part 31 shall apply [@CL-MNA-0455].

## How it works

Under paragraph (b)(4), restructuring costs means the costs, including both direct and indirect, of restructuring activities [@CL-MNA-0155], and restructuring costs that may be allowed include, but are not limited to, severance pay for employees, early retirement incentive payments for employees, employee retraining costs, relocation expense for retained employees, and relocation and rearrangement of plant and equipment [@CL-MNA-0373]. Under paragraph (c)(4)(i), the official designated in paragraph (c)(4)(ii) determines in writing that the audited projected savings, on a present value basis, for DoD resulting from the restructuring will exceed one of two alternatives: (A) the costs allowed by a factor of at least two to one [@CL-MNA-0377]; or (B) the costs allowed, and the business combination will result in the preservation of a critical capability that might otherwise be lost to DoD [@CL-MNA-0378]. Under paragraph (c)(4)(ii)(A), if the amount of restructuring costs is expected to exceed $25 million over a 5-year period, the designated official is the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment) or the Principal Deputy, and this authority may not be delegated below the level of an Assistant Secretary of Defense [@CL-MNA-0379]. Under paragraph (c)(4)(ii)(B), for all other cases, the designated official is the Director of the Defense Contract Management Agency, and the Director may not delegate this authority [@CL-MNA-0380].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Two defense contractors merge. The combined company plans a plant consolidation with severance and equipment moves. Audited projected savings are 50 million against 20 million of restructuring costs to be allowed, a ratio above two to one on a present value basis. Costs are expected to be 20 million over five years, so the Director of the Defense Contract Management Agency is the designated official. If the costs allocated to DoD contracts were only 2 million, the requirements of paragraph (c)(4) would not apply and the normal FAR part 31 allowability rules would.

## Never confuse

Under DFARS 231.205-70(c), restructuring costs associated with external restructuring activities shall not be allowed unless (1) such costs are allowable in accordance with FAR part 31 and DFARS part 231, (2) an audit of projected restructuring costs and restructuring savings is performed [@CL-MNA-0375], (3) the cognizant ACO reviews the audit report and the projected costs and projected savings, and negotiates an advance agreement in accordance with paragraph (d) of the subsection (see also [Advance agreements](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-advance-agreements.md) and [Restructuring costs and the DoD paragraph in the novation agreement](../deal-gates/mna-novation-restructuring-paragraph.md)) [@CL-MNA-0376], and (4)(i) the designated official makes the written savings determination in paragraph (c)(4)(i)(A) or (B) [@CL-MNA-0377; @CL-MNA-0378]; for purposes of the definition of restructuring costs in paragraph (b)(4), if restructuring costs associated with external restructuring activities allocated to DoD contracts are less than $2.5 million, the costs shall not be subject to the audit, review, and determination requirements of paragraph (c)(4), and instead the normal rules for determining cost allowability in accordance with FAR part 31 shall apply [@CL-MNA-0156; @CL-MNA-0374; @CL-MNA-0455]. External restructuring activities mean restructuring activities occurring after a business combination that affect the operations of companies not previously under common ownership or control, and do not include restructuring activities occurring after a business combination that affect the operations of only one of the companies not previously under common ownership or control, or, when there has been no business combination, restructuring activities undertaken within one company (see also [Organization costs and corporate restructuring](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-organization-costs.md) and [Asset valuations resulting from business combinations](../valuation/mna-asset-valuations-business-combinations.md)) [@CL-MNA-0370; @CL-MNA-0371; @CL-MNA-0454].
