## Purpose

Shows what the statement for a Rule 13e-3 (going-private) transaction must say about the purposes of the transaction, any alternatives that the subject company or affiliate considered, the reasons for its structure and timing, and its effects on the subject company, its affiliates and unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-0400; @CL-MNA-0401; @CL-MNA-0402; @CL-MNA-0403].

## The rule

| Applies to | The statement filed for a Rule 13e-3 transaction [@CL-MNA-0400] |

Under 17 CFR 229.1013(a) (Item 1013(a)), the statement must state the purposes for the Rule 13e-3 transaction [@CL-MNA-0400]. Under paragraph (b), if the subject company or affiliate considered alternative means to accomplish the stated purposes, the statement must briefly describe the alternatives and state the reasons for their rejection [@CL-MNA-0401]. Under paragraph (c), the statement must state the reasons for the structure of the Rule 13e-3 transaction and for undertaking the transaction at this time [@CL-MNA-0402]. Under paragraph (d), the statement must describe the effects of the Rule 13e-3 transaction on the subject company, its affiliates and unaffiliated security holders, including the federal tax consequences of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0403].

Under Instruction 1 to Item 1013, conclusory statements will not be considered sufficient disclosure in response to Item 1013 [@CL-MNA-0404]. Under Instruction 2 to Item 1013, the description required by paragraph (d) must include a reasonably detailed discussion of both the benefits and detriments of the Rule 13e-3 transaction to the subject company, its affiliates and unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-0405], and the benefits and detriments of the Rule 13e-3 transaction must be quantified to the extent practicable [@CL-MNA-0406]. Under Instruction 3 to Item 1013, if the statement is filed by an affiliate of the subject company, the description required by paragraph (d) must include, but not be limited to, the effect of the Rule 13e-3 transaction on the affiliate's interest in the net book value and net earnings of the subject company in terms of both dollar amounts and percentages [@CL-MNA-0407; @CL-MNA-0408].

## How it works

Item 1013 requires disclosing the purposes for the Rule 13e-3 transaction, any alternative means considered to accomplish the stated purposes, the reasons for the structure of the Rule 13e-3 transaction and for undertaking the transaction at this time, and the effects of the Rule 13e-3 transaction on the subject company, its affiliates and unaffiliated security holders, including the federal tax consequences of the transaction (see also the [Regulation M-A definitions](../diligence/mna-diligence-regulation-m-a-definitions-and-term-sheet.md) card) [@CL-MNA-0400; @CL-MNA-0401; @CL-MNA-0402; @CL-MNA-0403]. Under paragraph (b), if the subject company or affiliate considered alternative means to accomplish the stated purposes, it must briefly describe the alternatives and state the reasons for their rejection [@CL-MNA-0401]. Under Instruction 1 to Item 1013, conclusory statements will not be considered sufficient disclosure in response to Item 1013 [@CL-MNA-0404]. Under Instructions 2 and 3 to Item 1013, the description required by paragraph (d) must include a reasonably detailed discussion of both the benefits and detriments of the Rule 13e-3 transaction to the subject company, its affiliates and unaffiliated security holders, quantified to the extent practicable, and if the statement is filed by an affiliate of the subject company, the description required by paragraph (d) must include, but not be limited to, the effect of the Rule 13e-3 transaction on the affiliate's interest in the net book value and net earnings of the subject company in terms of both dollar amounts and percentages [@CL-MNA-0405; @CL-MNA-0406; @CL-MNA-0407; @CL-MNA-0408].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company proposes to take itself private at 40 per share and files the statement itself. The draft states the purpose as simplifying the ownership structure and gives reasons for the structure and the timing. It says that no other route was considered, and it asserts that the deal is good for the company and its holders. The answer: no description of alternatives is owed, because none was considered [@CL-MNA-0401]. The effects part fails. The assertion is conclusory [@CL-MNA-0404]. The draft gives no discussion of both benefits and detriments [@CL-MNA-0405] and no quantification, although nothing shows quantification is impracticable [@CL-MNA-0406]. It omits the federal tax consequences [@CL-MNA-0403]. The affiliate paragraph does not arise, because the company, not an affiliate, files [@CL-MNA-0407].

## Never confuse

Item 1013 requires stating the purposes for the Rule 13e-3 transaction, briefly describing any alternative means considered to accomplish the stated purposes and the reasons for their rejection, stating the reasons for the structure of the Rule 13e-3 transaction and for undertaking the transaction at this time, and describing the effects of the Rule 13e-3 transaction on the subject company, its affiliates and unaffiliated security holders, including the federal tax consequences of the transaction and a reasonably detailed discussion of both benefits and detriments quantified to the extent practicable [@CL-MNA-0400; @CL-MNA-0401; @CL-MNA-0402; @CL-MNA-0403; @CL-MNA-0405; @CL-MNA-0406], whereas Item 1014(a) requires stating whether the subject company or affiliate filing the statement reasonably believes that the Rule 13e-3 transaction is fair or unfair to unaffiliated security holders (see the [fairness item](mna-going-private-fairness.md) card) [@CL-MNA-0409].
