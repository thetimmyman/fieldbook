## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A Item 1015 statement for a Rule 13e-3 transaction must state about whether the subject company or affiliate has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party that is materially related to the Rule 13e-3 transaction, about any negotiation or report described in response to Item 1014(d) of Regulation M-A or Item 14(b)(6) of Schedule 14A concerning the terms of the transaction, and about the availability of the report, opinion or appraisal for inspection and copying [@CL-MNA-0424; @CL-MNA-0426; @CL-MNA-0434].

Under 17 CFR 229.1015(a) (Item 1015(a)), the statement must state whether or not the subject company or affiliate has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party that is materially related to the Rule 13e-3 transaction [@CL-MNA-0424], including, but not limited to: any report, opinion or appraisal relating to the consideration or the fairness of the consideration to be offered to security holders or the fairness of the transaction to the issuer or affiliate or to security holders who are not affiliates [@CL-MNA-0425].

Under paragraph (b), for each report, opinion or appraisal described in response to paragraph (a) or any negotiation or report described in response to Item 1014(d) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1014) or Item 14(b)(6) of Schedule 14A (§ 240.14a-101) concerning the terms of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0426]: (1) identify the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative, (2) briefly describe the qualifications of the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative, and (3) describe the method of selection of the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative [@CL-MNA-0427]; (4) describe any material relationship that existed during the past two years or is mutually understood to be contemplated and any compensation received or to be received as a result of the relationship between (i) the outside party, its affiliates, and/or unaffiliated representative and (ii) the subject company or its affiliates [@CL-MNA-0428; @CL-MNA-0429]; (5) if the report, opinion or appraisal relates to the fairness of the consideration, state whether the subject company or affiliate determined the amount of consideration to be paid or whether the outside party recommended the amount of consideration to be paid [@CL-MNA-0430]; and (6) furnish a summary concerning the negotiation, report, opinion or appraisal [@CL-MNA-0431].

Under paragraph (b)(6), the summary must include, but need not be limited to, the procedures followed, the findings and recommendations, the bases for and methods of arriving at such findings and recommendations, instructions received from the subject company or affiliate, and any limitation imposed by the subject company or affiliate on the scope of the investigation [@CL-MNA-0431; @CL-MNA-0432]. Under the Instruction to Item 1015(b), the information called for by paragraphs (b)(1), (2), and (3) must be given with respect to the firm that provides the report, opinion or appraisal rather than the employees of the firm that prepared the report [@CL-MNA-0433]. Under paragraph (c), the filing must furnish a statement to the effect that the report, opinion or appraisal will be made available for inspection and copying at the principal executive offices of the subject company or affiliate during its regular business hours by any interested equity security holder of the subject company or representative who has been so designated in writing [@CL-MNA-0434; @CL-MNA-0435]. This statement also may provide that a copy of the report, opinion or appraisal will be transmitted by the subject company or affiliate to any interested equity security holder of the subject company or representative who has been so designated in writing upon written request and at the expense of the requesting security holder [@CL-MNA-1113; @CL-MNA-1118].

## How it works

Item 1015(a) requires stating whether or not the subject company or affiliate has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party that is materially related to the Rule 13e-3 transaction [@CL-MNA-0424; @CL-MNA-0425]. Paragraph (b) applies to each report, opinion or appraisal described in response to paragraph (a) or any negotiation or report described in response to Item 1014(d) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1014) or Item 14(b)(6) of Schedule 14A (§ 240.14a-101) concerning the terms of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0426]. For each covered report, opinion, appraisal, or negotiation, the filing must identify the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative, briefly describe the qualifications and describe the method of selection of the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative (given with respect to the firm that provides the report, opinion or appraisal rather than the employees of the firm that prepared the report), describe any material relationship that existed during the past two years or is mutually understood to be contemplated and any compensation received or to be received as a result of the relationship between (i) the outside party, its affiliates, and/or unaffiliated representative and (ii) the subject company or its affiliates, and if the report, opinion or appraisal relates to the fairness of the consideration, state whether the subject company or affiliate determined the amount of consideration to be paid or whether the outside party recommended the amount of consideration to be paid [@CL-MNA-0427; @CL-MNA-0428; @CL-MNA-0429; @CL-MNA-0430; @CL-MNA-0433]. Paragraph (b)(6) requires furnishing a summary concerning the negotiation, report, opinion or appraisal that includes, but need not be limited to, the procedures followed, the findings and recommendations, the bases for and methods of arriving at such findings and recommendations, instructions received from the subject company or affiliate, and any limitation imposed by the subject company or affiliate on the scope of the investigation [@CL-MNA-0431; @CL-MNA-0432]. Paragraph (c) requires furnishing a statement that the report, opinion or appraisal will be made available for inspection and copying at the principal executive offices of the subject company or affiliate during its regular business hours by any interested equity security holder of the subject company or representative who has been so designated in writing, and this statement also may provide that a copy will be transmitted by the subject company or affiliate to such a holder or representative upon written request and at the expense of the requesting security holder [@CL-MNA-0434; @CL-MNA-0435; @CL-MNA-1113; @CL-MNA-1118].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An adviser produces a fairness report in a 300 million buyout and is paid 2 million, having also advised on an earlier sale process 18 months before. The draft names the adviser and nothing else. The answer: the filing must also describe the adviser's qualifications and how it was selected [@CL-MNA-0427]. The earlier engagement fell within the past two years, so, if that relationship is material, the filing must describe it and any compensation received or to be received as a result of it; treating the engagement for the report as itself a material relationship, the 2 million fee is compensation to be described [@CL-MNA-0428; @CL-MNA-0429]. Because the report concerns the fairness of the consideration, the filing must state whether the company set the price or the adviser recommended it [@CL-MNA-0430]. It must furnish a summary of the procedures, findings, bases, instructions and any limit on the investigation [@CL-MNA-0431; @CL-MNA-0432]. It must give the statement that the report is available for inspection and copying at the principal executive offices [@CL-MNA-0434; @CL-MNA-0435]. The report is also an exhibit category, as the [exhibits card](mna-going-private-exhibits.md) explains [@CL-MNA-0441].

## Never confuse

Item 1015(a) requires stating whether or not the subject company or affiliate has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party that is materially related to the Rule 13e-3 transaction [@CL-MNA-0424], whereas Item 1016(h) requires filing as an exhibit to the schedule any written opinion prepared by legal counsel at the filing person's request and communicated to the filing person pertaining to the tax consequences of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0436; @CL-MNA-0446]. Item 1014(d) requires stating whether or not a majority of directors who are not employees of the subject company has retained an unaffiliated representative to act solely on behalf of unaffiliated security holders for purposes of negotiating the terms of the Rule 13e-3 transaction and/or preparing a report concerning the fairness of the transaction (see the [fairness item](mna-going-private-fairness.md) card) [@CL-MNA-0414; @CL-MNA-0415], and Item 1015(b) applies to each report, opinion or appraisal described in response to Item 1015(a) or any negotiation or report described in response to Item 1014(d) or Item 14(b)(6) of Schedule 14A concerning the terms of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0426].
