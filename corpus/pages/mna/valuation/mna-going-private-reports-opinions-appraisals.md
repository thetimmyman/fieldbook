## Purpose

Shows what the statement for a going-private transaction must say about any report, opinion or appraisal that an outside party prepared and that is materially related to the transaction, about any negotiation or report by an unaffiliated representative, and about inspection of the document [@CL-MNA-0424; @CL-MNA-0426; @CL-MNA-0434].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | The subject company or affiliate, for each outside report, opinion or appraisal materially related to the transaction [@CL-MNA-0424] |

The statement must say whether or not the subject company or affiliate has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party that is materially related to the transaction [@CL-MNA-0424]. This reaches, but is not limited to, any report, opinion or appraisal on the consideration, on the fairness of the consideration to be offered to security holders, or on the fairness of the transaction to the issuer or affiliate or to security holders who are not affiliates [@CL-MNA-0425].

For each report, opinion or appraisal described in response to that paragraph, and for any negotiation or report on the terms of the transaction that is described in response to the unaffiliated-representative paragraph of the fairness item or to the cited item of the proxy schedule, the filing must give the following [@CL-MNA-0426]. It must identify the outside party and/or unaffiliated representative, briefly describe its or their qualifications and describe the method of selection [@CL-MNA-0427]. It must describe any material relationship that existed during the past two years or is mutually understood to be contemplated, and any compensation received or to be received as a result of it, between the outside party, its affiliates, and/or the unaffiliated representative on one side and the subject company or its affiliates on the other [@CL-MNA-0428; @CL-MNA-0429]. If the report, opinion or appraisal relates to the fairness of the consideration, it must state whether the subject company or affiliate determined the amount of consideration or the outside party recommended it [@CL-MNA-0430]. It must furnish a summary [@CL-MNA-0431].

The summary must include, but need not be limited to, the procedures followed, the findings and recommendations, the bases for and methods of arriving at them, the instructions received from the subject company or affiliate, and any limitation that the subject company or affiliate imposed on the scope of the investigation [@CL-MNA-0431; @CL-MNA-0432]. The identity, qualifications and selection information must be given for the firm that provides the report, opinion or appraisal, not for the employees of the firm who prepared it [@CL-MNA-0433]. The filing must also furnish a statement that the report, opinion or appraisal will be made available for inspection and copying at the principal executive offices of the subject company or affiliate during its regular business hours, to any interested equity security holder of the subject company or a representative designated in writing [@CL-MNA-0434; @CL-MNA-0435]. That statement also may provide that a copy will be sent to such a holder or representative on written request and at the requester's expense [@CL-MNA-1113; @CL-MNA-1118].

## How it works

The first paragraph is a yes-or-no question about receipt of an outside report, opinion or appraisal, and an opinion of counsel is outside it [@CL-MNA-0424]. If the answer is no, the detail paragraph still reaches any negotiation or report described in response to the unaffiliated-representative paragraph of the fairness item or the cited item of the proxy schedule [@CL-MNA-0426]. Where a report exists, the filing says who the party is, its qualifications, how it was selected, any material relationship in the past two years or mutually understood to be contemplated, any compensation, and, where the report, opinion or appraisal relates to the fairness of the consideration, whether the subject company or affiliate determined the amount of consideration or the outside party recommended it [@CL-MNA-0427; @CL-MNA-0428; @CL-MNA-0430]. The summary shows the method and the limits on the investigation [@CL-MNA-0431; @CL-MNA-0432]. The availability statement gives interested holders a way to inspect and copy the document [@CL-MNA-0434; @CL-MNA-0435].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An adviser produces a fairness report in a 300 million buyout and is paid 2 million, having also advised on an earlier sale process 18 months before. The draft names the adviser and nothing else. The answer: the filing must also describe the adviser's qualifications and how it was selected [@CL-MNA-0427]. The earlier engagement fell within the past two years, so, if that relationship is material, the filing must describe it and any compensation received or to be received as a result of it; treating the engagement for the report as itself a material relationship, the 2 million fee is compensation to be described [@CL-MNA-0428; @CL-MNA-0429]. Because the report concerns the fairness of the consideration, the filing must state whether the company set the price or the adviser recommended it [@CL-MNA-0430]. It must furnish a summary of the procedures, findings, bases, instructions and any limit on the investigation [@CL-MNA-0431; @CL-MNA-0432]. It must give the statement that the report is available for inspection and copying at the principal executive offices [@CL-MNA-0434; @CL-MNA-0435]. The report is also an exhibit category, as the [exhibits card](mna-going-private-exhibits.md) explains [@CL-MNA-0441].

## Never confuse

The first paragraph does not reach an opinion of counsel [@CL-MNA-0424]. A written opinion that legal counsel prepared at the filing person's request and communicated to the filing person on the tax consequences is a separate exhibit category [@CL-MNA-0446]. The [fairness item](mna-going-private-fairness.md) asks the filer to say whether a majority of non-employee directors retained an unaffiliated representative [@CL-MNA-0414]. This item then reaches any negotiation or report that is described in response to that paragraph [@CL-MNA-0426].
