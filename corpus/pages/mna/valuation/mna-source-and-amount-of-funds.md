## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A statement must say about the sources and total amount of funds or other consideration for the transaction, the conditions on the financing and any alternative plans, the expenses, and any borrowed funds.

Under 17 CFR 229.1007(a) (Item 1007(a)), the filing must state the specific sources and total amount of funds or other consideration to be used in the transaction [@CL-MNA-0300]. If the transaction involves a tender offer, the filing must disclose the amount of funds or other consideration required to purchase the maximum amount of securities sought in the offer [@CL-MNA-0301].

## How it works

Under paragraph (b), the filing must state any material conditions to the financing discussed in response to paragraph (a), disclose any alternative financing arrangements or alternative financing plans in the event the primary financing plans fall through, and if none, so state [@CL-MNA-0302].

Under paragraph (c), the filing must furnish a reasonably itemized statement of all expenses incurred or estimated to be incurred in connection with the transaction including, but not limited to, filing, legal, accounting and appraisal fees, solicitation expenses and printing costs [@CL-MNA-0303], and state whether or not the subject company has paid or will be responsible for paying any or all expenses [@CL-MNA-0304].

Under paragraph (d), if all or any part of the funds or other consideration required is, or is expected, to be borrowed, directly or indirectly, for the purpose of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0305]: (1) provide a summary of each loan agreement or arrangement containing the identity of the parties, the term, the collateral, the stated and effective interest rates, and any other material terms or conditions of the loan [@CL-MNA-0306]; and (2) briefly describe any plans or arrangements to finance or repay the loan, or, if no plans or arrangements have been made, so state [@CL-MNA-0307].

Under the Instruction to Item 1007(d), if the transaction is a third-party tender offer and the source of all or any part of the funds used in the transaction is to come from a loan made in the ordinary course of business by a bank as defined by section 3(a)(6) of the Act [@CL-MNA-0308], the name of the bank will not be made available to the public if the filing person so requests in writing and files the request, naming the bank, with the Secretary of the Commission [@CL-MNA-0309].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder makes a third-party cash tender offer. Buying every share sought would take 500,000,000, funded by 300,000,000 of cash and a 200,000,000 term loan from a bank, as defined by section 3(a)(6) of the Act, that makes the loan in the ordinary course of business. The statement gives both sources and the 500,000,000 total [@CL-MNA-0300] and the amount needed for the maximum amount of securities sought [@CL-MNA-0301]. It states the material conditions to the financing and says there is no alternative arrangement [@CL-MNA-0302]. It itemises the filing, legal and accounting fees and says whether the subject company has paid or will pay any of them [@CL-MNA-0303; @CL-MNA-0304]. Because part of the funds is borrowed, it summarises the loan's parties, term, collateral and interest rates and briefly describes how the loan will be repaid [@CL-MNA-0305; @CL-MNA-0306; @CL-MNA-0307]. The bidder, as filing person, asks in writing that the bank's name be kept from the public and files the request, naming the bank, with the Secretary of the Commission, so the name will not be made available to the public [@CL-MNA-0308; @CL-MNA-0309].

## Never confuse

Paragraph (b) requires stating any material conditions to the financing discussed in response to paragraph (a) and disclosing any alternative financing arrangements or alternative financing plans in the event the primary financing plans fall through (or, if none, so stating) [@CL-MNA-0302], whereas paragraph (d) requires, if all or any part of the funds or other consideration required is, or is expected, to be borrowed, directly or indirectly, for the purpose of the transaction, providing a summary of each loan agreement or arrangement (containing the identity of the parties, the term, the collateral, the stated and effective interest rates, and any other material terms or conditions of the loan) and briefly describing any plans or arrangements to finance or repay the loan (or, if none have been made, so stating) [@CL-MNA-0305; @CL-MNA-0306; @CL-MNA-0307]. Paragraph (c) requires furnishing a reasonably itemized statement of all expenses incurred or estimated to be incurred in connection with the transaction (including, but not limited to, filing, legal, accounting and appraisal fees, solicitation expenses and printing costs) and stating whether or not the subject company has paid or will be responsible for paying any or all expenses [@CL-MNA-0303; @CL-MNA-0304]. Under the Instruction to Item 1007(d), if the transaction is a third-party tender offer and the source of all or any part of the funds used in the transaction is to come from a loan made in the ordinary course of business by a bank as defined by section 3(a)(6) of the Act, the name of the bank will not be made available to the public if the filing person so requests in writing and files the request, naming the bank, with the Secretary of the Commission [@CL-MNA-0308; @CL-MNA-0309].
