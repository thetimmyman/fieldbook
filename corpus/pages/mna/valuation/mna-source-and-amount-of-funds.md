## Purpose

Shows what a Regulation M-A statement must say about the sources and total amount of funds or other consideration for the transaction, the conditions on the financing and any alternative plans, the expenses, and any borrowed funds.

| Applies to | Transaction statements under Regulation M-A that must give the source and amount of the funds [@CL-MNA-0300] |

The item is printed at 17 CFR 229.1007 [@CL-MNA-0300]. Paragraph (a) requires the filing to state the specific sources and total amount of funds or other consideration to be used in the transaction [@CL-MNA-0300]. If the transaction involves a tender offer, the filing must also disclose the amount of funds or other consideration required to purchase the maximum amount of securities sought in the offer [@CL-MNA-0301].

Paragraphs (a) to (d) are written as instructions to state or furnish and do not say who must file. Paragraph (c) mentions the subject company only as the party whose payment of expenses is to be stated [@CL-MNA-0304]. The instruction to paragraph (d) names the filing person, who makes the request described there [@CL-MNA-0309].

## How it works

Paragraph (b) requires the filing to state any material conditions to the financing discussed in response to paragraph (a), to disclose any alternative financing arrangements or plans in the event the primary financing plans fall through, and to say so if there are none [@CL-MNA-0302].

Paragraph (c) requires a reasonably itemized statement of all expenses incurred or estimated to be incurred in connection with the transaction, including, but not limited to, filing, legal, accounting and appraisal fees, solicitation expenses and printing costs [@CL-MNA-0303]. It also requires the filing to state whether or not the subject company has paid or will be responsible for paying any or all expenses [@CL-MNA-0304].

Paragraph (d) applies if all or any part of the funds or other consideration required is, or is expected to be, borrowed, directly or indirectly, for the purpose of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0305]. The filing must then summarise each loan agreement or arrangement, giving the identity of the parties, the term, the collateral, the stated and effective interest rates and any other material terms or conditions of the loan [@CL-MNA-0306]. It must also briefly describe any plans or arrangements to finance or repay the loan or, if none have been made, say so [@CL-MNA-0307].

The instruction to paragraph (d) applies if the transaction is a third-party tender offer and the source of all or any part of the funds is a loan made in the ordinary course of business by a bank as defined by section 3(a)(6) of the Act [@CL-MNA-0308]. In that case the name of the bank will not be made available to the public if the filing person so requests in writing and files the request, naming the bank, with the Secretary of the Commission [@CL-MNA-0309]. The instruction states no deadline for the request.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder makes a third-party cash tender offer. Buying every share sought would take 500,000,000, funded by 300,000,000 of cash and a 200,000,000 term loan from a bank, as defined by section 3(a)(6) of the Act, that makes the loan in the ordinary course of business. The statement gives both sources and the 500,000,000 total [@CL-MNA-0300] and the amount needed for the maximum amount of securities sought [@CL-MNA-0301]. It states the material conditions to the financing and says there is no alternative arrangement [@CL-MNA-0302]. It itemises the filing, legal and accounting fees and says whether the subject company has paid or will pay any of them [@CL-MNA-0303; @CL-MNA-0304]. Because part of the funds is borrowed, it summarises the loan's parties, term, collateral and interest rates and briefly describes how the loan will be repaid [@CL-MNA-0305; @CL-MNA-0306; @CL-MNA-0307]. The bidder, as filing person, asks in writing that the bank's name be kept from the public and files the request, naming the bank, with the Secretary of the Commission, so the name will not be made available to the public [@CL-MNA-0308; @CL-MNA-0309].

## Never confuse

The conditions in paragraph (b) are not the loan detail in paragraph (d): paragraph (b) asks for material conditions and any alternative financing, and paragraph (d) asks for the loan terms and the plans to finance or repay the loan [@CL-MNA-0302; @CL-MNA-0306; @CL-MNA-0307]. The expense statement in paragraph (c) covers expenses of the transaction, not the funds [@CL-MNA-0303]. The bank-name instruction is stated for a third-party tender offer and for a loan from a bank as defined by section 3(a)(6) of the Act, and it takes a written request [@CL-MNA-0308; @CL-MNA-0309].
