## Purpose

Sets out what a Regulation M-A Item 1014 statement for a Rule 13e-3 transaction must state about whether the subject company or affiliate filing the statement reasonably believes that the Rule 13e-3 transaction is fair or unfair to unaffiliated security holders, the material factors upon which that belief is based, approval of unaffiliated security holders, an unaffiliated representative, approval of directors who are not employees of the subject company, dissenting or abstaining directors, and other offers [@CL-MNA-0409; @CL-MNA-0411; @CL-MNA-0413; @CL-MNA-0414; @CL-MNA-0416; @CL-MNA-0410; @CL-MNA-0417].

| Applies to | The subject company or affiliate filing the statement in a Rule 13e-3 transaction [@CL-MNA-0409] |

Under 17 CFR 229.1014(a) (Item 1014(a)), the statement must state whether the subject company or affiliate filing the statement reasonably believes that the Rule 13e-3 transaction is fair or unfair to unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-0409]. Under Instruction 1 to Item 1014, a statement that the issuer or affiliate has no reasonable belief as to the fairness of the Rule 13e-3 transaction to unaffiliated security holders will not be considered sufficient disclosure in response to paragraph (a) [@CL-MNA-0418]. If any director dissented to or abstained from voting on the Rule 13e-3 transaction, the statement must identify the director, and indicate, if known, after making reasonable inquiry, the reasons for the dissent or abstention [@CL-MNA-0410].

Under paragraph (b), the statement must discuss in reasonable detail the material factors upon which the belief stated in paragraph (a) is based and, to the extent practicable, the weight assigned to each factor [@CL-MNA-0411]. The discussion must include an analysis of the extent, if any, to which the filing person's beliefs are based on the factors described in Instruction 2 to Item 1014, paragraphs (c), (d), and (e) of Item 1014, and Item 1015 of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1015) [@CL-MNA-0412]. Under Instruction 3 to Item 1014, conclusory statements, such as "The Rule 13e-3 transaction is fair to unaffiliated security holders in relation to net book value, going concern value and future prospects of the issuer," will not be considered sufficient disclosure in response to paragraph (b) [@CL-MNA-0449].

Under paragraph (c), the statement must state whether or not the transaction is structured so that approval of at least a majority of unaffiliated security holders is required [@CL-MNA-0413]. Under paragraph (d), the statement must state whether or not a majority of directors who are not employees of the subject company has retained an unaffiliated representative to act solely on behalf of unaffiliated security holders for purposes of negotiating the terms of the Rule 13e-3 transaction and/or preparing a report concerning the fairness of the transaction [@CL-MNA-0414; @CL-MNA-0415]. Under paragraph (e), the statement must state whether or not the Rule 13e-3 transaction was approved by a majority of the directors of the subject company who are not employees of the subject company [@CL-MNA-0416].

Under paragraph (f), if any offer of the type described in paragraph (viii) of Instruction 2 to Item 1014 has been received, the statement must describe the offer and state the reasons for its rejection [@CL-MNA-0417]. Paragraph (viii) of Instruction 2 covers firm offers of which the subject company or affiliate is aware made by any unaffiliated person, other than the filing persons, during the past two years for: (A) the merger or consolidation of the subject company with or into another company, or vice versa [@CL-MNA-0422]; (B) the sale or other transfer of all or any substantial part of the assets of the subject company; or (C) a purchase of the subject company's securities that would enable the holder to exercise control of the subject company [@CL-MNA-0423].

## How it works

Under Instruction 2 to Item 1014, the factors that are important in determining the fairness of a transaction to unaffiliated security holders and the weight, if any, that should be given to them in a particular context will vary [@CL-MNA-1112]. Normally such factors will include, among others, those referred to in paragraphs (c), (d), and (e) of Item 1014 and whether the consideration offered to unaffiliated security holders constitutes fair value in relation to [@CL-MNA-0419]: (i) current market prices, (ii) historical market prices, (iii) net book value, (iv) going concern value, and (v) liquidation value [@CL-MNA-0420]; (vi) purchase prices paid in previous purchases disclosed in response to Item 1002(f) of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1002(f)), and (vii) any report, opinion, or appraisal described in Item 1015 of Regulation M-A (§ 229.1015) [@CL-MNA-0421]; and (viii) firm offers of which the subject company or affiliate is aware made by any unaffiliated person, other than the filing persons, during the past two years for (A) the merger or consolidation of the subject company with or into another company, or vice versa [@CL-MNA-0422], (B) the sale or other transfer of all or any substantial part of the assets of the subject company, or (C) a purchase of the subject company's securities that would enable the holder to exercise control of the subject company [@CL-MNA-0423]. In addition to stating whether or not the conditions in paragraphs (c), (d), and (e) are met, the discussion under paragraph (b) must include an analysis of the extent, if any, to which the filing person's beliefs are based on the factors in Instruction 2, paragraphs (c), (d), and (e), and Item 1015 [@CL-MNA-0412; @CL-MNA-0413; @CL-MNA-0414; @CL-MNA-0415; @CL-MNA-0416].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company offers 25 per share against a market price of 18 and a net book value of 12 per share. The draft states only that the board believes the deal is fair. The answer: the draft states a belief, but a bare assertion is conclusory and the draft discusses no material factors or weights [@CL-MNA-0449; @CL-MNA-0411]. Normally the factors include whether the 25 is fair value in relation to current and historical market prices, net book value, going concern value and liquidation value, so the discussion should address them [@CL-MNA-0419; @CL-MNA-0420]. The draft must also say whether approval by a majority of unaffiliated holders is required, whether a majority of the non-employee directors retained an unaffiliated representative, and whether a majority of the non-employee directors approved the deal [@CL-MNA-0413; @CL-MNA-0414; @CL-MNA-0416]. If any director dissented or abstained, the draft must identify that director [@CL-MNA-0410].

## Never confuse

Item 1014(a) requires stating whether the subject company or affiliate filing the statement reasonably believes that the Rule 13e-3 transaction is fair or unfair to unaffiliated security holders [@CL-MNA-0409], whereas Item 1015(a) requires stating whether or not the subject company or affiliate has received any report, opinion (other than an opinion of counsel) or appraisal from an outside party that is materially related to the Rule 13e-3 transaction (see the [reports and opinions item](mna-going-private-reports-opinions-appraisals.md) card), and Item 1014(b) requires an analysis of the extent, if any, to which the filing person's beliefs are based on Item 1015 [@CL-MNA-0424; @CL-MNA-0412]. Paragraphs (c), (d), and (e) of Item 1014 require stating whether or not approval of at least a majority of unaffiliated security holders is required, an unaffiliated representative was retained, and a majority of non-employee directors approved the transaction, while paragraph (b) requires discussing in reasonable detail the material factors upon which the belief stated in paragraph (a) is based, including an analysis of the extent, if any, to which the filing person's beliefs are based on paragraphs (c), (d), and (e) [@CL-MNA-0411; @CL-MNA-0412; @CL-MNA-0413; @CL-MNA-0414; @CL-MNA-0415; @CL-MNA-0416]. Item 1013(d) requires describing the effects of the Rule 13e-3 transaction on the subject company, its affiliates and unaffiliated security holders, including the federal tax consequences of the transaction (see the [purposes item](mna-going-private-purposes-alternatives-reasons-effects.md) card) [@CL-MNA-0403].
