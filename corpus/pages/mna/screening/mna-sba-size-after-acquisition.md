## Purpose

Shows how an acquisition, a former affiliate or a segregable division changes the annual receipts and the employee count SBA uses to decide a concern's size.

| Applies to | Concerns that acquired an affiliate, were acquired as an affiliate, had a former affiliate whose affiliation ceased before the date used for determining size, or acquired or sold a segregable division during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which they self-certified as small [@CL-MNA-0516; @CL-MNA-0518; @CL-MNA-0519; @CL-MNA-0520; @CL-MNA-0521; @CL-MNA-0523; @CL-MNA-0524] |

Under 13 CFR 121.104(d)(2), if a concern has acquired an affiliate or been acquired as an affiliate during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which it self-certified as small, the annual receipts used in determining size status includes the receipts of the acquired or acquiring concern [@CL-MNA-0516]. Under 13 CFR 121.106(b)(4)(i), if a concern has acquired an affiliate or been acquired as an affiliate during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which it self-certified as small, the employees counted in determining size status include the employees of the acquired or acquiring concern [@CL-MNA-0521].

## How it works

Where the annual receipts used in determining size status include the receipts of the acquired or acquiring concern, this aggregation applies for the entire period of measurement, not just the period after the affiliation arose [@CL-MNA-0517], and where the employees counted in determining size status include the employees of the acquired or acquiring concern, this aggregation likewise applies for the entire period of measurement, not just the period after the affiliation arose [@CL-MNA-0522]. However, if a concern has acquired a segregable division of another business concern during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which it self-certified as small, the annual receipts used in determining size status do not include the receipts of the acquired division prior to the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0518]. Under 13 CFR 121.104(d)(4), the annual receipts of a former affiliate are not included if affiliation ceased before the date used for determining size, and this exclusion of annual receipts of such former affiliate applies during the entire period of measurement, rather than only for the period after which affiliation ceased [@CL-MNA-0519]; under 13 CFR 121.106(b)(4)(ii), the employees of a former affiliate are not counted if affiliation ceased before the date used for determining size, and this exclusion of employees of a former affiliate applies during the entire period of measurement, rather than only for the period after which affiliation ceased [@CL-MNA-0523]. However, if a concern has sold a segregable division to another business concern during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which it self-certified as small, the annual receipts used in determining size status will continue to include the receipts of the division that was sold [@CL-MNA-0520], and the employees used in determining size status will continue to include the employees of the division that was sold [@CL-MNA-0524].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A small contractor has average annual receipts of 4 million. It buys another concern whose average annual receipts were 3 million over the same years, and the two are now affiliates. It also buys a segregable division with 1 million of receipts, before it self-certifies as small.

Answer: the acquired concern's 3 million counts for the whole measurement period, not only after closing, so the receipts used are 7 million. The division's 1 million is not counted for the years before the acquisition.

## Never confuse

If a concern has acquired a segregable division of another business concern during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which it self-certified as small, the annual receipts used in determining size status do not include the receipts of the acquired division prior to the acquisition [@CL-MNA-0518], whereas if a concern has sold a segregable division to another business concern during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which it self-certified as small, the annual receipts used in determining size status will continue to include the receipts of the division that was sold [@CL-MNA-0520] and the employees used in determining size status will continue to include the employees of the division that was sold [@CL-MNA-0524]. Likewise, if a concern has acquired an affiliate or been acquired as an affiliate during the applicable period of measurement or before the date on which it self-certified as small, the aggregation of the acquired or acquiring concern's receipts and employees applies for the entire period of measurement, not just the period after the affiliation arose [@CL-MNA-0516; @CL-MNA-0517; @CL-MNA-0521; @CL-MNA-0522], whereas the annual receipts and employees of a former affiliate are not included or counted if affiliation ceased before the date used for determining size, during the entire period of measurement rather than only for the period after which affiliation ceased [@CL-MNA-0519; @CL-MNA-0523].
