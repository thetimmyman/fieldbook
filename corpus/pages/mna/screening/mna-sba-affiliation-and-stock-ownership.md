## Purpose

Shows how the Small Business Administration decides that concerns are affiliates, so a buyer or seller can see whose size is counted together.

## The rule

| Applies to | Concerns whose size SBA determines, counted together with all their domestic and foreign affiliates [@CL-MNA-0505] |

This card rests on an SBA regulation for small business programs, 13 CFR 121.103, and not on the FAR [@CL-MNA-0500]. Concerns and entities are affiliates of each other when one controls or has the power to control the other, or a third party or parties controls or has the power to control both, whether or not control is exercised [@CL-MNA-0500]. In deciding a concern's size, SBA counts the receipts, employees, or other measure of size of that concern and of all its domestic and foreign affiliates, regardless of whether the affiliates are organized for profit [@CL-MNA-0505].

Stock ownership gives three tests. A person that owns or has the power to control 50 percent or more of a concern's voting stock, or a block that is large compared to other blocks, controls or has the power to control the concern [@CL-MNA-0506]. The presumption rule covers two or more persons who each own, control or have the power to control less than 50 percent of a concern's voting stock [@CL-MNA-0553]. Where those minority holdings are equal or approximately equal in size and together are large as compared with any other stock holding, SBA presumes that each such person controls or has the power to control the concern whose size is at issue [@CL-MNA-0507], and that presumption may be rebutted by a showing that the control does not in fact exist [@CL-MNA-0508]. If voting stock is widely held and no block is large, the Board of Directors and CEO or President are deemed to have the power to control, absent evidence to the contrary [@CL-MNA-0509].

## How it works

SBA looks at the totality of the circumstances and may find affiliation even though no single factor is enough [@CL-MNA-0504]. Negative control includes, but is not limited to, instances where a minority shareholder can, under the charter, by-laws or shareholder's agreement, prevent a quorum or otherwise block action by the board of directors or shareholders [@CL-MNA-0501]. SBA will not find negative control where the minority shareholder's blocking authority concerns the extraordinary circumstances that the rule lists [@CL-MNA-0502]. The list includes adding a new equity stakeholder or increasing an equity stakeholder's investment, dissolution of the company, sale of the company or all of its assets, and the merger of the company [@CL-MNA-0503]. The list continues with the company declaring bankruptcy and amendment of the company's corporate governance documents to remove the shareholder's authority to block any of the circumstances listed before it [@CL-MNA-0550]. Another listed item is any other extraordinary action crafted solely to protect the investment of the minority shareholders, and not to impede the majority's ability to control the concern's operations or to conduct its business as it chooses [@CL-MNA-0551]. A buyer or seller screens each investor's blocking rights against that list.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A target has two investors. Investor A holds 30 percent of the voting stock and Investor B holds 30 percent. The rest is spread across many holders with no more than 5 percent each. Investor A's shareholder agreement lets it veto only a sale of the company and a merger.

Answer: neither investor reaches 50 percent, but the aggregate of the two equal blocks is large compared with any other stock holding, so SBA presumes each controls the target unless the investor shows that control does not in fact exist. A veto limited to a sale of the company and a merger falls within the extraordinary-circumstances list, so SBA will not find that the investor has negative control because of that veto.

## Never confuse

Affiliation is an SBA size concept. It is not a FAR representation about owners and it is not a novation. The contract side of a deal sits on the [Predecessor of Offeror provision](../diligence/mna-predecessor-of-offeror.md) card and the [Novation and change-of-name agreements](../diligence/mna-novation-agreements.md) card. Inside the SBA rule, SBA will not find negative control where a minority holder's blocking authority concerns the listed extraordinary actions [@CL-MNA-0502].
