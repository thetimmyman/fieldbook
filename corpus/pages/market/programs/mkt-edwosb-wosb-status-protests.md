## Purpose

Shows who may protest a firm's status as an economically disadvantaged women-owned small business (EDWOSB) or a women-owned small business (WOSB) eligible under the Women-Owned Small Business Program, the time limits, and what SBA decides.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0589] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.308 [@CL-MKT-0589] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0589] |
| Applies to | Protests of an offeror's EDWOSB or WOSB status [@CL-MKT-0589; @CL-MKT-0590] |

For sole source acquisitions, the contracting officer or SBA may protest the offeror's status as an economically disadvantaged women-owned small business concern or as a women-owned small business concern eligible under the Women-Owned Small Business Program [@CL-MKT-0589]. For all other acquisitions, an interested party may protest the apparent successful offeror's status [@CL-MKT-0590]. An interested party that wants to protest both size and WOSB or EDWOSB status shall file two separate protests [@CL-MKT-0654].

An offeror shall submit its protest to the contracting officer to be received by the close of business by the fifth business day after bid opening for sealed bid acquisitions [@CL-MKT-0591]. For negotiated acquisitions, one listed deadline is the close of business by the fifth business day after receipt of the special notification that identifies the apparently successful offeror [@CL-MKT-0592]. Any protest received after the designated time limit is untimely, unless it is from the contracting officer or SBA [@CL-MKT-0593].

## How it works

SBA determines the merits of the status protest within 15 business days after receipt of a protest, or within any extension of that time granted by the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-0594]. SBA notifies the contracting officer, the protester and the protested concern, and its determination is effective immediately and final unless overturned on appeal by OHA [@CL-MKT-0595]. OHA must receive an appeal no later than 10 business days after the date of receipt of the protest determination, and SBA will dismiss an untimely appeal [@CL-MKT-0596]. The section also covers the grounds SBA will consider, award before a determination, the effect of a sustained protest and the steps of an appeal, and this card does not restate them.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A women-owned set-aside draws a status protest, and the contracting officer withholds award. SBA sustains the protest and the protested concern receives the determination on a Monday. OHA receives the concern's appeal 9 business days later. The appeal is timely because it arrived inside the 10 business days. Without an appeal, the contracting officer may not award to the protested concern.

## Never confuse

Here SBA decides the status protest first [@CL-MKT-0594], and its determination is final unless overturned on appeal by OHA [@CL-MKT-0595], while for service-disabled veteran-owned protests OHA decides the merits [@CL-MKT-0587]. The service-disabled route is on the [Protesting a firm's service-disabled veteran-owned small business status](mkt-sdvosb-status-protests.md) card. A protest about size is not a status protest, and a party that wants both shall file two separate protests [@CL-MKT-0654]. The size route is on the [Protesting a small business representation](../taxonomy/mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) card. The conditions for a set-aside or sole-source award under the program are on the [Women-Owned Small Business Program set-asides and sole-source awards](mkt-wosb-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) card.
