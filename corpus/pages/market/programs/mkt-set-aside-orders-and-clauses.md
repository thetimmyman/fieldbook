## Purpose

Shows when orders under multiple-award contracts may be set aside and which provision or clause goes in which solicitations and contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0603] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.504 and FAR 19.507 [@CL-MKT-0602; @CL-MKT-0603]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-MKT-9241; @CL-MKT-9242] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0603] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for set-asides, reserves and multiple-award contracts, clause by clause [@CL-MKT-0603; @CL-MKT-0608] |

Contracting officers may, at their discretion, set aside orders placed under multiple-award contracts for any of the small business concerns identified in 19.000(a)(3) [@CL-MKT-0602]. The contracting officer shall state in the solicitation and resulting contract whether order set-asides will be discretionary or mandatory when the conditions in 19.502-2 are met at the time of order set-aside and the specific program eligibility requirements, as applicable, are also then met [@CL-MKT-0637]. When setting aside an order above the simplified acquisition threshold, the contracting officer shall first consider setting aside the order for the small business socioeconomic contracting programs, which the section names as 8(a), HUBZone, service-disabled veteran-owned small business and women-owned small business, before considering a small business set-aside [@CL-MKT-0638; @CL-MKT-9349]. Under a partial set-aside contract, only small business concerns awarded contracts for the portions that were set aside under the solicitation for the multiple-award contract may compete for orders issued under those portions [@CL-MKT-0639]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-6, Notice of Total Small Business Set-Aside, in solicitations and contracts involving total small business set-asides [@CL-MKT-0603]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-7, Notice of Partial Small Business Set-Aside, in solicitations and contracts involving partial small business set-asides [@CL-MKT-0604].

The FAR definitions section defines a multiple-award contract as a contract that is one of three alternatives. The first is a Multiple Award Schedule contract issued by GSA or by agencies granted Multiple Award Schedule contract authority by GSA as described in FAR part 38 [@CL-MKT-9241]. The second is a multiple-award task-order or delivery-order contract issued in accordance with FAR subpart 16.5, including Governmentwide acquisition contracts, and the third is any other indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contract entered into with two or more sources pursuant to the same solicitation [@CL-MKT-9242]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines a multiple-award contract through the same three alternatives [@CL-MKT-9269; @CL-MKT-9270; @CL-MKT-9241; @CL-MKT-9242].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting, in the solicitations and contracts that the paragraph then lists [@CL-MKT-0605]. One listed case is supplies, services, and construction, if any portion of the requirement is to be set aside for small business and the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-MKT-0606]. A further listed case is any solicitation or contract that is set aside or awarded on a sole-source basis in accordance with subparts 19.8, 19.13, 19.14, or 19.15, regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0607]. For contracts that are set aside, the contracting officer shall indicate in paragraph (f) of the clause on limitations on subcontracting whether compliance with the limitations on subcontracting is required at the contract or order level [@CL-MKT-0640]. Under FAR 19.507(e)(2), the contracting officer also inserts the clause at 52.219-14 in solicitations and contracts using the HUBZone price evaluation preference, but does not insert the clause in the resultant contract if the prospective contractor waived the use of the price evaluation preference or is an other than small business [@CL-MKT-1822]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-13, Notice of Set-Aside of Orders, in all solicitations for multiple-award contracts under which orders may be set aside for any of the small business concerns identified in 19.000(a)(3), and all contracts awarded from such solicitations [@CL-MKT-0608].

## How it works

For multiple-award contracts that have reserves, the contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.219-31, Notice of Small Business Reserve, in the solicitations [@CL-MKT-0609]. The contracting officer shall also insert the clause at 52.219-32, Orders Issued Directly Under Small Business Reserves, in solicitations and the resulting multiple-award contracts that have reserves [@CL-MKT-0610]. Under FAR 19.507(h)(1), the contracting officer inserts the clause at 52.219-33, Nonmanufacturer Rule, in solicitations and contracts, including multiple-award contracts, when orders may be set aside for small business concerns as described in 8.405-5 and 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F), and when orders may be issued directly to a small business concern as described in 19.504(c)(1)(ii), when both of the following conditions are met [@CL-MKT-1823; @CL-MKT-1831]: first, the item being acquired has been assigned a manufacturing or supply NAICS code [@CL-MKT-0641]; and second, any portion of the requirement is to be set aside for small business and is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, or is to be set aside or awarded on a sole-source basis in accordance with subparts 19.8, 19.13, 19.14, or 19.15 regardless of dollar value, or uses the HUBZone price evaluation preference (except that the clause is not inserted in the resultant contract if the prospective contractor waived the preference or is an other than small business) [@CL-MKT-0642; @CL-MKT-0643]. The contracting officer shall not insert the clause when SBA has waived the nonmanufacturer rule [@CL-MKT-0611].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buying office plans a multiple-award contract in which part of the requirement is set aside for small business and orders may be set aside. The partial set-aside clause goes in. The clause on notice of set-aside of orders goes in because orders may be set aside. If the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, the limitations on subcontracting clause goes in as well.

## Never confuse

Orders may be set aside at the contracting officer's discretion [@CL-MKT-0602], although the solicitation and contract state whether order set-asides will be discretionary or mandatory when the stated conditions are met [@CL-MKT-0637]. A reserve brings its own provision and clause [@CL-MKT-0609; @CL-MKT-0610], and the rules for reserves are on the [Set-aside determinations, rejected SBA recommendations and small business reserves](mkt-set-aside-determinations-sba-recommendations-and-reserves.md) card. The nonmanufacturer rule clause is a different clause from the limitations on subcontracting clause; see the [Limitations on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule](mkt-limitations-on-subcontracting-and-nonmanufacturer-rule.md) card.
