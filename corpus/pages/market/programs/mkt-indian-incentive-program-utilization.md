## Purpose

Explains, from the clause text, the contractor's duty to use best efforts to give Indian organizations and Indian-owned economic enterprises subcontract opportunities, the adjustment to the prime contract, the incentive payment, and how an eligibility challenge stops payment. The defense supplement version is on the [DFARS utilization of Indian organizations and Native Hawaiian small business concerns](mkt-dfars-indian-and-native-hawaiian-subcontracting-incentive.md) card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1017] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.226-1 [@CL-MKT-1017] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.226-1 [@CL-MKT-0177; @CL-MKT-0186] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1017] |
| Applies to | The subcontracts the contractor awards [@CL-MKT-1022] |

The contractor shall use its best efforts to give Indian organizations and Indian-owned economic enterprises the maximum practicable opportunity to participate in the subcontracts it awards, to the fullest extent consistent with efficient performance of its contract [@CL-MKT-1022]. An Indian organization is the governing body of any Indian tribe or an entity established or recognized by the governing body of an Indian tribe for the purposes of a chapter of title 25 of the United States Code that the clause cites [@CL-MKT-1082]. An Indian is any person who is a member of any Indian tribe, band, group, pueblo or community recognized by the Federal Government as eligible for services from the Bureau of Indian Affairs, and any Native as defined in the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act [@CL-MKT-1101; @CL-MKT-1102]. An Indian tribe is any Indian tribe, band, group, pueblo or community, including native villages and native groups as defined in that Act, that the Federal Government recognizes as eligible for services from the Bureau of Indian Affairs [@CL-MKT-1103; @CL-MKT-1104]. An Indian-owned economic enterprise is any Indian-owned commercial, industrial, or business activity, as determined by the Secretary of the Interior, established or organized for the purpose of profit, provided that Indian ownership constitutes not less than 51 percent of the enterprise [@CL-MKT-1083]. An interested party is a prime contractor or an actual or prospective offeror whose direct economic interest would be affected by the award of a subcontract or by the failure to award a subcontract [@CL-MKT-1084].

## How it works

Subject to the terms and conditions of the contract and the availability of funds, the contracting officer will authorize an incentive payment of 5 percent of the amount paid to the subcontractor [@CL-MKT-1017]. The contracting officer will seek funding in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-MKT-1089]. The clause also provides for an adjustment to the prime contract of 5 percent of the estimated cost, target cost, or firm-fixed-price included in the subcontract initially awarded to the Indian organization or Indian-owned economic enterprise [@CL-MKT-1018]. The contractor may request the adjustment to the estimated cost of a cost-type contract or to the target cost of a cost-plus-incentive-fee prime contract [@CL-MKT-1087]. It may also request it to the target cost and ceiling price of a fixed-price incentive prime contract or to the price of a firm-fixed-price prime contract [@CL-MKT-1088]. The contractor has the burden of proving the amount claimed and must assert its request for an adjustment prior to completion of contract performance [@CL-MKT-1021].

The contracting officer and the contractor, acting in good faith, may rely on the representation of an Indian organization or Indian-owned economic enterprise as to its eligibility, unless an interested party challenges its status or the contracting officer has independent reason to question that status [@CL-MKT-1019]. If a subcontractor's representation is challenged, the contracting officer will refer the matter to the Bureau of Indian Affairs of the Department of the Interior [@CL-MKT-1085]. That bureau will determine the eligibility and notify the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1086]. No incentive payment will be made within 50 working days of subcontract award or while a challenge is pending [@CL-MKT-1020]. If a subcontractor is determined to be an ineligible participant, no incentive payment will be made under the Indian Incentive Program [@CL-MKT-1023].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the definitions of Indian organization and Indian-owned economic enterprise [@CL-MKT-0173; @CL-MKT-1082; @CL-MKT-0174; @CL-MKT-1083]. Its definitions of Indian and Indian tribe describe the same persons and tribes in much the same words but print different statute references [@CL-MKT-0172; @CL-MKT-0175]. The Indian definition cites section 1452 of title 25 and section 1602 of title 43 of the United States Code [@CL-MKT-9001], where the codified definition cites section 1452(c) of title 25 and section 1601 of title 43 [@CL-MKT-1101; @CL-MKT-1102]. The Indian tribe definition ends with a separate sentence citing section 1452 of title 25 [@CL-MKT-9002], where the codified sentence cites section 1542(c) of title 25 [@CL-MKT-1103; @CL-MKT-1104]. Its definition of interested party says "failure to receive a subcontract" where the codified definition says "failure to award a subcontract" [@CL-MKT-0176; @CL-MKT-1084]. It says the contractor shall use its best efforts to give Indian organizations and Indian-owned economic enterprises maximum practicable opportunity to participate in its subcontracts "while still efficiently performing its contract", where the codified text says "to the fullest extent consistent with efficient performance of its contract" [@CL-MKT-0177; @CL-MKT-1022]. The RFO text lets the Contracting Officer and the Contractor rely on a representation of eligibility unless an interested party challenges the status or the Contracting Officer has independent reason to question it, without the codified words "acting in good faith" [@CL-MKT-0178; @CL-MKT-1019]. A challenged representation is referred to the Bureau of Indian Affairs, and the RFO paragraph names the Bureau's Acquisition Management Director and then gives a web address for that office [@CL-MKT-0179; @CL-MKT-9337; @CL-MKT-1085], where the codified text names the Chief, Division of Contracting and Grants Administration, and then gives a street address [@CL-MKT-9000; @CL-MKT-9338]. The Bureau will determine the eligibility and notify the Contracting Officer [@CL-MKT-0180; @CL-MKT-1086]. The RFO text says the Contractor shall not make an incentive payment within 50 working days of subcontract award or while a challenge is pending, and shall not make one if a subcontractor is determined to be ineligible, where the codified text says that no incentive payment will be made [@CL-MKT-0180; @CL-MKT-1020; @CL-MKT-0181; @CL-MKT-1023]. The RFO text lets the contractor request an adjustment to the estimated cost of a cost-type prime contract, the target cost of a cost-plus-incentive-fee prime contract, the target cost and ceiling price of a fixed-price incentive prime contract, and the price of a firm-fixed-price prime contract, where the codified text says "cost-type contract" [@CL-MKT-0182; @CL-MKT-1087; @CL-MKT-0183; @CL-MKT-1088]. The RFO text sets the adjustment at 5 percent of the estimated cost, target cost, or firm-fixed-price in the subcontract first awarded to the Indian organization or Indian-owned economic enterprise [@CL-MKT-0184], where the codified text says "initially awarded" [@CL-MKT-1018]; the meaning is the same. It says the Contractor must prove the amount claimed and must request an adjustment before completing contract performance, where the codified text says the contractor has the burden of proving the amount claimed and must assert its request prior to completion of contract performance; the meaning is the same [@CL-MKT-0185; @CL-MKT-1021]. Paragraph (c) says the Contracting Officer, subject to the terms and conditions of the contract and the availability of funds, will authorize an incentive payment of 5 percent of the amount paid to the subcontractor, and it has no sentence about seeking funding in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-MKT-0186; @CL-MKT-1017; @CL-MKT-1089].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subcontract initially awarded to an Indian-owned economic enterprise has a firm-fixed-price of 400,000, under a firm-fixed-price prime contract. The contractor asks for an adjustment to the prime contract price of 5 percent of 400,000, which is 20,000, and asserts the request before performance is complete. By the end of performance the contractor has paid the subcontractor 380,000, so the incentive payment the contracting officer may authorize, subject to the contract's terms and the availability of funds, is 5 percent of 380,000, which is 19,000. No incentive payment is made within 50 working days of the subcontract award or while a challenge to the subcontractor's status is pending, and none is made if the subcontractor is found to be an ineligible participant. Under the RFO text, the Contractor shall not make an incentive payment in those cases [@CL-MKT-0180; @CL-MKT-0181].

## Never confuse

The two 5 percent figures have different bases [@CL-MKT-1018; @CL-MKT-1017]. The adjustment is 5 percent of the estimated cost, target cost, or firm-fixed-price in the subcontract initially awarded [@CL-MKT-1018], while the incentive payment is 5 percent of the amount paid to the subcontractor [@CL-MKT-1017]. Neither is automatic: the incentive payment is subject to the terms of the contract and the availability of funds [@CL-MKT-1017], and the contractor must prove the amount it claims [@CL-MKT-1021]. The contracting officer and the contractor, acting in good faith, may rely on a participant's representation unless an interested party challenges its status or the contracting officer has independent reason to question it [@CL-MKT-1019]. The codified clause says no incentive payment will be made within 50 working days of subcontract award or while a challenge is pending [@CL-MKT-1020], while the RFO text says the Contractor shall not make an incentive payment in that period [@CL-MKT-0180].
