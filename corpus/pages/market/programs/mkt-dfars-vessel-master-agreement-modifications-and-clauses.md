## Purpose

Shows how a vessel repair master agreement is kept current, where changes must be made, which clauses the agreement and its job orders carry, and the inspection time allowed in an exchange of personal property offer.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1936] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 217.7103-6, 217.7104 and 217.7005 [@CL-MKT-1936; @CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-1942] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1936] |
| Applies to | Vessel repair master agreements, the solicitations and job orders under them, and nonexcess personal property offered for exchange [@CL-MKT-1936; @CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-1942] |

Each master agreement must be reviewed at least annually before the anniversary of its effective date and revised as necessary to conform to the requirements of the FAR and DFARS [@CL-MKT-1936]. Statutory or other mandatory changes may require review and revision earlier than one year [@CL-MKT-2035]. A master agreement shall be changed only by modifying the master agreement itself, and it shall not be changed through a job order [@CL-MKT-1937]. A modification to a master agreement shall not affect job orders issued before the effective date of the modification [@CL-MKT-1938].

The clauses listed in the paragraph must be used in solicitations for, and in, master agreements for repair and alteration of vessels [@CL-MKT-1939]. The listed clauses are Changes at 252.217-7003, Job Orders and Compensation at 252.217-7004, Inspection and Manner of Doing Work at 252.217-7005, Title at 252.217-7006, Payments at 252.217-7007 and Bonds at 252.217-7008 [@CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-2036]. They also include Default at 252.217-7009, Performance at 252.217-7010, Access to Vessel at 252.217-7011, Liability and Insurance at 252.217-7012 and Guarantees at 252.217-7013 [@CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-2037]. The list ends with Discharge of Liens at 252.217-7014, Safety and Health at 252.217-7015 and, as applicable, Plant Protection at 252.217-7016 [@CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-2038].

Solicitations for job orders, and the job orders themselves, must incorporate the clauses in the master agreement and any other clauses on subjects not covered by the master agreement but applicable to the job order to be awarded [@CL-MKT-1940]. The plant protection clause at 252.217-7016 must be used in job orders where performance is to occur at the contractor's facility [@CL-MKT-1941].

The provision at 252.217-7002, Offering Property for Exchange, must be used when offering nonexcess personal property for exchange [@CL-MKT-1942]. A minimum of 14 calendar days must be allowed for the inspection period in paragraph (b) of the clause if the exchange property is in the contiguous United States [@CL-MKT-1943]. At least 21 calendar days must be allowed outside the contiguous United States [@CL-MKT-1944].

## How it works

A master agreement shall be changed only by modifying the master agreement itself and shall not be changed through a job order, and a modification to a master agreement shall not affect job orders issued before the effective date of the modification [@CL-MKT-1937; @CL-MKT-1938]. Solicitations for job orders, and the job orders themselves, incorporate the clauses in the master agreement and any other clauses on subjects not covered by the master agreement but applicable to the job order to be awarded, and the plant protection clause at 252.217-7016 must be used in job orders where performance is to occur at the contractor's facility [@CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-1940; @CL-MKT-1941]. When offering nonexcess personal property for exchange, the provision at 252.217-7002 must be used, allowing a minimum of 14 calendar days for the inspection period in paragraph (b) of the clause if the exchange property is in the contiguous United States and at least 21 calendar days outside the contiguous United States [@CL-MKT-1942; @CL-MKT-1943; @CL-MKT-1944].

The definitions of master agreement and job order are on [Master agreements for vessel repair and the job orders placed under them](mkt-vessel-repair-master-agreements-and-job-orders.md). The purchase request and award steps for an exchange are on [Exchange of personal property: the purchase request, the solicitation and the award](mkt-personal-property-exchange-purchase-request-and-award.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A master agreement is reviewed before its anniversary and revised to conform to a new DFARS requirement [@CL-MKT-1936]. The revision is made by modifying the master agreement itself, and a job order issued before the modification takes effect keeps its old terms [@CL-MKT-1937; @CL-MKT-1938]. A contractor asks to alter a master agreement clause through a job order, which the section does not allow [@CL-MKT-1937]. A new job order for work at the contractor's own facility incorporates the clauses in the master agreement, any other applicable clauses on subjects the agreement does not cover, and the plant protection clause [@CL-MKT-1940; @CL-MKT-1941]. A solicitation that offers a trade-in item located in the contiguous United States allows an inspection period of at least 14 calendar days, and an offer of an item located outside it allows at least 21 calendar days [@CL-MKT-1942; @CL-MKT-1943; @CL-MKT-1944].

## Never confuse

A master agreement shall be changed only by modifying the master agreement itself and shall not be changed through a job order [@CL-MKT-1937]. The plant protection clause at 252.217-7016 is used as applicable in solicitations for, and in, master agreements for repair and alteration of vessels, and it must be used in job orders where performance is to occur at the contractor's facility [@CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-2038; @CL-MKT-1941]. The provision at 252.217-7002, Offering Property for Exchange, is used when offering nonexcess personal property for exchange, while DFARS 217.7104 prescribes the clauses for master agreements for repair and alteration of vessels and for job orders [@CL-MKT-1942; @CL-MKT-1939; @CL-MKT-1940].
