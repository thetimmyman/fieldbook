## Purpose

Sets out three DoD rules on orders: the procedures for architect-engineer orders under a multiple-award contract, the limit on requests for contractor qualification information, and the step a contracting officer follows when a competitive order above the simplified acquisition threshold draws only one offer.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1862] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 216.500(d) and DFARS 216.505-70(a) [@CL-MKT-1862; @CL-MKT-1863; @CL-MKT-1864] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1862] |
| Applies to | Task orders and delivery orders under multiple-award contracts [@CL-MKT-1862; @CL-MKT-1948] |

When awarding task orders or delivery orders for architect-engineer services under a multiple-award contract, DFARS says to follow the procedures for the selection of contractors and placement of orders at FAR 36.6, to implement section 3406(h)(1) of title 10, United States Code [@CL-MKT-1862]. The next paragraph says contracting officers shall not request additional information related to contractor qualifications, unless it is necessary to determine the most highly qualified contractor for the particular task order or delivery order [@CL-MKT-1863].

The section headed Orders under multiple-award contracts adds that if only one offer is received in response to an order exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold that is placed on a competitive basis, the contracting officer shall follow the procedures at 215.371 [@CL-MKT-1948; @CL-MKT-1864]. It also refers to PGI 216.505-70 for guidance regarding minimum labor category qualifications for orders issued under multiple-award services contracts [@CL-MKT-1865].

## How it works

The architect-engineer rule directs the FAR 36.6 procedures for selecting contractors and placing orders under a multiple-award contract [@CL-MKT-1862]. The information limit is a prohibition on requesting additional information related to contractor qualifications, unless it is necessary to determine the most highly qualified contractor for the particular task order or delivery order [@CL-MKT-1863]. The one-offer rule needs three facts together: the order exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, it is placed on a competitive basis, and only one offer is received [@CL-MKT-1864].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A multiple-award contract for architect-engineer services is in place, and the agency awards a task order under it. The agency follows the procedures at FAR 36.6 for selecting among the contract holders and placing the order [@CL-MKT-1862]. The contracting officer asks one holder for extra information about its qualifications only because the officer needs it to determine the most highly qualified contractor for that task order [@CL-MKT-1863]. In a separate case, a competitive order above the simplified acquisition threshold is placed under a multiple-award services contract and only one offer arrives, so the contracting officer follows the procedures at 215.371 [@CL-MKT-1864]. A competitive order that draws two offers does not meet the one-offer condition of that paragraph [@CL-MKT-1864].

## Never confuse

The procedures at FAR 36.6 apply when awarding task orders or delivery orders for architect-engineer services under a multiple-award contract [@CL-MKT-1862], and the procedures at 215.371 apply if only one offer is received in response to an order exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold that is placed on a competitive basis [@CL-MKT-1864]. PGI 216.505-70 provides guidance regarding minimum labor category qualifications for orders issued under multiple-award services contracts [@CL-MKT-1865]. The ordering rules for multiple-award orders generally are on [DoD ordering under multiple-award contracts: DFARS additions](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-ordering-multiple-award-contracts.md) and [Ordering under indefinite-delivery contracts: fair opportunity, exceptions and thresholds](../../estimating/pricing/est-far-ordering-indefinite-delivery-contracts.md).
