## Purpose

Shows the policy the clause states, the contractor's duty to carry it out when it awards subcontracts, and when the contractor may rely on a subcontractor's representation of its size and status.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0817] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-8 [@CL-MKT-0815; @CL-MKT-0817] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-8 [@CL-MKT-1239; @CL-MKT-1243] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0817] |
| Applies to | Contractors that agree to the clause in their contracts [@CL-MKT-0817] |

The clause states a policy of the United States that names six categories of concern: small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business and women-owned small business [@CL-MKT-0814]. Those concerns shall have the maximum practicable opportunity to participate in performing contracts let by any Federal agency, including contracts and subcontracts for subsystems, assemblies, components, and related services for major systems [@CL-MKT-0815]. It is further the policy that prime contractors establish procedures to ensure the timely payment of amounts due under their subcontracts with small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, and women-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0816; @CL-MKT-0975].

The contractor agrees to carry out this policy in the awarding of subcontracts to the fullest extent consistent with efficient contract performance [@CL-MKT-0817]. The contractor further agrees to cooperate in any studies or surveys as may be conducted by the United States Small Business Administration or the awarding agency of the United States as may be necessary to determine the extent of the contractor's compliance with FAR 52.219-8 [@CL-MKT-2013].

## How it works

The contractor agrees to carry out the policy in the awarding of subcontracts to the fullest extent consistent with efficient contract performance [@CL-MKT-0817]; subcontracting plan goals sit in the subcontracting plan clause on the [scope and required elements](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-scope-and-required-elements.md) card. On representations, unless the contractor has reason to question the representation, it may accept a subcontractor's written representations of its size and socioeconomic status as a small business, small disadvantaged business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, or women-owned small business if the subcontractor represents that the size and socioeconomic status representations with its offer are current, accurate, and complete as of the date of the offer for the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0818; @CL-MKT-0976; @CL-MKT-0819]. Unless the contractor has reason to question the representation, it may also accept a subcontractor's representations of its size and socioeconomic status as a small business, small disadvantaged business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, or a women-owned small business in the System for Award Management (SAM) if the subcontractor is registered in SAM and represents that the size and socioeconomic status representations made in SAM are current, accurate and complete as of the date of the offer for the subcontract [@CL-MKT-2015; @CL-MKT-9106; @CL-MKT-9107]. The contractor may not require the use of SAM for the purposes of representing size or socioeconomic status in connection with a subcontract [@CL-MKT-9108]. In accordance with 13 CFR 121.411, 126.900, 127.700, and 128.600, a contractor acting in good faith is not liable for misrepresentations made by its subcontractors regarding the subcontractor's size or socioeconomic status [@CL-MKT-0820; @CL-MKT-9109]. The contractor shall confirm that a subcontractor representing itself as a HUBZone small business concern is certified by SBA as a HUBZone small business concern by accessing SAM or by accessing DSBS, and if the subcontractor is a joint venture, the contractor shall confirm that at least one party to the joint venture is certified by SBA as a HUBZone small business concern, and may confirm the representation by accessing SAM [@CL-MKT-9110; @CL-MKT-9111]. Under codified FAR 52.219-8(a), a women-owned small business concern means a small business concern that is at least 51 percent owned by one or more women, or in the case of any publicly owned business at least 51 percent of the stock of which is owned by one or more women, and whose management and daily business operations are controlled by one or more women [@CL-MKT-9112].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.219-8 keeps the policy that small business concerns, VOSB concerns, SDVOSB concerns, HUBZone small business concerns, SDB concerns, and WOSB concerns shall have the maximum practicable opportunity to participate in performing contracts let by any Federal agency, including contracts and subcontracts for subsystems, assemblies, components, and related services for major systems [@CL-MKT-1239; @CL-MKT-1240], the policy that prime contractors establish procedures to ensure the timely payment of amounts due pursuant to the terms of their subcontracts with those concerns [@CL-MKT-1241; @CL-MKT-1242], and the contractor's agreement to carry out the policy in the awarding of subcontracts to the fullest extent consistent with efficient contract performance [@CL-MKT-1243]. RFO FAR 52.219-8(e)(1) states that, unless the Contractor has reason to question the representation, it may accept a subcontractor's written representations of its size and socioeconomic status as a small business or SDB if the subcontractor represents that the size and socioeconomic status representations with its offer are current, accurate, and complete as of the date of the offer for the subcontract [@CL-MKT-1244; @CL-MKT-1247]. RFO FAR 52.219-8(e)(2) states that, unless the Contractor has reason to question the representation, it may accept a subcontractor's representations of its size and socioeconomic status as a small business or SDB in the System for Award Management (SAM) if the subcontractor is registered in SAM and represents that the size and socioeconomic status representations made in SAM are current, accurate and complete as of the date of the offer for the subcontract [@CL-MKT-1248; @CL-MKT-1249]. RFO FAR 52.219-8(e)(3) states that the Contractor may not require that the subcontractor register in SAM for the purposes of representing size or socioeconomic status in connection with a subcontract [@CL-MKT-1250]. RFO FAR 52.219-8(e)(4) states that a contractor acting in good faith is not liable for misrepresentations made by its subcontractors regarding the subcontractor's size or socioeconomic status [@CL-MKT-1251]. RFO FAR 52.219-8(e)(5) states that the Contractor shall confirm that a subcontractor representing itself as a HUBZone small business concern, VOSB, SDVOSB, or WOSB concern is certified by SBA by checking SAM or SBS [@CL-MKT-1252]. In RFO FAR 52.219-8(a), the definition of a women-owned small business (WOSB) concern includes a third condition that it is certified by SBA or an approved third-party certifier in accordance with 13 CFR 127.300 and is designated by SBA as a WOSB concern in SBS and SAM [@CL-MKT-9113], and a service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) concern is defined as an SDVOSB concern that meets the requirements described in 13 CFR 128.300, is certified by SBA, and is designated by SBA as an SDVOSB concern in SBS and SAM [@CL-MKT-9114].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor awards a subcontract to a supplier that gives a written representation, with its offer, that it is small and that the representation is current, accurate and complete. The prime has no reason to question it. Answer: the prime may accept the representation. If the supplier's statement later proves wrong and the prime acted in good faith, the prime is not liable for it.

## Never confuse

FAR 52.219-8 states the policy and the contractor's agreement to carry it out in the awarding of subcontracts to the fullest extent consistent with efficient contract performance [@CL-MKT-0814; @CL-MKT-0815; @CL-MKT-0817], while subcontracting plan goals and plan content are on [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md). Under FAR 52.219-9(k), the failure of the contractor or a subcontractor to comply in good faith with the Utilization of Small Business Concerns clause or an approved plan shall be a material breach of the contract, as shown on the [master and commercial plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card [@CL-MKT-0871]. The policy for Government acquisitions is on the [Small business general policy](mkt-small-business-general-policy.md) card. Under codified FAR 52.219-8(e)(1)-(2), the written or SAM representations a contractor may accept cover small business, small disadvantaged business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, or women-owned small business status [@CL-MKT-0976; @CL-MKT-9106], while RFO FAR 52.219-8(e)(1)-(2) list small business or SDB [@CL-MKT-1244; @CL-MKT-1248]. A contractor acting in good faith is not liable for misrepresentations made by its subcontractors regarding the subcontractor's size or socioeconomic status [@CL-MKT-0820; @CL-MKT-9109]; the penalties for misrepresenting size are on the [Size misrepresentation penalties](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-misrepresentation-penalties-and-subcontract-size.md) card.
