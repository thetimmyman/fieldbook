## Purpose

Shows the time limits when the SBA reviews a referral, what happens when a Certificate of Competency is denied, and how the contracting agency and the SBA resolve a disagreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0484] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.602-2 and FAR 19.602-3 [@CL-MKT-0484; @CL-MKT-0487] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0484] |
| Applies to | Referrals of small business concerns found to lack certain elements of responsibility, with different steps by dollar value [@CL-MKT-0484; @CL-MKT-0487] |

Within 15 business days, or a longer period agreed to by the SBA and the contracting agency, after receiving a notice that a small business concern lacks certain elements of responsibility, the SBA Area Office will take the listed actions [@CL-MKT-0484]. One listed action is that, when the Area Director determines a Certificate of Competency is warranted for contracts valued at $25,000,000 or less, the Area Office notifies the contracting officer and offers options, the first being to accept the decision to issue the certificate and award the contract to the concern [@CL-MKT-0485]. There is no contracting officer's appeal when the Area Office proposes to issue a Certificate of Competency valued at $100,000 or less [@CL-MKT-0486].

For Certificates of Competency valued between $100,000 and $25,000,000, the contracting officer and the SBA shall make every effort to resolve disagreements about a concern's ability to perform before the SBA takes final action [@CL-MKT-0487]. In that range, if agreement cannot be reached with the Area Office, the contracting officer shall request that the Area Office suspend action and refer the matter to SBA Headquarters for review [@CL-MKT-0488]. The Area Office shall honor the request for review if the contracting officer agrees to withhold award until the review is concluded, and without that agreement the Area Office will issue the certificate under applicable SBA regulations [@CL-MKT-0578].

## How it works

Within the paragraph for certificates in that middle range, if the contracting agency decides to appeal the issuance of a Certificate of Competency, it must notify SBA Headquarters, through its procuring agency's Director, OSDBU, or other designated official and within 10 business days (or a period agreed by both agencies), that it intends to appeal the issuance of the Certificate of Competency [@CL-MKT-0487; @CL-MKT-0489]. The appeal and supporting documentation shall then be filed by that same official within 10 business days, or a period agreed by both agencies, after SBA Headquarters receives that notification [@CL-MKT-0490]. The SBA Associate Administrator for Government Contracting will make a final determination, in writing, to issue or to deny the certificate [@CL-MKT-0491]. Denial of a Certificate of Competency by the SBA does not preclude a contracting officer from awarding a contract to the referred concern, nor does it prevent the concern from making an offer on any other procurement [@CL-MKT-0492]. The SBA reserves the right to reconsider its issuance of a Certificate of Competency before contract award if the applicant submitted false information or omitted materially adverse information, or if the certificate has been issued for more than 60 days [@CL-MKT-0579]. This card does not cover the path for larger contracts, the Area Office's review steps before it decides, or what follows when the SBA reconsiders and reaffirms a certificate.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract worth 2,000,000 is referred to the SBA. The Area Office tells the concern of the finding within the review period and later decides a certificate is warranted. The contracting officer disagrees, asks the Area Office to suspend action and refer the matter to SBA Headquarters, and agrees to withhold award. The agency then tells SBA Headquarters of its intent to appeal within 10 business days and files the appeal within a further 10 business days. If the Associate Administrator denies the certificate, the contracting officer may still award to the concern.

## Never confuse

For Certificates of Competency valued between $100,000 and $25,000,000, the SBA Associate Administrator for Government Contracting will make a final determination, in writing, to issue or to deny the certificate [@CL-MKT-0487; @CL-MKT-0491]; the referral step is on the [Certificates of Competency: general rules and referral](mkt-coc-general-and-referral.md) card. A denial does not bar award to the concern and does not bar its other offers [@CL-MKT-0492]. An SBA appeal to the agency head on a program matter [@CL-MKT-0515] is on the [8(a) limitations on subcontracting and SBA appeals](mkt-8a-subcontracting-limits-and-appeals.md) card. Responsibility information gathered before the determination is on the [Obtaining responsibility information and documenting the determination](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-responsibility-information-and-determination.md) card.
