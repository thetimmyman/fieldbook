## Purpose

Sets out which undefinitized contract actions (UCAs) fall outside the subpart, how profit is set when the price is negotiated after substantial performance, and the management reports that cover larger actions.

## The rule

|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1553] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 217.7402, DFARS 217.7404-6 and DFARS 217.7405 [@CL-MKT-1554; @CL-MKT-1555; @CL-MKT-2012; @CL-MKT-1558] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1553] |
| Applies to | UCAs outside the subpart, profit on a UCA priced after substantial performance, and UCA management reporting [@CL-MKT-1553; @CL-MKT-1555; @CL-MKT-2012; @CL-MKT-1558] |

The UCAs that paragraph (a) excludes from the subpart are purchases at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, special access programs and congressionally mandated long-lead procurement contracts [@CL-MKT-1554; @CL-MKT-1553]. They are not subject to the subpart, but the contracting officer shall apply its policy and procedures to them to the maximum extent practicable [@CL-MKT-1554]. If the contracting officer determines that it is impracticable to adhere to the procedures for a particular action within one of those categories, the contracting officer shall provide prior notice [@CL-MKT-1616]. The notice goes through agency channels, electronically by email, to the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy (Contract Policy) [@CL-MKT-1617].

When the final price of a UCA is negotiated after a substantial portion of the required performance has been completed, the head of the contracting activity shall ensure that the profit allowed reflects three matters [@CL-MKT-1555]. The first is any reduced cost risk to the contractor for costs incurred during performance before the final price is negotiated [@CL-MKT-1618]. The second is any reduced cost risk for costs expected to be incurred in the rest of the contract after the final price is negotiated [@CL-MKT-1557]. The third is the requirements at DFARS 215.404-71-3(d)(2), and the risk assessment shall be documented in the price negotiation memorandum [@CL-MKT-1620].

When a contractor submits a qualifying proposal to definitize a UCA, and the contracting officer for such action definitizes the contract after the end of the 180-day period beginning on the date on which the contractor submitted the qualifying proposal, the profit allowed on the contract shall accurately reflect the cost risk of the contractor as such risk existed on the date the contractor submitted the qualifying proposal [@CL-MKT-1619; @CL-MKT-1556].

To provide for enhanced management and oversight of UCAs, departments and agencies shall prepare and maintain a Consolidated UCA Management Plan, and prepare semi-annual Consolidated UCA Management Reports addressing each UCA with an estimated value exceeding $5 million [@CL-MKT-2012; @CL-MKT-1558]. Consolidated UCA Management Reports and Consolidated UCA Management Plan updates shall be submitted to the Office of the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy (Contract Policy) by October 31 and April 30 of each year in accordance with the procedures at PGI 217.7405 [@CL-MKT-1621; @CL-MKT-1559]. Consolidated UCA Management Reports shall include information about all change orders that are not forward priced (that is, unpriced) and have an estimated value exceeding $5 million [@CL-MKT-1622].

## How it works

When the final price of a UCA is negotiated after a substantial portion of the required performance has been completed, the profit allowed must reflect any reduced cost risk to the contractor for costs incurred during contract performance before negotiation of the final price, any reduced cost risk for costs expected to be incurred during performance of the remainder of the contract, and the requirements at DFARS 215.404-71-3(d)(2) [@CL-MKT-1555; @CL-MKT-1618; @CL-MKT-1557; @CL-MKT-1620].

The exclusions take an action out of the subpart without taking it out of the policy, so the contracting officer still follows the procedures as far as practicable [@CL-MKT-1554]. Prior notice is required when the contracting officer determines that following them is impracticable for a particular action [@CL-MKT-1616]. A qualifying proposal fixes the reference date for profit when definitization comes after the 180-day period [@CL-MKT-1619; @CL-MKT-1556]. The reporting threshold is measured on the UCA's estimated value, and separately on the estimated value of each unpriced change order [@CL-MKT-1558; @CL-MKT-1622].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A UCA has an estimated value of 8,000,000 and a substantial portion of the required performance has been completed when the final price is negotiated. The head of the contracting activity must ensure the profit allowed reflects the reduced cost risk on the costs already incurred and on the costs still to come [@CL-MKT-1555; @CL-MKT-1618; @CL-MKT-1557], and the price negotiation memorandum documents the risk assessment [@CL-MKT-1620]. The contractor submitted its qualifying proposal on Day 1, and the contracting officer definitizes on Day 260, after the end of the 180-day period beginning on Day 1, so the profit allowed shall reflect the contractor's cost risk as it existed on Day 1 [@CL-MKT-1619; @CL-MKT-1556]. Because the estimated value exceeds 5,000,000, the UCA is addressed in the semi-annual management report [@CL-MKT-1558]. A separate UCA that is a purchase below the simplified acquisition threshold is outside the subpart, yet the contracting officer still applies the subpart's policy to the maximum extent practicable [@CL-MKT-1553; @CL-MKT-1554]. If that is impracticable for the purchase, the contracting officer gives prior notice to the Principal Director [@CL-MKT-1616; @CL-MKT-1617].

## Never confuse

An excluded UCA is outside the subpart but not outside its policy, which still applies to the maximum extent practicable [@CL-MKT-1554]. The reduced cost risk on incurred costs is an input to the profit allowed, and the late-definitization rule changes the date at which cost risk is measured [@CL-MKT-1618; @CL-MKT-1556]. The contract type risk factor and structured profit approaches are on [contract type risk](../../estimating/pricing/est-weighted-guidelines-contract-type-risk.md) and [profit structured approaches](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-profit-structured-approaches.md). Approval and obligation limits are on [approval and obligation limits](mkt-uca-approval-and-obligation-limits.md), and the definitization schedule is on [undefinitized contract actions](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-undefinitized-contract-actions.md).
