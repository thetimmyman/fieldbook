## Purpose

Separates the two women-owned notice clauses: the notice for economically disadvantaged women-owned small business (EDWOSB) concerns, and the notice for women-owned small business (WOSB) concerns eligible under the WOSB Program. The policy rules are on the [Women-Owned Small Business Program set-asides and sole-source awards](mkt-wosb-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) card, and status protests are on the [EDWOSB and WOSB status protests](mkt-edwosb-wosb-status-protests.md) card.

An EDWOSB concern means a small business concern that is at least 51 percent directly and unconditionally owned by, and the management and daily business operations of which are controlled by, one or more women who are citizens of the United States and are economically disadvantaged in accordance with 13 CFR part 127, and the concern is certified pursuant to 13 CFR 127.300 as an EDWOSB [@CL-MKT-1010; @CL-MKT-1011]. An EDWOSB automatically qualifies as a WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1012].

A WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program means a small business concern that is at least 51 percent directly and unconditionally owned by, and the management and daily business operations of which are controlled by, one or more women who are citizens of the United States, and the concern is certified by the Small Business Administration (SBA) or an approved third-party certifier in accordance with 13 CFR 127.300 as a WOSB [@CL-MKT-1072; @CL-MKT-1015]. A certified EDWOSB is automatically eligible as a certified WOSB [@CL-MKT-1073].

## How it works

The EDWOSB clause applies only to contracts that have been set aside for, or awarded on a sole-source basis to, EDWOSB concerns, to the part or parts of a multiple-award contract that have been set aside for EDWOSB concerns [@CL-MKT-1066], to orders set aside for EDWOSB concerns under multiple-award contracts as described in FAR 8.405-5 and 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F), and to orders issued directly to EDWOSB concerns under multiple-award contracts as described in FAR 19.504(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MKT-1066; @CL-MKT-1067]. The WOSB clause applies only to contracts that have been set aside for, or awarded on a sole-source basis to, WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program, to the part or parts of a multiple-award contract that have been set aside for WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1074], to orders set aside for WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program under multiple-award contracts as described in FAR 8.405-5 and 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F), and to orders issued directly to WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program under multiple-award contracts as described in FAR 19.504(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MKT-1074; @CL-MKT-1075].

In an EDWOSB set-aside, offers are solicited only from certified EDWOSB concerns or EDWOSB concerns with a pending application for certification in the Dynamic Small Business Search [@CL-MKT-1013]. In an EDWOSB sole-source award, offers are solicited only from certified EDWOSB concerns [@CL-MKT-1014]. Offers from other concerns will not be considered, and any award resulting from the solicitation will be made to a certified EDWOSB concern [@CL-MKT-1068]. For WOSB set-asides, offers are solicited only from certified WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program or WOSB concerns with a pending application for certification status in the Dynamic Small Business Search [@CL-MKT-1076]. For WOSB sole-source awards, offers are solicited only from certified WOSB concerns, offers received from other concerns shall not be considered, and any award resulting from the solicitation will be made to a certified WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1077].

A joint venture may be considered an EDWOSB concern if at least one party to the joint venture complies with the criteria defined in paragraph (a) and paragraph (c)(3) of the clause and 13 CFR 127.506(c), and each party to the joint venture qualifies as small under the size standard for the solicitation, or the protégé is small under the size standard for the solicitation in a joint venture comprised of a mentor and protégé with an approved mentor-protégé agreement under the SBA mentor-protégé program [@CL-MKT-1069; @CL-MKT-1070]. In a joint venture that complies with paragraph (d) of the clause, the EDWOSB party or parties to the joint venture shall perform at least 40 percent of the work performed by the joint venture, and work performed by the EDWOSB party or parties to the joint venture must be more than administrative functions [@CL-MKT-1016; @CL-MKT-1071]. A joint venture may be considered a WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program if at least one party to the joint venture complies with the criteria defined in paragraph (a) and (c)(3) of the clause and 13 CFR 127.506(c), and each party to the joint venture qualifies as small under the size standard for the solicitation, or the protégé is small under the size standard for the solicitation in a joint venture comprised of a mentor and protégé with an approved mentor-protégé agreement under the SBA mentor-protégé program [@CL-MKT-1078; @CL-MKT-1079]. In a joint venture that complies with paragraph (d) of the WOSB clause, the WOSB party or parties to the joint venture shall perform at least 40 percent of the work performed by the joint venture, and work performed by the WOSB party or parties to the joint venture must be more than administrative functions [@CL-MKT-1080; @CL-MKT-1081]. The general limits on subcontracting are on the [Limitations on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule](mkt-limitations-on-subcontracting-and-nonmanufacturer-rule.md) card.

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. In RFO FAR 52.219-29, an EDWOSB concern is a small business concern that is at least 51 percent directly and unconditionally owned by, and whose management and daily business operations are controlled by, one or more women who are citizens of the United States and who are economically disadvantaged in accordance with 13 CFR part 127, and the concern is certified pursuant to 13 CFR 127.300 as an EDWOSB [@CL-MKT-0102; @CL-MKT-0103]. A certified EDWOSB automatically qualifies as a certified WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-0103]. RFO FAR 52.219-29 applies only to contracts that have been set aside for, or awarded on a sole-source basis to, EDWOSB concerns, to the part or parts of a multiple-award contract that have been set aside for EDWOSB concerns, and to orders set aside for EDWOSB concerns under multiple-award contracts as described in 8.4 and 16.5 [@CL-MKT-0104; @CL-MKT-0105]. In RFO FAR 52.219-29, offers are solicited only from certified EDWOSB concerns, offers received from other concerns will not be considered, and any award resulting from the solicitation will be made to a certified EDWOSB concern [@CL-MKT-0106]. Under RFO FAR 52.219-29(d), a joint venture may be considered an EDWOSB concern if at least one party to the joint venture complies with the criteria defined in paragraph (a) and paragraph (c)(1) of the clause and 13 CFR 127.506(c), and each party to the joint venture qualifies as small under the size standard for the solicitation, or the protégé is small under the size standard for the solicitation in a joint venture comprised of a mentor and protégé with an approved mentor-protégé agreement under the SBA mentor-protégé program [@CL-MKT-0107; @CL-MKT-0108]. In a joint venture that complies with RFO FAR 52.219-29(d), the EDWOSB party or parties to the joint venture shall perform at least 40 percent of the work performed by the joint venture, and work performed by the EDWOSB party or parties to the joint venture must be more than administrative functions [@CL-MKT-0109; @CL-MKT-0110]. In RFO FAR 52.219-30, a WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program is a small business concern that is at least 51 percent directly and unconditionally owned by, and whose management and daily business operations are controlled by, one or more women who are citizens of the United States, and the concern is certified by SBA or an approved third-party certifier in accordance with 13 CFR 127.300 as a WOSB [@CL-MKT-0111; @CL-MKT-0112]. A certified EDWOSB is automatically eligible as a certified WOSB [@CL-MKT-0112]. RFO FAR 52.219-30 applies only to contracts that have been set aside for, or awarded on a sole-source basis to, WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program, to the part or parts of a multiple-award contract that have been set aside for WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program, and to orders set aside for WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program under multiple-award contracts as described in 8.4 and 16.5 [@CL-MKT-0113; @CL-MKT-0114]. In RFO FAR 52.219-30, offers are solicited only from certified WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program, offers received from other concerns shall not be considered, and any award resulting from the solicitation will be made to a certified WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-0115; @CL-MKT-0116]. Under RFO FAR 52.219-30(d), a joint venture may be considered a WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program if at least one party to the joint venture complies with the criteria defined in paragraph (a) and (c)(1) of the clause and 13 CFR 127.506(c), and each party to the joint venture qualifies as small under the size standard for the solicitation, or the protégé is small under the size standard for the solicitation in a joint venture comprised of a mentor and protégé with an approved mentor-protégé agreement under the SBA mentor-protégé program [@CL-MKT-0117; @CL-MKT-0118]. In a joint venture that complies with RFO FAR 52.219-30(d), the WOSB party or parties to the joint venture shall perform at least 40 percent of the work performed by the joint venture, and work performed by the WOSB party or parties to the joint venture must be more than administrative functions [@CL-MKT-0119; @CL-MKT-0120].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An EDWOSB concern with a certification application pending in the Dynamic Small Business Search offers 2,000,000 on an EDWOSB set-aside under codified FAR 52.219-29. Its offer is among those solicited [@CL-MKT-1013]. The same concern could not offer on an EDWOSB sole-source award, which is limited to certified EDWOSB concerns [@CL-MKT-1014]. In an EDWOSB joint venture that complies with paragraph (d) of the clause and performs 800,000 of work, the EDWOSB party must perform at least 320,000, which is 40 percent, and that work must be more than administrative functions [@CL-MKT-1016; @CL-MKT-1071]. In RFO FAR 52.219-29, offers are solicited only from certified EDWOSB concerns [@CL-MKT-0106].

## Never confuse

FAR 52.219-29 applies to EDWOSB concerns and FAR 52.219-30 applies to WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1066; @CL-MKT-1074]. In both codified clauses, set-aside offers are solicited only from certified concerns or concerns with a pending application for certification in the Dynamic Small Business Search, while sole-source offers are solicited only from certified concerns [@CL-MKT-1013; @CL-MKT-1014; @CL-MKT-1076; @CL-MKT-1077]. A certified EDWOSB is automatically eligible as a certified WOSB [@CL-MKT-1073], and the EDWOSB definition requires that the women owners be economically disadvantaged in accordance with 13 CFR part 127 and that the concern be certified in accordance with 13 CFR 127.300 as an EDWOSB [@CL-MKT-1010; @CL-MKT-1011]. In RFO FAR 52.219-29, offers are solicited only from certified EDWOSB concerns, and in RFO FAR 52.219-30, offers are solicited only from certified WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-0106; @CL-MKT-0115].
