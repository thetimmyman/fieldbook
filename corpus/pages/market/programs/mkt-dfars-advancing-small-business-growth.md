## Purpose

Shows what an offeror acknowledges by submitting an offer when the resulting contract could take it over its small business size standard.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0919] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.219-7000 [@CL-MKT-0919] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0919] |
| Applies to | Solicitations in which the provision is prescribed by DFARS 219.309(1) [@CL-MKT-0948] |

By submitting its offer, the offeror acknowledges that by accepting the resulting contract it may exceed the size standard for the NAICS code assigned to the contract [@CL-MKT-0919]. The provision states that by acceptance of the contract the offeror may exceed that size standard and would no longer qualify as a small business concern for that NAICS code [@CL-MKT-0920]. The provision encourages the offeror to develop the capabilities and characteristics typically desired in contractors that are competitive as other-than-small contractors in this industry [@CL-MKT-0921].

The acknowledgement is made by submission of the offer [@CL-MKT-0919].

## How it works

The offeror acknowledges that by acceptance of the contract resulting from the solicitation it may exceed the applicable small business size standard of the NAICS code assigned to the contract [@CL-MKT-0919]. How size is measured and when a contractor must rerepresent its size after award are on the linked cards.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A small business offers on a contract assigned a NAICS code whose size standard is 20 million in annual receipts. Its receipts are 18 million and the contract is worth 6 million a year.

Answer: by submitting the offer it acknowledges that winning may push it over 20 million, so it may no longer qualify as small for that code. The provision asks for no other notice.

## Never confuse

DFARS 252.219-7000 is an acknowledgment that accepting the resulting contract may push the offeror past the assigned NAICS size standard for future procurements [@CL-MKT-0919; @CL-MKT-0920], rather than the initial size representation on [Offeror size and status representation](../taxonomy/mkt-offeror-size-and-status-representation.md) [@CL-MKT-0209] or a mandatory postaward rerepresentation on [rerepresentation after award](../taxonomy/mkt-size-rerepresentation-after-award.md) [@CL-MKT-0214].

## Company calibration

Which of the company's offers are made as a small business, and how close it is to the size standard of each assigned NAICS code. [@Q-019]
