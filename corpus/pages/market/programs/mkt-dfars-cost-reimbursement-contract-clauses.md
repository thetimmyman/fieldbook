## Purpose

Shows which task orders under contracts awarded before the cutoff date, and which of those contracts themselves, must carry the DFARS clause on allowability of costs incurred in connection with a whistleblower proceeding, and the two conditions that bring a contract in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1845] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 216.307(a) [@CL-MKT-1845; @CL-MKT-1846] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1845] |
| Applies to | Task orders entered under, and contracts, awarded before September 30, 2013 that include the FAR Allowable Cost and Payment clause; a contract also must be modified to include the DFARS employee-rights clause dated September 2013 or later [@CL-MKT-1845; @CL-MKT-1846] |

DFARS directs use of the clause at 252.216-7009, Allowability of Costs Incurred in Connection With a Whistleblower Proceeding, in two situations [@CL-MKT-1845; @CL-MKT-1846], and says it does so as required by section 827 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2013 [@CL-MKT-1847]. The paragraph is written as a direction to use the clause and names no official who applies it.

First, the clause goes in task orders entered under contracts awarded before September 30, 2013 that include the FAR Allowable Cost and Payment clause [@CL-MKT-1845]. The condition looks at the award date and clauses of the underlying contract, not at the date of the order [@CL-MKT-1845].

Second, the clause goes in contracts awarded before September 30, 2013 that meet both of two conditions: they include the FAR Allowable Cost and Payment clause, and they are modified to include the DFARS clause on informing employees of whistleblower rights, dated September 2013 or later [@CL-MKT-1846].

## How it works

Start with the award date of the contract; a contract awarded on or after September 30, 2013 is outside this paragraph [@CL-MKT-1845; @CL-MKT-1846]. For a task order, check that the underlying contract includes the FAR Allowable Cost and Payment clause [@CL-MKT-1845]. For the contract itself, check that it includes that FAR clause and has been modified to add the DFARS employee-rights clause dated September 2013 or later [@CL-MKT-1846]. A contract that lacks the FAR clause does not meet the second route, even if it is modified to add the employee-rights clause [@CL-MKT-1846].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Contract A was awarded in 2012 and includes the FAR Allowable Cost and Payment clause. A task order entered under it in 2016 takes the whistleblower-proceeding clause, because the underlying contract was awarded before the cutoff and includes that FAR clause [@CL-MKT-1845]. Contract B was also awarded in 2012 and includes the FAR clause. Later it is modified to add the employee-rights clause dated October 2013, so the contract itself takes the whistleblower-proceeding clause [@CL-MKT-1846]. Contract C was awarded in 2012 without the FAR clause. Modifying it to add the employee-rights clause does not meet the first of the two conditions, so this paragraph does not call for the whistleblower-proceeding clause [@CL-MKT-1846]. Contract D was awarded in 2015, so neither route reaches it [@CL-MKT-1845; @CL-MKT-1846].

## Never confuse

The FAR Allowable Cost and Payment clause is a separate clause; here it is a condition for using the DFARS clause, not the whistleblower-proceeding clause itself [@CL-MKT-1845]. The DFARS clause on informing employees of whistleblower rights is also separate; modifying a contract to add it matters here only as the second condition of the second route [@CL-MKT-1846]. The related legal-costs clause is covered on [Foreign-controlled price representation, whistleblower legal costs and overseas choice of law (DFARS)](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-dfars-foreign-wage-price-whistleblower-legal-costs-and-choice-of-law.md).
