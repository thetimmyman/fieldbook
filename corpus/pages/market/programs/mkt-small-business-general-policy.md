## Purpose

Shows what the general small business policy commits the Government and its heads of contracting activities to, and what the policy text leaves to other rules.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0300] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.201 [@CL-MKT-0300; @CL-MKT-0301; @CL-MKT-0302] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0300] |
| Applies to | Government acquisitions, and executive agency contracts for the subcontracting opportunity [@CL-MKT-0300; @CL-MKT-0301] |

The policy is to provide maximum practicable opportunities in Government acquisitions to small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, and women-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0300]. Those concerns must also have the maximum practicable opportunity to participate as subcontractors in contracts awarded by any executive agency, consistent with efficient contract performance [@CL-MKT-0301].

Heads of contracting activities are responsible for effectively implementing the small business programs within their activities, including achieving program goals [@CL-MKT-0302].

## How it works

The policy is worded as an opportunity: maximum practicable opportunities in acquisitions [@CL-MKT-0300]. The subcontracting limb is qualified by efficient contract performance [@CL-MKT-0301]. Responsibility for implementation sits with the heads of contracting activities, and the text names achieving program goals as part of it [@CL-MKT-0302]. The steps a contracting officer takes to encourage participation are on the [Encouraging small business participation and data collection](mkt-small-business-participation-and-data-collection.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting activity makes 200 awards in a year and 30 go to small business concerns. Does the policy say that 30 is too few? No. The policy text sets no share. It requires maximum practicable opportunities, and the head of the activity is responsible for implementing the programs and achieving program goals. Any figure for a goal comes from a source that this section does not print.

## Never confuse

The criteria for small business set-asides are on the [Small business set-asides](mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card, and the thresholds for subcontracting plans are on the [When a subcontracting plan is required](mkt-subcontracting-plan-when-required.md) card. Under FAR 19.201(a), the general policy provides maximum practicable opportunities in acquisitions and in subcontracting consistent with efficient contract performance [@CL-MKT-0300; @CL-MKT-0301].
