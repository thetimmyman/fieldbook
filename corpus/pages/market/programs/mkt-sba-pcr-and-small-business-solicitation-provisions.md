## Purpose

Shows what an SBA procurement center representative may review and appeal, and names each small business solicitation provision and clause with its trigger.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0331] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.402 and FAR 19.309 [@CL-MKT-0331; @CL-MKT-0334; @CL-MKT-0359; @CL-MKT-0362; @CL-MKT-0363; @CL-MKT-0364] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0331] |
| Applies to | Contracting activities where SBA assigns a procurement center representative, and solicitations that meet the stated trigger of each provision or clause [@CL-MKT-0331; @CL-MKT-0334] |

The duties assigned by SBA to its procurement center representative include, but are not limited to, the listed duties, the first of which is reviewing proposed acquisitions to recommend, among other things, the set-aside or sole-source award to a small business of selected acquisitions [@CL-MKT-0331]. In reviewing proposed acquisition packages provided under FAR 19.202-1(e), if the representative (or, where none is assigned, the person paragraph (a) of that section refers to) believes that the acquisition, as proposed, makes it unlikely that small businesses can compete for the prime contract, the representative shall recommend any alternate contracting method that the representative reasonably believes will increase small business prime contracting opportunities [@CL-MKT-9344; @CL-MKT-0332]. That recommendation shall be made to the contracting officer within 15 days after receipt of the package [@CL-MKT-0364]. Where the conditions listed in FAR 19.202-1(e)(1) apply, the contracting officer provides a copy of the proposed acquisition package to the representative at least 30 days before the solicitation is issued, under the steps on the [Encouraging small business participation and data collection](mkt-small-business-participation-and-data-collection.md) card [@CL-MKT-0307]. One listed duty is appealing a contracting officer's rejection of the representative's recommendation, and the appeal must be in writing and filed and processed in accordance with the appeal procedures in 19.502-8 [@CL-MKT-0333]. Another listed duty is appealing to the chief of the contracting office any contracting officer's determination not to solicit a concern recommended by SBA for a particular acquisition, when not doing so results in no small business being solicited [@CL-MKT-0363].

The provision at 52.219-1, Small Business Program Representations, is to be inserted in solicitations exceeding the micro-purchase threshold when the contract is for supplies to be delivered or services to be performed in the United States or its outlying areas, which is one stated trigger [@CL-MKT-0334]. It is also to be inserted in solicitations exceeding the micro-purchase threshold when the contracting officer has applied this part in accordance with the cross-referenced paragraph of the scope section [@CL-MKT-0334; @CL-MKT-0359]. That provision is used with its Alternate I in solicitations issued by DoD, NASA, or the Coast Guard [@CL-MKT-0334; @CL-MKT-0335]. It is used with its Alternate II in solicitations that will result in a multiple-award contract with more than one NAICS code assigned, which is authorized for solicitations issued after October 1, 2028 [@CL-MKT-0334; @CL-MKT-0336]. When contracting by sealed bidding, the provision at 52.219-2, Equal Low Bids, is to be inserted in solicitations when the contract is for supplies to be delivered or services to be performed in the United States or its outlying areas, which is one stated trigger [@CL-MKT-0337]. When the contracting officer has applied this part in accordance with the cross-referenced paragraph of the scope section, the sealed-bidding provision is also to be inserted in solicitations [@CL-MKT-0337; @CL-MKT-0360]. The clause at 52.219-28, Postaward Small Business Program Rerepresentation, is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts exceeding the micro-purchase threshold in that same place-of-performance case, which is one stated trigger [@CL-MKT-0338]. That clause is also to be inserted in solicitations and contracts exceeding the micro-purchase threshold when the contracting officer has applied this part in accordance with the cross-referenced paragraph of the scope section [@CL-MKT-0338; @CL-MKT-0361]. It is used with its Alternate I in solicitations and the resulting multiple-award contracts with more than one NAICS code, which is authorized for solicitations issued after October 1, 2028 [@CL-MKT-0338; @CL-MKT-0362].

## How it works

The representative reviews and recommends, and one listed remedy for a rejected recommendation is a written appeal [@CL-MKT-0331; @CL-MKT-0332; @CL-MKT-0333]. The micro-purchase threshold amount is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.

The Director of the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization serves as the agency focal point for interfacing with the Small Business Administration, and the Director of the Office of Small Business Programs is the agency focal point for the Department of Defense [@CL-MKT-2003].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD contracting officer prepares a solicitation for supplies delivered in the United States, valued above the micro-purchase threshold. Answer: the representations provision is inserted with its Alternate I, and the postaward rerepresentation clause is inserted. If the buy is by sealed bidding, the equal low bids provision is inserted too. Separately, a procurement center representative who reviews the package and believes the acquisition as proposed makes it unlikely that small businesses can compete for the prime contract recommends an alternate method it reasonably believes will increase small business prime contracting opportunities, within 15 days after receipt. If the contracting officer rejects it, the representative may appeal in writing.

## Never confuse

An SBA procurement center representative reviews proposed acquisitions and recommends small business actions rather than making the award decision [@CL-MKT-0331], while inserting FAR 52.219-1 collects offeror representations in solicitations above the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-MKT-0334] instead of creating a set-aside by itself.
