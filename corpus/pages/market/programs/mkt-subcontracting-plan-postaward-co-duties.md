## Purpose

Lists the postaward duties of the contracting officer after a contract or modification with a subcontracting plan is awarded or a plan is amended.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0453] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.705-6 [@CL-MKT-0453; @CL-MKT-0454] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0453] |
| Applies to | Contracts and modifications that contain a subcontracting plan, and existing plans that are amended [@CL-MKT-0453] |

After a contract or contract modification containing a subcontracting plan is awarded or an existing subcontracting plan is amended, the contracting officer shall do the listed postaward actions [@CL-MKT-0453]. One listed action is to notify the SBA of the award by sending a copy of the award document to the Area Director, Office of Government Contracting, in the SBA area office where the contract will be performed [@CL-MKT-0454]. Another is to forward a copy of each commercial plan and any associated approvals to the Area Director, Office of Government Contracting, in the SBA area office where the contractor's headquarters is located [@CL-MKT-0455]. Another is to forward a copy of each plan, or a determination that there is no requirement for a subcontracting plan, to the cognizant contract administration office [@CL-MKT-0456].

Another listed action is to monitor the prime contractor's compliance with its subcontracting plan, to include ensuring that subcontracting reports are submitted into the eSRS within 30 days after the report ending date (for example, by October 30th for the fiscal year ended September 30th) [@CL-MKT-0457]. A further listed action is to initiate action to assess liquidated damages in accordance with FAR 19.705-7 upon a recommendation by the administrative contracting officer, if one is assigned, or receipt of other reliable evidence to indicate that assessing liquidated damages is warranted [@CL-MKT-0458].

Another listed action is to acknowledge receipt of or reject the ISR and the SSR in the eSRS; acknowledging receipt does not mean acceptance or approval of the report, and the report shall be rejected if it is not adequately completed, for instance, if there are errors, omissions, or incomplete data [@CL-MKT-0562]. Failure to meet the goals of the subcontracting plan is not a valid reason for rejecting the report [@CL-MKT-0563].

## How it works

After a contract or contract modification containing a subcontracting plan is awarded or an existing subcontracting plan is amended, the contracting officer sends the required notices and plan copies to the SBA and the cognizant contract administration office [@CL-MKT-0453; @CL-MKT-0454; @CL-MKT-0455; @CL-MKT-0456], monitors the prime contractor's compliance with its plan including timely report submission in the eSRS [@CL-MKT-0457], acknowledges receipt of or rejects the ISR and the SSR in the eSRS [@CL-MKT-0562; @CL-MKT-0563], and initiates action to assess liquidated damages in accordance with FAR 19.705-7 when warranted [@CL-MKT-0458]. Liquidated damages are covered on the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract with a subcontracting plan is awarded. The contracting officer sends the award document to the SBA area office where the work will be done, sends the plan to the contract administration office, and checks that the contractor files its report in the eSRS within 30 days after the report ending date.

## Never confuse

After award, the contracting officer sends the award document to the SBA Area Director where the contract is performed—or where the contractor's headquarters is located for a commercial plan [@CL-MKT-0453; @CL-MKT-0454; @CL-MKT-0455]—rather than sending preaward plan reviews or judging responsibility as on [Awards involving subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-awards.md) [@CL-MKT-0449].
