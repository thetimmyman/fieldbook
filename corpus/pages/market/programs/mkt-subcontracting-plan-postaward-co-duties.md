## Purpose

Lists the postaward duties of the contracting officer after a contract or modification with a subcontracting plan is awarded or a plan is amended.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0453] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.705-6 [@CL-MKT-0453; @CL-MKT-0454] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0453] |
| Applies to | Contracts and modifications that contain a subcontracting plan, and existing plans that are amended [@CL-MKT-0453] |

After a contract or modification containing a subcontracting plan is awarded, or an existing plan is amended, the contracting officer shall do the listed postaward actions [@CL-MKT-0453]. One listed action is to notify the SBA of the award by sending a copy of the award document to the Area Director, Office of Government Contracting, in the SBA area office where the contract will be performed [@CL-MKT-0454]. Another is to forward a copy of each commercial plan and any associated approvals to the Area Director in the SBA area office where the contractor's headquarters is located [@CL-MKT-0455]. Another is to forward a copy of each plan, or a determination that no plan is required, to the cognizant contract administration office [@CL-MKT-0456].

One listed monitoring duty is to ensure that subcontracting reports are submitted into the eSRS within 30 days after the report ending date [@CL-MKT-0457]. A further listed action is to initiate action to assess liquidated damages upon a recommendation by the administrative contracting officer, if one is assigned, or on receipt of other reliable evidence that assessing them is warranted [@CL-MKT-0458].

Acknowledging receipt of an ISR or SSR in the eSRS does not mean acceptance or approval of the report, and the report shall be rejected if it is not adequately completed, for instance if there are errors, omissions or incomplete data [@CL-MKT-0562]. Failure to meet the goals of the subcontracting plan is not a valid reason for rejecting the report [@CL-MKT-0563].

## How it works

Postaward work includes notices and copies of plans to the SBA and the contract administration office [@CL-MKT-0454; @CL-MKT-0455; @CL-MKT-0456], monitoring of the reports [@CL-MKT-0457; @CL-MKT-0562], and action where liquidated damages are warranted [@CL-MKT-0458]. The printed section also lists further duties on copies to the SBA procurement center representative, review and acknowledgment of reports, evaluation of good faith effort, enforcement after a notice from the contract administration office and the other items on the acknowledgment and rejection of reports, and these are not drafted on this card. Liquidated damages are covered on the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract with a subcontracting plan is awarded. The contracting officer sends the award document to the SBA area office where the work will be done, sends the plan to the contract administration office, and checks that the contractor files its report in the eSRS within 30 days after the report ending date.

## Never confuse

Notice after award goes to the SBA area office [@CL-MKT-0454], while notice before award goes to the procurement center representative and is covered on the [Awards involving subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-awards.md) card. A copy of the plan sent to the contract administration office [@CL-MKT-0456] is not the same as the administrative contracting officer's own duties during performance, which are on the [Responsibilities of the administrative contracting officer for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-aco-responsibilities.md) card. The contracting officer shall initiate action to assess liquidated damages upon a recommendation from the administrative contracting officer, if one is assigned, or on receipt of other reliable evidence that assessing them is warranted, so a recommendation is not the only trigger [@CL-MKT-0453; @CL-MKT-0458].
