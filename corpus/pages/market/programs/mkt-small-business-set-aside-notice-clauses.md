## Purpose

Shows who may submit offers under the total and partial set-aside notice clauses, how offers are submitted on a partial set-aside, and how the non-set-aside portion is treated. The set-aside rules themselves are on the partial and class set-aside card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0841] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-6 and FAR 52.219-7 [@CL-MKT-0841; @CL-MKT-0843] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-6 [@CL-MKT-1187; @CL-MKT-1188]; RFO FAR 52.219-7 [@CL-MKT-1189] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0841] |
| Applies to | Contracts totally set aside, and orders set aside under multiple-award contracts, for the first clause; contracts partially set aside for the second [@CL-MKT-0842; @CL-MKT-0843] |

Under the total set-aside notice clause, offers are solicited only from small business concerns, and offers from concerns that are not small business concerns shall be considered nonresponsive and will be rejected [@CL-MKT-0841]. That clause applies only to contracts totally set aside for small business concerns and to orders set aside under multiple-award contracts as the clause describes [@CL-MKT-0842]. The partial set-aside notice clause applies only to contracts partially set aside for small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0843].

## How it works

Under the partial clause, a portion of the requirement, identified elsewhere in the solicitation, has been set aside for award to one or more small business concerns identified in FAR 19.000(a)(3) [@CL-MKT-0844]. Offers from concerns that do not qualify as small business concerns shall be considered nonresponsive and rejected on the set-aside portion of the requirement [@CL-MKT-0845]. Small business concerns may submit offers and compete for the non-set-aside portion and the set-aside portion [@CL-MKT-0846]. As the contracting officer checks, the offeror submits either a separate offer for each portion for which it wants to compete or one offer that includes all portions for which it wants to compete [@CL-MKT-0847].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.219-6(b) states that it applies only to contracts that have been set aside for small business concerns and to orders set aside for small business concerns under multiple-award contracts as described in 8.4 and 16.5 [@CL-MKT-1188], whereas codified FAR 52.219-6(b) states "totally set aside" and cites 8.405-5 and 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F) [@CL-MKT-0842]. RFO FAR 52.219-6(c)(1) states that offers are solicited only from small business concerns and that offers from concerns that are not small business concerns shall be considered nonresponsive and will be rejected [@CL-MKT-1187], and codified FAR 52.219-6(c)(1) states the same [@CL-MKT-0841]. RFO FAR 52.219-7(b) states that it applies only to contracts partially set aside for small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1189], and codified FAR 52.219-7(b) states the same [@CL-MKT-0843]. It also keeps the rules that a portion of the requirement is set aside, that offers from concerns that do not qualify as small business concerns shall be considered nonresponsive and shall be rejected on the set-aside portion, that small business concerns may compete for both portions, and that the offeror submits separate offers or one offer as the contracting officer checks [@CL-MKT-1190] [@CL-MKT-1191] [@CL-MKT-1192] [@CL-MKT-1193] [@CL-MKT-0844] [@CL-MKT-0845] [@CL-MKT-0846] [@CL-MKT-0847].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation sets aside the first of two portions for small business. A large company wants to compete. Answer: its offer on the set-aside portion is nonresponsive and rejected. The notice says small businesses may compete for both portions. How the large company is treated on the other portion is not stated in these clauses.

## Never confuse

The total set-aside clause (FAR 52.219-6) restricts the entire solicitation to small business concerns and rejects offers from non-small firms as nonresponsive [@CL-MKT-0841], whereas the partial set-aside clause applies only to contracts that have been partially set aside rather than closing the unreserved portion to other-than-small offerors [@CL-MKT-0843].
