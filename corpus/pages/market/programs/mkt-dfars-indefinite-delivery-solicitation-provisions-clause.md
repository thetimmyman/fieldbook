## Purpose

Lists the two extra solicitation provisions and the postaward debriefing clause that DFARS directs for indefinite-delivery work, and the conditions for the debriefing clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1867] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 216.506-70(a) and (b) [@CL-MKT-1866; @CL-MKT-1867] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1867] |
| Applies to | For the clause, competitive negotiated solicitations and contracts where a multiple-award contract is contemplated and orders may be valued at 15 million dollars or more [@CL-MKT-1867; @CL-MKT-1868] |

DFARS says to use the provisions at 252.215-7007, Notice of Intent to Resolicit, and 252.215-7008, Only One Offer, as prescribed at 215.371-6 and 215.408(3), respectively [@CL-MKT-1866]. This paragraph does not state when either provision applies; the prescriptions it names do [@CL-MKT-1866].

DFARS also says to use the clause at 252.216-7010, Postaward Debriefings for Task Orders and Delivery Orders, in competitive negotiated solicitations and contracts [@CL-MKT-1867]. Those solicitations and contracts include ones using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-MKT-1869]. The clause is used when a multiple-award contract is contemplated and task orders or delivery orders placed under the contract may be valued at 15 million dollars or more [@CL-MKT-1868]. All of these conditions apply together [@CL-MKT-1867; @CL-MKT-1868].

The section is written as a direction to use the provisions and the clause and names no official who applies it.

## How it works

The two provisions in the first paragraph are applied through their own prescriptions, so this section only points to them [@CL-MKT-1866]. The clause in the second paragraph is directed when three conditions are all present: a competitive negotiated solicitation or contract, a contemplated multiple-award contract, and orders under it that may be valued at 15 million dollars or more [@CL-MKT-1867; @CL-MKT-1868]. The value condition looks at the possible value of the orders, not at the value of the multiple-award contract [@CL-MKT-1868]. Commercial acquisitions using FAR part 12 procedures are inside the first condition, not outside it [@CL-MKT-1869].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A competitive negotiated solicitation contemplates a multiple-award contract, and orders under it may be valued at 20 million dollars. All three conditions are met, so the postaward debriefing clause is used [@CL-MKT-1867; @CL-MKT-1868]. If the same solicitation uses FAR part 12 procedures for commercial services, the clause is still used, because those solicitations are included [@CL-MKT-1869]. In a second solicitation no order is expected to be valued above 10 million dollars, so the order-value condition is not met and this paragraph does not call for the clause [@CL-MKT-1868]. In a third, a single-award contract is contemplated, so the multiple-award condition is not met [@CL-MKT-1868].

## Never confuse

The postaward debriefing clause is not the Only One Offer provision; they are directed by different paragraphs and conditions [@CL-MKT-1866; @CL-MKT-1867]. The order-value condition belongs to the debriefing clause, and the paragraph on the two provisions states no value condition [@CL-MKT-1866; @CL-MKT-1868]. The debriefing duty for orders of this size is described on [DoD ordering under multiple-award contracts: DFARS additions](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-ordering-multiple-award-contracts.md).
