## Purpose

Explains when orders are set aside for small business concerns under this clause, what the notice covers, and how the basic clause differs from Alternate I.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1116] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-13 and its Alternate I [@CL-MKT-1116; @CL-MKT-1119] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-13 [@CL-MKT-1161]; Alternate I: No RFO equivalent [@CL-MKT-1164] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1116] |
| Applies to | Orders set aside for the small business concerns identified in FAR 19.000(a)(3) [@CL-MKT-1116; @CL-MKT-1118] |

Under the basic clause, the contracting officer may set aside orders for the small business concerns identified in FAR 19.000(a)(3) [@CL-MKT-1116]. The contracting officer will give notice of the order or orders, if any, to be set aside for those concerns and of the applicable small business program [@CL-MKT-1117]. The notice and its restrictions apply only to the specific orders that have been set aside [@CL-MKT-1118].

Alternate I substitutes its paragraph (a) for paragraph (a) of the basic clause [@CL-MKT-1177]. Under Alternate I, the contracting officer will set aside orders for those concerns when the conditions of FAR 19.502-2 and the specific program eligibility requirements are met, as applicable [@CL-MKT-1119].

## How it works

Under the basic clause the set-aside is a choice, because the text says the contracting officer may set aside orders [@CL-MKT-1116]. Under Alternate I the set-aside becomes a duty when the stated conditions and the program eligibility requirements, as applicable, are met [@CL-MKT-1119]. The clause text identifies the concerns by pointing to FAR 19.000(a)(3), and the notice names the applicable small business program [@CL-MKT-1116; @CL-MKT-1117]. The notice may name no orders, and it reaches only the specific orders that were set aside [@CL-MKT-1117; @CL-MKT-1118]. The set-aside orders and clauses card shows where each clause is used.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of the basic clause keeps the rule that the contracting officer may set aside orders for the identified small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1116; @CL-MKT-1161], the notice of the order or orders, if any, and of the applicable small business program [@CL-MKT-1117; @CL-MKT-1162], and the limit of the notice and its restrictions to the specific orders set aside [@CL-MKT-1118; @CL-MKT-1163]. The RFO text prints Alternate I as [Reserved], with no substitute paragraph (a) [@CL-MKT-1164], where the codified Alternate I substitutes a paragraph (a) under which the contracting officer will set aside orders [@CL-MKT-1177; @CL-MKT-1119].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract with the basic clause has ten orders. The contracting officer sets aside three of them for the identified small business concerns and gives notice naming those three orders and the applicable program [@CL-MKT-1116; @CL-MKT-1117]. The notice and its restrictions apply to those three orders only, and not to the other seven [@CL-MKT-1118]. On a second contract that carries Alternate I, an order meets the conditions of FAR 19.502-2 and the program eligibility requirements, so the contracting officer will set it aside [@CL-MKT-1119]. On an order under that contract that does not meet them, the Alternate I paragraph does not require a set-aside [@CL-MKT-1119]. Under the RFO text, Alternate I is printed as [Reserved], with no substitute paragraph (a) [@CL-MKT-1164].

## Never confuse

A set-aside order is not a set-aside contract: the notice restricts the specific orders that have been set aside [@CL-MKT-1118]. The basic clause and Alternate I are also different: the basic clause says "may" and Alternate I says "will" once its conditions are met [@CL-MKT-1116; @CL-MKT-1119]. The notice clauses for total and partial set-asides are on the [Notice of total and partial small business set-aside clauses](mkt-small-business-set-aside-notice-clauses.md) card. The codified Alternate I turns the set-aside into a duty [@CL-MKT-1119], but the RFO text prints Alternate I as [Reserved] [@CL-MKT-1164], and its basic clause paragraph (a) says may [@CL-MKT-1161].
