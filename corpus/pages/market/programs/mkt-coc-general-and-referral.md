## Purpose

Shows what a Certificate of Competency is, which acquisitions it covers, and when and how a contracting officer refers a small business found nonresponsible to the SBA.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0475] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.601 and FAR 19.602-1 [@CL-MKT-0475; @CL-MKT-0478] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0475] |
| Applies to | Government acquisitions other than 8(a) sole-source awards [@CL-MKT-0476] |

A Certificate of Competency is the certificate issued by the Small Business Administration stating that the holder is responsible [@CL-MKT-0475], with respect to all elements of responsibility, including, but not limited to, capability, competency, capacity, credit, integrity, perseverance, tenacity and limitations on subcontracting, for the purpose of receiving and performing a specific Government contract [@CL-MKT-1444]. The program applies to all Government acquisitions except 8(a) sole-source awards [@CL-MKT-0476]. A contracting officer shall refer an apparent successful small business offeror determined to be nonresponsible to the SBA for a possible Certificate of Competency, even if the next acceptable offer is from a small business [@CL-MKT-0477].

Upon determining and documenting that an apparent successful small business offeror lacks certain elements of responsibility [@CL-MKT-0565], the contracting officer has two duties: to withhold contract award and to refer the matter [@CL-MKT-0478]. The referral goes to the cognizant SBA Government Contracting Area Office serving the area of the offeror's headquarters, in accordance with agency procedures, except in the listed cases where referral is not necessary [@CL-MKT-0479]. Referral is not necessary if the concern meets one of the alternatives: it is determined to be unqualified and ineligible because it does not meet the standard in 9.104-1(g), provided the determination is approved by the chief of the contracting office [@CL-MKT-0480]; or it is suspended or debarred under Executive Order 11246 or subpart 9.4 [@CL-MKT-0480].

## How it works

The referral shall include a notice that the concern has been determined to be nonresponsible, specifying the elements of responsibility found lacking, and, if applicable, a copy of the listed documents [@CL-MKT-0481]. The listed documents include the solicitation, the concern's final offer, the abstract of bids or the price negotiation memorandum, the preaward survey and the technical data package [@CL-MKT-0482]. The contracting officer shall withhold contract award for 15 business days, or longer if agreed to by the SBA and the contracting officer, after the SBA Area Office receives a referral that includes all required documentation [@CL-MKT-0483].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An apparent successful small business offeror is found to lack the capacity to perform. The contracting officer documents the finding and sends the SBA Area Office a notice that names capacity as the lacking element, with the solicitation, the offer and the preaward survey. The referral is complete when it arrives, so award is withheld for 15 business days from that day. If the SBA and the contracting officer agree on a longer period, award waits for that period instead.

## Never confuse

A Certificate of Competency is the SBA's certificate that the holder is responsible [@CL-MKT-0475; @CL-MKT-1444]; a contracting officer refers an apparent successful small business offeror determined to be nonresponsible to the SBA for a possible Certificate of Competency [@CL-MKT-0477]. How the contracting officer gathers responsibility information is on the [Obtaining responsibility information and documenting the determination](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-responsibility-information-and-determination.md) card. Which acquisitions are set aside for small business is on the [Small business set-asides](mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card. What the SBA does after a referral is on the [Issuing or denying a Certificate of Competency and resolving differences with SBA](mkt-coc-issuance-denial-and-resolution.md) card.
