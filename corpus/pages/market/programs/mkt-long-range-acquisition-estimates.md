## Purpose

Shows when an agency head may release long-range acquisition estimates, the conditions every release must meet, and how a release can be announced.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0090] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.404-1; FAR 5.404-2 [@CL-MKT-0090; @CL-MKT-0094] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0090] |
| Applies to | Long-range acquisition estimates released by an agency [@CL-MKT-0090] |

The agency head, or a designee, may release long-range acquisition estimates if the information will meet the listed conditions [@CL-MKT-0090]. One is that it will assist industry in its planning and facilitate meeting the acquisition requirements [@CL-MKT-0090]. Two others are that it will not encourage undesirable practices and will not indicate the existing or potential mobilization of the industry as a whole [@CL-MKT-0090; @CL-MKT-0091].

The agency head shall ensure that classified information is released through existing security channels and that the information is publicized as widely as practicable to all parties simultaneously [@CL-MKT-0092]. The agency head shall also ensure that each release states that the estimate is based on the best information available and that the information is subject to modification and is in no way binding on the Government [@CL-MKT-0092; @CL-MKT-0093].

## How it works

Release is the agency head's choice, tested against the listed conditions [@CL-MKT-0090; @CL-MKT-0091]. Further publicizing may be accomplished by announcing through the GPE that the estimates have been published and are obtainable, upon request, from the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-0094]. By the required statement, a released estimate is subject to modification and is in no way binding on the Government [@CL-MKT-0093].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency head releases an estimate of 2,000 units of an item for a fiscal year. The release says the estimate rests on the best information available, may change and does not bind the Government. A company that planned capacity for 2,000 units cannot hold the agency to that quantity.

## Never confuse

A long-range estimate is not a notice of a proposed action. The release states that more specific information on an item is not furnished until the proposed action is synopsized or the solicitation is issued, so the estimate is an early planning signal only [@CL-MKT-0093; @CL-MKT-0154]. The synopsis of an award is a further, different notice, covered on the [Synopses of contract awards](../feeds/mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card. The dollar amount of the simplified acquisition threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.
