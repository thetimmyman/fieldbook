## Purpose

Sets out the clause text that divides the roles in an 8(a) contract: what the Small Business Administration (SBA) agrees to in the contract, and what the subcontractor agrees to in the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0990; @CL-MKT-0995]. The program rules for offering and administering this work are on the [offering and acceptance](mkt-8a-offering-and-acceptance.md) and [administration and release](mkt-8a-administration-and-release.md) cards.

## The rule

| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-11; FAR 52.219-12 [@CL-MKT-0990; @CL-MKT-1041] |

| Applies to | Contracts under which SBA subcontracts the work to an eligible concern, and the subcontracts awarded under them [@CL-MKT-0990; @CL-MKT-1041] |

In the contract clause, SBA agrees to furnish the supplies or services set forth in the contract according to the specifications and the terms and conditions of the contract by subcontracting with an eligible concern under section 8(a) of the Small Business Act [@CL-MKT-0990]. If SBA does not award a subcontract for all or part of the work, the contract may be terminated in whole or in part without cost to either party [@CL-MKT-0991]. The subcontract clause records that SBA has entered into the contract with the contracting agency and that a copy of the contract is attached to the subcontract [@CL-MKT-1041].

## How it works

Except for novation agreements, SBA delegates to the contracting agency the responsibility for administering the subcontract, with complete authority to take any action on behalf of the Government under the terms and conditions of the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0992]. The agency must give SBA advance notice before it issues a final notice terminating a subcontractor's right to proceed, in whole or in part, for default or for the convenience of the Government [@CL-MKT-0993]. SBA also agrees that payments under any subcontract are made directly to the subcontractor by the contracting agency [@CL-MKT-0994]. It agrees that the subcontractor has the right of appeal from decisions of the contracting officer cognizable under the Disputes clause of the subcontract [@CL-MKT-1039]. It agrees to notify the agency's contracting officer immediately upon notification by the subcontractor that the owner or owners on whom 8(a) eligibility was based plan to relinquish ownership or control of the concern [@CL-MKT-1040].

In the subcontract clause, the subcontractor agrees that it will, for and on behalf of SBA, fulfill and perform all of the requirements of the contract for the consideration stated in it, and that it has read and is familiar with every part of the contract [@CL-MKT-0995]. It acknowledges that, except for novation agreements, SBA has delegated to the contracting agency responsibility for administering the subcontract, with complete authority to take any action on behalf of the Government, although the end of that sentence is garbled in the stored original and its exact wording is not established [@CL-MKT-1042; @CL-MKT-9354; @CL-MKT-1203]. It must notify the contracting officer in writing immediately upon entering an agreement, oral or written, to transfer all or part of its stock or other ownership interest to any other party [@CL-MKT-0996]. Payments, including any progress payments, are made directly to the subcontractor by the contracting agency [@CL-MKT-0997].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.219-11 states SBA's agreements to subcontract with an eligible concern under section 8(a), to the termination without cost if SBA awards no subcontract, to the agency's administration of the subcontract with advance notice to SBA before a final termination notice, to direct payment by the agency, to the subcontractor's right of appeal, and to notice of a planned relinquishment of ownership or control [@CL-MKT-1194] [@CL-MKT-1195] [@CL-MKT-1196] [@CL-MKT-1197] [@CL-MKT-1198] [@CL-MKT-1199] [@CL-MKT-1200] [@CL-MKT-0990] [@CL-MKT-0991] [@CL-MKT-0992] [@CL-MKT-0993] [@CL-MKT-0994] [@CL-MKT-1039] [@CL-MKT-1040]. RFO FAR 52.219-12 states the statement that SBA has entered into the contract, the subcontractor's agreement to perform it, the delegation of administration except for novation agreements, the written notice of a stock or ownership transfer agreement, and direct payment [@CL-MKT-1201] [@CL-MKT-1202] [@CL-MKT-1203] [@CL-MKT-1204] [@CL-MKT-1205] [@CL-MKT-1041] [@CL-MKT-0995] [@CL-MKT-1042] [@CL-MKT-0996] [@CL-MKT-0997].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

SBA holds a contract for 1,000,000 and awards a subcontract for 900,000 of the work to an eligible concern. SBA awards no subcontract for the other 100,000, so that part of the contract may be terminated without cost to either party. The contracting agency pays the subcontractor directly, including any progress payments. Before the agency issues a final notice terminating the subcontractor's right to proceed for default, it gives SBA advance notice. If the subcontractor agrees to transfer part of its stock, it notifies the contracting officer in writing immediately, whether the agreement is oral or written.

## Never confuse

FAR 52.219-11 binds SBA in the prime contract with the procuring agency [@CL-MKT-0990], whereas FAR 52.219-12 binds the 8(a) participant as subcontractor performing on SBA's behalf with the prime contract attached [@CL-MKT-0995; @CL-MKT-1041], rather than merging both relationships into a single bilateral commercial subcontract.
