## Purpose

Sets out the clause text that divides the roles in an 8(a) contract: what the Small Business Administration (SBA) agrees to in the contract, and what the subcontractor agrees to in the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0990; @CL-MKT-0995]. The program rules for offering and administering this work are on the [offering and acceptance](mkt-8a-offering-and-acceptance.md) and [administration and release](mkt-8a-administration-and-release.md) cards.

In the contract clause, SBA agrees to furnish the supplies or services in the contract by subcontracting with an eligible concern under section 8(a) of the Small Business Act [@CL-MKT-0990]. If SBA does not award a subcontract for all or part of the work, the contract may be terminated in whole or in part without cost to either party [@CL-MKT-0991]. The subcontract clause records that SBA has entered into the contract with the contracting agency and that a copy of the contract is attached to the subcontract [@CL-MKT-1041].

## How it works

Except for novation agreements, SBA delegates to the contracting agency the responsibility for administering the subcontract, with complete authority to take any action on behalf of the Government under the terms and conditions of the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0992]. The agency must give SBA advance notice before it issues a final notice terminating a subcontractor's right to proceed, in whole or in part, for default or for the convenience of the Government [@CL-MKT-0993]. SBA also agrees that payments under any subcontract are made directly to the subcontractor by the contracting agency [@CL-MKT-0994]. It agrees that the subcontractor has the right of appeal from decisions of the contracting officer cognizable under the Disputes clause of the subcontract [@CL-MKT-1039]. It agrees to notify the agency's contracting officer immediately upon notification by the subcontractor that the owner or owners on whom 8(a) eligibility was based plan to relinquish ownership or control of the concern [@CL-MKT-1040].

In the subcontract clause, the subcontractor agrees that it will, for and on behalf of SBA, fulfill and perform all of the requirements of the contract for the consideration stated in it, and that it has read and is familiar with every part of the contract [@CL-MKT-0995]. It acknowledges that, except for novation agreements, SBA has delegated to the contracting agency responsibility for administering the subcontract, with complete authority to take any action on behalf of the Government [@CL-MKT-1042]. It must notify the contracting officer in writing immediately upon entering an agreement, oral or written, to transfer all or part of its stock or other ownership interest to any other party [@CL-MKT-0996]. Payments, including any progress payments, are made directly to the subcontractor by the contracting agency [@CL-MKT-0997]. The blanks for the contract number and for the names of the contracting agency and the subcontractor are contract-specific fill-ins.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

SBA holds a contract for 1,000,000 and awards a subcontract for 900,000 of the work to an eligible concern. SBA awards no subcontract for the other 100,000, so that part of the contract may be terminated without cost to either party. The contracting agency pays the subcontractor directly, including any progress payments. Before the agency issues a final notice terminating the subcontractor's right to proceed for default, it gives SBA advance notice. If the subcontractor agrees to transfer part of its stock, it notifies the contracting officer in writing immediately, whether the agreement is oral or written.

## Never confuse

The two clauses are two halves of one arrangement. The contract clause states what SBA agrees to, and the subcontract clause states what the subcontractor agrees and acknowledges [@CL-MKT-0990; @CL-MKT-0995]. In both clauses the agency's authority to administer the subcontract does not extend to novation agreements [@CL-MKT-0992; @CL-MKT-1042]. These conditions are not the competition notice or the award clause, which are on the [award and competition notification](mkt-section-8a-award-and-competition-notification.md) card.
