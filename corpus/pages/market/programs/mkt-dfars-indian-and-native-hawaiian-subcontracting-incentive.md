## Purpose

Shows the best-efforts duty, who is eligible, how eligibility is relied on and challenged, and the incentive a contractor may request for subcontracts with these firms.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0938] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.226-7001 [@CL-MKT-0938] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0938] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, prescribed in DFARS 226.104, and subcontracts exceeding a stated dollar amount through the flowdown paragraph [@CL-MKT-0938; @CL-MKT-0953; @CL-MKT-0943] |

The contractor shall use its best efforts to give Indian organizations, Indian-owned economic enterprises, and Native Hawaiian small business concerns the maximum practicable opportunity to participate in the subcontracts it awards, to the fullest extent consistent with efficient performance of the contract [@CL-MKT-0938]. The incentive amount that may be requested is 5 percent of the estimated cost, target cost, or fixed price included in the subcontract at the time of award to such a firm [@CL-MKT-0941]. No incentive payment will be made while a challenge is pending or if a subcontractor is determined to be an ineligible participant [@CL-MKT-0942].

In the case of a subcontract for commercial products or commercial services, the contractor may receive an incentive payment only if the subcontracted items are produced or manufactured in whole or in part by an Indian organization, Indian-owned economic enterprise, or Native Hawaiian small business concern [@CL-MKT-0968]. The contractor has the burden of proving the amount claimed and shall assert its request for an incentive payment prior to completion of contract performance [@CL-MKT-0969].

A representation of eligibility by one of these firms may be relied on by the contracting officer and the contractor, acting in good faith, unless an interested party challenges its status or the contracting officer has independent reason to question that status [@CL-MKT-9334; @CL-MKT-0939]. An Indian-owned economic enterprise is an Indian-owned (as determined by the Secretary of the Interior) commercial, industrial, or business activity established or organized for the purpose of profit, provided that Indian ownership constitutes not less than 51 percent of the enterprise [@CL-MKT-0940]. The contractor shall insert the substance of the clause, including its paragraph (g), in all subcontracts exceeding $500,000 [@CL-MKT-0943].

## How it works

The contractor shall use its best efforts to give the listed firms the maximum practicable opportunity to participate in the subcontracts it awards [@CL-MKT-0938]. Eligibility may be taken from the firm's own representation by the contracting officer and the contractor, acting in good faith, unless it is challenged or the contracting officer has independent reason to question it [@CL-MKT-9334; @CL-MKT-0939]. No incentive payment will be made while a challenge is pending [@CL-MKT-0942]. The incentive amount that may be requested is 5 percent of the estimated cost, target cost, or fixed price included in the subcontract at the time of award [@CL-MKT-0941]. The contractor has the burden of proving the amount claimed and shall assert its request prior to completion of contract performance [@CL-MKT-0969]; for a subcontract for commercial products or commercial services, the contractor may receive an incentive payment only if the subcontracted items are produced or manufactured in whole or in part by one of the listed firms [@CL-MKT-0968]. Other paragraphs of the printed clause, including those on who decides a challenge and on how an incentive payment is authorized and passed on, are not drafted on this card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime awards a subcontract with a target cost of 2 million to an eligible Indian-owned economic enterprise. Another bidder challenges the enterprise's status after award.

Answer: the amount that may be requested is 5 percent of 2 million, which is 100,000, and the prime must prove that amount and assert its request before contract performance is complete. No incentive payment is made while the challenge is pending, and none is made if the enterprise is found ineligible.

## Never confuse

The incentive amount that may be requested is 5 percent of the estimated cost, target cost, or fixed price included in the subcontract at the time of award [@CL-MKT-0941]. The clause lets the contractor and the contracting officer, acting in good faith, rely on a firm's own representation of its eligibility unless it is challenged or the contracting officer has independent reason to question it [@CL-MKT-9334; @CL-MKT-0939]. The DoD subcontracting plan clause is on the [DFARS small business subcontracting plan clause for DoD contracts](mkt-dfars-dod-small-business-subcontracting-plan.md) card. The general policy on small business opportunities is on the [Small business general policy](mkt-small-business-general-policy.md) card.
