## Purpose

Covers the DoD guidance on using options, the checks made before an option is exercised, and when the foreign military sales and surge option clauses are used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1758] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 217.202, 217.207 and 217.208-70 [@CL-MKT-1758; @CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1761] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1758] |
| Applies to | DoD solicitations and contracts that contain options, and DoD exercise of options [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1761] |

For guidance on the use of options, including options with foreign military sales and options with sole source major systems for U.S. and U.S./foreign military sales combined procurements, the supplement points to its procedures, guidance and information [@CL-MKT-1758]. For a contract initially awarded from the competitive selection of a proposal resulting from a broad agency announcement, it refers to DFARS 234.005-1 for the use of contract options for the development and demonstration or initial production of technology developed under the contract [@CL-MKT-1801], or for the delivery of initial or additional items [@CL-MKT-1802].

In addition to the requirements at FAR 17.207(c), an option is to be exercised only after the determinations and verifications listed in the DFARS paragraph [@CL-MKT-1803]. The FAR paragraph it adds to lets the contracting officer exercise options only after determining, among other things listed there, that funds are available and that the requirement covered by the option fulfills an existing Government need [@CL-CTF-1313], and that exercise is the most advantageous method of fulfilling the Government's need, price and other factors considered [@CL-CTF-1396]. The added checks are the following.

- Determining that the contractor's record in the System for Award Management database is active and that the contractor's unique entity identifier number, Commercial and Government Entity code, name and physical address are accurately reflected in the contract document [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1759].
- The supplement refers to its procedures, guidance and information for the requirement to perform cost or price analysis of spare parts before exercising any option for firm-fixed-price contracts containing spare parts [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1804; @CL-MKT-1451].
- Exercise also requires working with the program office or requiring activity to verify in the Supplier Performance Risk System that both of the matters listed in the next two items are met [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1451; @CL-MKT-1805; @CL-MKT-1452].
- First, the verification covers whether the summary level score of a current NIST SP 800-171 DoD Assessment, not more than 3 years old unless the solicitation specifies a lesser time, is posted for each covered contractor information system that is relevant to an offer, contract, task order or delivery order [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1805; @CL-MKT-1760; @CL-MKT-1452].
- Second, if the contractor is required to have a Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification status at a specific level, the verification covers whether the contractor has a current status at the level required by the contract, or higher, for each unique identifier applicable to each contractor information system that processes, stores or transmits Federal contract information or controlled unclassified information [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1805; @CL-MKT-1452; @CL-MKT-1806; @CL-MKT-1807].

## How it works

The foreign military sales option clause is used, in its basic or alternate form, in solicitations and contracts when an option may be used for foreign military sales requirements [@CL-MKT-1761]. The basic clause is used when the foreign military sales country is known at the time of solicitation or award, and the alternate I clause when the country is not known at that time [@CL-MKT-1762]. Neither is used in contracts for establishment or replenishment of DoD inventories or stocks, or in acquisitions made under DoD cooperative logistics support arrangements [@CL-MKT-1763].

When a surge option is needed in support of industrial capability production planning, the surge option clause is used in solicitations and contracts [@CL-MKT-1764]. The percentage or quantity of increase the option represents is inserted in paragraph (a) of the clause to ensure adequate quantities are available to meet item requirements [@CL-MKT-1808]. The 30 days in paragraphs (b)(2) and (d)(1) of the clause is changed to longer periods, if appropriate [@CL-MKT-1809], and the 24-month period in paragraph (c)(3) is changed, if appropriate [@CL-MKT-1810].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD contract carries an option that may be used for foreign military sales requirements. The customer country is not yet known, and the contract is not for establishment or replenishment of DoD inventories. The solicitation and contract therefore use the alternate I clause [@CL-MKT-1761; @CL-MKT-1762; @CL-MKT-1763]. The program also needs a surge option for industrial capability production planning, so the surge option clause is used, with the planned 25 percent increase inserted in paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-MKT-1764; @CL-MKT-1808].

Later, before an option is exercised, the contractor's System for Award Management record must be active, and its name, address, unique entity identifier and code must match the contract document [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1759]. The contractor's posted NIST assessment summary score is 4 years old and the solicitation did not specify a lesser time, so the score is older than the 3-year limit. The verification fails, and the option is not exercised until a current score is posted [@CL-MKT-1803; @CL-MKT-1805; @CL-MKT-1760].

## Never confuse

The clause prescriptions at DFARS 217.208-70 govern when the foreign military sales option clause and the surge option clause are used in solicitations and contracts [@CL-MKT-1761; @CL-MKT-1764], while DFARS 217.207(c) states determinations and verifications required before an option is exercised in addition to the requirements at FAR 17.207(c) [@CL-MKT-1803]. The foreign military sales option clause is used when an option may be used for foreign military sales requirements [@CL-MKT-1761], and the surge option clause is used when a surge option is needed in support of industrial capability production planning [@CL-MKT-1764].
