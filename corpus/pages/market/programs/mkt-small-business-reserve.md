## Purpose

Explains what the reserve notice in a solicitation says, what an eligible concern must submit, and when orders may be issued directly under a reserve.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1120] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-31 and FAR 52.219-32 [@CL-MKT-1120; @CL-MKT-1122] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-31 [@CL-MKT-1181]; FAR 52.219-32: No RFO equivalent [@CL-MKT-1183] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.219-31: Mar 2020 [@CL-MKT-1181]; FAR 52.219-32: MAR 2020 [@CL-MKT-9437] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1120] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that contain a reserve, and contracts that were reserved for the identified small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1120; @CL-MKT-1122] |

The provision states that the solicitation contains a reserve for one or more small business concerns identified at FAR 19.000(a)(3) and that the small business program eligibility requirements apply [@CL-MKT-1120]. A concern eligible for participation in the reserve must submit one offer that addresses each portion of the solicitation for which it wants to compete, and award of the contract will be based on criteria identified elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1121].

The orders clause applies only to contracts that were reserved for any of those small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1122]. If there is only one contract award to any one type of small business concern identified in FAR 19.000(a)(3) as a result of the reserve, the contracting officer may issue an order or orders directly to the concern [@CL-MKT-1123].

## How it works

Two texts work together: the provision goes in the solicitation, and the orders clause applies to the resulting reserved contracts [@CL-MKT-1120; @CL-MKT-1122]. The provision tells eligible concerns to submit a single offer that covers every portion they want, and the award criteria stay those identified elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1121]. The direct-order authority turns on a count: one contract award to one type of concern as a result of the reserve [@CL-MKT-1123]. The authority is permissive, because the text says the contracting officer may issue orders directly [@CL-MKT-1123]. FAR 19.504(c)(1)(i) provides that the contracting officer may set aside orders for the small business concerns identified in FAR 19.000(a)(3) when there are two or more contract awards for that type of concern [@CL-MKT-1245]. FAR 19.504(c)(1)(ii) states the direct-order authority for work that the concern can perform [@CL-MKT-1246]. Who decides to use a reserve is on the [Set-aside determinations, rejected SBA recommendations and small business reserves](mkt-set-aside-determinations-sba-recommendations-and-reserves.md) card.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.219-31 states that the solicitation contains a reserve and that the small business program eligibility requirements apply [@CL-MKT-1120; @CL-MKT-1181], and that an eligible concern submits one offer addressing each portion it wants to compete for, with award based on criteria identified elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1121; @CL-MKT-1182]. RFO FAR 52.219-32 is marked as reserved [@CL-MKT-1183], whereas codified FAR 52.219-32 applies only to reserved contracts and allows the contracting officer to issue orders directly when there is only one contract award to any one type of small business concern [@CL-MKT-1122; @CL-MKT-1123].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation contains a reserve for two types of small business concern, type A and type B. Each eligible concern submits one offer covering the portions it wants [@CL-MKT-1121]. The result is one contract award to a type A concern and three contract awards to type B concerns. The contracting officer may issue an order directly to the single type A awardee, because there is only one award to that type [@CL-MKT-1123]. The direct-issue sentence does not apply to the type B awardees, because there is more than one award to that type; for those awardees the contracting officer may instead set aside orders under FAR 19.504(c)(1)(i) [@CL-MKT-1245]. A contract that was not reserved is outside the orders clause [@CL-MKT-1122]. In RFO FAR part 52, section 52.219-32 is marked as reserved [@CL-MKT-1183].

## Never confuse

A small business reserve under FAR 52.219-31 reserves award slots on a multiple-award solicitation for FAR 19.000(a)(3) small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1120; @CL-MKT-1122], unlike [Notice of set-aside of orders](mkt-set-aside-of-orders.md), where FAR 52.219-13 sets aside competition on individual postaward orders under an unreserved or reserved multiple-award contract [@CL-MKT-1116].
