## Purpose

Explains who counts as a DoD acquisition official and a nondefense agency, the certification a nondefense agency must give before it acquires for the Department of Defense above the simplified acquisition threshold, the compliance test, the duties on each side, and the waiver.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1751] |
| Codified citation | FAR 17.701 and 17.703 [@CL-MKT-1751; @CL-MKT-1752] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1751] |
| Applies to | Requests by DoD acquisition officials for a nondefense agency to conduct an acquisition on behalf of the Department of Defense [@CL-MKT-1751; @CL-MKT-1752] |

A DoD acquisition official is a DoD contracting officer, or any other DoD official authorized to approve a direct acquisition or an assisted acquisition on behalf of the Department of Defense [@CL-MKT-1751]. A nondefense agency is any department or agency of the Federal Government other than the Department of Defense [@CL-MKT-1789].

The section also defines a nondefense agency that is an element of the intelligence community. It means the agencies identified in the intelligence community provision of the United States Code, which include the Office of the Director of National Intelligence and the Central Intelligence Agency [@CL-MKT-1790]. They also include the intelligence elements of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, Department of Energy and Drug Enforcement Agency, the Bureau of Intelligence and Research of the Department of State, and the Office of Intelligence and Analysis of the Department of the Treasury [@CL-MKT-1791]. The list continues with the Office of Intelligence and Analysis of the Department of Homeland Security and the Office of Intelligence of the Coast Guard [@CL-MKT-1792], and such other elements of any department or agency as the President has designated, or the Director of National Intelligence and the head of the department or agency concerned have jointly designated, as an element of the intelligence community [@CL-MKT-1793].

A DoD acquisition official may request a nondefense agency to conduct an acquisition on behalf of the Department of Defense in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold only if the head of the nondefense agency conducting the acquisition has certified that the agency will comply with applicable procurement requirements for that fiscal year, except when waived under the waiver paragraph [@CL-MKT-1752; @CL-MKT-1753].

A nondefense agency is compliant with applicable procurement requirements if its procurement policies, procedures and internal controls applicable to procuring supplies and services on behalf of DoD [@CL-MKT-1794], and the manner in which they are administered, are adequate to ensure its compliance with the Federal Acquisition Regulation and other laws and regulations that apply to procurements of supplies and services by Federal agencies [@CL-MKT-1795]. They must also be adequate to ensure compliance with laws and regulations that apply to procurements made by DoD through other Federal agencies, including DoD financial management regulations, the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) and DoD class deviations [@CL-MKT-1796], and the DFARS Procedures, Guidance, and Information [@CL-MKT-1797].

## How it works

Within 30 days of the beginning of each fiscal year, nondefense agency certifications of compliance are submitted to the Principal Director, Defense Pricing and Contracting at Department of Defense, Office of the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment), Defense Pricing and Contracting, Contract Policy, Room 3B938, 3060 Defense Pentagon, Washington DC 20301-3060 [@CL-MKT-1754; @CL-MKT-1711]. The head of the nondefense agency conducting the acquisition on DoD's behalf makes the certification [@CL-MKT-1753].

The DoD acquisition official shall provide the servicing nondefense agency contracting officer with any DoD-unique terms, conditions, other related statutes, regulations, directives and other applicable requirements for incorporation into the order or contract [@CL-MKT-1755]. If there are no DoD-unique requirements beyond the Federal Acquisition Regulation, the official shall so inform that contracting officer in writing [@CL-MKT-1756]. Those terms are provided for incorporation into the order or contract [@CL-MKT-1755]. Nondefense agency contracting officers are responsible for ensuring that the support they give in response to DoD's request meets the compliance test [@CL-MKT-1798].

The limitation on requests does not apply to the acquisition of supplies and services on behalf of DoD by a nondefense agency during any fiscal year for which the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment has determined in writing that it is necessary in the interest of DoD to acquire supplies and services through the nondefense agency during the fiscal year [@CL-MKT-1757; @CL-MKT-1799]. The written determination shall identify the acquisition categories to which the waiver applies [@CL-MKT-1800].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD contracting officer, who is a DoD acquisition official [@CL-MKT-1751], asks a civilian agency, which is a nondefense agency [@CL-MKT-1789], to run a 3,000,000 dollar competition. The amount is above the simplified acquisition threshold. The civilian agency head has certified compliance for the fiscal year, so the certification condition for the request is met [@CL-MKT-1752; @CL-MKT-1753]. The agency submitted the certification within 30 days of the start of the fiscal year [@CL-MKT-1754].

The DoD contracting officer sends the servicing nondefense agency contracting officer the DoD-unique terms, or tells that officer in writing that there are none beyond the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-MKT-1755; @CL-MKT-1756].

Had the civilian agency not certified, the request could go ahead only if the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment had determined in writing that using the agency was necessary in the interest of DoD for that fiscal year, with the written determination naming the acquisition categories covered, which would have to include this one [@CL-MKT-1757; @CL-MKT-1799; @CL-MKT-1800].

## Never confuse

Under FAR 17.703(a) and (e), the head of the nondefense agency conducting the acquisition on DoD's behalf certifies compliance for the fiscal year, unless waived by a written determination of the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment identifying the acquisition categories to which the waiver applies [@CL-MKT-1753; @CL-MKT-1757; @CL-MKT-1800]. Nondefense agencies acquiring supplies and services on behalf of DoD must also comply with the policy and procedures at subpart 17.7 in addition to subpart 17.5 [@CL-MKT-1728].

Which contracts the company performs for a nondefense agency acting for the Department of Defense, and who confirms the DoD-unique terms in each order. [@Q-004]
