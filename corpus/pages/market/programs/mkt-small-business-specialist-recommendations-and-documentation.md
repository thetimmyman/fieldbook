## Purpose

Explains whose recommendations the contracting officer must consider on small business program awards, and what the contract file must document when an acquisition is not set aside or a recommendation is not accepted.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1306] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.202 and FAR 19.506 [@CL-MKT-1306; @CL-MKT-1308] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1306] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers considering recommendations on small business program awards, and the file documentation for acquisitions not set aside [@CL-MKT-1306; @CL-MKT-1308] |

Contracting officers shall consider recommendations of the agency Director of the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization, or for the Department of Defense the Director of the Office of Small Business Programs, or the Director's designee [@CL-MKT-1306]. The recommendations are as to whether a particular acquisition should be awarded under subpart 19.5, 19.8, 19.13, 19.14, or 19.15 [@CL-MKT-1405].

Agencies shall establish procedures, including dollar thresholds, for review of acquisitions by the Director or the Director's designee for the purpose of making these recommendations [@CL-MKT-1307]. The section states no dollar figure; the agency procedures set the thresholds [@CL-MKT-1307].

## How it works

The contracting officer documents the rationale when a contract is not totally set aside for small business in accordance with 19.502-2 [@CL-MKT-1308]. The contracting officer also documents the rationale when a multiple-award contract is not partially set aside, not reserved, and does not allow for setting aside of orders, when these authorities could have been used [@CL-MKT-1309].

The contracting officer documents the contract file whenever the Director's recommendations are not accepted, in accordance with 19.506 [@CL-MKT-1406]. Under 19.506, the documentation includes, if applicable, the rationale for not accepting the recommendations made by the Director or, for the Department of Defense, the Director of the Office of Small Business Programs [@CL-MKT-1407].

Documentation under 19.506 is not required if a contract award is anticipated to a small business under subpart 19.5, 19.8, 19.13, 19.14, or 19.15 [@CL-MKT-1408].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency issues a multiple-award contract for services. The contract is not partially set aside, not reserved, and does not allow orders to be set aside, although these authorities could have been used, so the contracting officer documents the rationale [@CL-MKT-1309]. The agency's Director of the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization had recommended a set-aside, and the contracting officer did not accept it, so the file also records the rationale for not accepting the recommendation [@CL-MKT-1406; @CL-MKT-1407]. In a separate acquisition, award is anticipated to a small business under one of the listed subparts, so documentation under 19.506 is not required [@CL-MKT-1408].

## Never confuse

A recommendation is not a decision: the contracting officer considers the recommendation and documents the file when it is not accepted [@CL-MKT-1306; @CL-MKT-1406]. The recommending official on this card is the agency Director of the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization (or the Department of Defense equivalent), not the agency small business specialist who receives notice of a set-aside withdrawal [@CL-MKT-1306; @CL-MKT-1304]. The documentation duty under 19.506 is not universal, because an award anticipated to a small business under the listed subparts is outside it [@CL-MKT-1408]. The set-aside decisions themselves are on [the determinations and reserves card](mkt-set-aside-determinations-sba-recommendations-and-reserves.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the acquisition file holds the documented rationale wherever a set-aside was available and not used. [@Q-003]
