## Purpose

Shows who may protest a firm's service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) status, the time limits for filing, and what the Office of Hearings and Appeals (OHA) decides.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0580] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.307 [@CL-MKT-0580] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0580] |
| Applies to | Protests of the apparently successful offeror's SDVOSB status [@CL-MKT-0580; @CL-MKT-0581] |

For sole source acquisitions, the contracting officer, the Department of Veterans Affairs, or SBA may protest the apparently successful offeror's SDVOSB status [@CL-MKT-0580]. For all other acquisitions, any interested party may protest it [@CL-MKT-0581]. An interested party that wants to protest both size and SDVOSB status shall file two separate protests [@CL-MKT-0582]. The size route is on the [Protesting a small business representation](../taxonomy/mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) card.

For sealed bid acquisitions, an interested party's protest to the contracting officer is to be received by close of business on the fifth business day after bid opening, and contracting officers are referred to a separate paragraph [@CL-MKT-0583]. For negotiated acquisitions—including orders placed under multiple-award contracts where the contracting officer requested rerepresentation for the order, and orders set aside for SDVOSBs under multiple-award contracts that are not partially or totally set aside or reserved for SDVOSB concerns—an interested party's protest must be received by close of business on the fifth business day after receipt of the special notification from the contracting officer that identifies the apparently successful offeror [@CL-MKT-0584; @CL-MKT-1813; @CL-MKT-1833]. A protest received after the designated time limits is untimely except as the listed exceptions provide, and one listed exception is that the VA or SBA may file an SDVOSB status protest at any time [@CL-MKT-0585].

## How it works

The contracting officer forwards all protests to the U.S. Small Business Administration, Office of Hearings and Appeals, 409 Third Street SW, Washington, DC 20416, or by email at the SBA Office of Hearings and Appeals filing address that the paragraph gives, marked "Attn: SDVOSB Status Protest" [@CL-MKT-0586; @CL-MKT-1814]. OHA determines the merits of the status protest, has no standard timeline for issuing decisions, and issues a decision that is effective immediately and final [@CL-MKT-0587]. If the contracting officer withheld award and OHA sustained the protest and found that the concern is not an SDVOSB, the contracting officer shall not award the contract to the protested concern [@CL-MKT-0588].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer names the apparently successful offeror for a negotiated, set-aside service contract and sends the special notification on a Monday. A competitor files a written status protest on Thursday, inside the fifth business day. The contracting officer forwards the protest to OHA and withholds award. OHA sustains the protest and finds that the awardee is not an SDVOSB. The contracting officer may not award the contract to that firm.

## Never confuse

An SDVOSB status protest asks whether the firm is an SDVOSB, and a size protest asks whether it is small. A party that wants both shall file two separate protests [@CL-MKT-0582]. Here OHA decides the merits [@CL-MKT-0587], while for women-owned status protests SBA determines the merits within 15 business days after receipt of a protest, or within any extension of that time granted by the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-0594], and its determination is final unless overturned on appeal by OHA [@CL-MKT-0595]. The women-owned route is on the [Protesting a firm's economically disadvantaged or women-owned small business status](mkt-edwosb-wosb-status-protests.md) card. HUBZone status protests have their own card: [Protesting a firm's HUBZone status](../taxonomy/mkt-hubzone-status-protests.md). The conditions for an SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source award are on the [Service-disabled veteran-owned small business status, set-asides and sole-source awards](mkt-sdvosb-status-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) card.
