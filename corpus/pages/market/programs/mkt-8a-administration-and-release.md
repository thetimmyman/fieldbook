## Purpose

Shows who assigns administration of a program contract, what the contracting officer does when the contractor plans to transfer ownership or control, when a long contract's option may not be exercised, and how a requirement leaves the program.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0519] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.812 and FAR 19.815 [@CL-MKT-0519; @CL-MKT-0522] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0519] |
| Applies to | Contracts with 8(a) contractors and follow-on requirements to requirements accepted into the 8(a) program [@CL-MKT-0519; @CL-MKT-0522] |

The contracting officer shall assign contract administration functions, as required, based on the location of the 8(a) contractor [@CL-MKT-0519]. Once a requirement has been accepted by SBA into the 8(a) program, any follow-on requirements shall remain in the 8(a) program unless SBA agrees to release the requirement for a follow-on non-8(a) procurement [@CL-MKT-0522] or there is a mandatory source [@CL-MKT-0566]. The requirement that a follow-on procurement must be released from the program before it is fulfilled outside the program does not apply to task or delivery orders offered to and accepted into the 8(a) program where the basic contract was not accepted into the program [@CL-MKT-0730].

To obtain release of a requirement for a follow-on non-8(a) procurement, other than a mandatory source, the contracting officer shall make a written request to, and receive concurrence from, the SBA Associate Administrator for Business Development [@CL-MKT-0523].

## How it works

Generally, a requirement previously accepted into the 8(a) program will only be released for procurements outside the program when the contracting activity agency agrees to set aside the requirement under the small business, HUBZone, SDVOSB or WOSB programs [@CL-MKT-0524]. An 8(a) contract must be terminated for convenience if the 8(a) contractor transfers ownership or control of the firm or the contract is transferred or novated to another firm, unless the Administrator of the SBA waives the requirement for termination [@CL-MKT-0567]. A waiver is permitted only if the contractor's request is made to the SBA before the actual relinquishment of ownership or control, except that in the case of death or incapacity the waiver must be submitted within 60 calendar days after the occurrence [@CL-MKT-0568]. When the contracting officer receives information that an 8(a) contractor is planning to transfer ownership or control to another firm, the contracting officer shall take action immediately to preserve the option of waiving the termination requirement [@CL-MKT-0520]. Unless a waiver is approved by the SBA, the contracting officer must terminate the contract for convenience upon receipt of a written request by the SBA [@CL-MKT-0521].

For 8(a) contracts exceeding 5 years including options, the contracting officer shall verify in DSBS or SAM that the concern is an SBA-certified 8(a) participant no more than 120 days before the end of the fifth year of the contract [@CL-MKT-0569]. If the concern is not an SBA-certified 8(a) participant, the contracting officer shall not exercise the option [@CL-MKT-0570]. This card does not restate the notice rules for new requirements, the mandatory source notice, the distribution of contracts or the steps for a waiver request by the agency.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An 8(a) contractor is performing a services requirement that the SBA accepted into the program. The agency wants the follow-on work done outside the program. The contracting officer writes to the SBA Associate Administrator for Business Development, says the agency will set the follow-on aside for small business, and waits for the SBA's concurrence. Until the SBA concurs, the follow-on stays in the program. Separately, if the contractor says it will sell the firm, the contracting officer acts at once to keep the option of a waiver open.

## Never confuse

Release of a requirement from the program needs the SBA's concurrence on a written request [@CL-MKT-0523]. Unless a waiver is approved by the SBA, the contracting officer must terminate the contract for convenience upon receipt of a written request by the SBA [@CL-MKT-0521], and the contracting officer's duty to preserve the waiver option applies when the contracting officer receives information that a transfer of ownership or control is planned [@CL-MKT-0520]. A mandatory source is a listed exception to the rule that a follow-on stays in the program [@CL-MKT-0566], and an order accepted into the program under a basic contract that was not accepted into it needs no release to be fulfilled outside the program [@CL-MKT-0730]. Which clauses govern termination for convenience are on the [Termination for convenience clauses and the contracts they go in](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-termination-for-convenience-clauses.md) card, and small business set-asides are on the [Small business set-asides](mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card.
