## Purpose

Gives the clause text for a service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) set-aside or sole-source procurement: the definition, who may offer, joint ventures and the joint venture work share. The policy rules are on the [Service-disabled veteran-owned small business status, set-asides and sole-source awards](mkt-sdvosb-status-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) card, and status protests are on the [SDVOSB status protests](mkt-sdvosb-status-protests.md) card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-27 [@CL-MKT-1005] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-27 [@CL-MKT-0882; @CL-MKT-1136] |
| Edition date | FEB 2024 [@CL-MKT-9435] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1005] |
| Applies to | The cases the clause lists, from SDVOSB set-asides and sole-source awards to orders under multiple-award contracts [@CL-MKT-1055; @CL-MKT-1056] |

Under the first of the two alternatives in the definition, a small business concern must be not less than 51 percent owned and controlled by one or more service-disabled veterans or, for a publicly owned business, not less than 51 percent of its stock must be owned by them [@CL-MKT-1005]. Its management and daily business operations must also be controlled by one or more service-disabled veterans or, where a service-disabled veteran has a permanent and severe disability, by the spouse or permanent caregiver of the veteran [@CL-MKT-1057]. The alternative to that first alternative is a small business concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program in accordance with 13 CFR part 128 [@CL-MKT-9343; @CL-MKT-1058]. A service-disabled veteran is a veteran as defined in title 38 of the United States Code, section 101(2), with a service-connected disability as defined in section 101(16) of that title [@CL-MKT-1059]. The veteran must also be registered as a service-disabled veteran in the Beneficiary Identification and Records Locator Subsystem or its successor system maintained by the Veterans Benefits Administration of the Department of Veterans Affairs [@CL-MKT-1060].

An SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program is an SDVOSB concern that meets either of two alternatives, the first being that, effective January 1, 2024, it is designated in the System for Award Management (SAM) as certified by the Small Business Administration (SBA) [@CL-MKT-1061]. The certification is in accordance with 13 CFR 128.300, and a concern is alternatively eligible if it has represented that it is an SDVOSB concern in SAM and submitted a complete application for certification to SBA on or before December 31, 2023 [@CL-MKT-1062]. The SDVOSB Program authorizes contracting officers to limit competition, including award on a sole-source basis, to SDVOSB concerns eligible under it [@CL-MKT-1063].

## How it works

The clause applies only to the listed cases, the first being contracts set aside for, or awarded on a sole-source basis to, SDVOSB concerns eligible under the SDVOSB Program and the second being parts of a multiple-award contract set aside for them [@CL-MKT-1055]. It also applies to orders set aside for those concerns and to orders issued directly to them under multiple-award contracts [@CL-MKT-1056].

Effective January 1, 2024, in SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source procurements, offers are solicited only from, and awards resulting from the solicitation are made only to, the concerns the clause lists [@CL-MKT-1006]. Those are concerns designated in SAM as SDVOSB concerns certified by SBA, or that represented their SDVOSB status in SAM and submitted a complete application for certification to SBA on or before December 31, 2023 [@CL-MKT-1007]. Offers from concerns that meet neither test are not considered [@CL-MKT-1064].

A joint venture may be considered an SDVOSB concern if its managing partner complies with the criteria in the definition and with 13 CFR 128.402 [@CL-MKT-1008]. In a joint venture that complies with paragraph (d) of the clause, the SDVOSB party or parties shall perform at least 40 percent of the work performed by the joint venture [@CL-MKT-1009]. Their work must be more than administrative functions [@CL-MKT-1065]. The general limits on subcontracting are on the [Limitations on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule](mkt-limitations-on-subcontracting-and-nonmanufacturer-rule.md) card.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.219-27 states the first alternative in the definition—a small business concern not less than 51 percent owned and controlled by one or more service-disabled veterans, or for a publicly owned business with that share of its stock owned by them [@CL-MKT-1005; @CL-MKT-0882], with management and daily business operations controlled by one or more service-disabled veterans or, where there is a permanent and severe disability, the spouse or permanent caregiver [@CL-MKT-1057; @CL-MKT-0883]—and states the definition of a service-disabled veteran [@CL-MKT-1059; @CL-MKT-0885] with the registration requirement [@CL-MKT-1060; @CL-MKT-0891], the definition of the SDVOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1063; @CL-MKT-0893], the first two applicability items [@CL-MKT-1055; @CL-MKT-0894], the joint venture rule [@CL-MKT-1008; @CL-MKT-1138], the 40 percent work share [@CL-MKT-1009; @CL-MKT-1139], and the requirement that the work be more than administrative functions [@CL-MKT-1065; @CL-MKT-1140]. RFO FAR 52.219-27(a) states the second alternative as a small business concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program in accordance with 13 CFR part 128, cross-referencing FAR 19.106 [@CL-MKT-0884], whereas codified FAR 52.219-27(a) cross-references subpart 19.14 [@CL-MKT-1058]. RFO FAR 52.219-27(a) defines an SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program as an SDVOSB concern that is designated in SAM as certified by SBA in accordance with 13 CFR 128.300 [@CL-MKT-0892], whereas codified FAR 52.219-27(a) states effective January 1, 2024 and includes the alternative for a concern that represented that it is an SDVOSB concern in SAM and submitted a complete application for certification to SBA on or before December 31, 2023 [@CL-MKT-1061; @CL-MKT-1062]. RFO FAR 52.219-27(b)(3) covers orders set aside for SDVOSB concerns eligible under the SDVOSB Program under multiple-award contracts as described in 8.4 and 16.5 [@CL-MKT-0895], whereas codified FAR 52.219-27(b)(3)-(4) also covers orders issued directly to those concerns [@CL-MKT-1056]. RFO FAR 52.219-27(c) states that offers are solicited only from concerns designated in SAM as SDVOSB concerns certified by SBA, that offers received from concerns that are not SDVOSB concerns certified by SBA will not be considered [@CL-MKT-1136], and that any award resulting from the solicitation will be made only to such a concern [@CL-MKT-1137], whereas codified FAR 52.219-27(c) also includes a concern that represented its SDVOSB status in SAM and submitted a complete application for certification to SBA on or before December 31, 2023 [@CL-MKT-1006; @CL-MKT-1007; @CL-MKT-1064].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A joint venture whose managing partner meets the definition criteria and 13 CFR 128.402 performs 500,000 of work. The SDVOSB partner performs 200,000 of it, which is exactly 40 percent and meets the work share if that work is more than administrative functions. At 150,000, or 30 percent, the share would fall short. Separately, a concern that represented its SDVOSB status in SAM and submitted a complete certification application on December 30, 2023 is within the clause's second test. A concern that submitted its application on January 5, 2024 is not within that test and can be considered only if it is designated in SAM as certified. Under the RFO text, neither concern is within the test unless it is designated in SAM as certified by SBA [@CL-MKT-0892; @CL-MKT-1136].

## Never confuse

Under RFO FAR 52.219-27, meeting the 51 percent service-disabled veteran ownership and control test is not enough by itself [@CL-MKT-0882]: offers from concerns not designated in SAM as SBA-certified SDVOSB concerns are ineligible and rejected rather than evaluated on self-certification [@CL-MKT-1136].
