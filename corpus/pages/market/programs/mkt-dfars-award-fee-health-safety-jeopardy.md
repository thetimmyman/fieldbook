## Purpose

Shows how the safety of Government personnel enters an award-fee evaluation, who decides what, and how reduction, denial and recovery of award fee are to be considered.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1856] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 216.405-2-70(a), (b) and (c) [@CL-MKT-1855; @CL-MKT-1856; @CL-MKT-1859] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1856] |
| Applies to | Any award-fee plan, and contracts that include the clause at 252.216-7004 [@CL-MKT-1856; @CL-MKT-1859] |

The contracting officer shall include in the evaluation criteria of any award-fee plan a review of contractor and subcontractor actions that jeopardized the health or safety of Government personnel through gross negligence or reckless disregard for the safety of such personnel [@CL-MKT-1856]. The actions reviewed are as determined through a conviction in a criminal proceeding or a finding of fault and liability in a civil or administrative proceeding [@CL-MKT-1857; @CL-MKT-9333], or, if a contractor or a subcontractor at any tier is not subject to the jurisdiction of the United States courts, and is a final determination of contractor or subcontractor fault resulting from a DoD investigation [@CL-MKT-1858].

In evaluating the contractor's performance under a contract that includes the clause at 252.216-7004, the contracting officer shall consider reducing or denying award fees [@CL-MKT-1859]. The contracting officer shall consider reducing or denying award fees for a period if contractor or subcontractor actions cause serious bodily injury or death of civilian or military Government personnel during such period [@CL-MKT-1860]. The contracting officer's evaluation also shall consider recovering all or part of award fees previously paid for such period [@CL-MKT-1861]. Covered incident and serious bodily injury, as used in the section, are defined in the clause at 252.216-7004 [@CL-MKT-1855].

The review, the consideration of reducing or denying award fees and the consideration of recovery are each stated as duties of the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1856; @CL-MKT-1859; @CL-MKT-1861].

## How it works

The review of safety-jeopardizing actions is a criterion in the award-fee plan, and the section applies it to any award-fee plan [@CL-MKT-1856]. It counts the actions of subcontractors as well as the contractor, and it rests on a conviction, a civil or administrative finding, or, for a contractor or subcontractor not subject to the jurisdiction of the United States courts, a final DoD determination [@CL-MKT-1856; @CL-MKT-1857; @CL-MKT-1858]. The reduction and recovery consideration is stated for a contract that includes the clause at 252.216-7004: the contracting officer shall consider reducing or denying award fees for a period if contractor or subcontractor actions cause serious bodily injury or death of civilian or military Government personnel during such period, and the evaluation also shall consider recovering all or part of award fees previously paid for such period [@CL-MKT-1859; @CL-MKT-1860; @CL-MKT-1861].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An award-fee contract includes the clause at 252.216-7004 and is evaluated in six-month periods. Its award-fee plan includes a review of contractor and subcontractor actions that jeopardized the health or safety of Government personnel through gross negligence or reckless disregard, as the contracting officer must provide [@CL-MKT-1856]. In one period a subcontractor's reckless actions cause serious bodily injury to a Government employee, and a civil court finds the subcontractor at fault and liable [@CL-MKT-1857]. In evaluating the contractor's performance for that period, the contracting officer shall consider reducing or denying the award fee for it [@CL-MKT-1859; @CL-MKT-1860]. The evaluation also shall consider recovering all or part of the fee already paid for that period [@CL-MKT-1861]. On a second contract, a lower-tier subcontractor is not subject to the jurisdiction of the United States courts, so the determination of fault would be a final determination resulting from a DoD investigation [@CL-MKT-1858].

## Never confuse

The review in the plan's criteria concerns gross negligence or reckless disregard determined through a proceeding or a DoD investigation [@CL-MKT-1856; @CL-MKT-1857; @CL-MKT-1858]. The consideration of reducing or denying award fees concerns a period in which actions cause serious bodily injury or death during such period [@CL-MKT-1860]. Covered incident and serious bodily injury are defined in the clause at 252.216-7004 [@CL-MKT-1855]. The clause's own text on reduction and recovery is on [DoD cost-plus-award-fee contracts: final-period fee, base fee and health or safety](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-cost-plus-award-fee-requirements.md).

## Company calibration

How the company's award-fee plans record the health-and-safety review, and what evidence of a conviction, a civil or administrative finding or a DoD determination the company would hold. [@Q-004]
