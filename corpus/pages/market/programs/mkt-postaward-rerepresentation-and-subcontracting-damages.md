## Purpose

Shows when a contractor must rerepresent its size and status after award, and how liquidated damages are computed when a contractor fails to make a good faith effort to comply with its subcontracting plan.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0848] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-28 and FAR 52.219-16 [@CL-MKT-0848; @CL-MKT-0853] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-28 [@CL-MKT-0264]; RFO FAR 52.219-16 [@CL-MKT-0295] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.219-28: JAN 2025 [@CL-MKT-9433]; FAR 52.219-16: SEP 2021 [@CL-MKT-9434] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0848] |
| Applies to | Contractors that represented a listed small business status before award, for rerepresentation; contracts with a subcontracting plan, for liquidated damages [@CL-MKT-0848; @CL-MKT-0853] |

If the contractor represented before award that it was any of the small business concerns the clause cross-references, it shall rerepresent its size and socioeconomic status according to paragraph (f) of the clause or, if applicable, paragraph (h) of the clause, upon occurrence of any of the listed occasions [@CL-MKT-0848; @CL-MKT-9342]. The representation it makes before award is on the [Small business program representations provision](mkt-small-business-program-representations-provision.md) card. One listed occasion is within 30 days after execution of a novation agreement, or within 30 days after modification of the contract to include the clause if the novation agreement was executed before the clause was included [@CL-MKT-0849]. Another is within 30 days after a merger or acquisition that does not require a novation, or within 30 days after modification to include the clause if the merger or acquisition occurred before the clause was included [@CL-MKT-0850]. For long-term contracts, the listed occasions are within 60 to 120 days before the end of the fifth year of the contract, and within 60 to 120 days before the date specified for exercising any option thereafter [@CL-MKT-0851]. The term long-term contract does not include a contract that exceeds five years in duration because its period of performance has been extended for a cumulative period not to exceed six months under the option to extend services clause or other appropriate authority [@CL-MKT-0980].

## How it works

For liquidated damages, failure to make a good faith effort to comply with the subcontracting plan means a willful or intentional failure to perform in accordance with the requirements of the plan [@CL-MKT-0852], or willful or intentional action to frustrate the plan [@CL-MKT-0978]. The plan itself is on the [scope and required elements](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-scope-and-required-elements.md) card. One condition for liquidated damages is that the contractor has failed to meet its subcontracting goals and the contracting officer decides that it failed to make a good faith effort to comply with its plan [@CL-MKT-0853]. The failure to meet goals is judged at contract completion or, in the case of a commercial plan, at the close of the fiscal year for which the plan is applicable [@CL-MKT-0979]. The amount of probable damages is an amount equal to the actual dollar amount by which the contractor failed to achieve each subcontract goal [@CL-MKT-0854]. Performance is measured by applying the percentage goals to the total actual subcontracting dollars or, for a commercial plan, to the pro rata share of actual subcontracting dollars attributable to Government contracts covered by the commercial plan [@CL-MKT-0855]. Commercial plans are on the [master and commercial plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card. Under FAR 52.219-28(c), if the contractor represented its status as any of the small business concerns identified at 19.000(a)(3) before award, it must also rerepresent its size and socioeconomic status for the NAICS code assigned to an order, except that paragraphs (c)(1) through (3) do not apply to an order issued under a Federal Supply Schedule contract at subpart 8.4, and this requirement applies to an order set aside exclusively for a small business concern identified at 19.000(a)(3) that is issued under an unrestricted multiple-award contract unless issued under its reserved portion, and when the contracting officer explicitly requires it for an order issued under a multiple-award contract, including a Federal Supply Schedule contract [@CL-MKT-9072; @CL-MKT-9073; @CL-MKT-1713; @CL-MKT-1714]. See the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card for the notice steps.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.219-28 states the occasion within 30 days after execution of a novation agreement [@CL-MKT-0849; @CL-MKT-0265], the occasion within 30 days after a merger or acquisition that does not require a novation [@CL-MKT-0850; @CL-MKT-0266], and the long-term contract occasions at 60 to 120 days [@CL-MKT-0851; @CL-MKT-0267], and it states the exclusion from the long-term contract definition for a cumulative extension of not more than six months [@CL-MKT-0980; @CL-MKT-0268]. RFO FAR 52.219-28(b) applies where the contractor represented before award that it was a small business concern, a small disadvantaged business concern, or a joint venture that was one of the identified concerns [@CL-MKT-0263], whereas codified FAR 52.219-28(b) refers to any of the identified concerns [@CL-MKT-0848]. RFO FAR 52.219-28(b) refers to paragraph (e) or, if applicable, paragraph (g) of its clause [@CL-MKT-0264], whereas codified FAR 52.219-28(b) refers to paragraph (f) or (h) [@CL-MKT-0848]. In RFO FAR 52.219-28, paragraph (c) states that the contractor shall rerepresent its size status in accordance with the size standard in effect at the time of the rerepresentation that corresponds to the NAICS code(s) assigned to the contract [@CL-MKT-0269], whereas codified FAR 52.219-28(c) addresses rerepresentation for the NAICS code assigned to an order [@CL-MKT-9072; @CL-MKT-9073; @CL-MKT-1713; @CL-MKT-1714]. RFO FAR 52.219-16(a) begins with the Government measuring performance by applying the percentage goals to total actual subcontracting dollars or, for a commercial plan, to the pro rata share [@CL-MKT-0295; @CL-MKT-0881], whereas codified FAR 52.219-16(a)-(b) first defines failure to make a good faith effort to comply with the subcontracting plan as a willful or intentional failure to perform or willful or intentional action to frustrate the plan and then states how performance shall be measured [@CL-MKT-0852; @CL-MKT-0978; @CL-MKT-0855]. RFO FAR 52.219-16(a) states the timing at contract completion or at the close of the fiscal year for a commercial plan [@CL-MKT-0979; @CL-MKT-0296], the damages amount equal to the actual dollar amount by which each goal was missed [@CL-MKT-0854; @CL-MKT-0299], that the contracting officer's decision on good faith effort is made in accordance with paragraph (b) of the RFO clause [@CL-MKT-0297] rather than paragraph (c) in the codified clause [@CL-MKT-0853], and that the contractor shall pay the liquidated damages in a stated amount [@CL-MKT-0298].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A plan sets a goal of 400,000 for a group of concerns and, at contract completion, the contractor has placed 300,000. The shortfall is 100,000. The contracting officer decides after notice that the contractor did not make a good faith effort. Answer: the liquidated damages for that goal are 100,000, and the same calculation is made for each goal that was missed. If the contracting officer had found good faith effort, no liquidated damages would follow from the miss alone.

## Never confuse

FAR 52.219-28 requires a contractor that represented small business status before award to rerepresent size and socioeconomic status upon triggering postaward events [@CL-MKT-0264; @CL-MKT-0848], unlike the initial offer representation in [Small business program representations provision](mkt-small-business-program-representations-provision.md) [@CL-MKT-0806], while liquidated damages attach to an other-than-small contractor's failure to make a good-faith effort on subcontracting goals rather than missing a goal despite good faith [@CL-MKT-0821; @CL-MKT-0853].
