## Purpose

Shows the advance notice for research and development, the DoD notice of intent to bundle, and where the DoD procedure for an only one responsible source action is found.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0084; @CL-MKT-0086] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.205; DFARS 205.205; DFARS 205.205-70; DFARS 205.205-71 [@CL-MKT-0084; @CL-MKT-9161; @CL-MKT-0086; @CL-MKT-0089] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0084; @CL-MKT-0086] |
| Applies to | Research and development requirements, and DoD-funded acquisitions that potentially involve bundling [@CL-MKT-0084; @CL-MKT-0087] |

Contracting officers may transmit to the GPE advance notices of their interest in potential research and development programs whenever market research does not produce a sufficient number of concerns to obtain adequate competition, but advance notices must not be used where security considerations prohibit such publication [@CL-MKT-0084]. Contracting officers must synopsize all subsequent solicitations for research and development contracts, including those resulting from a previously synopsized advance notice, unless one of the exceptions to the notice requirement applies [@CL-MKT-0085].

The DoD bundling notification duty applies when a proposed acquisition is funded entirely using DoD funds and potentially involves bundling [@CL-MKT-0087]. Where that duty applies, the contracting officer shall publish in the Governmentwide point of entry a notification of the intent to bundle at least 30 days before the release of a solicitation or 30 days before placing an order without a solicitation [@CL-MKT-0086; @CL-MKT-0087]. If the agency has determined that measurably substantial benefits are expected from bundling, the notification shall include a brief description of those benefits [@CL-MKT-0088].

## How it works

The research and development advance notice is optional, but the synopsis of the later solicitation is not, unless an exception applies [@CL-MKT-0084; @CL-MKT-0085]. The DoD bundling notice is a separate step tied to the release of the solicitation or an order without one [@CL-MKT-0086]. Before soliciting a proposal without providing for full and open competition under FAR 6.302-1, the DFARS directs the contracting officer to follow the procedures at PGI 206.302-1(d) [@CL-MKT-0089]. Section 205.205, Special situations, says to see PGI 205.205 for instructions on the solicitation notice regarding timely definitization of equitable adjustments for change orders under construction contracts [@CL-MKT-9161].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD component plans a requirement funded entirely with DoD funds that may be bundled. It expects to release the solicitation on day 100, so the notification of intent to bundle must be published by day 70. If measurably substantial benefits are expected from bundling, the notice includes a short description of them.

## Never confuse

The DoD bundling notice is not the synopsis of the solicitation: it comes at least 30 days before release of a solicitation or an order without one [@CL-MKT-0086], while the general wait between a notice and a solicitation is at least 15 days [@CL-MKT-0062]. The optional research and development advance notice is also not the synopsis that must precede the later solicitation [@CL-MKT-0084; @CL-MKT-0085]. For the sole-source side, see the [Only One Offer provision](../../estimating/pricing/est-only-one-offer.md) card. The dollar amount of the simplified acquisition threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.
