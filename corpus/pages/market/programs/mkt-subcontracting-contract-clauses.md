## Purpose

Names each subcontracting clause and alternate and the contracts it goes in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0464] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.708 [@CL-MKT-0464; @CL-MKT-0466] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0464] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that meet the conditions listed for each clause [@CL-MKT-0464; @CL-MKT-0466; @CL-MKT-0472] |

The clause 52.219-8, Utilization of Small Business Concerns, is inserted in solicitations and contracts expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, subject to the listed exceptions [@CL-MKT-0464]. The listed exceptions are either a contemplated personal services contract or a contract that, with all its subcontracts, will be performed entirely outside the United States and its outlying areas [@CL-MKT-0465]. The clause 52.219-9, Small Business Subcontracting Plan, is inserted in solicitations and contracts that offer subcontracting possibilities, are expected to exceed $900,000 ($2 million for construction of any public facility), and are required to include clause 52.219-8, subject to a listed exception [@CL-MKT-0466]. That rule does not apply where the acquisition is set aside or is to be accomplished under the 8(a) program [@CL-MKT-0467].

Four alternates to the plan clause are tied to listed cases. Alternate I is used when contracting by sealed bidding rather than by negotiation [@CL-MKT-0468]. Alternate II is used when contracting by negotiation and subcontracting plans are required with initial proposals as provided for in FAR 19.705-2(d) [@CL-MKT-0469]. Alternate III is used when the contract action will not be reported in the Federal Procurement Data System under the cited paragraphs [@CL-MKT-0470]. Alternate IV is used when incorporating a subcontracting plan due to a modification as provided for in FAR 19.702(a)(1)(iii) [@CL-MKT-0471]. The clause 52.219-16, Liquidated Damages-Subcontracting Plan, is inserted in all solicitations and contracts containing clause 52.219-9 or its Alternate I, II, III or IV [@CL-MKT-0472]. A clause substantially the same as 52.219-10, Incentive Subcontracting Program, may be inserted when contracting by negotiation and a plan is required, subject to the conditions stated in the paragraph [@CL-MKT-0473]. The conditions in FAR 19.708(c)(1) are that inclusion of a monetary incentive is, in the judgment of the contracting officer, necessary to increase subcontracting opportunities for small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, and women-owned small business concerns, and is commensurate with the efficient and economical performance of the contract, unless the conditions in paragraph (c)(3) are applicable [@CL-MKT-0564; @CL-MKT-1816]. When small business subcontracting is a factor in determining the award fee in a cost-plus-award-fee contract, the contracting officer shall not use clause 52.219-10 [@CL-MKT-0474].

## How it works

A contract that carries the plan clause must also be one required to include clause 52.219-8 [@CL-MKT-0466]. Wherever the plan clause or an alternate is present, the liquidated damages clause goes in as well [@CL-MKT-0472]. The contracting officer may insert the incentive clause when contracting by negotiation and a plan is required, subject to the paragraph's further conditions, and does not use it where small business subcontracting is a factor in determining the award fee in a cost-plus-award-fee contract [@CL-MKT-0473; @CL-MKT-0474; @CL-MKT-0564]. The rules on goals when an incentive rewards exceeding them are on the [General rules for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-general-rules.md) card. The administrative contracting officer's duties under the plan clause are on the [Responsibilities of the administrative contracting officer for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-aco-responsibilities.md) card. The thresholds and plan content are on the [When a small business subcontracting plan is required](mkt-subcontracting-plan-when-required.md) card and the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A negotiated supply contract is expected to be worth 1,500,000, is not set aside, is not under the 8(a) program, has subcontracting possibilities, and requires plans with initial proposals. It carries the utilization clause, the plan clause with Alternate II, and the liquidated damages clause. A similar contract that is set aside does not carry the plan clause.

## Never confuse

The utilization clause 52.219-8 and the plan clause 52.219-9 are different clauses, and the plan clause goes into contracts that are required to include the utilization clause [@CL-MKT-0466]. The liquidated damages clause follows the plan clause [@CL-MKT-0472], while the incentive clause is a separate and optional monetary incentive [@CL-MKT-0473].
