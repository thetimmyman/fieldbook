## Purpose

Explains which forms the contracting officer may use for negotiated solicitations and awards, what an award document must carry when those forms are not used, and what Section I of the uniform contract format must hold.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-2127] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.210, 15.509 and 15.204-3 [@CL-MKT-2127; @CL-MKT-2131; @CL-MKT-2134] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-2127] |
| Applies to | Negotiated solicitations and awards, and Section I of the uniform contract format [@CL-MKT-2127; @CL-MKT-2131; @CL-MKT-2134] |

Prescribed forms are not required to prepare the solicitations described in FAR part 15, and the forms listed in FAR 15.210 may be used at the discretion of the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-2127]. Standard Form 33 and Optional Form 308 may be used to issue requests for proposals and requests for information [@CL-MKT-2128]. Standard Form 30 and Optional Form 309 may be used to amend solicitations of negotiated contracts [@CL-MKT-2129]. Optional Form 17 may be furnished with each request for proposal [@CL-MKT-2130].

Optional Form 307, Standard Form 26 or Standard Form 33 may be used to award negotiated contracts in which the signature of both parties on a single document is appropriate [@CL-MKT-2131]. If the Standard Form 26 is used for a negotiated procurement, block 18 is not to be used [@CL-MKT-2132]. If those forms are not used, the award document shall incorporate the agreement and award language from Optional Form 307 [@CL-MKT-2133].

Part II of the uniform contract format is headed Contract Clauses, and its Section I is headed Contract clauses [@CL-MKT-2157]. In Section I the contracting officer shall include the clauses required by law or by FAR part 15 and any additional clauses expected to be included in any resulting contract, if those clauses are not required in any other section of the uniform contract format [@CL-MKT-2134]. An index may be inserted if the format of that section is particularly complex [@CL-MKT-2135].

## How it works

For solicitations, no form is required, and the contracting officer decides whether to use any listed form [@CL-MKT-2127]. The award section says the three award forms may be used where one document carries both signatures; it does not name who chooses [@CL-MKT-2131]. It does require that an award document not on one of those forms carry the agreement and award language from Optional Form 307 [@CL-MKT-2133]. In Section I the duty falls on the contracting officer, and it does not reach a clause that another section of the format already requires [@CL-MKT-2134]. Standard Form 33 appears in both lists, so one form can serve as solicitation and as award [@CL-MKT-2128; @CL-MKT-2131].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer issues a request for proposals on Optional Form 308 and later amends it on Optional Form 309; neither form was required, and the choice was the contracting officer's [@CL-MKT-2127; @CL-MKT-2128; @CL-MKT-2129]. The award will be signed by both parties on one document on Standard Form 26, and block 18 is left unused because the procurement is negotiated [@CL-MKT-2131; @CL-MKT-2132]. Had the award been made on a document that is none of the three listed award forms, it would have to carry the Optional Form 307 agreement and award language [@CL-MKT-2133]. In Section I, the contracting officer lists the clauses required by law or FAR part 15 and one additional clause expected in the contract, and does not repeat a clause that another section already requires [@CL-MKT-2134]. An index may be added if the section is complex, and this one is short, so none is added [@CL-MKT-2135].

## Never confuse

A form that may be used is not a prescribed form: no form is required for these solicitations [@CL-MKT-2127]. The award forms fit where one document carries both signatures, and an award document that does not use them still carries the Optional Form 307 language [@CL-MKT-2131; @CL-MKT-2133]. Section I is only the clauses part of the uniform contract format; the full format is on [Uniform contract format](mkt-uniform-contract-format.md).
