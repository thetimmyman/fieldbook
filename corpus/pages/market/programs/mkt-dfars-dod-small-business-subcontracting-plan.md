## Purpose

Shows what the DoD subcontracting plan clause adds to the FAR plan clause, how reports are routed and accepted in the reporting system, which subcontracts count toward goals, and how the two alternates change the reporting and definition paragraphs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0896] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.219-7003 [@CL-MKT-0896] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0896] |
| Applies to | Contracts in which the basic clause or one of its alternates is prescribed, together with the FAR plan clause [@CL-MKT-0946; @CL-MKT-0904; @CL-MKT-0906] |

The basic clause states that it supplements the FAR small business subcontracting plan clause of the contract [@CL-MKT-0896], and it is prescribed in DFARS 219.708(b)(1)(A) and (b)(1)(A)(1) [@CL-MKT-0946]. In subcontracting plans that specifically identify small businesses, the contractor shall notify the administrative contracting officer of any substitution of a firm that is not small for a small business firm the plan identifies [@CL-MKT-0897]. Notifications of those substitutions shall be in writing and shall occur within a reasonable period of time after award of the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0958].

In the basic clause, for DoD the contractor shall submit reports in eSRS, and the Individual Subcontract Report goes to the contracting officer at the procuring contracting office even when contract administration has been delegated to the Defense Contract Management Agency [@CL-MKT-0898]. The clause also directs that the consolidated Summary Subcontract Report for an individual subcontracting plan be submitted to the Department of Defense [@CL-MKT-0899]. The authority to acknowledge receipt or reject the Individual Subcontract Report resides with the contracting officer who receives it [@CL-MKT-0900], and the authority to acknowledge receipt of or reject Summary Subcontract Reports for an individual plan resides with the SSR Coordinator [@CL-MKT-0901].

Subcontracts awarded to qualified nonprofit agencies designated by the Committee for Purchase From People Who Are Blind or Severely Disabled may be counted toward the contractor's small business subcontracting goal [@CL-MKT-0955]. A mentor firm under the Pilot Mentor-Protégé Program may count toward its small disadvantaged business goal subcontracts awarded to protégé firms which are qualified organizations employing the severely disabled [@CL-MKT-0956]. The master plan is approved by the cognizant contract administration activity for the contractor [@CL-MKT-0957].

## How it works

The clause works as a DoD layer on the FAR plan clause, adding the substitution notice, the routing of each report and the authority to accept or reject it [@CL-MKT-0896; @CL-MKT-0897; @CL-MKT-0898; @CL-MKT-0900]. The contractor includes the Test Program clause in subcontracts with Test Program participants [@CL-MKT-0902], if the subcontract is expected to exceed the applicable FAR threshold and to have further subcontracting opportunities [@CL-MKT-0903]. Alternate I, prescribed in DFARS 219.708(b)(1)(A) and (b)(1)(A)(2), uses a different reporting paragraph [@CL-MKT-0904], in which the Standard Form 294 is submitted in accordance with the instructions on that form [@CL-MKT-0905]. Alternate II, prescribed in DFARS 219.708(b)(1)(A) and (b)(1)(A)(3), uses different paragraphs (a) and (b) than the basic clause [@CL-MKT-0906], and its definition of an eligible contractor lists conditions, one of which is employing severely disabled individuals at a rate averaging not less than 33 percent of the workforce over the 12-month period before the solicitation [@CL-MKT-0907]. Other parts of the printed clause, such as the definition of the SSR Coordinator, the second category of firms in the mentor firm paragraph and the other conditions in the Alternate II definition, are not drafted on this card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor with an individual plan under the basic clause names three small businesses in its plan. After award it replaces one of them with a firm that is not small. It also files its Individual Subcontract Report for the contract.

Answer: the prime notifies the administrative contracting officer of the substitution, in writing and within a reasonable period after award of the subcontract. The report goes to the contracting officer at the procuring contracting office, even if administration was delegated.

## Never confuse

This clause supplements the FAR plan clause and does not replace it [@CL-MKT-0896]. The Test Program clause is a separate clause that the contractor includes in subcontracts with Test Program participants where the condition on the subcontract is met [@CL-MKT-0902; @CL-MKT-0903]. Its own rules are on the [DFARS small business subcontracting plan clause for the Test Program](mkt-dfars-small-business-subcontracting-plan-test-program.md) card. The clause on Indian organizations and Native Hawaiian small business concerns is a different subcontracting clause, on the [DFARS utilization of Indian organizations and Native Hawaiian small business concerns](mkt-dfars-indian-and-native-hawaiian-subcontracting-incentive.md) card. The FAR clause's scope, master and commercial plans, reporting and flowdown are on the [master and commercial plans, reporting and flowdown](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card and the [scope and required elements](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-scope-and-required-elements.md) card. The list of subcontracting clauses is on the [Subcontracting contract clauses](mkt-subcontracting-contract-clauses.md) card, and the administrative contracting officer's duties under a plan are on the [Responsibilities of the administrative contracting officer for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-aco-responsibilities.md) card.
