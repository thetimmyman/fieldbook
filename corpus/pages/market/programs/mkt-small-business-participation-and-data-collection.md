## Purpose

Names the steps a contracting officer takes to encourage small business participation, and the actions agencies take to measure it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0303] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.202-1 and FAR 19.202-5 [@CL-MKT-0303; @CL-MKT-0310; @CL-MKT-0374] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0303] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers on acquisitions where a listed action is applicable, and agencies measuring small business participation [@CL-MKT-0303; @CL-MKT-0310] |

Small business concerns shall be afforded an equitable opportunity to compete for all contracts that they can perform to the extent consistent with the Government's interest [@CL-MKT-0303]. When applicable, the contracting officer takes the listed actions, which are named here in turn [@CL-MKT-0303]. First, divide proposed acquisitions of supplies and services, except construction, into reasonably small lots, not less than economic production runs, so offers can be made on less than the total requirement [@CL-MKT-0304]. Second, plan acquisitions so that, if practicable, more than one small business concern may perform the work, if the work exceeds the amount for which a surety may be guaranteed by SBA against loss under the cited statute [@CL-MKT-0305]. Third, ensure that delivery schedules are realistic and encourage small business participation, to the extent consistent with the actual requirements of the Government [@CL-MKT-0306]. Fourth, encourage prime contractors to subcontract with small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0306]. Fifth, provide the proposed acquisition package and other reasonably obtainable information to the SBA procurement center representative, or to the alternative the cross-reference gives if none is assigned, at least 30 days before the solicitation is issued, when a condition listed in that paragraph applies [@CL-MKT-0307]. One listed condition is that the proposed acquisition is for supplies or services currently being provided by a small business and is of a quantity or estimated dollar value, the magnitude of which makes it unlikely that small businesses can compete for the prime contract [@CL-MKT-0307; @CL-MKT-0373]. The role of the representative is on the [SBA procurement center representatives and small business solicitation provisions](mkt-sba-pcr-and-small-business-solicitation-provisions.md) card.

Agencies shall measure the extent of small business participation in their acquisition programs by taking listed actions [@CL-MKT-0310]. One is to require each prospective contractor to represent whether it is a small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, women-owned small business, EDWOSB concern, or WOSB concern eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-0310; @CL-MKT-1815]. Another is to accurately measure the extent of participation by the listed small business categories in Government acquisitions in terms of the total value of contracts placed during each fiscal year, and to report data to the SBA at the end of each fiscal year [@CL-MKT-0374; @CL-MKT-0311].

## How it works

The fifth step has follow-on steps. For acquisitions described in the first three listed conditions, the contracting officer also provides a statement explaining why, among other matters, the proposed acquisition cannot be divided into reasonably small lots (not less than economic production runs) [@CL-MKT-0308]. If the contracting officer rejects the recommendation that the representative makes on reviewing the package, the contracting officer documents the basis for the rejection and notifies the representative [@CL-MKT-0309]. The reporting side is on the [Federal Procurement Data System reporting](../feeds/mkt-fpds-reporting-responsibilities-and-deadlines.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer plans to buy 10,000 identical gloves in a single lot. The officer asks whether the buy can be divided. Answer: when the action applies, the officer divides the buy into reasonably small lots, not less than economic production runs, so offers can be made on less than 10,000. If the acquisition meets a listed notice condition, the package goes to the SBA procurement center representative at least 30 days before the solicitation, with a statement of why it cannot be divided if it cannot.

## Never confuse

The set-aside tests are on the [Small business set-asides](mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card. The representation a prospective contractor makes is on the [Offeror size and status representation](../taxonomy/mkt-offeror-size-and-status-representation.md) card, and a representation made again after award is on the [Rerepresentation after award](../taxonomy/mkt-size-rerepresentation-after-award.md) card. Subcontracting plans are a separate requirement, on the [When a subcontracting plan is required](mkt-subcontracting-plan-when-required.md) card.
