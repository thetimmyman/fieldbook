## Purpose

Explains when a leader company may be designated to help a follower company become a source of supply, what limits the arrangement, and how the award may be structured.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1720] |
| Codified citation | FAR 17.401, 17.402 and 17.403 [@CL-MKT-1720; @CL-MKT-1721; @CL-MKT-1724] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1720] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions that use the leader company technique under agency procedures [@CL-MKT-1720] |

Leader company contracting is an extraordinary acquisition technique, limited to special circumstances and used only when its use is in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-MKT-1720]. A developer or sole producer of a product or system is designated the leader company and furnishes assistance and know-how under an approved contract to one or more designated follower companies, so that they can become a source of supply [@CL-MKT-1771].

The objectives are one or more of the following: reducing delivery time, achieving geographic dispersion of suppliers, maximizing the use of scarce tooling or special equipment, and achieving economies in production [@CL-MKT-1772]; ensuring uniformity and reliability in equipment, compatibility or standardization of components and interchangeability of parts, and eliminating problems in the use of proprietary data that cannot be resolved by more satisfactory solutions [@CL-MKT-1773]; and facilitating the transition from development to production and to subsequent competitive acquisition of end items or major components [@CL-MKT-1774].

The technique is to be used only when all four listed conditions hold [@CL-MKT-1721; @CL-MKT-1722]. The leader company has the necessary production know-how and is able to furnish required assistance to the follower or followers [@CL-MKT-1721]. No other source can meet the Government's requirements without the assistance of a leader company [@CL-MKT-1721]. The assistance required of the leader company is limited to that which is essential to enable the follower or followers to produce the items [@CL-MKT-1722]. Its use is authorized in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-MKT-1722].

When leader company contracting is used, the Government shall reserve the right to approve subcontracts between the leader company and the follower or followers [@CL-MKT-1723].

## How it works

The contracting officer may award a prime contract to the leader company, obligating it to subcontract a designated portion of the required end items to a specified follower company and to assist that follower to produce them [@CL-MKT-1724].

The contracting officer may instead award a prime contract to the leader company for the required assistance and a prime contract to the follower for production of the items, or award a prime contract to the follower, obligating it to subcontract with a designated leader company for the required assistance [@CL-MKT-1724; @CL-MKT-1725].

Whichever route is used, the contracting officer shall ensure that any contract awarded under the arrangement contains a firm agreement regarding disclosure, if any, of contractor trade secrets, technical designs or concepts, and specific data or software of a proprietary nature [@CL-MKT-1726]. The Government's right to approve subcontracts between the leader and the follower applies whenever the technique is used [@CL-MKT-1723].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency needs 200 units of a system that only one developer knows how to build. Agency procedures authorize the technique, no other source could meet the requirement without the developer's help, and the help needed is limited to what a second firm needs to produce the units [@CL-MKT-1721; @CL-MKT-1722].

The contracting officer awards the developer a prime contract for 200 units that obligates it to subcontract 60 units to a specified follower and to assist that follower [@CL-MKT-1724]. The contract reserves the Government's right to approve the subcontract between the two firms [@CL-MKT-1723] and contains a firm agreement on disclosure of the developer's trade secrets and proprietary data [@CL-MKT-1726].

The contracting officer could instead have awarded the developer a prime contract for the assistance and the follower a prime contract for production [@CL-MKT-1725].

## Never confuse

Leader company contracting is an extraordinary technique used only when no other source can meet Government requirements without the leader's production know-how and assistance to a designated follower [@CL-MKT-1720; @CL-MKT-1721; @CL-MKT-1724], rather than ordinary prime-subcontractor teaming or socioeconomic business development such as [Competitive 8(a) awards](mkt-8a-competitive-awards.md) [@CL-MKT-0507].

## Company calibration

Whether any program depends on a leader-follower arrangement, which agency procedure authorized it, and where its disclosure agreement is held. [@Q-004]
