## Purpose

Explains the Defense Department policy that limits when an undefinitized contract action may be used and requires it to be as complete and definite as practicable.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-2112] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 217.7403 [@CL-MKT-2112] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-2112] |
| Applies to | Undefinitized contract actions in the Defense Department [@CL-MKT-2112] |

DoD policy is that undefinitized contract actions shall be used only when the negotiation of a definitive contract action is not possible in sufficient time to meet the Government's requirements and the Government's interest demands that the contractor be given a binding commitment so that contract performance can begin immediately [@CL-MKT-2112; @CL-MKT-2113; @CL-MKT-2114].

DoD policy is also that undefinitized contract actions shall be as complete and definite as practicable under the particular circumstances [@CL-MKT-2112; @CL-MKT-2115]. Purchases at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, special access programs, and congressionally mandated long-lead procurement contracts are not subject to DFARS subpart 217.74, but the contracting officer shall apply the policy and procedures to them to the maximum extent practicable [@CL-MKT-1553; @CL-MKT-1554].

## How it works

DFARS subpart 217.74 prescribes policies and procedures implementing title 10 of the United States Code, section 3371 and the sections that follow it [@CL-MKT-2346]. Under DFARS 217.7403, an undefinitized contract action shall be used only when negotiation of a definitive contract action is not possible in sufficient time to meet the Government's requirements and the Government's interest demands that the contractor be given a binding commitment so that contract performance can begin immediately, and it shall be as complete and definite as practicable under the particular circumstances [@CL-MKT-2112; @CL-MKT-2113; @CL-MKT-2114; @CL-MKT-2115]. The approval route and the limits on obligations before definitization are on [Approval and obligation limits](mkt-uca-approval-and-obligation-limits.md). The exclusions, profit and reporting rules are on [Exceptions, profit and reporting](mkt-uca-exceptions-profit-and-reporting.md), and the wider topic is on [Undefinitized contract actions](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-undefinitized-contract-actions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A unit needs repairs to a critical vehicle within weeks. A definitive contract cannot be negotiated in time to meet that need, and the Government needs the contractor bound now so that work can start at once, so both conditions are met [@CL-MKT-2113; @CL-MKT-2114]. The action is written with its scope, deliverables and schedule defined as completely as the circumstances allow [@CL-MKT-2115]. If a definitive contract could be negotiated in time to meet the need, the first condition would fail and the policy would not support an undefinitized action [@CL-MKT-2112; @CL-MKT-2113].

## Never confuse

DFARS 217.7403 requires both conditions for using an undefinitized contract action and requires that the action be as complete and definite as practicable under the particular circumstances [@CL-MKT-2112; @CL-MKT-2113; @CL-MKT-2114; @CL-MKT-2115]. The approval rule, the limit on obligations before definitization, and the exclusions, profit and reporting rules are on [Approval and obligation limits](mkt-uca-approval-and-obligation-limits.md), [Exceptions, profit and reporting](mkt-uca-exceptions-profit-and-reporting.md), and [Undefinitized contract actions](../../estimating/pricing/est-dod-undefinitized-contract-actions.md).
