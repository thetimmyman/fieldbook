## Purpose

Sets out the definition of a women-owned business concern used in this provision and the narrow case in which an offeror completes this representation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1124] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-5 [@CL-MKT-1124] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.204-5 [@CL-MKT-1253; @CL-MKT-1256] |
| Edition date | Oct 2014 [@CL-MKT-1253] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1124] |
| Applies to | Offerors that are women-owned business concerns and have not represented themselves as small business concerns in paragraph (c)(1) of FAR 52.219-1 [@CL-MKT-1127] |

As used in FAR 52.204-5, a women-owned business concern means a concern that is at least 51 percent owned by one or more women, or in the case of any publicly owned business, at least 51 percent of its stock is owned by one or more women, and whose management and daily business operations are controlled by one or more women [@CL-MKT-1124; @CL-MKT-1125; @CL-MKT-1126].

The representation is to be completed only if the offeror is a women-owned business concern and has not represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) of FAR 52.219-1, Small Business Program Representations, of the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1127]. The representation states that the offeror represents that it is a women-owned business concern, and the text shows a box before those words [@CL-MKT-1128].

## How it works

The provision has a definition and a representation [@CL-MKT-1124; @CL-MKT-1128]. Ownership and control are separate parts, and the definition requires both [@CL-MKT-1124; @CL-MKT-1126]. For a publicly owned business, the ownership part is measured on its stock [@CL-MKT-1125]. A bracketed instruction before the representation limits who completes it, and it refers only to the representation in paragraph (c)(1) of FAR 52.219-1 [@CL-MKT-1127].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.204-5 keeps the definition of a women-owned business concern, with its 51 percent ownership requirement, its stock rule for any publicly owned business, and its management and daily business operations control requirement [@CL-MKT-1253; @CL-MKT-1254; @CL-MKT-1255], and keeps the bracketed instruction that the representation is completed only if the offeror is a women-owned business concern and has not represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) of FAR 52.219-1, Small Business Program Representations, of the solicitation, as well as the representation itself [@CL-MKT-1256; @CL-MKT-1257].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Offeror X is privately held, two women own 60 percent of it, and they control its management and daily business operations. X did not represent itself as a small business concern in the small business program representations. X meets the definition and completes the representation [@CL-MKT-1124; @CL-MKT-1126; @CL-MKT-1127]. Offeror Y is publicly owned and women hold 40 percent of its stock, so it does not meet the ownership part [@CL-MKT-1125]. Offeror Z is 70 percent owned by women but its management and daily operations are controlled by men, so it does not meet the control part [@CL-MKT-1126]. Offeror W is a women-owned business concern that did represent itself as a small business concern in those representations, so the instruction does not call for it to complete this representation [@CL-MKT-1127].

## Never confuse

The representation in FAR 52.204-5 is completed only if the offeror is a women-owned business concern and has not represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) of FAR 52.219-1, Small Business Program Representations, of the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1127]. Both at least 51 percent ownership by one or more women and control of management and daily business operations by one or more women are required to meet the definition [@CL-MKT-1124; @CL-MKT-1125; @CL-MKT-1126]. The definition in FAR 52.204-5(a) is stated as used in that provision [@CL-MKT-1124], while the Women-Owned Small Business Program set-aside and sole-source rules are on the [Women-Owned Small Business Program set-asides and sole-source awards](mkt-wosb-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) card. Small business size and status representations are on the [Small business program representations provision](mkt-small-business-program-representations-provision.md) card.
