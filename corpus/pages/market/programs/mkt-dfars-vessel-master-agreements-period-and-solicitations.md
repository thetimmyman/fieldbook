## Purpose

Explains how long a vessel repair master agreement lasts, who can end it and with what notice, whom the Government solicits for a job order, and when the contracting officer may order emergency work without a solicitation.

Master agreements remain in effect until canceled by either the contractor or the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1928]. Either of them can cancel a master agreement by giving 30 days written notice to the other [@CL-MKT-1929]. Cancellation of a master agreement does not affect the rights and liabilities under any job order existing at the time of cancellation [@CL-MKT-1930]. The contractor must continue to perform all work covered by any job order issued before the effective date of cancellation of the master agreement [@CL-MKT-1931].

When a requirement arises within the United States or its outlying areas for the type of work covered by the master agreement, offers must be solicited from prospective contractors that meet one of two alternatives: they previously executed a master agreement [@CL-MKT-1932]; or they have not previously executed a master agreement but possess the necessary qualifications to perform the work and agree to execute a master agreement before award of a job order [@CL-MKT-1932; @CL-MKT-1933]. The procedures at PGI 217.7103-3 are to be followed when preparing solicitations for job orders [@CL-MKT-2033].

The contracting officer, without soliciting offers, may issue a written job order to a contractor that has previously executed a master agreement when one of two alternative conditions exists: delay in the performance of necessary repair work would endanger a vessel, its cargo or stores [@CL-MKT-1934; @CL-MKT-9335]; or military necessity requires immediate work on a vessel [@CL-MKT-1934; @CL-MKT-1935]. For emergency work, the procedures at PGI 217.7103-4 are to be followed when processing this type of undefinitized contract action [@CL-MKT-2034].

## How it works

Master agreements remain in effect until canceled by either the contractor or the contracting officer by giving 30 days written notice to the other [@CL-MKT-1928; @CL-MKT-1929]. Cancellation of a master agreement does not affect the rights and liabilities under any job order existing at the time of cancellation, and the contractor must continue to perform all work covered by any job order issued before the effective date of cancellation of the master agreement [@CL-MKT-1930; @CL-MKT-1931]. When a requirement arises within the United States or its outlying areas for the type of work covered by the master agreement, offers must be solicited from prospective contractors that previously executed a master agreement, or that have not previously executed a master agreement but possess the necessary qualifications to perform the work and agree to execute a master agreement before award of a job order [@CL-MKT-1932; @CL-MKT-1933]. Under the emergency work rule, the contracting officer, without soliciting offers, may issue a written job order to a contractor that has previously executed a master agreement when delay in the performance of necessary repair work would endanger a vessel, its cargo or stores, or when military necessity requires immediate work on a vessel [@CL-MKT-1934; @CL-MKT-9335; @CL-MKT-1935]. For emergency work, the procedures at PGI 217.7103-4 are to be followed when processing this type of undefinitized contract action [@CL-MKT-2034]; the approval and obligation limits for undefinitized contract actions are on [Approval and obligation limits for undefinitized contract actions](mkt-uca-approval-and-obligation-limits.md).

How master agreements and job orders are defined, and which contractors the Government enters into agreements with, are on [Master agreements for vessel repair and the job orders placed under them](mkt-vessel-repair-master-agreements-and-job-orders.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor gives the contracting officer 30 days written notice cancelling its vessel repair master agreement [@CL-MKT-1929]. A job order for 400,000 that was issued before the cancellation takes effect still binds both sides, and the contractor must finish all of its work [@CL-MKT-1930; @CL-MKT-1931]. Later, a requirement arises in the United States for work the master agreements cover. The Government solicits offers from three contractors that hold master agreements and from one qualified contractor that has none but agrees to execute one before award [@CL-MKT-1932; @CL-MKT-1933]. In a separate case, a delay in necessary repair work would endanger a vessel, so the contracting officer issues a written job order, without soliciting offers, to a contractor that already holds a master agreement [@CL-MKT-1934; @CL-MKT-9335].

## Never confuse

Cancellation of a master agreement does not affect the rights and liabilities under any job order existing at the time of cancellation, and the contractor must continue to perform all work covered by any job order issued before the effective date of cancellation [@CL-MKT-1930; @CL-MKT-1931]. A prospective contractor that has not previously executed a master agreement may be solicited for a job order if it possesses the necessary qualifications to perform the work and agrees to execute a master agreement before award of a job order [@CL-MKT-1932; @CL-MKT-1933], while under the emergency work rule the contracting officer, without soliciting offers, may issue a written job order to a contractor that has previously executed a master agreement [@CL-MKT-1934].
