## Purpose

Explains the DoD definitions used in the multiyear contracting subpart, and the authority of the head of the contracting activity to buy electricity from renewable energy sources for a long-term contract, and the limit that applies to a longer period.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-1897] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 217.103 and 217.174 [@CL-MKT-2025; @CL-MKT-1897] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1897] |
| Applies to | Terms used in the subpart, and contracts for electricity from renewable energy sources entered into by the head of the contracting activity [@CL-MKT-2025; @CL-MKT-1897] |

The head of the contracting activity may enter into a contract for a period not to exceed 10 years for the purchase of electricity from sources of renewable energy, as that term is defined in section 203(b)(2) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 [@CL-MKT-1897]. The head of the contracting activity may use that authority for a contract period in excess of 5 years only if the head of the contracting activity determines, on the basis of a business case analysis prepared by the requiring activity, that both of the following are true [@CL-MKT-1898; @CL-MKT-1899; @CL-MKT-1900]. The first is that the proposed purchase of electricity under the contract is cost effective [@CL-MKT-1900]. The second is that it would not be possible to purchase electricity from the source in an economical manner without a contract period in excess of 5 years [@CL-MKT-1901]. The requiring activity prepares the business case analysis, and the section points to PGI 217.174 for a business case analysis template and guidance [@CL-MKT-1899]. Nothing in the section stops DoD from using its other multiyear contracting authority to purchase renewable energy [@CL-MKT-1902].

The definitions apply as used in the subpart [@CL-MKT-2025]. Advance procurement is an exception to the full funding policy that allows acquisition of long lead time items, or economic order quantities of items, in a fiscal year in advance of the year in which the related end item is to be acquired [@CL-MKT-1895]. Advance procurements may include materials, parts, components and effort that must be funded in advance to maintain a planned production schedule [@CL-MKT-1896]. The definition of congressional defense committees lists the Senate Committee on Armed Services, the Senate Committee on Appropriations and its Subcommittee on Defense [@CL-MKT-2026], and continues with the House Committee on Armed Services, the House Committee on Appropriations and its Subcommittee on Defense [@CL-MKT-2027]. A military installation is a base, camp, post, station, yard, center or other activity under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of a military department or, for an activity in a foreign country, under the operational control of the Secretary of a military department or the Secretary of Defense [@CL-MKT-2028].

## How it works

The head of the contracting activity decides, and the requiring activity prepares the analysis on which the decision rests [@CL-MKT-1898; @CL-MKT-1899]. A contract for a period in excess of 5 years may be entered into only if the head of the contracting activity makes the determination on the basis of the business case analysis [@CL-MKT-1898]. The contract period may not exceed 10 years [@CL-MKT-1897]. For a period in excess of 5 years, the determination is that the proposed purchase of electricity under the contract is cost effective [@CL-MKT-1900], and that it would not be possible to purchase electricity from the source in an economical manner without a contract period in excess of 5 years [@CL-MKT-1901].

The general multiyear contracting method is on [Multiyear contracting method and clauses](mkt-multiyear-contracting-method-and-clauses.md), and congressional notification and advance procurement are on [Multiyear contracts: congressional notification and advance procurement](../../industrialization/ramp/ind-multiyear-congressional-notification-and-advance-procurement.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A requiring activity wants a 7-year contract to buy electricity from a solar plant, and it prepares a business case analysis. The head of the contracting activity reviews the analysis and determines that the purchase is cost effective and that electricity could not be bought from the plant economically without a contract longer than 5 years [@CL-MKT-1898; @CL-MKT-1900; @CL-MKT-1901]. With both findings made, the 7-year period is allowed under this authority, because it is within the 10-year limit [@CL-MKT-1897]. If the head of the contracting activity could not make both findings, the period under this authority could not exceed 5 years [@CL-MKT-1898]. A 12-year term would exceed the 10-year limit of this section, which leaves DoD's other multiyear contracting authority available [@CL-MKT-1897; @CL-MKT-1902].

## Never confuse

Advance procurement is an exception to the full funding policy that permits early acquisition of long lead items or economic order quantities [@CL-MKT-1895]. The head of the contracting activity may enter into a contract for a period not to exceed 10 years for the purchase of electricity from sources of renewable energy [@CL-MKT-1897]. A period in excess of 5 years is allowed only if the head of the contracting activity determines, on the basis of a business case analysis prepared by the requiring activity, that the paragraph (b) conditions are met [@CL-MKT-1898; @CL-MKT-1899].
