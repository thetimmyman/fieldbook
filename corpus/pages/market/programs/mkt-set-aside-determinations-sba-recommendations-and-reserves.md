## Purpose

Shows who decides on a set-aside or reserve, when a reserve may not be used, and what follows when the contracting officer rejects an SBA recommendation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0597] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.501, FAR 19.502-8 and FAR 19.503 [@CL-MKT-0597; @CL-MKT-0599; @CL-MKT-0600] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0597] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions the contracting officer considers for a small business set-aside or reserve [@CL-MKT-0597] |

The contracting officer makes the determination to make a small business set-aside, in total or in part, or a reserve [@CL-MKT-0597]. The set-aside decision itself is on the [Small business set-asides](mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card and the partial case is on the [Partial and class set-asides](mkt-partial-and-class-small-business-set-asides.md) card. A small business reserve shall not be used when the acquisition can be set aside, in total or in part [@CL-MKT-0598]. Contracting officers may, at their discretion when conducting multiple-award procurements using full and open competition, reserve one or more contract awards for any of the small business concerns identified in 19.000(a)(3), when market research indicates each of the conditions that follow [@CL-MKT-0599]. The first condition is that a total set-aside is not feasible because there is no reasonable expectation of receiving offers that are competitive in terms of fair market prices, quality, and delivery from at least two responsible small business concerns identified in 19.000(a)(3) that can perform the entire requirement [@CL-MKT-0635; @CL-MKT-9345; @CL-MKT-0599]. The second condition, joined by "and", is that a partial set-aside is not feasible because the contracting officer is unable to divide the requirement into distinct portions or there is no reasonable expectation that at least two responsible small business concerns identified in 19.000(a)(3) can perform any portion of the requirement competitively in terms of fair market price, quality, and delivery [@CL-MKT-9345; @CL-MKT-1821; @CL-MKT-1830].

If the contracting officer rejects a recommendation of the SBA, written notice shall be furnished to the appropriate SBA representative within 5 working days of the contracting officer's receipt of the recommendation [@CL-MKT-0600]. The SBA procurement center representative (or, if none is assigned, as provided in 19.402(a)) may appeal the contracting officer's rejection to the head of the contracting activity within 2 working days after receiving the notice, except as 19.1305(d), 19.1405(e), and 19.1505(i) provide [@CL-MKT-0601]. The head of the contracting activity shall render a decision in writing, and provide it to the SBA representative within 7 working days [@CL-MKT-0630]. Pending issuance of a decision to the SBA representative, the contracting officer shall suspend action on the acquisition [@CL-MKT-0631]. A request to suspend action on an acquisition need not be honored if the contracting officer determines that proceeding to contract award and performance is in the public interest [@CL-MKT-0632]. If the head of the contracting activity agrees that the rejection was appropriate, both of the following apply [@CL-MKT-9347]. Within 2 working days, the SBA procurement center representative (or, if none is assigned, as 19.402(a) provides) may request the contracting officer to suspend action on the acquisition until the SBA Administrator appeals to the agency head [@CL-MKT-9360; @CL-MKT-9363], although a suspension request need not be honored if the contracting officer determines under paragraph (f) that proceeding to contract award and performance is in the public interest [@CL-MKT-0632]. SBA must then be allowed 15 working days after making that written request, within which the SBA Administrator may appeal to the Secretary of the Department concerned [@CL-MKT-9348] and must notify the contracting officer whether the further appeal has, in fact, been taken [@CL-MKT-9361]. If the contracting officer does not receive notification within the 15-day period, the SBA request to suspend the contract action is deemed withdrawn and an appeal to the Secretary is deemed not taken [@CL-MKT-9362]. The agency head shall reply to the SBA within 30 working days after receiving the appeal [@CL-MKT-0633]. The decision of the agency head shall be final [@CL-MKT-0634]. The program-specific set-asides are on the [HUBZone](mkt-hubzone-set-asides-and-price-preference.md), [service-disabled veteran-owned](mkt-sdvosb-status-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) and [women-owned](mkt-wosb-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) cards.

## How it works

After a rejection, the contracting officer must give written notice to SBA within a short working-day limit [@CL-MKT-0600]. The SBA procurement center representative then has a short window to appeal to the head of the contracting activity [@CL-MKT-0601]. The head of the contracting activity gives a written decision, and the contracting officer suspends action until it is issued [@CL-MKT-0630; @CL-MKT-0631]. A reserve is a discretionary tool for full and open multiple-award procurements that is not used where a set-aside is possible [@CL-MKT-0598; @CL-MKT-0599].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Market research for a multiple-award procurement shows that neither a total nor a partial set-aside is feasible. The contracting officer may reserve one or more awards for small business concerns identified in 19.000(a)(3) in the full and open procurement when market research indicates both listed conditions. For a different buy, SBA recommends a set-aside. The contracting officer receives the recommendation on a Monday and rejects it two days later. Written notice to SBA is due within 5 working days of receipt, so a notice sent by the following Monday is in time, assuming no holidays.

## Never confuse

A small business reserve shall not be used when the acquisition can be set aside, in total or in part [@CL-MKT-0598], and it is used at the contracting officer's discretion in multiple-award procurements conducted using full and open competition [@CL-MKT-0599]. The limitations on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule do not apply to reserves at the contract level, but shall apply to orders that are set aside or issued directly to one small business concern under 19.504(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MKT-0636]. See the [Limitations on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule](mkt-limitations-on-subcontracting-and-nonmanufacturer-rule.md) card, and the [Set-asides of orders under multiple-award contracts and the set-aside clauses](mkt-set-aside-orders-and-clauses.md) card for orders.
