## Purpose

Shows who the subcontracting plan clause excludes, what happens if no plan is negotiated, what an individual plan is, and one of the elements the plan must include. The content card covers the rest of the elements.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0821] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-9 [@CL-MKT-0821; @CL-MKT-0824] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-9 [@CL-MKT-1232; @CL-MKT-1235] |
| Edition date | JAN 2025 [@CL-MKT-9439] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0821] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause, except as to small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0821] |

The clause does not apply to small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0821]. Upon request by the contracting officer, the offeror shall submit and negotiate a subcontracting plan, where applicable [@CL-MKT-0880]. The plan separately addresses subcontracting with small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, and women-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-2014]. The plan shall be negotiated within the time specified by the Contracting Officer [@CL-MKT-0822]. Failure to submit and negotiate the subcontracting plan makes the offeror ineligible for award of a contract [@CL-MKT-0822; @CL-MKT-0880].

An individual subcontracting plan is a subcontracting plan that covers the entire contract period (including option periods), applies to a specific contract, and has goals that are based on the offeror's planned subcontracting in support of the specific contract, except that indirect costs incurred for common or joint purposes may be allocated on a prorated basis to the contract [@CL-MKT-1991; @CL-MKT-0823]. The offeror's subcontracting plan shall include the elements the clause lists in paragraph (d), including the name of the individual employed by the offeror who will administer the offeror's subcontracting program, and a description of the duties of the individual [@CL-MKT-0824; @CL-MKT-0825].

## How it works

The clause first excludes small business concerns, then states the time allowed for negotiating a plan and the consequence of not submitting and negotiating it, and then lists what the plan must include [@CL-MKT-0821; @CL-MKT-0822; @CL-MKT-0824]. See the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card for plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages. The assurances on flowdown and reporting, master plans, commercial plans and the material-breach rule are on the [master and commercial plans, reporting and flowdown](mkt-subcontracting-plan-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card. When a plan is required at all is on the [When a subcontracting plan is required](mkt-subcontracting-plan-when-required.md) card.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.219-9 keeps the rule that the clause does not apply to small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1232], the rule that the offeror, upon request by the contracting officer, shall submit and negotiate a subcontracting plan, where applicable, that separately addresses subcontracting with small business, veteran-owned small business (VOSB), service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB), HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business (SDB), and women-owned small business (WOSB) concerns [@CL-MKT-1233; @CL-MKT-1234], the definition of an individual subcontracting plan and the proration of indirect costs [@CL-MKT-1992; @CL-MKT-1236], the rule that the plan shall include the listed elements [@CL-MKT-1237], and the element naming the individual employed by the offeror who will administer the subcontracting program and describing that individual's duties [@CL-MKT-1238]. RFO FAR 52.219-9(c)(1) states that the offeror shall negotiate the subcontracting plan within the timeframe specified by the contracting officer, and if the offeror fails to submit and negotiate the subcontracting plan, then the offeror will not be eligible for award of a contract [@CL-MKT-1235].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A large company bids on a contract that includes the plan clause, and the contracting officer asks for a plan. It does not submit a plan within the time the contracting officer sets, and the negotiation fails. Answer: it is ineligible for award. A small business bidding on the same contract is outside the clause.

## Never confuse

The FAR 52.219-9 subcontracting plan clause applies to other-than-small offerors and never to small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0821; @CL-MKT-1232], and an individual plan sets contract-specific goals unlike a goal-free master plan incorporated from [master and commercial plans, reporting and flowdown](mkt-subcontracting-plan-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) [@CL-MKT-0824; @CL-MKT-0826].
