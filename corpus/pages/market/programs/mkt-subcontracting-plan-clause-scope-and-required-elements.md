## Purpose

Shows who the subcontracting plan clause excludes, what happens if no plan is negotiated, what an individual plan is, and one of the elements the plan must include. The content card covers the rest of the elements.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0821] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-9 [@CL-MKT-0821; @CL-MKT-0824] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0821] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause, except as to small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0821] |

The clause does not apply to small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0821]. Upon request by the contracting officer, the offeror shall submit and negotiate a subcontracting plan, where applicable [@CL-MKT-0880]. The plan shall be negotiated within the time specified by the Contracting Officer [@CL-MKT-0822]. Failure to submit and negotiate the subcontracting plan makes the offeror ineligible for award of a contract [@CL-MKT-0822; @CL-MKT-0880].

An individual subcontracting plan has goals based on the offeror's planned subcontracting in support of the specific contract, except that indirect costs incurred for common or joint purposes may be allocated on a prorated basis to the contract [@CL-MKT-0823]. The offeror's plan shall include the elements the clause then lists [@CL-MKT-0824]. One listed element is the name of the individual employed by the offeror who will administer its subcontracting program, with a description of that individual's duties [@CL-MKT-0825].

## How it works

The clause first excludes small business concerns, then states the time allowed for negotiating a plan and the consequence of not submitting and negotiating it, and then lists what the plan must include [@CL-MKT-0821; @CL-MKT-0822; @CL-MKT-0824]. The goals, statements of dollars, outreach descriptions and other elements are not restated here; see the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card. The assurances on flowdown and reporting, master plans, commercial plans and the material-breach rule are on the [master and commercial plans, reporting and flowdown](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card. When a plan is required at all is on the [When a subcontracting plan is required](mkt-subcontracting-plan-when-required.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A large company bids on a contract that includes the plan clause, and the contracting officer asks for a plan. It does not submit a plan within the time the contracting officer sets, and the negotiation fails. Answer: it is ineligible for award. A small business bidding on the same contract is outside the clause.

## Never confuse

The individual plan has goals based on the offeror's planned subcontracting in support of the specific contract [@CL-MKT-0823]. Commercial and master plans are on the [master and commercial plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card for this clause. The policy clause that sets the contractor's general duty, without goals, is on the [Utilization of small business concerns](mkt-utilization-of-small-business-concerns-clause.md) card.
