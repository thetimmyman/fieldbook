## Purpose

Shows what the small business program representations provision asks an offeror to state about its size and status, how the NAICS code and size standard enter, and what the notice in the provision says about misrepresentation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0806] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-1 [@CL-MKT-0806; @CL-MKT-0809] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0806] |
| Applies to | Offerors that make the representations as part of their offers [@CL-MKT-0808] |

The basic provision gives the NAICS code for the acquisition and the small business size standard as blanks to be inserted [@CL-MKT-0806]. In the provision, a small business concern is a concern, including its affiliates, that is independently owned and operated, not dominant in its field of operation, and qualified as a small business under the criteria in part 121 of title 13 of the Code of Federal Regulations and the size standard in paragraph (b) [@CL-MKT-0809]. For a concern that offers an end item it did not itself manufacture, process, or produce, on an acquisition other than construction or service, the size standard is 500 employees, or 150 employees for information technology value-added resellers under NAICS code 541519 [@CL-MKT-0807]. That standard applies if the acquisition is set aside for small business and has a value above the simplified acquisition threshold, or uses the HUBZone price evaluation preference regardless of dollar value unless the offeror waives it [@CL-MKT-0807; @CL-MKT-0872]. It also applies if the acquisition is an 8(a), HUBZone, service-disabled veteran-owned, economically disadvantaged women-owned, or women-owned small business set-aside or sole-source award regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0807; @CL-MKT-0873].

The offeror represents as part of its offer that it is, or is not, a small business concern [@CL-MKT-0808]. An alternative representation in the same paragraph is that the offeror is, or is not, a small business joint venture that complies with the requirements of sections 121.103(h) and 125.8(a) and (b) of title 13 of the Code of Federal Regulations [@CL-MKT-0974]. Under a substitute version of the paragraph, the offeror represents its small business size status for each NAICS code assigned to the acquisition under which it is submitting an offer [@CL-MKT-0813].

## How it works

Once the blanks are filled, the offeror states its size status against the NAICS code and size standard as part of its offer [@CL-MKT-0806; @CL-MKT-0808]. The provision has further representations for other statuses, such as disadvantaged, women-owned, veteran-owned and HUBZone, and this card does not restate them. The notice paragraph is aimed at any person who misrepresents a firm's status as small, HUBZone small, small disadvantaged, service-disabled veteran-owned small, economically disadvantaged women-owned small, or women-owned small eligible under the WOSB Program in order to obtain a contract [@CL-MKT-0810]. A person who does so to obtain a contract to be awarded under the preference programs established under listed sections of the Small Business Act, or other Federal law that references section 8(d) for program eligibility, shall be punished by fine, imprisonment, or both [@CL-MKT-0811]. One set of listed further consequences is administrative remedies, including suspension and debarment, and ineligibility for participation in programs conducted under the authority of the Act [@CL-MKT-0812].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation names a size standard of 600 employees. An offeror has 450 employees, counting its affiliates, and meets the other criteria. It can represent that it is a small business concern. With 700 employees it would represent that it is not.

## Never confuse

The representation is the offeror's own statement of status, made in its offer. It is not an SBA size determination. The rule on when and how the offeror makes the representation is on the [Offeror size and status representation](../taxonomy/mkt-offeror-size-and-status-representation.md) card, and the SBA penalty rules for misrepresenting size are on the [Size misrepresentation penalties](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-misrepresentation-penalties-and-subcontract-size.md) card. The representation after award is on the [Postaward rerepresentation and subcontracting liquidated damages](mkt-postaward-rerepresentation-and-subcontracting-liquidated-damages.md) card, and the notice clauses that reject offers from concerns that are not small on set-aside solicitations are on the [Small business set-aside notice clauses](mkt-small-business-set-aside-notice-clauses.md) card.
