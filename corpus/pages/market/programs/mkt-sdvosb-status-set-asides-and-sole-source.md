## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer verifies about SDVOSB status and the conditions for an SDVOSB set-aside and an SDVOSB sole-source award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0618] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.1403, FAR 19.1405 and FAR 19.1406 [@CL-MKT-0618; @CL-MKT-0619; @CL-MKT-0620; @CL-MKT-9250]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-MKT-9230; @CL-MKT-9231; @CL-MKT-9285] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-MKT-9286; @CL-MKT-9287; @CL-MKT-9288] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0618] |
| Applies to | SDVOSB set-asides and SDVOSB sole-source contracts [@CL-MKT-0618; @CL-MKT-0619; @CL-MKT-0620] |

For an SDVOSB concern that seeks an SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source contract, the contracting officer shall verify that the offeror, effective January 1, 2024, is designated in the System for Award Management as an SDVOSB concern certified by SBA, or meets the second listed alternative [@CL-MKT-0618; @CL-MKT-0646]. The second listed alternative is that the offeror has represented that it is an SDVOSB concern in SAM and submitted an application for certification to SBA on or before December 31, 2023 [@CL-MKT-0646]. A contracting officer may restrict competition to SDVOSB concerns eligible under the SDVOSB Program if there is a reasonable expectation based on market research that two or more such concerns will submit offers and that award will be made at a fair market price [@CL-MKT-1817; @CL-MKT-0619]. If the contracting officer receives only one acceptable offer from an SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program in response to a set-aside, the contracting officer should make an award to that concern [@CL-MKT-0648]. Effective January 1, 2024, a joint venture may be considered an SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program if the joint venture qualifies as small under 19.301-1(a)(2)(i), the managing SDVOSB joint venture partner is designated in SAM as an SDVOSB concern certified by SBA or has represented that it is an SDVOSB concern in SAM and submitted an application for certification to SBA on or before December 31, 2023, and the joint venture complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 128.402 [@CL-MKT-0649; @CL-MKT-1820; @CL-MKT-1829].

The FAR definitions section describes an SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program as an SDVOSB concern that, effective January 1, 2024, is designated in SAM as certified by SBA in accordance with 13 CFR 128.300 [@CL-MKT-9230], or that has represented that it is an SDVOSB concern in SAM and submitted a complete application for certification to SBA on or before December 31, 2023 [@CL-MKT-9231]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines an SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program under the same two criteria [@CL-MKT-9287; @CL-MKT-9288]. Both FAR 2.101 and RFO FAR 2.101 define the SDVOSB Program as a program that authorizes contracting officers to limit competition, including award on a sole-source basis, to SDVOSB concerns eligible under the SDVOSB Program [@CL-MKT-9285; @CL-MKT-9286].

A contracting officer shall consider a contract award to an SDVOSB concern on a sole source basis before considering small business set-asides, provided none of the listed exclusions apply and the listed conditions are met [@CL-MKT-0620]. One listed condition is that the contracting officer does not have a reasonable expectation that offers would be received from two or more service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-9250]. Another listed condition is that the anticipated award price of the contract, including options, will not exceed $8.5 million for a requirement within the NAICS codes for manufacturing or $5 million for a requirement within any other NAICS code [@CL-MKT-0621]. The remaining conditions are that the requirement is not currently being performed by an 8(a) participant under subpart 19.8 or has been accepted as a requirement by SBA under subpart 19.8, that the SDVOSB concern has been determined to be a responsible contractor with respect to performance, and that award can be made at a fair and reasonable price [@CL-MKT-1818; @CL-MKT-1819]. Effective January 1, 2024, a contracting officer shall only award a sole-source contract to a concern that is designated in SAM as an SDVOSB concern certified by SBA, or that meets the second listed alternative [@CL-MKT-0622]. For a sole-source contract, that alternative is the same representation in SAM and application for certification on or before December 31, 2023 [@CL-MKT-0647].

## How it works

The contracting officer verifies the offeror's status for an SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source contract [@CL-MKT-0618]. A set-aside depends on a reasonable expectation, based on market research, of two or more offers and of award at a fair market price [@CL-MKT-0619]. A sole-source award is considered provided none of the listed exclusions apply and the listed conditions, including the expectation about offers and the price ceiling, are met [@CL-MKT-0620; @CL-MKT-9250; @CL-MKT-0621]. 

The purpose of the service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) Program is to provide Federal contracting assistance to service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-2210]. The procedures in FAR subpart 19.14 apply to all Federal agencies that employ one or more contracting officers [@CL-MKT-2211].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A requirement in a non-manufacturing NAICS code has an anticipated award price, including options, of 4 million. That is under the 5 million ceiling, so the price condition is met. The other listed conditions must still be met, and the awardee must be a certified SDVOSB concern or meet the second alternative.

## Never confuse

A set-aside restricts competition among SDVOSB concerns and depends on a reasonable expectation of two or more offers [@CL-MKT-0619]. A sole-source award has its own conditions and price ceiling [@CL-MKT-0620; @CL-MKT-0621]. Size is a separate question; see the [Protesting a small business representation](../taxonomy/mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) card. Protests of SDVOSB status are on the [Protesting a firm's service-disabled veteran-owned small business status](mkt-sdvosb-status-protests.md) card.
