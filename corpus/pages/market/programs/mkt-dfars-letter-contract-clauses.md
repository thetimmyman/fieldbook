## Purpose

Shows the two DoD contract-clause directions at DFARS 216.603-4, Contract clauses, the section under Letter contracts [@CL-MKT-2005; @CL-MKT-1947].

## The rule

| Codified citation | DFARS 216.603-4(b)(2) and (b)(3) [@CL-MKT-1870; @CL-MKT-1871] |

For the clause at FAR 52.216-24, Limitation of Government Liability, the section says to see 217.7405(a) for additional guidance regarding its use [@CL-MKT-1870]. The section also says to use the clause at 252.217-7027, Contract Definitization, in accordance with its prescription at 217.7406(b), instead of the clause at FAR 52.216-25, Contract Definitization [@CL-MKT-1871].

The first direction is a pointer to guidance; it does not say to include or omit the Limitation of Government Liability clause [@CL-MKT-1870]. The second direction names no official who applies it, and the condition for using the DoD clause lies in the prescription at 217.7406(b), which this section does not set out [@CL-MKT-1871].

## How it works

The section does not restate when a definitization clause is needed; that depends on the prescription at 217.7406(b) [@CL-MKT-1871]. Where that prescription calls for the DoD clause, the clause takes the place of the FAR definitization clause and is not added to it [@CL-MKT-1871]. For the Limitation of Government Liability clause the section adds only a cross-reference to further guidance [@CL-MKT-1870]. The section sets no deadline, threshold or approval step of its own.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A letter contract is awarded, and a definitive contract is to replace it later. Assume the prescription at 217.7406(b) calls for the DoD Contract Definitization clause for this contract. The contract then carries the DoD clause instead of the FAR Contract Definitization clause [@CL-MKT-1871]. For the Limitation of Government Liability clause, the section points to the additional guidance at 217.7405(a) [@CL-MKT-1870].

## Never confuse

The DoD definitization clause replaces the FAR clause; it is not an addition to it [@CL-MKT-1871]. The section does not say the Limitation of Government Liability clause is replaced; it only cross-references guidance on its use [@CL-MKT-1870]. The definitization clause itself is described on [Contract definitization for letter contracts](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-contract-definitization.md).
