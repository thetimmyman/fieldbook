## Purpose

Sets out the defined terms for reverse auctions and the provision and clauses the contracting officer must insert when the method is used, when orders may be placed by it, or when reverse auction services are bought.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-2120] |
| Codified citation | FAR 17.801 and 17.805 [@CL-MKT-2120; @CL-MKT-2124] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-2120] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that use a reverse auction to award, may use one to place orders, or buy reverse auction services [@CL-MKT-2124; @CL-MKT-2125; @CL-MKT-2126] |

The terms in the definitions section are defined as used in the reverse auction subpart [@CL-MKT-2156]. Government data means any information, document, media, or machine-readable material, regardless of its physical form or characteristics, that is created or obtained by the Government in the course of official Government business [@CL-MKT-2120]. Government-related data means such material that a contractor creates or obtains through the storage, processing, or communication of Government data [@CL-MKT-2121]. Government-related data does not include a contractor's business records (e.g., financial records, legal records, etc.), or data such as operating procedures, software coding, or algorithms that are not uniquely applied to the Government data [@CL-MKT-2122]. A reverse auction service provider is a commercial or Government entity that provides a means for conducting reverse auctions when acquiring supplies or services to be used by the Government [@CL-MKT-2123].

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.217-10, Reverse Auction, in solicitations when using a reverse auction to award a contract or blanket purchase agreement [@CL-MKT-2124]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.217-11, Reverse Auction—Orders, in solicitations and contracts for a multiple-award contract or blanket purchase agreement, when a reverse auction may be used to place orders under the basic contract or blanket purchase agreement [@CL-MKT-2125]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.217-12, Reverse Auction Services, in all solicitations and contracts for the purchase of reverse auction services [@CL-MKT-2126].

## How it works

The 52.217-10 provision is prescribed for solicitations when using a reverse auction to award a contract or blanket purchase agreement, whereas the 52.217-11 and 52.217-12 clauses are prescribed for solicitations and contracts when a reverse auction may be used to place orders under a multiple-award contract or blanket purchase agreement and when purchasing reverse auction services [@CL-MKT-2124; @CL-MKT-2125; @CL-MKT-2126]. The definitions distinguish Government data created or obtained by the Government from Government-related data created or obtained by a contractor through the storage, processing, or communication of Government data, and exclude the contractor's business records (e.g., financial records, legal records, etc.) and data such as operating procedures, software coding, or algorithms that are not uniquely applied to the Government data [@CL-MKT-2120; @CL-MKT-2121; @CL-MKT-2122].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency uses a reverse auction to award a supply contract, so the solicitation carries the 52.217-10 provision [@CL-MKT-2124]. The agency buys the auction platform from a commercial provider by a separate acquisition, so that solicitation and contract carry the 52.217-12 clause [@CL-MKT-2126]. The agency also plans a multiple-award blanket purchase agreement and may later place orders by reverse auction, so the solicitation and the agreement carry the 52.217-11 clause [@CL-MKT-2125].

## Never confuse

Government data and Government-related data are different: the first is created or obtained by the Government, and the second is created or obtained by a contractor through storing, processing or communicating the first [@CL-MKT-2120; @CL-MKT-2121]. A contractor's business records and algorithms not uniquely applied to the Government data are not Government-related data [@CL-MKT-2122]. When a reverse auction is suitable and how it is run are on [Reverse auctions: suitability and award](mkt-reverse-auctions-suitability-and-award.md).
