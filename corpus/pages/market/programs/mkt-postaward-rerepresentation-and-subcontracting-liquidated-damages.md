## Purpose

Shows when a contractor must rerepresent its size and status after award, and how liquidated damages are computed when a contractor fails to make a good faith effort to comply with its subcontracting plan.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0848] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-28 and FAR 52.219-16 [@CL-MKT-0848; @CL-MKT-0853] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0848] |
| Applies to | Contractors that represented a listed small business status before award, for rerepresentation; contracts with a subcontracting plan, for liquidated damages [@CL-MKT-0848; @CL-MKT-0853] |

If the contractor represented before award that it was any of the small business concerns the clause cross-references, it shall rerepresent its size and socioeconomic status according to paragraph (f) of the clause or, if applicable, paragraph (h) of the clause [@CL-MKT-0848]. The representation it makes before award is on the [Small business program representations provision](mkt-small-business-program-representations-provision.md) card. One listed occasion is within 30 days after execution of a novation agreement, or within 30 days after modification of the contract to include the clause if the novation agreement was executed before the clause was included [@CL-MKT-0849]. Another is within 30 days after a merger or acquisition that does not require a novation, or within 30 days after modification to include the clause if the merger or acquisition occurred before the clause was included [@CL-MKT-0850]. For long-term contracts, one listed occasion is within 60 to 120 days before the end of the fifth year of the contract and within 60 to 120 days before the date specified for exercising any option thereafter [@CL-MKT-0851].

## How it works

For liquidated damages, failure to make a good faith effort to comply with the subcontracting plan includes a willful or intentional failure to perform in accordance with the requirements of the plan [@CL-MKT-0852]. The plan itself is on the [scope and required elements](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-scope-and-required-elements.md) card. One condition for liquidated damages is that the contractor has failed to meet its subcontracting goals and the contracting officer decides that it failed to make a good faith effort to comply with its plan [@CL-MKT-0853]. The amount of probable damages is an amount equal to the actual dollar amount by which the contractor failed to achieve each subcontract goal [@CL-MKT-0854]. Performance is measured by applying the percentage goals to the total actual subcontracting dollars or, for a commercial plan, to the pro rata share of actual subcontracting dollars attributable to Government contracts covered by the commercial plan [@CL-MKT-0855]. Commercial plans are on the [master and commercial plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card. The clause also has rerepresentation occasions for orders, the rerepresentation content, notice and appeal steps, which this card does not restate; see the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card for the notice steps.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A plan sets a goal of 400,000 for a group of concerns and the contractor places 300,000. The shortfall is 100,000. The contracting officer decides after notice that the contractor did not make a good faith effort. Answer: the liquidated damages for that goal are 100,000, and the same calculation is made for each goal that was missed. If the contracting officer had found good faith effort, no liquidated damages would follow from the miss alone.

## Never confuse

Rerepresentation is the contractor's own statement on the listed occasions; it is not a size protest, which is on the [Protesting a small business representation or rerepresentation](../taxonomy/mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) card. The general rule on rerepresenting after award is on the [Rerepresentation after award](../taxonomy/mkt-size-rerepresentation-after-award.md) card. Missing a goal is not by itself a failure to make a good faith effort, because the damages condition needs the contracting officer's decision on good faith [@CL-MKT-0853].
