## Purpose

Shows when a contractor must rerepresent its size and status after award, and how liquidated damages are computed when a contractor fails to make a good faith effort to comply with its subcontracting plan.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0848] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.219-28 and FAR 52.219-16 [@CL-MKT-0848; @CL-MKT-0853] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.219-28 [@CL-MKT-0264]; RFO FAR 52.219-16 [@CL-MKT-0295] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0848] |
| Applies to | Contractors that represented a listed small business status before award, for rerepresentation; contracts with a subcontracting plan, for liquidated damages [@CL-MKT-0848; @CL-MKT-0853] |

If the contractor represented before award that it was any of the small business concerns the clause cross-references, it shall rerepresent its size and socioeconomic status according to paragraph (f) of the clause or, if applicable, paragraph (h) of the clause [@CL-MKT-0848]. The representation it makes before award is on the [Small business program representations provision](mkt-small-business-program-representations-provision.md) card. One listed occasion is within 30 days after execution of a novation agreement, or within 30 days after modification of the contract to include the clause if the novation agreement was executed before the clause was included [@CL-MKT-0849]. Another is within 30 days after a merger or acquisition that does not require a novation, or within 30 days after modification to include the clause if the merger or acquisition occurred before the clause was included [@CL-MKT-0850]. For long-term contracts, the listed occasions are within 60 to 120 days before the end of the fifth year of the contract, and within 60 to 120 days before the date specified for exercising any option thereafter [@CL-MKT-0851]. The term long-term contract does not include a contract that exceeds five years in duration because its period of performance has been extended for a cumulative period not to exceed six months under the option to extend services clause or other appropriate authority [@CL-MKT-0980].

## How it works

For liquidated damages, failure to make a good faith effort to comply with the subcontracting plan includes a willful or intentional failure to perform in accordance with the requirements of the plan [@CL-MKT-0852], and also willful or intentional action to frustrate the plan [@CL-MKT-0978]. The plan itself is on the [scope and required elements](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-scope-and-required-elements.md) card. One condition for liquidated damages is that the contractor has failed to meet its subcontracting goals and the contracting officer decides that it failed to make a good faith effort to comply with its plan [@CL-MKT-0853]. The failure to meet goals is judged at contract completion or, in the case of a commercial plan, at the close of the fiscal year for which the plan is applicable [@CL-MKT-0979]. The amount of probable damages is an amount equal to the actual dollar amount by which the contractor failed to achieve each subcontract goal [@CL-MKT-0854]. Performance is measured by applying the percentage goals to the total actual subcontracting dollars or, for a commercial plan, to the pro rata share of actual subcontracting dollars attributable to Government contracts covered by the commercial plan [@CL-MKT-0855]. Commercial plans are on the [master and commercial plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-clause-master-commercial-reporting-flowdown.md) card. The codified clause also has a paragraph (c) on rerepresentation for the NAICS code assigned to an order, with an exception for orders under a Federal Supply Schedule contract [@CL-MKT-9072; @CL-MKT-9073], the rerepresentation content, notice and appeal steps, which this card does not restate; see the [Subcontracting plan content, good faith effort and liquidated damages](mkt-subcontracting-plan-content-and-compliance.md) card for the notice steps.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of the rerepresentation clause keeps the occasion within 30 days after execution of a novation agreement [@CL-MKT-0849; @CL-MKT-0265], the occasion within 30 days after a merger or acquisition that does not require a novation [@CL-MKT-0850; @CL-MKT-0266], and the long-term contract occasions at 60 to 120 days [@CL-MKT-0851; @CL-MKT-0267]. It keeps the exclusion from the long-term contract definition for a cumulative extension of not more than six months [@CL-MKT-0980; @CL-MKT-0268]. The RFO text says the duty applies where the contractor represented before award that it was a small business concern, a small disadvantaged business concern, or a joint venture that was one of the identified concerns [@CL-MKT-0263], where the codified text says any of the identified concerns [@CL-MKT-0848]. The RFO text points to paragraph (e) or, if applicable, paragraph (g) of its clause for how to rerepresent [@CL-MKT-0264], where the codified text points to paragraph (f) or (h) [@CL-MKT-0848]. The RFO clause prints no paragraph on rerepresentation for orders, and its paragraph (c) is the size-standard paragraph [@CL-MKT-0269], where the codified clause has a paragraph (c) on the NAICS code assigned to an order [@CL-MKT-9072; @CL-MKT-9073]. The RFO text of the liquidated damages clause says the Government will measure performance by applying the percentage goals to total actual subcontracting dollars or, for a commercial plan, to the pro rata share [@CL-MKT-0295], where the codified text says performance shall be measured that way [@CL-MKT-0855]. It keeps the timing at contract completion or at the close of the fiscal year for a commercial plan [@CL-MKT-0979; @CL-MKT-0296], and the damages amount equal to the actual dollar amount by which each goal was missed [@CL-MKT-0854; @CL-MKT-0299]. It says the contracting officer's decision on good faith effort is made in accordance with paragraph (b) of the RFO clause [@CL-MKT-0297], where the codified text points to paragraph (c) [@CL-MKT-0853], and that the contractor shall pay the liquidated damages in a stated amount [@CL-MKT-0298]. The RFO clause prints no definition of failure to make a good faith effort to comply with the subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0881], where the codified clause defines it as a willful or intentional failure to perform or a willful or intentional action to frustrate the plan [@CL-MKT-0852; @CL-MKT-0978].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A plan sets a goal of 400,000 for a group of concerns and, at contract completion, the contractor has placed 300,000. The shortfall is 100,000. The contracting officer decides after notice that the contractor did not make a good faith effort. Answer: the liquidated damages for that goal are 100,000, and the same calculation is made for each goal that was missed. If the contracting officer had found good faith effort, no liquidated damages would follow from the miss alone.

## Never confuse

Rerepresentation is the contractor's own statement on the listed occasions; it is not a size protest, which is on the [Protesting a small business representation or rerepresentation](../taxonomy/mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) card. The general rule on rerepresenting after award is on the [Rerepresentation after award](../taxonomy/mkt-size-rerepresentation-after-award.md) card. Missing a goal is not by itself a failure to make a good faith effort, because the damages condition needs the contracting officer's decision on good faith [@CL-MKT-0853]. The codified liquidated damages clause defines a failure to make a good faith effort as a willful or intentional failure or action [@CL-MKT-0852; @CL-MKT-0978], but the RFO clause prints no such definition [@CL-MKT-0881].
