## Purpose

Shows when performance on a purchase order under a program award may not begin, and how a nonprofit organization may compete for religious-related services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0932] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.219-7011 and DFARS 252.219-7012 [@CL-MKT-0932; @CL-MKT-0935] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0932] |
| Applies to | Purchase orders that carry the delay clause, prescribed in DFARS 219.811-3(3), and solicitations that carry the religious-services provision, prescribed in DFARS 219.270-3 [@CL-MKT-0932; @CL-MKT-0935; @CL-MKT-0951; @CL-MKT-0952] |

The contractor shall not begin performance under the purchase order until 2 working days have passed from the date of its receipt [@CL-MKT-0932]. Unless the contractor receives notification from the SBA that it is ineligible for the 8(a) award, or otherwise receives instructions from the contracting officer, performance may begin on the third working day following receipt [@CL-MKT-0933]. If a determination of ineligibility is issued within the 2-day period, the purchase order shall be considered canceled [@CL-MKT-0934].

A nonprofit organization is not precluded from competing for a contract for religious-related services to be performed on a United States military installation, even though it is not a small business concern identified in the FAR [@CL-MKT-0935]. If the apparently successful offeror has not represented in its offer or quotation that it is a small business concern identified in the FAR, as appropriate to the solicitation, the contracting officer will verify that the offeror is registered in the System for Award Management database as a nonprofit organization [@CL-MKT-0936; @CL-MKT-0937].

## How it works

The delay clause sets a waiting period during which the SBA may notify the contractor that the 8(a) award is ineligible [@CL-MKT-0933]. The competition provision says a nonprofit organization is not precluded from competing for a contract for religious-related services to be performed on a United States military installation, notwithstanding that it is not a small business concern identified in the FAR [@CL-MKT-0935].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD office places a purchase order with a program participant, and the contractor receives it on a Monday. No ineligibility notice arrives and the contracting officer gives no other instruction.

Answer: the contractor waits two working days and may begin on Thursday, the third working day. If an ineligibility determination had been issued in the first two days, the order would be treated as canceled.

## Never confuse

Under the delay clause, if a determination of ineligibility is issued within the 2-day period, the purchase order shall be considered canceled [@CL-MKT-0934]. Under the religious-services provision, a nonprofit organization is not precluded from competing for a contract for religious-related services to be performed on a United States military installation [@CL-MKT-0935]. The direct award and competition notice clauses for the same program are on the [direct award and limited competition notice](mkt-dfars-8a-direct-award-and-limited-competition-notice.md) card. How the SBA accepts a requirement is on the [offering and acceptance](mkt-8a-offering-and-acceptance.md) card, and registration in SAM is on the [SAM registration and awards](../feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md) card.
