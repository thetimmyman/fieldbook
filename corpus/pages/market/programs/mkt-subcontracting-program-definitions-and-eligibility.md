## Purpose

Defines the terms used in the small business subcontracting subpart and shows which subcontractors a prime contractor may count under the program.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0425] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.701 and FAR 19.703 [@CL-MKT-0425; @CL-MKT-0436] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0425] |
| Applies to | Subcontractors that represent themselves as listed small business concerns, except as the cited paragraph provides [@CL-MKT-0436] |

Except as provided in paragraph (c) of that section, which covers Alaska Native Corporations and Indian tribes, a concern must represent itself as a small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business or women-owned small business concern to be eligible as a subcontractor under the program [@CL-MKT-0436; @CL-MKT-0438; @CL-MKT-0550]. To make that representation, a concern must meet the appropriate definition [@CL-MKT-0550]. For subcontracting purposes, a concern is small if it does not exceed the size standard for the NAICS code that the prime contractor determines best describes the product or service being acquired by the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0437]. Under the listed procedures, subcontracts awarded to an ANC or Indian tribe are counted toward the small business and small disadvantaged business goals, regardless of the size or Small Business Administration certification status of the ANC or Indian tribe [@CL-MKT-0438].

The definition of an Alaska Native Corporation (ANC) covers the listed kinds of corporation organized under the laws of the State of Alaska in accordance with the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act and considered a minority and economically disadvantaged concern under the cited criteria [@CL-MKT-0425]. It also includes ANC direct and indirect subsidiary corporations, joint ventures and partnerships that meet the cited requirements [@CL-MKT-0551]. The definition of an Indian tribe covers the listed kinds of tribe, group or community that are recognized by the Federal Government as eligible for services from the Bureau of Indian Affairs [@CL-MKT-0429]. It also includes Indian-owned economic enterprises that meet the cited requirements [@CL-MKT-0552]. A subcontract is any agreement, other than one involving an employer-employee relationship, entered into by a Government prime contractor or subcontractor for supplies or services required for performance of the contract, a contract modification or a subcontract [@CL-MKT-0433]. Total contract dollars means the final anticipated dollar value of the contract, including the dollar value of all options [@CL-MKT-0434]. An individual subcontracting plan covers the entire contract period, including option periods [@CL-MKT-0555]. It applies to a specific contract and has goals based on the offeror's planned subcontracting in support of that contract, except that indirect costs incurred for common or joint purposes may be allocated on a prorated basis to the contract [@CL-MKT-0430]. A master subcontracting plan contains all the required elements of an individual plan except goals, and may be incorporated into individual plans if the master plan has been approved [@CL-MKT-0431]. A commercial plan is a subcontracting plan that covers the offeror's fiscal year and applies to the entire production of commercial products and performance of commercial services sold by the entire company or a portion of it [@CL-MKT-0426]. The Electronic Subcontracting Reporting System (eSRS) is the Governmentwide, electronic, web-based system for small business subcontracting program reporting [@CL-MKT-0427]. Failure to make a good faith effort to comply with the subcontracting plan means willful or intentional failure to perform in accordance with the plan's requirements, or willful or intentional action to frustrate the plan [@CL-MKT-0428]. A reduced payment is a payment for less than the amount agreed in a subcontract under its terms and conditions, for supplies and services for which the Government has paid the prime contractor [@CL-MKT-0432]. An untimely payment is a payment to a subcontractor that is more than 90 days past due under the subcontract terms for supplies and services for which the Government has paid the prime contractor [@CL-MKT-0435].

## How it works

The prime contractor picks the NAICS code for the size test of a subcontract [@CL-MKT-0437]. A subcontract to an ANC or Indian tribe counts toward the small business and small disadvantaged business goals whatever the size or certification status of the ANC or tribe [@CL-MKT-0438]. A master plan holds every required element of an individual plan except goals and can be incorporated into individual plans once approved [@CL-MKT-0431]. A commercial plan covers the offeror's fiscal year, while an individual plan covers the entire contract period, including option periods, and applies to a specific contract [@CL-MKT-0426; @CL-MKT-0430; @CL-MKT-0555]. A prime contractor acting in good faith is not liable for misrepresentations made by its subcontractors regarding the subcontractor's size or socioeconomic status [@CL-MKT-0553]. Under the ANC designation procedure, if the contracting officer does not receive a copy of the written designation within 30 days of the subcontract award, the contractor that awarded the subcontract to the ANC or Indian tribe will be considered the designated contractor [@CL-MKT-0554]. The source printed for this card does not state the amount of any size standard. When a plan is required is on the [When a small business subcontracting plan is required](mkt-subcontracting-plan-when-required.md) card, the general rules for plans are on the [General rules for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-general-rules.md) card, and the review of a plan is on the [Reviewing the subcontracting plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-review.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor awards a subcontract worth 100,000 to an Alaska Native Corporation that is above the size standard for the NAICS code. The subcontract still counts toward the prime contractor's small business and small disadvantaged business goals. A different subcontract is paid 60 days after its due date. That payment is not an untimely payment, which needs more than 90 days past due.

## Never confuse

A reduced payment is tested by amount against the agreed price [@CL-MKT-0432], while an untimely payment is tested by timing, more than 90 days past due [@CL-MKT-0435]. A master plan has no goals, while an individual plan does [@CL-MKT-0431; @CL-MKT-0430]. Total contract dollars include the value of all options [@CL-MKT-0434], and a subcontract excludes an agreement involving an employer-employee relationship [@CL-MKT-0433]. The paragraphs on how a prime contractor may accept a subcontractor's size representation, on protests and on the rest of the ANC designation procedure are in the same section and are not restated here.

## Company calibration

How the company records each subcontractor's size and status representation, including any ANC or Indian tribe supplier, and ties it to one supplier identity. [@Q-021]
