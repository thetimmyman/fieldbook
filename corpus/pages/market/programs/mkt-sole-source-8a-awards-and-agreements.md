## Purpose

Sets out how a sole-source 8(a) award is negotiated and approved, how orders under basic ordering agreements and blanket purchase agreements are accepted, and how the agency prepares the contract with the SBA [@CL-MKT-1272; @CL-MKT-1279; @CL-MKT-1282].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1272] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.808-1, FAR 19.804-5 and FAR 19.811-1 [@CL-MKT-1272; @CL-MKT-1279; @CL-MKT-1282] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1272] |
| Applies to | Sole-source 8(a) contracts, and orders under basic ordering agreements and blanket purchase agreements [@CL-MKT-1272; @CL-MKT-1279] |

The SBA may not accept for negotiation a sole-source 8(a) contract that exceeds $30 million unless the requesting agency has completed a justification in accordance with the requirements of 6.303 [@CL-MKT-1272]. The SBA is responsible for initiating negotiations with the agency within the time established by the agency [@CL-MKT-1273]. If the SBA does not do so within the agreed time and the agency cannot allow additional time, the agency may, after notifying the SBA, proceed with the acquisition from other sources [@CL-MKT-1274].

The SBA should participate, whenever practicable, in negotiating the contracting terms, and when mutually agreeable it may authorize the contracting officer to negotiate directly with the 8(a) participant [@CL-MKT-1275]. Whether or not direct negotiations take place, the SBA is responsible for approving the resulting contract before award [@CL-MKT-1276].

## How it works

### The participant

An 8(a) participant must represent that it is a small business under the size standard for the NAICS code assigned to the contract [@CL-MKT-1277]. The concern must be a current participant in the 8(a) program at the time of the sole-source award [@CL-MKT-1372]. A participant owned by an Alaska Native Corporation, Indian Tribe, Native Hawaiian Organization, or Community Development Corporation may not receive a sole-source follow-on award if the predecessor contract was performed by another participant, or former participant, owned by the same entity [@CL-MKT-1278; @CL-MKT-1373].

### Orders under agreements

The contracting office submits an offering letter for, and the SBA must accept, each order under a basic ordering agreement or blanket purchase agreement issued under part 13, in addition to the agency offering and the SBA accepting the agreement itself [@CL-MKT-1279; @CL-MKT-1374]. The SBA will not accept for sole-source award an order that would push the total orders under a specific agreement over the competitive threshold amount; the amounts are on the [competitive awards card](mkt-8a-competitive-awards.md) [@CL-MKT-1280]. The SBA will not accept new orders for a participant once its program term expires, it otherwise exits the program, or it becomes other than small for the NAICS code assigned under the agreement [@CL-MKT-1281].

### The agency contract

The agency prepares the contract to be awarded to the SBA in accordance with agency procedures and in the same detail as a contract with a business concern [@CL-MKT-1375]. The contracting officer uses the Standard Form 26 as the award form, except for construction contracts, where the Standard Form 1442 is used [@CL-MKT-1282].

The contracting officer prepares the contract that the SBA will award to the participant as if awarding directly to the participant, except for the listed items [@CL-MKT-1376]. The award form cites the statutory authority for other than full and open competition [@CL-MKT-1377]. The contract includes appropriate clauses, as necessary, to show that it is between the SBA and the 8(a) contractor [@CL-MKT-1378]. The SBA inserts its contract number, the effective date, the typed name and signature of its contracting officer, and the date signed [@CL-MKT-1379].

The SBA obtains the 8(a) contractor's signature before signing and returning the prime contract to the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1380]. The SBA will make every effort to return the contract, and any later bilateral modification, within a maximum of 10 working days [@CL-MKT-1381].

### The single-document alternative

Except where the SBA will make advance payments to its 8(a) contractor, the agency contracting officer may use a single contract document as an alternative to the separate contracts [@CL-MKT-1382]. It contains the authority citation, the clauses and the SBA-inserted items [@CL-MKT-1383]. Blocks on the Standard Form 26 or 1442 are asterisked and a continuation sheet is appended as a tripartite agreement [@CL-MKT-1384; @CL-MKT-1385]. For acquisitions not exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, the contracting officer may use the same alternative with the appropriate simplified acquisition forms [@CL-MKT-1386].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency wants a sole-source 8(a) award estimated at 40,000,000. The SBA may not accept it for negotiation until the agency has completed the justification [@CL-MKT-1272]. After that, the SBA does not start negotiations within the agreed time and the agency cannot allow more, so the agency notifies the SBA and may proceed with other sources [@CL-MKT-1274]. In a separate acquisition, a participant's program term has expired, so the SBA will not accept a new order for it under a blanket purchase agreement [@CL-MKT-1281]. For a third award, the SBA inserts its contract number and the effective date [@CL-MKT-1379], and the contracting officer may use a single contract document because the SBA is not making advance payments [@CL-MKT-1382].

## Never confuse

A sole-source 8(a) award is made through the SBA: the agency contract is awarded to the SBA, and the SBA awards the contract to the participant, unless the single-document alternative combines them in one tripartite document [@CL-MKT-1375; @CL-MKT-1376; @CL-MKT-1382]. The SBA's approval before award applies whether or not the contracting officer negotiates directly [@CL-MKT-1276]. An order under an agreement is not covered by the acceptance of the agreement itself; each order needs its own offering letter and acceptance [@CL-MKT-1374]. The offering and acceptance steps are on [the offering and acceptance card](mkt-8a-offering-and-acceptance.md), the competition notice is on [the award and competition notification card](mkt-section-8a-award-and-competition-notification.md), and the special conditions are on [the special conditions card](mkt-special-8a-contract-and-subcontract-conditions.md).
