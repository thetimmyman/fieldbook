## Purpose

Shows what the program requires of a participant on its own performance, and who may appeal to the agency head when the SBA and a contracting officer disagree.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0514] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.809-2 and FAR 19.810 [@CL-MKT-0514; @CL-MKT-0515] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0514] |
| Applies to | Participants awarded a contract or order under the 8(a) program, and disagreements between the SBA and a contracting officer [@CL-MKT-0514; @CL-MKT-0515] |

To be awarded a contract or order under the 8(a) program, the 8(a) participant is required to perform, for services except construction, at least 50 percent of the cost incurred for personnel with its own employees [@CL-MKT-0514]. For supplies or products, other than a procurement from a nonmanufacturer, the participant is required to perform at least 50 percent of the cost of manufacturing, not including the cost of materials [@CL-MKT-0514; @CL-MKT-0573]. For general construction the participant is required to perform at least 15 percent of the cost with its own employees, and for construction by special trade contractors at least 25 percent, not including the cost of materials in either case [@CL-MKT-0514; @CL-MKT-0574]. The general limits on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule are on the [Limitations on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule](mkt-limitations-on-subcontracting-and-nonmanufacturer-rule.md) card, and the SBA's own nonmanufacturer qualification is on the [How a nonmanufacturer qualifies as a small business on a supply contract](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-nonmanufacturer-qualification.md) card.

The SBA Administrator may submit the listed matters for determination to the agency head if the SBA and the contracting officer fail to agree on them [@CL-MKT-0515]. Two listed matters are the decision not to make a particular acquisition available under the 8(a) Program, and a contracting officer's decision to reject a specific participant after SBA's acceptance of the requirement [@CL-MKT-0516].

## How it works

Notification by SBA of an intent to appeal to the agency head must be received by the contracting officer within 5 working days after SBA is formally notified of the contracting officer's decision [@CL-MKT-0517]. SBA must send the written appeal to the agency head within 15 working days of SBA's notification of intent to appeal or the appeal may be considered withdrawn [@CL-MKT-0518]. Pending issuance of a decision by the agency head, the contracting officer shall suspend action on the acquisition [@CL-MKT-0571]. The contracting officer need not suspend action if the contracting officer makes a written determination that urgent and compelling circumstances that significantly affect the interests of the United States will not permit waiting for a decision [@CL-MKT-0572]. This card does not restate the other listed matters, who must receive the notice of intent to appeal, or what a decision on a denied appeal must contain.

The contracting officer should request a preaward survey of the 8(a) participant whenever considered useful [@CL-MKT-2103]. If the results of the preaward survey or other information available to the contracting officer raise substantial doubt as to the participant's ability to perform, the contracting officer shall refer the matter to SBA for Certificate of Competency consideration under FAR subpart 19.6 [@CL-MKT-2104].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The SBA accepts a requirement for the program. The contracting officer then rejects the participant the SBA proposed, and the SBA and the contracting officer cannot agree. The SBA tells the contracting officer of its intent to appeal on working day 3 after it is formally notified of the decision, which is in time. It sends the written appeal to the agency head on working day 12 after that notice of intent, which is also in time. If it had waited until working day 20, the appeal could be treated as withdrawn. The contracting officer suspends action on the acquisition until the agency head decides, unless a written determination of urgent and compelling circumstances is made.

## Never confuse

The contracting agency's notice of intent to appeal the issuance of a Certificate of Competency goes to SBA Headquarters [@CL-MKT-0489] and is on the [Issuing or denying a Certificate of Competency and resolving differences with SBA](mkt-coc-issuance-denial-and-resolution.md) card. The 8(a) performance requirements concern what the participant is required to perform [@CL-MKT-0514]; the SBA Administrator may submit listed matters to the agency head [@CL-MKT-0515], with notice due within 5 working days [@CL-MKT-0517] and the written appeal within 15 working days [@CL-MKT-0518].
