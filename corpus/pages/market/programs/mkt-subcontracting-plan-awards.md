## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer must do before making an award that requires a subcontracting plan, including notice to the SBA procurement center representative.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0449] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.705-5 [@CL-MKT-0449; @CL-MKT-0450] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0449] |
| Applies to | Awards that require a subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0449] |

In making an award that requires a subcontracting plan, the contracting officer shall consider the contractor's compliance with the subcontracting plans submitted on previous contracts as a factor in determining contractor responsibility [@CL-MKT-0449]. Two listed award responsibilities are to assure that a plan was submitted when required and to notify the SBA procurement center representative, or if none is assigned see the cross-referenced section, of the opportunity to review the proposed contract including the plan and supporting documentation [@CL-MKT-0450]. One listed award responsibility is to determine any fee that may be payable if an incentive is used in conjunction with the subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0561]. Another is to ensure that an acceptable plan is incorporated into and made a material part of the contract [@CL-MKT-0452].

The notice to the procurement center representative shall be issued in time to give the representative a reasonable time to review, and the representative's failure to respond in a reasonable period shall not delay contract award [@CL-MKT-0451].

Letter contracts and similar undefinitized instruments that would otherwise meet the cited plan requirements shall contain at least a preliminary basic plan addressing the plan content requirements [@CL-MKT-0559]. For such an instrument, the final plan must be negotiated within 90 days after award or before definitization, whichever occurs first [@CL-MKT-0560].

## How it works

Before award, the contracting officer checks the contractor's past compliance for responsibility [@CL-MKT-0449], confirms a plan was submitted and notifies the SBA procurement center representative of the opportunity to review the proposed contract and plan [@CL-MKT-0450]. The representative gets a reasonable time to review and to submit advisory recommendations, but a failure to respond in a reasonable period does not delay the award [@CL-MKT-0451]. The acceptable plan is incorporated into and made a material part of the contract [@CL-MKT-0452]. After award, a separate duty is to notify the SBA of the award by sending a copy of the award document to the Area Director, Office of Government Contracting, in the SBA area office where the contract will be performed [@CL-MKT-0454]. That postaward duty is on the [Postaward responsibilities of the contracting officer for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-postaward-co-duties.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A negotiated contract that needs a plan is ready for award. The contracting officer checks that the offeror followed its plans on earlier contracts, confirms a plan came in, and sends the proposed contract and plan to the SBA representative. After a reasonable time the representative has not answered. Award goes ahead, and the accepted plan is made part of the contract.

## Never confuse

Notice to the SBA before award goes to the procurement center representative for review [@CL-MKT-0450], while the postaward notice goes to the Area Director in the SBA area office where the contract will be performed [@CL-MKT-0454]. Past compliance feeds the responsibility decision [@CL-MKT-0449], which is not the same as the review of the plan itself, covered on the [Reviewing the subcontracting plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-review.md) card.
