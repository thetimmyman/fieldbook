## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer must do before making an award that requires a subcontracting plan, including notice to the SBA procurement center representative.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0449] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.705-5 [@CL-MKT-0449; @CL-MKT-0450] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0449] |
| Applies to | Awards that require a subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0449] |

In making an award that requires a subcontracting plan, the contracting officer shall consider the contractor's compliance with the subcontracting plans submitted on previous contracts as a factor in determining contractor responsibility [@CL-MKT-0449]. Two listed award responsibilities are to assure that a subcontracting plan was submitted when required and to notify the SBA procurement center representative (or, if a procurement center representative is not assigned, see FAR 19.402(a)) of the opportunity to review the proposed contract, including the plan and supporting documentation [@CL-MKT-0450]. One listed award responsibility is to determine any fee that may be payable if an incentive is used in conjunction with the subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0561]. Another is to ensure that an acceptable plan is incorporated into and made a material part of the contract [@CL-MKT-0452].

The notice to the procurement center representative shall be issued in sufficient time to provide the representative a reasonable time to review the material and submit advisory recommendations to the contracting officer, and failure of the representative to respond in a reasonable period of time shall not delay contract award [@CL-MKT-0451].

Letter contracts and similar undefinitized instruments that would otherwise meet the requirements of FAR 19.702(a)(1)(i) and (ii) shall contain at least a preliminary basic plan addressing the requirements of FAR 19.704, and in such cases require the negotiation of the final plan within 90 days after award or before definitization, whichever occurs first [@CL-MKT-0559; @CL-MKT-0560].

## How it works

Before award, the contracting officer considers the contractor's compliance on previous contracts in determining responsibility [@CL-MKT-0449], assures that a plan was submitted when required, and notifies the SBA procurement center representative (or see FAR 19.402(a) if none is assigned) of the opportunity to review the proposed contract, including the plan and supporting documentation [@CL-MKT-0450]. The notice is issued in sufficient time to provide the representative a reasonable time to review the material and submit advisory recommendations to the contracting officer, and failure of the representative to respond in a reasonable period of time does not delay contract award [@CL-MKT-0451]. The contracting officer also determines any fee payable if an incentive is used in conjunction with the plan [@CL-MKT-0561] and ensures that an acceptable plan is incorporated into and made a material part of the contract [@CL-MKT-0452]. After a contract or modification containing a subcontracting plan is awarded, or an existing plan is amended, one of the contracting officer's duties is to notify the SBA of the award by sending a copy of the award document to the Area Director, Office of Government Contracting, in the SBA area office where the contract will be performed [@CL-MKT-0453; @CL-MKT-0454], as covered on the [Postaward responsibilities of the contracting officer for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-postaward-co-duties.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A negotiated contract that needs a plan is ready for award. The contracting officer checks that the offeror followed its plans on earlier contracts, confirms a plan came in, and sends the proposed contract and plan to the SBA representative. After a reasonable time the representative has not answered. Award goes ahead, and the accepted plan is made part of the contract.

## Never confuse

At award, the contracting officer evaluates prior subcontracting compliance as part of responsibility and notifies the SBA procurement center representative [@CL-MKT-0449; @CL-MKT-0450], unlike the postaward duties on [Postaward responsibilities of the contracting officer for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-postaward-co-duties.md) that take effect after the contract or modification is awarded [@CL-MKT-0453].
