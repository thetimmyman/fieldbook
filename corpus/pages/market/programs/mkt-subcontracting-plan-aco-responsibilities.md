## Purpose

Lists what the administrative contracting officer and the contract administration office do to support the contracting officer on subcontracting plans.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0459] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.706 [@CL-MKT-0459; @CL-MKT-0460] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0459] |
| Applies to | Contracts that contain the clause prescribed in FAR 19.708(b) or a subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0459] |

The administrative contracting officer is responsible for assisting in evaluating subcontracting plans and for monitoring, evaluating and documenting contractor performance under the clause prescribed in FAR 19.708(b) and any subcontracting plan included in the contract [@CL-MKT-0459]. The contract administration office shall provide the necessary information and advice to support the contracting officer, as appropriate, by furnishing documentation on the contractor's performance and compliance with subcontracting plans under previous contracts [@CL-MKT-0460].

It also furnishes information on the extent to which the contractor is meeting the plan's goals for subcontracting with eligible small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, and women-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0460; @CL-MKT-1984; @CL-MKT-1985]. It also furnishes information on whether the contractor's efforts to ensure the participation of small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, and women-owned small business concerns are in accordance with its subcontracting plan, and on whether the contractor is requiring its subcontractors to adopt similar subcontracting plans [@CL-MKT-0460; @CL-MKT-1985; @CL-MKT-1986]. Further listed items furnished are immediate notice if, during performance, the contractor is failing to meet its commitments under the clause prescribed in FAR 19.708(b) or the subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0461]; immediate notice and rationale if, during performance, the contractor is failing to comply in good faith with the subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0462]; and immediate notice that performance under a contract is complete, that the goals were or were not met, and, if not met, whether there is any indication of a lack of a good faith effort to comply with the subcontracting plan [@CL-MKT-0463].

## How it works

Before award, the contract administration office furnishes documentation on past performance and compliance with subcontracting plans under previous contracts, which the contracting officer uses when determining the acceptability of a proposed plan as shown on the [Reviewing the subcontracting plan](mkt-subcontracting-plan-review.md) card [@CL-MKT-0460; @CL-MKT-0447]. During performance and at completion, the contract administration office furnishes information on goal progress, plan efforts and subcontractor plans [@CL-MKT-0460; @CL-MKT-1984; @CL-MKT-1985; @CL-MKT-1986] and gives immediate notices on missed commitments, lack of good faith compliance and contract completion [@CL-MKT-0461; @CL-MKT-0462; @CL-MKT-0463]. After a contract or modification containing a subcontracting plan is awarded or an existing plan is amended, the contracting officer shall initiate action to assess liquidated damages in accordance with FAR 19.705-7 upon a recommendation by the administrative contracting officer, if one is assigned, or receipt of other reliable evidence to indicate that assessing liquidated damages is warranted [@CL-MKT-0453; @CL-MKT-0458].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor is behind on its small business goals part way through a contract. The contract administration office sees that the contractor is failing to meet its commitments and gives the contracting officer immediate notice. At completion it sends a further notice that says whether the goals were met.

## Never confuse

The administrative contracting officer assists in evaluating plans and monitors, evaluates and documents contractor performance [@CL-MKT-0459], while the contracting officer initiates action to assess liquidated damages upon a recommendation by the administrative contracting officer or receipt of other reliable evidence [@CL-MKT-0458]. The contract administration office furnishes both documentation on performance and compliance under previous contracts and immediate notices during performance and at completion [@CL-MKT-0460; @CL-MKT-0461; @CL-MKT-0462; @CL-MKT-0463]. The contracting officer's postaward duties are on the [Postaward responsibilities of the contracting officer for subcontracting plans](mkt-subcontracting-plan-postaward-co-duties.md) card.
