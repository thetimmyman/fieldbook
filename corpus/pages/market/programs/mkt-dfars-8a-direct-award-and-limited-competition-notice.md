## Purpose

Shows the parties to a DoD direct award with a participant in the program, who is responsible for what, and the limits the competition notice puts on offerors that supply end items.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0922] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.219-7009 and DFARS 252.219-7010 [@CL-MKT-0922; @CL-MKT-0927] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0922] |
| Applies to | Contracts awarded directly under the SBA and DoD partnership agreement where the direct award clause is prescribed, and solicitations limited to eligible 8(a) participants where the competition notice is prescribed [@CL-MKT-0922; @CL-MKT-0927; @CL-MKT-0949; @CL-MKT-0950] |

The direct award clause is prescribed in DFARS 219.811-3(1) [@CL-MKT-0949]. It states that the contract is issued as a direct award between the contracting office and the 8(a) contractor under the Partnership Agreement between the Small Business Administration and the Department of Defense [@CL-MKT-0922]. It states that the SBA, even if not identified in Section A of the contract, is the prime contractor and retains responsibility for 8(a) certification, for 8(a) eligibility determinations and related issues, and for counseling and assistance to the 8(a) contractor [@CL-MKT-0923]. The contracting office is responsible for administering the contract and taking action for the Government under its terms, provided that it gives the SBA advance notice before it issues a final notice terminating performance [@CL-MKT-0924]. The contracting office shall also coordinate with the SBA prior to processing any novation agreement, and may assign contract administration functions to a contract administration office [@CL-MKT-0967].

The competition notice is prescribed in DFARS 219.811-3(2) [@CL-MKT-0950]. Under the notice, offers are solicited only from small business concerns expressly certified by the SBA for the 8(a) program that meet the listed criteria at the time of submission of offer [@CL-MKT-0927]. Among those criteria are that the offeror is in conformance with the 8(a) support limitation set forth in its approved business plan, and with the Business Activity Targets set forth in that plan or any remedial action directed by the SBA [@CL-MKT-0966; @CL-MKT-0927]. By submission of its offer, the offeror represents that it meets all of those criteria [@CL-MKT-0928; @CL-MKT-0927].

## How it works

The 8(a) contractor agrees to notify the contracting officer, at the same time as it notifies the SBA, when the owners upon whom 8(a) eligibility is based plan to relinquish ownership or control of the concern [@CL-MKT-0925]. Transfer of ownership or control shall result in termination of the contract for convenience, unless the SBA waives the requirement for termination before the actual relinquishing of ownership and control [@CL-MKT-0926]. In the competition notice, the party inserted as the SBA's contractor will notify the contracting officer of the named contracting agency in writing immediately upon entering an agreement, oral or written, to transfer all or part of its stock or other ownership interest to any other party [@CL-MKT-0963]. Under the competition notice, a small business concern that provides an end item it did not manufacture, process, or produce shall meet the requirements listed in paragraph (d), unless the SBA has waived the listed requirements [@CL-MKT-0929]. When the end item is a kit of supplies, at least 50 percent of the total cost of the components shall be manufactured, processed, or produced by small businesses in the United States or its outlying areas [@CL-MKT-0930], subject to that waiver and to the next exemption [@CL-MKT-0929; @CL-MKT-0931]. The end-item and kit requirements do not apply to construction or service contracts [@CL-MKT-0931]. The other end-item requirements in the first subparagraph of paragraph (d) are not drafted on this card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD office issues a direct award to a program participant. The firm's owners plan to sell a majority stake to a buyer, and the firm has not yet told anyone.

Answer: the firm tells the contracting officer when it tells the SBA. A transfer of ownership or control ends in termination for convenience unless the SBA waives the termination before the transfer is complete.

## Never confuse

The direct award clause governs a contract already awarded to a participant, while the competition notice governs who may offer in a solicitation limited to eligible participants. In the direct award the SBA is the prime contractor [@CL-MKT-0923], yet the contracting office administers the contract and acts for the Government [@CL-MKT-0924]. The delay clause for a purchase order under a program award is a separate clause, on the [DFARS notification to delay performance and religious-related services competition](mkt-dfars-delay-performance-and-religious-services-competition.md) card. Offering a requirement to the SBA and acceptance are on the [offering and acceptance](mkt-8a-offering-and-acceptance.md) card, competition thresholds are on the [competitive awards](mkt-8a-competitive-awards.md) card, and administration and release are on the [administration and release](mkt-8a-administration-and-release.md) card. The general limits on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule are on the [limitations on subcontracting](mkt-limitations-on-subcontracting-and-nonmanufacturer-rule.md) card.
