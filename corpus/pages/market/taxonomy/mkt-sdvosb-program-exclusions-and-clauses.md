## Purpose

Shows when the service-disabled veteran-owned small business subpart does not apply, and which notice clause the contracting officer must insert under the contract clauses section.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1958] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.1404 and 19.1408 [@CL-MKT-1982; @CL-MKT-1962] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1958] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for acquisitions set aside or awarded on a sole-source basis to service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1963] |

Section 19.1404 lists as alternatives what this subpart does not apply to [@CL-MKT-1982; @CL-MKT-1608]. Paragraph (a) covers requirements that can be satisfied through award to Federal Prison Industries, Inc. (see Subpart 8.6) or AbilityOne participating non-profit agencies for the blind or severely disabled (see Subpart 8.7) [@CL-MKT-1958]. Paragraph (b) covers orders under indefinite-delivery contracts, and paragraph (c) covers orders against Federal Supply Schedules [@CL-MKT-1959; @CL-MKT-1960]. Each of those two paragraphs adds a "But see" cross-reference to discretionary set-asides of orders, at 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F) for indefinite-delivery orders and at 8.405-5 for schedule orders [@CL-MKT-1959; @CL-MKT-1960; @CL-MKT-1608]. Paragraph (c) is joined by "or" to paragraph (d), which covers requirements currently being performed by an 8(a) participant or requirements SBA has accepted for performance under the authority of the 8(a) program, unless SBA has consented to release them from the 8(a) program [@CL-MKT-1608; @CL-MKT-1961]. SBA is the party whose consent ends the exclusion in paragraph (d) [@CL-MKT-1961].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-27, Notice of Set-Aside for, or Sole-Source Award to, Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) Concerns Eligible Under the SDVOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1962]. The insertion rule covers solicitations and contracts for acquisitions that are set aside, or awarded on a sole-source basis, to service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns under 19.1405 and 19.1406 [@CL-MKT-1963]. The paragraph says this includes multiple-award contracts when orders may be set aside for those concerns as described in 8.405-5 and 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F), or when orders may be issued directly to one service-disabled veteran-owned small business contractor in accordance with 19.504(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MKT-1964; @CL-MKT-1965]. Paragraphs (b) and (c) refer to the prescription at 19.507(e) for clause 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting, and to the prescription at 19.507(h) for clause 52.219-33, Nonmanufacturer Rule [@CL-MKT-1966].

## How it works

The subpart does not apply to the requirements listed as alternatives in the four paragraphs of 19.1404 [@CL-MKT-1982; @CL-MKT-1608]. Two of them carry a cross-reference to discretionary set-asides of orders. Orders under indefinite-delivery contracts and orders against Federal Supply Schedules are outside the subpart, but the cross-references point to discretionary set-asides of orders under 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F) and 8.405-5 [@CL-MKT-1959; @CL-MKT-1960]. The 8(a) paragraph carries a condition: the exclusion applies unless SBA has consented to release the requirements from the 8(a) program [@CL-MKT-1961]. The clause rule turns on how the acquisition is awarded, and the duty falls on the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1962]. A set-aside or sole-source award to service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns under 19.1405 and 19.1406 calls for 52.219-27 [@CL-MKT-1962; @CL-MKT-1963]. For multiple-award contracts where orders may be set aside for those concerns or issued directly to one, the rule includes the contract [@CL-MKT-1964; @CL-MKT-1965]. The [Service-disabled veteran-owned small business status, set-asides and sole-source awards](../programs/mkt-sdvosb-status-set-asides-and-sole-source.md) card covers how the set-aside or sole-source decision is made, and the [Service-disabled veteran-owned set-aside and sole-source notice](../programs/mkt-sdvosb-set-aside-and-sole-source-notice.md) card covers the clause. The [Set-asides of orders under multiple-award contracts and the set-aside clauses](../programs/mkt-set-aside-orders-and-clauses.md) card covers the clauses prescribed at 19.507 [@CL-MKT-1966], and the [8(a) contract administration and release of requirements](../programs/mkt-8a-administration-and-release.md) card covers release of 8(a) requirements.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer plans an order against a Federal Supply Schedule for work that a service-disabled veteran-owned small business concern could perform. Answer: paragraph (c) applies, so this subpart does not apply to the order, although the cross-reference to 8.405-5 points to discretionary set-asides of orders [@CL-MKT-1960]. For a separate acquisition set aside for service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns under 19.1405, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-27 [@CL-MKT-1962; @CL-MKT-1963].

## Never confuse

The exclusion of orders under indefinite-delivery contracts and Federal Supply Schedules does not by itself say that those orders cannot be set aside, because each exclusion points to discretionary set-asides of orders [@CL-MKT-1959; @CL-MKT-1960]. The 8(a) exclusion is conditional, because it does not apply once SBA consents to release the requirements [@CL-MKT-1961]. Paragraphs (b) and (c) of section 19.1408 refer to 19.507(e) and 19.507(h) for the Limitations on Subcontracting and Nonmanufacturer Rule clauses [@CL-MKT-1966].

## Company calibration

Whether the purchasing system review status and any customer consent needed for a subcontract are settled before the company relies on a service-disabled veteran-owned set-aside award. [@Q-040]
