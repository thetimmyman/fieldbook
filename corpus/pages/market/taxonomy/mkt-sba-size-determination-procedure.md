## Purpose

Shows, under 13 CFR 121.1008 and 121.1009 [@CL-MKT-0412; @CL-MKT-0416], what happens after SBA receives a size protest, what the protested concern must submit and how SBA decides.

When SBA receives a size protest, the SBA Area Director for Government Contracting, or designee, will notify the contracting officer, the protested concern and the protestor that the protest has been received [@CL-MKT-0412]. The protested concern or concern whose size is at issue must return the completed SBA Form 355 and all other requested information to SBA within 3 working days from the date of receipt of the blank form from SBA, and SBA has discretion to grant an extension of time to file the form [@CL-MKT-0414].

If no protest is pending under FAR subpart 33.1, the SBA Area Office will issue a formal size determination within 15 business days after receipt of a protest or a request for a formal size determination, if possible [@CL-MKT-0416].

## How it works

The size determination will be based primarily on the information supplied by the protestor or the requester and the information provided by the concern whose size status is at issue [@CL-MKT-0413]. The determination may also be based on grounds not raised in the protest or request for size determination [@CL-MKT-9402]. The concern whose size is under consideration has the burden of establishing its small business size [@CL-MKT-0417]. If a concern whose size status is at issue fails to submit a completed SBA Form 355, responses to the allegations of the protest, or other requested information within the time allowed by SBA [@CL-MKT-9401], or if it submits incomplete information, SBA may presume that disclosure of the information required by the form or other missing information would demonstrate that the concern is other than a small business [@CL-MKT-0415]. SBA will give greater weight to specific, signed, factual evidence than to general, unsupported allegations or opinions [@CL-MKT-0538]. A contracting officer shall not award a contract to a protested concern that the Area Office has determined is not an eligible small business for the procurement in question [@CL-MKT-0539]. Once SBA has determined that a concern is other than small for purposes of a particular procurement, the concern cannot later become eligible for the procurement by reducing its size [@CL-MKT-0537]. The section also covers a pending bid protest, notice of the decision, appeals and reopening of a determination, which this card does not detail.

Formal size determinations and NAICS code designations made by authorized SBA officials are binding upon the parties [@CL-MKT-2357]. Opinions that SBA officials otherwise provide to contracting officers or others are advisory in nature, and are not binding or appealable [@CL-MKT-2358]. The responsible Government Contracting Area Director or designee makes all formal size determinations in response to either a size protest or a request for a formal size determination, except that size determinations for purposes of the Disaster Loan Program will be made by the Disaster Area Office Director or designee responsible for the area in which the disaster occurred [@CL-MKT-2388; @CL-MKT-2389].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A protested concern receives a blank form from SBA on a Monday, and no bid protest is pending. The concern returns the completed form with its answers on the Wednesday of the same week, which is within three working days of receipt. The form is on time, and SBA aims to issue its determination within 15 business days of receiving the protest, if possible.

## Never confuse

A size determination is SBA's decision on a protest. It is not the protest itself, and it is not the contracting officer's choice of NAICS code. The protest and its filing deadlines are in [Small business size protests](../taxonomy/mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) and [Who may file a size protest and the time limits](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-protest-parties-and-deadlines.md). The date on which size is measured for the determination is in [When a concern's size status is measured](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-measurement-date.md), and the penalties for misrepresenting size are in [Size misrepresentation penalties and subcontract size](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-misrepresentation-penalties-and-subcontract-size.md).
