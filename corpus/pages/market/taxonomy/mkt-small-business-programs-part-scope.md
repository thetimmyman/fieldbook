## Purpose

Shows what the small business programs part covers, where contracting officers shall or may apply it, and what an offeror in a procurement under it must meet, under FAR 19.000 [@CL-MKT-1415; @CL-MKT-1420; @CL-MKT-1421].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1415] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.000(a) and (b) [@CL-MKT-1415; @CL-MKT-1420; @CL-MKT-1421] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1415] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers applying the part, and offerors in procurements under it [@CL-MKT-1420; @CL-MKT-1421] |

Paragraph (a) says the part implements the acquisition-related sections of the Small Business Act at title 15 of the United States Code, section 631 and following, applicable sections of the Armed Services Procurement Act at title 10 of the United States Code, sections 3063-3064 and 3203, title 41 of the United States Code, section 3104, and Executive Order 12138 of May 18, 1979 [@CL-MKT-1415].

The part covers these matters:

- The determination that a concern is eligible for participation in the programs the part identifies, and the respective roles of executive agencies and the Small Business Administration (SBA) in implementing the programs [@CL-MKT-1416].
- Setting acquisitions aside, in total or in part, for exclusive competitive participation by small business, 8(a) participants, HUBZone small business concerns, and service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) concerns eligible under the SDVOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1438]. The same matter extends to economically disadvantaged women-owned small business (EDWOSB) concerns and women-owned small business (WOSB) concerns eligible under the WOSB Program [@CL-MKT-1439].
- The certificate of competency program and the subcontracting assistance program [@CL-MKT-1440].
- The 8(a) business development program, under which agencies contract with SBA for goods or services to be furnished under a subcontract by a small disadvantaged business concern [@CL-MKT-1417].
- The use of a price evaluation preference for HUBZone small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1418].
- The use of veteran-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1441].
- Sole source awards to HUBZone small business concerns, service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns, and EDWOSB and WOSB concerns eligible under the WOSB Program, and the use of reserves [@CL-MKT-1419].

Unless otherwise specified in the part, with a cross-reference to subparts 19.6 and 19.7, contracting officers shall apply the part in the United States and its outlying areas, and may apply it outside them [@CL-MKT-1420]. Offerors that participate in any procurement under the part are required to meet the definition of small business concern at 2.101 and the definition of concern at 19.001 [@CL-MKT-1421].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) is a statement of scope that names the programs and the laws behind them, including the determination of a concern's eligibility as a covered matter [@CL-MKT-1415; @CL-MKT-1416].

Paragraph (b) puts one duty on each side. The contracting officer has a duty to apply the part inside the United States and its outlying areas, and a discretion to apply it outside them; both are subject to any different rule stated in the part [@CL-MKT-1420]. Offerors that participate in any procurement under the part are required to meet the definition of small business concern at 2.101 and the definition of concern at 19.001 [@CL-MKT-1421].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer buys laboratory services for a site outside the United States and its outlying areas, and the part specifies no different rule for this case. Applying the part there is a choice, not a duty [@CL-MKT-1420]. If the officer applies it, a firm that offers in that procurement must meet the definition of small business concern and the definition of concern [@CL-MKT-1421]. Had the same purchase been made inside the United States or its outlying areas, the officer would have had to apply the part, unless the part specified otherwise [@CL-MKT-1420].

## Never confuse

The scope of the part is not a set-aside test. The scope section names the programs. Whether a particular acquisition must be set aside is decided under the set-aside requirement, which is on the [When an acquisition must be set aside for small business](mkt-small-business-set-aside-requirement.md) card, and the wider set-aside rules are on the [Small business set-asides](../programs/mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card. The duty to apply the part inside the United States and its outlying areas is also not a statement that every program in it reaches every acquisition, because the unless clause leaves room for a different rule in the part [@CL-MKT-1420]. Nor is the general policy of the part the same as its scope; the policy is on the [Small business programs: the general policy](../programs/mkt-small-business-general-policy.md) card.
