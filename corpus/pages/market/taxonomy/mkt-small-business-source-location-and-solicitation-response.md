## Purpose

Shows how the contracting officer finds small business sources, to the extent practicable, and what the officer shall do to encourage their response to solicitations, under FAR 19.202-2 and 19.202-4 [@CL-MKT-1422; @CL-MKT-1426].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1422] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.202-2 and FAR 19.202-4 [@CL-MKT-1422; @CL-MKT-1426] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1422] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers, in locating small business sources and in the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1422; @CL-MKT-1426] |

For locating sources, FAR 19.202-2 says the contracting officer shall, to the extent practicable, encourage maximum participation by small business, veteran-owned small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business, HUBZone small business, small disadvantaged business, and women-owned small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1422]. The same sentence says the officer does so in acquisitions by taking the following actions [@CL-MKT-1423].

- Before issuing solicitations, one action is to make every reasonable effort to find additional small business concerns; the text cross-refers to 10.002(b)(2) [@CL-MKT-1424].
- This effort should include contacting the agency small business specialist and SBA PCR or, if a PCR is not assigned, the text says to see 19.402(a) [@CL-MKT-1442].
- Another action is to publicize solicitations and contract awards through the Governmentwide point of entry; the text cross-refers to subparts 5.2 and 5.3 [@CL-MKT-1425].

For the solicitation, FAR 19.202-4 says the contracting officer shall encourage maximum response to solicitations by the same six concern types by taking the following actions [@CL-MKT-1422; @CL-MKT-1426].

- Allow the maximum amount of time practicable for the submission of offers [@CL-MKT-1427].
- Furnish specifications, plans and drawings with solicitations, or furnish information as to where they may be obtained or examined [@CL-MKT-1428].
- Provide to any small business concern, upon its request, a copy of solicitations with respect to any contract to be let, and the name and telephone number of an agency contact to answer questions related to that prospective contract [@CL-MKT-1429].
- Provide, on the same request, adequate citations to each major Federal law or agency rule with which the concern must comply in performing the contract, other than laws or agency rules with which a small business must comply when doing business with other than the Government [@CL-MKT-1430].

## How it works

Both sections put the duty on the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1422; @CL-MKT-1426]. In the locating section, the lead sentence is qualified by "to the extent practicable" [@CL-MKT-1422]. The contact with the specialist and the PCR is stated as what the effort should include, not as a separate shall [@CL-MKT-1442]. The solicitation section's lead sentence has no practicable qualifier, but its time step asks for the maximum amount of time practicable [@CL-MKT-1426; @CL-MKT-1427].

The steps on copies, contact details and legal citations are triggered by a request from any small business concern [@CL-MKT-1429; @CL-MKT-1430].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An officer is about to issue a solicitation for grounds maintenance. Before issuing it, the officer makes every reasonable effort to find additional small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1424] and contacts the agency small business specialist and the SBA PCR, who is assigned in this case [@CL-MKT-1442]. The officer publicizes the solicitation through the Governmentwide point of entry [@CL-MKT-1425]. In this case the maximum amount of time practicable for offers is three weeks, and the officer allows it [@CL-MKT-1427]. The officer furnishes the specifications and drawings with the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1428]. A small firm then asks for a copy, an agency contact and the citations to the laws it must follow in performing the contract. The officer provides all three, because the firm is a small business concern and has asked [@CL-MKT-1429; @CL-MKT-1430].

## Never confuse

Publicizing a solicitation through the Governmentwide point of entry is not the same step as finding sources before the solicitation issues. The first is the publication action and the second is the effort to find additional small business concerns [@CL-MKT-1424; @CL-MKT-1425]. Encouraging participation in acquisitions is also not the same wording as encouraging response to solicitations, which is how the solicitation section puts the duty [@CL-MKT-1422; @CL-MKT-1426]. The specialist who is contacted while finding sources is covered on the [Small business specialist recommendations and documentation](../programs/mkt-small-business-specialist-recommendations-and-documentation.md) card, and the representative role is on the [SBA procurement center representatives and the small business solicitation provisions](../programs/mkt-sba-pcr-and-small-business-solicitation-provisions.md) card. Publicizing is covered on the [Publicizing and response time](../feeds/mkt-publicizing-and-response-time.md) card.
