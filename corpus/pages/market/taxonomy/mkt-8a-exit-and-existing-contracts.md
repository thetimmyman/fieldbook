## Purpose

Shows what happens to an 8(a) contractor's eligibility for new 8(a) contracts when it exits or is suspended from the program, what it must still finish, and the exception for a contractor that has completed its term [@CL-MKT-1675; @CL-MKT-1676; @CL-MKT-1677; @CL-MKT-1678].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1675] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.816 [@CL-MKT-1675] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1675] |
| Applies to | Contractors that exit or are suspended from the 8(a) program, contractors that have completed their term of participation, and their existing contracts [@CL-MKT-1675; @CL-MKT-1677; @CL-MKT-1678] |

Exit (paragraph (a)). Except as provided in paragraph (c) of the section, when a contractor exits the 8(a) program, it is no longer eligible to receive new 8(a) contracts [@CL-MKT-1675]. The contractor remains under contractual obligation to complete existing contracts, and any priced options that may be exercised [@CL-MKT-1676]. The duty falls on the contractor [@CL-MKT-1676]. Paragraph (d) places SBA's regulations on exiting the program at 13 CFR 124.301 through 124.305 and 13 CFR 124.507(d) [@CL-MKT-1680].

Suspension (paragraph (b)). If an 8(a) contractor is suspended from the program, it may not receive any new 8(a) contracts unless the head of the contracting agency makes a determination that it is in the best interest of the Government to issue the award and SBA adopts that determination [@CL-MKT-1677]. Both steps are needed: the agency head determines, and SBA adopts [@CL-MKT-1677].

Completed term (paragraph (c)). A contractor that has completed its term of participation in the 8(a) program may be awarded a competitive 8(a) contract if it was an 8(a) participant eligible for award of the contract on the initial date specified for receipt of offers contained in the solicitation [@CL-MKT-1678]. The award also requires that the contractor continue to meet all other applicable eligibility criteria, for which the section cites SBA's regulations at 13 CFR 124.507(d) [@CL-MKT-1679].

SBA's regulations (paragraph (d)). SBA's regulations on exiting the 8(a) program are found at 13 CFR 124.301 through 124.305, and 13 CFR 124.507(d) [@CL-MKT-1680].

Orders. A different section lets an 8(a) contractor continue to accept new orders under a multiple-award contract even if it exits the program or becomes other than small for the NAICS codes assigned to the contract, as the [Agency evaluation of a requirement for the 8(a) program and orders under 8(a) multiple-award contracts](mkt-8a-agency-evaluation-and-indefinite-delivery-orders.md) card explains [@CL-MKT-1660].

## How it works

Exit ends eligibility for new 8(a) contracts, and the contractor still has to finish existing contracts and any priced options that are exercised [@CL-MKT-1675; @CL-MKT-1676]. Paragraph (a) names the completed-term exception in paragraph (c) [@CL-MKT-1675]. It lets a contractor that finished its term take a competitive 8(a) contract if it was eligible on the initial date for receipt of offers in the solicitation and still meets the other applicable eligibility criteria [@CL-MKT-1678; @CL-MKT-1679].

Suspension is a separate rule. It blocks new 8(a) contracts unless the head of the contracting agency determines that the award is in the best interest of the Government and SBA adopts that determination [@CL-MKT-1677]. The section sends the reader to SBA's regulations for the exit rules [@CL-MKT-1680].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor exits the 8(a) program in the second year of a contract that has a 2 year base period and two 1 year priced options. Assume it has not completed its term of participation, so the exception in paragraph (c) does not apply, and it is no longer eligible to receive new 8(a) contracts [@CL-MKT-1675; @CL-MKT-1678]. It remains under contractual obligation to complete the contract and any priced option that is exercised [@CL-MKT-1676].

A contractor that has completed its term of participation bids on a competitive 8(a) solicitation. It was an 8(a) participant eligible for award on the initial date specified for receipt of offers, and it still meets the other applicable eligibility criteria. It may be awarded that contract [@CL-MKT-1678; @CL-MKT-1679].

A third contractor is suspended from the program. It may not receive a new 8(a) contract unless the head of the contracting agency determines that the award is in the best interest of the Government and SBA adopts that determination [@CL-MKT-1677].

## Never confuse

Exiting the program is not the same as being suspended from it. Exit ends eligibility for new 8(a) contracts, subject to the completed-term exception in paragraph (c) [@CL-MKT-1675; @CL-MKT-1678]. Suspension blocks new 8(a) contracts unless the agency head makes a best-interest determination and SBA adopts it [@CL-MKT-1677]. Finishing existing contracts and priced options is a contractual obligation, not a new 8(a) award, and contract administration is on the [8(a) contract administration and release of requirements](../programs/mkt-8a-administration-and-release.md) card [@CL-MKT-1676]. Accepting new orders under a multiple-award contract the contractor already holds is a separate rule in another section [@CL-MKT-1660].

## Company calibration

Whether the company's authorized acquisition mandate and decision criteria still cover the work it must complete under existing contracts and priced options after its program term ends. [@Q-028]
