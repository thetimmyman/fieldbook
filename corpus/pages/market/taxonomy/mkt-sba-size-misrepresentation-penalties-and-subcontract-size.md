## Purpose

Shows, under 13 CFR 121.108 and 121.411 [@CL-MKT-0418; @CL-MKT-0424], the penalties for misrepresenting size status and how a subcontractor's size is determined under the Section 8(d) subcontracting program.

| Applies to | Contracts, subcontracts and other awards set aside, reserved or classified as intended for small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0418] |

The presumption of loss to the United States covers every contract, subcontract, cooperative agreement, cooperative research and development agreement or grant that is set aside, reserved or otherwise classified as intended for award to small business concerns [@CL-MKT-0418]. Whenever it is established that a business concern other than a small business concern willfully sought and received the award by misrepresentation, the presumption of loss is based on the total amount expended [@CL-MKT-0419]. Listed actions shall be deemed affirmative, willful and intentional certifications of small business size and status [@CL-MKT-0541]. Each offer, proposal, bid or application for a Federal contract, subcontract or grant shall contain a certification concerning the small business size and status of the business concern seeking it [@CL-MKT-0542].

The presumption, deemed certification and signature paragraphs may be determined not to apply in the case of unintentional errors, technical malfunctions and similar situations that demonstrate that a misrepresentation of size was not affirmative, intentional, willful or actionable under the False Claims Act [@CL-MKT-0423; @CL-MKT-0418; @CL-MKT-0541; @CL-MKT-0542]. That limitation names only those three paragraphs. It does not name the suspension, debarment, civil penalty or criminal penalty paragraphs [@CL-MKT-0423].

The SBA or agency suspension and debarment official may suspend or debar a person or concern for misrepresenting a firm's size status [@CL-MKT-0420]. Persons or concerns are subject to severe penalties under the False Claims Act, the Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act and other applicable laws [@CL-MKT-0421]. They are also subject to severe criminal penalties for knowingly misrepresenting small business size status in connection with procurement programs [@CL-MKT-0422].

## How it works

For a section 8(d) subcontract, a concern must still qualify and self-certify as a small business at the time it submits its offer, even if it is on a small business source list [@CL-MKT-0424]. More generally, for subcontracting purposes a concern must qualify as small as of the date that it certifies that it is small for the subcontract [@CL-MKT-0387]. The self-certification of a concern subcontracting or proposing to subcontract under section 8(d) may be protested by the contracting officer, the prime contractor, the appropriate SBA official or any other interested party [@CL-MKT-0540]. The section on the subcontracting program also covers reliance on SAM, the form of self-certification and notice to unsuccessful offerors, which this card does not detail.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A concern with 900 employees offers as a small business on a set-aside contract and is awarded the work, and it is established that it willfully misrepresented its size. The Government has expended 1,500,000 dollars on the contract so far. The presumption of loss is based on the 1,500,000 dollars expended.

## Never confuse

The presumption of loss, which is based on the total amount expended, is a different thing from suspension, debarment, or civil and criminal penalties, which are listed separately [@CL-MKT-0419; @CL-MKT-0420; @CL-MKT-0421; @CL-MKT-0422]. The limit on the presumption and the deemed certification and signature paragraphs is not a limit on those listed penalties [@CL-MKT-0423]. The duty to represent size in good faith at initial offer is covered in [Offeror size and status representation](../taxonomy/mkt-offeror-size-and-status-representation.md). The date on which a subcontractor's size is measured is in [When a concern's size status is measured](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-measurement-date.md), and the parties who may protest a prime contract size are in [Who may file a size protest and the time limits](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-protest-parties-and-deadlines.md).
