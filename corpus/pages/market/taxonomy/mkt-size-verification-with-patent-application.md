## Purpose

Shows how a concern verifies its own size status with a patent application, who resolves questions about that verification, and what route an applicant has, under 13 CFR 121.805 [@CL-MKT-2052; @CL-MKT-2055].

## The rule

| Applies to | Concerns that verify their size when filing a patent application [@CL-MKT-2052] |

A concern verifies its size status with its submission of its patent application [@CL-MKT-2052]. Any attempt to establish small size status improperly, whether fraudulently, through gross negligence or otherwise, may result in remedial action by the Patent and Trademark Office [@CL-MKT-2053]. In the absence of credible information indicating otherwise, that office may accept the verification by the concern as a small business as true [@CL-MKT-2054]. Questions concerning the size verification are resolved initially by that office [@CL-MKT-2055]. If the applicant is not verified as small, it may request a formal SBA size determination [@CL-MKT-2055].

## How it works

The concern verifies its size when it submits the patent application [@CL-MKT-2052]. The Patent and Trademark Office may accept the verification as true in the absence of credible information indicating otherwise [@CL-MKT-2054]. The section does not say what that office does when it has such information. Questions about the verification are resolved initially by that office [@CL-MKT-2055]. An applicant that is not verified as small may request a formal SBA size determination [@CL-MKT-2055]. An improper attempt to establish small size status, whether fraudulent, grossly negligent or otherwise, may result in remedial action by that office [@CL-MKT-2053].

The size sections for reduced patent fees apply to size status for the purpose of paying reduced patent fees authorized by Pub. L. 97-247, 96 Stat. 317 [@CL-MKT-2380]. One condition for a concern to be eligible for reduced patent fees is that its number of employees, including affiliates, does not exceed 500 persons [@CL-MKT-2381]. The same sentence lists a second condition on rights in the invention, which this card does not state.

Size determinations by authorized SBA officials are formal actions, based upon a specific patent application pursuant to the rules of the Patent and Trademark Office, Department of Commerce, and are binding upon the parties [@CL-MKT-2382]. Other SBA opinions provided to patent applicants or others are only advisory, and are not binding or appealable [@CL-MKT-2383]. Size status is determined as of the date of the patent applicant's written verification of size [@CL-MKT-2384].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A concern files a patent application and verifies in it that it is a small business [@CL-MKT-2052]. The Patent and Trademark Office has no credible information to the contrary, so it may accept the verification as true [@CL-MKT-2054]. Had the concern not been verified as small, it could have requested a formal SBA size determination [@CL-MKT-2055]. Had it tried to establish small size status improperly, that office could have taken remedial action [@CL-MKT-2053].

## Never confuse

This section covers verification tied to a patent application [@CL-MKT-2052]. It is not the certification a concern makes in the Government's award management database, which is made in connection with specific size standards at least annually [@CL-MKT-2043]. Representation of size to a contracting officer is covered in [Offeror size and status representation](../taxonomy/mkt-offeror-size-and-status-representation.md).
