## Purpose

Shows that a size protest must pertain to a particular procurement, and who may appeal a NAICS code designation, to whom and by when, under 13 CFR 121.1007 and 121.1103 [@CL-MKT-0701; @CL-MKT-0702].

| Applies to | Size protests about a particular procurement or sale, and appeals of a contracting officer's NAICS code or size standard designation [@CL-MKT-0701; @CL-MKT-0704] |

A protest challenging the size of a concern that does not pertain to a particular procurement or sale will not be acted on by SBA [@CL-MKT-0701]. A size protest must include specific facts, be sufficiently specific to give reasonable notice of the grounds on which the protested concern's size is questioned, and give some basis for the belief or allegation stated [@CL-MKT-0726]. Protests that do not contain sufficient specificity will be dismissed by SBA [@CL-MKT-0727].

Any interested party adversely affected by a NAICS code designation may appeal the designation to the Office of Hearings and Appeals [@CL-MKT-0702], with one exception: for a sole source contract reserved under SBA's 8(a) Business Development program, only SBA's Associate Administrator for Business Development may appeal the NAICS code designation [@CL-MKT-0703]. An appeal from a contracting officer's NAICS code or size standard designation must be served and filed within 10 calendar days after the issuance of the solicitation or amendment affecting the NAICS code or size standard, but SBA may file a NAICS code appeal at any time before offers are due [@CL-MKT-0704]. The Office of Hearings and Appeals will summarily dismiss an untimely NAICS code appeal [@CL-MKT-0728]. Upon receipt of the service copy of a NAICS code appeal, the contracting officer must stay the date for the closing of receipt of offers [@CL-MKT-0729].

## How it works

The two rules cover different disputes. A size protest challenges the size of a concern in connection with a particular procurement or sale [@CL-MKT-0701]. A NAICS code appeal challenges the code or size standard the contracting officer designated, and goes to the Office of Hearings and Appeals [@CL-MKT-0702; @CL-MKT-0704]. Who may file a size protest and the protest time limits are on the [Who may file a size protest and the time limits](mkt-sba-size-protest-parties-and-deadlines.md) card and are not restated here. The printed text also gives examples of specific and non-specific protest allegations, the information an appeal must contain, how an appeal is served, and the other steps the contracting officer and the Office of Hearings and Appeals take after an appeal; this card does not state those.

Unless an appeal is made to the Office of Hearings and Appeals, the size determination made by an SBA Government Contracting Area Office or Disaster Area Office is the final decision of the agency [@CL-MKT-2394]. An appeal to that office is an administrative remedy that must be exhausted before judicial review of a formal size determination may be sought in a court [@CL-MKT-2395]. A NAICS code designation made by a procuring activity contracting officer may also be appealed to the Office of Hearings and Appeals [@CL-MKT-2396].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation is issued on day 0 and an amendment on day 10 changes its NAICS code. A small firm hurt by the new code has until day 20 to serve and file an appeal. SBA could file its own appeal at any time until offers are due on day 45. An appeal filed on day 25 would be summarily dismissed as untimely, and when a timely appeal is served the contracting officer must stay the closing date. A letter saying a rival is large, with no procurement attached, would not be acted on by SBA.

## Never confuse

A size protest challenges one concern's size on a procurement, and a NAICS appeal challenges the code the contracting officer chose. The contracting officer's assignment of the code, and the FAR side of these appeals, are on the [Size standards, NAICS codes and appeals of the contracting officer's code](mkt-size-standards-and-naics-codes.md) card. The FAR rules for protesting a representation are on the [Protesting a small business representation or rerepresentation](mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) card. A small business status advisory opinion does not stop a protest on a specific procurement; it is on the [Small business status advisory opinions](mkt-small-business-status-advisory-opinions.md) card.
