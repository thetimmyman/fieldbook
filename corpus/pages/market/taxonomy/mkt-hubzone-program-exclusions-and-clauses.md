## Purpose

Shows when the HUBZone subpart does not apply, and which clauses the contracting officer must insert under the contract clauses section.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1949] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.1304 and 19.1309 [@CL-MKT-1981; @CL-MKT-1954] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1949] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for acquisitions set aside or awarded on a sole-source basis to HUBZone small business concerns, and full and open competition acquisitions for the price evaluation preference clause [@CL-MKT-1954; @CL-MKT-1956] |

Section 19.1304 lists as alternatives what this subpart does not apply to [@CL-MKT-1981; @CL-MKT-0187]. Paragraph (a) covers requirements that can be satisfied through award to Federal Prison Industries, Inc. (see subpart 8.6) or to AbilityOne participating non-profit agencies for the blind or severely disabled (see subpart 8.7) [@CL-MKT-1949]. Paragraph (b) covers orders under indefinite-delivery contracts, and paragraph (c) covers orders against Federal Supply Schedules [@CL-MKT-1950; @CL-MKT-1951]. Each of those two paragraphs adds a "But see" cross-reference to discretionary set-asides of orders, at 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F) for indefinite-delivery orders and at 8.405-5 for schedule orders [@CL-MKT-1950; @CL-MKT-1951]. Paragraph (d) covers requirements currently being performed by an 8(a) participant or requirements SBA has accepted for performance under the authority of the 8(a) program, unless SBA has consented to release them from the 8(a) program, or paragraph (e) covers requirements for commissary or exchange resale items [@CL-MKT-1952; @CL-MKT-0187; @CL-MKT-1953]. SBA is the party whose consent ends the exclusion in paragraph (d) [@CL-MKT-1952].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-3, Notice of HUBZone Set-Aside or Sole-Source Award, in solicitations and contracts for acquisitions that are set aside or awarded on a sole-source basis to HUBZone small business concerns under 19.1305 or 19.1306 [@CL-MKT-1954]. The paragraph says this includes multiple-award contracts when orders may be set aside for HUBZone small business concerns as described in 8.405-5 and 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F), or when orders may be issued directly to one HUBZone small business concern in accordance with 19.504(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-MKT-1955]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-4, Notice of Price Evaluation Preference for HUBZone Small Business Concerns, in solicitations and contracts for acquisitions conducted using full and open competition [@CL-MKT-1956]. Paragraphs (c) and (d) refer to the prescription at 19.507(e) for clause 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting, and to the prescription at 19.507(h) for clause 52.219-33, Nonmanufacturer Rule [@CL-MKT-1957].

## How it works

The subpart does not apply to the requirements listed as alternatives in the five paragraphs of 19.1304 [@CL-MKT-1981; @CL-MKT-0187]. Two of them carry a cross-reference to discretionary set-asides of orders. Orders under indefinite-delivery contracts and orders against Federal Supply Schedules are outside the subpart, but the cross-references point to discretionary set-asides of orders under 16.505(b)(2)(i)(F) and 8.405-5 [@CL-MKT-1950; @CL-MKT-1951]. The 8(a) paragraph carries a condition: the exclusion applies unless SBA has consented to release the requirements from the 8(a) program [@CL-MKT-1952]. The clause rules then turn on how the acquisition is awarded, and each duty falls on the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1954; @CL-MKT-1956]. A set-aside or sole-source award to HUBZone concerns under 19.1305 or 19.1306 calls for 52.219-3, and an acquisition conducted using full and open competition calls for 52.219-4 [@CL-MKT-1954; @CL-MKT-1956]. For multiple-award contracts where orders may be set aside for HUBZone concerns or issued directly to one, the first rule includes the contract [@CL-MKT-1955]. The [HUBZone set-asides, sole-source awards and the price evaluation preference](../programs/mkt-hubzone-set-asides-and-price-preference.md) card covers when a HUBZone set-aside, sole-source award or price evaluation preference is used. The [Set-asides of orders under multiple-award contracts and the set-aside clauses](../programs/mkt-set-aside-orders-and-clauses.md) card covers the clauses prescribed at 19.507 [@CL-MKT-1957], and the [8(a) contract administration and release of requirements](../programs/mkt-8a-administration-and-release.md) card covers release of 8(a) requirements.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer considers a HUBZone set-aside for a requirement that an 8(a) participant is currently performing. SBA has not consented to release it. Answer: paragraph (d) applies, so the HUBZone subpart does not apply to that requirement [@CL-MKT-1952]. Later SBA consents to release the requirement from the 8(a) program. Answer: the exclusion in paragraph (d) no longer applies, and the other four exclusion paragraphs still apply if their terms are met [@CL-MKT-1952; @CL-MKT-1981; @CL-MKT-0187]. For a separate acquisition conducted using full and open competition, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-4 [@CL-MKT-1956].

## Never confuse

Being outside this subpart does not by itself say that an order cannot be set aside. Orders under indefinite-delivery contracts and orders against Federal Supply Schedules are excluded, but each exclusion points to discretionary set-asides of orders under the cross-referenced provisions [@CL-MKT-1950; @CL-MKT-1951]. The 8(a) exclusion is conditional, because SBA can consent to release the requirements [@CL-MKT-1952]. The clause at 52.219-3 is for set-aside and sole-source awards to HUBZone concerns, and the clause at 52.219-4 is for acquisitions conducted using full and open competition [@CL-MKT-1954; @CL-MKT-1956]. Paragraphs (c) and (d) of section 19.1309 refer to 19.507(e) and 19.507(h) for the Limitations on Subcontracting and Nonmanufacturer Rule clauses [@CL-MKT-1957].
