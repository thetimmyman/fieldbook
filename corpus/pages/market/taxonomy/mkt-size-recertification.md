## Purpose

Shows how a concern asks SBA to recertify it as small, what the request must contain, and what a grant or a denial does, under 13 CFR 121.1010 [@CL-MKT-2059; @CL-MKT-2061].

A concern may request SBA to recertify it as small at any time by filing an application for recertification with the Government Contracting Area Office responsible for the area in which the headquarters of the applicant is located, regardless of the location of parent companies or affiliates [@CL-MKT-2059]. No particular form is prescribed for the application, but the request for recertification must be accompanied by a current completed SBA Form 355 and any other information sufficient to show a significant change in its ownership, management, or other factors bearing on its status as a small concern [@CL-MKT-2060; @CL-MKT-2061].

Recertification will not be required, and the prohibition against future self-certification will not apply, if the adverse SBA size determination is based solely on a finding of affiliation limited to a particular Government procurement or property sale, such as an ostensible subcontracting relationship or non-compliance with the nonmanufacturer rule [@CL-MKT-2062; @CL-MKT-2063].

A denial of an application for recertification is a formal size determination and may be reviewed by OHA at the discretion of that office [@CL-MKT-2064]. The granting of an application for recertification has future effect only; while it is a formal size determination, notice of recertification is required to be given only to the applicant [@CL-MKT-2065].

## How it works

A concern may request SBA to recertify it as small at any time by filing an application for recertification with the Government Contracting Area Office responsible for the area in which the headquarters of the applicant is located, regardless of the location of parent companies or affiliates [@CL-MKT-2059].

The request must be accompanied by a current completed SBA Form 355 and any other information sufficient to show a significant change in its ownership, management, or other factors bearing on its status as a small concern [@CL-MKT-2061]. No particular form is prescribed for the application itself [@CL-MKT-2060].

Recertification will not be required, and the prohibition against future self-certification will not apply, if the adverse SBA size determination is based solely on a finding of affiliation limited to a particular Government procurement or property sale, such as an ostensible subcontracting relationship or non-compliance with the nonmanufacturer rule [@CL-MKT-2062; @CL-MKT-2063].

A denial of an application for recertification is a formal size determination that OHA may review at its discretion [@CL-MKT-2064]. The granting of an application for recertification has future effect only, and while it is a formal size determination, notice of recertification is required to be given only to the applicant [@CL-MKT-2065].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A concern whose ownership has changed asks SBA to recertify it as small. It files its application with the area office responsible for the area of its own headquarters, even though its parent company is located in another area. With the application it sends a current completed SBA Form 355 and information sufficient to show the significant change in its ownership. If the request is granted, the recertification has future effect only, and notice is required to be given only to the concern. If it is denied, the denial is a formal size determination that OHA may review at its discretion. Separately, had an adverse size determination rested solely on a finding of affiliation limited to one procurement, such as an ostensible subcontracting relationship, recertification would not have been required and the prohibition against future self-certification would not have applied.

## Never confuse

A recertification grant is not a ruling on the past: it has future effect only, and although it is a formal size determination, notice of it is required to be given only to the applicant [@CL-MKT-2065]. A denial is also a formal size determination, and OHA may review it at its discretion [@CL-MKT-2064]. Recertification under this section is a request to SBA [@CL-MKT-2059], whereas to be identified as a small business concern in the System for Award Management (SAM) database, a concern must certify its size in connection with specific size standards at least annually, and if it fails to certify within one year it will not be listed as a small business concern in SAM unless and until it recertifies its size [@CL-MKT-2043; @CL-MKT-2044]. See [SBA size standard designation](mkt-sba-size-standard-designation.md) and [Size rerepresentation after award](../taxonomy/mkt-size-rerepresentation-after-award.md).
