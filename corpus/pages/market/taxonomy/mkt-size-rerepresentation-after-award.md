## Purpose

Shows when a contractor that won a contract as a small business must rerepresent its size and status, and when a contractor that won as other than small may do so.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0214] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.301-2 and FAR 19.301-3 [@CL-MKT-0214; @CL-MKT-0218] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0214] |
| Applies to | Contractors that represented their status as one of the small business concerns the FAR identifies for this purpose before award, and contractors that represented as other than small and may rerepresent [@CL-MKT-0214; @CL-MKT-0218] |

A contractor that represented its status as any of the small business concerns the FAR identifies for this purpose before contract award is required to rerepresent its size and socioeconomic status under the postaward rerepresentation clause on the occasions the paragraph lists [@CL-MKT-0214]. A contractor that represented itself as other than small before award may, but is not required to, rerepresent its size status when the same conditions apply and it qualifies as a small business concern [@CL-MKT-0218]. That qualification is judged under the applicable size standard in effect at the time of its rerepresentation [@CL-MKT-0292].

A contractor is required to rerepresent its size status in accordance with the size standard in effect at the time of its rerepresentation that corresponds to the NAICS code that was initially assigned to the contract [@CL-MKT-0288]. For a multiple-award contract with more than one NAICS code assigned, the contractor is required to rerepresent its size status for each NAICS code assigned to the contract [@CL-MKT-9406].

## How it works

One set of the listed occasions applies for the NAICS code or codes in the contract [@CL-MKT-0287]. Some of those occasions are events and one is a date. One event is within 30 days after execution of a novation agreement, or within 30 days after modification of the contract to include the clause if the novation came first [@CL-MKT-0215]. Another event is within 30 days after a merger or acquisition of the contractor that does not require novation, whether the contractor acquires or is acquired by another company [@CL-MKT-0216], or within 30 days after modification of the contract to include the clause if the merger or acquisition came first [@CL-MKT-0289]. The date case applies to long-term contracts: within 60 to 120 days before the end of the fifth year and within 60 to 120 days before the date set for exercising any option after that [@CL-MKT-0217]. The section also lists order-level occasions for multiple-award contracts and defines a long-term contract, and this card does not restate either. A change in size status does not change the terms and conditions of the contract [@CL-MKT-0290], but the contracting officer may require a subcontracting plan for a contract containing the subcontracting plan clause if a prime contractor's size status changes from small to other than small as a result of a size rerepresentation [@CL-MKT-0291].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract is awarded to a small business on day 0. On day 100 the contractor merges with a larger company, and the merger does not need a novation. Answer: the contractor rerepresents its size and status for the NAICS code in the contract by day 130, which is 30 days after the merger. A different contractor that won as other than small may choose to rerepresent but is not required to, if one of the same occasions has occurred and it qualifies as small under the size standard in effect when it rerepresents.

## Never confuse

Required rerepresentation by a contractor that won as a small business is not the same as the optional rerepresentation open to a contractor that won as other than small [@CL-MKT-0214; @CL-MKT-0218]. Rerepresentation after award is also not the representation at initial offer, which has its own card, and it does not set how receipts and employees are counted after a deal.

The registration side is on the [System for Award Management registration](../feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md) card, how a novation is processed is on the [Novation agreements](../../mna/deal-gates/mna-novation-processing.md) card, and how an acquisition changes the size counted is on the [SBA size after an acquisition](../../mna/screening/mna-sba-size-after-acquisition.md) card. What the representation at initial offer covers is on the [Offeror size and status representation](mkt-offeror-size-and-status-representation.md) card, the size standards are on the [Size standards and NAICS codes](mkt-size-standards-and-naics-codes.md) card, and the subcontracting plan that a change of size can trigger is on the [When a subcontracting plan is required](../programs/mkt-subcontracting-plan-when-required.md) card.

## Company calibration

Which contracts were won on a small business basis, which of them run long enough to reach a fifth-year or option date, and which mergers, acquisitions or novations are pending. [@Q-003]
