## Purpose

Shows when a contracting officer shall set an individual acquisition or class of acquisitions aside for competition among small businesses, and which purchases are outside the requirement, under FAR 19.502-1 [@CL-MKT-1435; @CL-MKT-1436; @CL-MKT-1437].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-1435] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.502-1 [@CL-MKT-1435; @CL-MKT-1436; @CL-MKT-1437] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-1435] |
| Applies to | Individual acquisitions and classes of acquisitions, except purchases at or below the micro-purchase threshold and purchases from required sources under part 8 [@CL-MKT-1435; @CL-MKT-1437] |

The contracting officer shall set aside an individual acquisition or class of acquisitions for competition among small businesses when it is determined to be in the interest of maintaining or mobilizing the Nation's full productive capacity, war or national defense programs, or assuring that a fair proportion of Government contracts in each industry is placed with small business concerns, and the circumstances described in FAR 19.502-2 or 19.502-3(a) exist [@CL-MKT-1435; @CL-MKT-1436].

The requirement in paragraph (a) does not apply to purchases at or below the micro-purchase threshold, or to purchases from required sources under part 8 (for example, from the Committee for Purchase From People Who are Blind or Severely Disabled) [@CL-MKT-1437].

## How it works

The duty sits on the contracting officer and covers both a single acquisition and a class of acquisitions [@CL-MKT-1435]. The grounds in paragraph (a)(1) and (a)(2) are joined by "or", and the circumstances described in FAR 19.502-2 or 19.502-3(a) exist [@CL-MKT-1435; @CL-MKT-1436].

Two kinds of purchase are outside the requirement in paragraph (a): those at or below the micro-purchase threshold, and those from required sources under part 8 [@CL-MKT-1437]. The micro-purchase threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card. The circumstances in FAR 19.502-2 are on the [Small business set-asides and the socioeconomic programs](../programs/mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card, and those in FAR 19.502-3(a) are on the [Partial and class small business set-asides](../programs/mkt-partial-and-class-small-business-set-asides.md) card [@CL-MKT-1436].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency needs recurring custodial services at four sites for one year. The contracting officer treats the need as a class of acquisitions and determines that a set-aside is in the interest of maintaining the Nation's full productive capacity [@CL-MKT-1435], and finds that the circumstances described in FAR 19.502-2 or 19.502-3(a) exist [@CL-MKT-1436]. The purchase is above the micro-purchase threshold and is not from a required source, so the exclusion in paragraph (b) does not apply [@CL-MKT-1437]. The officer sets the class aside for competition among small businesses [@CL-MKT-1435]. A separate purchase of office supplies from a required source under part 8 is outside the requirement, so this section does not require a set-aside for it [@CL-MKT-1437]. A purchase at or below the micro-purchase threshold is outside it too [@CL-MKT-1437].

## Never confuse

The micro-purchase exclusion is not the test for setting aside acquisitions above the simplified acquisition threshold, which is on the [Small business set-asides and the socioeconomic programs](../programs/mkt-small-business-set-asides.md) card. A class set-aside also differs from a partial set-aside, which is on the [Partial and class small business set-asides](../programs/mkt-partial-and-class-small-business-set-asides.md) card. The requirement here is a duty ("shall") when paragraph (a) is met, subject to the exclusions in paragraph (b) [@CL-MKT-1435; @CL-MKT-1437]. It is a separate subject from the small business reserve, which is on the [Small business reserve](../programs/mkt-small-business-reserve.md) card.
