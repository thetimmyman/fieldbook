## Purpose

Shows the size certification and SBA review for a Participant in the 8(a) Business Development program on a particular subcontract, and the size standard for buying Government-owned timber, under 13 CFR 121.603 and 121.507 [@CL-MKT-0680; @CL-MKT-0684].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | 8(a) Participants offering on a particular 8(a) subcontract, and concerns buying Government-owned timber other than Special Salvage Timber [@CL-MKT-0680; @CL-MKT-0684] |

A Participant must certify that it qualifies as a small business under the NAICS code assigned to a particular 8(a) subcontract as part of its initial offer including price to the procuring agency [@CL-MKT-0680]. Within 30 days of receiving the self-certification, the SBA district office serving the area of the Participant's principal office reviews it and determines whether the Participant is small [@CL-MKT-0681]. If the Participant does not timely request a formal size determination, SBA may accept the procurement in support of another Participant, or may rescind its acceptance of the offer for the 8(a) Business Development program, as appropriate [@CL-MKT-0713].

To be small for the sale of Government-owned timber other than Special Salvage Timber, a concern must, among other requirements, be primarily engaged in the logging or forest products industry and not exceed 500 employees, taking into account its affiliates [@CL-MKT-0684]. One listed condition for a concern that intends at the time of offer to resell the timber is to agree not to sell more than 30 percent of the timber, or 50 percent if it is an Alaskan business, to a business that does not meet the stated industry and employee requirements [@CL-MKT-0685]. A concern that buys timber under a small business set-aside, other than through the Special Salvage Timber Sale program, must keep a record for three years [@CL-MKT-0686]. The record lists the name, address and size status of every concern to which it sells the timber or sawlogs, and the species, grades and volumes of sawlogs sold [@CL-MKT-0715].

## How it works

Once SBA verifies the selected Participant as small, later changes in size up to the date of award do not affect its status for that procurement, except changes due to merger with or acquisition by another business concern [@CL-MKT-0682]. After such a merger or acquisition between self-certification and award, the concern must recertify its size status, and SBA must verify the new certification before award can occur [@CL-MKT-0683]. The timber section states no measurement date, so this card gives none for timber; the date for other contracts is on the [When a concern's size status is measured](mkt-sba-size-measurement-date.md) card. The timber section also sets conditions for a concern that does not intend to resell, a condition on a later acquisition or change of control, and a waiver power; this card does not state them.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Participant certifies that it is small, with its initial offer, on day 0. SBA verifies on day 20. The Participant grows from 80 to 150 employees by day 60, without any merger or acquisition, and receives the award on day 90. Its size status for that procurement is unchanged. If it had instead been acquired by another firm on day 60, it would have to recertify, and SBA would have to verify the new certification before award.

## Never confuse

The timber standard applies to a concern buying timber that the Government sells. Size standards for procurements in which the Government buys are on the [Size standards, NAICS codes and appeals of the contracting officer's code](mkt-size-standards-and-naics-codes.md) card.

## Company calibration

Whether the company is or plans to be a Participant in the Business Development program, and whether it would ever buy Government timber. [@Q-019]
