## Purpose

Shows who may protest a firm's HUBZone status, the deadlines, who decides, and what a decision means for award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0320] |
| Codified citation | FAR 19.306 [@CL-MKT-0320; @CL-MKT-0321; @CL-MKT-0322; @CL-MKT-0323; @CL-MKT-0324; @CL-MKT-0325; @CL-MKT-0326; @CL-MKT-0327; @CL-MKT-0328; @CL-MKT-0329; @CL-MKT-0330; @CL-MKT-0356; @CL-MKT-0357; @CL-MKT-0358] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0320] |
| Applies to | Protests of the certified HUBZone status of a prospective contractor or an apparent successful offeror [@CL-MKT-0320] |

For sole-source procurements, SBA or the contracting officer may protest the prospective contractor's certified HUBZone status [@CL-MKT-0320]. For all other procurements, SBA, the contracting officer, or any other interested party may protest the apparent successful offeror's certified HUBZone status [@CL-MKT-0320]. The Director of SBA's Office of the HUBZone Program decides whether the concern has certified HUBZone status [@CL-MKT-0321]. An interested party must submit its written protest to the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-0322]. For sealed bids, one listed deadline is the close of business on the fifth business day after bid opening [@CL-MKT-0322]. For negotiated acquisitions, one listed deadline is the close of business on the fifth business day after receipt of the special notification of the apparently successful offeror [@CL-MKT-0323]. A protest received after the designated time limits is untimely, unless it is from the contracting officer or SBA [@CL-MKT-0324].

After receiving a protest involving the apparent successful offeror's status as a HUBZone small business concern, the contracting officer shall either withhold award until SBA determines the status of the protested concern or award the contract if the conditions of the second alternative are met [@CL-MKT-0325]. Those conditions are that SBA does not issue its decision within 15 business days after receipt of the protest and that the contracting officer determines in writing that there is an immediate need to award and that waiting will be disadvantageous to the Government [@CL-MKT-0325; @CL-MKT-0326]. SBA will determine the merits within 15 business days after receipt of a protest, or within any extension of time granted by the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-0327]. If the contracting officer has withheld award, the Director has determined that the protested concern is ineligible, and a timely appeal has not been filed, the contracting officer shall not award the contract to the protested concern [@CL-MKT-0328]. If the contracting officer has withheld award and SBA has determined that the protested concern is an eligible HUBZone or dismissed all protests against it, the contracting officer may award the contract to the protested concern [@CL-MKT-0356].

A ruling can also arrive after award. If the contracting officer made the written determination of immediate need, awarded the contract, and the ruling of the Director sustaining the protest is received after award, the contracting officer shall take one of two listed courses [@CL-MKT-0326; @CL-MKT-0357]. The courses are to terminate the contract, or to make a written determination that termination is not in the best interests of the Government and not exercise any options or award further task or delivery orders under the contract [@CL-MKT-0358].

## How it works

If SBA upholds the protest, it removes the concern's HUBZone status in the Dynamic Small Business Search [@CL-MKT-0321]. The determination is effective immediately and is final unless overturned on appeal by SBA's Associate Administrator, Office of Government Contracting and Business Development [@CL-MKT-0330]. The protested concern, the protester, or the contracting officer may appeal, and the appeal must be received no later than 5 business days after receipt of the protest determination [@CL-MKT-0329]. The section also covers the content of a protest, joint ventures, the referral letter, suspension of performance while an appeal is pending, and the steps of an appeal beyond its deadline, and this card does not restate those.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A sealed bid is opened on day 0. Another bidder protests the apparent successful offeror's HUBZone status. Answer: the protest must reach the contracting officer in writing by close of business on the fifth business day after bid opening, which is one listed deadline for sealed bids. Suppose the contracting officer withholds award. The Director of SBA's Office of the HUBZone Program decides within 15 business days unless the time is extended. If the Director finds the concern ineligible and no timely appeal is filed, the contracting officer shall not award it the contract.

## Never confuse

A HUBZone status protest asks whether a certified concern met the HUBZone requirements. A size protest asks whether an offeror is small, and an SBA Area Office decides it [@CL-MKT-0318], while the Director of SBA's Office of the HUBZone Program decides a HUBZone status protest [@CL-MKT-0321]. Size protests are on the [Protesting a small business representation or rerepresentation](mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) card, and the representation itself is on the [Offeror size and status representation](mkt-offeror-size-and-status-representation.md) card.
