## Purpose

Lists the size standards that the size standards table prints for aerospace and defense manufacturing codes (aircraft, engines and parts, guided missiles and space vehicles, ship building, search and navigation instruments, ammunition and military armored vehicles), and for engineering services and research and development with their listed exceptions. It also states the footnotes that bear on those rows. The general layout of the table is on [the table organization card](mkt-size-standards-table-organization.md).

Manufacturing rows print a number of employees.

- Aircraft manufacturing and aircraft engine and engine parts manufacturing both print 1,500 [@CL-MKT-2170; @CL-MKT-2171].
- Other aircraft parts and auxiliary equipment manufacturing prints 1,250 and carries footnote marker 7 [@CL-MKT-2172].
- Guided missile and space vehicle manufacturing prints 1,300, its propulsion unit and parts row prints 1,250, and the other parts and auxiliary equipment row prints 1,050 [@CL-MKT-2173; @CL-MKT-2174; @CL-MKT-2175].
- Ship building and repairing prints 1,300, and military armored vehicle, tank, and tank component manufacturing prints 1,500 [@CL-MKT-2176; @CL-MKT-2177].
- The search, detection, navigation, guidance, aeronautical, and nautical system and instrument row prints 1,350 [@CL-MKT-2178].
- Small arms ammunition manufacturing prints 1,300, ammunition other than small arms prints 1,500, and small arms, ordnance, and ordnance accessories manufacturing prints 1,000 [@CL-MKT-2179; @CL-MKT-2180; @CL-MKT-2181].

Engineering services rows print dollar figures in millions of dollars [@CL-MKT-2160; @CL-MKT-2162].

- Engineering Services under code 541330 prints $25.5 [@CL-MKT-2182].
- Three exceptions under the same code print $47.0 each: Military and Aerospace Equipment and Military Weapons, Contracts and Subcontracts for Engineering Services Awarded Under the National Energy Policy Act of 1992, and Marine Engineering and Naval Architecture [@CL-MKT-2183; @CL-MKT-2184; @CL-MKT-2185].

The research and development rows print employees and carry footnote marker 11 [@CL-MKT-2186; @CL-MKT-2187; @CL-MKT-2188].

- Research and Technology in Nanotechnology under code 541713 and Research and Technology in Biotechnology (except Nanobiotechnology) under code 541714 print 1,000 each [@CL-MKT-2186; @CL-MKT-2187].
- Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences (except Nanotechnology and Biotechnology) under code 541715 prints 1,000 [@CL-MKT-2188].
- Three exceptions under code 541715 print their own standards [@CL-MKT-2189; @CL-MKT-2190; @CL-MKT-2191]. Aircraft, aircraft engine and engine parts prints 1,500 [@CL-MKT-2189]. Other aircraft parts and auxiliary equipment prints 1,250 [@CL-MKT-2190]. Guided missiles and space vehicles, with their propulsion units and propulsion parts, prints 1,300 [@CL-MKT-2191].

## How it works

The size standard is the maximum number of employees or annual receipts that the concern and its affiliates may have and still be considered small [@CL-MKT-2161]. Each standard is stated in one measure or the other, unless the text specifies otherwise [@CL-MKT-2160]. Exceptions are printed as separate rows under the same code [@CL-MKT-2164].

Under FAR 19.102, unless paragraph (b)(2)(ii)(B) of that section requires otherwise, contracting officers must assign one NAICS code and its size standard to all solicitations, contracts, and task and delivery orders [@CL-MKT-0203]. How the contracting officer picks among exception rows is on [the designation card](mkt-sba-size-standard-designation.md).

The wholesale trade and retail trade sector notes say that those codes shall not be used to classify Government acquisitions for supplies, and that the applicable manufacturing code shall be used [@CL-MKT-2165; @CL-MKT-2341; @CL-MKT-2166]. Federal Government contractors also shall not use the trade codes when subcontracting for the acquisition for supplies [@CL-MKT-2168].

Footnote rules that bear on these rows:

- For rebuilding machinery or equipment on a factory basis, or equivalent, the footnote on subsectors 333, 334, 335 and 336 says to use the code for a newly manufactured product [@CL-MKT-2169]. Concerns performing major rebuilding or overhaul do not necessarily have to meet the criteria for being a manufacturer even though the activities may be classified under a manufacturing code, and ordinary repair services or preservation are not considered rebuilding [@CL-MKT-2192]. The footnote names those four subsectors and does not name the ammunition codes in the metal product subsector [@CL-MKT-2169].
- Contracts for the rebuilding or overhaul of aircraft ground support equipment on a contract basis are classified under code 336413, the row that carries marker 7 [@CL-MKT-2193; @CL-MKT-2172].
- For the research and development codes, Research and Development means laboratory or other physical research and development [@CL-MKT-2194]. It does not include economic, educational, engineering, operations, systems or other nonphysical research, or computer programming, data processing, or commercial or medical laboratory testing [@CL-MKT-2195].
- Under the same footnote, for research and development contracts requiring the delivery of a manufactured product, the appropriate size standard is that of the manufacturing industry [@CL-MKT-2196].
- Under the same footnote, Research and Development for guided missiles and space vehicles includes evaluations and simulation, and other services requiring thorough knowledge of complete missiles and spacecraft [@CL-MKT-2197].
- For the Small Business Innovation Research and Small Business Transfer Technology programs (SBIR and STTR), paragraph (c) of the same footnote defines the term research or research and development as any activity which is a systematic, intensive study directed toward greater knowledge or understanding of the subject studied, or a systematic study directed specifically toward applying new knowledge to meet a recognized need, or a systematic application of knowledge toward the production of useful materials, devices, and systems or methods, including design, development, and improvement of prototypes and new processes to meet specific requirements [@CL-MKT-2327; @CL-MKT-2328; @CL-MKT-2329; @CL-MKT-2330]. The footnote points to section 121.702 for size eligibility requirements for those programs [@CL-MKT-2331]; that section is on [the SBIR and STTR eligibility card](mkt-sbir-sttr-size-and-eligibility-standards.md). The footnote also cites other references and gives a web address, which this card does not state.
- The Environmental Remediation Services exception under code 562910 prints 1,000 employees and carries footnote marker 14 [@CL-MKT-2332]. Paragraph (b) of that footnote sets how a Government procurement is classified as Environmental Remediation Services. Its general purpose must be to restore or directly support the restoration of a contaminated environment, though it need not necessarily include remedial actions, and the footnote lists examples of such activities [@CL-MKT-2333; @CL-MKT-2334; @CL-MKT-2335]. It must be composed of activities in three or more separate industries with separate NAICS codes or, in some instances such as engineering, smaller sub-components of NAICS codes with separate, distinct size standards [@CL-MKT-2336]. The activities may include Engineering Services and Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering and Life Sciences, among other listed industries [@CL-MKT-2337; @CL-MKT-2338]. If any activity can be identified with a separate NAICS code, or a component of a code with a separate distinct size standard, and that industry accounts for 50 percent or more of the value of the entire procurement, the proper size standard is the one for that particular industry and not the Environmental Remediation Service size standard [@CL-MKT-2339; @CL-MKT-2340]. This card does not state paragraph (a) of that footnote.

A trade concern that offers or quotes on a supply acquisition is a nonmanufacturer that is deemed small if it has 500 or fewer employees and meets the requirements of section 121.406 [@CL-MKT-2167]. The conditions are on [the nonmanufacturer card](mkt-sba-nonmanufacturer-qualification.md).

This card does not state the footnotes on petroleum refiners, tires, financial institution assets, facilities support services, building and property specialty trade services and Job Corps centers, which do not bear on these rows, or the other footnotes of the table. Read them in the section itself.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A concern reports 1,200 employees counting its affiliates. It bids on a research and development contract for guided missiles and space vehicles that does not require the delivery of a manufactured product, and the contract is classified under the research and development exception for guided missiles and space vehicles, which prints 1,300 [@CL-MKT-2191]. The concern is within that ceiling. If the contract instead required the delivery of a manufactured product, the standard of the manufacturing industry would apply [@CL-MKT-2196]. If that industry were other guided missile and space vehicle parts and auxiliary equipment, whose row prints 1,050, the concern would not be small [@CL-MKT-2175].

A second concern has annual receipts of $40 million, counted as the size rules require. For an engineering contract classified under the military and aerospace equipment exception, the ceiling is $47.0 million and the concern is within it [@CL-MKT-2183]. Under the plain Engineering Services row, which prints $25.5 million, it would not be [@CL-MKT-2182].

A third synthetic procurement, for $5,000,000, meets the Environmental Remediation Services footnote's other conditions for classification [@CL-MKT-2333; @CL-MKT-2336], and $3,000,000 of it, which is 60 percent, is engineering services identified with a separate NAICS code. That industry accounts for 50 percent or more of the value of the entire procurement, so the proper size standard is the one for engineering and not the Environmental Remediation Service standard [@CL-MKT-2339; @CL-MKT-2340]. If the engineering share were $2,000,000, which is 40 percent, that 50 percent condition would not be met for engineering [@CL-MKT-2340].

## Never confuse

Aircraft manufacturing and aircraft engine and engine parts manufacturing are separate codes, each with its own row, and other aircraft parts and auxiliary equipment is a third code with a lower ceiling [@CL-MKT-2170; @CL-MKT-2171; @CL-MKT-2172].

The guided missile vehicle row is not the propulsion unit row, and the propulsion row prints a lower ceiling [@CL-MKT-2173; @CL-MKT-2174].

A research and development exception row is not the matching manufacturing row. For a contract that requires the delivery of a manufactured product, the manufacturing industry's standard applies [@CL-MKT-2196].

The engineering rows are in millions of dollars of annual receipts, while the manufacturing and research rows on this card are employee counts [@CL-MKT-2160; @CL-MKT-2162].

Selling defense supplies as a reseller is a different question, covered in [the nonmanufacturer qualification card](mkt-sba-nonmanufacturer-qualification.md) [@CL-MKT-2167].
