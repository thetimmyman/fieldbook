## Purpose

Shows what a contracting officer must do with a size protest and what the referral to an SBA Government Contracting Area Office must contain, under 13 CFR 121.1006 [@CL-MKT-2056; @CL-MKT-2057].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Size protests received by a contracting officer, other than a protest from SBA [@CL-MKT-2056] |

A contracting officer who receives a protest, other than a protest from SBA, must forward the protest promptly to the SBA Government Contracting Area Office serving the area in which the headquarters of the offeror is located [@CL-MKT-2056]. The contracting officer's referral must contain the protest and any accompanying materials, a copy of the self-certification as to size, and identification of the applicable size standard [@CL-MKT-2057]. It must also contain a copy of the solicitation, identification of the date of bid opening or of the notification provided to unsuccessful offerors, the date on which the protest was received, and a complete address and point of contact for the protested concern [@CL-MKT-2058].

## How it works

The duty is the contracting officer's, and it covers a protest that the officer receives and that does not come from SBA [@CL-MKT-2056]. The referral goes to the SBA Government Contracting Area Office serving the area of the offeror's headquarters [@CL-MKT-2056]. The referral must contain every listed item, three in the first part of the list and four in the second [@CL-MKT-2057; @CL-MKT-2058]. The section states the timing as prompt forwarding and sets no further deadline [@CL-MKT-2056].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer receives a size protest from a competitor after bid opening on a sealed-bid set-aside. The protest is not from SBA, so the officer must forward it promptly to the area office serving the area of the offeror's headquarters [@CL-MKT-2056]. The referral holds the protest and its accompanying materials, the self-certification as to size, and the applicable size standard [@CL-MKT-2057]. It also holds the solicitation, the date of bid opening, the date the protest was received, and the protested concern's complete address and point of contact [@CL-MKT-2058]. Had the protest come from SBA, this duty would not apply to it [@CL-MKT-2056].

## Never confuse

This section covers only the forwarding of a protest and the contents of the referral, and it does not say who decides the protest. It tells the contracting officer to forward the protest promptly and lists what the referral must contain [@CL-MKT-2056; @CL-MKT-2057; @CL-MKT-2058]. Who may protest and the deadlines are in [Small business size protests](../taxonomy/mkt-small-business-size-protests.md) and [Size protest parties and deadlines](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-protest-parties-and-deadlines.md). What SBA does after it receives a protest is in [SBA size determination procedure](../taxonomy/mkt-sba-size-determination-procedure.md).
