## Purpose

Shows how a competitive 8(a) contract is prepared with a tripartite continuation sheet that carries agency and SBA signature lines, and which 8(a) clauses the contracting officer shall insert [@CL-MKT-1665; @CL-MKT-1669].

| Codified citation | FAR 19.811-2 and FAR 19.811-3 [@CL-MKT-1665; @CL-MKT-1669] |

Preparation. The contract will be prepared in accordance with 14.408-1(d), except that appropriate blocks on the Standard Form 26 or 1442 will be asterisked and a continuation sheet appended as a tripartite agreement [@CL-MKT-1665]. The tripartite agreement includes the agency contracting activity, the prime contract number, the name of the agency contracting officer, and lines for signature, date signed and effective date [@CL-MKT-1666]. It also includes the SBA office, the SBA subcontract number, the name of the SBA contracting officer and lines for signature and date signed [@CL-MKT-1667].

The process for obtaining signatures shall be as specified in 19.811-1(b)(4) [@CL-MKT-1668]. That paragraph states that SBA will obtain the signature of the 8(a) contractor before signing and returning the prime contract to the contracting officer for signature [@CL-MKT-1683]. It also states that SBA will make every effort to obtain signatures and return the contract, and any subsequent bilateral modification, to the contracting officer within a maximum of 10 working days [@CL-MKT-1684].

Clauses. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-11, Special 8(a) Contract Conditions, in contracts between the SBA and the agency when the acquisition is accomplished using the procedures of 19.811-1(a) and (b) [@CL-MKT-1669]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-12, Special 8(a) Subcontract Conditions, in contracts between the SBA and its 8(a) contractor when the acquisition is accomplished using the same procedures [@CL-MKT-1670].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-17, Section 8(a) Award, in competitive solicitations and contracts when the acquisition is accomplished using the procedures of 19.805 and in sole source awards which utilize the alternative procedure in 19.811-1(c) [@CL-MKT-1671]. Under that alternative, except in procurements where SBA will make advance payments to its 8(a) contractor, the agency contracting officer may use a single contract document for both the prime contract between the agency and the SBA and its 8(a) contractor instead of the procedures in paragraphs (a) and (b) of 19.811-1 [@CL-MKT-1685; @CL-MKT-1686].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.219-18, Notification of Competition Limited to Eligible 8(a) Participants, in competitive solicitations and contracts when the acquisition is accomplished using the procedures of 19.805 [@CL-MKT-1672]. The clause at 52.219-18 is used with its Alternate I when competition is to be limited to 8(a) participants within one or more specific SBA districts pursuant to 19.804-2 [@CL-MKT-1673].

For contracts or orders resulting from the subpart, the section directs the reader to 19.507(e) for use of 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting, and to 19.507(h) for use of 52.219-33, Nonmanufacturer Rule [@CL-MKT-1674].

## How it works

Preparation and clause insertion are separate duties. The document is assembled as a tripartite agreement [@CL-MKT-1665]. The contracting officer shall insert the clauses that match the procedure used [@CL-MKT-1669; @CL-MKT-1670; @CL-MKT-1671; @CL-MKT-1672].

The trigger differs by clause. The two special conditions clauses track the procedures of 19.811-1(a) and (b), and the contracting officer inserts them in different contracts: 52.219-11 in contracts between SBA and the agency, and 52.219-12 in contracts between SBA and its 8(a) contractor [@CL-MKT-1669; @CL-MKT-1670]. The award clause 52.219-17 tracks 19.805 for competitive solicitations and contracts, and the alternative procedure in 19.811-1(c) for sole source awards [@CL-MKT-1671]. The notification clause 52.219-18 tracks 19.805, and takes Alternate I where competition is limited to specific SBA districts [@CL-MKT-1672; @CL-MKT-1673].

Signatures follow the process in 19.811-1(b)(4) [@CL-MKT-1668]. Under it, SBA obtains the contractor's signature first and returns the contract to the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-1683]. The 10 working day figure is part of SBA's every-effort commitment on returning the contract and any subsequent bilateral modification [@CL-MKT-1684].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting activity prepares a competitive 8(a) contract. It prepares the contract in accordance with 14.408-1(d), asterisks the appropriate blocks on the Standard Form 26, and appends a continuation sheet as a tripartite agreement [@CL-MKT-1665]. The sheet lists the agency contracting activity, the prime contract number, the agency contracting officer, and signature, date signed and effective date lines [@CL-MKT-1666]. It also lists the SBA office, the SBA subcontract number, the SBA contracting officer, and signature and date signed lines [@CL-MKT-1667]. SBA obtains the contractor's signature before it signs and returns the contract to the contracting officer, and it makes every effort to do so within 10 working days [@CL-MKT-1683; @CL-MKT-1684].

The acquisition is accomplished using the procedures of 19.805. The contracting officer therefore shall insert the Section 8(a) Award clause and the notification clause in the solicitation and the contract [@CL-MKT-1671; @CL-MKT-1672]. Competition is limited to participants in two specific SBA districts under 19.804-2, so the notification clause is used with Alternate I [@CL-MKT-1673]. The example does not state whether the acquisition uses the procedures of 19.811-1(a) and (b), so it leaves the two special conditions clauses undecided; their insertion depends on those procedures [@CL-MKT-1669; @CL-MKT-1670]. For limitations on subcontracting and the nonmanufacturer rule, the contracting officer turns to 19.507(e) and (h) [@CL-MKT-1674].

## Never confuse

The tripartite continuation sheet sets the form of the contract document, while the clauses tell the contracting officer what to insert [@CL-MKT-1665; @CL-MKT-1669]. The special conditions clauses depend on the procedures of 19.811-1(a) and (b), and the award and notification clauses depend on 19.805 [@CL-MKT-1669; @CL-MKT-1671; @CL-MKT-1672]. Special 8(a) Contract Conditions sits in the contract between SBA and the agency, and Special 8(a) Subcontract Conditions sits in the contract between SBA and its 8(a) contractor [@CL-MKT-1669; @CL-MKT-1670]. The award clause is named for competitive solicitations and contracts under 19.805 and for sole source awards under the alternative procedure in 19.811-1(c) [@CL-MKT-1671]. The notification clause is named for competitive solicitations and contracts under 19.805 [@CL-MKT-1672]. Alternate I of the notification clause is used when competition is to be limited to 8(a) participants within one or more specific SBA districts pursuant to 19.804-2 [@CL-MKT-1673]. The clause texts and prescriptions are on the [Special 8(a) contract and subcontract conditions](../programs/mkt-special-8a-contract-and-subcontract-conditions.md) card [@CL-MKT-1669]. The single contract document option is on the [Sole-source 8(a) awards, agreement orders and the agency contract](../programs/mkt-sole-source-8a-awards-and-agreements.md) card [@CL-MKT-1685].

## Company calibration

Whether the company's authorized acquisition mandate, decision criteria and diligence scope cover bidding where competition is limited to program participants and signing a tripartite agreement. [@Q-028]
