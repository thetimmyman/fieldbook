## Purpose

Shows what is posted before and after award for actions funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act, and which usual exceptions the postaward rules replace.

## The rule

| Codified citation | FAR 5.701 to 5.705 [@CL-MKT-2227; @CL-MKT-2228; @CL-MKT-2229; @CL-MKT-1887; @CL-MKT-0738; @CL-MKT-0739] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

Under FAR 5.704(a), agencies follow the publication procedures at FAR 5.201, and in addition, notices of proposed contract actions are required for orders exceeding $25,000, funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act, which are issued under task or delivery order contracts [@CL-MKT-1887; @CL-MKT-0738]. This requirement does not include modifications to existing orders, which are covered postaward under FAR 5.705 [@CL-MKT-0738]. These notices are for "informational purposes only," so FAR 5.203 does not apply, and contracting officers should concurrently use their usual solicitation practice (for example, eBuy) [@CL-MKT-0741].

Under FAR 5.705, agencies follow the usual publication procedures at FAR 5.301, except that the following supersede the exceptions at FAR 5.301(b)(2) through (7) [@CL-MKT-0739]: publicize the award notice for any action exceeding $500,000, funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act, including (i) contracts, (ii) modifications to existing contracts, (iii) orders which are issued under task or delivery order contracts, and (iv) modifications to orders under task or delivery order contracts [@CL-MKT-0740; @CL-MKT-0742].

## How it works

Regardless of dollar value, if the contract action, including all modifications and orders under task or delivery order contracts, is not both fixed-price and competitively awarded, the award notice must be publicized and its description must include the rationale for using other than a fixed-priced and/or competitive approach [@CL-MKT-0743; @CL-MKT-9393]. The description must include a statement specifically noting if the contract action was not awarded competitively, or was not fixed-price, or was neither competitive nor fixed-price [@CL-MKT-0744]. For the publication procedures in FAR 5.301 and the statement that FAR 5.203 does not apply to the notices under FAR 5.704(a)(2) [@CL-MKT-0739; @CL-MKT-0741], see the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card and the [Publicizing and response time](mkt-publicizing-and-response-time.md) card.

FAR subpart 5.7 prescribes posting requirements for presolicitation and award notices for actions funded in whole or in part by the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (Public Law 111-5) (Recovery Act), and the requirements of the subpart enhance transparency to the public [@CL-MKT-2227]. The subpart applies to all actions expected to exceed $25,000 funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act, and unlike FAR subparts 5.2 and 5.3, it includes additional requirements for orders and for actions that are not both fixed-price and competitive [@CL-MKT-2228].

In FAR subpart 5.7, task or delivery order contract means a "delivery order contract," and a "task order contract," as defined in FAR 16.501-1 [@CL-MKT-2229]. For example, it includes Governmentwide Acquisition Contracts (GWACs), multi-agency contracts (MACs), and other indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contracts, whether single award or multiple award [@CL-MKT-2230], and it also includes Federal Supply Schedule contracts (including Blanket Purchase Agreements under FAR subpart 8.4) [@CL-MKT-2231].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Recovery Act funded order of 30,000 is placed under a task order contract, and a second order of 20,000 is placed under the same kind of contract. The first exceeds the preaward notice amount and needs a notice, and the second does not. Separately, a Recovery Act funded contract of 400,000 is awarded without competition. It is below the postaward dollar rule, but it is not both fixed-price and competitive, so the award notice must still be posted with the rationale and a statement that it was not awarded competitively.

## Never confuse

FAR subpart 5.7 applies to all actions expected to exceed $25,000 funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act, and unlike FAR subparts 5.2 and 5.3, it includes additional requirements for orders and for actions that are not both fixed-price and competitive [@CL-MKT-2228]. Under FAR 5.705(a)(1), the award notice is publicized for any action exceeding $500,000 funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act [@CL-MKT-0740; @CL-MKT-0742], whereas under FAR 5.705(b), regardless of dollar value, if the contract action, including all modifications and orders under task or delivery order contracts, is not both fixed-price and competitively awarded, the award notice is publicized and its description includes the rationale for using other than a fixed-priced and/or competitive approach [@CL-MKT-0743; @CL-MKT-9393]. General preaward publicizing rules and exceptions are on the [When contract actions must be publicized and the exceptions](mkt-publicizing-requirements-and-exceptions.md) card, and general award synopses are on the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card.
