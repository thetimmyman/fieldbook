## Purpose

Shows what is posted before and after award for actions funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act, and which usual exceptions the postaward rules replace.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0738] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.704; FAR 5.705 [@CL-MKT-0738; @CL-MKT-0739] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0738] |
| Applies to | Actions funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act [@CL-MKT-0738; @CL-MKT-0740] |

Notices of proposed contract actions are required for orders exceeding $25,000 that are funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act and issued under task or delivery order contracts, a requirement that does not include modifications to existing orders, which are covered postaward [@CL-MKT-0738]. The notices that the preaward section requires for certain orders are for informational purposes only, so the exception section at 5.203 does not apply to them, and contracting officers should concurrently use their usual solicitation practice [@CL-MKT-0741].

The postaward section directs the contracting officer to follow the usual publication procedures at 5.301, except that the listed paragraphs supersede the exceptions at 5.301(b)(2) through (7) [@CL-MKT-0739]. The contracting officer shall publicize the award notice for any action exceeding $500,000 that is funded in whole or in part by the Recovery Act, including the types of action listed after the dash [@CL-MKT-0740]. The list that follows the dollar rule names contracts, modifications to existing contracts, orders issued under task or delivery order contracts, and modifications to orders under task or delivery order contracts [@CL-MKT-0742; @CL-MKT-0740].

## How it works

The preaward notice and the postaward notice have different triggers. The preaward notice is tied to orders over a stated amount under task or delivery order contracts [@CL-MKT-0738], while the postaward notice is tied to a larger amount and covers contracts, orders and their modifications [@CL-MKT-0740; @CL-MKT-0742]. Regardless of dollar value, if a contract action, including all modifications and orders under task or delivery order contracts, is not both fixed-price and competitively awarded, the award notice must be publicized and its description must include the rationale [@CL-MKT-0743]. The description must include a statement specifically noting if the contract action was not awarded competitively, or was not fixed-price, or was neither competitive nor fixed-price [@CL-MKT-0744]. The postaward section itself sets no number of days for posting; it points to the usual publication procedures, and the timing for those is on the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card and the [Publicizing and response time](mkt-publicizing-and-response-time.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Recovery Act funded order of 30,000 is placed under a task order contract, and a second order of 20,000 is placed under the same kind of contract. The first exceeds the preaward notice amount and needs a notice, and the second does not. Separately, a Recovery Act funded contract of 400,000 is awarded without competition. It is below the postaward dollar rule, but it is not both fixed-price and competitive, so the award notice must still be posted with the rationale and a statement that it was not awarded competitively.

## Never confuse

The printed text sets rules for Recovery Act funded actions only. It does not establish rules for other emergency or contingency actions. The general notice rule and its exceptions are on the [When contract actions must be publicized and the exceptions](mkt-publicizing-requirements-and-exceptions.md) card, and the general award synopsis rule is on the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card. The rationale rule here applies regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0743], so it must not be mixed up with the dollar rules.
