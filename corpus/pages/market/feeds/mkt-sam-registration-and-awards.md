## Purpose

Shows who must be registered in the System for Award Management before an offer or quotation, how the contracting officer checks it and passes the identifier to the payment office, and where the solicitation provision and clause are used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0029] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1101 [@CL-MKT-1612]; FAR 4.1102; FAR 4.1103; FAR 4.1105 [@CL-MKT-0029; @CL-MKT-0031; @CL-MKT-0034]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-MKT-9226] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0029] |
| Applies to | Offerors and quoters, except for the listed exceptions [@CL-MKT-0029] |

Offerors and quoters are required to be registered in SAM at the time an offer or quotation is submitted in order to comply with the annual representations and certifications requirements, subject to the exceptions listed in the same paragraph [@CL-MKT-0029]. Unless the acquisition is exempt, the contracting officer shall verify that the offeror or quoter is registered in SAM at the time an offer or quotation is submitted [@CL-MKT-0031]. The FAR definitions section describes the System for Award Management as the primary Government repository for prospective Federal awardee and Federal awardee information and the centralized Government system for certain contracting, grants, and other assistance-related processes [@CL-MKT-9226].

## How it works

One item in the list of exceptions is contracts awarded without providing for full and open competition due to unusual or compelling urgency [@CL-MKT-0030]. For such an award, the contractor is required to be registered in SAM within 30 days after contract award, or at least three days prior to submission of the first invoice, whichever occurs first [@CL-MKT-0032]. On contractual documents transmitted to the payment office, the contracting officer shall provide the unique entity identifier, or, if applicable, the Electronic Funds Transfer indicator, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-MKT-0033]. The provision on the System for Award Management is inserted in all solicitations except when the registration exceptions apply [@CL-MKT-0034]. The maintenance clause is inserted in solicitations that contain that provision and in the resulting contracts [@CL-MKT-0035]. As used in the subpart on the System for Award Management, agreement means basic agreement, basic ordering agreement, or blanket purchase agreement [@CL-MKT-1612].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a 2,000,000 supply contract has no registration exception. The contracting officer checks that each offeror is registered when its offer arrives. A separate urgent award is made without full and open competition, and the first invoice is not due for 60 days. The contractor registers 20 days after award, which is within the allowed 30 days. When the award is sent to the payment office, the document carries the unique entity identifier.

## Never confuse

Registration in SAM and the unique entity identifier reported in FPDS are related but separate steps: the contracting officer verifies registration at the time of the offer unless the acquisition is exempt [@CL-MKT-0031] and reports the identifier on the contract action [@CL-MKT-0024]. Passing the identifier to the payment office is a separate step again [@CL-MKT-0033]. For how payment requests are routed, see the [Electronic submission of payment requests: Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md) card.
