## Purpose

Shows who must be registered in the System for Award Management before an offer or quotation, how the contracting officer checks it and passes the identifier to the payment office, and where the solicitation provision and clause are used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0029]; RFO model text [@CL-MKT-9276; @CL-MKT-9308; @CL-MKT-9309] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1101 [@CL-MKT-1612]; FAR 4.1102; FAR 4.1103; FAR 4.1105 [@CL-MKT-0029; @CL-MKT-0031; @CL-MKT-0034]; FAR 4.605 [@CL-MKT-0024]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-MKT-9226] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-MKT-9276; @CL-MKT-9308; @CL-MKT-9309] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0029; @CL-MKT-9276] |
| Applies to | Offerors and quoters, except for the exceptions in FAR 4.1102(a) [@CL-MKT-0029] |

Offerors and quoters are required to be registered in SAM at the time an offer or quotation is submitted in order to comply with the annual representations and certifications requirements, except for the cases listed in FAR 4.1102(a) [@CL-MKT-0029]. Unless the acquisition is exempt under FAR 4.1102(a), the contracting officer shall verify that the offeror or quoter is registered in SAM (see FAR 4.1103(b)) at the time an offer or quotation is submitted [@CL-MKT-0031]. Under FAR 2.101, System for Award Management (SAM) means the primary Government repository for prospective Federal awardee and Federal awardee information and the centralized Government system for certain contracting, grants, and other assistance-related processes [@CL-MKT-9226]. Under RFO FAR 2.101, System for Award Management (SAM) means the primary Government repository for prospective Federal awardee and Federal awardee information and the centralized Government system for certain contracting, grants, and other assistance-related processes [@CL-MKT-9276], and it includes (1) data collected from prospective Federal awardees required for the conduct of business with the Government, (2) prospective contractor-submitted annual representations and certifications in accordance with FAR subpart 4.12 [@CL-MKT-9308], and (3) identification of those parties excluded from receiving Federal contracts, certain subcontracts, and certain types of Federal financial and non-financial assistance and benefits [@CL-MKT-9309].

## How it works

Item (5) of the exceptions in FAR 4.1102(a) is contracts awarded without providing for full and open competition due to unusual or compelling urgency (see FAR 6.302-2) [@CL-MKT-0030]. If the contract action is being awarded in accordance with FAR 4.1102(a)(5), the contractor is required to be registered in SAM within 30 days after contract award, or at least three days prior to submission of the first invoice, whichever occurs first [@CL-MKT-0032]. The contracting officer shall, on contractual documents transmitted to the payment office, provide the unique entity identifier, or, if applicable, the Electronic Funds Transfer indicator, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-MKT-0033]. The provision at FAR 52.204-7, System for Award Management, is inserted in all solicitations except when the conditions in FAR 4.1102(a) apply [@CL-MKT-0034]. The clause at FAR 52.204-13, System for Award Management Maintenance, is inserted in solicitations that contain the provision at FAR 52.204-7, and resulting contracts [@CL-MKT-0035]. As used in FAR subpart 4.11, agreement means basic agreement, basic ordering agreement, or blanket purchase agreement [@CL-MKT-1612].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a 2,000,000 supply contract has no registration exception. The contracting officer checks that each offeror is registered when its offer arrives. A separate urgent award is made without full and open competition, and the first invoice is not due for 60 days. The contractor registers 20 days after award, which is within the allowed 30 days. When the award is sent to the payment office, the document carries the unique entity identifier.

## Never confuse

Unless the acquisition is exempt under FAR 4.1102(a), the contracting officer shall verify that the offeror or quoter is registered in SAM at the time an offer or quotation is submitted [@CL-MKT-0031]. Under FAR 4.605(b), the contracting officer shall identify and report a unique entity identifier for the successful offeror on a contract action [@CL-MKT-0024]. Under FAR 4.1103(d), the contracting officer shall, on contractual documents transmitted to the payment office, provide the unique entity identifier, or, if applicable, the Electronic Funds Transfer indicator, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-MKT-0033]. For how payment requests are routed, see the [Electronic submission of payment requests: Wide Area WorkFlow payment instructions](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-wide-area-workflow-payment-instructions.md) card.
