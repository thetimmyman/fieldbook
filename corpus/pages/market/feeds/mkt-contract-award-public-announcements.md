## Purpose

Shows the award value above which information is made available for public announcement, when it may be released, which contracts are excluded, and the separate threshold and reporting rules the Department of Defense applies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.303; DFARS 205.303 [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |
| Applies to | Awards over the stated value, and for DoD awards over the DoD threshold [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |

Contracting officers shall make information available on awards over $5.5 million, unless another dollar amount is specified in agency acquisition regulations, in sufficient time for the agency concerned to announce it by 5 in the afternoon Washington, DC time on the day of award [@CL-MKT-0043]. Agencies shall not release information on awards before the public release time of 5 in the afternoon Washington, DC time [@CL-MKT-0044].

## How it works

Contracts excluded from the announcement reporting requirement include those placed with the Small Business Administration under section 8(a) of the Small Business Act, those placed with foreign firms when the place of delivery or performance is outside the United States and its outlying areas, and those for which synopsis was exempted under FAR 5.202(a)(1) [@CL-MKT-0045; @CL-MKT-9381]. Agencies may also release information on contract awards to the local press or other media, and when local announcements are made for contract awards in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold, they shall include one of two alternatives of information: for awards after sealed bidding, a statement that the contract was awarded after competition by sealed bidding, the number of offers solicited and received, and the basis for selection (for example, the lowest responsible bidder), or, for awards after negotiation, the information prescribed by FAR 15.503(b), and after competitive negotiation (either price or design competition), a statement to this effect, and in general terms the basis for selection [@CL-MKT-0046; @CL-MKT-9382; @CL-MKT-9383]. For DoD awards, the threshold is $9 million, and all contractual actions, including modifications, that have a face value, excluding unexercised options, of more than $9 million are reported [@CL-MKT-0047]. For undefinitized contractual actions, the not-to-exceed (NTE) amount is reported, and later, if the definitized amount exceeds the NTE amount by more than $9 million, only the amount exceeding the NTE is reported [@CL-MKT-0048]. For indefinite delivery, time and material, labor hour, and similar contracts, the initial award is reported if the estimated face value, excluding unexercised options, is more than $9 million [@CL-MKT-0049]. Orders up to the estimated value are not reported, but after the estimated value is reached, subsequent modifications and orders that have a face value of more than $9 million are reported [@CL-MKT-0157]. The same work is not reported twice [@CL-MKT-0158].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency awards a 7,000,000 contract to a domestic firm. That is above the general announcement threshold, so the information is made available in time for an announcement on the day of award. If a DoD component makes the same award, it is below the DoD threshold, so the DoD rule does not require it to be reported. A DoD undefinitized action with a not-to-exceed amount of 12,000,000 is above the DoD threshold, so the not-to-exceed amount is reported. A DoD indefinite-delivery contract announced at an estimated value of 50,000,000 does not produce a new announcement for a 10,000,000 order placed within that estimate.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 5.303(a), contracting officers shall make information available on awards over $5.5 million (unless another dollar amount is specified in agency acquisition regulations) in sufficient time for the agency concerned to announce it by 5 in the afternoon Washington, DC, time on the day of award [@CL-MKT-0043], whereas under FAR 5.301(a)(1), except for contract actions described in FAR 5.301(b) and as provided in FAR 5.003, contracting officers must synopsize through the GPE contract awards exceeding $25,000 that are covered by the World Trade Organization Government Procurement Agreement or a Free Trade Agreement or are likely to result in the award of any subcontracts [@CL-MKT-0036; @CL-MKT-0037]. For DoD awards, the public announcement threshold is $9 million [@CL-MKT-0047], and orders up to the estimated value of an indefinite delivery, time and material, labor hour, or similar contract are not reported, but after the estimated value is reached, subsequent modifications and orders that have a face value of more than $9 million are reported [@CL-MKT-0049; @CL-MKT-0157]. For the simplified acquisition threshold amount, see [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
