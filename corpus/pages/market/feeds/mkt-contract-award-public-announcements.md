## Purpose

Shows the award value above which information is made available for public announcement, when it may be released, which contracts are excluded, and the separate threshold and reporting rules the Department of Defense applies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.303; DFARS 205.303 [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |
| Applies to | Awards over the stated value, and for DoD awards over the DoD threshold [@CL-MKT-0043; @CL-MKT-0047] |

Contracting officers shall make information available on awards over $5.5 million, unless another dollar amount is specified in agency acquisition regulations, in sufficient time for the agency concerned to announce it by 5 in the afternoon Washington, DC time on the day of award [@CL-MKT-0043]. Agencies shall not release information on awards before the public release time of 5 in the afternoon Washington, DC time [@CL-MKT-0044].

## How it works

Contracts excluded from the announcement reporting requirement include those placed with the Small Business Administration under section 8(a) of the Small Business Act those placed with foreign firms when the place of delivery or performance is outside the United States and its outlying areas, and those for which synopsis was exempted under FAR 5.202(a)(1) [@CL-MKT-0045; @CL-MKT-9381]. Agencies may also release award information to the local press or other media, and local announcements of contract awards in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold shall include one of two alternatives of information: for awards after sealed bidding, a statement that the contract was awarded after competition by sealed bidding, the number of offers solicited and received, and the basis for selection, or, for awards after negotiation, the information prescribed by FAR 15.503(b) and, after competitive negotiation, a statement to this effect and in general terms the basis for selection [@CL-MKT-0046; @CL-MKT-9382; @CL-MKT-9383]. For DoD, the threshold is $9 million, and the general rule is that contractual actions, including modifications, that have a face value, excluding unexercised options, of more than $9 million are to be reported [@CL-MKT-0047], subject to the qualifications for undefinitized and indefinite-delivery actions below. For undefinitized contractual actions the not-to-exceed amount is reported, and if the definitized amount later exceeds that amount by more than $9 million, only the amount exceeding it is reported [@CL-MKT-0048]. For indefinite delivery, time and material, labor hour, and similar contracts, the initial award is reported if the estimated face value, excluding unexercised options, is more than $9 million [@CL-MKT-0049]. Orders up to that estimated value are not reported; after the estimated value is reached, subsequent modifications and orders with a face value of more than $9 million are reported [@CL-MKT-0157]. The same work is not reported twice [@CL-MKT-0158].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency awards a 7,000,000 contract to a domestic firm. That is above the general announcement threshold, so the information is made available in time for an announcement on the day of award. If a DoD component makes the same award, it is below the DoD threshold, so the DoD rule does not require it to be reported. A DoD undefinitized action with a not-to-exceed amount of 12,000,000 is above the DoD threshold, so the not-to-exceed amount is reported. A DoD indefinite-delivery contract announced at an estimated value of 50,000,000 does not produce a new announcement for a 10,000,000 order placed within that estimate.

## Never confuse

A public announcement is not a synopsis of the award: the announcement rule works from an award-value threshold and a release time [@CL-MKT-0043], while the synopsis rule is separate and has its own dollar threshold [@CL-MKT-0036]. The DoD threshold is also a different figure from the general one [@CL-MKT-0047; @CL-MKT-0043], and a DoD order is not reported while it stays up to the estimated value of an indefinite-delivery contract [@CL-MKT-0157]. For the simplified acquisition threshold amount, see [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
