## Purpose

Shows which proposed contract actions need a notice transmitted to the Governmentwide point of entry, and which cases the contracting officer need not notice.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0057] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.000 to 5.003 [@CL-MKT-2213; @CL-MKT-2214; @CL-MKT-2216; @CL-MKT-2218]; FAR 5.101 [@CL-MKT-1843; @CL-MKT-0050]; FAR 5.201; FAR 5.202 [@CL-MKT-1894; @CL-MKT-0057; @CL-MKT-0060] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | Proposed acquisitions of supplies and services, other than those covered by the exceptions in FAR 5.202 and the special situations in FAR 5.205 [@CL-MKT-0057] |

As required by the Small Business Act (title 15 of the United States Code, section 637(e)) and title 41 of the United States Code, section 1708, agencies must make notices of proposed contract actions available as specified in FAR 5.201(b) [@CL-MKT-1894]. For acquisitions of supplies and services, other than those covered by the exceptions in FAR 5.202 and the special situations in FAR 5.205, the contracting officer must transmit a notice to the GPE for each proposed (i) contract action meeting the threshold in FAR 5.101(a)(1) [@CL-MKT-0057], (ii) modification to an existing contract for additional supplies or services that meets the threshold in FAR 5.101(a)(1) [@CL-MKT-0058], or (iii) contract action in any amount when advantageous to the Government [@CL-MKT-0059].

The contracting officer need not submit the notice required by FAR 5.201 when (a) the contracting officer determines that one of the conditions in FAR 5.202(a) applies [@CL-MKT-0060], or (b) the head of the agency determines in writing after consultation with the Administrator for Federal Procurement Policy and the Administrator of the Small Business Administration that advance notice is not appropriate or reasonable [@CL-MKT-9392]. Under FAR 5.202(a)(13), one listed condition is when the proposed contract action (i) is for an amount not expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, (ii) will be made through a means that provides access to the notice of proposed contract action through the GPE, and (iii) permits the public to respond to the solicitation electronically [@CL-MKT-0060; @CL-MKT-0061; @CL-MKT-1885].

## How it works

Under FAR 5.101(a)(1), as required by the Small Business Act (title 15 of the United States Code, section 637(e)) and title 41 of the United States Code, section 1708, contracting officers must disseminate information on proposed contract actions expected to exceed $25,000 by synopsizing in the GPE (see FAR 5.201) [@CL-MKT-1843; @CL-MKT-0050].

FAR part 5 prescribes policies and procedures for publicizing contract opportunities and award information [@CL-MKT-2213]. As used in FAR part 5, contract action means an action resulting in a contract, as defined in FAR subpart 2.1, including actions for additional supplies or services outside the existing contract scope [@CL-MKT-2214], but not including actions that are within the scope and under the terms of the existing contract, such as contract modifications issued pursuant to the Changes clause, or funding and other administrative changes [@CL-MKT-2215].

Contracting officers must publicize contract actions in order to (a) increase competition, (b) broaden industry participation in meeting Government requirements [@CL-MKT-2216], and (c) assist small business concerns, veteran-owned small business concerns, service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns, HUBZone small business concerns, small disadvantaged business concerns, and women-owned small business concerns in obtaining contracts and subcontracts [@CL-MKT-2217]. For any requirement in the FAR to publish a notice, the contracting officer must transmit the notices to the Governmentwide point of entry (GPE) [@CL-MKT-2218].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer adds 60,000 of supplies to an existing contract by modification, and the modification meets the synopsis threshold. A notice goes to the GPE. A separate action, expected at 18,000, will be made through a means that gives access to the notice through the GPE and lets the public respond electronically. It is below the simplified acquisition threshold, so the listed carve-out for such actions may apply.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 5.201(b)(1), for acquisitions of supplies and services other than those covered by the exceptions in FAR 5.202 and the special situations in FAR 5.205, the contracting officer must transmit a notice to the GPE for each proposed contract action meeting the threshold in FAR 5.101(a)(1), each modification to an existing contract for additional supplies or services that meets the threshold in FAR 5.101(a)(1), or each contract action in any amount when advantageous to the Government [@CL-MKT-0057; @CL-MKT-0058; @CL-MKT-0059]. Synopses of contract awards are covered on the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card. The dollar amount of the simplified acquisition threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.
