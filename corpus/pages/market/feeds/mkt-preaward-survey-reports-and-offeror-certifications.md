## Purpose

Shows who completes the preaward survey reports, what they cover, what an offeror certifies to be eligible for award, how a capability survey of a nonprofit agency in the AbilityOne Program is requested, and the separate funding prohibition for certain institutions of higher education.

## The rule

| Applies to | Surveyed prospective contractors, offerors for contract award, contracting offices asked for a capability survey, and institutions of higher education named in the prohibition [@CL-MKT-0749; @CL-MKT-0750; @CL-MKT-0786; @CL-MKT-0751] |

The surveying activity shall complete the applicable parts of the general, technical and production preaward survey forms, SF 1403, SF 1404 and SF 1405 [@CL-MKT-0749]. The listed preaward survey forms also include SF 1406 for quality assurance, SF 1407 for financial capability and SF 1408 for the accounting system [@CL-MKT-0752]. When the contractor surveyed is a small business that has received preferential treatment on an ongoing contract under section 8(a) of the Small Business Act or has received a Certificate of Competency during the last 12 months, the surveying activity shall consult [@CL-MKT-0788]. The surveying activity's consultation is with the appropriate Small Business Administration field office, and it takes place before making an affirmative recommendation regarding the contractor's responsibility or nonresponsibility [@CL-MKT-0789]. When a preaward survey discloses previous unsatisfactory performance, the surveying activity shall specify the extent to which the prospective contractor plans, or has taken, corrective action [@CL-MKT-0753]. When the surveying activity possesses information that supports a recommendation of complete award without an on-site survey and no special areas for investigation have been requested, the surveying activity may provide a short-form preaward survey report [@CL-MKT-0754].

The FAR definitions section describes a preaward survey as an evaluation of a prospective contractor's capability to perform a proposed contract [@CL-MKT-9228]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines a preaward survey as an evaluation of a prospective contractor's capability to perform a proposed contract, the same in substance as the codified definition [@CL-MKT-9272; @CL-MKT-9228].

In order to be eligible for contract award, an offeror is required to meet the conditions listed after the dash [@CL-MKT-0750]. The listed paragraphs offer a certification route and an alternative route [@CL-MKT-0755; @CL-MKT-0783]. One listed item on the certification route is a certification that the offeror does not engage and has not engaged in any activity that contributed to or was a significant factor in the President's or Secretary of State's determination [@CL-MKT-0755]. The determination that the certification concerns may be that a foreign country is in violation of its obligations undertaken in any arms control, nonproliferation, or disarmament agreement to which the United States is a party, or that it is not adhering to its arms control, nonproliferation, or disarmament commitments in which the United States is a participating state [@CL-MKT-0781; @CL-MKT-1811]. One listed item is that the offeror similarly certify with regard to any entity owned or controlled by the offeror [@CL-MKT-0782]. As an alternative to certifying, an offeror may provide with its offer information that the President of the United States has waived application under subsection (d) or (e) of section 2593e of title 22 of the United States Code or has determined under subsection (g)(2) of that section that the entity has ceased all activities for which measures were imposed under subsection (b) [@CL-MKT-0783]. The authority section 9.109-1 says that it implements section 2593e of title 22 of the United States Code [@CL-MKT-9164].

The contracting officer may rely on an offeror's certification unless the contracting officer has reason to question the certification [@CL-MKT-0785]. Upon the determination of a false certification, an offeror will be subject to such remedies as suspension or debarment under the debarment subpart, or termination of any contract resulting from the false certification [@CL-MKT-0756]. In the paragraph on false certification, debarments pursued as a remedy under the debarment subpart shall be for a period of not less than 2 years, inclusive of any suspension period, if suspension precedes a debarment [@CL-MKT-0784]. The debarment subpart itself is summarized on the [Debarment and suspension policy and definitions](mkt-debarment-policy-and-definitions.md) card.

## How it works

The surveying activity, not the offeror, prepares the survey reports, and the forms divide the review into general, technical, production, quality assurance, financial capability and accounting system parts [@CL-MKT-0749; @CL-MKT-0752]. The offeror, by contrast, certifies or provides the information described above [@CL-MKT-0750; @CL-MKT-0782; @CL-MKT-0783]. For a nonprofit agency in the AbilityOne Program, the contracting office, upon request from the Committee, shall request a capability survey from the activity responsible for performing preaward surveys, or notify the Committee that the agency is capable, with supporting rationale, and that the survey is waived [@CL-MKT-0786]. The contracting office shall use the Standard Form 1403 to request a capability survey of organizations employing people who are blind or have other severe disabilities [@CL-MKT-0787]. The printed funding rule for institutions of higher education is a prohibition on the covered agency, not a certification by an offeror: except as provided in the exception paragraph, the statute prohibits the covered agency from providing funds by contract to an institution of higher education if the Secretary of Defense determines that the institution has a policy or practice that prohibits or in effect prevents certain listed activities, which this card does not set out [@CL-MKT-0751]. The funding prohibition does not apply to an institution of higher education if the Secretary of Defense determines that the institution has ceased the policy or practice described in the prohibition [@CL-MKT-0790]. The authority section 9.110-2 says that it implements section 983 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-MKT-9165]. The exception paragraph holds a further exception, which this card does not set out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer asks for a preaward survey of a bidder that missed delivery dates on an earlier contract. The surveying activity completes the applicable survey forms and, because the earlier performance was unsatisfactory, states how far the bidder plans to correct it or already has. For a second bidder, the activity has information supporting complete award and no one has asked for special areas to be checked, so it may give a short-form report instead of an on-site survey.

## Never confuse

A preaward survey report is not the responsibility determination itself. The standards are on the [Contractor responsibility standards and their application](../../mna/screening/mna-contractor-responsibility-standards.md) card, and the information gathering and documentation are on the [Obtaining responsibility information and documenting the determination](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-responsibility-information-and-determination.md) card. The arms control certification and the funding prohibition for institutions of higher education are separate subjects [@CL-MKT-0755; @CL-MKT-0751]. What the arms control certification clauses say is on the [Arms control treaty certification and ROTC and military recruiting access](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-arms-control-certification-and-rotc-clauses.md) card.
