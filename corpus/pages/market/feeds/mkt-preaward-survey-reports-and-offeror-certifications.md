## Purpose

Shows who completes the preaward survey reports, what they cover, what an offeror certifies to be eligible for award, how a capability survey of a nonprofit agency in the AbilityOne Program is requested, and the separate funding prohibition for certain institutions of higher education.

The surveying activity shall complete the applicable parts of SF 1403, Preaward Survey of Prospective Contractor (General), SF 1404, Preaward Survey of Prospective Contractor—Technical, SF 1405, Preaward Survey of Prospective Contractor—Production, SF 1406, Preaward Survey of Prospective Contractor—Quality Assurance, SF 1407, Preaward Survey of Prospective Contractor—Financial Capability, and SF 1408, Preaward Survey of Prospective Contractor—Accounting System, and provide a narrative discussion sufficient to support both the evaluation ratings and the recommendations [@CL-MKT-0749; @CL-MKT-0752; @CL-MKT-2021]. When the contractor surveyed is a small business that has received preferential treatment on an ongoing contract under section 8(a) of the Small Business Act or has received a Certificate of Competency during the last 12 months, the surveying activity shall consult with the appropriate Small Business Administration field office before making an affirmative recommendation regarding the contractor's responsibility or nonresponsibility [@CL-MKT-0788; @CL-MKT-0789]. When a preaward survey discloses previous unsatisfactory performance, the surveying activity shall specify the extent to which the prospective contractor plans, or has taken, corrective action [@CL-MKT-0753]. Lack of evidence that past failure to meet contractual requirements was the prospective contractor's fault does not necessarily indicate satisfactory performance, and the narrative shall report any persistent pattern of need for costly and burdensome Government assistance (such as engineering, inspection, or testing) provided in the Government's interest but not contractually required [@CL-MKT-2075; @CL-MKT-2076]. When the surveying activity possesses information that supports a recommendation of complete award without an on-site survey and no special areas for investigation have been requested, the surveying activity may provide a short-form preaward survey report [@CL-MKT-0754]. The short-form report shall consist solely of the Preaward Survey of Prospective Contractor (General), SF 1403, and Sections III and IV of that form shall be completed and block 21 shall be checked to show that the report is a short-form preaward report [@CL-MKT-2077].

FAR 2.101 defines a preaward survey as an evaluation of a prospective contractor's capability to perform a proposed contract [@CL-MKT-9228]. Under RFO FAR 2.101, a preaward survey is an evaluation of a prospective contractor's capability to perform a proposed contract [@CL-MKT-9272].

In order to be eligible for contract award, an offeror is required to either (1)(i) certify that the offeror does not engage and has not engaged in any activity that contributed to or was a significant factor in the President's or Secretary of State's determination that a foreign country is in violation of its obligations undertaken in any arms control, nonproliferation, or disarmament agreement to which the United States is a party, or is not adhering to its arms control, nonproliferation, or disarmament commitments in which the United States is a participating state, where the determinations are described in the most recent unclassified annual report provided to Congress pursuant to section 403 of the Arms Control and Disarmament Act (section 2593a of title 22 of the United States Code), and (ii) similarly certify with regard to any entity owned or controlled by the offeror [@CL-MKT-0750; @CL-MKT-0755; @CL-MKT-0781; @CL-MKT-1811; @CL-MKT-2080; @CL-MKT-2081; @CL-MKT-0782], or (2) provide with its offer information that the President of the United States has (i) waived application under subsection (d) or (e) of section 2593e of title 22 of the United States Code, or (ii) determined under subsection (g)(2) of section 2593e of title 22 of the United States Code that the entity has ceased all activities for which measures were imposed under subsection (b) of that section [@CL-MKT-0783]. If certifying in accordance with 52.209-13(b)(1), the Offeror is required to submit the certification with the offer, and it is not included in the annual representations and certifications in the System for Award Management [@CL-MKT-2082]. Section 9.109-1 implements section 2593e of title 22 of the United States Code [@CL-MKT-9164].

The contracting officer may rely on an offeror's certification unless the contracting officer has reason to question the certification [@CL-MKT-0785]. Upon the determination of a false certification under 52.209-13, an offeror will be subject to such remedies as suspension or debarment under subpart 9.4, or termination of any contract resulting from the false certification, and debarments pursued as a remedy under subpart 9.4 shall be for a period of not less than 2 years, inclusive of any suspension period, if suspension precedes a debarment [@CL-MKT-0756; @CL-MKT-0784]. The debarment subpart itself is summarized on the [Debarment and suspension policy and definitions](mkt-debarment-policy-and-definitions.md) card.

## How it works

The surveying activity completes the applicable parts of the survey forms and provides the narrative discussion supporting the evaluation ratings and recommendations [@CL-MKT-0749; @CL-MKT-0752; @CL-MKT-2021], while the offeror certifies under paragraph (a)(1)(i) and (ii) or provides with its offer the waiver or cessation information under paragraph (a)(2) [@CL-MKT-0750; @CL-MKT-0755; @CL-MKT-2081; @CL-MKT-0782; @CL-MKT-0783]. For a nonprofit agency participating in the AbilityOne Program, the contracting office, upon request from the Committee for Purchase From People Who Are Blind or Severely Disabled, shall request a capability survey from the activity responsible for performing preaward surveys, or notify the Committee that the AbilityOne participating nonprofit agency is capable, with supporting rationale, and that the survey is waived [@CL-MKT-0786]. The capability survey will focus on the technical and production capabilities and applicable preaward survey elements to furnish specific supplies or services being considered for addition to the Procurement List [@CL-MKT-2078]. The contracting office shall use the Standard Form 1403 to request a capability survey of organizations employing people who are blind or have other severe disabilities [@CL-MKT-0787], and shall furnish a copy of the completed survey, or notice that the AbilityOne participating nonprofit agency is capable and the survey is waived, to the Executive Director of the Committee [@CL-MKT-2079].

Section 9.110-2 implements section 983 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-MKT-9165]. Except as provided in paragraph (b) of section 9.110-3, section 983 of title 10 of the United States Code prohibits the covered agency from providing funds by contract to an institution of higher education if the Secretary of Defense determines that the institution has a policy or practice that prohibits or in effect prevents (1) the Secretary of a military department from maintaining, establishing, or operating a unit of the Senior Reserve Officer Training Corps (ROTC) at that institution, (2) a student at that institution from enrolling in a unit of the Senior ROTC at another institution of higher education, (3) the Secretary of a military department or the Secretary of Homeland Security from gaining access to campuses, or access to students (who are 17 years of age or older) on campuses, for purposes of military recruiting in a manner that is at least equal in quality and scope to the access to campuses and to students that is provided to any other employer, or (4) military recruiters from accessing student (i) name, address, and telephone listings and (ii) date and place of birth, educational level, academic majors, degrees received, and the most recent educational institution enrolled in by the student, pertaining to students (who are 17 years of age or older) enrolled at that institution [@CL-MKT-0751; @CL-MKT-2083; @CL-MKT-2084; @CL-MKT-2085; @CL-MKT-2086]. The prohibition in paragraph (a) does not apply to an institution of higher education if the Secretary of Defense determines that (1) the institution has ceased the policy or practice described in paragraph (a), or (2) the institution has a long-standing policy of pacifism based on historical religious affiliation [@CL-MKT-0790; @CL-MKT-2087].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer asks for a preaward survey of a bidder that missed delivery dates on an earlier contract. The surveying activity completes the applicable survey forms and, because the earlier performance was unsatisfactory, states how far the bidder plans to correct it or already has. For a second bidder, the activity has information supporting complete award and no one has asked for special areas to be checked, so it may give a short-form report instead of an on-site survey.

## Never confuse

A preaward survey on SF 1403, SF 1404 and SF 1405 evaluates a specific prospective contractor's capability to perform a proposed contract [@CL-MKT-0749], rather than imposing an agency-wide exclusion like debarment or suspension on [Debarment and suspension policy and definitions](mkt-debarment-policy-and-definitions.md) [@CL-MKT-0771], and differs from an AbilityOne capability survey requested through the Committee [@CL-MKT-0786].
