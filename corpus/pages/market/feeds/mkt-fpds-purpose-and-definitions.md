## Purpose

Explains what the Federal Procurement Data System is for, what counts as a contract action, and which contract actions the regulation says must be publicly reported. A finance reader uses it to see what this public feed is required to hold and what it leaves out.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0007] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.600 to 4.603 [@CL-MKT-1543; @CL-MKT-0001; @CL-MKT-0005; @CL-MKT-0007] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0007] |
| Applies to | Unclassified contract actions exceeding the micro-purchase threshold, and modifications that change previously reported data [@CL-MKT-0007] |

The subpart prescribes uniform reporting requirements for the Federal Procurement Data System (FPDS) [@CL-MKT-1543]. Executive agencies shall use FPDS to maintain publicly available information about all unclassified contract actions exceeding the micro-purchase threshold, and any modifications to those actions that change previously reported contract action report data, regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0007]. A contract action is any oral or written action that results in the purchase, rent, or lease of supplies or equipment, services, or construction using appropriated dollars over the micro-purchase threshold, or a modification to such an action regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0001].

FPDS provides a comprehensive web-based tool for agencies to report contract actions, and the resulting data provides a basis for recurring and special reports to the President, the Congress, the Government Accountability Office, Federal executive agencies, and the general public [@CL-MKT-0005].

FAR 2.101 defines a micro-purchase as an acquisition of supplies or services using simplified acquisition procedures, the aggregate amount of which does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-MKT-9215]. The micro-purchase threshold is $15,000 with listed exceptions [@CL-FND-0002]. Two of the exceptions are $2,000 for acquisitions of construction subject to chapter 31, subchapter IV, of title 40 of the United States Code, Wage Rate Requirements (Construction) [@CL-MKT-9216], and $2,500 for acquisitions of services subject to chapter 67 of title 41 of the United States Code, Service Contract Labor Standards [@CL-MKT-9217]. This card does not set out the other exceptions, which the definition goes on to list in paragraphs (3) and (4), including acquisitions that, as determined by the head of the agency, are to be used to support a contingency operation or to facilitate defense against or recovery from cyber, nuclear, biological, chemical or radiological attack, and acquisitions from institutions of higher education or related or affiliated nonprofit entities, or from nonprofit research organizations or independent research institutes [@CL-MKT-9217; @CL-MKT-9311; @CL-MKT-9314; @CL-MKT-9315]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines a micro-purchase as an acquisition of supplies or services, the aggregate amount of which does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold, when using the procedures in subpart 12.4, where the codified definition says using simplified acquisition procedures [@CL-MKT-9268; @CL-MKT-9215].

## How it works

A contract action report is the contract action data that is required to be entered into the Federal Procurement Data System [@CL-MKT-0003]. An indefinite delivery vehicle is an indefinite delivery contract or agreement that has one or more of the listed clauses, such as the ordering, order limitations, definite quantity, requirements and indefinite quantity clauses [@CL-MKT-0004]. The definition of a contract action leaves out grants, cooperative agreements, other transactions, real property leases, requisitions from Federal stock, training authorizations and other non-FAR based transactions [@CL-MKT-0002]. The definition of a contract action covers modifications to these actions regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0001], while the duty to maintain public information covers modifications that change previously reported contract action report data [@CL-MKT-0007]. The dollar value of the micro-purchase threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier order of 2,000 is paid with appropriated dollars and falls below the micro-purchase threshold, so it is not a contract action under the definition. A later order of 400,000 is above the threshold, so it is a contract action and is reported. A modification of 500 to that order changes previously reported data, so it is also reported, whatever its dollar value.

## Never confuse

FPDS holds what agencies report about contract actions, and it does not provide reports for certain acquisition information used in the award, such as subcontracting data, funding data or accounting data [@CL-MKT-0006]. It is also not a record of every transaction the government enters into, because grants, cooperative agreements and real property leases are outside the definition of a contract action [@CL-MKT-0002]. For the threshold amounts, see [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
