## Purpose

Explains what the Federal Procurement Data System is for, what counts as a contract action, and which contract actions the regulation says must be publicly reported. A finance reader uses it to see what this public feed is required to hold and what it leaves out.

## The rule

The subpart prescribes uniform reporting requirements for the Federal Procurement Data System (FPDS) [@CL-MKT-1543]. Executive agencies shall use FPDS to maintain publicly available information about all unclassified contract actions exceeding the micro-purchase threshold, and any modifications to those actions that change previously reported contract action report data, regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0007]. A contract action means any oral or written action that results in the purchase, rent, or lease of supplies or equipment, services, or construction using appropriated dollars over the micro-purchase threshold, or modifications to these actions regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0001].

FPDS provides a comprehensive web-based tool for agencies to report contract actions, and the resulting data provides a basis for recurring and special reports to the President, the Congress, the Government Accountability Office, Federal executive agencies, and the general public [@CL-MKT-0005].

Under FAR 2.101, a micro-purchase means an acquisition of supplies or services using simplified acquisition procedures, the aggregate amount of which does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-MKT-9215]. Under RFO FAR 2.101, a micro-purchase means an acquisition of supplies or services, the aggregate amount of which does not exceed the micro-purchase threshold when using the procedures in subpart 12.4 [@CL-MKT-9268]. Under FAR 2.101, the micro-purchase threshold means $15,000, except it means: (1) $2,000 for acquisitions of construction subject to chapter 31, subchapter IV, of title 40 of the United States Code, Wage Rate Requirements (Construction) [@CL-FND-0002; @CL-MKT-9216], (2) $2,500 for acquisitions of services subject to chapter 67 of title 41 of the United States Code, Service Contract Labor Standards [@CL-MKT-9217], (3) for acquisitions of supplies or services that, as determined by the head of the agency, are to be used to support a contingency operation, to facilitate defense against or recovery from cyber, nuclear, biological, chemical or radiological attack [@CL-MKT-9311], to support a request from the Secretary of State or the Administrator of the United States Agency for International Development to facilitate provision of international disaster assistance pursuant to title 22 of the United States Code, section 2292 and following, or to support response to an emergency or major disaster (title 42 of the United States Code, section 5122), as described in FAR 13.201(g)(1), except for construction subject to chapter 31, subchapter IV, of title 40 of the United States Code, Wage Rate Requirements (Construction) (title 41 of the United States Code, section 1903), (i) $25,000 in the case of any contract to be awarded and performed, or purchase to be made, inside the United States, and (ii) $40,000 in the case of any contract to be awarded and performed, or purchase to be made, outside the United States [@CL-MKT-9312; @CL-MKT-9313; @CL-MKT-9314], and (4) for acquisitions of supplies or services from institutions of higher education (title 20 of the United States Code, section 1001(a)) or related or affiliated nonprofit entities, or from nonprofit research organizations or independent research institutes, (i) $15,000, or (ii) a higher threshold, as determined appropriate by the head of the agency and consistent with clean audit findings under chapter 75 of title 31 of the United States Code, Requirements for Single Audits, an internal institutional risk assessment, or State law [@CL-MKT-9314; @CL-MKT-9315].

## How it works

A contract action report (CAR) means contract action data required to be entered into the Federal Procurement Data System (FPDS) [@CL-MKT-0003]. An indefinite delivery vehicle (IDV) means an indefinite delivery contract or agreement that has one or more of the following clauses: (1) 52.216-18, Ordering, (2) 52.216-19, Order Limitations, (3) 52.216-20, Definite Quantity, (4) 52.216-21, Requirements, or (5) 52.216-22, Indefinite Quantity [@CL-MKT-0004]. Contract action does not include grants, cooperative agreements, other transactions, real property leases, requisitions from Federal stock, training authorizations, or other non-FAR based transactions [@CL-MKT-0002]. The definition of a contract action covers modifications to these actions regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0001], and executive agencies shall use FPDS to maintain publicly available information about all unclassified contract actions exceeding the micro-purchase threshold and any modifications to those actions that change previously reported contract action report data, regardless of dollar value [@CL-MKT-0007]. The dollar value of the micro-purchase threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier order of 2,000 is paid with appropriated dollars and falls below the micro-purchase threshold, so it is not a contract action under the definition. A later order of 400,000 is above the threshold, so it is a contract action and is reported. A modification of 500 to that order changes previously reported data, so it is also reported, whatever its dollar value.

## Never confuse

FPDS does not provide reports for certain acquisition information used in the award of a contract action (for example, subcontracting data, funding data, or accounting data) [@CL-MKT-0006]. Contract action does not include grants, cooperative agreements, other transactions, real property leases, requisitions from Federal stock, training authorizations, or other non-FAR based transactions [@CL-MKT-0002]. For the threshold amounts, see [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
