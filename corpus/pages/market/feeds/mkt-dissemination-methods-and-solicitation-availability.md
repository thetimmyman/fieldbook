## Purpose

Shows how contracting officers must spread the word about a proposed contract action, how a solicitation is made available once it is synopsized, and where the DoD solicitation module is covered.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0056] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.101; FAR 5.102; DFARS 205.102-70 [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0054; @CL-MKT-0056]; DFARS 215.205 [@CL-MKT-2097] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0056] |
| Applies to | Proposed contract actions, by expected value, and solicitations synopsized through the GPE [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0054] |

Contracting officers must disseminate information on proposed contract actions expected to exceed $25,000 by synopsizing in the GPE [@CL-MKT-0050]. For an action expected to exceed $20,000 but not $25,000, the listed method is to display an unclassified notice of the solicitation, or a copy of it, in a public place or by any appropriate electronic means [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0051]. The contracting officer need not comply with the display requirements when the listed exemptions apply, when oral solicitations are used [@CL-MKT-0150], or when access to the notice and solicitation is provided through the GPE and the notice permits the public to respond electronically [@CL-MKT-0250].

The displayed information must be posted not later than the date the solicitation is issued, and it must remain posted for at least 10 days or until after quotations have been opened, whichever is later [@CL-MKT-0051; @CL-MKT-0052].

## How it works

Above the higher dollar figure the GPE synopsis is the required method, and between the two figures the display notice applies, subject to the exemptions [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0051; @CL-MKT-0150]. Paid advertisements are an extra method with limits, and one limit is that contracting officers shall place them only when it is anticipated that effective competition cannot be obtained otherwise [@CL-MKT-0053]. Once a solicitation is synopsized through the GPE, the contracting officer must make it available through the GPE, with the specifications, technical data and other pertinent information the contracting officer determines necessary, except in the cases the section excepts [@CL-MKT-0054]. One listed exception to the requirement in paragraph (a)(4) of the section is when disclosure would compromise the national security or create other security risks [@CL-MKT-0055; @CL-MKT-9384]. The fact that access to classified matter may be necessary to submit a proposal or perform the contract does not, in itself, justify use of that exception [@CL-MKT-0055; @CL-MKT-0151]. For DoD solicitations, the DFARS sends the reader to procedural guidance on the Solicitation Module [@CL-MKT-0056]. The DFARS section on issuing solicitations, DFARS 215.205, also refers to the procedures at PGI 205.102-70 for use of the Solicitation Module within the Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment [@CL-MKT-2097].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer plans three actions, expected at 40,000, 22,000 and 15,000. The 40,000 action is synopsized in the GPE. The 22,000 action may instead be displayed in a public place or by electronic means, and the notice stays posted for at least 10 days or until quotations are opened, whichever is later. The 15,000 action is below the range these paragraphs cover.

## Never confuse

The display notice for the lower band is not a GPE synopsis: it is posted in a public place or by electronic means and stays up for at least 10 days or until after quotations have been opened [@CL-MKT-0051; @CL-MKT-0052]. The synopsis of an award is a different notice with its own rule, on the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card. The dollar amount of the simplified acquisition threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.
