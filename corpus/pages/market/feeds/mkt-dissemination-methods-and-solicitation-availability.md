## Purpose

Shows how contracting officers must spread the word about a proposed contract action, how a solicitation is made available once it is synopsized, and where the DoD solicitation module is covered.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0056] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.101; FAR 5.102; DFARS 205.102-70 [@CL-MKT-1843; @CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0054; @CL-MKT-0056]; DFARS 215.205 [@CL-MKT-2097] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0056] |
| Applies to | Proposed contract actions, by expected value, and solicitations synopsized through the GPE [@CL-MKT-0050; @CL-MKT-0054] |

As required by the Small Business Act (title 15 of the United States Code, section 637(e)) and title 41 of the United States Code, section 1708, contracting officers must disseminate information on proposed contract actions as follows: for proposed contract actions expected to exceed $25,000, by synopsizing in the GPE (see FAR 5.201) [@CL-MKT-1843; @CL-MKT-0050]. For proposed contract actions expected to exceed $20,000, but not expected to exceed $25,000, the method is displaying in a public place, or by any appropriate electronic means, an unclassified notice of the solicitation or a copy of the solicitation satisfying the requirements of FAR 5.207(c) [@CL-MKT-0051]. The contracting officer need not comply with the display requirements of FAR 5.101 when the exemptions at FAR 5.202(a)(1), (a)(4) through (a)(9), or (a)(11) apply, when oral solicitations are used [@CL-MKT-0150], or when providing access to a notice of proposed contract action and solicitation through the GPE and the notice permits the public to respond to the solicitation electronically [@CL-MKT-0250].

The displayed information must be posted not later than the date the solicitation is issued, and must remain posted for at least 10 days or until after quotations have been opened, whichever is later [@CL-MKT-0052].

## How it works

In addition to the methods in FAR 5.101(a), one or more of the methods in FAR 5.101(b) may be used, including placing paid advertisements in newspapers or other communications media, subject to the stated limitations, one of which is that contracting officers shall place paid advertisements of proposed contracts only when it is anticipated that effective competition cannot be obtained otherwise (see FAR 5.205(d)) [@CL-MKT-1844; @CL-MKT-0053]. Except as provided in FAR 5.102(a)(5), the contracting officer must make available through the GPE solicitations synopsized through the GPE, including specifications, technical data, and other pertinent information determined necessary by the contracting officer [@CL-MKT-0054]. Under FAR 5.102(a)(5), the contracting officer need not make a solicitation available through the GPE as required in FAR 5.102(a)(4) when (i) disclosure would compromise the national security (for example, would result in disclosure of classified information, or information subject to export controls) or create other security risks [@CL-MKT-0055; @CL-MKT-9384], (ii) the nature of the file (for example, size or format) does not make it cost-effective or practicable for contracting officers to provide access to the solicitation through the GPE [@CL-MKT-1874], or (iii) the agency's senior procurement executive makes a written determination that access through the GPE is not in the Government's interest [@CL-MKT-1889]. The fact that access to classified matter may be necessary to submit a proposal or perform the contract does not, in itself, justify use of the national security exception [@CL-MKT-0151]. For the availability of DoD solicitations, DFARS 205.102-70 directs seeing PGI 205.102-70 for policy and procedures related to the Solicitation Module within the Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment [@CL-MKT-0056]. DFARS 215.205 also directs seeing PGI 205.102-70 for use of the Solicitation Module within the Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment [@CL-MKT-2097].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer plans three actions, expected at 40,000, 22,000 and 15,000. The 40,000 action is synopsized in the GPE. The 22,000 action may instead be displayed in a public place or by electronic means, and the notice stays posted for at least 10 days or until quotations are opened, whichever is later. The 15,000 action is below the range these paragraphs cover.

## Never confuse

For proposed contract actions expected to exceed $25,000, contracting officers disseminate information by synopsizing in the GPE [@CL-MKT-0050], whereas for proposed contract actions expected to exceed $20,000, but not expected to exceed $25,000, contracting officers disseminate information by displaying in a public place, or by any appropriate electronic means, an unclassified notice of the solicitation or a copy of the solicitation satisfying the requirements of FAR 5.207(c), posted not later than the date the solicitation is issued and remaining posted for at least 10 days or until after quotations have been opened, whichever is later [@CL-MKT-0051; @CL-MKT-0052]. Synopses of contract awards are covered on the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card. The dollar amount of the simplified acquisition threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.
