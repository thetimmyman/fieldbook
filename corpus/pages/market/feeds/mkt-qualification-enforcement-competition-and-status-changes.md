## Purpose

Shows the contracting officer side of qualification requirements: when a list may be enforced, which offers are considered, what is sent to the establishing activity, and how changes in status are reported and notified.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0757] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.206-1; FAR 9.206-3; FAR 9.207 [@CL-MKT-0757; @CL-MKT-0758; @CL-MKT-0759] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0757] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions subject to a qualification requirement [@CL-MKT-0760] |

Agencies may not enforce any QPL, QML, or QBL without first complying with the requirements of 9.202(a), but qualification requirements themselves may be enforced without regard to 9.202(a) if they are in either of the listed categories [@CL-MKT-0757]. The two listed categories are any qualification requirement established by statute before October 30, 1984 for civilian agencies, not including NASA, and any qualification requirement established by statute or administrative action before October 19, 1984 for DOD or NASA [@CL-MKT-0800].

If a qualification requirement applies, the contracting officer need consider only those offers identified as meeting the requirement or included on the applicable QPL, QML, or QBL, unless an offeror can satisfactorily demonstrate something to the contracting officer about itself or its product [@CL-MKT-0760]. The demonstration that excuses an offeror from the offers-considered limit is that it or its product, or its subcontractor or its product, can meet the standards established for qualification before the date specified for award [@CL-MKT-0761].

## How it works

If a qualification requirement applies to an acquisition, the contracting officer shall review the applicable QPL, QML, or QBL or other identification of those sources which have met the requirement before issuing a solicitation to ascertain whether the number of sources is adequate for competition [@CL-MKT-0801]. After solicitation, the contracting officer shall submit to the agency activity that established the qualification requirement the names and addresses of concerns that expressed interest in the acquisition but are not listed or identified as meeting the requirement [@CL-MKT-0758]. The contracting officer shall promptly report to the agency activity which established the qualification requirement any conditions which may merit removal or omission from a QPL, QML, or QBL or affect whether a source should continue to be otherwise identified as meeting the requirement [@CL-MKT-0759]. After considering conditions reasonably related to whether a product or source continues to meet the standards specified for qualification, an agency may take appropriate action without advance notification [@CL-MKT-0802]. The agency shall promptly notify the affected parties if a product or source is removed from a QPL, QML, or QBL, or will no longer be identified as meeting the standards specified for qualification [@CL-MKT-0762]. The same sections also hold what the contracting officer asks the establishing activity to do when the number of sources is inadequate and the list of reportable conditions, which this card does not set out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation covers a part with a qualification requirement, and only two suppliers are on the qualified list. A third supplier can show, before the award date, that its product meets the standards. The contracting officer need not limit consideration to the two listed offers, because the third has made the demonstration. A fourth concern asked about the buy but cannot show it, so the contracting officer sends its name and address to the activity that set the requirement.

## Never confuse

This card covers the contracting officer duties around enforcement, competition and status, not how a requirement is established or who pays for qualification testing, which are on the [Qualification requirements and the opportunity to qualify before award](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-qualification-requirements.md) card. What an offeror shows before award under the clause is on the [Qualification requirements clause: what the offeror shows before award](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-qualification-requirements-clause.md) card. Removal from a list and enforcement of a requirement are different steps: one concerns whether a source stays listed [@CL-MKT-0759; @CL-MKT-0762], the other whether the list may be enforced at all [@CL-MKT-0757].
