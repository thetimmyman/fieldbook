## Purpose

Shows the contracting officer side of qualification requirements: when a list may be enforced, which offers are considered, what is sent to the establishing activity, and how changes in status are reported and notified.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0757] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.206-1; FAR 9.206-3; FAR 9.207 [@CL-MKT-0757; @CL-MKT-1886; @CL-MKT-1893; @CL-MKT-0758; @CL-MKT-0759] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0757] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions subject to a qualification requirement [@CL-MKT-0760] |

Agencies may not enforce any QPL, QML, or QBL without first complying with the requirements of FAR 9.202(a), but qualification requirements themselves, whether or not previously embodied in a QPL, QML, or QBL, may be enforced without regard to FAR 9.202(a) if they are in either of the following categories [@CL-MKT-0757; @CL-MKT-1886]: (1) any qualification requirement established by statute prior to October 30, 1984, for civilian agencies (not including NASA), or (2) any qualification requirement established by statute or administrative action prior to October 19, 1984, for DOD or NASA [@CL-MKT-0800]. Qualification requirements established after those dates must comply with FAR 9.202(a) to be enforceable [@CL-MKT-1893].

If a qualification requirement applies, the contracting officer need consider only those offers identified as meeting the requirement or included on the applicable QPL, QML, or QBL, unless an offeror can satisfactorily demonstrate to the contracting officer that it or its product or its subcontractor or its product can meet the standards established for qualification before the date specified for award [@CL-MKT-0760; @CL-MKT-0761].

## How it works

If a qualification requirement applies to an acquisition, the contracting officer shall review the applicable QPL, QML, or QBL or other identification of those sources which have met the requirement before issuing a solicitation to ascertain whether the number of sources is adequate for competition [@CL-MKT-0801]. The contracting officer shall also submit to the agency activity which established the qualification requirement the names and addresses of concerns which expressed interest in the acquisition but are not included on the applicable QPL, QML, or QBL or identified as meeting the qualification requirement [@CL-MKT-0758]. The contracting officer shall promptly report to the agency activity which established the qualification requirement any conditions which may merit removal or omission from a QPL, QML, or QBL or affect whether a source should continue to be otherwise identified as meeting the requirement [@CL-MKT-0759]. After considering any of the conditions in FAR 9.207(a) or other conditions reasonably related to whether a product or source continues to meet the standards specified for qualification, an agency may take appropriate action without advance notification [@CL-MKT-0802]. The agency shall, however, promptly notify the affected parties if a product or source is removed from a QPL, QML, or QBL, or will no longer be identified as meeting the standards specified for qualification, and this notice shall contain specific information why the product or source no longer meets the qualification requirement [@CL-MKT-0762].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation covers a part with a qualification requirement, and only two suppliers are on the qualified list. A third supplier can show, before the award date, that its product meets the standards. The contracting officer need not limit consideration to the two listed offers, because the third has made the demonstration. A fourth concern asked about the buy but cannot show it, so the contracting officer sends its name and address to the activity that set the requirement.

## Never confuse

Agencies may not enforce any QPL, QML, or QBL without first complying with the requirements of FAR 9.202(a), except that qualification requirements in the two categories in FAR 9.206-1(a)(1) and (2) (established before October 30, 1984, and before October 19, 1984, respectively) may be enforced without regard to FAR 9.202(a) [@CL-MKT-0757; @CL-MKT-1886; @CL-MKT-0800]. Under FAR 9.207, the contracting officer shall promptly report to the agency activity which established the qualification requirement any conditions which may merit removal or omission from a QPL, QML, or QBL or affect whether a source should continue to be otherwise identified as meeting the requirement [@CL-MKT-0759], and the agency shall promptly notify the affected parties if a product or source is removed from a QPL, QML, or QBL or will no longer be identified as meeting the standards specified for qualification [@CL-MKT-0762]. Establishment of qualification requirements is covered on the [Qualification requirements and the opportunity to qualify before award](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-qualification-requirements.md) card, and the clause is covered on the [Qualification requirements clause: what the offeror shows before award](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-qualification-requirements-clause.md) card.
