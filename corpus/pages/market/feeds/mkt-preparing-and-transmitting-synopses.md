## Purpose

Shows what a presolicitation notice and a synopsis must contain, who may post a notice of a subcontracting opportunity, and where the DoD numbering procedure is found.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0083] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.204; FAR 5.206; FAR 5.207; DFARS 205.207 [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0077; @CL-MKT-0081; @CL-MKT-0083] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0083] |
| Applies to | Presolicitation notices and synopses transmitted to the GPE, and notices of subcontracting opportunities [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0077] |

Contracting officers must provide access to presolicitation notices through the GPE [@CL-MKT-0075], and the contracting officer must synopsize a proposed contract action before issuing any resulting solicitation [@CL-MKT-0076]. Each synopsis transmitted to the GPE must address the listed data elements, as applicable, beginning with the action code, date, year, contracting office ZIP code and product or service code [@CL-MKT-0081].

The listed entities may transmit a notice to the GPE to seek competition for subcontracts, to increase participation by HUBZone small business, small, small disadvantaged, women-owned small business, veteran-owned small business and service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns, and to meet established subcontracting plan goals [@CL-MKT-0077; @CL-MKT-9390]. One listed entity is a contractor awarded a contract exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold that is likely to result in the award of any subcontracts [@CL-MKT-0077; @CL-MKT-0078]. Another is a subcontractor or supplier, at any tier, under a contract exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, that has a subcontracting opportunity exceeding $20,000 [@CL-MKT-0077; @CL-MKT-0079].

## How it works

The synopsis comes before the solicitation, and the presolicitation notice is reached through the GPE [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0076]. When the acquisition provides for a total or partial small business program set-aside or a local area set-aside, the contracting officer shall identify the type of set-aside in the synopsis and in the solicitation [@CL-MKT-0082]. A notice of a subcontracting opportunity must describe the business opportunity, any prequalification requirements, and where to obtain the technical data needed to respond [@CL-MKT-0077; @CL-MKT-0080]. For numbering synopsis notices, the DFARS directs the contracting officer to the procedures at PGI 205.207(a)(i) [@CL-MKT-0083].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A first-tier supplier under a prime contract above the simplified acquisition threshold has a subcontracting opportunity of 30,000. That exceeds the dollar figure in the rule for suppliers, so the supplier may transmit a notice to the GPE. The notice describes the opportunity, any prequalification requirements and where to get the technical data. Another supplier with an opportunity of 12,000 is below that figure.

## Never confuse

A synopsis of a proposed action is not a synopsis of an award, which has its own rule on the [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md) card. A notice of a subcontracting opportunity is also not the contracting officer's synopsis: it is a notice that the contractor or a lower-tier supplier may transmit to seek competition for subcontracts [@CL-MKT-0077]. The dollar amount of the simplified acquisition threshold is on the [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) card.
