## Purpose

Shows what a presolicitation notice and a synopsis must contain, who may post a notice of a subcontracting opportunity, and where the DoD numbering procedure is found.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0083] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.204; FAR 5.206; FAR 5.207; DFARS 205.207 [@CL-MKT-1882; @CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0077; @CL-MKT-0081; @CL-MKT-0083] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0083] |
| Applies to | Presolicitation notices and synopses transmitted to the GPE, and notices of subcontracting opportunities [@CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0077] |

Contracting officers must provide access to presolicitation notices through the GPE (see FAR 15.201 and 36.213-2), and the contracting officer must synopsize a proposed contract action before issuing any resulting solicitation (see FAR 5.201 and 5.203) [@CL-MKT-1882; @CL-MKT-0075; @CL-MKT-0076]. Each synopsis transmitted to the GPE must address the following data elements, as applicable, beginning with action code, date, year, contracting office ZIP code, and product or service code [@CL-MKT-0081].

The following entities may transmit a notice to the GPE to seek competition for subcontracts, to increase participation by HUBZone small business, small, small disadvantaged, women-owned small business, veteran-owned small business and service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns, and to meet established subcontracting plan goals [@CL-MKT-0077; @CL-MKT-9390]: (1) a contractor awarded a contract exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold that is likely to result in the award of any subcontracts [@CL-MKT-0078], and (2) a subcontractor or supplier, at any tier, under a contract exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, that has a subcontracting opportunity exceeding $20,000 [@CL-MKT-0079].

## How it works

When the acquisition provides for a total or partial small business program set-aside (see FAR subpart 19.5), or when the acquisition provides for a local area set-aside (see FAR 26.202), the contracting officer shall identify the type of set-aside in the synopsis and in the solicitation [@CL-MKT-0082]. Notices of subcontracting opportunities must describe (1) the business opportunity, (2) any prequalification requirements, and (3) where to obtain technical data needed to respond to the requirement [@CL-MKT-0080]. Under DFARS 205.207(a)(i), for numbering the synopsis notice, contracting officers follow the procedures at PGI 205.207(a)(i) [@CL-MKT-0083].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A first-tier supplier under a prime contract above the simplified acquisition threshold has a subcontracting opportunity of 30,000. That exceeds the dollar figure in the rule for suppliers, so the supplier may transmit a notice to the GPE. The notice describes the opportunity, any prequalification requirements and where to get the technical data. Another supplier with an opportunity of 12,000 is below that figure.

## Never confuse

A presolicitation synopsis must be published through the GPE before the contracting officer issues any resulting solicitation [@CL-MKT-1882], unlike a postaward notice on [Synopses of contract awards](mkt-contract-award-synopses.md), which publicizes covered awards exceeding $25,000 after award [@CL-MKT-0036].
