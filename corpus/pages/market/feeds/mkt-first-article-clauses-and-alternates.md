## Purpose

Shows which first article approval clause goes into a solicitation or contract, depending on whether the contractor or the Government conducts the test, and when each alternate is used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-MKT-0763] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.301; FAR 9.308-1; FAR 9.308-2 [@CL-MKT-9166; @CL-MKT-0763; @CL-MKT-0764] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0763] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts where a fixed-price contract is contemplated and first article approval is intended [@CL-MKT-0765; @CL-MKT-0766], and, in a clause of substantially the same form, where a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-MKT-0803; @CL-MKT-0804] |

When the contractor conducts the first article test, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.209-3, First Article Approval—Contractor Testing, in solicitations and contracts when the conditions stated next are met [@CL-MKT-0763; @CL-MKT-0765]. The conditions are that a fixed-price contract is contemplated and that it is intended that the contract require first article approval and that the contractor be required to conduct the first article testing [@CL-MKT-0763; @CL-MKT-0765].

When the Government conducts the test, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.209-4, First Article Approval—Government Testing, in solicitations and contracts when the conditions stated next are met [@CL-MKT-0764; @CL-MKT-0766]. The conditions are that a fixed-price contract is contemplated and that it is intended that the contract require first article approval and that the Government will be responsible for conducting the first article test [@CL-MKT-0764; @CL-MKT-0766].

For a cost-reimbursement contract, the contracting officer shall insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at 52.209-3, First Article Approval—Contractor Testing, in solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated and it is intended that the contract require first article approval and that the contractor be required to conduct the first article test [@CL-MKT-0803; @CL-MKT-9385]. For a cost-reimbursement contract, the contracting officer shall likewise insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at 52.209-4, First Article Approval—Government Testing, in solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated and it is intended that the contract require first article approval and that the Government be responsible for conducting the first article test [@CL-MKT-0804; @CL-MKT-9386].

Section 9.301 says that, as used in its subpart, approval means the contracting officer's written notification to the contractor accepting the test results of the first article [@CL-MKT-9166].

## How it works

Each clause has two alternates, chosen by the facts. If it is intended that the contractor be required to produce the first article and the production quantity at the same facility, the contracting officer shall use the contractor-testing clause with its Alternate I [@CL-MKT-0763; @CL-MKT-0767], and the basic Government-testing clause with its Alternate I [@CL-MKT-0764; @CL-MKT-0768]. If it is necessary to authorize the contractor to purchase material or to commence production before first article approval, the contracting officer shall use the contractor-testing clause with its Alternate II [@CL-MKT-0763; @CL-MKT-0769], and the basic Government-testing clause with its Alternate II [@CL-MKT-0764; @CL-MKT-0770]. The same sections also have separate paragraphs for cost-reimbursement contracts, and those paragraphs have their own alternates, which this card does not set out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buyer plans a fixed-price contract for a new housing part and wants the supplier to test the first article. The 52.209-3 clause is used. The supplier will make the first article and the production lot in one plant, so Alternate I applies. If the buyer also needed the supplier to order material before approval, the condition for Alternate II would be met under its own paragraph. If the Government were to run the test, the 52.209-4 clause and its alternates would be used instead.

## Never confuse

Which clause applies depends on who conducts the test, not on whether first article approval is wanted. The contractor-testing clause and the Government-testing clause have different numbers and conditions [@CL-MKT-0763; @CL-MKT-0764], and each alternate has its own trigger [@CL-MKT-0767; @CL-MKT-0769]. When first article testing and approval is appropriate, and the waiver and exceptions, are on the [First article testing and approval: use, exceptions and waiver](../../industrialization/ramp/ind-first-article-testing-and-approval.md) card. The notice steps, the effect of approval or disapproval and the cost rules inside the clauses are on the [First article approval clauses: contractor testing and government testing](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-first-article-approval-clauses.md) card.
