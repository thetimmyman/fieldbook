## Purpose

Shows who is responsible for each contract action report, how soon after award it must be complete, and the extra rules the Department of Defense adds for contract writing systems, the contract file and annual certification.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0008; @CL-MKT-0013] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.604; DFARS 204.604 [@CL-MKT-0008; @CL-MKT-0013]; DFARS 204.602 [@CL-MKT-1544] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0008; @CL-MKT-0013] |
| Applies to | Contract action reports for awarded contract actions, and DoD components for the DoD rules [@CL-MKT-0008; @CL-MKT-0015] |

The contracting officer who awarded the contract action is responsible for the completion and accuracy of the individual contract action report, and a report in a draft or error status in FPDS is not considered complete [@CL-MKT-0008]. The contracting officer must confirm the report for accuracy before the contract award is released, and the report must then be completed in FPDS within three business days after contract award [@CL-MKT-0009]. A separate paragraph sets a longer period for certain actions: for any action awarded in accordance with FAR 6.302-2 or pursuant to any of the authorities listed at subpart 18.2, the report must be completed in FPDS within 30 days after contract award [@CL-MKT-0010].

## How it works

When the contracting officer receives written notification that a contractor has changed its size status under the postaward small business rerepresentation clause, the contracting officer shall update the size status in FPDS within 30 days after receipt of the notification [@CL-MKT-0011]. The chief acquisition officer of each agency required to report must submit an annual certification to the General Services Administration within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year [@CL-MKT-0012]. For DoD, the reporting process should, where possible, be automated by incorporating it into contract writing systems [@CL-MKT-0013]. Under the DFARS, the contracting officer may reference the report approval date in the contract file instead of including a paper copy of the electronically submitted report [@CL-MKT-0014]. The chief acquisition officer of each DoD component required to report shall submit its annual certification and data validation results by December 15th of each year [@CL-MKT-0015]. The DFARS general section says to see PGI 204.602 for additional information on FPDS and procedures for resolving technical or policy issues relating to FPDS [@CL-MKT-1544].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer awards a 500,000 contract on a Monday under full competition. The report is confirmed before the award is released and is completed by Thursday, which is within three business days. If the same award had been made under one of the authorities with the longer period, completion within 30 days would have been allowed.

## Never confuse

The three business day deadline and the 30 day deadline are set in separate paragraphs, and the longer one applies to actions awarded under the listed authorities [@CL-MKT-0009; @CL-MKT-0010]. The size-status update has its own 30 day deadline, which runs from receipt of the contractor's notification and not from award [@CL-MKT-0011]. The agency annual certification is not the DoD component certification, which has its own December date [@CL-MKT-0012; @CL-MKT-0015].
