## Purpose

Shows how orders for paid advertisements are placed, priced, supported with proof and paid, and how basic ordering agreements with advertising agencies are used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0731; @CL-MKT-9162] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.503; FAR 5.504; DFARS 205.502(a) [@CL-MKT-0731; @CL-MKT-0732; @CL-MKT-9162] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0731] |
| Applies to | Orders for paid advertisements placed directly with media or through an advertising agency [@CL-MKT-0731] |

Orders for paid advertisements may be placed directly with the media or through an advertising agency [@CL-MKT-0731]. The contracting officer shall use the SF 1449 for paper solicitations, and shall use it to make awards or place orders unless the award or order is made by electronic commerce or by the Governmentwide commercial purchase card for micropurchases [@CL-MKT-0733]. Advertisements may be paid for at rates not over the commercial rates charged private individuals, with the usual discounts [@CL-MKT-0734].

Every invoice for advertising shall be accompanied by a copy of the advertisement or an affidavit of publication furnished by the publisher, radio or television station, or advertising agency concerned [@CL-MKT-0735]. Upon receipt of an invoice supported by proof of advertising, the contracting officer shall attach a copy of the written authority and submit the invoice for payment under agency procedures [@CL-MKT-0736]. The paragraph points to the written authority by a cross-reference to another section and does not itself set out what that authority contains [@CL-MKT-0736].

## How it works

An order may go straight to the media or through an advertising agency [@CL-MKT-0731]. Basic ordering agreements may be placed with advertising agencies for assistance in producing and placing advertisements when a significant number will be placed in several publications and in national media [@CL-MKT-0732]. If the advertising agency will not agree to place advertisements at no cost, the agreement shall either provide that the Government may place orders directly with the media or specify an amount that the Government will pay if the agency places the orders [@CL-MKT-0737]. The invoice then goes forward with proof of advertising and a copy of the written authority [@CL-MKT-0735; @CL-MKT-0736].

In the subpart on paid advertising, advertisement means any single message prepared for placement in communication media, regardless of the number of placements [@CL-MKT-2223]. Publication means the placement of an advertisement in a newspaper, magazine, trade or professional journal, or any other printed medium, or the broadcasting of an advertisement over radio or television [@CL-MKT-2224].

Authority to approve the publication of paid advertisements in newspapers is vested in the head of each agency under title 44 of the United States Code, section 3702, and this approval authority may be delegated under title 5 of the United States Code, section 302(b) [@CL-MKT-2225]. Contracting officers shall obtain written authorization in accordance with agency procedures before advertising in newspapers, and, unless the agency head determines otherwise, advance written authorization is not required to place advertisements in media other than newspapers [@CL-MKT-2226]. In the defense supplement, heads of contracting activities are delegated authority to approve the publication of paid advertisements in newspapers, and the text names no official who delegates it [@CL-MKT-9162].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program office places one advertisement through an agency that holds a basic ordering agreement. The commercial rate charged private individuals, after the usual discounts, is 11,000. The agency invoices 11,000 and attaches an affidavit of publication from the publisher. The contracting officer attaches the written authority and sends the invoice for payment. The answer: the rate is not over the commercial rate, the proof is present, and the invoice may go forward. An invoice for 13,000 would be over the commercial rate and would not fit the pricing rule.

## Never confuse

Paid advertising is not the notice of a proposed contract action. The notice of a proposed action is covered on the [When contract actions must be publicized and the exceptions](mkt-publicizing-requirements-and-exceptions.md) card. A basic ordering agreement with an agency is also not an order for one advertisement; the text ties the agreement to a significant number of advertisements across several publications and national media [@CL-MKT-0732]. Whether the cost of advertising is allowable is a separate question, covered on the [Public relations and advertising costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-public-relations-and-advertising-costs.md) card.
