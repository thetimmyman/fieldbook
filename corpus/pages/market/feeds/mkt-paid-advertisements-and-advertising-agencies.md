## Purpose

Shows how orders for paid advertisements are placed, priced, supported with proof and paid, and how basic ordering agreements with advertising agencies are used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-MKT-0731; @CL-MKT-9162] |
| Codified citation | FAR 5.501; FAR 5.502; FAR 5.503; FAR 5.504; DFARS 205.502(a) [@CL-MKT-2223; @CL-MKT-2225; @CL-MKT-0731; @CL-MKT-0732; @CL-MKT-9162] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-MKT-0731] |
| Applies to | Orders for paid advertisements placed directly with media or through an advertising agency [@CL-MKT-0731] |

Orders for paid advertisements may be placed directly with the media or through an advertising agency [@CL-MKT-0731]. The contracting officer shall use the SF 1449 for paper solicitations, and the SF 1449 shall be used to make awards or place orders unless the award or order is made by using electronic commerce or by using the Governmentwide commercial purchase card for micropurchases [@CL-MKT-0733]. Advertisements may be paid for at rates not over the commercial rates charged private individuals, with the usual discounts (title 44 of the United States Code, section 3703) [@CL-MKT-1880; @CL-MKT-0734].

Every invoice for advertising shall be accompanied by a copy of the advertisement or an affidavit of publication furnished by the publisher, radio or television station, or advertising agency concerned (title 44 of the United States Code, section 3703) [@CL-MKT-1881; @CL-MKT-0735]. Upon receipt of an invoice supported by proof of advertising, the contracting officer shall attach a copy of the written authority (see FAR 5.502(a)) and submit the invoice for payment under agency procedures [@CL-MKT-0736].

## How it works

Basic ordering agreements may be placed with advertising agencies for assistance in producing and placing advertisements when a significant number will be placed in several publications and in national media [@CL-MKT-0732]. If the advertising agency will not agree to place advertisements at no cost, the agreement shall either provide that the Government may place orders directly with the media, or specify an amount that the Government will pay if the agency places the orders [@CL-MKT-0737].

As used in FAR subpart 5.5, advertisement means any single message prepared for placement in communication media, regardless of the number of placements [@CL-MKT-2223]. Publication means the placement of an advertisement in a newspaper, magazine, trade or professional journal, or any other printed medium, or the broadcasting of an advertisement over radio or television [@CL-MKT-2224].

Authority to approve the publication of paid advertisements in newspapers is vested in the head of each agency under title 44 of the United States Code, section 3702, and this approval authority may be delegated under title 5 of the United States Code, section 302(b) [@CL-MKT-2225]. Contracting officers shall obtain written authorization in accordance with agency procedures before advertising in newspapers, and, unless the agency head determines otherwise, advance written authorization is not required to place advertisements in media other than newspapers [@CL-MKT-2226]. Under DFARS 205.502(a), heads of contracting activities are delegated authority to approve the publication of paid advertisements in newspapers [@CL-MKT-9162].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A program office places one advertisement through an agency that holds a basic ordering agreement. The commercial rate charged private individuals, after the usual discounts, is 11,000. The agency invoices 11,000 and attaches an affidavit of publication from the publisher. The contracting officer attaches the written authority and sends the invoice for payment. The answer: the rate is not over the commercial rate, the proof is present, and the invoice may go forward. An invoice for 13,000 would be over the commercial rate and would not fit the pricing rule.

## Never confuse

Orders for paid advertisements may be placed directly with the media or through an advertising agency [@CL-MKT-0731], and basic ordering agreements may be placed with advertising agencies for assistance in producing and placing advertisements when a significant number will be placed in several publications and in national media [@CL-MKT-0732]. Contracting officers shall obtain written authorization in accordance with agency procedures before advertising in newspapers, whereas, unless the agency head determines otherwise, advance written authorization is not required to place advertisements in media other than newspapers [@CL-MKT-2226]. Notices of proposed contract actions are covered on the [When contract actions must be publicized and the exceptions](mkt-publicizing-requirements-and-exceptions.md) card, and allowability of advertising costs is covered on the [Public relations and advertising costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-public-relations-and-advertising-costs.md) card.
