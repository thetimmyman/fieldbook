## Purpose

Covers the notice steps, time limits and interest rule when the auditor reports an alleged noncompliance with Cost Accounting Standards to the CFAO.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0617] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.605 [@CL-RTE-0617] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0617] |
| Applies to | Reports of alleged noncompliance that the CFAO receives from the auditor [@CL-RTE-0617] |

Within 15 days of receiving a report of alleged noncompliance from the auditor, the CFAO shall either (i) notify the auditor that the CFAO disagrees with the alleged noncompliance, or (ii) issue a notice of potential noncompliance to the contractor and provide a copy to the auditor [@CL-RTE-0617]. The notice of potential noncompliance shall (i) notify the contractor in writing of the exact nature of the noncompliance, and (ii) allow the contractor 60 days or other mutually agreeable date to (A) agree or submit reasons why the contractor considers the existing practices to be in compliance, and (B) submit rationale to support any written statement that the cost impact of the noncompliance is immaterial [@CL-RTE-0618; @CL-RTE-0025].

## How it works

If the CFAO makes a determination of noncompliance, the CFAO shall follow the procedures in paragraphs (c) through (h) of FAR 30.605, as appropriate, unless the CFAO also determines the cost impact is immaterial [@CL-RTE-9155]. The clause at 52.230-6 requires the contractor to submit a description of any cost accounting practice change needed to correct a noncompliance within 60 days after the earlier of (i) agreement with the CFAO that there is a noncompliance, or (ii) notification by the CFAO of a determination of noncompliance [@CL-RTE-0027; @CL-RTE-0619]. The CFAO shall (1) separately identify interest on any increased cost paid, in the aggregate, as a result of the noncompliance, and (2) compute interest from the date of overpayment to the date of repayment using the rate specified in section 6621(a)(2) of title 26 of the United States Code [@CL-RTE-0620].

For noncompliances that involve estimating costs, the cost impact calculation shall determine the increased or decreased cost to the Government for fixed-price contracts and subcontracts, and when the negotiated contract or subcontract price exceeds what the negotiated price would have been had the contractor used a compliant practice, the difference is increased cost to the Government [@CL-RTE-0028; @CL-RTE-0724; @CL-RTE-0621].

If the CFAO makes a determination of noncompliance and also determines the cost impact is immaterial, the CFAO shall (i) inform the contractor in writing that (A) the noncompliance should be corrected, and (B) if the noncompliance is not corrected, the Government reserves the right to make appropriate contract adjustments should the noncompliance become material in the future, and (ii) conclude the cost-impact process with no contract adjustments [@CL-RTE-9155; @CL-RTE-0704; @CL-RTE-0029]. If the contractor does not correct the noncompliance or submit the proposal required in paragraph (d) or (f) of FAR 30.605 within the specified time, or any extension granted by the CFAO, the CFAO shall follow the procedures at 30.604(i) [@CL-RTE-0723].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The auditor reports an alleged estimating noncompliance, and the CFAO issues a notice of potential noncompliance to the contractor 10 days later. The CFAO then determines that there is a noncompliance, and the contractor describes its correction within 60 days. The noncompliance raised negotiated prices on fixed-price contracts by 100,000 above what a compliant practice would have produced, which is increased cost to the Government. The 100,000 was repaid after twelve months, and the interest computed under the statutory rate is 6,000, identified separately from the 100,000.

## Never confuse

If a contractor implements a change in cost accounting practice without submitting the notice as required in paragraph (c)(1) of FAR 30.603-2, the CFAO may determine the change a failure to follow a cost accounting practice consistently and process it as a noncompliance in accordance with 30.605 [@CL-RTE-0712; @CL-RTE-0040]. When the CFAO determines the cost impact is immaterial, the CFAO shall (1) make no contract adjustments and conclude the cost impact process, (2) document the rationale for the determination, and (3) in the case of noncompliance issues, inform the contractor that (i) the noncompliance should be corrected and (ii) if the noncompliance is not corrected, the Government reserves the right to make appropriate contract adjustments should the cost impact become material in the future [@CL-RTE-0603; @CL-RTE-0024]. See [Processing cost accounting practice changes](rte-processing-accounting-changes-gdm-dci.md) and [CAS materiality](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-materiality.md).
