## Purpose

Explains how the CFAO asks for, receives and evaluates general dollar magnitude (GDM) and detailed cost-impact (DCI) proposals for a change in cost accounting practice, and what follows if the contractor does not submit them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0718] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.604 [@CL-RTE-0718] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0718] |
| Applies to | Required, unilateral and desirable changes in cost accounting practices [@CL-RTE-0718] |

Upon receipt of the contractor's notification and description of the change in cost accounting practice, the CFAO should review the proposed change concurrently for adequacy and compliance, and if the description of the change is both adequate and compliant, the CFAO shall notify the contractor in writing [@CL-RTE-0022; @CL-RTE-0715] and either (i) for required or unilateral changes (except those requested to be determined desirable changes), request the contractor submit a general dollar magnitude (GDM) proposal by a specified date, unless the CFAO determines the cost impact is immaterial [@CL-RTE-0716]; or (ii) for unilateral changes that the contractor requests to be determined desirable changes, inform the contractor that the request shall include supporting rationale [@CL-RTE-9149] and either (A) for any request based on the criteria in 30.603-2(b)(3)(ii), the data necessary to demonstrate the required cost savings [@CL-RTE-9150], or (B) for any request other than those based on the criteria in 30.603-2(b)(3)(ii), a GDM proposal and any other data necessary for the CFAO to determine if the change is a desirable change [@CL-RTE-9151].

The contractor may submit a detailed cost-impact (DCI) proposal in lieu of a GDM proposal provided the DCI proposal is in accordance with paragraph (g) of FAR 30.604 [@CL-RTE-0612]. The CFAO shall promptly evaluate the GDM proposal; if the cost impact is immaterial, the CFAO shall notify the contractor in writing and conclude the cost-impact process with no contract adjustments, and otherwise the CFAO shall take one of two alternatives [@CL-RTE-9152]: (1) negotiate and resolve the cost impact (see 30.606), and if necessary the CFAO may request that the contractor submit a revised GDM proposal by a specified date with specific additional data needed to resolve the cost impact (for example, an expanded sample of affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts or a revised method of computing the increase or decrease in cost accumulations) [@CL-RTE-9153; @CL-RTE-0023]; or (2) request that the contractor submit a DCI proposal by a specified date if the CFAO determines that the GDM proposal is not sufficient to resolve the cost impact [@CL-RTE-0717].

## How it works

The GDM proposal provides information to the CFAO on the estimated overall impact of a change in cost accounting practice on affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts that were awarded based on the previous cost accounting practice [@CL-RTE-0713]. The DCI proposal shall calculate the cost impact in accordance with paragraph (h) of FAR 30.604 and shall show the estimated increase or decrease in cost accumulations for each affected CAS-covered contract and subcontract unless the CFAO and contractor agree to (i) include only those affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts exceeding a specified amount and (ii) estimate the total increase or decrease in cost accumulations for all affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts, using the results in paragraph (g)(2)(i) of FAR 30.604 [@CL-RTE-0714; @CL-RTE-9154]. The cost impact calculation shall include all affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts regardless of their status (open or closed) or the fiscal year(s) in which the costs are incurred (whether or not the final indirect rates have been established) [@CL-RTE-0613].

For unilateral changes, the cost impact calculation shall determine the increased or decreased cost to the Government for flexibly-priced contracts and subcontracts [@CL-RTE-0613; @CL-RTE-0719] and for fixed-price contracts and subcontracts [@CL-RTE-0720]. For flexibly-priced contracts and subcontracts, when the estimated cost to complete using the changed practice exceeds the estimated cost to complete using the current practice, the difference is increased cost to the Government [@CL-RTE-0614]. For fixed-price contracts and subcontracts, when the estimated cost to complete using the changed practice is less than the estimated cost to complete using the current practice, the difference is increased cost to the Government [@CL-RTE-0615].

If the contractor does not submit the accounting change description or the proposals required in paragraph (d) or (g) of FAR 30.604 within the specified time, or any extension granted by the CFAO, the CFAO shall (1) estimate the general dollar magnitude of the cost impact on affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts [@CL-RTE-0721; @CL-RTE-0026], and (2) take one or both of the following actions: (i) withhold an amount not to exceed 10 percent of each subsequent payment related to the contractor's CAS-covered contracts (up to the estimated general dollar magnitude of the cost impact), until the contractor furnishes the required information [@CL-RTE-0616]; and (ii) issue a final decision in accordance with 33.211 and unilaterally adjust the contract(s) by the estimated amount of the cost impact [@CL-RTE-0722].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor makes a unilateral change and submits a GDM proposal covering its affected CAS-covered contracts, open and closed. Under the changed practice the estimated cost to complete rises by 50,000 on flexibly-priced contracts and falls by 30,000 on fixed-price contracts. Under the section's tests both differences are increased cost to the Government, so the increased cost is 80,000, before any effect on incentives, fees and profits. Had the contractor missed the date the CFAO set, the CFAO could have withheld up to 10 percent of each later payment on the covered contracts, up to the estimated general dollar magnitude, until the information arrived.

## Never confuse

The GDM proposal provides information to the CFAO on the estimated overall impact of a change in cost accounting practice on affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts that were awarded based on the previous cost accounting practice [@CL-RTE-0713]. The DCI proposal shall calculate the cost impact in accordance with paragraph (h) of FAR 30.604 and shall show the estimated increase or decrease in cost accumulations for each affected CAS-covered contract and subcontract unless the CFAO and contractor agree to (i) include only those affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts exceeding a specified amount and (ii) estimate the total increase or decrease in cost accumulations for all affected CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts, using the results in paragraph (g)(2)(i) of FAR 30.604 [@CL-RTE-0714; @CL-RTE-9154]. When the description of the change is both adequate and compliant, for required or unilateral changes (except those requested to be determined desirable changes), the CFAO shall request the contractor submit a GDM proposal by a specified date, unless the CFAO determines the cost impact is immaterial [@CL-RTE-0022; @CL-RTE-0715; @CL-RTE-0716], and when the CFAO evaluates the GDM proposal and the cost impact is not immaterial, the alternative to negotiating and resolving the cost impact is to request that the contractor submit a DCI proposal by a specified date if the CFAO determines that the GDM proposal is not sufficient to resolve the cost impact [@CL-RTE-9152; @CL-RTE-9153; @CL-RTE-0023; @CL-RTE-0717]. The contractor may submit a DCI proposal in lieu of a GDM proposal provided the DCI proposal is in accordance with paragraph (g) of FAR 30.604 [@CL-RTE-0612]. For materiality, see [CAS materiality](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-materiality.md). For how the Defense Contract Audit Agency tests cost impact proposals, see [DCAA audits that test Cost Accounting Standards compliance](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-dcaa-cas-audits.md).
