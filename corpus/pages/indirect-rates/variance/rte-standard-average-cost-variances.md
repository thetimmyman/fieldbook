## Purpose

Shows when standard costs, average costs and pre-established labor rates may be used for direct costs, and how their variances are disposed of.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-RTE-0650] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.418-50(a)(2) and (h) [@CL-RTE-0650; @CL-RTE-0654] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0650] |
| Applies to | Business units that use standard costs, average costs or pre-established labor rates for direct costs [@CL-RTE-0650; @CL-RTE-0651] |

In accounting for direct costs a business unit shall use actual costs, except for the two alternative exceptions that follow [@CL-RTE-9156]. Under the first, standard costs may be used for estimating, accumulating and reporting costs of direct material and direct labor only when standard costs and related variances are appropriately accounted for at the level of the production unit and under the standard costing paragraph [@CL-RTE-0650]. A production unit is a grouping of activities that uses homogeneous inputs of direct material and direct labor or yields homogeneous outputs, so that the related costs or statistics are appropriate bases for allocating variances [@CL-RTE-0659].

Under the second exception, an average cost or pre-established labor rate may be used provided that the functions are not materially disparate and the employees are interchangeable, or that the functions are materially disparate but the employees either all work in one production unit yielding homogeneous outputs or perform their respective functions as an integral team [@CL-RTE-0651; @CL-RTE-0652]. Whenever such rates are used, the variances, if material, shall be disposed of at least annually by allocation to cost objectives in proportion to the costs previously allocated to them [@CL-RTE-0653].

## How it works

A labor-rate standard for a group of workers who perform disparate functions is allowed only in one of two cases [@CL-RTE-0654]. In the first case the group works in one production unit yielding homogeneous outputs, and the same labor-rate standard shall be applied to each worker [@CL-RTE-0655]. In the second case the group forms an integral team, and a labor-rate standard shall be set for each integral team [@CL-RTE-0656]. A contractor's established practice for variances accumulated by production unit shall be in accordance with one of two listed practices [@CL-RTE-0751]. The first allocates variances to cost objectives, including ending in-process inventory, at least annually [@CL-RTE-0657]. A variance related to material is then allocated on material cost at standard or, where outputs are homogeneous, on units of output [@CL-RTE-0657], and a variance related to labor on labor cost at standard, labor hours at standard or, where outputs are homogeneous, units of output [@CL-RTE-0752]. The second lets immaterial variances be included in appropriate indirect cost pools for allocation to applicable cost objectives [@CL-RTE-0658]. Under paragraphs (g)(2) and (g)(3) of the standard, where variances of a cost accounting period are material, these variances shall be disposed of by allocating them to cost objectives in proportion to the costs previously allocated to those cost objectives by use of the preestablished rates [@CL-RTE-0660].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A production unit sets a labor-rate standard for one integral team. Labor at standard cost 400,000 for the year, and actual labor cost 420,000, so the variance is 20,000. Contract A carries 300,000 of the labor at standard and ending in-process inventory carries 100,000. The variance is allocated on labor cost at standard: 15,000 to Contract A and 5,000 to ending inventory.

## Never confuse

A pre-established labor rate is not a pre-established indirect rate. The labor rate sits among the direct cost rules and depends on interchangeable employees, production units or integral teams [@CL-RTE-0651; @CL-RTE-0652]. The indirect rate covers a pool, and under paragraphs (g)(2) and (g)(3) its material variances are allocated to cost objectives in proportion to the costs previously allocated by the preestablished rates [@CL-RTE-0660]. For the wider direct versus indirect rule, see [Direct costs](../pools-bases/rte-direct-costs.md) and [the CAS direct and indirect cost card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-418-direct-indirect.md).
