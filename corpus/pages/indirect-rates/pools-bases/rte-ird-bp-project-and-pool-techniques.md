## Purpose

Shows what goes into an independent research and development or bid and proposal project, how the pools are formed, and which base spreads them at a home office and at a business unit.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-RTE-0688] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.420-50 [@CL-RTE-0688] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0688] |
| Applies to | IR&D and B&P project costs and segment cost pools [@CL-RTE-0687; @CL-RTE-0688] |

The IR&D and B&P project costs shall include (1) costs which, if incurred in like circumstances for a final cost objective, would be treated as direct costs of that final cost objective, and (2) the overhead costs of productive activities and other indirect costs related to the project based on the contractor's cost accounting practice or applicable Cost Accounting Standards for allocation of indirect costs [@CL-RTE-0687; @CL-RTE-0753]. The IR&D and B&P cost pools for a segment consist of the project costs plus allocable home office IR&D and B&P costs [@CL-RTE-0688]. When the costs of individual IR&D or B&P efforts are not material in amount, these costs may be accumulated in one or more projects within each of these two types of effort [@CL-RTE-0689].

The costs of IR&D and B&P projects accumulated at a home office shall be allocated to its segments as follows: (1) projects which can be identified with a specific segment or segments shall have their costs allocated to such segment or segments [@CL-RTE-0690]; and (2) the costs of all other IR&D and B&P projects shall be allocated among all segments by means of the same base used by the company to allocate its residual expenses in accordance with 9904.403, provided, however, that where a particular segment receives significantly more or less benefit from the IR&D or B&P costs than would be reflected by the allocation of such costs to the segment by the base, the Government and the contractor may agree to a special allocation of the IR&D or B&P costs to such segment commensurate with the benefits received [@CL-RTE-0691; @CL-RTE-0693; @CL-RTE-0031].

## How it works

The IR&D and B&P cost pools for a segment consist of the project costs plus allocable home office IR&D and B&P costs [@CL-RTE-0688]. For projects accumulated at a home office, projects which can be identified with a specific segment or segments shall have their costs allocated to such segment or segments, and the costs of all other IR&D and B&P projects shall be allocated among all segments by means of the same base used by the company to allocate its residual expenses in accordance with 9904.403, provided, however, that where a particular segment receives significantly more or less benefit from the IR&D or B&P costs than would be reflected by the allocation of such costs to the segment by the base, the Government and the contractor may agree to a special allocation of the IR&D or B&P costs to such segment commensurate with the benefits received [@CL-RTE-0690; @CL-RTE-0691; @CL-RTE-0693; @CL-RTE-0031]. For projects accumulated at a business unit, the IR&D and B&P cost pools which are not allocated under subparagraph (f)(1) of 48 CFR 9904.420-50 shall be allocated to all final cost objectives of the business unit by means of the same base used by the business unit to allocate its general and administrative expenses in accordance with 9904.410-50, provided, however, that where a particular final cost objective receives significantly more or less benefit from IR&D or B&P cost than would be reflected by the allocation of such costs, the Government and the contractor may agree to a special allocation of the IR&D or B&P costs to such final cost objective commensurate with the benefits received [@CL-RTE-0021; @CL-RTE-0692; @CL-RTE-0030; @CL-RTE-0754].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A home office has two IR&D projects. Project 1 costs 40,000 and is identified with Segment X, so Segment X is allocated 40,000. Project 2 costs 100,000 and is not identified with one segment. The residual expense base puts 70 percent on Segment X and 30 percent on Segment Y, so Project 2 allocates 70,000 to Segment X and 30,000 to Segment Y.

## Never confuse

For IR&D and B&P projects accumulated at a home office, the costs of all projects other than those that can be identified with a specific segment or segments shall be allocated among all segments by means of the same base used by the company to allocate its residual expenses in accordance with 9904.403, provided, however, that where a particular segment receives significantly more or less benefit from the IR&D or B&P costs than would be reflected by the allocation of such costs to the segment by the base, the Government and the contractor may agree to a special allocation of the IR&D or B&P costs to such segment commensurate with the benefits received [@CL-RTE-0690; @CL-RTE-0691; @CL-RTE-0693; @CL-RTE-0031]. For projects accumulated at a business unit, the IR&D and B&P cost pools which are not allocated under subparagraph (f)(1) of 48 CFR 9904.420-50 shall be allocated to all final cost objectives of the business unit by means of the same base used by the business unit to allocate its general and administrative expenses in accordance with 9904.410-50, provided, however, that where a particular final cost objective receives significantly more or less benefit from IR&D or B&P cost than would be reflected by the allocation of such costs, the Government and the contractor may agree to a special allocation of the IR&D or B&P costs to such final cost objective commensurate with the benefits received [@CL-RTE-0021; @CL-RTE-0692; @CL-RTE-0030; @CL-RTE-0754].
