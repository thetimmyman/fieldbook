## Purpose

Shows how indirect costs are grouped into pools and spread over an allocation base, and what period the allocation covers.

## The rule

| Field | Value |

| Codified citation | FAR 31.203 [@CL-RTE-0206] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.203 [@CL-RTE-0879; @CL-RTE-0880; @CL-RTE-0881; @CL-RTE-0882; @CL-RTE-0883; @CL-RTE-0884; @CL-RTE-0885; @CL-RTE-0886; @CL-RTE-0887; @CL-RTE-0888; @CL-RTE-0889; @CL-RTE-0890] |

After direct costs have been determined and charged directly to the contract or other work, indirect costs are those remaining to be allocated to intermediate or two or more final cost objectives [@CL-RTE-0207]. No final cost objective shall have allocated to it as an indirect cost any cost, if other costs incurred for the same purpose, in like circumstances, have been included as a direct cost of that or any other final cost objective [@CL-RTE-9134; @CL-RTE-0208]. The contractor shall accumulate indirect costs by logical cost groupings with due consideration of the reasons for incurring such costs, shall determine each grouping so as to permit use of an allocation base that is common to all cost objectives to which the grouping is to be allocated, and the base selected shall allocate the grouping on the basis of the benefits accruing to intermediate and final cost objectives [@CL-RTE-0209; @CL-RTE-0210; @CL-RTE-0019].

Once an appropriate base for allocating indirect costs has been accepted, the contractor shall not fragment the base by removing individual elements, and all items properly includable in an indirect cost base shall bear a pro rata share of indirect costs irrespective of their acceptance as Government contract costs [@CL-RTE-0211; @CL-RTE-0212]. For contracts subject to full or modified CAS coverage, the contractor shall follow the criteria and guidance in 48 CFR 9904.406 for selecting the cost accounting periods to be used in allocating indirect costs [@CL-RTE-0260]. For contracts other than those subject to paragraph (g)(1) of FAR 31.203, the base period for allocating indirect costs shall be the contractor's fiscal year used for financial reporting purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, and the fiscal year will normally be 12 months, though a different period may be appropriate, such as when a change in fiscal year occurs due to a business combination or other circumstances [@CL-RTE-0213; @CL-RTE-0261].

FAR 2.101 defines an indirect cost as any cost not directly identified with a single final cost objective, but identified with two or more final cost objectives or with at least one intermediate cost objective [@CL-RTE-9075]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines an indirect cost as any cost not directly identified with a single final cost objective, but identified with two or more final cost objectives or with at least one intermediate cost objective [@CL-RTE-9093].

## How it works

For contracts subject to full CAS coverage, allocation of indirect costs shall be based on the applicable provisions, and for all other contracts, the applicable CAS provisions in paragraphs (b) through (h) of FAR 31.203 apply [@CL-RTE-0206]. After direct costs have been determined and charged directly to the contract or other work, indirect costs are those remaining to be allocated to intermediate or two or more final cost objectives [@CL-RTE-0207]. The contractor shall accumulate indirect costs by logical cost groupings with due consideration of the reasons for incurring such costs, shall determine each grouping so as to permit use of an allocation base that is common to all cost objectives to which the grouping is to be allocated, and the base selected shall allocate the grouping on the basis of the benefits accruing to intermediate and final cost objectives [@CL-RTE-0209; @CL-RTE-0210; @CL-RTE-0019]. Once an appropriate base for allocating indirect costs has been accepted, the contractor shall not fragment the base by removing individual elements, and all items properly includable in an indirect cost base shall bear a pro rata share of indirect costs irrespective of their acceptance as Government contract costs [@CL-RTE-0211; @CL-RTE-0212]. For contracts other than those subject to paragraph (g)(1) of FAR 31.203, the base period for allocating indirect costs shall be the contractor's fiscal year used for financial reporting purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, which will normally be 12 months, though a different period may be appropriate, such as when a change in fiscal year occurs due to a business combination or other circumstances [@CL-RTE-0213; @CL-RTE-0261].

Contracting officers must use the FAR part 31, Contract Cost Principles and Procedures, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under the class deviation that revises and supersedes Class Deviation 2026-O0032, issued on December 19, 2025 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.203(a)(1) and (2), for contracts subject to full CAS coverage, allocation of indirect costs must be based on the applicable CAS regulations [@CL-RTE-0879], and for all other contracts, paragraphs (b) through (h) of RFO FAR 31.203 apply [@CL-RTE-0880]. Under RFO FAR 31.203(b)(1) and (2), after direct costs have been determined and charged directly to the contract or other work, indirect costs are those remaining to be allocated to intermediate or two or more final cost objectives [@CL-RTE-0881], and no final cost objective must have allocated to it as an indirect cost any cost, if other costs incurred for the same purpose, in like circumstances, have been included as a direct cost of that or any other final cost objective [@CL-RTE-0882]. Under RFO FAR 31.203(c)(1) through (3), the contractor must accumulate indirect costs by logical cost groupings with due consideration of the reasons for incurring such costs [@CL-RTE-0883], must determine each grouping so as to permit use of an allocation base that is common to all cost objectives to which the grouping is to be allocated [@CL-RTE-0884], and the base selected must allocate the grouping on the basis of the benefits accruing to intermediate and final cost objectives [@CL-RTE-0885]. Under RFO FAR 31.203(d)(1) and (2), once an appropriate base for allocating indirect costs has been accepted, the contractor must not fragment the base by removing individual elements [@CL-RTE-0886], and all items properly includable in an indirect cost base must bear a pro rata share of indirect costs irrespective of their acceptance as Government contract costs (for example, when a cost input base is used for the allocation of G&A costs, the contractor must include in the base all items that would properly be part of the cost input base, whether allowable or unallowable, and these items must bear their pro rata share of G&A costs) [@CL-RTE-0887; @CL-RTE-0020]. Under RFO FAR 31.203(g)(1) through (3), for contracts subject to full or modified CAS coverage, the contractor shall follow the criteria and guidance in 48 CFR 9904.406 for selecting the cost accounting periods to be used in allocating indirect costs [@CL-RTE-0888], the base period for allocating indirect costs must be the contractor's fiscal year used for financial reporting purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles [@CL-RTE-0889], and the fiscal year will normally be 12 months, but a different period may be appropriate, such as when a change in fiscal year occurs due to a business combination or other circumstances [@CL-RTE-0890].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company has 100,000 of overhead in one pool after direct costs are charged. The pool's allocation base is total direct labor, which is 500,000 across all jobs. One contract carries 50,000 of that direct labor, so it absorbs 10,000 of overhead. The base keeps every direct-labor dollar in it, allowed or not, and each bears its pro rata share.
