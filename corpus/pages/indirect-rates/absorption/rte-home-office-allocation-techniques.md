## Purpose

Shows how home office expenses are grouped, how centralized service functions are allocated to segments, and which formula applies to residual expenses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-RTE-0680] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.403-50 [@CL-RTE-0680; @CL-RTE-0682] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0680] |
| Applies to | Centralized service functions and residual expenses allocated to segments under a home office [@CL-RTE-0682; @CL-CMP-0555] |

Ordinarily, each service or management function will have to be separately identified for allocation by means of an appropriate allocation technique, but it is not necessary to identify and allocate different functions separately if allocation in accordance with the relevant requirements of 9904.403-40(b) can be made using a common allocation base [@CL-RTE-0680; @CL-RTE-0681].

The allocation of centralized service functions shall be governed by a hierarchy of preferable allocation techniques which represent beneficial or causal relationships, and the preferred representation of such relationships is a measure of the activity of the organization performing the function [@CL-RTE-0682]. Residual expenses shall be allocated to all segments under a home office by means of a base representative of the total activity of such segments, except where paragraph (c)(2) or (3) of 9904.403-40 applies [@CL-CMP-0555]. Where residual expenses are required to be allocated pursuant to 9904.403-40(c)(2), the three factor formula must be used [@CL-RTE-0686]. Residual expenses shall be allocated pursuant to 9904.403-50(c)(1) if the total amount of such expenses for the contractor's previous fiscal year (excluding any unallowable costs and before eliminating any amounts to be allocated in accordance with paragraph (c)(3) of 9904.403-40) exceeds the amount obtained by applying the following percentages to the aggregate revenue of all segments for that previous year: 3.35 percent of the first $100 million, 0.95 percent of the next $200 million, 0.30 percent of the next $2.7 billion, and 0.20 percent of all amounts over $3 billion [@CL-CMP-0630; @CL-CMP-0631; @CL-CMP-0556].

## How it works

Supporting functions are usually labor-oriented, machine-oriented, or space-oriented, and measures of the activities of such functions ordinarily can be expressed in terms of labor hours, machine hours, or square footage [@CL-RTE-0750]. The costs of these functions shall be allocated by use of a rate, such as a rate per labor hour, rate per machine hour or cost per square foot, unless such measures are unavailable or impractical to ascertain, in which case the basis for allocation shall be a measurement of the output of the supporting function [@CL-RTE-0683; @CL-RTE-0684]. Where neither activity nor output of the supporting function can be practically measured, a surrogate for the beneficial or causal relationship must be selected, and any surrogate used should be a reasonable measure of the services received and, logically, should vary in proportion to the services received [@CL-RTE-0685; @CL-RTE-0015].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A home office personnel department costs 60,000 a year. It both runs personnel services and sets personnel policy for the same segments, so one base serves both functions and they need not be split. Segment X has 100 employees and Segment Y has 200. On the number of personnel as the base, Segment X is allocated 20,000 and Segment Y is allocated 40,000.

## Never confuse

The allocation of centralized service functions shall be governed by a hierarchy of preferable allocation techniques which represent beneficial or causal relationships, where the preferred representation is a measure of the activity of the organization performing the function, the basis for allocation shall be a measurement of the output of the supporting function when activity-rate measures are unavailable or impractical to ascertain, and a surrogate for the beneficial or causal relationship must be selected where neither activity nor output of the supporting function can be practically measured [@CL-RTE-0682; @CL-RTE-0683; @CL-RTE-0684; @CL-RTE-0685]. Residual expenses shall be allocated to all segments under a home office by means of a base representative of the total activity of such segments, except where paragraph (c)(2) or (3) of 9904.403-40 applies [@CL-CMP-0555], and where residual expenses are required to be allocated pursuant to 9904.403-40(c)(2), the three factor formula must be used [@CL-RTE-0686]. For the underlying allocation requirements, see [the CAS home office card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-403-home-office-allocation.md).
