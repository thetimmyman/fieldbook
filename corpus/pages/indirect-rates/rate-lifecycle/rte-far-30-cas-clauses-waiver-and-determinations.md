## Purpose

Names the CAS clauses the FAR tells the contracting officer to insert, says who may grant a waiver, and states the determination on a Disclosure Statement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |

| Codified citation | FAR 30.000 [@CL-RTE-1000; @CL-RTE-1001], FAR 30.001, 30.201-4, 30.201-5 and 30.202-7 [@CL-RTE-0874; @CL-RTE-0841; @CL-RTE-0848; @CL-RTE-0849], and FAR 30.202-1, 30.202-2 and 30.202-3 [@CL-RTE-1005; @CL-RTE-1006; @CL-RTE-1007] |

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.230-2, Cost Accounting Standards, in negotiated contracts, unless the contract is exempted, the contract is subject to modified coverage, or the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) of that subsection is used [@CL-RTE-0841]. The clause at FAR 52.230-3, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, goes in negotiated contracts when the amount is over $2.5 million but less than $50 million [@CL-RTE-0842], and the offeror certifies it is eligible for and elects modified CAS coverage, unless the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) is used [@CL-RTE-0843]. The clause at 52.230-4, the Foreign Concerns version, goes in negotiated contracts with foreign concerns, unless the contract is otherwise exempt from CAS [@CL-RTE-0844]. Foreign concerns do not include foreign governments or their agents or instrumentalities [@CL-RTE-9120]. The clause at FAR 52.230-5, the Educational Institution version, goes in negotiated contracts awarded to educational institutions [@CL-RTE-0845], and is not inserted when the contract is exempted, is to be performed by an FFRDC, or the provision at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies [@CL-RTE-0846]. The clause at FAR 52.230-6, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards, goes in contracts containing any of the clauses prescribed in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), or (e) [@CL-RTE-0847].

The head of the agency may waive the applicability of CAS for a particular contract or subcontract under the conditions listed in paragraph (b) of the waiver section, and must not delegate this waiver authority to any official in the agency below the senior contract policymaking level [@CL-RTE-0848]. The cognizant Federal agency official (CFAO) is the contracting officer assigned by the cognizant Federal agency to administer the CAS [@CL-RTE-0874]. The CFAO shall determine if the Disclosure Statement adequately describes the contractor's cost accounting practices [@CL-RTE-0849]. Generally, the CFAO should furnish the contractor notification of adequacy or inadequacy within 30 days after the CFAO receives the Disclosure Statement [@CL-RTE-0873].

## How it works

Part 30 describes policies and procedures for applying the Cost Accounting Standards Board rules and regulations (48 CFR chapter 99) to negotiated contracts and subcontracts [@CL-RTE-1000]. It does not apply to sealed bid contracts or to any contract with a small business concern, (see 48 CFR 9903.201-1(b) for these and other exemptions) [@CL-RTE-1001].

FAR 30.202-1, General requirements: See 48 CFR 9903.202-1 [@CL-RTE-1005]. FAR 30.202-2, Impracticality of submission: See 48 CFR 9903.202-2 [@CL-RTE-1006]. FAR 30.202-3, Amendments and revisions: See 48 CFR 9903.202-3 [@CL-RTE-1007].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.230-6, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards, in contracts containing any of the clauses prescribed in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), or (e) of that subsection [@CL-RTE-0847]. For the waiver conditions and the request contents, see the [CAS waiver card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-waiver.md). The Administration clause is covered on the [Administration of Cost Accounting Standards card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-administration-of-cost-accounting-standards.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency official below the senior contract policymaking level signs a CAS waiver for a 10 million contract. Under the section the head of the agency must not delegate waiver authority below that level, so the section does not allow that delegation.

## Never confuse

FAR 30.201-4(b)(1): insert the clause at FAR 52.230-3, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, in negotiated contracts when the contract amount is over $2.5 million but less than $50 million [@CL-RTE-0842]. 48 CFR 9903.201-4(c)(1): insert the clause set forth below, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, in negotiated contracts when the contract amount is over the Truth in Negotiations Act threshold, as adjusted for inflation, but less than $100 million [@CL-RTE-0803]. In both, the offeror certifies it is eligible for and elects to use modified CAS coverage [@CL-RTE-0843; @CL-RTE-0804]. For the Cost Accounting Standards clause, FAR 30.201-4(a)(1) applies unless the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) of that subsection is used [@CL-RTE-0841], and 48 CFR 9903.201-4(a)(1) applies unless the clause prescribed in paragraph (e) of that section is used [@CL-RTE-0802]. For the Disclosure and Consistency clause, FAR 30.201-4(b)(1) refers to the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) [@CL-RTE-0843], and 48 CFR 9903.201-4(c)(1) refers to the clause prescribed in paragraph (d) of that subsection [@CL-RTE-0804]; paragraph (d) is [Reserved] [@CL-RTE-0851]. In the FAR, paragraph (c) prescribes the Foreign Concerns clause [@CL-RTE-0844], and in 48 CFR 9903.201-4 paragraph (e) prescribes the Educational Institutions clause [@CL-RTE-0805]. The CAS Board text is on the [CAS clauses card](rte-cas-solicitation-provisions-and-clauses.md), and the [CAS applicability card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-applicability.md) covers coverage.
