## Purpose

Names the CAS clauses the FAR tells the contracting officer to insert, says who may grant a waiver, and states the determination on a Disclosure Statement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0841] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.001, 30.201-4, 30.201-5 and 30.202-7 [@CL-RTE-0874; @CL-RTE-0841; @CL-RTE-0848; @CL-RTE-0849] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0841] |
| Applies to | Negotiated contracts, as each clause trigger states [@CL-RTE-0841; @CL-RTE-0842; @CL-RTE-0844; @CL-RTE-0845; @CL-RTE-0847] |

The clause at FAR 52.230-2, Cost Accounting Standards, must be inserted in negotiated contracts, unless the contract is exempted, is subject to modified coverage, or the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) is used [@CL-RTE-0841]. The clause at FAR 52.230-3, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, goes in negotiated contracts when the amount is over $2.5 million but less than $50 million [@CL-RTE-0842], and the offeror certifies it is eligible for and elects modified CAS coverage, unless the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) is used [@CL-RTE-0843]. The clause at 52.230-4, the Foreign Concerns version, goes in negotiated contracts with foreign concerns, unless the contract is otherwise exempt from CAS [@CL-RTE-0844]. The clause at FAR 52.230-5, the Educational Institution version, goes in negotiated contracts awarded to educational institutions [@CL-RTE-0845], and is not inserted when the contract is exempted, is to be performed by an FFRDC, or the provision at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies [@CL-RTE-0846]. The clause at FAR 52.230-6, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards, goes in contracts containing any of the clauses prescribed in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), or (e) [@CL-RTE-0847].

The head of the agency may waive the applicability of CAS for a particular contract or subcontract under the conditions listed in paragraph (b) of the waiver section, and must not delegate this waiver authority to any official in the agency below the senior contract policymaking level [@CL-RTE-0848]. The cognizant Federal agency official (CFAO) is the contracting officer assigned by the cognizant Federal agency to administer the CAS [@CL-RTE-0874]. The CFAO shall determine if the Disclosure Statement adequately describes the contractor's cost accounting practices [@CL-RTE-0849]. Generally, the CFAO should furnish the contractor notification of adequacy or inadequacy within 30 days after the CFAO receives the Disclosure Statement [@CL-RTE-0873].

## How it works

Each clause is keyed to a contract type or a contractor type, and the Administration clause goes in contracts containing any of the other four [@CL-RTE-0847]. The waiver conditions and the request contents are on the [CAS waiver card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-waiver.md), which this card does not restate; the FAR text prints its own list of conditions, so compare the two there. For Disclosure Statements, the printed target for notifying the contractor is a general one, stated as what the CFAO should do [@CL-RTE-0873]. The Administration clause is covered on the [Administration of Cost Accounting Standards card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-administration-of-cost-accounting-standards.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency official below the senior contract policymaking level signs a CAS waiver for a 10 million contract. Under the section the head of the agency must not delegate waiver authority below that level, so the section does not allow that delegation.

## Never confuse

The FAR and the CAS Board text each print clause triggers, and their printed figures and paragraph references differ. Each is stated here as printed, and neither printed section says which one controls. For the clause at FAR 52.230-3, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, the FAR prints a contract amount over $2.5 million but less than $50 million in negotiated contracts [@CL-RTE-0842]. For the clause of the same name, the CAS Board text prints a contract amount over the Truth in Negotiations Act threshold, as adjusted for inflation, but less than $100 million in negotiated contracts [@CL-RTE-0803]. Both also print the condition that the offeror certifies it is eligible for and elects modified CAS coverage [@CL-RTE-0843; @CL-RTE-0804]. For the Cost Accounting Standards clause, the FAR points to paragraph (c) of its subsection as the alternative clause [@CL-RTE-0841], and the CAS Board text points to paragraph (e) of its section [@CL-RTE-0802]. For the Disclosure and Consistency clause, the FAR points to paragraph (c) [@CL-RTE-0843], and the CAS Board text points to paragraph (d) of its subsection [@CL-RTE-0804], which the printed section marks reserved [@CL-RTE-0851]. In the FAR, paragraph (c) prescribes the Foreign Concerns clause [@CL-RTE-0844], and in the CAS Board text paragraph (e) prescribes the Educational Institutions clause [@CL-RTE-0805]. The CAS Board text is on the [CAS clauses card](rte-cas-solicitation-provisions-and-clauses.md), and the [CAS applicability card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-applicability.md) covers coverage.
