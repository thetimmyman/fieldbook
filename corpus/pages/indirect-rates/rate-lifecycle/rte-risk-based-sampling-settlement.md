## Purpose

Explains how incurred cost proposals are sampled for audit and the routes by which final indirect rates are settled.

## The rule

| Applies to | Incurred cost proposals assessed under risk-based sampling [@CL-RTE-0318] |

DCAA selects incurred cost proposals for audit under a risk-based sampling policy, assessing eligible proposals on sampling auditable dollar volume and risk factors [@CL-RTE-0318]. Unless an exception applies, incurred cost proposals with the following sampling auditable dollar volume are eligible for sampling [@CL-RTE-0320]: less than $100 million are eligible for sampling [@CL-RTE-0320; @CL-CMP-1715]; $100 million to $250 million must be audited every 5th year, or whenever the last four incurred costs years were closed with a risk-based sampling memo [@CL-RTE-9240]; greater than $250 million to $500 million must be audited every 4th year, or whenever the last three incurred costs years were closed with a risk-based sampling memo [@CL-RTE-9241]; greater than $500 million to less than $1 billion must be audited every other year, or whenever the last incurred cost year was closed with a risk-based sampling memo [@CL-RTE-9242]; and $1 billion or greater are not eligible for sampling and will be audited each year [@CL-RTE-0321; @CL-CMP-1716].

For proposals not randomly selected for audit, DCAA issues a risk-based sampling memorandum and a rate agreement letter, signed by both DCAA and the contractor, to finalize the indirect rates proposed [@CL-RTE-0319].

## How it works

For purposes of satisfying the FAR 42.505-1(b)(2) and FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(i) audit requirements for incurred cost proposals, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy authorized a Class Deviation – DCAA Policy and Procedures for Sampling Low-Risk Incurred Cost Proposals, dated July 24, 2012 [@CL-RTE-9239; @CL-RTE-0400]. This deviation instructs contracting officers to rely on either a DCAA audit report (attestation examination engagement) or a DCAA memorandum (advisory service) to satisfy the requirements of FAR 42.505 [@CL-RTE-0322]. The following methods ensure these indirect cost rates are established in a manner consistent with contract terms, applicable regulations, and sound accounting practices [@CL-RTE-9238]. Under audit determination, rates may be set based on the results of an audit or accepted as proposed if a low-risk sampling process is used, and the auditor's determination is generally final but contractors have the right to appeal [@CL-RTE-0323]. Under contracting officer determination, final rates can be negotiated between the administrative contracting officer and the contractor, based on the contractor's proposal and the audit report or advisory memorandum [@CL-RTE-0324]. In some cases, settlement may shift from negotiation to audit, especially when both parties agree the rates can be settled easily or when special circumstances apply [@CL-RTE-0325]. Procedures for establishing indirect cost rates for contracts at educational institutions, state and local governments, and nonprofit organizations are discussed in FAR 42.505-3 through 42.505-5, respectively [@CL-RTE-9237]. The rules for each route are on [Final indirect cost rates](rte-co-final-indirect-rates.md) and [auditor determination](rte-final-indirect-rate-auditor-determination.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit files a proposal with sampling auditable dollar volume of 60,000,000. It is eligible for sampling, is not selected, and closes with a sampling memorandum and a signed rate agreement letter. Another unit at 1,200,000,000 is not eligible for sampling and is audited that year.

## Never confuse

Eligible proposals are assessed based on their Sampling Auditable Dollar Volume (ADV) and various risk factors [@CL-RTE-0318]. Under audit determination, final indirect cost rates may be set based on the results of an audit or accepted as proposed if a low-risk sampling process is used [@CL-RTE-0323]. For how materiality shapes the procedures within an audit, see [Materiality in incurred cost audits](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-materiality-incurred-cost-audits.md).
