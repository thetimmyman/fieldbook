## Purpose

Explains the Predetermined Indirect Cost Rates clause: how the rates are applied, when the final rate proposal is due, and what happens when the parties cannot agree.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0333] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-15 [@CL-RTE-0333] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-15 [@CL-RTE-0002; @CL-RTE-0003] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0333] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Predetermined Indirect Cost Rates clause [@CL-RTE-0333] |

Notwithstanding the Allowable Cost and Payment clause, allowable indirect costs under the contract are obtained by applying predetermined indirect cost rates to bases agreed upon by the parties [@CL-RTE-0333]. The contractor still submits an adequate final indirect cost rate proposal to the contracting officer or cognizant Federal agency official and to the auditor within the six-month period after each fiscal year ends [@CL-RTE-0334].

Reasonable extensions, for exceptional circumstances only, may be requested in writing by the contractor and granted in writing by the contracting officer [@CL-RTE-0335]. The proposed rates rest on the contractor's actual cost experience for the period, and the final rates are established as promptly as practical after the proposal arrives [@CL-RTE-0336]. Allowability of costs and acceptability of cost allocation methods are determined in accordance with FAR subpart 31.3 in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-RTE-0337].

## How it works

The written agreement records the rates, their bases, the period, and the specific items treated as direct costs or any changes in the items previously agreed to be direct costs [@CL-RTE-0338]. It cannot change a monetary ceiling, contract obligation or specific allowance or disallowance in the contract [@CL-RTE-0339]. While rates for a period are pending, reimbursement continues at the prior period's fixed rates or at billing rates acceptable to the contracting officer, subject to adjustment when final rates are set [@CL-RTE-0340]. If the parties cannot agree on the rates for a period, allowable indirect costs are obtained by applying final indirect cost rates established under the Allowable Cost and Payment clause [@CL-RTE-0341].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause applies predetermined indirect cost rates to agreed bases in the same way, notwithstanding the Allowable Cost and Payment clause [@CL-RTE-0002; @CL-RTE-0333]. It has the same six-month final rate proposal and the same written-extension rule [@CL-RTE-0003; @CL-RTE-0004; @CL-RTE-0334; @CL-RTE-0335], the same actual-cost basis and prompt establishment of final rates [@CL-RTE-0005; @CL-RTE-0006; @CL-RTE-0336], and the same FAR subpart 31.3 allowability rule [@CL-RTE-0007; @CL-RTE-0337]. Its paragraph (d) has the same contents for the written agreement and the same bar on changing any monetary ceiling, contract obligation, or specific allowance or disallowance [@CL-RTE-0008; @CL-RTE-0009; @CL-RTE-0338; @CL-RTE-0339]. It has the same pending-period reimbursement rule and the same fallback to final rates under the Allowable Cost and Payment clause [@CL-RTE-0010; @CL-RTE-0011; @CL-RTE-0340; @CL-RTE-0012; @CL-RTE-0341].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract applies a predetermined overhead rate of 25 percent to a direct labor base, and the fiscal year ends with the contractor submitting its final rate proposal within the six-month window. The agreement records the rate, the base and the period. If the parties cannot agree on the next period's rates, indirect costs for that period are recovered using final rates under the Allowable Cost and Payment clause.

## Never confuse

Predetermined rates are not billing rates. A billing rate is established temporarily for interim reimbursement and adjusted as necessary pending final rates [@CL-RTE-0300], while the clause has predetermined rates applied to the agreed bases to obtain allowable indirect costs [@CL-RTE-0333]. Pending the predetermined rates, reimbursement continues at the previous period's fixed rates or at acceptable billing rates [@CL-RTE-0340]. A predetermined rate is also not a ceiling, because the agreement does not change any monetary ceiling in the contract [@CL-RTE-0339].

## Company calibration

Confirm which contracts carry the clause, whether each year's final rate proposal was submitted on time, and whether any extension was granted in writing [@CL-RTE-0334; @CL-RTE-0335]. [@Q-011]
