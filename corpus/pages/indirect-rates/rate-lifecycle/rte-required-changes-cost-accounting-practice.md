## Purpose

Outlines what the CFAO must determine before an equitable adjustment for a required change and which contracts such an adjustment can reach.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0604] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.603-1 [@CL-RTE-0604] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0604] |
| Applies to | Required changes under the cost accounting standards clauses [@CL-RTE-0730] |

Prior to making an equitable adjustment for a required change under the applicable paragraphs that address a required change in one of the clauses at FAR 52.230-2 (Cost Accounting Standards), 52.230-3 (Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices) or 52.230-5 (Cost Accounting Standards, Educational Institution) [@CL-RTE-0730; @CL-RTE-9146], the CFAO shall determine one of two alternatives. The first is that the cost accounting practice change is required to comply with a CAS, or a modification or interpretation thereof, that subsequently became applicable to one or more contracts or subcontracts [@CL-RTE-0604]. The second is that the former cost accounting practice was in compliance with applicable CAS and the change is necessary to remain in compliance [@CL-RTE-0705].

When a change is required to remain in compliance for reasons other than a contract award, or to comply with a new or modified standard, the clause at 52.230-6, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards, requires the contractor to [@CL-RTE-0706] (i) submit a description of the change to the CFAO not less than 60 days (or other mutually agreeable date) before implementation of the change [@CL-RTE-0605]; and (ii) submit rationale to support any contractor written statement that the cost impact of the change is immaterial [@CL-RTE-9236].

## How it works

Required changes made to comply with new or modified standards may require equitable adjustments, but only to those contracts awarded before the effective date of the new or modified standard [@CL-RTE-0606]. When a contractor elects to implement such a change prior to the applicability date of the standard, the CFAO shall administer the change as a unilateral change [@CL-RTE-0607]. Prior to the applicability date, contractors shall not receive an equitable adjustment that results in increased costs in the aggregate to the Government unless the CFAO determines that the unilateral change is a desirable change [@CL-RTE-0707].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A new Cost Accounting Standard becomes applicable to a business unit, and the contractor changes a practice to comply with it. Contracts awarded before the effective date of the new standard show an aggregate cost impact of 150,000. The CFAO determines that the change is required to comply with the new standard, so equitable adjustments can be considered for those earlier contracts. A contract awarded after the effective date is not reached by an equitable adjustment for this change.

## Never confuse

A required change is one that is required to comply with a CAS, or a modification or interpretation thereof, that subsequently became applicable to one or more contracts or subcontracts [@CL-RTE-0604], or one where the former cost accounting practice was in compliance with applicable CAS and the change is necessary to remain in compliance [@CL-RTE-0705]. The contractor may unilaterally change its disclosed or established cost accounting practices, but the Government shall not pay any increased cost, in the aggregate, as a result of the unilateral change [@CL-RTE-0608]. A required change made to comply with a new or modified standard that the contractor elects to implement before the applicability date of the standard is administered by the CFAO as a unilateral change [@CL-RTE-0607]. See [Unilateral and desirable changes](rte-unilateral-and-desirable-changes.md) and [Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-disclosure-consistency-modified-cas.md).
