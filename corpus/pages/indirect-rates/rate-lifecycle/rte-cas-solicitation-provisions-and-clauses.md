## Purpose

Names the solicitation provision and the contract clauses in the Cost Accounting Standards Board rules, and the contracts each one goes in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Cost Accounting Standards Board rules [@CL-RTE-0800] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.201-2 [@CL-RTE-1002], 48 CFR 9903.201-3 and 9903.201-4 [@CL-RTE-0800; @CL-RTE-0802] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0800] |
| Applies to | Solicitations for proposed contracts subject to CAS [@CL-RTE-0800], and negotiated contracts [@CL-RTE-0802] |

The contracting officer shall insert the provision Cost Accounting Standards Notices and Certification in solicitations for proposed contracts subject to CAS as specified in 9903.201 [@CL-RTE-0800]. The provision allows offerors to certify their Disclosure Statement status, to claim exemption from full CAS coverage and elect modified CAS coverage when appropriate [@CL-RTE-0801], and to certify whether award of the contemplated contract would require a change to existing cost accounting practices [@CL-RTE-0850].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause Cost Accounting Standards in negotiated contracts, unless the contract is exempted (see 9903.201-1), the contract is subject to modified coverage (see 9903.201-2), or the clause prescribed in paragraph (e) of 9903.201-4 is used [@CL-RTE-0802]. Paragraph (e) is headed Cost Accounting Standards—Educational Institutions [@CL-RTE-0851]. The Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause goes in negotiated contracts when the amount is over the Truth in Negotiations Act threshold, as adjusted for inflation, but less than $100 million [@CL-RTE-0803], and the offeror certifies it is eligible for and elects modified coverage, unless the clause prescribed in paragraph (d) is used [@CL-RTE-0804]. Paragraph (d) is [Reserved] [@CL-RTE-0851]. The Educational Institution clause is prescribed for negotiated contracts awarded to educational institutions, unless the contract is exempted, is to be performed by an FFRDC, or the provision at 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies [@CL-RTE-0805]. The Foreign Concerns clause goes in negotiated contracts when the contract is with a foreign concern and is not otherwise exempt under 9903.201-1 [@CL-RTE-0806].

## How it works

FAR 30.201-2, Types of CAS coverage: See 48 CFR 9903.201-2 [@CL-RTE-1002].

A solicitation for a proposed contract subject to CAS receives the provision [@CL-RTE-0800]. The clause Cost Accounting Standards goes in negotiated contracts unless the contract is exempted (see 9903.201-1), the contract is subject to modified coverage (see 9903.201-2), or the clause prescribed in paragraph (e) is used [@CL-RTE-0802]. The Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause goes in negotiated contracts when the amount is over the Truth in Negotiations Act threshold, as adjusted for inflation, but less than $100 million, and the offeror certifies it is eligible for and elects modified coverage, unless the clause prescribed in paragraph (d) is used [@CL-RTE-0803; @CL-RTE-0804]. The Educational Institution clause goes in negotiated contracts awarded to educational institutions, unless the contract is exempted, is to be performed by an FFRDC, or the provision at 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies [@CL-RTE-0805]. The Foreign Concerns clause goes in negotiated contracts when the contract is with a foreign concern and is not otherwise exempt under 9903.201-1 [@CL-RTE-0806]. For the thresholds that set coverage, see [CAS applicability](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-applicability.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit proposes a negotiated contract of 70 million, above the Truth in Negotiations Act threshold. In the solicitation provision it certifies that it is eligible for modified coverage and elects it. The contract takes the Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause, not the full Cost Accounting Standards clause. A contract of 150 million would be outside the range for that clause.

## Never confuse

The provision goes in solicitations for proposed contracts subject to CAS as specified in 9903.201 and allows offerors to certify their Disclosure Statement status, to claim exemption from full CAS coverage and elect modified CAS coverage when appropriate, and to certify whether award of the contemplated contract would require a change to existing cost accounting practices [@CL-RTE-0800; @CL-RTE-0801; @CL-RTE-0850], unlike the clause Cost Accounting Standards, which goes in negotiated contracts unless the contract is exempted (see 9903.201-1), the contract is subject to modified coverage (see 9903.201-2), or the clause prescribed in paragraph (e) is used [@CL-RTE-0802]. For whether a contract is covered at all, and at what level, see the applicability card. For the clause prescriptions printed in the FAR, see the [FAR clauses card](rte-far-30-cas-clauses-waiver-and-determinations.md). The findings the Contracting Officer or the cognizant Federal agency official makes before an adjustment under these clauses are on the [findings card](rte-cas-findings-and-subcontractor-disclosure-statements.md).
