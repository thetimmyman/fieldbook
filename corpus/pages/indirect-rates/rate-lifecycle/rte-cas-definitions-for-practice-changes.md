## Purpose

Shows which defined terms the Cost Accounting Standards Board definitions section prints in full and where the other entries point.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Cost Accounting Standards Board rules [@CL-RTE-0813] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.301 [@CL-RTE-0813] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0813] |
| Applies to | The definitions in 48 CFR 9903.301(a) apply to chapter 99 [@CL-RTE-9220]; CAS-covered contract is defined as used in the part and net awards as used in the chapter [@CL-RTE-0813; @CL-RTE-0814] |

In the definitions section, CAS-covered contract, as used in the part, means any negotiated contract or subcontract in which a CAS clause is required to be included [@CL-RTE-0813]. Net awards, as used in the chapter, means the total value of negotiated CAS-covered prime contract and subcontract awards, including the potential value of contract options, received during the reporting period minus cancellations, terminations, and other related credit transactions [@CL-RTE-0814].

Many entries give only a pointer: Cost accounting practice. See 9903.302-1 [@CL-RTE-0856]. Change to a cost accounting practice. See 9903.302-2 [@CL-RTE-0857]. Other entries give a full definition, for example Disclosure statement, as used in this part, means the Disclosure Statement required by 9903.202-1 [@CL-RTE-9221].

## How it works

The definitions set forth in 9903.301(a) apply to chapter 99 [@CL-RTE-9220]. For required changes, see [required changes](rte-required-changes-cost-accounting-practice.md); for unilateral and desirable changes, see [unilateral and desirable changes](rte-unilateral-and-desirable-changes.md); for who makes each finding, see the [findings card](rte-cas-findings-and-subcontractor-disclosure-statements.md). Examples of what is and is not a change to a cost accounting practice are on the [illustrations of changes](rte-changes-that-are-cost-accounting-practice-changes.md) and the [illustrations of changes that are not](rte-changes-that-are-not-cost-accounting-practice-changes.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit books 60 million of new negotiated CAS-covered prime contract and subcontract awards in a period, including the potential value of options, and 15 million of that is cancelled in the same period. Its net awards for the period are 45 million.

## Never confuse

Net awards, as used in this chapter, means the total value of negotiated CAS-covered prime contract and subcontract awards, including the potential value of contract options, received during the reporting period minus cancellations, terminations, and other related credit transactions [@CL-RTE-0814], unlike a CAS-covered contract, which as used in this part means any negotiated contract or subcontract in which a CAS clause is required to be included [@CL-RTE-0813]. What counts as a cost accounting practice, and what counts as a change to one, is on the [definitions card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cost-accounting-practice-definitions.md).
