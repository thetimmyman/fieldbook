## Purpose

Shows what a pricing proposal must say about a forward pricing rate agreement, how the agreed rates are used, and when certification attaches.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0326] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.407-3 [@CL-RTE-0326] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0326] |
| Applies to | Pricing actions priced with forward pricing rate agreement rates, and proposals that need certified cost or pricing data [@CL-RTE-0326; @CL-RTE-0328] |

When certified cost or pricing data are required, the offeror must describe any forward pricing rate agreement in each pricing proposal to which the rates apply and identify the latest cost or pricing data already submitted under it [@CL-RTE-0326]. All data submitted for the agreement, updated as needed, becomes part of the data certified as accurate, complete and current at the time of agreement on price [@CL-RTE-0327].

Contracting officers use the agreement rates as bases for pricing every contract, modification and other action performed during the period covered [@CL-RTE-0328]. For DoD the use is mandatory when rates are available, unless the head of the contracting activity waives them case by case, and each waived case is advised to the administrative contracting officer [@CL-RTE-0332].

## How it works

Contracting officers do not require certification at the time of agreement for data supplied in support of the agreement [@CL-RTE-0330]. Certification attaches later, on the action that requires a certificate and is priced with the rates, and it covers the agreement data plus all other data supporting the action [@CL-RTE-0331]. Conditions that may affect validity are reported promptly to the administrative contracting officer, who, if the officer determines a changed condition invalidates the agreement, notifies all interested parties of the extent of its effect and the status of efforts to establish a revised agreement [@CL-RTE-0329].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror prices a proposal with agreed overhead and general and administrative rates and describes the agreement in the proposal. A certificate is required for that action, so the certificate covers the data behind the agreement as well as the proposal data and is executed at the price agreement date. The agreement itself was signed earlier, with no certificate.

## Never confuse

The agreement is not a certificate. The agreement fixes rates to use for a period; the certificate is executed later, on the pricing action that relies on those rates. The agreement is also not permanent, because a changed condition can invalidate it.

## Company calibration

Confirm which agreements are in force, which proposals rely on them, and how the certificate at each pricing action covers the agreement data [@CL-RTE-0331]. [@Q-011]
