## Purpose

Shows what a pricing proposal must say about a forward pricing rate agreement, how the agreed rates are used, and when certification attaches.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0326] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.407-3 [@CL-RTE-0326] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.405-3 [@CL-RTE-0895]; RFO FAR 15.105-4(b)(1)(i) [@CL-RTE-0901] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0326] |
| Applies to | Pricing actions priced with forward pricing rate agreement rates, and proposals that need certified cost or pricing data [@CL-RTE-0326; @CL-RTE-0328] |

When certified cost or pricing data are required, the offeror must describe any forward pricing rate agreement in each pricing proposal to which the rates apply and identify the latest cost or pricing data already submitted under it [@CL-RTE-0326]. All data submitted for the agreement, updated as needed, becomes part of the data certified as accurate, complete, and current at the time of agreement on price for an initial contract or for a contract modification [@CL-RTE-0327].

Contracting officers will use the agreement rates as bases for pricing all contracts, modifications and other contractual actions to be performed during the period covered [@CL-RTE-0328]. The DFARS direction is to use the rates when they are available, unless the head of the contracting activity waives them on a case-by-case basis, and to advise the administrative contracting officer of each case waived [@CL-RTE-0332].

## How it works

Contracting officers shall not require certification at the time of agreement for data supplied in support of forward pricing rate agreements or other advance agreements [@CL-RTE-0330]. When the agreement is used to price a contract action that requires a certificate, the certificate supporting that action shall cover the data supplied to support the agreement and all other data supporting the action [@CL-RTE-0331]. Conditions that may affect validity shall be reported promptly to the administrative contracting officer, who, if the officer determines a changed condition invalidates the agreement, shall notify all interested parties of the extent of its effect and the status of efforts to establish a revised agreement [@CL-RTE-0329].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.405-3(a) says all data submitted in connection with the forward pricing rate agreement, updated as necessary, form a part of the total data that the offeror certifies to be accurate, complete, and current at the time of agreement on price for an initial contract or for a contract modification [@CL-RTE-0895] [@CL-RTE-0327]. The codified sentence on describing the agreement in each pricing proposal appears instead in RFO FAR 15.105-4(b)(1)(i), which says offerors must describe any forward pricing rate agreements or other advanced agreements in each specific pricing proposal to which the rates apply and identify the latest cost or pricing data already submitted in accordance with the FPRA [@CL-RTE-0901; @CL-RTE-0326]. That RFO sentence is item (i) of paragraph (b)(1), headed Certified cost or pricing data, under paragraph (b), headed Format for submission of data, of RFO FAR 15.105-4 [@CL-RTE-0902; @CL-RTE-0901]. It reads "Offerors must describe" where the codified sentence begins "When certified cost or pricing data are required, offerors are required to describe", and it refers to forward pricing rate agreements or other advanced agreements where the codified sentence refers to forward pricing rate agreements [@CL-RTE-0901; @CL-RTE-0326]. RFO FAR 15.405-3(b) says contracting officers will use FPRA rates as bases for pricing all contracts, modifications, and other contractual actions to be performed during the period covered by the agreement [@CL-RTE-0896; @CL-RTE-0328], that conditions that may affect the agreement's validity must be reported promptly to the administrative contracting officer [@CL-RTE-0897; @CL-RTE-0329], and that if the administrative contracting officer determines that a changed condition invalidates the agreement, that officer must notify all interested parties of the extent of its effect and status of efforts to establish a revised agreement [@CL-RTE-0898; @CL-RTE-0329]. RFO FAR 15.405-3(c) says contracting officers must not require certification at the time of agreement for data supplied in support of FPRA's or other advance agreements [@CL-RTE-0899; @CL-RTE-0330], and that when such an agreement is used to price a contract action that requires a certificate, the certificate supporting that action must cover the data supplied to support the agreement and all other data supporting the action [@CL-RTE-0900; @CL-RTE-0331].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror prices a proposal with agreed overhead and general and administrative rates and describes the agreement in the proposal. A certificate is required for that action, so the certificate covers the data behind the agreement as well as the proposal data and is executed at the price agreement date. The agreement itself was signed earlier, with no certificate.

## Never confuse

Contracting officers shall not require certification at the time of agreement for data supplied in support of forward pricing rate agreements or other advance agreements [@CL-RTE-0330]. If the administrative contracting officer determines that a changed condition invalidates the agreement, that officer shall notify all interested parties of the extent of its effect and status of efforts to establish a revised agreement [@CL-RTE-0329].

In the codified text the duty to describe the agreement in each pricing proposal is in the forward pricing rate agreements section [@CL-RTE-0326], while RFO FAR 15.105-4(b)(1)(i), in the section on requests for cost or pricing data, states it [@CL-RTE-0901; @CL-RTE-0902].
