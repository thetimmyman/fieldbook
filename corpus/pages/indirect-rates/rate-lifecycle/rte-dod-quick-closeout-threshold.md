## Purpose

Shows how the DoD supplement lets administrative contracting officers settle a contract early, and the threshold it uses for unsettled costs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-RTE-0314] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 242.708 [@CL-RTE-0314] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0314] |
| Applies to | Contracts, task orders and delivery orders closed under the DoD quick-closeout procedure [@CL-RTE-0314] |

Defense Contract Management Agency administrative contracting officers may settle direct and indirect costs for a specific contract, task order or delivery order before final costs and rates are determined, regardless of the dollar value or percentage of unsettled costs [@CL-RTE-0314; @CL-RTE-0315]. In lieu of the thresholds at FAR 42.708(a)(2)(i) and (ii), the amount of unsettled direct costs and indirect costs to be allocated to the contract, task order, or delivery order will be considered relatively insignificant when the total unsettled direct costs and indirect costs to be allocated to any one contract, task order, or delivery order do not exceed $2 million, regardless of the total contract, task order, or delivery order amount [@CL-RTE-0316; @CL-RTE-0317].

## How it works

The settlement of direct and indirect costs is for a specific contract, task order, or delivery order to be closed in advance of the determination of final direct costs and indirect rates [@CL-RTE-0314], regardless of the dollar value or percentage of unsettled direct or indirect costs allocable to the contract, task order, or delivery order [@CL-RTE-0315]. In lieu of the thresholds at FAR 42.708(a)(2)(i) and (ii), the unsettled costs are considered relatively insignificant when the total to be allocated to any one contract, task order, or delivery order does not exceed $2 million [@CL-RTE-0316; @CL-RTE-0317].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A delivery order worth 12,000,000 still has 1,800,000 of unsettled direct and indirect costs. That total is under 2,000,000, so the unsettled costs count as relatively insignificant under the DoD test. A second order with 2,400,000 unsettled does not meet the test.
