## Purpose

Explains how CAS price adjustments and noncompliance determinations at the subcontract level pass up the chain of CFAOs, and how subcontractor disclosure statements fit in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0627] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.607 and FAR 30.202-8 [@CL-RTE-0627; @CL-RTE-0630] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0627] |
| Applies to | CAS price adjustments and noncompliance determinations required at the subcontract level [@CL-RTE-0627] |

When a negotiated CAS price adjustment or a determination of noncompliance is required at the subcontract level, the CFAO for the subcontractor shall furnish a copy of the negotiation memorandum or the determination to the CFAO for the contractor of the next higher-tier subcontractor [@CL-RTE-0627]. The CFAO of the contractor or the next higher-tier subcontractor shall not change the determination of the CFAO for the lower-tier subcontractor [@CL-RTE-0628].

For subcontract awards, the contractor awarding the subcontract must follow the procedures at FAR 52.230-6(l), (m), and (n) [@CL-RTE-0701].

## How it works

If the subcontractor refuses to submit a GDM or DCI proposal, remedies are made at the prime contractor level [@CL-RTE-0629]. When the Government requires determinations of adequacy of subcontractor disclosure statements, the CFAO for the subcontractor shall provide this determination to the CFAO for the contractor or next higher-tier subcontractor [@CL-RTE-0630]. Any determination that it is impractical to secure a subcontractor Disclosure Statement must be made in accordance with 48 CFR 9903.202-2 [@CL-RTE-0631].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A first-tier subcontractor has a cost accounting practice noncompliance that raises costs by 75,000. The CFAO for the subcontractor makes the determination and sends a copy to the CFAO for the prime contractor. The prime contractor's CFAO receives the copy and does not change the determination. If the subcontractor had refused to submit a general dollar magnitude proposal, the remedies would be made at the prime contractor level.

## Never confuse

The CFAO for the contractor of the next higher-tier subcontractor is furnished a copy of the lower-tier determination but shall not change it [@CL-RTE-0627; @CL-RTE-0628]. If the subcontractor refuses to submit a GDM or DCI proposal, remedies are made at the prime contractor level [@CL-RTE-0629]. See [Administration of Cost Accounting Standards clause](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-administration-of-cost-accounting-standards.md) and [CAS applicability](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-applicability.md).
