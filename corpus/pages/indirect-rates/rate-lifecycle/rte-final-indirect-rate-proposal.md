## Purpose

Gives the deadline for the final indirect cost rate proposal under the Allowable Cost and Payment clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR clause [@CL-RTE-0001] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(i) [@CL-RTE-0001] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(iv) [@CL-RTE-9039; @CL-RTE-9040] |
| Edition date | 2018-08 [@CL-RTE-0001] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0001] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the Allowable Cost and Payment clause [@CL-RTE-0001] |

The contractor shall submit an adequate final indirect cost rate proposal to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and the auditor within the 6-month period following the expiration of each of its fiscal years [@CL-RTE-9142; @CL-RTE-0001]. The clause allows reasonable extensions for exceptional circumstances only, which the contractor may request in writing and the contracting officer may grant in writing [@CL-RTE-9119].

## How it works

Final annual indirect cost rates and the appropriate bases shall be established in accordance with subpart 42.7 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) in effect for the period covered by the indirect cost rate proposal [@CL-RTE-9232]. The proposed rates shall be based on the Contractor's actual cost experience for that period, and the appropriate Government representative and the Contractor shall establish the final indirect cost rates as promptly as practical after receipt of the Contractor's proposal [@CL-RTE-9233].

RFO FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(iv) says the following supplemental information is not required to determine if a proposal is adequate, but may be required during the audit process [@CL-RTE-9039]. One item is general organizational information and limitation on allowability of compensation for certain contractor personnel; see 31.205-6(p) [@CL-RTE-9040].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's fiscal year ends on the last day of month 12. Six months later, at the end of month 6 of the next year, the proposal is due. A proposal filed in month 8 without an approved extension is late.

## Never confuse

Proposed final annual indirect cost rates are based on the Contractor's actual cost experience for the covered period [@CL-RTE-9232; @CL-RTE-9233], unlike a billing rate, which is an indirect cost rate established temporarily for interim reimbursement of incurred indirect costs and adjusted as necessary until final indirect cost rates are established [@CL-RTE-0300], and under RFO FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(iv) the listed supplemental information is not required to determine if a proposal is adequate, though it may be required during the audit process [@CL-RTE-9039]; for billing rates and final rates, see [final and billing rate purpose](rte-final-and-billing-rate-purpose.md).
