## Purpose

Provides a walkthrough of the required schedules and procedural steps for preparing and submitting a contractor incurred cost submission under the Allowable Cost and Payment clause.

## The rule

Under the Allowable Cost and Payment clause at FAR 52.216-7, the contractor shall submit an adequate final indirect cost rate proposal to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and the cognizant auditor within the 6-month period following the expiration of each of its fiscal years [@CL-RTE-9142; @CL-RTE-9228; @CL-RTE-0001]. Reasonable extensions of the proposal period, for exceptional circumstances only, may be requested in writing by the contractor and granted in writing by the contracting officer [@CL-RTE-9119; @CL-RTE-0001]. The proposed rates shall be based on the contractor's actual cost experience for that period, and the appropriate Government representative and the contractor shall establish the final indirect cost rates as promptly as practical after receipt of the proposal [@CL-RTE-9233]. To be adequate, an indirect cost rate proposal shall include the data listed in FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(iii) unless otherwise specified by the cognizant Federal agency official [@CL-RTE-9360]. The supplemental information listed in FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(iv) is not required to determine if a proposal is adequate, but may be required during the audit process [@CL-RTE-9039].

## How it works

1. Confirm the contractor's fiscal year end and establish the submission deadline within the 6-month period following the expiration of that fiscal year, requesting any extension in writing for exceptional circumstances only [@CL-RTE-9142; @CL-RTE-9119; @CL-RTE-0001].
2. Prepare Schedule A to summarize all claimed indirect expense rates based on actual cost experience, showing the pool, base, and calculated indirect rate for each pool [@CL-RTE-9361; @CL-RTE-9233; @CL-RTE-9360].
3. Detail the claimed expense pools by element of cost from accounting records (Chart of Accounts): Schedule B for General and Administrative expenses [@CL-RTE-9362], Schedule C for overhead expenses for each final indirect cost pool [@CL-RTE-9363], and Schedule D for intermediate occupancy expenses and their reallocation to final indirect cost pools [@CL-RTE-9364].
4. Prepare Schedule E documenting the claimed allocation bases, by element of cost, used to distribute indirect costs [@CL-RTE-9365].
5. Compute facilities capital cost of money factors on Schedule F where facilities capital cost of money is claimed [@CL-RTE-9366].
6. Reconcile the books of account (General Ledger) and claimed direct costs by major cost element on Schedule G [@CL-RTE-9367].
7. Schedule direct costs by contract and subcontract and indirect expense applied at claimed rates, together with the subsidiary schedule of Government participation percentages in each allocation base amount, on Schedule H [@CL-RTE-9368].
8. Detail cumulative direct and indirect costs claimed and billed by contract and subcontract on Schedule I [@CL-RTE-9369].
9. Compile subcontract information on Schedule J, listing subcontracts awarded to companies for which the contractor is prime or upper-tier, including prime and subcontract numbers, subcontract value, award type, amount claimed during the fiscal year, and subcontractor contact details [@CL-RTE-9370].
10. Summarize time-and-materials and labor-hour contract details on Schedule K, including labor categories, labor rates, hours, amounts, direct materials, other direct costs, and indirect expense applied at claimed rates [@CL-RTE-9371].
11. Reconcile total payroll per IRS Form 941 to total labor costs distribution on Schedule L [@CL-RTE-9372].
12. Prepare Schedule M listing decisions, agreements, and approvals, and describing any accounting or organizational changes [@CL-RTE-9373].
13. Execute the Certificate of Final Indirect Costs on Schedule N, certifying that the costs are allowable; no proposal is accepted and no agreement to establish final indirect cost rates is made unless the costs are certified [@CL-RTE-9374; @CL-RTE-0218], and the contractor must affirmatively identify and exclude expressly unallowable costs that cost principles state in direct terms are unallowable [@CL-CMP-1725], recognizing that the administrative contracting officer determines penalties on unallowable costs and the auditor must communicate all unallowable costs potentially subject to penalty regardless of materiality [@CL-CMP-1723; @CL-CMP-1724].
14. Complete Schedule O providing contract closing information for contracts physically completed in the fiscal year, including contract numbers, periods of performance, contract ceiling amounts, fee computations, level of effort, and indication of whether each contract is ready to close [@CL-RTE-9375].
15. Submit the complete proposal package to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and the cognizant auditor [@CL-RTE-9142; @CL-RTE-9228].
16. The cognizant auditor reviews the adequacy of the proposal for audit in support of negotiating final indirect cost rates and provides a written description of any inadequacies to the contractor and contracting officer [@CL-RTE-0106; @CL-RTE-9229].
17. Following an adequacy determination, the proposal proceeds to audit or risk-based sampling: proposals with Sampling Auditable Dollar Volume under $100 million are eligible for sampling [@CL-RTE-0318; @CL-RTE-0320], proposals of $1 billion or greater are not eligible and are audited annually [@CL-RTE-0321], and audits evaluate whether costs are necessary, contract-compliant, and allowable, allocable, and reasonable under FAR 31 [@CL-CMP-1717; @CL-CMP-1720; @CL-CMP-1700; @CL-CMP-1701; @CL-RTE-0215].
18. Establish final indirect cost rates through negotiation with the administrative contracting officer or via auditor determination, or accept proposed rates under a risk-based sampling memorandum and signed rate agreement letter (see [the billing-to-final cycle](rte-billing-to-final-rate-cycle.md)) [@CL-RTE-0323; @CL-RTE-0324; @CL-RTE-0319], after which the contractor must update billings on all contracts and update Schedule I within 60 days of settlement [@CL-RTE-9376].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

For a fiscal year ending 31 December, the six-month submission period ends 30 June of the following year. A contractor prepares its Schedule A summary of claimed indirect expense rates based on its general ledger and allocation bases:

| Indirect cost pool | Claimed pool (USD) | Claimed base (USD) | Calculated rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fringe | 300,000 | 1,000,000 | 30% |
| Overhead | 520,000 | 1,300,000 | 40% |
| G&A | 276,000 | 2,300,000 | 12% |

The fringe pool of 300,000 divided by the direct labor base of 1,000,000 yields a calculated rate of 30 percent. The overhead pool of 520,000 divided by the direct labor plus fringe base of 1,300,000 yields a calculated rate of 40 percent. The G&A pool of 276,000 divided by the total cost input base of 2,300,000 yields a calculated rate of 12 percent.

## Never confuse

An incurred cost submission is the contractor's annual proposal of final indirect cost rates with supporting schedules, not an audit; an audit or risk-based sampling review by the auditor may follow receipt of the submission [@CL-RTE-0318; @CL-RTE-0215].

Adequacy of a proposal for audit is not acceptance of the proposed rates; an adequacy review confirms only that the submission contains the required supporting data, whereas final rates are established only upon subsequent settlement, negotiation, or rate agreement [@CL-RTE-0106; @CL-RTE-9229; @CL-RTE-0319; @CL-RTE-0323; @CL-RTE-0324].

The schedules listed in FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(iii) (Schedules A through O) are required to determine that a proposal is adequate [@CL-RTE-9360], whereas the supplemental information listed in FAR 52.216-7(d)(2)(iv) is not required to determine if a proposal is adequate but may be required during the audit process [@CL-RTE-9039].

The Allowable Cost and Payment clause designates the submission as a final indirect cost rate proposal [@CL-RTE-9142], whereas DCAA's Contract Audit Manual refers to it as an incurred cost proposal [@CL-RTE-0318].

DCAA publishes contractor submission tools including the ICE Model at https://www.dcaa.mil/Checklists-Tools/ICE-Model/ and the Incurred Cost Submission Adequacy Checklist at https://www.dcaa.mil/Checklists-Tools/Incurred-Cost-Submission-Adequacy-Checklist/ ; the Corpus does not hold that guidance, so no statement on this page rests on it.
