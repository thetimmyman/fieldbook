## Purpose

Shows when the auditor, rather than the contracting officer, establishes final indirect cost rates and how the procedure runs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-RTE-0214] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.705-2(a)(1) [@CL-RTE-0214] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.505-2 [@CL-RTE-0940; @CL-RTE-0941] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-RTE-0214] |
| Applies to | Business units not covered in 42.705-1(a), and covered units when the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and auditor agree and any of the circumstances in 42.705-2(a)(2) apply [@CL-RTE-0214; @CL-RTE-0262] |

The cognizant Government auditor shall establish final indirect cost rates for business units not covered in 42.705-1(a) [@CL-RTE-0214].

Auditor determination may also be used for business units covered in 42.705-1(a) when the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and the auditor agree that the indirect costs can be settled with little difficulty and any one of four circumstances applies [@CL-RTE-0262]. Two of them are that the unit has primarily fixed-price contracts with only minor involvement in cost-reimbursement contracts, and that the administrative cost of contracting officer determination would exceed the expected benefits [@CL-RTE-0263]. The other two are that the unit has no history of disputes and few cost problems, and that the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and the auditor agree that special circumstances require auditor determination [@CL-RTE-0264].

Once the proposal has been determined adequate for audit in support of negotiating final indirect cost rates, the auditor audits it and prepares an advisory audit report including a listing of any relevant advance agreements or restrictive terms of specific contracts [@CL-RTE-0215]. The auditor shall also seek agreement on indirect costs with the contractor and prepare an indirect cost rate agreement conforming to the requirements of the contracts, and the agreement shall be signed by the contractor and the auditor [@CL-RTE-0215; @CL-RTE-0216].

If agreement with the contractor is not reached, the auditor forwards the audit report to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) identified in the Directory of Contract Administration Services Components, who will then resolve the disagreement [@CL-RTE-0215; @CL-RTE-0217].

## How it works

Once a proposal has been determined to be adequate for audit in support of negotiating final indirect cost rates, the auditor shall audit the proposal and prepare an advisory audit report [@CL-RTE-0215], seek agreement on indirect costs with the contractor, and prepare an indirect cost rate agreement conforming to the requirements of the contracts, which shall be signed by the contractor and the auditor [@CL-RTE-0216]. If agreement with the contractor is not reached, the auditor shall forward the audit report to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) identified in the Directory of Contract Administration Services Components, who will then resolve the disagreement [@CL-RTE-0217].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers shall use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text, as Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(a)(1) says the cognizant Government auditor must establish final indirect cost rates for business units not covered in RFO 42.505-1(a), where the codified text says "shall" and cites 42.705-1(a) [@CL-RTE-0940; @CL-RTE-0214]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(a)(2) says auditor determination may also be used for business units covered in RFO 42.505-1(a) when the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and auditor agree that the indirect costs can be settled with little difficulty and any of the following circumstances apply, where the codified text opens with "In addition," [@CL-RTE-0941; @CL-RTE-0262]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(a)(2)(i) and (ii) keep the circumstances that the business unit primarily has fixed-price contracts with only minor involvement in cost-reimbursement contracts and that the administrative cost of contracting officer determination would exceed the expected benefits [@CL-RTE-0942; @CL-RTE-0263]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(a)(2)(iii) and (iv) keep the circumstances that the business unit does not have a history of disputes and there are few cost problems, and that the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) and auditor agree that special circumstances require auditor determination [@CL-RTE-0943; @CL-RTE-0264]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2) keeps the rule that, once a proposal has been determined to be adequate for audit in support of negotiating final indirect cost rates, the auditor audits the proposal and prepares an advisory audit report, including a listing of any relevant advance agreements or restrictive terms of specific contracts [@CL-RTE-0944; @CL-RTE-0215]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(i) also says that copies of the audit report must be furnished to the affected contracting offices [@CL-RTE-0945]; FAR 42.706(b) says copies of the audit report prepared under auditor determination shall be furnished, as appropriate, to the contracting offices and Government audit offices [@CL-CMP-7819]. Among the auditor's items, RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(ii) and (iii) are to seek agreement on indirect costs with the contractor and to prepare an indirect cost rate agreement conforming to the requirements of the contracts, and they say the agreement must be signed by the contractor and the auditor, where the codified text says "shall" [@CL-RTE-0946; @CL-RTE-0944; @CL-RTE-0216]. Item (iii) goes on to say that a copy of the agreement must be distributed to the contractor and to each affected contracting agency, while the codified text deals with distributing executed copies of the agreement in FAR 42.706(a), which says the contracting officer or auditor shall promptly distribute them and provide copies for the contract files [@CL-RTE-0947; @CL-CMP-7818]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(iv) keeps the item that, if agreement with the contractor is not reached, the audit report is forwarded to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) identified in the Directory of Contract Administration Services Components, who will then resolve the disagreement [@CL-RTE-0948; @CL-RTE-0217]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(v) says to promptly distribute resultant documents, where the codified item (v) says to distribute resulting documents in accordance with 42.706 [@CL-RTE-0950; @CL-RTE-0951].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit outside the contracting-officer procedure submits a final indirect rate proposal. The auditor finds the proposal adequate, audits it, and reaches agreement with the contractor at a final overhead rate of 95 percent. Both sign the rate agreement, and the agreed rate is used to settle the unit's covered years.

## Never confuse

For contracting officer determination, see [Final indirect cost rates](rte-co-final-indirect-rates.md).

The codified auditor determination procedure at FAR 42.705-2 [@CL-RTE-0214] corresponds to RFO FAR 42.505-2 [@CL-RTE-0940], which states the furnishing of audit report copies and the distribution of the rate agreement in that section [@CL-RTE-0945; @CL-RTE-0947], where the codified text states them in FAR 42.706 [@CL-CMP-7819; @CL-CMP-7818].
