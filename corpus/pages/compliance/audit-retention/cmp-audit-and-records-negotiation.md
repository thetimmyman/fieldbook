## Purpose

Shows the records, cost-examination, plant-inspection, and certified-data audit rights established by the negotiation audit clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0601] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-2 [@CL-CMP-0601] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-2 [@CL-CMP-0227] |
| Edition date | JUN 2020 [@CL-CMP-10049] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0601] |
| Applies to | Cost-reimbursement, incentive, time-and-materials, labor-hour, or price redeterminable contracts, or any combination of these, for cost examination, and contracts where the contractor has been required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with any pricing action relating to the contract [@CL-CMP-0602; @CL-CMP-0189] |

As used in the clause, records includes books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of whether such items are in written form, in the form of computer data, or in any other form [@CL-CMP-0601]. If the contract is a cost-reimbursement, incentive, time-and-materials, labor-hour, or price redeterminable contract, or any combination of these [@CL-CMP-0602], the Contractor shall maintain, and the Contracting Officer, or an authorized representative of the Contracting Officer, shall have the right to examine and audit, all records and other evidence sufficient to reflect properly all costs claimed to have been incurred or anticipated to be incurred directly or indirectly in performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-0603], and this right of examination shall include inspection at all reasonable times of the Contractor's plants, or parts of them, engaged in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-0604; @CL-CMP-8536].

## How it works

The right of examination under paragraph (b) includes inspection at all reasonable times of the Contractor's plants, or parts of them, engaged in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-0604]. If the Contractor has been required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with any pricing action relating to the contract, the Contracting Officer, or an authorized representative of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-0189], in order to evaluate the accuracy, completeness, and currency of the certified cost or pricing data, shall have the right to examine and audit all of the Contractor's records, including computations and projections [@CL-CMP-0605], related to any one of four alternatives: the proposal for the contract, subcontract, or modification; the discussions conducted on the proposal(s), including those related to negotiating; pricing of the contract, subcontract, or modification; or performance of the contract, subcontract, or modification [@CL-CMP-0190].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified FAR part 52 text, as Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.215-2 defines records to include books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of whether such items are in written form, in the form of computer data, or in any other form [@CL-CMP-0227]. For a cost-reimbursement, incentive, time-and-materials, labor-hour, or price redeterminable contract, or any combination of these [@CL-CMP-0228], the Contractor shall maintain, and the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative of the Contracting Officer shall have the right to examine and audit, all records and other evidence sufficient to reflect properly all costs claimed to have been incurred or anticipated to be incurred directly or indirectly in performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-0229; @CL-CMP-0230]. This right of examination shall include inspection at all reasonable times of the Contractor's plants, or parts of them, engaged in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-0231]. If the Contractor has been required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with any pricing action relating to the contract [@CL-CMP-0232], the Contracting Officer, or an authorized representative of the Contracting Officer, in order to evaluate the accuracy, completeness, and currency of the certified cost or pricing data [@CL-CMP-0233], shall have the right to examine and audit all of the Contractor's records, including computations and projections, related to any one of four alternatives: the proposal for the contract, subcontract, or modification; the discussions conducted on the proposal(s), including those related to negotiating [@CL-CMP-0234]; pricing of the contract, subcontract, or modification; or performance of the contract, subcontract, or modification [@CL-CMP-0235].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor stores records electronically for a cost-reimbursement contract. An authorized representative asks for records supporting 25,000 of claimed costs. The records fall within the clause even though they are not paper books.

## Never confuse

The clause reaches two different things: records includes books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of whether such items are in written form, in the form of computer data, or in any other form [@CL-CMP-0601], while the right of examination under paragraph (b) includes inspection at all reasonable times of the Contractor's plants, or parts of them, engaged in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-0604].
