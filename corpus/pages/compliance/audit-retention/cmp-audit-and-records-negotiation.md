## Purpose

Shows the records, cost-examination, plant-inspection, and certified-data audit rights established by the negotiation audit clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0601] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-2 [@CL-CMP-0601] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-2 [@CL-CMP-0227] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0601] |
| Applies to | Listed contract types for cost examination and certified-data records when required [@CL-CMP-0602; @CL-CMP-0605] |

The clause defines records broadly, regardless of type or form [@CL-CMP-0601]. For the listed contract types, the contractor must maintain cost records and evidence sufficient to reflect properly costs claimed to have been incurred or anticipated to be incurred directly or indirectly in performance of the contract, and the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative has examination and audit rights, including inspection at all reasonable times of the Contractor's plants, or parts of them, engaged in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-0602; @CL-CMP-0603; @CL-CMP-8536].

## How it works

The examination right includes reasonable-time inspection of plants or plant parts engaged in contract performance [@CL-CMP-0604]. When certified cost or pricing data is required, the audit right supports evaluation of its accuracy, completeness, and currency and extends to related records, computations, and projections [@CL-CMP-0605].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified FAR part 52 text, as Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.215-2 keeps the definition under which records includes books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of whether such items are in written form, in the form of computer data, or in any other form [@CL-CMP-0227; @CL-CMP-0601]. For a cost-reimbursement, incentive, time-and-materials, labor-hour, or price redeterminable contract, or any combination of these [@CL-CMP-0228; @CL-CMP-0602], it keeps the rule that the Contractor shall maintain, and the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative shall have the right to examine and audit, all records and other evidence sufficient to reflect properly all costs claimed to have been incurred or anticipated to be incurred directly or indirectly in performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-0229; @CL-CMP-0230; @CL-CMP-0603]. It keeps the rule that the right of examination includes inspection at all reasonable times of the Contractor's plants, or parts of them, engaged in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-0231; @CL-CMP-0604]. If the Contractor has been required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with any pricing action relating to the contract [@CL-CMP-0232], it keeps the right of the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative, in order to evaluate the accuracy, completeness, and currency of that data [@CL-CMP-0233], to examine and audit all of the Contractor's records, including computations and projections, related to the proposal for the contract, subcontract, or modification and the discussions conducted on the proposal or proposals, including those related to negotiating [@CL-CMP-0234], and to pricing and performance of the contract, subcontract, or modification [@CL-CMP-0235; @CL-CMP-0605].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor stores records electronically for a cost-reimbursement contract. An authorized representative asks for records supporting 25,000 of claimed costs. The records fall within the clause even though they are not paper books.

## Never confuse

Records are broader than paper books or accounting files. Examination and audit of records also should not be confused with plant inspection; the clause provides for both [@CL-CMP-0601; @CL-CMP-0604].
