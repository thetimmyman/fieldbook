## Purpose

Shows what the contract administration office confirms before a contract file is closed, and which of those items touch money.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2439] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.804-5 [@CL-CMP-2439; @CL-CMP-2440] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2439] |
| Applies to | Administrative closeout of a contract after the contract administration office receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2439] |

The contract administration office is responsible for initiating administrative closeout of the contract, automated or manual, after receiving evidence of its physical completion [@CL-CMP-2439]. At the outset, it must review the contract funds status and notify the contracting office of any excess funds it might deobligate [@CL-CMP-2517]. When complete, the administrative closeout procedures must ensure that the listed conditions are met, the first two being that disposition of classified material is completed and that the final patent report is cleared [@CL-CMP-2440]. If a final patent report is required, the contracting officer may proceed with contract closeout in accordance with the procedures that follow that paragraph in the regulation, or as otherwise prescribed by agency procedures; this card does not restate those patent procedures [@CL-CMP-2522]. When the files under the time standards for firm-fixed-price contracts other than those using simplified acquisition procedures, for contracts requiring settlement of indirect cost rates, and for all other contracts are closed, the contracting officer shall use these procedures, although the actions may be modified to reflect the extent of administration performed [@CL-CMP-2436; @CL-CMP-2433; @CL-CMP-2434; @CL-CMP-2435]. The time standards are on [Closeout time standards for contract files](cmp-contract-file-closeout-time-standards.md).

The next listed conditions are that the final royalty report is cleared, there is no outstanding value engineering change proposal, the plant clearance report is received, and property clearance is received [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2441]. They also include that all interim or disallowed costs are settled, price revision is completed, subcontracts are settled by the prime contractor, and prior year indirect cost rates are settled [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2442]. The remaining listed conditions are that the termination docket is completed, the contract audit is completed, the contractor's closing statement is completed, the contractor's final invoice has been submitted, and the contract funds review is completed with excess funds deobligated [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2443].

## How it works

When the listed actions have been verified, the contracting officer administering the contract must ensure that a contract completion statement, containing the required information, is prepared [@CL-CMP-2444]. The conditions that touch money include the settled interim or disallowed costs, the completed price revision, subcontracts settled by the prime contractor, the settled prior year indirect cost rates, the contractor's final invoice, and the funds review that deobligates excess funds [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2442; @CL-CMP-2443]. The funds review begins at the outset of closeout, when the contract administration office reviews the funds status and notifies the contracting office of any excess funds it might deobligate [@CL-CMP-2517]. The rate side is on [Final indirect cost rates: contracting officer determination procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-co-final-indirect-rates.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract of 1,500,000 is physically complete, so the contract administration office starts closeout. It confirms that plant clearance and property clearance are received, that prior year indirect rates are settled, that the audit is done, and that the contractor's closing statement and final invoice are in. The funds review shows 40,000 of excess funds, which are deobligated. Answer: the contracting officer then ensures the contract completion statement is prepared.

## Never confuse

The contract completion statement that the contracting officer must ensure is prepared is not the contractor's closing statement, which is a listed condition to be completed [@CL-CMP-2444; @CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2443]. Confirming these conditions is also not the time standard for closing the file; quick closeout is a separate early settlement route on [Quick-closeout procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-quick-closeout.md).
