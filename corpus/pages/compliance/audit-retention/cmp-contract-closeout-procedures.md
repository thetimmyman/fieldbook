## Purpose

Shows what the contract administration office confirms before a contract file is closed, and which of those items touch money.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2439] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.804-5 [@CL-CMP-2439; @CL-CMP-2440] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2439] |
| Applies to | Administrative closeout of a contract after the contract administration office receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2439] |

The contract administration office is responsible for initiating (automated or manual) administrative closeout of the contract after receiving evidence of its physical completion [@CL-CMP-2439]. At the outset of this process, the contract administration office must review the contract funds status and notify the contracting office of any excess funds the contract administration office might deobligate [@CL-CMP-2517]. When complete, the administrative closeout procedures must ensure that the listed conditions are met, the first two being that disposition of classified material is completed and that the final patent report is cleared [@CL-CMP-2440]. If a final patent report is required, the contracting officer may proceed with contract closeout in accordance with the following procedures, or as otherwise prescribed by agency procedures [@CL-CMP-2522]: final patent reports should be cleared within 60 days of receipt [@CL-CMP-0198]; if the final patent report is not received, the contracting officer shall notify the contractor of the contractor's obligations and the Government's rights under the applicable patent rights clause, in accordance with FAR 27.303 [@CL-CMP-0199]; and if the contractor fails to respond to this notification, the contracting officer may proceed with contract closeout upon consultation with the agency legal counsel responsible for patent matters regarding the contractor's failure to respond [@CL-CMP-0200]. When closing out the contract files covered by FAR 4.804-1(a)(2), (3), and (4) — files for firm-fixed-price contracts, other than those using simplified acquisition procedures, which should be closed within 6 months after the date on which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2433]; files for contracts requiring settlement of indirect cost rates, which should be closed within 36 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2434]; and files for all other contracts, which should be closed within 20 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2435] — the contracting officer shall use the closeout procedures at FAR 4.804-5, though these actions may be modified to reflect the extent of administration that has been performed [@CL-CMP-2436]. The time standards are on [Closeout time standards for contract files](cmp-contract-file-closeout-time-standards.md).

The next listed conditions are that the final royalty report is cleared, there is no outstanding value engineering change proposal, the plant clearance report is received, and property clearance is received [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2441]. They also include that all interim or disallowed costs are settled, price revision is completed, subcontracts are settled by the prime contractor, and prior year indirect cost rates are settled [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2442]. The remaining listed conditions are that the termination docket is completed, the contract audit is completed, the contractor's closing statement is completed, the contractor's final invoice has been submitted, and the contract funds review is completed and excess funds deobligated [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2443].

## How it works

When the actions in paragraph (a) have been verified, the contracting officer administering the contract must ensure that a contract completion statement, containing the following information, is prepared [@CL-CMP-2444]: contract administration office name and address (if different from the contracting office); contracting office name and address; contract number; last modification number; last call or order number; contractor name and address; dollar amount of excess funds, if any [@CL-CMP-0219]; voucher number and date, if final payment has been made; invoice number and date, if the final approved invoice has been forwarded to a disbursing office of another agency or activity and the status of the payment is unknown [@CL-CMP-0220]; a statement that all required contract administration actions have been fully and satisfactorily accomplished; name and signature of the contracting officer; and date [@CL-CMP-0221]. The conditions in paragraph (a) include that all interim or disallowed costs are settled, price revision is completed, subcontracts are settled by the prime contractor, prior year indirect cost rates are settled, the contractor's final invoice has been submitted, and the contract funds review is completed and excess funds deobligated [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2442; @CL-CMP-2443]. At the outset of the process, the contract administration office must review the contract funds status and notify the contracting office of any excess funds the contract administration office might deobligate [@CL-CMP-2517]. The rate side is on [Final indirect cost rates: contracting officer determination procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-co-final-indirect-rates.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract of 1,500,000 is physically complete, so the contract administration office starts closeout. It confirms that plant clearance and property clearance are received, that prior year indirect rates are settled, that the contract audit is done, and that the contractor's closing statement and final invoice are in. The funds review shows 40,000 of excess funds, which are deobligated. Answer: the contracting officer then ensures the contract completion statement is prepared.

## Never confuse

When the actions in paragraph (a) — including that the contractor's closing statement is completed [@CL-CMP-2440; @CL-CMP-2443] — have been verified, the contracting officer administering the contract must ensure that a contract completion statement containing the required items in paragraph (b) is prepared [@CL-CMP-2444; @CL-CMP-0219; @CL-CMP-0220; @CL-CMP-0221]. See [Closeout time standards for contract files](cmp-contract-file-closeout-time-standards.md) and [Quick-closeout procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-quick-closeout.md).
