## Purpose

Shows what agencies must do when notified that a contractor is in bankruptcy proceedings, which solicitations and contracts get the Bankruptcy clause, and what notice the clause requires of the contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2585] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.902, FAR 42.903 and FAR 52.242-13 [@CL-CMP-2585; @CL-CMP-2589; @CL-CMP-2650] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2585] |
| Applies to | Agencies notified of bankruptcy proceedings [@CL-CMP-2585]. Solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, for the Bankruptcy clause [@CL-CMP-2589]. A contractor under the clause that enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy, whether voluntary or involuntary [@CL-CMP-2650] |

When notified of bankruptcy proceedings, agencies shall, as a minimum, furnish the notice of bankruptcy to legal counsel and other appropriate agency offices and affected buying activities [@CL-CMP-2585]. Another listed minimum action is to determine the amount of the Government's potential claim against the contractor, identifying and reviewing any contracts that have not been closed out [@CL-CMP-2586]. Two further listed minimum actions are to take actions necessary to protect the Government's financial interests and safeguard Government property, and to furnish pertinent contract information to the legal counsel representing the Government [@CL-CMP-2587].

The contracting officer shall consult the legal counsel, whenever possible, before taking any action regarding the contractor's bankruptcy proceedings [@CL-CMP-2588]. The contracting officer shall insert the Bankruptcy clause in all solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-2589]. Under the Bankruptcy clause, if the contractor enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy, whether voluntary or involuntary, it agrees to furnish written notification of the bankruptcy by certified mail or an electronic commerce method authorized by the contract [@CL-CMP-2650]. The notification goes to the contracting officer responsible for administering the contract [@CL-CMP-2672]. It shall be furnished within five days of the initiation of the proceedings relating to bankruptcy filing [@CL-CMP-2651]. It shall include the date on which the bankruptcy petition was filed, the identity of the court, and a listing of Government contract numbers and contracting offices for all Government contracts against which final payment has not been made [@CL-CMP-2652]. The obligation remains in effect until final payment under the contract [@CL-CMP-2653].

## How it works

The agency learns of the proceedings and passes the notice to counsel, other offices and affected buying activities [@CL-CMP-2585]. It sizes the Government's potential claim by reviewing contracts that are not closed out [@CL-CMP-2586]. It then protects the Government's financial interests and property and gives counsel the contract information [@CL-CMP-2587]. The contracting officer consults counsel, whenever possible, before taking any action regarding the bankruptcy proceedings [@CL-CMP-2588]. The contractor's own duty under the clause is the written notification to the contracting officer, within five days, with the listed content [@CL-CMP-2651; @CL-CMP-2652].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor files for bankruptcy protection and the agency is notified. The agency finds three contracts that are not closed out, one of them physically complete, with possible Government claims of 150,000 in total. The agency sends the notice to counsel and the affected buying activities and reviews the three contracts to size the claim. The contracting officer consults counsel before taking any action on the bankruptcy. The contractor's contract carries the Bankruptcy clause, so the contractor notifies the contracting officer within five days of the start of the proceedings and lists every Government contract without final payment.

## Never confuse

Determining the Government's potential claim is not collecting it. The printed procedure covers assessing the claim, protecting the Government's interests and informing counsel [@CL-CMP-2586; @CL-CMP-2587]. The agency's notice of bankruptcy to counsel is also not the contractor's notice to the contracting officer: the first is an agency duty [@CL-CMP-2585], and the second is a duty the contractor takes on under the clause [@CL-CMP-2650]. Demands for payment of contract debts are on [Contract debts demand deferment and interest](../../contract-finance/cash/ctf-contract-debts.md).
