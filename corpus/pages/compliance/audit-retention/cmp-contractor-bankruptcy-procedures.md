## Purpose

Shows what agencies must do when notified that a contractor is in bankruptcy proceedings, which solicitations and contracts get the Bankruptcy clause, and what notice the clause requires of the contractor.

FAR subpart 42.9 prescribes policies and procedures regarding actions to be taken when a contractor enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy [@CL-CMP-9670], establishes a requirement for the contractor to notify the contracting officer upon filing a petition for bankruptcy [@CL-CMP-9671], and further establishes minimum requirements for agencies to follow in the event of a contractor bankruptcy [@CL-CMP-9672]. The contract administration office shall take prompt action to determine the potential impact of a contractor bankruptcy on the Government in order to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-CMP-9673].

When notified of bankruptcy proceedings, agencies shall, as a minimum, furnish the notice of bankruptcy to legal counsel and other appropriate agency offices (e.g., contracting, financial, property) and affected buying activities [@CL-CMP-2585]. Another listed minimum action is to determine the amount of the Government's potential claim against the contractor (in assessing this impact, identify and review any contracts that have not been closed out, including those physically completed or terminated) [@CL-CMP-2586]. Two further listed minimum actions are to take actions necessary to protect the Government's financial interests and safeguard Government property, and to furnish pertinent contract information to the legal counsel representing the Government [@CL-CMP-2587].

The contracting officer shall consult the legal counsel, whenever possible, prior to taking any action regarding the contractor's bankruptcy proceedings [@CL-CMP-2588]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.242-13, Bankruptcy, in all solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-2589]. Under the Bankruptcy clause, in the event the contractor enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy, whether voluntary or involuntary, the contractor agrees to furnish, by certified mail or electronic commerce method authorized by the contract, written notification of the bankruptcy [@CL-CMP-2650] to the contracting officer responsible for administering the contract [@CL-CMP-2672]. This notification shall be furnished within five days of the initiation of the proceedings relating to bankruptcy filing [@CL-CMP-2651]. This notification shall include the date on which the bankruptcy petition was filed, the identity of the court in which the bankruptcy petition was filed, and a listing of Government contract numbers and contracting offices for all Government contracts against which final payment has not been made [@CL-CMP-2652]. This obligation remains in effect until final payment under the contract [@CL-CMP-2653].

## How it works

When notified of bankruptcy proceedings, agencies shall, as a minimum, furnish the notice of bankruptcy to legal counsel and other appropriate agency offices (e.g., contracting, financial, property) and affected buying activities [@CL-CMP-2585], determine the amount of the Government's potential claim against the contractor (in assessing this impact, identify and review any contracts that have not been closed out, including those physically completed or terminated) [@CL-CMP-2586], take actions necessary to protect the Government's financial interests and safeguard Government property, and furnish pertinent contract information to the legal counsel representing the Government [@CL-CMP-2587]. The contracting officer shall consult the legal counsel, whenever possible, prior to taking any action regarding the contractor's bankruptcy proceedings [@CL-CMP-2588]. Under the Bankruptcy clause, if the contractor enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy, whether voluntary or involuntary, the contractor agrees to furnish written notification of the bankruptcy by certified mail or electronic commerce method authorized by the contract to the contracting officer responsible for administering the contract [@CL-CMP-2650; @CL-CMP-2672] within five days of the initiation of the proceedings relating to bankruptcy filing, including the date on which the bankruptcy petition was filed, the identity of the court in which the bankruptcy petition was filed, and a listing of Government contract numbers and contracting offices for all Government contracts against which final payment has not been made [@CL-CMP-2651; @CL-CMP-2652].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761], and use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 42.700 states that the subpart prescribes policies and procedures for actions to be taken when a contractor enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy [@CL-CMP-8955], and requires contractors to notify the contracting officer upon filing a petition for bankruptcy and establishes minimum requirements for agencies to follow in the event of a contractor bankruptcy [@CL-CMP-7372]. RFO FAR 42.701 states that the contract administration office must take prompt action to determine the potential impact of a contractor bankruptcy to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-CMP-7373]. RFO FAR 42.702(a) states that when notified of bankruptcy proceedings, agencies must, at a minimum, furnish the notice of bankruptcy to legal counsel and other appropriate agency offices (e.g., contracting, financial, property) and affected buying activities [@CL-CMP-6704]; determine the amount of the Government's potential claim against the contractor (in assessing this impact, identify and review any contracts that have not been closed out, including those physically completed or terminated) [@CL-CMP-6705]; and take actions necessary to protect the Government's financial interests and safeguard Government property, and furnish pertinent contract information to the legal counsel representing the Government [@CL-CMP-6706]. RFO FAR 42.702(b) states that the contracting officer will consult with legal counsel, whenever possible, prior to taking any action regarding the contractor's bankruptcy proceedings [@CL-CMP-6707]. RFO FAR 42.703 states that the contracting officer must insert the clause at 52.242-13, Bankruptcy, in all solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-6708]. RFO FAR 52.242-13 states that in the event the contractor enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy, whether voluntary or involuntary, the contractor agrees to furnish, by certified mail or electronic commerce method authorized by the contract, written notification of the bankruptcy [@CL-CMP-6709] to the contracting officer responsible for administering the contract [@CL-CMP-6710]. This notification shall be furnished within five days of the initiation of the proceedings relating to bankruptcy filing [@CL-CMP-6711] and shall include the date on which the bankruptcy petition was filed, the identity of the court in which the bankruptcy petition was filed, and a listing of Government contract numbers and contracting offices for all Government contracts against which final payment has not been made [@CL-CMP-6712]. This obligation remains in effect until final payment under the contract [@CL-CMP-6713].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor files for bankruptcy protection and the agency is notified. The agency finds three contracts that are not closed out, one of them physically complete, with possible Government claims of 150,000 in total. The agency sends the notice to counsel and the affected buying activities and reviews the three contracts to size the claim. The contracting officer consults counsel before taking any action on the bankruptcy. The contractor's contract carries the Bankruptcy clause, so the contractor notifies the contracting officer within five days of the start of the proceedings and lists every Government contract without final payment.

## Never confuse

When notified of bankruptcy proceedings, agencies shall, as a minimum, furnish the notice of bankruptcy to legal counsel and other appropriate agency offices (e.g., contracting, financial, property) and affected buying activities [@CL-CMP-2585], determine the amount of the Government's potential claim against the contractor (identifying and reviewing any contracts that have not been closed out, including those physically completed or terminated) [@CL-CMP-2586], take actions necessary to protect the Government's financial interests and safeguard Government property, and furnish pertinent contract information to the legal counsel representing the Government [@CL-CMP-2587], whereas under the Bankruptcy clause, if the contractor enters into proceedings relating to bankruptcy, whether voluntary or involuntary, the contractor agrees to furnish written notification of the bankruptcy by certified mail or electronic commerce method authorized by the contract to the contracting officer responsible for administering the contract [@CL-CMP-2650; @CL-CMP-2672]. Demands for payment of contract debts are on [Contract debts demand deferment and interest](../../contract-finance/cash/ctf-contract-debts.md).
