## Question

Which control topics and records should the first audit and compliance meetings cover, with which owner roles?

## Recommendation

Open with business-system status, incurred-cost status and unallowable cost screening, because each carries money or a deadline. Ask for the records named below and record every answer as discovery, not a finding.

## Evidence

- DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective 2026-03-16: a final determination of material weaknesses with a notice to withhold takes 5 percent of payments [@CL-CMP-0004], up to 10 percent across several systems [@CL-CMP-0006]. Ask the compliance owner for each system's status and determination letter.
- Codified FAR 52.216-7: a final indirect cost rate proposal is due within 6 months of year end [@CL-RTE-0001]. Ask the rates owner for incurred-cost status by year.
- Codified FAR 42.709-1(b): the unallowable cost penalty reaches contracts over $1 million, except the excluded fixed-price types [@CL-CMP-0005]. Ask the accounting owner for the unallowable account list and screening.
- CAS Board rule, 48 CFR 9903, effective 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0001]: full CAS coverage and the Disclosure Statement start at $100 million [@CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-0003]. Ask for each unit's coverage and Disclosure Statement.
- DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0011 Rev 1, DFARS 234.201-70, effective 2026-08-05 (it supersedes the version of 2025-12-18 [@CL-PRG-9161]; the start of these values is not established), for cost or incentive contracts and subcontracts except the exempt contracts [@CL-PRG-0003]: the earned value system must follow EIA-748 at $50 million or more [@CL-PRG-0001], and at $100 million or more the cognizant agency must have found it compliant [@CL-PRG-0002]. Codified DFARS 234.201 sets them at $20 million and $50 million [@CL-PRG-9144; @CL-PRG-9145]. Ask the program finance owner which contracts fall in each band, under which text, with any determination letter.

## Options

Walking the audit manual chapter by chapter was rejected: it is long and not tied to decisions. Starting from open findings alone was rejected: which are open is unknown.

- Open control findings and external requests, and their owners [@Q-014].
- Whether the purchasing system has been reviewed [@Q-040].
- Which contracts carry an earned value requirement [@Q-037].
