## Question

Which control topics and records should the first audit and compliance meetings cover, with which owner roles, and why?

## Recommendation

Open with business-system status, incurred-cost status and unallowable cost screening, because each carries money or a deadline. Ask for the records named below. Record every answer as discovery: nothing here is a finding about the company.

## Evidence

- A final determination of material weaknesses with a notice to withhold takes 5 percent of payments [@CL-CMP-0004], up to 10 percent across several systems [@CL-CMP-0006]. Ask the compliance owner for each system's status and latest determination letter.
- A final indirect cost rate proposal is due within 6 months of year end [@CL-RTE-0001]. Ask the rates owner for the incurred-cost status by year and the submission log.
- The unallowable cost penalty reaches contracts over $1 million [@CL-CMP-0005]. Ask the accounting owner for the unallowable account list and how proposals are screened.
- Full CAS coverage and the Disclosure Statement start at $100 million [@CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-0003]. Ask for each unit's coverage and Disclosure Statement.
- A compliant earned value system is required at $100 million [@CL-PRG-0002]. Ask the program finance owner for any validation letter.

## Options

Walking the audit manual chapter by chapter was rejected: it is long and not tied to decisions. Starting from open findings alone was rejected: which findings are open is itself unknown.

## Unknowns

- The government status of each contractor business system [@Q-034].
- Which fiscal years are open for incurred cost [@Q-035].
- How unallowable costs are kept out of rate and billing pools [@Q-044].
- CAS coverage by business unit [@Q-033].
- Which control findings and external requests are open, and who owns each [@Q-014].
- Whether the purchasing system has been reviewed [@Q-040].
- Which contracts carry an earned value requirement [@Q-037].

## Next action

The compliance lead revisits this brief when the first meeting's records arrive, or a month after the meeting if they have not.
