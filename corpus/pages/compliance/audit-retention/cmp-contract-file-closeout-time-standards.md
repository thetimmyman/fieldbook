## Purpose

Shows how soon each kind of contract file should be closed, what starts each clock, and when a file must stay open.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2433] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.804-1 [@CL-CMP-2433; @CL-CMP-2521] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2433] |
| Applies to | Closing out contract files, except as provided in FAR 4.804-1(c) [@CL-CMP-2521; @CL-CMP-2438] |

Except as provided in FAR 4.804-1(c), the time standards for closing out contract files are as follows [@CL-CMP-2521]: files for contracts using simplified acquisition procedures should be considered closed when the contracting officer receives evidence of receipt of property and final payment, unless otherwise specified by agency regulations [@CL-CMP-2521; @CL-CMP-2432]; files for firm-fixed-price contracts, other than those using simplified acquisition procedures, should be closed within 6 months after the date on which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2521; @CL-CMP-2433]; files for contracts requiring settlement of indirect cost rates should be closed within 36 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2521; @CL-CMP-2434]; and files for all other contracts should be closed within 20 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2521; @CL-CMP-2435].

A contract file shall not be closed if the contract is in litigation or under appeal, or if, in the case of a termination, all termination actions have not been completed [@CL-CMP-2438].

## How it works

Files for firm-fixed-price contracts, other than those using simplified acquisition procedures, should be closed within 6 months after the date on which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2433]; files for contracts requiring settlement of indirect cost rates should be closed within 36 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2434]; and files for all other contracts should be closed within 20 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion [@CL-CMP-2435]. Files for contracts using simplified acquisition procedures should be considered closed when the contracting officer receives evidence of receipt of property and final payment, unless otherwise specified by agency regulations [@CL-CMP-2432]. When closing out the contract files covered by FAR 4.804-1(a)(2), (3), and (4), the contracting officer shall use the closeout procedures at FAR 4.804-5, although these actions may be modified to reflect the extent of administration that has been performed [@CL-CMP-2436]. The procedures are on [Procedures for closing out contract files](cmp-contract-closeout-procedures.md). Quick closeout procedures (see FAR 42.708) should be used, when appropriate, to reduce administrative costs and to enable deobligation of excess funds [@CL-CMP-2437]. The quick-closeout procedure and its DoD threshold are on [Quick-closeout procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-quick-closeout.md) and [DoD quick-closeout threshold for unsettled costs](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-dod-quick-closeout-threshold.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Evidence of physical completion arrives in March of year 1 for three contracts. A firm-fixed-price contract of 1,000,000 should be closed within 6 months of that date, so by September of year 1. A contract needing indirect rate settlement should be closed within 36 months of March, so by March of year 4. Any other contract should be closed within 20 months of March, so by November of year 2. If the first contract is under appeal, its file is not closed. Answer: the contract type sets the target, and litigation or an unfinished termination holds the file open.

## Never confuse

Except as provided in FAR 4.804-1(c), files should be closed within 6 months after the date on which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion for firm-fixed-price contracts other than those using simplified acquisition procedures [@CL-CMP-2521; @CL-CMP-2433], within 36 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion for contracts requiring settlement of indirect cost rates [@CL-CMP-2434], and within 20 months of the month in which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion for all other contracts [@CL-CMP-2435], whereas a contract file shall not be closed if the contract is in litigation or under appeal, or if, in the case of a termination, all termination actions have not been completed [@CL-CMP-2438]. Quick closeout procedures (see FAR 42.708) should be used, when appropriate, to reduce administrative costs and to enable deobligation of excess funds [@CL-CMP-2437], as covered on the linked pages.
