## Purpose

Shows who signs a contract for each kind of contractor, and how the contractor's taxpayer identification information reaches the payment office.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-2400; @CL-CMP-9756] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.102 and FAR 4.203 [@CL-CMP-2400; @CL-CMP-2409]; DFARS 204.203(b) [@CL-CMP-9756] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2400] |
| Applies to | Contracts with individuals, partnerships, corporations, joint venturers and agents, and the contractor's TIN and type of organization sent to the payment office [@CL-CMP-2400; @CL-CMP-2401; @CL-CMP-2402; @CL-CMP-2403; @CL-CMP-2404; @CL-CMP-2408] |

A contract with an individual shall be signed by that individual, and a contract with an individual doing business as a firm shall be signed by that individual, with the signature followed by the individual's typed, stamped, or printed name and the words ", an individual doing business as [name of firm]" [@CL-CMP-2400; @CL-CMP-0243]. A contract with a partnership shall be signed in the partnership name, and before signing for the Government, the contracting officer shall obtain a list of all partners and ensure that the individual(s) signing for the partnership have authority to bind the partnership [@CL-CMP-2401]. A contract with a corporation shall be signed in the corporate name, followed by the word "by" and the signature and title of the person authorized to sign, and the contracting officer shall ensure that the person signing for the corporation has authority to bind the corporation [@CL-CMP-2402]. A contract with joint venturers may involve any combination of individuals, partnerships, or corporations, and the contract shall be signed by each participant in the joint venture in the manner prescribed in FAR 4.102(a) through (c) [@CL-CMP-2403]; when a corporation is participating, the contracting officer shall verify that the corporation is authorized to participate in the joint venture [@CL-CMP-2523]. When an agent is to sign the contract, other than as stated in FAR 4.102(a) through (d), the agent's authority to bind the principal must be established by evidence satisfactory to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-2404].

Taxpayer identification information consists of the contractor's Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and type of organization [@CL-CMP-2408]. Where the contractor has furnished a TIN when completing the solicitation provision at FAR 52.204-3, Taxpayer Identification, or paragraph (l) of the solicitation provision at FAR 52.212-3, Offeror Representations and Certifications—Commercial Products and Commercial Services, the contracting officer shall, unless otherwise provided in agency procedures, attach a copy of the completed solicitation provision as the last page of the copy of the contract sent to the payment office [@CL-CMP-2405; @CL-CMP-2406].

## How it works

Where the contractor has furnished a TIN when completing the solicitation provision at FAR 52.204-3 or FAR 52.212-3(l), the contracting officer shall, unless otherwise provided in agency procedures, attach a copy of the completed solicitation provision as the last page of the copy of the contract sent to the payment office [@CL-CMP-2405; @CL-CMP-2406]. If the TIN or type of organization is derived from a source other than the provision at FAR 52.204-3 or FAR 52.212-3(l), the contracting officer shall, unless the information is being provided to the payment office in accordance with agency procedures, annotate the last page of the contract or order forwarded to the payment office to state the contractor's TIN and type of organization [@CL-CMP-2407; @CL-CMP-2408]. If the contractor provides its TIN or type of organization to the contracting officer after award, the contracting officer shall forward the information to the appropriate payment office within 7 days of its receipt [@CL-CMP-2409].

Under DFARS 204.203(b), the procedure at FAR 4.203(b) does not apply to contracts that include the provision at FAR 52.204-7, System for Award Management, and the payment office obtains the taxpayer identification number and the type of organization from the System for Award Management database [@CL-CMP-9756].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A two-person partnership signs a 600,000 supply contract. Before signing for the Government, the contracting officer obtains the list of both partners and checks that the signers can bind the partnership. The partnership sends its TIN 3 days after award, and the contracting officer forwards it to the payment office within 7 days of receipt, so by day 10 at the latest. Answer: the contract is signed in the partnership name, the partner list is on file, and the TIN reaches the payment office by day 10.

## Never confuse

Who may sign differs by entity: for a partnership, the contracting officer shall obtain a list of all partners and ensure that the individual(s) signing for the partnership have authority to bind the partnership [@CL-CMP-2401]; for a corporation, the contracting officer shall ensure that the person signing for the corporation has authority to bind the corporation [@CL-CMP-2402]; and when an agent is to sign the contract, other than as stated in FAR 4.102(a) through (d), the agent's authority to bind the principal must be established by evidence satisfactory to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-2404]. See [Making payments](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-making-payments.md) for how payments are made.
