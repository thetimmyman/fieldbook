## Purpose

Shows how agencies handle, store and dispose of contract files, and which retention periods matter to a finance reader and what starts each one.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2445] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.805 [@CL-CMP-2445; @CL-CMP-2446] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2445] |
| Applies to | Agency contract files and the listed record types in the retention table [@CL-CMP-2445; @CL-CMP-2446] |

Agencies must prescribe procedures for the handling, storing and disposing of contract files, in accordance with the NARA General Records Schedule 1.1, Financial Management and Reporting Records [@CL-CMP-2445]. The retention table sets a period of 6 years after final payment for contracts and related records or documents, including successful and unsuccessful proposals, except contractor payrolls submitted under construction contracts [@CL-CMP-2446].

Files for canceled solicitations have a retention period of 6 years after cancellation [@CL-CMP-2447]. One listed row covers investigations, cases pending or in litigation (including protests), or similar matters (including enforcement actions) [@CL-CMP-2448]. That row sets the retention period as until final clearance or settlement, or, if related to a document identified in paragraphs (c)(1) through (7) of FAR 4.805, for the retention period specified for the related document in paragraphs (c)(1) through (7), whichever is later [@CL-CMP-2448; @CL-CMP-2449].

An agency that requires a shorter retention period than those identified in Table 4-1 shall request approval from NARA through the agency's records officer [@CL-CMP-2519]. Unless law or other regulations require signed originals to be kept, agencies may destroy signed originals after the responsible agency official verifies that record copies on alternate media and copies reproduced from the record copy are accurate, complete, and clear representations of the originals [@CL-CMP-2518].

## How it works

For contracts (and related records or documents, including successful and unsuccessful proposals, except contractor payrolls submitted under construction contracts), the retention period is 6 years after final payment [@CL-CMP-2446]; for files for canceled solicitations, the retention period is 6 years after cancellation [@CL-CMP-2447]; and for investigations, cases pending or in litigation (including protests), or similar matters (including enforcement actions), the retention period is until final clearance or settlement, or, if related to a document identified in paragraphs (c)(1) through (7), for the retention period specified for the related document in paragraphs (c)(1) through (7), whichever is later [@CL-CMP-2448; @CL-CMP-2449].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Final payment on a 4,000,000 contract is made at the end of year 2, so the contract file is kept until the end of year 8. A protest related to that contract is still pending at the end of year 8. Answer: the file is kept until final clearance or settlement, because that is later than the end of year 8.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 4.805, agencies must prescribe procedures for the handling, storing, and disposing of contract files in accordance with the NARA General Records Schedule 1.1, Financial Management and Reporting Records [@CL-CMP-2445], whereas under FAR 4.703, contractors must make available records, which include books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of whether such items are in written form, in the form of computer data, or in any other form, and other supporting evidence to satisfy contract negotiation, administration, and audit requirements of the contracting agencies and the Comptroller General [@CL-CMP-2417; @CL-CMP-8206] for 3 years after final payment or, for certain records, the period specified in FAR 4.705 through 4.705-3, whichever of these periods expires first [@CL-CMP-2418], and contractors must retain records for a longer period than that specified in FAR 4.703(a) under the conditions in FAR 4.703(b), such as if the contractor, for its own purposes, retains records relating to contract performance for a longer period or if a contract clause requires retention for a longer period [@CL-CMP-2419]. That rule is on [Contractor records retention policy](cmp-contractor-records-retention-policy.md), and the Government's file contents are on [Government contract files: file types and contents](cmp-government-contract-files.md). For investigations, cases pending or in litigation (including protests), or similar matters (including enforcement actions), the retention period is until final clearance or settlement, or, if related to a document identified in paragraphs (c)(1) through (7), for the retention period specified for the related document in paragraphs (c)(1) through (7), whichever is later [@CL-CMP-2448; @CL-CMP-2449].
