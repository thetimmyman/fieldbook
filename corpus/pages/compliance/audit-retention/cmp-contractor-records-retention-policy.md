## Purpose

Shows how long a contractor must keep contract records available for negotiation, administration and audit, which contracts the retention rules cover, the specific periods for financial, pay and acquisition records, and which cases make the period longer.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2417] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.702, FAR 4.703 and FAR 4.705 [@CL-CMP-2500; @CL-CMP-2418; @CL-CMP-2423] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2417] |
| Applies to | Records generated under contracts that contain one of the FAR 4.702(a) audit and records clauses, and record retention periods under contracts subject to 10 U.S.C. chapter 137 legacy provisions (10 U.S.C. 3064) and 10 U.S.C. 3016 and chapter 203 or 40 U.S.C. 101, et seq., except Department of Energy contracts for which the Comptroller General allows alternative retention periods [@CL-CMP-2500; @CL-CMP-2501; @CL-CMP-0224] |

FAR subpart 4.7 applies to records generated under contracts that contain one of the following clauses: Audit and Records—Sealed Bidding (FAR 52.214-26) or Audit and Records—Negotiation (FAR 52.215-2) [@CL-CMP-2500]. FAR subpart 4.7 is not mandatory on Department of Energy contracts for which the Comptroller General allows alternative records retention periods [@CL-CMP-2501]; apart from this exception, the subpart applies to record retention periods under contracts that are subject to chapter 137 legacy provisions of title 10 of the United States Code (section 3064 of title 10) and section 3016 and chapter 203 of title 10 or section 101 and following of title 40 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-0224].

Except as stated in FAR 4.703(b), contractors must make available records, which include books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of whether such items are in written form, in the form of computer data, or in any other form, and other supporting evidence to satisfy contract negotiation, administration, and audit requirements of the contracting agencies and the Comptroller General for 3 years after final payment or, for certain records, the period specified in FAR 4.705 through 4.705-3, whichever of these periods expires first [@CL-CMP-2417; @CL-CMP-8206; @CL-CMP-2418].

FAR 4.705-1, 4.705-2, and 4.705-3 apply to the records generated under contracts in each of those areas — financial and cost accounting records, pay administration records, and acquisition and supply records — and the contractor must retain those records for the periods designated, provided retention is required under FAR 4.702; FAR 4.703(a) sets 3 years after final payment or, for certain records, the period specified in FAR 4.705 through 4.705-3, whichever of these periods expires first [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2418; @CL-CMP-2500]. Records are identified in FAR subpart 4.7 in terms of their purpose or use and not by specific name or form number [@CL-CMP-2502].

### Specific periods that matter to finance

Each period below is one of the specific periods designated in FAR 4.705-1 through 4.705-3, which apply, provided retention is required under FAR 4.702 [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2418; @CL-CMP-2500].

- Accounts receivable invoices, adjustments to the accounts, invoice registers, carrier freight bills, shipping orders, and other documents which detail the material or services billed on the related invoices: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2424].
- Material, work order, or service order files, consisting of purchase requisitions or purchase orders for material or services, or orders for transfer of material or supplies: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2503].
- Cash advance recapitulations, prepared as posting entries to accounts receivable ledgers for amounts of expense vouchers prepared for employees' travel and related expenses: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2504].
- Paid, canceled, and voided checks, other than those issued for the payment of salary and wages: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2505].
- Labor cost distribution cards or equivalent documents: retain 2 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2506].
- Petty cash records showing description of expenditures, to whom paid, name of person authorizing payment, and date, including copies of vouchers and other supporting documents: retain 2 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2507].
- Payroll sheets, registers, or their equivalent, of salaries and wages paid to individual employees for each payroll period; change slips; and tax withholding statements: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2508].
- Clock cards or other time and attendance cards: retain 2 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2509].
- Paid checks, receipts for wages paid in cash, or other evidence of payments for services rendered by employees: retain 2 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2510].
- Store requisitions for materials, supplies, equipment, and services: retain 2 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2511].
- Work orders for maintenance and other services: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2512].
- Equipment records, consisting of equipment usage and status reports and equipment repair orders: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2513].
- Expendable property records, reflecting accountability for the receipt and use of material in the performance of a contract: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2514].
- Receiving and inspection report records, consisting of reports reflecting receipt and inspection of supplies, equipment, and materials: retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2515].
- Property records (see FAR 45.101 and 52.245-1): retain 4 years [@CL-CMP-2423; @CL-CMP-2516].

## How it works

Contractors must retain records for a longer period than that specified in FAR 4.703(a) under any one of three alternatives [@CL-CMP-2419]: if the contractor, for its own purposes, retains the foregoing records and supporting evidence for a longer period, the retention period shall be the period of the contractor's retention or 3 years after final payment, whichever period expires first [@CL-CMP-2419; @CL-CMP-2420]; if a contract clause specifies a retention period longer than the period specified in FAR subpart 4.7, then the retention period for those records shall be the period specified in the clause [@CL-CMP-2419]; or if the contractor does not meet the original due date for submission of final indirect cost rate proposals specified in paragraph (d)(2) of the clause at FAR 52.216-7, Allowable Cost and Payment [@CL-CMP-2421], the retention periods in FAR 4.705 are automatically extended one day for each day the proposal is not submitted after the original due date [@CL-CMP-2422]. Nothing in FAR 4.703 shall be construed to preclude a contractor from duplicating or storing original records in electronic form unless they contain significant information not shown on the record copy, and original records need not be maintained or produced in an audit if the contractor or subcontractor provides photographic or electronic images of the original records and meets the following requirements [@CL-CMP-2425]: the contractor or subcontractor has established procedures to ensure that the imaging process preserves accurate images of the original records, including signatures and other written or graphic images, and that the imaging process is reliable and secure so as to maintain the integrity of the records [@CL-CMP-0225; @CL-CMP-0226]; the contractor or subcontractor maintains an effective indexing system to permit timely and convenient access to the imaged records [@CL-CMP-0226]; and the contractor or subcontractor retains the original records for a minimum of one year after imaging to permit periodic validation of the imaging systems [@CL-CMP-2426].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost contract receives final payment at the end of year 1, and no clause, specific section or own-retention choice changes the period. The records are made available through the end of year 4, which is 3 years after final payment. Separately, a contract clause on another contract requires the records to be kept 7 years after final payment, so those records are made available through the end of year 8 under that clause. Answer: the basic period runs from final payment, and a longer period specified in a contract clause applies instead of the shorter basic period.

## Never confuse

Except as stated in FAR 4.703(b), contractors must make available records and other supporting evidence to satisfy contract negotiation, administration, and audit requirements of the contracting agencies and the Comptroller General for 3 years after final payment or, for certain records, the period specified in FAR 4.705 through 4.705-3, whichever of these periods expires first [@CL-CMP-2417; @CL-CMP-8206; @CL-CMP-2418]. For the Audit and Records, Negotiation clause and agency contract file storage and disposal, see [Audit and Records, Negotiation clause](cmp-audit-and-records-negotiation.md) and [Storage, handling and disposal of contract files](cmp-contract-file-storage-and-disposal.md).
