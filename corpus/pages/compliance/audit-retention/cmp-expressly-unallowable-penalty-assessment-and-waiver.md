## Purpose

Sets out when the contracting officer assesses the penalty on unallowable costs, how the penalty amount and interest are worked out, and when the penalty must be waived.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-1728] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.709-4 [@CL-CMP-1728] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.508-4 [@CL-CMP-0047; @CL-CMP-0048; @CL-CMP-0049; @CL-CMP-0050] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-1728] |
| Applies to | Proposals with a submitted cost that is expressly unallowable under a cost principle, or that was determined unallowable for the contractor before the proposal was submitted [@CL-CMP-1728; @CL-CMP-1729] |

Unless a waiver is granted pursuant to FAR 42.709-6, the cognizant contracting officer shall take the actions in FAR 42.709-4 [@CL-CMP-1857]. The first two are alternatives: assessing the penalty in FAR 42.709-2(a)(1) when the submitted cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR or an executive agency supplement that defines the allowability of specific selected costs [@CL-CMP-1728], or assessing the penalty in FAR 42.709-2(a)(2) when the submitted cost was determined to be unallowable for that contractor prior to submission of the proposal [@CL-CMP-1729]. The third action is issuing a final decision (see FAR 33.211) which includes a demand for payment of any penalty assessed under paragraph (a) or (b) of FAR 42.709-4, and the letter shall state that the determination is a final decision under the Disputes clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-1730]. Which contracts are covered is on [Penalties for unallowable costs in final rate proposals](../cas-allowability/cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md).

Under FAR 42.709-2(a)(1), if the indirect cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR, or an executive agency supplement to the FAR, that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, the penalty is equal to the amount of the disallowed costs allocated to contracts that are subject to the section for which an indirect cost proposal has been submitted, plus interest on the paid portion, if any, of the disallowance [@CL-CMP-0482; @CL-CMP-1859]. Under FAR 42.709-2(a)(2), if the indirect cost was determined to be unallowable for that contractor before proposal submission, the penalty is two times the amount in paragraph (a)(1)(i) of that section [@CL-CMP-1860; @CL-CMP-1859]. The cognizant contracting officer shall waive the penalties at FAR 42.709-2(a) when any one of three alternatives applies: the contractor withdraws the proposal before the Government formally initiates an audit of the proposal and the contractor submits a revised proposal [@CL-CMP-1733]; the amount of the unallowable costs under the proposal which are subject to the penalty is $10,000 or less (i.e., if the amount of expressly or previously determined unallowable costs which would be allocated to the contracts specified in FAR 42.709-1(b) is $10,000 or less) [@CL-CMP-1734]; or the contractor demonstrates, to the cognizant contracting officer's satisfaction [@CL-CMP-1864], both that it has established policies and personnel training and an internal control and review system that provide assurance that unallowable costs subject to penalties are precluded from being included in the contractor's final indirect cost rate proposals (for example, the types of controls required for satisfactory participation in Department of Defense sponsored self-governance programs, specific accounting controls over indirect costs, compliance tests which demonstrate that the controls are effective, and Government audits which have not disclosed recurring instances of expressly unallowable costs) [@CL-CMP-1863; @CL-CMP-0246; @CL-CMP-0247] and that the unallowable costs subject to the penalty were inadvertently incorporated into the proposal, i.e., their inclusion resulted from an unintentional error, notwithstanding the exercise of due care [@CL-CMP-1735].

## How it works

Unless a waiver is granted pursuant to FAR 42.709-6, the cognizant contracting officer assesses the penalty in FAR 42.709-2(a)(1) when the submitted cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR or an executive agency supplement that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, or assesses the penalty in FAR 42.709-2(a)(2) when the submitted cost was determined to be unallowable for that contractor prior to submission of the proposal [@CL-CMP-1857; @CL-CMP-1728; @CL-CMP-1729]. For FAR 42.709-2(a)(1)(ii), interest on any paid portion of the disallowed cost is computed under FAR 42.709-5 [@CL-CMP-1861]: the overpayment is considered to have occurred, and interest to have begun accumulating, from the midpoint of the contractor's fiscal year, with an alternate equitable method used if the cost was not paid evenly over the fiscal year [@CL-CMP-1731]; the interest rate specified by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to Public Law 92-41 (85 Stat 97) is used [@CL-CMP-0248]; interest is computed from the date of overpayment to the date of the demand letter for payment of the penalty [@CL-CMP-1732]; and the paid portion of the disallowed costs is determined in consultation with the contract auditor [@CL-CMP-0249]. Demanding payment of the penalty is separate from demanding repayment of any paid portion of the disallowed cost [@CL-CMP-1858]. For the withdrawal waiver ground, an audit will be deemed to be formally initiated when the Government provides the contractor with written notice, or holds an entrance conference, indicating that audit work on a specific final indirect cost proposal has begun [@CL-CMP-1862].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.508-4 states that unless a waiver is granted pursuant to 42.508-6, the cognizant contracting officer must [@CL-CMP-0047] assess the penalty in 42.508-2(a)(1) when the submitted cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR or an executive agency supplement that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, or assess the penalty in 42.508-2(a)(2) when the submitted cost was determined to be unallowable for that contractor prior to submission of the proposal [@CL-CMP-0048; @CL-CMP-0049], and issue a final decision (see part 33) which includes a demand for payment of any penalty assessed under paragraph (a) or (b) of that section, with the letter stating that the determination is a final decision under the Disputes clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-0050]. The demand for payment of the penalty is separate from a demand for repayment of any paid portion of the disallowed cost [@CL-CMP-0051]. Under RFO FAR 42.508-2(a), if the indirect cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR, or an executive agency supplement to the FAR, that defines the allowability of specific selected costs [@CL-CMP-0081], the penalty is equal to the amount of the disallowed costs allocated to contracts that are subject to the section for which an indirect cost proposal has been submitted, plus interest on the paid portion, if any, of the disallowance [@CL-CMP-0052]; and if the indirect cost was determined to be unallowable for that contractor before proposal submission, the penalty is two times the amount in paragraph (a)(1)(i) of that section [@CL-CMP-0053]. RFO FAR 42.508-5 states that the overpayment is considered to have occurred, and interest to have begun accumulating, from the midpoint of the contractor's fiscal year, with an alternate equitable method used if the cost was not paid evenly over the fiscal year [@CL-CMP-0054], and that interest is computed from the date of overpayment to the date of the demand letter for payment of the penalty [@CL-CMP-0055]. RFO FAR 42.508-6 states that the cognizant contracting officer must waive the penalties at 42.508-2(a) when any one of three alternatives applies [@CL-CMP-0056]: the contractor withdraws the proposal before formal audit initiation (written notice to the contractor or an entrance conference with the contractor is held, indicating that audit work on a specific final indirect cost proposal has begun) and submits a revised proposal as required by section 4303(c) of title 41 and section 3743(c) of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-0057; @CL-CMP-0058]; the unallowable costs subject to the penalty are $10,000 or less [@CL-CMP-0059]; or the contractor demonstrates, to the cognizant contracting officer's satisfaction [@CL-CMP-0060], both that it has established policies and personnel training and an internal control and review system that provide assurance that unallowable costs subject to penalties are precluded from being included in the contractor's final indirect cost rate proposals [@CL-CMP-0061] and that the unallowable costs subject to the penalty were inadvertently included into the proposal despite due care [@CL-CMP-0062].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor includes 8,000 of expressly unallowable costs in an indirect proposal, all allocated to covered contracts. Because the amount subject to the penalty is 10,000 or less, the contracting officer waives the penalty. In a second case, 50,000 of expressly unallowable costs is allocated to covered contracts and part of it was paid during the year. The first penalty is 50,000 plus interest on the paid part, with interest running from the midpoint of the fiscal year to the date of the demand letter. In a third case, a cost the government had already determined unallowable for the contractor is allocated at 20,000, so the second penalty is 40,000.

## Never confuse

Demanding payment of the penalty is separate from demanding repayment of any paid portion of the disallowed cost [@CL-CMP-1858]. Under FAR 42.709-4 and 42.709-2(a), the first penalty applies when the submitted indirect cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR or an executive agency supplement that defines the allowability of specific selected costs, and equals the disallowed costs allocated to contracts subject to the section for which an indirect cost proposal has been submitted plus interest on the paid portion, if any, whereas the second penalty applies when the indirect cost was determined to be unallowable for that contractor before proposal submission, at two times the amount in paragraph (a)(1)(i) [@CL-CMP-1728; @CL-CMP-0482; @CL-CMP-1859; @CL-CMP-1729; @CL-CMP-1860]. For who recommends and who decides, see [Penalties on expressly unallowable costs: auditor and contracting officer roles](cmp-expressly-unallowable-penalty-roles.md).

Under the codified waiver in FAR 42.709-6(c)(2), the second showing is that the unallowable costs subject to the penalty were inadvertently incorporated into the proposal, i.e., their inclusion resulted from an unintentional error, notwithstanding the exercise of due care [@CL-CMP-1735], while under RFO FAR 42.508-6(c)(2), the second showing is that the unallowable costs subject to the penalty were inadvertently included into the proposal despite due care [@CL-CMP-0062].
