## Purpose

Explains what the imputed cost of money is, the conditions that make it allowable, and why actual interest cannot be substituted for it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0756] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-10 [@CL-CMP-0756] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-10 [@CL-CMP-9385] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0756] |
| Applies to | Cost of money as an incurred cost under applicable cost-reimbursement contracts and for progress payment purposes under fixed-price contracts [@CL-CMP-0757] |

Cost of money is an imputed cost, and it is not a form of interest on borrowings [@CL-CMP-0756]. It is an incurred cost for cost-reimbursement purposes under applicable cost-reimbursement contracts and for progress payment purposes under fixed-price contracts [@CL-CMP-0757]. It refers to facilities capital cost of money and to cost of money as an element of the cost of capital assets under construction, each under its own cost accounting standard [@CL-CMP-0758].

Cost of money is allowable, provided the three listed conditions are all met [@CL-CMP-9408] [@CL-CMP-8279]. The first is that it is measured, assigned, and allocated to contracts in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.414 or measured and added to the cost of capital assets under construction in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.417, as applicable [@CL-CMP-0759]. The second is that the requirements of 31.205-52, which limit the allowability of cost of money, are followed [@CL-CMP-0760]. The third is that the estimated facilities capital cost of money is specifically identified and proposed in cost proposals relating to the contract under which the cost is to be claimed [@CL-CMP-0761]. Actual interest cost in lieu of the calculated imputed cost of money is unallowable [@CL-CMP-0762].

## How it works

Cost of money is an incurred cost for cost-reimbursement purposes under applicable cost-reimbursement contracts and for progress payment purposes under fixed-price contracts [@CL-CMP-0757]. It is allowable, provided it is measured, assigned, and allocated to contracts in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.414 or measured and added to the cost of capital assets under construction in accordance with 48 CFR 9904.417, as applicable [@CL-CMP-9408] [@CL-CMP-0759], and provided the requirements of 31.205-52 are followed [@CL-CMP-0760] and the estimated facilities capital cost of money is specifically identified and proposed in cost proposals relating to the contract under which the cost is to be claimed [@CL-CMP-0761]. Actual interest cost in lieu of the calculated imputed cost of money is unallowable [@CL-CMP-0762].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-10, cost of money is an imputed cost that is not a form of interest on borrowings [@CL-CMP-9385], and it is an incurred cost for cost-reimbursement purposes under applicable cost-reimbursement contracts and for progress payment purposes under fixed-price contracts [@CL-CMP-9386]. It refers to facilities capital cost of money and to cost of money as an element of the cost of capital assets under construction [@CL-CMP-9387]. RFO FAR 31.205-10(b) introduces the listed allowability conditions with "allowable when" where the codified text says "allowable, provided" [@CL-CMP-9388; @CL-CMP-9408]. The conditions are measurement, assignment and allocation under the applicable cost accounting standard [@CL-CMP-9388], observance of the requirements of 31.205-52 [@CL-CMP-9389] and specific identification of the estimated facilities capital cost of money in the cost proposals [@CL-CMP-9390]. Actual interest cost in lieu of the calculated imputed cost of money is unallowable [@CL-CMP-9391].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor measures and allocates 600,000 of facilities capital cost of money to a contract under the facilities capital standard, but the cost proposals for that contract never identify the estimated amount. The condition that the estimate be specifically identified and proposed for the contract is not met, so the allowability test fails for that contract.

## Never confuse

Cost of money is an imputed cost that is not a form of interest on borrowings [@CL-CMP-0756], and actual interest cost in lieu of the calculated imputed cost of money is unallowable [@CL-CMP-0762]. Cost of money refers to facilities capital cost of money (48 CFR 9904.414), which has its own card at [Facilities capital cost of money](cmp-cas-414-facilities-capital-cost-of-money.md) [@CL-CMP-0758].

## Company calibration

Whether the company computes cost of money only under the applicable cost accounting standard and names the estimated facilities capital amount in each proposal [@Q-024].
