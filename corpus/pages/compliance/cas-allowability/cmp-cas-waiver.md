## Purpose

Shows the routes to a waiver of the applicability of the Cost Accounting Standards to a single contract or subcontract, who may decide, and what the decision or request has to say.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-CMP-1758] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.201-5 [@CL-CMP-1758] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-1758] |
| Applies to | A contract or subcontract of an executive agency; the commercial-item route is limited to a value below 100 million dollars [@CL-CMP-1758; @CL-CMP-1760] |

The head of an executive agency may waive the applicability of the Cost Accounting Standards for a contract or subcontract with a value of less than $100 million if the official determines, in writing, certain facts about the business unit that will perform the work [@CL-CMP-1758].

The determination must be that the unit is primarily engaged in the sale of commercial items and would not otherwise be subject to the standards under that chapter [@CL-CMP-1759].

A second route lets the agency head waive the standards for a contract or subcontract under exceptional circumstances when necessary to meet the needs of the agency [@CL-CMP-1760].

## How it works

The commercial-item route requires the head of the executive agency to determine, in writing, that the business unit of the contractor or subcontractor that will perform the work is primarily engaged in the sale of commercial items and would not otherwise be subject to the Cost Accounting Standards under that chapter [@CL-CMP-1758; @CL-CMP-1759].

The exceptional-circumstances waiver likewise has to be set forth in writing, with a statement of the circumstances that justify it [@CL-CMP-1761].

The agency head may not delegate either authority to any official below the senior policymaking level in the agency [@CL-CMP-1762].

Each agency head reports the waivers granted under those two routes to the Cost Accounting Standards Board on an annual basis, not later than 90 days after the close of the Government's fiscal year [@CL-CMP-1763].

Separately, upon request of an agency head or his designee, the Cost Accounting Standards Board may waive all or any part of the standards clause requirements or of the disclosure and consistency requirements, with respect to a contract subject to the Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-1764; @CL-CMP-8120]. Any request for a waiver must describe the proposed contract or subcontract for which the waiver is sought [@CL-CMP-8121] and must contain, among other things, an unequivocal statement that the proposed contractor or subcontractor refuses to accept a contract containing all or a specified part of a CAS clause, with the specific reason for the refusal [@CL-CMP-1901].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit that is primarily a commercial seller seeks relief on a subcontract worth 40,000,000. The agency head signs a written determination that the unit is primarily engaged in the sale of commercial items and would not otherwise be subject to the standards. The commercial-item route is available because the value is under 100,000,000 and both findings are recorded. If the value had been 150,000,000, that route would not be available, and the exceptional-circumstances route, with its written statement of the circumstances, or a Board waiver on an agency request would be the routes to consider. The answer: the same business unit can fit the commercial-item route at 40,000,000 but would have to use a different route at 150,000,000.

## Never confuse

The commercial-item waiver is a waiver that the head of an executive agency may grant for a contract or subcontract with a value of less than $100 million, if that official determines, in writing, that the business unit is primarily engaged in the sale of commercial items and would not otherwise be subject to the Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-1758; @CL-CMP-1759].

The categories of contracts and subcontracts that are exempt from all CAS requirements are listed in the coverage rules [@CL-CMP-8116], described in [CAS applicability](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-applicability.md); a waiver under this section is a decision about a contract or subcontract [@CL-CMP-1758; @CL-CMP-1760].
