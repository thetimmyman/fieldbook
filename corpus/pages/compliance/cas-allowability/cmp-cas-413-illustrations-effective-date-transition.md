## Purpose

Collects the illustrations, the effective date and the transition rule of the pension adjustment and allocation standard, so they can be found without reopening the rule cards.

In one illustration, actuarial gains and losses measured for periods beginning on or after the Applicability Date for the CAS Pension Harmonization Rule shall be amortized over a ten-year period [@CL-CMP-3411]. In the same illustration, if the actuarial gain or loss is measured for a period beginning before that Applicability Date, the gain or loss shall be amortized over a fifteen-year period [@CL-CMP-3440]. In another illustration, the actuarial value of the assets of the pension plan must fall within a corridor from 80 to 120 percent of market [@CL-CMP-3412]. In that illustration the asset value reached by the contractor, $7,650,000, falls outside the corridor, so the value reached must be adjusted to equal the nearest boundary of the corridor, $8 million [@CL-CMP-3441].

CAS 413 is effective as of February 27, 2012, and is applicable for cost accounting periods after June 30, 2012 [@CL-CMP-3413]. Under the transition method section, all adjustments shall be prospective only, although costs and prices of prior and existing contracts not subject to price adjustment may be considered in determining the appropriate transition method or adjustment amount for the computation of costs and prices of contracts subject to the standard [@CL-CMP-3414].

The exemption section of the pension adjustment and allocation standard says there is no exemption for it [@CL-CMP-8659].

## How it works

The illustrations show the amortization periods for gains and losses, which depend on whether the period begins before or on or after the Applicability Date [@CL-CMP-3411; @CL-CMP-3440], and the corridor test on the actuarial value of the assets of the pension plan [@CL-CMP-3412; @CL-CMP-3441]. The effective date sets the cost accounting periods in which the standard applies [@CL-CMP-3413]. Under the transition rule all adjustments shall be prospective only, but costs and prices of prior and existing contracts not subject to price adjustment may be considered in determining the appropriate transition method or adjustment amount [@CL-CMP-3414]. The [adjustment and allocation card](cmp-cas-413-pension-adjustment-allocation.md) and the [segment closing card](../../mna/diligence/mna-segment-closing-pension-adjustment.md) hold the rules themselves. The [pension harmonization transition card](cmp-cas-412-effective-date-transition.md) covers the phase-in of the harmonization rule under the pension cost standard, and the [pension cost illustrations card](cmp-cas-412-illustrations.md) shows worked outcomes under it.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A plan's actuarial value of assets, by its valuation method, is 150 while market value is 200. The corridor runs from 160 to 240. Because 150 is outside the corridor, the value is adjusted to the nearest boundary, 160.

A gain of 30 is measured for a period beginning on or after the Applicability Date. It is amortized over ten years, which is 3 a year before the interest equivalent.

## Never confuse

The corridor requires the actuarial value of the assets of the pension plan to fall within 80 to 120 percent of market [@CL-CMP-3412]; in the illustration, a value that falls outside the corridor must be adjusted to the nearest boundary of the corridor [@CL-CMP-3441]. All adjustments under the transition rule shall be prospective only, but costs and prices of prior and existing contracts not subject to price adjustment may be considered in determining the appropriate transition method or adjustment amount [@CL-CMP-3414].
