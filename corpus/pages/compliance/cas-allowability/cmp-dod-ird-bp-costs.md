## Purpose

Adds the DoD supplement details that the general independent research and development and bid and proposal page leaves out: what a covered contract is, which divisions fall outside the limitations, and the reporting rules.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-0875] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 231.205-18 [@CL-CMP-0875] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0875] |
| Applies to | Covered contracts: DoD prime contracts and subcontracts above the simplified acquisition threshold, other than fixed-price contracts or subcontracts without cost incentives [@CL-CMP-0875; @CL-CMP-0876] |

A covered contract is a DoD prime contract for an amount exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, except for a fixed-price contract without cost incentives [@CL-CMP-0875]. A subcontract for an amount exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold is also a covered contract, except for a fixed-price subcontract without cost incentives under such a prime contract [@CL-CMP-0876].

The definitions of covered segment and major contractor, the project reporting duty and the cap on allowable independent research and development cost are set out on [IR&D and B&P costs and major contractor thresholds](cmp-ird-and-bp-costs.md). A covered segment is a product division of the contractor that allocated more than $1,100,000 of independent research and development cost and bid and proposal cost to covered contracts during the preceding fiscal year [@CL-CMP-0877], and for a contractor with no product divisions the term means the contractor as a whole [@CL-CMP-1402]. A major contractor is any contractor whose covered segments allocated a total of more than $11 million of those costs to covered contracts during the preceding fiscal year [@CL-CMP-0879]. A product division that allocated less than $1,100,000 of independent research and development cost and bid and proposal cost to covered contracts during the preceding fiscal year is not subject to the limitations in paragraph (c) of the section [@CL-CMP-0878]. Segments allocating less than $1,100,000 are also left out when the dollar threshold amounts for the major contractor definition are calculated [@CL-CMP-0880].

## How it works

Departments and agencies shall not supplement the section in any way that limits independent research and development cost allowability and bid and proposal cost allowability [@CL-CMP-0881]. See DFARS 225.7303-2(c) for allowability provisions affecting foreign military sale contracts [@CL-CMP-0882]. For independent research and development costs that major contractors incurred on covered contracts to be allowable, the contractor is required to report the projects generating those costs to the Defense Technical Information Center (DTIC) [@CL-CMP-1403] using the DTIC's online input form and instructions [@CL-CMP-8328], and the contractor is required to update its DTIC inputs at least annually, no later than 3 months after the end of the contractor's fiscal year, and when the project is completed [@CL-CMP-0883]. Contractors that are not major contractors are encouraged to use the same online input form to report their projects [@CL-CMP-0886]. Incurred independent research and development cost must be reported separately from indirect costs, and incurred bid and proposal cost separately from other indirect costs [@CL-CMP-0887].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has three product divisions that put independent research and development cost and bid and proposal cost onto covered contracts last year: Alpha 8,000,000, Beta 4,000,000 and Gamma 900,000. Gamma is below the 1,100,000 floor, so it is left out of the major contractor calculation. Alpha and Beta total 12,000,000, which is above the 11,000,000 major contractor threshold, so the contractor is a major contractor. Its fiscal year ends on 31 December, so its annual update of project inputs is due by 31 March, and also when a project is completed.

## Never confuse

The DoD supplement is the stricter layer: it defines a covered contract [@CL-CMP-0875; @CL-CMP-0876] and provides that the amount of independent research and development costs allowable under DoD contracts shall not exceed the lesser of [@CL-CMP-0884] such contracts' allocable share of total incurred independent research and development costs [@CL-CMP-8329] or the total amount of incurred independent research and development costs that the chief executive officer of the contractor has determined will advance the needs of DoD for future technology and advanced capability as DoD describes such needs in communications referenced at 242.771-3(c)(1)(i) [@CL-CMP-0885]. The general principle is in [IR&D and B&P costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-ird-and-bp-costs.md) and the allocation question is in [the allocation standard for these costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-420-ird-bp-allocation.md).
