## Purpose

Shows which contracts and modifications fall in the commercial-organization category and how the cost principles apply to them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2708] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.103 [@CL-CMP-2706] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.103 [@CL-CMP-9562; @CL-CMP-9564; @CL-CMP-9565] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2706] |
| Applies to | Contracts and contract modifications with commercial organizations, with the listed exclusions [@CL-CMP-2706; @CL-CMP-2707] |

The category includes all contracts and contract modifications for supplies, services, or experimental, developmental, or research work negotiated with organizations other than educational institutions [@CL-CMP-2706], construction and architect-engineer contracts, State and local governments and nonprofit organizations on the basis of cost [@CL-CMP-2707].

The cost principles and procedures in subpart 31.2 and agency supplements shall be used in pricing negotiated supply, service, experimental, developmental, and research contracts and contract modifications with commercial organizations whenever cost analysis is performed as required by FAR 15.404-1(c) [@CL-CMP-2708]. In addition, the contracting officer shall incorporate those principles and the agency supplements by reference in contracts with commercial organizations as the basis for the purposes the section lists [@CL-CMP-2709]. One listed purpose is determining reimbursable costs under cost-reimbursement contracts and cost-reimbursement subcontracts performed by commercial organizations, and under the cost-reimbursement portion of time-and-materials contracts except when material is priced on a basis other than at cost [@CL-CMP-2710]. Three further listed purposes are negotiating indirect cost rates, proposing, negotiating, or determining costs under terminated contracts, and price revision of fixed-price incentive contracts [@CL-CMP-2769]. The last two listed purposes are price redetermination of price redetermination contracts and pricing changes and other contract modifications [@CL-CMP-2770].

## How it works

The principles reach a commercial-organization contract in two ways: for pricing, when cost analysis is performed [@CL-CMP-2708], and by incorporation by reference as the basis for the listed purposes [@CL-CMP-2709]. For the reimbursable-cost purpose, a time-and-materials contract is reached through its cost-reimbursement portion, except when material is priced on a basis other than at cost [@CL-CMP-2710]. The section lists further purposes [@CL-CMP-2769; @CL-CMP-2770]. The pricing use applies whenever cost analysis is performed as required by FAR 15.404-1(c) [@CL-CMP-2708].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.103, the principles must be used in pricing negotiated contracts and contract modifications with commercial organizations whenever cost analysis is performed as required by FAR 15.404-1(c) [@CL-CMP-9564], and the contracting officer must incorporate them by reference as the basis for the items that follow [@CL-CMP-9565]. Those items include the reimbursable-cost purpose with its time-and-materials exception [@CL-CMP-9566] and the further purposes [@CL-CMP-9567; @CL-CMP-9568], and the category includes contracts, subcontracts, and modifications to contracts and subcontracts negotiated with organizations other than educational institutions [@CL-CMP-9562], construction and architect-engineer contracts, State and local governments and nonprofit organizations on the basis of cost [@CL-CMP-9563]. The RFO and codified texts differ in four places. The RFO category sentence names contracts, subcontracts, and modifications to contracts and subcontracts, where the codified sentence names all contracts and contract modifications for supplies, services, or experimental, developmental, or research work [@CL-CMP-9562; @CL-CMP-2706]. RFO FAR 31.103(a) refers to negotiated contracts and contract modifications with commercial organizations, where codified FAR 31.103(a) refers to negotiated supply, service, experimental, developmental, and research contracts and contract modifications and to subpart 31.2 and agency supplements [@CL-CMP-9564; @CL-CMP-2708]. RFO FAR 31.103(b) opens with the contracting officer incorporating the cost principles and procedures in subpart 31.2 by reference, where codified FAR 31.103(b) opens with the words in addition and refers to subpart 31.2 and agency supplements [@CL-CMP-9565; @CL-CMP-2709]. The RFO reimbursable-cost item reads cost-reimbursement subcontracts under contracts performed by commercial organizations, where the codified item reads cost-reimbursement subcontracts under these contracts performed by commercial organizations [@CL-CMP-9566; @CL-CMP-2710].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial supplier holds a time-and-materials contract. Labor is billed at fixed hourly rates, and 5,000 of material is billed at cost. For the reimbursable-cost purpose, the exception applies when material is priced on a basis other than at cost, and this material is priced at cost. The answer: the exception does not apply to the 5,000 of material, so the cost principles can be used to determine how much of it is reimbursable.

## Never confuse

The commercial-organization category covers contracts negotiated with organizations other than educational institutions [@CL-CMP-2706], construction and architect-engineer contracts, State and local governments and nonprofit organizations on the basis of cost [@CL-CMP-2707]. For the construction category, see [Cost principles for construction and architect-engineer contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-construction-architect-engineer.md), and for State, local and tribal governments, see [Cost principles for State, local and tribal government contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-state-local-tribal-governments.md). For payments to subcontractors, see [Application of cost principles to subcontracts and uncovered costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-application-of-cost-principles-subcontracts-selected-costs.md). The cost principles and procedures shall be used in pricing whenever cost analysis is performed as required by FAR 15.404-1(c) [@CL-CMP-2708]; in addition, the contracting officer shall incorporate them by reference as the basis for the listed purposes [@CL-CMP-2709]. For the contract types themselves, see [Cost-reimbursement contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-reimbursement-contracts.md) and [Time-and-materials contracts](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md). For the allowability tests, see [Factors determining cost allowability](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md).
