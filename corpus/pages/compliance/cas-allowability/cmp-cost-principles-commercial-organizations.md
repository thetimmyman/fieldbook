## Purpose

Shows which contracts and modifications fall in the commercial-organization category and how the cost principles apply to them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2708] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.103 [@CL-CMP-2706] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2706] |
| Applies to | Contracts and contract modifications with commercial organizations, with the listed exclusions [@CL-CMP-2706; @CL-CMP-2707] |

The category includes all contracts and contract modifications for supplies, services, or experimental, developmental, or research work negotiated with organizations other than educational institutions [@CL-CMP-2706]. The same sentence goes on to exclude construction and architect-engineer contracts, State and local governments and nonprofit organizations, and it ends with the words on the basis of cost [@CL-CMP-2707]. The exclusion names construction and architect-engineer contracts, not contractors [@CL-CMP-2707].

The cost principles and procedures in subpart 31.2 and agency supplements shall be used in pricing negotiated supply, service, experimental, developmental, and research contracts and contract modifications with commercial organizations whenever cost analysis is performed as required by FAR 15.404-1(c) [@CL-CMP-2708]. In addition, the contracting officer shall incorporate those principles and the agency supplements by reference in contracts with commercial organizations as the basis for the purposes the section lists [@CL-CMP-2709]. One listed purpose is determining reimbursable costs under cost-reimbursement contracts and cost-reimbursement subcontracts performed by commercial organizations, and under the cost-reimbursement portion of time-and-materials contracts, except when material is priced on a basis other than at cost [@CL-CMP-2710]. Three further listed purposes are negotiating indirect cost rates, proposing, negotiating, or determining costs under terminated contracts, and price revision of fixed-price incentive contracts [@CL-CMP-2769]. The last two listed purposes are price redetermination of price redetermination contracts and pricing changes and other contract modifications [@CL-CMP-2770].

## How it works

The principles reach a commercial-organization contract in two ways: for pricing, when cost analysis is performed [@CL-CMP-2708], and by incorporation by reference as the basis for the listed purposes [@CL-CMP-2709]. For the reimbursable-cost purpose, a time-and-materials contract is reached through its cost-reimbursement portion, and material priced on a basis other than at cost is outside that purpose [@CL-CMP-2710]. The section lists further purposes beyond that one, which are printed in the rule above [@CL-CMP-2769; @CL-CMP-2770]. The section does not say when cost analysis is required; it refers to FAR 15.404-1(c) [@CL-CMP-2708].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial supplier holds a time-and-materials contract. Labor is billed at fixed hourly rates, and 5,000 of material is billed at cost. For the reimbursable-cost purpose, the exception removes only material priced on a basis other than at cost, and this material is priced at cost. The answer: the exception does not remove the 5,000 of material, so the cost principles can be used to determine how much of it is reimbursable.

## Never confuse

The commercial-organization category is not the category for educational institutions, State and local governments or nonprofit organizations, and it does not cover construction and architect-engineer contracts [@CL-CMP-2706; @CL-CMP-2707]. For the construction category, see [Cost principles for construction and architect-engineer contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-construction-architect-engineer.md), and for State, local and tribal governments, see [Cost principles for State, local and tribal government contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-state-local-tribal-governments.md). For payments to subcontractors, see [Application of cost principles to subcontracts and uncovered costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-application-of-cost-principles-subcontracts-selected-costs.md). Use for pricing depends on cost analysis being performed, while incorporation by reference is the contract basis for the listed purposes [@CL-CMP-2708; @CL-CMP-2709]. For the contract types themselves, see [Cost-reimbursement contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-reimbursement-contracts.md) and [Time-and-materials contracts](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md). For the allowability tests, see [Factors determining cost allowability](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md).
