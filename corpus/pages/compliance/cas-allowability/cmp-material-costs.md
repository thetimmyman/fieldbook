## Purpose

Governs the composition, credit adjustments, and pricing of material items and interorganizational transfers charged to government contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0513] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-26 [@CL-CMP-0513] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-26 [@CL-CMP-9458; @CL-CMP-9459; @CL-CMP-9463] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0513] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to the FAR cost principles [@CL-CMP-0513] |

Material costs include the costs of items such as raw materials, parts, subassemblies, components, and manufacturing supplies, whether purchased or manufactured by the contractor, and may include collateral items such as inbound transportation and in-transit insurance [@CL-CMP-0513].

The contractor shall adjust the costs of material for income and other credits, including available trade discounts, refunds, rebates, allowances, and cash discounts, and credits for scrap, salvage, and material returned to vendors [@CL-CMP-0514].

Allowance for all materials, supplies and services that are sold or transferred between any divisions, subdivisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of the contractor under a common control shall be on the basis of cost incurred in accordance with this subpart [@CL-CMP-0515]. However, allowance may be at price when both of two conditions are met [@CL-CMP-0830; @CL-CMP-0831].

## How it works

Under codified FAR 31.205-26(e), allowance for all materials, supplies and services that are sold or transferred between any divisions, subdivisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of the contractor under a common control shall be on the basis of cost incurred in accordance with this subpart [@CL-CMP-0515]. However, allowance may be at price when (1) it is the established practice of the transferring organization to price interorganizational transfers at other than cost for commercial work of the contractor or any division, subsidiary or affiliate of the contractor under a common control [@CL-CMP-0830]; and (2) the item being transferred qualifies for an exception under 15.403-1(b) and the contracting officer has not determined the price to be unreasonable [@CL-CMP-0831].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-26(a)(1), material costs include the costs of items, such as raw materials, parts, subassemblies, components, and manufacturing supplies, whether purchased or manufactured by the contractor, and may include collateral items such as inbound transportation and in-transit insurance [@CL-CMP-9458]. Under paragraph (b), the contractor must (1) adjust the costs of material for income and other credits, including available trade discounts, refunds, rebates, allowances, and cash discounts, and credits for scrap, salvage, and material returned to vendors [@CL-CMP-9459]; and (2) credit such income and other credits either directly to the cost of the material or allocate such income and other credits as a credit to indirect costs, and when the contractor can demonstrate that failure to take cash discounts was reasonable, the contractor does not need to credit lost discounts [@CL-CMP-9460]. The actual purchase cost of materials purchased specifically for and identifiable solely with performance under a contract should be charged to the contract [@CL-CMP-9461], and if material is issued from stores, any generally recognized method of pricing such material is acceptable if that method is consistently applied and the results are equitable [@CL-CMP-9462]. Under paragraph (e), allowance for materials, supplies and services that are sold or transferred between any divisions, subdivisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of the contractor under a common control (1) must be on the basis of cost incurred in accordance with this subpart [@CL-CMP-9463]; or (2) may be at price when (i) it is the established practice of the transferring organization to price interorganizational transfers at other than cost for commercial work of the contractor or any division, subsidiary or affiliate of the contractor under a common control [@CL-CMP-9464]; and (ii) the item being transferred qualifies for an exception under 15.403-1(b) and the contracting officer has not determined the price to be unreasonable [@CL-CMP-9465].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor purchases components for 200,000 for a defense contract and receives a prompt payment cash discount of 4,000 and a supplier volume rebate of 6,000. The contractor also recovers 2,000 from selling scrap metal generated during fabrication. The contractor credits these sums against material costs, reducing the net allowable direct material expense to 188,000.

## Never confuse

The codified text numbers the two conditions for allowance at price (e)(1) and (e)(2) [@CL-CMP-0830; @CL-CMP-0831]. The RFO text numbers them (e)(2)(i) and (e)(2)(ii) [@CL-CMP-9464; @CL-CMP-9465].
