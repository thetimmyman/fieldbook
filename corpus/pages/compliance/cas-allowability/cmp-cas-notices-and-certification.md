## Purpose

Shows what an offeror states and certifies about the Cost Accounting Standards and its Disclosure Statement when it answers a solicitation that carries this provision, and what the Alternate I certificate adds for an educational institution.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5916] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-1 [@CL-CMP-5917] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-1 [@CL-CMP-0260] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5916] |
| Applies to | Offerors other than small businesses and foreign governments [@CL-CMP-5916] |

The notice is in three parts, and the offeror must examine each part and provide the requested information to determine the CAS requirements for any resulting contract [@CL-CMP-5917]. For an educational institution, Part II does not apply unless the contemplated contract will be subject to full or modified CAS coverage under the cited Cost Accounting Standards Board rules [@CL-CMP-5918].

In Part I, a contract resulting from the solicitation that is in excess of the lower CAS threshold in the FAR will be subject to the Cost Accounting Standards Board requirements [@CL-CMP-6486], except for contracts exempt under the cited exemption rule [@CL-CMP-6487]. The provision names the threshold by reference and does not print the amount [@CL-CMP-6486]. An offeror whose proposal, if accepted, would result in a contract subject to those requirements must, as a condition of contracting, submit a Disclosure Statement as required by 48 CFR 9903.202, so an offeror exempt under that rule does not submit one [@CL-CMP-5919].

## How it works

### Part I: when the Disclosure Statement is submitted

When it is required, the Disclosure Statement must be submitted as a part of the offeror's proposal, unless the offeror has already submitted a Disclosure Statement disclosing the practices used in connection with the pricing of this proposal [@CL-CMP-5920]. If an applicable Disclosure Statement has already been submitted, the offeror may satisfy the requirement by providing the information requested in the certificate paragraph of Part I [@CL-CMP-6488].

### Part I: the certificates

The offeror checks the appropriate box [@CL-CMP-6489]. The certificates leave blanks for the date of the Disclosure Statement, the name and address of the cognizant officer or Federal official and, under Alternate I, the Filing Due Date and the number of months [@CL-CMP-6491; @CL-CMP-6492; @CL-CMP-6506; @CL-CMP-6507; @CL-CMP-6508].

Under the certificate of concurrent submission, the offeror certifies that copies of the Disclosure Statement have been submitted as a part of the offer [@CL-CMP-6489]. The original and one copy go to the cognizant administrative contracting officer or the Federal official authorized to act in that capacity, and one copy goes to the cognizant Federal auditor [@CL-CMP-5922]. The Disclosure Statement must be on the applicable Cost Accounting Standards Board form, and the forms may be obtained from the cognizant officer or Federal official [@CL-CMP-6490]. The certificate has fields for the date of the Disclosure Statement and where it was filed, and the offeror further certifies that the practices used in estimating costs in pricing the proposal are consistent with the practices disclosed in the Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-6491].

Under the certificate of previously submitted Disclosure Statement, the offeror certifies that the required Disclosure Statement was filed, and the certificate has fields for the date and where it was filed [@CL-CMP-6492]. The offeror further certifies that the practices used in estimating costs in pricing the proposal are consistent with the practices disclosed in the applicable Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-6493].

Under the certificate of monetary exemption, the offeror certifies that, together with all divisions, subsidiaries and affiliates under common control, it did not receive net awards of negotiated prime contracts and subcontracts subject to CAS totaling $50 million or more [@CL-CMP-5923]. The certificate covers the cost accounting period immediately preceding the period in which the proposal was submitted, and the offeror further certifies that it will advise the contracting officer immediately if such status changes before an award resulting from the proposal [@CL-CMP-6494].

Under the certificate of interim exemption, the offeror certifies that it first exceeded the monetary exemption for disclosure in the cost accounting period immediately preceding the period in which the offer was submitted [@CL-CMP-6495], and that, in accordance with the cited Cost Accounting Standards Board rule, it is not yet required to submit a Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-6496]. It further certifies that if an award resulting from the proposal has not been made within 90 days after the end of that period, it will immediately submit a revised certificate to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-5924]. The revised certificate is in the form of the concurrent submission certificate or the previously submitted certificate, as appropriate, to verify submission of a completed Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-6497; @CL-CMP-6489; @CL-CMP-6492].

Two limits apply to the interim exemption. An offeror currently required to disclose because it was awarded a CAS-covered prime contract or subcontract of $50 million or more in the current cost accounting period may not claim it [@CL-CMP-6498]. The exemption applies only to proposals submitted before expiration of the 90-day period following the cost accounting period in which the monetary exemption was exceeded [@CL-CMP-6499].

### Part II: modified coverage

An offeror that is eligible to use the modified provisions of the cited Cost Accounting Standards Board rule and elects to do so checks the Part II box [@CL-CMP-6500]. Checking the box means the resulting contract is subject to the Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause instead of the Cost Accounting Standards clause [@CL-CMP-5925]. The box states that the offeror claims an exemption from the Cost Accounting Standards clause and certifies that it is eligible for the other clause [@CL-CMP-6501]. The stated basis is that, during the cost accounting period immediately preceding the period in which the proposal was submitted, the offeror received less than $50 million in awards of CAS-covered prime contracts and subcontracts [@CL-CMP-5926]. The offeror further certifies that it will advise the contracting officer immediately if that status changes before an award resulting from the proposal [@CL-CMP-6502].

An offeror may not claim this eligibility if the proposal is expected to result in the award of a CAS-covered contract of $50 million or more [@CL-CMP-6503], or if, during its current cost accounting period, it has been awarded a single CAS-covered prime contract or subcontract of $50 million or more [@CL-CMP-6504].

### Part III: changes affecting existing contracts

The offeror must indicate whether award of the contemplated contract would, under the Cost Accounting Standards clause, require a change in established cost accounting practices affecting existing contracts and subcontracts [@CL-CMP-5927].

### Alternate I

Alternate I adds a further certificate to Part I [@CL-CMP-6505]. It is for an offeror that is an educational institution that, under the transition provisions of the Disclosure Statement rules, is or will be required to submit a Disclosure Statement after receipt of the award, and the offeror checks one option and completes it [@CL-CMP-5928]. The first option certifies that a Disclosure Statement Filing Due Date, shown in a blank, has been established with the cognizant Federal agency [@CL-CMP-6506]. The second option certifies that the Disclosure Statement will be submitted within the 6-month period ending a stated number of months after receipt of the award [@CL-CMP-6507]. The certificate also has a field for the name and address of the cognizant officer or Federal official where the Disclosure Statement is to be filed [@CL-CMP-6508].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO provision keeps the three-part notice, in which the offeror must examine each part and provide the requested information to determine the CAS requirements applicable to any resultant contract [@CL-CMP-0261; @CL-CMP-5917]. It keeps the rule that, for an educational institution, Part II does not apply unless the contemplated contract will be subject to full or modified CAS coverage under 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(5) or 9903.201-2(c)(6), respectively [@CL-CMP-0262; @CL-CMP-5918]. It keeps the exception for contracts that are exempt as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201-1 from the Part I(a) coverage [@CL-CMP-0264; @CL-CMP-6487], and the condition of contracting that an offeror whose proposal, if accepted, would result in a contract subject to 48 CFR chapter 99 submits a Disclosure Statement as required by 48 CFR 9903.202 [@CL-CMP-0265; @CL-CMP-5919]. It keeps the monetary exemption certificate, in which the offeror certifies that, together with all divisions, subsidiaries and affiliates under common control, it did not receive net awards of negotiated prime contracts and subcontracts subject to CAS totaling $50 million or more in the cost accounting period immediately preceding the period in which the proposal was submitted [@CL-CMP-0266; @CL-CMP-5923]. It keeps the interim exemption certificate's promise to submit a revised certificate immediately if an award resulting from the proposal has not been made within 90 days after the end of that period [@CL-CMP-0267; @CL-CMP-5924], in the form specified under paragraph (c)(1) or (c)(2) of Part I, as appropriate, to verify submission of a completed Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-0268; @CL-CMP-6497]. It keeps the Part II rule that checking the box means the resulting contract is subject to the Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause instead of the Cost Accounting Standards clause [@CL-CMP-0269; @CL-CMP-5925], and the Part III question on a change in established cost accounting practices affecting existing contracts and subcontracts [@CL-CMP-0270; @CL-CMP-5927]. In Part I(a) the RFO text says FAR 30.205(b) for the lower CAS threshold, where the codified text says FAR 30.201-4(b) [@CL-CMP-0263; @CL-CMP-0264; @CL-CMP-6486]. The RFO text of the provision ends with the words (End of provision) [@CL-CMP-0271].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror's most recent cost accounting period ended 30 days ago. In that period the offeror, with its affiliates under common control, received net CAS-covered awards of 60 million, the first time it passed the 50 million monetary exemption for disclosure. It has received no single award of 50 million or more in the current period. It submits a proposal now, 30 days into the 90-day window. It checks the interim exemption certificate, not the monetary exemption certificate, because the monetary exemption would be untrue at 60 million, and it sends no Disclosure Statement with the proposal. No award has been made when the 90 days after the end of that period run out, so at that point the offeror immediately submits a revised certificate to the contracting officer, in the form of the concurrent submission or previously submitted certificate, to verify that a completed Disclosure Statement has been submitted. It does not wait for the award, which comes 110 days after that period ended. A proposal submitted 100 days after the period ended could not have used the interim exemption.

A second offeror received 20 million in CAS-covered awards in its preceding period and has received no single award of 50 million or more in its current period. Its proposal is expected to result in a 40 million award, which is above the current 35 million exemption from CAS on the applicability card and below the 50 million printed in the provision, so it may check the Part II box, and the resulting contract takes the Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause. If the proposal were expected to result in a 55 million award, it could not check the box.

## Never confuse

Absent specific regulations or agreement, a practice disclosed in a Disclosure Statement is not by that disclosure alone a proper, approved or agreed-to practice [@CL-CMP-5921]. The Part II election concerns which clause the resulting contract carries [@CL-CMP-5925], while the monetary and interim exemption certificates in Part I concern the Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-6495; @CL-CMP-6496].

The dollar amounts here are the amounts printed in this provision. The [CAS applicability card](cmp-cas-applicability.md) covers the coverage and Disclosure Statement thresholds now in force, and the offeror reads both. The consequences of modified coverage are on the [modified coverage card](cmp-disclosure-consistency-modified-cas.md).

The codified provision has an Alternate I that adds a certificate to Part I for an educational institution [@CL-CMP-6505; @CL-CMP-5928], while the RFO text of the provision ends with the words (End of provision) [@CL-CMP-0271].
