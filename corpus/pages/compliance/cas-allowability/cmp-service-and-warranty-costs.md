## Purpose

Explains the allowability of service and warranty costs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0810] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-39 [@CL-CMP-0810] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-39 [@CL-CMP-9532; @CL-CMP-9533; @CL-CMP-9534] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0810] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Service and warranty costs include those arising from fulfillment of any contractual obligation of a contractor to provide services such as installation, training, correcting defects in the products, replacing defective parts, and making refunds in the case of inadequate performance [@CL-CMP-0810]. When not inconsistent with the terms of the contract, service and warranty costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-0811]. However, care should be exercised to avoid duplication of the allowance as an element of both estimated product cost and risk [@CL-CMP-0811].

## How it works

Under FAR 31.205-39, service and warranty costs include those arising from fulfillment of any contractual obligation of a contractor to provide services such as installation, training, correcting defects in the products, replacing defective parts, and making refunds in the case of inadequate performance [@CL-CMP-0810]. When not inconsistent with the terms of the contract, service and warranty costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-0811].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-39(a), service and warranty costs include those arising from the fulfillment of any contractual obligation of a contractor to provide services, such as installation, training, correcting defects in the products, replacing defective parts, and making refunds in the case of inadequate performance [@CL-CMP-9532]. Under paragraph (b), when consistent with the terms of the contract, service and warranty costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-9533]. However, care should be exercised to avoid duplication of the allowance as an element of both estimated product cost and risk [@CL-CMP-9534].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers specialized equipment under a contract that obligates the contractor to correct manufacturing defects and provide on-site technical support during initial deployment. The contractor incurs 45,000 in labor and parts to correct defective wiring discovered after installation. Because the work fulfills a contractual obligation to correct defects in the products and is not inconsistent with the terms of the contract, the 45,000 is allowable, and care is exercised to avoid duplicating the allowance as an element of both estimated product cost and risk.

## Never confuse

Under the codified text, service and warranty costs are allowable when not inconsistent with the terms of the contract [@CL-CMP-0811]. Under the RFO text, they are allowable when consistent with the terms of the contract [@CL-CMP-9533]. See [Allowability factors](cmp-allowability-factors.md) and [Reasonableness](cmp-reasonableness.md).
