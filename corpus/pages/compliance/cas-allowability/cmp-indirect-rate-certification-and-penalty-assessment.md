## Purpose

Shows where the cost principles send a contractor for certification of indirect rates and for penalties on unallowable costs left in a final settlement proposal.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7506] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.110 [@CL-CMP-7506] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7506] |
| Applies to | Contracts that require certification of indirect rates proposed for final payment, and final settlement proposals that include unallowable costs [@CL-CMP-7506; @CL-CMP-7507] |

FAR 31.110 has two paragraphs and each points elsewhere for the procedure [@CL-CMP-7506; @CL-CMP-7507]. Certain contracts, not all, require certification of the indirect cost rates proposed for final payment purposes, and the administrative procedures for the certification provisions and the related contract clause prescription are in FAR 42.703-2 [@CL-CMP-7506]. If unallowable costs are included in final indirect cost settlement proposals, penalties may be assessed, and the administrative procedures for the penalty assessment provisions and the related contract clause prescription are in FAR 42.709 [@CL-CMP-7507].

## How it works

Certification attaches to the indirect cost rates proposed for final payment purposes, but only under contracts that require it [@CL-CMP-7506]. The section does not say which contracts require it, who signs, who may waive it, or when; the reader goes to the administrative procedures [@CL-CMP-7506].

A penalty is possible when unallowable costs are included in a final indirect cost settlement proposal. The word is "may": the section makes the assessment discretionary on its face and does not say who assesses it, in what amount, on which contracts, or with what exceptions; the reader goes to the administrative procedures at FAR 42.709 [@CL-CMP-7507].

The section sets no amount, threshold or deadline of its own. The exposure to a penalty arises from including unallowable costs in the proposal, which the party submitting the proposal controls [@CL-CMP-7507].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor files a final indirect cost settlement proposal and a 30,000 cost in the overhead pool is unallowable. Including an unallowable cost in the proposal means penalties may be assessed [@CL-CMP-7507]. The section does not compute a penalty: whether one is assessed, its amount and any exception are not stated there, and the reader goes to the administrative procedures it points to [@CL-CMP-7507]. Separately, whether the contract requires certification of the proposed rates is decided by the contract, and the procedures are at the provision it points to [@CL-CMP-7506].

## Never confuse

Certification of the rates proposed for final payment is a different question from a penalty for unallowable costs in the settlement proposal: the first concerns the rates and their certification, the second concerns costs left in the proposal [@CL-CMP-7506; @CL-CMP-7507]. The certification procedure is in [certificate of indirect costs](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-certificate-of-indirect-costs.md), and the penalty side is worked through in [penalties for unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md) and [assessment, interest and waiver](../audit-retention/cmp-expressly-unallowable-penalty-assessment-and-waiver.md).
