## Purpose

Shows where the cost principles send a contractor for certification of indirect rates and for penalties on unallowable costs left in a final settlement proposal.

## How it works

Certain contracts require certification of the indirect cost rates proposed for final payment purposes [@CL-CMP-7506]. FAR 31.110(a) says to see 42.703-2 for administrative procedures regarding the certification provisions and the related contract clause prescription [@CL-CMP-7506].

If unallowable costs are included in final indirect cost settlement proposals, penalties may be assessed [@CL-CMP-7507]. FAR 31.110(b) says to see 42.709 for administrative procedures regarding the penalty assessment provisions and the related contract clause prescription [@CL-CMP-7507].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. In the RFO text, this section is RFO FAR 31.109 [@CL-CMP-9668], and RFO FAR 31.110 is titled Advance agreements [@CL-CMP-9647]. RFO FAR 31.109(a) says certain contracts require certification of the indirect cost rates proposed for final payment purposes and refers to 42.703-2 [@CL-CMP-9668]. RFO FAR 31.109(b) says that if unallowable costs are included in final indirect cost settlement proposals, penalties may be assessed, and refers to both 42.703-2 and 42.709 [@CL-CMP-9669].

## Never confuse

Certification of the indirect cost rates proposed for final payment purposes is addressed in FAR 31.110(a) [@CL-CMP-7506], and penalties for unallowable costs included in final indirect cost settlement proposals in FAR 31.110(b) [@CL-CMP-7507]. The certification procedure is in [certificate of indirect costs](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-certificate-of-indirect-costs.md), and the penalty side is worked through in [penalties for unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md) and [assessment, interest and waiver](../audit-retention/cmp-expressly-unallowable-penalty-assessment-and-waiver.md). Codified FAR 31.110(b) says to see 42.709 for administrative procedures regarding the penalty assessment provisions and the related contract clause prescription [@CL-CMP-7507], and RFO FAR 31.109(b) says to see 42.703-2 and 42.709 [@CL-CMP-9669]. Codified FAR 31.110 is the certification section [@CL-CMP-7506]; in the RFO text the certification section is 31.109 [@CL-CMP-9668] and 31.110 is titled Advance agreements [@CL-CMP-9647].
