## Purpose

Rental costs under operating leases are allowable when the rates are reasonable at the time of the lease decision, with sale and leaseback and common-control charges capped to prevent recovering more than ownership or duplicated costs.

## The rule

|---|---|

| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-36 [@CL-CMP-0461] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-36 [@CL-CMP-9510] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0461] |
| Applies to | Costs of renting or leasing real or personal property acquired under operating leases [@CL-CMP-0462] |

The subsection applies to the cost of renting or leasing real or personal property acquired under operating leases [@CL-CMP-0462]. Rental costs under operating leases are allowable to the extent the rates are reasonable at the time of the lease decision [@CL-CMP-0461; @CL-CMP-0827]. Rental costs under a sale and leaseback arrangement are allowable only up to the amount the contractor would be allowed if it retained title [@CL-CMP-0463].

## How it works

Reasonableness is judged at the time of the lease decision, weighing comparable property, market conditions, the type and condition of the property, alternatives, and other agreement provisions. A sale and leaseback is capped at the amount the contractor would have been allowed had it kept title. Charges in the nature of rent between divisions, subsidiaries, or organizations under common control are allowable to the extent they do not exceed normal costs of ownership and do not duplicate another allowed cost; personal property leased from a common-control affiliate that has an established practice of leasing the same or similar property to unaffiliated lessees is allowed under the general reasonableness test for operating-lease rentals.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.205-36 keeps the test that the rates for rental costs under operating leases are reasonable at the time of the lease decision, after consideration of the same factors [@CL-CMP-9510; @CL-CMP-9511; @CL-CMP-0827], but allows those costs "when the rates are reasonable" where the codified paragraph says "to the extent that" [@CL-CMP-9510; @CL-CMP-0461]. It keeps the sale and leaseback limit [@CL-CMP-9512] and the rule that charges in the nature of rent between organizations under common control are allowable to the extent that they do not exceed the normal costs of ownership, which exclude interest and other unallowable costs pursuant to part 31 [@CL-CMP-9510; @CL-CMP-9513; @CL-CMP-8222], with the proviso, in the RFO wording, that "no part of such costs must duplicate any other allowed cost" [@CL-CMP-9514]. Paragraph (a) defines operating leases by FASB ASC 842, Leases [@CL-CMP-9509]. The rule for personal property leased from an affiliate under common control that has an established practice of leasing the same or similar property to unaffiliated lessees is a separate paragraph (b)(4) [@CL-CMP-9515].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor leases office equipment under an operating lease at a monthly rate that matches what it would pay for comparable equipment in its area. The rate was reasonable at the time the lease was signed, so the rental cost is allowable.

## Never confuse

An operating lease, which this subsection covers [@CL-CMP-0462], is not a capital lease. Rental costs are judged for reasonableness at the time of the lease decision, and a sale and leaseback is capped at the retained-title amount.
