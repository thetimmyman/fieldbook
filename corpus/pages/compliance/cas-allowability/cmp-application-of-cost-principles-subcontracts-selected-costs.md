## Purpose

Shows how the cost principles apply to selected items of cost, to payments under subcontracts, and to costs that no selected item covers.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2733] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.204 [@CL-CMP-2733] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2733] |
| Applies to | Costs under the FAR cost principles, including subcontract payments of the listed types [@CL-CMP-2733; @CL-CMP-2734] |

Costs are allowable to the extent they are reasonable, allocable, and determined to be allowable under FAR 31.201, 31.202, 31.203 and 31.205, and these criteria apply to all of the selected items that follow even if particular guidance is provided for certain items for emphasis or clarity [@CL-CMP-2733]. Section 31.205 does not cover every element of cost, failure to include an item does not imply it is either allowable or unallowable, and allowability is determined from the principles and standards of the subpart and the treatment of similar or related selected items [@CL-CMP-2738].

For the listed subcontract types, costs incurred as reimbursements or payments to a subcontractor are allowable to the extent the reimbursements or payments are for costs incurred by the subcontractor that are consistent with this part [@CL-CMP-2734]. The listed types are cost-reimbursement, fixed-price incentive, and price redeterminable subcontracts [@CL-CMP-2735], and the requirement applies to any tier above the first firm-fixed-price subcontract or fixed-price subcontract with economic price adjustment provisions [@CL-CMP-2736]. Costs incurred as payments under firm-fixed-price subcontracts or fixed-price subcontracts with economic price adjustment provisions, or modifications thereto, for which subcontract cost analysis was performed are allowable if the price was negotiated in accordance with FAR 31.102 [@CL-CMP-2737].

## How it works

The reasonable, allocable and allowable test applies even to items that carry their own guidance [@CL-CMP-2733]. A cost the selected items do not mention is judged by the principles and standards of the subpart and by similar or related selected items, and its absence from the list does not make it allowable or unallowable [@CL-CMP-2738]. When more than one subsection is relevant to a cost, the cost is apportioned among the applicable subsections and each portion is judged under its own subsection [@CL-CMP-2739]. For subcontract payments, the subcontract type decides which path applies [@CL-CMP-2734; @CL-CMP-2737]. When a cost to which more than one subsection of FAR 31.205 is relevant cannot be apportioned, the determination of allowability shall be based on the guidance in the subsection that most specifically deals with, or best captures the essential nature of, the cost at issue [@CL-CMP-2773].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime holds a cost-reimbursement contract and pays a cost-reimbursement subcontractor 200,000. Of that amount, 20,000 is for costs the subcontractor incurred that are inconsistent with the cost principles. The answer: only the part for consistent costs, 180,000, is allowable to the prime.

## Never confuse

Payments under the listed subcontract types are tested against the subcontractor's costs, while payments under firm-fixed-price types depend on cost analysis and a price negotiated in accordance with FAR 31.102 [@CL-CMP-2734; @CL-CMP-2737]. Silence in the selected items is not permission, because a missing item is neither allowable nor unallowable by its absence [@CL-CMP-2738]. For the reasonableness and allocability tests, see [Reasonableness of contract costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-reasonableness.md) and [Allocability of costs to government contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-allocability.md), and for the full allowability factors, see [Factors determining cost allowability](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md). For the contract categories whose cost principles reach cost-reimbursement subcontracts [@CL-CMP-2710; @CL-CMP-2772], see [Cost principles for contracts with commercial organizations](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-commercial-organizations.md) and [Cost principles for construction and architect-engineer contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-construction-architect-engineer.md). For excluding unallowable costs from billings, claims and proposals, see [Accounting for unallowable and directly associated costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-accounting-for-unallowable-and-associated-costs.md).
