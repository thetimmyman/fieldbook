## Purpose

Sets out what counts as a cost accounting practice and what counts as a change to one, including the alterations the section excludes.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-CMP-1770] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.302-1 and 9903.302-2 [@CL-CMP-1770; @CL-CMP-1773] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-1770] |
| Applies to | Cost accounting practices, whether or not covered by a Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-1773] |

A cost accounting practice is any disclosed or established accounting method or technique used to allocate cost to cost objectives, to assign cost to cost accounting periods, or to measure cost [@CL-CMP-1770].

Allocation to cost objectives covers both direct and indirect allocation [@CL-CMP-1772].

A change is any alteration in such a practice, whether or not the practice is covered by a Disclosure Statement, apart from the exclusions that follow in the section [@CL-CMP-1773].

## How it works

Setting the amount paid for a unit of goods or services, or changing that amount, is not itself a cost accounting practice [@CL-CMP-1771].

One exclusion is the first adoption of a practice, when a cost is first incurred or a function is created [@CL-CMP-1774].

Another is the partial or total elimination of a cost or of the cost of a function [@CL-CMP-1775].

Revising a practice for a cost that had previously been immaterial is excluded as well [@CL-CMP-1776].

Any other alteration in a cost accounting practice is a change even where no Disclosure Statement covers the practice [@CL-CMP-1773].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor first incurs a cost for a new function and adopts a method for assigning it to periods. Later it stops the function entirely. In a different year it revises the method for a cost that had been immaterial. The answer: none of the three events is a change to a cost accounting practice under this section.

## Never confuse

A change in practice is not a change in the price paid for a unit of goods or services, and the first adoption of a practice is not a change either [@CL-CMP-1771; @CL-CMP-1774].

Eliminating a cost is excluded, while an alteration in how a remaining cost is allocated, assigned or measured is a change unless another exclusion applies [@CL-CMP-1770; @CL-CMP-1773; @CL-CMP-1775]. The disclosure, consistency and practice-change requirements of the standards clause are covered in [Cost accounting standards clause](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-accounting-standards.md), and how materiality is judged in [Materiality](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-materiality.md).

The modified-coverage position is described in [Disclosure and consistency under modified coverage](../cas-allowability/cmp-disclosure-consistency-modified-cas.md).

## Company calibration

Whether each business unit can show which practices are disclosed and how each alteration was screened against the exclusions [@Q-033].
