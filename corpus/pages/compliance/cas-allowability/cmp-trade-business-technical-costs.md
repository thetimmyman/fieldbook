## Purpose

Sets out the allowable trade, business, technical and professional activity costs: memberships, subscriptions and meeting costs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0364] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-43(a) [@CL-CMP-0364] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-43 [@CL-CMP-8928; @CL-CMP-8929] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0364] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Under FAR 31.205-43, the following types of costs are allowable: (a) memberships in trade, business, technical, and professional organizations [@CL-CMP-0364]; (b) subscriptions to trade, business, professional, or other technical periodicals [@CL-CMP-0365]; and (c) when the principal purpose of a meeting, convention, conference, symposium, or seminar is the dissemination of trade, business, technical or professional information or the stimulation of production or improved productivity [@CL-CMP-0366]: (1) costs of organizing, setting up, and sponsoring the meetings, conventions, symposia, etc., including rental of meeting facilities, transportation, subsistence, and incidental costs [@CL-CMP-0367]; (2) costs of attendance by contractor employees, including travel costs (see 31.205-46) [@CL-CMP-0368]; and (3) costs of attendance by individuals who are not employees of the contractor, provided (i) such costs are not also reimbursed to the individual by the employing company or organization [@CL-CMP-8582; @CL-CMP-0369], and (ii) the individual's attendance is essential to achieve the purpose of the conference, meeting, convention, symposium, etc. [@CL-CMP-0370].

## How it works

Under paragraph (c)(3) [@CL-CMP-8582], the costs of attendance by individuals who are not employees of the contractor are allowable provided that (i) such costs are not also reimbursed to the individual by the employing company or organization, and (ii) the individual's attendance is essential to achieve the purpose of the conference, meeting, convention, symposium, etc. [@CL-CMP-8582; @CL-CMP-0369; @CL-CMP-0370].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-43, the following types of costs are allowable: (a) memberships in trade, business, technical, and professional organizations, and (b) subscriptions to trade, business, professional, or other technical periodicals [@CL-CMP-8928]. Under paragraph (c), when the principal purpose of a meeting, convention, conference, symposium, or seminar is the dissemination of trade, business, technical or professional information or the stimulation of production or improved productivity [@CL-CMP-8929], the following are allowable: (1) costs of organizing, setting up, and sponsoring the meetings, conventions, symposia, etc., including rental of meeting facilities, transportation, subsistence, and incidental costs, and (2) costs of attendance by contractor employees, including travel costs (see 31.205-46) [@CL-CMP-8930]; and (3) costs of attendance by individuals who are not employees of the contractor when (i) such costs are not also reimbursed to the individual by the employing company or organization [@CL-CMP-8931], and (ii) the individual's attendance is essential to achieve the purpose of the conference, meeting, convention, symposium, etc. [@CL-CMP-8932].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The company pays 3,000 to organize and sponsor a technical symposium, 1,500 for an engineer to attend it, and 900 for a customer's specialist to attend. All three are allowable if the principal purpose of the symposium is technical information exchange, the specialist is not reimbursed by her own employer, and her attendance is essential to that purpose.

## Never confuse

For the treatment of employee travel costs, see [Travel costs](cmp-travel-costs.md). Where these costs are indirect, see [Final indirect cost rate proposal](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).

The codified item (c)(3) introduces the two conditions for attendance by individuals who are not employees with the word provided [@CL-CMP-8582; @CL-CMP-0370], and the RFO item (c)(3) introduces them with the word when [@CL-CMP-8931; @CL-CMP-8932].
