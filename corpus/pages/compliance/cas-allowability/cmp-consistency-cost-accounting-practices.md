## Purpose

Explains the foreign-concern version of the disclosure and consistency clause, which names fewer cost accounting standards than the clause at the neighbouring page.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-1601] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-4 [@CL-CMP-1601] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-4 [@CL-CMP-0276] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-1601] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the foreign-concerns clause [@CL-CMP-1601] |

The contractor must comply with the standards on consistency in estimating, accumulating, and reporting costs and on consistency in allocating costs incurred for the same purpose [@CL-CMP-0967]. Both apply as in effect on the date of award of the contract [@CL-CMP-0968]. A business unit of a company required to submit a Disclosure Statement must disclose its cost accounting practices in writing, on contracts covered by the cost accounting standards only [@CL-CMP-0969].

## How it works

Beyond the standards it names, the clause is built like the clause described on [the neighbouring page](cmp-disclosure-consistency-modified-cas.md), which has the detail. Practices are followed consistently, and a change is negotiated [@CL-CMP-0970] and applied prospectively [@CL-CMP-0976]. When the parties agree to a change and the contracting officer has made the required finding, the contractor negotiates an equitable adjustment [@CL-CMP-0971]. A failure to comply that raises costs paid by the Government brings an adjustment with interest [@CL-CMP-1605; @CL-CMP-0972], a failure to agree whether the contractor has complied with an applicable CAS rule or regulation, and as to any cost adjustment the Government demands, is a dispute [@CL-CMP-0973], and authorized Government representatives may examine records on compliance [@CL-CMP-0975]. A subcontract awarded to a business unit under other types of coverage takes the applicable clause prescribed in the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CMP-0974].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the two standards, 48 CFR 9904.401 and 48 CFR 9904.402, in effect on the date of award of the contract [@CL-CMP-0277; @CL-CMP-0967; @CL-CMP-0968]. It keeps the consistent following of cost accounting practices, with either the U.S. Government or the contractor able to propose a change whose terms are negotiated with the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-0278; @CL-CMP-0970]. It keeps the prospective application of an agreed change, with the Disclosure Statement amended if it is affected [@CL-CMP-0279; @CL-CMP-0976], and the negotiation of an equitable adjustment when the contracting officer has made the finding required in 48 CFR 9903.201-6(c) that the change is desirable and not detrimental to the interests of the U.S. Government [@CL-CMP-0280; @CL-CMP-0971]. In the absence of the required finding, the RFO clause bars any agreement under the clause that will increase costs paid by the U.S. Government [@CL-CMP-0323]. It keeps the adjustment of the contract price or cost allowance when a failure to comply or to follow a practice increases costs paid by the U.S. Government [@CL-CMP-0281; @CL-CMP-1605], with interest at the annual rate established under section 6621(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, computed for the period from the time the payment by the U.S. Government was made to the time the adjustment is effected [@CL-CMP-0282; @CL-CMP-0324; @CL-CMP-0972]. For a subcontract awarded to a business unit under other types of coverage, the RFO text says the applicable clause is the one prescribed in FAR 30.205, where the codified text says FAR 30.201-4 [@CL-CMP-0283; @CL-CMP-0974].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A foreign-concern contract carries 250,000 of cost in a pool, of which 150,000 was incurred before the allocation practice changed and 100,000 after. The contracting officer makes the required finding and the change is agreed. Only the 100,000 incurred after the change follows the new practice, so the earlier 150,000 stays where it was booked.

## Never confuse

The two consistency clauses look alike but name different standards. The foreign-concern clause names two standards, on estimating, accumulating and reporting costs and on allocating costs for the same purpose [@CL-CMP-0967]. The clause on [the neighbouring page](cmp-disclosure-consistency-modified-cas.md) names four, adding the standards on accounting for unallowable costs and on the cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-0951].

For a subcontract awarded to a business unit under other types of CAS coverage, the codified clause points to FAR 30.201-4 [@CL-CMP-0974] and the RFO clause points to FAR 30.205 [@CL-CMP-0283].
