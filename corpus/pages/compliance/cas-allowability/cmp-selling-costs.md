## Purpose

Shows which selling efforts the cost principle addresses, which of them are allowable, and when a payment to a seller or agent qualifies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0773] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-38 [@CL-CMP-0773] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-38 [@CL-CMP-9517] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0773] |
| Applies to | Selling efforts to market the contractor's products or services [@CL-CMP-0773] |

Under FAR 31.205-38(a), selling is a generic term encompassing all efforts to market the contractor's products or services, some of which are covered specifically in other subsections of 31.205 [@CL-CMP-0773]. The costs of any selling efforts other than those addressed in this cost principle are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0774].

Under paragraph (b)(5), direct selling efforts are those acts or actions to induce particular customers to purchase particular products or services of the contractor, and direct selling is characterized by person-to-person contact [@CL-CMP-0775]; the cost of direct selling efforts is allowable [@CL-CMP-0776]. Under paragraph (b)(4), long-range market planning costs are subject to the allowability provisions of 31.205-12, and other market planning costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-0777]. Under paragraph (b)(3), bid and proposal costs are defined at 31.205-18 and are subject to the allowability provisions of that subsection [@CL-CMP-0779].

Under paragraph (c), notwithstanding any other provision of the subsection, sellers' or agents' compensation, fees, commissions, percentages, retainer or brokerage fees, whether or not contingent upon the award of contracts, are allowable only when paid to bona fide employees or established commercial or selling agencies maintained by the contractor for the purpose of securing business [@CL-CMP-1113; @CL-CMP-0778].

## How it works

The cost of direct selling efforts is allowable [@CL-CMP-0776]. Long-range market planning costs are subject to the allowability provisions of 31.205-12, and other market planning costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-0777]. Bid and proposal costs are defined at 31.205-18 and are subject to the allowability provisions of that subsection [@CL-CMP-0779]. Sellers' or agents' compensation, fees, commissions, percentages, retainer or brokerage fees are allowable only when paid to bona fide employees or established commercial or selling agencies maintained by the contractor for the purpose of securing business [@CL-CMP-1113; @CL-CMP-0778].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-38(a)(1), selling encompasses all efforts to market the contractor's products or services, some of which are addressed in other sections of 31.205 [@CL-CMP-9516]. Under paragraph (a)(2), the costs of any selling efforts other than those addressed in this cost principle are unallowable [@CL-CMP-9517]. Under paragraph (b)(5), direct selling efforts are those acts or actions to induce particular customers to purchase particular products or services of the contractor, and direct selling is characterized by person-to-person contact [@CL-CMP-9520], and the cost of direct selling efforts is allowable [@CL-CMP-9521]. Under paragraph (b)(4), long-range market planning costs are subject to the allowability provisions of 31.205-12, and other market planning costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-9519]. Under paragraph (b)(3), bid and proposal costs are defined at 31.205-18 and are subject to the allowability provisions of that section [@CL-CMP-9518]. Under paragraph (c), notwithstanding any other limitation of this section, seller or agent compensation, fees, commissions, percentages, retainer or brokerage fees, whether or not contingent upon the award of contracts, are allowable only when paid to bona fide employees or established commercial or selling agencies maintained by the contractor for the purpose of securing business [@CL-CMP-9522; @CL-CMP-9523].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company spends 40,000 on a general awareness campaign, 60,000 on direct selling visits to named customers, and 25,000 on a commission to an outside broker who is not an established selling agency. The direct selling cost is allowable; the general awareness spend is not direct selling; the broker commission is unallowable because the broker is neither a bona fide employee nor an established commercial or selling agency maintained by the contractor.

## Never confuse

Direct selling differs from bid and proposal work: bid and proposal cost has its own page at [Bid and proposal costs](cmp-ird-and-bp-costs.md). The rule that the costs of selling efforts other than those addressed in the cost principle are unallowable is in codified paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-0774] and RFO paragraph (a)(2) [@CL-CMP-9517]. Under the codified text, direct selling efforts are those acts or actions to induce particular customers to purchase particular products or services of the contractor [@CL-CMP-0775], and a seller's or agent's commission is allowable only when paid to bona fide employees or established commercial or selling agencies maintained by the contractor for the purpose of securing business [@CL-CMP-0778].

## Company calibration

Whether the company tags each selling cost to a category the principle addresses and pays commissions only to qualifying employees or established agencies [@Q-044].
