## Purpose

Shows which CAS solicitation provisions FAR 30.201-3 directs, who is told to insert each, and when an insertion is excused [@CL-CMP-7029; @CL-CMP-7030; @CL-CMP-7031; @CL-CMP-7032].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7029] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.201-3 [@CL-CMP-7029] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7029] |
| Applies to | Solicitations for proposed contracts subject to CAS as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201 [@CL-CMP-7029; @CL-CMP-7032] |

Paragraph (a): the contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.230-1, Cost Accounting Standards Notices and Certification, in solicitations for proposed contracts subject to CAS as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201 [@CL-CMP-7029].

Paragraph (b): if an award to an educational institution is contemplated prior to July 1, 1997, the contracting officer shall insert the basic provision at 52.230-1 with its Alternate I [@CL-CMP-7030]. That applies unless the contract is to be performed by a Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC) (see 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(5)), or the provision at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies [@CL-CMP-7031].

Paragraph (c): insert the provision at FAR 52.230-7, Proposal Disclosure—Cost Accounting Practice Changes, in solicitations for contracts subject to CAS as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201 [@CL-CMP-7032].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) applies to solicitations for proposed contracts subject to CAS as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201, and paragraph (c) to solicitations for contracts subject to CAS as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201 [@CL-CMP-7029; @CL-CMP-7032]. Paragraph (b) applies if an award to an educational institution is contemplated prior to July 1, 1997 [@CL-CMP-7030]. Under FAR 30.202-6(a), the contracting officer is responsible for determining when a proposed contract may require CAS coverage and for including the appropriate notice in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7033]. See [Contracting officer, auditor and CFAO responsibilities for Disclosure Statements and CAS coverage](cmp-cas-disclosure-statement-responsibilities.md) and [CAS applicability, coverage and Disclosure Statement thresholds](cmp-cas-applicability.md).

The contracting officer shall insert the provisions in paragraphs (a) and (b) [@CL-CMP-7029; @CL-CMP-7030].

In paragraph (b), the contracting officer shall insert the basic provision set forth at 52.230-1 with its Alternate I if an award to an educational institution is contemplated prior to July 1, 1997 [@CL-CMP-7030], unless the contract is to be performed by a Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC), or the provision at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies [@CL-CMP-7031].

For what each provision asks of an offeror, see [CAS notices and certification in a solicitation](cmp-cas-notices-and-certification.md), [Disclosure of cost accounting practice changes in a proposal](cmp-cas-practice-change-proposal-disclosure.md) and [Cost Accounting Standards for educational institutions](cmp-cas-educational-institution.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation is issued for a proposed contract that is subject to CAS as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201 [@CL-CMP-7029]. The contracting officer inserts the provision at 52.230-1 [@CL-CMP-7029]. The provision at FAR 52.230-7 also goes in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7032]. No award to an educational institution is contemplated prior to July 1, 1997, so the Alternate I direction is not in play [@CL-CMP-7030]. Two provisions are therefore called for here.

A second solicitation contemplates an award to an educational institution prior to July 1, 1997, but the contract is to be performed by a Federally Funded Research and Development Center. The Alternate I direction does not apply [@CL-CMP-7031]. The section does not say what is inserted instead, and paragraph (a) is a separate direction for solicitations for proposed contracts subject to CAS [@CL-CMP-7029], so the example does not decide that point and stops here.

## Never confuse

The provision at 52.230-1 is Cost Accounting Standards Notices and Certification [@CL-CMP-7029], and the provision at FAR 52.230-7 is Proposal Disclosure—Cost Accounting Practice Changes [@CL-CMP-7032].

If an award to an educational institution is contemplated prior to July 1, 1997, the contracting officer shall insert the basic provision set forth at 52.230-1 with its Alternate I [@CL-CMP-7030], unless the contract is to be performed by an FFRDC, or the provision at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies [@CL-CMP-7031].
