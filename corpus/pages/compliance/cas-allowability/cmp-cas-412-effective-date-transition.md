## Purpose

Says when the revised pension cost standard applies to a contractor and how the five-period phase-in of the harmonization rule works.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-CMP-3404] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.412-62, 48 CFR 9904.412-63 and 48 CFR 9904.412-64 [@CL-CMP-8658; @CL-CMP-3404; @CL-CMP-3408] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3404] |
| Applies to | Contractors and subcontractors following the award of a contract or subcontract subject to the standard on or after the Effective Date [@CL-CMP-3405] |

CAS 412 is effective as of February 27, 2012, and is applicable for cost accounting periods after June 30, 2012 [@CL-CMP-3404]. Following the award of a contract or subcontract subject to the standard on or after the Effective Date, contractors shall follow the standard, as amended, beginning with its next cost accounting period beginning after the later of the Implementation Date or the award date [@CL-CMP-3405]. The award date in that rule is the award date of a contract or subcontract to which the standard is applicable [@CL-CMP-3434]. The first day of the cost accounting period in which the amended standard is first applicable to a contractor or subcontractor is the Applicability Date of the CAS Pension Harmonization Rule [@CL-CMP-3406]. Before that Applicability Date, contractors or subcontractors shall follow the version of the standard in effect before the Effective Date [@CL-CMP-3407].

Following the award of a contract or subcontract subject to the standard received on or after the Effective Date, contractors with contracts or subcontracts subject to the standard that were received before the Effective Date shall continue to follow the version of the standard in effect before the Effective Date [@CL-CMP-8043; @CL-CMP-3435]. Beginning with the Applicability Date, those contractors shall follow the standard, as amended, for all contracts or subcontracts subject to the standard [@CL-CMP-3435; @CL-CMP-3436].

The Pension Harmonization Rule Transition Period is the five cost accounting periods beginning with a contractor's first cost accounting period beginning after June 30, 2012, and it is independent of the receipt date of a contract or subcontract subject to the standard [@CL-CMP-3408]. During each successive accounting period of the Transition Period, the contractor shall recognize on a scheduled basis the amount by which the minimum actuarial liability differs from the actuarial accrued liability, and the amount by which the sum of the minimum normal cost plus any expense load differs from the sum of the normal cost plus any expense load [@CL-CMP-3409; @CL-CMP-7358]. One listed schedule gives 0 percent for the first cost accounting period, 25 percent for the second, 50 percent for the third, 75 percent for the fourth and 100 percent for the fifth [@CL-CMP-3410]. In each period, the transitional minimum actuarial liability shall be set equal to the actuarial accrued liability adjusted by an amount equal to the difference between the minimum actuarial liability and the actuarial accrued liability, multiplied by the scheduled applicable percentage for that period [@CL-CMP-3449].

The standard also holds a transition method for the change from the version in effect before March 30, 1995: to be acceptable, any method of transition from compliance with the standard in effect prior to March 30, 1995, to compliance with the standard effective March 30, 1995, must follow the equitable principle that costs which have been previously provided for shall not be redundantly provided for under revised methods, and costs that have not previously been provided for must be provided for under the revised method [@CL-CMP-8044; @CL-CMP-3437]. In one transition illustration using the transition method of paragraph (a)(1) of that subsection, the contractor shall establish an assignable cost deficit equal to $214,000, being $200,000 multiplied by 1.07, which is the prior unfunded assigned cost plus interest [@CL-CMP-3438].

The exemption section of the pension cost standard says there is no exemption for it [@CL-CMP-8658].

## How it works

Two clocks run side by side. The first fixes when the amended standard starts to apply to a contractor, which depends on the award of a subject contract and the contractor's own cost accounting periods [@CL-CMP-3405; @CL-CMP-3406]. Following the award of a subject contract received on or after the Effective Date, contractors that already held subject contracts received before the Effective Date continue on the earlier version until their Applicability Date and then follow the amended standard for all their subject contracts [@CL-CMP-8043; @CL-CMP-3407; @CL-CMP-3435; @CL-CMP-3436]. The second clock is the phase-in, which counts five cost accounting periods from the first one beginning after June 30, 2012 and is independent of the receipt date of a subject contract [@CL-CMP-3408]. The scheduled percentages start at 0 percent and reach 100 percent in the fifth period [@CL-CMP-3410], and each period's transitional minimum actuarial liability is set equal to the actuarial accrued liability adjusted by that percentage of the difference between the minimum actuarial liability and the actuarial accrued liability [@CL-CMP-3449].

The [pension composition card](cmp-cas-412-pension-composition.md) explains the measurement that the phase-in changes. The [definitions card](cmp-cas-412-definitions.md), the [techniques card](cmp-cas-412-techniques.md) and the [illustrations card](cmp-cas-412-illustrations.md) cover the terms, the rules and the worked outcomes of the standard. The [companion standard card](cmp-cas-413-illustrations-effective-date-transition.md) records the effective date of the companion standard [@CL-CMP-3413].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has calendar-year cost accounting periods. The first period that begins after June 30, 2012 is the first period of the Transition Period, and the schedule gives 0 percent for it.

In the fourth period, the actuarial accrued liability is 1,000 and the minimum actuarial liability is 1,100. The difference is 100. The scheduled percentage for the fourth period is 75 percent, so the transitional minimum actuarial liability is 1,075.

## Never confuse

The Effective Date is February 27, 2012 [@CL-CMP-3404]. The Applicability Date is the first day of the cost accounting period in which the amended standard is first applicable to a contractor or subcontractor [@CL-CMP-3406]. Prior to the Applicability Date, contractors or subcontractors shall follow the standard in effect prior to the Effective Date [@CL-CMP-3407].
