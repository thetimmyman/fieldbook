## Purpose

Explains who keeps a Disclosure Statement accurate, how amendments and revisions arrive, how the cognizant Federal agency decides that a Disclosure Statement is adequate, and where the Cost Accounting Standards preambles can be obtained.

### Amendments and revisions

Contractors and subcontractors are responsible for maintaining accurate Disclosure Statements and complying with disclosed practices [@CL-CMP-7541]. Amendments and revisions to Disclosure Statements may be submitted at any time and may be proposed by either the contractor or the Government [@CL-CMP-7542]. Resubmission of complete, updated Disclosure Statements is discouraged except when extensive changes require it to assist the review process [@CL-CMP-7543].

### Adequacy

Federal agencies shall prescribe regulations and establish internal procedures by which each, when serving as the cognizant Federal agency for a particular contractor location, will promptly determine on behalf of the Government that a Disclosure Statement has adequately disclosed the practices [@CL-CMP-7544]. The practices are those required to be disclosed by the Cost Accounting Standards Board's rules, regulations and Standards [@CL-CMP-7545]. The determination of adequacy shall be distributed to all affected agencies [@CL-CMP-7546]. Agencies are urged to coordinate on the development of such regulations [@CL-CMP-7547].

### Preambles

Preambles to the Cost Accounting Standards published by the original Cost Accounting Standards Board, as well as those published by the signatories to the Federal Acquisition Regulation respecting changes made under their regulatory authorities, are available [@CL-CMP-7548]. They are available by writing to the Publications Office, Office of Administration, Executive Office of the President, 725 17th Street NW., room 2200, Washington, DC 20500, or by calling (202) 395-7332 [@CL-CMP-7549].

## How it works

Contractors and subcontractors are responsible for maintaining accurate Disclosure Statements and complying with disclosed practices [@CL-CMP-7541]. Amendments and revisions may be submitted at any time and may be proposed by either the contractor or the Government [@CL-CMP-7542]. Resubmission of complete, updated Disclosure Statements is discouraged except when extensive changes require it to assist the review process [@CL-CMP-7543].

Federal agencies shall prescribe regulations and establish internal procedures by which each, when serving as the cognizant Federal agency for a particular contractor location, will promptly determine on behalf of the Government that a Disclosure Statement has adequately disclosed the practices required to be disclosed by the Cost Accounting Standards Board's rules, regulations and Standards [@CL-CMP-7544; @CL-CMP-7545]. The determination of adequacy shall be distributed to all affected agencies [@CL-CMP-7546], and agencies are urged to coordinate on the development of such regulations [@CL-CMP-7547].

The preambles are available [@CL-CMP-7548], by writing to the Publications Office, Office of Administration, Executive Office of the President, or by calling the number given [@CL-CMP-7549].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor changes how it describes a service center in its Disclosure Statement. It proposes an amendment mid-year, and the Government could equally have proposed the change [@CL-CMP-7542]. The amendment may be submitted at any time, and the contractor remains responsible for keeping the statement accurate and for complying with the practices it discloses [@CL-CMP-7541; @CL-CMP-7542]. Resubmission of a complete, updated statement is discouraged except when extensive changes require it to assist the review process, and a change this narrow does not [@CL-CMP-7543]. The cognizant Federal agency for that location determines, under its own procedures, whether the statement has adequately disclosed the practices required to be disclosed, and distributes the determination to all affected agencies [@CL-CMP-7544; @CL-CMP-7546]. To read the preamble for a Standard, the contractor's analyst can write to the Publications Office or call it [@CL-CMP-7549].
