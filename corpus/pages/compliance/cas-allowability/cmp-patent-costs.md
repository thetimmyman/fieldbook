## Purpose

Shows when work on patents can be charged to a Government contract, and where patent work stops being a contract cost.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0851] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-30 [@CL-CMP-0851] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0851] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

The patent costs listed in paragraph (a) of the section are allowable to the extent that they are incurred as requirements of a Government contract [@CL-CMP-0851]. The list covers the preparation of invention disclosures, reports and other documents [@CL-CMP-0852], the art search needed to make those disclosures [@CL-CMP-0853], and other costs of filing and prosecuting a United States patent application where title or a royalty-free license is to be conveyed to the Government [@CL-CMP-0854].

General counseling services relating to patent matters, such as advice on patent laws, regulations, clauses and employee agreements, are allowable [@CL-CMP-0855]. Other than those for general counseling services, patent costs not required by the contract are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0856].

## How it works

The test for the listed patent costs is whether the contract requires the work [@CL-CMP-0851]. Patent costs that the contract does not require are unallowable, apart from general counseling services [@CL-CMP-0856]. General counseling services are allowable without that requirement [@CL-CMP-0855; @CL-CMP-0856]. Both the list in paragraph (a) and the counseling rule in paragraph (b) carry a cross-reference to FAR 31.205-33 [@CL-CMP-0852; @CL-CMP-0855].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract requires the company to file a United States application and convey a royalty-free license to the Government. Filing and prosecution cost 12,000, the art search before the disclosure costs 3,000, and general patent counseling costs 5,000. All three are allowable, 20,000 in total.

## Never confuse

A patent cost under this section is a cost of disclosing, searching for, filing or prosecuting a patent, or of counseling about patents. A royalty is a payment for the right to use a patent, and the royalties section treats it separately; see [Royalties and other costs for use of patents](cmp-patent-royalties.md). For the general allowability test, see [Allowability factors](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md).

## Company calibration

Whether contract-required patent work is tagged at the source so that non-required patent cost never enters a rate or billing pool. [@Q-044]
