## Purpose

Shows when a negotiated contract is exempt from the Cost Accounting Standards, when a business unit takes full coverage, and when it must file a Disclosure Statement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Cost Accounting Standards Board rules [@CL-CMP-0001] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.201-1(b)(2), 9903.201-2(a) and 9903.202-1(b)(1) [@CL-CMP-0001] [@CL-CMP-0002] [@CL-CMP-0003] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0001] |
| Applies to | The Board's regulation, effective 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0001]. A business unit under the earlier full-coverage line may transition to modified coverage [@CL-CMP-10037; @CL-CMP-10038]. How the new values otherwise apply to an award made before that date is not established |

Negotiated contracts and subcontracts not in excess of $35 million are exempt from all CAS requirements [@CL-CMP-8116; @CL-CMP-0001]. Full CAS coverage applies to a contractor business unit that receives a single CAS-covered contract award of $100 million or more, or that received $100 million or more in net CAS-covered awards during its preceding cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-8117; @CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-7069]. A business unit selected for a CAS-covered contract or subcontract of $100 million or more must submit a Disclosure Statement before award [@CL-CMP-0003]. A business unit or segment that received net awards of negotiated prime contracts and subcontracts subject to CAS totaling $100 million or more in its most recent cost accounting period must also submit a Disclosure Statement before award of its first CAS-covered contract in the immediately following cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-7070]. However, if that first CAS-covered contract is received within 90 days of the start of the cost accounting period, the contractor is not required to file until the end of 90 days [@CL-CMP-7071]. These values are stated in the Cost Accounting Standards Board's regulation in 48 CFR 9903 and took effect on 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0001; @CL-CMP-0002].

Before 2026-10-01 the exemption line was $2.5 million, and the same rule ended the $7.5 million trigger contract [@CL-CMP-10034; @CL-CMP-0001]. The full-coverage and Disclosure Statement lines were $50 million [@CL-CMP-10035]. A contractor or subcontractor subject to full coverage based on that $50 million line may transition the affected contracts or subcontracts to modified coverage at the start of the business unit's next full cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-10037], the period beginning on or after October 1, 2026, if the business unit has no unresolved CAS noncompliances and does not meet the criteria for full coverage at $100 million [@CL-CMP-10038]. How the new values otherwise apply to an award made before that date is not established here.

The codified FAR text and provisions in the Corpus still print earlier figures: FAR 30.201-4(b)(1) prints $2.5 million and $50 million [@CL-RTE-0842], and the FAR 52.230-1 certificates print $50 million [@CL-CMP-5923; @CL-CMP-5926]. That is a different instrument from the Board's regulation, whose figures above took effect on 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-0003]. Which of the two sets of figures applies where the FAR text still prints the earlier one is not established here.

## How it works

First test the contract: a contract or subcontract in one of the listed exempt categories, such as a negotiated contract not in excess of the exemption threshold, is exempt from all CAS requirements [@CL-CMP-8116; @CL-CMP-0001]. Full coverage applies to a contractor business unit that receives a single covered award at or above the full-coverage threshold, or that received net covered awards at or above it during its preceding cost accounting period, and requires compliance with all of the CAS specified in part 9904 in effect on the date of the contract award and any CAS that become applicable because of later award of a CAS-covered contract [@CL-CMP-8117; @CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-7069]. Modified coverage may be applied to a covered contract below the full-coverage threshold awarded to a business unit that received less than that threshold in net CAS-covered awards in the immediately preceding cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-8118]. A Disclosure Statement is a written description of a contractor's cost accounting practices and procedures [@CL-CMP-8119].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit wins a negotiated award of 40 million, above an exemption threshold of 35 million, and no other exemption applies, such as small business or commercial products, so the award is CAS-covered. Its CAS-covered awards in the preceding period total 60 million, below a full-coverage threshold of 100 million, so modified coverage may be applied to the award. No Disclosure Statement is required: the award is below a Disclosure Statement threshold of 100 million, and so are the preceding period's net covered awards of 60 million.

## Never confuse

A business unit means any segment of an organization, or an entire business organization that is not divided into segments [@CL-RTE-9063].

The Cost Accounting Standards statute itself, and how a contract reaches it through the FAR part on Cost Accounting Standards administration, are covered in [What the Cost Accounting Standards statute requires and who it applies to](cmp-cas-statute-scope-and-application.md).
