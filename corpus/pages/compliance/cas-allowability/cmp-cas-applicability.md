## Purpose

Shows when a negotiated contract is exempt from the Cost Accounting Standards, when a business unit takes full coverage, and when it must file a Disclosure Statement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Cost Accounting Standards Board rules [@CL-CMP-0001] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.201-1(b)(2), 9903.201-2(a) and 9903.202-1(b)(1) [@CL-CMP-0001] [@CL-CMP-0002] [@CL-CMP-0003] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0001] |
| Applies to | Awards from 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0001] |

Negotiated contracts and subcontracts of $35 million or less are exempt from all CAS requirements, tested at award [@CL-CMP-0001]. A business unit takes full CAS coverage when it receives a single CAS-covered award of $100 million or more, or $100 million or more in net CAS-covered awards in its preceding cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-7069]. A business unit selected for a CAS-covered contract or subcontract of $100 million or more must submit a Disclosure Statement before award [@CL-CMP-0003]. A business unit or segment that received net awards of negotiated prime contracts and subcontracts subject to CAS totaling $100 million or more in its most recent cost accounting period must also submit a Disclosure Statement before award of its first CAS-covered contract in the immediately following cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-7070]. These values took effect on 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0001].

## How it works

First test the contract: below the exemption threshold, or within another listed exemption, no CAS applies. A covered contract then takes full coverage if the business unit receives a single covered award at or above the full-coverage threshold, or had net covered awards at or above it in its preceding period, and modified coverage otherwise [@CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-7069]. Full coverage brings all the standards; modified coverage brings a short subset. The Disclosure Statement describes the unit's cost accounting practices, and the government relies on it when it judges a later change in those practices.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit wins a negotiated award of 40 million, above an exemption threshold of 35 million, and no other exemption applies, such as small business or commercial products, so the award is CAS-covered. Its CAS-covered awards in the preceding period total 60 million, below a full-coverage threshold of 100 million, so the award takes modified coverage. No Disclosure Statement is required: the award is below a Disclosure Statement threshold of 100 million, and so are the preceding period's net covered awards of 60 million.

## Never confuse

CAS coverage decides which cost accounting rules apply; Allowability decides whether a cost may be charged at all. A business unit is a segment, or the whole organization if it has no segments.

The Cost Accounting Standards statute itself, and how a contract reaches it through the FAR part on Cost Accounting Standards administration, are covered in [What the Cost Accounting Standards statute requires and who it applies to](cmp-cas-statute-scope-and-application.md).
