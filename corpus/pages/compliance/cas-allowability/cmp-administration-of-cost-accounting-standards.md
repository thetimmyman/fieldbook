## Purpose

Explains how the CAS administration clause identifies affected contracts, assigns administration, and defines a desirable practice change.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0611] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-6 [@CL-CMP-0611] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0611] |
| Applies to | CAS requirements under contracts containing this clause [@CL-CMP-0611] |

The clause requires the contractor to take its stated CAS administration steps [@CL-CMP-0611]. It identifies affected contracts by changed or noncompliant practices and assigns CAS administration to the cognizant Federal agency official [@CL-CMP-0612; @CL-CMP-0613; @CL-CMP-0614].

## How it works

One affected-contract case uses different practices for estimating and for accumulating and reporting costs [@CL-CMP-0612]. Another uses a noncompliant practice for either purpose [@CL-CMP-0613]. A desirable change must be compliant and found desirable and not detrimental to the Government by the cognizant official [@CL-CMP-0615].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor estimates a subcontract with one practice and accumulates and reports costs with a changed practice. If the subcontract is subject to CAS rules and regulations, it is an affected CAS-covered subcontract under the changed-practice case.

## Never confuse

A changed practice and a noncompliant practice are separate affected-contract cases. A desirable change must be compliant and requires the cognizant official's findings [@CL-CMP-0612; @CL-CMP-0613; @CL-CMP-0615].
