## Purpose

Explains how the CAS administration clause identifies affected contracts, assigns administration, and defines a desirable practice change.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0611] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-6 [@CL-CMP-0611] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-6 [@CL-CMP-0236] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0611] |
| Applies to | CAS requirements under contracts containing this clause [@CL-CMP-0611] |

For the purpose of administering the Cost Accounting Standards requirements under the contract, the Contractor must take the steps outlined in paragraphs (b) through (i) and (k) through (n) of the clause [@CL-CMP-0611]. The clause defines an affected CAS-covered contract or subcontract as one subject to CAS rules and regulations for which the Contractor or subcontractor either used one practice to estimate costs and a changed practice to accumulate and report costs, or used a noncompliant practice for purposes of estimating or accumulating and reporting costs [@CL-CMP-8028; @CL-CMP-0612; @CL-CMP-0613]. It defines the cognizant Federal agency official as the Contracting Officer assigned by the cognizant Federal agency to administer the CAS [@CL-CMP-0614].

## How it works

One of the two alternatives for an affected contract or subcontract is that the Contractor or subcontractor used one practice to estimate costs and a changed practice to accumulate and report costs [@CL-CMP-8028; @CL-CMP-0612]. The other is that it used a noncompliant practice for purposes of estimating or accumulating and reporting costs [@CL-CMP-8028; @CL-CMP-0613]. A desirable change must be compliant and found desirable and not detrimental to the Government by the cognizant official [@CL-CMP-0615].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified FAR part 52 text, as Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.230-6 keeps the requirement that, for the purpose of administering the Cost Accounting Standards requirements under the contract, the Contractor take the steps outlined in paragraphs (b) through (i) and (k) through (n) of the clause [@CL-CMP-0236; @CL-CMP-0611]. It keeps the two alternative affected-contract cases: a contract or subcontract subject to CAS rules and regulations for which the Contractor or subcontractor used one cost accounting practice to estimate costs and a changed cost accounting practice to accumulate and report costs [@CL-CMP-0237; @CL-CMP-0238; @CL-CMP-0612; @CL-CMP-8028], or used a noncompliant practice for purposes of estimating or accumulating and reporting costs [@CL-CMP-0237; @CL-CMP-0239; @CL-CMP-0613]. It keeps the definition of the cognizant Federal agency official as the Contracting Officer assigned by the cognizant Federal agency to administer the CAS [@CL-CMP-0240; @CL-CMP-0614]. It keeps the definition of a desirable change as a compliant change to a Contractor's established or disclosed cost accounting practices that the cognizant Federal agency official finds is desirable and not detrimental to the Government [@CL-CMP-0241; @CL-CMP-0615], and is, therefore, not subject to the no increased cost prohibition provisions of CAS-covered contracts and subcontracts affected by the change [@CL-CMP-0242].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor estimates a subcontract with one practice and accumulates and reports costs with a changed practice. If the subcontract is subject to CAS rules and regulations, it is an affected CAS-covered subcontract under the changed-practice case.

## Never confuse

A changed practice and a noncompliant practice are the two alternatives in the definition of an affected contract or subcontract [@CL-CMP-8028; @CL-CMP-0612; @CL-CMP-0613]. A desirable change must be compliant and requires the cognizant official's findings [@CL-CMP-0612; @CL-CMP-0613; @CL-CMP-0615].
