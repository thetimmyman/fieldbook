## Purpose

Shows the duties the Cost Accounting Standards clause for educational institutions puts on a contractor: disclosure, consistency, compliance, adjustments, access to records and flowdown to subcontracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5929] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-5 [@CL-CMP-5929] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-5 [@CL-CMP-0251] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5929] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the clause, unless the contract is exempt under the cited CAS exemption rules [@CL-CMP-5929] |

Unless the contract is exempt under the cited exemption rules, the provisions of 48 CFR part 9903 are incorporated by reference and the contractor carries out the duties the clause lists [@CL-CMP-5929]. Where a business unit must submit a Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-6509], the practices disclosed for the contract must be the same as the practices currently disclosed and applied on all other contracts and subcontracts that the contractor is performing and that contain a CAS clause [@CL-CMP-5930].

## How it works

### Disclosure

The disclosure duty applies to CAS-covered contracts only [@CL-CMP-6509]. It applies if a business unit of an educational institution, defined as an institution of higher education in the OMB Uniform Guidance and the statute it cites, is required to submit a Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-6509]. The business unit must disclose in writing its cost accounting practices as the cited regulations require, including methods of distinguishing direct costs from indirect costs and the basis used for accumulating and allocating indirect costs [@CL-CMP-6510]. If the contractor has notified the contracting officer that the Disclosure Statement contains trade secrets and privileged and confidential commercial or financial information, the Disclosure Statement is protected and is not released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-6511].

### Consistency and practice changes

The contractor must follow its cost accounting practices consistently in accumulating and reporting contract performance cost data for this contract [@CL-CMP-5931]. A change in cost accounting practices made for the purposes of any contract or subcontract subject to CAS requirements must be applied prospectively to this contract, and the Disclosure Statement, if required, must be amended accordingly [@CL-CMP-5932]. If an accounting principle change mandated under the OMB Uniform Guidance requires a change in the contractor's practices after the date of contract award, that change also applies prospectively to this contract [@CL-CMP-6512]. The Disclosure Statement, if required, is amended accordingly, and if the contract price or cost allowance is affected by such changes, adjustment is made under the paragraph on equitable adjustments or the paragraph on noncompliance, as appropriate [@CL-CMP-6513; @CL-CMP-5934; @CL-CMP-5935].

### Standards in effect

The contractor must comply with all CAS, including modifications and interpretations, contained in 48 CFR part 9905 in effect on the date of award or, if it has submitted certified cost or pricing data, on the date of final agreement on price [@CL-CMP-5933], which is as shown on its signed certificate of current cost or pricing data [@CL-CMP-6606]. It must also comply with any CAS, or modifications to CAS, that hereafter become applicable to a contract or subcontract, and compliance is required prospectively from the date of applicability to that contract or subcontract [@CL-CMP-6514].

### Equitable adjustments for changes in practice

If the contract cost is affected by a change that the contractor is required to make to its established cost accounting practices under the compliance paragraph, the contractor agrees to an equitable adjustment as provided in the Changes clause [@CL-CMP-5934]. The contractor must negotiate with the contracting officer to determine the terms and conditions under which a change may be made to a cost accounting practice, other than a change made under another provision of the same paragraph [@CL-CMP-6515]. No agreement may be made under that provision that will increase costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-6516]. When the parties agree to such a change, other than a required change or an accounting principle amendment change, the contractor negotiates an equitable adjustment as provided in the Changes clause [@CL-CMP-6517]. The contractor also agrees to an equitable adjustment as provided in the Changes clause if the contract cost is materially affected by an accounting principle amendment required under the OMB Uniform Guidance [@CL-CMP-6518], being one that, on becoming effective after the date of contract award, requires the contractor to make a change to its established cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-6519].

### Noncompliance

If the contractor or a subcontractor fails to comply with an applicable Cost Accounting Standard, or to follow any cost accounting practice consistently, and the failure results in increased costs paid by the United States, an adjustment of the contract price or cost allowance is made, as appropriate [@CL-CMP-5935]. The adjustment provides for recovery of the increased costs to the United States, together with interest computed at the annual rate established under section 6621(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 [@CL-CMP-5936], for the period from the time the payment by the United States was made to the time the adjustment is effected [@CL-CMP-6520]. Subject to an exception stated in the same sentence, the Government does not recover costs greater than the increased cost to the Government, in the aggregate, on the relevant contracts subject to the price adjustment [@CL-CMP-6521]. The cap does not apply if the contractor made a change in its cost accounting practices of which it was aware or should have been aware at the time of price negotiations and which it failed to disclose to the Government [@CL-CMP-6522].

### Disputes and records

A failure to agree whether the contractor or a subcontractor has complied with an applicable CAS or CAS rule, and as to any cost adjustment demanded by the United States, constitutes a dispute under chapter 71 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-5937]. The contractor must permit authorized representatives of the Government to examine and make copies of documents, papers or records relating to compliance with the clause [@CL-CMP-6523].

### Subcontracts

The contractor must include the substance of the clause, except the paragraph on disputes, in all negotiated subcontracts it enters into, and must require that inclusion in all other subcontracts, of any tier [@CL-CMP-5938; @CL-CMP-5937]. The substance includes the obligation to comply with all applicable CAS in effect on the subcontractor's award date or, if the subcontractor has submitted certified cost or pricing data, on the date of final agreement on price [@CL-CMP-6524]. Three exceptions follow. If the subcontract is awarded to a business unit that is subject to other types of CAS coverage, the substance of the applicable clause in 48 CFR 9903.201-4 is inserted instead [@CL-CMP-5939]. The requirement applies only to negotiated subcontracts in excess of the lower CAS threshold in FAR 30.201-4(b) on the date of subcontract award [@CL-CMP-5940]. It does not apply to negotiated subcontracts otherwise exempt from the requirement to include a CAS clause [@CL-CMP-5941].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO section keeps the incorporation of 48 CFR part 9903 by reference unless the contract is exempt under 48 CFR 9903.201-1 and 9903.201-2 [@CL-CMP-0252; @CL-CMP-5929]. It keeps the scope of paragraph (a)(1), which applies to CAS-covered contracts only and applies if a business unit of an educational institution, defined as an institution of higher education in the OMB Uniform Guidance and the statute it cites, is required to submit a Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-0253; @CL-CMP-6509]. It keeps the rule that an accounting principle change mandated under the OMB Uniform Guidance that requires a change in the contractor's cost accounting practices after the date of contract award is applied prospectively to the contract, that the Disclosure Statement, if required, is amended accordingly, and that any effect on the contract price or cost allowance is adjusted under the paragraph on equitable adjustments or the paragraph on noncompliance, as appropriate [@CL-CMP-0254; @CL-CMP-8138; @CL-CMP-6512; @CL-CMP-6513]. It keeps the contractor's agreement to an equitable adjustment as provided in the Changes clause if the contract cost is affected by a change which, under paragraph (a)(3) of the clause, the contractor is required to make to its established cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-0255; @CL-CMP-5934]. It keeps the cap on recovery for noncompliance, subject to an exception stated in the same sentence, at the increased cost to the Government in the aggregate on the relevant contracts subject to the price adjustment [@CL-CMP-0256; @CL-CMP-6521], and it keeps the exception for a change in cost accounting practices of which the contractor was aware or should have been aware at the time of price negotiations and which it failed to disclose to the Government [@CL-CMP-0257; @CL-CMP-6522]. It keeps the rule that the subcontract requirement does not apply to negotiated subcontracts otherwise exempt from the requirement to include a CAS clause as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201-1 [@CL-CMP-0259; @CL-CMP-5941]. For the lower CAS threshold in the subcontract requirement, the RFO text says FAR 30.205(b), where the codified text says FAR 30.201-4(b) [@CL-CMP-0258; @CL-CMP-5940].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A university research unit performs a CAS-covered contract that carries this clause. After award, an accounting principle amendment required under the OMB Uniform Guidance takes effect and forces a change in the unit's established practices. The change raises the contract cost by 40,000, and that effect is material. The change applies prospectively to the contract, the unit amends its Disclosure Statement, and the unit agrees to an equitable adjustment as provided in the Changes clause.

Separately, the unit fails to follow a disclosed practice for part of a year, and the cost paid by the United States rises by 30,000. The price or cost allowance is adjusted to recover that 30,000 with interest from the time of payment to the time the adjustment is effected. The costs recovered on the relevant contracts in the aggregate are capped at the increased cost of 30,000. Had the unit made a change in its practices that it knew of at the time of price negotiations and did not disclose, the cap would not apply.

The unit awards two negotiated subcontracts. One is above the lower CAS threshold on its award date and goes to a business unit under another type of CAS coverage, so the unit inserts the substance of the applicable clause for that coverage instead. The other is below that threshold on its award date, so the clause requirement does not reach it.

## Never confuse

An equitable adjustment for a change the contractor is required to make [@CL-CMP-5934] is not the recovery for noncompliance, which carries interest [@CL-CMP-5936]. The cap on that recovery has an exception for an undisclosed change [@CL-CMP-6522].

A contract for which the offeror elected modified coverage in Part II of the notice carries the Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause instead of the Cost Accounting Standards clause [@CL-CMP-5925]; that clause is covered on the [modified coverage card](cmp-disclosure-consistency-modified-cas.md). The thresholds that decide coverage are on the [CAS applicability card](cmp-cas-applicability.md).

The codified clause points to FAR 30.201-4(b) for the lower CAS threshold on subcontracts [@CL-CMP-5940], while the RFO clause points to FAR 30.205(b) [@CL-CMP-0258].
