## Purpose

Shows which corporate structure and capital raising expenditures are unallowable as organization costs, and what the compensation carve-out takes out of that bar.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0357] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-27(a)(1) [@CL-CMP-0357] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-27(a)(1)(i) to (iii) [@CL-CMP-8569; @CL-CMP-8570; @CL-CMP-8571] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0357] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Except for the compensation carve-out, expenditures in connection with each of the following are unallowable: planning or executing the organization or reorganization of the corporate structure, mergers and acquisitions, resisting or planning to resist a reorganization or a change in controlling interest, and raising capital measured as net worth plus long-term liabilities [@CL-CMP-0357; @CL-CMP-0358; @CL-CMP-0359].

Such expenditures include but are not limited to incorporation fees and costs of attorneys, accountants, brokers, promoters and organizers, management consultants and investment counselors, whether or not employees of the contractor [@CL-CMP-0360]. Unallowable reorganization costs include the cost of any change in the contractor's financial structure, excluding administrative costs of short-term borrowings for working capital, resulting in alterations in the rights and interests of security holders, whether or not additional capital is raised [@CL-CMP-0361]. The cost of activities primarily intended to provide compensation will not be considered organizational costs subject to this subsection, but will be governed by 31.205-6 [@CL-CMP-0362]. These activities include acquiring stock for (1) executive bonuses, (2) employee savings plans, and (3) employee stock ownership plans [@CL-CMP-0363].

## How it works

Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, expenditures in connection with planning or executing the organization or reorganization of the corporate structure of a business, including mergers and acquisitions, resisting or planning to resist the reorganization of the corporate structure of a business or a change in the controlling interest in the ownership of a business, and raising capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities) are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0357; @CL-CMP-0358; @CL-CMP-0359]. Such expenditures include but are not limited to incorporation fees and costs of attorneys, accountants, brokers, promoters and organizers, management consultants and investment counselors, whether or not employees of the contractor [@CL-CMP-0360]. The cost of activities primarily intended to provide compensation will not be considered organizational costs subject to this subsection, but will be governed by 31.205-6 [@CL-CMP-0362].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-27(a)(1), except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, expenditures in connection with the following are unallowable: (i) planning or executing the organization or reorganization of the corporate structure of a business, including mergers and acquisitions [@CL-CMP-8569]; (ii) resisting or planning to resist the reorganization of the corporate structure of a business or a change in the controlling interest in the ownership of a business [@CL-CMP-8570]; and (iii) raising capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities) [@CL-CMP-8571]. Under paragraph (a)(3), unallowable reorganization costs include the cost of any change in the contractor's financial structure, excluding administrative costs of short-term borrowings for working capital, resulting in alterations in the rights and interests of security holders, whether or not additional capital is raised [@CL-CMP-8573]. Under paragraph (b), the cost of activities primarily intended to provide compensation will not be considered organizational costs subject to this section but will be governed by 31.205-6 [@CL-CMP-8574], and these activities include acquiring stock for (1) executive bonuses, (2) employee savings plans, and (3) employee stock ownership plans [@CL-CMP-8575]. Its paragraph (a)(2) says such expenditures include incorporation fees and costs of attorneys, accountants, brokers, promoters and organizers, management consultants and investment counselors, whether or not employees of the contractor [@CL-CMP-8572].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The company pays 400,000 to advisers on an acquisition, 60,000 to resist a change of control, and 20,000 in fees on a short-term working capital line. The advisory and defence costs are unallowable organization costs, and the administrative costs of the short-term working capital borrowing are excluded from the unallowable reorganization costs for a change in the contractor's financial structure [@CL-CMP-0361].

## Never confuse

The cost of activities primarily intended to provide compensation will not be considered organizational costs subject to this subsection, but will be governed by 31.205-6 [@CL-CMP-0362]; these activities include acquiring stock for executive bonuses, employee savings plans, and employee stock ownership plans [@CL-CMP-0363]. For penalties on unallowable costs in final rate proposals, see [Penalties for unallowable costs in final rate proposals](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md).

Codified FAR 31.205-27(a) says such expenditures include but are not limited to the listed fees and costs [@CL-CMP-0360]. RFO paragraph (a)(2) says such expenditures include incorporation fees and costs of attorneys, accountants, brokers, promoters and organizers, management consultants and investment counselors, whether or not employees of the contractor [@CL-CMP-8572].
