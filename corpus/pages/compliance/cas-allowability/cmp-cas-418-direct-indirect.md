## Purpose

Shows how a business unit classifies direct and indirect costs and pools and allocates the indirect costs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-CMP-0216] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.418-40(a) [@CL-CMP-0216] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0216] |
| Applies to | Direct and indirect costs of a business unit [@CL-CMP-0216] |

A business unit must have a written statement of accounting policies and practices for classifying costs as direct or indirect, which must be consistently applied [@CL-CMP-0216]. Indirect costs are accumulated in homogeneous indirect cost pools [@CL-CMP-0217]. Pooled costs are allocated to cost objectives in reasonable proportion to the beneficial or causal relationship of the costs to the objectives [@CL-CMP-0218].

## How it works

Indirect costs must be accumulated in indirect cost pools that are homogeneous [@CL-CMP-0217]. For an indirect cost pool that includes a material amount of the costs of management or supervision of activities involving direct labor or direct material costs [@CL-RTE-9131], where those costs cannot be allocated on measures of a specific beneficial or causal relationship, the base selected to measure the allocation of the pooled costs to cost objectives shall be a base representative of the activity being managed or supervised [@CL-RTE-0665]. For homogeneous indirect cost pools that do not include material amounts of those costs [@CL-RTE-9132], the pooled costs shall be allocated using an appropriate measure of resource consumption [@CL-RTE-0668]. If consumption measures are unavailable or impractical to ascertain, the next best representation of the beneficial or causal relationship for allocation is a measure of the output of the activities of the indirect cost pool [@CL-RTE-0669]. If neither resources consumed nor output of the activities can be measured practically, a surrogate that varies in proportion to the services received shall be used to measure the resources consumed [@CL-CMP-8203].

The Board's final rule on CAS 407, effective October 1, 2026, rescinded that standard and moved its retained requirements on standard costs and related variances at the production unit level into CAS 418 [@CL-CMP-1516; @CL-CMP-1517; @CL-CMP-1518]. The printed text of 9904.418-30 now defines a production unit [@CL-CMP-1527]. The printed text of 9904.418-50 now holds paragraph (h) on standard costing, which provides that a labor-rate standard may be set to cover a group of direct labor workers who perform disparate functions only under the conditions in either paragraph (h)(1)(i) or (ii) of that section [@CL-CMP-1526].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit pools 500,000 of supervision cost over a fabrication department and allocates it on direct labor hours because the pool is dominated by management of direct labor. A second pool of 200,000 for a quality-test activity with no supervision cost is allocated on the number of tests performed.

## Never confuse

A business unit shall have a written statement of accounting policies and practices for classifying costs as direct or indirect which shall be consistently applied [@CL-CMP-0216]. Indirect costs shall be accumulated in indirect cost pools which are homogeneous [@CL-CMP-0217].
