## Purpose

Shows which agency should lead administration of the Cost Accounting Standards for a contractor location, and how agencies are to coordinate and set up their own procedures.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-CMP-1765] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.201-7 [@CL-CMP-1765] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-1765] |
| Applies to | Administration of the CAS requirements for a contractor organization or location [@CL-CMP-1765] |

The requirements are, to the maximum extent practicable, administered by the cognizant Federal agency responsible for the contractor organization or location, usually the agency responsible for negotiating indirect cost rates on behalf of the Government [@CL-CMP-1765].

That agency should take the lead role in administering the requirements and coordinate CAS administrative actions with all affected agencies [@CL-CMP-1766].

## How it works

Where there are multiple CAS-covered contracts or more than one agency is involved, agencies should discourage contracting officers from administering CAS individually, contract by contract [@CL-CMP-1767]. The section says coordinated actions give greater assurances that a contractor follows its cost accounting practices consistently under all its CAS-covered contracts [@CL-CMP-1902].

Agencies must prescribe regulations and internal policies and procedures for administering the requirements of CAS-covered contracts, with particular emphasis on inter-agency coordination [@CL-CMP-1768].

Those policies must provide for designating the office or officials responsible for administering CAS under the agency's CAS-covered contracts at each contractor business unit, and for delegating the necessary contracting authority to agency individuals authorized to administer the terms and conditions of CAS-covered contracts, such as administrative contracting officers or other agency officials authorized to perform in that capacity [@CL-CMP-1769; @CL-CMP-8047].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds CAS-covered work with three agencies across two business units, and one agency negotiates the indirect rates for both units. That agency is the cognizant Federal agency, so it should take the lead and coordinate CAS actions with the others, and each agency's internal policy names the office or official that administers CAS at each business unit. The answer: administration should run through the cognizant agency and the named officials, not through each contract separately.

## Never confuse

The cognizant agency is not the same as the contracting officer on a single contract: the cognizant agency is responsible for a contractor organization or location, and the section says agencies should discourage contract-by-contract administration where several contracts or agencies are involved [@CL-CMP-1765; @CL-CMP-1767].

The clause that assigns administration to the cognizant Federal agency official is set out in [Administration of cost accounting standards](../cas-allowability/cmp-administration-of-cost-accounting-standards.md), and the wider split of oversight duties in [Oversight roles](../audit-retention/cmp-oversight-roles.md).
