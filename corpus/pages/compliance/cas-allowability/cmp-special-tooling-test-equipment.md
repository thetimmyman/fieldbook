## Purpose

Shows how the cost of special tooling and special test equipment is treated, including the cases where it is allowable only as depreciation or amortization.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0863] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-40 [@CL-CMP-0863] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-40 [@CL-CMP-9632; @CL-CMP-9633; @CL-CMP-9634; @CL-CMP-9635] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0863] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Under FAR 31.205-40(b), the cost of special tooling and special test equipment used in performing one or more Government contracts is allowable and shall be allocated to the specific Government contract or contracts for which acquired [@CL-CMP-0863], except that the cost of (1) items acquired by the contractor before the effective date of the contract (or replacement of such items), whether or not altered or adapted for use in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-1401], and (2) items which the contract schedule specifically excludes, shall be allowable only as depreciation or amortization [@CL-CMP-0864; @CL-CMP-0865].

## How it works

Under paragraph (c), when items are disqualified as special tooling or special test equipment because with relatively minor expense they can be made suitable for general purpose use and have a value as such commensurate with their value as special tooling or special test equipment [@CL-CMP-0866], the cost of adapting the items for use under the contract and the cost of returning them to their prior configuration are allowable [@CL-CMP-0867].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-40(a), except as provided in paragraph (b), the cost of special tooling and special test equipment used in performing one or more Government contracts is allowable and must be allocated to the specific Government contract or contracts for which it is acquired [@CL-CMP-9632]. Under paragraph (b), depreciation or amortization is the only allowable cost for special tooling and special test equipment (1) acquired or replaced by the contractor before the effective date of the contract, whether or not altered or adapted for use in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-9633]; or (2) specifically excluded in the contract schedule [@CL-CMP-9634]. Under paragraph (c), the cost to adapt contractor-owned special tooling or special test equipment for use under a contract is an allowable cost, and the cost to return the equipment to its prior configuration is also an allowable cost [@CL-CMP-9635].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract needs a 300,000 test rig bought for that contract, so its cost is allowable and allocated to the contract. The company also holds a 90,000 tool bought before the contract took effect, and a 20,000 item the contract schedule excludes; both are allowable only as depreciation or amortization. Under the codified text, a general purpose jig, disqualified as special tooling because minor work made it suitable for general use and its general value matches its value as tooling, needed 4,000 of work to suit the contract. That 4,000 adaptation cost is allowable.

## Never confuse

Under the codified text, items disqualified because with relatively minor expense they can be made suitable for general purpose use have adaptation and return costs that are allowable [@CL-CMP-0867], and items which the contract schedule specifically excludes are allowable only as depreciation or amortization [@CL-CMP-0865]. The codified paragraph refers to items acquired by the contractor before the effective date of the contract (or replacement of such items) [@CL-CMP-1401], and the RFO paragraph refers to special tooling and special test equipment acquired or replaced by the contractor before the effective date of the contract [@CL-CMP-9633]. The RFO text makes the cost to adapt contractor-owned special tooling or special test equipment for use under a contract allowable [@CL-CMP-9635]. The general test for a charge is in [Allowability factors](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md).

## Company calibration
