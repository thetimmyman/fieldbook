## Purpose

Depreciation on plant, equipment, and other capital facilities is an allowable contract cost subject to limits, including a residual-value threshold, consistency with financial accounting, and bars on no-cost and fully depreciated property.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0464] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-11 [@CL-CMP-0464] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-11 [@CL-CMP-9399] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0464] |
| Applies to | Depreciation on a contractor's plant, equipment, and other capital facilities [@CL-CMP-0464] |

Depreciation on a contractor's plant, equipment, and other capital facilities is an allowable contract cost, subject to the limitations in this cost principle [@CL-CMP-0464]. For tangible personal property, only estimated residual values that exceed 10 percent of the capitalized cost need be used in establishing depreciable costs [@CL-CMP-0465]. Depreciation, rental, or use charges are unallowable on property acquired from the Government at no cost by the contractor or by any division, subsidiary, or affiliate of the contractor under common control [@CL-CMP-0466]. No depreciation or rental is allowed on property fully depreciated by the contractor or by any division, subsidiary, or affiliate of the contractor under common control [@CL-CMP-0467].

## How it works

Depreciation is allowable but must not exceed the amount used for financial accounting, and must be determined in a manner consistent with the segment's non-Government depreciation policies, when the CAS standard does not apply, except as indicated in paragraphs (g) and (h) of the cost principle. Depreciation is unallowable on property acquired from the Government at no cost and on fully depreciated property, though a reasonable charge for using fully depreciated property may be agreed upon.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.205-11 keeps the rule that depreciation on a contractor's plant, equipment, and other capital facilities is an allowable contract cost, subject to the limitations in the cost principle [@CL-CMP-9399], and the 10 percent residual value threshold for tangible personal property [@CL-CMP-9400]. It keeps the limit that allowable depreciation not exceed the amount used for financial accounting purposes and be consistent with the segment's non-Government policies for contracts not subject to 48 CFR 9904.409 [@CL-CMP-9401], with its pointer to paragraphs (g) and (h) [@CL-CMP-9402]. It keeps the unallowability of depreciation, rental, or use charges on property acquired from the Government at no cost [@CL-CMP-9403] and the bar on depreciation or rental for fully depreciated property [@CL-CMP-9404]. It points to 31.110(b)(2) for the reasonable charge for using fully depreciated property, where the codified text points to 31.109(h)(2) [@CL-CMP-9405; @CL-CMP-0467]. It addresses a finance lease under FASB ASC 842 where the codified text addresses a capital lease under FASB ASC 840 [@CL-CMP-9406; @CL-CMP-9407], and the RFO paragraph has no counterpart to the codified sentence that requires capital leases to be treated as purchased assets [@CL-CMP-9406; @CL-CMP-9411].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor buys a machine for 100,000 and estimates a residual value of 5,000. The residual value is below the residual-value threshold, so it does not need to be used in establishing depreciable cost, although the depreciation claimed remains subject to the cost principle's other limits, such as not exceeding the amount used for financial accounting purposes.

## Never confuse

Depreciation is not rental: a capital lease is treated as a purchased asset and depreciated, while an operating lease is rented use whose rates are tested for reasonableness. Depreciation for covered contracts must also stay consistent with the CAS depreciation standard when it applies; see [CAS applicability](cmp-cas-applicability.md). The codified text speaks of a capital lease under FASB ASC 840 [@CL-CMP-9407], while the RFO text speaks of a finance lease under FASB ASC 842 [@CL-CMP-9406]. The codified text goes on to say that FASB ASC 840 requires capital leases to be treated as purchased assets [@CL-CMP-9411], a sentence the RFO paragraph does not repeat [@CL-CMP-9406].

Gains and losses on the disposition or impairment of depreciable property are covered in [Gains and losses on disposition or impairment of depreciable property](cmp-depreciable-property-gains-losses.md).
