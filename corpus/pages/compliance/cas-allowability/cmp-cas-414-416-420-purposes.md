## Purpose

States what each of three Cost Accounting Standards says it is for, so a reader knows the intended target before working through the detailed techniques [@CL-CMP-8690; @CL-CMP-8692; @CL-CMP-8694].

CAS 414 states that its purpose is to establish criteria for the measurement and allocation of the cost of capital committed to facilities as an element of contract cost [@CL-CMP-8690]. It adds that consistent application of those criteria will improve cost measurement by providing for allocation of the cost of contractor investment in facilities capital to negotiated contracts [@CL-CMP-8691].

CAS 416 states that its purpose is to provide criteria for the measurement of insurance costs, the assignment of those costs to cost accounting periods, and their allocation to cost objectives [@CL-CMP-8692]. It adds that application of those criteria should increase the probability that insurance costs are allocated to cost objectives in a uniform and consistent manner [@CL-CMP-8693].

CAS 420 states that its purpose is to provide criteria for the accumulation of independent research and development costs and bid and proposal costs [@CL-CMP-8694], and for the allocation of those costs to cost objectives based on the beneficial or causal relationship between the costs and the cost objectives [@CL-CMP-8695]. It adds that consistent application of those criteria will improve cost allocation [@CL-CMP-8696].

## How it works

CAS 414 is about the cost of capital committed to facilities as an element of contract cost [@CL-CMP-8690], and consistent application of its criteria will improve cost measurement by providing for allocation of cost of contractor investment in facilities capital to negotiated contracts [@CL-CMP-8691]. CAS 416 provides criteria for the measurement of insurance costs, the assignment of such costs to cost accounting periods, and their allocation to cost objectives [@CL-CMP-8692], and the application of those criteria should increase the probability that insurance costs are allocated to cost objectives in a uniform and consistent manner [@CL-CMP-8693]. CAS 420 provides criteria for the accumulation of independent research and development costs and bid and proposal costs [@CL-CMP-8694] and for the allocation of such costs to cost objectives based on the beneficial or causal relationship between such costs and cost objectives [@CL-CMP-8695], and consistent application of those criteria will improve cost allocation [@CL-CMP-8696]. The detailed insurance rules are on [Accounting for insurance costs under the Cost Accounting Standards](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-416-insurance.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor to which these standards apply holds facilities capital, pays insurance premiums, and spends 400,000 on independent research and development and 100,000 on bid and proposal work. The standards state that CAS 414 sets criteria for measuring and allocating the cost of capital committed to facilities [@CL-CMP-8690], CAS 416 sets criteria for measuring insurance costs, assigning them to periods and allocating them to cost objectives [@CL-CMP-8692], and CAS 420 sets criteria for accumulating the 400,000 and the 100,000 and allocating them on a beneficial or causal relationship [@CL-CMP-8694; @CL-CMP-8695]. The purpose sections give no rate, base or dollar limit, so the example reaches no figure for any of the three costs.

## Never confuse

CAS 414 addresses the cost of capital committed to facilities [@CL-CMP-8690], CAS 416 addresses insurance costs [@CL-CMP-8692], and CAS 420 addresses independent research and development costs and bid and proposal costs [@CL-CMP-8694]. The detailed cards are [Facilities capital cost of money](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-414-facilities-capital-cost-of-money.md), [Insurance](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-416-insurance.md) and [IR&D and B&P allocation](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-420-ird-bp-allocation.md).
