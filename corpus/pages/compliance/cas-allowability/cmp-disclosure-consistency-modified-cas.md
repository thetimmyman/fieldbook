## Purpose

Explains what the Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause requires of the contractor, and how a change to a cost accounting practice is handled and paid for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-1600] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-3 [@CL-CMP-1600] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-3 [@CL-CMP-0290] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-1600] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-CMP-1600] |

The contractor must comply with the requirements of four cost accounting standards: 48 CFR 9904.401 (Consistency in Estimating, Accumulating, and Reporting Costs), 48 CFR 9904.402 (Consistency in Allocating Costs Incurred for the Same Purpose), 48 CFR 9904.405 (Accounting for Unallowable Costs), and 48 CFR 9904.406 [@CL-CMP-0951]. They apply as in effect on the date of award of the contract [@CL-CMP-0952].

The contractor must follow its cost accounting practices consistently, but either the Government or the contractor may propose a change, and the contractor agrees to negotiate the terms with the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-0955]. Once the terms are agreed, the change is applied prospectively and the Disclosure Statement is amended if it is affected [@CL-CMP-0956].

## How it works

On a contract covered by the cost accounting standards, a business unit of a company required to submit a Disclosure Statement must disclose its cost accounting practices in writing, as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5 [@CL-CMP-0953]. A Disclosure Statement that the contractor has told the contracting officer contains privileged and confidential trade secrets and commercial or financial information is protected and not released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-0954]. When the parties agree to a change and the contracting officer has made the finding required in 48 CFR 9903.201-6(c) that the change is desirable and not detrimental to the interests of the Government, the contractor negotiates an equitable adjustment [@CL-CMP-0957]. In the absence of the required finding, no agreement may be made under the clause that will increase costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-0958]. Where the contractor or a subcontractor fails to comply with applicable standards or to follow a practice and the failure increases costs paid by the United States, the contractor agrees to an adjustment that recovers the increase with interest, running from the time the payment by the United States was made to the time the adjustment is effected [@CL-CMP-0959; @CL-CMP-0960; @CL-CMP-8045]. A failure to agree whether the contractor has complied with an applicable CAS, rule, or regulation as specified in 48 CFR parts 9903 and 9904, and as to any cost adjustment demanded by the United States, constitutes a dispute under chapter 71 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-0961]. The contractor must also let authorized Government representatives examine and copy records on compliance [@CL-CMP-0962].

The contractor must pass the substance of the clause, except the dispute paragraph, to its negotiated subcontracts and require it in other subcontracts of any tier, subject to listed exceptions [@CL-CMP-0963]. The requirement reaches only negotiated subcontracts above the lower cost accounting standards threshold on the date of subcontract award [@CL-CMP-0965], and it does not reach negotiated subcontracts that are otherwise exempt [@CL-CMP-0966]. If a subcontract is awarded to a business unit that is subject to other types of CAS coverage under 48 CFR 9903.201-2, the substance of the applicable clause set forth in FAR section 30.201-4 must be inserted [@CL-CMP-0964].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the four standards, 48 CFR 9904.401, 9904.402, 9904.405 and 9904.406 [@CL-CMP-0291; @CL-CMP-0951], applying in the version in effect on the date of award of the contract, as indicated in 48 CFR part 9904 [@CL-CMP-0292; @CL-CMP-0952]. It keeps the consistent following of cost accounting practices, with either the Government or the contractor able to propose a change and the contractor agreeing to negotiate its terms with the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-0293; @CL-CMP-0955]. It keeps the prospective application of an agreed change, with the Disclosure Statement amended if it is affected [@CL-CMP-0294; @CL-CMP-0956], and the negotiation of an equitable adjustment when the contracting officer has made the finding required in 48 CFR 9903.201-6(c) that the change is desirable and not detrimental to the interests of the Government [@CL-CMP-0295; @CL-CMP-0957]. In the absence of the required finding, it keeps the bar on any agreement under the clause that will increase costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-0326; @CL-CMP-0958]. It keeps the adjustment of the contract price or cost allowance when the contractor or a subcontractor fails to comply with applicable standards or to follow any cost accounting practice and the failure results in increased costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-0296; @CL-CMP-0959]. For a subcontract awarded to a business unit under other types of coverage, the RFO text says the applicable clause is the one in FAR section 30.205, where the codified text says section 30.201-4 [@CL-CMP-0297; @CL-CMP-0964], and for the lower threshold it says FAR 30.205(b), where the codified text says FAR 30.201-4(b) [@CL-CMP-0298; @CL-CMP-0965].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit proposes a change to its cost accounting practice after award, and the price effect is 100,000. The contracting officer makes the required finding, so the parties agree an equitable adjustment of 100,000 that applies only to performance after the change.

## Never confuse

Following a practice consistently is not the same as freezing it: either party may propose a change, and the change then moves forward prospectively [@CL-CMP-0955; @CL-CMP-0956]. The disclosure duty is a separate obligation: it is marked for CAS-covered contracts only and applies to a business unit of a company required to submit a Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-0953]. The foreign-concern clause names fewer standards; see [Disclosure and consistency for foreign concerns](cmp-consistency-cost-accounting-practices.md). The Cost Accounting Standards clause has its own page, [Cost Accounting Standards clause](cmp-cost-accounting-standards.md).

For the subcontract clause and the lower threshold, the codified clause points to FAR 30.201-4 and 30.201-4(b) [@CL-CMP-0964; @CL-CMP-0965], while the RFO clause points to FAR 30.205 and 30.205(b) [@CL-CMP-0297; @CL-CMP-0298].
