## Purpose

Explains what the Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices clause requires of the contractor, and how a change to a cost accounting practice is handled and paid for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-1600] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-3 [@CL-CMP-1600] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-3 [@CL-CMP-0290] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-1600] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause [@CL-CMP-1600] |

The contractor, in connection with the contract, shall [@CL-CMP-8324] comply with the requirements of 48 CFR 9904.401 (Consistency in Estimating, Accumulating, and Reporting Costs), 48 CFR 9904.402 (Consistency in Allocating Costs Incurred for the Same Purpose), 48 CFR 9904.405 (Accounting for Unallowable Costs), and 48 CFR 9904.406 [@CL-CMP-0951], in effect on the date of award of the contract as indicated in 48 CFR part 9904 [@CL-CMP-0952].

The contractor shall follow its cost accounting practices consistently, but a change to such practices may be proposed by either the Government or the Contractor, and the Contractor agrees to negotiate with the Contracting Officer the terms and conditions under which a change may be made [@CL-CMP-0955]. After the terms and conditions under which the change is to be made have been agreed to, the change must be applied prospectively to the contract, and the Disclosure Statement, if affected, must be amended accordingly [@CL-CMP-0956].

## How it works

On a contract covered by the cost accounting standards, a business unit of a company required to submit a Disclosure Statement must disclose its cost accounting practices in writing, as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5 [@CL-CMP-0953]. If the contractor has notified the Contracting Officer that the Disclosure Statement contains trade secrets and commercial or financial information which is privileged and confidential, the Disclosure Statement shall be protected and shall not be released outside of the Government [@CL-CMP-0954]. The contractor shall, when the parties agree to a change to a cost accounting practice and the Contracting Officer has made the finding required in 48 CFR 9903.201-6(c) that the change is desirable and not detrimental to the interests of the Government, negotiate an equitable adjustment [@CL-CMP-0957] as provided in the Changes clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-8326]. In the absence of the required finding, no agreement may be made under the clause that will increase costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-0958]. The contractor agrees to an adjustment of the contract price or cost allowance, as appropriate, if the contractor or a subcontractor fails to comply with the applicable CAS or to follow any cost accounting practice and such failure results in any increased costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-0959]. That adjustment shall provide for recovery of the increased costs to the United States together with interest computed at the annual rate established under section 6621(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (section 6621(a)(2) of title 26 of the United States Code), from the time the payment by the United States was made to the time the adjustment is effected [@CL-CMP-0960; @CL-CMP-8045]. If the parties fail to agree whether the contractor has complied with an applicable CAS, rule, or regulation as specified in 48 CFR parts 9903 and 9904 and as to any cost adjustment demanded by the United States, such failure to agree will constitute a dispute under chapter 71 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-0961]. The contractor shall permit any authorized representatives of the Government to examine and make copies of any documents, papers, and records relating to compliance with the requirements of the clause [@CL-CMP-0962].

The contractor must pass the substance of the clause, except the dispute paragraph, to its negotiated subcontracts and require it in other subcontracts of any tier, subject to listed exceptions [@CL-CMP-0963]. The requirement applies only to negotiated subcontracts in excess of the lower CAS threshold specified in FAR 30.201-4(b) on the date of subcontract award [@CL-CMP-0965], and it shall not apply to negotiated subcontracts otherwise exempt from the requirement to include a CAS clause as specified in 48 CFR 9903.201-1 [@CL-CMP-0966]. If a subcontract is awarded to a business unit that is subject to other types of CAS coverage under 48 CFR 9903.201-2, the substance of the applicable clause set forth in FAR section 30.201-4 must be inserted [@CL-CMP-0964].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. Under paragraph (a) of the RFO clause, the contractor, in connection with the contract, shall [@CL-CMP-8325] comply with the requirements of 48 CFR 9904.401, 9904.402, 9904.405 and 9904.406 [@CL-CMP-0291], in effect on the date of award of the contract as indicated in 48 CFR part 9904 [@CL-CMP-0292]. The contractor shall follow its cost accounting practices consistently, a change to such practices may be proposed by either the Government or the Contractor, and the Contractor agrees to negotiate with the Contracting Officer the terms and conditions under which a change may be made [@CL-CMP-0293]. After the terms and conditions are agreed to, the change must be applied prospectively to the contract, and the Disclosure Statement, if affected, must be amended accordingly [@CL-CMP-0294]. The contractor shall, when the parties agree to a change and the Contracting Officer has made the finding required in 48 CFR 9903.201-6(c) that the change is desirable and not detrimental to the interests of the Government, negotiate an equitable adjustment [@CL-CMP-0295] as provided in the Changes clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-8327]. In the absence of the required finding, no agreement may be made under the clause that will increase costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-0326]. The contractor agrees to an adjustment of the contract price or cost allowance, as appropriate, if the contractor or a subcontractor fails to comply with the applicable CAS or to follow any cost accounting practice and such failure results in any increased costs paid by the United States [@CL-CMP-0296]. For a subcontract awarded to a business unit that is subject to other types of CAS coverage under 48 CFR 9903.201-2, the RFO clause says the substance of the applicable clause set forth in FAR section 30.205 shall be inserted [@CL-CMP-0297], where the codified text says section 30.201-4 [@CL-CMP-0964]. The RFO clause says the lower CAS threshold is specified in FAR 30.205(b) [@CL-CMP-0298], where the codified text says FAR 30.201-4(b) [@CL-CMP-0965].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit proposes a change to its cost accounting practice after award, and the price effect is 100,000. The contracting officer makes the required finding, so the parties agree an equitable adjustment of 100,000 that applies only to performance after the change.

## Never confuse

This modified-coverage clause differs from the full Cost Accounting Standards clause, which has its own page, [Cost Accounting Standards clause](cmp-cost-accounting-standards.md): the contractor shall follow its cost accounting practices consistently, and a change to such practices may be proposed by either the Government or the Contractor [@CL-CMP-0955]; after agreement the change must be applied prospectively to the contract [@CL-CMP-0956]. The disclosure paragraph is headed CAS-covered Contracts Only and applies if the contractor is a business unit of a company required to submit a Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-0953]. For the foreign-concern version of the clause, see [Disclosure and consistency for foreign concerns](cmp-consistency-cost-accounting-practices.md).

For the subcontract clause and the lower threshold, the codified clause points to FAR 30.201-4 and 30.201-4(b) [@CL-CMP-0964; @CL-CMP-0965], while the RFO clause points to FAR 30.205 and 30.205(b) [@CL-CMP-0297; @CL-CMP-0298].
