## Purpose

Cost Accounting Standard 411 addressed accounting for acquisition costs of material. This concept hub sets out the requirements and current status of the standard and links the detailed compliance, clause, and inventory pages across the corpus.

## The rule

The Cost Accounting Standards Board's final rule on CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 wholly rescinds CAS 408 and 411 and rescinds most provisions of CAS 404 and 409 [@CL-CMP-1500]. The final rule on CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 has an effective date of August 7, 2026 [@CL-CMP-1501]. The rule removes and reserves subpart 9904.411, which held the sections of CAS 411 [@CL-CMP-1507]. The printed codified text of 48 CFR part 9904 shows section 9904.411 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1524].

The Board does not expect the CAS 411 rescission to justify treating an accounting change a contractor subsequently makes as a required change under 9903.201-6(b)(2) [@CL-CMP-1511]. The Board states that no exemption from the price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 is warranted for the CAS 411 rescission [@CL-CMP-1514].

## How it works

Not established. The rule removes and reserves subpart 9904.411, which held the sections of CAS 411 [@CL-CMP-1507], and the printed codified text of 48 CFR part 9904 shows section 9904.411 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1524]. Under the final rule, CAS 411 is wholly rescinded [@CL-CMP-1500] with an effective date of August 7, 2026 [@CL-CMP-1501].

The Board does not expect the rescission to justify treating an accounting change a contractor subsequently makes as a required change under 9903.201-6(b)(2) [@CL-CMP-1511], and states that no exemption from the price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 is warranted for the CAS 411 rescission [@CL-CMP-1514].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's disclosed practice issues direct materials costing 1,000,000 across contracts using an established moving average inventory method. Following the rescission of CAS 411, it reviews whether to adopt a first-in, first-out method that would shift 50,000 between accounting periods. The rescission removes and reserves subpart 9904.411, so no codified CAS 411 text remains in force. The contractor does not treat the revision as a required change under 9903.201-6(b)(2), because the Board does not expect the rescission to justify that status, and no exemption from part 9903 price and cost adjustment rules applies. The contractor continues its existing disclosed practice for now, so no accounting change occurs. This example is illustrative only, and the contract itself governs.

## Never confuse

Although the Board wholly rescinded CAS 411 effective August 7, 2026, and removed and reserved subpart 9904.411 [@CL-CMP-1500; @CL-CMP-1501; @CL-CMP-1507; @CL-CMP-1524], the DFARS Material Management and Accounting System clause remains active for planning, controlling, and accounting for material [@CL-CLS-0109; @CL-CLS-0110]. Under DFARS 252.242-7004, a contractor's costing methodology may be standard or actual cost or any of the inventory costing methods in 48 CFR 9904.411-50(b), while maintaining consistency across all contract and customer types and accounting periods [@CL-CLS-0117]. In addition, the Board determined that no exemption from part 9903 price and cost adjustment requirements is warranted for the CAS 411 rescission [@CL-CMP-1514], and does not expect the rescission to justify treating an accounting change as a required change [@CL-CMP-1511].
