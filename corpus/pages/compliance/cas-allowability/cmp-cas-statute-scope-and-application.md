## Purpose

Shows what the Cost Accounting Standards statute requires of certain contractors and subcontractors, and how a contract that refers to FAR part 30 reaches the CAS and the other regulations of the CASB [@CL-CMP-7026; @CL-CMP-7027; @CL-CMP-7028].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7026] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.101 [@CL-CMP-7026]; 48 CFR 9903.101 [@CL-CMP-9677] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7026] |
| Applies to | Certain contractors and subcontractors [@CL-CMP-7026] |

The statute is title 41 of the United States Code, chapter 15, Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-7026]. It requires certain contractors and subcontractors to comply with the Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) and to disclose in writing and follow consistently their cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-7026]. The CAS Board text at 48 CFR 9903.101 names Public Law 100-679, codified at section 422 of title 41 of the United States Code, as requiring certain contractors and subcontractors to comply with Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) and to disclose in writing and follow consistently their cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-9677].

A contract that refers to FAR part 30 for the purpose of applying the policies, procedures, standards and regulations that the CASB promulgated under that statute is deemed to refer to the CAS and to any other regulations promulgated by the CASB [@CL-CMP-7027]. The same sentence states that the CAS and those other regulations, cross-referenced to 48 CFR chapter 99, are all hereby incorporated in FAR part 30 [@CL-CMP-7028].

## How it works

The statute reaches certain contractors and subcontractors, not every one [@CL-CMP-7026]. This section does not say which ones, and it prints no dollar threshold, exemption or coverage test. For those, see [CAS applicability, coverage and Disclosure Statement thresholds](cmp-cas-applicability.md).

The duty has two parts: compliance with the CAS, and written disclosure of cost accounting practices together with consistent following of them [@CL-CMP-7026]. Both parts are stated for the same certain contractors and subcontractors [@CL-CMP-7026].

The deemed-reference rule is conditional. It applies only to a contract that refers to FAR part 30 for the purpose of applying the CASB's policies, procedures, standards and regulations [@CL-CMP-7027]. The section names no official who decides, and no deadline or filing step.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company holds 2 contracts. The terms of both refer to FAR part 30 for the purpose of applying the CASB's policies, procedures, standards and regulations. Each contract is therefore deemed to refer to the CAS and to any other CASB regulations [@CL-CMP-7027]. Those regulations are incorporated in FAR part 30 [@CL-CMP-7028].

The example does not decide whether the company is one of the certain contractors that the statute covers, because the section does not say [@CL-CMP-7026]. If it is, the statute requires it to comply with the CAS and to disclose in writing and follow consistently its cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-7026].

## Never confuse

The statutory duty to disclose in writing and follow consistently is not a Disclosure Statement submission or an adequacy determination [@CL-CMP-7026]. A separate section bars the contracting officer from awarding a CAS-covered contract until the cognizant Federal agency official has made a written determination that a required Disclosure Statement is adequate, unless the agency head, on a nondelegable basis and in order to protect the Government's interest, authorizes award without obtaining submission of the required Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-7035; @CL-CMP-7036]. That section says the official issues the determinations of adequacy and compliance [@CL-CMP-7039]. See [Contracting officer, auditor and CFAO responsibilities for Disclosure Statements and CAS coverage](cmp-cas-disclosure-statement-responsibilities.md).

The deemed-reference rule is not a general rule for all contracts. It covers a contract that refers to FAR part 30 for the stated purpose [@CL-CMP-7027].
