## Purpose

Shows what the Cost Accounting Standards statute requires of certain contractors and subcontractors, and how a contract that refers to FAR part 30 reaches the CAS and the other regulations of the CASB [@CL-CMP-7026; @CL-CMP-7027; @CL-CMP-7028].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7026] |
| Codified citation | FAR 30.101 [@CL-CMP-7026]; 48 CFR 9903.101 [@CL-CMP-9677] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7026] |
| Applies to | Certain contractors and subcontractors [@CL-CMP-7026] |

The statute is title 41 of the United States Code, chapter 15, Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-7026]. It requires certain contractors and subcontractors to comply with the Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) and to disclose in writing and follow consistently their cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-7026]. The CAS Board text at 48 CFR 9903.101 [@CL-CMP-9677] names Public Law 100-679 (41 U.S.C. 422) [@CL-CMP-9677] as requiring certain contractors and subcontractors to comply with Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) and to disclose in writing and follow consistently their cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-9677].

A contract that refers to FAR part 30 for the purpose of applying the policies, procedures, standards and regulations that the CASB promulgated under that statute is deemed to refer to the CAS and to any other regulations promulgated by the CASB [@CL-CMP-7027]. The CAS and any other regulations promulgated by the CASB (see 48 CFR chapter 99) are all hereby incorporated in FAR part 30 [@CL-CMP-7028].

## How it works

The statute requires certain contractors and subcontractors to comply with the CAS and to disclose in writing and follow consistently their cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-7026]. For the coverage and Disclosure Statement thresholds, see [CAS applicability, coverage and Disclosure Statement thresholds](cmp-cas-applicability.md).

Contracts that refer to FAR part 30 for the purpose of applying the policies, procedures, standards and regulations promulgated by the CASB pursuant to chapter 15 of title 41 of the United States Code shall be deemed to refer to the CAS, and any other regulations promulgated by the CASB [@CL-CMP-7027].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company holds 2 contracts. The terms of both refer to FAR part 30 for the purpose of applying the CASB's policies, procedures, standards and regulations. Each contract is therefore deemed to refer to the CAS and to any other CASB regulations [@CL-CMP-7027]. Those regulations are incorporated in FAR part 30 [@CL-CMP-7028].

The example does not decide whether the company is one of the certain contractors that the statute covers, because the section does not say [@CL-CMP-7026]. If it is, the statute requires it to comply with the CAS and to disclose in writing and follow consistently its cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-7026].

## Never confuse

Under FAR 30.202-6(b), the contracting officer shall not award a CAS-covered contract until the cognizant Federal agency official (CFAO) has made a written determination that a required Disclosure Statement is adequate [@CL-CMP-7035], unless, in order to protect the Government's interest, the agency head, on a nondelegable basis, authorizes award without obtaining submission of the required Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-7036]. The CFAO is responsible for issuing determinations of adequacy and compliance of the Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-7039]. See [Contracting officer, auditor and CFAO responsibilities for Disclosure Statements and CAS coverage](cmp-cas-disclosure-statement-responsibilities.md).
