## Purpose

Explains the cost principle on goodwill: costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill are unallowable.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0507] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-49 [@CL-CMP-0507] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-49 [@CL-CMP-9412; @CL-CMP-9413; @CL-CMP-9414] |
| Edition date | Not established. |

Any costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill, however represented, are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0507].

Goodwill originates under the purchase method of accounting for a business combination when the price paid by the acquiring company exceeds the sum of the identifiable individual assets acquired less liabilities assumed, based upon their fair values [@CL-CMP-0508].

Goodwill may arise from the acquisition of a company as a whole or a portion of an entity [@CL-CMP-0509].

## How it works

Goodwill, an unidentifiable intangible asset, originates under the purchase method of accounting for a business combination when the price paid by the acquiring company exceeds the sum of the identifiable individual assets acquired less liabilities assumed, based upon their fair values [@CL-CMP-8375]. Costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill, however represented, are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0507].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-49(a), goodwill is an unidentifiable intangible asset that originates under the purchase method of accounting for a business combination when the price paid by the acquiring company exceeds the sum of the identifiable individual assets acquired less liabilities assumed, based upon their fair values [@CL-CMP-9413]. Paragraph (c) says goodwill may arise from the acquisition of a company as a whole or a portion thereof [@CL-CMP-9414]. Paragraph (d) says any costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill are unallowable [@CL-CMP-9412].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor acquires a business unit for 15,000,000, with net identifiable assets valued at fair value of 10,000,000, resulting in 5,000,000 of recorded goodwill. Under commercial financial accounting, the contractor records an annual goodwill impairment charge of 500,000. Under the cost principles, the entire 500,000 impairment charge is unallowable and cannot be allocated to government contracts.

## Never confuse

The unallowability follows the cost, not the label: any costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill (however represented) are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0507]. RFO FAR 31.205-49(d) says any costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill are unallowable [@CL-CMP-9412].
