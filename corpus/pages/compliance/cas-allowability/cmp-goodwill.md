## Purpose

Explains the absolute cost principle prohibition against charging goodwill amortization or write-downs to government contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0507] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-49 [@CL-CMP-0507] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0507] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to the FAR cost principles [@CL-CMP-0507] |

Any costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill, however represented, are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0507].

Goodwill originates under the purchase method of accounting for a business combination when the acquisition price exceeds the fair value of net identifiable assets acquired [@CL-CMP-0508].

Goodwill may arise from the acquisition of a company as a whole or a portion of an entity [@CL-CMP-0509].

## How it works

Goodwill is an unidentifiable intangible asset that represents the premium paid over the fair market value of net assets acquired in a business combination. Under the cost principles, the government does not pay for this acquisition premium through contract reimbursement. Contractors cannot amortize goodwill into indirect expense pools or write off goodwill impairments against contract revenues. Costs for amortization, expensing, write-off, or write-down of goodwill, however represented, are unallowable [@CL-CMP-0507].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor acquires a business unit for 15,000,000, with net identifiable assets valued at fair value of 10,000,000, resulting in 5,000,000 of recorded goodwill. Under commercial financial accounting, the contractor records an annual goodwill impairment charge of 500,000. Under the cost principles, the entire 500,000 impairment charge is unallowable and cannot be allocated to government contracts.

## Never confuse

Goodwill is an unidentifiable intangible asset, so it must not be confused with the identifiable individual assets whose fair values are used to measure it. Goodwill represents only the unidentifiable excess purchase price and is completely unallowable.
