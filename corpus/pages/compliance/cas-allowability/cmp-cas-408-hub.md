## Purpose

Cost Accounting Standard 408 addressed accounting for costs of compensated personal absence. This concept hub sets out the requirements and current status of the standard and links the detailed compliance, clause, and estimation pages across the corpus.

## The rule

The Cost Accounting Standards Board's final rule on CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 wholly rescinds CAS 408 and 411 and rescinds most provisions of CAS 404 and 409 [@CL-CMP-1500]. The final rule on CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 has an effective date of August 7, 2026 [@CL-CMP-1501].

The rule removes and reserves subparts 9904.408 and 9904.409, which held the sections of CAS 408 and CAS 409 [@CL-CMP-1506]. The printed codified text of 48 CFR part 9904 shows sections 9904.407 through 9904.409 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1523].

## How it works

Not established. The rule removes and reserves subparts 9904.408 and 9904.409, which held the sections of CAS 408 and CAS 409 [@CL-CMP-1506], and the printed codified text shows sections 9904.407 through 9904.409 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1523].

Under the final rule, CAS 408 is wholly rescinded [@CL-CMP-1500] with an effective date of August 7, 2026 [@CL-CMP-1501].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's disclosed practice accrues vacation and sick leave costs of 400,000 across its fiscal year and allocates them to contract cost objectives. After the rescission of CAS 408, it reviews whether to change that treatment. The rescission removes and reserves the subpart, so no codified CAS 408 text remains to read. The contractor continues its disclosed practice for now, so no change in practice occurs. If it later changes the practice, it checks whether the change is directly associated with conformance of CAS 408 to GAAP, which the part 9903 adjustment requirements do not reach, and whether it is made and disclosed in the fiscal year directly following the effective date of the rule. This example is illustrative only, and the contract itself governs.

## Never confuse

The Board's final rule wholly rescinds CAS 408 and CAS 411, whereas for CAS 409 the Board rescinded most provisions but retained the requirements of CAS 409-50(e)(5), 409-50(j)(1) and 409-50(j)(4) [@CL-CMP-1500; @CL-CMP-1503]. Although the rule removes and reserves subparts 9904.408, 9904.409 and 9904.411 [@CL-CMP-1506; @CL-CMP-1507], with codified text showing sections 9904.407 through 9904.409 and 9904.411 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1523; @CL-CMP-1524], the Board stated that no exemption from the price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 is warranted for the CAS 409 or CAS 411 rescissions [@CL-CMP-1513; @CL-CMP-1514] and does not expect those rescissions to justify treating an accounting change as a required change [@CL-CMP-1510; @CL-CMP-1511]. In contrast, the contract price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 do not apply to changes directly associated with the conformance of CAS 408 to GAAP [@CL-CMP-1508], which must be disclosed and made during the contractor's fiscal year directly following the effective date of the final rule rescinding CAS 408 [@CL-CMP-1528].
