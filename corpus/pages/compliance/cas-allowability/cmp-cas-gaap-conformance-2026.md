## Question

What did the 2026 conformance rules change for CAS 404, 407, 408, 409 and 411, and what should a contractor's finance team check? [@CL-CMP-1500; @CL-CMP-1516]

## Recommendation

Treat CAS 404, 407, 408, 409 and 411 as removed from the codified standards [@CL-CMP-1522; @CL-CMP-1523; @CL-CMP-1524]. Follow the retained requirements where they now sit, in the [CAS 405 page](cmp-cas-405-unallowable-costs.md) [@CL-CMP-1525] and the [CAS 418 page](cmp-cas-418-direct-indirect.md) [@CL-CMP-1526]. Check each business unit's Disclosure Statement and written practices against them, and keep to disclosed practices [@CL-CMP-1515].

## Evidence

- CAS 408 and 411 are wholly rescinded, and most of CAS 404 and 409 is rescinded [@CL-CMP-1500]. CAS 407 is rescinded and one definition moves [@CL-CMP-1516].
- What remains of CAS 404 and 409 sits in one new paragraph of 9904.405-40 [@CL-CMP-1502; @CL-CMP-1503; @CL-CMP-1504].
- The rule removes and reserves subpart 9904.404 [@CL-CMP-1505], subparts 9904.408 and 9904.409 [@CL-CMP-1506], and subpart 9904.411 [@CL-CMP-1507]; those subparts held the sections of CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 [@CL-CMP-1505; @CL-CMP-1506; @CL-CMP-1507].
- What remains of CAS 407 moves to CAS 418 [@CL-CMP-1518; @CL-CMP-1521].
- Effective dates: August 7, 2026 for CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 [@CL-CMP-1501]; October 1, 2026 for CAS 407 [@CL-CMP-1517].
- CAS 408: price and cost adjustment requirements do not apply to conformance changes [@CL-CMP-1508], which must be made in the next fiscal year [@CL-CMP-1528].
- CAS 404, 409 and 411: no exemption from adjustment requirements [@CL-CMP-1512; @CL-CMP-1513; @CL-CMP-1514], and no change is expected to be a required change [@CL-CMP-1509; @CL-CMP-1510; @CL-CMP-1511].
- CAS 407: the Board states that any current or future change to standard costing of direct material or labor, or to the treatment of variances, is considered unilateral [@CL-CMP-1519]. A FIFO to LIFO change needs disclosure [@CL-CMP-1520].

## Options

Leaving practices alone fits the Board's finding for CAS 404, 409 and 411 [@CL-CMP-1509; @CL-CMP-1510; @CL-CMP-1511]. A review against the moved text is still wise, because it binds [@CL-CMP-1525].

## Unknowns

- How the rules apply to contracts awarded before the effective dates. Not established.
- Which business units are covered, and which have a Disclosure Statement on file [@Q-033].
- Whether Disclosure Statement text must change for the moved content. Not established.
