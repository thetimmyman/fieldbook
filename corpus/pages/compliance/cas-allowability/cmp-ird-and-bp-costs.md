## Purpose

Governs the allowability of independent research and development and bid and proposal costs, and establishes DoD reporting thresholds for major contractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CMP-0112; @CL-CMP-0113] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-18 and DFARS 231.205-18 [@CL-CMP-0112; @CL-CMP-0113] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0112] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the cost principles clause [@CL-CMP-0112] |

Except as provided in paragraphs (d) and (e) of FAR 31.205-18 or in agency regulations, costs for independent research and development and bid and proposal efforts are allowable as indirect expenses on contracts to the extent that they are allocable and reasonable [@CL-CMP-0112]. Under defense rules, a major contractor is any contractor whose covered segments allocated a total of more than $11 million in these costs to covered contracts in the preceding fiscal year [@CL-CMP-0113]. A covered segment is a product division of the contractor that allocated more than $1,100,000 in IR&D and B&P costs to covered contracts during the preceding fiscal year, or the contractor as a whole if it has no product divisions [@CL-CMP-0114].

## How it works

These efforts cannot be sponsored by a grant or required in contract performance. On contracts that are not CAS-covered or have modified CAS coverage, IR&D and B&P costs are allocated to final cost objectives on the same basis as the G&A expense grouping of the profit center where they are incurred (through other profit centers' or corporate G&A when they clearly benefit them), and the contracting officer may approve a different base if that allocation is not equitable; fully CAS-covered contracts follow the cost accounting standard for independent research and development and bid and proposal costs. For a major contractor's IR&D costs incurred on covered contracts to be allowable, the contractor must report the IR&D projects generating those costs to the Defense Technical Information Center through its online input form and update its inputs at least annually and when the project is completed. The IR&D costs allowable under DoD contracts may not exceed the lesser of the contracts' allocable share of total incurred IR&D costs or the total incurred IR&D costs that the contractor's chief executive officer has determined will advance DoD's needs for future technology and advanced capability.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A defense contractor has two product divisions. Division Alpha allocates 8 million in research and proposal costs to covered defense contracts, while Division Beta allocates 5 million. Both divisions exceed the 1,100,000 segment threshold, and their combined 13 million exceeds the 11 million major contractor threshold. Because the firm is a major contractor, for its IR&D costs on covered contracts to be allowable it must report the IR&D projects generating those costs to the defense database and update its inputs at least annually.

## Never confuse

Independent research is initiated by the contractor; contracted research is performed under a contract requirement. Bid and proposal costs prepare offers on potential contracts, whereas contract performance costs execute awarded work. Screening and indirect rate treatment are reviewed during the rate settlement process; see [Final indirect cost rate proposal](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md) and [Unallowable-cost penalties](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md).
