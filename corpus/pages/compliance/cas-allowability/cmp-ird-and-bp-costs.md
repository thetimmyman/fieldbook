## Purpose

Governs the allowability of independent research and development and bid and proposal costs, and gives the DoD thresholds that define a major contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CMP-0112; @CL-CMP-0113] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-18 and DFARS 231.205-18 [@CL-CMP-0112; @CL-CMP-0113] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-18 [@CL-CMP-9739; @CL-CMP-9740; @CL-CMP-9741; @CL-CMP-9742; @CL-CMP-9743; @CL-CMP-9744; @CL-CMP-9745; @CL-CMP-9746; @CL-CMP-9747; @CL-CMP-9748] |
| Edition date | Not established. |

Costs for independent research and development and bid and proposal efforts are allowable as indirect expenses on contracts to the extent that those costs are allocable and reasonable, except as provided in paragraphs (d) and (e) of FAR 31.205-18 or as provided in agency regulations [@CL-CMP-0112]. Under DFARS 231.205-18(a), a major contractor is any contractor whose covered segments allocated a total of more than $11 million in these costs to covered contracts in the preceding fiscal year [@CL-CMP-0113]. A covered segment is a product division of the contractor that allocated more than $1,100,000 in IR&D and B&P costs to covered contracts during the preceding fiscal year [@CL-CMP-0114]. For a contractor with no product divisions, the term means the contractor as a whole [@CL-CMP-1402].

## How it works

Under DFARS 231.205-18(c)(iii), for IR&D costs that major contractors incurred on covered contracts to be allowable, the contractor is required to report the projects generating those costs to the Defense Technical Information Center (DTIC) [@CL-CMP-1403] using the DTIC's online input form and instructions [@CL-CMP-8328], and is required to update its DTIC inputs at least annually, no later than 3 months after the end of the contractor's fiscal year, and when the project is completed [@CL-CMP-0883]. The amount of IR&D costs allowable under DoD contracts shall not exceed the lesser of [@CL-CMP-0884] such contracts' allocable share of total incurred IR&D costs [@CL-CMP-8329] or the total amount of incurred IR&D costs that the chief executive officer of the contractor has determined will advance the needs of DoD for future technology and advanced capability as DoD describes such needs in communications referenced at 242.771-3(c)(1)(i) [@CL-CMP-0885]. For allocation of these costs under the cost accounting standard, see [the allocation standard for these costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-420-ird-bp-allocation.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the part 31 text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Paragraph (c) of RFO FAR 31.205-18 says that, except as provided in paragraphs (d) and (e) of that section, costs for IR&D and B&P are allowable as indirect expenses on contracts if those costs are allocable and reasonable [@CL-CMP-9739]. Under paragraph (a), the IR&D term does not include the costs of effort sponsored by a grant or required in the performance of a contract [@CL-CMP-9740], and the B&P term does not include the costs of effort sponsored by a grant or cooperative agreement or required in the performance of a contract [@CL-CMP-9741]. Under paragraph (b)(1), fully-CAS-covered contracts must be subject to all requirements of 48 CFR 9904.420 [@CL-CMP-9742]. Under paragraph (b)(2)(i), non-CAS-covered contracts or contracts that contain terms or conditions requiring modified CAS coverage must be subject to all requirements of 48 CFR 9904.420 except 48 CFR 9904.420-50(e)(2) and 48 CFR 9904.420-50(f)(2) [@CL-CMP-9743]. Under paragraph (b)(2)(ii), non-CAS-covered contracts or modified CAS-covered contracts awarded when the contractor has CAS-covered contracts requiring compliance with 48 CFR 9904.420 must be subject to all the requirements of 48 CFR 9904.420 [@CL-CMP-9748]. When the requirements of 48 CFR 9904.420-50(e)(2) and 48 CFR 9904.420-50(f)(2) are not applicable, the following apply [@CL-CMP-9747]. IR&D and B&P costs must be allocated to final cost objectives on the same basis of allocation used for the general and administrative (G&A) expense grouping of the profit center (see 31.002) in which the costs are incurred (paragraph (b)(2)(iii)(A)(1)) [@CL-CMP-9744]. When IR&D and B&P costs clearly benefit other profit centers or benefit the entire company, those costs must be allocated through the G&A of the other profit centers or through the corporate G&A, as appropriate (paragraph (b)(2)(iii)(A)(2)) [@CL-CMP-9745]. If allocations of IR&D or B&P through the G&A base do not provide equitable cost allocation, the contracting officer may approve use of a different base (paragraph (b)(2)(iii)(B)) [@CL-CMP-9746].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A defense contractor has two product divisions. Division Alpha allocates 8 million in research and proposal costs to covered defense contracts, while Division Beta allocates 5 million. Both divisions exceed the 1,100,000 segment threshold, and their combined 13 million exceeds the 11 million major contractor threshold. Because the firm is a major contractor, for its IR&D costs on covered contracts to be allowable it must report the IR&D projects generating those costs to the Defense Technical Information Center and update its inputs at least annually, no later than 3 months after the end of its fiscal year, and when the project is completed [@CL-CMP-1403; @CL-CMP-0883].

## Never confuse

For indirect rate proposals and penalties on unallowable costs, see [Final indirect cost rate proposal](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md) and [Unallowable-cost penalties](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md).

Codified FAR 31.205-18(c) says that, except as provided in paragraphs (d) and (e) of that subsection, or as provided in agency regulations, costs for IR&D and B&P are allowable as indirect expenses on contracts to the extent that those costs are allocable and reasonable [@CL-CMP-0112]. RFO FAR 31.205-18(c) says that, except as provided in paragraphs (d) and (e) of that section, costs for IR&D and B&P are allowable as indirect expenses on contracts if those costs are allocable and reasonable [@CL-CMP-9739].
