## Purpose

Identifies allowable types of recruitment expenses and the specific restrictions that make help-wanted advertising unallowable.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0803] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-34 [@CL-CMP-0803] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0803] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Subject to the limits on help-wanted advertising, the costs listed in the principle are allowable [@CL-CMP-1120]. They include costs of help-wanted advertising and of operating an employment office needed to secure and maintain an adequate labor force [@CL-CMP-0803], costs of operating an aptitude and educational testing program, travel costs of employees engaged in recruiting personnel and travel costs of applicants for interviews [@CL-CMP-1121], and costs for employment agencies not in excess of standard commercial rates [@CL-CMP-0804].

Help-wanted advertising costs are unallowable if the advertising does not describe specific positions or classes of positions, or includes material that is not relevant for recruitment purposes, such as extensive illustrations or descriptions of the company's products or capabilities [@CL-CMP-0805].

## How it works

The cost principle lists help-wanted advertising, and operating an employment office needed to secure and maintain an adequate labor force, among the allowable recruitment costs [@CL-CMP-0803]. It also lists a testing program, recruiter travel, applicant travel for interviews, and employment agency costs within standard commercial rates [@CL-CMP-1121; @CL-CMP-0804]. However, help-wanted advertising is unallowable if it does not describe specific positions or classes of positions, or if it includes material that is not relevant for recruitment purposes [@CL-CMP-0805].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor incurs 30,000 for recruitment advertising during the fiscal year. Of this total, 20,000 is spent on job advertisements listing specific engineering vacancies and qualification requirements, while 10,000 is spent on promotional materials showcasing general company capabilities without identifying open positions. The 20,000 for specific job postings is allowable, whereas the 10,000 for general promotional capability advertisements is unallowable.

## Never confuse

Allowable recruitment costs are the items listed in the section, such as help-wanted advertising, an employment office and agency costs within standard commercial rates [@CL-CMP-0803; @CL-CMP-0804], whereas help-wanted advertising that does not describe specific positions or classes of positions is unallowable [@CL-CMP-0805]. Travel costs of applicants for interviews and of employees engaged in recruiting personnel are listed as allowable recruitment costs [@CL-CMP-1121]; see [Travel costs](cmp-travel-costs.md) and [Allowability factors](cmp-allowability-factors.md).
