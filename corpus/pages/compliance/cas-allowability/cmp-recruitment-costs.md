## Purpose

Identifies allowable types of recruitment expenses and the specific restrictions that make help-wanted advertising unallowable.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0803] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-34 [@CL-CMP-0803] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-34 [@CL-CMP-9600; @CL-CMP-9601; @CL-CMP-9602; @CL-CMP-9603] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0803] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Subject to paragraph (b), the following costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-1120]: (1) costs of help-wanted advertising and (2) costs of operating an employment office needed to secure and maintain an adequate labor force [@CL-CMP-0803]; (3) costs of operating an aptitude and educational testing program, (4) travel costs of employees engaged in recruiting personnel, and (5) travel costs of applicants for interviews [@CL-CMP-1121]; and (6) costs for employment agencies, not in excess of standard commercial rates [@CL-CMP-0804].

Under paragraph (b), help-wanted advertising costs are unallowable if the advertising (1) does not describe specific positions or classes of positions [@CL-CMP-0805]; or (2) includes material that is not relevant for recruitment purposes, such as extensive illustrations or descriptions of the company's products or capabilities [@CL-CMP-0805].

## How it works

Paragraph (a) lists the allowable costs, subject to paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-1120], and paragraph (b) makes help-wanted advertising costs unallowable in the cases it sets out [@CL-CMP-0805].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.205-34(a) provides that the following costs are allowable: (1) costs for employment agencies, not in excess of standard commercial rates [@CL-CMP-9600]; (2) costs of operating an employment office needed to secure and maintain an adequate labor force and (3) costs of operating an aptitude and educational testing program [@CL-CMP-9601]; and (4) travel costs of employees engaged in recruiting personnel, (5) travel costs of applicants for interviews, and (6) costs of help-wanted advertising [@CL-CMP-9602]. Under RFO paragraph (b), help-wanted advertising costs are unallowable if the advertising (1) does not describe specific positions or classes of positions [@CL-CMP-9603]; or (2) includes material that is not relevant for recruitment purposes, such as extensive illustrations or descriptions of the company's products or capabilities [@CL-CMP-9603].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor incurs 30,000 for recruitment advertising during the fiscal year. Of this total, 20,000 is spent on job advertisements listing specific engineering vacancies and qualification requirements, while 10,000 is spent on promotional materials showcasing general company capabilities without identifying open positions. The 20,000 for specific job postings is allowable, whereas the 10,000 for general promotional capability advertisements is unallowable.

## Never confuse

The codified list of allowable costs opens with the words subject to paragraph (b) of this subsection [@CL-CMP-1120], and the RFO list opens with the words the following costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-9600]. Employment agency costs are item (a)(6) in the codified list [@CL-CMP-0804] and item (a)(1) in the RFO list [@CL-CMP-9600]. For travel, see [Travel costs](cmp-travel-costs.md), and for the factors that determine allowability, see [Allowability factors](cmp-allowability-factors.md).
