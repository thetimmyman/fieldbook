## Purpose

Explains when costs incurred prior to contract award may be recognized as allowable contract costs.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0801] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-32 [@CL-CMP-0801] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-32 [@CL-CMP-9483; @CL-CMP-9484] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0801] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Precontract costs are costs incurred before the effective date of the contract directly pursuant to negotiations and in anticipation of award when necessary to comply with the proposed delivery schedule [@CL-CMP-0801]. Such costs are allowable to the extent that they would have been allowable if incurred after the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-0802].

## How it works

A cost is a precontract cost when it is incurred before the effective date of the contract, directly pursuant to the negotiation and in anticipation of the contract award, when that incurrence is necessary to comply with the proposed contract delivery schedule [@CL-CMP-0801]. These costs are allowable to the extent that they would have been allowable if incurred after the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-0802].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-32, precontract costs are costs incurred before the effective date of the contract directly pursuant to the negotiation and in anticipation of the contract award when such incurrence is necessary to comply with the proposed contract delivery schedule [@CL-CMP-9483]. These costs are allowable to the extent that they would have been allowable if incurred after the date of the contract (see 31.110) [@CL-CMP-9484]. In the RFO text, 31.110 is titled Advance agreements [@CL-CMP-9485].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor incurs 50,000 in material and labor costs directly pursuant to negotiations and in anticipation of an upcoming award to meet a tight delivery schedule. Had the 50,000 been incurred after the date of the contract, it would have been allowable. The entire 50,000 is therefore allowable as a precontract cost under the contract.

## Never confuse

Precontract costs are costs incurred before the effective date of the contract, directly pursuant to the negotiation and in anticipation of the award, when necessary to meet the proposed delivery schedule; they are allowable to the extent they would have been allowable if incurred after the contract date [@CL-CMP-0801; @CL-CMP-0802]. They must be incurred directly pursuant to the negotiation and in anticipation of the contract award [@CL-CMP-0801]; see [Advance agreements](cmp-advance-agreements.md) and [Allowability factors](cmp-allowability-factors.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company's precontract costs were incurred directly pursuant to the negotiation and in anticipation of the award, and whether they would have been allowable after the contract date. [@Q-043]
