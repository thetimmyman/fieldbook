## Purpose

Shows which contracts expose a contractor to penalties when unallowable indirect costs are left in a final indirect cost rate proposal.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.709-1(b) [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.508-1(b) [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| Applies to | Contracts over $1 million, except the excluded fixed-price types [@CL-CMP-0005] |

The penalty applies to all contracts over $1 million, except fixed-price contracts without cost incentives and firm-fixed-price contracts for commercial products or services [@CL-CMP-0005].

## How it works

The rule covers indirect costs in two places: the final indirect cost rate proposal, and the final statement of costs under a fixed-price incentive contract. An expressly unallowable cost left in the proposal draws a penalty equal to the cost allocated to covered contracts, plus interest on the paid part of the disallowed cost. A cost the government had already determined unallowable for the contractor draws a larger penalty. The contracting officer decides whether to assess the penalty, and the auditor only recommends; the contracting officer must waive it when one of the listed waiver conditions is met.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A final rate proposal includes 50,000 of an expressly unallowable cost in the overhead pool. Covered contracts absorb 40 percent of that pool, so 20,000 is allocated to them. The penalty equals the 20,000 allocated, and the 20,000 itself is disallowed.

## Never confuse

An unallowable cost is any cost that may not be charged; an expressly unallowable cost is one that a cost principle in the FAR or an agency supplement names as unallowable. Costs already determined unallowable for the contractor are penalised too. The contractor screens costs before it files the incurred cost submission; see [Final indirect cost rate proposal](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).
