## Purpose

Covers the penalties on unallowable indirect costs and the contracts to which the penalty section applies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.709-1(b) [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.508-1(a) [@CL-CMP-8951; @CL-CMP-8952]; RFO FAR 42.508-1(b) [@CL-CMP-0005; @CL-CMP-8953] |

Under FAR 42.709-1(b), this section applies to all contracts in excess of $1 million, except fixed-price contracts without cost incentives or any firm-fixed-price contracts for the purchase of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-0005].

## How it works

Under FAR 42.709-1(a) [@CL-CMP-8494], the section covers the assessment of penalties against contractors which include unallowable indirect costs in (1) final indirect cost rate proposals; or (2) the final statement of costs incurred or estimated to be incurred under a fixed-price incentive contract [@CL-CMP-8494]. Under FAR 42.709-2(a), the following penalties apply to contracts covered by the section: (1) if the indirect cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR, or an executive agency supplement to the FAR, that defines the allowability of specific selected costs [@CL-CMP-8495], the penalty is equal to (i) the amount of the disallowed costs allocated to contracts that are subject to this section for which an indirect cost proposal has been submitted; plus (ii) interest on the paid portion, if any, of the disallowance [@CL-CMP-1859]; and (2) if the indirect cost was determined to be unallowable for that contractor before proposal submission, the penalty is two times the amount in paragraph (a)(1)(i) [@CL-CMP-1860]. Under FAR 42.709-3(a), the cognizant contracting officer is responsible for determining whether the penalties in 42.709-2(a) should be assessed and whether such penalties should be waived pursuant to 42.709-6 [@CL-CMP-1726]. In the review and/or the determination of final indirect cost proposals for contracts subject to the section, the contract auditor is responsible for recommending to the contracting officer which costs may be unallowable and subject to the penalties in 42.709-2(a) [@CL-CMP-1727]. For the amounts, interest and waiver in detail, see [Indirect rate certification and penalty assessment](cmp-indirect-rate-certification-and-penalty-assessment.md).

Under the RFO text, contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 42 in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9759], effective March 16, 2026 under Class Deviation 2026-O0050 [@CL-CMP-9761]. Under RFO FAR 42.508-1(a), this section implements section 3743 of title 10 of the United States Code and section 4303 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-8951], and it covers the assessment of penalties against contractors which include unallowable indirect costs in (1) final indirect cost rate proposals; or (2) the final statement of costs incurred or estimated to be incurred under a fixed-price incentive contract [@CL-CMP-8952]. Under RFO FAR 42.508-1(b), this section applies to all contracts in excess of $1 million, except fixed-price contracts without cost incentives or any firm-fixed-price contracts for the purchase of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-8953].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A final rate proposal includes 50,000 of an expressly unallowable cost in the overhead pool. Covered contracts absorb 40 percent of that pool, so 20,000 is allocated to them. The penalty equals the 20,000 allocated, plus interest on the paid portion, if any, of the disallowance, and the 20,000 itself is disallowed.

## Never confuse

The penalty applies where the indirect cost is expressly unallowable under a cost principle in the FAR, or an executive agency supplement to the FAR, that defines the allowability of specific selected costs [@CL-CMP-8495], or was determined to be unallowable for that contractor before proposal submission [@CL-CMP-1860]. For the final indirect cost rate proposal, see [Final indirect cost rate proposal](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).
