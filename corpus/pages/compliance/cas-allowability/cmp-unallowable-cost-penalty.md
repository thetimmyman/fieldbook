## Purpose

Shows which contracts expose a contractor to penalties when unallowable indirect costs are left in a final indirect cost rate proposal.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.709-1(b) [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.508-1(a) [@CL-CMP-8951; @CL-CMP-8952]; RFO FAR 42.508-1(b) [@CL-CMP-0005; @CL-CMP-8953] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0005] |
| Applies to | Contracts over $1 million, except the excluded fixed-price types [@CL-CMP-0005] |

The penalty section applies to all contracts in excess of $1 million, except fixed-price contracts without cost incentives or any firm-fixed-price contracts for the purchase of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-0005].

## How it works

The rule covers indirect costs in two places: the final indirect cost rate proposal, and the final statement of costs under a fixed-price incentive contract. An expressly unallowable cost left in the proposal draws a penalty equal to the cost allocated to covered contracts, plus interest on the paid part of the disallowed cost. A cost that was determined to be unallowable for that contractor before proposal submission draws a penalty of two times the amount in paragraph (a)(1)(i) of the section [@CL-CMP-1860], which is the amount of the disallowed costs allocated to contracts subject to the section for which an indirect cost proposal has been submitted. The penalty assessment card covers the amounts, interest and waiver in detail. The contracting officer decides whether to assess the penalty, and the auditor only recommends; the contracting officer must waive it when one of the listed waiver conditions is met.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.508-1(a) says the section implements section 3743 of title 10 of the United States Code and section 4303 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-8951] and covers the assessment of penalties against contractors which include unallowable indirect costs in (1) final indirect cost rate proposals or (2) the final statement of costs incurred or estimated to be incurred under a fixed-price incentive contract [@CL-CMP-8952]. RFO FAR 42.508-1(b) says the section applies to all contracts in excess of $1 million, except fixed-price contracts without cost incentives or any firm-fixed-price contracts for the purchase of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-8953].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A final rate proposal includes 50,000 of an expressly unallowable cost in the overhead pool. Covered contracts absorb 40 percent of that pool, so 20,000 is allocated to them. The penalty equals the 20,000 allocated, and the 20,000 itself is disallowed.

## Never confuse

An unallowable cost is any cost that may not be charged; an expressly unallowable cost is one that a cost principle in the FAR or an agency supplement names as unallowable. Costs already determined unallowable for the contractor are penalised too. The contractor screens costs before it files the incurred cost submission; see [Final indirect cost rate proposal](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-proposal.md).
