## Purpose

Explains the disclosure, consistency, practice-change, and confidentiality requirements in the Cost Accounting Standards clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-2 [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| Applies to | CAS-covered contracts unless the stated exemptions apply [@CL-CMP-0606; @CL-CMP-0607] |

Unless an exemption applies, the clause incorporates the CAS contract requirements by reference [@CL-CMP-0606]. A CAS-covered contractor discloses its practices, follows them consistently, and applies covered practice changes prospectively while amending the Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-0607; @CL-CMP-0608; @CL-CMP-0609].

## How it works

The Disclosure Statement describes how direct and indirect costs are distinguished and the basis used to allocate indirect costs [@CL-CMP-0607]. When the contractor has notified the Contracting Officer that the Disclosure Statement contains privileged and confidential trade secrets and commercial or financial information, the statement is protected from release outside the Government [@CL-CMP-0610].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor changes the practice used to allocate indirect costs for a CAS-covered contract. It applies the change prospectively and amends the Disclosure Statement. Those actions follow the clause's change requirement.

## Never confuse

Disclosure is not the same as consistent practice. The Disclosure Statement describes practices, while the consistency requirement governs accumulation and reporting of contract performance cost data [@CL-CMP-0607; @CL-CMP-0608].

## Company calibration

Whether each business unit has full CAS coverage, modified coverage, or neither, and whether a Disclosure Statement is on file. [@Q-033]
