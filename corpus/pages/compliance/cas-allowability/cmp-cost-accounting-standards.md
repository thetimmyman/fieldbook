## Purpose

Explains the disclosure, consistency, practice-change, and confidentiality requirements in the Cost Accounting Standards clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-2 [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-2 [@CL-CMP-0284] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| Applies to | Contracts that are not exempt under 48 CFR 9903.201-1 and 9903.201-2 [@CL-CMP-0606] |

Unless the contract is exempt under 48 CFR 9903.201-1 and 9903.201-2, the clause incorporates the provisions of 48 CFR part 9903 by reference [@CL-CMP-0606]. Under paragraph (a)(1) the contractor discloses its cost accounting practices in writing, by submission of a Disclosure Statement, as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5 [@CL-CMP-0607]; paragraph (a)(1) of the RFO clause is headed "CAS-covered Contracts Only" [@CL-CMP-0325]. Under paragraph (a)(2) the contractor follows its cost accounting practices consistently in accumulating and reporting contract performance cost data concerning the contract [@CL-CMP-0608]. If any change in cost accounting practices is made for the purposes of any contract or subcontract subject to CAS requirements, the change must be applied prospectively to the contract and the Disclosure Statement must be amended accordingly [@CL-CMP-0609]. The Disclosure Statement discloses the contractor's cost accounting practices as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5 [@CL-CMP-0607].

## How it works

The practices disclosed in the Disclosure Statement include methods of distinguishing direct costs from indirect costs and the basis used for allocating indirect costs [@CL-CMP-0607]. If the contractor has notified the Contracting Officer that the Disclosure Statement contains trade secrets and commercial or financial information that is privileged and confidential, the Disclosure Statement must be protected and must not be released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-0610].

Under the DoD class deviation, contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. Under the RFO clause, unless the contract is exempt under 48 CFR 9903.201-1 and 9903.201-2, the provisions of 48 CFR part 9903 are incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-0285]. Paragraph (a)(1) of the RFO clause is headed "CAS-covered Contracts Only" [@CL-CMP-0325]. Under that paragraph the contractor discloses its cost accounting practices in writing by submission of a Disclosure Statement as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5, including methods of distinguishing direct costs from indirect costs and the basis used for allocating indirect costs [@CL-CMP-0286]. If the contractor has notified the Contracting Officer that the Disclosure Statement contains trade secrets and commercial or financial information that is privileged and confidential, the Disclosure Statement must be protected and must not be released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-0287]. Under paragraph (a)(2) of the RFO clause the contractor follows its cost accounting practices consistently in accumulating and reporting contract performance cost data concerning the contract [@CL-CMP-0288]. If any change in cost accounting practices is made for the purposes of any contract or subcontract subject to CAS requirements, the change must be applied prospectively to the contract and the Disclosure Statement must be amended accordingly [@CL-CMP-0289].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor that has a Disclosure Statement changes the practice used to allocate indirect costs for a CAS-covered contract. It applies the change prospectively and amends the Disclosure Statement. Those actions follow the clause's change requirement.

## Never confuse

The Disclosure Statement discloses the contractor's cost accounting practices as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5 [@CL-CMP-0607]. The consistency requirement is that the contractor follows its cost accounting practices consistently in accumulating and reporting contract performance cost data concerning the contract [@CL-CMP-0608].

## Company calibration

Whether each business unit has full CAS coverage, modified coverage, or neither, and whether a Disclosure Statement is on file. [@Q-033]
