## Purpose

Explains the disclosure, consistency, practice-change, and confidentiality requirements in the Cost Accounting Standards clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-2 [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-2 [@CL-CMP-0284] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0606] |
| Applies to | CAS-covered contracts unless the stated exemptions apply [@CL-CMP-0606; @CL-CMP-0607] |

Unless an exemption applies, the clause incorporates the CAS contract requirements by reference [@CL-CMP-0606]. A contractor on a CAS-covered contract discloses its practices in a Disclosure Statement as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5, follows them consistently, and applies covered practice changes prospectively while amending the Disclosure Statement [@CL-CMP-0607; @CL-CMP-0608; @CL-CMP-0609]. The clause ties the Disclosure Statement to what those provisions require, so CAS coverage alone does not settle whether one is required [@CL-CMP-0607].

## How it works

Where a Disclosure Statement is required under those provisions, it describes how direct and indirect costs are distinguished and the basis used to allocate indirect costs [@CL-CMP-0607]. When the contractor has notified the Contracting Officer that the Disclosure Statement contains privileged and confidential trade secrets and commercial or financial information, the statement is protected from release outside the Government [@CL-CMP-0610].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause keeps the incorporation of 48 CFR part 9903 by reference unless the contract is exempt under 48 CFR 9903.201-1 and 9903.201-2 [@CL-CMP-0285; @CL-CMP-0606]. Paragraph (a)(1) of the RFO clause applies to CAS-covered contracts only [@CL-CMP-0325]. It keeps the disclosure of cost accounting practices in a Disclosure Statement as required by 48 CFR 9903.202-1 through 9903.202-5, including methods of distinguishing direct costs from indirect costs and the basis used for allocating indirect costs [@CL-CMP-0286; @CL-CMP-0607]. It keeps the protection of a Disclosure Statement that the contractor has notified the Contracting Officer contains trade secrets and commercial or financial information that is privileged and confidential, which is not released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-0287; @CL-CMP-0610]. It keeps the consistent following of cost accounting practices in accumulating and reporting contract performance cost data for the contract [@CL-CMP-0288; @CL-CMP-0608], and the prospective application of a change in cost accounting practices made for the purposes of any contract or subcontract subject to CAS requirements, with the Disclosure Statement amended accordingly [@CL-CMP-0289; @CL-CMP-0609].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor that has a Disclosure Statement changes the practice used to allocate indirect costs for a CAS-covered contract. It applies the change prospectively and amends the Disclosure Statement. Those actions follow the clause's change requirement.

## Never confuse

Disclosure is not the same as consistent practice. The Disclosure Statement describes practices where one is required, while the consistency requirement governs accumulation and reporting of contract performance cost data [@CL-CMP-0607; @CL-CMP-0608].

## Company calibration

Whether each business unit has full CAS coverage, modified coverage, or neither, and whether a Disclosure Statement is on file. [@Q-033]
