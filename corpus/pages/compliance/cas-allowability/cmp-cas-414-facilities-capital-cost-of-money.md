## Purpose

Shows how facilities capital is measured and turned into a cost of money charge on each contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-CMP-0567] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.414-40 [@CL-CMP-0567] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0567] |
| Applies to | A contractor's facilities capital and the cost of capital committed to facilities, computed separately for each contract [@CL-CMP-0567; @CL-CMP-0569] |

Facilities capital is measured and allocated under the standard, and the allocated amount is the base for applying a cost of money rate [@CL-CMP-0567]. The rate rests on rates determined by the Secretary of the Treasury under the public law named in the standard [@CL-CMP-0568]. The cost of capital committed to facilities is computed separately for each contract using facilities capital cost of money factors computed for each cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-0569].

## How it works

The standard does not set a market rate of its own; it delegates the rate to Treasury determinations [@CL-CMP-0568]. Facilities capital is measured and allocated first, then the rate is applied to that allocated base [@CL-CMP-0567]. Each contract gets its own figure, built from factors computed for the period [@CL-CMP-0569].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A segment holds facilities capital of 4 million. A cost of money rate of 5 percent applied to the allocated base gives a charge of 200,000 for the period; the amount for each contract is then computed separately using the facilities capital cost of money factors for that period [@CL-CMP-0567; @CL-CMP-0569].

## Never confuse

Cost of money on facilities capital is a computed charge on the allocated facilities capital base, using a rate based on rates determined by the Secretary of the Treasury [@CL-CMP-0567; @CL-CMP-0568]. The factors are computed for each cost accounting period rather than once for the whole contract [@CL-CMP-0569].

## Company calibration

Whether facilities capital and the cost of money factors are recorded for each business unit and each contract [@Q-024].
