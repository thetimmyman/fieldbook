## Purpose

Shows what an offeror states in a proposal when award would change a cost accounting practice, and what it sends and how it builds the price proposal when it answers yes.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5942] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.230-7 [@CL-CMP-5942] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.230-7 [@CL-CMP-0272] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5942] |
| Applies to | Offerors whose contract award will result in a required or unilateral change in cost accounting practice [@CL-CMP-5942] |

The offeror shall check yes if the contract award will result in a required or unilateral change in cost accounting practice, including unilateral changes requested to be desirable changes [@CL-CMP-5942]. The two numbered duties of the provision apply if the offeror checked yes [@CL-CMP-6526].

## How it works

The question reaches unilateral changes that the offeror requests to be desirable changes, as well as required changes [@CL-CMP-5942]. If the offeror checked yes, the price proposal must be prepared in response to the solicitation using the changed practice for the period of performance for which the practice will be used [@CL-CMP-5943; @CL-CMP-6526]. The offeror must also submit a description of the changed cost accounting practice to the contracting officer and the Cognizant Federal Agency Official as pricing support for the proposal [@CL-CMP-5944; @CL-CMP-6526]. FAR 30.201-3(c) prescribes that the text be inserted as a provision [@CL-CMP-6525].

Under the RFO text, contracting officers shall use the RFO FAR part 52 text in lieu of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO provision keeps the yes check for a required or unilateral change in cost accounting practice, including unilateral changes requested to be desirable changes [@CL-CMP-0273; @CL-CMP-5942]. It keeps the duty, if the offeror checked yes, to prepare the price proposal in response to the solicitation using the changed practice for the period of performance for which the practice will be used [@CL-CMP-0274; @CL-CMP-5943; @CL-CMP-6526], and the duty to submit a description of the changed cost accounting practice to the contracting officer and the Cognizant Federal Agency Official as pricing support for the proposal [@CL-CMP-0275; @CL-CMP-5944]. The RFO provision is also inserted as a provision, as prescribed in FAR 30.204(c), where the codified provision is prescribed in FAR 30.201-3(c) [@CL-CMP-0272; @CL-CMP-6525].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor bids a five-year contract. If it wins, it will change how it allocates engineering cost, moving 300,000 a year from an indirect pool to a direct cost objective, starting in year three. That is a unilateral change that award will bring about, so the contractor checks yes. It prepares the price proposal using the changed practice for years three to five, the period in which the practice will be used, and it sends a description of the change to the contracting officer and the Cognizant Federal Agency Official as pricing support for the proposal.

## Never confuse

The changed practice and its description are two different submissions: if the offeror checked yes, the offeror shall prepare the price proposal in response to the solicitation using the changed practice for the period of performance for which the practice will be used [@CL-CMP-5943], and submit a description of the changed cost accounting practice to the Contracting Officer and the Cognizant Federal Agency Official as pricing support for the proposal [@CL-CMP-5944]. Whether the contract is CAS-covered at all is covered on the [CAS applicability card](cmp-cas-applicability.md), and the notices and certificates of the solicitation are on the [CAS notices card](cmp-cas-notices-and-certification.md).

The codified provision is prescribed by FAR 30.201-3(c) [@CL-CMP-6525], and the RFO provision is prescribed by FAR 30.204(c) [@CL-CMP-0272].

## Company calibration

Who decides that a bid will change a practice, who confirms the period of performance in which the changed practice applies, and who signs the description sent with the proposal. [@Q-003]
