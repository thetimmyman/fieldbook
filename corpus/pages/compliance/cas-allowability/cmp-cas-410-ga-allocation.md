## Purpose

Shows how a business unit groups and allocates its general and administrative expenses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-CMP-0213] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.410-40(a) [@CL-CMP-0213] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0213] |
| Applies to | Business unit general and administrative expenses [@CL-CMP-0213] |

Business unit general and administrative expenses are grouped in a separate indirect cost pool and allocated only to final cost objectives [@CL-CMP-0213]. The pool is allocated to final cost objectives of the period by a cost input base representing the total activity of the business unit, except as provided in subparagraph (b)(2) of the subsection [@CL-CMP-0214]. The allocation of the pool to any particular final cost objectives which receive benefits significantly different from the benefits accruing to other final cost objectives is determined by special allocation [@CL-CMP-0215].

## How it works

The G&A expense pool of a business unit is allocated to final cost objectives of the cost accounting period by means of a cost input base representing the total activity of the business unit, except as provided for final cost objectives subject to special allocation [@CL-CMP-0214; @CL-CMP-0215]. The cost input base selected is the one which best represents the total activity of a typical cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-8198]. The standard provides criteria for allocating home office expenses received by a segment to that segment's cost objectives [@CL-EST-2912]. Home office expenses received by a segment shall be allocated to segment cost objectives as required by 9904.410-50(g) [@CL-CMP-7887]. Any costs which do not satisfy the definition of G&A expense but which have been classified by a business unit as G&A expenses can remain in the G&A expense pool unless they can be allocated to business unit cost objectives on a beneficial or causal relationship which is best measured by a base other than a cost input base [@CL-CMP-8199; @CL-CMP-8200].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit has 800,000 of general and administrative expenses and a total cost input base of 8,000,000, giving a ten percent rate applied to each final cost objective. One contract receives significantly different benefit from the G&A expense pool than the others, so its share is set by special allocation instead of the ten percent rate.

## Never confuse

Business unit G&A expenses are grouped in a separate indirect cost pool which is allocated only to final cost objectives [@CL-CMP-0213]. The allocation of the pool to any particular final cost objectives which receive benefits significantly different from the benefits accruing to other final cost objectives is determined by special allocation [@CL-CMP-0215].
