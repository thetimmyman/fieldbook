## Purpose

Shows when costs tied to counterfeit and suspect counterfeit electronic parts are unallowable, and every condition under which they may be allowed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-2744] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 231.205-71 [@CL-CMP-2744] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2744] |
| Applies to | Costs of counterfeit and suspect counterfeit electronic parts and of rework or corrective action to remedy their use or inclusion [@CL-CMP-2744] |

The costs of counterfeit electronic parts and suspect counterfeit electronic parts, and the costs of rework or corrective action that may be required to remedy the use or inclusion of such parts, are unallowable unless the conditions listed in the following items are met [@CL-CMP-2744]. The listed conditions are joined by the word and before the final one, so the costs are unallowable unless every listed condition is met: the system condition, the parts source condition, and both the awareness condition and the notice condition [@CL-CMP-2744; @CL-CMP-2774]. The printed section does not itself name the contract types it covers.

One listed condition is that the contractor has an operational system to detect and avoid counterfeit electronic parts and suspect counterfeit electronic parts that has been reviewed and approved by DoD pursuant to DFARS 244.303(b) [@CL-CMP-2745]. A second is that the parts are Government-furnished property as defined in FAR 45.101 or were obtained by the contractor in accordance with the clause at DFARS 252.246-7008, Sources of Electronic Parts [@CL-CMP-2746]. A third has the contractor becoming aware of the parts through inspection, testing, and authentication efforts of the contractor or its subcontractors, through a Government Industry Data Exchange Program alert, or by other means [@CL-CMP-2747]. That third item also requires timely written notice, meaning within 60 days after the contractor becomes aware, to the cognizant contracting officer(s) [@CL-CMP-2747; @CL-CMP-2748], and to the Government Industry Data Exchange Program, unless the contractor is a foreign corporation or partnership that does not have an office, place of business, or fiscal paying agent in the United States, or the part is the subject of an on-going criminal investigation [@CL-CMP-2749].

## How it works

The default is unallowability, and allowance depends on the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-2744]. The second item ends with the word and, which joins it to the final item, so the list is one conjunctive list and a cost that meets only some of the conditions stays unallowable [@CL-CMP-2746; @CL-CMP-2774]. The final item has two parts joined by the word and, which are awareness through inspection, testing, an alert or other means, and timely written notice, so both parts are needed [@CL-CMP-2747]. The system condition points to DFARS 244.303(b) for the DoD review and approval, and the printed section does not set out what that review involves [@CL-CMP-2745]. The printed section does not define suspect counterfeit electronic part.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor finds suspect counterfeit parts in a delivered assembly and spends 12,000 on rework. It has a DoD-approved detection system, the parts were obtained in accordance with the sources clause, and it learned of the parts through its own testing. It gives written notice to the contracting officer and to the exchange program 20 days after becoming aware. The answer: every listed condition is met, so this section does not make the 12,000 unallowable. Had the notice come after 75 days, the notice condition would fail and the 12,000 would be unallowable.

## Never confuse

The section makes the cost unallowable unless the conditions are met, and it does not say that meeting them makes the cost allowable under every other test. For the general tests, see [Factors determining cost allowability](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md). The general rule for identifying and excluding unallowable costs from billings, claims and proposals is on [Accounting for unallowable and directly associated costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-accounting-for-unallowable-and-associated-costs.md). Allowability is also separate from the contract duties on counterfeit items, such as disposition instructions and reporting nonconforming items; see [Price reduction, withholds, rejection notices and counterfeit items](../../operating-model/corrective-action/opm-price-reduction-rejection-notice-and-counterfeit-items.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company has a DoD-approved counterfeit-part detection system, buys electronic parts under the sources clause, and tracks the notice deadline for each finding. [@Q-004; @Q-014]
