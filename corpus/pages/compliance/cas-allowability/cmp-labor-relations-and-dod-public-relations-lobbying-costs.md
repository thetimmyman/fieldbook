## Purpose

Separates allowable labor relations work from the persuasion costs the FAR makes unallowable, then adds the DoD supplement rules on public relations, advertising, lobbying and political activity.

### FAR labor relations costs

Costs incurred in maintaining satisfactory relations between the contractor and its employees (other than those made unallowable in paragraph (b) of this section), including costs of shop stewards, labor management committees, employee publications, and other related activities, are allowable [@CL-CMP-7524]. As required by Executive Order 13494, Economy in Government Contracting, costs of any activities undertaken to persuade employees, of any entity, to exercise or not to exercise, or concerning the manner of exercising, the right to organize and bargain collectively through representatives of the employees' own choosing are unallowable [@CL-CMP-7632; @CL-CMP-7525].

Examples of unallowable costs under this paragraph include, but are not limited to, the costs of (1) preparing and distributing materials and (2) hiring or consulting legal counsel or consultants [@CL-CMP-7526]. The examples also include (3) meetings, including paying the salaries of the attendees at meetings held for this purpose [@CL-CMP-7633], and planning or conducting activities by managers, supervisors or union representatives during work hours [@CL-CMP-7634].

### DoD supplements

Unallowable public relations and advertising costs also include monies paid to the Government associated with the leasing of Government equipment, including lease payments and reimbursement for support services, except for foreign military sales contracts as provided for at DFARS 225.7303-2 [@CL-CMP-7528]. The allowability provisions affecting foreign military sales contracts are at DFARS 225.7303-2(e) [@CL-CMP-7527].

Costs associated with preparing any material, report, list or analysis on the actual or projected economic or employment impact in a particular State or congressional district [@CL-CMP-7529] of an acquisition program for which all research, development, testing and evaluation has not been completed also are unallowable [@CL-CMP-7530].

## How it works

Costs incurred in maintaining satisfactory relations between the contractor and its employees (other than those made unallowable in paragraph (b) of this section), including costs of shop stewards, labor management committees, employee publications, and other related activities, are allowable [@CL-CMP-7524]. Under paragraph (b), costs of any activities undertaken to persuade employees, of any entity, to exercise or not to exercise, or concerning the manner of exercising, the right to organize and bargain collectively through representatives of the employees' own choosing are unallowable [@CL-CMP-7525]. Examples of unallowable costs under that paragraph include, but are not limited to, the costs of preparing and distributing materials and hiring or consulting legal counsel or consultants [@CL-CMP-7526], meetings (including paying the salaries of the attendees at meetings held for this purpose) [@CL-CMP-7633], and planning or conducting activities by managers, supervisors, or union representatives during work hours [@CL-CMP-7634].

The leasing rule makes lease payments and support-service reimbursements paid to the Government unallowable as public relations and advertising costs, and the exception is for foreign military sales contracts as provided for at DFARS 225.7303-2 [@CL-CMP-7528]. The cross-reference sends the reader to DFARS 225.7303-2(e) for allowability provisions affecting foreign military sales contracts [@CL-CMP-7527]. Under DFARS 231.205-22(a), costs associated with preparing any material, report, list, or analysis on the actual or projected economic or employment impact in a particular State or congressional district [@CL-CMP-7529] of an acquisition program for which all research, development, testing, and evaluation has not been completed also are unallowable [@CL-CMP-7530].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-21(a), costs incurred in maintaining satisfactory relations between the contractor and its employees, including costs of shop stewards, labor management committees, employee publications, and other related activities, are allowable [@CL-CMP-9436]. As required by Executive Order 13494 [@CL-CMP-9437], costs of any activities undertaken to persuade employees of any entity to exercise, or not to exercise, or concerning the manner of exercising, the right to organize and bargain collectively, through representatives of the employees' own choosing, are unallowable [@CL-CMP-9438]. Examples of unallowable costs are preparing and distributing materials and hiring or consulting legal counsel or consultants [@CL-CMP-9439], and meetings (including paying the salaries of the attendees at meetings held for this purpose) and planning or conducting activities by managers, supervisors, or union representatives during work hours [@CL-CMP-9440].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A plant runs an employee newsletter costing 12,000 a year and pays 40,000 to a consultancy that drafts letters urging employees to vote against a union. The newsletter is an employee publication that supports satisfactory relations, so it is allowable [@CL-CMP-7524]. The 40,000 is a cost of an activity undertaken to persuade employees about the right to organize, and hiring consultants for it is a listed example, so it is unallowable [@CL-CMP-7525; @CL-CMP-7526]. If managers also spend paid work hours costing 600 planning the letter campaign, that planning cost is likewise an example of an unallowable cost [@CL-CMP-7634].

On a DoD contract, the contractor pays the Government 5,000 to lease Government-owned equipment and the payment is not under a foreign military sales contract as provided for at DFARS 225.7303-2. The lease payment is an unallowable public relations and advertising cost [@CL-CMP-7528]. Separately, the contractor spends 2,000 preparing an analysis of the employment impact in one congressional district of a program whose research, development, testing and evaluation is not complete; that cost is unallowable [@CL-CMP-7529; @CL-CMP-7530].

## Never confuse

The labor relations persuasion prohibition is in FAR 31.205-21(b) [@CL-CMP-7525], and the DoD supplement rule on impact material for an acquisition program for which all research, development, testing, and evaluation has not been completed is in DFARS 231.205-22(a) [@CL-CMP-7530]. Codified paragraph (a) allows relations costs "(other than those made unallowable in paragraph (b) of this section)" [@CL-CMP-7524]. RFO paragraph (b) says costs of any activities undertaken to persuade employees of any entity to exercise, or not to exercise, or concerning the manner of exercising, the right to organize and bargain collectively, through representatives of the employees' own choosing are unallowable [@CL-CMP-9438]. DFARS 231.205-1(f) says unallowable public relations and advertising costs also include monies paid to the Government associated with the leasing of Government equipment, except for foreign military sales contracts as provided for at 225.7303-2 [@CL-CMP-7528], and DFARS 231.205-1(e) says to see 225.7303-2(e) for allowability provisions affecting foreign military sales contracts [@CL-CMP-7527]. The neighbouring cards are [public relations and advertising costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-public-relations-and-advertising-costs.md) and [lobbying and political activity costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-lobbying-and-political-activity-costs.md).
