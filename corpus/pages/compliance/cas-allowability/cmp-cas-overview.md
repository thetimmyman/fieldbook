## Purpose

Introduces the Cost Accounting Standards (CAS): what the Cost Accounting Standards govern, when they apply, and what coverage brings with it.

## The rule

Certain contractors and subcontractors are required to comply with Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) and to disclose in writing and follow consistently their cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-9677]. The coverage figures here are stated in the Cost Accounting Standards Board's regulation, 48 CFR 9903, and took effect on 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0001; @CL-CMP-0002]. Negotiated contracts and subcontracts not in excess of $35 million are exempt from all CAS requirements [@CL-CMP-8116; @CL-CMP-0001]. Full coverage applies to a contractor business unit that receives a single CAS-covered contract award of $100 million or more, or that received $100 million or more in net CAS-covered awards during its preceding cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-8117; @CL-CMP-0002; @CL-CMP-7069]. A business unit selected to receive a CAS-covered contract or subcontract of $100 million or more shall submit a Disclosure Statement before award [@CL-CMP-0003]. A business unit or segment with net awards subject to CAS totaling $100 million or more in its most recent cost accounting period must also submit one before award of its first CAS-covered contract in the immediately following period [@CL-CMP-7070]. However, if that first CAS-covered contract is received within 90 days of the start of the cost accounting period, the contractor is not required to file until the end of 90 days [@CL-CMP-7071].

## How it works

Full coverage requires the business unit to comply with all of the CAS specified in part 9904 that are in effect on the date of the contract award and with any CAS that become applicable because of later award of a CAS-covered contract [@CL-CMP-8117]. Modified coverage may be applied to a covered contract of less than $100 million awarded to a business unit that received less than $100 million in net CAS-covered awards in the immediately preceding cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-8118]. All contractors, whether subject to full or modified CAS coverage, are subject to CAS 401 and CAS 402 and will continue to be required to consistently follow their disclosed or actual cost accounting practices [@CL-CMP-1515]. The codified FAR text and provisions in the Corpus still print earlier figures, such as $50 million in the FAR 52.230-1 certificate [@CL-CMP-5923] and $2.5 million and $50 million in FAR 30.201-4(b)(1) [@CL-RTE-0842]; the Board's regulation in 48 CFR 9903 states coverage from 2026-10-01 [@CL-CMP-0002]. The clause cards for single standards give the detail. See [CAS applicability](cmp-cas-applicability.md) for the thresholds.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit wins its first negotiated award of 60 million. The award exceeds the exemption, so it is covered, but the unit has no prior covered awards large enough for full coverage, so modified coverage may be applied.

## Never confuse

A cost is allowable only when it complies with all of the requirements listed in FAR 31.201-2(a), one of which is standards promulgated by the CAS Board, if applicable [@CL-CMP-0101; @CL-CMP-8030].
