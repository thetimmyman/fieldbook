## Purpose

Sets the period in which deferred compensation cost is charged and the measure used for it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-CMP-0570] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.415-40 [@CL-CMP-0570] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0570] |
| Applies to | Awards of deferred compensation, including ESOP contributions [@CL-CMP-0572] |

Deferred compensation cost is assigned to the period in which the contractor incurs the obligation to compensate the employee [@CL-CMP-0570]. If no obligation is incurred before payment, the cost is the amount paid and is assigned to the period of payment [@CL-CMP-0571]. For awards other than an ESOP the cost is the present value of the future benefits to be paid; for an ESOP it is the amount contributed [@CL-CMP-0572; @CL-CMP-0573]. Each award is measured and assigned separately, though group measurement is allowed where it is reasonably accurate for the employees covered [@CL-CMP-0574].

## How it works

The trigger is the obligation rather than the cash payment, so the cost lands in the period the promise is made [@CL-CMP-0570]. Where the promise comes after the cash, the amount paid is the measure and the payment period is where the cost sits [@CL-CMP-0571]. Measurement differs by vehicle: present value for ordinary awards, contribution amount for an ESOP [@CL-CMP-0572; @CL-CMP-0573]. The separate-award rule gives way to group treatment only where group measurement stays reasonably accurate [@CL-CMP-0574].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company promises an award whose future benefits are 500,000, payable in later years, and the present value at the obligation date is 400,000. The cost assigned to the period of obligation is 400,000, not the 500,000 eventually paid.

## Never confuse

Deferred compensation for an ordinary award is measured as a present value, which is not the cash the employee will eventually receive [@CL-CMP-0572]. An ESOP contribution is measured at the amount contributed, so no present value step is applied to it [@CL-CMP-0573].
