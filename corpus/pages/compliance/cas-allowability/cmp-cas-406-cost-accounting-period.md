## Purpose

Shows which period a contractor uses to accumulate costs and to establish the allocation base.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | CAS [@CL-CMP-0210] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.406-40(a) [@CL-CMP-0210] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0210] |
| Applies to | A contractor's cost accounting period [@CL-CMP-0210] |

A contractor uses its fiscal year as its cost accounting period, subject to the listed exceptions [@CL-CMP-0210; @CL-CMP-0835; @CL-CMP-0836; @CL-CMP-0837; @CL-CMP-0212]. The same cost accounting period is used for accumulating costs in an indirect cost pool as for establishing its allocation base, except that the contracting parties may agree to use a different period for establishing an allocation base as provided in 9904.406-50(e) [@CL-CMP-0211]. Under paragraph (e) the parties may agree to use an annual period which does not coincide precisely with the cost accounting period for developing the data used in establishing an allocation base, provided the practice is necessary to obtain significant administrative convenience and is consistently followed by the contractor [@CL-EST-2887; @CL-EST-2888], the annual period used is representative of the activity of the cost accounting period for which the indirect costs to be allocated are accumulated [@CL-EST-2969], and the practice can reasonably be estimated to provide a distribution to cost objectives of the cost accounting period not materially different from that which otherwise would be obtained [@CL-EST-2970]. When the fiscal year changes, the contractor uses a transitional period of a length other than a year [@CL-CMP-0212].

## How it works

Costs of an indirect function that exists for only part of a cost accounting period may be allocated to cost objectives of that same part of the period [@CL-CMP-0835], on the basis of data for that part of the period if the cost is material in amount, accumulated in a separate indirect cost pool, and allocated on the basis of an appropriate direct measure of the activity or output of the function during that part of the period [@CL-EST-2879; @CL-EST-2880]. An annual period other than the fiscal year may, as provided in 9904.406-50(d), be used as the cost accounting period if its use is an established practice of the contractor [@CL-CMP-0836]; the standard lets a contractor, upon mutual agreement with the Government, use a fixed annual period other than its fiscal year if the use of such a period is an established practice and is consistently used for managing and controlling the business, and appropriate accruals, deferrals or other adjustments are made with respect to such annual periods [@CL-EST-2885; @CL-EST-2886]. The contracting parties may also agree to use a different period for establishing an allocation base as provided in 9904.406-50(e) [@CL-CMP-0211], on the conditions described above [@CL-EST-2887; @CL-EST-2888; @CL-EST-2969; @CL-EST-2970].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor accumulates 1,200,000 of overhead and a matching allocation base over its fiscal year. Mid-cycle it changes its fiscal year, so it uses a transitional cost accounting period of a length other than a year for the change.

## Never confuse

The pool's period and the base's period differ only by agreement: the same cost accounting period is to be used for accumulating costs in an indirect cost pool as for establishing its allocation base, except that the contracting parties may agree to use a different period for establishing an allocation base as provided in 9904.406-50(e) [@CL-CMP-0211].
