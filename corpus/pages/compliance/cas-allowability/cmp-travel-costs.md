## Purpose

Sets allowability boundaries, maximum per diem limitations, and documentation requirements for contractor business travel.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0109] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-46 [@CL-CMP-0109] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-46 [@CL-CMP-0109] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0109] |
| Applies to | Government contracts subject to the cost principles [@CL-CMP-0109] |

Costs incurred by contractor personnel on official company business for transportation, lodging, and meals are allowable subject to regulatory limitations and per diem ceilings [@CL-CMP-0109]. When actual costs exceeding per diem are authorized in special situations, a receipt is required for each expenditure of $75.00 or more [@CL-CMP-0110]. Airfare costs in excess of the lowest priced airfare available during normal business hours are unallowable unless an exception applies and is documented [@CL-CMP-0111].

## How it works

Allowable lodging, meals, and incidental expenses are capped on a daily basis by the maximum per diem rates established in government travel regulations. In special or unusual situations, actual costs above the maximum per diem rates are allowable, up to the higher amounts authorized for Federal civilian employees, only if all of these conditions are met: a condition warranting the actual expense method under the cited travel regulations exists, a written justification is approved by an officer of the contractor's organization or designee, advance contracting officer approval is obtained for repetitive or continuing use in a particular area, and the actual costs are documented, with a receipt for each expenditure of $75.00 or more [@CL-CMP-0110]. Airfare above the lowest priced airfare available during normal business hours is unallowable unless one of the listed conditions applies, such as circuitous routing, unreasonable hours or the traveler's physical or medical needs, and that condition is documented and justified [@CL-CMP-0111].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An employee travels on official business where the standard per diem ceiling for lodging is 150 per night. The employee incurs actual lodging of 220 per night due to an emergency conference. If a condition warranting the actual expense method under the cited travel regulations exists, 220 does not exceed the higher amount authorized for Federal civilian employees, and the written justification is approved by a company officer and retained, the full 220 is allowable, with a receipt required because the lodging expenditure is 75.00 or more.

## Never confuse

Official travel on company business is allowable subject to the limitations of the travel cost principle, including the daily per diem ceilings for lodging, meals and incidental expenses, and only if the date and place, purpose of the trip, and traveler's name and title or relationship to the contractor are documented. Using the actual expense method requires an approved written justification and a receipt for each expenditure of $75.00 or more rather than automatic reimbursement [@CL-CMP-0110]. Including unallowable travel costs in rate submissions exposes the contractor to penalties; see [Unallowable-cost penalties](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md).
