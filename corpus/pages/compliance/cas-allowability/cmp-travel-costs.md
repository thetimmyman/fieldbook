## Purpose

Sets allowability boundaries, maximum per diem limitations, and documentation requirements for contractor business travel.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0109] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-46 [@CL-CMP-0109] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-46 [@CL-CMP-8933; @CL-CMP-8934] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0109] |
| Applies to | Government contracts subject to the cost principles [@CL-CMP-0109] |

Under FAR 31.205-46(a)(1), costs incurred by contractor personnel on official company business are allowable, subject to the limitations contained in this subsection [@CL-CMP-0109]. Except as provided in paragraph (a)(3) of this section [@CL-CMP-8491], under paragraph (a)(2), costs incurred for lodging, meals, and incidental expenses (as defined in the regulations cited in paragraphs (a)(2)(i) through (iii) of this section) shall be considered to be reasonable and allowable only to the extent that they do not exceed on a daily basis the maximum per diem rates [@CL-CMP-8820] in effect at the time of travel as set forth in the regulations listed in paragraph (a)(2), including the Federal Travel Regulation, prescribed by the General Services Administration, for travel in the contiguous United States [@CL-CMP-9750].

## How it works

Under paragraph (a)(3), in special or unusual situations, actual costs in excess of the above-referenced maximum per diem rates are allowable provided that such amounts do not exceed the higher amounts authorized for Federal civilian employees as permitted in the regulations referenced in paragraph (a)(2)(i), (ii), or (iii) [@CL-CMP-8583]. For such higher amounts to be allowable, all of the following conditions must be met: (i) one of the conditions warranting approval of the actual expense method, as set forth in the regulations referred in paragraph (a)(2)(i), (ii), or (iii), must exist [@CL-CMP-8584]; (ii) a written justification for use of the higher amounts must be approved by an officer of the contractor's organization or designee [@CL-CMP-8585]; (iii) if it becomes necessary to exercise the authority to use the higher actual expense method repetitively or on a continuing basis in a particular area, the contractor must obtain advance approval from the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-8813]; and (iv) documentation to support actual costs incurred shall be in accordance with the contractor's established practices, subject to paragraph (a)(7), and provided that a receipt is required for each expenditure of $75.00 or more [@CL-CMP-8492; @CL-CMP-0110].

Under paragraph (a)(7) [@CL-CMP-8819], costs shall be allowable only if the following information is documented: (i) date and place (city, town, or other similar designation) of the expenses; (ii) purpose of the trip; and (iii) name of person on trip and that person's title or relationship to the contractor [@CL-CMP-8819].

Under paragraph (b), airfare costs in excess of the lowest priced airfare available to the contractor during normal business hours are unallowable except when such accommodations require circuitous routing, require travel during unreasonable hours, excessively prolong travel [@CL-CMP-0111], result in increased cost that would offset transportation savings, are not reasonably adequate for the physical or medical needs of the traveler, or are not reasonably available to meet mission requirements [@CL-CMP-0812]. However, in order for airfare costs in excess of the above airfare to be allowable, the applicable condition(s) set forth above must be documented and justified [@CL-CMP-0813].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-46(a)(1), costs incurred by contractor personnel on official company business are allowable, subject to the limitations contained in this section [@CL-CMP-8933]. Except as provided in paragraph (a)(3), under paragraph (a)(2), costs incurred for lodging, meals, and incidental expenses, must be considered to be reasonable and allowable only to the extent that they do not exceed, on a daily basis, the maximum per diem rates [@CL-CMP-8493]. Under paragraph (a)(3), in unusual situations, actual costs in excess of the maximum per diem rates are allowable provided that such amounts do not exceed the higher amounts authorized for Federal civilian employees as permitted in the regulations referenced in paragraph (a)(2)(i), (ii), or (iii) [@CL-CMP-8934]. For such higher amounts to be allowable, the following conditions must be met: (i) one of the conditions warranting approval of the actual expense method, as set forth in the regulations referred in paragraph (a)(2)(i), (ii), or (iii), must exist [@CL-CMP-8935]; (ii) a written justification for use of the higher amounts must be approved by an official of the contractor's organization [@CL-CMP-8936]; (iii) if it becomes necessary to exercise the authority to use the higher actual expense method repetitively or on a continuing basis in a particular area, the contractor must obtain advance approval from the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-8937]; and (iv) documentation to support actual costs incurred must be in accordance with the contractor's established practices, subject to paragraph (a)(7), and a receipt is required for each expenditure of $75.00 or more [@CL-CMP-8938]. Under paragraph (a)(6)(i), the maximum per diem rates referenced in paragraph (a)(2) generally would not constitute a reasonable daily charge (A) when no lodging costs are incurred and/or (B) on partial travel days (e.g., day of departure and return) [@CL-CMP-8939]. Under paragraph (a)(6)(ii), the contractor must make appropriate downward adjustments from the maximum per diem rates under these circumstances, and while these adjustments need not be calculated in accordance with the FTR or JTR, they must result in a reasonable charge [@CL-CMP-8940]. Under paragraph (a)(7) [@CL-CMP-8941], costs must be allowable only if the following information is documented: (i) date and place (city, town, or other similar designation) of the expenses; (ii) purpose of the trip; and (iii) name of person on trip and that person's title or relationship to the contractor [@CL-CMP-8941]. Under paragraph (b)(1) [@CL-CMP-8942], airfare costs in excess of the lowest priced airfare available to the contractor during normal business hours are unallowable except when the lowest price airfare results in (i) circuitous routing; (ii) travel during unreasonable hours; (iii) excessively prolonged travel [@CL-CMP-8942]; (iv) increased costs that would offset transportation savings; (v) unreasonable or inadequate conditions for the physical or medical needs of the traveler; or (vi) an inability to meet mission requirements [@CL-CMP-8943]. Under paragraph (b)(2), airfare costs in excess of the lowest priced airfare that meet the criteria in paragraph (b)(1) are allowable if documented and justified [@CL-CMP-8944].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An employee travels on official business where the standard per diem ceiling for lodging is 150 per night. The employee incurs actual lodging of 220 per night due to an emergency conference. If a condition warranting the actual expense method under the cited travel regulations exists, 220 does not exceed the higher amount authorized for Federal civilian employees, and the written justification is approved by a company officer and retained, the full 220 is allowable, with a receipt required because the lodging expenditure is 75.00 or more.

## Never confuse

For penalties on unallowable costs in rate submissions, see [Unallowable-cost penalties](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md).

The codified text says that for airfare above the lowest priced airfare to be allowable the applicable condition must be documented and justified [@CL-CMP-0813], whereas the RFO paragraph (b)(2) says airfare above the lowest priced airfare that meets the paragraph (b)(1) criteria is allowable if documented and justified [@CL-CMP-8944].

For the higher actual-cost amounts, the codified text says in special or unusual situations [@CL-CMP-8583], whereas the RFO text says in unusual situations [@CL-CMP-8934]. The codified text says all of the following conditions must be met [@CL-CMP-8584], whereas the RFO text says the following conditions must be met [@CL-CMP-8935]. The codified text has the written justification approved by an officer of the contractor's organization or designee [@CL-CMP-8585], whereas the RFO text has it approved by an official of the contractor's organization [@CL-CMP-8936].
