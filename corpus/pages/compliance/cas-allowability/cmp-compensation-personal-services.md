## Purpose

Compensation for personal services is allowable subject to the cost principle's general criteria and additional requirements [@CL-CMP-8258], including current-year work, reasonableness, and conformity to the terms and conditions of the contractor's established compensation plan or practice followed so consistently as to imply, in effect, an agreement to make the payment [@CL-CMP-0451; @CL-CMP-0452; @CL-CMP-8261].

## The rule

| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-6 [@CL-CMP-0451] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-6 [@CL-CMP-9939] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0451] |
| Applies to | Compensation for personal services [@CL-CMP-0451] |

Compensation for personal services must be for work performed in the current year and must not be a retroactive adjustment of prior years' salaries or wages, (but see paragraphs (g), (h), (j), (k), (m), and (o) of the codified cost principle) [@CL-CMP-0451]. The total compensation for individual employees or job classes of employees must be reasonable for the work performed; however, specific restrictions on individual compensation elements apply when prescribed [@CL-CMP-0452]. No presumption of allowability exists where the contractor introduces major revisions of existing compensation plans or new plans and has not given the cognizant ACO, either before implementation or within a reasonable period after it, an opportunity to review the allowability of the changes [@CL-CMP-0453; @CL-CMP-0823]. For owners of closely held companies, compensation in excess of the costs that are deductible as compensation under the Internal Revenue Code and regulations under it is unallowable [@CL-CMP-0454].

## How it works

Compensation for personal services must be for work performed by the employee in the current year and must not represent a retroactive adjustment of prior years' salaries or wages (but see paragraphs (g), (h), (j), (k), (m), and (o) of the codified cost principle) [@CL-CMP-0451]. The total compensation for individual employees or job classes of employees must be reasonable for the work performed [@CL-CMP-0452]. In testing the reasonableness of compensation for particular employees or job classes of employees, the codified text says to consider factors determined to be relevant by the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-9607]; factors that may be relevant include, but are not limited to, conformity with compensation practices of other firms of the same size, in the same industry, in the same geographic area, and engaged in similar non-Government work under comparable circumstances [@CL-CMP-9608]. If a contractor introduces major revisions of existing compensation plans, or new plans, without giving the cognizant ACO an opportunity to review allowability either before implementation or within a reasonable period after it, no presumption of allowability exists [@CL-CMP-0453; @CL-CMP-0823]. Compensation costs for certain individuals give rise to the need for special consideration; such individuals include owners of closely held corporations, members of limited liability companies, partners, sole proprietors, or members of their immediate families [@CL-CMP-8259], and persons who are contractually committed to acquire a substantial financial interest in the contractor's enterprise [@CL-CMP-8260]. For these individuals, compensation must be reasonable for the personal services rendered and must not be a distribution of profits [@CL-CMP-9610].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.205-6(a)(1) says compensation for personal services must be for work performed by the employee in the current year and must not represent a retroactive adjustment of prior years' salaries or wages, with the words except see paragraphs (f), (g), (i), (j), (l), and (n) of that section [@CL-CMP-9939]. RFO paragraph (a)(2) says the total compensation for individual employees or job classes must be reasonable for the work performed, except as otherwise specified in that section [@CL-CMP-9940], where the codified paragraph says that specific restrictions on individual compensation elements apply when prescribed [@CL-CMP-0452]. RFO paragraph (b)(2)(ii) says to consider factors that may be relevant, including conformity with compensation practices of other firms of the same size, in the same industry, in the same geographic area and engaged in similar non-Government work under comparable circumstances [@CL-CMP-9609], where the codified paragraph says to consider factors determined to be relevant by the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-9607]. RFO paragraph (a)(4) keeps the rule that no presumption of allowability exists where major revisions of existing compensation plans, or new plans, have not been provided to the cognizant administrative contracting officer, either before implementation or within a reasonable period after it, with an opportunity to review the allowability of the changes [@CL-CMP-9941; @CL-CMP-9942]. RFO paragraph (a)(6)(iii) keeps the rule that for owners of closely held companies, compensation in excess of the costs deductible as compensation under the Internal Revenue Code and regulations under it is unallowable [@CL-CMP-9943].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An employee receives a base salary plus a year-end bonus. The company keeps both within the total compensation its plan pays for comparable jobs in its industry and region. The compensation is allowable subject to being for current-year work, reasonable for the work performed, and based on and in conformity with the established plan or practice. The bonus is allowable provided it is paid or accrued under a good-faith agreement made before the services were rendered or under an established plan or policy followed so consistently as to imply an agreement to make the payment, and its basis is supported.

## Never confuse

The total compensation for individual employees or job classes of employees must be reasonable for the work performed; however, specific restrictions on individual compensation elements apply when prescribed [@CL-CMP-0452]. Under the codified text, any compensation which is calculated, or valued, based on changes in the price of corporate securities is unallowable [@CL-CMP-9611]; the RFO text lists the same kind of compensation as the first item of unallowable compensation under its paragraph (h) [@CL-CMP-9612]. The codified current-year rule says to see paragraphs (g), (h), (j), (k), (m), and (o) [@CL-CMP-0451], while the RFO text says to see paragraphs (f), (g), (i), (j), (l), and (n) [@CL-CMP-9939].

## Company calibration

Whether each compensation plan is established and consistent, and whether major plan revisions were shared with the cognizant ACO for allowability review. [@Q-043]
