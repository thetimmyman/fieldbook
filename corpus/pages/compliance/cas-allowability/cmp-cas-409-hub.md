## Purpose

Cost Accounting Standard 409 establishes criteria for the depreciation of tangible capital assets. This concept hub sets out the requirements and current status of the standard and links the detailed compliance, clause, and rate pages across the corpus.

## The rule

The Cost Accounting Standards Board's final rule on CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 wholly rescinds CAS 408 and 411 and rescinds most provisions of CAS 404 and 409 [@CL-CMP-1500]. The final rule on CAS 404, 408, 409 and 411 has an effective date of August 7, 2026 [@CL-CMP-1501]. In CAS 409, the Board retains only the requirements of CAS 409-50(e)(5), 409-50(j)(1) and 409-50(j)(4), treating every other provision of CAS 409 as unnecessary [@CL-CMP-1503]. The rule removes and reserves subparts 9904.408 and 9904.409, which held the sections of CAS 408 and CAS 409 [@CL-CMP-1506]. The printed codified text of 48 CFR part 9904 shows sections 9904.407 through 9904.409 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1523].

The Board does not expect the CAS 409 rescission to justify treating a contractor's change related to depreciation costs as a required change under 9903.201-6(b)(2) [@CL-CMP-1510]. The Board states that no exemption from the price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 is warranted for the CAS 409 rescission [@CL-CMP-1513].

## How it works

Not established. The rule removes and reserves subpart 9904.409, which held the sections of CAS 409 [@CL-CMP-1506], and the printed codified text shows sections 9904.407 through 9904.409 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1523]. Under the final rule, most provisions of CAS 409 are rescinded [@CL-CMP-1500] with an effective date of August 7, 2026 [@CL-CMP-1501], retaining only the requirements of CAS 409-50(e)(5), 409-50(j)(1) and 409-50(j)(4) [@CL-CMP-1503].

The Board does not expect the rescission to justify treating a contractor's change related to depreciation costs as a required change under 9903.201-6(b)(2) [@CL-CMP-1510], and it found no exemption from the price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 warranted [@CL-CMP-1513].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's disclosed practice depreciates capital equipment costing 500,000 on a straight line basis over ten years, which is 50,000 a year. After the rescission of most of CAS 409, it considers moving to a five-year accelerated method to match its commercial accounting, which would raise the first year's charge to covered contracts by 60,000. The contractor does not assume that the rescission itself makes the change a required change under 9903.201-6(b)(2), because the Board does not expect it to, and it does not assume an exemption from the adjustment requirements, because the Board found none warranted. It measures the cost impact of the change before adopting it. If it keeps the straight line practice, its practice does not change. This example is illustrative only, and the contract itself governs.

## Never confuse

CAS 409 is rescinded where FAR 31.205-11 stands: although the Board's final rule rescinded most provisions of CAS 409 and removed and reserved subpart 9904.409 [@CL-CMP-1500; @CL-CMP-1506; @CL-CMP-1523], FAR 31.205-11 remains active and governs the allowability of depreciation on plant, equipment, and other capital facilities [@CL-CMP-0464]. Under FAR 31.205-11, allowable depreciation is subject to specific regulatory limits, including disallowing depreciation on property acquired from the Government at no cost [@CL-CMP-0466] and barring depreciation on fully depreciated property [@CL-CMP-0467]. In addition, CAS 414 remains active to measure and allocate the imputed cost of capital committed to facilities [@CL-CMP-8690; @CL-IND-0031], applying a cost of money rate to facilities capital consisting of the net book value of capital assets [@CL-CMP-0567; @CL-IND-0032].
