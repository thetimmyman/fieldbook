## Purpose

Lists the types of recurring business costs that the cost principle makes allowable.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0868] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-28 [@CL-CMP-0868] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-28 [@CL-CMP-9466; @CL-CMP-9467; @CL-CMP-9468; @CL-CMP-9469] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0868] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

The following types of recurring costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-0868]: (a) registry and transfer charges resulting from changes in ownership of securities issued by the contractor [@CL-CMP-0869]; (b) cost of shareholders' meetings, (c) normal proxy solicitations, and (d) preparing and publishing reports to shareholders [@CL-CMP-0870]; (e) preparing and submitting required reports and forms to taxing and other regulatory bodies and (f) incidental costs of directors' and committee meetings [@CL-CMP-0871]; and (g) other similar costs [@CL-CMP-0872].

## How it works

Under codified FAR 31.205-28, the following types of recurring costs are allowable [@CL-CMP-0868], and the list ends with (g) other similar costs [@CL-CMP-0872].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-28, the following types of recurring costs are allowable: (a) registry and transfer charges resulting from changes in ownership of securities issued by the contractor [@CL-CMP-9466]; (b) cost of shareholders' meetings, (c) normal proxy solicitations, and (d) preparing and publishing reports to shareholders [@CL-CMP-9467]; (e) preparing and submitting required reports and forms to taxing and other regulatory bodies and (f) incidental costs of directors' and committee meetings [@CL-CMP-9468]; and (g) other similar costs [@CL-CMP-9469].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Annual shareholders' meeting and proxy work cost 15,000, the reports to shareholders cost 9,000, and the filings with a state taxing body cost 6,000. All three are allowable recurring costs, 30,000 in total.

## Never confuse

The two report kinds go to different audiences: preparing and publishing reports to shareholders is item (d) [@CL-CMP-0870], and preparing and submitting required reports and forms to taxing and other regulatory bodies is item (e) [@CL-CMP-0871]. For public relations and advertising costs, see [Public relations and advertising](../cas-allowability/cmp-public-relations-and-advertising-costs.md).

## Company calibration

Whether shareholder and regulatory housekeeping costs are booked as recurring indirect cost rather than charged to a contract. [@Q-044]
