## Purpose

Shows which recurring costs of running a company the section makes allowable.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0868] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-28 [@CL-CMP-0868] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0868] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

The section makes a listed set of recurring business costs allowable [@CL-CMP-0868]. The list reaches registry and transfer charges that follow a change in ownership of securities issued by the contractor [@CL-CMP-0869], the cost of shareholders' meetings, normal proxy solicitations and reports to shareholders [@CL-CMP-0870], the reports and forms the company must prepare and submit to taxing and other regulatory bodies, and the incidental costs of directors' and committee meetings [@CL-CMP-0871]. The list closes with other similar costs [@CL-CMP-0872].

## How it works

The section works by listing types of recurring cost and making those types allowable [@CL-CMP-0868]. The closing item, other similar costs, lets a cost like the named ones fall under the same section [@CL-CMP-0872]. The printed section does not say which cost pool these costs belong to.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Annual shareholders' meeting and proxy work cost 15,000, the reports to shareholders cost 9,000, and the filings with a state taxing body cost 6,000. All three are allowable recurring costs, 30,000 in total.

## Never confuse

Shareholder servicing costs and public relations or advertising costs sit under separate cost principles. A report to shareholders and a required report to a taxing or other regulatory body are separate items on the list [@CL-CMP-0870; @CL-CMP-0871]. The neighbouring limits are set out in [Public relations and advertising](../cas-allowability/cmp-public-relations-and-advertising-costs.md).

## Company calibration

Whether shareholder and regulatory housekeeping costs are booked as recurring indirect cost rather than charged to a contract. [@Q-044]
