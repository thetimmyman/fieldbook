## Purpose

Shows how annual actuarial gains and losses and the value of plan assets are handled, and how pension cost reaches each segment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-CMP-0562] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.413-40 [@CL-CMP-0562] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0562] |
| Applies to | Segments with participants in a pension plan [@CL-CMP-0564] |

Actuarial gains and losses are calculated every year and assigned to the period of the valuation and to later periods [@CL-CMP-0562]. The actuarial value of plan assets comes from a valuation method that takes unrealized appreciation and depreciation of market value into account, and that value shall be used in measuring the components of pension costs [@CL-CMP-0563; @CL-CMP-0832]. Pension cost is allocated to every segment that has participants in the plan [@CL-CMP-0564]. A separate calculation of pension costs for a segment is required when the conditions set forth in 9904.413-50(c)(2) or (3) are present [@CL-CMP-0565]. When these conditions are not present, allocations may be made by calculating a composite pension cost for two or more segments and allocating this cost to them by means of an allocation base [@CL-CMP-0833]. When pension costs are separately computed for a segment or segments, the provisions of Cost Accounting Standard 9904.412 regarding the assignable cost limitation shall be based on the actuarial value of assets, actuarial accrued liability and normal cost for the segment or segments for purposes of such computations [@CL-CMP-8112; @CL-CMP-8113]. In addition, for purposes of 9904.412-50(c)(2)(iii), the amount of pension cost assignable to a segment or segments shall not exceed the sum of (i) the maximum tax-deductible amount computed for the plan as a whole [@CL-CMP-8113; @CL-CMP-0566] and (ii) the accumulated value of prepayment credits not already allocated to segments apportioned among the segment(s) [@CL-CMP-7060].

## How it works

Gains and losses are calculated annually and assigned to the cost accounting period for which the valuation is made and to subsequent periods [@CL-CMP-0562]. The actuarial value of plan assets is determined under an asset valuation method that takes into account unrealized appreciation and depreciation of market value, including the accumulated value of permitted unfunded accruals [@CL-CMP-0563; @CL-CMP-0832]. Contractors allocate pension costs to each segment having participants in a pension plan, and the allocation may rest on a separate calculation for the segment or, when the conditions for a separate calculation are not present, on a composite pension cost spread by an allocation base [@CL-CMP-0564; @CL-CMP-0565; @CL-CMP-0833]. Where pension costs are separately computed, the assignable amount shall not exceed the sum described above [@CL-CMP-8113; @CL-CMP-0566; @CL-CMP-7060].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Two segments share a composite pension cost of 8 million, and none of the conditions that require a separate segment calculation is present, so the composite may be allocated by an allocation base, here taken to be pensionable payroll [@CL-CMP-0565; @CL-CMP-0833]. Payroll splits 70 percent and 30 percent, so the segments receive 5.6 million and 2.4 million.

## Never confuse

For defined-benefit pension plans other than those accounted for under the pay-as-you-go cost method, the components of pension cost for a cost accounting period include an adjustment for any actuarial gains and losses [@CL-CMP-8038; @CL-CMP-0557]; the annual calculation and assignment of those gains and losses is covered here [@CL-CMP-0562]. A segment's own calculation and the composite alternative differ in when they apply: a separate calculation of pension costs for a segment is required when the conditions set forth in 9904.413-50(c)(2) or (3) are present, and when those conditions are not present allocations may be made by a composite pension cost and an allocation base [@CL-CMP-0565; @CL-CMP-0833].
