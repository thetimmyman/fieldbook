## Purpose

Collects three Cost Accounting Standards program provisions: the relief from the price and cost adjustment requirements for compliant practice changes tied to external restructuring, the agency head route when a Disclosure Statement cannot practically be secured, and the protection of a Disclosure Statement that the offeror or contractor says holds privileged and confidential content [@CL-CMP-8685; @CL-CMP-8686; @CL-CMP-8689].

Under 48 CFR 9903.201-8, the contract price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 are not applicable to compliant cost accounting practice changes directly associated with external restructuring activities that are subject to and meet the requirements of section 2325 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-8685].

Under 48 CFR 9903.202-2, the agency head may determine that it is impractical to secure the Disclosure Statement, although submission is required, and authorize contract award without obtaining the Statement [@CL-CMP-8686]. The agency head shall, within 30 days of having made that determination and authorized award, submit a report to the Cost Accounting Standards Board setting forth all material facts [@CL-CMP-8687]. The authority may not be delegated [@CL-CMP-8688].

Under 48 CFR 9903.202-4, if the offeror or contractor notifies the contracting officer that the Disclosure Statement contains trade secrets and commercial or financial information, which is privileged and confidential, the Disclosure Statement shall be protected and shall not be released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-8689].

## How it works

The contract price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 are not applicable to compliant cost accounting practice changes directly associated with external restructuring activities that are subject to and meet the requirements of section 2325 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-8685].

Under section 9903.202-2, the agency head may determine that it is impractical to secure the Disclosure Statement, although submission is required, and authorize contract award without obtaining the Statement [@CL-CMP-8686]. The agency head shall, within 30 days of having done so, submit a report to the Cost Accounting Standards Board setting forth all material facts [@CL-CMP-8687]. This authority may not be delegated [@CL-CMP-8688].

Under section 9903.202-4, if the offeror or contractor notifies the contracting officer that the Disclosure Statement contains trade secrets and commercial or financial information, which is privileged and confidential, the Disclosure Statement shall be protected and shall not be released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-8689].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

On 2026-03-02 an agency head determines that it is impractical to secure the Disclosure Statement for an award of 3,000,000, although submission is required, and authorizes award without obtaining the Statement [@CL-CMP-8686]. The report of all material facts to the Cost Accounting Standards Board is due within 30 days of having done so, that is, by 2026-04-01 on a count of 30 days from 2026-03-02 [@CL-CMP-8687]. A deputy cannot make the determination in the agency head's place, because the authority may not be delegated [@CL-CMP-8688]. The offeror separately notifies the contracting officer that the Statement contains trade secrets and commercial or financial information, which is privileged and confidential, so the Statement shall be protected and shall not be released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-8689].

## Never confuse

The agency head may determine that it is impractical to secure the Disclosure Statement, although submission is required, and authorize contract award without obtaining the Statement [@CL-CMP-8686]. Under FAR 30.202-6(b), where the agency head has authorized award without obtaining submission of the required Disclosure Statement, the contractor shall submit the required Disclosure Statement and the CFAO shall make a determination of adequacy as soon as possible after the award [@CL-CMP-7036; @CL-CMP-7037].

Where the offeror or contractor has notified the contracting officer that the Disclosure Statement contains privileged and confidential trade secrets and commercial or financial information, the Disclosure Statement shall be protected and shall not be released outside the Government [@CL-CMP-8689].
