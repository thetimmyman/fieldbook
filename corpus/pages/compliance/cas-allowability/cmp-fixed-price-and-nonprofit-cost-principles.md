## Purpose

Explains when the cost principles reach fixed-price work and which subpart supplies the principles for a contract with a nonprofit organization.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7500] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.102 [@CL-CMP-7500] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.102 [@CL-CMP-9595; @CL-CMP-9596; @CL-CMP-9597] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7500] |
| Applies to | Fixed-price contracts, subcontracts and modifications where cost analysis is performed or a clause requires costs to be determined or negotiated [@CL-CMP-7500] |

The applicable subparts of FAR part 31 shall be used in the pricing of a fixed-price contract, subcontract or modification to either, whenever cost analysis is performed or a fixed-price contract clause requires the determination or negotiation of costs [@CL-CMP-7500].

Application of cost principles to fixed-price contracts and subcontracts shall not be construed as a requirement to negotiate agreements on individual elements of cost in arriving at agreement on the total price, and the final price the parties accept reflects agreement only on the total price [@CL-CMP-7501; @CL-CMP-7502]. Notwithstanding the mandatory use of the principles, the objective will continue to be to negotiate prices that are fair and reasonable, cost and other factors considered [@CL-CMP-7503].

## How it works

The applicable subparts of part 31 shall be used in the pricing of fixed-price contracts, subcontracts, and modifications to contracts and subcontracts whenever (a) cost analysis is performed, or (b) a fixed-price contract clause requires the determination or negotiation of costs [@CL-CMP-7500].

Subpart 31.7 provides principles and standards for determining costs applicable to contracts with nonprofit organizations other than educational institutions (see subpart 31.3), State and local governments (see subpart 31.6) [@CL-CMP-7504], and those nonprofit organizations exempted under the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, appendix VIII (see subpart 31.2 for the cost principles applicable to nonprofit organizations exempt from the cost principles in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200) [@CL-CMP-7505].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.102(a), the applicable subparts of part 31 must be used in the pricing of fixed-price contracts, subcontracts, and modifications to contracts and subcontracts whenever cost analysis is performed, or a fixed-price contract clause requires the determination or negotiation of costs [@CL-CMP-9595]. Under RFO FAR 31.102(b)(1), the application of cost principles to fixed-price contracts and subcontracts must not be construed as a requirement to negotiate agreements on individual elements of cost in arriving at agreement on the total price [@CL-CMP-9596], and the final price accepted by the parties reflects agreement only on the total price [@CL-CMP-9597]. RFO FAR 31.108 says subpart 31.7 provides principles and standards for determining costs applicable to contracts and contract modifications with nonprofit organizations other than educational institutions (see subpart 31.3), State and local governments (see subpart 31.6) [@CL-CMP-9598], and those nonprofit organizations exempted under the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, appendix VIII (see subpart 31.2 for the cost principles applicable to nonprofit organizations exempt from the cost principles in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200) [@CL-CMP-9599].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price subcontract is priced with cost analysis, so the applicable subparts of FAR part 31 shall be used in pricing it [@CL-CMP-7500]. The parties agree a total price of 800,000 and sign nothing that addresses individual cost elements. Application of cost principles shall not be construed as a requirement to negotiate agreements on individual elements of cost [@CL-CMP-7501]. The final price accepted by the parties reflects agreement only on the total price [@CL-CMP-7502]. The parties still aim for a price that is fair and reasonable, cost and other factors considered [@CL-CMP-7503].

## Never confuse

Application of cost principles to fixed-price contracts and subcontracts shall not be construed as a requirement to negotiate agreements on individual elements of cost in arriving at agreement on the total price [@CL-CMP-7501]. The final price accepted by the parties reflects agreement only on the total price [@CL-CMP-7502]. Notwithstanding the mandatory use of cost principles, the objective will continue to be to negotiate prices that are fair and reasonable, cost and other factors considered [@CL-CMP-7503]. The codified text points to subpart 31.3 for educational institutions and subpart 31.6 for State and local governments [@CL-CMP-7504], and to subpart 31.2 for the cost principles applicable to nonprofit organizations exempt from the cost principles in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200 [@CL-CMP-7505]. For the other subparts, see [cost principles for commercial organizations](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-commercial-organizations.md) and [cost principles for State, local and tribal government contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-state-local-tribal-governments.md).
