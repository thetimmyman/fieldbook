## Purpose

Shows how pension cost is measured, assigned to cost accounting periods and allocated to cost objectives, with the limits and the exceptions for pay-as-you-go and nonqualified plans.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-CMP-3383] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.412-50 [@CL-CMP-3383] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3383] |
| Applies to | Defined-benefit pension plans, with the listed rules for pay-as-you-go and nonqualified plans [@CL-CMP-3387; @CL-CMP-3394] |

The portions of unfunded actuarial liability that the paragraph on components of pension cost goes on to list are included as a separately identified part of the pension cost of a cost accounting period, in equal annual installments [@CL-CMP-3383]. Each installment consists of an amortized portion of the unfunded actuarial liability plus an interest equivalent on the unamortized portion of such liability, and the period of amortization is established by the rules that follow [@CL-CMP-8041]. One listed rule sets the period: where amortization has not begun before the standard first becomes applicable to a contractor, the period begins with the period in which the standard becomes applicable and is no more than 30 years nor less than 10 years [@CL-CMP-3384]. That rule carries an exception: if the plan was in existence as of January 1, 1974, the period shall be no more than 40 years nor less than 10 years [@CL-CMP-3385]. Where amortization had already begun before the standard first became applicable, no change in the amortization period is required by the standard [@CL-CMP-3426]. A further listed rule amortizes each increase or decrease in unfunded actuarial liability that results from an assignable cost deficit or credit over a period of 10 years [@CL-CMP-3386].

For a defined-benefit plan accounted for under the pay-as-you-go cost method, the amount of pension cost assignable to a period is measured as the sum of listed components [@CL-CMP-3387]. The sum is made up of the net amount for any periodic benefits paid for that period and the level annual installment required to amortize over 15 years any amounts paid to irrevocably settle an obligation for periodic benefits due in current or future cost accounting periods [@CL-CMP-3388].

For a qualified defined-benefit plan, the pension cost measured for a period is assigned to that period subject to listed adjustments, in order of application [@CL-CMP-3389]. One adjustment assigns any amount measured below zero to future periods as an assignable cost credit and sets the amount assigned to the period at zero [@CL-CMP-3390]. Among the consequences listed for the case where the pension cost equals or exceeds the assignable cost limitation, the amount, as adjusted under the first adjustment, shall not exceed that limitation [@CL-CMP-3391], and all amounts described in 9904.412-50(a)(1) and 9904.413-50(a) which are required to be amortized shall be considered fully amortized [@CL-CMP-8042]. A third provides that an amount of pension cost of a qualified pension plan, adjusted pursuant to paragraphs (c)(2)(i) and (ii) of this subsection [@CL-CMP-7061], that exceeds the sum of the maximum tax-deductible amount, determined in accordance with the Internal Revenue Code, and the accumulated value of prepayment credits shall be assigned to future accounting periods as an assignable cost deficit [@CL-CMP-3427]. Separately, any portion of the adjusted pension cost that exceeds the amount required to be funded under a waiver granted under the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 shall not be assigned to the current period [@CL-CMP-3428].

The cost of a nonqualified defined-benefit plan is assigned in the same manner as for qualified plans, apart from the paragraph on the tax-deductible cap, which is the third adjustment above, under listed conditions [@CL-CMP-3392; @CL-CMP-3427]. The conditions are that the contractor, in disclosing or establishing its cost accounting practices, elects to have the plan so accounted for, that the plan is funded through the use of a funding agency, and that the right to a pension benefit is nonforfeitable and is communicated to the participants [@CL-CMP-3393]. A nonqualified plan that does not meet all of the listed requirements is assigned using the pay-as-you-go cost method [@CL-CMP-3394].

Except for nonqualified defined-benefit plans, the costs of a pension plan assigned to a cost accounting period are allocable to the extent that they are funded [@CL-CMP-3395]. Funding of pension cost is considered to have taken place within the cost accounting period if it is accomplished by the corporate tax filing date for such period including any permissible extensions [@CL-CMP-3429]. An excise tax assessed because of excess, inadequate, or delayed funding of a pension plan is not a component of pension cost [@CL-CMP-3430].

## How it works

Pension cost is measured first, then assigned to a cost accounting period subject to adjustments, and then, for plans other than nonqualified plans, allocated to cost objectives to the extent it is funded [@CL-CMP-3389; @CL-CMP-3395]. A nonqualified plan that does not meet the listed requirements falls back to the pay-as-you-go cost method [@CL-CMP-3393; @CL-CMP-3394].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A nonqualified plan fails the funding condition, so it uses the pay-as-you-go cost method. In the year, benefits paid are 100 and the installment amortizing an earlier lump-sum settlement is 12. The assignable pension cost for the year is 112.

A qualified plan has a computed pension cost of 30, an assignable cost limitation of 25, no prepayment credits and a maximum tax-deductible amount well above 25. The assigned cost cannot exceed 25. The plan funds 20, so 20 is allocable to cost objectives.

## Never confuse

For a qualified defined-benefit plan, the pension cost measured for a cost accounting period is assigned to that period subject to the listed adjustments [@CL-CMP-3389]. Except for nonqualified defined-benefit plans, the costs of a pension plan assigned to a cost accounting period are allocable to the extent that they are funded [@CL-CMP-3395]. When the pension cost equals or exceeds the assignable cost limitation, the amount of pension cost, adjusted pursuant to paragraph (c)(2)(i), shall not exceed the limitation [@CL-CMP-3391].
