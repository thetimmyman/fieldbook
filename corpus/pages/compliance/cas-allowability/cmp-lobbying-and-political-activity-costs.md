## Purpose

Governs the unallowability of political campaign, election, and legislative lobbying expenses and requires their separate identification in indirect cost proposals.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0311] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-22 [@CL-CMP-0311] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-22 [@CL-CMP-8556; @CL-CMP-8560] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0311] |
| Applies to | Government contracts subject to the cost principles [@CL-CMP-0311] |

Under codified FAR 31.205-22(a), costs associated with the following activities are unallowable [@CL-CMP-8811]. The listed activities include (1) attempts to influence the outcomes of any Federal, State, or local election, referendum, initiative, or similar procedure, through in kind or cash contributions, endorsements, publicity, or similar activities [@CL-CMP-0311]; (2) establishing, administering, contributing to, or paying the expenses of a political party, campaign, political action committee, or other organization established for the purpose of influencing the outcomes of elections [@CL-CMP-8812]; (3) any attempt to influence (i) the introduction of Federal, state, or local legislation, or (ii) the enactment or modification of any pending Federal, state, or local legislation through communication with any member or employee of the Congress or state legislature [@CL-CMP-8586]; and (4) any attempt to influence (i) the introduction of Federal, state, or local legislation, or (ii) the enactment or modification of any pending Federal, state, or local legislation by preparing, distributing or using publicity or propaganda [@CL-CMP-8587]. The following activities are excepted from the coverage of paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-8377]: (1) providing a technical and factual presentation of information on a topic directly related to the performance of a contract through hearing testimony, statements or letters to the Congress or a state legislature, or subdivision, member, or cognizant staff member thereof, in response to a documented request (including a Congressional Record notice requesting testimony or statements for the record at a regularly scheduled hearing) [@CL-CMP-0312; @CL-CMP-0819] made by the recipient member, legislative body or subdivision, or a cognizant staff member thereof; provided such information is readily obtainable and can be readily put in deliverable form [@CL-CMP-0820]; and further provided that costs under this section for transportation, lodging or meals are unallowable unless incurred for the purpose of offering testimony at a regularly scheduled Congressional hearing [@CL-CMP-0821] pursuant to a written request for such presentation made by the Chairman or Ranking Minority Member of the Committee or Subcommittee conducting such hearing [@CL-CMP-0822]. When a contractor seeks reimbursement for indirect costs, total lobbying costs shall be separately identified in the indirect cost rate proposal, and thereafter treated as other unallowable activity costs [@CL-CMP-0313]. Contractors shall maintain adequate records to demonstrate that the certification of costs as being allowable or unallowable (see 42.703-2) pursuant to this subsection complies with the requirements of this subsection [@CL-CMP-0314].

## How it works

Costs associated with attempts to influence the outcomes of any Federal, State, or local election, referendum, initiative, or similar procedure are unallowable [@CL-CMP-8811; @CL-CMP-0311]. Codified paragraphs (a)(3) and (a)(4) list any attempt to influence legislation through communication with any member or employee of the Congress or state legislature [@CL-CMP-8586] and any attempt to influence legislation by preparing, distributing or using publicity or propaganda [@CL-CMP-8587]. Paragraph (b) lists the activities excepted from the coverage of paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-8377]: (1) providing a technical and factual presentation of information on a topic directly related to the performance of a contract through hearing testimony, statements or letters to the Congress or a state legislature, or subdivision, member, or cognizant staff member thereof, in response to a documented request (including a Congressional Record notice requesting testimony or statements for the record at a regularly scheduled hearing) [@CL-CMP-0312; @CL-CMP-0819] made by the recipient member, legislative body or subdivision, or a cognizant staff member thereof; provided such information is readily obtainable and can be readily put in deliverable form [@CL-CMP-0820]; and further provided that costs under this section for transportation, lodging or meals are unallowable unless incurred for the purpose of offering testimony at a regularly scheduled Congressional hearing [@CL-CMP-0821] pursuant to a written request for such presentation made by the Chairman or Ranking Minority Member of the Committee or Subcommittee conducting such hearing [@CL-CMP-0822]; and (2) any lobbying made unallowable by paragraph (a)(3) of the subsection to influence state or local legislation in order to directly reduce contract cost, or to avoid material impairment of the contractor's authority to perform the contract [@CL-CMP-8588]. When a contractor seeks reimbursement for indirect costs, total lobbying costs shall be separately identified in the indirect cost rate proposal, and thereafter treated as other unallowable activity costs [@CL-CMP-0313]. Contractors shall maintain adequate records to demonstrate that the certification of costs as being allowable or unallowable (see 42.703-2) pursuant to this subsection complies with the requirements of this subsection [@CL-CMP-0314].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-22(a), costs associated with the following activities are unallowable: (1) attempts to influence the outcomes of any Federal, State, or local election, referendum, initiative, or similar procedure, through in-kind or cash contributions, endorsements, publicity, or similar activities [@CL-CMP-8556]. Paragraph (a)(3) covers any attempt to influence the introduction of Federal, state, or local legislation, or enactment or modification of any pending Federal, state, or local legislation (i) through communication with any member or employee of the Congress or state legislature [@CL-CMP-8557], including efforts to influence state or local officials to engage in similar lobbying activity, or with any government official or employee in connection with a decision to sign or veto enrolled legislation [@CL-CMP-8558]; or (ii) by preparing, distributing or using publicity or propaganda, or by urging members of the general public or any segment thereof to contribute to or participate in any mass demonstration, march, rally, fund raising drive, lobbying campaign or letter writing or telephone campaign [@CL-CMP-8559]. Under RFO paragraph (b), costs associated with the following activities are excepted from paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-8560]: (1) providing a technical and factual presentation of information on a topic directly related to the performance of a contract through a hearing testimony, statements, or letters to the Congress or a state legislature, or subdivision, member, or cognizant staff member thereof [@CL-CMP-8561], in response to a documented request (including a Congressional Record notice requesting testimony or statements for the record at a regularly scheduled hearing) [@CL-CMP-8562] made by the recipient member, legislative body, or subdivision, or a cognizant staff member thereof, provided such information is readily obtainable and can be readily put in deliverable form [@CL-CMP-8563]; and provided that costs under this section for transportation, lodging or meals are unallowable unless incurred for the purpose of offering testimony at a regularly scheduled Congressional hearing [@CL-CMP-8564] pursuant to a written request for such presentation made by the Chairman or Ranking Minority Member of the Committee or Subcommittee conducting such hearing [@CL-CMP-8565]; and (2) any lobbying made unallowable by paragraph (a)(3) of this section to influence state or local legislation in order to directly reduce contract cost, or to avoid material impairment of the contractor's authority to perform the contract [@CL-CMP-8566]. Under RFO paragraphs (c) and (d), when a contractor seeks reimbursement for indirect costs, total lobbying costs must be separately identified in the indirect cost rate proposal, and thereafter treated as other unallowable activity costs [@CL-CMP-8567], and contractors must maintain adequate records to demonstrate that the certification of costs as being allowable or unallowable (see 42.703-2) pursuant to this section complies with the requirements of this section [@CL-CMP-8568].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor incurs 80,000 for government affairs personnel advocating for pending legislative appropriations, and 15,000 for travel and preparation to deliver technical contract progress testimony requested in writing by a congressional committee chairman. The 80,000 in legislative lobbying expense is unallowable and must be separately identified in the indirect rate submission. The 15,000 is allowable under the documented legislative request exception only if the information is readily obtainable and the travel is to offer testimony at a regularly scheduled Congressional hearing under the committee chairman's written request; otherwise the travel part is unallowable.

## Never confuse

Codified paragraph (a)(3) concerns attempts to influence legislation through communication with any member or employee of the Congress or state legislature [@CL-CMP-8586], and paragraph (a)(4) concerns attempts to influence legislation by preparing, distributing or using publicity or propaganda [@CL-CMP-8587]. Paragraph (b)(1) concerns a technical and factual presentation of information on a topic directly related to the performance of a contract, in response to a documented request, provided such information is readily obtainable and can be readily put in deliverable form [@CL-CMP-0312; @CL-CMP-0819; @CL-CMP-0820]. See [Unallowable-cost penalties](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md) for the consequences of including unallowable costs in indirect cost proposals.

In the RFO text, paragraph (a)(3) has an item (i) on communication with any member or employee of the Congress or state legislature [@CL-CMP-8557] and an item (ii) on preparing, distributing or using publicity or propaganda [@CL-CMP-8559], and paragraph (b)(2) refers to lobbying made unallowable by paragraph (a)(3) [@CL-CMP-8566]. In the codified text, paragraph (b)(2) refers to lobbying made unallowable by paragraph (a)(3) [@CL-CMP-8588], which concerns communication with any member or employee of the Congress or state legislature [@CL-CMP-8586].
