## Purpose

Collects the DoD rules on compensation and fringe benefits, the limits on indirect cost reimbursement for institutions of higher education, and the principles that apply to educational institutions, with a pointer to the card on State, local and tribal governments.

### DFARS 231.205-6: compensation

Costs for bonuses or other payments in excess of the normal salary paid by the contractor to an employee, that are part of restructuring costs associated with a business combination, are unallowable under DoD contracts funded by fiscal year 1996 or subsequent appropriations [@CL-CMP-7531]. The paragraph is stated in accordance with Section 8122 of Public Law 104-61 and similar sections in subsequent Defense appropriations acts [@CL-CMP-7636]. This limitation does not apply to severance payments or early retirement incentive payments [@CL-CMP-7532]. The definitions of business combination and restructuring costs are at DFARS 231.205-70(b) [@CL-CMP-7635]. Fringe benefit costs that are contrary to law, employer-employee agreement, or an established policy of the contractor are unallowable [@CL-CMP-7533].

### DFARS 231.303: institutions of higher education

No limitation may be placed on the reimbursement of otherwise allowable indirect costs incurred by an institution of higher education under a DoD contract awarded on or after November 30, 1993 [@CL-CMP-7534], unless that same limitation is applied uniformly to all other organizations performing similar work under DoD contracts [@CL-CMP-7535]. The 26 percent limitation imposed on administrative indirect costs by OMB Circular A-21 shall not be applied to DoD contracts awarded on or after November 30, 1993, to institutions of higher education, because the same limitation is not applied to other organizations performing similar work [@CL-CMP-7536]. The cognizant administrative contracting officer may waive the prohibition if the governing body of the institution requests the waiver to simplify the institution's overall management of DoD cost reimbursements under DoD contracts [@CL-CMP-7537]. Under the statute the section cites, the costs cited in DFARS 231.205-22(a) are unallowable [@CL-CMP-7538].

### FAR 31.302: educational institutions

The OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix III, provides principles for determining the costs applicable to research and development, training, and other work performed by educational institutions under contracts with the Government [@CL-CMP-7539]. Educational institutions are defined as institutions of higher education in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart A, and in the statute cited there [@CL-CMP-7540].

## How it works

A restructuring bonus or other excess payment that is part of business-combination restructuring costs is unallowable on covered DoD contracts, while severance and early retirement incentive payments are outside that limitation [@CL-CMP-7531; @CL-CMP-7532]. Whether a payment is a bonus within the limitation depends on the definitions of business combination and restructuring costs, which are not in these sections [@CL-CMP-7635]. The fringe benefit rule turns on three tests: contrary to law, contrary to an employer-employee agreement, or contrary to an established policy of the contractor, and any one makes the cost unallowable [@CL-CMP-7533]. No official decides either rule in these paragraphs.

The education rule turns on uniformity: the prohibition on limiting indirect cost reimbursement gives way where the same limitation is applied uniformly to all other organizations performing similar work under DoD contracts [@CL-CMP-7535]. The 26 percent administrative cap is a separate sentence with its own reason, and it is not to be applied to these institutions on DoD contracts awarded on or after November 30, 1993 [@CL-CMP-7536]. A waiver is a second route. The decision belongs to the cognizant administrative contracting officer, who may waive the prohibition if the institution's governing body requests it to simplify the institution's overall management of DoD cost reimbursements [@CL-CMP-7537]. The paragraph does not set a deadline. The costs cited in DFARS 231.205-22(a) are also unallowable under the statute that DFARS 231.303 cites [@CL-CMP-7538].

For educational institutions the cost principles come from the OMB Uniform Guidance, and institutions of higher education are defined there and in the statute [@CL-CMP-7539; @CL-CMP-7540]. The cited sections contain no operative rule for State, local and tribal governments; see the linked card for their cost principles.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An institution of higher education bills 900,000 of otherwise allowable indirect cost under a DoD contract awarded after November 30, 1993 [@CL-CMP-7534]. An agency proposes to hold administrative indirect cost to the 26 percent limitation in OMB Circular A-21. That limitation shall not be applied to this contract [@CL-CMP-7536]. The agency then proposes a different cap that it applies to no other organization doing similar work. No limitation may be placed on reimbursement of the otherwise allowable indirect cost, because the limitation is not applied uniformly to all those organizations [@CL-CMP-7534; @CL-CMP-7535]. If the institution's governing body asks the cognizant administrative contracting officer to waive the prohibition to simplify the institution's overall management of DoD cost reimbursements, the officer may waive it [@CL-CMP-7537].

In a second case, a contractor pays 30,000 to an employee as a bonus in excess of normal salary, as part of restructuring costs of a business combination, under a DoD contract funded by fiscal year 1996 appropriations. The bonus is unallowable [@CL-CMP-7531]. A 10,000 severance payment in the same restructuring is outside that limitation [@CL-CMP-7532].

## Never confuse

The 26 percent administrative indirect cost limitation is not a general DoD cap: the section says it shall not be applied to these contracts, because other organizations are not subject to the same limitation [@CL-CMP-7536]. Disallowing a restructuring bonus is not the same as disallowing a severance payment, because severance and early retirement incentives sit outside that limitation [@CL-CMP-7531; @CL-CMP-7532]. The principles that FAR 31.302 gives for educational institutions are those of the OMB Uniform Guidance [@CL-CMP-7539]; see [cost principles for commercial organizations](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-commercial-organizations.md), [cost principles for State, local and tribal governments](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-state-local-tribal-governments.md) and [allowable compensation for personal services](../cas-allowability/cmp-compensation-personal-services.md). For restructuring costs after a business combination, see [DoD external restructuring costs](../../mna/valuation/mna-dod-external-restructuring-costs.md).
