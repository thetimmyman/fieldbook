## Purpose

Sets out the codified FAR text and the overhaul (RFO) text on losses on other contracts and plant protection costs, and three DoD provisions: the clause requirements for insurance costs, the prescription for the supplemental cost principles clause, and the statutory unallowability stated in two further DFARS sections, one in the subpart for contracts with State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments and one in the subpart for contracts with nonprofit organizations [@CL-CMP-8697; @CL-CMP-8699; @CL-CMP-8701; @CL-CMP-8702; @CL-CMP-8703; @CL-CMP-8704; @CL-CMP-8709; @CL-CMP-8710].

Losses on other contracts. The codified section says that an excess of costs over income under any other contract, including the contractor's contributed portion under cost-sharing contracts, is unallowable [@CL-CMP-8697]. The RFO section says that losses on other contracts, including the contractor's contributed portion under cost-sharing contracts, are unallowable [@CL-CMP-8698].

Plant protection costs. The codified section says that costs of items such as wages, uniforms and equipment of personnel engaged in plant protection, depreciation on plant protection capital assets, and necessary expenses to comply with military requirements are allowable [@CL-CMP-8699]. The RFO section says that allowable plant protection costs include the same three kinds of cost [@CL-CMP-8700]. The codified text says "items such as" [@CL-CMP-8699] and the RFO text says "include" [@CL-CMP-8700].

Insurance (DFARS 231.205-19(e)): In addition to the cost limitations in FAR 31.205-19(e), self-insurance and purchased insurance costs are subject to the requirements of the clauses at 252.217-7012, Liability and Insurance, and 252.228-7001, Ground and Flight Risk [@CL-CMP-8701].

Clause prescription (DFARS 231.100-70): The instruction is to use the clause at 252.231-7000, Supplemental Cost Principles, in all solicitations and contracts which are subject to the principles and procedures described in FAR subpart 31.1, 31.2, 31.6 or 31.7 [@CL-CMP-8702].

Statutory unallowability (DFARS 231.603 and 231.703): Each section says that, under section 4652 of title 10 of the United States Code, the costs cited in 231.205-22(a) are unallowable [@CL-CMP-8703; @CL-CMP-8704]. Those costs are the costs associated with preparing any material, report, list, or analysis on the actual or projected economic or employment impact in a particular State or congressional district [@CL-CMP-8705], where the impact is that of an acquisition program for which all research, development, testing, and evaluation has not been completed, and that paragraph calls them also unallowable [@CL-CMP-8706]. DFARS 231.603 is printed in the subpart for contracts with State, local, and federally recognized Indian tribal governments [@CL-CMP-8709], and DFARS 231.703 in the subpart for contracts with nonprofit organizations [@CL-CMP-8710].

## How it works

Losses on another contract are unallowable under the codified rule, which reaches an excess of costs over income under any other contract, including the contractor's contributed portion under cost-sharing contracts [@CL-CMP-8697]. The RFO text states the rule in terms of losses on other contracts [@CL-CMP-8698]. A DoD class deviation directs contracting officers to use the RFO part 31 text in place of the codified part 31 text [@CL-CMP-9350], and Revision 1 of that class deviation took effect immediately [@CL-CMP-9351].

Plant protection costs of the listed kinds are allowable under both texts [@CL-CMP-8699; @CL-CMP-8700].

For DoD, self-insurance and purchased insurance costs are subject to the requirements of the clauses named in DFARS 231.205-19(e) in addition to the cost limitations in FAR 31.205-19(e) [@CL-CMP-8701]. The FAR insurance rules are on [Insurance and indemnification](../cas-allowability/cmp-insurance-and-indemnification.md). The supplemental cost principles clause is prescribed for solicitations and contracts under the FAR cost principles subparts named in DFARS 231.100-70 [@CL-CMP-8702]; the clause itself is on [Supplemental cost principles and data other than certified data from the Canadian Commercial Corporation](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-dfars-supplemental-cost-principles-and-ccc-data.md).

DFARS 231.603 appears in the subpart for contracts with State, local, and federally recognized Indian tribal governments [@CL-CMP-8709] and DFARS 231.703 in the subpart for contracts with nonprofit organizations [@CL-CMP-8710]; each says that, under section 4652 of title 10 of the United States Code, the costs cited in 231.205-22(a) are unallowable [@CL-CMP-8703; @CL-CMP-8704]. For the FAR lobbying principle, see [Lobbying and political activity costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-lobbying-and-political-activity-costs.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company's costs on another contract exceed that contract's income by 25,000, of which 6,000 is its own contributed portion under a cost-sharing contract. Under the codified section the whole 25,000 excess is unallowable, including the 6,000 [@CL-CMP-8697]. Under the RFO text, if the 25,000 is a loss on another contract, that text also calls it unallowable, including the contributed portion [@CL-CMP-8698]. On the current contract the company spends 8,000 on guard wages and plant protection equipment. Those are items of the kind the plant protection sections call allowable [@CL-CMP-8699; @CL-CMP-8700]. The example tests no other allowability rule. On a DoD contract, if the company also buys insurance, the cost is subject to the clause requirements at 252.217-7012 and 252.228-7001 in addition to the FAR cost limitations in FAR 31.205-19(e) [@CL-CMP-8701].

## Never confuse

The plant protection sections call listed kinds of cost allowable [@CL-CMP-8699; @CL-CMP-8700], and the loss sections call an excess of costs over income under another contract unallowable [@CL-CMP-8697; @CL-CMP-8698].

The codified text speaks of an excess of costs over income under any other contract [@CL-CMP-8697]. The unallowable cost card at [Accounting for unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-405-unallowable-costs.md) covers the separate unallowable cost classes.
