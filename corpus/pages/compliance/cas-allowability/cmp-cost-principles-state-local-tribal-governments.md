## Purpose

Shows what the requirements are for contracts that rely on the State, local and tribal government subpart, and which other guidance decides allowability.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2740] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.603 [@CL-CMP-2740] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.603 [@CL-CMP-9590; @CL-CMP-9591; @CL-CMP-9592; @CL-CMP-9593] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2740] |
| Applies to | Contracts that refer to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs under contracts with State, local and Indian tribal governments [@CL-CMP-2740] |

Contracts that refer to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs under contracts with State, local and Indian tribal governments shall be deemed to refer to, and shall have the allowability of costs determined by the contracting officer in accordance with, the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII, in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-2740; @CL-CMP-2741].

Agencies are not expected to place additional restrictions on individual items of cost [@CL-CMP-2742]. However, under section 3744 of title 10, section 4304 of title 41, section 3730 of title 31, and section 4310 of title 41 of the United States Code, the following costs are unallowable [@CL-CMP-2742]. Three of the listed items are costs of any membership in any social, dining, or country club or organization, costs of alcoholic beverages, and contributions or donations regardless of the recipient [@CL-CMP-2743].

## How it works

A contract that refers to the subpart is deemed to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII, in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-2740; @CL-CMP-2741]. Paragraph (a) sets that reference and paragraph (b) sets the list of unallowable items [@CL-CMP-2740; @CL-CMP-2742]. Some listed items carry exceptions; for example, item (15) applies unless any of the exceptions at 31.205-47(c) or (d) apply [@CL-CMP-9606]. For related FAR cost principles, see [Alcoholic beverages are unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-alcoholic-beverages.md) and [Contributions and donations as unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-contributions-donations.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.603(a), contracts that refer to the subpart for determining allowable costs under contracts with State, local and Indian tribal governments must be deemed to refer to [@CL-CMP-9590], and must have the allowability of costs determined by the contracting officer in accordance with, the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII, in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-9591]. RFO paragraph (b) states that agencies are not expected to place additional restrictions on individual items of cost, and paragraph (c) says that in accordance with section 3744 of title 10, section 4304 of title 41, section 3730 of title 31, and section 4310 of title 41 of the United States Code, the following are unallowable [@CL-CMP-9592], where the codified paragraph (b) continues with the word However before the statutes and says the following costs are unallowable [@CL-CMP-2742]. RFO paragraph (c)(5) to (7) are costs of any membership in any social, dining, or country club or organization, costs of alcoholic beverages, and contributions or donations regardless of the recipient [@CL-CMP-9593], which are items (b)(5) to (7) in the codified text [@CL-CMP-2743]. Among the unallowable items, RFO paragraph (c)(14)(i) is, unless an exception at 31.205-47(c) or (d) applies, costs incurred by a contractor in connection with any criminal, civil, or administrative proceedings that result in dispositions described at 31.205-47(b)(1) through (5) commenced by [@CL-CMP-9594], where codified item (b)(15) is, unless any of the exceptions at 31.205-47(c) or (d) apply, costs incurred by a contractor or subcontractor in connection with such proceedings commenced by [@CL-CMP-9606]. It covers, unless an exception at 31.205-47(c) or (d) applies, costs incurred by a contractor in connection with any criminal, civil, or administrative proceedings that result in dispositions described at 31.205-47(b)(1) through (5) commenced by one of the parties the item goes on to list [@CL-CMP-9594], where the codified item covers costs incurred by a contractor or subcontractor, with the same lead-in to a list of who commenced the proceeding [@CL-CMP-9606].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A State agency contract refers to the subpart for allowable costs. The contractor, a local government unit, includes 700 of alcoholic beverages and 3,000 of ordinary supplies in its costs. The answer: the beverages are unallowable under the listed items, and the supplies are tested under the Uniform Guidance in effect on the date of the contract.

## Never confuse

For a contract that refers to this subpart, the allowability of costs is determined by the contracting officer in accordance with the OMB Uniform Guidance in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-2740; @CL-CMP-2741]. The unallowable items listed in paragraph (b) are costs that are unallowable under the cited statutes [@CL-CMP-2742]. For the FAR allowability factors used on other contracts, see [Factors determining cost allowability](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md). The commercial-organization category covers contracts negotiated with organizations other than educational institutions, construction and architect-engineer contracts, State and local governments and nonprofit organizations on the basis of cost [@CL-CMP-2706; @CL-CMP-2707], and the construction category covers contracts negotiated on the basis of cost with organizations other than educational institutions, State and local governments, and nonprofit organizations except those exempted under OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, appendix VIII [@CL-CMP-2712]; see [Cost principles for contracts with commercial organizations](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-commercial-organizations.md) and [Cost principles for construction and architect-engineer contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-construction-architect-engineer.md). The definition of cost objective in FAR 31.001 [@CL-CMP-2703] reads cost objective means (except for subpart 31.6) a function, organizational subdivision, contract, or other work unit for which cost data are desired and for which provision is made to accumulate and measure the cost of processes, products, jobs, capitalized projects, etc. [@CL-CMP-2703]; see [Definitions used in the FAR cost principles](../cas-allowability/cmp-far-cost-principles-definitions.md).

The codified text lists the unallowable items in paragraph (b), with memberships, alcoholic beverages and contributions at (b)(5) to (7) [@CL-CMP-2743], while the RFO text lists them in paragraph (c), at (c)(5) to (7) [@CL-CMP-9593].
