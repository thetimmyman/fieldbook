## Purpose

Shows what the requirements are for contracts that rely on the State, local and tribal government subpart, and which other guidance decides allowability.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2740] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.603 [@CL-CMP-2740] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.603 [@CL-CMP-9590; @CL-CMP-9591; @CL-CMP-9592; @CL-CMP-9593] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2740] |
| Applies to | Contracts that refer to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs under contracts with State, local and Indian tribal governments [@CL-CMP-2740] |

Contracts that refer to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs under contracts with State, local and Indian tribal governments are deemed to refer to the guidance that the rest of the same sentence names [@CL-CMP-2740]. That sentence names the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII, in effect on the date of the contract, as the guidance in accordance with which the contracting officer determines allowability [@CL-CMP-2741].

Agencies are not expected to place additional restrictions on individual items of cost, but under the four statutes the sentence lists, the costs in the items that follow are unallowable [@CL-CMP-2742]. Three listed items are costs of any membership in any social, dining, or country club or organization, costs of alcoholic beverages, and contributions or donations regardless of the recipient [@CL-CMP-2743].

## How it works

A contract's reference to the subpart is treated as a reference to the Uniform Guidance, and the date of the contract fixes the version of the guidance [@CL-CMP-2740; @CL-CMP-2741]. The section itself sets the reference in its first paragraph and the list of unallowable items in its second, and it does not set out the rules of the Uniform Guidance. Some of the listed unallowable items carry their own exceptions or conditions, so each item should be read in the section before it is applied. The listed items overlap with cost principles on other cards, such as [Alcoholic beverages are unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-alcoholic-beverages.md) and [Contributions and donations as unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-contributions-donations.md), but those cards cover the corresponding FAR cost principles, not this section.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.603(a) keeps the reference rule: contracts that refer to the subpart for determining allowable costs under contracts with State, local and Indian tribal governments must be deemed to refer to the guidance the paragraph names [@CL-CMP-9590], and must have allowability determined by the contracting officer in accordance with the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII, in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-9591]. The list of unallowable items moves: RFO paragraph (b) states that agencies are not expected to place additional restrictions on individual items of cost, and paragraph (c) introduces the list, and the codified word however does not appear [@CL-CMP-9592; @CL-CMP-2742]. The memberships, alcoholic beverages and contributions items keep the numbers (5) to (7) but are now in paragraph (c), not (b) [@CL-CMP-9593; @CL-CMP-2743]. The RFO proceedings item is numbered (14)(i), where the codified item is (15) [@CL-CMP-9594; @CL-CMP-9606]. It covers, unless an exception at 31.205-47(c) or (d) applies, costs incurred by a contractor in connection with any criminal, civil, or administrative proceedings that result in dispositions described at 31.205-47(b)(1) through (5) [@CL-CMP-9594], where the codified item covers costs incurred by a contractor or subcontractor [@CL-CMP-9606].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A State agency contract refers to the subpart for allowable costs. The contractor, a local government unit, includes 700 of alcoholic beverages and 3,000 of ordinary supplies in its costs. The answer: the beverages are unallowable under the listed items, and the supplies are tested under the Uniform Guidance in effect on the date of the contract.

## Never confuse

For a contract that refers to this subpart, allowability is determined in accordance with the Uniform Guidance, not by the commercial-organization card [@CL-CMP-2740; @CL-CMP-2741]. The unallowable items listed in the section are not the whole test, because the rest of allowability comes from that guidance [@CL-CMP-2742; @CL-CMP-2741]. For the FAR allowability factors used on other contracts, see [Factors determining cost allowability](../cas-allowability/cmp-allowability-factors.md). Both the commercial-organization category and the construction category exclude State and local governments [@CL-CMP-2707; @CL-CMP-2712]; see [Cost principles for contracts with commercial organizations](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-commercial-organizations.md) and [Cost principles for construction and architect-engineer contracts](../cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-construction-architect-engineer.md). Several definitions in the cost principles carry a carve-out for this subpart [@CL-CMP-2703]; see [Definitions used in the FAR cost principles](../cas-allowability/cmp-far-cost-principles-definitions.md).

The codified text lists the unallowable items in paragraph (b), with memberships, alcoholic beverages and contributions at (b)(5) to (7) [@CL-CMP-2743], while the RFO text lists them in paragraph (c), at (c)(5) to (7) [@CL-CMP-9593].

## Company calibration

Whether any contract with a State, local or tribal government refers to this subpart, and who confirms the guidance version in effect on its date. [@Q-004]
