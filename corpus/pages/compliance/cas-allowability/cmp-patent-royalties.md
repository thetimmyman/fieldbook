## Purpose

Shows when a royalty paid for the use of a patent, or amortization of the cost of buying patent rights, can be charged to a Government contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0857] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-37 [@CL-CMP-0857] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-37 [@CL-CMP-9477; @CL-CMP-9482] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0857] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Royalties on a patent, and amortization of the cost of purchasing a patent or patent rights, are allowable where they are necessary for the proper performance of the contract and applicable to contract products or processes, unless one of the following applies [@CL-CMP-0857]: the Government has a license or the right to a free use of the patent [@CL-CMP-0858]; the patent has been adjudicated invalid or administratively determined to be invalid [@CL-CMP-0857; @CL-CMP-0859]; the patent is considered unenforceable [@CL-CMP-0857; @CL-CMP-0860]; or the patent is expired [@CL-CMP-0857; @CL-CMP-0860].

Where the patent was formerly owned by the contractor, the royalty amount allowed should not exceed the cost that would have been allowed had the contractor retained title [@CL-CMP-0862].

## How it works

Care should be exercised in determining reasonableness when the royalties may have been arrived at as a result of less-than-arm's-length bargaining [@CL-CMP-0861]; examples are royalties paid to persons, including corporations, affiliated with the contractor, royalties paid to unaffiliated parties, including corporations, under an agreement entered into in contemplation that a Government contract would be awarded, or royalties paid under an agreement entered into after the contract award [@CL-CMP-1400]. FAR 31.205-37(d) says to see 31.109 regarding advance agreements [@CL-CMP-1404].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. Under RFO FAR 31.205-37(a), royalties on a patent or amortization of the cost of purchasing a patent or patent rights necessary for the proper performance of the contract and applicable to contract products or processes are allowable [@CL-CMP-9477] unless (1) the Government has a license or the right to a free use of the patent [@CL-CMP-9478]; (2) the patent has been adjudicated to be invalid, or has been administratively determined to be invalid [@CL-CMP-9478]; (3) the patent is considered to be unenforceable [@CL-CMP-9478]; or (4) the patent is expired [@CL-CMP-9478]. Under paragraph (b), care should be exercised in determining reasonableness when the royalties may have been arrived at as a result of less-than-arm's-length bargaining, including royalties (1) paid to persons, including corporations, affiliated with the contractor [@CL-CMP-9479]; (2) paid to unaffiliated parties, including corporations, under an agreement entered into in contemplation that a Government contract would be awarded [@CL-CMP-9480]; or (3) paid under an agreement entered into after the contract award [@CL-CMP-9480]. Under paragraph (c), the royalty amount allowed for a patent formerly owned by a contractor should not exceed the cost that would have been allowed had the contractor retained title [@CL-CMP-9481].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The company pays a 40,000 royalty on a patent needed to build the contract product, bargained at arm's length. The Government holds no license, and the patent is valid, enforceable and unexpired. The royalty is allowable. If the same 40,000 went to an affiliated company under an agreement signed in contemplation of the award, care should be exercised in determining reasonableness.

## Never confuse

For patent costs, see [Patent costs](cmp-patent-costs.md). FAR 31.205-37(a) covers both royalties on a patent and amortization of the cost of purchasing a patent or patent rights [@CL-CMP-0857]. For advance agreements, see [Advance agreements](../cas-allowability/cmp-advance-agreements.md). Codified FAR 31.205-37(d) says to see 31.109 regarding advance agreements [@CL-CMP-1404]. In the RFO text, 31.110 is titled Advance agreements [@CL-CMP-9485].

## Company calibration

Whether each royalty the company pays was bargained at arm's length and whether an advance agreement covers it. [@Q-043]
