## Purpose

Identifies interest on borrowings, bond discounts, and capital financing expenses as unallowable costs on government contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0309] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-20 [@CL-CMP-0309] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-20 [@CL-CMP-8552; @CL-CMP-8553; @CL-CMP-8554] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0309] |
| Applies to | Government contracts subject to the cost principles [@CL-CMP-0309] |

Interest on borrowings however represented, bond discounts, costs of financing and refinancing capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities), legal and professional fees paid in connection with preparing prospectuses, and costs of preparing and issuing stock rights are unallowable (but see 31.205-28) [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. However, interest assessed by State or local taxing authorities under the conditions specified in 31.205-41(a)(3) of the codified text is allowable [@CL-CMP-0310].

## How it works

Interest on borrowings is unallowable under the section [@CL-CMP-0309]. The prohibition covers interest on borrowings however represented, and bond discounts (but see 31.205-28) [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. Costs of financing and refinancing capital, legal and professional fees paid in connection with preparing prospectuses, and costs of preparing and issuing stock rights are unallowable (but see 31.205-28) [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. An exception permits interest assessed by State or local taxing authorities under the conditions specified in 31.205-41(a)(3) of the codified text [@CL-CMP-0310].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. The RFO text of 31.205-20 says in paragraph (a) that interest on borrowings however represented, bond discounts, and costs of financing and refinancing capital are unallowable, and sets out the other unallowable costs as separate list items [@CL-CMP-8552; @CL-CMP-8553]. Its cross-reference to 31.205-28 appears only on the items for legal and professional fees paid in connection with preparing prospectuses and costs of preparing and issuing stock rights [@CL-CMP-8553]. Paragraph (b) says interest assessed by State or local taxing authorities under the conditions specified in 31.205-41(b)(3) is allowable [@CL-CMP-8554], and RFO 31.205-41(b)(3) covers interest or penalties incurred by the contractor for non-payment of any tax at the direction of the contracting officer or by reason of the failure of the contracting officer to ensure timely direction after a prompt request [@CL-CMP-8555].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor takes out a commercial line of credit to finance working capital during contract performance, incurring 45,000 in bank interest, 10,000 in financing loan fees, and 5,000 in interest assessed by a municipal taxing authority on contested local property taxes meeting all tax cost principle conditions. The 45,000 borrowing interest and the 10,000 loan financing fee are unallowable financial costs. The 5,000 taxing authority interest is allowable under the explicit tax interest exception.

## Never confuse

Interest on borrowings however represented is unallowable, subject to the 'but see' cross-reference [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. Costs of financing and refinancing capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities) are unallowable, subject to the 'but see' cross-reference [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. See [Unallowable-cost penalties](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md) for the consequences of including unallowable costs in indirect cost proposals.

The codified sentence ends the list of unallowable costs with a single (but see 31.205-28) [@CL-CMP-0818], while the RFO text prints its cross-reference to 31.205-28 only on the prospectus fees and stock rights items [@CL-CMP-8553]. The codified exception for taxing authority interest cites 31.205-41(a)(3) [@CL-CMP-0310], while the RFO text cites 31.205-41(b)(3) [@CL-CMP-8554].
