## Purpose

Identifies interest on borrowings, bond discounts, and capital financing expenses as unallowable costs on government contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-0309] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.205-20 [@CL-CMP-0309] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.205-20 [@CL-CMP-8552; @CL-CMP-8553; @CL-CMP-8554] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0309] |
| Applies to | Government contracts subject to the cost principles [@CL-CMP-0309] |

Interest on borrowings however represented, bond discounts, costs of financing and refinancing capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities), legal and professional fees paid in connection with preparing prospectuses, and costs of preparing and issuing stock rights are unallowable (but see 31.205-28) [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. However, interest assessed by State or local taxing authorities under the conditions specified in 31.205-41(a)(3) of the codified text is allowable [@CL-CMP-0310].

## How it works

Under codified FAR 31.205-20, interest on borrowings (however represented), bond discounts, costs of financing and refinancing capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities), legal and professional fees paid in connection with preparing prospectuses, and costs of preparing and issuing stock rights are unallowable (but see 31.205-28) [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818]. However, interest assessed by State or local taxing authorities under the conditions specified in 31.205-41(a)(3) is allowable [@CL-CMP-0310].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.205-20(a) says the following costs are unallowable [@CL-CMP-8552]: interest on borrowings, however represented; bond discounts; and costs of financing and refinancing capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities) [@CL-CMP-8552]. Its list continues with (4) legal and professional fees paid in connection with preparing prospectuses (except see 31.205-28) and (5) costs of preparing and issuing stock rights (except see 31.205-28) [@CL-CMP-8553]. Paragraph (b) says interest assessed by State or local taxing authorities under the conditions specified in 31.205-41(b)(3) is allowable [@CL-CMP-8554], and RFO 31.205-41(b)(3) covers interest or penalties incurred by the contractor for non-payment of any tax at the direction of the contracting officer or by reason of the failure of the contracting officer to ensure timely direction after a prompt request [@CL-CMP-8555].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor takes out a commercial line of credit to finance working capital during contract performance, incurring 45,000 in bank interest, 10,000 in financing loan fees, and 5,000 in interest assessed by a municipal taxing authority on contested local property taxes meeting all tax cost principle conditions. The 45,000 borrowing interest is unallowable; the 10,000 loan financing fee is not shown to be a cost of financing or refinancing capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities). The 5,000 taxing authority interest is allowable under the explicit tax interest exception.

## Never confuse

Interest on borrowings however represented is unallowable, subject to the 'but see' cross-reference [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. Costs of financing and refinancing capital (net worth plus long-term liabilities) are unallowable, subject to the 'but see' cross-reference [@CL-CMP-0309; @CL-CMP-0818; @CL-CMP-0310]. See [Unallowable-cost penalties](cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md) for the consequences of including unallowable costs in indirect cost proposals.

Codified FAR 31.205-20 follows its list of unallowable costs with "(but see 31.205-28)" [@CL-CMP-0818]. RFO FAR 31.205-20(a)(4) and (5) each say "(except see 31.205-28)" [@CL-CMP-8553]. The codified provision on interest assessed by State or local taxing authorities cites 31.205-41(a)(3) [@CL-CMP-0310], and RFO paragraph (b) cites 31.205-41(b)(3) [@CL-CMP-8554].
