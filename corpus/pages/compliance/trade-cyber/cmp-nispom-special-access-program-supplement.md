## Purpose

Shows what the supplement adds to the baseline NISPOM rule for special access programs and related controls, and how those added requirements reach a contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |

| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Contractors performing on SAP contracts [@CL-CMP-3492; @CL-CMP-3493] |

Given the sensitive nature of Alternative Compensatory Control Measures (ACCM), SAPs, SCI, RD, FRD, TFNI, and NNPI, the security requirements prescribed in the supplement exceed baseline standards for the rule and must be applied, as applicable, through specific contract requirements [@CL-CMP-3490]. Where a CSA-created program may require security measures that exceed the standards of the supplement, the CSA-imposed higher standards specifically detailed in the contract or conveyed through other applicable directives will be binding on USG and contractor participants [@CL-CMP-3491]. The contractor will comply with the security measures reflected in the supplement and other documents specifically referenced, when applied by the GCA or designee as part of a contract [@CL-CMP-3521]. Acceptance of the contract security measures is a prerequisite to any negotiations leading to program participation and an area accreditation [@CL-CMP-3494].

Under the paragraph on DoD SAP contracts, contractors will implement the security requirements for SAPs codified in SAP-related policy, when established by contract [@CL-CMP-3492]. Contractors performing on SAP contracts issued by non-DoD GCAs will implement SAP protection requirements imposed in their contracts [@CL-CMP-3493]. Contractors may participate in ACCMs, or be directed to participate, only when such access and the associated security plan are identified in DD Form 254 or equivalent [@CL-CMP-3522]. Contractors will report all unauthorized disclosures involving RD, FRD and TFNI information to the CSA [@CL-CMP-3523]. All contracts which grant access to NNPI must require compliance with the specific safeguarding requirements contained in OPNAVINST N9210.3 [@CL-CMP-3524]. Classified NNPI may not be processed on any contractor information system unless approved by the cognizant authorizing authority with concurrence from Naval Reactors [@CL-CMP-3525].

## How it works

The security requirements prescribed in the supplement exceed baseline standards for the rule and must be applied, as applicable, through specific contract requirements [@CL-CMP-3490]. The contractor will comply with the security measures reflected in the supplement and other documents specifically referenced, when applied by the GCA or designee as part of a contract [@CL-CMP-3521]. Where a CSA-created program may require security measures that exceed the standards of the supplement, the CSA-imposed higher standards specifically detailed in the contract or conveyed through other applicable directives will be binding on USG and contractor participants [@CL-CMP-3491]. Acceptance of the contract security measures is a prerequisite to any negotiations leading to program participation and an area accreditation [@CL-CMP-3494]. For DoD SAP contracts, contractors will implement the security requirements for SAPs codified in SAP-related policy, when established by contract [@CL-CMP-3492], and contractors performing on SAP contracts issued by non-DoD GCAs will implement SAP protection requirements imposed in their contracts [@CL-CMP-3493]. Contractors may participate in ACCMs, or be directed to participate, only when such access and the associated security plan are identified in DD Form 254 or equivalent [@CL-CMP-3522], and all contracts which grant access to NNPI must require compliance with the specific safeguarding requirements contained in OPNAVINST N9210.3 [@CL-CMP-3524]. For baseline storage rules, see [NISPOM safeguarding of classified information](cmp-nispom-safeguarding-classified-information.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company is invited to negotiate for a SAP contract from a non-DoD GCA. The solicitation lists security measures that go beyond the baseline.

Answer: the company must accept the contract security measures before it negotiates for program participation. It then implements the SAP protection requirements imposed in the contract.
