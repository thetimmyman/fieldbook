## Purpose

Shows what the supplement adds to the baseline NISPOM rule for special access programs and related controls, and how those added requirements reach a contractor.

## The rule

| Applies to | Contractors performing on SAP contracts [@CL-CMP-3492; @CL-CMP-3493] |

This card rests on the NISPOM, the rule for work involving classified information, and not on the FAR. Given the sensitive nature of ACCM, SAPs, SCI, RD, FRD, TFNI and NNPI, the security requirements prescribed in the supplement exceed baseline standards for the rule and must be applied, as applicable, through specific contract requirements [@CL-CMP-3490]. Where a CSA-created program may require security measures that exceed the standards of the supplement, the CSA-imposed higher standards specifically detailed in the contract or conveyed through other applicable directives will be binding on USG and contractor participants [@CL-CMP-3491]. The contractor will comply with the security measures reflected in the supplement and other documents specifically referenced, when applied by the GCA or designee as part of a contract [@CL-CMP-3521]. Acceptance of the contract security measures is a prerequisite to any negotiations leading to program participation and an area accreditation [@CL-CMP-3494].

Under the paragraph on DoD SAP contracts, contractors will implement the security requirements for SAPs codified in SAP-related policy, when established by contract [@CL-CMP-3492]. Contractors performing on SAP contracts issued by non-DoD GCAs will implement SAP protection requirements imposed in their contracts [@CL-CMP-3493]. Contractors may participate in ACCMs, or be directed to participate, only when such access and the associated security plan are identified in DD Form 254 or equivalent [@CL-CMP-3522]. Contractors will report all unauthorized disclosures involving RD, FRD and TFNI information to the CSA [@CL-CMP-3523]. All contracts which grant access to NNPI must require compliance with the specific safeguarding requirements contained in OPNAVINST N9210.3 [@CL-CMP-3524]. Classified NNPI may not be processed on any contractor information system unless approved by the cognizant authorizing authority with concurrence from Naval Reactors [@CL-CMP-3525]. This card does not restate the other requirements in the supplement.

## How it works

The supplement adds requirements that go beyond the baseline rule, and they must be applied, as applicable, through specific contract requirements [@CL-CMP-3490]. The contractor complies with them when the GCA or its designee applies them as part of a contract [@CL-CMP-3521]. A CSA can set still higher standards, which bind both the USG and the contractor once they are in the contract or in other applicable directives [@CL-CMP-3491]. Accepting the contract's security measures is a prerequisite to any negotiations leading to program participation and an area accreditation [@CL-CMP-3494]. For SAPs, a contractor on a DoD contract implements the security requirements codified in SAP-related policy when the contract establishes them [@CL-CMP-3492], and a contractor on a contract from a non-DoD GCA implements the SAP protection requirements imposed in its contract [@CL-CMP-3493]. Participation in an ACCM depends on the access and security plan being identified in DD Form 254 or equivalent [@CL-CMP-3522], and contracts that grant access to NNPI must require compliance with the specific safeguarding requirements [@CL-CMP-3524]. The baseline storage rules are on the [NISPOM safeguarding of classified information](cmp-nispom-safeguarding-classified-information.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company is invited to negotiate for a SAP contract from a non-DoD GCA. The solicitation lists security measures that go beyond the baseline.

Answer: the company must accept the contract security measures before it negotiates for program participation. It then implements the SAP protection requirements imposed in the contract.

## Never confuse

The supplement is not a second copy of the baseline rule. Its requirements exceed the baseline and are applied, as applicable, through specific contract requirements [@CL-CMP-3490]. The rules for CNWDI and COMSEC are on the [NISPOM CNWDI and COMSEC](cmp-nispom-cnwdi-and-comsec.md) card, and the baseline marking rules are on the [NISPOM marking requirements](cmp-nispom-marking-requirements.md) card. A higher standard for a CSA-created program is also separate from the baseline, and it binds participants once it is detailed in the contract or in other applicable directives [@CL-CMP-3491].
