## Purpose

Shows what the offeror's additional representation covers, what the contractor may not provide, and what it must report during performance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-2857; @CL-CMP-2858] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.204-7017(d) [@CL-CMP-2857]; DFARS 252.204-7018(b) [@CL-CMP-2858] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2858] |
| Applies to | Offerors whose annual SAM representations say they provide covered equipment or services [@CL-CMP-2856]; contractors providing equipment, systems, or services to carry out covered missions [@CL-CMP-2858] |

If in its annual representations and certifications in SAM the offeror has represented in paragraph (c) of the provision at 252.204-7016 that it does provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-2856], and has represented that it does provide them as a part of its offered products or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract, or other contractual instrument, then the offeror must complete the additional representation [@CL-CMP-2914]. The additional representation states that the offeror will or will not provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to DoD in the performance of any award resulting from the solicitation [@CL-CMP-2857]. An offeror that has represented in paragraph (d) of the provision that it will provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services must provide the information listed in the provision as part of the offer [@CL-CMP-2915]. Under DFARS 252.204-7007 Alternate A paragraph (d)(1), the representation at 252.204-7016, Covered Defense Telecommunications Equipment or Services, in the SAM database applies to all solicitations [@CL-CMP-2922]; that paragraph is on the [Contracted services reporting and Alternate A representations](cmp-contracted-services-reporting-and-alternate-a-representations.md) card.

Subject to the waiver exception, the contractor shall not provide to the Government any equipment, system, or service to carry out covered missions that uses covered defense telecommunications equipment or services as a substantial or essential component of any system, or as critical technology as part of any system [@CL-CMP-2858; @CL-CMP-2859]. The prohibition does not apply where the covered defense telecommunication equipment or services are covered by a waiver described in DFARS 204.2104 [@CL-CMP-2859].

## How it works

A contractor that identifies covered defense telecommunications equipment or services used as a substantial or essential component of any system, or as critical technology as part of any system, during contract performance must report the information in paragraph (d)(2) of the clause [@CL-CMP-2860; @CL-CMP-8050]. Within 3 business days from the date of identification or notification, the contractor's report includes, among other listed items, the contract number, order numbers if applicable, supplier name, brand, model number, and item description [@CL-CMP-2861]. Within 30 business days of submitting that information, the contractor must report any further available information about mitigation actions undertaken or recommended [@CL-CMP-2862]. In addition to the further mitigation information, the contractor shall describe the efforts it undertook to prevent use or submission of covered defense telecommunications equipment or services and any additional efforts that will be incorporated to prevent future use or submission [@CL-CMP-2916]. This card does not set out the full definitions of covered equipment, covered missions or critical technology.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor finds that a component used as a substantial or essential component of a system it delivers during contract performance is covered equipment and has no waiver. It records the identification date, files the first report with the contract number, supplier, brand, model and description on business day 2, and files the follow-up on mitigation 25 business days later.

## Never confuse

The offeror's additional representation states that the offeror will or will not provide covered defense telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to DoD in the performance of any award resulting from the solicitation [@CL-CMP-2857]. The contractor shall not provide to the Government any equipment, system, or service to carry out covered missions that uses covered defense telecommunications equipment or services as a substantial or essential component of any system, or as critical technology as part of any system, unless the covered defense telecommunication equipment or services are covered by a waiver described in DFARS 204.2104 [@CL-CMP-2858; @CL-CMP-2859]. A contractor that identifies such equipment or services during contract performance shall report the information in paragraph (d)(2) of the clause [@CL-CMP-2860; @CL-CMP-8050].
