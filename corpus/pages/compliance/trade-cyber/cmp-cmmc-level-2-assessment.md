## Purpose

Shows how a Level 2 status is earned by self-assessment or C3PAO certification, what Conditional and Final status mean, and the deadlines that follow [@CL-CMP-2069; @CL-CMP-2074].

| Applies to | OSAs with a contract or subcontract that requires Level 2 (Self) or Level 2 (C3PAO) status [@CL-CMP-2069; @CL-CMP-2074] |

To maintain compliance with Level 2 (Self) status, the OSA must conduct a Level 2 self-assessment every three years and submit the results in SPRS within three years of the status date of the Conditional Level 2 (Self) status [@CL-CMP-2069]. The OSA achieves Conditional Level 2 (Self) status if its Level 2 self-assessment results in a POA&M that meets all the Level 2 POA&M requirements in the POA&M section of the part [@CL-CMP-2070], and Final Level 2 (Self) status if the self-assessment results in a passing score, whether upon initial self-assessment or as the result of a POA&M closeout self-assessment [@CL-CMP-2073]. To achieve Level 2 (C3PAO) status the OSC must achieve a MET result for all security requirements specified in the model section for Level 2 and must obtain a Level 2 certification assessment from an authorized or accredited C3PAO [@CL-CMP-2074]. To maintain Level 2 (C3PAO) status, the Level 2 certification assessment must be completed within three years of the status date of the Conditional Level 2 (C3PAO) status [@CL-CMP-2075].

The closing of a POA&M must be confirmed by a POA&M closeout assessment within 180 days of the Conditional status date [@CL-CMP-2087]. For a Conditional Level 2 (Self) status the OSA must remediate any NOT MET requirements, perform a POA&M closeout self-assessment and post compliance results to SPRS within 180 days of the status date [@CL-CMP-2071]. For a Conditional Level 2 (C3PAO) status the OSC must remediate any NOT MET requirements and undergo a POA&M closeout certification assessment from a C3PAO, which must post compliance results into eMASS within 180 days of the status date [@CL-CMP-2076]. If the POA&M is not closed out in time, the Conditional status for the information system expires [@CL-CMP-2087]. If a Conditional Level 2 (Self) status expires within the period of performance of a contract, standard contractual remedies apply, and the OSA is ineligible for additional awards requiring Level 2 (Self) or higher for the information system within the assessment scope until a new status is achieved [@CL-CMP-2072; @CL-CMP-2077]. In a self-assessment the OSA itself performs the closeout, and in a certification the C3PAO performs the closeout assessment and posts the results [@CL-CMP-2071; @CL-CMP-2076].

## How it works

To achieve Level 2 (C3PAO) status the OSC must obtain a Level 2 certification assessment from an authorized or accredited C3PAO [@CL-CMP-2074]. The OSA achieves Conditional Level 2 (Self) status if its Level 2 self-assessment results in a POA&M that meets all the Level 2 POA&M requirements, and Final Level 2 (Self) status if the self-assessment results in a passing score, whether upon initial self-assessment or as the result of a POA&M closeout self-assessment [@CL-CMP-2070; @CL-CMP-2073]. The closing of the POA&M must be confirmed within 180 days of the Conditional status date, or the Conditional status for the information system expires [@CL-CMP-2087]. The three-year periods are counted from the status date of the Conditional status [@CL-CMP-2069; @CL-CMP-2075]. The conditions a POA&M must meet, and how points are subtracted, are on the [POA&M conditions and scoring](cmp-cmmc-poam-and-scoring.md) card. The OSA affirms continuing compliance after every assessment, including POA&M closeout, as the [affirmation and flowdown](cmp-cmmc-affirmation-and-flowdown.md) card explains [@CL-CMP-2094].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor completes a Level 2 self-assessment at month 0, and the result is a POA&M that meets every POA&M requirement, so it holds Conditional Level 2 (Self) status. It remediates the NOT MET items, performs the closeout self-assessment and posts the results to SPRS on day 150. That is inside the 180-day limit, so Final status can follow. Had it posted on day 200, the Conditional status would have expired.

## Never confuse

The 180-day closeout limit runs from the status date of the Conditional status [@CL-CMP-2071], and the three-year cycle also counts from the status date of the Conditional status [@CL-CMP-2069]. For a Conditional Level 2 (Self) status the OSA must perform a POA&M closeout self-assessment [@CL-CMP-2071], and for a Conditional Level 2 (C3PAO) status the OSC must undergo a POA&M closeout certification assessment from a C3PAO [@CL-CMP-2076]. A Final Level 2 (C3PAO) status for the information systems within the Level 3 assessment scope is a prerequisite to undergo a Level 3 certification assessment [@CL-CMP-2078], as the [Level 3 assessment](cmp-cmmc-level-3-assessment.md) card explains.
