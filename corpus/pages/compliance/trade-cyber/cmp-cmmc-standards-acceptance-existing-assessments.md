## Purpose

Shows when a completed DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment is accepted as a CMMC Status, for how long, and who identifies the qualifying assessments and verifies SPRS.

| Applies to | OSCs that completed a DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment aligned with CMMC Level 2 Scoping, under the conditions the section sets [@CL-CMP-8465] |

Paragraph (a) is headed NIST SP 800-171 R2 DoD assessments [@CL-CMP-8486]. Paragraph (a)(2) and paragraph (b) are marked [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-8485].

To avoid duplication of efforts, thereby reducing the aggregate cost to industry and the Department, OSCs that have completed a DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment aligned with CMMC Level 2 Scoping will be given the CMMC Status of Final Level 2 (C3PAO) under the following conditions [@CL-CMP-8465].

An OSC that achieved a perfect score with no open POA&M from a DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment conducted prior to the effective date of the rule will be given a CMMC Status of Level 2 Final (C3PAO), with a validity period of three years from the date of the original DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment [@CL-CMP-8466].

DCMA DIBCAC will identify assessments that meet these criteria and verify that SPRS accurately reflects the CMMC Status [@CL-CMP-8467]. Eligible DCMA DIBCAC High Assessments include ones conducted with Joint Surveillance in accordance with the DCMA Manual 2302-01 Surveillance [@CL-CMP-8468].

The scope of the Level 2 certification assessment is identical to the scope of the DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment [@CL-CMP-8469].

In accordance with § 170.17(a)(2), the OSC must also submit an affirmation in SPRS and annually thereafter to achieve contractual eligibility [@CL-CMP-8470].

## How it works

Who decides. DCMA DIBCAC will identify the assessments that meet the criteria and verify that SPRS accurately reflects the CMMC Status [@CL-CMP-8467]. In accordance with § 170.17(a)(2), the OSC must also submit an affirmation in SPRS and annually thereafter to achieve contractual eligibility [@CL-CMP-8470]. See [CMMC Program Management Office and DCMA DIBCAC roles](cmp-cmmc-pmo-and-dcma-dibcac-roles.md).

Thresholds and conditions. The criterion is a perfect score with no open POA&M from a DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment that was conducted prior to the effective date of the rule [@CL-CMP-8466]. The assessment must also be aligned with CMMC Level 2 Scoping [@CL-CMP-8465]. On the Level 2 certification assessment, see [CMMC Level 2 assessment, certification and POA&M closeout](cmp-cmmc-level-2-assessment.md) [@CL-CMP-8469]. On NIST SP 800-171 R2 DoD assessments, see [NIST SP 800-171 DoD assessment requirements](cmp-nist-sp-800-171-dod-assessment-requirements.md) [@CL-CMP-8486].

Timing. The three-year validity period is counted from the date of the original DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment [@CL-CMP-8466]. The OSC must submit the affirmation in SPRS and annually thereafter, to achieve contractual eligibility [@CL-CMP-8470].

Contractor duty. In accordance with § 170.17(a)(2), the OSC must also submit the affirmation to achieve contractual eligibility [@CL-CMP-8470]. See [CMMC affirmation and subcontractor flowdown](cmp-cmmc-affirmation-and-flowdown.md).

Scope. The scope of the Level 2 certification assessment is identical to the scope of the DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment [@CL-CMP-8469]. Eligible assessments include ones conducted with Joint Surveillance, in accordance with the DCMA Manual 2302-01 Surveillance [@CL-CMP-8468].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Company A completed a DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment aligned with CMMC Level 2 Scoping on day 0, before the rule's effective date. It scored perfectly and had 0 open POA&M items. It will be given a CMMC Status of Level 2 Final (C3PAO) with a validity period of three years from day 0, the date of the original assessment [@CL-CMP-8466]. DCMA DIBCAC identifies the assessment and verifies that SPRS reflects the status [@CL-CMP-8467]. Company A must also submit an affirmation in SPRS and annually thereafter to achieve contractual eligibility [@CL-CMP-8470].

Company B completed a High Assessment before the effective date that left 1 POA&M item open. Company B does not have a perfect score with no open POA&M [@CL-CMP-8466].

## Never confuse

An OSC that achieved a perfect score with no open POA&M from a DCMA DIBCAC High Assessment conducted prior to the effective date of the rule will be given a CMMC Status of Level 2 Final (C3PAO) with a validity period of three years from the date of the original assessment [@CL-CMP-8466]. The Level 2 certification assessment scope is identical to the High Assessment scope [@CL-CMP-8469].

Status and contractual eligibility differ: the validity period is three years from the date of the original assessment [@CL-CMP-8466], and the OSC must also submit an affirmation in SPRS and annually thereafter to achieve contractual eligibility [@CL-CMP-8470].
