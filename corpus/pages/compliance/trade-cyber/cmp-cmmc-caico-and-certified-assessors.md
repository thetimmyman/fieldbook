## Purpose

Shows what the CMMC Assessor and Instructor Certification Organization does and what a CMMC Certified Assessor must meet to be certified and to assess.

## The rule

| Applies to | The CAICO and the CCAs who support a C3PAO [@CL-CMP-3232; @CL-CMP-3234] |

The CAICO is responsible for training, testing, authorizing, certifying, and recertifying CMMC assessors, instructors, and related professionals [@CL-CMP-3232]. Only the CAICO may make decisions relating to examination certifications, including granting, maintaining, recertifying, expanding, and reducing the scope of certification, and suspending or withdrawing certification in accordance with current ISO/IEC 17024:2012(E) [@CL-CMP-3233].

CCAs, in support of a C3PAO, conduct Level 2 certification assessments of OSCs in accordance with NIST SP 800-171A Jun2018, the assessment processes defined in section 170.17, and the scoping requirements defined in section 170.19(c) [@CL-CMP-3234].

## How it works

CCAs shall obtain and maintain certification from the CAICO, and that certification is valid for 3 years from the date of issuance [@CL-CMP-3235]. That item opens the list of CCA requirements. One listed requirement is that a CCA be a CCP who has at least 3 years of cybersecurity experience, at least 1 year of assessment or audit experience, and at least one foundational qualification aligned to at least the Intermediate Proficiency Level of the Security Control Assessor (612) Work Role [@CL-CMP-3236; @CL-CMP-3235]. One listed requirement is that a CCA qualify as a Lead CCA by having, among other elements, at least 5 years of cybersecurity experience, 5 years of management experience, and 3 years of assessment or audit experience [@CL-CMP-3237; @CL-CMP-3235]. The CCA section lists further requirements that this card does not set out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A CCA is certified by the CAICO at the start of year 1. Under the 3-year validity period the certification runs to the end of year 3. A person with 4 years of cybersecurity experience and 2 years of assessment or audit experience does not meet the Lead CCA experience figures, which need 5, 5 and 3 years.

## Never confuse

The Accreditation Body is responsible for authorizing and ensuring the accreditation of C3PAOs [@CL-CMP-3218]. The CAICO is responsible for training, testing, authorizing, certifying, and recertifying CMMC assessors, instructors, and related professionals [@CL-CMP-3232]. CCAs, in support of a C3PAO, conduct Level 2 certification assessments of OSCs [@CL-CMP-3234]. The Accreditation Body is on the [Accreditation Body](cmp-cmmc-accreditation-body.md) card, the duties of the third-party assessment organizations are on the [third-party assessment organization requirements](cmp-cmmc-c3pao-requirements.md) card, and the people the CAICO certifies as instructors and professionals are on the [instructors and certified professionals](cmp-cmmc-instructors-and-certified-professionals.md) card.
