## Purpose

Shows what a CMMC Third-Party Assessment Organization must meet to be authorized and what it must and must not do when it assesses a contractor.

## The rule

| Codified citation | Not established. |

| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | C3PAOs, the organizations that conduct Level 2 certification assessments [@CL-CMP-3224] |

C3PAOs are responsible for conducting Level 2 certification assessments and issuing Certificates of CMMC Status to OSCs based on the results, and they must be accredited or authorized by the Accreditation Body [@CL-CMP-3224]. C3PAOs shall obtain authorization or accreditation from the Accreditation Body in accordance with the cited paragraphs of the Accreditation Body section, and that item opens the list of C3PAO requirements [@CL-CMP-3225].

One listed requirement is that a C3PAO comply with the Accreditation Body policies for conflict of interest, code of professional conduct, and ethics, and achieve and maintain compliance with ISO/IEC 17020:2012(E) within 27 months of authorization [@CL-CMP-3226; @CL-CMP-3225]. One listed requirement is that a C3PAO undergo a Level 2 certification assessment meeting all requirements for a Final Level 2 (C3PAO), with listed exceptions, one of which is that the assessment will be conducted by DCMA DIBCAC [@CL-CMP-3228; @CL-CMP-3225]. The other listed exception is that the assessment will not result in a CMMC Status of Level 2 (C3PAO) nor receive a Certificate of CMMC Status [@CL-CMP-3335]. One listed requirement is that a C3PAO require all C3PAO company personnel participating in the Level 2 certification assessment process to complete a Tier 3 background investigation resulting in a determination of national security eligibility, which includes the CMMC Assessment Team and the quality assurance individual [@CL-CMP-3333; @CL-CMP-3225]. One listed requirement is that those personnel who are not eligible to obtain a Tier 3 background investigation meet the equivalent of a favorably adjudicated Tier 3 background investigation [@CL-CMP-3334; @CL-CMP-3225].

## How it works

A C3PAO must ensure that all Level 2 certification assessment activities are performed on the information system within the CMMC Assessment Scope, as one listed requirement [@CL-CMP-3227; @CL-CMP-3225]. One listed requirement is that a C3PAO Assessment Team include at least two people, a Lead CCA and at least one other CCA [@CL-CMP-3230; @CL-CMP-3225]. One listed requirement is that a C3PAO implement a quality assurance function that ensures the accuracy and completeness of assessment data prior to upload into the CMMC instantiation of eMASS [@CL-CMP-3336; @CL-CMP-3225]. Any individual fulfilling the quality assurance function must be a CCA and cannot be a member of an Assessment Team for which they are performing a quality assurance role [@CL-CMP-3231]. One listed requirement is that a C3PAO, unless disposition is otherwise authorized by the CMMC PMO, maintain all assessment related records for a period of six years [@CL-CMP-3229; @CL-CMP-3225].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A C3PAO plans an assessment for a supplier. It names one Lead CCA and two other CCAs to the Assessment Team. It names a fourth CCA to review quality, and that person is not on the team. The C3PAO keeps the working papers and the other assessment records for six years unless the CMMC PMO allows another disposition.

## Never confuse

The assessor and the accredited differ in role: C3PAOs are responsible for conducting Level 2 certification assessments [@CL-CMP-3224], while the Accreditation Body is responsible for authorizing and ensuring the accreditation of C3PAOs [@CL-CMP-3218]. The Accreditation Body is described on the [Accreditation Body](cmp-cmmc-accreditation-body.md) card. A C3PAO must itself undergo a Level 2 certification assessment, with the exception that the assessment will be conducted by DCMA DIBCAC [@CL-CMP-3228]. The assessors who carry out a contractor assessment are on the [CAICO and certified assessors](cmp-cmmc-caico-and-certified-assessors.md) card, and the DIBCAC role is on the [PMO and DIBCAC roles](cmp-cmmc-pmo-and-dcma-dibcac-roles.md) card.
