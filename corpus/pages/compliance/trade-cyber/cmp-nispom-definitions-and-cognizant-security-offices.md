## Purpose

Lists the NISPOM acronyms and definitions that finance and contracts staff meet most often, and says where cognizant security office information is found.

In the acronym list of the definitions section, FCL stands for facility (security) clearance, FGI for foreign government information and FOCI for foreign ownership, control, or influence [@CL-CMP-3495]. GCA stands for government contracting activity [@CL-CMP-3496], KMP for key management personnel [@CL-CMP-3497] and SMO for senior management official [@CL-CMP-3498]. A CDC is a subset of contractors cleared under the NISP who have classified contracts with the DoD [@CL-CMP-3499].

The definitions themselves add detail. A contractor means any industrial, educational, commercial, or other entity that has been granted an entity eligibility determination by a CSA, and the term also includes licensees, grantees, or certificate holders of the USG with an entity eligibility determination granted by a CSA [@CL-CMP-3515]. An FCL means an administrative determination that, from a security viewpoint, an entity is eligible for access to classified information of a certain level and all lower levels [@CL-CMP-3516]. An entity eligibility determination for the DHS CCIPP is not the equivalent of an FCL and does not meet the requirements for FCL reciprocity [@CL-CMP-0148]. A foreign interest means any foreign government, agency of a foreign government or representative of a foreign government, and any form of business enterprise or legal entity organized, chartered or incorporated under the laws of any country other than the United States or its territories, and any person who is not a citizen or national of the United States [@CL-CMP-3517; @CL-CMP-7359]. KMP means an entity's senior management official, facility security officer and insider threat program senior official, and all other entity officials who either hold majority interest or stock in the entity, or have direct or indirect authority to influence or decide issues affecting the management or operations of the entity or classified contract performance [@CL-CMP-3518; @CL-CMP-7360]. Need-to-know is a determination made by an authorized holder of classified information that a prospective recipient has a requirement for access to, knowledge of, or possession of the classified information to perform tasks or services essential to the fulfillment of a classified contract or program [@CL-CMP-3519; @CL-CMP-0149]. The SMO is an entity employee occupying a position in the entity with ultimate authority over the facility's operations and the authority to direct actions necessary for the safeguarding of classified information in the facility [@CL-CMP-3520]. The SMO's authority includes the authority to direct actions necessary to safeguard classified information when the access to classified information by the facility's employees is solely at other contractor facilities or USG locations [@CL-CMP-0150].

For the DoD, the cognizant security office information refers the reader to the DCSA website for a listing of office locations and areas of responsibility and for information on verification of facility clearances and safeguarding [@CL-CMP-3500].

## How it works

The acronym list gives the words behind FCL, GCA, KMP and SMO [@CL-CMP-3495; @CL-CMP-3496; @CL-CMP-3497; @CL-CMP-3498], and separate definitions cover FCL, KMP and SMO [@CL-CMP-3516; @CL-CMP-3518; @CL-CMP-3520]. A CDC is a subset of contractors cleared under the NISP who have classified contracts with the DoD [@CL-CMP-3499]. For the DoD, the cognizant security office entry refers the reader to the DCSA website for a listing of office locations and areas of responsibility and for information on verification of facility clearances and safeguarding [@CL-CMP-3500]. Entity eligibility is covered on the [NISPOM entity eligibility determination (facility clearance)](cmp-nispom-entity-eligibility-determination.md) card, and the senior management official's role is on the [NISPOM security officials, insider threat program and self-inspection](cmp-nispom-general-procedures-and-security-officials.md) card. Foreign interests and foreign government information come up on the [NISPOM international transfers and foreign government information](cmp-nispom-international-transfers-and-foreign-government-information.md) card and the [NISPOM international visits and foreign national access](cmp-nispom-international-visits-and-foreign-national-access.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A finance analyst reads a customer contract that mentions an FCL, a GCA and the KMP of a joint venture partner.

Answer: the acronym list reads these as facility (security) clearance, government contracting activity and key management personnel. The definition of KMP names the senior management official, the facility security officer and the insider threat program senior official.

## Never confuse

In the acronym list, FCL stands for facility (security) clearance, KMP for key management personnel and SMO for senior management official [@CL-CMP-3495; @CL-CMP-3497; @CL-CMP-3498], and KMP and SMO each also have a separate definition [@CL-CMP-3518; @CL-CMP-3520]. A CDC is a subset of contractors cleared under the NISP who have classified contracts with the DoD [@CL-CMP-3499].
