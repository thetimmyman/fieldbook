## Purpose

Shows what the Government must do to distribute contracts, how it treats electronic commerce, and which service contracts are reported and by whom.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-2993; @CL-CMP-9755] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.201 [@CL-CMP-2993]; DFARS 204.201(a) [@CL-CMP-9755]; FAR 4.500 [@CL-CMP-10001]; FAR 4.502 [@CL-CMP-2994]; FAR 4.1703 [@CL-CMP-2996] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2993] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers distributing contracts, agencies using electronic commerce, and service contracts and orders at the listed thresholds in FAIR Act agencies other than DoD [@CL-CMP-2993; @CL-CMP-2994; @CL-CMP-2996; @CL-CMP-3602] |

Contracting officers shall distribute copies of contracts or modifications within 10 working days after execution by all parties [@CL-CMP-2993]. The defense supplement says that, in lieu of the requirement at FAR 4.201(a), contracting officers shall distribute one signed copy or reproduction of the signed contract to the contractor [@CL-CMP-9755]. The electronic commerce subpart provides policy and procedures for the establishment and use of electronic commerce in Federal acquisition as required by title 41 of the United States Code, section 2301 [@CL-CMP-10001]. The Federal Government shall use electronic commerce whenever practicable or cost-effective [@CL-CMP-2994].

The service contract reporting subpart applies to all FAIR Act agencies, except DoD as specified in FAR 4.1705 [@CL-CMP-3602], and where the clause section prescribes the service contract reporting clause, it says that clause is not required for actions entirely funded by DoD, contracts awarded with a generic entity identifier, or in classified solicitations, contracts, or orders [@CL-CMP-3603]. Within that scope, except as exempted by OFPP guidance, service contractor reporting shall be required for contracts and first-tier subcontracts for services based on type of contract and estimated total value [@CL-CMP-2948]. Reporting is required for all first-tier subcontracts for services as prescribed in the two threshold paragraphs [@CL-CMP-2949]. One listed reporting threshold covers all cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials, and labor-hour service contracts and orders with an estimated total value above the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-2996]. The second listed category covers all fixed-price service contracts awarded and orders issued according to listed thresholds [@CL-CMP-2995]. One listed threshold under that category is an estimated total value of $500,000 or greater for contracts awarded or orders issued in Fiscal Year 2016 and subsequent years [@CL-CMP-2997]. The section also deals with indefinite-delivery contracts, which this card does not set out.

## How it works

If the agency believes revisions to contractor reported information are warranted, it shall notify the contractor no later than November 15, and by November 30 the contractor shall revise the report or document its rationale for the agency [@CL-CMP-2998]. Information not collected in FPDS will be provided by the contractor, as specified in the two service contract reporting clauses [@CL-CMP-2999]. Agencies shall submit a service contract inventory to OMB by January 15 annually, and each agency must then post the inventory on its Web site and publish a Federal Register Notice of Availability by February 15 annually [@CL-CMP-2947]. For the defense supplement's reporting clause, see [Contracted services reporting and Alternate A representations](cmp-contracted-services-reporting-and-alternate-a-representations.md). For FPDS reporting, see [FPDS reporting responsibilities and deadlines](../../market/feeds/mkt-fpds-reporting-responsibilities-and-deadlines.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract is signed by all parties on a Monday, and the contracting officer sends the copies within the 10 working days. A fixed-price service order issued in Fiscal Year 2027 has an estimated value of 400,000 dollars, which is below the 500,000 dollar threshold in the rule, so this threshold does not reach it.

## Never confuse

Distribution of a signed contract is a duty of the contracting officer to send copies [@CL-CMP-2993]. Service contract reporting is a duty of the contractor to provide information that FPDS does not already hold [@CL-CMP-2999].
