## Purpose

Shows what a company must meet to hold an entity eligibility determination for access to classified information, who sponsors it, and what an invalidation or a termination does.

| Applies to | All contractors with entity eligibility determinations, except as provided for the CCIPP under the cognizance of DHS [@CL-CMP-3307] |

The section applies to all contractors with entity eligibility determinations, except as provided in section 117.22 for entity eligibility determinations for participation in the CCIPP under the cognizance of DHS [@CL-CMP-3307]. Neither the contractor nor its employees will be permitted access to classified information until the CSA has made an entity eligibility determination [@CL-CMP-3166]. A contractor or prospective contractor cannot apply for its own entity eligibility determination, and a GCA or a currently cleared contractor may sponsor an entity for one at any point during the contracting or agreement life cycle at which the entity must have access to classified information to participate, including the solicitation or competition phase [@CL-CMP-3167; @CL-CMP-8129]. To be eligible for an initial entity eligibility determination or to maintain an existing one, the entity must, among other listed requirements, need access to classified information in connection with a legitimate USG or foreign government requirement, and that access be consistent with United States national security interests as determined by the CSA [@CL-CMP-3168; @CL-CMP-8130]. Two of the numbered requirements in the same paragraph are that the entity be located in the United States or its territorial areas and have a record of integrity and lawful conduct in its business dealings [@CL-CMP-3168; @CL-CMP-3169]. Another is that the entity have an SMO, FSO and ITPSO who have and maintain eligibility for access to classified information and are not excluded from participating in USG contracts or agreements in accordance with § 117.7(b)(1) through § 117.7(b)(3) [@CL-CMP-3168; @CL-CMP-3170]. Another is that the entity not be under FOCI to such a degree that a favorable entity eligibility determination would be inconsistent with the national interest, in the judgment of the CSA [@CL-CMP-3168; @CL-CMP-3171].

Contractors are eligible for storage of classified material in connection with a legitimate USG or foreign government requirement if they have a favorable entity eligibility determination and a classified information safeguarding capability approved by the CSA [@CL-CMP-3323]. Contractors will maintain the original CSA designated forms for the duration of the entity eligibility determination in accordance with CSA-provided guidance [@CL-CMP-3309].

The CSA may invalidate an existing entity eligibility determination [@CL-CMP-3308]. While an entity eligibility determination is in an invalidated status, the contractor may not bid on or be awarded new classified contracts or solicitations, but may continue to work on existing classified contracts if the GCA agrees [@CL-CMP-3172]. If the entity eligibility determination is terminated or revoked, the contractor will return all classified material in its possession to the appropriate GCA or dispose of the material as instructed by the CSA [@CL-CMP-3173].

## How it works

A contractor or prospective contractor cannot apply for its own entity eligibility determination, and a GCA or a currently cleared contractor may sponsor an entity for one at any point during the contracting or agreement life cycle at which the entity must have access to classified information to participate [@CL-CMP-3167; @CL-CMP-8129]. To be eligible for an initial entity eligibility determination or to maintain one, the entity must, among other listed requirements, need access to classified information in connection with a legitimate USG or foreign government requirement [@CL-CMP-3168], and access must be consistent with U.S. national security interests as determined by the CSA [@CL-CMP-8130]. Other listed requirements are a record of integrity and lawful conduct in its business dealings [@CL-CMP-3169] and not being under FOCI to such a degree that a favorable determination would be inconsistent with the national interest, in the judgment of the CSA [@CL-CMP-3171]. Neither the contractor nor its employees will be permitted access to classified information until the CSA has made an entity eligibility determination [@CL-CMP-3166]. Contractors are eligible for storage of classified material in connection with a legitimate USG or foreign government requirement if they have a favorable entity eligibility determination and a classified information safeguarding capability approved by the CSA, and the [NISPOM safeguarding of classified information](cmp-nispom-safeguarding-classified-information.md) card covers the safeguarding side [@CL-CMP-3323]. A subcontractor must possess an appropriate entity eligibility determination and a classified information safeguarding capability if possession of classified information will be required, as the [NISPOM subcontracting and information system security](cmp-nispom-subcontracting-and-information-system-security.md) card explains [@CL-CMP-3196]. The contractor keeps the CSA designated forms for as long as the determination lasts [@CL-CMP-3309].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company with an invalidated determination is invited to bid on a new classified follow-on worth 5,000,000.

Answer: it may not bid on or be awarded new classified contracts or solicitations while the determination is invalidated, and it may continue to work on an existing classified contract if the GCA agrees.

## Never confuse

The CSA may invalidate an existing determination; while it is invalidated, the contractor may not bid on or be awarded new classified contracts or solicitations, and may continue to work on existing classified contracts if the GCA agrees [@CL-CMP-3308; @CL-CMP-3172]. If the determination is terminated or revoked, the contractor will return all classified material in its possession to the appropriate GCA or dispose of the material as instructed by the CSA [@CL-CMP-3173]. FOCI is one of the listed requirements [@CL-CMP-3171]; the mitigation measures are on the [NISPOM foreign ownership, control or influence and its mitigation](../../mna/diligence/mna-nispom-foci-and-mitigation.md) card. Employee eligibility is on the [NISPOM personnel eligibility for access to classified information](cmp-nispom-personnel-eligibility-determination.md) card.
