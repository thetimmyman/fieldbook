## Purpose

Defines the two terms printed in the FAR subpart on the prohibition on contracting for hardware, software, and services developed or provided by Kaspersky Lab: Kaspersky Lab covered article and Kaspersky Lab covered entity.

## The rule

|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8471] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.2001 [@CL-CMP-8471] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8471] |
| Applies to | Use of the defined terms in the subpart where they are printed [@CL-CMP-8471] |

The section prints the definitions at FAR 4.2001 [@CL-CMP-8471]. The terms have those meanings as used in the subpart [@CL-CMP-8471].

Kaspersky Lab covered article means any hardware, software, or service that is developed or provided by a Kaspersky Lab covered entity [@CL-CMP-8472]. It also means any hardware, software, or service that includes any hardware, software, or service developed or provided in whole or in part by a Kaspersky Lab covered entity [@CL-CMP-8473]. It also means any hardware, software, or service that contains components using any hardware or software developed in whole or in part by a Kaspersky Lab covered entity [@CL-CMP-8474]. The definition lists no exclusion [@CL-CMP-8474].

Kaspersky Lab covered entity means Kaspersky Lab and any successor entity to Kaspersky Lab, including any change in name, for example Kaspersky [@CL-CMP-8475]. It also means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with Kaspersky Lab, and any entity of which Kaspersky Lab has a majority ownership [@CL-CMP-8476]. The definition lists no exclusion [@CL-CMP-8476].

| Term | Definition as printed |
|---|---|
| Kaspersky Lab covered article | Any hardware, software, or service that (1) is developed or provided by a Kaspersky Lab covered entity; (2) includes any hardware, software, or service developed or provided in whole or in part by a Kaspersky Lab covered entity; or (3) contains components using any hardware or software developed in whole or in part by a Kaspersky Lab covered entity. No exclusion is listed. [@CL-CMP-8472; @CL-CMP-8473; @CL-CMP-8474] |
| Kaspersky Lab covered entity | (1) Kaspersky Lab; (2) any successor entity to Kaspersky Lab, including any change in name, for example Kaspersky; (3) any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with Kaspersky Lab; or (4) any entity of which Kaspersky Lab has a majority ownership. No exclusion is listed. [@CL-CMP-8475; @CL-CMP-8476] |

## How it works

The three covered-article limbs are alternatives joined by "or" [@CL-CMP-8472; @CL-CMP-8473; @CL-CMP-8474]. The first limb asks who developed or provided the item. The second reaches an item that includes hardware, software, or a service that a covered entity developed or provided in whole or in part [@CL-CMP-8472; @CL-CMP-8473]. The third reaches an item that contains components using hardware or software that a covered entity developed in whole or in part [@CL-CMP-8474].

The covered-entity definition reaches Kaspersky Lab, its successors including a change in name, entities under common control or in a control relationship with it, and entities that it majority owns [@CL-CMP-8475; @CL-CMP-8476].

This section defines terms only. They state no duty, dollar threshold, deadline or exception for any contractor or agency [@CL-CMP-8471]. The prohibition that uses these terms, and how it is prescribed and flowed down, are not restated here. See [Kaspersky and ByteDance prohibitions](../regulatory-framework/cmp-kaspersky-and-bytedance-prohibitions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company reviews 3 products.

Product 1 is software provided by a firm under common control with Kaspersky Lab. That firm is a Kaspersky Lab covered entity [@CL-CMP-8476], so product 1 is provided by a covered entity and is a Kaspersky Lab covered article [@CL-CMP-8472].

Product 2 is a service the company built. One library inside it was developed in part by a Kaspersky Lab covered entity. The service includes software developed in part by a covered entity, so it is a Kaspersky Lab covered article [@CL-CMP-8473].

Product 3 is not developed or provided by a covered entity, includes nothing so developed or provided, and contains no components using hardware or software that a covered entity developed. None of the three limbs is met [@CL-CMP-8472; @CL-CMP-8473; @CL-CMP-8474].

## Never confuse

A covered article with a covered entity. The entity is the organization the definition lists, while the article is the hardware, software, or service [@CL-CMP-8472; @CL-CMP-8475].

The second covered-article limb with the third. The second concerns an item that includes hardware, software, or a service developed or provided by a covered entity, and the third concerns components using hardware or software that a covered entity developed [@CL-CMP-8473; @CL-CMP-8474].

## Company calibration

Which storage, transfer and assistant tools IT approves for company data, which other hardware, software and services the company uses or supplies, and who confirms whether each is a covered article. Not established. [@Q-016]
