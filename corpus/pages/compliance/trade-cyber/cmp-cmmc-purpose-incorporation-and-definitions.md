## Purpose

Shows what the CMMC Program rule is for, which standards it draws on, and the exact meaning of the terms a contractor meets in an assessment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Contractor information systems that process, store, or transmit FCI or CUI, among the system types the rule lists [@CL-CMP-3201] |

The rule establishes requirements for defense contractors and subcontractors to implement prescribed cybersecurity standards for safeguarding FCI and CUI [@CL-CMP-3200]. It also establishes requirements for conducting an assessment of compliance with the applicable prescribed standard for contractor information systems that process, store, or transmit FCI or CUI, among the system types the paragraph lists [@CL-CMP-3201]. Who the rule binds and how it phases in are on the [program scope and phases](cmp-cmmc-program-scope-and-phases.md) card and are not restated here.

The program utilizes the security standards set forth in 48 CFR 52.204-21 and in NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 [@CL-CMP-3202]. It also utilizes, as applicable, selected requirements from NIST SP 800-172 [@CL-CMP-3203; @CL-CMP-3202]. The rule defines the CMMC Security Requirements as the 15 Level 1 requirements listed in 48 CFR 52.204-21(b)(1), the 110 Level 2 requirements from NIST SP 800-171 R2, and the 24 Level 3 requirements selected from NIST SP 800-172 [@CL-CMP-3205].

## How it works

Certain material is incorporated by reference into the rule with the approval of the Director of the Federal Register [@CL-CMP-3204]. An assessment is the testing or evaluation of security controls to determine the extent to which the controls are implemented correctly, operating as intended, and producing the desired outcome with respect to meeting the security requirements for an information system or organization [@CL-CMP-3208]. A Level 2 certification assessment is the activity a C3PAO performs to evaluate an OSC's information system when the OSC seeks a CMMC Status of Level 2 (C3PAO) [@CL-CMP-3209]. CMMC Status is the result of meeting or exceeding the minimum required score for the corresponding assessment [@CL-CMP-3206]. The status of an OSA information system is officially stored in SPRS, and it is also presented on a Certificate of CMMC Status if the assessment was conducted by a C3PAO or DCMA DIBCAC [@CL-CMP-3207]. In the acronym list, OSA stands for Organization Seeking Assessment and OSC stands for Organization Seeking Certification [@CL-CMP-3326].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A supplier with 40 employees runs one enclave that holds CUI. It asks a C3PAO to evaluate the enclave. The C3PAO's team tests the controls and the supplier meets or exceeds the minimum required score. The result is a CMMC Status of Level 2 (C3PAO). The status is stored in SPRS and shown on a Certificate of CMMC Status. The assessment was the activity, and the status is the result.

## Never confuse

An assessment and a CMMC Status are different things. The assessment is the testing or evaluation of security controls [@CL-CMP-3208], and the status is the result of meeting or exceeding the minimum required score for the corresponding assessment [@CL-CMP-3206]. The kinds of assessment differ by who performs them. A Level 2 certification assessment belongs to a C3PAO [@CL-CMP-3209], while a Level 3 certification assessment belongs to DCMA DIBCAC [@CL-CMP-3325]. The levels and what each one assesses are on the [model levels and scoping](cmp-cmmc-model-levels-and-scoping.md) card and the [certification assessment](cmp-cmmc-level-2-assessment.md) card. The roles of the assessing parties are on the [third-party assessment organization requirements](cmp-cmmc-c3pao-requirements.md) card, the [DIBCAC assessment](cmp-cmmc-level-3-assessment.md) card and the [PMO and DIBCAC roles](cmp-cmmc-pmo-and-dcma-dibcac-roles.md) card. The bodies that accredit and certify them are on the [Accreditation Body](cmp-cmmc-accreditation-body.md) card, the [CAICO and certified assessors](cmp-cmmc-caico-and-certified-assessors.md) card and the [instructors and certified professionals](cmp-cmmc-instructors-and-certified-professionals.md) card.
