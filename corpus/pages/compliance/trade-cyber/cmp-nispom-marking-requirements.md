## Purpose

Shows what markings classified material carries, where the printed section sends the contractor for the detailed marking formats, and how markings change after a downgrade or declassification.

| Applies to | Contractors marking classified information and material [@CL-CMP-3463] |

This card rests on the NISPOM, the rule for work involving classified information, and not on the FAR. Contractors will clearly mark all classified information and material to convey to the holder the level of classification assigned, the portions that contain or reveal classified information and the period of time protection is required [@CL-CMP-3463]. The markings must also convey the identity of the classifier by name and position or personal identifier, the sources for derivative classification, and any other notations required for protection of the information [@CL-CMP-3464]. For the detailed formats, contractors will use the marking guidance conveyed in 32 CFR 2001.22 through 2001.26 and its companion ISOO booklet on marking classified national security information, subject to the alternative guidance named in the rest of the sentence [@CL-CMP-3465]. CSA specific provided guidance for marking derivatively classified information and material, and what the applicable security classification guide requires, are named as an alternative introduced by the word or [@CL-CMP-3466].

Before taking any action to downgrade or declassify information, the contractor will seek guidance from the GCA, and if the GCA approves the action the contractor will cancel all old classification markings with the new markings substituted, whenever practical [@CL-CMP-3467]. The next sentence says that for documents, at a minimum the outside of the front cover, the title page, the first page and the outside of the back will reflect the new classification markings or include the designation UNCLASSIFIED [@CL-CMP-3468]. When a contractor removes downgraded or declassified documents or material from storage for use or for transmittal outside the contractor location, they must be re-marked as the paragraph on downgrading and declassifying sets out [@CL-CMP-3503]. U.S. documents containing FGI must be marked on the front with a statement naming the country of origin, and the portions must be marked to identify both the country and the classification level [@CL-CMP-3469].

## How it works

Marking tells the holder what level applies, which portions are classified and how long protection lasts [@CL-CMP-3463]. It also records who classified the material and from what source [@CL-CMP-3464]. For the general detailed formats the section sends the contractor to the guidance it names [@CL-CMP-3465; @CL-CMP-3466]. A downgrade or declassification starts with GCA guidance, and for documents the re-marking reaches the outside of the front cover, the title page, the first page and the outside of the back [@CL-CMP-3467; @CL-CMP-3468]. Downgraded or declassified material that a contractor removes from storage for use or for transmittal outside the contractor location must be re-marked as that paragraph sets out [@CL-CMP-3503].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds a report that the GCA has approved for declassification. The contractor takes the report out of storage to send it outside its location, cancels the old markings and substitutes the new ones where practical. The report's front cover, title page, first page and back cover then show the new markings or the word UNCLASSIFIED.

Answer: the contractor asks the GCA first, then re-marks at least those four places on the document.

## Never confuse

Marking a document is not the same as deciding its classification. The contractor seeks guidance from the GCA before any downgrade or declassification action [@CL-CMP-3467]. The duties of employees who classify derivatively are on the [NISPOM classification duties and challenges](cmp-nispom-classification-duties-and-challenges.md) card, the handling of foreign government information itself is on the [NISPOM international transfers and foreign government information](cmp-nispom-international-transfers-and-foreign-government-information.md) card, and storage of marked material is on the [NISPOM safeguarding of classified information](cmp-nispom-safeguarding-classified-information.md) card.
