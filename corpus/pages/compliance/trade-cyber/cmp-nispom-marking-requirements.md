## Purpose

Shows what markings classified material carries, where the printed section sends the contractor for the detailed marking formats, and how markings change after a downgrade or declassification.

## The rule

| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Contractors marking classified information and material [@CL-CMP-3463] |

Contractors will clearly mark all classified information and material to convey to the holder the level of classification assigned, the portions that contain or reveal classified information, the period of time protection is required [@CL-CMP-3463], the identity (by name and position or personal identifier) of the classifier, the source(s) for derivative classification, and any other notations required for protection of the information [@CL-CMP-3464]. Contractors will use the marking guidance conveyed in 32 CFR 2001.22 through 2001.26 and its companion document, ISOO booklet "Marking Classified National Security Information," or CSA specific provided guidance for marking derivatively classified information and material and as required by applicable security classification guide [@CL-CMP-3465; @CL-CMP-3466].

Prior to taking any action to downgrade or declassify information, the contractor will seek guidance from the GCA, and if the GCA approves such action, the contractor will cancel all old classification markings with the new markings substituted, whenever practical [@CL-CMP-3467]. For documents, at a minimum the outside of the front cover, the title page, the first page, and the outside of the back will reflect the new classification markings, or include the designation UNCLASSIFIED [@CL-CMP-3468]. When a contractor removes documents or material that have been downgraded or declassified from storage for use or for transmittal outside the contractor location, the documents or material must be re-marked pursuant to paragraph (i)(1)(i) or (i)(1)(ii) in the section [@CL-CMP-3503]. U.S. documents containing FGI must be marked on the front, "THIS DOCUMENT CONTAINS (indicate country of origin) INFORMATION," and in addition the portions must be marked to identify both the country and classification level (for example, (UK-C), (GE-C)) [@CL-CMP-3469].

## How it works

Contractors will clearly mark all classified information and material to convey to the holder the level of classification assigned, the portions that contain or reveal classified information, the period of time protection is required, the identity (by name and position or personal identifier) of the classifier, the source(s) for derivative classification, and any other notations required for protection of the information [@CL-CMP-3463; @CL-CMP-3464]. Contractors will use the marking guidance conveyed in 32 CFR 2001.22 through 2001.26 and its companion ISOO booklet, or CSA specific provided guidance for marking derivatively classified information and material and as required by applicable security classification guide [@CL-CMP-3465; @CL-CMP-3466]. Prior to taking any action to downgrade or declassify information, the contractor will seek guidance from the GCA, and if the GCA approves such action, will cancel all old classification markings with the new markings substituted whenever practical; for documents, at a minimum the outside of the front cover, the title page, the first page, and the outside of the back will reflect the new classification markings or include the designation UNCLASSIFIED [@CL-CMP-3467; @CL-CMP-3468]. When a contractor removes documents or material that have been downgraded or declassified from storage for use or for transmittal outside the contractor location, the documents or material must be re-marked pursuant to paragraph (i)(1)(i) or (i)(1)(ii) in the section [@CL-CMP-3503].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds a report that the GCA has approved for declassification. The contractor takes the report out of storage to send it outside its location, cancels the old markings and substitutes the new ones where practical. The report's front cover, title page, first page and back cover then show the new markings or the word UNCLASSIFIED.

Answer: the contractor asks the GCA first, then re-marks at least those four places on the document.
