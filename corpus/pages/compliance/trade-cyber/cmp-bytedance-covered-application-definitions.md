## Purpose

Defines the two terms printed in the FAR subpart on the prohibition on a ByteDance covered application: covered application and information technology.

## The rule

|---|---|

| RFO citation | Not established. |

The section prints the definitions at FAR 4.2201 [@CL-CMP-8477]. The terms have those meanings as used in the subpart [@CL-CMP-8477].

Covered application means the social networking service TikTok or any successor application or service developed or provided by ByteDance Limited or an entity owned by ByteDance Limited [@CL-CMP-8478].

The definition of information technology is introduced as the term defined in section 11101(6) of title 40 of the United States Code, followed by three numbered limbs [@CL-CMP-8479].

Limb 1: information technology means any equipment or interconnected system or subsystem of equipment, used in the automatic acquisition, storage, analysis, evaluation, manipulation, management, movement, control, display, switching, interchange, transmission, or reception of data or information by the executive agency [@CL-CMP-8480]. Limb 1 applies if the equipment is used by the executive agency directly or is used by a contractor under a contract with the executive agency that requires the use of that equipment, or the use of that equipment to a significant extent in the performance of a service or the furnishing of a product [@CL-CMP-8481].

Limb 2: information technology includes computers, ancillary equipment (including imaging peripherals, input, output, and storage devices necessary for security and surveillance), and peripheral equipment designed to be controlled by the central processing unit of a computer [@CL-CMP-8482]. It also includes software, firmware and similar procedures, services (including support services), and related resources [@CL-CMP-8483].

Limb 3: information technology does not include any equipment acquired by a Federal contractor incidental to a Federal contract [@CL-CMP-8484].

## How it works

The covered-application definition has two parts: the named service TikTok, and any successor application or service developed or provided by ByteDance Limited or by an entity owned by ByteDance Limited [@CL-CMP-8478].

Limb 1 of the information technology definition turns on what the equipment is used for, namely the automatic acquisition, storage, analysis, evaluation, manipulation, management, movement, control, display, switching, interchange, transmission, or reception of data or information by the executive agency [@CL-CMP-8480], and on who uses it: the executive agency uses it directly, or a contractor uses it under a contract with the executive agency that requires the use [@CL-CMP-8481]. Limb 2 lists what the term includes [@CL-CMP-8482; @CL-CMP-8483]. Limb 3 excludes equipment acquired by a Federal contractor incidental to a Federal contract [@CL-CMP-8484].

The definitions apply as used in the subpart [@CL-CMP-8477]. For the prohibition that uses these terms, see [Kaspersky and ByteDance prohibitions](../regulatory-framework/cmp-kaspersky-and-bytedance-prohibitions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor uses 3 items on a contract with an executive agency.

The first is TikTok on a work phone. It is the named social networking service, so it is a covered application [@CL-CMP-8478].

The second is a printer that the contractor acquired incidental to the contract. Peripheral equipment designed to be controlled by a computer's central processing unit is within limb 2 [@CL-CMP-8482]. Limb 3 still excludes equipment acquired by a Federal contractor incidental to a Federal contract, so the printer is not information technology under this definition [@CL-CMP-8484].

The third is a workstation that the contract requires the contractor to use and that the contractor did not acquire incidental to the contract. Assume it is used in the automatic storage and manipulation of data or information by the executive agency [@CL-CMP-8480]. It is a computer [@CL-CMP-8482], and a contractor uses it under a contract with the executive agency that requires the use of that equipment [@CL-CMP-8481], so it is information technology.

## Never confuse

A covered application with information technology. The covered application is the named social networking service TikTok or any successor application or service developed or provided by ByteDance Limited or an entity owned by ByteDance Limited [@CL-CMP-8478], while information technology includes computers, ancillary equipment, peripheral equipment, software, firmware and similar procedures, services and related resources [@CL-CMP-8482; @CL-CMP-8483].

Limb 2 with limb 3: limb 2 lists what information technology includes, while limb 3 excludes equipment acquired by a Federal contractor incidental to a Federal contract [@CL-CMP-8482; @CL-CMP-8484].
