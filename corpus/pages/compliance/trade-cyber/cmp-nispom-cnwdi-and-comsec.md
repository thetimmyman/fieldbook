## Purpose

Shows who may have access to CNWDI and COMSEC information and which briefings, approvals and records the contractor must keep.

## The rule

| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | Access to DoD CNWDI and access to COMSEC information by a contractor [@CL-CMP-3482; @CL-CMP-3486] |

For CNWDI, access shall be granted to the absolute minimum number of employees who require it for the accomplishment of assigned responsibilities on a classified contract [@CL-CMP-3482]. Before having access to CNWDI, employees will be briefed on its sensitivity by the FSO or the FSO's alternate, and the FSO will be initially briefed by a USG representative [@CL-CMP-3483]. Contractors will not disclose CNWDI to subcontractors without the prior written approval of the GCA [@CL-CMP-3484]. Contractors will annotate CNWDI access in the CSA-designated database for all employees who have been authorized access to CNWDI [@CL-CMP-3485].

For COMSEC, access by a contractor requires a final entity eligibility determination and a USG-issued final PCL at the appropriate level, except that an Interim TOP SECRET entity eligibility determination or PCL is valid for access to COMSEC at the SECRET and CONFIDENTIAL levels [@CL-CMP-3486]. All contractor employees who require access to classified COMSEC information in the performance of their duties will be briefed before access is granted [@CL-CMP-3487]. COMSEC debriefings are not required, and the contractor will maintain a record of all COMSEC briefings as specified by the appropriate COR [@CL-CMP-3488]. An employee granted access to cryptographic information will be debriefed and execute Section II of the SD 572 not later than 90 days from the date access is no longer required [@CL-CMP-3489].

## How it works

CNWDI access is granted to the absolute minimum number of employees who require it for the accomplishment of assigned responsibilities on a classified contract [@CL-CMP-3482]. Employees are briefed on its sensitivity before having access [@CL-CMP-3483], and the contractor annotates CNWDI access in the CSA-designated database for all employees who have been authorized access [@CL-CMP-3485]. CNWDI is not disclosed to a subcontractor without the prior written approval of the GCA [@CL-CMP-3484]. COMSEC access by a contractor requires a final entity eligibility determination and a USG-issued final PCL at the appropriate level [@CL-CMP-3486]. All contractor employees who require access to classified COMSEC information in the performance of their duties will be briefed before access is granted [@CL-CMP-3487]. The contractor will maintain a record of all COMSEC briefings as specified by the appropriate COR [@CL-CMP-3488]. Entity eligibility is explained on the [NISPOM entity eligibility determination (facility clearance)](cmp-nispom-entity-eligibility-determination.md) card, and the general briefings are on the [NISPOM security briefings, visits and classified meetings](cmp-nispom-training-briefings-visits-and-meetings.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has a final entity eligibility determination and wants three employees to work on a CNWDI task. Only one employee needs the information for the contract.

Answer: access goes to the one employee who needs it, who is briefed on CNWDI's sensitivity before having access, and whose access is annotated in the CSA-designated database. Any disclosure to a subcontractor needs the GCA's prior written approval.

## Never confuse

COMSEC debriefings are not required, but an employee granted access to cryptographic information will be debriefed and execute Section II of the SD 572 not later than 90 days from the date access is no longer required [@CL-CMP-3488; @CL-CMP-3489]. CNWDI access is held to the absolute minimum number of employees who require it [@CL-CMP-3482], and COMSEC access by a contractor requires a final entity eligibility determination and a USG-issued final PCL at the appropriate level, except that an Interim TOP SECRET entity eligibility determination or PCL is valid for access to COMSEC at the SECRET and CONFIDENTIAL levels [@CL-CMP-3486]. The general rules for storing and protecting classified information are on the [NISPOM safeguarding of classified information](cmp-nispom-safeguarding-classified-information.md) card, and the supplement for special access programs and nuclear-related information is on the [NISPOM supplement for special access programs and related controls](cmp-nispom-special-access-program-supplement.md) card.
