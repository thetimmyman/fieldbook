## Purpose

Shows what CMMC Instructors and CMMC Certified Professionals must meet, and the limits on what each role may do.

## The rule

| Applies to | CMMC Instructors and CCPs [@CL-CMP-3238; @CL-CMP-3241] |

CMMC Instructors shall obtain and maintain instructor designation or certification, as appropriate, from the CAICO, and that item opens the list of instructor requirements [@CL-CMP-3238]. One listed requirement is that a CMMC Instructor not provide CMMC consulting services while serving as an instructor, although subject to the Code of Professional Conduct and Conflict of Interest policies an instructor can serve on an assessment team [@CL-CMP-3239; @CL-CMP-3238]. A CCI with a valid CCP certification may instruct CCP candidates, while a CCI with a valid CCA certification may instruct CCP, CCA, and CCI candidates [@CL-CMP-3240].

A CMMC Certified Professional completes rigorous training on CMMC and the assessment process to provide advice, consulting, and recommendations to their OSA clients [@CL-CMP-3337]. CCPs are eligible to become CMMC Certified Assessors and can participate as a CCP on Level 2 certification assessments with CCA oversight where the CCA makes all final determinations [@CL-CMP-3241]. CCPs shall obtain and maintain certification from the CAICO, and that certification is valid for 3 years from the date of issuance [@CL-CMP-3242].

## How it works

The CAICO is responsible for certifying instructors and related professionals [@CL-CMP-3232], and only the CAICO may make decisions relating to examination certifications [@CL-CMP-3233]. For an instructor, the limits are about what may be taught and what else the instructor may do. A CCI's certification sets which candidates it may instruct [@CL-CMP-3240], and an instructor must not provide CMMC consulting services while serving as an instructor [@CL-CMP-3239]. For a CCP, the limit is about who decides. The CCP takes part in a Level 2 certification assessment under CCA oversight and the CCA makes all final determinations [@CL-CMP-3241]. The printed text lists further requirements for each role that this card does not set out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A person holds a valid CCP certification issued in year 1 and works as a CCI. Under the 3-year validity period the certification runs to the end of year 3. As a CCI with a valid CCP certification the person may instruct CCP candidates.

## Never confuse

A CCP is not a CCA. A CCP can take part in a Level 2 certification assessment, but only with CCA oversight, and the CCA makes all final determinations [@CL-CMP-3241]. An instructor and a consultant are different roles at any one time. An instructor must not provide CMMC consulting services while serving as an instructor [@CL-CMP-3239]. A CCP, by contrast, trains to provide advice, consulting, and recommendations to OSA clients [@CL-CMP-3337]. The assessment process itself is on the [certification assessment](cmp-cmmc-level-2-assessment.md) card. The body that certifies instructors is on the [CAICO and certified assessors](cmp-cmmc-caico-and-certified-assessors.md) card. The Accreditation Body is on the [Accreditation Body](cmp-cmmc-accreditation-body.md) card.
