## Purpose

Explains the solicitation representation in which a DoD offeror states whether it anticipates that cloud computing will be used under the contract that results from the solicitation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-9075] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.239-7009 [@CL-CMP-9075] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-9075] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to a solicitation that includes the provision [@CL-CMP-9077; @CL-CMP-9075] |

The offeror shall indicate, by checking the appropriate blank in paragraph (c) of the provision, whether the use of cloud computing is anticipated under the resultant contract [@CL-CMP-9075]. Paragraph (c) is a representation by the offeror [@CL-CMP-9275]. One blank states that the offeror does anticipate that cloud computing services will be used in the performance of any contract or subcontract resulting from the solicitation [@CL-CMP-9076]. The other states that it does not anticipate that use [@CL-CMP-9274].

The provision is used as prescribed in DFARS 239.7604(a) [@CL-CMP-9077].

## How it works

The provision first defines cloud computing as a model for enabling ubiquitous, convenient, on-demand network access to a shared pool of configurable computing resources, such as networks, servers, storage, applications and services [@CL-CMP-9073]. The resources are ones that can be rapidly provisioned and released with minimal management effort or service provider interaction [@CL-CMP-9272]. The definition includes other commercial terms such as on-demand self-service, broad network access, resource pooling, rapid elasticity and measured service [@CL-CMP-9273]. It also includes commercial offerings for software-as-a-service, infrastructure-as-a-service and platform-as-a-service [@CL-CMP-9074].

The duty to indicate falls on the offeror, and the offeror chooses the blank [@CL-CMP-9075]. Paragraph (b) asks whether the use of cloud computing is anticipated under the resultant contract [@CL-CMP-9075], and the blanks in paragraph (c) refer to any contract or subcontract resulting from the solicitation [@CL-CMP-9076; @CL-CMP-9274].

The offeror's answer matters later. Under the separate cloud computing services clause, a contractor that indicated in its offer, in response to provision 252.239-7009, that it does not anticipate the use of cloud computing services in the performance of a resultant contract, and that proposes after award to use cloud computing services, shall obtain approval from the Contracting Officer before using them [@CL-CMP-3990; @CL-CMP-3991]. That clause is covered in [Cloud computing services clause](cmp-cloud-computing-services-clause.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror expects to run part of the work on a hosted platform service and keep data in pooled storage, and 3 subcontractors will also use cloud services. The offeror checks the blank saying that it does anticipate cloud computing services [@CL-CMP-9076]. The definition covers platform-as-a-service and a model for on-demand network access to a shared pool of configurable resources [@CL-CMP-9073; @CL-CMP-9074]. The blank reaches subcontracts as well as the prime contract [@CL-CMP-9076].

A second offeror expects to do all the work on its own premises and checks the blank saying that it does not anticipate cloud computing services [@CL-CMP-9274]. If it later proposes after award to use cloud computing services in performance of the contract, the separate clause requires it to obtain approval from the Contracting Officer before using them, where that clause's offer condition is met [@CL-CMP-3990; @CL-CMP-3991].

## Never confuse

The representation provision and the clause differ in stage: the offeror indicates its answer by checking the appropriate blank in paragraph (c) of the provision [@CL-CMP-9075], while the contract clause on cloud computing services is at [Cloud computing services clause](cmp-cloud-computing-services-clause.md).

The definition includes commercial offerings for software-as-a-service, infrastructure-as-a-service, and platform-as-a-service [@CL-CMP-9074].

The blanks refer to any contract or subcontract resulting from the solicitation [@CL-CMP-9076; @CL-CMP-9274].
