## Purpose

Shows the DFARS definitions and policy for safeguarding covered defense information and reporting cyber incidents, and which provision or clause the DFARS prescribes for which solicitations and contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3246; @CL-CMP-3247] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 204.7304 [@CL-CMP-3338; @CL-CMP-3339; @CL-CMP-3340; @CL-CMP-3341] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3246; @CL-CMP-3247] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for which the DFARS prescribes the listed provisions and clauses, subject to the listed exceptions [@CL-CMP-3246; @CL-CMP-3247; @CL-CMP-3248; @CL-CMP-3249] |

The definition of covered defense information begins with unclassified controlled technical information or other information as described in the Controlled Unclassified Information Registry [@CL-CMP-3345]. That information must require safeguarding or dissemination controls pursuant to and consistent with law, regulations, and Governmentwide policies, and meet one of the two alternatives the definition then lists [@CL-CMP-3346]. The first alternative is that the information is marked or otherwise identified in the contract, task order, or delivery order and provided to the contractor by or on behalf of DoD in support of the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-3348]. The second alternative is that the information is collected, developed, received, transmitted, used, or stored by or on behalf of the contractor in support of the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-3347]. A covered contractor information system is an unclassified information system that is owned, or operated by or for, a contractor and that processes, stores, or transmits covered defense information [@CL-CMP-3344]. Rapidly report means within 72 hours of discovery of any cyber incident [@CL-CMP-3243]. Contractors and subcontractors are required to provide adequate security on all covered contractor information systems [@CL-CMP-3244]. They are also required to rapidly report cyber incidents directly to DoD [@CL-CMP-3245].

The DFARS section on solicitation provisions and contract clauses directs use of the provision at 252.204-7008, Compliance with Safeguarding Covered Defense Information Controls [@CL-CMP-3338]. That use covers all solicitations, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services, except solicitations solely for the acquisition of COTS items [@CL-CMP-3246; @CL-CMP-3338]. It directs use of the clause at 252.204-7012, Safeguarding Covered Defense Information and Cyber Incident Reporting [@CL-CMP-3339]. That use covers all solicitations and contracts, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services, except those solely for the acquisition of COTS items [@CL-CMP-3247; @CL-CMP-3339].

## How it works

The same section directs use of the provision at 252.204-7019, Notice of NIST SP 800-171 DoD Assessment Requirements [@CL-CMP-3340]. That use covers all solicitations, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services, except solicitations solely for the acquisition of COTS items [@CL-CMP-3248; @CL-CMP-3340]. It directs use of the clause at 252.204-7020, NIST SP 800-171 DoD Assessment Requirements [@CL-CMP-3341]. That use covers all solicitations and contracts, task orders, or delivery orders, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services, except those that are solely for the acquisition of COTS items [@CL-CMP-3249; @CL-CMP-3341]. The section also directs use of the clause at 252.204-7009, Limitations on the Use or Disclosure of Third-Party Contractor Reported Cyber Incident Information [@CL-CMP-3342]. That use covers solicitations and contracts, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services, for services that include support for the Government's activities related to safeguarding covered defense information and cyber incident reporting [@CL-CMP-3343; @CL-CMP-3342]. That clause has its own card, [limits on use of cyber incident and litigation support information](cmp-cyber-incident-information-and-litigation-support-limits.md). What the safeguarding clause requires is on the [safeguarding covered defense information](cmp-safeguarding-covered-defense-information.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buyer issues a solicitation for 500 custom parts and the solicitation is not solely for COTS items. The buyer includes the provision 252.204-7008 and the provision 252.204-7019. The resulting contract includes the clause 252.204-7012 and the clause 252.204-7020. A second solicitation is solely for COTS items, so those four are not directed for it.

## Never confuse

The two provisions, 252.204-7008 and 252.204-7019, are directed for all solicitations, with the stated exception for solicitations solely for COTS items [@CL-CMP-3246; @CL-CMP-3248], while the two clauses, 252.204-7012 and 252.204-7020, are directed for all solicitations and contracts, with the stated exception for COTS-only acquisitions [@CL-CMP-3247; @CL-CMP-3249]. For the CMMC Program, see the [program scope and phases](cmp-cmmc-program-scope-and-phases.md) card. The assessment provision and clause are on the [DoD assessment notice and requirements](cmp-nist-sp-800-171-dod-assessment-requirements.md) card.
