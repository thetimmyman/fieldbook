## Purpose

Shows what must be reported on contracted services and when, and what Alternate A changes in the annual representations and certifications provision.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-2869; @CL-CMP-2873] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.204-7023(b) [@CL-CMP-2869]; DFARS 252.204-7007 [@CL-CMP-2873] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2869] |
| Applies to | Contracts and orders that include the contracted services reporting clause [@CL-CMP-2869] |

Under the basic clause, the contractor shall report annually, by October 31, on the services performed under the contract or order, including any first-tier subcontracts, during the preceding Government fiscal year [@CL-CMP-2869]. For the contract or order, the contractor shall report the total dollar amount invoiced for services performed during the preceding Government fiscal year [@CL-CMP-2870]. It shall also report the number of contractor direct labor hours, including first-tier subcontractor direct labor hours as applicable, expended on the services performed during the previous Government fiscal year [@CL-CMP-2870; @CL-CMP-2871].

Under Alternate I, the contractor shall report annually, by October 31, on services performed during the preceding Government fiscal year under the contract or agreement [@CL-CMP-0136], for each order, including any first-tier subcontract, which exceeds $3 million for services in the following service acquisition portfolio groups: logistics management, equipment-related, knowledge-based, and electronics and communications services [@CL-CMP-2872].

## How it works

Under the clause, if the Government believes that revisions to the contractor's reported information are warranted, it will notify the contractor, and upon notification the contractor shall revise the reported information or provide the Government with a supporting rationale for the information [@CL-CMP-2921]. Alternate A substitutes its paragraphs (b), (d), and (e) for paragraphs (b) and (d) of the provision at FAR 52.204-8 [@CL-CMP-2873]. The offeror has completed the annual representations and certifications electronically via the SAM website, and after reviewing the SAM database information the offeror verifies [@CL-CMP-0137] that the applicable representations and certifications currently posted electronically have been entered or updated within the last 12 months, are current, accurate, complete and applicable to the solicitation, including the business size standard applicable to the NAICS code referenced for it, as of the date of the offer, and are incorporated in the offer by reference, except for the changes identified below in the provision [@CL-CMP-2874; @CL-CMP-8051; @CL-CMP-8052]. The amended representations and certifications are also incorporated in the offer and are current, accurate, and complete as of the date of the offer [@CL-CMP-0138]. Any changes the offeror provides with its offer are applicable to the solicitation only and do not result in an update to the representations and certifications in the SAM database [@CL-CMP-2924]. Under Alternate A paragraph (d)(1), the representation at 252.204-7016, Covered Defense Telecommunications Equipment or Services, in the SAM database applies to all solicitations [@CL-CMP-2922]; the [Prohibition on covered defense telecommunications equipment or services](cmp-covered-defense-telecommunications-equipment-prohibition.md) card covers that representation.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor holds a services order. For the preceding fiscal year it invoiced 2,000,000 and its staff and first-tier subcontractors worked 18,000 direct labor hours. It reports both figures by October 31. Under Alternate I, only an order over 3,000,000 in the listed groups would be reported.

## Never confuse

Under Alternate I, the contractor reports on each order which exceeds $3 million for services in the listed service acquisition portfolio groups [@CL-CMP-2872]. Alternate A substitutes its paragraphs (b), (d), and (e) for paragraphs (b) and (d) of the provision at FAR 52.204-8 [@CL-CMP-2873].
