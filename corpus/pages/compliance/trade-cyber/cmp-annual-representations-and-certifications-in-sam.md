## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must keep current in SAM, and which solicitation provision and contract clause apply.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2982] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1201 [@CL-CMP-2982]; FAR 4.1202 [@CL-CMP-2984] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2982] |
| Applies to | Offerors and quoters, as a part of required registration in SAM [@CL-CMP-2982] |

Offerors and quoters are required to complete electronic annual representations and certifications in SAM as a part of required registration, and the section cross-refers to FAR 4.1102 on registration [@CL-CMP-2982]. All registrants are required to review and update the representations and certifications submitted to SAM as necessary, but at least annually, to ensure they are kept current, accurate, and complete [@CL-CMP-0086; @CL-CMP-2983], and the representations and certifications are effective until one year from date of submission or update to SAM [@CL-CMP-2983].

The provision at 52.204-8, Annual Representations and Certifications, is to be inserted in solicitations, except for solicitations for commercial products or commercial services issued under part 12 [@CL-CMP-2984]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.204-19, Incorporation by Reference of Representations and Certifications, in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-2985]. The contracting officer shall incorporate the representations and certifications by reference in the contract, with a pointer to the clause at 52.204-19 or, for commercial products or commercial services, to 52.212-4(v) [@CL-CMP-2945].

## How it works

All registrants are required to review and update the representations and certifications in SAM as necessary, but at least annually [@CL-CMP-0086; @CL-CMP-2983]. They are effective until one year from the date of submission or update [@CL-CMP-2983]. The contracting officer incorporates them by reference in the contract [@CL-CMP-2945]. Registration itself is on [SAM registration and awards](../../market/feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md), and the defense supplement's Alternate A to the annual representations provision is on [Contracted services reporting and Alternate A representations](cmp-contracted-services-reporting-and-alt-a-representations.md). The section has further rules on small business updates and contract file records, which this card does not set out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror submits its SAM representations on a given day. They stay effective until the same day one year later unless the offeror submits an update first. The offeror reviews them at least annually and updates them as necessary, so it updates sooner if a fact changes.

## Never confuse

The provision and the clause differ in where they go: the provision at 52.204-8 is inserted in solicitations, with an exception for commercial products or services under part 12 [@CL-CMP-2984], while the clause at 52.204-19 is inserted in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-2985]. This card does not set out what the provision's paragraphs or its alternate contain.
