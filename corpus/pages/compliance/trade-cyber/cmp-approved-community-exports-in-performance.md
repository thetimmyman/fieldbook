## Purpose

Shows the definitions, the marking, handling and re-transfer duties, and the flowdown in the clause on exports by approved community members in performance of the contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4027] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7047 [@CL-CMP-4027] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | JUN 2013 [@CL-CMP-10292] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4027] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, for exports or transfers of qualifying defense articles in performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4030] |

The clause defines Approved Community as the United States Government, United States entities that are registered and eligible exporters, and certain government and industry facilities in Australia or the United Kingdom that are approved and listed by the United States Government [@CL-CMP-4027]. It defines qualifying defense articles as defense articles that are not exempt from the scope of the defense trade cooperation treaties as defined in the cited sections of the International Traffic in Arms Regulations [@CL-CMP-4028].

The permission to use the treaties in performance of the contract is subject to the other terms and conditions of the contract that affect the acceptability of foreign sources or foreign end products, components, parts, or materials [@CL-CMP-4029]. Within that limit, Approved Community members are permitted, but not required, to use the treaties for exports or transfers of qualifying defense articles in performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4029; @CL-CMP-4030]. If the contractor is an Approved Community member, it agrees to comply with the requirements of the treaties, the implementing arrangements, the International Traffic in Arms Regulations, and corresponding regulations of the United States Government and the government of Australia or the government of the United Kingdom, as applicable [@CL-CMP-4031; @CL-CMP-0087]. Under the same agreement, paragraph (e)(2)(i) requires the contractor, before the export or transfer of a qualifying defense article, to mark, identify, transmit, store, and handle any defense articles provided for the purpose of responding to such solicitations, as well as any defense articles provided with or developed pursuant to their responses to such solicitations, in accordance with the treaties, the implementing arrangements, and corresponding regulations of the United States Government and the government of Australia or the government of the United Kingdom, as applicable, including, but not limited to, the marking and classification requirements described in the applicable regulations [@CL-CMP-4031; @CL-CMP-4032; @CL-CMP-7361; @CL-CMP-7362; @CL-CMP-7363]. Paragraph (e)(2)(ii) provides that the contractor shall comply with the re-transfer or re-export provisions of the treaties, the implementing arrangements, and corresponding regulations of the United States Government and the government of Australia or the government of the United Kingdom, as applicable [@CL-CMP-4059]. Any conduct by the contractor that falls outside the scope of the treaties, the implementing arrangements, and the cited sections of the International Traffic in Arms Regulations is subject to all applicable requirements of those regulations, including criminal, civil, and administrative penalties or sanctions, as well as all other United States statutory and regulatory requirements outside of those regulations, including regulations of the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives at 27 CFR parts 447, 478 and 479, which the treaties do not affect [@CL-CMP-4058; @CL-CMP-7368]. The contractor shall include the substance of the clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all subcontracts that may require exports or transfers of qualifying defense articles in connection with deliveries under the contract [@CL-CMP-4033].

## How it works

The clause defines who is in the Approved Community and which defense articles qualify, and then, subject to the other terms and conditions of the contract that affect the acceptability of foreign sources or foreign end products, components, parts, or materials, permits, but does not require, treaty use for those articles [@CL-CMP-4027; @CL-CMP-4028; @CL-CMP-4029; @CL-CMP-4030]. A contractor that is a member agrees to comply with the treaties and regulations and takes on marking, handling and re-transfer duties before an export or transfer of a qualifying defense article, and conduct outside the treaties is subject to the International Traffic in Arms Regulations and to all other United States statutory and regulatory requirements [@CL-CMP-4031; @CL-CMP-0087; @CL-CMP-4032; @CL-CMP-4059; @CL-CMP-4058; @CL-CMP-7368]. The contractor shall include the substance of the clause in all subcontracts that may require exports or transfers of qualifying defense articles in connection with deliveries [@CL-CMP-4033].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers a sensor kit to a partner facility in the United Kingdom that is on the approved list. The kit is a qualifying defense article, so the contractor may use the treaty route, but it is not required to. It marks and handles the articles as the treaty rules require before the export. It puts the clause into the subcontract for the housing supplier, because that subcontract may require such an export for deliveries.

## Never confuse

The clause and the provision differ in phase: this clause covers exports and transfers in performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4030], while the provision on exports in response to a solicitation covers exports and transfers in preparing a response to the solicitation [@CL-CMP-4023] and has an offeror representation [@CL-CMP-4025]; see [Exports by approved community members in response to a solicitation](cmp-approved-community-exports-in-response-to-solicitation.md). The export-controlled items clause requires the contractor to comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding export-controlled items [@CL-CMP-4017]; see [Export-controlled items clause](cmp-export-controlled-items-clause.md).
