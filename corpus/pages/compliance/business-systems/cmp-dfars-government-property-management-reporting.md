## Purpose

Shows what the contractor must report about Government property, how fast, and which data elements its property records must be able to record.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3867] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.245-7005 [@CL-CMP-3867] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3867] |
| Applies to | Contractors holding Government property under contracts with the clause [@CL-CMP-3867] |

The Contractor shall use the Government Furnished Property module of the Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment to report, among listed matters, receipt of Government-furnished property (GFP) and the transfer of that property to another DoD contract [@CL-CMP-3864]. The module is also to be used to report the shipment of GFP to the Government or to a contractor [@CL-CMP-3896], to report when serially-managed items of GFP are incorporated into a higher-level component, assembly, or end item, and to report the loss of Government property in accordance with paragraph (f)(1)(vii) of the FAR 52.245-1 clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3897]. It is further to be used to complete the plant clearance inventory schedule in accordance with paragraph (j)(2) of the FAR 52.245-1 clause, unless disposition instructions are otherwise included in the contract [@CL-CMP-3898], and to submit a request to buy back or to convert to GFP items of Contractor-acquired property [@CL-CMP-3899]. The Contractor shall report the updated status of the property to the module within 7 business days of the date the change in status occurs, unless otherwise specified in the contract [@CL-CMP-3865]. When the property is in the possession of subcontractors, the Contractor shall ensure that reporting is accomplished using the data elements required for the records [@CL-CMP-3866]. The status-report deadline applies to the first four reporting duties listed in paragraph (b)(1) of the clause, receipt, transfer, shipment and incorporation into a higher-level item [@CL-CMP-3864; @CL-CMP-3865; @CL-CMP-3896; @CL-CMP-3897].

To facilitate that reporting, the Contractor's property records, in addition to the requirements of paragraph (f)(1)(iii) of the FAR 52.245-1 clause, shall enable recording of the listed data elements [@CL-CMP-3867]. Two listed elements are the national stock number, with the manufacturer's CAGE code and part number or the model number used if a national stock number is not available, and the CAGE code on the accountable Government contract [@CL-CMP-3868]. Four others are the received or sent date, the accountable Government contract number, the serial number for serially-managed items that do not have a unique item identifier (UII), and the supply condition code, which is only required for reporting of reparable items [@CL-CMP-3869].

Separately, paragraph (d)(1) has the Contractor assign the UII to, and mark with an item unique identification data matrix, the reparable items identified as serially managed in the GFP attachment, when the technical drawing for the item is accessible to the Contractor and includes the data matrix location and marking method [@CL-CMP-3900]. The clause for item unique identification generally is on [Item unique identification and valuation clause](cmp-dfars-item-unique-identification.md).

## How it works

The module is the reporting channel, and the data elements in the property records are what feed it [@CL-CMP-3864; @CL-CMP-3867]. The status report is due within 7 business days unless the contract says otherwise [@CL-CMP-3865]. Where subcontractors hold the property, the Contractor remains the party that ensures reporting uses the required data elements [@CL-CMP-3866]. The printed text of this clause sets out reporting and records duties, but it does not set out property management system criteria or rules for when a property management system is acceptable or unacceptable, so this source does not establish them; see [Contractor Property Management System Administration clause](../../close-revrec/fixed-assets/cls-property-management-system.md). The definition of contractor business systems lists the property management system, if the contract includes the clause at 252.245-7003, which is numbered differently from this clause [@CL-CMP-1202]; see [Contractor business systems](cmp-contractor-business-systems.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor receives a reparable item as Government-furnished property on a Monday. The change in status must reach the Government Furnished Property module within 7 business days unless the contract says otherwise. The contractor's record for the item must be able to hold a national stock number, the accountable contract number, the received date, the serial number if the item has no UII, and the supply condition code.

## Never confuse

The records and reporting duties here add to the requirements of the FAR Government property clause rather than replace them [@CL-CMP-3867]; see [Government property clause](../../close-revrec/fixed-assets/cls-government-property-clause.md). Having records that can hold the data elements does not show that a property management system meets any criteria, and this clause does not state such criteria.

## Company calibration

Whether the company's property management system is currently approved and whether its property records can hold every listed data element. [@Q-034]
