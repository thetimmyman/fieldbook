## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must submit about its earned value management system (EVMS) with a proposal, and how the two proposal-value tiers differ.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3834] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.234-7001 [@CL-CMP-3834] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3834] |
| Applies to | Solicitations where the DFARS prescribes the provision [@CL-CMP-3834] |

The provision is titled Notice of Earned Value Management System, carries the date April 2008, and is prescribed at DFARS 234.203(1) [@CL-CMP-3834]. If the offeror submits a proposal in the amount of $50,000,000 or more, the offeror shall provide documentation that the Cognizant Federal Agency has determined that the proposed system complies with the EVMS guidelines [@CL-CMP-3835]. If it proposes a system not determined to be in compliance, it shall submit a comprehensive plan for compliance with the guidelines in ANSI/EIA-748 [@CL-CMP-3836], and that plan must provide milestones that indicate when the offeror anticipates that the system will be compliant [@CL-CMP-3837].

If the offeror submits a proposal in an amount less than $50,000,000, the offeror shall submit a written description of the management procedures it will use and maintain in the performance of any resultant contract to comply with the requirements of the Earned Value Management System clause [@CL-CMP-3838].

## How it works

The proposal value sets the tier: a proposal of $50,000,000 or more is treated differently from one below that amount [@CL-CMP-3835; @CL-CMP-3838]. In the higher tier the proof is a Cognizant Federal Agency determination, with a compliance plan as the fallback [@CL-CMP-3835; @CL-CMP-3836]. In that tier the Government reserves the right to perform reviews of the system when deemed necessary to verify compliance [@CL-CMP-3888]. In the lower tier the written description includes, as one listed element, a matrix that correlates each guideline in ANSI/EIA-748 to the corresponding process in the offeror's written management procedures [@CL-CMP-3839], and an offeror whose system the agency has determined to be in compliance may submit a copy of that determination instead [@CL-CMP-3840]. The provision also requires the offeror to identify the subcontractors, or the subcontracted effort if subcontractors have not been selected, to whom the EVMS requirements will apply, and the Government must agree to them [@CL-CMP-3841]. The offeror is responsible for ensuring that the selected subcontractors comply with the requirements of the Earned Value Management System clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3889].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror submits a proposal of 80,000,000 using a system that has no determination of compliance. It must submit a comprehensive plan for compliance with milestones. A second proposal of 20,000,000 uses a system that the Cognizant Federal Agency has determined to be compliant. The offeror may submit a copy of that determination instead of the written description.

## Never confuse

This is the proposal-stage notice, not the clause that governs performance; the written description in the lower tier is aimed at complying with the Earned Value Management System clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3838]. The definition of contractor business systems lists the earned value management system, if the contract includes the clause at 252.234-7002, which is a different clause from this notice provision [@CL-CMP-1200]; see [Contractor business systems](cmp-contractor-business-systems.md). For the clause itself, see [Earned Value Management System clause](../../program-finance/evms/prg-earned-value-management-system.md). The FAR version of the notice is on [Notice of earned value management system, preaward and postaward review](../../program-finance/evms/prg-far-evms-notice-provisions.md), and how a compliance determination is made is on [EVMS compliance determination](../../program-finance/evms/prg-evms-compliance-determination.md).
