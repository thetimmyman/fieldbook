## Purpose

Shows when the contracting officer uses the contractor business systems clause, and what DoD policy requires of a contractor's material management and accounting system (MMAS) and who decides whether that system is acceptable.

The clause at DFARS 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, is used in solicitations and contracts, other than in contracts with educational institutions, Federally Funded Research and Development Centers, or University Affiliated Research Centers operated by educational institutions [@CL-CMP-7262]. Within that scope it is used when the resulting contract will be a covered contract as defined in DFARS 242.7000(a) and the solicitation or contract includes any of the clauses that the section lists [@CL-CMP-7263]. A covered contract is a contract that is subject to the Cost Accounting Standards under chapter 15 of title 41 of the United States Code, as implemented in regulations found at 48 CFR 9903.201-1 [@CL-CMP-7449].

The six listed clauses are DFARS 252.215-7002, Cost Estimating System Requirements, DFARS 252.234-7002, Earned Value Management System, and DFARS 252.242-7004, Material Management and Accounting System [@CL-CMP-7447], together with DFARS 252.242-7006, Accounting System Administration, DFARS 252.244-7001, Contractor Purchasing System Administration, and DFARS 252.245-7003, Contractor Property Management System Administration [@CL-CMP-7448].

## How it works

DoD policy is for its contractors to have an MMAS that conforms to the standards in paragraph (d) of the clause at DFARS 252.242-7004, Material Management and Accounting System, so that the system reasonably forecasts material requirements [@CL-CMP-7264]. The policy also calls for the system to ensure that the costs of purchased and fabricated material charged or allocated to a contract are based on valid time-phased requirements [@CL-CMP-7450], and to maintain a consistent, equitable and unbiased logic for costing of material transactions [@CL-CMP-7451].

The policies, procedures, and standards in the subpart apply only when the contractor has contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold that are not for the acquisition of commercial products or commercial services and are either cost-reimbursement contracts or fixed-price contracts with progress payments made on the basis of costs incurred by the contractor as work progresses under the contract [@CL-CMP-2275; @CL-CMP-7498; @CL-CMP-7499]. They do not apply to small businesses, educational institutions, or nonprofit organizations [@CL-CMP-7600].

The cognizant contracting officer, in consultation with the auditor and functional specialist, if appropriate, shall determine the acceptability of the contractor's MMAS, approve or disapprove the system, and pursue correction of any weaknesses or deficiencies [@CL-CMP-7265]. In evaluating the acceptability of the contractor's MMAS, the contracting officer, in consultation with the auditor and functional specialist, if appropriate, shall determine whether the contractor's MMAS complies with the system criteria for an acceptable MMAS as prescribed in the clause at DFARS 252.242-7004 [@CL-CMP-0170; @CL-CMP-7266].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a contract that will be subject to the Cost Accounting Standards includes the clause at DFARS 252.242-7004. The contract is a covered contract and it includes one of the listed clauses, so the contracting officer uses the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005 [@CL-CMP-7263; @CL-CMP-7449; @CL-CMP-7447]. A second solicitation is for a contract with an educational institution that includes the same listed clause. The contracting officer does not use the business systems clause there, because contracts with educational institutions are outside the scope [@CL-CMP-7262].

For the first contract, the contractor is not a small business, an educational institution or a nonprofit organization, and the contract is a cost-reimbursement contract above the simplified acquisition threshold that is not for commercial products or commercial services, so the MMAS policy applies [@CL-CMP-7498; @CL-CMP-7499; @CL-CMP-7600]. The cognizant contracting officer reviews the contractor's MMAS in consultation with the auditor and functional specialist, if appropriate. The officer decides whether the system complies with the criteria in the clause at DFARS 252.242-7004, approves or disapproves it, and pursues correction of any weaknesses [@CL-CMP-7265; @CL-CMP-0170; @CL-CMP-7266].

## Never confuse

The business systems clause and the MMAS policy are different instruments: the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005 is used in solicitations and contracts, other than in contracts with educational institutions, Federally Funded Research and Development Centers, or University Affiliated Research Centers operated by educational institutions, when the resulting contract will be a covered contract as defined in DFARS 242.7000(a) and the solicitation or contract includes any of the listed clauses [@CL-CMP-7262; @CL-CMP-7263], while DFARS 242.7202 states DoD policy for its contractors to have an MMAS that conforms to the standards in paragraph (d) of the clause at DFARS 252.242-7004 [@CL-CMP-7264]. The cognizant contracting officer, in consultation with the auditor and functional specialist, if appropriate, determines the acceptability of the contractor's MMAS and approves or disapproves the system [@CL-CMP-7265].

In evaluating the acceptability of the contractor's MMAS, the contracting officer, in consultation with the auditor and functional specialist, if appropriate, determines whether the contractor's MMAS complies with the system criteria for an acceptable MMAS as prescribed in the clause at DFARS 252.242-7004 [@CL-CMP-0170; @CL-CMP-7266]. The clause card for the material management and accounting system is [Material management and accounting system clause](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-material-management-accounting-system.md), and the review steps are on [MMAS review procedures](cmp-mmas-review-procedures.md).
