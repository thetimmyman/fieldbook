## Purpose

Shows how much a DoD contracting officer withholds from payments when a contractor business system has material weaknesses, and the caps on the total.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD class deviation [@CL-CMP-0004] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.242-7005(e)(1) [@CL-CMP-0004] |
| RFO citation | Class Deviation 2026-O0050, DFARS 252.242-7998(e)(1) [@CL-CMP-0004] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text [@CL-CMP-0004] |
| Applies to | DoD contracts with the business-systems clause that are subject to the Cost Accounting Standards, from 2026-03-16 [@CL-CMP-0004] |

When the contracting officer's final determination on material weaknesses includes a notice to withhold, the contracting officer withholds 5 percent of amounts due from progress and performance-based payments [@CL-CMP-0004]. When the weaknesses are in more than one business system, the total withhold from each payment may not exceed 10 percent [@CL-CMP-0006].

## How it works

The contracting officer first issues an initial determination, the contractor responds, and a final determination follows. Only a final determination that finds material weaknesses and carries a notice to withhold starts the withhold. The same paragraph directs the contractor to withhold from its own interim cost vouchers. The withhold drops to a lower rate once the contractor submits an acceptable corrective action plan and is carrying it out, and ends when the contracting officer decides the weaknesses are corrected. Under the codified clause the total withheld from each progress payment, performance-based payment or interim cost voucher may not exceed 5 percent for material weaknesses in any single contractor business system [@CL-CMP-0906] and 10 percent for material weaknesses in multiple contractor business systems [@CL-CMP-0907]. The codified clause has the contracting officer reduce the withholding directly related to the weaknesses covered under the corrective action plan to 2 percent from progress payments and performance-based payments, and direct the contractor in writing to reduce the percentage withheld on interim cost vouchers to 2 percent [@CL-CMP-0905]. That reduction depends on two conditions: the contractor submits an acceptable corrective action plan within 45 days of receipt of the notice of the contracting officer's intent to withhold payments [@CL-CMP-1205], and the contracting officer, in consultation with the auditor or functional specialist, determines that the contractor is effectively implementing the plan [@CL-CMP-1206].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A progress payment request of 2,000,000 arrives after a final determination with a notice to withhold for one system. At 5 percent, 100,000 is withheld and 1,900,000 is paid. If a second system also had material weaknesses, the total withhold could rise to no more than 10 percent, 200,000.

## Never confuse

A material weakness is a deficiency serious enough to put the reliability of the system's information at risk; the withhold follows only the contracting officer's final determination, not an audit finding by itself. The withhold is not a reduction in the progress payment rate; see [Progress payments](../../contract-finance/financing/ctf-progress-payments.md).
