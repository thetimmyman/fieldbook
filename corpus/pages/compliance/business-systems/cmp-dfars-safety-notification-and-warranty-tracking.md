## Purpose

Shows when the contractor must notify the Government of a potential safety issue, and what warranty data it must furnish for serialized warranted items.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.246-7003 and 252.246-7006 [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |
| Applies to | Contracts with either clause [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |

The Contractor shall provide notification, in accordance with paragraph (c), of, among listed categories, all nonconformances for parts identified as critical safety items acquired by the Government under this contract [@CL-CMP-3877], and of all nonconformances or deficiencies that may result in a safety impact for systems, or subsystems, assemblies, subassemblies, or parts integral to a system, acquired by or serviced for the Government under this contract [@CL-CMP-3906]. It shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Procuring Contracting Officer as soon as practicable, but not later than 72 hours, after discovering or acquiring credible information concerning nonconformances and deficiencies described in paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-3878]. It shall also provide a written notification to both within 5 working days that includes, among listed items, a summary of the defect or nonconformance and a chronology of pertinent events [@CL-CMP-3879].

For warranty tracking, the Contractor shall provide the information required by the Warranty Tracking Information attachment on each contract line item number, subline item number, or exhibit line item number for warranted items no later than the time of award [@CL-CMP-3881]. That information shall include such information as duration, fixed expiration, item type, starting event, usage, warranty administrator enterprise identifier, and warranty guarantor enterprise identifier [@CL-CMP-3882].

## How it works

The notice duty is triggered by credible information, and the first deadline runs from discovery or acquisition of that information [@CL-CMP-3878]. The warranty data comes in two steps: the attachment information no later than the time of award [@CL-CMP-3881], and later, no later than when the warranted items are presented for receipt and/or acceptance, the unique item identifier for each warranted item [@CL-CMP-3883] and the warranty repair source information and instructions for each warranted item required by the attachment entitled Source of Repair Instructions [@CL-CMP-3905]. The unique item identifier is the subject of [Item unique identification and valuation clause](cmp-dfars-item-unique-identification.md). The Contractor is responsible for the notification of potential safety issues occurring with regard to an item furnished by any subcontractor, and shall facilitate direct communication between the Government and the subcontractor as necessary [@CL-CMP-3908]. The safety notification clause is also passed down: the Contractor shall include its substance in subcontracts for, among listed categories, parts identified as critical safety items and systems and subsystems, assemblies, and subassemblies integral to a system [@CL-CMP-3880].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor receives credible information on a Tuesday morning that a bracket delivered under the contract does not conform to its requirements. The bracket is a part identified as a critical safety item, so it notifies both contracting officers within the notice window and follows with a written notice that gives a summary and a chronology. Separately, it lists 200 serialized warranted items on the warranty attachment no later than the time of award.

## Never confuse

A safety notification reports a nonconformance or deficiency, while warranty tracking furnishes data about a warranted item; they are separate clauses with separate triggers [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881]. Who bears the cost of a warranty remedy is a cost question on [Service and warranty costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-service-and-warranty-costs.md), and warranty clauses in general are on [Warranty contract clauses](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-warranty-contract-clauses.md); this card does not restate them.
