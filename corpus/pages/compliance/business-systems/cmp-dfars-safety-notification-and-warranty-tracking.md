## Purpose

Shows when the contractor must notify the Government of a potential safety issue, and what warranty data it must furnish for serialized warranted items.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.246-7003 and 252.246-7006 [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |
| Applies to | Contracts with either clause [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881] |

The Contractor shall provide notification, in accordance with paragraph (c) of the clause, of all nonconformances for parts identified as critical safety items acquired by the Government under the contract [@CL-CMP-3877], and all nonconformances or deficiencies that may result in a safety impact for systems, or subsystems, assemblies, subassemblies, or parts integral to a system, acquired by or serviced for the Government under the contract [@CL-CMP-3906]. It shall notify the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Procuring Contracting Officer as soon as practicable, but not later than 72 hours, after discovering or acquiring credible information concerning nonconformances and deficiencies described in paragraph (b) of the clause [@CL-CMP-3878]. It shall also provide a written notification to the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Procuring Contracting Officer within 5 working days that includes a summary of the defect or nonconformance, a chronology of pertinent events, the identification of potentially affected items to the extent known at the time of notification, a point of contact to coordinate problem analysis and resolution, and any other relevant information [@CL-CMP-3879; @CL-CMP-0179].

For warranty tracking, the Contractor shall provide the information required by the attachment entitled Warranty Tracking Information on each contract line item number, subline item number, or exhibit line item number for warranted items no later than the time of award [@CL-CMP-3881]. Information required in the warranty attachment shall include such information as duration, fixed expiration, item type, starting event, usage, warranty administrator enterprise identifier, and warranty guarantor enterprise identifier [@CL-CMP-3882].

## How it works

The 72-hour notification deadline runs from discovering or acquiring credible information concerning nonconformances and deficiencies described in paragraph (b) of the clause [@CL-CMP-3878]. Under the warranty tracking clause, the Contractor provides the information required by the attachment entitled Warranty Tracking Information on each contract line item number, subline item number, or exhibit line item number for warranted items no later than the time of award [@CL-CMP-3881], and provides, no later than when the warranted items are presented for receipt and/or acceptance, the unique item identifier for each warranted item required by the attachment entitled Warranty Tracking Information [@CL-CMP-3883] and the warranty repair source information and instructions for each warranted item required by the attachment entitled Source of Repair Instructions [@CL-CMP-3905]. The unique item identifier is the subject of [Item unique identification and valuation clause](cmp-dfars-item-unique-identification.md). The Contractor is responsible for the notification of potential safety issues occurring with regard to an item furnished by any subcontractor, and shall facilitate direct communication between the Government and the subcontractor as necessary [@CL-CMP-3908]. The Contractor shall include the substance of the safety notification clause, including paragraph (f), in subcontracts for parts identified as critical safety items, systems and subsystems, assemblies, and subassemblies integral to a system, or repair, maintenance, logistics support, or overhaul services for systems and subsystems, assemblies, subassemblies, and parts integral to a system [@CL-CMP-3880; @CL-CMP-0180].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor receives credible information on a Tuesday morning that a bracket delivered under the contract does not conform to its requirements. The bracket is a part identified as a critical safety item, so it notifies both contracting officers within the notice window and follows with a written notice that gives a summary and a chronology. Separately, it lists 200 serialized warranted items on the warranty attachment no later than the time of award.

## Never confuse

Under DFARS 252.246-7003, the Contractor notifies the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Procuring Contracting Officer after discovering or acquiring credible information concerning nonconformances and deficiencies described in paragraph (b), while under DFARS 252.246-7006, the Contractor provides the information required by the attachment entitled Warranty Tracking Information on each contract line item number, subline item number, or exhibit line item number for warranted items no later than the time of award [@CL-CMP-3878; @CL-CMP-3881]. See [Service and warranty costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-service-and-warranty-costs.md) and [Warranty contract clauses](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-warranty-contract-clauses.md).
