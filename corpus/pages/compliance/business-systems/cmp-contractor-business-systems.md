## Purpose

Defines the contractor business systems that the umbrella clause covers, sets out what a material weakness is, and gives the response and plan clocks. The withhold percentages and their limits are on a separate page.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-0901] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.242-7005 [@CL-CMP-0901] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0901] |
| Applies to | Covered contracts subject to the Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-0901] |

The clause applies only to covered contracts that are subject to the Cost Accounting Standards under federal statute as implemented in 48 CFR 9903.201-1 [@CL-CMP-0901]. A material weakness is a deficiency or combination of deficiencies in internal control over information in contractor business systems, such that there is a reasonable possibility that a material misstatement of that information will not be prevented, or detected and corrected, on a timely basis [@CL-CMP-0902]. A reasonable possibility exists when the likelihood of an event occurring is probable, or more than remote but less than likely [@CL-CMP-1203].

The contractor must respond in writing within 30 days to an initial determination that there are one or more material weaknesses in one or more of its business systems [@CL-CMP-0903]. If the contracting officer issues the final determination with a notice to withhold payments for material weaknesses, the contractor must, within 45 days of receipt of the notice, either correct the weaknesses or submit an acceptable corrective action plan showing milestones and actions to eliminate them [@CL-CMP-0904].

## How it works

The definition of contractor business systems lists the accounting system and the earned value management system, each if the contract includes the clause for that system [@CL-CMP-1200]. It also lists the estimating system and the material management and accounting system on the same condition [@CL-CMP-1201]. It ends with the property management system and the purchasing system on the same condition, which makes six systems in all [@CL-CMP-1202]. Acceptable contractor business systems are those that comply with the terms and conditions of the applicable system clauses listed in that definition [@CL-CMP-1204].

The withhold percentages, the limits on the total withheld, the reduction under a corrective action plan and the release of withheld amounts are covered in [Business system withholds](cmp-business-system-withholds.md). Payment withholding does not apply to payments on fixed-price line items where performance is complete and the items were accepted by the Government [@CL-CMP-0908]. The payment withholding authorized in the clause is not subject to the interest-penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CMP-0909].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor subject to full coverage under the Cost Accounting Standards has a contract that includes the estimating system clause and the purchasing system clause but no other business system clause. The contracting officer issues an initial determination that the estimating system has a material weakness. The contractor responds in writing within 30 days. The contracting officer then issues a final determination that confirms the weakness and carries a notice to withhold payments. Within 45 days of receiving that notice, the contractor submits an acceptable corrective action plan with milestones. A fixed-price line item that was completed and accepted before the notice is not subject to the withholding, and no interest penalty under the Prompt Payment Act applies to the withholding that is authorized.

## Never confuse

This umbrella clause covers a system if the contract includes that system's own clause, and it sets the common material weakness test, response clock and plan clock [@CL-CMP-0902; @CL-CMP-0903; @CL-CMP-0904]. Each system in the definition carries that condition [@CL-CMP-1200; @CL-CMP-1201; @CL-CMP-1202]. The technical standards for each system are not in this clause; they sit in the separate system clauses, which the definition of acceptable systems points to [@CL-CMP-1204]. See [Accounting system criteria](cmp-accounting-system-criteria.md) for one of them.
