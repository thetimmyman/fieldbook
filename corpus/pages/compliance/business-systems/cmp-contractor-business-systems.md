## Purpose

Defines the contractor business systems that the umbrella clause covers, sets out what a material weakness is, and gives the response and plan clocks. The withhold percentages and their limits are on a separate page.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-0901] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.242-7005 [@CL-CMP-0901] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0901] |
| Applies to | Covered contracts subject to the Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-0901] |

The clause only applies to covered contracts that are subject to the Cost Accounting Standards under chapter 15 of title 41 of the United States Code, as implemented in regulations found at 48 CFR 9903.201-1 [@CL-CMP-0901]. A material weakness is a deficiency or combination of deficiencies in the internal control over information in contractor business systems, such that there is a reasonable possibility that a material misstatement of such information will not be prevented, or detected and corrected, on a timely basis [@CL-CMP-0902]. A reasonable possibility exists when the likelihood of an event occurring is probable, or more than remote but less than likely [@CL-CMP-1203].

The contractor shall respond, in writing, within 30 days to an initial determination that there are one or more material weaknesses in one or more of the contractor's business systems [@CL-CMP-0903]. If the contracting officer determines that the contractor's business system contains material weaknesses, the final determination will include a notice to withhold payments [@CL-CMP-0172], and the contractor shall, within 45 days of receipt of the notice, either correct the weaknesses or submit an acceptable corrective action plan showing milestones and actions to eliminate the weaknesses [@CL-CMP-0904].

## How it works

The definition of contractor business systems lists the accounting system, if the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.242-7006, Accounting System Administration, and the earned value management system, if the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.234-7002, Earned Value Management System [@CL-CMP-1200]. It also lists the estimating system, if the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.215-7002, Cost Estimating System Requirements, and the material management and accounting system, if the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.242-7004, Material Management and Accounting System [@CL-CMP-1201]. It ends with the property management system, if the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.245-7003, Contractor Property Management System Administration, and the purchasing system, if the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.244-7001, Contractor Purchasing System Administration [@CL-CMP-1202]. Acceptable contractor business systems are contractor business systems that comply with the terms and conditions of the applicable business system clauses listed in the definition of contractor business systems in the clause [@CL-CMP-1204].

The withhold percentages, the limits on the total withheld, the reduction under a corrective action plan and the release of withheld amounts are covered in [Business system withholds](cmp-business-system-withholds.md). Payment withholding shall not apply to payments on fixed-price line items where performance is complete and the items were accepted by the Government [@CL-CMP-0908]. The payment withholding authorized in the clause is not subject to the interest-penalty provisions of the Prompt Payment Act [@CL-CMP-0909].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor subject to full coverage under the Cost Accounting Standards has a contract that includes the estimating system clause and the purchasing system clause but no other business system clause. The contracting officer issues an initial determination that the estimating system has a material weakness. The contractor responds in writing within 30 days. The contracting officer then issues a final determination that confirms the weakness and carries a notice to withhold payments. Within 45 days of receiving that notice, the contractor submits an acceptable corrective action plan with milestones. A fixed-price line item that was completed and accepted before the notice is not subject to the withholding, and no interest penalty under the Prompt Payment Act applies to the withholding that is authorized.

## Never confuse

Each business system in the definition of contractor business systems is included if the contract includes the specified clause for that system [@CL-CMP-1200; @CL-CMP-1201; @CL-CMP-1202], and the clause sets the material weakness definition, the 30-day response requirement and the 45-day corrective action or corrective action plan requirement [@CL-CMP-0902; @CL-CMP-0903; @CL-CMP-0904]. Acceptable contractor business systems are contractor business systems that comply with the terms and conditions of the applicable business system clauses listed in the definition of contractor business systems in the clause [@CL-CMP-1204]. See [Accounting system criteria](cmp-accounting-system-criteria.md) for one of them.

## Company calibration

Determining the current approval status of each contractor business system, monitoring open material weaknesses, and managing corrective action plan milestones. [@Q-034]
