## Purpose

Shows who must keep a counterfeit electronic part detection and avoidance system, what the system must cover, and what an unacceptable system can lead to.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3851] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.246-7007 [@CL-CMP-3851] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3851] |
| Applies to | Contractors subject to the Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-3850] |

Paragraphs (a) through (e) of the clause do not apply unless the Contractor is subject to the Cost Accounting Standards as implemented in regulations found at 48 CFR 9903.201-1 [@CL-CMP-3850]. Paragraph (b) is one of those paragraphs, so the condition applies to it: the Contractor shall establish and maintain an acceptable counterfeit electronic part detection and avoidance system [@CL-CMP-3850; @CL-CMP-3851].

Under the same condition, failure to maintain an acceptable system, as defined in the clause, may result in disapproval of the purchasing system by the Contracting Officer and/or withholding of payments [@CL-CMP-3850; @CL-CMP-3852]. Failure may also affect the allowability of costs of counterfeit electronic parts or suspect counterfeit electronic parts and the cost of rework or corrective action that may be required to remedy the use or inclusion of such parts [@CL-CMP-3901].

## How it works

Under the same condition, the system shall include risk-based policies and procedures that address, at a minimum, the listed areas, one of which is the training of personnel [@CL-CMP-3850; @CL-CMP-3853]. Another listed area is the inspection and testing of electronic parts, including criteria for acceptance and rejection [@CL-CMP-3854]. Another is tracking of electronic parts from the original manufacturer to product acceptance by the Government, in accordance with paragraph (c) of the clause at 252.246-7008, Sources of Electronic Parts [@CL-CMP-3903]; the duties in that clause are on [Sources of electronic parts clause](cmp-dfars-sources-of-electronic-parts.md). A further listed area is flowdown of the detection and avoidance requirements to subcontractors at all levels in the supply chain that are responsible for buying or selling electronic parts or assemblies containing electronic parts, among others [@CL-CMP-3855]. The clause lists further areas that this card does not restate [@CL-CMP-3853].

The Contractor shall include the substance of the clause, excluding the introductory text and including only paragraphs (a) through (e), in subcontracts, including subcontracts for commercial products, for electronic parts or assemblies containing electronic parts [@CL-CMP-3856]. The condition about the Cost Accounting Standards is in the introductory text, which the flowdown excludes [@CL-CMP-3850; @CL-CMP-3856].

Review and evaluation of the Contractor's policies and procedures under the clause will be accomplished as part of the evaluation of the Contractor's purchasing system in accordance with 252.244-7001, basic or Alternate I [@CL-CMP-3902]. The definition of contractor business systems lists the purchasing system, if the contract includes the clause at 252.244-7001 [@CL-CMP-1202]; see [Contractor business systems](cmp-contractor-business-systems.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor subject to the Cost Accounting Standards buys parts through 30 subcontractors. Its written procedures reach only 10 of them. The flowdown area is not met for the other 20, so the Contracting Officer may find the system unacceptable and may disapprove the purchasing system or withhold payments.

## Never confuse

An unacceptable system can lead to purchasing system disapproval or withheld payments [@CL-CMP-3852]; what follows a disapproval is on [DoD business system deficiencies: payment withholding and release](cmp-business-system-deficiency-determinations.md). The clause also says failure may affect the allowability of the costs of counterfeit parts and of rework [@CL-CMP-3901], but whether a given counterfeit part cost is unallowable turns on the cost rules the clause points to, covered on [Costs of counterfeit and suspect counterfeit electronic parts](../cas-allowability/cmp-counterfeit-electronic-part-costs.md); this card does not restate them. The purchasing system itself is covered on [Contractor Purchasing System Administration clause](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-contractor-purchasing-system.md), and payment withholds on [Business system withholds](cmp-business-system-withholds.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company is subject to the Cost Accounting Standards, and the current Government status of its purchasing system. [@Q-034]
