## Purpose

Shows the criteria an acceptable accounting system must meet, and how the Government handles material weaknesses and disapproval.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-0403] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.242-7006(c) [@CL-CMP-0403] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0403] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the accounting system administration clause [@CL-CMP-0403] |

The contractor shall establish and maintain an acceptable accounting system [@CL-CMP-1604], and failure to maintain one, as defined in the clause, shall result in the withholding of payments if the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, and also may result in disapproval of the system [@CL-CMP-0403]. An acceptable accounting system is one that complies with the system criteria in paragraph (c) of the clause to provide reasonable assurance that applicable laws and regulations are complied with [@CL-CMP-0977], the accounting system and cost data are reliable, risk of misallocations and mischarges is minimized, and contract allocations and charges are consistent with billing procedures [@CL-CMP-0978; @CL-CMP-0401]. The accounting system is the contractor's system or systems for accounting methods, procedures, and controls established to gather, record, classify, analyze, summarize, interpret, and present accurate and timely financial data for reporting in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and management decisions [@CL-CMP-0402; @CL-CMP-0979], and it may include subsystems for specific areas such as indirect and other direct costs, compensation, billing, labor, and general information technology [@CL-CMP-1603].

## How it works

The contractor's accounting system shall provide for a sound internal control environment, an accounting framework and an organizational structure [@CL-CMP-0159; @CL-CMP-0404]. It shall provide for proper segregation of direct costs from indirect costs, identification and accumulation of direct costs by contract [@CL-CMP-0983], and a logical and consistent method for the accumulation and allocation of indirect costs to intermediate and final cost objectives [@CL-CMP-0405]. Subsidiary cost ledgers and cost objectives reconcile to the general ledger [@CL-CMP-0406; @CL-CMP-0984], and billings reconcile to the cost accounts for both current and cumulative amounts claimed and comply with contract terms [@CL-CMP-0410; @CL-CMP-0987]. A timekeeping system identifies employees' labor by intermediate or final cost objectives [@CL-CMP-0407], costs charged to a contract are determined on an interim (at least monthly) basis through routine posting of books of account [@CL-CMP-0408; @CL-CMP-0985], and amounts that are not allowable in terms of FAR part 31, Contract Cost Principles and Procedures, and other contract provisions are excluded from costs charged to Government contracts [@CL-CMP-0409; @CL-CMP-0986]. Accounting practices are in accordance with standards promulgated by the Cost Accounting Standards Board, if applicable, and otherwise Generally Accepted Accounting Principles [@CL-CMP-0411; @CL-CMP-0988]. The contractor shall respond within 30 days to a written initial determination from the contracting officer that identifies material weaknesses in the contractor's accounting system [@CL-CMP-0412]. If the contractor receives the contracting officer's final determination of material weaknesses, the contractor shall, within 45 days of receipt of the final determination, either correct the material weaknesses or submit an acceptable corrective action plan showing milestones and actions to eliminate the material weaknesses [@CL-CMP-0160; @CL-CMP-0413; @CL-CMP-0992].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's billings for the quarter total 500,000 while the cost accounts show 460,000, and the unreconciled 40,000 traces to a labor distribution entry posted against the wrong cost objective. The contractor corrects the entry, reconciles the billings to the cost accounts, and documents the adjusting entry.

## Never confuse

The accounting system criteria require exclusion from costs charged to Government contracts of amounts that are not allowable in terms of FAR part 31, Contract Cost Principles and Procedures, and other contract provisions [@CL-CMP-0409; @CL-CMP-0986]. If the contracting officer makes a final determination to disapprove the contractor's accounting system, and the contract includes the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, the contracting officer will withhold payments in accordance with that clause [@CL-CMP-0993; @CL-CMP-0414].
