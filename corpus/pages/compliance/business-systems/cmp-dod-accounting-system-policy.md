## Purpose

Explains how the Department of Defense decides whether a contractor's accounting system is acceptable, and what follows when the review finds material weaknesses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-0701] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 242.7502 [@CL-CMP-0701] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-0701] |
| Applies to | Cost-reimbursement, incentive type, time-and-materials and labor-hour contracts, and contracts with progress payments based on costs or stage of completion [@CL-CMP-0701] |

Contractors receiving cost-reimbursement, incentive type, time-and-materials, or labor-hour contracts, or contracts which provide for progress payments based on costs or on a percentage or stage of completion, shall maintain an accounting system [@CL-CMP-0701].

In evaluating the acceptability of a contractor's accounting system, the contracting officer, in consultation with the auditor or functional specialist, shall determine whether the contractor's accounting system complies with the system criteria for an acceptable accounting system as prescribed in the clause at DFARS 252.242-7006, Accounting System Administration [@CL-CMP-0702; @CL-CMP-0183].

## How it works

The auditor shall document findings and recommendations in a report to the contracting officer, and if the auditor identifies any material weakness, the report shall describe the weaknesses or deficiencies in sufficient detail to allow the contracting officer to understand the weaknesses or deficiencies [@CL-CMP-1170].

If there are no material weaknesses, the contracting officer shall promptly notify the contractor, in writing, that the contractor's accounting system is acceptable and approved [@CL-CMP-1172]. If the contracting officer finds that there are one or more material weaknesses due to the contractor's failure to meet one or more of the accounting system criteria in the clause at DFARS 252.242-7006, the contracting officer shall promptly make an initial written determination on any material weaknesses and notify the contractor, in writing, providing a description of each material weakness in sufficient detail to allow the contractor to understand the weakness [@CL-CMP-1171; @CL-CMP-0184], and request the contractor to respond, in writing, to the initial determination within 30 days [@CL-CMP-0703].

The contracting officer shall make a final determination and notify the contractor, in writing, that the contractor's accounting system is acceptable and approved, and no material weaknesses remain, or that material weaknesses remain [@CL-CMP-1173]. If material weaknesses remain, the notice shall identify any remaining material weaknesses and indicate the adequacy of any proposed or completed corrective action, and the contracting officer shall request that the contractor, within 45 days of receipt of the final determination, either correct the weaknesses or submit an acceptable corrective action plan showing milestones and actions to eliminate the weaknesses [@CL-CMP-0185; @CL-CMP-0704]. The contracting officer shall also make a determination to disapprove the system in accordance with the clause at DFARS 252.242-7006 [@CL-CMP-1174] and withhold payments in accordance with the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, if the clause is included in the contract [@CL-CMP-0705].

The contracting officer shall promptly approve a previously disapproved accounting system and notify the contractor when the contracting officer determines that there are no remaining material weaknesses [@CL-CMP-0706].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An audit reports 3 material weaknesses and the contracting officer issues an initial determination. The contractor answers within the 30 day window. Two weaknesses are closed, so the final determination still lists 1. The contractor files an acceptable corrective action plan within the 45 day window, and once the last weakness is corrected and the contracting officer determines that no material weaknesses remain, the contracting officer approves the system.

## Never confuse

In evaluating the acceptability of a contractor's accounting system, the contracting officer determines whether the contractor's accounting system complies with the system criteria for an acceptable accounting system as prescribed in the clause at DFARS 252.242-7006, Accounting System Administration, and makes a final determination to approve or disapprove the system [@CL-CMP-0702; @CL-CMP-0183; @CL-CMP-1173; @CL-CMP-1174]. Where material weaknesses remain after the final determination, the contracting officer withholds payments in accordance with the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, if the clause is included in the contract [@CL-CMP-0705].

Whether each contract carries the Accounting System Administration clause, and whether the system is approved, disapproved, not reviewed or not required. [@Q-034]
