## Purpose

Shows who decides on payment withholding after a business system is disapproved, how the withheld amount is capped, and how it is reduced or released.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-2217] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 242.7000 [@CL-CMP-2217] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2217] |
| Applies to | Covered contracts that are subject to the Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-CMP-2216] |

The business systems that the business systems clause covers are listed on [Contractor business systems](cmp-contractor-business-systems.md). A covered contract is a contract that is subject to the Cost Accounting Standards under chapter 15 of title 41 of the United States Code, as implemented in regulations found at 48 CFR 9903.201-1 [@CL-CMP-2216].

If the contracting officer makes a final determination to disapprove a contractor's business system in accordance with the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, the contracting officer's determination-to-withhold duties apply [@CL-CMP-2217]. The contracting officer has the sole discretion to identify the covered contracts from which to withhold payments [@CL-CMP-2220]. In identifying them, the contracting officer shall ensure that the total amount of payment withholding under DFARS 252.242-7005 does not exceed 10 percent of progress payments, performance-based payments, and interim payments under cost-reimbursement, labor-hour, and time-and-materials contracts billed under each of the identified covered contracts [@CL-CMP-2218]. For each business system, the payment withholding does not exceed 5 percent of progress payments, performance-based payments, and interim payments under cost-reimbursement, labor-hour, and time-and-materials contracts billed under each of the identified covered contracts [@CL-CMP-2219].

The contracting officer, in consultation with the auditor or functional specialist, shall monitor the contractor's progress in correcting the weaknesses, and shall notify the contractor of any decision to decrease or increase the amount of payment withholding in accordance with the clause at DFARS 252.242-7005 [@CL-CMP-2249; @CL-CMP-0164]. If the contractor notifies the contracting officer that the contractor has corrected the material weaknesses, the contracting officer shall request the auditor or functional specialist to review the correction to verify that the weaknesses have been corrected [@CL-CMP-2214]. If, after receipt of verification, the contracting officer determines that the contractor has corrected all material weaknesses as directed by the contracting officer's final determination, the contracting officer shall discontinue the withholding of payments, release any payments previously withheld, and approve the system, unless other material weaknesses remain [@CL-CMP-2250; @CL-CMP-2221].

Within 90 days of receipt of the contractor notification that the contractor has corrected the material weaknesses, the contracting officer shall either make a determination under paragraph (d)(3)(i) or reduce withholding under paragraph (d)(3)(ii) [@CL-CMP-2222; @CL-CMP-2223]. Under paragraph (d)(3)(i), the contracting officer makes a determination that the contractor has corrected all material weaknesses as directed by the contracting officer's final determination in accordance with paragraph (d)(1) [@CL-CMP-2222; @CL-CMP-0165], that there is a reasonable expectation that the corrective actions have been implemented in accordance with paragraph (d)(2) [@CL-CMP-0165; @CL-CMP-0166], or that the contractor has not corrected all material weaknesses as directed by the final determination in accordance with paragraph (d)(1) or there is not a reasonable expectation that the corrective actions have been implemented in accordance with paragraph (d)(2) [@CL-CMP-0166; @CL-CMP-0167]. Under paragraph (d)(3)(ii), the contracting officer shall reduce withholding directly related to the material weaknesses covered under the corrective action plan by at least 50 percent of the amount being withheld from progress payments and performance-based payments, and direct the contractor, in writing, to reduce the percentage withheld on interim cost vouchers by at least 50 percent, until the contracting officer makes a determination in accordance with paragraph (d)(3)(i) [@CL-CMP-2223; @CL-CMP-2251]. If, at any time, the contracting officer determines that the contractor has failed to correct the material weaknesses identified in the contractor's notification, the contracting officer will continue, reinstate, or increase withholding from progress payments and performance-based payments, and direct the contractor, in writing, to continue, reinstate, or increase the percentage withheld on interim cost vouchers to the percentage initially withheld, until the contracting officer determines that the contractor has corrected all material weaknesses as directed by the contracting officer's final determination [@CL-CMP-2224; @CL-CMP-0168; @CL-CMP-0169].

The response and plan clocks are on [Contractor business systems](cmp-contractor-business-systems.md), and the clause withhold rates and the deviation text are on [Business-system payment withholds](cmp-business-system-withholds.md). The review procedure for the material management and accounting system is on [Material management and accounting system review procedures](cmp-mmas-review-procedures.md).

## How it works

A final determination to disapprove a contractor's business system in accordance with DFARS 252.242-7005 triggers the contracting officer's duties on withholding payments [@CL-CMP-2217]. The contracting officer has sole discretion to identify the covered contracts from which to withhold payments and ensures the withholding stays within the 10 percent total cap and the 5 percent per-system cap [@CL-CMP-2220; @CL-CMP-2218; @CL-CMP-2219]. The contracting officer monitors the contractor's progress in correcting the weaknesses in consultation with the auditor or functional specialist, notifies the contractor of any decision to decrease or increase the amount of payment withholding in accordance with DFARS 252.242-7005, and requests the auditor or functional specialist to review a reported correction to verify that the weaknesses have been corrected [@CL-CMP-2249; @CL-CMP-0164; @CL-CMP-2214]. Receipt of the contractor notification that the contractor has corrected the material weaknesses starts the 90-day period [@CL-CMP-2222].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has two covered contracts with progress payments. The contracting officer disapproves the estimating system and picks one contract, on which 1,000,000 has been billed. The cap for that one system is 50,000. Later the contractor reports the weaknesses corrected. Within 90 days the officer either makes a determination or cuts the related withholding by at least half until a determination is made. If the auditor verifies the fix and no other weakness remains, the officer releases the withheld 50,000 and approves the system.

## Never confuse

If the contracting officer makes a final determination to disapprove a contractor's business system in accordance with DFARS 252.242-7005, the contracting officer's withholding duties apply [@CL-CMP-2217], while the contracting officer monitors the contractor's progress in correcting the weaknesses and notifies the contractor of any decision to decrease or increase the amount of payment withholding in accordance with DFARS 252.242-7005 [@CL-CMP-2249; @CL-CMP-0164]. Total payment withholding under DFARS 252.242-7005 does not exceed 10 percent, and payment withholding for each business system does not exceed 5 percent, of the specified payments billed under each identified covered contract [@CL-CMP-2218; @CL-CMP-2219]. For how the audit side reports findings, see [DCAA business system audits](cmp-dcaa-business-system-audits.md).
