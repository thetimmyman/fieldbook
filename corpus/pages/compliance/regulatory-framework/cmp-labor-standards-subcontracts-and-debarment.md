## Purpose

Shows the labor standards clauses a prime contractor must pass down, who answers for subcontractor compliance, the paperwork that evidences the flowdown, and the consequence of a breach.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5839] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.406-5, FAR 52.222-11, FAR 52.222-12 and FAR 52.222-13 [@CL-CMP-5839; @CL-CMP-5843; @CL-CMP-5844; @CL-CMP-7628] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-11 [@CL-CMP-1396; @CL-CMP-1407], RFO FAR 52.222-12 [@CL-CMP-1415; @CL-CMP-1416] and RFO FAR 52.222-13 [@CL-CMP-1417] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5839] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the subcontracts clause as prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5839] |

The contractor inserts the listed labor standards clauses in any subcontract for construction, alterations and repairs within the United States [@CL-CMP-5840]. The first group is Construction Wage Rate Requirements, Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards—Overtime Compensation (if the clause is included in the contract), Apprentices and Trainees, Payrolls and Basic Records, a fifth clause whose title begins "Compliance with" and ends "Act Requirements", and Withholding of Funds [@CL-CMP-6180]. The second group is Subcontracts (Labor Standards), Contract Termination—Debarment, Disputes Concerning Labor Standards, Compliance with Construction Wage Rate Requirements and Related Regulations, and Certification of Eligibility [@CL-CMP-6181].

The prime contractor is responsible for compliance by any subcontractor or lower tier subcontractor performing construction within the United States with all the clauses listed in the flowdown paragraph [@CL-CMP-5841]. The contractor also inserts the substance of the subcontracts clause, including paragraph (e), in all subcontracts for construction within the United States [@CL-CMP-6184].

In accordance with the requirements of the clause at 52.222-11, Subcontracts (Labor Standards), the contractor and subcontractors at any tier are required to submit a fully executed SF 1413, Statement and Acknowledgment, upon award of each subcontract [@CL-CMP-7628].

## How it works

In this clause, construction, alteration or repair means all types of work done by laborers and mechanics employed by the construction contractor or construction subcontractor on a particular building or work at the site thereof, including without limitation the types of work the clause lists [@CL-CMP-6179]. Those types include altering, remodeling and installation, if appropriate, on the site of the work of items fabricated off-site, painting and decorating, and manufacturing or furnishing of materials, articles, supplies or equipment on the site of the building or work [@CL-CMP-6449]. They also include transportation of materials and supplies between the site of the work within the meaning of paragraphs (a)(1)(i) and (ii) of the site of the work definition in the Construction Wage Rate Requirements clause [@CL-CMP-6450], and a facility dedicated to the construction of the building or work that is deemed part of the site of the work within the meaning of paragraph (2) of that definition [@CL-CMP-6451]. Finally they include transportation of portions of the building or work between a secondary site where a significant portion of the building or work is constructed, which is part of the site of the work definition in paragraph (a)(1)(ii) of that clause [@CL-CMP-6452], and the physical place or places where the building or work will remain [@CL-CMP-6453].

The flowdown is evidenced in writing. Within 14 days after award of the contract, the contractor delivers to the Contracting Officer a completed Standard Form 1413 for each construction subcontract within the United States [@CL-CMP-5842]. Each form includes the subcontractor's signed and dated acknowledgment that the listed clauses have been included in the subcontract [@CL-CMP-6182]. For a subcontract awarded later, an updated completed form goes to the Contracting Officer within 14 days after that subcontract is awarded [@CL-CMP-6183].

A breach of the Subcontracts (Labor Standards), Compliance with Construction Wage Rate Requirements and Related Regulations, or Certification of Eligibility clause may be grounds for termination of the contract and for debarment as a contractor and subcontractor under 29 CFR 5.12 [@CL-CMP-5843]. The termination clause names the same consequence for a breach of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements, Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards—Overtime Compensation, Apprentices and Trainees, Payrolls and Basic Records clauses, and of the fifth clause of the first group, whose title begins "Compliance with" and ends "Act Requirements" [@CL-CMP-5843; @CL-CMP-6185]. The Compliance with Construction Wage Rate Requirements and Related Regulations clause incorporates by reference all rulings and interpretations of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements and related statutes contained in 29 CFR parts 1, 3 and 5 [@CL-CMP-5844].

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.222-11 keeps the definition of construction, alteration or repair [@CL-CMP-1396] [@CL-CMP-6179], the types of work it lists [@CL-CMP-1397] [@CL-CMP-1398] [@CL-CMP-1399] [@CL-CMP-1405] [@CL-CMP-1406] [@CL-CMP-6449] [@CL-CMP-6450] [@CL-CMP-6451] [@CL-CMP-6452] [@CL-CMP-6453] and the two groups of clauses to insert in subcontracts [@CL-CMP-1407] [@CL-CMP-1408] [@CL-CMP-9261] [@CL-CMP-1409] [@CL-CMP-5840] [@CL-CMP-6180] [@CL-CMP-6181]. It also keeps the prime contractor's responsibility for subcontractor compliance [@CL-CMP-1410] [@CL-CMP-5841], the 14-day delivery of Standard Form 1413 with the subcontractor's acknowledgment [@CL-CMP-1411] [@CL-CMP-1412] [@CL-CMP-5842] [@CL-CMP-6182], the 14-day updated form for a later subcontract [@CL-CMP-1413] [@CL-CMP-6183] and the insertion of the substance of the clause in subcontracts [@CL-CMP-1414] [@CL-CMP-6184]. The RFO text prints "must" where the codified text prints "shall" in these paragraphs [@CL-CMP-1407] [@CL-CMP-1410] [@CL-CMP-1411] [@CL-CMP-1413] [@CL-CMP-1414] and the codified text [@CL-CMP-5840] [@CL-CMP-5841] [@CL-CMP-5842] [@CL-CMP-6183] [@CL-CMP-6184]. The RFO text of FAR 52.222-12 keeps the grounds for termination and debarment under 29 CFR 5.12 [@CL-CMP-1415] [@CL-CMP-9262] [@CL-CMP-1416] [@CL-CMP-5843] [@CL-CMP-6185], and the RFO text of FAR 52.222-13 keeps the incorporation by reference of the rulings and interpretations in 29 CFR parts 1, 3 and 5 [@CL-CMP-1417] [@CL-CMP-5844]. The RFO text prescribes the Subcontracts (Labor Standards) clause at FAR 22.402-4(a)(6) where the codified text prescribes it at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-9265] [@CL-CMP-5839].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor is awarded a construction contract on day 0 and has 4 construction subcontracts in the United States at award. Each subcontract includes the listed clauses, and each subcontractor signs and dates an acknowledgment. The prime contractor delivers the 4 completed Standard Form 1413 documents to the Contracting Officer by day 14. A fifth subcontract is awarded on day 40, so its updated form is due by day 54. If a subcontractor at any tier fails to comply with a listed clause, the prime contractor remains responsible for that compliance.

## Never confuse

Flowdown is not delegation. The subcontractor owes the labor standards, but the prime contractor stays responsible for that subcontractor's compliance [@CL-CMP-5841]. The termination clause is also not a separate obligation, because it attaches consequences to the clauses that are already in the contract [@CL-CMP-5843; @CL-CMP-6185]. Not every flowdown clause appears on the termination list: Withholding of Funds, Disputes Concerning Labor Standards and Contract Termination—Debarment itself are in the flowdown list [@CL-CMP-6180; @CL-CMP-6181] but are not named in the termination clause [@CL-CMP-6185; @CL-CMP-5843].

The insertion rules for these clauses are covered in [Construction labor standards disputes and the contract clauses and provision to insert](cmp-construction-labor-disputes-and-contract-clauses.md), and the companion clauses on disputes, certification of eligibility and approval of wage rates are covered in [Labor standards disputes, certification of eligibility and approval of wage rates](cmp-labor-standards-disputes-and-eligibility.md).
