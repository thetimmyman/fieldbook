## Purpose

Shows the labor standards clauses a prime contractor must pass down, who answers for subcontractor compliance, the paperwork that evidences the flowdown, and the consequence of a breach.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5839] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.406-5, FAR 52.222-11, FAR 52.222-12 and FAR 52.222-13 [@CL-CMP-5839; @CL-CMP-5843; @CL-CMP-5844; @CL-CMP-7628] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-11 [@CL-CMP-1396; @CL-CMP-1407], RFO FAR 52.222-12 [@CL-CMP-1415; @CL-CMP-1416] and RFO FAR 52.222-13 [@CL-CMP-1417] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.222-11: MAY 2014 [@CL-CMP-10223]; FAR 52.222-12: May 2014 [@CL-CMP-1415]; FAR 52.222-13: May 2014 [@CL-CMP-1417] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5839] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the subcontracts clause as prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5839] |

The contractor shall insert in any subcontracts for construction, alterations and repairs within the United States the clauses entitled Construction Wage Rate Requirements, Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards—Overtime Compensation (if the clause is included in the contract), Apprentices and Trainees, Payrolls and Basic Records, Compliance with Copeland Act Requirements, and Withholding of Funds [@CL-CMP-5840] [@CL-CMP-6180], and Subcontracts (Labor Standards), Contract Termination—Debarment, Disputes Concerning Labor Standards, Compliance with Construction Wage Rate Requirements and Related Regulations, and Certification of Eligibility [@CL-CMP-6181].

The prime contractor is responsible for compliance by any subcontractor or lower tier subcontractor performing construction within the United States with all the clauses listed in the flowdown paragraph [@CL-CMP-5841]. The contractor shall also insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e), in all subcontracts for construction within the United States [@CL-CMP-6184].

In accordance with the requirements of the clause at 52.222-11, Subcontracts (Labor Standards), the contractor and subcontractors at any tier are required to submit a fully executed SF 1413, Statement and Acknowledgment, upon award of each subcontract [@CL-CMP-7628].

## How it works

In this clause, construction, alteration or repair means all types of work done by laborers and mechanics employed by the construction contractor or construction subcontractor on a particular building or work at the site thereof, including without limitation the types of work the clause lists [@CL-CMP-6179]. Those types include altering, remodeling and installation, if appropriate, on the site of the work of items fabricated off-site, painting and decorating, and manufacturing or furnishing of materials, articles, supplies or equipment on the site of the building or work [@CL-CMP-6449]. They also include transportation of materials and supplies between the site of the work within the meaning of paragraphs (a)(1)(i) and (ii) of the site of the work definition in the Construction Wage Rate Requirements clause [@CL-CMP-6450], and a facility dedicated to the construction of the building or work that is deemed part of the site of the work within the meaning of paragraph (2) of that definition [@CL-CMP-6451]. Finally they include transportation of portions of the building or work between a secondary site where a significant portion of the building or work is constructed, which is part of the site of the work definition in paragraph (a)(1)(ii) of that clause [@CL-CMP-6452], and the physical place or places where the building or work will remain [@CL-CMP-6453].

Within 14 days after award of the contract, the contractor shall deliver to the Contracting Officer a completed Standard Form 1413 for each subcontract for construction within the United States [@CL-CMP-5842], including the subcontractor's signed and dated acknowledgment that the clauses set forth in paragraph (b) of the clause have been included in the subcontract [@CL-CMP-6182]. Within 14 days after the award of any subsequently awarded subcontract the contractor shall deliver to the Contracting Officer an updated completed SF 1413 for such additional subcontract [@CL-CMP-6183].

A breach of the contract clauses entitled Construction Wage Rate Requirements, Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards—Overtime Compensation, Apprentices and Trainees, Payrolls and Basic Records, Compliance with Copeland Act Requirements, Subcontracts (Labor Standards), Compliance with Construction Wage Rate Requirements and Related Regulations, or Certification of Eligibility may be grounds for termination of the contract, and for debarment as a Contractor and subcontractor as provided in 29 CFR 5.12 [@CL-CMP-6185] [@CL-CMP-5843]. The clause at 52.222-13 incorporates by reference all rulings and interpretations of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements and related statutes contained in 29 CFR parts 1, 3 and 5 [@CL-CMP-5844].

Under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR part 52 in place of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.222-11 provides that "construction, alteration or repair," as used in the clause, means all types of work done by laborers and mechanics employed by the construction Contractor or construction subcontractor on a particular building or work at the site thereof, including without limitation the types of work listed [@CL-CMP-1396] [@CL-CMP-1397] [@CL-CMP-1398] [@CL-CMP-1399] [@CL-CMP-1405] [@CL-CMP-1406]. It provides that the Contractor must insert in any subcontracts for construction, alterations and repairs within the United States the clauses entitled the eleven listed [@CL-CMP-1407] [@CL-CMP-1408] [@CL-CMP-9261] [@CL-CMP-1409], that the prime Contractor must be responsible for compliance by any subcontractor or lower tier subcontractor performing construction within the United States with all the contract clauses cited in paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-1410], and that the Contractor must insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e), in all subcontracts for construction within the United States [@CL-CMP-1414]. Within 14 days after award of the contract, the Contractor must deliver to the Contracting Officer a completed Standard Form 1413 for each subcontract for construction within the United States, including the subcontractor's signed and dated acknowledgment that the clauses set forth in paragraph (b) have been included in the subcontract [@CL-CMP-1411] [@CL-CMP-1412], and within 14 days after the award of any subsequently awarded subcontract the Contractor must deliver an updated completed SF 1413 for such additional subcontract [@CL-CMP-1413]. The RFO text prints "must" [@CL-CMP-1407] [@CL-CMP-1410] [@CL-CMP-1411] [@CL-CMP-1413] [@CL-CMP-1414] where the codified text prints "shall" [@CL-CMP-5840] [@CL-CMP-5841] [@CL-CMP-5842] [@CL-CMP-6183] [@CL-CMP-6184] in these paragraphs. The RFO text of FAR 52.222-12 provides that a breach of the listed contract clauses may be grounds for termination of the contract, and for debarment as a Contractor and subcontractor as provided in 29 CFR 5.12 [@CL-CMP-1415] [@CL-CMP-9262] [@CL-CMP-1416], and the RFO text of FAR 52.222-13 provides that all rulings and interpretations of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements and related statutes contained in 29 CFR parts 1, 3, and 5 are incorporated by reference in the contract [@CL-CMP-1417]. The RFO text prescribes the Subcontracts (Labor Standards) clause at FAR 22.402-4(a)(6) [@CL-CMP-9265], and the codified text prescribes it at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5839].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor is awarded a construction contract on day 0 and has 4 construction subcontracts in the United States at award. Each subcontract includes the listed clauses, and each subcontractor signs and dates an acknowledgment. The prime contractor delivers the 4 completed Standard Form 1413 documents to the Contracting Officer by day 14. A fifth subcontract is awarded on day 40, so its updated form is due by day 54. If a subcontractor at any tier fails to comply with a listed clause, the prime contractor remains responsible for that compliance.

## Never confuse

Responsibility for a subcontractor's compliance sits with the prime rather than with the subcontractor alone: the prime contractor shall be responsible for compliance by any subcontractor or lower tier subcontractor performing construction within the United States with all the contract clauses cited in paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-5841]. A breach of the listed contract clauses may be grounds for termination of the contract, and for debarment as a Contractor and subcontractor as provided in 29 CFR 5.12 [@CL-CMP-6185] [@CL-CMP-5843]. The insertion rules for these clauses are covered in [Construction labor standards disputes and the contract clauses and provision to insert](cmp-construction-labor-disputes-and-contract-clauses.md), and the companion clauses on disputes, certification of eligibility and approval of wage rates are covered in [Labor standards disputes, certification of eligibility and approval of wage rates](cmp-labor-standards-disputes-and-eligibility.md).
