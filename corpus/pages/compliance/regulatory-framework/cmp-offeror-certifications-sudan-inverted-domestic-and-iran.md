## Purpose

Explains what an offeror certifies or represents in paragraphs (m), (n) and (o) of the commercial offeror representations provision, which cover restricted business operations in Sudan, inverted domestic corporations, and certain activities and transactions relating to Iran, and what each paragraph says follows.

## The rule

|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-9010] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.212-3(m), (n) and (o) [@CL-CMP-9010; @CL-CMP-9011; @CL-CMP-9013] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.240-90(e) and (f) [@CL-CMP-1486; @CL-CMP-1490; @CL-CMP-1491; @CL-CMP-1492; @CL-CMP-1493; @CL-CMP-1494; @CL-CMP-1495; @CL-CMP-1496; @CL-CMP-1497; @CL-CMP-1498] and RFO FAR 52.209-2(b) and (c) [@CL-CMP-1487; @CL-CMP-1488; @CL-CMP-1489]; no RFO equivalent of the provision at FAR 52.212-3 itself, which the RFO text lists as reserved [@CL-CMP-1485] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-9010] |
| Applies to | Offerors, who make the Sudan certification and the Iran answers by submitting an offer, and Government agencies, which the inverted domestic corporation paragraph limits [@CL-CMP-9010; @CL-CMP-9013; @CL-CMP-9011] |

An offeror that has completed the annual representations and certification electronically in the System for Award Management completes only paragraph (b) of the provision [@CL-CMP-9250]. An offeror that has not completed them electronically shall complete only paragraphs (c) through (v), which include paragraphs (m), (n) and (o) [@CL-CMP-9251].

Sudan, paragraph (m). By submission of its offer, the offeror certifies that it does not conduct any restricted business operations in Sudan [@CL-CMP-9010].

Inverted domestic corporations, paragraph (n)(1): Government agencies are not permitted to use appropriated (or otherwise made available) funds for contracts with an inverted domestic corporation or a subsidiary of an inverted domestic corporation, unless the exception at FAR 9.108-2(b) applies or the requirement is waived in accordance with the procedures at FAR 9.108-4 [@CL-CMP-7882; @CL-CMP-9011].

Inverted domestic corporations, paragraph (n)(2): The offeror represents whether it is, or is not, an inverted domestic corporation, and whether it is, or is not, a subsidiary of an inverted domestic corporation [@CL-CMP-9012].

Iran, paragraph (o)(1): The offeror shall email questions concerning sensitive technology to the Department of State [@CL-CMP-9190].

Iran, paragraph (o)(2): Unless a waiver is granted or an exception applies as provided in paragraph (o)(3), the offeror makes three Iran answers by submission of its offer [@CL-CMP-9013]. First, it represents, to the best of its knowledge and belief, that it does not export any sensitive technology to the government of Iran or any entities or individuals owned or controlled by, or acting on behalf or at the direction of, the government of Iran [@CL-CMP-9014]. Second, it certifies that it, or any person owned or controlled by it, does not engage in any activities for which sanctions may be imposed under section 5 of the Iran Sanctions Act [@CL-CMP-9015]. Third, it certifies that it, and any person owned or controlled by it, does not knowingly engage in any transaction that exceeds the threshold at FAR 25.703-2(a)(2) with Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps or any of its officials, agents, or affiliates [@CL-CMP-9016]. The provision describes the property and interests in property of those parties as blocked pursuant to the International Emergency Economic Powers Act, and it refers the offeror to the Office of Foreign Assets Control list of Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons [@CL-CMP-9191; @CL-CMP-9192].

Iran, paragraph (o)(3): The requirements of paragraph (o)(2) do not apply if two conditions are both met [@CL-CMP-9017; @CL-CMP-9193]. One is that the solicitation includes a trade agreements certification (for example 52.212-3(g) or a comparable agency provision) [@CL-CMP-9017]. The other is that the offeror has certified that all the offered products to be supplied are designated country end products [@CL-CMP-9193].

## How it works

The Sudan certification and the Iran answers are made by submission of the offer [@CL-CMP-9010; @CL-CMP-9013]. The export representation is made to the best of the offeror's knowledge and belief [@CL-CMP-9014]. The two Iran certifications reach the offeror and any person it owns or controls [@CL-CMP-9015; @CL-CMP-9016].

The Iran requirements apply unless a waiver is granted or an exception applies as provided in paragraph (o)(3) [@CL-CMP-9013]. The exception in paragraph (o)(3) applies if the solicitation includes a trade agreements certification and the offeror has certified that all the offered products to be supplied are designated country end products [@CL-CMP-9017; @CL-CMP-9193].

Paragraph (n)(1) says Government agencies are not permitted to use appropriated funds for contracts with an inverted domestic corporation or a subsidiary of one [@CL-CMP-7882; @CL-CMP-9011], and paragraph (n)(2) has the offeror represent whether it is, or is not, an inverted domestic corporation and a subsidiary of an inverted domestic corporation [@CL-CMP-9012].

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text lists FAR 52.212-3 as [Reserved], so the codified paragraphs (m), (n) and (o), and the codified sentences telling an offeror to complete only paragraph (b) or only paragraphs (c) through (v), have no text under that number [@CL-CMP-1485] [@CL-CMP-9250] [@CL-CMP-9251]. The RFO text carries the Sudan certification and the Iran representation and certifications in paragraphs (e) and (f) of FAR 52.240-90, and the inverted domestic corporation paragraphs in FAR 52.209-2 [@CL-CMP-1486] [@CL-CMP-1490] [@CL-CMP-1487] [@CL-CMP-1489].

For Sudan, the RFO text says the offeror certifies by submission of its offer, after conducting a reasonable inquiry that looks at any information in its possession but does not need to include an internal or third-party audit, that it does not conduct any restricted business operations in Sudan [@CL-CMP-1486]. The codified paragraph states the certification without a reasonable inquiry [@CL-CMP-9010].

For inverted domestic corporations, the RFO text states the limit on agency funding for contracts with an inverted domestic corporation or its subsidiary [@CL-CMP-1487] [@CL-CMP-9011], but it names the exception at 9.108-3(b) and the waiver procedures at 9.108-5 [@CL-CMP-1488]. The codified paragraph names the exception at 9.108-2(b) and the procedures at 9.108-4 [@CL-CMP-9011]. The RFO text keeps the offeror's representation about its own status and its subsidiary status [@CL-CMP-1489] [@CL-CMP-9012].

For Iran, the RFO text says the offeror makes the representation and certifications except as provided in paragraph (f)(2) or if a waiver has been granted in accordance with FAR 40.203-3 [@CL-CMP-1490], and after a reasonable inquiry that looks at any information in its possession but does not need to include an internal or third-party audit [@CL-CMP-1491]. The codified paragraph says the offeror makes them unless a waiver is granted or an exception applies [@CL-CMP-9013]. The RFO text keeps the export representation made to the best of the offeror's knowledge and belief [@CL-CMP-1492] [@CL-CMP-9014]. It says the sanctions certification covers any person, as defined at section 15 of the Iran Sanctions Act of 1996, owned or controlled by the offeror [@CL-CMP-1493], where the codified paragraph covers any person owned or controlled by the offeror [@CL-CMP-9015]. The RFO paragraph also adds a sentence saying the sanctioned activities are in the areas of development of the petroleum resources of Iran, production of refined petroleum products in Iran, sale and provision of refined petroleum products to Iran, and contributing to Iran's ability to acquire or develop certain weapons or technologies [@CL-CMP-9260], which the codified paragraph does not have [@CL-CMP-9015]. For the certification about transactions with Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps or its officials, agents or affiliates, the RFO text states the threshold as $15,000 for any transaction that the offeror, or any person owned or controlled by it, knowingly engages in [@CL-CMP-1494], where the codified paragraph refers to the threshold at FAR 25.703-2(a)(2) [@CL-CMP-9016]. The same RFO sentence goes on to describe the blocked property and to refer to the Office of Foreign Assets Control list, as the codified sentence does [@CL-CMP-1495] [@CL-CMP-9191] [@CL-CMP-9192].

The RFO text states the trade agreements exception with two conditions. The first says the solicitation includes a trade agreements notice or certification and gives 52.225-6, Trade Agreements Certificate, as the example [@CL-CMP-1496], where the codified first condition is a trade agreements certification with 52.212-3(g) or a comparable agency provision as the example [@CL-CMP-9017]. The second says the offeror has certified that all the offered products to be supplied are designated country end products or designated country construction material [@CL-CMP-1497], where the codified second condition names only designated country end products [@CL-CMP-9193]. The RFO text puts the instruction to email questions about sensitive technology to the Department of State in paragraph (f)(2)(iii), where the codified text numbers it paragraph (o)(1) [@CL-CMP-1498] [@CL-CMP-9190].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror submits an offer to supply commercial products. It marks that it is not an inverted domestic corporation and not a subsidiary of one [@CL-CMP-9012]. Because no inverted domestic corporation or subsidiary is a party, the funding limit in paragraph (n)(1) does not reach a contract with it [@CL-CMP-9011]. The Sudan certification is made by submitting the offer, with no separate document [@CL-CMP-9010].

The solicitation includes a trade agreements certification, and the offeror has certified that all the products it offers are designated country end products. Both conditions are met, so the Iran answers in paragraph (o)(2) do not apply to this offer [@CL-CMP-9017; @CL-CMP-9193]. A second offeror in the same solicitation has not certified that all of its offered products are designated country end products and holds no waiver, so it makes the three Iran answers by submitting its offer [@CL-CMP-9013; @CL-CMP-9017].

## Never confuse

The Sudan certification is that the offeror does not conduct any restricted business operations in Sudan [@CL-CMP-9010]. The offeror's representation in paragraph (n)(2) is whether it is, or is not, an inverted domestic corporation and a subsidiary of one [@CL-CMP-9012]. The Iran requirements in paragraph (o)(2) do not apply if the paragraph (o)(3) conditions are both met [@CL-CMP-9017; @CL-CMP-9193]. The codified Iran certification about transactions refers to the threshold at FAR 25.703-2(a)(2) [@CL-CMP-9016], where the RFO text states the threshold as $15,000 [@CL-CMP-1494]. The codified Sudan certification is stated without a reasonable inquiry [@CL-CMP-9010], where the RFO text makes the certification after a reasonable inquiry [@CL-CMP-1486].

## Company calibration

The company must confirm who checks these certifications against its own operations before each offer is submitted [@Q-004].
