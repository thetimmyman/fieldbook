## Purpose

Explains the limit on modifying a provision or clause, and where changed wording and fill-in information are placed for a text incorporated by reference and for a text in full text.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8865] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.104 [@CL-CMP-8865] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.104 [@CL-CMP-1458; @CL-CMP-1459; @CL-CMP-1460; @CL-CMP-1461; @CL-CMP-1462; @CL-CMP-1463] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8865] |
| Applies to | Provisions and clauses that the FAR authorizes the contracting officer to modify, and provisions and clauses with blanks to be completed, whether incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8865; @CL-CMP-8866; @CL-CMP-8868] |

In this subpart, modification means a minor change in the details of a provision or clause that is specifically authorized by the FAR and does not alter the substance of the provision or clause [@CL-CMP-8864]. The contracting officer must not modify provisions and clauses unless the FAR authorizes their modification [@CL-CMP-8865]. The FAR's examples of such an authorization are a period shorter than 60 days but not less than 30 days in a stated paragraph of a clause, and the substitution of the words "task order" for the word "Schedule" wherever it appears in a clause [@CL-CMP-8908].

When a provision or clause incorporated by reference is modified, the changed wording is inserted directly below the title, identifying to the lowest level necessary, such as paragraph, sentence or word, what is being modified [@CL-CMP-8866]. When one incorporated in full text is modified, the language is modified directly by substituting the changed wording as permitted [@CL-CMP-8867].

When blanks in a provision or clause incorporated by reference are completed, the fill-in information is inserted directly below the title, identifying to the lowest level necessary the blanks being filled in [@CL-CMP-8868]. When blanks in one incorporated in full text are completed, the fill-in information is inserted in the blanks [@CL-CMP-8869].

## How it works

The contracting officer must not modify provisions and clauses unless the FAR authorizes their modification [@CL-CMP-8865]. The examples given are that the contracting officer may use a period shorter than 60 days (but not less than 30 days) in paragraph (x) of the clause, or may substitute the words ‘task order’ for the word ‘Schedule’ wherever that word appears in the clause [@CL-CMP-8908].

For a text incorporated by reference, the changed wording or the fill-in information is inserted directly below the title at the level necessary [@CL-CMP-8866; @CL-CMP-8868]. For a text in full text, the change is made in the language and the fill-in goes in the blanks [@CL-CMP-8867; @CL-CMP-8869].

Provisions and clauses should be incorporated by reference to the maximum practical extent, rather than being incorporated in full text, even if they require modification or the insertion by the Government of fill-in material, or require completion by the offeror or prospective contractor [@CL-CMP-8848; @CL-CMP-8850]. The card on [incorporating provisions and clauses by reference](cmp-incorporating-provisions-and-clauses-by-reference.md) covers that rule.

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.104 keeps the limit that the contracting officer must not modify provisions and clauses unless the FAR authorizes their modification [@CL-CMP-1458] [@CL-CMP-8865], and the same two examples of an authorization [@CL-CMP-1459] [@CL-CMP-8908]. It keeps the placement rules: changed wording goes directly below the title for a text incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-1460] [@CL-CMP-8866], the language is changed directly for a text in full text [@CL-CMP-1461] [@CL-CMP-8867], and fill-in information goes below the title for a text incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-1462] [@CL-CMP-8868] or in the blanks for a text in full text [@CL-CMP-1463] [@CL-CMP-8869].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A clause incorporated by reference carries a response period of 60 days. Suppose the FAR text that prescribes it says, as in the section's illustration, that the contracting officer may use a period shorter than 60 days but not less than 30 days [@CL-CMP-8908]. The contracting officer chooses 45 days. The change is authorized, so it may be made [@CL-CMP-8865]. The contracting officer writes directly below the clause title that the period in the named paragraph is 45 days, identifying the paragraph and the words changed, and leaves the rest of the clause untouched [@CL-CMP-8866]. A period of 20 days would fall outside that authorization and could not be made [@CL-CMP-8865; @CL-CMP-8908]. If the same clause were printed in full text, the contracting officer would substitute the changed wording in the paragraph itself [@CL-CMP-8867].

A provision incorporated by reference has a blank for a figure to be filled in. The fill-in information goes directly below the provision title, identifying the blank being filled in [@CL-CMP-8868]. In a provision printed in full text, the figure goes into the blank itself [@CL-CMP-8869].

## Never confuse

In this subpart, modification covers only a minor change in the details of a provision or clause that the FAR specifically authorizes and that does not alter its substance [@CL-CMP-8864]. The contracting officer must not modify provisions and clauses unless the FAR authorizes their modification [@CL-CMP-8865]. The identification is also used if the FAR provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation, except that the contracting officer then inserts “(DEVIATION)” after the date; see the card for [identifying provisions and clauses](cmp-identifying-provisions-and-clauses.md) [@CL-CMP-8858].

Provisions and clauses should be incorporated by reference to the maximum practical extent, rather than being incorporated in full text, even if they require modification or the insertion by the Government of fill-in material [@CL-CMP-8848; @CL-CMP-8850].
