## Purpose

Explains the indemnity and the medical liability insurance a contractor carries under a contract for health care services [@CL-CMP-5612].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5612] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.237-7 [@CL-CMP-5612] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.237-7 [@CL-CMP-1418; @CL-CMP-1446; @CL-CMP-1447] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5612] |
| Applies to | Contracts in which the clause is inserted as prescribed [@CL-CMP-5612] |

The clause states that it is expressly agreed and understood that the contract is a nonpersonal services contract, as defined in FAR 37.101, under which the professional services rendered by the contractor are rendered in its capacity as an independent contractor [@CL-CMP-6245]. The Government may evaluate the quality of professional and administrative services provided, but retains no control over professional aspects of the services rendered, including the contractor's professional medical judgment, diagnosis or specific medical treatments [@CL-CMP-5753]. The contractor is solely liable for, and agrees to indemnify the Government against, liability-producing acts or omissions by itself or its employees or agents [@CL-CMP-5613]. It must maintain liability insurance from a responsible carrier of not less than the amount stated per specialty per occurrence for the contract term [@CL-CMP-5614]. The clause prints the amount or amounts as a blank [@CL-CMP-5614].

An apparently successful offeror, upon the contracting officer's request, must furnish before contract award evidence of its insurability concerning the medical liability insurance required by paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-5754].

## How it works

Liability insurance may be on an occurrences basis or a claims-made basis, and a claims-made policy must also carry an extended reporting endorsement, or tail, for a period of not less than 3 years after the end of the contract term [@CL-CMP-5615]. Evidence of insurance documenting the required coverage for each health care provider who will perform under the contract must be provided to the contracting officer before services under the contract begin [@CL-CMP-5755]. If the insurance is claims-made and evidence of the extended reporting endorsement is not provided before services begin, that evidence must be provided before the contract expires [@CL-CMP-5756]. Final payment is withheld until evidence of the extended reporting endorsement is provided to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-5757].

The policy must contain an endorsement that a cancellation or material change adversely affecting the Government's interest is not effective until 30 days after the insurer or the contractor gives written notice to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-5616]. If during the performance period of the contract the Contractor changes insurance providers, the Contractor must provide evidence that the Government will be indemnified to the limits specified in paragraph (a) for the entire period of the contract, either under the new policy or a combination of old and new policies [@CL-CMP-5759; @CL-CMP-5758].

The contractor must insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (f), in all subcontracts under the contract for health care services, and must require those subcontractors to provide evidence of and maintain insurance in accordance with paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-5760]. At least 5 days before any subcontractor begins work, the contractor must furnish the contracting officer evidence of that insurance [@CL-CMP-5617].

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.237-7(a) says that the contract is a nonpersonal services contract under which the professional services rendered by the Contractor are rendered in its capacity as an independent contractor, where the codified sentence adds that the nonpersonal services contract is as defined in FAR 37.101 [@CL-CMP-1418] [@CL-CMP-6245]. The rest of the RFO clause keeps the codified rules: the Government's right to evaluate quality without control over medical judgment [@CL-CMP-1419] [@CL-CMP-5753], the contractor's sole liability and indemnity [@CL-CMP-1446] [@CL-CMP-5613], the required liability insurance [@CL-CMP-1447] [@CL-CMP-5614] and the evidence of insurability before award [@CL-CMP-1448] [@CL-CMP-5754]. It also keeps the occurrences or claims-made basis with a tail of not less than 3 years [@CL-CMP-1449] [@CL-CMP-5615], the evidence of coverage before services begin [@CL-CMP-1450] [@CL-CMP-5755], the evidence of the tail before the contract expires [@CL-CMP-1451] [@CL-CMP-5756], the withholding of final payment [@CL-CMP-1452] [@CL-CMP-5757], the 30-day cancellation endorsement [@CL-CMP-1453] [@CL-CMP-5616], the evidence of continued indemnity when insurers change [@CL-CMP-1454] [@CL-CMP-1455] [@CL-CMP-5758] [@CL-CMP-5759] and the subcontract flowdown with evidence at least 5 days before work begins [@CL-CMP-1456] [@CL-CMP-1457] [@CL-CMP-5760] [@CL-CMP-5617].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A clinic contract runs for 2 years with a limit of 1,000,000 per specialty per occurrence. The policy is written on a claims-made basis, so the contractor provides a tail of 4 years, which exceeds the 3-year minimum. The contractor gave the contracting officer evidence of each provider's coverage, including the tail, before services began. A subcontractor is due to start on day 20, and the contractor sends the subcontractor's insurance evidence on day 12. That is 8 days before the start, which meets the 5-day minimum.

The policy carries the required endorsement, so if the carrier gives the contracting officer written notice of cancellation on day 100 of the contract, the cancellation cannot take effect before day 130. Had the tail evidence not been given before services began, it would be due before the contract expired, and final payment would be held until it arrived.

## Never confuse

Liability insurance may be on either an occurrences basis or on a claims-made basis; if the policy is on a claims-made basis, an extended reporting endorsement (tail) for a period of not less than 3 years after the end of the contract term must also be provided [@CL-CMP-5615]. The Contractor shall be solely liable for and expressly agrees to indemnify the Government with respect to any liability producing acts or omissions by it or by its employees or agents [@CL-CMP-5613], and shall maintain during the term of this contract liability insurance issued by a responsible insurance carrier [@CL-CMP-5614]. The Government may evaluate the quality of professional and administrative services provided, but retains no control over professional aspects of the services rendered [@CL-CMP-5753]. The codified paragraph (a) refers to the definition of a nonpersonal services contract in FAR 37.101 [@CL-CMP-6245], where RFO FAR 52.237-7(a) states the sentence without that reference [@CL-CMP-1418].

The cost principle on insurance and indemnification is covered in [Insurance and indemnification](../cas-allowability/cmp-insurance-and-indemnification.md), and the Government indemnity under a public law is covered in [Indemnification under that public law](cmp-pub-law-85-804-indemnification.md).
