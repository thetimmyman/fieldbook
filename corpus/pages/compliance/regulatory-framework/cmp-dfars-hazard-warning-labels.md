## Purpose

Shows which hazardous materials need a hazard warning label under the clause, the standard that governs the label, and what the offeror lists.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4492] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.223-7001 [@CL-CMP-4492] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4492] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, used as prescribed in DFARS 223.304 [@CL-CMP-4543] |

The Contractor must label the item package (unit container) of any hazardous material to be delivered under the contract in accordance with the Hazard Communication Standard [@CL-CMP-4492]. The Standard requires that the hazard warning label conform to the requirements of the standard unless the material is otherwise subject to the labelling requirements of one of the following statutes [@CL-CMP-4541]. Hazardous material has the meaning set by the Hazardous Material Identification and Material Safety Data clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-4491].

The statutes are the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide and Rodenticide Act; the Federal Food, Drug and Cosmetics Act; the Consumer Product Safety Act; the Federal Hazardous Substances Act; or the Federal Alcohol Administration Act [@CL-CMP-4493]. The Contractor must also comply with MIL-STD-129, Marking for Shipment and Storage, including revisions adopted during the term of the contract [@CL-CMP-4497].

## How it works

The Offeror must list which hazardous material, from those listed in the identification and data sheet clause, will be labelled in accordance with one of the Acts in the clause's list of statutes instead of the Hazard Communication Standard [@CL-CMP-4494]. Any hazardous material the Offeror does not list is interpreted to mean that a label is required in accordance with the Standard [@CL-CMP-4495]. The apparently successful Offeror agrees to submit, before award, a copy of the hazard warning label for all hazardous materials it did not list [@CL-CMP-4496]. The Offeror must submit the label with the Material Safety Data Sheet being furnished under the identification and data sheet clause [@CL-CMP-4542].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror will deliver two hazardous materials. One is subject to the labelling requirements of one of the listed statutes, so the offeror lists it with that statute in its offer. The other is not listed, so a label under the Hazard Communication Standard is required for it. The apparently successful offeror sends a copy of that label, with the data sheet, before award.

## Never confuse

The hazard warning label duty is a labelling duty rather than a storage or disposal bar: the Contractor must label the item package (unit container) of any hazardous material to be delivered under the contract in accordance with the Hazard Communication Standard [@CL-CMP-4492]. For the bar on storing or disposing of non-DoD hazardous materials on an installation, see [Prohibition on storage, treatment and disposal of toxic or hazardous materials (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-toxic-hazardous-material-storage-prohibition.md) and [Safety precautions for ammunition and explosives (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-ammunition-explosives-safety-precautions.md). The FAR clause on hazardous material identification and material safety data is covered in [Hazardous material identification and material safety data](cmp-hazardous-material-identification-and-material-safety-data.md).
