## Purpose

Shows when an apprentice or trainee may be paid below the determined rate, the ratio, pay and fringe benefit rules that bound that discount, and the Copeland Act duty the contract incorporates.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5832] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-9 and FAR 52.222-10 [@CL-CMP-5832; @CL-CMP-5838] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5832] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the apprentices clause as prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5832] |

An apprentice is permitted to work at less than the predetermined rate for the work performed when employed pursuant to and individually registered in a bona fide apprenticeship program registered with the United States Department of Labor [@CL-CMP-5833]. The registration may instead be with a State Apprenticeship Agency recognized by the Office of Apprenticeship Training, Employer, and Labor Services (OATELS) [@CL-CMP-5834]. An apprentice in the first 90 days of probationary employment in such a program may also work below the predetermined rate without individual registration, if OATELS or a State Apprenticeship Agency certifies the apprentice as eligible for probationary employment [@CL-CMP-5835].

Trainees are not permitted to work at less than the predetermined rate for the work performed unless they are employed pursuant to and individually registered in a program that has received prior approval, evidenced by formal certification by the Department of Labor's Office of Apprenticeship Training, Employer, and Labor Services (OATELS), except as provided in 29 CFR 5.16 [@CL-CMP-5837; @CL-CMP-6468].

## How it works

The apprentice discount is bounded by a ratio. The allowable ratio of apprentices to journeymen on the job site in any craft classification may not be greater than the ratio permitted to the contractor for its entire work force under the registered program [@CL-CMP-5836]. A worker listed on a payroll at an apprentice rate who is not registered or otherwise employed as the clause states is paid not less than the applicable wage determination for the classification of work actually performed [@CL-CMP-6166]. An apprentice performing work on the job site in excess of the permitted ratio is paid not less than the applicable wage rate on the wage determination for the work actually performed [@CL-CMP-6167]. Where a contractor performs construction in a locality other than the one in which its program is registered, the ratios and wage rates in its registered program, expressed as percentages of the journeyman's hourly rate, are observed [@CL-CMP-6447].

An apprentice within the discount is paid at not less than the rate specified in the registered program for the apprentice's level of progress, expressed as a percentage of the journeyman hourly rate in the applicable wage determination [@CL-CMP-6168]. Fringe benefits are paid in accordance with the apprenticeship program, and if the program does not specify fringe benefits the apprentice is paid the full amount of fringe benefits listed on the wage determination for the applicable classification [@CL-CMP-6169]. If the Administrator determines that a different practice prevails for the applicable apprentice classification, fringes are paid in accordance with that determination [@CL-CMP-6170]. If OATELS or a recognized State Apprenticeship Agency withdraws approval of an apprenticeship program, the contractor may no longer use apprentices below the applicable predetermined rate until an acceptable program is approved [@CL-CMP-6171].

Trainees follow a parallel pattern. The ratio of trainees to journeymen on the job site may not be greater than the plan approved by OATELS permits [@CL-CMP-6172]. Every trainee is paid at not less than the rate specified in the approved program for the trainee's level of progress, expressed as a percentage of the journeyman hourly rate in the applicable wage determination [@CL-CMP-6173]. Trainees are paid fringe benefits in accordance with the trainee program, and if the program does not mention fringe benefits they are paid the full amount of fringe benefits listed in the wage determination [@CL-CMP-6174]. That full-fringe rule yields where the Administrator of the Wage and Hour Division determines that an apprenticeship program associated with the corresponding journeyman rate provides for less than full fringe benefits for apprentices [@CL-CMP-6175]. An employee listed at a trainee rate who is not registered and participating in a training plan approved by OATELS is paid not less than the applicable wage rate in the wage determination for the classification of work actually performed [@CL-CMP-6176]. A trainee performing work on the job site in excess of the permitted ratio is paid not less than the applicable wage rate for the work actually performed [@CL-CMP-6177]. If OATELS withdraws approval of a training program, the contractor may no longer use trainees below the applicable predetermined rate until an acceptable program is approved [@CL-CMP-6178].

The Copeland Act compliance clause adds an incorporation by reference, because the contractor must comply with 29 CFR part 3 [@CL-CMP-5838]. The use of apprentices, trainees and journeymen must also conform to the equal employment opportunity requirements of Executive Order 11246 and 29 CFR part 30 [@CL-CMP-6448].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The wage determination sets a journeyman rate of 40 per hour in wages and 10 per hour in fringe benefits. A crew carries 4 journeymen and 3 apprentices. The registered program permits 1 apprentice for every 2 journeymen, so the crew may keep 2 apprentices at the apprentice rate. The third apprentice is paid not less than the wage determination rate for the work actually performed, which is 40 per hour in wages and 10 per hour in fringe benefits.

An apprentice at the 60 percent level of progress is paid at least 24 per hour in wages, because 60 percent of 40 is 24. If the program does not specify fringe benefits and the Administrator has not determined a different practice, the apprentice also receives the full 10 per hour in fringe benefits.

A worker who is listed at an apprentice rate but is not registered, and is not in the first 90 days of certified probationary employment, is paid the wage determination rate for the work actually performed.

## Never confuse

An apprentice or trainee rate is not a general training allowance. It depends on registered or OATELS-approved program status, on staying inside the permitted ratio, and on paying at least the program rate for the worker's level of progress [@CL-CMP-5833; @CL-CMP-5836; @CL-CMP-5837; @CL-CMP-6468; @CL-CMP-6168; @CL-CMP-6172; @CL-CMP-6173]. The Copeland Act clause is also not a wage rate clause, because it adds an external body of requirements by reference [@CL-CMP-5838].
