## Purpose

Shows the rights categories the Government takes in computer software and software documentation that are not commercial, including restricted rights.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3775] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7014 [@CL-CMP-3775] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3775] |
| Applies to | Other than commercial computer software and other than commercial computer software documentation, subject to an exception [@CL-CMP-3775] |

The clause governs all other than commercial computer software or other than commercial computer software documentation, subject to an exception that names the clause at DFARS 252.227-7018 [@CL-CMP-3775]. The clause's definition of computer software does not include computer databases or computer software documentation [@CL-CMP-3776]. Computer software documentation means owner's manuals, user's manuals, installation instructions, operating instructions, and other similar items, regardless of storage medium, that explain the capabilities of the computer software or provide instructions for using the software [@CL-CMP-3829]. Restricted rights apply only to other than commercial computer software and mean the listed rights of the Government [@CL-CMP-3777]. One listed restricted right is to use a computer program with one computer at one time, and the program may not be accessed by more than one terminal or central processing unit or time shared unless the contract otherwise permits [@CL-CMP-3777; @CL-CMP-3778].

Two listed categories in which the Government shall have unlimited rights are computer software developed exclusively with Government funds and computer software documentation required to be delivered under this contract [@CL-CMP-3779]. Except as the unlimited rights paragraph provides, the Government shall have government purpose rights in computer software developed with mixed funding [@CL-CMP-3780]. Government purpose rights shall remain in effect for a period of five years unless a different period has been negotiated, and upon expiration the Government shall have unlimited rights in the software or documentation [@CL-CMP-3781]. The Government shall have restricted rights in other than commercial computer software required to be delivered or otherwise provided under this contract that were developed exclusively at private expense [@CL-CMP-3782].

## How it works

The Government shall have unlimited rights in computer software developed exclusively with Government funds [@CL-CMP-3779], government purpose rights, for 5 years unless a different period has been negotiated, in computer software developed with mixed funding [@CL-CMP-3780; @CL-CMP-3781], and restricted rights in other than commercial computer software required to be delivered or otherwise provided under the contract that were developed exclusively at private expense [@CL-CMP-3782]. Computer software documentation required to be delivered under the contract is also listed under unlimited rights [@CL-CMP-3779].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers a simulation program and its user's manual. The program was written at the contractor's own expense and cost 200. The Government has restricted rights in the program, and unlimited rights in the manual if the contract requires the manual to be delivered. If the same program had been developed half at the contractor's expense and half under a contract, the Government would have government purpose rights for five years and then unlimited rights.

## Never confuse

Restricted rights in software are not limited rights in technical data: under this clause the Government shall have restricted rights in other than commercial computer software developed exclusively at private expense [@CL-CMP-3782], while under the technical data clause, subject to listed exceptions, the Government shall have limited rights in technical data pertaining to items, components, or processes developed exclusively at private expense and marked with the limited rights legend [@CL-CMP-3772], and the license rights it lists are in technical data other than computer software documentation [@CL-CMP-3768]. For commercial software, see [Commercial computer software license clause](cmp-commercial-computer-software-license.md). For technical data, see [DoD rights in technical data: definitions and rights categories](cmp-dod-technical-data-rights-categories.md). For validation of restrictions asserted on software, see [DoD validation of asserted restrictions on computer software](cmp-dod-software-restriction-validation.md), and for the offer-time list, see the card for [identification and assertion of restrictions](cmp-dod-assertion-of-data-restrictions.md).
