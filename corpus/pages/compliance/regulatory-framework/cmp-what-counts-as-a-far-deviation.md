## Purpose

Shows what makes an action, a clause change or a local policy a deviation from the Federal Acquisition Regulation, so a contract file or handbook can be checked for one.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8330] |
| Codified citation | FAR 1.401 [@CL-CMP-8330] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8330] |
| Applies to | Policies, procedures, solicitation provisions, contract clauses, methods and practices of conducting acquisition actions [@CL-CMP-8331] |

A deviation means any one of the six listed forms, or any combination of them [@CL-CMP-8330]. The first form is the issuance or use of a policy, procedure, solicitation provision, contract clause, method or practice of conducting acquisition actions of any kind, at any stage of the acquisition process, that is inconsistent with the FAR [@CL-CMP-8331]. The second is the omission of any solicitation provision or contract clause when its prescription requires its use [@CL-CMP-8332]. The third is the use of any solicitation provision or contract clause with modified or alternate language that the FAR does not authorize [@CL-CMP-8333].

The fourth is the use of a provision or clause that the FAR prescribes on a substantially as follows or substantially the same as basis, if the use is inconsistent with the intent, principle or substance of the prescription, or with related FAR coverage on the subject matter [@CL-CMP-8334; @CL-CMP-8670]. The fifth is the authorization of lesser or greater limitations on the use of any solicitation provision, contract clause, policy or procedure that the FAR prescribes [@CL-CMP-8335]. The sixth is the issuance of policies or procedures that govern the contracting process, or otherwise control contracting relationships, and are not incorporated into agency acquisition regulations in accordance with the FAR policy on agency acquisition regulations [@CL-CMP-8336].

## How it works

The section is a definition. It does not say who may authorize a deviation or how; those rules are on [Deviations from the FAR: who authorizes them and what cannot be deviated from](../../operating-model/decision-rights/opm-deviations-from-the-far.md).

The definition measures each form against the FAR. The first form asks whether a policy, procedure, solicitation provision, contract clause, method or practice is inconsistent with the FAR [@CL-CMP-8331], and the fifth compares a limitation with what the FAR prescribes [@CL-CMP-8335]. The second and third forms compare a clause or provision in a solicitation or contract with its prescription and with the language the FAR authorizes [@CL-CMP-8332; @CL-CMP-8333]. The fourth asks whether a substantially as follows or substantially the same as use is inconsistent with the intent, principle or substance of the prescription or related FAR coverage [@CL-CMP-8334; @CL-CMP-8670]. The sixth form turns on whether a policy or procedure that governs the contracting process or controls contracting relationships has been incorporated into agency acquisition regulations, not on a comparison with a FAR prescription [@CL-CMP-8336].

A reader checking a contract therefore compares each solicitation provision and contract clause with its FAR prescription and with the modified or alternate language the FAR authorizes [@CL-CMP-8332; @CL-CMP-8333], and checks a substantially-the-same use against the intent and substance of the prescription [@CL-CMP-8334; @CL-CMP-8670]. How the FAR relates to agency supplements is on [The FAR, the DFARS, the overhaul and class deviations](cmp-acquisition-regulations.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract file lacks a clause whose prescription requires its use in contracts of that type. Omitting it is a deviation of the second form [@CL-CMP-8332]. A second contract uses a clause with reworded text that shortens a notice period, and the FAR does not authorize the rewording, so it is a deviation of the third form [@CL-CMP-8333]. A third contract uses a clause on a substantially as follows basis, and its wording keeps the intent, principle and substance of the prescription and fits the related FAR coverage. The fourth form is not met, because the use is not inconsistent with them [@CL-CMP-8334; @CL-CMP-8670]. Whether an approval exists for either of the first two is a separate question, answered on the card on who authorizes deviations.

## Never confuse

The definition of a deviation is not the same as an authorization to deviate. The definition says what counts as a deviation, and the authorization rules say who may grant one and in which cases [@CL-CMP-8330; @CL-OPM-0021]. A deviation is also not the same as using a modified clause, because modified or alternate language that the FAR authorizes is not a deviation under the third form [@CL-CMP-8333].
