## Purpose

Shows the disability equal opportunity duties, how the clause flows into subcontracts, and what the statement of equivalent rates for Federal hires is for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5891] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-36 and 52.222-42 [@CL-CMP-5891; @CL-CMP-5894] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-36 [@CL-CMP-6609; @CL-CMP-6610; @CL-CMP-6611; @CL-CMP-6612; @CL-CMP-6613; @CL-CMP-6614; @CL-CMP-6615] and RFO FAR 52.222-42 [@CL-CMP-6616; @CL-CMP-6617] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5891] |
| Applies to | Contracts carrying the disability equal opportunity clause, and contracts that carry the equivalent rates statement [@CL-CMP-5892; @CL-CMP-5894] |

The contractor must abide by the requirements of the equal opportunity clause at 41 CFR 60-741.5(a) as of March 24, 2014 [@CL-CMP-5891]. The clause prohibits discrimination against qualified individuals on the basis of disability and requires affirmative action by the contractor to employ and advance in employment qualified individuals with disabilities [@CL-CMP-5892].

The equivalent rates statement identifies the classes of service employees expected to be employed under the contract and states the wages and fringe benefits payable to each if employed by the contracting agency subject to the cited pay statutes [@CL-CMP-5894].

## How it works

The contractor must include the clause's terms in every subcontract or purchase order above the threshold specified in the acquisition regulation on the date of subcontract award, unless the Secretary's rules, regulations or orders exempt it [@CL-CMP-5893], so that such provisions are binding upon each subcontractor or vendor [@CL-CMP-6633]. The contractor must also act as specified by the Director of the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs of the Department of Labor to enforce the terms of the clause in subcontracts, including action for noncompliance [@CL-CMP-6388]. Such necessary changes in language may be made as are appropriate to identify properly the parties and their undertakings [@CL-CMP-6632]. Alternate I adds a preamble stating that the listed terms of the disability clause are waived for the contract, and the list is filled in per contract [@CL-CMP-6389].

The statement of equivalent rates lists each expected employee class with the wage and fringe benefit an agency hire in that class would receive [@CL-CMP-5894]. It is for information only and is not a wage determination [@CL-CMP-5895].

Under the RFO text, which DoD contracting officers use in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762], the comparison with the codified text is as follows. The RFO clause at FAR 52.222-36 keeps the duty to abide by the equal opportunity clause at 41 CFR 60-741.5(a), with "shall abide" now "must abide" [@CL-CMP-5891; @CL-CMP-6609], and the prohibition of discrimination with the affirmative action duty [@CL-CMP-5892; @CL-CMP-6610]. Its subcontract paragraph says "must include" where the codified clause says "shall include", and it names FAR 22.1401-2(a)(1) for the threshold where the codified clause names FAR 22.1408(a) [@CL-CMP-5893; @CL-CMP-6611]. The rest of the subcontract paragraph keeps the provisions binding on each subcontractor or vendor [@CL-CMP-6633; @CL-CMP-6612], the action specified by the Director for enforcement [@CL-CMP-6388; @CL-CMP-6613] and the changes in language needed to identify the parties [@CL-CMP-6632; @CL-CMP-6614]. Alternate I keeps the notice that listed terms are waived [@CL-CMP-6389; @CL-CMP-6615]. The RFO clause at FAR 52.222-42 keeps the identification of the expected employee classes with their wages and fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-5894; @CL-CMP-6616] and the statement that it is for information only and is not a wage determination [@CL-CMP-5895; @CL-CMP-6617].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract carries the disability clause and the equivalent rates statement. A purchase order whose value is above the threshold on its award date must include the clause's terms, unless the Secretary's rules, regulations or orders exempt it; one at or below the threshold need not. The statement lists two employee classes whose agency wage would be 22 and 27 per hour plus fringe benefits. It shows what the contracting agency would pay those classes under the cited pay statutes; it is for information only and is not a wage determination.

## Never confuse

The equivalent rates statement is often mistaken for a wage determination. The clause says it is for information only and is not a wage determination [@CL-CMP-5895], and its content is the agency-side wage and fringe benefit for each expected employee class [@CL-CMP-5894]. The disability clause binds the contractor to the equal opportunity clause at 41 CFR 60-741.5(a) [@CL-CMP-5891], not to the general equal opportunity clause for race, sex and the other listed bases. The codified clause points to FAR 22.1408(a) for the subcontract threshold, but the RFO clause points to FAR 22.1401-2(a)(1) [@CL-CMP-5893; @CL-CMP-6611].
