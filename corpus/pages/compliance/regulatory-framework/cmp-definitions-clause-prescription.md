## Purpose

States the prescription that places the Definitions clause in a solicitation or contract. The printed section defines no terms, so the glossary table lists the terms the section uses and says where each is established.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | RFO model text [@CL-CMP-8615] |
| Codified citation | FAR 2.201 [@CL-CMP-10000; @CL-CMP-8615] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 2.201 [@CL-CMP-8615] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8615] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-8615] |

The prescription directs that the clause at 52.202-1, Definitions, be inserted in solicitations and contracts that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-8615]. The codified FAR 2.201 gives the same direction for the clause at 52.202-1, Definitions, and the same scope of solicitations and contracts that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-10000; @CL-CMP-8615]. The section does not name who inserts the clause. The threshold is measured on the solicitation or contract [@CL-CMP-8615]. The section prints no dollar figure; the codified definition of the threshold is $350,000, with listed exceptions [@CL-FND-0001]. The printed section ends there. It states no definition for any term, so this card records none.

## How it works

The prescription is a placement rule keyed to the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-8615]. It says where the Definitions clause goes. It does not state the terms the clause defines, and it says nothing about solicitations and contracts that do not exceed the threshold [@CL-CMP-8615].

### Glossary

| Term | What the printed section establishes |
|---|---|
| Definitions clause | The clause at 52.202-1, titled Definitions, which the section directs be inserted [@CL-CMP-8615] |

| Any other term | Not established; the printed section states no definition. |

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation has an estimated value of 500,000, and a second one has an estimated value of 100,000. Assume the simplified acquisition threshold is 350,000 and none of its listed exceptions applies [@CL-FND-0001]. The first solicitation exceeds the threshold, so the Definitions clause at 52.202-1 goes into it and the resulting contract [@CL-CMP-8615]. This section does not direct insertion in the second solicitation [@CL-CMP-8615]. Answer: the clause is inserted in the first, and no term definition is recorded on this card.

## Never confuse

The prescription is not the clause text. This section says when to insert the Definitions clause, while the clause itself, which the printed section does not reproduce, is not set out here [@CL-CMP-8615]. The [clause's own content, including which definition governs a word](../regulatory-framework/cmp-contract-approval-survey-and-definitions.md) and the [threshold values](../regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md) have their own cards.
