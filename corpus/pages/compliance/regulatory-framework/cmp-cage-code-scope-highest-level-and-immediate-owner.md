## Purpose

Explains what the commercial and Government entity code subpart is for and how it defines the ownership chain behind an offeror [@CL-CMP-8314; @CL-CMP-8319; @CL-CMP-8321].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8314] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1800 and 4.1801 [@CL-CMP-8314; @CL-CMP-8319] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8314] |
| Applies to | Identification of commercial and government entities, including the offeror's immediate owner and highest-level owner [@CL-CMP-8314; @CL-CMP-8319; @CL-CMP-8321] |

The subpart prescribes policies and procedures for identification of commercial and government entities [@CL-CMP-8314]. The CAGE code system may be used, among other things, for the purposes the section lists [@CL-CMP-8596]. One listed use is to exchange data with another contracting activity, including contract administration activities and contract payment activities [@CL-CMP-8315]. Another is to exchange data with another system that requires the unique identification of a contractor entity [@CL-CMP-8316]. A third is to identify when offerors are owned or controlled by another entity [@CL-CMP-8317]. The unique entity identifier is a different identifier, and information on it is at 4.605 and in the provisions at 52.204-6, Unique Entity Identifier, and 52.204-7, System for Award Management [@CL-CMP-8318].

The definitions in section 4.1801 apply as used in part 4 [@CL-CMP-8595]. An immediate owner is an entity, other than the offeror, that has direct control of the offeror [@CL-CMP-8321]. Indicators of control include, but are not limited to, one or more of ownership or interlocking management, identity of interests among family members, shared facilities and equipment, and the common use of employees [@CL-CMP-8322]. The highest-level owner is the entity that owns or controls an immediate owner of the offeror, or that owns or controls one or more entities that control an immediate owner of the offeror [@CL-CMP-8319]. No entity owns or exercises control of the highest level owner [@CL-CMP-8320].

## How it works

The CAGE code system may be used, among other things, for the three listed uses [@CL-CMP-8596; @CL-CMP-8315; @CL-CMP-8316; @CL-CMP-8317]. The ownership questions that an offeror answers are in the provisions described in [Ownership or Control of Offeror provision](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-ownership-or-control-of-offeror.md) and [Procurement instrument identifiers, CAGE code verification and the CAGE provisions](cmp-piid-and-cage-verification.md).

An entity, other than the offeror, that has direct control of the offeror is the immediate owner [@CL-CMP-8321]. The highest-level owner is the entity that owns or controls an immediate owner of the offeror, and no entity owns or exercises control of it [@CL-CMP-8319; @CL-CMP-8320]. The highest-level owner can own or control the immediate owner directly, or through one or more entities that control the immediate owner [@CL-CMP-8319]. The definition lists indicators of control, one or more of which may be present, and the listed indicators are not the only ones [@CL-CMP-8322]. The unique entity identifier is a different identifier from the code, and the section points elsewhere for it; see [Unique entity identifier in contract reporting](../../market/feeds/mkt-unique-entity-identifier-contract-reporting.md) [@CL-CMP-8318].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror is controlled directly by a holding entity, which is in turn controlled by a family trust, and no entity owns or controls the trust. The holding entity is the immediate owner, because it has direct control of the offeror [@CL-CMP-8321]. The trust is the highest-level owner, because it controls an immediate owner of the offeror and no entity owns or exercises control of it [@CL-CMP-8319; @CL-CMP-8320]. Shared facilities and the common use of employees between the offeror and another entity are among the listed indicators of control [@CL-CMP-8322].

## Never confuse

The subpart says the unique entity identifier is a different identifier and points to 4.605 and to the provisions at 52.204-6 and 52.204-7 for it [@CL-CMP-8318]. The highest-level owner is the entity that owns or controls an immediate owner of the offeror, and an immediate owner is an entity, other than the offeror, that has direct control of the offeror [@CL-CMP-8319; @CL-CMP-8321].
