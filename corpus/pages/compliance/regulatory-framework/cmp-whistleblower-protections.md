## Purpose

Sets out the notice a contractor owes its workforce under the Recovery Act whistleblower clause and under the contractor employee whistleblower rights clause, and the flowdown each clause requires.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5561] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-15 and 52.203-17 [@CL-CMP-5561; @CL-CMP-5563] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.203-17 [@CL-CMP-6604; @CL-CMP-6605; @CL-CMP-6607; @CL-CMP-6608]; No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.203-15, which RFO part 52 lists as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-6603] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5561] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Recovery Act whistleblower clause, prescribed by FAR 3.907-7, or the contractor employee whistleblower rights clause, prescribed by FAR 3.906 [@CL-CMP-5649; @CL-CMP-5650] |

The Recovery Act clause requires the contractor to post notice of employees rights and remedies for whistleblower protections provided under section 1553 of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 [@CL-CMP-5561].

The contractor employee clause subjects the contract and the employees working on it to the whistleblower rights and remedies established at section 4712 of title 41, United States Code, and FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-5563]. It also requires the contractor to inform its employees in writing, in the predominant language of the workforce, of employee whistleblower rights and protections under that section, as described in FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-5564].

## How it works

The Recovery Act clause requires the contractor to include the substance of the clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all subcontracts that are funded in whole or in part with Recovery Act funds [@CL-CMP-5562].

The contractor employee clause requires the contractor to insert the substance of the clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all subcontracts [@CL-CMP-5565].

Under the RFO text, which DoD contracting officers use in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762], the comparison with the codified text is as follows. The RFO clause at FAR 52.203-17 keeps the rule that the contract and the employees working on it are subject to the whistleblower rights and remedies at section 4712 of title 41 and FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-5563; @CL-CMP-6604], the duty to inform employees in writing, in the predominant language of the workforce [@CL-CMP-5564; @CL-CMP-6605], and the duty to insert the substance of the clause in all subcontracts [@CL-CMP-5565; @CL-CMP-6607]. It is prescribed in FAR 3.906, as in the codified text [@CL-CMP-5650; @CL-CMP-6608]. The RFO text lists FAR 52.203-15 as [Reserved], where the codified text has the Recovery Act whistleblower clause [@CL-CMP-5561; @CL-CMP-6603].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract carries both clauses, and the contractor has 9 subcontracts, of which 2 are funded in whole or in part with Recovery Act funds. It posts the notice of rights and remedies and tells its workforce in writing, in the language most of them speak, about employee whistleblower rights. It puts the substance of the contractor employee clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all 9 subcontracts, and the substance of the Recovery Act clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in the 2 funded ones. Under the RFO text FAR 52.203-15 is listed as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-6603], while the written notice and the flowdown in all subcontracts remain in FAR 52.203-17 [@CL-CMP-6605; @CL-CMP-6607].

## Never confuse

Posting a notice is not the same as the written notice to employees. The Recovery Act clause requires a posted notice of rights and remedies, while the contractor employee clause requires the contractor to inform employees in writing in the predominant language of the workforce [@CL-CMP-5561; @CL-CMP-5564]. The codified text has one clause requiring a posted notice under the Recovery Act and one requiring written notice to employees [@CL-CMP-5561; @CL-CMP-5564], but the RFO text lists the posted-notice clause number FAR 52.203-15 as [Reserved] and keeps the written notice duty at FAR 52.203-17 [@CL-CMP-6603; @CL-CMP-6605].
