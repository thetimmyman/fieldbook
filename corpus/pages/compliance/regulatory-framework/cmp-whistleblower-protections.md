## Purpose

Sets out the notice a contractor owes its workforce under the Recovery Act whistleblower clause and under the contractor employee whistleblower rights clause, and the flowdown each clause requires.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5561] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-15 and 52.203-17 [@CL-CMP-5561; @CL-CMP-5563]; FAR 3.906 [@CL-CMP-9717]; FAR 3.907-7 [@CL-CMP-9719] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.203-17 [@CL-CMP-6604; @CL-CMP-6605; @CL-CMP-6607; @CL-CMP-6608]; No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.203-15, which RFO part 52 lists as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-6603] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5561] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Recovery Act whistleblower clause, prescribed by FAR 3.907-7, or the contractor employee whistleblower rights clause, prescribed by FAR 3.906 [@CL-CMP-5649; @CL-CMP-5650] |

The Recovery Act clause requires the contractor to post notice of employees rights and remedies for whistleblower protections provided under section 1553 of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 [@CL-CMP-5561].

The contractor employee clause subjects the contract and the employees working on it to the whistleblower rights and remedies established at section 4712 of title 41, United States Code, and FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-5563]. It also requires the contractor to inform its employees in writing, in the predominant language of the workforce, of employee whistleblower rights and protections under that section, as described in FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-5564].

## How it works

The Recovery Act clause requires the contractor to include the substance of the clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all subcontracts that are funded in whole or in part with Recovery Act funds [@CL-CMP-5562].

The contractor employee clause requires the contractor to insert the substance of the clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all subcontracts [@CL-CMP-5565].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.203-17, which is prescribed in FAR 3.906 [@CL-CMP-6608], the contract and employees working on the contract are subject to the whistleblower rights and remedies established at section 4712 of title 41 of the United States Code and FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-6604], the Contractor shall inform its employees in writing, in the predominant language of the workforce, of employee whistleblower rights and protections under section 4712 of title 41, as described in FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-6605], and the Contractor shall insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), in all subcontracts [@CL-CMP-6607]. RFO FAR 52.203-15 is listed as `[Reserved]` [@CL-CMP-6603].

The contracting officer shall insert the contractor employee whistleblower rights clause at FAR 52.203-17, Contractor Employee Whistleblower Rights, in all solicitations and contracts, except solicitations and contracts of DoD, NASA, the Coast Guard, or applicable elements of the intelligence community (see FAR 3.900(a)) [@CL-CMP-9717]. The Recovery Act whistleblower clause at FAR 52.203-15 is used in all solicitations and contracts funded in whole or in part with Recovery Act funds [@CL-CMP-9719].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract carries both clauses, and the contractor has 9 subcontracts, of which 2 are funded in whole or in part with Recovery Act funds. It posts the notice of rights and remedies and tells its workforce in writing, in the language most of them speak, about employee whistleblower rights. It puts the substance of the contractor employee clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all 9 subcontracts, and the substance of the Recovery Act clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in the 2 funded ones. In RFO FAR part 52, 52.203-15 is `[Reserved]` [@CL-CMP-6603], while the written notice and the flowdown in all subcontracts are in RFO FAR 52.203-17 [@CL-CMP-6605; @CL-CMP-6607].

## Never confuse

Under FAR 52.203-15, the Contractor shall post notice of employees rights and remedies for whistleblower protections provided under section 1553 of the Recovery Act [@CL-CMP-5561], whereas under FAR 52.203-17, the Contractor shall inform its employees in writing, in the predominant language of the workforce, of employee whistleblower rights and protections under section 4712 of title 41 of the United States Code, as described in FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-5564]. In RFO FAR part 52, 52.203-15 is `[Reserved]` and RFO FAR 52.203-17 requires the Contractor to inform its employees in writing, in the predominant language of the workforce, of employee whistleblower rights and protections under section 4712 of title 41 of the United States Code, as described in FAR 3.900 through 3.905 [@CL-CMP-6603; @CL-CMP-6605].
