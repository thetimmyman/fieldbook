## Purpose

Shows when a corporate administrative contracting officer (CACO) is assigned, how the location and responsible agency are chosen, and how Government visits to a contractor facility are coordinated.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2207] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.602, FAR 42.603 and FAR 42.402 [@CL-CMP-2207; @CL-CMP-2259; @CL-CMP-2213] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.402 [@CL-CMP-0096; @CL-CMP-0097; @CL-CMP-0098; @CL-CMP-0099; @CL-CMP-0135]; RFO FAR 42.401 [@CL-CMP-0093]; RFO FAR 42.403 [@CL-CMP-0100; @CL-CMP-0115] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2207] |
| Applies to | Contractors with at least two locations with resident ACOs, or where the agency head or designee approves the need [@CL-CMP-2207] |

A CACO may be assigned only when the contractor has at least two locations with resident administrative contracting officers, or when the agency head or designee approves the need [@CL-CMP-2207]. For the rule on when a CACO may be assigned, a nonresident officer counts as resident if at least 75 percent of that officer's effort is devoted to a single contractor [@CL-CMP-2208].

In choosing where to put the CACO, the responsible agency considers such factors as the location of corporate records, the corporate office, the major plant, the cognizant government auditor and overall cost effectiveness [@CL-CMP-2209]. A decision to start or end the assignment should rest on such factors as the benefits of coordination and liaison at the corporate level and the volume of Government sales [@CL-CMP-2210]. When all locations of a corporate entity sit under a single agency, that agency is responsible for assigning the CACO [@CL-CMP-2211]. When they sit under more than one agency, the agencies agree on the responsible agency, normally the one with the largest dollar balance, including options, of affected contracts [@CL-CMP-2212].

The CACO shall perform, on a corporate-wide basis, the contract administration functions as designated by the responsible agency [@CL-CMP-2259]. Typical functions include the determination of final indirect cost rates for cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-CMP-2260].

Government personnel planning to visit a contractor facility on one or more Government contracts shall provide prior notification to the cognizant contract administration office, with the information listed in FAR 42.402(a), sufficiently in advance to permit it to make necessary arrangements [@CL-CMP-2213]. Where a visit will result in reviewing, auditing or obtaining information from the contractor relating to contract administration functions, the prospective visitor shall identify the information in sufficient detail [@CL-CMP-2258]. If the contract administration office, after consultation with the contractor and the cognizant audit office, determines that adequate information has recently been reviewed by or is available within the Government, it will discourage the visit and refer the prospective visitor to the Government office where such information is located [@CL-CMP-7676; @CL-CMP-2215].

## How it works

A decision to initiate or discontinue a CACO assignment should be based on such factors as the benefits of coordination and liaison at the corporate level [@CL-CMP-2210]. When all locations of a corporate entity are under the contract administration cognizance of a single agency, that agency is responsible for assigning a CACO [@CL-CMP-2211]; when the locations are under more than one agency, the agencies concerned shall agree on the responsible agency [@CL-CMP-2212]. Government personnel planning to visit a contractor's facility shall provide prior notification to the cognizant contract administration office sufficiently in advance to permit it to make necessary arrangements [@CL-CMP-2213]. If the contract administration office, after consultation with the contractor and the cognizant audit office, determines that adequate information has recently been reviewed by or is available within the Government, it will discourage the visit and refer the prospective visitor to the Government office where such information is located [@CL-CMP-7676; @CL-CMP-2215]. The CACO shall perform the contract administration functions as designated by the responsible agency [@CL-CMP-2259]. A corporate ACO's role on IR&D and B&P costs is covered in [DoD IR&D and B&P responsibilities](../cas-allowability/cmp-dod-ird-bp-responsibilities.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.402(a) keeps the rule that a CACO may be assigned only when the contractor has at least two locations with resident ACOs or the agency head or designee approves the assignment, where the codified paragraph says the need for a CACO is approved by the agency head or designee [@CL-CMP-0096; @CL-CMP-2207]. RFO paragraph (a) keeps the rule that, for the purpose of paragraph (a)(2), a nonresident ACO is considered as resident if at least 75 percent of the ACO's effort is devoted to a single contractor [@CL-CMP-0097; @CL-CMP-2208]. RFO paragraph (a) goes from the sentence on designating a resident ACO to paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-0098], where the codified paragraph (a) goes on to the factors for the location of the CACO, such as the corporate records, corporate office, major plant, cognizant government auditor and overall cost effectiveness [@CL-CMP-2209]. RFO paragraph (b) says agencies must base decisions to initiate or discontinue a CACO assignment on such factors as the benefits of coordination and liaison at the corporate level and the volume of Government sales, where the codified paragraph says a decision should be based on them [@CL-CMP-0099; @CL-CMP-2210]. RFO FAR 42.401 says the CFA may assign a CACO to deal with corporate management and to perform selected contract administration functions on a corporate-wide basis [@CL-CMP-0093], and RFO paragraph (c) of FAR 42.402 says the CASD referenced in 42.203 includes a listing of CACOs and the contractors to whom they are assigned responsibility [@CL-CMP-0135], where codified FAR 42.602(c) says who is responsible for assigning a CACO according to whether the locations are under one agency or more than one [@CL-CMP-2211; @CL-CMP-2212]. RFO FAR 42.403(a) keeps the rule that the CACO performs, on a corporate-wide basis, the contract administration functions as designated by the responsible agency, and says 'will perform' where the codified paragraph says 'shall perform' [@CL-CMP-0100; @CL-CMP-2259]. It keeps the determination of final indirect cost rates for cost-reimbursement contracts as a typical CACO function [@CL-CMP-0115; @CL-CMP-2260]. The RFO section numbered 42.402 is headed Assignment and location [@CL-CMP-0096], where codified FAR 42.402 covers visits to contractors' facilities and prior notice to the cognizant contract administration office [@CL-CMP-2213].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has plants in two states, each with a resident administrative contracting officer. A CACO may be assigned when the contractor has at least two locations with resident ACOs [@CL-CMP-2207]. A buying office then plans to visit one plant to ask for rate data that the contract administration office reviewed last month, so it provides prior notification to the cognizant contract administration office [@CL-CMP-2213] and identifies the information in sufficient detail [@CL-CMP-2258]. The data has recently been reviewed by or is available within the Government, so the contract administration office will discourage the visit and refer the buying office to the Government office where the data is located [@CL-CMP-7676; @CL-CMP-2215]. The visit steps in this example follow codified FAR 42.402 [@CL-CMP-2213; @CL-CMP-2215]; the RFO section numbered 42.402 is headed Assignment and location [@CL-CMP-0096].

## Never confuse

A facility visit is not a CACO assignment: Government personnel planning to visit a contractor's facility shall provide prior notification to the cognizant CAO [@CL-CMP-2213]. The related cognizant Federal agency role is covered in [Cognizant Federal agency](../cas-allowability/cmp-cas-cognizant-agency.md). The wider set of oversight roles is covered in [Oversight roles](../audit-retention/cmp-oversight-roles.md). The section number 42.402 is visits to contractors' facilities in the codified text [@CL-CMP-2213] but the assignment and location of a CACO in the RFO text [@CL-CMP-0096]. A decision to start or end a CACO assignment should be based on such factors as the benefits of coordination and liaison at the corporate level and the volume of Government sales under codified FAR 42.602(b) [@CL-CMP-2210], while RFO FAR 42.402(b) says agencies must base such decisions on those factors [@CL-CMP-0099].
