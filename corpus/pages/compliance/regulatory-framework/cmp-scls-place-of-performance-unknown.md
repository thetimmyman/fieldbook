## Purpose

Shows how wage determinations are handled when the place of performance of a Service Contract Labor Standards contract is unknown when the solicitation is issued.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-4851] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-49 [@CL-CMP-4851] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-49 [@CL-CMP-5015; @CL-CMP-5016; @CL-CMP-5017; @CL-CMP-5018; @CL-CMP-5019] |
| Edition date | MAY 2014 [@CL-CMP-10268] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4851] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to the statute where the place of performance was unknown at solicitation [@CL-CMP-4851] |

The contract is subject to the Service Contract Labor Standards statute, and the place of performance was unknown when the solicitation was issued [@CL-CMP-4851]. In addition to places or areas identified in wage determinations attached to the solicitation, wage determinations have also been requested for the places or areas inserted in the clause [@CL-CMP-5004]. The contracting officer will request wage determinations for additional places or areas of performance if asked to do so in writing by the time and date inserted in the clause [@CL-CMP-4852]. The places, areas, time and date are fill-ins that each contract supplies.

Offerors who intend to perform in a place or area of performance for which a wage determination has not been attached or requested may nevertheless submit bids or proposals [@CL-CMP-5005]. However, a wage determination shall be requested and incorporated in the resultant contract retroactive to the date of contract award, and there shall be no adjustment in the contract price [@CL-CMP-4853].

## How it works

The contracting officer will request wage determinations for additional places or areas of performance if asked to do so in writing by the inserted time and date [@CL-CMP-4852]. An offeror that intends to perform in a place with no attached or requested determination can still bid or propose [@CL-CMP-5005]. In that case a wage determination shall be requested and incorporated retroactive to the award date, and there shall be no adjustment in the contract price [@CL-CMP-4853].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of the clause keeps the statement that the contract is subject to the statute and that the place of performance was unknown when the solicitation was issued [@CL-CMP-5015; @CL-CMP-4851], and that wage determinations have also been requested for the places or areas inserted in the clause, in addition to those in wage determinations attached to the solicitation [@CL-CMP-5016; @CL-CMP-5004]. It keeps the rule that the contracting officer will request wage determinations for additional places or areas if asked in writing by the time and date inserted in the clause [@CL-CMP-5017; @CL-CMP-4852], and that offerors may still submit bids or proposals for a place with no attached or requested determination [@CL-CMP-5018; @CL-CMP-5005]. It keeps the rule that a wage determination must be requested and incorporated in the resultant contract retroactive to the date of contract award, and there shall be no adjustment in the contract price [@CL-CMP-5019; @CL-CMP-4853].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation attaches a wage determination for site A and has requested one for site B. An offeror intends to perform at site C, which has neither, and bids 500,000. The offeror wins. A determination for site C is requested and incorporated retroactive to the award date, and the 500,000 price is not adjusted. Had the offeror asked in writing by the inserted time and date, the contracting officer would have requested a determination for site C.

## Never confuse

This clause is not a price adjustment clause. An unknown place brings the determination in retroactively with no change in the contract price [@CL-CMP-4853], while a price adjustment clause adjusts the price for covered wage changes [@CL-CMP-4806]. The agency-side steps for unidentified places are on [unidentified places and hearings](cmp-scls-remanufacturing-unidentified-places-and-hearings.md).
