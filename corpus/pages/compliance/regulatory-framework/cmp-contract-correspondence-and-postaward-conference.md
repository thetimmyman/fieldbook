## Purpose

Shows how correspondence on assigned contract administration functions moves between the contracting office, the contract administration office and the contractor, and who arranges and reports a postaward conference.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7796] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.401, FAR 42.503-1 and FAR 42.503-3 [@CL-CMP-7796; @CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7803] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.302(a)(3) lists conducting post-award orientation conferences among the functions the contracting officer normally delegates to a CAO [@CL-CMP-0026; @CL-CMP-0091]. The RFO sections numbered 42.401, 42.5 and 42.503-1 cover other subjects [@CL-CMP-0093; @CL-CMP-0094; @CL-CMP-0095]. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7796] |
| Applies to | Correspondence relating to assigned contract administration functions, and a postaward conference that the contracting officer decides is needed [@CL-CMP-7796; @CL-CMP-7799] |

The contracting officer, or other contracting agency personnel, normally shall forward correspondence relating to assigned contract administration functions to the contractor through the cognizant contract administration office, and shall provide a copy for that office's file [@CL-CMP-7796]. When urgency requires sending such correspondence directly to the contractor, a copy shall be sent concurrently to the contract administration office [@CL-CMP-7797]. The contract administration office shall send the contracting office a copy of pertinent correspondence conducted between that office and the contractor [@CL-CMP-7798].

The contracting officer who decides that a conference is needed is responsible for establishing the time and place of the conference and for preparing the agenda when necessary [@CL-CMP-7799]. That officer is also responsible for notifying appropriate Government representatives and the contractor, designating or acting as the chairperson, and conducting a preliminary meeting of Government personnel [@CL-CMP-7800]. The same officer is responsible for preparing a summary report of the conference [@CL-CMP-7801]. When the contracting office initiates a conference, the arrangements may be made by that office or, at its request, by the contract administration office [@CL-CMP-7802].

## How it works

The chairperson shall prepare and sign a report of the postaward conference [@CL-CMP-7803]. The report shall cover all items discussed, including areas requiring resolution, controversial matters, the names of the participants assigned responsibility for further actions, and the due dates for the actions [@CL-CMP-7804]. The chairperson shall furnish copies of the report to the contracting office, the contract administration office, the contractor, and others who require the information [@CL-CMP-7805].

The contracting officer who decides a conference is needed is responsible for preparing a summary report of the conference [@CL-CMP-7801], and the chairperson shall prepare and sign a report of the postaward conference [@CL-CMP-7803]. The contracting officer is also responsible for designating or acting as the chairperson [@CL-CMP-7800]. Postaward orientation is covered in [Postaward orientation](../../operating-model/cadence/opm-postaward-orientation.md). The functions of the contract administration office are on [Contract administration functions](../audit-retention/cmp-contract-administration-functions.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.302(a) says the contracting officer normally delegates the listed contract administration functions to a CAO [@CL-CMP-0026], and item (3) of that list is conducting post-award orientation conferences [@CL-CMP-0091]. The RFO section numbered 42.401 says that in these circumstances the CFA may assign a corporate administrative contracting officer to deal with corporate management and to perform selected contract administration functions on a corporate-wide basis [@CL-CMP-0093], where codified FAR 42.401 covers correspondence relating to assigned contract administration functions [@CL-CMP-7796]. The RFO subpart numbered 42.5 prescribes policies and procedures for establishing billing rates and final indirect cost rates [@CL-CMP-0094], and RFO FAR 42.503-1(a) says a single agency is responsible for establishing final indirect cost rates for each business unit [@CL-CMP-0095], where codified FAR 42.503-1 says who is responsible for arranging a postaward conference [@CL-CMP-7799].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The contracting officer on a 3,000,000 supply contract decides that a postaward conference is needed. That officer sets the time and place, prepares a short agenda because the contract has unusual requirements, notifies the contract administration office and the contractor, and acts as chairperson after a preliminary meeting of Government personnel [@CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7800]. The conference has 6 Government attendees and 3 contractor attendees. The contracting office initiated it, so the arrangements may be made by that office or, at its request, by the contract administration office [@CL-CMP-7802]. As chairperson, the contracting officer prepares and signs a report that lists 4 open actions, each with the participant responsible and a due date [@CL-CMP-7803; @CL-CMP-7804]. Copies go to the contracting office, the contract administration office, the contractor and the finance office, one of the others who require the information [@CL-CMP-7805]. Separately, the contracting officer forwards a letter about an assigned administration function to the contractor through the contract administration office and provides a copy for that office's file [@CL-CMP-7796]. The answer: the contracting officer who decided the conference was needed is responsible for the arrangements [@CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7800], which the contracting office or, at its request, the contract administration office may make [@CL-CMP-7802], and the signed report goes to the contractor and the offices named [@CL-CMP-7803; @CL-CMP-7805]. Under the RFO text, conducting a postaward orientation conference is a function the contracting officer normally delegates to a CAO [@CL-CMP-0026; @CL-CMP-0091].

## Never confuse

The conference arrangements are the responsibility of the contracting officer who decides a conference is needed [@CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7800], and when the contracting office initiates it, they may be made by that office or, at its request, by the contract administration office [@CL-CMP-7802]. The chairperson shall prepare and sign the report and furnish copies of it [@CL-CMP-7803; @CL-CMP-7805].

Codified FAR 42.503-1 makes the contracting officer who decides that a conference is needed responsible for its arrangements [@CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7800], while RFO FAR 42.302(a)(3) lists conducting post-award orientation conferences among the functions the contracting officer normally delegates to a CAO [@CL-CMP-0026; @CL-CMP-0091]. The section number 42.401 is contract correspondence in the codified text [@CL-CMP-7796] but the assignment of a corporate administrative contracting officer in the RFO text [@CL-CMP-0093].
