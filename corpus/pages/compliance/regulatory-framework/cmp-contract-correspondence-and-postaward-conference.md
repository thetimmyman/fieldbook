## Purpose

Shows how correspondence on assigned contract administration functions moves between the contracting office, the contract administration office and the contractor, and who arranges and reports a postaward conference. The duties fall on Government personnel. The contractor is a recipient of the correspondence and of the report, and is notified of the conference.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7796] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.401, FAR 42.503-1 and FAR 42.503-3 [@CL-CMP-7796; @CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7803] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7796] |
| Applies to | Correspondence relating to assigned contract administration functions, and a postaward conference that the contracting officer decides is needed [@CL-CMP-7796; @CL-CMP-7799] |

The contracting officer, or other contracting agency personnel, normally shall forward correspondence relating to assigned contract administration functions to the contractor through the cognizant contract administration office, and shall provide a copy for that office's file [@CL-CMP-7796]. When urgency requires sending such correspondence directly to the contractor, a copy shall be sent concurrently to the contract administration office [@CL-CMP-7797]. The contract administration office shall send the contracting office a copy of pertinent correspondence conducted between that office and the contractor [@CL-CMP-7798]. The section does not say who sends the concurrent copy.

The contracting officer who decides that a conference is needed is responsible for establishing the time and place of the conference and for preparing the agenda when necessary [@CL-CMP-7799]. That officer is also responsible for notifying appropriate Government representatives and the contractor, designating or acting as the chairperson, and conducting a preliminary meeting of Government personnel [@CL-CMP-7800]. The same officer is responsible for preparing a summary report of the conference [@CL-CMP-7801]. When the contracting office initiates a conference, the arrangements may be made by that office or, at its request, by the contract administration office [@CL-CMP-7802].

## How it works

The chairperson shall prepare and sign a report of the postaward conference [@CL-CMP-7803]. The report shall cover all items discussed, including areas requiring resolution, controversial matters, the names of the participants assigned responsibility for further actions, and the due dates for the actions [@CL-CMP-7804]. The chairperson shall furnish copies of the report to the contracting office, the contract administration office, the contractor, and others who require the information [@CL-CMP-7805].

The text assigns a summary report to the contracting officer who decides a conference is needed [@CL-CMP-7801], and a report to the chairperson [@CL-CMP-7803]. It does not say whether these are one document. When the contracting officer acts as chairperson [@CL-CMP-7800], both duties fall on that officer. Who decides whether a postaward orientation is held, and how the chairperson runs the meeting, are on [Postaward orientation](../../operating-model/cadence/opm-postaward-orientation.md). The functions of the contract administration office are on [Contract administration functions](../audit-retention/cmp-contract-administration-functions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The contracting officer on a 3,000,000 supply contract decides that a postaward conference is needed. That officer sets the time and place, prepares a short agenda because the contract has unusual requirements, notifies the contract administration office and the contractor, and acts as chairperson after a preliminary meeting of Government personnel [@CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7800]. The conference has 6 Government attendees and 3 contractor attendees. The contracting office initiated it, so the arrangements may be made by that office or, at its request, by the contract administration office, which the section permits [@CL-CMP-7802]. As chairperson, the contracting officer prepares and signs a report that lists 4 open actions, each with the participant responsible and a due date [@CL-CMP-7803; @CL-CMP-7804]. Copies go to the contracting office, the contract administration office, the contractor and the finance office, one of the others who require the information [@CL-CMP-7805]. Separately, the contracting officer forwards a letter about an assigned administration function to the contractor through the contract administration office and provides a copy for that office's file [@CL-CMP-7796]. The answer: the contracting officer who decided the conference was needed is responsible for the arrangements, which the contracting office or, at its request, the contract administration office may make, and the signed report goes to the contractor and the offices named.

## Never confuse

The conference arrangements are the responsibility of the contracting officer who decides a conference is needed [@CL-CMP-7799; @CL-CMP-7800], and when the contracting office initiates it, they may be made by that office or, at its request, by the contract administration office [@CL-CMP-7802]. The report belongs to the chairperson, who prepares, signs and distributes it [@CL-CMP-7803; @CL-CMP-7805].
