## Purpose

Shows when a solicitation or contract must carry the clause protecting the Government's interest when subcontracting with contractors that are debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment or voluntarily excluded.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8237] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.409 [@CL-CMP-8237] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8237] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts where the contract value exceeds $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237] |

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.209-6, Protecting the Government's Interest when Subcontracting with Contractors Debarred, Suspended, Proposed for Debarment, or Voluntarily Excluded, in solicitations and contracts where the contract value exceeds $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall insert the clause in solicitations and contracts where the contract value exceeds $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237]. A contract valued at exactly $45,000 does not exceed $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237]. The contractor's own duties under the clause, and the subcontract threshold the clause uses, are covered in the [debarred, suspended or proposed-for-debarment subcontractors](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-debarred-suspended-subcontractors.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation is for a contract valued at 50,000 dollars, so the contract value exceeds $45,000 and the contracting officer shall insert the clause [@CL-CMP-8237]. A second contract is valued at 40,000 dollars, which does not exceed $45,000, so the stated condition for inserting the clause is not met [@CL-CMP-8237]. A third contract is valued at 60,000 dollars. The contract value exceeds $45,000, so the contracting officer shall insert the clause [@CL-CMP-8237].

## Never confuse

The clause protects the Government's interest rather than barring the subcontract: the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.209-6, about subcontracting with contractors debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment or voluntarily excluded, in solicitations and contracts where the contract value exceeds $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237].
