## Purpose

Shows when a solicitation or contract must carry the clause protecting the Government's interest when subcontracting with contractors that are debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment or voluntarily excluded.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8237] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.409 [@CL-CMP-8237] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8237] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts where the contract value exceeds $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237] |

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.209-6, Protecting the Government's Interest when Subcontracting with Contractors Debarred, Suspended, Proposed for Debarment, or Voluntarily Excluded, in solicitations and contracts where the contract value exceeds $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237].

## How it works

The duty to insert the clause falls on the contracting officer, not on the contractor [@CL-CMP-8237]. It reaches both solicitations and contracts, and the measure is the contract value, which must exceed $45,000 [@CL-CMP-8237]. A contract valued at exactly $45,000 does not exceed it [@CL-CMP-8237]. This section does not measure anything against the value of a subcontract. The contractor's own duties under the clause, and the subcontract threshold the clause uses, are on the [debarred, suspended or proposed-for-debarment subcontractors](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-debarred-suspended-subcontractors.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation is for a contract valued at 50,000 dollars, so the contract value exceeds $45,000 and the contracting officer shall insert the clause [@CL-CMP-8237]. A second contract is valued at 40,000 dollars, so this section does not require the clause in it [@CL-CMP-8237]. A third contract is valued at 60,000 dollars and the contractor plans a 20,000 dollar subcontract. The contracting officer shall insert the clause because the contract value exceeds $45,000, and the small subcontract does not change that [@CL-CMP-8237]. Whether the clause's own subcontract rule then applies to the 20,000 dollar subcontract is decided by the clause, not by this section.

## Never confuse

The clause is not the debarment or suspension decision. Those are separate actions under their own procedures; this section only says when the contracting officer shall insert a clause about subcontracting with contractors in one of those statuses [@CL-CMP-8237]. The contract value threshold in this section is also not the subcontract threshold that the clause applies to the contractor's own subcontracts.
