## Purpose

Shows when a contractor reports the compensation of its most highly paid executives, what it reports on first-tier subcontract awards, and which exceptions remove the duty.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3538] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-10 [@CL-CMP-3538; @CL-CMP-3543] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3538] |
| Applies to | Contractors under contracts that include the clause, if the listed conditions are met [@CL-CMP-3538] |

Within its annual registration requirement in SAM, a prime contractor shall report the names and total compensation of each of its five most highly compensated executives for its preceding completed fiscal year if the conditions listed in the paragraph are met [@CL-CMP-3538]. One listed condition is that the contractor received 80 percent or more of its annual gross revenues from Federal contracts and subcontracts, loans, grants and subgrants, cooperative agreements, and other forms of Federal financial assistance [@CL-CMP-3539]. Another is that it received $25,000,000 or more in annual gross revenues from the same kinds of source [@CL-CMP-3540]. A further listed condition is that the public does not have access to the executives' compensation through periodic reports under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or section 6104 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 [@CL-CMP-3541].

The paragraph on first-tier subcontract information opens with two exceptions, one where the Contracting Officer directs otherwise and one as provided in the exemption paragraph of the clause [@CL-CMP-3542]. Subject to them, the contractor shall report the listed information at the FSRS website by the end of the month following the month of award of a first-tier subcontract valued at or above the threshold specified in FAR 4.1403(a) on the date of award [@CL-CMP-3542; @CL-CMP-3543]. The clause does not print a figure for that threshold; the FAR section it points to prescribes the clause, except as provided in its second paragraph, for solicitations and contracts of $40,000 or more [@CL-CMP-3587]. The first four items to report are the unique entity identifier for the subcontractor and for its parent company if any, the subcontractor's name, the amount of the award, and the date of the award [@CL-CMP-3583]. The contractor shall not split or break down first-tier subcontract awards to a value below that threshold, on the date of subcontract award, to avoid the reporting requirements [@CL-CMP-3585].

A contractor whose gross income from all sources in the previous tax year was under $300,000 is exempt from the requirement to report subcontractor awards [@CL-CMP-3545]. If a subcontractor in the previous tax year had gross income from all sources under $300,000, the contractor does not need to report awards for that subcontractor [@CL-CMP-3586].

## How it works

The duty has three parts: the contractor's own executive compensation, first-tier subcontract awards, and the executive compensation of a first-tier subcontractor [@CL-CMP-3538; @CL-CMP-3543; @CL-CMP-3544]. The executive compensation of the prime contractor is reported as part of its annual registration requirement in SAM, which is on [SAM registration and maintenance clauses: keeping the record current](cmp-sam-registration-and-maintenance-clauses.md) [@CL-CMP-3538]. For a first-tier subcontractor, unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise, the report is due by the end of the month following the month of award of a first-tier subcontract valued at or above the threshold specified in FAR 4.1403(a) on the date of award, and annually thereafter, calculated from the prime contract award date [@CL-CMP-3584]. The contractor then shall report the names and total compensation of each of the five most highly compensated executives for that first-tier subcontractor, for the subcontractor's preceding completed fiscal year, at the FSRS website if the conditions listed in that paragraph are met [@CL-CMP-3544]. The clause lists further data items to report for each first-tier subcontract, and this card does not set them out. The clause also sets its definitions, the rule on continued reporting, and the correction duty for SAM data, which this card does not set out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has 40 million in annual gross revenues, 90 percent from Federal sources, and the public cannot see its executive pay in any public filing. It reports its five highest paid executives. In March it awards a first-tier subcontract above the threshold. It reports the award by the end of April unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise or an exemption applies.

## Never confuse

The revenue share condition and the revenue amount condition end with "and" in the clause text, so the clause does not make meeting one of them enough [@CL-CMP-3539; @CL-CMP-3540]. The exemption for low gross income is worded for the requirement to report subcontractor awards [@CL-CMP-3545]; the executive compensation duty turns on the separate conditions listed above [@CL-CMP-3538]. For how SAM registration works at offer and award, see [SAM registration and the unique entity identifier in awards](../../market/feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md).
