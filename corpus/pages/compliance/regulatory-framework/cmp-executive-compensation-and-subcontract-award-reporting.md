## Purpose

Shows when a contractor reports the compensation of its most highly paid executives, what it reports on first-tier subcontract awards, and which exceptions remove the duty.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3538] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-10 [@CL-CMP-3538; @CL-CMP-3543] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.204-10 [@CL-CMP-1159] |
| Edition date | JUN 2020 [@CL-CMP-10199] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3538] |
| Applies to | Contractors under contracts that include the clause, if the listed conditions are met [@CL-CMP-3538] |

As a part of its annual registration requirement in SAM [@CL-CMP-7766], a prime contractor shall report the names and total compensation of each of its five most highly compensated executives for its preceding completed fiscal year [@CL-CMP-3538], if in that fiscal year it received 80 percent or more of its annual gross revenues from Federal contracts and subcontracts, loans, grants and subgrants, cooperative agreements, and other forms of Federal financial assistance [@CL-CMP-3539], and it received $25,000,000 or more in annual gross revenues from the same kinds of source [@CL-CMP-3540], and the public does not have access to information about the executives' compensation through periodic reports filed under section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or section 6104 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 [@CL-CMP-3541].

The paragraph on first-tier subcontract information opens with two exceptions, one where the Contracting Officer directs otherwise and one as provided in the exemption paragraph of the clause [@CL-CMP-3542]. Subject to them, the contractor shall report the listed information at the FSRS website by the end of the month following the month of award of a first-tier subcontract valued at or above the threshold specified in FAR 4.1403(a) on the date of award [@CL-CMP-3542; @CL-CMP-3543]. FAR 4.1403(a) provides that, except as provided in paragraph (b) of that section, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.204-10 in all solicitations and contracts of $40,000 or more [@CL-CMP-3587]. The first four items to report are the unique entity identifier for the subcontractor and for its parent company if any, the subcontractor's name, the amount of the award, and the date of the award [@CL-CMP-3583]. The contractor shall not split or break down first-tier subcontract awards to a value below that threshold, on the date of subcontract award, to avoid the reporting requirements [@CL-CMP-3585].

A contractor whose gross income from all sources in the previous tax year was under $300,000 is exempt from the requirement to report subcontractor awards [@CL-CMP-3545]. If a subcontractor in the previous tax year had gross income from all sources under $300,000, the contractor does not need to report awards for that subcontractor [@CL-CMP-3586].

## How it works

The duty has three parts: the contractor's own executive compensation, first-tier subcontract awards, and the executive compensation of a first-tier subcontractor [@CL-CMP-3538; @CL-CMP-3543; @CL-CMP-3544]. The executive compensation of the prime contractor is reported as a part of its annual registration requirement in SAM [@CL-CMP-7766], which is on [SAM registration and maintenance clauses: keeping the record current](cmp-sam-registration-and-maintenance-clauses.md) [@CL-CMP-3538]. For a first-tier subcontractor, unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise, the report is due by the end of the month following the month of award of a first-tier subcontract valued at or above the threshold specified in FAR 4.1403(a) on the date of award, and annually thereafter, calculated from the prime contract award date [@CL-CMP-3584]. The contractor then shall report the names and total compensation of each of the five most highly compensated executives for that first-tier subcontractor, for the subcontractor's preceding completed fiscal year, at the FSRS website if the conditions listed in that paragraph are met [@CL-CMP-3544]. The clause lists further data items to report for each first-tier subcontract, and this card does not set them out. The clause also sets its definitions, the rule on continued reporting, and the correction duty for SAM data, which this card does not set out.

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.204-10 says the contractor reports, unless otherwise directed by the Contracting Officer or as provided in paragraph (f) of the clause, in the System for Award Management at https://www.sam.gov, where the codified paragraph names the FSRS website for subcontract information and points to paragraph (g) [@CL-CMP-1159; @CL-CMP-3542; @CL-CMP-3543; @CL-CMP-3544]. For the prime contractor's own executives it says the contractor shall report the names and total compensation of each of the five most highly compensated executives for its preceding completed fiscal year, without the codified opening words about the annual registration requirement in SAM [@CL-CMP-1160; @CL-CMP-3538]. It keeps the 80 percent revenue condition, the $25,000,000 revenue condition and the public access condition [@CL-CMP-1161; @CL-CMP-1162; @CL-CMP-1163; @CL-CMP-3539; @CL-CMP-3540; @CL-CMP-3541]. For first-tier subcontract information it says to report by the end of the month following the month of award of each first-tier subcontract award, and the lead-in of that paragraph prints no subcontract-value threshold, where the codified paragraph says a first-tier subcontract valued at or above the threshold specified in FAR 4.1403(a) [@CL-CMP-1164; @CL-CMP-3543]. It names the subcontractor's ultimate parent company where the codified text says parent company [@CL-CMP-1165; @CL-CMP-3583]. It makes the report on a first-tier subcontractor's executive compensation due by the end of the month following the month of award of a first-tier subcontract award and annually thereafter, calculated from the prime contract award date, in words that print no subcontract-value threshold [@CL-CMP-1166; @CL-CMP-3584]. For the first-tier subcontractor's preceding completed fiscal year, it keeps the 80 percent revenue condition, the $25,000,000 revenue condition and the public access condition [@CL-CMP-1192; @CL-CMP-1193; @CL-CMP-1194; @CL-CMP-1195; @CL-CMP-3544]. Its restriction says the contractor shall not split or break down subcontracts to a value below the threshold at Federal Acquisition Regulation 4.208(e) to avoid the reporting requirements in paragraph (c), where the codified text points to FAR 4.1403(a) and paragraph (d) [@CL-CMP-1167; @CL-CMP-3585]. It keeps the exemptions for a contractor, and for a subcontractor, whose gross income from all sources in the previous tax year was under $300,000 [@CL-CMP-1168; @CL-CMP-1169; @CL-CMP-3545; @CL-CMP-3586].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has 40 million in annual gross revenues, 90 percent from Federal sources, and the public cannot see its executive pay in any public filing. It reports its five highest paid executives. In March it awards a first-tier subcontract above the threshold. It reports the award by the end of April unless the Contracting Officer directs otherwise or an exemption applies. The codified clause ties this to a subcontract at or above the threshold [@CL-CMP-3543], while the RFO paragraph covers each first-tier subcontract award [@CL-CMP-1164].

## Never confuse

The prime contractor's executive compensation report is due if it received 80 percent or more of its annual gross revenues from the listed Federal sources [@CL-CMP-3539], and $25,000,000 or more in annual gross revenues from the same kinds of source [@CL-CMP-3540], and the public does not have access to the compensation information [@CL-CMP-3541]. The exemption for a contractor whose gross income from all sources in the previous tax year was under $300,000 is an exemption from the requirement to report subcontractor awards [@CL-CMP-3545]. For how SAM registration works at offer and award, see [SAM registration and the unique entity identifier in awards](../../market/feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md).

The codified text has the contractor report first-tier subcontract information at the FSRS website for a subcontract valued at or above the threshold specified in FAR 4.1403(a) [@CL-CMP-3543], but the RFO text has the contractor report in the System for Award Management and asks for each first-tier subcontract award [@CL-CMP-1159; @CL-CMP-1164].
