## Purpose

Shows what past performance information is, what it covers, and when the Payments to Small Business Subcontractors clause is inserted.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2596] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1501, FAR 42.1504 and FAR 52.242-5 [@CL-CMP-2596; @CL-CMP-2599; @CL-CMP-2668] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.1101 [@CL-CMP-6878; @CL-CMP-6879; @CL-CMP-6880]; RFO FAR 42.1104 [@CL-CMP-6881]; RFO FAR 52.242-5 [@CL-CMP-6882; @CL-CMP-6883; @CL-CMP-6884; @CL-CMP-6885] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2596] |
| Applies to | Past performance information regarding a contractor's actions under previously awarded contracts or orders [@CL-CMP-2596]. The payments clause, in solicitations and contracts containing the Small Business Subcontracting Plan clause [@CL-CMP-2599] |

Past performance information, including the ratings and supporting narratives, is relevant information, for future source selection purposes, regarding a contractor's actions under previously awarded contracts or orders [@CL-CMP-2596]. It includes, for example, the contractor's record of conforming to requirements and to standards of good workmanship, forecasting and controlling costs, and adherence to schedules, including the administrative aspects of performance [@CL-CMP-2597]. Agencies shall monitor their compliance with the past performance evaluation requirements and use the Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System metric tools to measure the quality and timely reporting of past performance information [@CL-CMP-2598].

The Payments to Small Business Subcontractors clause is inserted in all solicitations and contracts containing the Small Business Subcontracting Plan clause [@CL-CMP-2599]. Under the payments clause, the contractor shall notify the contracting officer in writing not later than 14 days after a small business subcontractor was entitled to payment under the subcontract and the contractor made a reduced or untimely payment [@CL-CMP-2668]. The clause defines an untimely payment as a payment more than 90 days past due under the terms and conditions of a subcontract, for supplies and services for which the Government has paid the prime contractor [@CL-CMP-2669]. The contractor shall include the reasons for making the reduced or untimely payment in any notice required under the clause [@CL-CMP-2670].

## How it works

Past performance information records what a contractor did under earlier contracts or orders, and source selection later uses it [@CL-CMP-2596]. The list of examples includes the contractor's record of conforming to requirements, forecasting and controlling costs, and adherence to schedules [@CL-CMP-2597]. Agencies measure how well and how quickly they report this information using the reporting system tools [@CL-CMP-2598]. The payments clause goes into all solicitations and contracts that contain the subcontracting plan clause, and it requires the contractor to notify the contracting officer in writing not later than 14 days after a small business subcontractor was entitled to payment and the contractor made a reduced or untimely payment [@CL-CMP-2599; @CL-CMP-2668]. Past performance evaluations are covered in [Past performance evaluations](../../operating-model/cadence/opm-past-performance-evaluations.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761], and use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 42.1101(a) says past performance information, including the ratings and supporting narratives, is relevant information, for future purposes, regarding a contractor's actions under previously awarded contracts or orders [@CL-CMP-6878], where codified FAR 42.1501(a) says for future source selection purposes [@CL-CMP-2596]. RFO FAR 42.1101(a)(1)-(3) keeps the examples of the contractor's record of conforming to requirements and to standards of good workmanship, forecasting and controlling costs, and adherence to schedules, including the administrative aspects of performance [@CL-CMP-6879; @CL-CMP-2597]. RFO FAR 42.1101(b) says agencies must monitor their compliance with the past performance evaluation requirements (see 42.1102) and use the Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System metric tools to measure the quality and timely reporting of past performance information [@CL-CMP-6880], where the codified paragraph refers to FAR 42.1502 [@CL-CMP-2598]. RFO FAR 42.1104 keeps the rule that the Payments to Small Business Subcontractors clause is inserted in all solicitations and contracts containing the Small Business Subcontracting Plan clause [@CL-CMP-6881; @CL-CMP-2599]. RFO FAR 52.242-5(b) keeps the duty to notify the contracting officer in writing not later than 14 days after a small business subcontractor was entitled to payment under the subcontract and the contractor engaged in the listed conduct [@CL-CMP-6882; @CL-CMP-2668]; the listed conduct is that the contractor made a reduced or untimely payment to the small business subcontractor, or failed to make a payment, which is now untimely [@CL-CMP-6883]. The RFO clause keeps the definition of an untimely payment as a payment more than 90 days past due under the terms and conditions of a subcontract, for supplies and services for which the Government has paid the prime contractor [@CL-CMP-6884; @CL-CMP-2669], and keeps the duty to include the reasons for making the reduced or untimely payment in any notice required under the notice paragraph [@CL-CMP-6885; @CL-CMP-2670].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract worth 3,000,000 contains the small business subcontracting plan clause, so the payments clause is inserted in it [@CL-CMP-2599]. Years later, the contractor's record shows that it met schedules but overran its cost forecast by 10 percent [@CL-CMP-2597]. A source selection team reads that record when it considers the contractor for a future award [@CL-CMP-2596].

## Never confuse

Past performance evaluations are covered in [Past performance evaluations](../../operating-model/cadence/opm-past-performance-evaluations.md). The clause prescription says when the payments clause is inserted [@CL-CMP-2599], and the clause sets a notice from the contractor to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-2668]. Subcontractor nonpayment assertions and information requests are covered in [Subcontractor nonpayment assertions and information requests](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-subcontractor-nonpayment-and-information-requests.md).

The codified section describes past performance information as relevant for future source selection purposes [@CL-CMP-2596], while RFO FAR 42.1101(a) says it is relevant information for future purposes [@CL-CMP-6878].
