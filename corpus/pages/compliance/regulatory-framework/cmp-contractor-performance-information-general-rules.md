## Purpose

Shows what past performance information is, what it covers, and when the Payments to Small Business Subcontractors clause is inserted.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2596] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1501, FAR 42.1504 and FAR 52.242-5 [@CL-CMP-2596; @CL-CMP-2599; @CL-CMP-2668] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2596] |
| Applies to | Past performance information regarding a contractor's actions under previously awarded contracts or orders [@CL-CMP-2596]. The payments clause, in solicitations and contracts containing the Small Business Subcontracting Plan clause [@CL-CMP-2599] |

Past performance information, including the ratings and supporting narratives, is relevant information, for future source selection purposes, regarding a contractor's actions under previously awarded contracts or orders [@CL-CMP-2596]. It includes, for example, the contractor's record of conforming to requirements and to standards of good workmanship, forecasting and controlling costs, and adherence to schedules, including the administrative aspects of performance [@CL-CMP-2597]. Agencies shall monitor their compliance with the past performance evaluation requirements and use the Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System metric tools to measure the quality and timely reporting of past performance information [@CL-CMP-2598].

The Payments to Small Business Subcontractors clause is inserted in all solicitations and contracts containing the Small Business Subcontracting Plan clause [@CL-CMP-2599]. Under the payments clause, the contractor shall notify the contracting officer in writing not later than 14 days after a small business subcontractor was entitled to payment under the subcontract and the contractor made a reduced or untimely payment, among other listed circumstances [@CL-CMP-2668]. The clause defines an untimely payment as a payment more than 90 days past due under the terms and conditions of a subcontract, for supplies and services for which the Government has paid the prime contractor [@CL-CMP-2669]. The contractor shall include the reasons for making the reduced or untimely payment in any notice required under the clause [@CL-CMP-2670].

## How it works

Past performance information records what a contractor did under earlier contracts or orders, and source selection later uses it [@CL-CMP-2596]. The printed list of examples is illustrative and covers conformance, cost control and schedule adherence, among other items [@CL-CMP-2597]. Agencies measure how well and how quickly they report this information using the reporting system tools [@CL-CMP-2598]. The payments clause goes into all solicitations and contracts that contain the subcontracting plan clause, and it requires the contractor to notify the contracting officer of the listed reduced or untimely payments [@CL-CMP-2599; @CL-CMP-2668]. When evaluations are prepared and how contractors respond are on [Past performance evaluations](../../operating-model/cadence/opm-past-performance-evaluations.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract worth 3,000,000 contains the small business subcontracting plan clause, so the contracting officer inserts the payments clause at award. Years later, the contractor's record shows that it met schedules but overran its cost forecast by 10 percent. A source selection team reads that record when it considers the contractor for a future award.

## Never confuse

The general rules on what past performance information is differ from the evaluation duties, which are on [Past performance evaluations](../../operating-model/cadence/opm-past-performance-evaluations.md). The clause prescription says when the payments clause is inserted [@CL-CMP-2599], and the clause sets a notice from the contractor to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-2668]. The clause text does not say how a late payment to a subcontractor is assessed in an evaluation. What a subcontractor may do when it says it was not paid is on [Subcontractor nonpayment assertions and information requests](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-subcontractor-nonpayment-and-information-requests.md).
