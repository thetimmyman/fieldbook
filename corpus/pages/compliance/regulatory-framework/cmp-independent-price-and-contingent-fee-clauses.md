## Purpose

Shows what an offeror certifies about how its prices were reached, what a contractor warrants about contingent fees, and what the Government may do for breach of the warranty.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2600; @CL-CMP-2605] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-2 and FAR 52.203-5 [@CL-CMP-2600; @CL-CMP-2605] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2600; @CL-CMP-2605] |
| Applies to | Solicitations where a firm-fixed-price contract or fixed-price contract with economic price adjustment is contemplated, subject to listed exceptions, and solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold other than those for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-2679; @CL-CMP-2680; @CL-CMP-2681] |

The independent price provision is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.103-1 [@CL-CMP-2600]. The contracting officer inserts it in solicitations when a firm-fixed-price contract or fixed-price contract with economic price adjustment is contemplated, subject to the exceptions that the section lists [@CL-CMP-2679]. It is not inserted if the acquisition is under the simplified acquisition procedures, if the solicitation is a request for technical proposals under two-step sealed bidding procedures, or if the solicitation is for utility services for which rates are set by law or regulation [@CL-CMP-2680]. Paragraph (a) of the provision lists matters that the offeror certifies [@CL-CMP-2601]. One is that the prices were arrived at independently, without, for the purpose of restricting competition, any consultation, communication, or agreement with any other offeror or competitor relating to those prices, the intention to submit an offer, or the methods or factors used to calculate the prices [@CL-CMP-2602]. Another is that the prices have not been and will not be knowingly disclosed by the offeror, directly or indirectly, to any other offeror or competitor before bid opening for a sealed bid, or before contract award for a negotiated solicitation, unless otherwise required by law [@CL-CMP-2603]. A third is that no attempt has been or will be made by the offeror to induce any other concern to submit or not to submit an offer for the purpose of restricting competition [@CL-CMP-2604].

The covenant clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.404 [@CL-CMP-2605]. The contracting officer inserts it in all solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, other than those for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-2681]. In it, the Contractor warrants that no person or agency has been employed or retained to solicit or obtain the contract upon an agreement or understanding for a contingent fee, except a bona fide employee or agency [@CL-CMP-2606]. For breach or violation of that warranty, the Government shall have the right to annul the contract without liability, or to deduct from the contract price or consideration, or otherwise recover, the full amount of the contingent fee [@CL-CMP-2607].

## How it works

The independent price provision is a set of certifications made with the offer. The section prints no remedy for a false certification, so what follows a breach of it is not established here. The provision also has paragraphs on the effect of a signature and on deleting or modifying one certification, and this card does not set them out. The covenant clause is different, because it prints its own remedy for breach of the warranty [@CL-CMP-2607]. The amount the Government may deduct or recover is the full amount of the contingent fee [@CL-CMP-2607]. The clause also defines bona fide employee, bona fide agency and improper influence, and this card does not set those definitions out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor agrees to pay an intermediary a success fee of 80,000 if it wins a contract worth 4 million. The contract is not for commercial products or commercial services. The intermediary is neither a bona fide employee nor a bona fide agency. After award, the Government may annul the contract without liability, or deduct or recover the full 80,000. In a separate firm-fixed-price sealed bid, an offeror tells a competitor its bid price before bid opening. That offeror cannot truthfully certify that its prices were not disclosed.

## Never confuse

The price certification concerns how prices were reached and who saw them. The covenant concerns payment for obtaining the contract, and a contingent fee arrangement with a bona fide employee or agency is excepted from the warranty [@CL-CMP-2606]. Annulment for breach of the covenant is not the rescission that the cancellation clause allows, which is on [Cancellation, rescission and price or fee adjustment for illegal or improper activity](cmp-improper-activity-cancellation-and-price-adjustment.md). The definitions, the insertion rule and who decides on a suspected breach are on [Covenant against contingent fees](cmp-contingent-fees.md).
