## Purpose

Explains what the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification subpart covers, when the contract clause and the notice provision are used, and who makes the determination that triggers the clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7940] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 204.7500 [@CL-CMP-7940; @CL-CMP-8430; @CL-CMP-8431; @CL-CMP-7941] and DFARS 204.7504 [@CL-CMP-7942; @CL-CMP-7948] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7940] |
| Applies to | Unclassified contractor information systems [@CL-CMP-7941], and solicitations and contracts for which the program office or requiring activity makes the determination described below [@CL-CMP-7945; @CL-CMP-7947] |

The subpart prescribes policies and procedures for including the certification level requirements in DoD contracts [@CL-CMP-7940]. CMMC is a framework, with a reference to 32 CFR part 170, for assessing a contractor's information security protections [@CL-CMP-8430]. The subpart applies to unclassified contractor information systems [@CL-CMP-7941].

The subpart does not abrogate any other requirements regarding contractor physical, personnel, information, technical or general administrative security operations governing the protection of unclassified information, and it does not affect requirements of the National Industrial Security Program [@CL-CMP-8431].

Unless the requirements at 32 CFR 170.5(d) are met, the prescription directs use of the clause at 252.204-7021 [@CL-CMP-7942]. It then splits into two periods.

Until November 9, 2028, the prescription covers solicitations, contracts, task orders and delivery orders, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CMP-7943]. Those solely for the acquisition of commercially available off-the-shelf (COTS) items are excepted [@CL-CMP-7944]. The clause is used if the program office or requiring activity determines that the contractor is required to have a specific certification level [@CL-CMP-7945].

On or after November 10, 2028, the prescription covers the same kinds of solicitations, contracts, task orders and delivery orders [@CL-CMP-7946]. Those solely for the acquisition of COTS items are again excepted [@CL-CMP-8432]. The clause is then used if the program office or requiring activity determines that the contractor is required to use contractor information systems in performance of the contract, task order or delivery order to process, store or transmit FCI or CUI [@CL-CMP-7947].

The provision at 252.204-7025 is used in solicitations that include the clause at 252.204-7021 [@CL-CMP-7948].

## How it works

The program office or requiring activity makes the determination in both periods [@CL-CMP-7945; @CL-CMP-7947].

The trigger changes on the later date. Before it, the question is whether the contractor must hold a specific certification level; from it, the question is whether the contractor must use its information systems to process, store or transmit FCI or CUI [@CL-CMP-7945; @CL-CMP-7947]. The first period runs until November 9, 2028 and the second applies on or after November 10, 2028 [@CL-CMP-7943; @CL-CMP-7946].

The exception is for solicitations, contracts, task orders or delivery orders solely for the acquisition of COTS items [@CL-CMP-7944; @CL-CMP-8432].

The provision follows the clause: it is used in solicitations that include the clause [@CL-CMP-7948].

These sections say when to use the clause at 252.204-7021 and the provision at 252.204-7025 [@CL-CMP-7942; @CL-CMP-7948]. The clause is covered in [Contractor compliance with CMMC level requirements](../trade-cyber/cmp-cmmc-level-compliance-clause.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume the requirements at 32 CFR 170.5(d) are not met. A solicitation for a task order for a commercial service is issued in 2027. The program office determines that the contractor must hold a specific certification level, and the order is not solely for COTS items, so the clause at 252.204-7021 is used [@CL-CMP-7942; @CL-CMP-7943; @CL-CMP-7944; @CL-CMP-7945]. The solicitation includes the clause, so the provision at 252.204-7025 is used in it too [@CL-CMP-7948].

A second solicitation in 2027 is solely for COTS items, so it is excepted from the prescription [@CL-CMP-7944].

Assume the requirements at 32 CFR 170.5(d) are not met for a third solicitation, issued in December 2028 for a commercial service that is not solely for COTS items. The clause is used if the program office determines that the contractor is required to use contractor information systems in the performance of the contract, task order, or delivery order to process, store, or transmit FCI or CUI [@CL-CMP-7946; @CL-CMP-8432; @CL-CMP-7947].

## Never confuse

The certification subpart prescribes policies and procedures for including the CMMC level requirements in DoD contracts [@CL-CMP-7940]. The safeguarding subpart applies to contracts and subcontracts requiring contractors and subcontractors to safeguard covered defense information that resides in or transits through covered contractor information systems by applying specified network security requirements [@CL-CMP-7960], and it also requires reporting of cyber incidents [@CL-CMP-7961].

Until November 9, 2028 the trigger is a determination that the contractor is required to have a specific CMMC level [@CL-CMP-7945]; on or after November 10, 2028 it is a determination that the contractor is required to use contractor information systems in the performance of the contract, task order, or delivery order to process, store, or transmit FCI or CUI [@CL-CMP-7947].

See [Safeguarding covered defense information](../trade-cyber/cmp-safeguarding-covered-defense-information.md) and [Contractor compliance with CMMC level requirements](../trade-cyber/cmp-cmmc-level-compliance-clause.md).
