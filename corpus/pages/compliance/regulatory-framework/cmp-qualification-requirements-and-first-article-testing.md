## Purpose

Explains the two qualification lists and the factors the contracting officer must consider before requiring first article testing and approval.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7988] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.201 [@CL-CMP-7986; @CL-CMP-7987; @CL-CMP-8450] and FAR 9.302 [@CL-CMP-7988; @CL-CMP-7989] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7988] |
| Applies to | The definitions of the two lists, and acquisitions where the contracting officer is considering first article testing and approval [@CL-CMP-7986; @CL-CMP-7987; @CL-CMP-7989] |

The two list definitions apply as the terms are used in the subpart that holds section 9.201 [@CL-CMP-8450]. A qualified bidders list is a list of bidders who have had their products examined and tested and who have satisfied all applicable qualification requirements for that product, or have otherwise satisfied all applicable qualification requirements [@CL-CMP-7986]. A qualified manufacturers list is a list of manufacturers who have had their products examined and tested and who have satisfied all applicable qualification requirements for that product [@CL-CMP-7987].

First article testing and approval ensures that the contractor can furnish a product that conforms to all contract requirements for acceptance [@CL-CMP-7988]. Before requiring testing and approval, the contracting officer shall consider the impact on cost or time of delivery, the risk to the Government of foregoing such test, and the availability of other, less costly, methods of ensuring the desired quality [@CL-CMP-7989].

## How it works

The duty to consider falls on the contracting officer and comes before the requirement is imposed [@CL-CMP-7989]. The text lists three factors, all of which the contracting officer considers; it sets no weighting, no threshold and no deadline, and it does not say what result any factor produces [@CL-CMP-7989].

The factors trade cost and delivery time against the Government's risk in forgoing the test, with cheaper methods of ensuring quality weighed alongside [@CL-CMP-7989].

The two lists differ in who is listed. A qualified bidders list names bidders, and a bidder can be listed either by having had its products examined and tested and satisfying the applicable requirements for that product, or by otherwise satisfying all applicable qualification requirements [@CL-CMP-7986]. A qualified manufacturers list names manufacturers whose products were examined and tested and who satisfied the applicable requirements for that product [@CL-CMP-7987].

These sections define the lists and set the factors to consider. When first article testing may be appropriate, and how qualification requirements are established, are covered in the linked cards.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer is considering requiring first article testing and approval for a machined housing. Testing would add six weeks to delivery and a fixed cost, and skipping it risks a failed acceptance test later. A less costly alternative is incoming inspection of the first lot. Before requiring the test, the contracting officer considers the cost and delivery impact, the risk to the Government of foregoing the test, and the availability of that cheaper method, before any requirement is imposed [@CL-CMP-7989].

If the contracting officer requires the test, its purpose is to ensure that the contractor can furnish a product that conforms to all contract requirements for acceptance [@CL-CMP-7988].

A manufacturer is on a qualified manufacturers list because its products were examined and tested and it satisfied the applicable requirements for that product. A bidder that has not had its products examined and tested can still be on a qualified bidders list if it has otherwise satisfied all applicable qualification requirements [@CL-CMP-7986; @CL-CMP-7987].

## Never confuse

Qualification is not first article approval [@CL-CMP-7986; @CL-CMP-7988]. A list records bidders or manufacturers that satisfied the qualification requirements, while first article testing and approval ensures that a contractor can furnish a conforming product for acceptance under the contract [@CL-CMP-7986; @CL-CMP-7987; @CL-CMP-7988].

A qualified bidders list and a qualified manufacturers list are not the same: only the bidders list allows a bidder to be listed without having had its products examined and tested, provided it otherwise satisfied all applicable qualification requirements [@CL-CMP-7986; @CL-CMP-7987].
