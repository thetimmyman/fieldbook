## Purpose

Shows what the contractor performance information subpart covers and excludes, and the deadline and notice the contracting officer must meet when a small business concern sends a written request about a contract administration matter.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7823] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1500 and FAR 42.1601 [@CL-CMP-7823; @CL-CMP-7826] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.1100 [@CL-CMP-0128]; RFO FAR 42.1201 [@CL-CMP-0129; @CL-CMP-0130; @CL-CMP-0131] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7823] |
| Applies to | Recording and maintaining contractor performance information [@CL-CMP-7823]; written requests to the contracting officer from small business concerns about a contract administration matter [@CL-CMP-7826] |

The subpart provides policies and establishes responsibilities for recording and maintaining contractor performance information [@CL-CMP-7823]. It does not apply to procedures used by agencies in determining fees under award or incentive fee contracts [@CL-CMP-7824]. However, the fee amount paid to contractors should be reflective of the contractor's performance, and the past performance evaluation should closely parallel and be consistent with the fee determinations [@CL-CMP-7825].

The contracting officer shall make every reasonable effort to respond in writing within 30 days to any written request to the contracting officer from a small business concern with respect to a contract administration matter [@CL-CMP-7826]. If the contracting officer cannot respond within the 30-day period, the contracting officer shall, within the period, transmit to the contractor a written notification of the specific date the contracting officer expects to respond [@CL-CMP-7827]. The 30-day response rule does not apply to a request for a contracting officer decision under chapter 71 of title 41 of the United States Code, Contract Disputes [@CL-CMP-7826; @CL-CMP-7828].

## How it works

The duty to respond or to give notice falls on the contracting officer, and the request must be written and come to the contracting officer from a small business concern about a contract administration matter [@CL-CMP-7826]. The standard for the response is every reasonable effort, and the duty to send the notice is stated as a "shall" [@CL-CMP-7826; @CL-CMP-7827]. A request for a contracting officer decision under the Contract Disputes chapter is outside this rule [@CL-CMP-7828]. The disputes clause is on [Disputes clause](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-disputes-clause.md).

The fee sentence in the subpart's scope is an expectation, as the word "should" shows, and it is not an exclusion of the subpart [@CL-CMP-7825]. The general rules on past performance information are on [Contractor performance information: general rules and the small business payments clause](cmp-contractor-performance-information-general-rules.md), and the evaluation cadence is on [Past performance evaluations](../../operating-model/cadence/opm-past-performance-evaluations.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.1100, Scope of subpart, consists of the sentence that the subpart provides policies and establishes responsibilities for recording and maintaining contractor performance information [@CL-CMP-0128], where codified FAR 42.1500 has that sentence and goes on to exclude procedures used by agencies in determining fees under award or incentive fee contracts and to say the fee amount should be reflective of the contractor's performance [@CL-CMP-7823; @CL-CMP-7824; @CL-CMP-7825]. RFO FAR 42.1201 keeps the rule that the contracting officer must make every reasonable effort to respond in writing within 30 days to any written request from a small business concern regarding a contract administration matter [@CL-CMP-0129; @CL-CMP-7826]. It keeps the rule that, if the contracting officer cannot respond within the 30-day period, the contracting officer must, within the period, communicate to the contractor a written notification of the specific date the contracting officer expects to respond [@CL-CMP-0130; @CL-CMP-7827]. It keeps the exception that the provision does not apply to a request for a contracting officer decision under chapter 71 of title 41 of the United States Code, Contract Disputes [@CL-CMP-0131; @CL-CMP-7828].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

On day 0 a small business concern sends the contracting officer a written request about the delivery schedule of its contract. The officer answers in writing on day 20, inside the 30-day period, so no separate notice is needed [@CL-CMP-7826].

On another contract, a small business concern sends a written request on day 0 and the officer cannot answer within 30 days. By day 30 the officer must send the contractor a written notice that names the specific date the officer expects to respond, for example day 45 [@CL-CMP-7827]. If the same concern sends a request for a contracting officer decision under the Contract Disputes chapter, the 30-day rule does not apply to it [@CL-CMP-7828]. The answer: respond in writing within 30 days if possible, and otherwise send within the period a written notice of the expected response date.

## Never confuse

The scope exclusion for fee-determination procedures is not an exclusion of the whole subpart, because the fee sentence says the past performance evaluation should closely parallel the fee determination [@CL-CMP-7824; @CL-CMP-7825]. These two sections also do not cover novation and change-of-name agreements, which are on [Novation and change-of-name agreements](../../mna/diligence/mna-novation-agreements.md).

The scope section is codified FAR 42.1500 and the small business response rule is codified FAR 42.1601 [@CL-CMP-7823; @CL-CMP-7826], and the RFO text numbers them FAR 42.1100 and FAR 42.1201 [@CL-CMP-0128; @CL-CMP-0129]. Codified FAR 42.1500 excludes fee-determination procedures from the subpart [@CL-CMP-7824], while RFO FAR 42.1100 consists of the scope sentence on recording and maintaining contractor performance information [@CL-CMP-0128].
