## Purpose

Shows which representation provisions belong in a solicitation and which entity identifiers an offeror has to supply before award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7221] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.607, 4.1802 and 4.2105 [@CL-CMP-7221; @CL-CMP-7223; @CL-CMP-7225] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7221] |
| Applies to | Solicitations, and contract actions above the micro-purchase threshold [@CL-CMP-7221; @CL-CMP-7223] |

The women-owned business representation provision at FAR 52.204-5 goes in all solicitations that are not set aside for small business concerns, exceed the simplified acquisition threshold and are for contracts that will be performed in the United States or its outlying areas [@CL-CMP-7221]. All three conditions must be met for the provision to go in [@CL-CMP-7221].

The unique entity identifier provision at FAR 52.204-6 goes in solicitations that do not contain the System for Award Management provision at FAR 52.204-7, or that meet a condition in 4.605(c)(2) [@CL-CMP-7222]. The clause at FAR 52.204-12, Unique Entity Identifier Maintenance, goes in solicitations and resulting contracts that contain the provision at FAR 52.204-6 [@CL-CMP-7319].

## How it works

An offeror must give the contracting officer the CAGE code assigned to its location before award of a contract action above the micro-purchase threshold when there is a requirement to be registered in the System for Award Management [@CL-CMP-7223]. The same duty applies when there is a requirement to have a unique entity identifier in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7329]. The contracting officer must include the contractor's CAGE code in the contract and in any electronic transmissions of the contract data to other systems when it is provided under that requirement [@CL-CMP-7320].

An offeror that is owned or controlled by another entity must give the contracting officer that entity's CAGE code and legal name before award of a contract action above the micro-purchase threshold when there is a requirement to be registered in SAM [@CL-CMP-7224]. The same applies when there is a requirement to have a unique entity identifier in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7330].

The contracting officer must insert the provision at FAR 52.204-24, the representation regarding certain telecommunications and video surveillance services or equipment, in all solicitations for contracts [@CL-CMP-7225]. Under indefinite delivery contracts, that provision goes in all notices of intent to place an order, or solicitations for an order [@CL-CMP-7321]. The contracting officer must insert the clause at FAR 52.204-25, the prohibition on contracting for certain telecommunications and video surveillance services or equipment, in all solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-7226]. The contracting officer must insert the provision at FAR 52.204-26, the covered telecommunications equipment or services representation, in all solicitations [@CL-CMP-7322].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a contract to be performed in the United States exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and is not set aside for small business concerns. It meets all three conditions, so the women-owned business representation provision goes in it [@CL-CMP-7221]. A second solicitation for a similar contract is set aside for small business concerns, so that provision is not required under this section [@CL-CMP-7221].

In a separate case, an offeror is to receive a contract action of 300,000 for commercial services, and the solicitation requires SAM registration. The offeror gives the contracting officer the CAGE code for its location before award, and because a parent controls it, also gives the parent's CAGE code and legal name [@CL-CMP-7223; @CL-CMP-7224]. The contracting officer includes the offeror's CAGE code in the contract [@CL-CMP-7320].

The first two solicitations both contain the provisions at FAR 52.204-24 and 52.204-26, and the clause at FAR 52.204-25 [@CL-CMP-7225; @CL-CMP-7322; @CL-CMP-7226].

## Never confuse

A provision placed in a solicitation is not the offeror's own duty to give a CAGE code. The women-owned business and telecommunications representation provisions go into solicitations [@CL-CMP-7221; @CL-CMP-7225], while the CAGE code duty rests on the offeror before award above the micro-purchase threshold when SAM registration or a unique entity identifier is required [@CL-CMP-7223; @CL-CMP-7329].
