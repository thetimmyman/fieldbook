## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must disclose when its response includes royalty costs over the stated amount, and what the contracting officer may ask for before award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3651] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.227-6 [@CL-CMP-3651] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.227-6 [@CL-CMP-2530; @CL-CMP-2531; @CL-CMP-2532; @CL-CMP-2533; @CL-CMP-2534; @CL-CMP-9777] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3651] |
| Applies to | Solicitation responses with royalty costs or charges totaling more than $250 [@CL-CMP-3651] |

When the response to the solicitation contains costs or charges for royalties totaling more than $250, the listed information shall be included in the response relating to each separate item of royalty or license fee [@CL-CMP-3651]. One listed group of items is the name and address of the licensor, the date of the license agreement, the patent numbers, patent application serial numbers, or other basis on which the royalty is payable, and a brief description including any part or model numbers of each contract item or component on which the royalty is payable [@CL-CMP-3651; @CL-CMP-3652].

If specifically requested by the Contracting Officer before execution of the contract, the offeror shall furnish a copy of the current license agreement and an identification of applicable claims of specific patents [@CL-CMP-3653]. The other listed items are the percentage or dollar rate of royalty per unit, the unit price of the contract item, the number of units, and the total dollar amount of royalties [@CL-CMP-3651; @CL-CMP-3676]. Alternate I substitutes, for the introductory portion of paragraph (a) of the basic provision, a version that applies when the response covers charges for special construction or special assembly that contain costs or charges for royalties totaling more than $250 [@CL-CMP-4231]. This card does not restate the rest of that alternate.

## How it works

The provision is triggered by the total of royalty costs or charges in the response, not by any single royalty [@CL-CMP-3651]. Once triggered, the response carries the listed information for each separate royalty or license fee item [@CL-CMP-3651; @CL-CMP-3652; @CL-CMP-3676]. The copy of the license is a separate request that the Contracting Officer makes before the contract is executed [@CL-CMP-3653].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO paragraph (a) keeps the rule that, when the response to the solicitation contains costs or charges for royalties totaling more than $250, the listed information shall be included in the response relating to each separate item of royalty or license fee [@CL-CMP-2530; @CL-CMP-3651]. It keeps the first listed group of items, which are the name and address of the licensor, the date of the license agreement, the patent numbers, patent application serial numbers, or other basis on which the royalty is payable, and a brief description including any part or model numbers of each contract item or component on which the royalty is payable [@CL-CMP-2531; @CL-CMP-3652], and the other listed items, which are the percentage or dollar rate of royalty per unit, the unit price of the contract item, the number of units, and the total dollar amount of royalties [@CL-CMP-2533; @CL-CMP-3676]. RFO paragraph (b) keeps the rule that, if specifically requested by the Contracting Officer before execution of the contract, the offeror shall furnish a copy of the current license agreement and an identification of applicable claims of specific patents [@CL-CMP-2532; @CL-CMP-3653]. RFO Alternate I keeps the substitution, for the introductory portion of paragraph (a) of the basic provision, of a version that applies when the response covers charges for special construction or special assembly that contain costs or charges for royalties totaling more than $250 [@CL-CMP-2534; @CL-CMP-4231], and the substituted introductory text goes on to say that the following information shall be included in the response relating to each separate item of royalty or license fee [@CL-CMP-9777].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A response includes royalty charges of 180 on one item and 90 on another, 270 in total. The total is more than 250, so the response must give the listed information for each of the two items. If the total had been 240, the provision would not have required it. Before the contract is executed, the Contracting Officer asks for a copy of one license, and the offeror furnishes it.

## Never confuse

Royalty information is a disclosure made with the offer. It does not decide whether the royalty cost is allowable; see [Royalties and other costs for use of patents](../cas-allowability/cmp-patent-royalties.md) and, for other patent costs, [Patent costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-patent-costs.md). It is also not the refund of royalties clause, covered on the [patent indemnity and refund of royalties card](cmp-patent-indemnity-and-refund-of-royalties.md), which works from the statement of royalties furnished before final payment [@CL-CMP-3619].

The Government licensee notice, the major system minimum rights clause and the proposal data clause are covered in [Government licensee notice, major system minimum rights and proposal data](cmp-patent-licensee-notice-and-data-rights.md).
