## Purpose

States the safety duties the accident prevention clause imposes, the standards it points to, the notice of noncompliance and stop-work right that enforce it, the written plan and meeting that Alternate I adds, and the flowdown to subcontracts.

## The rule

| Codified citation | FAR 52.236-13 [@CL-CMP-4888] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.236-13 [@CL-CMP-0646] |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause. Alternate I applies where the contract involves work of a long duration or hazardous nature, or performance on a Government facility with the hazards that the alternate describes [@CL-CMP-4888; @CL-CMP-5069; @CL-CMP-5070] |

### Safety duties of the basic clause

The contractor must provide and maintain work environments and procedures that safeguard the public and Government personnel, property, materials, supplies and equipment exposed to its operations and activities [@CL-CMP-4888]. Those work environments and procedures must also avoid interruptions of Government operations and delays in project completion dates, and control costs in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4889].

On contracts for construction or dismantling, demolition or removal of improvements, the contractor must provide appropriate safety barricades, signs and signal lights and must comply with the standards issued by the Secretary of Labor at 29 CFR part 1926 and 29 CFR part 1910 [@CL-CMP-4890]. Under the same paragraph, it must ensure that any additional measures the contracting officer determines to be reasonably necessary for the purposes stated in paragraph (a) of the clause are taken [@CL-CMP-4891].

If a contract is for construction or dismantling, demolition or removal of improvements with any Department of Defense agency or component, the contractor must comply with all pertinent provisions of the latest version of the United States Army Corps of Engineers Safety and Health Requirements Manual [@CL-CMP-4892]. The manual is the latest version in effect on the date of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-5065].

### Notice of noncompliance and stop-work order

Whenever the contracting officer becomes aware of any noncompliance with the safety requirements or any condition that poses a serious or imminent danger to the health or safety of the public or Government personnel, the officer must notify the contractor orally, with written confirmation [@CL-CMP-5066]. The officer must also request immediate initiation of corrective action, and the notice, when delivered to the contractor or its representative at the work site, is deemed sufficient notice of the noncompliance and that corrective action is required [@CL-CMP-5067]. After receiving the notice, the contractor must immediately take corrective action [@CL-CMP-5068].

If the contractor fails or refuses to promptly take corrective action, the contracting officer may issue an order stopping all or part of the work until satisfactory corrective action has been taken [@CL-CMP-4893]. The contractor is not entitled to any equitable adjustment of the contract price or extension of the performance schedule on any stop work order issued under the clause [@CL-CMP-4894].

### Flowdown

The contractor must insert the clause, including paragraph (e), with appropriate changes in the designation of the parties, in subcontracts [@CL-CMP-4895].

### Alternate I

Alternate I applies if the contract will involve work of a long duration or hazardous nature [@CL-CMP-5069], or performance on a Government facility that, on the advice of technical representatives, involves hazardous materials or operations that might endanger the safety of the public and/or Government personnel or property [@CL-CMP-5070]. In either case it adds a paragraph (f) to the basic clause [@CL-CMP-5070]. Before commencing the work, the contractor must submit a written proposed plan for implementing the clause, and the plan must include an analysis of the significant hazards to life, limb and property inherent in contract work performance and a plan for controlling these hazards [@CL-CMP-5071]. It must also meet with representatives of the contracting officer to discuss and develop a mutual understanding relative to administration of the overall safety program [@CL-CMP-5072].

### Records and reports

The clause provides for the contracting officer's written confirmation of the oral notice [@CL-CMP-5066] and, under Alternate I, the contractor's written proposed plan [@CL-CMP-5071].

## How it works

The duties run in layers: a general standard for work environments and procedures, concrete measures and standards issued by the Secretary of Labor on construction and demolition work, and a manual requirement on Department of Defense work [@CL-CMP-4888; @CL-CMP-4890; @CL-CMP-4892]. Enforcement starts with an oral notice that the contracting officer confirms in writing and that is sufficient when delivered at the work site [@CL-CMP-5066; @CL-CMP-5067]. If the contractor then fails or refuses to act promptly, the officer may stop all or part of the work, with no price or schedule relief for the contractor [@CL-CMP-4893; @CL-CMP-4894]. The clause passes down the chain because the contractor must insert it in subcontracts [@CL-CMP-4895]. The written plan and the meeting before commencing the work are required under Alternate I [@CL-CMP-5071; @CL-CMP-5072].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.236-13 says the same in substance on the safety duties: the contractor must provide and maintain work environments and procedures that safeguard the public and Government personnel, property, materials, supplies and equipment exposed to its operations and activities [@CL-CMP-0646; @CL-CMP-4888], and its other listed items are to avoid interruptions of Government operations and delays in project completion dates and to control costs [@CL-CMP-0647; @CL-CMP-4889]. On contracts for construction or dismantling, demolition or removal of improvements, paragraph (b) opens with In addition where the codified paragraph opens with For these purposes, and requires appropriate safety barricades, signs and signal lights and compliance with the Secretary of Labor standards at 29 CFR part 1926 and 29 CFR part 1910 [@CL-CMP-0648; @CL-CMP-4890], and requires that any additional measures the contracting officer determines to be reasonably necessary for the purposes are taken [@CL-CMP-0649; @CL-CMP-4891]. For such work with a Department of Defense agency or component, paragraph (c) requires compliance with all pertinent provisions of the latest version of the Army Corps of Engineers Safety and Health Requirements Manual in effect on the date of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-0650; @CL-CMP-0657; @CL-CMP-4892; @CL-CMP-5065]. The notice and stop-work steps in paragraph (d) say the same in substance: if the contracting officer becomes aware of any noncompliance with these requirements or any condition that poses a serious or imminent danger to the health or safety of the public or Government personnel, the contracting officer notifies the contractor orally with written confirmation [@CL-CMP-0658; @CL-CMP-5066] and requests immediate initiation of corrective action, and the notice delivered at the work site is deemed sufficient [@CL-CMP-0659; @CL-CMP-5067]; the contractor must immediately take corrective action, and if it fails or refuses to do so promptly the contracting officer may order all or part of the work stopped [@CL-CMP-0660; @CL-CMP-5068; @CL-CMP-4893]; and the contractor is not entitled to an equitable adjustment or schedule extension on the stop-work order [@CL-CMP-0661; @CL-CMP-4894]. Alternate I applies on the same two conditions and adds paragraph (f) [@CL-CMP-0663; @CL-CMP-5069] [@CL-CMP-0664; @CL-CMP-5070], under which the contractor submits a written proposed plan before commencing the work, with the hazard analysis and the plan for controlling the hazards [@CL-CMP-0665; @CL-CMP-0666; @CL-CMP-5071], and meets with representatives of the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-0667; @CL-CMP-5072]. The subcontract paragraph (e) differs in wording: the RFO paragraph says the contractor must insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e), in subcontracts [@CL-CMP-0662], where the codified paragraph says to insert the clause, including paragraph (e), with appropriate changes in the designation of the parties [@CL-CMP-4895].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Department of Defense construction contract includes the clause and Alternate I, because the work is on a facility with hazardous operations [@CL-CMP-5070]. The contractor must comply with the latest version of the Army Corps of Engineers manual in effect on the date of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-4892; @CL-CMP-5065]. Before commencing the work it submits a written proposed plan that analyzes the significant hazards and plans their control [@CL-CMP-5071], and it meets with representatives of the contracting officer about administration of the safety program [@CL-CMP-5072].

During the work the contracting officer becomes aware of an unguarded excavation. The officer notifies the contractor's foreman at the work site orally, confirms in writing, and requests immediate corrective action [@CL-CMP-5066; @CL-CMP-5067]. The notice is deemed sufficient notice [@CL-CMP-5067]. The crew takes no corrective action that day, so the officer stops the work in that area [@CL-CMP-4893]. The stop lasts 3 days and the contractor claims 15,000 of added cost and a 3-day extension. Neither is allowed, because the contractor is not entitled to any equitable adjustment of the contract price or extension of the performance schedule on any stop work order issued under the clause [@CL-CMP-4894]. The contractor's excavation subcontract must contain the clause, including paragraph (e) [@CL-CMP-4895]. Under the RFO text, that subcontract must contain the substance of the clause, including paragraph (e) [@CL-CMP-0662].

## Never confuse

The contractor is not entitled to any equitable adjustment of the contract price or extension of the performance schedule on any stop work order issued under the clause [@CL-CMP-4894]. The stop-work order clause is covered in [Stop-work orders](cmp-stop-work-orders.md). Airfield safety precautions and ammunition and explosives safety precautions are covered in [airfield safety precautions](cmp-dfars-airfield-safety-precautions.md) and [ammunition and explosives safety precautions](cmp-dfars-ammunition-explosives-safety-precautions.md). The codified paragraph (e) says to insert the clause with appropriate changes in the designation of the parties [@CL-CMP-4895], and the RFO paragraph (e) says to insert the substance of the clause [@CL-CMP-0662].
