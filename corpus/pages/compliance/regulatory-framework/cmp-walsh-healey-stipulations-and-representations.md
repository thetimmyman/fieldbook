## Purpose

Shows what the materials and supplies clause requires when a contract crosses the Walsh-Healey threshold, and what the previous contracts and affirmative action representations record.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5221] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-20, 52.222-22 and 52.222-25 [@CL-CMP-5221; @CL-CMP-5226; @CL-CMP-5228] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-20 [@CL-CMP-6560; @CL-CMP-6561; @CL-CMP-6562; @CL-CMP-6563; @CL-CMP-6564]; No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.222-22 or FAR 52.222-25, which RFO part 52 lists as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-6565; @CL-CMP-6602] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5221] |
| Applies to | Contracts for materials, supplies, articles or equipment above the Walsh-Healey threshold, plus two offeror representation provisions [@CL-CMP-5221] |

The clause is written for a contract to manufacture or furnish materials, supplies, articles or equipment in an amount that exceeds or may exceed the FAR 22.602 threshold on the date of award [@CL-CMP-5221]. The terms apply where the contract is also subject to chapter 65 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-5222]. The clause names the threshold by reference to FAR 22.602, so the dollar figure comes from that section and not from the clause [@CL-CMP-5221].

All stipulations required by that chapter and by the Secretary of Labor's regulations in 41 CFR chapter 50 are incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-5223], and they are subject to all applicable rulings and interpretations of the Secretary of Labor that are now, or may hereafter, be in effect [@CL-CMP-5335]. All employees whose work relates to the contract must be paid not less than the minimum wage that the Secretary of Labor's regulations prescribe [@CL-CMP-5224].

## How it works

Learners, student learners, apprentices and workers with disabilities may be employed below that minimum wage to the same extent that section 14 of the Fair Labor Standards Act permits [@CL-CMP-5225].

The provision at FAR 52.222-22 has the offeror represent whether it has, or has not, participated in a previous contract or subcontract subject to the Equal Opportunity clause of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-5226]. It also has the offeror represent whether it has, or has not, filed all required compliance reports, and that representations of submission of required compliance reports, signed by proposed subcontractors, will be obtained before subcontract awards [@CL-CMP-5227].

The provision at FAR 52.222-25 has the offeror represent that it has, or has not, developed and kept on file at each establishment the affirmative action programs that the Secretary of Labor's regulations require [@CL-CMP-5228]. Alternatively, the offeror represents that it has not previously had contracts subject to the written affirmative action programs requirement of those regulations [@CL-CMP-5336].

Under the RFO text, which DoD contracting officers use in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762], the comparison with the codified text is as follows. The RFO clause at FAR 52.222-20 keeps the condition that the contract is subject to chapter 65 of title 41 [@CL-CMP-5222; @CL-CMP-6561], the incorporation of the stipulations and the rulings and interpretations language [@CL-CMP-5223; @CL-CMP-5335; @CL-CMP-6562], and the minimum wage duty for employees whose work relates to the contract, with "shall be paid" now "must be paid" [@CL-CMP-5224; @CL-CMP-6563]. It also keeps the rule that learners, student learners, apprentices and workers with disabilities may be employed below that minimum wage to the same extent that section 14 of the Fair Labor Standards Act permits [@CL-CMP-5225; @CL-CMP-6564]. The RFO clause names FAR 22.601-1 for the threshold where the codified clause names FAR 22.602 [@CL-CMP-5221; @CL-CMP-6560]. The RFO text lists FAR 52.222-22 and FAR 52.222-25 as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-6565; @CL-CMP-6602], where the codified text has the previous contracts and compliance reports provision and the affirmative action compliance provision [@CL-CMP-5226; @CL-CMP-5228].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror bids to supply 500 machined brackets under a solicitation that carries the clause. It checks that the order value is above the FAR 22.602 threshold, that the contract is subject to chapter 65 of title 41, and that its payroll pays every employee whose work relates to the order at least the prescribed minimum wage. In its representations it marks that it has participated in a previous contract subject to the Equal Opportunity clause, that it has filed all required compliance reports, and that it has developed and keeps on file affirmative action programs at both of its establishments. It also plans to obtain a signed representation about compliance reports from each proposed subcontractor before it awards any subcontract. Under the RFO text the threshold check would name FAR 22.601-1 [@CL-CMP-6560], and FAR 52.222-22 and FAR 52.222-25 are listed as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-6565; @CL-CMP-6602].

## Never confuse

The incorporated stipulations are not restated in the contract: the clause pulls them in by reference [@CL-CMP-5223]. The minimum wage duty is not a general wage floor for the whole company; it runs to employees whose work relates to the contract [@CL-CMP-5224]. The wage duty belongs to the materials and supplies clause, not to the two representation provisions, which ask the offeror to state its history, its report filing and its program status [@CL-CMP-5224; @CL-CMP-5226; @CL-CMP-5227; @CL-CMP-5228]. Under the codified text the threshold sits at FAR 22.602 and the two representation provisions are FAR 52.222-22 and FAR 52.222-25 [@CL-CMP-5221; @CL-CMP-5226; @CL-CMP-5228], but the RFO clause points to FAR 22.601-1 and the RFO text lists the two provision numbers as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-6560; @CL-CMP-6565; @CL-CMP-6602].
