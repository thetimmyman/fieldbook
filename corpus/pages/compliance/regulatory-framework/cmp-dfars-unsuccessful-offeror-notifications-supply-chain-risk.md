## Purpose

Covers the DFARS rule on what a notification to an unsuccessful offeror may reveal when the Government has used the supply chain risk authority.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-8428] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.503 [@CL-CMP-8428] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8428] |
| Applies to | Notifications to unsuccessful offerors when the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d) [@CL-CMP-8428] |

If the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d), the notifications to unsuccessful offerors, whether preaward or postaward, shall not reveal any information that is determined to be withheld from disclosure in accordance with section 3252 of title 10, United States Code [@CL-CMP-8428]. The provision refers to subpart 239.73 [@CL-CMP-8429].

## How it works

The trigger is the Government's exercise of the authority provided in 239.7305(d) [@CL-CMP-8428]. Once the authority is exercised, the notifications to unsuccessful offerors, either preaward or postaward, shall not reveal any information that is determined to be withheld from disclosure [@CL-CMP-8428].

The information the notifications must not reveal is information determined to be withheld from disclosure in accordance with section 3252 of title 10, United States Code, and the provision refers to subpart 239.73 [@CL-CMP-8429].

For when notices are sent and what they otherwise contain, see [Notices to unsuccessful offerors](../../estimating/pricing/est-notices-to-unsuccessful-offerors.md). For the related supply chain risk notice to offerors, see [Information assurance training and supply chain risk notice (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-ia-training-and-supply-chain-risk-notice.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buy uses the authority provided in 239.7305(d) [@CL-CMP-8428], and two offerors are not selected. The draft postaward letter explains the exclusion in detail, including information that has been determined to be withheld from disclosure. The letter must not reveal that information [@CL-CMP-8428], so the writer removes that detail. The same limit applies to a preaward notice to an offeror that is excluded earlier [@CL-CMP-8428].

## Never confuse

The supply-chain limit is not the ordinary notice rule: it applies if the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d) [@CL-CMP-8428], and covers information that is determined to be withheld from disclosure [@CL-CMP-8429]. For the ordinary notice rule, see [Notices to unsuccessful offerors](../../estimating/pricing/est-notices-to-unsuccessful-offerors.md).
