## Purpose

Covers the DFARS rule on what a notification to an unsuccessful offeror may reveal when the Government has used the supply chain risk authority.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-8428] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.503 [@CL-CMP-8428] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8428] |
| Applies to | Notifications to unsuccessful offerors when the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d) [@CL-CMP-8428] |

If the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d), the notifications to unsuccessful offerors, whether preaward or postaward, shall not reveal any information that is determined to be withheld from disclosure in accordance with section 3252 of title 10, United States Code [@CL-CMP-8428]. The section adds a cross-reference to subpart 239.73 [@CL-CMP-8429].

## How it works

The trigger is the Government's exercise of the authority provided in 239.7305(d) [@CL-CMP-8428]. Once the authority is used, the limit applies to every notification to an unsuccessful offeror, whether it is given before or after award [@CL-CMP-8428]. The duty falls on the Government, which issues the notifications.

What is protected is information determined to be withheld from disclosure in accordance with section 3252 of title 10, United States Code [@CL-CMP-8429]. The section does not say who makes that determination, and it sets no deadline or threshold; it cross-references subpart 239.73, which is outside the text printed here [@CL-CMP-8429]. The authority itself is not described in this section either, so this card does not state its conditions.

The section changes only what the notifications may reveal. When notices are sent and what they otherwise contain are on [Notices to unsuccessful offerors](../../estimating/pricing/est-notices-to-unsuccessful-offerors.md). The related supply chain risk notice to offerors is on [Information assurance training and supply chain risk notice (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-information-assurance-training-and-supply-chain-risk-notice.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buy uses the authority provided in 239.7305(d) [@CL-CMP-8428], and two offerors are not selected. The draft postaward letter explains the exclusion in detail, including information that has been determined to be withheld from disclosure. The letter must not reveal that information [@CL-CMP-8428], so the writer removes that detail. The same limit applies to a preaward notice to an offeror that is excluded earlier [@CL-CMP-8428].

## Never confuse

This section is not the ordinary notice rule. It limits what the notifications may reveal when the authority is used, while the notice rule itself is on [Notices to unsuccessful offerors](../../estimating/pricing/est-notices-to-unsuccessful-offerors.md).

The limit is also not tied to any one offer being rejected. It applies because the Government exercised the authority [@CL-CMP-8428], and it protects only information determined to be withheld from disclosure [@CL-CMP-8429].
