## Purpose

Shows when a corporate administrative contracting officer may be assigned, which agency approves a State or local government's indirect costs, and who receives the executed rate agreement and its supporting documents.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7814] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.601, FAR 42.705-4 and FAR 42.706 [@CL-CMP-7814; @CL-CMP-7816; @CL-CMP-7818] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.401 [@CL-CMP-0092; @CL-CMP-0093]; RFO FAR 42.505-4 [@CL-CMP-0116; @CL-CMP-0117]; RFO FAR 42.505-1(b)(5)(iv) [@CL-CMP-0118; @CL-CMP-0119]; RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2) [@CL-CMP-0120; @CL-CMP-0121; @CL-CMP-0122] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7814] |
| Applies to | Contractors with more than one operational location [@CL-CMP-7814]; State and local governments with federally-funded programs and activities [@CL-CMP-7991]; executed indirect cost rate agreements [@CL-CMP-7818] |

Contractors with more than one operational location often have corporate-wide policies, procedures and activities requiring Government review and approval and affecting the work of more than one administrative contracting officer [@CL-CMP-7814]. In those circumstances, effective and consistent contract administration may require the assignment of a corporate administrative contracting officer to deal with corporate management and to perform selected contract administration functions on a corporate-wide basis [@CL-CMP-7815].

For State and local governments, the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix V, concerning cost principles for state and local governments, establishes the cognizant agency concept and the procedures for determining a cognizant agency [@CL-CMP-7816]. That agency is the one for approving State and local government indirect costs associated with federally-funded programs and activities [@CL-CMP-7991]. The indirect cost rates negotiated and approved by the cognizant agency for indirect costs will be used by all Federal agencies that also award contracts to these same State and local governments [@CL-CMP-7817].

## How it works

The contracting officer or auditor shall promptly distribute executed copies of the indirect cost rate agreement to the contractor and to each affected contracting agency, and shall provide copies of the agreement for the contract files [@CL-CMP-7818]. They do this in accordance with the guidance for contract modifications in FAR subpart 4.2, Contract Distribution [@CL-CMP-7992]. The contracting officer determination procedure and the auditor determination procedure are covered in [Final indirect cost rates: contracting officer determination procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-co-final-indirect-rates.md) and [Final indirect cost rates: auditor determination procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-auditor-determination.md).

Copies of the negotiation memorandum prepared under contracting officer determination, or the audit report prepared under auditor determination, shall be furnished, as appropriate, to the contracting offices and Government audit offices [@CL-CMP-7819].

How the cognizant agency is set for other contractors is covered in [Cognizant Federal agency, completion invoices and educational institution rate settlement](../audit-retention/cmp-cognizant-agency-and-completion-invoices.md). The contract administration functions are covered in [Contract administration functions](../audit-retention/cmp-contract-administration-functions.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.401 keeps the statement that contractors with more than one operational location often have corporate-wide policies, procedures and activities requiring Government review and approval and affecting the work of more than one administrative contracting officer [@CL-CMP-0092; @CL-CMP-7814]. It then says the CFA may assign a corporate administrative contracting officer to deal with corporate management and to perform selected contract administration functions on a corporate-wide basis, where the codified section says effective and consistent contract administration may require the assignment of one [@CL-CMP-0093; @CL-CMP-7815]. RFO FAR 42.505-4 says the guidance on cost principles for state and local governments establishes the procedures for determining the cognizant agency for State and local government indirect costs associated with federally-funded programs and activities [@CL-CMP-0116], where the codified section says it establishes the cognizant agency concept and the procedures for determining a cognizant agency for approving those costs [@CL-CMP-7816; @CL-CMP-7991]. RFO FAR 42.505-4 says rates approved by the cognizant agency for indirect costs must be used by all Federal agencies that also award contracts to these same State and local governments, where the codified section says the rates negotiated and approved by that agency will be used [@CL-CMP-0117; @CL-CMP-7817]. RFO FAR 42.505-1(b)(5)(iv), in the contracting officer determination procedure, says the contracting officer must promptly distribute executed copies of the indirect cost rate agreement to the contractor and to each affected contracting agency and include copies of the agreement in the contract file [@CL-CMP-0118], where codified FAR 42.706(a) names the contracting officer or auditor and refers to the guidance for contract modifications in subpart 4.2 [@CL-CMP-7818; @CL-CMP-7992]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(iii), in the auditor determination procedure, says the agreement must be signed by the contractor and the auditor and a copy must be distributed to the contractor and to each affected contracting agency [@CL-CMP-0120; @CL-CMP-7818], and RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(v) includes the step to promptly distribute resultant documents [@CL-CMP-0122]. Under RFO FAR 42.505-1(b)(5)(iv), the contracting officer must include a copy of the negotiation memorandum in the contract file and furnish it, as appropriate, to the affected contracting offices and Government auditor [@CL-CMP-0119; @CL-CMP-7819]. Under RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(i), copies of the audit report must be furnished to the affected contracting offices [@CL-CMP-0121], where codified FAR 42.706(b) names the contracting offices and Government audit offices [@CL-CMP-7819].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has 4 divisions, and each division has its own administrative contracting officer. The contractor's corporate-wide policies need Government review and approval and affect the work of all 4 officers, so effective and consistent administration may require a corporate administrative contracting officer to deal with corporate management [@CL-CMP-7814; @CL-CMP-7815].

A county government has 3 federally-funded programs and holds contracts from 2 Federal agencies. The cognizant agency for indirect costs negotiates and approves its indirect cost rates, and both Federal agencies use those rates [@CL-CMP-7991; @CL-CMP-7817]. The contracting officer then distributes executed copies of the rate agreement promptly to the county and to both affected agencies, provides copies for the contract files, and follows the distribution guidance in subpart 4.2 [@CL-CMP-7818; @CL-CMP-7992]. The negotiation memorandum goes, as appropriate, to the contracting offices and the Government audit offices [@CL-CMP-7819]. The answer: one approved rate set used by every Federal agency awarding contracts to the county [@CL-CMP-7817], with the executed agreement sent to the county and each affected agency [@CL-CMP-7818]. This example follows the codified sections; under the RFO text, the contracting officer must promptly distribute executed copies of the rate agreement to the contractor and to each affected contracting agency and include copies in the contract file [@CL-CMP-0118], and rates approved by the cognizant agency must be used by all Federal agencies that also award contracts to the county [@CL-CMP-0117].

## Never confuse

A corporate administrative contracting officer deals with corporate management and corporate-wide functions across locations [@CL-CMP-7815]. The cognizant agency is the agency for approving State and local government indirect costs associated with federally-funded programs and activities [@CL-CMP-7816; @CL-CMP-7991].

Codified FAR 42.706 puts the distribution of the rate agreement and of the memorandum or audit report in one section [@CL-CMP-7818; @CL-CMP-7819], while the RFO text states distribution steps within the contracting officer determination procedure at FAR 42.505-1(b)(5)(iv) [@CL-CMP-0118; @CL-CMP-0119] and the auditor determination procedure at FAR 42.505-2(b)(2) [@CL-CMP-0120; @CL-CMP-0121; @CL-CMP-0122]. Rates approved by the cognizant agency for State and local government indirect costs must be used by all Federal agencies that also award contracts to those governments under the RFO text [@CL-CMP-0117], where the codified text says the rates will be used [@CL-CMP-7817].
