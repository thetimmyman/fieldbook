## Purpose

Explains how a solicitation shows that an award will be a rated order, what the contract clause says about a rated order, and what the contractor must follow.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5995] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.211-14; FAR 52.211-15 [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-5997] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5995] |
| Applies to | Any contract awarded as a result of a solicitation that carries the notice, and contracts that carry the clause [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-5997] |

The notice provision says that any contract awarded as a result of the solicitation will be a DX rated order or a DO rated order [@CL-CMP-5995]. The provision directs the contracting officer to check the appropriate box [@CL-CMP-6696]. The rated order is certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use under the Defense Priorities and Allocations System, and the contractor will be required to follow all of the requirements of that regulation [@CL-CMP-5996].

The contract clause states that the order is a rated order certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use, and that the contractor shall follow all the requirements of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation [@CL-CMP-5997].

## How it works

The solicitation notice names the rating that the awarded contract will carry through the box the contracting officer checks, which is either the DX box or the DO box [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-6696]. The same sentence says that the order is certified for the listed uses and that the contractor will be required to follow all of the requirements of the regulation [@CL-CMP-5996].

The contract clause states the certification and the duty again in the contract, and its text does not name a rating level [@CL-CMP-5997]. The duty is to follow all the requirements of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation, which the clause names as 15 CFR part 700 [@CL-CMP-5997]. What a DX or DO rating means, what a rated order must contain, and the duties on acceptance, rejection and flowdown are on [DPAS: rated orders](../../industrialization/capacity/ind-dpas-rated-orders.md) and [DPAS: acceptance, rejection, scheduling and flowdown of rated orders](../../industrialization/capacity/ind-dpas-acceptance-rejection-and-flowdown.md), and this card does not restate them.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation checks the box for a DO rated order. The supplier that wins takes a contract that is a DO rated order certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use, and it is required to follow all the requirements of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation [@CL-CMP-5996]. The contract also carries the clause, which says again that the order is a rated order and that the contractor shall follow all the requirements of that regulation [@CL-CMP-5997]. The clause does not say whether the rating is DO or DX, and in these two texts the level appears only in the box checked in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-5997]. Had the solicitation checked the DX box instead, the certification and the duty to follow the regulation would read the same way [@CL-CMP-5996; @CL-CMP-5997].

## Never confuse

The notice provision is not the contract clause. The provision tells offerors what any resulting contract will be [@CL-CMP-5995], and the clause is the statement in the contract that it is a rated order and that the contractor shall follow the regulation [@CL-CMP-5997].

The difference between the DX and DO levels is not in these two texts. This card says only that the contract will be one or the other [@CL-CMP-5995]; the effect of each level is on [DPAS: rated orders](../../industrialization/capacity/ind-dpas-rated-orders.md).
