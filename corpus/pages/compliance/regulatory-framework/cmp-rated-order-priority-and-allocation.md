## Purpose

Explains how a solicitation shows that an award will be a rated order, what the contract clause says about a rated order, and what the contractor must follow.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5995] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.211-14; FAR 52.211-15 [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-5997] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.211-14; RFO FAR 52.211-15 [@CL-CMP-2155; @CL-CMP-2157] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5995] |
| Applies to | Any contract awarded as a result of a solicitation that carries the notice, and contracts that carry the clause [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-5997] |

The notice provision says that any contract awarded as a result of the solicitation will be a DX rated order or a DO rated order [@CL-CMP-5995]. The provision directs the contracting officer to check the appropriate box [@CL-CMP-6696]. The rated order is certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use under the Defense Priorities and Allocations System, and the contractor will be required to follow all of the requirements of that regulation [@CL-CMP-5996].

The contract clause states that the order is a rated order certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use, and that the contractor shall follow all the requirements of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation [@CL-CMP-5997].

## How it works

The solicitation notice states that any contract awarded as a result of the solicitation will be a DX rated order or a DO rated order, and directs the contracting officer to check the appropriate box [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-6696]. The same sentence states that the rated order is certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use under the Defense Priorities and Allocations System (15 CFR 700), and that the Contractor will be required to follow all of the requirements of that regulation [@CL-CMP-5996].

The contract clause states that the order is a rated order certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use, and that the Contractor shall follow all the requirements of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation (15 CFR part 700) [@CL-CMP-5997]. For DX and DO ratings, rated order contents, and acceptance, rejection, scheduling and flowdown, see [DPAS: rated orders](../../industrialization/capacity/ind-dpas-rated-orders.md) and [DPAS: acceptance, rejection, scheduling and flowdown of rated orders](../../industrialization/capacity/ind-dpas-acceptance-rejection-and-flowdown.md).

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.211-14, any contract awarded as a result of the solicitation will be a DX rated order or a DO rated order, certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use under the Defense Priorities and Allocations System (DPAS) (15 CFR 700), the Contractor will be required to follow all of the requirements of that regulation, and the Contracting Officer is directed to check the appropriate box [@CL-CMP-2155; @CL-CMP-2156]. Under RFO FAR 52.211-15, the order is a rated order certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use, and the Contractor shall follow all the requirements of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation (15 CFR part 700) [@CL-CMP-2157].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation checks the box for a DO rated order. The supplier that wins takes a contract that is a DO rated order certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use, and it is required to follow all the requirements of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation [@CL-CMP-5996]. The contract also carries the clause, which says again that the order is a rated order and that the contractor shall follow all the requirements of that regulation [@CL-CMP-5997]. Had the solicitation checked the DX box instead, the certification and the duty to follow the regulation would read the same way [@CL-CMP-5996; @CL-CMP-5997].

## Never confuse

The notice provision states that any contract awarded as a result of the solicitation will be a DX rated order or a DO rated order certified for national defense, emergency preparedness, and energy program use under DPAS (15 CFR 700) and that the Contractor will be required to follow all of the requirements of that regulation [@CL-CMP-5995; @CL-CMP-5996], whereas the contract clause states in the contract that the order is a rated order certified for those uses and that the Contractor shall follow all the requirements of the DPAS regulation (15 CFR part 700) [@CL-CMP-5997].

For the effect of DX and DO ratings, see [DPAS: rated orders](../../industrialization/capacity/ind-dpas-rated-orders.md).
