## Purpose

Shows which drawings and other works become Government property, what the Government may do with shop drawings, and the duty to get approval and give notice before using a sole source design.

## The rule

|---|---|

| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7023, 252.227-7033 and 252.227-7024 [@CL-CMP-4278; @CL-CMP-4281; @CL-CMP-4282] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4278] |
| Applies to | Contracts using the property clause where DFARS 227.7107-1(b) prescribes it [@CL-CMP-4376]; the Rights in Shop Drawings clause as prescribed in 227.7107-(1)(c) [@CL-CMP-8628]; the Notice and Approval of Restricted Designs clause as prescribed at 227.7107-3 [@CL-CMP-8629] |

Under the Drawings and Other Data to Become Property of Government clause, all designs, drawings, specifications, notes and other works developed in the performance of the contract become the sole property of the Government and may be used on any other design or construction without additional compensation to the Contractor [@CL-CMP-4278]. The Government is considered the person for whom the work was prepared for the purpose of authorship in any copyrightable work under section 201(b) of title 17 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-4377], and the Contractor agrees, with respect to that authorship, not to assert or authorize others to assert any rights nor establish any claim under the design patent or copyright laws [@CL-CMP-4379]. The Contractor also agrees, for three years after completion of the project, to furnish all retained works on the request of the Contracting Officer, and unless the contract provides otherwise the Contractor has the right to retain copies of all works beyond that period [@CL-CMP-4279].

In the Rights in Shop Drawings clause, shop drawings for construction are drawings submitted to the Government by the Construction Contractor, subcontractor or any lower-tier subcontractor pursuant to a construction contract, showing in detail both (i) the proposed fabrication and assembly of structural elements and (ii) the installation (that is, form, fit and attachment details) of materials or equipment [@CL-CMP-4280; @CL-CMP-8627]. Under that clause the Government may duplicate, use and disclose in any manner and for any purpose shop drawings delivered under the contract [@CL-CMP-4281]. The clause, including its subcontract paragraph, is to be included in all subcontracts under the contract at any tier [@CL-CMP-4378].

Under the Notice and Approval of Restricted Designs clause, in the performance of the contract the Contractor shall, to the extent practicable, make maximum use of structures, machines, products, materials, construction methods and equipment that are readily available through Government or competitive commercial channels, or through standard or proven production techniques, methods and processes [@CL-CMP-4380; @CL-CMP-8630]. Unless approved by the Contracting Officer, the Contractor shall not produce a design or specification that requires in the construction work the use of structures, products, materials, construction equipment or processes that are known by the Contractor to be available only from a sole source [@CL-CMP-4282]. The Contractor shall promptly report any such design or specification to the Contracting Officer and give the reason why it is considered necessary to so restrict the design or specification [@CL-CMP-4283].

## How it works

Works developed in performance become the sole property of the Government, which may reuse them without extra payment, and the Contractor agrees not to assert design patent or copyright claims with respect to the authorship the clause describes [@CL-CMP-4278; @CL-CMP-4377; @CL-CMP-4379]. The Contractor agrees to furnish retained works on the request of the Contracting Officer for three years after completion of the project, and unless the contract provides otherwise may retain copies of all works beyond that period [@CL-CMP-4279]. The Government may duplicate, use and disclose delivered shop drawings in any manner and for any purpose, and the shop drawings clause is to be included in all subcontracts under the contract at any tier [@CL-CMP-4281; @CL-CMP-4378]. Under the restricted design clause the Contractor shall, to the extent practicable, make maximum use of readily available structures, machines, products, materials, construction methods and equipment [@CL-CMP-4380; @CL-CMP-8630]. Unless the Contracting Officer approves, the Contractor shall not produce a design or specification that requires items known by the Contractor to be available only from a sole source [@CL-CMP-4282], and the Contractor shall promptly report any such design or specification to the Contracting Officer with the reason it is considered necessary [@CL-CMP-4283].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A construction contractor develops structural drawings under the contract. The drawings become Government property and the Government reuses them on a second building without paying more. A steel fabricator submits shop drawings under the construction contract, which the Government may copy, use and disclose for any purpose, and the contractor includes the shop drawings clause in the fabricator's subcontract. The contractor's design calls for a fitting sold by one supplier only. The contractor needs the Contracting Officer's approval and must promptly report the design with its reason.

## Never confuse

Designs that become Government property are not the same as the license in shop drawings: under the property clause, all designs, drawings, specifications, notes and other works developed in the performance of the contract become the sole property of the Government and may be used on any other design or construction without additional compensation to the Contractor [@CL-CMP-4278], while under the Rights in Shop Drawings clause the Government may duplicate, use and disclose shop drawings delivered under the contract in any manner and for any purpose [@CL-CMP-4281]. For licenses in technical data, see [Technical data rights categories](cmp-dod-technical-data-rights-categories.md) and [Rights in data, general](cmp-rights-in-data-general-definitions-and-allocation.md). The existing works and unlimited rights clauses are in [Rights in data for existing works and Government unlimited rights](cmp-dfars-existing-works-and-unlimited-rights-in-data.md).
