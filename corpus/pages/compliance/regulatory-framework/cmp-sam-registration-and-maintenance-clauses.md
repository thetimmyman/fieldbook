## Purpose

Shows what a contractor must keep current in the System for Award Management after award, and what it must tell the Contracting Officer when its name changes, its contract assets move or its unique entity identifier changes.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3526] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-7; FAR 52.204-13 [@CL-CMP-3576; @CL-CMP-3526] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3526] |
| Applies to | Contractors under contracts that include the maintenance clause [@CL-CMP-3526] |

The registration provision requires an offeror to be registered in SAM when submitting an offer or quotation and at time of award, and it points to the maintenance clause for the requirement to maintain registration during performance and through final payment [@CL-CMP-3576]. The maintenance clause requires the contractor to maintain its registration in SAM during contract performance and through final payment of any contract, basic agreement, basic ordering agreement, or blanket purchasing agreement [@CL-CMP-3526]. To remain registered after the initial registration, the contractor is required to review and update its information in SAM on an annual basis, counted from the date of initial registration or subsequent updates, so that it is current, accurate and complete [@CL-CMP-3527]. The contractor is responsible for the currency, accuracy and completeness of the data within SAM, and for any liability resulting from the Government's reliance on inaccurate or incomplete data [@CL-CMP-3580].

If the solicitation contained the registration provision with its Alternate I and the contractor was unable to register before award, the contractor shall be registered in SAM within 30 days after award or before three days prior to submission of the first invoice, whichever occurs first [@CL-CMP-3528].

## How it works

The notice paragraph applies where a contractor has legally changed its business name or its doing business as name, whichever is shown on the contract, or has transferred the assets used in performing the contract, but has not completed the novation and change-of-name requirements in the FAR subpart on those agreements [@CL-CMP-3530]. The contractor then shall provide the responsible Contracting Officer a minimum of one business day's written notification of its intention to change the name in SAM, comply with the requirements of that subpart, and agree in writing to the timeline and procedures [@CL-CMP-3529]. If the contractor fails to comply with the notice requirements, or fails to perform the written agreement, and there is no properly executed novation or change-of-name agreement [@CL-CMP-3577], SAM information that shows a contractor other than the one indicated in the contract will be considered incorrect information within the meaning of the suspension of payment paragraph of the electronic funds transfer clause [@CL-CMP-3578]. The contractor shall not change the name or address for EFT payments or manual payments in its SAM record to reflect an assignee for the purpose of assignment of claims, and assignees shall be separately registered in SAM [@CL-CMP-3579]. The contractor must also tell the Contracting Officer of any change to its unique entity identifier within 30 days after the change, so that an appropriate modification can update the data on the contract [@CL-CMP-3531]. A change in the unique entity identifier does not necessarily require a novation to be accomplished [@CL-CMP-3581]. The clause text also sets out the definitions, and this card does not set them out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor first registers in SAM and, one year later, must review and update its information unless it updated sooner. In the ninth month its unique entity identifier changes. It tells the Contracting Officer within 30 days so that a modification can update the contract. Separately, a contractor that has legally changed its name but has not finished the change-of-name agreement gives the Contracting Officer one business day's written notice of its plan before it changes the name in SAM.

## Never confuse

The registration an offeror needs when it submits an offer and at award comes from the provision [@CL-CMP-3576], while the maintenance clause covers performance and final payment [@CL-CMP-3526]; for how registration works in awards, see [SAM registration and the unique entity identifier in awards](../../market/feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md). A change of the unique entity identifier carries a 30-day notice to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-3531], while the name-change and asset-transfer notice is a minimum of one business day [@CL-CMP-3529]. The novation and change-of-name agreements themselves are a separate process; see [Novation agreements](../../mna/diligence/mna-novation-agreements.md) and [Change-of-name agreements](../../mna/diligence/mna-change-of-name-agreements.md).

The representations and certifications posted in SAM are used under the offer provision described on [Annual representations and certifications provision: how its paragraphs work](cmp-annual-representations-provision-paragraphs.md). The executive compensation report is made as part of the contractor's annual registration requirement in SAM [@CL-CMP-3538] and is on [Reporting executive compensation and first-tier subcontract awards](cmp-executive-compensation-and-subcontract-award-reporting.md).
