## Purpose

Shows what the contractor may count as wages, how the overtime rate is set, and how a classification missing from the wage determination is checked and approved.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3030] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.406-2 and FAR 22.406-3 [@CL-CMP-3030; @CL-CMP-3032] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3030] |
| Applies to | Laborers and mechanics paid under a construction wage determination, and classifications the determination does not list [@CL-CMP-3030; @CL-CMP-3032] |

In computing wages paid to a laborer or mechanic, the contractor may include only listed items, the first being amounts paid in cash or deducted from payments under the conditions in 29 CFR 3.5 [@CL-CMP-3030]. In computing required overtime payments, the contractor shall use the basic hourly rate in the wage determination, or the basic hourly rate actually paid by the contractor if higher [@CL-CMP-3031]. For that computation, the basic rate of pay includes employee contributions to fringe benefits but excludes the contractor's contributions, costs, or payment of cash equivalents for fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-3119; @CL-CMP-3031]. The other listed items that count as wages are not drafted on this card.

If any laborer or mechanic is to be employed in a classification not listed in the wage determination applicable to the contract, the contracting officer shall, pursuant to the clause at 52.222-6, require the contractor to submit Standard Form 1444 [@CL-CMP-7672; @CL-CMP-7679]. The contracting officer shall review the request to determine whether it meets listed criteria, the first being that the classification is appropriate and its work is not performed by any classification contained in the applicable wage determination [@CL-CMP-3033]. Two further listed criteria are that the classification is used in the area by the construction industry and that the proposed wage rate, including fringe benefits, bears a reasonable relationship to the wage rates in the contract's wage determination [@CL-CMP-3034; @CL-CMP-3033].

## How it works

The contracting officer reviews the request to determine whether it meets the listed criteria [@CL-CMP-3033; @CL-CMP-3034]. A route to approval applies where the criteria are met, the contractor and the laborers or mechanics to be employed in the additional classification (if known) or their representatives agree to the proposed classification, and the contracting officer approves [@CL-CMP-3035]. In that listed case, the contracting officer submits a report, including a copy of Standard Form 1444, of that action to the Administrator, Wage and Hour Division, for approval, modification, or disapproval of the additional classification and wage rate [@CL-CMP-3047; @CL-CMP-3035]. Within 30 days of receiving the report, the Administrator will complete action and advise the contracting officer, or will notify the contracting officer that additional time is necessary [@CL-CMP-3036; @CL-CMP-3047]. If the criteria are not met, the contracting officer shall submit a report giving the views of all interested parties and the contracting officer's recommendation to the Administrator, Wage and Hour Division, for determination of appropriate classification and wage rate [@CL-CMP-3120; @CL-CMP-3033]. The part of that paragraph on a failure of the parties to agree is not restated on this card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A worker's base rate in the wage determination is 20 an hour, but the contractor actually pays 22. Overtime is computed on 22, because the rate actually paid is higher [@CL-CMP-3031]. Separately, the contractor needs a classification the determination does not list and submits the form with a proposed rate of 30 an hour [@CL-CMP-7672; @CL-CMP-7679]. The contracting officer finds the work is not done by any listed classification [@CL-CMP-3033], the trade is used locally, and 30 is close to nearby listed rates [@CL-CMP-3034]. The parties agree and the contracting officer approves [@CL-CMP-3035], so the contracting officer sends the report to the Administrator [@CL-CMP-3047], who answers within 30 days or asks for more time [@CL-CMP-3036].
