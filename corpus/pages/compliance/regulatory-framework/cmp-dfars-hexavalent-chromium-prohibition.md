## Purpose

Shows what the clause prohibits in deliverables, the exceptions and the authority for approval, and the flowdown.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4533] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.223-7008 [@CL-CMP-4533] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4533] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the clause, used as prescribed in DFARS 223.7306 [@CL-CMP-4563] |

Unless otherwise specified by the Contracting Officer, the Contractor must not provide any deliverable or construction material under the contract that either contains hexavalent chromium in a concentration greater than 0.1 percent by weight in any homogenous material [@CL-CMP-4533], or requires the removal or reapplication of hexavalent chromium materials during subsequent sustainment phases of the deliverable or construction material [@CL-CMP-4534; @CL-CMP-8635].

As used in the clause, homogeneous material means a material that cannot be mechanically disjointed into different materials and is of uniform composition throughout [@CL-CMP-4561]. Homogeneous material does not include conversion coatings that chemically modify the substrate [@CL-CMP-4562]. The prohibition does not apply to hexavalent chromium produced as a by-product of manufacturing processes [@CL-CMP-4535].

## How it works

One of the two alternatives in the prohibition is a deliverable or construction material containing hexavalent chromium in a concentration greater than 0.1 percent by weight in any homogenous material, unless the Contracting Officer specifies otherwise [@CL-CMP-4533; @CL-CMP-8635]. If authorization for incorporation of hexavalent chromium in a deliverable or construction material is required, the Contractor must submit a request to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4536]. The Contractor must include the substance of the clause, including its subcontracts paragraph, in all subcontracts, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services, that are for supplies, maintenance and repair services, or construction materials [@CL-CMP-4537].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor plans a deliverable with a resin component that holds 0.5 percent hexavalent chromium by weight (assumed). The contract has no Contracting Officer specification allowing it. That is above the clause's 0.1 percent concentration, so the contractor may not provide the deliverable, and if authorization for incorporating the hexavalent chromium is required, the contractor submits a request to the Contracting Officer. A by-product of the manufacturing process is a different case, and the prohibition does not apply to it.

## Never confuse

The prohibition does not apply to hexavalent chromium produced as a by-product of manufacturing processes [@CL-CMP-4535]. For the fire-fighting agent bar, see [Prohibition of procurement of fluorinated fire-fighting agent for use on military installations (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-fluorinated-fire-fighting-agent-prohibition.md). For how commercial subcontracts take clause terms in general, see [Subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services (DFARS)](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-dfars-commercial-subcontract-flowdown.md).
