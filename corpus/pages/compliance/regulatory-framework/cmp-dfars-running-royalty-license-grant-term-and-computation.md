## Purpose

Shows the license granted under a running royalty arrangement, how long it lasts and how royalties accrue and are computed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4258] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7006, 252.227-7007 and 252.227-7008 [@CL-CMP-4258; @CL-CMP-4260; @CL-CMP-4261] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4259] |
| Applies to | Patent releases, license agreements and assignments [@CL-CMP-4258] |

Under the running royalty grant the Contractor grants the Government, as represented by the Secretary named in the clause, an irrevocable, nonexclusive, nontransferable license under the listed patents, applications for patent and patents granted on such applications [@CL-CMP-4259]. The license lets the Department named in the grant practice the licensed inventions, or have them practiced for it, throughout the world in manufacturing and using articles or materials, in using methods or processes, and in disposing of articles or materials in accordance with law [@CL-CMP-4365]. Under the License Term—Running Royalty clause the license remains in full force and effect for the full term of each patent referred to in the License Grant clause and of any patent hereafter issued on applications referred to there, unless sooner terminated as elsewhere provided in the agreement [@CL-CMP-4260].

Under the Computation of Royalties clause, royalties accrue to the Contractor on all articles or materials embodying, or manufactured by the use of, inventions claimed under any unexpired United States patent licensed, upon acceptance by the Department named in the clause [@CL-CMP-4261]. The clause begins by making the accrual of royalties subject to the conditions it says are hereinafter stated [@CL-CMP-4262]. For articles whether manufactured by the Government or procured under a fixed price contract, the clause provides blanks for a percent of the net selling price of the articles or materials, with an alternative amount per named item [@CL-CMP-4263]. For articles acquired or manufactured by a Contractor performing under a cost-reimbursement contract, it provides a rate stated as an amount per named item [@CL-CMP-4264]. For articles or materials made by the named Department, net selling price means the actual cost of direct labor and materials without allowance for overhead and supervision [@CL-CMP-4265]. A closing note to the clause says to use bracketed matter as appropriate [@CL-CMP-4366].

## How it works

The grant names the Department that may practice the inventions, and the term follows the life of the licensed patents unless the license is sooner terminated as the agreement provides [@CL-CMP-4365; @CL-CMP-4260]. Royalties accrue on acceptance of articles that use a licensed unexpired United States patent, subject to the conditions the clause states [@CL-CMP-4261; @CL-CMP-4262]. The rate form depends on how the article is obtained: a percent of net selling price or an alternative amount per item for articles the Government manufactures or procures under a fixed price contract, and an amount per item for articles a cost-reimbursement contractor acquires or manufactures [@CL-CMP-4263; @CL-CMP-4264]. None of the three clauses printed on this card sets a procedure for reporting or paying royalties. The reporting clause is covered in [Patent license and release contracts](cmp-dfars-patent-license-release-readjustment-and-royalty-reporting.md), where a written report is to be delivered to the Contractor, subject to military security regulations, on or before the sixtieth day following the end of each yearly period during which royalties have accrued [@CL-CMP-3961].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The agreed rate is 5 percent of net selling price, and the part embodies an invention claimed under an unexpired United States patent licensed under the clause. The Department makes a part itself, using direct labor of 600 and materials of 400, with a further 300 of overhead. The overhead is left out, so net selling price is 1,000. The royalty is 50 per part, accruing on acceptance and subject to the clause's conditions.

## Never confuse

This card covers royalties that accrue to the Contractor from the Government under a license. It does not cover royalty costs the Contractor pays to others; see [Royalties and other costs for use of patents](../cas-allowability/cmp-patent-royalties.md). The clauses on reporting and readjusting royalties for patent license contracts are in [Patent license and release contracts](cmp-dfars-patent-license-release-readjustment-and-royalty-reporting.md). The license in the License Grant clause is described as paid up [@CL-CMP-4250], which is a different grant clause covered in [Patent license grant, term and termination](cmp-dfars-patent-license-grant-term-and-termination.md). The release of past infringement and the license to other Government agencies are in [Non-estoppel, release and license to other agencies](cmp-dfars-non-estoppel-release-and-license-to-other-agencies.md).
