## Purpose

Shows the license granted under a running royalty arrangement, how long it lasts and how royalties accrue and are computed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4258] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7006, 252.227-7007 and 252.227-7008 [@CL-CMP-4258; @CL-CMP-4260; @CL-CMP-4261] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | AUG 1984 [@CL-CMP-10169] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4259] |
| Applies to | Patent releases, license agreements and assignments [@CL-CMP-4258] |

Under the running royalty grant the Contractor grants the Government, as represented by the Secretary named in the clause, an irrevocable, nonexclusive, nontransferable license under the listed patents, applications for patent and patents granted on such applications, and under any patents which may issue as the result of any reissue, division or continuation of them [@CL-CMP-4259; @CL-CMP-7696]. The license lets the Department named in the grant practice the licensed inventions, or have them practiced for it, throughout the world in manufacturing and using articles or materials, in using methods or processes, and in disposing of articles or materials in accordance with law [@CL-CMP-4365]. Under the License Term—Running Royalty clause the license remains in full force and effect for the full term of each patent referred to in the License Grant clause and of any patent hereafter issued on applications referred to there, unless sooner terminated as elsewhere provided in the agreement [@CL-CMP-4260].

Under the Computation of Royalties clause, royalties accrue to the Contractor on all articles or materials embodying, or manufactured by the use of, inventions claimed under any unexpired United States patent licensed, upon acceptance by the Department named in the clause [@CL-CMP-4261]. The clause begins by making the accrual of royalties subject to the conditions it says are hereinafter stated [@CL-CMP-4262]. For articles whether manufactured by the Government or procured under a fixed price contract, the clause provides blanks for a percent of the net selling price of the articles or materials, with an alternative amount per named item [@CL-CMP-4263]. For articles acquired or manufactured by a Contractor performing under a cost-reimbursement contract, it provides a rate stated as an amount per named item [@CL-CMP-4264]. For articles or materials made by the named Department, net selling price means the actual cost of direct labor and materials without allowance for overhead and supervision [@CL-CMP-4265]. A closing note to the clause says to use bracketed matter as appropriate [@CL-CMP-4366].

## How it works

The grant names the Department that may practice the inventions, and the term follows the life of the licensed patents unless the license is sooner terminated as the agreement provides [@CL-CMP-4365; @CL-CMP-4260]. Royalties accrue on acceptance of articles that use a licensed unexpired United States patent, subject to the conditions the clause states [@CL-CMP-4261; @CL-CMP-4262]. For articles whether manufactured by the Government or procured under a fixed price contract the clause provides blanks for a percent of the net selling price or an alternative amount per named item [@CL-CMP-4263], and for articles acquired or manufactured by a Contractor performing under a cost-reimbursement contract it provides a rate stated as an amount per named item [@CL-CMP-4264]. For the reporting clause, see [Patent license and release contracts](cmp-dfars-patent-license-release-and-royalty-reporting.md), under which the Contracting Officer or the designated office, in accordance with agency procedures, is the party named to deliver to the Contractor, subject to military security regulations, a written report on or before the sixtieth day following the end of each yearly period during which royalties have accrued [@CL-CMP-8644; @CL-CMP-3961].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The agreed rate is 5 percent of net selling price, and the part embodies an invention claimed under an unexpired United States patent licensed under the clause. The Department makes a part itself, using direct labor of 600 and materials of 400, with a further 300 of overhead. The overhead is left out, so net selling price is 1,000. The royalty is 50 per part, accruing on acceptance and subject to the clause's conditions.

## Never confuse

A running royalty accrues article by article rather than being paid up once: under the Computation of Royalties clause, royalties accrue to the Contractor [@CL-CMP-4261], while the License Grant clause grants a paid up license [@CL-CMP-4250]. For royalty costs the Contractor pays to others, see [Royalties and other costs for use of patents](../cas-allowability/cmp-patent-royalties.md). The clauses on reporting and readjusting royalties for patent license contracts are in [Patent license and release contracts](cmp-dfars-patent-license-release-and-royalty-reporting.md). For the License Grant clause under which the Contractor grants a paid up license [@CL-CMP-4250], see [Patent license grant, term and termination](cmp-dfars-patent-license-grant-term-and-termination.md). For the release of past infringement and the license to other Government agencies, see [Non-estoppel, release and license to other agencies](cmp-dfars-non-estoppel-release-and-license-to-other-agencies.md).
