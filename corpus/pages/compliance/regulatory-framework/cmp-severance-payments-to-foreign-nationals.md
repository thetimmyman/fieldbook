## Purpose

Explains the limit on the cost allowability of severance payments to foreign nationals under a service contract performed outside the United States, the matters the agency determines before waiving it, and the waiver clause [@CL-CMP-5618; @CL-CMP-5623].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5618; @CL-CMP-5762] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.237-8 and FAR 52.237-9 [@CL-CMP-5618; @CL-CMP-5762] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.237-8 and RFO FAR 52.237-9 [@CL-CMP-6031; @CL-CMP-6038] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5618; @CL-CMP-5762] |
| Applies to | Service contracts performed outside the United States, through the provision and the waiver clause used as prescribed [@CL-CMP-5619; @CL-CMP-5618; @CL-CMP-5762] |

The FAR limits the cost allowability of severance payments to foreign nationals employed under a service contract performed outside the United States unless the agency grants a waiver pursuant to FAR 37.113-1 before contract award [@CL-CMP-5619].

## How it works

In making the determination concerning the granting of a waiver, the agency will determine that the matters listed in paragraph (b) hold [@CL-CMP-5761]. One is that applying the severance pay limitations would adversely affect the continuation of a program, project or activity providing significant support services for members of the armed forces stationed or deployed outside the United States, or for employees of an executive agency posted outside the United States [@CL-CMP-5620]. Another is that the contractor has taken, or has established plans to take, appropriate actions within its control to minimize the amount and number of severance payments to foreign-national employees under the contract [@CL-CMP-5621]; and a third is that payment is necessary to comply with a law generally applicable to a significant number of businesses in the country where the foreign national performed services, or to comply with a collective bargaining agreement [@CL-CMP-7044; @CL-CMP-5622].

In the waiver clause, the cost allowability limitations in FAR 31.205-6(g)(6) are waived under the statutory provision that the clause cites, as applicable [@CL-CMP-5623]. The waiver clause may be incorporated into subcontracts issued under the contract, if approved by the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-5763].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO provision says the FAR limits the cost allowability of severance payments to foreign nationals employed under a service contract performed outside the United States unless the agency grants a waiver before contract award, but it names FAR 31.205-6(f)(6) for the limit and FAR 37.802-3 for the waiver [@CL-CMP-6031; @CL-CMP-5619]. It keeps the lead-in to the matters the agency determines [@CL-CMP-6032; @CL-CMP-5761], the program support matter [@CL-CMP-6033; @CL-CMP-6034; @CL-CMP-5620], the contractor's actions to minimize severance payments [@CL-CMP-6035; @CL-CMP-5621] and the compliance matter [@CL-CMP-7045; @CL-CMP-7044; @CL-CMP-6036; @CL-CMP-6037; @CL-CMP-5622]. In the waiver clause, the RFO text waives the cost allowability limitations in FAR 31.205-6(f)(6) under the same statutory provisions [@CL-CMP-6038; @CL-CMP-5623], and it keeps that the clause may be incorporated into subcontracts if approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-6039; @CL-CMP-5763].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A service contract performed abroad covers 60 local employees, and severance would cost 300,000 in one year. The agency determines that applying the limit would adversely affect continuation of a program providing significant support services to deployed armed forces members, that the contractor has a plan to cut the amount and number of severance payments, and that a local law generally applicable to a significant number of businesses requires the payments. The waiver is granted before award and the waiver clause is included, so the limitation no longer restricts the allowability of the 300,000. The contractor may incorporate the waiver clause into a subcontract if the contracting officer approves.

## Never confuse

A limit on cost allowability is a limit on cost, not a statement about the payment itself. The limit applies unless the agency grants a waiver before contract award [@CL-CMP-5619]. The waiver rests on the matters the agency determines [@CL-CMP-5761; @CL-CMP-5620], and the waiver clause states that the cost allowability limitations are waived [@CL-CMP-5623]. The waiver clause may be incorporated into a subcontract if the contracting officer approves, so approval is a separate step [@CL-CMP-5763].

The codified waiver clause waives the limitations in FAR 31.205-6(g)(6) [@CL-CMP-5623], while the RFO waiver clause waives those in FAR 31.205-6(f)(6) [@CL-CMP-6038].

The DFARS clause on compliance with local labor laws overseas is covered in [Compliance with local labor laws overseas (DFARS clause)](cmp-dfars-local-labor-laws-overseas.md).
