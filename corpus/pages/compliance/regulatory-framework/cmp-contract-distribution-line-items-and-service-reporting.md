## Purpose

Shows which contract attachments go to the Electronic Data Access (EDA) system, which documents and contracts the line item numbering procedures cover, and when a contractor must report on services performed in the System for Award Management (SAM).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7251] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 204.270-1 [@CL-CMP-7251]; DFARS 204.7102 [@CL-CMP-7253]; DFARS 204.1703 [@CL-CMP-7254] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7251] |
| Applies to | Contract attachments and posted contract documents [@CL-CMP-7251; @CL-CMP-7432]; contracts with separate contract line item numbers [@CL-CMP-7253]; contracts and orders for services in the listed portfolio groups above $3 million [@CL-CMP-7254] |

Contract attachments must be uploaded to EDA, except attachments that are classified or too sensitive for widespread distribution, such as personally identifiable information and Privacy Act and Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act material [@CL-CMP-7251]. An attachment that cannot be practicably converted to electronic format, such as samples, drawings and models, is also excepted [@CL-CMP-7430]. Section J, or a similar location when the Uniform Contract Format is not used, must include the annotation "provided under separate cover" for any attachment not uploaded to EDA [@CL-CMP-7252].

When posting documents, including contractual instruments, to EDA, agencies are responsible for the timely distribution of documents and for internal controls [@CL-CMP-7432]. Those controls must ensure that the electronic version of a contract document and the contract data in EDA accurately represent the contract [@CL-CMP-7433].

## How it works

The line item numbering procedures apply to all solicitations, solicitation line and subline item numbers, contracts as defined in FAR Subpart 2.1, and contract line and subline item numbers [@CL-CMP-7434]. They also apply to all exhibits, exhibit line items and any other document expected to become part of the contract [@CL-CMP-7437].

The procedures are mandatory for all contracts where separate contract line item numbers are assigned [@CL-CMP-7253]. There are two exceptions. One is an indefinite-delivery contract for petroleum products against which posts, camps and stations issue delivery orders for products to be consumed by them [@CL-CMP-7253]. The other is a communications service authorization issued by the Defense Information Systems Agency's Defense Information Technology Contracting Organization [@CL-CMP-7431].

Service contractor reporting in SAM is required when a contract or order has a total estimated value, including options, that exceeds $3 million and is for services in the listed service acquisition portfolio groups [@CL-CMP-7254]. The groups are logistics management services, equipment-related services, knowledge-based services, and electronics and communications services, and PGI 204.1703 lists the applicable product and service codes [@CL-CMP-7435].

The basic and the alternate of the clause at DFARS 252.204-7023 require contractors to report annually, by October 31, on the services performed under the contract or order, including any first-tier subcontracts, during the preceding Government fiscal year [@CL-CMP-7255]. If the agency believes that revisions to the contractor-reported information are warranted, it must notify the contractor [@CL-CMP-7436].

For an indefinite-delivery contract, basic ordering agreement or blanket purchase agreement, contractor reporting is required for each order that meets the requirements for contracts and orders above [@CL-CMP-7256]. Service contract reporting is not required for the basic contract or agreement [@CL-CMP-7438].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract for knowledge-based services has a base value of 2 million dollars and options worth 1.5 million dollars, so its total estimated value including options is 3.5 million dollars, above the $3 million threshold, and service contractor reporting in SAM is required [@CL-CMP-7254; @CL-CMP-7435]. The contractor reports by October 31 on the services performed in the preceding Government fiscal year, including those of its first-tier subcontractors [@CL-CMP-7255].

An indefinite-delivery contract for the same services has one order worth 4 million dollars and one worth 1 million dollars. The 4 million dollar order meets the threshold and is reported, the 1 million dollar order does not meet it, and the basic contract is not reported [@CL-CMP-7256; @CL-CMP-7254; @CL-CMP-7438].

Among the attachments to the first contract are a spreadsheet of personally identifiable information that is too sensitive for widespread distribution and a set of physical samples that cannot practicably be converted to electronic format. Neither is uploaded to EDA, and Section J carries the annotation "provided under separate cover" for each [@CL-CMP-7251; @CL-CMP-7430; @CL-CMP-7252].

## Never confuse

Service contractor reporting is required when a contract or order has a total estimated value, including options, that exceeds $3 million and is for services in the listed portfolio groups [@CL-CMP-7254]. For indefinite-delivery contracts, contractor reporting is required for each order that meets those requirements [@CL-CMP-7256], and service contract reporting is not required for the basic contract or agreement [@CL-CMP-7438].

An attachment left out of EDA is marked in Section J as provided under separate cover [@CL-CMP-7252]. Contract distribution, electronic commerce and service reporting is covered in [Contract distribution, electronic commerce and service reporting](../trade-cyber/cmp-contract-distribution-e-commerce-and-service-reporting.md).
