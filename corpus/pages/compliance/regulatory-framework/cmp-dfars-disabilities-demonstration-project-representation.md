## Purpose

Shows what the offeror represents under the demonstration project provision and the conditions that make a contractor eligible.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4459] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.226-7002 [@CL-CMP-4459] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4459] |
| Applies to | Solicitations with the provision, used as prescribed in section 226.7203, which are issued pursuant to the Demonstration Project [@CL-CMP-4459; @CL-CMP-4482] |

The solicitation containing the provision is issued pursuant to the Demonstration Project for Contractors Employing Persons with Disabilities [@CL-CMP-4482]. The offeror represents that it either is or is not an eligible contractor as defined in paragraph (a) of the provision [@CL-CMP-4464]. The purpose of the Demonstration Project is to provide defense contracting opportunities for entities that employ severely disabled individuals, and to be eligible for award an offeror must be an eligible contractor as defined in paragraph (a) of the provision [@CL-CMP-4463].

An eligible contractor is a business entity operated on a for-profit or nonprofit basis that, among other listed conditions, employs severely disabled individuals at a rate that averages not less than 33 percent of its total workforce over the 12-month period prior to issuance of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-4460]. It also pays not less than the minimum wage prescribed pursuant to section 206 of title 29 of the United States Code to the employees who are severely disabled individuals [@CL-CMP-4461]. It also provides, for its employees, health insurance and a retirement plan comparable to those provided for employees by business entities of similar size in its industrial sector or geographic region [@CL-CMP-4465].

## How it works

The offeror makes one choice in the representation: it is an eligible contractor, or it is not [@CL-CMP-4464]. The choice is tested against the definition, which has three conditions on workforce share, wages and benefits [@CL-CMP-4460; @CL-CMP-4461; @CL-CMP-4465]. Eligibility is required for award under the demonstration project [@CL-CMP-4463]. The definition of a severely disabled individual is an individual with a disability, as defined in section 12102 of title 42 of the United States Code, who has a severe physical or mental impairment that seriously limits one or more functional capacities [@CL-CMP-4462].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror has 100 employees. Over the 12 months before the solicitation, severely disabled individuals averaged 30 of them. They are paid at least the minimum wage, and the offeror gives health insurance and a retirement plan comparable to its sector. Answer: 30 of 100 is 30 percent, which is below 33 percent, so the offeror marks "is not" an eligible contractor. Assumed for the example: the 30 are all severely disabled individuals as defined and the 12-month average is 30.

## Never confuse

The eligibility test is about the business and its workforce share over a stated period [@CL-CMP-4460]. The definition of a severely disabled individual is about a person [@CL-CMP-4462]. The representation is the offeror's statement, and the definition is the test that statement is measured against. The drug-free work force clause is a different kind of text: it sets program duties for a Contractor's own employees, and it is not an eligibility test for award; see [Drug-free work force](cmp-dfars-drug-free-work-force.md).
