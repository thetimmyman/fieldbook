## Purpose

Sets out which procurement instruments the line item and subline item rules reach, including amendments, modifications and change orders to them [@CL-CMP-8286; @CL-CMP-8597].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8286] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1002 [@CL-CMP-8286]; FAR 4.1000 [@CL-CMP-8597; @CL-CMP-8598] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8286] |
| Applies to | Solicitations, contracts, agreements with pre-priced supplies or services, and task and delivery orders, with their amendments, modifications and change orders [@CL-CMP-8286; @CL-CMP-8287; @CL-CMP-8288; @CL-CMP-8289; @CL-CMP-8290] |

The subpart on line items prescribes policies and procedures for assigning line items and subline items and their identifiers [@CL-CMP-8597]. Its applicability section says the policies of the subpart shall apply to the listed procurement instruments, and that they reach amendments, modifications, and change orders to those instruments [@CL-CMP-8286]. Four kinds of instrument are listed. Solicitations are covered [@CL-CMP-8287]. Contracts are covered, including, but not limited to, Governmentwide acquisition contracts, multi-agency contracts, Federal Supply Schedule contracts, indefinite-delivery contracts, and purchase orders, so the contract list is not exhaustive [@CL-CMP-8288]. Agreements that include pre-priced supplies or services are covered [@CL-CMP-8289]. Task and delivery orders are covered [@CL-CMP-8290]. The applicability section states no exclusion [@CL-CMP-8286].

## How it works

The applicability section works as a coverage list rather than as a set of duties: it states that the line item policies shall apply and then enumerates the instruments [@CL-CMP-8286]. It does not say who applies the policies, and it names no approving official and sets no threshold or exception, and it does not tie coverage to the value of the instrument [@CL-CMP-8286; @CL-CMP-8288]. The scope section gives agencies until October 1, 2019, to apply the requirements of 4.1002 through 4.1008 so that they have time to transition their information systems [@CL-CMP-8598]. Because the reach extends to amendments, modifications and change orders, a later change to a covered instrument stays inside the same policies [@CL-CMP-8286]. The policies apply to the listed procurement instruments [@CL-CMP-8286], and purchase orders are among the contracts listed [@CL-CMP-8288]. For what a line item and a subline item are and when a separate line item is needed, see [Separately identified line items and subline items](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-line-items-and-subline-items.md). For the data each line item carries, see [Contract line items: subline items and required data elements](../../market/feeds/mkt-contract-line-items-and-data-elements.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A task order is placed against a multiple-award contract with a ceiling of 50 million. The task order is a covered instrument, so its supplies or services are set out as line items and, as needed, subline items [@CL-CMP-8290; @CL-CMP-8597]. A later modification to that same task order is covered too, because the policies reach modifications to covered instruments [@CL-CMP-8286]. A purchase order for 2,000 is also covered, because purchase orders are among the contracts listed [@CL-CMP-8288].

## Never confuse

The line item rules are not the identifier rules for the procurement instrument itself: the subpart on line items covers the identifiers of line items and subline items, while the separate subpart on procurement instrument identifiers covers the unique identifier of each solicitation, contract, agreement, or order [@CL-CMP-8597; @CL-CMP-8599]. This card covers the first of those two; for the second, see [Procurement instrument identifiers and supplementary identifiers](../trade-cyber/cmp-piid-and-supplementary-piid.md). The part-wide definitions of a unique identifier and a supplementary identifier are on [Scope of FAR part 4 and its identifier definitions](cmp-part-4-administrative-matters-scope-and-definitions.md) [@CL-CMP-8283; @CL-CMP-8284].

Alternative line item proposals, and the market research duty before certain subcontract awards, are covered in [Market research and alternative line item proposals](../../estimating/pricing/est-market-research-and-alternative-line-items.md).
