## Purpose

Shows the license the Government receives in technical data for commercial products and services developed exclusively at private expense, the data it may use without restriction, and the limits on release outside the Government.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3783] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7015 [@CL-CMP-3783] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3783] |
| Applies to | Technical data on any portion of a commercial product or commercial service developed exclusively at private expense [@CL-CMP-3783] |

The clause governs the technical data pertaining to any portion of a commercial product or commercial service that was developed exclusively at private expense [@CL-CMP-3783]. The Government shall have the unrestricted right to use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data, and to permit others to do so, that fall within any of the clause's listed categories [@CL-CMP-3784]. Three of the listed categories of technical data are form, fit, and function data; a correction or change to technical data furnished to the Contractor by the Government; and data necessary for operation, maintenance, installation, or training, other than detailed manufacturing or process data [@CL-CMP-3784; @CL-CMP-3785].

Except as provided in paragraph (c)(1) of the clause, the Government may use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data within the Government only, and the Government shall not use the technical data to manufacture additional quantities of the commercial products [@CL-CMP-3786], or release, perform, display, disclose, or authorize use of the technical data outside the Government without the Contractor's written permission unless a release, disclosure, or permitted use is necessary for emergency repair or overhaul of the commercial products furnished under the contract, or for performance of work by covered Government support contractors [@CL-CMP-3786; @CL-CMP-3787; @CL-CMP-3831].

Under paragraph (e), the Contractor agrees that the Government, and other persons to whom the Government may have released or disclosed technical data delivered or otherwise furnished under the contract, shall have no liability for any release or disclosure of technical data that are not marked to indicate that such data are licensed data subject to use, modification, reproduction, release, performance, display, or disclosure restrictions [@CL-CMP-0441; @CL-CMP-3830]. For the marking rules of the clause that governs noncommercial items, see [DoD rights in technical data: markings](cmp-dod-technical-data-markings.md).

## How it works

Except as provided in paragraph (c)(1) of the clause, the Government may use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data within the Government only [@CL-CMP-3786]. Release, performance, display, disclosure, or authorized use of the technical data outside the Government requires the Contractor's written permission, unless a release, disclosure, or permitted use is necessary for emergency repair or overhaul of the commercial products furnished under the contract, or for performance of work by covered Government support contractors [@CL-CMP-3786; @CL-CMP-3787; @CL-CMP-3831]. The Contractor agrees that the Government, and other persons to whom the Government may have released or disclosed technical data delivered or otherwise furnished under the contract, shall have no liability for any release or disclosure of technical data that are not marked to indicate that such data are licensed data subject to use, modification, reproduction, release, performance, display, or disclosure restrictions [@CL-CMP-0441; @CL-CMP-3830]. The Government has the unrestricted right to use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data in the listed categories, and to permit others to do so [@CL-CMP-3784; @CL-CMP-3785]. Except as provided in paragraph (b)(2) of DFARS 252.227-7013, that clause governs all technical data pertaining to other than commercial products or commercial services or to any portion of a commercial product or commercial service that was developed in any part at Government expense [@CL-CMP-3759].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial pump was developed entirely with the supplier's own funds, and the contract delivers its form, fit, and function data and its detailed manufacturing data. The Government may use the form, fit, and function data without restriction. It may use the manufacturing data inside the Government, but it may not use that data to make more pumps. It may not give the manufacturing data to an outside firm to make pumps without the supplier's written permission. The Government also is not liable for releasing a drawing that the supplier delivered without any marking to show it is licensed data subject to restrictions.
