## Purpose

Sets out how a FAR provision or clause, a supplemental provision or clause, and a text used with an authorized deviation are identified in a solicitation or contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8857] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.103 [@CL-CMP-8857] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.103 [@CL-CMP-1287; @CL-CMP-1288] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8857] |
| Applies to | FAR provisions and clauses, and provisions and clauses that supplement the FAR, used in solicitations and contracts by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8857; @CL-CMP-8860] |

Whenever a FAR provision or clause is used without deviation in a solicitation or contract, whether it is incorporated by reference or in full text, it shall be identified by number, title and date [@CL-CMP-8857]. The same identification shall also be used where a FAR provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation, except that the contracting officer shall then insert "(DEVIATION)" after the date [@CL-CMP-8858].

Any provision or clause that supplements the FAR shall be clearly identified by number, title, date and name of the regulation, whether it is incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8860]. When a supplemental provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation, "(DEVIATION)" is inserted after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8861]. A provision or clause developed for use at a suborganizational level of an agency, not meant for repetitive use, but intended to meet the needs of an individual acquisition and, thus, impractical to include in either an agency or suborganization acquisition regulation [@CL-CMP-8883], shall be identified by the title, date and the name of the agency or suborganization within the agency that developed it [@CL-CMP-8862].

## How it works

Whenever any FAR or supplemental provision is used with an authorized deviation, the contracting officer shall identify it by the same number, title and date assigned to the provision when it is used without deviation, include the regulation name for any supplemental provision, except that the contracting officer shall insert "(DEVIATION)" after the date of the provision [@CL-CMP-8912; @CL-CMP-8913]. The same applies to any FAR or supplemental clause used with an authorized deviation, with "(DEVIATION)" inserted after the date of the clause [@CL-CMP-8914; @CL-CMP-8915]. The card on [what counts as a deviation](cmp-what-counts-as-a-far-deviation.md) covers what a deviation is.

Solicited firms and contractors will be advised of the meaning of the marker through the provision at 52.252-5, Authorized Deviations in Provisions, or the clause at 52.252-6, Authorized Deviations in Clauses [@CL-CMP-8859]. Those two texts are prescribed in 52.107(e) and (f) [@CL-CMP-8904]. The contracting officer shall insert the provision in solicitations that include any FAR or supplemental provision with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8878], and the clause in solicitations and contracts that include any FAR or supplemental clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8879]. The card on [the provisions and clauses prescribed in this subpart](cmp-provisions-and-clauses-prescribed-in-subpart-52-1.md) covers them, and the card on [contract alterations and authorized deviations](cmp-contract-alterations-and-authorized-deviations.md) covers their text.

For a supplemental provision or clause used with an authorized deviation, 52.103(b) says to insert "(DEVIATION)" after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8861], and the contracting officer's insertion rule in 52.107(e) and (f) says to include the regulation name and insert "(DEVIATION)" after the date of the provision or clause [@CL-CMP-8913; @CL-CMP-8915].

For supplemental texts, except those of the individual-acquisition kind covered by paragraph (c), the section gives two hypothetical examples of how they shall be identified [@CL-CMP-8863]. In the first, part 14 of the X Agency Acquisition Regulation, published in the Federal Register and codified as Chapter 99 in 48 CFR, prescribes the use of a provision entitled Bid Envelopes, dated October 1983 [@CL-CMP-7837], and if that provision is sequentially the first provision or clause appearing in Section 52.214 of that regulation, then the identification of that provision shall be 9952.214-70, Bid Envelopes (OCT 1983) [@CL-CMP-8905]. A supplemental provision or clause included in an agency acquisition regulation that is published in the Federal Register and codified in 48 CFR has its number preceded by the chapter number within 48 CFR assigned by the CFR staff [@CL-CMP-8842]. In the second, Y, a major organizational element of the X Agency, is authorized to issue the Y Acquisition Regulation, which is not published in the Federal Register and codified in 48 CFR [@CL-CMP-8906]. If part 36 of that regulation prescribes the use of a clause entitled Refrigerated Display Cases, dated March 1983, pertaining to a specialized type of construction work [@CL-CMP-7838], and that clause is sequentially the second provision or clause appearing in Section 52.236, then the identification of that clause shall be 52.236-71, Refrigerated Display Cases (MAR 1983), Y Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CMP-8907].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs it, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.103 keeps the rule that a FAR provision or clause used without deviation, whether incorporated by reference or in full text, is identified by number, title and date [@CL-CMP-1287; @CL-CMP-8857]. It keeps the rule that the contracting officer inserts "(DEVIATION)" after the date when the provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-1288; @CL-CMP-8858]. It keeps the pointer to the Authorized Deviations in Provisions provision at 52.252-5 and the Authorized Deviations in Clauses clause at 52.252-6 [@CL-CMP-1289; @CL-CMP-8859], prescribed in 52.107(e) and (f) [@CL-CMP-1290; @CL-CMP-8904]. It keeps the rule that a supplemental provision or clause is identified by number, title, date and name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-1291; @CL-CMP-8860], with "(DEVIATION)" inserted after the name of the regulation when it is used with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-1292; @CL-CMP-8861]. It keeps the identification of a text developed for an individual acquisition by title, date and the name of the agency or suborganization that developed it [@CL-CMP-1293; @CL-CMP-8862]. It keeps the identification each of its two hypothetical examples produces, 9952.214-70, Bid Envelopes, in the first and 52.236-71, Refrigerated Display Cases, Y Acquisition Regulation, in the second [@CL-CMP-1294; @CL-CMP-8905; @CL-CMP-1295; @CL-CMP-8907].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer uses a FAR clause without deviation in a contract, so the contract identifies it by its number, title and date, whether it is incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8857]. A second FAR clause is used with an authorized deviation. It keeps its number, title and date, and the contracting officer inserts "(DEVIATION)" after the date [@CL-CMP-8858]. The contract also includes the Authorized Deviations in Clauses clause, which tells the contractor what the marker means [@CL-CMP-8859; @CL-CMP-8879].

A third clause comes from a Z Acquisition Regulation that is not published in the Federal Register and codified in 48 CFR. It is the second text in section 52.236 of that regulation, titled "Site Badges" and dated JUN 2020. Following the section's second hypothetical, it is identified as 52.236-71, Site Badges (JUN 2020), Z Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CMP-8906; @CL-CMP-8907]. If it were used with an authorized deviation, paragraph (b) would place the marker after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8861], while the contracting officer's insertion rule would place it after the date [@CL-CMP-8915]. The card does not resolve the two.

## Never confuse

The "(DEVIATION)" marker is inserted after the date where a FAR provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8858].

A supplemental provision or clause is identified by number, title, date and name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8860], and a text developed for an individual acquisition is identified by title, date and the name of the agency or suborganization that developed it [@CL-CMP-8862].
