## Purpose

Sets out how a FAR provision or clause, a supplemental provision or clause, and a text used with an authorized deviation are identified in a solicitation or contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8857] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.103 [@CL-CMP-8857] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8857] |
| Applies to | FAR provisions and clauses, and provisions and clauses that supplement the FAR, used in solicitations and contracts by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8857; @CL-CMP-8860] |

Whenever a FAR provision or clause is used without deviation in a solicitation or contract, whether it is incorporated by reference or in full text, it shall be identified by number, title and date [@CL-CMP-8857]. The same identification shall also be used where a FAR provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation, except that the contracting officer shall then insert "(DEVIATION)" after the date [@CL-CMP-8858].

Any provision or clause that supplements the FAR shall be clearly identified by number, title, date and name of the regulation, whether it is incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8860]. When a supplemental provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation, "(DEVIATION)" is inserted after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8861]. A provision or clause developed at a suborganizational level for an individual acquisition, not meant for repetitive use [@CL-CMP-8883], shall be identified by the title, date and the name of the agency or suborganization within the agency that developed it [@CL-CMP-8862].

## How it works

The identification travels with the text however it is brought in, so a text incorporated by reference is still identified by number, title and date [@CL-CMP-8857].

For a deviated FAR text the number, title and date stay as they are without the deviation, and the contracting officer inserts the marker after the date [@CL-CMP-8858]. Where a FAR or supplemental provision is used with an authorized deviation, the contracting officer shall identify it by the same number, title and date assigned to it when it is used without deviation [@CL-CMP-8912], and the same is said of a clause [@CL-CMP-8914]. The section does not say who authorizes a deviation. The card on [what counts as a deviation](cmp-what-counts-as-a-far-deviation.md) covers what a deviation is.

Solicited firms and contractors will be advised of the meaning of the marker through the provision at 52.252-5, Authorized Deviations in Provisions, or the clause at 52.252-6, Authorized Deviations in Clauses [@CL-CMP-8859]. Those two texts are prescribed in 52.107(e) and (f) [@CL-CMP-8904]. The contracting officer shall insert the provision in solicitations that include any FAR or supplemental provision with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8878], and the clause in solicitations and contracts that include any FAR or supplemental clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8879]. The card on [the provisions and clauses prescribed in this subpart](cmp-provisions-and-clauses-prescribed-in-subpart-52-1.md) covers them, and the card on [contract alterations and authorized deviations](cmp-contract-alterations-and-authorized-deviations.md) covers their text.

Where the marker goes for a supplemental text is stated two ways. This section puts it after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8861], while the contracting officer's insertion rule for deviated provisions and clauses puts it after the date [@CL-CMP-8913; @CL-CMP-8915]. This card reports both and does not resolve them.

For supplemental texts, except those of the individual-acquisition kind covered by paragraph (c), the section gives two hypothetical examples of how they shall be identified [@CL-CMP-8863]. In the first, a provision that is sequentially the first provision or clause in section 52.214 of the X Agency Acquisition Regulation is identified as 9952.214-70, Bid Envelopes (OCT 1983) [@CL-CMP-8905]. The chapter number comes first where the regulation is published in the Federal Register and codified in 48 CFR [@CL-CMP-8842]. In the second, the Y Acquisition Regulation is not published in the Federal Register and codified in 48 CFR [@CL-CMP-8906], and a clause that is sequentially the second provision or clause in section 52.236 is identified as 52.236-71, Refrigerated Display Cases (MAR 1983), Y Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CMP-8907]. The two identifications differ in whether the regulation name is written after the date, and the section does not say why [@CL-CMP-8905; @CL-CMP-8907].

The section sets no threshold or deadline.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer uses a FAR clause without deviation in a contract, so the contract identifies it by its number, title and date, whether it is incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8857]. A second FAR clause is used with an authorized deviation. It keeps its number, title and date, and the contracting officer inserts "(DEVIATION)" after the date [@CL-CMP-8858]. The contract also includes the Authorized Deviations in Clauses clause, which tells the contractor what the marker means [@CL-CMP-8859; @CL-CMP-8879].

A third clause comes from a Z Acquisition Regulation that is not published in the Federal Register and codified in 48 CFR. It is the second text in section 52.236 of that regulation, titled "Site Badges" and dated JUN 2020. Following the section's second hypothetical, it is identified as 52.236-71, Site Badges (JUN 2020), Z Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CMP-8906; @CL-CMP-8907]. If it were used with an authorized deviation, paragraph (b) would place the marker after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8861], while the contracting officer's insertion rule would place it after the date [@CL-CMP-8915]. The card does not resolve the two.

## Never confuse

A deviation marker is used where the deviation is authorized [@CL-CMP-8858]. The section does not say who authorizes it.

A supplement is not a FAR text under another number. It is identified by number, title, date and name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-8860], and a text developed for an individual acquisition is identified by title, date and the name of the agency or suborganization that developed it [@CL-CMP-8862].
