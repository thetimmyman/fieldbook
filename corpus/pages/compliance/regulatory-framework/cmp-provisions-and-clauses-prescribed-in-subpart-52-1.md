## Purpose

Lists the reference-incorporation and alteration texts and the two deviation texts that the subpart prescribes, who inserts each, where, and the exceptions.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8874] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.107 [@CL-CMP-8874] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.107 [@CL-CMP-2108] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8874] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that incorporate provisions or clauses by reference, carry alterations, or use a FAR or supplemental provision or clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8874; @CL-CMP-8875; @CL-CMP-8876; @CL-CMP-8877; @CL-CMP-8878; @CL-CMP-8879] |

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.252-1, Solicitation Provisions Incorporated by Reference, in solicitations in order to incorporate provisions by reference [@CL-CMP-8874]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.252-2, Clauses Incorporated by Reference, in solicitations and contracts in order to incorporate clauses by reference [@CL-CMP-8875].

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.252-3, Alterations in Solicitation, in solicitations to revise or supplement, as necessary, other parts of the solicitation that apply to the solicitation phase only, except for any provision authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-8876]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.252-4, Alterations in Contract, in solicitations and contracts to revise or supplement, as necessary, other parts of the contract, or parts of the solicitations that apply to the contract phase, except for any clause authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-8877].

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.252-5, Authorized Deviations in Provisions, in solicitations that include any FAR or supplemental provision with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8878]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.252-6, Authorized Deviations in Clauses, in solicitations and contracts that include any FAR or supplemental clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8879].

Whenever a FAR or supplemental provision is used with an authorized deviation, the contracting officer shall identify it by the same number, title and date assigned to the provision when it is used without deviation [@CL-CMP-8912]. The identification includes the regulation name for any supplemental provision, except that the contracting officer shall insert "(DEVIATION)" after the date of the provision [@CL-CMP-8913]. The same is said of a clause used with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8914; @CL-CMP-8915].

## How it works

Every insertion and identification duty in the section falls on the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-8874; @CL-CMP-8875; @CL-CMP-8876; @CL-CMP-8877; @CL-CMP-8878; @CL-CMP-8879; @CL-CMP-8912; @CL-CMP-8914]. The section sets no threshold or deadline, and it does not name who authorizes a deviation.

The two incorporation texts are the mechanism for incorporation by reference in a solicitation or contract. The provision is inserted in solicitations, and the clause in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-8874; @CL-CMP-8875]. The permission in 52.102(b) to incorporate by reference excludes the provisions and clauses prescribed in this section [@CL-CMP-8851]. The rules on what may be incorporated, and when full text is required, are on the card for [incorporating provisions and clauses by reference](cmp-incorporating-provisions-and-clauses-by-reference.md).

The alteration texts carry revisions and supplements, and the prescription of each carries an exception for any provision (for the solicitation text) or any clause (for the contract text) authorized for use with a deviation; the section does not elaborate on that exception [@CL-CMP-8876; @CL-CMP-8877]. Provisions or clauses may not be incorporated by reference by being listed in the alterations provision or clause [@CL-CMP-8856].

The deviation provision is inserted in solicitations that include a FAR or supplemental provision with an authorized deviation, the deviation clause in solicitations and contracts that include such a clause, and solicited firms and contractors are advised of the meaning of the "(DEVIATION)" marker through them [@CL-CMP-8878; @CL-CMP-8879; @CL-CMP-8859]. How a marked text is identified is on the card for [identifying provisions and clauses](cmp-identifying-provisions-and-clauses.md). What each of these texts says is on the cards for [incorporation by reference and solicitation alterations](cmp-incorporation-by-reference-and-solicitation-alterations.md) and for [contract alterations and authorized deviations](cmp-contract-alterations-and-authorized-deviations.md).

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page (the RFO text) in place of the codified part 52 text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO section 52.107 keeps the contracting officer's duty to insert the provision at 52.252-1 in solicitations in order to incorporate provisions by reference, and the clause at 52.252-2 in solicitations and contracts in order to incorporate clauses by reference [@CL-CMP-2108; @CL-CMP-2109; @CL-CMP-8874; @CL-CMP-8875]. It keeps the duty to insert the alterations provision in solicitations and the alterations clause in solicitations and contracts, each with the exception for a provision or clause authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-2110; @CL-CMP-2111; @CL-CMP-8876; @CL-CMP-8877]. It keeps the duty to insert the authorized deviations provision in solicitations that include any FAR or supplemental provision with an authorized deviation, and the authorized deviations clause in solicitations and contracts that include any FAR or supplemental clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-2112; @CL-CMP-2115; @CL-CMP-8878; @CL-CMP-8879]. It keeps the identification rule for a provision or clause used with an authorized deviation: the same number, title and date it has without the deviation, the regulation name for any supplemental provision or clause, and "(DEVIATION)" after the date [@CL-CMP-2113; @CL-CMP-2114; @CL-CMP-2116; @CL-CMP-2117; @CL-CMP-8912; @CL-CMP-8913; @CL-CMP-8914; @CL-CMP-8915].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation lists several provisions and clauses by reference. The contracting officer inserts the Solicitation Provisions Incorporated by Reference provision in the solicitation for the provisions [@CL-CMP-8874] and the Clauses Incorporated by Reference clause in the solicitation and the resulting contract for the clauses [@CL-CMP-8875].

One clause is used with an authorized deviation, so the contracting officer also inserts the Authorized Deviations in Clauses clause in the solicitation and the contract [@CL-CMP-8879]. The clause is identified by the number, title and date it has without the deviation, with "(DEVIATION)" inserted after the date [@CL-CMP-8858; @CL-CMP-8914; @CL-CMP-8915].

The solicitation also needs a change to an instruction that applies to the solicitation phase only, so the contracting officer inserts the Alterations in Solicitation provision [@CL-CMP-8876]. The contracting officer does not use the Alterations in Contract clause for the deviated clause, because that clause's prescription excepts any clause authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-8877]. Nor does the contracting officer list a clause in the Alterations in Contract clause as a way to incorporate it by reference [@CL-CMP-8856].

## Never confuse

A deviation notice text is not the deviation itself. It is the text that goes into a solicitation or contract that includes a FAR or supplemental provision or clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-8878; @CL-CMP-8879].

An alterations text is not a container for reference-incorporated clauses. Listing a clause there does not incorporate it by reference [@CL-CMP-8856].

The provision for provisions incorporated by reference is inserted in solicitations, while the clause for clauses incorporated by reference is inserted in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-8874; @CL-CMP-8875].
