## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must tell the Government when work is planned at a secondary site of the work, and how a wage determination is obtained for that site.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5860] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-5 [@CL-CMP-5860] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5860] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that carry the provision as prescribed at FAR 22.407(h) [@CL-CMP-5860] |

The offeror notifies the Government when it intends to perform work at any secondary site of the work, as that term is defined in the Construction Wage Rate Requirements clause of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-5861]. If the offeror is unsure whether a planned site meets the criteria, it requests a determination from the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-5862].

## How it works

Where the wage determination given for the primary site does not apply to the secondary site, the offeror requests a wage determination from the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-5863]. The request does not move the closing date, because the due date for offers is not extended for it [@CL-CMP-5864].

A wage determination that is incorporated for the secondary site is effective from the first day on which work under the contract was performed at that site, and it is incorporated without any adjustment in contract price or estimated cost [@CL-CMP-6131].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror plans to build 3 of 10 modules at a yard in the United States that it leases only for this project, and the offeror treats those modules as a significant portion of the work. The yard is a planned secondary site of the work, so the offeror notifies the Government and, because the wage determination for the primary site does not apply there, requests a wage determination for it. If the offeror were unsure whether the yard meets the criteria, it would instead request a determination from the Contracting Officer. The closing date stays where it was.

## Never confuse

A secondary site is not any off-site yard. It is a site where a significant portion of the building or work is constructed, located in the United States and established specifically for the performance of the contract or project [@CL-CMP-5818]. A request for a wage determination is also not a request for more time, because the offer due date is not extended for it [@CL-CMP-5864].
