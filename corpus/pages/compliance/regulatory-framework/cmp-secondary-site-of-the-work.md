## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must tell the Government when work is planned at a secondary site of the work, and how a wage determination is obtained for that site.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5860] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-5 [@CL-CMP-5860] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-5 [@CL-CMP-5332; @CL-CMP-5333; @CL-CMP-5662; @CL-CMP-5663; @CL-CMP-6202] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5860] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that carry the provision as prescribed at FAR 22.407(h) [@CL-CMP-5860] |

The offeror shall notify the Government if it intends to perform work at any secondary site of the work, as defined in paragraph (a)(1)(ii) of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements clause at 52.222-6 [@CL-CMP-5861]. If the offeror is unsure whether a planned work site satisfies the criteria for a secondary site of the work, it shall request a determination from the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-5862].

## How it works

If the wage determination the Government provided for the primary site is not applicable to the secondary site, the offeror shall request a wage determination from the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-5863]. The request does not move the closing date, because the due date for offers is not extended for it [@CL-CMP-5864].

Any wage determination incorporated for a secondary site of the work shall be effective from the first day on which work under the contract was performed at that site and shall be incorporated without any adjustment in contract price or estimated cost [@CL-CMP-6131].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO provision keeps these offeror rules: the offeror must notify the Government if it intends to perform work at any secondary site of the work [@CL-CMP-5332; @CL-CMP-5861], and must request a determination from the Contracting Officer if it is unsure whether a planned site meets the criteria [@CL-CMP-5333; @CL-CMP-5862]. It also keeps that the offeror must request a wage determination from the Contracting Officer where the wage determination for the primary site does not apply to the secondary site [@CL-CMP-5662; @CL-CMP-5863], and that the due date for offers is not extended for that request [@CL-CMP-5663; @CL-CMP-5864]. The RFO provision is inserted as prescribed in 22.402-4(g), where the codified provision is inserted as prescribed in FAR 22.407(h) [@CL-CMP-6202; @CL-CMP-5860].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror plans to build 3 of 10 modules at a yard in the United States that it leases only for this project, and the offeror treats those modules as a significant portion of the work. The yard is a planned secondary site of the work, so the offeror notifies the Government and, because the wage determination for the primary site does not apply there, requests a wage determination for it. If the offeror were unsure whether the yard meets the criteria, it would instead request a determination from the Contracting Officer. The closing date stays where it was.

## Never confuse

A secondary site is not just any other site. It is a site where a significant portion of the building or work is constructed, provided the site is both located in the United States and established specifically for the performance of the contract or project [@CL-CMP-5818]. A request for a wage determination is also not a request for more time, because the offer due date is not extended for it [@CL-CMP-5864].

The Construction Wage Rate Requirements clause is covered in [Construction wage rate requirements: minimum wages, fringe benefits and posting](cmp-construction-wage-rate-requirements.md), and how general and project wage determinations are requested is covered in [Construction wage determinations: general and project types and how each is requested](cmp-construction-wage-determination-types-and-requests.md).
