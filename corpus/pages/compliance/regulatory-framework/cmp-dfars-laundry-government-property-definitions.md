## Purpose

Sets out how the DoD clause on special definitions of Government property classifies the articles a contractor receives to launder or dry-clean, including articles owned by individual Government personnel.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-9070] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.237-7018 [@CL-CMP-9070] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-9070] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause, for articles delivered to the contractor to be laundered or dry-cleaned [@CL-CMP-9070; @CL-CMP-9072] |

Articles delivered to the contractor to be laundered or dry-cleaned, including any articles actually owned by individual Government personnel, are Government-owned property, not Government-furnished property [@CL-CMP-9070].

Government-owned property does not fall under the requirements of any Government-furnished property clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-9071].

The clause is used as prescribed in DFARS 237.7101(g) [@CL-CMP-9072].

## How it works

The classification covers articles delivered to the contractor to be laundered or dry-cleaned, including any articles actually owned by individual Government personnel [@CL-CMP-9070]. Because these articles are Government-owned property and not Government-furnished property, Government-owned property does not fall under the requirements of any Government-furnished property clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-9070; @CL-CMP-9071].

Other laundry clauses are covered in [DFARS laundry delivery tickets and loss or damage](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-dfars-laundry-delivery-tickets-and-loss-or-damage.md) and [DFARS laundry offeror instructions and individual laundry](cmp-dfars-laundry-offeror-instructions-and-individual-laundry.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A laundry contract that includes the clause receives 400 uniform items, of which 50 are owned by individual service members. All 400 are Government-owned property, not Government-furnished property, including the 50 that individuals own. The Government-furnished property clauses of the contract therefore do not apply to any of the 400.

## Never confuse

Government-owned property is not the same as Government-furnished property. The clause puts the delivered articles in the first category and says the Government-furnished property clauses of the contract do not reach it [@CL-CMP-9070; @CL-CMP-9071].

Personal ownership by an individual Government person does not take an article outside the clause, because the clause expressly includes those articles [@CL-CMP-9070].
