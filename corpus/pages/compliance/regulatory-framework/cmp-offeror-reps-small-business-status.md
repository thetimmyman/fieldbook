## Purpose

Shows the size and socioeconomic status representations an offeror makes in paragraph (c) of the commercial offeror representations provision, when the contracting officer's program rules make them apply, which answers gate which later answers, and what the Alternate I paragraph adds.

Offerors must complete the paragraph (c) representations when the resulting contract is for supplies to be delivered or services to be performed in the United States or its outlying areas, or when the contracting officer has applied FAR part 19 in accordance with FAR 19.000(b)(1)(ii), and must check all that apply [@CL-CMP-8968].

Small business, paragraph (c)(1): the offeror represents as part of its offer either whether it is or is not a small business concern, or whether it is or is not a small business joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 121.103(h) and 13 CFR 125.8(a) and (b) [@CL-CMP-8969]. For the joint venture representation, the offeror shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9133].

Veteran-owned, paragraph (c)(2): the representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not a veteran-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8970].

Service-disabled veteran-owned, paragraphs (c)(3) and (c)(4): the representation in paragraph (c)(3) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a veteran-owned small business concern in paragraph (c)(2), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not an SDVOSB concern [@CL-CMP-8971]. In paragraph (c)(4) the offeror represents whether it is or is not an SDVOSB joint venture eligible under the SDVOSB Program that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 128.402 [@CL-CMP-9134]. That representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as an SDVOSB concern in paragraph (c)(3), and the offeror shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9135].

Small disadvantaged business, paragraph (c)(5): the representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not a small disadvantaged business concern as defined in 13 CFR 124.1001 [@CL-CMP-8972].

Women-owned small business, paragraphs (c)(6) to (c)(8): the representation in paragraph (c)(6) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not a women-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8973]. In paragraph (c)(7) the offeror represents whether it is or is not a joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 127.506(a) through (c) for the WOSB Program, and shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9136]. In paragraph (c)(8) it represents whether it is or is not an economically disadvantaged women-owned small business joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 127.506(a) through (c), and shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9137].

Women-owned business that is not small, paragraph (c)(9), and tie bid priority, paragraph (c)(10): paragraphs (c)(9) and (c)(10) are completed only if the solicitation is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-9138]. The simplified acquisition threshold means $350,000, except for the cases the definition lists [@CL-FND-0001]. Paragraph (c)(9) is completed only if the offeror is a women-owned business concern and did not represent itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents that it is a women-owned business concern [@CL-CMP-9139]. In paragraph (c)(10), if the solicitation is an invitation for bid, small business offerors may identify the labor surplus areas in which costs to be incurred on account of manufacturing or production, by the offeror or first-tier subcontractors, amount to more than 50 percent of the contract price [@CL-CMP-9140].

HUBZone, paragraph (c)(11): the representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) [@CL-CMP-9141]. In paragraph (c)(11)(i) the offeror represents whether it is or is not a HUBZone small business concern listed, on the date of the representation, as having been certified by SBA as a HUBZone small business concern in the Dynamic Small Business Search and SAM [@CL-CMP-8974]. The representation also states that the offeror will attempt to maintain an employment rate of HUBZone residents of 35 percent of its employees during performance of a HUBZone contract [@CL-CMP-8975]. In paragraph (c)(11)(ii) the offeror represents whether it is or is not a HUBZone joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 126.616(a) through (c), and shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9142]. Each HUBZone small business concern participating in the HUBZone joint venture shall provide representation of its HUBZone status [@CL-CMP-9143].

Alternate I, paragraph (c)(12): Alternate I adds paragraph (c)(12), which the offeror completes if it has represented itself as disadvantaged in paragraph (c)(5) [@CL-CMP-9144]. The paragraph lists Black American, Hispanic American, Native American and Asian-Pacific American as groups [@CL-CMP-9145]. It also lists Subcontinent Asian (Asian-Indian) American and an individual or concern other than one of the preceding groups [@CL-CMP-9146].

## How it works

The veteran-owned, small disadvantaged, women-owned small business and HUBZone representations are each completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the SDVOSB representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a veteran-owned small business concern in paragraph (c)(2) [@CL-CMP-8970; @CL-CMP-8971; @CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9141]. The SDVOSB joint venture representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as an SDVOSB concern in paragraph (c)(3) [@CL-CMP-9135].

The small business joint venture representation in paragraph (c)(1)(ii) and the women-owned joint venture representations in paragraphs (c)(7) and (c)(8) each include the instruction that the offeror shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9133; @CL-CMP-9136; @CL-CMP-9137]. Paragraph (c)(9) is completed only if the offeror is a women-owned business concern and did not represent itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and paragraphs (c)(9) and (c)(10) are completed only if the solicitation is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-9139; @CL-CMP-9138].

SBA determines affiliation based on the factors set forth at 13 CFR 121.103 [@CL-CMP-9117], and the HUBZone representation names certification by SBA [@CL-CMP-8974]. The SDVOSB Program and WOSB Program definitions name certification by SBA, and the WOSB Program definition also names approved third-party certifiers [@CL-CMP-9128; @CL-CMP-9113]. The paragraph (c) representations are required when the contracting officer has applied FAR part 19 in accordance with FAR 19.000(b)(1)(ii), or when the contract is for supplies to be delivered or services to be performed in the United States or its outlying areas [@CL-CMP-8968]. Under Alternate I, the offeror completes paragraph (c)(12) if it has represented itself as disadvantaged in paragraph (c)(5) [@CL-CMP-9144].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.212-3 as reserved, and the small business representations appear in paragraph (c) of FAR 52.219-1 instead [@CL-CMP-1621] [@CL-CMP-1622]. RFO paragraph (c) of FAR 52.219-1 goes from its heading to paragraph (c)(1), without a counterpart to the codified opening sentence on when offerors must complete the representations and check all that apply [@CL-CMP-8968] [@CL-CMP-9281]. RFO paragraph (c)(1) says the same in substance as codified paragraph (c)(1), including the name and unique entity identifier of each party to a small business joint venture [@CL-CMP-8969] [@CL-CMP-1622] [@CL-CMP-9133] [@CL-CMP-1623]. RFO paragraph (c)(1)(ii) is followed directly by paragraph (c)(2), so the RFO paragraph has no counterpart to codified paragraph (c)(2), the veteran-owned small business concern representation [@CL-CMP-8970] [@CL-CMP-9282]. RFO paragraph (c)(2) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) and asks whether it is a "women-owned small disadvantage business concern", where codified paragraph (c)(5) asks whether it is a small disadvantaged business concern as defined in 13 CFR 124.1001 [@CL-CMP-8972] [@CL-CMP-1624]. The women-owned small business joint venture representation comes next as RFO paragraph (c)(3), and RFO paragraphs (c)(1) through (c)(6) are followed by paragraph (d), so none of them asks, as codified paragraphs (c)(3) and (c)(6) do, whether the offeror is an SDVOSB concern or a women-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8971] [@CL-CMP-8973] [@CL-CMP-1624] [@CL-CMP-1626] [@CL-CMP-1628] [@CL-CMP-1629] [@CL-CMP-1631] [@CL-CMP-1632]. RFO paragraphs (c)(3) and (c)(4) say the same in substance as codified paragraphs (c)(7) and (c)(8) for the women-owned and economically disadvantaged women-owned joint ventures, each with the name and unique entity identifier of each party [@CL-CMP-9136] [@CL-CMP-1625] [@CL-CMP-1626] [@CL-CMP-9137] [@CL-CMP-1627] [@CL-CMP-1628]. RFO paragraph (c)(5) says the SDVOSB joint venture representation is completed only if the offeror is certified as a SDVOSB concern, where codified paragraph (c)(4) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as an SDVOSB concern in paragraph (c)(3) [@CL-CMP-9135] [@CL-CMP-1629] [@CL-CMP-1630]. RFO paragraph (c)(6) says the HUBZone joint venture representation is completed only if the offeror is a HUBZone small business concern, and each HUBZone small business concern participating in the joint venture must be certified as a HUBZone concern [@CL-CMP-9142] [@CL-CMP-9143] [@CL-CMP-1631] [@CL-CMP-1632]. RFO paragraph (c)(6) prints no counterpart to the HUBZone small business concern representation and the 35 percent employment-rate statement of codified paragraph (c)(11)(i) [@CL-CMP-8974] [@CL-CMP-8975] [@CL-CMP-1631]. The women-owned business concern representation that codified paragraph (c)(9) holds appears in paragraph (b) of RFO FAR 52.204-5, tied to the offeror's answer in paragraph (c)(1) of FAR 52.219-1 [@CL-CMP-9139] [@CL-CMP-1633]. The bracketed condition in that RFO paragraph names no limit to solicitations expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, and the provision ends after paragraph (b), where codified paragraphs (c)(9) and (c)(10) are completed only if the solicitation is expected to exceed that threshold [@CL-CMP-9138] [@CL-CMP-1633] [@CL-CMP-9283]. RFO FAR 52.219-2 says it applies to small business concerns only [@CL-CMP-9284]. The labor surplus area identification that codified paragraph (c)(10) holds, for small business offerors when the solicitation is an invitation for bid, appears in paragraph (b) of RFO FAR 52.219-2, where a bidder that wishes to be considered for tie-bid priority must identify the area in which the costs to be incurred on account of manufacturing or production, by the bidder or the first-tier subcontractors, amount to more than 50 percent of the contract price [@CL-CMP-9140] [@CL-CMP-1634]. The RFO text prints no Alternate I under FAR 52.212-3, where the codified Alternate I adds paragraph (c)(12) [@CL-CMP-9144] [@CL-CMP-1635].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The contracting officer applies FAR part 19 to a solicitation for supplies delivered in the United States, so the offeror completes paragraph (c) [@CL-CMP-8968]. This example follows the codified provision, whose paragraphs (c)(2) to (c)(4) differ from the RFO paragraphs of the same number [@CL-CMP-1624] [@CL-CMP-1627] [@CL-CMP-1629]. The solicitation is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, so paragraphs (c)(9) and (c)(10) are also completed where they apply [@CL-CMP-9138].

Birch Fabrication represents that it is a small business concern [@CL-CMP-8969]. Because it represented itself as small, it answers the veteran-owned question and says it is a veteran-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8970]. Because it represented itself as veteran-owned, it answers the SDVOSB question and says it is not an SDVOSB concern [@CL-CMP-8971]. It did not represent itself as an SDVOSB concern, so it does not complete paragraph (c)(4) [@CL-CMP-9135]. It answers the small disadvantaged and women-owned small business questions and says it is neither [@CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973]. It represents that it is listed as certified by SBA in the Dynamic Small Business Search and SAM on the date of the representation, and that it will attempt to keep an employment rate of HUBZone residents of 35 percent of its employees [@CL-CMP-9141; @CL-CMP-8974; @CL-CMP-8975]. With 40 employees, 35 percent is 14 employees [@CL-CMP-8975]. It states that it is not a HUBZone joint venture [@CL-CMP-9142].

Harbor Systems represents that it is not a small business concern [@CL-CMP-8969]. It does not complete the veteran-owned, SDVOSB, small disadvantaged, women-owned small business or HUBZone representations [@CL-CMP-8970; @CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9141]. It is a women-owned business concern, so it completes paragraph (c)(9) [@CL-CMP-9139; @CL-CMP-9138].

## Never confuse

The veteran-owned, small disadvantaged, women-owned small business and HUBZone representations are each completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) [@CL-CMP-8970; @CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9141].

Paragraph (c)(6) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and asks whether it is a women-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8973]. Paragraph (c)(9) is completed only if the offeror is a women-owned business concern and did not represent itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) [@CL-CMP-9139]. Paragraph (c)(8) is the economically disadvantaged women-owned small business joint venture representation, which asks whether the offeror is a joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 127.506(a) through (c) [@CL-CMP-9137].

Alternate I adds paragraph (c)(12) to the basic provision, to be completed if the offeror has represented itself as disadvantaged in paragraph (c)(5) [@CL-CMP-9144].

The codified paragraph (c)(2) asks whether the offeror is a veteran-owned small business concern, but RFO paragraph (c)(2) of FAR 52.219-1 asks whether the offeror is a "women-owned small disadvantage business concern", followed by the women-owned small business joint venture representation in paragraph (c)(3) [@CL-CMP-8970] [@CL-CMP-1624] [@CL-CMP-9282]. The codified SDVOSB joint venture representation in paragraph (c)(4) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as an SDVOSB concern in paragraph (c)(3), while the RFO paragraph is completed only if the offeror is certified as a SDVOSB concern [@CL-CMP-9135] [@CL-CMP-1629].
