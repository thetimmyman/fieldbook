## Purpose

Shows the size and socioeconomic status representations an offeror makes in paragraph (c) of the commercial offeror representations provision, when the contracting officer's program rules make them apply, which answers gate which later answers, and what the Alternate I paragraph adds.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8968] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.212-3(c) [@CL-CMP-8968] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8968] |
| Applies to | Contracts for supplies to be delivered or services to be performed in the United States or its outlying areas, and contracts where the contracting officer has applied FAR part 19 in accordance with FAR 19.000(b)(1)(ii) [@CL-CMP-8968] |

Offerors must complete the paragraph (c) representations when the resulting contract is for supplies to be delivered or services to be performed in the United States or its outlying areas, or when the contracting officer has applied FAR part 19 in accordance with FAR 19.000(b)(1)(ii), and must check all that apply [@CL-CMP-8968]. The second trigger turns on whether the contracting officer has applied part 19, and the offeror makes the representations as part of its offer [@CL-CMP-8968; @CL-CMP-8969].

Small business, paragraph (c)(1): the offeror represents as part of its offer either whether it is or is not a small business concern, or whether it is or is not a small business joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 121.103(h) and 13 CFR 125.8(a) and (b) [@CL-CMP-8969]. For the joint venture representation, the offeror shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9133].

Veteran-owned, paragraph (c)(2): the representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not a veteran-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8970].

Service-disabled veteran-owned, paragraphs (c)(3) and (c)(4): the representation in paragraph (c)(3) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a veteran-owned small business concern in paragraph (c)(2), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not an SDVOSB concern [@CL-CMP-8971]. In paragraph (c)(4) the offeror represents whether it is or is not an SDVOSB joint venture eligible under the SDVOSB Program that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 128.402 [@CL-CMP-9134]. That representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as an SDVOSB concern in paragraph (c)(3), and the offeror shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9135].

Small disadvantaged business, paragraph (c)(5): the representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not a small disadvantaged business concern as defined in 13 CFR 124.1001 [@CL-CMP-8972].

Women-owned small business, paragraphs (c)(6) to (c)(8): the representation in paragraph (c)(6) is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents whether it is or is not a women-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8973]. In paragraph (c)(7) the offeror represents whether it is or is not a joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 127.506(a) through (c) for the WOSB Program, and shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9136]. In paragraph (c)(8) it represents whether it is or is not an economically disadvantaged women-owned small business joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 127.506(a) through (c), and shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9137].

Women-owned business that is not small, paragraph (c)(9), and tie bid priority, paragraph (c)(10): paragraphs (c)(9) and (c)(10) are completed only if the solicitation is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-9138]. The simplified acquisition threshold is $350,000, with higher values for the listed contingency and defense purposes [@CL-FND-0001]. Paragraph (c)(9) is completed only if the offeror is a women-owned business concern and did not represent itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1), and the offeror represents that it is a women-owned business concern [@CL-CMP-9139]. In paragraph (c)(10), if the solicitation is an invitation for bid, small business offerors may identify the labor surplus areas in which costs to be incurred on account of manufacturing or production, by the offeror or first-tier subcontractors, amount to more than 50 percent of the contract price [@CL-CMP-9140].

HUBZone, paragraph (c)(11): the representation is completed only if the offeror represented itself as a small business concern in paragraph (c)(1) [@CL-CMP-9141]. In paragraph (c)(11)(i) the offeror represents whether it is or is not a HUBZone small business concern listed, on the date of the representation, as having been certified by SBA as a HUBZone small business concern in the Dynamic Small Business Search and SAM [@CL-CMP-8974]. The representation also states that the offeror will attempt to maintain an employment rate of HUBZone residents of 35 percent of its employees during performance of a HUBZone contract [@CL-CMP-8975]. In paragraph (c)(11)(ii) the offeror represents whether it is or is not a HUBZone joint venture that complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 126.616(a) through (c), and shall enter the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9142]. Each HUBZone small business concern participating in the HUBZone joint venture shall provide representation of its HUBZone status [@CL-CMP-9143].

Alternate I, paragraph (c)(12): Alternate I adds paragraph (c)(12), which the offeror completes if it has represented itself as disadvantaged in paragraph (c)(5) [@CL-CMP-9144]. The paragraph lists Black American, Hispanic American, Native American and Asian-Pacific American as groups [@CL-CMP-9145]. It also lists Subcontinent Asian (Asian-Indian) American and an individual or concern other than one of the preceding groups [@CL-CMP-9146].

## How it works

Paragraph (c)(1) comes first and gates most of the rest [@CL-CMP-8969; @CL-CMP-8970; @CL-CMP-8972]. The veteran-owned, small disadvantaged, women-owned small business and HUBZone representations each depend on a small business answer in paragraph (c)(1), and the SDVOSB representation depends on a veteran-owned answer in paragraph (c)(2) [@CL-CMP-8970; @CL-CMP-8971; @CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9141]. The SDVOSB joint venture representation depends on an SDVOSB answer in paragraph (c)(3) [@CL-CMP-9135]. A concern that represents it is not small does not go on to those gated statuses.

The women-owned joint venture representations in paragraphs (c)(7) and (c)(8) and the small business joint venture representation in paragraph (c)(1)(ii) carry no gate in the text, and each asks for the name and unique entity identifier of each party to the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9136; @CL-CMP-9137; @CL-CMP-9133]. A concern that is not small but is women-owned answers paragraph (c)(9) when the solicitation is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-9139; @CL-CMP-9138].

SBA decides affiliation for size purposes under 13 CFR 121.103 [@CL-CMP-9117], and the HUBZone representation names certification by SBA [@CL-CMP-8974]. The SDVOSB Program and WOSB Program definitions name certification by SBA, and the WOSB Program definition also names approved third-party certifiers [@CL-CMP-9128; @CL-CMP-9113]. The paragraph (c) representations are required when the contracting officer has applied FAR part 19 in accordance with FAR 19.000(b)(1)(ii), or when the contract is for supplies to be delivered or services to be performed in the United States or its outlying areas [@CL-CMP-8968]. Under Alternate I, the offeror completes paragraph (c)(12) if it has represented itself as disadvantaged in paragraph (c)(5) [@CL-CMP-9144]. The consequences of a misrepresentation and of later rerepresentation sit in the linked small business cards, not in paragraph (c).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The contracting officer applies FAR part 19 to a solicitation for supplies delivered in the United States, so the offeror completes paragraph (c) [@CL-CMP-8968]. The solicitation is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, so paragraphs (c)(9) and (c)(10) are also completed where they apply [@CL-CMP-9138].

Birch Fabrication represents that it is a small business concern [@CL-CMP-8969]. Because it represented itself as small, it answers the veteran-owned question and says it is a veteran-owned small business concern [@CL-CMP-8970]. Because it represented itself as veteran-owned, it answers the SDVOSB question and says it is not an SDVOSB concern [@CL-CMP-8971]. It did not represent itself as an SDVOSB concern, so it does not complete paragraph (c)(4) [@CL-CMP-9135]. It answers the small disadvantaged and women-owned small business questions and says it is neither [@CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973]. It represents that it is listed as certified by SBA in the Dynamic Small Business Search and SAM on the date of the representation, and that it will attempt to keep an employment rate of HUBZone residents of 35 percent of its employees [@CL-CMP-9141; @CL-CMP-8974; @CL-CMP-8975]. With 40 employees, 35 percent is 14 employees [@CL-CMP-8975]. It states that it is not a HUBZone joint venture [@CL-CMP-9142].

Harbor Systems represents that it is not a small business concern [@CL-CMP-8969]. It does not complete the veteran-owned, SDVOSB, small disadvantaged, women-owned small business or HUBZone representations [@CL-CMP-8970; @CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9141]. It is a women-owned business concern, so it completes paragraph (c)(9) [@CL-CMP-9139; @CL-CMP-9138].

## Never confuse

Small business status is not the same thing as a socioeconomic status. The small business answer gates the later statuses, but each status is a separate representation with its own definition and condition [@CL-CMP-8970; @CL-CMP-8972; @CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9141].

A women-owned small business concern answering paragraph (c)(6) is not the same as a women-owned business concern answering paragraph (c)(9) [@CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9139]. Paragraph (c)(6) depends on a small business answer, while paragraph (c)(9) is for a women-owned business that did not represent itself as small [@CL-CMP-8973; @CL-CMP-9139]. Paragraph (c) asks about economically disadvantaged women-owned small business status through the joint venture representation in paragraph (c)(8) [@CL-CMP-9137].

The Alternate I group list in paragraph (c)(12) is not part of the basic provision, and it is completed if the offeror represented itself as disadvantaged in paragraph (c)(5) [@CL-CMP-9144].
