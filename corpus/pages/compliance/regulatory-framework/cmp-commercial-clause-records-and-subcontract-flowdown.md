## Purpose

Shows when the Comptroller General record examination paragraph applies to a commercial contract, how long records must stay available, and which clauses the contractor must flow down in subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-9051] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.212-5(d) and (e) [@CL-CMP-9051; @CL-CMP-9057] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.212-4(t) [@CL-CMP-0633]; RFO FAR 52.244-6 [@CL-CMP-0641]; RFO FAR 52.212-5 is [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-0632] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-9051] |
| Applies to | Paragraph (d): contracts that meet three conditions. Paragraph (e): subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-9051; @CL-CMP-9052; @CL-CMP-9057] |

Examination of records, paragraph (d). The Contractor shall comply with paragraph (d) if the contract meets three conditions. Two are that the contract was awarded using other than sealed bid and is in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101, on the date of award [@CL-CMP-9051]. The third is that the contract does not contain the clause at 52.215-2, Audit and Records—Negotiation [@CL-CMP-9052].

The Comptroller General of the United States, or an authorized representative of the Comptroller General, shall have access to and right to examine any of the Contractor's directly pertinent records involving transactions related to the contract [@CL-CMP-9053]. The Contractor shall make available at its offices at all reasonable times the records, materials, and other evidence for examination, audit, or reproduction [@CL-CMP-9209]. They stay available until 3 years after final payment under the contract or for any shorter period specified in FAR Subpart 4.7, Contractor Records Retention, of the other clauses of the contract [@CL-CMP-9054]. If the contract is completely or partially terminated, the records relating to the work terminated shall be made available for 3 years after any resulting final termination settlement [@CL-CMP-9055]. Records relating to appeals under the disputes clause or to litigation or the settlement of claims arising under or relating to the contract shall be made available until the appeals, litigation, or claims are finally resolved [@CL-CMP-9210].

As used in the clause, records include books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of form [@CL-CMP-9056]. The paragraph does not require the Contractor to create or maintain any record that the Contractor does not maintain in the ordinary course of business or pursuant to a provision of law [@CL-CMP-9211].

Alternates. Alternate I, as prescribed in FAR 12.301(b)(4)(i), deletes paragraph (d) from the basic clause and redesignates paragraph (e) as paragraph (d), and revises the reference in the redesignated paragraph (d) to read paragraphs (a), (b), and (c) of the clause [@CL-CMP-9212; @CL-CMP-8541]. Alternate II, as prescribed in FAR 12.301(b)(4)(ii), substitutes paragraphs (d)(1) and (e)(1) for those of the basic clause [@CL-CMP-7667]. Under it, the Comptroller General, an appropriate Inspector General appointed under section 3 or 8G of the Inspector General Act of 1978, or an authorized representative of either shall have access to and right to examine any of the Contractor's or any subcontractors' records that pertain to, and involve transactions relating to, the contract, and to interview any officer or employee regarding such transactions [@CL-CMP-9213; @CL-CMP-9214]. In the Alternate II paragraph (e)(1), item (i) says that paragraph (d) flows down to all subcontracts, except that the authority of the Inspector General under paragraph (d)(1)(ii) does not flow down [@CL-CMP-9215].

Flowdown, paragraph (e). Notwithstanding the requirements of the clauses in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), and (d), the Contractor is not required to flow down any FAR clause, other than those in paragraph (e)(1), in a subcontract for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-9057]. Unless otherwise indicated in the paragraph (e)(1) list, the extent of the flow down shall be as required by the clause [@CL-CMP-9216]. The list includes 52.203-13, Contractor Code of Business Ethics and Conduct [@CL-CMP-9217].

Four entries say where the extent of flow down is set. For 52.222-40, paragraph (e)(1)(xiv) says flow down is required in accordance with paragraph (f) of that clause [@CL-CMP-9219]. For 52.226-6, paragraph (e)(1)(xxiv) says it is required in accordance with paragraph (e) of that clause [@CL-CMP-9220]. For 52.232-40, paragraph (e)(1)(xxv) says it is required in accordance with paragraph (c) of that clause [@CL-CMP-9221]. For 52.247-64, paragraph (e)(1)(xxvii) says it is required in accordance with paragraph (d) of that clause [@CL-CMP-9222].

Paragraph (e)(1)(viii) lists 52.219-8, Utilization of Small Business Concerns, in all subcontracts that offer further subcontracting opportunities [@CL-CMP-9058]. If the subcontract, other than a subcontract to a small business concern, exceeds the applicable threshold specified in FAR 19.702(a) on the date of subcontract award, the subcontractor must include 52.219-8 in lower tier subcontracts that offer subcontracting opportunities [@CL-CMP-9218]. While not required, the Contractor may include in its subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services a minimal number of additional clauses necessary to satisfy its contractual obligations [@CL-CMP-9059].

## How it works

Paragraph (d) is conditional, so a contract awarded by sealed bid, one at or below the simplified acquisition threshold on the date of award, or one that contains the clause at 52.215-2, does not meet its conditions [@CL-CMP-9051; @CL-CMP-9052]. When the conditions are met, the Comptroller General has the examination right and the Contractor has the duty to keep the records available at its offices [@CL-CMP-9053; @CL-CMP-9209].

The retention clock starts at final payment, and a shorter period specified in FAR Subpart 4.7 of the other clauses can apply instead [@CL-CMP-9054]. A termination adds a separate period for the terminated work, measured from the final termination settlement [@CL-CMP-9055]. Records relating to appeals under the disputes clause or to litigation or the settlement of claims shall be made available until the appeals, litigation, or claims are finally resolved [@CL-CMP-9210]. The Contractor is not required to create or maintain any record that it does not maintain in the ordinary course of business or pursuant to a provision of law [@CL-CMP-9211].

On the subcontract side, other than the clauses in paragraph (e)(1), the Contractor need not flow down a FAR clause in a subcontract for commercial products or commercial services, and the extent of flow down for a listed clause is the extent the clause itself requires unless the list says otherwise [@CL-CMP-9057; @CL-CMP-9216]. For 52.219-8, if the subcontract (except subcontracts to small business concerns) exceeds the applicable threshold specified in FAR 19.702(a) on the date of subcontract award, the subcontractor must include 52.219-8 in lower tier subcontracts that offer subcontracting opportunities [@CL-CMP-9218]. While not required, the Contractor may include in its subcontracts a minimal number of additional clauses necessary to satisfy its contractual obligations [@CL-CMP-9059].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The record examination rule sits in RFO FAR 52.212-4(t), and RFO FAR 52.212-5 is [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-0633; @CL-CMP-0632]. Paragraph (t) says it applies if the contract was awarded using other than sealed bid procedures and is in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold on the date of award, and that sentence names no other condition and does not repeat the reference to FAR 2.101 for the threshold, where codified paragraph (d) also requires that the contract not contain the clause at 52.215-2 and refers to FAR 2.101 for the threshold [@CL-CMP-0633; @CL-CMP-9051; @CL-CMP-9052]. The access sentence for the Comptroller General says the same in substance [@CL-CMP-0634; @CL-CMP-9053]. Paragraph (t)(2) says the records stay available until 3 years after final payment or for any shorter period specified in FAR part 4, longer period required by statute, or periods specified in other clauses, where codified paragraph (d) refers to FAR Subpart 4.7 and the other clauses [@CL-CMP-0635; @CL-CMP-9054]. The termination sentence, the appeals and litigation sentence, the definition of records and the sentence on creating records say the same in substance [@CL-CMP-0636; @CL-CMP-9055] [@CL-CMP-0637; @CL-CMP-9210] [@CL-CMP-0638; @CL-CMP-9056] [@CL-CMP-0639; @CL-CMP-9211]. RFO Alternate I of 52.212-4, for contemplating a time-and-materials or labor-hour contract, substitutes paragraphs (a), (b), (i), (l), and (m), and paragraph (t) is not among them [@CL-CMP-0640], where codified Alternate I of 52.212-5 deletes paragraph (d) [@CL-CMP-9212]. For subcontracts, RFO FAR 52.244-6(b)(2) says that if a clause in its table is included in the contract, the Contractor must insert the clause in subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services and must flow down the requirements as indicated in the specific clause, where codified paragraph (e)(1) is written as a limit on the FAR clauses that must be flowed down [@CL-CMP-0641; @CL-CMP-9057]. The RFO table lists 52.203-13, Contractor Code of Business Ethics and Conduct [@CL-CMP-0642; @CL-CMP-9217].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial contract is awarded using other than sealed bid, is in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold on the date of award, and does not contain the clause at 52.215-2, so the Contractor shall comply with paragraph (d) [@CL-CMP-9051; @CL-CMP-9052]. The Contractor keeps contract records available at its offices until 3 years after final payment, and no shorter period is specified in FAR Subpart 4.7 or the other clauses [@CL-CMP-9209; @CL-CMP-9054]. When the contract is partially terminated, records for the terminated work stay available for 3 years after the final termination settlement [@CL-CMP-9055]. A claim over that work is still in litigation when the 3 years end, so the related records stay available until the litigation is finally resolved [@CL-CMP-9210].

The Contractor places a commercial subcontract with a large business that offers further subcontracting opportunities, so the subcontract includes 52.219-8 [@CL-CMP-9058]. The large business's own subcontract from the Contractor exceeded the FAR 19.702(a) threshold on its award date and is not a subcontract to a small business concern, so the large business must include 52.219-8 in a lower tier subcontract that offers subcontracting opportunities [@CL-CMP-9218]. The Contractor adds one further clause that its own obligations need, which paragraph (e)(2) permits but does not require [@CL-CMP-9059].

## Never confuse

Paragraph (d) applies to a contract that does not contain the clause at 52.215-2, Audit and Records—Negotiation [@CL-CMP-9052]. Records stay available until 3 years after final payment or for any shorter period specified in FAR Subpart 4.7 of the other clauses [@CL-CMP-9054]; if the contract is completely or partially terminated, records relating to the work terminated stay available for 3 years after any resulting final termination settlement [@CL-CMP-9055]; and records relating to appeals, litigation or claims stay available until finally resolved [@CL-CMP-9210]. The flowdown cap in paragraph (e)(1) says the Contractor is not required to flow down any FAR clause other than those it lists, and unless the list indicates otherwise, the extent of the flowdown is as the clause requires [@CL-CMP-9057; @CL-CMP-9216]. As used in the clause, records include books, documents, accounting procedures and practices, and other data, regardless of type and regardless of form [@CL-CMP-9056]. The paragraph does not require the Contractor to create or maintain any record that it does not maintain in the ordinary course of business or pursuant to a provision of law [@CL-CMP-9211]. RFO FAR 52.212-4(t) applies if the contract was awarded using other than sealed bid procedures and is in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold on the date of award [@CL-CMP-0633], and codified paragraph (d) adds that the contract not contain the clause at 52.215-2 [@CL-CMP-9052]. Codified paragraph (d)(2) refers to FAR Subpart 4.7 [@CL-CMP-9054], and RFO paragraph (t)(2) refers to FAR part 4, a longer period required by statute, or periods specified in other clauses [@CL-CMP-0635].

## Company calibration

The company must confirm which of its commercial contracts fall inside paragraph (d) and who tracks the subcontract flowdown choices [@Q-004].
