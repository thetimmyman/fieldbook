## Purpose

Explains what an alternate is, how it is titled, dated and cited, and the limit that keeps an alternate with its own provision or clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8870] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.105 [@CL-CMP-8870] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.105 [@CL-CMP-6339; @CL-CMP-6341; @CL-CMP-6343] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8870] |
| Applies to | Provisions and clauses that the FAR prescribes with one or more alternates [@CL-CMP-8870; @CL-CMP-8872] |

The FAR accommodates a major variation in a provision or clause by use of an alternate, and prescribes the alternates in the FAR subject text where the provision or clause is prescribed [@CL-CMP-8870]. The alternates to each provision or clause are titled "Alternate I," "Alternate II," "Alternate III," and so on [@CL-CMP-8909]. The prescription includes all conditions, requirements and instructions for using the provision or clause and its alternates, if any [@CL-CMP-8887].

When an alternate is used, its date shall be cited along with the date of the basic provision or clause [@CL-CMP-8871]. In the FAR's example, a clause used with an alternate is cited with the date of the basic clause followed by the alternate and its date [@CL-CMP-8910].

A provision or clause may be used with two or more alternates under certain circumstances, and each of the applicable alternates shall then be cited, whether incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8872]. In the FAR's example, a clause used with two alternates is cited with the date of the basic clause followed by Alternate I with its date and Alternate II with its date [@CL-CMP-8911]. Under no circumstances may an alternate to a specific provision or clause be applied to any other provision or clause [@CL-CMP-8873].

## How it works

The section does not name who selects an alternate or say when one is used. The conditions are in the prescription in the FAR subject text, which covers the provision or clause and its alternates [@CL-CMP-8870; @CL-CMP-8887]. The section sets no threshold or deadline.

All provisions, clauses and alternates are dated [@CL-CMP-8896], and the date shall be included, whether the text is incorporated by reference or in full text, to avoid questions about which version is operative in a given solicitation or contract [@CL-CMP-8847]. For an alternate the date is cited together with the date of the basic text [@CL-CMP-8871].

Each applicable alternate is cited, so incorporation by reference does not hide one of them [@CL-CMP-8872]. An alternate stays with the provision or clause it was written for [@CL-CMP-8873]. How the identification of a text is written is on the card for [identifying provisions and clauses](cmp-identifying-provisions-and-clauses.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. On the points below the RFO section reads the same as the codified section [@CL-CMP-6339; @CL-CMP-6341; @CL-CMP-6343]. It says the FAR accommodates a major variation in a provision or clause by use of an alternate and prescribes alternates to a given provision or clause in the FAR subject text where the provision or clause is prescribed [@CL-CMP-6339; @CL-CMP-8870], and it titles the alternates "Alternate I," "Alternate II," "Alternate III," and so on [@CL-CMP-6340; @CL-CMP-8909]. When an alternate is used, its date shall be cited along with the date of the basic provision or clause [@CL-CMP-6341; @CL-CMP-8871]. A provision or clause may be used with two or more alternates under certain circumstances, and each of the applicable alternates shall then be cited, whether incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-6342; @CL-CMP-6343; @CL-CMP-8872]. Under no circumstances may an alternate to a specific provision or clause be applied to any other provision or clause [@CL-CMP-6344; @CL-CMP-8873].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A clause has two alternates, and its prescribing FAR text calls for both in this buy [@CL-CMP-8870; @CL-CMP-8872]. The basic clause is dated MAR 2020, Alternate I is dated JUN 2021 and Alternate II is dated FEB 2022. The solicitation cites the basic clause with its date, then Alternate I with its date and Alternate II with its date, whether the clause is incorporated by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8871; @CL-CMP-8872; @CL-CMP-8911].

The drafter notices that a neighboring clause has no alternate that fits the buy and is tempted to borrow Alternate I of the first clause. The drafter does not, because under no circumstances may an alternate to a specific provision or clause be applied to any other provision or clause [@CL-CMP-8873].

## Never confuse

An alternate is not a free-standing clause. It varies a specific provision or clause and cannot be applied to another [@CL-CMP-8873].

An alternate date does not replace the basic date. Both are cited [@CL-CMP-8871].

An alternate is not a modification. A modification is a minor change in the details of a provision or clause that is specifically authorized by the FAR and does not alter its substance [@CL-CMP-8864], while an alternate accommodates a major variation [@CL-CMP-8870].
