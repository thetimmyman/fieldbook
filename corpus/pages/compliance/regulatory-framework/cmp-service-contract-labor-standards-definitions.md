## Purpose

Gives the full meaning of the five terms that the service contract labor standards subpart defines: contractor, multiple year contracts, United States, Wage and Hour Division, and wage determination.

The definitions apply to the terms as used in this subpart [@CL-CMP-8103]. The section defines five terms [@CL-CMP-8081; @CL-CMP-8082; @CL-CMP-8083; @CL-CMP-8085; @CL-CMP-8086]. Each is summarized below.

| Term | Full definition | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| Contractor | Includes a subcontractor at any tier whose subcontract is subject to the provisions of the statute. The sentence lists no exclusion. | [@CL-CMP-8081] |
| Multiple year contracts | Contracts having a term of more than 1 year regardless of fiscal year funding. The term includes multi-year contracts (see 17.103). | [@CL-CMP-8082] |
| United States | The 50 States, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the United States Virgin Islands, Johnston Island, Wake Island, and the outer Continental Shelf as defined in the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act, but not any other place subject to United States jurisdiction or any United States base or possession within a foreign country (29 CFR 4.112). | [@CL-CMP-8083; @CL-CMP-8084] |
| Wage and Hour Division | The unit in the Department of Labor to which is assigned functions of the Secretary of Labor under the Service Contract Labor Standards statute. | [@CL-CMP-8085] |
| Wage determination | A determination of minimum wages or fringe benefits made under title 41 of the United States Code, section 6703 or 6707(c), applicable to the employment in a given locality of one or more classes of service employees. | [@CL-CMP-8086] |

## How it works

The section creates no duty, threshold, deadline or filing. It fixes the meaning of terms that the rest of the subpart uses [@CL-CMP-8103].

Contractor: where the subpart says contractor, the term includes a subcontractor at any tier whose subcontract is subject to the provisions of the statute [@CL-CMP-8081].

Multiple year contracts: the test is a term of more than 1 year, and fiscal year funding does not change the result [@CL-CMP-8082]. Multi-year contracts are included, with a cross-reference to FAR 17.103 [@CL-CMP-8082].

United States: the definition names the 50 States, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the United States Virgin Islands, Johnston Island, Wake Island, and the outer Continental Shelf as defined in the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act, but, in the same sentence, does not include any other place subject to United States jurisdiction or any United States base or possession within a foreign country [@CL-CMP-8083; @CL-CMP-8084].

Wage and Hour Division: the Department of Labor unit that holds the Secretary of Labor's functions under the statute [@CL-CMP-8085]. The section does not say what the unit decides.

Wage determination: a determination of minimum wages or fringe benefits made under section 6703 or 6707(c) of title 41 of the United States Code that is applicable to the employment in a given locality of one or more classes of service employees [@CL-CMP-8086]. How a wage determination is obtained and applied is on [the wage determinations and successorship card](cmp-scls-wage-determinations-and-successorship.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company holds a services order with a 24-month term, funded one fiscal year at a time. A term of more than 1 year makes it a multiple year contract, and the annual funding does not change that [@CL-CMP-8082]. The company has a second-tier subcontractor whose subcontract is subject to the statute. The definition counts that subcontractor as a contractor [@CL-CMP-8081].

Work is performed in Guam and at a base the United States holds in a foreign country. Guam is a listed place [@CL-CMP-8083]. A United States base within a foreign country is excluded [@CL-CMP-8084]. The example does not say what follows for the wage determination, because this section only defines terms.

## Never confuse

Multiple year contracts means contracts having a term of more than 1 year, and the term includes multi-year contracts [@CL-CMP-8082]. Fiscal year funding does not change whether a term of more than 1 year makes a contract a multiple year contract [@CL-CMP-8082].

The Wage and Hour Division is a unit of the Department of Labor, not the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CMP-8085]. A wage determination is a determination of minimum wages or fringe benefits, not an organization [@CL-CMP-8086]. The definition of the Wage and Hour Division does not say that the unit makes wage determinations [@CL-CMP-8085].
