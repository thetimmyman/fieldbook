## Purpose

States the Government policy against age discrimination by contractors and subcontractors in employment, and against maximum age limits in solicitations or advertisements for Government contract work. Each rule has an exception for three stated bases.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8074] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.901 and FAR 22.902 [@CL-CMP-8074; @CL-CMP-8077; @CL-CMP-8067; @CL-CMP-8068] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8074] |
| Applies to | Contractors and subcontractors under paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-8074]; contractors and subcontractors, or persons acting on their behalf, under paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-8077] |

Executive Order 11141, dated February 12, 1964, states the Government policy that the section then sets out [@CL-CMP-8076].

Paragraph (a): contractors and subcontractors shall not, in connection with employment, advancement, or discharge of employees, or the terms, conditions, or privileges of their employment, discriminate against persons because of their age [@CL-CMP-8074]. The exception is a distinction made upon the basis of a bona fide occupational qualification, retirement plan, or statutory requirement [@CL-CMP-8075].

Paragraph (b): contractors and subcontractors, or persons acting on their behalf, shall not specify in solicitations or advertisements for employees to work on Government contracts a maximum age limit for employment [@CL-CMP-8077]. The bar does not apply where the specified limit is based upon a bona fide occupational qualification, retirement plan, or statutory requirement [@CL-CMP-8078].

Paragraph (c): agencies will bring the policy to the attention of contractors [@CL-CMP-8079]. The use of contract clauses is not required [@CL-CMP-8080].

Agencies shall bring complaints regarding a contractor's compliance with this policy to that contractor's attention (in writing, if appropriate) [@CL-CMP-8067], stating the policy, indicating that the contractor's compliance has been questioned, and requesting that the contractor take any appropriate steps that may be necessary to comply [@CL-CMP-8068].

## How it works

Duties fall on three parties. Contractors and subcontractors carry both the employment rule and the advertisement rule [@CL-CMP-8074; @CL-CMP-8077]. Persons acting on their behalf carry the advertisement rule only [@CL-CMP-8077]. Agencies carry the duty to bring the policy to the attention of contractors [@CL-CMP-8079].

The three bases are the same in both paragraphs: a bona fide occupational qualification, a retirement plan, or a statutory requirement [@CL-CMP-8075; @CL-CMP-8078].

The section does not require a contract clause [@CL-CMP-8080]. Related equal opportunity rules are on [the equal employment opportunity definitions card](cmp-eeo-definitions-and-general-rules.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A staffing firm acts on behalf of a services contractor and posts an advertisement for a technician to work on a Government contract. The advertisement states a maximum age of 40 and ties the limit to no qualification, retirement plan, or statutory requirement. Paragraph (b) bars that limit unless it is based on one of those three bases. No basis is stated, so the bar applies. If a statutory requirement did support the limit, the exception in paragraph (b) would apply.

Separately, the contractor declines to advance an employee because of age and relies on no basis. Paragraph (a) bars that decision unless a bona fide occupational qualification, retirement plan, or statutory requirement supports it.

## Never confuse

Paragraph (a) covers employment, advancement, discharge, and the terms, conditions, or privileges of employment [@CL-CMP-8074]. Paragraph (b) covers maximum age limits in solicitations or advertisements for employees to work on Government contracts [@CL-CMP-8074; @CL-CMP-8077]. Only paragraph (b) names persons acting on a contractor's behalf [@CL-CMP-8077].

The two exceptions use different words: paragraph (a) says "except upon the basis of" and paragraph (b) says "unless the specified maximum age limit is based upon" [@CL-CMP-8075; @CL-CMP-8078].
