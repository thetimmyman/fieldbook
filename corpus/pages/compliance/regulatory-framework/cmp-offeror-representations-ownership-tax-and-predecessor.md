## Purpose

Explains what an offeror represents in paragraphs (p), (q) and (r) of the commercial offeror representations provision, which cover who owns or controls the offeror, delinquent Federal tax liability and felony convictions, and the offeror's predecessors, and what the provision says follows.

An offeror that has completed the annual representations and certification electronically in the System for Award Management completes only paragraph (b) of the provision [@CL-CMP-9250]. An offeror that has not completed them electronically shall complete only paragraphs (c) through (v), which include paragraphs (p), (q) and (r) [@CL-CMP-9251].

Ownership, paragraph (p). The paragraph applies in all solicitations when there is a requirement to be registered in SAM or a requirement to have a unique entity identifier in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-9018]. The offeror represents that it has or does not have an immediate owner [@CL-CMP-9019]. An offeror that has one enters the immediate owner's CAGE code and legal name, without a doing business as name, and states whether that owner is owned or controlled by another entity [@CL-CMP-9020]. If the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity, the offeror enters the highest-level owner's CAGE code and legal name, again without a doing business as name [@CL-CMP-9021]. An offeror with more than one immediate owner, such as a joint venture, shall respond to paragraph (p)(2) and, if applicable, paragraph (p)(3) for each participant in the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9019].

Tax and felony, paragraph (q)(1): As required by sections 744 and 745 of Division E of the Consolidated and Further Continuing Appropriations Act, 2015, and similar provisions in subsequent appropriations acts, the Government will not enter into a contract with any corporation that meets either description in the paragraph [@CL-CMP-9194]. The first description is a corporation that has any unpaid Federal tax liability that has been assessed, for which all judicial and administrative remedies have been exhausted or have lapsed, and that is not being paid in a timely manner pursuant to an agreement with the authority responsible for collecting the tax liability [@CL-CMP-9022]. That description applies where the awarding agency is aware of the unpaid tax liability, unless an agency has considered suspension or debarment of the corporation and made a determination that suspension or debarment is not necessary to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-CMP-9023]. The second description is a corporation that was convicted of a felony criminal violation under any Federal law within the preceding 24 months, where the awarding agency is aware of the conviction [@CL-CMP-9024]. It does not apply if an agency has considered suspension or debarment of the corporation and made a determination that this action is not necessary to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-CMP-9025].

Tax and felony, paragraph (q)(2): The offeror represents whether it is, or is not, a corporation that has any unpaid Federal tax liability that has been assessed, for which all judicial and administrative remedies have been exhausted or have lapsed, and that is not being paid in a timely manner pursuant to an agreement with the authority responsible for collecting the tax liability [@CL-CMP-9026; @CL-CMP-9223]. The offeror also represents whether it is, or is not, a corporation that was convicted of a felony criminal violation under a Federal law within the preceding 24 months [@CL-CMP-9027].

Predecessor, paragraph (r). The paragraph applies in all solicitations that include the provision at 52.204-16, Commercial and Government Entity Code Reporting [@CL-CMP-9028]. The offeror represents that it is or is not a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-CMP-9029]. An offeror that has indicated it is a successor enters information for all predecessors that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years, listing them in reverse chronological order if there is more than one [@CL-CMP-9030]. For each predecessor it enters the CAGE code, or marks Unknown, and the legal name, without a doing business as name [@CL-CMP-9195].

## How it works

The offeror represents whether it has or does not have an immediate owner, and an offeror with more than one immediate owner (such as a joint venture) shall respond for each participant in the joint venture [@CL-CMP-9019]. The immediate owner entry and the highest-level owner entry each call for a CAGE code and a legal name, without a doing business as name [@CL-CMP-9020; @CL-CMP-9021].

Under paragraph (q)(1), the Government will not enter into a contract with a corporation described there where the awarding agency is aware of the unpaid tax liability or the conviction, unless an agency has considered suspension or debarment of the corporation and made a determination that suspension or debarment is not necessary to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-CMP-9194; @CL-CMP-9023; @CL-CMP-9024; @CL-CMP-9025]. Under paragraph (q)(2), the offeror represents whether it is, or is not, a corporation that has the described unpaid Federal tax liability and whether it is, or is not, a corporation that was convicted of a felony criminal violation under a Federal law within the preceding 24 months [@CL-CMP-9026; @CL-CMP-9027].

In paragraph (r), the offeror represents whether it is or is not a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years, and enters information for all such predecessors, listing them in reverse chronological order if there is more than one [@CL-CMP-9029; @CL-CMP-9030].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text marks FAR 52.212-3 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1531]. The owner and predecessor information is in RFO FAR 52.204-90, which an Offeror that will not have an active SAM registration when it submits its offer completes in paragraphs (c) and (d) [@CL-CMP-1544]. Paragraph (d)(2) of that provision calls for the CAGE code and legal business name, not a "doing business as" name, for the immediate owner or owners, the highest-level owner and any predecessor, or predecessor of an Offeror's predecessor, that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-CMP-1545]. That paragraph prints no choice of whether the offeror has an immediate owner, no question whether the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity and no choice of whether the offeror is a successor, which the codified paragraphs (p) and (r) print [@CL-CMP-1545; @CL-CMP-9019; @CL-CMP-9020; @CL-CMP-9029]. Paragraph (d)(3) keeps the rule that an offeror with more than one immediate owner, such as a joint venture, gives the information for each owner, and the rule that predecessors are listed in reverse chronological order [@CL-CMP-9019; @CL-CMP-9030; @CL-CMP-1546]. The predecessor CAGE code may still be marked "Unknown" [@CL-CMP-9195; @CL-CMP-1547]. The tax and felony bars are in RFO FAR 52.209-11 [@CL-CMP-1548]. Its paragraph (a) opens with "The Government will not enter into a contract" and prints no lead-in naming sections 744 and 745 of Division E of the Consolidated and Further Continuing Appropriations Act, 2015, which the codified paragraph (q)(1) prints [@CL-CMP-1548; @CL-CMP-9194]. Paragraph (a)(1) says the tax bar applies where the awarding agency is aware of the unpaid tax liability [@CL-CMP-1549], and does not apply if an agency has considered suspension or debarment and made a determination that suspension or debarment is not necessary to protect the interests of the Government [@CL-CMP-1550]. Paragraph (a)(2) says the felony bar applies where the awarding agency is aware of a conviction within the preceding 24 months [@CL-CMP-1551], and does not apply if an agency has made the same kind of determination that this action is not necessary [@CL-CMP-1552]. Paragraph (b) says the Offeror represents whether it is or is not a corporation that has any unpaid Federal tax liability that has been assessed, for which all judicial and administrative remedies have been exhausted or have lapsed, and that is not being paid in a timely manner pursuant to an agreement with the authority responsible for collecting the tax liability [@CL-CMP-1553; @CL-CMP-1554] and whether it is or is not a corporation convicted of a felony criminal violation under a Federal law within the preceding 24 months [@CL-CMP-1555].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation requires SAM registration and includes the entity code reporting provision, so the ownership and predecessor paragraphs apply [@CL-CMP-9018; @CL-CMP-9028]. The offeror is a joint venture of two members, so it responds to the immediate owner questions, and where a member's immediate owner is itself owned or controlled by another entity, to the highest-level owner questions, for each participant [@CL-CMP-9019]. One member is owned by a holding company that is owned by a parent, so that member enters the holding company as its immediate owner and the parent as its highest-level owner, each by CAGE code and legal name [@CL-CMP-9020; @CL-CMP-9021].

The offeror marks that it is not a corporation with an assessed, unpaid Federal tax liability that is not being paid in a timely manner, and that it is not a corporation convicted of a Federal felony within the preceding 24 months [@CL-CMP-9026; @CL-CMP-9027]. It became a successor to one predecessor that held a Federal contract two years ago, which is within the look-back, so it marks that it is a successor and enters the predecessor's legal name and CAGE code, or Unknown if the code is not known [@CL-CMP-9029; @CL-CMP-9030; @CL-CMP-9195].

## Never confuse

If the Offeror indicates “yes” in paragraph (p)(2), indicating that the immediate owner is owned or controlled by another entity, it enters the highest-level owner's CAGE code and legal name [@CL-CMP-9021]. The Government's bar in paragraph (q)(1) applies where the awarding agency is aware of the unpaid tax liability or of the conviction [@CL-CMP-9023; @CL-CMP-9024]. The offeror's representation in paragraph (q)(2) is whether it is, or is not, the described corporation [@CL-CMP-9026; @CL-CMP-9027]. Paragraph (r) concerns a successor to a predecessor that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years [@CL-CMP-9029].

The codified paragraphs (p) and (r) have the offeror answer yes-or-no questions about owners and successor status, where RFO FAR 52.204-90(d)(2) asks the Offeror to provide the codes and names directly [@CL-CMP-9019; @CL-CMP-9029; @CL-CMP-1545]. The codified paragraph (q)(1) opens with a statutory lead-in, where RFO FAR 52.209-11(a) opens without that lead-in [@CL-CMP-9194; @CL-CMP-1548].

## Company calibration

The company must confirm which of its offers carry these answers and who assembles the owner and predecessor records [@Q-004].
