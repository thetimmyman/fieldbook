## Purpose

Shows what a lead system integrator may not have, and what an offeror represents about its role and its financial interests.

## The rule

| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4106; @CL-CMP-4136] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.209-7006 and DFARS 252.209-7007 [@CL-CMP-4106; @CL-CMP-4136] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4106; @CL-CMP-4136] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include the provision, as prescribed in DFARS 209.570-4(a) [@CL-CMP-4106], and contracts that include the clause, as prescribed in DFARS 209.570-4(b) [@CL-CMP-4136] |

Unless an exception is granted, no contractor performing lead system integrator functions in the acquisition of a major system by the Department of Defense may have any direct financial interest in the development or construction of any individual system or element of any system of systems [@CL-CMP-4107]. The contract clause says the same for the Contractor, who shall not have such an interest while performing lead system integrator functions in the acquisition of a major system by the Department of Defense under this contract, unless an exception is granted [@CL-CMP-4110; @CL-CMP-8026].

In its representations the offeror states whether it does or does not propose to perform the contract as a lead system integrator with system responsibility, and separately whether it does or does not propose to perform it as a lead system integrator without system responsibility [@CL-CMP-4108]. If the offeror answered in the affirmative to either lead system integrator representation, it also represents whether it does or does not have any direct financial interest as described in paragraph (b) of the provision with respect to the system(s), subsystem(s), system of systems, or services described in this solicitation [@CL-CMP-4109; @CL-CMP-7057].

The Contractor agrees that during performance of the contract it will not acquire any direct financial interest as described in paragraph (b) of the clause, or, if it does acquire or plan to acquire such interest, it will immediately notify the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4111]. If the Contracting Officer determines that the Contractor misrepresented its financial interests at the time of award or has violated that agreement, the Government may terminate the contract for default for the Contractor's material failure to comply with the terms and conditions of award or may take other remedial measures as appropriate in the Contracting Officer's sole discretion [@CL-CMP-4112; @CL-CMP-8027].

## How it works

The provision collects representations at the offer stage: the offeror's role first, then its financial interest if it answered in the affirmative on the role [@CL-CMP-4108; @CL-CMP-4109]. The clause carries the limit into the contract and adds a duty to notify the Contracting Officer at once if a direct financial interest is acquired or planned [@CL-CMP-4110; @CL-CMP-4111]. If the Contracting Officer determines that the Contractor misrepresented its financial interests at the time of award or has violated that agreement, the Government may terminate the contract for default for the Contractor's material failure to comply with the terms and conditions of award or may take other remedial measures as appropriate in the Contracting Officer's sole discretion [@CL-CMP-4112; @CL-CMP-8027]. A lead system integrator with system responsibility is a prime contractor for the development or production of a major system, if the prime contractor is not expected at the time of award to perform a substantial portion of the work on the system and the major subsystems [@CL-CMP-4113]. The definition of a lead system integrator without system responsibility begins with a prime contractor under a contract for the procurement of services, the primary purpose of which is to perform acquisition functions closely associated with inherently governmental functions [@CL-CMP-4134].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor will develop a major system and expects to pass nearly all of the work on the system and the major subsystems to 6 subcontractors. After award, it plans to buy a stake in one subcontractor that builds a key subsystem. Assume the stake is a direct financial interest in building an element of the system.

Answer: the prime fits the description of a lead system integrator with system responsibility. The planned stake is then a direct financial interest in building an element of the system, so the prime must notify the Contracting Officer at once. Unless an exception is granted, the interest is not permitted.

## Never confuse

The provision asks the offeror to represent facts at the offer stage, and the clause binds the Contractor during performance [@CL-CMP-4108; @CL-CMP-4111]. A lead system integrator with system responsibility is defined by its expected share of the work [@CL-CMP-4113]. The clause defines a lead system integrator without system responsibility as a prime contractor under a contract for the procurement of services, the primary purpose of which is to perform acquisition functions closely associated with inherently governmental functions [@CL-CMP-4134].
