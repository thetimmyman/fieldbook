## Purpose

Shows what license the contractor grants the Government in a patent release, license agreement or assignment, how far it reaches, how long it lasts and how the Government may end it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4249] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7004, 252.227-7005 and 252.227-7003 [@CL-CMP-4250; @CL-CMP-4253; @CL-CMP-4256] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4250] |
| Applies to | Patent releases, license agreements and assignments, for the License Grant clause [@CL-CMP-4249]; the License Term clause is prescribed with an instruction to insert one of its alternates in the same kinds of agreement [@CL-CMP-4373] |

The Contractor grants the Government an irrevocable, nonexclusive, nontransferable and paid up license under the patents and patent applications listed in the clause, and patents granted on those applications, and under any patents which may issue as the result of any reissue, division or continuation of them [@CL-CMP-4250; @CL-CMP-7695]. The Government may practice the licensed inventions, or have them practiced for it, throughout the world in manufacturing and using articles or materials, in using methods or processes, and in disposing of articles or materials in accordance with law [@CL-CMP-4251]. The license reaches corresponding foreign patents and foreign applications for patents insofar as the Contractor has the right to grant licenses under them without incurring an obligation to pay royalties or other compensation to others solely on account of the grant [@CL-CMP-4252]. No rights are granted or implied by the agreement under any other patents other than as provided in the clause or by operation of law, and nothing in the clause limits any rights the Government may have obtained through prior contracts or by operation of law or otherwise [@CL-CMP-4364].

The License Term clause is prescribed with an instruction to insert one of its two alternates [@CL-CMP-4373]. Under Alternate I the license remains in full force and effect for the full term of each patent referred to in the License Grant clause and of any patent hereafter issued on an application referred to in that clause [@CL-CMP-4253]. Under Alternate II the license terminates on a date that the contract fills in [@CL-CMP-4254], and Alternate II provides that this termination is without prejudice to completing any contract the Government entered into before the date of termination, or to the later use or disposition of articles or materials manufactured by or for the Government under the license [@CL-CMP-4255]. Separately, notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, the Termination clause gives the Government the right to terminate the license, in whole or in part, by giving the Contractor not less than 30 days notice in writing of the date the termination is to be effective [@CL-CMP-4256]. Termination under that clause does not affect the Government's obligation to pay royalties that have accrued before the effective date of the termination [@CL-CMP-4257].

## How it works

The Government may practice, or cause to be practiced for it, the inventions under the listed patents throughout the world [@CL-CMP-4250; @CL-CMP-4251]. Under Alternate I of the License Term clause the license remains in force for the full term of each referred patent [@CL-CMP-4253], and under Alternate II it terminates on the date the contract fills in [@CL-CMP-4254]. Notwithstanding any other provision of the contract, the Government has the right to terminate the license, in whole or in part, by giving the Contractor not less than 30 days notice in writing of the date the termination is to be effective [@CL-CMP-4256]. Termination under that clause does not affect the Government's obligation to pay royalties which have accrued prior to the effective date of the termination [@CL-CMP-4257].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A license agreement lists two patents and uses Alternate I, so the license would run for the full term of both. The Government sends written notice on day 100 that it ends the license in part, effective day 140. The notice gives 40 days, which is more than the 30-day minimum, so the termination is effective on day 140. Any royalties that accrued before day 140 are still owed.
