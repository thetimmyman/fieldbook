## Purpose

Shows two points in the defense supplement. DFARS 242.602, on the assignment and location of a corporate administrative contracting officer, says where a matter goes if the agencies cannot agree [@CL-CMP-8766; @CL-CMP-8777]. DFARS 242.705-1, the contracting officer determination procedure for final indirect cost rates, says who decides how negotiations are conducted [@CL-CMP-8767; @CL-CMP-8779].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-8766; @CL-CMP-8767] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 242.602(c)(2) [@CL-CMP-8766] and DFARS 242.705-1(a)(1) [@CL-CMP-8767; @CL-CMP-8768] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8766] |
| Applies to | A matter on which the agencies cannot agree, under paragraph (c)(2) of the assignment and location section [@CL-CMP-8766]; negotiations under the contracting officer determination procedure [@CL-CMP-8767] |

DFARS 242.602 is headed Assignment and location and sits in Subpart 242.6, Corporate Administrative Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-8766; @CL-CMP-8777]. Only paragraph (c)(2) is printed: if the agencies cannot agree, the matter is to be referred to the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy [@CL-CMP-8766]. The paragraph does not say which agencies are meant, who makes the referral, or what the Principal Director then does. It sets no threshold and no deadline for the referral [@CL-CMP-8766]. FAR 42.602(c)(2) has the agencies concerned agree on the responsible agency when the locations are under the contract administration cognizance of more than one agency, normally on the basis of the agency with the largest dollar balance, including options, of affected contracts [@CL-CMP-2212]. See [Corporate ACO assignment and Government visits](cmp-corporate-aco-and-facility-visits.md).

DFARS 242.705-1 is headed Contracting officer determination procedure [@CL-CMP-8767]. It follows DFARS 242.705, Final indirect cost rates, in Subpart 242.7, Indirect Cost Rates [@CL-CMP-8767; @CL-CMP-8779; @CL-CMP-8778]. Its paragraph (a) is headed Applicability and responsibility. The corporate administrative contracting officer and individual administrative contracting officers shall jointly decide how to conduct negotiations [@CL-CMP-8767]. The printed paragraph does not say which of them leads, and it sets no deadline [@CL-CMP-8767].

The same paragraph says to follow the procedures at PGI 242.705-1(a)(1) when negotiations are conducted on a coordinated basis [@CL-CMP-8768]. The sentence does not name who follows them [@CL-CMP-8768].

## How it works

The duty in DFARS 242.705-1(a)(1) falls on the corporate administrative contracting officer and the individual administrative contracting officers [@CL-CMP-8767]. They decide jointly [@CL-CMP-8767]. When negotiations are conducted on a coordinated basis, the named PGI procedures are to be followed as well [@CL-CMP-8768]. Neither printed paragraph places a duty on the contractor [@CL-CMP-8766; @CL-CMP-8767].

The referral in DFARS 242.602(c)(2) is for the case where the agencies cannot agree [@CL-CMP-8766]. The printed text gives the escalation point. It does not say that the Principal Director decides the matter. The FAR rules on the contractor's final indirect cost rate proposal and the negotiation record are on [Final indirect cost rates: contracting officer determination procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-co-final-indirect-rates.md). For how a contract comes to be assigned to a contract administration office, see [Assignment of contract administration](cmp-assignment-of-contract-administration.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Two agencies cannot agree on a matter that paragraph (c)(2) covers. The matter is referred to the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy [@CL-CMP-8766]. The printed paragraph does not say what the Principal Director does next, so the example stops at the referral.

Separately, a contractor's final indirect cost rates are negotiated under the contracting officer determination procedure. The corporate administrative contracting officer and the individual administrative contracting officers jointly decide how to conduct the negotiations [@CL-CMP-8767]. If the negotiations are conducted on a coordinated basis, the procedures at PGI 242.705-1(a)(1) are to be followed [@CL-CMP-8768].

## Never confuse

Assignment and location is not the determination procedure. The first section sits in the corporate administrative contracting officer subpart and sends a disagreement between agencies to the Principal Director [@CL-CMP-8766; @CL-CMP-8777]. The second sits in the indirect cost rates subpart and has the administrative contracting officers decide how to conduct negotiations [@CL-CMP-8767; @CL-CMP-8778].

The referral is for a disagreement between agencies. It is not the route for a disagreement between the corporate administrative contracting officer and an individual officer, which the printed text does not address [@CL-CMP-8766; @CL-CMP-8767].
