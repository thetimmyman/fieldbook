## Purpose

Shows what it means when a solicitation or contract lists provisions or clauses by reference, how full text can be obtained, what the offeror may do with blocks to be completed, and how an alteration to a solicitation is introduced.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5998] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.252-1; FAR 52.252-2; FAR 52.252-3 [@CL-CMP-5998; @CL-CMP-5999; @CL-CMP-6004] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.252-1 [@CL-CMP-1333]; RFO FAR 52.252-2 [@CL-CMP-1337]; RFO FAR 52.252-3 [@CL-CMP-1339] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5998] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that incorporate provisions by reference, contracts that incorporate clauses by reference, and solicitations that carry an alteration [@CL-CMP-5998; @CL-CMP-5999; @CL-CMP-6004] |

A solicitation that incorporates one or more solicitation provisions by reference gives them the same force and effect as if they were given in full text [@CL-CMP-5998]. A contract that incorporates one or more clauses by reference gives them the same force and effect as if they were given in full text [@CL-CMP-5999].

Upon request, the contracting officer will make the full text of the incorporated provisions available [@CL-CMP-6000], and the same is said for the incorporated clauses [@CL-CMP-6698]. The full text of a solicitation provision may also be accessed electronically at the one or more addresses inserted in the solicitation for that purpose [@CL-CMP-6697]. The full text of a clause may be accessed electronically at the one or more addresses inserted in the contract for that purpose [@CL-CMP-6001].

## How it works

### Obtaining the text

The solicitation provision and the clause each carry a bracketed instruction to insert one or more Internet addresses [@CL-CMP-6697; @CL-CMP-6001].

### Blocks to be completed

The offeror is cautioned that the listed provisions may include blocks that must be completed by the offeror and submitted with its quotation or offer [@CL-CMP-6002]. In lieu of submitting the full text of those provisions, the offeror may identify the provision by paragraph identifier and provide the appropriate information with its quotation or offer [@CL-CMP-6003].

### Alterations in a solicitation

The alterations provision is inserted in solicitations to revise or supplement, as necessary, other parts of the solicitation that apply to the solicitation phase only, except for any provision authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-6004]. The provision is to include clear identification of what is being altered [@CL-CMP-6699]. The provision states that portions of the solicitation are altered as follows [@CL-CMP-6700].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.252-1 keeps the same force and effect as full text for solicitation provisions incorporated by reference, and the Contracting Officer's making their full text available on request [@CL-CMP-1333; @CL-CMP-5998; @CL-CMP-6000]. It keeps the caution about blocks to be completed [@CL-CMP-1334; @CL-CMP-6002] and the offeror's option to identify a provision by paragraph identifier [@CL-CMP-1335; @CL-CMP-6003]. RFO FAR 52.252-2 keeps the same force and effect as full text for clauses incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-1337; @CL-CMP-5999] and the Contracting Officer's making their full text available on request [@CL-CMP-1337; @CL-CMP-6698]. Both RFO texts keep electronic access to the full text at the addresses inserted [@CL-CMP-1336; @CL-CMP-6697; @CL-CMP-1338; @CL-CMP-6001], and each prints three blank lines before the bracketed instruction to insert one or more Internet addresses [@CL-CMP-1336; @CL-CMP-1338].

RFO FAR 52.252-3 keeps the insertion of the provision in solicitations to revise or supplement other parts of the solicitation that apply to the solicitation phase only, except for any provision authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-1339; @CL-CMP-6004], and the clear identification of what is being altered [@CL-CMP-1340; @CL-CMP-6699]. After the statement that portions of the solicitation are altered as follows, RFO FAR 52.252-3 prints three blank lines [@CL-CMP-1341; @CL-CMP-6700].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation lists four provisions by reference, and one of them contains a block that the offeror must complete. The offeror does not ask for the full text. It returns its quotation with the provision identified by paragraph identifier and the block's information supplied [@CL-CMP-6003], and the provision has the same force and effect as if it had been printed in full [@CL-CMP-5998]. If the offeror wanted the full text, it could ask the contracting officer, who will make it available [@CL-CMP-6000], or use the address that the solicitation gives for electronic access [@CL-CMP-6697].

The same solicitation carries an alteration that revises the instruction on how quotations are submitted, a part that applies to the solicitation phase only [@CL-CMP-6004]. The alteration says clearly which instruction it changes and how [@CL-CMP-6699]. The contracting officer does not use the alteration to change a provision that is authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-6004].

## Never confuse

Incorporation by reference is not an omission: provisions and clauses incorporated by reference have the same force and effect as if they were given in full text [@CL-CMP-5998; @CL-CMP-5999]. The alterations provision differs, being inserted to revise or supplement other parts of the solicitation that apply to the solicitation phase only, except for any provision authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-6004]. The marking of deviations, and the alterations that apply after award, are on [Alterations in a contract and authorized deviations in provisions and clauses](cmp-contract-alterations-and-authorized-deviations.md). Representations and certifications that are incorporated by reference are on [Incorporation of representations and certifications, and law for a breach claim](cmp-reps-and-certs-incorporation-and-breach-law.md). The alterations provision states that portions of the solicitation are altered as follows [@CL-CMP-6700], and RFO FAR 52.252-3 prints three blank lines after that statement [@CL-CMP-1341].
