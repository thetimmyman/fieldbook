## Purpose

Defines the two terms FAR 22.1901 prints, United States and worker, as used in FAR subpart 22.19 on the minimum wage for contractors, where a worker is a person working on or in connection with a contract covered by Executive Order 14026 [@CL-CMP-8172]. The definitions are stated so that contract coverage, geographic scope and headcount rest on the printed definitions.

United States means the 50 States, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the United States Virgin Islands, Johnston Island, Wake Island, and the outer Continental Shelf as defined in the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (title 43 of the United States Code, section 1331 and following) [@CL-CMP-8171].

Worker is defined in accordance with 29 CFR 23.20. It means any person engaged in performing work on, or in connection with, a contract covered by Executive Order 14026 [@CL-CMP-8172]. Paragraph (1)(i)(A): the person's wages under the contract are governed by the Fair Labor Standards Act (title 29 of the United States Code, chapter 8), the Service Contract Labor Standards statute (title 41, chapter 67), or the Wage Rate Requirements (Construction) statute (title 40, chapter 31, subchapter IV) [@CL-CMP-8173].

Paragraph (1)(i)(B): the person is other than an individual employed in a bona fide executive, administrative, or professional capacity, as those terms are defined in 29 CFR part 541 [@CL-CMP-8174].

Paragraph (1)(i)(C): the definition applies regardless of the contractual relationship alleged to exist between the individual and the employer [@CL-CMP-8190].

Paragraph (1)(ii): the definition also includes workers performing on, or in connection with, the contract whose wages are calculated pursuant to special certificates issued under section 214(c) of title 29 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-8175].

Paragraph (1)(iii): it also includes any person working on, or in connection with, the contract and individually registered in a bona fide apprenticeship or training program registered with the Department of Labor's Employment and Training Administration, Office of Apprenticeship, or with a State Apprenticeship Agency recognized by the Office of Apprenticeship [@CL-CMP-8176; @CL-CMP-8177].

Paragraph (2) says when a worker performs on a contract and when a worker performs in connection with it [@CL-CMP-8178; @CL-CMP-8179].

## How it works

The worker definition has a coverage element and a wage element. The person must be engaged in work on, or in connection with, a contract covered by Executive Order 14026, and the person's wages under the contract must be governed by one of the three named statutes [@CL-CMP-8172; @CL-CMP-8173].

Executive, administrative and professional employees as defined in 29 CFR part 541 are outside paragraph (1)(i) of the definition [@CL-CMP-8174]. The content of those part 541 terms is Not established here.

The label the parties give the relationship does not decide who is a worker. The definition applies regardless of the contractual relationship alleged to exist between the individual and the employer [@CL-CMP-8190].

Two groups are added by their own paragraphs. They are workers paid under special certificates, and persons individually registered in a bona fide apprenticeship or training program of the kind described [@CL-CMP-8175; @CL-CMP-8176; @CL-CMP-8177].

Two terms describe the link to the contract. Directly performing the specific services called for by the contract is performing on the contract. Doing work that is necessary to performance but is not those services is performing in connection with it [@CL-CMP-8178; @CL-CMP-8179].

The section does not say who decides whether a person is a worker, and it prints no headcount or dollar threshold. That is Not established here.

The coverage and exclusion rules that use this definition are on [Executive order minimum wage coverage and paid sick leave exclusions](cmp-eo-wage-and-sick-leave-coverage-and-exclusions.md) and [Minimum wage and paid sick leave policy on covered contracts](cmp-minimum-wage-and-paid-sick-leave-policy.md). They are not restated here.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cleaning crew of 40 works at a site under a contract covered by Executive Order 14026 [@CL-CMP-8172]. Thirty crew members perform the cleaning services named in the contract, so they perform on the contract. Ten keep the supply inventory, which is necessary to performance but not a service the contract calls for, so they perform in connection with it [@CL-CMP-8178; @CL-CMP-8179].

All 40 are workers if their wages under the contract are governed by one of the three named statutes and none is employed in a bona fide executive, administrative, or professional capacity [@CL-CMP-8172; @CL-CMP-8173; @CL-CMP-8174]. A site manager who is not one of the 40 and is assumed to be employed in a bona fide executive capacity under 29 CFR part 541, is outside paragraph (1)(i) of the definition [@CL-CMP-8174].

If the company calls the 10 inventory staff independent contractors, that label alone does not remove them from the definition [@CL-CMP-8190].

## Never confuse

Never confuse performing on a contract with performing in connection with a contract. Direct work on the specific services the contract calls for is the first, and work that is necessary to performance but is not those services is the second [@CL-CMP-8178; @CL-CMP-8179].

Never confuse paragraph (1)(i) of the worker definition with the whole of it. Special certificate workers and registered apprentices or trainees are added by paragraphs (1)(ii) and (1)(iii) [@CL-CMP-8175; @CL-CMP-8176; @CL-CMP-8177].
