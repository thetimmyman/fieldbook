## Purpose

Shows whom the Recovery Act whistleblower sections cover, the reprisal they prohibit, who may see the Inspector General file, and the court relief available.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2338] |
| Codified citation | FAR 3.907-1, FAR 3.907-2, FAR 3.907-5 and FAR 3.907-6 [@CL-CMP-2338; @CL-CMP-2462; @CL-CMP-2340; @CL-CMP-2341] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2338] |
| Applies to | Non-Federal employers that receive Recovery Act funds [@CL-CMP-2339; @CL-CMP-2462] |

Non-Federal employers are prohibited from discharging, demoting, or otherwise discriminating against an employee as a reprisal for disclosing covered information to any of the following entities or their representatives: the Board; an Inspector General; the Comptroller General; a member of Congress; a State or Federal regulatory or law enforcement agency; a person with supervisory authority over the employee or such other person working for the employer who has the authority to investigate, discover, or terminate misconduct; a court or grand jury; or the head of a Federal agency [@CL-CMP-2462; @CL-CMP-0339; @CL-CMP-0340]. Covered information means information that the employee reasonably believes is evidence of gross mismanagement of the contract or subcontract related to covered funds, gross waste of covered funds, a substantial and specific danger to public health or safety related to the implementation or use of covered funds, an abuse of authority related to the implementation or use of covered funds, or a violation of law, rule, or regulation related to an agency contract (including the competition for or negotiation of a contract) awarded or issued relating to covered funds [@CL-CMP-2463; @CL-CMP-0341; @CL-CMP-0342].

Covered funds means any contract payment, grant payment, or other payment received by a contractor if the Federal Government provides any portion of the money or property that is provided, requested, or demanded and at least some of the funds are appropriated or otherwise made available by the Recovery Act [@CL-CMP-2338]. A non-Federal employer means any employer that receives Recovery Act funds, including a contractor, subcontractor, or other recipient of funds pursuant to a contract or other agreement awarded and administered in accordance with the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CMP-2339].

The employee alleging reprisal under the section shall have access to the investigation file of the Inspector General, in accordance with the Privacy Act, section 552a of title 5 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-2340]. In any action brought under FAR 3.907-6, the court may grant appropriate relief, including injunctive relief, compensatory and exemplary damages, and attorneys fees and costs [@CL-CMP-2341].

## How it works

Covered funds means any contract payment, grant payment, or other payment received by a contractor if both conditions are met: the Federal Government provides any portion of the money or property that is provided, requested, or demanded, and at least some of the funds are appropriated or otherwise made available by the Recovery Act [@CL-CMP-2338]. A non-Federal employer is any employer that receives Recovery Act funds, including a contractor, subcontractor, or other recipient of funds pursuant to a contract or other agreement awarded and administered in accordance with the FAR [@CL-CMP-2339]. Non-Federal employers are prohibited from discharging, demoting, or otherwise discriminating against an employee as a reprisal for disclosing covered information to any of the eight entities in FAR 3.907-2 or their representatives [@CL-CMP-2462; @CL-CMP-0339; @CL-CMP-0340].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subcontractor receives a payment financed entirely from non-Recovery Act appropriations. That payment is not covered funds, so these sections do not reach it. A second payment includes some Recovery Act money, so it is covered funds. An employee of the subcontractor who is demoted for telling the Inspector General about gross waste of the second payment's funds suffers a reprisal that the section prohibits.

## Never confuse

Covered funds under these sections require that the Federal Government provide any portion of the money or property that is provided, requested, or demanded and that at least some of the funds be appropriated or otherwise made available by the Recovery Act [@CL-CMP-2338]. For general whistleblower protections for contractor employees, see [Whistleblower protections for contractor employees: policy and remedies](cmp-contractor-employee-whistleblower-protections.md).
