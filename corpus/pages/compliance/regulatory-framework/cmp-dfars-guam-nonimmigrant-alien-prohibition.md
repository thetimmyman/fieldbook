## Purpose

Shows which workers may not perform the contract work under the Guam clause, and which workers are excepted.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4419] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.222-7005 [@CL-CMP-4419] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4419] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the Prohibition on Use of Nonimmigrant Aliens—Guam clause, used as prescribed in section 222.7302 [@CL-CMP-4419] |

The work required by the contract shall not be performed by any alien who is issued a visa or otherwise provided nonimmigrant status under Section 101(a)(15)(H)(ii) of the Immigration and Nationality Act [@CL-CMP-4420].

The prohibition does not apply to work performed by lawfully admitted citizens of the freely associated states of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, the Federated States of Micronesia, or the Republic of Palau [@CL-CMP-4421].

## How it works

The work required by the contract shall not be performed by any alien who is issued a visa or otherwise provided nonimmigrant status under Section 101(a)(15)(H)(ii) of the Immigration and Nationality Act [@CL-CMP-4420]. The prohibition does not apply to work performed by lawfully admitted citizens of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, the Federated States of Micronesia, or the Republic of Palau [@CL-CMP-4421].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract with the Guam clause needs 10 workers for a task. Four hold a visa under the barred section. Six are lawfully admitted citizens of the Republic of Palau. Answer: the four may not perform the contract work, and the six may. Assumed for the example: the four hold the barred status and none of them is a citizen of one of the three named states.

## Never confuse

The prohibition is a visa-status bar rather than a general employment bar: the work required by the contract shall not be performed by any alien who is issued a visa or otherwise provided nonimmigrant status under Section 101(a)(15)(H)(ii) of the Immigration and Nationality Act [@CL-CMP-4420], and the prohibition does not apply to work performed by lawfully admitted citizens of the freely associated states of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, the Federated States of Micronesia, or the Republic of Palau [@CL-CMP-4421]. For the residency clause, see [Restrictions on employment of personnel](cmp-dfars-restrictions-on-employment-of-personnel.md).
