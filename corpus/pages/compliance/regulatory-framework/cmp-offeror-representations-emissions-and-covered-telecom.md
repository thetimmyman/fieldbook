## Purpose

Explains the greenhouse gas disclosure representation in paragraph (t), the representation on internal confidentiality agreements or statements that restrict lawful reporting of waste, fraud or abuse in paragraph (u), and the covered telecommunications equipment or services representation in paragraph (v) of the commercial offeror representations provision, and what each asks of the offeror.

An offeror that has completed the annual representations and certification electronically in the System for Award Management completes only paragraph (b) of the provision [@CL-CMP-9250]. An offeror that has not completed them electronically shall complete only paragraphs (c) through (v), which include paragraphs (t), (u) and (v) [@CL-CMP-9251].

Greenhouse gas, paragraph (t). The paragraph applies in all solicitations that require offerors to register in SAM, as FAR 12.301(d)(1) describes [@CL-CMP-9031]. The representation shall be completed if the offeror received $7.5 million or more in contract awards in the prior Federal fiscal year, and it is optional if the offeror received less than $7.5 million in Federal contract awards in the prior Federal fiscal year [@CL-CMP-9032].

The offeror, itself or through its immediate owner or highest-level owner, checks whether it does or does not publicly disclose greenhouse gas emissions [@CL-CMP-9033]. That means making available on a publicly accessible Web site the results of a greenhouse gas inventory, performed in accordance with an accounting standard with publicly available and consistently applied criteria, such as the Greenhouse Gas Protocol Corporate Standard [@CL-CMP-9196]. The offeror, again itself or through its immediate owner or highest-level owner, checks whether it does or does not publicly disclose a quantitative greenhouse gas emissions reduction goal [@CL-CMP-9034]. That means making available on a publicly accessible Web site a target to reduce absolute emissions or emissions intensity by a specific quantity or percentage [@CL-CMP-9197]. A publicly accessible Web site includes the offeror's own Web site or a recognized, third-party greenhouse gas emissions reporting program [@CL-CMP-9198]. An offeror that checked does in paragraph (t)(2)(i) or (t)(2)(ii) shall provide the publicly accessible Web site or sites where greenhouse gas emissions or reduction goals are reported [@CL-CMP-9035].

Internal confidentiality agreements, paragraph (u). Paragraph (u)(1) rests on section 743 of Division E, Title VII, of the Consolidated and Further Continuing Appropriations Act, 2015 (Public Law 113-235) and its successor provisions in subsequent appropriations acts, as extended in continuing resolutions [@CL-CMP-9252]. Government agencies are not permitted to use appropriated (or otherwise made available) funds for contracts with an entity that requires employees or subcontractors of such entity seeking to report waste, fraud, or abuse to sign internal confidentiality agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-9253]. The agreements or statements covered are those prohibiting or otherwise restricting such employees or subcontractors from lawfully reporting such waste, fraud, or abuse to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information [@CL-CMP-9254]. Paragraph (u)(2) says the prohibition in paragraph (u)(1) does not contravene requirements applicable to Standard Form 312 (Classified Information Nondisclosure Agreement) or Form 4414 (Sensitive Compartmented Information Nondisclosure Agreement) [@CL-CMP-9255], or to any other form issued by a Federal department or agency governing the nondisclosure of classified information [@CL-CMP-9258]. In paragraph (u)(3), by submission of its offer, the offeror represents that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with internal confidentiality agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-9256]. Those are agreements or statements prohibiting or otherwise restricting such employees or subcontractors from lawfully reporting waste, fraud, or abuse related to the performance of a Government contract [@CL-CMP-9257], to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information (e.g., agency Office of the Inspector General) [@CL-CMP-9259].

Covered telecommunications, paragraph (v). The representation is tied to section 889(a)(1)(A) and section 889(a)(1)(B) of Public Law 115-232 [@CL-CMP-9199]. The offeror shall review the list of excluded parties in the System for Award Management [@CL-CMP-9036]. The review is for entities excluded from receiving federal awards for covered telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-9200]. The offeror then represents whether it does or does not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract, or other contractual instrument [@CL-CMP-9037]. After conducting a reasonable inquiry for purposes of the representation, it also represents whether it does or does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or any equipment, system, or service that uses covered telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-9038].

## How it works

The offeror (itself or through its immediate owner or highest-level owner) checks whether it does or does not publicly disclose greenhouse gas emissions and a quantitative greenhouse gas emissions reduction goal [@CL-CMP-9033; @CL-CMP-9034; @CL-CMP-9196; @CL-CMP-9197]. The representation shall be completed if the offeror received $7.5 million or more in contract awards in the prior Federal fiscal year, and is optional if it received less than $7.5 million in Federal contract awards in the prior Federal fiscal year [@CL-CMP-9032]. If the offeror checked "does", it shall provide the publicly accessible Web site or sites where greenhouse gas emissions or reduction goals are reported [@CL-CMP-9035].

Under paragraph (u)(1), Government agencies are not permitted to use appropriated (or otherwise made available) funds for contracts with an entity that requires employees or subcontractors of such entity seeking to report waste, fraud, or abuse to sign internal confidentiality agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-9253]. Under paragraph (u)(3), by submission of its offer, the Offeror represents that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with internal confidentiality agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-9256]. The prohibition in paragraph (u)(1) does not contravene requirements applicable to Standard Form 312, Form 4414, or any other form issued by a Federal department or agency governing the nondisclosure of classified information [@CL-CMP-9255; @CL-CMP-9258].

The offeror shall review the list of excluded parties in the System for Award Management for entities excluded from receiving federal awards for covered telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-9036]. The offeror represents whether it does or does not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to the Government [@CL-CMP-9037], and, after conducting a reasonable inquiry for purposes of the representation, whether it does or does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or any equipment, system, or service that uses them [@CL-CMP-9038].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text marks FAR 52.212-3 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1531]. The greenhouse gas representation of paragraph (t) has no RFO text under its stand-alone provision number, because the RFO text marks FAR 52.223-22 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1532], and the codified FAR 52.223-22 holds the greenhouse gas representation [@CL-CMP-5447]. The confidentiality agreement representation is in RFO FAR 52.203-18 [@CL-CMP-1533]. Its paragraph (b) keeps the statutory basis, the funding limit on Government agencies and the agreements or statements covered [@CL-CMP-9252; @CL-CMP-9253; @CL-CMP-9254; @CL-CMP-1533; @CL-CMP-1534; @CL-CMP-1535]. Its paragraph (c) says the prohibition in paragraph (b) does not contravene requirements applicable to Standard Form 312, where the codified text refers to paragraph (u)(1) [@CL-CMP-1536; @CL-CMP-9255], and it goes on to name Form 4414 and any other form issued by a Federal department or agency governing the nondisclosure of classified information [@CL-CMP-1537]. Its paragraph (d) keeps the offeror's representation, made by submission of its offer, and the scope of the agreements or statements it covers [@CL-CMP-9256; @CL-CMP-9257; @CL-CMP-9259; @CL-CMP-1538; @CL-CMP-1539]. The covered telecommunications representation is in RFO FAR 52.240-90 [@CL-CMP-1540]. Its paragraph (b)(1) keeps the review of the list of excluded parties in SAM [@CL-CMP-9036; @CL-CMP-1540]. Its paragraph (c) puts the reasonable inquiry wording in the lead-in to both representations, where the codified text puts it only before the use representation [@CL-CMP-1541; @CL-CMP-9038]. Paragraph (c)(1) says the Offeror represents that it will not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract or other contractual instrument resulting from this solicitation, except as waived by the solicitation or as disclosed in paragraph (g) [@CL-CMP-1542], and paragraph (c)(2) says the Offeror represents that it does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or any equipment, system, or service that uses them, with the same exceptions [@CL-CMP-1543]. The codified text has the offeror check whether it does or does not provide and use them [@CL-CMP-9037; @CL-CMP-9038].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror has not completed the annual representations and certifications electronically, so it completes paragraphs (c) through (v) [@CL-CMP-9251]. It received $9 million in contract awards in the prior Federal fiscal year, which is above the figure in the paragraph, so it shall complete the greenhouse gas representation [@CL-CMP-9032]. Its immediate owner publishes the results of an emissions inventory, performed under the Greenhouse Gas Protocol Corporate Standard, on a recognized third-party reporting program, so the offeror checks that it does disclose emissions through its owner, and it provides the Web site [@CL-CMP-9033; @CL-CMP-9196; @CL-CMP-9198; @CL-CMP-9035]. The immediate owner publishes no target to reduce emissions by a quantity or percentage, so the offeror checks that it does not disclose a reduction goal [@CL-CMP-9034; @CL-CMP-9197]. A second offeror that received $6 million in Federal contract awards finds the representation optional [@CL-CMP-9032].

By submitting its offer, the first offeror also represents that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with internal confidentiality agreements or statements restricting their lawful reporting of waste, fraud, or abuse related to the performance of a Government contract to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information [@CL-CMP-9256; @CL-CMP-9257; @CL-CMP-9259]. A classified information nondisclosure agreement on Standard Form 312 follows the requirements applicable to that form, and the prohibition in paragraph (u)(1) does not contravene them [@CL-CMP-9255].

For the telecommunications paragraph, the first offeror reviews the excluded-party list in the System for Award Management, then marks that it does not provide covered equipment or services to the Government and, after a reasonable inquiry, that it does not use them [@CL-CMP-9036; @CL-CMP-9037; @CL-CMP-9038].

## Never confuse

The greenhouse gas representation asks whether the offeror (itself or through its immediate owner or highest-level owner) does or does not publicly disclose greenhouse gas emissions, meaning it makes available on a publicly accessible Web site the results of a greenhouse gas inventory, and whether it does or does not publicly disclose a quantitative greenhouse gas emissions reduction goal, meaning a target to reduce absolute emissions or emissions intensity by a specific quantity or percentage [@CL-CMP-9033; @CL-CMP-9196; @CL-CMP-9034; @CL-CMP-9197]. In paragraph (v)(2), the first representation is whether the offeror does or does not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services as a part of its offered products or services to the Government, and the second, after conducting a reasonable inquiry for purposes of the representation, is whether it does or does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or any equipment, system, or service that uses them [@CL-CMP-9037; @CL-CMP-9038]. The offeror shall review the list of excluded parties in the System for Award Management for entities excluded from receiving federal awards for covered telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-9036].

The codified paragraph (v) has the offeror check whether it does or does not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services, where RFO FAR 52.240-90(c) states that the Offeror will not provide, and does not use, covered telecommunications equipment or services, except as waived by the solicitation or as disclosed in paragraph (g) [@CL-CMP-9037; @CL-CMP-9038; @CL-CMP-1542; @CL-CMP-1543]. RFO part 52 marks FAR 52.223-22 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1532].

## Company calibration

The company must confirm, for each solicitation, whether its emissions disclosure sits with the offeror or with an owner and who records the answer [@Q-004].
