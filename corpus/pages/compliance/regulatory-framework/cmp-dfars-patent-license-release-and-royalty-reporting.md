## Purpose

Shows what the Government receives and pays under a DoD patent license and release contract, how later license terms can readjust payments, and the duties to report and pay royalties.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3958] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7012, 252.227-7002 and 252.227-7009 [@CL-CMP-3958; @CL-CMP-3959; @CL-CMP-3961] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3958] |
| Applies to | Patent releases, license agreements and assignments [@CL-CMP-3958] |

The patent license and release contract clause is inserted in patent releases, license agreements and assignments [@CL-CMP-3958]. Payment under its payment article is in full compensation for the rights granted and agreed to be granted, and for a license on a running royalty basis the article directs insertion of the clause at 252.227-7006 and also the clauses at 252.227-7002, 252.227-7009 and 252.227-7010 [@CL-CMP-3957].

The readjustment clause applies if any license, under substantially the same patents and authorizing substantially the same acts as the contract, has been or shall hereafter be granted within the United States on royalty terms more favorable to the licensee than those in the contract [@CL-CMP-3959]. The Government shall be entitled to the benefit of those more favorable terms for royalties accruing after the date the more favorable terms become effective [@CL-CMP-3960]. The clause also requires the Contractor to promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing of the granting of such more favorable terms [@CL-CMP-3986].

Under the reporting clause, the Contracting Officer or the designated office is to deliver to the Contractor, subject to military security regulations, a report in writing on or before the sixtieth day next following the end of each yearly period during which royalties have accrued [@CL-CMP-8644; @CL-CMP-3961]. Royalties that have accrued during the yearly period shall be paid to the Contractor, if appropriations are or become available, within sixty days following receipt of a voucher from the Contractor [@CL-CMP-3962], a voucher submitted in accordance with the report referred to in paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-CMP-3988]. The clause also provides that the Government shall not be obligated to pay, in respect of any such yearly period, on account of the combined royalties accruing under the contract directly and under any separate licenses granted pursuant to the License to Other Government Agencies clause (if any), an amount greater than the dollar amount stated in the clause [@CL-CMP-8645; @CL-CMP-8646]; if such combined royalties exceed the said maximum yearly obligation, each department or agency shall pay a pro rata share of the said maximum yearly obligation as determined by the proportion its accrued royalties bear to the combined total of accrued royalties [@CL-CMP-3963].

## How it works

For a license on a running royalty basis, the payment article directs insertion of the clause at 252.227-7006 in accordance with the instructions therein, and also the clauses at 252.227-7002, 252.227-7009 and 252.227-7010 [@CL-CMP-3957]. If a license under substantially the same patents and authorizing substantially the same acts has been or shall hereafter be granted within the United States on royalty terms more favorable to the licensee, the Government shall be entitled to the benefit of those terms for royalties accruing after the date they become effective [@CL-CMP-3959; @CL-CMP-3960]. Royalties accrued during the yearly period shall be paid to the Contractor, if appropriations are or become available, within sixty days following receipt of a voucher from the Contractor [@CL-CMP-3962], and if combined royalties exceed the said maximum yearly obligation, each department or agency shall pay a pro rata share [@CL-CMP-3963].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A license pays royalties yearly. Royalties accrue during a year ending on a set date. The designated office sends the contractor a written report within 60 days of that date. The contractor sends a voucher and is paid within 60 days of receiving it, if funds are available. Later, the contractor grants another license for the same patents in the United States at a lower rate, and the Government is entitled to the benefit of the lower rate for royalties accruing after the date it becomes effective.
