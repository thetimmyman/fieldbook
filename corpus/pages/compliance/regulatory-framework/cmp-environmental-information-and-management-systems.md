## Purpose

Collects three separate environmental duties from three clauses: the information a Federal facility needs for right-to-know reporting, a waste reduction program, and conformity with environmental management system controls.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5181] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.223-5, FAR 52.223-10 and FAR 52.223-19 [@CL-CMP-5181; @CL-CMP-5185; @CL-CMP-5187] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.223-5 [@CL-CMP-1105]; RFO FAR 52.223-10 and RFO FAR 52.223-19 print as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1117; @CL-CMP-1118] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5181] |
| Applies to | Contractors whose contracts carry the pollution prevention and right-to-know clause, the waste reduction clause or the environmental management systems clause [@CL-CMP-5182; @CL-CMP-5185; @CL-CMP-5187] |

Federal facilities are required to comply with the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act and the Pollution Prevention Act, and the contractor provides all information the facility needs for the listed requirements [@CL-CMP-5181; @CL-CMP-5182].

Consistent with a provision of the executive order the clause names, the contractor must establish a program to promote cost-effective waste reduction in all operations and facilities covered by the contract, and the program must comply with applicable Federal, State and local requirements [@CL-CMP-5185; @CL-CMP-5186].

The contractor's work under the contract must conform with all operational controls identified in the applicable agency or facility Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5187], and the contractor must provide monitoring and measurement information necessary for the Government to address environmental performance relative to the goals of the Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5188].

## How it works

The information the contractor provides for the facility covers the emergency planning reporting requirements and the emergency notice requirements [@CL-CMP-5182], the list of material safety data sheets and the emergency and hazardous chemical inventory forms [@CL-CMP-5183], and the toxic chemical release inventory with its reduction and recycling information [@CL-CMP-5184]. A toxic chemical is a chemical or chemical category listed in the regulation the clause cites [@CL-CMP-5288].

The program's compliance with applicable requirements must specifically include those of the section of the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act and the implementing regulations that the clause names [@CL-CMP-5186]. Waste reduction means preventing or decreasing the amount of waste being generated through waste prevention, recycling, or purchasing recycled and environmentally preferable products [@CL-CMP-5291]. Waste prevention is any change in the design, manufacturing, purchase or use of materials or products, including packaging, to reduce their amount or toxicity before they are discarded, and it also refers to the reuse of products or materials [@CL-CMP-5290]. Recycling is the series of activities, including collection, separation and processing, by which products or other materials are recovered from the solid waste stream for use as raw materials in making products other than fuel for producing heat or power by combustion [@CL-CMP-5289].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(b), Federal facilities are required to comply with the provisions of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act of 1986 and the Pollution Prevention Act of 1990 [@CL-CMP-1105]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(c)(1) and (c)(2), the contractor shall provide all information needed by the Federal facility to comply with the emergency planning reporting requirements of section 302 and the emergency notice requirements of section 304 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-1106]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(c)(3) and (c)(4), the items listed are the list of Material Safety Data Sheets required by section 311 and the emergency and hazardous chemical inventory forms of section 312 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-1107]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(c)(5), the item listed is the toxic chemical release inventory of Section 313, which includes the reduction and recycling information required by section 6607 of the Pollution Prevention Act [@CL-CMP-1108]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(a), toxic chemical means a chemical or chemical category listed in 40 CFR 372.65 [@CL-CMP-1109]. RFO FAR 52.223-10 prints as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1117], and RFO FAR 52.223-19 prints as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1118].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor runs a warehouse service at a Federal facility under a contract that carries all three clauses. It gives the facility the list of material safety data sheets, the chemical inventory forms for 12 chemicals, and its toxic chemical release figures with the reduction and recycling data the facility needs for its reports. It sets up a program that promotes cost-effective waste reduction across all operations covered by the contract, using its own target of cutting packaging waste. It follows the facility's operational controls for solvent handling and gives the facility the monitoring and measurement readings the Government needs.

## Never confuse

The contractor's work under the contract shall conform with all operational controls identified in the applicable agency or facility Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5187]. The contractor shall establish a program to promote cost-effective waste reduction in all operations and facilities covered by the contract [@CL-CMP-5185].

Federal facilities are required to comply with the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act of 1986 and the Pollution Prevention Act of 1990 [@CL-CMP-5181]. The contractor shall provide all information needed by the Federal facility to comply with the listed requirements [@CL-CMP-5182].

In the codified FAR, clause 52.223-10 contains the duty to establish a waste reduction program [@CL-CMP-5185], and clause 52.223-19 contains the duty to conform with operational controls identified in the Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5187]. RFO FAR 52.223-10 and RFO FAR 52.223-19 print as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1117; @CL-CMP-1118].

The clauses on hazardous material identification and material safety data and on notice of radioactive materials are covered in [Hazardous material identification and material safety data](cmp-hazardous-material-identification-and-material-safety-data.md) and [Notice of radioactive materials](cmp-notice-of-radioactive-materials.md).
