## Purpose

Collects three separate environmental duties from three clauses: the information a Federal facility needs for right-to-know reporting, a waste reduction program, and conformity with environmental management system controls.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5181] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.223-5, FAR 52.223-10 and FAR 52.223-19 [@CL-CMP-5181; @CL-CMP-5185; @CL-CMP-5187] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.223-5 [@CL-CMP-1105]; RFO FAR 52.223-10 and RFO FAR 52.223-19 print as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1117; @CL-CMP-1118] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5181] |
| Applies to | Contractors whose contracts carry the pollution prevention and right-to-know clause, the waste reduction clause or the environmental management systems clause [@CL-CMP-5182; @CL-CMP-5185; @CL-CMP-5187] |

Federal facilities are required to comply with the provisions of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act of 1986 and the Pollution Prevention Act of 1990 [@CL-CMP-5181], and the Contractor shall provide all information needed by the Federal facility to comply with the emergency planning reporting requirements of section 302 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, the emergency notice requirements of section 304 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-5182], the list of Material Safety Data Sheets required by section 311 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, the emergency and hazardous chemical inventory forms of section 312 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-5183], and the toxic chemical release inventory of section 313 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, which includes the reduction and recycling information required by section 6607 of the Pollution Prevention Act [@CL-CMP-5184].

Consistent with the requirements of section 207 of Executive Order 14057, the Contractor shall establish a program to promote cost-effective waste reduction in all operations and facilities covered by the contract [@CL-CMP-5185], and any such program shall comply with applicable Federal, State, and local requirements, specifically including Section 6002 of the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act and implementing regulations (40 CFR part 247) [@CL-CMP-5186].

The Contractor's work under the contract shall conform with all operational controls identified in the applicable agency or facility Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5187], and the Contractor shall provide monitoring and measurement information necessary for the Government to address environmental performance relative to the goals of the Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5188].

## How it works

The information the Contractor provides for the Federal facility covers the emergency planning reporting requirements of section 302 and the emergency notice requirements of section 304 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-5182], the list of Material Safety Data Sheets required by section 311 and the emergency and hazardous chemical inventory forms of section 312 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-5183], and the toxic chemical release inventory of section 313 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, which includes the reduction and recycling information required by section 6607 of the Pollution Prevention Act [@CL-CMP-5184]. Toxic chemical means a chemical or chemical category listed in 40 CFR 372.65 [@CL-CMP-5288].

Any waste reduction program shall comply with applicable Federal, State, and local requirements, specifically including Section 6002 of the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act and implementing regulations (40 CFR part 247) [@CL-CMP-5186]. Waste reduction means preventing or decreasing the amount of waste being generated through waste prevention, recycling, or purchasing recycled and environmentally preferable products [@CL-CMP-5291]. Waste prevention means any change in the design, manufacturing, purchase, or use of materials or products (including packaging) to reduce their amount or toxicity before they are discarded, and waste prevention also refers to the reuse of products or materials [@CL-CMP-5290]. Recycling means the series of activities, including collection, separation, and processing, by which products or other materials are recovered from the solid waste stream for use in the form of raw materials in the manufacture of products other than fuel for producing heat or power by combustion [@CL-CMP-5289].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(b), Federal facilities are required to comply with the provisions of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act of 1986 and the Pollution Prevention Act of 1990 [@CL-CMP-1105]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(c)(1) and (c)(2), the contractor shall provide all information needed by the Federal facility to comply with the emergency planning reporting requirements of section 302 and the emergency notice requirements of section 304 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-1106]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(c)(3) and (c)(4), the items listed are the list of Material Safety Data Sheets required by section 311 and the emergency and hazardous chemical inventory forms of section 312 of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act [@CL-CMP-1107]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(c)(5), the item listed is the toxic chemical release inventory of Section 313, which includes the reduction and recycling information required by section 6607 of the Pollution Prevention Act [@CL-CMP-1108]. Under RFO FAR 52.223-5(a), toxic chemical means a chemical or chemical category listed in 40 CFR 372.65 [@CL-CMP-1109]. RFO FAR 52.223-10 prints as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1117], and RFO FAR 52.223-19 prints as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1118].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor runs a warehouse service at a Federal facility under a contract that carries all three clauses. It gives the facility the list of material safety data sheets, the chemical inventory forms for 12 chemicals, and its toxic chemical release figures with the reduction and recycling data the facility needs for its reports. It sets up a program that promotes cost-effective waste reduction across all operations covered by the contract, using its own target of cutting packaging waste. It follows the facility's operational controls for solvent handling and gives the facility the monitoring and measurement readings the Government needs.

## Never confuse

The waste reduction program differs from the environmental management system duty: the Contractor's work under the contract shall conform with all operational controls identified in the applicable agency or facility Environmental Management Systems and provide monitoring and measurement information necessary for the Government to address environmental performance relative to the goals of the Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5187; @CL-CMP-5188], while consistent with the requirements of section 207 of Executive Order 14057, the Contractor shall establish a program to promote cost-effective waste reduction in all operations and facilities covered by the contract [@CL-CMP-5185]. Federal facilities are required to comply with the provisions of the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act of 1986 and the Pollution Prevention Act of 1990 [@CL-CMP-5181]. The Contractor shall provide all information needed by the Federal facility to comply with the emergency planning reporting, emergency notice, Material Safety Data Sheets, chemical inventory, and toxic chemical release inventory requirements [@CL-CMP-5182; @CL-CMP-5183; @CL-CMP-5184]. In the codified FAR, clause 52.223-10 requires the Contractor, consistent with section 207 of Executive Order 14057, to establish a program to promote cost-effective waste reduction in all operations and facilities covered by the contract [@CL-CMP-5185], and clause 52.223-19 requires the Contractor's work under the contract to conform with all operational controls identified in the applicable agency or facility Environmental Management Systems and provide monitoring and measurement information necessary for the Government to address environmental performance relative to the goals of the Environmental Management Systems [@CL-CMP-5187; @CL-CMP-5188]. RFO FAR 52.223-10 and RFO FAR 52.223-19 print as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1117; @CL-CMP-1118]. The clauses on hazardous material identification and material safety data and on notice of radioactive materials are covered in [Hazardous material identification and material safety data](cmp-hazardous-material-identification-and-material-safety-data.md) and [Notice of radioactive materials](cmp-notice-of-radioactive-materials.md).
