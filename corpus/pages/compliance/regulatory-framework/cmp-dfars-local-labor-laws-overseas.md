## Purpose

Shows what the Contractor must comply with overseas, who bears claims about local labor law compliance, and which fines, penalties and litigation costs are reimbursed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4422] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.222-7002 [@CL-CMP-4422] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | JUN 1997 [@CL-CMP-10152] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4422] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the Compliance With Local Labor Laws (Overseas) clause, used as prescribed in section 222.7201(a) [@CL-CMP-4422] |

The Contractor shall comply with all local laws, regulations, and labor union agreements governing work hours, and with labor regulations including collective bargaining agreements, workers' compensation, working conditions, fringe benefits, and labor standards or labor contract matters [@CL-CMP-4423]. The Contractor indemnifies and holds harmless the United States Government from all claims arising out of the requirements of the clause [@CL-CMP-4424].

The indemnity includes the Contractor's obligation to handle and settle, without cost to the United States Government, any claims or litigation concerning allegations that the Contractor or the United States Government, or both, have not fully complied with local labor laws or regulations relating to the performance of work required by the contract [@CL-CMP-8637; @CL-CMP-4425]. Paragraph (c) applies notwithstanding paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-4426]. Under it, the Contractor will be reimbursed for the costs of all fines, penalties, and reasonable litigation expenses incurred as a result of compliance with specific contract terms and conditions or written instructions from the Contracting officer [@CL-CMP-4427].

## How it works

The duty has two parts: local laws and union agreements on work hours, and a wider set of labor regulations [@CL-CMP-4423]. The Contractor's indemnity obligation includes handling and settling, without cost to the Government, claims or litigation concerning allegations that the Contractor or the Government, or both, have not fully complied with local labor laws or regulations relating to the performance of work required by the contract [@CL-CMP-8637; @CL-CMP-4425]. Under paragraph (c) the Contractor is reimbursed for the costs of all fines, penalties and reasonable litigation expenses incurred as a result of compliance with specific contract terms and conditions or written instructions from the Contracting officer [@CL-CMP-4427]. Paragraph (c) is stated to be consistent with paragraphs 31.205-15(a) and 31.205-47(d) of the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-CMP-4426].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An overseas contract is subject to a local rule on work hours. A union claims that the Contractor and the Government broke it. The Contractor handles and settles the claim without cost to the Government. Later a local authority fines the Contractor 5,000 for following a written instruction from the Contracting officer. Answer: the Contractor is reimbursed the 5,000 under paragraph (c). Assumed for the example: the claim concerns local labor rules on the contract work.

## Never confuse

The paragraph (c) reimbursement is not swallowed by the paragraph (b) indemnity: under paragraph (b) the Contractor indemnifies and holds harmless the United States Government from all claims arising out of the requirements of the clause [@CL-CMP-4424]; notwithstanding paragraph (b), under paragraph (c) the Contractor will be reimbursed for the costs of all fines, penalties, and reasonable litigation expenses incurred as a result of compliance with specific contract terms and conditions or written instructions from the Contracting officer [@CL-CMP-4426; @CL-CMP-4427]. For the FAR treatment of agency impartiality in labor disputes, see [Labor law definitions and labor relations policy](cmp-labor-law-definitions-and-labor-relations-policy.md). For which law and forum govern an overseas contract, see [DFARS foreign wage, whistleblower legal costs and choice of law](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-dfars-foreign-wage-whistleblower-legal-and-choice-of-law.md). For the Italian work permit, see [Permit from the Italian Inspectorate of Labor](cmp-dfars-italian-labor-permit.md), and for Spanish social security proof and insurance, see [Spanish social security laws and Spanish laws and insurance](cmp-dfars-spanish-social-security-and-insurance.md). For the duty to employ residents of a named place, see [Restrictions on employment of personnel](cmp-dfars-restrictions-on-employment-of-personnel.md). The restriction on severance payments to foreign nationals, and its waiver, are covered in [Severance payments to foreign nationals: restriction and waiver](cmp-severance-payments-to-foreign-nationals.md).
