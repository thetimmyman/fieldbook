## Purpose

Names the construction labor standards clauses and provision, says which contracts each goes in, and shows how a labor standards dispute is routed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3043] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.406-10 and FAR 22.407 [@CL-CMP-3049; @CL-CMP-3043] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3043] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts in excess of $2,000 for construction within the United States, with a further clause for cost-reimbursement construction [@CL-CMP-3043; @CL-CMP-3045; @CL-CMP-3048] |

The clauses 52.222-6 Construction Wage Rate Requirements, 52.222-7 Withholding of Funds and 52.222-8 Payrolls and Basic Records are inserted in solicitations and contracts in excess of $2,000 for construction within the United States [@CL-CMP-3043]. One listed group of items in that clause list is 52.222-9 Apprentices and Trainees, 52.222-10 Compliance with Copeland Act Requirements, 52.222-11 Subcontracts (Labor Standards) and 52.222-12 Contract Termination—Debarment [@CL-CMP-3044; @CL-CMP-3043]. Another listed group is 52.222-13 Compliance with Construction Wage Rate Requirements and Related Regulations, 52.222-14 Disputes Concerning Labor Standards and 52.222-15 Certification of Eligibility [@CL-CMP-3046; @CL-CMP-3043]. The clause at 52.222-16, Approval of Wage Rates, is inserted in solicitations and contracts in excess of $2,000 for cost-reimbursement construction to be performed within the United States, except for contracts with a State or political subdivision [@CL-CMP-3045]. The provision at 52.222-5, Construction Wage Rate Requirements, Secondary Site of the Work, is inserted in solicitations in excess of $2,000 for construction within the United States [@CL-CMP-3048].

Where the requirements of the subpart apply to construction work under the nonconstruction-contract rule, the applicable construction labor standards clauses are inserted in such solicitations and contracts and the items of construction work to which the clauses apply are identified [@CL-CMP-3124].

When requesting the contractor to take corrective action in labor violation cases, the contracting officer shall inform the contractor of listed matters [@CL-CMP-3123]. One of them is that disputes concerning the labor standards requirements of the contract are handled under the clause at 52.222-14 and not under the clause at 52.233-1 [@CL-CMP-3049; @CL-CMP-3123].

## How it works

Clause insertion turns on contract type and value. A solicitation or contract for construction within the United States above the stated amount carries the listed clauses [@CL-CMP-3043; @CL-CMP-3044; @CL-CMP-3046]. Cost-reimbursement construction adds the approval clause, with a carve-out for contracts with a State or political subdivision [@CL-CMP-3045]. Solicitations in excess of the stated amount for construction within the United States carry the secondary site provision [@CL-CMP-3048]. When corrective action is requested in a labor violation case, the contractor is told that a labor standards dispute is routed through the labor standards disputes clause, not the general disputes clause [@CL-CMP-3049; @CL-CMP-3123]. Which construction work in a mixed contract is covered is on [Construction wage rate requirements: definitions and which contracts are covered](cmp-construction-wage-definitions-and-applicability.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price building contract to be performed in the United States is worth 3,000,000. It is above the stated amount, so the ten listed clauses go in [@CL-CMP-3043; @CL-CMP-3044; @CL-CMP-3046], and the solicitation carries the secondary site provision [@CL-CMP-3048]. The approval of wage rates clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts for cost-reimbursement construction, and this contract is not cost-reimbursement [@CL-CMP-3045]. A later disagreement over overtime pay is a dispute concerning the labor standards requirements of the contract, handled under the clause at 52.222-14 and not under the clause at 52.233-1 [@CL-CMP-3049]. A cost-reimbursement construction contract with a State government above the same amount takes the ten clauses [@CL-CMP-3043; @CL-CMP-3044; @CL-CMP-3046], but the approval clause is not inserted in contracts with a State or political subdivision [@CL-CMP-3045].

## Never confuse

Disputes concerning the labor standards requirements of the contract are handled under the clause at 52.222-14 and not under the clause at 52.233-1, Disputes [@CL-CMP-3049]. The approval of wage rates clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts in excess of $2,000 for cost-reimbursement construction [@CL-CMP-3045], and the clauses at 52.222-6, 52.222-7 and 52.222-8 in solicitations and contracts in excess of $2,000 for construction within the United States [@CL-CMP-3043]. The rules for withholding and suspension are on [Construction wage rate requirements: withholding from and suspension of contract payments](cmp-construction-wage-withholding-and-suspension.md). The payroll and investigation steps that follow from the payroll clause are on [Construction payrolls and statements of compliance, and labor standards investigations](cmp-construction-payrolls-statements-and-investigations.md).

The clauses on labor standards disputes, certification of eligibility and approval of wage rates are covered in [Labor standards disputes, certification of eligibility and approval of wage rates](cmp-labor-standards-disputes-and-eligibility.md), and the clauses on subcontracts, termination for debarment and incorporated rulings are covered in [Labor standards subcontracts, termination for debarment and incorporated rulings](cmp-labor-standards-subcontracts-and-debarment.md).
