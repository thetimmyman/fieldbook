## Purpose

Shows the license the Government holds when the contractor keeps title, the cases where the contractor must assign title, the march-in acknowledgment, the reports the contractor owes, the subcontract notices and the withholding of payment.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3935] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7038 [@CL-CMP-3935] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3935] |
| Applies to | Subject inventions in which the Contractor retains ownership [@CL-CMP-3935] |

If the Contractor retains ownership of any subject invention, the Government shall have a nonexclusive, nontransferable, irrevocable, paid-up license to practice, or have practiced for or on behalf of the United States, the subject invention throughout the world [@CL-CMP-3935]. The Contractor shall assign title to the agency, upon written request, in the listed cases, one of which is where the Contractor elects not to retain title [@CL-CMP-3936]. Another listed case is where the Contractor fails to disclose or elect within the times specified and the agency requests title within 60 days after learning of the failure [@CL-CMP-3937]. With respect to any subject invention in which the Contractor has retained ownership, the agency has the right to require licensing pursuant to sections 203 and 210(c) of title 35 of the United States Code, 37 CFR 401.6, and any supplemental regulations of the agency in effect on the date of contract award [@CL-CMP-3938].

The Contractor shall furnish the Contracting Officer interim reports every 12 months (or any longer period the Contracting Officer specifies) from the date of the contract, listing subject inventions during that period and stating that all subject inventions have been disclosed or that there are no subject inventions [@CL-CMP-3939; @CL-CMP-8647]. Among the items the Contractor is to furnish to the Contracting Officer is a final report, within 3 months after completion of the contracted work, listing all subject inventions or stating that there were none, and listing all subcontracts at any tier containing a patent rights clause or stating that there were none [@CL-CMP-3984]. The Contractor shall, upon request, submit periodic reports no more frequently than annually on the utilization of a subject invention or on efforts to obtain utilization that are being made by the Contractor or its licensees or assignees [@CL-CMP-3940; @CL-CMP-7386]. The Contractor shall also promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing on the award of any subcontract at any tier containing a patent rights clause, identifying among other listed items the subcontractor, the applicable patent rights clause and the work to be performed [@CL-CMP-3941].

Under the paragraph on withholding of payment, which does not apply to subcontracts, any time before final payment under the contract the Contracting Officer may, in the Government's interest, withhold payment until a reserve not exceeding $50,000 or 5 percent of the amount of the contract, whichever is less, is set aside if, in the Contracting Officer's opinion, the Contractor fails to meet any of the requirements the paragraph then lists [@CL-CMP-8648; @CL-CMP-3985].

## How it works

If the Contractor retains ownership of any subject invention, the Government shall have the paid-up license [@CL-CMP-3935]. The Contractor shall assign title to the agency, upon written request, in the listed cases [@CL-CMP-3936; @CL-CMP-3937]. With respect to any subject invention in which the Contractor has retained ownership, the agency has the right to require licensing [@CL-CMP-3938]. The Contractor furnishes the Contracting Officer interim reports every 12 months, or any longer period the Contracting Officer specifies, from the date of the contract [@CL-CMP-3939; @CL-CMP-8647], and promptly notifies the Contracting Officer in writing on the award of any subcontract at any tier containing a patent rights clause [@CL-CMP-3941]. Any time before final payment, the Contracting Officer may withhold payment until a reserve is set aside if the Contractor fails to meet any of the listed requirements [@CL-CMP-8648; @CL-CMP-3985].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor keeps title to a new sensor design. The Government holds a paid-up license to practice it, or have it practiced for the Government, anywhere in the world. Every 12 months the contractor sends the Contracting Officer a list of inventions made in the period. It awards a subcontract containing a patent rights clause and tells the Contracting Officer in writing, naming the subcontractor and the work.
