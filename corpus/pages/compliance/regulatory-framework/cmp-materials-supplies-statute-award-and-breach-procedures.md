## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer must furnish the contractor when a covered contract is awarded, and what the contracting officer must do when a stipulation required under the statute is violated.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8012] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.608 and FAR 22.610 [@CL-CMP-8012; @CL-CMP-7709] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8012] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to the statute [@CL-CMP-8012]; the section does not define them, and 22.602 separately requires such contracts for supplies, in any amount exceeding $20,000 and subject to the exemptions at 22.604, to carry the statute's stipulations [@CL-CMP-8022; @CL-CMP-8024; @CL-CMP-8023] |

Paragraph (a), award. When a contract subject to the statute is awarded, the contracting officer, in accordance with regulations or instructions issued by the Secretary of Labor and individual agency procedures, must furnish to the contractor Department of Labor publication WH-1313, Notice to Employees Working on Government Contracts [@CL-CMP-8012].

Paragraph (b), breach of stipulation. In the event of a violation of a stipulation required under the statute, the contracting officer must, in accordance with agency procedures, notify the appropriate regional office of the Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, and furnish any information available [@CL-CMP-8013]. The paragraph refers to 29 CFR part 1, Appendix B [@CL-CMP-8013].

Separately, 22.602 states that, except for the exemptions at 22.604, all contracts subject to the statute and entered into by any executive department, independent establishment, or other agency or instrumentality of the United States, among the contracting entities the section names [@CL-CMP-8022], for the manufacture or furnishing of materials, supplies, articles and equipment, called supplies in the subpart [@CL-CMP-8024], in any amount exceeding $20,000, shall include or incorporate by reference the stipulations required by the statute on such matters as minimum wages, maximum hours, child labor, convict labor, and safe and sanitary working conditions [@CL-CMP-8023].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.222-20, Contracts for Materials, Supplies, Articles, and Equipment, in solicitations and contracts covered by the statute (see 22.603, 22.604, and 22.605) [@CL-CMP-7709].

## How it works

Who acts. Both duties fall on the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-8012; @CL-CMP-8013]. The contractor receives the publication. The regional office receives the notice.

Events. Paragraph (a) is triggered by award of a contract subject to the statute [@CL-CMP-8012]. Paragraph (b) is triggered by a violation of a stipulation required under the statute [@CL-CMP-8013].

Who decides. Regulations or instructions issued by the Secretary of Labor and individual agency procedures govern how the publication is furnished [@CL-CMP-8012]. Agency procedures govern the notice [@CL-CMP-8013]. The section does not say who determines that a violation occurred.

Threshold and exception. The $20,000 threshold and the exemption carve-out sit in the stipulation requirement that 22.602 states, which applies to amounts exceeding $20,000 and excepts the exemptions at 22.604 [@CL-CMP-8022; @CL-CMP-8023]. This section itself states no threshold. The rules on applicability and the exemptions are on the linked pages.

Deadline. The section sets none for either step. Award is the event for paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-8012].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract for the furnishing of supplies for a made-up $50,000 is awarded by a Federal executive department. Assume the contract is subject to the statute and is not exempt under 22.604. The amount is above $20,000, so the contract shall include or incorporate by reference the stipulations required by the statute [@CL-CMP-8022; @CL-CMP-8024; @CL-CMP-8023]. On award the contracting officer furnishes the contractor publication WH-1313, following the regulations or instructions issued by the Secretary of Labor and the agency's procedures [@CL-CMP-8012]. Later a stipulation required under the statute is violated. The contracting officer notifies the appropriate regional office of the Wage and Hour Division, following agency procedures, and furnishes any information available [@CL-CMP-8013].

## Never confuse

Never confuse the publication furnished at award with the notice of a violation. The first goes to the contractor, and the second goes to the appropriate regional office of the Wage and Hour Division [@CL-CMP-8012; @CL-CMP-8013].

The $20,000 figure is not a limit in this section, because it comes from the stipulation requirement in 22.602 [@CL-CMP-8023].
