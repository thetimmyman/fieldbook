## Purpose

Shows the license the Government takes in existing works, and what a contractor must declare, revise and risk having withheld for technical data on a major system.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3722] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.227-18 and FAR 52.227-21 [@CL-CMP-3722; @CL-CMP-3723] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.227-21 [@CL-CMP-1175]; RFO FAR 52.227-18 prints as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1184] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3722] |
| Applies to | Technical data that relate to a major system and are delivered or required to be delivered under the contract, for the declaration clause [@CL-CMP-3723] |

Except as otherwise provided in the contract, the Contractor grants the Government and others acting on its behalf a paid-up nonexclusive, irrevocable, worldwide license to reproduce, prepare derivative works, and perform and display publicly, by or on behalf of the Government [@CL-CMP-3722]. The license covers all the material or subject matter called for under the contract, or for which the clause is specifically made applicable [@CL-CMP-3751]. This card does not set out the indemnity paragraph of the existing works clause.

The Contractor shall provide, in accordance with title 41 of the United States Code, section 2302(e)(7), the declaration with respect to all [@CL-CMP-7767] technical data that relate to a major system and are delivered or required to be delivered under the contract, or are delivered within 3 years after acceptance of all items other than technical data, unless the contract sets a different period [@CL-CMP-3723]. The declaration states that, to the best of the Contractor's knowledge and belief, the technical data delivered are complete, accurate, and comply with the contract requirements concerning such technical data [@CL-CMP-3724]. The Government may, at any time during the period covered by the clause, direct correction of deficiencies not in compliance with contract requirements, and the corrections shall be made at the Contractor's expense [@CL-CMP-3725]. Unauthorized markings on data shall not be considered a deficiency for the purpose of the clause, but will be treated in accordance with paragraph (e) of the [Rights in Data—General clause](cmp-rights-in-data-general-copyright-withholding-and-markings.md) included in the contract [@CL-CMP-3749].

The Contractor also shall, at the request of the Contracting Officer, revise technical data subject to the clause [@CL-CMP-7768] to reflect engineering design changes made during contract performance that affect the form, fit, and function of any item other than technical data delivered under the contract [@CL-CMP-3726]. The Contractor may submit a request for an equitable adjustment to the terms and conditions of the contract for any revisions to technical data made pursuant to the revision paragraph [@CL-CMP-3750]. At any time before final payment, the Contracting Officer may withhold payment as a reserve up to an amount not exceeding $100,000 or 5 percent of the amount of the contract, whichever is less, if the Contractor fails to take any of the listed actions [@CL-CMP-3727]. The failures are alternatives: the reserve may be withheld if the Contractor fails to make timely delivery of the technical data, or to provide the declaration required by the declaration paragraph, or to make the corrections required by the correction paragraph, or to make revisions requested under the revision paragraph [@CL-CMP-3727; @CL-CMP-3748]. The Contracting Officer may withhold the reserve until the Contractor has complied with the direction or requests of the Contracting Officer, or until a determination that the deficiencies relating to delivered data arose out of causes beyond the control of the Contractor and without the fault or negligence of the Contractor [@CL-CMP-3727; @CL-CMP-3728].

## How it works

The declaration clause works in three steps: the contractor declares the technical data complete, accurate and compliant when it delivers [@CL-CMP-3724], the Government may direct correction of deficiencies at the contractor's expense [@CL-CMP-3725], and the contractor revises the data when the Contracting Officer asks, after design changes that affect form, fit, and function [@CL-CMP-3726]. The withholding is a reserve that the Contracting Officer may hold back, capped at the lesser of two amounts, and the Contracting Officer may hold it until the contractor has complied with the direction or requests of the Contracting Officer, or until a determination that the deficiencies arose out of causes beyond the contractor's control and without its fault or negligence [@CL-CMP-3727; @CL-CMP-3728].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.227-21 keeps the declaration, which the contractor provides in accordance with section 2302(e)(7) of title 41 of the United States Code, for technical data that relate to a major system and are delivered or required to be delivered, or delivered within 3 years after acceptance of all items other than technical data unless a different period is set [@CL-CMP-1196; @CL-CMP-1175; @CL-CMP-3723]. It keeps the declaration that, to the best of the contractor's knowledge and belief, the technical data are complete, accurate, and comply with the contract requirements concerning such technical data [@CL-CMP-1176; @CL-CMP-3724]. It keeps the Government's right to direct correction of deficiencies at the contractor's expense [@CL-CMP-1177; @CL-CMP-3725], and the rule that unauthorized markings are not a deficiency but are treated under paragraph (e) of the Rights in Data-General clause [@CL-CMP-1178; @CL-CMP-3749]. It keeps the revision at the request of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-1179; @CL-CMP-3726] and the request for an equitable adjustment [@CL-CMP-1180; @CL-CMP-3750]. It keeps the reserve of up to $100,000 or 5 percent of the amount of the contract, whichever is less, before final payment, for the four listed failures [@CL-CMP-1181; @CL-CMP-1182; @CL-CMP-3727; @CL-CMP-3748], and the hold until the contractor complies or the Contracting Officer determines the cause was beyond the contractor's control [@CL-CMP-1183; @CL-CMP-3728]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.227-18 as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1184].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract for a major system has a price of 1,000,000. The contractor fails to deliver the declaration with a batch of technical data. The reserve cannot exceed the lesser of 100,000 and 5 percent of 1,000,000, which is 50,000, so it is capped at 50,000. The Contracting Officer may hold the 50,000 until the contractor complies.

## Never confuse

The withholding is a reserve of up to $100,000 or 5 percent of the amount of the contract, whichever is less [@CL-CMP-3727]; for payments on deliveries, see [Making payments: due dates, partial payments and early payment](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-making-payments.md). The existing works license also differs from [unlimited rights](cmp-rights-in-data-general-definitions-and-allocation.md): its wording covers reproducing, preparing derivative works, and performing and displaying publicly, and does not list distributing copies to the public [@CL-CMP-3722; @CL-CMP-3679].

In the codified text, FAR 52.227-18 carries the existing works license [@CL-CMP-3722], but the RFO text prints that number as [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-1184].
