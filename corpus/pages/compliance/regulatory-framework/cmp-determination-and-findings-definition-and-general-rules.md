## Purpose

Sets out what a determination and findings is and the general rules for an individual one, so a contract file can be tested against the approval it relies on.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8337] |
| Codified citation | FAR 1.701; FAR 1.702 [@CL-CMP-8337; @CL-CMP-8340] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8337] |
| Applies to | Certain contract actions for which a statute or regulation requires a written approval as a prerequisite [@CL-CMP-8337] |

A determination and findings is a special form of written approval by an authorized official that a statute or regulation requires as a prerequisite to taking certain contract actions [@CL-CMP-8337]. The determination is a conclusion or decision supported by the findings [@CL-CMP-8339]. The findings are statements of fact or rationale essential to support the determination, and they must cover each requirement of the statute or regulation [@CL-CMP-8338].

## How it works

A determination and findings shall ordinarily be for an individual contract action [@CL-CMP-8340]. Unless otherwise prohibited, class documents may be executed for classes of contract action; the class rules are on [Class determinations and findings: authority and limits](cmp-class-determinations-and-findings.md) [@CL-CMP-8341]. The approval granted is restricted to the proposed contract action or actions reasonably described in the document [@CL-CMP-8342]. A document may provide for a reasonable degree of flexibility, and in its application reasonable variations in estimated quantities or prices are permitted, unless the document specifies otherwise [@CL-CMP-8671; @CL-CMP-8343].

When an option is anticipated, the document shall state the approximate quantity to be awarded initially and the extent of the increase the option permits [@CL-CMP-8344]. The document is a written approval given by an authorized official [@CL-CMP-8337]; the content and signature rules are on [Determinations and findings: content and signature](../../operating-model/decision-rights/opm-determinations-and-findings.md), and expiry and supersession are on [Supersession, modification and expiration of determinations and findings](cmp-df-supersession-and-expiration.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An office plans to buy 12 vehicles and expects to add 4 more under an option. Because an option is anticipated, the document states the approximate quantity of 12 to be awarded initially and the increase of 4 that the option permits [@CL-CMP-8344]. The approval covers only the purchase the document reasonably describes, so a later purchase of spare parts that the document does not describe is outside it [@CL-CMP-8342]. Whether a small change in the estimated quantity is a reasonable variation depends on the document, because it controls if it specifies otherwise [@CL-CMP-8343].

## Never confuse

The determination is not the findings: the determination is a conclusion or decision supported by the findings [@CL-CMP-8339], and the findings are statements of fact or rationale essential to support the determination [@CL-CMP-8338].
