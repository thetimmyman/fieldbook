## Purpose

Defines the terms the uniform contract line item numbering subpart uses, and states when the clause for items not separately priced and the cost voucher billing clause are prescribed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |

| Codified citation | DFARS 204.7101 [@CL-CMP-7927; @CL-CMP-7928; @CL-CMP-7929; @CL-CMP-7930; @CL-CMP-7931; @CL-CMP-7932; @CL-CMP-7933]; DFARS 204.7109 [@CL-CMP-7934; @CL-CMP-7935] |

| Applies to | Contract line item schedules, attachments and exhibits [@CL-CMP-7927; @CL-CMP-7928; @CL-CMP-7930]; solicitations and contracts for items not separately priced [@CL-CMP-7934]; solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials or labor-hour contract is contemplated [@CL-CMP-7935] |

An accounting classification reference number is any combination of a two position alpha/numeric code used as a method of relating the accounting classification citation to detailed line item information contained in the schedule [@CL-CMP-7927]. An attachment is any documentation, appended to a contract or incorporated by reference, which does not establish a requirement for deliverables [@CL-CMP-7928]. An exhibit is a document, referred to in a contract, which is attached and establishes requirements for deliverables, and the term shall not be used to refer to any other kind of attachment to a contract [@CL-CMP-7930]. The DD Form 1423, Contract Data Requirements List, is always an exhibit, rather than an attachment [@CL-CMP-7931].

As used in the subpart, a definitized item is an item for which a firm price has been established in the basic contract or by modification [@CL-CMP-7929], and an undefinitized item is an item for which a price has not been established in the basic contract or by modification [@CL-CMP-7933]. A nonseverable deliverable is a deliverable item that is a single end product or undertaking, entire in nature, that cannot feasibly be subdivided into discrete elements or phases without losing its identity [@CL-CMP-7932].

The clause at 252.204-7002, Payment for Contract Line or Subline Items Not Separately Priced, is to be used in solicitations and contracts when the price for items not separately priced is included in the price of another contract line or subline item [@CL-CMP-7934]. The clause at 252.204-7006, Billing Instructions - Cost Vouchers, is to be used in solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement contract, a time-and-materials contract or a labor-hour contract is contemplated [@CL-CMP-7935].

## How it works

An attachment is documentation, appended to a contract or incorporated by reference, which does not establish a requirement for deliverables [@CL-CMP-7928]. An exhibit is a document, referred to in a contract, which is attached and establishes requirements for deliverables, and the term shall not be used to refer to any other kind of attachment [@CL-CMP-7930]. A definitized item is an item for which a firm price has been established in the basic contract or by modification, and an undefinitized item is an item for which a price has not been established in the basic contract or by modification [@CL-CMP-7929; @CL-CMP-7933].

The clause at 252.204-7002 is to be used in solicitations and contracts when the price for items not separately priced is included in the price of another contract line or subline item [@CL-CMP-7934]. The clause at 252.204-7006 is to be used in solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement contract, a time-and-materials contract, or a labor-hour contract is contemplated [@CL-CMP-7935]. The clause text itself, including what the contractor must do, is covered in [the line item payment card](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-dfars-nsp-line-item-payment.md) and [the cost voucher billing card](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-dfars-billing-instructions-cost-vouchers.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A schedule line covers a deliverable with a firm price set in the basic contract, so it is a definitized item [@CL-CMP-7929]. A second line has no price set in the basic contract or by modification, so it is an undefinitized item [@CL-CMP-7933]. Drawings appended to the contract do not establish a requirement for deliverables, so they are an attachment, and calling them an exhibit would misuse the term [@CL-CMP-7928; @CL-CMP-7930]. A data requirements list on DD Form 1423 is attached to the contract, and it is an exhibit [@CL-CMP-7931]. The solicitation prices the training line inside the price of the hardware line, so the clause for items not separately priced is used [@CL-CMP-7934]. The solicitation contemplates a labor-hour contract, so the cost voucher billing clause is used [@CL-CMP-7935].

## Never confuse

The DD Form 1423, Contract Data Requirements List, is always an exhibit rather than an attachment [@CL-CMP-7931].

## Company calibration

Where the executed contracts and modifications sit, and who confirms how each line item, attachment and exhibit is classified and which of the two clauses each solicitation and contract carries. [@Q-004]
