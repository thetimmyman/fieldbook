## Purpose

Shows how long SBIR data is protected, how it is marked, and what the Government may do with it during and after the protection period.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Codified citation | FAR 52.227-20 [@CL-CMP-3716; @CL-CMP-3717] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.227-20 [@CL-CMP-2293; @CL-CMP-2294; @CL-CMP-2295; @CL-CMP-2296; @CL-CMP-2297; @CL-CMP-2298; @CL-CMP-2299] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3716] |

One element of the SBIR data definition is that the data were first produced by a Contractor that is a small business concern in performance of a small business innovation research contract issued under the authority of section 638 of title 15 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-3716]. The definition continues that the data are not generally known and, without obligation as to confidentiality, have not been made available to others by the Contractor or are not already available to the Government [@CL-CMP-3747]. The clause used here is titled for the SBIR program, and its text does not establish how the STTR program is covered.

The SBIR Rights Notice sets a protection period of 4 years after acceptance of all items to be delivered under the contract, unless extended in accordance with FAR 27.409(h), during which the Government will use the data for Government purposes only [@CL-CMP-3717]. During the same period the data shall not be disclosed outside the Government, including for procurement purposes, without the Contractor's permission, except that, subject to those prohibitions, the data may be disclosed for use by support Contractors [@CL-CMP-3718]. After the protection period, the Government has a paid-up license to use, and to authorize others to use on its behalf, the data for Government purposes, but is relieved of all disclosure prohibitions and assumes no liability for unauthorized use of the data by third parties [@CL-CMP-3719].

## How it works

Protection depends on marking. The Contractor is authorized to affix the SBIR Rights Notice to SBIR data delivered under the contract, and the Government will treat the data in accordance with the notice, subject to the provisions of paragraphs (e) and (f) of the clause [@CL-CMP-3720]. Data delivered without any notice authorized by the SBIR rights paragraph shall be deemed furnished with unlimited rights, and the Government assumes no liability for its disclosure, use, or reproduction [@CL-CMP-3721]. The general clause has a similar rule for data delivered without restrictive markings [@CL-CMP-3695]; see [Rights in data, general: copyright, withholding and markings](cmp-rights-in-data-general-copyright-withholding-and-markings.md). The clause text used here does not set out how an extension of the protection period is obtained, so this card does not either.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO paragraph (a) keeps the element of the SBIR data definition that the data were first produced by a Contractor that is a small business concern in performance of a small business innovation research contract issued under the authority of section 638 of title 15 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-2293; @CL-CMP-3716], and the continuation that the data are not generally known and, without obligation as to confidentiality, have not been made available to others by the Contractor or are not already available to the Government [@CL-CMP-2294; @CL-CMP-3747]. RFO paragraph (d)(1) keeps the SBIR Rights Notice protection period of 4 years after acceptance of all items to be delivered under the contract, unless extended in accordance with FAR 27.409(h), during which the Government will use the data for Government purposes only [@CL-CMP-2295; @CL-CMP-3717]. It keeps the rule that, during that period, the data shall not be disclosed outside the Government, including for procurement purposes, without the Contractor's permission, except that, subject to those prohibitions, the data may be disclosed for use by support Contractors [@CL-CMP-2296; @CL-CMP-3718]. It keeps the rule that, after the protection period, the Government has a paid-up license to use, and to authorize others to use on its behalf, the data for Government purposes, but is relieved of all disclosure prohibitions and assumes no liability for unauthorized use of the data by third parties [@CL-CMP-2297; @CL-CMP-3719]. It keeps the Contractor's authority to affix the SBIR Rights Notice to SBIR data delivered under the contract, with the Government treating the data in accordance with the notice, subject to the provisions of paragraphs (e) and (f) of the clause [@CL-CMP-2298; @CL-CMP-3720]. RFO paragraph (e)(1) keeps the rule that data delivered to the Government without any notice authorized by paragraph (d) shall be deemed to have been furnished with unlimited rights and that the Government assumes no liability for the disclosure, use, or reproduction of such data [@CL-CMP-2299; @CL-CMP-3721]. RFO FAR 52.227-14(f)(1) keeps the similar rule for data delivered without any restrictive markings under the general clause [@CL-CMP-2187; @CL-CMP-3695].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A small business delivers SBIR data and affixes the SBIR Rights Notice. The Government accepts all items on a given day. For the protection period that follows, the Government uses the data for Government purposes only and does not disclose it outside the Government without the contractor's permission, except for use by support contractors. When the period ends, the Government may use the data and authorize others to use it for Government purposes, and the disclosure limits no longer apply.

## Never confuse

The protection period is a limit on disclosure, and it does not make the data the contractor's alone: the Government may use the data for Government purposes during the period [@CL-CMP-3717]. Data rights are also a different question from whether a concern is small enough to take SBIR awards; for that, see [SBIR and STTR size and eligibility standards](../../market/taxonomy/mkt-sbir-sttr-size-and-eligibility-standards.md).
