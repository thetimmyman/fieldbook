## Purpose

Shows what counts as an accounting system, what makes one acceptable, and when the DoD tells its users to put the clause that tests it in a solicitation or contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7853] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 242.7501 [@CL-CMP-7853]; DFARS 242.7503 [@CL-CMP-7857] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7853] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when contemplating a cost-reimbursement, incentive type, time-and-materials or labor-hour contract, or a contract with progress payments based on costs incurred or on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CMP-7857; @CL-CMP-7858] |

The definitions apply as the terms are used in the subpart that contains the section [@CL-CMP-8071]. An accounting system is the contractor's system or systems for accounting methods, procedures and controls established to gather, record, classify, analyze, summarize, interpret and present accurate and timely financial data [@CL-CMP-7855]. That data is for reporting in compliance with applicable laws, regulations and management decisions [@CL-CMP-8072]. The system may include subsystems for specific areas such as indirect and other direct costs, compensation, billing, labor and general information technology [@CL-CMP-7856].

An acceptable accounting system is one that complies with the system criteria in paragraph (c) of the clause at 252.242-7006, Accounting System Administration [@CL-CMP-7853]. The definition is aimed at reasonable assurance that applicable laws and regulations are complied with, that the accounting system and cost data are reliable, that the risk of misallocations and mischarges is minimized, and that contract allocations and charges are consistent with billing procedures [@CL-CMP-7854]. The section does not say who decides whether a system complies.

The clause at 252.242-7006 is to be used in solicitations and contracts when contemplating a cost-reimbursement, incentive type, time-and-materials or labor-hour contract [@CL-CMP-7857]. It is to be used as an alternative when contemplating a contract with progress payments made on the basis of costs incurred by the contractor or on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CMP-7858]. Either route is enough; the section sets no dollar threshold and no exclusion for any kind of awardee [@CL-CMP-7857; @CL-CMP-7858].

## How it works

The definition reaches the methods, procedures and controls that produce financial data, and it expressly allows subsystems for specific areas such as indirect and other direct costs and billing [@CL-CMP-7855; @CL-CMP-7856]. Acceptance turns on the system criteria in the clause at 252.242-7006 [@CL-CMP-7853]. The prescription follows the kind of contract being contemplated. A cost-reimbursement, incentive type, time-and-materials or labor-hour contract brings the clause in [@CL-CMP-7857]. A contract with progress payments based on costs incurred, or on a percentage or stage of completion, also brings it in [@CL-CMP-7858].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor bills labor through a labor subsystem and allocates indirect costs through a separate subsystem. Both are part of its accounting system, because subsystems for indirect costs and labor are inside the definition [@CL-CMP-7855; @CL-CMP-7856].

A time-and-materials contract is contemplated, so the clause at 252.242-7006 is to be used in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7857]. A fixed-price contract with progress payments paid on a percentage of completion is contemplated for a different requirement, and the clause is to be used there as well [@CL-CMP-7858]. A firm-fixed-price contract without progress payments is contemplated for a third requirement, and it matches neither listed route, so this section does not call for the clause [@CL-CMP-7857; @CL-CMP-7858].

## Never confuse

Acceptance of an accounting system is measured against the system criteria in the clause at 252.242-7006, not against the prescription that decides when the clause is used [@CL-CMP-7853; @CL-CMP-7857]. The definition covers subsystems for areas such as indirect costs, compensation, billing and labor, while the prescription turns on the contract type or the progress payment basis [@CL-CMP-7856; @CL-CMP-7857; @CL-CMP-7858]. The prescription for the material management and accounting system clause is narrower. It names cost-reimbursement contracts and fixed-price contracts with progress payments based on costs incurred as work progresses, and does not name those based on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CMP-7852; @CL-CMP-7858]. Its use is prescribed in all solicitations and contracts that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, are not for the acquisition of commercial products or commercial services, and both are not awarded to small businesses, educational institutions or nonprofit organizations and are either cost-reimbursement contracts or fixed-price contracts with progress payments based on costs incurred as work progresses; all of these conditions apply together [@CL-CMP-7850; @CL-CMP-7851; @CL-CMP-7852]. For the administration of the system, see [DoD accounting system administration](../business-systems/cmp-dod-accounting-system-administration.md); for the criteria, see [Accounting system criteria](../business-systems/cmp-accounting-system-criteria.md).
