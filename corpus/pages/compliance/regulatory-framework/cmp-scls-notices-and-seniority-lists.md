## Purpose

Explains the advance notice owed to an incumbent contractor and its bargaining agent, what the contracting officer tells the contractor after award, the publication given for posting to service employees, and the seniority list that follows the work to a successor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-6756] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.1010, 22.1018 and 22.1020 [@CL-CMP-6756; @CL-CMP-6760; @CL-CMP-6763] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-6756] |
| Applies to | Service contracts with represented employees, award notices to service contractors, and contracts performed at a Federal facility with a succeeding contractor [@CL-CMP-6756; @CL-CMP-6760; @CL-CMP-6763] |

The contracting officer should determine whether the service employees of the incumbent prime contractor and its subcontractors who perform on the current contract are represented by a collective bargaining agent [@CL-CMP-6917]. If there is a collective bargaining agent, the contracting officer must give both the incumbent contractor and the agent written notification of the matters listed in the paragraph [@CL-CMP-6756]. The notification covers whichever of three matters applies, as the case may be. One is the forthcoming successor contract and the applicable acquisition dates, which are issuance of solicitation, opening of bids, commencement of negotiations, award of contract, or start of performance [@CL-CMP-6757]. Another is the forthcoming contract modification and the applicable acquisition dates, which are exercise of option, extension of contract, change in scope, or start of performance [@CL-CMP-6758]. The third is the forthcoming multiple year contract anniversary date, which is the annual anniversary date or biennial date [@CL-CMP-6918].

The written notification must be given at least 30 days in advance of the earliest applicable acquisition date or the applicable annual or biennial anniversary date in order for the time-of-receipt limitations in FAR 22.1012-2(a) and (b) to apply [@CL-CMP-6759]. The contracting officer must retain a copy of the notification in the contract file [@CL-CMP-6919]. Those limitations are covered on [SCLS collective bargaining and successor wages](../regulatory-framework/cmp-scls-collective-bargaining-successor-wages.md).

## How it works

The contracting officer must take the listed steps to ensure that service employees are notified of minimum wages and fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-6920]. As soon as possible after contract award, the contracting officer must inform the contractor of the labor standards requirements of the contract relating to the Service Contract Labor Standards statute and of the contractor's responsibilities under them, unless it is clear that the contractor is fully informed [@CL-CMP-6760]. At the time of award, the contracting officer must furnish the contractor Department of Labor Publication WH-1313, Notice to Employees Working on Government Contracts, for posting at a prominent and accessible place at the worksite before contract performance begins [@CL-CMP-6761]. The contracting officer must attach any applicable wage determination to the publication [@CL-CMP-6762]. The publication advises employees of the compensation required to be paid or furnished and satisfies the notice requirements in paragraph (g) of the clause at FAR 52.222-41 [@CL-CMP-6921].

If a contract is performed at a Federal facility where employees may be hired or retained by a succeeding contractor, the incumbent prime contractor must furnish a certified list of all service employees on the contractor's or subcontractor's payroll during the last month of the contract [@CL-CMP-6763]. The list must include anniversary dates of employment and must reach the contracting officer no later than 10 days before contract completion [@CL-CMP-6922]. At the commencement of the succeeding contract, the contracting officer must provide a copy of the list to the successor contractor [@CL-CMP-6764], for determining employee eligibility for vacation or other fringe benefits based upon length of service, including service with predecessor contractors if such benefit is required by an applicable wage determination [@CL-CMP-6923].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cleaning contract is performed at a Federal facility, and the incumbent's employees are represented by a bargaining agent. The contracting officer determines this [@CL-CMP-6917]. The contracting officer sends written notice of the forthcoming successor contract and its acquisition dates to the incumbent and to the agent 40 days before the solicitation issues, so the notice is at least 30 days ahead of the earliest acquisition date [@CL-CMP-6756; @CL-CMP-6757; @CL-CMP-6759]. A copy goes in the contract file [@CL-CMP-6919].

At award the contractor receives Publication WH-1313 with the wage determination attached [@CL-CMP-6761; @CL-CMP-6762], and posts it at the loading dock before performance begins [@CL-CMP-6761]. The incumbent sends the certified list with anniversary dates 12 days before the old contract ends, which is in time [@CL-CMP-6763; @CL-CMP-6922]. At the start of the new contract the contracting officer gives a copy to the successor, who uses it to set vacation eligibility [@CL-CMP-6764; @CL-CMP-6923].

## Never confuse

The notice to the bargaining agent is not the notice to employees. The first is a written notification from the contracting officer to the incumbent and the agent, and its timing decides whether the time-of-receipt limits apply [@CL-CMP-6756; @CL-CMP-6759]. The second is Publication WH-1313, furnished to the contractor at award for posting at the worksite [@CL-CMP-6761]. The seniority list is a third item: the incumbent furnishes it to the contracting officer, who passes it to the successor [@CL-CMP-6763; @CL-CMP-6764].

## Company calibration

Confirm who identifies an incumbent bargaining agent, who sends the advance notice and keeps the file copy, and who posts the publication at each worksite. [@Q-003]
