## Purpose

Explains when a contract that carries the approval clause becomes binding, what the voluntary preaward survey invites offerors to do, and which definition governs a word or term used in a provision or clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5971] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.103 [@CL-CMP-9751]; FAR 52.204-1; FAR 52.201-1; FAR 52.202-1 [@CL-CMP-5971; @CL-CMP-5972; @CL-CMP-5975] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.202-1 [@CL-CMP-1018]; no RFO equivalent for FAR 52.204-1, which the RFO text prints as reserved [@CL-CMP-1022]; no RFO equivalent for FAR 52.201-1, which the RFO text prints as reserved [@CL-CMP-1023] |

The contract is subject to the written approval of the designated agency official the clause identifies and is not binding until so approved [@CL-CMP-5971]. The clause text includes a bracketed instruction to identify the title of the designated agency official [@CL-CMP-6662]. The contracting officer shall insert the approval clause at 52.204-1 in solicitations and contracts if required by agency procedures [@CL-CMP-9751].

The survey provision encourages all actual and potential offerors to provide feedback on the preaward and debriefing processes, as applicable [@CL-CMP-6663]. A word or term used in a provision or clause has the same meaning as the definition in FAR 2.101 in effect when the solicitation was issued, unless one of the exceptions listed in the definitions clause applies [@CL-CMP-5975].

## How it works

### Approval

Until the designated official approves the contract in writing, the contract is not binding [@CL-CMP-5971]. The clause has a bracketed place to identify the title of the designated agency official, and this card does not name any official [@CL-CMP-6662].

### Voluntary survey

Feedback on the preaward and debriefing processes may be provided to agencies up to 45 days after award [@CL-CMP-5972]. The feedback is anonymous, unless the participant identifies itself in the survey [@CL-CMP-5974]. Offerors can take part by selecting the link that the provision gives [@CL-CMP-6664].

The contracting officer will not review the information provided until after contract award and will not consider it in the award decision [@CL-CMP-5973]. The survey is voluntary and does not convey any protections, rights, or grounds for protest [@CL-CMP-6665]. It creates a way for actual and potential offerors to give the Government constructive feedback about the preaward and debriefing processes used for a specific acquisition [@CL-CMP-6666].

### Definitions

The FAR 2.101 meaning in effect when the solicitation was issued governs a word or term that the FAR defines [@CL-CMP-5975]. The FAR definition does not control in five listed cases. The first is where the solicitation, or an amended solicitation, provides a different definition [@CL-CMP-6667]. The second is where the contracting parties agree to a different definition [@CL-CMP-6668]. The third is where the part, subpart, or section of the FAR that prescribes the provision or clause provides a different meaning [@CL-CMP-5976]. The fourth is where the word or term is defined in FAR part 31 for use in the cost principles and procedures [@CL-CMP-6669]. The fifth is where the word or term defines an acquisition-related threshold and the threshold is adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR 1.109(a): the changed threshold then applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-CMP-5977].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.202-1 keeps the rule that a word or term the FAR defines has the same meaning as the definition in FAR 2.101 in effect at the time the solicitation was issued, unless an exception listed in the clause applies [@CL-CMP-1018; @CL-CMP-5975]. It keeps the exceptions for a different definition in the solicitation or an amended solicitation and for a different definition agreed by the contracting parties [@CL-CMP-1019; @CL-CMP-6667; @CL-CMP-6668], and for a different meaning in the part, subpart or section of the FAR that prescribes the provision or clause and for a definition in FAR part 31 for the cost principles and procedures [@CL-CMP-1020; @CL-CMP-5976; @CL-CMP-6669]. Its exception for an acquisition-related threshold adjusted for inflation says the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment, and points to FAR 1.108 [@CL-CMP-1021; @CL-CMP-5977]; the codified exception points to FAR 1.109(a) and FAR 1.109(d) instead [@CL-CMP-5977]. The RFO text prints sections 52.204-1 and 52.201-1 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1022; @CL-CMP-1023].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract is signed and the designated agency official approves it in writing 19 days later. The contract is not binding before that approval [@CL-CMP-5971]. An offeror that lost the competition gives survey feedback 30 days after award, which is inside the 45-day period [@CL-CMP-5972], and does not identify itself, so the feedback is anonymous [@CL-CMP-5974]. The offeror cannot use the survey as a protest ground [@CL-CMP-6665], and the contracting officer did not consider it in the award decision [@CL-CMP-5973]. The approval clause and the survey provision are the codified ones, and the RFO text prints their section numbers as reserved [@CL-CMP-5971; @CL-CMP-5972; @CL-CMP-1022; @CL-CMP-1023].

A solicitation is issued in March. A clause in the contract uses a term that the FAR defines, and nothing in the solicitation, the contract or the prescribing part of the FAR gives a different meaning. The meaning in the FAR as it stood in March governs, even if the FAR text is later amended [@CL-CMP-5975]. A separate clause refers to a dollar threshold that the FAR set at 100 when the solicitation was issued. The FAR adjusts it for inflation to 110 in month 8 of performance. From that month the contract uses 110 for the rest of its term, and only a later adjustment would change it again [@CL-CMP-5977].

## Never confuse

The survey is voluntary and does not convey any protections, rights, or grounds for protest [@CL-CMP-6665], and the contracting officer will not review the information provided until after contract award and will not consider it in the award decision [@CL-CMP-5973].

The approval clause addresses when the contract is binding [@CL-CMP-5971]; the survey provision addresses feedback on the preaward and debriefing processes [@CL-CMP-6663]; and the definitions clause addresses the meaning of words and terms that the FAR defines [@CL-CMP-5975].

Where the word or term defines an acquisition-related threshold and the threshold is adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR 1.109(a), the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-CMP-5977]. The FAR 2.101 meaning also gives way where the word or term is defined in FAR part 31, for use in the cost principles and procedures [@CL-CMP-6669].

The approval clause and the survey provision are codified texts, and the RFO text prints their section numbers, FAR 52.204-1 and FAR 52.201-1, as reserved [@CL-CMP-5971; @CL-CMP-5972; @CL-CMP-1022; @CL-CMP-1023].
