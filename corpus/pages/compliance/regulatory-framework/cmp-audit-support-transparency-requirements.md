## Purpose

Shows what an audit-support firm represents and discloses about disciplinary proceedings when it offers, and what it discloses again after award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4392] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.237-7025 and DFARS 252.237-7026 [@CL-CMP-4392; @CL-CMP-4393] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | DFARS 252.237-7025: OCT 2022 [@CL-CMP-10076]; DFARS 252.237-7026: OCT 2022 [@CL-CMP-10077] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4392] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to a solicitation that includes the preaward provision, which is used as prescribed in DFARS 237.270(e)(3), and contractors under contracts that include the postaward clause, which is used as prescribed in DFARS 237.270(e)(4) [@CL-CMP-4392; @CL-CMP-4393] |

In the preaward provision, the offeror represents, for the 3-year period preceding the offer, whether it or any of its principals or employees have been the subject of disciplinary proceedings before an entity with the authority to enforce compliance with rules or laws applying to audit services [@CL-CMP-4328]. The representation covers rules or laws applying to audit services or audit remediation services offered by the offeror, and the proceedings it reaches fall in two categories, those not yet fully adjudicated or settled and those fully adjudicated or settled against the offeror and/or its principals or employees [@CL-CMP-4328; @CL-CMP-4329].

An offeror that checked the "have" box shall, at a minimum, disclose for each such proceeding the entity hearing the case and the case or file number, among the listed items [@CL-CMP-4330]. The Government will safeguard and treat as confidential all statements provided under the provision where the statement has been marked "confidential" or "proprietary" by the offeror [@CL-CMP-4331].

## How it works

Prior to each contract action under the contract, including renewal or modification, the contractor shall disclose the details of any disciplinary proceedings with respect to the firm and/or its principals or employees [@CL-CMP-4332]. The disclosure covers proceedings before an entity with the authority to enforce compliance with rules or laws applying to audit services or audit remediation services offered by the contractor, and whether there has been any change with regard to previously reported proceedings since the last contract action [@CL-CMP-4333]. At a minimum it includes the entity hearing the case, the case or file number, and a brief description of the allegation or conduct at issue and, if fully adjudicated or settled, of the outcome [@CL-CMP-4334]. The preaward representation looks back 3 years [@CL-CMP-4328]. The postaward text prints no dollar threshold and no look-back period [@CL-CMP-4332; @CL-CMP-4333]. Updates are due prior to each contract action [@CL-CMP-4332].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A firm offers audit remediation services. Two years ago one of its employees was the subject of a proceeding that is still open. The firm checks "have" and lists the entity hearing the case, the case number, and the conduct at issue. After award, before a contract modification, it discloses again and says the case has since been settled. The answer: the offer-time disclosure and the pre-modification update are both required, and the update states what changed.

## Never confuse

The preaward provision is a representation and disclosure made with the offer [@CL-CMP-4330], and it carries a look-back period [@CL-CMP-4328]; the postaward clause differs, being a disclosure made before each contract action that also reports changes since the last action [@CL-CMP-4333]. Neither is the contracting officer's request for audit services, which is covered on [Contract audit services](../../estimating/pricing/est-contract-audit-services.md). The responsibility standards that apply to an audit services offeror are a separate provision, covered on [Special standards of responsibility and compliance with audit standards](cmp-audit-services-responsibility-and-standards.md).
