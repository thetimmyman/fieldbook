## Purpose

Shows what the Rights in Data—General clause means by data and by unlimited rights, which data the Government gets unlimited rights in, and what the contractor keeps.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3679] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.227-14(a) and (b) [@CL-CMP-3679; @CL-CMP-3684] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.227-14(a) and (b); RFO FAR 52.227-19 (reserved) [@CL-CMP-2199; @CL-CMP-2281; @CL-CMP-2282; @CL-CMP-2283; @CL-CMP-2284; @CL-CMP-2285; @CL-CMP-2286; @CL-CMP-2287; @CL-CMP-2288; @CL-CMP-2289; @CL-CMP-2290; @CL-CMP-2291; @CL-CMP-2292; @CL-CMP-2191; @CL-CMP-0645] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.227-14: May 2014 [@CL-CMP-2191]; FAR 52.227-19: DEC 2007 [@CL-CMP-10262] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3679] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

Unless the copyright paragraph provides otherwise, the Government shall have unlimited rights in data first produced in the performance of the contract and in form, fit, and function data delivered under the contract [@CL-CMP-3684]. Under the same allocation paragraph, the Government also has unlimited rights in data delivered under the contract, except for restricted computer software, that constitute manuals or instructional and training material for installation, operation, or routine maintenance and repair of items, components, or processes delivered or furnished for use under the contract [@CL-CMP-3684; @CL-CMP-3685]. A last listed category is all other data delivered under the contract, unless provided otherwise for limited rights data or restricted computer software under the withholding paragraph [@CL-CMP-3684; @CL-CMP-3686].

Unlimited rights are the Government's rights to use, disclose, reproduce, prepare derivative works, distribute copies to the public, and perform and display publicly, in any manner and for any purpose, and to have or permit others to do so [@CL-CMP-3679].

The Contractor keeps the right to assert copyright in data first produced in the performance of the contract, to the extent the copyright paragraph provides [@CL-CMP-3687]. It also keeps the right to protect limited rights data and restricted computer software from unauthorized disclosure and use, to the extent the withholding paragraph provides [@CL-CMP-3688].

## How it works

The clause starts from a default of unlimited rights for the Government, and then lets some privately funded data stay protected. Data means recorded information in any form, including technical data and computer software, and it leaves out information incidental to contract administration [@CL-CMP-3680]. Two protected classes are defined: limited rights data, which is data other than computer software that embody trade secrets or are commercial or financial and confidential or privileged, to the extent they pertain to items, components, or processes developed at private expense, including minor modifications [@CL-CMP-3681], and restricted computer software, which is computer software developed at private expense that is a trade secret, is commercial or financial and confidential or privileged, or is copyrighted computer software, including minor modifications [@CL-CMP-3682]. Form, fit, and function data includes data relating to items, components, or processes that is sufficient to enable physical and functional interchangeability, and, except as provided in the copyright paragraph, the Government has unlimited rights in it when it is delivered under the contract [@CL-CMP-3683; @CL-CMP-3684]. For computer software, it means data identifying source, functional characteristics, and performance requirements, and it specifically excludes the source code, algorithms, processes, formulas, and flow charts of the software [@CL-CMP-3745]. How the copyright, marking and withholding paragraphs work is set out on the separate card [Rights in data, general: copyright, withholding and markings](cmp-rights-in-data-general-copyright-withholding-and-markings.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO paragraph (b)(1) keeps the rule that, except as provided in the copyright paragraph, the Government shall have unlimited rights in data first produced in the performance of the contract and in form, fit, and function data delivered under the contract [@CL-CMP-2281; @CL-CMP-3684]. It keeps, as a listed category, data delivered under the contract, except for restricted computer software, that constitute manuals or instructional and training material for installation, operation, or routine maintenance and repair of items, components, or processes delivered or furnished for use under the contract [@CL-CMP-2282; @CL-CMP-3685], and, as the last listed category, all other data delivered under the contract unless provided otherwise for limited rights data or restricted computer software under the withholding paragraph [@CL-CMP-2283; @CL-CMP-3686]. RFO paragraph (b)(2) keeps the Contractor's right to assert copyright in data first produced in the performance of the contract to the extent provided in the first-produced-data copyright paragraph [@CL-CMP-2284; @CL-CMP-3687], and its right to protect from unauthorized disclosure and use those data that are limited rights data or restricted computer software, to the extent provided in the withholding paragraph [@CL-CMP-2285; @CL-CMP-3688]. RFO paragraph (a) keeps the meaning of unlimited rights as the Government's rights to use, disclose, reproduce, prepare derivative works, distribute copies to the public, and perform and display publicly, in any manner and for any purpose, and to have or permit others to do so [@CL-CMP-2199; @CL-CMP-3679]. It keeps the definition of data as recorded information, regardless of form or the media on which it may be recorded, which includes technical data and computer software [@CL-CMP-2286; @CL-CMP-3680], and its last sentence on data says the term does not include information incidental to contract administration, such as financial, administrative, cost or pricing, or management information [@CL-CMP-2287]. It keeps the definition of limited rights data in the basic clause as data, other than computer software, that embody trade secrets or are commercial or financial and confidential or privileged, to the extent that such data pertain to items, components, or processes developed at private expense, including minor modifications [@CL-CMP-2288; @CL-CMP-3681], and the definition of restricted computer software as computer software developed at private expense that is a trade secret, is commercial or financial and confidential or privileged, or is copyrighted computer software, including minor modifications of the computer software [@CL-CMP-2289; @CL-CMP-3682]. It keeps the definition of form, fit, and function data as data relating to items, components, or processes that are sufficient to enable physical and functional interchangeability, and data identifying source, size, configuration, mating and attachment characteristics [@CL-CMP-2290; @CL-CMP-2291; @CL-CMP-3683], and the definition goes on to functional characteristics and performance requirements [@CL-CMP-2291]. For computer software, it keeps the meaning of form, fit, and function data as data identifying source, functional characteristics, and performance requirements but specifically excluding the source code, algorithms, processes, formulas, and flow charts of the software [@CL-CMP-2292; @CL-CMP-3745]. It also keeps the rule that the Contractor may withhold from delivery qualifying limited rights data or restricted computer software that are not data identified in paragraphs (b)(1)(i), (ii), and (iii) [@CL-CMP-2190; @CL-CMP-3697], and that, as a condition to this withholding, the Contractor shall identify the data being withheld and furnish form, fit, and function data instead [@CL-CMP-2191; @CL-CMP-3698]. RFO FAR part 52 prints the number 52.227-19, which the codified text uses for a clause on commercial computer software delivered under a contract [@CL-CMP-3712], as reserved [@CL-CMP-0645].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract calls for a test report first produced in the work and a manual for operating the delivered machine. The Government has unlimited rights in both, so it may reproduce them and give copies to the public. The contractor may assert copyright in the test report, to the extent the copyright paragraph allows. The contractor also owns a program built at its own expense that qualifies as restricted computer software. The first three listed categories do not cover it, so the contractor may withhold it, if it identifies the program and furnishes form, fit, and function data instead.

## Never confuse

Limited rights data is data other than computer software, while restricted computer software is software [@CL-CMP-3681; @CL-CMP-3682]. Neither is the same as form, fit, and function data, which the contractor shall furnish instead, as a condition of withholding qualifying limited rights data or restricted computer software [@CL-CMP-3683; @CL-CMP-3697; @CL-CMP-3698]. Information incidental to contract administration, such as financial, administrative, or cost or pricing information, is not data under this clause [@CL-CMP-3680]. Commercial computer software delivered under a contract may not be used, reproduced, or disclosed by the Government except as listed or as the contract expressly states otherwise [@CL-CMP-3712]; see [Commercial computer software license clause](cmp-commercial-computer-software-license.md). That card describes the codified clause: RFO FAR part 52 prints only a reserved line under the number 52.227-19, so the RFO text has no clause text under that number [@CL-CMP-0645; @CL-CMP-3712].
