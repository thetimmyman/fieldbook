## Purpose

Shows why companies form contractor team arrangements, when the FAR says they may be used, and when they are normally formed. The Government's recognition of arrangements is on the linked supply chain card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8251] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.602 [@CL-CMP-8251] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8251] |
| Applies to | Complex research and development acquisitions and other appropriate acquisitions, including production [@CL-CMP-8253] |

Contractor team arrangements may be desirable from both a Government and industry standpoint in order to enable the companies involved to complement each other's unique capabilities [@CL-CMP-8251]. They may also be desirable to enable the companies to offer the Government the best combination of performance, cost and delivery for the system or product being acquired [@CL-CMP-8252].

Contractor team arrangements may be particularly appropriate in complex research and development acquisitions, but may be used in other appropriate acquisitions, including production [@CL-CMP-8253]. The companies involved normally form a contractor team arrangement before submitting an offer, but they may enter into an arrangement later in the acquisition process, including after contract award [@CL-CMP-8254].

## How it works

The section is permissive: it says arrangements may be desirable and may be used [@CL-CMP-8251; @CL-CMP-8253]. The companies involved form the arrangement, and the section names no official who approves it. Timing is a normal practice rather than a requirement, because the next sentence allows an arrangement later in the acquisition, including after contract award [@CL-CMP-8254]. The section does not say how the Government recognizes an arrangement or what the companies must disclose. That is on the [contractor team arrangements](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-contractor-team-arrangements.md) card, and the small business side is on the [small business teaming](../../supply-chain/procure-to-pay/sup-small-business-teaming-and-disaster-set-aside.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Two firms agree to offer jointly on a 12,000,000 dollar development effort, pairing one firm's payload design with the other firm's integration work. They sign the arrangement ten days before the offer is due. Answer: this is the normal pattern, an arrangement formed before the offer to complement each other's unique capabilities [@CL-CMP-8251; @CL-CMP-8254]. If the same two firms had instead signed after contract award, the section would still allow it, because companies may enter into an arrangement later in the acquisition process, including after contract award [@CL-CMP-8254].

## Never confuse

The arrangement is not tied to the moment of the offer: companies normally form it before submitting an offer, but they may enter into one later, including after award [@CL-CMP-8254]. Desirability is also not a rule for every acquisition. The section names complex research and development acquisitions as particularly appropriate, while allowing other appropriate acquisitions, including production [@CL-CMP-8253].
