## Purpose

Shows what a material management and accounting system is, what makes it acceptable, and which solicitations and contracts the DoD tells its users to put the clause on.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7846] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 242.7201 [@CL-CMP-7846]; DFARS 242.7204 [@CL-CMP-7850] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7846] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts above the simplified acquisition threshold that are not for commercial products or services, are not awarded to small businesses, educational institutions or nonprofit organizations, and are cost-reimbursement or fixed-price with progress payments based on costs incurred [@CL-CMP-7850; @CL-CMP-7851; @CL-CMP-7852] |

The definitions are for the terms as used in this subpart [@CL-CMP-8070]. A material management and accounting system is the contractor's system or systems for planning, controlling and accounting for the acquisition, use, issuing and disposition of material [@CL-CMP-7847]. An acceptable system is one that generally complies with the system criteria in paragraph (d) of the clause at 252.242-7004, Material Management and Accounting System [@CL-CMP-7846].

The clause at 252.242-7004 is to be used in all solicitations and contracts that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold and are not for commercial products or commercial services, and that also meet two further conditions [@CL-CMP-7850]. First, the contract is not awarded to a small business, an educational institution or a nonprofit organization [@CL-CMP-7851]. Second, the contract is either a cost-reimbursement contract or a fixed-price contract with progress payments made on the basis of costs incurred by the contractor as work progresses under the contract [@CL-CMP-7852]. A contract must meet all of these conditions [@CL-CMP-7850; @CL-CMP-7851; @CL-CMP-7852].

## How it works

A system may be manual or automated, and it may stand alone or be integrated with planning, engineering, estimating, purchasing, inventory, accounting or other systems [@CL-CMP-7848]. The section also defines valid time-phased requirements as material that is both needed to fulfil the production plan, including reasonable quantities for scrap, shrinkage and yield, and charged or billed to contracts or other cost objectives in a manner consistent with that need [@CL-CMP-7849]. The prescription turns on the contract: it must exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, must not be for commercial products or services, and must not be awarded to the excluded kinds of awardee [@CL-CMP-7850; @CL-CMP-7851]. The contract type must also be one of the two listed [@CL-CMP-7852]. An acceptable material management and accounting system generally complies with the system criteria in paragraph (d) of the clause at 252.242-7004 [@CL-CMP-7846]. For the amount of the threshold, see [Acquisition thresholds](cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor runs a manual system that plans material for a production line and bills it to cost objectives as the line consumes it. The system generally complies with the system criteria in the clause at 252.242-7004, so it is an acceptable system [@CL-CMP-7846; @CL-CMP-7848].

A cost-reimbursement contract worth 3,000,000 dollars is awarded to a small business, so the clause is not prescribed for it [@CL-CMP-7851]. A cost-reimbursement contract of the same size is awarded to a large business for a non-commercial requirement. It is above the simplified acquisition threshold and is a listed contract type, so the clause is prescribed [@CL-CMP-7850; @CL-CMP-7852]. A third contract is also worth 3,000,000 dollars but is firm-fixed-price with no progress payments. It does not meet the contract-type condition, so the clause is not prescribed even though it is above the threshold [@CL-CMP-7852].

## Never confuse

An acceptable material management and accounting system is one that generally complies with the system criteria in paragraph (d) of the clause at 252.242-7004 [@CL-CMP-7846]. A material management and accounting system is the contractor's system or systems for planning, controlling and accounting for the acquisition, use, issuing and disposition of material [@CL-CMP-7847]. DFARS 242.7204(b)(2) refers to fixed-price contracts with progress payments made on the basis of costs incurred by the contractor as work progresses under the contract [@CL-CMP-7852]. DFARS 242.7503(b) refers to a contract with progress payments made on the basis of costs incurred by the contractor or on a percentage or stage of completion [@CL-CMP-7858]. For the clause itself, see [Material management and accounting system](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-material-management-accounting-system.md); for the surrounding business-system policy, see [Business systems clause and MMAS policy](../business-systems/cmp-business-systems-clause-and-mmas-policy.md).
