## Purpose

Shows the two notification duties that run outside the wage flow: a report to two agencies when a visa is denied for a listed reason, and the posted employee rights notice with its electronic posting, sanctions and subcontract flowdown.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5237] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-29 and 52.222-40 [@CL-CMP-5237; @CL-CMP-5241] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5237] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the visa denial clause or the employee rights notice clause [@CL-CMP-5239; @CL-CMP-5241] |

The visa denial clause makes it a violation of Executive Order 11246 for a contractor to refuse to employ any applicant, or not to assign any person hired, in the United States, Puerto Rico, the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the United States Virgin Islands or Wake Island [@CL-CMP-5237], on the basis that the individual's race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or national origin is not compatible with the policies of the country where or for whom the work will be performed [@CL-CMP-5238]. The contractor must notify the Department of State's Assistant Secretary, Bureau of Political-Military Affairs, and the Department of Labor's Deputy Assistant Secretary for Federal Contract Compliance [@CL-CMP-5239] when it has knowledge of any employee or potential employee being denied an entry visa to a country where the contract will be performed and believes the denial is attributable to that person's race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or national origin [@CL-CMP-5240].

## How it works

The trigger for the visa notice is knowledge of the denial, together with a belief about its cause [@CL-CMP-5240].

During the term of the contract, the contractor must post an employee notice, of the size, form and content the Secretary of Labor prescribes, in conspicuous places in and about its plants and offices [@CL-CMP-5241]. The posting must be where employees covered by the National Labor Relations Act engage in activities relating to performance of the contract, including all places where notices to employees are customarily posted both physically and electronically, in the languages employees speak [@CL-CMP-5242]. Physical posting of the employee notice must be in conspicuous places in and about the contractor's plants and offices [@CL-CMP-6017], so that the notice is prominent and readily seen by employees who are covered by the National Labor Relations Act and engage in activities related to the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-5774]. A contractor that customarily posts notices to employees electronically must also post the required notice electronically, by prominent display [@CL-CMP-5376]. The electronic notice is a prominently displayed link, on any Web site the contractor maintains and customarily uses for notices to employees about terms and conditions of employment, to the Department of Labor's Web site containing the full text of the poster [@CL-CMP-5377]. The link to the Department's Web site, as referenced in paragraph (b)(3) of the clause, must read, “Important Notice about Employee Rights to Organize and Bargain Collectively with Their Employers” [@CL-CMP-5775]. The contractor must comply with all provisions of the employee notice and related rules, regulations and orders of the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CMP-5378].

The required employee notice, printed by the Department of Labor, may be obtained from the Division of Interpretations and Standards, Office of Labor-Management Standards, at the address and telephone number that the clause gives [@CL-CMP-5776]. The notice may also be obtained from any field office of the Office of Labor-Management Standards or Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, or be provided by the Federal contracting agency if requested [@CL-CMP-5777]. The notice may be downloaded from the Web site of the Office of Labor-Management Standards, or be reproduced and used as exact duplicate copies of the Department of Labor's official poster [@CL-CMP-5778]. The required text of the employee notice is located at Appendix A, Subpart A, of 29 CFR Part 471 [@CL-CMP-5779].

If the contractor does not comply with paragraphs (a) through (d) of the clause, the contract may be terminated or suspended in whole or in part, and the contractor may be suspended or debarred [@CL-CMP-5243]. Other sanctions or remedies may be imposed as provided by 29 CFR part 471, which implements Executive Order 13496, or as otherwise provided by law [@CL-CMP-5379].

The contractor must include the substance of the clause, including its subcontract paragraph, in every subcontract that exceeds $10,000 and will be performed wholly or partially in the United States [@CL-CMP-5244], unless the rules, regulations or orders of the Secretary of Labor exempt the subcontract [@CL-CMP-5245]. It must not procure supplies or services in a way designed to avoid the applicability of Executive Order 13496 or the clause [@CL-CMP-5380]. It must take the action with respect to any such subcontract that the Secretary of Labor directs as a means of enforcing the provisions, including the imposition of sanctions for noncompliance [@CL-CMP-5381]. If that direction leaves the contractor in, or threatened with, litigation with a subcontractor, it may request the United States, through the Secretary of Labor, to enter into the litigation to protect the interests of the United States [@CL-CMP-5382].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A project manager learns that a certified welder was refused an entry visa for a site country and believes the refusal turned on the welder's national origin. The company sends the notice to both named offices and files a copy with the contract papers. Posting is unchanged: the site trailer board and the intranet page used for employee notices still carry the employee rights notice, with an intranet link that reads “Important Notice about Employee Rights to Organize and Bargain Collectively with Their Employers” and leads to the Department of Labor poster, in the languages the crew speaks. The poster on the trailer board is an exact duplicate copy of the Department of Labor's official poster, which the company downloaded from the Office of Labor-Management Standards Web site. The company then reviews its subcontracts. A 9,000 dollar subcontract performed in the United States is below the 10,000 dollar flowdown test, while a 15,000 dollar subcontract performed wholly in the United States carries the clause unless the Secretary of Labor's rules exempt it.

## Never confuse

The employee rights notice is a posting duty, not a statement of the contractor's own bargaining position: the notice has the size, form and content the Secretary of Labor prescribes [@CL-CMP-5241], and the contractor must comply with it and the related rules and orders [@CL-CMP-5378]. The visa denial notice depends on what the contractor knows and believes about the denial [@CL-CMP-5240].
