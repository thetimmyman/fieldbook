## Purpose

Shows how offers are priced under the count-of-articles and bulk weight instructions, and what individual laundry service requires.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4685; @CL-CMP-4687; @CL-CMP-4689] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.237-7012, DFARS 252.237-7013 and DFARS 252.237-7017 [@CL-CMP-4685; @CL-CMP-4687; @CL-CMP-4689] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4685; @CL-CMP-4687; @CL-CMP-4689] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include the count-of-articles or bulk weight instruction to offerors, and contracts that include the Individual Laundry clause [@CL-CMP-4685; @CL-CMP-4687; @CL-CMP-4689] |

Under the count-of-articles provision, the offeror shall include unit prices for each item in a lot, and those unit prices shall include all costs to the Government of providing the services, including pickup and delivery charges. Failure to offer on any item in a lot shall be cause for rejection of the offer on that lot [@CL-CMP-4685]. The Contracting Officer will evaluate offers based on the estimated quantities in the solicitation. Award generally will be made to a single offeror for all lots, but the Contracting Officer may award by individual lot when it is more advantageous to the Government [@CL-CMP-4686].

Under the bulk weight provision, offers shall be submitted on a unit price per pound of serviced laundry, and the unit prices shall include all costs to the Government of providing the service, including pickup and delivery charges [@CL-CMP-4687]. Under both instructions, prospective offerors may inspect the types of articles to be serviced and contact the Contracting Officer to make inspection arrangements [@CL-CMP-4797; @CL-CMP-4798]. The Contracting Officer will evaluate bids based on the estimated pounds of serviced laundry stated in the solicitation. Award generally will be made to a single offeror for all lots, but the Contracting Officer may award by individual lot when it is more advantageous to the Government [@CL-CMP-4688].

## How it works

The individual laundry clause requires the Contractor to provide laundry service on both a unit bundle and on a piece-rate bundle basis for individual personnel [@CL-CMP-4689]. The total number of pieces in the schedule's Estimated Quantity column is the estimated amount of individual laundry for the contract [@CL-CMP-4799]. The estimate of individual laundry in the clause is for information only and is not a representation of the amount of individual laundry to be ordered, and individuals may elect whether or not to use the laundry services [@CL-CMP-4690]. Charges for individual laundry will be on a per unit bundle or a piece-rate basis [@CL-CMP-4777]. The contractor shall provide individual laundry bundle delivery tickets for the individuals to designate a unit bundle or a piece-rate bundle, and each bundle will be accompanied by a delivery ticket listing its contents [@CL-CMP-4800]. The maximum number of pieces allowed per bundle is as specified in the schedule and as the clause lists, which includes a bundle of 26 pieces including the laundry bag [@CL-CMP-4778] and a bundle of 13 pieces including the laundry bag [@CL-CMP-4779]. Each bundle will contain approximately the stated number of pieces of outer garments, which shall be starched and pressed, and outer garments include shirts, trousers, jackets, dresses and coats [@CL-CMP-4801; @CL-CMP-4802].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Under the count-of-articles instruction, an offeror prices every item in a lot at a unit price that includes pickup and delivery, for example 2 per item. If it leaves one item in the lot unpriced, the offer on that lot can be rejected. The Contracting Officer evaluates offers on the estimated quantities in the solicitation, and award generally goes to a single offeror for all lots, unless awarding by lot is more advantageous to the Government. Under the bulk weight instruction, another offeror prices 1.50 per pound, again including pickup and delivery, and the Contracting Officer evaluates on the estimated pounds stated in the solicitation. Before pricing, either offeror may inspect the types of articles to be serviced by arranging it with the Contracting Officer. If the contract is awarded, individual bundles then go out with delivery tickets listing their contents, and outer garments in each bundle are starched and pressed.

## Never confuse

The two pricing instructions differ in the unit offered, which is the item under one and the pound under the other [@CL-CMP-4685; @CL-CMP-4687]. Neither is the delivery ticket or loss or damage clause, which is on [Laundry delivery tickets and loss or damage](../../supply-chain/sourcing-evidence/sup-dfars-laundry-delivery-tickets-and-loss-or-damage.md). Mortuary services are a separate services topic, on [Group interment, preparation history, and transfer and adoption of military animals](cmp-dfars-mortuary-group-interment-preparation-and-military-animals.md).
