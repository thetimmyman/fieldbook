## Purpose

Shows what makes a payment to the Government a voluntary refund, and when a program manager may place a technical representative at a contractor facility.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7840] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 242.7100 [@CL-CMP-7840]; DFARS 242.7400 [@CL-CMP-7842] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7840] |
| Applies to | Payments or credits that no contractual or other legal obligation requires [@CL-CMP-7840]; program managers who conclude that technical representation is needed at a contractor facility [@CL-CMP-7842] |

A voluntary refund is a payment or credit to the Government, under one or more contracts or subcontracts, from a contractor or subcontractor, that no contractual or other legal obligation requires [@CL-CMP-7840]. The section directs that the procedures at PGI 242.7100 be followed for voluntary refunds [@CL-CMP-7841]. It does not name who follows them.

Program managers may conclude that they need technical representation in contractor facilities to perform non-contract administration service technical duties and to provide liaison, guidance and assistance on systems and programs [@CL-CMP-7842]. In those cases the program manager may assign technical representatives under the procedures in 242.7401 [@CL-CMP-7843]. The conclusion and the assignment are the program manager's choice, and the section gives the contractor no role in either.

A technical representative is a representative of a DoD program, project or system office who performs non-contract administration service technical duties at or near a contractor facility [@CL-CMP-7844]. A technical representative is not a representative of a contract administration or contract audit component, and is not a contracting officer's representative [@CL-CMP-7845].

## How it works

A voluntary refund is defined by what is missing: no contractual or other legal obligation requires the payment or credit, whether it comes from a contractor or a subcontractor and whether it adjusts one contract or several [@CL-CMP-7840]. The handling procedures are in PGI 242.7100, not in the section itself [@CL-CMP-7841]. The technical representation rules are permissions for program managers. A program manager may conclude that representation is needed and may then assign technical representatives under the procedures in 242.7401 [@CL-CMP-7842; @CL-CMP-7843]. The section also says what a technical representative is and is not, so the role is kept apart from contract administration, contract audit and contracting officer's representative roles [@CL-CMP-7844; @CL-CMP-7845].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor sends the Government a credit of 40,000 dollars under one of its contracts. No contract term or other legal obligation requires the credit, so it is a voluntary refund, and the procedures at PGI 242.7100 are the ones to follow [@CL-CMP-7840; @CL-CMP-7841].

A program manager concludes that the program needs engineering liaison at a contractor plant and assigns a technical representative under the procedures in 242.7401 [@CL-CMP-7842; @CL-CMP-7843]. The representative works for the program office on technical duties, so the representative is not a contracting officer's representative and not a representative of a contract audit component [@CL-CMP-7844; @CL-CMP-7845].

## Never confuse

A voluntary refund is not a payment that a contract or other legal obligation requires; the absence of such an obligation is what makes it voluntary [@CL-CMP-7840]. A voluntary disclosure of defective pricing is not a voluntary refund as defined in DFARS 242.7100 [@CL-EST-2453]; see [DoD price reasonableness data and voluntary defective pricing disclosure](../../estimating/pricing/est-dfars-price-reasonableness-data-and-voluntary-defective-pricing-disclosure.md). A technical representative is not a contract administration or contract audit representative, and not a contracting officer's representative [@CL-CMP-7845]. For other Government visits to a contractor facility, see [Corporate ACO and facility visits](cmp-corporate-aco-and-facility-visits.md).
