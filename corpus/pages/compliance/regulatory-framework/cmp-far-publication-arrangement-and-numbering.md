## Purpose

Explains where the FAR is published, how it is arranged, how a FAR citation number is built, and how a FAR reference is written inside and outside the FAR.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8394] |
| Codified citation | FAR 1.105-1, 1.105-2 and 1.105-3 [@CL-CMP-8394; @CL-CMP-8400; @CL-CMP-9728] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8394] |
| Applies to | Readers who cite FAR text, and agencies that codify agency acquisition regulations [@CL-CMP-8398; @CL-CMP-8401] |

The FAR is published in the daily issue of the Federal Register, in cumulated form in the Code of Federal Regulations, and in a separate edition [@CL-CMP-8394]. It is issued as Chapter 1 of Title 48 [@CL-CMP-8395]. Subsequent chapters are reserved for agency acquisition regulations that implement or supplement the FAR [@CL-CMP-8396]. The CFR Staff will assign chapter numbers to requesting agencies [@CL-CMP-8397].

Each numbered unit or segment of an agency acquisition regulation that is codified in the CFR shall begin with the chapter number [@CL-CMP-8398]. The chapter number assigned to the FAR will not be included in the numbered units or segments of the FAR [@CL-CMP-8399].

## How it works

The FAR is divided into subchapters, parts, subparts, sections and subsections, and each part covers a separate aspect of acquisition [@CL-CMP-8400]. The numbering system permits the discrete identification of every FAR paragraph [@CL-CMP-8753]. The digits to the left of the decimal point represent the part number, and the numbers to the right of the decimal point and to the left of the dash represent, in order, the subpart of one or two digits and the section of two digits [@CL-CMP-8401]. The number to the right of the dash represents the subsection [@CL-CMP-8402].

Subdivisions may be used at the section and subsection level to identify individual paragraphs [@CL-CMP-8754]. Subdivisions below the section or subsection level consist of parenthetical alpha numerics in the sequence (a)(1)(i)(A)(1)(i) [@CL-CMP-8403]. Subchapters are not used with FAR number citations [@CL-CMP-8404].

Unless otherwise stated, cross-references indicate parts, subparts, sections, subsections, paragraphs, subparagraphs or subdivisions of the regulation itself [@CL-CMP-8405]. The regulation may be referred to as the Federal Acquisition Regulation or the FAR [@CL-CMP-8406]. In the FAR's own illustration that uses 9.106-4(d), a part is written "FAR part 9" outside the FAR and "part 9" within it [@CL-CMP-8755], and a subpart is written "FAR subpart 9.1" outside and "subpart 9.1" within [@CL-CMP-8756]. A section, subsection or paragraph takes the prefix "FAR" outside the FAR, as in "FAR 9.106-4(d)", and has no prefix within it [@CL-CMP-8757]. Citations of authority in the FAR, such as statutes or executive orders, shall follow the Federal Register form guides [@CL-CMP-8407].

These sections set no threshold, deadline or approval route. The two "shall" rules, the chapter-number prefix for codified agency regulations and the form of citations of authority in the FAR, bind agencies and the drafters of FAR text, not contractors.

Copies of the FAR in CFR form may be purchased from the Bookstore of the Government Publishing Office (GPO) [@CL-CMP-9728].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A requisition cites FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) and the reviewer needs to know what each piece means. The digits to the left of the decimal point, 15, are the part number [@CL-CMP-8401]. The digits between the decimal point and the dash, 403, are the subpart, 4, and the section, 03, so the section is 15.403 [@CL-CMP-8401]. The number to the right of the dash, 4, is the subsection [@CL-CMP-8402], and (a)(1) is a subdivision that identifies a paragraph [@CL-CMP-8403; @CL-CMP-8754]. Outside the FAR the reference carries the prefix "FAR", as written here, and within the FAR it is written without it [@CL-CMP-8757]. The chapter number of the FAR is not part of the number [@CL-CMP-8399].

## Never confuse

A subsection is not the same as a paragraph. The number to the right of the dash identifies the subsection [@CL-CMP-8402], while parenthetical alpha numerics below the section or subsection level identify the individual paragraphs [@CL-CMP-8403; @CL-CMP-8754].

The FAR number is also not an agency regulation number. A codified agency acquisition regulation begins each numbered unit with its assigned chapter number [@CL-CMP-8398], while the chapter number of the FAR is left out of FAR numbers [@CL-CMP-8399]. How agency regulations are numbered and published is on [Publication, codification and control of agency acquisition regulations](cmp-agency-acquisition-regulations-publication-and-control.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company's citation practice reads the FAR number as printed, part through paragraph and without the chapter number. [@Q-004]
