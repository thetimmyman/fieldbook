## Purpose

Shows when a successor contractor must pay at least the wages and fringe benefits in the predecessor's collective bargaining agreement, and which exceptions and timing limits the printed text lists.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3259] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.1002-2, FAR 22.1002-3 and FAR 22.1012-2 [@CL-CMP-3259; @CL-CMP-3263; @CL-CMP-8069; @CL-CMP-8148] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3259] |
| Applies to | Successor contractors on contracts over $2,500 for substantially the same services in the same locality [@CL-CMP-3259] |

Successor contractors performing on contracts in excess of $2,500 for substantially the same services performed in the same locality must pay wages and fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-3259]. The wages and fringe benefits must be at least equal to those in any bona fide collective bargaining agreement entered into under the predecessor contract, including accrued wages and benefits and prospective increases [@CL-CMP-3260]. The requirement is self-executing and is not contingent upon incorporating a wage determination or the wage and fringe benefit terms of the predecessor contractor's collective bargaining agreement in the successor contract [@CL-CMP-3360]. The requirement does not apply if the Secretary of Labor determines after a hearing that the wages and fringe benefits are substantially at variance with those prevailing for services of a similar character in the locality [@CL-CMP-3261]. It also does not apply if the Secretary of Labor determines that the wages and fringe benefits are not the result of arm's length negotiations [@CL-CMP-3262].

Timing can also keep a new or changed collective bargaining agreement from taking effect. In sealed bidding, notice received by the contracting agency of its terms less than 10 days before bid opening is one of two joined conditions [@CL-CMP-3263]. The other is that the contracting officer determines there is not reasonable time to incorporate the new or changed terms in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-3357]. For other contractual actions, one condition is receipt of notice after award of a successor contract or of a modification the section specifies [@CL-CMP-3264; @CL-CMP-3265], and that rule carries the proviso that the contract start of performance is within 30 days of the award of the contract or of the specified modification [@CL-CMP-3265]. In one of the two alternative cases the section states, namely that contract performance does not commence within 30 days of the award or of the specified modification, notice received by the agency not less than 10 days before commencement of the work is effective for purposes of the successor contract under section 6707(c) of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-3358; @CL-CMP-8209]. These limitations apply only if the timely notification required in section 22.1010 has been given [@CL-CMP-3359].

Contractors performing on service contracts in excess of $2,500 to which no predecessor contractor's collective bargaining agreement applies shall pay their employees at least the wages and fringe benefits found by the Department of Labor to prevail in the locality [@CL-CMP-8069] or, in the absence of a wage determination, the minimum wage set forth in the Fair Labor Standards Act [@CL-CMP-8148].

## How it works

The pay duty depends on substantially the same services in the same locality and on a bona fide agreement under the predecessor contract [@CL-CMP-3259; @CL-CMP-3260]. It is self-executing [@CL-CMP-3360], and it yields when the Secretary of Labor makes one of the listed determinations [@CL-CMP-3261; @CL-CMP-3262]. A separate timing rule decides whether a new or changed agreement takes effect, on the listed conditions and only after timely notification [@CL-CMP-3263; @CL-CMP-3357; @CL-CMP-3264; @CL-CMP-3265; @CL-CMP-3358; @CL-CMP-3359]. The sections also cover notification of procurement dates, a follow-up when the Department of Labor does not answer a wage determination request, and the steps for requesting a wage determination, and this card does not draft them.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A predecessor's collective bargaining agreement sets 20 per hour plus 5 in fringe benefits. A successor wins a 100,000 contract for substantially the same services in the same locality. The answer: the successor must pay at least 20 plus 5 per hour. The requirement does not apply if the Secretary of Labor determines after a hearing that these rates are substantially at variance with prevailing rates. It also does not apply if the Secretary determines that these rates are not the result of arm's length negotiations.

## Never confuse

The successor pay duty sets a pay floor [@CL-CMP-3260], while the timing rule decides whether a new or changed agreement takes effect [@CL-CMP-3263; @CL-CMP-3264]. When the clause goes into a contract and when wage determinations are obtained are on [Service contract labor standards clause and wage determinations](../../operating-model/talent/opm-service-contract-labor-standards.md). Requests for wage determinations and the incumbent's agreement are on [Obtaining wage determinations and successor pay](cmp-scls-wage-determinations-and-successorship.md). Requests for a hearing on a variance or an arm's length question are on [Service Contract Labor Standards: repair or remanufacturing, unidentified places and hearing requests](cmp-scls-remanufacturing-unidentified-places-and-hearings.md). None is restated here.
