## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer does when a project wage determination expires or a wage determination is modified, and how the answer depends on bid opening and award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3017] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.404-5 and FAR 22.404-6 [@CL-CMP-3017; @CL-CMP-3020] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3017] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for construction that carry a wage determination, handled by sealed bidding or by negotiation [@CL-CMP-3017; @CL-CMP-3019; @CL-CMP-3020] |

In sealed bidding, if a project wage determination for the primary site of the work expires before bid opening, or it appears before bid opening that one may expire before award, the contracting officer shall request a new determination early enough to ensure its receipt before bid opening [@CL-CMP-3017; @CL-CMP-8527]. Under the same procedure, if a project wage determination for the primary site of the work expires after bid opening but before award, the contracting officer shall request an extension of its expiration date from the Administrator, Wage and Hour Division [@CL-CMP-3018; @CL-CMP-3017]. That request shall be supported by a written finding, which includes a brief statement of factual support, that the extension is necessary and proper in the public interest to prevent injustice or undue hardship or to avoid serious impairment of the conduct of Government business [@CL-CMP-3111; @CL-CMP-3018]. In contracting by negotiation, if a project wage determination will or does expire before contract award, the contracting officer shall request a new wage determination from the Department of Labor [@CL-CMP-3019].

In sealed bidding, a written action modifying a wage determination is effective if the contracting agency receives it, or it is published on the Wage Determinations at SAM.gov website, 10 or more calendar days before the date of bid opening [@CL-CMP-3020]. Under the same sealed-bidding rules, if award is not made within 90 days after bid opening, a modification to a general wage determination published before award is effective for any resultant contract unless an extension of the 90-day period is obtained [@CL-CMP-3022; @CL-CMP-3020]. If an effective modification is received by the contracting officer after award, the contracting officer shall modify the contract to incorporate it retroactive to the date of award and equitably adjust the contract price for any increased or decreased cost of performance resulting from any changed wage rates [@CL-CMP-3023; @CL-CMP-3113; @CL-CMP-3020]. A modification received by the contracting agency, or published on the Wage Determinations at SAM.gov, less than 10 calendar days before the date of bid opening is also effective, unless the contracting officer finds that there is not reasonable time available before bid opening to notify the prospective bidders [@CL-CMP-3112; @CL-CMP-3020]. The rules for modifications that arrive after bid opening, for negotiated contracts and for the exercise of an option are not drafted on this card.

## How it works

Timing against bid opening and award decides which route applies. If a project determination expires before bid opening, or may expire before award, a new determination is requested early enough to ensure its receipt before bid opening [@CL-CMP-3017; @CL-CMP-8527]. If it expires after bid opening but before award, an extension is requested [@CL-CMP-3018], and under negotiation a new determination is requested whenever one will or does expire before award [@CL-CMP-3019]. A modification counts in sealed bidding if it is received or published 10 or more calendar days before bid opening [@CL-CMP-3020]. The 90-day point after bid opening and the retroactive adjustment after award are the later limits [@CL-CMP-3022; @CL-CMP-3023; @CL-CMP-3020].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A sealed bid is due to open on a set date. A modification to a general wage determination is published 12 calendar days before bid opening. It is more than the 10-day minimum, so it is effective and the contracting officer amends the solicitation before opening. Award is then delayed to 120 days after bid opening, and a later modification to the general determination is published before award. Because award came after 90 days, that modification applies to the resulting contract unless an extension of the 90 days was obtained.

## Never confuse

Expiration and modification are different events. A project wage determination is effective for 180 calendar days from its date [@CL-CMP-3013], and its expiry is handled by requesting a new determination or an extension [@CL-CMP-3017; @CL-CMP-3018; @CL-CMP-3019]. In sealed bidding, a modification to an existing determination is judged by when the agency receives it or it is published, measured against the date of bid opening [@CL-CMP-3020]. A notice from the Department of Labor that a solicitation carries the wrong determination is handled separately, on [Improper wage determinations before award, option-period wage determinations and Department of Labor construction regulations](cmp-improper-wage-determinations-and-option-wage-pricing.md). The ordinary request rules are on [Construction wage determinations: general and project types and how each is requested](cmp-construction-wage-determination-types-and-requests.md). Revised wage determinations on service contracts follow another rule, covered on [Service Contract Labor Standards: revised wage determinations and additional classes of service employees](cmp-scls-revised-wage-determinations-and-conformance.md).
