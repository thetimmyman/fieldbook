## Purpose

Shows how the annual representations and certifications provision works in a solicitation: when the SAM paragraph applies, what the offeror verifies by submitting an offer, and where the size representation sits.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3532] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-8 [@CL-CMP-3532; @CL-CMP-3534] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3532] |
| Applies to | Offerors on solicitations that include the annual representations provision, with the SAM paragraph applying as described below [@CL-CMP-3532; @CL-CMP-3533] |

If the System for Award Management provision is included in the solicitation, the SAM paragraph of the annual provision applies [@CL-CMP-3532]. If that provision is not included and the offeror has an active registration in SAM, the offeror may choose to use the SAM paragraph [@CL-CMP-3533].

By submission of its offer, the offeror verifies that the representations and certifications currently posted electronically that apply to the solicitation, as indicated in the provision's list, have been entered or updated within the last 12 months and are current, accurate and complete [@CL-CMP-3534]. The verification includes the business size standard applicable to the NAICS code referenced for the solicitation as of the date of the offer, and the representations are incorporated in the offer by reference, except for the changes the offeror identifies in the provision [@CL-CMP-3535].

## How it works

The basic provision states the NAICS code for the acquisition and the small business size standard as blanks to be filled in [@CL-CMP-3536]. A later paragraph lists the representations or certifications in SAM that are applicable to the solicitation as indicated in each listed item [@CL-CMP-3537]. One listed item is the covered telecommunications representation, which the list states applies to all solicitations [@CL-CMP-3582]; it is described on [Covered telecommunications: the offeror representations and the contractor reports](cmp-covered-telecommunications-representation-and-reporting.md). The basic provision also sets a separate size standard for certain nonmanufacturers, and this card does not set it out. The other listed items, the items the Contracting Officer selects and the alternate paragraph are in the source section and are not set out here.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror answers a solicitation that includes the registration provision. Its SAM representations were last updated 8 months before the offer. It reviews them and finds that one certification has changed since the update. It identifies that change in the offer and verifies the rest as current, accurate and complete.

## Never confuse

Where the registration provision is included, the SAM paragraph applies [@CL-CMP-3532], and where it is not included an offeror with an active registration in SAM may choose that paragraph [@CL-CMP-3533]. The annual provision does not set the registration duty itself; for what an offeror must have registered, see [SAM registration and maintenance clauses: keeping the record current](cmp-sam-registration-and-maintenance-clauses.md) and [SAM registration and the unique entity identifier in awards](../../market/feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md). For where the provision is inserted and how long representations stay effective, see [Annual representations and certifications in SAM](../trade-cyber/cmp-annual-representations-and-certifications-in-sam.md).
