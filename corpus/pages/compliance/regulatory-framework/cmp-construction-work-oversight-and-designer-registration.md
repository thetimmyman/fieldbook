## Purpose

Shows the oversight the Contracting Officer keeps over the work in cost-reimbursement construction contracts and in architect-engineer contracts, the registration requirement for designers, and the preconstruction conference clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-4924; @CL-CMP-4926] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.236-18 and FAR 52.236-24 through 52.236-26 [@CL-CMP-4924; @CL-CMP-4927; @CL-CMP-5121; @CL-CMP-5123] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.236-18 [@CL-CMP-0946]; RFO FAR 52.236-24 [@CL-CMP-0948]; RFO FAR 52.236-25 [@CL-CMP-0950; @CL-CMP-0994]; no RFO equivalent for FAR 52.236-26, which the RFO text prints as reserved [@CL-CMP-0995] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4924] |
| Applies to | Contracts where the prescriptions direct insertion, including cost-reimbursement construction and architect-engineer contracts [@CL-CMP-4925; @CL-CMP-4927; @CL-CMP-5121; @CL-CMP-5123] |

The work oversight clause for cost-reimbursement construction is inserted in solicitations and contracts when such contracts are contemplated, as prescribed in FAR 36.518 [@CL-CMP-4925]. The architect-engineer oversight clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 36.609-3 [@CL-CMP-4927]. The registration clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 36.609-4 [@CL-CMP-5121], and the preconstruction conference clause as prescribed in FAR 36.522 [@CL-CMP-5123].

In a cost-reimbursement construction contract, the extent and character of the work is subject to the general supervision, direction, control and approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4924]. In an architect-engineer contract, it is subject to the general oversight, supervision, direction, control and approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4926].

## How it works

Architects or engineers registered to practice in the particular professional field involved in a State, the District of Columbia or an outlying area of the United States must prepare or review and approve the design [@CL-CMP-4928]. The design covered is that of architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, civil or other engineering features of the work [@CL-CMP-5122].

Where the Contracting Officer decides to hold a preconstruction conference, the successful offeror is notified and is required to attend [@CL-CMP-4929]. The notification includes the date, time and location, any need for attendance by subcontractors, and information on the items to be discussed [@CL-CMP-4930].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. In a cost-reimbursement construction contract, RFO FAR 52.236-18 keeps the rule that the extent and character of the work is subject to the general supervision, direction, control and approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-0946; @CL-CMP-4924]. In an architect-engineer contract, RFO FAR 52.236-24 keeps the rule that the extent and character of the work is subject to the general oversight, supervision, direction, control and approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-0948; @CL-CMP-4926]. RFO FAR 52.236-25 keeps the rule that architects or engineers registered to practice in the particular professional field involved in a State, the District of Columbia or an outlying area of the United States must prepare or review and approve the design of architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, civil, or other engineering features of the work [@CL-CMP-0950; @CL-CMP-0994; @CL-CMP-4928; @CL-CMP-5122]. The RFO prescriptions cite different FAR sections: the work oversight clause points to FAR 36.101-7(j) [@CL-CMP-0945], the architect-engineer oversight clause to FAR 36.102-4(c) [@CL-CMP-0947] and the registration clause to FAR 36.102-4(d) [@CL-CMP-0949], where the codified text points to FAR 36.518, FAR 36.609-3 and FAR 36.609-4 [@CL-CMP-4925; @CL-CMP-4927; @CL-CMP-5121]. The RFO prescription for the work oversight clause does not repeat the codified condition that the clause goes in when cost-reimbursement construction contracts are contemplated [@CL-CMP-0945; @CL-CMP-4925]. The RFO text prints section 52.236-26 as reserved [@CL-CMP-0995].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement construction contract is underway and the contractor wants to substitute a lighter wall assembly over 40 percent of the perimeter. The extent and character of the work is subject to the Contracting Officer's supervision, direction, control and approval, so the contractor asks for direction and records the answer before installing anything. The substitution waits for that direction, and, because the contract includes the registration clause, a registered architect or engineer prepares or reviews and approves the changed structural design.

Separately, the Contracting Officer decides to hold a preconstruction conference. The notice gives the date, time and location, asks the framing subcontractor to attend, and lists the items to discuss, and the successful offeror attends. That is the codified Preconstruction Conference clause, and the RFO text prints section 52.236-26 as reserved [@CL-CMP-5123; @CL-CMP-0995].

## Never confuse

Registration is not the same as oversight, because the registered professional prepares or reviews and approves the design while the oversight clauses leave the extent and character of the work under Contracting Officer control [@CL-CMP-4928; @CL-CMP-4924]. The two oversight clauses also differ in wording, because the cost-reimbursement construction clause speaks of supervision, direction, control and approval, while the architect-engineer clause adds the word oversight [@CL-CMP-4924; @CL-CMP-4926]. The preconstruction conference is not the oversight clause, because it is a meeting the successful offeror must attend when the Contracting Officer decides to hold one [@CL-CMP-4929].

The codified FAR 52.236-26 is the Preconstruction Conference clause [@CL-CMP-5123], but the RFO text prints section 52.236-26 as reserved [@CL-CMP-0995].

The clauses on design within funding limitations and architect-engineer responsibility are covered in [Design within funding limitations and architect-engineer responsibility](cmp-design-within-funding-limitations-and-architect-engineer-responsibility.md), and the preconstruction conference and site visit provisions are covered in [Preconstruction conference, site visit and preparation of construction proposals](cmp-construction-preconstruction-conference-site-visit-and-proposals.md).
