## Purpose

Shows what the offeror identifies for ammunition and explosives work and when written approval is needed to change the place of performance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4513] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.223-7003 [@CL-CMP-4513] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | DEC 1991 [@CL-CMP-10125] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4513] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause; the clause text names the DFARS section that prescribes its use [@CL-CMP-4553] |

The Offeror must identify, in the Place of Performance provision of the solicitation, the place of performance of all ammunition and explosives work covered by the Safety Precautions for Ammunition and Explosives clause of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-4513]. Failure to furnish the place of performance information with the offer may result in rejection of the offer [@CL-CMP-4514].

After the date set for receipt of offers, the Offeror agrees not to change the place of performance of any portion of the offer covered by the Safety Precautions for Ammunition and Explosives clause without the written approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4515]. If a contract results from the offer, the Contractor agrees not to change any place of performance previously cited without the advance written approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4517].

## How it works

The Offeror identifies the place of performance of all covered ammunition and explosives work, and failure to furnish that information with the offer may result in rejection of the offer [@CL-CMP-4513; @CL-CMP-4514]. After the date set for receipt of offers, the Offeror agrees not to change the place of performance of any portion of the offer covered by the Safety Precautions for Ammunition and Explosives clause without the written approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4515]. The Contracting Officer grants that approval only if there is enough time for the Government to perform the necessary safety reviews on the new proposed place of performance [@CL-CMP-4516]. If a contract results, the Contractor agrees not to change any place of performance previously cited without the advance written approval of the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-4517].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror names one plant as the place of performance for its explosives work. Two weeks after offers are due (an assumed interval), it wants to move the work to a second plant. It must have the Contracting Officer's written approval first. If the Government has no time left to review the second plant, approval is not granted. If the offeror wins the contract, the same move would then need advance written approval.

## Never confuse

The place-of-performance identification is not the approval of a later change: the Offeror identifies the place of performance of all ammunition and explosives work covered by the Safety Precautions for Ammunition and Explosives clause [@CL-CMP-4513], while under the safety precautions clause, when a proposed subcontract represents a change in the place of performance, the Contractor must request approval in accordance with this clause [@CL-CMP-4551]. See [Safety precautions for ammunition and explosives (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-ammunition-explosives-safety-precautions.md).
