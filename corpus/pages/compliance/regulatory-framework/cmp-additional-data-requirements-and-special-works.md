## Purpose

Shows how long the Government may order additional data, who pays for it, and what rights the Government and the contractor have in data under the special works clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3705] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.227-16 and FAR 52.227-17 [@CL-CMP-3705; @CL-CMP-3708] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.227-16 [@CL-CMP-0299] and RFO FAR 52.227-17 [@CL-CMP-0300] |
| Edition date | FAR 52.227-16: Jun 1987 [@CL-CMP-0299]; FAR 52.227-17: Dec 2007 [@CL-CMP-0300] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3705] |
| Applies to | Not established. |

The Contracting Officer may, at any time during contract performance or within a period of 3 years after acceptance of all items to be delivered under the contract, order any data first produced or specifically used in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-3705]. Nothing in the Additional Data Requirements clause requires the Contractor to deliver data whose withholding is authorized by the Rights in Data—General or other equivalent clause, or data specifically identified in the contract as not subject to the clause [@CL-CMP-3706]. When data are to be delivered under that clause, the Contractor will be compensated for converting the data into the prescribed form, for reproduction, and for delivery [@CL-CMP-3707].

Under the Special Works clause, and except as provided in its copyright paragraph, the Government shall have unlimited rights in all data delivered under the contract and in all data first produced in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-3708]. The Contractor shall not assert or authorize others to assert any claim to copyright subsisting in any data first produced in the performance of the contract without the Contracting Officer's prior written permission [@CL-CMP-3709]. Except as otherwise specifically provided in the contract, the Contractor shall not use, release, reproduce, distribute, or publish that data, or authorize others to do so, without the Contracting Officer's written permission [@CL-CMP-3710].

## How it works

The two clauses do different jobs. Under the Additional Data Requirements clause the Contracting Officer may, at any time during contract performance or within a period of 3 years after acceptance of all items to be delivered, order any data first produced or specifically used in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-3705]; when data are to be delivered under the clause, the Contractor will be compensated for converting the data into the prescribed form, for reproduction, and for delivery [@CL-CMP-3707]. The Special Works clause makes the contractor get written permission both to claim copyright and to use or release the data it first produced [@CL-CMP-3709; @CL-CMP-3710]. If the Government desires to obtain copyright in data first produced in the performance of the contract and permission has not been granted under the copyright paragraph, the Contracting Officer shall direct the Contractor to assign (with or without registration), or obtain the assignment of, the copyright to the Government or its designated assignee [@CL-CMP-3752; @CL-CMP-7657]. This card does not set out the indemnity paragraph of the Special Works clause, its rules on copyright notices, licenses and data not first produced in the contract, or the Contracting Officer's power to release the Contractor from the Additional Data Requirements clause.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO Additional Data Requirements clause keeps the Contracting Officer's right to order, at any time during contract performance or within a period of 3 years after acceptance of all items to be delivered, any data first produced or specifically used in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-0315; @CL-CMP-3705]. It keeps the rule that nothing in the clause requires the Contractor to deliver data whose withholding is authorized by the Rights in Data-General or other equivalent clause, or data specifically identified in the contract as not subject to the clause [@CL-CMP-0316; @CL-CMP-3706], and the compensation for converting the data into the prescribed form, for reproduction, and for delivery [@CL-CMP-0317; @CL-CMP-3707]. The RFO Special Works clause keeps the Government's unlimited rights, except as provided in the copyright paragraph, in all data delivered under the contract and in all data first produced in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-0318; @CL-CMP-3708]. It keeps the bar on asserting or authorizing others to assert copyright in data first produced without the Contracting Officer's prior written permission [@CL-CMP-0319; @CL-CMP-3709]. It keeps the Contracting Officer's direction to assign (with or without registration), or obtain the assignment of, the copyright to the Government or its designated assignee, if the Government desires to obtain copyright in data first produced and permission has not been granted as set forth in paragraph (c)(1)(i) of the clause [@CL-CMP-0320; @CL-CMP-0321; @CL-CMP-3752; @CL-CMP-7657]. It keeps the bar, except as otherwise specifically provided in the contract, on using, releasing, reproducing, distributing or publishing data first produced, or authorizing others to do so, without the Contracting Officer's written permission [@CL-CMP-0322; @CL-CMP-3710].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract ends and all items are accepted. Two years later the Contracting Officer orders a set of drawings first produced in the work. The order is inside the window, so the contractor must deliver the drawings and is paid for putting them in the prescribed form, reproducing them and delivering them. The contractor may still withhold any data that the Rights in Data—General clause lets it withhold.

## Never confuse

An order for additional data is not a duty to deliver withheld data: nothing in the Additional Data Requirements clause requires the Contractor to deliver data whose withholding is authorized by the [Rights in Data—General clause](cmp-rights-in-data-general-definitions-and-allocation.md) or other equivalent clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3706]. The withholding rules are on [Rights in data, general: copyright, withholding and markings](cmp-rights-in-data-general-copyright-withholding-and-markings.md). Under the Special Works clause, except as provided in the copyright paragraph, the Government has unlimited rights in all delivered data and all first-produced data [@CL-CMP-3708].
