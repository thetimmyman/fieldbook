## Purpose

Sets out which contracts the safeguarding subpart covers, what else it requires and leaves in place, and the three risk definitions that the Supplier Performance Risk System subpart uses.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7960] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 204.7300 [@CL-CMP-7960; @CL-CMP-7961; @CL-CMP-7962; @CL-CMP-8441] and DFARS 204.7601 [@CL-CMP-7963; @CL-CMP-7964; @CL-CMP-7965; @CL-CMP-8442] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | Contracts and subcontracts requiring contractors and subcontractors to safeguard covered defense information [@CL-CMP-7960] |

The safeguarding subpart applies to contracts and subcontracts requiring contractors and subcontractors to safeguard covered defense information that resides in or transits through covered contractor information systems by applying specified network security requirements [@CL-CMP-7960]. It also requires reporting of cyber incidents [@CL-CMP-7961].

The subpart does not abrogate any other requirements regarding contractor physical, personnel, information, technical or general administrative security operations governing the protection of unclassified information [@CL-CMP-7962]. It also does not affect requirements of the National Industrial Security Program [@CL-CMP-8441].

The risk terms are defined as used in the subpart that holds DFARS 204.7601 [@CL-CMP-8442]. Item risk means the probability that a product, based on intended use, will introduce performance risk resulting in safety issues, mission degradation or monetary loss [@CL-CMP-7963]. Price risk means the measure of whether a proposed price for a product or service is consistent with historical prices paid for that item or service [@CL-CMP-7964]. Supplier risk means the probability that an award may subject the procurement to the risk of unsuccessful performance or to supply chain risk, and the definition refers to section 239.7301 for supply chain risk [@CL-CMP-7965].

## How it works

The subpart applies to contracts and subcontracts requiring contractors and subcontractors to safeguard covered defense information that resides in or transits through covered contractor information systems by applying specified network security requirements [@CL-CMP-7960].

The subpart also requires reporting of cyber incidents [@CL-CMP-7961].

Other security requirements stay in force: the subpart neither abrogates the other requirements for unclassified information nor affects National Industrial Security Program requirements [@CL-CMP-7962; @CL-CMP-8441].

Item risk concerns a product based on intended use [@CL-CMP-7963], price risk concerns whether a proposed price is consistent with historical prices paid [@CL-CMP-7964], and supplier risk concerns an award and the risk of unsuccessful performance or supply chain risk [@CL-CMP-7965].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subcontract requires the subcontractor to protect covered defense information that resides in a covered contractor information system by applying specified network security requirements [@CL-CMP-7960]. The subpart applies to that subcontract [@CL-CMP-7960], and the subpart also requires reporting of cyber incidents [@CL-CMP-7961]. The other requirements governing the protection of unclassified information, such as those for physical and personnel security, are not abrogated [@CL-CMP-7962].

An evaluator compares a proposed price of 120 with historical prices paid of about 100 for the same item [@CL-CMP-7964]. Price risk is the measure of whether the proposed price is consistent with those historical prices [@CL-CMP-7964].

## Never confuse

The safeguarding requirements attach to covered defense information that resides in or transits through covered contractor information systems, and other requirements for unclassified information stay in place [@CL-CMP-7960; @CL-CMP-7962].

The safeguarding subpart's scope is at DFARS 204.7300 [@CL-CMP-7960], and the risk terms are defined as used in the subpart that holds DFARS 204.7601 [@CL-CMP-8442].

The safeguarding requirements are covered in [Covered contractor information system safeguarding](cmp-covered-contractor-information-system-safeguarding.md).
