## Purpose

Explains what a class determination and findings authorizes, what its findings and period must look like, and where its authority stops.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8345] |
| Codified citation | FAR 1.703 [@CL-CMP-8345] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8345] |
| Applies to | A class of contract actions covered by one document [@CL-CMP-8345] |

A class determination and findings provides authority for a class of contract actions [@CL-CMP-8345]. A class may consist of contract actions for the same or related supplies or services, or other contract actions that require essentially identical justification [@CL-CMP-8346]. Unless otherwise prohibited, class documents may be executed for classes of contract action, although a determination and findings is ordinarily for an individual contract action [@CL-CMP-8341; @CL-CMP-8340].

## How it works

The findings in a class document shall fully support the proposed action, either for the class as a whole or for each action [@CL-CMP-8347]. A class document shall be for a specified period, with the expiration date stated in the document [@CL-CMP-8348]. The contracting officer shall ensure that individual actions taken under the authority of a class document are within its scope [@CL-CMP-8349]. The FAR requires expiration dates for class documents; when the authority ends, and how it continues for a solicitation already furnished, are on [Supersession, modification and expiration of determinations and findings](cmp-df-supersession-and-expiration.md) [@CL-CMP-8352].

A determination and findings is a special form of written approval by an authorized official that is required by statute or regulation as a prerequisite to taking certain contract actions [@CL-CMP-8337]. When one is required, it shall be signed by the appropriate official in accordance with agency regulations, and authority to sign or delegate signature authority is as shown in the applicable FAR part [@CL-OPM-0031]. The signature rules are on [Determinations and findings: content and signature](../../operating-model/decision-rights/opm-determinations-and-findings.md) [@CL-OPM-0031].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A class document covers recurring purchases of the same repair service for one year and states its expiration date, so it meets the period requirement [@CL-CMP-8346; @CL-CMP-8348]. A later purchase of 3 unrelated training courses is not in the described class [@CL-CMP-8346]. The contracting officer shall ensure that individual actions taken pursuant to the authority of a class document are within its scope, and this purchase is not within the described class [@CL-CMP-8349].

## Never confuse

A determination and findings shall ordinarily be for an individual contract action, and unless otherwise prohibited, class documents may be executed for classes of contract action [@CL-CMP-8340; @CL-CMP-8341]. A class deviation is a deviation, which FAR 1.401 defines as any one or combination of the forms it lists, that affects more than one contract action [@CL-CMP-8330; @CL-OPM-0024]. A class document is a determination and findings, a special form of written approval by an authorized official, that provides authority for a class of contract actions [@CL-CMP-8337; @CL-CMP-8345]. The deviation rules are on [Deviations from the FAR: who authorizes them and what cannot be deviated from](../../operating-model/decision-rights/opm-deviations-from-the-far.md).
