## Purpose

Shows when a revised prevailing wage determination must go into a contract action and how a class of service employees missing from the determination is added.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2384] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.1012-1, FAR 22.1007 and FAR 22.1019 [@CL-CMP-2384; @CL-CMP-2484; @CL-CMP-2387] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2384] |
| Applies to | Solicitations, contracts and contract modifications that use a prevailing wage determination, and contracts whose performance involves classes of service employees not in the determination [@CL-CMP-2384; @CL-CMP-2387] |

The need for inclusion of a revised prevailing wage determination in a solicitation, contract or contract modification is determined by the date of receipt of the revised prevailing wage determination by the contracting agency [@CL-CMP-2384]. When contracting by sealed bidding, a revised determination is not effective if the agency receives it less than 10 days before the opening of bids and the contracting officer finds that there is not reasonable time to incorporate the revision in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-2385]. For contractual actions other than sealed bidding, a revised determination received after award of a new contract or a specified modification is not effective, provided that the start of performance is within 30 days of the award or the specified modification [@CL-CMP-2386]. The printed section also covers a start of performance later than that window, and this card does not draft it.

Revisions that are published on the Wage Determinations at SAM.gov website or otherwise communicated to the contracting officer within the timeframes for sealed bidding and for other contract actions are effective and must be included in the resulting contract [@CL-CMP-2483]. The contracting officer shall obtain wage determinations for each new service solicitation and contract in excess of $2,500 [@CL-CMP-2484]. A contract modification that brings the contract above $2,500 and extends the existing contract pursuant to an option clause or otherwise, or changes the scope of the contract whereby labor requirements are affected significantly, is also on that list of actions for which wage determinations are obtained [@CL-CMP-2484; @CL-CMP-2485]. The printed section on obtaining determinations also lists multiple year contracts at their anniversary dates, and this card does not draft that part.

If the contracting officer is aware that contract performance involves classes of service employees not included in the wage determination, the contracting officer shall require the contractor to classify the unlisted classes so as to provide a reasonable relationship [@CL-CMP-2387]. The contractor shall initiate the conforming procedure before unlisted classes of employees perform contract work and shall submit Standard Form 1444, Request For Authorization of Additional Classification and Rate [@CL-CMP-2388]. Within 30 days of receipt of the request, the Wage and Hour Division will approve, modify, or disapprove it when the parties are in agreement, or render a final determination when they disagree [@CL-CMP-2389]. If the Wage and Hour Division will require more than 30 days to take action, it will notify the contracting officer within 30 days of receipt of the request that additional time is necessary [@CL-CMP-2486]. Conformance may not be used to artificially split or subdivide classifications listed in the wage determination [@CL-CMP-2487]. The printed section also gives other limits on what may be conformed and sets the contracting officer's review and referral steps, and this card does not draft them.

## How it works

Whether a revised determination reaches a contract action is judged by when the contracting agency receives it and by the stated sealed bidding and other-action exceptions [@CL-CMP-2384; @CL-CMP-2385; @CL-CMP-2386; @CL-CMP-2483]. A class that the determination does not list is added by the conforming procedure. The contractor classifies the unlisted class with a reasonable relationship to the listed ones, submits the form before the class does contract work, and the Wage and Hour Division then acts on the request or says that it needs more time [@CL-CMP-2387; @CL-CMP-2388; @CL-CMP-2389; @CL-CMP-2486].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A sealed bid opens on day 40. A revised determination reaches the contracting agency on day 35, and the contracting officer finds there is not reasonable time to put it in the solicitation. The answer: it was received less than 10 days before bid opening, so it is not effective for that solicitation. If it had arrived on day 20, the stated exception would not apply.

## Never confuse

A revised determination changes the wage determination itself, while a conformed class adds a class the determination lacks [@CL-CMP-2384; @CL-CMP-2387]. They run on separate clocks: receipt by the agency for the first [@CL-CMP-2384], and the Wage and Hour Division's review of the request for the second [@CL-CMP-2389; @CL-CMP-2486]. The clause and the duty to obtain determinations are on [Service contract labor standards clause and wage determinations](../../operating-model/talent/opm-service-contract-labor-standards.md) and are not restated here. How a determination is first requested and how a successor's pay is tied to an incumbent's agreement are on [obtaining wage determinations and successor pay](cmp-scls-wage-determinations-and-successorship.md).
