## Purpose

Explains the recovered material content certification an offeror gives when it signs its offer, the estimate of recovered material content a contractor submits when the contract ends, and the officer certification that Alternate I adds.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5176] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.223-4 and FAR 52.223-9 [@CL-CMP-5176; @CL-CMP-5177] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5176] |
| Applies to | Offerors whose offers carry the recovered material certification provision, and contractors whose contracts carry the estimate clause [@CL-CMP-5176; @CL-CMP-5177] |

By signing its offer the offeror certifies that the percentage of recovered materials content for EPA-designated items to be delivered or used will be at least the amount the applicable contract specifications or other contractual requirements require [@CL-CMP-5176].

On completion of the contract the contractor estimates the percentage of total recovered material content for the EPA-designated items delivered or used, including, if applicable, the percentage of post-consumer material content [@CL-CMP-5177]. It submits that estimate to the official named in a blank that the contracting officer completes in accordance with agency procedures, so the recipient is specific to the contract [@CL-CMP-5178].

## How it works

Recovered material means waste materials and by-products recovered or diverted from solid waste, but it excludes materials and by-products generated from, and commonly reused within, an original manufacturing process [@CL-CMP-5179]. Postconsumer material is a material or finished product that has served its intended use and has been discarded for disposal or recovery, having completed its life as a consumer item [@CL-CMP-5283]. It is a part of the broader category of recovered material [@CL-CMP-5284].

The certification at offer concerns the percentage that the items to be delivered or used will have, while the estimate at completion concerns the percentage in the items delivered or used [@CL-CMP-5176; @CL-CMP-5177].

Alternate I redesignates paragraph (b) of the basic clause as paragraph (c) and adds a new paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-5285]. In the new paragraph the contractor must execute the certification that the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act requires [@CL-CMP-5286]. An officer or employee responsible for the performance of the contract certifies that the percentage of recovered material content for EPA-designated items met the applicable contract specifications or other contractual requirements [@CL-CMP-5180]. The form provides for the signature, typed name and title of the officer or employee, the name of the company, firm or organization, and the date [@CL-CMP-5287].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract for EPA-designated items requires at least 30 percent recovered material content. By signing its offer the offeror certifies that the delivered items will have at least that percentage. At completion the contractor estimates that the delivered items had 35 percent total recovered material content, of which 10 percentage points were postconsumer material, and it submits that estimate to the official named in the blank the contracting officer filled in. The 10 points sit inside the 35 and are not added to it. If the contract includes Alternate I, an officer responsible for performance also signs the certification that the percentage met the contract specifications, and 35 percent is above the required 30 percent.

## Never confuse

A postconsumer percentage is reported inside the recovered material total, not added to it, because postconsumer material is a part of the broader category of recovered material [@CL-CMP-5284; @CL-CMP-5177].

The estimate at completion does not replace the certification at offer, and the Alternate I certification is a further statement given by an officer or employee [@CL-CMP-5176; @CL-CMP-5177; @CL-CMP-5180].

Recovered material content is not biobased content: the recovered material provisions concern EPA-designated items [@CL-CMP-5176], while the biobased certification concerns products in categories that the United States Department of Agriculture lists [@CL-CMP-5164].

## Company calibration

Whether item records can produce the recovered and postconsumer percentages for each EPA-designated item at contract completion, who completes the submission blank under agency procedures, and whether the contract uses Alternate I. [@Q-004]
