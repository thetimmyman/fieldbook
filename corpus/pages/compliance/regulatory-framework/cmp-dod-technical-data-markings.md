## Purpose

Shows how the technical data clause for items that are not commercial controls markings: marking as the way to assert a restriction, the authorized legends, what happens to unmarked and nonconforming data, and how unjustified markings are removed.

## The rule

| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3809] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7013 [@CL-CMP-3809] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3809] |
| Applies to | Technical data to be delivered under a contract with the clause, when the Contractor or a subcontractor or supplier asserts a restriction on it [@CL-CMP-3809] |

The Contractor, and its subcontractors or suppliers, may only assert restrictions on the Government's rights to use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data to be delivered under the contract by marking the deliverable data subject to restriction [@CL-CMP-3809].

### Authorized legends

Except as provided in paragraph (g)(6) of the clause, only the following legends are authorized under the contract: the government purpose rights legend at paragraph (g)(3) of the clause, the limited rights legend at paragraph (g)(4) of the clause [@CL-CMP-3810], the special license rights legend at paragraph (g)(5) of the clause, and a notice of copyright as prescribed under section 401 or 402 of title 17 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-3811].

Data delivered or otherwise furnished to the Government with government purpose rights shall be marked with a legend headed Government Purpose Rights that has fields for the contract number, contractor name, contractor address and expiration date [@CL-CMP-3832]. Data delivered or otherwise furnished to the Government with limited rights shall be marked with a legend headed Limited Rights that has fields for the contract number, contractor name and contractor address [@CL-CMP-3833].

### How to mark

The Contractor, or its subcontractors or suppliers, shall conspicuously and legibly mark the appropriate legend on all technical data that qualify for such markings [@CL-CMP-3812]. The authorized legends shall be placed on the transmittal document or storage container and, for printed material, each page of the printed material containing technical data for which restrictions are asserted [@CL-CMP-3813].

### Omitted markings

Technical data delivered or otherwise provided under the contract without restrictive markings will be presumed to have been delivered with unlimited rights [@CL-CMP-3814]. The Contractor may request permission to have conforming and justified restrictive markings placed on unmarked technical data at its expense [@CL-CMP-3816]. The request must be received by the Contracting Officer within 6 months following the furnishing or delivery of such technical data, or any extension of that time approved by the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-3817]. The Contractor shall identify the technical data that should have been marked, demonstrate that the omission of the marking was inadvertent and that the proposed marking is justified and conforms with the requirements for the marking of technical data contained in the clause [@CL-CMP-0442], and acknowledge, in writing, that the Government has no liability with respect to any disclosure, reproduction, or use of the technical data made prior to the addition of the marking or resulting from the omission of the marking [@CL-CMP-0443]. To the extent practicable, if the Contractor has requested permission (see paragraph (g)(2)(ii) of the clause) to correct an inadvertent omission of markings, the Contracting Officer will not release or disclose the technical data pending evaluation of the request [@CL-CMP-3815].

### Nonconforming markings

A nonconforming marking is a marking placed on technical data delivered or otherwise furnished to the Government under the contract that is not in the format authorized by the contract [@CL-CMP-3820]. Correction of nonconforming markings is not subject to the DFARS 252.227-7037, Validation of Asserted Restrictions on Technical Data, clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3821]. If the Contracting Officer notifies the Contractor of a nonconforming marking and the Contractor fails to remove or correct such marking within 60 days, the Government may ignore or, at the Contractor's expense, remove or correct any nonconforming marking [@CL-CMP-3822].

### Unjustified markings

The Government may ignore or, at the Contractor's expense, correct or strike a marking if, in accordance with the procedures in the Validation of Asserted Restrictions on Technical Data clause of the contract, a restrictive marking is determined to be unjustified [@CL-CMP-3819]. That procedure is covered on the card for [validation of asserted restrictions on technical data](cmp-dod-technical-data-restriction-validation.md).

### Contractor procedures and records

The Contractor and its subcontractors or suppliers that will deliver technical data with other than unlimited rights shall have, maintain, and follow written procedures sufficient to assure that restrictive markings are used only when authorized by the terms of the clause [@CL-CMP-3818], and maintain records sufficient to justify the validity of any restrictive markings on technical data delivered under the contract [@CL-CMP-0445].

## How it works

The Contractor, and its subcontractors or suppliers, may only assert restrictions on the Government's rights to use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data to be delivered under the contract by marking the deliverable data subject to restriction with one of the authorized legends [@CL-CMP-3809; @CL-CMP-3810; @CL-CMP-3811]. Technical data delivered or otherwise provided under the contract without restrictive markings will be presumed to have been delivered with unlimited rights, and the Contractor may request permission to have conforming and justified restrictive markings placed on unmarked technical data at its expense within 6 months following the furnishing or delivery of the data, or any extension approved by the Contracting Officer, by identifying the data, making the required demonstration, and acknowledging in writing that the Government has no liability for prior or omission-related disclosure, reproduction, or use [@CL-CMP-3814; @CL-CMP-3816; @CL-CMP-3817; @CL-CMP-0442; @CL-CMP-0443]. A marking that is not in the format authorized by the contract is a nonconforming marking; correction of nonconforming markings is not subject to the DFARS 252.227-7037 validation clause, and if the Contracting Officer notifies the Contractor of a nonconforming marking and the Contractor fails to remove or correct it within 60 days, the Government may ignore or, at the Contractor's expense, remove or correct any nonconforming marking [@CL-CMP-3820; @CL-CMP-3821; @CL-CMP-3822]. The Government may ignore or, at the Contractor's expense, correct or strike a marking if, in accordance with the procedures in the Validation of Asserted Restrictions on Technical Data clause, a restrictive marking is determined to be unjustified [@CL-CMP-3819]. Except as provided in paragraphs (c)(1)(ii) and (c)(1)(iv) through (c)(1)(ix) of the clause, the Government shall have limited rights in technical data pertaining to items, components, or processes developed exclusively at private expense and marked with the limited rights legend prescribed in paragraph (g) of the clause, or created exclusively at private expense in the performance of a contract that does not require the development, manufacture, construction, or production of items, components, or processes [@CL-CMP-3772; @CL-CMP-0476].

### Data the Contractor has marked

Under paragraph (c)(3)(iv)(C), the Contractor (or the party asserting restrictions as identified in the limited rights legend) may require each covered Government support contractor to enter into a nondisclosure agreement directly with the Contractor (or the party asserting restrictions) regarding the covered Government support contractor's use of such data, or alternatively, the Contractor (or party asserting restrictions) may waive in writing the requirement for a nondisclosure agreement [@CL-CMP-3774; @CL-CMP-0444]. Limited rights means the rights to use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data, in whole or in part, within the Government [@CL-CMP-3764]. The Contractor has the exclusive right, including the right to license others, to use technical data in which the Government has obtained government purpose rights under the contract for any commercial purpose during the time period specified in the government purpose rights legend [@CL-CMP-3773].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 10 drawings. It marks 9 with the limited rights legend, in the authorized format, and leaves one unmarked by mistake. The unmarked drawing is presumed to be delivered with unlimited rights. The contractor asks the Contracting Officer in month 2 for permission to add a conforming and justified marking, inside the 6-month window, and the Contracting Officer does not release the drawing while the request is evaluated, where practicable. On a later delivery the contractor writes a restriction note in its own words instead of the authorized legend. That is a nonconforming marking. The Contracting Officer notifies the contractor, and if the contractor has not fixed it within 60 days, the Government may ignore the note or correct it at the contractor's expense, without using the validation procedure.

## Never confuse

A nonconforming marking is a marking placed on technical data delivered or otherwise furnished to the Government under the contract that is not in the format authorized by the contract, and correction of nonconforming markings is not subject to the DFARS 252.227-7037, Validation of Asserted Restrictions on Technical Data, clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3820; @CL-CMP-3821]. Except as provided in paragraphs (c)(1)(ii) and (c)(1)(iv) through (c)(1)(ix) of the clause, the Government shall have limited rights in technical data pertaining to items, components, or processes developed exclusively at private expense and marked with the limited rights legend prescribed in paragraph (g) of the clause, or created exclusively at private expense in the performance of a contract that does not require the development, manufacture, construction, or production of items, components, or processes [@CL-CMP-3772; @CL-CMP-0476].

For the general FAR clause, see [Rights in data, general: copyright, withholding and markings](cmp-rights-in-data-general-copyright-withholding-and-markings.md). For the rights categories the legends refer to, see [DoD rights in technical data: definitions and rights categories](cmp-dod-technical-data-rights-categories.md). For the list of assertions made with an offer, see [DoD identification and assertion of restrictions](cmp-dod-assertion-of-data-restrictions.md). For commercial products, see [DoD technical data for commercial products and services](cmp-dod-commercial-technical-data-license.md), where under DFARS 252.227-7015(e) the Contractor agrees that the Government, and other persons to whom the Government may have released or disclosed technical data delivered or otherwise furnished under the contract, shall have no liability for any release or disclosure of technical data that are not marked to indicate that such data are licensed data subject to use, modification, reproduction, release, performance, display, or disclosure restrictions [@CL-CMP-0441; @CL-CMP-3830].
