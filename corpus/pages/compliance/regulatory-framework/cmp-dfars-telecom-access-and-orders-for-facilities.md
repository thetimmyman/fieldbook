## Purpose

Shows when the Government must let a telecommunications contractor into its own furnished facilities, and how the contractor acknowledges, charges for and is paid for ordered facilities and services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4641; @CL-CMP-4645] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.239-7002 and DFARS 252.239-7004 [@CL-CMP-4641; @CL-CMP-4645] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4641; @CL-CMP-4645] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the Access clause or the Orders for Facilities and Services clause [@CL-CMP-4641; @CL-CMP-4645] |

Subject to military security regulations, the Government shall permit the Contractor access at all reasonable times to Contractor furnished facilities [@CL-CMP-4641]. If the Government is unable to permit access, it shall at its own risk and expense maintain these facilities [@CL-CMP-4642], and the Contractor shall not be responsible for the service involving any of them during the period of nonaccess unless the service failure results from the Contractor's fault or negligence [@CL-CMP-4643].

During periods when the Government does not permit access, the Government will reimburse the Contractor at mutually acceptable rates for the loss of or damage to the equipment due to the fault or negligence of the Government [@CL-CMP-4644]. Failure to agree on those rates shall be a dispute concerning a question of fact within the meaning of the Disputes clause [@CL-CMP-4708].

## How it works

The Contractor acknowledges a communication service authorization or other type order for supplies and facilities either by commencing performance after receipt of an order or by written acceptance by a duly authorized representative [@CL-CMP-4645]. The Contractor shall furnish the services and facilities in accordance with all applicable tariffs, rates, charges, regulations, requirements, terms, and conditions of the kinds of service that the paragraph goes on to list [@CL-CMP-4698]. The Government will not prepay for services [@CL-CMP-4646]. Recurring charges start with the satisfactory beginning of service or provision of facilities or equipment and are payable monthly in arrears [@CL-CMP-4647]. For nontariffed services, the Contractor shall charge the Government at the lowest rate and under the most favorable terms and conditions for similar service and facilities offered to any other customer [@CL-CMP-4648].

Three other order rules apply. The Government shall pay expediting charges only when they are provided for in the tariff established by a governmental regulatory body or are authorized in a communication service authorization or other contractual document [@CL-CMP-4699]. When services normally provided are technically unacceptable and special equipment must be developed, fabricated or manufactured, the Government may provide the equipment or direct the Contractor to acquire the equipment or facilities [@CL-CMP-4700]. If the Government defers or changes its orders for any of the services but does not cancel or terminate them, the amount paid or payable for the services deferred or modified shall be equitably adjusted [@CL-CMP-4701].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A carrier receives a written order for a leased circuit and starts work the same week. Starting work after receipt of the order is one way to acknowledge it. Service begins satisfactorily on the 20th of the month, so the monthly charge of 1,000 starts on that day. The carrier bills it at the end of each month, not in advance. Separately, the carrier asks to add 300 of overtime to get the circuit in earlier than normal. The Government pays that only if the tariff or the authorization provides for expediting charges.

## Never confuse

The Access clause deals with the Contractor's entry to Contractor furnished facilities and who bears the risk while entry is refused [@CL-CMP-4641; @CL-CMP-4642]. It is not the clause that sets how orders are acknowledged, charged or paid, which is the Orders for Facilities and Services clause [@CL-CMP-4645; @CL-CMP-4647]. What the Government owes when it cancels or terminates an order is a separate clause: see [Cancellation or termination of orders](cmp-dfars-telecom-cancellation-or-termination-of-orders.md). Special construction charges, title and the term of the basic agreement are on [Special construction, title and term](cmp-dfars-telecom-special-construction-title-and-term.md). Neither is the Government-wide ban on buying covered telecommunications equipment, which is on [Covered telecommunications equipment or services](../trade-cyber/cmp-covered-telecommunications-definitions-and-prohibition.md). The Disputes clause that the Access clause names for a failure to agree is explained on [Disputes clause, claims and certification](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-disputes-clause.md) [@CL-CMP-4708].
