## Purpose

Tells a reader which FAR part defines terms, which part carries provisions and clauses, and what each scope statement does and does not settle, so that the governing text is the one used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8830] |
| Codified citation | FAR 2.000, FAR 52.000 and FAR 52.100 [@CL-CMP-8830; @CL-CMP-8833; @CL-CMP-8836] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.000 [@CL-CMP-6888; @CL-CMP-6889; @CL-CMP-6890]; RFO FAR 52.100 [@CL-CMP-6891; @CL-CMP-6892] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8830] |
| Applies to | Readers and drafters who work with FAR definitions and with provisions and clauses in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-8830; @CL-CMP-8833] |

FAR part 2 defines the words and terms that are frequently used in the FAR [@CL-CMP-8830]. It also provides cross-references to other definitions in the FAR of the same word or term, and provides for incorporating these definitions in solicitations and contracts by reference [@CL-CMP-8831]. Other parts, subparts and sections of 48 CFR chapter 1 may define other words or terms, and those definitions apply only to the part, subpart or section where the word or term is defined [@CL-CMP-8832].

FAR part 52 gives instructions for using provisions and clauses in solicitations and/or contracts [@CL-CMP-8833], sets forth the solicitation provisions and contract clauses prescribed by the regulation [@CL-CMP-8834], and presents a matrix listing the FAR provisions and clauses applicable to each principal contract type and/or purpose [@CL-CMP-8835]. Subpart 52.1 gives instructions for using part 52, including the explanation and use of provision and clause numbers, prescriptions, prefaces and the matrix [@CL-CMP-8836]. It prescribes procedures for incorporating, identifying and modifying provisions and clauses in solicitations and contracts, and for using alternates, and it describes the derivation of FAR provisions and clauses [@CL-CMP-8837].

These are statements of scope. They set no threshold, deadline or approval route, name no decision-maker, and place no duty on a contractor. The duties sit in the sections that the linked cards cover.

## How it works

Part 2 is where to look for the meaning of a frequently used word or term [@CL-CMP-8830]. Before applying a part 2 meaning to a word used in another part, check whether that part, subpart or section defines the same word for itself, because such a definition applies where it is given [@CL-CMP-8831; @CL-CMP-8832].

Part 52 is where to look for how a provision or clause is used [@CL-CMP-8833]. The instructions are in subpart 52.1 [@CL-CMP-8836; @CL-CMP-8837], and the texts of all FAR provisions and clauses are in subpart 52.2, each in its own separate subsection, arranged by subject matter and keyed to the parts of the FAR [@CL-CMP-8838].

The matrix lists the provisions and clauses applicable to each principal contract type and purpose [@CL-CMP-8835]. For the required-when-applicable and optional entries the matrix does not provide sufficient information to determine applicability, so contracting officers shall refer to the FAR text cited in the matrix that prescribes the use [@CL-CMP-8846]. The card on [numbering, prescriptions and the matrix](cmp-far-part-52-numbering-prescriptions-and-matrix.md) covers this.

The procedures that subpart 52.1 prescribes [@CL-CMP-8837] each have a card: [incorporating by reference](cmp-incorporating-provisions-and-clauses-by-reference.md), [identifying](cmp-identifying-provisions-and-clauses.md), [modifying and completing](cmp-modifying-and-completing-provisions-and-clauses.md), [using alternates](cmp-using-alternates-to-provisions-and-clauses.md) and [the provisions and clauses the subpart prescribes](cmp-provisions-and-clauses-prescribed-in-subpart-52-1.md). How the FAR is arranged and cited is on the card for [publication, arrangement and numbering](cmp-far-publication-arrangement-and-numbering.md), and agency supplements are on the card for [the FAR System and agency supplements](cmp-far-system-and-agency-supplements.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.000 keeps the statements that part 52 gives instructions for using provisions and clauses in solicitations and/or contracts [@CL-CMP-6888; @CL-CMP-8833], sets forth the solicitation provisions and contract clauses prescribed by the regulation [@CL-CMP-6889; @CL-CMP-8834], and presents a matrix listing the FAR provisions and clauses applicable to each principal contract type and/or purpose [@CL-CMP-6890; @CL-CMP-8835]. RFO FAR 52.100 keeps the statement that subpart 52.1 gives instructions for using part 52, including the explanation and use of provision and clause numbers, prescriptions, prefaces and the matrix [@CL-CMP-6891; @CL-CMP-8836], and keeps the statements that it prescribes procedures for incorporating, identifying and modifying provisions and clauses in solicitations and contracts, and for using alternates, and describes the derivation of FAR provisions and clauses [@CL-CMP-6892; @CL-CMP-8837].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A drafter reading a solicitation meets a word in a clause that comes from one FAR part and wants its meaning. The drafter first checks whether part 2 defines it [@CL-CMP-8830]. The drafter then checks the part, subpart or section that prescribes the clause, because another definition of the same word may sit there and applies only to that part, subpart or section [@CL-CMP-8831; @CL-CMP-8832].

The drafter next needs the clause text and the rules for putting it in the solicitation. The text is in subpart 52.2 [@CL-CMP-8838], and the rules for incorporating, identifying and modifying it are in subpart 52.1 [@CL-CMP-8837]. To see which clauses a fixed-price supply buy would normally carry, the drafter opens the matrix column for that contract type [@CL-CMP-8835]. A clause marked required-when-applicable or optional is then checked against the FAR text cited in the matrix, which prescribes its use [@CL-CMP-8846].

## Never confuse

Part 2 does not hold every definition in the FAR [@CL-CMP-8832]. Other parts, subparts and sections may define other words or terms, and those definitions apply only where they are given [@CL-CMP-8832].

The conditions for using a provision or clause sit in its prescription, not in the part 52 scope statements [@CL-CMP-8887]. Each provision or clause is prescribed where its subject matter receives its primary treatment [@CL-CMP-8844], and for the required-when-applicable and optional entries the matrix does not provide sufficient information to determine applicability [@CL-CMP-8846].
