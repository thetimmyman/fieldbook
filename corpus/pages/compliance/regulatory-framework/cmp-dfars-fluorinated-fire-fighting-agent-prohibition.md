## Purpose

Shows what the clause bars the contractor from providing, the definition it uses, and the flowdown.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4539] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.223-7009 [@CL-CMP-4539] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4539] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause; the clause text names the DFARS section that prescribes its use [@CL-CMP-4564] |

The Contractor must not provide or use under the contract any fire-fighting agent that contains perfluoroalkyl substances or polyfluoroalkyl substances in excess of one part per billion [@CL-CMP-4539].

## How it works

The clause gives perfluoroalkyl substances and polyfluoroalkyl substances the meanings given in section 322(f) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2020 [@CL-CMP-4538]. The Contractor must include the substance of the clause, including its subcontracts paragraph, in all subcontracts, including subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services, relating to fire-fighting on a military installation [@CL-CMP-4540].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor supplies foam concentrate for a base fire station. The lab report shows the agent contains the listed substances at 2 parts per billion (assumed). That is above the clause's one-part-per-billion limit, so the contractor may not provide or use it under the contract and must switch to an agent within the limit. A subcontract for fire-fighting on the base carries the substance of the clause, including for a commercial subcontractor.

## Never confuse

The bar reaches providing or using the agent under the contract, not only delivering it [@CL-CMP-4539]. The meanings of the two substance groups come from the statute the clause cites, not from text printed in the clause [@CL-CMP-4538]. The hexavalent chromium bar covers deliverables and construction material, not fire-fighting agents; see [Prohibition of hexavalent chromium (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-hexavalent-chromium-prohibition.md). For how commercial subcontracts take clause terms in general, see [Subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services (DFARS)](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-dfars-commercial-subcontract-flowdown.md).
