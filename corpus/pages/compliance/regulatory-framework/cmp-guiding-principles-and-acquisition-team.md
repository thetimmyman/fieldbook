## Purpose

Sets out the introduction and vision in the discussion of the Guiding Principles of the Federal Acquisition System, and the purpose of defining the acquisition team, which is the frame a finance team sits inside when it applies acquisition rules.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8380] |
| Codified citation | FAR 1.102-1 and 1.102-4 [@CL-CMP-8380; @CL-CMP-8384] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8380] |
| Applies to | All participants in the System, from the customer to the contractor [@CL-CMP-8382; @CL-CMP-8384] |

The Guiding Principles statement is a concise statement designed to be user-friendly for all participants in Government acquisition [@CL-CMP-8380]. The discussion that follows it is provided in order to illuminate the meaning of the terms and phrases used [@CL-CMP-8750]. The framework for the System includes the Guiding Principles and the supporting policies and procedures in the FAR [@CL-CMP-8381].

All participants in the System are responsible for making acquisition decisions that deliver the best value product or service to the customer [@CL-CMP-8382]. Best value must be viewed from a broad perspective and is achieved by balancing the many competing interests in the System [@CL-CMP-8383]. The vision describes the result as a system which works better and costs less [@CL-CMP-8751].

## How it works

The best-value duty falls on every participant in the System, not on one office. These sections name no single decision maker and set no approval route, threshold or deadline, so who may decide a particular matter comes from other FAR provisions; see [Who may bind the Government](cmp-contracting-authority-and-the-acquisition-team.md).

The purpose of defining the Federal Acquisition Team in the Guiding Principles is to ensure that participants in the System are identified, beginning with the customer and ending with the contractor of the product or service [@CL-CMP-8384]. Identifying the members this way encourages teamwork, unity of purpose and open communication among them in sharing the vision and achieving the goal of the System [@CL-CMP-8385]. Individual team members will participate in the acquisition process at the appropriate time [@CL-CMP-8386]. The section does not say who sets that time.

The text states no contractor-specific duty, price effect or compliance step. It places the contractor at the end of the team [@CL-CMP-8384], and the contractor is one of the participants who share the best-value responsibility [@CL-CMP-8382].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A buy review lists three competing interests: a lower unit price, a shorter lead time and a lower support burden. The analyst scores only price and recommends award. The answer: every participant is responsible for decisions that deliver the best value to the customer [@CL-CMP-8382], and best value is achieved by balancing the many competing interests from a broad perspective [@CL-CMP-8383], so the recommendation is redone on all three interests.

## Never confuse

The Guiding Principles statement is not the whole framework. The framework also includes the supporting policies and procedures in the FAR [@CL-CMP-8381]. The acquisition team is not one office or function: it is defined to identify participants from the customer to the contractor [@CL-CMP-8384]. For the performance standards and early industry communication, see [Guiding principles and communication with industry](cmp-guiding-principles-and-industry-communication.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company's reviews treat every participant, from the customer through the contractor, as inside the finance remit. [@Q-003]
