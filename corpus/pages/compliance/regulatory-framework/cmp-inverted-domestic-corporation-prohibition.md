## Purpose

States the representation an offeror must make about inverted domestic corporations, the provision and clause the contracting officer includes, and the duties to share supply chain risk information [@CL-CMP-7593; @CL-CMP-7596; @CL-CMP-7598].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7593] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.108-3, 9.108-5 and 4.2302 [@CL-CMP-7593; @CL-CMP-7596; @CL-CMP-7598] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7593] |
| Applies to | Offerors seeking contract award, solicitations and contracts for products or services including construction, and executive agencies that determine a substantial supply chain risk [@CL-CMP-7593; @CL-CMP-7596; @CL-CMP-7597; @CL-CMP-7598] |

To be eligible for contract award, an offeror must represent that it is neither an inverted domestic corporation nor a subsidiary of an inverted domestic corporation [@CL-CMP-7593]. An offeror that cannot make that representation is ineligible for award of a contract unless the prohibition is waived in accordance with the waiver procedures [@CL-CMP-7594]. The contracting officer may rely on an offeror's representation that it is not an inverted domestic corporation unless the contracting officer has reason to question the representation [@CL-CMP-7595]. The waiver procedures are at FAR 9.108-4 [@CL-CMP-7594].

The contracting officer shall include the provision at FAR 52.209-2 in each solicitation for the acquisition of products or services, including construction [@CL-CMP-7878; @CL-CMP-7596]. The contracting officer shall also include the clause at FAR 52.209-10 in each solicitation and contract for the acquisition of products or services, including construction [@CL-CMP-7878; @CL-CMP-7597].

Executive agencies are required to share relevant supply chain risk information with the FASC if the executive agency has determined that there is a reasonable basis to conclude that a substantial supply chain risk associated with a source or covered article exists [@CL-CMP-7598]. In support of the information sharing described in paragraph (a) of the section [@CL-CMP-7879], the contracting officer shall work with the program office or requiring activity, in accordance with agency procedures, regarding the sharing of relevant information on actual or potential supply chain risk determined to exist during the procurement process [@CL-CMP-7599].

## How it works

Eligibility turns on a representation the offeror makes, and the contracting officer may rely on it unless the officer has reason to question it [@CL-CMP-7593; @CL-CMP-7595]. An offeror that cannot represent that it is neither an inverted domestic corporation nor a subsidiary of one is ineligible unless the prohibition is waived [@CL-CMP-7594]. The prescription requires the provision in each solicitation and the clause in each solicitation and contract[@CL-CMP-7596; @CL-CMP-7597]. The provision and the clause named there are covered on the linked card.

Executive agencies are required to share relevant supply chain risk information with the FASC if the executive agency has made the determination the rule describes [@CL-CMP-7598]. In support of that sharing, the contracting officer shall work with the program office or requiring activity under agency procedures regarding the sharing of relevant information on actual or potential supply chain risk determined to exist during the procurement process [@CL-CMP-7879; @CL-CMP-7599].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency issues a solicitation for construction services. The contracting officer includes the representation provision in the solicitation and the clause in the solicitation and the resulting contract [@CL-CMP-7596; @CL-CMP-7597]. One offeror represents that it is neither an inverted domestic corporation nor a subsidiary of one. The officer has no reason to question the answer, may rely on it, and the offeror is eligible on that point [@CL-CMP-7593; @CL-CMP-7595]. A second offeror cannot make that representation, so it is ineligible for award unless the prohibition is waived in accordance with the waiver procedures [@CL-CMP-7594].

During the procurement the agency determines that there is a reasonable basis to conclude that a substantial supply chain risk exists for a source, so it must share the relevant information with the FASC, and the contracting officer works with the program office on the information found [@CL-CMP-7598; @CL-CMP-7599].

## Never confuse

An offeror that cannot make the representation is ineligible for award of a contract, unless waived in accordance with the procedures at FAR 9.108-4 [@CL-CMP-7594]. The contracting officer may rely on the offeror's representation unless the contracting officer has reason to question it [@CL-CMP-7595]. Executive agencies share supply chain risk information with the FASC under FAR 4.2302 [@CL-CMP-7598; @CL-CMP-7599]. The representation provision and the tax matters certification are covered on [Inverted domestic corporation representation and tax matters certification](cmp-inverted-domestic-corporation-and-tax-matters.md).
