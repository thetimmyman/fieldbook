## Purpose

Shows what changes when the offeror is a pool rather than a single firm, and which approvals carry small business preferences.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7209] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.702 and 9.701 [@CL-CMP-7210; @CL-CMP-7310; @CL-CMP-7382] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7209] |
| Applies to | Offers submitted by a pool, or by a group representing that it is a pool [@CL-CMP-7210; @CL-CMP-7211] |

A pool is, among other requirements, a group of concerns that have associated together to obtain and perform defense production or research and development contracts, jointly or in conjunction with each other [@CL-CMP-7310]. The concerns must also have entered into an agreement governing their organization, relationship and procedures and obtained approval of that agreement by one of two authorities [@CL-CMP-7382]. The approval may be given either by the Small Business Administration under section 9 or 11 of the Small Business Act [@CL-CMP-7383] or by a designated official under Part V of Executive Order 10480, August 14, 1953 (18 FR 4939, August 20, 1953) and section 708 of the Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. App. 2158) [@CL-CMP-7383; @CL-CMP-7048]. Except as the subpart specifies, a pool is treated the same as any other prospective or actual contractor [@CL-CMP-7209].

The contracting officer may not award a contract to a pool unless the offer leading to the contract is submitted by the pool in its own name or by an individual pool member expressly stating that the offer is on behalf of the pool [@CL-CMP-7210].

On receipt of an offer submitted by a group representing that it is a pool, the contracting officer must verify its approved status with the SBA District Office Director or other approving agency and document in the contract file that the verification was made [@CL-CMP-7211]. Pools approved by the SBA under the Small Business Act are entitled to the preferences and privileges accorded to small business concerns, and approval under the Defense Production Act does not confer those preferences and privileges [@CL-CMP-7212].

## How it works

Before awarding a contract to an unincorporated pool, the contracting officer must require each pool member participating in the contract to furnish a certified copy of a power of attorney identifying the agent authorized to sign the offer or contract on that member's behalf [@CL-CMP-7213]. The contracting officer must then attach a copy of each power of attorney to each signed copy of the contract retained by the Government [@CL-CMP-7309].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Two firms form an unincorporated pool, and one member submits the offer on an order worth 500,000, expressly stating that the offer is on behalf of the pool. Because a pool member submitted the offer with that express statement, the offer meets the condition for an award to a pool [@CL-CMP-7210]. On receipt, the contracting officer verifies the pool's approved status with the SBA District Office Director and documents in the contract file that the verification was made [@CL-CMP-7211]. The SBA approved the pool under the Small Business Act, so the pool is entitled to the preferences and privileges accorded to small business concerns [@CL-CMP-7212].

Before award, each member participating in the contract furnishes a certified copy of a power of attorney naming the agent authorized to sign for that member [@CL-CMP-7213]. After the contract is signed, the contracting officer attaches a copy of each power of attorney to each signed copy that the Government retains [@CL-CMP-7309]. If the approval had instead been given under the Defense Production Act, the pool would not receive those preferences [@CL-CMP-7212].

## Never confuse

Approval of a pool by the SBA under the Small Business Act is not the same as approval under the Defense Production Act. The first entitles the pool to small business preferences and privileges, while the second does not confer them. The contracting officer verifies approved status on receipt of an offer from any group that represents itself as a pool [@CL-CMP-7211; @CL-CMP-7212].

## Company calibration

Whether the company participates in any pool, on what terms its members sign for the pool, and how approved status would be evidenced [@Q-003].
