## Purpose

Shows how the price of a multiple year or option year service contract is adjusted when a Department of Labor wage determination changes the pay that the contract requires.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-4804] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-43 [@CL-CMP-4804] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-43 [@CL-CMP-5159; @CL-CMP-5160; @CL-CMP-9808; @CL-CMP-9809; @CL-CMP-5161; @CL-CMP-5162; @CL-CMP-5163; @CL-CMP-5308; @CL-CMP-5309; @CL-CMP-5310; @CL-CMP-5311; @CL-CMP-5312; @CL-CMP-5313; @CL-CMP-5314; @CL-CMP-5315] |
| Edition date | AUG 2018 [@CL-CMP-10270] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4804] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to area prevailing wage determinations and contracts subject to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-4804] |

The clause applies to contracts subject to area prevailing wage determinations and to contracts subject to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-4804]. The wage determination issued by the Wage and Hour Division of the Department of Labor that is current on the anniversary date of a multiple year contract or the beginning of each renewal option period applies to the contract [@CL-CMP-4955]. If no wage determination has been made applicable to the contract, the clause turns to the Federal minimum wage established by the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938, as amended [@CL-CMP-4956], and the rate current on that date applies [@CL-CMP-4957].

The contract price, contract unit price labor rates, or fixed hourly labor rates are adjusted to reflect the contractor's actual increase or decrease in applicable wages and fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-4954], to the extent that the increase is made to comply with, or the decrease is voluntarily made by the contractor as a result of, a covered cause [@CL-CMP-4806]. There are three covered causes. The first is the Department of Labor wage determination applicable on the anniversary date of the multiple year contract or at the beginning of the renewal option period [@CL-CMP-4807]. The second is an increased or decreased wage determination otherwise applied to the contract by operation of law [@CL-CMP-4808]. The third is an amendment to the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 that is enacted after award, affects the minimum wage and becomes applicable to the contract under law [@CL-CMP-4960].

The contractor warrants that the prices include no allowance for any contingency to cover increased costs for which the clause provides an adjustment [@CL-CMP-4805]. An adjustment is limited to the wage and fringe benefit changes described above and to the accompanying changes in social security and unemployment taxes and workers' compensation insurance [@CL-CMP-4809]. It does not otherwise include any amount for general and administrative costs, overhead or profit [@CL-CMP-4810].

## How it works

The clause's own example shows the measure of an increase. The prior wage determination required 4.00 an hour and the contractor chose to pay 4.10 [@CL-CMP-4958]. The new determination raises the minimum rate to 4.50, so the allowable adjustment is 0.40 an hour even if the contractor voluntarily pays 4.75 [@CL-CMP-4958; @CL-CMP-4959].

The contractor must notify the contracting officer of any increase claimed within 30 days after receiving a new wage determination, unless the contracting officer extends that period in writing [@CL-CMP-4811]. The contractor must promptly notify the contracting officer of any decrease, and nothing in the clause stops the government from asserting a claim within the period the law permits [@CL-CMP-4812]. The notice states the amount claimed and, for a time-and-materials or labor-hour contract, the change in fixed hourly rates, with any relevant supporting data, including payroll records, that the contracting officer may reasonably require [@CL-CMP-4813]. Upon agreement of the parties, the contract price, contract unit price labor rates, or fixed hourly rates shall be modified in writing [@CL-CMP-4961]. The contractor must continue performance pending agreement on or determination of the adjustment and its effective date [@CL-CMP-4962].

The contracting officer or an authorized representative may examine the contractor's directly pertinent books, documents, papers and records until 3 years after final payment under the contract [@CL-CMP-4814].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of the clause keeps its coverage of contracts subject to area prevailing wage determinations and contracts subject to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-5159; @CL-CMP-4804], and the rule that the wage determination current on the anniversary date or at the beginning of each renewal option period applies [@CL-CMP-5160; @CL-CMP-4955], and the rule that if no such determination has been made applicable, the Federal minimum wage as established by section 6(a)(1) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938, as amended, current on that date applies [@CL-CMP-9808; @CL-CMP-9809; @CL-CMP-4956; @CL-CMP-4957]. It keeps the adjustment to reflect the contractor's actual increase or decrease in applicable wages and fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-5161; @CL-CMP-4954], to the extent the increase is made to comply with, or the decrease is voluntarily made as a result of, a covered cause [@CL-CMP-5162; @CL-CMP-4806]. It keeps the three covered causes [@CL-CMP-5163; @CL-CMP-4807] [@CL-CMP-5310; @CL-CMP-4808] [@CL-CMP-5311; @CL-CMP-4960] and the clause's own example [@CL-CMP-5308; @CL-CMP-5309; @CL-CMP-4958; @CL-CMP-4959]. It keeps the limit of the adjustment to those wage and fringe benefit changes and the accompanying taxes and insurance [@CL-CMP-5312; @CL-CMP-4809], and the bar on any other amount for general and administrative costs, overhead or profit [@CL-CMP-5313; @CL-CMP-4810]. It keeps the 30-day notice of any increase after receiving a new wage determination, unless extended in writing [@CL-CMP-5314; @CL-CMP-4811], and the 3-year examination right [@CL-CMP-5315; @CL-CMP-4814].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

The prior wage determination required 10.00 an hour, and the company chose to pay a worker 10.50. A new determination raises the required rate to 11.50, and the company raises the worker to 12.00. The allowable adjustment is 1.00 an hour, the step from 10.50 to 11.50, and not the 1.50 the company chose to pay. Social security and unemployment taxes and workers' compensation insurance that rise with the wage add 0.10 an hour, so the claim is 1.10 an hour. A charge of 0.05 an hour for overhead and any amount for profit are not allowed. The company sends its notice within 30 days after receiving the new determination, with payroll records.

## Never confuse

This is not the economic price adjustment for labor and material on [economic price adjustment for labor and material](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-economic-price-adjustment-labor-and-material.md). It follows the three covered causes: the adjustment applies to the extent that the increase is made to comply with, or the decrease is voluntarily made as a result of, one of them [@CL-CMP-4806]. The companion price adjustment clause names two causes, wage determinations applied by operation of law and later amendments to the statute [@CL-CMP-4817; @CL-CMP-4818]. It is on [price adjustment](cmp-scls-price-adjustment-annual.md).
