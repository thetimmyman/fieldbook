## Purpose

Shows what the personal identity verification subpart responds to, and when its contract clause must and must not be inserted [@CL-CMP-8302; @CL-CMP-8305; @CL-CMP-8307].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8302] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1300 and 4.1303 [@CL-CMP-8302; @CL-CMP-8305] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8302] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers prescribing the clause for work that requires routine access to a Federally-controlled facility or information system [@CL-CMP-8305; @CL-CMP-8306] |

The subpart provides policy and procedures associated with Personal Identity Verification as required by two documents [@CL-CMP-8302]. One is Federal Information Processing Standards Publication Number 201, Personal Identity Verification of Federal Employees and Contractors [@CL-CMP-8303]. The other is Office of Management and Budget Guidance M-05-24, dated August 5, 2005, on implementing Homeland Security Presidential Directive 12 [@CL-CMP-8304].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.204-9, Personal Identity Verification of Contractor Personnel, in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-8305]. The duty applies when contract performance requires contractors to have routine physical access to a Federally-controlled facility, routine access to a Federally-controlled information system, or both [@CL-CMP-8306]. The clause shall not be used when contractors require only intermittent access to Federally-controlled facilities [@CL-CMP-8307].

## How it works

The duty to insert the clause falls on the contracting officer, not on the contractor, and the trigger is the routine access the work requires [@CL-CMP-8305; @CL-CMP-8306]. The text joins routine physical access to a facility and routine access to an information system with "and/or", so either one, or both, meets the trigger [@CL-CMP-8306]. The prohibition is narrower than the trigger: it is stated for contractors who require only intermittent access to Federally-controlled facilities [@CL-CMP-8307]. The section names no threshold, deadline or approval route, and it states no other exception [@CL-CMP-8305; @CL-CMP-8307]. The contractor's own obligations under the clause are in [Personal identity verification of contractor personnel](cmp-personal-identity-verification.md); see also [Security requirements for classified access and personal identity verification](cmp-security-requirements-and-personal-identity-verification.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

One contract requires 20 staff to work daily inside a Federally-controlled facility, which is routine physical access, so the contracting officer inserts the clause in the solicitation and the contract [@CL-CMP-8305; @CL-CMP-8306]. A second contract needs only 2 visits a year to a Federally-controlled facility and no information system access, which is only intermittent access to facilities, so the clause shall not be used [@CL-CMP-8307]. A third contract requires routine access to a Federally-controlled information system and no facility access, so the trigger is met by the system access alone [@CL-CMP-8306].

## Never confuse

Routine access is not intermittent access: the clause is inserted when performance requires routine access of the kinds described, and it shall not be used when contractors require only intermittent access to Federally-controlled facilities [@CL-CMP-8306; @CL-CMP-8307].
