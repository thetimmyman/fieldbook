## Purpose

Shows the acts the clause says are prohibited, the procedures and written report the contractor owes, and the Government's rights to offset and withhold.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2608] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-7 [@CL-CMP-2608] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.203-7 [@CL-CMP-0377] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2608] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts exceeding $200,000, other than those for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-2682] |

The clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.502-3 [@CL-CMP-2608]. The contracting officer inserts it in solicitations and contracts exceeding $200,000, other than those for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-2682]. It says the Kickbacks statute prohibits any person from providing, attempting to provide, or offering to provide any kickback, and from soliciting, accepting, or attempting to accept any kickback [@CL-CMP-2609]. A third listed prohibited act is including, directly or indirectly, the amount of any kickback in the contract price charged by a prime contractor to the United States, or in the price charged by a subcontractor to a prime contractor or higher tier subcontractor [@CL-CMP-2610].

The Contractor shall have in place and follow reasonable procedures designed to prevent and detect possible violations of those prohibitions in its own operations and direct business relationships [@CL-CMP-2611]. When the Contractor has reasonable grounds to believe a listed violation may have occurred, it shall promptly report the possible violation in writing [@CL-CMP-2612]. The report goes to the inspector general of the contracting agency, the head of the agency if it has no inspector general, or the Attorney General [@CL-CMP-2613]. The Contracting Officer may offset the amount of the kickback against monies owed by the United States under the prime contract, direct the Prime Contractor to withhold it from sums owed a subcontractor, or do both [@CL-CMP-2614]. The Contractor shall cooperate fully with any Federal agency investigating a possible violation of the listed prohibitions [@CL-CMP-2684]. The Contractor shall incorporate the substance of the clause, except its paragraph (c)(1), in all subcontracts under the contract that exceed the threshold specified in FAR 3.502-2(i) on the date of subcontract award [@CL-CMP-2683].

## How it works

The clause splits the duties. The contractor builds and follows procedures for its own operations and direct business relationships, and reports possible violations in writing [@CL-CMP-2611; @CL-CMP-2612]. The Contracting Officer may offset the amount of the kickback against monies owed under the prime contract and/or direct that the Prime Contractor withhold the amount of any kickback from sums owed a subcontractor [@CL-CMP-2614]. The Contractor must also cooperate fully with any Federal agency investigating a possible violation [@CL-CMP-2684], and the substance of the clause flows down to subcontracts above a threshold, without the procedures paragraph [@CL-CMP-2683]. The printed clause also has rules on paying withheld money over to the Government and on notice to the Contracting Officer when money is withheld, and this card does not set them out. The definition of a kickback, and when the clause is inserted, are on [Anti-Kickback Act and the Anti-Kickback Procedures clause](cmp-anti-kickback-act.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.502-3, the same section as the codified clause [@CL-CMP-0377; @CL-CMP-2608]. It keeps the prohibition on any person providing, attempting to provide or offering to provide any kickback and on soliciting, accepting or attempting to accept any kickback [@CL-CMP-0378; @CL-CMP-2609]. It keeps the third prohibited act of including, directly or indirectly, the amount of any kickback in a contract price charged by a prime contractor to the United States or by a subcontractor to a prime contractor or higher tier subcontractor [@CL-CMP-0379; @CL-CMP-2610]. It keeps the duty to have in place and follow reasonable procedures designed to prevent and detect possible violations described in paragraph (b) in the Contractor's own operations and direct business relationships [@CL-CMP-0380; @CL-CMP-2611]. It keeps the duty to report a possible violation promptly in writing when the Contractor has reasonable grounds to believe a violation described in paragraph (b) may have occurred [@CL-CMP-0381; @CL-CMP-2612], to the inspector general of the contracting agency, the head of the contracting agency if the agency does not have an inspector general, or the Attorney General [@CL-CMP-0382; @CL-CMP-2613]. It keeps the duty to cooperate fully with any Federal agency investigating a possible violation described in paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-0383; @CL-CMP-2684]. It keeps the flowdown of the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c)(5) but excepting paragraph (c)(1), in all subcontracts under the contract that exceed the threshold specified in FAR 3.502-2(i) on the date of subcontract award [@CL-CMP-0385; @CL-CMP-2683]. In the offset and withholding paragraph, the RFO text says the Contracting Officer may direct that the Prime Contractor withhold from sums owed a subcontractor under the prime contract the amount of the kickback, where the codified clause says the amount of any kickback [@CL-CMP-0384; @CL-CMP-2614].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor holds a 5 million contract that is not for commercial products or commercial services. It pays a subcontractor 600,000 under a subcontract above the flowdown threshold. Of that price, 30,000 is a kickback to a purchasing employee. The Contracting Officer may offset 30,000 against what the Government owes under the prime contract, or direct the prime to withhold the same 30,000 from sums owed to the subcontractor. The prime must also report the possible violation in writing if it has reasonable grounds to believe one occurred.

## Never confuse

The prohibited acts reach any person, while the procedures duty is the contractor's own and covers its own operations and direct business relationships [@CL-CMP-2609; @CL-CMP-2611]. The Government's rights recover the kickback amount. The printed clause states no separate monetary penalty.

The codified withholding right is measured by the amount of any kickback, while the RFO text says the amount of the kickback [@CL-CMP-2614; @CL-CMP-0384].
