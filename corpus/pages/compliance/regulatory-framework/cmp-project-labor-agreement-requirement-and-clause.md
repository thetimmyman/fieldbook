## Purpose

Shows when a construction solicitation demands a project labor agreement, what the agreement must contain, when the copy is due under the basic provision and its alternates, and how the clause keeps the agreement current and flows down.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5246] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-33 and 52.222-34 [@CL-CMP-5246; @CL-CMP-5253] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-33 and 52.222-34 [@CL-CMP-1974; @CL-CMP-1986] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5246] |
| Applies to | Construction solicitations that carry the provision, and construction contracts that carry the clause [@CL-CMP-5246; @CL-CMP-5253] |

Under the basic provision, offerors must negotiate or become a party to a project labor agreement with one or more labor organizations for the term of the resulting construction contract, and must require their subcontractors to become a party to the resulting agreement [@CL-CMP-5246]. The agreement must bind the offeror and the subcontractors engaged in construction on the project to comply with it [@CL-CMP-5247], and must allow the offeror and all subcontractors to compete for contracts and subcontracts without regard to whether they are otherwise parties to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-5248]. It must contain guarantees against strikes, lockouts and similar job disruptions, and set out effective, prompt and mutually binding procedures for resolving labor disputes during its term [@CL-CMP-5249]. It must also provide other mechanisms for labor-management cooperation on matters of mutual interest and concern, including productivity, quality of work, safety and health, and must fully conform to all statutes, regulations, Executive orders and agency requirements [@CL-CMP-5383]. The agreement does not change the terms of the resulting contract and does not provide for any price adjustment by the Government [@CL-CMP-5250].

## How it works

Under the basic provision, the offeror submits a copy of the agreement to the Contracting Officer with its offer [@CL-CMP-5251].

Alternate I changes who negotiates and when the copy is due [@CL-CMP-5384; @CL-CMP-5385]. The apparent successful offeror must negotiate or become a party to the agreement and require its subcontractors to become a party [@CL-CMP-5384], and must submit a copy to the Contracting Officer before contract award [@CL-CMP-5385]. Alternate II substitutes a single paragraph (b) in place of paragraphs (b) through (e) of the basic provision [@CL-CMP-5386]. Under it, an offeror that is awarded the contract must negotiate or become a party to the agreement and require its subcontractors to become a party [@CL-CMP-5387].

The clause defines a large-scale construction project as a Federal construction project within the United States whose total estimated cost of the construction contract or contracts to the Federal Government is $35 million or more [@CL-CMP-5252]. A project labor agreement is a pre-hire collective bargaining agreement with one or more labor organizations that establishes the terms and conditions of employment for a specific construction project and is an agreement described in section 158(f) of title 29 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-5792]. Construction means construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation, modernization, alteration, conversion, extension, repair, or improvement of buildings, structures, highways, or other real property [@CL-CMP-5804]. A labor organization is a labor organization as defined in section 152(5) of title 29 of the United States Code of which building and construction employees are members [@CL-CMP-5805]. Under the basic clause, the contractor must maintain in a current status throughout the life of the contract the project labor agreement entered into before award [@CL-CMP-5253], and must include the substance of the clause, including its subcontract paragraph, in subcontracts with subcontractors engaged in construction on the project [@CL-CMP-5254].

Alternate I of the clause has the contractor negotiate or become a party to a project labor agreement for the term of the construction contract and submit an executed copy to the Contracting Officer as required in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-5388]. Under Alternate II of the clause, a contractor notified by the agency that an order will use a project labor agreement must negotiate or become a party to one for the term of the order [@CL-CMP-5389], and the Contracting Officer must require that an executed copy be submitted to the agency at one of three points: with the order offer, before award of the order, or after award of the order [@CL-CMP-5390].

Under Alternates I and II of the clause, the project labor agreement must bind the contractor and subcontractors engaged in construction on the construction project to comply with the project labor agreement [@CL-CMP-5793]. Under Alternate I, the agreement must allow the contractor and all subcontractors to compete for contracts and subcontracts without regard to whether they are otherwise parties to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-5806]. Under Alternate II, the agreement must allow all contractors and subcontractors to compete for contracts and subcontracts without regard to whether they are otherwise parties to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-5807]. Under Alternates I and II, the agreement must also contain guarantees against strikes, lockouts and similar job disruptions, and set forth effective, prompt and mutually binding procedures for resolving labor disputes arising during its term [@CL-CMP-5794]. Under Alternates I and II, the agreement must also provide other mechanisms for labor-management cooperation on matters of mutual interest and concern, including productivity, quality of work, safety and health, and must fully conform to all statutes, regulations, Executive orders and agency requirements [@CL-CMP-5795]. Under Alternates I and II, any project labor agreement reached under the clause does not change the terms of the contract or provide for any price adjustment by the Government [@CL-CMP-5796].

Under Alternate I, the contractor must maintain in a current status throughout the life of the contract the project labor agreement entered into pursuant to the clause [@CL-CMP-5797], and must require subcontractors engaged in construction on the construction project to agree to any project labor agreement negotiated by the prime contractor pursuant to the clause [@CL-CMP-5799]. Under Alternates I and II, the contractor must include the substance of paragraphs (d) through (f) of the clause in subcontracts with subcontractors engaged in construction on the construction project [@CL-CMP-5808]. Under Alternate II, the contractor must maintain in a current status throughout the life of the order any project labor agreement entered into pursuant to the clause [@CL-CMP-5798]. Under Alternate II, for each order that uses a project labor agreement, the contractor must require subcontractors engaged in construction on the construction project to agree to any project labor agreement negotiated by the prime contractor pursuant to the clause [@CL-CMP-5800].

Under Alternate III of the provision, if awarded the contract, the offeror may be required by the agency to negotiate or become a party to a project labor agreement with one or more labor organizations for the term of the order [@CL-CMP-5801]. Under Alternate III, the Contracting Officer will require that an executed copy of the project labor agreement be submitted to the agency with the order offer, prior to award of the order, or after award of the order [@CL-CMP-5802]. Under Alternate III, the offeror must require its subcontractors to become a party to the resulting project labor agreement for the term of the order [@CL-CMP-5803].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page (the RFO text) in place of the codified part 52 text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO provision at 52.222-33 keeps the requirement that offerors negotiate or become a party to a project labor agreement with one or more labor organizations for the term of the resulting construction contract and require their subcontractors to become a party [@CL-CMP-1974; @CL-CMP-5246]. It keeps the requirement that the agreement bind the offeror and the subcontractors engaged in construction on the project, and allow the offeror and all subcontractors to compete without regard to whether they are otherwise parties to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-1975; @CL-CMP-1976; @CL-CMP-5247; @CL-CMP-5248]. It keeps the required strike and lockout guarantees, the dispute procedures, the other cooperation mechanisms and the conformity requirement [@CL-CMP-1977; @CL-CMP-1978; @CL-CMP-5249; @CL-CMP-5383]. It keeps the rule that the agreement does not change the terms of the resulting contract or provide for any price adjustment by the Government, and the rule that the offeror submits a copy to the Contracting Officer with its offer [@CL-CMP-1979; @CL-CMP-5250; @CL-CMP-5251].

The RFO provision keeps the Alternate I duties, under which the apparent successful offeror negotiates or becomes a party to the agreement and submits a copy before contract award [@CL-CMP-1980; @CL-CMP-1981; @CL-CMP-5384; @CL-CMP-5385]. It keeps the Alternate II duty that an offeror awarded the contract negotiate or become a party to the agreement and require its subcontractors to become a party [@CL-CMP-1982; @CL-CMP-5387]. It keeps the Alternate III rules: the agency may require the awarded offeror to negotiate or become a party for the term of the order, the Contracting Officer requires an executed copy at one of three points, and the offeror requires its subcontractors to become a party [@CL-CMP-1983; @CL-CMP-1984; @CL-CMP-1985; @CL-CMP-5801; @CL-CMP-5802; @CL-CMP-5803].

The RFO clause at 52.222-34 keeps the four definitions, including the $35 million large-scale construction project threshold [@CL-CMP-1986; @CL-CMP-1987; @CL-CMP-1988; @CL-CMP-1989; @CL-CMP-5804; @CL-CMP-5805; @CL-CMP-5252; @CL-CMP-5792]. It keeps the duty to maintain the pre-award agreement in a current status throughout the life of the contract and the duty to include the substance of the clause in subcontracts with subcontractors engaged in construction on the project [@CL-CMP-1990; @CL-CMP-1991; @CL-CMP-5253; @CL-CMP-5254].

Of the clause alternates, the RFO text of Alternate I keeps the duty to negotiate or become a party and to submit an executed copy as required in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-1992; @CL-CMP-5388]. It keeps the rules that the agreement must bind the contractor and subcontractors engaged in construction on the project, allow the contractor and all subcontractors to compete, contain the strike and lockout guarantees and the dispute procedures, provide the other cooperation mechanisms, and conform to all statutes, regulations, Executive orders and agency requirements [@CL-CMP-9342; @CL-CMP-1993; @CL-CMP-9344; @CL-CMP-9345; @CL-CMP-5793; @CL-CMP-5806; @CL-CMP-5794; @CL-CMP-5795]. It keeps the rule that the agreement does not change the terms of the contract or provide for any price adjustment by the Government [@CL-CMP-9346; @CL-CMP-5796], and the duty to maintain the agreement throughout the life of the contract [@CL-CMP-1994; @CL-CMP-5797]. It keeps the duty to require subcontractors engaged in construction to agree to the agreement the prime contractor negotiates and to include the substance of paragraphs (d) through (f) in their subcontracts [@CL-CMP-1995; @CL-CMP-9347; @CL-CMP-5799; @CL-CMP-5808].

The RFO text of Alternate II keeps the duty of a contractor notified by the agency that an order will use an agreement to negotiate or become a party for the term of the order, and the three points at which the Contracting Officer requires an executed copy [@CL-CMP-1996; @CL-CMP-1997; @CL-CMP-5389; @CL-CMP-5390]. It keeps the rules that the agreement must bind the contractor and subcontractors engaged in construction on the project, allow all contractors and subcontractors to compete, contain the strike and lockout guarantees and the dispute procedures, provide the other cooperation mechanisms, and conform to all statutes, regulations, Executive orders and agency requirements [@CL-CMP-9343; @CL-CMP-1998; @CL-CMP-9344; @CL-CMP-9345; @CL-CMP-5793; @CL-CMP-5807; @CL-CMP-5794; @CL-CMP-5795]. It keeps the rule that the agreement does not change the terms of the contract or provide for any price adjustment by the Government [@CL-CMP-9346; @CL-CMP-5796], and the duty to maintain the agreement throughout the life of the order [@CL-CMP-1999; @CL-CMP-5798]. It keeps the duty, for each order that uses an agreement, to require subcontractors engaged in construction to agree to the agreement the prime contractor negotiates and to include the substance of paragraphs (d) through (f) in their subcontracts [@CL-CMP-9348; @CL-CMP-9349; @CL-CMP-5800; @CL-CMP-5808].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency estimates a Federal courthouse project in the United States at 40 million dollars, so the project meets the clause's definition of a large-scale construction project. The solicitation carries the basic provision. The offeror negotiates a project labor agreement with the labor organizations for the term of the contract, requires its construction subcontractors to become a party, and submits a copy with its offer. The agreement guarantees against strikes and lockouts and sets binding dispute procedures, and it also lets subcontractors compete whether or not they are parties to collective bargaining agreements. When the offeror asks the Government for a price adjustment to cover the agreement, the answer is no. Had the solicitation carried Alternate I, the copy would instead be due from the apparent successful offeror before award. Under Alternate I of the clause, the contractor then keeps the agreement in a current status throughout the life of the contract and requires its construction subcontractors to agree to the agreement it negotiated. The agreement is a pre-hire collective bargaining agreement for this specific construction project.

## Never confuse

Under the basic provision, a project labor agreement requirement is not a price term: the agreement does not change the resulting contract and provides no price adjustment [@CL-CMP-5250]. Nor, under the basic provision, does it close the field to firms outside collective bargaining: the agreement must let subcontractors compete whether or not they are parties to collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-5248].
