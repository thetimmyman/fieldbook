## Purpose

Shows what an offeror for audit services must meet and show to be found responsible, and which auditing standards a contractor must follow while performing audit services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4396] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.237-7000 and DFARS 252.237-7001 [@CL-CMP-4396; @CL-CMP-4397] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4396] |
| Applies to | Offerors responding to a solicitation that includes the provision, which is used as prescribed in DFARS 237.270(e)(1), and contractors under contracts that include the clause, which is used as prescribed in DFARS 237.270(e)(2), for audit services [@CL-CMP-4396; @CL-CMP-4397; @CL-CMP-4348] |

To be determined responsible, the offeror must meet the general standards of responsibility in the FAR and three listed criteria, qualifications, independence, and quality control, as described in the general standards chapter of Government Auditing Standards [@CL-CMP-4346]. The apparently successful offeror, before award, shall give the contracting officer evidence that it is licensed by the cognizant licensing authority in the state or other political jurisdiction where the offeror operates its professional practice [@CL-CMP-4347].

The contractor, in performance of all audit services under the contract, shall comply with Government Auditing Standards issued by the Comptroller General of the United States [@CL-CMP-4348].

## How it works

The provision adds audit-specific criteria on top of the general responsibility standards, which are covered on [Contractor responsibility standards and their application](../../mna/screening/mna-contractor-responsibility-standards.md) [@CL-CMP-4346]. The license evidence is owed by the apparently successful offeror before award [@CL-CMP-4347]. After award the clause makes Government Auditing Standards the standard for all audit services under the contract [@CL-CMP-4348]. The printed sections do not set out the content of those standards.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A firm is the apparently successful offeror for an audit services contract. Before award it gives the contracting officer proof of its state license. During performance it carries out two audits and applies the Government standards to both. The answer: the license evidence is due before award, and the standards duty covers all audit services under the contract, so both audits are covered.

## Never confuse

The provision is about responsibility before award, and the clause is about performance after award [@CL-CMP-4346; @CL-CMP-4348]. To be determined responsible, an audit services offeror must meet the general standards of responsibility and the three listed criteria [@CL-CMP-4346], and the apparently successful offeror must also give evidence of licensing before award [@CL-CMP-4347]. How the Government requests audits of a contractor is covered on [Contract audit services](../../estimating/pricing/est-contract-audit-services.md). The disclosure of disciplinary proceedings is a separate pair of texts, covered on [Preaward and postaward transparency for firms supporting Department of Defense audits](cmp-audit-support-transparency-requirements.md).

## Company calibration

Whether the company offers audit services to the Department of Defense, and who holds the license evidence and the standards compliance file. [@Q-004]
