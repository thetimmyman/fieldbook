## Purpose

Shows whom the Contractor must employ for contract work in a named place under this clause, and what it must pass down to subcontractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4416] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.222-7000 [@CL-CMP-4416] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | MAR 2000 [@CL-CMP-10168] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4416] |
| Applies to | Contracts with the Restrictions on Employment of Personnel clause, used as prescribed in section 222.7004 [@CL-CMP-4416] |

For the part of the contract work in the place named in the clause, the Contractor shall employ individuals who are residents of that place and who, for any craft or trade, have or could promptly acquire the skills needed to perform the contract [@CL-CMP-4417].

The Contractor shall insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (b), in each subcontract awarded under the contract [@CL-CMP-4418].

## How it works

The printed clause shows a blank where the place of the contract work is named, and the residency duty applies to the part of the work in that place [@CL-CMP-4417]. The skills test has two branches: the individual already has the skills for a craft or trade, or would be able to acquire them promptly [@CL-CMP-4417]. The Contractor must insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (b), in each subcontract awarded under the contract [@CL-CMP-4418].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract names a harbor town as the place for part of its work. That part needs 20 electricians. The Contractor hires 20 residents of the town. Some already hold electrician skills and the rest could pick them up promptly. It also writes the substance of the clause into each subcontract. Answer: the hiring meets paragraph (a) and the subcontract step meets paragraph (b). Assumed for the example: the contract names the town in the blank of paragraph (a).

## Never confuse

The residency requirement is not the Guam nonimmigrant bar: the Contractor shall employ, for the part of the contract work in the place named in the clause, individuals who are residents of that place and who, for any craft or trade, have or could promptly acquire the skills needed to perform the contract [@CL-CMP-4417]. For the Guam clause on workers holding a particular nonimmigrant status, see [Prohibition on use of nonimmigrant aliens on Guam](cmp-dfars-guam-nonimmigrant-alien-prohibition.md). For the rule on named individuals in roles on a contract, see [Felony-convicted persons prohibition](cmp-felony-convicted-persons-prohibition.md). For duties to follow local labor law and union agreements overseas, see [Compliance with local labor laws overseas](cmp-dfars-local-labor-laws-overseas.md).
