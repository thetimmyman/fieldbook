## Purpose

Covers two scope sections: the one for representations and certifications submitted and maintained in SAM, and the one for reporting executive compensation and first-tier subcontract awards [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8300].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8295] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1200 and 4.1400 [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8299] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8295] |
| Applies to | Contractors submitting representations and certifications in SAM, and contractors reporting subcontract award data and executive compensation [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8300] |

The representations and certifications subpart prescribes policies and procedures requiring submission and maintenance of representations and certifications via SAM [@CL-CMP-8295]. Its stated purposes are to eliminate the administrative burden on contractors of submitting the same information to various contracting offices [@CL-CMP-8296], to establish a common source for this information to procurement offices across the Government [@CL-CMP-8297], and to incorporate by reference the contractor's representations and certifications in the awarded contract [@CL-CMP-8298].

The reporting subpart implements section 2 of the Federal Funding Accountability and Transparency Act of 2006, as amended by section 6202 of the Government Funding Transparency Act of 2008 [@CL-CMP-8299]. That statute requires contractors to report subcontract award data and the total compensation of the five most highly compensated executives of the contractor and subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8300]. The public may view first-tier subcontract award data at the Government's spending data website [@CL-CMP-8301].

## How it works

Neither scope section names an approving official, a threshold, a deadline or an exception [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8300]. The representations section states that submission and maintenance run through SAM and lists three purposes [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8296; @CL-CMP-8297; @CL-CMP-8298]. One of the purposes is that the contractor's representations and certifications are incorporated by reference in the awarded contract [@CL-CMP-8298]. For how the provision's paragraphs work, see [Annual representations and certifications provision: how its paragraphs work](cmp-annual-representations-provision-paragraphs.md) and [Annual representations and certifications in SAM](../trade-cyber/cmp-annual-representations-and-certifications-in-sam.md).

The reporting section places the reporting duty on contractors and describes it as the statute's requirement [@CL-CMP-8300]. It covers subcontract award data and the compensation of the five most highly compensated executives of both the contractor and the subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8300]. It states no dollar threshold and no filing date. For the clause that carries the conditions, thresholds and exemptions, see [Reporting executive compensation and first-tier subcontract awards](cmp-executive-compensation-and-subcontract-award-reporting.md). The subcontract award data is published where the public can view it [@CL-CMP-8301].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor submits and maintains its representations and certifications in SAM, and one stated purpose of that arrangement is that the awarded contract incorporates them by reference [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8298]. The statute the reporting subpart implements requires contractors to report subcontract award data and the total compensation of the five most highly compensated executives of the contractor and subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8300]. The scope section does not settle whether a particular first-tier award or executive is reportable, or by when; the reporting clause carries those conditions. The public can view the first-tier subcontract award data [@CL-CMP-8301].

## Never confuse

Representations kept in SAM are not the same as subcontract and compensation reporting: the first is a common source of contractor representations and certifications, which the subpart aims to incorporate by reference in the award, while the second is subcontract award and executive compensation data that contractors report [@CL-CMP-8298; @CL-CMP-8300].
