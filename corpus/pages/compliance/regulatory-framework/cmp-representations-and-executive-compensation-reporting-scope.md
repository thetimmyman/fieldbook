## Purpose

Covers two scope sections: the one for representations and certifications submitted and maintained in SAM, and the one for reporting executive compensation and first-tier subcontract awards [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8300].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8295] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1200 and 4.1400 [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8299] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8295] |
| Applies to | Contractors submitting representations and certifications in SAM, and contractors reporting subcontract award data and executive compensation [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8300] |

The representations and certifications subpart prescribes policies and procedures requiring submission and maintenance of representations and certifications via SAM [@CL-CMP-8295]. Its stated purposes are to eliminate the administrative burden on contractors of submitting the same information to various contracting offices [@CL-CMP-8296], to establish a common source for this information to procurement offices across the Government [@CL-CMP-8297], and to incorporate by reference the contractor's representations and certifications in the awarded contract [@CL-CMP-8298].

The reporting subpart implements section 2 of the Federal Funding Accountability and Transparency Act of 2006, as amended by section 6202 of the Government Funding Transparency Act of 2008 [@CL-CMP-8299]. That statute requires contractors to report subcontract award data and the total compensation of the five most highly compensated executives of the contractor and subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8300]. The public may view first-tier subcontract award data at the Government's spending data website [@CL-CMP-8301].

## How it works

The representations and certifications scope section prescribes policies and procedures for requiring submission and maintenance of representations and certifications via SAM to eliminate the administrative burden for contractors of submitting the same information to various contracting offices, establish a common source for this information to procurement offices across the Government, and incorporate by reference the contractor's representations and certifications in the awarded contract [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8296; @CL-CMP-8297; @CL-CMP-8298]. For how the provision's paragraphs work, see [Annual representations and certifications provision: how its paragraphs work](cmp-annual-representations-provision-paragraphs.md) and [Annual representations and certifications in SAM](../trade-cyber/cmp-annual-representations-and-certifications-in-sam.md).

The reporting scope section implements section 2 of the Federal Funding Accountability and Transparency Act of 2006 (Public Law 109-282), as amended by section 6202 of the Government Funding Transparency Act of 2008 (Public Law 110-252), which requires contractors to report subcontract award data and the total compensation of the five most highly compensated executives of the contractor and subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8299; @CL-CMP-8300]. For the reporting clause, see [Reporting executive compensation and first-tier subcontract awards](cmp-executive-compensation-and-subcontract-award-reporting.md). The public may view first-tier subcontract award data at https://www.usaspending.gov [@CL-CMP-8301].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor submits and maintains its representations and certifications in SAM, and one stated purpose of that arrangement is that the awarded contract incorporates them by reference [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8298]. The statute the reporting subpart implements requires contractors to report subcontract award data and the total compensation of the five most highly compensated executives of the contractor and subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8300]. The public may view first-tier subcontract award data at https://www.usaspending.gov [@CL-CMP-8301].

## Never confuse

Representations and certifications submitted and maintained via SAM establish a common source of information for procurement offices across the Government and are incorporated by reference in the awarded contract [@CL-CMP-8295; @CL-CMP-8297; @CL-CMP-8298], whereas the reporting subpart implements the statutory requirement for contractors to report subcontract award data and the total compensation of the five most highly compensated executives of the contractor and subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8299; @CL-CMP-8300].
