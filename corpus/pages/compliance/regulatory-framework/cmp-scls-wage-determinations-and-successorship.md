## Purpose

Shows when the contracting officer must request a wage determination from the Department of Labor and what a successor contractor must pay when the incumbent's employees have a collective bargaining agreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2378] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.1008-1 and FAR 22.1008-2 [@CL-CMP-2378; @CL-CMP-2379] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2378] |
| Applies to | Service contract actions that need a wage determination, and successor contracts that meet the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-2378; @CL-CMP-2379; @CL-CMP-2380; @CL-CMP-2381] |

If the Wage Determinations at SAM.gov database does not contain the applicable prevailing wage determination for a contract action, the contracting officer must use the e98 process to request a wage determination from the Department of Labor [@CL-CMP-2378].

A successor contractor must pay wages and fringe benefits, including accrued wages and benefits and prospective increases, to service employees at least equal to those agreed upon by a predecessor contractor under the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-2379]. The listed conditions include that the services will be substantially the same as those furnished by an incumbent contractor whose contract the proposed contract will succeed, and that they will be performed in the same locality [@CL-CMP-2379; @CL-CMP-2380]. A further listed condition is that the incumbent prime contractor or subcontractor is furnishing the services through service employees whose wages and fringe benefits are the subject of one or more collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-2379; @CL-CMP-2381].

The successor wage rule will not apply if the incumbent contractor enters into a collective bargaining agreement for the first time and the agreement does not become effective until after the expiration of the incumbent's contract [@CL-CMP-2382]. It also will not apply if the Secretary of Labor determines, after a hearing, that the wages and fringe benefits in the predecessor contractor's collective bargaining agreement are substantially at variance with those which prevail for services of a similar character in the locality [@CL-CMP-2383]. It will not apply either if the Secretary of Labor determines that those wages and fringe benefits are not the result of arm's length negotiations [@CL-CMP-8204]. The printed section lists further limits, including one for a new or revised agreement made during the incumbent's performance, and this card does not draft them.

## How it works

The contracting officer first needs the applicable wage determination, from the database or by the e98 request [@CL-CMP-2378]. For a successor contract, the statute also ties the minimum pay to the wages and fringe benefits agreed by the predecessor, under the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-2379]. The rule is subject to the listed limits, such as a first agreement that takes effect only after the incumbent's contract ends [@CL-CMP-2382]. The printed section also gives steps for obtaining the incumbent's agreement and for listing covered and uncovered classes, and this card does not draft them.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An incumbent's collective bargaining agreement sets 20 per hour plus 5 in fringe benefits, with a raise to 21 agreed for next year. A successor will perform substantially the same services in the same locality. The answer: if the listed conditions are met and no limit applies, the successor must pay at least what the predecessor agreed, including the agreed increase, and a wage determination from the database does not replace that duty.

## Never confuse

A prevailing wage determination from the database or an e98 request [@CL-CMP-2378] is not the same as the successor wage rule, which turns on what the predecessor agreed under a collective bargaining agreement [@CL-CMP-2379; @CL-CMP-2381]. When the clause goes into a contract and when determinations must be obtained are on [Service contract labor standards clause and wage determinations](../../operating-model/talent/opm-service-contract-labor-standards.md) and are not restated here. Whether a later revised determination reaches a contract action is on [revised wage determinations and additional classes](cmp-scls-revised-wage-determinations-and-conformance.md). Solicitations and contracts subject to the successor wage provision are among the listed exceptions to the services exemption described on [administrative exemptions](cmp-scls-exemptions-and-variations.md) [@CL-CMP-2377; @CL-CMP-2379].
