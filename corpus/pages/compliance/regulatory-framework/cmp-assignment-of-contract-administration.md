## Purpose

Shows how a contracting officer delegates contract administration to a contract administration office, what the delegation contains, when it can be recalled or declined, and what the DoD supplement adds.

## The rule

|---|---|

| Codified citation | FAR 42.202, FAR 42.203 and DFARS 242.202 [@CL-CMP-2559; @CL-CMP-2566; @CL-CMP-2567] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2559; @CL-CMP-2567] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers delegating contract administration, and DoD activities retaining contracts for administration [@CL-CMP-2559; @CL-CMP-2567] |

As provided in agency procedures, contracting officers may delegate contract administration or specialized support services, either through interagency agreements or by direct request to the cognizant contract administration office listed in the Federal Directory of Contract Administration Services Components [@CL-CMP-2559]. The Defense Contract Management Agency maintains that directory [@CL-CMP-2566]. The delegation should include, among other items, the name and address of the designated office, which also shall be entered in the contract, and any special instructions, including any functions withheld or any specific authorization to perform listed functions [@CL-CMP-2560]. For individual contracts or groups of contracts, the contracting office may delegate functions not listed in the functions section, provided that listed conditions are met, one being prior coordination with the office to ensure that required resources are available [@CL-CMP-2561]. The contracting officer at the requesting agency may rescind or recall a delegation, except for functions pertaining to cost accounting standards and negotiation of forward pricing rates and indirect cost rates [@CL-CMP-2562]. The requesting agency must coordinate with the office to establish a reasonable transition period before it rescinds or recalls a delegation [@CL-CMP-2563].

An agency may decline a request for contract administration services on a case-by-case basis if its resources are inadequate to accomplish the tasks, and declinations shall be in writing [@CL-CMP-2565]. Supporting contract administration shall not be used for subcontracts unless the Government otherwise would incur undue cost, successful completion of the prime contract is threatened, or it is authorized under paragraph (f) of the section or elsewhere in the regulation [@CL-CMP-2564]. For DoD, activities shall not retain for administration any contract that requires performance of any contract administration function at or near contractor facilities, except listed categories of contract, which include contracts for the National Security Agency and research and development with universities [@CL-CMP-2567]. Contract administration functions for base, post, camp and station contracts on a military installation are normally the responsibility of the installation or tenant commander [@CL-CMP-2568]. However, the Defense Contract Management Agency shall, upon request of the military department and subject to prior agreement, perform contract administration services on a military installation [@CL-CMP-2673].

## How it works

As provided in agency procedures, the contracting officer delegates through an interagency agreement or a direct request to the listed office, and the delegation should name the designated office and any withheld functions [@CL-CMP-2559; @CL-CMP-2560]. The contracting officer at the requesting agency may rescind or recall a delegation, except for the cost accounting standards and rate functions [@CL-CMP-2562]. An agency asked to administer may decline on a case-by-case basis when its resources are inadequate [@CL-CMP-2565]. The printed sections do not list the functions that may be delegated; they point to the separate functions section, which is on [Contract administration functions](../audit-retention/cmp-contract-administration-functions.md). The DoD items on functions are on [DoD additions to contract administration functions](cmp-dod-contract-administration-additions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer sends a direct request to a listed administration office to administer a cost-reimbursement contract worth 5,000,000. The request names the office, withholds one function and says so in the special instructions. Later the officer wants to recall the function of negotiating indirect cost rates. The recall is not allowed, because that function is excepted from rescission. The officer may recall other functions after agreeing a reasonable transition period with the office.

## Never confuse

The assignment of administration, which says which office administers a contract, is not the list of functions that office performs. The functions are on [Contract administration functions](../audit-retention/cmp-contract-administration-functions.md), and the roles of the offices are on [Oversight roles](../audit-retention/cmp-oversight-roles.md).
