## Purpose

Shows the contractor's duty to justify asserted restrictions on technical data, how the Government challenges them, the time limits, and what follows when the restriction is sustained or not justified.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3793] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7037 [@CL-CMP-3793] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3793] |
| Applies to | Contractors and subcontractors at any tier that assert restrictions on technical data [@CL-CMP-3793] |

The Contractor or subcontractor at any tier is responsible for maintaining records sufficient to justify the validity of its asserted restrictions on the rights of the Government and others to use, duplicate, release, or disclose technical data delivered, required to be delivered, or otherwise provided to the Government under the contract or subcontract [@CL-CMP-3793; @CL-CMP-0446]. If the Contracting Officer determines that a challenge to the asserted restriction is warranted, the Contracting Officer will send a written challenge notice to the Contractor or subcontractor making the asserted restriction [@CL-CMP-3794]. Such challenge will state the specific grounds for challenging the asserted restriction including, for commercial products or commercial services, sufficient information to reasonably demonstrate that the commercial product or commercial service was not developed exclusively at private expense [@CL-CMP-3826], and require a response within 60 days justifying and providing sufficient evidence as to the current validity of the asserted restriction [@CL-CMP-3826; @CL-CMP-3795]. The Contractor's or subcontractor's written response shall be considered a claim within the meaning of section 7101 of title 41 of the United States Code, Contract Disputes, and shall be certified in the form prescribed at FAR 33.207, regardless of dollar amount [@CL-CMP-3801].

If the Contracting Officer determines that the Contractor or subcontractor has justified the validity of the asserted restriction, the Contracting Officer will issue a final decision to the Contractor or subcontractor that sustains the validity of the asserted restriction and that states that the Government will continue to be bound by the asserted restriction [@CL-CMP-3796; @CL-CMP-0447]. The Contracting Officer will issue that final decision within 60 days after receipt of the Contractor's or subcontractor's response to the challenge notice, or within such longer period that the Contracting Officer has notified the Contractor or subcontractor that the Government will require, and will provide notification of any longer period within 60 days after receipt of the response to the challenge notice [@CL-CMP-0447; @CL-CMP-0448]. If the Contracting Officer determines that the validity of the asserted restriction is not justified, the Contracting Officer will issue a final decision to the Contractor or subcontractor in accordance with the Disputes clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3797]; notwithstanding paragraph (e) of the Disputes clause, the final decision will be issued within 60 days after receipt of the Contractor's or subcontractor's response to the challenge notice, or within such longer period that the Contracting Officer has notified the Contractor or subcontractor that the Government will require, with notification of a longer period made within 60 days after receipt of the response to the challenge notice [@CL-CMP-0449; @CL-CMP-0450]. The Government agrees that it will continue to be bound by the asserted restriction for a period of 90 days from the issuance of the Contracting Officer's final decision under paragraph (g)(2)(i) of the clause [@CL-CMP-3798]. If the Contractor or subcontractor fails to appeal, file suit, or provide a notice of intent to file suit to the Contracting Officer within the 90-day period, the Government may cancel or ignore the restrictive markings that are based on the asserted restrictions, and the failure of the Contractor or subcontractor to take the required action constitutes agreement with such Government action [@CL-CMP-3799; @CL-CMP-0468]. Under paragraph (g)(2)(iii), the Government agrees that it will continue to be bound by the asserted restriction where a notice of intent to file suit in the United States Court of Federal Claims is provided to the Contracting Officer within 90 days from the issuance of the final decision under paragraph (g)(2)(i) of the clause, and the Government will no longer be bound, and the Contractor or subcontractor agrees that the Government may strike or ignore the restrictive marking based on the asserted restriction, if the Contractor or subcontractor fails to file its suit within 1 year after issuance of the final decision [@CL-CMP-0469; @CL-CMP-0470; @CL-CMP-0471]. Under paragraph (g)(2)(iv), the Government agrees that it will be bound by the asserted restrictions where an appeal or suit is filed pursuant to the Contract Disputes statute until final disposition by an agency Board of Contract Appeals or the United States Court of Federal Claims [@CL-CMP-0472]. During the period within 6 years of final payment on a contract or within 6 years of delivery of the technical data to the Government, whichever is later, the Contracting Officer may review and make a written determination to challenge the restriction [@CL-CMP-3800]. The Government may, however, challenge a restriction on the release, disclosure, or use of technical data at any time if such technical data are publicly available, have been furnished to the United States without restriction [@CL-CMP-3827], have been otherwise made available without restriction, or are the subject of a fraudulently asserted use or release restriction [@CL-CMP-3828].

## How it works

The Contractor or subcontractor at any tier is responsible for maintaining records sufficient to justify the validity of its asserted restrictions on technical data delivered, required to be delivered, or otherwise provided to the Government under the contract or subcontract [@CL-CMP-3793; @CL-CMP-0446]. If the Contracting Officer determines that a challenge to the asserted restriction is warranted, the Contracting Officer sends a written challenge notice that states the specific grounds and requires a response within 60 days justifying and providing sufficient evidence as to the current validity of the asserted restriction [@CL-CMP-3794; @CL-CMP-3826; @CL-CMP-3795]. Whether the Contracting Officer sustains the validity of the asserted restriction or determines that it is not justified, the final decision is issued within 60 days after receipt of the response to the challenge notice, or within such longer period that the Contracting Officer has notified the Contractor or subcontractor within 60 days after receipt of the response that the Government will require [@CL-CMP-3796; @CL-CMP-0447; @CL-CMP-0448; @CL-CMP-3797; @CL-CMP-0449; @CL-CMP-0450]. After a final decision under paragraph (g)(2)(i) that the restriction is not justified, the Government continues to be bound for 90 days from issuance of the final decision [@CL-CMP-3798], continues to be bound where a notice of intent to file suit in the United States Court of Federal Claims is provided to the Contracting Officer within 90 days unless the Contractor or subcontractor fails to file suit within 1 year after issuance of the final decision [@CL-CMP-0469; @CL-CMP-0470; @CL-CMP-0471], and continues to be bound where an appeal or suit is filed pursuant to the Contract Disputes statute until final disposition by an agency Board of Contract Appeals or the United States Court of Federal Claims [@CL-CMP-0472]. If the Contractor or subcontractor fails to appeal, file suit, or provide a notice of intent to file suit to the Contracting Officer within the 90-day period, the Government may cancel or ignore the restrictive markings that are based on the asserted restrictions, and that failure constitutes agreement with such Government action [@CL-CMP-3799; @CL-CMP-0468]. The Contracting Officer may review and make a written determination to challenge the restriction within 6 years of final payment on a contract or within 6 years of delivery of the technical data to the Government, whichever is later, and the Government may challenge a restriction at any time if the technical data fall in one of the four listed categories [@CL-CMP-3800; @CL-CMP-3827; @CL-CMP-3828].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has marked a set of drawings with a restriction. The Contracting Officer sends a challenge notice, and the contractor responds with its funding records and the required certification. The Contracting Officer later finds the restriction not justified and issues a final decision. The Government stays bound by the restriction for 90 days after that decision. If the contractor appeals, sues, or gives notice of intent to sue within that time, the Government stays bound for a further period that the clause sets. If it does nothing, the Government may cancel or ignore the markings. Separately, if the drawings had been posted publicly without restriction, the Government could challenge the restriction even after the 6-year period.

## Never confuse

The Offeror's assertions, including the assertions of its subcontractors or suppliers or potential subcontractors or suppliers, shall be submitted as an attachment to its offer in the prescribed format, dated and signed by an official authorized to contractually obligate the Offeror [@CL-CMP-3790]; for that step, see the card on [identification and assertion of restrictions](cmp-dod-assertion-of-data-restrictions.md). The Contractor, and its subcontractors or suppliers, may only assert restrictions on the Government's rights to use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data to be delivered under the contract by marking the deliverable data subject to restriction [@CL-CMP-3809]; for that step, see the card on [markings](cmp-dod-technical-data-markings.md). Under this clause, if the Contracting Officer determines that a challenge to the asserted restriction is warranted, the Contracting Officer will send a written challenge notice to the Contractor or subcontractor making the asserted restriction [@CL-CMP-3794]. For validation of computer software restrictions, see the card for [validation of asserted restrictions on computer software](cmp-dod-software-restriction-validation.md). Correction of nonconforming markings is not subject to the DFARS 252.227-7037, Validation of Asserted Restrictions on Technical Data, clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3821]. If the Contracting Officer determines that the validity of the asserted restriction is not justified, the Contracting Officer will issue a final decision to the Contractor or subcontractor in accordance with the Disputes clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-3797]; for the Disputes clause, see [Disputes clause, claims and certification](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-disputes-clause.md).
