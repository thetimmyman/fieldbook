## Purpose

Explains the greenhouse gas disclosure representation an offeror completes and the requirements that apply to the sustainable products and services a contract buys.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5447] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.223-22 and FAR 52.223-23 [@CL-CMP-5447; @CL-CMP-5452] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5447] |
| Applies to | Offerors completing the representation, and contractors supplying the sustainable products and services their contract requires [@CL-CMP-5447; @CL-CMP-5452] |

Representation. The representation must be completed if the Offeror received $7.5 million or more in Federal contract awards in the prior Federal fiscal year, and it is optional if the Offeror received less than $7.5 million in Federal contract awards in the prior Federal fiscal year [@CL-CMP-5447]. The first paragraph of the representation concerns publicly disclosing greenhouse gas emissions, which means making available on a publicly accessible web site the results of a greenhouse gas inventory, performed in accordance with an accounting standard with publicly available and consistently applied criteria, such as the Greenhouse Gas Protocol Corporate Standard [@CL-CMP-5448]. The second paragraph concerns publicly disclosing a quantitative greenhouse gas emissions reduction goal, meaning making available on a publicly available web site a target to reduce absolute emissions or emissions intensity by a specific quantity or percentage [@CL-CMP-5449]. If the Offeror checked "does" in the first or second paragraph, respectively, it must provide the publicly accessible web site or sites where greenhouse gas emissions or reduction goals are reported [@CL-CMP-5450]. A publicly accessible web site includes the Offeror's own web site or a recognized, third-party greenhouse gas emissions reporting program [@CL-CMP-6105]. In the first paragraph the Offeror, itself or through its immediate owner or highest-level owner, checks whether it does or does not publicly disclose greenhouse gas emissions [@CL-CMP-6103], and in the second it does the same for a quantitative greenhouse gas emissions reduction goal [@CL-CMP-6104].

## How it works

The sustainable products and services that apply to the contract, including the purchasing program and type of product or service, and any products or services that are not subject to the clause, will be set forth in the statement of work or elsewhere in the contract [@CL-CMP-5451].

The Contractor must ensure that the sustainable products and services required by the contract are delivered to the Government, furnished for use by the Government, and incorporated into the construction of a public building or public work [@CL-CMP-5452]. The Contractor must also ensure that they are furnished for use in performing services under the contract, where the cost of the products is a direct cost to the contract rather than costs normally applied to the Contractor's general and administrative expenses or indirect costs [@CL-CMP-5523].

The requirement includes services performed by contractors performing management and operation of Government-owned facilities to the same extent that, at the time of award, an agency would be required to comply if an agency operated or supported the facility [@CL-CMP-6124].

Except as provided in paragraph (b)(3)(ii) of the clause, sustainable products and services must meet the applicable standards, specifications, or other program requirements at time of quote or offer submission [@CL-CMP-5453]. Under paragraph (b)(3)(ii), sustainable products and services must meet the EPA Recommendations of Specifications, Standards, and Ecolabels in effect as of October 2023 [@CL-CMP-6106].

Resource. The Green Procurement Compilation provides a comprehensive list of sustainable products and services and sustainable acquisition guidance [@CL-CMP-6107].

Definitions. Recovered material means waste materials and by-products recovered or diverted from solid waste, but not those generated from, and commonly reused within, an original manufacturing process [@CL-CMP-6108]. A biobased product is a product determined by the United States Department of Agriculture to be a commercial product or industrial product, other than food or feed, that is composed, in whole or in significant part, of biological products [@CL-CMP-6125]. A biobased product also includes renewable domestic agricultural materials and forestry materials, or an intermediate ingredient or feedstock. [@CL-CMP-6287] The term biobased product includes, with respect to forestry materials, forest products that meet biobased content requirements, notwithstanding the market share the product holds, the age of the product, or whether the market for the product is new or emerging. [@CL-CMP-6288]

Sustainable products and services. Sustainable products and services means products and services that are subject to and meet the applicable statutory mandates and directives for purchasing, the first group being statutory purchasing programs. [@CL-CMP-6289] The first statutory purchasing program covers products containing recovered material designated by the United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) under the Comprehensive Procurement Guidelines. [@CL-CMP-6290] Another statutory purchasing program covers energy- and water-efficient products that are ENERGY STAR certified or Federal Energy Management Program (FEMP)-designated products. [@CL-CMP-6291] Another statutory purchasing program covers biobased products meeting the content requirement of the United States Department of Agriculture under the BioPreferred program. [@CL-CMP-6292] Another statutory purchasing program covers acceptable chemicals, products, and manufacturing processes listed under the EPA's Significant New Alternatives Policy (SNAP) program, which ensures a safe and smooth transition away from substances that contribute to the depletion of stratospheric ozone. [@CL-CMP-6293] The required EPA purchasing programs include WaterSense labeled (water efficient) products and services and Safer Choice-certified products, which are products that contain safer chemical ingredients. [@CL-CMP-6294] The required EPA purchasing programs also include products and services that meet EPA Recommendations of Specifications, Standards, and Ecolabels in effect as of October 2023. [@CL-CMP-6295]

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror received 9,000,000 dollars in Federal contract awards last fiscal year, so it must complete the representation. An offeror that received exactly 7,500,000 dollars must also complete it, because the test is $7.5 million or more. The first offeror checks "does" for the reduction goal paragraph and so provides the public web site where the goal is reported. A third offeror received 3,000,000 dollars, which is less than $7.5 million, so completing the representation is optional for it.

On the product side, a contract's statement of work lists the sustainable products that apply and names some items that are not subject to the clause. The contractor delivers the listed items and ensures they meet the applicable standards at the time it submits its offer, except that under paragraph (b)(3)(ii) they must meet the EPA Recommendations of Specifications, Standards, and Ecolabels in effect as of October 2023.

## Never confuse

The representation is about revealing emissions and goals, while the sustainability clause is about what the contractor delivers or incorporates [@CL-CMP-5447; @CL-CMP-5452]. Completing the representation is optional below the threshold, but the clause duty applies to the products and services the contract lists [@CL-CMP-5447; @CL-CMP-5451].
