## Purpose

Shows what the Construction Wage Rate Requirements clause requires a contractor to pay, post and conform when laborers and mechanics work on the site of the work, and where the site of the work begins and ends.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5816] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-6 [@CL-CMP-5816] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-6 [@CL-CMP-0925] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5816] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the clause as prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5816] |

All laborers and mechanics employed or working upon the site of the work must be paid unconditionally and not less often than once a week [@CL-CMP-5819]. Payment is made without subsequent deduction or rebate on any account, except the payroll deductions that regulations issued by the Secretary of Labor at 29 CFR part 3 permit [@CL-CMP-6129].

The amount due at the time of payment is the full amount of wages and bona fide fringe benefits, or cash equivalents, at rates not less than those in the Secretary of Labor wage determination attached to the contract [@CL-CMP-5820]. The rates may be those of a wage determination incorporated for a secondary site of the work, and they apply regardless of any contractual relationship alleged to exist between the contractor and the laborers and mechanics [@CL-CMP-6130]. A wage determination incorporated for a secondary site is effective from the first day on which work under the contract was performed at that site, and it is incorporated without any adjustment in contract price or estimated cost [@CL-CMP-6131]. Laborers employed by the construction contractor or subcontractor who transport portions of the building or work between the secondary site and the primary site are paid in accordance with the wage determination applicable to the primary site [@CL-CMP-6438].

## How it works

The site of the work means the primary site, which is the physical place or places where the construction will remain when the work is completed, and the secondary site, if any [@CL-CMP-5817]. A secondary site is any other site where a significant portion of the building or work is constructed, provided that the site is located in the United States and established specifically for the performance of the contract or project [@CL-CMP-5818].

Except as the exclusions below provide, fabrication plants, mobile factories, batch plants, borrow pits, job headquarters, tool yards and similar facilities are part of the site of the work if they are dedicated exclusively, or nearly so, to performance of the contract or project [@CL-CMP-6126]. They must also be adjacent or virtually adjacent to the primary site or the secondary site [@CL-CMP-6127]. Permanent home offices, branch plant establishments, fabrication plants and tool yards of a contractor or subcontractor are not part of the site of the work when their locations and continuance in operation are determined wholly without regard to a particular Federal contract or project [@CL-CMP-6128]. Fabrication plants, batch plants, borrow pits, job headquarters, yards and similar facilities of a commercial or material supplier are also outside it if the supplier established them for the project before opening of bids and not on the project site [@CL-CMP-6436]. Such permanent, previously established facilities stay outside even if their operations for a period of time are dedicated exclusively, or nearly so, to the performance of a contract [@CL-CMP-6437].

Contributions made, or costs reasonably anticipated, for bona fide fringe benefits under the Construction Wage Rate Requirements statute are considered wages paid, subject to paragraph (e) of the clause [@CL-CMP-6133]. Regular contributions made or costs incurred for more than a weekly period, but not less often than quarterly, under plans, funds or programs that cover the particular weekly period are deemed to be made or incurred during that weekly period [@CL-CMP-6439]. When the minimum wage rate for a class includes a fringe benefit that is not expressed as an hourly rate, the contractor either pays the benefit as stated in the wage determination or pays another bona fide fringe benefit or an hourly cash equivalent [@CL-CMP-6136]. If the contractor does not make payments to a trustee or other third person, it may count as wages the costs reasonably anticipated in providing bona fide fringe benefits under a plan or program [@CL-CMP-6137]. That treatment is allowed provided the Secretary of Labor has found, on the contractor's written request, that the applicable statutory standards have been met [@CL-CMP-6138]. The Secretary of Labor may require the contractor to set aside assets in a separate account to meet obligations under the plan or program [@CL-CMP-6440].

Workers are paid not less than the wage rate and fringe benefits in the wage determination for the classification of work actually performed, without regard to skill, except as the Apprentices and Trainees clause provides [@CL-CMP-6134]. A laborer or mechanic who works in more than one classification may be paid the rate for each classification for the time actually worked in it, provided the employer's payroll records accurately set forth the time spent in each classification [@CL-CMP-6135].

The wage determination, including any additional classifications and wage rates conformed under the clause, and the required poster must be posted at all times by the contractor and its subcontractors [@CL-CMP-5821]. The posting goes at the primary site and any secondary site, in a prominent and accessible place where workers can easily see it [@CL-CMP-6132].

A class of laborers or mechanics that the wage determination does not list and that is to be employed under the contract must be classified in conformance with the wage determination [@CL-CMP-5822]. The Contracting Officer approves an additional classification with its wage rate and fringe benefits only when all of the listed criteria are met [@CL-CMP-5823]. The work must not be performed by a classification in the wage determination, and the classification must be used in the area by the construction industry [@CL-CMP-6139]. The proposed rate, including bona fide fringe benefits, must bear a reasonable relationship to the wage rates in the wage determination, and for helpers the classification must prevail in the area where the work is performed [@CL-CMP-6140].

If the contractor, the laborers and mechanics to be employed in the classification if known or their representatives, and the Contracting Officer agree on the classification and wage rate [@CL-CMP-6141], the Contracting Officer sends a report of the action taken to the Administrator of the Wage and Hour Division [@CL-CMP-6142]. The Administrator or an authorized representative approves, modifies or disapproves every additional classification action within 30 days of receipt and so advises the Contracting Officer, or notifies the Contracting Officer within that 30-day period that additional time is necessary [@CL-CMP-6143].

If there is no agreement on the proposed classification and wage rate, the Contracting Officer refers the questions [@CL-CMP-6144]. The referral includes the views of all interested parties and the recommendation of the Contracting Officer and goes to the Administrator for determination [@CL-CMP-6145]. The Administrator or an authorized representative issues a determination within 30 days of receipt and so advises the Contracting Officer, or notifies the Contracting Officer within that 30-day period that additional time is necessary [@CL-CMP-6146]. The rate and fringe benefits determined by either route are paid to all workers performing work in the classification from the first day on which work is performed in the classification [@CL-CMP-6147].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 22.402-4(a)(1), where the codified clause points to FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-0942; @CL-CMP-5816]. It keeps the definition of the site of the work as the primary site and the secondary site [@CL-CMP-0925; @CL-CMP-0926; @CL-CMP-5817; @CL-CMP-5818]. Except as paragraph (3) of the definition provides, it keeps the inclusion of fabrication plants, mobile factories, batch plants, borrow pits, job headquarters, tool yards and similar facilities if they are dedicated exclusively, or nearly so, to the contract or project and are adjacent or virtually adjacent to the primary or secondary site [@CL-CMP-0927; @CL-CMP-0928; @CL-CMP-6126; @CL-CMP-6127]. It keeps the exclusion of permanent home offices, branch plant establishments, fabrication plants and tool yards whose locations and continuance in operation are determined wholly without regard to a particular Federal contract or project [@CL-CMP-0929; @CL-CMP-6128].

In paragraph (b), the RFO text keeps the rule that laborers and mechanics on the site of the work are paid unconditionally and not less often than once a week [@CL-CMP-0930; @CL-CMP-5819], without subsequent deduction or rebate except the permitted payroll deductions, which it ties to regulations at 29 CFR Part 3 [@CL-CMP-0931; @CL-CMP-0943; @CL-CMP-6129]. It keeps payment of the full amount of wages and bona fide fringe benefits at rates not less than the attached wage determination [@CL-CMP-0932; @CL-CMP-5820], which may be a determination incorporated for a secondary site, regardless of any contractual relationship alleged to exist between the contractor and the laborers and mechanics [@CL-CMP-0933; @CL-CMP-6130]. It keeps the rule that contributions made or costs reasonably anticipated for bona fide fringe benefits are considered wages paid, subject to paragraph (e) [@CL-CMP-0934; @CL-CMP-6133], and the rule that workers are paid for the classification of work actually performed, without regard to skill, except as the Apprentices and Trainees clause provides [@CL-CMP-0935; @CL-CMP-6134]. In paragraph (e), it keeps the rule that a contractor that does not make payments to a trustee or other third person may consider as wages the costs reasonably anticipated in providing bona fide fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-0940; @CL-CMP-6137], provided the Secretary of Labor has found, on the contractor's written request, that the statutory standards have been met [@CL-CMP-0941; @CL-CMP-6138].

Paragraph (c) on additional classifications is rewritten [@CL-CMP-0936; @CL-CMP-0937; @CL-CMP-5822; @CL-CMP-5823]. The RFO text says the contractor must complete and submit Standard Form 1444 if a class of laborers or mechanics not listed in the wage determination is to be employed, and it does not print the codified words that the contracting officer must require such a class to be classified in conformance with the wage determination [@CL-CMP-0936; @CL-CMP-5822]. It says that on receipt of a complete SF 1444 the contracting officer will submit a report to the Administrator, Wage and Hour Division, for approving, modifying, disapproving or rendering a final determination of the additional classification and wage rate including any amount designated for fringe benefits [@CL-CMP-0937; @CL-CMP-0938; @CL-CMP-6142]. The RFO paragraph (c) prints no list of criteria for an additional classification, no agreement route and no referral route, and no 30-day period for the Administrator [@CL-CMP-0936; @CL-CMP-0937; @CL-CMP-0938; @CL-CMP-0939; @CL-CMP-5823; @CL-CMP-6143; @CL-CMP-6146]. It says the wage rate determined pursuant to subparagraph (c) must be paid to all workers performing work in the classification from the first day on which work is performed in it, where the codified clause refers to the rate determined under paragraphs (c)(2) and (c)(3) [@CL-CMP-0939; @CL-CMP-6147].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A crew of laborers works on the primary site of a covered contract. The attached wage determination sets 30 per hour in wages and 10 per hour in fringe benefits for the classification of work performed. Paying 32 per hour with no fringe benefit leaves each worker short, because the required total is 40 per hour. The full 40 per hour of wages and fringe benefits must be paid unconditionally and at least weekly, with no deduction or rebate beyond permitted payroll deductions. If the employer provides the 10 per hour as contributions to a bona fide plan, the fringe benefit part is met, but the 30 per hour in wages must still be paid.

A mechanic works 3 of 8 hours in one classification and 5 hours in another. The employer may pay each classification's rate for the time worked in it, because the payroll records show the 3 and 5 hours separately.

A class of worker the wage determination does not list is to be employed. The Contracting Officer, not the employer, decides whether to approve an additional classification, and only if all the listed criteria are met. If the parties agree, the Administrator acts within 30 days of receipt or says that more time is needed. If they disagree, the Contracting Officer refers the questions, and the rate that results is paid from the first day on which work is performed in the classification. Under the RFO text, the contractor completes and submits SF 1444, the contracting officer submits a report to the Administrator, and the rate determined is paid from the first day on which work is performed in the classification [@CL-CMP-0936; @CL-CMP-0937; @CL-CMP-0939].

## Never confuse

The site of the work is not every place where the contractor performs support work. A permanent home office or a fabrication plant whose location and continuance in operation are determined wholly without regard to a particular Federal contract is outside it [@CL-CMP-6128], and a secondary site must be located in the United States and established specifically for the performance of the contract or project [@CL-CMP-5818]. The wage determination, not the employer's own pay scale, sets the floor [@CL-CMP-5820]. An unlisted class of worker is also not paid at an employer-chosen rate: it is classified in conformance with the wage determination, and an additional classification needs the Contracting Officer's approval [@CL-CMP-5822; @CL-CMP-5823]. Under the codified clause the contracting officer approves an additional classification only when all the listed criteria are met [@CL-CMP-5823], while the RFO paragraph (c) has the contractor complete and submit Standard Form 1444 and the contracting officer submit a report to the Administrator, and it prints no criteria [@CL-CMP-0936; @CL-CMP-0937; @CL-CMP-0938].

The provision on secondary site of the work notifications and wage determination requests is covered in [Secondary site of the work notifications and wage determination requests](cmp-secondary-site-of-the-work.md).
