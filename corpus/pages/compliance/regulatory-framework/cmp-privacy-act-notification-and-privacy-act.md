## Purpose

Shows what the Privacy Act notification and the Privacy Act clause require of a contractor that designs, develops or operates a system of records on individuals to accomplish an agency function, how the clause defines its terms, and how the notification and the clause travel into subcontracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5524] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.224-1 and 52.224-2 [@CL-CMP-5524; @CL-CMP-5625] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.224-1 [@CL-CMP-1910] and RFO FAR 52.224-2 [@CL-CMP-1912] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5524] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts where the design, development or operation of a system of records on individuals is required to accomplish an agency function [@CL-CMP-5524; @CL-CMP-5625] |

The notification clause tells the contractor that it will be required to design, develop or operate a system of records on individuals to accomplish an agency function subject to the Privacy Act of 1974 and applicable agency regulations [@CL-CMP-5525; @CL-CMP-5624]. It also warns that violation of the Act may involve the imposition of criminal penalties [@CL-CMP-5526].

The Privacy Act clause has the contractor agree to comply with the Act and the agency rules and regulations issued under it in designing, developing or operating any system of records on individuals to accomplish an agency function, when the contract specifically identifies the systems of records and the design, development or operation work that the contractor is to perform [@CL-CMP-5527; @CL-CMP-5626]. It also has the contractor agree to carry the notification and the clause itself into subcontracts, in the cases described below [@CL-CMP-5627; @CL-CMP-5528; @CL-CMP-5529].

## How it works

The duty to comply applies when the contract specifically identifies the systems of records and the design, development or operation work that the contractor is to perform [@CL-CMP-5527; @CL-CMP-5626]. The contract text that does this is specific to each contract.

In the Privacy Act clause, operation of a system of records means performance of any of the activities associated with maintaining the system, including the collection, use and dissemination of records [@CL-CMP-5628].

A record is any item, collection or grouping of information about an individual that an agency maintains, including education, financial transactions, medical history, and criminal or employment history [@CL-CMP-5629]. To be a record it must also contain the person's name, or the identifying number, symbol or other identifying particular assigned to the individual, such as a fingerprint, voiceprint or photograph [@CL-CMP-5630].

A system of records on individuals is a group of any records under the control of any agency from which information is retrieved by the name of the individual or by some identifying number, symbol or other identifying particular assigned to the individual [@CL-CMP-5631].

The contractor must include the notification contained in the contract in every solicitation and resulting subcontract, and in every subcontract awarded without a solicitation [@CL-CMP-5627]. That duty applies when the work statement in the proposed subcontract requires the design, development or operation of a system of records on individuals that is subject to the Act [@CL-CMP-5528].

The contractor must also include the Privacy Act clause, including its flowdown subparagraph, in all subcontracts awarded under the contract that require the design, development or operation of such a system of records [@CL-CMP-5529].

In the event of violations of the Act [@CL-CMP-7052], a civil action may be brought against the agency involved if the violation concerns designing, developing or operating a system of records on individuals to accomplish an agency function [@CL-CMP-5530]. In the same event, criminal penalties may be imposed on the officers or employees of the agency if the violation concerns operating such a system [@CL-CMP-5531; @CL-CMP-7052]. When the contract is for operating such a system, the contractor and any contractor employee are considered employees of the agency for purposes of the Act [@CL-CMP-5532]. The RFO text of this paragraph keeps that qualification [@CL-CMP-1917].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified FAR part 52 text, as Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.224-1 keeps the notice that the Contractor will be required to design, develop or operate a system of records on individuals to accomplish an agency function subject to the Privacy Act of 1974 and applicable agency regulations [@CL-CMP-1910; @CL-CMP-5525; @CL-CMP-5624], and the warning that violation of the Act may involve criminal penalties [@CL-CMP-1911; @CL-CMP-5526]. The RFO text of FAR 52.224-2 keeps the agreement to comply with the Act and the agency rules and regulations in designing, developing or operating any system of records on individuals, when the contract specifically identifies the systems of records and the work [@CL-CMP-1912; @CL-CMP-1913; @CL-CMP-5527; @CL-CMP-5626]. It keeps the agreement to include the notification in every solicitation and resulting subcontract and in every subcontract awarded without a solicitation [@CL-CMP-1914; @CL-CMP-5627], when the work statement in the proposed subcontract requires the design, development or operation of a system of records on individuals that is subject to the Act [@CL-CMP-1915; @CL-CMP-5528]. It keeps the agreement to include the clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all subcontracts that require that work [@CL-CMP-1916; @CL-CMP-5529]. It keeps the rule that the contractor and any contractor employee are considered agency employees for purposes of the Act when the contract is for the operation of a system of records on individuals to accomplish an agency function [@CL-CMP-1917; @CL-CMP-5532], and the meanings of operation of a system of records [@CL-CMP-1918; @CL-CMP-5628] and of a system of records on individuals [@CL-CMP-1919; @CL-CMP-5631]. The RFO text prints the prescription lines for both clauses as "As prescribed in 24.104, insert the following clause in solicitations and contracts:", with no condition about a system of records [@CL-CMP-1908; @CL-CMP-1909], where the codified lines apply when the design, development or operation of a system of records on individuals is required to accomplish an agency function [@CL-CMP-5524; @CL-CMP-5625].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract specifically identifies a benefits system of records and requires the contractor to operate it for an agency, so the contractor and its employees are treated as agency employees for purposes of the Act. The contractor puts the notification in its solicitation for a data entry subcontract whose work statement requires collecting records for that system, and puts the Privacy Act clause, including the flowdown subparagraph, in the awarded subcontract. A separate janitorial subcontract does not require any work on the system of records, so the conditions for carrying the notification and the clause into it are not met.

## Never confuse

A system of records on individuals is not any collection of personal data. The clause defines it as records under agency control from which information is retrieved by the individual's name or an identifying number, symbol or particular [@CL-CMP-5631].

The notification and the Privacy Act clause are different clauses. The notification warns the contractor, while the Privacy Act clause carries the agreement to comply and the flowdown duties [@CL-CMP-5525; @CL-CMP-5527; @CL-CMP-5529].

The codified prescription conditions each clause on the design, development or operation of a system of records on individuals being required to accomplish an agency function [@CL-CMP-5524; @CL-CMP-5625], but the RFO prescription lines print no such condition [@CL-CMP-1908; @CL-CMP-1909]. In the RFO clause text, the duty to comply still applies when the contract specifically identifies the systems of records and the work the contractor is to perform [@CL-CMP-1913; @CL-CMP-5626].

The privacy training clause is covered in [Privacy training for contractor employees](cmp-privacy-training.md).
