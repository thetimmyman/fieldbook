## Purpose

Explains what a construction solicitation must say when the wage determination for the primary site of the work is not yet in hand, how a clerical error in a determination is corrected, and what the contracting officer must do when a contract is awarded without the required determination.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-6726] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.404-4, 22.404-7 and 22.404-9 [@CL-CMP-6726; @CL-CMP-6729; @CL-CMP-6732] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-6729] |
| Applies to | Construction solicitations and contracts that need a wage determination for the primary site of the work [@CL-CMP-6726; @CL-CMP-6898] |

A solicitation issued before the wage determination for the primary site of the work is obtained must include a notice that the schedule of minimum wage rates to be paid under the contract will be issued as an amendment to the solicitation [@CL-CMP-6726]. In sealed bidding, bids may not be opened until a reasonable time after the wage determination for the primary site of the work has been furnished to all bidders [@CL-CMP-6727]. In negotiated acquisitions, the contracting officer may open proposals and conduct negotiations before obtaining the determination [@CL-CMP-6896], but must incorporate it into the solicitation before best and final offers are submitted [@CL-CMP-6728].

Upon the Department of Labor's own initiative or at the request of the contracting agency, the Administrator, Wage and Hour Division, may correct any wage determination found to contain clerical errors [@CL-CMP-6729]. A correction is effective immediately and applies to any solicitation or active contract [@CL-CMP-6897].

A contract is awarded without the required wage determination when it incorporates no determination, contains a clearly inapplicable general wage determination, or contains a project determination that is inapplicable because of an inaccurate description of the project or its location [@CL-CMP-6898]. Upon discovering the error, the contracting officer must initiate action at once to incorporate the required determination in the contract [@CL-CMP-6899]. If the required determination, meaning a valid determination in effect on the date of award, is not available, the contracting officer must expeditiously request one from the Department of Labor, with a statement explaining the circumstances and giving the date of the contract award [@CL-CMP-6900]. The contracting officer must either modify the contract to incorporate the required determination, retroactive to the date of award, and equitably adjust the contract price if appropriate, or terminate the contract [@CL-CMP-6732].

## How it works

The Administrator decides whether to correct a clerical error, acting on the Department's own initiative or at an agency's request [@CL-CMP-6729]. The contracting officer then applies the procedure for the stage the contract has reached [@CL-CMP-6730; @CL-CMP-6731]. Before award, the contracting officer must follow the listed paragraphs of FAR 22.404-5 for sealed bidding or for negotiation, according to the method used [@CL-CMP-6730]. After award, the contracting officer must follow the procedures at FAR 22.404-6(b)(5) [@CL-CMP-6731]; for a modification that exercises an option to extend the term of the contract, the contracting officer must follow the procedures at FAR 22.404-6(d)(2) instead [@CL-CMP-6731].

The after-award procedure at FAR 22.404-6(b)(5) applies if an effective modification is received by the contracting officer after award: the contracting officer must modify the contract to incorporate the wage modification retroactive to the date of award and equitably adjust the contract price for any increased or decreased cost of performance resulting from any changed wage rates [@CL-CMP-3023; @CL-CMP-3113]. At an option exercise, the contracting officer must modify the contract to incorporate the modified wage determination, and any changed wage rates, effective as of the date that the option to extend was effective [@CL-CMP-6928]. That duty applies whether the effective modified determination is received before or after execution of the option modification [@CL-CMP-6927]. The sealed-bidding timing rules for expiration and modification are on [Project wage determination expiration and modifications](../regulatory-framework/cmp-project-wage-determination-expiration-and-modifications.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A construction solicitation is issued before the primary-site determination arrives, so it carries a notice that the schedule of minimum wage rates will be issued as an amendment [@CL-CMP-6726]. In sealed bidding, bids are opened only after a reasonable time has passed since the determination was furnished to all bidders [@CL-CMP-6727]. In a separate negotiated buy, the contracting officer opens proposals and starts discussions before the determination arrives [@CL-CMP-6896], and incorporates the determination into the solicitation before best and final offers are submitted [@CL-CMP-6896; @CL-CMP-6728].

On a third contract, the Administrator corrects a clerical error in one rate at the request of the contracting agency, and the correction applies to the active contract at once [@CL-CMP-6729; @CL-CMP-6897]. The contracting officer follows the after-award procedure [@CL-CMP-6731]. Had the correction arrived at an option exercise, the contracting officer would follow the option procedure instead [@CL-CMP-6731; @CL-CMP-6928].

On a fourth contract, the contracting officer finds some weeks after award that a clearly inapplicable general determination was incorporated [@CL-CMP-6898]. The contracting officer starts action at once to incorporate the required determination [@CL-CMP-6899]. No determination valid on the award date is on hand, so the contracting officer requests one from the Department of Labor with a statement of the circumstances and the award date [@CL-CMP-6900]. When it arrives, the contracting officer modifies the contract to incorporate it retroactive to the award date and adjusts the price if appropriate, or terminates the contract [@CL-CMP-6732].

## Never confuse

An award without the required determination is not a clerical correction. For a clerical error the Administrator corrects the determination, and the correction applies immediately [@CL-CMP-6729; @CL-CMP-6897]. For an award without the required determination, the contracting officer acts and must either modify the contract retroactive to the date of award or terminate it [@CL-CMP-6732]. Pricing at an option exercise is on [Improper wage determinations and option wage pricing](../regulatory-framework/cmp-improper-wage-determinations-and-option-wage-pricing.md). The types of primary-site determination, and how one is requested, are on [Construction wage determination types and requests](../regulatory-framework/cmp-construction-wage-determination-types-and-requests.md).
