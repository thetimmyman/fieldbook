## Purpose

Shows what the contractor must tell its employees about whistleblower rights, how the duty reaches subcontracts, and which Inspector General office the code of business ethics clause means on DoD contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4191] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.203-7002 and 252.203-7003 [@CL-CMP-4191; @CL-CMP-4193] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4191] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clauses [@CL-CMP-4191; @CL-CMP-4193] |

The Contractor must inform its employees in writing, in the predominant native language of the workforce, of contractor employee whistleblower rights and protections under the statute the clause cites, as described in the subpart of the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement that the clause cites [@CL-CMP-4191]. The Contractor must include the substance of the clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in all subcontracts [@CL-CMP-4192].

The agency office of the Inspector General referenced in the paragraphs of the FAR code of business ethics clause that name it is the DoD Office of Inspector General [@CL-CMP-4193]. The clause also prints an address, a telephone number and a website for that office, which this card does not restate. The FAR clause itself is covered on [Contractor code of business ethics](cmp-contractor-code-of-business-ethics.md).

## How it works

The notice duty is written, is given in the predominant native language of the workforce, and concerns rights and protections under the cited statute [@CL-CMP-4191]. The duty passes to all subcontracts [@CL-CMP-4192]. The second clause designates the office: it says which Inspector General the FAR code of business ethics paragraphs mean on these contracts [@CL-CMP-4193]. For the general FAR reprisal rules see [Whistleblower protections for contractor employees](cmp-contractor-employee-whistleblower-protections.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's field staff mostly speak Spanish. It gives every employee a written notice in Spanish describing their whistleblower rights and protections.

It also writes the notice requirement into every subcontract, including a small support subcontract. When it later has a matter to disclose under its code of ethics, it knows the agency Inspector General is the DoD Office of Inspector General.

## Never confuse

The notice clause is not the hotline poster clause. This one requires a written notice to employees [@CL-CMP-4191], and its printed text has no posting requirement. The poster duty is a separate clause covered on [DFARS display of hotline posters](cmp-dfars-hotline-posters.md).

It is also not the posting rule in the deployed-forces clause. That clause separately requires the Contractor to post these rights in employee work spaces in English and in any foreign language spoken by a significant portion of the workforce [@CL-CMP-4208]. It is covered on [the card on definitions and pre-deployment](cmp-deployed-contractor-personnel-definitions-and-predeployment.md).

The notice is also not a restriction on arbitration agreements. That is a separate clause covered on [Restrictions on mandatory arbitration agreements](cmp-mandatory-arbitration-restrictions.md).

Designating the Inspector General office is not the same as requiring a disclosure. The designation says which office the FAR paragraphs mean [@CL-CMP-4193].
