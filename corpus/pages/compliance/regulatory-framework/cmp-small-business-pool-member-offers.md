## Purpose

Shows when a pool member may offer on its own, when the contracting officer shall not consider that offer, and what the pool agreement does to the responsibility question.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8255] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.703 [@CL-CMP-8255; @CL-CMP-8257] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8255] |
| Applies to | Individual offers submitted by pool members, independent of the pool [@CL-CMP-8255] |

Pool members may submit individual offers, independent of the pool [@CL-CMP-8255]. However, the contracting officer shall not consider an independent offer by a pool member if that pool member participates in a competing offer submitted by the pool [@CL-CMP-8256].

If a pool member submits an individual offer, independent of the pool, the contracting officer shall consider the pool agreement, along with other factors, in determining whether that pool member is a responsible prospective contractor under subpart 9.1 [@CL-CMP-8257].

## How it works

The member's freedom to offer alone is qualified by the competing-offer bar [@CL-CMP-8255; @CL-CMP-8256]. The bar applies if the member participates in a competing offer submitted by the pool [@CL-CMP-8256]. Both duties fall on the contracting officer, who shall not consider an independent offer in the barred case and who weighs the pool agreement in the responsibility determination [@CL-CMP-8256; @CL-CMP-8257]. The pool agreement is one factor, considered along with other factors [@CL-CMP-8257]. What a pool is, and how the contracting officer verifies its approved status, is on the [small business pool contracting](./cmp-small-business-pool-contracting.md) card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A pool submits an offer on a requirement, and Member A participates in that pool offer. Member A also submits its own offer on the same requirement. Answer: the contracting officer shall not consider Member A's independent offer, because Member A participates in a competing offer submitted by the pool [@CL-CMP-8256]. Member B belongs to the same pool but does not participate in the pool's offer, and submits its own offer. The bar in the section does not apply to Member B, so the section does not bar consideration of Member B's offer, and the contracting officer shall consider the pool agreement, along with other factors, in deciding whether Member B is a responsible prospective contractor [@CL-CMP-8256; @CL-CMP-8257].

## Never confuse

A member offering alone is not the same as the pool offering. The bar is stated for the case where the member participates in a competing offer submitted by the pool [@CL-CMP-8256]. The pool agreement is also not ignored in the responsibility question, because the contracting officer shall consider it along with other factors [@CL-CMP-8257].
