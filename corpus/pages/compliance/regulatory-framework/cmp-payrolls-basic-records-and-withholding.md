## Purpose

Shows the payroll records a contractor must keep, the weekly submission and Statement of Compliance that go to the Contracting Officer, the inspection and sanctions that back them, and how payments are withheld when required wages go unpaid.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5824] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-8 and FAR 52.222-7 [@CL-CMP-5824; @CL-CMP-5831] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-8 [@CL-CMP-1879] and RFO FAR 52.222-7 [@CL-CMP-1889] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5824] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the payrolls clause as prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5824] |

Payrolls and basic records relating to the work are maintained by the contractor during the course of the work and preserved for 3 years afterwards for all laborers and mechanics working at the site of the work [@CL-CMP-5825]. The records contain the name, address and social security number of each worker, the worker's correct classification, and the hourly rates of wages paid, including rates of contributions or costs anticipated for bona fide fringe benefits or cash equivalents of the types described in 40 United States Code 3141(2)(B) [@CL-CMP-6152; @CL-CMP-7369]. They also show the daily and weekly number of hours worked, the deductions made and the actual wages paid [@CL-CMP-5826; @CL-CMP-7369].

An added record duty applies whenever the Secretary of Labor has found, under the Construction Wage Rate Requirements clause, that the wages of a laborer or mechanic include costs reasonably anticipated in providing benefits under a plan or program described in the Construction Wage Rate Requirements statute [@CL-CMP-6153]. In that case the contractor maintains records which show that the commitment to provide the benefits is enforceable, that the plan or program is financially responsible, and that it has been communicated in writing to the laborers or mechanics affected [@CL-CMP-6154]. The contractor also maintains records which show the costs anticipated or the actual cost incurred in providing those benefits [@CL-CMP-6446]. A contractor employing apprentices or trainees under approved programs also maintains written evidence of the registration of apprenticeship programs and certification of trainee programs, the registration of the apprentices and trainees, and the ratios and wage rates prescribed in the applicable programs [@CL-CMP-6155; @CL-CMP-6467].

## How it works

The contractor submits a copy of all payrolls to the Contracting Officer weekly, for each week in which any contract work is performed [@CL-CMP-5827]. The payrolls set out accurately and completely all of the information required to be maintained, except that full social security numbers and home addresses are not included on weekly transmittals [@CL-CMP-6156]. Instead the payrolls need only include an individually identifying number for each employee, such as the last four digits of the employee's social security number [@CL-CMP-6157]. The prime contractor is responsible for the submission of copies of payrolls by all subcontractors [@CL-CMP-6158]. The weekly payroll information may be submitted in any form desired, and Optional Form WH-347 is available for that purpose and may be obtained from the Department of Labor Wage and Hour Division website [@CL-CMP-6441; @CL-CMP-6466]. A prime contractor may require a subcontractor to provide addresses and social security numbers for the prime contractor's own records, without weekly submission to the Contracting Officer, and that is not a violation [@CL-CMP-6445]. Contractors and subcontractors maintain the full social security number and current address of each covered worker [@CL-CMP-6443]. They provide those numbers and addresses on request to the Contracting Officer, the contractor or the Wage and Hour Division of the Department of Labor for an investigation or audit of compliance with prevailing wage requirements [@CL-CMP-6444].

Each payroll is accompanied by a Statement of Compliance signed by the contractor or subcontractor, or the agent who pays or supervises the payment of the persons employed under the contract [@CL-CMP-5828]. The statement certifies that the payroll contains the information required to be maintained and that the information is correct and complete [@CL-CMP-6159]. It certifies that each laborer or mechanic, including each helper, apprentice and trainee, employed on the contract during the payroll period has been paid the full weekly wages earned, without rebate, directly or indirectly [@CL-CMP-6160]. It also certifies that no deductions have been made from the full wages earned other than permissible deductions as set forth in the regulations in 29 CFR part 3 [@CL-CMP-6161]. A further certification is that each laborer or mechanic has been paid not less than the applicable wage rates and fringe benefits, or cash equivalents, for the classification of work performed, as the applicable wage determination specifies [@CL-CMP-6162]. Falsifying any of the certifications may subject the contractor or subcontractor to civil or criminal prosecution [@CL-CMP-6163]. The weekly submission of a properly executed certification on the reverse side of Optional Form WH-347 satisfies the requirement to submit the Statement of Compliance [@CL-CMP-6442].

The records must be open to inspection, copying or transcription by the Contracting Officer or authorized representatives of the Contracting Officer or the Department of Labor [@CL-CMP-5829]. The contractor or subcontractor must also permit those officials to interview employees during working hours on the job [@CL-CMP-6164]. If the contractor or subcontractor fails to submit required records or to make them available, the Contracting Officer may, after written notice to the contractor, take the action necessary to cause suspension of any further payment [@CL-CMP-5830]. Failure to submit the required records upon request or to make them available may also be grounds for debarment action pursuant to 29 CFR 5.12 [@CL-CMP-6165].

Under the withholding clause the Contracting Officer, on the officer's own action or on the written request of an authorized representative of the Department of Labor, withholds or causes withholding from the contractor under this contract or any other Federal contract with the same prime contractor [@CL-CMP-5831]. The withholding also reaches any other federally assisted contract subject to prevailing wage requirements that the same prime contractor holds [@CL-CMP-6148]. The amount withheld is so much of the accrued payments or advances as may be considered necessary to pay laborers and mechanics, including apprentices, trainees and helpers, employed by the contractor or any subcontractor the full amount of wages required by the contract [@CL-CMP-6149].

The suspension power applies where any laborer or mechanic, including any apprentice, trainee or helper, employed or working on the site of the work is not paid all or part of the wages required by the contract [@CL-CMP-6150]. The Contracting Officer may then, after written notice to the contractor, take the action necessary to suspend any further payment, advance or guarantee of funds until the violations have ceased [@CL-CMP-6151].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified FAR part 52 text, as Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.222-8 keeps the rule that payrolls and basic records are maintained during the course of the work and preserved for 3 years afterwards for all laborers and mechanics working at the site of the work [@CL-CMP-1879; @CL-CMP-5825]. It keeps the weekly submission of all payrolls to the Contracting Officer for each week in which contract work is performed [@CL-CMP-1880; @CL-CMP-5827], with full social security numbers and home addresses left off weekly transmittals [@CL-CMP-1881; @CL-CMP-6156], and the prime contractor's responsibility for subcontractor payrolls [@CL-CMP-1882; @CL-CMP-6158]. It keeps the duty to maintain the full social security number and current address of each covered worker [@CL-CMP-1883; @CL-CMP-6443] and to provide them on request for an investigation or audit of compliance with prevailing wage requirements [@CL-CMP-1884; @CL-CMP-6444]. It keeps the Statement of Compliance that each payroll must accompany [@CL-CMP-1885; @CL-CMP-5828], with its certifications that the payroll is correct and complete [@CL-CMP-9306; @CL-CMP-6159], that each laborer or mechanic has been paid the full weekly wages earned without rebate [@CL-CMP-9307; @CL-CMP-6160], that no deductions have been made other than permissible deductions as set forth in the regulations in 29 CFR part 3 [@CL-CMP-9308; @CL-CMP-6161], and that each laborer or mechanic has been paid not less than the applicable wage rates and fringe benefits or cash equivalents [@CL-CMP-9309; @CL-CMP-6162]. It keeps the Optional Form WH-347 certification that satisfies the Statement of Compliance requirement [@CL-CMP-1886; @CL-CMP-6442]. It keeps the inspection of the records [@CL-CMP-1887; @CL-CMP-5829] and the suspension of further payment after written notice where records are not submitted or made available [@CL-CMP-1888; @CL-CMP-5830]. The RFO text of FAR 52.222-7 keeps the withholding on the Contracting Officer's own action or on the written request of an authorized representative of the Department of Labor, from this contract or any other Federal contract with the same prime contractor [@CL-CMP-1889; @CL-CMP-5831], its reach to other federally assisted contracts subject to prevailing wage requirements held by the same prime contractor [@CL-CMP-1890; @CL-CMP-6148], and the amount withheld [@CL-CMP-1891; @CL-CMP-6149]. It keeps the suspension power where a laborer or mechanic is not paid the wages required [@CL-CMP-1892; @CL-CMP-6150], and the suspension of further payment, advance or guarantee of funds after written notice until the violations have ceased [@CL-CMP-1893; @CL-CMP-6151]. The RFO text prints the prescription line for the payrolls and basic records clause as FAR 22.402-4(a)(3) [@CL-CMP-1878], where the codified clause is prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5824]. It prints the prescription line for the withholding clause as FAR 22.402-4(a)(2) [@CL-CMP-9304], where the codified clause is prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-9305].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A site runs for 3 weeks with 12 laborers. For each of the 3 weeks the contractor sends all payrolls to the Contracting Officer with a signed Statement of Compliance. The payrolls carry the last four digits of each worker's social security number and no home addresses, while the contractor's own records keep the full numbers and addresses. In the fourth week no work is performed, so no payroll is due. An authorized representative of the Department of Labor then asks for the records, and the contractor produces them for copying, lets that representative interview employees on the job, and keeps the records for 3 years after the work.

In week 2 one laborer was underpaid by 120. The Contracting Officer withholds, on the officer's own action or on the written request of an authorized representative of the Department of Labor, so much of the accrued payments or advances as is considered necessary to pay that 120, and the withholding may be taken from another Federal contract held by the same prime contractor. If the underpayment is not corrected, the officer may, after written notice, suspend further payments until the violation has ceased.

## Never confuse

Withholding is not a fixed sum. It is sized to what may be considered necessary to pay the wages required by the contract, and it may reach other contracts held by the same prime contractor [@CL-CMP-6149; @CL-CMP-5831; @CL-CMP-6148]. Suspension of further payment is a separate step that follows written notice [@CL-CMP-6151].

Keeping records is not the same as sending them. The maintained records hold each worker's social security number [@CL-CMP-6152], the weekly transmittal leaves out full numbers and home addresses [@CL-CMP-6156], and the contractor sends payrolls for each week in which contract work is performed [@CL-CMP-5827].

The codified payrolls clause is prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-5824], but the RFO text prints its prescription as FAR 22.402-4(a)(3) [@CL-CMP-1878]. Likewise, the codified withholding clause is prescribed at FAR 22.407(a) [@CL-CMP-9305], but the RFO text prints its prescription as FAR 22.402-4(a)(2) [@CL-CMP-9304].

The policy sections on construction payrolls and statements of compliance, and on withholding and suspension of contract payments, are covered in [Construction payrolls and statements of compliance, and labor standards investigations](cmp-construction-payrolls-statements-and-investigations.md) and [Construction wage rate requirements: withholding from and suspension of contract payments](cmp-construction-wage-withholding-and-suspension.md).

## Company calibration

That recordkeeping and weekly submission duties are assigned for every covered contract, and who owns the response to any recordkeeping finding. [@Q-014]
