## Purpose

Shows that the Secretary of Labor is authorized and directed to enforce the Service Contract Labor Standards statute, make rules and regulations, issue orders, hold hearings, make decisions, and take other appropriate action [@CL-CMP-8099], and lists the Department of Labor regulations that FAR 22.1004 names [@CL-CMP-8100; @CL-CMP-8101; @CL-CMP-8102].

## How it works

The duty and the authority fall on the Secretary of Labor. The Secretary is authorized and directed to enforce the statute, make rules and regulations, issue orders, hold hearings, make decisions, and take other appropriate action [@CL-CMP-8099]. The section places no duty on a contractor, a contracting officer or an agency head, and it states no threshold, deadline, price, payment, filing or remedy.

The section lists regulations that the Department of Labor has issued "on such matters as" the eight items, so the list is not stated to be complete [@CL-CMP-8100]. The regulations are in the Code of Federal Regulations at the parts and subparts shown in the table [@CL-CMP-8100; @CL-CMP-8101; @CL-CMP-8102]. The Wage and Hour Division is the unit in the Department of Labor to which functions of the Secretary of Labor under the statute are assigned [@CL-CMP-8085]; it is defined on [the definitions card](cmp-service-contract-labor-standards-definitions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company holds a services order. It has a question on how a wage determination is made and, on another order, receives notice of an administrative proceeding that enforces service contract labor standards.

For the first question, the section lists wage determination procedures at 29 CFR part 4, subparts A and B [@CL-CMP-8100]. For the proceeding, it lists rules of practice at 29 CFR part 6 and practice before the Administrative Review Board at 29 CFR part 8 [@CL-CMP-8102]. The section does not say which of these rules governs a given stage, so the company reads the regulation text itself.

## Never confuse

This section describes the Secretary of Labor's authority, not a contracting officer's. It authorizes the Secretary of Labor to enforce, make rules and regulations, issue orders, hold hearings and make decisions [@CL-CMP-8099]. It does not describe a contracting officer decision.

The list of regulations is a list of matters the Department of Labor has issued regulations on. The section does not restate the content of those regulations [@CL-CMP-8100; @CL-CMP-8101; @CL-CMP-8102].
