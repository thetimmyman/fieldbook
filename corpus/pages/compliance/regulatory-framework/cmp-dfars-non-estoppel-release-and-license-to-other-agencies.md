## Purpose

Shows what the Government does not concede by taking a patent license, which past claims the contractor releases, and which other Government agencies may ask for the same license.

| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7000, 252.227-7001 and 252.227-7010 [@CL-CMP-4266; @CL-CMP-4268; @CL-CMP-4271] |

The Government reserves the right at any time to contest the enforceability, validity, scope of, or title to any patent or patent application licensed, without waiving or forfeiting any right under the contract [@CL-CMP-4267].

In the Release of Past Infringement clause the Contractor releases every claim and demand it now has or may hereafter have against the Government for the manufacture or use by or for the Government, before the effective date of the contract, of any inventions covered by the patents and applications listed in two numbered items [@CL-CMP-4268; @CL-CMP-8642]. The first numbered item is any of the patents and applications for patent identified in the contract [@CL-CMP-4269]. The second numbered item is any other patent or application for patent owned or hereafter acquired by the Contractor, insofar as and only to the extent that it covers the manufacture, use or disposition of the subject matter described in the bracketed blank [@CL-CMP-4270]. Bracketed portions of the clause may be omitted when not appropriate or not encompassed by the release as negotiated [@CL-CMP-4367].

Under the License to Other Government Agencies clause the Contractor agrees to grant a separate license under the patents, applications for patents and improvements referred to in the License Grant clause, on the same terms and conditions as appear in the license contract [@CL-CMP-4271]. That separate license is to be granted to any other department or agency of the Government at any time on receipt of a written request for such a license from that department or agency [@CL-CMP-4272]. As to royalties that accrue under such separate licenses, reports and payments are made directly to the Contractor by each such other department or agency pursuant to the terms of the separate licenses [@CL-CMP-4273]. The Contractor shall notify the Licensee promptly upon receipt of any request for a license under the clause [@CL-CMP-4368].

## How it works

The Government reserves the right at any time to contest the enforceability, validity, scope of, or title to any patent or patent application licensed [@CL-CMP-4267]. The release covers claims for manufacture or use by or for the Government before the effective date of the contract [@CL-CMP-4268]. The Contractor agrees to grant a separate license on the same terms and conditions to any other department or agency of the Government at any time on receipt of a written request for such a license from that department or agency [@CL-CMP-4271; @CL-CMP-4272]. Each such agency reports and pays royalties directly to the Contractor, and the Contractor must promptly notify the Licensee of the request [@CL-CMP-4273; @CL-CMP-4368].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor licenses a patent to Department A. Department B later asks in writing for a license. The contractor must grant a separate license to Department B on the same terms, and must promptly notify Department A, the licensee, of the request. Department B reports and pays any royalties directly to the contractor. Department A's earlier use of an invention covered by a patent identified in the contract, before the contract's effective date, is released.
