## Purpose

Shows what a contract administration office must work to when it performs contract administration functions for an assigned contract, who may carry out onsite inspections or evaluations of that office's performance, and how the defense supplement treats contract administration services performed outside the United States. The general section is FAR 42.301 [@CL-CMP-8761], the evaluation section is FAR 42.403 [@CL-CMP-8763], and the overseas section is DFARS 242.301 [@CL-CMP-8764].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CMP-8761; @CL-CMP-8764] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.301 [@CL-CMP-8761; @CL-CMP-8762], FAR 42.403 [@CL-CMP-8763] and DFARS 242.301 [@CL-CMP-8764; @CL-CMP-8765] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.301 [@CL-CMP-8947; @CL-CMP-8948] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8761] |
| Applies to | Contracts assigned for administration under Subpart 42.2 [@CL-CMP-8761]; onsite inspections or evaluations of a contract administration office's assigned functions [@CL-CMP-8763]; contract administration services performed outside the United States [@CL-CMP-8764] |

FAR 42.301 is headed General and sits in Subpart 42.3, Contract Administration Office Functions [@CL-CMP-8761; @CL-CMP-8774]. When a contract is assigned for administration under Subpart 42.2, the contract administration office (CAO) shall perform contract administration functions in accordance with 48 CFR Chapter I and the contract terms [@CL-CMP-8761]. The CAO shall also perform them in accordance with the applicable regulations of the servicing agency, unless otherwise agreed to in an interagency agreement (see FAR 42.002) [@CL-CMP-8762]. The duty falls on the CAO. The section states no threshold or deadline, and it places no duty on the contractor [@CL-CMP-8761; @CL-CMP-8762].

FAR 42.403 is headed Evaluation of contract administration offices and sits in Subpart 42.4, Correspondence and Visits [@CL-CMP-8763; @CL-CMP-8775]. Onsite inspections or evaluations of the performance of the assigned functions of a CAO shall be accomplished only by or under the direction of the agency of which that office is a part [@CL-CMP-8763]. The section states no exception, threshold or deadline [@CL-CMP-8763].

DFARS 242.301 is headed General and sits in Subpart 242.3, Contract Administration Office Functions [@CL-CMP-8764; @CL-CMP-8776]. Contract administration services performed outside the United States should be performed in accordance with FAR 42.301 unless there are no policies and procedures covering a given situation [@CL-CMP-8764]. For that case, proposed actions are to be coordinated with the appropriate United States country teams or commanders of unified and specified commands [@CL-CMP-8765]. The text does not say who performs the services or who coordinates.

## How it works

The sections apply in this order. Assignment of the contract under Subpart 42.2 comes first, and the CAO's duties under FAR 42.301 follow from it [@CL-CMP-8761]. The CAO works to Chapter I and the contract terms [@CL-CMP-8761], and to the applicable regulations of the servicing agency unless otherwise agreed to in an interagency agreement [@CL-CMP-8762]. The section does not say who decides whether an interagency agreement provides otherwise, and it points to FAR 42.002 for interagency agreements [@CL-CMP-8762]. See [Interagency agreements for contract administration and audit](cmp-interagency-agreements-for-administration-and-audit.md) for that section.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.301 has the same substance as the codified section, with "will perform" where the codified sentence says "shall perform": when a contract is assigned for administration under subpart 42.2, the CAO will perform contract administration functions in accordance with 48 CFR Chapter 1 and the contract terms [@CL-CMP-8947; @CL-CMP-8761], and, unless otherwise agreed to in an interagency agreement (see RFO FAR 42.001), the applicable regulations of the servicing agency [@CL-CMP-8948; @CL-CMP-8762].

Onsite inspections or evaluations of the performance of the CAO's assigned functions are accomplished only by or under the direction of the agency of which that office is a part [@CL-CMP-8763]. For the functions that a CAO performs and the assignment itself, see [Contract administration functions](../audit-retention/cmp-contract-administration-functions.md) and [Assignment of contract administration](cmp-assignment-of-contract-administration.md). For the DoD additions, see [DoD additions to contract administration functions](cmp-dod-contract-administration-additions.md). For the wider set of oversight roles, see [Oversight roles](../audit-retention/cmp-oversight-roles.md).

Outside the United States, services should follow FAR 42.301 [@CL-CMP-8764]. Where there are no policies and procedures covering a given situation, proposed actions are to be coordinated with the appropriate U.S. country teams or commanders of unified and specified commands [@CL-CMP-8765].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract is assigned for administration under Subpart 42.2 to a CAO that belongs to Agency A [@CL-CMP-8761]. An interagency agreement provides that the servicing agency's regulations do not apply to one function. For that function, the CAO is not bound by those regulations, because FAR 42.301 yields to an interagency agreement that provides otherwise [@CL-CMP-8762]. The CAO's duty to follow 48 CFR Chapter I and the contract terms is stated without that exception [@CL-CMP-8761].

Agency B placed the order and wants a team to inspect the CAO's work onsite. Under FAR 42.403 the inspection shall be accomplished only by or under the direction of Agency A, because the CAO is part of Agency A [@CL-CMP-8763]. Agency B's team may take part only if the inspection is carried out under Agency A's direction.

Some of the services are performed in another country. They should follow FAR 42.301 [@CL-CMP-8764]. If no policies and procedures cover one situation, the proposed action is to be coordinated with the appropriate United States country team or the commander of the unified or specified command [@CL-CMP-8765].

## Never confuse

The overseas DFARS sentence is not the general rule and does not use its verb: FAR 42.301 sets what the CAO works to [@CL-CMP-8761; @CL-CMP-8762], and FAR 42.403 sets who may accomplish onsite inspections or evaluations of the performance of the CAO's assigned functions [@CL-CMP-8763]; FAR 42.301 and FAR 42.403 say shall [@CL-CMP-8761; @CL-CMP-8763], while DFARS 242.301 says should [@CL-CMP-8764]. The instruction to coordinate proposed actions applies in the case where there are no policies and procedures covering a given situation [@CL-CMP-8764; @CL-CMP-8765].
