## Purpose

Shows who may direct enforcement action against a contractor found to be in violation, what precedes that direction, and which actions are listed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8014] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.806, FAR 22.808 and FAR 22.809 [@CL-CMP-8014; @CL-CMP-8063; @CL-CMP-8064; @CL-CMP-8065; @CL-CMP-8066] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8014] |
| Applies to | Contractors found to be in violation of E.O. 11246, the regulations of the Secretary of Labor, or the applicable contract clauses [@CL-CMP-8015] |

Upon written notification to the contracting officer, the Deputy Assistant Secretary may direct one or more of the listed actions, as well as administrative sanctions and penalties, be taken against contractors found to be in violation of E.O. 11246, the regulations of the Secretary of Labor, or the applicable contract clauses [@CL-CMP-8014; @CL-CMP-8015]. The Deputy Assistant Secretary is the Deputy Assistant Secretary for Federal Contract Compliance, Department of Labor, or a designee [@CL-CMP-6984].

The listed actions are:

- (a) publication of the names of the contractor or its unions [@CL-CMP-8016];
- (b) cancellation, termination or suspension of the contractor's contracts or a portion of them [@CL-CMP-8017];
- (c) debarment from future Government contracts, or extensions or modifications of existing contracts, until the contractor has established and carried out personnel and employment policies in compliance with E.O. 11246 and the regulations of the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CMP-8018];
- (d) referral by the Deputy Assistant Secretary of any matter arising under E.O. 11246 to the Department of Justice or to the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) for the institution of appropriate civil or criminal proceedings [@CL-CMP-8019].

## How it works

Who decides. The Deputy Assistant Secretary directs the actions [@CL-CMP-8014]. The section does not say who makes the finding of violation or how it is made [@CL-CMP-8015].

What precedes the direction. Written notification to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-8014]. The section does not say who gives it.

Scope of the direction. The Deputy Assistant Secretary may direct one action or several, and may add administrative sanctions and penalties [@CL-CMP-8014]. The section does not say who carries the actions out.

Against whom. Contractors found in violation of E.O. 11246, the regulations of the Secretary of Labor, or the applicable contract clauses [@CL-CMP-8015].

Debarment. Paragraph (c) runs until the contractor has established and carried out the compliant policies [@CL-CMP-8018]. The section does not say who decides that the condition is met.

Referral. Paragraph (d) is a referral by the Deputy Assistant Secretary to the Department of Justice or the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission for the institution of proceedings [@CL-CMP-8019].

Not stated here. The section sets no deadline and no dollar threshold. The linked pages cover the roles and the clause.

An inquiry from a contractor regarding status of its compliance with E.O. 11246, or rights of appeal to any of the actions in 22.809, shall be referred to the regional office of the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs (OFCCP) [@CL-CMP-8063]. Labor union inquiries regarding the revision of a collective bargaining agreement in order to comply with E.O. 11246 shall be referred to the Deputy Assistant Secretary [@CL-CMP-8064]. Complaints received by the contracting officer alleging violation of the requirements of E.O. 11246 shall be referred immediately to the OFCCP regional office, and the complainant shall be advised in writing of the referral [@CL-CMP-8065]. The contractor that is the subject of a complaint shall not be advised in any manner or for any reason of the complainant's name, the nature of the complaint, or the fact that the complaint was received [@CL-CMP-8066].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor with two contracts is found in violation of E.O. 11246 [@CL-CMP-8015]. Upon written notification to the contracting officer, the Deputy Assistant Secretary may direct suspension of one contract [@CL-CMP-8014; @CL-CMP-8017]. The Deputy Assistant Secretary may also refer the matter to the Department of Justice for civil proceedings [@CL-CMP-8019]. The direction may combine these actions and may add administrative sanctions and penalties [@CL-CMP-8014]. If debarment is directed instead, it runs until the contractor has established and carried out compliant personnel and employment policies [@CL-CMP-8018].

## Never confuse

The actions are for contractors found to be in violation of E.O. 11246, the regulations of the Secretary of Labor, or the applicable contract clauses [@CL-CMP-8015].

Debarment under paragraph (c) is from future Government contracts, or extensions or modifications of existing contracts, until the contractor has established and carried out personnel and employment policies in compliance with E.O. 11246 and the regulations of the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CMP-8018].

Under paragraph (d), the Deputy Assistant Secretary refers a matter arising under E.O. 11246 to the Department of Justice or the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission for the institution of appropriate civil or criminal proceedings [@CL-CMP-8019].
