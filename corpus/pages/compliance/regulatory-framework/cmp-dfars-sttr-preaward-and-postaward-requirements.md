## Purpose

Shows what an STTR offer must contain about its agreement with a research institution, and what the contractor must do when that agreement changes after award.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3922] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7040 and 252.227-7041 [@CL-CMP-3922; @CL-CMP-3926] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3922] |
| Applies to | Offers in response to a solicitation with the preaward provision [@CL-CMP-3922] |

Offers shall include the written agreement between the Offeror and a research institution, which shall contain, among its listed items, a specific allocation of ownership, rights and responsibilities for intellectual property [@CL-CMP-3922]. One listed content item is identification of which party may obtain United States or foreign patents or otherwise protect inventions that result from an STTR award [@CL-CMP-3923]. The provision lists, as the second item an offer shall include, the Offeror's written representation that it is satisfied with the agreement and that the agreement does not conflict with the requirements of the solicitation [@CL-CMP-3922; @CL-CMP-3924]. Failure to submit the written agreement or the written representation with the offer may render the offer ineligible for award [@CL-CMP-3925].

After award, the Contractor shall not allow any modification to its written agreement with the research institution unless the modified agreement contains, among its listed items, a specific allocation of ownership, rights and responsibilities for intellectual property [@CL-CMP-3926]. Within 30 days of execution of a modified agreement, the Contractor shall submit a copy of the updated agreement and the updated written representation to the Contracting Officer [@CL-CMP-3927].

## How it works

The preaward provision sets what an offer must carry, and the postaward clause keeps the agreement in line with those terms after award. The agreement and the representation are submissions, and failure to make them can make an offer ineligible for award [@CL-CMP-3922; @CL-CMP-3924; @CL-CMP-3925]. A change to the agreement after award is allowed only if the modified text still holds the listed content [@CL-CMP-3926]. The updated agreement then goes to the Contracting Officer within a fixed time [@CL-CMP-3927]. How SBIR and STTR data are marked, what procedures and records the contractor must keep, and when the Government may release data are on [DoD SBIR and STTR data rights: definitions, protection period and markings](cmp-dfars-sbir-sttr-data-rights-definitions-and-period.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A small business answers a solicitation with the STTR preaward provision. Its offer includes the agreement with a university and its signed representation. After award, the two parties change who may file patents, and the change is signed on a given day. The company sends the updated agreement and updated representation to the Contracting Officer 20 days later. That is inside the 30-day limit.

## Never confuse

The agreement allocates intellectual property between the Offeror and the research institution, and it is separate from the Government's rights in SBIR and STTR data, which are set by the rights clause and covered on [DoD SBIR and STTR data rights: definitions, protection period and markings](cmp-dfars-sbir-sttr-data-rights-definitions-and-period.md). A concern's size and eligibility for the programs are a different question, covered on [SBIR and STTR size and eligibility standards](../../market/taxonomy/mkt-sbir-sttr-size-and-eligibility-standards.md). The FAR clause on SBIR rights is covered on [Rights in data under the SBIR and STTR program](cmp-rights-in-data-sbir-and-sttr-program.md).
