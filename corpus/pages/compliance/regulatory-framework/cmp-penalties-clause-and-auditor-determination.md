## Purpose

Shows which solicitations and contracts carry the penalties for unallowable costs clause, and what the DoD auditor issues when agreement with the contractor on final indirect cost rates cannot be reached.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CMP-7820; @CL-CMP-7822] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.709-7 and DFARS 242.705-2 [@CL-CMP-7820; @CL-CMP-7822] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.508-7 [@CL-CMP-0125; @CL-CMP-0126] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7820] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts over $1 million, except the excluded fixed-price types [@CL-CMP-7820]; the DoD auditor determination procedure when agreement cannot be reached with the contractor [@CL-CMP-7822] |

The clause at FAR 52.242-3, Penalties for Unallowable Costs, is to be used in all solicitations and contracts over $1 million, except fixed-price contracts without cost incentives or any firm-fixed-price contract for the purchase of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-7820]. The section says "use" and does not name who inserts the clause. Generally, covered contracts are those that contain one of the clauses at FAR 52.216-7, 52.216-16 or 52.216-17, or a similar clause from an executive agency's supplement to the FAR [@CL-CMP-7821].

Under the DoD auditor determination procedure, when agreement cannot be reached with the contractor, the auditor will issue a DCAA Form 1, Notice of Contract Costs Suspended and/or Disapproved, in addition to the advisory report to the administrative contracting officer [@CL-CMP-7822]. The printed DoD section contains only that paragraph.

## How it works

The clause prescription and the auditor step are separate. The prescription turns on the dollar value of the award and on two exceptions: a fixed-price contract without cost incentives, and a firm-fixed-price contract for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-7820]. A fixed-price contract with cost incentives is not within the first exception, and a fixed-price contract that is not firm-fixed-price is not within the second [@CL-CMP-7820]. The second sentence says "generally", so this page treats the list of clauses as a guide to which contracts are covered [@CL-CMP-7821]. The dollar threshold and the exceptions in the first sentence are the stated rule [@CL-CMP-7820].

The auditor step belongs to the DoD procedure for final indirect cost rates. When agreement cannot be reached with the contractor, the auditor issues the notice in addition to the advisory report, so the report to the administrative contracting officer still goes [@CL-CMP-7822]. Under the FAR procedure, once a proposal has been determined to be adequate for audit in support of negotiating final indirect cost rates, the auditor shall, if agreement with the contractor is not reached, forward the audit report to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) identified in the Directory of Contract Administration Services Components, who will then resolve the disagreement [@CL-RTE-0215; @CL-RTE-0217]. The auditor procedure is on [Final indirect cost rates: auditor determination procedure](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-final-indirect-rate-auditor-determination.md). The penalty itself, who assesses it and the waiver rules are on [Penalties for unallowable costs in final rate proposals](../cas-allowability/cmp-unallowable-cost-penalty.md) and [Indirect cost rate certification and penalties on unallowable costs](../cas-allowability/cmp-indirect-rate-certification-and-penalty-assessment.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.508-7 keeps the rule that the clause at 52.242-3, Penalties for Unallowable Costs, is used in all solicitations and contracts over $1 million, except fixed-price contracts without cost incentives or any firm-fixed-price contract for the purchase of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-0125; @CL-CMP-7820]. It keeps the statement that covered contracts are generally those that contain one of the clauses at 52.216-7, 52.216-16 or 52.216-17, or a similar clause from an executive agency's supplement to the FAR [@CL-CMP-0126; @CL-CMP-7821]. RFO FAR 42.505-2(b)(2)(iv), in the auditor determination procedure, keeps the step that, if agreement with the contractor is not reached, the audit report is forwarded to the contracting officer (or cognizant Federal agency official) identified in the Directory of Contract Administration Services Components, who will then resolve the disagreement [@CL-CMP-0127; @CL-RTE-0217].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a fixed-price incentive contract with a cost incentive is for 3,000,000, so the clause is used in the solicitation and the contract [@CL-CMP-7820]. A firm-fixed-price purchase of commercial services worth 1,800,000 is over $1 million, but it falls within the commercial exception and does not take the clause [@CL-CMP-7820]. A cost-reimbursement contract worth 900,000 is not over $1 million, so the prescription does not call for the clause [@CL-CMP-7820].

On a DoD contract, the auditor and the contractor cannot agree on the indirect cost rates after the audit questions 120,000 of cost. The auditor issues a DCAA Form 1 notice of costs suspended or disapproved, and the advisory report still goes to the administrative contracting officer [@CL-CMP-7822]. The answer: the dollar threshold and the two exceptions decide which contract carries the clause, and a failed agreement adds the notice to the advisory report.

## Never confuse

The clause prescription decides whether a solicitation or contract carries the penalties clause [@CL-CMP-7820]. The auditor's notice is a separate step that follows a failure to reach agreement and is issued in addition to the advisory report [@CL-CMP-7822].

Codified FAR 42.709-7 prescribes the penalties clause [@CL-CMP-7820], and the same prescription is numbered FAR 42.508-7 in the RFO text [@CL-CMP-0125].
