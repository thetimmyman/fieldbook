## Purpose

Sets out the three duties the policy section places on contractors and subcontractors for qualified individuals with disabilities, names who each duty falls on, and states what the section leaves to other texts.

## The rule

FAR 22.1401 states what contractors and subcontractors are required to do when entering into contracts and subcontracts subject to the Act [@CL-CMP-8158]. Under FAR 22.1400, the subpart prescribes policies and procedures for implementing section 503 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended (section 793 of title 29 of the United States Code) (the Act), Executive Order 11758, January 15, 1974, and the regulations of the Secretary of Labor (41 CFR part 60-741) [@CL-CMP-8823]. In the subpart, the terms contract and contractor include subcontract and subcontractor [@CL-CMP-8824].

Paragraph (a): they must take affirmative action to employ, and advance in employment, qualified individuals with disabilities, and must otherwise treat qualified individuals without discrimination based on their physical or mental disability [@CL-CMP-8159].

Paragraph (b): they must undertake appropriate outreach and positive recruitment activities that are reasonably designed to effectively recruit qualified individuals with disabilities [@CL-CMP-8160].

Paragraph (c): they must compare the utilization of individuals with disabilities in their workforces to the utilization goal, as prescribed in the regulations of the Secretary of Labor, on an annual basis [@CL-CMP-8161].

## How it works

When entering into contracts and subcontracts subject to the Act, contractors and subcontractors are required to meet all three requirements in paragraphs (a), (b), and (c) of FAR 22.1401 [@CL-CMP-8158; @CL-CMP-8159; @CL-CMP-8160; @CL-CMP-8161].

Under paragraph (a), contractors and subcontractors must take affirmative action to employ, and advance in employment, qualified individuals with disabilities, and otherwise treat qualified individuals without discrimination based on their physical or mental disability [@CL-CMP-8159]. Under paragraph (b), they must undertake appropriate outreach and positive recruitment activities that are reasonably designed to effectively recruit qualified individuals with disabilities [@CL-CMP-8160].

Under paragraph (c), they must compare the utilization of individuals with disabilities in their workforces to the utilization goal, as prescribed in the regulations of the Secretary of Labor, on an annual basis [@CL-CMP-8161].

For the disability equal opportunity clause, see [Workers with disabilities and the statement of equivalent rates for Federal hires](cmp-workers-with-disabilities-and-equivalent-rates.md). For waivers, see [Forced or indentured child labor list and waivers for workers with disabilities](cmp-forced-child-labor-list-and-disability-waivers.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor enters into a contract subject to the Act and has a workforce of 400 employees. It counts 16 individuals with disabilities in that workforce, and the goal in the Secretary of Labor's regulations is assumed to be 7 percent. Sixteen of 400 is 4 percent, so utilization is below the assumed goal.

The contractor compares the utilization of individuals with disabilities in its workforce to the utilization goal on an annual basis, as paragraph (c) requires [@CL-CMP-8161].

The contractor must also take affirmative action to employ and advance in employment qualified individuals with disabilities and treat them without discrimination under paragraph (a), and undertake appropriate outreach and positive recruitment activities under paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-8159; @CL-CMP-8160].

## Never confuse

FAR 22.1401 requires all three duties: taking affirmative action to employ and advance in employment qualified individuals with disabilities and treating them without discrimination based on physical or mental disability under paragraph (a), undertaking appropriate outreach and positive recruitment activities reasonably designed to effectively recruit qualified individuals with disabilities under paragraph (b), and comparing workforce utilization of individuals with disabilities to the utilization goal prescribed in the regulations of the Secretary of Labor on an annual basis under paragraph (c) [@CL-CMP-8158; @CL-CMP-8159; @CL-CMP-8160; @CL-CMP-8161].
