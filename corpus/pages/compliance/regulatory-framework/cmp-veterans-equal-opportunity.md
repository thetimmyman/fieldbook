## Purpose

Defines the main veteran categories, states the duty to list openings, and names the veterans clause, the reporting clause and the reporting provision with their thresholds.

The subpart prescribes policies and procedures for implementing the Vietnam Era Veterans' Readjustment Assistance Act of 1972 (sections 4211 and 4212 of title 38 of the United States Code), called the Act, and the Veterans Employment Opportunities Act of 1998 (Public Law 105-339) [@CL-CMP-8508], together with the Jobs for Veterans Act (Public Law 107-288), Executive Order 11701 of January 24, 1973, and the regulations of the Secretary of Labor at 41 CFR parts 60-300 and 61-300 [@CL-CMP-8509].

The definition of protected veteran specifies a veteran who may be classified as a disabled veteran, a recently separated veteran, an active duty wartime or campaign badge veteran, or an Armed Forces service medal veteran, as defined in the definitions section [@CL-CMP-3083]. A recently separated veteran is any veteran during the three-year period beginning on the date of the veteran's discharge or release from active duty in the military, ground, naval, or air service of the United States [@CL-CMP-3084]. The same section also defines the other listed terms, and this card does not set them out.

Contractors and subcontractors entering into contracts and subcontracts subject to the Act are required to list all employment openings with the appropriate employment service delivery system where the opening occurs, except for listed positions [@CL-CMP-3085]. The positions excepted from the duty to list employment openings are executive and senior management positions, positions to be filled from within the contractor's organization, and positions lasting three days or less [@CL-CMP-3142]. The Equal Opportunity for Veterans clause is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts if the expected value is $200,000 or more, except in the listed cases [@CL-CMP-3086]. One listed case in which the veterans clause is not inserted is work performed outside the United States by employees recruited outside the United States [@CL-CMP-3087]. Another is that the Director of the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs has waived, or the head of the agency has waived, all of the terms of the clause [@CL-CMP-3088]. If the Director of the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs or the head of the agency waives one or more, but not all, of the terms of the clause, the basic clause is to be used with its Alternate I [@CL-CMP-3137]. The Employment Reports on Veterans clause is to be inserted in solicitations and contracts containing the Equal Opportunity for Veterans clause [@CL-CMP-3089]. The Compliance with Veterans' Employment Reporting Requirements provision is to be inserted in solicitations when it is anticipated the contract award will exceed the simplified acquisition threshold and the contract is not for acquisition of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-3090].

## How it works

The veterans clause goes in when the expected value reaches the threshold and neither listed exception applies [@CL-CMP-3086; @CL-CMP-3087; @CL-CMP-3088]. The reporting clause goes in solicitations and contracts containing the veterans clause [@CL-CMP-3089]. The reporting provision depends on the simplified acquisition threshold and on the contract not being for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-3090], and the figure for that threshold is on [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md). If performance under the veterans clause may necessitate a revision of a collective bargaining agreement, the contracting officer must advise the affected labor unions that the Department of Labor will give them appropriate opportunity to present their views [@CL-CMP-8589]. However, neither the contracting officer nor any representative of the contracting officer may discuss with the contractor or any labor representative any aspect of the collective bargaining agreement [@CL-CMP-8747]. Following agency procedures, the contracting office must forward any complaints received about the administration of the Act to the Veterans' Employment and Training Service of the Department of Labor, or to the Director, Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs (OFCCP), or to any OFCCP regional, district, or area office or through the local Veterans' Employment Representative or designee, at the local State employment office [@CL-CMP-8748; @CL-CMP-8749]. Upon notification by the appropriate agency official, the contracting officer must take necessary action as soon as possible to implement any sanctions imposed on a contractor by the Department of Labor for violations of the veterans clause [@CL-CMP-8821]. These sanctions (see 41 CFR 60-300.66) may include withholding progress payments, termination or suspension of the contract, or debarment of the contractor [@CL-CMP-8822]. The policy section also covers affirmative action, outreach, a hiring benchmark and a bar on obligating funds when the annual report is missing, and this card does not set those out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer plans a services contract with an expected value of 250,000, to be performed in the United States, and no waiver has been granted. The answer: the veterans clause goes in because the value is above 200,000 and no listed exception applies, and the reporting clause goes in with it. A second contract with an expected value of 150,000 would not trigger the veterans clause.

## Never confuse

A protected veteran is not only a disabled veteran: the definition lists disabled, recently separated, active duty wartime or campaign badge, and Armed Forces service medal veterans [@CL-CMP-3083]. The reporting clause and the reporting provision are also different. The clause goes into solicitations and contracts containing the veterans clause [@CL-CMP-3089], and the provision goes into solicitations when the award is anticipated to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold and the contract is not for acquisition of commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-3090].
