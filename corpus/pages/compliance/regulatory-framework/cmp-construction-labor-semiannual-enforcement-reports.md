## Purpose

Shows which agency must report on the enforcement of construction labor standards, which periods the report covers, what it must contain and when it is due.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8002] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.406-13 [@CL-CMP-8002] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8002] |
| Applies to | Each contracting agency, on the construction labor standards requirements of the two statutes [@CL-CMP-8002] |

A semiannual report on compliance with and enforcement of the construction labor standards requirements of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements statute and the Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards statute is required from each contracting agency [@CL-CMP-8002].

The reporting periods are October 1 through March 31 and April 1 through September 30 [@CL-CMP-8003].

The reports shall contain only information as to the enforcement actions of the contracting agency and shall be prepared as prescribed in Department of Labor memoranda and submitted to the Department of Labor within 30 days after the end of the reporting period [@CL-CMP-8004].

The report has been assigned interagency report control number 1482-DOL-SA [@CL-CMP-8021].

## How it works

Each contracting agency is required to submit a semiannual report on compliance with and enforcement of the construction labor standards requirements of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements statute and the Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards statute [@CL-CMP-8002]. The reports shall be prepared as prescribed in Department of Labor memoranda and submitted to the Department of Labor within 30 days after the end of the reporting period [@CL-CMP-8004]. The two reporting periods are October 1 through March 31 and April 1 through September 30 [@CL-CMP-8003], and the reports shall contain only information as to the enforcement actions of the contracting agency [@CL-CMP-8004]. See [Agency programs for enforcing construction labor standards](cmp-construction-labor-standards-enforcement.md) and [Construction payrolls, compliance statements and investigations](cmp-construction-payrolls-statements-and-investigations.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A reporting period ends on March 31. The report is due within 30 days after that date, which is April 30. The next period runs April 1 through September 30. Its report is due within 30 days after September 30, which is October 30. Each report lists only the agency's own enforcement actions and follows the Department of Labor memoranda.

## Never confuse

The semiannual report is required from each contracting agency and shall contain only information as to the enforcement actions of the contracting agency, prepared as prescribed in Department of Labor memoranda [@CL-CMP-8002; @CL-CMP-8004].

The reporting periods are October 1 through March 31 and April 1 through September 30, and each report shall be submitted to the Department of Labor within 30 days after the end of the reporting period [@CL-CMP-8003; @CL-CMP-8004].
