## Purpose

Shows which agency must report on the enforcement of construction labor standards, which periods the report covers, what it must contain and when it is due.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8002] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.406-13 [@CL-CMP-8002] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8002] |
| Applies to | Each contracting agency, on the construction labor standards requirements of the two statutes [@CL-CMP-8002] |

A semiannual report on compliance with and enforcement of the construction labor standards requirements of the Construction Wage Rate Requirements statute and the Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards statute is required from each contracting agency [@CL-CMP-8002].

The reporting periods are October 1 through March 31 and April 1 through September 30 [@CL-CMP-8003].

The reports must contain only information as to the enforcement actions of the contracting agency [@CL-CMP-8004]. They must be prepared as prescribed in Department of Labor memoranda [@CL-CMP-8004]. They must be submitted to the Department of Labor within 30 days after the end of the reporting period [@CL-CMP-8004].

The report has been assigned interagency report control number 1482-DOL-SA [@CL-CMP-8021].

## How it works

Who must file. The duty falls on each contracting agency [@CL-CMP-8002]. The section does not name an office inside the agency. It states no duty on a contractor.

Who decides the form. The Department of Labor does, through its memoranda [@CL-CMP-8004].

Deadline. Each report is submitted to the Department of Labor within 30 days after the end of its reporting period [@CL-CMP-8004]. The two periods are October 1 through March 31 and April 1 through September 30 [@CL-CMP-8003].

Content limit. A report holds only the contracting agency's own enforcement actions [@CL-CMP-8004].

Not stated here. The section does not state a consequence for a late report. The linked pages cover the agency program for enforcing construction labor standards and the contractor's payroll records.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A reporting period ends on March 31. The report is due within 30 days after that date, which is April 30 [@CL-CMP-8004]. The next period runs April 1 through September 30 [@CL-CMP-8003]. Its report is due within 30 days after September 30, which is October 30 [@CL-CMP-8004]. Each report lists only the agency's own enforcement actions and follows the Department of Labor memoranda [@CL-CMP-8004].

## Never confuse

Never confuse this agency report with a contractor's own labor standards records. The semiannual report contains only the contracting agency's enforcement actions, prepared as the Department of Labor prescribes [@CL-CMP-8004].

Never confuse the two reporting periods with the calendar year. The first runs from October 1 through March 31 [@CL-CMP-8003].
