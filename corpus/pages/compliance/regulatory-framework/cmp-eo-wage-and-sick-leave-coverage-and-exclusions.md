## Purpose

Shows which contracts the executive order minimum wage subpart covers and which employees the paid sick leave subpart excludes.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3295] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.1903, FAR 22.2101 and FAR 22.2104 [@CL-CMP-3295; @CL-CMP-3373; @CL-CMP-3298] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3295] |
| Applies to | Contracts covered by the Service Contract Labor Standards statute or the Wage Rate Requirements (Construction) statute, with the coverage sentence's reference to performance in whole or in part within the United States as the coverage sentence defines it [@CL-CMP-3295; @CL-CMP-3296; @CL-CMP-8208] |

The minimum wage subpart applies to contracts covered by the Service Contract Labor Standards statute [@CL-CMP-3295], or by the Wage Rate Requirements (Construction) statute [@CL-CMP-3296]. The coverage sentence refers to contracts that require performance in whole or in part within the United States [@CL-CMP-3296]. The sentence's parenthetical defines the United States as the 50 States, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the United States Virgin Islands, Johnston Island, Wake Island and the outer Continental Shelf as defined in the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act [@CL-CMP-8208]. When performance is in part within and in part outside the United States, the subpart applies to the part of the contract that is performed within the United States [@CL-CMP-3297]. The subpart applies to workers as defined in section 22.1901, and under that definition workers are covered regardless of the contractual relationship alleged to exist between the contractor or subcontractor and the worker [@CL-CMP-3371].

The paid sick leave subpart excludes employees performing in connection with contracts covered by the executive order for less than 20 percent of their work hours in a given workweek [@CL-CMP-3298]. An employee performs in connection with a contract if the employee's work activities are necessary to the performance of a contract but are not the specific services called for by the contract [@CL-CMP-3373]. That exclusion is inapplicable to employees performing on covered contracts, meaning those directly engaged in performing the specific work called for by the contract, at any point during the workweek [@CL-CMP-3299]. The Government's unilateral exercise of a pre-negotiated option to renew an existing contract that does not contain the clause at 52.222-62 will not automatically trigger the application of that clause [@CL-CMP-3372]. Paid sick leave means compensated absence from employment that is required by Executive Order 13706 and part 13 of title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations [@CL-CMP-3374].

## How it works

Coverage starts with the statute that governs the contract and with where the work is performed [@CL-CMP-3295; @CL-CMP-3296; @CL-CMP-3297]. The paid sick leave exclusion depends on the share of an employee's work hours spent in connection with the contract in a workweek [@CL-CMP-3298; @CL-CMP-3373]. An employee directly engaged in the specific work called for by the contract is not excluded at any point in the workweek [@CL-CMP-3299]. The printed sections also cover further minimum wage exclusions, the other paid sick leave definitions and the collective bargaining agreement exclusion, and this card does not draft them.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A services contract covered by the Service Contract Labor Standards statute is performed 60 percent in the United States and 40 percent abroad. The answer: the minimum wage subpart applies to the part performed in the United States. An office worker spends 10 percent of one week's hours in connection with the contract and never works directly on the specific work called for. The answer: that worker is excluded from paid sick leave for that week. If the worker directly performs the specific work for one hour that week, the exclusion is inapplicable.

## Never confuse

Contract coverage and employee exclusion answer different questions: the first sets which contracts are covered [@CL-CMP-3295; @CL-CMP-3296], and the second removes some employees from paid sick leave coverage [@CL-CMP-3298]. Working in connection with a contract is not the same as directly performing its specific work [@CL-CMP-3373; @CL-CMP-3299]. The rate notice, enforcement and accrual rules are on [Executive order minimum wage and paid sick leave](cmp-eo-minimum-wage-and-paid-sick-leave.md). The clause and wage determination steps are on [Service contract labor standards clause and wage determinations](../../operating-model/talent/opm-service-contract-labor-standards.md). Neither is restated here.
