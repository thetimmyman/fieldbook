## Purpose

Shows the conditions an offeror certifies to exempt equipment maintenance, calibration or repair from Service Contract Labor Standards, and what follows when the exemption fails.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-4832] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-48 and FAR 52.222-51 [@CL-CMP-4972; @CL-CMP-4983] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-48 [@CL-CMP-4639; @CL-CMP-4640; @CL-CMP-9796; @CL-CMP-9797; @CL-CMP-9798; @CL-CMP-4952; @CL-CMP-4953; @CL-CMP-5006; @CL-CMP-9799; @CL-CMP-9800; @CL-CMP-9801; @CL-CMP-5007]; RFO FAR 52.222-51 [@CL-CMP-5008; @CL-CMP-5009; @CL-CMP-5010; @CL-CMP-5011; @CL-CMP-5012; @CL-CMP-5013; @CL-CMP-5014] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4832] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts for maintenance, calibration or repair of equipment that meets the certified exemption conditions [@CL-CMP-4832; @CL-CMP-4972] |

The offeror checks a certification that it does or does not certify to the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-4972]. The first condition is that the equipment to be serviced is used regularly for other than Government purposes and is sold or traded by the offeror, or by the subcontractor in the case of an exempt subcontractor, in substantial quantities to the general public in the course of normal business operations [@CL-CMP-4832]. The second is that the services are furnished at prices that are, or are based on, established catalog or market prices for the maintenance, calibration or repair of equipment [@CL-CMP-4833]. The third is that the compensation plan for all service employees performing work under the contract is the same as that used for these employees and equivalent employees servicing the same equipment of commercial customers [@CL-CMP-4834].

An established catalog price is a price included in a catalog, price list, schedule or other form that is regularly maintained by the manufacturer or the offeror and is published or otherwise available for inspection by customers [@CL-CMP-4973]. It also states prices at which sales currently, or were last, made to a significant number of buyers constituting the general public [@CL-CMP-4974]. An established market price is a current price, established in the usual course of trade between buyers and sellers free to bargain, which can be substantiated from sources independent of the manufacturer or offeror [@CL-CMP-4975].

Certification by the offeror about its compliance with respect to the contract also certifies the compliance of a subcontractor if the offeror subcontracts out the exempt services [@CL-CMP-4835]. The requirements clause states the same three conditions as contract terms [@CL-CMP-4983; @CL-CMP-4984; @CL-CMP-4985].

## How it works

If the offeror certifies to the conditions, the contracting officer determines under the FAR whether the Service Contract Labor Standards statute applies to the offeror [@CL-CMP-4976]. If the contracting officer determines that the statute will not apply, the Service Contract Labor Standards clause in the solicitation is not included in any resultant contract to that offeror [@CL-CMP-4977]. If the contracting officer determines that the statute will apply, the requirements clause for the equipment exemption is not included in any resultant contract awarded to that offeror [@CL-CMP-4978; @CL-CMP-4979], and the offeror may be provided an opportunity to submit a new offer on that basis [@CL-CMP-4979].

If the offeror does not certify to the conditions [@CL-CMP-4980], the requirements clause is not included in any resultant contract awarded to that offeror [@CL-CMP-4981]. The offeror must notify the contracting officer as soon as possible if the contracting officer did not attach a Service Contract Labor Standards wage determination to the solicitation [@CL-CMP-4982]. The contracting officer may not make an award to the offeror if the offeror fails to execute the certification or to contact the contracting officer as the provision requires [@CL-CMP-4836].

Under the requirements clause the contractor is responsible for its subcontractors' compliance with all the conditions of the exemption, and it must determine whether the exemption applies to any subcontract on or before subcontract award [@CL-CMP-4837]. In judging that the exemption applies, the contractor must consider all factors and make an affirmative determination that all of the conditions in the first three paragraphs of the clause will be met [@CL-CMP-4986]. If the Department of Labor determines that any of those conditions has not been met, the exemption is deemed inapplicable and the contract becomes subject to the Service Contract Labor Standards statute [@CL-CMP-4838]. The procedures in the Department of Labor regulations that the clause names will then be followed [@CL-CMP-4987]. The contractor must include the substance of the requirements clause, including its flowdown paragraph, in subcontracts for exempt services under the contract [@CL-CMP-4839].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO certification provision keeps the three certified conditions on the equipment, the prices and the compensation plan [@CL-CMP-4639; @CL-CMP-4640; @CL-CMP-4952; @CL-CMP-4832; @CL-CMP-4833; @CL-CMP-4834], and the rule that the offeror's certification also certifies a subcontractor's compliance if the offeror subcontracts out the exempt services [@CL-CMP-4953; @CL-CMP-4835]. It keeps the bar on award where the offeror fails to execute the certification or to contact the contracting officer as the provision requires [@CL-CMP-5007; @CL-CMP-4836]. It says the contracting officer's determination whether the statute applies is made in accordance with FAR 22.1002-1(e)(3), where the codified provision cites FAR 22.1003-4(c)(3) [@CL-CMP-5006; @CL-CMP-4976]. It keeps the definitions of an established catalog price and an established market price [@CL-CMP-9796; @CL-CMP-9797; @CL-CMP-9798; @CL-CMP-4973; @CL-CMP-4974; @CL-CMP-4975]. It keeps the outcomes of that determination: if the statute will not apply, the Service Contract Labor Standards clause in the solicitation will not be included in any resultant contract to the offeror [@CL-CMP-9799; @CL-CMP-4977], and if it will apply, the clause at 52.222-51 will not be included in any resultant contract awarded to the offeror, which may be provided an opportunity to submit a new offer on that basis [@CL-CMP-9800; @CL-CMP-9801; @CL-CMP-4978; @CL-CMP-4979]. The RFO requirements clause keeps the same three conditions as contract terms [@CL-CMP-5008; @CL-CMP-5009; @CL-CMP-5010; @CL-CMP-4983; @CL-CMP-4984; @CL-CMP-4985], the contractor's responsibility for its subcontractors' compliance and its duty to determine whether the exemption applies on or before subcontract award [@CL-CMP-5011; @CL-CMP-4837], and the affirmative determination that all of the conditions in the first three paragraphs will be met [@CL-CMP-5012; @CL-CMP-4986]. It keeps the rule that the exemption is deemed inapplicable, and the contract becomes subject to the statute, if the Department of Labor determines that a condition has not been met [@CL-CMP-5013; @CL-CMP-4838], and the flowdown of the substance of the clause in subcontracts for exempt services [@CL-CMP-5014; @CL-CMP-4839].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor services laboratory instruments and sells the same models in volume to commercial laboratories. Assume the instruments fall in a listed equipment category. Its service prices come from a published catalog available to any customer, and service staff are paid on the same plan as staff serving commercial customers. The offeror checks the box that it does certify the conditions. If the Department of Labor later finds that the pay plan for service staff differs from the one used for commercial customers, the exemption is deemed inapplicable and the contract becomes subject to the Service Contract Labor Standards statute.

## Never confuse

This equipment exemption is not the services exemption on [exemption for certain services](cmp-scls-services-exemption.md), which adds a limit on the share of each employee's time spent on the government contract [@CL-CMP-4842]. The equipment categories and the administrative exemptions by contract type are on [administrative exemptions](cmp-scls-exemptions-and-variations.md). This card covers the certification and the requirements clause.
