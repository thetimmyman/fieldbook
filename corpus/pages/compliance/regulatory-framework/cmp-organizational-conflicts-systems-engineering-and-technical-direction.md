## Purpose

Shows when a company that designs or directs a system is barred from supplying it, and how a solicitation and clause express the resulting restraint.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7200] |
| Codified citation | FAR 9.505-1, 9.503, 9.507-1 and 9.507-2 [@CL-CMP-7200; @CL-CMP-7380; @CL-CMP-7205; @CL-CMP-7207] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7200] |
| Applies to | Contractors that provide systems engineering and technical direction without overall contractual responsibility [@CL-CMP-7200] |

A contractor that provides systems engineering and technical direction for a system, without overall contractual responsibility for its development, its integration, assembly and checkout, or its production, may not be awarded a contract to supply the system [@CL-CMP-7200]. The bar covers the system's major components as well, and the contractor may not be a subcontractor or consultant to a supplier of the system or any of its major components [@CL-CMP-7201]. The agency head or a designee may waive any general rule or procedure of the subpart by determining that its application in a particular situation would not be in the Government's interest [@CL-CMP-7380]. Any request for a waiver must be in writing, must set forth the extent of the conflict and requires approval by the agency head or a designee [@CL-CMP-7381].

Significant potential organizational conflicts of interest are normally resolved by imposing some restraint, appropriate to the nature of the conflict, upon the contractor's eligibility for future contracts or subcontracts [@CL-CMP-7305]. An affected solicitation must therefore contain a provision that invites offerors' attention to the subpart, states the nature of the potential conflict as the contracting officer sees it, and states the nature of the proposed restraint upon future contractor activities [@CL-CMP-7205]. Depending on the nature of the acquisition, the provision must also state whether the terms of any proposed clause and the application of the subpart to the contract are subject to negotiation [@CL-CMP-7206].

If, as a condition of award, the contractor's eligibility for future prime contract or subcontract awards will be restricted or the contractor must agree to some other restraint, the solicitation must contain a proposed clause that specifies both the nature and the duration of the proposed restraint [@CL-CMP-7207].

## How it works

Systems engineering includes a combination of substantially all of these activities: determining specifications, identifying and resolving interface problems, developing test requirements, evaluating test data and supervising design [@CL-CMP-7202]. Technical direction includes a combination of substantially all of these activities: developing work statements, determining parameters, directing other contractors' operations and resolving technical controversies [@CL-CMP-7203].

A contractor performing these activities occupies a highly influential and responsible position in determining a system's basic concepts and supervising their execution by other contractors, so it should not be in a position to make decisions favoring its own products or capabilities [@CL-CMP-7204].

The contracting officer must include the clause in the contract, first negotiating its final terms with the successful offeror if it is appropriate to do so [@CL-CMP-7306]. The restraint must be limited to a fixed term of reasonable duration, sufficient to avoid the circumstance of unfair competitive advantage or potential bias [@CL-CMP-7208]. The period varies. It might end when the first production contract using the contractor's specifications or work statement is awarded, or it might extend through the entire life of a system for which the contractor has performed systems engineering and technical direction [@CL-CMP-7307]. In every case the restriction must specify termination by a specific date or upon the occurrence of an identifiable event [@CL-CMP-7308].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A segment determines the specifications, resolves interface problems, writes the test requirements, evaluates the test data and supervises the design of a radar. It also writes the work statements, sets the parameters, directs the other contractors' operations and resolves technical controversies. That is a combination of substantially all of the activities that make up systems engineering and technical direction [@CL-CMP-7202; @CL-CMP-7203]. The segment holds no contract with overall responsibility for the radar's development, integration, assembly and checkout, or production.

Unless the agency head or a designee waives the rule, the segment may not be awarded a 2,000,000 contract to supply the radar, or to supply the radar's antenna, which is one of its major components [@CL-CMP-7200; @CL-CMP-7201; @CL-CMP-7380]. It may not act as a subcontractor or consultant to the antenna supplier either [@CL-CMP-7201].

A separate follow-on solicitation for the radar's production would carry a provision that invites offerors' attention to the subpart, states the conflict as the contracting officer sees it and states the proposed restraint [@CL-CMP-7205]. If award were conditioned on a restraint on future eligibility, the solicitation would contain a proposed clause stating its nature and duration, with a fixed term that ends on a specific date or on an identifiable event, such as the award of the first production contract [@CL-CMP-7207; @CL-CMP-7208; @CL-CMP-7307; @CL-CMP-7308].

## Never confuse

The bar in the rule on systems engineering and technical direction is not the same as a restraint written into a solicitation clause. The bar attaches to a contractor that provides those services without overall contractual responsibility, while a clause states the nature and duration of a restraint on future eligibility [@CL-CMP-7200; @CL-CMP-7207].

## Company calibration

Whether any segment would hold a systems engineering and technical direction role on a system that it or an affiliate might later supply [@Q-003].
