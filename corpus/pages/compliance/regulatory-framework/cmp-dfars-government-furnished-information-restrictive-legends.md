## Purpose

Shows what a contractor may do with Government-furnished technical data and computer software that carries a restrictive legend, the nondisclosure agreement, and the extra duties of a covered Government support contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3942] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7025 [@CL-CMP-3942] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3942] |
| Applies to | Technical data or computer software provided to the Contractor as Government-furnished information under the contract [@CL-CMP-3978] |

The clause addresses technical data or computer software provided to the Contractor as Government-furnished information under the contract, which may be subject to restrictions on use, modification, reproduction, release, performance, display, or further disclosure [@CL-CMP-3978]. The Contractor shall use, modify, reproduce, perform, or display technical data received from the Government with limited rights legends, computer software received with restricted rights legends, or SBIR/STTR technical data or computer software received with SBIR/STTR data rights legends (during the SBIR/STTR data protection period) only in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-8633; @CL-CMP-3942]. The Contractor shall not, without the express written permission of the party whose name appears in the legend, release or disclose such data or software to any unauthorized person [@CL-CMP-3942; @CL-CMP-3943].

The Contractor shall use technical data or computer software received from the Government with government purpose rights legends for government purposes only [@CL-CMP-3944]. Under the same paragraph, the Contractor shall not, without the express written permission of the party whose name appears in the restrictive legend, use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, or display such data or software for any commercial purpose, or disclose such data or software to a person other than its subcontractors, suppliers, or prospective subcontractors or suppliers who require the data or software to submit offers for, or perform, contracts under the contract [@CL-CMP-3979; @CL-CMP-7388]. Before disclosing the data or software, the Contractor shall require the persons to whom disclosure will be made to complete and sign the nondisclosure agreement at DFARS 227.7103-7 [@CL-CMP-3945].

A covered Government support contractor receiving technical data or computer software marked with restrictive legends pursuant to paragraphs (b)(1)(ii), (b)(3)(ii), or (b)(4)(ii) of the clause further agrees and acknowledges the obligations that follow [@CL-CMP-3946]. One listed obligation is that the data or software will be accessed and used for the sole purpose of furnishing independent and impartial advice or technical assistance directly to the Government in support of its management and oversight of the program or effort to which the data or software relates, as stated in the contract [@CL-CMP-3947; @CL-CMP-3948]. The same listed obligation adds that the data or software shall not be used to compete for any Government or non-Government contract [@CL-CMP-3948]. Another is that the Contractor will ensure the party named in the legend is notified of the access or use within 30 days [@CL-CMP-3949].

## How it works

Information marked with a limited rights or restricted rights legend, and SBIR/STTR information marked with an SBIR/STTR data rights legend during the SBIR/STTR data protection period, is to be used only in the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-8633; @CL-CMP-3942]. Information marked with a government purpose rights legend is to be used for government purposes only [@CL-CMP-3944]. For information with a government purpose rights legend, the Contractor shall require the persons to whom disclosure will be made to complete and sign the nondisclosure agreement before disclosure [@CL-CMP-3945]. A covered Government support contractor receiving marked technical data or computer software further agrees and acknowledges the obligations that follow [@CL-CMP-3946]. For contracts in which the Government will furnish technical data, the term covered Government support contractor is defined in DFARS 252.227-7013 [@CL-CMP-8634].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor receives Government-furnished technical data marked with a limited rights legend. It uses the data to perform the contract and does not release it to a supplier without the written permission of the party named in the legend. A second item arrives marked with a government purpose rights legend. The contractor may show it to a prospective subcontractor who needs it to bid, after that subcontractor signs the nondisclosure agreement.

## Never confuse

For the Government's rights in data the contractor delivers, see [Rights in data, general: definitions and allocation](cmp-rights-in-data-general-definitions-and-allocation.md) and [Rights in data, general: copyright, withholding and markings](cmp-rights-in-data-general-copyright-withholding-and-markings.md). For the rights clause for SBIR and STTR data, see [DoD SBIR and STTR data rights: definitions, protection period and markings](cmp-dfars-sbir-sttr-data-rights-definitions-and-period.md).
