## Purpose

Shows what a contractor may do with Government-furnished technical data and computer software that carries a restrictive legend, the nondisclosure agreement, and the extra duties of a covered Government support contractor.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3942] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7025 [@CL-CMP-3942] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3942] |
| Applies to | Technical data or computer software provided to the Contractor as Government-furnished information under the contract [@CL-CMP-3978] |

The clause addresses technical data or computer software provided to the Contractor as Government-furnished information under the contract, which may be subject to restrictions on use, modification, reproduction, release, performance, display, or further disclosure [@CL-CMP-3978]. The Contractor shall use only in the performance of the contract technical data received from the Government with limited rights legends, computer software received with restricted rights legends, and SBIR/STTR technical data or computer software received with SBIR/STTR data rights legends during the SBIR/STTR data protection period [@CL-CMP-3942]. The Contractor shall not, without the express written permission of the party whose name appears in the legend, release or disclose such data or software to any unauthorized person [@CL-CMP-3942; @CL-CMP-3943].

The Contractor shall use technical data or computer software received from the Government with government purpose rights legends for government purposes only [@CL-CMP-3944]. Under the same paragraph, the Contractor shall not, without the express written permission of the party whose name appears in the restrictive legend, use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, or display such data or software for any commercial purpose [@CL-CMP-3979]. Before disclosing that data or software to persons who require it to submit offers for, or perform, contracts under the contract, the Contractor shall require them to complete and sign the nondisclosure agreement at DFARS 227.7103-7 [@CL-CMP-3945].

A covered Government support contractor receiving marked technical data or computer software under the listed paragraphs further agrees and acknowledges the listed obligations [@CL-CMP-3946]. One listed obligation is that the data or software will be accessed and used for the sole purpose of furnishing independent and impartial advice or technical assistance directly to the Government in support of its management and oversight of the program or effort [@CL-CMP-3947]. Another is that it shall not be used to compete for any Government or non-Government contract [@CL-CMP-3948]. Another is that the Contractor will ensure the party named in the legend is notified of the access or use within 30 days [@CL-CMP-3949].

## How it works

The kind of legend decides the limits on use and disclosure [@CL-CMP-3942; @CL-CMP-3944]. For information with a government purpose rights legend, the Contractor requires the persons to whom disclosure will be made to sign the nondisclosure agreement before disclosure [@CL-CMP-3945]. A covered Government support contractor takes on added duties on top of those limits [@CL-CMP-3946]. The clause points to the rights clauses for the definition of a covered Government support contractor. This card does not set out the paragraphs on specially negotiated licenses, commercial restrictive legends, indemnification or employees' obligations.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor receives Government-furnished technical data marked with a limited rights legend. It uses the data to perform the contract and does not release it to a supplier without the written permission of the party named in the legend. A second item arrives marked with a government purpose rights legend. The contractor may show it to a prospective subcontractor who needs it to bid, after that subcontractor signs the nondisclosure agreement.

## Never confuse

This clause limits what the contractor does with information the Government furnishes. It does not set the Government's rights in data the contractor delivers, which are covered on [Rights in data, general: definitions and allocation](cmp-rights-in-data-general-definitions-and-allocation.md) and [Rights in data, general: copyright, withholding and markings](cmp-rights-in-data-general-copyright-withholding-and-markings.md). A government purpose rights legend also differs from a limited rights legend: the first allows use for government purposes, with the nondisclosure agreement required before disclosure, and the second restricts use to the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-3944; @CL-CMP-3945; @CL-CMP-3942]. The rights clause for SBIR and STTR data, which defines the SBIR/STTR legend, is covered on [DoD SBIR and STTR data rights: definitions, protection period and markings](cmp-dfars-sbir-sttr-data-rights-definitions-and-period.md).
