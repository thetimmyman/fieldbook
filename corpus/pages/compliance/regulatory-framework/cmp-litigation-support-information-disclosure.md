## Purpose

Explains what counts as litigation information, sensitive information and the related technical terms, the policy on releasing sensitive information to a litigation support contractor, and the two clauses that carry the rules into solicitations and contracts.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7953] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 204.7401 [@CL-CMP-7949], DFARS 204.7402 [@CL-CMP-7953] and DFARS 204.7403 [@CL-CMP-7957; @CL-CMP-7959] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7949] |
| Applies to | Releases of litigation information that includes sensitive information to litigation support contractors, and solicitations and contracts that involve litigation support services [@CL-CMP-7953; @CL-CMP-7957; @CL-CMP-7959] |

Litigation information is any information, including sensitive information, that is furnished to the contractor by or on behalf of the Government, or that is generated or obtained by the contractor in performing litigation support under a contract [@CL-CMP-7949]. It does not include information that is lawfully, publicly available without restriction, including information in a publicly available solicitation [@CL-CMP-7950]. Litigation support means administrative, technical or professional services provided in support of the Government during or in anticipation of litigation [@CL-CMP-8433].

A litigation support contractor is a contractor, including its experts, technical consultants, subcontractors and suppliers, that provides litigation support under a contract containing the clause at 252.204-7014 [@CL-CMP-7952].

Sensitive information means controlled unclassified information of a commercial, financial, proprietary or privileged nature [@CL-CMP-7951]. It includes technical data and computer software, but not information that is lawfully, publicly available without restriction [@CL-CMP-8434]. Technical data means recorded information, regardless of the form or method of the recording, of a scientific or technical nature, including computer software documentation [@CL-CMP-8435]; it does not include computer software or data incidental to contract administration, such as financial or management information [@CL-CMP-8436]. Computer software means computer programs, source code, source code listings, object code listings, design details, algorithms, processes, flow charts, formulae and related material that would enable the software to be reproduced, recreated or recompiled [@CL-CMP-8437]; it does not include computer data bases or computer software documentation [@CL-CMP-8438].

Any release or disclosure of litigation information that includes sensitive information to a litigation support contractor, and that contractor's use and handling of the information, shall comply with the requirements of section 129d of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-7953].

To the maximum extent practicable, DoD will give notice to an offeror or contractor that information it submits, delivers or otherwise provides to DoD in connection with an offer or contract performance may be released or disclosed to litigation support contractors [@CL-CMP-7954].

Information that is publicly available without restriction, including publicly available solicitations for litigation support services, will not be protected from disclosure as litigation information [@CL-CMP-7955].

When sharing sensitive information with a litigation support contractor, contracting officers shall ensure that all other applicable requirements for handling and safeguarding the relevant types of sensitive information are included in the contract [@CL-CMP-7956]. The text illustrates these with FAR subparts 4.4 and 24.1 and DFARS subparts 204.4 and 224.1 [@CL-CMP-8439].

The clause at 252.204-7014 is used in all solicitations and contracts that involve litigation support services [@CL-CMP-7957], including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CMP-7958]. The clause at 252.204-7015 is used in solicitations and contracts that involve litigation support services, including those using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CMP-7959; @CL-CMP-8440].

## How it works

Each duty has its own party. The release, and the litigation support contractor's use and handling, must comply with the statute [@CL-CMP-7953]. The contracting officer must ensure that the other applicable handling and safeguarding requirements are in the contract [@CL-CMP-7956]. DoD, not the offeror or contractor, gives the notice, and only to the maximum extent practicable; the text sets no deadline for it [@CL-CMP-7954].

The statutory compliance rule applies only where the litigation information includes sensitive information [@CL-CMP-7953]. Litigation information that is not sensitive information is outside that sentence, although the clauses are still used wherever the acquisition involves litigation support services [@CL-CMP-7953; @CL-CMP-7957].

A subcontractor or supplier of a litigation support contractor is itself within the contractor definition [@CL-CMP-7952].

The clause at 252.204-7014 carries the limits on use or disclosure into the contract, and the clause at 252.204-7015 carries the notice of authorized disclosure [@CL-CMP-7957; @CL-CMP-7959]. The two clauses share one trigger and both reach commercial acquisitions under FAR part 12 [@CL-CMP-7958; @CL-CMP-8440].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency awards a contract for document review in support of an appeal. The contract contains the clause at 252.204-7014, so the firm is a litigation support contractor, and its subcontractor and experts fall within the definition [@CL-CMP-7952]. The clause is used because the contract involves litigation support services [@CL-CMP-7957].

The agency furnishes the firm a privileged memo that is controlled unclassified information of a privileged nature. The memo is sensitive information and, because it is furnished to the contractor by the Government, litigation information [@CL-CMP-7951; @CL-CMP-7949]. The release to the firm, and the firm's use and handling of the memo, must comply with the statute, and the contracting officer must ensure that the other applicable handling and safeguarding requirements are in the contract [@CL-CMP-7953; @CL-CMP-7956].

The agency's own publicly available solicitation for the review services is information that is publicly available without restriction, so it is not litigation information and is not protected from disclosure as such [@CL-CMP-7950; @CL-CMP-7955].

## Never confuse

Litigation information and sensitive information are different terms. Litigation information is any information furnished to the contractor by or for the Government or generated or obtained in the support work [@CL-CMP-7949], while sensitive information is controlled unclassified information of a commercial, financial, proprietary or privileged nature [@CL-CMP-7951]. The statutory rule applies to a release of litigation information that includes sensitive information [@CL-CMP-7953].

Technical data and computer software are separate terms: technical data excludes computer software, and computer software excludes software documentation, which is technical data [@CL-CMP-8435; @CL-CMP-8436; @CL-CMP-8438].

Public availability removes information from both definitions and from protection as litigation information [@CL-CMP-7950; @CL-CMP-7955; @CL-CMP-8434].

These clauses are not the cyber incident rules; see [Cyber incident information and litigation support limits](../trade-cyber/cmp-cyber-incident-information-and-litigation-support-limits.md).
