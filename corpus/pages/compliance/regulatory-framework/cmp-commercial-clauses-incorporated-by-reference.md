## Purpose

Shows the seven FAR clauses that paragraph (a) of the clause on contract terms and conditions required to implement statutes or Executive orders incorporates by reference in a commercial contract, and what the paragraph says about each.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-9039] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.212-5(a) [@CL-CMP-9039] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.212-5 is [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-0644] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-9039] |
| Applies to | Contracts in which the clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 12.301(b)(4) [@CL-CMP-9201] |

The Contractor shall comply with the FAR clauses listed in paragraph (a), which are incorporated in the contract by reference, to implement provisions of law or Executive orders applicable to acquisitions of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-CMP-9039]. Paragraph (a) lists the clauses without the words "that the Contracting Officer has indicated", which appear in paragraph (b) [@CL-CMP-9039; @CL-CMP-9045].

For each entry the paragraph gives the clause number and title and the statute that the entry cites. Paragraph (a)(1) lists 52.203-19, Prohibition on Requiring Certain Internal Confidentiality Agreements or Statements, and cites section 743 of Division E, Title VII, of the Consolidated and Further Continuing Appropriations Act, 2015 [@CL-CMP-9040]. That citation extends to the successor provisions of the section in subsequent appropriations acts, as extended in continuing resolutions [@CL-CMP-9202]. Paragraph (a)(2) lists 52.204-23, Prohibition on Contracting for Hardware, Software, and Services Developed or Provided by Kaspersky Lab Covered Entities, and cites section 1634 of Public Law 115-91 [@CL-CMP-9041]. Paragraph (a)(3) lists 52.204-25, Prohibition on Contracting for Certain Telecommunications and Video Surveillance Services or Equipment, and cites section 889(a)(1)(A) of Public Law 115-232 [@CL-CMP-9042]. Paragraph (a)(4) lists 52.209-10, Prohibition on Contracting with Inverted Domestic Corporations [@CL-CMP-9043]. Paragraph (a)(5) lists 52.232-40, Providing Accelerated Payments to Small Business Subcontractors, and cites 31 United States Code section 3903 and 10 United States Code section 3801 [@CL-CMP-9203]. Paragraph (a)(6) lists 52.233-3, Protest After Award, and cites 31 United States Code section 3553 [@CL-CMP-9044]. Paragraph (a)(7) lists 52.233-4, Applicable Law for Breach of Contract Claim, and cites Public Laws 108-77 and 108-78 [@CL-CMP-9204].

## How it works

The Contractor shall comply with the clauses listed in paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-9039], in a contract in which the clause is inserted as prescribed in 12.301(b)(4) [@CL-CMP-9201]. The paragraph gives each entry's clause number, title and the authority cited [@CL-CMP-9040; @CL-CMP-9041; @CL-CMP-9042].

Flow down to subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services is addressed in paragraph (e)(1) of the clause, where the Contractor is not required to flow down any FAR clause other than those in that paragraph [@CL-CMP-9057].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.212-5 is [Reserved], so the codified paragraph (a) list of clauses incorporated by reference has no text under that section number in the RFO text [@CL-CMP-0644; @CL-CMP-9039].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A commercial products contract includes the clause. The contractor's compliance register lists the seven entries in paragraph (a): the confidentiality agreements prohibition [@CL-CMP-9040], the Kaspersky Lab prohibition [@CL-CMP-9041], the telecommunications and video surveillance prohibition [@CL-CMP-9042], the inverted domestic corporation prohibition [@CL-CMP-9043], accelerated payments to small business subcontractors [@CL-CMP-9203], protest after award [@CL-CMP-9044], and the law for a breach of contract claim [@CL-CMP-9204]. The Contractor shall comply with each of them as a clause incorporated by reference in the contract [@CL-CMP-9039]. The register keeps a separate section for the FAR clauses in paragraph (b) that the Contracting Officer has indicated as being incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-9045].

## Never confuse

Paragraph (a) lists the FAR clauses incorporated in the contract by reference [@CL-CMP-9039], while paragraph (b) covers the FAR clauses that the Contracting Officer has indicated as being incorporated in the contract by reference [@CL-CMP-9045]. RFO FAR 52.212-5 is [Reserved] [@CL-CMP-0644], while the paragraph (a) list is in codified FAR 52.212-5 [@CL-CMP-9039].

## Company calibration

The company must confirm that its contract register records these by-reference clauses and the owner of each compliance duty [@Q-004].
