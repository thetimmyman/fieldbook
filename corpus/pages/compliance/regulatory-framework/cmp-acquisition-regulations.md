## Purpose

Explains which rule text governs a DoD contract today: the codified FAR and DFARS, the overhaul model text, or a class deviation.

## The rule

The Federal Acquisition Regulations System is established for the codification and publication of uniform policies and procedures for acquisition by all executive agencies, and consists of the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), which is the primary document, and agency acquisition regulations that implement or supplement the FAR [@CL-CMP-2000; @CL-CMP-2001]. Agency acquisition regulations implementing or supplementing the FAR are, for the military departments and defense agencies, issued subject to the authority of the Secretary of Defense [@CL-CMP-2006]. Class deviations affect more than one contract action, and for DoD, class deviations shall be controlled, processed, and approved in accordance with the Defense FAR Supplement [@CL-OPM-0024; @CL-OPM-0026]. DoD's December 19, 2025 issuance announces the initial release of the Department's Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Phase 1 class deviations, consisting of 31 class deviations to the FAR and DFARS codified text, with the remaining class deviations being developed and planned for release in January 2026 [@CL-CMP-0696]. Effective on February 1, 2026, those class deviations retain Department-specific statutory direction and direction determined necessary for sound procurement within the streamlined RFO structure [@CL-CMP-0697], and Phase 2 of the RFO continues throughout 2026 with the issuance of proposed FAR and DFARS rules for public notice and comment [@CL-CMP-0698]. Under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52, Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762].

## How it works

Unless otherwise specified, FAR changes apply to solicitations issued on or after the effective date of the change [@CL-CMP-2012]. Contracting officers may, at their discretion, include the FAR changes in solicitations issued before the effective date, provided award of the resulting contract(s) occurs on or after the effective date, and may, at their discretion, include the changes in any existing contract with appropriate consideration [@CL-CMP-2013; @CL-CMP-2014]. Under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A new DoD solicitation asks for certified data above 12 million, while a reference book from last year quotes a lower figure. The book predates the deviation, so the solicitation's figure is current, and the book is marked as possibly stale.

## Never confuse

Class deviations affect more than one contract action and, for DoD, are controlled, processed, and approved in accordance with the Defense FAR Supplement [@CL-OPM-0024; @CL-OPM-0026], whereas Phase 2 of the RFO continues with the issuance of proposed FAR and DFARS rules for public notice and comment [@CL-CMP-0698], the opportunity to submit written comments on proposed significant revisions shall be provided by placing a notice in the Federal Register [@CL-CMP-8366], and FAR changes apply to solicitations issued on or after the effective date of the change unless otherwise specified [@CL-CMP-2012]. See the threshold changes in [Rule changes to know before Day 1](cmp-brief-rule-changes.md) and [Acquisition thresholds](cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
