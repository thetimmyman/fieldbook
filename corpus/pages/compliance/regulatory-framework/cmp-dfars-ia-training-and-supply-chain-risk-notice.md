## Purpose

Shows which contractor personnel must hold information assurance certifications, what the contractor must document, and what the supply chain risk notice tells offerors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4602; @CL-CMP-4606] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.239-7001 [@CL-CMP-4602]; DFARS 252.239-7017 [@CL-CMP-4606] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4602; @CL-CMP-4606] |
| Applies to | Contractor personnel accessing information systems to perform information assurance functions [@CL-CMP-4602], and offerors and their supply chains under the notice provision [@CL-CMP-4606] |

The contractor must ensure that personnel accessing information systems have the proper and current information assurance certification to perform information assurance functions in accordance with DoD 8570.01-M, Information Assurance Workforce Improvement Program [@CL-CMP-4602]. The Contractor shall meet the applicable information assurance certification requirements, including (1) DoD-approved information assurance workforce certifications appropriate for each category and level as listed in the current version of DoD 8570.01-M, and (2) appropriate operating system certification for information assurance technical positions as required by DoD 8570.01-M [@CL-CMP-8636; @CL-CMP-4603]. Upon request by the Government, the contractor must provide documentation supporting the certification status of personnel performing information assurance functions [@CL-CMP-4604]. Contractor personnel who do not have proper and current certifications shall be denied access to DoD information systems for the purpose of performing information assurance functions [@CL-CMP-4605].

Supply chain risk means the risk that an adversary may sabotage, maliciously introduce unwanted function, or otherwise subvert the design, integrity, manufacturing, production, distribution, installation, operation, or maintenance of a covered system [@CL-CMP-4734], so as to surveil, deny, disrupt, or otherwise degrade the function, use, or operation of that system [@CL-CMP-4782]. To manage supply chain risk, the Government may use the authorities provided by section 3252 of title 10 of the United States Code, and in exercising those authorities it may consider public and non-public information, including all-source intelligence, relating to an offeror and its supply chain [@CL-CMP-4606]. If the Government exercises the authority provided in section 3252 of title 10 of the United States Code to limit disclosure of information, no action undertaken by the Government under that authority shall be subject to review in a bid protest before the Government Accountability Office or in any Federal court [@CL-CMP-4607].

## How it works

Contractor personnel who do not have proper and current certifications shall be denied access to DoD information systems for the purpose of performing information assurance functions [@CL-CMP-4605]. Upon request by the Government, the contractor must provide documentation supporting the information assurance certification status of personnel performing information assurance functions [@CL-CMP-4604]. In the notice provision, the Government may consider information, public and non-public, including all-source intelligence, relating to an offeror and its supply chain [@CL-CMP-4606]. If the Government exercises the authority provided in section 3252 of title 10 of the United States Code to limit disclosure of information, no action undertaken by the Government under that authority shall be subject to review in a bid protest before the Government Accountability Office or in any Federal court [@CL-CMP-4607].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Assume a contractor has 40 staff on a help desk, and 12 of them perform information assurance functions. Two of the 12 have lapsed certifications. Those two are denied access to DoD information systems for the purpose of performing information assurance functions. When the Government asks, the contractor sends documentation of the certification status of all 12.
