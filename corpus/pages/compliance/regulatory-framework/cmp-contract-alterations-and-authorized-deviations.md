## Purpose

Shows how an alteration to a contract is introduced, and how a provision or clause used with an authorized deviation is marked in the solicitation or contract.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-6005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.252-4; FAR 52.252-5; FAR 52.252-6 [@CL-CMP-6005; @CL-CMP-6007; @CL-CMP-6010] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.252-4 [@CL-CMP-0996]; RFO FAR 52.252-5 [@CL-CMP-0999]; RFO FAR 52.252-6 [@CL-CMP-1014] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-6005] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that carry an alteration, and solicitations and contracts that use a provision or clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-6005; @CL-CMP-6702; @CL-CMP-6703] |

The alterations clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts to revise or supplement other parts of the contract, or parts of the solicitation that apply after contract award, except for any clause authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-6005]. It must include clear identification of what is being altered [@CL-CMP-6006]. The clause states that portions of the contract are altered as follows [@CL-CMP-6701].

When a FAR or supplemental provision or clause is used with an authorized deviation, the contracting officer identifies it by the same number, title, and date assigned to it when it is used without deviation, includes the regulation name for any supplemental provision or clause, and inserts (DEVIATION) after the date [@CL-CMP-6007; @CL-CMP-6010].

## How it works

### Alterations

The clause must include clear identification of what is being altered [@CL-CMP-6006].

### Deviations in provisions

The authorized deviations provision is inserted in solicitations that include any FAR or supplemental provision with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-6702]. The use in the solicitation of any FAR provision with an authorized deviation is indicated by the addition of (DEVIATION) after the date of the provision [@CL-CMP-6008]. The use of a supplemental provision with an authorized deviation is indicated by the addition of (DEVIATION) after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-6009].

### Deviations in clauses

The authorized deviations clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts that include any FAR or supplemental clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-6703]. The use of any FAR clause with an authorized deviation is indicated by the addition of (DEVIATION) after the date of the clause [@CL-CMP-6012]. The use of a supplemental clause with an authorized deviation is indicated by the addition of (DEVIATION) after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-6011].

### Blanks

In paragraph (b) of FAR 52.252-5 and FAR 52.252-6, the regulation name and the 48 CFR reference are blanks, with a bracketed instruction to insert the regulation name [@CL-CMP-6009; @CL-CMP-6011].

### Two statements about the marker

The opening sentence of each text says the contracting officer inserts (DEVIATION) after the date [@CL-CMP-6007; @CL-CMP-6010]. Paragraph (b) of each text says the marker for a supplemental provision or clause comes after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-6009; @CL-CMP-6011].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.252-4 keeps the rule that the alterations clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts to revise or supplement other parts of the contract, or parts of the solicitation that apply after contract award, except for any clause authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-0996; @CL-CMP-6005], and keeps the instruction to include clear identification of what is being altered [@CL-CMP-0997; @CL-CMP-6006]. The RFO clause text says that portions of the contract are altered as follows and then prints three blank lines, where the codified text ends after the colon [@CL-CMP-0998; @CL-CMP-6701]. RFO FAR 52.252-5 keeps the rule that a provision used with an authorized deviation is identified by the same number, title and date assigned to it when it is used without deviation [@CL-CMP-0999; @CL-CMP-6007], with the regulation name included for a supplemental provision and (DEVIATION) inserted after the date of the provision [@CL-CMP-1000; @CL-CMP-6007]. It also keeps the marker rules: the use of a FAR provision with an authorized deviation is indicated by the addition of (DEVIATION) after the date of the provision [@CL-CMP-1012; @CL-CMP-6008], and the use of a supplemental provision with an authorized deviation by the addition of (DEVIATION) after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-1013; @CL-CMP-6009]. RFO FAR 52.252-6 keeps the same rules for a clause used with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-1014; @CL-CMP-1015; @CL-CMP-6010], including the marker after the date of a FAR clause [@CL-CMP-1016; @CL-CMP-6012] and after the name of the regulation for a supplemental clause [@CL-CMP-1017; @CL-CMP-6011].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract carries the alterations clause, which says that portions of the contract are altered as follows. The contracting officer then writes under it that the delivery paragraph for one line item is changed, and identifies the paragraph clearly [@CL-CMP-6701; @CL-CMP-6006].

A FAR clause is used in the same contract with an authorized deviation. It keeps the number, title and date it has without the deviation, so a clause dated JAN 2020 appears with its usual number and title, and the contracting officer inserts (DEVIATION) after the date [@CL-CMP-6010; @CL-CMP-6012]. The authorized deviations clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts that include any FAR or supplemental clause with an authorized deviation [@CL-CMP-6703]. A clause from a departmental supplement carries the name of that regulation [@CL-CMP-6010]. Paragraph (b) indicates its use with an authorized deviation by the addition of (DEVIATION) after the name of the regulation [@CL-CMP-6011], while the opening sentence says the contracting officer shall insert (DEVIATION) after the date of the clause [@CL-CMP-6010].

## Never confuse

An alteration is not a deviation: the alterations clause is inserted to revise or supplement other parts of the contract, except for any clause authorized for use with a deviation [@CL-CMP-6005], while a provision or clause used with an authorized deviation is identified by the same number, title and date it has without deviation, and the contracting officer inserts (DEVIATION) after the date [@CL-CMP-6007; @CL-CMP-6010]. The alterations provision in a solicitation is covered in [Provisions and clauses incorporated by reference, and alterations in a solicitation](cmp-incorporation-by-reference-and-solicitation-alterations.md). The authorized deviations provision is inserted in solicitations [@CL-CMP-6702], and the authorized deviations clause in solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-6703].
