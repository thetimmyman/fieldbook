## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer inserts in a solicitation and contract when a project labor agreement is used for a construction project, and which alternate fits the way the agency takes the agreement.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8005] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.505 [@CL-CMP-8005] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8005] |
| Applies to | Construction projects for which a project labor agreement is used [@CL-CMP-8005] |

Both paragraphs bind the contracting officer, and both apply when a project labor agreement is used for a construction project [@CL-CMP-8005].

Provision, paragraph (a). The contracting officer must insert the provision at 52.222-33, Notice of Requirement for Project Labor Agreement, in solicitations containing the clause at 52.222-34, Project Labor Agreement [@CL-CMP-8005].

The contracting officer uses the provision:

- with Alternate I if the agency will require the submission of a project labor agreement from only the apparent successful offeror, prior to contract award [@CL-CMP-8006];
- with Alternate II if an agency allows submission of a project labor agreement after contract award, except when Alternate III is used [@CL-CMP-8007];
- with Alternate III when Alternate II of 52.222-34 is used [@CL-CMP-8008].

Clause, paragraph (b). The contracting officer must insert the clause at 52.222-34, Project Labor Agreement, in solicitations and contracts associated with the construction project [@CL-CMP-8009].

The contracting officer uses the clause:

- with Alternate I if an agency allows submission of the project labor agreement after contract award, except when Alternate II is used [@CL-CMP-8010];
- with Alternate II in IDIQ contracts when the agency will have project labor agreements negotiated on an order-by-order basis and anticipates one or more orders may not use a project labor agreement [@CL-CMP-8011].

## How it works

Who acts. The contracting officer inserts the provision and the clause and chooses the alternates [@CL-CMP-8005; @CL-CMP-8009]. The facts that pick the alternate are the agency's: whether it will require, or allows, submission, and whether it will negotiate order by order [@CL-CMP-8006; @CL-CMP-8007; @CL-CMP-8010; @CL-CMP-8011].

Where each goes. The provision goes in solicitations that contain the clause. The clause goes in solicitations and contracts associated with the construction project [@CL-CMP-8005; @CL-CMP-8009].

Pairing. Alternate III of the provision is used when Alternate II of the clause is used [@CL-CMP-8008]. Alternate II of the clause is used in IDIQ contracts when the agency will have project labor agreements negotiated on an order-by-order basis and anticipates one or more orders may not use a project labor agreement [@CL-CMP-8011]. Alternate II of the provision is not used when Alternate III is used, and Alternate I of the clause is not used when Alternate II is used [@CL-CMP-8007; @CL-CMP-8010].

Timing. Submission before award applies to the provision's Alternate I, and it covers only the apparent successful offeror [@CL-CMP-8006]. Submission after award applies to Alternate II of the provision and Alternate I of the clause, subject to the exceptions above [@CL-CMP-8007; @CL-CMP-8010].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency uses a project labor agreement on a construction project let as a single contract, not an IDIQ contract, and will allow the agreement to be submitted after award. The contracting officer inserts the provision at 52.222-33 in a solicitation that contains the clause at 52.222-34 [@CL-CMP-8005]. The clause is inserted in the solicitation and contract [@CL-CMP-8009]. Alternate II of the clause is for IDIQ contracts, so it is not used here [@CL-CMP-8011]. The clause therefore takes Alternate I [@CL-CMP-8010]. Alternate III of the provision is used when Alternate II of the clause is used, and Alternate II of the clause is not used here, so Alternate III is not used and the provision takes Alternate II [@CL-CMP-8007; @CL-CMP-8008].

Now take an IDIQ contract where the agency will negotiate agreements order by order and expects that one or more orders will not use one. The clause takes Alternate II [@CL-CMP-8011]. The provision then takes Alternate III [@CL-CMP-8008].

## Never confuse

The provision at 52.222-33 is inserted in solicitations containing the clause at 52.222-34 [@CL-CMP-8005]. The clause at 52.222-34 is inserted in solicitations and contracts associated with the construction project [@CL-CMP-8009].

For submission after contract award, the provision's Alternate II is used except when Alternate III is used [@CL-CMP-8007], and the clause's Alternate I is used except when Alternate II is used [@CL-CMP-8010]. Alternate III of the provision is used when Alternate II of 52.222-34 is used [@CL-CMP-8008].
