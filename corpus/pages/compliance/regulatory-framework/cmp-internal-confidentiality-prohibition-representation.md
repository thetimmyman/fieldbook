## Purpose

Explains the funding prohibition on contracts with entities that require certain internal confidentiality agreements, and the offeror representation that is required for contract award. The solicitation provision and contract clause are on the linked page and are not restated here.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7749; @CL-CMP-7752] |
| Codified citation | FAR 3.909-1 and FAR 3.909-2 [@CL-CMP-7749; @CL-CMP-7752] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7749; @CL-CMP-7752] |
| Applies to | The Government's use of fiscal year 2015 and subsequent fiscal year funds for a contract with the entity, and offerors seeking contract award [@CL-CMP-7749; @CL-CMP-7752] |

The Government is prohibited from using fiscal year 2015 and subsequent fiscal year funds for a contract with an entity that requires employees or subcontractors of such entity seeking to report waste, fraud, or abuse to sign internal confidentiality agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-7749]. The bar covers agreements or statements that prohibit or otherwise restrict those employees or subcontractors from lawfully reporting such waste, fraud, or abuse to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information [@CL-CMP-7750].

The paragraph refers the reader to section 743 of Division E, Title VII, of the Consolidated and Further Continuing Appropriations Act, 2015 (Public Law 113-235) and its successor provisions in subsequent appropriations acts, as extended in continuing resolutions [@CL-CMP-7762].

The prohibition does not contravene requirements applicable to Standard Form 312 (Classified Information Nondisclosure Agreement), Form 4414 (Sensitive Compartmented Information Nondisclosure Agreement), or any other form issued by a Federal department or agency governing the nondisclosure of classified information [@CL-CMP-7751; @CL-CMP-7759].

## How it works

The Government is prohibited from using the funds described above for a contract with the entity [@CL-CMP-7749], and an offeror must make the representation in order to be eligible for contract award [@CL-CMP-7752].

To be eligible for contract award, an offeror must represent that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign internal confidentiality agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-7752]. The representation covers agreements or statements that prohibit or otherwise restrict those employees or subcontractors from lawfully reporting waste, fraud, or abuse related to the performance of a Government contract [@CL-CMP-7753]. The reporting is to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information, for example the agency Office of the Inspector General [@CL-CMP-7760]. Any offeror that does not so represent is ineligible for award of a contract [@CL-CMP-7754].

The contracting officer may rely on an offeror's representation unless the contracting officer has reason to question the representation [@CL-CMP-7755].

The term internal confidentiality agreement or statement is defined in the definitions section as used in its subpart [@CL-CMP-7761]. It means a confidentiality agreement or any other written statement that the contractor requires any of its employees or subcontractors to sign regarding nondisclosure of contractor information, but it does not include confidentiality agreements arising out of civil litigation or confidentiality agreements that contractor employees or subcontractors sign at the behest of a Federal agency [@CL-CMP-7742; @CL-CMP-7743].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Three entities respond to a solicitation that will use fiscal year 2016 funds. The first leaves out the representation, so it is an offeror that does not so represent and is ineligible for award [@CL-CMP-7754]. The second makes the representation, and the contracting officer has no reason to question it, so the officer may rely on it [@CL-CMP-7755]. The third requires its employees to sign a statement that bars them from lawfully reporting contract fraud to the agency Office of the Inspector General. The statement is not a form governing the nondisclosure of classified information, so the exception for such forms does not apply [@CL-CMP-7751; @CL-CMP-7759]. The Government is prohibited from using fiscal year 2015 and subsequent fiscal year funds for a contract with an entity that requires such statements [@CL-CMP-7749; @CL-CMP-7750].

## Never confuse

The Government is prohibited from using fiscal year 2015 and subsequent fiscal year funds for a contract with an entity that requires employees or subcontractors of such entity seeking to report waste, fraud, or abuse to sign internal confidentiality agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-7749]. In order to be eligible for contract award, an offeror must represent that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign internal confidentiality agreements or statements, and an offeror that does not so represent is ineligible for award [@CL-CMP-7752; @CL-CMP-7754]. The representation names reporting waste, fraud, or abuse related to the performance of a Government contract [@CL-CMP-7753], and the prohibition names reporting such waste, fraud, or abuse [@CL-CMP-7750].

The prohibition does not contravene requirements applicable to Standard Form 312, Form 4414, or any other form issued by a Federal department or agency governing the nondisclosure of classified information [@CL-CMP-7751; @CL-CMP-7759]. The defined term does not include confidentiality agreements arising out of civil litigation or confidentiality agreements that contractor employees or subcontractors sign at the behest of a Federal agency [@CL-CMP-7743]. The underlying provision and clause are on [Internal confidentiality agreement prohibition](cmp-internal-confidentiality-agreement-prohibition.md).
