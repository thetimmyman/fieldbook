## Purpose

Shows who may void and rescind a contract, the procedure the agency must follow, and the amounts the Government may recover.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2317] |
| Codified citation | FAR 3.704 and FAR 3.705 [@CL-CMP-2318; @CL-CMP-2317] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2317] |
| Applies to | Contracts with respect to which a final conviction has been entered [@CL-CMP-2317]; also the cases of a conviction or agency determination that the further paragraph describes [@CL-CMP-2318] |

Following an assessment of the facts, the agency head or designee may declare void and rescind contracts with respect to which a final conviction has been entered, and recover the amounts expended and the property transferred by the agency under the terms of the contracts involved [@CL-CMP-2317].

A further paragraph is triggered by a final conviction for an offense punishable under section 2105 of title 41 of the United States Code, or by a determination of the head of the agency or designee, based upon a preponderance of the evidence, that the contractor or someone acting for it engaged in conduct constituting such an offense [@CL-CMP-2318]. In those cases the head of the contracting activity shall consider, in addition to any other penalty prescribed by law or regulation, (1) declaring void and rescinding contracts, as appropriate, and recovering the amounts expended using the FAR 3.705 procedures [@CL-CMP-2319], and (2) recommending the initiation of suspension or debarment proceedings in accordance with subpart 9.4 [@CL-CMP-7041].

## How it works

Agencies shall as a minimum provide listed procedures, including a written notice of the proposed action sent by certified mail, return receipt requested [@CL-CMP-2320], and a thirty calendar day period after receipt of the notice for the contractor to submit pertinent information before any final decision is made [@CL-CMP-2321]. The section also provides a hearing on request, which this card does not restate. If the agency declares the contract void and rescinded, the final decision shall specify the amounts due and property to be returned and reflect consideration of the fair value of any tangible benefits received and retained by the agency [@CL-CMP-2322]. The finance consequence is therefore a demand to return amounts expended and property transferred, with the fair value of tangible benefits the agency keeps taken into account in the decision [@CL-CMP-2317; @CL-CMP-2322]. The agency head or designee decides, and the head of the contracting activity considers the action in the cases the further paragraph describes [@CL-CMP-2317; @CL-CMP-2319]. Rescission of contracts under the authority of the Act, and the demand for recovery of the amounts expended and property transferred, is not a claim within the meaning of the Contract Disputes chapter or the FAR part on disputes [@CL-CMP-2323]. Therefore the procedures required by the statute and the FAR for the issuance of a final contracting officer decision are not applicable to final agency decisions under this subpart, and shall not be followed [@CL-CMP-2466].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has drawn 900,000 in payments, and the agency holds tangible benefits it values at 200,000. After a final conviction, the agency head proposes to rescind the contract. The agency sends written notice by certified mail and the contractor replies within the allowed period. The final decision states the amount due and the property to return, and it reflects consideration of the 200,000 value of benefits the agency keeps.

## Never confuse

Voiding and rescission is a separate remedy from suspension or debarment, which is covered in [Causes for debarment](../../supply-chain/supplier-identity/sup-causes-for-debarment.md). It is also not the profit recapture remedy listed for a possible procurement integrity violation, which is covered in [Procurement integrity: protection, marking and possible violations](cmp-procurement-integrity-marking-and-violations.md). Because rescission is not a claim under the Contract Disputes chapter [@CL-CMP-2323], the final contracting officer decision procedures do not apply to it [@CL-CMP-2466].
