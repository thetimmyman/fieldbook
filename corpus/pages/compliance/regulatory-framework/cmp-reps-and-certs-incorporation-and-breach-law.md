## Purpose

Shows which representations and certifications the contract reads in by reference and which law resolves a claim of breach.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5964] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-19 and FAR 52.233-4 [@CL-CMP-5964; @CL-CMP-5965] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.204-19 [@CL-CMP-2172]; RFO FAR 52.233-4 [@CL-CMP-2173] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5964] |

The contractor's representations and certifications, including those completed electronically via the System for Award Management, are incorporated by reference into the contract [@CL-CMP-5964]. United States law will apply to resolve any claim of breach of the contract [@CL-CMP-5965].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.204-19, the Contractor's representations and certifications, including those completed electronically via the System for Award Management (SAM), are incorporated by reference into the contract [@CL-CMP-5964]. Under FAR 52.233-4, United States law will apply to resolve any claim of breach of the contract [@CL-CMP-5965].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 52 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760; @CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO FAR 52.204-19, the Contractor's representations and certifications, including those completed electronically via the System for Award Management (SAM), are incorporated by reference into the contract [@CL-CMP-2172]. Under RFO FAR 52.233-4, United States law will apply to resolve any claim of breach of the contract [@CL-CMP-2173].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor files its representations and certifications electronically in the System for Award Management. Later the buyer brings a claim that the contractor breached the contract by missing a milestone, with 500,000 of payment withheld. The electronic representations and certifications are incorporated by reference into the contract, and United States law applies to resolve the breach claim.

## Never confuse

The incorporation clause is not the law clause: FAR 52.233-4 states that United States law will apply to resolve any claim of breach of the contract [@CL-CMP-5965], while FAR 52.204-19 incorporates the Contractor's representations and certifications, including those completed electronically via SAM, by reference into the contract [@CL-CMP-5964].
