## Purpose

Shows which representations and certifications the contract reads in by reference and which law resolves a claim of breach.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5964] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-19 and FAR 52.233-4 [@CL-CMP-5964; @CL-CMP-5965] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.204-19 [@CL-CMP-2172]; RFO FAR 52.233-4 [@CL-CMP-2173] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5964] |
| Applies to | Contracts that carry the two clauses [@CL-CMP-5964; @CL-CMP-5965] |

The contractor's representations and certifications, including those completed electronically via the System for Award Management, are incorporated by reference into the contract [@CL-CMP-5964]. United States law will apply to resolve any claim of breach of the contract [@CL-CMP-5965].

## How it works

The first clause names the contractor's representations and certifications as incorporated into the contract [@CL-CMP-5964]. Those completed electronically via the System for Award Management are included [@CL-CMP-5964]. The second clause states the law for a claim of breach of the contract [@CL-CMP-5965].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.204-19 keeps the rule that the Contractor's representations and certifications, including those completed electronically via the System for Award Management, are incorporated by reference into the contract [@CL-CMP-2172; @CL-CMP-5964]. RFO FAR 52.233-4 keeps the rule that United States law will apply to resolve any claim of breach of the contract [@CL-CMP-2173; @CL-CMP-5965].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor files its representations and certifications electronically in the System for Award Management. Later the buyer brings a claim that the contractor breached the contract by missing a milestone, with 500,000 of payment withheld. The electronic representations and certifications are incorporated by reference into the contract, and United States law applies to resolve the breach claim.

## Never confuse

The second clause speaks to a claim of breach of the contract, so a different kind of dispute needs its own source [@CL-CMP-5965]. Under the first clause the contract reads the representations and certifications in by reference, including the electronic filings [@CL-CMP-5964].
