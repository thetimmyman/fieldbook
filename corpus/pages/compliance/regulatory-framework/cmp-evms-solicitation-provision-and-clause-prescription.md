## Purpose

Shows which solicitations and contracts use the DoD earned value management system provision and clause instead of the FAR versions.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-7864] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 234.203 [@CL-CMP-7864] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7864] |
| Applies to | Cost or incentive contracts valued at $20,000,000 or more, and other contracts for which the earned value management system will be applied under 234.201(1)(iii) and (iv) [@CL-CMP-7864] |

The section applies to cost or incentive contracts valued at $20,000,000 or more, and to other contracts for which the earned value management system will be applied in accordance with 234.201(1)(iii) and (iv) [@CL-CMP-7864]. For those contracts the solicitation is to use the provision at 252.234-7001, Notice of Earned Value Management System, instead of the FAR pre-award and post-award notice provisions at FAR 52.234-2 and FAR 52.234-3 [@CL-CMP-7865]. The clause at 252.234-7002, Earned Value Management System, is to be used in the solicitation and the contract instead of the clause at FAR 52.234-4 [@CL-CMP-7866]. The provision direction and the clause direction cover the same contracts [@CL-CMP-7864; @CL-CMP-7865; @CL-CMP-7866].

## How it works

The trigger is either a cost or incentive contract valued at or above the threshold, or another contract to which the earned value management system will be applied under the cited subparagraphs [@CL-CMP-7864]. Where the trigger is met, the DoD provision replaces both FAR notice provisions in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7865]. The DoD clause replaces the FAR earned value clause in both the solicitation and the contract [@CL-CMP-7866].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost contract is valued at 30,000,000 dollars. It is above the threshold, so the solicitation uses the provision at 252.234-7001 and the clause at 252.234-7002, and the contract carries that clause [@CL-CMP-7864; @CL-CMP-7865; @CL-CMP-7866].

An incentive contract is valued at exactly 20,000,000 dollars. The threshold is stated as 20,000,000 dollars or more, so the contract is inside it [@CL-CMP-7864].

A firm-fixed-price contract is valued at 5,000,000 dollars, and the earned value management system is not applied to it under 234.201(1)(iii) or (iv). It is not a cost or incentive contract, and the system is not applied to it under those subparagraphs, so this section prescribes neither the provision nor the clause [@CL-CMP-7864].

## Never confuse

For the contracts the section describes [@CL-CMP-7864], the DoD provision at 252.234-7001 is used instead of the FAR provisions at FAR 52.234-2 and FAR 52.234-3 in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7865], and the DoD clause at 252.234-7002 is used instead of the clause at FAR 52.234-4 in the solicitation and contract [@CL-CMP-7866]. For the notice provision, see [EVMS notice provision](../business-systems/cmp-dfars-evms-notice-provision.md); for the FAR clause, see [FAR EVMS clause](../../program-finance/evms/prg-far-evms-clause.md); for when the DoD applies the requirement, see [Earned value management system requirement](../../program-finance/evms/prg-evms-requirement.md).
