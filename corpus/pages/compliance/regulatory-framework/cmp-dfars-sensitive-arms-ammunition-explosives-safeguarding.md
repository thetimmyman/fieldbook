## Purpose

Shows which items the clause covers, the manual that governs their physical security, and the reporting and flowdown duties.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4529] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.223-7007 [@CL-CMP-4529] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4529] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause; the clause text names the DFARS section that prescribes its use [@CL-CMP-4560] |

In the clause, arms, ammunition, and explosives (AA&E) means those items within the scope of DoD Manual 5100.76, Physical Security of Sensitive Conventional Arms, Ammunition, and Explosives [@CL-CMP-4527]. The requirements of that manual apply to the AA&E items listed in the clause by nomenclature, national stock number and sensitivity category that are being developed, produced, manufactured, or purchased for the Government, or provided to the Contractor as Government-furnished property under the contract [@CL-CMP-4528].

The Contractor must comply with DoD Manual 5100.76 as specified in the statement of work, and the edition in effect on the date of issuance of the solicitation applies [@CL-CMP-4529]. Nothing in the clause relieves the Contractor of its responsibility for complying with applicable Federal, state, and local laws, ordinances, codes, and regulations, including requirements for obtaining licenses and permits, in connection with the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4559].

## How it works

The Contractor must allow representatives of the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency (DCSA), and representatives of other appropriate offices of the Government, access at all reasonable times into its facilities and those of its subcontractors [@CL-CMP-4530]. The access is for the purpose of performing surveys, inspections, and investigations necessary to review compliance with the physical security standards applicable to the contract [@CL-CMP-4558]. The Contractor must notify the cognizant DCSA field office of any subcontract involving AA&E within 10 days after award of the subcontract [@CL-CMP-4531]. It must ensure that the requirements of the clause are included in all subcontracts, at every tier, for the development, production, manufacture, or purchase of AA&E, or when AA&E will be provided to the subcontractor as Government-furnished property [@CL-CMP-4532].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor awards a subcontract on day 0 for the development of a listed item (assumed). The subcontract involves AA&E. The contractor must tell the cognizant DCSA field office within the clause's 10-day window, counted from award. Telling it on day 6 (assumed) meets the window. The subcontract must also carry the clause requirements.

## Never confuse

The manual here is the physical security manual, not the contractors' safety manual used under the safety precautions clause; the two are different documents [@CL-CMP-4527; @CL-CMP-4500]. See [Safety precautions for ammunition and explosives (DFARS)](cmp-dfars-ammunition-explosives-safety-precautions.md). The notice to the field office is about a subcontract, and it runs from the award of the subcontract [@CL-CMP-4531]. Items the Government provides to the Contractor as Government-furnished property are within the clause when they are listed AA&E [@CL-CMP-4528]; the general property duties sit in the [Government Property clause](../../close-revrec/fixed-assets/cls-government-property-clause.md).
