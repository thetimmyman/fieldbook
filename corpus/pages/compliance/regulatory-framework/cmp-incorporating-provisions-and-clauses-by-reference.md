## Purpose

Explains when a provision or clause is brought into a solicitation or contract by reference instead of in full text, what the contracting officer must supply or state, and when full text is required.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8848] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.102 [@CL-CMP-8851] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.102 [@CL-CMP-1319; @CL-CMP-1324] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8848] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that incorporate FAR provisions and clauses, or agency provisions and clauses, by reference or in full text [@CL-CMP-8851; @CL-CMP-8855] |

Provisions and clauses should be incorporated by reference to the maximum practical extent, rather than in full text, even if they are used with one or more alternates or on an optional basis [@CL-CMP-8848]. The same instruction applies where a provision or clause is prescribed on a "substantially as follows" or "substantially the same as" basis, provided it is used verbatim [@CL-CMP-8849]. It applies where a provision or clause requires modification or the insertion by the Government of fill-in material, and where it requires completion by the offeror or prospective contractor [@CL-CMP-8850]. It also applies to provisions completed as annual representations and certifications [@CL-CMP-8897].

Except for the provisions and clauses prescribed in 52.107, any provision or clause that can be accessed electronically by the offeror or prospective contractor may be incorporated by reference in solicitations and/or contracts [@CL-CMP-8851]. The contracting officer, upon request, shall provide the full text of any provision or clause incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-8852].

Agency approved provisions and clauses prescribed in agency acquisition regulations, and FAR provisions and clauses that are not authorized to be incorporated by reference as shown in the matrix, need not be incorporated in full text [@CL-CMP-8901; @CL-CMP-8853]. That is provided the contracting officer includes in the solicitation and contract a statement that meets three requirements [@CL-CMP-8853]. The statement identifies all provisions and clauses that require completion by the offeror or prospective contractor [@CL-CMP-8898]. It specifies that they must be completed by the offeror or prospective contractor and submitted with the quotation or offer [@CL-CMP-8899]. It identifies at least one electronic address where the full text may be accessed [@CL-CMP-8900].

## How it works

The statement falls on the contracting officer, who includes it in the solicitation and contract [@CL-CMP-8853], and the contracting officer, upon request, shall provide the full text of any provision or clause incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-8852]. The statement specifies that the provisions and clauses must be completed by the offeror or prospective contractor and must be submitted with the quotation or offer [@CL-CMP-8899].

An agency may develop a group listing of provisions and clauses that apply to a specific category of contracts [@CL-CMP-8902]. An agency group listing may be incorporated by reference in solicitations and/or contracts in lieu of citing the provisions and clauses individually, provided the group listing is made available electronically to offerors and prospective contractors [@CL-CMP-8854].

A provision or clause that is not available electronically to offerors and prospective contractors shall be incorporated in solicitations and/or contracts in full text if it is one of the alternatives the paragraph lists [@CL-CMP-8855]. The alternatives are a FAR provision or clause that otherwise is not authorized to be incorporated by reference, or a provision or clause prescribed for use in an agency acquisition regulation [@CL-CMP-8903].

Provisions or clauses may not be incorporated by reference by being listed in the Alterations in Solicitations provision or the Alterations in Contract clause [@CL-CMP-8856].

The reference-incorporation texts that a solicitation or contract carries are on the card for [the provisions and clauses prescribed in this subpart](cmp-provisions-and-clauses-prescribed-in-subpart-52-1.md). A FAR provision or clause used without deviation, whether it is incorporated by reference or in full text, shall be identified by number, title and date [@CL-CMP-8857]. Where a modification or fill-in is needed, the card on [modifying and completing](cmp-modifying-and-completing-provisions-and-clauses.md) says where it goes.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.102(a) keeps the preference for incorporating provisions and clauses by reference to the maximum practical extent, including where they are used with alternates or on an optional basis [@CL-CMP-1319; @CL-CMP-8848], are prescribed on a "substantially as follows" or "substantially the same as" basis and used verbatim [@CL-CMP-1320; @CL-CMP-8849], need modification, fill-in material or completion [@CL-CMP-1320; @CL-CMP-8850], or are completed as annual representations and certifications [@CL-CMP-1321; @CL-CMP-8897]. It keeps the permission in paragraph (b) to incorporate by reference a provision or clause that can be accessed electronically, except those prescribed in 52.107 [@CL-CMP-1322; @CL-CMP-8851], and the duty of the contracting officer to provide the full text on request [@CL-CMP-1323; @CL-CMP-8852].

RFO FAR 52.102(c) says agency approved provisions and clauses prescribed in agency acquisition regulations, and provisions and clauses not authorized by subpart 52.3 to be incorporated by reference, need not be incorporated in full text, provided the contracting officer includes a statement in the solicitation and contract [@CL-CMP-1324; @CL-CMP-1325]. It keeps the three things the statement does: it identifies the provisions and clauses that require completion [@CL-CMP-1326; @CL-CMP-8898], specifies that they must be completed and submitted with the quotation or offer [@CL-CMP-1326; @CL-CMP-8899], and identifies at least one electronic address where the full text may be accessed [@CL-CMP-1327; @CL-CMP-8900]. RFO FAR 52.102 keeps the agency group listing rule in paragraph (d) [@CL-CMP-1328; @CL-CMP-1329; @CL-CMP-8902; @CL-CMP-8854], the full-text rule in paragraph (e) [@CL-CMP-1330; @CL-CMP-1331; @CL-CMP-8855; @CL-CMP-8903] and the prohibition in paragraph (f) [@CL-CMP-1332; @CL-CMP-8856].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for a supply buy lists twelve clauses. The contracting officer incorporates them by reference because each can be accessed electronically by offerors and none is prescribed in 52.107 [@CL-CMP-8851]. One is used with an alternate [@CL-CMP-8848], and one needs a fill-in that the Government inserts [@CL-CMP-8850]. An offeror asks for the full text of one clause, and the contracting officer provides it [@CL-CMP-8852].

The solicitation also includes an agency approved provision prescribed in the agency's own acquisition regulation that the offeror must complete. The contracting officer does not print it and instead includes in the solicitation and contract a statement that names it as a provision requiring completion, specifies that it must be completed and submitted with the offer, and gives an electronic address for the full text [@CL-CMP-8901; @CL-CMP-8853; @CL-CMP-8898; @CL-CMP-8899; @CL-CMP-8900]. Had the provision not been available electronically, it would have had to be printed in full text, because it is prescribed for use in an agency acquisition regulation [@CL-CMP-8855; @CL-CMP-8903].

The agency also keeps a group listing of clauses for one category of contracts and makes it available electronically to offerors. The solicitation cites the listing instead of each clause [@CL-CMP-8902; @CL-CMP-8854]. The contracting officer does not list a clause in the Alterations in Contract clause as a way to incorporate it by reference [@CL-CMP-8856].

## Never confuse

Under the Clauses Incorporated by Reference clause, a contract incorporates one or more clauses by reference with the same force and effect as if they were given in full text [@CL-CMP-5999]. Under the Solicitation Provisions Incorporated by Reference provision, a solicitation incorporates one or more solicitation provisions by reference with the same force and effect as if they were given in full text [@CL-CMP-5998]. Under FAR 52.102(b), the contracting officer, upon request, shall provide the full text of any provision or clause incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-8852]. The card on [incorporation by reference and solicitation alterations](cmp-incorporation-by-reference-and-solicitation-alterations.md) covers those texts.

Under paragraph (a), provisions and clauses "should" be incorporated by reference to the maximum practical extent [@CL-CMP-8848], and under paragraph (b) a provision or clause that can be accessed electronically "may" be incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-8851]. The contracting officer, upon request, shall provide the full text [@CL-CMP-8852]. The contracting officer includes the statement in the solicitation and contract when relying on paragraph (c) [@CL-CMP-8853]. A provision or clause that is not available electronically shall be incorporated in full text if it is one of the alternatives in paragraph (e) [@CL-CMP-8855]. Under paragraph (f), provisions or clauses may not be incorporated by reference by being listed in the Alterations in Solicitations provision or the Alterations in Contract clause [@CL-CMP-8856].

An agency group listing may stand in for individual citation, provided it is made available electronically to offerors and prospective contractors [@CL-CMP-8854].

How alterations work is on the card for [contract alterations and authorized deviations](cmp-contract-alterations-and-authorized-deviations.md).

The codified paragraph (c) points to the matrix for FAR provisions and clauses not authorized to be incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-8853], while the RFO paragraph (c) points to subpart 52.3 for provisions and clauses not authorized to be incorporated by reference [@CL-CMP-1324].
