## Purpose

Shows the time limits for deferred delivery and deferred ordering of technical data and computer software, how the contractor is paid for an order, and what an offeror must identify about data previously delivered.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4284] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7026, 252.227-7027 and 252.227-7028 [@CL-CMP-4284; @CL-CMP-4286; @CL-CMP-4289] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | APR 1988 [@CL-CMP-10133] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4284] |
| Applies to | Contracts using the deferred delivery clause where DFARS 227.7103-8(a) prescribes it [@CL-CMP-4381], for technical data or computer software items identified in the contract as deferred delivery [@CL-CMP-4285] |

Under the Deferred Delivery of Technical Data or Computer Software clause the Government has the right to require delivery, at any time during performance of the contract, within two years after the later of acceptance of all items other than data or computer software or termination of the contract, of the items the clause covers [@CL-CMP-4284]. The clause covers any technical data or computer software item identified in the contract as deferred delivery data or computer software [@CL-CMP-4285]. For technical data required to be prepared by a subcontractor and pertaining to an item obtained from it, the obligation to furnish expires two years after the date the Contractor accepts the last delivery of that item from that subcontractor for use in performing the contract [@CL-CMP-4382].

Under the Deferred Ordering of Technical Data or Computer Software clause, in addition to technical data or computer software specified elsewhere in the contract to be delivered, the Government may, at any time during performance of the contract or within three years after acceptance of all items other than technical data or computer software or the termination of the contract, order the technical data or computer software the clause covers [@CL-CMP-8625; @CL-CMP-4286]. The clause covers any technical data or computer software generated in the performance of the contract or any subcontract under it [@CL-CMP-4287]. When the technical data or computer software is ordered, the Contractor shall be compensated for converting it into the prescribed form, for reproduction and for delivery [@CL-CMP-4288]. For the technical data of a subcontractor pertaining to an item obtained from it, the obligation to deliver expires three years after the date the Contractor accepts the last delivery of that item from that subcontractor under the contract [@CL-CMP-4383]. The Government's rights to use the data or computer software are pursuant to the Rights in Technical Data and Computer Software clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-4384].

Under the provision on technical data or computer software previously delivered to the Government, the offeror shall attach to its offer an identification of all documents or other media incorporating technical data or computer software it intends to deliver under the contract with other than unlimited rights that are identical or substantially similar to documents or other media [@CL-CMP-4289]. The documents or media the offeror's media are compared with are those the offeror has produced for, delivered to, or is obligated to deliver to the Government under any contract or subcontract [@CL-CMP-4290]. The attachment shall identify the contract number under which the data or software were produced and the contract number, and the name and address of the organization, under and to which they were most recently delivered or will be delivered [@CL-CMP-4291]. The third item the attachment shall identify is any limitations on the Government's rights to use or disclose the data or software, including, when applicable, the earliest date the limitations expire [@CL-CMP-4385].

## How it works

Deferred delivery covers items named in the contract, with a clock that runs from acceptance or termination and a separate subcontractor period [@CL-CMP-4284; @CL-CMP-4285; @CL-CMP-4382]. Deferred ordering covers data generated in performance, with its own clock and its own subcontractor period, and the Contractor is compensated for conversion, reproduction and delivery [@CL-CMP-4286; @CL-CMP-4287; @CL-CMP-4383; @CL-CMP-4288]. The Government's rights to use the ordered data or computer software are pursuant to the Rights in Technical Data and Computer Software clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-4384]. Under the previously delivered provision, the offeror shall attach to its offer an identification of documents or media it intends to deliver under the contract with other than unlimited rights that are identical or substantially similar to those it has produced for, delivered to, or is obligated to deliver to the Government [@CL-CMP-4289; @CL-CMP-4290]. The attachment shall identify the contract number under which the data or software were produced, the contract number and the name and address of the organization under and to which they were most recently delivered or will be delivered, and any limitations on the Government's rights to use or disclose them [@CL-CMP-4291; @CL-CMP-4385].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract lists a software manual as deferred delivery data. All other items are accepted at the end of year three, and the contract is not terminated later. The Government may require the manual up to the end of year five. Separately, it orders test data generated in performance, and the contractor is paid to convert, reproduce and deliver it. At the offer stage, the offeror attached the contract number of an earlier delivery of a similar manual.

## Never confuse

Deferred delivery is not deferred ordering: deferred delivery covers any technical data or computer software item identified in the contract as deferred delivery data or computer software [@CL-CMP-4285]; deferred ordering covers any technical data or computer software generated in the performance of the contract or any subcontract under it [@CL-CMP-4287]. For the list of data restrictions an offeror asserts, see [Assertion of data restrictions](cmp-dod-assertion-of-data-restrictions.md). Categories of rights are in [Technical data rights categories](cmp-dod-technical-data-rights-categories.md), and rights in computer software are in [Noncommercial computer software rights](cmp-dod-noncommercial-software-rights.md). Withholding of payment for late or deficient technical data is in [Technical data payment withholding, foreign data rights and invention reporting](cmp-dfars-data-withholding-foreign-data-and-invention-reports.md).
