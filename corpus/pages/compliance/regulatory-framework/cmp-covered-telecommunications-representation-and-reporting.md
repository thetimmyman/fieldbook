## Purpose

Shows what the offeror represents in the covered telecommunications representation provision, what it must disclose, and the deadlines for contractor reports under the prohibition clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3558] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-24; FAR 52.204-25 [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3561] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.240-90 [@CL-CMP-1045; @CL-CMP-1047]; RFO FAR 52.240-91 [@CL-CMP-1049; @CL-CMP-1050]; the RFO text prints FAR 52.204-24 and FAR 52.204-25 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1042; @CL-CMP-1043] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3558] |
| Applies to | Offerors on solicitations with the representation provision, and contractors under contracts with the prohibition clause [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3561] |

In the first representation the offeror states whether it will or will not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract or other contractual instrument resulting from the solicitation [@CL-CMP-3558]. In the second representation, after conducting a reasonable inquiry, the offeror states whether it does or does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or use any equipment, system, or service that uses covered telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-3559]. If the offeror has responded "will" in the representation in paragraph (d)(1), the offeror shall provide the information listed in the provision as part of the offer [@CL-CMP-3560].

Under the prohibition clause, in the event the Contractor identifies covered telecommunications equipment or services used as a substantial or essential component of any system, or as critical technology as part of any system, during contract performance [@CL-CMP-7678], the Contractor shall report the information in paragraph (d)(2) [@CL-CMP-7677]. Within one business day from the date of such identification or notification, the information includes the contract number, the order number(s) if applicable, and the supplier name [@CL-CMP-3561]. Within 10 business days of submitting the information in paragraph (d)(2)(i), the information is any further available information about mitigation actions undertaken or recommended [@CL-CMP-3562].

## How it works

The first report is due within one business day from the date of such identification or notification [@CL-CMP-3561]. The further mitigation information is due within 10 business days of submitting the information in paragraph (d)(2)(i) [@CL-CMP-3562]. If the offeror has responded "does" in the representation in paragraph (d)(2), the offeror shall provide the information listed in the provision as part of the offer [@CL-CMP-3591]. The contractor shall insert the substance of the prohibition clause, including paragraph (e) and excluding paragraph (b)(2), in all subcontracts and other contractual instruments, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-3592]. The provision at FAR 52.204-26, Covered Telecommunications Equipment or Services-Representation, applies to all solicitations [@CL-CMP-3582]; the annual representations provision is covered in [Annual representations and certifications provision: how its paragraphs work](cmp-annual-representations-provision-paragraphs.md). The definitions and the agency prohibitions are covered in [Covered telecommunications equipment or services: definitions and prohibition](../trade-cyber/cmp-covered-telecommunications-definitions-and-prohibition.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints sections 52.204-24 and 52.204-25 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1042; @CL-CMP-1043]. It states the covered telecommunications representations in paragraph (c) of FAR 52.240-90, made by submission of the offer after a reasonable inquiry [@CL-CMP-1044]. The first says the offeror will not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract or other contractual instrument resulting from the solicitation, except as waived by the solicitation, or as disclosed in paragraph (g) [@CL-CMP-1045], and the second says the offeror does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or use any equipment, system, or service that uses covered telecommunications equipment or services, with the same exceptions [@CL-CMP-1046]; the codified provision instead has the offeror state whether it will or will not and whether it does or does not [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3559]. The RFO disclosure applies where the offeror is not able to represent compliance with the prohibitions in paragraphs (c) or (d), and the offeror must disclose within 72 hours to the contracting office identified in paragraph (g)(2) [@CL-CMP-1047], and the offeror submits the disclosure to the website at https://dibnet.dod.mil for a Department of Defense contracting office and to the Contracting Officer for all other contracting offices [@CL-CMP-9109], where the codified provision has an offeror that responded will or does give its disclosure as part of the offer [@CL-CMP-3560; @CL-CMP-3591]. For reports, FAR 52.240-91(h)(1) applies where the contractor identifies, or is notified by any source including a subcontractor at any tier, that any product or service provided or used (or to be provided or used) during contract performance does not comply with any prohibition in the clause [@CL-CMP-1048], and it requires the contractor to report the following information, or as much information as is known, in writing to the contracting office within 72 hours [@CL-CMP-1049]; the codified clause sets one business day for the first report and 10 business days for the further mitigation information [@CL-CMP-3561; @CL-CMP-3562]. The RFO report goes to the website at https://dibnet.dod.mil for a Department of Defense contracting office and to the Contracting Officer for all other contracting offices [@CL-CMP-9224], and for indefinite delivery contracts to both the contracting office for the indefinite delivery contract and the contracting office for any affected order [@CL-CMP-9225]. If the report does not contain any of the information required by paragraph (h)(1) and the contractor later discovers new information that paragraph (h)(1) requires, the RFO clause requires a subsequent report within 72 hours of discovering the new information [@CL-CMP-9226; @CL-CMP-9227]. The RFO subcontract paragraph requires the contractor to insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (j) but excluding subparagraphs (d)(1) and (i)(1), in all subcontracts and other contractual instruments, including subcontracts for acquiring commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-1050], where the codified clause excludes paragraph (b)(2) [@CL-CMP-3592].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror completes both representations [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3559]. It answers that it will not provide covered equipment in the first [@CL-CMP-3558], but after a reasonable inquiry it finds that it does use a covered item, so it answers does in the second [@CL-CMP-3559]. It provides the information with its offer that the provision lists for a "does" response [@CL-CMP-3591]. Later, a contractor identifies covered equipment used as a substantial or essential component of a system during contract performance [@CL-CMP-7678]. It reports the contract number, order number and supplier name within one business day from the date of identification [@CL-CMP-3561], and any further available information about mitigation actions within 10 business days of submitting that first information [@CL-CMP-3562]. Those are the codified deadlines, and the RFO clause requires the report within 72 hours [@CL-CMP-1049].

## Never confuse

The representation provision is not the reporting clause: in the representation provision the offeror states whether it will provide, and whether it uses, covered telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3559], while under the prohibition clause the first report is due within one business day from the date of such identification or notification, and further mitigation information within 10 business days of submitting the first [@CL-CMP-3561; @CL-CMP-3562]. Other prohibition clauses have their own report deadlines, covered in [Kaspersky and ByteDance prohibitions: what is barred, reported and flowed down](cmp-kaspersky-and-bytedance-prohibitions.md) and [FASCSA order clauses: representation, search and reporting](cmp-fascsa-order-representation-search-and-reporting.md). The reporting deadlines differ between the texts: the codified clause sets one business day and 10 business days [@CL-CMP-3561; @CL-CMP-3562], and the RFO clause sets 72 hours for the report and 72 hours after discovering new information for a subsequent report [@CL-CMP-1049; @CL-CMP-9226; @CL-CMP-9227].
