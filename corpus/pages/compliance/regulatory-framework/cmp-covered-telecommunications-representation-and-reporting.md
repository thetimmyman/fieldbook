## Purpose

Shows what the offeror represents in the covered telecommunications representation provision, what it must disclose, and the deadlines for contractor reports under the prohibition clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3558] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.204-24; FAR 52.204-25 [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3561] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.240-90 [@CL-CMP-1045; @CL-CMP-1047]; RFO FAR 52.240-91 [@CL-CMP-1049; @CL-CMP-1050]; the RFO text prints FAR 52.204-24 and FAR 52.204-25 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1042; @CL-CMP-1043] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3558] |
| Applies to | Offerors on solicitations with the representation provision, and contractors under contracts with the prohibition clause [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3561] |

In the first representation the offeror states whether it will or will not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract or other contractual instrument resulting from the solicitation [@CL-CMP-3558]. In the second representation, after conducting a reasonable inquiry, the offeror states whether it does or does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or use any equipment, system, or service that uses covered telecommunications equipment or services [@CL-CMP-3559]. An offeror that has responded will in the first representation shall provide the information listed in the first disclosure paragraph as part of its offer [@CL-CMP-3560].

Under the prohibition clause, one listed reporting deadline requires the contractor to report, within one business day from the date of identification or notification, the contract number, the order number or numbers if applicable, and the supplier name, among the listed items [@CL-CMP-3561]. Within 10 business days of submitting that first set of information, the contractor shall report any further available information about mitigation actions undertaken or recommended [@CL-CMP-3562].

## How it works

The first report is timed from the date of identification or notification [@CL-CMP-3561]. The second report follows within 10 business days of the first [@CL-CMP-3562]. An offeror that has responded does in the second representation shall provide the information listed in the second disclosure paragraph as part of its offer [@CL-CMP-3591]. The contractor shall insert the substance of the prohibition clause, including its subcontracts paragraph and excluding the second prohibition paragraph, in all subcontracts and other contractual instruments, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-3592]. The separate representation provision on covered telecommunications equipment or services applies to all solicitations according to the annual representations list [@CL-CMP-3582]; that list is on [Annual representations and certifications provision: how its paragraphs work](cmp-annual-representations-provision-paragraphs.md). The opening paragraph of the representation provision says when an offeror does not complete a representation it has already made elsewhere, and this card does not set that out. The clause also names the trigger and the recipient of the report, the full list of data items, the description of prevention efforts that goes with the second report, and the exceptions; this card does not set those out. The definitions and the agency prohibitions are on [Covered telecommunications equipment or services: definitions and prohibition](../trade-cyber/cmp-covered-telecommunications-definitions-and-prohibition.md) and are not restated here.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints sections 52.204-24 and 52.204-25 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1042; @CL-CMP-1043]. It states the covered telecommunications representations in paragraph (c) of FAR 52.240-90, made by submission of the offer after a reasonable inquiry [@CL-CMP-1044]. The first says the offeror will not provide covered telecommunications equipment or services to the Government in the performance of any contract, subcontract or other contractual instrument resulting from the solicitation, except as waived by the solicitation, or as disclosed in paragraph (g) [@CL-CMP-1045], and the second says the offeror does not use covered telecommunications equipment or services, or use any equipment, system, or service that uses covered telecommunications equipment or services, with the same exceptions [@CL-CMP-1046]; the codified provision instead has the offeror state whether it will or will not and whether it does or does not [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3559]. The RFO disclosure applies where the offeror is not able to represent compliance with the prohibitions in paragraphs (c) or (d), and the offeror must disclose within 72 hours to the contracting office identified in paragraph (g)(2) [@CL-CMP-1047], and the offeror submits the disclosure to the website at https://dibnet.dod.mil for a Department of Defense contracting office and to the Contracting Officer for all other contracting offices [@CL-CMP-9109], where the codified provision has an offeror that responded will or does give its disclosure as part of the offer [@CL-CMP-3560; @CL-CMP-3591]. For reports, FAR 52.240-91(h)(1) applies where the contractor identifies, or is notified by any source including a subcontractor at any tier, that any product or service provided or used during contract performance does not comply with any prohibition in the clause [@CL-CMP-1048], and it requires the contractor to report the following information, or as much information as is known, in writing to the contracting office within 72 hours [@CL-CMP-1049]; the codified clause sets one business day for the first report and 10 business days for the further mitigation information [@CL-CMP-3561; @CL-CMP-3562]. The RFO report goes to the website at https://dibnet.dod.mil for a Department of Defense contracting office and to the Contracting Officer for all other contracting offices [@CL-CMP-9224], and for indefinite delivery contracts to both the contracting office for the indefinite delivery contract and the contracting office for any affected order [@CL-CMP-9225]. If the report does not contain any of the information required by paragraph (h)(1) and the contractor later discovers new information that paragraph (h)(1) requires, the RFO clause requires a subsequent report within 72 hours of discovering the new information [@CL-CMP-9226; @CL-CMP-9227]. The RFO subcontract paragraph requires the contractor to insert the substance of the clause, including paragraph (j) but excluding subparagraphs (d)(1) and (i)(1), in all subcontracts and other contractual instruments, including subcontracts for acquiring commercial products or commercial services [@CL-CMP-1050], where the codified clause excludes paragraph (b)(2) [@CL-CMP-3592].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror completes both representations. It answers that it will not provide covered equipment in the first, but after a reasonable inquiry it finds that it does use a covered item, so it answers does in the second. It gives the disclosure the provision requires for the second answer with its offer. Later, a contractor learns of covered equipment in a system during performance. It reports the contract number, order number and supplier name the next business day, and sends the mitigation details two weeks after that. Those are the codified deadlines, and the RFO clause sets 72 hours for the report [@CL-CMP-3561; @CL-CMP-3562; @CL-CMP-1049].

## Never confuse

The representation provision asks what the offeror will provide and what it uses [@CL-CMP-3558; @CL-CMP-3559], whereas the reporting duty in the clause is a separate duty that runs from identification or notification [@CL-CMP-3561]. The first report and the second report have different deadlines: one business day and 10 business days [@CL-CMP-3561; @CL-CMP-3562]. Other prohibition clauses have their own report deadlines, such as those on [Kaspersky and ByteDance prohibitions: what is barred, reported and flowed down](cmp-kaspersky-and-bytedance-prohibitions.md) and [FASCSA order clauses: representation, search and reporting](cmp-fascsa-order-representation-search-and-reporting.md).

The reporting deadlines differ between the texts: the codified clause sets one business day and 10 business days [@CL-CMP-3561; @CL-CMP-3562], and the RFO clause sets 72 hours for the report and 72 hours after discovering new information for a subsequent report [@CL-CMP-1049; @CL-CMP-9226; @CL-CMP-9227].
