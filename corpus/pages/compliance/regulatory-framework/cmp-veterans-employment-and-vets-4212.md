## Purpose

Shows the veterans equal opportunity duties, the annual veterans employment report, and the offer-stage representation that the most recent report was filed.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-5884] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-35, 52.222-37 and 52.222-38 [@CL-CMP-5884; @CL-CMP-5886; @CL-CMP-5890] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-35 [@CL-CMP-6345; @CL-CMP-6472; @CL-CMP-6474]; RFO FAR 52.222-37 [@CL-CMP-6475; @CL-CMP-6479; @CL-CMP-6480]; FAR 52.222-38: No RFO equivalent [@CL-CMP-6484] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-5884] |
| Applies to | Contracts carrying the veterans equal opportunity clause and subcontracts at or above its dollar threshold [@CL-CMP-5885] |

The contractor must abide by the requirements of the equal opportunity clause at 41 CFR 60-300.5(a) as of March 24, 2014 [@CL-CMP-6379]. The veterans clause prohibits discrimination against qualified protected veterans and requires affirmative action by the contractor to employ and advance in employment qualified protected veterans [@CL-CMP-5884].

The reporting clause requires a contractor to report at least annually, as the Secretary of Labor requires, unless the contractor is a State or local government agency [@CL-CMP-5886].

## How it works

The contractor must insert the terms of the veterans clause in subcontracts valued at or above the threshold specified in the acquisition regulation on the date of subcontract award, unless the Secretary of Labor's rules, regulations or orders exempt them [@CL-CMP-5885]. It must also act as specified by the Director of the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs to enforce those terms in subcontracts, including action for noncompliance [@CL-CMP-6378]. Such necessary changes in language may be made as are appropriate to identify properly the parties and their undertakings [@CL-CMP-6631]. Alternate I adds a preamble stating that the listed terms of the veterans clause are waived for the contract, and the list is filled in per contract [@CL-CMP-6380].

Unless the contractor is a State or local government agency, the report states the total number of employees in the contractor's workforce, by job category and hiring location, who are protected veterans, meaning active duty wartime or campaign badge veterans, Armed Forces service medal veterans, disabled veterans and recently separated veterans [@CL-CMP-5887; @CL-CMP-5886]. It states the total number of new employees hired during the period covered by the report and, of that total, the number of protected veterans [@CL-CMP-5888]. It also states the maximum number and minimum number of employees of the contractor or subcontractor at each hiring location during the period covered by the report [@CL-CMP-6381].

The contractor reports these items by filing the VETS-4212 Federal Contractor Veterans' Employment Report [@CL-CMP-6382]. It files the VETS-4212 report no later than September 30 of each year [@CL-CMP-5889]. The employment activity report reflects total new hires, and the maximum and minimum number of employees, during the most recent 12-month period preceding the ending date selected for the report [@CL-CMP-6383]. A contractor may select an ending date at the end of any pay period between July 1 and August 31 of the year the report is due [@CL-CMP-6384]. It may instead select December 31 if it has prior written approval from the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission to do so for purposes of submitting the Employer Information Report EEO-1 [@CL-CMP-6385].

The number of veterans reported must be based on data known to the contractor when it completes the VETS-4212 [@CL-CMP-6636]. That knowledge may come from an invitation to applicants to self-identify under 41 CFR 60-300.42, voluntary self-disclosure by employees, or the contractor's actual knowledge of veteran status, among other ways [@CL-CMP-6637]. Basing the count on known data does not relieve an employer of liability for discrimination under the veterans' employment statute [@CL-CMP-6638].

The contractor must insert the terms of the employment reports clause in subcontracts valued at or above the threshold specified in the acquisition regulation on the date of subcontract award, unless the Secretary of Labor's rules, regulations or orders exempt them [@CL-CMP-6386].

By submission of its offer, the offeror makes a representation [@CL-CMP-6387]. The representation is that, if it is subject to the veterans reporting requirements of the cited statute because it has any contract containing the employment reports on veterans clause, it has filed the most recent VETS-4212 report that clause requires [@CL-CMP-5890].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.222-38 as reserved, so the offer-stage representation has no RFO text under that number [@CL-CMP-6484; @CL-CMP-5890]. In RFO FAR 52.222-35(b) [@CL-CMP-6345], the Contractor must abide by the requirements of 38 U.S.C. 4212(a)(1) and (2), and those requirements prohibit discrimination against qualified protected veterans and require affirmative action to employ and advance in employment qualified protected veterans [@CL-CMP-6345; @CL-CMP-5884], where the codified paragraph names the equal opportunity clause at 41 CFR 60-300.5(a) as of March 24, 2014 [@CL-CMP-6379]. Paragraph (c) of that clause points to FAR 22.1302-1(a)(2) for the subcontract threshold, where the codified paragraph points to FAR 22.1303(a) [@CL-CMP-6472; @CL-CMP-5885]. It keeps the duty to act as specified by the Director of the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, including action for noncompliance, and the allowance for necessary changes in language [@CL-CMP-6473; @CL-CMP-6378; @CL-CMP-6631]. Alternate I keeps the preamble stating that the listed terms are waived for the contract [@CL-CMP-6474; @CL-CMP-6380]. In RFO FAR 52.222-37, unless the Contractor is a State or local government agency, the Contractor must report at least annually, as required by the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CMP-6475; @CL-CMP-5886], keeping the count of protected veterans in the workforce by job category and hiring location [@CL-CMP-6476; @CL-CMP-5887] and the count of new hires and protected veterans among them [@CL-CMP-6477; @CL-CMP-5888]. The item on the maximum and minimum number of employees of the Contractor or subcontractor during the period covered by the report has no words "at each hiring location", which the codified item has [@CL-CMP-6478; @CL-CMP-6381]. The Contractor must report by filing the VETS-4212 report [@CL-CMP-6479; @CL-CMP-6382] no later than September 30 of each year [@CL-CMP-6480; @CL-CMP-5889]. Paragraph (e) says the employment activity report must reflect total new hires, and maximum and minimum number of employees, during the most recent 12-month period preceding the ending date selected for the report, and it prints no list of ending dates a contractor may select [@CL-CMP-6481; @CL-CMP-6383]. The number of veterans reported must still be based on data known to the contractor when completing the VETS-4212 [@CL-CMP-6482; @CL-CMP-6636]. The subcontract paragraph points to FAR 22.1302-1(b) for the threshold, where the codified paragraph points to FAR 22.1303(a) [@CL-CMP-6483; @CL-CMP-6386].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has one hiring location. Under the codified text, it picks an ending date of August 20, which is the end of a pay period between July 1 and August 31 of the year the report is due [@CL-CMP-6384]. The report covers the most recent 12-month period before August 20. It states 18 protected veterans in the workforce by job category, 90 new hires of whom 12 are protected veterans, and a maximum of 640 and a minimum of 570 employees at the location. The contractor files the VETS-4212 report on September 15, before the September 30 due date. Its subcontract of 30,000 is covered if the acquisition regulation threshold on the award date is at or below that amount, and it is exempt if the Secretary of Labor's rules exempt it. Under the codified text, a contractor with no such approval from the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission cannot select December 31 as its ending date [@CL-CMP-6385]. Under the RFO text, paragraph (e) prints no ending-date choices [@CL-CMP-6481].

## Never confuse

Veterans duties are often folded into the general equal opportunity clause. Under the codified text a contractor may select an ending date in the pay-period window of July 1 to August 31 or, with approval from the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, December 31 [@CL-CMP-6384; @CL-CMP-6385], and the RFO paragraph (e) prints no such choice [@CL-CMP-6481]. The codified text also has an offer-stage representation on the most recent VETS-4212 report [@CL-CMP-5890], and the RFO text prints FAR 52.222-38 as reserved [@CL-CMP-6484]. They sit in separate clauses: the veterans clause carries a subcontract flow-down that turns on a threshold in the acquisition regulation [@CL-CMP-5885], and the reporting clause carries an annual filing due by September 30 [@CL-CMP-5889] that is the VETS-4212 report [@CL-CMP-6382]. The offer-stage representation concerns whether the most recent required report has been filed, not the content of the report [@CL-CMP-5890].
