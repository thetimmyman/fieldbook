## Purpose

Explains the place of manufacture indication in paragraph (j), the certificates regarding exemptions from the Service Contract Labor Standards in paragraph (k), and the taxpayer identification number entries in paragraph (l) of the commercial offeror representations provision, including who checks which box and what follows if an offeror does not certify.

Place of manufacture, paragraph (j). The paragraph does not apply unless the solicitation is predominantly for the acquisition of manufactured end products, and for statistical purposes only the offeror shall indicate whether the place of manufacture of the end products it expects to provide in response to the solicitation is predominantly in the United States or outside the United States [@CL-CMP-9003; @CL-CMP-9171]. The box for the United States is the one to check if the total anticipated price of offered end products manufactured in the United States exceeds the total anticipated price of offered end products manufactured outside the United States [@CL-CMP-9171]. The terms manufactured end product and place of manufacture are defined in paragraph (a) [@CL-CMP-9130; @CL-CMP-8967; @CL-CMP-9132].

Service Contract Labor Standards exemption certificates, paragraph (k). Certification by the offeror as to its compliance with respect to the contract also constitutes its certification as to compliance by its subcontractor if it subcontracts out the exempt services [@CL-CMP-9004]. The contracting officer checks a box to indicate whether paragraph (k)(1) or paragraph (k)(2) applies [@CL-CMP-9172].

Paragraph (k)(1), maintenance, calibration or repair of certain equipment: the paragraph covers maintenance, calibration or repair of certain equipment as described in FAR 22.1003-4(c)(1), and the offeror checks whether it does or does not certify to the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-9173]. The first condition is that the items of equipment to be serviced under the contract are used regularly for other than Governmental purposes and are sold or traded by the offeror, or by the subcontractor in the case of an exempt subcontract, in substantial quantities to the general public in the course of normal business operations [@CL-CMP-9174]. The second is that the services will be furnished at prices which are, or are based on, established catalog or market prices for the maintenance, calibration or repair of such equipment [@CL-CMP-9175]. The third is that the compensation (wage and fringe benefits) plan for all service employees performing work under the contract will be the same as that used for these employees and equivalent employees servicing the same equipment of commercial customers [@CL-CMP-9005].

Paragraph (k)(2), certain services: the paragraph covers certain services as described in FAR 22.1003-4(d)(1), and the offeror checks whether it does or does not certify to the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-9176]. The first condition is that the services under the contract are offered and sold regularly to non-Governmental customers and are provided by the offeror, or by the subcontractor in the case of an exempt subcontract, to the general public in substantial quantities in the course of normal business operations [@CL-CMP-9177]. The second is that the contract services will be furnished at prices that are, or are based on, established catalog or market prices [@CL-CMP-9178]. The third is that each service employee who will perform the services under the contract will spend only a small portion of his or her time, a monthly average of less than 20 percent of the available hours on an annualized basis [@CL-CMP-9006]. If the contract period is less than a month, the measure is less than 20 percent of the available hours during the contract period, and the time counted is time spent servicing the Government contract [@CL-CMP-9179]. The fourth is that the compensation plan for all service employees performing work under the contract is the same as that used for these employees and equivalent employees servicing commercial customers [@CL-CMP-9180].

Paragraph (k)(3), notice and award: paragraph (k)(3) applies if paragraph (k)(1) or paragraph (k)(2) applies [@CL-CMP-9189]. If the offeror does not certify to the conditions in paragraph (k)(1) or (k)(2) and the Contracting Officer did not attach a Service Contract Labor Standards wage determination to the solicitation, the offeror shall notify the Contracting Officer as soon as possible [@CL-CMP-9181]. The Contracting Officer may not make an award to the offeror if the offeror fails to execute the certification in paragraph (k)(1) or (k)(2) or to contact the Contracting Officer as required in paragraph (k)(3)(i) [@CL-CMP-9007].

Taxpayer identification number, paragraph (l). The paragraph does not apply to an offeror that is required to provide that information to SAM to be eligible for award [@CL-CMP-9008]. All offerors must submit the information required in paragraphs (l)(3) through (l)(5) to comply with debt collection requirements, reporting requirements and implementing regulations [@CL-CMP-9182]. The Government may use the taxpayer identification number to collect and report on any delinquent amounts arising out of the offeror's relationship with the Government [@CL-CMP-9183]. If the resulting contract is subject to the payment reporting requirements described in FAR 4.904, the number may be matched with IRS records to verify its accuracy [@CL-CMP-9184].

In paragraph (l)(3) the offeror enters its number, indicates that the number has been applied for, or indicates that the number is not required because of one of the listed reasons [@CL-CMP-9009]. One reason is that the offeror is a nonresident alien, foreign corporation or foreign partnership that does not have income effectively connected with the conduct of a trade or business in the United States and does not have an office or place of business or a fiscal paying agent in the United States [@CL-CMP-9185]. Another is that the offeror is an agency or instrumentality of a foreign government or of the Federal Government [@CL-CMP-9186]. In paragraph (l)(4) the offeror states its type of organization from the listed choices, which are sole proprietorship, partnership, corporate entity (not tax-exempt), corporate entity (tax-exempt), government entity (Federal, State, or local), foreign government, international organization per 26 CFR 1.6049-4, and other [@CL-CMP-9187]. In paragraph (l)(5) it states either that it is not owned or controlled by a common parent or the name and taxpayer identification number of the common parent [@CL-CMP-9188].

## How it works

Paragraph (j) is for statistical purposes only and does not apply unless the solicitation is predominantly for the acquisition of manufactured end products [@CL-CMP-9003]. The box for the United States is the one to check if the total anticipated price of offered end products manufactured in the United States exceeds the total anticipated price of offered end products manufactured outside the United States [@CL-CMP-9171].

The contracting officer checks a box to indicate whether paragraph (k)(1) or (k)(2) applies [@CL-CMP-9172]. Under each of those paragraphs the offeror checks whether it does or does not certify to the listed conditions [@CL-CMP-9173; @CL-CMP-9176], and paragraph (k)(3) applies if paragraph (k)(1) or (k)(2) applies [@CL-CMP-9189]. The offeror's certification as to its own compliance also covers its subcontractor if it subcontracts out the exempt services [@CL-CMP-9004]. The notice duty in paragraph (k)(3)(i) is stated for the case in which the offeror does not certify and the Contracting Officer did not attach a wage determination [@CL-CMP-9181]. The Contracting Officer may not make an award if the offeror fails to execute the certification or to contact the Contracting Officer as required [@CL-CMP-9007]. The exemption conditions themselves are in the linked Service Contract Labor Standards card.

Paragraph (l) is not applicable if the offeror is required to provide this information to SAM to be eligible for award [@CL-CMP-9008]. All offerors must submit the information required in paragraphs (l)(3) through (l)(5) [@CL-CMP-9182].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text marks FAR 52.212-3 as reserved [@CL-CMP-1531]. RFO FAR 52.225-18(b) says that, for statistical purposes only, the offeror shall indicate whether the place of manufacture of the end products it expects to provide is predominantly in the places the paragraph lists, and it prints no parenthetical limiting it to solicitations predominantly for manufactured end products, which the codified paragraph (j) prints [@CL-CMP-1595; @CL-CMP-9003]. In RFO FAR 52.222-48 and FAR 52.222-52 the offeror itself must check the certification, and the paragraph prints no instruction for the contracting officer to check a box, which the codified paragraph (k) prints [@CL-CMP-1596; @CL-CMP-1607; @CL-CMP-9172]. RFO FAR 52.222-48(b) keeps the rule that the offeror's certification as to its own compliance also constitutes its certification as to compliance by its subcontractor if it subcontracts out the exempt services [@CL-CMP-9004; @CL-CMP-1597]. Its paragraph (c) applies if the offeror does not certify to the conditions in paragraph (a) of the provision [@CL-CMP-1598], and then the offeror must notify the Contracting Officer as soon as possible if the Contracting Officer did not attach a Service Contract Labor Standards wage determination to the solicitation [@CL-CMP-9181; @CL-CMP-1599]. Its paragraph (d) says the Contracting Officer may not make an award to the offeror if the offeror fails to execute the certification in paragraph (a) or to contact the Contracting Officer as required in paragraph (c) [@CL-CMP-1606], where the codified text refers to paragraphs (k)(1), (k)(2) and (k)(3)(i) [@CL-CMP-9007]. Under RFO FAR 52.204-90, if the Offeror will not have an active Federal Government contracts registration in the System for Award Management when submitting its offer, it shall complete paragraphs (c) and (d) of the provision and include its responses with its offer [@CL-CMP-1544]. Paragraph (c) says the Offeror shall provide with its offer the information necessary to comply with debt collection requirements, reporting requirements and the implementing IRS regulations, and it prints no separate parenthetical excluding offerors required to give the information to SAM, which the codified paragraph (l) prints [@CL-CMP-1608; @CL-CMP-9008; @CL-CMP-9182]. Paragraph (c)(4) says the TIN may be matched with IRS records to verify its accuracy and the Government may use it to collect and report on delinquent amounts, and it prints no condition tied to the payment reporting requirements described in FAR 4.904, which the codified paragraph prints [@CL-CMP-1609; @CL-CMP-9183; @CL-CMP-9184]. RFO FAR 52.204-7(b)(2)(i) has the Offeror provide its TIN or related information for the same debt collection and reporting purposes, and consent to TIN validation, where the codified paragraph (l) says it is not applicable if the offeror is required to provide this information to SAM to be eligible for award [@CL-CMP-9326; @CL-CMP-9008].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation is predominantly for manufactured end products, so Eastgate Instruments completes paragraph (j) [@CL-CMP-9003]. It offers end products with a total anticipated price of $300,000 manufactured in the United States and $180,000 manufactured outside the United States. The United States total is larger, so Eastgate checks the box for the United States [@CL-CMP-9171].

The contracting officer checks the paragraph (k)(1) box for calibration of equipment [@CL-CMP-9172]. Eastgate checks that it does certify. It certifies that the equipment is used regularly for other than Governmental purposes and sold in substantial quantities to the general public, that the services will be furnished at established catalog or market prices, and that its service employees' compensation plan will be the same as for employees servicing the same equipment of commercial customers [@CL-CMP-9174; @CL-CMP-9175; @CL-CMP-9005].

On a different solicitation the contracting officer checks the paragraph (k)(2) box. A service employee will spend a monthly average of 12 percent of the available hours on the contract, which is less than 20 percent, so Eastgate can certify the time condition [@CL-CMP-9006]. On a 3-week contract with 120 available hours, a service employee will spend 15 percent of the available hours, which is 18 hours, below 20 percent, which is 24 hours [@CL-CMP-9179]. If Eastgate cannot certify to the conditions and no Service Contract Labor Standards wage determination is attached, it must notify the Contracting Officer as soon as possible [@CL-CMP-9181]. If it fails to execute the certification or fails to contact the Contracting Officer as required, the Contracting Officer may not make an award to it [@CL-CMP-9007].

Eastgate is not required to provide its taxpayer identification information to SAM to be eligible for award, so paragraph (l) applies to it [@CL-CMP-9008]. It enters its number and states that it is a corporate entity that is not owned or controlled by a common parent [@CL-CMP-9009; @CL-CMP-9187; @CL-CMP-9188]. A foreign partnership to which paragraph (l) applies, with no income effectively connected with a United States trade or business and no office, place of business or fiscal paying agent in the United States states instead that the number is not required [@CL-CMP-9008; @CL-CMP-9185].

## Never confuse

The place of manufacture indication is for statistical purposes only and does not apply unless the solicitation is predominantly for manufactured end products [@CL-CMP-9003].

Paragraph (k)(1) covers maintenance, calibration, or repair of certain equipment as described in FAR 22.1003-4(c)(1), and paragraph (k)(2) covers certain services as described in FAR 22.1003-4(d)(1), and in each the offeror checks whether it does or does not certify to the listed conditions, and the contracting officer checks a box to indicate which applies [@CL-CMP-9173; @CL-CMP-9176; @CL-CMP-9172].

Under paragraph (k)(3)(i), the offeror shall notify the Contracting Officer as soon as possible if it does not certify to the conditions in paragraph (k)(1) or (k)(2) and the Contracting Officer did not attach a Service Contract Labor Standards wage determination to the solicitation [@CL-CMP-9181]. Under paragraph (k)(3)(ii), the Contracting Officer may not make an award to the offeror if the offeror fails to execute the certification or to contact the Contracting Officer as required in paragraph (k)(3)(i) [@CL-CMP-9007].

In paragraph (l)(3) the offeror enters its number, indicates that the number has been applied for, or indicates that the number is not required because of one of the listed reasons [@CL-CMP-9009; @CL-CMP-9185; @CL-CMP-9186].

The codified text provides that, if the resulting contract is subject to the payment reporting requirements described in FAR 4.904, the TIN may be matched with IRS records, and RFO FAR 52.204-90(c)(4) prints no such condition [@CL-CMP-9184; @CL-CMP-1609]. The codified paragraph (j) does not apply unless the solicitation is predominantly for the acquisition of manufactured end products, and RFO FAR 52.225-18(b) prints no such limit in the paragraph [@CL-CMP-9003; @CL-CMP-1595].
