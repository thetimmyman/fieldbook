## Purpose

Shows what an offeror represents about internal confidentiality agreements and what a contractor must not require of its employees and subcontractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2640; @CL-CMP-2642] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-18 and FAR 52.203-19 [@CL-CMP-2640; @CL-CMP-2642] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.203-18 [@CL-CMP-1352; @CL-CMP-1353] and RFO FAR 52.203-19 [@CL-CMP-1355; @CL-CMP-1356] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2640; @CL-CMP-2642] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when using funding subject to the prohibitions in the cited FAR section, other than personal services contracts with individuals [@CL-CMP-2696; @CL-CMP-2698; @CL-CMP-2697] |

The representation provision is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.909-3(a) [@CL-CMP-2640]. The contracting officer includes it when using funding subject to the prohibitions in the cited FAR section [@CL-CMP-2696]. The exception is that it is not inserted in solicitations for a personal services contract with an individual if the services are to be performed entirely by the individual, rather than by an employee of the contractor or a subcontractor [@CL-CMP-2698]. By submission of its offer, the Offeror represents that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with internal confidentiality agreements or statements prohibiting or otherwise restricting such employees or subcontractors from lawfully reporting waste, fraud, or abuse related to the performance of a Government contract to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information, for example the agency Office of the Inspector General [@CL-CMP-2641; @CL-CMP-7877].

The clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.909-3(b) [@CL-CMP-2642]. Under the same lead-in about funding, the prescription directs inclusion of the clause in all solicitations and resultant contracts, other than personal services contracts with individuals [@CL-CMP-2696; @CL-CMP-2697]. The Contractor shall not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with the same kind of agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-2643]. The reporting that the clause protects is reporting related to the performance of a Government contract to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information, for example the agency Office of the Inspector General [@CL-CMP-2644].

## How it works

By submission of its offer, the Offeror represents that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with the agreements or statements described [@CL-CMP-2641]. Under the clause, the Contractor shall not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with them [@CL-CMP-2643].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.203-18 keeps the insertion of the representation provision as prescribed in FAR 3.909-3(a) [@CL-CMP-1352; @CL-CMP-2640]. It keeps the representation that, by submission of its offer, the Offeror will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with internal confidentiality agreements or statements prohibiting or otherwise restricting them from lawfully reporting waste, fraud, or abuse [@CL-CMP-1353; @CL-CMP-2641].

RFO FAR 52.203-19 keeps the insertion of the clause as prescribed in FAR 3.909-3(b) [@CL-CMP-1355; @CL-CMP-2642] and the rule that the Contractor shall not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with the same kind of agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-1356; @CL-CMP-2643]. Both RFO FAR 52.203-18 and RFO FAR 52.203-19 describe the protected reporting in the same words: reporting related to the performance of a Government contract to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information, for example the agency Office of the Inspector General [@CL-CMP-1354; @CL-CMP-1357; @CL-CMP-2644].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company that is using funding subject to the prohibition has a contract that is not a personal services contract with an individual. Its onboarding packet makes new employees sign a statement that bars them from telling anyone outside the company about any matter, including fraud on a Government contract. Under the clause, the company may not require employees to sign or comply with that statement so far as it restricts lawful reporting of waste, fraud, or abuse related to contract performance to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative. The company must change the statement to that extent.
