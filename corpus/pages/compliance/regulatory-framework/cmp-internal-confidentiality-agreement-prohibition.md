## Purpose

Shows what an offeror represents about internal confidentiality agreements and what a contractor must not require of its employees and subcontractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2640; @CL-CMP-2642] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.203-18 and FAR 52.203-19 [@CL-CMP-2640; @CL-CMP-2642] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2640; @CL-CMP-2642] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts when using funding subject to the prohibitions in the cited FAR section, other than personal services contracts with individuals [@CL-CMP-2696; @CL-CMP-2698; @CL-CMP-2697] |

The representation provision is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.909-3(a) [@CL-CMP-2640]. The contracting officer includes it when using funding subject to the prohibitions in the cited FAR section [@CL-CMP-2696]. The exception is that it is not inserted in solicitations for a personal services contract with an individual if the services are to be performed entirely by the individual, rather than by an employee of the contractor or a subcontractor [@CL-CMP-2698]. By submission of its offer, the Offeror represents that it will not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with internal confidentiality agreements or statements prohibiting or otherwise restricting such employees or subcontractors from lawfully reporting waste, fraud, or abuse, with the protected reporting described further in the provision [@CL-CMP-2641].

The clause is inserted as prescribed in FAR 3.909-3(b) [@CL-CMP-2642]. Under the same lead-in about funding, the prescription directs inclusion of the clause in all solicitations and resultant contracts, other than personal services contracts with individuals [@CL-CMP-2696; @CL-CMP-2697]. The Contractor shall not require its employees or subcontractors to sign or comply with the same kind of agreements or statements [@CL-CMP-2643]. The reporting that the clause protects is reporting related to the performance of a Government contract to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative of a Federal department or agency authorized to receive such information, for example the agency Office of the Inspector General [@CL-CMP-2644].

## How it works

The provision and the clause use the same words for what is barred. The provision is a representation made with the offer, and the clause is the duty that applies during the contract. The provision's own text goes on to describe the protected reporting in the same words as the clause. Both carry a carve-out for classified-information nondisclosure forms, and the clause defines the term internal confidentiality agreement or statement with listed exclusions, and this card does not set those out. The clause also requires the Contractor to notify current employees and subcontractors that inconsistent restrictions in preexisting agreements are no longer in effect, and it ties a funding prohibition to a Government finding of non-compliance, and this card does not set those out either.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company that is using funding subject to the prohibition has a contract that is not a personal services contract with an individual. Its onboarding packet makes new employees sign a statement that bars them from telling anyone outside the company about any matter, including fraud on a Government contract. Under the clause, the company may not require employees to sign or comply with that statement so far as it restricts lawful reporting of waste, fraud, or abuse related to contract performance to a designated investigative or law enforcement representative. The company must change the statement to that extent.

## Never confuse

The prohibition is not the display of a hotline poster, which is covered on [Display of hotline poster clause](cmp-hotline-poster-clause.md). It concerns what a contractor may require employees and subcontractors to sign, while whistleblower protection concerns relief for reprisal against those who make protected disclosures. The reprisal rules are covered in [Whistleblower protections for contractor employees: policy and remedies](cmp-contractor-employee-whistleblower-protections.md).
