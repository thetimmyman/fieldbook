## Purpose

Shows how the price of a service contract is adjusted when a wage determination applied by operation of law or a statutory minimum wage change alters the pay that the contract requires.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-4815] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.222-44 [@CL-CMP-4815] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.222-44 [@CL-CMP-5020; @CL-CMP-5021; @CL-CMP-5022; @CL-CMP-5023; @CL-CMP-5093; @CL-CMP-5154; @CL-CMP-5155; @CL-CMP-5156; @CL-CMP-5157; @CL-CMP-5158] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4815] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to area prevailing wage determinations and contracts subject to contractor collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-4815] |

The clause applies to contracts subject to area prevailing wage determinations and to contracts subject to contractor collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-4815]. The contract price, contract unit price labor rates, or fixed hourly labor rates are adjusted to reflect the contractor's increases or decreases in wages and fringe benefits to the extent that they are made to comply with a covered cause [@CL-CMP-4963]. There are two covered causes. One is an increased or decreased wage determination applied to the contract by operation of law [@CL-CMP-4817]. The other is an amendment to the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 that is enacted after award, affects the minimum wage and becomes applicable to the contract under law [@CL-CMP-4818].

The contractor warrants that the prices include no allowance for any contingency to cover increased costs for which the clause provides an adjustment [@CL-CMP-4816]. An adjustment is limited to the wage and fringe benefit changes described above and to the accompanying changes in social security and unemployment taxes and workers' compensation insurance [@CL-CMP-4819]. It does not otherwise include any amount for general and administrative costs, overhead or profit [@CL-CMP-4820].

## How it works

The contractor must notify the contracting officer of any increase claimed within 30 days after the effective date of the wage change, unless the contracting officer extends that period in writing [@CL-CMP-4821]. The contractor must promptly notify the contracting officer of any decrease, and nothing in the clause stops the government from asserting a claim within the period the law permits [@CL-CMP-4964]. The notice states the amount and, for a time-and-materials or labor-hour contract, the change in fixed hourly rates, with any relevant supporting data that the contracting officer may reasonably require [@CL-CMP-4965]. Upon agreement of the parties, the contract price, contract unit price labor rates, or fixed hourly rates shall be modified in writing [@CL-CMP-4966]. The contractor must continue performance pending agreement on or determination of the adjustment and its effective date [@CL-CMP-4967].

The contracting officer or an authorized representative may examine the contractor's directly pertinent books, documents, papers and records until 3 years after final payment under the contract [@CL-CMP-4822].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of the clause keeps its coverage of contracts subject to area prevailing wage determinations and contracts subject to contractor collective bargaining agreements [@CL-CMP-5020; @CL-CMP-4815], and the contractor's warranty that the prices include no allowance for contingencies [@CL-CMP-5021; @CL-CMP-4816]. It keeps the adjustment of the contract price or rates to reflect the contractor's increases or decreases in wages and fringe benefits to the extent they are made to comply with a covered cause [@CL-CMP-5022; @CL-CMP-4963], and the two covered causes [@CL-CMP-5023; @CL-CMP-4817] [@CL-CMP-5093; @CL-CMP-4818]. It keeps the limit of the adjustment to those wage and fringe benefit changes and the accompanying taxes and insurance [@CL-CMP-5154; @CL-CMP-4819], and the bar on any other amount for general and administrative costs, overhead or profit [@CL-CMP-5155; @CL-CMP-4820]. It keeps the 30-day notice of any increase, unless extended in writing [@CL-CMP-5156; @CL-CMP-4821], the prompt notice of any decrease with the Government's claim preserved [@CL-CMP-5157; @CL-CMP-4964], and the 3-year examination right [@CL-CMP-5158; @CL-CMP-4822].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract pays a technician 25.00 an hour. A wage determination applied by operation of law raises the required rate to 27.00, and the company pays 27.00, so the wage change is 2.00 an hour. Social security and unemployment taxes and workers' compensation insurance that rise with the wage add 0.20 an hour, so the allowable adjustment is 2.20 an hour. A charge of 0.10 an hour for overhead and any amount for profit are not allowed. The company notifies the contracting officer within 30 days after the effective date of the wage change, unless the contracting officer has extended that period in writing.

## Never confuse

The clause for multiple year and option contracts adds a third cause, the wage determination applicable on the anniversary date or at the beginning of a renewal option period [@CL-CMP-4807]. It is on [price adjustment for multiple year and option contracts](cmp-scls-price-adjustment-multiple-year-and-option.md).
