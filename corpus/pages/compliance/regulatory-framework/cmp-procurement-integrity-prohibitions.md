## Purpose

Shows what counts as contractor bid or proposal information and who may not disclose or obtain it before award, so a finance team knows which pricing data to protect.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-2300] |
| Codified citation | FAR 3.104-1 and FAR 3.104-3 [@CL-CMP-2300; @CL-CMP-2303; @CL-CMP-2306] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2300] |
| Applies to | Information submitted to a Federal agency with a bid or proposal for a Federal agency procurement contract, if not previously made public [@CL-CMP-2300] |

Contractor bid or proposal information is information submitted to a Federal agency as part of or in connection with a bid or proposal for a Federal agency procurement contract, if it has not previously been made available to the public or disclosed publicly [@CL-CMP-2300]. Listed categories include cost or pricing data, with respect to procurements subject to the statutory sections the definition cites, and indirect costs and direct labor rates [@CL-CMP-2301]. They also include information marked by the contractor as bid or proposal information in accordance with applicable law or regulation, and information marked under the FAR paragraph the definition names [@CL-CMP-2302]. The definition lists further categories that this card does not restate.

A person described below may not, other than as provided by law, knowingly disclose this information, or source selection information, before the award of the contract to which it relates [@CL-CMP-2303]. The disclosure ban reaches a person who is a present or former official of the United States, or who is acting or has acted for or on behalf of, or who is advising or has advised, the United States with respect to a Federal agency procurement [@CL-CMP-2304]. That person must also, by virtue of that office, employment, or relationship, have or have had access to the information [@CL-CMP-2305]. Separately, a person must not, other than as provided by law, knowingly obtain the information before award of the contract to which it relates [@CL-CMP-2306].

## How it works

For a finance team, cost or pricing data (for procurements subject to the cited statutory sections) and indirect costs and direct labor rates fall within the definition when they are submitted as part of or in connection with a bid or proposal and have not previously been made available to the public or disclosed publicly [@CL-CMP-2300; @CL-CMP-2301]. The disclosure ban is written for officials and advisers who had access [@CL-CMP-2304; @CL-CMP-2305]. The obtaining ban is written for any person, so a company employee who knowingly obtains, before award, another offeror's submitted rates that fall within the listed categories is within it, unless the law provides otherwise [@CL-CMP-2301; @CL-CMP-2306]. Both bans are framed around the period before award of the contract the information relates to [@CL-CMP-2303; @CL-CMP-2306]. The same section also has rules on employment contacts and on pay to former officials, which this card does not cover. The protection section refers to the general definitions section of the FAR for source selection information, which this card does not restate [@CL-CMP-2470]. See [Procurement integrity: protection, marking and possible violations](cmp-procurement-integrity-marking-and-violations.md) for who may receive the information, how it is protected and what follows a possible violation.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company submits a proposal with direct labor rates and an overhead rate. Before award, a pricing analyst is offered a rival's rate sheet by a friend inside the agency. Unless the law provides otherwise, the analyst must not knowingly obtain it, and the company's own submitted rates stay protected until made public.

## Never confuse

Contractor bid or proposal information is information submitted as part of or in connection with a bid or proposal, in the categories the definition lists [@CL-CMP-2300]. Source selection information is a separate category, defined in the general definitions section of the FAR [@CL-CMP-2470]. Cost or pricing data is one listed category inside the definition, not the whole of it; see [Certified cost or pricing data](../../estimating/pricing/est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md). Duties to report credible evidence of violations are a different topic; see [Contractor code of business ethics](cmp-contractor-code-of-business-ethics.md).
