## Purpose

Shows which contractor employees are covered by the personal conflict of interest rules and what the contractor must do.

## The rule

A covered employee is an individual who performs an acquisition function closely associated with inherently governmental functions and is either an employee of the contractor or a subcontractor that is a self-employed individual treated as a covered employee of the contractor [@CL-CMP-2342]. An acquisition function closely associated with inherently governmental functions means supporting or providing advice or recommendations with regard to listed activities of a Federal agency, the first of which is planning acquisitions [@CL-CMP-2343]. The listed activities include determining whether contract costs are reasonable, allocable, and allowable [@CL-CMP-2473].

By use of the contract clause at FAR 52.203-16, as prescribed at FAR 3.1106, the contracting officer shall require each contractor whose employees perform such functions to take the listed actions [@CL-CMP-2346]. That clause is prescribed for solicitations and contracts that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-CMP-2471] and that include a requirement for services by contractor employees that involve performance of acquisition functions closely associated with inherently governmental functions for, or on behalf of, a Federal agency or department [@CL-CMP-2472].

## How it works

A personal conflict of interest is a situation in which a covered employee has a financial interest, personal activity, or relationship that could impair the employee's ability to act impartially and in the best interest of the Government when performing under the contract [@CL-CMP-2344]. A de minimis interest that would not impair that ability is not covered [@CL-CMP-2345]. The listed actions include procedures to screen covered employees by obtaining and maintaining a disclosure of interests when the employee is initially assigned to the task [@CL-CMP-2347]. They also include requiring each covered employee to update the disclosure statement whenever the employee's personal or financial circumstances change in such a way that a new personal conflict of interest might occur because of the task [@CL-CMP-2467]. Two further listed requirements, for each covered employee, are to prohibit use of non-public information accessed through performance of a Government contract for personal gain, and to obtain a signed non-disclosure agreement to prohibit disclosure of non-public information accessed through performance of a Government contract [@CL-CMP-2348]. The contractor must report to the contracting officer any violation by a covered employee as soon as identified, with a description and the proposed actions, together with follow-up reports of corrective actions taken, as necessary [@CL-CMP-2349; @CL-CMP-7692]. The section lists further actions, including oversight and discipline, which this card does not restate.

The subpart implements policy on personal conflicts of interest by employees of Government contractors as required by section 2303 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-CMP-9724]. In the policy section, the Government's policy is to require contractors to identify and prevent personal conflicts of interest of their covered employees and to prohibit covered employees who have access to non-public information by reason of performance on a Government contract from using such information for personal gain [@CL-CMP-8716] [@CL-CMP-9725]. If the contracting officer suspects violation by the contractor of a requirement of paragraph (b), (c)(3), or (d) of the clause at FAR 52.203-16, Preventing Personal Conflicts of Interest, the contracting officer shall contact the agency legal counsel for advice and/or recommendations on a course of action [@CL-CMP-9726].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor assigns an analyst to review proposals for the agency. The analyst holds a large stock position in an offeror. The contractor obtains the analyst's disclosure at assignment. If the analyst later breaks the contractor's conflict safeguards, the contractor reports the violation to the contracting officer as soon as it is identified, with the actions it proposes to take.

## Never confuse

A personal conflict of interest is a situation in which a covered employee has a financial interest, personal activity, or relationship that could impair the employee's ability to act impartially and in the best interest of the Government when performing under the contract [@CL-CMP-2344]. For organizational conflicts of interest, see [Organizational conflicts of interest: general rules](../../mna/diligence/mna-organizational-conflicts-of-interest-general-rules.md). Duties to disclose credible evidence of violations are covered in [Contractor code of business ethics](cmp-contractor-code-of-business-ethics.md).
