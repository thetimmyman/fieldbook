## Purpose

Shows what an offeror must list in its offer when it wants the Government's rights in technical data or computer software restricted, the format of the list, what happens to the list after award, and how a restriction first asserted after award is handled for technical data.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-3788] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.227-7017 [@CL-CMP-3788] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3788] |
| Applies to | Technical data, including computer software documentation, or computer software to be delivered with other than unlimited rights [@CL-CMP-3788] |

The identification and assertion requirements in the provision apply only to technical data, including computer software documentation, or computer software to be delivered with other than unlimited rights [@CL-CMP-3788]. Offers shall identify, to the extent known at the time an offer is submitted to the Government, the technical data or computer software that the Offeror, its subcontractors or suppliers, or potential subcontractors or suppliers assert should be furnished to the Government with restrictions on use, release, or disclosure [@CL-CMP-3789; @CL-CMP-7055].

The Offeror's assertions, including those of its subcontractors or suppliers or potential subcontractors or suppliers, shall be submitted as an attachment to its offer in the stated format, dated and signed by an official authorized to contractually obligate the Offeror [@CL-CMP-3790]. The stated format has four headings: the technical data or computer software to be furnished with restrictions, the basis for assertion, the asserted rights category, and the name of the person asserting restrictions [@CL-CMP-3791]. An offeror's failure to submit, complete, or sign the notification and identification required by the format paragraph with its offer may render the offer ineligible for award [@CL-CMP-3792].

### After award

If the Offeror is awarded a contract, the assertions identified in the format paragraph of the provision shall be listed in an attachment to that contract [@CL-CMP-3823].

For technical data, the clause on rights in technical data adds that, in addition to the assertions made in the attachment to the contract, other assertions may be identified after award when based on new information or inadvertent omissions, unless the inadvertent omissions would have materially affected the source selection decision [@CL-CMP-3824]. An identification and assertion made after award shall be submitted to the Contracting Officer as soon as practicable prior to the scheduled date for delivery of the data, in the prescribed format, and signed by an official authorized to contractually obligate the Contractor [@CL-CMP-3825].

## How it works

The provision works at offer time, because offers identify what is asserted [@CL-CMP-3789]. The offeror lists each asserted restriction in the stated format and attaches the signed list to its offer [@CL-CMP-3790; @CL-CMP-3791]. A failure to submit, complete, or sign the list with the offer may make the offer ineligible for award [@CL-CMP-3792]. After award, the assertions go into an attachment to the contract, and a restriction first asserted later, for technical data, follows the paragraph in the technical data clause described above [@CL-CMP-3823; @CL-CMP-3824].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes to deliver a software tool and a design report. It lists the tool, states that the tool was developed at its own expense, names the rights category it asserts, and names itself as the person asserting. An official who can bind the company dates and signs the list and attaches it to the offer. Leaving the list unsigned could make the offer ineligible for award. If the offeror wins, the listed assertions go into an attachment to the contract. A year later the offeror learns of new information that supports one more assertion on the design report, and it sends the new assertion to the Contracting Officer, signed, before the scheduled delivery date.

## Never confuse

Restrictions come only by marking rather than by assertion alone: under the clause, a contractor and its subcontractors or suppliers may only assert restrictions on the Government's rights to use, modify, reproduce, release, perform, display, or disclose technical data to be delivered under the contract by marking the deliverable data subject to restriction [@CL-CMP-3809]; see [DoD rights in technical data: markings](cmp-dod-technical-data-markings.md). Validation of restrictions is covered in [validation for technical data](cmp-dod-technical-data-restriction-validation.md) and [validation for computer software](cmp-dod-software-restriction-validation.md). The FAR representation about limited rights data and restricted computer software is covered in [Representation of limited rights data and restricted computer software](cmp-representation-limited-rights-data-and-restricted-software.md).
