## Purpose

Shows how an agency identifies each procurement action with a procurement instrument identifier (PIID), how the contracting officer confirms a CAGE code before award, and which CAGE provisions and clause go into solicitations and contracts [@CL-CMP-7566; @CL-CMP-7570; @CL-CMP-7572].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-7566] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1602, 4.1803 and 4.1804 [@CL-CMP-7566; @CL-CMP-7570; @CL-CMP-7572] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-7566] |
| Applies to | Agency solicitations and contract award documentation, offerors whose CAGE code is verified before award, and solicitations and contracts that meet the insertion conditions for the CAGE provisions and clause [@CL-CMP-7566; @CL-CMP-7570; @CL-CMP-7572; @CL-CMP-7574] |

Agencies must include all PIIDs for all related procurement actions as identified in paragraphs (a)(1) through (5) of the section [@CL-CMP-7566]. The inclusion duty sits with the agency, and the contractor is not given a duty in these paragraphs [@CL-CMP-7566].

Each kind of action is identified as follows.

- Solicitations: the PIID for every solicitation, and for an amendment a supplementary PIID in conjunction with the PIID for the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7567].
- Contracts and purchase orders: the PIID [@CL-CMP-7713].
- Delivery and task orders under a contract, such as indefinite delivery indefinite quantity, multi-agency, Governmentwide acquisition or Multiple Award Schedule contracts: the PIID for the order and the PIID for the contract [@CL-CMP-7568].
- Blanket purchase agreements issued in accordance with 13.303, and basic agreements and basic ordering agreements issued in accordance with subpart 16.7: the PIID [@CL-CMP-7714].
- A blanket purchase agreement issued in accordance with subpart 8.4 under a Multiple Award Schedule contract: the PIID for the agreement and the PIID for the schedule contract [@CL-CMP-7726].
- An order against a basic ordering agreement or a blanket purchase agreement issued in accordance with 13.303: the PIID for the order and the PIID for the agreement [@CL-CMP-7715].
- An order against a blanket purchase agreement established under a Multiple Award Schedule contract: the PIIDs for the order, the agreement and the schedule contract [@CL-CMP-7716].
- A modification to one of the actions for contracts, purchase orders, delivery and task orders, or agreements: a supplementary PIID in conjunction with the PIID for the contract, order or agreement being modified, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CMP-7569].

When a form has no space or field for the PIID or supplementary PIID, the PIID is identified in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CMP-7717]. If agency procedures require additional identification information for administrative purposes, that information must be separate and clearly identified from the PIID [@CL-CMP-7718].

The contracting officer must verify the offeror's CAGE code by reviewing the entity's registration in the System for Award Management, and active registrations have had the associated CAGE codes verified [@CL-CMP-7570]. For an entity that is not required to be registered in the System for Award Management, the contracting officer must validate the CAGE code using the CAGE code search feature [@CL-CMP-7571].

The CAGE code reporting provision is inserted in all solicitations that include the unique entity identifier provision or the System for Award Management provision [@CL-CMP-7572]. The ownership or control of offeror provision and the predecessor of offeror provision are each inserted in all solicitations that include the CAGE code reporting provision [@CL-CMP-7573; @CL-CMP-7719]. The CAGE code maintenance clause is inserted in all solicitations and contracts when the solicitation contains the CAGE code reporting provision [@CL-CMP-7574]. The insertion paragraphs are written as directions and do not name who inserts.

## How it works

Identifiers are layered. A base PIID identifies the action, a supplementary PIID covers amendments and modifications, and orders carry the identifier of the contract or agreement they sit under [@CL-CMP-7567; @CL-CMP-7568; @CL-CMP-7569]. When a form has no space or field for the PIID or supplementary PIID, the PIID is identified in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CMP-7717]. If agency procedures require additional identification information for administrative purposes, that information must be separate and clearly identified from the PIID [@CL-CMP-7718].

The CAGE check is the contracting officer's step before award. The officer reviews the registration where the entity is registered and uses the search feature where it is not required to register [@CL-CMP-7570; @CL-CMP-7571]. The provisions then follow from the reporting provision: the ownership or control and predecessor provisions go into solicitations with it, and the maintenance clause goes into solicitations and contracts when the solicitation contains it [@CL-CMP-7573; @CL-CMP-7719; @CL-CMP-7574].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency issues a solicitation and then an amendment. The amendment carries a supplementary PIID together with the PIID for the solicitation [@CL-CMP-7567]. The agency later places a task order under the resulting indefinite delivery indefinite quantity contract. The order is identified by its own PIID and the PIID for the contract [@CL-CMP-7568]. A later modification to the task order carries a supplementary PIID together with the PIID for the task order, in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-CMP-7569].

Before award, the contracting officer reviews the first offeror's active registration in the System for Award Management to verify its CAGE code [@CL-CMP-7570]. A second offeror is not required to register there, so the officer validates its code with the CAGE code search feature [@CL-CMP-7571]. The solicitation includes the System for Award Management provision, so it also carries the CAGE code reporting provision and, because it contains that provision, the maintenance clause goes into the solicitation and the contract [@CL-CMP-7572; @CL-CMP-7574].

## Never confuse

A supplementary PIID is not a replacement for the base PIID, because it is used in conjunction with it [@CL-CMP-7567; @CL-CMP-7569]. An active registration in the System for Award Management is also not a separate check. The contracting officer's verification is the review of that registration, and the search feature applies to an entity not required to register [@CL-CMP-7570; @CL-CMP-7571]. For the PIID format, see [Procurement instrument identifiers and supplementary identifiers](../trade-cyber/cmp-piid-and-supplementary-piid.md).
