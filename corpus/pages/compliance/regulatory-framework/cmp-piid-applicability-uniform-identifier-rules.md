## Purpose

Sets out which procurement instruments the identifier rules reach, including amendments, modifications and change orders to them [@CL-CMP-8286].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8286] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1002 [@CL-CMP-8286] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8286] |
| Applies to | Solicitations, contracts, agreements with pre-priced supplies or services, and task and delivery orders, with their amendments, modifications and change orders [@CL-CMP-8286; @CL-CMP-8287; @CL-CMP-8288; @CL-CMP-8289; @CL-CMP-8290] |

The policies of the identifier subpart shall apply to the listed procurement instruments, and they reach amendments, modifications, and change orders to those instruments [@CL-CMP-8286]. Four kinds of instrument are listed. Solicitations are covered [@CL-CMP-8287]. Contracts are covered, including, but not limited to, Governmentwide acquisition contracts, multi-agency contracts, Federal Supply Schedule contracts, indefinite-delivery contracts, and purchase orders, so the contract list is not exhaustive [@CL-CMP-8288]. Agreements that include pre-priced supplies or services are covered [@CL-CMP-8289]. Task and delivery orders are covered [@CL-CMP-8290].

## How it works

The section works as a coverage list rather than as a set of duties: it states that the subpart's policies shall apply and then enumerates the instruments [@CL-CMP-8286]. It does not say who applies the policies. It names no approving official and sets no threshold, deadline or exception, and it does not tie coverage to the value of the instrument [@CL-CMP-8286; @CL-CMP-8288]. Because the reach extends to amendments, modifications and change orders, a later change to a covered instrument stays inside the same policies [@CL-CMP-8286]. Coverage turns on the type of instrument, and a purchase order is a covered contract [@CL-CMP-8288]. For how the identifier is built and kept unique, see [Procurement instrument identifier and supplementary identifier](../trade-cyber/cmp-piid-and-supplementary-piid.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A task order is placed against a multiple-award contract with a ceiling of 50 million. The task order is a covered instrument [@CL-CMP-8290]. A later modification to that same task order is covered too, because the policies reach modifications to covered instruments [@CL-CMP-8286]. A purchase order for 2,000 is also covered, because purchase orders are among the contracts listed [@CL-CMP-8288].

## Never confuse

Coverage of an instrument is not the same as its numbering format: the applicability section lists what is covered, while the part-wide definitions say what a unique identifier and a supplementary identifier are [@CL-CMP-8284; @CL-CMP-8286].
