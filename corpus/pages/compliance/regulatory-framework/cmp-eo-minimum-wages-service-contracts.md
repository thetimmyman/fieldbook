## Purpose

Records the Executive Order minimum wage rates that FAR 22.1002-5 states for service contracts [@CL-CMP-8087; @CL-CMP-8088], how the rate changes [@CL-CMP-8089], and the clause that requires the Executive Order 14026 rate to be paid if it is higher than other minimum wage rates [@CL-CMP-8091].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8087] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.1002-4 and FAR 22.1002-5 [@CL-CMP-8087; @CL-CMP-8149] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8087] |
| Applies to | Certain workers, as the section words it [@CL-CMP-8087; @CL-CMP-8088]; the clause at FAR 52.222-55 [@CL-CMP-8091] |

Executive Order 13658 established minimum wages for certain workers at $10.10 per hour [@CL-CMP-8087]. That rate has increased each year since 2015, rising to $11.25 on January 1, 2022 [@CL-CMP-8087].

As of January 30, 2022, Executive Order 13658 is superseded by Executive Order 14026 to the extent that it is inconsistent with Executive Order 14026, and the minimum wage rate for certain workers is increased to $15.00 per hour [@CL-CMP-8088].

The next sentence says the wage rate is subject to annual increases by an amount determined by the Secretary of Labor [@CL-CMP-8089]. The section then cross-references subpart 22.19 [@CL-CMP-8090].

The clause at FAR 52.222-55 requires the Executive Order 14026 minimum wage rate to be paid if it is higher than other minimum wage rates, such as the subpart 22.10 statutory wage determination amount [@CL-CMP-8091].

No contractor or subcontractor holding a service contract for any dollar amount shall pay any of its employees working on the contract less than the minimum wage specified in section 6(a)(1) of the Fair Labor Standards Act (title 29 of the United States Code, section 206) [@CL-CMP-8149].

## How it works

The Secretary of Labor determines the amount of each annual increase [@CL-CMP-8089]. The section gives no deadline for that determination. Subpart 22.19 is the cross-referenced place to look [@CL-CMP-8090]. The annual rate notice, enforcement and the coverage of that subpart are on [the Executive Order minimum wage and paid sick leave card](cmp-eo-minimum-wage-and-paid-sick-leave.md) and [the coverage and exclusions card](cmp-eo-wage-and-sick-leave-coverage-and-exclusions.md).

The clause carries the duty. It requires the Executive Order 14026 rate to be paid if that rate is higher than other minimum wage rates, such as the subpart 22.10 statutory wage determination amount [@CL-CMP-8091]. The sentence does not name who pays or who decides which rate is higher. It states no exception, and it says nothing about what the clause requires when the Executive Order rate is not higher.

The section says "certain workers" and does not define them in these sentences [@CL-CMP-8087; @CL-CMP-8088].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A wage determination amount for one class is $14.00 per hour. The Executive Order 14026 rate in effect for the work is $16.00 per hour. The clause requires the Executive Order 14026 rate to be paid if it is higher than the subpart 22.10 statutory wage determination amount [@CL-CMP-8091]. The $16.00 rate is higher, so the clause requires it to be paid.

For a second class, the wage determination amount is $18.00 per hour and the Executive Order rate is $16.00 per hour. The condition "if it is higher" is not met [@CL-CMP-8091]. This sentence of the section then adds no requirement for the Executive Order rate in that case.

## Never confuse

The clause compares the Executive Order 14026 rate with other minimum wage rates, such as the subpart 22.10 statutory wage determination amount [@CL-CMP-8091]. It requires the Executive Order 14026 rate to be paid if that rate is higher [@CL-CMP-8091].

The $15.00 rate is stated as of January 30, 2022 [@CL-CMP-8088]. The next sentence makes the wage rate subject to annual increases [@CL-CMP-8089]. Executive Order 14026 supersedes Executive Order 13658 only to the extent that the two are inconsistent [@CL-CMP-8088].
