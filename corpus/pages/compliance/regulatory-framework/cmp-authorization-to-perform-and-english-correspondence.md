## Purpose

Shows what an offeror represents about authority to do business where the contract is performed, and the language rule for correspondence addressed to the Government.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-CMP-4182; @CL-CMP-4183] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.225-7042 and 252.225-7041 [@CL-CMP-4182; @CL-CMP-4183] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4182; @CL-CMP-4183] |
| Applies to | Offerors, for the authorization representation, and the Contractor, for correspondence addressed to the United States Government [@CL-CMP-4182; @CL-CMP-4183] |

The offeror represents that it has been duly authorized to operate and to do business in the country or countries in which the contract is to be performed [@CL-CMP-4182]. The Contractor must ensure that all contract correspondence addressed to the United States Government is submitted in English or with an English translation [@CL-CMP-4183].

## How it works

The authorization representation speaks to the country or countries of performance [@CL-CMP-4182]. The language rule covers correspondence addressed to the United States Government and allows an English translation in place of English text [@CL-CMP-4183]. The printed provision does not say how authorization is documented or who checks it. For duties of contractor personnel working in a designated operational area outside the United States, see [the card on definitions and pre-deployment](cmp-deployed-contractor-personnel-definitions-and-predeployment.md) and [the private security card](cmp-private-security-functions-outside-the-united-states.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror plans performance in two countries. By submitting its offer it represents that it is duly authorized to operate and do business in both.

Later it sends a letter to the contracting office written in another language. It must send the letter in English or attach an English translation.

## Never confuse

The two rules are not the same kind of rule. One is a representation made by the offeror [@CL-CMP-4182], and the other is a duty of the Contractor on correspondence during the contract [@CL-CMP-4183].
