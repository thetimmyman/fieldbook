## Purpose

Explains what the System for Award Management subpart is for, and when a contracting officer must consult the Disaster Response Registry [@CL-CMP-8291; @CL-CMP-8293; @CL-CMP-8294].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-8291] |
| Codified citation | FAR 4.1100 and 4.1104 [@CL-CMP-8291; @CL-CMP-8293] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-8291] |
| Applies to | Contractor registration in SAM, and contracting officers contracting for disaster or emergency relief activities inside the United States and outlying areas [@CL-CMP-8291; @CL-CMP-8293; @CL-CMP-8294] |

The subpart prescribes policies and procedures requiring contractor registration in the System for Award Management, to increase visibility of vendor sources, including their geographical locations, for specific supplies and services [@CL-CMP-8291]. A second stated purpose is to establish a common source of vendor data for the Government [@CL-CMP-8292]. The scope section states those purposes and names no approving official, threshold or deadline [@CL-CMP-8291; @CL-CMP-8292].

Separately, contracting officers shall consult the Disaster Response Registry in SAM through its registry search [@CL-CMP-8293]. The duty applies when contracting for debris removal, distribution of supplies, reconstruction, and other disaster or emergency relief activities inside the United States and outlying areas [@CL-CMP-8294]. The section cross-refers to 26.205 [@CL-CMP-8593].

## How it works

Registration gives the Government one place to see who the vendors are and where they are [@CL-CMP-8291; @CL-CMP-8292]. The scope section states purposes. The contractor's own registration duties are in the registration clauses, described in [SAM registration and maintenance clauses: keeping the record current](cmp-sam-registration-and-maintenance-clauses.md) and in [SAM registration and the unique entity identifier in awards](../../market/feeds/mkt-sam-registration-and-awards.md) [@CL-CMP-8291].

The registry consultation is a duty on the contracting officer, not on the contractor [@CL-CMP-8293]. It is triggered by the subject of the buy, and the list of triggering activities ends with other disaster or emergency relief activities, so it is not limited to the first three named [@CL-CMP-8294]. The section limits the duty to work inside the United States and outlying areas [@CL-CMP-8294]. It does not say whether the consultation comes before or after award, and it states no threshold or exception [@CL-CMP-8293; @CL-CMP-8294].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting office buys debris removal after a storm for 2 million, with the work inside the United States. The contracting officer consults the Disaster Response Registry through the registry search in SAM, because debris removal is a listed activity inside the covered area [@CL-CMP-8293; @CL-CMP-8294]. A contracting office buying routine office supplies for a headquarters, with no disaster or emergency relief purpose, is not contracting for any of the listed activities, so this consultation duty is not triggered by that buy [@CL-CMP-8294].

## Never confuse

SAM registration and the Disaster Response Registry are not the same check: registration is the vendor data requirement, while the registry consultation is a step the contracting officer takes for disaster and emergency relief buys [@CL-CMP-8291; @CL-CMP-8293].
