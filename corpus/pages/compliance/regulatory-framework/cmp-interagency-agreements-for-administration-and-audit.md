## Purpose

Shows how agencies use interagency agreements to avoid duplicate audits and reviews of a contractor, who reimburses whom for the services, and how the DoD supplement treats payment from outside DoD.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-CMP-2550; @CL-CMP-2552] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.002 and DFARS 242.002 [@CL-CMP-2550; @CL-CMP-2552] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.001 [@CL-CMP-0123; @CL-CMP-0124] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-2550; @CL-CMP-2552] |
| Applies to | Agencies, for audits, reviews, inspections and examinations of contractors or subcontractors [@CL-CMP-2550] |

Agencies shall avoid duplicate audits, reviews, inspections and examinations of contractors or subcontractors by more than one agency, through the use of interagency agreements [@CL-CMP-2550]. Subject to the fiscal regulations of the agencies and applicable interagency agreements, the requesting agency shall reimburse the servicing agency for rendered services in accordance with the Economy Act [@CL-CMP-2551].

For DoD, the supplement requires reimbursement from non-DoD organizations at a rate set by the Under Secretary of Defense (Comptroller/Chief Financial Officer), except for listed cases [@CL-CMP-2552]. The listed exceptions include quality assurance, contract administration and audit services provided under a no-charge reciprocal agreement, and services performed under subcontracts awarded by the Small Business Administration under the small business subpart of the FAR [@CL-CMP-2553]. A further listed exception covers quality assurance requests performed for the Canadian Department of National Defence and pricing services performed for Public Works and Government Services Canada [@CL-CMP-2554].

## How it works

The servicing agency renders the service and the requesting agency reimburses it, subject to the fiscal regulations and the agreement [@CL-CMP-2551]. A department or agency may ask the Under Secretary of Defense (Comptroller/Chief Financial Officer) for an exception to the DoD reimbursement policy, and the request must show that an exception is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-CMP-2555]. Departments and agencies must pay for services performed by non-DoD activities, foreign governments or international organizations, unless reciprocal agreements provide otherwise [@CL-CMP-2556]. Foreign governments and international organizations may request contract administration services on their direct purchases from U.S. producers [@CL-CMP-2557], and other foreign governments, including Canadian government organizations other than the Canadian public services agency named in the section, and international organizations send their requests for contract administration services to the DoD Central Control Point at the Headquarters, Defense Contract Management Agency, International and Federal Business Team, and contract administration offices provide services only upon request from the Central Control Point [@CL-CMP-2558; @CL-CMP-7876]. The functions themselves are listed on [Contract administration functions](../audit-retention/cmp-contract-administration-functions.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0050, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.001(a) says agencies must use interagency agreements to prevent duplicate audits, reviews, inspections and examinations of contractors or subcontractors by more than one agency, where the codified paragraph says agencies shall avoid them through the use of interagency agreements [@CL-CMP-0123; @CL-CMP-2550]. RFO FAR 42.001(b) says that, subject to the fiscal regulations of the agencies and applicable interagency agreements, the requesting agency must pay the servicing agency for services rendered in accordance with the Economy Act, where the codified paragraph says shall reimburse the servicing agency for rendered services [@CL-CMP-0124; @CL-CMP-2551].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A civilian agency asks a DoD office to perform an audit, and the work is worth 40,000 at the set rate. No exception applies and there is no reciprocal agreement. The civilian agency is a non-DoD organization, so DoD requires it to reimburse DoD for the work, and no second agency repeats the audit.

## Never confuse

Agencies shall avoid duplicate audits, reviews, inspections and examinations of contractors or subcontractors by more than one agency, through the use of interagency agreements [@CL-CMP-2550]. For interagency acquisitions, see [Interagency acquisitions](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-interagency-acquisitions.md). The roles of the offices that perform the services are on [Oversight roles](../audit-retention/cmp-oversight-roles.md).

The section number 42.002 is interagency agreements in the codified text [@CL-CMP-2550] but the cognizant Federal agency in the RFO text [@CL-CMP-0007], and the RFO section on interagency agreements is numbered 42.001 [@CL-CMP-0123].
