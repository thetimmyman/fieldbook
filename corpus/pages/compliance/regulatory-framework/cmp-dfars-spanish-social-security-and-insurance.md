## Purpose

Shows what the Contractor must pay, prove and insure in Spain under the two Spanish clauses, and what the Government may do if the social security clause is not met.

### Social security clause

The Contractor shall comply with all Spanish Government social security laws and regulations [@CL-CMP-4433]. Within 30 calendar days after the start of contract performance, the Contractor shall ensure that copies of the documents identified in paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(5) of the clause are available at all reasonable times for inspection by the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative [@CL-CMP-4434]. The first four documents are the TC1 Certificate of Social Security Payments, the TC2 List of Employees, the TC2/1 Certificate of Social Security Payments for Trainees, and the Nominal pay statements signed by both the employee and the Contractor [@CL-CMP-4435]. The fifth is the Informa de Situacion de Empressa, the Report of the Condition of the Enterprise from the Spanish labor ministry and social security treasury, annotated with the pertinent contract number(s) next to the employee's name [@CL-CMP-4466]. The Contractor shall retain the records in accordance with the Audit and Records clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-4467].

All TC1's, TC2's, and TC2/1's shall contain a representation that they have been paid by either the Social Security Administration office or the Contractor's bank or savings institution [@CL-CMP-4436]. Failure to comply with the requirements of the clause may result in termination of the contract under the Default clause of the contract [@CL-CMP-4437].

### Spanish laws and insurance clause

The requirements of the clause apply only if the Contractor is not a Spanish concern [@CL-CMP-4439]. Without additional expense to the United States Government, the Contractor shall comply with all applicable Spanish Government laws pertaining to sanitation, traffic, security, employment of labor, and all other laws relevant to the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4440]. The Contractor shall hold the United States Government harmless and free from any liability resulting from its failure to comply with such laws [@CL-CMP-4441].

At its own expense, the Contractor shall provide and maintain during the entire performance of the contract all workmen's compensation, employees' liability, bodily injury insurance, and other required insurance adequate to cover the risk it assumed [@CL-CMP-4442]. It shall also indemnify and hold harmless the United States Government from liability resulting from all claims for damages as a result of death or injury to personnel or damage to real or personal property related to the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4443].

The Contractor must represent in writing to the Contracting Officer, prior to commencement of work and not later than 15 days after the date of the Notice to Proceed, that it has obtained the required types of insurance in the stated minimum amounts [@CL-CMP-4444]. The table lists Comprehensive General Liability coverage of $300,000 per person, $1,000,000 per accident, and $100,000 for property damage [@CL-CMP-4445]. The representation shall also state that the Contractor will promptly notify the Contracting Officer of any notice of cancellation of insurance or material change in insurance coverage that could affect the United States Government's interests [@CL-CMP-4468]. The Contractor shall provide a similar representation for all subcontracts with non-Spanish concerns that will perform work in Spain under the contract [@CL-CMP-4446].

The policies shall be purchased from Spanish insurance companies or other insurance companies legally authorized to conduct business in Spain [@CL-CMP-4447]. They shall conform to Spanish laws and regulations and shall contain or omit the provisions listed in paragraphs (f)(1) through (f)(5) of the clause [@CL-CMP-4469]. The policies shall contain provisions requiring submission to Spanish law and jurisdiction of any problem about the interpretation or application of the clauses and conditions of the policy [@CL-CMP-4470]. They shall contain a provision authorizing the insurance company, as subrogee of the insured entity, to assume and attend to directly, with respect to any person damaged, the legal consequences arising from the occurrence of such damages [@CL-CMP-4471]. They shall contain a provision worded as follows: the insurance company waives any right of subrogation against the United States of America that may arise by reason of any payment under the policy [@CL-CMP-4472]. They shall not contain any deductible amount or similar limitation or any provisions requiring submission to any type of arbitration [@CL-CMP-4448].

## How it works

Under the social security clause the Contractor shall comply with the Spanish social security laws and regulations and shall ensure that copies of the five listed documents are available for inspection within 30 calendar days after the start of contract performance [@CL-CMP-4433; @CL-CMP-4434; @CL-CMP-4435; @CL-CMP-4466]. All TC1's, TC2's, and TC2/1's shall contain a representation that they have been paid, either by the Social Security Administration office or by the Contractor's bank or savings institution [@CL-CMP-4436]. Failure to comply with the requirements of that clause may result in termination of the contract under the Default clause [@CL-CMP-4437]. The requirements of the insurance clause apply only if the Contractor is not a Spanish concern [@CL-CMP-4439]; that Contractor agrees to represent in writing, prior to commencement of work and not later than 15 days after the date of the Notice to Proceed, that it has obtained the required types of insurance in the stated minimum amounts, and shall provide a similar representation for all subcontracts with non-Spanish concerns that will perform work in Spain [@CL-CMP-4444; @CL-CMP-4446]. The Contractor shall also hold the Government harmless from liability resulting from its failure to comply with the Spanish laws, and indemnify and hold it harmless from liability resulting from claims for damages as a result of death or injury to personnel or damage to real or personal property related to the performance of the contract [@CL-CMP-4441; @CL-CMP-4443].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A non-Spanish Contractor receives the Notice to Proceed on day 10 and plans to begin work on day 20. On day 15 it gives the Contracting Officer a written representation of cover. The cover is Comprehensive General Liability of 300,000 per person, 1,000,000 per accident and 100,000 for property damage, the minimum amounts the clause lists. The policy has a 5,000 deductible. Answer: day 15 is before work begins and not later than 15 days after the Notice to Proceed, which is day 25, so the timing is met. The 5,000 deductible breaks the rule that the policies contain no deductible amount. Assumed for the example: the amounts held equal the minimums and the Contractor has no subcontract with a non-Spanish concern.

## Never confuse

The social security duty is a compliance duty rather than a records duty in itself: the Contractor shall comply with all Spanish Government social security laws and regulations [@CL-CMP-4433], and the Contractor shall retain the records in accordance with the Audit and Records clause [@CL-CMP-4467]; for negotiated contracts see [Audit and Records, Negotiation clause](../audit-retention/cmp-audit-and-records-negotiation.md), and for the default clauses for fixed-price contracts see [Default clauses for fixed-price contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-default-clauses.md). For whether insurance cost is allowable, see [Insurance and indemnification](../cas-allowability/cmp-insurance-and-indemnification.md). For the duty to follow local labor law in general, see [Compliance with local labor laws overseas](cmp-dfars-local-labor-laws-overseas.md); for the Italian permit, see [Permit from the Italian Inspectorate of Labor](cmp-dfars-italian-labor-permit.md); for Spanish tax exemptions, see [Spain tax exemptions and the matching representation](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-dfars-spain-tax-exemptions-and-representation.md).
