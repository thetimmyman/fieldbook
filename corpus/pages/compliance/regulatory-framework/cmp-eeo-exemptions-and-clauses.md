## Purpose

Lists the main exemptions from the equal employment opportunity requirements, with their conditions. Names the provisions and clauses the contracting officer inserts and the contracts they go in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3063] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.807 and FAR 22.810 [@CL-CMP-3063; @CL-CMP-3069] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3063] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to Executive Order 11246, with the exemptions and insertion conditions listed in the rule [@CL-CMP-0477; @CL-CMP-3064; @CL-CMP-3069] |

Under the exemptions in FAR 22.807(a), all or part of the requirements of Executive Order 11246 may be excluded from a contract subject to Executive Order 11246 [@CL-CMP-0477]. Under one exemption, the agency head determines that a contract is essential to the national security and that the award of the contract without complying with one or more of the requirements of the subpart is necessary to the national security [@CL-CMP-0477; @CL-CMP-3063]. Upon making such a determination, the agency shall notify the Deputy Assistant Secretary in writing within 30 days [@CL-CMP-3132]. Under another exemption, the Deputy Assistant Secretary may exempt an agency from requiring the inclusion of one or more of the requirements of Executive Order 11246 in any contract if the Deputy Assistant Secretary deems that special circumstances in the national interest so require [@CL-CMP-0477; @CL-CMP-3064]. The exemptions in FAR 22.807(b) apply even though a contract or subcontract contains the Equal Opportunity clause [@CL-CMP-0478]. Under paragraph (b)(1), the Equal Opportunity clause is required to be included in prime contracts and subcontracts by FAR 22.802(a), and individual prime contracts or subcontracts of $10,000 or less are exempt from application of the Equal Opportunity clause, unless the aggregate value of all prime contracts or subcontracts awarded to a contractor in any 12-month period exceeds, or can reasonably be expected to exceed, $10,000 [@CL-CMP-0478; @CL-CMP-3065]. A note to that exemption states that Government bills of lading, regardless of amount, are not exempt [@CL-CMP-3136]. Contracts are exempt from the requirements of the Equal Opportunity clause for work performed outside the United States by employees who were not recruited within the United States [@CL-CMP-0478; @CL-CMP-3066]. The requirements of the Equal Opportunity clause in any contract with a State or local government (or any agency, instrumentality, or subdivision thereof) shall not be applicable to any agency, instrumentality, or subdivision of such government that does not participate in work on or under the contract [@CL-CMP-0478; @CL-CMP-3067]. For indefinite-quantity contracts and subcontracts, the Equal Opportunity clause applies unless the contracting officer has reason to believe that the amount to be ordered in any year under such contract will not exceed $10,000 [@CL-CMP-0478; @CL-CMP-3068]. The applicability of the Equal Opportunity clause shall be determined by the contracting officer at the time of award for the first year, and annually thereafter for succeeding years, if any [@CL-CMP-0479]. Notwithstanding the preceding sentence, the Equal Opportunity clause shall be applied to such contract whenever the amount of a single order exceeds $10,000 [@CL-CMP-0479; @CL-CMP-3133]. Once the Equal Opportunity clause is determined to be applicable, the contract shall continue to be subject to such clause for its duration, regardless of the amounts ordered, or reasonably expected to be ordered in any year [@CL-CMP-3134].

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.222-26, Equal Opportunity, in solicitations and contracts unless the contract is exempt from all of the requirements of Executive Order 11246 [@CL-CMP-3069]. If the contract is exempt from one or more, but not all, of the requirements of Executive Order 11246, the contracting officer shall use the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CMP-3135]. When a contract is contemplated that will include the clause at FAR 52.222-26, Equal Opportunity, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.222-21, Prohibition of Segregated Facilities, in the solicitation and contract, and the provision at FAR 52.222-22, Previous Contracts and Compliance Reports, in the solicitation [@CL-CMP-3070; @CL-CMP-0480]. The contracting officer shall insert the provision at FAR 52.222-23, Notice of Requirement for Affirmative Action to Ensure Equal Employment Opportunity for Construction, in solicitations for construction when a contract is contemplated that will include the clause at FAR 52.222-26, Equal Opportunity, and the amount of the contract is expected to be in excess of $10,000 [@CL-CMP-3071; @CL-CMP-3072]. The contracting officer shall insert the provision at FAR 52.222-24, Preaward On-Site Equal Opportunity Compliance Evaluation, in solicitations other than those for construction when a contract is contemplated that will include the clause at FAR 52.222-26, Equal Opportunity, and the amount of the contract is expected to be $10 million or more [@CL-CMP-3073; @CL-CMP-3074].

## How it works

Under the exemptions in FAR 22.807(a), all or part of the requirements of Executive Order 11246 may be excluded from a contract subject to Executive Order 11246 by a determination of the agency head for national security or an exemption by the Deputy Assistant Secretary for special circumstances in the national interest [@CL-CMP-0477; @CL-CMP-3063; @CL-CMP-3064]. The construction notice provision is inserted in solicitations for construction when a contract is contemplated that will include the Equal Opportunity clause and the amount of the contract is expected to be in excess of $10,000 [@CL-CMP-3071; @CL-CMP-3072]. The preaward on-site provision is inserted in solicitations other than those for construction when a contract is contemplated that will include the Equal Opportunity clause and the amount of the contract is expected to be $10 million or more [@CL-CMP-3073; @CL-CMP-3074]. For terms such as the Equal Opportunity clause, see [Equal employment opportunity: definitions and what the Executive Order requires](cmp-eeo-definitions-and-general-rules.md), and for the preaward clearance process, see [Equal employment opportunity: construction goals and preaward clearance](cmp-eeo-construction-affirmative-action-and-preaward-clearance.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor wins one prime contract worth 6,000 and expects no other awards in any 12-month period. The answer: the contract is within the small-transaction exemption, so the Equal Opportunity clause does not apply to it. If the contractor also won a second contract worth 7,000 in the same 12 months, the combined 13,000 would exceed 10,000, and the clause would apply.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer shall insert the Equal Opportunity clause in solicitations and contracts unless the contract is exempt from all of the requirements of Executive Order 11246 [@CL-CMP-3069]. If the contract is exempt from one or more, but not all, of the requirements of Executive Order 11246, the contracting officer shall use the Equal Opportunity clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CMP-3135]. Under FAR 22.807(b), which lists exemptions that apply even though a contract or subcontract contains the Equal Opportunity clause, individual prime contracts or subcontracts of $10,000 or less are exempt from application of the Equal Opportunity clause, unless the aggregate value of all prime contracts or subcontracts awarded to a contractor in any 12-month period exceeds, or can reasonably be expected to exceed, $10,000 [@CL-CMP-0478; @CL-CMP-3065]. For indefinite-quantity contracts and subcontracts, the Equal Opportunity clause applies unless the contracting officer has reason to believe that the amount to be ordered in any year under the contract will not exceed $10,000 [@CL-CMP-0478; @CL-CMP-3068].
