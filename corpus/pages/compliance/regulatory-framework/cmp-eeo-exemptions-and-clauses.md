## Purpose

Lists the main exemptions from the equal employment opportunity requirements, with their conditions. Names the provisions and clauses the contracting officer inserts and the contracts they go in.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-3063] |
| Codified citation | FAR 22.807 and FAR 22.810 [@CL-CMP-3063; @CL-CMP-3069] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-3063] |
| Applies to | Contracts subject to the Executive Order, with the exemptions and insertion conditions listed in the rule [@CL-CMP-3064; @CL-CMP-3069] |

Listed exemptions include one that lets the agency head determine that a contract is essential to the national security and that award without complying with one or more of the requirements is necessary to the national security [@CL-CMP-3063]. Upon making a national security determination under that exemption, the agency shall notify the Deputy Assistant Secretary in writing within 30 days [@CL-CMP-3132]. Another lets the Deputy Assistant Secretary exempt an agency from requiring the inclusion of one or more of the requirements in any contract if special circumstances in the national interest so require [@CL-CMP-3064]. Individual prime contracts or subcontracts of $10,000 or less are exempt from application of the Equal Opportunity clause, unless the aggregate value of all prime contracts or subcontracts awarded to a contractor in any 12-month period exceeds, or can reasonably be expected to exceed, $10,000 [@CL-CMP-3065]. A note to that exemption states that Government bills of lading, regardless of amount, are not exempt [@CL-CMP-3136]. Contracts are exempt from the requirements for work performed outside the United States by employees who were not recruited within the United States [@CL-CMP-3066]. The requirements in any contract with a State or local government are not applicable to any agency, instrumentality, or subdivision of that government that does not participate in work on or under the contract [@CL-CMP-3067]. For indefinite-quantity contracts and subcontracts, the Equal Opportunity clause applies unless the contracting officer has reason to believe that the amount to be ordered in any year under the contract will not exceed $10,000 [@CL-CMP-3068]. For an indefinite-quantity contract, the clause shall be applied to the contract whenever the amount of a single order exceeds $10,000 [@CL-CMP-3133]. Once the clause is determined to be applicable to an indefinite-quantity contract, the contract continues to be subject to the clause for its duration regardless of the amounts ordered, or reasonably expected to be ordered, in any year [@CL-CMP-3134].

The contracting officer shall insert the Equal Opportunity clause in solicitations and contracts unless the contract is exempt from all of the requirements of the Executive Order [@CL-CMP-3069]. If the contract is exempt from one or more, but not all, of the requirements, the contracting officer shall use the Equal Opportunity clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CMP-3135]. When a contract is contemplated that will include that clause, the contracting officer shall insert the Prohibition of Segregated Facilities clause in the solicitation and contract [@CL-CMP-3070]. The contracting officer shall insert the Notice of Requirement for Affirmative Action to Ensure Equal Employment Opportunity for Construction provision in solicitations for construction when a contract is contemplated that will include the Equal Opportunity clause [@CL-CMP-3071], and the amount of the contract is expected to be in excess of $10,000 [@CL-CMP-3072]. The contracting officer shall insert the Preaward On-Site Equal Opportunity Compliance Evaluation provision in solicitations other than those for construction when a contract is contemplated that will include the Equal Opportunity clause [@CL-CMP-3073], and the amount of the contract is expected to be $10 million or more [@CL-CMP-3074].

## How it works

The first two exemptions are decided by the agency head or by the Deputy Assistant Secretary [@CL-CMP-3063; @CL-CMP-3064]. The small-transaction, place-of-work, State or local government and indefinite-quantity exemptions turn on the tests in the rule [@CL-CMP-3065; @CL-CMP-3066; @CL-CMP-3067; @CL-CMP-3068]. The insertion paragraph then ties the construction notice provision and the preaward on-site provision to the type of solicitation and the expected amount [@CL-CMP-3072; @CL-CMP-3074]. Terms such as the Equal Opportunity clause are defined on [Equal employment opportunity: definitions and what the Executive Order requires](cmp-eeo-definitions-and-general-rules.md), and the preaward clearance process is on [Equal employment opportunity: construction goals and preaward clearance](cmp-eeo-construction-affirmative-action-and-preaward-clearance.md). The exemption section also lists exemptions for work on or near Indian reservations, for facilities not connected with contracts and for religious entities, the timing of the indefinite-quantity test, how an exemption is requested, and a rule on withdrawing an exemption, and the insertion section lists further provisions and clauses, and this card does not set those out.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor wins one prime contract worth 6,000 and expects no other awards in any 12-month period. The answer: the contract is within the small-transaction exemption, so the Equal Opportunity clause does not apply to it. If the contractor also won a second contract worth 7,000 in the same 12 months, the combined 13,000 would exceed 10,000, and the clause would apply.

## Never confuse

The clause is inserted unless the contract is exempt from all of the requirements [@CL-CMP-3069], so an exemption from only some requirements does not by itself remove it, and the contracting officer then uses the clause with its Alternate I [@CL-CMP-3135]. The small-transaction exemption looks at the aggregate value of contracts in any 12-month period, while the indefinite-quantity test looks at the amount to be ordered in any year [@CL-CMP-3065; @CL-CMP-3068].
