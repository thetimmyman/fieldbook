## Purpose

Shows how much of a construction contract the contractor must perform itself, who must superintend and direct the work, and what organization chart a cost-reimbursement construction contract calls for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-CMP-4854] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.236-1, FAR 52.236-6 and FAR 52.236-19 [@CL-CMP-4854; @CL-CMP-4856; @CL-CMP-4857] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.236-6 [@CL-CMP-0846]; no RFO equivalent for FAR 52.236-1 and FAR 52.236-19 [@CL-CMP-0847; @CL-CMP-0848] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-CMP-4854] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clauses. The organization and direction clause is prescribed for solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement construction contract is contemplated [@CL-CMP-5024] |

### Performance of work by the contractor

The contractor must perform on the site, and with its own organization, work equivalent to at least the percentage of the total amount of work that the clause states [@CL-CMP-4854]. The percentage is a blank that each contract fills in, consistent with the complexity and magnitude of the work and customary or necessary specialty subcontracting [@CL-CMP-4854; @CL-CMP-5092]. It may be reduced by a supplemental agreement if, while performing the work, the contractor requests a reduction and the contracting officer determines that the reduction would be to the advantage of the Government [@CL-CMP-4855].

### Superintendence

At all times during performance of the contract, and until the work is completed and accepted, the contractor must directly superintend the work or assign and have on the work a competent superintendent who is satisfactory to the contracting officer and has authority to act for the contractor [@CL-CMP-4856].

### Organization and direction of the work

When the contract is executed, the contractor must submit to the contracting officer a chart showing the general executive and administrative organization, the personnel to be employed in connection with the work, and their respective duties [@CL-CMP-4857]. The contractor must keep the data furnished current by supplementing it as additional information becomes available [@CL-CMP-5025].

Work performance must be under the full-time resident direction [@CL-CMP-4858] of the contractor if it is an individual, of one or more principal partners if it is a partnership, or of one or more senior officers if it is a corporation, association or similar legal entity [@CL-CMP-4859]. If the contracting officer approves, the contractor may be represented in the direction of the work by a specific person or persons holding positions other than those identified [@CL-CMP-4860].

## How it works

The contractor must perform on the site, and with its own organization, work equivalent to at least the stated percentage of the total amount of work [@CL-CMP-4854], and the percentage may be reduced by a supplemental agreement if the contractor requests a reduction while performing the work and the contracting officer determines that the reduction would be to the advantage of the Government [@CL-CMP-4855]. The superintendent must be satisfactory to the contracting officer and have authority to act for the contractor, and the duty runs until the work is completed and accepted [@CL-CMP-4856]. Under the organization and direction clause the contractor submits the organization chart when the contract is executed [@CL-CMP-4857] and keeps the data current [@CL-CMP-5025], and work performance is under the full-time resident direction of the persons listed [@CL-CMP-4858; @CL-CMP-4859]. If the contracting officer approves, the contractor may be represented in the direction of the work by a specific person or persons holding positions other than those identified [@CL-CMP-4860].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO superintendence clause says that during performance of the contract and until the work is completed and accepted, the contractor must directly superintend the work or assign and have on site a competent superintendent who is satisfactory to the contracting officer and has authority to act for the contractor [@CL-CMP-0846; @CL-CMP-4856]. The RFO sentence begins "During performance of this contract" and says "on site" [@CL-CMP-0846], where the codified sentence begins "At all times during performance of this contract" and says "on the work" [@CL-CMP-4856]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.236-1 and FAR 52.236-19 each as reserved [@CL-CMP-0847; @CL-CMP-0848], where the codified text prints the percentage clause at FAR 52.236-1 [@CL-CMP-4854] and the organization chart and full-time resident direction clause at FAR 52.236-19 [@CL-CMP-4857; @CL-CMP-4858].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement construction contract that includes all three clauses fills the self-performance blank with 40 percent [@CL-CMP-4854; @CL-CMP-5092], and the total work is 5,000,000 in value. The contractor must perform at least 2,000,000 of work on the site with its own organization [@CL-CMP-4854]. Partway through, it requests a reduction to 25 percent, which would be 1,250,000. The contracting officer determines that the reduction would not be to the advantage of the Government, and the percentage stays at 40 percent [@CL-CMP-4855]. Under the RFO text, FAR 52.236-1 is printed as reserved [@CL-CMP-0847], where the codified text prints the percentage clause [@CL-CMP-4854].

The contractor is a corporation, so work performance is under the full-time resident direction of one or more senior officers [@CL-CMP-4858; @CL-CMP-4859], and it proposes that a project engineer who is not a senior officer represent it in directing the work. If the contracting officer approves, the contractor may be represented in the direction of the work by a specific person holding a position other than those identified [@CL-CMP-4860]. The contractor also names a site manager who cannot commit the company, but the superintendent it assigns and has on the work must have authority to act for the contractor [@CL-CMP-4856]. When the contract is executed the contractor submits the organization chart [@CL-CMP-4857], and when a new foreman joins it supplements the data [@CL-CMP-5025]. Under the RFO text, FAR 52.236-19 is printed as reserved [@CL-CMP-0848], where the codified text prints the chart and full-time resident direction clause [@CL-CMP-4857; @CL-CMP-4858].

## Never confuse

The percentage applies to work performed on the site with the contractor's own organization [@CL-CMP-4854]. Superintendence is the duty to directly superintend the work or assign and have on the work a competent superintendent [@CL-CMP-4856]. The organization chart shows the general executive and administrative organization, the personnel and their duties [@CL-CMP-4857], and the superintendent must be satisfactory to the contracting officer [@CL-CMP-4856]. Resident direction comes from the contractor, principal partners or senior officers, and if the contracting officer approves, the contractor may be represented by a specific person or persons holding other positions [@CL-CMP-4859; @CL-CMP-4860]. Construction labor standards disputes are covered in [Construction labor standards disputes](cmp-construction-labor-disputes-and-contract-clauses.md). The codified text prints the percentage clause and the resident direction duty at FAR 52.236-1 and FAR 52.236-19 [@CL-CMP-4854; @CL-CMP-4858], while the RFO text prints both sections as reserved [@CL-CMP-0847; @CL-CMP-0848]. The RFO superintendence sentence begins "During performance of this contract" [@CL-CMP-0846], where the codified sentence begins "At all times during performance of this contract" [@CL-CMP-4856].
