## Purpose

Defines parametric estimating and the cost estimating relationship behind it.

Parametric estimating uses cost estimating relationships to estimate the cost of developing, making, or modifying an end item [@CL-EST-0735]. A cost estimating relationship is a quantifiable correlation between system costs and other variables, using independent variables to predict a dependent variable such as cost [@CL-EST-0736; @CL-EST-0737].

## How it works

Parametric cost estimating is a technique that employs one or more cost estimating relationships to estimate costs of development, manufacture, or modification of an end item [@CL-EST-0735]. Each relationship uses one or more independent variables to predict or estimate a dependent variable, for example cost [@CL-EST-0737].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost estimating relationship predicts cost from weight: cost equals 1,000 per kilogram. A 2-kilogram part is estimated at 2,000.

## Never confuse

Parametric estimating is the technique, while a cost estimating relationship is the correlation the technique employs [@CL-EST-0735; @CL-EST-0736]. The variables may be of a cost or technical nature, so a relationship is not necessarily built on a technical variable [@CL-EST-0736].

## Company calibration

That each cost estimating relationship is supported and validated against actual outcomes. [@Q-023]
