## Purpose

Defines parametric estimating and the cost estimating relationship behind it.

Parametric cost estimating has been defined as a technique employing one or more cost estimating relationships to estimate costs associated with the development, manufacture, or modification of an end item [@CL-EST-0735]. A cost estimating relationship expresses a quantifiable correlation between certain system costs and other system variables, and such relationships are said to represent the use of one or more independent variables to predict or estimate a dependent variable such as cost [@CL-EST-0736; @CL-EST-0737].

## How it works

Parametric cost estimating has been defined as a technique that employs one or more cost estimating relationships to estimate costs of development, manufacture, or modification of an end item [@CL-EST-0735]. Cost estimating relationships are said to represent the use of one or more independent variables to predict or estimate a dependent variable, for example cost [@CL-EST-0737].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost estimating relationship predicts cost from weight: cost equals 1,000 per kilogram. A 2-kilogram part is estimated at 2,000.

## Never confuse

Parametric estimating is the technique, while a cost estimating relationship is the correlation the technique employs [@CL-EST-0735; @CL-EST-0736]. The other system variables may be of a cost or technical nature, so a relationship is not necessarily built on a technical variable [@CL-EST-0736].

## Company calibration

That each cost estimating relationship is supported and validated against actual outcomes. [@Q-023]
