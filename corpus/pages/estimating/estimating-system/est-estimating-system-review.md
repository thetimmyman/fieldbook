## Purpose

Explains how reviewing an estimating system narrows the review work done on individual proposals, and how the DoD rules assign the review, the report and the determinations between the auditor and the contracting officer.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-0768] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.407-5-70 [@CL-EST-0768] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0768] |
| Applies to | Large business contractors meeting the preceding fiscal year thresholds [@CL-EST-0769; @CL-EST-0770] |

The FAR side of the subject sits in Part 15: an acceptable estimating system benefits both the Government and the contractor by increasing the accuracy and reliability of individual proposals [@CL-EST-0763]. Where appropriate, cognizant audit activities establish and manage regular programs for reviewing selected contractors' estimating systems or methods, in order to reduce the scope of reviews performed on individual proposals [@CL-EST-0764].

The DoD section defines the estimating system as the contractor's policies, procedures and practices for budgeting and planning controls and for generating the cost estimates and other data in proposals submitted in the expectation of receiving a contract award [@CL-EST-0768]. A large business contractor is subject to the disclosure, maintenance and review requirements if, in its preceding fiscal year, it received DoD prime contracts or subcontracts totaling $50 million or more for which certified cost or pricing data were required [@CL-EST-0769]. A second, lower band covers a contractor with such contracts totaling $10 million or more but less than $50 million [@CL-EST-0770]. For that band the contracting officer, with concurrence or at the request of the ACO, determines whether applying the requirements is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-EST-0861]. The clause side of these thresholds is on [Contractor estimating system requirements](est-estimating-system-criteria.md).

## How it works

Review results are documented in survey reports [@CL-EST-0765], and the auditor sends a copy of the survey report and of the official notice of corrective action required to each contracting office and contract administration office having substantial business with the contractor [@CL-EST-0766]. Significant deficiencies not corrected by the contractor are a consideration in later proposal analyses and negotiations [@CL-EST-0767].

Under the DoD section the auditor conducts the reviews [@CL-EST-0864]. The auditor documents findings and recommendations in a report to the contracting officer, and a report of material weaknesses describes the underlying deficiencies in enough detail for the contracting officer to understand them [@CL-EST-0865]. The contracting officer then decides: if material weaknesses are found, an initial determination follows with a 30-day written response [@CL-EST-0862; @CL-EST-0771], and if a final determination finds that weaknesses remain, the contractor has 45 days to correct them or submit a corrective action plan [@CL-EST-0863; @CL-EST-0772]. The contractor-side steps and the withholding consequence are on [Contractor estimating system requirements](est-estimating-system-criteria.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A large business contractor's preceding fiscal year carries 60 million of DoD prime contracts requiring certified cost or pricing data, so the disclosure, maintenance and review requirements apply. Had the same year carried 20 million instead, they would apply only if the contracting officer determined it to be in the best interest of the Government.

## Never confuse

The estimating system review is not an individual proposal audit: the survey report documents the system that produces estimates, and uncorrected significant deficiencies carry forward into later proposal analyses and negotiations [@CL-EST-0765; @CL-EST-0767]. The review checks the system that generates estimates, which is defined by its policies, procedures and practices for budgeting, planning controls and proposal estimates [@CL-EST-0768]. For withholding, see [Contractor business systems](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-contractor-business-systems.md) and [Business system withholds](../../compliance/business-systems/cmp-business-system-withholds.md).
