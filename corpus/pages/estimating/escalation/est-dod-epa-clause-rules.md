## Purpose

Shows when DoD says to use the three FAR economic price adjustment clauses and when it prescribes its own special clauses for steel and mill products and for foreign-government controlled wages or prices.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1175] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 216.203-4 and DFARS 216.203-4-70 [@CL-EST-1175; @CL-EST-1177] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1175] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use the economic price adjustment clauses named in the DFARS text [@CL-EST-1175; @CL-EST-1177] |

The DFARS says to use the clauses at FAR 52.216-2, 52.216-3 and 52.216-4 generally only when the conditions that follow are met [@CL-EST-1175]. The two conditions are that the total contract price exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and that delivery or performance will not be completed within 6 months after contract award [@CL-EST-1176]. The printed DFARS text does not state the amount of the threshold.

The DFARS also prescribes three special clauses. The price adjustment clause for basic steel, aluminum, brass, bronze, or copper mill products may be used in fixed-price supply solicitations and contracts for those products when an established catalog or market price exists for the particular product being acquired [@CL-EST-1177]. The nonstandard steel clause may be used in fixed-price supply contracts when the contractor is a steel producer that makes the standard steel mill item in the base steel index definition and the items bought are nonstandard steel items made wholly or in part of standard steel mill items [@CL-EST-1179; @CL-EST-1180]. The foreign-government clause may be used in fixed-price supply and service solicitations and contracts when the work is to be performed wholly or in part in a foreign country and a foreign government controls wage rates or material prices and may impose a mandatory change during performance [@CL-EST-1182; @CL-EST-1183].

## How it works

The six-month and threshold conditions apply to the three FAR clauses and are introduced by the word "generally", so the text does not say they can never be departed from [@CL-EST-1175; @CL-EST-1176]. The special clauses have their own prescriptions with their own conditions [@CL-EST-1177; @CL-EST-1180; @CL-EST-1183]. For the mill products clause, the 10 percent figure in the clause shall not be exceeded unless approval is obtained at a level above the contracting officer [@CL-EST-1178]. For the nonstandard steel clause, the ten percent figure in the clause carries the same approval requirement [@CL-EST-1181]. The section names no office for the approval, only a level above the contracting officer [@CL-EST-1178; @CL-EST-1181].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD buyer plans a supply contract for aluminum plate with a catalog price and a total price above the simplified acquisition threshold. Delivery is planned over 12 months, so the general conditions are met. The mill products clause fits because a catalog price exists. The unit price is 50, so the clause allows increases of up to 5 per unit in aggregate. A request to allow up to 7 per unit needs approval above the contracting officer.

## Never confuse

The general DFARS conditions are not the same as the special clause prescriptions. The first paragraph names the three FAR clauses and sets the price and timing conditions [@CL-EST-1175; @CL-EST-1176]. The second section prescribes the special DFARS clauses on different grounds, such as the product bought or a foreign government controlling wages [@CL-EST-1177; @CL-EST-1183]. The FAR prescriptions are on [Prescriptions for the economic price adjustment clauses](est-epa-clause-prescriptions.md). The mechanics of the special clauses are on [Economic price adjustment for basic steel, aluminum, brass, bronze and copper mill products](est-epa-mill-products-clause.md) and [Economic price adjustment for nonstandard steel items and foreign-government controlled wages or prices](est-epa-nonstandard-steel-and-foreign-government.md), and the auditor's view is on [Economic price adjustments in proposals and proposal audits](est-economic-price-adjustments.md). The simplified acquisition threshold is described on [Acquisition thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).
