## Purpose

States where the facilities capital cost of money Standard does not reach in full: contractors outside full coverage on a fixed date, where it applies only to later fully-covered contracts, and arrangements where another Federal procurement regulation already sets the use rate or allowance for tangible capital assets, where it does not apply.

## The rule

|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-EST-2921] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.414-62 [@CL-EST-2921] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Applies to | Fully-covered contracts of a contractor that was outside full coverage at the publication date, and not to compensation based on use rates or allowances from other appropriate Federal procurement regulations [@CL-EST-2921; @CL-EST-2922] |

For a contractor that was not subject to full coverage as of the date the part was published as a final rule, the Standard applies only to fully-covered contracts with subsequent dates of award and pricing certification [@CL-EST-2921]. The Standard does not apply where compensation for the use of tangible capital assets is based on use rates or allowances provided for by other appropriate Federal procurement regulations [@CL-EST-2922]. The section lists examples of such regulations: those governing educational institutions, those governing State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments, or those governing construction equipment rates, the last with a reference to 48 CFR 31.105(d) [@CL-EST-2923].

## How it works

The first limit works from coverage status at one fixed date, the publication of the part as a final rule [@CL-EST-2921]. A contractor outside full coverage at that date is reached only through fully-covered contracts with subsequent dates of award and pricing certification [@CL-EST-2921]. The second limit drops the Standard where compensation for the use of tangible capital assets is based on use rates or allowances provided for by other appropriate Federal procurement regulations [@CL-EST-2922]. The other appropriate Federal procurement regulations are described as "such as" those governing educational institutions, State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments, or construction equipment rates (see 48 CFR 31.105(d)) [@CL-EST-2922; @CL-EST-2923].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor was not subject to full coverage on the publication date. It later holds one fully-covered contract with dates of award and pricing certification subsequent to that publication, and one contract that is not fully covered. The Standard applies to the fully-covered contract only [@CL-EST-2921].

On the fully-covered contract, the contractor's compensation for the use of tangible capital assets, namely its construction equipment, is based on use rates provided for by the regulations governing construction equipment rates, one of the settings the section lists, with a reference to 48 CFR 31.105(d). The Standard does not apply to that compensation [@CL-EST-2922; @CL-EST-2923].

## Never confuse

For contractors who are not subject to full CAS-coverage as of the date of publication of part 99 as a final rule, the Standard applies only to those fully-covered contracts with subsequent dates of award and pricing certification [@CL-EST-2921]. The Standard does not apply where compensation for the use of tangible capital assets is based on use rates or allowances provided for by other appropriate Federal procurement regulations, such as those governing educational institutions, State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments, or construction equipment rates [@CL-EST-2922; @CL-EST-2923]. The Standard's requirements are in [the facilities capital cost of money card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-414-facilities-capital-cost-of-money.md) and its definitions in [the cost of money definitions card](../../industrialization/investment-case/ind-cas-414-definitions.md).

## Company calibration

Whether each business unit was outside full coverage at the publication date, and which of its contracts have dates of award and pricing certification after it. [@Q-033]
