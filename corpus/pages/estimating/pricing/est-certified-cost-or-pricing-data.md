## Purpose

Shows the dollar threshold above which a prime contract needs certified cost or pricing data, and how DoD's threshold differs from the codified FAR.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD class deviation [@CL-EST-0002]; codified FAR [@CL-EST-0001] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-0001] |
| RFO citation | Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Rev 1, DFARS 215.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0002]; RFO FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-3213] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text for DoD [@CL-EST-0002]; final for the codified FAR [@CL-EST-0001] |
| Applies to | Prime contracts by award date [@CL-EST-0001] [@CL-EST-0002] |

Under the codified FAR, the threshold for obtaining certified cost or pricing data is $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018 [@CL-EST-0001]. Unless an exception applies, certified cost or pricing data are required before the listed actions expected to exceed the current threshold or, in the case of existing contracts, the threshold specified in the contract [@CL-EST-9458]. For DoD prime contracts, the deviation says to obtain certified cost or pricing data when a prime contract is expected to exceed $10 million and is awarded after June 30, 2026, unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-0002; @CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457]. The threshold is $2.5 million when the prime contract is awarded on or before June 30, 2026, unless otherwise stated in the contract [@CL-EST-9457].

## How it works

Check the exceptions first: the contracting officer shall not require certified cost or pricing data when one of the listed circumstances applies [@CL-EST-0152]. Under the codified FAR, unless an exception applies, certified cost or pricing data are required before the listed actions expected to exceed the current threshold [@CL-EST-9458], and under the DoD deviation the $10 million threshold applies unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-0002; @CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457]. When certified cost or pricing data are required, the contracting officer shall require the contractor to execute a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data, using the format in the paragraph, and must include the executed certificate in the contract file [@CL-EST-9460; @CL-EST-0170]. When certified cost or pricing data are not required by 15.403-4, the contracting officer shall obtain data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price, generally using an order of preference in determining the type of data required [@CL-EST-1252].

The FAR's general definitions say cost or pricing data means all facts that, as of the date of price agreement, or, if applicable, an earlier date agreed upon between the parties that is as close as practicable to the date of agreement on price, prudent buyers and sellers would reasonably expect to affect price negotiations significantly [@CL-EST-9275]. They also say cost or pricing data are factual, not judgmental, and are verifiable [@CL-EST-9276], that they do not indicate the accuracy of the prospective contractor's judgment about estimated future costs or projections but do include the data forming the basis for that judgment [@CL-EST-9417], that they are more than historical accounting data and are all the facts that can be reasonably expected to contribute to the soundness of estimates of future costs and to the validity of determinations of costs already incurred [@CL-EST-9418], and that they include, but are not limited to, such factors as vendor quotations, nonrecurring costs, information on changes in production methods and in production or purchasing volume, data supporting projections of business prospects and objectives and related operations costs, unit-cost trends such as those associated with labor efficiency, make-or-buy decisions, estimated resources to attain business goals, and information on management decisions that could have a significant bearing on costs [@CL-EST-9419; @CL-EST-9420]. Certified cost or pricing data means cost or pricing data that were required to be submitted in accordance with FAR 15.403-4 and 15.403-5 and have been certified, or are required to be certified, in accordance with 15.406-2 [@CL-EST-9277], and the certification states that, to the best of the person's knowledge and belief, the cost or pricing data are accurate, complete, and current as of a date certain before contract award, and the definition goes on to say that cost or pricing data are required to be certified in certain procurements (chapter 271 of title 10 and chapter 35 of title 41 of the United States Code) [@CL-EST-9278; @CL-EST-9399]. The first sentence of the RFO model text definition of certified cost or pricing data in FAR 2.101 reads the same as the codified first sentence, except that it says is required to be certified where the codified text says are required to be certified [@CL-EST-9352; @CL-EST-9277]. The RFO text goes on to say that the certification states that, to the best of the person's knowledge and belief, the cost or pricing data is accurate, complete, and current as of a date certain before contract award [@CL-EST-9352; @CL-EST-9393]. It also says that cost or pricing data is required to be certified in certain procurements (chapter 271 of title 10 and chapter 35 of title 41 of the United States Code) [@CL-EST-9394].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD prime contract expected at 8 million is awarded after the DoD threshold change, with no competition and no exception. At a threshold of 10 million, certified data are not required [@CL-EST-0002], though the contracting officer shall still obtain data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1252]. The same award under a 2.5 million threshold would have needed certified data.

## Never confuse

Certified cost or pricing data are cost or pricing data that were required to be submitted in accordance with FAR 15.403-4 and 15.403-5 and have been certified, or are required to be certified, in accordance with 15.406-2 [@CL-EST-9277]; data other than certified cost or pricing data are what the contracting officer shall obtain when certified data are not required by 15.403-4 [@CL-EST-1252]. The FAR definition says cost or pricing data are required to be certified in certain procurements under chapter 271 of title 10 and chapter 35 of title 41 of the United States Code [@CL-EST-9399], and chapter 35 of title 41 is cross-referenced as Truthful Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-CMP-8392].
