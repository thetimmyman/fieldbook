## Purpose

Shows the dollar threshold above which a prime contract needs certified cost or pricing data, and how DoD's threshold differs from the codified FAR.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | DoD class deviation [@CL-EST-0002]; codified FAR for other agencies [@CL-EST-0001] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-0001] |
| RFO citation | Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Rev 1, DFARS 215.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0002]; RFO FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0001] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text for DoD [@CL-EST-0002]; final for the codified FAR [@CL-EST-0001] |
| Applies to | Prime contracts by award date [@CL-EST-0001] [@CL-EST-0002] |

Under the codified FAR, certified cost or pricing data are required above $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018, unless an exception applies [@CL-EST-0001]. For DoD prime contracts awarded after June 30, 2026, the threshold is $10 million, unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-0002]. The cited deviation text states the $10 million threshold for contracts awarded after June 30, 2026 [@CL-EST-0002]. It states no threshold for earlier DoD awards and no subcontract rule.

## How it works

Check the exceptions first: adequate price competition, prices set by law or regulation, commercial products and services, a waiver, and the DoD-specific exceptions. If none applies and the expected value is above the threshold, the offeror submits certified cost or pricing data and signs the certificate when price is agreed. Below the threshold, the contracting officer may still ask for data other than certified cost or pricing data.

The FAR's general definitions say cost or pricing data means all facts that, as of the date of price agreement, or, if applicable, an earlier date agreed upon between the parties that is as close as practicable to the date of agreement on price, prudent buyers and sellers would reasonably expect to affect price negotiations significantly [@CL-EST-9275]. They also say cost or pricing data are factual, not judgmental, and are verifiable [@CL-EST-9276]. Certified cost or pricing data means cost or pricing data that were required to be submitted in accordance with FAR 15.403-4 and 15.403-5 and have been certified, or are required to be certified, in accordance with 15.406-2 [@CL-EST-9277], and the certification states that, to the best of the person's knowledge and belief, the cost or pricing data are accurate, complete, and current as of a date certain before contract award, and the definition goes on to say that cost or pricing data are required to be certified in certain procurements (chapter 271 of title 10 and chapter 35 of title 41 of the United States Code) [@CL-EST-9278; @CL-EST-9399]. The first sentence of the RFO model text definition of certified cost or pricing data in FAR 2.101 reads the same as the codified first sentence, except that it says is required to be certified where the codified text says are required to be certified [@CL-EST-9352; @CL-EST-9277]. The RFO text goes on to say that the certification states that, to the best of the person's knowledge and belief, the cost or pricing data is accurate, complete, and current as of a date certain before contract award [@CL-EST-9352; @CL-EST-9393]. It also says that cost or pricing data is required to be certified in certain procurements (chapter 271 of title 10 and chapter 35 of title 41 of the United States Code) [@CL-EST-9394].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD prime contract expected at 8 million is awarded after the DoD threshold change, with no competition and no exception. At a threshold of 10 million, certified data are not required, though other data may be. The same award under a 2.5 million threshold would have needed certified data.

## Never confuse

Certified cost or pricing data are not the same as data other than certified cost or pricing data; only the first carries a certificate. The Truthful Cost or Pricing Data statute is the law behind the rule; this page is about the regulation's threshold.
