## Purpose

Shows what TINA Lite changes about certified cost or pricing data, and what it does not change.

| Applies to | Prime contracts greater than $50 million, except where a TINA exception applies [@CL-EST-0740] |

TINA Lite is the informal name for the authority to tailor the type and extent of certified cost or pricing data required for eligible, approved sole-source acquisitions [@CL-EST-0738]. It allows price reasonableness determinations to be grounded primarily in actual historical cost data from prior relevant acquisitions of the same or similar products, while still requiring traditional certified cost or pricing data for non-recurring, changed, unique, or new elements of the requirement [@CL-EST-9712; @CL-EST-0739]. It applies to prime contracts over fifty million dollars and is not used where a TINA exception already applies [@CL-EST-0740].

## How it works

TINA Lite is not a waiver of the truthful cost or pricing data requirements and is not an exception to them [@CL-EST-0741]. Certified cost or pricing data is still required and certified through a tailored certificate, and the Government retains its remedies for defective certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0742]. What changes is whether the contractor provides the full traditional set of certified cost or pricing data, required under FAR Part 15 and DFARS Part 215 and submitted in the format prescribed by FAR Part 15, or what the contracting officer determines is necessary [@CL-EST-0743]. Historical actuals are not a reliable basis for pricing non-recurring effort, changed requirements, or new work not represented in the historical record [@CL-EST-0744]. The authority may be flowed down to subcontracts subject to TINA at the contracting officer's discretion [@CL-EST-0745]. A post-award defective pricing review remains available and should likewise be scoped to the tailored certified cost or pricing data the contractor was required to submit and certify [@CL-EST-0746].

## Never confuse

TINA Lite is not a waiver of the Truthful Cost or Pricing Data requirements and is not an exception to them [@CL-EST-0741]. It is not used where a TINA exception already applies [@CL-EST-0740]. It tailors the type and extent of certified cost or pricing data required, and certification of the tailored data is still required [@CL-EST-0738; @CL-EST-0742].
