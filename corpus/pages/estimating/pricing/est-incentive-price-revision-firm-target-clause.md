## Purpose

Shows the data the contractor submits, how the total final price is computed, the ceiling, and how billing prices move under the firm target clause. The contract-type card covers when a fixed-price incentive contract fits, so this card does not repeat it: [Fixed-price incentive contracts and letter contracts](est-fixed-price-incentive-and-letter-contracts.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1659] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-16 [@CL-EST-1659] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-16 [@CL-EST-0273; @CL-EST-0274; @CL-EST-0275; @CL-EST-0276; @CL-EST-0277; @CL-EST-0278; @CL-EST-0279; @CL-EST-0280; @CL-EST-0281; @CL-EST-0282] |
| Edition date | JAN 2022 [@CL-EST-9792] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1659] |
| Applies to | Schedule items the clause identifies as subject to price revision [@CL-EST-1660] |

The clause makes the Schedule items it identifies subject to price revision, provided that in no event may their total final price exceed the stated ceiling price [@CL-EST-1660].

The contractor submits its data within a number of days set by the contracting officer after the end of the month in which it delivered the last unit of supplies and completed the services for the items [@CL-EST-1661]. Two listed data items are a detailed statement of all costs incurred up to the end of that month in performing all work under the items and an estimate of costs of further performance, if any, that may be necessary to complete all work under the items [@CL-EST-1662]. The data are submitted in the format of Table 15-2, FAR 15.408, or in any other form on which the parties agree [@CL-EST-9512]. The listed data items also include a list of all residual inventory and an estimate of its value, and any other relevant data that the contracting officer may reasonably require [@CL-EST-1703].

## How it works

Upon the contracting officer's receipt of the data, the contracting officer and the contractor shall promptly establish the total final price of the items by applying an adjustment for profit or loss to final negotiated cost [@CL-EST-9676]. The adjustment is applied to the total final negotiated cost, and it equals the total target profit when total final negotiated cost equals total target cost [@CL-EST-1663]. Above target cost, the adjustment is the total target profit less a stated percent of the overrun [@CL-EST-1664]; below target cost, it is the total target profit plus a stated percent of the underrun [@CL-EST-1665]. Pending the final price, billing prices are the target prices shown in the contract [@CL-EST-1666], and the parties shall negotiate a reduction if it appears from the contractor's information that billing prices will be substantially greater than estimated final prices [@CL-EST-1667]. The parties may negotiate an increase in billing prices by any or all of the difference between target prices and the ceiling price, on factual data showing that final cost will be substantially greater than target cost [@CL-EST-1668].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026, because the DoD part 52 class deviation directs its use [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-16 keeps the price revision of the Schedule items the clause identifies [@CL-EST-0273; @CL-EST-1660] and the ceiling on their total final price [@CL-EST-9021; @CL-EST-1660], the timing of the contractor's data submission [@CL-EST-0274; @CL-EST-1661], and the listed data items [@CL-EST-0275; @CL-EST-1662; @CL-EST-0276; @CL-EST-1703]. The RFO sentence on the format of the data names Table 15-1 of FAR 15.408 where the codified sentence names Table 15-2 [@CL-EST-9020; @CL-EST-9512]. The RFO text keeps the adjustment for profit or loss applied to the total final negotiated cost [@CL-EST-0277; @CL-EST-1663], with its overrun case [@CL-EST-0278; @CL-EST-1664] and its underrun case [@CL-EST-0279; @CL-EST-1665]. In the billing-price paragraph the RFO text says the billing prices are the target prices shown in the contract, but its cross-reference reads "paragraph (e) of this clause" where the codified text reads "paragraph (e) above" [@CL-EST-0280; @CL-EST-1666]. For a reduction, it says the parties shall negotiate one if the contractor's information shows billing prices will be substantially greater than estimated final prices, with a cross-reference that reads "paragraph (g)(2) of this clause" where the codified text reads "subparagraph (g)(2) below" [@CL-EST-0281; @CL-EST-1667]. It keeps the possible increase in billing prices by any or all of the difference between target prices and the ceiling price, on the contractor's factual data [@CL-EST-0282; @CL-EST-1668].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Target cost is 1,000,000, target profit is 100,000 and the ceiling price is 1,200,000. The clause fills in a 20 percent share of any overrun.

Final negotiated cost is 1,100,000. The overrun is 100,000, so the adjustment is 100,000 less 20,000, which is 80,000. The total final price is 1,180,000, which is under the ceiling.

## Never confuse

FAR 52.216-16 revises billing prices to a final contract price after the last scheduled item is delivered using firm targets set at award [@CL-EST-0273; @CL-EST-0274; @CL-EST-1659], unlike FAR 52.216-17 on [Incentive price revision clause with successive targets](est-incentive-price-revision-successive-targets-clause.md), which resets initial targets during performance before final pricing [@CL-EST-1669].
