## Purpose

Shows what the step-two invitation is, who may bid in step two of two-step sealed bidding, and how each bid is tied to an acceptable step-one technical proposal.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2161] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-25 [@CL-EST-2161] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2161] |
| Applies to | Bidders that have submitted acceptable technical proposals in step one [@CL-EST-2161] |

The invitation for bids that contains the provision is issued to initiate step two of two-step sealed bidding under subpart 14.5 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation [@CL-EST-2197].

The only bids the Contracting Officer may consider for award of a contract are those received from bidders that have submitted acceptable technical proposals in step one of the acquisition under the step-one request that the provision identifies in a blank [@CL-EST-2161]. The Contracting Officer is to insert in the blank the identification of the step-one request for technical proposals [@CL-EST-2229], so the identification is contract-specific.

A bidder that has submitted multiple technical proposals in step one may submit a separate bid on each technical proposal that was determined to be acceptable to the Government [@CL-EST-2162].

## How it works

A bid may be considered for award only if the bidder has submitted acceptable technical proposals in step one under the identified request [@CL-EST-2161]. A bidder with several acceptable proposals may submit a separate bid on each [@CL-EST-2162]. The separate-bid permission is stated for technical proposals that the Government determined to be acceptable [@CL-EST-2162]. The late and withdrawal rules for step-one technical proposals are on [Late technical proposals and withdrawals under two-step sealed bidding](est-two-step-technical-proposals-late-and-withdrawals.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

In step one, Bidder A submitted two technical proposals. The Government determined one acceptable and the other not. Bidder A may submit a separate bid on the acceptable proposal.

Bidder B submitted three technical proposals and all three were determined acceptable. Bidder B may submit three separate bids, one on each.

Bidder C's only technical proposal was determined not acceptable. Bidder C has not submitted an acceptable technical proposal, so its bids are not among those the Contracting Officer may consider.

Bidder D submitted an acceptable technical proposal under a different request for technical proposals than the one identified in the blank. Its bid is not among those the Contracting Officer may consider under this invitation.

## Never confuse

An acceptable technical proposal is not a bid. The step-two invitation for bids is what initiates step two, and a bidder with multiple acceptable proposals may make a separate bid on each [@CL-EST-2197; @CL-EST-2162].

Eligibility turns on acceptable proposals under the identified step-one request, not on having taken part in some other acquisition [@CL-EST-2161]. Ordinary sealed bid award is covered on [Contract award under sealed bidding](est-sealed-bidding-contract-award.md).
