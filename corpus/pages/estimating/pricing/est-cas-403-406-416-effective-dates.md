## Purpose

Shows when the home office allocation Standard, the cost accounting period Standard and the insurance Standard became effective, and when a contractor must begin to follow each one. The three effective date sections carry the same wording.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-EST-2511] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.403-63, 9904.406-63 and 9904.416-63 [@CL-EST-2511; @CL-EST-2643; @CL-EST-2646] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2511] |
| Applies to | Contractors that receive a contract to which CAS 403, 406 or 416 is applicable [@CL-EST-2512; @CL-EST-2645; @CL-EST-2648] |

Each of the three Standards is effective as of April 17, 1992: CAS 403 [@CL-EST-2511], CAS 406 [@CL-EST-2643] and CAS 416 [@CL-EST-2646].

For each Standard, contractors with prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage shall continue the applicability of the Standard upon receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2642; @CL-EST-2644; @CL-EST-2647].

For each Standard, contractors with no previous contracts subject to the Standard shall apply it beginning with the contractor's next full fiscal year beginning after the receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2512; @CL-EST-2645; @CL-EST-2648].

## How it works

The transition turns on the contractor's history. A contractor that already has prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage continues the Standard's applicability when it receives a contract the Standard applies to [@CL-EST-2642; @CL-EST-2644; @CL-EST-2647].

A contractor with no previous contract subject to the Standard begins to apply it with its next full fiscal year that begins after it receives a contract the Standard applies to [@CL-EST-2512; @CL-EST-2645; @CL-EST-2648].

The sections do not say more than this. Each states only the effective date and these two transition cases.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor has a calendar fiscal year and no previous contract subject to CAS 406. It receives a contract to which CAS 406 is applicable in September of Year 1. It applies CAS 406 beginning with its next full fiscal year beginning after receipt, which starts in January of Year 2 [@CL-EST-2645].

A second contractor already holds prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage. When it receives a contract to which CAS 416 is applicable, it continues the applicability of CAS 416 upon receipt [@CL-EST-2647].

## Never confuse

The effective date is not the date a proposal is priced or a rate is settled. It is the date the Standard became effective, and the transition cases say when a contractor begins to follow it [@CL-EST-2511; @CL-EST-2642; @CL-EST-2512].

The two transition cases are separate. Which one applies depends on whether the contractor has prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage or no previous contract subject to the Standard [@CL-EST-2642; @CL-EST-2512].

The mechanics of each Standard are on the [home office allocation](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-403-home-office-allocation.md), [cost accounting period](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-406-cost-accounting-period.md) and [insurance](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-416-insurance.md) cards.

## Company calibration

Whether each business unit already carries CAS-covered contracts under full coverage, which decides between the continuing rule and the next full fiscal year rule. [@Q-033]
