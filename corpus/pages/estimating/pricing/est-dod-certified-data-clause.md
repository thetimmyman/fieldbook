## Purpose

Shows what the DoD provision asks of an offeror that seeks an exception from certified cost or pricing data, and what it asks when the offeror supplies data other than certified.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-0233] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.215-7010 [@CL-EST-0233] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0233] |
| Applies to | Offerors under the DoD provision, which is used when reasonably certain that certified cost or pricing data or data other than certified cost or pricing data will be required, or when using the provision at 252.215-7008 [@CL-EST-1522; @CL-EST-1619], and subcontracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-0243] |

The provision is the DoD route: in lieu of certified cost or pricing data the offeror may submit a written request for exception with the information the provision describes [@CL-EST-0233], and the contracting officer may require additional supporting information only to the extent necessary to decide the exception and whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0234]. Where no exception is granted [@CL-EST-0444], under the basic provision the offeror prepares and submits certified data and supporting attachments following the instructions in Table 15-2 of FAR 15.408 [@CL-EST-0235] and, as soon as practicable after price agreement but before award, except for unpriced actions such as letter contracts, submits a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-0236].

## How it works

If the offeror is not granted an exception [@CL-EST-0444], it is responsible for determining whether a subcontractor qualifies for an exception from the requirement for submission of certified cost or pricing data on the basis of adequate price competition, that is, two or more responsible offerors, competing independently, submit priced offers that satisfy the Government's expressed requirement [@CL-EST-0237; @CL-EST-0238]. Under the basic provision, data other than certified cost or pricing data must carry the minimum information necessary to permit a determination that the proposed price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0239], and where uncertified cost data is required it is given in the form the offeror or prospective subcontractor regularly maintains in its business operations [@CL-EST-0240]. Under the basic provision, within 10 days of a written request from the contracting officer for additional information to permit an adequate evaluation of the proposed price in accordance with FAR 15.403-3 or DFARS 234.7002(e), the offeror provides either the requested information or a written explanation of the inability to fully comply [@CL-EST-0241; @CL-EST-0258]. No cost data may be required from a subcontractor where sufficient non-Government sales of the same item establish reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-0242]. The offeror inserts the substance of the provision in subcontracts above the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-0243].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD solicitation contains the provision. An offeror claims the commercial product exception and submits three prior commercial sales, the catalogue price, and a description of the differences from the commercial item. The contracting officer finds the material adequate to judge commerciality and price reasonableness. The answer is that the offeror has supplied information of the kind the provision asks for at a minimum in a commercial exception request, though the provision also lists further information by type of item that this card does not set out; whether the exception is granted is the contracting officer's decision, and the provision still flows into subcontracts above the simplified acquisition threshold either way.

## Never confuse

For a commercial product or commercial service exception, the Offeror shall submit, at a minimum, information adequate for determining commerciality and evaluating the reasonableness of the price for this acquisition [@CL-EST-0244], including the prices at which the same or similar products or services have been sold in the commercial market [@CL-EST-0445]. Under the basic provision, data other than certified cost or pricing data shall include the minimum information necessary to permit a determination that the proposed price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0239]. If the Offeror is not granted an exception [@CL-EST-0444], it shall submit a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data as soon as practicable after agreement on price, but before contract award (except for unpriced actions such as letter contracts) [@CL-EST-0236].
