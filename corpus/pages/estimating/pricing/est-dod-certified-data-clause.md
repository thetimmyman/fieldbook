## Purpose

Shows what the DoD provision asks of an offeror that seeks an exception from certified cost or pricing data, and what it asks when the offeror supplies data other than certified.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-0233] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.215-7010 [@CL-EST-0233] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0233] |
| Applies to | DoD solicitations and contracts that include the provision [@CL-EST-0243] |

The provision is the DoD route: in lieu of certified cost or pricing data the offeror may submit a written request for exception with the information the provision describes [@CL-EST-0233], and the contracting officer may require additional supporting information only to the extent necessary to decide the exception and whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0234]. Where no exception is granted [@CL-EST-0444], under the basic provision the offeror prepares and submits certified data and supporting attachments following the instructions in Table 15-2 of FAR 15.408 [@CL-EST-0235] and, as soon as practicable after price agreement but before award, except for unpriced actions such as letter contracts, submits a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-0236].

## How it works

The offeror screens its own subcontracts: it decides whether a subcontractor qualifies for an exception on the basis of adequate price competition, meaning two or more responsible offerors competing independently submit priced offers that satisfy the Government's expressed requirement [@CL-EST-0237; @CL-EST-0238]. Under the basic provision, data other than certified cost or pricing data must carry the minimum information necessary to permit a determination that the proposed price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0239], and where uncertified cost data is required it is given in the form the offeror or prospective subcontractor regularly maintains in its business operations [@CL-EST-0240]. Within 10 days of a written request from the contracting officer for additional information, the offeror provides either the requested information or a written explanation of the inability to fully comply [@CL-EST-0241; @CL-EST-0258]. No cost data may be required from a subcontractor where sufficient non-Government sales of the same item establish reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-0242]. The offeror inserts the substance of the provision in subcontracts above the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-0243].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD solicitation contains the provision. An offeror claims the commercial product exception and submits three prior commercial sales, the catalogue price, and a description of the differences from the commercial item. The contracting officer finds the material adequate to judge commerciality and price reasonableness. The answer is that the offeror has supplied what the provision asks for a commercial exception request; whether the exception is granted is the contracting officer's decision, and the provision still flows into subcontracts above the simplified acquisition threshold either way.

## Never confuse

An exception request is not a refusal to supply information: a commercial product or commercial service exception request must include, at a minimum, information adequate for determining commerciality and evaluating the reasonableness of the price for this acquisition [@CL-EST-0244], including the prices at which the same or similar products or services have been sold in the commercial market [@CL-EST-0445]. Certified data and data other than certified are different products; under the basic provision only the minimum information necessary to show a fair and reasonable price is asked for the second [@CL-EST-0239]. The certificate is not the proposal: it is a separate Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data submitted as soon as practicable after price agreement but before award, except for unpriced actions such as letter contracts [@CL-EST-0236].
