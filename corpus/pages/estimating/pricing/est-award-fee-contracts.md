## Purpose

Shows when award-fee provisions fit, what the award-fee plan and the determination and findings must cover, and how the fee is earned.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1064] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.305, 16.404, 16.405-2 and 16.401(e) [@CL-EST-1064; @CL-EST-1065; @CL-EST-1067; @CL-EST-1056] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1064] |
| Applies to | Cost-plus-award-fee contracts and fixed-price contracts with award fees [@CL-EST-1064; @CL-EST-1065] |

A cost-plus-award-fee contract is a cost-reimbursement contract with a fee made of a base amount, which may be zero, fixed at inception and an award amount based on a judgmental evaluation by the Government [@CL-EST-1064]. The award amount is one the contractor may earn in whole or in part during performance, sufficient to motivate excellence in cost, schedule and technical performance [@CL-EST-1067]. Award-fee provisions may also be used in fixed-price contracts when the Government wishes to motivate a contractor and other incentives cannot be used because performance cannot be measured objectively [@CL-EST-1065].

The amount of award fee earned shall be commensurate with the contractor's overall cost, schedule and technical performance against the criteria in the award-fee plan [@CL-EST-1058]. No award fee is earned if overall performance in the aggregate is below satisfactory [@CL-EST-1059].

## How it works

An award-fee contract is suitable for use under three listed conditions [@CL-EST-1056; @CL-EST-1101; @CL-EST-1057]. The first is that it is neither feasible nor effective to devise predetermined objective incentive targets applicable to cost, schedule and technical performance [@CL-EST-1056]. The second is that the likelihood of meeting acquisition objectives will be enhanced by a contract that effectively motivates the contractor toward exceptional performance and provides the Government with the flexibility to evaluate both actual performance and the conditions under which it was achieved [@CL-EST-1101]. The third is that any additional administrative effort and cost to monitor and evaluate performance are justified by the expected benefits, as documented by a risk and cost benefit analysis to be included in the Determination and Findings [@CL-EST-1057]. Every contract providing for award fees needs an award-fee plan with procedures and an Award-Fee Board [@CL-EST-1060]. The plan is approved by the FDO unless agency procedures authorize otherwise [@CL-EST-1061], and it uses the adjectival ratings and pool percentages in the regulation's table [@CL-EST-1062]. A fixed-price contract with award fee pays the fixed price, including normal profit, for satisfactory performance, and any award fee earned comes in addition [@CL-EST-1066]. Before an award-fee contract is awarded, the limitations that apply only to cost-reimbursement contracts must be complied with and an award-fee plan must be completed [@CL-EST-1063], and a determination and finding addressing all of the suitability items must be completed [@CL-EST-1102].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-award-fee contract has a base fee of zero and an award-fee pool of 100,000 for the first evaluation period. The Government rates the period Good, and the plan's table gives a percentage band for Good that includes 60 percent, so it awards 60,000. In the next period overall performance in the aggregate is below satisfactory, so no award fee is earned. The base fee of zero is allowed because the base amount may be zero.

## Never confuse

The award amount of a cost-plus-award-fee contract rests on a judgmental evaluation by the Government [@CL-EST-1064]. The fee of a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract is instead adjusted by a formula on the relationship of total allowable costs to total target costs [@CL-EST-1090]. A fixed-price contract with award fee also differs from the fixed-price incentive contracts on the other cards: its fixed price is paid for satisfactory performance and the award fee is added to it [@CL-EST-1066]. See [Incentive Fee clause](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-incentive-fee.md) and [Cost-reimbursement contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-cost-reimbursement-contracts.md).
