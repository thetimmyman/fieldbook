## Purpose

Explains when the Government keeps the choice between one award and several for order contracts, and when it states an intention to award several for advisory and assistance services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1929; @CL-EST-1930] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-27 [@CL-EST-1929]; FAR 52.216-28 [@CL-EST-1930] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-27 [@CL-EST-0616]; RFO FAR 52.216-28 [@CL-EST-0617] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1929] |
| Applies to | Solicitations for indefinite-quantity contracts that may result in multiple contract awards, for the single or multiple awards provision [@CL-CTF-1227]; solicitations that include the advisory and assistance services provision [@CL-EST-1930] |

Under the single or multiple awards provision, the Government may elect to award a single delivery order contract or task order contract, or to award multiple delivery order contracts or task order contracts for the same or similar supplies or services to two or more sources under the solicitation [@CL-EST-1929].

Under the advisory and assistance services provision, the Government intends to award multiple contracts for the same or similar advisory and assistance services to two or more sources, unless it determines after evaluation of offers that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the level of quality required [@CL-EST-1930].

## How it works

The first provision leaves the choice between one award and several to the Government [@CL-EST-1929]. The second provision states an intention to make several awards [@CL-EST-1930]. Its only stated exception is a determination, made after evaluation of offers, that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the level of quality required [@CL-EST-1930]. Where the single or multiple awards provision is inserted is covered on [Indefinite-delivery solicitation clauses](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-indefinite-delivery-solicitation-clauses.md).

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-27 keeps the Government's election to award a single delivery order contract or task order contract, or to award multiple delivery order contracts or task order contracts for the same or similar supplies or services to two or more sources under the solicitation [@CL-EST-0616] [@CL-EST-1929]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-28 keeps the Government's intention to award multiple contracts for the same or similar advisory and assistance services to two or more sources under the solicitation, unless the Government determines, after evaluation of offers, that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the level of quality required [@CL-EST-0617] [@CL-EST-1930].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for task order contracts for the same services draws five offers. The Government may elect to award one contract or to award contracts to two or more sources. Separately, a solicitation for advisory and assistance services draws five offers. After evaluation, the Government determines that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the required level of quality, so it makes one award. Had two offerors been capable at that level, the exception would not apply and the stated intention to award multiple contracts would stand.

## Never confuse

The stated intention to award several advisory and assistance contracts is not a promise of several awards, because the determination after evaluation can leave one capable offeror [@CL-EST-1930]. The first provision is not a stated intention at all: it says the Government may elect between a single award and multiple awards [@CL-EST-1929]. Neither provision tells a bidder how many awards will be made. Orders placed under multiple award contracts by the Department of Defense have further rules on [Ordering under multiple award contracts](est-dod-ordering-multiple-award-contracts.md).
