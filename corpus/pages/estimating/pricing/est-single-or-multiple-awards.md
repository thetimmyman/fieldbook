## Purpose

Explains when the Government keeps the choice between one award and several for order contracts, and when it states an intention to award several for advisory and assistance services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1929; @CL-EST-1930] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-27 [@CL-EST-1929]; FAR 52.216-28 [@CL-EST-1930] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-27 [@CL-EST-0616]; RFO FAR 52.216-28 [@CL-EST-0617]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9376] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1929] |
| Applies to | Solicitations for indefinite-quantity contracts that may result in multiple contract awards, for the single or multiple awards provision [@CL-CTF-1227], which is not to be used for advisory and assistance services contracts that exceed 3 years and $20 million (including all options) [@CL-CTF-1256]; solicitations that include the advisory and assistance services provision [@CL-EST-1930] |

Under the single or multiple awards provision, the Government may elect to award a single delivery order contract or task order contract, or to award multiple delivery order contracts or task order contracts for the same or similar supplies or services to two or more sources under the solicitation [@CL-EST-1929].

Under the advisory and assistance services provision, the Government intends to award multiple contracts for the same or similar advisory and assistance services to two or more sources, unless it determines after evaluation of offers that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the level of quality required [@CL-EST-1930].

The FAR's general definitions say advisory and assistance services means those services provided under contract by nongovernmental sources to support or improve organizational policy development, decision-making, management and administration, program and/or project management and administration, or R&D activities [@CL-EST-9273]. The codified definition goes on to say that advisory and assistance services can also mean the furnishing of professional advice or assistance rendered to improve the effectiveness of Federal management processes or procedures (including those of an engineering and technical nature) [@CL-EST-9409], that, in rendering the foregoing services, outputs may take the form of information, advice, opinions, alternatives, analyses, evaluations, recommendations, training and the day-to-day aid of support personnel needed for the successful performance of ongoing Federal operations [@CL-EST-9410], and that all advisory and assistance services are classified in one of its definitional subdivisions (management and professional support services, studies, analyses and evaluations, and engineering and technical services) [@CL-EST-9411; @CL-EST-9412; @CL-EST-9413; @CL-EST-9414; @CL-EST-9415; @CL-EST-9416]. Under the RFO, FAR 2.101 has the same first sentence word for word, and goes on to say that advisory and assistance services can also mean the furnishing of professional advice or assistance rendered to improve the effectiveness of Federal management processes or procedures (including those of an engineering and technical nature), and that, in rendering the foregoing services, outputs may take the form of information, advice, opinions, alternatives, analyses, evaluations, recommendations, training and the day-to-day aid of support personnel needed for the successful performance of ongoing Federal operations, and that all advisory and assistance services are classified in one of the definition's subdivisions (management and professional support services, studies, analyses and evaluations, and engineering and technical services), each of which the definition goes on to describe [@CL-EST-9376; @CL-EST-9397; @CL-EST-9398; @CL-EST-9403; @CL-EST-9404; @CL-EST-9405; @CL-EST-9406; @CL-EST-9407; @CL-EST-9408; @CL-EST-9273].

## How it works

The first provision leaves the choice between one award and several to the Government [@CL-EST-1929]. The second provision states an intention to make several awards [@CL-EST-1930]. The intention applies unless the Government determines, after evaluation of offers, that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the level of quality required [@CL-EST-1930]. Where the single or multiple awards provision is inserted is covered on [Indefinite-delivery solicitation clauses](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-indefinite-delivery-solicitation-clauses.md).

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. Under the RFO, FAR 52.216-27 keeps the Government's election to award a single delivery order contract or task order contract, or to award multiple delivery order contracts or task order contracts for the same or similar supplies or services to two or more sources under the solicitation [@CL-EST-0616] [@CL-EST-1929]. Under the RFO, FAR 52.216-28 keeps the Government's intention to award multiple contracts for the same or similar advisory and assistance services to two or more sources under the solicitation, unless the Government determines, after evaluation of offers, that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the level of quality required [@CL-EST-0617] [@CL-EST-1930].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for task order contracts for the same services draws five offers. The Government may elect to award one contract or to award contracts to two or more sources. Separately, a solicitation for advisory and assistance services draws five offers. After evaluation, the Government determines that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the required level of quality, so it makes one award. Had two offerors been capable at that level, the exception would not apply and the stated intention to award multiple contracts would stand.

## Never confuse

The advisory and assistance services provision states an intention to award multiple contracts, unless the Government determines, after evaluation of offers, that only one offeror is capable of providing the services at the level of quality required [@CL-EST-1930]. The single or multiple awards provision says the Government may elect between a single award and multiple awards [@CL-EST-1929]. Orders placed under multiple award contracts by the Department of Defense have further rules on [Ordering under multiple award contracts](est-dod-ordering-multiple-award-contracts.md).
