## Purpose

Shows that the Paperwork Reduction Act requires Federal agencies to obtain approval from the Office of Management and Budget before collecting information from ten or more members of the public, that the information collection and recordkeeping requirements in the regulation have been approved, and which control numbers were assigned.

## The rule

|---|---|

| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.102 [@CL-EST-2945] |

The Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 imposes a requirement on Federal agencies to obtain approval from the Office of Management and Budget before collecting information from ten or more members of the public [@CL-EST-2945]. The information collection and recordkeeping requirements contained in the regulation have been approved by the Office of Management and Budget [@CL-EST-2946]. The Office of Management and Budget has assigned Control Numbers 0348-0051 and 0348-0055 to the paperwork, recordkeeping and forms associated with the regulation [@CL-EST-2947].

## How it works

The section states the statutory duty that makes the approval necessary, then states that the regulation's own collection and recordkeeping requirements have it [@CL-EST-2945; @CL-EST-2946]. It closes by recording the control numbers the Office of Management and Budget assigned to the paperwork, recordkeeping and forms [@CL-EST-2947].

The duty to obtain approval falls on Federal agencies, and the Office of Management and Budget is the body that approves [@CL-EST-2945]. The section states no duty on a contractor and no deadline or expiry date, and its only threshold is information collected from ten or more members of the public [@CL-EST-2945].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Federal agency plans to collect information from twelve members of the public. The Paperwork Reduction Act requires the agency to obtain approval from the Office of Management and Budget before it collects, because the group is ten or more [@CL-EST-2945]. For the regulation's own information collection and recordkeeping requirements, the section states that approval has been obtained, and it records the control numbers the Office of Management and Budget assigned to the associated paperwork and forms [@CL-EST-2946; @CL-EST-2947].

## Never confuse

The control numbers are an approval record rather than a new duty: the section reports that the regulation's collection and recordkeeping requirements are approved and records the numbers assigned [@CL-EST-2946; @CL-EST-2947]. The approval requirement the section states is imposed on Federal agencies [@CL-EST-2945]. The Standards behind those collections are summarised in [the Cost Accounting Standards overview](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-overview.md).
