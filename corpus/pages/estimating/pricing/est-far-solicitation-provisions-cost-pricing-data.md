## Purpose

Shows which solicitation provisions and contract clauses the contracting officer inserts for make-or-buy, price reduction for defective data, subcontractor data, unit prices, pension and ownership changes, facilities capital cost of money, the certified data requirements and pass-through charges, which alternates apply, and what triggers each one.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1501] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.408 [@CL-EST-1501] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.110 [@CL-EST-3277; @CL-EST-3278; @CL-EST-3582; @CL-EST-3279; @CL-EST-3280; @CL-EST-3281; @CL-EST-3282; @CL-EST-3283; @CL-EST-3284; @CL-EST-3285; @CL-EST-3286; @CL-EST-3287; @CL-EST-3288; @CL-EST-3289; @CL-EST-3290; @CL-EST-3291; @CL-EST-3292; @CL-EST-3293; @CL-EST-3294; @CL-EST-3295; @CL-EST-3296; @CL-EST-3297; @CL-EST-3298; @CL-EST-3299; @CL-EST-3300; @CL-EST-3583] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1501] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that meet the trigger in each paragraph [@CL-EST-1501; @CL-EST-1508] |

The clause at 52.215-9 for changes or additions to a make-or-buy program goes in solicitations and contracts when it is contemplated that a make-or-buy program will be incorporated in the contract [@CL-EST-1500]. If a less economical make or buy categorization is selected for one or more items of significant value, the contracting officer shall use that clause with its Alternate I, if a fixed-price incentive contract is contemplated, or its Alternate II, if a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract is contemplated [@CL-EST-1600; @CL-EST-1601]. When contracting by negotiation, the price reduction clause at 52.215-10 goes in solicitations and contracts when it is contemplated that certified cost or pricing data will be required from the contractor or any subcontractor (see 15.403-4) [@CL-EST-1501; @CL-EST-9681].

The contracting officer shall, when contracting by negotiation, insert the clause at 52.215-11 [@CL-EST-1602] in solicitations and contracts when it is contemplated that certified cost or pricing data will be required from the contractor or any subcontractor (see 15.403-4) [@CL-EST-1502] for the pricing of contract modifications, and the clause prescribed in paragraph (b) of the section, the price reduction clause at 52.215-10, has not been included [@CL-EST-1503; @CL-EST-1501]. The contracting officer shall insert the subcontractor clause at 52.215-12 in solicitations and contracts when the clause prescribed in paragraph (b) of the section is included [@CL-EST-1504], or, upon the request of a contractor that was required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with a prime contract entered into before July 1, 2018, shall modify the contract without requiring consideration, to replace clause 52.215-12 with its Alternate I [@CL-EST-9682; @CL-EST-9683]. The contracting officer shall insert the subcontractor modifications clause at 52.215-13 in solicitations and contracts when the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) of the section is included [@CL-EST-1505], or, upon the request of a contractor that was required to submit certified cost or pricing data in connection with a prime contract entered into before July 1, 2018, shall modify the contract without requiring consideration, to replace clause 52.215-13 with its Alternate I [@CL-EST-9684; @CL-EST-9685].

The clause at 52.215-14 on integrity of unit prices goes in solicitations and contracts except for the listed exceptions [@CL-EST-1506]. They include acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold and construction or architect-engineer services under part 36 [@CL-EST-1506]. They also include utility services under part 41, service contracts where supplies are not required, acquisitions of commercial products and commercial services, and contracts for petroleum products [@CL-EST-1603]. The contracting officer inserts that clause with its Alternate I when contracting without adequate price competition or when prescribed by agency regulations [@CL-EST-1604].

The pension clause at 52.215-15 goes in solicitations and contracts for which it is anticipated that certified cost or pricing data will be required or for which any preaward or postaward cost determinations will be subject to part 31 [@CL-EST-1508]. The facilities capital cost of money provision at 52.215-16 goes in solicitations expected to result in contracts subject to the cost principles for contracts with commercial organizations [@CL-EST-1509]. If the prospective contractor does not propose facilities capital cost of money in its offer, the waiver clause at 52.215-17 goes in the resulting contract [@CL-EST-1510]. The postretirement benefits clause at 52.215-18 goes in solicitations and contracts for which it is anticipated that certified cost or pricing data will be required [@CL-EST-1511], or for which any preaward or postaward cost determinations will be subject to part 31 [@CL-EST-1605]. The ownership change clause at 52.215-19 goes in solicitations and contracts for which it is contemplated that certified cost or pricing data will be required or for which any preaward or postaward cost determination will be subject to subpart 31.2 [@CL-EST-1512].

Considering the hierarchy at 15.402, the contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.215-20 in solicitations if it is reasonably certain that certified cost or pricing data or data other than certified cost or pricing data will be required [@CL-EST-9686; @CL-EST-1513]. The contracting officer shall use the provision with its Alternate I to specify a format for certified cost or pricing data other than the format required by Table 15-2 of that section, and with its Alternate II if copies of the proposal are to be sent to the ACO and contract auditor [@CL-EST-1606]. The contracting officer shall use it with its Alternate III if submission via electronic media is required [@CL-EST-1607], and shall replace the basic provision with its Alternate IV if certified cost or pricing data are not expected to be required because an exception may apply, but data other than certified cost or pricing data will be required as described in 15.403-3 [@CL-EST-1608].

Considering the hierarchy at 15.402, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.215-21 in solicitations and contracts [@CL-EST-9687; @CL-EST-1514] if it is reasonably certain that certified cost or pricing data or data other than certified cost or pricing data will be required for modifications [@CL-EST-1544]. The contracting officer shall use the clause with its Alternate I to specify a format for certified cost or pricing data other than the format required by Table 15-2 of that section, and with its Alternate II if copies of the proposal are to be sent to the ACO and contract auditor [@CL-EST-1609], with its Alternate III if submission via electronic media is required [@CL-EST-1610], and shall replace the basic clause with its Alternate IV if certified cost or pricing data are not expected to be required because an exception may apply, but data other than certified cost or pricing data will be required as described in 15.403-3 [@CL-EST-1611].

The provision at 52.215-22 goes in solicitations containing the pass-through charges clause at 52.215-23 [@CL-EST-1515]. Except as provided in paragraph (n)(2)(ii), the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.215-23 in solicitations and contracts, including task or delivery orders, as follows [@CL-EST-1516]. For civilian agencies it goes in when the total estimated contract or order value exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold as defined in section 2.101 and the contemplated contract type is expected to be a cost-reimbursement type contract as defined in Subpart 16.3 [@CL-EST-1612]. For DoD it goes in when the total estimated contract or order value exceeds the threshold for obtaining cost or pricing data in 15.403-4 and the contemplated contract type is expected to be any contract type except a firm-fixed-price contract awarded on the basis of adequate price competition, a fixed-price contract with economic price adjustment awarded on the basis of adequate price competition [@CL-EST-1613; @CL-EST-9688], a firm-fixed-price contract for the acquisition of a commercial product or commercial service, a fixed-price contract with economic price adjustment for the acquisition of a commercial product or commercial service [@CL-EST-9689], a fixed-price incentive contract awarded on the basis of adequate price competition, or a fixed-price incentive contract for the acquisition of a commercial product or commercial service [@CL-EST-3584]. The clause may be used below those thresholds and for any contract type when the contracting officer determines that inclusion is appropriate [@CL-EST-1614]. Alternate I is used when the contracting officer determines that the prospective contractor has demonstrated that its functions provide added value to the contracting effort and there are no excessive pass-through charges [@CL-EST-1615]. What the clause does once it is in a contract is on the linked pass-through page.

## How it works

The subcontractor clauses go in when the matching prime clause is included [@CL-EST-1504; @CL-EST-1505]. The waiver clause for facilities capital cost of money goes in the resulting contract if the prospective contractor does not propose facilities capital cost of money in its offer [@CL-EST-1510]. After the basic prescription, a paragraph may name the condition that selects an alternate, such as a different data format or copies to the ACO and contract auditor [@CL-EST-1601; @CL-EST-1606; @CL-EST-1609].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.110(i) says to insert the clause at 52.215-9 in RFPs and contracts when it is contemplated that a make-or-buy program will be incorporated in the contract [@CL-EST-3277], where the codified paragraph (a) says solicitations and contracts [@CL-EST-1500], and RFO FAR 15.110(v) holds the pass-through provision where codified paragraph (n) holds it [@CL-EST-3295; @CL-EST-1515]. RFO FAR 15.110(i) says that if a less economical make or buy categorization is selected for one or more items of significant value, the contracting officer must use the make-or-buy clause with its Alternate I if a fixed-price incentive contract is contemplated [@CL-EST-3278], and with its Alternate II if a cost-plus-incentive-fee contract is contemplated [@CL-EST-3582], as the codified paragraph says for both alternates [@CL-EST-1600; @CL-EST-1601]. RFO FAR 15.110(j) says to insert the clause at 52.215-10 in RFPs and contracts when it is contemplated that certified cost or pricing data will be required from the contractor or any subcontractor [@CL-EST-3279], in a sentence with neither the words about contracting by negotiation nor the cross-reference to 15.403-4 that appear in the codified paragraph [@CL-EST-1501; @CL-EST-9681]. RFO FAR 15.110(k) gives the same two conditions for the clause at 52.215-11, namely that certified data are contemplated for the pricing of contract modifications [@CL-EST-3280] and that the clause prescribed in paragraph (j) has not been included [@CL-EST-3281], again without the words about contracting by negotiation and without the cross-reference to 15.403-4, which the codified paragraph has [@CL-EST-1602; @CL-EST-1502; @CL-EST-1503]. RFO FAR 15.110(l)(1) and (m)(1) tie the subcontractor clauses at 52.215-12 and 52.215-13 to the clauses prescribed in paragraphs (j) and (k) [@CL-EST-3282; @CL-EST-3283], as the codified paragraphs tie them to the clauses prescribed in paragraphs (b) and (c) [@CL-EST-1504; @CL-EST-1505]. RFO FAR 15.110(n) lists the same exceptions to the unit prices clause at 52.215-14 as the codified text [@CL-EST-3284; @CL-EST-3285; @CL-EST-1506; @CL-EST-1603], and says to use its Alternate I when contracting without adequate price competition or when prescribed by agency regulations [@CL-EST-3286; @CL-EST-1604]. RFO FAR 15.110(o), (q), (r) and (s) state the same triggers as the codified paragraphs for the pension clause, the waiver of facilities capital cost of money, the postretirement benefits clause and the ownership change clause, with RFPs in place of solicitations where the codified sentences say solicitations [@CL-EST-3287; @CL-EST-3289; @CL-EST-3290; @CL-EST-3291; @CL-EST-1508; @CL-EST-1510; @CL-EST-1511; @CL-EST-1605; @CL-EST-1512]. RFO FAR 15.110(p) states the trigger for the provision at 52.215-16 as RFPs expected to result in contracts subject to the cost principles for contracts with commercial organizations [@CL-EST-3288], where the codified paragraph adds a reference to subpart 31.2 [@CL-EST-1509]. RFO FAR 15.110(t) and (u) say to insert the provision at 52.215-20 in RFPs, and the clause at 52.215-21 in RFPs and contracts, if it is reasonably certain that the data will be required [@CL-EST-3292; @CL-EST-3294], where the codified paragraphs say solicitations for the provision [@CL-EST-1513] and solicitations and contracts for the clause [@CL-EST-1514; @CL-EST-1544]. RFO FAR 15.110(t) says to use Alternate I of the provision at 52.215-20 to specify a format for certified cost or pricing data other than the format required by Table 15-1 at 15.408-2, and Alternate II if copies of the proposal are to be sent to the ACO and contract auditor [@CL-EST-3293], where the codified paragraph names Table 15-2 of its own section as the required format [@CL-EST-1606]. RFO FAR 15.110(v) prescribes the provision at 52.215-22 in RFPs containing the clause at 52.215-23 [@CL-EST-3295], and inserts the clause, except as provided in paragraph (v)(2)(ii), in RFPs and contracts including task or delivery orders in the cases it lists [@CL-EST-3296], as the codified paragraph does [@CL-EST-1515; @CL-EST-1516]. For civilian agencies the RFO trigger is the same as the codified one, with the definitions cited to part 2 and part 16 where the codified paragraph cites section 2.101 and subpart 16.3 [@CL-EST-3297; @CL-EST-1612]. For DoD the RFO trigger is a total estimated value above the threshold for obtaining cost or pricing data in 15.403-3(a) and any contract type except those listed after the word except [@CL-EST-3298], where the codified paragraph cites 15.403-4 [@CL-EST-1613]. The RFO list of excepted types ends with a fixed-price contract with economic price adjustment for the acquisition of a commercial product or commercial service and a fixed-price incentive contract awarded on the basis of adequate price competition [@CL-EST-3583], where the codified list continues with a further type, a fixed-price incentive contract for the acquisition of a commercial product or commercial service [@CL-EST-3584]. RFO FAR 15.110(v)(2)(ii) and (iii) allow the clause to be used below the thresholds identified in paragraph (v)(2)(i), and for any contract type, when the contracting officer determines that inclusion is appropriate, and call for its Alternate I when the contracting officer determines that the prospective contractor has demonstrated that its functions provide added value to the contracting effort and there are no excessive pass-through charges [@CL-EST-3299; @CL-EST-3300], as the codified paragraphs do [@CL-EST-1614; @CL-EST-1615].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A negotiated solicitation expects a contract worth 12 million, with certified data required from the prime and from a major subcontractor. The price reduction clause goes in, and so does the subcontractor clause. The offeror proposes no facilities capital cost of money, so the waiver clause goes in the resulting contract. The answer: the contracting officer checks each paragraph in turn and inserts the instrument whose trigger is met.

## Never confuse

The threshold and exceptions for certified cost or pricing data are on [Certified cost or pricing data threshold](est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md). The facilities capital cost of money item at 52.215-16 is a provision for solicitations [@CL-EST-1509], while the unit prices item at 52.215-14 is a clause for solicitations and contracts [@CL-EST-1506]. Paragraph letters differ: the price reduction clause is codified FAR 15.408(b) [@CL-EST-1501] and RFO FAR 15.110(j) [@CL-EST-3279]. The threshold cited for the DoD pass-through trigger is 15.403-4 in the codified text [@CL-EST-1613] but 15.403-3(a) in the RFO text [@CL-EST-3298], and the required format for the alternate of 52.215-20 is Table 15-2 in the codified text [@CL-EST-1606] but Table 15-1 at 15.408-2 in the RFO text [@CL-EST-3293]. The codified list of contract types excepted from the DoD pass-through trigger includes a fixed-price incentive contract for a commercial product or commercial service [@CL-EST-3584], while the RFO list ends with a fixed-price incentive contract awarded on the basis of adequate price competition [@CL-EST-3583]. For the pass-through rules, see [Limitations on pass-through charges](est-pass-through-charge-limitations.md). For the subcontractor side of certified data, see [Subcontractor certified cost or pricing data](est-subcontractor-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md). The DoD additions to these provisions are on [DFARS solicitation provisions and contract clauses for negotiated contracts](est-dfars-solicitation-provisions-negotiated.md).
