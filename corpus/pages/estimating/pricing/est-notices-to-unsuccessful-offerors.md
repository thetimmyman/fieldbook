## Purpose

Shows which written notices an offeror can receive when it is not selected, when each one is sent, and what each must say.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1400] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.503 [@CL-EST-1400] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.206-1 [@CL-EST-3321; @CL-EST-3322; @CL-EST-3323; @CL-EST-3324]; RFO FAR 15.207-2 [@CL-EST-3585; @CL-EST-3325; @CL-EST-3326; @CL-EST-3327; @CL-EST-3328; @CL-EST-3329; @CL-EST-3330] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1400] |
| Applies to | Offerors excluded from the competitive range, offerors in the competitive range who were not selected, and, when a small business program procedure is used, each offeror [@CL-EST-1400; @CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1406] |

Three notices are named: a preaward notice of exclusion, a preaward notice for small business programs, and a postaward notice [@CL-EST-1400; @CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1406]. The notice of exclusion is sent promptly, in writing, when a proposal is excluded from the competitive range or otherwise eliminated from the competition [@CL-EST-1400]. The small business program notice is sent in addition to it, in writing, before award and on completion of negotiations and responsibility determinations, when a small business set-aside is used [@CL-EST-1401] or when one of three further listed program procedures is used [@CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1402]. That notice is not required in two listed cases: when the contracting officer determines in writing that urgency necessitates award without delay, and when the contract is entered into under the 8(a) program [@CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1405].

The postaward notice goes to each offeror in the competitive range who was not selected, or who was not notified earlier under the preaward-notice paragraph, and it is sent within 3 days after contract award [@CL-EST-1406]. Offerors that received a preaward notice of exclusion receive the same information upon request [@CL-EST-1410], and so do unsuccessful offerors in solicitations using simplified acquisition procedures [@CL-EST-1460]. Where only the total contract price is furnished in the postaward notice, the items, quantities and any stated unit prices of each award shall still be made publicly available upon request [@CL-EST-1459]. The postaward notice paragraph also says that in no event shall an offeror's cost breakdown, profit, overhead rates, trade secrets, manufacturing processes and techniques, or other confidential business information be disclosed to any other offeror [@CL-EST-1458].

## How it works

The notice of exclusion states the basis for the determination and that a proposal revision will not be considered [@CL-EST-1400]. The small business program notice states, among its listed contents, the name and address of the apparently successful offeror and that the Government will not consider later revisions [@CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1403], and that no response is required unless a basis exists to challenge the size status or small business status of that offeror [@CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1403; @CL-EST-1404]. The postaward notice lists, among its contents, the number of offerors solicited, the number of proposals received, and the name and address of each awardee [@CL-EST-1406; @CL-EST-1407]. It also lists the items, quantities and any stated unit prices of each award, with only the total contract price needed in the notice where listing any stated unit prices is impracticable at that time [@CL-EST-1406; @CL-EST-1407; @CL-EST-1408], and gives the reason the proposal was not accepted in general terms, unless the price information readily reveals it [@CL-EST-1406; @CL-EST-1407; @CL-EST-1409].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.206-1(a) says contracting officers must notify offerors promptly in writing when their proposals are excluded from the competitive range or otherwise eliminated from the competition, and that the notice must state the basis for the determination and that proposal revisions will not be considered [@CL-EST-3321], as the codified paragraph says [@CL-EST-1400]. RFO FAR 15.206-1(b)(1) says that when using a set-aside for small business, HUBZone, the Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business Program, or the Women-Owned Small Business Program, contracting officers must notify each offeror, in writing, prior to award and upon completion of negotiations and determinations of responsibility [@CL-EST-3322], where the codified paragraph lists a small business set-aside and the use of the HUBZone, service-disabled veteran-owned small business and Women-Owned Small Business Program procedures, and cites the procedures by section number [@CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1402]. RFO FAR 15.206-1(b)(1)(i)-(ii) lists two contents for that notice, the name and address of the apparently successful offeror and that the Government will not consider subsequent revisions of the offeror's proposal [@CL-EST-3323], where the codified paragraph lists a third content, that no response is required unless a basis exists to challenge the size status or small business status of the apparently successful offeror [@CL-EST-1403; @CL-EST-1404]. RFO FAR 15.206-1(b)(2)-(3) says the notice is in addition to any notice of exclusion from competitive range and is not required when the contracting officer determines in writing that the urgency of the requirement necessitates award without delay or when the contract is entered into under the 8(a) program [@CL-EST-3324], as the codified paragraphs say, without the codified cross-reference to 19.805-2 [@CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1405]. RFO FAR 15.207-2(a) says that Day, as used in that subsection, has the meaning set forth at part 33 [@CL-EST-3585], in the same section that sets the 3-day deadline for the postaward notice [@CL-EST-3325]. RFO FAR 15.207-2(b) says that within 3 days after the date of contract award contracting officers must provide written notification to each offeror whose proposal was in the competitive range but was not selected for award or had not previously received a notice in accordance with 15.206-1(a) [@CL-EST-3325], as the codified paragraph says with the earlier notice cited as paragraph (a) of the codified section [@CL-EST-1406]. RFO FAR 15.207-2(b)(1)-(3) lists the same first three contents of the postaward notice as the codified paragraph, the number of offerors solicited, the number of proposals received, and the name and address of each offeror receiving an award [@CL-EST-3326; @CL-EST-1407]. RFO FAR 15.207-2(b)(4) says that if the number of items or other factors makes listing any stated unit prices impracticable at that time only the total contract price need be furnished, and that the items, quantities, and any stated unit prices of each award must be made publicly available, upon request [@CL-EST-3327], as the codified paragraph says [@CL-EST-1408; @CL-EST-1459]. RFO FAR 15.207-2(b)(5) says the notice must include, in general terms, the reason the offeror's proposal was not accepted, unless the price information in paragraph (b)(4) readily reveals the reason [@CL-EST-3328], as the codified paragraph says [@CL-EST-1409], and says an offeror's cost breakdown, profit, overhead rates, trade secrets, manufacturing processes and techniques, or other confidential business information must not be disclosed to any other offeror [@CL-EST-3329], as the codified paragraph says in no event shall they be disclosed [@CL-EST-1458]. RFO FAR 15.207-2(c) says that upon request contracting officers must provide the information in paragraph (b) to unsuccessful offerors that received a preaward notice of exclusion from the competitive range [@CL-EST-3330], where the codified text also names a separate request right for unsuccessful offerors in solicitations using simplified acquisition procedures [@CL-EST-1410; @CL-EST-1460].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A small set-aside acquisition has four offers. One is excluded from the competitive range and gets a written notice of exclusion with the basis. Before award, all four get the small business program notice naming the apparently successful offeror. After award, the offerors that were in the range but not chosen get the postaward notice with the award facts and a general reason. The answer: each stage has its own notice and its own content.

## Never confuse

A notice of exclusion is not a debriefing. The notice states the basis for the determination [@CL-EST-1400], and an offeror excluded before award may then ask for a debriefing [@CL-EST-1411]. The debriefing rules are on [Preaward debriefing of offerors](est-preaward-debriefing.md) and [Postaward debriefing of offerors](est-postaward-debriefing.md). The preaward small business notice is also not the postaward notice: the first names the apparently successful offeror and says no response is required unless a basis exists to challenge its size status or small business status [@CL-EST-1401; @CL-EST-1403; @CL-EST-1404], and the second reports the award facts after award [@CL-EST-1406; @CL-EST-1407]. The codified text lists, as a content of the small business program notice, that no response is required unless a basis exists to challenge size status or small business status [@CL-EST-1404], while RFO FAR 15.206-1(b)(1)(i)-(ii) lists only the name and address of the apparently successful offeror and that subsequent revisions will not be considered [@CL-EST-3323]. The codified text also lets unsuccessful offerors in solicitations using simplified acquisition procedures ask for the postaward information [@CL-EST-1460], while RFO FAR 15.207-2(c) names the unsuccessful offerors that received a preaward notice of exclusion [@CL-EST-3330]. The set-aside rules themselves are on [Small business set-asides](../../market/programs/mkt-small-business-set-asides.md).
