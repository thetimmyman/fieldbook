## Purpose

Shows when a practice change that conforms a Cost Accounting Standard to GAAP falls outside the contract price and cost adjustment process, and how full coverage and modified coverage contracts can be performed side by side.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-EST-2476] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9903.201-9 and 9903.304 [@CL-EST-2476; @CL-EST-2482] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2476] |
| Applies to | Current disclosed practices that use the CAS 403 three-factor formula or already include revenue as an allocation basis, and contracts under full or modified coverage [@CL-EST-2477; @CL-EST-2482] |

The contract price and cost adjustment requirements of part 9903 are not applicable to changes directly associated with conformance of operating revenue to revenue reported in accordance with GAAP [@CL-EST-2476].

That exemption applies only to current disclosed practices where the contractor is required to use the three-factor formula prescribed in CAS 403 for residual expenses, or where its current disclosed and compliant accounting practice includes revenue as a basis for allocating costs to cost objectives [@CL-EST-2477].

A change to the current practice that would move it to or from using revenue as a basis for allocation is a unilateral change, subject to the normal cost impact and resolution process [@CL-EST-2479].

The contract price and cost adjustment requirements are also not applicable to changes directly associated with the conformance of CAS 408 to GAAP [@CL-EST-2480]. Those changes must be disclosed and made during the contractor's fiscal year directly following the effective date of the final rule rescinding CAS 408 [@CL-EST-2481].

A contract subject to full coverage may be performed during a period in which a previously awarded contract subject to modified coverage is being performed [@CL-EST-2482]. Compliance with full coverage may compel the use of practices that modified coverage does not require [@CL-EST-2626]. Under these circumstances the practices applicable to the modified coverage contracts need not be changed [@CL-EST-2627], and any resulting differences between the two coverages are not a violation of CAS 401 and CAS 402 [@CL-EST-2483]. The same principle applies when a modified coverage contract is performed during a period in which a previously awarded full coverage contract is being performed [@CL-EST-2628].

## How it works

The revenue exemption removes the contract price and cost adjustment requirements for a conforming change [@CL-EST-2476]. It reaches only the two disclosed situations in the rule, so a change outside them has no exemption [@CL-EST-2477].

A move onto or away from a revenue base is treated as a unilateral change that goes through the normal cost impact and resolution process [@CL-EST-2479]. The section does not describe that process; see the linked cards.

The CAS 408 exemption has its own timing condition [@CL-EST-2481]. The change must be disclosed and made in the contractor's fiscal year directly following the effective date of the final rule rescinding CAS 408 [@CL-EST-2481]. The GAAP conformance card holds the rescission and its dates.

The concurrent coverage section applies in both directions: a full coverage contract performed beside an earlier modified coverage contract [@CL-EST-2482], and a modified coverage contract performed beside an earlier full coverage contract [@CL-EST-2628]. It protects the modified coverage practices from a forced change [@CL-EST-2627], and it keeps the resulting differences from counting as a violation of CAS 401 and CAS 402 [@CL-EST-2483].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's current disclosed and compliant practice allocates one pool on a revenue base. It restates that revenue to GAAP revenue. The change is directly associated with conforming operating revenue to GAAP revenue and the practice already includes revenue as an allocation basis, so the contract price and cost adjustment requirements do not apply to it [@CL-EST-2476; @CL-EST-2477].

In a later year the contractor proposes to move that pool from the revenue base to a headcount base. That is a change away from revenue as an allocation basis, so it is a unilateral change and goes through the normal cost impact and resolution process [@CL-EST-2479].

For CAS 408, the contractor has a calendar fiscal year and the final rule rescinding CAS 408 takes effect partway through Year 1. A conforming change must be disclosed and made during Year 2, the fiscal year directly following the effective date [@CL-EST-2481].

The contractor then wins a full coverage contract while an earlier modified coverage contract is still in performance. The modified coverage practices need not be changed, and the resulting differences are not a violation of CAS 401 and CAS 402 [@CL-EST-2482; @CL-EST-2627; @CL-EST-2483].

## Never confuse

This is not a pass for every practice change that follows GAAP. The revenue exemption applies only to the two disclosed situations the section names, and a move to or from a revenue base stays a unilateral change [@CL-EST-2477; @CL-EST-2479].

The protection for concurrent coverage covers differences that result from performing the two kinds of contract side by side [@CL-EST-2483]. The consistency Standard keeps its own card; see [the consistency Standard](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-401-consistency.md).

How the conformance is documented and dated is on the [GAAP conformance](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-gaap-conformance-2026.md) and [disclosure and consistency](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-disclosure-consistency-modified-cas.md) cards. Which contracts carry full or modified coverage is on the [CAS applicability](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-applicability.md) card.
