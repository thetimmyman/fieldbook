## Purpose

Shows how the audit agency's manual allocates responsibility for pricing subcontracts inside a prime proposal, how it treats foreign subcontracts and transfers inside a company group, and when audit results on a subcontractor may go to the prime.

Prime contractors and upper-tier subcontractors bear primary responsibility for evaluating lower-tier subcontractor proposals and flowing down certified cost or pricing data requirements [@CL-EST-0675]; the underlying duties are set out in [Subcontract pricing considerations](est-subcontract-pricing-considerations.md). For a competitive subcontract, the contractor's data must show the degree and validity of competition achieved in source selection [@CL-EST-0676]. For a noncompetitive subcontract, the contractor must obtain complete certified cost or pricing data when the subcontract is above the threshold and no exception applies, and submit its own documented cost or price analysis with the prime proposal [@CL-EST-0677].

## How it works

If the auditor identifies, based on risk factors, a need for an assist audit of subcontract proposals, or the prime has not completed the required cost or price analysis, the manual points to another chapter for the process of requesting audit assistance [@CL-EST-0912]. When a proposal includes costs from a foreign entity, the auditor coordinates early with the contracting officer to arrange any required audit assistance [@CL-EST-0913]. The government has entered into agreements for financial audit services with Canada, France, Germany, The Netherlands and The United Kingdom, under which host nation audit agencies perform pricing and audit services [@CL-EST-0914]. If a proposal includes foreign subcontract costs under a reciprocal agreement and a need for audit is identified from risk factors, the auditor leaves those costs out of the audit scope and tells the contracting officer to request the audit through the international office of the contract management agency [@CL-EST-0678].

The manual's section on transfers inside a company group addresses transfers between divisions, subsidiaries or affiliates [@CL-EST-0915]. What falls under common control is treated as the prime contractor's own effort, so the prime does not perform a separate cost or price analysis but must meet certain requirements if the transfer is based on cost or price [@CL-EST-0679]. A transfer based on cost is generally proposed at cost, excluding interdivisional profit or fee, and supported by detailed cost data [@CL-EST-0680]. A transfer may be priced at catalog, market or commercial price only when that is the contractor's established practice and the item qualifies for an exception to certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0681].

Subcontractor pricing data is proprietary business information, and coordination with the subcontractor is critical to protect it [@CL-EST-9552]. Audit teams must coordinate with the subcontractor at the start of the Government's evaluation to determine whether it consents to or restricts release of audit results to the prime or a higher-tier subcontractor [@CL-EST-0916]. With consent, the Government may give the prime a summary identifying unsupported or questioned cost amounts [@CL-EST-0917]. Without consent, the Government is prohibited from releasing proprietary subcontractor details or audit findings to the prime [@CL-EST-0918].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime proposes a purchased assembly from a single source, above the certified data threshold and with no exception. The prime must get the supplier's certified data and attach its own cost analysis. The same prime also draws a part from a sister division at its cost without interdivisional profit, supported by cost detail, and does not run a separate analysis of that transfer.

## Never confuse

A noncompetitive subcontract with an outside supplier and an internal transfer are not treated alike. The outside subcontract needs certified data when above the threshold and no exception applies, plus the prime's own analysis [@CL-EST-0677], while the internal transfer is the prime's own effort, with cost or price support requirements instead [@CL-EST-0679]. The contracting officer's side of subcontract review is in [Subcontract pricing considerations](est-subcontract-pricing-considerations.md).
