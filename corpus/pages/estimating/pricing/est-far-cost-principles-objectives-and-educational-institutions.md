## Purpose

Shows why the cost principles are grouped by organization type, who must approve a deviation, and how the educational institution cost principles are incorporated, used as a guide and read.

The cost principles and procedures in the succeeding subparts are grouped basically by organizational type, for example commercial concerns and educational institutions [@CL-EST-2513]. The overall objective is that, to the extent practicable, all organizations of similar types doing similar work will follow the same cost principles and procedures [@CL-EST-2649].

To achieve this uniformity, individual deviations concerning cost principles require advance approval of the agency head or designee [@CL-EST-2514]. Class deviations for the civilian agencies require advance approval of the Civilian Agency Acquisition Council [@CL-EST-2650]. Class deviations for the National Aeronautics and Space Administration require advance approval of the Deputy Chief Acquisition Officer [@CL-EST-2515]. Class deviations for the Department of Defense require advance approval of the Principal Director, Defense Pricing and Contracting, Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment [@CL-EST-2516].

The educational institution category includes all contracts and contract modifications for research and development, training, and other work performed by educational institutions, defined as institutions of higher education in the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart A, and the statute it cites [@CL-EST-2517].

The contracting officer shall incorporate the cost principles and procedures in subpart 31.3 by reference in cost-reimbursement contracts with educational institutions [@CL-EST-2518]. They are the basis for determining reimbursable costs under the contracts and the cost-reimbursement subcontracts under them that are performed by educational institutions [@CL-EST-2651], for negotiating indirect cost rates [@CL-EST-2652], and for settling costs of cost-reimbursement terminated contracts [@CL-EST-2653].

The cost principles are to be used as a guide in evaluating costs in connection with negotiating fixed-price contracts and termination settlements [@CL-EST-2519].

A contract that refers to subpart 31.3 for determining allowable costs under contracts with educational institutions is deemed to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix III, in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-EST-2520; @CL-EST-2654]. The contracting officer determines the allowability of costs in accordance with that guidance [@CL-EST-2654]. Agencies are not expected to place additional restrictions on individual items of cost [@CL-EST-2655].

Subpart 31.1 describes the applicability of the cost principles and procedures in the succeeding subparts of part 31 to various types of contracts and subcontracts [@CL-EST-9194], and it also describes the need for advance agreements [@CL-EST-9195]. Specific principles and procedures for evaluating and determining costs in connection with contracts and subcontracts for construction, and architect-engineer contracts related to construction projects, are in 31.105 [@CL-EST-9196], and the applicability of those principles and procedures is set forth in 31.000 and 31.100 [@CL-EST-9197].

Subpart 31.3 provides the principles for determining the cost of research and development, training, and other work performed by educational institutions under contracts with the Government [@CL-EST-9198]. Contractors needing assistance in developing or improving their accounting systems and procedures may request a copy of Defense Contract Audit Agency Pamphlet No. 7641.90, Information for Contractors [@CL-EST-9193].

## How it works

The organization type picks the cost principles. A deviation from them needs advance approval at the level the section names: the agency head or designee for an individual deviation, and the civilian, NASA or DoD body or official above for a class deviation [@CL-EST-2514; @CL-EST-2650; @CL-EST-2515; @CL-EST-2516].

On a cost-reimbursement contract with an educational institution, the contracting officer incorporates subpart 31.3 by reference, and it governs reimbursable costs, indirect cost rate negotiation and the settlement of costs of terminated cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-EST-2518; @CL-EST-2651; @CL-EST-2652; @CL-EST-2653].

For fixed-price contracts and termination settlements the section calls the cost principles a guide in evaluating costs [@CL-EST-2519].

Where a contract refers to subpart 31.3 for allowable costs, the reference is read as the OMB Uniform Guidance text in effect on the contract date, and the contracting officer determines allowability [@CL-EST-2520; @CL-EST-2654].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.101 is reserved [@CL-EST-3117], so the codified statement of the overall objective, that organizations of similar types doing similar work follow the same cost principles and procedures [@CL-EST-2649], is not part of that section, and the grouping of the cost principles by organization type is in RFO FAR 31.100(a) [@CL-EST-3116]. Approval of deviations moved to RFO FAR 31.004, where, under the heading for civilian agencies, individual deviations from the cost principles require approval by the agency head and class deviations require approval by the Civilian Agency Acquisition Council, without the word advance that the codified text uses [@CL-EST-3118; @CL-EST-3119; @CL-EST-2514; @CL-EST-2650], and for DoD and NASA the paragraph refers readers to agency supplements for the approval of deviations [@CL-EST-3120]. The RFO text describes the educational institution category as contracts, subcontracts, and modifications to contracts and subcontracts performed by educational institutions [@CL-EST-3121], where the codified text reads all contracts and contract modifications for research and development, training, and other work performed by educational institutions [@CL-EST-2517]. Its first listed basis reads reimbursable costs under cost-reimbursement contracts and cost-reimbursement subcontracts performed by educational institutions [@CL-EST-3123], where the codified text reads under the contracts and cost-reimbursement subcontracts thereunder [@CL-EST-2651]; the bases of negotiating indirect cost rates and settling costs of cost-reimbursement terminated contracts are also listed [@CL-EST-3123]. The RFO text keeps the rules that the contracting officer incorporates subpart 31.3 by reference in cost-reimbursement contracts with educational institutions, that the principles are a guide for fixed-price contracts and termination settlements, and that a contract referring to subpart 31.3 for allowable costs is deemed to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance in effect on the date of the contract, with no additional restrictions on individual items of cost expected [@CL-EST-3122; @CL-EST-3124; @CL-EST-3125; @CL-EST-3126; @CL-EST-3127].

RFO FAR 31.000 states the scope of the part: it contains cost principles and procedures for the pricing of contracts, subcontracts, and modifications to contracts and subcontracts whenever cost analysis is performed (see 15.404-1(c)), and for the determination, negotiation, or allowance of costs when required by a contract clause, which is the same in substance as the codified text [@CL-EST-9256; @CL-CMP-8711]. RFO FAR 31.301 says the subpart provides the principles for determining the cost of work performed by educational institutions under contracts and contract modifications with the Government, where the codified text names research and development, training, and other work and says under contracts with the Government [@CL-EST-9257; @CL-EST-9198]. RFO FAR 31.302 says the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix III, provides principles for determining the costs applicable to work performed by educational institutions under contracts with the Government, without the codified list of research and development, training, and other work and without the codified parenthetical that defines educational institutions [@CL-EST-9258; @CL-CMP-7539; @CL-CMP-7540].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An educational institution holds a cost-reimbursement research contract. The contracting officer incorporates subpart 31.3 by reference [@CL-EST-2518]. It is the basis for reimbursable costs, for negotiating the institution's indirect cost rates, and for settling costs if the contract is terminated [@CL-EST-2651; @CL-EST-2652; @CL-EST-2653].

The same institution is later offered a firm-fixed-price contract. In negotiating it, the cost principles are a guide in evaluating costs [@CL-EST-2519].

The cost-reimbursement contract refers to subpart 31.3 for allowable costs. It is read as referring to the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix III as in effect on the contract date, and the contracting officer determines allowability in accordance with it [@CL-EST-2520; @CL-EST-2654].

A program office asks to depart from one cost principle for a single contract. That is an individual deviation, which needs advance approval of the agency head or designee [@CL-EST-2514] and, in the RFO paragraph for civilian agencies, approval by the agency head [@CL-EST-3118]. A departure meant to apply to a class of contracts across the civilian agencies would be a class deviation, which needs advance approval of the Civilian Agency Acquisition Council [@CL-EST-2650], and the RFO paragraph for civilian agencies also requires approval by that Council for class deviations [@CL-EST-3119].

## Never confuse

The principles are the basis for settling costs of terminated cost-reimbursement contracts [@CL-EST-2653], and a guide in evaluating costs in connection with negotiating fixed-price contracts and termination settlements [@CL-EST-2519].

A contract that refers to subpart 31.3 for allowable costs is deemed to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance, and the contracting officer determines allowability in accordance with it [@CL-EST-2520; @CL-EST-2654].

A deviation needs approval in advance, not afterwards. Individual and class deviations each need advance approval at the level the section names [@CL-EST-2514; @CL-EST-2650; @CL-EST-2515; @CL-EST-2516].

The codified text requires advance approval of the agency head or designee for individual deviations and names the approving official for NASA and DoD class deviations [@CL-EST-2514; @CL-EST-2515; @CL-EST-2516], while RFO FAR 31.004 requires approval by the agency head for individual deviations under its civilian agencies heading, without the word advance, and refers readers to agency supplements for DoD and NASA [@CL-EST-3118; @CL-EST-3120; @CL-EST-2514].
