## Purpose

Shows what the prospective price redetermination clause asks the contractor to submit, and what follows when the parties do not agree. The contract-type card covers when this contract type may be used, so this card does not repeat it: [Prospective price redetermination contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-prospective-price-redetermination.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1650] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-5 [@CL-EST-1650] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-5 [@CL-EST-0491] |
| Edition date | JAN 2022 [@CL-EST-9808] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1650] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the clause as prescribed [@CL-EST-1650] |

The clause divides performance into successive periods: the first runs from the contract date to a stated point, and each later period runs for a stated number of months from the end of the last, except that the parties may agree to vary the length of the final period [@CL-EST-1652]. Prices are periodically redetermined, subject to listed exceptions that include prices for supplies delivered and services performed before the first effective date of price redetermination, which remain fixed [@CL-EST-1651]. A second listed exception is that in no event may the total amount paid under the contract exceed any ceiling price included in the contract [@CL-EST-1700].

The contractor submits its data within a window set by a stated maximum and a stated minimum number of days before the end of each redetermination period except the last [@CL-EST-1653]. One listed submission is proposed prices for supplies that may be delivered or services that may be performed in the next period, with an estimate and breakdown of their costs in the Table 15-2 format or another agreed form [@CL-EST-1654].

## How it works

Two further listed items accompany the cost estimate: sufficient data to support its accuracy and reliability, and an explanation of its differences from the original or last preceding estimate [@CL-EST-1655]. A second listed submission is a statement of all costs incurred through the end of a stated month before the proposed prices are submitted [@CL-EST-1656]. If the contractor fails to submit the required data within the time specified, the contracting officer may suspend payments until the data are furnished [@CL-EST-1657]. If the parties fail to agree upon redetermined prices for any price redetermination period within 60 days, or another agreed period, after the date on which the data are to be submitted, the contracting officer shall promptly issue a decision [@CL-EST-1658], and that decision is issued in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-EST-1701]. Pending final settlement of the disagreement on appeal, by failure to appeal, or by agreement, the decision is treated as an executed contract modification for the clause's paragraphs (f), (g), and (h) [@CL-EST-1702], which are headed Contract modifications, Adjusting billing prices, and Quarterly limitation on payments statement [@CL-EST-9553; @CL-EST-9554; @CL-EST-9555]. The clause leaves a blank, marked as the appropriate number to insert, for the number of months in each later period [@CL-EST-1652].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.216-5 keeps the successive periods, in which the first runs from the date of the contract to a stated point [@CL-EST-0488; @CL-EST-1652] and each later period runs for a stated number of months from the end of the last preceding period, except that the parties may agree to vary the length of the final period [@CL-EST-0489; @CL-EST-1652]. It keeps the periodic redetermination of prices [@CL-EST-0490; @CL-EST-1651], except that prices for supplies delivered and services performed before the first effective date of price redetermination remain fixed and the total amount paid may not exceed any ceiling price in the contract [@CL-EST-0491; @CL-EST-1651; @CL-EST-1700]. It keeps the submission window of a stated maximum and minimum number of days before the end of each redetermination period except the last [@CL-EST-0492; @CL-EST-1653], the sufficient supporting data and the explanation of differences from the original or last preceding estimate [@CL-EST-0494; @CL-EST-1655], the statement of all costs incurred through the end of a stated month before the proposed prices are submitted [@CL-EST-0495; @CL-EST-1656], and the Contracting Officer’s right to suspend payments if the data are not submitted within the time specified [@CL-EST-0496; @CL-EST-1657]. If the parties fail to agree within 60 days, or within another agreed period, after the data are to be submitted, it keeps the Contracting Officer’s prompt decision in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-EST-0497; @CL-EST-0498; @CL-EST-1658; @CL-EST-1701]. It also keeps the treatment of that decision, pending final settlement of the disagreement on appeal, by failure to appeal, or by agreement, as an executed contract modification for paragraphs (f), (g), and (h) [@CL-EST-9147; @CL-EST-1702]. The differences this page records are the two table references: RFO paragraph (d)(1)(i)(A) says the cost estimate is in the format of Table 15-1 of FAR 15.408, where the codified paragraph says Table 15-2 [@CL-EST-0493; @CL-EST-1654], and RFO paragraph (d)(1)(ii) says the statement of all costs is in the format of Table 15-1 of FAR 15.408 (or any other form the parties agree), where the codified paragraph says Table 15-2 [@CL-EST-9146; @CL-EST-9148].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract sets twelve-month periods. The submission window runs from 90 days to 60 days before a period ends.

The contractor sends proposed prices, a cost breakdown and the cost statement 75 days before the period ends. That is inside the window, so the data are on time and no payment suspension arises. If the contractor had sent nothing, the contracting officer could suspend payments until the data arrived.

## Never confuse

FAR 52.216-5 holds initial-period prices fixed and redetermines prices forward for stated future periods [@CL-EST-0491; @CL-EST-1650; @CL-EST-1652], unlike [Fixed ceiling price with retroactive price redetermination](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-ceiling-price-retroactive-price-redetermination.md), which reprices past performance retroactively within a fixed ceiling after contract completion [@CL-CLS-0556].
