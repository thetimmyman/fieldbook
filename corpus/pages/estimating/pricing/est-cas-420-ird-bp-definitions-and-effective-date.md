## Purpose

Gives each term the Standard on independent research and development and bid and proposal costs defines, in the words the definitions section uses, and states when the Standard takes effect and when a contractor begins to apply it. The allocation requirements sit in the Standard's main card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-EST-2924] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.420-30 and 9904.420-63 [@CL-EST-2924; @CL-EST-2940] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2924] |

The definitions section defines terms that are prominent in the Standard [@CL-EST-3037]. Other terms defined elsewhere in the chapter keep the meanings those definitions give them unless the modification paragraph of this definitions section requires otherwise [@CL-EST-2924]. The modification paragraph makes no modifications to those terms [@CL-EST-2939].

Allocate means to assign an item of cost, or a group of items of cost, to one or more cost objectives, and the term includes both direct assignment of cost and the reassignment of a share from an indirect cost pool [@CL-EST-2925].

Bid and proposal cost means the cost incurred in preparing, submitting or supporting any bid or proposal whose effort is neither sponsored by a grant nor required in the performance of a contract [@CL-EST-2926].

Business unit means any segment of an organization, or an entire business organization that is not divided into segments [@CL-EST-2927].

General and administrative expense means any management, financial and other expenses incurred by or allocated to a business unit and for the general management and administration of the business unit as a whole [@CL-EST-2928]. It does not include management expenses whose beneficial or causal relationship to cost objectives can be more directly measured by a base other than a cost input base representing the total activity of a business unit during a cost accounting period [@CL-EST-2929].

Home office means an office responsible for directing or managing two or more, but not necessarily all, segments of an organization [@CL-EST-2930]. It typically establishes policy for and provides guidance to the segments in their operations, usually performs management, supervisory or administrative functions, and may also perform service functions in support of the operations of the various segments [@CL-EST-2931]. An organization with intermediate levels such as groups may have several home offices reporting to a common home office, and an intermediate organization may be both a segment and a home office [@CL-EST-2932].

Independent research and development means the cost of effort that is neither sponsored by a grant nor required in the performance of a contract and that falls within any of three areas [@CL-EST-2933]. The areas are basic and applied research, development, and systems and other concept formulation studies [@CL-EST-2934].

Indirect cost means any cost not directly identified with a single final cost objective but identified with two or more final cost objectives or with at least one intermediate cost objective [@CL-EST-2935].

Segment means one of two or more divisions, product departments, plants or other subdivisions of an organization reporting directly to a home office, usually identified with responsibility for profit and/or producing a product or service [@CL-EST-2936]. The term includes Government-owned contractor-operated facilities and joint ventures and subsidiaries, domestic and foreign, in which the organization has a majority ownership [@CL-EST-2937]. It also includes joint ventures and subsidiaries, domestic and foreign, in which the organization has less than a majority of ownership but over which it exercises control [@CL-EST-2938].

The Standard is effective as of April 17, 1992 [@CL-EST-2940]. Contractors with prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage shall continue the Standard's applicability upon receipt of a contract to which the Standard is applicable [@CL-EST-2941]. A contractor with no previous contracts subject to the Standard applies it beginning with its second full fiscal year beginning after it receives a contract to which the Standard is applicable [@CL-EST-2942].

## How it works

The definitions fix the vocabulary the Standard uses: allocate, bid and proposal cost, business unit, general and administrative expense, home office, independent research and development, indirect cost and segment [@CL-EST-2925; @CL-EST-2926; @CL-EST-2927; @CL-EST-2928; @CL-EST-2930; @CL-EST-2933; @CL-EST-2935; @CL-EST-2936]. Several definitions carry a condition or exclusion: bid and proposal cost and independent research and development each cover only effort that is neither sponsored by a grant nor required in the performance of a contract, and general and administrative expense leaves out management expenses that another base measures more directly [@CL-EST-2926; @CL-EST-2933; @CL-EST-2929]. The segment definition includes Government-owned contractor-operated facilities and joint ventures and subsidiaries, whether majority owned or controlled with less than a majority [@CL-EST-2937; @CL-EST-2938].

The effective date section starts the Standard at a fixed date, continues it for contractors that already held full coverage, and phases it in for contractors with no earlier contracts subject to it [@CL-EST-2940; @CL-EST-2941; @CL-EST-2942]. The sections name no official who decides and set no money threshold; the only timing rules are those in the effective date section.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor with a calendar fiscal year receives its first contract subject to the Standard in July of Year 1 and has no earlier contracts subject to it. The first full fiscal year beginning after the receipt is Year 2 and the second is Year 3, so the Standard is applied beginning with Year 3 [@CL-EST-2942]. A second contractor already held CAS-covered contracts with full coverage, so it continues the Standard's applicability when it receives the new contract [@CL-EST-2941].

A company spends 60,000 preparing a proposal that no grant sponsors and no contract requires, which fits the definition of bid and proposal cost [@CL-EST-2926]. It also spends 40,000 on a research effort that a contract requires in performance. That effort is not independent research and development under the definition, because the definition covers only effort not required in the performance of a contract [@CL-EST-2933].

## Never confuse

Bid and proposal cost is not independent research and development. The first is cost incurred in preparing, submitting or supporting a bid or proposal, and the second is cost of effort within basic and applied research, development, or systems and other concept formulation studies; both exclude effort sponsored by a grant or required in the performance of a contract [@CL-EST-2926; @CL-EST-2933; @CL-EST-2934]. The effective date is also not the date a contractor begins to apply the Standard: the Standard is effective as of April 17, 1992, yet a contractor with no previous contracts subject to it applies it beginning with its second full fiscal year beginning after it receives a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2940; @CL-EST-2942]. The allocation requirements are in [the Standard's main card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-420-ird-bp-allocation.md) and worked illustrations in [the illustrations card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-418-and-420-illustrations.md).
