## Purpose

Shows how the retroactive redetermination clause sets the price and its ceiling, what the Contractor bills and may be paid while the price is open, and how the two no-fee cost clauses limit a fee and hold back a reserve.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1826] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-6, FAR 52.216-11 and FAR 52.216-12 [@CL-EST-1826; @CL-EST-1830; @CL-EST-1833] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1826] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the retroactive redetermination clause, the cost contract no fee clause or the cost-sharing contract no fee clause [@CL-EST-1826; @CL-EST-1830; @CL-EST-1833]; the retroactive clause is inserted when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the listed conditions apply [@CL-EST-1863] |

The unit price and the total price are redetermined under the retroactive clause, and in no event may the total amount paid exceed the ceiling price inserted in the clause [@CL-EST-1826]. Under the cost contract clause the Government shall not pay the Contractor a fee for performing the contract [@CL-EST-1830], and the cost-sharing clause says the same [@CL-EST-1833].

While price redetermination is open, the quarterly limitation on payments paragraph applies until final price redetermination under the contract has been completed [@CL-EST-1895]. It requires the Contractor, within 45 days after the end of the quarter of its fiscal year in which a delivery is first made or services are first performed and accepted by the Government, and for each quarter thereafter, to submit to the contract administration office [@CL-EST-1860] a statement that goes with a copy to the contracting office and the cognizant contract auditor and is cumulative from the beginning of the contract [@CL-EST-1896]. The statement shows, first, the total contract price of all supplies delivered or services performed and accepted by the Government for which final prices have been established [@CL-EST-1897]; second, the total costs, estimated to the extent necessary, reasonably incurred for and properly allocable solely to the supplies delivered or services performed and accepted for which final prices have not been established [@CL-EST-1898]; third, the portion of the total interim profit, used in establishing the initial contract price or agreed to for the purpose of the paragraph, that is in direct proportion to the supplies delivered or services performed and accepted by the Government and for which final prices have not been established [@CL-EST-1899]; and fourth, the total amount of all invoices or vouchers for supplies delivered or services performed and accepted by the Government, including amounts applied or to be applied to liquidate progress payments [@CL-EST-1900]. If on a quarterly statement the amount under the fourth listed item exceeds the sum due the Contractor as computed from the first three, the Contractor shall immediately refund or credit the excess to the Government [@CL-EST-1861]. That test applies notwithstanding any provision of the contract authorizing greater payments [@CL-EST-1911]. The Contractor may, when appropriate, reduce a quarterly refund or credit by the amount of previous refunds or credits effected under the clause [@CL-EST-1901]. If the Contractor fails to submit the quarterly statement within 45 days after the end of each quarter and it is later determined that the Government has overpaid it, the Contractor shall repay the excess to the Government immediately [@CL-EST-1862]. Unless an excess from a missed quarterly statement is repaid within 30 days after the end of the statement submittal period, it bears interest, computed from the date the quarterly statement was due to the date of repayment, at the rate established in accordance with the Interest clause [@CL-EST-1902].

## How it works

The Contractor shall submit the data within the number of days the Contracting Officer inserts after delivery of all supplies to be delivered and completion of all services to be performed under the contract [@CL-EST-1890]. The data are proposed prices, a statement of all costs incurred in performing the contract in the format of the FAR cost or pricing data table or another form the parties agree on, and any other relevant data that the Contracting Officer may reasonably require [@CL-EST-1891]. If the Contractor fails to submit the required data within the time specified, the Contracting Officer may suspend payments under the contract until the data are furnished [@CL-EST-1892]. Unless an excess is repaid within 30 days after the end of the data submittal period, it bears interest, computed from the date the data were due to the date of repayment, at the rate established in accordance with the Interest clause [@CL-EST-1893]. On receipt of the required data, the Contracting Officer and the Contractor shall promptly negotiate to redetermine fair and reasonable prices for supplies delivered and services performed [@CL-EST-1827]. Pending the modification that records the redetermined price, the Contractor submits invoices or vouchers at the billing prices stated in the contract [@CL-EST-1828]. If it appears that the then-current billing prices will be substantially greater than the estimated final prices, or the Contractor submits data showing that the redetermined prices will be substantially greater than the current billing prices, the parties shall negotiate an appropriate decrease or increase in billing prices [@CL-EST-1894]. If the parties fail to agree within the stated period, the Contracting Officer shall promptly issue a decision under the Disputes clause [@CL-EST-1829]. Under the cost contract clause the Contracting Officer may withhold allowable cost after payment of 80 percent of the total estimated cost shown in the Schedule until a reserve is set aside [@CL-EST-1831], and that reserve shall not exceed the lesser of one percent of the total estimated cost shown in the Schedule or $100,000 [@CL-EST-1832]. The cost contract clause may be modified by substituting 10,000 dollars in lieu of 100,000 dollars as the maximum reserve if the Contractor is a nonprofit organization [@CL-EST-1903]. Under Alternate I, in a contract for research and development with an educational institution or a nonprofit organization for which the contracting officer has determined that withholding of a portion of allowable costs is not required, paragraph (b) of the basic clause is deleted [@CL-EST-1905]. Under the cost-sharing clause, after paying 80 percent of the Government's share of the total estimated cost of performance shown in the Schedule, the Contracting Officer may withhold further payment of allowable cost until a reserve is set aside [@CL-EST-1907], and that reserve shall not exceed the lesser of one percent of the Government's share of the total estimated cost shown in the Schedule or $100,000 [@CL-EST-1834]. The cost-sharing clause may be modified by substituting 10,000 dollars in lieu of 100,000 dollars as the maximum reserve if the contract is with a nonprofit organization [@CL-EST-1904]. Its Alternate I deletes paragraph (b) in a contract for research and development with an educational institution for which the contracting officer has determined that withholding of a portion of allowable cost is not required [@CL-EST-1906].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A retroactive contract carries a ceiling price of 1,000,000. Assume no earlier refunds or credits have been made. The first delivery is accepted in a quarter that ends on the last day of March, so the first quarterly statement is due within 45 days after that day. If the statement shows 600,000 under the fourth listed item against a sum due of 550,000 from the first three, the Contractor refunds or credits 50,000 at once, even if another provision of the contract would authorize paying the greater amount. After delivery the Contractor submits its cost data and the parties negotiate a redetermined price of 900,000, recorded in a modification. Until then the Contractor bills at the contract billing prices. On a separate cost contract, assuming the Contractor is not a nonprofit organization and Alternate I does not apply, with a total estimated cost of 500,000, payment of 80 percent is 400,000. One percent of the estimate is 5,000, which is below the 100,000 dollar limit, so the reserve cannot exceed 5,000.

## Never confuse

The cost contract clause measures its reserve on the total estimated cost [@CL-EST-1832], while the cost-sharing clause measures it on the Government's share of that cost [@CL-EST-1834]. The quarterly statement is submitted by the Contractor to the contract administration office [@CL-EST-1860], while the redetermined price is negotiated after the required data are received [@CL-EST-1827]. Retroactive redetermination also differs from the prospective kind, which has its own card: [Prospective price redetermination clause](est-prospective-price-redetermination-clause.md). The contract-level view is in [Fixed-ceiling-price contracts with retroactive price redetermination](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-ceiling-price-retroactive-price-redetermination.md), and the contract type a solicitation names is covered in [Type of contract and letter contract provisions](est-type-of-contract-letter-contract-and-time-and-materials-provisions.md).
