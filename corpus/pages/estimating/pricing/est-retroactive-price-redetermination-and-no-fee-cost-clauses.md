## Purpose

Shows how the retroactive redetermination clause sets the price and its ceiling, what the Contractor bills and may be paid while the price is open, and how the two no-fee cost clauses limit a fee and hold back a reserve.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1826] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-6, FAR 52.216-11 and FAR 52.216-12 [@CL-EST-1826; @CL-EST-1830; @CL-EST-1833] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-6 [@CL-EST-0507]; RFO FAR 52.216-11 [@CL-EST-0529]; RFO FAR 52.216-12 [@CL-EST-0534] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1826] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include the retroactive redetermination clause, the cost contract no fee clause or the cost-sharing contract no fee clause [@CL-EST-1826; @CL-EST-1830; @CL-EST-1833]; when contracting by negotiation, the contracting officer shall insert the retroactive clause in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the conditions in FAR 16.206-2 and 16.206-3(a) through (d) apply [@CL-EST-1863] |

The unit price and the total price are redetermined under the retroactive clause, and in no event may the total amount paid exceed the ceiling price inserted in the clause [@CL-EST-1826]. Under the cost contract clause the Government shall not pay the Contractor a fee for performing the contract [@CL-EST-1830], and the cost-sharing clause says the same [@CL-EST-1833].

While price redetermination is open, the quarterly limitation on payments paragraph applies until final price redetermination under the contract has been completed [@CL-EST-1895]. It requires the Contractor, within 45 days after the end of the quarter of its fiscal year in which a delivery is first made or services are first performed and accepted by the Government, and for each quarter thereafter, to submit to the contract administration office [@CL-EST-1860] a statement that goes with a copy to the contracting office and the cognizant contract auditor and is cumulative from the beginning of the contract [@CL-EST-1896]. The statement shows, first, the total contract price of all supplies delivered or services performed and accepted by the Government for which final prices have been established [@CL-EST-1897]; second, the total costs, estimated to the extent necessary, reasonably incurred for and properly allocable solely to the supplies delivered or services performed and accepted for which final prices have not been established [@CL-EST-1898]; third, the portion of the total interim profit, used in establishing the initial contract price or agreed to for the purpose of the paragraph, that is in direct proportion to the supplies delivered or services performed and accepted by the Government and for which final prices have not been established [@CL-EST-1899]; and fourth, the total amount of all invoices or vouchers for supplies delivered or services performed and accepted by the Government, including amounts applied or to be applied to liquidate progress payments [@CL-EST-1900]. If on a quarterly statement the amount under the fourth listed item exceeds the sum due the Contractor as computed from the first three, the Contractor shall immediately refund or credit the excess to the Government [@CL-EST-1861]. That test applies notwithstanding any provision of the contract authorizing greater payments [@CL-EST-1911]. The Contractor may, when appropriate, reduce a quarterly refund or credit by the amount of previous refunds or credits effected under the clause [@CL-EST-1901], and must provide complete details to support any claimed reduction [@CL-EST-9433]. If the Contractor fails to submit the quarterly statement within 45 days after the end of each quarter and it is later determined that the Government has overpaid it, the Contractor shall repay the excess to the Government immediately [@CL-EST-1862]. Unless an excess from a missed quarterly statement is repaid within 30 days after the end of the statement submittal period, it bears interest, computed from the date the quarterly statement was due to the date of repayment, at the rate established in accordance with the Interest clause [@CL-EST-1902].

## How it works

The Contractor shall submit the data within the number of days the Contracting Officer inserts after delivery of all supplies to be delivered and completion of all services to be performed under the contract [@CL-EST-1890]. The data are proposed prices, a statement of all costs incurred in performing the contract in the format of table 15-2, FAR 15.408, or in any other form on which the parties may agree, and any other relevant data that the Contracting Officer may reasonably require [@CL-EST-1891]. If the Contractor fails to submit the required data within the time specified, the Contracting Officer may suspend payments under the contract until the data are furnished [@CL-EST-1892]. If it is later determined that the Government has overpaid the Contractor, the excess shall be repaid to the Government immediately [@CL-EST-9546]. Unless the excess is repaid within 30 days after the end of the data submittal period, it bears interest, computed from the date the data were due to the date of repayment, at the rate established in accordance with the Interest clause [@CL-EST-1893]. On receipt of the required data, the Contracting Officer and the Contractor shall promptly negotiate to redetermine fair and reasonable prices for supplies delivered and services performed [@CL-EST-1827]. Pending the modification that records the redetermined price, the Contractor submits invoices or vouchers at the billing prices stated in the contract [@CL-EST-1828]. If it appears that the then-current billing prices will be substantially greater than the estimated final prices, or the Contractor submits data showing that the redetermined prices will be substantially greater than the current billing prices, the parties shall negotiate an appropriate decrease or increase in billing prices [@CL-EST-1894; @CL-EST-9707]. If the parties fail to agree upon redetermined prices within 60 days, or within such other period as the parties agree, after the date on which the data are to be submitted, the Contracting Officer shall promptly issue a decision in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-EST-1829]. Under the cost contract clause the Contracting Officer may withhold further payment of allowable cost after payment of 80 percent of the total estimated cost shown in the Schedule until a reserve is set aside in an amount that the Contracting Officer considers necessary to protect the Government's interest [@CL-EST-1831], and that reserve shall not exceed the lesser of one percent of the total estimated cost shown in the Schedule or $100,000 [@CL-EST-1832]. The cost contract clause may be modified by substituting 10,000 dollars in lieu of 100,000 dollars as the maximum reserve if the Contractor is a nonprofit organization [@CL-EST-1903]. Under Alternate I, in a contract for research and development with an educational institution or a nonprofit organization for which the contracting officer has determined that withholding of a portion of allowable costs is not required, paragraph (b) of the basic clause is deleted [@CL-EST-1905]. Under the cost-sharing clause, after paying 80 percent of the Government's share of the total estimated cost of performance shown in the Schedule, the Contracting Officer may withhold further payment of allowable cost until a reserve is set aside [@CL-EST-1907] in an amount that the Contracting Officer considers necessary to protect the Government's interest [@CL-EST-9547], and that reserve shall not exceed the lesser of one percent of the Government's share of the total estimated cost shown in the Schedule or $100,000 [@CL-EST-1834]. The cost-sharing clause may be modified by substituting 10,000 dollars in lieu of 100,000 dollars as the maximum reserve if the contract is with a nonprofit organization [@CL-EST-1904]. Its Alternate I deletes paragraph (b) in a contract for research and development with an educational institution for which the contracting officer has determined that withholding of a portion of allowable cost is not required [@CL-EST-1906].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.216-6 keeps the redetermination of the unit price and the total price, with the total amount paid not exceeding the ceiling price inserted in the clause [@CL-EST-0507; @CL-EST-1826]. It keeps the submission of data within the number of days the Contracting Officer inserts after delivery of all supplies and completion of all services [@CL-EST-0508; @CL-EST-1890], the Contracting Officer’s right to suspend payments if the data are not submitted within the time specified [@CL-EST-0510; @CL-EST-1892], and the interest, at the rate established in accordance with the Interest clause, on an excess that is not repaid within 30 days after the end of the data submittal period, computed from the date the data were due to the date of repayment [@CL-EST-9149; @CL-EST-1893]. It says the Contractor submits the cost statement in the format of Table 15-1 of FAR 15.408, or in any other form on which the parties may agree, where the codified paragraph says table 15-2 [@CL-EST-0509; @CL-EST-1891]. It keeps the prompt negotiation by the Contracting Officer and the Contractor, upon receipt of the data required by paragraph (c), to redetermine fair and reasonable prices for supplies delivered and services performed by the Contractor under the contract [@CL-EST-9150; @CL-EST-1827], the submission of invoices or vouchers in accordance with the billing prices stated in the contract pending execution of the contract modification [@CL-EST-9151; @CL-EST-1828], and the parties’ negotiation of an appropriate decrease or increase in billing prices if at any time it appears that the then-current billing prices will be substantially greater than the estimated final prices, or if the Contractor submits data showing that the redetermined prices will be substantially greater than the current billing prices [@CL-EST-9152; @CL-EST-9153; @CL-EST-1894]. If the parties fail to agree within 60 days, or within another agreed period, it keeps the Contracting Officer’s prompt decision in accordance with the Disputes clause [@CL-EST-0511; @CL-EST-1829]. It keeps the application of the quarterly limitation on payments statement paragraph until final price redetermination under the contract has been completed [@CL-EST-9154; @CL-EST-1895]. It keeps the quarterly statement due within 45 days after the end of the quarter in which a delivery is first made and accepted [@CL-EST-0512; @CL-EST-1860], with a copy to the contracting office and the cognizant contract auditor and cumulative from the beginning of the contract [@CL-EST-0513; @CL-EST-1896], showing the four listed items [@CL-EST-0514; @CL-EST-1897] [@CL-EST-0515; @CL-EST-1898] [@CL-EST-0516; @CL-EST-1899] [@CL-EST-0517; @CL-EST-1900]. It keeps the immediate refund or credit of an excess, notwithstanding any provision authorizing greater payments [@CL-EST-0518; @CL-EST-1911] [@CL-EST-0519; @CL-EST-1861], the Contractor’s ability, when appropriate, to reduce that refund or credit by the amount of previous refunds or credits effected under the clause, with complete details to support any claimed reduction [@CL-EST-9155; @CL-EST-1901; @CL-EST-9434; @CL-EST-9433], and the immediate repayment if the quarterly statement is not submitted in time and an overpayment is later found [@CL-EST-0528; @CL-EST-1862], with interest, at the rate established in accordance with the Interest clause, on an excess that is not repaid within 30 days after the end of the statement submittal period, computed from the date the quarterly statement was due to the date of repayment [@CL-EST-9156; @CL-EST-1902]. RFO FAR 52.216-11 keeps the rule that the Government shall not pay a fee [@CL-EST-0529; @CL-EST-1830], the withholding after payment of 80 percent of the total estimated cost shown in the Schedule [@CL-EST-0530; @CL-EST-1831] and the reserve limit of one percent of that estimated cost or $100,000, whichever is less [@CL-EST-0531; @CL-EST-1832]. RFO FAR 52.216-12 keeps the no-fee rule [@CL-EST-0534; @CL-EST-1833], the withholding after paying 80 percent of the Government’s share, until a reserve is set aside in an amount that the Contracting Officer considers necessary to protect the Government’s interest [@CL-EST-0535; @CL-EST-0536; @CL-EST-1907], and the reserve limit measured on the Government’s share [@CL-EST-0536; @CL-EST-1834]. The RFO prescription for FAR 52.216-11 reads as prescribed in 16.305(e), insert the following clause [@CL-EST-0532], and the RFO prescription for FAR 52.216-12 reads as prescribed in 16.307(f), insert the following clause [@CL-EST-0537]. RFO Alternate I of FAR 52.216-11 reads that, as prescribed in 16.305(e)(2), paragraph (b) of the basic clause is deleted [@CL-EST-0533], and RFO Alternate I of FAR 52.216-12 reads that, as prescribed in 16.305(f)(2), paragraph (b) of the basic clause is deleted [@CL-EST-0538].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A retroactive contract carries a ceiling price of 1,000,000. Assume no earlier refunds or credits have been made. The first delivery is accepted in a quarter that ends on the last day of March, so the first quarterly statement is due within 45 days after that day. If the statement shows 600,000 under the fourth listed item against a sum due of 550,000 from the first three, the Contractor refunds or credits 50,000 at once, even if another provision of the contract would authorize paying the greater amount. After delivery the Contractor submits its cost data and the parties negotiate a redetermined price of 900,000, recorded in a modification. Until then the Contractor bills at the contract billing prices. On a separate cost contract, assuming the Contractor is not a nonprofit organization and Alternate I does not apply, with a total estimated cost of 500,000, payment of 80 percent is 400,000. One percent of the estimate is 5,000, which is below the 100,000 dollar limit, so the reserve cannot exceed 5,000.

## Never confuse

The cost contract clause measures its reserve on the total estimated cost [@CL-EST-1832], while the cost-sharing clause measures it on the Government's share of that cost [@CL-EST-1834]. The quarterly statement is submitted by the Contractor to the contract administration office [@CL-EST-1860], while the redetermined price is negotiated after the required data are received [@CL-EST-1827]. The prospective kind has its own card: [Prospective price redetermination clause](est-prospective-price-redetermination-clause.md). The contract-level view is in [Fixed-ceiling-price contracts with retroactive price redetermination](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-ceiling-price-retroactive-price-redetermination.md), and the contract type a solicitation names is covered in [Type of contract and letter contract provisions](est-type-of-contract-letter-contract-and-time-and-materials-provisions.md).

The codified cost clauses may be modified by substituting $10,000 in lieu of $100,000 as the maximum reserve if the Contractor is a nonprofit organization (FAR 52.216-11) or if the contract is with a nonprofit organization (FAR 52.216-12) [@CL-EST-1903; @CL-EST-1904], while the RFO prescriptions read as prescribed in 16.305(e) or 16.307(f), insert the following clause [@CL-EST-0532; @CL-EST-0537]. The codified Alternate I clauses delete paragraph (b) of the basic clause in a contract for research and development with an educational institution (FAR 52.216-11 also names a nonprofit organization) for which the contracting officer has determined that withholding of a portion of allowable cost is not required [@CL-EST-1905; @CL-EST-1906], while the RFO Alternate I clauses read that, as prescribed in 16.305(e)(2) or 16.305(f)(2), paragraph (b) of the basic clause is deleted [@CL-EST-0533; @CL-EST-0538]. The codified retroactive clause points to table 15-2 for the cost statement [@CL-EST-1891], while RFO FAR 52.216-6 points to Table 15-1 [@CL-EST-0509].
