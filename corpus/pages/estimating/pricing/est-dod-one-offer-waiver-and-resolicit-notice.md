## Purpose

Explains the DoD policy for a competitive solicitation that receives only one offer [@CL-EST-2800], the head of the contracting activity's authority to waive the resolicitation requirement [@CL-EST-2803], and the provision used in a competitive solicitation that will be solicited for fewer than 30 days [@CL-EST-2805]. The section heading is Only one offer [@CL-EST-2811]. The resolicitation steps, the exceptions and the price determination are on [the DoD one-offer price determination card](est-dod-one-offer-price-determination.md), the offeror's own agreement to submit data is on [the one-offer card](est-only-one-offer.md), and the provision's text is on [the resolicit notice card](est-dfars-resolicit-notice-and-selected-reserve-clause.md).

It is DoD policy, if only one offer is received in response to a competitive solicitation, to take the required actions to promote competition under 215.371-2 [@CL-EST-2800]. The policy also covers ensuring that the price is fair and reasonable under 215.371-3 [@CL-EST-2801] and complying with the statutory requirement for certified cost or pricing data under FAR 15.403-4 [@CL-EST-2802].

The head of the contracting activity is authorized to waive the requirement at 215.371-2 to resolicit for an additional period of at least 30 days [@CL-EST-2803]. This waiver authority cannot be delegated below one level above the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2804].

Use the provision at 252.215-7007, Notice of Intent to Resolicit, in competitive solicitations that will be solicited for fewer than 30 days, unless an exception at 215.371-4 applies or the requirement is waived in accordance with 215.371-5 [@CL-EST-2805].

## How it works

The policy has two strands: competition actions, and price assurance that includes the statutory certified cost or pricing data requirement [@CL-EST-2800; @CL-EST-2801; @CL-EST-2802].

The waiver sits with the head of the contracting activity [@CL-EST-2803]. Any delegation cannot go below one level above the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2804]. The waiver authority covers the requirement at 215.371-2 to resolicit for an additional period of at least 30 days [@CL-EST-2803].

The provision is the Notice of Intent to Resolicit at 252.215-7007, used when a competitive solicitation will be solicited for fewer than 30 days [@CL-EST-2805]. It is used unless an exception at 215.371-4 applies or the requirement is waived in accordance with 215.371-5 [@CL-EST-2805]. Section 215.371-4 provides that the requirements at 215.371-2 do not apply to listed acquisitions, including acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-1286], acquisitions, as determined by the head of the contracting activity, in support of contingency or humanitarian or peacekeeping operations [@CL-EST-1286], and acquisitions, as determined by the head of the contracting activity, to facilitate defense against or recovery from cyber, nuclear, biological, chemical, or radiological attack, to facilitate the provision of international disaster assistance, or to support response to an emergency or major disaster [@CL-EST-9656]. The resolicitation steps and the price determination are on [the DoD one-offer price determination card](est-dod-one-offer-price-determination.md), and the offeror's own agreement to submit data is on [the one-offer card](est-only-one-offer.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A competitive solicitation will be open for 10 days, which is fewer than 30 days. Assume no exception at 215.371-4 applies and the requirement is not waived when the solicitation is issued. Then the notice of intent to resolicit provision is used [@CL-EST-2805]. The solicitation receives one offer. DoD policy is then to take the required actions to promote competition under 215.371-2 and to ensure a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-2800; @CL-EST-2801]. Suppose the head of the contracting activity then waives the requirement at 215.371-2 to resolicit for an additional period of at least 30 days [@CL-EST-2803]. The waiver is of that requirement [@CL-EST-2803]. The policy on a fair and reasonable price is stated separately [@CL-EST-2801].

## Never confuse

The waiver covers the requirement at 215.371-2 to resolicit for an additional period of at least 30 days [@CL-EST-2803]. The policy to ensure that the price is fair and reasonable and to comply with the statutory requirement for certified cost or pricing data is at 215.371-1(b) [@CL-EST-2801; @CL-EST-2802]. The notice provision is used in competitive solicitations that will be solicited for fewer than 30 days, unless an exception at 215.371-4 applies or the requirement is waived in accordance with 215.371-5 [@CL-EST-2805]. The head of the contracting activity holds the waiver authority, and it cannot be delegated below one level above the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2803; @CL-EST-2804].
