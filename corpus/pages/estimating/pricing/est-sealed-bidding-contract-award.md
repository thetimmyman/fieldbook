## Purpose

Sets out the award basis in sealed bidding, the Government's rights to reject bids, waive irregularities and take part of a bid, when an award makes a binding contract, and how materially unbalanced prices are treated, under the general and the construction award provisions.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1978] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-10 [@CL-EST-1978]; FAR 52.214-19 [@CL-EST-1984] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.214-10 [@CL-EST-0563]; RFO FAR 52.214-19 [@CL-EST-0572] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1978] |
| Applies to | Sealed bidding solicitations that include the general award provision [@CL-EST-2086] or the construction award provision [@CL-EST-2091] |

In the general award provision the Government will evaluate bids without discussions [@CL-EST-2087] and will award to the responsible bidder whose bid conforms to the solicitation and is most advantageous to the Government, considering only price and the price-related factors specified elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-EST-1978]. The construction provision says the same about evaluation without discussions [@CL-EST-2092] and about the award basis [@CL-EST-1984].

The Government may reject any or all bids, accept other than the lowest bid, and waive informalities or minor irregularities in bids received [@CL-EST-1979]. In the construction provision the Government may reject any or all bids and waive informalities or minor irregularities [@CL-EST-1985].

The Government may accept any item or group of items of a bid unless the bidder qualifies the bid by specific limitations [@CL-EST-1980]. In construction it may accept any item or combination of items, unless a restrictive limitation in the solicitation or the bid precludes it [@CL-EST-2093].

Unless the Schedule provides otherwise, bids may be submitted for quantities less than those specified [@CL-EST-2088]. The Government reserves the right to make an award on any item for a quantity less than the quantity offered, at the unit prices offered, unless the bidder specifies otherwise in the bid [@CL-EST-2089].

A written award or acceptance of a bid, mailed or otherwise furnished to the successful bidder within the time for acceptance specified in the bid, results in a binding contract without further action by either party [@CL-EST-2090].

A bid may be rejected as nonresponsive if its prices are materially unbalanced between line items or subline items [@CL-EST-1981; @CL-EST-1986].

## How it works

A bid is materially unbalanced when it is based on prices significantly less than cost for some work and prices significantly overstated in relation to cost for other work, and there is a reasonable doubt that the bid will result in the lowest overall cost to the Government even though it may be the low evaluated bid [@CL-EST-1982; @CL-EST-2135]. A bid is also materially unbalanced if it is so unbalanced as to be tantamount to allowing an advance payment [@CL-EST-1983]. The construction provision gives the same definition and the same two grounds [@CL-EST-2094; @CL-EST-2136; @CL-EST-2095].

The general award provision is inserted as prescribed in 14.201-6(e) [@CL-EST-2086], and the construction award provision as prescribed in 14.201-6(m) [@CL-EST-2091]. How a bidder shows unit and extended prices, including how a disagreement between them is read, is covered on [Preparing and signing a sealed bid](est-bid-preparation-and-signature.md). The time for acceptance that a bid states is covered on [Place of performance and the bid acceptance period](est-bid-acceptance-period-and-place-of-performance.md).

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.214-10 keeps the general award provision's evaluation of bids without discussions [@CL-EST-0562] [@CL-EST-2087] and its award to the responsible bidder whose bid, conforming to the solicitation, will be most advantageous to the Government considering only price and the price-related factors specified elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-EST-0563] [@CL-EST-1978]. It keeps the Government's right to reject any or all bids, accept other than the lowest bid, and waive informalities or minor irregularities in bids received [@CL-EST-0564] [@CL-EST-1979], and its right to accept any item or group of items of a bid unless the bidder qualifies the bid by specific limitations [@CL-EST-0565] [@CL-EST-1980]. It keeps the rule that, unless otherwise provided in the Schedule, bids may be submitted for quantities less than those specified [@CL-EST-0566] [@CL-EST-2088], and the Government's right to make an award on any item for a quantity less than the quantity offered, at the unit prices offered, unless the bidder specifies otherwise in the bid [@CL-EST-0566] [@CL-EST-2089]. It keeps the rule that a written award or acceptance of a bid mailed or otherwise furnished to the successful bidder within the time for acceptance specified in the bid results in a binding contract without further action by either party [@CL-EST-0567] [@CL-EST-2090]. It keeps the Government's right to reject a bid as nonresponsive if the prices bid are materially unbalanced between line items or subline items [@CL-EST-0568] [@CL-EST-1981], and the definition: a bid is materially unbalanced when it is based on prices significantly less than cost for some work and prices significantly overstated in relation to cost for other work, and there is a reasonable doubt that the bid will result in the lowest overall cost to the Government [@CL-EST-0569] [@CL-EST-1982], even though it may be the low evaluated bid [@CL-EST-0570] [@CL-EST-2135], or if it is so unbalanced as to be tantamount to allowing an advance payment [@CL-EST-0570] [@CL-EST-1983]. RFO FAR 52.214-19 keeps the construction provision's evaluation of bids without discussions [@CL-EST-0571] [@CL-EST-2092] and its award to the responsible bidder whose bid, conforming to the solicitation, will be most advantageous to the Government, considering only price and the price-related factors specified elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-EST-0572] [@CL-EST-1984]. It keeps the right to reject any or all bids and waive informalities or minor irregularities in bids received [@CL-EST-0573] [@CL-EST-1985], and the right to accept any item or combination of items unless doing so is precluded by a restrictive limitation in the solicitation or the bid [@CL-EST-0574] [@CL-EST-2093]. It keeps the right to reject a bid as nonresponsive if the prices bid are materially unbalanced between line items or subline items [@CL-EST-0575] [@CL-EST-1986], with the same definition and the same two grounds [@CL-EST-0576] [@CL-EST-0577] [@CL-EST-2094] [@CL-EST-2136] [@CL-EST-2095].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A sealed-bid solicitation for supplies has a Schedule that permits quantities less than those specified. Bidder A offers 100 units of item 1 at 20 each and 100 units of item 2 at 65 each, a total of 8,500, the lowest. The Government's cost data show that item 1 costs about 40 per unit and item 2 about 40 per unit, so item 1 is priced significantly below cost and item 2 significantly above it. There is a reasonable doubt that the bid gives the lowest overall cost, even though it is the low evaluated bid. The Government may reject Bidder A's bid as nonresponsive for material unbalance.

Bidder B offers 100 units of item 1 at 40 each and 100 units of item 2 at 50 each, a total of 9,000, and states no limitation. The Government may award B 60 units of item 2 at 50 each, because the Schedule permits quantities less than those specified and B did not specify otherwise. If the Government mails a written award to B on day 40 and B's bid allows 60 days for acceptance, a binding contract results without further action by either party.

## Never confuse

The Government may reject a bid as nonresponsive if the prices bid are materially unbalanced between line items or subline items [@CL-EST-1981; @CL-EST-1986], and the definition of a materially unbalanced bid applies even though the bid may be the low evaluated bid [@CL-EST-1982; @CL-EST-2135].

Unless the bidder specifies otherwise in the bid, the Government reserves the right to make an award on any item for a quantity less than the quantity offered, at the unit prices offered [@CL-EST-2089].

A written award or acceptance of a bid, mailed or otherwise furnished to the successful bidder within the time for acceptance specified in the bid, results in a binding contract without further action by either party [@CL-EST-2090].

## Company calibration
