## Purpose

Shows how fixed fee is paid and withheld under the construction fee clause, and what the task-order and delivery-order ombudsman does under its clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1688; @CL-EST-1718] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-9 and FAR 52.216-32 [@CL-EST-1688; @CL-EST-1718] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-9 and RFO FAR 52.216-32 [@CL-EST-0265; @CL-EST-0266; @CL-EST-0267; @CL-EST-0268; @CL-EST-0269; @CL-EST-0270; @CL-EST-0271; @CL-EST-0272] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1688; @CL-EST-1718] |
| Applies to | Cost-plus-fixed-fee construction contracts for the fee clause, and contracts that include the ombudsman clause [@CL-EST-1688; @CL-EST-1718] |

The fee clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when a cost-plus-fixed-fee construction contract is contemplated [@CL-EST-1688]. Payment of the fixed fee is made in installments based upon the percentage of completion of the work, as determined from estimates submitted to and approved by the contracting officer, but subject to the withholding provisions [@CL-EST-1689].

The contracting officer shall withhold a reserve not to exceed 15 percent of the total fixed fee or $100,000, whichever is less, to protect the Government's interest [@CL-EST-1690].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall release 75 percent of all fee withholds after receipt of an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal covering the year of physical completion [@CL-EST-1691]. The proviso to that release is that the contractor has satisfied all other contract terms and conditions, including the submission of the final patent and royalty reports, and is not delinquent in submitting final vouchers on prior years' settlements [@CL-EST-1716]. The contracting officer may release up to 90 percent of the fee withholds based on the contractor's past performance related to the submission and settlement of final indirect cost rate proposals [@CL-EST-1717].

The ombudsman clause is a separate clause on a separate subject, used as prescribed in the FAR [@CL-EST-1718]. Its ombudsman must review the contractor's complaints about all task-order and delivery-order actions for the contract and ensure the contractor is afforded a fair opportunity for consideration in the award of orders, consistent with the procedures in the contract [@CL-EST-1692]. Consulting an ombudsman does not alter or postpone the timeline for any other process, such as protests [@CL-EST-1719]. The ordering rules for indefinite-delivery contracts are on a related card: [Order limitations and indefinite quantity clauses](est-order-limitations-and-indefinite-quantity-clauses.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026, because the DoD part 52 class deviation directs its use [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-9(c) keeps the reserve limit of 15 percent of the total fixed fee or $100,000, whichever is less [@CL-EST-0267; @CL-EST-1690]. It keeps the release of 75 percent of all fee withholds after receipt of an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal covering the year of physical completion [@CL-EST-0268; @CL-EST-1691], with the same proviso on other terms, patent and royalty reports and delinquent final vouchers [@CL-EST-0269; @CL-EST-1716], and the release of up to 90 percent based on past performance [@CL-EST-0270; @CL-EST-1717]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-9(b) says the fixed fee is paid in installments based upon the percentage of completion as estimated and approved, subject to the withholding provisions of paragraph (c) of this section [@CL-EST-0266]; the codified paragraph points to paragraph (c) below [@CL-EST-1689]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-32 keeps the ombudsman's duty to review complaints about all task-order and delivery-order actions [@CL-EST-0271; @CL-EST-1692] and the rule that consulting an ombudsman does not alter or postpone the timeline for any other process [@CL-EST-0272; @CL-EST-1719]. One difference is in the prescription line: the RFO text of FAR 52.216-9 says the clause is inserted as prescribed in 16.305(c) and does not repeat the words saying it goes in solicitations and contracts when a cost-plus-fixed-fee construction contract is contemplated [@CL-EST-0265], whereas the codified line cites 16.307(c) and states that condition [@CL-EST-1688].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A construction contract has a total fixed fee of 400,000. Fifteen percent of the fee is 60,000, and that is less than 100,000, the dollar figure in the clause.

So the reserve withheld cannot exceed 60,000. Fee installments follow the approved percentage of completion, subject to that withholding.

## Never confuse

Withholding the reserve is not the same as releasing it. Release of a stated share of the withholds follows receipt of an adequate certified final indirect cost rate proposal, provided the contractor has met the other terms and conditions [@CL-EST-1691; @CL-EST-1716].

Consulting the ombudsman is not a substitute for another process. It does not alter or postpone the timeline for any other process, such as protests [@CL-EST-1719].
