## Purpose

Shows when an offeror must ask for a debriefing after award, what the debriefing must contain at a minimum and what it may not reveal.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1417] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.506 [@CL-EST-1417] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.301-1 [@CL-EST-3331; @CL-EST-3332; @CL-EST-3333; @CL-EST-3334; @CL-EST-3335; @CL-EST-3336; @CL-EST-3337; @CL-EST-3338; @CL-EST-3339; @CL-EST-3340; @CL-EST-3341; @CL-EST-3342; @CL-EST-3343]; RFO FAR 15.301 [@CL-EST-2248]; RFO FAR 15.301-2 [@CL-EST-2249] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1417] |
| Applies to | Offerors that make a written request within the deadline after notification of award [@CL-EST-1417] |

An offeror whose written request is received by the agency within 3 days after it received notification of contract award shall be debriefed and furnished the basis for the selection decision and contract award [@CL-EST-1417]. To the maximum extent practicable, the debriefing should occur within 5 days after receipt of the written request [@CL-EST-1418].

An offeror that was notified of exclusion from the competition before award but failed to submit a timely request is not entitled to a debriefing, although untimely debriefing requests may be accommodated [@CL-EST-1450].

## How it works

The debriefing information shall include at a minimum, among its listed items, the Government's evaluation of significant weaknesses or deficiencies in the offeror's proposal, if applicable [@CL-EST-1419], and the overall evaluated cost or price (including unit prices) and technical rating, if applicable, of the successful offeror and the debriefed offeror, and past performance information on the debriefed offeror [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1420]. The overall ranking of all offerors is included when the agency developed one, together with a summary of the rationale for award [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1421], and for commercial products the make and model of the successful offeror's product is included [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1422]. The last listed item is reasonable responses to relevant questions about whether source selection procedures contained in the solicitation, applicable regulations, and other applicable authorities were followed [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1451]. The debriefing shall not include point-by-point comparisons of the debriefed offeror's proposal with those of other offerors [@CL-EST-1423], and it shall not reveal information barred from disclosure or exempt from release, including trade secrets [@CL-EST-1424], privileged or confidential manufacturing processes and techniques [@CL-EST-1424; @CL-EST-1452], privileged or confidential commercial and financial information such as cost breakdowns, or the names of individuals providing reference information about an offeror's past performance [@CL-EST-1424; @CL-EST-1425].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(a)(1) says offerors may request a postaward debriefing by submitting a written request to the contracting officer within 3 days after receipt of the award notice in accordance with 15.207-2, and that such offerors must be debriefed and furnished the basis for the selection decision and contract award [@CL-EST-3331], where the codified paragraph speaks of a written request received by the agency within 3 days after the offeror received notification of contract award [@CL-EST-1417]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(a)(2) says that, to the maximum extent practicable, the debriefing should occur within 5 days after receipt of the written request [@CL-EST-3332], as the codified paragraph says [@CL-EST-1418]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(a)(3)-(4) says untimely debriefing requests may be accommodated, and that an offeror notified of its exclusion from the competitive range is not entitled to a postaward debriefing if it failed to submit a timely preaward debriefing request, however such a request may be accommodated [@CL-EST-3333], where the codified paragraph speaks of an offeror notified of exclusion from the competition that failed to submit a timely request [@CL-EST-1450]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(c)(1)(i) says the debriefing information must include at a minimum the Government's evaluation of the significant weaknesses or deficiencies in the offeror's proposal, if applicable [@CL-EST-3334], as the codified paragraph says [@CL-EST-1419]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(c)(1)(ii) lists the overall evaluated cost or price and technical rating of the successful offeror and the debriefed offeror, and past performance information on the debriefed offeror [@CL-EST-3335], where the codified item reads overall evaluated cost or price (including unit prices) and technical rating, if applicable [@CL-EST-1420]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(c)(1)(iii)-(vi) lists the overall ranking of all offerors when any ranking was developed, a summary of the rationale for award, the make and model of the product for acquisitions of commercial products, and reasonable responses to relevant questions about whether source selection procedures in the request for proposals, applicable regulations, and other applicable authorities were followed [@CL-EST-3336; @CL-EST-3337], as the codified paragraphs list [@CL-EST-1421; @CL-EST-1422; @CL-EST-1451]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(c)(1)(vii) lists, for DoD contracts in excess of $10 million but not in excess of $100 million with a small business or nontraditional defense contractor, an option for the contractor to request disclosure of the agency's written source selection decision document [@CL-EST-3338], redacted to protect the confidential and proprietary information of other offerors for the contract award [@CL-EST-3339]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(c)(1)(viii) lists, for award of a DoD contract in excess of $100 million, disclosure of the agency's written source selection decision document, redacted to protect the information of other offerors for the contract award, in accordance with paragraph (d) of that subsection [@CL-EST-3340]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(d) says the debriefing must not include point-by-point comparisons of the debriefed offeror's proposal with those of other offerors [@CL-EST-3341], as the codified paragraph says [@CL-EST-1423]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(d) also says the debriefing must not reveal information prohibited from disclosure by part 24 or exempt from release under the Freedom of Information Act, including trade secrets and privileged or confidential manufacturing processes and techniques [@CL-EST-3342], where the codified paragraph cites 24.202 [@CL-EST-1424; @CL-EST-1452]. RFO FAR 15.301-1(d)(3)-(4) lists privileged or confidential commercial and financial information, including cost breakdowns, profit, indirect cost rates, and similar information, and the names of individuals providing reference information about an offeror's past performance, as information the debriefing must not reveal [@CL-EST-3343], as the codified paragraph lists [@CL-EST-1425]. RFO FAR 15.301 says that day, as used in that section, has the meaning set forth at part 33 [@CL-EST-2248]. RFO FAR 15.301-2 says that, if time permits, contracting officers may provide successful and unsuccessful offerors an opportunity to submit follow up questions after the postaward debriefing [@CL-EST-2249].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror is told on day 1 that it lost. It sends a written request on day 2. The debriefing sets out the weaknesses in its proposal, the winner's evaluated price and rating, the overall ranking and the rationale for award. It does not compare the two proposals line by line and does not show the winner's cost breakdown. The answer: the request was timely and the content follows the minimum list.

## Never confuse

The overall evaluated price of the successful offeror is part of the minimum content [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1420], but privileged or confidential cost breakdowns, profit and indirect cost rates are not to be revealed [@CL-EST-1424; @CL-EST-1425]. The overall ranking of all offerors is included when one exists [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1421], but a point-by-point comparison of proposals is not [@CL-EST-1423]. The codified list of minimum items runs from the evaluation of weaknesses or deficiencies to reasonable responses on source selection procedures [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1451], while the RFO list adds, for DoD contracts in excess of $10 million but not in excess of $100 million with a small business or nontraditional defense contractor, an option for the contractor to request disclosure of the agency's written source selection decision document [@CL-EST-3338], redacted to protect the confidential and proprietary information of other offerors for the contract award [@CL-EST-3339], and, for award of a DoD contract in excess of $100 million, disclosure of that document, redacted to protect the information of other offerors for the contract award, in accordance with paragraph (d) of that subsection [@CL-EST-3340]. The codified request clock runs from a written request received by the agency [@CL-EST-1417], while the RFO wording speaks of submitting a written request to the contracting officer [@CL-EST-3331]. The preaward rules are on [Preaward debriefing of offerors](est-preaward-debriefing.md), and the notice that starts the request clock is on [Notices to unsuccessful offerors](est-notices-to-unsuccessful-offerors.md).
