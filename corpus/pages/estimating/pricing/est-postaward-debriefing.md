## Purpose

Shows when an offeror must ask for a debriefing after award, what the debriefing must contain at a minimum and what it may not reveal.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1417] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.506 [@CL-EST-1417] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1417] |
| Applies to | Offerors that make a written request within the deadline after notification of award [@CL-EST-1417] |

An offeror whose written request is received by the agency within 3 days after it received notification of contract award shall be debriefed and furnished the basis for the selection decision and contract award [@CL-EST-1417]. To the maximum extent practicable, the debriefing should occur within 5 days after receipt of the written request [@CL-EST-1418].

An offeror that was notified of exclusion from the competition before award but failed to submit a timely request is not entitled to a debriefing, although untimely debriefing requests may be accommodated [@CL-EST-1450].

## How it works

The debriefing information shall include at a minimum, among its listed items, the Government's evaluation of significant weaknesses or deficiencies in the offeror's proposal, if applicable [@CL-EST-1419], and the overall evaluated cost or price (including unit prices) and technical rating, if applicable, of the successful offeror and the debriefed offeror, and past performance information on the debriefed offeror [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1420]. The overall ranking of all offerors is included when the agency developed one, together with a summary of the rationale for award [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1421], and for commercial products the make and model of the successful offeror's product is included [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1422]. The last listed item is reasonable responses to relevant questions about whether source selection procedures contained in the solicitation, applicable regulations, and other applicable authorities were followed [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1451]. The debriefing shall not include point-by-point comparisons of proposals [@CL-EST-1423], and it shall not reveal information barred from disclosure or exempt from release, including trade secrets [@CL-EST-1424], privileged or confidential manufacturing processes and techniques [@CL-EST-1424; @CL-EST-1452], privileged or confidential commercial and financial information such as cost breakdowns, or the names of past performance references [@CL-EST-1424; @CL-EST-1425].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror is told on day 1 that it lost. It sends a written request on day 2. The debriefing sets out the weaknesses in its proposal, the winner's evaluated price and rating, the overall ranking and the rationale for award. It does not compare the two proposals line by line and does not show the winner's cost breakdown. The answer: the request was timely and the content follows the minimum list.

## Never confuse

The overall evaluated price of the successful offeror is part of the minimum content [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1420], but privileged or confidential cost breakdowns, profit and indirect cost rates are not to be revealed [@CL-EST-1424; @CL-EST-1425]. The overall ranking of all offerors is included when one exists [@CL-EST-1419; @CL-EST-1421], but a point-by-point comparison of proposals is not [@CL-EST-1423]. The preaward rules are on [Preaward debriefing of offerors](est-preaward-debriefing.md), and the notice that starts the request clock is on [Notices to unsuccessful offerors](est-notices-to-unsuccessful-offerors.md).
