## Purpose

Shows how DoD orders the evaluation of offers by small business tier, when the contracting officer may issue a tiered solicitation, and what a past performance evaluation must take in when the small business clauses are in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2434; @CL-EST-2436; @CL-EST-2439].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-2434] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.203-70 [@CL-EST-2434]; DFARS 215.305 [@CL-EST-2439]; FAR 15.101-3 [@CL-EST-2050] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.105-3 [@CL-EST-2241] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2434] |
| Applies to | Solicitations with a tiered evaluation of offers, and solicitations in which FAR 15.304 requires a past performance evaluation and the small business clauses are included [@CL-EST-2436; @CL-EST-2439; @CL-EST-2440] |

The tiered or cascading order of precedence used for tiered evaluation of offers must be consistent with FAR part 19 [@CL-EST-2434]. Consideration must be given to the tiers of small businesses, for example 8(a), HUBZone small business, service-disabled veteran-owned small business and small business, before offers from other than small business concerns are evaluated [@CL-EST-2435]. Under FAR 15.101-3, an agency shall not create a tiered (or "cascading") evaluation of offers, as described in 13 CFR 125.2, for multiple-award contracts unless an agency has statutory authority [@CL-EST-2050].

The contracting officer is prohibited from issuing a solicitation with a tiered evaluation of offers unless the contracting officer conducts market research, in accordance with FAR Part 10 and Part 210, to determine whether the criteria in FAR part 19 are met for setting aside the acquisition for small business [@CL-EST-2436]. The alternative, for a task or delivery order, is to determine whether there are a sufficient number of qualified small business concerns available to justify limiting competition under the terms of the contract [@CL-EST-2437]. The contracting officer must also, if the contracting officer cannot determine whether the criteria in paragraph (c)(1) of DFARS 215.203-70 are met, include a written explanation in the contract file as to why such a determination could not be made [@CL-EST-9749; @CL-EST-2438].

When a past performance evaluation is required by FAR 15.304 and the solicitation includes the clause at FAR 52.219-8, Utilization of Small Business Concerns, the evaluation factors must include the past performance of offerors in complying with requirements of that clause [@CL-EST-2439]. The same applies where the solicitation includes the clause at FAR 52.219-9, Small Business Subcontracting Plan [@CL-EST-2440]. Contracting officers must consider an offeror's failure to make a good faith effort to comply with its comprehensive subcontracting plan under the Test Program described at 219.702-70 as part of the evaluation of past performance [@CL-EST-2441]. When evaluating the past performance of an offeror that is a small business concern in response to a competitive solicitation, contracting officers must consider relevant past performance information provided for affiliates of the offeror [@CL-EST-2442].

## How it works

The tiered or cascading order of precedence used for tiered evaluation of offers must be consistent with FAR part 19, and consideration must be given to the tiers of small businesses before evaluating offers from other than small business concerns [@CL-EST-2434; @CL-EST-2435]. The contracting officer is prohibited from issuing a solicitation with a tiered evaluation of offers unless the contracting officer conducts market research to determine whether the FAR part 19 criteria for setting aside the acquisition for small business are met or, for a task or delivery order, whether there are a sufficient number of qualified small business concerns available to justify limiting competition under the terms of the contract [@CL-EST-2436; @CL-EST-2437], and, if the contracting officer cannot determine whether the criteria in paragraph (c)(1) of DFARS 215.203-70 are met, the contracting officer includes a written explanation in the contract file as to why such a determination could not be made [@CL-EST-9749; @CL-EST-2438].

When a past performance evaluation is required by FAR 15.304 and the solicitation includes the clause at FAR 52.219-8 or the clause at FAR 52.219-9, the evaluation factors must include the past performance of offerors in complying with requirements of that clause [@CL-EST-2439; @CL-EST-2440]. Contracting officers must consider an offeror's failure to make a good faith effort to comply with its comprehensive subcontracting plan under the Test Program described at 219.702-70 as part of the evaluation of the past performance [@CL-EST-2441]. For a small business offeror responding to a competitive solicitation, the contracting officer must consider relevant past performance information provided for its affiliates [@CL-EST-2442].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. Under RFO FAR 15.105-3, when soliciting for a multiple award contract, agencies must not create a tiered or cascading evaluation of small business offers, as described in 13 CFR 125.2, unless the agency has statutory authority to do so [@CL-EST-2241].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer plans a solicitation with a tiered evaluation of offers. Before issuing it, the contracting officer conducts market research under FAR Part 10 and Part 210 to determine whether the FAR part 19 criteria for a small business set-aside are met [@CL-EST-2436]. The research cannot settle the question, so the contracting officer places a written explanation of why in the contract file before issuing the solicitation [@CL-EST-2438]. When the solicitation is issued, the small business tiers are considered before offers from other than small business concerns are evaluated [@CL-EST-2435].

The solicitation requires a past performance evaluation and includes the clause at FAR 52.219-8, so the evaluation factors include each offeror's past performance in complying with that clause [@CL-EST-2439]. A small business offeror with a short record of its own responds to this competitive solicitation with relevant past performance information for an affiliate, and the contracting officer considers it [@CL-EST-2442].

## Never confuse

The market research is to determine whether the criteria in FAR part 19 are met for setting aside the acquisition for small business or, for a task or delivery order, whether there are a sufficient number of qualified small business concerns available to justify limiting competition under the terms of the contract [@CL-EST-2436; @CL-EST-2437]. The tiered or cascading order of precedence used for tiered evaluation of offers shall be consistent with FAR part 19 [@CL-EST-2434].

## Company calibration

That the company can produce the tier order and its own and affiliate past performance records before a DoD competitive solicitation. [@Q-012]
