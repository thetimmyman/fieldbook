## Purpose

Shows when the Government is entitled to a price reduction after award because certified cost or pricing data were defective, and what the contractor can set against that reduction.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0101] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.407-1(b) [@CL-EST-0102] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.304(a) [@CL-EST-3231; @CL-EST-3233]; RFO FAR 52.215-10(a), (b), (c) and (d) [@CL-EST-9241; @CL-EST-9242; @CL-EST-9243; @CL-EST-9244; @CL-EST-9245; @CL-EST-9246; @CL-EST-9247; @CL-EST-9248; @CL-EST-9249; @CL-EST-9250; @CL-EST-9251; @CL-EST-9252; @CL-EST-9259; @CL-EST-9260; @CL-EST-9261; @CL-EST-9262; @CL-EST-9263; @CL-EST-9264; @CL-EST-9265; @CL-EST-9266] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0102] |
| Applies to | Contracts that include one of the prescribed price reduction clauses [@CL-EST-0102] |

Under the codified text, if, before agreement on price, the contracting officer learns that any certified cost or pricing data submitted are inaccurate, incomplete, or noncurrent [@CL-EST-9621], the contracting officer shall immediately bring the matter to the attention of the prospective contractor, whether the defective data increase or decrease the contract price [@CL-EST-0101]. If, after award, certified cost or pricing data are found to be inaccurate, incomplete, or noncurrent as of the date of final agreement on price or an earlier date agreed upon by the parties given on the contractor's or subcontractor's Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data, the Government is entitled to a price adjustment, including profit or fee, of any significant amount by which the price was increased because of the defective data [@CL-EST-9478; @CL-EST-0102].

## How it works

The entitlement is ensured by including in the contract one of the clauses prescribed in FAR 15.408(b) and (c), and is set forth in the clauses at FAR 52.215-10 and 52.215-11 [@CL-EST-9477]. The clauses recognize that the Government's right to a price adjustment is not affected by the contractor or subcontractor having been a sole source supplier or otherwise in a superior bargaining position, among the listed circumstances [@CL-EST-9476; @CL-EST-0104]. Subject to paragraphs (b)(5) and (6) of FAR 15.407-1 [@CL-EST-9479], the contracting officer shall allow an offset for any understated certified cost or pricing data submitted in support of price negotiations, up to the amount of the Government's claim for overstated pricing data [@CL-EST-0103] arising out of the same pricing action [@CL-EST-9480]. Overpayment arises only from payment for accepted supplies or services, not from amounts paid as contract financing [@CL-EST-0105]. Before taking any contractual actions concerning penalties, the contracting officer shall obtain the advice of counsel [@CL-EST-0106]. For each advisory audit received based on a postaward review that indicates defective pricing [@CL-EST-9622], the contracting officer shall make a determination as to whether or not the data submitted were defective and relied upon [@CL-EST-0108]. When a prime contractor includes defective subcontract data in arriving at the price but later awards the subcontract to a lower priced subcontractor (or does not subcontract for the work) [@CL-EST-9623], any adjustment in the prime contract price due to defective subcontract data is limited to the difference, plus applicable indirect cost and profit markups, between the subcontract price used for pricing the prime contract [@CL-EST-0109] and either the actual subcontract price or the actual cost to the contractor, if not subcontracted, provided the data on which the actual subcontract price is based are not themselves defective [@CL-EST-0245].

The price reduction clause applies if any price, including profit or fee, negotiated in connection with the contract, or any cost reimbursable under it, was increased by any significant amount because of one of three alternative grounds [@CL-EST-9624; @CL-EST-9629]. The first ground is that the Contractor or a subcontractor furnished certified cost or pricing data that were not complete, accurate and current as certified in its Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-0176]. The second is that a subcontractor or prospective subcontractor furnished the contractor such data [@CL-EST-0252]. The third is that any of these parties furnished data of any description that were not accurate [@CL-EST-9625]. Where the clause applies, the price or cost shall be reduced accordingly and the contract shall be modified to reflect the reduction [@CL-EST-0177]. A reduction for defective data from a prospective subcontractor that did not receive the subcontract is limited to the amount, plus applicable overhead and profit markup, by which the actual subcontract, or the contractor's actual cost where there was no subcontract, was less than the prospective estimate, provided the actual subcontract price was not itself affected by defective data [@CL-EST-0178; @CL-EST-0253; @CL-EST-0254]. If the Contracting Officer determines under paragraph (a) that a price or cost reduction should be made, the Contractor agrees not to raise the following matters as a defense [@CL-EST-9626; @CL-EST-0179]: that the Contractor or subcontractor was a sole source supplier or otherwise in a superior bargaining position and thus the price of the contract would not have been modified even if accurate, complete and current certified cost or pricing data had been submitted [@CL-EST-0255]; that the Contracting Officer should have known that the certified cost or pricing data in issue were defective even though the Contractor or subcontractor took no affirmative action to bring the character of the data to the attention of the Contracting Officer [@CL-EST-0256]; that the contract was based on an agreement about the total cost of the contract and there was no agreement about the cost of each item procured; and that the Contractor or subcontractor did not submit a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-0257]. If any reduction in the contract price under the clause reduces the price of items for which payment was made prior to the date of the modification reflecting the price reduction, the Contractor shall be liable to and shall pay the United States at the time the overpayment is repaid [@CL-EST-9627]: interest compounded daily on the amount of the overpayment [@CL-EST-0181], computed from the date or dates of overpayment to the date the Government is repaid at the applicable underpayment rate effective for each quarter prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury under 26 U.S.C. 6621(a)(2) [@CL-EST-9628]; and a penalty equal to the amount of the overpayment if the Contractor or subcontractor knowingly submitted certified cost or pricing data that were incomplete, inaccurate or noncurrent [@CL-EST-0180].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. The after-award rule is in RFO FAR 15.304(a), which says that if, after award, certified cost or pricing data are found to be inaccurate, incomplete, or noncurrent [@CL-EST-3231] as of the date of final agreement on price or an earlier date agreed upon by the parties given on the contractor's or subcontractor's Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-3232; @CL-EST-9478], the Government is entitled to a price adjustment, including profit or fee, of any significant amount by which the price was increased because of the defective data [@CL-EST-3233; @CL-EST-0102]. The entitlement is ensured by including in the contract one of the clauses prescribed in 15.110(j) and (k), where the codified text points to 15.408(b) and (c) [@CL-EST-3234; @CL-EST-9477], and the clauses give the Government the right to a price adjustment for defects in data submitted by the contractor, a prospective subcontractor, or an actual subcontractor [@CL-EST-3235]. The Government's right is still not affected by the contractor or subcontractor having been a sole source supplier or otherwise in a superior bargaining position [@CL-EST-3236; @CL-EST-9476]. Subject to paragraphs (a)(5) and (6), contracting officers must allow an offset for any understated certified cost or pricing data submitted in support of price negotiations, up to the amount of the Government's claim for overstated pricing data [@CL-EST-3237; @CL-EST-9479] arising out of the same pricing action [@CL-EST-3238; @CL-EST-9480]. An offset must be allowed only in an amount supported by the facts and if the contractor certifies to the contracting officer that, to the best of the contractor's knowledge and belief, the contractor is entitled to the offset in the amount requested [@CL-EST-3257], and proves that the certified cost or pricing data were available before the "as of" date specified on the Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data but were not submitted [@CL-EST-3258]. An offset must not be allowed if the understated data were known by the contractor to be understated before that "as of" date [@CL-EST-3259], or if the Government proves that the facts demonstrate that the price would not have increased in the amount to be offset even if the available data had been submitted before that date [@CL-EST-3260]. Overpayment still occurs only when payment is made for supplies or services accepted by the Government, and amounts paid as contract financing do not create an overpayment [@CL-EST-3239; @CL-EST-0105]. In arriving at the amount due for penalties on contracts where the submission of defective data was a knowing submission, contracting officers must obtain an amount equal to the amount of overpayment made [@CL-EST-3240; @CL-EST-0106], and before taking contractual actions concerning penalties the contracting officer must obtain the advice of counsel [@CL-EST-0106]. RFO FAR 15.304(b) says contracting officers must not reprice the contract solely because the profit was greater than forecast or because a contingency specified in the submission failed to materialize [@CL-EST-3241; @CL-EST-0107]. The duty before agreement on price is in RFO FAR 15.405-1(a): if the contracting officer learns that any certified cost or pricing data submitted are inaccurate, incomplete, or noncurrent [@CL-EST-3242; @CL-EST-0101], the contracting officer must immediately bring the matter to the attention of the prospective contractor, whether the defective data increase or decrease the contract price [@CL-EST-3243; @CL-EST-0101].

The RFO text of the price reduction clause at 52.215-10 carries the same rules in its part 52 text, which DoD contracting officers must use from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. Under RFO paragraph (a), the rule applies if any price, including profit or fee, negotiated in connection with the contract, or any cost reimbursable under it, was increased by any significant amount because of the grounds listed [@CL-EST-9241]. One ground is that the Contractor or a subcontractor furnished certified cost or pricing data that were not complete, accurate, and current as certified in its Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-9242; @CL-EST-0176]. Another is that a subcontractor or prospective subcontractor furnished the Contractor such data [@CL-EST-9243; @CL-EST-0252]. A third is that any of these parties furnished data of any description that were not accurate, in which case the price or cost shall be reduced accordingly and the contract shall be modified to reflect the reduction [@CL-EST-9244; @CL-EST-0177]. If the Contracting Officer determines under paragraph (a) that a reduction should be made, the Contractor agrees not to raise the listed matters as a defense [@CL-EST-9245; @CL-EST-0179]. They are that the Contractor or subcontractor was a sole source supplier or otherwise in a superior bargaining position, and thus the price would not have been modified even if accurate, complete, and current certified cost or pricing data had been submitted [@CL-EST-9246; @CL-EST-0255], that the Contracting Officer should have known the data in issue were defective even though the Contractor or subcontractor took no affirmative action to bring the character of the data to the Contracting Officer's attention [@CL-EST-9247; @CL-EST-0256], that the contract was based on an agreement about total cost with none about the cost of each item procured, and that no Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data was submitted [@CL-EST-9248; @CL-EST-0257]. Where a reduction lowers the price of items paid for before the date of the modification reflecting the reduction, the Contractor shall be liable to and shall pay the United States, at the time the overpayment is repaid, the amounts in paragraph (d) [@CL-EST-9249]. Those are interest compounded daily on the overpayment, computed from the date or dates of overpayment to the date the Government is repaid [@CL-EST-9250; @CL-EST-0181], at the applicable underpayment rate for each quarter that the Secretary of the Treasury prescribes under section 6621(a)(2) of title 26 of the United States Code [@CL-EST-9251], and a penalty equal to the amount of the overpayment if the Contractor or subcontractor knowingly submitted certified cost or pricing data that were incomplete, inaccurate, or noncurrent [@CL-EST-9252; @CL-EST-0180]. Under RFO paragraph (b), any reduction in the contract price under paragraph (a) due to defective data from a prospective subcontractor that was not subsequently awarded the subcontract shall be limited to the amount, plus applicable overhead and profit markup [@CL-EST-9259; @CL-EST-0178], by which the actual subcontract, or the actual cost to the Contractor if there was no subcontract, was less than the prospective subcontract cost estimate submitted by the Contractor [@CL-EST-9260; @CL-EST-0253], provided that the actual subcontract price was not itself affected by defective certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-9261; @CL-EST-0254]. Under RFO paragraph (c)(2)(i), except as prohibited by paragraph (c)(2)(ii), an offset in an amount the Contracting Officer determines appropriate based upon the facts shall be allowed against the amount of a contract price reduction [@CL-EST-9262], if the Contractor certifies to the Contracting Officer that, to the best of the Contractor's knowledge and belief, the Contractor is entitled to the offset in the amount requested [@CL-EST-9263], and proves that the certified cost or pricing data were available before the "as of" date specified on its Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data and were not submitted before that date [@CL-EST-9264]. Under RFO paragraph (c)(2)(ii), an offset shall not be allowed if the understated data were known by the Contractor to be understated before that "as of" date [@CL-EST-9265], or if the Government proves that the facts demonstrate that the contract price would not have increased in the amount to be offset even if the available data had been submitted before that date [@CL-EST-9266].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prime contractor certified cost or pricing data for a price of 1,200,000 that included a subcontract quote of 400,000. Before the date of final agreement on price, the subcontractor had already offered 340,000, and the prime did not disclose the new offer. The data were not current, and they raised the price by 60,000 plus the indirect cost and profit applied to it, so the Government is entitled to a price reduction of that amount.

## Never confuse

Defective pricing is an adjustment measured by the amount the price was increased because certified data were inaccurate, incomplete or noncurrent [@CL-EST-9478; @CL-EST-0102]. The contracting officer shall not reprice the contract solely because the profit was greater than forecast or because a contingency specified in the submission failed to materialize [@CL-EST-0107]. A citation to FAR 15.304 means the evaluation factors rule in the codified text [@CL-EST-1294] but the after-award defective data entitlement in the RFO text [@CL-EST-3231].

## Company calibration

That estimate versions, source quotations and assumptions are frozen and compared with actuals before certified data are signed. [@Q-023]
