## Purpose

Shows what the DoD clause for contract modifications requires from the Canadian Commercial Corporation in place of certified cost or pricing data.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1725] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.215-7004 [@CL-EST-1725; @CL-EST-1774] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1725] |
| Applies to | Contracts that use this clause as prescribed at DFARS 215.408(2)(ii) [@CL-EST-1774], and the clause applies only if award is to the Canadian Commercial Corporation [@CL-EST-1725] |

The clause is the one to use as prescribed at DFARS 215.408(2)(ii) [@CL-EST-1774]. It is used in lieu of FAR 52.215-21, and it applies only if award is to the Canadian Commercial Corporation [@CL-EST-1725]. Under the clause, submission of certified cost or pricing data is not required [@CL-EST-1726]. The Canadian Commercial Corporation shall obtain and provide the listed items for modifications that exceed $150,000, or a higher dollar value specified by the United States Contracting Officer in the solicitation [@CL-EST-1727].

The clause lists three items. The first is the profit rate or fee, as applicable [@CL-EST-1727; @CL-EST-1728]. The second is the analysis that Public Works and Government Services Canada provided to the Canadian Commercial Corporation to determine a fair and reasonable price, comparable to the analysis required at FAR 15.404-1 [@CL-EST-1727; @CL-EST-1729]. The third is data other than certified cost or pricing data that is necessary to permit the United States Contracting Officer to determine that the proposed price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-1727; @CL-EST-1730]. The clause text also carries a bracketed instruction for the United States Contracting Officer to insert a description of the data required in accordance with FAR 15.403-3(a)(1) [@CL-EST-1786].

## How it works

The clause takes the place of FAR 52.215-21 where it applies, and under it certified cost or pricing data is not required [@CL-EST-1725; @CL-EST-1726]. The Canadian Commercial Corporation instead supplies the three listed items for modifications that exceed the dollar value stated in the clause or the higher value set in the solicitation [@CL-EST-1727; @CL-EST-1728; @CL-EST-1729; @CL-EST-1730]. The description of the data for the third item is left to the United States Contracting Officer to insert [@CL-EST-1786]. Beyond the three listed items and that insert, the printed clause states no form or format for the data, so the source does not establish one. The printed clause also has no flowdown paragraph, so the source does not establish a flowdown to subcontracts. The matching provision for the Canadian Commercial Corporation is covered on [Supplemental cost principles and data other than certified data from the Canadian Commercial Corporation](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-dfars-supplemental-cost-principles-and-ccc-data.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract was awarded to the Canadian Commercial Corporation with this clause, and the solicitation set no higher dollar value. A modification is priced at 200,000 dollars, which is over the clause value. The corporation provides its profit rate or fee, the analysis from the Canadian government office, and the other data the contracting officer needs to find the price fair and reasonable. It does not provide certified cost or pricing data.

## Never confuse

Data other than certified cost or pricing data is not certified data, and this clause says certified data is not required [@CL-EST-1726]. The only-one-offer provision also has a Canadian Commercial Corporation route, but it works from a notice by the Contracting Officer; see [Only one offer received](est-only-one-offer.md). A different DFARS clause asks for a price breakdown with any proposal for a contract modification; see [Modification price breakdown and bidding schedule pricing](est-dfars-modification-price-breakdown-and-bidding-schedules.md). The format rules for data other than certified data are on [Data submission format and instructions](est-data-submission-format-and-instructions.md).

## Company calibration

Whether any expected award or modification will run through the Canadian Commercial Corporation and so may use this clause instead of certified data. [@Q-047]
