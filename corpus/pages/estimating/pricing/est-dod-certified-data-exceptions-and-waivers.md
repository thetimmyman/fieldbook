## Purpose

Shows which DoD actions do not need certified cost or pricing data, who can waive the requirement, and what the contracting officer must still require.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1272] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.403-1 [@CL-EST-1272]; DFARS 215.403-3 [@CL-EST-1280] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1272] |
| Applies to | DoD acquisitions covered by the DFARS rules on certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-1272] |

Submission of certified cost or pricing data shall not be required in the case of a contract, subcontract, or modification of a contract or subcontract to the extent such data relates to an indirect offset [@CL-EST-1272]. DoD has waived the requirement for submission of certified cost or pricing data for the Canadian Commercial Corporation and its subcontractors (but see 215.408(3) and 225.870-4(c)) [@CL-EST-1279].

DoD has waived certified cost or pricing data requirements for nonprofit organizations, including education institutions, on cost-reimbursement-no-fee contracts [@CL-EST-1276]. Contracting officers shall not determine the price of a contract or subcontract to be fair and reasonable based solely on historical prices paid by the Government [@CL-EST-1280].

## How it works

The head of the contracting activity may, without power of delegation, apply the exceptional circumstances authority when a determination is made that all three listed conditions are met [@CL-EST-1273; @CL-EST-1275]. One listed condition is that the property or services cannot reasonably be obtained under the contract, subcontract, or modification, without the granting of the waiver [@CL-EST-1274], and the two further listed conditions are that the price can be determined to be fair and reasonable without certified data and that there are demonstrated benefits to granting the waiver [@CL-EST-1275]. For nonprofit organizations, including education institutions, on cost-reimbursement-no-fee contracts, the contracting officer shall require data other than certified cost or pricing data to the extent necessary to determine reasonableness and cost realism [@CL-EST-1277]. A second listed requirement is certified data from subcontractors that are not nonprofit organizations when the subcontractor's proposal exceeds the certified cost or pricing data threshold at FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-1277; @CL-EST-1278]. Under the codified FAR, unless an exception applies, certified cost or pricing data are required before the listed actions that are expected to exceed the current threshold or, in the case of existing contracts, the threshold specified in the contract [@CL-EST-9458], and that threshold is $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018 [@CL-EST-0001]. Under Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Rev 1, certified cost or pricing data are to be obtained when a prime contract is expected to exceed $10 million and is awarded after June 30, 2026, unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-0002; @CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457]. The deviation sets the threshold at $2.5 million when the prime contract is awarded on or before June 30, 2026, unless otherwise stated in the contract [@CL-EST-9457]. The threshold and the general FAR exceptions are on [Certified cost or pricing data threshold](est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md) and [Exceptions to the requirement for certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-data-exceptions.md). For a single offer to a competitive solicitation, the DFARS standard for adequate price competition refers the reader to the one-offer section [@CL-EST-1321], covered on [DoD price determination when only one offer is received](est-dod-one-offer-price-determination.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A university proposes a cost-reimbursement-no-fee research contract of 3,000,000. Certified data are waived, so the contracting officer asks for other data to judge reasonableness and cost realism. The university plans a subcontract with a for-profit firm whose proposal exceeds the threshold, so certified data are required from that firm.

## Never confuse

Under the nonprofit waiver, the contracting officer shall require data other than certified cost or pricing data to the extent necessary [@CL-EST-1277], and shall not rely solely on historical prices paid by the Government [@CL-EST-1280]. The head of the contracting activity may, without power of delegation, apply the exceptional circumstances authority [@CL-EST-1273], while the nonprofit requirements fall on the contracting officer [@CL-EST-1277]. What the data is for in the negotiation file is on [Price negotiation and the price negotiation memorandum](est-price-negotiation-documentation.md), and the data the contracting officer asks for is on [Pricing policy and the order of preference for data](est-pricing-policy-data-preference.md).
