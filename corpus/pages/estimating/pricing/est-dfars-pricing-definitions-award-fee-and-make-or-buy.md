## Purpose

States the DoD definitions of market prices and relevant sales data, the requirements for developing a fee objective for a cost-plus-award-fee contract, and the minimum dollar amount stated in the make-or-buy program requirements.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-2769] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.401, DFARS 215.404-74 and DFARS 215.407-2 [@CL-EST-2769; @CL-EST-2771; @CL-EST-2775] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Applies to | Terms used in the DoD contract pricing subpart, the contracting officer's fee objective for cost-plus-award-fee contracts, and make-or-buy program requirements for items and work included [@CL-EST-2788; @CL-EST-2787; @CL-EST-2775] |

The terms below are defined as used in the DoD contract pricing subpart [@CL-EST-2788]. Market prices means current prices that are established in the course of ordinary trade between buyers and sellers free to bargain and that can be substantiated through competition or from sources independent of the offerors [@CL-EST-2769]. Relevant sales data means information provided by an offeror of sales of the same or similar items that can be used to establish price reasonableness taking into consideration the age, volume and nature of the transactions [@CL-EST-2770], including any related discounts, refunds, rebates, offsets or other adjustments [@CL-EST-2790].

In developing a fee objective for cost-plus-award-fee contracts, the contracting officer shall carry out the duties in the paragraphs of DFARS 215.404-74 [@CL-EST-2787]. The contracting officer shall follow the guidance in FAR 16.405-2 and 216.405-2 [@CL-EST-2771]. The contracting officer shall not use the weighted guidelines method or alternate structured approach [@CL-EST-2772]. The contracting officer shall apply the offset policy in 215.404-73(b)(2) for facilities capital cost of money, that is, reduce the base fee by the amount of facilities capital cost of money [@CL-EST-2773]. The contracting officer shall not complete a DD Form 1547 [@CL-EST-2774].

In the make-or-buy program requirements for items and work included, the minimum dollar amount is $1.5 million [@CL-EST-2775]. The section refers the reader to PGI 215.407-2 for guidance on factors to consider when deciding whether to request a make-or-buy plan and when evaluating make-or-buy plan submissions [@CL-EST-2789].

## How it works

The fee objective for a cost-plus-award-fee contract is developed without the weighted guidelines method or alternate structured approach and without completing a DD Form 1547 [@CL-EST-2772; @CL-EST-2774]. The contracting officer instead follows the guidance in FAR 16.405-2 and 216.405-2 and applies the facilities capital offset, which lowers the base fee by the amount of facilities capital cost of money [@CL-EST-2771; @CL-EST-2773]. All four duties fall on the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2787].

The make-or-buy paragraph gives one figure for items and work included [@CL-EST-2775]. The text printed in this section does not say what a plan must contain for an item above or below that amount, and this card does not establish it. The award-fee distribution and base fee limits are on [DoD cost-plus-award-fee contracts](est-dod-cost-plus-award-fee-requirements.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-plus-award-fee proposal carries a base fee of 100,000 and facilities capital cost of money of 8,000. The contracting officer applies the offset policy and reduces the base fee by the 8,000, to 92,000 [@CL-EST-2773]. The contracting officer follows the guidance in FAR 16.405-2 and 216.405-2 [@CL-EST-2771], does not use weighted guidelines or the alternate structured approach [@CL-EST-2772], and does not complete a DD Form 1547 for the fee objective [@CL-EST-2774].

A contractor submits sales data for the same item at a list price, with a volume rebate granted on the sale. Relevant sales data are information the offeror provides on sales of the same or similar items, considered with the age, volume and nature of the transactions, including any related rebates [@CL-EST-2770; @CL-EST-2790].

A make-or-buy plan lists one item valued at 2 million and another valued at 1 million. The stated minimum dollar amount for items and work included is $1.5 million, so the first item is above it and the second is below it [@CL-EST-2775]. This card does not establish what follows for the lower item.

## Never confuse

Market prices and relevant sales data are different evidence. Market prices are current prices established in ordinary trade between buyers and sellers free to bargain, substantiated through competition or from sources independent of the offerors. Relevant sales data are information an offeror provides on sales of the same or similar items [@CL-EST-2769; @CL-EST-2770].

The cost-plus-award-fee fee objective is not set with the weighted guidelines method. For that method elsewhere, see [DoD profit policy: structured approaches and when weighted guidelines are not used](est-dod-profit-structured-approaches.md) [@CL-EST-2772].
