## Purpose

Shows how the advisory multi-step process works and which provisions and clauses the contracting officer inserts, and when.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1392; @CL-EST-1394] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.202 [@CL-EST-1392]; FAR 15.209 [@CL-EST-1394] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.101(e) [@CL-EST-3178; @CL-EST-3179]; RFO FAR 15.110 [@CL-EST-3180] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1392] |
| Applies to | Contracting by negotiation, for the provisions and clauses [@CL-EST-1495] |

The agency may publish a presolicitation notice that invites potential offerors to submit information that allows the Government to advise them about their potential to be viable competitors [@CL-EST-1392]. The agency shall inform all respondents that, notwithstanding the advice the Government gave in response to their submissions, they may participate in the resultant acquisition [@CL-EST-1393].

## How it works

When contracting by negotiation, the contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.215-1 in all competitive solicitations where the Government intends to award a contract without discussions [@CL-EST-1394]. Where the Government intends award after discussions with offerors within the competitive range, the contracting officer shall use the basic provision with its Alternate I [@CL-EST-1394; @CL-EST-1395]. If the Government would be willing to accept alternate proposals, the contracting officer shall alter the basic clause to add a paragraph substantially the same as Alternate II [@CL-EST-1394; @CL-EST-1497]. Discussions are on [Exchanges with offerors after receipt of proposals](est-exchanges-after-proposals-and-competitive-range.md).

When contracting by negotiation, and except as provided in a cross-referenced paragraph, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.215-2, Audit and Records-Negotiation, in solicitations and contracts except those for listed acquisitions, one of which is an acquisition not exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1396; @CL-EST-1398]. For cost-reimbursement contracts with State and local Governments, educational institutions, and other nonprofit organizations, the contracting officer shall use the audit clause with its Alternate II [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1498]. The audit clause is explained on [Audit and Records, Negotiation clause](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-audit-and-records-negotiation.md).

When contracting by negotiation and issuing a solicitation for information or planning purposes, the contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.215-3 and clearly mark the face of the solicitation as for information or planning purposes [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1397]. The provision at 52.215-5, Facsimile Proposals, goes in solicitations if facsimile proposals are authorized [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1496]. The provision at 52.215-6, Place of Performance, goes in solicitations unless the place of performance is specified by the Government [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1399]. The clause at 52.215-8 goes in solicitations and contracts using the uniform contract format [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1382]. Other provisions and alternates in the section are not covered here.

Talks with industry before proposals, which include presolicitation notices, are on [Exchanges with industry before receipt of proposals](est-exchanges-before-proposals.md). What a request for proposals describes is on [Requests for proposals and amending the solicitation](est-rfp-and-solicitation-amendments.md), and when proposals are due is on [Submitting, modifying, withdrawing and late proposals](est-proposal-submission-and-late-proposals.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. The advisory multi-step process is in RFO FAR 15.101(e), where agencies may publish a notice that provides a general description of the scope or purpose of the acquisition and invites potential offerors to submit information that allows the Government to advise offerors about their potential to be viable competitors [@CL-EST-3178]; the codified paragraph says a presolicitation notice that invites potential offerors to submit information that allows the Government to advise them about their potential to be viable competitors [@CL-EST-1392]. RFO FAR 15.101(e)(2) says that when using this process agencies must take the listed steps [@CL-EST-9464], one of which is to inform all respondents that, notwithstanding the advice provided by the Government in response to their submissions, they may participate in the resultant acquisition [@CL-EST-3179], which matches the codified requirement that the agency inform all respondents of that [@CL-EST-1393]. The provisions and clauses are in RFO FAR 15.110 [@CL-EST-3180]. RFO FAR 15.110(a) says to insert the provision at 52.215-1 in all competitive RFPs where the Government intends to award a contract without negotiations, where the codified text says without discussions [@CL-EST-3180; @CL-EST-1394]. It says to use the provision with its Alternate I where the Government intends to make award after negotiating with offerors within the competitive range, where the codified text says after discussions [@CL-EST-3181; @CL-EST-1395]. It keeps the rule that, if the Government would be willing to accept alternate proposals, the clause is altered to add a paragraph substantially the same as Alternate II [@CL-EST-3182; @CL-EST-1497]. RFO FAR 15.110(b)(1) says to insert the audit clause at 52.215-2 in RFPs and contracts except those for listed acquisitions, one of which is an acquisition not exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, as the codified text does [@CL-EST-3183; @CL-EST-1398]; it opens with that instruction, while the codified paragraph opens with an exception for a cross-referenced paragraph [@CL-EST-3183; @CL-EST-1396], and the RFO paragraph (b)(2) is printed as reserved [@CL-EST-3184]. It keeps the rule that for cost-reimbursement contracts with State and local Governments, educational institutions, and other nonprofit organizations the clause is used with its Alternate II [@CL-EST-3185; @CL-EST-1498]. RFO FAR 15.110 prints paragraphs (c), (d) and (e) each as reserved [@CL-EST-3186], where the codified paragraphs (c) and (e) hold the instructions for the information-or-planning provision and the facsimile proposals provision [@CL-EST-1397; @CL-EST-1496]. It keeps the rule that the place of performance provision goes in RFPs unless the place of performance is specified by the Government [@CL-EST-3187; @CL-EST-1399]. Its instruction for the clause at 52.215-8 points to the format at 15.108, where the codified text points to the format at 15.204 [@CL-EST-3188; @CL-EST-1382]; the RFO section titled Uniform contract format is 15.109, which says RFPs using that format should include Parts I, II, III, and IV [@CL-EST-3253].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency plans to award after discussions. The contracting officer inserts the basic instructions provision with its alternate for award after discussions. The solicitation does not state the place of performance, so the place of performance provision is included. A separate market survey is issued only to gather information, so it carries the information-or-planning provision and is marked on its face under the codified text [@CL-EST-1397], while RFO FAR 15.110(c) is printed as reserved [@CL-EST-3186].

## Never confuse

The advisory multi-step process advises respondents about their potential to be viable competitors, and every respondent is told it may still take part in the resulting acquisition [@CL-EST-1392; @CL-EST-1393]. It is not the provision on instructions to offerors, whose basic version or Alternate I turns on whether discussions are intended [@CL-EST-1394; @CL-EST-1395]. A citation to FAR 15.202 means the advisory multi-step process in the codified text [@CL-EST-1392] but evaluating competitive proposals in the RFO text [@CL-EST-3189]; the RFO text puts the advisory multi-step process at 15.101(e) [@CL-EST-3178].
