## Purpose

Shows how the advisory multi-step process works and which provisions and clauses the contracting officer inserts, and when.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1392; @CL-EST-1394] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.202 [@CL-EST-1392]; FAR 15.209 [@CL-EST-1394] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1392] |
| Applies to | Contracting by negotiation, for the provisions and clauses [@CL-EST-1495] |

The agency may publish a presolicitation notice that invites potential offerors to submit information that allows the Government to advise them about their potential to be viable competitors [@CL-EST-1392]. The agency shall inform all respondents that, notwithstanding the advice the Government gave in response to their submissions, they may participate in the resultant acquisition [@CL-EST-1393].

## How it works

When contracting by negotiation, the contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.215-1 in all competitive solicitations where the Government intends to award a contract without discussions [@CL-EST-1394]. Where the Government intends award after discussions with offerors within the competitive range, the contracting officer shall use the basic provision with its Alternate I [@CL-EST-1394; @CL-EST-1395]. If the Government would be willing to accept alternate proposals, the contracting officer shall alter the basic clause to add a paragraph substantially the same as Alternate II [@CL-EST-1394; @CL-EST-1497]. Discussions are on [Exchanges with offerors after receipt of proposals](est-exchanges-after-proposals-and-competitive-range.md).

When contracting by negotiation, and except as provided in a cross-referenced paragraph, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.215-2, Audit and Records-Negotiation, in solicitations and contracts except those for listed acquisitions, one of which is an acquisition not exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1396; @CL-EST-1398]. For cost-reimbursement contracts with State and local Governments, educational institutions, and other nonprofit organizations, the contracting officer shall use the audit clause with its Alternate II [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1498]. The audit clause is explained on [Audit and Records, Negotiation clause](../../compliance/audit-retention/cmp-audit-and-records-negotiation.md).

When contracting by negotiation and issuing a solicitation for information or planning purposes, the contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.215-3 and clearly mark the face of the solicitation as for information or planning purposes [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1397]. The provision at 52.215-5, Facsimile Proposals, goes in solicitations if facsimile proposals are authorized [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1496]. The provision at 52.215-6, Place of Performance, goes in solicitations unless the place of performance is specified by the Government [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1399]. The clause at 52.215-8 goes in solicitations and contracts using the uniform contract format [@CL-EST-1495; @CL-EST-1382]. Other provisions and alternates in the section are not covered here.

Talks with industry before proposals, which include presolicitation notices, are on [Exchanges with industry before receipt of proposals](est-exchanges-before-proposals.md). What a request for proposals describes is on [Requests for proposals and amending the solicitation](est-rfp-and-solicitation-amendments.md), and when proposals are due is on [Submitting, modifying, withdrawing and late proposals](est-proposal-submission-and-late-proposals.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency plans to award after discussions. The contracting officer inserts the basic instructions provision with its alternate for award after discussions. The solicitation does not state the place of performance, so the place of performance provision is included. A separate market survey is issued only to gather information, so it carries the information-or-planning provision and is marked on its face.

## Never confuse

The advisory multi-step process advises respondents about their potential to be viable competitors, and every respondent is told it may still take part in the resulting acquisition [@CL-EST-1392; @CL-EST-1393]. It is not the provision on instructions to offerors, whose basic version or Alternate I turns on whether discussions are intended [@CL-EST-1394; @CL-EST-1395].
