## Purpose

Explains how the contract pricing subpart defines price and subcontract, and the two other circumstances in which certified cost or pricing data are not required.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2746] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.401 and FAR 15.403-2 [@CL-EST-2746; @CL-EST-2748] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.401 [@CL-EST-3363; @CL-EST-3364]; RFO FAR 15.403-3(b)(3)(iii) [@CL-EST-3365; @CL-EST-3366] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2746] |
| Applies to | Terms used in the contract pricing subpart, option exercises at the established price, and proposals used solely for overrun funding or interim billing price adjustments [@CL-EST-2777; @CL-EST-2748; @CL-EST-2749] |

The terms below are defined as used in the contract pricing subpart [@CL-EST-2777]. Price means cost plus any fee or profit applicable to the contract type [@CL-EST-2746]. Subcontract, except as used in 15.407-2, also includes a transfer of commercial products or commercial services between divisions, subsidiaries or affiliates of a contractor or a subcontractor [@CL-EST-2747].

The exercise of an option at the price established at contract award or initial negotiation does not require submission of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-2748]. Certified cost or pricing data are not required for proposals used solely for overrun funding or interim billing price adjustments [@CL-EST-2749].

## How it works

A price therefore includes any fee or profit that applies to the contract type, not cost alone [@CL-EST-2746]. The subcontract definition reaches transfers inside a corporate family: a transfer of commercial products or commercial services between divisions, subsidiaries or affiliates of a contractor or a subcontractor is also a subcontract for the pricing subpart, except where the term is used in 15.407-2 [@CL-EST-2747].

Each of the two exceptions carries a condition. For an option, the exercise must be at the price established at contract award or initial negotiation [@CL-EST-2748]. For a proposal, it must be used solely for overrun funding or interim billing price adjustments [@CL-EST-2749]. Neither paragraph names who decides or sets a deadline [@CL-EST-2748; @CL-EST-2749]. Other routes to a no-certified-data result are on [Exceptions to certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-data-exceptions.md), and the data requirement itself is on [Certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. In the RFO text of the pricing subpart, price still means cost plus any fee or profit applicable to the contract type [@CL-EST-2746; @CL-EST-3363]. Subcontract still also includes a transfer of commercial products or commercial services between divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of a contractor or a subcontractor, but the exception points to 15.405-2 where the codified text points to 15.407-2 [@CL-EST-2747; @CL-EST-3364]. The RFO text states the two exceptions in RFO FAR 15.403-3(b)(3)(iii): certified cost or pricing data are not required for modifications solely for overrun funding or interim billing price adjustments [@CL-EST-2749; @CL-EST-3365], or to exercise an option at the price established at contract award or initial negotiation [@CL-EST-2748; @CL-EST-3366].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract includes an option priced at 500,000 at award. The option is exercised at that same price. No certified cost or pricing data are required for the exercise [@CL-EST-2748]. If the parties instead renegotiated the option price, the exercise would not be one at the price established at award or initial negotiation, and this paragraph would not apply [@CL-EST-2748].

On another contract, a proposal is requested only to support an overrun funding adjustment of 80,000 and an interim billing price adjustment. Because it is used solely for those purposes, certified cost or pricing data are not required for it [@CL-EST-2749]. If the same proposal also supported a new price for added work, it would not be used solely for those purposes, and this exception would not apply [@CL-EST-2749]. Whether another exception applies is a separate question on the exceptions card.

A contractor's parts division transfers a commercial product to its assembly division. For the pricing subpart, that transfer is a subcontract, except where the term is used in 15.407-2 [@CL-EST-2747]; the RFO text names 15.405-2 for that exception [@CL-EST-3364].

## Never confuse

Price is not cost. Price means cost plus any fee or profit applicable to the contract type [@CL-EST-2746].

Exercising an option at the established price is not the same as negotiating a new price. The paragraph addresses the exercise of an option at the price established at contract award or initial negotiation [@CL-EST-2748].

The overrun and billing exception is not a rule for any revised proposal. The paragraph is framed for proposals used solely for overrun funding or interim billing price adjustments [@CL-EST-2749].

The codified text states the exceptions for the exercise of an option at the established price and for proposals used solely for overrun funding or interim billing price adjustments [@CL-EST-2748; @CL-EST-2749], while the RFO text states them as exceptions for modifications [@CL-EST-3365; @CL-EST-3366]. The codified subcontract definition excludes use in 15.407-2 [@CL-EST-2747], and the RFO definition excludes use in 15.405-2 [@CL-EST-3364].
