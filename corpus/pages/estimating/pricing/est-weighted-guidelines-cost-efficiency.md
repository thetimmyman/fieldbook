## Purpose

Explains the cost efficiency special factor, the ceiling on the profit it can add, and the record form that carries the weighted guidelines result.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-0793] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.404-71-5 [@CL-EST-0793] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0793] |
| Applies to | A special factor used where demonstrated cost reduction efforts benefit the pending contract [@CL-EST-0794] |

The factor gives contractors an incentive to reduce costs [@CL-EST-0793]. To the extent that the contractor can demonstrate cost reduction efforts that benefit the pending contract, the contracting officer may increase the prenegotiation profit objective by an amount not exceeding 4 percent of total objective cost [@CL-EST-0794]. Total objective cost is Block 20 of DD Form 1547, and the increase recognizes these efforts at Block 29 [@CL-EST-0872].

The cost efficiency special factor has no normal value, and the contracting officer shall exercise sound business judgment in selecting a value when this special factor is used [@CL-EST-0795].

## How it works

To determine if using the factor is appropriate, the contracting officer shall consider criteria, such as the contractor's participation in Single Process Initiative improvements and actual cost reductions achieved on prior contracts, to evaluate the benefit the contractor's cost reduction efforts will have on the pending contract [@CL-EST-9719; @CL-EST-0796]. When selecting the percentage to use for this special factor, the contracting officer has maximum flexibility in determining the best way to evaluate that benefit [@CL-EST-0798]. However, the contracting officer shall consider the impact that quantity differences, learning, changes in scope, and economic factors such as inflation and deflation will have on cost reduction [@CL-EST-0797]. DD Form 1547 is the Record of Weighted Guidelines Application [@CL-EST-0789]. The procedures at PGI 215.404-70 for use of DD Form 1547 are to be followed whenever a structured approach to profit analysis is required [@CL-EST-0799].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Total objective cost is 3,000,000 and the contracting officer accepts a cost efficiency value of 2 percent, so the profit objective rises by 60,000, still below the 4 percent ceiling of 120,000.

## Never confuse

The cost efficiency special factor has no normal value [@CL-EST-0795], while each other profit factor has a normal value and a designated range of values [@CL-EST-0774]. For this factor the contracting officer may increase the prenegotiation profit objective by an amount not to exceed 4 percent of total objective cost [@CL-EST-0794]. The weighted guidelines method focuses on four profit factors: performance risk, contract type risk, facilities capital employed, and cost efficiency [@CL-EST-0773].

## Company calibration

Whether the company can evidence cost reduction efforts that benefit a specific pending proposal, and where the record of the weighted guidelines result and its approval route sit. [@Q-012]
