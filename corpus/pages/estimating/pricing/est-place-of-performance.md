## Purpose

Shows what an offeror states about where the work will be performed, and what details it must add when another location is used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1926] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-6 [@CL-EST-1926] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-6 [@CL-EST-0427; @CL-EST-0428; @CL-EST-0429] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1926] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include the place of performance provision, which is inserted unless the Government specifies the place of performance [@CL-EST-1399] |

In the provision, the offeror or respondent checks one block to state whether, in the performance of any contract resulting from the solicitation, it intends or does not intend to use one or more plants or facilities located at a different address from its own address as indicated in the proposal or response to a request for information [@CL-EST-2048; @CL-EST-1926].

If the offeror or respondent checks that it intends to use another plant or facility, it shall insert the required information in the spaces provided, which for the place of performance are street address, city, state, county and zip code [@CL-EST-1927]. The spaces also call for the name and address of the owner and operator of the plant or facility, if other than the offeror or respondent [@CL-EST-1928].

## How it works

The offeror or respondent answers by checking a block [@CL-EST-1926]. The address spaces are filled in when it checks that it intends to use another plant or facility [@CL-EST-1927]. A separate part of the spaces asks for the owner and operator when they are someone other than the offeror or respondent [@CL-EST-1928].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.215-6(a) says that the offeror or respondent, in the performance of any contract resulting from this request for proposals, checks one block to state whether it intends or does not intend to use one or more plants or facilities located at a different address from its own address as indicated in the proposal or response to request for information [@CL-EST-0427], where the codified paragraph says this solicitation [@CL-EST-2048]. Paragraph (b) keeps the rule that an offeror or respondent that checks intends shall insert the required information in the spaces provided, which for the place of performance are street address, city, state, county and ZIP code [@CL-EST-0428; @CL-EST-1927], and keeps the space for the name and address of the owner and operator of the plant or facility if other than the offeror or respondent [@CL-EST-0429; @CL-EST-1928].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror's response gives its own address as 100 Main Street. It will assemble the units at a plant at 40 River Road that a third party owns and operates. It checks intends, inserts the street address, city, state, county and zip code of that plant, and gives the third party's name and address as owner and operator. Had all work stayed at 100 Main Street, it would have checked does not intend.

## Never confuse

The block asks about plants or facilities at a different address from the offeror's or respondent's own address, not about the offeror's own address [@CL-EST-1926]. The place of performance spaces and the owner and operator spaces are separate: the owner and operator are named if they are someone other than the offeror or respondent [@CL-EST-1927; @CL-EST-1928]. When the provision is inserted is covered on [Advisory multi-step process and source selection provisions and clauses](est-advisory-multi-step-and-source-selection-provisions.md).

## Company calibration

Who confirms, for each response, whether work will run from another plant and which address and operator details go into the form. [@Q-004]
