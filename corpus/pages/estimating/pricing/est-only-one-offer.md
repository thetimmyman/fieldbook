## Purpose

Shows what an offeror agrees to do when the Contracting Officer notifies it that only one offer was received: submit additional cost or pricing data and, above the threshold and subject to the provision's exceptions, certify.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-0351] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.215-7008 [@CL-EST-0351] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | The provision as prescribed at DFARS 215.408(3) [@CL-EST-0351] |

The provision is prescribed at DFARS 215.408(3) [@CL-EST-0351]. After initial submission of offers, if the Contracting Officer notifies the offeror that only one offer was received, the offeror agrees to submit any additional cost or pricing data that is required to determine whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0352]. That additional data also covers data needed to comply with the statutory requirement for certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0460]. The offeror also agrees, in addition to the data submission, that except as provided for the Canadian Commercial Corporation, if the acquisition exceeds the certified cost or pricing data threshold and no exception under FAR 15.403-1(b)(2) through (5) applies, it will certify all cost or pricing data in accordance with paragraph (c) of DFARS provision 252.215-7010 [@CL-EST-0352; @CL-EST-9515; @CL-EST-0353; @CL-EST-9675].

If the offeror is the Canadian Commercial Corporation, certified cost or pricing data are not required [@CL-EST-0354]. If the Contracting Officer notifies the Canadian Commercial Corporation that additional data other than certified cost or pricing data are required in accordance with DFARS 225.870-4(c), it shall obtain and provide the data the provision lists [@CL-EST-0461], which includes the profit rate or fee, as applicable, and the analysis that Public Works and Government Services Canada provided to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-0462]. Unless the offeror is the Canadian Commercial Corporation, the offeror shall insert the substance of the provision in all subcontracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold defined in FAR part 2 [@CL-EST-0355].

## How it works

The offeror's agreement applies after the Contracting Officer notifies it that only one offer was received [@CL-EST-0352]. There are two agreements, joined by "and". The first is the submission of additional cost or pricing data needed to determine whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0352]. The second is the certification of all cost or pricing data, which applies where the offeror is not the Canadian Commercial Corporation, the acquisition exceeds the certified cost or pricing data threshold, and no listed exception applies [@CL-EST-9515; @CL-EST-0353]. The Canadian Commercial Corporation follows a separate route [@CL-EST-0354; @CL-EST-0461].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor submits a proposal of 5,000,000 for communications hardware. After offers close, the Contracting Officer notifies it that only one offer was received. The contractor sends the additional cost or pricing data needed to decide whether the price is fair and reasonable. The acquisition exceeds the certified cost or pricing data threshold and no listed exception applies, so the contractor also certifies all of its cost or pricing data.

## Never confuse

Both the duty to submit additional data and the duty to certify apply after the Contracting Officer's notification [@CL-EST-0352; @CL-EST-0353], and the duty to certify also depends on the offeror not being the Canadian Commercial Corporation, the acquisition exceeding the certified cost or pricing data threshold, and no listed exception applying [@CL-EST-0352; @CL-EST-0353]. Receiving only one offer therefore does not by itself trigger the duty to certify; the threshold and exception conditions also apply. For the general rules, see [Certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md) and [Certified data exceptions](est-certified-data-exceptions.md).
