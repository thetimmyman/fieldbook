## Purpose

Shows the exception route an offeror or Contractor can use instead of certified data, what must be submitted when no exception is granted, and the format and certificate that go with it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1819] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-20 and FAR 52.215-21 [@CL-EST-1819; @CL-EST-1824] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-20 [@CL-EST-0075; @CL-EST-0079] and RFO FAR 52.215-21 [@CL-EST-0085; @CL-EST-0087] |

In lieu of submitting certified cost or pricing data, offerors may submit a written request for exception by submitting the information the provision describes [@CL-EST-1819]. The Contracting Officer may require additional supporting information, but only to the extent necessary to determine whether an exception should be granted and whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-1887]. The information described in the following subparagraphs includes identification of the law or regulation establishing the price offered, with a copy of the controlling document attached if the price is controlled under law by periodic rulings, reviews or similar actions of a governmental body, unless it was previously submitted to the contracting office [@CL-EST-1819; @CL-EST-1864]. For the commercial product and commercial service exception, the offeror shall submit at a minimum information on prices at which the same or similar items were previously sold in the commercial market that is adequate for evaluating the reasonableness of the price for this acquisition [@CL-EST-1820]. The offeror grants the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative the right to examine, at any time before award, books, records, documents or other directly pertinent records to verify any request for an exception under this provision, and the reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-1865]. For items priced using catalog or market prices, or law or regulation, the right to examine records does not extend to cost or profit information or other data relevant solely to the offeror's determination of the prices to be offered in the catalog or marketplace [@CL-EST-1885].

In lieu of submitting certified cost or pricing data for modifications under the contract [@CL-EST-9742], for price adjustments expected to exceed the threshold set forth in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) on the date of the agreement on price or the date of the award, whichever is later, the Contractor may submit a written request for exception [@CL-EST-1824] by submitting the information described in paragraphs (a)(1)(i) and (ii) of the clause [@CL-EST-9743]. The modifications clause likewise calls for identification of the law or regulation establishing the price [@CL-EST-1866], and the Contractor grants the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative the right to examine, at any time before award, books, records, documents, or other directly pertinent records to verify any request for an exception under this clause, and the reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-1867]. Under the modifications clause the Contracting Officer may likewise require additional supporting information only to the extent necessary to determine whether an exception should be granted and whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-1888], and for items priced using catalog or market prices, or law or regulation, the right to examine records does not extend to cost or profit information or other data relevant solely to the Contractor's determination of the prices to be offered in the catalog or marketplace [@CL-EST-1886]. If the threshold specified in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) is adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR 1.109(a), then pursuant to FAR 1.109(d) the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-1889]. An offeror not granted an exception from the certified data requirement shall prepare and submit certified cost or pricing data, data other than certified cost or pricing data, and supporting attachments [@CL-EST-1822]. That offeror shall submit a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data as soon as practicable after agreement on price, but before contract award except for unpriced actions such as letter contracts [@CL-EST-1822; @CL-EST-1823].

## How it works

In lieu of submitting certified cost or pricing data, the offeror may submit a written request for exception [@CL-EST-1819]. An offeror not granted an exception shall prepare and submit certified cost or pricing data, data other than certified cost or pricing data, and supporting attachments [@CL-EST-1822]. The instructions in Table 15-2 are incorporated as a mandatory format to be used in this contract, unless the Contracting Officer and the Contractor agree to a different format and change the clause to use Alternate I [@CL-EST-1821]. That offeror shall submit the Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data as soon as practicable after agreement on price, but before contract award, except for unpriced actions such as letter contracts [@CL-EST-1822; @CL-EST-1823]. Alternate II adds a paragraph requiring that, when the proposal is submitted, the offeror also submit one copy each to the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Contract Auditor [@CL-EST-1825]. For modifications under the contract with price adjustments expected to exceed the threshold set forth in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) on the date of the agreement on price or the date of the award, whichever is later, the modifications clause lets the Contractor submit a written request for exception [@CL-EST-1824].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO provision keeps the written request for exception in lieu of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0075] [@CL-EST-1819], the limit that the Contracting Officer may require additional supporting information only to the extent necessary to determine whether an exception should be granted and whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-0076] [@CL-EST-1887], and the identification of the law or regulation establishing the price offered, with a copy of the controlling document unless it was previously submitted [@CL-EST-0077] [@CL-EST-1864]. It also keeps the minimum price information for the commercial product and commercial service exception [@CL-EST-0078] [@CL-EST-1820], the right to examine records at any time before award [@CL-EST-0079] [@CL-EST-1865] and the limit on that access for items priced using catalog or market prices, or law or regulation [@CL-EST-0080] [@CL-EST-1885]. The RFO modifications clause keeps the same right to examine records [@CL-EST-0087] [@CL-EST-1867] and the same limit on access [@CL-EST-0088] [@CL-EST-1886]. Alternate II to the RFO provision adds the same paragraph (c) requiring one copy each to the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Contract Auditor, but it is prescribed in FAR 15.110(t)(2) where the codified Alternate II is prescribed in FAR 15.408(l) [@CL-EST-0084] [@CL-EST-1825]. The RFO provision says the instructions in Table 15-1 are a mandatory format unless the Contracting Officer and the Contractor agree to a different format and change the clause to use Alternate I, where the codified provision names Table 15-2 [@CL-EST-0082] [@CL-EST-1821]. The RFO provision names Table 15-1 of FAR 15.408-2 and incorporates it by reference with the same force and effect as though it were inserted in full text [@CL-EST-0081] [@CL-EST-9067]. It says the Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data is as prescribed by FAR 15.403-4, where the codified provision cites FAR 15.406-2 [@CL-EST-0083] [@CL-EST-1823]. The RFO modifications clause says the threshold is the one set forth in FAR 15.403-3(a), where the codified clause cites FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-0085] [@CL-EST-1824], and says an inflation-adjusted threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment, citing FAR part 1 where the codified clause cites FAR 1.109(a) and FAR 1.109(d) [@CL-EST-0086] [@CL-EST-1889].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes a catalog item at 80,000 and asks for the commercial exception, attaching its catalog pages and recent sales at similar quantities. It also agrees that the Contracting Officer may examine directly pertinent records before award to verify the request and the price, but that this access does not reach cost or profit data relevant solely to its own determination of the catalog price. The Contracting Officer asks for no additional supporting information beyond what is necessary to decide the exception and the fairness of the price. The Contracting Officer grants the exception, so no certified data or certificate is needed. A second proposal for custom work gets no exception. That offeror submits certified data in the FAR table format, copies the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Contract Auditor if Alternate II applies, and signs the certificate after price is agreed.

## Never confuse

Solicitation provision FAR 52.215-20 governs preaward offeror submissions and exception requests [@CL-EST-0075; @CL-EST-1819], whereas FAR 52.215-21 governs postaward contract modifications expected to exceed the certified data threshold [@CL-EST-1824], and both govern data submission rather than defective-pricing recovery under [Price reduction for defective certified data](est-price-reduction-and-subcontractor-data-modification-clauses.md) [@CL-EST-1806].
