## Purpose

Shows where DoD points for deciding whether to perform a program or overhead should-cost review, how major weapon system program reviews are conducted, and the makeup of the review team.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1535] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.407-4 [@CL-EST-1535] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1535] |
| Applies to | DoD decisions to perform a program or overhead should-cost review [@CL-EST-1535] |

See PGI 215.407-4 for guidance on determining whether to perform a program or overhead should-cost review [@CL-EST-1535]. Major weapon system should-cost program reviews shall be conducted in a manner that is transparent, objective, and provides for the efficiency of the DoD systems acquisition process [@CL-EST-1622]. The program should-cost clause at 252.215-7015 is used in all solicitations and contracts for the development or production of a major weapon system, as defined in 234.7001 [@CL-EST-1528].

## How it works

The should-cost review team shall be comprised of members, including third-party experts if necessary, with the training, skills, and experience in analysis of cost elements, production or sustainment processes, and technologies relevant to the program under review [@CL-EST-1537].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer has a development contract for a major weapon system worth 200 million and is asked whether to run a review. The officer checks the guidance at PGI 215.407-4 on whether to perform the review. The answer: the decision follows that guidance, and the contract carries the program should-cost clause.

## Never confuse

The FAR description of what a should-cost review is appears on [Should-cost review: program and overhead reviews](est-should-cost-review.md). The reference to the guidance names both a program and an overhead should-cost review [@CL-EST-1535]; the paragraph on program should-cost review covers major weapon system should-cost program reviews [@CL-EST-1622] and the review team [@CL-EST-1537]. Where the program should-cost clause goes is on [DFARS solicitation provisions and contract clauses for negotiated contracts](est-dfars-solicitation-provisions-negotiated.md).
