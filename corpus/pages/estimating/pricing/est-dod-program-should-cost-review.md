## Purpose

Shows what the DoD supplement says about when a program should-cost review or an overhead should-cost review is considered, and what it says about the review team.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1535] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.407-4 [@CL-EST-1535] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1535] |
| Applies to | DoD decisions to perform a program or overhead should-cost review [@CL-EST-1535] |

The section refers to PGI 215.407-4 for guidance on determining whether to perform a program or overhead should-cost review [@CL-EST-1535]. Paragraph (a) of the section gives no test, threshold or trigger for choosing a program review or an overhead review and leaves that choice to the guidance. Major weapon system should-cost program reviews shall be conducted in a manner that is transparent, objective, and provides for the efficiency of the DoD systems acquisition process [@CL-EST-1622]. A solicitation or contract for the development or production of a major weapon system carries the program should-cost clause at 252.215-7015 [@CL-EST-1528].

## How it works

The should-cost review team shall be comprised of members, including third-party experts if necessary, with the training, skills, and experience in analysis of cost elements, production or sustainment processes, and technologies relevant to the program under review [@CL-EST-1537]. Paragraph (b) of the section covers program reviews, and the section has no paragraph on overhead reviews, so what an overhead review involves is not established by this section.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer has a development contract for a major weapon system worth 200 million and is asked whether to run a review. The officer checks the guidance the section points to. The answer: the section itself gives no threshold or test, so the decision follows that guidance, and the contract carries the program should-cost clause.

## Never confuse

The section on when to review is not the FAR description of what a should-cost review is; that is on [Should-cost review: program and overhead reviews](est-should-cost-review.md). A program review and an overhead review are named together in the reference to the guidance [@CL-EST-1535], but this section gives rules only for program reviews [@CL-EST-1622; @CL-EST-1537]. Where the program should-cost clause goes is on [DFARS solicitation provisions and contract clauses for negotiated contracts](est-dfars-solicitation-provisions-negotiated.md).
