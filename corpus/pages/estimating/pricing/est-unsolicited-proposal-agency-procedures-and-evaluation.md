## Purpose

Shows the duties that the agency, its agency contact point and its evaluators carry when an unsolicited proposal is received, handled and comprehensively evaluated.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2756] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.606 and FAR 15.606-2 [@CL-EST-2756; @CL-EST-2759] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.504 [@CL-EST-3487; @CL-EST-3489] and RFO FAR 15.505 [@CL-EST-3490; @CL-EST-3491] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2756] |
| Applies to | Agency handling and comprehensive evaluation of unsolicited proposals under the unsolicited proposal subpart [@CL-EST-2756; @CL-EST-2759] |

Agencies shall establish procedures for controlling the receipt, evaluation and timely disposition of unsolicited proposals consistent with the requirements of FAR subpart 15.6 [@CL-EST-2756]. The procedures shall include controls on the reproduction and disposition of proposal material, particularly data identified by the offeror as subject to duplication, use or disclosure restrictions [@CL-EST-2757]. Agencies shall establish agency points of contact to coordinate the receipt and handling of unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-2758].

Comprehensive evaluations shall be coordinated by the agency contact point, who shall attach or imprint on each unsolicited proposal circulated for evaluation the legend required by FAR 15.609(d) [@CL-EST-2759]. When performing a comprehensive evaluation, evaluators shall consider the listed factors, in addition to any others appropriate for the particular proposal [@CL-EST-2760]. The listed factors are: unique, innovative and meritorious methods, approaches or concepts demonstrated by the proposal [@CL-EST-2780]; overall scientific, technical or socioeconomic merits of the proposal [@CL-EST-2781]; potential contribution of the effort to the agency's specific mission [@CL-EST-2782]; the offeror's capabilities, related experience, facilities, techniques or unique combinations of these that are integral factors for achieving the proposal objectives [@CL-EST-2783]; the qualifications, capabilities and experience of the proposed principal investigator, team leader or key personnel critical to achieving the proposal objectives [@CL-EST-2784]; and the realism of the proposed cost [@CL-EST-2761]. The evaluators shall notify the agency point of contact of their recommendations when the evaluation is completed [@CL-EST-2762].

## How it works

Agencies shall establish the procedures, the procedures shall include controls on the reproduction and disposition of proposal material, and agencies shall establish agency points of contact [@CL-EST-2756; @CL-EST-2757; @CL-EST-2758]. Comprehensive evaluations shall be coordinated by the agency contact point, who shall attach or imprint the legend on each unsolicited proposal circulated for evaluation [@CL-EST-2759]. Evaluators shall consider the listed factors, in addition to any others appropriate for the particular proposal [@CL-EST-2760], and shall notify the agency point of contact of their recommendations when the evaluation is completed [@CL-EST-2762].

The cost factor is the realism of the proposed cost [@CL-EST-2761]. The agency's controls on the reproduction and disposition of proposal material apply particularly to data identified by the offeror as subject to duplication, use, or disclosure restrictions [@CL-EST-2757].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.504(a) says agencies must establish procedures for controlling the receipt, evaluation, and timely disposition of unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-3487], where codified FAR 15.606(a) adds that the procedures are to be consistent with the requirements of the subpart [@CL-EST-2756], and that the procedures must include controls on the reproduction and disposition of proposal material, particularly data identified by the offeror as subject to duplication, use, or disclosure restrictions [@CL-EST-3488; @CL-EST-2757]. The same RFO paragraph continues the list of what the procedures must include with identifying agency points of contact to coordinate the receipt and handling of unsolicited proposals, where codified FAR 15.606(b) says agencies shall establish agency points of contact [@CL-EST-3489; @CL-EST-2758]. RFO FAR 15.505(b) says the agency point of contact must coordinate the evaluation of proposals and attach the legend at 15.504-2(a)(2) to each copy of the proposal distributed for evaluation, where codified FAR 15.606-2(a) says the agency contact point shall attach or imprint the legend required by 15.609(d) on each unsolicited proposal circulated for evaluation [@CL-EST-3490; @CL-EST-2759]. RFO FAR 15.505(c) says that when evaluating unsolicited proposals, evaluators should consider, at a minimum, the following factors [@CL-EST-3491], where codified FAR 15.606-2(a) says evaluators shall consider the following factors, in addition to any others appropriate for the particular proposal [@CL-EST-2760]: unique, innovative and meritorious methods, approaches, or concepts demonstrated by the proposal, overall scientific, technical, or socioeconomic merits of the proposal, and potential contribution of the effort to the agency's specific mission [@CL-EST-3492; @CL-EST-2780; @CL-EST-2781; @CL-EST-2782]; offeror's capabilities, related experience, facilities, techniques, or unique combinations of these that are integral factors for achieving the proposal objectives [@CL-EST-3493; @CL-EST-2783]; qualifications, capabilities, and experience of the proposed principal investigator, team leader, or key personnel critical to achieving the proposal objectives [@CL-EST-3494; @CL-EST-2784]; and realism of the proposed cost [@CL-EST-3495; @CL-EST-2761]. RFO FAR 15.505(d) says evaluators must notify the agency point of contact of their recommendations when the evaluation is complete [@CL-EST-3496; @CL-EST-2762].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An unsolicited proposal arrives with several pages marked as restricted. The agency's procedures include controls on reproduction and disposition of the proposal material, particularly the data the offeror identified as restricted [@CL-EST-2757]. The agency contact point coordinates the comprehensive evaluation and attaches or imprints the legend required by FAR 15.609(d) on each proposal circulated for evaluation [@CL-EST-2759].

The evaluators consider the six listed factors, including the realism of the proposed cost [@CL-EST-2760; @CL-EST-2761]. They also consider a further factor suited to this proposal, which the text allows [@CL-EST-2760]. When the evaluation is complete, they notify the agency point of contact of their recommendations [@CL-EST-2762].

## Never confuse

The agency contact point's evaluation legend under FAR 15.609(d) is attached by the Government when circulating an unsolicited proposal [@CL-EST-2756; @CL-EST-2759], unlike the offeror's own title-page restrictive legend on [Unsolicited proposals: data legend](est-unsolicited-proposal-data-legend.md) [@CL-EST-1440], and comprehensive evaluation begins only after initial validity screening on [Unsolicited proposals: review and prohibitions](../../market/programs/mkt-unsolicited-proposals-review-and-prohibitions.md) [@CL-MKT-1347].
