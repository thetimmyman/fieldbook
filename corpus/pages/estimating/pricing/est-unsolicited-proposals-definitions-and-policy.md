## Purpose

Defines advertising material, a commercial product or commercial service offer and a contribution as the unsolicited proposal subpart uses them, and states the Government policy that encourages new and innovative ideas and lets ideas outside publicized topic areas be submitted as unsolicited proposals.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2750] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.601 and FAR 15.602 [@CL-EST-2750; @CL-EST-2754]; DFARS 215.602 [@CL-EST-2060]; FAR 15.600 [@CL-EST-9192] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.501 [@CL-EST-3519]; RFO FAR 15.503-1(a) [@CL-EST-3523] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2750] |
| Applies to | Terms used in the unsolicited proposal subpart, and new and innovative ideas that do not fall under publicized topic areas [@CL-EST-2778; @CL-EST-2755] |

The terms below are defined as used in the unsolicited proposal subpart [@CL-EST-2778]. Advertising material means material designed to acquaint the Government with a prospective contractor's present products, services or potential capabilities, or designed to stimulate the Government's interest in buying such products or services [@CL-EST-2750]. A commercial product or commercial service offer means an offer of a commercial product or commercial service that the vendor wishes to see introduced in the Government's supply system as an alternate or a replacement for an existing supply item [@CL-EST-2751]. That term does not include innovative or unique configurations or uses of commercial products or commercial services that are being offered for further development and that may be submitted as an unsolicited proposal [@CL-EST-2752]. A contribution means a concept, suggestion or idea presented to the Government for its use with no indication that the source intends to devote any further effort to it on the Government's behalf [@CL-EST-2753].

It is the policy of the Government to encourage the submission of new and innovative ideas in response to Broad Agency Announcements, Small Business Innovation Research topics, Small Business Technology Transfer Research topics, Program Research and Development Announcements, or any other Government-initiated solicitation or program [@CL-EST-2754; @CL-EST-2779]. When the new and innovative ideas do not fall under topic areas publicized under those programs or techniques, the ideas may be submitted as unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-2755].

FAR 2.101 defines a broad agency announcement as a general announcement of an agency's research interest including criteria for selecting proposals and soliciting the participation of all offerors capable of satisfying the Government's needs (see 6.102(d)(2)) [@CL-EST-9274]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines a broad agency announcement as a general announcement of an agency's research interest including criteria for selecting proposals and soliciting the participation of all offerors capable of satisfying the Government's needs (see 6.102(d)(2)) [@CL-EST-9347].

Subpart 15.6 sets forth policies and procedures concerning the submission, receipt, evaluation, and acceptance or rejection of unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-9192].

## How it works

It is the policy of the Government to encourage the submission of new and innovative ideas in response to Broad Agency Announcements, Small Business Innovation Research topics, Small Business Technology Transfer Research topics, Program Research and Development Announcements, or any other Government-initiated solicitation or program [@CL-EST-2754; @CL-EST-2779]. When the new and innovative ideas do not fall under topic areas publicized under those programs or techniques, the ideas may be submitted as unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-2755].

Advertising material is material designed to acquaint the Government with a prospective contractor's present products, services, or potential capabilities, or designed to stimulate the Government's interest in buying such products or services [@CL-EST-2750]. A commercial product or commercial service offer is an offer of a commercial product or commercial service that the vendor wishes to see introduced in the Government's supply system as an alternate or a replacement for an existing supply item [@CL-EST-2751]. A contribution is a concept, suggestion, or idea presented to the Government for its use with no indication that the source intends to devote any further effort to it on the Government's behalf [@CL-EST-2753]. Under FAR 15.603(b), advertising material, commercial product or commercial service offers, contributions and routine correspondence on technical issues are not unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-1429]. See [Unsolicited proposals: general requirements](est-unsolicited-proposals-general.md).

DFARS 215.602 says the policy at FAR 15.602 applies to commercial solutions openings [@CL-EST-2060].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.501 defines, as used in that subpart, advertising material as material designed to acquaint the Government with a prospective contractor's present products, services, or potential capabilities, or designed to stimulate the Government's interest in buying such products or services [@CL-EST-3519]; a commercial product or commercial service offer as an offer of a commercial product or commercial service that the vendor wishes to see introduced in the Government's supply system as an alternate or a replacement for an existing supply item [@CL-EST-3520]; and a contribution as a concept, suggestion, or idea presented to the Government for its use with no indication that the source intends to devote any further effort to it on the Government's behalf [@CL-EST-3522]. It also says a commercial product or commercial service offer does not include innovative or unique configurations or uses of commercial products or commercial services that are being offered for further development and that may be submitted as an unsolicited proposal [@CL-EST-3521]. The codified definitions state the same terms [@CL-EST-2778; @CL-EST-2750; @CL-EST-2751; @CL-EST-2752; @CL-EST-2753]. On policy, RFO FAR 15.503-1(a) says unsolicited proposals may be submitted for new and innovative ideas that do not already fall under topic areas publicized under BAA, SBIR topics, STTR topics, program research and development announcements, or any other Government-initiated RFP or program [@CL-EST-3523]. The codified section states the policy of the Government to encourage the submission of new and innovative ideas in response to Broad Agency Announcements, Small Business Innovation Research topics, Small Business Technology Transfer Research topics, Program Research and Development Announcements, or any other Government-initiated solicitation or program [@CL-EST-2754; @CL-EST-2779], and says that when the ideas do not fall under topic areas publicized under those programs or techniques, the ideas may be submitted as unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-2755].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A vendor sends a brochure about an item it already sells. The brochure is designed to acquaint the Government with the vendor's present products, so it is advertising material [@CL-EST-2750]. The vendor separately offers that item as a replacement for an item already in the supply system, so that is a commercial product offer [@CL-EST-2751].

A third submission describes an innovative configuration of the same item that is offered for further development. It is outside the commercial offer definition and may be submitted as an unsolicited proposal [@CL-EST-2752].

A researcher emails a one-paragraph idea and says nothing about doing further work on it. That is a contribution [@CL-EST-2753]. Another researcher has a new and innovative idea that falls under no publicized topic area. The idea may be submitted as an unsolicited proposal [@CL-EST-2755].

## Never confuse

Codified FAR 15.602 states the policy of the Government to encourage the submission of new and innovative ideas in response to Broad Agency Announcements, Small Business Innovation Research topics, Small Business Technology Transfer Research topics, Program Research and Development Announcements, or any other Government-initiated solicitation or program [@CL-EST-2754; @CL-EST-2779]. RFO FAR 15.503-1(a) says unsolicited proposals may be submitted for new and innovative ideas that do not already fall under topic areas publicized under BAA, SBIR topics, STTR topics, program research and development announcements, or any other Government-initiated RFP or program [@CL-EST-3523].

The term commercial product or commercial service offer does not include innovative or unique configurations or uses of commercial products or commercial services that are being offered for further development and that may be submitted as an unsolicited proposal [@CL-EST-2752].

Contributions, as defined in 15.601, are not unsolicited proposals [@CL-EST-1429]. A contribution means a concept, suggestion, or idea presented to the Government for its use with no indication that the source intends to devote any further effort to it on the Government's behalf [@CL-EST-2753].
