## Purpose

Shows which proposals the audit agency's manual names for field pricing support, who sets the scope of the work, what each service type delivers, and how the manual treats time, technical-input and quantity constraints.

Price proposal evaluations assist contracting officers in determining whether proposed prices are fair, reasonable, and compliant with applicable acquisition regulations and cost accounting standards [@CL-EST-0650]. The manual's table of primary proposal categories [@CL-EST-0901] lists fixed-price proposals above $10 million [@CL-EST-0651] and cost-reimbursement proposals above $100 million [@CL-EST-0652]. It also lists indefinite delivery and indefinite quantity pricing together with modifications and adjustments [@CL-EST-0653], and forward pricing rates and factors [@CL-EST-0654].

A contracting officer may ask for audit support below these thresholds in exceptional circumstances based on documented risk, and may ask for an audit of a lower-dollar subcontract or cost element when the total prime proposal exceeds the threshold [@CL-EST-0655].

## How it works

Field pricing support gives contracting officers independent financial analysis to negotiate fair and reasonable contract prices [@CL-EST-0902]. The contracting officer defines the scope of services requested, and the audit agency decides the audit procedures needed to meet professional standards [@CL-EST-0656]. If the contracting officer requests an audit of parts of a proposal, the auditor should notify the contracting officer if risks in other parts of the proposal are identified that may warrant a full audit [@CL-EST-0657]. Auditors collaborate with and advise procurement teams but are prohibited from acting as formal members of integrated product teams or taking part in procurement decisions [@CL-EST-0658]. The output follows the service: an audit of the complete proposal ends in a report with an opinion or a disclaimer of opinion [@CL-EST-0659], agreed upon procedures end in an attestation report of factual findings with no opinion [@CL-EST-0660], advisory services end in a memorandum with no audit opinion [@CL-EST-0661], a forward pricing rate proposal audit ends in a report with an opinion or a disclaimer of opinion [@CL-EST-0662], a performance audit ends in a detailed narrative report with findings, conclusions and recommendations [@CL-EST-0903], and a cost realism service ends in a report with an opinion on cost realism [@CL-EST-0904]. Before the report is prepared, the audit team needs to coordinate with the requestor so that the report addresses customer needs and maximizes the value of the audit results for the contracting officer's negotiation objectives [@CL-EST-0908].

The manual also sets rules for constraints. If customer timelines prevent a complete proposal audit, the team coordinates alternative support and elevates the issue to management if no acceptable solution is reached [@CL-EST-0905]. If missing technical findings prevent sufficient evidence on material costs, the report includes a scope limitation [@CL-EST-0906]. For proposals with several quantity ranges, the contracting officer is advised that examining only one tier leaves potential pricing anomalies not examined [@CL-EST-0907].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer asks for support on a proposal for a new production lot. The request covers only the material costs. The auditor sees that the labor hours look thin and tells the contracting officer that a wider audit may be needed. The contracting officer then widens the request, and the output becomes an audit report with an opinion.

## Never confuse

An advisory service returns a memorandum with no audit opinion [@CL-EST-0661], while an audit of the complete proposal returns an opinion or a disclaimer [@CL-EST-0659]. Agreed upon procedures differ again: the tests are fixed in advance with the requestor, and the report gives factual findings but no opinion [@CL-EST-0660]. The buying activity's own request rules are in [Data to support proposal analysis](est-data-to-support-proposal-analysis.md).
