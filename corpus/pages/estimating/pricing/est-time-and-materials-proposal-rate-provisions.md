## Purpose

Shows what the offeror must state about fixed hourly rates, by labor category and source of labor, in the two time-and-materials and labor-hour proposal provisions, one titled for adequate price competition and one for the absence of it. The contract-type card covers the contract type itself: [Time-and-materials contracts](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1683; @CL-EST-1684] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-29 and FAR 52.216-30 [@CL-EST-1683; @CL-EST-1684] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-29 [@CL-EST-0818; @CL-EST-9096] and RFO FAR 52.216-30 [@CL-EST-0819; @CL-EST-0821; @CL-EST-9097] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1683; @CL-EST-1684] |
| Applies to | The provision titled Other Than Commercial Acquisition With Adequate Price Competition for the first, and Without Adequate Price Competition for the second, each inserted as prescribed [@CL-EST-1683; @CL-EST-1684] |

Under FAR 52.216-29, the offeror must specify whether the fixed hourly rate for each labor category applies to labor performed by the offeror, subcontractors, and/or divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of the offeror under a common control [@CL-EST-1685].

Under FAR 52.216-30, the offeror must specify separate fixed hourly rates in its offer that include wages, overhead, general and administrative expenses, and profit for each category of labor to be performed by the offeror and each subcontractor, among other listed sources [@CL-EST-1686]. The list of sources also includes each division, subsidiary, or affiliate of the offeror under a common control [@CL-EST-1715].

## How it works

The second provision also limits profit on internal transfers. Unless exempt under paragraph (d) of that provision, hourly rates for services transferred between divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates under common control shall not include profit for the transferring organization [@CL-EST-1687], though those rates may include profit for the prime contractor [@CL-EST-1712]. The exemption in that paragraph concerns fixed hourly rates for services that meet the definition of commercial service that are transferred between divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates under common control [@CL-EST-1713]. Those rates may be the established catalog or market rate when it is the established practice of the transferring organization to price interorganizational transfers at other than cost for commercial work of the offeror or any division, subsidiary or affiliate under common control [@CL-EST-1714].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-29 says the offeror must specify whether the fixed hourly rate for each labor category applies to labor performed by the offeror, by subcontractors, and/or by divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates under a common control, which is the same requirement as the codified provision [@CL-EST-0818; @CL-EST-1685]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-30 has the same requirement of separate fixed hourly rates by source, including each division, subsidiary, or affiliate under a common control [@CL-EST-0819; @CL-EST-0820; @CL-EST-1686; @CL-EST-1715]. It has the same rule that, unless exempt under paragraph (d), rates for services transferred between divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates under a common control shall not include profit for the transferring organization but may include profit for the prime Contractor [@CL-EST-0821; @CL-EST-0822; @CL-EST-1687; @CL-EST-1712]. Its paragraph (d) has the same exemption for services that meet the definition of commercial service, with the same catalog or market rate condition [@CL-EST-0823; @CL-EST-0824; @CL-EST-1713; @CL-EST-1714]. The RFO text of FAR 52.216-29 says it is inserted as prescribed in 16.601-4(a), where the codified provision says 16.601(f)(1) [@CL-EST-9096; @CL-EST-1683], and the RFO text of FAR 52.216-30 says 16.601-4(b), where the codified provision says 16.601(f)(2) [@CL-EST-9097; @CL-EST-1684].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes two labor categories. One is performed by the offeror and one by a subcontractor.

Under the second provision, it states separate fixed hourly rates for each category and source, each covering wages, overhead, general and administrative expenses and profit. Under the first provision, it states whether each category's rate covers offeror labor, subcontractor labor or affiliate labor.

## Never confuse

These provisions govern what an offeror proposes. The contract-type card covers how a time-and-materials contract works once awarded. The two provisions differ by whether their titles say with or without adequate price competition [@CL-EST-1683; @CL-EST-1684].

Uncompensated overtime changes the hourly rate that is applied to proposed hours, under a separate provision covered on its own card: [Integrity of unit prices and identification of uncompensated overtime](est-unit-price-integrity-and-uncompensated-overtime.md) [@CL-EST-1697].
