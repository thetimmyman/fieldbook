## Purpose

Shows what the FAR multiyear contracting subpart implements, which supply contracts of DoD, NASA and the Coast Guard its authorities do not reach, and what a contracting officer must follow, and how option charges should be handled, once options are included in a multiyear contract.

| Applies to | Multiyear contracting under FAR subpart 17.1 [@CL-EST-3038]; for DoD, NASA and the Coast Guard, not contracts for the purchase of supplies to which title 40 of the United States Code, section 759 applies [@CL-EST-3039]; options included in a multiyear contract [@CL-EST-3040] |

Authority. The subpart implements title 41 of the United States Code, section 3903, and title 10, section 3501, and provides policy and procedures for the use of multi-year contracting [@CL-EST-3038].

Applicability. For DoD, NASA and the Coast Guard, the authorities cited in the authority section do not apply to contracts for the purchase of supplies to which title 40 of the United States Code, section 759 applies [@CL-EST-3039]. The section labels these information resource management supply contracts [@CL-EST-3039]. The sentence is written for those three agencies and for contracts for the purchase of supplies [@CL-EST-3039].

Options. Benefits may accrue by including options in a multiyear contract [@CL-EST-3040]. In that event, contracting officers must follow the requirements of subpart 17.2 [@CL-EST-3040]. Options should not include charges for plant and equipment already amortized, or other nonrecurring charges that were included in the basic contract [@CL-EST-3041].

## How it works

Work through the three sections in order. First, the authority section says what the subpart implements [@CL-EST-3038]. Second, if the buyer is DoD, NASA or the Coast Guard and the purchase is a supply contract to which the cited title 40 provision applies, the authorities cited in the authority section do not apply [@CL-EST-3039]. Third, if options are included in the multiyear contract, the duty to follow subpart 17.2 falls on the contracting officer [@CL-EST-3040].

The charge sentence is written as "should not" [@CL-EST-3041]. It does not name who applies it. It concerns charges for plant and equipment already amortized, and other nonrecurring charges that were included in the basic contract [@CL-EST-3041].

None of these sentences names a decision maker, a dollar threshold or a deadline [@CL-EST-3038; @CL-EST-3039; @CL-EST-3040; @CL-EST-3041]. They place no duty on a contractor by name. The definition of a multiyear contract and the approval conditions are on [Multiyear contracting: definition, term limit and approval conditions](../../industrialization/ramp/ind-multiyear-contracting.md), and cancellation ceilings and nonrecurring costs are on [Multiyear cancellation ceilings and nonrecurring costs](../../industrialization/ramp/ind-multiyear-cancellation-ceilings.md). The options subpart is covered on [Use of options: when options are and are not included](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-use-of-options.md), [Options in solicitations and contracts: evaluation and limits](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-options-in-solicitations-and-contracts.md) and [Exercise of options: notice, determinations and price comparison](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-exercise-of-options.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer awards a multiyear contract that includes two option years, and the contract meets the conditions that other sections set. The basic contract price of 4,000,000 includes 500,000 of nonrecurring charges. An offeror prices each option year at 600,000 for recurring costs and does not add the 500,000 again, because options should not include nonrecurring charges that were included in the basic contract [@CL-EST-3041]. The two option years add 1,200,000, so the total with options is 5,200,000. Because the contract includes options, the contracting officer follows the requirements of subpart 17.2 [@CL-EST-3040].

## Never confuse

The exclusion described reaches contracts for the purchase of supplies to which the cited title 40 provision applies, for DoD, NASA and the Coast Guard [@CL-EST-3039]. The sentence does not name services contracts or other agencies [@CL-EST-3039].

Including options in a multiyear contract does not replace one rule with another. The contracting officer follows the requirements of subpart 17.2 [@CL-EST-3040], and the sentence on plant, equipment and nonrecurring charges is a separate statement in the same section [@CL-EST-3041].
