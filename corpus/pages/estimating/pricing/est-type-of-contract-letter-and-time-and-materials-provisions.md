## Purpose

Shows what the type of contract provision tells offerors, how a letter contract is accepted and work begins, and what a time-and-materials or labor-hour offer must state about hourly rates.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1842] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-1, FAR 52.216-23 and FAR 52.216-31 [@CL-EST-1842; @CL-EST-1843; @CL-EST-1845] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.216-1 [@CL-EST-0844], RFO FAR 52.216-23 [@CL-EST-0846; @CL-EST-0847; @CL-EST-9101] and RFO FAR 52.216-31 [@CL-EST-0848; @CL-EST-0849] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1842] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that carry the type of contract provision, the letter contract clause or the time-and-materials proposal provision [@CL-EST-1842; @CL-EST-1843; @CL-EST-1845] |

In the type of contract provision, the Government contemplates award of the type of contract that the Contracting Officer inserts in the blank, resulting from the solicitation [@CL-EST-1842].

The Contractor indicates acceptance of a letter contract by signing three copies of the contract and returning them to the Contracting Officer not later than the date inserted [@CL-EST-1843]. On acceptance by both parties, the Contractor shall proceed with performance of the work, including purchase of necessary materials [@CL-EST-1844].

## How it works

Under FAR 52.216-31, the offeror must specify fixed hourly rates in its offer that include wages, overhead, general and administrative expenses, and profit [@CL-EST-1845]. The offeror must also say whether the fixed hourly rate for each labor category applies to labor performed by the offeror, subcontractors, and/or divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of the offeror under a common control [@CL-EST-1846].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO type of contract provision says the Government contemplates award of the type of contract the Contracting Officer inserts in the blank, the same as the codified provision [@CL-EST-0844; @CL-EST-1842]. The RFO also has an Alternate I of that provision, prescribed in 16.105, to be included as paragraph (a) of the basic provision: offerors may propose an alternative contract type [@CL-EST-0845]. The RFO letter contract clause has the same acceptance by signing three copies and the same start of work after acceptance by both parties [@CL-EST-0846; @CL-EST-0847; @CL-EST-1843; @CL-EST-1844]. The RFO prescription for FAR 52.216-23 says to insert the clause as prescribed in 16.603-4(b)(1) [@CL-EST-9101], where the codified prescription says to insert the clause in solicitations and contracts when a letter contract is contemplated, except that it may be omitted from letter contracts awarded on SF 26 [@CL-EST-9102]. RFO FAR 52.216-31 has the same fixed hourly rate requirement and the same statement of the labor to which the rate applies [@CL-EST-0848; @CL-EST-0849; @CL-EST-1845; @CL-EST-1846].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror answers a time-and-materials solicitation with an engineer rate of 150 per hour. The rate is built from wages, overhead, general and administrative expense and profit. The offer says the rate applies to the offeror's own staff and to an affiliate under common control, but that subcontractor labor is priced at a separate rate. On a letter contract, the Contractor signs and returns the copies by the stated date and starts work, including buying necessary materials.

## Never confuse

FAR 52.216-31 requires fixed hourly rates loaded with wages, overhead, G&A and profit [@CL-EST-1845], and FAR 52.216-30 also requires separate loaded rates for the offeror and each subcontractor and, unless exempt, bars profit for the transferring organization on services transferred between affiliates under common control [@CL-EST-1686; @CL-EST-1687]—unlike FAR 52.216-29, which has the offeror specify whether each fixed hourly rate applies to its own, subcontractor or affiliate labor [@CL-EST-1685]; see [Time-and-materials and labor-hour proposal provisions](est-time-and-materials-proposal-rate-provisions.md)—while the type-of-contract notice is preaward information rather than a binding letter-contract acceptance signed in three copies [@CL-EST-1842; @CL-EST-1843].
