## Purpose

Shows where the cost principles for contracts and subcontracts with State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments are set out, and what those principles are and are not for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2943] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.602 [@CL-EST-2943]; FAR 31.601 [@CL-EST-9199] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 31.602 [@CL-EST-3147; @CL-EST-3148] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2943] |
| Applies to | Determining the allowable costs of contracts and subcontracts with State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments [@CL-EST-2943] |

The OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII sets forth the principles for determining the allowable costs of contracts and subcontracts with State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments [@CL-EST-2943]. These principles are for cost determination and are not intended to identify the circumstances or dictate the extent of Federal and State or local participation in financing a particular contract [@CL-EST-2944].

Subpart 31.6 provides the principles for determining allowable cost of contracts and subcontracts with State, local, and federally recognized Indian tribal governments [@CL-EST-9199].

## How it works

The OMB Uniform Guidance sets forth the principles for determining the allowable costs of contracts and subcontracts with those governments [@CL-EST-2943]. The principles are for cost determination and are not intended to identify the circumstances or dictate the extent of Federal and State or local participation in financing a particular contract [@CL-EST-2944].

FAR 31.603(a) deems a contract that refers to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance in effect on the date of the contract and has the contracting officer determine allowability in accordance with it [@CL-EST-2709; @CL-EST-2710]. The exceptions and the other requirements of the subpart are on [the State, local and tribal cost principles card](est-far-cost-principles-state-local-tribal.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 31 text in place of the codified text [@CL-CMP-9350], effective immediately under Revision 1 signed on 2026-08-04 [@CL-CMP-9351]. RFO FAR 31.602 keeps the rule described above: the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII sets forth the principles for determining the allowable costs of contracts and subcontracts with those governments [@CL-EST-3147], and the principles are for cost determination and not intended to identify the circumstances or dictate the extent of Federal and State or local participation in financing a particular contract [@CL-EST-3148]. RFO FAR 31.603(a) also keeps the rule that a contract referring to subpart 31.6 for allowable costs is deemed to refer to the same guidance in effect on the date of the contract, with the contracting officer determining allowability [@CL-EST-3132; @CL-EST-3133].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Federal agency awards a cost-reimbursement contract to a State agency, and the State agency awards a subcontract to a local government for part of the work. The principles for determining allowable cost are the ones in the OMB Uniform Guidance the section names, and they cover the subcontract as well as the contract [@CL-EST-2943]. If the contract refers to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs, the contracting officer determines the allowability of costs in accordance with that guidance as in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-EST-2709; @CL-EST-2710]. How much the Federal Government and the State each finance is a separate question that the principles are not intended to answer [@CL-EST-2944].

## Never confuse

These principles are for cost determination and are not intended to identify the circumstances or dictate the extent of Federal and State or local participation in financing a particular contract [@CL-EST-2944]. Under FAR 31.603(a), a contract that refers to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs has the allowability of costs determined by the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2709; @CL-EST-2710].
