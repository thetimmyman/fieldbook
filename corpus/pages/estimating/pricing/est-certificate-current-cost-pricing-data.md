## Purpose

Shows when a contractor executes a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data and what the certificate covers.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0170] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.406-2 [@CL-EST-0170] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0170] |
| Applies to | Actions where certified cost or pricing data are required [@CL-EST-0170] |

When certified cost or pricing data are required, the contracting officer shall require the contractor to execute a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data using the format in the subsection, and must include the executed certificate in the contract file [@CL-EST-0170]. The certificate states that, to the best of the signer's knowledge and belief, the submitted cost or pricing data are accurate, complete, and current as of the date inserted, which is the date price negotiations were concluded and price agreement was reached or an earlier agreed date [@CL-EST-0175]. It also covers the cost or pricing data supporting any advance agreements and forward pricing rate agreements that are part of the proposal [@CL-EST-0171].

## How it works

The certificate applies to the data upon which the contractor's judgment or estimate was based, not to the accuracy of that judgment on future costs or projections [@CL-EST-0172]. Possession of a certificate is not a substitute for examining and analyzing the contractor's proposal [@CL-EST-0173]. If certified data are requested by the Government and submitted but an exception is later found to apply, the data are not considered certified cost or pricing data and shall not be certified in accordance with that subsection [@CL-EST-0174].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor submits certified cost or pricing data in support of a proposal and executes a certificate stating the data are accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement. Later, the contracting officer finds a commercial-product exception applies. The data are then not treated as certified cost or pricing data.

## Never confuse

The certificate certifies facts and data, not the contractor's judgment on future costs; it is a statement about the data, not a substitute for the government's examination of the proposal [@CL-EST-0172; @CL-EST-0173].
