## Question

Which threshold for certified cost or pricing data applies to a DoD prime contract proposal, and what must be known before applying it?

## Recommendation

Check the exceptions first. Then test the expected value against the DoD deviation threshold when the prime award follows the deviation's cutover date [@CL-EST-0002], and against the codified FAR threshold otherwise [@CL-EST-0001]. Confirm in the solicitation or contract which text governs, and read any older prime's flowdown before testing a subcontract.

## Evidence

- The codified FAR sets the threshold at $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018 (and $950,000 before) [@CL-EST-0001]. Unless an exception applies, certified cost or pricing data are required before the listed actions expected to exceed the current threshold or, for existing contracts, the threshold specified in the contract [@CL-EST-9458].
- For DoD, the deviation says to obtain certified cost or pricing data when a prime contract is expected to exceed $10 million and is awarded after June 30, 2026, unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-0002; @CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457].
- The deviation sets the threshold at $2.5 million when the prime contract is awarded on or before June 30, 2026, unless otherwise stated in the contract [@CL-EST-9457]. It requires an offeror for a subcontract at any tier to submit certified cost or pricing data before subcontract award if the prime contractor and each higher tier subcontractor must submit them, and the prime contract was entered into after June 30, 2026 with a subcontract price expected to exceed $10 million, or on or before June 30, 2026 with a subcontract price expected to exceed $2.5 million, unless otherwise stated in the prime contract [@CL-EST-9735; @CL-EST-9736].
- DoD lists the deviation as its current negotiation text [@S-0021-v1].

## Options

Using only the codified threshold or only the new deviation threshold was rejected because DoD applies the deviation by prime award date and contract terms. Testing by award date and contract terms matches both.

## Unknowns

- Proposals in the next two quarters that may need certified data [@Q-047].
- Owner of proposal pricing and the estimating system description [@Q-036].
- Location of executed contracts and owner of clause applicability [@Q-004].
- Whether the deviation exempts nontraditional defense contractors unless waived. Not established.

## Next action

Revisit this brief when the deviation is revised or codified, or when a proposal approaches either threshold.
