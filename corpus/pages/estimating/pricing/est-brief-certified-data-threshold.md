## Question

Which threshold for certified cost or pricing data applies to a DoD prime contract proposal, and what must be known before applying it?

## Recommendation

Check the exceptions first, then test the expected value against the deviation threshold for the prime award date: $10 million after June 30, 2026 [@CL-EST-0002], $2.5 million on or before it unless the contract says otherwise [@CL-EST-9457]. The codified FAR threshold [@CL-EST-0001] is the comparison point. Confirm in the contract which text governs, and read any older prime's flowdown before testing subcontracts.

## Evidence

- Codified FAR 15.403-4 sets the threshold at $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018 (and $950,000 before) [@CL-EST-0001]. Unless an exception applies, certified cost or pricing data are required before the listed actions expected to exceed the current threshold or, for existing contracts, the threshold specified in the contract [@CL-EST-9458].
- DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Rev 1, DFARS 215.403-3(a), effective 2026-07-22 (it supersedes the version of 2026-03-05 [@CL-EST-3177]), applies in lieu of RFO FAR 15.403-3(a), which sits at codified FAR 15.403-4 [@CL-EST-0263; @CL-EST-3213; @CL-EST-0001]. It says to obtain certified cost or pricing data when a prime contract is expected to exceed $10 million and is awarded after June 30, 2026, unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-0002; @CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457].
- For subcontracts the deviation requires an offeror at any tier to submit certified cost or pricing data before subcontract award if the prime contractor and each higher tier subcontractor must submit them, and the prime contract was entered into after June 30, 2026 with a subcontract price expected to exceed $10 million, or on or before June 30, 2026 with a subcontract price expected to exceed $2.5 million, unless otherwise stated in the prime contract [@CL-EST-9735; @CL-EST-9736].
- Unless waived, nontraditional defense contractors are not required to submit certified cost and pricing data [@CL-EST-9760].

## Options

Using only the codified or only the deviation threshold was rejected: DoD applies the deviation by prime award date and contract terms.

## Unknowns

- Proposals in the next two quarters that may need certified data [@Q-047].
- Owner of proposal pricing and the estimating system description [@Q-036].
- Location of executed contracts and owner of clause applicability [@Q-004].
- Whether a given offeror is a nontraditional defense contractor. Not established.

## Next action

Revisit this brief when the deviation is revised or codified, or a proposal nears either threshold.
