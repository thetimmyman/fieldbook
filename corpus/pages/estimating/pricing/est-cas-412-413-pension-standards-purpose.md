## Purpose

States what the Standard on composition and measurement of pension cost and the Standard on adjustment and allocation of pension cost are for, and which plans the composition Standard leaves out.

| Applies to | Determining, measuring and assigning pension cost to cost accounting periods, adjusting it for actuarial gains and losses, and allocating it to segments of an organization; the composition Standard leaves out certain employee stock ownership plans [@CL-EST-2914; @CL-EST-2915; @CL-EST-2918; @CL-EST-2919; @CL-EST-2917] |

The purpose of the composition and measurement Standard is to provide guidance for determining and measuring the components of pension cost, and the Standard establishes the basis on which pension costs shall be assigned to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2914; @CL-EST-2915]. The provisions of that Standard should enhance uniformity and consistency in accounting for pension costs and thereby increase the probability that those costs are properly allocated to cost objectives [@CL-EST-2916]. It does not cover the cost of employee stock ownership plans that meet the definition of a pension plan [@CL-EST-2917]. Those plans are considered a form of deferred compensation and are covered under 9904.415 [@CL-EST-3036].

A purpose of the adjustment and allocation Standard is to provide guidance for adjusting pension cost by measuring actuarial gains and losses and assigning those gains and losses to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2918]. It also provides the bases on which pension cost shall be allocated to segments of an organization [@CL-EST-2919]. The provisions of that Standard should enhance uniformity and consistency in accounting for pension costs [@CL-EST-2920].

## How it works

The two Standards divide the subject: one says how the components of pension cost are determined and measured and fixes the basis for assigning pension cost to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2914; @CL-EST-2915]. The other adjusts pension cost by measuring actuarial gains and losses, assigns those gains and losses to cost accounting periods, and gives the bases for allocating pension cost to segments [@CL-EST-2918; @CL-EST-2919]. Both describe their provisions as enhancing uniformity and consistency in accounting for pension costs [@CL-EST-2916; @CL-EST-2920]. The composition Standard excludes employee stock ownership plans that meet the definition of a pension plan, and the section treats them as deferred compensation covered under 9904.415 [@CL-EST-2917; @CL-EST-3036].

The bases and techniques are in the main cards.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor sponsors a defined benefit pension plan and an employee stock ownership plan that meets the definition of a pension plan. The purpose of the composition Standard is to provide guidance for determining and measuring the components of the pension plan's cost, and it establishes the basis on which that cost is assigned to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2914; @CL-EST-2915]. The cost of the employee stock ownership plan is outside that Standard, because it is considered deferred compensation covered under 9904.415 [@CL-EST-2917; @CL-EST-3036].

The pension plan shows an actuarial gain of 300,000 in one period. A purpose of the adjustment Standard is to provide guidance for measuring that gain and assigning it to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2918]. It also provides the bases on which the plan's pension cost is allocated to the contractor's segments [@CL-EST-2919].

## Never confuse

The composition Standard does not cover the cost of employee stock ownership plans that meet the definition of a pension plan [@CL-EST-2917]; such plans are considered a form of deferred compensation and are covered under 9904.415 [@CL-EST-3036]. The composition Standard provides guidance for determining and measuring the components of pension cost and establishes the basis on which pension costs shall be assigned to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2914; @CL-EST-2915], and the adjustment Standard provides guidance for adjusting pension cost by measuring actuarial gains and losses and assigning them to cost accounting periods, and provides the bases on which pension cost shall be allocated to segments [@CL-EST-2918; @CL-EST-2919]. The composition rules are in [the composition card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-412-pension-composition.md), the adjustment and allocation rules in [the adjustment and allocation card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-413-pension-adjustment-allocation.md), and the transition rules in [the effective date and transition card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-412-effective-date-transition.md).
