## Purpose

Shows who gets a copy of a proposal when it is submitted and how the cost portion is sent when the solicitation calls for electronic media.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1731; @CL-EST-1732] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.215-7011 [@CL-EST-1731]; DFARS 252.215-7012 [@CL-EST-1732] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1731; @CL-EST-1732] |
| Applies to | Offerors in solicitations that use the copies provision as prescribed at DFARS 215.408(5)(ii) [@CL-EST-1775] and the electronic media provision as prescribed at DFARS 215.408(5)(iii) [@CL-EST-1776] |

The copies provision is the one to use as prescribed at DFARS 215.408(5)(ii) [@CL-EST-1775]. Under it, when the proposal is submitted, the Offeror shall also submit one copy each to the Administrative Contracting Officer and to the Contract Auditor [@CL-EST-1731].

The electronic media provision is the one to use as prescribed at DFARS 215.408(5)(iii) [@CL-EST-1776]. Under it, the Offeror shall submit the cost portion of the proposal via the electronic media format that the solicitation inserts, such as electronic spreadsheet format or electronic mail [@CL-EST-1732].

## How it works

When the proposal is submitted, the Offeror shall also submit one copy each to the Administrative Contracting Officer and to the Contract Auditor [@CL-EST-1731]. The electronic media provision covers the cost portion of the proposal, and the media format is an insert that the solicitation fills in [@CL-EST-1732]. Spreadsheet and electronic mail are examples in the insert, and the format is whatever the solicitation inserts [@CL-EST-1732].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation includes both provisions and fills in the media format as a spreadsheet. On the day the offeror submits the proposal, it also sends one copy to the administrative contracting officer and one to the contract auditor. The cost portion goes in spreadsheet form.

## Never confuse

DFARS 252.215-7011 routes parallel proposal copies to the Administrative Contracting Officer and Contract Auditor and specifies the cost volume's electronic media format [@CL-EST-1731; @CL-EST-1732; @CL-EST-1775], rather than deciding whether certified data are required on [Data submission format and instructions](est-data-submission-format-and-instructions.md) [@CL-EST-1265] or replacing timely receipt at the designated contracting office on [Proposal submission and late proposals](est-proposal-submission-and-late-proposals.md) [@CL-EST-1369].
