## Purpose

Shows what the DoD alternate adds to the time-and-materials and labor-hour proposal provision about separate hourly rates by labor category and source.

## The rule

As prescribed in DFARS 216.601(e), the paragraph is substituted for paragraph (c) of the provision at FAR 52.216-29 [@CL-EST-1780]. The alternate carries the title Alternate A, Time-and-Materials/Labor-Hour Proposal Requirements, Other Than Commercial Acquisition With Adequate Price Competition [@CL-EST-1744]. In the substituted paragraph, the offeror must establish fixed hourly rates using separate rates for each category of labor to be performed by each subcontractor and for each category of labor to be performed by the offeror [@CL-EST-1745]. The separate rates also cover each category of labor to be transferred between divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of the offeror under a common control [@CL-EST-1746].

## How it works

The offeror must establish fixed hourly rates using separate rates for each category of labor to be performed by each subcontractor, for each category of labor to be performed by the offeror, and for each category of labor to be transferred between divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of the offeror under a common control [@CL-EST-1745; @CL-EST-1746]. As prescribed in DFARS 216.601(e), this paragraph (c) is substituted for paragraph (c) of the provision at FAR 52.216-29 [@CL-EST-1780].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror will perform one labor category itself, use a subcontractor for a second, and move a third from an affiliate under common control. It states three separate fixed hourly rates, one for each category and source, and does not blend them into one rate.

## Never confuse

This alternate is substituted for paragraph (c) of the FAR provision [@CL-EST-1780], which is covered on [Time-and-materials and labor-hour proposal provisions](est-time-and-materials-proposal-rate-provisions.md). The contract type itself is on [Time-and-materials contracts](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md), and DoD limits on using the type are on [DoD time-and-materials limitations](est-dod-time-and-materials-limitations.md).
