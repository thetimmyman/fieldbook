## Purpose

Shows what the contracting officer must do after a single offer to competitive procedures, including when cost or pricing data must be certified.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1281] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.371-3 [@CL-EST-1281]; DFARS 215.371-4 [@CL-EST-1286]; DFARS 215.371-2 [@CL-EST-1319] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1281] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions above the simplified acquisition threshold where only one offer is received under competitive procedures and resolicitation is not necessary [@CL-EST-1281] |

For acquisitions that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, if only one offer is received when competitive procedures were used and it is not necessary to resolicit under the cross-referenced paragraph, the contracting officer shall comply with the listed steps [@CL-EST-1281]. Except as provided in sections 215.371-4 and 215.371-5, if only one offer is received when competitive procedures were used and the solicitation allowed fewer than 30 days for receipt of proposals, the contracting officer shall carry out the listed duties [@CL-EST-1319]. One of those duties is to resolicit, allowing an additional period of at least 30 days for receipt of proposals [@CL-EST-1320].

The first listed step applies when no additional cost or pricing data are required to determine through cost or price analysis that the offered price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-1305]. In that case, the contracting officer shall require that any cost or pricing data provided in the proposal be certified if the acquisition exceeds the certified cost or pricing data threshold and no exception at FAR 15.403-1(b)(2) through (5) applies [@CL-EST-1305; @CL-EST-1282].

Otherwise, the contracting officer shall obtain additional cost or pricing data to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1283]. The cost or pricing data shall be certified if the acquisition exceeds the certified cost or pricing data threshold and no exception at FAR 15.403-1(b)(2) through (5) applies [@CL-EST-1283; @CL-EST-1284].

## How it works

Where additional cost or pricing data are required to determine through cost or price analysis that the offered price is fair and reasonable, a proposal team should expect the contracting officer to obtain additional cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-1305; @CL-EST-1283]. That data shall be certified where the acquisition exceeds the certified cost or pricing data threshold and an exception to the requirement for certified cost or pricing data at FAR 15.403-1(b)(2) through (5) does not apply [@CL-EST-1284]. Under the codified FAR, unless an exception applies, certified cost or pricing data are required before accomplishing any of the listed actions expected to exceed the current threshold or, in the case of existing contracts, the threshold specified in the contract [@CL-EST-9458]; the threshold is $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018 [@CL-EST-0001]. Under the DoD class deviation, certified cost or pricing data are to be obtained when a prime contract is expected to exceed $10 million and is awarded after June 30, 2026 [@CL-EST-0002], unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457]. The deviation threshold is $2.5 million when the prime contract is awarded on or before June 30, 2026, unless otherwise stated in the contract [@CL-EST-9457]. If the contracting officer is still unable to determine that the offered price is fair and reasonable, the contracting officer shall enter into negotiations with the offeror to establish a fair and reasonable price, and the negotiated price should not exceed the offered price [@CL-EST-1285]. If the contracting officer is unable to negotiate a fair and reasonable price, see FAR 15.405(d) [@CL-EST-1306]. The requirements at DFARS 215.371-2, which cover resolicitation after one offer [@CL-EST-1319; @CL-EST-1320], do not apply to listed acquisitions, including acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-1286], acquisitions, as determined by the head of the contracting activity, in support of contingency or humanitarian or peacekeeping operations [@CL-EST-1286], acquisitions, as determined by the head of the contracting activity, to facilitate defense against or recovery from cyber, nuclear, biological, chemical, or radiological attack, to facilitate the provision of international disaster assistance, or to support response to an emergency or major disaster [@CL-EST-9656], and acquisitions of commercial products and commercial services using FAR part 12 procedures [@CL-EST-1318]. The applicability of a listed exception does not eliminate the need for the contracting officer to seek maximum practicable competition and to ensure a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1287].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A competitive solicitation for a 14,000,000 DoD supply contract awarded after the current DoD threshold took effect draws one offer, and the solicitation allowed 45 days for proposals, so resolicitation is not required. Analysis of the offer alone cannot show that the price is fair and reasonable, so the contracting officer obtains additional cost or pricing data. The acquisition is above the certified data threshold and no listed exception applies, so the data must be certified. If the contracting officer is still unable to find the price fair and reasonable, the contracting officer negotiates, and the negotiated price should not exceed the offered price.

## Never confuse

This card covers the contracting officer's pricing steps after one offer. The offeror's own agreement to submit data is the provision on [Only One Offer provision](est-only-one-offer.md), and the general certification rules are on [Certified cost or pricing data threshold](est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md). The exceptions at DFARS 215.371-4(a) relieve the requirements at DFARS 215.371-2 [@CL-EST-1286], and the steps on this card are at DFARS 215.371-3 [@CL-EST-1281]. The exceptions to certified data are on [DoD exceptions, waivers and limits](est-dod-certified-data-exceptions-and-waivers.md), and DFARS 215.403-1(c)(1)(B) says that if only one offer is received in response to a competitive solicitation, see 215.371-3 [@CL-EST-1321]. The data the contracting officer asks for is on [Pricing policy and the order of preference for data](est-pricing-policy-data-preference.md), and the negotiation file is on [Price negotiation and the price negotiation memorandum](est-price-negotiation-documentation.md).
