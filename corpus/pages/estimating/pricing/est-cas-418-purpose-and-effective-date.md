## Purpose

States the purpose and the start rules of the Standard on allocation of direct and indirect costs. The Standard's requirements are on the [direct and indirect costs card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-418-direct-indirect.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-EST-2902] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.418-20 and 9904.418-63 [@CL-EST-2902; @CL-EST-2904] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | Contractors receiving a contract to which the Standard is applicable, including contractors with prior full-coverage CAS contracts [@CL-EST-2904; @CL-EST-2905] |

The purpose of the Standard is to provide for consistent determination of direct and indirect costs and criteria for the accumulation of indirect costs, including service center and overhead costs, in indirect cost pools [@CL-EST-2902]. It also provides guidance on selecting allocation measures based on the beneficial or causal relationship between an indirect cost pool and cost objectives [@CL-EST-2903]. Consistent application of the criteria and guidance will improve the classification of costs as direct and indirect and the allocation of indirect costs [@CL-EST-2974].

The Standard is effective as of 17 April 1992, and contractors with prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage shall continue its applicability upon receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2904]. For contractors with no previous contracts subject to the Standard, it shall be applied beginning with the contractor's second full fiscal year beginning after the receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2905].

## How it works

The purpose section states these aims: consistent determination of direct and indirect costs, criteria for accumulating indirect costs in pools, and guidance on selecting allocation measures from the beneficial or causal relationship between a pool and cost objectives [@CL-EST-2902; @CL-EST-2903]. The start rule has two cases: contractors with prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage shall continue the Standard's applicability upon receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2904], and for contractors with no previous contracts subject to the Standard it shall be applied beginning with the contractor's second full fiscal year beginning after that receipt [@CL-EST-2905]. The requirements themselves are on the main card.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A segment keeps 2,000,000 of factory occupancy cost in a single pool and allocates it on floor space. Whether floor space reflects the beneficial or causal relationship between that pool and the cost objectives is the question the Standard's guidance on allocation measures addresses [@CL-EST-2903].

A contractor with a calendar fiscal year has never held a contract subject to the Standard and receives one in September of Year 1. Its first full fiscal year beginning after receipt starts in January of Year 2 and its second in January of Year 3, so it applies the Standard from January of Year 3 [@CL-EST-2905]. A second contractor already holds prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage and continues the Standard's applicability when it receives an applicable contract [@CL-EST-2904].

## Never confuse

The purpose section states the aims of consistent determination of direct and indirect costs, criteria for the accumulation of indirect costs in indirect cost pools, and guidance on the selection of allocation measures [@CL-EST-2902; @CL-EST-2903]. For contractors with no previous contracts subject to this Standard, it is applied beginning with the contractor's second full fiscal year beginning after the receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2905], and the capital assets under construction Standard is applied beginning with the contractor's next full fiscal year beginning after that receipt [@CL-EST-2901].
