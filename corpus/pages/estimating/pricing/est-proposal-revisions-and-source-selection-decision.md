## Purpose

Shows what happens to proposal revisions after discussions, who may request or allow them, when final proposal revisions are requested, who fixes the common cut-off date, and what the source selection authority must base, decide and document.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2737] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.307 and FAR 15.308 [@CL-EST-2737; @CL-EST-2742] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.204-2 [@CL-EST-3404]; RFO FAR 15.205 [@CL-EST-3410]; RFO FAR 15.001 [@CL-EST-3409] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2737] |
| Applies to | Offerors in the competitive range, the contracting officer, and the source selection authority [@CL-EST-2737; @CL-EST-2739; @CL-EST-2742] |

If an offeror's proposal is eliminated or otherwise removed from the competitive range, no further revisions to that offeror's proposal shall be accepted or considered [@CL-EST-2737]. The contracting officer may request or allow proposal revisions to clarify and document understandings reached during negotiations [@CL-EST-2738]. At the conclusion of discussions, each offeror still in the competitive range shall be given an opportunity to submit a final proposal revision [@CL-EST-2739]. The contracting officer is required to establish a common cut-off date only for receipt of final proposal revisions [@CL-EST-2740]. Requests for final proposal revisions shall advise offerors that the final proposal revisions shall be in writing and that the Government intends to make award without obtaining further revisions [@CL-EST-2741].

The source selection authority's decision shall be based on a comparative assessment of proposals against all source selection criteria in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2742]. While the source selection authority may use reports and analyses prepared by others, the source selection decision shall represent the authority's independent judgment [@CL-EST-2743]. The decision shall be documented, and the documentation shall include the rationale for any business judgments and tradeoffs made or relied on by the authority, including benefits associated with additional costs [@CL-EST-2744]. Although the rationale must be documented, the documentation need not quantify the tradeoffs that led to the decision [@CL-EST-2745].

## How it works

The contracting officer may request or allow proposal revisions to clarify and document understandings reached during negotiations [@CL-EST-2738]. For a final proposal revision, each offeror still in the competitive range shall be given the opportunity at the conclusion of discussions [@CL-EST-2739]. The contracting officer is required to establish a common cut-off date only for receipt of final proposal revisions [@CL-EST-2740]. The request shall advise offerors that their final revisions shall be in writing and that the Government intends to make award without obtaining further revisions [@CL-EST-2741].

The source selection authority's decision shall be based on a comparative assessment of proposals against all source selection criteria in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2742]. The contracting officer is designated as the source selection authority, unless the agency head appoints another individual for a particular acquisition or group of acquisitions [@CL-EST-1376]. See [Source selection responsibilities and evaluation](est-source-selection-responsibilities-and-evaluation.md). The authority may rely on reports and analyses prepared by others, but the decision shall be the authority's own independent judgment, documented with its rationale [@CL-EST-2743; @CL-EST-2744].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.204-2(c) says a proposal must be eliminated from consideration for award when the contracting officer determines that it should no longer be included in the competitive range [@CL-EST-3404], and no further revisions to an eliminated proposal can be accepted or considered, where the codified text says no further revisions shall be accepted or considered and speaks of a proposal eliminated or otherwise removed from the competitive range [@CL-EST-2737; @CL-EST-3405]. RFO FAR 15.001 defines a proposal revision as a change to material elements of a proposal made after the RFP closing date, at the request of or as allowed by a contracting officer, as the result of negotiations [@CL-EST-3409]. RFO FAR 15.204-2(d) speaks of a proposal revision where the codified text speaks of a final proposal revision: when negotiations with an offeror are finished, and that offeror has not been eliminated from the competitive range, contracting officers must provide the offeror with an opportunity to submit a proposal revision [@CL-EST-2739; @CL-EST-3406], and a notice requiring the proposal revision in writing and stating that the Government intends to make award without obtaining further revisions [@CL-EST-2741; @CL-EST-3407]. That paragraph states the timing as an equal amount of time for each offeror with a proposal still within the competitive range [@CL-EST-2740; @CL-EST-3408]. RFO FAR 15.205 keeps the requirement that the source selection decision be based on a comparative assessment of proposals against all source selection criteria in the RFP [@CL-EST-2742; @CL-EST-3411], and that it be documented with the rationale for any business judgments and tradeoffs made or relied on, including benefits associated with additional costs [@CL-EST-2744; @CL-EST-3412], without a need to quantify the tradeoffs [@CL-EST-2745; @CL-EST-3413]. It states that the decision must represent the source selection authority's independent judgement [@CL-EST-3410].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Three offerors are in the competitive range. When discussions conclude, each of the three is given the opportunity to submit a final proposal revision [@CL-EST-2739]. The contracting officer sets one common cut-off date, ten days after the request, for receipt of final proposal revisions [@CL-EST-2740]. The request states that the revisions shall be in writing and that the Government intends to make award without obtaining further revisions [@CL-EST-2741].

Before the cut-off date, one offeror is removed from the competitive range. Any further revision it sends is not accepted or considered [@CL-EST-2737]. The other two submit written final revisions by the cut-off date.

The source selection authority compares the two proposals against all source selection criteria in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2742]. The authority reads the evaluation team's reports and may use them, but the decision it signs must represent its own independent judgment, not simply the team's recommendation [@CL-EST-2743]. It selects the proposal priced 200,000 higher and documents the rationale, including the benefits associated with the additional cost [@CL-EST-2744]. The documentation does not need to put a dollar value on each tradeoff [@CL-EST-2745].

## Never confuse

The contracting officer is required to establish it only for receipt of final proposal revisions [@CL-EST-2740].

The contracting officer may request or allow proposal revisions to clarify and document understandings reached during negotiations [@CL-EST-2738], while each offeror still in the competitive range shall be given an opportunity to submit a final proposal revision at the conclusion of discussions [@CL-EST-2739].

The source selection authority may use reports and analyses prepared by others, but the decision shall represent its own independent judgment [@CL-EST-2743].

Codified FAR 15.307(b) gives each offeror still in the competitive range an opportunity to submit a final proposal revision at the conclusion of discussions [@CL-EST-2739], while RFO FAR 15.204-2(d)(1) provides the opportunity when negotiations with that offeror are finished and it has not been eliminated from the competitive range [@CL-EST-3406]. Codified FAR 15.307(b) requires a common cut-off date only for receipt of final proposal revisions [@CL-EST-2740], while RFO FAR 15.204-2(d)(2) requires each offeror with a proposal still within the competitive range to be given an equal amount of time to submit its proposal revision [@CL-EST-3408].
