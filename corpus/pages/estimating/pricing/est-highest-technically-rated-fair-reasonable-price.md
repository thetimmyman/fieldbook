## Purpose

Explains the source-selection approach in which the Government decides in advance not to trade price against non-price factors, and awards to the highest technically rated responsible offeror whose price is fair and reasonable, and what the solicitation must tell offerors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | RFO FAR model text [@CL-EST-2530] |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.103-3(a)(1) [@CL-EST-2530; @CL-EST-2531]; RFO FAR 15.103-3(a)(2) [@CL-EST-2532; @CL-EST-2533]; RFO FAR 15.103-3(a)(3) [@CL-EST-2534; @CL-EST-2535; @CL-EST-2536]; RFO FAR 15.103-3(b) [@CL-EST-2537] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Deviation text [@CL-EST-2530] |
| Applies to | Source selections in which the Government has determined in advance that tradeoffs would not be advantageous [@CL-EST-2530] |

The approach is appropriate when the Government determines in advance that it would not be advantageous to consider tradeoffs between cost or price and non-cost or non-price factors [@CL-EST-2530]. The same sentence adds that the acquisition warrants paying any fair and reasonable price for the highest quality performance [@CL-EST-2531]. All proposals are evaluated on the non-price factors outlined in the solicitation, and the source selection authority determines the highest technically rated proposal [@CL-EST-2532]. The price of that proposal is evaluated to determine if it is fair and reasonable in accordance with subpart 15.4 [@CL-EST-2533]. If the price is fair and reasonable, the proposal is selected for award [@CL-EST-2534]. If it is not, the source selection authority must determine the next highest technically rated proposal and evaluate whether that price is fair and reasonable in accordance with subpart 15.4 [@CL-EST-2535]. The process continues until a contract is awarded to the highest technically rated responsible offeror with a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-2536]. When using this approach, the request for proposals (RFP) must advise offerors that the Government will not consider tradeoffs between cost or price and non-cost or non-price factors, and that the highest technically rated proposal will be selected for award if it offers a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-2537].

## How it works

The Government makes the advance determination that tradeoffs would not be advantageous. The source selection authority determines the highest technically rated proposal and, if a price fails, the next highest technically rated proposal [@CL-EST-2532; @CL-EST-2535]. The price of the highest technically rated proposal is evaluated to determine if it is fair and reasonable in accordance with subpart 15.4 [@CL-EST-2533].

Proposals are evaluated on the non-price factors the solicitation outlines [@CL-EST-2532]. Price is then evaluated for the highest technically rated proposal and, if that price is not fair and reasonable, for the next highest technically rated proposal, and so on while the process continues [@CL-EST-2533; @CL-EST-2535; @CL-EST-2536]. Award goes to the highest technically rated responsible offeror with a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-2536].

When this approach is used, the RFP must advise offerors that tradeoffs will not be considered and that the highest technically rated proposal will be selected if it offers a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-2537].

DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 15 in place of the codified text [@CL-EST-3176], under Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Revision 1, which took effect immediately and supersedes the version issued on March 5, 2026 [@CL-EST-3177].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A service solicitation tells offerors that tradeoffs will not be considered and that the highest technically rated proposal will be selected if its price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-2537]. Three proposals are evaluated on the non-price factors, and the source selection authority determines the highest technically rated one [@CL-EST-2532]. It is priced at 1,200,000. Evaluation of its price in accordance with subpart 15.4 finds that amount fair and reasonable, so the proposal is selected for award [@CL-EST-2533; @CL-EST-2534]. In a second competition the highest technically rated proposal is priced at 2,400,000, and its price is found not fair and reasonable. The source selection authority must then determine the next highest technically rated proposal and evaluate whether its price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-2535]. The contract goes to the highest technically rated responsible offeror with a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-2536].

## Never confuse

In this approach the RFP says that tradeoffs will not be considered [@CL-EST-2537], and the price of the highest technically rated proposal is evaluated to determine if it is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-2533]. Other source selection approaches are on the linked cards.
