## Purpose

Shows the exception route an offeror or Contractor can use instead of certified data, what must be submitted when no exception is granted, and the format and certificate that go with it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1819] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-20 and FAR 52.215-21 [@CL-EST-1819; @CL-EST-1824] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1819] |
| Applies to | Offerors under the solicitation provision, and Contractors with modifications above the certified data threshold [@CL-EST-1819; @CL-EST-1824] |

In lieu of submitting certified cost or pricing data, offerors may submit a written request for exception by submitting the information the provision describes [@CL-EST-1819]. The Contracting Officer may require additional supporting information, but only to the extent necessary to determine whether an exception should be granted and whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-1887]. That information includes identification of the law or regulation establishing the price offered, with a copy of the controlling document attached if the price is controlled under law by periodic rulings, reviews or similar actions of a governmental body, unless it was previously submitted to the contracting office [@CL-EST-1864]. For the commercial product and commercial service exception, the offeror shall submit at a minimum information on prices at which the same or similar items were previously sold in the commercial market that is adequate for evaluating the reasonableness of the price [@CL-EST-1820]. The offeror grants the Contracting Officer or an authorized representative the right to examine, at any time before award, books, records, documents or other directly pertinent records to verify any request for an exception and the reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-1865]. For items priced using catalog or market prices, or law or regulation, the right to examine records does not extend to cost or profit information or other data relevant solely to the offeror's determination of the prices to be offered in the catalog or marketplace [@CL-EST-1885].

For modifications under the contract with price adjustments expected to exceed the certified data threshold on the date of agreement on price or award, whichever is later, the Contractor may submit a written request for exception [@CL-EST-1824]. The modifications clause likewise calls for identification of the law or regulation establishing the price [@CL-EST-1866], and the Contractor grants the same right to examine records before award [@CL-EST-1867]. Under the modifications clause the Contracting Officer may likewise require additional supporting information only to the extent necessary to determine whether an exception should be granted and whether the price is fair and reasonable [@CL-EST-1888], and for items priced using catalog or market prices, or law or regulation, the right to examine records does not extend to cost or profit information or other data relevant solely to the Contractor's determination of the prices to be offered in the catalog or marketplace [@CL-EST-1886]. If the threshold specified in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) is adjusted for inflation, the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-1889]. An offeror not granted an exception from the certified data requirement shall prepare and submit certified cost or pricing data, data other than certified cost or pricing data, and supporting attachments [@CL-EST-1822]. The provision requires a Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data as soon as practicable after agreement on price, but before contract award except for unpriced actions such as letter contracts [@CL-EST-1823].

## How it works

The offeror first decides whether to ask for an exception [@CL-EST-1819]. If none is granted, the data go in the FAR table format, which is mandatory unless the Contracting Officer and the Contractor agree to a different format and change the clause to use Alternate I [@CL-EST-1821]. The certificate follows once price is agreed [@CL-EST-1823]. Alternate II adds a paragraph requiring that, when the proposal is submitted, the offeror also submit one copy each to the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Contract Auditor [@CL-EST-1825]. The modifications clause opens the same exception route for price adjustments above the threshold [@CL-EST-1824].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror proposes a catalog item at 80,000 and asks for the commercial exception, attaching its catalog pages and recent sales at similar quantities. It also agrees that the Contracting Officer may examine directly pertinent records before award to verify the request and the price, but that this access does not reach cost or profit data relevant solely to its own determination of the catalog price. The Contracting Officer asks for no additional supporting information beyond what is necessary to decide the exception and the fairness of the price. The Contracting Officer grants the exception, so no certified data or certificate is needed. A second proposal for custom work gets no exception. That offeror submits certified data in the FAR table format, copies the Administrative Contracting Officer and the Contract Auditor if Alternate II applies, and signs the certificate after price is agreed.

## Never confuse

Certified cost or pricing data and data other than certified cost or pricing data are named separately in the submission requirement [@CL-EST-1822]. The provision speaks to offerors [@CL-EST-1819], while the modifications clause speaks to the Contractor on awarded work [@CL-EST-1824]. Neither is the price reduction clause: that clause reduces a modification price after the fact when certified data prove defective, and it is covered in [Price reduction for defective certified data](est-price-reduction-and-subcontractor-data-modification-clauses.md). The general exceptions are covered in [Exceptions to the requirement for certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-data-exceptions.md), format rules are in [Instructions for submitting cost or pricing data](est-data-submission-format-and-instructions.md), and the general proposal instructions are in [Instructions to offerors in competitive acquisitions](est-instructions-to-offerors-competitive-acquisition.md).
