## Purpose

Shows which DoD provisions and clauses go in negotiated solicitations and contracts, which cases and alternates apply, and what triggers each one.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1517] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.408 [@CL-EST-1517] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1517] |
| Applies to | DoD solicitations and contracts that meet the trigger in each paragraph [@CL-EST-1517; @CL-EST-1520] |

The cost estimating system clause at 252.215-7002 goes in all solicitations and contracts to be awarded on the basis of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-1517]. When contracting with the Canadian Commercial Corporation, the provision at 252.215-7003 is used in lieu of 252.215-7010 in the cases the paragraph lists [@CL-EST-1518]. One of the listed alternatives is a sole source acquisition from the Canadian Commercial Corporation that is cost-reimbursement with a contract value expected to exceed 700000 dollars, or fixed-price with a contract value expected to exceed 500 million dollars [@CL-EST-1616]. A second listed case is a sole source acquisition from the Canadian Commercial Corporation that does not meet those thresholds, if approval is obtained as required at the section the paragraph cites [@CL-EST-1618]. The paragraph also says not to use 252.225-7003 in lieu of 252.215-7010 in competitive acquisitions, as the paragraph prints that number [@CL-EST-9646].

The clause at 252.215-7004 for the Canadian Commercial Corporation is used in a solicitation in the cases the paragraph lists [@CL-EST-1519]. One of the listed alternatives is a sole source acquisition from the Canadian Commercial Corporation and resultant contract that is cost-reimbursement with a contract value expected to exceed 700000 dollars, or fixed-price with a contract value expected to exceed 500 million dollars [@CL-EST-1617]. Another of the listed alternatives is a sole source acquisition from the Canadian Commercial Corporation and resultant contract that does not meet those thresholds, if approval is obtained as required at the section the paragraph cites [@CL-EST-9647].

The provision at 252.215-7008 on only one offer is used in competitive solicitations that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold, including solicitations using FAR part 12 procedures for commercial products and commercial services [@CL-EST-1520]. When the solicitation requires certified cost or pricing data, the contracting officer should include the proposal adequacy checklist at 252.215-7009 [@CL-EST-1521].

When reasonably certain that certified data or data other than certified data will be required, or when using the only one offer provision, the basic or an alternate of the provision at 252.215-7010 is used [@CL-EST-1522], in lieu of the provision at FAR 52.215-20 [@CL-EST-1619]. The basic provision is used when submission of certified data is required to be in the FAR Table 15-2 format, or if it is anticipated, at the time of solicitation, that submission of certified data may not be required [@CL-EST-1620]. Alternate I is used to specify a format for certified data other than the format required by that table [@CL-EST-1621]. The provision at 252.215-7011 is used with 252.215-7010 when copies of the proposal are to be sent to the ACO and contract auditor [@CL-EST-1523], and the provision at 252.215-7012 is used with 252.215-7010 when submission via electronic media is required [@CL-EST-1524].

The provision at 252.215-7013 on nontraditional defense contractors is used in all solicitations [@CL-EST-1525]. The clause at 252.215-7014 is used in solicitations and contracts that contain the provision at 252.215-7010 [@CL-EST-1526], when it is reasonably certain that the contract is expected to include costs associated with an indirect offset and that the submission of certified cost or pricing data or data other than certified cost or pricing data will be required [@CL-EST-1527]. The clause at 252.215-7015 on program should-cost review is used in all solicitations and contracts for the development or production of a major weapon system, as defined in 234.7001 [@CL-EST-1528].

## How it works

Some entries apply to every solicitation, such as the nontraditional defense contractor provision [@CL-EST-1525]. Others depend on a trigger: certified data for the cost estimating system clause [@CL-EST-1517], the Canadian Commercial Corporation cases the paragraph lists for its provision and clause [@CL-EST-1518; @CL-EST-1519], or the development or production of a major weapon system for the program should-cost clause [@CL-EST-1528]. The indirect offset clause needs both a solicitation that contains 252.215-7010 and the further stated conditions [@CL-EST-1526; @CL-EST-1527]. What the basic and alternate provisions at 252.215-7010 require is on [DoD clause on certified cost or pricing data and data other than certified](est-dod-certified-data-clause.md) [@CL-EST-1522].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD solicitation for production of a major weapon system expects a contract worth 40 million and will need certified data. It carries the cost estimating system clause, the program should-cost clause and the nontraditional defense contractor provision. The contracting officer also includes the proposal adequacy checklist. The answer: the officer should include the checklist because certified data are required, and the other three apply for the reasons stated in the section.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer should include the proposal adequacy checklist in a solicitation that requires the submission of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-1521]. What the checklist asks of the offeror is on [Proposal adequacy checklist provision](est-dod-proposal-adequacy-checklist-provision.md), and the auditor's use of the checklist is on [Proposal adequacy, the audit opinion and denial of access](est-proposal-adequacy-audit-opinion.md). The program should-cost clause at 252.215-7015 is prescribed for solicitations and contracts for the development or production of a major weapon system [@CL-EST-1528]; how a review runs is on [Should-cost review](est-should-cost-review.md) and, for the DoD section, on [DoD should-cost review](est-dod-program-should-cost-review.md). The indirect offset clause is on [Foreign military sales offset exception and postaward debriefing notice](est-dod-fms-offset-exception-and-postaward-debrief-notice.md). The only one offer provision is detailed on [Only one offer](est-only-one-offer.md). The FAR provisions that these DoD provisions replace or add to are on [FAR solicitation provisions and contract clauses for cost or pricing data and price reduction](est-far-solicitation-provisions-cost-pricing-data.md).
