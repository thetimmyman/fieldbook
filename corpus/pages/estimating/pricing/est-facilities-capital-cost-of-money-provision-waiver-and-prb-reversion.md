## Purpose

Shows when facilities capital cost of money is allowable on a contemplated contract, what the waiver clause does when the cost is not proposed, and what the Contractor must do when postretirement benefit plan assets revert.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1814] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-16, FAR 52.215-17 and FAR 52.215-18 [@CL-EST-1814; @CL-EST-1816; @CL-EST-1817] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1814] |
| Applies to | Prospective Contractors who propose the cost, and contracts that carry the waiver clause or the postretirement benefits clause [@CL-EST-1814; @CL-EST-1815; @CL-EST-1817] |

Facilities capital cost of money will be an allowable cost under the contemplated contract if the allowability criteria are met, and one criterion requires the prospective Contractor to propose it in its offer [@CL-EST-1814]. If the prospective Contractor does not propose it, the resulting contract will include the clause Waiver of Facilities Capital Cost of Money [@CL-EST-1815]. Under that clause the cost is unallowable under the contract [@CL-EST-1816].

For postretirement benefit plans, the Contractor shall promptly notify the Contracting Officer in writing when it determines that it will terminate or reduce the benefits of a plan [@CL-EST-1817]. If fund assets revert or inure to the Contractor, or are constructively received by it, the Contractor shall make a refund or give a credit to the Government for its equitable share [@CL-EST-1818]. When determining or agreeing on the method for recovery of that share, the parties should consider cost reduction, amortizing the credit over a number of years with appropriate interest, cash refund, or some other agreed upon method [@CL-EST-1868]. Should the parties be unable to agree on the method through good faith negotiations, the Contracting Officer shall designate the method of recovery [@CL-EST-1883]. The Contractor shall insert the substance of the postretirement benefits clause in all subcontracts that meet the applicability requirements of FAR 15.408(j) [@CL-EST-1884].

## How it works

The decision is made at proposal time, because proposing the cost in the offer is one of the allowability criteria [@CL-EST-1814]. Leaving it out brings in the waiver clause, and the cost is then unallowable under that contract [@CL-EST-1815; @CL-EST-1816]. The postretirement benefits clause works after award: written notice of a plan termination or benefit cut comes first [@CL-EST-1817], and a refund or credit of the Government's equitable share follows if fund assets revert [@CL-EST-1818]. The recovery method is a matter for the parties to consider and agree, using the methods the clause lists [@CL-EST-1868], and the Contracting Officer designates it if they cannot agree through good faith negotiations [@CL-EST-1883].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A prospective Contractor leaves facilities capital cost of money out of its offer for a cost-type contract. The contract then includes the waiver clause, so any such cost later claimed on the contract is unallowable. Separately, a plan termination returns 500,000 of fund assets to the Contractor. If the Government's equitable share is taken as 40 percent, the Contractor refunds or credits 200,000. The parties could agree to take it as a cash refund or as a credit spread over several years with interest. If they cannot agree through good faith negotiations, the Contracting Officer designates the method of recovery. The Contractor also puts the substance of the clause into each subcontract that meets the applicability requirements of FAR 15.408(j).

## Never confuse

Waiving the cost is a decision about allowability on one contract, made by not proposing it [@CL-EST-1816]. How the amount is measured is a different question, covered in [Cost of money as an imputed cost](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cost-of-money.md) and [Facilities capital cost of money under the cost accounting standard](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-414-facilities-capital-cost-of-money.md). The profit factor for facilities capital employed is a separate DoD profit topic, covered in [DoD weighted guidelines: facilities capital employed](est-weighted-guidelines-facilities-capital.md). The reversion rule here is about postretirement benefit plans other than pensions; pension adjustments and asset reversions are on [Pension adjustments, asset reversions and notification of ownership changes](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-pension-adjustment-and-ownership-change-notices.md). The offer in which the cost must be proposed is governed by the competitive instructions in [Instructions to offerors in competitive acquisitions](est-instructions-to-offerors-competitive-acquisition.md).
