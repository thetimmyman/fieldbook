## Purpose

Shows what the English language and United States currency provisions require of an offer and what happens to an offer that does not comply.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2181; @CL-EST-2182] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-34 [@CL-EST-2181]; FAR 52.214-35 [@CL-EST-2182] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.214-34 [@CL-EST-0368]; RFO FAR 52.214-35 [@CL-EST-0369] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2181; @CL-EST-2182] |
| Applies to | Offers submitted in response to a solicitation that includes the provision [@CL-EST-2181; @CL-EST-2182] |

Offers submitted in response to the solicitation shall be in the English language, and offers received in other than English shall be rejected [@CL-EST-2181].

Offers submitted in response to the solicitation shall be in terms of United States dollars, and offers received in other than United States dollars shall be rejected [@CL-EST-2182].

## How it works

Each provision sets one requirement and one effect. The language provision requires English and the currency provision requires United States dollars [@CL-EST-2181; @CL-EST-2182]. Both state the same effect, which is rejection of an offer received in other than English or in other than United States dollars [@CL-EST-2181; @CL-EST-2182]. An offer therefore has to meet both requirements when both provisions are in the solicitation.

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.214-34 keeps the rule that offers submitted in response to the solicitation shall be in the English language and that offers received in other than English shall be rejected [@CL-EST-0368; @CL-EST-2181]. RFO FAR 52.214-35 keeps the rule that offers shall be in terms of United States dollars and that offers received in other than United States dollars shall be rejected [@CL-EST-0369; @CL-EST-2182].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation includes both provisions. Offeror A submits an offer written in English with every price in United States dollars. It meets both requirements.

Offeror B submits an offer written in English with prices in another currency. It is received in other than United States dollars, so it is rejected.

Offeror C submits an offer written in another language with prices in United States dollars. It is received in other than English, so it is rejected.

## Never confuse

The two provisions are separate. An offer in English that is priced in another currency still fails the currency provision, and an offer in dollars written in another language still fails the language provision [@CL-EST-2181; @CL-EST-2182].

Each provision says that an offer received in the other language or currency shall be rejected [@CL-EST-2181; @CL-EST-2182]. Signature and preparation of a bid are covered on [Preparing and signing a sealed bid](est-bid-preparation-and-signature.md).

## Company calibration

Confirm who checks language and currency on each offer before it is released, and what review evidence is kept [@Q-012].
