## Purpose

Shows when the Contractor must obtain certified cost or pricing data from a subcontractor, and how that data is certified and flowed down.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0401] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-12 [@CL-EST-0401] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-12 [@CL-EST-0620]; RFO FAR 52.215-12 Alternate I [@CL-EST-0628] |
| Edition date | JUN 2020 [@CL-EST-9819] |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0401] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold, and subcontract modifications whose pricing adjustment is expected to exceed it [@CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408] |

Before awarding a subcontract expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later, or before pricing a subcontract modification whose pricing adjustment is expected to exceed it, the Contractor must require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408], unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) applies [@CL-EST-0601]. The Contractor must require the subcontractor to certify, in substantially the form prescribed in FAR 15.406-2, that to the best of its knowledge and belief the data submitted under paragraph (a) were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price [@CL-EST-0402].

In each subcontract that, when entered into, exceeds the threshold, the Contractor must insert either (1) the substance of this clause, including paragraph (c), if paragraph (a) requires submission of certified cost or pricing data for the subcontract [@CL-EST-0403; @CL-EST-0603], or (2) the substance of the clause at FAR 52.215-13, Subcontractor Certified Cost or Pricing Data—Modifications [@CL-EST-0602].

## How it works

Before awarding a subcontract expected to exceed the threshold, or before pricing a modification whose pricing adjustment is expected to exceed it, the Contractor requires the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data, unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) applies [@CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408; @CL-EST-0601]. The Contractor must also require the subcontractor to certify the data as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price [@CL-EST-0402]. The expectation that a subcontract will exceed the threshold is measured on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-0408]. If the threshold is adjusted for inflation, the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-0404]. Alternate I substitutes a paragraph (a) under which, unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1 applies, the Contractor must require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data in the cases listed next [@CL-EST-0600]. It then gives alternative items: modifying a subcontract awarded before July 1, 2018, when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $950,000 [@CL-EST-0405; @CL-EST-9708]; or awarding a subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million on or after July 1, 2018, or modifying a subcontract awarded on or after that date when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $2.5 million [@CL-EST-0406].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO paragraph (a) applies before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed the threshold for submission of certified cost or pricing data, measured on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later, and points to the threshold in FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0620], where the codified paragraph points to FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-0408]. It requires the Contractor, before pricing any subcontract modification involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed that threshold, to require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0621] [@CL-EST-0407], unless an exception under FAR 15.403-2 applies, where the codified paragraph names FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-0622] [@CL-EST-0601]. If the threshold is adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR part 1, the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-0623]; the codified paragraph names FAR 1.109(a) for the adjustment and FAR 1.109(d) for the changed threshold [@CL-EST-0404]. The RFO paragraph (b) says the subcontractor is to certify, in substantially the form prescribed in FAR 15.403-4, that to the best of its knowledge and belief the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price of the subcontract or subcontract modification [@CL-EST-0624], where the codified paragraph names FAR 15.406-2 for the form [@CL-EST-0402]. In paragraph (c) the RFO text keeps the two flow-down options, the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), where paragraph (a) requires certified cost or pricing data for the subcontract, or the substance of the clause at FAR 52.215-13 [@CL-EST-0626] [@CL-EST-0603] [@CL-EST-0602], and applies them to each subcontract that, when entered into, exceeds the threshold in FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0625] [@CL-EST-0403]. The RFO Alternate I substitutes a paragraph (a) in place of paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-EST-9709]. That paragraph requires the Contractor, unless an exception under FAR 15.403-2 applies, to require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0627], where the codified Alternate I names FAR 15.403-1 [@CL-EST-0600]. It applies before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million under any prime contract awarded on or after July 1, 2018 [@CL-EST-0628]. The codified Alternate I states items for modifying a subcontract awarded before July 1, 2018, when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $950,000, and for awarding a subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million on or after July 1, 2018, or modifying a subcontract awarded on or after that date when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $2.5 million [@CL-EST-0405] [@CL-EST-0406].

For DoD, DFARS 215.403-3(a)(2) under Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Rev 1 directs that an offeror for a subcontract at any tier be required to submit certified cost or pricing data before the award of the subcontract if the prime contractor and each higher tier subcontractor are required to submit certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-9735]. It applies where the prime contract was entered into after June 30, 2026 and the subcontract price is expected to exceed $10 million, or on or before June 30, 2026 and the subcontract price is expected to exceed $2.5 million, unless otherwise stated in the prime contract [@CL-EST-9736]. Under paragraph (a)(3), the deviation directs that the subcontractor for a subcontract covered by paragraph (a)(2) be required to submit certified cost or pricing data before the pricing of a change or modification to the subcontract if the price adjustment is expected to exceed $2.5 million [@CL-EST-9759].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Under Alternate I, a subcontract awarded in 2019 is being modified, and the pricing adjustment is expected to reach 3 million. That exceeds 2.5 million, so the Contractor must require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data before pricing the modification, unless an exception applies. The subcontractor certifies that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price.

That follows the codified Alternate I [@CL-EST-0406]; the RFO Alternate I names only awards of subcontracts expected to exceed $2.5 million under a prime contract awarded on or after July 1, 2018, not modifications [@CL-EST-0628].

## Never confuse

Basic FAR 52.215-12 requires subcontractor certified cost or pricing data before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed the current FAR threshold [@CL-EST-0401; @CL-EST-0620], whereas RFO Alternate I applies a $2.5 million subcontract threshold on covered prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018 rather than the general threshold [@CL-EST-0628].
