## Purpose

Shows when the Contractor must obtain certified cost or pricing data from a subcontractor, and how that data is certified and flowed down.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0401] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-12 [@CL-EST-0401] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0401] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts and subcontract modifications expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold [@CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408] |

Before awarding a subcontract expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold, or before pricing a subcontract modification whose pricing adjustment is expected to exceed it, the Contractor must require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408]. That requirement does not apply where an exception under the cited exceptions section applies [@CL-EST-0601]. The Contractor must require the subcontractor to certify, in substantially the prescribed form, that to the best of its knowledge and belief the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price [@CL-EST-0402].

In each subcontract that, when entered into, exceeds the threshold, the Contractor must insert either the substance of this clause, including paragraph (c), or another alternative the clause allows [@CL-EST-0403]. The substance of this clause is the option where paragraph (a) requires certified cost or pricing data for the subcontract [@CL-EST-0603], and the other option is the substance of the clause at FAR 52.215-13, Subcontractor Certified Cost or Pricing Data—Modifications [@CL-EST-0602].

## How it works

Before award, or before pricing a modification, the Contractor obtains certified cost or pricing data from the subcontractor, with the subcontractor's certification [@CL-EST-0402; @CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408]. The expectation that a subcontract will exceed the threshold is measured on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-0408]. If the threshold is adjusted for inflation, the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-0404]. Alternate I substitutes a paragraph (a) under which, unless an exception under the cited section applies, the Contractor must require certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0600]. Its first item covers modifying a subcontract awarded before July 1, 2018, when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $950,000 [@CL-EST-0405]. Its second item covers awarding a subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million on or after July 1, 2018, and modifying a subcontract awarded on or after that date when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $2.5 million [@CL-EST-0406].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Under Alternate I, a subcontract awarded in 2019 is being modified, and the pricing adjustment is expected to reach 3 million. That exceeds 2.5 million, so the Contractor must require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data before pricing the modification, unless an exception applies. The subcontractor certifies that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price.

## Never confuse

This clause is not the only flow-down option. In a subcontract above the threshold the Contractor inserts either this clause, which is the option where paragraph (a) requires certified cost or pricing data for the subcontract, or the clause at FAR 52.215-13 [@CL-EST-0403; @CL-EST-0602; @CL-EST-0603].
