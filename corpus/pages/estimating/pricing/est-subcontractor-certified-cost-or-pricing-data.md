## Purpose

Shows when the Contractor must obtain certified cost or pricing data from a subcontractor, and how that data is certified and flowed down.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0401] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-12 [@CL-EST-0401] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-12 [@CL-EST-0620]; RFO FAR 52.215-12 Alternate I [@CL-EST-0628] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0401] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts and subcontract modifications expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold [@CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408] |

Before awarding a subcontract expected to exceed the certified cost or pricing data threshold on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later, or before pricing a subcontract modification whose pricing adjustment is expected to exceed it, the Contractor must require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408]. That requirement does not apply where an exception under the cited exceptions section applies [@CL-EST-0601]. The Contractor must require the subcontractor to certify, in substantially the prescribed form, that to the best of its knowledge and belief the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price [@CL-EST-0402].

In each subcontract that, when entered into, exceeds the threshold, the Contractor must insert either the substance of this clause, including paragraph (c), or another alternative the clause allows [@CL-EST-0403]. The substance of this clause is the option where paragraph (a) requires certified cost or pricing data for the subcontract [@CL-EST-0603], and the other option is the substance of the clause at FAR 52.215-13, Subcontractor Certified Cost or Pricing Data—Modifications [@CL-EST-0602].

## How it works

Before award, or before pricing a modification, the Contractor obtains certified cost or pricing data from the subcontractor, with the subcontractor's certification [@CL-EST-0402; @CL-EST-0407; @CL-EST-0408]. The expectation that a subcontract will exceed the threshold is measured on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-0408]. If the threshold is adjusted for inflation, the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-0404]. Alternate I substitutes a paragraph (a) under which, unless an exception under the cited section applies, the Contractor must require certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0600]. Its first item covers modifying a subcontract awarded before July 1, 2018, when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $950,000 [@CL-EST-0405]. Its second item covers awarding a subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million on or after July 1, 2018, and modifying a subcontract awarded on or after that date when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $2.5 million [@CL-EST-0406].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO paragraph (a) applies before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed the threshold for submission of certified cost or pricing data, measured on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later, and points to the threshold in FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0620], where the codified paragraph points to FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-0408]. It requires the Contractor, before pricing any subcontract modification involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed that threshold, to require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-0621] [@CL-EST-0407], unless an exception under FAR 15.403-2 applies, where the codified paragraph names FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-0622] [@CL-EST-0601]. If the threshold is adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR part 1, the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-0623]; the codified paragraph names FAR 1.109(a) for the adjustment and FAR 1.109(d) for the changed threshold [@CL-EST-0404]. The RFO paragraph (b) says the subcontractor is to certify, in substantially the form prescribed in FAR 15.403-4, that to the best of its knowledge and belief the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price [@CL-EST-0624], where the codified paragraph names FAR 15.406-2 for the form [@CL-EST-0402]. In paragraph (c) the RFO text keeps the two flow-down options, the substance of the clause, including paragraph (c), where paragraph (a) requires certified cost or pricing data for the subcontract, or the substance of the clause at FAR 52.215-13 [@CL-EST-0626] [@CL-EST-0603] [@CL-EST-0602], and applies them to each subcontract that, when entered into, exceeds the threshold in FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0625] [@CL-EST-0403]. The RFO Alternate I replaces paragraph (a) with a paragraph that requires the subcontractor to submit the data unless an exception under FAR 15.403-2 applies [@CL-EST-0627] [@CL-EST-0600], before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million under any prime contract awarded on or after July 1, 2018 [@CL-EST-0628]. That is its only timing item, so it has no item for modifying a subcontract and no $950,000 item, both of which the codified Alternate I has [@CL-EST-0627] [@CL-EST-0628] [@CL-EST-0405] [@CL-EST-0406].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Under Alternate I, a subcontract awarded in 2019 is being modified, and the pricing adjustment is expected to reach 3 million. That exceeds 2.5 million, so the Contractor must require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data before pricing the modification, unless an exception applies. The subcontractor certifies that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price.

That follows the codified Alternate I [@CL-EST-0406]; the RFO Alternate I names only awards of subcontracts expected to exceed $2.5 million under a prime contract awarded on or after July 1, 2018, not modifications [@CL-EST-0628].

## Never confuse

This clause is not the only flow-down option. In a subcontract above the threshold the Contractor inserts either this clause, which is the option where paragraph (a) requires certified cost or pricing data for the subcontract, or the clause at FAR 52.215-13 [@CL-EST-0403; @CL-EST-0602; @CL-EST-0603].

Alternate I differs between the two texts. The codified Alternate I covers modifying a subcontract awarded before July 1, 2018 when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $950,000, and awarding a subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million on or after that date or modifying a subcontract awarded on or after that date when the pricing adjustment is expected to exceed $2.5 million [@CL-EST-0405] [@CL-EST-0406]. The RFO Alternate I speaks only of awarding a subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million under a prime contract awarded on or after July 1, 2018 [@CL-EST-0628].
