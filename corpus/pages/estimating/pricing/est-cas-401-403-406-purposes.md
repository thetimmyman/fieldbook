## Purpose

Sets out what the consistency Standard, the home office allocation Standard and the cost accounting period Standard are for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-EST-2492] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.401-20, 9904.403-20 and 9904.406-20 [@CL-EST-2492; @CL-EST-2495; @CL-EST-2500] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2492] |
| Applies to | Contractor practices for estimating costs, allocating home office expenses and selecting cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2492; @CL-EST-2495; @CL-EST-2500] |

The purpose of CAS 401 is to ensure that each contractor's practices used in estimating costs for a proposal are consistent with the cost accounting practices the contractor uses in accumulating and reporting costs [@CL-EST-2492]. Consistency in the application of cost accounting practices is necessary to enhance the likelihood that comparable transactions are treated alike [@CL-EST-2493]. For individual contracts, consistent application facilitates reliable cost estimates used in pricing a proposal and their comparison with the costs of performing the resulting contract [@CL-EST-2494]. Such comparisons provide one important basis for financial control over costs during contract performance and aid in establishing accountability for cost in the manner agreed to by both parties at the time of contracting [@CL-EST-2632]. They also provide an improved basis for evaluating estimating capabilities [@CL-EST-2633].

The purpose of CAS 403 is to establish criteria for allocating the expenses of a home office to the segments of the organization based on the beneficial or causal relationship between those expenses and the receiving segments [@CL-EST-2495]. It provides for identifying expenses for direct allocation to segments to the maximum extent practical [@CL-EST-2496]. It also provides for accumulating significant nondirectly allocated expenses into logical and relatively homogeneous pools to be allocated on bases reflecting the relationship of the expenses to the segments concerned [@CL-EST-2497], and for allocating any remaining or residual home office expenses to all segments [@CL-EST-2498]. Appropriate implementation will limit the amount of home office expenses classified as residual to the expenses of managing the organization as a whole [@CL-EST-2634]. CAS 403 does not cover the reallocation of a segment's share of home office expenses to contracts and other cost objectives [@CL-EST-2499].

The purpose of CAS 406 is to provide criteria for selecting the time periods used as cost accounting periods for contract cost estimating, accumulating and reporting [@CL-EST-2500]. It will reduce the effects of variations in the flow of costs within each cost accounting period [@CL-EST-2501]. It will also enhance objectivity, consistency and verifiability, and promote uniformity and comparability in contract cost measurements [@CL-EST-2635].

## How it works

CAS 401 is to ensure that the practices used in estimating costs for a proposal are consistent with the practices used in accumulating and reporting costs [@CL-EST-2492]. Consistency in the application of cost accounting practices is necessary to enhance the likelihood that comparable transactions are treated alike [@CL-EST-2493]. For individual contracts, it facilitates reliable cost estimates used in pricing a proposal and their comparison with the costs of performing the resulting contract [@CL-EST-2494]. Such comparisons provide one important basis for financial control over costs during contract performance and aid in establishing accountability for cost, and they also provide an improved basis for evaluating estimating capabilities [@CL-EST-2632; @CL-EST-2633].

CAS 403 establishes criteria for allocating home office expenses to segments based on the beneficial or causal relationship between the expenses and the receiving segments [@CL-EST-2495]. It provides for identifying expenses for direct allocation to the maximum extent practical, accumulating significant nondirectly allocated expenses into pools, and allocating any remaining or residual home office expenses to all segments [@CL-EST-2496; @CL-EST-2497; @CL-EST-2498]. It does not cover the reallocation of a segment's share of home office expenses to contracts and other cost objectives [@CL-EST-2499].

CAS 406 provides criteria for the selection of the time periods used as cost accounting periods for contract cost estimating, accumulating and reporting [@CL-EST-2500], and will reduce the effects of variations in the flow of costs within each cost accounting period [@CL-EST-2501].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor prices a proposal with the same pools and bases it uses in its books. That applies the CAS 401 purpose, which is that estimating practices in a proposal are consistent with the practices used to accumulate and report costs [@CL-EST-2492]. After award it compares the proposal estimate with performance cost, which gives a basis for financial control over costs [@CL-EST-2494; @CL-EST-2632].

The home office has 900,000 of expenses. Of that, 200,000 can be identified with one segment and is allocated to it directly. Another 500,000 of purchasing and information technology service cost goes into pools, each allocated on a base related to the segments it serves. The remaining 200,000 is the cost of managing the organization as a whole, so it is the residual allocated to all segments. The arithmetic is 200,000 plus 500,000 plus 200,000, which equals 900,000 [@CL-EST-2496; @CL-EST-2497; @CL-EST-2498; @CL-EST-2634].

Spreading that segment's share over its contracts is a later step that CAS 403 does not cover [@CL-EST-2499].

## Never confuse

Each of these sections states the purpose of its Standard [@CL-EST-2492; @CL-EST-2495; @CL-EST-2500].

CAS 403 establishes criteria for allocating home office expenses to segments based on the beneficial or causal relationship [@CL-EST-2495], and it does not cover the reallocation of a segment's share of home office expenses to contracts and other cost objectives [@CL-EST-2499].

The consistency idea is covered in [the consistency Standard](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-401-consistency.md), the allocation mechanics in [home office allocation](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-403-home-office-allocation.md), and the period rules in [cost accounting period](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-406-cost-accounting-period.md).
