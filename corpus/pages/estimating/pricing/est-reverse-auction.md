## Purpose

Shows how an offeror takes part in a reverse auction under a solicitation, what the Government may show other offerors, how the offeror can leave the auction, and how price and proposal information is protected.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1931] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.217-10 [@CL-EST-1931]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9372] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.217-10 [@CL-EST-0555]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9373] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1931] |
| Applies to | Solicitations under which the Government intends to conduct a reverse auction to award a contract or blanket purchase agreement [@CL-EST-1932] |

A reverse auction is the process for obtaining pricing, usually supported by an electronic tool, in which offerors see competing offerors' prices without disclosure of the competing offerors' identity and have the opportunity to submit lower priced offers until the close of the auction [@CL-EST-1931]. The FAR definitions section also defines a reverse auction in those terms [@CL-EST-9372], and RFO FAR 2.101 defines reverse auction in the same words [@CL-EST-9373; @CL-EST-9372]. The Government states that it intends to conduct a reverse auction under the solicitation to award a contract or blanket purchase agreement [@CL-EST-1932].

By submitting a quote or proposal in response to the solicitation, the offeror agrees to participate in the reverse auction and agrees that the Government may reveal the offered prices in the auction to all offerors without revealing any offeror's identity, except for the awardee's identity subsequent to an award resulting from the auction [@CL-EST-1933; @CL-EST-1934]. The offeror may withdraw its agreement to further participate by withdrawing its offer before the close of the auction, notifying the Contracting Officer via the contact method identified in the solicitation [@CL-EST-1935]. If the reverse auction produces only one offer, the Government reserves the right to cancel the auction [@CL-EST-1936].

The Government may use a reverse auction service provider to conduct the reverse auction [@CL-EST-2020]. A reverse auction service provider is a commercial or Government entity that provides a means for conducting reverse auctions when acquiring supplies or services to be used by the Government [@CL-EST-2019]. Any price or proposal information or source selection information that the provider receives in relation to the reverse auction shall not be released outside the Government, unless otherwise required by law [@CL-EST-1937]. This does not prevent the Government from revealing to all offerors the offered prices in the auction without revealing any offeror's identity [@CL-EST-2021]. Price or proposal information includes, but is not limited to, contractor bid or proposal information as defined at Federal Acquisition Regulation 3.104-1 [@CL-EST-2022], and information identified by the offeror as restricted from duplication, use, or disclosure, in whole or in part, for any purpose other than to evaluate the offeror's price or proposal [@CL-EST-2023]. The contact method for withdrawal is the one identified in the solicitation [@CL-EST-1935].

## How it works

The auction is a process for obtaining pricing in which offerors see competing offerors' prices but not their identities [@CL-EST-1931]. Agreement comes from submitting a quote or proposal in response to the solicitation, and it includes agreement that the Government may reveal to all offerors the offered prices in the auction, without revealing any offeror's identity, except for the awardee's identity subsequent to an award resulting from the auction [@CL-EST-1933; @CL-EST-1934]. The offeror may withdraw its agreement to further participate by withdrawing its offer before the close of the auction [@CL-EST-1935]. If the reverse auction produces only one offer, the Government reserves the right to cancel the auction [@CL-EST-1936]. Information identified by the offeror as restricted from duplication, use, or disclosure, in whole or in part, for any purpose other than to evaluate the offeror's price or proposal is price or proposal information, and any such information received by the reverse auction service provider shall not be released outside the Government unless otherwise required by law [@CL-EST-1937; @CL-EST-2023].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.217-10 keeps the definitions of reverse auction [@CL-EST-9157; @CL-EST-1931] and reverse auction service provider [@CL-EST-9158; @CL-EST-2019]. It keeps the statement that the Government intends to conduct a reverse auction under the solicitation to award a contract or blanket purchase agreement [@CL-EST-0555; @CL-EST-1932], the offeror’s agreement, by submitting a quote or proposal, to participate in the auction and to the Government revealing the offered prices to all offerors without revealing any offeror’s identity [@CL-EST-0556; @CL-EST-1933], except for the awardee’s identity subsequent to an award resulting from the auction [@CL-EST-0557; @CL-EST-1934]. It keeps the offeror’s right to withdraw its agreement by withdrawing its offer before the close of the auction by notifying the Contracting Officer via the contact method identified in the solicitation [@CL-EST-0558; @CL-EST-1935], and the Government’s right to cancel the auction if it produces only one offer [@CL-EST-0559; @CL-EST-1936]. It keeps the Government’s ability to use a reverse auction service provider to conduct the reverse auction [@CL-EST-9159; @CL-EST-2020] and the bar on releasing outside the Government, unless otherwise required by law, any price or proposal information or source selection information that the reverse auction service provider receives in relation to the auction [@CL-EST-0560; @CL-EST-1937]. It keeps the statement that this does not prevent the Government from revealing to all offerors the offered prices in the auction without revealing any offeror’s identity [@CL-EST-9160; @CL-EST-2021], and the listing, among the price or proposal information, of contractor bid or proposal information as defined at Federal Acquisition Regulation 3.104-1 [@CL-EST-9161; @CL-EST-2022] and information identified by the offeror as restricted from duplication, use, or disclosure for any purpose other than to evaluate the offeror’s price or proposal [@CL-EST-9162; @CL-EST-2023].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Three offerors quote in an auction for a blanket purchase agreement: A at 250,000, B at 240,000 and C at 238,000. Each sees the competing offerors' prices without names. Offeror A decides to leave and notifies the Contracting Officer, through the contact method named in the solicitation, that it withdraws its offer before the close. Two offers remain at the close, so the one-offer cancellation right does not arise. Had B also withdrawn before the close, only C's offer would remain and the Government could cancel the auction. If the Government awards to C, its identity as awardee may be revealed after the award, and the exception covers only the awardee.

## Never confuse

In a reverse auction, offerors see competing offerors' prices without disclosure of the competing offerors' identity and have the opportunity to submit lower priced offers until the close of the auction [@CL-EST-1931]. The sealed bidding rules are on [Sealed bidding: amendments, bid submission and explanations](est-sealed-bidding-essentials.md). The offeror may withdraw its agreement to further participate by withdrawing its offer before the close of the auction [@CL-EST-1935]. Any price or proposal information or source selection information received by the reverse auction service provider in relation to the reverse auction shall not be released outside the Government unless otherwise required by law [@CL-EST-1937], but this does not prevent the Government from revealing to all offerors the offered prices in the auction, without revealing any offeror's identity [@CL-EST-2021]. Orders placed under an existing contract or agreement are covered on [Reverse auction for orders under a contract or agreement](est-reverse-auction-orders.md), and the provider's own duties are on [Duties of a reverse auction service provider](est-reverse-auction-services.md).
