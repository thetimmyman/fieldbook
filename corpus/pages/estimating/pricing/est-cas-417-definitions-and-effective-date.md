## Purpose

States the purpose of the Standard on cost of money as an element of the cost of capital assets under construction, gives its two asset definitions, and states its effective date and start rules. The Standard's requirements are on the [capital assets under construction card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-417-capital-assets-under-construction.md) and the [cost of money card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cost-of-money.md).

## The rule

| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.417-20, 9904.417-30 and 9904.417-63 [@CL-EST-2897; @CL-EST-2898; @CL-EST-2900] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |

| Applies to | Contractors receiving a contract to which the Standard is applicable, including contractors with prior full-coverage CAS contracts [@CL-EST-2900; @CL-EST-2901] |

The purpose of the Standard is to establish criteria for the measurement of the cost of money attributable to capital assets under construction, fabrication or development as an element of the cost of those assets [@CL-EST-2897]. Consistent application of the criteria will improve cost measurement by recognizing the cost of contractor investment in assets under construction and will provide greater uniformity in accounting for asset acquisition costs [@CL-EST-2973].

An intangible capital asset means an asset that has no physical substance, has more than minimal value, and is expected to be held by an enterprise for continued use or possession beyond the current accounting period for the benefits it yields, and it includes right-of-use assets acquired under leases [@CL-EST-2898]. A tangible capital asset means an asset that has physical substance, more than minimal value, and is expected to be held by an enterprise for continued use or possession beyond the current accounting period for the services it yields [@CL-EST-2899]. Other terms defined elsewhere in part 99 have the meanings given in those definitions unless paragraph (b) of the section requires otherwise [@CL-EST-2966], and paragraph (b) applies no modification to those terms for this Standard [@CL-EST-2967].

The Standard is effective as of 17 April 1992, and contractors with prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage shall continue its applicability upon receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2900]. For contractors with no previous contracts subject to the Standard, it shall be applied beginning with the contractor's next full fiscal year beginning after the receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2901].

## How it works

The purpose ties cost of money to the assets under construction, fabrication or development, as an element of the cost of those assets [@CL-EST-2897]. An intangible capital asset is an asset that has no physical substance and includes right-of-use assets acquired under leases [@CL-EST-2898], and a tangible capital asset is an asset that has physical substance [@CL-EST-2899]. The start rule depends on the contractor's history: a contractor already holding full-coverage CAS contracts continues on receipt of a contract to which the Standard applies [@CL-EST-2900], and a contractor with no previous contract subject to the Standard begins with its next full fiscal year after receipt [@CL-EST-2901]. The measurement criteria themselves are on the main cards.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor builds a 3,000,000 production line for its own use. The line has physical substance and is expected to be held beyond the current accounting period for the services it yields, so it is a tangible capital asset [@CL-EST-2899]. A right-of-use asset acquired under a lease is an intangible capital asset by definition [@CL-EST-2898].

A contractor with a calendar fiscal year and no previous contract subject to the Standard receives a contract to which the Standard is applicable in September of Year 1. It applies the Standard beginning with its next full fiscal year beginning after receipt, which starts in January of Year 2 [@CL-EST-2901]. A second contractor already holds prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage. It continues the Standard's applicability when it receives an applicable contract [@CL-EST-2900].

## Never confuse

The purpose is to establish criteria for measuring the cost of money attributable to capital assets under construction, fabrication or development as an element of the cost of those assets [@CL-EST-2897]. An intangible capital asset has no physical substance, has more than minimal value, and is expected to be held by an enterprise for continued use or possession beyond the current accounting period for the benefits it yields [@CL-EST-2898]. A tangible capital asset has physical substance, more than minimal value, and is expected to be held by an enterprise for continued use or possession beyond the current accounting period for the services it yields [@CL-EST-2899]. For contractors with no previous contracts subject to this Standard, it is applied beginning with the contractor's next full fiscal year beginning after the receipt of a contract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2901], and the direct and indirect cost Standard is applied beginning with the contractor's second full fiscal year beginning after that receipt [@CL-EST-2905].
