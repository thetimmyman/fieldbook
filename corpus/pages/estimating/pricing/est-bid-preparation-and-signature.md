## Purpose

Covers what a sealed bidder must fill in and sign, how erasures, price discrepancies and delivery times are handled, and what the construction provision adds on forms, pricing bases, bidding on all items and alternate bids.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1987] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-12 [@CL-EST-1987]; FAR 52.214-18 [@CL-EST-1995] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.214-12 [@CL-EST-0037; @CL-EST-0038]; RFO FAR 52.214-18 [@CL-EST-0046; @CL-EST-0047] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1987] |
| Applies to | Sealed bids under the general preparation provision [@CL-EST-2096] and, for construction, the construction preparation provision [@CL-EST-2100] |

Bidders are expected to examine the drawings, specifications, Schedule and all instructions, and failure to do so is at the bidder's risk [@CL-EST-1987]. Each bidder must furnish the information required by the solicitation [@CL-EST-2097].

The bidder must sign the bid and print or type its name on the Schedule and on each continuation sheet on which it makes an entry [@CL-EST-1988]. Erasures or other changes must be initialed by the person signing the bid [@CL-EST-1989]. A bid signed by an agent must be accompanied by evidence of the agent's authority unless that evidence was previously furnished to the issuing office [@CL-EST-1990]. The FAR's general definitions say signature or signed means the discrete, verifiable symbol of an individual that, when affixed to a writing with the knowledge and consent of the individual, indicates a present intention to authenticate the writing [@CL-EST-9386]. The RFO model text of FAR 2.101 defines it in the same words, apart from quotation marks around the word signed in the term [@CL-EST-9387; @CL-EST-9386].

## How it works

For each item offered, the bidder must show the unit price, which includes packaging, packing and preservation unless otherwise specified [@CL-EST-1991], and must enter the extended price in the Amount column of the Schedule [@CL-EST-1992]. Where a unit price and an extended price disagree, the unit price is presumed correct, subject to correction to the same extent and in the same manner as any other mistake [@CL-EST-1993].

Bids for supplies or services other than those specified will not be considered unless the solicitation authorizes them [@CL-EST-2098]. Bidders must state a definite time for delivery of supplies or for performance of services, unless the solicitation specifies otherwise [@CL-EST-1994]. Time stated as a number of days includes Saturdays, Sundays and holidays [@CL-EST-2099].

The construction provision adds its own rules. A construction bid must be submitted on the forms the Government furnishes or on copies of them, and must be manually signed [@CL-EST-1995]. The person signing must initial each erasure or change on any bid form [@CL-EST-1996]. The bid form may require prices on various bases, including lump sum bidding, alternate prices, units of construction, or any combination of those three [@CL-EST-2101].

If the solicitation requires bidding on all items, failure to bid on all items disqualifies the bid [@CL-EST-1997]. If bidding on all items is not required, bidders should insert the words no bid in the space provided for any item on which no price is submitted [@CL-EST-2102]. Alternate bids will not be considered unless the solicitation authorizes their submission [@CL-EST-1998].

How the Government reads the prices it receives is covered on [Contract award under sealed bidding](est-sealed-bidding-contract-award.md), and the acceptance period a bidder states is covered on [Place of performance and the bid acceptance period](est-bid-acceptance-period-and-place-of-performance.md).

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.214-12(a) keeps the expectation that bidders examine the drawings, specifications, Schedule, and all instructions, and the rule that failure to do so will be at the bidder's risk [@CL-EST-0037; @CL-EST-1987]. RFO FAR 52.214-12(b) keeps the rules that each bidder furnishes the information required by the solicitation [@CL-EST-0038; @CL-EST-2097] and signs the bid and prints or types its name on the Schedule and each continuation sheet on which it makes an entry [@CL-EST-0038; @CL-EST-1988]. It keeps the rule that a bid signed by an agent is accompanied by evidence of that agent's authority, unless that evidence has been previously furnished to the issuing office [@CL-EST-0040; @CL-EST-1990]. The RFO paragraph says erasures or other changes "shall be initialed" by the person signing the bid, where the codified paragraph says "must be initialed" [@CL-EST-0039; @CL-EST-1989]. RFO FAR 52.214-12(c) keeps the rules that, for each item offered, the bidder shows the unit price, including, unless otherwise specified, packaging, packing, and preservation [@CL-EST-0041; @CL-EST-1991], and enters the extended price for the quantity of each item offered in the Amount column of the Schedule [@CL-EST-0041; @CL-EST-1992]. It keeps the presumption that, in case of discrepancy between a unit price and an extended price, the unit price is correct, subject to correction to the same extent and in the same manner as any other mistake [@CL-EST-0042; @CL-EST-1993]. RFO FAR 52.214-12(d) keeps the rule that bids for supplies or services other than those specified will not be considered unless authorized by the solicitation [@CL-EST-0043; @CL-EST-2098]. RFO FAR 52.214-12(e) says bidders "shall state" a definite time for delivery of supplies or for performance of services, unless otherwise specified in the solicitation, where the codified paragraph says "must state" [@CL-EST-0044; @CL-EST-1994]. RFO FAR 52.214-12(f) keeps the rule that time, if stated as a number of days, will include Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays [@CL-EST-0045; @CL-EST-2099]. RFO FAR 52.214-18(a) keeps the rules that bids must be submitted on the forms furnished by the Government or on copies of those forms and must be manually signed [@CL-EST-0046; @CL-EST-1995], and that the person signing a bid must initial each erasure or change appearing on any bid form [@CL-EST-0047; @CL-EST-1996]. RFO FAR 52.214-18(b) keeps the rule that the bid form may require bid prices for one or more items on various bases, including lump sum bidding, alternate prices, units of construction, or any combination of those three [@CL-EST-0048; @CL-EST-2101]. RFO FAR 52.214-18(c) keeps the rule that, if the solicitation requires bidding on all items, failure to do so will disqualify the bid [@CL-EST-0049; @CL-EST-1997], and that, if bidding on all items is not required, bidders should insert the words no bid in the space provided for any item on which no price is submitted [@CL-EST-0050; @CL-EST-2102]. RFO FAR 52.214-18(d) keeps the rule that alternate bids will not be considered unless the solicitation authorizes their submission [@CL-EST-0051; @CL-EST-1998].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A bidder for supplies offers 40 units of an item at a unit price of 25 and writes 900 as the extended price. The unit price is presumed correct, so the price is presumed to be 1,000, although the discrepancy can be corrected to the same extent and in the same manner as any other mistake. The bidder changes a figure on the Schedule, and the person signing the bid initials the change. An agent signs the bid, so evidence of the agent's authority accompanies it unless the issuing office already has it. The bidder states delivery within 30 days, and those days include Saturdays, Sundays and holidays.

A construction bidder answers a solicitation that requires bidding on all items. The bid form has 12 items, and the bidder leaves item 9 blank without writing no bid. Because bidding on all items is required, the bid is disqualified. Had bidding on all items not been required, the bidder should have written no bid in the space for item 9.

## Never confuse

The presumption for the unit price is not a bar to correction, because it is subject to correction in the same manner as any other mistake [@CL-EST-1993].

Initialing is stated twice: the general provision requires the person signing the bid to initial erasures or other changes [@CL-EST-1989], and the construction provision requires that person to initial each erasure or change on any bid form [@CL-EST-1996].

A bid for something other than what the solicitation specifies is a different rule from alternate bids in construction, although both are considered only if the solicitation authorizes them [@CL-EST-2098; @CL-EST-1998].

The words no bid are for items on which no price is submitted when bidding on all items is not required; where bidding on all items is required, leaving an item out disqualifies the bid [@CL-EST-2102; @CL-EST-1997].

## Company calibration

Confirm who signs bids, who initials changes, and what evidence of agent authority the company keeps on file [@Q-012].
