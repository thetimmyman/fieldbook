## Purpose

Gives the meanings that the Standard on consistency in estimating, accumulating and reporting costs assigns to its prominent terms. The Standard's requirements are on the [consistency card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-401-consistency.md).

| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.401-30 [@CL-EST-2856] |

Accumulating costs means the collecting of cost data in an organized manner, such as through a system of accounts [@CL-EST-2856]. Estimating costs means the process of forecasting a future result in terms of cost, based on information available at the time [@CL-EST-2858]. Reporting costs means provision of cost information to others [@CL-EST-2862]. Actual cost means an amount determined on the basis of cost incurred, as distinguished from forecasted cost, including standard cost properly adjusted for applicable variance [@CL-EST-2857].

An indirect cost pool means a grouping of incurred costs identified with two or more objectives but not identified specifically with any final cost objective [@CL-EST-2859]. Pricing means the process of establishing the amount or amounts to be paid in return for goods or services [@CL-EST-2860]. A proposal means any offer or other submission used as a basis for pricing a contract, contract modification or termination settlement, or for securing payments under it [@CL-EST-2861].

Other terms defined elsewhere in part 99 have the meanings given in those definitions unless paragraph (b) of the section requires otherwise [@CL-EST-2956]. Paragraph (b) applies no modification to those terms for this Standard [@CL-EST-2957].

## How it works

The definitions separate a forecast from an incurred amount: estimating costs is forecasting a future result from the information available at the time, while actual cost rests on cost incurred as distinguished from forecasted cost [@CL-EST-2858; @CL-EST-2857]. Standard cost is included in actual cost when it is properly adjusted for applicable variance [@CL-EST-2857]. A proposal is wider than a bid for a new contract: it also covers any submission used as a basis for pricing a contract modification or a termination settlement, or for securing payments [@CL-EST-2861]. Accumulating collects cost data in an organized manner, and reporting provides cost information to others [@CL-EST-2856; @CL-EST-2862]. The definitions create no duty by themselves; the duties sit in the Standard's requirements.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An estimator forecasts 500,000 of engineering cost for a follow-on lot from the information available at the time, which is estimating costs [@CL-EST-2858]. The ledger later records 480,000 for the work actually done. That amount rests on cost incurred rather than on a forecast, so it is actual cost [@CL-EST-2857]. The priced offer that carries the 500,000 forecast is a proposal, because it is a submission used as a basis for pricing a contract [@CL-EST-2861].

## Never confuse

Estimating costs is not actual cost: one forecasts a future result from information available at the time, and the other rests on cost incurred [@CL-EST-2858; @CL-EST-2857]. Pricing is not estimating: pricing establishes the amount to be paid, while estimating forecasts a cost result [@CL-EST-2860; @CL-EST-2858]. Accumulating costs is not reporting costs: accumulating is collecting cost data in an organized manner, and reporting is providing cost information to others [@CL-EST-2856; @CL-EST-2862]. The consistency requirement that ties these activities together is on the [consistency card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-401-consistency.md), not in these definitions.
