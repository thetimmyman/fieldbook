## Purpose

Covers the DoD evaluation factor for Selected Reserve staffing, the award value at which the postaward debriefing notification provision is used, and the deadlines for follow-up questions and written responses after a required postaward debriefing.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-2763] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.370-2, DFARS 215.570 and DFARS 215.506-70 [@CL-EST-2763; @CL-EST-2764; @CL-EST-2766] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2763] |
| Applies to | The contracting officer's use of the Selected Reserve factor, competitive negotiated solicitations for contract awards valued at $15 million or more, and required postaward debriefings of successful and unsuccessful offerors [@CL-EST-2763; @CL-EST-2764; @CL-EST-2786] |

The contracting officer may use an evaluation factor that considers whether an offeror intends to perform the contract using employees or individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2763]. The section refers the reader to PGI 215.370-2 for guidance on use of the factor [@CL-EST-2785].

The postaward debriefing notification provision at DFARS 252.215-7016 is to be used in competitive negotiated solicitations for contract awards valued at $15 million or more, including solicitations using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services [@CL-EST-2765; @CL-EST-2764].

When providing a required postaward debriefing to successful and unsuccessful offerors, contracting officers shall do the following [@CL-EST-2786]. They shall provide an opportunity to submit additional written questions related to the required debriefing not later than 2 business days after receiving the postaward debriefing [@CL-EST-2766]. They shall respond in writing to timely submitted additional questions within 5 business days after receipt of the questions [@CL-EST-2767]. They shall not consider the postaward debriefing to be concluded until the later of the date it is delivered, orally or in writing, or, if additional written questions related to the debriefing are timely received, the date the agency delivers its written response [@CL-EST-2768].

## How it works

The Selected Reserve factor is discretionary, because the text says the contracting officer may use it [@CL-EST-2763]. The provision is tied to the value of the contract award and the type of solicitation: a competitive negotiated solicitation, valued at $15 million or more, including one that uses FAR part 12 procedures [@CL-EST-2764].

After a required postaward debriefing, two periods run for the contracting officer. The first is the opportunity to submit written questions, which runs to not later than 2 business days after receiving the postaward debriefing [@CL-EST-2766]. The second is the written response, due within 5 business days after receipt of the questions, and the duty covers timely submitted questions [@CL-EST-2767]. The debriefing is concluded at the later of its delivery or, where timely written questions were received, the delivery of the written response [@CL-EST-2768]. For the full debriefing rules, see [Postaward debriefing of offerors](est-postaward-debriefing.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A DoD competitive negotiated solicitation expects an award valued at 20 million dollars. The postaward debriefing notification provision is to be used in it [@CL-EST-2764]. A similar solicitation expects an award valued at 12 million dollars, which is below the $15 million figure, so this section does not call for the provision there [@CL-EST-2764]. In either solicitation the contracting officer may use a Selected Reserve evaluation factor, or not use one [@CL-EST-2763].

A required postaward debriefing is delivered orally on a Monday. An unsuccessful offeror sends additional written questions on Tuesday, within 2 business days after receiving the debriefing [@CL-EST-2766]. The agency receives them on Tuesday, so its written response is due within 5 business days after receipt, which is the following Tuesday [@CL-EST-2767]. It responds on Friday. The debriefing is concluded on Friday, the later of the Monday delivery and the Friday written response [@CL-EST-2768].

Another offeror sends no questions. Its debriefing is concluded on the date it is delivered [@CL-EST-2768].

## Never confuse

The Selected Reserve evaluation factor is not the Selected Reserve contract clause. The factor is something the contracting officer may use in evaluation [@CL-EST-2763]. The clause at DFARS 252.215-7006 is a separate clause prescribed in DFARS 215.370-3 [@CL-EST-1779]. See [Notice of intent to resolicit and the Selected Reserve clause](est-dfars-resolicit-notice-and-selected-reserve-clause.md).

The notification provision is not the follow-up question rule. The provision is used in solicitations [@CL-EST-2764], while the follow-up duties fall on contracting officers giving a required postaward debriefing [@CL-EST-2786].

A delivered debriefing is not always a concluded one. Where timely written questions are received, it is concluded when the agency delivers its written response [@CL-EST-2768].
