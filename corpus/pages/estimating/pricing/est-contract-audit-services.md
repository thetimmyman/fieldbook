## Purpose

Shows what an auditor is responsible for, which agency normally audits a contractor, and how a contracting officer asks for audit services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1446] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.101 [@CL-EST-1446] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1446] |
| Applies to | Contractors audited for a requesting activity, normally by the Defense Contract Audit Agency [@CL-EST-1446; @CL-EST-1447] |

The auditor is responsible for, among other listed duties, submitting information and advice to the requesting activity, based on analysis of the contractor's financial and accounting records or other related data, on the acceptability of the contractor's incurred and estimated costs [@CL-EST-1446]. Normally, for contractors other than educational institutions and nonprofit organizations, the Defense Contract Audit Agency is the responsible Government audit agency, although another agency may desire cognizance of a particular contractor [@CL-EST-1447]. Where an agency other than the Defense Contract Audit Agency desires cognizance of a particular contractor, the two agencies shall agree on the most efficient and economical approach to meet contract audit requirements [@CL-EST-1474].

The three audit sections covered by this card state no dollar threshold for requesting audit services.

## How it works

As provided in agency procedures or interagency agreements, contracting officers may request audit services directly from the responsible audit agency cited in the Directory of Federal Contract Audit Offices [@CL-EST-1448]. The request should include a suspense date and should identify any information the contracting officer needs [@CL-EST-1448; @CL-EST-1449]. The responsible audit agency may decline requests for services on a case-by-case basis if its resources are inadequate to accomplish the tasks, and declinations shall be in writing [@CL-EST-1472]. The normal rule naming the Defense Contract Audit Agency is stated for contractors other than educational institutions and nonprofit organizations [@CL-EST-1447].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer holds a contractor's proposal and wants the estimated costs reviewed by a set date. Under the agency's procedures the officer asks the responsible audit agency directly. The request gives the due date and lists the points the officer needs answered. The answer: the officer names the date and the information wanted in the request.

## Never confuse

The responsible audit agency is not always the Defense Contract Audit Agency, because another agency may desire cognizance of a particular contractor [@CL-EST-1447]. A request for audit services is also not an audit opinion: the opinion rules sit on [Proposal adequacy and the audit opinion](est-proposal-adequacy-audit-opinion.md), the manual's service types on [Field pricing support](est-field-pricing-support.md), and the audit products aimed at standards compliance on [DCAA audits that test Cost Accounting Standards compliance](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-dcaa-cas-audits.md).
