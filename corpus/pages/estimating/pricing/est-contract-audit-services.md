## Purpose

Shows what an auditor is responsible for, which agency normally audits a contractor, and how a contracting officer asks for audit services.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1446] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.101 [@CL-EST-1446]; FAR 42.000 [@CL-EST-9201] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.101 [@CL-EST-3550; @CL-EST-3551] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1446] |
| Applies to | Contractors audited for a requesting activity, normally by the Defense Contract Audit Agency [@CL-EST-1446; @CL-EST-1447] |

The auditor is responsible for, among other listed duties, submitting information and advice to the requesting activity, based on analysis of the contractor's financial and accounting records or other related data, on the acceptability of the contractor's incurred and estimated costs [@CL-EST-1446]. Normally, for contractors other than educational institutions and nonprofit organizations, the Defense Contract Audit Agency is the responsible Government audit agency, although another agency may desire cognizance of a particular contractor [@CL-EST-1447]. Where an agency other than the Defense Contract Audit Agency desires cognizance of a particular contractor, the two agencies shall agree on the most efficient and economical approach to meet contract audit requirements [@CL-EST-1474].

The three audit sections covered by this card state no dollar threshold for requesting audit services.

FAR part 42 prescribes policies and procedures for assigning and performing contract administration and contract audit services [@CL-EST-9201].

## How it works

As provided in agency procedures or interagency agreements, contracting officers may request audit services directly from the responsible audit agency cited in the Directory of Federal Contract Audit Offices [@CL-EST-1448]. The request should include a suspense date and should identify any information the contracting officer needs [@CL-EST-1448; @CL-EST-1449]. The responsible audit agency may decline requests for services on a case-by-case basis if its resources are inadequate to accomplish the tasks, and declinations shall be in writing [@CL-EST-1472]. The normal rule naming the Defense Contract Audit Agency is stated for contractors other than educational institutions and nonprofit organizations [@CL-EST-1447].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified text because Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs it, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO FAR 42.101(a)(1) keeps the auditor's responsibility, among other listed duties, to analyze contractor financial records or other related data and advise the requesting activity on the acceptability of the contractor's incurred and estimated costs, in reworded form that speaks of financial records rather than financial and accounting records [@CL-EST-3550; @CL-EST-1446]. The first sentence of RFO FAR 42.101(b) says normally the Defense Contract Audit Agency is the responsible Government audit agency and does not contain the words "for contractors other than educational institutions and nonprofit organizations" that the codified sentence has [@CL-EST-3551; @CL-EST-1447]. Where an agency other than the Defense Contract Audit Agency desires cognizance of a particular contractor, the RFO text says the two agencies must agree on the most efficient and economical approach to meet contract audit requirements, where the codified text says "shall" [@CL-EST-3552; @CL-EST-1474]. Another sentence of RFO FAR 42.101(b) covers educational institutions and nonprofit organizations, with the definitions the sentence gives, and says audit cognizance will be determined according to the provisions of the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200 [@CL-EST-3557; @CL-EST-3553]. RFO FAR 42.102(a) lets contracting officers request audit services directly from the responsible audit agency listed in the Directory of Federal Contract Audit Offices, and the printed paragraph does not contain the codified opening phrase "As provided in agency procedures or interagency agreements" [@CL-EST-3554; @CL-EST-1448]. It says requests must include a suspense date and identify any information needed by the contracting officer, where the codified text says the audit request should do so [@CL-EST-3555; @CL-EST-1449]. RFO FAR 42.102(b) lets the responsible audit agency decline requests for services on a case-by-case basis if they lack adequate resources and says declinations must be in writing, where the codified text says "shall" [@CL-EST-3556; @CL-EST-1472].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer holds a contractor's proposal and wants the estimated costs reviewed by a set date. Under the agency's procedures the officer asks the responsible audit agency directly. The request gives the due date and lists the points the officer needs answered. The answer: the officer names the date and the information wanted in the request.

## Never confuse

The responsible audit agency is not always the Defense Contract Audit Agency, because another agency may desire cognizance of a particular contractor [@CL-EST-1447]. A request for audit services is also not an audit opinion: the opinion rules sit on [Proposal adequacy and the audit opinion](est-proposal-adequacy-audit-opinion.md), the manual's service types on [Field pricing support](est-field-pricing-support.md), and the audit products aimed at standards compliance on [DCAA audits that test Cost Accounting Standards compliance](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-dcaa-cas-audits.md).

Under the codified text the Defense Contract Audit Agency is normally the responsible audit agency for contractors other than educational institutions and nonprofit organizations [@CL-EST-1447], but the first RFO sentence naming it does not contain those words [@CL-EST-3551], and another RFO sentence of paragraph (b) sends audit cognizance for educational institutions and nonprofit organizations to the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200 [@CL-EST-3557; @CL-EST-3553].
