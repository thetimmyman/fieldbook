## Purpose

Explains how long a sealed bid stays open for acceptance, how a minimum acceptance period set by the Government works, and what the bidder states about plants or facilities at another address.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1999] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-14 [@CL-EST-1999]; FAR 52.214-15 [@CL-EST-2000]; FAR 52.214-16 [@CL-EST-2001] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1999] |
| Applies to | Invitations for bids that include the place of performance provision [@CL-EST-2103] or the period for acceptance provision [@CL-EST-2105]; the minimum period provision is inserted in invitations for bids, except for construction, if the Contracting Officer determines that a minimum acceptance period must be specified [@CL-EST-2004] |

In the period for acceptance provision, the bid is open for acceptance within a stated number of calendar days from the date specified in the solicitation for receipt of bids, which is 60 calendar days unless the bidder inserts a different period [@CL-EST-2000]. If the bid is accepted within that period, the bidder agrees to furnish any or all items upon which prices are bid at the price set opposite each item, delivered at the designated points, within the time specified in the Schedule [@CL-EST-2000; @CL-EST-2106].

In the minimum bid acceptance period provision, the acceptance period is the number of calendar days available to the Government for awarding a contract, counted from the date specified in the solicitation for receipt of bids [@CL-EST-2001]. The provision supersedes any language on the acceptance period that appears elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2107]. The Government states its minimum in a blank that the Contracting Officer fills in with a number of calendar days [@CL-EST-2108]. In the space provided, bidders may specify a longer acceptance period than the minimum [@CL-EST-2002]. A bid allowing less than the minimum will be rejected [@CL-EST-2003].

The bidder agrees to execute all that it has undertaken to do, in compliance with its bid, if the bid is accepted in writing within the minimum period or within any longer period the bidder stated [@CL-EST-2109].

## How it works

In the place of performance provision, the bidder checks one box to state whether it intends or does not intend to use one or more plants or facilities located at a different address from the bidder's address in the bid [@CL-EST-1999]. A bidder that checks intends must insert the required information in the spaces provided: the place of performance (street address, city, county, state, zip code) and the name and address of the owner and operator of the plant or facility if other than the bidder [@CL-EST-2104].

The blanks and boxes are contract-specific: the Government's minimum number of calendar days [@CL-EST-2108], any longer period the bidder writes [@CL-EST-2002], the period the bidder inserts in place of the default [@CL-EST-2000], and the plant or facility details [@CL-EST-2104]. The negotiated-acquisition version of the place of performance statement is covered on [Place of performance in a solicitation response](est-place-of-performance.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An invitation sets a minimum acceptance period of 45 calendar days and a receipt date of 1 June. Bidder A writes 30 calendar days in the space for a longer period, so its bid allows less than the minimum and will be rejected.

Bidder B writes 60 calendar days, which is longer than the minimum. The Government accepts B's bid in writing on 20 July, 49 days after 1 June, which is inside B's period, so B agrees to execute all that it has undertaken to do in compliance with its bid.

Bidder B also checks intends in the place of performance provision. It inserts the address of a plant that another company owns and operates, and it names that company and its address as owner and operator.

## Never confuse

The acceptance period is not a delivery period: it counts the calendar days available to the Government for awarding a contract [@CL-EST-2001], while the delivery time is the one stated in the Schedule [@CL-EST-2106].

A longer period than the minimum is allowed, and a shorter one is not: a bid allowing less than the minimum will be rejected [@CL-EST-2002; @CL-EST-2003].

The default period in the period for acceptance provision is not the minimum; the minimum provision supersedes any language on the acceptance period elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2000; @CL-EST-2107].

The place of performance box concerns plants or facilities at an address different from the bidder's address in the bid [@CL-EST-1999].

## Company calibration

Confirm who sets the acceptance period the company offers and who signs off on the place of performance statement [@Q-012].
