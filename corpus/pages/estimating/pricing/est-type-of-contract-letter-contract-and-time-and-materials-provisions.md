## Purpose

Shows what the type of contract provision tells offerors, how a letter contract is accepted and work begins, and what a time-and-materials or labor-hour offer must state about hourly rates.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1842] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.216-1, FAR 52.216-23 and FAR 52.216-31 [@CL-EST-1842; @CL-EST-1843; @CL-EST-1845] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1842] |
| Applies to | Solicitations and contracts that carry the type of contract provision, the letter contract clause or the time-and-materials proposal provision [@CL-EST-1842; @CL-EST-1843; @CL-EST-1845] |

The type of contract provision tells offerors that the Government contemplates award of the type of contract the Contracting Officer inserts in the blank, resulting from the solicitation [@CL-EST-1842].

The Contractor indicates acceptance of a letter contract by signing three copies of the contract and returning them to the Contracting Officer not later than the date inserted [@CL-EST-1843]. On acceptance by both parties, the Contractor shall proceed with performance of the work, including purchase of necessary materials [@CL-EST-1844].

## How it works

Under the commercial time-and-materials and labor-hour proposal provision, the offeror must specify fixed hourly rates in its offer that include wages, overhead, general and administrative expenses, and profit [@CL-EST-1845]. The offeror must also say whether the fixed hourly rate for each labor category applies to labor performed by the offeror, by subcontractors, or by divisions, subsidiaries or affiliates under common control [@CL-EST-1846]. The type of contract provision names the contemplated type [@CL-EST-1842]. The letter contract clause covers signing and starting work [@CL-EST-1843; @CL-EST-1844].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror answers a time-and-materials solicitation with an engineer rate of 150 per hour. The rate is built from wages, overhead, general and administrative expense and profit. The offer says the rate applies to the offeror's own staff and to an affiliate under common control, but that subcontractor labor is priced at a separate rate. On a letter contract, the Contractor signs and returns the copies by the stated date and starts work, including buying necessary materials.

## Never confuse

The proposal provision is about fixed hourly rates and who performs the labor [@CL-EST-1845; @CL-EST-1846], while the letter contract clause is about signing the contract and starting work [@CL-EST-1843; @CL-EST-1844]. The contract type itself is explained in [Time-and-materials contracts](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-time-and-materials-contracts.md), and the companion proposal provisions are in [Time-and-materials and labor-hour proposal provisions](est-time-and-materials-proposal-rate-provisions.md). The proposal as a whole is governed by the general rules in [Instructions to offerors in competitive acquisitions](est-instructions-to-offerors-competitive-acquisition.md), and other contract types a solicitation may name are covered in [Retroactive price redetermination and no-fee cost clauses](est-retroactive-price-redetermination-and-no-fee-cost-clauses.md).
