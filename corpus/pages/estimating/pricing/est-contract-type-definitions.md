## Purpose

Collects the terms that the definitions section of the contract-types part defines, each with its full definition: the Award-Fee Board, the established price, the Fee-Determining Official and rollover of unearned award fee.

| Codified citation | FAR 16.001 [@CL-EST-0973]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9331; @CL-EST-9332] |

Section 16.001 opens by saying the terms are defined for use in part 16 [@CL-EST-0973]. It then defines four terms [@CL-EST-0940; @CL-EST-0941; @CL-EST-0942; @CL-EST-0943].

An Award-Fee Board is the team of individuals identified in the award-fee plan who have been designated to assist the Fee-Determining Official in making award-fee determinations [@CL-EST-0940].

A Fee-Determining Official is the designated Agency official or officials who review the recommendations of the Award-Fee Board in determining the amount of award fee to be earned by the contractor for each evaluation period [@CL-EST-0942].

Rollover of unearned award fee is the process of transferring unearned award fee, which the contractor had an opportunity to earn, from one evaluation period to a subsequent evaluation period [@CL-EST-0943]. It gives the contractor an additional opportunity to earn that previously unearned award fee [@CL-EST-0943].

An established price is a price that meets both of two conditions [@CL-EST-0941]. The first is that it is an established catalog or market price for a commercial product sold in substantial quantities to the general public [@CL-EST-0941]. The second is that it is the net price after applying any standard trade discounts offered by the contractor [@CL-EST-0941].

## How it works

The definitions name two award-fee roles. The board assists the Fee-Determining Official [@CL-EST-0940]. The official reviews the board's recommendations in determining the amount of award fee to be earned for each evaluation period [@CL-EST-0942]. For the conditions under which award-fee contracts are used and how fee is earned, see [award-fee contracts](est-award-fee-contracts.md).

Rollover moves unearned award fee from one evaluation period to a later one and gives the contractor another opportunity to earn it [@CL-EST-0943]. The definition describes the unearned award fee as fee which the contractor had an opportunity to earn [@CL-EST-0943].

An established price is a price that is an established catalog or market price for a commercial product sold in substantial quantities to the general public and is the net price after applying any standard trade discounts offered by the contractor [@CL-EST-0941].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An award-fee plan names a board of five people. The plan sets evaluation periods of six months. The contractor earns 40,000 of a possible 60,000 award fee in the first period, so 20,000 stays unearned. Rollover is the process of transferring that unearned 20,000, which the contractor had an opportunity to earn, to a subsequent evaluation period, which gives the contractor an additional opportunity to earn it [@CL-EST-0943]. The Fee-Determining Official reviews the board's recommendations in determining the award fee to be earned for each evaluation period [@CL-EST-0942]. Separately, a commercial product has an established catalog price of 100 and is sold in substantial quantities to the general public. The contractor offers a standard trade discount of 10 percent, so the net price is 90. The second element of the definition asks for the net price after applying that discount, which is 90 and not the catalog price of 100 [@CL-EST-0941].

## Never confuse

An established price is an established catalog or market price for a commercial product sold in substantial quantities to the general public that is also the net price after applying any standard trade discounts offered by the contractor [@CL-EST-0941]. The FAR definitions section defines a catalog price for purposes of the services described in paragraph (2) of its definition of commercial service, as a price included in a catalog, price list, schedule, or other form that is regularly maintained by the manufacturer or vendor [@CL-EST-9331]. Under that definition the price is also either published or otherwise available for inspection by customers, and it states prices at which sales are currently, or were last, made to a significant number of buyers constituting the general public [@CL-EST-9332]. The board assists in making award-fee determinations, and the Fee-Determining Official reviews the board's recommendations in determining the award fee to be earned [@CL-EST-0940; @CL-EST-0942].
