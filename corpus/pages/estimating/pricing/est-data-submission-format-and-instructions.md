## Purpose

Shows what the solicitation must say about cost or pricing data, which formats are allowed, and what DoD adds for forward pricing rate proposals.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and DFARS [@CL-EST-1265; @CL-EST-1269] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.403-5 [@CL-EST-1265]; DFARS 215.403-5 [@CL-EST-1269] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1265; @CL-EST-1269] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that call for cost or pricing data, and DoD forward pricing rate proposals under the contract cost principles [@CL-EST-1265; @CL-EST-1269] |

Taking into consideration the pricing policy, the contracting officer shall specify in the solicitation, among listed items, whether certified cost or pricing data are required [@CL-EST-1265]. When certification is required, the contracting officer may require submission of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-1303] in the format indicated in the cross-referenced table, specify an alternative format, or permit submission in the contractor's format, unless the data are required to be submitted on one of the termination forms [@CL-EST-1266].

When data other than certified cost or pricing data are required by the contracting officer [@CL-EST-1304], they may be submitted in the offeror's own format unless the contracting officer decides that use of a specific format is essential for evaluating and determining that the price is fair and reasonable and the format has been described in the solicitation [@CL-EST-1267]. Data supporting forward pricing rate agreements or final indirect cost proposals shall be submitted in a form acceptable to the contracting officer [@CL-EST-1268].

## How it works

The contracting officer decides whether a specific format is essential for data other than certified cost or pricing data, and the format must be described in the solicitation [@CL-EST-1267]. The DoD addition applies to contractors following the contract cost principles pursuant to the FAR procedures, where the administrative contracting officer shall require compliance with the submission items in the DFARS table [@CL-EST-1269]. For those contractors, the contracting officer should request that the forward pricing rate proposal be submitted at least 90 days prior to the proposed effective date of the rates [@CL-EST-1269; @CL-EST-1270]. The contracting officer shall also request that the contractor complete the Contractor Forward Pricing Rate Proposal Adequacy Checklist and submit it with the forward pricing rate proposal [@CL-EST-1269; @CL-EST-1271].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company receives a solicitation that says certified cost or pricing data are required, and that data other than certified data may go in its own format. It submits its own format. Separately, its administrative contracting officer asks for a forward pricing rate proposal with the adequacy checklist, to be delivered well ahead of the date the rates should take effect.

## Never confuse

A forward pricing rate proposal is not a proposal for a contract price. The DoD checklist applies to the rate proposal [@CL-EST-1271], and data supporting rate agreements shall be submitted in a form acceptable to the contracting officer [@CL-EST-1268]. The rate side is covered on [Forward pricing rate agreements](../../indirect-rates/rate-lifecycle/rte-forward-pricing-rate-agreements.md). The data the contracting officer asks for is on [Pricing policy and the order of preference for data](est-pricing-policy-data-preference.md).
