## Purpose

Shows what an invoice under the invoices clause must state, and what the notice to supplier says about firm orders, invoices and a supplier that cannot perform exactly as ordered.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2293] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.213-2 [@CL-EST-2293]; FAR 52.213-3 [@CL-EST-2295] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent [@CL-EST-0588] [@CL-EST-0589] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2293] |
| Applies to | Invoices for subscription delivery under the invoices clause [@CL-EST-2294], and orders that carry the notice to supplier [@CL-EST-2295] |

Invoices must be submitted before payment can be made [@CL-EST-2293]. Payment is made on the basis of the invoice, which must state the starting and ending dates of the subscription delivery and either that orders have been placed in effect for the addressees required or that the orders will be placed in effect upon receipt of payment [@CL-EST-2294].

## How it works

The order is a firm order only if the supplier's price does not exceed the maximum line item or total price in the Schedule, and the supplier is to submit invoices to the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2295]. A supplier that cannot perform in exact accordance with the order must withhold performance and notify the contracting officer immediately, giving its quotation [@CL-EST-2296].

Class Deviation 2026-O0038 directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text lists FAR 52.213-2 as [Reserved] and prints no clause text under that number [@CL-EST-0588], and lists FAR 52.213-3 as [Reserved] and prints no clause text under that number [@CL-EST-0589].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A subscription order for 12 monthly issues is priced at 6,000 against a Schedule maximum of 6,000. The price does not exceed the maximum, so the order is a firm order. The supplier's invoice goes to the contracting officer and states the first and last delivery dates and that the orders have been placed in effect for the required addressees.

Had the supplier priced the same order at 6,400, above the Schedule maximum of 6,000, the order would not be a firm order. A supplier that cannot deliver the issues exactly as ordered withholds performance and tells the contracting officer immediately, with its quotation.

This example applies the codified clauses at FAR 52.213-2 and FAR 52.213-3 [@CL-EST-2293] [@CL-EST-2295]; the RFO text lists both numbers as [Reserved] [@CL-EST-0588] [@CL-EST-0589].

## Never confuse

A firm order is conditional: the order is firm only if the price does not exceed the Schedule maximum [@CL-EST-2295]. A supplier that cannot perform in exact accordance with the order withholds performance and notifies the contracting officer immediately, giving its quotation [@CL-EST-2296].

An invoice must be submitted before payment can be made, and the invoice states either that orders are already in effect or that they will be placed in effect on receipt of payment [@CL-EST-2293; @CL-EST-2294]. See [Commercial contract terms: invoice content and overpayments](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-commercial-terms-invoice-content-and-overpayments.md).

The codified clauses at FAR 52.213-2 and FAR 52.213-3 print the invoice contents and the notice to supplier [@CL-EST-2293] [@CL-EST-2295], but the RFO text lists both numbers as [Reserved] with no clause text [@CL-EST-0588] [@CL-EST-0589].
