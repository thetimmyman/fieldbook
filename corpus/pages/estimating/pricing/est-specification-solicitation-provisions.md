## Purpose

Shows which availability provision the contracting officer must insert when a solicitation cites a specification, and how the choice follows where the document is listed and how it can be reached.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1029] |
| Codified citation | FAR 11.204 [@CL-EST-1029] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1029] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that cite specifications, by where the document is listed and how it can be obtained or examined [@CL-EST-1030; @CL-EST-1032; @CL-EST-1034; @CL-EST-1036] |

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.211-1, on the availability of specifications listed in the GSA Index [@CL-EST-1029], in solicitations that cite specifications listed in the Index that are not furnished with the solicitation [@CL-EST-1030].

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.211-2, on the availability of Defense specifications, standards and data item descriptions in ASSIST [@CL-EST-1031], in solicitations that cite specifications available in ASSIST that are not furnished with the solicitation [@CL-EST-1032].

The contracting officer shall insert a provision substantially the same as the provision at 52.211-3, on the availability of specifications not listed in the GSA Index [@CL-EST-1033], in solicitations that cite specifications that are not listed in the Index and are not furnished with the solicitation, but may be obtained from a designated source [@CL-EST-1034].

The contracting officer shall insert a provision substantially the same as the provision at 52.211-4, on the availability for examination of specifications not listed in the GSA Index [@CL-EST-1035], in solicitations that cite specifications that are not listed in the Index and are available for examination at a specified location [@CL-EST-1036].

## How it works

The contracting officer chooses the provision from facts about each cited specification: whether it is listed in the GSA Index or available in ASSIST, whether it is furnished with the solicitation, and, for a specification outside the Index, whether it can be obtained from a designated source or examined at a specified location [@CL-EST-1030; @CL-EST-1032; @CL-EST-1034; @CL-EST-1036].

The first two prescriptions call for the provisions at 52.211-1 and 52.211-2 [@CL-EST-1029; @CL-EST-1031]. The second two allow a provision substantially the same as the provisions at 52.211-3 and 52.211-4 [@CL-EST-1033; @CL-EST-1035].

The prescriptions for the provisions at 52.211-1, 52.211-2 and 52.211-3 apply to specifications not furnished with the solicitation [@CL-EST-1030; @CL-EST-1032; @CL-EST-1034]. The prescription for the provision at 52.211-4 applies to solicitations that cite specifications that are not listed in the Index and are available for examination at a specified location [@CL-EST-1036].

For how an offeror reaches the document under each provision, see [Where to get specifications, standards and data item descriptions](est-availability-of-specifications-standards-and-dids.md) and [Examining specifications and proposing alternatives to Government-unique standards](est-specification-examination-and-alternative-standards.md). For the duty to identify documents and to furnish them or give instructions, see [Identifying, citing and maintaining specifications and standards](est-specification-identification-and-maintenance.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation cites one coating specification listed in the GSA Index and not furnished with the solicitation. The contracting officer inserts the provision at 52.211-1 [@CL-EST-1029; @CL-EST-1030]. The solicitation also cites one Defense standard available in ASSIST and not furnished. The contracting officer inserts the provision at 52.211-2 [@CL-EST-1031; @CL-EST-1032]. It cites a third document, an agency handbook that is not listed in the Index, that a depot lets offerors read by appointment. For it, the contracting officer inserts a provision substantially the same as the provision at 52.211-4 [@CL-EST-1035; @CL-EST-1036]. If the handbook could instead be obtained from a designated source and was not furnished, a provision substantially the same as the provision at 52.211-3 would fit [@CL-EST-1033; @CL-EST-1034].

## Never confuse

The four provisions are chosen by where the cited document is listed and how it can be reached [@CL-EST-1030; @CL-EST-1032; @CL-EST-1034; @CL-EST-1036].

The prescriptions for the provisions at 52.211-3 and 52.211-4 call for a provision substantially the same as the named provision [@CL-EST-1033; @CL-EST-1035], while those for the provisions at 52.211-1 and 52.211-2 call for the named provision [@CL-EST-1029; @CL-EST-1031].

## Company calibration

The company must confirm which availability provision each solicitation carries and that its pricing file matches the document version that provision points to. [@Q-004]
