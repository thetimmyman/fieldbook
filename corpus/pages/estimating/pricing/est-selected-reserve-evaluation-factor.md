## Purpose

Explains the DoD evaluation factor for offerors that intend to use employees or individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve, the definition of Selected Reserve that goes with it, and the contract clause used when a solicitation includes the factor. The subpart heading is Evaluation factor for employing or subcontracting with members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2810]. Whether a contracting officer may use the factor is on [the Selected Reserve debriefing card](est-dfars-selected-reserve-debriefing-and-follow-up.md), and the related provision and clause are on [the resolicit notice card](est-dfars-resolicit-notice-and-selected-reserve-clause.md).

The definition at 215.370-1 says that, as used in this section, Selected Reserve has the meaning given that term in section 10143 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-EST-2796]. Selected Reserve members normally attend regular drills throughout the year and are the group of Reserves most readily available to the President [@CL-EST-2797].

The section directs use of the clause at 252.215-7006, Use of Employees or Individual Subcontractors Who Are Members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2798]. The clause is used in solicitations and resulting contracts that include an evaluation factor considering whether an offeror intends to perform the contract using employees or individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2799].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for services covers 40 positions, and its evaluation factor considers whether an offeror intends to use individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve for 12 of them. The offeror states that intent in its proposal. Because the solicitation includes that evaluation factor, the clause at 252.215-7006 is used in the solicitation and in the resulting contract [@CL-EST-2798; @CL-EST-2799].
