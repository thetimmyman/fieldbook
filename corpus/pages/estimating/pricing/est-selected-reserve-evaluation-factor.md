## Purpose

Explains the DoD evaluation factor for offerors that intend to use employees or individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve, the definitions that go with it, and the contract clause used when a solicitation includes the factor. The subpart heading is Evaluation factor for employing or subcontracting with members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2810]. Whether a contracting officer may use the factor is on [the Selected Reserve debriefing card](est-dfars-selected-reserve-debriefing-and-follow-up.md), and the related provision and clause are on [the resolicit notice card](est-dfars-resolicit-notice-and-selected-reserve-clause.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-2796; @CL-EST-2798] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.370-1 [@CL-EST-2796]; DFARS 215.370-3 [@CL-EST-2798] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2796; @CL-EST-2798] |
| Applies to | The definitions apply as used in section 215.370-1 [@CL-EST-2796]; the clause is used in solicitations and resulting contracts that include the Selected Reserve evaluation factor [@CL-EST-2798; @CL-EST-2799] |

As used in section 215.370-1, Selected Reserve has the meaning given that term in section 10143 of title 10 of the United States Code [@CL-EST-2796]. Selected Reserve members normally attend regular drills throughout the year and are the group of Reserves most readily available to the President [@CL-EST-2797].

The section directs use of the clause at 252.215-7006, Use of Employees or Individual Subcontractors Who Are Members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2798]. The clause is used in solicitations and resulting contracts that include an evaluation factor considering whether an offeror intends to perform the contract using employees or individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2799].

### Terms used

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Selected Reserve | Has the meaning given that term in section 10143 of title 10 of the United States Code, as used in section 215.370-1 [@CL-EST-2796]; the same entry goes on to describe Selected Reserve members, in the next row [@CL-EST-2797] |
| Selected Reserve members | Normally attend regular drills throughout the year and are the group of Reserves most readily available to the President [@CL-EST-2797] |
| Evaluation factor that calls for the clause (described in the section, not defined) | A factor considering whether an offeror intends to perform the contract using employees or individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2799] |
| Contract clause | 252.215-7006, Use of Employees or Individual Subcontractors Who Are Members of the Selected Reserve [@CL-EST-2798] |

The section lists no exclusions from either definition [@CL-EST-2796; @CL-EST-2797].

## How it works

The first definition works by reference. It adopts the statutory meaning instead of restating it [@CL-EST-2796]. The second sentence describes Selected Reserve members by two traits: regular drills through the year, and availability to the President [@CL-EST-2797]. The word "normally" sits with the drill-attendance trait [@CL-EST-2797].

The clause follows the evaluation factor. It is used in both the solicitation and the resulting contract when the factor is included [@CL-EST-2799]. The section does not name who inserts the clause, and it does not state the clause's own terms [@CL-EST-2798].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation for services covers 40 positions, and its evaluation factor considers whether an offeror intends to use individual subcontractors who are members of the Selected Reserve for 12 of them. The offeror states that intent in its proposal. Because the solicitation includes that evaluation factor, the clause at 252.215-7006 is used in the solicitation and in the resulting contract [@CL-EST-2798; @CL-EST-2799].

## Never confuse

An evaluation factor is not the clause. The factor is what the solicitation considers about the offeror's staffing intent. The clause is what the solicitation and the resulting contract include when that factor is present [@CL-EST-2799]. The first definitional sentence points to the statute and does not restate it, and the second sentence describes how members normally serve [@CL-EST-2796; @CL-EST-2797].

## Company calibration

Whether any planned bid will carry the Selected Reserve evaluation factor, who confirms the clause is in both the solicitation and the contract, and what review evidence supports the staffing intent stated in the proposal. [@Q-012]
