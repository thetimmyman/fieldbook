## Purpose

Separates three rules that decide how an offeror answers a solicitation: the information request used for planning, facsimile proposals, and the order that ranks conflicting documents. It also points to the place of performance card.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1918; @CL-EST-1920; @CL-EST-1925] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-3 [@CL-EST-1918]; FAR 52.215-5 [@CL-EST-1920]; FAR 52.215-8 [@CL-EST-1925] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-8 [@CL-EST-0432]; RFO part 52 lists FAR 52.215-3 as reserved [@CL-EST-0430] and FAR 52.215-5 as reserved [@CL-EST-0431] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1918] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include the information or planning provision [@CL-EST-1397]; solicitations that include the facsimile provision, which is inserted if facsimile proposals are authorized [@CL-EST-1496]; solicitations and contracts that include the order of precedence clause [@CL-EST-1382] |

Under the information provision, the Government does not intend to award a contract on the basis of the solicitation or otherwise to pay for the information solicited, except as an allowable cost under other contracts as provided in the Federal Acquisition Regulation subsection on bid and proposal costs [@CL-EST-1918]. Although the words proposal and offeror are used in the request, the response is treated as information only and shall not be used as a proposal [@CL-EST-1919]. The provision also has a fill-in paragraph that states the purpose of the solicitation, which is contract-specific [@CL-EST-1965].

A facsimile proposal is a proposal, a revision or modification of a proposal, or a withdrawal of a proposal that is transmitted to and received by the Government via facsimile machine [@CL-EST-1966]. Offerors may submit facsimile proposals as responses to the solicitation, and facsimile proposals are subject to the same rules as paper proposals [@CL-EST-1920]. The provision names the telephone number of the receiving facsimile equipment in a fill-in paragraph, which is contract-specific [@CL-EST-1967].

If any portion of a facsimile proposal received by the Contracting Officer is unreadable to the degree that conformance to the essential requirements of the solicitation cannot be ascertained from the document, the Contracting Officer immediately shall notify the offeror [@CL-EST-1921] and permit the offeror to resubmit the proposal [@CL-EST-2018]. The Contracting Officer prescribes the method and time for resubmission after consultation with the offeror [@CL-EST-1922]. The resubmission is considered as if it were received at the date and time of the original unreadable submission for the purpose of determining timeliness, provided the offeror complies with the time and format requirements for resubmission that the Contracting Officer prescribes [@CL-EST-1923].

The Government reserves the right to make award solely on the facsimile proposal [@CL-EST-1924]. If the Contracting Officer requests it, the apparently successful offeror promptly shall submit the complete original signed proposal [@CL-EST-1924].

Any inconsistency in the solicitation or contract is resolved by giving precedence in this order: the Schedule (excluding the specifications), representations and other instructions, contract clauses, other documents, exhibits and attachments, and last the specifications [@CL-EST-1925].

The place of performance provision is covered on [Place of performance in a solicitation response](est-place-of-performance.md). In it, the offeror or respondent checks a block to state whether, in the performance of any contract resulting from the solicitation, it intends or does not intend to use plants or facilities at a different address from its own [@CL-EST-2048; @CL-EST-1926].

## How it works

The information provision changes what a response is: it is information only and cannot be used as a proposal [@CL-EST-1919]. The facsimile provision keeps a facsimile submission a proposal, subject to the paper rules [@CL-EST-1920]. Award may rest on the facsimile proposal alone, and the Contracting Officer may ask the apparently successful offeror for the signed original [@CL-EST-1924]. The order of precedence clause ranks the document classes so that an inconsistency is resolved by class [@CL-EST-1925]. The purpose statement and the facsimile telephone number are filled in for each solicitation [@CL-EST-1965; @CL-EST-1967].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.215-3 as reserved [@CL-EST-0430] and FAR 52.215-5 as reserved [@CL-EST-0431], with no provision text under those numbers. RFO FAR 52.215-8 says that an inconsistency in the request for proposal or contract is resolved by giving precedence in the same order as the codified clause: the Schedule excluding the specifications, representations and other instructions, contract clauses, other documents, exhibits and attachments, and the specifications [@CL-EST-0432; @CL-EST-1925]. Where the codified clause says an inconsistency in the solicitation or contract, the RFO clause says in the request for proposal or contract [@CL-EST-0432; @CL-EST-1925].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror faxes a proposal of 12 pages. Page 4 arrives so badly degraded that the Contracting Officer cannot tell whether the essential requirements are met. The Contracting Officer notifies the offeror at once and, after consulting it, sets resubmission for 3 pm the next day in the same format. The offeror resubmits on time and in that format, so the resubmission counts as received when the original fax arrived. Later, the apparently successful offeror is asked for the complete original signed proposal and sends it promptly.

Separately, the Schedule prices 20 hours of testing while an attached specification calls for 40 hours. The Schedule excluding the specifications ranks first and the specifications rank last, so the Schedule governs. If a contract clause and the representations page conflict, the representations and other instructions rank above contract clauses.

## Never confuse

An information request is not a solicitation for award: its response shall not be used as a proposal [@CL-EST-1919]. A facsimile proposal is the opposite: it is a proposal and follows the paper rules [@CL-EST-1920]. The order of precedence is the ranking that resolves an inconsistency [@CL-EST-1925]. When proposals are due and how late ones are treated are on [Submitting, modifying, withdrawing and late proposals](est-proposal-submission-and-late-proposals.md), and when each provision is inserted is on [Advisory multi-step process and source selection provisions and clauses](est-advisory-multi-step-and-source-selection-provisions.md).

The RFO text prints no provision text under FAR 52.215-3 or FAR 52.215-5 [@CL-EST-0430; @CL-EST-0431], where the codified text of those sections sets out the information-only rule for a response to a request for information [@CL-EST-1919] and the facsimile proposal rules [@CL-EST-1920].

The facsimile bid provision for sealed bidding is covered in [Facsimile bids: conditions, receipt rules and the original bid](../../market/programs/mkt-facsimile-bids.md).
