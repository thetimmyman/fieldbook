## Purpose

Sets out the written determination that a letter contract needs before it is used and the three limits that the limitations section places on letter contracts, including the limit on amending one.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0968] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.603-3 [@CL-EST-0968] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0968] |
| Applies to | Any use of a letter contract [@CL-EST-0968] |

A letter contract may be used only after the head of the contracting activity or a designee determines in writing that no other contract is suitable [@CL-EST-0968]. The head of the contracting activity or the designee decides, and the determination must be in writing [@CL-EST-0968].

Letter contracts shall not do any of three things [@CL-EST-0969; @CL-EST-0970; @CL-EST-0971]:

- Paragraph (a): commit the Government to a definitive contract in excess of the funds available at the time the letter contract is executed [@CL-EST-0969].
- Paragraph (b): be entered into without competition when competition is required by part 6 [@CL-EST-0970].
- Paragraph (c): be amended to satisfy a new requirement unless that requirement is inseparable from the existing letter contract [@CL-EST-0971].

Any such amendment is subject to the same requirements and limitations as a new letter contract [@CL-EST-0972].


## How it works

The gate is a written determination by the head of the contracting activity or a designee that no other contract is suitable. Use is permitted only after that determination [@CL-EST-0968]. Three limits then apply to the letter contract.

It may not commit the Government to a definitive contract in excess of the funds available at the time the letter contract is executed [@CL-EST-0969]. It may not be entered into without competition when competition is required by part 6 [@CL-EST-0970]. It may not be amended to satisfy a new requirement unless that requirement is inseparable from the existing letter contract [@CL-EST-0971]. An amendment to satisfy a new requirement is subject to the same requirements and limitations as a new letter contract [@CL-EST-0972].

The competition limit is conditional. It applies when part 6 requires competition [@CL-EST-0970]. The amendment limit has one exception: a new requirement that is inseparable from the existing letter contract [@CL-EST-0971]. For when a letter contract fits and how it is definitized, see [fixed-price incentive contracts and letter contracts](est-fixed-price-incentive-and-letter-contracts.md). For the offeror provisions about letter contracts, see [type of contract and letter contract provisions](est-type-of-contract-letter-and-time-and-materials-provisions.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An agency needs work started at once and has 500,000 available. The head of the contracting activity signs a written determination that no other contract is suitable, so a letter contract may be used, provided the limits below are also met [@CL-EST-0968]. If part 6 requires competition for this work, the letter contract may not be entered into without it [@CL-EST-0970]. Because 500,000 is the amount available when the letter contract is executed, the letter contract may not commit the Government to a definitive contract in excess of 500,000 [@CL-EST-0969]. Later the program wants to add a new requirement that can be split from the existing letter contract. The letter contract may not be amended to satisfy that requirement, because it is not inseparable from the existing letter contract [@CL-EST-0971]. If the new requirement were inseparable, the bar on amending for a new requirement would not apply to it, and the amendment would be subject to the same requirements and limitations as a new letter contract [@CL-EST-0971; @CL-EST-0972].

## Never confuse

The written determination under the first sentence supports use of the letter contract. The limit on amending for a new requirement is a separate limit that must be met on its own [@CL-EST-0968; @CL-EST-0971]. An amendment for a new requirement must be for a requirement that is inseparable from the existing letter contract, and it is subject to the same requirements and limitations as a new letter contract [@CL-EST-0971; @CL-EST-0972]. The funds limit refers to the funds available at the time the letter contract is executed [@CL-EST-0969].
