## Purpose

Shows when DoD requires a structured approach to the profit or fee objective and when the weighted guidelines are replaced by another approach.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1288] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.404-4 [@CL-EST-1288]; DFARS 215.404-72 [@CL-EST-1316]; DFARS 215.404-73 [@CL-EST-1307] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1288] |
| Applies to | Negotiated contract actions where certified cost or pricing data is obtained, other than cost-plus-award-fee contracts and contracts with Federally Funded Research and Development Centers [@CL-EST-1288; @CL-EST-1289] |

Contracting officers shall use a structured approach for developing a prenegotiation profit or fee objective on any negotiated contract action when certified cost or pricing data is obtained, subject to listed exceptions that include cost-plus-award-fee contracts [@CL-EST-1288]. A further listed exception is contracts with Federally Funded Research and Development Centers [@CL-EST-1289].

When using a structured approach, the contracting officer shall use the weighted guidelines method, except as provided in two other listed paragraphs [@CL-EST-1290]. One of those paragraphs requires the modified weighted guidelines method on contract actions with nonprofit organizations other than FFRDCs [@CL-EST-1290; @CL-EST-1291].

## How it works

When a structured approach is used, the weighted guidelines method applies except where the modified method or an alternate structured approach is provided for, the modified method being for nonprofit organizations other than FFRDCs [@CL-EST-1290; @CL-EST-1291; @CL-EST-1292]. The contracting officer may use an alternate structured approach when the contract action is at or below the certified cost or pricing data threshold, is for architect-engineer or construction work, or is primarily for delivery of material from subcontractors, among other listed cases [@CL-EST-1292]. A further listed case is a termination settlement [@CL-EST-1292; @CL-EST-1308]. A second listed basis is that the weighted guidelines method does not produce a reasonable overall profit objective and the head of the contracting activity approves use of the alternate approach in writing [@CL-EST-1292; @CL-EST-1293].

For all other nonprofit organizations except FFRDCs, a fee objective for covered actions is computed using the weighted guidelines method, modified as described in the cross-referenced paragraph [@CL-EST-1316]. In the modification to performance risk, if the contracting officer assigns a value from the standard designated range, the fee objective is reduced by an amount equal to 1 percent of the costs in Block 20 of the DD Form 1547 [@CL-EST-1315]. The contracting officer may design the structure of the alternate approach, but it shall include consideration of the three basic components of profit: performance risk, contract type risk including working capital, and facilities capital employed [@CL-EST-1307]. That consideration does not require the contracting officer to complete Blocks 21 through 30 of the DD Form 1547 [@CL-EST-1314]. A listed offset for facilities capital cost of money requires the contracting officer to reduce the overall prenegotiation profit objective by the amount of facilities capital cost of money under Cost Accounting Standard 414 [@CL-EST-1307; @CL-EST-1317]. The cost-plus-award-fee and FFRDC exceptions each cross-refer to separate DFARS sections [@CL-EST-1288; @CL-EST-1289].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company proposes a cost-plus-fixed-fee action for 2,000,000 with certified data obtained, so the contracting officer uses the weighted guidelines. A nonprofit organization that is not an FFRDC would be handled with the modified method. A small action at or below the certified data threshold could use an alternate structured approach, which still has to consider performance risk, contract type risk and facilities capital employed.

## Never confuse

An exception to the structured-approach requirement removes that requirement for the contract type, but it does not itself set the profit or fee, and the text points to separate DFARS sections for those contract types [@CL-EST-1288; @CL-EST-1289]. The factor-by-factor method is on [general policy and performance risk](est-weighted-guidelines-general-and-performance-risk.md), [contract type risk](est-weighted-guidelines-contract-type-risk.md), [facilities capital](est-weighted-guidelines-facilities-capital.md) and [cost efficiency](est-weighted-guidelines-cost-efficiency.md), and the cost-based profit policy is on [Profit analysis in cost-based price negotiations](est-profit-analysis.md). The negotiation file records the basis for the profit or fee objective, as covered on [Price negotiation and the price negotiation memorandum](est-price-negotiation-documentation.md).
