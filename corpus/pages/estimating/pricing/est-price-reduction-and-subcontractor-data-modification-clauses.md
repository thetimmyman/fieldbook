## Purpose

Shows when the Government can reduce the price of a modification because certified data were defective, how an offset and the payback charges work, and what the Contractor must require of subcontractors.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1806] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.215-11 and FAR 52.215-13 [@CL-EST-1806; @CL-EST-1812] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.215-11 [@CL-EST-0433]; RFO FAR 52.215-13 [@CL-EST-0457] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1806] |
| Applies to | Modifications whose pricing adjustment is expected to exceed the certified data threshold on the date of execution [@CL-EST-1806; @CL-EST-1857] |

The price reduction clause becomes operative only for a modification involving a pricing adjustment [@CL-EST-9522] that is expected to exceed the certified data threshold on the date of execution of the modification, and it does not apply to any modification if an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) applies [@CL-EST-1806]. The reduction paragraph is triggered when any price, including profit or fee, negotiated in connection with any modification under the clause, or any cost reimbursable under the contract, was increased by any significant amount because of the causes it lists [@CL-EST-1851]. One listed ground is an increase of any significant amount caused by the Contractor or a subcontractor furnishing certified data that were not complete, accurate and current as certified [@CL-EST-1807]. A second listed ground is an increase of any significant amount caused by a subcontractor or prospective subcontractor furnishing the Contractor certified data that were not complete, accurate and current as certified in the Contractor's Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-9680]. A third listed ground is that any of these parties furnished data of any description that were not accurate [@CL-EST-1808]. Where the price or cost was increased by any significant amount because of a listed ground, the price or cost shall be reduced accordingly and the contract shall be modified to reflect the reduction [@CL-EST-1851; @CL-EST-1808]. The right to a price reduction is limited to that resulting from defects in data relating to modifications for which the clause becomes operative [@CL-EST-1852].

The subcontractor data clause is operative only for a modification to the contract involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed the certified data threshold on the date of execution [@CL-EST-1857]. Under it, the Contractor shall require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data, actually or by specific identification in writing, in accordance with the FAR data table instructions [@CL-EST-1858]. The requirement applies unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) applies [@CL-EST-1859]. The Contractor must insert the substance of the clause, including its paragraph (d), in each subcontract above the certified data threshold on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-1812]. The requirement to obtain subcontractor data applies before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed the certified data threshold on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-1879], or before pricing any subcontract modification involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed the certified data threshold [@CL-EST-1880]. The subcontractor's certified data include any information reasonably required to explain the subcontractor's estimating process, such as the judgmental factors applied and the mathematical or other methods used in the estimate, including those used in projecting from known data, and the nature and amount of any contingencies included in the price [@CL-EST-1881; @CL-EST-1859]. If the threshold specified in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) is adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR 1.109(a), then pursuant to FAR 1.109(d) the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-1882]. The subcontractor's certification, in substantially the form prescribed in FAR 15.406-2, is that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, the data submitted under paragraph (b) of the clause were accurate, complete and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price of the subcontract or subcontract modification [@CL-EST-1813].

## How it works

An offset in an amount the Contracting Officer finds appropriate on the facts is allowed against the amount of a contract price reduction, except as prohibited by the clause, if both the following conditions are met [@CL-EST-1809; @CL-EST-1853; @CL-EST-1854]. The Contractor must certify that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, it is entitled to the offset in the amount requested [@CL-EST-1853], and must prove that the certified data were available before the as-of date on its certificate and were not submitted before that date [@CL-EST-1854]. No offset is allowed if the Contractor knew the data were understated before the as-of date [@CL-EST-1855], or if the Government proves that the facts demonstrate that the contract price would not have increased in the amount to be offset even if the available data had been submitted before that date [@CL-EST-1856]. Where defective data come from a prospective subcontractor that was not subsequently awarded the subcontract, the reduction is limited to the amount, plus applicable overhead and profit markup, by which the actual subcontract, or the actual cost to the Contractor if there was no subcontract, was less than the prospective subcontract cost estimate submitted by the Contractor, provided that the actual subcontract price was not itself affected by defective certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-1876; @CL-EST-1877]. If a reduction in the contract price reduces the price of items for which payment was made prior to the date of the modification reflecting the price reduction, the Contractor shall be liable to and shall pay the United States, at the time the overpayment is repaid, interest compounded daily [@CL-EST-1810]. The interest is computed on the overpayment from the date or dates of overpayment to the date the Government is repaid, at the applicable underpayment rate effective for each quarter that the Secretary of the Treasury prescribes under section 6621(a)(2) of title 26 of the United States Code [@CL-EST-1878]. The Contractor is also liable for a penalty equal to the amount of the overpayment if the Contractor or a subcontractor knowingly submitted certified cost or pricing data that were incomplete, inaccurate or noncurrent [@CL-EST-1810; @CL-EST-1811].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.215-11(a) says the clause becomes operative only for a modification involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed the threshold for submission of certified cost or pricing data in FAR 15.403-3(a) on the date of execution of the modification [@CL-EST-0433], where the codified paragraph names the threshold at FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-1806]. It says the clause does not apply to any modification if an exception under FAR 15.403-2 applies [@CL-EST-0434], where the codified paragraph cites FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-1806]. Paragraph (b) keeps the reduction trigger for a price or cost increased by any significant amount [@CL-EST-0435; @CL-EST-1851], the ground of certified data that were not complete, accurate, and current as certified [@CL-EST-0436; @CL-EST-1807], the rule that data of any description that were not accurate reduce the price or cost and the contract is modified [@CL-EST-0437; @CL-EST-1808], and the limit to modifications for which the clause becomes operative [@CL-EST-0438; @CL-EST-1852]. RFO FAR 52.215-11(b)(2) states the second ground, that a subcontractor or prospective subcontractor furnished the Contractor certified cost or pricing data that were not complete, accurate, and current as certified in the Contractor's Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data [@CL-EST-9048; @CL-EST-9680]. Paragraph (c) keeps the limit on a reduction for defective data from a prospective subcontractor that did not receive the subcontract [@CL-EST-0446; @CL-EST-1876], and keeps its measure, the amount by which the actual subcontract or the actual cost to the Contractor was less than the prospective subcontract cost estimate, provided the actual subcontract price was not itself affected by defective certified data [@CL-EST-0447; @CL-EST-1877]. Paragraph (d)(2)(i) says an offset is allowed except as prohibited by paragraph (d)(2)(ii), where the codified paragraph says subdivision (d)(2)(ii) [@CL-EST-0448; @CL-EST-1809], and keeps the Contractor’s certification [@CL-EST-0449; @CL-EST-1853] and proof that the data were available before the as of date and not submitted before it [@CL-EST-0450; @CL-EST-1854]. Paragraph (d)(2)(ii) keeps the bars on an offset where the Contractor knew the data were understated [@CL-EST-0451; @CL-EST-1855] or the Government proves the price would not have increased in the amount offset [@CL-EST-0452; @CL-EST-1856]. Paragraph (e) keeps the Contractor’s liability to pay the United States, when an overpayment is repaid, interest compounded daily [@CL-EST-0453; @CL-EST-0454; @CL-EST-1810] at the underpayment rate for each quarter [@CL-EST-0455; @CL-EST-1878], and a penalty equal to the overpayment for knowing submission of incomplete, inaccurate, or noncurrent certified data [@CL-EST-0456; @CL-EST-1811]. RFO FAR 52.215-13(a) says the requirements of paragraphs (b) and (c) of the clause [@CL-EST-9042] become operative only for a modification involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed the threshold at FAR 15.403-3(a) on the date of execution [@CL-EST-0457], where the codified paragraph names the threshold at FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-1857], and are limited to such modifications [@CL-EST-9043]. Paragraph (b) says the Contractor requires subcontractor data before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed that threshold on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-0458; @CL-EST-1879], or before pricing any subcontract modification involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed it [@CL-EST-0459; @CL-EST-1880], and keeps the requirement that the subcontractor submit certified cost or pricing data, actually or by specific identification in writing [@CL-EST-0463; @CL-EST-1858]. It says the data follow FAR 15.408-2, Table 15-1 [@CL-EST-0464], where the codified paragraph cites FAR 15.408, Table 15-2 [@CL-EST-1858], and that the requirement applies unless an exception under FAR 15.403-2 applies [@CL-EST-0465; @CL-EST-1859], where the codified paragraph cites FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-1859]. It says a threshold adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR part 1 applies throughout the remaining term of the contract [@CL-EST-0466; @CL-EST-1882], where the codified paragraph cites FAR 1.109(a) and FAR 1.109(d) [@CL-EST-1882]. Paragraph (c) says the subcontractor certifies in substantially the form prescribed in FAR 15.403-4 [@CL-EST-0467], where the codified paragraph says FAR 15.406-2 [@CL-EST-1813], and the negotiated price it refers to is that of the subcontract or subcontract modification [@CL-EST-9044]. Paragraph (d) says the Contractor must insert the substance of the clause in each subcontract above the threshold at FAR 15.403-3(a) on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-0468; @CL-EST-1812].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A modification above the threshold is priced at 4,000,000 from certified data. Assume the vendor was an actual subcontractor, not a prospective subcontractor that did not receive the subcontract. After award, the Government finds a vendor quote that was available before the as-of date and that lowered one material price, cutting the price by 100,000 (ignoring markups). The price is reduced by 100,000 and the contract is modified. Separately, the Contractor shows a different cost of 20,000 that was available before the as-of date but never submitted. It certifies that it is entitled to the offset and proves the data were available and unsubmitted. The Contracting Officer may allow an offset of 20,000, which would leave a net reduction of 80,000. No offset is allowed if the Contractor knew that cost was understated before the as-of date, or if the Government proves the price would not have risen by 20,000 anyway. If the reduced amount was already paid, interest compounded daily runs from the dates of overpayment to the date the Government is repaid, at the underpayment rate for each quarter. If the omission was knowing, a penalty equal to the overpayment applies as well.

## Never confuse

The subcontractor data clause makes the Contractor require subcontractor data [@CL-EST-1858] and insert the substance of the clause in subcontracts [@CL-EST-1812], while the price reduction clause provides that the price or cost shall be reduced accordingly and the contract modified [@CL-EST-1808]. An offset is allowed against the amount of a contract price reduction if the Contractor certifies that it is entitled to the offset in the amount requested and proves that the certified data were available before the as-of date on its certificate and were not submitted before that date [@CL-EST-1809; @CL-EST-1853; @CL-EST-1854]. The wider treatment of defective data sits in [Defective pricing](est-defective-pricing.md), the subcontractor side sits in [Subcontractor Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause](est-subcontractor-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md), and the exceptions to the requirement for certified cost or pricing data are covered in [Requirements for certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-data-requirement-provision-and-modifications.md).

Both RFO clauses name the certified cost or pricing data threshold at FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-0433; @CL-EST-0457], where the codified clauses name it at FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-1806; @CL-EST-1857], and cite FAR 15.403-2 for the exception [@CL-EST-0434; @CL-EST-0465], where the codified clauses cite FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-1806; @CL-EST-1859].
