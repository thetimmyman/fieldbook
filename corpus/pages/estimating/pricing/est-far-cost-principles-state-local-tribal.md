## Purpose

Shows which contracts the State, local and tribal government cost principles cover, which contracts they exclude and who approves other exceptions. It also shows how a contract that refers to the nonprofit organization subpart is read.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2521] |
| Codified citation | FAR 31.107, 31.603, 31.108, 31.701, 31.702 and 31.703 [@CL-EST-2521; @CL-EST-2709; @CL-EST-2706; @CL-EST-2711; @CL-EST-2714; @CL-EST-2524] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2521] |
| Applies to | Contracts with State, local and federally recognized Indian tribal governments, and contracts that refer to the nonprofit organization subpart for allowable costs [@CL-EST-2521; @CL-EST-2524] |

Subpart 31.6 provides principles and standards for determining costs applicable to contracts with State, local, and federally recognized Indian tribal governments [@CL-EST-2521]. They provide the basis for a uniform approach to the problem of determining costs and to promote efficiency and better relationships between those governments and Federal Government entities [@CL-EST-2656].

The principles and standards apply to all programs that involve those contracts, except for the listed exceptions. One is contracts with publicly financed educational institutions subject to subpart 31.3 [@CL-EST-2522]. The other is contracts with publicly owned hospitals and other providers of medical care subject to requirements promulgated by the sponsoring Government agencies [@CL-EST-2523].

The Office of Management and Budget will approve any other exceptions in particular cases when adequate justification is presented [@CL-EST-2657].

A contract that refers to subpart 31.6 for determining allowable costs under contracts with State, local and Indian tribal governments is deemed to refer to the guidance that FAR 31.603(a) names [@CL-EST-2709]. That guidance is the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII, in effect on the date of the contract, and the contracting officer determines the allowability of costs in accordance with it [@CL-EST-2710].

Subpart 31.7 provides the principles for determining the cost applicable to work performed by nonprofit organizations under contracts with the Government [@CL-EST-2660]. For identification, a nonprofit organization is a business entity organized and operated exclusively for charitable, scientific, or educational purposes, of which no part of the net earnings inure to the benefit of any private shareholder or individual [@CL-EST-2711]. No substantial part of its activities is carrying on propaganda or otherwise attempting to influence legislation or participating in any political campaign on behalf of any candidate for public office [@CL-EST-2712], and it must be exempt from federal income taxation under section 501 of the Internal Revenue Code [@CL-EST-2713]. The OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix IV, sets forth principles for determining the costs applicable to work performed by nonprofit organizations [@CL-EST-2714]. Subpart 31.7 covers nonprofit organizations other than educational institutions and State and local governments [@CL-EST-2706], and other than those nonprofit organizations exempted under the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, appendix VIII [@CL-EST-2707]; FAR 31.108 refers to subpart 31.2 for the cost principles applicable to nonprofit organizations exempt from the cost principles in that guidance [@CL-EST-2708]. A contract which refers to subpart 31.7 for determining allowable costs is deemed to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix IV in effect on the date of the contract [@CL-EST-2524; @CL-EST-2658]. The contracting officer determines the allowability of costs in accordance with that guidance [@CL-EST-2658].

Agencies are not expected to place additional restrictions on individual items of cost [@CL-EST-2525]. Under section 3744 of title 10 and section 4304 of title 41 of the United States Code, the costs cited in FAR 31.603(b) are unallowable [@CL-EST-2659]. Section 31.108 contains exceptions to the cost principles for nonprofit organizations [@CL-EST-2661].

## How it works

Two organization types sit behind this card, and each works differently [@CL-EST-2521; @CL-EST-2660].

For State, local and tribal government contracts, subpart 31.6 provides the principles and standards, and a contract that refers to it for allowable costs is read as referring to the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendices V and VII [@CL-EST-2521; @CL-EST-2709; @CL-EST-2710]. The principles and standards have two listed exclusions: publicly financed educational institutions subject to subpart 31.3, and publicly owned hospitals and other medical care providers subject to requirements of their sponsoring Government agencies [@CL-EST-2522; @CL-EST-2523]. Any other exception is approved by the Office of Management and Budget in particular cases when adequate justification is presented [@CL-EST-2657].

For nonprofit organizations, a contract that refers to subpart 31.7 is read as referring to the OMB Uniform Guidance appendix for that organization type as in effect on the contract date [@CL-EST-2524; @CL-EST-2658]. The contracting officer determines allowability in accordance with it [@CL-EST-2524; @CL-EST-2658]. Subpart 31.7 does not cover educational institutions, State and local governments, or nonprofit organizations exempted under appendix VIII of that guidance [@CL-EST-2706; @CL-EST-2707].

Agencies are not expected to add restrictions on individual items of cost, but the costs cited in FAR 31.603(b) are unallowable under the two statutes the section cites [@CL-EST-2525; @CL-EST-2659]. FAR 31.702 points to 31.108 for exceptions to the cost principles for nonprofit organizations [@CL-EST-2661].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Federal agency awards a cost-reimbursement contract to a State agency under a program covered by subpart 31.6. The State, local and tribal government cost principles apply to it [@CL-EST-2521; @CL-EST-2522].

A publicly owned hospital under the same program has a contract subject to requirements promulgated by its sponsoring Government agency, so it is excluded [@CL-EST-2523]. A contract with a publicly financed university subject to subpart 31.3 is excluded as well [@CL-EST-2522]. If the agency wants another exception for one contract, the Office of Management and Budget approves other exceptions in particular cases when adequate justification is presented [@CL-EST-2657].

Separately, a contract with a nonprofit research organization refers to subpart 31.7 for determining allowable costs. It is deemed to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix IV in effect on the contract date, and the contracting officer determines allowability in accordance with it [@CL-EST-2524; @CL-EST-2658]. If the organization's cost submission included a cost that FAR 31.603(b) cites, that cost would be unallowable as that paragraph describes it [@CL-EST-2659].

## Never confuse

These are not the commercial organization rules and not the educational institution rules.

A publicly financed educational institution that is subject to subpart 31.3 is excluded from the State, local and tribal government principles, and a publicly owned hospital or other medical care provider subject to its sponsoring Government agencies' requirements is excluded too [@CL-EST-2522; @CL-EST-2523].

Subpart 31.7 provides the nonprofit principles, but a contract that refers to it for allowable costs is deemed to refer to the OMB Uniform Guidance, and subpart 31.7 does not reach educational institutions, State and local governments, or nonprofit organizations exempted under appendix VIII [@CL-EST-2660; @CL-EST-2658; @CL-EST-2706; @CL-EST-2707]. The costs cited in FAR 31.603(b) [@CL-EST-2659] are listed on the [State, local and tribal governments](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cost-principles-state-local-tribal-governments.md) card.
