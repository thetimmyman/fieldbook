## Purpose

Shows what a contracting officer must do to reach a fair and reasonable price, which data comes first, and what a proposal team should expect to be asked for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1250] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.402 [@CL-EST-1250] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1250] |
| Applies to | Contracting officers purchasing supplies and services [@CL-EST-1250] |

Contracting officers shall purchase supplies and services from responsible sources at fair and reasonable prices [@CL-EST-1250]. In establishing the reasonableness of offered prices, the contracting officer shall obtain certified cost or pricing data when required by the cross-referenced section, along with data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1251].

When certified cost or pricing data are not required, the contracting officer shall obtain data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary, generally using a listed order of preference [@CL-EST-1252]. One listed duty is to obtain the type and quantity of data necessary, but not more data than is necessary [@CL-EST-1256]. Under the codified FAR, certified cost or pricing data are required above $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018, unless an exception applies [@CL-EST-0001]. For DoD prime contracts awarded after June 30, 2026, they are required when the contract is expected to exceed $10 million, unless an exception applies [@CL-EST-0002]. The threshold is covered on [Certified cost or pricing data threshold](est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md).

## How it works

One listed item in the order of preference is no additional data from the offeror when the price is based on adequate price competition, except as provided by a cross-referenced paragraph [@CL-EST-1253]. Another listed item is data other than certified cost or pricing data, such as data related to prices, including established catalog or market prices and sales to non-governmental and governmental entities [@CL-EST-1301]. For data related to prices, the contracting officer relies first on data available within the Government, second on data from sources other than the offeror, and, if necessary, on data from the offeror [@CL-EST-1254]. When obtaining data from the offeror is necessary, then unless an exception under the cross-referenced paragraphs applies, such data shall include, at a minimum, appropriate data on the prices at which the same or similar items have been sold previously [@CL-EST-1255], and prior-sale price data required from the offeror shall be adequate for evaluating the reasonableness of the price [@CL-EST-1300]. A further listed item is cost data to the extent necessary for the contracting officer to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1302]. If the analyses of the data obtained to date cannot establish a fair and reasonable price, the contracting officer shall require additional data sufficient to support the determination [@CL-EST-1257]. A proposal team should therefore expect requests for price data, including prior-sale prices where data must come from the offeror, and a further request if the contracting officer cannot establish a fair and reasonable price from what has been submitted [@CL-EST-1255; @CL-EST-1257].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company offers a spare part at 12,000 per unit, and the price is not based on adequate price competition. The contracting officer finds no usable price data inside the Government or from other sources, so asks the company for the prices at which the same part sold before. Those sales support the offered price, so no further data is requested. Had the sales data been too thin to establish a fair and reasonable price, the contracting officer would have required more data.

## Never confuse

Data other than certified cost or pricing data are not certified cost or pricing data. The policy asks for certified data when the cross-referenced section requires it, and otherwise for the data necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1251; @CL-EST-1252]. The exceptions to certification are on [Exceptions to the requirement for certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-data-exceptions.md) and, for DoD, on [DoD exceptions, waivers and limits](est-dod-certified-data-exceptions-and-waivers.md). The analysis methods are on [Proposal analysis techniques](est-proposal-analysis.md), the format for submitting the data is on [Instructions for submitting cost or pricing data](est-data-submission-format-and-instructions.md), and what the negotiation file records is on [Price negotiation and the price negotiation memorandum](est-price-negotiation-documentation.md).
