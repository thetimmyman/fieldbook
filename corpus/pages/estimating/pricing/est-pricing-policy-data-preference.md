## Purpose

Shows what a contracting officer must do to reach a fair and reasonable price, which data comes first, and what a proposal team should expect to be asked for.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1250] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.402 [@CL-EST-1250]; FAR 15.400 [@CL-EST-9191]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9318] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.202(d) [@CL-EST-3367]; RFO FAR 15.402 [@CL-EST-3368; @CL-EST-3379]; RFO FAR 15.403-1 [@CL-EST-3370]; RFO FAR 15.400 [@CL-EST-2256]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9363] |

| Applies to | Contracting officers purchasing supplies and services [@CL-EST-1250] |

Contracting officers shall purchase supplies and services from responsible sources at fair and reasonable prices [@CL-EST-1250]. In establishing the reasonableness of the offered prices, the contracting officer shall take one of two alternatives, the first being to obtain certified cost or pricing data when required by FAR 15.403-4, along with data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1251].

The second alternative applies when certified cost or pricing data are not required by FAR 15.403-4: the contracting officer shall obtain data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price, generally using the following order of preference in determining the type of data required [@CL-EST-1252]. One listed duty is to obtain the type and quantity of data necessary, but not more data than is necessary [@CL-EST-1256]. Under the codified FAR, the threshold for obtaining certified cost or pricing data is $2.5 million for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018 [@CL-EST-0001], and, unless an exception applies, the data are required before accomplishing any of the listed actions expected to exceed the current threshold or, in the case of existing contracts, the threshold specified in the contract [@CL-EST-9458]. Under Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Revision 1, in lieu of the requirements of FAR 15.403-3(a) [@CL-EST-9756], certified cost or pricing data are to be obtained when a prime contract is expected to exceed $10 million and is awarded after June 30, 2026, unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-0002; @CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457]. The threshold is $2.5 million when the prime contract is awarded on or before June 30, 2026, unless otherwise stated in the contract [@CL-EST-9457]. The threshold is covered on [Certified cost or pricing data threshold](est-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md).

Subpart 15.4 prescribes the cost and price negotiation policies and procedures for pricing negotiated prime contracts (including subcontracts) and contract modifications, including modifications to contracts awarded by sealed bidding [@CL-EST-9191].

Pricing means the process of establishing a reasonable amount or amounts to be paid for supplies or services, as defined in FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9318]. RFO FAR 2.101 defines pricing as the process of establishing a reasonable amount or amounts to be paid for supplies or services [@CL-EST-9363], in the same words as the codified definition [@CL-EST-9318].

## How it works

One listed item in the order of preference is no additional data from the offeror when the price is based on adequate price competition, except as provided by FAR 15.403-3(b) [@CL-EST-1253]. Another listed item is data other than certified cost or pricing data, such as data related to prices, including established catalog or market prices and sales to non-governmental and governmental entities [@CL-EST-1301]. For data related to prices, the contracting officer relies first on data available within the Government, second on data from sources other than the offeror, and, if necessary, on data from the offeror [@CL-EST-1254]. When obtaining data from the offeror is necessary, unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b)(1) or (2) applies, such data submitted by the offeror shall include, at a minimum, appropriate data on the prices at which the same or similar items have been sold previously [@CL-EST-1255], and prior-sale price data required from the offeror shall be adequate for evaluating the reasonableness of the price [@CL-EST-1300]. A further listed item is cost data to the extent necessary for the contracting officer to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1302]. If a fair and reasonable price cannot be established by the contracting officer from the analyses of the data obtained or submitted to date, the contracting officer shall require the submission of additional data sufficient for the contracting officer to support the determination [@CL-EST-1257]. For a proposal team, the offeror's submission shall include appropriate data on the prices at which the same or similar items have been sold previously when data from the offeror is necessary and no exception applies [@CL-EST-1255], and additional data sufficient to support the determination shall be required if a fair and reasonable price cannot be established from the data obtained or submitted to date [@CL-EST-1257].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.202(d) says agencies must purchase supplies and services from responsible sources at fair and reasonable prices [@CL-EST-3367]. The codified paragraph says contracting officers shall purchase supplies and services from responsible sources at fair and reasonable prices [@CL-EST-1250]. RFO FAR 15.402(a) says that in establishing the reasonableness of the offered prices contracting officers must take one of two alternatives, the first being to obtain certified cost or pricing data when required by 15.403-3, along with data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-3368]. The codified paragraph states the first alternative with "shall" and the cross-reference 15.403-4 [@CL-EST-1251]. RFO FAR 15.402(a)(2) says the second alternative applies when certified cost or pricing data are not required by 15.403-3 and has contracting officers obtain data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-3369]. The codified paragraph adds that the contracting officer generally uses the order of preference in determining the type of data required [@CL-EST-1252]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(a) says that for acquisitions that do not require certified cost or pricing data, contracting officers must generally use the following order of preference to determine the type of data to require [@CL-EST-3370]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(a)(1) lists first no additional data from the offeror, when adequate price competition exists (see 15.403-2) [@CL-EST-3371], and the codified first item says no additional data from the offeror, if the price is based on adequate price competition, except as provided by 15.403-3(b) [@CL-EST-1253]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(e) says that if adequate price competition exists, but there are unusual circumstances in which additional data are necessary to determine the reasonableness of price, contracting officers must obtain the additional data from sources other than the offeror, to the maximum extent practicable [@CL-EST-3372]. Under FAR 15.403-3(b), if there are unusual circumstances where it is concluded that additional data are necessary to determine the reasonableness of price, the contracting officer shall, to the maximum extent practicable, obtain the additional data from sources other than the offeror [@CL-EST-0525]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(a)(2) lists data related to prices (e.g., established catalog or market prices, sales to non-governmental and governmental entities), relying first on data available within the Government, then on data obtained from sources other than the offeror, and then, if necessary, on data obtained from the offeror [@CL-EST-3373; @CL-EST-3374], and RFO FAR 15.403-1(a)(3) lists cost data to the extent necessary for the contracting officer to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-3375]. The codified paragraphs list the same data in the same order [@CL-EST-1254; @CL-EST-1302]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(b)(1) says contracting officers must require submission of data other than certified cost or pricing data to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-3376], and that at a minimum the data must include appropriate data on the prices at which the same item or similar items have previously been sold, adequate for determining the reasonableness of the price [@CL-EST-3377]. It also says this data is not required when price reasonableness is established through adequate price competition or prices set by law or regulation, unless paragraph (e) of that subsection applies [@CL-EST-3378]. The codified paragraph says that when obtaining data from the offeror is necessary, unless an exception under 15.403-1(b)(1) or (2) applies, such data submitted by the offeror shall include, at a minimum, appropriate data on the prices at which the same or similar items have been sold previously, adequate for evaluating the reasonableness of the price [@CL-EST-1255; @CL-EST-1300]. RFO FAR 15.402(b) says contracting officers must obtain the type and quantity of data necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price, but not more data than is needed [@CL-EST-3379], and must require the submission of additional data sufficient for the contracting officer to support the determination of the fair and reasonable price, if the contracting officer cannot establish a fair and reasonable price from an analysis of the data obtained or submitted to date [@CL-EST-3380]. The codified paragraph states the corresponding duties with "shall" [@CL-EST-1256; @CL-EST-1257]. RFO FAR 15.400 says that its subpart contains cost and price negotiation policies and procedures for pricing negotiated prime contracts (including subcontracts) and contract modifications, including modifications to contracts awarded by sealed bidding [@CL-EST-2256].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company offers a spare part at 12,000 per unit, and the price is not based on adequate price competition. The contracting officer finds no usable price data inside the Government or from other sources, so asks the company for the prices at which the same part sold before. Those sales support the offered price, so no further data is requested. Had the sales data been too thin to establish a fair and reasonable price, the contracting officer would have required more data.

## Never confuse

The contracting officer shall obtain certified cost or pricing data when required by FAR 15.403-4, along with data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1251], or, when certified cost or pricing data are not required by FAR 15.403-4, shall obtain data other than certified cost or pricing data as necessary to establish a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-1252]. The exceptions to certification are on [Exceptions to the requirement for certified cost or pricing data](est-certified-data-exceptions.md) and, for DoD, on [DoD exceptions, waivers and limits](est-dod-certified-data-exceptions-and-waivers.md). The analysis methods are on [Proposal analysis techniques](est-proposal-analysis.md), the format for submitting the data is on [Instructions for submitting cost or pricing data](est-data-submission-format-and-instructions.md), and what the negotiation file records is on [Price negotiation and the price negotiation memorandum](est-price-negotiation-documentation.md).

The codified paragraph says that when obtaining data from the offeror is necessary, unless an exception under 15.403-1(b)(1) or (2) applies, such data submitted by the offeror shall include appropriate data on the prices at which the same or similar items have been sold previously [@CL-EST-1255]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(b)(1) says the prior-sale data is not required when price reasonableness is established through adequate price competition or prices set by law or regulation, unless paragraph (e) of that subsection applies [@CL-EST-3378].
