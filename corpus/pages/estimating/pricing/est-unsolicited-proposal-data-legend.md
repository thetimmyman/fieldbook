## Purpose

Shows the legend an offeror may use to restrict data in an unsolicited proposal, what the legend protects and where its protection stops.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1440] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.609 [@CL-EST-1440] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.503-3 [@CL-EST-3524]; RFO FAR 15.504-1 [@CL-EST-3530]; RFO FAR 15.504-2 [@CL-EST-3529] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1440] |
| Applies to | Unsolicited proposals that include data the offeror wants restricted [@CL-EST-1440] |

If the offeror wishes to restrict data in an unsolicited proposal that it does not want disclosed to the public for any purpose or used by the Government except for evaluation purposes, the title page must be marked with a prescribed legend [@CL-EST-1440]. The offeror shall also mark each sheet of data it wishes to restrict with a prescribed sheet legend that refers to the restriction on the title page [@CL-EST-1444].

The agency point of contact shall return to the offeror any proposal marked with a legend different from the prescribed title page legend [@CL-EST-1445].

## How it works

The legend says the marked data shall not be disclosed outside the Government and shall not be duplicated, used, or disclosed, in whole or in part, for any purpose other than to evaluate the proposal [@CL-EST-1441]. It also says that if a contract is awarded to the offeror as a result of or in connection with the submission of the data, the Government shall have the right to duplicate, use, or disclose the data to the extent provided in the resulting contract [@CL-EST-1442]. The restriction does not limit the Government's right to use information contained in the data if it is obtained from another source without restriction [@CL-EST-1443].

### Cover sheet and outside evaluation

The agency point of contact shall place a prescribed cover sheet on the proposal or clearly mark it with the prescribed notice, unless the offeror clearly states in writing that no restrictions are imposed on the disclosure or use of the data contained in the proposal [@CL-EST-1461]. The notice tells all Government personnel to exercise extreme care to ensure that the information in the proposal is not disclosed to an individual who has not been authorized access to such data in accordance with FAR 3.104 [@CL-EST-1462], and says the information is not to be duplicated, used, or disclosed in whole or in part for any purpose other than evaluation of the proposal without the written permission of the offeror [@CL-EST-1462; @CL-EST-1463], but if a contract is awarded on the basis of the proposal the terms of the contract control disclosure and use, and the notice does not limit the Government's right to use information in the proposal if it is obtainable from another source without restriction [@CL-EST-9430]. The notice is to be used solely as a manner of handling unsolicited proposals that will be compatible with that subpart [@CL-EST-9716]. However, agencies are told not to use the notice to justify withholding of a record, or to improperly deny the public access to a record, where an obligation is imposed by the Freedom of Information Act [@CL-EST-1464].

When an agency receives an unsolicited proposal without any restrictive legend from an educational or nonprofit organization or institution, and an evaluation outside the Government is necessary, the agency point of contact shall do three things [@CL-EST-1465]. The first is to attach a cover sheet clearly marked with the legend in paragraph (d) [@CL-EST-1466]. The second is to change the beginning of this legend to read “All Government and non-Government personnel * * * ” [@CL-EST-9714]. The third is to require any non-Government evaluator to agree in writing that data in the proposal will not be disclosed to others outside the Government [@CL-EST-1467]. If the proposal is received with the restrictive legend, the modified cover sheet shall also be used and permission shall be obtained from the offeror before release of the proposal for evaluation by non-Government personnel [@CL-EST-1468].

When an agency receives an unsolicited proposal, with or without a restrictive legend, from other than an educational or nonprofit organization or institution, and evaluation by Government personnel outside the agency or by experts outside of the Government is necessary [@CL-EST-1469], written permission must be obtained from the offeror before release of the proposal for evaluation [@CL-EST-1470]. The agency point of contact shall clearly mark the cover sheet with the legend in paragraph (d) or as modified in paragraph (f) [@CL-EST-9715], and obtain a written agreement from any non-Government evaluator stating that data in the proposal will not be disclosed to persons outside the Government [@CL-EST-1471].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.503-3(a) says unsolicited proposals may include data that the offeror does not want disclosed to the public for any purpose or used by the Government except for evaluation purposes, and that if the offeror wishes to restrict the use and disclosure of data, the title page must be marked the legend that follows [@CL-EST-3524]. The RFO legend says the marked data must not be disclosed outside the Government and must not be duplicated, used, or disclosed, in whole or in part, for any purpose other than to evaluate the proposal [@CL-EST-3525], says that if a contract is awarded to the offeror as a result of, or in connection with, the submission of the data, the Government must have the right to duplicate, use, or disclose the data to the extent provided in the resulting contract [@CL-EST-3526], and says the restriction does not limit the Government's right to use information contained in the data if it is obtained from another source without restriction [@CL-EST-3527]. The codified legend says shall in the places where the RFO legend says must [@CL-EST-1441; @CL-EST-1442; @CL-EST-3525; @CL-EST-3526]. RFO FAR 15.503-3(a)(1) says the offeror must also mark each sheet of data it wishes to restrict with a legend saying that use or disclosure of data contained on the sheet is subject to the restriction on the title page [@CL-EST-3528], where codified FAR 15.609(b) says the offeror shall also mark each sheet [@CL-EST-1444]. RFO FAR 15.504-2(a) says that when an unsolicited proposal meeting the requirements at 15.504-1(a) is marked with a legend different from that at 15.503-3, the agency point of contact must return the proposal to the offeror and notify them that the proposal cannot be considered [@CL-EST-3529], because it is impracticable for the Government to comply with the legend, and that the agency will consider the proposal if it is resubmitted with the proper legend [@CL-EST-3541]. The requirements at RFO FAR 15.504-1(a) begin with the test that the proposal is a valid unsolicited proposal as described at 15.503-1(b) [@CL-EST-3530] and end with the test that it complies with the marking requirements of this subpart [@CL-EST-3542]. Item (a)(2) of RFO FAR 15.504-2 is an item of that same paragraph, in which, for a proposal meeting the requirements at 15.504-1(a) and marked with a legend different from that at 15.503-3, the agency point of contact must, unless the offeror clearly states in writing that no restrictions are imposed on the disclosure or use of the data contained in the proposal, mark the unsolicited proposal with the Use of Data Limited notice [@CL-EST-3529; @CL-EST-3531]. Codified FAR 15.609(d) says the agency point of contact shall place a cover sheet on the proposal or clearly mark it with that notice, unless the offeror clearly states in writing that no restrictions are imposed on the disclosure or use of the data contained in the proposal [@CL-EST-1461]. The RFO notice tells all Government personnel to exercise extreme care to ensure that the information in the proposal is not disclosed to an individual who has not been authorized access to such data in accordance with FAR part 3 [@CL-EST-3532], where the codified notice says FAR 3.104 [@CL-EST-1462]. It says the information is not to be duplicated, used, or disclosed in whole or in part for any purpose other than evaluation of the proposal without the written permission of the offeror [@CL-EST-3533], and that if a contract is awarded on the basis of the proposal the terms of the contract must control disclosure and use and the notice does not limit the Government's right to use information in the proposal if it is obtainable from another source without restriction [@CL-EST-9431]. RFO FAR 15.504-2(a)(3) says not to use the notice to justify withholding of a record, or to improperly deny the public access to a record, where an obligation is imposed by the Freedom of Information Act [@CL-EST-3534]. For evaluation outside the agency, RFO FAR 15.504-2(b)(1) says that for a proposal from other than an educational or nonprofit organization or institution, when an evaluation by Government personnel outside the agency or non-Government personnel is necessary, written permission must be obtained from the offeror before releasing the proposal for evaluation [@CL-EST-3535]. The agency point of contact should attach a cover sheet marked with the legend in paragraph (a)(2) [@CL-EST-3536], change the beginning of the legend to read “All Government and non-Government personnel * * * ” [@CL-EST-3543], and require any non-Government evaluator to agree in writing that data in the proposal will not be disclosed to persons outside the Government [@CL-EST-3537]. RFO FAR 15.504-2(b)(2) says that for a proposal without a restrictive legend from an educational or nonprofit organization or institution, when evaluation outside of the Government is necessary, the agency point of contact should follow paragraphs (b)(1)(i) through (iii) [@CL-EST-3538]. RFO FAR 15.504-2(b)(3) says that for a proposal with a restrictive legend from an educational or nonprofit organization or institution, when evaluation by non-Government personnel is necessary, the agency point of contact must follow paragraphs (b)(1)(i) through (iii) and obtain written permission from the offeror before releasing the proposal for evaluation [@CL-EST-3539; @CL-EST-3540].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror includes a cost model it wants protected. It places the prescribed legend on the title page and marks each sheet of the model with the sheet legend. It words the title page legend in its own way instead of using the prescribed text. The agency returns the proposal to the offeror; RFO FAR 15.504-2(a) says the return applies to a proposal meeting the requirements at 15.504-1(a) that is marked with a different legend [@CL-EST-3529]. The answer: the wording of the legend is not negotiable.

## Never confuse

The legend restricts the marked data to use for evaluation purposes [@CL-EST-1441], and its own terms add the resulting-contract and other-source exceptions: the Government shall have the right to duplicate, use, or disclose the data to the extent provided in the resulting contract if a contract is awarded to the offeror as a result of, or in connection with, the submission of the data [@CL-EST-1442], and the restriction does not limit the Government's right to use information contained in the data if it is obtained from another source without restriction [@CL-EST-1443]. A proposal marked with a different legend is returned to the offeror [@CL-EST-1445], and RFO FAR 15.504-2(a) says the return applies to a proposal meeting the requirements at 15.504-1(a) [@CL-EST-3529]. For outside evaluation of a proposal without any restrictive legend from an educational or nonprofit organization or institution, codified FAR 15.609(f) says the agency point of contact shall attach the cover sheet, change the beginning of the legend and require the written agreement [@CL-EST-1465; @CL-EST-1466; @CL-EST-9714; @CL-EST-1467], while RFO FAR 15.504-2(b)(2) says the point of contact should follow paragraphs (b)(1)(i) through (iii) [@CL-EST-3538], and (b)(3) says it must follow them for a proposal with a restrictive legend from an educational or nonprofit organization or institution [@CL-EST-3539; @CL-EST-3540]. The title page legend is marked when the offeror wishes to restrict the data [@CL-EST-1440], and the agency point of contact places the cover sheet or marks the proposal with the notice unless the offeror clearly states in writing that no restrictions are imposed on the disclosure or use of the data [@CL-EST-1461]. What makes a proposal valid is on [Valid unsolicited proposals and agency points of contact](est-unsolicited-proposals-general.md), and what it should contain is on [Content of unsolicited proposals](est-unsolicited-proposal-content.md).
