## Purpose

Shows how a protest is served on the contracting officer and when a computer generated version of a required form may be used.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2307] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.233-2 [@CL-EST-2307]; FAR 52.253-1 [@CL-EST-2397] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.233-2 [@CL-EST-0500]; RFO FAR 52.253-1 [@CL-EST-0502] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2307] |
| Applies to | Protests filed directly with an agency and copies of protests filed with the Government Accountability Office [@CL-EST-2307], and data required on forms prescribed by the FAR or an agency supplement [@CL-EST-2397; @CL-EST-2398] |

Protests as defined in FAR 33.101 that are filed directly with an agency, and copies of protests filed with the Government Accountability Office, shall be served on the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2307]. Service is made by obtaining written and dated acknowledgment of receipt from the official or location that the contracting officer designates in a blank as the place where a protest may be served on the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2396]. The copy of any protest filed with the Government Accountability Office shall be received in the designated office within one day of filing [@CL-EST-2308].

## How it works

Any data required to be submitted on a Standard or Optional Form prescribed by the FAR may be submitted on a computer generated version of the form [@CL-EST-2397]. That is allowed provided there is no change to the name, content or sequence of the data elements and the form carries the form number and edition date [@CL-EST-2309].

Unless agency regulations prohibit it, data required on an agency unique form prescribed by an agency supplement to the FAR may be submitted on a computer generated version of the form [@CL-EST-2398]. That version is allowed provided there is no change to the name, content or sequence of the data elements on the form and provided the form carries the agency form number and edition date [@CL-EST-2399].

If the contractor submits a computer generated version that is different from the required form, the rights and obligations of the parties are determined based on the content of the required form [@CL-EST-2310].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0038, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. RFO FAR 52.233-2 defines protests by reference to FAR 33.102, where the codified provision refers to section 33.101 [@CL-EST-0499; @CL-EST-2307]. It has protests filed directly with an agency, and copies of protests filed with the Government Accountability Office, served on the Contracting Officer identified in the solicitation by obtaining written and dated acknowledgment of receipt from them, where the codified provision has the Contracting Officer designate an official or location in a blank [@CL-EST-0500; @CL-EST-2307; @CL-EST-2396]. Its paragraph (b) uses the codified words, with “must” for “shall”, that the copy of any protest must be received in the office designated above within one day of filing a protest with the Government Accountability Office [@CL-EST-0501; @CL-EST-2308]; paragraph (a) refers to the Contracting Officer identified in the solicitation [@CL-EST-0500]. RFO FAR 52.253-1 keeps the rule that data required on a Standard or Optional Form prescribed by the FAR may be submitted on a computer generated version of the form [@CL-EST-0502; @CL-EST-2397], provided there is no change to the name, content, or sequence of the data elements and the form carries the form number and edition date [@CL-EST-0503; @CL-EST-2309]. It keeps the same rule, unless prohibited by agency regulations, for an agency unique form prescribed by an agency supplement to the FAR [@CL-EST-0504; @CL-EST-2398] [@CL-EST-0505; @CL-EST-2399], and the rule that a version different than the required form leaves the rights and obligations of the parties determined by the content of the required form [@CL-EST-0506; @CL-EST-2310].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A protest is filed with the Government Accountability Office on a Monday. The copy is received in the designated office on the Tuesday, which is within one day of filing. Had the copy arrived on the Wednesday, it would not have been received within one day. Service on the contracting officer is made by obtaining written and dated acknowledgment of receipt from the person the contracting officer designated in the provision's blank (under the RFO text, the acknowledgment is instead obtained from the Contracting Officer identified in the solicitation [@CL-EST-0500]). The one-day period in the provision is stated for the copy of a protest filed with the Government Accountability Office.

A contractor submits a computer generated version of a Standard Form that reorders the data elements and omits the edition date. The version is different from the required form, so the rights and obligations of the parties are determined based on the content of the required form.

## Never confuse

The copy of any protest shall be received in the designated office within one day of filing a protest with the Government Accountability Office [@CL-EST-2308], and service is made by obtaining written and dated acknowledgment of receipt [@CL-EST-2307; @CL-EST-2396].

If the Contractor submits a computer generated version of a form that is different than the required form, the rights and obligations of the parties will be determined based on the content of the required form [@CL-EST-2310].

The codified provision has the Contracting Officer designate in a blank the official or location where a protest may be served on the Contracting Officer [@CL-EST-2396], while RFO FAR 52.233-2(a) says the Contracting Officer is identified in the solicitation [@CL-EST-0500].
