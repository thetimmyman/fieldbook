## Purpose

Shows when the DoD special termination costs clause may be used on an incrementally funded contract, how the amount is set, and where the limit on pricing a terminated effort is found.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1096] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 249.501-70 and 249.109-70 [@CL-EST-1096; @CL-EST-1099] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1096] |
| Applies to | Incrementally funded contracts where the agency head approves use of the clause [@CL-EST-1096] |

The Special Termination Costs clause may be used in an incrementally funded contract when the agency head approves its use [@CL-EST-1096]. The clause is authorized when all of these apply: the contract term is 2 years or more [@CL-EST-1097]; the contract is estimated to require either total RDT&E financing in excess of $25 million or total production investment in excess of $100 million [@CL-EST-1097; @CL-EST-9500]; and adequate funds are available to cover the contingent reserve liability for special termination costs [@CL-EST-9500; @CL-EST-1107].

The contractor and the contracting officer must agree upon their best estimate of the total special termination costs to which the contractor would be entitled on termination, and insert that amount in paragraph (c) of the clause [@CL-EST-1098].

## How it works

The clause may be used in an incrementally funded contract, and the agreed amount is the parties' best estimate of the total special termination costs to which the contractor would be entitled if the contract were terminated [@CL-EST-1096; @CL-EST-1098]. On a termination for convenience, partial or whole, or a change that reduces scope, follow the procedures at PGI 249.109-70 for limitation on pricing of the terminated or reduced effort [@CL-EST-1099].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A development contract is funded in increments, has a term of 3 years, and is estimated to need 30 million of RDT&E financing. The agency head approves the clause, and adequate funds are available to cover the contingent reserve liability. The contractor and the contracting officer agree a best estimate of 2,000,000 for special termination costs and insert it in the clause. If the contract is later terminated for convenience in part, the PGI procedures govern how the terminated effort is priced.

## Never confuse

Authorizing the Special Termination Costs clause on qualifying multi-year incrementally funded contracts (2 years or more with over $25 million RDT&E or over $100 million production financing) requires agency-head approval up front [@CL-EST-1096; @CL-EST-1097], rather than replacing the allowability and settlement rules applied after termination on [Termination costs](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md) and [Termination for convenience settlement](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md) [@CL-CLS-0213; @CL-CTF-0351].
