## Purpose

Shows when the DoD special termination costs clause may be used on an incrementally funded contract, how the amount is set, and where the limit on pricing a terminated effort is found.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1096] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 249.501-70 and 249.109-70 [@CL-EST-1096; @CL-EST-1099] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1096] |
| Applies to | Incrementally funded contracts where the agency head approves use of the clause [@CL-EST-1096] |

The Special Termination Costs clause may be used in an incrementally funded contract when the agency head approves its use [@CL-EST-1096]. Among the conditions for authorizing it are a contract term of 2 years or more, and an estimate of either more than $25 million of total RDT&E financing or more than $100 million of total production investment [@CL-EST-1097]. Another listed condition is that adequate funds are available to cover the contingent reserve liability for special termination costs [@CL-EST-1107].

The contractor and the contracting officer must agree upon their best estimate of the total special termination costs to which the contractor would be entitled on termination, and insert that amount in the clause [@CL-EST-1098].

## How it works

The clause may be used in an incrementally funded contract, and the agreed amount is the parties' best estimate of the total special termination costs to which the contractor would be entitled if the contract were terminated [@CL-EST-1096; @CL-EST-1098]. On a termination for convenience, partial or whole, or a change that reduces scope, the procedures at PGI for the limitation on pricing of the terminated or reduced effort are to be followed [@CL-EST-1099]. The printed DFARS text does not set out those procedures.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A development contract is funded in increments, has a term of 3 years, and is estimated to need 30 million of RDT&E financing. The agency head approves the clause, and adequate funds are available to cover the contingent reserve liability. The contractor and the contracting officer agree a best estimate of 2,000,000 for special termination costs and insert it in the clause. If the contract is later terminated for convenience in part, the PGI procedures govern how the terminated effort is priced.

## Never confuse

The agreed special termination cost amount is an estimate of the costs to which the contractor would be entitled on termination [@CL-EST-1098]. Settling an actual termination is a separate step. For settlement, see [Termination costs](../../close-revrec/inventory-wip/cls-termination-costs.md) and [Termination for convenience settlement](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-termination-for-convenience-settlement.md).
