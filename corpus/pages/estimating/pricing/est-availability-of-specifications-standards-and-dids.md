## Purpose

Shows where the solicitation provisions say Federal and Defense specifications and standards are obtained, how a Defense document that is not on ASSIST is requested, and what an offeror does when a cited specification is not in the GSA index.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2183; @CL-EST-2184; @CL-EST-2186] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.211-1 [@CL-EST-2183]; FAR 52.211-2 [@CL-EST-2184]; FAR 52.211-3 [@CL-EST-2186] |
| RFO citation | No RFO equivalent [@CL-EST-0023; @CL-EST-0024; @CL-EST-0025] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2183; @CL-EST-2184; @CL-EST-2186] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that include the matching availability provision [@CL-EST-2183; @CL-EST-2184; @CL-EST-2186] |

Under FAR 52.211-1, the GSA Index of Federal Specifications, Standards and Commercial Item Descriptions, FPMR Part 101-29, and copies of Federal specifications, standards, and product descriptions can be downloaded from the ASSIST website [@CL-EST-2183]. If the General Services Administration, Department of Agriculture, or Department of Veterans Affairs issued the solicitation, a copy of specifications, standards, and commercial item descriptions cited in the solicitation may be obtained from the ASSIST website identified in paragraph (a) [@CL-EST-2219].

Under FAR 52.211-2, most unclassified Defense specifications and standards may be downloaded from the ASSIST website [@CL-EST-2184]. Defense documents not available from ASSIST may be requested from the Defense Standardization Program Office [@CL-EST-2185]. One way to request one is by using the ASSIST feedback module [@CL-EST-2220]. Another is to contact the Defense Standardization Program Office by telephone at 571-767-6888 or by email [@CL-EST-2221].

Under FAR 52.211-3, for specifications not listed in the GSA index, the specifications cited in the solicitation may be obtained from the activity, address, telephone number and person to be contacted that the provision names [@CL-EST-2186]. The request should identify the solicitation number and the specification requested by date, title, and number, as cited in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2222]. The activity and contact details are filled in for each solicitation and are contract-specific. The FAR prescribes a provision substantially the same as the text of FAR 52.211-3, so a solicitation's wording may differ from that text [@CL-EST-2223].

## How it works

The route depends on the kind of document and on who issued the solicitation. Index documents and Federal specifications are downloaded from ASSIST [@CL-EST-2183]. Where the General Services Administration, Department of Agriculture, or Department of Veterans Affairs issued the solicitation, cited specifications, standards and commercial item descriptions can be obtained from ASSIST [@CL-EST-2219]. Most unclassified Defense specifications and standards are also downloaded from ASSIST, and a Defense document that is not there is requested from the Defense Standardization Program Office [@CL-EST-2184; @CL-EST-2185]. A specification outside the index is requested from the activity named in the solicitation, identified by solicitation number and by date, title, and number [@CL-EST-2186; @CL-EST-2222].

Under the RFO text, the DoD class deviation directs DoD contracting officers to use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text, effective February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO FAR part 52 text prints FAR 52.211-1 [@CL-EST-0023], FAR 52.211-2 [@CL-EST-0024] and FAR 52.211-3 [@CL-EST-0025] as [Reserved], with no provision text under those numbers. The ASSIST and Defense Standardization Program Office routes described above come from the codified text [@CL-EST-2183; @CL-EST-2184; @CL-EST-2186], so they have no text under those three RFO section numbers.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation cites a Federal specification, a Defense standard and a specification that is not in the GSA index. The offeror downloads the Federal specification and the Defense standard from ASSIST.

A different solicitation, issued by the Department of Agriculture, cites a commercial item description. The offeror may obtain a copy from the ASSIST website.

A Defense document the offeror also needs is not on ASSIST, so the offeror sends a request to the Defense Standardization Program Office through the ASSIST feedback module. It could instead have contacted that office by telephone or email.

For the specification outside the index, the offeror writes to the activity and contact named in the solicitation. The request states the solicitation number, for example 0001, and identifies the specification by its date, title, and number as cited.

## Never confuse

Downloading from ASSIST and requesting from the Defense Standardization Program Office are different routes, and the second applies to Defense documents not available from ASSIST [@CL-EST-2184; @CL-EST-2185].

The provision for specifications not listed in the GSA index names an activity to obtain copies from [@CL-EST-2186]. That differs from the provision under which cited specifications are not available for distribution and may only be examined at stated locations [@CL-EST-2187], which is covered on [Examining specifications and proposing alternatives to Government-unique standards](est-specification-examination-and-alternative-standards.md).

Codified FAR 52.211-1, 52.211-2 and 52.211-3 each carry availability text [@CL-EST-2183; @CL-EST-2184; @CL-EST-2186], while the RFO FAR part 52 text prints those three numbers as [Reserved] [@CL-EST-0023; @CL-EST-0024; @CL-EST-0025].
