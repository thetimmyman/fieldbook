## Purpose

Shows the debriefing duty that applies to DoD awards valued at 15 million dollars or more, the extra minimum content for larger awards, and the limit on what may be revealed [@CL-EST-2464; @CL-EST-2465; @CL-EST-2466; @CL-EST-2467].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-2464] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 215.506 [@CL-EST-2464] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2464] |
| Applies to | Contract awards valued at 15 million dollars or more, when requested by a successful or unsuccessful offeror [@CL-EST-2464] |

Notwithstanding FAR 15.506(b), when requested by a successful or unsuccessful offeror, a written or oral debriefing is required for contract awards valued at 15 million dollars or more [@CL-EST-2464]. In addition to the requirements of FAR 15.506(d), the minimum debriefing information must include the items listed in paragraph (d) [@CL-EST-2527].

For an award in excess of 15 million dollars and not in excess of 150 million dollars with a small business or nontraditional defense contractor, the minimum debriefing information includes an option for that contractor to request disclosure of the agency's written source selection decision document [@CL-EST-2465]. That document is redacted to protect the confidential and proprietary information of other offerors for the contract award [@CL-EST-2528]. For an award in excess of 150 million dollars, the agency's written source selection decision document is disclosed, redacted to protect the confidential and proprietary information of other offerors for the contract award [@CL-EST-2466].

If the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d), the debriefing must not reveal any information that is determined to be withheld from disclosure in accordance with section 3252 of title 10, United States Code [@CL-EST-2467].

## How it works

The trigger is an award valued at 15 million dollars or more and a request, and either a successful or an unsuccessful offeror may make it [@CL-EST-2464]. The content then grows in steps: for an award above 15 million dollars and up to 150 million dollars with a small business or nontraditional defense contractor, that contractor has an option to request the redacted decision document, and above 150 million dollars the redacted document is disclosed [@CL-EST-2465; @CL-EST-2466; @CL-EST-2528]. These items are in addition to the content that FAR 15.506(d) requires [@CL-EST-2527]. Redaction protects the confidential and proprietary information of other offerors for that award [@CL-EST-2466; @CL-EST-2528]. If the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d), information determined to be withheld from disclosure under section 3252 of title 10, United States Code, stays out of the debriefing [@CL-EST-2467].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An award is made at 40 million dollars to a small business. An unsuccessful offeror asks for a debriefing, so a written or oral debriefing is required [@CL-EST-2464]. The small business awardee has an option to request disclosure of the source selection decision document, redacted to protect the confidential and proprietary information of other offerors [@CL-EST-2465; @CL-EST-2528]. Had the award been 200 million dollars, the redacted document would have been disclosed [@CL-EST-2466]. Had the award been exactly 15 million dollars, a debriefing would still be required on request [@CL-EST-2464], but the added content for awards in excess of 15 million dollars would not apply [@CL-EST-2465; @CL-EST-2466].

## Never confuse

A debriefing at this threshold is not limited to the ordinary content list, because the section adds, in addition to FAR 15.506(d), an option, for a small business or nontraditional defense contractor on an award above 15 million dollars and up to 150 million dollars, to request the redacted decision document and, above 150 million dollars, disclosure of it [@CL-EST-2527; @CL-EST-2465; @CL-EST-2466]. The postaward debriefing card and the foreign military sales notice card cover the ordinary rules and are not repeated here.

## Company calibration

That the capture team logs which awards cross the debriefing threshold and who answers a request for the decision document. [@Q-012]
