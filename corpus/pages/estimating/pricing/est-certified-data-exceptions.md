## Purpose

Shows when a contracting officer may not require certified cost or pricing data, and what data may be required instead.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0152] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-0152] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0152] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, and acquisitions that meet a listed exception [@CL-EST-0151; @CL-EST-0152] |

Certified cost or pricing data shall not be obtained for acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-0151]. The contracting officer shall not require certified cost or pricing data to support any action, including contracts, subcontracts, or modifications, when one of the listed circumstances applies [@CL-EST-0152].

The exceptions include adequate price competition, prices set by law or regulation, a commercial product or commercial service, and a waiver [@CL-EST-0153; @CL-EST-0154; @CL-EST-0155; @CL-EST-0156]. In an acquisition that does not require certified cost or pricing data, the contracting officer carries out the steps in paragraph (a)(1) of FAR 15.403-3 [@CL-EST-0520]. These include requiring data other than certified cost or pricing data from the offeror to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-0521], if the contracting officer determines that adequate data from sources other than the offeror are not available [@CL-EST-0522].

## How it works

Adequate price competition generally requires that two or more responsible offerors, competing independently, submit priced offers that satisfy the Government's expressed requirement, that award be made to the best-value offeror with price a substantial factor in source selection, and that there be no finding that the otherwise successful offeror's price is unreasonable; however, for agencies other than DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard, a price may also be based on adequate price competition under specific conditions involving a reasonable expectation of competition or price analysis, even if only one offer is received [@CL-EST-0157; @CL-EST-0247]. The head of the contracting activity may, without power of delegation, waive the requirement in exceptional cases [@CL-EST-0158]. Where data other than certified cost or pricing data are required, the data the offeror submits must at a minimum include appropriate data on the prices at which the same item or similar items have previously been sold, unless the exception for adequate price competition or for prices set by law or regulation applies [@CL-EST-0523; @CL-EST-0153; @CL-EST-0154]. When adequate price competition exists, generally no additional data are necessary to determine the reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-0524]. If there are unusual circumstances where additional data are necessary, the contracting officer shall, to the maximum extent practicable, obtain the additional data from sources other than the offeror [@CL-EST-0525]. An offeror who does not comply with a requirement to submit data in accordance with paragraph (a)(1) of FAR 15.403-3 is ineligible for award unless the head of the contracting activity determines that award to that offeror is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-EST-0526]. That determination considers the effort made to obtain the data, the need for the item or service, and increased cost or significant harm to the Government if award is not made [@CL-EST-0527].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company submits a quote priced at 80,000, below the simplified acquisition threshold, so no certified cost or pricing data is obtained. On a separate 2 million bid, two responsible offerors compete independently and the contracting officer finds adequate price competition, so certified data is not required and, absent unusual circumstances, no additional data is required from the offerors to support the price.

## Never confuse

Data other than certified cost or pricing data are what the contracting officer must require, to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price, when certified cost or pricing data are not required and adequate data from sources other than the offeror are not available [@CL-EST-0520; @CL-EST-0521; @CL-EST-0522].
