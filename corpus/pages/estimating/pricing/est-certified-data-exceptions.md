## Purpose

Shows when a contracting officer may not require certified cost or pricing data, and what data may be required instead.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0152] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-0152] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.403-2(b) [@CL-EST-3198] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0152] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, and acquisitions that meet a listed exception [@CL-EST-0151; @CL-EST-0152] |

Certified cost or pricing data shall not be obtained for acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-0151]. The contracting officer shall not require certified cost or pricing data to support any action, including contracts, subcontracts, or modifications, when one of the alternatives listed in FAR 15.403-1(b) applies, but may require data other than certified cost or pricing data, as defined in FAR 2.101, to support a determination of a fair and reasonable price or cost realism [@CL-EST-0152; @CL-EST-9739].

The alternatives are: the contracting officer determines that prices agreed upon are based on adequate price competition [@CL-EST-0153]; the contracting officer determines that prices agreed upon are based on prices set by law or regulation [@CL-EST-0154]; a commercial product or commercial service is being acquired [@CL-EST-0155]; a waiver has been granted [@CL-EST-0156]; or a contract or subcontract for commercial products or commercial services is being modified [@CL-EST-9739; @CL-EST-9615]. In an acquisition that does not require certified cost or pricing data, the contracting officer shall carry out the steps listed in paragraph (a)(1) of FAR 15.403-3 [@CL-EST-0520]. These include requiring data other than certified cost or pricing data from the offeror to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-0521], if the contracting officer determines that adequate data from sources other than the offeror are not available [@CL-EST-0522].

FAR 2.101 defines data other than certified cost or pricing data as pricing data, cost data, and judgmental information necessary for the contracting officer to determine a fair and reasonable price or to determine cost realism [@CL-EST-9291]. Such data may include the identical types of data as certified cost or pricing data, consistent with Table 15-2 of FAR 15.408, but without the certification [@CL-EST-9292]. The data may also include, for example, sales data and any information reasonably required to explain the offeror's estimating process, including, but not limited to (1) the judgmental factors applied and the mathematical or other methods used in the estimate, including those used in projecting from known data, and (2) the nature and amount of any contingencies included in the proposed price [@CL-EST-9400; @CL-EST-9401]. The RFO definition of data other than certified cost or pricing data begins with the same words [@CL-EST-9353; @CL-EST-9291]. It says such data may include the identical types of data as certified cost or pricing data, consistent with Table 15-2 of 15.408, but without the certification, and that the data may also include, for example, sales data and any information reasonably required to explain the offeror's estimating process, including, but not limited to (1) the judgmental factors applied and the mathematical or other methods used in the estimate, including those used in projecting from known data, and (2) the nature and amount of any contingencies included in the proposed price [@CL-EST-9395; @CL-EST-9396].

## How it works

A price is based on adequate price competition when two or more responsible offerors, competing independently, submit priced offers that satisfy the Government's expressed requirement, award will be made to the offeror whose proposal represents the best value where price is a substantial factor in source selection, and there is no finding that the price of the otherwise successful offeror is unreasonable [@CL-EST-9466; @CL-EST-0157; @CL-EST-0247]. Any finding that the price is unreasonable must be supported by a statement of the facts and approved at a level above the contracting officer [@CL-EST-9469]. For agencies other than DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard, a price is also based on adequate price competition in the circumstances that paragraph (c)(1)(ii) of FAR 15.403-1 goes on to list [@CL-EST-9468]. The head of the contracting activity may, without power of delegation, waive the requirement in exceptional cases [@CL-EST-0158], and may consider waiving it if the price can be determined to be fair and reasonable without submission of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-9617]. Among those steps, the contracting officer shall require that the data submitted by the offeror include, at a minimum, appropriate data on the prices at which the same item or similar items have previously been sold, adequate for determining the reasonableness of the price, unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b)(1) or (2) applies [@CL-EST-0523]; those paragraphs cover the contracting officer determining that prices agreed upon are based on adequate price competition or on prices set by law or regulation [@CL-EST-0153; @CL-EST-0154]. When adequate price competition exists, generally no additional data are necessary to determine the reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-0524]. If there are unusual circumstances where it is concluded that additional data are necessary to determine the reasonableness of price, the contracting officer shall, to the maximum extent practicable, obtain the additional data from sources other than the offeror [@CL-EST-0525]. An offeror who does not comply with a requirement to submit data for a contract or subcontract in accordance with paragraph (a)(1) of FAR 15.403-3 is ineligible for award unless the head of the contracting activity determines that award to that offeror is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-EST-0526]. That determination considers the effort made to obtain the data, the need for the item or service, and increased cost or significant harm to the Government if award is not made [@CL-EST-0527].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers must use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.403-2(a), headed At or below the simplified acquisition threshold, says certified cost or pricing data must not be obtained for acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-3197], and codified FAR 15.403-1(a) says certified cost or pricing data shall not be obtained for acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-0151]. RFO FAR 15.403-2(b) says contracting officers must not require certified cost or pricing data to support any contracts, subcontracts, or modifications in the circumstances it lists [@CL-EST-3198]. Its lead-in runs straight from those supported actions to the circumstances, where the codified lead-in adds a parenthetical that the contracting officer may require data other than certified cost or pricing data as defined in FAR 2.101 to support a determination of a fair and reasonable price or cost realism [@CL-EST-3198; @CL-EST-0152]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(b)(2) says contracting officers may require data other than certified cost or pricing data to determine the cost realism of competing offers or to evaluate competing approaches [@CL-EST-3255]. The RFO list of alternatives includes the contracting officer determining that prices agreed upon are based on adequate price competition or on prices set by law or regulation [@CL-EST-3199], a commercial product or commercial service being acquired and a waiver having been granted [@CL-EST-3200], and, as its last alternative, modifying a contract or subcontract for commercial products or commercial services [@CL-EST-9740; @CL-EST-9616]. Under RFO FAR 15.403-2(c)(1)(i), a price is based on adequate price competition when two or more responsible offerors, competing independently, submit priced offers that satisfy the Government's expressed requirement [@CL-EST-9467; @CL-EST-3201], award will be made to the offeror whose proposal represents the best value where price is a substantial factor in source selection, and there is no finding that the price of the otherwise successful offeror is unreasonable [@CL-EST-3202]; any finding that the price is unreasonable must be supported by a statement of the facts and approved at a level above the contracting officer [@CL-EST-9470]. RFO FAR 15.403-2(c)(4)(i) says that in exceptional cases the head of the contracting activity may, without power of delegation, waive the requirement for submission of certified cost or pricing data if the price can be determined to be fair and reasonable without submission of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-3203]; codified FAR 15.403-1(c)(4) says the head of the contracting activity may, without power of delegation, waive the requirement in exceptional cases [@CL-EST-0158], and may consider waiving the requirement if the price can be determined to be fair and reasonable without submission of certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-9617]. RFO FAR 15.403-1 is headed Data other than certified cost or pricing data, and its paragraph (a) says that for acquisitions that do not require certified cost or pricing data, contracting officers must generally use an order of preference to determine the type of data to require [@CL-EST-3204], where codified FAR 15.403-3(a)(1) says that in those acquisitions the contracting officer shall carry out listed steps [@CL-EST-0520]. The first item of that order of preference is no additional data from the offeror, when adequate price competition exists [@CL-EST-3205], where codified FAR 15.403-3(b) says that when adequate price competition exists, generally no additional data are necessary to determine the reasonableness of price [@CL-EST-0524]. The second item is data related to prices, for example established catalog or market prices and sales to non-governmental and governmental entities [@CL-EST-3256], relying first on data available within the Government, then on data obtained from sources other than the offeror, and then, if necessary, on data obtained from the offeror [@CL-EST-3209]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(b)(1) says contracting officers must require submission of data other than certified cost or pricing data to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-3206]. Codified FAR 15.403-3(a)(1)(ii) says the contracting officer shall require submission of that data from the offeror to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-0521] if the contracting officer determines that adequate data from sources other than the offeror are not available [@CL-EST-0522]. When acquiring a commercial product or commercial service, RFO FAR 15.403-1(f) lists, among the things contracting officers must do, requiring the offeror to submit data other than certified cost or pricing data when the contracting officer is unable to determine a price fair and reasonable after obtaining data from sources other than the offeror [@CL-EST-3261; @CL-EST-3562]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(b)(1) goes on to say that the data must include, at a minimum, appropriate data on the prices at which the same item or similar items have previously been sold, adequate for determining the reasonableness of the price [@CL-EST-3207]. It then says this data is not required when price reasonableness is established through adequate price competition or prices set by law or regulation, unless paragraph (e) of that subsection applies [@CL-EST-3208], where the codified paragraph says unless an exception under 15.403-1(b)(1) or (2) applies [@CL-EST-0523]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(e) says that if adequate price competition exists, but there are unusual circumstances in which additional data are necessary to determine the reasonableness of price, contracting officers must obtain the additional data from sources other than the offeror, to the maximum extent practicable [@CL-EST-3210]. RFO FAR 15.403-1(d) says an offeror who does not comply with a requirement to submit data for a contract or subcontract in accordance with paragraph (a)(1) of that subsection is ineligible for award unless the head of the contracting activity determines that it is in the best interest of the Government to make the award to that offeror [@CL-EST-3211; @CL-EST-9741]; paragraph (a)(1) of RFO FAR 15.403-1 is the item on no additional data from the offeror when adequate price competition exists [@CL-EST-3205]. That determination considers the effort made to obtain the data, the need for the item or service, and increased cost or significant harm to the Government if award is not made [@CL-EST-3212].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company submits a quote priced at 80,000, below the simplified acquisition threshold, so no certified cost or pricing data is obtained. On a separate 2 million bid, two responsible offerors compete independently and the contracting officer finds adequate price competition, so certified data is not required and, absent unusual circumstances, no additional data is required from the offerors to support the price.

## Never confuse

In an acquisition that does not require certified cost or pricing data, the contracting officer shall require submission of data other than certified cost or pricing data from the offeror to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price [@CL-EST-0520; @CL-EST-0521] if the contracting officer determines that adequate data from sources other than the offeror are not available [@CL-EST-0522]. That requirement is at codified FAR 15.403-3(a)(1)(ii) [@CL-EST-0521]. RFO FAR 15.403-3 is headed Certified cost or pricing data and says that, unless an exception at 15.403-2 applies, contracting officers must obtain certified cost or pricing data when an action meets or exceeds the threshold of $2.5 million (for prime contracts awarded on or after July 1, 2018) [@CL-EST-3213], and RFO FAR 15.403-1 is headed Data other than certified cost or pricing data [@CL-EST-3204]. That $2.5 million is the RFO model text [@CL-EST-3213].

For DoD, the RFO threshold is not the operative one for a prime contract awarded after June 30, 2026 [@CL-EST-0263; @CL-EST-0002]. DFARS 215.403-3(a) under Class Deviation 2026-O0048 Rev 1 replaces RFO FAR 15.403-3(a) for DoD [@CL-EST-0263; @CL-EST-0002]. Under it, certified cost or pricing data are to be obtained when a prime contract is expected to exceed $10 million and is awarded after June 30, 2026 [@CL-EST-0002]. That threshold applies unless an exception at FAR 15.403-2 or DFARS 215.403-71 applies [@CL-EST-9456; @CL-EST-9457], and the threshold is $2.5 million when the prime contract is awarded on or before June 30, 2026, unless otherwise stated in the contract [@CL-EST-9457]. The three texts word the boundary differently. The codified FAR requires data before actions expected to exceed the threshold [@CL-EST-9458], the RFO text says an action that meets or exceeds $2.5 million [@CL-EST-3213], and the deviation says a prime contract expected to exceed $10 million [@CL-EST-0002].
