## Purpose

Shows when a contracting officer may not require certified cost or pricing data, and what data may be required instead.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-0152] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-0152] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-0152] |
| Applies to | Acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, and acquisitions that meet a listed exception [@CL-EST-0151; @CL-EST-0152] |

Certified cost or pricing data shall not be obtained for acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-EST-0151]. The contracting officer shall not require certified cost or pricing data to support any action, including contracts, subcontracts, or modifications, when an exception applies [@CL-EST-0152].

The exceptions include adequate price competition, prices set by law or regulation, a commercial product or commercial service, and a waiver [@CL-EST-0153; @CL-EST-0154; @CL-EST-0155; @CL-EST-0156]. Where certified data are not required, the contracting officer must require data other than certified cost or pricing data to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price, except that this data is not required when price reasonableness is established through adequate price competition or prices set by law or regulation, unless unusual circumstances call for additional data [@CL-EST-0159; @CL-EST-0248].

## How it works

Adequate price competition generally requires that two or more responsible offerors, competing independently, submit priced offers that satisfy the Government's expressed requirement, that award be made to the best-value offeror with price a substantial factor in source selection, and that there be no finding that the otherwise successful offeror's price is unreasonable; however, for agencies other than DoD, NASA, and the Coast Guard, a price may also be based on adequate price competition under specific conditions involving a reasonable expectation of competition or price analysis, even if only one offer is received [@CL-EST-0157; @CL-EST-0247]. The head of the contracting activity may, without power of delegation, waive the requirement in exceptional cases [@CL-EST-0158]. Where data other than certified cost or pricing data are required, they must at a minimum include appropriate data on the prices at which the same item or similar items have previously been sold, but this data is not required when price reasonableness is established through adequate price competition or prices set by law or regulation, unless unusual circumstances call for additional data [@CL-EST-0160; @CL-EST-0248]. An offeror who does not comply with a requirement to submit data for a contract or subcontract in accordance with paragraph (a)(1) is ineligible for award unless the head of the contracting activity determines award is in the Government's best interest, considering the effort made to obtain the data, the need for the item or service, and the increased cost or significant harm to the Government if award is not made [@CL-EST-0161; @CL-EST-0249; @CL-EST-0250].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company submits a quote priced at 80,000, below the simplified acquisition threshold, so no certified cost or pricing data is obtained. On a separate 2 million bid, two responsible offerors compete independently and the contracting officer finds adequate price competition, so certified data is not required and, absent unusual circumstances, no additional data is required from the offerors to support the price.

## Never confuse

Data other than certified cost or pricing data are what the contracting officer must require, to the extent necessary to determine a fair and reasonable price, when certified cost or pricing data are not required [@CL-EST-0159; @CL-EST-0248].
