## Purpose

States what the Standard on business unit general and administrative expense is for: criteria for allocating that expense to business unit final cost objectives on a beneficial or causal basis, and criteria for allocating home office expense that a segment receives to the cost objectives of that segment.

The purpose of the Standard is to provide criteria for allocating business unit general and administrative expenses to business unit final cost objectives based on their beneficial or causal relationship [@CL-EST-2910]. Those expenses represent the cost of the management and administration of the business unit as a whole [@CL-EST-2911]. The Standard also provides criteria for allocating home office expenses received by a segment to the cost objectives of that segment [@CL-EST-2912]. It will increase the likelihood of objectivity in allocating expenses to final cost objectives and of comparability of cost data among contractors in similar circumstances [@CL-EST-2913].

## How it works

Two flows sit in one Standard: business unit general and administrative expense allocated to the unit's final cost objectives, and home office expense received by a segment and allocated to that segment's cost objectives [@CL-EST-2910; @CL-EST-2912]. The basis for the first flow is the beneficial or causal relationship between the expense and the final cost objectives, and the section describes the expense as the cost of managing and administering the unit as a whole [@CL-EST-2910; @CL-EST-2911]. The section states the effect as a greater likelihood of objectivity in allocating expenses to final cost objectives and of comparability of cost data among contractors in similar circumstances [@CL-EST-2913].

The section is a statement of purpose. It names no official who decides, no approval route, no threshold, no deadline and no exception, and it states no duty for a particular party. It does not name an allocation base; the Standard's main card covers the techniques.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A business unit holds 400,000 of general and administrative expense for managing the unit as a whole, and the Standard supplies the criteria for allocating it to the unit's final cost objectives on a beneficial or causal basis [@CL-EST-2910; @CL-EST-2911]. A segment of the same organization receives 150,000 of home office expense, and the Standard supplies the criteria for allocating that amount to the cost objectives of the segment [@CL-EST-2912]. The two amounts are separate flows under the one Standard [@CL-EST-2910; @CL-EST-2912].

## Never confuse

Expense allocated from the business unit to its final cost objectives is a different flow from home office expense received by a segment, even though one Standard covers both [@CL-EST-2910; @CL-EST-2912]. The mechanics are in [the Standard's main card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-410-ga-allocation.md) and the worked illustrations in [the illustrations card](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-410-illustrations.md).
