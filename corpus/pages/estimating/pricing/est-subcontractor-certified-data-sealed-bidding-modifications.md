## Purpose

Shows when a Contractor must require certified cost or pricing data and a certificate from a subcontractor under a sealed bidding contract, the exception, how inflation adjustments and Alternate I change the thresholds, and how the requirement flows down.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2175] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.214-28 [@CL-EST-2175] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.214-28 [@CL-EST-0800; @CL-EST-0801] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2175] |
| Applies to | Modifications expected to exceed the certified data threshold on the date of execution [@CL-EST-2175; @CL-EST-2231] |

The requirements of paragraphs (b) and (c) of the clause become operative only for a modification to the contract involving aggregate increases and/or decreases in costs, plus applicable profits [@CL-EST-2175]. The modification must be expected to exceed the threshold for submission of certified cost or pricing data in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) on the date of execution of the modification [@CL-EST-2231]. The requirements of paragraphs (b) and (c) are limited to those modifications [@CL-EST-2209].

The Contractor's duty under paragraph (b) has two triggers. One is awarding any subcontract expected to exceed the certified data threshold, measured on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later [@CL-EST-2176]. The other is pricing any subcontract modifications involving aggregate increases and/or decreases in costs, plus applicable profits, expected to exceed the certified data threshold [@CL-EST-2177]. Before either, the Contractor shall require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data, actually or by specific identification in writing, as part of the subcontractor's proposal in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2 [@CL-EST-2210]. That requirement does not apply where an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) applies [@CL-EST-2178].

The submission is to include any information reasonably required to explain the subcontractor's estimating process, such as the judgmental factors applied and the mathematical or other methods used in the estimate [@CL-EST-2211]. The methods used include those used in projecting from known data, and the information includes the nature and amount of any contingencies included in the price [@CL-EST-2234].

If the threshold in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) is adjusted for inflation as set forth in FAR 1.109(a), the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract under FAR 1.109(d) [@CL-EST-2212]. It applies unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-2235].

## How it works

The Contractor shall require the subcontractor to certify in substantially the form prescribed in subsection 15.406-2 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, the data submitted under paragraph (b) were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price of the subcontract or subcontract modification [@CL-EST-2179; @CL-EST-2213].

The Contractor shall insert the substance of the clause, including its paragraph (d), in each subcontract that, when entered into, exceeds the threshold for submission of certified cost or pricing data in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-2180].

Alternate I (OCT 2025), as prescribed in FAR 14.201-7(c)(1)(ii), is substituted for paragraph (b) of the basic clause [@CL-EST-2214]. Under Alternate I, unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) applies, the Contractor shall require the subcontractor to submit certified cost or pricing data, actually or by specific identification in writing, as part of the subcontractor's proposal in accordance with FAR 15.408, Table 15-2, in the two alternative cases below [@CL-EST-2215]. Alternate I also includes the parenthetical on information explaining the subcontractor's estimating process [@CL-EST-2216; @CL-EST-2236].

One time under Alternate I is before modifying any subcontract that was awarded before July 1, 2018, involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed $950,000 [@CL-EST-2217]. The other is before awarding any subcontract expected to exceed $2.5 million on or after July 1, 2018, or modifying any subcontract that was awarded on or after July 1, 2018, involving a pricing adjustment expected to exceed $2.5 million [@CL-EST-2218]. Alternate I states dollar figures of $950,000 and $2.5 million [@CL-EST-2217; @CL-EST-2218], where the basic paragraph (b) refers to the threshold in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) [@CL-EST-2176; @CL-EST-2177].

The matching price reduction clause is on [Price reduction for defective certified data on modifications under sealed bidding](est-price-reduction-defective-data-sealed-bidding-modifications.md).

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO clause says that paragraphs (b) and (c) become operative only for a modification involving aggregate increases and/or decreases in costs, plus applicable profits, expected to exceed the threshold in FAR 15.403-4(a)(1) on the date of execution of the modification, and are limited to such modifications [@CL-EST-0800; @CL-EST-0801], as in the codified clause [@CL-EST-2175; @CL-EST-2231; @CL-EST-2209]. Its paragraph (b) has the same two subcontract triggers, the same requirement to submit certified cost or pricing data with the estimating process information, and the same exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) [@CL-EST-0802; @CL-EST-0803; @CL-EST-0804; @CL-EST-0805; @CL-EST-0806; @CL-EST-0807; @CL-EST-2176; @CL-EST-2177; @CL-EST-2210; @CL-EST-2211; @CL-EST-2234; @CL-EST-2178]. Its paragraph (b) cites FAR 1.108 for the inflation adjustment and says that the changed threshold applies throughout the remaining term of the contract, unless there is a subsequent threshold adjustment [@CL-EST-0808], where the codified paragraph cites FAR 1.109(a) and says that pursuant to FAR 1.109(d) the changed threshold applies [@CL-EST-2212; @CL-EST-2235]. Its paragraph (c) says section 15.406-2 where the codified paragraph says subsection 15.406-2, and the matter the subcontractor certifies is worded the same [@CL-EST-0809; @CL-EST-0810; @CL-EST-2179; @CL-EST-2213]. Its paragraph (d) has the same insertion of the substance of the clause in each subcontract that exceeds the threshold when entered into [@CL-EST-0811; @CL-EST-2180]. Its Alternate I says it is prescribed in 14.208(c)(1) where the codified Alternate I says 14.201-7(c)(1)(ii) [@CL-EST-0812; @CL-EST-2214], while the opening of Alternate I paragraph (b) and its estimating process parenthetical are worded the same [@CL-EST-0813; @CL-EST-0814; @CL-EST-0815; @CL-EST-2215; @CL-EST-2216; @CL-EST-2236], and its two times for requiring the data, with the same $950,000 and $2.5 million figures and the same July 1, 2018 dates, are the same as the codified ones [@CL-EST-0816; @CL-EST-0817; @CL-EST-2217; @CL-EST-2218].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Basic clause. Assume the certified data threshold on the date of execution is 2,000,000. A modification to the prime contract is expected to change costs, plus profit, by 3,000,000 in aggregate, so paragraphs (b) and (c) are operative. A modification expected to change them by 1,000,000 would not make them operative.

In connection with that 3,000,000 modification, the Contractor plans a subcontract expected to be 2,500,000. The threshold is measured on the date of agreement on price or the date of award, whichever is later, and the subcontract is expected to exceed it. Before awarding it, the Contractor requires the subcontractor to submit certified data as part of its proposal, with the information that explains its estimating process, unless an exception under FAR 15.403-1(b) applies. The subcontractor certifies, in substantially the prescribed form, that the data were accurate, complete, and current as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price of the subcontract.

The Contractor inserts the substance of the clause, including its paragraph (d), in that subcontract because it exceeds the threshold when entered into. A subcontract expected to be 1,500,000 does not exceed the assumed threshold, so the flowdown is not required for it.

Later, in connection with the same prime modification, the Contractor prices a subcontract modification that changes costs, plus profit, by an aggregate 2,200,000. It must require certified data for that modification before pricing it, unless an exception applies.

Suppose the threshold is later adjusted for inflation from 2,000,000 to 2,200,000. The changed threshold applies for the rest of the term unless it is adjusted again, so a subcontract modification that changes costs, plus profit, by an aggregate 2,100,000 and is priced after the change does not exceed it.

Alternate I. For a prime modification that makes paragraphs (b) and (c) operative, a subcontract awarded before July 1, 2018 is modified with a pricing adjustment expected to be 1,000,000. That exceeds 950,000, so certified data are required before the modification, unless an exception applies. A subcontract awarded on or after July 1, 2018 is modified with a pricing adjustment expected to be 1,000,000. That does not exceed 2.5 million, so Alternate I does not require data for it. A new subcontract expected to be 3,000,000 and awarded on or after July 1, 2018 exceeds 2.5 million, so data are required before awarding it.

## Never confuse

This clause has the Contractor require certified cost or pricing data from the subcontractor and insert the substance of the clause in subcontracts that exceed the threshold [@CL-EST-2210; @CL-EST-2180]. For the price reduction clause, see [Price reduction for defective certified data on modifications under sealed bidding](est-price-reduction-defective-data-sealed-bidding-modifications.md).

The certificate speaks as of the date of agreement on the negotiated price of the subcontract or subcontract modification [@CL-EST-2213].

Alternate I is substituted in place of paragraph (b) of the basic clause [@CL-EST-2214].

The negotiated-contract versions are on [Price reduction for defective certified data and subcontractor certified data clauses for modifications](est-price-reduction-and-subcontractor-data-modification-clauses.md), and the subcontractor data requirement is also covered on [Subcontractor Certified Cost or Pricing Data clause](est-subcontractor-certified-cost-or-pricing-data.md).

The codified paragraph (b) cites FAR 1.109(a) and FAR 1.109(d) for the inflation adjustment [@CL-EST-2212], while the RFO paragraph (b) cites FAR 1.108 [@CL-EST-0808].
