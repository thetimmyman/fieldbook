## Purpose

Separates the Standard that deals with the identification and accounting treatment of unallowable cost from the Standard that measures deferred compensation, and fixes when the revised deferred compensation Standard applies.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified CAS [@CL-EST-2636] |
| Codified citation | 48 CFR 9904.405-20, 9904.415-20 and 9904.415-63 [@CL-EST-2636; @CL-EST-2505; @CL-EST-2507] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2636] |
| Applies to | Contractors identifying unallowable cost and measuring deferred compensation [@CL-EST-2502; @CL-EST-2505] |

The purpose of CAS 405 is to facilitate the negotiation, audit, administration and settlement of contracts by establishing guidelines covering two matters [@CL-EST-2636]. The first is the identification of costs specifically described as unallowable, at the time such costs first become defined or authoritatively designated as unallowable [@CL-EST-2502]. The second is the cost accounting treatment to be accorded such identified unallowable costs, to promote the consistent application of sound cost accounting principles covering all incurred costs [@CL-EST-2637].

CAS 405 is predicated on the proposition that costs incurred in carrying on the activities of an enterprise, regardless of their allowability under Government contracts, are allocable to the cost objectives with which they are identified on the basis of their beneficial or causal relationships [@CL-EST-9459] [@CL-EST-2503]. It does not govern the allowability of costs [@CL-EST-2504]. Allowability is a function of the appropriate procurement or reviewing authority [@CL-EST-2638].

The purpose of CAS 415 is to provide criteria for measuring the cost of deferred compensation and assigning that cost to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2505]. Applying the criteria should increase the probability that the cost is allocated to cost objectives in a uniform and consistent manner [@CL-EST-2639]. It is applicable to the cost of all deferred compensation except the cost for compensated personal absence and the cost for pension plans that do not meet the definition of an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP), which other Cost Accounting Standards cover [@CL-EST-2506].

CAS 415 is effective as of June 2, 2008 [@CL-EST-2507]. It shall be followed by each contractor on or after the start of its next cost accounting period beginning after the receipt of a contract or subcontract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2640]. Contractors with prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage shall continue to follow the version in effect before June 2, 2008 until the revised Standard becomes applicable following receipt of a contract or subcontract to which it applies [@CL-EST-2508].

For contractors and subcontractors that established advance agreements before June 2, 2008 on recognizing the costs of existing ESOPs, the awarding agency and contractor shall comply with those agreements for those ESOPs [@CL-EST-2509], regardless of whether the ESOP was previously subject to CAS 412 or 415 [@CL-EST-2641]. Those advance agreements may be modified, by mutual agreement, to incorporate the requirements effective on June 2, 2008 [@CL-EST-2510].

## How it works

CAS 405 establishes guidelines covering the identification of costs specifically described as unallowable and the cost accounting treatment to be accorded such identified unallowable costs [@CL-EST-2502; @CL-EST-2637]. CAS 415 provides criteria for the measurement of the cost of deferred compensation and the assignment of such cost to cost accounting periods [@CL-EST-2505].

CAS 405 does not govern the allowability of costs [@CL-EST-2504], which is a function of the appropriate procurement or reviewing authority [@CL-EST-2638].

CAS 415 shall be followed by each contractor on or after the start of its next cost accounting period beginning after the receipt of a contract or subcontract to which it is applicable [@CL-EST-2640]. Contractors with prior CAS-covered contracts with full coverage shall continue to follow the version in effect before June 2, 2008 until the revised Standard becomes applicable following receipt of a contract or subcontract to which it applies [@CL-EST-2508].

For contractors and subcontractors that established advance agreements before June 2, 2008 on the recognition of the costs of existing ESOPs, the awarding agency and contractor shall comply with those agreements for those ESOPs [@CL-EST-2509]. Those advance agreements may be modified, by mutual agreement, to incorporate the requirements effective on June 2, 2008 [@CL-EST-2510].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor's cost accounting period is its calendar fiscal year. It receives a subcontract covered by CAS 415 in May of Year 1. The revised Standard applies from the start of its next cost accounting period beginning after receipt, which is January of Year 2 [@CL-EST-2640]. The contractor also has older full coverage contracts, so it stays on the version in effect before June 2, 2008 until the revised Standard becomes applicable following receipt of the covered subcontract, which means through December of Year 1 [@CL-EST-2508; @CL-EST-2640].

Separately, an advance agreement signed before June 2, 2008 covers the costs of an existing ESOP. The awarding agency and the contractor comply with it for that ESOP, even if the ESOP was once subject to CAS 412 [@CL-EST-2509; @CL-EST-2641]. It may be modified by mutual agreement to incorporate the requirements effective on June 2, 2008 [@CL-EST-2510].

A pension plan that does not meet the definition of an ESOP is outside CAS 415 and is covered by other Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-EST-2506].

## Never confuse

CAS 405 does not govern the allowability of costs [@CL-EST-2504]. It establishes guidelines on the identification of costs specifically described as unallowable and the cost accounting treatment to be accorded them [@CL-EST-2502; @CL-EST-2637], while allowability is a function of the appropriate procurement or reviewing authority [@CL-EST-2638].

CAS 415 is applicable to the cost of all deferred compensation except the cost for compensated personal absence and the cost for pension plans that do not meet the definition of an ESOP, which are covered in other Cost Accounting Standards [@CL-EST-2506].

The rules of each Standard are on the [unallowable costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-405-unallowable-costs.md), [accounting for unallowable costs](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-accounting-for-unallowable-and-associated-costs.md) and [deferred compensation](../../compliance/cas-allowability/cmp-cas-415-deferred-compensation.md) cards.
