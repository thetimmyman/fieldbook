## Purpose

Shows the market research a contractor owes before certain subcontract awards, and how alternative line items may be proposed and what happens to them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2297] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.210-1 [@CL-EST-2297]; FAR 52.204-22 [@CL-EST-2299] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2297] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts for other than commercial acquisitions that are over the simplified acquisition threshold on the date of subcontract award [@CL-EST-2297], and solicitations that invite alternative line item proposals [@CL-EST-2299] |

Before awarding subcontracts for other than commercial acquisitions where the subcontracts are over the simplified acquisition threshold as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, the contractor must conduct market research [@CL-EST-2297]. The research determines whether commercial products, commercial services or, where suitable commercial products are not available, nondevelopmental items are available [@CL-EST-2385] that meet the agency's requirements, could be modified to meet them, or could meet them if the requirements were modified to a reasonable extent [@CL-EST-2298]. It also determines the extent to which commercial products, commercial services or nondevelopmental items could be incorporated at the component level [@CL-EST-2386].

## How it works

The offeror is invited to propose alternative line items, so that the resulting contract is economically and administratively advantageous to the Government and the offeror [@CL-EST-2299]. The offeror may submit one or more additional proposals with alternative line items, provided the alternative line items are consistent with FAR subpart 4.10 [@CL-EST-2300]. Acceptance of an alternative proposal is a unilateral decision made solely at the discretion of the Government [@CL-EST-2301]. Offers that do not comply with the line items the solicitation specifies may be determined to be nonresponsive or unacceptable [@CL-EST-2387].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

On the date of subcontract award the simplified acquisition threshold is 350,000 in this example. A contractor plans three subcontracts. The first is for other than commercial products and services and is priced at 500,000, so market research is required before award. The second is also non-commercial but priced at 90,000, which is below the threshold, so the clause does not require the research. The third is for commercial products and is priced at 500,000, so it is not a subcontract for other than commercial acquisitions.

In a separate solicitation, an offeror whose own practice bills three of the specified line items as one submits an additional proposal with that alternative line item, consistent with FAR subpart 4.10. The Government may decline to accept the alternative proposal. A separate offer that simply ignores the specified line items may be determined to be nonresponsive or unacceptable.

## Never confuse

Market research is the contractor's own duty before placing certain subcontracts [@CL-EST-2297], while alternative line items are an offeror's proposals in a solicitation, which the Government may decline [@CL-EST-2300; @CL-EST-2301]. An alternative proposal is not an offer that ignores the specified line items, which may be determined to be nonresponsive or unacceptable [@CL-EST-2387].

## Company calibration

Where the market research record for each subcontract award is kept and reviewed. [@Q-023]
