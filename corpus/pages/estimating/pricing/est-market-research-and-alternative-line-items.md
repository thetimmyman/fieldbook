## Purpose

Shows the market research a contractor owes before certain subcontract awards, and how alternative line items may be proposed and what happens to them.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2297] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.210-1 [@CL-EST-2297]; FAR 52.204-22 [@CL-EST-2299] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 52.210-1 [@CL-EST-0339; @CL-EST-0340; @CL-EST-0341]; No RFO equivalent for FAR 52.204-22, which the RFO text prints as reserved [@CL-EST-0342]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9358] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2297] |
| Applies to | Subcontracts for other than commercial acquisitions that are over the simplified acquisition threshold on the date of subcontract award [@CL-EST-2297], and solicitations that invite alternative line item proposals [@CL-EST-2299] |

Before awarding subcontracts for other than commercial acquisitions where the subcontracts are over the simplified acquisition threshold as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award, the contractor must conduct market research [@CL-EST-2297]. The research determines whether commercial products, commercial services or, to the extent commercial products suitable to meet the agency's needs are not available, nondevelopmental items are available [@CL-EST-2385] that meet the agency's requirements, could be modified to meet them, or could meet them if the requirements were modified to a reasonable extent [@CL-EST-2298]. It also determines the extent to which commercial products, commercial services or nondevelopmental items could be incorporated at the component level [@CL-EST-2386].

FAR 2.101 defines market research as collecting and analyzing information about capabilities within the market to satisfy agency needs [@CL-EST-9293]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines market research as collecting and analyzing information about capabilities within the market to satisfy agency needs, the same in substance as the codified definition [@CL-EST-9358; @CL-EST-9293].

## How it works

The offeror is invited to propose alternative line items for which bids, proposals or quotes are requested in the solicitation, so that the resulting contract is economically and administratively advantageous to the Government and the offeror [@CL-EST-2299]. The offeror may submit one or more additional proposals with alternative line items, provided the alternative line items are consistent with FAR subpart 4.10 [@CL-EST-2300]. Acceptance of an alternative proposal is a unilateral decision made solely at the discretion of the Government [@CL-EST-2301]. Offers that do not comply with the line items the solicitation specifies may be determined to be nonresponsive or unacceptable [@CL-EST-2387].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 52 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026, because the DoD part 52 class deviation directs its use [@CL-CMP-9760] [@CL-CMP-9762]. The RFO text of FAR 52.210-1(b) says the contractor conducts market research before awarding subcontracts for noncommercial acquisitions over the simplified acquisition threshold, as defined in FAR 2.101 on the date of subcontract award [@CL-EST-0339], where the codified text says other than commercial acquisitions [@CL-EST-2297]. It asks, in the following order of priority [@CL-EST-0339], whether a commercial product or commercial service can meet the agency's requirements and whether the requirements could be modified so the agency could use an existing commercial product or commercial service [@CL-EST-0340], then whether a commercial product or commercial service could be modified to meet the requirements, and whether the requirement can only be satisfied by a nondevelopmental item [@CL-EST-0341]. The codified text instead asks whether commercial products, commercial services or, to the extent commercial products suitable to meet the agency's needs are not available, nondevelopmental items are available [@CL-EST-2385] that meet the requirements, could be modified to meet them, or could meet them if the requirements were modified to a reasonable extent [@CL-EST-2298]. The RFO list ends after item (4) and the clause ends there, with no item on the extent to which items could be incorporated at the component level [@CL-EST-0341], which the codified text has [@CL-EST-2386]. The RFO text prints FAR 52.204-22, the number of the codified alternative line item provision, as reserved [@CL-EST-0342]; the alternative line item rules described above come from the codified provision [@CL-EST-2299].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

On the date of subcontract award the simplified acquisition threshold is 350,000 in this example. A contractor plans three subcontracts. The first is for other than commercial products and services and is priced at 500,000, so market research is required before award. The second is also non-commercial but priced at 90,000, which is below the threshold, so the clause does not require the research. The third is for commercial products and is priced at 500,000, so it is not a subcontract for other than commercial acquisitions.

In a separate solicitation, an offeror whose own practice bills three of the specified line items as one submits an additional proposal with that alternative line item, consistent with FAR subpart 4.10. The Government may decline to accept the alternative proposal. This part of the example follows the codified provision [@CL-EST-2299; @CL-EST-2301], whose number the RFO text prints as reserved [@CL-EST-0342]. A separate offer that simply ignores the specified line items may be determined to be nonresponsive or unacceptable.

## Never confuse

Market research is the contractor's own duty before placing certain subcontracts [@CL-EST-2297], while alternative line items are an offeror's proposals in a solicitation, whose acceptance is a unilateral decision made solely at the discretion of the Government [@CL-EST-2300; @CL-EST-2301]. Offers that do not comply with the line items the solicitation specifies may be determined to be nonresponsive or unacceptable [@CL-EST-2387]. The research list is built differently in the two texts: the RFO list runs in a stated order of priority and ends by asking whether the requirement can only be satisfied by a nondevelopmental item [@CL-EST-0339; @CL-EST-0341], while the codified list asks about nondevelopmental items to the extent suitable commercial products are not available [@CL-EST-2385].

The offeror may submit alternative line items provided they are consistent with FAR subpart 4.10 [@CL-EST-2300], and the instruments that the line item rules cover are described in [Which procurement instruments the line item rules cover](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-contract-line-item-rules-applicability.md).
