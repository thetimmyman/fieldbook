## Purpose

Shows who may ask for a debriefing before award, by when, what it includes and what it may not disclose.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-1411] |
| Codified citation | FAR 15.501 [@CL-EST-2054]; FAR 15.505 [@CL-EST-1411] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 15.206-2 [@CL-EST-3346]; RFO FAR 15.206(a) [@CL-EST-2246] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1411] |
| Applies to | Offerors excluded from the competitive range or otherwise excluded from the competition before award [@CL-EST-1411] |

Offerors excluded from the competitive range or otherwise excluded from the competition before award may request a debriefing before award [@CL-EST-1411]. The offeror requests it by submitting a written request to the contracting officer within 3 days after receipt of the notice of exclusion [@CL-EST-1412].

An offeror that does not submit a timely request need not be given either a preaward or a postaward debriefing, and offerors are entitled to no more than one debriefing for each proposal [@CL-EST-1414]. The postaward debriefing section adds that an offeror notified of exclusion that failed to submit a timely request is not entitled to a debriefing, although untimely requests may be accommodated [@CL-EST-1450].

The contracting officer shall make every effort to debrief the unsuccessful offeror as soon as practicable, but may refuse the request if, for compelling reasons, it is not in the best interests of the Government to conduct a debriefing at that time [@CL-EST-1453].

## How it works

The word day, as used in FAR subpart 15.5, has the meaning set forth at FAR 33.101 [@CL-EST-2054], and the 3-day request period above is stated in FAR 15.505 [@CL-EST-1412].

At the offeror's request the debriefing may be delayed until after award, and a debriefing delayed that way shall include all information normally provided in a postaward debriefing [@CL-EST-1413]. If the contracting officer delays the debriefing, it shall be provided no later than the time postaward debriefings are provided, and it shall include the information that a postaward debriefing includes at a minimum [@CL-EST-1454]. At a minimum a preaward debriefing includes the agency's evaluation of significant elements in the offeror's proposal and a summary of the rationale for eliminating the offeror [@CL-EST-1415], and reasonable responses to relevant questions about whether source selection procedures contained in the solicitation, applicable regulations, and other applicable authorities were followed in the process of eliminating the offeror [@CL-EST-1415; @CL-EST-1457]. A preaward debriefing shall not disclose the number of offerors, the identity of other offerors, the content of other offerors' proposals, the ranking of other offerors, the evaluation of other offerors, or any information the postaward debriefing rule bars from disclosure [@CL-EST-1416].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 15 text in place of the codified text under Class Deviation 2026-O0048, whose Revision 1 took effect immediately and supersedes the March 5, 2026 version [@CL-EST-3176] [@CL-EST-3177]. RFO FAR 15.206-2 keeps the rule that offerors excluded from the competitive range or otherwise excluded from the competition before award may request a debriefing before award [@CL-EST-1411; @CL-EST-3346], by a written request to the contracting officer within 3 days after receipt of the notice of exclusion [@CL-EST-1412; @CL-EST-3347]. It keeps the rule that an offeror that does not submit a timely request need not be given either a preaward or a postaward debriefing, and that offerors are entitled to no more than one debriefing for each proposal [@CL-EST-1414; @CL-EST-3349]. A debriefing delayed until after award at the offeror's request must still include all information normally provided in a postaward debriefing, but the RFO sentence carries no cross-reference to 15.506(d), which the codified sentence has [@CL-EST-1413; @CL-EST-3348]. The minimum content keeps the agency's evaluation of significant elements in the offeror's proposal and the summary of the rationale for eliminating the offeror [@CL-EST-1415; @CL-EST-3350]. The nondisclosure list keeps all six items, with the last item pointing to 15.301-1(d) where the codified item points to 15.506(e) [@CL-EST-1416; @CL-EST-3352]. The contracting officer must still make every effort to debrief as soon as practicable and may refuse for compelling reasons [@CL-EST-1453; @CL-EST-3353], and a delayed debriefing must still be provided no later than postaward debriefings, with the cross-references now 15.301-1 and 15.301-1(c) [@CL-EST-1454; @CL-EST-3354]. RFO FAR 15.206(a) says that day, as used in that section, has the meaning set forth at part 33 [@CL-EST-2246], where the codified definition covers the word as used in FAR subpart 15.5 and points to FAR 33.101 [@CL-EST-2054].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

An offeror is told it is excluded from the competition. It sends a written request on day 2 and gets a debriefing covering the agency's evaluation of its own proposal and why it was eliminated. The debriefing does not say how many offerors there were or how the others ranked. The answer: the offeror hears about its own proposal and its elimination, not about the other offerors.

## Never confuse

A preaward debriefing is not a postaward debriefing, although a delayed one takes on the postaward content [@CL-EST-1413]. A preaward debriefing may not disclose the number of offerors or the ranking of other offerors [@CL-EST-1416]. The postaward rules are on [Postaward debriefing of offerors](est-postaward-debriefing.md), and the notice that triggers the request is on [Notices to unsuccessful offerors](est-notices-to-unsuccessful-offerors.md).

The codified text asks for reasonable responses to questions about whether source selection procedures were followed in the process of eliminating the offeror from the competition [@CL-EST-1457], while the RFO text asks about whether they were followed by the agency [@CL-EST-3351].
