## Purpose

Shows what an offer on a commercial products and commercial services solicitation must contain, and how submission, product samples, late receipt, withdrawal, the acceptance period, multiple offers, multiple awards, the award basis and debriefing work under the instructions to offerors provision.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2261] |
| Codified citation | FAR 52.212-1 [@CL-EST-2261] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2261] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that carry the instructions to offerors provision, which is inserted as prescribed in FAR 12.301(b)(1) [@CL-EST-2311] |

An offer is signed and dated and reaches the office named in the solicitation at or before the exact time the solicitation sets [@CL-EST-2261]. Offers may be submitted on the SF 1449, on letterhead stationery, or as the solicitation otherwise specifies [@CL-EST-2316].

As a minimum an offer shows the solicitation number, the time set for receipt of offers, and the offeror's name, address and telephone number [@CL-EST-2262]. It also shows a technical description of the items offered in enough detail to evaluate compliance, which may include product literature or other documents if necessary [@CL-EST-2263]. It shows the terms of any express warranty, its price and any discount terms, and a remit-to address if that differs from the mailing address [@CL-EST-2317]. It shows a completed copy of the representations and certifications at FAR 52.212-3, completed electronically where FAR 52.212-3(b) requires, and an acknowledgment of solicitation amendments [@CL-EST-2318]. When past performance is an evaluation factor, it shows past performance information, including recent and relevant contracts for the same or similar items and other references with contract numbers and points of contact [@CL-EST-2319].

An offer that is not submitted on the SF 1449 includes a statement specifying the extent of its agreement with all terms, conditions and provisions in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2320]. An offer that fails to furnish required representations or information, or that rejects the terms and conditions of the solicitation, may be excluded from consideration [@CL-EST-2264].

Prices stay firm for 30 calendar days from the date specified for receipt of offers, unless an addendum to the solicitation specifies another period [@CL-EST-2265].

The NAICS code and small business size standard for the acquisition appear elsewhere in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2400]. However, the provision sets a small business size standard for a concern that proposes to furnish an end item it did not itself manufacture, process or produce, other than on a construction or service acquisition: 500 employees [@CL-EST-2312], or 150 employees for information technology value-added resellers under NAICS code 541519 [@CL-EST-2313]. That standard applies if the acquisition meets one of three conditions: it is set aside for small business and has a value above the simplified acquisition threshold, or it uses the HUBZone price evaluation preference regardless of dollar value unless the offeror waives that preference [@CL-EST-2314]; or it is an 8(a), HUBZone, service-disabled veteran-owned, economically disadvantaged women-owned or women-owned small business set-aside or sole-source award regardless of dollar value [@CL-EST-2315].

## How it works

Offerors are responsible for getting offers, modifications, revisions and withdrawals to the designated Government office by the time the solicitation specifies [@CL-EST-2323]. If the solicitation specifies no time, the time for receipt is 4:30 in the afternoon, local time, for the designated office on the date offers or revisions are due [@CL-EST-2324].

An offer, modification, revision or withdrawal received at the designated office after the exact time specified for receipt is late [@CL-EST-2325]. A late item is not considered unless it is received before award is made and the contracting officer determines that accepting it would not unduly delay the acquisition [@CL-EST-2266]. In addition, one of three further conditions must be met. The first is that the item was transmitted by an electronic commerce method the solicitation authorized and reached the initial point of entry to the Government infrastructure not later than 5:00 in the afternoon one working day before the date specified for receipt [@CL-EST-2326]. The second is that acceptable evidence shows it was received at the Government installation designated for receipt and was under the Government's control before the time set [@CL-EST-2327]. The third applies to a request for proposals: the late proposal was the only proposal received [@CL-EST-2328].

Acceptable evidence of the time of receipt at the Government installation includes the installation's time and date stamp on the offer wrapper, other documentary evidence of receipt that the installation maintains, and oral testimony or statements of Government personnel [@CL-EST-2329]. A late modification of an otherwise successful offer that makes its terms more favorable to the Government is considered at any time it is received and may be accepted [@CL-EST-2267].

If an emergency or unanticipated event interrupts normal Government processes so that offers cannot be received at the designated office by the exact time specified [@CL-EST-2330], and urgent Government requirements preclude amendment of the solicitation or other notice of an extension of the closing date [@CL-EST-2331], the time for receipt is deemed extended to the same time of day on the first work day on which normal Government processes resume [@CL-EST-2332].

An offer may be withdrawn by written notice received at any time before the exact time set for receipt, and an oral offer made in response to an oral solicitation may be withdrawn orally [@CL-EST-2268]. If the solicitation authorizes facsimile offers, an offer may be withdrawn by facsimile received before that time, subject to the solicitation's conditions on facsimile offers [@CL-EST-2333]. An offer may also be withdrawn in person by the offeror or its authorized representative if, before that time, the person's identity is established and the person signs a receipt for the offer [@CL-EST-2334].

When the solicitation requires product samples, they are submitted at or before the time set for receipt of offers [@CL-EST-2321]. Unless the solicitation says otherwise, samples are submitted at no expense to the Government and returned at the sender's request and expense, unless they are destroyed during preaward testing [@CL-EST-2322].

Offerors are encouraged to submit multiple offers presenting alternative terms and conditions, including alternative line items consistent with FAR subpart 4.10, or alternative commercial products or commercial services [@CL-EST-2271]. Each offer is evaluated separately [@CL-EST-2272].

Except on an invitation for bids, the Government intends to evaluate offers and award a contract without discussions, so the initial offer should contain the offeror's best price and technical terms [@CL-EST-2269]. It reserves the right to conduct discussions if the contracting officer later determines they are necessary [@CL-EST-2335]. It may reject any or all offers if that is in the public interest, accept other than the lowest offer, and waive informalities and minor irregularities [@CL-EST-2270].

The Government may accept any item or group of items of an offer unless the offeror qualifies the offer by specific limitations [@CL-EST-2336]. Unless the Schedule provides otherwise, offers may not be submitted for quantities less than those specified [@CL-EST-2337]. The Government reserves the right to award any item for a quantity less than the quantity offered, at the unit prices offered, unless the offeror specifies otherwise in the offer [@CL-EST-2338].

The General Services Administration index of Federal specifications and standards, and copies of Federal specifications, standards and product descriptions, can be downloaded from the ASSIST website [@CL-EST-2339]. Nongovernment (voluntary) standards must be obtained from the organization responsible for their preparation, publication or maintenance [@CL-EST-2340]. Other sources of requirements documents, including those for Defense specifications and standards and for solicitations issued by certain agencies, are covered on [Availability of specifications, standards and data item descriptions](est-availability-of-specifications-standards-and-dids.md).

The unique entity identifier paragraph applies to all offers that exceed the micro-purchase threshold, and to offers at or below that threshold if the solicitation requires registration in the System for Award Management [@CL-EST-2341]. The offeror enters, in the block with its name and address on the cover page of its offer, the annotation Unique Entity Identifier followed by its identifier [@CL-EST-2342]. It also enters its Electronic Funds Transfer indicator, if applicable, which is a four-character suffix to the identifier [@CL-EST-2343]. An offeror without an identifier should contact the entity designated at www.sam.gov to obtain one [@CL-EST-2401].

If a post-award debriefing is given to requesting offerors, the Government discloses, if applicable, its evaluation of the significant weak or deficient factors in the debriefed offeror's offer [@CL-EST-2344]. It also discloses the overall evaluated cost or price and technical rating of the successful and debriefed offerors, past performance information on the debriefed offeror, and the overall ranking of all offerors when the agency developed any ranking [@CL-EST-2345]. It discloses a summary of the rationale for award and, for acquisitions of commercial products, the make and model of the product the successful offeror will deliver [@CL-EST-2346]. It gives reasonable responses to relevant questions about whether the source-selection procedures in the solicitation, applicable regulations and other authorities were followed [@CL-EST-2347]. See [Postaward debriefing](est-postaward-debriefing.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation sets receipt for 2:00 in the afternoon on 12 March. An offeror on its own letterhead, not the SF 1449, submits a base offer at 250,000 and an additional offer at 238,000 with alternative line items consistent with FAR subpart 4.10. Each includes a statement of the extent of its agreement with the solicitation's terms. The two offers are evaluated separately, and both prices stay firm for 30 calendar days from 12 March.

The offeror then mails a revision of the base offer. It reaches the designated office at 2:20 in the afternoon, after the exact time, so it is late. The mailroom stamp at the Government installation shows it was received there at 1:40 in the afternoon, and it was under Government control before 2:00. Award has not been made, and the contracting officer determines that accepting the revision would not unduly delay the acquisition. The revision can be considered because the installation stamp is acceptable evidence of receipt at the installation before the time set.

## Never confuse

The time of receipt decides which rule applies. Written notice received before the exact time set for receipt withdraws an offer [@CL-EST-2268], while an offer, modification, revision or withdrawal received after that time is late and is considered only under the late-submission conditions [@CL-EST-2325; @CL-EST-2266]. A late modification that makes an otherwise successful offer more favorable to the Government is treated separately: it is considered at any time it is received and may be accepted [@CL-EST-2267].

Multiple offers and multiple awards are different. Submitting multiple offers is the offeror's choice, and each is evaluated separately [@CL-EST-2271; @CL-EST-2272]. Multiple awards are the Government's choice to accept any item or group of items of an offer unless the offeror qualifies the offer by specific limitations [@CL-EST-2336].

The emergency extension needs both an interruption and urgent Government requirements that preclude amendment or other notice of an extension [@CL-EST-2330; @CL-EST-2331]. See also [Instructions to offerors for competitive acquisitions](est-instructions-to-offerors-competitive-acquisition.md) and [Late bid submissions and withdrawals](est-late-bid-submissions-and-withdrawals.md).
