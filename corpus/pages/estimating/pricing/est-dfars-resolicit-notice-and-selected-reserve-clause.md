## Purpose

Shows when a short solicitation may be cancelled and reissued after a single offer, and what a contractor owes if its offer states an intent to use Selected Reserve members.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS [@CL-EST-1739; @CL-EST-1772] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.215-7007 [@CL-EST-1739; @CL-EST-1778]; DFARS 252.215-7006 [@CL-EST-1772; @CL-EST-1779] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-1739; @CL-EST-1772] |
| Applies to | Solicitations that use the notice provision as prescribed at DFARS 215.371-6 [@CL-EST-1778], and contracts that include the Selected Reserve clause as prescribed in DFARS 215.370-3 [@CL-EST-1779] where the offer stated an intent to use Selected Reserve members [@CL-EST-1772] |

The notice provision is the one to use as prescribed at DFARS 215.371-6 [@CL-EST-1778]. It tells offerors that the solicitation provides fewer than 30 days to submit proposals [@CL-EST-1739]. If only one offer is received, the Contracting Officer may cancel the solicitation and resolicit for an additional period of at least 30 days in accordance with the cited DFARS section [@CL-EST-1740].

The Selected Reserve clause is the one to use as prescribed in DFARS 215.370-3 [@CL-EST-1779]. It defines Selected Reserve by reference to the statute it names [@CL-EST-1741]. It lists duties and a consequence that apply if the Contractor stated in its offer that it intends to use members of the Selected Reserve in the performance of the contract [@CL-EST-1772]. The first listed duty is that the Contractor shall use employees, or individual subcontractors, who are members of the Selected Reserve in the performance of the contract to the fullest extent consistent with efficient contract performance [@CL-EST-1772; @CL-EST-1742]. The listed consequence is that the Government has the right to terminate the contract for default if the Contractor willfully or intentionally fails to use members of the Selected Reserve, as employees or individual subcontractors, in the performance of the contract [@CL-EST-1772; @CL-EST-1743].

## How it works

The notice provision is a warning to offerors: the solicitation is short, and a single offer may lead to cancellation and a second round of at least 30 days [@CL-EST-1739; @CL-EST-1740]. The provision says the Contracting Officer may resolicit, not that the officer must [@CL-EST-1740]. The duties and the default right in the Selected Reserve clause are listed under the condition that the contractor said in its offer that it intends to use Selected Reserve members [@CL-EST-1772]. The printed clause does not say how an offer's stated intent is scored in source selection, so the source does not establish an evaluation method.

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A solicitation gives offerors 20 days and carries the notice provision. Only one offer arrives, so the Contracting Officer cancels it and resolicits for another 30 days. Separately, a contractor that stated an intent to use Selected Reserve members hires two individual subcontractors who are members. It is meeting the duty to the extent consistent with efficient performance.

## Never confuse

The notice provision is about cancelling and resolicitation, while the pricing steps after a single offer are on [Price determination after one offer](est-dod-one-offer-price-determination.md). The additional data an offeror agrees to provide after a single offer is on [Only one offer received](est-only-one-offer.md). The Selected Reserve duties are listed under the condition of a stated intent in the offer, so an offer that says nothing on the point does not meet that condition [@CL-EST-1772].
