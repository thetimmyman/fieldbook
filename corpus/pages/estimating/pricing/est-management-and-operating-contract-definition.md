## Purpose

Fixes what a management and operating contract is under FAR subpart 17.6, which prescribes policies and procedures for those contracts for the Department of Energy and any other agency having requisite statutory authority [@CL-EST-3054]. Also states which acquisitions FAR subpart 17.7 covers, being acquisitions by nondefense agencies on behalf of DoD [@CL-EST-3056], and which it excludes [@CL-EST-3045].

Definition. A management and operating contract is an agreement under which the Government contracts for the operation, maintenance, or support, on its behalf, of a Government-owned or -controlled research, development, special production, or testing establishment wholly or principally devoted to one or more major programs of the contracting Federal agency [@CL-EST-3042; @CL-EST-3043].

Applicability. Subpart 17.7 applies to all acquisitions made by nondefense agencies on behalf of DoD [@CL-EST-3044]. It does not apply to contracts entered into by a nondefense agency that is an element of the intelligence community for the performance of a joint program conducted to meet the needs of DoD and the nondefense agency [@CL-EST-3045]. Compliance with subpart 17.7 is in addition to the policies and procedures for interagency acquisitions set forth in subpart 17.5 [@CL-EST-3055].

## How it works

The definition has five elements. There must be an agreement under which the Government contracts. It must be for operation, maintenance, or support, on the Government's behalf. The establishment must be Government-owned or -controlled and be a research, development, special production, or testing establishment. It must be wholly or principally devoted to one or more major programs. Those programs must be those of the contracting Federal agency [@CL-EST-3042; @CL-EST-3043]. Who may authorize such a contract, what it may not cover and how it is renewed are on [Management and operating contracts: policy, limitations and identification](../../market/programs/mkt-management-and-operating-contracts-policy-and-limitations.md).

The applicability sentences turn on who buys. Subpart 17.7 covers an acquisition when a nondefense agency makes it on behalf of DoD [@CL-EST-3044]. The exclusion turns on three facts together: the contracting nondefense agency is an element of the intelligence community, the contract is for a joint program, and the program is conducted to meet the needs of DoD and that agency [@CL-EST-3045]. The definitions of the agency terms and the duties on DoD acquisition officials and nondefense agencies are on [Acquisitions by nondefense agencies on behalf of the Department of Defense](../../market/programs/mkt-nondefense-agency-acquisitions-for-dod.md). The rules for interagency acquisitions generally are on [Interagency acquisitions: scope, general rules and reporting](../../market/programs/mkt-interagency-acquisitions-scope-and-reporting.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A Government-owned research laboratory is devoted wholly to one of the contracting agency's major programs, and the agency contracts a firm to operate and maintain it on the agency's behalf. Each element of the definition is met [@CL-EST-3042; @CL-EST-3043]. A second Government-owned research laboratory does no work for any major program of the contracting agency, so a contract to operate it does not meet the "wholly or principally devoted" element [@CL-EST-3043].

A nondefense agency that is not an element of the intelligence community places a 9,000,000 acquisition on behalf of DoD, so subpart 17.7 applies [@CL-EST-3044]. An element of the intelligence community enters into a 3,000,000 contract for a joint program conducted to meet the needs of DoD and that agency, so subpart 17.7 does not apply to that contract [@CL-EST-3045].

## Never confuse

In a management and operating contract, the establishment is a Government-owned or -controlled research, development, special production, or testing establishment wholly or principally devoted to one or more major programs of the contracting Federal agency [@CL-EST-3042; @CL-EST-3043].

Subpart 17.7 applies to all acquisitions made by nondefense agencies on behalf of DoD [@CL-EST-3044], and it does not apply to contracts entered into by a nondefense agency that is an element of the intelligence community for the performance of a joint program conducted to meet the needs of DoD and the nondefense agency [@CL-EST-3045].
