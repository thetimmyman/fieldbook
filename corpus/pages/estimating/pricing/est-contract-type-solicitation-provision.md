## Purpose

Shows which solicitations must carry the type of contract provision, and when a negotiated acquisition that contemplates a fixed-price contract must carry the prospective price redetermination clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.105 and 16.205-4 [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-EST-9314] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976] |
| Applies to | The provision: solicitations, except the two excluded acquisition types [@CL-EST-2975]. The clause: solicitations and contracts, when contracting by negotiation, a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the specified conditions apply [@CL-EST-2976] |

The contracting officer must complete and insert the provision at 52.216-1, Type of Contract, in a solicitation [@CL-EST-2975]. There are two exceptions. The duty does not arise for a solicitation for a fixed-price acquisition made under simplified acquisition procedures, or for a solicitation for information or planning purposes [@CL-EST-2975]. The FAR definitions section describes a solicitation provision as a term or condition used only in solicitations and applying only before contract award [@CL-EST-9314]. The RFO text of FAR 2.101 defines a solicitation provision, or provision, as a term or condition used only in solicitations and applying only before contract award, the same in substance as the codified definition [@CL-EST-9366; @CL-EST-9314].

When contracting by negotiation, the contracting officer must insert the clause at 52.216-5, Price Redetermination—Prospective, in solicitations and contracts [@CL-EST-2976]. The duty arises when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the conditions specified in 16.205-2 and 16.205-3(a) through (d) apply [@CL-EST-2976].

Both sections put the duty on the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall complete and insert the provision in a solicitation unless it is for a fixed-price acquisition made under simplified acquisition procedures or for information or planning purposes [@CL-EST-2975]. The provision tells offerors that the Government contemplates award of the type of contract the contracting officer inserts in the blank [@CL-EST-1842]. See [the type of contract provision](est-type-of-contract-letter-and-time-and-materials-provisions.md).

The contracting officer shall, when contracting by negotiation, insert the clause in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the conditions specified in 16.205-2 and 16.205-3(a) through (d) apply [@CL-EST-2976].

Section 16.205-2 says a fixed-price contract with prospective price redetermination may be used in acquisitions of quantity production or services for which it is possible to negotiate a fair and reasonable firm fixed price for an initial period, but not for subsequent periods of contract performance [@CL-CLS-0551]. Section 16.205-3 says that the contract type shall not be used unless negotiations have established that the conditions for a firm-fixed-price contract are not present and that a fixed-price incentive contract would not be more appropriate [@CL-CLS-0554]; the contractor's accounting system is adequate for price redetermination; the prospective pricing periods can be made to conform with operation of the contractor's accounting system; and there is reasonable assurance that price redetermination actions will take place promptly at the specified times [@CL-CLS-0555; @CL-CLS-0193]. See [prospective price redetermination contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-prospective-price-redetermination.md). For what the clause requires of the contractor, see [the prospective price redetermination clause](est-prospective-price-redetermination-clause.md). For fixed-price contracts in general, see [fixed-price contracts in general](est-fixed-price-contracts-general.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Solicitation A is a negotiated acquisition. It contemplates a fixed-price production contract with a firm price for an initial period and redetermination for later periods, and the conditions in 16.205-2 and 16.205-3(a) through (d) apply [@CL-EST-2976]. It is not for information or planning purposes and is not a fixed-price acquisition under simplified acquisition procedures [@CL-EST-2975]. The contracting officer inserts the clause in the solicitation and in the contract [@CL-EST-2976], and inserts the provision in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2975].

Solicitation B is a negotiated fixed-price production acquisition, not for information or planning purposes and not under simplified acquisition procedures, in which the conditions in 16.205-2 and 16.205-3(a) through (d) are not met. The contracting officer inserts the provision [@CL-EST-2975], and the duty to insert the clause does not arise [@CL-EST-2976].

Solicitation C buys office supplies at a firm fixed price under simplified acquisition procedures. The duty to insert the provision does not arise [@CL-EST-2975].

## Never confuse

FAR 52.216-1 is a preaward solicitation provision naming the contemplated contract type (omitted in fixed-price simplified acquisitions and planning solicitations) [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-9314], unlike FAR 52.216-5, which is a binding contract clause governing prospective price redetermination during performance [@CL-EST-2976; @CL-CLS-0550].

## Company calibration

Whether each solicitation in the pipeline carries the contract type provision and, for a negotiated fixed-price award, the prospective price redetermination clause, and which review evidence records both [@Q-012]
