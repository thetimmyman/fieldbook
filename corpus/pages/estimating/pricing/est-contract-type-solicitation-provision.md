## Purpose

Shows which solicitations must carry the type of contract provision, and when a negotiated acquisition that contemplates a fixed-price contract must carry the prospective price redetermination clause.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976] |
| Codified citation | FAR 16.105 and 16.205-4 [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976] |
| Applies to | The provision: solicitations, except the two excluded acquisition types [@CL-EST-2975]. The clause: solicitations and contracts, when contracting by negotiation, a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the specified conditions apply [@CL-EST-2976] |

The contracting officer must complete and insert the provision at 52.216-1, Type of Contract, in a solicitation [@CL-EST-2975]. There are two exceptions. The duty does not arise for a solicitation for a fixed-price acquisition made under simplified acquisition procedures, or for a solicitation for information or planning purposes [@CL-EST-2975].

When contracting by negotiation, the contracting officer must insert the clause at 52.216-5, Price Redetermination—Prospective, in solicitations and contracts [@CL-EST-2976]. The duty arises when a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the conditions specified in 16.205-2 and 16.205-3(a) through (d) apply [@CL-EST-2976].

Both sections put the duty on the contracting officer [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976]. Neither names a dollar threshold or a deadline, and neither says who decides whether an exception or a condition applies.

## How it works

The provision is the default for a solicitation. Only the two exceptions remove it, and any other solicitation carries it [@CL-EST-2975]. The provision tells offerors that the Government contemplates award of the type of contract the contracting officer inserts in the blank [@CL-EST-1842]. See [the type of contract provision](est-type-of-contract-letter-contract-and-time-and-materials-provisions.md).

The clause has three prerequisites. The acquisition must be by negotiation, a fixed-price contract must be contemplated, and the conditions specified in the two cross-referenced sections must apply [@CL-EST-2976]. The clause goes into both the solicitation and the contract [@CL-EST-2976].

Those conditions are not stated here. Section 16.205-2 says when the contract type may be used [@CL-CLS-0551]. Section 16.205-3 says that the contract type shall not be used unless negotiations have established that the conditions for a firm-fixed-price contract are not present and that a fixed-price incentive contract would not be more appropriate [@CL-CLS-0554], and it lists the further limits in paragraphs (b) through (d) [@CL-CLS-0555]. See [prospective price redetermination contracts](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-fixed-price-prospective-price-redetermination.md). For what the clause requires of the contractor, see [the prospective price redetermination clause](est-prospective-price-redetermination-clause.md). For fixed-price contracts in general, see [fixed-price contracts in general](est-fixed-price-contracts-general.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

Solicitation A is a negotiated acquisition. It contemplates a fixed-price production contract with a firm price for an initial period and redetermination for later periods, and the conditions in 16.205-2 and 16.205-3(a) through (d) apply [@CL-EST-2976]. It is not for information or planning purposes and is not a fixed-price acquisition under simplified acquisition procedures [@CL-EST-2975]. The contracting officer inserts the clause in the solicitation and in the contract [@CL-EST-2976], and inserts the provision in the solicitation [@CL-EST-2975].

Solicitation B is a negotiated fixed-price production acquisition, not for information or planning purposes and not under simplified acquisition procedures, in which the conditions in 16.205-2 and 16.205-3(a) through (d) are not met. The contracting officer inserts the provision [@CL-EST-2975], and the duty to insert the clause does not arise [@CL-EST-2976].

Solicitation C buys office supplies at a firm fixed price under simplified acquisition procedures. The duty to insert the provision does not arise [@CL-EST-2975].

## Never confuse

The provision and the clause are different instruments with different triggers. The provision goes in a solicitation unless one of two exceptions applies [@CL-EST-2975]. The clause goes in solicitations and contracts, and only when the acquisition is negotiated, a fixed-price contract is contemplated and the specified conditions apply [@CL-EST-2976]. The two exceptions to the provision do not govern the clause, and the three prerequisites for the clause do not govern the provision [@CL-EST-2975; @CL-EST-2976].

## Company calibration

Whether each solicitation in the pipeline carries the contract type provision and, for a negotiated fixed-price award, the prospective price redetermination clause, and which review evidence records both [@Q-012]
