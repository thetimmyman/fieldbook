## Purpose

The first stage of the curriculum: how Fieldbook states things, how to check them, and what to bring to the first meetings.

## The rule

Read the pages in order. Each page states only what its sources support, every figure cites a claim, and every company unknown cites an open question. Nothing in this stage is a fact about the company.
