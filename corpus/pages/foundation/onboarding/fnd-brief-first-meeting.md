## Question

What should the first meeting with finance, program and commercial owners cover, and what should be requested first?

## Recommendation

Spend the meeting on scope, decision rights and systems. Request the contract list with types and values, the approved rate sets, and the open-findings log, because public rules turn on contract type, value and dates. Keep all finance vocabulary provisional until the company's definitions are confirmed.

## Evidence

- Which rules apply turns on contract value: the CAS exemption [@CL-CMP-0001], the DoD certified data threshold [@CL-EST-0002] and the earned value threshold [@CL-PRG-0001].
- Financing terms differ by business size: the DoD customary progress payment rates [@CL-CTF-0001; @CL-CTF-0002].
- A fully funded cost contract with the Limitation of Cost clause requires notice when costs expected in the next 60 days will take total costs past 75 percent of the estimated cost [@CL-CTF-0003].
- Final indirect cost rate proposals are due within 6 months of year end [@CL-RTE-0001].

## Options

Opening with a systems walkthrough was rejected: it needs approved access first. Opening with financial results was rejected: the measures and their definitions are not yet known.

## Unknowns

- Which outcomes and decisions take priority in the first month [@Q-001].
- Who may propose, review, approve and execute each material financial decision [@Q-002].
- Which entities, units and contracts are inside the finance remit [@Q-003].
- Which systems are authoritative, and what access is approved [@Q-007].
- Which rate sets are approved for each purpose and period [@Q-011].
- Which measures management uses, and how they tie to the ledger [@Q-017].
- The contract mix by type and revenue [@Q-038].
