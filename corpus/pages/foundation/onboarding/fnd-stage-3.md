## Purpose

The third stage of the curriculum: which costs a government contract may carry, how they must be measured and allocated, and how they become prices and rates.

## The rule

Read the groups in order after [Stage 2: The contract world](fnd-stage-2.md). The general allowability tests come before any single cost principle, because every cost principle is applied through them.

## Never confuse

Allowability asks whether a cost may be charged to a government contract; the Cost Accounting Standards ask how a cost is measured, assigned to periods and allocated. A cost can pass one test and fail the other.
