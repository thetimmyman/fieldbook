## Purpose

Shows how Item 601 of Regulation S-K uses the exhibit table, the exhibit index and its general instructions to decide which documents are filed with a registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1166; @CL-FPA-1167; @CL-FPA-1168; @CL-FPA-1171]. The exhibit categories a finance reader uses are on the [exhibit categories card](fpa-exhibits-categories.md).

The exhibit table indicates those documents that must be filed as exhibits to the respective forms listed [@CL-FPA-1176]. The “X” designation indicates the documents which are required to be filed with each form even if filed previously with another document, Provided, However, that such previously filed documents may be incorporated by reference to satisfy the filing requirements [@CL-FPA-1177]. The number used in the far left column of the table refers to the appropriate subsection in paragraph (b) where a description of the exhibit can be found [@CL-FPA-1178]. Item 601 applies only to the forms specified in the exhibit table [@CL-FPA-1171].

Subject to Rule 411(c) under the Securities Act and Rule 12b-23(c) under the Exchange Act regarding incorporation of exhibits by reference [@CL-FPA-1166], the exhibits required in the exhibit table must be filed as indicated, as part of the registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1167].

Each registration statement or report shall contain an exhibit index, which must appear before the required signatures in the registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1168]. Each exhibit shall be listed in the exhibit index according to the number assigned to it in the exhibit table [@CL-FPA-1169]. If an exhibit is incorporated by reference, this must be noted in the exhibit index [@CL-FPA-1170].

If a material contract or plan of acquisition, reorganization, arrangement, liquidation or succession is executed or becomes effective during the reporting period reflected by a Form 10-Q or Form 10-K, it shall be filed as an exhibit to the Form 10-Q or Form 10-K filed for the corresponding period [@CL-FPA-1175].

Schedules (or similar attachments) to the exhibits required by Item 601 are not required to be filed provided that they do not contain information material to an investment or voting decision and that information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document [@CL-FPA-1172]. Each exhibit filed must contain a list briefly identifying the contents of all omitted schedules, but a separate list of omitted information need not be prepared if that information is already included within the exhibit in a manner that conveys the subject matter of the omitted schedules and attachments [@CL-FPA-1173; @CL-FPA-1226]. In addition, the registrant must provide a copy of any omitted schedule to the Commission or its staff upon request [@CL-FPA-1244]. The registrant may redact information from exhibits required to be filed by Item 601 if disclosure of such information would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (e.g., disclosure of bank account numbers, social security numbers, home addresses, and similar information) [@CL-FPA-1174].

## How it works

Item 601 applies only to the forms specified in the exhibit table, where the “X” designation indicates the documents required to be filed with each form (or incorporated by reference if previously filed with another document) and the far-left number refers to the subsection in paragraph (b) describing the exhibit [@CL-FPA-1171; @CL-FPA-1176; @CL-FPA-1177; @CL-FPA-1178]. Subject to Rule 411(c) under the Securities Act and Rule 12b-23(c) under the Exchange Act regarding incorporation of exhibits by reference, the required exhibits must be filed as indicated as part of the registration statement or report, and each registration statement or report shall contain an exhibit index appearing before the required signatures that lists each exhibit by its table number and notes any incorporation by reference [@CL-FPA-1166; @CL-FPA-1167; @CL-FPA-1168; @CL-FPA-1169; @CL-FPA-1170]. A material contract or plan of acquisition, reorganization, arrangement, liquidation or succession executed or becoming effective during a Form 10-Q or Form 10-K reporting period shall be filed as an exhibit to the corresponding Form 10-Q or Form 10-K [@CL-FPA-1175]. Schedules (or similar attachments) not containing information material to an investment or voting decision and not otherwise disclosed need not be filed, provided each exhibit contains a list briefly identifying omitted schedules unless the exhibit already conveys their subject matter, and a copy of any omitted schedule must be provided to the Commission or its staff upon request; information whose disclosure would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy may be redacted [@CL-FPA-1172; @CL-FPA-1173; @CL-FPA-1226; @CL-FPA-1244; @CL-FPA-1174].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company signs a credit agreement in the fourth quarter that is a material contract. It files the agreement as an exhibit to its annual report for that year, numbers it 10.1 in the exhibit index and notes in the index that an earlier exhibit is incorporated by reference. It leaves out a schedule of lender addresses that holds no information material to an investment or voting decision, and the agreement lists the omitted schedule by title.

## Never confuse

Under Item 601(a), the exhibit table indicates which documents must be filed as exhibits to the listed forms and refers by its far-left number to the subsection in paragraph (b) describing each exhibit, whereas schedules or similar attachments not containing information material to an investment or voting decision and not otherwise disclosed need not be filed if an identifying list is provided [@CL-FPA-1176; @CL-FPA-1177; @CL-FPA-1178; @CL-FPA-1172; @CL-FPA-1173]; for the exhibit categories in paragraph (b), see [Exhibits: the categories a finance reader uses](fpa-exhibits-categories.md). For the code of ethics and insider trading policy disclosures and exhibit provisions, see [Code of ethics and insider trading arrangements and policies](fpa-code-of-ethics-and-insider-trading-policies.md).
