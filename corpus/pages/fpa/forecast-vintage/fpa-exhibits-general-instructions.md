## Purpose

Shows how Item 601 of Regulation S-K uses the exhibit table, the exhibit index and its general instructions to decide which documents are filed with a registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1166; @CL-FPA-1167; @CL-FPA-1168; @CL-FPA-1171]. The exhibit categories a finance reader uses are on the [exhibit categories card](fpa-exhibits-categories.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | A registrant, for the exhibits required in the exhibit table of the forms the table specifies [@CL-FPA-1167; @CL-FPA-1171] |

The exhibit table indicates those documents that must be filed as exhibits to the respective forms listed [@CL-FPA-1176]. The “X” designation indicates the documents which are required to be filed with each form even if filed previously with another document, Provided, However, that such previously filed documents may be incorporated by reference to satisfy the filing requirements [@CL-FPA-1177]. The number used in the far left column of the table refers to the appropriate subsection in paragraph (b) where a description of the exhibit can be found [@CL-FPA-1178]. Item 601 applies only to the forms specified in the exhibit table [@CL-FPA-1171].

Subject to Rule 411(c) under the Securities Act and Rule 12b-23(c) under the Exchange Act regarding incorporation of exhibits by reference [@CL-FPA-1166], the exhibits required in the exhibit table must be filed as indicated, as part of the registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1167].

Each registration statement or report shall contain an exhibit index, which must appear before the required signatures in the registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1168]. Each exhibit shall be listed in the exhibit index according to the number assigned to it in the exhibit table [@CL-FPA-1169]. If an exhibit is incorporated by reference, this must be noted in the exhibit index [@CL-FPA-1170].

If a material contract or plan of acquisition, reorganization, arrangement, liquidation or succession is executed or becomes effective during the reporting period reflected by a Form 10-Q or Form 10-K, it shall be filed as an exhibit to the Form 10-Q or Form 10-K filed for the corresponding period [@CL-FPA-1175].

Schedules (or similar attachments) to the exhibits required by Item 601 are not required to be filed provided that they do not contain information material to an investment or voting decision and that information is not otherwise disclosed in the exhibit or the disclosure document [@CL-FPA-1172]. Each exhibit filed must contain a list briefly identifying the contents of all omitted schedules [@CL-FPA-1173]. The registrant may redact information from exhibits required to be filed by Item 601 if disclosure of such information would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (e.g., disclosure of bank account numbers, social security numbers, home addresses, and similar information) [@CL-FPA-1174].

The card does not restate the rules on active hyperlinks in the exhibit index, on amendments to previously filed exhibits, on preliminary exhibits and substantially identical documents, on filing copies, and on paper and confirming electronic copies. It also leaves out the form-by-form marks in the table and its footnotes.

## How it works

The reader finds the form in the exhibit table and reads across to see which exhibit numbers are marked [@CL-FPA-1176; @CL-FPA-1177]. Each number points to the description of that exhibit in paragraph (b) [@CL-FPA-1178]. The filing then carries each required exhibit, or an incorporation by reference to one filed earlier [@CL-FPA-1167; @CL-FPA-1177], and lists every exhibit in the index by its table number before the signatures [@CL-FPA-1168; @CL-FPA-1169]. A schedule that is not material and is not otherwise disclosed can be left out if the exhibit lists what was omitted [@CL-FPA-1172; @CL-FPA-1173].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company signs a credit agreement in the fourth quarter that is a material contract. It files the agreement as an exhibit to its annual report for that year, numbers it 10.1 in the exhibit index and notes in the index that an earlier exhibit is incorporated by reference. It leaves out a schedule of lender addresses that holds no information material to an investment or voting decision, and the agreement lists the omitted schedule by title.

## Never confuse

The exhibit table says which documents are filed; the disclosure those documents support sits elsewhere in the filing, for example the code of ethics and insider trading policy items on [Code of ethics and insider trading policies](fpa-code-of-ethics-and-insider-trading-policies.md). An exhibit description in paragraph (b) is not the same as the table: the table marks the forms, and the description says what the document is, as set out on the [exhibit categories card](fpa-exhibits-categories.md).
