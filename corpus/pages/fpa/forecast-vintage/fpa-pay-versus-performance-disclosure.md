## Purpose

Shows the table and descriptions that set the compensation actually paid to executives beside company performance over five years under paragraph (v) of Item 402 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-1130; @CL-FPA-1131; @CL-FPA-1132; @CL-FPA-1140].

| Applies to | A registrant, in a proxy or information statement for which the Commission's rules require executive compensation disclosure, other than an emerging growth company's [@CL-FPA-1130] |

Paragraph (v) applies to any proxy or information statement for which the rules of the Commission require executive compensation disclosure pursuant to Item 402, excluding any proxy or information statement of an emerging growth company as defined in § 230.405 or § 240.12b-2 [@CL-FPA-1130]. The registrant provides the information specified in paragraph (v)(2) for each of its last five completed fiscal years in the tabular format set out in paragraph (v)(1) [@CL-FPA-1131]. The table includes the registrant's net income for each fiscal year in column (h) [@CL-FPA-1139].

For columns (c) and (e) of the table, executive compensation actually paid must be the total compensation for the covered fiscal year for each named executive officer as provided in paragraph (c)(2)(x), or paragraph (n)(2)(x) for smaller reporting companies, adjusted as the paragraph lists [@CL-FPA-1132; @CL-FPA-1133]. One adjustment is to deduct the aggregate change in the actuarial present value of the named executive officer's accumulated benefit under all defined benefit and actuarial pension plans reported in the Summary Compensation Table in accordance with paragraph (c)(2)(viii)(A) [@CL-FPA-1134]. A further adjustment is to deduct the amounts reported in the Summary Compensation Table pursuant to paragraphs (c)(2)(v) and (vi) and then include an amount calculated as listed for all stock awards and all option awards [@CL-FPA-1135]: (i) add the fair value as of the end of the covered fiscal year of all awards granted during the covered fiscal year that are outstanding and unvested as of the end of the covered fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1136]; and (ii) add the amount equal to the change as of the end of the covered fiscal year (from the end of the prior fiscal year) in fair value (whether positive or negative) of any awards granted in any prior fiscal year that are outstanding and unvested as of the end of the covered fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1137].

For cumulative total shareholder return (column (f)) and peer group cumulative total shareholder return (column (g)), the term measurement period is the period beginning at the measurement point established by the market close on the last trading day before the registrant's earliest fiscal year in the table, through and including the end of the fiscal year for which cumulative total shareholder return is being calculated, and the closing price at the measurement point must be converted into a fixed investment of one hundred dollars, stated in dollars, in the registrant's stock (or in the stocks represented by the peer group) [@CL-FPA-1138]. The registrant uses the information in the table to provide a clear description (graphically, narratively, or a combination of the two) of the relationships listed in paragraph (v)(5) [@CL-FPA-1140]. Under paragraph (v)(5)(iv), the description provided in response to paragraph (v)(5)(i) must also include a comparison of the cumulative total shareholder return of the registrant (column (f)) and cumulative total shareholder return of the registrant's peer group (column (g)) over the same period [@CL-FPA-1141].

The registrant also provides a tabular list of at least three, and up to seven, financial performance measures, which in the registrant's assessment represent the most important financial performance measures used by the registrant to link compensation actually paid to the registrant's named executive officers, for the most recently completed fiscal year, to company performance [@CL-FPA-1142; @CL-FPA-1143]. If the registrant used fewer than three financial performance measures for its most recently completed fiscal year to link compensation actually paid to its named executive officers to company performance, the Tabular List must include all such measures that were used, if any [@CL-FPA-1144].

The disclosure provided pursuant to paragraph (v), including, but not limited to, any disclosure provided pursuant to paragraphs (v)(3) and (6), must appear with, and in the same format as, the rest of the disclosure required to be provided pursuant to Item 402 and, in addition, must be provided in an Interactive Data File in accordance with § 232.405 and the EDGAR Filer Manual [@CL-FPA-1145; @CL-FPA-1146]. A smaller reporting company may provide the information required by paragraph (v) for three years, instead of five years, and may provide the disclosure required by paragraph (v) for only two fiscal years in the first filing in which it provides this disclosure, and is not required to provide the disclosure required by paragraph (v)(2)(iv) or (v)(5) with respect to the total shareholder return of any peer group, the Company-Selected Measure disclosure required by paragraph (v)(2)(vi), or the Tabular List provided pursuant to paragraph (v)(6) [@CL-FPA-1147; @CL-FPA-1223; @CL-FPA-1224; @CL-FPA-1225]. The information required by paragraph (v) will not be deemed to be incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act or the Exchange Act, except to the extent that the registrant specifically incorporates it by reference [@CL-FPA-1148].

## How it works

The registrant starts from the total compensation for the covered fiscal year in paragraph (c)(2)(x) (or paragraph (n)(2)(x) for smaller reporting companies) [@CL-FPA-1132; @CL-FPA-1133], applies the pension and equity award adjustments to calculate executive compensation actually paid [@CL-FPA-1134; @CL-FPA-1135; @CL-FPA-1136; @CL-FPA-1137], presents the five-year table (or three years, or two years in the first filing, for a smaller reporting company) with net income and cumulative total shareholder return converted into a fixed investment of one hundred dollars [@CL-FPA-1131; @CL-FPA-1139; @CL-FPA-1138; @CL-FPA-1147; @CL-FPA-1223], describes the paragraph (v)(5) relationships and the registrant-versus-peer-group cumulative total shareholder return comparison [@CL-FPA-1140; @CL-FPA-1141; @CL-FPA-1224], and provides the Tabular List of at least three and up to seven (or all used, if fewer than three) financial performance measures for the most recently completed fiscal year (unless exempted as a smaller reporting company) in the same format as the rest of Item 402 and in an Interactive Data File [@CL-FPA-1142; @CL-FPA-1143; @CL-FPA-1144; @CL-FPA-1145; @CL-FPA-1146; @CL-FPA-1225; @CL-FPA-1148].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company shows five years. In the latest year the Summary Compensation Table total for its PEO is 5,000,000 dollars, of which 3,000,000 dollars is the grant date fair value of stock awards. The share price has fallen, and the year-end fair value of those unvested awards is 1,000,000 dollars. Ignoring pension items, compensation actually paid is 5,000,000 minus 3,000,000 plus 1,000,000, which is 3,000,000 dollars.

## Never confuse

Under Item 402(v)(2)(iii), executive compensation actually paid starts from the total compensation reported in the Summary Compensation Table under paragraph (c)(2)(x) (or paragraph (n)(2)(x) for smaller reporting companies) and adjusts it for defined benefit and actuarial pension plan benefit changes and stock and option award fair values [@CL-FPA-1132; @CL-FPA-1133; @CL-FPA-1134; @CL-FPA-1135; @CL-FPA-1136; @CL-FPA-1137]; for the Summary Compensation Table itself, see [Executive compensation scope, discussion and summary compensation table](fpa-executive-compensation-scope-and-summary-compensation-table.md). Item 402(v) requires the five-year pay versus performance table and relationship descriptions in proxy or information statements requiring Item 402 disclosure [@CL-FPA-1130; @CL-FPA-1131; @CL-FPA-1140]; for the pay ratio disclosure, see [Pay ratio disclosure](fpa-pay-ratio-disclosure.md), and for the performance graph and equity compensation plan table, see [Market price, dividends and equity compensation plans](fpa-market-price-dividends-and-equity-compensation-plans.md).
