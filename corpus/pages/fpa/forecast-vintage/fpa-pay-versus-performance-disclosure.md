## Purpose

Shows the table and descriptions that set the compensation actually paid to executives beside company performance over five years under paragraph (v) of Item 402 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-1130; @CL-FPA-1131; @CL-FPA-1132; @CL-FPA-1140].

| Applies to | A registrant, in a proxy or information statement for which the Commission's rules require executive compensation disclosure, other than an emerging growth company's [@CL-FPA-1130] |

Paragraph (v) applies to any proxy or information statement for which the rules of the Commission require executive compensation disclosure under Item 402, excluding any proxy or information statement of an emerging growth company [@CL-FPA-1130]. The registrant provides the information for each of its last five completed fiscal years [@CL-FPA-1131]. The table includes the registrant's net income for each fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1139].

For columns (c) and (e) of the table, executive compensation actually paid must be the total compensation for the covered fiscal year for each named executive officer as provided in paragraph (c)(2)(x), adjusted as the paragraph then lists [@CL-FPA-1132; @CL-FPA-1133]. For smaller reporting companies the starting amount is instead the total compensation provided in paragraph (n)(2)(x) [@CL-FPA-1133]. One adjustment is to deduct the aggregate change in the actuarial present value of the officer's accumulated benefit under all defined benefit and actuarial pension plans reported in the Summary Compensation Table [@CL-FPA-1134]. A further adjustment is to deduct the amounts reported in the Summary Compensation Table for stock awards and option awards and then include an amount calculated for those awards [@CL-FPA-1135]. The first listed step adds the year-end fair value of awards granted in the year that are outstanding and unvested [@CL-FPA-1136]. The second adds the change in fair value of unvested awards granted in a prior year, whether positive or negative [@CL-FPA-1137].

The shareholder return measure is based on a fixed investment of one hundred dollars in the registrant's stock, or in the stocks represented by the peer group, converted from the closing price at the measurement point [@CL-FPA-1138]. The registrant uses the table to describe, graphically, narratively or both, the relationships listed in paragraph (v)(5) [@CL-FPA-1140]. The description of the relationship with total shareholder return must also include a comparison of the registrant's cumulative total shareholder return and that of its peer group over the same period [@CL-FPA-1141].

The registrant also provides a tabular list of at least three, and up to seven, financial performance measures that in its assessment are the most important measures used to link compensation actually paid to its named executive officers [@CL-FPA-1142]. The list is for the most recently completed fiscal year and links compensation actually paid to company performance [@CL-FPA-1143]. If fewer than three such measures were used, the list must include all that were used, if any [@CL-FPA-1144].

The disclosure must appear with, and in the same format as, the rest of the Item 402 disclosure [@CL-FPA-1145], and must in addition be provided in an Interactive Data File [@CL-FPA-1146]. A smaller reporting company may provide the information for three years, instead of five, and in the first filing in which it provides the disclosure it may provide it for only two fiscal years; it is also not required to provide the peer group total shareholder return disclosure, the Company-Selected Measure disclosure or the Tabular List [@CL-FPA-1147; @CL-FPA-1223; @CL-FPA-1224; @CL-FPA-1225]. The information is not deemed incorporated by reference into a Securities Act or Exchange Act filing, except to the extent the registrant specifically incorporates it [@CL-FPA-1148].

The printed paragraph also gives the pension service cost and prior service cost add-backs, the remaining equity award steps, the fair value method and its footnote rules, the full total shareholder return method and peer group rules, the Company-Selected Measure, the footnotes naming the officers, the relationships with net income and the Company-Selected Measure, the use of non-financial measures, the further relief for smaller reporting companies and the transition relief, which these claims do not restate.

## How it works

The registrant starts from the Summary Compensation Table totals [@CL-FPA-1132], adjusts them to compensation actually paid [@CL-FPA-1134; @CL-FPA-1135], sets them beside net income and shareholder return for five years [@CL-FPA-1131; @CL-FPA-1139; @CL-FPA-1138], describes how pay and performance relate [@CL-FPA-1140; @CL-FPA-1141] and lists its most important measures [@CL-FPA-1142].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company shows five years. In the latest year the Summary Compensation Table total for its PEO is 5,000,000 dollars, of which 3,000,000 dollars is the grant date fair value of stock awards. The share price has fallen, and the year-end fair value of those unvested awards is 1,000,000 dollars. Ignoring pension items, compensation actually paid is 5,000,000 minus 3,000,000 plus 1,000,000, which is 3,000,000 dollars.

## Never confuse

Compensation actually paid is not the Summary Compensation Table total; the table total is explained in [Executive compensation scope, discussion and summary compensation table](fpa-executive-compensation-scope-and-summary-compensation-table.md). This disclosure sets pay beside company results and is not the pay ratio, which is in [Pay ratio disclosure](fpa-pay-ratio-disclosure.md). The performance graph and the plans under which equity can be issued are in [Market price, dividends and equity compensation plans](fpa-market-price-dividends-and-equity-compensation-plans.md).
