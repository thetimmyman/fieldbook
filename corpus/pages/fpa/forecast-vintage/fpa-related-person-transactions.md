## Purpose

Shows which transactions with related persons a registrant describes under Item 404 of Regulation S-K, who counts as a related person and what it says about its review policies [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0148; @CL-FPA-0150].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for transactions in which it is a participant and a related person has a direct or indirect material interest [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0147] |

Item 404(a) calls for a description of a transaction since the beginning of the registrant's last fiscal year, or any currently proposed transaction, in which the registrant was or is to be a participant and the amount involved exceeds $120,000 [@CL-FPA-0146]. It also requires that any related person had or will have a direct or indirect material interest in the transaction [@CL-FPA-0147].

Item 404(d) says a registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company, as defined in the cited section, must provide the information the paragraph lists in order to comply with Item 404 [@CL-FPA-0177]. Item 404(d)(1) lists the information required by paragraph (a) for a transaction in which the amount involved exceeds the lesser of $120,000 or one percent of the average of the smaller reporting company's total assets at year end for the last two completed fiscal years [@CL-FPA-0178]. Instruction 2 to Item 404(d) adds, for smaller reporting companies, the fiscal year preceding the smaller reporting company's last fiscal year to the period specified in paragraph (a) [@CL-FPA-0257]. Item 404(d) also lists the information required by paragraph (c) and a list of all parents showing the basis of control [@CL-FPA-0258]. The paragraph (d) list does not include the paragraph (b) disclosures about review policies [@CL-FPA-0177; @CL-FPA-0178; @CL-FPA-0258]. The printed instructions also give several exclusions, which these claims do not restate.

One listed category of related person in the instructions is any director or executive officer of the registrant who was in the category at any time during the specified period [@CL-FPA-0148]. Another is a security holder covered by Item 403(a), when a transaction in which the holder had a direct or indirect material interest occurred or existed [@CL-FPA-0149]. The printed list has further categories, including nominees and immediate family members, which these claims do not restate.

Item 404(b)(1) requires the registrant to describe its policies and procedures for the review, approval, or ratification of any transaction required to be reported under paragraph (a) [@CL-FPA-0150]. Item 404(b)(2) requires it to identify any such transaction since the beginning of its last fiscal year where those policies and procedures did not require review, approval or ratification or were not followed [@CL-FPA-0151].

## How it works

The registrant tests each transaction against the amount involved and its own participation, using the smaller reporting company threshold, and the longer disclosure period that comes with it, if it qualifies [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0178; @CL-FPA-0257]. It then asks whether a related person had or will have a direct or indirect material interest [@CL-FPA-0147] and whether that person falls in a listed category [@CL-FPA-0148; @CL-FPA-0149]. A registrant that is not a smaller reporting company then describes its review policies and identifies any transaction required to be reported under paragraph (a) where those policies did not require review, approval or ratification or were not followed [@CL-FPA-0150; @CL-FPA-0151]. A smaller reporting company follows the paragraph (d) list instead, which gives paragraph (a) information, paragraph (c) information and a parent list, and does not list the paragraph (b) policy disclosures [@CL-FPA-0177; @CL-FPA-0178; @CL-FPA-0258].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant that is not a smaller reporting company sells goods to a firm owned by a director for 150,000 in its last fiscal year. The amount is above the 120,000 threshold and the director has a material interest, so the transaction is described. A second sale of 100,000 to a different director's firm is below the threshold. The registrant also says whether its written policy required review of the first sale.

## Never confuse

The amount involved test is about the transaction, while the material interest test is about the related person [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0147]. Describing the transaction under paragraph (a) is also different from describing the review policies under paragraph (b) [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0150]. A related person transaction is not a pending legal proceeding, which Item 103 covers instead [@CL-FPA-0107]; see [Legal proceedings disclosure in Regulation S-K](fpa-legal-proceedings-disclosure.md).
