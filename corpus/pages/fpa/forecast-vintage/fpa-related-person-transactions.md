## Purpose

Shows which transactions with related persons a registrant describes under Item 404 of Regulation S-K, who counts as a related person and what it says about its review policies [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0148; @CL-FPA-0150].

Item 404(a) calls for a description of any transaction, since the beginning of the registrant's last fiscal year, or any currently proposed transaction, in which the registrant was or is to be a participant and the amount involved exceeds $120,000, and in which any related person had or will have a direct or indirect material interest [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0147].

Item 404(d) says a registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company, as defined by § 229.10(f)(1), must provide the information the paragraph lists in order to comply with Item 404 [@CL-FPA-0177]. Item 404(d)(1) lists the information required by paragraph (a) for the period specified there for a transaction in which the amount involved exceeds the lesser of $120,000 or one percent of the average of the smaller reporting company's total assets at year end for the last two completed fiscal years [@CL-FPA-0178]. Instruction 2 to Item 404(d) requires a smaller reporting company to provide the information for the period specified in paragraph (a) of Item 404 and, in addition, for the fiscal year preceding the smaller reporting company's last fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0257]. Item 404(d) also requires the information required by paragraph (c) of Item 404 and a list of all parents of the smaller reporting company showing the basis of control and as to each parent the percentage of voting securities owned or other basis of control by its immediate parent, if any [@CL-FPA-0258].

One listed category of related person in Instruction 1 to Item 404(a) is any director or executive officer of the registrant who was in the category at any time during the specified period for which disclosure under paragraph (a) is required [@CL-FPA-0148]. Another is a security holder covered by Item 403(a) when a transaction in which such person had a direct or indirect material interest occurred or existed [@CL-FPA-0149].

Item 404(b)(1) requires the registrant to describe its policies and procedures for the review, approval, or ratification of any transaction required to be reported under paragraph (a) [@CL-FPA-0150]. Item 404(b)(2) requires it to identify any transaction required to be reported under paragraph (a) since the beginning of the registrant's last fiscal year where such policies and procedures did not require review, approval or ratification or where such policies and procedures were not followed [@CL-FPA-0151].

## How it works

Under Item 404(a), the registrant describes any transaction since the beginning of its last fiscal year, or any currently proposed transaction, in which the registrant was or is to be a participant, the amount involved exceeds $120,000, and any related person (such as a director or executive officer during the specified period, or a security holder covered by Item 403(a) when the transaction occurred or existed) had or will have a direct or indirect material interest [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0147; @CL-FPA-0148; @CL-FPA-0149]. Under Item 404(b), the registrant describes its policies and procedures for the review, approval, or ratification of any transaction required to be reported under paragraph (a) and identifies any such transaction since the beginning of its last fiscal year where such policies and procedures did not require review, approval or ratification or were not followed [@CL-FPA-0150; @CL-FPA-0151]. A registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company under § 229.10(f)(1) complies with Item 404 by providing the information in paragraph (d): the paragraph (a) information where the amount involved exceeds the lesser of $120,000 or one percent of average year-end total assets for the last two completed fiscal years (covering the paragraph (a) period plus the preceding fiscal year), the paragraph (c) information, and the list of all parents showing the basis of control and immediate-parent ownership percentage [@CL-FPA-0177; @CL-FPA-0178; @CL-FPA-0257; @CL-FPA-0258].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant that is not a smaller reporting company sells goods to a firm owned by a director for 150,000 in its last fiscal year. The amount is above the 120,000 threshold and the director has a material interest, so the transaction is described. A second sale of 100,000 to a different director's firm is below the threshold. The registrant also says whether its written policy required review of the first sale.

## Never confuse

In Item 404(a), the $120,000 threshold applies to the amount involved in a transaction in which the registrant was or is to be a participant, while the material interest requirement applies to a related person's direct or indirect material interest in the transaction [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0147]. Item 404(a) requires a description of the transaction [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0147], while Item 404(b)(1) requires a description of the registrant's policies and procedures for the review, approval, or ratification of any transaction required to be reported under paragraph (a) [@CL-FPA-0150]. Material pending legal proceedings to which the registrant or any of its subsidiaries is a party, or of which any of their property is the subject, are covered by Item 103 [@CL-FPA-0107]; see [Legal proceedings disclosure in Regulation S-K](fpa-legal-proceedings-disclosure.md).
