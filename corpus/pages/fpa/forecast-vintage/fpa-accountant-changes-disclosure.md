## Purpose

Shows when a registrant discloses a change of independent accountant, what counts as a disagreement and how the former accountant's letter works under Item 304 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0123; @CL-FPA-0125; @CL-FPA-0129].

| Applies to | A registrant whose principal accountant, or an independent accountant previously engaged to audit a significant subsidiary on whom the principal accountant expressed reliance in its report, resigned, indicated it declined to stand for re-election after completion of the current audit, or was dismissed during the registrant's two most recent fiscal years or any subsequent interim period [@CL-FPA-0124; @CL-FPA-0168; @CL-FPA-0123] |

Item 304(a)(1) looks at the registrant's two most recent fiscal years or any subsequent interim period and covers an independent accountant previously engaged as the principal accountant to audit the registrant's financial statements [@CL-FPA-0124]. It also covers an independent accountant previously engaged to audit a significant subsidiary and on whom the principal accountant expressed reliance in its report, who has resigned (or indicated it has declined to stand for re-election after the completion of the current audit) or was dismissed [@CL-FPA-0168]. When an accountant of the kind the paragraph covers has resigned, indicated it has declined to stand for re-election after the completion of the current audit, or was dismissed, the registrant shall state which occurred and the date [@CL-FPA-0123].

Instruction 4 says disagreements is interpreted broadly to include any difference of opinion concerning accounting principles or practices, financial statement disclosure, or auditing scope or procedure [@CL-FPA-0125]. The instruction limits a disagreement to a difference that, if not resolved to the former accountant's satisfaction, would have caused it to make reference to the subject matter in connection with its report, and an argument is not necessary [@CL-FPA-0126]. The disagreements contemplated are those at the decision-making level, between registrant personnel responsible for presenting its financial statements and accounting firm personnel responsible for rendering its report [@CL-FPA-0127]. The disagreements reported include both those resolved and those not resolved to the former accountant's satisfaction [@CL-FPA-0128]. The instruction also says the term disagreements does not include initial differences of opinion based on incomplete facts or preliminary information that were later resolved to the former accountant's satisfaction [@CL-FPA-0169]. That exclusion rests on resolution by obtaining additional relevant facts or information, providing the registrant and the accountant do not continue to have a difference of opinion upon obtaining them [@CL-FPA-0170].

The registrant shall request the former accountant to furnish a letter addressed to the Commission stating whether it agrees with the registrant's statements and, if not, the respects in which it does not agree [@CL-FPA-0129]. If the letter is unavailable at filing, the registrant shall request it as promptly as possible so that it can be filed within ten business days [@CL-FPA-0130]. Those ten business days run after the filing of the report or registration statement, and notwithstanding that period the registrant shall file the letter by amendment within two business days of receipt, and if the letter is received on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday on which the Commission is not open for business, the two business day period begins to run on and includes the first business day thereafter [@CL-FPA-0171; @CL-FPA-1220].

## How it works

The registrant checks the two most recent fiscal years or any subsequent interim period and the accountant's role, including an independent accountant previously engaged to audit a significant subsidiary on whom the principal accountant expressed reliance in its report [@CL-FPA-0124; @CL-FPA-0168]. If the accountant resigned, indicated it declined to stand for re-election after the completion of the current audit, or was dismissed, the registrant states which occurred and the date [@CL-FPA-0123]. It then tests each past matter against the broad meaning of disagreements, the report-reference condition, the decision-making level, and the exclusion for initial differences based on incomplete facts or preliminary information resolved to the former accountant's satisfaction by obtaining additional relevant facts or information [@CL-FPA-0125; @CL-FPA-0126; @CL-FPA-0127; @CL-FPA-0169; @CL-FPA-0170]. It requests the former accountant to furnish a letter addressed to the Commission and files the letter within the stated business-day periods [@CL-FPA-0129; @CL-FPA-0130; @CL-FPA-0171; @CL-FPA-1220].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant dismisses its auditor. Before the dismissal, the finance lead and the firm's engagement partner differed on how to account for a transaction, and the firm said it would have referred to the matter in its report. The difference was not resolved. It was at the decision-making level, so it is reported as a disagreement, and the former auditor is asked for a letter.

## Never confuse

Under Instruction 4, it is not necessary for a disagreement that there have been an argument [@CL-FPA-0126]; any difference of opinion concerning accounting principles or practices, financial statement disclosure, or auditing scope or procedure is included if, when not resolved to the former accountant's satisfaction, it would have caused the former accountant to make reference to the subject matter of the disagreement in connection with its report [@CL-FPA-0125; @CL-FPA-0126]. An initial difference of opinion based on incomplete facts or preliminary information is excluded where it was later resolved to the former accountant's satisfaction by obtaining additional relevant facts or information, providing the registrant and the accountant do not continue to have a difference of opinion upon obtaining them [@CL-FPA-0169; @CL-FPA-0170]. Under Item 304(a)(1), a resignation, indication of declining to stand for re-election after completion of the current audit, or dismissal requires the registrant to state which occurred and the date [@CL-FPA-0123], while Instruction 4 defines what counts as a disagreement [@CL-FPA-0125; @CL-FPA-0126]. Under Item 304(a)(3), the registrant requests the former accountant to furnish a letter addressed to the Commission stating whether it agrees with the statements made by the registrant in response to Item 304(a) [@CL-FPA-0129], whereas Item 308(b) requires the registered public accounting firm's attestation report on internal control over financial reporting in the annual report of a registrant, other than an emerging growth company, that is an accelerated filer or a large accelerated filer, and states that a management's report would not be sufficient [@CL-FPA-0142; @CL-FPA-0143; @CL-FPA-0267]; see [Internal control over financial reporting disclosure](fpa-icfr-disclosure.md).
