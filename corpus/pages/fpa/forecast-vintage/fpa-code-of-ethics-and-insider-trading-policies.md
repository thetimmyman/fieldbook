## Purpose

Shows what a registrant discloses about its code of ethics under Item 406 of Regulation S-K and about insider trading arrangements and policies under Item 408 [@CL-FPA-0199; @CL-FPA-0203; @CL-FPA-0205].

| Applies to | A registrant, for its code of ethics covering the named officers and for the trading arrangements of its directors and officers and its insider trading policies [@CL-FPA-0199; @CL-FPA-0203; @CL-FPA-0205] |

Item 406(a) requires disclosure of whether the registrant has adopted a code of ethics that applies to its principal executive officer, principal financial officer, principal accounting officer or controller, or persons performing similar functions [@CL-FPA-0199]. If the registrant has not adopted the code of ethics that the paragraph describes, it must explain why it has not done so [@CL-FPA-0239]. Item 406(b) defines a code of ethics as written standards reasonably designed to deter wrongdoing and to promote: (1) honest and ethical conduct, including the ethical handling of actual or apparent conflicts of interest between personal and professional relationships [@CL-FPA-0200], (2) full, fair, accurate, timely, and understandable disclosure in reports and documents that a registrant files with, or submits to, the Commission and in other public communications made by the registrant, (3) compliance with applicable governmental laws, rules and regulations, (4) the prompt internal reporting of violations of the code to an appropriate person or persons identified in the code, and (5) accountability for adherence to the code [@CL-FPA-0200; @CL-FPA-0046; @CL-FPA-0047]. Item 406(c) says the registrant must do one of three alternatives joined by "or": (1) file with the Commission, as an exhibit to its annual report, a copy of its code of ethics that applies to the registrant's principal executive officer, principal financial officer, principal accounting officer or controller, or persons performing similar functions [@CL-FPA-0201], (2) post the text of such code of ethics on its Internet website and disclose, in its annual report, its Internet address and the fact that it has posted such code of ethics on its Internet Web site [@CL-FPA-0202], or (3) undertake in its annual report to provide to any person without charge, upon request, a copy of such code of ethics and explain the manner in which such request may be made [@CL-FPA-0202; @CL-FPA-0240].

Item 408(a)(1) requires disclosure of whether, during the registrant's last fiscal quarter (the registrant's fourth fiscal quarter in the case of an annual report), any director or officer (as defined in 17 CFR 240.16a-1(f)) adopted or terminated: (i) any contract, instruction or written plan for the purchase or sale of securities of the registrant intended to satisfy the affirmative defense conditions of Rule 10b5-1(c) (a Rule 10b5-1 trading arrangement) [@CL-FPA-0203; @CL-FPA-0204], and/or (ii) any non-Rule 10b5-1 trading arrangement as defined in paragraph (c) of Item 408 [@CL-FPA-0203; @CL-FPA-0204; @CL-FPA-0048]. Item 408(a)(2) requires identifying whether the trading arrangement is intended to satisfy the affirmative defense of Rule 10b5-1(c) [@CL-FPA-1230] and providing a description of the material terms, other than terms with respect to the price at which the individual executing the Rule 10b5-1 trading arrangement or non-Rule 10b5-1 trading arrangement is authorized to trade, such as: (A) the name and title of the director or officer; (B) the date on which the director or officer adopted or terminated the trading arrangement; (C) the duration of the trading arrangement; and (D) the aggregate number of securities to be purchased or sold pursuant to the trading arrangement [@CL-FPA-0241; @CL-FPA-0049]. Item 408(b)(1) requires disclosure of whether the registrant has adopted insider trading policies and procedures governing the purchase, sale, and/or other dispositions of its securities by directors, officers and employees, or the registrant itself [@CL-FPA-0205]. It describes them as policies and procedures reasonably designed to promote compliance with insider trading laws, rules and regulations and any applicable listing standards, and requires a registrant that has not adopted such policies and procedures to explain why it has not done so [@CL-FPA-0242]. Item 408(b)(2) requires a registrant that has adopted such insider trading policies and procedures to file them as an exhibit, and if all of the registrant's insider trading policies and procedures are included in its code of ethics and the code of ethics is filed as an exhibit pursuant to Item 406(c)(1), that would satisfy the Item 408(b)(2) exhibit requirement [@CL-FPA-0243; @CL-FPA-1222]. Under Item 408(c), a director or officer (each a covered person) has entered into a non-Rule 10b5-1 trading arrangement where both conditions (1) and (2) apply [@CL-FPA-0206; @CL-FPA-1231]: (1) the covered person asserts that at a time when they were not aware of material nonpublic information about the security or the issuer of the security they had adopted a written arrangement for trading the securities [@CL-FPA-0206; @CL-FPA-1231]; and (2) the trading arrangement (i) specified the amount of securities to be purchased or sold and the price at which and the date on which the securities were to be purchased or sold, (ii) included a written formula or algorithm, or computer program, for determining the amount of securities to be purchased or sold and the price at which and the date on which the securities were to be purchased or sold, or (iii) did not permit the covered person to exercise any subsequent influence over how, when, or whether to effect purchases or sales, provided, in addition, that any other person who, pursuant to the trading arrangement, did exercise such influence must not have been aware of material nonpublic information when doing so [@CL-FPA-1231; @CL-FPA-0315; @CL-FPA-0316; @CL-FPA-0317].

## How it works

The registrant discloses whether it has adopted a code of ethics for the named officers, or explains why it has not done so [@CL-FPA-0199; @CL-FPA-0239], and fulfills one of the three filing, website-posting, or free-copy undertaking alternatives in Item 406(c) [@CL-FPA-0201; @CL-FPA-0202; @CL-FPA-0240]. Under Item 408, it discloses whether any director or officer adopted or terminated a Rule 10b5-1 or non-Rule 10b5-1 trading arrangement during the last fiscal quarter, identifies whether the arrangement is intended to satisfy Rule 10b5-1(c), and describes its material terms other than price terms [@CL-FPA-0203; @CL-FPA-0204; @CL-FPA-0048; @CL-FPA-1230; @CL-FPA-0241; @CL-FPA-0049], and discloses whether it has adopted insider trading policies and procedures (or explains why not) and files them as an exhibit unless satisfied through an Item 406(c)(1) code-of-ethics exhibit [@CL-FPA-0205; @CL-FPA-0242; @CL-FPA-0243; @CL-FPA-1222].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company's code applies to its chief executive, chief financial officer and controller. It posts the code on its website and gives the address in its annual report. In the last fiscal quarter a director adopted a written plan to sell 10,000 shares that was meant to satisfy Rule 10b5-1. The company discloses that adoption and says it has written insider trading policies.

## Never confuse

Item 406(a) and (b) address a registrant's code of ethics for its principal executive officer, principal financial officer, principal accounting officer or controller, or persons performing similar functions [@CL-FPA-0199; @CL-FPA-0200; @CL-FPA-0046; @CL-FPA-0047] (for the contract clause on a contractor code of business ethics, see [Contractor code of business ethics](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-contractor-code-of-business-ethics.md)), unlike Item 408(b)(1), which addresses insider trading policies and procedures governing the purchase, sale, and/or other dispositions of the registrant's securities by directors, officers and employees, or the registrant itself, reasonably designed to promote compliance with insider trading laws, rules and regulations and applicable listing standards [@CL-FPA-0205; @CL-FPA-0242]. Who the directors and officers are is covered in [Directors, executive officers, promoters and control persons](fpa-directors-officers-and-control-persons.md), and board committees in [Corporate governance disclosure](fpa-corporate-governance-disclosure.md). Which documents are filed as exhibits, and how the exhibit table and index work, is on [Exhibits: the exhibit table, the index and the general instructions](fpa-exhibits-general-instructions.md).

## Company calibration

How the company documents its code of ethics, its website posting, its trading arrangement reports and its insider trading policy. [@Q-017]
