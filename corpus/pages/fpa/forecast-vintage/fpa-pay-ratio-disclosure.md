## Purpose

Shows what the pay ratio compares and who counts as an employee for it under paragraph (u) of Item 402 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-1113; @CL-FPA-1117; @CL-FPA-1118].

The registrant discloses (A) the median of the annual total compensation of all employees of the registrant (except the PEO), (B) the annual total compensation of the PEO of the registrant, and (C) the ratio of the amount in (A) to the amount in (B) [@CL-FPA-1113]. For purposes of the ratio, the amount in (A) must be expressed as a ratio in which that amount equals one, or narratively in terms of the multiple that the amount in (B) bears to the amount in (A) [@CL-FPA-1114]. Total compensation for both the median of the annual total compensation of all employees of the registrant and the PEO of the registrant must be determined in accordance with paragraph (c)(2)(x) of Item 402 [@CL-FPA-1115]. For purposes of paragraph (u), registrant means the registrant and its consolidated subsidiaries [@CL-FPA-1116].

Employee or employee of the registrant means an individual employed by the registrant or any of its consolidated subsidiaries, whether as a full-time, part-time, seasonal, or temporary worker, as of a date chosen by the registrant within the last three months of the registrant's last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1117; @CL-FPA-1118], and the registrant must disclose the date used to identify the median employee [@CL-FPA-1123]. That definition does not include those workers who are employed, and whose compensation is determined, by an unaffiliated third party but who provide services to the registrant or its consolidated subsidiaries as independent contractors or “leased” workers [@CL-FPA-1119]. Under the de minimis exemption, if a registrant's non-United States employees account for 5% or less of the registrant's total employees, the registrant may exclude all non-United States employees in identifying the median employee, and if it chooses to exclude any non-United States employees under this exemption, it must exclude all non-United States employees [@CL-FPA-1120].

A registrant is required to identify its median employee only once every three years and calculate total compensation for that employee each year, provided that, during a registrant's last completed fiscal year, there has been no change in its employee population or employee compensation arrangements that it reasonably believes would result in a significant change to its pay ratio disclosure [@CL-FPA-1121; @CL-FPA-1122]. Registrants may use reasonable estimates both in the methodology used to identify the median employee and in calculating the annual total compensation or any elements of total compensation for employees other than the PEO [@CL-FPA-1125], and must briefly describe the methodology used to identify the median employee [@CL-FPA-1126]. A registrant may annualize the total compensation for all permanent employees (full-time or part-time) that were employed by the registrant for less than the full fiscal year, or who were on an unpaid leave of absence during the period [@CL-FPA-1127], and may not annualize the total compensation for employees in temporary or seasonal positions or make a full-time equivalent adjustment for any employee [@CL-FPA-1128].

Pay ratio information for the last completed fiscal year is not required to be disclosed until the filing of the annual report on Form 10-K for that last completed fiscal year or, if later, the filing of a definitive proxy or information statement relating to its next annual meeting of shareholders (or written consents in lieu of such a meeting) following the end of such fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1245; @CL-FPA-1246]. That required pay ratio information must, however, in any event be filed as provided in General Instruction G(3) of Form 10-K not later than 120 days after the end of such fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1124].

## How it works

The registrant chooses a date within the last three months of its last completed fiscal year to determine its employee population across the registrant and its consolidated subsidiaries (excluding independent contractors or leased workers employed and paid by an unaffiliated third party, and applying the 5% non-United States de minimis exemption on an all-or-nothing basis if elected) and discloses that date [@CL-FPA-1116; @CL-FPA-1117; @CL-FPA-1118; @CL-FPA-1119; @CL-FPA-1120; @CL-FPA-1123]. It identifies its median employee (required only once every three years if no change occurred in the last completed fiscal year that it reasonably believes would significantly change the pay ratio disclosure), using reasonable estimates where elected and briefly describing the methodology [@CL-FPA-1121; @CL-FPA-1122; @CL-FPA-1125; @CL-FPA-1126], determines annual total compensation for the median employee and the PEO in accordance with paragraph (c)(2)(x) (annualizing permanent employees employed less than the full year if elected, without annualizing temporary or seasonal positions or making full-time equivalent adjustments) [@CL-FPA-1115; @CL-FPA-1127; @CL-FPA-1128], and discloses the two amounts and their ratio within the timing rules of Instruction 6 [@CL-FPA-1113; @CL-FPA-1114; @CL-FPA-1245; @CL-FPA-1246; @CL-FPA-1124].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company with a December year end picks a November date to count employees. Its median employee has annual total compensation of 60,000 dollars and its PEO has 6,000,000 dollars, so the ratio is 100 to 1, or the PEO is paid 100 times the median. A permanent employee hired in July has pay annualized to a full year. A seasonal worker's pay is not annualized.

## Never confuse

Under Item 402(u), the pay ratio discloses the median of the annual total compensation of all employees of the registrant (except the PEO), the annual total compensation of the PEO, and the ratio of the first amount to the second, with total compensation for both determined in accordance with paragraph (c)(2)(x) [@CL-FPA-1113; @CL-FPA-1114; @CL-FPA-1115]. For the pay versus performance disclosure, see [Pay versus performance disclosure](fpa-pay-versus-performance-disclosure.md); for total compensation in the Summary Compensation Table, see [Executive compensation scope, discussion and summary compensation table](fpa-executive-compensation-scope-and-summary-compensation-table.md); and for disclosure of directors and executive officers, see [Directors, executive officers, promoters and control persons](fpa-directors-officers-and-control-persons.md).
