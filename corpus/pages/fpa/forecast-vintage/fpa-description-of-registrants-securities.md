## Purpose

Shows what a registrant describes about capital stock, debt securities and warrants or rights that are to be registered, under Item 202 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0220; @CL-FPA-0222; @CL-FPA-0225].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant registering capital stock, debt securities or securities offered under warrants or rights [@CL-FPA-0220; @CL-FPA-0222; @CL-FPA-0225] |

Item 202(a) requires the registrant, if capital stock is to be registered, to state the title of the class and describe such of the matters listed in paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(5) as are relevant, and states that a complete legal description of the securities need not be given [@CL-FPA-0220]. Item 202(a)(1) lists matters to outline briefly, including: (i) dividend rights, (ii) terms of conversion, (iii) sinking fund provisions, (iv) redemption provisions, and (v) voting rights, including any provisions specifying the vote required by security holders to take action [@CL-FPA-0221].

If debt securities are to be registered, Item 202(b) requires the registrant to state the title of such securities, the principal amount being offered, and, if a series, the total amount authorized and the total amount outstanding as of the most recent practicable date [@CL-FPA-0222]. For debt securities, Item 202(b) says to describe such of the matters listed in paragraphs (b)(1) through (b)(10) as are relevant, and that a complete legal description of the securities need not be given [@CL-FPA-0252]. Item 202(b) lists debt matters to outline briefly, including: (1) provisions with respect to maturity, interest, conversion, redemption, amortization, sinking fund, or retirement [@CL-FPA-0223], and (2) provisions with respect to the kind and priority of any lien securing the securities, together with a brief identification of the principal properties subject to such lien [@CL-FPA-0223; @CL-FPA-0348]. Item 202(b)(6) lists the general type of event that constitutes a default and whether or not any periodic evidence is required to be furnished as to the absence of default or as to compliance with the terms of the indenture [@CL-FPA-0224]. If the securities described are to be offered pursuant to warrants or rights, Item 202(c) requires the registrant to state: (1) the amount of securities called for by such warrants or rights [@CL-FPA-0225], (2) the period during which and the price at which the warrants or rights are exercisable [@CL-FPA-0225], (3) the amount of warrants or rights outstanding [@CL-FPA-0225], (4) provisions for changes to or adjustments in the exercise price [@CL-FPA-0253], and (5) any other material terms of such rights on warrants [@CL-FPA-0225; @CL-FPA-0253].

## How it works

For capital stock to be registered, the registrant states the title of the class and describes such of the matters listed in paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(5) as are relevant, without needing to give a complete legal description [@CL-FPA-0220; @CL-FPA-0221]. For debt securities to be registered, it states the title, the principal amount being offered (and, if a series, the total amount authorized and total amount outstanding as of the most recent practicable date) and describes such of the matters in paragraphs (b)(1) through (b)(10) as are relevant [@CL-FPA-0222; @CL-FPA-0252; @CL-FPA-0223; @CL-FPA-0348; @CL-FPA-0224], and for securities offered pursuant to warrants or rights, it states the five items listed in Item 202(c) [@CL-FPA-0225; @CL-FPA-0253].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company registers one class of common stock and one series of notes. For the stock it outlines dividend rights, conversion terms and voting rights. For the notes it states the title, the 50 million dollar principal amount offered, the maturity, the lien priority and the events that count as default. It has no warrants, so it says nothing under that heading.

## Never confuse

Item 202(a), (b) and (c) require describing the title and relevant terms of capital stock, debt securities (including maturity, interest, conversion, redemption, amortization, sinking fund or retirement, kind and priority of any lien with principal properties, and events of default and periodic compliance evidence), and warrants or rights to be registered without needing a complete legal description of capital stock [@CL-FPA-0220; @CL-FPA-0221; @CL-FPA-0222; @CL-FPA-0252; @CL-FPA-0223; @CL-FPA-0348; @CL-FPA-0224; @CL-FPA-0225; @CL-FPA-0253], unlike Item 601(b)(4)(i), under which the underlying instruments defining the rights of holders of registered equity or debt securities are filed as exhibits [@CL-FPA-1183] on [Exhibits: the categories a finance reader uses](fpa-exhibits-categories.md). For principal United States trading markets and equity compensation plan disclosures, see [Market price, dividends and equity compensation plans](fpa-market-price-dividends-and-equity-compensation-plans.md); for use of proceeds and plan of distribution, see [Use of proceeds and plan of distribution](fpa-use-of-proceeds-and-plan-of-distribution.md).
