## Purpose

Shows what a registrant describes about capital stock, debt securities and warrants or rights that are to be registered, under Item 202 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0220; @CL-FPA-0222; @CL-FPA-0225].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Applies to | A registrant registering capital stock, debt securities or securities offered under warrants or rights [@CL-FPA-0220; @CL-FPA-0222; @CL-FPA-0225] |

Item 202(a) requires the registrant, if capital stock is to be registered, to state the title of the class and describe the relevant matters in paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(5), and a complete legal description of the securities need not be given [@CL-FPA-0220]. Item 202(a)(1) lists matters to outline briefly, including dividend rights, terms of conversion, sinking fund provisions, redemption provisions and voting rights [@CL-FPA-0221].

If debt securities are to be registered, Item 202(b) requires the registrant to state the title, the principal amount being offered and, if a series, the total amount authorized and the total amount outstanding as of the most recent practicable date [@CL-FPA-0222]. For debt securities, Item 202(b) says to describe such of the matters listed in paragraphs (b)(1) through (b)(10) as are relevant, and that a complete legal description of the securities need not be given [@CL-FPA-0252]. Item 202(b) lists debt matters to outline briefly, including provisions on maturity, interest, conversion, redemption, amortization, sinking fund or retirement and on the kind and priority of any lien securing the securities [@CL-FPA-0223]. One listed debt matter is the general type of event that constitutes a default and whether or not any periodic evidence is required to be furnished as to the absence of default or as to compliance with the terms of the indenture [@CL-FPA-0224]. If securities are to be offered pursuant to warrants or rights, Item 202(c) lists the amount of securities called for by the warrants or rights and the period during which and the price at which they are exercisable as matters to state [@CL-FPA-0225]. Item 202(c) also lists the amount of warrants or rights outstanding, provisions for changes to or adjustments in the exercise price, and any other material terms of such rights or warrants [@CL-FPA-0253]. The printed section also covers further matters for capital stock and debt securities, other securities, market information, American Depositary Receipts, instructions and a note on exchange listing, which these claims do not restate.

## How it works

The registrant describes such of the listed matters as are relevant to the class it registers, and need not give a complete legal description [@CL-FPA-0220; @CL-FPA-0221; @CL-FPA-0252]. For debt it adds the title, the amounts and the relevant listed provisions [@CL-FPA-0222; @CL-FPA-0223; @CL-FPA-0224; @CL-FPA-0252], and for warrants or rights it states the amount called for, the exercise terms and the other listed terms [@CL-FPA-0225; @CL-FPA-0253].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company registers one class of common stock and one series of notes. For the stock it outlines dividend rights, conversion terms and voting rights. For the notes it states the title, the 50 million dollar principal amount offered, the maturity, the lien priority and the events that count as default. It has no warrants, so it says nothing under that heading.

## Never confuse

This card is about the rights and terms of the securities. The [market price card](fpa-market-price-dividends-and-equity-compensation-plans.md) is about where the common equity trades, and the [use of proceeds card](fpa-use-of-proceeds-and-plan-of-distribution.md) is about what the offering money will be spent on. A debt description also covers default events and lien priority, which have nothing to do with trading markets [@CL-FPA-0223; @CL-FPA-0224].
