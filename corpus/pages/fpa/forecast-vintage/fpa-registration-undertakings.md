## Purpose

Shows which undertakings a registrant includes in a registration statement under Item 512 of Regulation S-K, mainly for securities registered under Rule 415 and for registration statements that incorporate later Exchange Act documents [@CL-FPA-1151; @CL-FPA-1152; @CL-FPA-1162].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | A registrant, for each undertaking that is applicable to the offering being registered [@CL-FPA-1151] |

Item 512 says to include each of its undertakings that is applicable to the offering being registered [@CL-FPA-1151]. Paragraph (a) says to include its undertaking if the securities are registered pursuant to Rule 415 under the Securities Act [@CL-FPA-1152].

Under that undertaking the registrant is to file, during any period in which offers or sales are being made, a post-effective amendment to include any prospectus required by section 10(a)(3) of the Securities Act of 1933 [@CL-FPA-1153]. The amendment is also to reflect in the prospectus any facts or events arising after the effective date of the registration statement (or the most recent post-effective amendment thereof) which, individually or in the aggregate, represent a fundamental change in the information set forth in the registration statement [@CL-FPA-1154]. It is also to include any material information with respect to the plan of distribution not previously disclosed in the registration statement or any material change to such information in the registration statement, subject to provisos, one of which is that paragraphs (a)(1)(i), (ii) and (iii) do not apply if the registration statement is on Form S-1, Form S-3, Form SF-3 or Form F-3 and the information is contained in reports filed with or furnished to the Commission under section 13 or section 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 that are incorporated by reference in the registration statement (or, on Form S-3, Form SF-3 or Form F-3, in a prospectus filed pursuant to § 230.424(b) that is part of it) [@CL-FPA-1158; @CL-FPA-1227; @CL-FPA-1228; @CL-FPA-1229].

An increase or decrease in volume of securities offered (if the total dollar value of securities offered would not exceed that which was registered) and any deviation from the low or high end of the estimated maximum offering range may be reflected in the form of prospectus filed with the Commission pursuant to Rule 424(b) if, in the aggregate, the changes in volume and price represent no more than 20% change in the maximum aggregate offering price [@CL-FPA-1155; @CL-FPA-1156]. The maximum aggregate offering price in that comparison is the one set forth in the “Calculation of Filing Fee Tables” or “Calculation of Registration Fee” table, as applicable, in the effective registration statement [@CL-FPA-1157].

For the purpose of determining any liability under the Securities Act of 1933, each such post-effective amendment is deemed to be a new registration statement relating to the securities offered therein [@CL-FPA-1159], and the offering of such securities at that time is deemed to be the initial bona fide offering thereof [@CL-FPA-1160]. The Rule 415 undertaking also says to remove from registration by means of a post-effective amendment any of the securities being registered which remain unsold at the termination of the offering [@CL-FPA-1161].

Paragraph (b) says to include its undertaking if the registration statement incorporates by reference any Exchange Act document filed subsequent to the effective date of the registration statement [@CL-FPA-1162]. For purposes of determining any liability under the Securities Act of 1933, that undertaking concerns each filing of the registrant's annual report pursuant to section 13(a) or section 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 [@CL-FPA-1163]. It also concerns, where applicable, each filing of an employee benefit plan's annual report pursuant to section 15(d) of that Act that is incorporated by reference in the registration statement [@CL-FPA-1164]. Each such filing is deemed to be a new registration statement relating to the securities offered therein, and the offering of such securities at that time is deemed to be the initial bona fide offering thereof [@CL-FPA-1165].

The card does not restate the other provisos that follow the first undertaking under paragraph (a) and excuse some other forms from part of it, the undertakings for foreign private issuers, for the timing of the prospectus deemed part of the registration statement, for communications in a primary offering and for asset-backed shelf registrations, or the undertakings for securities offered for resale, for registration statements that omit pricing information, for the eligibility of a trustee and for asset-backed securities that incorporate third-party reports. The undertaking on indemnification that goes with a request for acceleration is also not restated; the [use of proceeds card](fpa-use-of-proceeds-and-plan-of-distribution.md) refers to it. The delaying amendment rule that the cover page item points to is not in the source stored for this card, so the card does not describe it.

## How it works

The registrant picks the undertakings that fit the offering [@CL-FPA-1151]. For securities registered under Rule 415 it undertakes to file a post-effective amendment for a required prospectus, a fundamental change and new or changed plan of distribution information, subject to provisos, including one for certain forms and incorporated reports [@CL-FPA-1152; @CL-FPA-1153; @CL-FPA-1154; @CL-FPA-1158; @CL-FPA-1227], and to remove unsold securities at the end of the offering [@CL-FPA-1161]. Small volume and price moves can be reflected in a prospectus filed under Rule 424(b) while they stay within the stated limit [@CL-FPA-1155; @CL-FPA-1156]. If the registration statement incorporates later annual reports, each of those filings is treated as a new registration statement [@CL-FPA-1162; @CL-FPA-1165].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company registers 100 million dollars of debt securities for continuous sale under Rule 415. During the offering it sells slightly more than planned and prices below the expected range, and the changes together move the maximum aggregate offering price by 12 percent, while the total dollar value sold stays within what was registered. It reflects the changes in a prospectus filed under Rule 424(b) instead of a post-effective amendment. When the offering ends it removes the 5 million dollars of securities still unsold by post-effective amendment.

## Never confuse

The undertakings are statements the registrant includes in the filing; the terms of the securities themselves are in [Description of registrant's securities](fpa-description-of-registrants-securities.md). They are also not the disclosure of what the proceeds will be used for or of the underwriting arrangements, which is on [Use of proceeds and plan of distribution](fpa-use-of-proceeds-and-plan-of-distribution.md).
