## Purpose

Shows what a registrant discloses about director independence, board meetings, standing committees and the audit committee financial expert under Item 407 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0207; @CL-FPA-0209; @CL-FPA-0210; @CL-FPA-0247].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | A registrant, for its directors and nominees, its board meetings, its standing committees and its audit committee [@CL-FPA-0207; @CL-FPA-0209; @CL-FPA-0210; @CL-FPA-0247] |

Item 407(a) requires the registrant to identify each director, and when the disclosure called for by paragraph (a) is being presented in a proxy or information statement relating to the election of directors, each nominee for director, that is independent under the independence standards applicable to the registrant [@CL-FPA-0207]. For each director and nominee for director that is identified as independent, Item 407(a)(3) requires the registrant to describe, by specific category or type, any transactions, relationships or arrangements not disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a), or for investment companies, Item 22(b) of Schedule 14A, that were considered by the board of directors under the applicable independence definitions in determining that the director is independent [@CL-FPA-0244; @CL-FPA-0208; @CL-FPA-0259]. Item 407(b)(1) requires the registrant to state the total number of meetings of the board of directors (including regularly scheduled and special meetings) which were held during the last full fiscal year and to name each incumbent director who during the last full fiscal year attended fewer than 75 percent of the aggregate of: (1) the total number of meetings of the board of directors held during the period for which he has been a director [@CL-FPA-0209], and (2) the total number of meetings held by all committees of the board on which he served during the periods that he served [@CL-FPA-0209; @CL-FPA-0245].

Item 407(b)(3) requires the registrant to state whether or not the registrant has standing audit, nominating and compensation committees of the board of directors, or committees performing similar functions [@CL-FPA-0210]. If the registrant has such committees, it must identify each committee member, state the number of committee meetings held by each such committee during the last fiscal year and describe briefly the functions performed by each such committee [@CL-FPA-0211]. Such disclosure need not be provided to the extent it is duplicative of disclosure provided in accordance with paragraph (c), (d) or (e) of Item 407 [@CL-FPA-0246]. If the registrant does not have a standing nominating committee or committee performing similar functions, Item 407(c)(1) requires it to state the basis for the view of the board of directors that it is appropriate for the registrant not to have such a committee and to identify each director who participates in the consideration of director nominees [@CL-FPA-0212].

Item 407(d)(5)(i)(A) requires the registrant to disclose that its board of directors has determined that the registrant either: (1) has at least one audit committee financial expert serving on its audit committee [@CL-FPA-0247], or (2) does not have an audit committee financial expert serving on its audit committee [@CL-FPA-0247]. If the registrant provides the disclosure required by paragraph (d)(5)(i)(A)(2), Item 407(d)(5)(i)(C) requires it to explain why it does not have an audit committee financial expert [@CL-FPA-0248].

## How it works

The registrant identifies each director (and in an election proxy or information statement, each nominee) who is independent under the applicable independence standards [@CL-FPA-0207] and describes, by specific category or type, any transactions, relationships or arrangements not disclosed under Item 404(a) (or Item 22(b) of Schedule 14A for investment companies) that the board considered under the applicable independence definitions in determining independence [@CL-FPA-0244; @CL-FPA-0208; @CL-FPA-0259]. It states the total number of board meetings (including regularly scheduled and special meetings) held during the last full fiscal year and names each incumbent director who attended fewer than 75 percent of the aggregate of board and committee meetings during the director's periods of service [@CL-FPA-0209; @CL-FPA-0245]. It states whether or not it has standing audit, nominating and compensation committees (or committees performing similar functions), identifies each member, states each committee's meeting count during the last fiscal year, and briefly describes each committee's functions to the extent not duplicative of paragraphs (c), (d) or (e), or if it has no standing nominating committee or committee performing similar functions, states the basis for the board's view that not having one is appropriate and identifies each director who participates in considering nominees [@CL-FPA-0210; @CL-FPA-0211; @CL-FPA-0246; @CL-FPA-0212]. It also discloses whether its board has determined that it has at least one audit committee financial expert serving on its audit committee or does not have one, and explains why if it does not [@CL-FPA-0247; @CL-FPA-0248].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A board has 8 directors, and the applicable standard finds 6 independent. The company names those 6 and describes, by category, a consulting arrangement the board considered. The board met 9 times. It has determined that the company has one audit committee financial expert, so the company says so. One director attended 70 percent of the aggregate of board and committee meetings, so the company names that director. The board has no nominating committee, so it states why that is appropriate.

## Never confuse

Under Item 407(a)(3), the description by specific category or type for each independent director and nominee covers transactions, relationships or arrangements not disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a) (or for investment companies, Item 22(b) of Schedule 14A) that were considered by the board under the applicable independence definitions in determining that the director is independent [@CL-FPA-0244; @CL-FPA-0208; @CL-FPA-0259]; see [Transactions with related persons](fpa-related-person-transactions.md). Who the directors are and what their records show is covered in [Directors, executive officers, promoters and control persons](fpa-directors-officers-and-control-persons.md), and the code of ethics in [Code of ethics and insider trading arrangements and policies](fpa-code-of-ethics-and-insider-trading-policies.md). Under Item 407(b)(3), a registrant with standing audit, nominating and compensation committees (or committees performing similar functions) identifies each member, states the number of meetings held during the last fiscal year and briefly describes the functions performed [@CL-FPA-0210; @CL-FPA-0211], whereas under Item 407(c)(1) a registrant without a standing nominating committee or committee performing similar functions states the basis for the board's view that it is appropriate not to have such a committee and identifies each director who participates in the consideration of director nominees [@CL-FPA-0212].
