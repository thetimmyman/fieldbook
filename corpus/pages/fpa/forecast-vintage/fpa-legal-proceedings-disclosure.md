## Purpose

Shows which pending legal proceedings a registrant describes under Item 103 of Regulation S-K and which it may leave out [@CL-FPA-0107; @CL-FPA-0109].

| Applies to | A registrant, for material pending legal proceedings to which it or any subsidiary is a party or of which any of their property is the subject [@CL-FPA-0107] |

Item 103(a) requires a brief description of material pending legal proceedings, other than ordinary routine litigation incidental to the business, to which the registrant or any of its subsidiaries is a party or of which any of their property is the subject [@CL-FPA-0107]. The description names the court or agency, the date instituted, the principal parties, the alleged factual basis and the relief sought [@CL-FPA-0108]. Item 103(a) also requires similar information as to any such proceedings known to be contemplated by governmental authorities [@CL-FPA-0163]. Information may be provided by hyperlink or cross-reference to legal proceedings disclosure elsewhere in the document, such as in Management's Discussion and Analysis, Risk Factors and notes to the financial statements [@CL-FPA-0164].

Paragraph (b) of Item 103 states that no information need be given under the section for the proceedings it lists [@CL-FPA-0109]. The list covers proceedings: (1) that involve negligence or other claims or actions if the business ordinarily results in such claims or actions, unless the claim or action departs from the normal kind of such claims or actions [@CL-FPA-0157]; or (2) that involve primarily a claim for damages if the amount involved, exclusive of interest and costs, does not exceed 10 percent of the current assets of the registrant and its subsidiaries on a consolidated basis [@CL-FPA-0110]. However, if any proceeding presents in large degree the same legal or factual issues as other proceedings pending or known to be contemplated, the amount involved in such other proceedings shall be included in computing such percentage [@CL-FPA-0158]. Paragraph (c) provides that, notwithstanding paragraph (b), disclosure under the section shall include, but shall not be limited to: (1) any material bankruptcy, receivership, or similar proceeding with respect to the registrant or any of its significant subsidiaries [@CL-FPA-0111; @CL-FPA-0339]; and (2) any material proceedings to which any director, officer or affiliate of the registrant, any owner of record or beneficially of more than five percent of any class of voting securities of the registrant, or any associate of any such director, officer, affiliate of the registrant, or security holder is a party adverse to the registrant or any of its subsidiaries or has a material interest adverse to the registrant or any of its subsidiaries [@CL-FPA-0111; @CL-FPA-0339; @CL-FPA-0340].

Item 103(c)(3) covers administrative or judicial proceedings (including proceedings which present in large degree the same issues) arising under any Federal, State, or local provisions that have been enacted or adopted regulating the discharge of materials into the environment or primarily for the purpose of protecting the environment [@CL-FPA-0112; @CL-FPA-0159]. Such proceedings shall not be deemed ordinary routine litigation incidental to the business and shall be described if any one of the following conditions is met [@CL-FPA-0113]: (i) such proceeding is material to the business or financial condition of the registrant [@CL-FPA-0160]; (ii) such proceeding involves primarily a claim for damages, or involves potential monetary sanctions, capital expenditures, deferred charges or charges to income and the amount involved, exclusive of interest and costs, exceeds 10 percent of the current assets of the registrant and its subsidiaries on a consolidated basis [@CL-FPA-0114]; or (iii) a governmental authority is a party to such proceeding and such proceeding involves potential monetary sanctions, unless the registrant reasonably believes that such proceeding will result in no monetary sanctions, or in monetary sanctions, exclusive of interest and costs, of less than $300,000 [@CL-FPA-0115].

In paragraph (c)(3)(iii), the registrant may instead elect another threshold that (A) the registrant determines is reasonably designed to result in disclosure of any such proceeding that is material to the business or financial condition, (B) the registrant discloses (including any change thereto) in each annual and quarterly report, and (C) does not exceed the lesser of $1 million or one percent of the current assets of the registrant and its subsidiaries on a consolidated basis [@CL-FPA-0116; @CL-FPA-0161], provided, however, that such proceedings that are similar in nature may be grouped and described generically [@CL-FPA-0162].

## How it works

Under Item 103(a), the registrant briefly describes any material pending legal proceedings, other than ordinary routine litigation incidental to the business, to which it or any of its subsidiaries is a party or of which any of their property is the subject (and includes similar information for such proceedings known to be contemplated by governmental authorities), stating the court or agency, the date instituted, the principal parties, the alleged factual basis and the relief sought, and may provide the information by hyperlink or cross-reference to disclosure elsewhere in the document [@CL-FPA-0107; @CL-FPA-0108; @CL-FPA-0163; @CL-FPA-0164]. It checks the omissions in paragraph (b), including the consolidated-current-assets damages test where proceedings presenting in large degree the same legal or factual issues are included together [@CL-FPA-0109; @CL-FPA-0157; @CL-FPA-0110; @CL-FPA-0158]. Notwithstanding paragraph (b), disclosure under paragraph (c) includes material bankruptcy or receivership proceedings, material adverse proceedings involving directors, officers, affiliates, over-five-percent voting security holders or their associates, and environmental proceedings meeting condition (i), (ii) or (iii) [@CL-FPA-0111; @CL-FPA-0339; @CL-FPA-0340; @CL-FPA-0112; @CL-FPA-0159; @CL-FPA-0113; @CL-FPA-0160; @CL-FPA-0114; @CL-FPA-0115; @CL-FPA-0116; @CL-FPA-0161; @CL-FPA-0162].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant has consolidated current assets of 500,000. A claim for damages alone is 40,000, below the 50,000 that is ten percent of current assets, so the damages omission can apply if no paragraph (c) matter is involved. A separate environmental proceeding has a governmental authority as a party and potential monetary sanctions of 400,000. That is above 300,000, so it is described unless the registrant reasonably believes the proceeding will result in no monetary sanctions or sanctions below 300,000. If the registrant had elected another threshold, the election would not help here: one percent of current assets is 5,000, which is lower than 1 million, so an elected threshold could not exceed 5,000, and 400,000 is well above it.

## Never confuse

Under Item 103(a), ordinary routine litigation incidental to the business is excluded from the general requirement [@CL-FPA-0107], whereas under Item 103(c)(3) administrative or judicial proceedings arising under Federal, State, or local environmental provisions shall not be deemed ordinary routine litigation incidental to the business and shall be described if condition (i), (ii) or (iii) is met [@CL-FPA-0112; @CL-FPA-0159; @CL-FPA-0113]. Paragraph (b)(2) omits a proceeding involving primarily a claim for damages if the amount involved, exclusive of interest and costs, does not exceed 10 percent of consolidated current assets [@CL-FPA-0110], while paragraph (c), which applies notwithstanding paragraph (b) [@CL-FPA-0111], requires description of an environmental proceeding under paragraph (c)(3)(ii) when the amount involved, exclusive of interest and costs, exceeds 10 percent of consolidated current assets [@CL-FPA-0114]. Item 404(a) covers any transaction, since the beginning of the registrant's last fiscal year, or any currently proposed transaction, in which the registrant was or is to be a participant and the amount involved exceeds $120,000, and in which any related person had or will have a direct or indirect material interest [@CL-FPA-0146; @CL-FPA-0147]; see [Transactions with related persons](fpa-related-person-transactions.md). Item 103 information may be provided by hyperlink or cross-reference to Risk Factors disclosure elsewhere in the document [@CL-FPA-0164]; see [Risk factor disclosure for public registrants](fpa-risk-factors.md).

Whether the company would measure consolidated current assets and track pending proceedings, sanctions and amounts involved the way the threshold tests need. [@Q-017]
