## Purpose

Shows what a registrant describes about cybersecurity risk management, strategy and governance under Item 106 of Regulation S-K, and how the Item defines an incident and a threat [@CL-FPA-0119; @CL-FPA-0121; @CL-FPA-0117; @CL-FPA-0118].

| Applies to | A registrant describing its cybersecurity processes and governance [@CL-FPA-0119; @CL-FPA-0121] |

Item 106(a) defines a cybersecurity incident as an unauthorized occurrence, or a series of related unauthorized occurrences, on or conducted through a registrant's information systems that jeopardizes the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of those systems or any information residing in those systems [@CL-FPA-0117; @CL-FPA-0165]. Item 106(a) defines a cybersecurity threat as any potential unauthorized occurrence on or conducted through those systems that may result in adverse effects on their confidentiality, integrity, or availability or on information residing in them [@CL-FPA-0118].

Under Item 106(b)(1), the registrant describes its processes, if any, for assessing, identifying, and managing material risks from cybersecurity threats in sufficient detail for a reasonable investor to understand those processes [@CL-FPA-0119]. In providing such disclosure, a registrant should address, as applicable, the following non-exclusive list of disclosure items: (i) whether and how any such processes have been integrated into the registrant's overall risk management system or processes; (ii) whether the registrant engages assessors, consultants, auditors, or other third parties in connection with any such processes; and (iii) whether the registrant has processes to oversee and identify such risks from cybersecurity threats associated with its use of any third-party service provider [@CL-FPA-0120; @CL-FPA-0320]. Item 106(b)(2) requires the registrant to describe whether any risks from cybersecurity threats, including as a result of any previous cybersecurity incidents, have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect the registrant, including its business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition, and if so, how [@CL-FPA-0166].

Item 106(c)(1) requires the registrant to describe the board of directors' oversight of risks from cybersecurity threats and, if applicable, to identify any board committee or subcommittee responsible for the oversight of risks from cybersecurity threats and describe the processes by which the board or such committee is informed about such risks [@CL-FPA-0121; @CL-FPA-0167]. Item 106(c)(2) requires the registrant to describe management's role in assessing and managing the registrant's material risks from cybersecurity threats, addressing, as applicable, the following non-exclusive list of disclosure items: (i) whether and which management positions or committees are responsible for assessing and managing such risks, and the relevant expertise of such persons or members in such detail as necessary to fully describe the nature of the expertise [@CL-FPA-0122; @CL-FPA-0321], (ii) the processes by which such persons or committees are informed about and monitor the prevention, detection, mitigation, and remediation of cybersecurity incidents [@CL-FPA-0322], and (iii) whether such persons or committees report information about such risks to the board of directors or a committee or subcommittee of the board of directors [@CL-FPA-0122; @CL-FPA-0321; @CL-FPA-0322].

## How it works

The registrant applies the Item 106(a) definitions of cybersecurity incident and cybersecurity threat [@CL-FPA-0117; @CL-FPA-0165; @CL-FPA-0118]. Under Item 106(b), it describes its processes, if any, for assessing, identifying, and managing material risks from cybersecurity threats in sufficient detail for a reasonable investor to understand those processes, addressing the non-exclusive list in paragraph (b)(1) as applicable [@CL-FPA-0119; @CL-FPA-0120; @CL-FPA-0320], and describes whether any risks from cybersecurity threats, including as a result of any previous cybersecurity incidents, have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect the registrant, including its business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition, and if so, how [@CL-FPA-0166]. Under Item 106(c), it describes the board of directors' oversight of risks from cybersecurity threats (including any responsible board committee or subcommittee and the processes by which the board or committee is informed, if applicable) [@CL-FPA-0121; @CL-FPA-0167] and management's role in assessing and managing the registrant's material risks from cybersecurity threats, addressing the non-exclusive list in paragraph (c)(2) as applicable [@CL-FPA-0122; @CL-FPA-0321; @CL-FPA-0322].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant has an outside firm that tests its systems each year and a risk committee of its board that receives a quarterly report from the security lead. Its description says whether the testing processes sit inside the overall risk process, names the committee, and says which management role reports to the committee.

## Never confuse

Under Item 106(a), a cybersecurity threat is any potential unauthorized occurrence on or conducted through a registrant's information systems that may result in adverse effects on the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of a registrant's information systems or any information residing therein [@CL-FPA-0118], whereas a cybersecurity incident is an unauthorized occurrence, or a series of related unauthorized occurrences, on or conducted through a registrant's information systems that jeopardizes the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of a registrant's information systems or any information residing therein [@CL-FPA-0117; @CL-FPA-0165]. Item 106(c)(1) requires disclosure of the board of directors' oversight of risks from cybersecurity threats [@CL-FPA-0121; @CL-FPA-0167], while Item 106(c)(2) requires disclosure of management's role in assessing and managing the registrant's material risks from cybersecurity threats [@CL-FPA-0122; @CL-FPA-0321; @CL-FPA-0322]. Item 106(b)(1) requires a description of the registrant's processes, if any, for assessing, identifying, and managing material risks from cybersecurity threats [@CL-FPA-0119; @CL-FPA-0120; @CL-FPA-0320], while Item 106(b)(2) requires a description of whether any risks from cybersecurity threats, including as a result of any previous cybersecurity incidents, have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect the registrant, including its business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition, and if so, how [@CL-FPA-0166].

The risk factor disclosure is covered in [Risk factor disclosure for public registrants](fpa-risk-factors.md), and the corporate governance disclosure is covered in [Corporate governance disclosure](fpa-corporate-governance-disclosure.md).
