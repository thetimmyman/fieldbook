## Purpose

Shows how a registrant presents the material factors that make an investment in the registrant or offering speculative or risky under Item 105 of Regulation S-K, including how they are organized, explained, and summarized [@CL-FPA-0041; @CL-FPA-0042; @CL-FPA-0044; @CL-FPA-0045; @CL-FPA-0329]. Regulation S-K states the requirements applicable to the content of the non-financial statement portions of: (1) registration statements under the Securities Act to the extent provided in the forms to be used for registration under such Act [@CL-FPA-0105]; and (2) registration statements under section 12, annual or other reports under sections 13 and 15(d), going-private transaction statements under section 13, tender offer statements under sections 13 and 14, annual reports to security holders and proxy and information statements under section 14, and any other documents required to be filed under the Exchange Act, to the extent provided in the forms and rules under such Act [@CL-FPA-0105; @CL-FPA-0318; @CL-FPA-0319; @CL-FPA-0106].

Item 105(a) of Regulation S-K requires the registrant, where appropriate, to provide under the caption “Risk Factors” a discussion of the material factors that make an investment in the registrant or offering speculative or risky [@CL-FPA-0041]. This discussion must be organized logically with relevant headings and each risk factor should be set forth under a subcaption that adequately describes the risk [@CL-FPA-0042]. The presentation of risks that could apply generically to any registrant or any offering is discouraged, and to the extent generic risk factors are presented, disclose them at the end of the risk factor section under the caption “General Risk Factors” [@CL-FPA-0043].

Under Item 105(b), the registrant must concisely explain how each risk affects the registrant or the securities being offered [@CL-FPA-0044]. If the discussion is longer than 15 pages, the registrant must include in the forepart of the prospectus or annual report, as applicable, a series of concise, bulleted or numbered statements that is no more than two pages summarizing the principal factors that make an investment in the registrant or offering speculative or risky [@CL-FPA-0045; @CL-FPA-0329].

## How it works

Where appropriate, the registrant provides under the caption “Risk Factors” a logically organized discussion with relevant headings, setting forth each risk factor under a subcaption that adequately describes the risk and concisely explaining how each risk affects the registrant or the securities being offered [@CL-FPA-0041; @CL-FPA-0042; @CL-FPA-0044]. Generic risk factors that could apply to any registrant or any offering are discouraged and, to the extent presented, must be disclosed at the end of the risk factor section under the caption “General Risk Factors” [@CL-FPA-0043]. If the discussion is longer than 15 pages, the forepart of the prospectus or annual report must include a series of concise, bulleted or numbered statements of no more than two pages summarizing the principal factors that make an investment in the registrant or offering speculative or risky [@CL-FPA-0045; @CL-FPA-0329].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor lists a risk that its main Government program may be terminated early. It states the effect: the program supplies most of a segment's revenue. It places a generic risk about economic conditions at the end under a general heading.

## Never confuse

Item 105(a) covers the material factors that make an investment in the registrant or offering speculative or risky [@CL-FPA-0041], whereas Item 303(b)(3) covers critical accounting estimates made in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles that involve a significant level of estimation uncertainty and have had or are reasonably likely to have a material impact on financial condition or results of operations, requiring qualitative and quantitative information necessary to understand the estimation uncertainty and impact [@CL-FPA-0035; @CL-FPA-0036]; see [Critical accounting estimates in MD&A](fpa-critical-accounting-estimates.md).

The description of cybersecurity risk management, strategy and governance is covered in [Cybersecurity disclosure in Regulation S-K](fpa-cybersecurity-disclosure.md).
