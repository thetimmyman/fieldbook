## Purpose

Shows which executives and directors the executive compensation disclosure covers, what the compensation discussion and analysis explains and what the Summary Compensation Table reports under Item 402 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-1051; @CL-FPA-1053; @CL-FPA-1060; @CL-FPA-1065].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for its named executive officers and the directors covered by paragraph (k), unless it uses the scaled disclosure available to a smaller reporting company or an emerging growth company [@CL-FPA-1051; @CL-FPA-1071; @CL-FPA-1072] |

Item 402 requires clear, concise and understandable disclosure of all plan and non-plan compensation awarded to, earned by, or paid to the named executive officers and the directors covered by paragraph (k) [@CL-FPA-1051]. The compensation covered is compensation by any person for all services rendered in all capacities to the registrant and its subsidiaries, unless otherwise specifically excluded from disclosure [@CL-FPA-1052].

The named executive officers include all individuals serving as principal executive officer or acting in a similar capacity during the last completed fiscal year, and all individuals serving as principal financial officer or acting in a similar capacity in that year, regardless of compensation level [@CL-FPA-1053; @CL-FPA-1054]. They also include the three most highly compensated executive officers other than those two who were serving as executive officers at the end of that year [@CL-FPA-1055], and up to two additional individuals who would have been in that group but for not serving as an executive officer at year end [@CL-FPA-1056]. Most highly compensated is judged by total compensation for the last completed fiscal year, reduced by the amount required under paragraph (c)(2)(viii) [@CL-FPA-1057; @CL-FPA-1058]. No disclosure need be provided for an executive officer other than the PEO and PFO whose total compensation, as so reduced, does not exceed $100,000 [@CL-FPA-1058]. If the PEO or PFO served in that capacity during any part of a fiscal year with respect to which information is required, information should be provided as to all of his or her compensation for the full fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1059].

The compensation discussion and analysis discusses the compensation awarded to, earned by, or paid to the named executive officers and explains all material elements of it [@CL-FPA-1060]. It describes, among other listed items, the objectives of the registrant's compensation programs, what they are designed to reward, each element of compensation and why the registrant chooses to pay each element [@CL-FPA-1061]. Registrants are not required to disclose target levels with respect to specific quantitative or qualitative performance-related factors considered by the compensation committee or the board of directors, or any other factors or criteria involving confidential trade secrets or confidential commercial or financial information, the disclosure of which would result in competitive harm for the registrant [@CL-FPA-1062; @CL-FPA-1063]. The standard for deciding whether disclosure would cause competitive harm is the same standard that would apply when a registrant requests confidential treatment of such information under Securities Act Rule 406 and Exchange Act Rule 24b-2, each of which incorporates the criteria for non-disclosure under Exemption 4 of the Freedom of Information Act [@CL-FPA-1232; @CL-FPA-1233]. A registrant that determines the disclosure would cause competitive harm need not seek confidential treatment under Securities Act Rule 406 and Exchange Act Rule 24b-2, but it must then discuss how difficult it will be for the executive or how likely it will be for the registrant to achieve the undisclosed target levels or other factors [@CL-FPA-1211; @CL-FPA-1064].

The Summary Compensation Table gives the compensation of the named executive officers for each of the registrant's last three completed fiscal years [@CL-FPA-1065]. It reports base salary and bonus [@CL-FPA-1066], the grant date fair value of stock awards and of option awards computed under FASB ASC Topic 718 [@CL-FPA-1067; @CL-FPA-1068], and all other compensation, which must include perquisites and other personal benefits unless the aggregate amount is less than $10,000 [@CL-FPA-1069]. The total for each officer is the sum of all amounts reported in columns (c) through (i) [@CL-FPA-1070].

A smaller reporting company or emerging growth company may provide the scaled disclosure in paragraphs (m) through (r) instead of paragraphs (a) through (k), (s) and (u) [@CL-FPA-1071; @CL-FPA-1072]. Its named executive officers include its two most highly compensated executive officers other than the PEO who were serving at the end of the last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1073]. Its Summary Compensation Table covers its last two completed fiscal years [@CL-FPA-1074].

The printed item also gives rules on foreign private issuers, definitions, the other examples for the discussion, the pension and non-equity incentive plan columns, footnotes, the other scaled paragraphs, compensation risk, golden parachute compensation, recovery of erroneously awarded compensation and option grant timing, which these claims do not restate.

## How it works

The registrant picks the named executive officers [@CL-FPA-1053; @CL-FPA-1055], explains each element of their pay in the discussion [@CL-FPA-1061], and reports the dollar amounts for each of the last three years in the table [@CL-FPA-1065; @CL-FPA-1070].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company has a chief executive, a chief financial officer and five other executive officers. It reports the first two whatever they were paid. Of the other five, it considers the three with the highest total compensation, reduced as the item requires. One of the three has a reduced total of 90,000 dollars, so no disclosure is needed for that person. Perquisites of 8,000 dollars in total are under the 10,000 dollar line and can be left out of all other compensation.

## Never confuse

Pay in the table is a different matter from stock ownership, which is covered in the [security ownership card](fpa-security-ownership-and-section-16a-compliance.md). The awards behind the stock and option columns are reported in [Executive equity award tables](fpa-executive-equity-award-tables.md). The executive compensation a contractor reports to the government is a separate duty; see [Reporting executive compensation and first-tier subcontract awards](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-executive-compensation-and-subcontract-award-reporting.md).
