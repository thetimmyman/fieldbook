## Purpose

Shows when quarterly supplementary information, recent sales of unregistered securities and issuer repurchases are disclosed under Items 302, 701 and 703 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0152; @CL-FPA-0155; @CL-FPA-0156].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for each Item as described below; a smaller reporting company, as defined in the cited section, is not required to provide Item 302 information [@CL-FPA-0154] |

Item 302(a) concerns one or more retrospective changes to the statements of comprehensive income for any of the quarters within the two most recent fiscal years or any subsequent interim period for which financial statements are included or are required to be included by Article 3 of Regulation S-X [@CL-FPA-0152; @CL-FPA-0363]. Where such changes individually or in the aggregate are material, it requires an explanation of the reasons and, for each affected quarterly period and the fourth quarter in the affected year, summarized financial information related to the statements of comprehensive income, as specified in Rule 1-02(bb)(1)(ii) of Regulation S-X, and earnings per share reflecting such changes [@CL-FPA-0153; @CL-FPA-0364]. Item 302(a) applies to any registrant, except a foreign private issuer, that has securities registered under section 12(b) of the Exchange Act (other than mutual life insurance companies) or section 12(g) of the Exchange Act, after the registrant's initial registration of securities under those sections [@CL-FPA-0181; @CL-FPA-0366]. Item 302(c) says a registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company, as defined in the cited section, is not required to provide the information required by Item 302 [@CL-FPA-0154].

Item 701 requires the registrant to furnish the listed information as to all securities of the registrant sold by the registrant within the past three years which were not registered under the Securities Act [@CL-FPA-0155]. Item 701(d) requires the registrant to indicate the section of the Securities Act or the rule of the Commission under which exemption from registration was claimed and to state briefly the facts relied upon to make the exemption available [@CL-FPA-0180].

Item 703(a) covers any purchase made by or on behalf of the issuer or any “affiliated purchaser,” as defined in § 240.10b-18(a)(3), of shares or other units of any class of the issuer's equity securities that is registered by the issuer pursuant to section 12 of the Exchange Act [@CL-FPA-0179]. Item 703(b) requires the issuer purchases table to include, for each class or series of securities for each month included in the period covered by the report: (1) the total number of shares (or units) purchased (column (a)) [@CL-FPA-0156], (2) the average price paid per share (or unit) (column (b)) [@CL-FPA-0331], (3) the total number of shares (or units) purchased as part of publicly announced repurchase plans or programs (column (c)) [@CL-FPA-0331], and (4) the maximum number (or approximate dollar value) of shares (or units) that may yet be purchased under the plans or programs (column (d)) [@CL-FPA-0156; @CL-FPA-0331; @CL-FPA-0332].

## How it works

For Item 302, a registrant within paragraph (a)(2) (any registrant except a foreign private issuer that has securities registered under section 12(b), other than mutual life insurance companies, or section 12(g) of the Exchange Act after its initial registration of securities under those sections, and not a smaller reporting company under Item 302(c)) checks whether there are one or more retrospective changes to the statements of comprehensive income for any of the quarters within the two most recent fiscal years or any subsequent interim period for which financial statements are included or required by Article 3 of Regulation S-X that individually or in the aggregate are material, and if so provides an explanation of the reasons and summarized financial information and earnings per share reflecting such changes for each affected quarterly period and the fourth quarter in the affected year [@CL-FPA-0152; @CL-FPA-0363; @CL-FPA-0153; @CL-FPA-0364; @CL-FPA-0181; @CL-FPA-0366; @CL-FPA-0154]. For Item 701, the registrant furnishes the required information as to all securities of the registrant sold by the registrant within the past three years that were not registered under the Securities Act, including the section or rule under which exemption was claimed and the facts relied upon [@CL-FPA-0155; @CL-FPA-0180]. For Item 703, the registrant reports in the tabular format, by month and class or series, columns (a) through (d) for any purchase made by or on behalf of the issuer or any affiliated purchaser of shares or other units of any class of the issuer's equity securities registered pursuant to section 12 of the Exchange Act [@CL-FPA-0179; @CL-FPA-0156; @CL-FPA-0331; @CL-FPA-0332].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant restates income for one quarter and the change is material. It explains why and shows the summarized income information for each affected quarter and the fourth quarter. It also lists a private sale of shares made last year that was not registered. Its repurchase table shows the shares bought in each month of the quarter.

## Never confuse

Item 701 covers all securities of the registrant sold by the registrant within the past three years which were not registered under the Securities Act [@CL-FPA-0155; @CL-FPA-0180], while Item 703 covers any purchase made by or on behalf of the issuer or any affiliated purchaser of shares or other units of any class of the issuer's equity securities registered pursuant to section 12 of the Exchange Act, reported for each month included in the period covered by the report [@CL-FPA-0179; @CL-FPA-0156; @CL-FPA-0331; @CL-FPA-0332]. Item 302(a) covers one or more retrospective changes to the statements of comprehensive income for any of the quarters within the two most recent fiscal years or any subsequent interim period for which financial statements are included or required by Article 3 of Regulation S-X that individually or in the aggregate are material [@CL-FPA-0152; @CL-FPA-0363; @CL-FPA-0153; @CL-FPA-0364].
