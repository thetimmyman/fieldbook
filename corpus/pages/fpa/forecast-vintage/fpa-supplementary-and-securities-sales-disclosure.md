## Purpose

Shows when quarterly supplementary information, recent sales of unregistered securities and issuer repurchases are disclosed under Items 302, 701 and 703 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0152; @CL-FPA-0155; @CL-FPA-0156].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |

| Applies to | A registrant, for each Item as described below; a smaller reporting company, as defined in the cited section, is not required to provide Item 302 information [@CL-FPA-0154] |

Item 302(a) concerns one or more retrospective changes to the statements of comprehensive income for any of the quarters within the two most recent fiscal years or any subsequent interim period [@CL-FPA-0152]. Where such changes individually or in the aggregate are material, it requires an explanation of the reasons and, for each affected quarterly period and the fourth quarter in the affected year, summarized financial information related to the statements of comprehensive income [@CL-FPA-0153]. Item 302(a) applies to any registrant, except a foreign private issuer, that has securities registered under section 12(b) of the Exchange Act (other than mutual life insurance companies) or section 12(g) of the Exchange Act, after the registrant's initial registration [@CL-FPA-0181]. Item 302(c) says a registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company, as defined in the cited section, is not required to provide the information required by Item 302 [@CL-FPA-0154].

Item 701 requires the registrant to furnish the listed information as to all securities of the registrant sold by the registrant within the past three years which were not registered under the Securities Act [@CL-FPA-0155]. Item 701(d) requires the registrant to indicate the section of the Securities Act or the rule of the Commission under which exemption from registration was claimed and to state briefly the facts relied upon to make the exemption available [@CL-FPA-0180]. The printed Item lists further content, including dates, amounts, purchasers, consideration and terms of conversion or exercise, and a report on the use of proceeds, which these claims do not restate.

Item 703(a) covers any purchase made by or on behalf of the issuer or any affiliated purchaser, as defined in the cited section, of shares or other units of any class of the issuer's equity securities that is registered by the issuer pursuant to section 12 of the Exchange Act [@CL-FPA-0179]. Item 703(b) says the issuer purchases table includes, for each class or series of securities for each month in the period covered by the report, the total number of shares (or units) purchased [@CL-FPA-0156]. The printed Item lists further columns and instructions, which these claims do not restate.

## How it works

For Item 302, the registrant first checks that the paragraph applies to it [@CL-FPA-0181], then looks for retrospective quarterly changes within the period and tests them for materiality [@CL-FPA-0152; @CL-FPA-0153]. A smaller reporting company need not provide the Item 302 information [@CL-FPA-0154]. For Item 701, it lists its unregistered sales over the look-back period and states the exemption claimed [@CL-FPA-0155; @CL-FPA-0180]. For Item 703, it fills the table by month and class or series for purchases within the scope of the Item [@CL-FPA-0179; @CL-FPA-0156].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant restates income for one quarter and the change is material. It explains why and shows the summarized income information for each affected quarter and the fourth quarter. It also lists a private sale of shares made last year that was not registered. Its repurchase table shows the shares bought in each month of the quarter.

## Never confuse

Item 701 looks at securities the registrant sold without registration, over a look-back period of years [@CL-FPA-0155], while Item 703 reports purchases in a table by month [@CL-FPA-0156]. Item 302 concerns restated quarterly income information and is a different matter from both [@CL-FPA-0152; @CL-FPA-0153].
