## Purpose

Shows what a registrant presents about where its common equity trades, its dividends, its equity compensation plans and its performance graph under Item 201 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0213; @CL-FPA-0249; @CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0217].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for its common equity and its compensation plans; the plan table need not be provided in a registration statement under the Securities Act except where it is part of a document incorporated by reference into a prospectus; a smaller reporting company is not required to provide the performance graph information [@CL-FPA-0213; @CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0251; @CL-FPA-0219] |

Item 201(a)(1)(i) requires the registrant to identify the principal United States market or markets and the trading symbol or symbols for each class of its common equity [@CL-FPA-0213]. Item 201(c)(2) encourages a registrant with a record of paying no cash dividends although earnings indicate an ability to do so to consider its intention to pay cash dividends in the foreseeable future and, if it has no such intention, to state that fact [@CL-FPA-0249]. Item 201(d)(1) requires the information specified in paragraph (d)(2), as of the end of the most recently completed fiscal year, for compensation plans (including individual compensation arrangements) under which equity securities of the registrant are authorized for issuance [@CL-FPA-0214]. Two listed items in the Item 201(d)(2) table are the number of securities to be issued on exercise of outstanding options, warrants and rights (column (a)) and their weighted-average exercise price (column (b)) [@CL-FPA-0215]. One more listed item is, other than securities to be issued on exercise of outstanding options, warrants and rights, the number of securities remaining available for future issuance under the plan (column (c)) [@CL-FPA-0216]. Item 201(d)(3) requires, for each compensation plan under which equity securities of the registrant are authorized for issuance that was adopted without the approval of security holders, a brief narrative description of the material features of the plan [@CL-FPA-0250]. Instruction 9 to paragraph (d) says that, except where it is part of a document that is incorporated by reference into a prospectus, the information required by paragraph (d) need not be provided in any registration statement filed under the Securities Act [@CL-FPA-0251].

Item 201(e)(1) requires a line graph comparing the yearly percentage change in the registrant's cumulative total shareholder return on a class of common stock registered under section 12 of the Exchange Act, measured by dividing the sum of the cumulative amount of dividends for the measurement period, assuming dividend reinvestment, and the difference between the registrant's share price at the end and the beginning of the measurement period, by the share price at the beginning of the measurement period, and compared with the items the paragraph lists [@CL-FPA-0217; @CL-FPA-0346; @CL-FPA-0347]. The measurement period begins at the market close on the last trading day before the beginning of the registrant's fifth preceding fiscal year and runs through the end of its last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0218]. A registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company, as defined by § 229.10(f)(1), is not required to provide the information required by paragraph (e) of Item 201 [@CL-FPA-0219]. The printed section also covers statements for markets that are not exchanges, holders, the comparison indexes, instructions on where the graph appears and instructions for foreign issuers, which these claims do not restate.

## How it works

The registrant names its markets and trading symbols [@CL-FPA-0213], is encouraged, if it has a record of paying no cash dividends although earnings indicate an ability to do so, to consider its intention to pay cash dividends in the foreseeable future and, if it has no such intention, to state that fact [@CL-FPA-0249], and presents the equity compensation plan information as of the fiscal year end, with a narrative for plans adopted without security holder approval [@CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0215; @CL-FPA-0216; @CL-FPA-0250]. Unless it is a smaller reporting company, it adds the performance graph over the measurement period [@CL-FPA-0217; @CL-FPA-0218; @CL-FPA-0219].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A calendar-year company trades on one exchange and names it with its symbol. Its plan table shows 400,000 securities to be issued on exercise at a weighted-average price of 12 dollars and 250,000 still available. Its graph starts at the market close before the fifth preceding fiscal year began and ends at the last year end, so it shows a five-year line. A smaller reporting company would skip the graph.

## Never confuse

This card is about where the stock trades and what plans can issue it. The rights and terms of the securities are in [Description of registrant's securities](fpa-description-of-registrants-securities.md), and who owns the stock is in the [security ownership card](fpa-security-ownership-and-section-16a-compliance.md). Sales of unregistered securities and issuer repurchases are different disclosures; see [Supplementary information and securities sales](fpa-supplementary-and-securities-sales-disclosure.md). The plan table also counts securities that can still be issued, not only those already granted [@CL-FPA-0215; @CL-FPA-0216].
