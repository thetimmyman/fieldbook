## Purpose

Shows what a registrant presents about where its common equity trades, its dividends, its equity compensation plans and its performance graph under Item 201 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0213; @CL-FPA-0249; @CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0217].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for its common equity and its compensation plans; the plan table need not be provided in a registration statement under the Securities Act except where it is part of a document incorporated by reference into a prospectus; a smaller reporting company is not required to provide the performance graph information [@CL-FPA-0213; @CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0251; @CL-FPA-0219] |

Item 201(a)(1)(i) requires the registrant to identify the principal United States market(s) and the corresponding trading symbol(s) for each class of the registrant's common equity [@CL-FPA-0213]. Item 201(c)(2) states that where registrants have a record of paying no cash dividends although earnings indicate an ability to do so, they are encouraged to consider the question of their intention to pay cash dividends in the foreseeable future and, if no such intention exists, to make a statement of that fact [@CL-FPA-0249]. Item 201(d)(1) requires the registrant to provide the information specified in paragraph (d)(2) as of the end of the most recently completed fiscal year with respect to compensation plans (including individual compensation arrangements) under which equity securities of the registrant are authorized for issuance, aggregated as follows: (i) all compensation plans previously approved by security holders [@CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0323], and (ii) all compensation plans not previously approved by security holders [@CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0323]. The Item 201(d)(2) table requires: (i) the number of securities to be issued upon the exercise of outstanding options, warrants and rights (column (a)) [@CL-FPA-0215], (ii) the weighted-average exercise price of the outstanding options, warrants and rights disclosed pursuant to paragraph (d)(2)(i) (column (b)) [@CL-FPA-0215], and (iii) other than securities to be issued upon the exercise of the outstanding options, warrants and rights disclosed in paragraph (d)(2)(i), the number of securities remaining available for future issuance under the plan (column (c)) [@CL-FPA-0215; @CL-FPA-0216]. Item 201(d)(3) requires, for each compensation plan under which equity securities of the registrant are authorized for issuance that was adopted without the approval of security holders, a brief description, in narrative form, of the material features of the plan [@CL-FPA-0250]. Instruction 9 to paragraph (d) states that, except where it is part of a document that is incorporated by reference into a prospectus, the information required by paragraph (d) need not be provided in any registration statement filed under the Securities Act [@CL-FPA-0251].

Item 201(e)(1) requires a line graph comparing the yearly percentage change in the registrant's cumulative total shareholder return on a class of common stock registered under section 12 of the Exchange Act (as measured by dividing the sum of the cumulative amount of dividends for the measurement period, assuming dividend reinvestment, and the difference between the registrant's share price at the end and the beginning of the measurement period, by the share price at the beginning of the measurement period) with: (i) the cumulative total return of a broad equity market index assuming reinvestment of dividends, that includes companies whose equity securities are traded on the same exchange or are of comparable market capitalization, provided, however, that if the registrant is a company within the Standard & Poor's 500 Stock Index, the registrant must use that index [@CL-FPA-0217; @CL-FPA-0346; @CL-FPA-0347; @CL-FPA-0324]; and (ii) the cumulative total return, assuming reinvestment of dividends, of: (A) a published industry or line-of-business index, (B) peer issuer(s) selected in good faith (and if the registrant does not select its peer issuer(s) on an industry or line-of-business basis, the registrant shall disclose the basis for its selection), or (C) issuer(s) with similar market capitalization(s), but only if the registrant does not use a published industry or line-of-business index and does not believe it can reasonably identify a peer group, in which case the graph shall be accompanied by a statement of the reasons for this selection [@CL-FPA-0325; @CL-FPA-0326; @CL-FPA-0327]. The measurement period is the period beginning at the measurement point established by the market close on the last trading day before the beginning of the registrant's fifth preceding fiscal year, through and including the end of the registrant's last completed fiscal year, although if the class of securities has been registered under section 12 of the Exchange Act for a shorter period of time, the period covered by the comparison may correspond to that time period [@CL-FPA-0218; @CL-FPA-1221]. A registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company, as defined by § 229.10(f)(1), is not required to provide the information required by paragraph (e) of Item 201 [@CL-FPA-0219].

## How it works

The registrant identifies its principal United States market(s) and corresponding trading symbol(s) for each class of common equity [@CL-FPA-0213], is encouraged where it has a record of paying no cash dividends although earnings indicate an ability to do so to consider its intention to pay cash dividends in the foreseeable future and state if no such intention exists [@CL-FPA-0249], and provides the equity compensation plan table as of the end of the most recently completed fiscal year (aggregated between plans previously approved by security holders and plans not previously approved by security holders) along with a brief narrative of the material features of each plan adopted without security holder approval, except in a Securities Act registration statement where not incorporated by reference into a prospectus [@CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0323; @CL-FPA-0215; @CL-FPA-0216; @CL-FPA-0250; @CL-FPA-0251]. Unless it qualifies as a smaller reporting company under § 229.10(f)(1), it provides the Item 201(e)(1) performance graph over the measurement period comparing its cumulative total shareholder return with the required broad equity market index and the index, peer group, or market-capitalization comparator [@CL-FPA-0217; @CL-FPA-0346; @CL-FPA-0347; @CL-FPA-0324; @CL-FPA-0325; @CL-FPA-0326; @CL-FPA-0327; @CL-FPA-0218; @CL-FPA-1221; @CL-FPA-0219].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A calendar-year company trades on one exchange and names it with its symbol. Its plan table shows 400,000 securities to be issued on exercise at a weighted-average price of 12 dollars and 250,000 still available. Its graph starts at the market close before the fifth preceding fiscal year began and ends at the last year end, so it shows a five-year line. A smaller reporting company would skip the graph.

## Never confuse

Item 201 covers principal United States markets and trading symbols for common equity, dividend policy encouragement, equity compensation plan disclosures, and the cumulative total shareholder return performance graph [@CL-FPA-0213; @CL-FPA-0249; @CL-FPA-0214; @CL-FPA-0217]. In the Item 201(d)(2) plan table, column (a) reports the number of securities to be issued upon the exercise of outstanding options, warrants and rights and column (b) their weighted-average exercise price, unlike column (c), which reports, other than those securities, the number of securities remaining available for future issuance under the plan [@CL-FPA-0215; @CL-FPA-0216]. For the rights and terms of registered securities, see [Description of registrant's securities](fpa-description-of-registrants-securities.md); for beneficial ownership and insider reporting compliance, see [Security ownership and insider reporting compliance](fpa-security-ownership-and-section-16a-compliance.md); for unregistered sales and issuer repurchases, see [Supplementary information and securities sales](fpa-supplementary-and-securities-sales-disclosure.md); and for awards granted to, held by, and exercised or vested by named executive officers, see [Executive equity award tables](fpa-executive-equity-award-tables.md).
