## Purpose

Shows what Item 601(b) of Regulation S-K says about the exhibit categories a finance reader uses: the underwriting agreement, the plan of acquisition, the articles and bylaws, the instruments defining the rights of security holders, material contracts, subsidiaries, consents, certifications and the Interactive Data File [@CL-FPA-1179; @CL-FPA-1180; @CL-FPA-1181; @CL-FPA-1183; @CL-FPA-1188; @CL-FPA-1193; @CL-FPA-1194; @CL-FPA-1195; @CL-FPA-1198]. How the exhibit table and index work is on the [general instructions card](fpa-exhibits-general-instructions.md).

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for the exhibit categories in Item 601(b)(1), (2), (3), (4), (10), (21), (23), (31), (32) and (101) [@CL-FPA-1179; @CL-FPA-1180; @CL-FPA-1181; @CL-FPA-1182; @CL-FPA-1183; @CL-FPA-1188; @CL-FPA-1193; @CL-FPA-1194; @CL-FPA-1195; @CL-FPA-1196; @CL-FPA-1198] |

Exhibit (b)(1) is each underwriting contract or agreement with a principal underwriter pursuant to which the securities being registered are to be distributed, or, if the terms have not been determined, the proposed forms thereof [@CL-FPA-1179]. Exhibit (b)(2) is any material plan of acquisition, disposition, reorganization, readjustment, succession, liquidation, or arrangement and any amendments thereto described in the statement or report [@CL-FPA-1180].

Exhibit (b)(3)(i) is the articles of incorporation of the registrant or instruments corresponding thereto as currently in effect and any amendments thereto, and whenever the registrant files an amendment to its articles, it must file a complete copy of the articles as amended [@CL-FPA-1181]; however, if such amendment is being reported on Form 8-K, the registrant is required to file only the text of the amendment as a Form 8-K exhibit [@CL-FPA-1213], and in such case a complete copy of the articles of incorporation as amended must be filed as an exhibit to the next Securities Act registration statement or periodic report filed by the registrant to which this exhibit requirement applies [@CL-FPA-1214]. Exhibit (b)(3)(ii) is the bylaws of the registrant or instruments corresponding thereto as currently in effect and any amendments thereto, and whenever the registrant files an amendment to the bylaws, it must file a complete copy of the amended bylaws [@CL-FPA-1182]; however, if such amendment is being reported on Form 8-K, the registrant is required to file only the text of the amendment as a Form 8-K exhibit [@CL-FPA-1215], and in such case a complete copy of the bylaws as amended must be filed as an exhibit to the next Securities Act registration statement or periodic report filed by the registrant to which this exhibit requirement applies [@CL-FPA-1216].

Exhibit (b)(4)(i) is all instruments defining the rights of holders of the equity or debt securities being registered including, where applicable, the relevant portion of the articles of incorporation or by-laws of the registrant [@CL-FPA-1183]. Where the instrument defines the rights of holders of long-term debt of the registrant and its consolidated subsidiaries and for any of its unconsolidated subsidiaries for which financial statements are required to be filed, paragraph (b)(4)(iii) lists what need not be filed [@CL-FPA-1184]. One item is any instrument with respect to long-term debt not being registered if the total amount of securities authorized thereunder does not exceed 10 percent of the total assets of the registrant and its subsidiaries on a consolidated basis and if there is filed an agreement to furnish a copy of such agreement to the Commission upon request [@CL-FPA-1185; @CL-FPA-1186]. For each class of the registrant's securities that is registered as of the end of the period covered by the report pursuant to Section 12 of the Exchange Act, exhibit (b)(4)(vi) requires the registrant to provide the information required by Item 202(a) through (d) and (f) of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-1187].

Exhibit (b)(10)(i)(A) is every contract not made in the ordinary course of business that is material to the registrant and is to be performed in whole or in part at or after the filing of the registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1188]. The only contracts that need to be filed are those to which the registrant or a subsidiary of the registrant is a party or has succeeded to a party by assumption or assignment or in which the registrant or such subsidiary has a beneficial interest [@CL-FPA-1189]. If the contract is such as ordinarily accompanies the kind of business conducted by the registrant and its subsidiaries, it will be deemed to have been made in the ordinary course of business and need not be filed unless it falls within one or more of the listed categories, in which case it shall be filed except where immaterial in amount or significance [@CL-FPA-1190; @CL-FPA-1191]. One category is any contract calling for the acquisition or sale of any property, plant or equipment for a consideration exceeding 15 percent of such fixed assets of the registrant on a consolidated basis [@CL-FPA-1192].

Exhibit (b)(21)(i) is a list of all subsidiaries of the registrant, the state or other jurisdiction of incorporation or organization of each, and the names under which such subsidiaries do business [@CL-FPA-1193]. Under exhibit (b)(23)(i) for Securities Act filings, all written consents required to be filed shall be dated and manually signed [@CL-FPA-1194]. Exhibit (b)(31)(i) is the certifications required by Rule 13a-14(a) or Rule 15d-14(a) exactly as set forth in the paragraph [@CL-FPA-1195]. Exhibit (b)(32)(i) is the certifications required by Rule 13a-14(b) or Rule 15d-14(b) and Section 1350 of Chapter 63 of Title 18 of the United States Code [@CL-FPA-1196]. A certification furnished pursuant to exhibit (b)(32) will not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Exchange Act, or otherwise subject to the liability of that section [@CL-FPA-1197].

Where a registrant prepares its financial statements in accordance with either generally accepted accounting principles as used in the United States or International Financial Reporting Standards as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board, exhibit (b)(101) concerns an Interactive Data File [@CL-FPA-1198]. It is required to be submitted to the Commission in the manner provided by § 232.405 if the registrant is not registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940, except for the cases listed after it [@CL-FPA-1199]. One listed case is that an Interactive Data File is required for a registration statement under the Securities Act only if the registration statement contains a price or price range [@CL-FPA-1200].

## How it works

Item 601(b) sets the content for each exhibit category: underwriting contracts or proposed forms [@CL-FPA-1179], material plans of acquisition or reorganization described in the statement or report [@CL-FPA-1180], articles of incorporation and bylaws (with the Form 8-K text-only exception and next-filing complete-copy requirement) [@CL-FPA-1181; @CL-FPA-1213; @CL-FPA-1214; @CL-FPA-1182; @CL-FPA-1215; @CL-FPA-1216], and instruments defining the rights of security holders (including the 10 percent consolidated-assets exclusion for unregistered long-term debt when an agreement to furnish a copy upon request is filed, and the Item 202(a)-(d) and (f) exhibit for Section 12 classes) [@CL-FPA-1183; @CL-FPA-1184; @CL-FPA-1185; @CL-FPA-1186; @CL-FPA-1187]. Contracts not made in the ordinary course of business that are material and to be performed in whole or in part at or after filing, and contracts in the listed categories (such as property, plant or equipment acquisitions or sales exceeding 15 percent of consolidated fixed assets) except where immaterial in amount or significance, are filed under exhibit (b)(10) [@CL-FPA-1188; @CL-FPA-1189; @CL-FPA-1190; @CL-FPA-1191; @CL-FPA-1192]. The subsidiary list, dated and manually signed Securities Act consents, Rule 13a-14(a)/15d-14(a) and Section 1350 certifications, and the Interactive Data File are covered by exhibits (b)(21), (23), (31), (32) and (101) [@CL-FPA-1193; @CL-FPA-1194; @CL-FPA-1195; @CL-FPA-1196; @CL-FPA-1197; @CL-FPA-1198; @CL-FPA-1199; @CL-FPA-1200].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company with total assets of 800 million dollars registers notes. It also has an unregistered long-term loan whose authorized amount is 60 million dollars, which is below 10 percent of its total assets, so it files an agreement to furnish a copy of the loan instrument to the Commission on request instead of filing the instrument. It separately signs a contract to buy a plant for an amount equal to 20 percent of its consolidated fixed assets, which is above 15 percent, so it files that contract unless it is immaterial in amount or significance.

## Never confuse

Item 601(b)(4)(i) and (vi) cover the instruments defining the rights of holders of registered equity or debt securities and the exhibit providing Item 202(a) through (d) and (f) information for each class of securities registered under Section 12 of the Exchange Act [@CL-FPA-1183; @CL-FPA-1187], and the security description rules are covered on [Description of registrant's securities](fpa-description-of-registrants-securities.md). Item 601(b)(1) covers each underwriting contract or agreement with a principal underwriter [@CL-FPA-1179]; for the prospectus disclosure of underwriting and distribution terms, see [Use of proceeds and plan of distribution](fpa-use-of-proceeds-and-plan-of-distribution.md). The exhibit table and general instructions are covered on [Exhibits: the exhibit table, the index and the general instructions](fpa-exhibits-general-instructions.md).
