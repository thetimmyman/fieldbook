## Purpose

Shows what Item 601(b) of Regulation S-K says about the exhibit categories a finance reader uses: the underwriting agreement, the plan of acquisition, the articles and bylaws, the instruments defining the rights of security holders, material contracts, subsidiaries, consents, certifications and the Interactive Data File [@CL-FPA-1179; @CL-FPA-1180; @CL-FPA-1181; @CL-FPA-1183; @CL-FPA-1188; @CL-FPA-1193; @CL-FPA-1194; @CL-FPA-1195; @CL-FPA-1198]. How the exhibit table and index work is on the [general instructions card](fpa-exhibits-general-instructions.md).

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | A registrant, for the exhibit descriptions in paragraph (b) of Item 601 that the card restates [@CL-FPA-1179; @CL-FPA-1188] |

Exhibit (b)(1) is each underwriting contract or agreement with a principal underwriter pursuant to which the securities being registered are to be distributed, or, if the terms have not been determined, the proposed forms thereof [@CL-FPA-1179]. Exhibit (b)(2) is any material plan of acquisition, disposition, reorganization, readjustment, succession, liquidation, or arrangement and any amendments thereto described in the statement or report [@CL-FPA-1180].

Exhibit (b)(3)(i) is the articles of incorporation or corresponding instruments as currently in effect and any amendments, and whenever the registrant files an amendment to its articles, it must file a complete copy of the articles as amended [@CL-FPA-1181]. Exhibit (b)(3)(ii) is the bylaws on the same basis, and whenever the registrant files an amendment to the bylaws, it must file a complete copy of the amended bylaws [@CL-FPA-1182].

Exhibit (b)(4)(i) is all instruments defining the rights of holders of the equity or debt securities being registered including, where applicable, the relevant portion of the articles of incorporation or by-laws of the registrant [@CL-FPA-1183]. For instruments defining the rights of holders of long-term debt of the registrant and its consolidated subsidiaries, and of unconsolidated subsidiaries for which financial statements are required, paragraph (b)(4)(iii) lists what need not be filed [@CL-FPA-1184]. One item is any instrument for long-term debt not being registered if the total amount of securities authorized thereunder does not exceed 10 percent of the total assets of the registrant and its subsidiaries on a consolidated basis [@CL-FPA-1185]. That exclusion also depends on there being filed an agreement to furnish a copy of such agreement to the Commission upon request [@CL-FPA-1186]. For each class of securities registered under Section 12 of the Exchange Act, exhibit (b)(4)(vi) calls for the information required by Item 202(a) through (d) and (f) of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-1187].

Exhibit (b)(10)(i)(A) is every contract not made in the ordinary course of business that is material to the registrant and is to be performed in whole or in part at or after the filing of the registration statement or report [@CL-FPA-1188]. The only contracts that need to be filed are those to which the registrant or a subsidiary is a party or has succeeded to a party by assumption or assignment, or in which the registrant or such subsidiary has a beneficial interest [@CL-FPA-1189]. A contract that ordinarily accompanies the kind of business conducted is deemed made in the ordinary course of business and need not be filed unless it falls within one or more of the item's listed categories [@CL-FPA-1190]. A contract in one of those categories shall be filed except where immaterial in amount or significance [@CL-FPA-1191]. One category is any contract for the acquisition or sale of property, plant or equipment for a consideration exceeding 15 percent of such fixed assets of the registrant on a consolidated basis [@CL-FPA-1192].

Exhibit (b)(21)(i) is a list of all subsidiaries, the state or other jurisdiction of incorporation or organization of each, and the names under which they do business [@CL-FPA-1193]. Under exhibit (b)(23)(i) for Securities Act filings, all written consents required to be filed shall be dated and manually signed [@CL-FPA-1194]. Exhibit (b)(31)(i) is the certifications required by Rule 13a-14(a) or Rule 15d-14(a), exactly as set forth in the text that follows it [@CL-FPA-1195]. Exhibit (b)(32)(i) is the certifications required by Rule 13a-14(b) or Rule 15d-14(b) and Section 1350 of Chapter 63 of Title 18 of the United States Code [@CL-FPA-1196]. A certification furnished pursuant to exhibit (b)(32) will not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Exchange Act, or otherwise subject to the liability of that section [@CL-FPA-1197].

Where a registrant prepares its financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles as used in the United States or International Financial Reporting Standards as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board, exhibit (b)(101) concerns an Interactive Data File [@CL-FPA-1198]. It is required to be submitted to the Commission in the manner provided by § 232.405 if the registrant is not registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940, except for the cases listed after it [@CL-FPA-1199]. One listed case is that it is required for a registration statement under the Securities Act only if the registration statement contains a price or price range [@CL-FPA-1200].

The card leaves out the other exhibit categories: the legality and tax opinions, the voting trust agreement, the reports to security holders, the code of ethics and insider trading policies, the accountant and director letters, the subsidiary guarantor list, the power of attorney, the trustee eligibility statement, the asset-backed servicing exhibits, the mine safety, technical report, compensation recovery and de-SPAC exhibits, the cover page data file, the static pool and the filing fee table. Within the categories it covers, it leaves out the redaction rules, the text of the certifications, the compensation plan rules, the newly reporting registrant rules, the other debt and trust indenture rules, the significant subsidiary omission, the other Interactive Data File cases and the remaining material contract categories.

## How it works

A finance reader finds the distribution terms in the underwriting agreement [@CL-FPA-1179], the transaction in the plan of acquisition [@CL-FPA-1180], the issuer's charter and bylaws [@CL-FPA-1181; @CL-FPA-1182] and the terms of its equity and debt in the defining instruments [@CL-FPA-1183; @CL-FPA-1184]. Contracts outside the ordinary course that are material, and contracts in the listed categories, are the filed material contracts [@CL-FPA-1188; @CL-FPA-1190; @CL-FPA-1191]. The subsidiary list, the consents and the certifications sit beside them [@CL-FPA-1193; @CL-FPA-1194; @CL-FPA-1195], and the Interactive Data File carries the financial statement data [@CL-FPA-1198; @CL-FPA-1199].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company with total assets of 800 million dollars registers notes. It also has an unregistered long-term loan whose authorized amount is 60 million dollars, which is below 10 percent of its total assets, so it files an agreement to furnish a copy of the loan instrument to the Commission on request instead of filing the instrument. It separately signs a contract to buy a plant for an amount equal to 20 percent of its consolidated fixed assets, which is above 15 percent, so it files that contract unless it is immaterial in amount or significance.

## Never confuse

An exhibit is a document filed with the registration statement or report; the disclosure that describes it sits in the body of the filing, for example the terms of the securities in [Description of registrant's securities](fpa-description-of-registrants-securities.md) and the underwriting arrangements in [Use of proceeds and plan of distribution](fpa-use-of-proceeds-and-plan-of-distribution.md). The list of subsidiaries is not a financial statement of any subsidiary. The table and index rules are on the [general instructions card](fpa-exhibits-general-instructions.md).
