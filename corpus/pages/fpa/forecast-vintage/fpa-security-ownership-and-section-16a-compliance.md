## Purpose

Shows who is listed in the beneficial ownership tables under Item 403 of Regulation S-K and which late Section 16(a) reports are disclosed under Item 405 [@CL-FPA-0192; @CL-FPA-0193; @CL-FPA-0196; @CL-FPA-0197].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for holders of more than five percent of a class of voting securities and for its directors and executive officers; Item 405 applies to a registrant with a class of equity securities registered under Section 12 and to closed-end investment companies [@CL-FPA-0192; @CL-FPA-0193; @CL-FPA-0195] |

Item 403(a) applies to any person (including any “group” as that term is used in section 13(d)(3) of the Exchange Act) who is known to the registrant to be the beneficial owner of more than five percent of any class of the registrant's voting securities [@CL-FPA-0192]. Item 403(b) covers each class of equity securities of the registrant or any of its parents or subsidiaries, other than directors' qualifying shares, beneficially owned by all directors and nominees, naming them, each of the named executive officers as defined in Item 402(a)(3), and all directors and executive officers of the registrant as a group, without naming them [@CL-FPA-0193]. It also requires the registrant to indicate, by footnote or otherwise, the amount of shares that are pledged as security and the amount of shares with respect to which such persons have the right to acquire beneficial ownership as specified in Rule 13d-3(d)(1) under the Exchange Act [@CL-FPA-0194]. Item 403(c) requires the registrant to describe any arrangements, known to the registrant, including any pledge by any person of securities of the registrant or any of its parents, the operation of which may at a subsequent date result in a change in control of the registrant [@CL-FPA-0236].

Item 405(a) is addressed to every registrant having a class of equity securities registered pursuant to Section 12 of the Exchange Act and every closed-end investment company registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940 [@CL-FPA-0195]. Item 405(a)(1) requires the registrant to identify each person who, at any time during the fiscal year, was a director, officer, beneficial owner of more than ten percent of any class of equity securities of the registrant registered pursuant to Section 12 of the Exchange Act, or any other person subject to Section 16 of the Exchange Act with respect to the registrant because of the requirements of Section 30 of the Investment Company Act (each a “reporting person”) that failed to file on a timely basis reports required by Section 16(a) of the Exchange Act during the most recent fiscal year or prior fiscal years [@CL-FPA-0196; @CL-FPA-0345; @CL-FPA-0197]. For each such person, Item 405(a)(2) requires the registrant to set forth the number of late reports, the number of transactions that were not reported on a timely basis, and any known failure to file a required Form [@CL-FPA-0198]. Instruction 2 to paragraph (a) says the registrant is only required to disclose a failure to file timely once [@CL-FPA-0238]. Under Item 405(b), in determining whether disclosure is required pursuant to paragraph (a), the registrant may rely only on the following: (1) a review of Forms 3 and 4 and amendments thereto filed electronically with the Commission during the registrant's most recent fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0237; @CL-FPA-0350], (2) a review of Forms 5 and amendments thereto filed electronically with the Commission with respect to the registrant's most recent fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0330], and (3) any written representation from the reporting person that no Form 5 is required [@CL-FPA-0237; @CL-FPA-0350; @CL-FPA-0330].

## How it works

Under Item 403, the registrant reports beneficial owners known to it of more than five percent of any class of its voting securities [@CL-FPA-0192], reports equity securities of the registrant or any of its parents or subsidiaries (other than directors' qualifying shares) beneficially owned by all directors and nominees, each named executive officer, and all directors and executive officers as a group (indicating pledged shares and Rule 13d-3(d)(1) right-to-acquire shares) [@CL-FPA-0193; @CL-FPA-0194], and describes any known arrangement, including any pledge, whose operation may at a subsequent date result in a change in control [@CL-FPA-0236]. Under Item 405, a registrant with Section 12 registered equity securities or a registered closed-end investment company relies only on its review of electronically filed Forms 3, 4 and 5 (and amendments) for the most recent fiscal year and any written representation from the reporting person that no Form 5 is required to identify each reporting person that failed to file timely Section 16(a) reports, and sets forth the number of late reports, the number of untimely reported transactions, and any known failure to file a required Form, disclosing a failure to file timely only once [@CL-FPA-0195; @CL-FPA-0196; @CL-FPA-0345; @CL-FPA-0197; @CL-FPA-0198; @CL-FPA-0238; @CL-FPA-0237; @CL-FPA-0350; @CL-FPA-0330].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A holder owns 6 percent of a class of voting securities and is listed in the ownership table because the holding is above five percent. A holder with 4 percent is not listed under that test. A director filed one report late during the year for one purchase. The registrant discloses one late report and one transaction for that director.

## Never confuse

Item 403(a) covers any person (including any group) known to the registrant to be the beneficial owner of more than five percent of any class of the registrant's voting securities [@CL-FPA-0192], while Item 403(b) covers equity securities of the registrant or any of its parents or subsidiaries (other than directors' qualifying shares) beneficially owned by all directors and nominees, each named executive officer, and all directors and executive officers as a group [@CL-FPA-0193], and Item 405(a)(1) covers each person who at any time during the fiscal year was a director, officer, beneficial owner of more than ten percent of any class of equity securities registered pursuant to Section 12, or other person subject to Section 16 because of Section 30 of the Investment Company Act [@CL-FPA-0196; @CL-FPA-0345]. For related-person transactions, see [Transactions with related persons](fpa-related-person-transactions.md); for directors, executive officers, promoters and control persons, see [Directors, executive officers, promoters and control persons](fpa-directors-officers-and-control-persons.md); for executive and director compensation, see [Executive compensation scope, discussion and summary compensation table](fpa-executive-compensation-scope-and-summary-compensation-table.md), [Executive equity award tables](fpa-executive-equity-award-tables.md), [Pension, deferred compensation, termination payments and director pay](fpa-pension-deferred-compensation-termination-and-director-pay.md), [Pay ratio disclosure](fpa-pay-ratio-disclosure.md) and [Pay versus performance disclosure](fpa-pay-versus-performance-disclosure.md).
