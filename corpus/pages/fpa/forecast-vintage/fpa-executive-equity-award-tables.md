## Purpose

Shows how a registrant reports plan-based awards granted, awards outstanding at fiscal year end, and option exercises and stock vesting for its named executive officers under Item 402 of Regulation S-K, with the narrative that explains them [@CL-FPA-1075; @CL-FPA-1082; @CL-FPA-1089; @CL-FPA-1081].

| Applies to | A registrant, for each named executive officer, for awards granted, outstanding at the end of the last completed fiscal year, exercised or vested [@CL-FPA-1075; @CL-FPA-1082; @CL-FPA-1089; @CL-FPA-1090] |

The grants of plan-based awards table gives the information specified in paragraph (d)(2) about each grant of an award made to a named executive officer in the last completed fiscal year under any plan, including awards that subsequently have been transferred [@CL-FPA-1075]. Disclosure on a separate line is to be provided for each grant of an award made during the fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1078]. The table shows the dollar value of the estimated future payout upon satisfaction of the conditions in question under non-equity incentive plan awards granted in the fiscal year, or the applicable range of estimated payouts denominated in dollars (threshold, target and maximum amount) [@CL-FPA-1076], and the grant date fair value of each equity award computed under FASB ASC Topic 718 [@CL-FPA-1077]. In the grants table, threshold is the minimum amount payable for a certain level of performance under the plan, and target is the amount payable if the specified performance targets are reached [@CL-FPA-1079]. Maximum is the maximum payout possible under the plan [@CL-FPA-1080].

A narrative description is to be provided of any material factors necessary to an understanding of the information in the tables required by paragraphs (c) and (d) [@CL-FPA-1081].

The outstanding equity awards table covers unexercised options, stock that has not vested and equity incentive plan awards for each named executive officer outstanding as of the end of the last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1082]. It reports, on an award-by-award basis, the number of securities underlying unexercised options, including awards that have been transferred other than for value, that are exercisable in column (b) and that are unexercisable in column (c) and that are not reported in column (d) [@CL-FPA-1083; @CL-FPA-1084], and, for each instrument reported in columns (b), (c) and (d), as applicable, the exercise or base price in column (e) and the expiration date in column (f) [@CL-FPA-1085]. The vesting dates of options, shares of stock and equity incentive plan awards held at fiscal-year end must be disclosed by footnote to the applicable column where the outstanding award has been reported [@CL-FPA-1086]. The market value of stock reported in column (h) and of equity incentive plan awards of stock reported in column (j) is computed by multiplying the closing market price of the registrant's stock at the end of the last completed fiscal year by the number of shares or units of stock or the amount of equity incentive plan awards, respectively [@CL-FPA-1087; @CL-FPA-1088].

The option exercises and stock vested table covers, on an aggregated basis, each exercise of stock options, SARs and similar instruments, and each vesting of stock, including restricted stock, restricted stock units and similar instruments, during the last completed fiscal year for each of the named executive officers [@CL-FPA-1089; @CL-FPA-1090]. The dollar Value Realized on Exercise (column (c)) and the dollar Value Realized on Vesting (column (e)) are calculated by determining, for an exercise, the difference between the market price of the underlying securities at exercise and the exercise or base price of the options, and for vesting, by multiplying the number of shares of stock or units by the market value of the underlying shares on the vesting date [@CL-FPA-1091; @CL-FPA-1092].

## How it works

The registrant provides a separate line in the grants of plan-based awards table for each grant of an award made to a named executive officer during the fiscal year under any plan (including awards that subsequently have been transferred), showing estimated future payouts for non-equity incentive plan awards and the grant date fair value of each equity award computed in accordance with FASB ASC Topic 718 [@CL-FPA-1075; @CL-FPA-1078; @CL-FPA-1076; @CL-FPA-1077]. It reports unexercised options, unvested stock and equity incentive plan awards outstanding at the end of the last completed fiscal year, multiplying the closing market price at year end by the number of shares or units or the amount of equity incentive plan awards for columns (h) and (j) [@CL-FPA-1082; @CL-FPA-1087; @CL-FPA-1088], and reports option exercises and stock vesting during the year on an aggregated basis with the dollar values realized [@CL-FPA-1089; @CL-FPA-1090; @CL-FPA-1091; @CL-FPA-1092]. It also provides a narrative description of any material factors necessary to an understanding of the information disclosed in the tables required by paragraphs (c) and (d) [@CL-FPA-1081].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A named executive officer receives one option grant and one stock grant in the year, so the grants table has two lines. At year end the outstanding table shows 1,000 exercisable options and 500 unexercisable options, each with its exercise price and expiration date, and 200 unvested shares. With a closing price of 40 dollars at year end, the market value of the unvested shares is 8,000 dollars. During the year the officer exercised 300 options when the market price was 10 dollars above the exercise price, so the amount realized is 3,000 dollars.

## Never confuse

The tables in paragraphs (d), (f) and (g) of Item 402 cover grants of plan-based awards, outstanding equity awards at fiscal year-end, and option exercises and stock vested for named executive officers [@CL-FPA-1075; @CL-FPA-1082; @CL-FPA-1089]. Compensation plans under which equity securities of the registrant are authorized for issuance are covered on [Market price, dividends and equity compensation plans](fpa-market-price-dividends-and-equity-compensation-plans.md). The Summary Compensation Table reports in column (e), for awards of stock, and in column (f), for awards of options with or without tandem SARs (including awards that subsequently have been transferred), the aggregate grant date fair value computed in accordance with FASB ASC Topic 718 [@CL-FPA-1067; @CL-FPA-1068], as covered on [Executive compensation scope, discussion and summary compensation table](fpa-executive-compensation-scope-and-summary-compensation-table.md). Retirement, nonqualified deferred compensation, termination payments and director compensation are covered in [Pension, deferred compensation, termination payments and director pay](fpa-pension-deferred-compensation-termination-and-director-pay.md).
