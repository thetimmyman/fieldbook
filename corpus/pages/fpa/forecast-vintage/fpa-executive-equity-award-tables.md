## Purpose

Shows how a registrant reports plan-based awards granted, awards outstanding at fiscal year end, and option exercises and stock vesting for its named executive officers under Item 402 of Regulation S-K, with the narrative that explains them [@CL-FPA-1075; @CL-FPA-1082; @CL-FPA-1089; @CL-FPA-1081].

## The rule

| Applies to | A registrant, for each named executive officer, for awards granted, outstanding at the end of the last completed fiscal year, exercised or vested [@CL-FPA-1075; @CL-FPA-1082; @CL-FPA-1089; @CL-FPA-1090] |

The grants of plan-based awards table gives the information specified in paragraph (d)(2) about each grant of an award made to a named executive officer in the last completed fiscal year under any plan, including awards that subsequently have been transferred [@CL-FPA-1075]. Disclosure on a separate line is to be provided for each grant of an award made during the fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1078]. The table shows the dollar value of the estimated future payout upon satisfaction of the conditions in question under non-equity incentive plan awards granted in the fiscal year, or the applicable range of estimated payouts denominated in dollars (threshold, target and maximum amount) [@CL-FPA-1076], and the grant date fair value of each equity award computed under FASB ASC Topic 718 [@CL-FPA-1077]. In the grants table, threshold is the minimum amount payable for a certain level of performance under the plan, and target is the amount payable if the specified performance targets are reached [@CL-FPA-1079]. Maximum is the maximum payout possible under the plan [@CL-FPA-1080].

A narrative description is to be provided of any material factors necessary to an understanding of the information in the tables required by paragraphs (c) and (d) [@CL-FPA-1081].

The outstanding equity awards table covers unexercised options, stock that has not vested and equity incentive plan awards for each named executive officer outstanding as of the end of the last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1082]. It reports, award by award, the number of securities underlying exercisable options in column (b) and unexercisable options in column (c) that are not reported in column (d) [@CL-FPA-1083; @CL-FPA-1084], and, for each instrument reported in columns (b), (c) and (d), as applicable, the exercise or base price and the expiration date [@CL-FPA-1085]. The vesting dates of options, shares of stock and equity incentive plan awards held at fiscal-year end must be disclosed by footnote to the applicable column [@CL-FPA-1086]. The market value of stock in column (h) and of equity incentive plan awards of stock in column (j) is computed by multiplying the closing market price of the registrant's stock at the end of the last completed fiscal year by a number [@CL-FPA-1087]. That number is the shares or units of stock for column (h) or the amount of equity incentive plan awards for column (j), respectively [@CL-FPA-1088].

The option exercises and stock vested table covers each exercise of stock options, SARs and similar instruments, and each vesting of stock, including restricted stock, restricted stock units and similar instruments [@CL-FPA-1089]. It covers the last completed fiscal year for each named executive officer on an aggregated basis [@CL-FPA-1090]. The amount realized on exercise is the difference between the market price of the underlying securities at exercise and the exercise or base price of the options [@CL-FPA-1091]. The amount realized on vesting is the number of shares of stock or units multiplied by the market value of the underlying shares on the vesting date [@CL-FPA-1092].

The printed item also gives the share columns and extra columns of the grants table, repricing and modification rules, tandem grants, performance-condition valuation, transferred awards, aggregation of awards, the rules for performance awards in the outstanding table and the footnote on deferred amounts, which these claims do not restate.

## How it works

The registrant lists each award granted in the year [@CL-FPA-1078], lists what is outstanding at year end, valued at the closing market price at that date where the item calls for it [@CL-FPA-1082; @CL-FPA-1087], and lists what was exercised or vested during the year with the amounts realized [@CL-FPA-1089; @CL-FPA-1091; @CL-FPA-1092]. The narrative then explains the material factors [@CL-FPA-1081].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A named executive officer receives one option grant and one stock grant in the year, so the grants table has two lines. At year end the outstanding table shows 1,000 exercisable options and 500 unexercisable options, each with its exercise price and expiration date, and 200 unvested shares. With a closing price of 40 dollars at year end, the market value of the unvested shares is 8,000 dollars. During the year the officer exercised 300 options when the market price was 10 dollars above the exercise price, so the amount realized is 3,000 dollars.

## Never confuse

These tables cover awards to named executive officers. The plans under which equity can be issued to anyone are summarized in [Market price, dividends and equity compensation plans](fpa-market-price-dividends-and-equity-compensation-plans.md). The Summary Compensation Table reports the grant date fair value of stock and option awards [@CL-FPA-1067; @CL-FPA-1068]; see [Executive compensation scope, discussion and summary compensation table](fpa-executive-compensation-scope-and-summary-compensation-table.md). Retirement and deferral plans are in [Pension, deferred compensation, termination payments and director pay](fpa-pension-deferred-compensation-termination-and-director-pay.md).
