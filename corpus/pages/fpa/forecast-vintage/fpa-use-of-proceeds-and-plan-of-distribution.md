## Purpose

Shows what a registrant discloses about the use of offering proceeds, including proceeds used to acquire assets or businesses, and about underwriting, under Items 504 and 508 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0226; @CL-FPA-0227; @CL-FPA-0230; @CL-FPA-0231].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Not established. |
| Codified citation | Not established. |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Not established. |
| Applies to | A registrant, for the net proceeds from securities to be offered and, for the underwriting disclosure, securities offered through underwriters [@CL-FPA-0226; @CL-FPA-0230] |

Item 504 requires the registrant to state the principal purposes for which the net proceeds from the securities to be offered are intended to be used and the approximate amount intended for each purpose [@CL-FPA-0226]. Where the registrant has no current specific plan for the proceeds, or a significant portion of them, Item 504 says it shall so state and discuss the principal reasons for the offering [@CL-FPA-0254]. If any material amount of the proceeds is to be used to acquire assets, otherwise than in the ordinary course of business, Instruction 5 to Item 504 calls for a brief description and the cost of the assets and, where the assets are to be acquired from affiliates of the registrant or their associates, the names of the persons from whom they are to be acquired and the principle followed in determining the cost to the registrant [@CL-FPA-0227; @CL-FPA-0349]. Instruction 6 to Item 504 applies where the registrant indicates that the proceeds may, or will, be used to finance acquisitions of other businesses [@CL-FPA-0228]. It says the identity of such businesses if known, otherwise the nature of the businesses to be sought, the status of any negotiations with respect to the acquisition, and a brief description of such business shall be included [@CL-FPA-0229]. Instruction 6 also has a further provision for the case where pro forma financial statements reflecting the acquisition are not required by Regulation S-X to be included in the registration statement [@CL-FPA-0255]. Where pro forma financial statements are not required to be included, it says the possible terms of any transaction, the identification of the parties thereto or the nature of the business sought need not be disclosed, to the extent that the registrant reasonably determines that public disclosure of such information would jeopardize the acquisition [@CL-FPA-0255; @CL-FPA-0256]. The printed instruction also calls for a more detailed description where financial statements of the business to be acquired are required, and the other printed instructions give rules on priority of uses, other funds, debt and affiliates, which these claims do not restate.

If the securities are to be offered through underwriters, Item 508(a) requires the registrant to name the principal underwriters, state the amounts each underwrites, and identify each underwriter with a material relationship to the registrant and the nature of that relationship [@CL-FPA-0230]. Item 508(e) requires a table setting out the nature of the compensation and the amount of discounts and commissions to be paid to the underwriter for each security and in total, showing separately the amounts paid by the company and the selling shareholders [@CL-FPA-0231]. The printed plan of distribution section has further paragraphs, including new underwriters, other distributions, stabilization and indemnification, which these claims do not restate.

Other registration statement items sit beside these. Item 505(a) applies where common equity is being registered for which there is no established public trading market for purposes of paragraph (a) of Item 201 of Regulation S-K (§ 229.201(a)) [@CL-FPA-1002]. It also applies where there is a material disparity between the offering price of the common equity being registered and the market price of outstanding shares of the same class, and in either case the registrant is to describe the various factors considered in determining the offering price [@CL-FPA-1003].

If any of the securities to be registered are to be offered for the account of security holders, Item 507 says each such security holder is to be named [@CL-FPA-1004]. For each such holder the filing is also to indicate the nature of any position, office or other material relationship within the past three years with the registrant or any of its predecessors or affiliates [@CL-FPA-1005], and to state the amount of securities of the class owned before the offering, the amount to be offered for the holder's account, and the amount and (if one percent or more) the percentage of the class to be owned after completion of the offering [@CL-FPA-1006].

Item 511 calls for a reasonably itemized statement of all expenses in connection with the issuance and distribution of the securities to be registered, other than underwriting discounts and commissions [@CL-FPA-1007]. If any of the securities to be registered are to be offered for the account of security holders, the portion of those expenses to be borne by such security holder is to be indicated [@CL-FPA-1008]. Item 702 calls for a statement of the general effect of any statute, charter provisions, by-laws, contract or other arrangements under which any controlling persons, director or officer of the registrant is insured or indemnified in any manner against liability that the person may incur in that capacity [@CL-FPA-1009].

Item 509 says that, when the conditions that precede it in the item are met, a brief statement of the nature of the contingent basis, interest or connection is to be furnished [@CL-FPA-1010]. Instruction 1 to Item 509 says the interest of an expert other than an accountant, or of counsel, will not be deemed substantial and need not be disclosed if the interest, including the fair market value of all securities of the registrant owned, received and to be received, meets a further condition [@CL-FPA-1011; @CL-FPA-1012]. That condition is that the interest, including securities subject to options, warrants or rights received or to be received by the expert or counsel, does not exceed $50,000 [@CL-FPA-1012]. For this instruction, expert or counsel includes the firm, corporation, partnership or other entity that employs the expert or counsel or of which the expert or counsel is a member or of counsel, all attorneys in the case of counsel, and all nonclerical personnel in the case of named experts who participate in the matter on behalf of that firm or entity, so the $50,000 test covers their interests too [@CL-FPA-1019; @CL-FPA-1020]. Instruction 2 says accountants providing a report on the financial statements should note § 210.2-01 of Regulation S-X, which discusses disqualifying interests [@CL-FPA-1013].

Item 510 applies, in addition to the disclosure Item 702 prescribes, when two conditions both hold: the undertaking required by paragraph (h) of Item 512 is not required to be included in the registration statement because acceleration of the effective date is not being requested, and waivers comparable to those specified in paragraph (h) have not been obtained [@CL-FPA-1014; @CL-FPA-1015]. When both hold, it calls for a brief description of the indemnification provisions relating to directors, officers and controlling persons of the registrant against liability arising under the Securities Act [@CL-FPA-1015; @CL-FPA-1016]. That description includes any underwriting-agreement provision on the registrant's indemnification of the underwriter or its controlling persons against those liabilities where a director, officer or controlling person of the registrant is such an underwriter or controlling person of one, or a member of a firm that is such an underwriter [@CL-FPA-1021; @CL-FPA-1022]. The description shall be included in the prospectus, together with a statement in substantially the following form [@CL-FPA-1016; @CL-FPA-1017]. That statement says the registrant has been informed that in the opinion of the Securities and Exchange Commission such indemnification is against public policy as expressed in the Act and is therefore unenforceable [@CL-FPA-1017; @CL-FPA-1018].

## How it works

The registrant states each principal purpose of the proceeds with an approximate amount [@CL-FPA-0226], or says it has no current specific plan and discusses the principal reasons for the offering [@CL-FPA-0254]. If any material amount of the proceeds is to be used to acquire assets, or if the registrant indicates that the proceeds may, or will, be used to finance acquisitions of other businesses, it adds the listed descriptions [@CL-FPA-0227; @CL-FPA-0228; @CL-FPA-0229]. If underwriters are used, it names them, states their amounts and relationships, and shows their compensation [@CL-FPA-0230; @CL-FPA-0231].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company plans a 200 million dollar offering. It states that 150 million will buy a plant and gives a brief description and the cost. It states that 50 million may finance acquisitions of other businesses, names a target if one is known and gives the status of talks. It names its lead underwriter with the amount underwritten and shows the underwriting discount in the compensation table.

## Never confuse

Use of proceeds says what the money will be spent on; it is not the liquidity discussion in the management discussion, see [Management discussion objective and liquidity](../planning-chain/fpa-mdna-objective-and-liquidity.md). The terms of the securities offered are described in [Description of registrant's securities](fpa-description-of-registrants-securities.md). It also does not decide when separate financial statements of an acquired business are filed; see [Financial statements of acquired businesses](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-acquired-business-financial-statements.md). The undertakings that a registrant includes in the registration statement are on [Undertakings in a registration statement](fpa-registration-undertakings.md).
