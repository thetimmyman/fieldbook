## Purpose

Shows what a registrant discloses about the use of offering proceeds, including proceeds used to acquire assets or businesses, and about underwriting, under Items 504 and 508 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0226; @CL-FPA-0227; @CL-FPA-0230; @CL-FPA-0231].

Item 504 requires the registrant to state the principal purposes for which the net proceeds to the registrant from the securities to be offered are intended to be used and the approximate amount intended to be used for each such purpose [@CL-FPA-0226]. Where the registrant has no current specific plan for the proceeds, or a significant portion thereof, the registrant shall so state and discuss the principal reasons for the offering [@CL-FPA-0254]. If any material amount of the proceeds is to be used to acquire assets, otherwise than in the ordinary course of business, Instruction 5 to Item 504 calls for a brief description and the cost of the assets and, where the assets are to be acquired from affiliates of the registrant or their associates, the names of the persons from whom they are to be acquired and the principle followed in determining the cost to the registrant [@CL-FPA-0227; @CL-FPA-0349]. Instruction 6 to Item 504 applies where the registrant indicates that the proceeds may, or will, be used to finance acquisitions of other businesses [@CL-FPA-0228]. It requires the identity of such businesses, if known, or, if not known, the nature of the businesses to be sought, the status of any negotiations with respect to the acquisition, and a brief description of such business to be included [@CL-FPA-0229]. Where Regulation S-X, including § 210.8-04 (Rule 8-04 of Regulation S-X) for smaller reporting companies, as applicable, would require financial statements of the business to be acquired to be included, the description of the business to be acquired shall be more detailed [@CL-FPA-0333]. Instruction 6 further provides that where pro forma financial statements reflecting such acquisition are not required by Regulation S-X (including Rule 8-05 for smaller reporting companies) to be included in the registration statement, the possible terms of any transaction, the identification of the parties thereto or the nature of the business sought need not be disclosed, to the extent that the registrant reasonably determines that public disclosure of such information would jeopardize the acquisition [@CL-FPA-0255; @CL-FPA-0256].

If the securities are to be offered through underwriters, Item 508(a) requires the registrant to give the names of the principal underwriters, the respective amounts underwritten, and to identify each such underwriter having a material relationship with the registrant and state the nature of the relationship [@CL-FPA-0230]. Item 508(e) requires a table setting out the nature of the compensation and the amount of discounts and commissions to be paid to the underwriter for each security and in total, showing separately the amounts paid by the company and the selling shareholders [@CL-FPA-0231].

Item 505(a) applies where common equity is being registered for which there is no established public trading market for purposes of paragraph (a) of Item 201 of Regulation S-K (§ 229.201(a)) or where there is a material disparity between the offering price of the common equity being registered and the market price of outstanding shares of the same class, and requires the registrant to describe the various factors considered in determining such offering price [@CL-FPA-1002; @CL-FPA-1003].

If any of the securities to be registered are to be offered for the account of security holders, Item 507 requires the filing to name each such security holder [@CL-FPA-1004], indicate the nature of any position, office, or other material relationship which the selling security holder has had within the past three years with the registrant or any of its predecessors or affiliates [@CL-FPA-1005], and state the amount of securities of the class owned by such security holder prior to the offering, the amount to be offered for the security holder's account, the amount and (if one percent or more) the percentage of the class to be owned by such security holder after completion of the offering [@CL-FPA-1006].

Item 511 calls for a reasonably itemized statement of all expenses in connection with the issuance and distribution of the securities to be registered, other than underwriting discounts and commissions [@CL-FPA-1007]. If any of the securities to be registered are to be offered for the account of security holders, the filing is to indicate the portion of such expenses to be borne by such security holder [@CL-FPA-1008]. Item 702 requires the registrant to state the general effect of any statute, charter provisions, by-laws, contract or other arrangements under which any controlling persons, director or officer of the registrant is insured or indemnified in any manner against liability which he may incur in his capacity as such [@CL-FPA-1009].

Under Item 509, if (a) any expert named in the registration statement as having prepared or certified any part thereof (or named as having prepared or certified a report or valuation for use in connection with the registration statement), or (b) counsel for the registrant, underwriters or selling security holders named in the prospectus as having given an opinion upon the validity of the securities being registered or upon other legal matters in connection with the registration or offering of such securities, was employed for such purpose on a contingent basis, or at the time of such preparation, certification or opinion or at any time thereafter, through the date of effectiveness of the registration statement or that part of the registration statement to which such preparation, certification or opinion relates, had, or is to receive in connection with the offering, a substantial interest, direct or indirect, in the registrant or any of its parents or subsidiaries or was connected with the registrant or any of its parents or subsidiaries as a promoter, managing underwriter (or any principal underwriter, if there are no managing underwriters), voting trustee, director, officer, or employee, the registrant must furnish a brief statement of the nature of such contingent basis, interest, or connection [@CL-FPA-0334; @CL-FPA-0335; @CL-FPA-0336; @CL-FPA-0337; @CL-FPA-0338; @CL-FPA-1010]. Instruction 1 to Item 509 says the interest of an expert (other than an accountant) or counsel will not be deemed substantial and need not be disclosed if the interest, including the fair market value of all securities of the registrant owned, received and to be received, or subject to options, warrants or rights received or to be received, does not exceed $50,000 [@CL-FPA-1011; @CL-FPA-1012]. For the purpose of Instruction 1, the term expert or counsel includes the firm, corporation, partnership or other entity, if any, by which such expert or counsel is employed or of which he is a member or of counsel to and all attorneys in the case of counsel, and all nonclerical personnel in the case of named experts, participating in such matter on behalf of such firm, corporation, partnership or entity [@CL-FPA-1019; @CL-FPA-1020]. Instruction 2 says accountants providing a report on the financial statements should note § 210.2-01 of Regulation S-X for a discussion of the circumstances under which accountants are not deemed to be independent [@CL-FPA-1013].

Item 510 applies, in addition to the disclosure prescribed by Item 702, if the undertaking required by paragraph (h) of Item 512 is not required to be included in the registration statement because acceleration of the effective date of the registration statement is not being requested, and if waivers have not been obtained comparable to those specified in paragraph (h) [@CL-FPA-1234; @CL-FPA-1014; @CL-FPA-1015]. When both conditions hold, a brief description of the indemnification provisions relating to directors, officers and controlling persons of the registrant against liability arising under the Securities Act, including any provision of the underwriting agreement which relates to indemnification of the underwriter or its controlling persons by the registrant against such liabilities where a director, officer or controlling person of the registrant is such an underwriter or controlling person thereof or a member of any firm which is such an underwriter, shall be included in the prospectus, together with a statement in substantially the prescribed form that insofar as indemnification for liabilities arising under the Securities Act of 1933 may be permitted to directors, officers or persons controlling the registrant pursuant to the foregoing provisions, the registrant has been informed that in the opinion of the Securities and Exchange Commission such indemnification is against public policy as expressed in the Act and is therefore unenforceable [@CL-FPA-1015; @CL-FPA-1021; @CL-FPA-1022; @CL-FPA-1016; @CL-FPA-1017; @CL-FPA-1018].

## How it works

Under Item 504, the registrant states the principal purposes for which the net proceeds are intended to be used and the approximate amount for each purpose (or states that it has no current specific plan and discusses the principal reasons for the offering), and provides the Instruction 5 and Instruction 6 disclosures when a material amount of proceeds is to be used to acquire assets outside the ordinary course or when the registrant indicates that proceeds may or will be used to finance acquisitions of other businesses [@CL-FPA-0226; @CL-FPA-0254; @CL-FPA-0227; @CL-FPA-0349; @CL-FPA-0228; @CL-FPA-0229; @CL-FPA-0333; @CL-FPA-0255; @CL-FPA-0256]. Under Item 508, if the securities are offered through underwriters, it names the principal underwriters, states the respective amounts underwritten and any material relationships, and sets out the underwriters' compensation table [@CL-FPA-0230; @CL-FPA-0231]. Where applicable, it also provides the offering-price factors under Item 505(a), selling security holder information under Item 507, itemized issuance and distribution expenses under Item 511, expert and counsel contingent-basis, interest or connection statements under Item 509, and indemnification disclosures under Items 702 and 510 [@CL-FPA-1002; @CL-FPA-1003; @CL-FPA-1004; @CL-FPA-1005; @CL-FPA-1006; @CL-FPA-1007; @CL-FPA-1008; @CL-FPA-1009; @CL-FPA-0334; @CL-FPA-0335; @CL-FPA-0336; @CL-FPA-0337; @CL-FPA-0338; @CL-FPA-1010; @CL-FPA-1011; @CL-FPA-1012; @CL-FPA-1234; @CL-FPA-1014; @CL-FPA-1015; @CL-FPA-1021; @CL-FPA-1022; @CL-FPA-1016; @CL-FPA-1017; @CL-FPA-1018].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company plans a 200 million dollar offering. It states that 150 million will buy a plant and gives a brief description and the cost. It states that 50 million may finance acquisitions of other businesses, names a target if one is known and gives the status of talks. It names its lead underwriter with the amount underwritten and shows the underwriting discount in the compensation table.

## Never confuse

Item 504 requires disclosure of the principal purposes for which the net proceeds from the securities to be offered are intended to be used and the approximate amount intended for each purpose, and Instruction 6 addresses when proceeds may or will be used to finance acquisitions of other businesses (requiring a more detailed description where Regulation S-X would require financial statements of the business to be acquired, and permitting omission of terms, parties or nature of the business sought where pro forma financial statements are not required and public disclosure would jeopardize the acquisition) [@CL-FPA-0226; @CL-FPA-0228; @CL-FPA-0229; @CL-FPA-0333; @CL-FPA-0255; @CL-FPA-0256]. For Management's Discussion and Analysis of liquidity, see [Management discussion objective and liquidity](../planning-chain/fpa-mdna-objective-and-liquidity.md); for the description of the securities being registered, see [Description of registrant's securities](fpa-description-of-registrants-securities.md); for when financial statements of an acquired business are required under Regulation S-X, see [Financial statements of acquired businesses](../../mna/diligence/mna-sx-acquired-business-financial-statements.md); and for registration statement undertakings, see [Undertakings in a registration statement](fpa-registration-undertakings.md).
