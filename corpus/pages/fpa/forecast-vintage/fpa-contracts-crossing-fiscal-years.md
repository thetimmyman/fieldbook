## Purpose

Shows when a contract funded by annual appropriations may cross fiscal years and when a contract, option, or order for severable services may begin in one fiscal year and end in the next [@CL-FPA-0063; @CL-FPA-0066].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR and codified DFARS [@CL-FPA-0063; @CL-FPA-0068] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.703-3; DFARS 232.703-3 [@CL-FPA-0063; @CL-FPA-0066; @CL-FPA-0068] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.703-3 [@CL-FPA-0283; @CL-FPA-0284; @CL-FPA-0285; @CL-FPA-0286; @CL-FPA-0287] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-FPA-0063] |
| Applies to | Contracts funded by annual appropriations [@CL-FPA-0063], and contracts, options or orders for severable services for a period that does not exceed one year [@CL-FPA-0066] |

A contract that is funded by annual appropriations may not cross fiscal years, except in accordance with statutory authorization (for example, section 6302 of title 41, section 1308 of title 31, section 2459a of title 42, section 3515 of title 42, United States Code, and paragraph (b) of FAR 32.703-3) [@CL-FPA-0063], or when the contract calls for an end product that cannot feasibly be subdivided for separate performance in each fiscal year (for example, contracts for expert or consultant services) [@CL-FPA-0064].

Paragraph (b) of FAR 32.703-3 is listed among the examples of statutory authorization [@CL-FPA-0063]. Under it, the head of an executive agency, except NASA, may enter into a contract, exercise an option, or place an order under a contract for severable services for a period that begins in one fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year if the period of the contract awarded, option exercised, or order placed does not exceed one year (section 3133 of title 10 and section 3902 of title 41, United States Code) [@CL-FPA-0065; @CL-FPA-0066]. Funds made available for a fiscal year may be obligated for the total amount of an action entered into under this authority [@CL-FPA-0067]. DFARS 232.703-3(b) provides that the contracting officer may enter into a contract, exercise an option, or place an order under a contract for severable services for a period that begins in one fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year if the period of the contract awarded, option exercised, or order placed does not exceed 1 year (section 3133 of title 10, United States Code) [@CL-FPA-0068].

## How it works

A contract funded by annual appropriations may not cross fiscal years except in accordance with statutory authorization or when the contract calls for an end product that cannot feasibly be subdivided for separate performance in each fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0063; @CL-FPA-0064]. Under the severable-services authority in paragraph (b), the head of an executive agency (except NASA) under the FAR, or the contracting officer under the DFARS, may enter into a contract, exercise an option, or place an order under a contract for severable services for a period that begins in one fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year if the period of the contract awarded, option exercised, or order placed does not exceed one year [@CL-FPA-0065; @CL-FPA-0066; @CL-FPA-0068]. Funds made available for a fiscal year may be obligated for the total amount of an action entered into under this authority [@CL-FPA-0067].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers must use the revised FAR part 32 published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.703-3(a) states that a contract funded by annual appropriations may not cross fiscal years, except in accordance with statutory authorization (listing section 1308 of title 31, section 6302 of title 41, section 3515 of title 42, United States Code, and paragraph (b) of that section) [@CL-FPA-0283], whereas the codified FAR 32.703-3(a) example list also includes section 2459a of title 42, United States Code [@CL-FPA-0063]. RFO FAR 32.703-3(a) also includes the exception when the contract calls for an end product that cannot feasibly be subdivided for separate performance in each fiscal year (for example, contracts for expert or consultant services) [@CL-FPA-0284], matching codified FAR 32.703-3(a) [@CL-FPA-0064]. RFO FAR 32.703-3(b) provides that the head of an executive agency, except NASA, may enter into a contract, exercise an option, or place an order under a contract for severable services for a period that begins in one fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year if the period of the contract awarded, option exercised, or order placed does not exceed one year (citing section 3133 of title 10 and section 3902 of title 41, United States Code) [@CL-FPA-0285; @CL-FPA-0286], and that funds made available for a fiscal year may be obligated for the total amount of an action entered into under this authority [@CL-FPA-0287], matching codified FAR 32.703-3(b) [@CL-FPA-0065; @CL-FPA-0066; @CL-FPA-0067].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A severable-services order of 1,200,000 covers twelve months and starts in month ten of a fiscal year. Three months fall in the first fiscal year and nine in the second. Funds made available for the first fiscal year may be obligated for the whole 1,200,000. The forecast shows 300,000 of revenue in the first year and 900,000 in the second, with a note that the order rests on the severable-services authority.

## Never confuse

FAR 32.703-3(a) bars a contract funded by annual appropriations from crossing fiscal years except in accordance with statutory authorization or when the contract calls for an end product that cannot feasibly be subdivided for separate performance in each fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0063; @CL-FPA-0064]. FAR 32.703-3(b) and DFARS 232.703-3(b) allow a contract, option exercise, or order under a contract for severable services to begin in one fiscal year and end in the next fiscal year if the period of the contract awarded, option exercised, or order placed does not exceed one year [@CL-FPA-0065; @CL-FPA-0066; @CL-FPA-0068]. For contracts conditioned on availability of funds, see [Contracts conditioned on availability of funds](../backlog/fpa-funds-availability-conditioned-contracts.md). For the funding notices on contracts that are funded in steps, see [Contract funding: incremental funding policy](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-incremental-funding-policy.md).
