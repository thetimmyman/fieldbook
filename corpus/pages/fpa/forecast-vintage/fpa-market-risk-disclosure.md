## Purpose

Shows the three ways a registrant presents quantitative market risk information, the qualitative items and who is not required to provide the information under Item 305 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0131; @CL-FPA-0135; @CL-FPA-0137].

Item 305(a)(1) requires registrants to provide, in their reporting currency, quantitative information about market risk as of the end of the latest fiscal year, in accordance with one of three disclosure alternatives [@CL-FPA-0131]. The first alternative is a tabular presentation of information related to market risk sensitive instruments, including fair values and contract terms sufficient to determine future cash flows, categorized by expected maturity dates [@CL-FPA-0132]. The second is sensitivity analysis disclosures that express the potential loss in future earnings, fair values, or cash flows of market risk sensitive instruments resulting from one or more selected hypothetical changes in interest rates, foreign currency exchange rates, commodity prices, and other relevant market rates or prices over a selected period of time, where the magnitude of the selected changes may differ among and within market risk exposure categories, and registrants shall provide a description of the model, assumptions, and parameters necessary to understand those disclosures [@CL-FPA-0133; @CL-FPA-0172; @CL-FPA-0365]. The third is value at risk disclosures that express the potential loss in future earnings, fair values, or cash flows of market risk sensitive instruments over a selected period of time, with a selected likelihood of occurrence, from changes in interest rates, foreign currency exchange rates, commodity prices, and other relevant market rates or prices [@CL-FPA-0134; @CL-FPA-0328]. In preparing this quantitative information, registrants may choose one of the three alternatives for all of the required quantitative disclosures, or may choose one alternative for market risk sensitive instruments entered into for trading purposes and another alternative for other than trading purposes [@CL-FPA-0173]. Registrants shall also discuss material limitations that cause the information required under paragraph (a)(1) not to reflect fully the net market risk exposures of the entity [@CL-FPA-0174].

Item 305(b)(1) says that, to the extent material, the registrant shall describe its primary market risk exposures and how those exposures are managed [@CL-FPA-0135]. Another listed item is changes in either the primary market risk exposures or how those exposures are managed, when compared to what was in effect during the most recently completed fiscal year and what is known or expected to be in effect in future reporting periods [@CL-FPA-0136]. Item 305(e) says a registrant that qualifies as a smaller reporting company, as defined by § 229.10(f)(1), is not required to provide the information required by Item 305 [@CL-FPA-0137].

## How it works

Unless the registrant qualifies as a smaller reporting company under § 229.10(f)(1) [@CL-FPA-0137], it provides quantitative market risk information in its reporting currency as of the end of the latest fiscal year under one of the three alternatives (or one alternative for trading instruments and another for other-than-trading instruments): the tabular presentation of fair values and contract terms sufficient to determine future cash flows categorized by expected maturity dates, the sensitivity analysis of potential loss from selected hypothetical changes in rates or prices over a selected period (with a description of the model, assumptions, and parameters), or the value at risk disclosure of potential loss over a selected period with a selected likelihood of occurrence from changes in rates or prices [@CL-FPA-0131; @CL-FPA-0132; @CL-FPA-0133; @CL-FPA-0172; @CL-FPA-0365; @CL-FPA-0134; @CL-FPA-0328; @CL-FPA-0173]. It also discusses material limitations that cause the paragraph (a)(1) information not to reflect fully its net market risk exposures [@CL-FPA-0174] and, to the extent material, describes its primary market risk exposures, how those exposures are managed, and changes in either compared to the most recently completed fiscal year and what is known or expected in future reporting periods [@CL-FPA-0135; @CL-FPA-0136].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A registrant has variable-rate debt of 100 and chooses the sensitivity analysis alternative. It selects a hypothetical rise of one percentage point in its borrowing rate over a one-year period. The potential loss in annual earnings is 1. It then describes in words that interest rate risk is its primary exposure and how it manages that exposure.

## Never confuse

Under Item 305(a)(1), sensitivity analysis expresses potential loss in future earnings, fair values, or cash flows of market risk sensitive instruments resulting from one or more selected hypothetical changes in interest rates, foreign currency exchange rates, commodity prices, and other relevant market rates or prices over a selected period of time [@CL-FPA-0133; @CL-FPA-0172], unlike value at risk, which expresses potential loss in future earnings, fair values, or cash flows over a selected period of time, with a selected likelihood of occurrence, from changes in interest rates, foreign currency exchange rates, commodity prices, and other relevant market rates or prices [@CL-FPA-0134; @CL-FPA-0328], and the tabular alternative presents information related to market risk sensitive instruments, including fair values and contract terms sufficient to determine future cash flows, categorized by expected maturity dates [@CL-FPA-0132]. Item 305(a)(1) covers quantitative information about market risk [@CL-FPA-0131], while Item 305(b)(1) covers qualitative descriptions, to the extent material, of primary market risk exposures, how they are managed, and changes in either [@CL-FPA-0135; @CL-FPA-0136]. The management discussion of liquidity is covered in [MD&A objective and liquidity for public registrants](../planning-chain/fpa-mdna-objective-and-liquidity.md), and the risk factor disclosure is covered in [Risk factor disclosure for public registrants](fpa-risk-factors.md).
