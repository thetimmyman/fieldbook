## Purpose

Shows what a registrant discloses about each director, executive officer, promoter and control person under Item 401 of Regulation S-K, and how far back each disclosure looks [@CL-FPA-0182; @CL-FPA-0187; @CL-FPA-0189; @CL-FPA-0191].

| Applies to | A registrant, for its directors, nominees and executive officers; the promoter rule reaches a registrant that has not been subject to the reporting requirements of section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Exchange Act for the twelve months immediately before the filing and that had a promoter at any time during the past five fiscal years, the control person rule reaches a registrant not subject to those reporting requirements for those twelve months, and paragraph (g) does not apply to any subsidiary of a registrant that has been reporting pursuant to section 13(a) or 15(d) for those twelve months [@CL-FPA-0182; @CL-FPA-0183; @CL-FPA-0188; @CL-FPA-0189; @CL-FPA-0190; @CL-FPA-0235] |

Item 401(a) requires the registrant to list the names and ages of all directors and of all persons nominated or chosen to become directors, to indicate all positions and offices held with the registrant, to state each person's term of office as director and any period during which the person has served as such, and to describe briefly any arrangement or understanding between the person and any other person, naming that other person, pursuant to which the person was or is to be selected as a director or nominee [@CL-FPA-0182; @CL-FPA-0342]. Item 401(b) asks the same of executive officers and of persons chosen to become executive officers, with the term of office as officer and the period during which the person has served as such, and a brief description of any arrangement or understanding between the person and any other person, naming that other person, pursuant to which the person was or is to be selected as an officer [@CL-FPA-0183; @CL-FPA-0343]. Item 401(e)(1) adds a brief description of the business experience during the past five years of each director, executive officer, person nominated or chosen to become a director or executive officer, and each person named in answer to paragraph (c) of Item 401 [@CL-FPA-0184]. For each director or person nominated or chosen to become a director, it also calls for a brief discussion of the specific experience, qualifications, attributes or skills that led to the conclusion that the person should serve as a director for the registrant at the time that the disclosure is made, in light of the registrant's business and structure [@CL-FPA-0185; @CL-FPA-0344]. Item 401(e)(2) requires the registrant to indicate any other directorships held, including any other directorships held during the past five years, by each director or person nominated or chosen to become a director in any company with a class of securities registered pursuant to section 12 of the Exchange Act [@CL-FPA-0186], or subject to the requirements of section 15(d) of such Act, or any company registered as an investment company under the Investment Company Act of 1940, naming such company [@CL-FPA-0232].

Item 401(f) requires the registrant to describe any of the listed events that occurred during the past ten years and that are material to an evaluation of the ability or integrity of any director, person nominated to become a director or executive officer of the registrant [@CL-FPA-0187]. For purposes of computing the ten-year period, the date of a reportable event shall be deemed the date on which the final order, judgment or decree was entered, or the date on which any rights of appeal from preliminary orders, judgments, or decrees have lapsed [@CL-FPA-0233]. With respect to bankruptcy petitions, the computation date shall be the date of filing for uncontested petitions or the date upon which approval of a contested petition became final [@CL-FPA-0234]. Item 401(g)(1) is addressed to a registrant that has not been subject to the reporting requirements of section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Exchange Act for the twelve months immediately prior to the filing of the registration statement, report, or statement to which Item 401 is applicable, and that had a promoter at any time during the past five fiscal years [@CL-FPA-0188; @CL-FPA-0189]. Such a registrant shall describe with respect to any promoter any of the events enumerated in paragraphs (f)(1) through (f)(6) of Item 401 that occurred during the past five years and that are material to a voting or investment decision [@CL-FPA-0189]. Item 401(g)(2) requires a registrant that has not been subject to the reporting requirements of section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Exchange Act for the twelve months immediately prior to the filing to describe with respect to any control person any of the events enumerated in paragraphs (f)(1) through (f)(6) of Item 401 that occurred during the past five years and that are material to a voting or investment decision [@CL-FPA-0190; @CL-FPA-0191]. Instruction 2 to paragraph (g) states that paragraph (g) does not apply to any subsidiary of a registrant which has been reporting pursuant to Section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Exchange Act for the twelve months immediately prior to the filing of the registration statement, report or statement [@CL-FPA-0235].

## How it works

The registrant lists each director, nominee and executive officer with age, positions and offices, term of office, period of service, and any selection arrangement or understanding [@CL-FPA-0182; @CL-FPA-0183; @CL-FPA-0342; @CL-FPA-0343]. It describes five-year business experience, director qualifications at the time the disclosure is made in light of the registrant's business and structure, and other directorships in section 12, section 15(d) or registered investment companies [@CL-FPA-0184; @CL-FPA-0185; @CL-FPA-0344; @CL-FPA-0186; @CL-FPA-0232], and describes material listed events over the past ten years using the computation dates in the instruction [@CL-FPA-0187; @CL-FPA-0233; @CL-FPA-0234]. A registrant that has not been subject to section 13(a) or 15(d) reporting for the twelve months immediately before the filing (and is not a subsidiary of a registrant that has been reporting for those twelve months) describes events in paragraphs (f)(1) through (f)(6) from the past five years that are material to a voting or investment decision for any promoter (if it had a promoter at any time during the past five fiscal years) and for any control person [@CL-FPA-0188; @CL-FPA-0189; @CL-FPA-0190; @CL-FPA-0191; @CL-FPA-0235].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company that has never filed reports prepares its first registration statement. It lists a director with her age, positions and term. She served on the boards of two other public companies over the past four years, so both are named. A court order that was never reversed enjoined her from acting as a broker. It was entered eight years ago and is material, so it is described. A similar order entered twelve years ago is outside the ten-year look-back. The company also had a promoter two years ago, so it describes the promoter's listed events from the past five years that are material to a voting or investment decision.

## Never confuse

Item 401(f) covers events that occurred during the past ten years and are material to an evaluation of the ability or integrity of any director, person nominated to become a director or executive officer of the registrant [@CL-FPA-0187], whereas Item 401(g)(1) and (g)(2) cover events in paragraphs (f)(1) through (f)(6) that occurred during the past five years and are material to a voting or investment decision with respect to a promoter or control person of a registrant not subject to section 13(a) or 15(d) reporting for the twelve months immediately prior to the filing [@CL-FPA-0188; @CL-FPA-0189; @CL-FPA-0190; @CL-FPA-0191]. For transactions with related persons, see [Transactions with related persons](fpa-related-person-transactions.md); for beneficial ownership and insider reporting, see [Security ownership and insider reporting compliance](fpa-security-ownership-and-section-16a-compliance.md); for director independence and board committees, see [Corporate governance disclosure](fpa-corporate-governance-disclosure.md); and for director and executive compensation, see [Pension, deferred compensation, termination payments and director pay](fpa-pension-deferred-compensation-termination-and-director-pay.md).
