## Purpose

Shows how a registrant reports retirement plans, nonqualified deferred compensation, payments on termination or change in control, and director compensation under Item 402 of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-1093; @CL-FPA-1099; @CL-FPA-1102; @CL-FPA-1109].

| Applies to | A registrant, for plans that provide for payments or other benefits at, following, or in connection with retirement, plans that defer compensation on a basis that is not tax-qualified, certain termination and change in control arrangements with a named executive officer, and its directors [@CL-FPA-1093; @CL-FPA-1099; @CL-FPA-1102; @CL-FPA-1109] |

The pension benefits table covers each plan that provides for payments or other benefits at, following, or in connection with retirement [@CL-FPA-1093]. It applies to each plan that provides for specified retirement payments and benefits, or payments and benefits that will be provided primarily following retirement, including but not limited to tax-qualified defined benefit plans and supplemental executive retirement plans [@CL-FPA-1096]. It excludes tax-qualified defined contribution plans and nonqualified defined contribution plans [@CL-FPA-1097]. Column (d) shows the actuarial present value of the named executive officer's accumulated benefit under the plan, computed as of the same pension plan measurement date used for financial statement reporting purposes [@CL-FPA-1094]. That measurement date is the one used for the registrant's audited financial statements for the last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1095]. The registrant must disclose in the accompanying textual narrative the valuation method and all material assumptions applied in quantifying the present value of the current accrued benefit [@CL-FPA-1098].

The nonqualified deferred compensation table covers each defined contribution or other plan that provides for the deferral of compensation on a basis that is not tax-qualified [@CL-FPA-1099]. It shows aggregate executive contributions in column (b) and aggregate registrant contributions in column (c), each during the last fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1100]. It also shows aggregate interest or other earnings accrued in column (d) and all withdrawals and distributions in column (e) during that year [@CL-FPA-1101].

Paragraph (j) applies to each contract, agreement, plan or arrangement, whether written or unwritten, that provides for payment to a named executive officer at, following, or in connection with any termination [@CL-FPA-1102]. A termination for this purpose includes resignation, severance, retirement or a constructive termination, and paragraph (j) also covers a change in control of the registrant or a change in the officer's responsibilities [@CL-FPA-1103]. For each named executive officer the registrant describes and explains the specific circumstances that would trigger payment or other benefits, including perquisites and health care benefits [@CL-FPA-1104]. It also describes and quantifies the estimated payments and benefits in each covered circumstance, whether they would or could be lump sum or annual, disclosing the duration and by whom they would be provided [@CL-FPA-1105]. The quantitative disclosure applies the assumption that the triggering event took place on the last business day of the last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1106], and that the price per share of the registrant's securities is the closing market price as of that date [@CL-FPA-1107]. The registrant need not provide information about arrangements to the extent they do not discriminate in scope, terms or operation in favor of executive officers and are available generally to all salaried employees [@CL-FPA-1108].

The director compensation table gives the information specified in paragraph (k)(2) about the compensation of the directors for the last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1109]. Column (b) shows the aggregate dollar amount of all fees earned or paid in cash for services as a director, including annual retainer fees, committee and/or chairmanship fees, and meeting fees [@CL-FPA-1110]. One footnote item listed for each director is the aggregate number of stock awards and option awards outstanding at fiscal year end [@CL-FPA-1111]. The registrant provides a narrative description of any material factors necessary to an understanding of the director compensation disclosed in the table [@CL-FPA-1112].

The printed item also gives the remaining pension and deferral columns and instructions, the narrative factors for those plans, the other termination and change in control instructions, the other director table columns and the rules on director legacy programs and grouping, which these claims do not restate.

## How it works

The registrant reports each retirement plan in the pension table [@CL-FPA-1093] and each nonqualified deferral plan in the deferred compensation table [@CL-FPA-1099]. It describes what each triggering event would pay, measured as of the last business day of the last completed fiscal year [@CL-FPA-1105; @CL-FPA-1106].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A named executive officer is in one supplemental executive retirement plan and one deferral plan. The pension table shows a present value of 2,000,000 dollars at the measurement date. The deferral table shows 100,000 dollars of executive contributions and 40,000 dollars of registrant contributions. The termination disclosure prices a change in control as if it happened on the last business day of the year. A director's row shows 80,000 dollars of cash fees.

## Never confuse

A defined contribution plan that defers compensation on a basis that is not tax-qualified falls in the deferred compensation table, not the pension table [@CL-FPA-1097; @CL-FPA-1099]. Who the directors are and their records is in [Directors, executive officers, promoters and control persons](fpa-directors-officers-and-control-persons.md). Pay to the named executive officers is in [Executive compensation scope, discussion and summary compensation table](fpa-executive-compensation-scope-and-summary-compensation-table.md). Transactions with a director that are not compensation belong with [Transactions with related persons](fpa-related-person-transactions.md).
