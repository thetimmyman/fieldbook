## Purpose

Shows how a registrant describes the general development of the business of the registrant, its subsidiaries, and any predecessors under Item 101(a) of Regulation S-K [@CL-FPA-0001; @CL-FPA-0002; @CL-FPA-0004; @CL-FPA-0003; @CL-FPA-0361; @CL-FPA-0005; @CL-FPA-0100; @CL-FPA-0101]. Regulation S-K states the requirements applicable to the content of the non-financial statement portions of registration statements under the Securities Act to the extent provided in the forms to be used for registration under such Act [@CL-FPA-0105], and registration statements under section 12, annual or other reports under sections 13 and 15(d), going-private transaction statements under section 13, tender offer statements under sections 13 and 14, annual reports to security holders and proxy and information statements under section 14, and any other documents required to be filed under the Exchange Act, to the extent provided in the forms and rules under that Act [@CL-FPA-0380; @CL-FPA-0381; @CL-FPA-0106].

Item 101(a) of Regulation S-K requires the registrant to describe the general development of the business of the registrant, its subsidiaries, and any predecessors [@CL-FPA-0001]. Under paragraph (a)(1), in describing developments, only information material to an understanding of the general development of the business is required, and disclosure may include, but should not be limited to, the topics in paragraphs (a)(1)(i) through (iv) [@CL-FPA-0002].

Those topics are (i) any material changes to a previously disclosed business strategy and (ii) the nature and effects of any material bankruptcy, receivership, or any similar proceeding with respect to the registrant or any of its significant subsidiaries [@CL-FPA-0004], together with (iii) the nature and effects of any material reclassification, merger or consolidation of the registrant or any of its significant subsidiaries and (iv) the acquisition or disposition of any material amount of assets otherwise than in the ordinary course of business [@CL-FPA-0003].

## How it works

In describing developments of the business of the registrant, its subsidiaries, and any predecessors [@CL-FPA-0001], only information material to an understanding of the general development of the business is required, and disclosure may include, but should not be limited to, the topics in Item 101(a)(1)(i) through (iv) [@CL-FPA-0002; @CL-FPA-0004; @CL-FPA-0003]. Under Item 101(a)(2), notwithstanding the provisions of § 230.411(b) or § 240.12b-23(a), as applicable, a registrant may only forgo providing a full discussion of the general development of its business for a filing other than an initial registration statement if it provides an update to the general development of its business, disclosing all of the material developments that have occurred since the most recent registration statement or report that includes a full discussion of the general development of its business [@CL-FPA-0361; @CL-FPA-0005; @CL-FPA-0100]. In addition, the registrant must incorporate by reference, and include one active hyperlink to, one registration statement or report that includes the full discussion of the general development of the registrant's business [@CL-FPA-0101].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A listed contractor buys a smaller firm for 40 million, which is material to its business. It describes the purchase and its effects in the development discussion. A private contractor making the same purchase has no filing duty and may record the same description in its planning narrative.

## Never confuse

Item 101(a) requires describing the general development of the business of the registrant, its subsidiaries, and any predecessors, where only information material to an understanding of the general development of the business is required and disclosure may include, but should not be limited to, any material changes to a previously disclosed business strategy, the nature and effects of any material bankruptcy, receivership, or any similar proceeding or of any material reclassification, merger or consolidation of the registrant or any of its significant subsidiaries, and the acquisition or disposition of any material amount of assets otherwise than in the ordinary course of business [@CL-FPA-0001; @CL-FPA-0002; @CL-FPA-0004; @CL-FPA-0003].
