## Purpose

Shows the FAR 32.702 limit on creating or authorizing Government obligations in excess of available funds or in advance of appropriations, and the two alternative actions the contracting officer shall take before executing any contract [@CL-FPA-0050; @CL-FPA-0051].

No officer or employee of the Government may create or authorize an obligation in excess of the funds available, or in advance of appropriations (Anti-Deficiency Act, section 1341 of title 31), unless otherwise authorized by law [@CL-FPA-0050]. Before executing any contract, the contracting officer shall (a) obtain written assurance from responsible fiscal authority that adequate funds are available or (b) expressly condition the contract upon availability of funds in accordance with FAR 32.703-2 [@CL-FPA-0051].

## How it works

Under FAR 32.702, no officer or employee of the Government may create or authorize an obligation in excess of the funds available, or in advance of appropriations (Anti-Deficiency Act, section 1341 of title 31), unless otherwise authorized by law [@CL-FPA-0050]. Before executing any contract, the contracting officer shall either (a) obtain written assurance from responsible fiscal authority that adequate funds are available or (b) expressly condition the contract upon availability of funds in accordance with FAR 32.703-2 [@CL-FPA-0051]. For incremental funding policy, see [Contract funding: incremental funding policy](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-incremental-funding-policy.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.702 states that no officer or employee of the Government may create or authorize an obligation in excess of the funds available, or in advance of appropriations (Anti-Deficiency Act, section 1341 of title 31), unless otherwise authorized by law [@CL-FPA-0272]. RFO FAR 32.702 further provides that before executing any contract, the contracting officer must obtain written assurance from responsible fiscal authority that adequate funds are available or expressly condition the contract upon availability of funds in accordance with 32.703-2 [@CL-FPA-0273], whereas codified FAR 32.702 states that the contracting officer shall (a) obtain written assurance from responsible fiscal authority that adequate funds are available or (b) expressly condition the contract upon availability of funds in accordance with 32.703-2 [@CL-FPA-0051].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contract has a total value of 2,000,000. The file holds written assurance from fiscal authority that adequate funds are available for all 2,000,000. The planner counts the whole 2,000,000 as funded. A second contract of 500,000 is expressly conditioned on availability of funds, and no funds are available yet. The planner counts the 500,000 as unfunded until the condition is met.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 32.702, no Government officer or employee may create or authorize an obligation in excess of available funds or in advance of appropriations unless authorized by law [@CL-FPA-0050; @CL-FPA-0272], so before executing any contract the contracting officer must either obtain written assurance from responsible fiscal authority that adequate funds are available or expressly condition the contract upon availability of funds in accordance with FAR 32.703-2 rather than executing an unconditioned unfunded obligation [@CL-FPA-0051; @CL-FPA-0273]. See [Contracts conditioned on availability of funds](fpa-funds-availability-conditioned-contracts.md).
