## Purpose

Shows how the clause at DFARS 252.232-7007, Limitation of Government's Obligation, caps the contractor's performance obligation and the Government's reimbursement obligation at the amount allotted for incrementally funded contract line items, and sets the contractor's written notice requirement and the Contracting Officer's termination duty when additional funds are not allotted [@CL-FPA-0080; @CL-FPA-1241; @CL-FPA-0081; @CL-FPA-0082; @CL-FPA-0083; @CL-FPA-1242; @CL-FPA-0085; @CL-FPA-0086; @CL-FPA-0341; @CL-FPA-0088; @CL-FPA-1243].

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified DFARS clause [@CL-FPA-0081] |
| Codified citation | DFARS 252.232-7007 [@CL-FPA-0081] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-FPA-0081] |
| Applies to | Incrementally funded contract line items identified in paragraph (a) of the clause [@CL-FPA-0080; @CL-FPA-1241] |

Under paragraph (a), the clause identifies contract line items as incrementally funded and states for those items the sum of the total price that is presently available for payment and allotted to the contract [@CL-FPA-0080]. For the items identified in paragraph (a) of the clause, paragraph (b) has the Contractor agree to perform up to the point at which the total amount payable by the Government, including reimbursement in the event of termination of those items for the Government's convenience, approximates the total amount currently allotted to the contract [@CL-FPA-1241; @CL-FPA-0081]. The Contractor is not authorized to continue work on those items beyond that point [@CL-FPA-0082]. The Government will not be obligated in any event to reimburse the Contractor in excess of the amount allotted to the contract for those items regardless of anything to the contrary in the clause entitled "Termination for Convenience of the Government" [@CL-FPA-0083].

As used in the clause, the total amount payable by the Government in the event of termination of applicable contract line items for convenience includes costs, profit, and estimated termination settlement costs for those items [@CL-FPA-0084]. Under paragraph (j), the parties contemplate that the Government will allot funds to the contract in accordance with the schedule set out in the clause [@CL-FPA-0090].

## How it works

Notwithstanding the dates specified in the allotment schedule in paragraph (j) of the clause, the Contractor will notify the Contracting Officer in writing at least ninety days prior to the date when, in the Contractor's best judgment, the work will reach the point at which the total amount payable by the Government, including any cost for termination for convenience, will approximate 85 percent of the total amount then allotted to the contract for performance of the applicable items [@CL-FPA-1242; @CL-FPA-0085; @CL-FPA-0086; @CL-FPA-0341]. The notification will state (1) the estimated date when that point will be reached and (2) an estimate of additional funding, if any, needed to continue performance of applicable line items up to the next scheduled date for allotment of funds identified in paragraph (j) of the clause, or to a mutually agreed upon substitute date [@CL-FPA-0087; @CL-FPA-0103]. The notification will also advise the Contracting Officer of the estimated amount of additional funds that will be required for the timely performance of the items funded pursuant to the clause, for a subsequent period as may be specified in the allotment schedule in paragraph (j) of the clause, or otherwise agreed to by the parties [@CL-FPA-0356; @CL-FPA-0357]. If after such notification additional funds are not allotted by the date identified in the Contractor's notification, or by an agreed substitute date, the Contracting Officer will terminate any items for which additional funds have not been allotted, pursuant to the clause of the contract entitled "Termination for Convenience of the Government" [@CL-FPA-0088; @CL-FPA-1243]. Under paragraph (g), the clause no longer applies once the contract is fully funded except with regard to the rights or obligations of the parties concerning equitable adjustments negotiated under paragraphs (d) or (e) of the clause [@CL-FPA-0089].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price line item has a total price of 4,000,000, of which 1,500,000 is allotted. Costs to date plus estimated termination settlement costs reach 1,275,000, which is 85 percent of the allotment. The contractor must have given notice at least ninety days before reaching that point. If no more funds are allotted by the date it gave, the Contracting Officer terminates the item. The Government will not reimburse more than the 1,500,000 allotted.

## Never confuse

Under DFARS 252.232-7007(a), for incrementally funded contract line items, the clause states the sum of the total price that is presently available for payment and allotted to the contract [@CL-FPA-0080]. For the items identified in paragraph (a) of the clause, the Contractor agrees to perform up to the point at which the total amount payable by the Government, including reimbursement in the event of termination of those items for the Government's convenience, approximates the total amount currently allotted to the contract, and the Contractor is not authorized to continue work on those items beyond that point [@CL-FPA-1241; @CL-FPA-0081; @CL-FPA-0082]. The Government will not be obligated in any event to reimburse the Contractor in excess of the amount allotted to the contract for those items regardless of anything to the contrary in the clause entitled "Termination for Convenience of the Government" [@CL-FPA-0083]. As used in the clause, the total amount payable by the Government in the event of termination of applicable contract line items for convenience includes costs, profit, and estimated termination settlement costs for those items [@CL-FPA-0084]. Under DFARS 232.706-70, the contracting officer may revise the contractor's notification period, in paragraph (c) of the clause, from ninety to thirty or sixty days, as appropriate [@CL-FPA-0079]; see [DoD incrementally funded fixed-price contracts](fpa-dod-incrementally-funded-fixed-price.md). For the cost-reimbursement clause, see [Limitation of Funds clause](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-limitation-of-funds.md).
