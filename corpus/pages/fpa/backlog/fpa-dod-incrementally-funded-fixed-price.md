## Purpose

Shows when a fixed-price contract may be incrementally funded under the DFARS, and what written notice the contracting officer shall provide upon receipt of the contractor's notice under paragraph (c) of the clause at DFARS 252.232-7007, Limitation of Government's Obligation [@CL-FPA-0069; @CL-FPA-0070; @CL-FPA-0071; @CL-FPA-0073].

Under DFARS 232.703-1(1), a fixed-price contract may be incrementally funded only if one of two alternatives is met [@CL-FPA-0069; @CL-FPA-0070; @CL-FPA-0071]. Under the first alternative, in paragraph (1)(i), the contract (excluding any options) or any exercised option is for severable services, does not exceed one year in length, and is incrementally funded using funds available (unexpired) as of the date the funds are obligated [@CL-FPA-0069; @CL-FPA-0070]. Under the second alternative, in paragraph (1)(ii), the contract uses funds available from multiple (two or more) fiscal years and either the contract is funded with research and development appropriations or Congress has otherwise authorized incremental funding [@CL-FPA-0071].

An incrementally funded fixed-price contract shall be fully funded as soon as funds are available [@CL-FPA-0072]. In the DFARS, incremental funding means the partial funding of a contract or an exercised option, with additional funds anticipated to be provided at a later time [@CL-FPA-0371]. Fixed-price contracts shall be fully funded except as permitted by DFARS 232.703-1 [@CL-FPA-0372].

## How it works

Upon receipt of the contractor's notice under paragraph (c) of the clause at 252.232-7007, Limitation of Government's Obligation, the contracting officer shall promptly provide written notice to the contractor that the Government is taking one of three alternative actions: (1) allotting additional funds for continued performance and increasing the Government's limitation of obligation in a specified amount, (2) terminating the contract, or (3) considering whether to allot additional funds [@CL-FPA-0073; @CL-FPA-0074]. Under the third alternative, considering whether to allot additional funds, the notice also states that (i) the contractor is entitled by the contract terms to stop work when the Government's limitation of obligation is reached, and (ii) any costs expended beyond the Government's limitation of obligation are at the contractor's risk [@CL-FPA-0075]. Upon learning that the contract will receive no further funds, the contracting officer shall promptly give the contractor written notice of the Government's decision and terminate for the convenience of the Government [@CL-FPA-0076]. Under DFARS 232.704-70(c), the contracting officer shall ensure that, in accordance with paragraph (b) of the clause at 252.232-7007, Limitation of Government's Obligation, sufficient funds are allotted to the contract to cover the total amount payable to the contractor in the event of termination for the convenience of the Government [@CL-FPA-0352; @CL-FPA-0077].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A fixed-price research and development contract of 3,000,000 uses funds from two fiscal years and is funded with research and development appropriations. 1,000,000 is allotted now. The planner counts 1,000,000 as funded and 2,000,000 as unfunded, and the contract is to be fully funded as soon as funds are available. When the contractor warns that the limit is near, the contracting officer tells it in writing whether more funds are coming, the contract is ending, or the Government is still deciding.

## Never confuse

DFARS 232.706-70 directs using the clause at 252.232-7007, Limitation of Government's Obligation, in solicitations and resultant incrementally funded fixed-price contracts, and provides that the contracting officer may revise the contractor's notification period, in paragraph (c) of the clause, from ninety to thirty or sixty days, as appropriate [@CL-FPA-0078; @CL-FPA-0079]. See [Limitation of Government's obligation clause](fpa-limitation-of-government-obligation.md) and [Limitation of Funds clause](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-limitation-of-funds.md).
