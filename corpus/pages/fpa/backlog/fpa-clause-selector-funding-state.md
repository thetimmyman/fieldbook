## Purpose

Shows when the contracting officer inserts the Limitation of Cost clause or the Limitation of Funds clause in solicitations and contracts for a cost-reimbursement contract, and when the Limitation of Government Liability clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when a letter contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0091; @CL-FPA-0092; @CL-FPA-0093; @CL-FPA-0351].

## The rule

| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.706-2 [@CL-FPA-0268; @CL-FPA-0269; @CL-FPA-0270; @CL-FPA-0271] |

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-20, Limitation of Cost, in solicitations and contracts if a fully funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated, whether or not the contract provides for payment of a fee [@CL-FPA-0091]. The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-22, Limitation of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if an incrementally funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0092]. As prescribed in FAR 16.603-4(b)(2), the Limitation of Government Liability clause at FAR 52.216-24 is inserted in solicitations and contracts when a letter contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0093; @CL-FPA-0351].

## How it works

Under FAR 32.706-2, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-20, Limitation of Cost, in solicitations and contracts if a fully funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated, whether or not the contract provides for payment of a fee [@CL-FPA-0091], and shall insert the clause at 52.232-22, Limitation of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if an incrementally funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0092]. Under FAR 52.216-24, in performing the contract, the Contractor is not authorized to make expenditures or incur obligations exceeding the stated dollar amount, and the maximum amount for which the Government shall be liable if the contract is terminated is the stated dollar amount [@CL-FPA-0094]. For the operation of the cost-reimbursement limitation clauses, see [Limitation of Cost notice](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-limitation-of-cost.md) and [Limitation of Funds clause](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-limitation-of-funds.md).

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.706-2(a) directs inserting the clause at 52.232-20, Limitation of Cost, in solicitations and contracts if a fully funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated, whether or not the contract provides for payment of a fee [@CL-FPA-0268], whereas codified FAR 32.706-2(a) states that the contracting officer shall insert the clause [@CL-FPA-0091]. RFO FAR 32.706-2(a) also provides that the 60-day period may be varied from 30 to 90 days and the 75 percent from 75 to 85 percent [@CL-FPA-0270], and that "Task Order" or other appropriate designation may be substituted for "Schedule" wherever that word appears in the clause [@CL-FPA-0271]. RFO FAR 32.706-2(b) directs inserting the clause at 52.232-22, Limitation of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if an incrementally funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0269], whereas codified FAR 32.706-2(b) states that the contracting officer shall insert the clause [@CL-FPA-0092].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A cost-reimbursement contract with an estimated cost of 2,000,000 is funded for the full 2,000,000, so it takes limitation of cost. A second contract of the same size has 800,000 allotted with more to come, so it takes limitation of funds. A letter contract states a spending limit of 500,000 and a maximum termination liability of 500,000.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 32.706-2, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-20, Limitation of Cost, in solicitations and contracts if a fully funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated, whether or not the contract provides for payment of a fee [@CL-FPA-0091], and shall insert the clause at 52.232-22, Limitation of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if an incrementally funded cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0092]. RFO FAR 32.706-2(a) provides that the 60-day period may be varied from 30 to 90 days and the 75 percent from 75 to 85 percent [@CL-FPA-0270], and that "Task Order" or other appropriate designation may be substituted for "Schedule" wherever that word appears in the clause [@CL-FPA-0271]. Under FAR 52.216-24, as prescribed in FAR 16.603-4(b)(2), the Limitation of Government Liability clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when a letter contract is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0093; @CL-FPA-0351], whereas DFARS 232.706-70 directs using the clause at 252.232-7007, Limitation of Government's Obligation, in solicitations and resultant incrementally funded fixed-price contracts [@CL-FPA-0078]; see [Limitation of Government's obligation clause](fpa-limitation-of-government-obligation.md). For contract types, see [Contract types](../../contract-finance/billing-payment/ctf-contract-types.md).
