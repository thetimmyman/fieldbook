## Purpose

Shows when the contracting officer may initiate a contract action before funds of the new fiscal year are available when a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services funded by annual appropriations may extend beyond the fiscal year in which it begins, when the Government may accept supplies or services under a contract conditioned upon the availability of funds, and which clauses apply [@CL-FPA-0052; @CL-FPA-0055; @CL-FPA-0102; @CL-FPA-0056; @CL-FPA-0057; @CL-FPA-0353; @CL-FPA-0354; @CL-FPA-0058].

Under FAR 32.703-2(a), the contracting officer may initiate a contract action properly chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year before these funds are available, provided that the contract includes the clause at 52.232-18, Availability of Funds (see 32.706-1(a)) [@CL-FPA-0052]. This authority may be used only for operation and maintenance and continuing services (e.g., rentals, utilities, and supply items not financed by stock funds) that are (1) necessary for normal operations and (2) for which Congress previously had consistently appropriated funds, unless specific statutory authority exists permitting applicability to other requirements [@CL-FPA-0053; @CL-FPA-0054].

Under FAR 32.703-2(b), a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services that is funded by annual appropriations may extend beyond the fiscal year in which it begins, provided that (1) any specified minimum quantities are certain to be ordered in the initial fiscal year (see 37.106) and (2) the contract includes the clause at 52.232-19, Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year (see 32.706-1(b)) [@CL-FPA-0055; @CL-FPA-0102]. Under FAR 32.703-2(c), the Government shall not accept supplies or services under a contract conditioned upon the availability of funds until the contracting officer has given the contractor notice, to be confirmed in writing, that funds are available [@CL-FPA-0056].

## How it works

FAR 32.706-1(a) directs inserting the clause at 52.232-18, Availability of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if the contract will be chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year and the contract action will be initiated before the funds are available [@CL-FPA-0057]. Under FAR 32.706-1(b), the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-19, Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year, in solicitations and contracts if a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services is contemplated and the contract (1) is funded by annual appropriations and (2) is to extend beyond the initial fiscal year (see 32.703-2(b)) [@CL-FPA-0353; @CL-FPA-0354; @CL-FPA-0058]. Under the clause at FAR 52.232-18, funds are not presently available for the contract, the Government's obligation under the contract is contingent upon the availability of appropriated funds from which payment for contract purposes can be made, and no legal liability on the part of the Government for any payment may arise until funds are made available to the Contracting Officer for the contract and until the Contractor receives notice of such availability, to be confirmed in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-FPA-0059; @CL-FPA-0060]. Under the clause at FAR 52.232-19, funds are not presently available for performance under the contract beyond the stated date, the Government's obligation for performance of the contract beyond that date is contingent upon the availability of appropriated funds from which payment for contract purposes can be made, and no legal liability on the part of the Government for any payment may arise for performance under the contract beyond that date until funds are made available to the Contracting Officer for performance and until the Contractor receives notice of availability, to be confirmed in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-FPA-0061; @CL-FPA-0062; @CL-FPA-0355].

Effective February 1, 2026, DoD contracting officers shall use the revised FAR Part 32, Contract Financing, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.703-2(a) provides that the contracting officer may initiate a contract action properly chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year before these funds are available, provided that the contract includes the clause at 52.232-18, Availability of Funds (see 32.706-1(a)) [@CL-FPA-0274]. RFO FAR 32.703-2(a) states that this authority may be used only for operation and maintenance and continuing services (e.g., rentals, utilities, and supply items not financed by stock funds) that are (1) necessary for normal operations and (2) for which Congress previously had consistently appropriated funds, unless specific statutory authority exists permitting applicability to other requirements [@CL-FPA-0275; @CL-FPA-0276]. RFO FAR 32.703-2(b) provides that a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services that is funded by annual appropriations may extend beyond the fiscal year in which it begins, provided that (1) any specified minimum quantities are certain to be ordered in the initial fiscal year (see part 37) and (2) the contract includes the clause at 52.232-19, Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year (see 32.706-1(b)) [@CL-FPA-0277; @CL-FPA-0278], whereas codified FAR 32.703-2(b)(1) refers to 37.106 [@CL-FPA-0055]. RFO FAR 32.703-2(c) states that the Government will not accept supplies or services under a contract conditioned upon the availability of funds until the contracting officer has given the contractor notice, to be confirmed in writing, that funds are available [@CL-FPA-0279], whereas codified FAR 32.703-2(c) states that the Government shall not accept them [@CL-FPA-0056]. RFO FAR 32.706-1(a) directs inserting the clause at 52.232-18, Availability of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if the contract will be chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year and the contract action will be initiated before the funds are available [@CL-FPA-0280]. RFO FAR 32.706-1(b) directs inserting the clause at 52.232-19, Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year, in solicitations and contracts if a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services is contemplated and the contract (1) is funded by annual appropriations and (2) is to extend beyond the initial fiscal year (see 32.703-2(b)) [@CL-FPA-0281; @CL-FPA-0282], whereas codified FAR 32.706-1(b) states that the contracting officer shall insert the clause [@CL-FPA-0353; @CL-FPA-0354; @CL-FPA-0058].

RFO FAR 52.232-18, Availability of Funds, states that funds are not presently available for the contract and that the Government's obligation under the contract is contingent upon the availability of appropriated funds from which payment for contract purposes can be made [@CL-FPA-1023]. RFO FAR 52.232-18 also states that no legal liability on the part of the Government for any payment may arise until funds are made available to the Contracting Officer for the contract and until the Contractor receives notice of such availability, to be confirmed in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-FPA-1024]. RFO FAR 52.232-19, Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year, states that funds are not presently available for performance under the contract beyond the stated date, and that the Government's obligation for performance of the contract beyond that date is contingent upon the availability of appropriated funds from which payment for contract purposes can be made [@CL-FPA-1025]. RFO FAR 52.232-19 also states that no legal liability on the part of the Government for any payment may arise for performance under the contract beyond that date until funds are made available to the Contracting Officer for performance and until the Contractor receives notice of availability, to be confirmed in writing by the Contracting Officer [@CL-FPA-1026; @CL-FPA-1027].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A one-year requirements contract for services starts three months before the end of a fiscal year. Its value is 1,200,000, or 100,000 a month. The minimum quantities are certain to be ordered in the first fiscal year, and the contract names the end of that year as the date in the Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year clause. The planner counts 300,000 as funded and the other 900,000 as value that waits for funds and written notice. No acceptance is planned past the stated date until the notice arrives.

## Never confuse

FAR 32.706-1(a) directs inserting the clause at 52.232-18, Availability of Funds, in solicitations and contracts if the contract will be chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year and the contract action will be initiated before the funds are available [@CL-FPA-0057]. Under FAR 32.706-1(b), the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.232-19, Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year, in solicitations and contracts if a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services is contemplated and the contract (1) is funded by annual appropriations and (2) is to extend beyond the initial fiscal year (see 32.703-2(b)), and FAR 52.232-19 states that funds are not presently available for performance under the contract beyond the stated date and that the Government's obligation for performance of the contract beyond that date is contingent upon the availability of appropriated funds from which payment for contract purposes can be made [@CL-FPA-0353; @CL-FPA-0354; @CL-FPA-0058; @CL-FPA-0061].
