## Purpose

Shows what a planner can and cannot assume when a contract starts before funds are available or runs past the end of a fiscal year, and which two clauses carry the condition.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-FPA-0052] |
| Codified citation | FAR 32.703-2; FAR 32.706-1; FAR 52.232-18; FAR 52.232-19 [@CL-FPA-0052; @CL-FPA-0057; @CL-FPA-0058; @CL-FPA-0059; @CL-FPA-0061] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 32.703-2; RFO FAR 32.706-1 [@CL-FPA-0274; @CL-FPA-0275; @CL-FPA-0276; @CL-FPA-0277; @CL-FPA-0278; @CL-FPA-0279; @CL-FPA-0280; @CL-FPA-0281; @CL-FPA-0282] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-FPA-0052] |
| Applies to | Contracts in which the Availability of Funds clause or the Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year clause is inserted [@CL-FPA-0057; @CL-FPA-0058] |

The contracting officer may initiate a contract action properly chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year before those funds are available, provided that the contract includes the Availability of Funds clause [@CL-FPA-0052]. Subject to a statutory-authority exception, this authority may be used only for operation and maintenance and continuing services necessary for normal operations and for which Congress previously had consistently appropriated funds [@CL-FPA-0053; @CL-FPA-0054].

A one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services funded by annual appropriations may extend beyond the fiscal year in which it begins, provided among other conditions that any specified minimum quantities are certain to be ordered in the initial fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0055]. A further condition is that the contract includes the Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year clause [@CL-FPA-0102]. The Government shall not accept supplies or services under a contract conditioned upon the availability of funds until the contracting officer has given the contractor notice, to be confirmed in writing, that funds are available [@CL-FPA-0056].

## How it works

The Availability of Funds clause is inserted in solicitations and contracts when the contract will be chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year and the action will be initiated before those funds are available [@CL-FPA-0057]. The Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year clause is inserted when a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services is contemplated, is funded by annual appropriations, and is to extend beyond the initial fiscal year [@CL-FPA-0058]. Under the first clause, funds are not presently available, the Government's obligation is contingent upon the availability of appropriated funds, and no legal liability for payment may arise until funds are made available and the contractor receives notice [@CL-FPA-0059; @CL-FPA-0060]. The second clause says the same for performance beyond a stated date [@CL-FPA-0061; @CL-FPA-0062].

Under the RFO text, DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 32 text in place of the codified text from February 1, 2026 [@CL-CTF-4046]. RFO FAR 32.703-2(a) keeps the authority to initiate a contract action properly chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year before these funds are available, provided that the contract includes the clause at 52.232-18, Availability of Funds (see 32.706-1(a)) [@CL-FPA-0274; @CL-FPA-0052]. It keeps the limit of that authority to operation and maintenance and continuing services (such as rentals, utilities, and supply items not financed by stock funds) that are necessary for normal operations [@CL-FPA-0275; @CL-FPA-0053] and for which Congress previously had consistently appropriated funds, unless specific statutory authority exists permitting applicability to other requirements [@CL-FPA-0276; @CL-FPA-0054]. RFO FAR 32.703-2(b) keeps the rule that a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services that is funded by annual appropriations may extend beyond the fiscal year in which it begins, provided that any specified minimum quantities are certain to be ordered in the initial fiscal year, but its cross-reference for that condition is to part 37 where the codified text says 37.106 [@CL-FPA-0277; @CL-FPA-0055]. It keeps the second condition, that the contract includes the clause at 52.232-19, Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year (see 32.706-1(b)) [@CL-FPA-0278; @CL-FPA-0102]. RFO FAR 32.703-2(c) says the Government will not accept supplies or services under a contract conditioned upon the availability of funds until the contracting officer has given the contractor notice, to be confirmed in writing, that funds are available, where the codified text says the Government shall not accept them [@CL-FPA-0279; @CL-FPA-0056]. RFO FAR 32.706-1(a) keeps the rule that the clause at 52.232-18 is inserted in solicitations and contracts if the contract will be chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year and the contract action will be initiated before the funds are available [@CL-FPA-0280; @CL-FPA-0057]. RFO FAR 32.706-1(b) keeps the rule that the clause at 52.232-19 is inserted in solicitations and contracts if a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services is contemplated [@CL-FPA-0281] and the contract is funded by annual appropriations and is to extend beyond the initial fiscal year (see 32.703-2(b)) [@CL-FPA-0282; @CL-FPA-0058], and states it as an imperative where the codified text says the contracting officer shall insert the clause [@CL-FPA-0281; @CL-FPA-0058].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A one-year requirements contract for services starts three months before the end of a fiscal year. Its value is 1,200,000, or 100,000 a month. The minimum quantities are certain to be ordered in the first fiscal year, and the contract names the end of that year as the date in the Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year clause. The planner counts 300,000 as funded and the other 900,000 as value that waits for funds and written notice. No acceptance is planned past the stated date until the notice arrives.

## Never confuse

The Availability of Funds clause covers a contract chargeable to funds of the new fiscal year that are not yet available [@CL-FPA-0057]. The Availability of Funds for the Next Fiscal Year clause covers a one-year indefinite-quantity or requirements contract for services whose funds are not presently available for performance beyond a date stated in the clause [@CL-FPA-0058; @CL-FPA-0061]. For incrementally funded contracts, see [Contract funding: incremental funding policy](../../contract-finance/funding/ctf-incremental-funding-policy.md) and [DoD incrementally funded fixed-price contracts](fpa-dod-incrementally-funded-fixed-price.md). The general funding rule is on [Funded versus unfunded contract value: the policy](fpa-funded-versus-unfunded-policy.md).
