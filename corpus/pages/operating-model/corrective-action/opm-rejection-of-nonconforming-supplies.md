## Purpose

Shows the usual remedy for nonconforming supplies or services and who may accept a critical, major or minor nonconformance.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-OPM-0062] |
| Codified citation | FAR 46.407(a) to (d) [@CL-OPM-0062; @CL-OPM-0063; @CL-OPM-0065; @CL-OPM-0068] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-OPM-0062] |
| Applies to | Supplies or services that do not conform in all respects to contract requirements [@CL-OPM-0062] |

The contracting officer should reject supplies or services not conforming in all respects to contract requirements (see FAR 46.102), and in those instances where deviation from that policy is found to be in the Government's interest, such supplies or services may be accepted only as authorized in FAR 46.407 [@CL-OPM-0062; @CL-OPM-0170]. The contracting officer ordinarily must give the contractor an opportunity to correct or replace nonconforming supplies or services when this can be accomplished within the required delivery schedule [@CL-OPM-0063]. Unless the contract specifies otherwise (as may be the case in some cost-reimbursement contracts), correction or replacement must be without additional cost to the Government [@CL-OPM-0064].

In situations not covered by FAR 46.407(b), the contracting officer ordinarily must reject supplies or services when the nonconformance is critical or major or the supplies or services are otherwise incomplete [@CL-OPM-0065], and there may be circumstances (e.g., reasons of economy or urgency) when the contracting officer determines acceptance or conditional acceptance of supplies or services is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-OPM-0066]. Before making a decision to accept, the contracting officer must obtain the concurrence of the activity responsible for the technical requirements of the contract and, where health factors are involved, of the responsible health official of the agency concerned [@CL-OPM-0067]. If the nonconformance is minor, the cognizant contract administration office may make the determination to accept or reject, except where this authority is withheld by the contracting office of the contracting activity [@CL-OPM-0068].

## How it works

The contracting officer should reject supplies or services not conforming in all respects to contract requirements (see FAR 46.102) [@CL-OPM-0062] and ordinarily must give the contractor an opportunity to correct or replace nonconforming supplies or services when this can be accomplished within the required delivery schedule [@CL-OPM-0063]. In situations not covered by FAR 46.407(b), the contracting officer ordinarily must reject supplies or services when the nonconformance is critical or major or the supplies or services are otherwise incomplete [@CL-OPM-0065], or may determine in circumstances such as reasons of economy or urgency that acceptance or conditional acceptance is in the best interest of the Government [@CL-OPM-0066]. Under FAR 46.407(c)(1), the contracting officer must make that determination based upon advice of the technical activity that the item is safe to use and will perform its intended purpose, information regarding the nature and extent of the nonconformance or otherwise incomplete supplies or services [@CL-OPM-0171], a request from the contractor for acceptance of the nonconforming or otherwise incomplete supplies or services (if feasible), a recommendation for acceptance, conditional acceptance, or rejection, with supporting rationale [@CL-OPM-0172], and the contract adjustment considered appropriate, including any adjustment offered by the contractor [@CL-OPM-0173]. Under FAR 46.407(c)(2), the cognizant contract administration office, or other Government activity directly involved, must furnish that data to the contracting officer in writing, except that in urgent cases it may be furnished orally and later confirmed in writing [@CL-OPM-0174], and before making a decision to accept, the contracting officer must obtain the concurrence of the activity responsible for the technical requirements of the contract and, where health factors are involved, of the responsible health official of the agency concerned [@CL-OPM-0067]. Under FAR 46.407(d), if the nonconformance is minor, the cognizant contract administration office may make the determination to accept or reject, except where this authority is withheld by the contracting office of the contracting activity, and to assist in making that determination, the contract administration office may establish a joint contractor-contract administrative office review group, while acceptance of supplies and services with critical or major nonconformances is outside the scope of the review group [@CL-OPM-0068; @CL-OPM-0069].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 200 units on time and 10 of them have a minor defect. Because the schedule allows, the contracting officer first lets the contractor correct or replace the 10 units at no extra cost to the Government. Separately, because the defect is minor, the cognizant contract administration office may decide whether to accept or reject those units, unless the contracting office withheld that authority. Had the defect been critical, the contracting officer would ordinarily reject, and could accept only with the concurrence of the activity responsible for the technical requirements and, where health factors are involved, of the responsible health official. The answer: the contract administration office decides the minor case and the contracting officer decides the critical case.

## Never confuse

Under FAR 46.407(b), the contracting officer ordinarily must give the contractor an opportunity to correct or replace nonconforming supplies or services when this can be accomplished within the required delivery schedule [@CL-OPM-0063], whereas FAR 46.407(c)(1) applies in situations not covered by FAR 46.407(b) when the nonconformance is critical or major or the supplies or services are otherwise incomplete [@CL-OPM-0065]. For the inspection clause, see [Inspection of Supplies—Fixed-Price clause](../../close-revrec/revenue/cls-inspection-of-supplies-fixed-price.md).
