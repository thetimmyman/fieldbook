## Purpose

Shows the price reduction and withholds on accepted nonconforming supplies, the written notice of rejection, and the counterfeit item and data exchange reporting rules.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-OPM-0070] |
| Codified citation | FAR 46.407(f) to (h) and FAR 46.317 [@CL-OPM-0070; @CL-OPM-0072; @CL-OPM-0074; @CL-OPM-0075] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-OPM-0070] |
| Applies to | Supplies or services accepted with critical or major nonconformances, rejected supplies or services, and contracts with the reporting nonconforming items clause [@CL-OPM-0070; @CL-OPM-0072; @CL-OPM-0075] |

When supplies or services are accepted with critical or major nonconformances as authorized in FAR 46.407(c), the contracting officer must modify the contract to provide for an equitable price reduction or other consideration [@CL-OPM-0070]. In the case of conditional acceptance, amounts withheld from payments generally should be at least sufficient to cover the estimated cost and related profit to correct deficiencies and complete unfinished work [@CL-OPM-0071], and the contracting officer must document in the contract file the basis for the amounts withheld [@CL-OPM-0122]. For services, the contracting officer can consider identifying the value of the individual work requirements or tasks (subdivisions) that may be subject to price or fee reduction, and this value may be used to determine an equitable adjustment for nonconforming services [@CL-OPM-0123]. However, when supplies or services involving minor nonconformances are accepted, the contract need not be modified unless it appears that the savings to the contractor in fabricating the nonconforming supplies or performing the nonconforming services will exceed the cost to the Government of processing the modification [@CL-OPM-0124; @CL-OPM-0168]. Notices of rejection must include the reasons for rejection and be furnished promptly to the contractor [@CL-OPM-0072], and the notice must be in writing if the supplies or services have been rejected at a place other than the contractor's plant, if the contractor persists in offering nonconforming supplies or services for acceptance, or if delivery or performance was late without excusable cause [@CL-OPM-0073]. Promptness in giving this notice is essential because, if timely nature of rejection is not furnished, acceptance may in certain cases be implied as a matter of law [@CL-OPM-0130].

The contracting officer shall provide disposition instructions for counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items in accordance with agency policy, and agency policy may require the contracting officer to direct the contractor to retain such items for investigative or evidentiary purposes [@CL-OPM-0074]. Except as provided in FAR 46.317(b), the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.246-26, Reporting Nonconforming Items, in solicitations and contracts as set out in FAR 46.317(a) [@CL-OPM-0075].

## How it works

Under FAR 46.317(a)(1), the clause at FAR 52.246-26 is inserted for an acquisition by any agency, including the Department of Defense, of any items that are subject to higher-level quality standards in accordance with the clause at FAR 52.246-11, Higher-Level Contract Quality Requirement [@CL-OPM-0126], or any items that the contracting officer, in consultation with the requiring activity, determines to be critical items for which use of the clause is appropriate [@CL-OPM-0127]. Under FAR 46.317(a)(2), in addition (as required by paragraph (c)(4) of section 818 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2012 (Public Law 112-81)), the clause is inserted for an acquisition that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and is by, or for, the Department of Defense of electronic parts or end items, components, parts, or materials containing electronic parts, whether or not covered in FAR 46.317(a)(1) [@CL-OPM-0169; @CL-OPM-0076]. Under FAR 46.317(a)(3), the clause is also inserted for the acquisition of services, if the contractor will furnish, as part of the service, any items that meet the criteria specified in FAR 46.317(a)(1) through (a)(2) [@CL-OPM-0128]. Under FAR 46.317(b), the contracting officer shall not insert the clause at FAR 52.246-26, Reporting Nonconforming Items, in solicitations and contracts when acquiring commercial products and commercial services using part 12 procedures [@CL-OPM-0077], or medical devices that are subject to the Food and Drug Administration reporting requirements at 21 CFR 803 [@CL-OPM-0099]. Under FAR 46.317(c), if required by agency policy, the contracting officer may modify paragraph (b)(4) of the clause at FAR 52.246-26, but only to change the responsibility for the contractor to submit reports to the agency rather than to the Government-Industry Data Exchange Program (GIDEP) [@CL-OPM-0078], so that the agency instead of the contractor submits reports to GIDEP within the mandatory 60 days [@CL-OPM-0079].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor delivers 100 pumps and the contracting officer conditionally accepts them. The estimated cost to correct the deficiencies is 4,000 and the related profit is 500. The withhold should generally be at least 4,500. The pumps are later rejected at the customer site, so the notice of rejection must be in writing and state the reasons. The answer: the withhold covers cost plus related profit, and the rejection notice is written because the rejection was away from the contractor's plant.

## Never confuse

When supplies or services are accepted with critical or major nonconformances as authorized in FAR 46.407(c), the contracting officer must modify the contract to provide for an equitable price reduction or other consideration [@CL-OPM-0070], whereas in the case of conditional acceptance, amounts withheld from payments generally should be at least sufficient to cover the estimated cost and related profit to correct deficiencies and complete unfinished work, and the contracting officer must document in the contract file the basis for the amounts withheld [@CL-OPM-0071; @CL-OPM-0122]. Under FAR 46.407(h), the contracting officer shall provide disposition instructions for counterfeit or suspect counterfeit items in accordance with agency policy, and agency policy may require the contracting officer to direct the contractor to retain such items for investigative or evidentiary purposes [@CL-OPM-0074], while FAR 46.317(a) prescribes when the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.246-26, Reporting Nonconforming Items, in solicitations and contracts, except as provided in FAR 46.317(b) [@CL-OPM-0075].
