## Purpose

Explains the criticality designators A, B and C, the link to DX-rated contracts, the production progress reports and the delay report.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-OPM-0055] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1105 and FAR 42.1106 [@CL-OPM-0055; @CL-OPM-0058] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.804 [@CL-OPM-0157; @CL-OPM-0158; @CL-OPM-0159]; RFO FAR 42.805 [@CL-OPM-0160; @CL-OPM-0161], which matches codified 42.1107 [@CL-CMP-2572; @CL-CMP-2573]; the RFO sections cited here print nothing that matches codified 42.1106 [@CL-OPM-0058; @CL-OPM-0059; @CL-OPM-0060] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-OPM-0055] |
| Applies to | Contracts to which the contracting officer assigns a criticality designator [@CL-OPM-0055] |

Contracting officers shall assign a criticality designator to each contract in the space for designating the contract administration office [@CL-OPM-0055]. Designator A covers critical contracts, including DX-rated contracts, contracts citing the unusual and compelling urgency authority, and contracts for major systems [@CL-OPM-0056]. Designator B covers contracts other than those designated A for items needed to maintain a Government or contractor production or repair line, to preclude out-of-stock conditions or to meet user needs for nonstock items, and designator C covers all contracts other than those designated A or B [@CL-OPM-0057].

When information on contract performance status is needed, contracting officers may require contractors to submit production progress reports [@CL-OPM-0058]. Reporting requirements shall be limited to that information essential to Government needs and shall take maximum advantage of data output generated by contractor management systems [@CL-OPM-0059].

## How it works

The contracting officer assigns the designator [@CL-OPM-0055] and may require the contractor to submit production progress reports [@CL-OPM-0058]. The contract administration office may at any time initiate a report to advise the contracting officer, and the inventory manager if one is designated in the contract, of any potential or actual delay in performance [@CL-OPM-0060]. That advice shall be in writing, be provided in sufficient time for the contracting officer to take necessary action, and provide a definite recommendation, if action is appropriate [@CL-OPM-0061]. The DX-rated reference cites subpart 11.6, which this card does not explain [@CL-OPM-0056]. The paragraph on progress reports sets no due date or form for them.

DoD contracting officers use the RFO FAR part 42 text in place of the codified FAR part 42 text because DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs it, effective March 16, 2026 [@CL-CMP-9759] [@CL-CMP-9761]. RFO 42.804 keeps the duty to assign a criticality designator to each contract in the space for designating the contract administration office [@CL-OPM-0157] [@CL-OPM-0055] and keeps designators B and C [@CL-OPM-0159] [@CL-OPM-0057]. Designator A keeps the same categories, but its cross-references change: DX-rated contracts cite part 11, the urgency authority cites part 6 and major systems cite part 34 [@CL-OPM-0158] [@CL-OPM-0056]. RFO 42.805(a) prescribes the clause at 52.242-2, Production Progress Reports, when production progress reporting is required, unless a construction contract or a Federal Supply Schedule contract is contemplated [@CL-OPM-0160], as codified 42.1107(a) does [@CL-CMP-2572], and codified 42.1106(a) points to that paragraph [@CL-OPM-0058]. RFO 42.805(b) says to specify appropriate reporting instructions in the Schedule when the clause is used [@CL-OPM-0161], where codified 42.1107(b) has the contracting officer specify them and refers to 42.1106(a) [@CL-CMP-2573]. The RFO sections cited here do not print the codified sentences that let contracting officers require production progress reports when information on contract performance status is needed [@CL-OPM-0058], that limit reporting requirements to information essential to Government needs [@CL-OPM-0059], or that provide for the delay report [@CL-OPM-0060]. The RFO list of contract administration functions has an item to perform production support, surveillance and status reporting, including timely reporting of potential and actual slippages in contract delivery schedules [@CL-OPM-0162], in the same words as codified 42.302(a)(31) [@CL-OPM-0167]; the item does not say that the contract administration office may initiate a report at any time to advise the contracting officer, as the codified delay report paragraph does [@CL-OPM-0060].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company holds a contract for a major system. The contracting officer assigns designator A and the contract requires a monthly production progress report. In month 7 the contract administration office sees that a supplier part will arrive late and sends the contracting officer a written note with a recommended action. The answer: the contract is designator A, the monthly report is a contractor submission required by the contracting officer, and the delay note is the Government's own written report.

## Never confuse

Designator A is not the same as a DX rating: DX-rated contracts fall under A, but A also covers urgent-authority contracts and contracts for major systems [@CL-OPM-0056]. The delay report is not the production progress report: contracting officers may require contractors to submit progress reports [@CL-OPM-0058], while the contract administration office may initiate the delay report [@CL-OPM-0060]. The paragraph on the delay report names the contract administration office; it does not name the administrative contracting officer. The authority to require production progress reports and the clause prescription are different sentences: codified 42.1106(a) lets contracting officers require the reports when information on contract performance status is needed [@CL-OPM-0058], while the clause prescription is codified 42.1107(a) and RFO 42.805(a), which says to insert the production progress reports clause when production progress reporting is required [@CL-CMP-2572] [@CL-OPM-0160].
