## Purpose

Explains the criticality designators A, B and C, the link to DX-rated contracts, the production progress reports and the delay report.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

Contracting officers shall assign a criticality designator to each contract in the space for designating the contract administration office [@CL-OPM-0055]. Designator A covers critical contracts, including DX-rated contracts (see FAR subpart 11.6), contracts citing the authority in FAR 6.302-2 (unusual and compelling urgency), and contracts for major systems [@CL-OPM-0056]. Designator B covers contracts (other than those designated A) for items needed to maintain a Government or contractor production or repair line, to preclude out-of-stock conditions or to meet user needs for nonstock items, and designator C covers all contracts other than those designated A or B [@CL-OPM-0057].

When information on contract performance status is needed, contracting officers may require contractors to submit production progress reports (see FAR 42.1107(a)) [@CL-OPM-0058]. Reporting requirements shall be limited to that information essential to Government needs and shall take maximum advantage of data output generated by contractor management systems [@CL-OPM-0059].

## How it works

The contracting officer shall assign a criticality designator to each contract in the space for designating the contract administration office [@CL-OPM-0055] and, when information on contract performance status is needed, may require contractors to submit production progress reports (see FAR 42.1107(a)) [@CL-OPM-0058]. Under FAR 42.1107(a) and (b), the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.242-2, Production Progress Reports, in solicitations and contracts when production progress reporting is required, unless a construction contract or a Federal Supply Schedule contract is contemplated [@CL-CMP-2572], and when the clause at FAR 52.242-2 is used, the contracting officer shall specify appropriate reporting instructions in the Schedule (see FAR 42.1106(a)) [@CL-CMP-2573]. The contract administration office may at any time initiate a report to advise the contracting officer (and the inventory manager, if one is designated in the contract) of any potential or actual delay in performance [@CL-OPM-0060]. This advice shall be in writing, be provided in sufficient time for the contracting officer to take necessary action, and provide a definite recommendation, if action is appropriate [@CL-OPM-0061].

Effective March 16, 2026, DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 42, Contract Administration and Audit Services, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9759; @CL-CMP-9761]. Under RFO FAR 42.804, contracting officers must assign a criticality designator to each contract in the space for designating the contract administration office in the contract [@CL-OPM-0157]: designator A covers critical contracts, including DX-rated contracts (see part 11), contracts citing the authority in part 6 for unusual and compelling urgency, and contracts for major systems (see part 34) [@CL-OPM-0158], designator B covers contracts (other than those designated A) for items needed to maintain a Government or contractor production or repair line, to preclude out-of-stock conditions, or to meet user needs for nonstock items, and designator C covers all contracts other than those designated A or B [@CL-OPM-0159]. Under RFO FAR 42.805(a) and (b), insert the clause at FAR 52.242-2, Production Progress Reports, in solicitations and contracts when production progress reporting is required, unless a construction contract or a Federal Supply Schedule contract is contemplated [@CL-OPM-0160], and when the clause at FAR 52.242-2 is used, specify appropriate reporting instructions in the Schedule [@CL-OPM-0161]. Under RFO FAR 42.302(a)(31), to the extent they apply to a specific contract, the contracting officer normally delegates to a CAO the contract administration functions in RFO FAR 42.302(a), where item (31) is to perform production support, surveillance, and status reporting, including timely reporting of potential and actual slippages in contract delivery schedules [@CL-OPM-0149; @CL-CMP-0027; @CL-OPM-0162]. Under codified FAR 42.302(a)(31), to the extent they apply, the contracting officer normally delegates to a CAO the contract administration functions in FAR 42.302(a), where item (31) is to perform production support, surveillance, and status reporting, including timely reporting of potential and actual slippages in contract delivery schedules [@CL-CMP-1006; @CL-CMP-1007; @CL-OPM-0167].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A company holds a contract for a major system. The contracting officer assigns designator A and the contract requires a monthly production progress report. In month 7 the contract administration office sees that a supplier part will arrive late and sends the contracting officer a written note with a recommended action. The answer: the contract is designator A, the monthly report is a contractor submission required by the contracting officer, and the delay note is the Government's own written report.

## Never confuse

Designator A covers critical contracts, including DX-rated contracts (see FAR subpart 11.6), contracts citing the authority in FAR 6.302-2 (unusual and compelling urgency), and contracts for major systems [@CL-OPM-0056]. When information on contract performance status is needed, contracting officers may require contractors to submit production progress reports (see FAR 42.1107(a)) [@CL-OPM-0058], whereas the contract administration office may at any time initiate a report to advise the contracting officer (and the inventory manager, if one is designated in the contract) of any potential or actual delay in performance [@CL-OPM-0060]. Under FAR 42.1107(a), the contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.242-2, Production Progress Reports, in solicitations and contracts when production progress reporting is required, unless a construction contract or a Federal Supply Schedule contract is contemplated [@CL-CMP-2572], and under RFO FAR 42.805(a), the clause at FAR 52.242-2, Production Progress Reports, is inserted in solicitations and contracts when production progress reporting is required, unless a construction contract or a Federal Supply Schedule contract is contemplated [@CL-OPM-0160].
