## Purpose

Shows what a determination and findings must contain, who signs it, and how signature authority is shown in the applicable FAR part.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-OPM-0027] |
| Codified citation | FAR 1.700; FAR 1.704; FAR 1.707 [@CL-OPM-1023; @CL-OPM-0027; @CL-OPM-0031] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-OPM-0027] |
| Applies to | Any D&F that is required [@CL-OPM-0031] |

Each D&F shall set forth enough facts and circumstances to clearly and convincingly justify the specific determination made and, as a minimum, shall include, in the prescribed agency format, the identification of the agency and of the contracting activity and specific identifications of the document as a Determination and Findings, the nature and/or description of the action being approved, the citation of the appropriate statute and/or regulation upon which the D&F is based [@CL-OPM-0027; @CL-OPM-0028], findings that detail the particular circumstances, facts, or reasoning essential to support the determination (with necessary supporting documentation obtained from appropriate requirements and technical personnel) [@CL-OPM-0029], a determination, based on the findings, that the proposed action is justified under the applicable statute or regulation, the expiration date of the D&F, if required (see FAR 1.706(b)), and the signature of the official authorized to sign the D&F (see FAR 1.706) and the date signed [@CL-OPM-0030].

When a D&F is required, it shall be signed by the appropriate official in accordance with agency regulations, and authority to sign or delegate signature authority for the various D&Fs is as shown in the applicable FAR part [@CL-OPM-0031].

The determinations and findings subpart prescribes general policies and procedures for the use of determinations and findings (D&Fs), and requirements for specific types of D&Fs can be found with the appropriate subject matter [@CL-OPM-1023].

## How it works

When a D&F is required, it shall be signed by the appropriate official in accordance with agency regulations, and authority to sign or delegate signature authority for the various D&Fs is as shown in the applicable FAR part [@CL-OPM-0031]. Contracting officers have authority to enter into, administer, or terminate contracts and make related determinations and findings, and may bind the Government only to the extent of the authority delegated to them [@CL-OPM-0001]. Each D&F shall set forth enough facts and circumstances to clearly and convincingly justify the specific determination made and, as a minimum, include in the prescribed agency format the agency and contracting activity identification and document identification, the nature and/or description of the action being approved, the citation of the appropriate statute and/or regulation [@CL-OPM-0027; @CL-OPM-0028], findings detailing the essential circumstances, facts, or reasoning with necessary supporting documentation from appropriate requirements and technical personnel [@CL-OPM-0029], the determination based on the findings, the expiration date if required (see FAR 1.706(b)), and the signature of the official authorized to sign (see FAR 1.706) and the date signed [@CL-OPM-0030]. For FAR structure, see [The FAR, the DFARS, the overhaul and class deviations](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-regulations.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer files a D&F for an action worth 5,000,000. The D&F names the agency, cites a statute, lists findings with technical support, states the determination, shows an expiration date, and is signed by the official the applicable FAR part names. The finance reader confirms that the D&F includes an expiration date, as required by FAR 1.706(b).

## Never confuse

A deviation and a determination and findings are not the same tool: unless precluded by law, executive order, or regulation, deviations from the FAR may be granted as specified in FAR subpart 1.4 when necessary to meet the specific needs and requirements of each agency [@CL-OPM-0021], whereas a D&F sets forth enough facts and circumstances to clearly and convincingly justify the specific determination made and includes a determination, based on the findings, that the proposed action is justified under the applicable statute or regulation [@CL-OPM-0027; @CL-OPM-0030].
