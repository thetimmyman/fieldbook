## Purpose

Shows what a determination and findings must contain, who signs it, and what the printed text leaves to the FAR part that requires it.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-OPM-0027] |
| Codified citation | FAR 1.700; FAR 1.704; FAR 1.707 [@CL-OPM-1023; @CL-OPM-0027; @CL-OPM-0031] |
| RFO citation | Not established. |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-OPM-0027] |
| Applies to | Any D&F that is required [@CL-OPM-0031] |

Each D&F shall set forth enough facts and circumstances to clearly and convincingly justify the specific determination made and, as a minimum, shall include the listed information in the prescribed agency format [@CL-OPM-0027]. Three listed items are the identification of the agency and contracting activity and of the document as a Determination and Findings, the nature or description of the action being approved, and the citation of the statute or regulation on which the D&F is based [@CL-OPM-0028]. One listed item is findings that detail the particular circumstances, facts, or reasoning essential to support the determination, with necessary supporting documentation obtained from appropriate requirements and technical personnel [@CL-OPM-0029]. Three further listed items are a determination based on the findings that the proposed action is justified under the applicable statute or regulation, an expiration date if required, and the signature of the official authorized to sign and the date signed [@CL-OPM-0030].

When a D&F is required, it shall be signed by the appropriate official in accordance with agency regulations, and authority to sign or delegate signature authority for the various D&Fs is as shown in the applicable FAR part [@CL-OPM-0031]. The section does not name any level of signature authority that cannot be delegated. It points to the applicable FAR part for each D&F instead [@CL-OPM-0031].

The determinations and findings subpart prescribes general policies and procedures for the use of determinations and findings (D&F's), and requirements for specific types of D&F's can be found with the appropriate subject matter [@CL-OPM-1023].

## How it works

The D&F is signed by the appropriate official in accordance with agency regulations, and the FAR part that requires it shows the authority to sign or delegate signature authority [@CL-OPM-0031]. The D&F states the facts, cites the statute or regulation, makes the determination, and carries the signature and date [@CL-OPM-0028; @CL-OPM-0029; @CL-OPM-0030]. The section does not list the finance decisions a D&F supports. It does say that contracting officers have authority to make determinations and findings related to contracts [@CL-OPM-0001]. A finance reader can check that each listed item is present, that the signer is the authorized official, and that any expiration date has not passed [@CL-OPM-0028; @CL-OPM-0029; @CL-OPM-0030]. FAR structure is explained on [The FAR, the DFARS, the overhaul and class deviations](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-regulations.md).

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contracting officer files a D&F for an action worth 5,000,000. The D&F names the agency, cites a statute, lists findings with technical support, states the determination, shows an expiration date, and is signed by the official the applicable FAR part names. The finance reader sees that the expiration date has passed and asks the contracting officer whether a new D&F is needed before relying on it.

## Never confuse

A D&F is not a deviation from the FAR. Unless precluded by law, executive order, or regulation, a deviation may be granted as specified in the subpart when necessary to meet the specific needs and requirements of each agency [@CL-OPM-0021], while a D&F records a determination that an action is justified under a statute or regulation [@CL-OPM-0030].
