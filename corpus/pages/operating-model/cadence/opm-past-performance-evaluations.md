## Purpose

Shows which contracts get a past performance evaluation, the deadlines that run from it, who reviews disagreements, how long the information is used, and how late small business subcontract payments enter the evaluation.

## The rule

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | Codified FAR [@CL-OPM-0038] |
| Codified citation | FAR 42.1502; FAR 42.1503 [@CL-OPM-0038; @CL-OPM-0046]; FAR 2.101 [@CL-OPM-1103] |
| RFO citation | RFO FAR 42.1102 [@CL-OPM-0131; @CL-OPM-0132; @CL-OPM-0133]; RFO FAR 42.1103 [@CL-OPM-0142; @CL-OPM-0117; @CL-OPM-0145; @CL-OPM-0148]; RFO FAR 2.101 [@CL-OPM-1121] |
| Edition date | Not established. |
| Text status | Final [@CL-OPM-0038] |
| Applies to | Each contract (as defined in FAR part 2) that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and each order that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold, except as provided in FAR 42.1502(e), (f), and (h) [@CL-OPM-0039] |

Past performance evaluations shall be prepared at least annually and at the time the work under a contract or order is completed [@CL-OPM-0038]. Except as provided in FAR 42.1502(e), (f), and (h), agencies shall prepare evaluations of contractor performance for each contract (as defined in FAR part 2) that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and for each order that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-OPM-0039]. Under FAR 42.1502(h), agencies shall not evaluate performance for contracts awarded under FAR subpart 8.7 [@CL-OPM-0112]. Under FAR 42.1502(e), past performance evaluations shall be prepared for each construction contract of $900,000 or more, and for each construction contract terminated for default regardless of contract value [@CL-OPM-0040]. Under FAR 42.1502(f), past performance evaluations shall be prepared for each architect-engineer services contract of $45,000 or more, and for each architect-engineer services contract that is terminated for default regardless of contract value [@CL-OPM-0041]. For the simplified acquisition threshold, see [Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase thresholds](../../compliance/regulatory-framework/cmp-acquisition-thresholds.md).

Under FAR 2.101, past performance means an offeror's or contractor's performance on active and physically completed contracts (see FAR 4.804-4) [@CL-OPM-1103]. Under RFO FAR 2.101, past performance means an offeror's or contractor's performance on active and physically completed contracts (see FAR 4.804-4) [@CL-OPM-1121].

Past performance evaluations shall include an assessment of the contractor's performance against, and efforts to achieve, the goals identified in the small business subcontracting plan when the contract includes the clause at FAR 52.219-9, Small Business Subcontracting Plan, and reduced or untimely payments (as defined in FAR 19.701), made to small business subcontractors, determined by the contracting officer to be unjustified [@CL-OPM-0042; @CL-OPM-0043]. The contracting officer shall consider and evaluate a contractor's written explanation for a reduced or an untimely payment when determining whether the reduced or untimely payment is justified, and determine that a history of unjustified reduced or untimely payments has occurred when the contractor has reported three or more occasions of unjustified reduced or untimely payments under a single contract within a 12-month period [@CL-OPM-0044; @CL-OPM-0045]. The following payment or nonpayment situations are not considered to be unjustified: there is a contract dispute on performance, a partial payment is made for amounts not in dispute, a payment is reduced due to past overpayments, there is an administrative mistake, or late performance by the subcontractor leads to later payment by the prime contractor [@CL-OPM-0115; @CL-OPM-0116].

## How it works

Contractors shall be afforded up to 14 calendar days from the date of notification of availability of the past performance evaluation to submit comments, rebutting statements, or additional information [@CL-OPM-0046]. Agencies shall provide for review at a level above the contracting officer to consider disagreements between the parties regarding the evaluation, and the ultimate conclusion on the performance evaluation is a decision of the contracting agency [@CL-OPM-0047]. Agencies shall prepare and submit all past performance evaluations electronically in CPARS [@CL-OPM-0113]. These evaluations, including any contractor-submitted information (with indication whether agency review is pending), become available for source selection officials not later than 14 days after the date on which the contractor is notified of the evaluation's availability for comment [@CL-OPM-0048]. Past performance evaluations for classified contracts and special access programs shall not be reported in CPARS, but will be reported as stated in FAR subpart 42.15 and in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-OPM-0114]. Agencies shall use the past performance information in CPARS that is within three years (six for construction and architect-engineer contracts) of the completion of performance of the evaluated contract or order, and information contained in the Federal Awardee Performance and Integrity Information System (FAPIIS), e.g., terminations for default or cause [@CL-OPM-0049; @CL-OPM-0163]. Under FAR 42.1502(i), agencies shall promptly report other contractor information in accordance with FAR 42.1503(h) [@CL-OPM-0164].

Effective March 16, 2026, DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0050 directs contracting officers to use the revised FAR part 42, Contract Administration and Audit Services, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 [@CL-CMP-9759; @CL-CMP-9761]. Under RFO FAR 42.1102(a), past performance evaluations must be prepared at least annually and at the time the work under a contract or order is completed [@CL-OPM-0131]. Under RFO FAR 42.1102(b)(1), except as provided in paragraphs (e), (f), and (h) of that section, agencies must prepare evaluations of contractor performance for each contract and order that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold and for each order that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold [@CL-OPM-0132]. Under RFO FAR 42.1102(e) and (f), agencies must prepare past performance evaluations for each construction contract of $900,000 or more and each construction contract terminated for default regardless of contract value [@CL-OPM-0134], and past performance evaluations must be prepared for each architect-engineer services contract of $45,000 or more and each architect-engineer services contract that is terminated for default regardless of contract value [@CL-OPM-0135]. Under RFO FAR 42.1102(g), past performance evaluations must include an assessment of the contractor's performance against, and efforts to achieve, the goals identified in the small business subcontracting plan when the contract includes the clause at FAR 52.219-9, Small Business Subcontracting Plan, and reduced or untimely payments (as defined in part 19), made to small business subcontractors, determined by the contracting officer to be unjustified [@CL-OPM-0136; @CL-OPM-0137]. Under RFO FAR 42.1102(g)(2)(i) and (ii), the contracting officer must consider and evaluate a contractor's written explanation for a reduced or an untimely payment when determining whether the reduced or untimely payment is justified, and determine that a history of unjustified reduced or untimely payments has occurred when the contractor has reported three or more occasions of unjustified reduced or untimely payments under a single contract within a 12-month period [@CL-OPM-0138; @CL-OPM-0139], with the same five payment or nonpayment situations not considered to be unjustified [@CL-OPM-0140; @CL-OPM-0141]. Under RFO FAR 42.1102(h), agencies must promptly report other contractor information in accordance with RFO FAR 42.1103(g) [@CL-OPM-0133]. Under RFO FAR 42.1103(d)(2) and (3), contractors must be afforded up to 14 calendar days from the date of notification of availability of the past performance evaluation to submit comments, rebutting statements, or additional information [@CL-OPM-0142], and while the evaluation is an agency decision, agencies must provide for review at a level above the contracting officer in the event of disagreements between the parties [@CL-OPM-0143]. Under RFO FAR 42.1103(e)(1), (2), and (5), agencies must prepare and submit all past performance evaluations electronically in CPARS at https://www.cpars.gov [@CL-OPM-0144], the agency must make the past performance evaluation, including any contractor response, including any contractor-submitted information (with indication whether agency review is pending), available to source selection officials in the CPARS system within 14 days after the contractor notification of the evaluation's availability for comment [@CL-OPM-0117; @CL-OPM-0145], and past performance evaluations for classified contracts and special access programs must not be reported in CPARS but will be reported as stated in that subpart and in accordance with agency procedures [@CL-OPM-0146]. Under RFO FAR 42.1103(f), agencies must use the past performance information in CPARS that is within 3 years (6 years for construction and architect-engineer contracts) of the completion of performance of the evaluated contract or order, and information contained in responsibility/qualification reports in the System Award Management (SAM), at SAM.gov, e.g., terminations for default or cause [@CL-OPM-0147; @CL-OPM-0148].

## Worked example

Synthetic example. The numbers are made up.

A contractor is told on the first of the month that an evaluation of its 10,000,000 contract is ready for comment. It can comment for up to 14 calendar days from that notice. Source selection officials must be able to see the evaluation no later than 14 days after the notice. Separately, two late payments to small business subcontractors on one contract are found unjustified in the first six months, and a third is found in month eleven. The contractor reports all three occasions, so the contracting officer determines that a history has occurred.

## Never confuse

Agencies shall provide for review at a level above the contracting officer to consider disagreements between the parties regarding the evaluation, and the ultimate conclusion on the performance evaluation is a decision of the contracting agency [@CL-OPM-0047]. Both the period of up to 14 calendar days for contractor comments, rebutting statements, or additional information and the 14-day period after which the evaluation and any contractor-submitted information (with indication whether agency review is pending) become available for source selection officials run from notification to the contractor of the evaluation's availability [@CL-OPM-0046; @CL-OPM-0048]. Under FAR 42.1502(h), agencies shall not evaluate performance for contracts awarded under FAR subpart 8.7 [@CL-OPM-0112], and under FAR 42.1502(i), agencies shall promptly report other contractor information in accordance with FAR 42.1503(h) [@CL-OPM-0164], whereas under RFO FAR 42.1102(h), agencies must promptly report other contractor information in accordance with RFO FAR 42.1103(g) [@CL-OPM-0133].
